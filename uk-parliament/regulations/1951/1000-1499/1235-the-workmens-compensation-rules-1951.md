# The Workmen's Compensation Rules 1951

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1951 No. 1235

Workmen's Compensation

The Workmen'S Compensation Rules, 1951

Made

9th July 1951

Coming into Operation

23rd July 1951

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1951.

**2.** The Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1926, as amended, shall be further amended as follows:—

- (a) After sub-paragraph (d) of paragraph (10) of Rule 60, the following proviso shall be inserted:—

  > Provided that orders for payment out of court of any sum of money may be made by the registrar.
- (b) Rule 64B shall be deleted.

  We, the undersigned, members of the Rule Committee appointed under s. 99 of the County Courts Act, 1934, having by virtue of the powers vested in us by s. 27 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1925, as amended by s. 8 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1943, made the foregoing Rules, do hereby certify the same under our hands and submit them to the Lord Chancellor accordingly.

Donald Hurst

J. Alun Pugh

William Stewart

Edgar T. Dale

W.M. Andrew

R.T. Monier-Williams

Gilbert Hicks

I allow these Rules which shall come into force on the 23rd day of July, 1951.

Jowitt, C

Dated the 9th day of July, 1951

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1926, as amended so as to give the Registrar of a County Court power to order payment out of Court of money in Court.
