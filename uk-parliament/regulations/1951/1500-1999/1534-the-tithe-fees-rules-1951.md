# The Tithe Fees Rules 1951

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1951 No. 1534

TITHE, ENGLAND

The Tithe Fees Rules, 1951

Made

20th August 1951

Laid before Parliament

21st August 1951

Coming into Operation

1st September 1951

By virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred by subsection (2) of Section 7 and by subsection (4) of Section 10 of the Tithe Act, 1951, the Tithe Redemption Commission, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following rules:—

**1.** In these Rules—

- (a) “**Commission**” means the “Tithe Redemption Commission” and
- (b) “**corn rent annuity**” means an annuity charged on land for the redemption of tithe rent charge or of a corn rent, rentcharge or money payment redeemed under the Tithe Acts, 1936 to 1936.

**2.** These Rules may be cited as The Tithe Fees Rules, 1951, and shall come into operation on the first day of September, 1951.

**3.** The fees set out in the Schedules to these Rules shall be payable for and in respect of the matters therein more particularly described.

In witness whereof the Tithe Redemption Commission have caused their Official Seal to be hereunto affixed this 15th day of August, 1951.

H. G. Richardson

Secretary to the Tithe Redemption Commission

Approved.

W. A. Wilkins

H. W. Bowden

Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury

20th August 1951

## FIRST SCHEDULE

### PART I

For copies of or extracts from documents relating to tithes or tithe rentcharges and made under the Tithe Acts 1836 to 1925, a fee (exclusive of any fee for a map) at the rate of 4d. per folio of 72 words with a minimum of 3s. 6d.

### PART II

For a copy of or extract from a map forming part of or referred to in any of the documents mentioned in Part I of this Schedule, a fee calculated at the following rates per square foot, or part thereof, of the copy or extract:—

- Tracing:—On linen, 10s. 6d.; on paper, 7s. 6d.
- Photographic copy (where practicable):—2s.

### PART III

Additional fee for the certification of the truth of any such copy or extract as is mentioned in this Schedule:—

- (1) in respect of documents other than maps, a fee of 1s. and
- (2) in respect of maps, a fee equal to one half of the fee for the supply of the copy or extract.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules prescribe the fees payable to the Tithe Redemption Commission for the supply of copies of documents made under the Tithe Acts, 1836 to 1951. They also prescribe fees for the apportionment and redemption of annuities charged on land for the redemption of tithe rentcharge, corn rents, rentcharges or money payments redeemed under the Tithe Acts, 1836 to 1925, which are private property and are distinct from redemption annuities charged in respect of land and payable to the Crown by virtue of the Tithe Act, 1936 (26 Geo. 5. & 1 Edw. 8. c. 43).
