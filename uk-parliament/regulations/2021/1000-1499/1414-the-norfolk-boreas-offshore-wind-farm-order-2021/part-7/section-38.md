# The Norfolk Boreas Offshore Wind Farm Order 2021, section 38

> Text as at 2025-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Arbitration[^c-8675465b052f855257f1c37a6ceaf382]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to article 41 (saving provisions for Trinity House), any difference under any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for, must be referred to and settled in arbitration in accordance with the rules at Schedule 15 (arbitration rules) of this Order, by a single arbitrator to be agreed upon by the parties, within 14 days of receipt of the notice of arbitration, or if the parties fail to agree within the time period stipulated, to be appointed on application of either party (after giving written notice to the other) by the Secretary of State.

(2) Any matter for which the consent or approval of the Secretary of State or the Marine Management Organisation is required under any provision of this Order shall not be subject to arbitration.
