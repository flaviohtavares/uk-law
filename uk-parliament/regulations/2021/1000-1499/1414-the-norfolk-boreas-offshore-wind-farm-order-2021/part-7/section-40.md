# The Norfolk Boreas Offshore Wind Farm Order 2021, section 40

> Text as at 2025-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Abatement of works abandoned or decayed[^c-349546f0ad1491d79214d835b86707e1]

*Extent: E+W*

Where Work Nos. 1(a) to (f) or Work No. 2 or any part of those works is abandoned or allowed to fall into decay the Secretary of State may, following consultation with the undertaker, issue a written notice requiring the undertaker at its own expense to repair and restore or remove Work Nos. 1(a) to (f) or Work No. 2 or any relevant part of those works and, without prejudice to any notice served under section 105(2) of the 2004 Act[^f00042], to restore the site of the relevant part(s) of Work Nos. 1(a) to (f) or Work No. 2 to a safe and proper condition within an area and to such an extent as may be specified in the notice.
