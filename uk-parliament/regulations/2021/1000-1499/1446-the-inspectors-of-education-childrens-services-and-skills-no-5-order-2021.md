# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 1446

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2021

Made

15th December 2021

Coming into force

16th December 2021

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 15th day of December 2021

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2021 and comes into force on 16th December 2021.

## 2. Appointment as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Person Appointed as one of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 16th December 2021

Article 2

Susan Jane Will

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the person named in the Schedule as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
