# The National Security and Investment Act 2021 (Notifiable Acquisition) (Specification of Qualifying Entities) Regulations 2021, SCHEDULE 12

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 12 — Military and dual-use

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 2

### 1. Interpretation[^key-d0daebac1ca25b0e4d0068b123220b77]

In this Schedule—

- “restricted goods” and “restricted technology” are respectively goods and technology, including software or information (other than information in the public domain), the export or transfer of which is controlled by virtue of their being specified in the relevant export control legislation;
- “**relevant export control legislation**” means—
  - (a) Schedules 2 and 3 to the Export Control Order 2008[^f00045];
  - (b) the Schedule to the Export of Radioactive Sources (Control) Order 2006[^f00046];
  - (c) Annex I to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2009/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1942/2009)8 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items.

### 2. Activity - military and dual-use[^key-1aba25e72d07378b32c352798af22471]

A qualifying entity carrying on activities that consist of or include researching, developing or producing restricted goods or restricted technology.
