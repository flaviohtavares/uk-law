## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-aeb26210cfb5f1d59f81dca4b017c682]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Security and Investment Act 2021 (Notifiable Acquisition) (Specification of Qualifying Entities) Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 4th January 2022.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
