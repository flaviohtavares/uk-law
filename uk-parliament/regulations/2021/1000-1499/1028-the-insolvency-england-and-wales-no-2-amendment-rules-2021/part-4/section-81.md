# The Insolvency (England and Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) Rules 2021, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**81.** Schedule 2 makes amendments of the Insolvency Rules in consequence of the repeal of Schedule A1 to the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00015].

## Annotations

[^f00015]: Footnote: Schedule A1 was repealed by paragraph 30 of Schedule 3 to the Corporate Insolvency and Governance Act 2020.
