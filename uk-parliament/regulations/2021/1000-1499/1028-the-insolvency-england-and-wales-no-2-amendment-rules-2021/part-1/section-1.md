# The Insolvency (England and Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) Rules 2021, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation commencement and interpretation

These Rules may be cited as the Insolvency (England and Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) Rules 2021 and come into force on 1st October 2021.

**2.** In these rules the “Insolvency Rules” means the Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016[^f00002].
