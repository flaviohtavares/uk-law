> Text as at 2025-02-24.

## 4. Amendment of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015[^c-ebdd859aa3907eca867bc5084fb90f43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015[^f00005] (interpretation), in the definition of “NACE Rev 2”, for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.
