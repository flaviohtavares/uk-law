# The National Security and Investment Act 2021 (Prescribed Form and Content of Notices and Validation Applications) Regulations 2021, section 1

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-ae3e6e128fb43e6fe2244bf0fe08d943]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Security and Investment Act 2021 (Prescribed Form and Content of Notices and Validation Applications) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 4th January 2022.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
