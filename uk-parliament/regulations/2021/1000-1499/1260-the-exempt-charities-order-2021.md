# The Exempt Charities Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 1260

Charities

The Exempt Charities Order 2021

Made

10th November 2021

Coming into force

11th November 2021

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 10th day of November 2021

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph 11(A) of Schedule 3 to the Charities Act 2011[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 2021 and shall come into force on 11th November 2021.

**2.** TEDI-London is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 2011.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares TEDI-London to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 2011 with effect from 11th November 2021. An exempt charity cannot be registered with the Charity Commission and the Commission cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except:

- (a) it may exercise advisory and permissive powers at the charity’s request, following consultation with any principal regulator appointed under the Charities Act 2011;
- (b) it may open a statutory inquiry and use powers to act for the protection of charities at the invitation of any principal regulator appointed under the Charities Act.

The Office for Students (OfS) has been appointed principal regulator for exempt Higher Education Providers in England, and automatically becomes principal regulator for TEDI-London.
