# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 240

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 240. Preliminary[^key-2e71f60f7b2557693ffdeceddd567379]

*Extent: E+W*

The rules in this Part—

- (a) relate to the permission required under section 216 of the IA 1986[^f00025] for a person to act in all or any of the ways specified in section 216(3) in relation to an institution with a prohibited name, and
- (b) prescribe the cases excepted from section 216, that is to say, those in which a person to whom that section applies may so act without that permission.
