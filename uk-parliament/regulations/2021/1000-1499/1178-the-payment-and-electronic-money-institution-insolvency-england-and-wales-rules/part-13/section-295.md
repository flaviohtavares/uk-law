# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 295

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 295. Application of the IA 1986 and the Company Directors Disqualification Act[^key-e4240de84fe94593d7ca9489977dac35]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of these Rules, any reference in IA 1986 or the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^f00030] to “leave” of the court is to be construed as meaning “permission” of the court.
