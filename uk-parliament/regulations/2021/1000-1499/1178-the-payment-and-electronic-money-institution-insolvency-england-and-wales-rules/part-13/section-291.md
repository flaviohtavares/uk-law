# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 291

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 291. Venue[^c-bed4f9b1c7754cfe9a1c9b7881aa541c][^c-4703db87d7b2be98edf56b53d169aa73]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6a3a93cee58cc5443ec8f02e8e122dcc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Reference to a “venue”—

- (a) in relation to any proceeding or attendance before the court is to the time, date and place or platform for the proceeding or attendance,
- (b) in relation to an examination under rule 235 is to the time, date and place for the examination, and
- (c) in relation to a meeting—
  - (i) is to the time, date and place for the meeting, or
  - (ii)[^c-9d2dda0e5fa0a76e438c3c5ab277cc6d] in the case of a meeting to be conducted and held in accordance with rule 80(2) or section 246A(3) of the IA 1986, is to the time and date for the meeting and the arrangements the administrator proposes to enable persons to exercise their rights to speak and vote at the meeting.
