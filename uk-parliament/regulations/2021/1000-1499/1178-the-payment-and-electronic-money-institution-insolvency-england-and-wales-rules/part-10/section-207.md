# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 207

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 207. Adjournment of the hearing of an application[^c-952306af0e629fcb5d7c1c1243d634f9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may adjourn the hearing of an application on such terms as it thinks just.

(2) The court may give directions as to the manner in which any evidence is to be provided at a resumed hearing and in particular as to—

- (a) the taking of evidence wholly or partly by witness statement or orally,
- (b) the cross-examination of the maker of a witness statement, or
- (c) any report to be made by the administrator.
