# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 209

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 209. Applications under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A[^key-cbcb422ee1d7fec47fc387b6145866fa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application under section 176A(5) must be accompanied by a witness statement by the administrator.

(2) The witness statement must state—

- (a) that the institution is in special administration,
- (b) a summary of the financial position of the institution, and
- (c) the information substantiating the administrator’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
