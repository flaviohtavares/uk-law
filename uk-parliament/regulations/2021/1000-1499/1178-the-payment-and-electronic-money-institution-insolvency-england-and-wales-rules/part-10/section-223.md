# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 223

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 223. Application of Chapter 10[^key-81d1a20aecd126e3da3d72573b4696e8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to costs in connection with the special administration.

(2) In this Chapter, a reference to costs includes charges and expenses.
