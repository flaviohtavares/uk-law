> Text as at 2022-08-10.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 232. Enforcement of court orders[^c-8237f646dda3ca8f39c850e3e08ada58]

*Extent: E+W*

In a special administration, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
