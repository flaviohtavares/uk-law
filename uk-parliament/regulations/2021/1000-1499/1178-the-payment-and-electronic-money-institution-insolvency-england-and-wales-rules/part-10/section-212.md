> Text as at 2022-08-10.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 212. Application of section 236 of the IA 1986[^c-9111ac889d117af3e5bd5733e0eb68f2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter applies to applications to the court for an order under section 236 of the IA 1986.

(2) In this Chapter, “the respondent” means the person in respect of whom an order is applied for.
