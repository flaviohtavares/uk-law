# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 227

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 227. Costs paid otherwise than out of the institution’s estate[^c-94b6937ae3e59e988bd6c045d7348fcb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the amount of costs is decided by detailed assessment under an order of the court directing that those costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the institution’s estate or out of the asset pool, the costs officer must note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
