# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 188

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 188. Shorthand writers — nomination, appointment, remuneration and costs[^key-13b13a91f05aa9bf2da8acbf4cbd92da]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The judge or registrar may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.

(2) The court may, at any time in the course of the special administration appoint a shorthand writer to take down evidence of a person examined under section 236 of the IA 1986[^f00021].

(3) The remuneration of a shorthand writer appointed under this rule must be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made, or out of the insolvent estate, or otherwise, as the court may direct.

(4) Any question arising as to the rates of remuneration payable under this rule must be determined by the court.
