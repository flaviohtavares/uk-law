# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 199

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 199. Service of orders staying proceedings[^c-8ec3f0b24b0b6ddfb69639bed32774fb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of the institution, the order may be served within the jurisdiction by serving a sealed copy of the order on the address for service of the claimant or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
