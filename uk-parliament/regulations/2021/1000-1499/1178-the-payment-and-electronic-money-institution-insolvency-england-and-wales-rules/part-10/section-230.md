# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 230

> Text as at 2022-08-10.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 230. Costs and expenses of witnesses[^c-9feea1e6cefd28b46be679c2fe43aedb]

*Extent: E+W*

Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court is to be made to an officer of the institution to which the proceedings relate.
