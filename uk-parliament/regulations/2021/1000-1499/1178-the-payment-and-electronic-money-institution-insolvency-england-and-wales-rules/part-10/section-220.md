# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 220

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 220. Witness statement in support of application[^c-30a58152ec0bf689d29e905d4b4c78e0]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under rule 219(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
