# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 287

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 287. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court[^key-31db79106555a16cf7332f632fb1de99]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a special administration is commenced in a court other than the High Court, that court may order the transfer of the proceedings to the High Court.
