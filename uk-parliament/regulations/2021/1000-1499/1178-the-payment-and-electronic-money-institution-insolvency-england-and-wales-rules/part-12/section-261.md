# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 261

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 261. Electronic delivery of special administration documents to court[^key-9fdc1e31702884baa933ba570588870f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A notice or other document may not be delivered to a court by electronic means unless expressly permitted by the CPR, a practice direction, or these Rules.

(2) A document delivered to the court by electronic means is to be treated as delivered to the court at the time it is recorded by the court as having been received or otherwise as the CPR, a practice direction or these Rules provide.
