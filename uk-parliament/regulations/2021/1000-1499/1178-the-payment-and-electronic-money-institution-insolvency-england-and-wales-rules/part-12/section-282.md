# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 282

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 282. Right to copy documents[^key-4b2c9adb71e35eee01dc12aa79790013]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Regulations or these Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of obtaining copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00028], and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
