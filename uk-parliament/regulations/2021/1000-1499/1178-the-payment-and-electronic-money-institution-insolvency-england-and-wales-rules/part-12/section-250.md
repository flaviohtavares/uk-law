> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 250. Personal delivery[^key-0dda19e859c11342a942c46f7655c8e0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Personal delivery of a notice or other document is permissible in any case.

(2) A document is personally delivered if it is delivered in accordance with the rules for personal service in CPR Part 6.
