# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 283

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 283. Charges for copy documents[^key-0b58b9571cd22b81c769f166ec3f5085]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where prohibited by these Rules, the administrator is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee for the supply of documents requested by a creditor, customer, member, contributory or member of the creditors’ committee.
