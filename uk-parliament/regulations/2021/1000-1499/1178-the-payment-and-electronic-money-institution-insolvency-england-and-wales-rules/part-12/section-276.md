# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 276

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 276. Notifications relating to documents[^key-e07af2a41838bcff66105c9ba06afa60]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by rule 274, a notification relating to a document other than a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the document, and
- (b) either—
  - (i) the date of the document, or
  - (ii) where the document relates to a period of time, the period of time to which the document relates.
