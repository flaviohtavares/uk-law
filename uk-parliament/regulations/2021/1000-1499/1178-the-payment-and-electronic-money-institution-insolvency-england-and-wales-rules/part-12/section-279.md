> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 279. Notifications of more than one nature[^key-809ace6ef45f44ede30727bca60b6b69]

*Extent: E+W*

A notification which includes two or more of the types of notification set out in rules 274 to 278 must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
