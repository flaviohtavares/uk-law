# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 268

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 268. Omission of unobtainable information[^key-84f9dece8dcf745659214e260aebaac3]

*Extent: E+W*

Information required under rule 266 or 267 to be included in a notice to be gazetted may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
