# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 252

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 252. Notice etc to authorised recipients[^key-c61c042809a0e0b6224a3247ebf7b25d]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a notice or other document is to be given, delivered or sent to a person under the Regulations or these Rules, it may be given, delivered or sent instead to any other person authorised in writing to accept delivery on behalf of the first-mentioned person.
