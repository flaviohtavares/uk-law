> Text as at 2022-08-10.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 277. Notifications relating to court orders[^c-f057cbc5aae5beb4951edfc02f2ad7d7]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by rule 274, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order, and
- (b) the date of the order.
