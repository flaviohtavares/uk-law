# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 263

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 263. Execution overtaken by judgment debtor’s insolvency[^c-ac17412e8ab1ac8b7a0ddb3237be2625]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where execution has been taken out against property of a judgment debtor, and notice is given to the enforcement officer or other officer charged with the execution that the judgment debtor has entered special administration.

(2) Subject to rule 264, the notice must be delivered to the office of the enforcement officer or of the officer charged with the execution—

- (a) by hand, or
- (b) by any other means of delivery which enables proof of receipt of the document at the relevant address.
