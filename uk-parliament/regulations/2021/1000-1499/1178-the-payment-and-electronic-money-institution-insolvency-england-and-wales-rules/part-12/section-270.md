# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 270

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 270. Content of notices advertised other than in the Gazette[^c-807db8a5db8896e2fe4e7d07e4272392]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to rule 272, where under the Regulations or these Rules a notice may be advertised otherwise than in the Gazette, in addition to any content specifically required by the Regulations or any other provision of these Rules, the content of such a notice must be as set out in paragraph and rule 271.

(2) All notices published must specify insofar as it is applicable in relation to the particular notice—

- (a) the name and business address of the administrator acting in the special administration to which the notice relates, and
- (b) either an e-mail address, or a telephone number, through which the administrator may be contacted.
