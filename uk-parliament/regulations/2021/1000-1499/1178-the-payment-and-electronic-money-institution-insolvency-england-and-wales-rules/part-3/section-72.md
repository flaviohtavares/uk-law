# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 72

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. The chair at meetings[^key-09355b08810e85781efb19a06be5cf30]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The chair at any meeting of the creditors’ committee must be the administrator, or a person appointed by the administrator in writing to act.

(2) A person so appointed must be—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the institution, or
- (b) an employee of the administrator or the administrator’s firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
