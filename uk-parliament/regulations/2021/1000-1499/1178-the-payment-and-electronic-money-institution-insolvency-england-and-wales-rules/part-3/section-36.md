# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 36

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Venue[^key-2152d00ccfaf15611fccfadaa7f3145c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In fixing the venue for a meeting, the convener must have regard to the convenience of those attending.

(2) Meetings must be summoned for commencement between 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day (subject to any direction by the court).

(3) In this rule, “meeting” includes an adjourned meeting.
