# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 73

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Quorum[^key-c50ca545483257d7b135834322da738f]

*Extent: E+W*

A meeting of the creditors’ committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least two members are present or represented.
