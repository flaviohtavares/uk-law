# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 58

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Voting at meetings of creditors and customers[^c-3a3208affd1d5f54092b1650362f0667]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to meetings of creditors and customers.

(2) If the administrator thinks it appropriate, the creditors and customers may vote on the same resolution at the meeting, however the creditors and the customers must vote in separate classes on the resolution.
