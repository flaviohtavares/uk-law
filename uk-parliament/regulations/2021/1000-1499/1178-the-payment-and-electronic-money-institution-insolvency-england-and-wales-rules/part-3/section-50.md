> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Suspension[^c-846b9ea69b33af859af0e761d22dcdff]

*Extent: E+W*

Once only in the course of a meeting, the chair may, without an adjournment, declare it suspended for any period up to one hour.
