# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 60

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Requisite majorities at contributories’ meetings[^key-6056329e429f2003cdf175d3cc0d9f73]

*Extent: E+W*

At a meeting of contributories, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the institution, subject to any provision of the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the institution is in liquidation.
