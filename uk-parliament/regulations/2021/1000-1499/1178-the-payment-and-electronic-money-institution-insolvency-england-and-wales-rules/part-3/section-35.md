# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 35

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Meetings generally[^c-d95f7a3b7312763910cd9fa0516ba6ac]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where different provision is made in the Regulations or these Rules, this Chapter applies to meetings summoned by the administrator under—

- (a) paragraph 51;
- (b) paragraph 54(2);
- (c) paragraph 62;

or following a request or a direction from the court under paragraph 56.
