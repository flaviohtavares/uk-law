# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 77

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. Removal[^key-3633c14ea2addbc1aa2d85563e4fd060]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A member of the creditors’ committee may be removed by resolution at a meeting of creditors and customers, at least fourteen days’ notice having been given of the intention to move that resolution.

(2) The resolution in paragraph (1) will be voted on only by the relevant class of voter in respect of the member to be removed.
