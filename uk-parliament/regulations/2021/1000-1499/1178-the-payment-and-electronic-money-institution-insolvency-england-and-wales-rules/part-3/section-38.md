# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 38

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Notice of meeting by individual notice: content and accompanying documents[^key-834e3e267607a0a91a46caf70cb121a1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies except where the court orders under rule 39 that notice of a meeting be given by advertisement only.

(2) Notice summoning a meeting must specify the purpose of and venue for the meeting and state that a creditor or customer wishing to vote at the meeting must lodge claims (including relevant funds claims) or proofs and (if applicable) proxies at a specified place not later than 12.00 hours on the business day before the date fixed for the meeting.

(3) A blank proxy complying with rule 90 must be sent out with every notice summoning a meeting.
