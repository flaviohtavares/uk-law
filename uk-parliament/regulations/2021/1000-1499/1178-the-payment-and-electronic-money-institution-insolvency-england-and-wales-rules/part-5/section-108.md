# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 108

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 108. Withdrawal or variation of relevant funds claim[^key-10350e3f7d677be1b2af986759e0f449]

*Extent: E+W*

A relevant funds claim may at any time, with the agreement of the administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
