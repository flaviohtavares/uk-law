# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 106

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 106. Admission and rejection of relevant funds claim[^key-dfaea62225121c8602aaec54f5a729a8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The administrator may admit or reject a relevant funds claim in whole or in part.

(2) If the administrator rejects a relevant funds claim in whole or in part, the administrator must prepare a written statement of reasons for doing so, and deliver it as soon as is reasonably practicable to the claimant.
