# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 97

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 97. Expenses of voluntary arrangement[^key-402354d5fbf1170a860e2d854791d412]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a special administration order is made and a voluntary arrangement under Part 1 of the IA 1986 is in force for the institution, any expenses properly incurred as expenses of the administration of the arrangement in question are payable in priority to any expenses in rule 98.
