# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 161

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 161. Disclaimer presumed valid and effective[^c-0ab42db0aac6ab8c60ca2b95449e9029]

*Extent: E+W*

Any disclaimer of property by the administrator is presumed valid and effective, unless it is proved that the administrator has been in breach of their duty with respect to the giving of notice of disclaimer, or otherwise, under sections 178 to 180 of the IA 1986 or under this Chapter of these Rules.
