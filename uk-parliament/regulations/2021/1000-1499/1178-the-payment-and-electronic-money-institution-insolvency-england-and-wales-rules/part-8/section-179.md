# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 179

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 179. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint administrator[^key-846bc0a8698a1f8311b9c62efcdf2212]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to rule 180, where, after an initial appointment has been made, an additional person or persons are to be appointed as joint administrator, the same rules apply in respect of giving notice of and advertising the appointment as applied in relation to the initial appointment.
