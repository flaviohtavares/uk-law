# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 157

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 157. Additional notices[^key-909bd7bdad727ece330071a3c9a212c2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The administrator disclaiming property may at any time send or give copies of the notice of the disclaimer to any persons who in the administrator’s opinion ought, in the public interest or otherwise, to be informed of the disclaimer.

(2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to the administrator’s obligations under sections 178, 179 and 180 of the IA 1986[^f00018] and rules 155 and 156.
