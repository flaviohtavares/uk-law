# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 136

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 136. Debt payable at a future time[^key-b5f1fc38f45a690b7069de3e5bdedf02]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to rule 153, a creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the institution entered special administration.
