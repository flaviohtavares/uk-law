# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 117

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 117. Debts of institution to rank equally[^c-cd070a878a770920a4399df63d83d519]

*Extent: E+W*

Debts, other than preferential debts, rank equally between themselves in the special administration and, after the preferential debts, must be paid in full unless the assets are insufficient for meeting them, in which case they abate in equal proportions between themselves.
