# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 148

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 148. Notice of no dividend or no further dividend[^c-dba4faad13e3f43cb76c8ca89ca1cc3d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the administrator gives notice to creditors that no dividend (or as the case may be, no further dividend) can be declared, the notice must contain a statement to the effect either—

- (a) that no funds have been realised, or
- (b) that the funds realised have already been distributed or used or allocated for paying the expenses of the special administration.

(2) The notice to creditors in paragraph (1) must also be given to the FCA.
