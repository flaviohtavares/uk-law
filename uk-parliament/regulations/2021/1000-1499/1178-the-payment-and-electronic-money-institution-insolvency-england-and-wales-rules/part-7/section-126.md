# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 126

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Withdrawal or variation of proof[^c-5d4b3043f5272d10beaca54b97f1a6f6]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement with the administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
