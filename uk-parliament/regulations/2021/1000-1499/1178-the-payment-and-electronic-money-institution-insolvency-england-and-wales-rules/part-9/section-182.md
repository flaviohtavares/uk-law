# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 182

> Text as at 2022-08-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 182. Final progress reports[^c-64827cbfbeb861175ea5f37d6b6219f4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, reference to a progress report is to a report in the form specified in rule 87.

(2) The final progress report means a progress report which includes a summary of—

- (a) the administrator’s proposals (including whether the FCA has given a direction under regulation 38 and whether that direction has been withdrawn),
- (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals,
- (c) the steps taken during the special administration, and
- (d) the outcome.
