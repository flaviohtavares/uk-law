# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2021, section 186

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 186. Moving from special administration to dissolution[^key-75836356bcf6b692ed01eb2b5835b68c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, for the purposes of paragraph 84(1), the administrator sends a notice of moving from special administration to dissolution to the registrar of companies, the administrator must attach to that notice a copy of the final progress report.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable, a copy of the notice and the attached document must be sent to all other persons who received notice of the administrator’s appointment.

(3) Where a court makes an order under paragraph 84(7) it must, where the applicant is not the administrator, give a copy of the order to the administrator.
