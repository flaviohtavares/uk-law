# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014 (Amendment) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 940

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014 (Amendment) Order 2021

Made

13th August 2021

Coming into force

1st September 2021

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 45(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014 (Amendment) Order 2021 and shall come into force on 1st September 2021.

## 2. Variation of Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014

(1) The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014 is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) For article 2, substitute—

> **2.** The Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust (also known as ‘Ashridge’) shall be competent to grant awards of the kind mentioned in paragraph (a) of section 76(2) of the said Act.

Jean Arnold

Head of Compliance and Student Protection

For the Office for Students

13th August 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 45(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017, provides for a variation of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust) Order of Council 2014, so that Ashridge (Bonar Law Memorial) Trust’s existing Degree Awarding Powers authorisation remains valid for an indefinite period. This Order will come into force on 1st September 2021.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
