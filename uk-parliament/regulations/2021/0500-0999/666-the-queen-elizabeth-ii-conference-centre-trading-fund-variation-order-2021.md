# The Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund (Variation) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 666

Government Trading Funds

The Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund (Variation) Order 2021

Made

4th June 2021

Laid before Parliament

8th June 2021

Coming into force

29th June 2021

The Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund was established under the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001] (“the 1973 Act”) by the Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund Order 1997[^f00002] (“the 1997 Order”) with effect from 1st April 1997.

The Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government has determined that article 5 of the 1997 Order should be varied so that the maximum borrowing amount specified therein is increased.

The Secretary of State in exercise of powers conferred by sections 1(1), 2C(1) and 6(1) of the 1973 Act, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund (Variation) Order 2021 and comes into force on 29th June 2021.

## 2. Variation

(1) The Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund Order 1997 is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In article 5 of the 1997 Order, for “£2,000,000” substitute “£12,000,000”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government.

Eddie Hughes

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government

2nd June 2021

We concur

4th June 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the maximum amount of money that can be loaned to The Queen Elizabeth II Conference Centre Trading Fund in accordance with the Government Trading Funds Act [1973 (c. 63)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/63) from £2,000,000 to £12,000,000.

A full impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has no impact on business, charities, voluntary bodies or the public sector.
