# The Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 678 (C. 28)

Defence

The Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2021

Made

7th June 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act 2021[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2021.

## 2. Commencement

The Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act 2021 comes into force on 30th June 2021 in so far as it is not already in force.

Ben Wallace

Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

7th June 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring the provisions of the Overseas Operations (Service Personnel and Veterans) Act [2021 (c.23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/23) into force on 30th June 2021 in so far as they are not already in force. Part 3 of that Act came into force on Royal Assent.
