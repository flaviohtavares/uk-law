# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 44

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Remittance of charges[^key-b00161c82e38941a3d612d16bb802dfa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Environment Agency must pay the Secretary of State any charge received by it.

(2) SEPA must pay the Scottish Ministers any charge received by it.

(3) NRW must pay the Welsh Ministers any charge received by it.

(4) The chief inspector must pay the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs any charge received by it.
