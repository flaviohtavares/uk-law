# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 41

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Electronic data exchange and use of automated systems[^c-eee8b9a5faa608024d64a78b84bf13be]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Regulator may require verification bodies to use electronic templates or specific file formats for Verification Reports[^c-c3854e1b7ffcff8e6299e6ba2242361e].

(2) Standardised electronic templates or file format specifications may be made available for further types of communication between the aeroplane operator, Regulator, verification bodies and national accreditation body.
