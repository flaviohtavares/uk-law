# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 24

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Issue of Emissions Monitoring Plans[^key-468bd14a665e320b2442cb5b46f8b86d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an aeroplane operator submits an Emissions Monitoring Plan for approval by the Regulator in accordance with article 23, a Regulator must approve and issue the Emissions Monitoring Plan by notice to the aeroplane operator unless[^key-b25a0d5c112c34d869079628da60403f]—

- (a) the Regulator is not satisfied that the application complies with the requirements of this Order,[^key-707d1f9b122d5a597e8538bb34451906] and
- (b) the aeroplane operator has not agreed to amendments of the application required to satisfy the Regulator that the application does so comply.

(2) An Emissions Monitoring Plan issued under paragraph (1) replaces any Emissions Monitoring Plan previously issued to the aeroplane operator.

(3) An Emissions Monitoring Plan[^key-3a68eaf7a8e3ace599885238f3093ae3] may contain any conditions the Regulator considers necessary to give proper effect to the requirements of this Order.

(4) An aeroplane operator must comply with any condition included in its Emissions Monitoring Plan.
