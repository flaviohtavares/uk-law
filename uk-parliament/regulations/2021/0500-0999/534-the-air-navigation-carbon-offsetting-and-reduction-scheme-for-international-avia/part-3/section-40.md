# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 40

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Rounding of data[^key-495eaa1c04f9f4456f6b76a94f9256e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Total annual emissions must be reported as tonnes of CO<sub>2</sub> rounded to the nearest whole number.

(2) Unless otherwise provided in this Order, all variables used to calculate the emissions must be rounded to the nearest whole number for the purpose of calculating and reporting emissions.
