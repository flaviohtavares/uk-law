# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 60

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Failure to comply with enforcement notice given by a Regulator[^key-7b52d8b94667607c8e81d8b3e2008805]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is liable to a civil penalty where the person fails to comply, or to comply on time, with the requirements of an enforcement notice given by a Regulator under article 52(1).

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000,[^key-3d64f34684adffed9bb08b82191725c5] and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £1,000 for each day that the person fails to comply with the requirements of the notice, beginning with the day on which the initial notice under article 53(4) is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
