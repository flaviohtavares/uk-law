# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 59

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Failure to keep records[^c-5dc260abb83107ee9e375dd32b232ff1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is liable to a civil penalty of £50,000 where the person fails to keep the appropriate records in accordance with article 12(1).
