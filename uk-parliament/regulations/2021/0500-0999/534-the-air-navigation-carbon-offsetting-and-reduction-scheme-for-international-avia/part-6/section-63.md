# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 63

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Inspection: refusal to allow access to premises[^c-1b9b7f94662c36c493334aba22ebeddc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person in control of premises is liable to a civil penalty of £50,000 where the person does not allow a Regulator or authorised person, within the meaning of article 45, access to the premises contrary to article 46(3).
