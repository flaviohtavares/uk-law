# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 58

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Failure to report emissions[^c-5da4c89a2522f549950d50898cc5320e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aeroplane operator is liable to a civil penalty where the aeroplane operator fails to submit, or to submit on time, a verified Emissions Report to a Regulator, contrary to article 31(1).

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000,[^c-b30d0007cbb07707df7c9e9e868caa0e] and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £500 for each day that the report is not submitted, beginning with the day on which the initial notice under article 53(4) is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
