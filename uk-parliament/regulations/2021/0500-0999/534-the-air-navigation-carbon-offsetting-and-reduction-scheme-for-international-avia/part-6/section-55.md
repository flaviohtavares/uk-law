# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 55

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Failure to apply or make revised application for approval of Emissions Monitoring Plan[^c-d84276554fc1b790e770b828a48dcf44]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aeroplane operator is liable to a civil penalty where the aeroplane operator fails—

- (a) to apply, or to apply on time, to a Regulator for the approval of an Emissions Monitoring Plan, contrary to article 23,[^c-eeb6e33ee6dda79def66b1af27063d8b] or
- (b) to make a revised application, or to make a revised application on time, for the approval of an Emissions Monitoring Plan, where required to do so under article 26(2).

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000, and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £500 for each day that the application is not submitted or, as the case may be, the revised application is not resubmitted, beginning with the day on which the initial notice set out in article 53(4) is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
