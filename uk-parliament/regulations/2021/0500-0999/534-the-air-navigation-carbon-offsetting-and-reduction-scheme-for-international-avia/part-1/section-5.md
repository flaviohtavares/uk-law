# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 5

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Meaning of aeroplane operator[^c-cdf6ff40781aa0837b942956227a108c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order, a person is an “aeroplane operator” where that person is an operator that produces annual CO<sub>2</sub> emissions greater than 10,000 tonnes from the use of an aeroplane with a maximum certificated take-off mass greater than 5,700 kg conducting international flights.
