# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 6

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Units[^key-516784b7e5b26dba276fe4c0eb670124]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The non-SI units listed in column 2 of the Table must be used either in lieu of, or in addition to, SI units[^f00015] as primary units of measurement under this Order.

Table

Non-SI units for use with SI units

| Specific quantity | Non-SI Unit | Symbol | Definition (in terms of SI units) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mass | tonne | T | 1 t = 10<sup>3</sup> kg |
| Time | hour | H | 1 h = 60 min = 3,600 s |
| Volume | litre | L | 1 L = 1 dm<sup>3</sup> = 10<sup>-3</sup> m<sup>3</sup> |
