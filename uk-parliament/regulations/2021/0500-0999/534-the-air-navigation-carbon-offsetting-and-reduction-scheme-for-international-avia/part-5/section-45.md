# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 45

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Authorised persons[^c-c04136bcc05df1e3e2965b749a26fa59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Regulator may authorise a person to exercise, on behalf of the Regulator and in accordance with the terms of the authorisation, the Regulator’s powers set out in this Part.

(2) In this Part, “authorised person” means a person authorised under paragraph (1).
