# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 50

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Information notices[^c-167a4863c9a920b86bdcc5afc661209b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Regulator may, by giving a notice (an “information notice”) to a person, require the person to provide information for purposes connected with the exercise of functions under this Order.

(2) The information notice must set out—

- (a) the information to be provided,[^c-5f46243adb8b83ff3bbcd0cc77ce3918]
- (b) the form in which the information must be provided,
- (c) the period within which or the time when the information must be provided, and
- (d) the place where the information must be provided.

(3) The information that a person may be required to provide includes information that, although it is not in the person’s possession or it would not otherwise come into the person’s possession, is information that it is reasonable to require the person to obtain or compile for the purpose of complying with the information notice.
