# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 51

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Legal professional privilege[^c-9aa933d7a1880c94ea840fef42056f54]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part requires any person to produce a document which that person would be entitled to withhold the production of on grounds of legal professional privilege.
