# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 66

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Appeal body[^c-145b27afd70804cdaf73b8ca5d3346a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In an appeal against a decision of SEPA, the appeal body is the Scottish Land Court[^f00046].

(2) In an appeal against a decision of the chief inspector, the appeal body is the Planning Appeals Commissions (Northern Ireland)[^c-c8eaba05ef9505c3a8a24d7286e45794].

(3) In an appeal against any other decision, the appeal body is the First-tier Tribunal[^f00048].
