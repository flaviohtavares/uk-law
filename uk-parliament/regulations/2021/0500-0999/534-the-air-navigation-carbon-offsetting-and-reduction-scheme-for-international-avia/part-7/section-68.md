# The Air Navigation (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation) Order 2021, section 68

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Determination of appeals[^c-7b25e6958cd5a31d8d956408659408b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining an appeal made under article 65 the appeal body may—

- (a) affirm the decision,[^c-7b46da7b59f8c1b44a49b75097a5a733]
- (b) quash the decision or vary any of its terms,
- (c) substitute a deemed refusal with a decision of the appeal body,
- (d) give directions as to the exercise of the Regulator’s functions under this Order.

(2) The appeal body may not make a determination that would result in a decision which could not otherwise have been made under this Order.
