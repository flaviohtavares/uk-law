# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Amendment of Schedule 2A to the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002

(1) Schedule 2A to the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002[^f00011] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 9 (amount of additional payment)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (2), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment (within the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021[^f00012])”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (3), after paragraph (a), insert—

  > - (aa) entitled to the care component of child disability payment at the highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021; or

  .
