# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, SCHEDULE 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 11 — Amendment of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013

Article 20

### 1. Amendment of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013: general

The Universal Credit Regulations 2013 are amended as follows.

### 2. Amendment of regulation 2 (interpretation)

In regulation 2—

- (a) after the definition of “carer element”, insert—

  > - “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the DACYP Regulations;

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “course of advanced education”, insert—

  > - “**the DACYP Regulations**” means the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

  .

### 3. Amendment of regulation 14 (exceptions to the requirement not to be receiving education)

In regulation 14(1)(b), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

### 4. Amendment of regulation 24 (the child element)

In regulation 24(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)(i), after “highest rate”, insert “, the care component of child disability payment at the highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations”.

### 5. Amendment of regulation 41 (when an assessment may be carried out)

In regulation 41(2)(a), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

### 6. Amendment of regulation 83 (exceptions – entitlement or receipt of certain benefits)

In regulation 83(1), after paragraph (h), insert—

> - (ha) a claimant, or a child or qualifying young person for whom a claimant is responsible, is receiving child disability payment;

.

### 7. Amendment of Schedule 3 (claimant treated as occupying or not occupying accommodation)

(1) Schedule 3 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 5(3)(b), after sub-paragraph (i), insert—

> - (ia) the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations,

.

### 8. Amendment of Schedule 4 (housing costs element for renters)

(1) Schedule 4 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 12—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (3)(a), after sub-paragraph (i), insert—

  > - (ia) the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations;

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (6), after paragraph (a), insert—

  > - (aa) in receipt of the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations;

  ;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (6A)(a), after sub-paragraph (i), insert—

  > - (ia) the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations;

  .

(3) In paragraph 15(2), after paragraph (b), insert—

> - (ba) a person in receipt of the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations;

.

(4) In paragraph 29(5), after paragraph (a), insert—

> - (aa) the care component of child disability payment at the middle or highest rate in accordance with regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations;

.
