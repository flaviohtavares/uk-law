# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendment of the Housing Benefit and Council Tax Benefit (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2001

(1) The Housing Benefit and Council Tax Benefit (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2001[^f00009] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1(2) (interpretation etc), after the definition of “benefit week”, insert—

> - “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

.

(3) In regulation 4(7B)(b) (revision of decisions)—

- (a) after “Act”, insert “or child disability payment”;
- (b) after the second occurrence of “benefit”, insert “or child disability payment”.

(4) In regulation 7(2)(i)(ii) (decisions superseding earlier decisions)—

- (a) after “Act”, insert “or child disability payment”;
- (b) after the second occurrence of “benefit”, insert “or child disability payment”.

(5) In regulation 8(14) (date from which a decision superseding an earlier decision takes effect) after both occurrences of “benefit”, insert “or child disability payment”.
