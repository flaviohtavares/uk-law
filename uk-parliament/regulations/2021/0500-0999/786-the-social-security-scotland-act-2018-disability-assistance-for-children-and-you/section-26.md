# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

(1) Regulation 41 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00031] (remunerative work) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)(c)—

- (a) in head (ii), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”;
- (b) in head (iv), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

(3) In paragraph (4)—

- (a) after the definition of “armed forces independence payment”, insert—

  > - “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

  ;
- (b) for the definition of “care component” substitute—

  > - “**care component**” means—
  >   - (a) the care component of disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate prescribed under section 72(3) of the Benefits Act; or
  >   - (b) the care component of child disability payment at the highest or middle rate provided for in regulation 11(5) of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

  .
