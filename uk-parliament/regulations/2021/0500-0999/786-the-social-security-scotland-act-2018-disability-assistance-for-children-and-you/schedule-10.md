# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, SCHEDULE 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 10 — Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Article 17

### 1. Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008: general

The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 are amended as follows.

### 2. Amendment of regulation 2 (interpretation)

In regulation 2(1)—

- (a) after the definition of “child”, insert—

  > - “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in the DACYP Regulations (see regulation 2 of those Regulations);

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “couple”, insert—

  > - “**the DACYP Regulations**” means the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

  .

### 3. Amendment of regulation 18 (circumstances in which the condition that the claimant is not receiving education does not apply)

In regulation 18, after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

### 4. Amendment of regulation 158 (meaning of “person in hardship”)

(1) Regulation 158 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d)(ii), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

(3) For paragraph (7), substitute—

> (7) In this regulation, “**care component**” means—
>
> - (a) the care component of disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate prescribed under section 72(3) of the Contributions and Benefits Act, or
> - (b) the care component of child disability payment at the highest or middle rate provided for in regulation 11(5) of the DACYP Regulations.

.

### 5. Amendment of Schedule 6 (Housing Costs)

(1) Schedule 6 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 15(11)(b), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

(3) In paragraph 19(6)(b), after sub-paragraph (ii), insert —

> - (iia) the care component of child disability payment;

.
