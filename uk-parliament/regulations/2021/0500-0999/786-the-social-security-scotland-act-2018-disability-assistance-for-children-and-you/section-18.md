# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Amendment of the Child Support Maintenance Calculation Regulations 2012

(1) Regulation 64 of the Child Support Maintenance Calculation Regulations 2012[^f00020] (illness or disability of relevant other child) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i), after “disability living allowance” insert “, child disability payment”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(ii), after “disability living allowance” insert “, or the care component of child disability payment in accordance with regulation 11 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021,”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (ab) “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021

  .

(3) In paragraph (3)(b) after “disability living allowance” insert “, child disability payment”.
