# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance for Children and Young People) (Consequential Modifications) Order 2021, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

(1) Regulation 94 of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00032] (meaning of “**person in hardship**”) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c)(ii), after “disability living allowance”, insert “, child disability payment”.

(3) In paragraph (7), for the definition of “care component” substitute—

> - “**care component**” means—
>   - (a) the care component of disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate prescribed under section 72(3) of the Contributions and Benefits Act; or
>   - (b) the care component of child disability payment at the highest or middle rate provided for in regulation 11(5) of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;
> - “**child disability payment**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021;

.
