# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Verification procedure for market surveillance purposes

The market surveillance authority[^f00020] must use the verification procedure set out in Schedule 28 when verifying the compliance of a product with the requirements of these Regulations.

## Annotations

[^f00020]: Footnote: See Article 2(28) of the Framework Regulation for the meanings of “market surveillance” and “market surveillance authority”. Point (28) was inserted by [S.I. 2019/539](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/539), as amended by [S.I. 2020/1528](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1528).
