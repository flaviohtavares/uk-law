# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Obligations of internet hosting platforms

Where a hosting service provider as referred to in regulation 19 of the Electronic Commerce (EC Directive) Regulations 2002[^f00018] allows the direct selling of refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function through its internet site, the service provider must—

- (a) enable the showing of the electronic label and the product information sheet provided by the dealer on the display mechanism in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 27; and
- (b) inform the dealer of the obligation to display them.
