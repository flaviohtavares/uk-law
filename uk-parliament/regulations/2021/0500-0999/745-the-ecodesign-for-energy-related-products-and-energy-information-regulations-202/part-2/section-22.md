# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Ecodesign requirements

(1) A household washing machine or household washer-dryer must conform to the ecodesign requirements set out in Schedules 9 and 12 when it is placed on the market or put into service.

(2) Manufacturers, authorised representatives and importers or of household washing machines and household washer-dryers must comply with paragraphs 18 and 19 of Schedule 9 (resource efficiency and information requirements).
