# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Ecodesign requirements

An electrical motor or variable speed drive must conform to the ecodesign requirements set out in Schedule 16 when it is placed on the market or put into service.
