# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Application and interpretation

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), this Chapter applies to electric mains-operated household dishwashers, including—

- (a) built-in dishwashers; and
- (b) dishwashers that can also be powered by batteries.

(2) Nothing in this Chapter applies to—

- (a) dishwashers in the scope of the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008[^f00011];
- (b) battery-operated household dishwashers that can be connected to the mains through an AC/DC converter purchased separately.

(3) In this Chapter and Schedules 6 to 8—

- “built-in dishwasher” means a household dishwasher that is designed, tested and marketed exclusively—
  - (a) to be installed in cabinetry or encased (top, bottom and sides) by panels;
  - (b) to be securely fastened to the sides, top or floor of the cabinetry or panels; and
  - (c) to be equipped with an integral factory-finished face or to be fitted with a custom front panel;
- “household dishwasher” means a machine which cleans and rinses tableware, and which is declared by the manufacturer in the declaration of conformity to comply with the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016[^f00012] or the Radio Equipment Regulations 2017[^f00013];
- “mains” or “electric mains” means the electricity supply from the electricity grid of 230 (± 10 per cent) volts of alternating current at 50 hertz (Hz).
