# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Verification procedure for market surveillance purposes

The market surveillance authority must use the verification procedure set out in Schedule 11 when verifying the compliance of a product with the requirements of these Regulations.
