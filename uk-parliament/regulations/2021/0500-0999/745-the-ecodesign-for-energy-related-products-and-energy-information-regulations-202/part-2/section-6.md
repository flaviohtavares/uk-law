# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Verification procedure for market surveillance purposes

The market surveillance authority[^f00006] must use the verification procedure set out in Schedule 2 when verifying the compliance of a product with the requirements of these Regulations.

## Annotations

[^f00006]: Footnote: The meanings of “market surveillance” and “market surveillance authority” are set out in Article 2(17) and (18) respectively of EU Regulation 765/2008 (RAMS), and are amended by paragraph 3 of Schedule 33 to [S.I. 2019/696](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/696). Definitions in RAMS are applied to the 2010 regulations by regulation 2(2) of those regulations (as amended by [S.I. 2019/539](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/539)).
