# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Amendment of EU Regulation 1275/2008

(1) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1275) of 17 December 2008 implementing [Directive 2005/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for standby and off mode, and networked standby, electric power consumption of electrical and electronic household and office equipment is amended as follows.

(2) Omit the following entries in paragraph 1 of Annex 1—

- (a) “Washing machines”;
- (b) “Dish washing machines”;

(3) For paragraph 2 of Annex 1, substitute—

> Information technology equipment intended primarily for use in the domestic environment, but excluding desktop computers, integrated desktop computers and notebook computers as defined in [Commission Regulation (EU) No 617/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0617)[^f00026], as well as electronic displays covered by the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021[^f00027].

.

(4) In paragraph 3 of Annex 1, for the last entry substitute—

> And other equipment for the purpose of recording or reproducing sound or images, including signals or other technologies for the distribution of sound and image other than by telecommunications, but excluding electronic displays covered by the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021.

.
