# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, SCHEDULE 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 29 — Revocations

Regulation 56

**1.** [Commission Regulation (EC) No 640/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0640) of 22 July 2009 implementing [Directive 2005/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for electric motors.

**2.** [Commission Regulation (EC) No 642/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0642) of 22 July 2009 implementing [Directive 2005/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for televisions.

**3.** [Commission Regulation (EC) No 643/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0643) of 22 July 2009 implementing [Directive 2005/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for household refrigerating appliances.

**4.** [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1015/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1015) of 10 November 2010 implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for household washing machines;

**5.** [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1016/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1016) of 10 November 2010 implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for household dishwashers.

**6.** Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1781 of 1 October 2019[^f00040] laying down ecodesign requirements for electric motors and variable speed drives pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending Regulation [(EC) No 641/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0641) with regard to ecodesign requirements for glandless standalone circulators and glandless circulators integrated in products and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 640/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0640).

**7.** Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2019 of 1 October 2019[^f00041] laying down ecodesign requirements for refrigerating appliances pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 643/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0643).

**8.** Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2021 of 1 October 2019[^f00042] laying down ecodesign requirements for electronic displays pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1275) and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 642/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0642);

**9.** Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2022 of 1 October 2019[^f00043] laying down ecodesign requirements for household dishwashers pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1275) and repealing [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1016/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1016).

**10.** Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2023 of 1 October 2019[^f00044] laying down ecodesign requirements for household washing machines and household washer-dryers pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1275) and repealing [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1015).
