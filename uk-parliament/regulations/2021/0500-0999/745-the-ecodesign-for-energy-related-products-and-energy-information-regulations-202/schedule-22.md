# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations 2021, SCHEDULE 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 22 — Energy labelling of refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function – energy efficiency classes

Regulation 46

**1.** (1) The energy efficiency class of a refrigerating appliance with a direct sales function must be determined on the basis of its EEI as set out in the Table below.

(2) The EEI is determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 4.

Table 34

Energy efficiency classes of refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function

| Energy efficiency class | EEI |
| --- | --- |
| A | EEI \< 10 |
| B | 10 ≤ EEI \< 20 |
| C | 20 ≤ EEI \< 35 |
| D | 35 ≤ EEI \< 50 |
| E | 50 ≤ EEI \< 65 |
| F | 65 ≤ EEI \< 80 |
| G | EEI ≥ 80 |
