# The Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Landlord And Tenant, England

The Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2021

Made

21st June 2021

Laid before Parliament

24th June 2021

Coming into force

30th June 2021

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 82(12) of the Coronavirus Act 2020[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 30th June 2021.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England.

## 2. Extension of “relevant period”

25th March 2022 is specified for the purposes of paragraph (b) in the definition of “relevant period” in section 82(12) of the Coronavirus Act 2020[^f00002].

## 3. Revocation

The Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) Regulations 2021[^f00003] are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 82 of the Coronavirus Act provides that a right of re-entry or forfeiture, under a relevant business tenancy, for non-payment of rent may not be enforced, by action or otherwise, during the relevant period. “Relevant period” is defined in subsection (12) as the period starting with the day after Royal Assent and ending with 30th June 2020 (or such later date as may be specified in regulations).

The relevant period was extended to 30th September 2020 by the Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/602](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/602)). It was further extended to 31st December 2020 by the Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/994)), to 31st March 2021 by the Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) (No. 3) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1472](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1472)) and to 30th June 2021 by the Business Tenancies (Protection from Forfeiture: Relevant Period) (Coronavirus) (England) Regulations 2021 ([S.I. 2021/283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/283)).

These Regulations provide that the relevant period is extended to 25th March 2022. They also revoke [S.I. 2021/283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/283).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument due to the temporary nature of the provision.
