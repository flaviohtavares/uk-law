# The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations (No. 2) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 883

Education, England

The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations (No. 2) Order 2021

Made

21st July 2021

Coming into force

22nd July 2021

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 21st day of July 2021

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1)(za) of Schedule 9 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations (No. 2) Order 2021 and comes into force on 22nd July 2021.

## 2. Extent and Application

(1) This Order extends to England and Wales.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England only.

## 3. Appointment of chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations

Jo Saxton is appointed as the chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation and is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations, from 18th September 2021.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Jo Saxton to the role of chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation, which is established by section 127 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009. In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 9 to that Act, the person appointed to that position is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations.
