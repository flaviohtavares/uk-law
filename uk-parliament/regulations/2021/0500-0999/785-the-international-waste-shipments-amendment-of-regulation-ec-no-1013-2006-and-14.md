# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment of Regulation (EC) No 1013/2006 and 1418/2007) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 785

Environmental Protection

Made

30th June 2021

Laid before Parliament

1st July 2021

Coming into force

29th July 2021

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by: the first paragraph of Article 37(2); and Articles 58(1)(a) and 59a(3)(a), of Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council on shipments of waste (“Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013)”)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the International Waste Shipments (Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) and 1418/2007) Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 29th July 2021.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment to Annex 7 to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013)

In table note (4) in Annex 7 to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council on shipments of waste omit “, B3010”.

## 3. Amendments to the Table in the Annex to Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418)

The Table in the Annex to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) concerning the export for recovery of certain waste listed in Annex III or IIIA to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council to certain countries to which the OECD Decision on the control of transboundary movements of wastes does not apply[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) concerning the export for recovery of certain waste listed in Annex III or IIIA to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council (“Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013)”) to certain countries to which the OECD Decision on the control of transboundary movements of wastes does not apply (“Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418)”) forms part of domestic law on and after IP completion day (11 p.m. on 31st December 2020) (see section 3 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16).

These Regulations amend the Annex to Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) to set out export control procedures for a new category of sorted, non-hazardous, plastic waste classified as B3011, which had previously been a subset of classification B3010.

These Regulations also amend both Annex 7 to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) and the Annex to Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) in order to remove defunct references to plastic waste classified as B3010, which is no longer a classification used in the Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal, adopted on 22nd March 1989[^f00003], amended by Decision BC-14/12[^f00004].

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
