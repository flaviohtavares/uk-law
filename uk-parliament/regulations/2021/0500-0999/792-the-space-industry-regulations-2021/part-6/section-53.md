# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 53

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Application[^c-883665146c8177e61f3785850c8f971e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies where a spaceflight operator is authorised by a licence to provide range control services in respect of the operator’s spaceflight activities carried out by that spaceflight operator.
