## 150. Consequential amendments[^c-ff92155356a21f04a2661df478779e83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In consequence of the provisions of this Part, the amendments to the Air Navigation (Cosmic Radiation: Protection of Air Crew and Space Crew and Consequential Amendments) Order 2019[^f00047] in Schedule 7 have effect.
