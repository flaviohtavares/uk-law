# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 146

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Monitoring of exposure to cosmic radiation: classified crew[^key-a5538bef79b40e0aaa806f1e65e729cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A spaceflight operator must ensure that the exposure to cosmic radiation of each classified crew member is individually monitored.

(2) Monitoring of the crew of a carrier aircraft or a launch vehicle under this regulation may be undertaken by proper use of any of the following computer programs, which calculate the effective dose of cosmic radiation received by an individual on board a carrier aircraft or a launch vehicle, or of a computer program that performs an equivalent function—

- (a) CARI-7;
- (b) EPCARD;
- (c) SIEVERT PN;
- (d) PCAire.

(3) In this regulation, “**CARI-7**”, “**EPCARD**”, “**SIEVERT PN**” and “**PCAire**” have the same meaning as in regulation 139.
