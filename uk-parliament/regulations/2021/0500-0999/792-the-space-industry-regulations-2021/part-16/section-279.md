# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 279

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 279. Providing false information[^c-1878dbce6097cf95d52ba448bb655fb1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person who, in purported compliance with regulation 271, makes a statement they know to be false in a material particular, or recklessly makes a statement that is false in a material particular, commits an offence.
