> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 272. Objective of an occurrence report[^key-c9137f78fef111b8b50907f8becc0eb0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The sole objective of an occurrence report is the prevention of spaceflight accidents or major accidents, without the apportionment of blame or liability.
