# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 195

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Monitoring and oversight of US technology[^c-52e4d55323667bb387af74621c980bb4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A licensee must permit any person whom the US Government has authorised to do so to have access to and monitor any US launch vehicle, US spacecraft or US related equipment, in accordance with that person’s authorisation from the US Government.

(2) A licensee must not prevent a US licensee from accessing or monitoring the US technology in respect of which that US licensee has an export or transfer licence or authorisation from the US Government.
