# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 200

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Return of US technology if export licence etc. is revoked[^key-a5573b0e1d8d6a195ef7139e91eecfdc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A licensee which uses US technology for its licensed activities must ensure that in the event that a US export licence or authorisation for export or transfer of any of that US technology is revoked by the US Government, anything imported under the revoked licence or authorisation is either—

- (a) returned to the US in accordance with the US export licence or authorisation, or
- (b) sent to another location, if authorised by the US Government.
