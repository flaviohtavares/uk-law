# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 154

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Requirement to retain the safety case[^c-87539af13c60f99d8fa424b7e43c96a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A spaceport licensee must retain its current safety case for the duration of the licence.
