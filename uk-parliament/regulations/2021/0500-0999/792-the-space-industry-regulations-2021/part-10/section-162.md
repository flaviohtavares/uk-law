# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 162

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Safety equipment[^c-6a6abb9a23a13f6964d6246a38a7524b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a spaceport licensee owns, manages or controls any system or ground equipment used to protect persons or property at the spaceport or to comply with these Regulations, that licensee must ensure that any such system or equipment is—

- (a) maintained in efficient working order and in good repair, and
- (b) thoroughly examined and, where appropriate, tested at suitable intervals.
