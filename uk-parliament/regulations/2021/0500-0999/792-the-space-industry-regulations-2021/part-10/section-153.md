# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 153

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Horizontal spaceport location requirement[^key-c3e54118fac32dbcb71c17f38cfb8aa0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A horizontal spaceport must be located at an aerodrome which is either—

- (a) certified, or
- (b) CAA licensed.
