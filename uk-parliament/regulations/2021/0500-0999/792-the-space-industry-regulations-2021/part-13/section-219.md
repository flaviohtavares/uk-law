# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 219

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 219. Prescribed cases or circumstances under which a limit on the operator’s liability to government does not apply[^key-ed930c951a88d07422ecdf3b1a7b6903]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following cases and circumstances are prescribed for the purposes of section 36(3)(a) (obligation to indemnify government etc. against claims)—

- (a) cases where the operator is liable in respect of gross negligence[^f00064] or wilful misconduct in the performance of its obligations under the Act or regulations made under the Act;
- (b) circumstances where damage or loss is caused as a result of the non-compliance by the operator with—
  - (i) any conditions of its licence;
  - (ii) the requirements under the Act or regulations made under the Act.
