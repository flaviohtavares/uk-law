# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 221

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 221. Duty of the Secretary of State to indemnify[^key-174a545b47da301fe1c8f0393c19eba1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The cases and circumstances prescribed for the purposes of section 35(5)(b) (power or duty of Secretary of State to indemnify) are—

- (a) cases where the operator is liable in respect of gross negligence or wilful misconduct in the performance of its obligations under the Act or regulations made under the Act;
- (b) circumstances where damage or loss is caused as a result of the non-compliance by the operator with—
  - (i) any conditions of its licence;
  - (ii) any requirements under the Act or regulations made under the Act.
