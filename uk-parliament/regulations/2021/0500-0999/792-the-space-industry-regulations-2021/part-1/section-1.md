# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-f969bf2c4e6657351ffa4b33aedfdf23]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Space Industry Regulations 2021 and come into force on the twenty-first day after the day on which they are made.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
