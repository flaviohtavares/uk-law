> Text as at 2025-04-01.

## 24. Withdrawal of an application for a licence[^key-74cc2629bcce5ea141e6f6fee68751ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An applicant may withdraw an application for a licence by giving notice to the regulator at any time before the regulator determines the application.
