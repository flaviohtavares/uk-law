# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 16

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Interpretation of this Chapter[^key-d30e5eb87f90264baf102ca65424c315]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter “**licence**” means an operator licence, a range control licence or a spaceport licence.
