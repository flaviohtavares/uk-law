# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 3

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Appointment of Civil Aviation Authority[^c-200524dc21308fa66307f104c2edd1b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The CAA[^f00018] is appointed to exercise every function conferred on the regulator by or under the Act.
