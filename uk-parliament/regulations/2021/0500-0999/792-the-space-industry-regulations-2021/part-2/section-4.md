## 4. Concurrent appointment for functions of issuing guidance[^c-a7892a35c5cfe7664fdc392679fa99c7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the CAA is appointed to exercise functions under regulation 3, it is appointed instead of the Secretary of State, except for exercising functions of issuing guidance conferred by section 2(4), 7(7) or 11(6).

(2) For exercising the functions of issuing guidance mentioned in paragraph (1), the CAA is appointed as regulator concurrently with the Secretary of State.
