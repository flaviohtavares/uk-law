# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 267

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 267. Completion certificate[^c-7696b77c122323cd771295743605ae06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where, after service of the notice, the regulator is satisfied that the relevant person has taken the steps specified in the stop notice, the regulator must issue a certificate to that effect.

(2) A certificate under paragraph (1) is referred to in this Part as a “**completion certificate**”.

(3) The stop notice ceases to have effect on the issue of a completion certificate.

(4) The relevant person may at any time apply for a completion certificate.

(5) The regulator must make a decision as to whether to issue a completion certificate within 14 days of that application.
