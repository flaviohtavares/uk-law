# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 266

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 266. Contents of stop notice[^key-c5532fdd4e1dfe96254e87b7857a3c9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A stop notice must include information as to—

- (a) the grounds for serving the notice,
- (b) rights of appeal, and
- (c) the consequences of non-compliance.
