# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 227

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 227. Persons to whom obligation applies[^key-e39188652c587b594a413c65a25368f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies to a person who—

- (a) carries out spaceflight activities,
- (b) operates a spaceport,
- (c) occupies land forming part of a spaceport or is allowed access to such land for the purposes of the activities of a business carried on by the person,
- (d) provides range control services under a range control licence, or
- (e) carries out associated activities.
