# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 230

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Offence of failing to comply with information notice[^key-9a9d45f2dc029a7276f4964b5bef9b74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who contravenes regulation 229 commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with such an offence to show that they took all reasonable steps and exercised all due diligence to avoid committing the offence.
