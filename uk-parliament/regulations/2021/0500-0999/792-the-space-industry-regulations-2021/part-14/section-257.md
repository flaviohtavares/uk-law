# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 257

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 257. Defences to offence of disclosing protected information[^key-2ae14fac7d3310797a6ecf88af94c1c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under regulation 256 to prove—

- (a) that the person did not know, and had no reason to suspect, that the information disclosed was protected information, or
- (b) that the person took all reasonable precautions, and exercised all due diligence, to avoid committing the offence.
