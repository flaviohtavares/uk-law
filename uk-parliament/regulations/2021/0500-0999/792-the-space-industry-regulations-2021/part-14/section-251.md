# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 251

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 251. Supplementary powers[^key-3d897a5fd79a14b1da7f29081973aa5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A power conferred by this Section includes power to do anything incidental that is necessary for the inspector to fulfil the purpose of the inspector’s appointment.
