> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Anonymised information[^key-908f0c18741999d6156199297d2cde40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 255 does not prohibit a disclosure of protected information which is made in a form calculated to prevent a particular person or case, to whom the information relates, from being identified.
