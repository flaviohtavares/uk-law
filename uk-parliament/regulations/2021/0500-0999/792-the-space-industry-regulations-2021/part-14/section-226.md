> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 226. Penalty for impersonating inspector[^c-4b33cb84be433d6311bf2a8a37de2039]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under regulation 225 is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine;
- (b) in Scotland or Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
