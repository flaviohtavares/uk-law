# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 229

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 229. Obligation to provide information to regulator[^key-d2bf0ac2433409c5d4d6e252f8cd9002]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who receives an information notice must, before the end of the period specified in the notice, provide, or cause to be provided, to the regulator such information as is specified or described in the information notice.
