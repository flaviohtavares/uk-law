> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 238. Duties of inspector[^c-d0270e8badca113f2c9292029b118f72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An inspector must carry out all monitoring and enforcement activities and tasks that the regulator thinks necessary to discharge its obligations in section 26(1) for the purposes set out in section 26(2).
