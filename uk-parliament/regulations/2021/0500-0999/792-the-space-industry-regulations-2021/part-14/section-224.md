## 224. Penalty for obstructing inspector or regulator[^key-ab1d2ccc9aa25a1a569b40a438523eca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under regulation 223 is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to a fine;
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland or Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (c) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or a fine, or both.
