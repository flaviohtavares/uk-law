> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 232. Offence of providing false information[^c-ff42d154248ba3b38f2c90994bb468c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is an offence for a person, in purported compliance with regulation 229—

- (a) to make a statement that the person knows to be false in a material particular, or
- (b) recklessly to make a statement that is false in a material particular.
