# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 246

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246. Power to require the use of facilities and assistance[^c-64a813f2236eca69653490e9558feecc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A power conferred by this Section includes power to require any person to provide any facilities or assistance relating to matters or things—

- (a) within the person’s control, or
- (b) in relation to which the person has responsibilities,

which are needed in order to enable an inspector to exercise the power.
