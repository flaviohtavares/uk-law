# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 231

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 231. Penalty for failing to comply with information notice[^key-94f269e63d052ae3cf783da9a6e4929d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under regulation 230 is liable, on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine;
- (b) in Scotland or Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
