> Text as at 2025-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 223. Offence to obstruct inspector or regulator[^c-3558c85b451df4c1470f3620dac85cf2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is an offence for a person intentionally to obstruct or impede any person who is exercising a power or performing a duty under this Part.
