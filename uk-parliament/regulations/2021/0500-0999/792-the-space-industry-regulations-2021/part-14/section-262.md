# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 262

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Disclosure required under legislation[^key-44219e3e2b766badda69aaa6223534b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 255 does not prohibit a disclosure of protected information which is made in accordance with an obligation under—

- (a) the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00070], or
- (b) the Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00071].
