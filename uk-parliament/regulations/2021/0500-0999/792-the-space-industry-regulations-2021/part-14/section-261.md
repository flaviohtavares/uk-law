# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 261

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 261. Disclosure to authorities[^key-17759d1551994b58245d37823af54dfc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 255 does not prohibit a disclosure to a public or local authority, or an officer of that authority.

(2) Where protected information is disclosed by virtue of this paragraph, the person to whom the information is disclosed may only use the information for the purposes of the authority in question.
