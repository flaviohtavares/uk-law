# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 240

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 240. References to inspector[^key-90795ed753c4840e55c63306b0ca23d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter, references to an inspector, in relation to any power, are to the inspector exercising or proposing to exercise the power.
