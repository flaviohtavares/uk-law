# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 214

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 214. What happens after the consent form is signed[^c-5926d7fcf39d692214fff43e6c3dae8d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A human occupant must give the duplicate consent form to the spaceflight operator by hand or send such duplicate form to the spaceflight operator.
