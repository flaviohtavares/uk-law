# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 204

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. Prescribed role or capacity[^c-6c2c20cc451b9285196d236a37b2beb9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of section 17(1)—

- (a) a member of the crew is a prescribed role, and
- (b) a spaceflight participant is a prescribed capacity.
