# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 208

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 208. No derogation from statements in the consent form[^key-6ce4589b543dca63ac35cf6b417eab8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The consent form must not contain any provision which derogates from the statements required to be included in the form by regulation 207.
