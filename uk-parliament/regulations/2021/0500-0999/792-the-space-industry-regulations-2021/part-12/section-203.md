# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 203

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Interpretation[^c-e8e0f9a525fd10b7c10f531acee474b9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- (a) “**injury**” means personal injury, and
- (b) except in regulation 209, a reference to “**operator’s spaceflight activities**” is a reference to those activities to be carried out by a spaceflight operator in which a human occupant is to take part.
