# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 212

> Text as at 2025-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Who prepares the consent form[^key-66b64a93e461acbfa4efe757741de15a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The spaceflight operator must prepare the consent form in writing and in duplicate.
