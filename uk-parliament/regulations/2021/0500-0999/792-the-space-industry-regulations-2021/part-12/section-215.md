# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 215

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. A written record of the information provided to the human occupant[^key-6e5d202f0a5ea6807ccf5c0ace8102c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The spaceflight operator must keep a written record of—

- (a) the information provided to a human occupant in accordance with regulations 209 to 211, and
- (b) the date on which and time at which such information was provided to that individual.

(2) Before a human occupant signs the consent form, the spaceflight operator must give a copy of the written record referred to in paragraph (1) to that individual.
