# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 213

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. When a human occupant signs the consent form[^c-06779f1cb782538ff983267727c93a80]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A human occupant must sign both copies of the consent form no more than 24 hours before taking part in the operator’s spaceflight activities or, if the form relates to more than one flight, no more than 24 hours before taking part in the first of those flights.
