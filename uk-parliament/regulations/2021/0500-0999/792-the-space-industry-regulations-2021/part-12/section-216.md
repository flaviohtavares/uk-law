# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 216

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 216. The signification of consent[^key-6894d56f1d034c5ae0c8e0430bca9fc6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A human occupant must sign the consent form.

(2) A human occupant must date the consent form and record on the form the time at which the consent form was signed.

(3) An electronic signature satisfies the requirements to sign the consent form in paragraph (1).

(4) In this regulation “**electronic signature**” has the same meaning as in section 7(2) (electronic signatures and related certificates) of the Electronic Communications Act 2000[^f00061].
