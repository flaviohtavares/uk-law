> Text as at 2025-04-01.

## 127. Failure of pilot in command or remote pilot to carry out flight safely[^key-be553d8007021982702a9bc68c20b276]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A pilot in command, or a remote pilot, who fails to comply with the obligations in regulation 115(1), commits an offence.
