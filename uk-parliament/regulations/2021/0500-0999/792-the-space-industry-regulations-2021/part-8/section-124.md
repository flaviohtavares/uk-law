# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 124

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Failure of launch director to check conditions met before operator’s spaceflight activities commence[^key-c0a9f631c9638579662ef5a07dab0fba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A launch director who fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with the obligation to check that the conditions in regulation 99(2) are met before an operator’s spaceflight activities commence, commits an offence.
