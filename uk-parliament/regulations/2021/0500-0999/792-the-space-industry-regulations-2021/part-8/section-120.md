# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 120

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Prohibiting the launch vehicle carrying a spaceflight participant[^key-5e3d5a9b842dd0f6edc15d53e53f9593]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A spaceflight operator must not carry a spaceflight participant in a launch vehicle unless that vehicle is fit for the operator’s spaceflight activities in accordance with regulation 91.
