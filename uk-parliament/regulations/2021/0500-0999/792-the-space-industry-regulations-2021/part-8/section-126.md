# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 126

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Failure of a pilot in command or remote pilot to carry out obligations before the flight[^key-5976bf08359dbf9dbc67791a46ffa088]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A pilot in command, or a remote pilot, who fails to comply with the obligations in regulation 114(a) or (b) before the flight, commits an offence.
