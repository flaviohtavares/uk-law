# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 122

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Availability of seating requirement to a spaceflight participant[^c-a29e6e76c2e548d35e88289107c29164]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A spaceflight operator must make a copy of the requirement relating to seating in regulation 121 available to each spaceflight participant.
