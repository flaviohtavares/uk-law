# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 123

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Information about the operator’s spaceflight activities[^key-4db5092f8b2e0820ca786be7c5bbe472]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before an operator’s spaceflight activities commence, the spaceflight operator must give each human occupant the information referred to in regulations 209 and 210 which has become available since that occupant signed the consent form referred to in section 17.
