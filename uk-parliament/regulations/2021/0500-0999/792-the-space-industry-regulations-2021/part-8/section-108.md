# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 108

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Authority to pilot in command or remote pilot[^key-e09cae8b1f5288193206c429dcf7a3f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the launch vehicle has a pilot in command or a remote pilot, a spaceflight operator must give that pilot the authority to give the commands, make the decisions or take the actions referred to in regulation 115.
