# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 95

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. The spaceport (or other place of launch or landing) and the range[^c-94d73aeccf3899e5daae8b9640928595]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A spaceflight operator must ensure that the spaceport or other place used for the operator’s spaceflight activities is fit for those activities.

(2) A spaceflight operator must ensure that the range for the operator’s spaceflight activities is fit for those activities.
