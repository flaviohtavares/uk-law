# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 115

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Obligations of pilot in command or remote pilot to carry out flight safely[^key-9ab18ff89349a5c0b850e0d26b884cc5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a launch vehicle has a pilot in command or a remote pilot, that pilot must give commands, make appropriate decisions and take appropriate actions during the flight of that vehicle which are necessary to ensure that the flight is carried out safely.

(2) A pilot in command or a remote pilot must inform the spaceflight operator and the regulator at the earliest practicable opportunity if any of the commands, decisions or actions referred to in paragraph (1) did not comply with provisions contained in or made under the Act and the conditions of the launch operator licence or return operator licence.
