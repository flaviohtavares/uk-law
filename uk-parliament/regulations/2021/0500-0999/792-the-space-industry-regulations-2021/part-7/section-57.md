## 57. Specified capacities and criteria[^c-d6916735c2a084ac38f8122178b6986c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “Spaceflight participant” is a specified capacity for the purposes of section 18(4)(a).

(2) The criteria in paragraph 52 of Schedule 3 are specified criteria in relation to spaceflight participants for the purposes of section 18(4).
