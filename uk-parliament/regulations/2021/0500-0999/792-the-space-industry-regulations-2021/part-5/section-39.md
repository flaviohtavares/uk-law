# The Space Industry Regulations 2021, section 39

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. “Members of the public”: prescribed meaning under section 2(7) for the purpose of section 10(a) (grant of a spaceport licence)[^c-81d5a35659669513131790a2736f7c2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“Members of the public” for the purpose of the reference to “public safety” in section 10(a) does not include any person who is—

- (a) listed in regulation 40, and
- (b) voluntarily in close proximity to a source of danger at a spaceport.
