The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 25th May 2021.

(2) These Regulations extend to Great Britain.

## 2. Amendment of Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 1191/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/1191)

(1) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 1191/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/1191) determining the format and means for submitting the report referred to in Article 19 of [Regulation (EU) No 517/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0517) of the European Parliament and of the Council on fluorinated greenhouse gases[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Annex—

- (a) in row 1C\_a of the table in Section 1, for “Member State” substitute “part of Great Britain”;
- (b) in the bold heading to Section 6, for “EU market” substitute “market”;
- (c) in the fifth paragraph after the bold heading to Section 8, for “the EU”, in both places it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under section 8(2)(a)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

These Regulations make amendments to legislation in the field of fluorinated greenhouse gases.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sector is foreseen.
