# The Official Controls (Extension of Transitional Periods) Regulations 2021

> Text as at 2025-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

2021 No. 809

Animals

Food

Plant Health

The Official Controls (Extension of Transitional Periods) Regulations 2021

Made

7th July 2021

Laid before Parliament

8th July 2021

Coming into force

30th July 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by:

- (a) Article 144(6) of, and paragraph 2 of Annex 6 to, Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products [^c24553921],
- (b) regulation 71(1) of the Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 [^c24553931], and
- (c) regulation 33(2) of the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 [^c24553941].

In accordance with Article 144(7) of Regulation (EU) 2017/625, before making these Regulations, the Secretary of State has consulted such bodies and persons as appear to the Secretary of State to be representative of the interests likely to be substantially affected by these Regulations and such other bodies or persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

In accordance with Article 3(2B) of Regulation (EU) 2017/625 [^c24553951], regulation 71(2) of the Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, and regulation 35(2) of the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020, the Welsh Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales, and the Scottish Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Scotland, have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application[^key-c7968c5add83b0c84d9bdf84af2bc8ce]

(1) These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Official Controls (Extension of Transitional Periods) Regulations 2021, and
- (b) come into force on 30th July 2021.

(2) This regulation, and regulations 2, 3, 4, 7 and 8 extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.

(3) Regulation 5 applies in relation to England only.

(4) Regulation 6 extends to Scotland only.

## 2. End of the transitional staging period[^M_E_3c1fa7e5-b1e8-4f15-add2-08b52c67710e][^key-0571c9b19846b474292a766d3784d9f8]

*Extent: E+S*

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations amend transitional periods relating to official controls requirements for certain types of animals and goods being imported into Great Britain.

Regulation 2 appoints the end date for “the transitional staging period” (defined in Annex 6 to Regulation (EU) 2017/625 (EUR 2017/625)) as 28th February 2022. For certain types of product, the extension of the transitional staging period is to earlier dates: 30th September 2021 and 31st December 2021.

Regulation 3 appoints the end date for the general transitional provision for certain model certificates, as provided for in regulation 33 of the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 (S.I. 2020/1631), as 30th September 2021. Regulations 7 and 8 make amendments also providing for a transitional period for model certificates or other documents to end on 30th September 2021.

Regulations 4 to 6 make amendments resulting from these changes.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact, or no significant impact, on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
