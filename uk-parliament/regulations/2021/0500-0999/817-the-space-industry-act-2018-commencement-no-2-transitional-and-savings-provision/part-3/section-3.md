# The Space Industry Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2, Transitional and Savings Provisions) Regulations 2021, section 3

> Text as at 2021-07-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Transitional and savings provisions relating to the grant of a licence[^key-d2b044177fd8d39dc840751cdca5cb34]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraphs (1) and (2) of this regulation apply where—

- (a) an application for a licence has been made under the Outer Space Act 1986 [^c24555931] before the coming into force of the provisions in or made under the Act which enables licences to be granted,
- (b) the application has not yet been determined, and
- (c) the application falls to be determined under the Act.

(2) The application shall continue to be processed and determined under the Outer Space Act 1986.

(3) A licence which has been determined in accordance with paragraph (2) after the date of the coming into force of the provisions in or made under the Act which enables licences to be granted shall be treated as if granted under the Act.
