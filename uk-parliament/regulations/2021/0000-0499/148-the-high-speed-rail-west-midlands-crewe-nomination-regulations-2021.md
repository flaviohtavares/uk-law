# The High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) (Nomination) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 148

Transport

The High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) (Nomination) Regulations 2021

Made

11th February 2021

Coming into force

12th February 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1) of the High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) Act 2021[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) (Nomination) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 12th February 2021.

## 2. Appointment of nominated undertaker

High Speed Two (HS2) Ltd is the nominated undertaker for—

- (a) all purposes of the scheduled works; and
- (b) the purposes of any other provision of the High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) Act 2021 so far as connected with or in any way related to those works or otherwise for Phase 2a purposes.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Andrew Stephenson

Minister of State

Department for Transport

11th February 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations appoints High Speed Two (HS2) Ltd as the nominated undertaker for all purposes of the scheduled works of the High Speed Rail (West Midlands – Crewe) Act 2021 and for any other purposes under that Act in so far as those purposes are connected to those works or otherwise for Phase 2a purposes.
