# The Riverside Energy Park (Correction) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 273

Infrastructure Planning

The Riverside Energy Park (Correction) Order 2021

Made

9th March 2021

Coming into force

10th March 2021

The Riverside Energy Park Order 2020 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed the London Borough of Bexley and Dartford Borough Council, as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00005], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Riverside Energy Park (Correction) Order 2021.

(2) This Order comes into force on 10th March 2021.

## 2. Corrections

The Riverside Energy Park Order 2020 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Gareth Leigh

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

9th March 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors and omissions in the Riverside Energy Park Order 2020 ([S.I. 2020/419](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/419)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
