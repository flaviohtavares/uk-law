# The Registration of Marriages Regulations 2021, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Repeal of sections 53 to 62 of the 1949 Act

Omit sections 53 to 62 of the 1949 Act (registration of marriages)[^f00031].
