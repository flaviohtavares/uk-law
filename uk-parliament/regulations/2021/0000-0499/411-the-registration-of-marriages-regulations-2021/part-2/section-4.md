# The Registration of Marriages Regulations 2021, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment of section 28B of the 1949 Act

In section 28B of the 1949 Act (provision of evidence)[^f00025], after subsection (1), insert—

> (1A) If a party to the marriage is a relevant national within the meaning of paragraph (ab) of the definition of “relevant national” in section 78(1)[^f00026], the notice of marriage under section 27 must also be accompanied—
>
> - (a) where the party falls within paragraph (ab)(i) of that definition, by an electronic certificate which confirms that the leave referred to in that provision has been granted; or
> - (b) where the party falls within paragraph (ab)(ii) of that definition—
>   - (i) by a certificate of application which confirms that the application referred to in regulation 4 of the Citizens’ Rights (Application Deadline and Temporary Protection) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^f00027] has been made; and
>   - (ii) by evidence that the relevant period referred to in regulation 4 of those Regulations has not expired.

.
