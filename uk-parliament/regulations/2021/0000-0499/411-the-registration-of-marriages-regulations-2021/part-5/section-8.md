# The Registration of Marriages Regulations 2021, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendment of the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Act 1915

(1) Section 1 of the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Act 1915 (facilities for marriages between British subjects resident in the United Kingdom and British subjects resident elsewhere)[^f00032] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “in the United Kingdom” (in the first place it appears) substitute “in Scotland or Northern Ireland”;
- (b) omit “certificates for marriage issued by superintendent registrars in England and of”.

(3) In paragraph (a) of subsection (1)—

- (a) for “the United Kingdom” substitute “Scotland or Northern Ireland”;
- (b) for “England, Scotland,” substitute “Scotland”;
- (c) omit “in England have the same effect as a certificate for marriage issued by a superintendent registrar, and”.

(4) In paragraph (b) of subsection (1)—

- (a) for “England, Scotland,” (in both places it appears) substitute “Scotland”;
- (b) omit “in England by a superintendent registrar, or”.

## Annotations

[^f00032]: Footnote: [1915 c. 40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/40). Section 1 was amended by the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Amendment Act 1916. The Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Act 1915 was repealed in Scotland by Schedule 3 to the Marriage (Scotland) Act [1977 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/15), save that by section 27(3) nothing in that Act affected the validity of any marriage solemnised or contracted before 1st January 1978.
