# The Registration of Marriages Regulations 2021, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Amendment Act 1916

In section 1 of the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Amendment Act 1916[^f00033], for “England, Scotland,” substitute “Scotland” (in each place it appears).

## Annotations

[^f00033]: Footnote: [1916 c. 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1916/21). The Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Amendment Act 1916 was repealed in Scotland by Schedule 3 of the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977, save that by section 27(3) nothing in that Act affect the validity of any marriage solemnised or contracted before 1st January 1978.
