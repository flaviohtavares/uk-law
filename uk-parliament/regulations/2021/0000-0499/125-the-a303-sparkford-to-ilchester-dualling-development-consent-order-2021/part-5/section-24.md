# The A303 Sparkford to Ilchester Dualling Development Consent Order 2021, section 24

> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-31cd5476e86a228353294f4e3eb5cbc5]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24442511] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “ the undertaker ”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “ authorised development ”.
