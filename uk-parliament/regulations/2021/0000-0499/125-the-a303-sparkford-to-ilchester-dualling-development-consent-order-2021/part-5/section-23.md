# The A303 Sparkford to Ilchester Dualling Development Consent Order 2021, section 23

> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-28ed013d1615615dfafb64e70275c7e6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required to carry out or to facilitate, or is incidental to, the authorised development.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 26 (compulsory acquisition of rights) and paragraph (8) of article 33 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
