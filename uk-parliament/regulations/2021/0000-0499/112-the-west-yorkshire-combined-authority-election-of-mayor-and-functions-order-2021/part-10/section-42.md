## 42. Modifications to the Local Government Act 1972[^c-8880fae4e4ae91e9ebe29c02d7f3ca95]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 86 of the Local Government Act 1972 [^c24434051] applies in relation to the Mayor with the following modification.

(2) After section 86(1)(c), insert—

> or
>
> - (d) ceases to be the Mayor by virtue of section 63 of the 2011 Act;

.
