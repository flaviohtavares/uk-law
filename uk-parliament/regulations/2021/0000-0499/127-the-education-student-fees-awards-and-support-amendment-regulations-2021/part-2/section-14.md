# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2021, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Amendment of regulation 69

In regulation 69(3)[^f00020] (qualifying conditions for the loan for living costs – current system students), for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) the only paragraph or paragraphs in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the student falls is one or more of paragraphs 2A, 9, 9A, 9C, 9D, 10 and 10ZA;

.
