# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2021, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Amendment of regulation 8

In regulation 8[^f00083] (events)—

- (a) omit paragraph (c);
- (b) for paragraph (d) substitute—

  > - (d) the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 10A(1)(a), 10B(1)(a), 10C(1)(a) or 10D(1)(a) of Schedule 1;

  ;
- (c) omit paragraph (f);
- (d) in paragraph (g), for “7(1)(a)” substitute “7A(1)(a)”;
- (e) for paragraph (h) substitute—

  > - (h) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 12A(a) of Schedule 1;

  .
