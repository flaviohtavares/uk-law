# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2021, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Amendment of regulation 6

In regulation 6(2)[^f00074] (qualifying person: effect of event during academic year)—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (c);
- (b) for sub-paragraph (d) substitute—

  > - (d) the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 10A(1)(a), 10B(1)(a), 10C(1)(a), 10D(1)(a) or 10E(1)(a) in Part 2 of the Schedule;

  ;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (f);
- (d) in sub-paragraph (g), for “paragraph 7(1)(a)” substitute “paragraph 7A(1)(a) in Part 2 of the Schedule”;
- (e) for sub-paragraph (h) substitute—

  > - (h) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 12A(a) in Part 2 of the Schedule;

  .
