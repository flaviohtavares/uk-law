# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2021, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Amendment of regulation 7

In regulation 7[^f00062] (students becoming eligible other than prior to the beginning of a designated further education course)—

- (a) omit paragraph (c);
- (b) for paragraph (d) substitute—

  > - (d) the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 9A(1)(a), 9B(1)(a), 9C(1)(a) or 9D(1)(a) of Schedule 1;

  ;
- (c) omit paragraph (f);
- (d) in paragraph (g), for “6(1)(a)” substitute “6A(1)(a)”;
- (e) for paragraph (h) substitute—

  > - (h) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 11A(a) of Schedule 1;

  .
