# The A1 Birtley to Coal House Development Consent Order 2021, section 24

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-3895663a4eba63ba6b72b5760d2de9a6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24427541] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications, that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “ the undertaker ”; and
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “ authorised development ”.
