# The A1 Birtley to Coal House Development Consent Order 2021, section 39

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39. Scheduled Monuments[^key-3951cb2feb7277fd19075c13d0d6918b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker is authorised to carry out the works specified in column (2) of Schedule 10 (scheduled monuments) in relation to the scheduled monuments specified in column (1) of that Schedule.
