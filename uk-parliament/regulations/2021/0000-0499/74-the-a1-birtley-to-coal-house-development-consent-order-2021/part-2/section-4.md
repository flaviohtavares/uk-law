# The A1 Birtley to Coal House Development Consent Order 2021, section 4

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised development[^c-8a1e900acc9339f521df4c11abb94b4e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
