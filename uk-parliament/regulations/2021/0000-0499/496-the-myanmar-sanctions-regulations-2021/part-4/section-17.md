# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 17

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Immigration[^c-77ce404dc05032da4110c8485e099c0e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c24500181].
