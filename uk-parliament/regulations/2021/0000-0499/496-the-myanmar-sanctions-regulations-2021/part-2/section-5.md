# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^c-78965abe5a60a612ffdfa1d770d844d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons [^c24500171] by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^c-12aca5a6dc8efa38cf2ac5a0dee6fac7] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc.);
- (aa)[^c-59961b5687aaca0f338abea6d2db8319] regulation 16A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 17 (immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
