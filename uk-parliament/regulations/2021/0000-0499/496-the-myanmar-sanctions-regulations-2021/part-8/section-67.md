# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-d4b78ae926f1f792cc2f44983758754c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c24500491] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 47 (finance: licensing offences).
