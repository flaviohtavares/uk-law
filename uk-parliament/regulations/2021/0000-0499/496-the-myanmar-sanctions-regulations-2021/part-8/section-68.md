# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Monetary penalties[^c-3de5859f81ea975e2c143801b545346e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 5 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c24500501].
