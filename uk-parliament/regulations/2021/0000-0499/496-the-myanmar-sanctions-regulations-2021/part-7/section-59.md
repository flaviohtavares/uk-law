# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 59

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Part 7: supplementary[^c-a7ef2e404cef83a2d20523c11039e85a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A disclosure of information under regulation 58 (disclosure of information) or 58A (finance: disclosure to the Treasury)[^c-bdff6eb258803e7452e9aa7ce28a94cb] does not breach any restriction on such disclosure imposed by statute or otherwise.

(2) But nothing in those regulations[^c-16b438a36a54bbef29fc7f19d7b6f554] authorises a disclosure that—

- (a) contravenes the data protection legislation, or
- (b) is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 7 or Chapter 1 of Part 9 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 [^c24500351].

(3) Nothing in this Part is to be read as requiring a person who has acted or is acting as counsel or solicitor for any person to disclose any privileged information in their possession in that capacity.

(4) Regulations 58 and 58A do[^c-ef659033631b60feea0c0e22cdfa717d] not limit the circumstances in which information may be disclosed apart from those regulations[^c-15fd00e7b62de3145bf8e38e0fed2456].

(5) Nothing in this Part limits any conditions which may be contained in a Treasury licence or a trade licence.

(6) In this regulation—

- “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act) [^c24500361];
- “**privileged information**” means information with respect to which a claim to legal professional privilege (in Scotland, to confidentiality of communications) could be maintained in legal proceedings.
