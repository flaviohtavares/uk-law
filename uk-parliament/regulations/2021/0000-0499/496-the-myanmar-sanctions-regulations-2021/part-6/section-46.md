# The Myanmar (Sanctions) Regulations 2021, section 46

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Licences: general provisions[^key-c86c630fffde71101ff4d4fc61a18a48]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^key-12a035d3931e14d6bdc2c08d3e4573f9] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-6fc8b80e7d44918d6fc3ab998112f6fa]**P**”), issues[^key-25cd1765e65fd71be8b12c165badfab2] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-f528ef195356945013b4e2a5a31e2caf] must give written notice to P[^key-bff7889f9068c4876cca67556b3453cc] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
