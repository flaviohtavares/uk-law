# The Charities (Exception from Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2655

CHARITIES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Charities (Exception from Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made

10 September 2007

Laid before Parliament

10 September 2007

Coming into force

1st October 2007

The Minister for the Cabinet Office makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(5)(b) and (13)(b) of the Charities Act 1993[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Charities (Exception from Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 1st October 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 1996

The Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 1996[^f00002] are amended as follows:

- (a) in regulation 4(1) for “until 1st October 2007”, substitute “until 1st October 2012”;
- (b) in regulation 4(2) for “a body”, substitute “one or more of the bodies”;
- (c) for regulation 4(2)(a), substitute—

  > - (a) having as its trustee, or one of its trustees, a trust corporation connected with—
  >   - (i) the body; or
  >   - (ii) one of the bodies,
  >
  >   with which the charity is connected.

## 3. Revocation

The Charities (Exception From Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2002[^f00003] are revoked.

Phil Hope

Minister for the Third Sector

10 September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 1996. They extend the temporary exception granted to certain religious charities from the requirement to be registered under the Charities Act 1993. The temporary exception, which would have originally expired on 1st March 2001, was extended until 1st October 2002 and then again, by the Charities (Exception From Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2002, until 1st October 2007. These Regulations further extend the temporary exception until 1st October 2012 and revoke the 2002 Regulations.

These Regulations also make a minor amendment to clarify the scope of the temporary exception.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
