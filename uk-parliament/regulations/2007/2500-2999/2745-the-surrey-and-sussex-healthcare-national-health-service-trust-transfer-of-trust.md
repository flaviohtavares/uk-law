# The Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2745

National health service, england

The Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007

Made

16th September 2007

Laid before Parliament

21st September 2007

Coming into force

1st November 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of the Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> November 2007.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust; and
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the two schedules of trust property more specifically described in paragraphs (1) and (2) of article 2.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On the date that this Order comes into force the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the Surrey Primary Care Trust[^f00003] and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 18<sup>th</sup> June 2007 and on behalf of the Surrey Primary Care Trust on 27<sup>th</sup> June 2007, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Surrey Primary Care Trust.

(2) On the date that this Order comes into force the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the Worthing and Southlands Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00004] and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 18<sup>th</sup> June 2007 and on behalf of the Worthing and Southlands Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 27<sup>th</sup> June 2007, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Worthing and Southlands Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

## 3. Construction of references

In any instrument relating to any of the trust property, any reference in relation to that property to the trustees of that property or to the old Trust, shall be construed as a reference to—

- (a) in so far as the reference relates to property referred to in article 2(1), the Surrey Primary Care Trust; and
- (b) in so far as the reference relates to property referred to in article 2(2), the Worthing and Southlands Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Ben Bradshaw

Minister of State

Department of Health

16th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Surrey Primary Care Trust and the Worthing and Southlands Hospitals National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the Surrey and Sussex Healthcare National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedules of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
