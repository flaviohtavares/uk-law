Made

6th September 2007

Coming into force

12 noon on 8th September 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Disease Control (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007 and comes into force at 12.00 noon on 8th September 2007.

## 2. Amendment

The Disease Control (England) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended by omitting article 3A (emergency measures).

Phil Woolas

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

6th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Disease Control (England) Order 2003 ([S.I. 2003/1729](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1729)) to remove the emergency measures that increase the standstill period from 6 to 20 days and disapply paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 and paragraph 13 of Schedule 2 in respect of certain movements in a sole occupancy group.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order.
