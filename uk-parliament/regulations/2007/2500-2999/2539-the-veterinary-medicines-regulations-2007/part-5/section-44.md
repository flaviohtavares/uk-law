# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2007, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Revocation

The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2006[^f00019] (other than paragraphs (2) to (4) of regulation 44 and Schedules 8 and 9) are revoked.
