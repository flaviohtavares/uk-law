# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2007, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Products to which these Regulations do not apply

(1) These Regulations do not apply to a veterinary medicinal product based on radio-active isotopes.

(2) They do not apply in relation to the administration of a product in the course of a procedure licensed under the Animals (Scientific Procedure) Act 1986[^f00010], except that, if the animals are to be put into the human food chain, the only products that may be administered to the animals are—

- (a) authorised veterinary medicinal products administered in accordance with their marketing authorisation, or
- (b) products administered in accordance with an animal test certificate granted under paragraph 9 of Schedule 4.
