# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2754 (C. 107 )

Police, Scotland

POLICE, Northern IRELAND

The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007

Made

17th September 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001].

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland and Northern Ireland.

**2.** Section 41 (immigration and asylum enforcement function: complaints and misconduct) of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force in Scotland and Northern Ireland on 10th October 2007.

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

17th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 10th October 2007 in Scotland and Northern Ireland section 41 of the Police and Justice Act 2006.
