# The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2007/2175](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2175) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

2007 No. 2800 (L.28)

FAMILY PROCEEDINGS, ENGLAND AND WALES

supreme court of england and wales

county courts, england and wales

The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

24th September 2007

Laid before Parliament

25th September 2007

Coming into force

28th September 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001].

In accordance with section 92(1) of the Courts Act 2003 he has obtained the consent of the Treasury.

In accordance with section 92(5) and (6) of the Courts Act 2003 he has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice and the Civil Justice Council.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 28th September 2007.

**2.** The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following articles.

**3.** In column 1 in fee 8.2 in Schedule 1 in the Schedule omit the words “(excluding VAT and disbursements)”.

**4.** For paragraph 2(1)(b) in Schedule 1A in the Schedule substitute—

> - (b) is in receipt of legal advice and assistance under Part II or Part III of the Legal Aid Act 1988 in connection with the matter to which the proceedings relate; or

.

Jack Straw

Lord Chancellor

19th September 2007

We consent,

Claire M Ward

Steve McCabe

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

24th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/2175 (L.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2175)) so as to correct mistakes in that Order.
