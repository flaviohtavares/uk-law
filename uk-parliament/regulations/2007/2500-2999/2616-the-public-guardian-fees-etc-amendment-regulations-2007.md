# The Public Guardian (Fees, etc) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> Text as at 2007-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2007/2051](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2051) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2007 No. 2616

MENTAL CAPACITY, ENGLAND & WALES

The Public Guardian (Fees, etc) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made

4th September 2007

Laid before Parliament

7th September 2007

Coming into force

1st October 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 58(3) and (4) and 65(1)(b) of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-3e47b5e58405810db504ed99477ec7c8]

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Guardian (Fees, etc) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st October 2007.

## 2. Amendment to the Public Guardian (Fees, etc) Regulations 2007[^key-fa3cb121a22baa08dbec2463ea4cc43c]

(1) Regulation 9 (exemptions) of the Public Guardian (Fees, etc) Regulations 2007[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 9(7)(b)(ii) omit the reference to “child”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

4th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations makes a minor amendment to regulation 9(7)(b)(ii) of the Public Guardian (Fees, etc) Regulations 2007.
