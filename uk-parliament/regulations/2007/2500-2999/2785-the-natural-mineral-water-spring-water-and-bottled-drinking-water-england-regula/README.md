# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2007 No. 2785

FOOD, ENGLAND

The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007

Made

19th September 2007

Laid before Parliament

*27*th September 2007

Coming into force

31st October 2007

[^c18903671][^c18903681]The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1), 17(1), 26(1)(a) and (3), 31 and 48(1) of and paragraphs 1 and 4(b) of Schedule 1 to the Food Safety Act 1990 and now vested in him .

In accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, he has taken into account relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety [^c18903691] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.[^c-c6a41cea99ace03776f6de48142dfa77]

Regulation 13(4) of [S.I. 2000/656](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/656) expressly authorises the Secretary of State to amend or revoke existing Regulations made or having effect as if made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (whether with others or not) under the Food Safety Act 1990.
