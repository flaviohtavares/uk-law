# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007, section 3

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**3.** The Community instruments are—

- (a) Council Directive [80/777/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0777) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the exploitation and marketing of natural mineral waters (OJ No. L229, 30.8.80, p.1) as last amended by Regulation [(EC) No. 1882/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1882) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L284, 31.10.2003, p.1);
- (b) Commission Directive [2003/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0040) establishing the list, concentration limits and labelling requirements for the constituents of natural mineral waters and the conditions for using ozone-enriched air for the treatment of natural mineral waters and spring waters (OJ No. L126, 22.5.2003, p34) ; and
- (c) in relation to spring water and bottled drinking water, Council Directive [98/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0083) relating to the quality of water intended for human consumption (OJ No. L330, 3.11.98, p.32) .
