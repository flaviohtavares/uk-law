# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007, section 14

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Marking, labelling and advertising of bottled drinking water

*Extent: E+W*

No person shall—

- (a) cause any drinking water [^c-efd039c4554237588be0962c72e63d8d]... to be bottled in a bottle marked or labelled with—
  - (i) any designation, proprietary name, trade mark, brand name, illustration or other sign, whether emblematic or not, the use of which is liable to cause confusion of the water with a natural mineral water, or
  - (ii) the description “mineral water”; or
- (b) cause any bottled drinking water [^c-4abb525f311d0859873023b90ffd57f3]... to be advertised under—
  - (i) any designation, proprietary name, trade mark, brand name, illustration or other sign, whether emblematic or not, the use of which is liable to cause confusion of the water with a natural mineral water, or
  - (ii) the description “mineral water”.
