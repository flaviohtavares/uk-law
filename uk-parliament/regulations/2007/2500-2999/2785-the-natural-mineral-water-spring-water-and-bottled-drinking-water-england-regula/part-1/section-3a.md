# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007, section 3A

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3A. Additional language[^c-049fb49bc5e1568923eee90cdae710b2]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in these Regulations prevents a bottle being marked or labelled with any other language in addition to English.
