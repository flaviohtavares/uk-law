# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007, section 1

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations may be cited as the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (England) Regulations 2007, apply in relation to England only and come into force on 31st October 2007.
