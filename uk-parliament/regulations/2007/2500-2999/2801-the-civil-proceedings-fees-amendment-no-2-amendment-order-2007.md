# The Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2007/2176](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2176) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

2007 No. 2801 (L.29)

supreme court of england and wales

county courts, england and wales

The Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

24th September 2007

Laid before Parliament

25th September 2007

Coming into force

28th September 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001].

In accordance with section 92(1) of the Courts Act 2003 he has obtained the consent of the Treasury.

In accordance with section 92(5) and (6) of the Courts Act 2003 he has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice and the Civil Justice Council.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 28th September 2007.

**2.** (1) Schedule 1 in the Schedule to the Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007[^f00002], is amended as follows.

(2) In column 2—

- (a) in fee 1.1(c), for “£60” substitute “£65”;
- (b) in fee 1.1(d), for “£70” substitute “£75”;
- (c) in fee 1.1(e), for “£80” substitute “£85”;
- (d) in fee 1.3(c), for “£65” substitute “£60”;
- (e) in fee 1.3(d), for “£75” substitute “£70”;
- (f) in fee 1.3(e), for “£85” substitute “£80”.

(3) In column 1—

- (a) in fee 5.2, omit the words “(excluding VAT and disbursements)”; and
- (b) in fee 10, omit the heading “foreign process”.

(4) Omit fee 10.6.

Jack Straw

Lord Chancellor

19th September 2007

We consent,

Claire M Ward

Steve McCabe

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

24th September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/2176 (L.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2176)) so as to correct mistakes in that Order.

The table below correctly sets out the fees that were incorrect in [S.I. 2007/2176](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2176).

| Fee number | Incorrect fee | Correct fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1(c) Money claim over £500 not over £1000 | £60 | £65 |
| 1.1(d) Money claim over £1000 not over £1500 | £70 | £75 |
| 1.1(e) Money claim over £1500 not over £3000 | £80 | £85 |
| 1.3(c) Money claim by Money Claim Online users over £500 not over £1000 | £65 | £60 |
| 1.3(d) Money claim by Money Claim Online users over £1000 not over £1500 | £75 | £70 |
| 1.3(e) Money claim by Money Claim Online users over £1500 not over £3000 | £85 | £80 |
