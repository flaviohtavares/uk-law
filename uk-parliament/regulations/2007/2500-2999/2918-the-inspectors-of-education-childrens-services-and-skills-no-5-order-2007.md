# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.5) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2918

education, england

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.5) Order 2007

Made

10th October 2007

Coming into force

11th October 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of October 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2007 and comes into force on 11th October 2007.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### Persons appointed on 11th October 2007

- Bernard Campbell
- Linda Margaret Christie
- David Muir

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
