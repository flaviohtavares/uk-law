# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.4) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2144

education, england

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.4) Order 2007

Made

25th July 2007

Coming into force

26th July 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 25th day of July 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2007 and comes into force on 26th July 2007.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills with effect from 1st September 2007.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### Persons appointed on 1st September 2007

- David Charles Edwards
- Grahame Maurice Sherfield
- Julie Anne Marguerite Winyard

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
