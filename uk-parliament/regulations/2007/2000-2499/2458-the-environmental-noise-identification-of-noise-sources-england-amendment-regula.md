[^key-c9154886bd8054a9f9938094fc0df0a2]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 4 to the Environmental Noise (Identification of Noise Sources) (England) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/415](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/415)). Schedule 4 lists the major airports for the purposes of the Environmental Noise (England) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/3228](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3228)) which implement Directive [2002/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0049) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 June 2002 relating to the assessment and management of environmental noise (O.J. No. L 189, 18.07.2002 p. 12. Regulation 2 of these Regulations removes three airports (Gloucestershire, London Biggin Hill and Shoreham) from the table in Schedule 4.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
