# The Finance Act 2007 Schedule 1 (Appointed Date) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2172 (C. 82)

EXCISE

The Finance Act 2007 Schedule 1 (Appointed Date) Order 2007

Made

26th July 2007

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(2) of the Finance Act 2007[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2007 Schedule 1 (Appointed Date) Order 2007.

**2.** 1st September 2007 is appointed as the date on or after which the amendments made by Schedule 1 to the Finance Act 2007 (remote gaming duty) have effect in respect of the provision of facilities for remote gaming.

Steve Lamey

Dave Hartnett

Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs

26th July 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st September 2007 as the date on or after which the amendments made by Schedule 1 to the Finance Act [2007 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/11) have effect.

Those amendments[^f00002] charge a duty of excise to be known as remote gaming duty on the provision of facilities for remote gaming.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
