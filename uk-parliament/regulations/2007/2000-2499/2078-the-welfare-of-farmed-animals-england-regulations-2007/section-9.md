# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (England) Regulations 2007, section 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Penalties

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 7 is liable on summary conviction to—

- (a) imprisonment for a term not exceeding 51 weeks;
- (b) a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale; or
- (c) both the term of imprisonment referred to sub-paragraph (a) and the fine referred to in sub-paragraph (b).

(2) In relation to an offence committed before the commencement of section 281(5) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00011], the reference in paragraph (1)(a) to 51 weeks must be taken as a reference to 6 months.
