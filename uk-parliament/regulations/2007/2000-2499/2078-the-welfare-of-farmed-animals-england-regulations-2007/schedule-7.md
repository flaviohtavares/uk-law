# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (England) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Additional conditions that apply to the keeping of cattle

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 5

**1.** Where lactating dairy cows or calving cows are kept in a building, they must have access at all times to a well-drained and bedded lying area.

**2.** A pen or yard in a building used for calving cows must be of such a size as to permit a person to attend the cows.

**3.** Calving cows which are kept in a building must be kept separate from livestock other than calving cows.
