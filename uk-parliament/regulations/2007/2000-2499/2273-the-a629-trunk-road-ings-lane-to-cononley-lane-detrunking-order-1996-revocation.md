# The A629 Trunk Road (Ings Lane to Cononley Lane) (Detrunking) Order 1996 (Revocation) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2273

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A629 Trunk Road (Ings Lane to Cononley Lane) (Detrunking) Order 1996 (Revocation) Order 2007

Made

12th July 2007

Coming into force

1st October 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001].

**1.** The A629 Trunk Road (Ings Lane to Cononley Lane) (Detrunking) Order 1996[^f00002] is revoked.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the A629 Trunk Road (Ings Lane to Cononley Lane) (Detrunking) Order 1996 (Revocation) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st October 2007.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Arthur Ashburner

Divisional Director Highways Agency

12th July 2007
