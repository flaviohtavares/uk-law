# The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2491 (C. 94)

northern ireland

The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

Made

20th August 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(3)(b) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 20(1) of, and Schedule 8 to, the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (reconstitution of district policing partnerships) shall come into force on 4th September 2007.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

20 August 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 4th September 2007 section 20(1) of, and Schedule 8 to, the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006, which make provision for the reconstitution of district policing partnerships.
