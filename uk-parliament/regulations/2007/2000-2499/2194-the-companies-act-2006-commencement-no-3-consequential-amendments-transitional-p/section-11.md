# The Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3, Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2007, section 11

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Revocation of spent transitional adaptations[^c-bb050dc1c3dbb569ad3a903a7ca89359]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions (which make transitional adaptations that are no longer needed as a result of this Order) are revoked—

- (a) in Schedule 1 to the Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2006[^f00006], paragraphs 12(2) and 16;
- (b) in Schedule 1 to the Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2, Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2007[^f00007], paragraph 4.
