# The Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3, Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2007, section 12

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. General savings[^c-ac3cce45ccf41d6ec6f73023346d7c83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amendments and repeals made by this Order do not affect the operation of section 1297 of the Companies Act 2006 (continuity of the law).

(2) Nothing in this Order affects any provision of the 1985 Act or the 1986 Order as applied by the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001[^f00008] or the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00009] to limited liability partnerships.
