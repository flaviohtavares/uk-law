# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Security Establishments in Northern Ireland) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2376

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Security Establishments in Northern Ireland) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made

9th August 2007

Coming into force

31st August 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport no longer deems it necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in the vicinity of certain security establishments in Northern Ireland and, in exercise of her powers under article 96 of the Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Security Establishments in Northern Ireland) (Revocation) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 31st August 2007.

**2.** The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Security Establishments in Northern Ireland) Regulations 1996[^f00002] are revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

9th August 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Security Establishments in Northern Ireland) Regulations 1996 as amended.
