# The Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2007-07-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2007/2089
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Modifications of the Election Petition Rules 1960[^c-65e7eda6c98300f77851d0dc114c30e2]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 15

| (1) Provision modified | (2) Modification |
| --- | --- |
| Rule 2(2)[^f00151] | After the definition of “the Act” insert—<br>> - “the 2007 Regulations” means the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2007;<br>.<br>After the definition of “local election petition” insert—<br>> - “referendum petition” means a petition under the 2007 Regulations;”<br>.<br>In the definition of “petition” insert at the end “or a referendum petition”.<br>For the definition of “constituency” substitute—<br>> - “constituency” in relation to—<br>>   - (a) a local election petition, means the local government area to which the petition relates;<br>>   - (b) a referendum petition, means the local government area in which the referendum is held;<br>.<br>After the definition of “returning officer” insert “and, in relation to a referendum petition, any reference to a provision of the Act must be construed as a reference to that provision as applied by the 2007 Regulations.” |
| Rule 2(3)[^f00152] | After “local government Act” insert “and referendums under the 2007 Regulations”. |
| Rule 4(1)[^f00153] | Omit sub-paragraph (a).<br>For sub-paragraph (b) substitute—<br>> - (b) the date and result of the referendum to which the petition relates;<br>.<br>In sub-paragraph (c) for the words from “petition” to “the Act” substitute “referendum petition”. |
| Rule 10[^f00154] | Omit. |
| Rule 12(3)[^f00155] | For “the election” substitute “the referendum”. |
| Rule 14(2)[^f00156] | For “the election may” substitute “the referendum may”. |
| Rule 16(3)[^f00157] | For “the election may” substitute “the referendum may”. |
| Rule 18[^f00158] | After “local election petition” insert “or a referendum petition”. |
| The Schedule[^f00159] | For “a Parliamentary (or Local Government) Election for (*state place*) .. .. .. ..” substitute “a referendum in (*state area*)”.<br>In paragraph 1—<br>- (a) for “election”, in the first place where that word occurs, substitute “referendum”,<br>- (b) omit “(or was a candidate at the above election) (or in the case of a parliamentary election claims to have had a right to be elected or returned at the above election)”.<br>For paragraph 2 substitute—<br>> **2.** That referendum was held on the \[*insert day*\] day of \[*insert month*\] \[*insert year*\]<br>.<br>In paragraph 4 omit “in the case of a petition mentioned in section 122(2) or (3) or”.<br>For paragraph (1) of the Prayer substitute—<br>> (1) That it may be determined \[that the result of the referendum was not in accordance with the votes cast\]\* \[that the referendum is avoided\]\* \[that the referendum is tainted as mentioned in regulation 17(3) of the 2007 Regulations\]\*.<br>*\* Include or omit as the circumstances require.*” |
