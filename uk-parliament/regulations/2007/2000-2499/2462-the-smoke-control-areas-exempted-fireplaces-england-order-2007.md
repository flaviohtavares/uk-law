# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2462

clean air, england

The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order 2007

Made

20th August 2007

Laid before Parliament

24th August 2007

Coming into force

1st October 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21 of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is satisfied that the fireplaces exempted by this Order can be used for burning fuel other than authorised fuels[^f00002] without producing any smoke or substantial quantity of smoke.

## 1. Citation, application and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order 2007; it applies in England and comes into force on 1st October 2007.

## 2. Fireplaces exempted from section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993

The fireplaces in the Schedule are exempt from section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (prohibition of smoke emissions in smoke control area), subject to the conditions in the Schedule.

## 3. Revocation

The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2006[^f00003] is revoked.

Phil Woolas

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

20th August 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces with amendments the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2006. It exempts the fireplaces in the Schedule from the provisions of section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (prohibition of smoke emissions in smoke control area), subject to the conditions in the Schedule. A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
