# The Income Tax (Qualifying Child Care) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2478

income tax

The Income Tax (Qualifying Child Care) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

Made

30th August 2007

Laid before the House of Commons

3rd September 2007

Coming into force

1st October 2007

The Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 318D(2) of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Qualifying Child Care) (No. 2) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st October 2007.

## 2. Amendment of section 318C(2) of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003

In section 318C(2) of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003 (qualifying childcare in England)[^f00002] omit paragraph (d).

Steve McCabe

Claire Ward

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

30th August 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend section 318C(2) of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act [2003 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/1) in consequence of the revocation, in relation to England, of the Tax Credit (New Category of Child Care Provider) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/3110](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/3110)) and the consequential amendment of regulation 14 of the Working Tax Credit (Entitlement and Maximum Rate) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2005)).

An impact assessment has not been prepared in respect of this instrument as it is not expected that it will have any impact on business or the voluntary sector.
