# The Licensing Act 2003 (Amendment of Schedule 4) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2075

licenCEs and licensing

The Licensing Act 2003 (Amendment of Schedule 4) Order 2007

Made

18th July 2007

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2007

Coming into force

11th August 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 113(2) of the Licensing Act 2003[^f00001] (“the Act”):

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 2003 (Amendment of Schedule 4) Order 2007 and comes into force on 11th August 2007.

(2) In this Order “the amending provision” means paragraph 20(4) of Schedule 16 to the Gambling Act 2005[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment

Immediately after the amending provision comes into force, the second of the two paragraphs numbered 21 in Schedule 4 to the Act (which paragraph refers to the Fraud Act 2006[^f00003]) is renumbered as paragraph 22.

Gerry Sutcliffe

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

18th July 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes the effect of a paragraph numbering error in paragraph 34 of Schedule 1 to the Fraud Act [2006 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/35).

Paragraph 34 inserted, with effect from 15th January 2007, a new paragraph 21 into Schedule 4 (Personal licence: relevant offences) to the Licensing Act [2003 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/17). However, paragraph 20(4) of Schedule 16 to the Gambling Act [2005 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/19), which is intended to come into force on 1st September 2007, will from that date insert a different paragraph into Schedule 4, also numbered 21.

The effect of this Order is to renumber the latter of the two Schedule 4 paragraphs (the paragraph inserted by the Fraud Act) as 22 rather than 21.
