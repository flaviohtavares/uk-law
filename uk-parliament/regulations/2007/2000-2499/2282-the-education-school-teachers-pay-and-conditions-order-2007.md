# The Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2282

education, england and wales

The Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 2007

Made

31st July 2007

Laid before Parliament

8th August 2007

Coming into force

1st September 2007

Following the reference to them of certain matters by the Secretary of State under section 120(1) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“the Act”), the School Teachers’ Review Body[^f00002] (“the STRB”) have, after following the procedure set out in section 121 of the Act, reported to the Prime Minister and to the Secretary of State in accordance with section 120(3) of the Act[^f00003], and the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State have arranged for publication of their report under section 120(4) of the Act[^f00004].

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families, having considered the STRB’s report in accordance with section 125(1)(b) of the Act, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1), 123 and 124 of the Act. The document to which this Order refers also makes subsidiary provision about matters which have not been referred to the STRB under section 120 of the Act, in reliance upon section 125(2)(a) of the Act.

In accordance with section 126 of the Act, the Secretary of State has consulted such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governing bodies of schools and such bodies representing the interests of teachers as appeared to him to be appropriate having regard to the contents of this Order.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 2007 and comes into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2007.

(2) In this Order, “the Document” means the document entitled “School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Document 2007 and Guidance on School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions” published by The Stationery Office Limited[^f00005].

## 2. Application

This Order applies to school teachers in England and Wales.

## 3. The Document

For the purposes of making provision for the determination of the remuneration of school teachers and other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties and working time, the provisions set out in section 2 of the Document have effect from 1<sup>st</sup> September 2007.

## 4. Revocations

The Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) (No.2) Order 2006[^f00006], the Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2006[^f00007] and the Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) (No.2) (Amendment) Order 2007[^f00008] are revoked.

Kevin Brennan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under the Education Act 2002 (“the Act”). It makes provision for the determination of the remuneration of school teachers (within the meaning of section 122 of the Act) in England and Wales and other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties and working time.

The Order makes this provision by reference to section 2 of a document entitled “School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Document 2007 and Guidance on School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions” (“the Document”), which is published by The Stationery Office Limited and is obtainable from Government book shops and from booksellers. It can also be found on the internet at www.teachernet.gov.uk/pay.

The Order provides that the provisions set out in section 2 of the Document have effect from 1<sup>st</sup> September 2007.

The Order revokes the Order that made such provision for school teachers by reference to section 2 of the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Document 2006 and Guidance on School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions and subsequent Orders that amended those provisions.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
