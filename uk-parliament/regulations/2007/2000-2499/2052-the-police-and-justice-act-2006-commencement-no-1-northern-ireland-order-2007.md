# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2052 (C. 77)

police, northern ireland

The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007

Made

17th July 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001].

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

(2) This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

**2.** Section 13 (supply of information to police etc by Registrar General) of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force in Northern Ireland on 30th July 2007.

Paul Goggins

One of her Majesty’s Ministers of State

Northern Ireland Office

17 July 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in Northern Ireland section 13 of the Police and Justice Act 2006 on 30th July 2007.
