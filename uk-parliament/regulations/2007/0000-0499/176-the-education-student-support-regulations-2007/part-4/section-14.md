# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Requirement to enter a contract for a loan for fees

To receive a loan for fees payable under this Part, an eligible student must enter into a contract with the Secretary of State.
