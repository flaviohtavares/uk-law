# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 31

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Amount of the grant for fees for a course at a private institution[^c-3d0958b5d6c1570fd769923ed6b4ad54]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a private institution where regulation 30 does not apply is the lesser of—

- (a) £1,150; and
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year.

(2) In the case of a designated course at the University of Buckingham, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £2,905.
