# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Application of contribution – current system students

In the case of a current system student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 95 until it is extinguished.
