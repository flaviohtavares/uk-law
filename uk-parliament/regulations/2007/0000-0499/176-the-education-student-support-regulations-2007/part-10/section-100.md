# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. General

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) —

- (a) fee loans and fee contribution loans are payable in accordance with regulation 103;
- (b) college fee loans are payable in accordance with regulation 104;
- (c) loans for living costs are payable in accordance with regulation 105;

(2) Regulations 101 and 102 apply in respect of the payment of all loans.
