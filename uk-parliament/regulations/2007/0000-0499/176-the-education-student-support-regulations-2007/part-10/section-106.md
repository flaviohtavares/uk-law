# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 106

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 106. Overpayments of fee support[^c-13cac60a86931ff00c74534d9bb85a0b]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Any overpayment of fee support is recoverable by the Secretary of State from the academic authority.
