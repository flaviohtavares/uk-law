# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Overpayments of college fee loan

Any overpayment of college fee loan is recoverable by the Secretary of State from the college or permanent private hall.
