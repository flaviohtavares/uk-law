# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 1st March 2007.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), these Regulations apply in relation to England[^f00002].

(3) Regulation 91(1) also applies in relation to Wales.

(4) Regulation 91(2) extends to Northern Ireland.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: In relation to Wales, the functions of the Secretary of State under section 22 of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998 were transferred to the National Assembly for Wales under section 44 of the Higher Education Act 2004 except so far as they relate to the making of any provision authorised by subsection (2)(a), (c), (j) or (k), (3)(e) or (f) or (5) of section 22.
