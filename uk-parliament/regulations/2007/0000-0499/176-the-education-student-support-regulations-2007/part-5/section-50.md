# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Amount of the grant for travel – courses in medicine and dentistry

The amount of grant payable under regulation 49 in respect of an academic year is equal to the reasonable expenditure that the Secretary of State determines the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purposes set out in that regulation less £290.
