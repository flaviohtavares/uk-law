# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 36

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Current system students[^c-26d2600cb5f73efff015d3345646ff86]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The following grants are available to a current system student in connection with a designated course if he meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part—

- (a) disabled students’ allowance;
- (b) grant for dependants;
- (c) grant for travel;
- (d) maintenance grant or special support grant.
