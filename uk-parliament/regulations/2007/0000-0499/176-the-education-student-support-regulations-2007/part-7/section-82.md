# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 82

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Disabled students[^c-8935549561eee5be86929b60f8ee5ad5]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A disabled student who is undertaking a qualifying course in the United Kingdom but who is not in attendance because he is unable to attend for a reason which relates to his disability is treated as if he were in attendance on the qualifying course for the purpose of qualifying for the college fee loan.
