# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. General

(1) The maximum amount of a loan for living costs in respect of an academic year is calculated as follows—

- (a) where the eligible student is a current system student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 65 or 66;
- (b) where the eligible student is an old system student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 67;
- (c) where the eligible student is a student with reduced entitlement, in accordance with regulation 68.
