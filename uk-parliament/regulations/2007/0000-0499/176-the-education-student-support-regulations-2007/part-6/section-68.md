# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Students with reduced entitlement

(1) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year of a course other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 38(3)(a)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,660;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £3,100;
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £2,210.
- (b) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 38(3)(b) or (5)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,660;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £3,100;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £2,645;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £2,210;
- (c) where the student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/176)where—

  *X* is—

  - (i) for a student in category A, £2,620;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £4,735;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £4,030;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £3,385;

  *Y* is the amount specified in paragraph (d);
- (d) the specified amount is—
  - (i) £615 where the student is a type 1 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £615;
  - (ii) £1,230 where the student is a type 2 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £1,230;
  - (iii) nil where the student is not a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student.

(2) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 38(3)(a)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,260;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £2,375;
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £1,720;
- (b) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 38(3)(b) or (5)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,260;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £2,375;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £1,930;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £1,720;
- (c) where the student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/176)where—

  *X* is—

  - (i) for a student in category A, £2,365;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £4,315;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £3,505;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £3,130;

  *Y* is the amount specified in paragraph (d);
- (d) the specified amount is—
  - (i) £615 where the student is a type 1 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £615;
  - (ii) £1,230 where the student is a type 2 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £1,230;
  - (iii) nil where the student is not a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student.
