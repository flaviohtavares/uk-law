# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Current system students with full entitlement

(1) This regulation applies to a current system student with full entitlement (other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil).

(2) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/176)where—

- *X* is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,495;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £6,315;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £5,375;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £4,510;
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount.

(3) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/176)where—

- *X* is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,155;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £5,750;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £4,675;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £4,175;
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount.

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” is—

- (a) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant not exceeding £1,230, the amount of maintenance grant payable;
- (b) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,230, £1,230; and
- (c) where no maintenance grant is payable, nil.
