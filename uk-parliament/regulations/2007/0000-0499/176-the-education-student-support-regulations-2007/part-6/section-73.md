# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 73

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Students who are treated as in attendance on a course[^c-1d574f33b79816c7753eeefe110444cd]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as if he were in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for the loan for living costs.

(2) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a compressed degree student;
- (b) a disabled student who—
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and
  - (ii) is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because he is unable to attend for a reason which relates to his disability.
