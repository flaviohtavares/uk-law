# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Old system students with full entitlement

(1) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,495;
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,315;
- (c) for a student in category C, £5,375;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,510.

(2) Subject to Chapter 3 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,155;
- (b) for a student in category B, £5,750;
- (c) for a student in category C, £4,675;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,175.
