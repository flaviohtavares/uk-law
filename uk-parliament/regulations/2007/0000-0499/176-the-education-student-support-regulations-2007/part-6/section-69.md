# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 69

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Quarters in respect of which the loan for living costs is payable[^c-aea613c3474f8d5016e63d67768fe50f]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to regulation 72, the loan for living costs is payable in respect of three quarters of the academic year.

(2) The loan for living costs is not payable—

- (a) in the case of a compressed degree student, in respect of the quarter nominated by the Secretary of State;
- (b) in any other case, in respect of the quarter in which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, the longest of any vacation occurs.
