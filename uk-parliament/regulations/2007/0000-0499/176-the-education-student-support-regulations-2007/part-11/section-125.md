# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2007, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Payment of grants for fees

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the Secretary of State must pay the grant in respect of fees for which the student qualifies to the appropriate academic authority after a valid request for payment has been received.

(2) The Secretary of State may make payments under paragraph (1) at such times and in such instalments as he sees fit.

(3) The Secretary of State may make provisional payments under paragraph (1) in such cases as he deems appropriate.
