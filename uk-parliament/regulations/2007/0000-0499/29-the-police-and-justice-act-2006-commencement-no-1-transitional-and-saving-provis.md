# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1, Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2006/3364](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3364) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

2007 No. 29 (C. 1)

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND and WALES

The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1, Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

10th January 2007

Coming into force

15th January 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1, Transitional and Saving Provisions)(Amendment) Order 2007 and comes into force on 15th January 2007.

**2.** In sub-paragraph (k) of article 2 of the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2006[^f00002] for “3, 4 and 61” substitute “3, 4, 9, 10, 61 and 62”.

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

10th January 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 15th January 2007, amends the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No.1, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2006 to provide for the commencement on 15th January 2007 of the additional consequential amendments set out in paragraphs 9, 10 and 62 of Schedule 14 to the Police and Justice Act 2006.
