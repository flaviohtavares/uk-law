# The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 92 (C. 4)

Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2007

Made

17th January 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(3)(a) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 4 (remuneration of members of the Northern Ireland Assembly) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 shall come into force on 26th January 2007.

Peter Hain

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

17th January 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 4 of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 on 26th January 2007.
