# The NHS Direct National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 478

National HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The NHS Direct National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2007

Made

20th February 2007

Coming into force

1st April 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3 of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health has completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the NHS Direct National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 2006 Act” means the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00003];
- “health service professional” means any person who is regulated by any of the regulatory bodies mentioned in section 25(3) of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (bodies within the remit of Council for the Regulation of Health Care Professionals)[^f00004];
- “health service provider” means any person (other than an NHS body or health service professional), providing pharmaceutical services, primary dental services, primary medical services or primary or general ophthalmic services;
- “general ophthalmic services” means services which are general ophthalmic services for the purposes of Part II of National Health Service Act 1977[^f00005];
- “NHS Bodies” means Strategic Health Authorities, Special Health Authorities, Primary Care Trusts, NHS trusts and NHS foundation trusts within the meaning of Part 2 of the 2006 Act;
- “operational date” has the meaning assigned to it in paragraph 5(5) of Schedule 4 to the 2006 Act;
- “pharmaceutical services” means services provided-
  - (a) under section 126 of the 2006 Act;
  - (b) in accordance with a direction under section 127 of the 2006 Act; or
  - (c) local pharmaceutical services or piloted services within the meaning of section 134(7) of the 2006 Act;
- “primary dental services” means services which are primary dental services for the purposes of Part 5 of the 2006 Act;
- “primary medical services” means services which are primary medical services for the purposes of Part 4 of the 2006 Act;
- “primary ophthalmic services” means services which are primary ophthalmic services for the purposes of Part 6 of the 2006 Act;
- “statutory health bodies” means any body (other than an NHS body or health service provider), established by or under an enactment and —
  - (a) providing services in connection with the provision of, or
  - (b) exercising functions in relation to,

  the health service in England; and
- “the trust” means NHS Direct National Health Service Trust established by article 2 of this Order.

## 2. Establishment and name of the trust

There is established a National Health Service trust which shall be called NHS Direct National Health Service Trust.

## 3. Nature and functions of the trust

(1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 25(1) of the 2006 Act.

(2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services for the purposes of the health service and, in particular, to provide—

- (a) health related information to the public, NHS bodies, health service professionals, health service providers and statutory health bodies;
- (b) clinical advice to the public in relation to persons with symptomatic conditions; and
- (c) such other services as can reasonably be carried out in conjunction with the provision of the services specified in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b).

## 4. Directors of the trust

The trust shall have 8 non-executive directors (including the chairman), and 5 executive directors.

## 5. Operational date and accounting date of the trust

(1) The operational date of the trust shall be 1st April 2007.

(2) The accounting date of the trust shall be 31st March.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Andy Burnham

Minister of State,

20th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes NHS Direct National Health Service Trust as a National Health Service trust under the terms set out in section 25 of the National Health Service Act 2006.

The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3), the number of non-executive and executive directors of the trust (article 4) the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions) and the accounting date of the trust (article 5).

This Order should be read in conjunction with the NHS Direct Special Health Authority Abolition Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/504](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/504)).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies.
