# The Colours in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 453

food, england

The Colours in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007

Made

14th February 2007

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2007

Coming into force

6th April 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1) (a), 17(1), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and now vested in her[^f00002].

In accordance with section 48 (4A) of that Act, she has had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the following Regulations.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Colours in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007, apply in relation to England only and come into force on 6th April 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

(1) The Colours in Food Regulations 1995[^f00004] are amended in so far as they apply in relation to England in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” there is substituted the following definition —

> - “**Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)**” means Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) laying down specific criteria of purity concerning colours for use in foodstuffs[^f00005] as amended by Directive [1999/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0075)[^f00006], Directive [2001/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0050)[^f00007], Directive [2004/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0047)[^f00008] and Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033)[^f00009];

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations apply in relation to England only. They implement in relation to England Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amending Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) as regards sunset yellow FCF (E110) and titanium dioxide (E171) (OJ No. L82, 21.3.2006, p.10).

**2.** They do so by updating the definition of “**Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)**” (Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) laying down specific criteria of purity concerning colours for use in foodstuffs, OJ No. L226, 29.9.1995, p.1) that appears in regulation 2(1) of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3124), as already amended), which extend to the whole of Great Britain, to reflect the amendments made to that Directive by Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) *(regulation 2)*.

**3.** Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amends Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) by substituting revised purity criteria for the colours sunset yellow FCF (E110) and titanium dioxide (E171).

**4.** A full Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.

**5.** A Transposition Note has been prepared and placed in the Library of each House of Parliament, setting out how the main elements of the European legislation referred to above are transposed in these Regulations. Copies of this document may be obtained from the Novel Foods, Additives and Supplements Division of the Food Standards Agency, Aviation House, 125 Kingsway, London WC2B 6NH.
