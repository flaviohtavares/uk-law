# The Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges) (Amendment)(England) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 384

HOUSING, ENGLAND

The Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges) (Amendment)(England) Order 2007

Made

13th February 2007

Laid before Parliament

20th February 2007

Coming into force

26th March 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 16D of Part 3 of Schedule 6 to the Housing Act 1985[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges)(Amendment)(England) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 26<sup>th</sup> March 2007.

(2) This Order applies in England only.

## 2. Amendment of the Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges) Order 1986

In Article 2 (interpretation) of the Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges) Order 1986[^f00002], for the definition of “index figure” substitute―

> “index figure” means an index figure, other than a provisional index figure, in the table of “Output Deflators for Direct Labour: Public Housing Repairs and Maintenance” in the “Quarterly building price and cost indices: public sector construction works” published by the Department for Trade and Industry,

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Yvette Cooper

Minister of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

13th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Housing (Right to Buy)(Service Charges) Order 1986 (“the principal Order”) which sets out the calculation for the inflation allowance to be added to a landlord’s estimate of the service charges payable by the tenant for repairs and major works in the initial period of a right to buy lease.

Article 2 of this Order amends the definition of “index figure” in Article 2 of the principal Order. This is necessary to reflect the fact that the relevant index is now published by the Department of Trade and Industry under the name “Output Deflators for Direct Labour: Public Housing Repairs and Maintenance” in the “Quarterly building price and cost indices: public sector construction works”.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the cost of businesses, charities or voluntary bodies.
