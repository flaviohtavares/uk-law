# The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 74 (C.3)

criminal law, england and wales

criminal law, northern ireland

The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2007

Made

15th January 2007

The Secretary of State being the relevant national authority under section 66(3)(b) makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 66(2) of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2007.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 shall come into force on 12th February 2007—

- (a) section 42 (increase of maximum sentences for offences of having knives etc.);
- (b) section 54 and Schedule 4 (sexual offences: forfeiture and detention of vehicles etc.);
- (c) section 55 (continuity of sexual offences law); and
- (d) section 57 (amendment of section 82 of the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00002]).

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

15th January 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 42, 54, 55 and 57 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 on 12th February 2007.
