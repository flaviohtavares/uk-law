# The Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 308

northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

*6*th February 2007

Coming into force

7th February 2007

In accordance with section 7(2)(h) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00001], the Electoral Commission has been consulted prior to making this Order.

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with section 96(2)(b) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00002] and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(4) and (6) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00003], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections)(Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

**2.** (1) The left-hand column of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) Order 2001[^f00004] (application with modifications of statutory provisions relating to elections) is amended as follows.

(2) For the entries in respect of section 90A (meaning of election expenses) and section 90B (incurring of election expenses for the purpose of section 90A) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00005], substitute “Section 90ZA (meaning of “election expenses”)”[^f00006].

(3) After the entry in respect of Schedule 3 to the Representation of the People Act 1983 insert “SCHEDULE 4A (ELECTION EXPENSES)”[^f00007].

David Hanson

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

6th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) Order 2001([S.I.2001/2599](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2599))(“2001 Order”) which applies provisions of the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c.2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2) to elections to the Northern Ireland Assembly with the general modifications in article 3(3) and the specific modifications in Schedule 1 to the Order . This Order amends the 2001 Order to apply the provisions of the Representation of the People Act 1983 relating to election expenses which were inserted by the Electoral Administration Act [2006 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/22) to elections to the Northern Ireland Assembly.
