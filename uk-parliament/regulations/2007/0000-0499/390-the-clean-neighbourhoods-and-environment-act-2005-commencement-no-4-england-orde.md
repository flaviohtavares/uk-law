# The Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No.4) (England) Order 2007

> Text as at 2007-02-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2007 No. 390 (C. 15)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND

The Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No.4) (England) Order 2007

Made

13th February 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 108(1)(a) of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) (England) Order 2007.

(2) In this Order “the 2005 Act” means the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005.

(3) This Order applies in England.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The following provisions of the 2005 Act shall come into force on 6th March 2007—

- (a) paragraphs 5 to 9 of Schedule 4 (making minor amendments to the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00002]);
- (b) paragraphs 14 to 19 of Schedule 4 (making minor amendments to the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003[^f00003]);
- (c) Part 2 of Schedule 5 to the extent it is not already in force (repealing certain provisions of the Environmental Protection Act 1990); and
- (d) Part 9 of Schedule 5 to the extent it is not already in force.

Ben Bradshaw

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

13th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force various provisions of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 which make some minor amendments to the Environmental Protection Act 1990 and the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003. It also brings into force Parts 2 and 9 of Schedule 5 of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 to the extent they are not already in force which repeal certain provisions of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 and the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003.
