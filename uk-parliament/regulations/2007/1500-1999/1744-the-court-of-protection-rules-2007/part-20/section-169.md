# The Court of Protection Rules 2007, section 169

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 169. Scope of this Part

This Part applies to an appeal against any decision of the court except where, in relation to those cases that are to be dealt with in accordance with Part 22 (transitory and transitional provisions), Part 22 makes different provision.
