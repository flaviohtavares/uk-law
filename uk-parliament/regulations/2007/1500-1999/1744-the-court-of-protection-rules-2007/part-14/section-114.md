# The Court of Protection Rules 2007, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 114. Interpretation

In this Section—

- (a) “**Regulation State**” has the same meaning as “**Member State**” in the Taking of Evidence Regulation, that is all Member States except Denmark; and
- (b) “**the Taking of Evidence Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1206/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1206)[^f00013] of 28 May 2001 on co-operation between the courts of Member States in the taking of evidence in civil and commercial matters.
