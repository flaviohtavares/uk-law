# The Court of Protection Rules 2007, section 151

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 151. LSC funded clients

(1) Where the certificate of any person (“**A**”) who is an LSC funded client is revoked or discharged—

- (a) the solicitor who acted for A will cease to be the solicitor acting in the case as soon as his retainer is determined under regulation 4 of the Community Legal Services (Costs) Regulations 2000[^f00014]; and
- (b) if A wishes to continue and appoints a solicitor to act on his behalf, rule 150(2), (3) and (4) will apply as if A had previously conducted the application in person.

(2) In this rule, “**certificate**” means a certificate issued under the Funding Code (approved under section 9 of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00015]).
