# The Court of Protection Rules 2007, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocations

The following rules are revoked—

- (a) the Court of Protection Rules 2001[^f00003]; and
- (b) the Court of Protection (Enduring Powers of Attorney) Rules 2001[^f00004].
