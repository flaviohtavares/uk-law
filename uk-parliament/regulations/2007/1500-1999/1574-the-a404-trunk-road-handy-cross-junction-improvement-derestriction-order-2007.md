# The A404 Trunk Road (Handy Cross Junction Improvement) (Derestriction) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2007 No. 1574

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A404 Trunk Road (Handy Cross Junction Improvement) (Derestriction) Order 2007

Made

24th May 2007

Coming into force

9th June 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 82(2) and 83(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], having consulted with such representative organisations as he thought fit, makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A404 Trunk Road (Handy Cross Junction Improvement) (Derestriction) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 9th June 2007.

**2.** The lengths of the Trunk Road specified in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be restricted roads for the purposes of section 81 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

**3.** In this Order:

- “the Trunk Road” means the A404 Trunk Road in the District of Wycombe in the County of Buckinghamshire.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P. C. Arnold

Highways Agency

Department for Transport

24th May 2007

## THE SCHEDULE

The length of the Trunk Road in the District of Wycombe in the County of Buckinghamshire to be a derestricted Road is:

- From the roundabout at the junction of the Trunk Road with the M40 Motorway to a point approximately 340 metres south of the roundabout, in both directions.
- The roundabout at the junction of the Trunk Road with the M40 Motorway and the link road across the roundabout.
