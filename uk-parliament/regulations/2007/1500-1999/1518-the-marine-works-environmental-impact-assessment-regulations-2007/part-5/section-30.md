> Text as at 2026-08-09.

## 30. Transitional and saving provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) These Regulations shall not apply in relation to an application made before 24th June 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The revocation made by regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 29 does not affect the application of the provisions referred to in that regulation in relation to an application made before 24th June 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
