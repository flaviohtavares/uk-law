# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 31

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 31. Review (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4317cce41bc44835d355ea8f6ae372b2][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The Secretary of State must, from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) carry out a review of the regulatory provision contained in these Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) publish a report setting out the conclusions of the review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The first report must be published before 16th May 2022. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Subsequent reports must be published at intervals not exceeding 5 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Section 30(3) of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 requires that a review carried out under this regulation must, so far as is reasonable, have regard to how the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive is implemented in other member States. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) Section 30(4) of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 requires that a report published under this regulation must, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by the regulatory provision referred to in paragraph (1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) if those objectives remain appropriate, assess the extent to which they could be achieved in another way which involves less onerous regulatory provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(6) In this regulation, “regulatory provision” has the same meaning as in sections 28 to 32 of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 (see section 32 of that Act). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
