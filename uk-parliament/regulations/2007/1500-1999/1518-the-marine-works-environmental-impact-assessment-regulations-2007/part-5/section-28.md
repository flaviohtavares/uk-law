## 28. Access to review procedure before a court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1)[^c-76d22eff9c78b9854aeffc99626c5122] In relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Scotland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], any non-governmental organisation promoting environmental protection and meeting any other requirements under the law shall be deemed to have an interest for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Article 11(1)(a)[^c-000bc5bbb6d33a7da9879ca64965f240] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] and rights capable of being impaired for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Article 11(1)(b)[^c-ffbfc8e3a3ba5569f5670de4f296f2ea] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].

(2)[^c-90c4cf4a437216eb1b893794de166f7b] For the purpose of paragraph (1), Article 11(1) of the EIA Directive is to be read as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) in the words before point (a), “Member States” were “Scottish Ministers”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
