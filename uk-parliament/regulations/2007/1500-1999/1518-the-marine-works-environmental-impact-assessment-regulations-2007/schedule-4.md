# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## SCHEDULE 4 (affected by repeal, see note) — SCOPING OPINIONS (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Regulation 13(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 1. Request for a scoping opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

A request for a scoping opinion must be accompanied by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a chart, plan or map sufficient to identify the location of the regulated activity and of other activities to be carried out in the course of the project; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b)[^c-46a118996765bb8cf3cc9fade458d529] a brief description of the specific characteristics of the regulated activity and the project, including their nature, purpose, location and technical capacity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (ba (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ddc1bf4a29c3a8f08a393c786728d7f1][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] an explanation of the likely significant effects of the regulated activity and the project on the environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) such other information or representations as the applicant may wish to provide or make. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 2. Information required where another application has been made (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

Where the regulated activity is to be carried out in the course of a project in respect of which the applicant has made an application to a consenting authority other than the regulator, an applicant seeking a scoping opinion must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) inform the appropriate authority and the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) of any such application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if any such consenting authority has requested an environmental statement in respect of that project, inform the appropriate authority and the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) of that request; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) if so requested by the appropriate authority or the regulator, provide the appropriate authority with a copy of any environmental statement and of any other environmental information provided to any such consenting authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 3. Payment of a fee for a scoping opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority may require an applicant to pay a reasonable fee in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the administrative expenses of providing a scoping opinion; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the cost of carrying out any examinations or tests that, in the opinion of the appropriate authority, are necessary or expedient to enable the appropriate authority to produce its scoping opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) If the appropriate authority considers that it is appropriate to do so, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) require the applicant to make a reasonable advance payment against the fee that it is entitled to charge for its scoping opinion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) determine the balance of the fee payable after carrying out the work necessary to produce its scoping opinion in accordance with the remaining provisions of this Schedule; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) require the applicant to pay the balance of the fee that it is entitled to charge prior to the notification of its scoping opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 4. Procedure for reaching a scoping opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority must, if it considers that it has not been provided with sufficient information to enable it to give a scoping opinion, notify the applicant in writing of the matters on which it requires further information and the applicant must supply that further information to the appropriate authority within such period as the appropriate authority may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The applicant must supply the appropriate authority with such number of additional copies of the documentation as the appropriate authority may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The appropriate authority need not deal further with the request for a scoping opinion until the applicant has complied with the requirements of sub-paragraphs (1) and (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Where an applicant has failed to comply with the requirements of sub-paragraph (1) or (2) within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified, or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the appropriate authority may treat the request as having been withdrawn; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the regulator may treat the application to which the request relates as having been withdrawn; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to treat the application as withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 5. Matters to be considered in reaching a scoping opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

In reaching a scoping opinion, the appropriate authority must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] take into account[^c-39473985b5dc7e14e18f617282d59645] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]—

- (a) the specific characteristics of the project *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], including its location and technical capacity[^c-17c300515f7b6c8ea4e9d7236b9329fc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483];
- (b) the nature and purpose of regulated activities of the type concerned in the project; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the environmental features likely to be affected by the project; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d)[^c-17695ead1710734b8a7f8a7e44b89859] any information provided by the applicant about the project and the regulated activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 6. Consultation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority must consult such of the consultation bodies as it considers appropriate before giving a scoping opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) When carrying out any consultation under sub-paragraph (1), the appropriate authority must allow the consultation body a reasonable period within which to respond and that period must not be less than 28 days from the date of the letter that the consultation body receives from the appropriate authority or such other period as may be agreed between the consultation body and the appropriate authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 7. Notification of a scoping opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

The appropriate authority must, as soon as reasonably practicable, provide its scoping opinion and a written statement of the reasons for its opinion to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if the appropriate authority is not also the regulator, the regulator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) such of the consultation bodies as it consulted in accordance with paragraph 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 8. Availability of scoping opinions for inspection (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1)[^c-efbd68f3623b360301a1c9adcecbe9f3] Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the appropriate authority must ensure that, as soon as possible after being sent to the applicant, its scoping opinion is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) publicised in such a manner as it considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in the case of an activity requiring regulatory approval under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 1985 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or the 2009 Act[^c-18d8169e68f4f2239ac898775d73d177] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], made available on the relevant Public Register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not require disclosure of any excluded information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
