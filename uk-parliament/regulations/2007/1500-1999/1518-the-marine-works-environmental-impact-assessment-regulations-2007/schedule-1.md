# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Matters relevant to consideration of whether or not a Schedule A2 project is likely to have significant effects on the environment (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-70579a283b700a740efee94d3f144f1a][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Regulation 8(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 1. Characteristics of the project (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

The characteristics of the project, with particular regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the size and design of the whole project; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) cumulation with other existing or approved projects ; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the use of natural resources, in particular land, soil, water and biodiversity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) the production of waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) pollution and nuisances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) the risk of major accidents or disasters which are relevant to the project concerned, including those caused by climate change, in accordance with scientific knowledge; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) the risks to human health (for example due to water contamination or air pollution). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 2. Location of the project (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

The environmental sensitivity of geographical areas likely to be affected by the project, with particular regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the existing and approved land use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the relative abundance, availability, quality and regenerative capacity of natural resources (including soil, land, water and biodiversity) in the area and below ground in that area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the absorption capacity of the natural environment, paying particular attention to the following areas— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) wetlands, riparian areas, river mouths; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] coastal zones and the marine environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] mountain and forest areas; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] nature reserves and parks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (v) areas classified or protected under national legislation, Natura 2000 areas designated or classified by member States pursuant to the Habitats Directive or the Wild Birds Directive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] areas in which there has already been a failure to meet the environmental quality standards laid down in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] legislation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] as it applied in the United Kingdom immediately prior to exit day, or in[^key-465e70e40f568040999ebe1ce45816c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] assimilated[^key-ca37cb6ce3af55ab65f6c6687a3d51bf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] law *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] and relevant to the project, or in which it is considered that there is such a failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (vii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] densely populated areas; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (viii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] landscapes and sites of historical, cultural or archaeological significance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 3. Types and characteristics of the potential impact (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

The likely significant effects of projects on the environment in relation to the matters set out in paragraphs 1 and 2, with regard to the impact of the project on the factors specified in regulation 21A(2)(a) to (e) and with regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the magnitude and spatial extent of the impact (for example the geographical area and the size of the population likely to be affected); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the nature of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the transboundary nature of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) the intensity and complexity of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) the probability of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) the expected onset, duration, frequency and reversibility of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) the cumulation of the impact with the impact of other existing or approved projects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (h) the possibility of effectively reducing the impact. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
