# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 13

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 13. Scoping opinions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The applicant may request a scoping opinion from the appropriate authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The procedures for requesting and giving scoping opinions are set out in Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Where a scoping opinion is given— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the appropriate authority must not deliver its environmental impact assessment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

unless the applicant has submitted an environmental statement containing all of the information specified in the scoping opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
