## 24. Effect of (affected by repeal, see note) EIA consent decision (affected by repeal, see note) on application and regulatory decision (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1)[^c-4f2e41f50f48162ab8c52e3001db2af3] Where the appropriate authority has given *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent in respect of a regulated activity, the regulator may proceed to deal with the application and take its regulatory decision in accordance with the relevant legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1A)[^c-4ae986c3f6135b417dcfc6d762ccd136] When the regulator proceeds to deal with the application and take its regulatory decision in accordance with paragraph (1), the regulator must have regard to the written confirmation of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision and in particular to any conditions, including any mitigating or monitoring measures, attached to that decision pursuant to regulation 22(1)(c) to (e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1B) Where the regulator decides to grant regulatory approval, the regulator must incorporate in the regulatory decision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the conclusion reached by the appropriate authority in accordance with regulation 21A(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) any conditions, including any mitigating or monitoring measures, attached to the regulatory decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a statement including— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) the main reasons and considerations on which the regulatory decision is based including, if relevant, information about the participation of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a summary of the results of the consultations undertaken, and information gathered, in respect of the application and how those results (in particular, in circumstances where regulation 18 applies, the comments received from an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State pursuant to consultation under that regulation) have been incorporated or otherwise addressed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1C) Where the regulator decides to refuse regulatory approval, the regulator must state the main reasons for the refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1D) The regulator must be satisfied that the information incorporated in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision, under regulation 23(2), is up to date at the time that the regulatory decision is taken, but that information must be taken to be up to date if, in the opinion of the regulator, it addresses the significant effects that are likely to arise as a result of the project proposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1E) The regulatory decision must be taken within a period of time which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) is reasonable, taking into account the nature and complexity of the regulated activity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) begins with the date on which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision is published. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Where the appropriate authority has refused *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA consent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in respect of a regulated activity, the regulator may not grant a regulatory approval for that regulated activity and must treat the application for that regulated activity as having been withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
