# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 15

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 15. Availability of information held by regulator (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The regulator and the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may make available to the applicant any information in their possession which may be relevant to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the preparation of the environmental statement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the provision of the further information required in accordance with regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 14(1).

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), the regulator and the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) must make such information available if the applicant so requests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not require the disclosure of any excluded information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Where an applicant requests information under paragraph (2), the regulator or the appropriate authority (as the case may be) may impose, as a condition of providing the information, a reasonable charge reflecting the cost of identifying, preparing and copying the information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
