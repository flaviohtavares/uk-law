## 20. Consultation of (affected by repeal, see note) EEA (affected by repeal, see note) States (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

The appropriate authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) consult the authorities of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State to which information has been provided under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 18 [^key-4e00d7aa120fea413486ce7acabce6a1]...; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b)[^key-0402f70c5ef5809c48eb298e11a54b45] allow such reasonable period as may have been agreed with those authorities for them to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) ensure that the authorities and the public concerned in that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State are given an opportunity to forward their opinion on the information supplied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] make representations as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the possible significant effects of the regulated activity on the environment in that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the measures envisaged to reduce or eliminate such effects. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
