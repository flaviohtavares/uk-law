(2)[^c-13ce4bb6d63a9086441cf3fdfc1d06c2] The written confirmation must include the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a statement which includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a summary of the results of the consultations undertaken, and information gathered, in respect of the application and how those results (in particular, in circumstances where regulation 20 applies, the comments received from an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State pursuant to consultation under that regulation) have been incorporated or otherwise addressed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision is to grant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] any conditions attached to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision pursuant to regulation 22(1)(c) to (e), including any mitigating or monitoring measures; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision is to refuse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent, the main reasons for the refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The appropriate authority must, as soon as possible after written confirmation is sent to the applicant pursuant to paragraph (1), ensure that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) notice of its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision is published— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) on the appropriate authority’s website; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in those newspapers or other publications where the application was published under regulation 16(1)(b)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in such other manner (if any) as the appropriate authority considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) it promptly makes available for public inspection the information referred to in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4)[^c-d367e84fcdafe7041d53d7e284179d9b] In paragraph (3)(b), “public inspection” means: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
