## 18. Provision of information to affected (affected by repeal, see note) EEA (affected by repeal, see note) States (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a2dc2efed98a87835e8faeef9b2c661d][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The appropriate authority must as soon as possible comply with paragraph (2) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) it comes to the attention of the appropriate authority that a proposed project is the subject of an environmental impact assessment and is likely to have significant effects on the environment in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] an[^c-8d7b04bb8351c121e23fb650f25817d3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) an[^c-dd063ea52b636ce77baa41d66cd8272b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State likely to be significantly affected by the project so requests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The appropriate authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) send to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State the particulars mentioned in paragraph (3) and such of the information referred to in paragraph (5) as is relevant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) publish, or direct the applicant to publish, those particulars in a notice placed in the relevant Gazette indicating the address where additional information is available; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) give the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State a reasonable time in which to indicate whether it wishes to participate in the procedure for which these Regulations provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The particulars referred to in paragraph (2)(a) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a description of the project together with any available information on its possible significant effect on the environment in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] that[^c-d8f431e52c37d5c33e71242ea76b13a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) information on the nature of the decision which may be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The appropriate authority must send these particulars, and such of the information referred to in paragraph (5) as is relevant, to the authorities of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) as soon as possible; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in any event, no later than the date on which the particulars are published in the relevant Gazette. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) The information referred to in paragraph (2)(a) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a copy of the application for regulatory approval; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a copy of the environmental statement in respect of the project to which that application relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a copy of any further information provided by the applicant pursuant to a notification under regulation 14(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) any relevant information regarding the procedure under these Regulations including an explanation of the decisions that the regulator can make in relation to the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(6) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State indicates, in accordance with paragraph (2)(c), that it wishes to participate in the procedure for which these Regulations provide, the appropriate authority must, as soon as possible, send to that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State the following information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) any information provided by the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the results of any relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] environmental assessment which are reasonably available to the appropriate authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) such of the selection criteria set out in Schedule 1 as are relevant to the project. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(7) Paragraphs (2) and (6) do not require the disclosure of any excluded information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(8) Neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, until the period for consultation under regulation 20 has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(9) In this regulation, “the relevant Gazette” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) in relation to projects in the English inshore region or the English offshore region, the London Gazette; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in relation to projects in the Welsh inshore region or the Welsh offshore region, the London Gazette; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) in relation to projects in the Northern Ireland inshore region or the Northern Ireland offshore region, the Belfast Gazette; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) in relation to projects in the Scottish inshore region or the Scottish offshore region, the Edinburgh Gazette. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(10) In paragraph (9), the expressions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) English inshore region, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) English offshore region, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) Northern Ireland inshore region, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) Northern Ireland offshore region, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) Welsh inshore region, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) Welsh offshore region, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

have the meanings given by section 322 of the 2009 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
