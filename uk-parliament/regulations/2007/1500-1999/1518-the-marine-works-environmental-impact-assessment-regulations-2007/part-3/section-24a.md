# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 24A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 24A. Fees in relation to the assessment (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note) of the results of monitoring measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94aaede3f81df64e2b21961ef3d785bf][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) A decision to grant regulatory approval which includes a monitoring condition may include a fee condition of the kind described in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, a monitoring condition is a condition requiring any measure to be taken relating to monitoring of a kind referred to in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 23(2)(b)(ii)[^key-6e231f9592dc3e6acf018a321c4843d2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].

(3) A fee condition of the kind referred to in paragraph (1) is a condition as to the payment of a reasonable fee, determined in accordance with regulation 3(4) and (5), in respect of expenses incurred in assessing and interpreting the results of any monitoring measure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
