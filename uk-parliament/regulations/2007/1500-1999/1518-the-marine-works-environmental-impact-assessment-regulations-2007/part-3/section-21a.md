# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 21A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 21A. Conclusion about environmental impact (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bb0aa8cd5596eb93e279547d3a819000][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The appropriate authority must consider (ensuring that in doing so it has or has access to any expertise it considers necessary)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the environmental statement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) any further information provided by the applicant pursuant to a notification under regulation 14(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the outcome of the process set out in Schedule 5 in relation to any representations received pursuant to the statement referred to in regulation 16(2)(g); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) any representations in response to consultation made by the consultation bodies pursuant to the letter referred to in regulation 17(1)(a)(iv); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) the outcome of any consultation of the authorities of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^key-f6f00ddb064ea9d022c0a2a3107490ed]... EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] States carried out in accordance with regulation 20; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^key-15726166b2decbcac20f3101184c1cc5]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) any features of the project in relation to the regulated activity being carried out, or measures which the applicant proposes to take, which would have the effect of avoiding, preventing, reducing or offsetting any likely significant adverse environmental effects of the regulated activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] ; and[^key-e3a10cdaa1f0924b07bb0b7651927461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) in relation to a regulated activity for which an environmental impact assessment is required under regulation 8A, any other matter necessary to give effect to Article 31(1)(b) and (c) of the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Following the consideration required by paragraph (1), the appropriate authority must reach a conclusion about the likely significant effects of the project (including the expected effects deriving from the vulnerability of the project to risks of major accidents or disasters) on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) population and human health; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) biodiversity, with particular attention to species and habitats protected under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] any law of any part of the United Kingdom that implemented[^key-e83dc158891a664833f571c6968015c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the Habitats Directive and the Wild Birds Directive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) land, soil, water, air and climate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) material assets, cultural heritage and the landscape; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) the interaction between the factors referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) to (d). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
