# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 12

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 12. Application for a regulatory approval in relation to a regulated activity (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Where an application is made for a regulatory approval in relation to a regulated activity to which this Part applies, neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority may deal with the application or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to it until the appropriate authority has received the following material from the applicant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a chart or map (or both) sufficient to identify where the regulated activity would be carried out and the extent of any operations which it would involve; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a description (including a plan) of the nature of the project, identifying the regulated activity to be carried out in the course of that project; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a statement of the working methods to be used in the course of the project and in carrying out the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) an environmental statement in respect of the project; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) a copy of any environmental statement in respect of the project provided or to be provided to any other consenting authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) An environmental statement must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) be in writing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-c059fc9e48d7b42a6c8831f23d0635ea]...
- (b)[^c-de1fd470bd55ec6bb6ec958b589db6f1] include at least— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) a description of the project and the regulated activity, comprising information on the site, design, size and other relevant features of the project and the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a description of the likely significant effects of the project and the regulated activity on the environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a description of the features of the project and the regulated activity or the measures envisaged in order to avoid, prevent or reduce and, if possible, offset likely significant adverse effects on the environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a description of the reasonable alternatives studied by the applicant which are relevant to the project and its specific characteristics, and an indication of the main reasons for the option chosen, taking into account the effects of the project on the environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (v) a non-technical summary of the information referred to in paragraphs (i) to (iv); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] any additional information specified in Schedule 3 relevant to the specific characteristics of a particular project or type of project and to the environmental features likely to be affected; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c)[^c-95a8c38789ee4af924d3068917268d4d] where a scoping opinion has been given in accordance with regulation 13 and Schedule 4, be based on the most recent scoping opinion given (so far as the project remains materially the same as the project which was subject to that opinion); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) include the information reasonably required for reaching a conclusion about the significant effects of the project on the environment, taking into account current knowledge and methods of assessment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) (with a view to avoiding duplication of assessments), be prepared taking into account the results of any relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] environmental assessment which are reasonably available to the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) be prepared by competent persons; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) be accompanied by a statement from the applicant outlining the relevant experience or qualifications of such persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The appropriate authority may specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the format in which the applicant must provide the material referred to in paragraph (1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the number of copies of the material in that format that the applicant must provide to it and to the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3A)[^c-0faed7be7ab06f39fccb511a42a4715a] The appropriate authority must ensure that it has, or has access as necessary to, sufficient expertise to examine the environmental statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The applicant must comply with any reasonable requirement made in accordance with paragraph (3) and, until this has been done— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) Where an applicant has failed to comply with the requirements of paragraphs (1) and (2), or any requirements of the appropriate authority under paragraph (3), within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the regulator may treat the application as having been withdrawn; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
