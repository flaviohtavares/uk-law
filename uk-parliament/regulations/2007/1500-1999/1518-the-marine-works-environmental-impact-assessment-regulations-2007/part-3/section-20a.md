## 20A. Projects in (affected by repeal, see note) an (affected by repeal, see note) EEA (affected by repeal, see note) State likely to have significant transboundary effects (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-53fe0d8ec5095e165f8aa234e718cd4c][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-7cb2fb4d4e80a7fa93b1d71d5c03b06a][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Where an appropriate authority receives from an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^c-1846970912859ad816169143baaeeae1]... information which that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State has gathered in respect of a proposed project in that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State which is likely to have significant effects on the environment in the relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] area, the appropriate authority must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^c-dd6b2aeb1bf171dd4cdfb3d881f4251b]...—

- (a) enter into consultations with that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State regarding the potential transboundary effects of the proposed project on the environment and the measures envisaged to reduce or eliminate such effects; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) determine in agreement with that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State a reasonable period, before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent for the project is granted, during which members of the public may submit to the competent authority in that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State representations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^c-8c3300aa4130f730902823e407518392]....

(2) The appropriate authority must also— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) arrange for the information referred to in paragraph (1) to be made available within a reasonable time, and for a period of no fewer than 30 days, to the authorities which are likely to be concerned by the project by reason of their specific environmental responsibilities and to the public concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) ensure that those authorities and the public concerned are given an opportunity, before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent for the project is granted, to forward to the competent authority in the relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State, within a reasonable time, their opinion on the information supplied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) make available to the public concerned any information received from the competent authority of the relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^c-c7953657ed3b453f2a06a01cfd28751a]....

(3) In paragraph (1), the “relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] area” means the United Kingdom and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] marine area but does not include the Scottish inshore region, except in respect of activities and operations in that region for which a licence is required under Part 2 of the 1985 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
