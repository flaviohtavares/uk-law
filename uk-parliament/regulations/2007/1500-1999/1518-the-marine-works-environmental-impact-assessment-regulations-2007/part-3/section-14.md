# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 14

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 14. Provision of further information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1)[^c-7f9f25c95c73e73f4d1676840ee3eac8] Where the appropriate authority reasonably considers that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) it requires relevant further information, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the applicant is (or should be) able to provide such information, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

the appropriate authority must notify the applicant in writing of the matters on which it requires further information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1A)[^c-34038998635dda92f683c47a5d7c2d91] In paragraph (1), “relevant further information” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) further information properly to consider the likely significant environmental effects of the project which gives rise to the regulated activity covered by the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) the applicant has submitted an environmental statement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a scoping opinion has been given in accordance with regulation 13 and Schedule 4, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

  further information which is directly relevant to reaching a conclusion about the significant effects of the project described in the environmental statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, until any further information required in accordance with paragraph (1) has been provided to the appropriate authority and to the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Where an applicant has failed to provide any information required in accordance with paragraph (1) within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified, or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the regulator may treat the application as having been withdrawn; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
