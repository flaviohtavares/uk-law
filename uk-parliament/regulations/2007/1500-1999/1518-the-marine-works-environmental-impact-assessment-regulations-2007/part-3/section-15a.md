# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 15A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 15A. Co-ordination (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fbb6f180f883bf53fcbe0f4b73578053][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Where, in respect of a regulated activity, there is a requirement to carry out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) an environmental impact assessment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) an assessment under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] any law of any part of the United Kingdom that implemented[^key-6e713527f909c8023bfb27c000339444] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the Habitats Directive or the Wild Birds Directive, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

the appropriate authority must, where appropriate, ensure that the assessments are co-ordinated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
