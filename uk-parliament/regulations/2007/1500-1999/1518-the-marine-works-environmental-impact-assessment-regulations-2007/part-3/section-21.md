# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 21

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 21. Consideration of representations from the public (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the appropriate authority must apply the provisions of Schedule 5 in relation to each representation it receives pursuant to the statement referred to in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 16(2)(g).

(2) To the extent that the appropriate authority considers that representations made to it pursuant to the statement referred to in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 16(2)(g) are similar in material respects or deal with similar or related issues, it may group such representations and apply the provisions of Schedule 5 to each such group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
