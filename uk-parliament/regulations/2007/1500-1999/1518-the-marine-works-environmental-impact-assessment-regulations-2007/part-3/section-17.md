# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 17

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 17. Consultation on proposed regulated activity (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The appropriate authority must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] as soon as reasonably possible[^key-398c347fc538bda89ee962c8d633c23b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) supply the following material to such of the consultation bodies as it considers appropriate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) a copy of the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a copy of the environmental statement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a copy of any further information supplied by the applicant to the appropriate authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a letter stating that any representations in response to consultation regarding the application should be made in writing to the appropriate authority, at an address specified by the appropriate authority, within 42 days from the date of the letter (or such longer period as may be agreed between the consultation body and the appropriate authority in accordance with paragraph (2)); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) direct the applicant to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The appropriate authority may agree a longer consultation period with a consultation body where, in the opinion of the appropriate authority, it is reasonable to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, until the applicant has complied with any direction made in accordance with paragraph (1)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Where the applicant has failed to comply with a direction made in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified, or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the regulator may treat the application as having been withdrawn, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) Neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, until the consultation period (including any extension agreed in accordance with paragraph (2)) has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
