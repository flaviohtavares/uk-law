# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 16

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 16. Publicity (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1)[^key-a90a3a67d5608e4c2d7d5050da36ef33] The appropriate authority must, as soon as reasonably possible, publicise the application and the environmental statement in respect of the project to which it relates or, as the case may be, any further information provided by the applicant pursuant to a notification under regulation 14, by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) publishing a notice containing the information set out in paragraph (2) on the appropriate authority’s website; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) publishing, or directing the applicant to publish, the notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) in two successive weeks, in such newspapers or other publications as the authority thinks fit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in such other manner (if any) as the authority considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The information referred to in paragraph (1)(a)(i) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the applicant’s name and address; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a statement that an application for a licence or consent for a regulated activity has been made and that the environmental statement has been prepared or, as the case may be, that the further information has been furnished to the appropriate authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a statement of the nature, size and location of the project; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d)[^key-1a7cb4744ba49cb698f7d066cd59f21d] the fact that the project is subject to an environmental impact assessment and, where relevant, that regulation 18 applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c0bf3c9142a42cf8b57c2da050f2e0d3][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] details of the appropriate authority responsible for making the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] consent decision and, where the appropriate authority is not the regulator, details of the regulator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (db (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the nature of possible decisions, including any decision to grant regulatory approval; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e)[^key-4a24a89f117619a83de40268795b8de0] the address of an office of the appropriate authority, or other place nominated by the appropriate authority, at which the documents listed in paragraph (2A) may be inspected free of charge at all reasonable hours within 42 days beginning with the date of first publication of the notice under paragraph (1)(b)(i); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f)[^key-aa9ef31244ee77493f1c856b9ba5461e] the contact details of the appropriate authority for obtaining copies of the documents listed in paragraph (2A) and, if a charge is to be made for a copy, the amount (not exceeding a reasonable charge for copying), of the charge; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) a statement that any person wishing to make representations regarding the application and environmental statement or, as the case may be, the further information should make them in writing to the appropriate authority at an address specified by the appropriate authority, within 42 days beginning *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] with the date of first publication of the notice under paragraph (1)(b)(i); and[^key-56ffb8fea098f313b0c52af7555c0ee7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (h)[^key-64417ab530c25cade87e344c1f827449] details of the arrangements for public participation in accordance with Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2A)[^key-52151495ebd90a2104c47171e809d009] The appropriate authority must take steps to secure that the following documents relating to the application are made available for public inspection at all reasonable hours at the address referred to in paragraph (2)(e)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) any screening opinion given in accordance with regulation 11 and Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) any scoping opinion given in accordance with regulation 13 and Schedule 4 (including any written statement of reasons); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the environmental statement and any further information provided by the applicant pursuant to a notification under regulation 14; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) in accordance with national legislation, the main reports or advice issued to the appropriate authority at the time when the public concerned is first notified under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2B) The appropriate authority must ensure that the documents listed in paragraph (2A) are electronically accessible to the public, through at least an appropriate central portal or easily accessible points of access. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2C) The applicant must ensure that a reasonable number of copies of the environmental statement and any further information provided by the applicant pursuant to a notification under regulation 14 are available at the address notified under paragraph (2)(e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The applicant must comply with any reasonable direction made in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) and neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, unless or until this has been done. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Where the applicant has failed to comply with a direction made in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified, or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the regulator may treat the application as having been withdrawn, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) Neither the regulator nor the appropriate authority need deal further with, or exercise any functions under these Regulations in relation to, the application, and the regulator must not reach its regulatory decision, until the period for representations has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
