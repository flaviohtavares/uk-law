# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 24ZA

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 24ZA (affected by repeal, see note). Notification and publication of regulatory decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-71a1162f1d85b3fd0326b5301019a53d][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The regulator must, as soon as reasonably possible, send a copy of its regulatory decision to-- *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority, the appropriate authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) any person from whom the appropriate authority received representations pursuant to the statement referred to in regulation 16(2)(g); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) any consultation body that responded to the consultation pursuant to the letter referred to in regulation 17(1)(a)(iv); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) the authorities of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] State who were consulted in accordance with regulation 20. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The regulator must, as soon as possible after its decision is sent to the applicant pursuant to paragraph (1), ensure that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) notice of that decision is published— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) on the regulator’s website; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in any newspapers or other publications where the application was published under regulation 16(1)(b)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in such other manner as it considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) it promptly makes a written copy of the regulatory decision available for public inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) In paragraph (2)(b), “public inspection” means: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) in the case of an activity requiring regulatory approval under the 1985 Act or the 2009 Act, inspection on the relevant Public Register; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in the case of other regulated activities, inspection at the address nominated under regulation 16(2)(e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The notice in paragraph (2)(a) above must state— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) that the regulator has made available for public inspection the written copy of the regulatory decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the times at which the relevant Public Register or the information at the address nominated under regulation 16(2)(e) may be inspected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
