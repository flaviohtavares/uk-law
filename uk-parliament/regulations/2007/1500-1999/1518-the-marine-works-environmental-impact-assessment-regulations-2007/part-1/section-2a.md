# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 2A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 2A. Proposed activity which would otherwise be a regulated activity (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4c879ac61f57f712b9b9b8cca5f5b316][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) an activity is proposed to be carried out which would be a regulated activity if carried out by a person other than the person who would be the regulator in relation to that activity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the person by whom the activity is proposed to be carried out (“the relevant person”) is the person who would be the regulator in relation to that activity if it were carried out by any other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Where this paragraph applies, these Regulations apply in relation to that proposal as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the activity were a regulated activity, the relevant person had made an application for regulatory approval in respect of that activity and, in relation to the carrying out of that activity by that person, that person were also the regulator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) accordingly— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) references to the regulator’s dealing with the application or to a regulatory decision were references to determining whether to carry out the proposal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] references to granting a regulatory approval were references to a decision to proceed to carry out the proposal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] references to treating the application as withdrawn were references to treating the proposal as abandoned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
