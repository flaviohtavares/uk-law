# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 3

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 3. Fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1596305c9b7704d59fb72e5147785974][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) An appropriate authority may require an applicant for a regulatory approval to pay to it reasonable fees in respect of relevant expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) In paragraph (1) “relevant expenses” means administrative and other expenses which the authority reasonably incurs under these Regulations in its capacity as an appropriate authority (including any expenses in respect of examinations and tests carried out for that purpose), but does not include any expenses in respect of which a fee may be charged under any other provision of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Paragraph (4) applies to any requirement imposed under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) paragraph (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b43a823b49390e83ef03fd2b98d89a63][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] regulation 10(4J); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) paragraph 2 of Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) paragraph 3 of Schedule 4; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) regulation 24A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The determination of the amount of a reasonable fee in accordance with any requirement to which this paragraph applies must be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) where the appropriate authority is the Secretary of State, by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^key-4bdb61cf9e22f3fe6cafa5974bc8bfc7]...;
- (b) where the appropriate authority is the Marine Management Organisation, by the Marine Management Organisation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] with the consent of the Secretary of State;[^key-2f5c9220c94649e5b3ce7ef0711bf09f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c)[^key-d86013b3db6dd49fec6baa275048d5b1] where the appropriate authority is the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland, by that Department with the consent of the Department of Finance in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) where the appropriate authority is the Welsh Ministers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] , examining authority[^key-7f522a3a04a6ee5f18a3524a0773e6da] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-f220f2fc5efddc45c0fdf1a3e272e196] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], by the Welsh Ministers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [^key-780b704c216141a6474f50ae405f2a89]...; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) where the appropriate authority is the Scottish Ministers, by the Scottish Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) Before determining the amount of a fee which is imposed under any requirement referred to in paragraph (3), the authority must consult such organisations as appear to it to represent persons who are likely to apply for regulatory approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
