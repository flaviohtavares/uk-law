# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULEA1

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## SCHEDULE (affected by repeal, see note) A1 — Descriptions of projects that are Schedule A1 Projects (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0833d88be990f7ecf2dd69c1a50b31de][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Regulations 7, 10 and 10A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**1.** Crude‑oil refineries (excluding undertakings manufacturing only lubricants from crude‑oil) and installations for the gasification and liquefaction of 500 tonnes or more of coal or bituminous shale per day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**2.** Thermal power stations and other combustion installations with a heat output of 300 megawatts or more. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**3.** Nuclear power stations and other nuclear reactors including the dismantling or decommissioning of such power stations or reactors (except research installations for the production and conversion of fissionable and fertile material, whose maximum power does not exceed 1 kilowatt continuous thermal load). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**4.** Installations for the reprocessing of irradiated nuclear fuel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**5.** Installations designed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) for the production or enrichment of nuclear fuel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) for the processing of irradiated nuclear fuel or high‑level radioactive waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) for the final disposal of irradiated nuclear fuel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) solely for the final disposal of radioactive waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) solely for the storage (planned for more than ten years) of irradiated nuclear fuels or radioactive waste in a different site from the production site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**6.** Integrated works for the initial smelting of cast‑iron and steel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**7.** Installations for the production of non‑ferrous crude metals from ore, concentrates or secondary raw materials by metallurgical, chemical or electrolytic processes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**8.** Installations for the extraction of asbestos and for the processing and transformation of asbestos and products containing asbestos— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) for asbestos‑cement products, with an annual production of more than 20,000 tonnes of finished products; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) for friction material, with an annual production of more than 50 tonnes of finished products; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) for other uses of asbestos, utilisation of more than 200 tonnes per year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**9.** Integrated chemical installations, that is to say, installations for the manufacture on an industrial scale of substances using chemical conversion processes, in which several units are juxtaposed and are functionally linked to one another and which are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) for the production of basic organic chemicals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) for the production of basic inorganic chemicals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) for the production of phosphorous–, nitrogen– or potassium‑based fertilisers (simple or compound fertilisers); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) for the production of basic plant health products and of biocides; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) for the production of basic pharmaceutical products using a chemical or biological process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) for the production of explosives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**10.** Construction of lines for long‑distance railway traffic and of airports with a basic runway length of 2,100 metres or more. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**11.** Construction of motorways and express roads. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**12.** Construction of a new road of four or more lanes, or realignment or widening of an existing road of two lanes or less, so as to provide four or more lanes, where such new road, or realigned or widened section of road, would be 10 kilometres or more in a continuous length. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**13.** Inland waterways and ports for inland‑waterway traffic which permit the passage of vessels of over 1,350 tonnes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**14.** Trading ports, piers for loading and unloading connected to land and outside ports (excluding ferry piers) which can take vessels of over 1,350 tonnes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**15.** Waste disposal installations for the incineration, chemical treatment (as defined in Annex I to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 2008/98/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/0098)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^f00028], as last amended by[^key-8af1cc96df03837331ac8ab524879239] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive (EU) 2018/851[^key-bc74c5022f68aeffcb161d25a83e0410] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], under heading D9), or landfill of hazardous waste as defined in Article 3(2) of that Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**16.** Waste disposal installations for the incineration or chemical treatment (as defined in Annex I to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 2008/98/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] under heading D9) of non‑hazardous waste with a capacity exceeding 100 tonnes per day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**17.** Groundwater abstraction or artificial groundwater recharge schemes where the annual volume of water abstracted or recharged is equivalent to or exceeds 10 million cubic metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**18.** Works for the transfer of water resources, other than piped drinking water, between river basins where the transfer aims at preventing possible shortages of water and where the amount of water transferred exceeds 100 million cubic metres per year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**19.** In all other cases, works for the transfer of water resources, other than piped drinking water, between river basins where the multi‑annual average flow of the basin of abstraction exceeds 2,000 million cubic metres per year and where the amount of water transferred exceeds 5% of this flow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**20.** Waste water treatment plants with a capacity exceeding 150,000 population equivalent as defined in Article 2(6) of Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 91/271/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0271)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning urban waste-water treatment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^f00029].

**21.** Extraction of petroleum and natural gas for commercial purposes where the amount extracted exceeds 500 tonnes per day in the case of petroleum and 500,000 cubic metres per day in the case of gas. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**22.** Dams and other installations designed for the holding back or permanent storage of water, where a new or additional amount of water held back or stored exceeds 10 million cubic metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**23.** Pipelines with a diameter of more than 800 millimetres and a length of more than 40 kilometres for the transport of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) gas, oil or chemicals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) carbon dioxide streams for the purposes of geological storage, including associated booster stations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**24.** Installations for the intensive rearing of poultry or pigs with more than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) 85,000 places for broilers or 60,000 places for hens; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) 3,000 places for production pigs (over 30 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] kg *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) 900 places for sows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**25.** Industrial plants for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the production of pulp from timber or similar fibrous materials; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the production of paper and board with a production capacity exceeding 200 tonnes per day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**26.** Quarries and open‑cast mining where the surface of the site exceeds 25 hectares, or peat extraction where the surface of the site exceeds 150 hectares. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**27.** Construction of overhead electrical power lines with a voltage of 220 kV or more and a length of more than 15 km. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**28.** Installations for storage of petroleum, petrochemical or chemical products with a capacity of 200,000 tonnes or more. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**29.** Storage sites pursuant to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Chapter 3 of Part 1 of the Energy Act 2008 and any law of any part of the United Kingdom that implemented[^key-e535e36785f2a769997b533b84808031] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 2009/31/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0031)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the geological storage of carbon dioxide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**30.** Installations for the capture of carbon dioxide streams for the purposes of geological storage pursuant to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Chapter 3 of Part 1 of the Energy Act 2008 and any law of any part of the United Kingdom that implemented[^key-304eae3cd88bb6d74aeaf49c7e205564] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 2009/31/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0031)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] from installations referred to in this Schedule, or where the total yearly capture of carbon dioxide is 1.5 megatonnes or more *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**31.** Any change to or extension of project specified in this Schedule where such a change or extension in itself meets the thresholds, if any, or description of development set out in this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**32.** In this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- “airport” means an airport which complies with the definition in the 1944 Chicago Convention setting up the International Civil Aviation Organisation (Annex 14); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- “express road” means a road which complies with the definition in the European Agreement on Main International Traffic Arteries of 15th November 1975; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- “nuclear power station” and “other nuclear reactor” do not include an installation from the site of which all nuclear fuel and other radioactive contaminated materials have been permanently removed; and development for the purpose of dismantling or decommissioning a nuclear power station or other nuclear reactor is to be treated as development of the description mentioned in paragraph 3 of this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
