# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 26

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 26. Offences committed by bodies corporate (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Where an offence under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 25 which has been committed by a body corporate is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of, a director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body corporate, or any person who was purporting to act in any such capacity, he as well as the body corporate is guilty of that offence and liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Where the affairs of a body corporate are managed by its members, paragraph (1) applies in relation to the acts and defaults of a member in connection with his functions of management as if he were a director of the body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
