# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 27

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 27. Offences committed by Scottish partnerships (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Where an offence under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 25 which has been committed by a Scottish partnership is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of, one or more of the partners or any person who was purporting to act in such capacity, he as well as the partnership is guilty of that offence and liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
