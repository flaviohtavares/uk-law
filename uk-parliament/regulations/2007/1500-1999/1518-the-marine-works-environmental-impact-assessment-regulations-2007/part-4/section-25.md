## 25. Provision of false (affected by repeal, see note) etc (affected by repeal, see note) information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) A person is guilty of an offence if, for the purpose of procuring or obtaining an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA consent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] (whether for the benefit of himself, another or both), he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) makes a statement that he knows to be false in a material particular; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) recklessly makes a statement which is false in a material particular; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) intentionally fails to disclose any material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under paragraph (1) is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine of an amount not exceeding the statutory maximum; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
