# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 10A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 10A. Further provisions in relation to Article 2(4) of the (affected by repeal, see note) EIA (affected by repeal, see note) Directive (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7854bb35ba153121f1bd9beb57f44f95][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The Marine Management Organisation may not make a determination under regulation 10(1)(a)(i) unless the Secretary of State has given a direction under paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The Secretary of State may direct that an environmental impact assessment is not required in relation to any regulated activity that is to be carried out in the course of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A1[^key-70f367859db1d1d8bb98caed95436dbe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A2[^key-77d358bd4ade7d3b30cd17a90d2446ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project, if the Secretary of State is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the Marine Management Organisation is the appropriate authority having the function of determining whether an environmental impact assessment is required in relation to the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a direction that an environmental impact assessment is not required for the regulated activity can be justified in accordance with Article 2(4) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive (exemption for exceptional cases); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the regulated activity would not be likely to have significant effects on the environment of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] an EEA State[^key-414c372a33d3c414c5e72260faa28189] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].

(3) As soon as practicable after making any such direction, the Secretary of State must send a copy of the direction to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the Marine Management Organisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) where the Marine Management Organisation is not also the regulator, the regulator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) any relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The Natural Resources Body for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or examining authority[^key-91cdbf4d6fd68f4a5e3a8b3797a15a49] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] may not make a determination under regulation 10(1)(a)(i) unless the Welsh Ministers have given a direction under paragraph (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) The Welsh Ministers may direct that an environmental impact assessment is not required in relation to any regulated activity that is to be carried out in the course of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A1[^key-2d80df9584657040a2559ef1d262d49a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A2[^key-a0e0a9ad6c17f07200417161ad285b28] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project, if the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the Natural Resources Body for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or examining authority[^key-c85a27c66d87467d9bb93a87bb9da6e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] is the appropriate authority having the function of determining whether an environmental impact assessment is required in relation to the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a direction that an environmental impact assessment is not required for the regulated activity can be justified in accordance with Article 2(4) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive (exemption for exceptional cases); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the regulated activity would not be likely to have significant effects on the environment of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] an EEA State[^key-842ff49f6f8a04d76ad5c6d987c7e742] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].

(6) As soon as practicable after making any such direction, the Welsh Ministers must send a copy of the direction to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the Natural Resources Body for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) where the Natural Resources Body for Wales is not also the regulator, the regulator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) any relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(7) The Natural Resources Body for Wales must provide the Welsh Ministers with such information as they require to comply with the obligations referred to in regulation 10(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(8)[^key-2b28fe7b0562680847f054a5b29a3f64] For the purpose of this regulation, Article 2(4) of the EIA Directive is to be read as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) in the first sub-paragraph, the words “Without prejudice to Article 7,” were omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) “Member States”, in both places where it occurs, were read as “appropriate authority”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in the second sub-paragraph, point (c) were omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the third and fourth sub-paragraphs were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
