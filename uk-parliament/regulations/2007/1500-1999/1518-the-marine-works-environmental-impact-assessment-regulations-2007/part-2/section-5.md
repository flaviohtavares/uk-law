# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 5

> Text as at 2026-08-09.

## 5. Requirement of assessment by agreement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

An environmental impact assessment is required in relation to a regulated activity if the applicant so agrees with the appropriate authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
