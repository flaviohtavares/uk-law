## 8. Determination: (affected by repeal, see note) Schedule A2 (affected by repeal, see note) projects (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-ae778f013e8296b55e99029db4099f47][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) Subject to regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 9 to 10[^c-1cb91c9efec4c8af5e8626bce56d3eca] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], the appropriate authority must determine that an environmental impact assessment is required in relation to a regulated activity that is to be carried out in the course of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A2[^c-dc15760d14f563259e32a315ef8ba08c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project, if it concludes that the project in question is likely, because of its size, nature or location, to have significant effects on the environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) In reaching a conclusion as to whether or not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a Schedule A2[^c-3b70dedcd0768ce916f06790177f9cd9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] project is likely to have significant effects on the environment, the appropriate authority must have regard to the criteria set out in Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
