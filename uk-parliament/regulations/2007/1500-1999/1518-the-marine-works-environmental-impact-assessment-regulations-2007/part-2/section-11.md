# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 11

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 11. Screening opinions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) An applicant may request a screening opinion from the appropriate authority at any time before he applies for a regulatory approval in relation to a regulated activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) If the request is made and the applicant does not defer making his application until the screening opinion is given, the regulator must not deal with the application until after the appropriate authority has given its screening opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) If an applicant makes an application for a regulatory approval in relation to a regulated activity without having requested a screening opinion and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the regulator considers that the regulated activity is or may be one in relation to which an environmental impact assessment is required under regulation 7[^key-5c149ffb31792da1e22ad02bfb4104e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] , 8 or 8A[^key-ac1227c004455383df7c01604d9582f1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], the regulator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) must direct the applicant to request a screening opinion from the appropriate authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) must not deal with the application until after the appropriate authority has given its screening opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3A)[^key-ff9de4d6cfb4052004d5e815f7c2721d] The regulator must decide that a BBNJ activity may be an activity in relation to which an environmental impact assessment is required under regulation 8A if the regulator considers that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the activity may have more than a minor or transitory effect on the marine environment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the effects of the activity are unknown or poorly understood. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3B) Where the regulator decides that paragraph (3A) does not apply in respect of a BBNJ activity, the regulator must give the applicant a notice stating that the activity does not require an environmental impact assessment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3C) Terms used in paragraph (3A)(a) and (b) have the same meaning that they have in the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement (see in particular Article 30(1)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) The procedures for requesting and giving screening opinions are set out in Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5) If the screening opinion is that an environmental impact assessment is not required for the project in the course of which the regulated activity would be carried out, the application may (subject to regulation 10(3) or (4), if either applies) proceed in accordance with the relevant legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(6) If the screening opinion is that an environmental impact assessment is required for the regulated activity, the regulator must reject the application unless it is one which is capable of being dealt with in accordance with Part 3 without changes being made to the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(7) Where paragraph (2), (3), (5) or (6) applies in relation to an application under relevant legislation that provides that an applicant may proceed to carry out a regulated activity without further consent unless the regulator takes some step within a specified period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) any time prior to the giving of the screening opinion by the appropriate authority does not count in the calculation of that period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) where the appropriate authority gives a screening opinion to the effect that an environmental impact assessment is required for the regulated activity, the regulator is to be treated for the purposes of the relevant legislation as having taken, within the specified period, a step of such a kind as precludes the applicant from proceeding to carry out the regulated activity without further consent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(8) Paragraphs (2), (3) (5), (6) and (7) apply notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in the relevant legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
