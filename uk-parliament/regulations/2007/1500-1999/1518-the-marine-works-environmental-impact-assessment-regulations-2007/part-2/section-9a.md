# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 9A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 9A. Projects in response to a civil emergency (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b6455e9ac3a833d38b848e3411dcbd26][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The appropriate authority may direct that an environmental impact assessment is not required in relation to a regulated activity where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the project in relation to which the regulated activity is being carried out has the response to a civil emergency as its sole purpose; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority considers that an environmental impact assessment in relation to the regulated activity would have an adverse effect on the fulfilment of that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Before making any such direction, the appropriate authority must notify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) where the appropriate authority is not also the regulator, the regulator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) where the appropriate authority is the Marine Management Organisation or the Natural Resources Body for Wales, the Secretary of State, or the appropriate devolved authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) any relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
