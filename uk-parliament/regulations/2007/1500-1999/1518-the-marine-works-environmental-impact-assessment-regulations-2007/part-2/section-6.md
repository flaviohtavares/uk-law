## 6. Requirement of assessment by determination (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

An environmental impact assessment is required in relation to a regulated activity if the appropriate authority so determines under regulation 7 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] , 8 or 8A[^c-fe4fa2fe935b3d0085d02e76f1520946] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].
