# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, section 8A

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 8A. Determination: areas beyond national jurisdiction (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-64dc6c4e82f226ba114c7d97cc3de0b9][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) The appropriate authority must determine that an environmental impact assessment is required in relation to a BBNJ activity if the authority concludes that there are reasonable grounds for believing that the activity may cause— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) substantial pollution of, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) significant and harmful changes to, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

the marine environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) In considering whether or not there are such reasonable grounds, the appropriate authority must have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the criteria set out in Schedule 1, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) any other matter necessary to give effect to Article 30(1)(b) of the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Terms used in paragraph (1)(a) and (b) have the same meaning that they have in the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement (see in particular Article 30(1)(b)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) This regulation is subject to regulations 9 and 9A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
