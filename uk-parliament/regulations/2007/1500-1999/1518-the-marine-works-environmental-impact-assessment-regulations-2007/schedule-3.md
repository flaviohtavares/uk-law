# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE3

> Text as at 2026-08-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## SCHEDULE (affected by repeal, see note) 3 — Information to be included in an environmental statement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a951f3b88f869cd20a1517d75ed5c4a2][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Regulation 12(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**1.** A description of the project and of the regulated activity, including in particular: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a description of the location of the project and the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) a description of the physical characteristics of the whole project and regulated activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], including where relevant, requisite demolition works, and the land-use requirements during the construction and operational phases; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a description of the main characteristics of the operational phase of the project and the regulated activity (in particular any production process): for instance, energy demand and energy used, the nature and quantity of the materials and natural resources (including water, land, soil and biodiversity) used; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) an estimate, by type and quantity, of expected residues and emissions (such as water, air, soil and subsoil pollution, noise, vibration, light, heat, radiation and quantities and types of waste produced during the construction and operation phases) resulting from the operation of the proposed project and the regulated activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**2.** A description of the reasonable alternatives (for example in terms of project design, technology, location, size and scale) studied by the applicant, which are relevant to the proposed project, the regulated activity and their specific characteristics, and an indication of the main reasons for selecting the chosen option, including a comparison of the environmental effects. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**3.** A description of the relevant aspects of the current state of the environment (baseline scenario), and an outline of the likely evolution thereof without implementation of the project, as far as natural changes from the baseline scenario can be assessed with reasonable effort on the basis of the availability of environmental information and scientific knowledge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**4.** A description of the factors specified in regulation 21A(2)(a) to (e) likely to be significantly affected by the project and the regulated activity: population, human health, biodiversity (for example, fauna and flora), land (for example, land take), soil (for example, organic matter, erosion, compaction, sealing), water (for example, hydromorphological changes, quantity and quality), air, climate (for example, greenhouse gas emissions, impacts relevant to adaptation), material assets, cultural heritage, including architectural and archaeological aspects, and landscape. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**5.** A description of the likely significant effects of the project and the regulated activity on the environment resulting from, inter alia— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the construction and existence of the project and the regulated activity, including, where relevant, demolition works; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the use of natural resources, in particular land, soil, water and biodiversity, considering as far as possible the sustainable availability of these resources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) the emission of pollutants, noise, vibration, light, heat and radiation, the creation of nuisances, and the disposal and recovery of waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) the risks to human health, cultural heritage or the environment (for example, due to accidents or disasters); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e) the cumulation of effects with other existing or approved projects, taking into account any existing environmental problems relating to areas of particular environmental importance likely to be affected or the use of natural resources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (f) the impact of the project on climate (for example, the nature and magnitude of greenhouse gas emissions) and the vulnerability of the project to climate change; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (g) the technologies and the substances used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**6.** The description of the likely significant effects on the factors specified in regulation 21A(2)(a) to (e) must cover the direct effects and any indirect, secondary, cumulative, transboundary, short-term, medium-term and long-term, permanent and temporary, positive and negative effects of the project and the regulated activity. This description must take into account the environmental protection objectives *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] in[^c-3fec4bf8f2a640069cfef600425d56c7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] assimilated[^c-0e12d4b4112529657f4b5e0e382f296c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] law or under the law of any part of the United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] which are relevant to the project and the regulated activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**7.** A description of the forecasting methods or evidence used to identify and assess the significant effects on the environment including details of difficulties (for example, technical deficiencies or lack of knowledge) encountered compiling the required information and the main uncertainties involved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**8.** A description of the measures envisaged to avoid, prevent, reduce or if possible offset any identified significant adverse effects on the environment and, where appropriate, of any proposed monitoring arrangements (for example, the preparation of a post-project analysis). That description must explain the extent to which significant adverse effects on the environment are avoided, prevented, reduced or offset, and must cover both the construction and operational phases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**9.** A description of the expected significant adverse effects of the project and the regulated activity on the environment deriving from the vulnerability of the project and the regulated activity to risks of major accidents or disasters which are relevant to the project and the regulated activity concerned. Relevant information available and obtained through risk assessments pursuant to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] assimilated[^c-8ba72097f1f0895953b7046482b743a0][^c-fe3aad67c239ef6b5b607b7215e7ff6b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] law such as any law that implemented *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 2012/18/EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2012/0018)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the control of major accident hazards involving dangerous substances, amending and subsequently repealing Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] [Directive 96/82/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0082)[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or Council Directive 2009/71/Euratom establishing a Community framework for the nuclear safety of nuclear installations or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] environmental assessments may be used for this purpose provided that the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] any law that implemented[^c-ddaa9ad9f2276672c636875ae2a57bd3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive are met. Where appropriate, this description must include measures envisaged to prevent or mitigate the significant adverse effects of such events on the environment and details of the preparedness for and proposed response to such emergencies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**9A.**[^c-4fa5fa34538951c87047e676d7e987b9] In relation to a BBNJ activity, any other information that is necessary to enable the appropriate authority to give effect to Article 31(1)(c) of the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

**10.** A non-technical summary of the information provided under paragraphs 1 to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 9A[^c-a56e898d52fa99de8dc30232ddf11f2f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483].

**11.** A reference list detailing the sources used for the descriptions and assessments included in the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
