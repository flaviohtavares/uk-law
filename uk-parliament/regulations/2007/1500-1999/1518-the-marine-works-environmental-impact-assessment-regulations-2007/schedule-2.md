# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2026-08-09.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — SCREENING OPINIONS (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

Regulation 11(4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 1. Request for a screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) A request for a screening opinion must be accompanied by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) a chart or map (or both) sufficient to identify the location of the project and of the regulated activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b)[^key-ece10f2a3d1f0ce6cdcc6fafe814251f] a description of the project, including in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) a description of the physical characteristics of the whole project and, where relevant, of demolition works; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] a description of the location of the project, with particular regard to the environmental sensitivity of geographical areas likely to be affected; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) a description of the aspects of the environment likely to be significantly affected by the project; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (d) a description of any likely significant effects of the project on the environment, to the extent of the information available on such effects resulting from— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) the expected residues and emission and the production of waste, where relevant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the use of natural resources, in particular soil, land, water and biodiversity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (e)[^key-304ccadff1f720b9d5e3271fe6be8efe] such further information or representations as the applicant may wish to provide or make, including a description of any features of the project or measures envisaged to avoid or prevent what might otherwise have been significant adverse effects on the environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1A)[^key-b305a02837becbb41362a3c11d0cec28] The selection criteria set out in Schedule 1 must be taken into account, where relevant, when compiling the information required by paragraph (1)(b) to (d) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1B) The applicant must take into account any other relevant and reasonably obtainable assessment carried out in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] an assimilated[^key-3864c1449f7555a9469af35114750044][^key-81049a63e7e01c4293aa5baac8ed2082] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] obligation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] under the law of any part of the United Kingdom other than under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Where the regulated activity comprises the whole of (or forms part of) a project in respect of which the applicant has made an application to a consenting authority other than the regulator, an applicant seeking a screening opinion must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) inform the appropriate authority and the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) of any such application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if any such consenting authority has requested an environmental statement in respect of that project, inform the appropriate authority and the regulator (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) of that request; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) if so requested by the appropriate authority, provide the appropriate authority with a copy of any environmental statement and of any other environmental information provided to any such consenting authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 2. Payment of a fee for a screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority may require an applicant to pay a reasonable fee in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the administrative expenses of providing a screening opinion; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the cost of carrying out any examinations or tests that, in the opinion of the appropriate authority, are necessary or expedient to enable the appropriate authority to produce its screening opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) If the appropriate authority considers that it is appropriate to do so, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) require the applicant to make a reasonable advance payment against the fee that it is entitled to charge for its screening opinion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) determine the balance of the fee payable after carrying out the work necessary to produce its screening opinion in accordance with the remaining provisions of this Schedule; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) require the applicant to pay the balance of the fee that it is entitled to charge prior to the notification of its screening opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 3. Procedure for reaching a screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority must, if it considers that it has not been provided with sufficient information to enable it to give a screening opinion, notify the applicant in writing of the matters on which it requires further information and the applicant must supply that further information to the appropriate authority within such period as the appropriate authority may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The applicant must supply the appropriate authority with such number of additional copies of the documentation as the appropriate authority may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) The appropriate authority need not deal further with the request for a screening opinion until the applicant has complied with the requirements of sub-paragraphs (1) and (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(4) Where an applicant has failed to comply with the requirements of sub-paragraph (1) or (2) within such reasonable period as the appropriate authority has specified, or such longer period as the appropriate authority may reasonably allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the regulator may treat the application to which the request relates as having been withdrawn, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the appropriate authority (if the regulator is not also the appropriate authority) may direct the regulator to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(5)[^key-3a5e7563cfa2328d34ae2f2077a4b221] Where the appropriate authority must give a screening opinion under these Regulations, the authority must take into account in making that decision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) any information provided by the applicant in accordance with paragraph 1(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) the results of any relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] environmental assessment which are reasonably available to the authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) such of the selection criteria set out in Schedule 1 as are relevant to the project. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 4. Consultation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) The appropriate authority must consult such of the consultation bodies as it considers appropriate before giving a screening opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) When carrying out any consultation under sub-paragraph (1), the appropriate authority must allow the consultation body a reasonable period within which to respond, and that period must not be less than 28 days from the date of the letter to the consultation body from the appropriate authority or such other period as may be agreed between the consultation body and the appropriate authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 4A. Giving a screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f3aac90770f6a4efdff468c669add7c2][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1)[^key-2c53a5ba1f0952331be9c5cc0f8481c9] Where the appropriate authority gives a screening opinion, the screening opinion must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) state the main reasons for the conclusion with reference to the relevant criteria listed in Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if it is determined that the regulated activity does not require an environmental impact assessment, state any features of the project or measures envisaged to avoid, or prevent what might otherwise have been, significant adverse effects on the environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2)[^key-52fe8775f77fae9dc378b59040f8ed03] A screening opinion given in relation to a BBNJ activity must include provision stating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) whether or not the regulator considers that an environmental impact assessment is required under regulation 8A in relation to the activity, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) in a case where the regulator considers that an environmental impact assessment is not required under regulation 8A in relation to the activity, whether the regulator considers that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (i) the activity may have more than a minor or transitory effect on the marine environment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] the effects of the activity are unknown or poorly understood. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) Terms used in sub-paragraph (2)(b)(i) or (ii) have the same meaning that they have in the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction Agreement (see in particular Article 30(1)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 5. Notification of a screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-53bbaf1e390a51b2411de09713f820ec][^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) Where the applicant requests a screening opinion under regulation 11(1) in accordance with paragraph 1(1), the appropriate authority must provide its screening opinion to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b) if the appropriate authority is not also the regulator, the regulator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (c) such of the consultation bodies as it consulted in accordance with paragraph 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) The appropriate authority must provide the opinion as soon as possible within a period of 90 days beginning with the day on which the request is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(3) In exceptional cases, where the nature, complexity, location or size of the project demands a longer period for determination, the appropriate authority may extend the period specified in sub-paragraph (2), informing the applicant in writing of the reasons justifying the extension and of the date on which its screening opinion is expected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

### 6. Availability of screening opinions for inspection (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the appropriate authority must ensure that, as soon as possible after being sent to the applicant, its screening opinion is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

- (a) publicised in such manner as it considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
- (b)[^key-7e1b40714c282a1762f03ecef5906a8d] in the case of an activity requiring regulatory approval under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] 1985 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483] or the 2009 Act[^key-01d5eb420b5549a27ad566c586f8714c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483], made available on the relevant Public Register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not require disclosure of any excluded information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-02c0b69fbc163db02681d8d002687483]
