# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Meaning of the appointed day

In this Chapter, “the appointed day” means the day referred to in section 1(2) of this Order, that is to say the date of commencement of this Constitution.
