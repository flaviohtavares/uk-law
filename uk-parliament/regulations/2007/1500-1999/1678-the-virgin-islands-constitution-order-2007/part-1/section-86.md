# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. General elections

A general election shall be held at such time within two months, but not earlier than twenty-one days, after every dissolution of the House of Assembly as the Governor shall appoint by proclamation published in the *Gazette*.
