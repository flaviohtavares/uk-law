# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Consolidated Fund

All revenues or other moneys raised or received by or for the purposes of the Government of the Virgin Islands (not being revenues or other moneys that are payable by or under any law into some other fund established for any specific purpose or that may, by or under any law, be retained by the authority that received them for the purpose of defraying the expenses of that authority) shall be paid into and form a Consolidated Fund.
