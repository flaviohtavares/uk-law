# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Composition of Legislature

There shall be a Legislature of the Virgin Islands which shall consist of Her Majesty and a House of Assembly.
