# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Subordinate courts and tribunals

There shall be such courts and tribunals in and for the Virgin Islands subordinate to the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court, and such courts and tribunals shall have such jurisdiction and powers, as may be prescribed by any law for the time being in force in the Virgin Islands.
