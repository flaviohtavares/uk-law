# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Attorney General

(1) There shall be an Attorney General of the Virgin Islands, whose office shall be a public office and who shall be appointed in accordance with section 95.

(2) The Attorney General shall be the principal legal adviser to the Government of the Virgin Islands.
