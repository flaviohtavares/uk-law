# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Proceedings which might affect freedom of conscience

If a court’s determination of any question arising under this Chapter might affect the exercise by a religious organisation (itself or its members collectively) or by an individual of the right to freedom of conscience as defined and protected by section 21, it must have particular regard to the importance of that right.
