# The Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Oaths and affirmations

Every member of the Cabinet and the Cabinet Secretary, and every member of the National Security Council (except the Governor), shall, before entering upon the duties of his or her office, make before the Governor an oath or affirmation of allegiance and an oath or affirmation for the due execution of that office in the forms set out in Schedule 1.
