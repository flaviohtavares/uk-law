# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking area) (City of Manchester) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1736

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking area) (City of Manchester) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

14th June 2007

Laid before Parliament

19th June 2007

Coming into force

13th July 2007

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001].

Manchester City Council has applied to the Secretary of State for an Order to be made under paragraphs 1(1) and 2(1) of that Schedule with respect to part of its area.

The Secretary of State has consulted the chief officer of Greater Manchester Police in accordance with the requirements of paragraphs 1(3) and 2(3) of that Schedule and the Council on Tribunals in accordance with the requirements of section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002].

Accordingly the Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 13th July 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999

Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999[^f00003] is amended as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

G Merron

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999 (“the principal order”) which lists the roads excluded from the permitted parking and special parking areas designated by the principal order. Schedule 1 is amended by substituting a new Part 1 that omits a number of the roads previously excluded from the permitted parking and special parking areas designated by the principal order.
