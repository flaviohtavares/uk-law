# The Home Information Pack (Revocation) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1525

housing, england and wales

The Home Information Pack (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made

24th May 2007

Laid before Parliament

24th May 2007

Coming into force

31st May 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 161, 163, 164, 165 and 250(2) of, and paragraph 11(b) of Schedule 8 to, the Housing Act 2004[^f00001].

In accordance with section 250(3) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the National Assembly for Wales in relation to residential properties in Wales.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) —These Regulations may be cited as the Home Information Pack (Revocation) Regulations 2007.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 31<sup>st</sup> May 2007.

## 2. Revocation of the Home Information Pack Regulations 2006 and the Home Information Pack Regulations 2007

The Home Information Pack Regulations 2006[^f00002] and the Home Information Pack Regulations 2007[^f00003] are revoked.

Yvette Cooper

Minister of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

24th May 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Home Information Pack Regulations 2006 and the Home Information Pack Regulations 2007 with effect from 31st May 2007.
