# The Armed Forces (Service Police Amendments) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1861

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces (Service Police Amendments) Order 2007

Made

27th June 2007

Coming into force

28th June 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 379(1)(b) of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00001].

In accordance with section 373(3)(a) of the Armed Forces Act 2006 a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces (Service Police Amendments) Order 2007 and shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

**2.** The Schedule to this Order shall have effect.

Derek Twigg

Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

27th June 2007

## SCHEDULE 1 — Modification of certain Statutory Instruments

Article 2

### 1. Amendment of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Application to the Armed Forces) Order 1997

(1) The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Application to the Armed Forces) Order 1997[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) In column 2 of the Schedule, for “Royal Navy Regulating Branch” substitute “Royal Navy Police”.

### 2. Amendment of the Crime and Disorder Strategies (Prescribed Descriptions) Order 1998

(1) The Crime and Disorder Strategies (Prescribed Descriptions) Order 1998[^f00003] is amended as follows—

- (a) In Article 3(4), for “Royal Navy Regulating Branch” substitute “Royal Navy Police”.

(1) The Regulation of Investigatory Powers (Directed Surveillance and Covert Human Intelligence Sources) Order 2003[^f00004] is amended as follows—

### 4. Amendment of the Armed Forces (Entry, Search and Seizure) Order 2003

(1) The Armed Forces (Entry, Search and Seizure) Order 2003[^f00005] is amended as follows—

### 5. Amendment of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers (Communications Data) Order 2003

### 6. Amendment of the Crime and Disorder Strategies (Prescribed Descriptions) (England) Order 2004

(1) The Crime and Disorder Strategies (Prescribed Descriptions) (England) Order 2004[^f00007] is amended as follows—

- (a) In Article 3(4), for “Royal Navy Regulating Branch” substitute “Royal Navy Police”.

### 7. Amendment of the Summary Appeal Courts (Warrant Officers) Order 2004

(1) The Summary Appeal Courts (Warrant Officers) Order 2004[^f00008] is amended as follows—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes minor changes to nine statutory instruments. The amendments provide for consequential changes in the legislation following the change of title of the Royal Naval Regulating Branch to that of the Royal Navy Police. Various amendments in Schedule 16 to the Armed Forces Act 2006 that provide for the change of name from the Royal Navy Regulating Branch to that of the Royal Navy Police in primary legislation have already been commenced, and this Order supplements those changes by changing the name in the subordinate legislation.
