# The Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) (Revocation) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1536

housing, england and wales

The Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) (Revocation) Order 2007

Made

25th May 2007

Laid before Parliament

31st May 2007

Coming into force

31st May 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 172 of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) (Revocation) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 31st May 2007.

## 2. Revocation of the Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) Order 2007

The Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) Order 2007[^f00002] is revoked.

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Postal Services

Department of Trade and Industry

25th May 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Home Information Pack (Redress Scheme) Order 2007.
