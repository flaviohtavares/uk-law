# The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Legal proceedings and court orders

Any person may make a primary or secondary disclosure for the purposes of—

- (a) the establishment, exercise or defence of legal rights; or
- (b) an order of a court.
