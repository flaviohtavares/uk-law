# The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Information from which no particular property is identifiable

Any person may make a primary or secondary disclosure where no particular property would be identifiable in doing so.
