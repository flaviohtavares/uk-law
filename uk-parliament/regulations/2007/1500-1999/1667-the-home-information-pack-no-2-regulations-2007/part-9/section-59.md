# The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Disclosures for the purposes of the 2004 Act or these Regulations

Any person may make a primary or secondary disclosure for the purposes of—

- (a) section 156(1), (2) and (11) of the 2004 Act; or
- (b) regulations 5, 6, 9(a) and 24.
