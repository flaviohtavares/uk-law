# The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Prevention of crime

Any person may make a primary or secondary disclosure for the purposes of or to facilitate—

- (a) the prevention or detection of crime; or
- (b) the apprehension or prosecution of offenders.
