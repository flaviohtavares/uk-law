# The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Internal processing of information

Any person may make a primary or secondary disclosure, where necessary for the purposes of processing information—

- (a) within a body of persons corporate or unincorporate;
- (b) between principal and agent;
- (c) between an employer and employee; or
- (d) in order to manage the register.
