# The Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1865

education, england

The Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made

27th June 2007

Laid before Parliament

5th July 2007

Coming into force

1st September 2008

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 24(6) and 47 of the Higher Education Act 2004[^f00001].

In accordance with section 26(2)(a)(i) and (b)(i) of that Act he is satisfied that the increases to the basic and higher amounts are no greater than are required to maintain the value of those amounts in real terms.

In accordance with section 26(3) of that Act he has had regard to the index of prices specified in regulations made by him under that subsection[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 1st September 2008.

## 2. Increases to the prescribed basic and higher amounts

The Student Fees (Amounts) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00003] are amended as follows:

- (a) in regulation 4 for “£1225” substitute “£1255” and for “£3070” substitute “£3145”, and
- (b) in regulation 5 for “£610” substitute “£625” and for “£1535” substitute “£1570”.

## 3. Revocation of the Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

The Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006[^f00004] are revoked.

Bill Rammell

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

27th June 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Student Fees (Amounts) (England) Regulations 2004 (“the 2004 Regulations”) by increasing the basic and higher amounts prescribed in the 2004 Regulations. The amendments come into force on 1st September 2008.

The 2004 Regulations prescribe the basic and higher amounts which higher education institutions in England may charge by way of tuition fees. Regulation 4 of the 2004 Regulations sets out the basic and higher amounts which ordinarily apply. Regulation 5 sets out the lower basic and higher amounts which apply in respect of an academic year of specified courses.

The 2004 Regulations were amended by the Student Fees (Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 (“the 2006 Regulations”), which come into force on 1st September 2007. These Regulations revoke the 2006 Regulations.
