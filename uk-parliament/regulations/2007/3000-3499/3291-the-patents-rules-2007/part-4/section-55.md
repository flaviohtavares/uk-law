# The Patents Rules 2007, section 55

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Bibliographic information about an unpublished application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of section 118(3)(b) the following bibliographic information is prescribed—

- (a) the name of the applicant;
- (b) the title of the invention;
- (c) the number of the application;
- (d) the date of filing of the application;
- (e) where a declaration has been made for the purposes of section 5(2)—
  - (i) the date of filing of each earlier relevant application specified in the declaration,
  - (ii) its application number, and
  - (iii) the country it was filed in or in respect of;
- (f) where an application has been terminated or withdrawn, that information; and
- (g) where a transaction, instrument or event mentioned in section 32(2)(b) or 33(3) is notified to the comptroller, that information.
