# The Patents Rules 2007, section 64

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Interpretation relating to international applications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part the following have the same meaning as they have in the Patent Co-operation Treaty—

- “competent receiving Office”;
- “International Preliminary Examination Report”;
- “International Preliminary Report on Patentability”;
- “International Search Report”;
- “International Searching Authority”;
- “receiving Office”.
