# The Patents Rules 2007, section 63

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Communication of information to the European Patent Office

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The comptroller may authorise any information in the files of the Patent Office to be communicated to the European Patent Office or to a competent authority of any country which is a party to the European Patent Convention, except where that information cannot be communicated under section 118.
