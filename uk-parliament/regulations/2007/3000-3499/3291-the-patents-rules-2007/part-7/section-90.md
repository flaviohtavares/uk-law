# The Patents Rules 2007, section 90

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Licences following entitlement proceedings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The period prescribed for the purposes of section 11(3) and (3A) shall be two months beginning immediately after[^c-d595262a37352447d79733339ea850c9]—

- (a) where section 11 is applied by section 12(5), the date on which the order under section 12(1) was made; and
- (b) in any other case, the date on which the order under section 8 was made.

(2) The period prescribed for the purposes of section 38(3) shall be two months beginning immediately after[^c-aa30ad6ef1a1ed2d7f248d6be30c1096] the date on which the order mentioned in section 38(2) was made.
