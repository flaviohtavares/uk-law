# The Patents Rules 2007, section 81

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Alteration of time limits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The comptroller may extend or shorten (or further extend or shorten) any period of time which has been specified under any provision of this Part.

(2) An extension may be granted under paragraph (1) notwithstanding the period of time specified has expired.
