# The Patents Rules 2007, section 14

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Size and presentation of application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The contents of all documents (including annotations to drawings and photographs[^c-7a4299a6b64d1cf8f7747cb8f8260454]) contained in an application for a patent must be in English or Welsh.

(2) The requirements for the documents contained in an application for a patent (other than drawings and photographs[^c-73c7cfc183cbbaaa77be50b1b787e195]) are set out in Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 2.

(3) The requirements for a drawing and a photograph[^c-0de998ab55ef8399ae850982f0dea7cb] contained in an application are set out in Parts 1 and 3 of that Schedule.

(4) All documents contained in an application (including drawings and photographs[^c-377b296bfb8c3ed1b81b94ed494b4204]) must comply with the requirements set out in Part 4 of that Schedule.

(5) Paragraphs (2) and (3) do not apply to an application, or a sequence listing contained in an application, which is delivered in electronic form or using electronic communications.
