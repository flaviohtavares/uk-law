# The Patents Rules 2007, section 120

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Transitional provisions and revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 5 (transitional provisions) shall have effect.

(2) The instruments set out in Schedule 6 (revocations) shall be revoked to the extent specified.
