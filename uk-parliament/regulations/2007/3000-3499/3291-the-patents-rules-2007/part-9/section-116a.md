# The Patents Rules 2007, section 116A

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116A. Notifications relating to supplementary protection certificates[^c-0e239faed287dfc0fe38561a095071d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-e5a5b9a9b4460ab5b78333eab47ff8f0] Notifications under Article 5(2)(b) and (c) of the Medicinal Products Regulation must be made on Patents Form SP5.

(2)[^c-4e017cf3784aa0b1e8801a6e04da29d1] Notifications under Article 13A of Regulation [(EC) 1610/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1610) and Article 13A of Regulation [(EC) 469/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0469) must be made on Patents Form SP6.
