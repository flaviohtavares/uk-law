# The Patents Rules 2007, section 102

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Appointing advisers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The comptroller may appoint an adviser to assist him in any proceeding before him and shall settle any question or instructions to be given to the adviser.
