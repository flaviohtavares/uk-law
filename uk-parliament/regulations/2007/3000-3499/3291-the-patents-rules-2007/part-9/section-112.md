# The Patents Rules 2007, section 112

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Copies available to the comptroller

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where an applicant is not required to file a copy of an application at the Patent Office because that application or a copy of that application is available to the comptroller.

(2) Where this rule applies the comptroller shall make a copy (or further copy) of that application and certify it accordingly.
