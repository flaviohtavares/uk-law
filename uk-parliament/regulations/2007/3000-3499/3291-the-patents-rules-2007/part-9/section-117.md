# The Patents Rules 2007, section 117

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. The journal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The comptroller must publish a journal containing—

- (a) particulars of applications for and grants of patents and of other proceedings under the Act;
- (b) any directions given under section 120(1) specifying hours of business or excluded days;
- (c) any directions under section 123(2A) setting out forms; and
- (d) any other information that the comptroller considers to be generally useful or important.
