# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Differences between the Territories and the United Kingdom

(1) This Order adopts certain amendments that have been made to the Annexes to the Convention on International Civil Aviation signed at Chicago on 7<sup>th</sup> December 1944 (the Chicago Convention) that have not yet been implemented in the United Kingdom, largely due to the fact that many of such amendments will be implemented through European Union legislation and the Rules of the European Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) in due course.

(2) This Order also reflects the fact that certain activities undertaken in the United Kingdom are not undertaken in the Overseas Territories, such as aircraft manufacturing and certification.
