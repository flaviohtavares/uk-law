# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 146

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 146. Exemption from Order

The Governor may exempt from any of the provisions of this Order (other than articles 35, 36, 123, 135, 136, 137, 138, and 147) or any regulations made thereunder, any aircraft or persons or classes of aircraft or persons, either absolutely or subject to such conditions as he thinks fit.
