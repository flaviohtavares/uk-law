# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 151

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 151. Approval for persons to furnish reports

In relation to any of his functions under any of the provisions of this Order the Governor may approve a person as qualified to furnish reports to him and may accept such reports.
