# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Application of Order

The provisions of this Order apply to the Territories mentioned in Schedule 12, any one of which is in this Order referred to in the expression “the Territory”.
