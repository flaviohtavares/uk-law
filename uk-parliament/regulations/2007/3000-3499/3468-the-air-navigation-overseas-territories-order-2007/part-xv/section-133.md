# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 133

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 133. Stowaways

A person shall not secrete himself for the purpose of being carried in an aircraft without the consent of either the operator or the pilot-in-command or of any other person entitled to give consent to his being carried in the aircraft.
