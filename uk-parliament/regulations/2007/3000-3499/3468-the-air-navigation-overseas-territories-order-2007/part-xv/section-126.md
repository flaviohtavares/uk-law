# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Endangering safety of any person or property

A person shall not recklessly or negligently cause or permit an aircraft to endanger any person or property.
