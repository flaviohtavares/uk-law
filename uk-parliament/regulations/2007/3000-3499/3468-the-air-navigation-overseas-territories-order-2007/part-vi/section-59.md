# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Duties of pilot-in-command – search and rescue

Without prejudice to any other provision of this order, the pilot-in-command of an aircraft that is either registered in the Territory or if not so registered flying within the Territory airspace shall cooperate in fulfilling the instructions of the Governor in relation to the provision of assistance to aircraft in distress or in relation to search and rescue.
