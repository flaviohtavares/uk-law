# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 121

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 121. Validity of certificates, licences, authorisations, approvals and permissions

Wherever in this Order there is provision for the giving of an instruction or the issue or grant of a certificate, licence, authorisation, approval or permission by the Governor, unless otherwise provided, such an instruction or a certificate, licence, authorisation, approval or permission—

- (a) shall be in writing;
- (b) may be issued or granted subject to such conditions as the Governor thinks fit; and
- (c) may be issued or granted, subject to article 122, for such periods as the Governor thinks fit; and
- (d) may be renewed from time to time by the Governor upon such conditions and for such further period or periods as he thinks fit.
