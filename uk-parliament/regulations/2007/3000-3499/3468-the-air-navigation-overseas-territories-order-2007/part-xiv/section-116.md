# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 116

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 116. Documents to be carried

(1) An aircraft shall not fly unless it carries the documents that it is required to carry under the law of the country in which it is registered.

(2) An aircraft registered in the Territory shall, when in flight, carry documents in accordance with instructions given or requirements specified by the Governor.
