# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Power to inspect and copy documents and records

An authorised person shall have the power to inspect and copy any certificate, licence, log book, document or record that he has the power under this Order to require to be produced to him.
