# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Noise and vibration caused by aircraft on aerodromes

(1) The Governor may specify the conditions under which noise and vibration may be caused by aircraft (including military aircraft) on Government aerodromes, certificated aerodromes or on licensed aerodromes in accordance with regulation 4 in Schedule 9 to this Order.

(2) Section 41(2) of the Act as set out in Schedule 2 of the Civil Aviation Act 1949 (Overseas Territories) Order 1969[^f00005] applies to any such aerodrome.
