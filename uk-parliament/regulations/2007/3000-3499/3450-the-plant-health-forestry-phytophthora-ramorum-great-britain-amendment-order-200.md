[^key-d0f051d6328f7947258bf223e49a29a7]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (Forestry) (*Phytophthora ramorum*) (Great Britain) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3213](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3213)) (“the Order”) so as to implement Commission Decision [2007/201/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0201) amending Decision [2002/757/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0757) on provisional emergency phytosanitary measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Phytophthora ramorum* Werres, De Cock & Man in ‘t Veld sp.nov (O.J. No. L 90, 30.3.2007, p. 83). The principal change is to amend the description of susceptible material to which the Order applies (article 2(2) and (4)).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
