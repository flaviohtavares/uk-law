# The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3225

EDUCATION, Wales

The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 2) Order 2007

Made

14th November 2007

Coming into force

1st December 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of November 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

The Welsh Ministers have, in accordance with section 19(6) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001], advised the Secretary of State for Wales on a recommendation to be made to Her Majesty as to the exercise of the power conferred by section 19(1) of that Act.

The Secretary of State for Wales has made a recommendation to Her Majesty in accordance with that advice.

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 19(1) of the Education Act 2005 and in accordance with that recommendation, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 2) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st December 2007.

**2.** Miss Susan Lewis is hereby re-appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a period of two months commencing on 1st December 2007.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order re-appoints Susan Lewis to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a further period of two months from 1st December 2007. Her present appointment under the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2007 ends on 30th November 2007.
