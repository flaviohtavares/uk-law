# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Conclusion of the proceedings

(1) When each charge on the charge sheet has been disposed of, the president of the board shall announce in open court that the proceedings are concluded.

(2) The judge advocate shall dissolve the court.
