# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Delegation of the court administration officer’s functions

The court administration officer may delegate any of his functions to a member of the Military Court Service.
