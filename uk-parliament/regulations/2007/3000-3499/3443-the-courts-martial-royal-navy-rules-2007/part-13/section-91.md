# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Revocations and transitional proceedings

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Rules set out in Schedule 6 to these Rules are hereby revoked.

(2) The Rules set out in Schedule 6 shall continue to apply in relation to any trial that commenced before 1st January 2008 until the court is dissolved.

(3) The revocations shall not affect the validity of anything done under those Rules any in relation to any proceedings pending at the commencement of these Rules.
