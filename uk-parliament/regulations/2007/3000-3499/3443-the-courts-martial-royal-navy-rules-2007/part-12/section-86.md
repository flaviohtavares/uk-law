# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Reasons

Where the reviewing authority completes a review in accordance with section 70 of the Act, it shall—

- (a) if a petition has been presented in accordance with section 70(1) of the Act, or
- (b) if it exercises any of its powers under section 71 of the Act,

give reasons for its decision.
