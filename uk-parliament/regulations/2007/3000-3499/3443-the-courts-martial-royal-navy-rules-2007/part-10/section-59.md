# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Opening address

Before calling the witnesses for the prosecution, the prosecuting authority may make an opening address.
