# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Witnesses for the defence

Except with the leave of the judge advocate, if the accused elects to give evidence he shall be called before any other witness for the defence.
