# The Courts-Martial (Royal Navy) Rules 2007, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Expert evidence

(1) Expert evidence shall not be adduced at a trial without the leave of the judge advocate unless the party proposing to rely on it has served on every other party and the court administration officer, not less than 14 days before the date appointed for the trial, a statement of the substance of the expert evidence.

(2) The statement referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in writing unless every other party consents to it being made orally.
