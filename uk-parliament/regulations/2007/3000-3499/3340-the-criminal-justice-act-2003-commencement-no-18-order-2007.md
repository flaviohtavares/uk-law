# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3340 (C. 138)

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2007

Made

26th November 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 336(3) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2007.

(2) The Order extends to Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement on 3rd December 2007

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 shall come into force on 3rd December 2007—

- (a) section 328 and 332;
- (b) Schedule 35, paragraphs 1, 6(1), 6(2) and 6(3), 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12(a) and 12(b); and
- (c) Schedule 37, Part 11.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

26th November 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences for Northern Ireland provisions in the Criminal Justice Act 2003 that make amendments to Part V of the Police Act 1997 dealing with criminal records.

(This note is not part of the Order)
