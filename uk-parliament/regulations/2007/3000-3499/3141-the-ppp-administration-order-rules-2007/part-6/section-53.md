# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 53

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Court records[^key-ba3d2e71074072916d9e77cbd7196de0]

*Extent: E+W*

The court shall keep records of all PPP administration proceedings, and shall cause to be entered in the records the taking of any step in the proceedings, and such decisions of the court in relation thereto, as the court thinks fit.
