# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 68

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. Affidavit in support of application[^key-51ad386e34a5e4625f8bd0cd2a510fd7]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under rule 67(3) shall be supported by an affidavit of a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
