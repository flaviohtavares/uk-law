# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 66

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Introductory[^c-a60fd971021538012281bf2d8744e50a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The rules in this Chapter apply where in PPP administration proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who is incapable of managing and administering his property and affairs either—

- (a) by reason of a lack of capacity (within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005)[^f00017]; or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “**the incapacitated person**”.
