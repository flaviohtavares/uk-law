# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 79

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 79. Payment into court[^key-b20fdae82ae6b27139175ef7c054fd11]

*Extent: E+W*

The CPR relating to payment into and out of court of money lodged in court as security for costs apply to money lodged in court under these Rules.
