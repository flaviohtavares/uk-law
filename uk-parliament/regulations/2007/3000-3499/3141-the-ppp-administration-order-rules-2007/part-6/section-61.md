# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 61

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Costs paid otherwise than out of the assets of the PPP company[^c-4da54b00297fc57b401cb41a8df3656f]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the amount of costs is decided by detailed assessment under an order of the court directing that the costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the assets of the PPP company, the costs officer shall note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
