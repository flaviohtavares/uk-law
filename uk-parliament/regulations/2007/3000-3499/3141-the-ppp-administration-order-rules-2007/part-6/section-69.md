> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Service of notices following appointment[^key-bd5601cba2b281a2321bcab1ce622c26]

*Extent: E+W*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under rule 67 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
