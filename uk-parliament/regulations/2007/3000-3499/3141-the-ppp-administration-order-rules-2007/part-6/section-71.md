> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Procedure on appeal[^key-f15e4b00dae6980911a977e25d2c5a40]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the procedure and practice of the Supreme Court relating to appeals to the Court of Appeal apply to appeals in PPP administration proceedings.

(2) In relation to any appeal to a single judge of the High Court under Rule 70 above, any reference in the CPR to the Court of Appeal is replaced by a reference to that judge and any reference to the registrar of civil appeals is replaced by a reference to the registrar of the High Court who deals with PPP administration proceedings.

(3) In PPP administration proceedings, the procedure under CPR Part 52 (appeals to the Court of Appeal) is by an application in Form PPP20 and not by application notice.
