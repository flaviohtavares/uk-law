# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 85

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Retention of proxies[^key-7942906f930e5a1b144a01b14612e981]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chairman of the meeting.

(2) The chairman shall deliver the proxies forthwith after the meeting to the special PPP administrator, where that is someone other than himself.
