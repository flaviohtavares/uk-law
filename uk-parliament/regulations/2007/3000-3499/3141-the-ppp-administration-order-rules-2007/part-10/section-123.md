# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 123

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. “The appropriate fee”[^key-52b0304fb3567b9d4da893f9563e4ab9]

*Extent: E+W*

“**The appropriate fee**” means 15 pence per A4 or A5 page and 30 pence per A3 page.
