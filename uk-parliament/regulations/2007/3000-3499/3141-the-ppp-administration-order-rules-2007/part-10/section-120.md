# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 120

> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. “Petition”[^key-8f79ab95a35fe3c749c4d1fad12ff012]

*Extent: E+W*

“**Petition**” means a petition for a PPP administration order to be made in relation to a PPP company.
