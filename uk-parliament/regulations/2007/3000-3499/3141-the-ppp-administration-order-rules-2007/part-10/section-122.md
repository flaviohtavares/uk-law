> Text as at 2018-02-26.

## 122. “PPP administration proceedings”[^c-2d1752a5d120a7400a6b3dfd9993bee0]

*Extent: E+W*

“PPP administration proceedings” means any proceedings under sections 220 to 224 of, and Schedule 14 to, the 1999 Act.
