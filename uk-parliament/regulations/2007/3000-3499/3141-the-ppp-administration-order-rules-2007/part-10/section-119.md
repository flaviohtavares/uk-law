# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 119

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Notice to joint special PPP administrators[^key-329e475c721293982ab401266e316abf]

*Extent: E+W*

Where 2 or more persons are acting jointly as the special PPP administrator in any proceedings, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
