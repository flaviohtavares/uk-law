# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 118

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Notice, etc. to solicitors[^key-c6181b492c1fa184638d2f31773ddc35]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in PPP administration proceedings a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given to a person, it may, if he has indicated that his solicitor is authorised to accept service on his behalf, be given instead to the solicitor.
