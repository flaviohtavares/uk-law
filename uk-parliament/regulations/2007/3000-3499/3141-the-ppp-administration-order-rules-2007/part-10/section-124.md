# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 124

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Expressions used generally[^key-0a6a9f8952fa39a7624e4a60048cabfd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) “**File in court**” means deliver to the court for filing.

(2) “**The Gazette**” means The London Gazette.

(3) “**Business day**” means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday or a day which is a bank holiday in any part of Great Britain under or by virtue of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971[^f00019].
