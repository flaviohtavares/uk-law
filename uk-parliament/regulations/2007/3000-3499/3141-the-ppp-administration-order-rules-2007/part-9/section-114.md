## 114. Punishment of offences[^key-64a853a2582a16c1953d4bebb84bacb3]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 431 (summary proceedings) of the 1986 Act, as it applies to England and Wales, has effect in relation to offences under these Rules as to offences under that Act.
