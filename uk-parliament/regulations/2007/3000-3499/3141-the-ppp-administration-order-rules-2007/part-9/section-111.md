# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 111

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111. Right to have list of creditors[^key-ade006dc6ff00a75d2d5bd0d6745c36f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In any PPP administration proceedings a creditor who under these Rules has the right to inspect documents on the court file also has the right to require the special PPP administrator to furnish him with a list of the creditors of the PPP company and the amounts of their respective debts.

This does not apply if a statement of the PPP company’s affairs has been filed in court.

(2) The special PPP administrator, on being required by any person to furnish that list, shall send it to him, but is entitled to charge the appropriate fee for doing so.
