# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 104

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 104. General provisions as to service and notice[^c-6bd6c2116a5fd61c6f142ee6c30a9d0b]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to rules 103, 105(1) and 117, CPR Part 6 (service of documents) applies as regards any matter relating to the service of documents and the giving of notice in PPP administration proceedings.
