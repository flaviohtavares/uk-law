> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. Evidence of proceedings at meeting[^c-046317b5b9ea4757ac72fd7e30def6c0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A minute of proceedings at a meeting (held under the 1986 Act or these Rules) of the creditors or the members of a PPP company called or summoned by the special PPP administrator, signed by a person describing himself as, or appearing to be, the chairman of that meeting is admissible in PPP administration proceedings without further proof.

(2) The minute is prime facie evidence that—

- (a) the meeting was duly convened and held;
- (b) all resolutions passed at the meeting were duly passed; and
- (c) all proceedings at the meeting duly took place.
