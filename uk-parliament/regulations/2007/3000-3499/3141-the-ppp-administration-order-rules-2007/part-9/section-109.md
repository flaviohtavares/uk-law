# The PPP Administration Order Rules 2007, section 109

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 109. Charge for copy documents[^key-2cf5b1f384eb3624a4e391d273b3b03a]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in PPP administration proceedings the special PPP administrator is requested by a creditor or member to supply copies of any documents, he is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee in respect of the supply of the documents.
