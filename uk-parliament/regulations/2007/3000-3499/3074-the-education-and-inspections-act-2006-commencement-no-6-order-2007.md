# The Education and Inspections Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3074 (C. 123)

Education, england

The Education and Inspections Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2007

Made

25th October 2007

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 181 and 188(3) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education and Inspections Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2007.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Education and Inspections Act 2006.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st January 2008

Section 5 of the Act comes into force in respect of special schools[^f00002] in relation to the local education authorities listed in Part 1 of the Schedule on 1st January 2008.

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 1st April 2008

Section 5 of the Act comes into force in respect of special schools in relation to the local education authorities listed in Part 2A of the Schedule and, in respect of primary schools[^f00003], in relation to the local education authorities listed in Part 2B of the Schedule on 1st April 2008.

Jim Knight

Minister of State

Department for Children, Schools and Families

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the sixth commencement order made under the Education and Inspections Act 2006 (“the Act”) and contains provisions to be commenced on the 1st January 2008 and 1st April 2008.

Articles 2 and 3 commence section 5 of the Act. This section imposes a duty upon local education authorities in England to appoint a school improvement partner in relation to each school they maintain. It is commenced on 1st January 2008 in relation to special schools in the local authority areas listed in Part 1 of the Schedule, and on 1st April 2008 in respect of the special schools and primary schools listed in Parts 2A and B of the Schedule. With effect from 1st April 2008 the section will be in force in relation to all maintained schools in all local education authorities.
