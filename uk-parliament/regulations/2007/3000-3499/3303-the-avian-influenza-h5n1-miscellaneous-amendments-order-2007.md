# The Avian Influenza (H5N1) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2007

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2006/3247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3247) & 3249 and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those Statutory Instruments.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Avian Influenza (H5N1) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2007; and
- (b) comes into force on 23rd November 2007.

## 2. Amendment

(1) After article 14(2)(j) of the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) (England) Order 2006[^f00003] insert—

> - (ja) raw petfood, within the meaning of paragraph 48 of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774), which complies with the requirements in Part B of Chapter II of Annex VIII to that Regulation;

.

(2) After paragraph 13(2)(j) of Schedule 1 to the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) Order 2006[^f00004] insert—

> - (ja) raw petfood, within the meaning of paragraph 48 of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774), which complies with the requirements in Part B of Chapter II of Annex VIII to that Regulation;

.

Jonathan Shaw

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

22nd November 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) (England) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/3247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3247)) and the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) (England) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/3249](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3249)) to add raw petfood to the lists of by-products which can be moved under the authority of a licence issued by a veterinary inspector under each Order.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
