# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 259

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>

## 259. Amendment of section 46

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 46 (grant or refusal of registration under Part 3)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “application” insert “ under section 45(1) of this Act ”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(c), for “subsection (4) below” substitute “ section 46B of this Act ”; and
- (c) omit subsections (4) to (7).
