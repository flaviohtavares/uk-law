# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 258

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 258. Amendment of section 45

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 45 (applications for registration under Part 3)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “Part” insert “ , other than for registration in the visiting European part of the register, ”; and
- (b) after that subsection insert—

  > (1A) An application is not required for registration in the visiting European part of the register.
