# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 264

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Amendment of section 57

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 57 (power of Council to make rules), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) Rules made by the Council under subsection (2) may not make provision for the payment of fees in connection with registration in the visiting European part of the register.
