# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 55

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Amendment of article 23

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 23 (appeals against decisions on inclusion in the Registers)—

- (a) after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) A decision by the Registrar of the GMC to refuse to register a person—
  >
  > - (a) in the General Practitioner Register pursuant to article 10(2)(d), or
  > - (b) in the Specialist Register pursuant to article 13(2)(d),
  >
  > shall also be treated as an appealable registration decision under Schedule 3A to the Medical Act and the procedure in that Schedule shall accordingly apply.

  ; and
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “article 16(1) within the time specified in article 16(2) or, where applicable, article 16(4)” substitute “ article 16(2) within the time specified in article 16(3) ”.
