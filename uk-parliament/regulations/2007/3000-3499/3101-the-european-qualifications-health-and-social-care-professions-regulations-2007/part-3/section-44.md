# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 44

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Amendment of article 12

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 12 [^c18972861] (acquired rights of general practitioners), for paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) For the purposes of article 30(1) of the Directive (requirement for every relevant European State to determine the acquired rights that it recognises for the purpose of pursuing general medical practice in the framework of its national social security system without the evidence of formal qualifications listed in Annex V, point 5.1.4 of the Directive), a person has an acquired right to practise as a general practitioner in the United Kingdom if he falls within one of the categories of persons set out in Schedule 6.

.
