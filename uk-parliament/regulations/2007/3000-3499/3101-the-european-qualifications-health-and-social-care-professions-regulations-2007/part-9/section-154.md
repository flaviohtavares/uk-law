# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 154

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Amendment of the Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004 [^c18973851], in regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of “dentist” omit the words “or entered in the list of visiting EEC practitioners under Schedule 4 to that Act”.
