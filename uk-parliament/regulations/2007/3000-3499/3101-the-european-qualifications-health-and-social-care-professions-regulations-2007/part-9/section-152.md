# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 152

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Amendment of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 [^c18973811], in section 37 (interpretation), in subsection (1), in the definition of “dentist”[^c18973821] omit the words “or entered in the list of visiting EEA practitioners under Schedule 4 to that Act”.

## Annotations

[^c18973811]: Marginal citation: [1971 c.38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38).

[^c18973821]: Marginal citation: The definition of “dentist” was amended by the Dentists Act 1984, section 54(1), Schedule 5, paragraph 3, and by [S.I. 1996/1496](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1496).
