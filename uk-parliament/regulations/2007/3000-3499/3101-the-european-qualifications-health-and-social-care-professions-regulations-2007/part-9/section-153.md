# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 153

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Amendment of the Poisons Act 1972

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Poisons Act 1972 [^c18973831], in section 11 (interpretation), in subsection (2), in the definition of “dentist”[^c18973841] omit the words “or a person entered in the list of visiting EEA practitioners under Schedule 4 to that Act”.

## Annotations

[^c18973831]: Marginal citation: [1972 c.66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/66).

[^c18973841]: Marginal citation: The definition of “dentist” was amended by the Dentists Act 1984, section 54(1), Schedule 5, paragraph 4, and by [S.I. 1996/1496](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1496).
