# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 151

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Amendment of the Medicines Act 1968

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Medicines Act 1968 [^c18973791], in section 132 (general interpretation provisions), in subsection (1), in the definition of “dentist”[^c18973801] omit the words “or entered in the list of visiting EEA practitioners under Schedule 4 to that Act”.
