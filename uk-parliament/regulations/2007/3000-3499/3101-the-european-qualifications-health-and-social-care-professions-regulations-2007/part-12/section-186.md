# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 186

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. Amendment of section 29

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 29 [^c18974091] (provision as to death or bankruptcy of registered optician), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) In subsections (1) and (2)—
>
> - “**registered optometrist**” does not include a person registered in the register maintained under section 8B(1)(a);
> - “**registered dispensing optician**” does not include a person registered in the register maintained under section 8B(1)(b).

.
