# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 181

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Amendment of section 10

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 10 [^c18974041] (general provisions as to registers and lists)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(c), for “prescribing” substitute “ subject to subsection (1ZA), prescribing ”; and
- (b) after subsection (1) insert—

  > (1ZA) Rules under subsection (1) may not make provision for the payment of fees in connection with registration in a register maintained under section 8B.

  .
