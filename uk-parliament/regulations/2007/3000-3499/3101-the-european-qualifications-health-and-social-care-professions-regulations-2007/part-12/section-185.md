# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 185

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Amendment of section 28

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 28 [^c18974081] (penalty for pretending to be registered etc), in subsection (1)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “registered in the register of optometrists” substitute “ a registered optometrist ”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “registered in the register of dispensing opticians” substitute “ a registered dispensing optician ”; and
- (c) in paragraph (c), for “registered in the register of optometrists maintained under section 7 above” substitute “ a registered optometrist ”.
