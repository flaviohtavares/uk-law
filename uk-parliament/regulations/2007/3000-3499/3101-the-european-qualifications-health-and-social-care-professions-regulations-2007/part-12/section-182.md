# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 182

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Amendment of section 10A

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 10A [^c18974051] (insurance for individual registrants and persons applying for their name to be registered), after subsection (6) insert—

> (6A) Subsections (1) to (6) do not apply in relation to persons who are registered, or who are seeking registration, in a register maintained under section 8B.
