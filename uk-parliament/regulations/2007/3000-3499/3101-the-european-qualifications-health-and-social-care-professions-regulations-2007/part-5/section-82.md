# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 82

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Amendment of article 22

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 22 [^c18973041] (entitlement to registration in the Register of Pharmacy Technicians), in paragraphs (1) and (2), for “the Register of Pharmacy Technicians” substitute “ Part 1 or 2 of the Register of Pharmacy Technicians ”.

## Annotations

[^c18973041]: Marginal citation: Article 22 has not yet been brought into force.
