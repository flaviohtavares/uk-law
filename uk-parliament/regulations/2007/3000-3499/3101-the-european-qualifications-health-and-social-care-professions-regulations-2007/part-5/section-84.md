# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 84

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Amendment of article 25

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 25 [^c18973061] (specific obligations of the Society in respect of pharmacy technicians' and prospective pharmacy technicians' education, training and the acquisition of experience), in paragraph (f)—

- (a) after “article 22(1)(a)(iii) and (iv),” insert “ for the purposes of rules under article 39(1), ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (v), after “Part 2” insert “ or Part 3 ”.
