## 74. Amendment of article 11

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 11 (entitlement to registration in the Register of Pharmacists)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a)(i), after “article 12(1)” insert “ and (1A) ”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3)(b), for “the Register of Pharmacists” substitute “ Part 1 or 2 of the Register of Pharmacists ”.
