# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 73

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Amendment of article 10

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 10 (the Register of Pharmacists), for paragraph (2)(b) substitute—

> - (b) shall be divided into three parts, and—
>   - (i) the first part shall be known as “**Part 1 – Practising Pharmacists**”,
>   - (ii) the second part shall be known as “**Part 2 – Non-practising Pharmacists**”, and
>   - (iii) the third part shall be known as “**Part 3 – Visiting Pharmacists from relevant European States**”.
