# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 92

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Amendment of article 40

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 40 (fees in connection with registration)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “The Council” substitute “ Subject to paragraph (1A), the Council ”; and
- (b) after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) Rules under paragraph (1) shall not provide for fees in respect of registration in Part 3 of one of the Society's registers.
