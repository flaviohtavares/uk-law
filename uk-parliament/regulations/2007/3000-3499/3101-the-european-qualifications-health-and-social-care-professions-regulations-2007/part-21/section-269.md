# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 269

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 269. Amendment of article 8

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 8 (access to register etc), in paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “EEA State” substitute “ relevant European State ” ; and
- (b) for “the relevant Directive” substitute “ the relevant provisions of Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0036) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7th September 2005 on the recognition of professional qualifications [^c18974591], as those provisions are amended from time to time ”.
