# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 70

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Amendment of the General Medical Council (Fitness to Practise) Rules 2004

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the General Medical Council (Fitness to Practise) Rules 2004 [^c18972981], after rule 17 (procedure before a FTP Panel) insert—

> **17A. Notification of determination of FTP Panel**
>
> The Registrar shall serve notification of the determination of the FTP Panel, and the manner in which the FTP Panel has disposed of the case, on the maker of the allegation (if any).
