# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 66

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Amendment of the European Primary Medical Qualifications Regulations 1996

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the European Primary Medical Qualifications Regulations 1996 [^c18972941], regulation 2 (the competent authority for certain EEA purposes), regulation 5 (visiting EEA practitioners), Schedule 1 (primary European qualifications) and paragraphs 7, 9(c), 10, 11, 12, 13(2)(a) and (b) and 14(3) of Schedule 2 (minor and consequential amendments) are revoked.
