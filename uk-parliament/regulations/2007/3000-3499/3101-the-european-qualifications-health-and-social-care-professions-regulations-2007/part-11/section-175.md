# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 175

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 175. The Nurses and Midwives (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2004

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Nurses and Midwives (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2004 [^c18973931], after article 7 add—

> **8. Annotations denoting visiting nurses or midwives from relevant European States**
>
> The entries in the register are to include such annotation as the Council considers appropriate to denote that a registrant is a visiting nurse or midwife from a relevant European State [^c18973941].

.

## Annotations

[^c18973931]: Marginal citation: [S.I. 2004/1765](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1765); there are no relevant amending instruments.

[^c18973941]: Marginal citation: “Visiting nurse or midwife from a relevant European State” is defined in Schedule 4 to the Order as a nurse or midwife registered in exercise of entitlement under article 39A of or Schedule 2A to the Order.
