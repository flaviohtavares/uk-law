# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 157

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Amendment of article 7

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 7 (the register: supplemental provisions), after paragraph (3) add—

> (4) Rules under this article shall not provide for fees to be charged in respect of a person's registration in exercise of entitlement under article 39A or Schedule 2A (visiting nurses and midwives from relevant European States).
