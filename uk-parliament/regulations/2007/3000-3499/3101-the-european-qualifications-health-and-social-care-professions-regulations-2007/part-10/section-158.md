# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 158

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Amendment of article 8

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 8 (access to register etc), in paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “EEA State” substitute “ relevant European State ”; and
- (b) for “the relevant Directive” substitute “ the relevant provisions of the Directive ”.
