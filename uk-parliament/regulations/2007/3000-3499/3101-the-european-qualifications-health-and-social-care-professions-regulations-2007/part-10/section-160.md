# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 160

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. Amendment of article 10

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 10 (renewal of registration and readmission)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “Where a person” substitute “ Subject to paragraph (6), where a person ”; and
- (b) after paragraph (5) add—

  > (6) This article does not apply to a visiting nurse or midwife from a relevant European State.
