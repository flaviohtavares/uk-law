# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 217

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 217. Amendment of the General Osteopathic Council (Recognition of Qualifications) Rules 2000

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the General Osteopathic Council (Recognition of Qualifications) Rules 2000 [^c18974381], in rule 4 (non-United Kingdom qualifications)—

- (a) the existing provision shall be numbered as paragraph (1); and
- (b) after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (2) In considering whether an exempt person [^c18974391] has reached the required standard of proficiency for the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), the Registrar—
  >
  > - (a) shall take into account all his relevant qualifications, knowledge and experience, wherever acquired; and
  > - (b) if the applicant holds a qualification which—
  >   - (i) was granted otherwise than in a relevant European State, but
  >   - (ii) has been accepted by a relevant European State, other than the United Kingdom, as qualifying him to practise as an osteopath in that State,
  >
  >   shall take that acceptance into account.

  .
