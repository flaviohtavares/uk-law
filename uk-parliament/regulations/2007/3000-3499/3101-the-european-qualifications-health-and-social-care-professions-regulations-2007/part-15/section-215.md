# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 215

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Amendment of the General Osteopathic Council (Registration) Rules 1998

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the General Osteopathic Council (Registration) Rules 1998 [^c18974351]—

- (a) in rule 3 (the form of the register)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1), for “European Economic Area State” (in both places) substitute “ relevant European State ”,
  - (ii) in paragraph (3)—
    - (aa) in sub-paragraph (b), for “fully registered or conditionally registered” substitute “ fully registered, conditionally registered or temporarily registered [^c18974361] ”,
    - (bb) in sub-paragraph (d), for “ “full” or “conditional”” substitute “ “full”, “conditional” or “temporary” ”, and
  - (iii) in paragraph (4), omit sub-paragraph (b); and
- (b) in rule 7 (issue and form of certificates), in paragraph (2)(a)(iv), after “fully registered osteopath” insert “ , a temporarily registered osteopath ”.

## Annotations

[^c18974351]: Marginal citation: Scheduled to [S.I. 1998/1328](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1328), amended by [S.I. 2004/1947](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1947).

[^c18974361]: Marginal citation: “Temporarily registered osteopath” is defined in section 41 of the Osteopaths Act 1993.
