## 101. Amendment of the Gender Recognition (Disclosure of Information) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) (No. 2) Order 2005

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Gender Recognition (Disclosure of Information) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) (No. 2) Order 2005 [^c18973221], in article 5 (disclosure for medical purposes), in paragraph (3)(c), after “Part 1” insert “ or 3 ”.
