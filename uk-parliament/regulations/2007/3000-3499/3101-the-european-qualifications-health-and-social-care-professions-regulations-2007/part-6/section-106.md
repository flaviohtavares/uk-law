# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 106

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Amendment of the Controlled Drugs (Supervision of Management and Use) Regulations 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Controlled Drugs (Supervision of Management and Use) Regulations 2006 [^c18973291], in regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), for the definition of “registered pharmacist” substitute—

> “**registered pharmacist**” means a person registered in Part 1 or 3 of the Register of Pharmacists maintained under article 10(1) of the Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians Order 2007 [^c18973301];

.
