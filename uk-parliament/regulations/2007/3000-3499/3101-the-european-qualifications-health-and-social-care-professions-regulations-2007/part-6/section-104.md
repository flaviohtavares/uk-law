## 104. Amendment of the National Health Service Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service Act 2006 [^c18973251], in section 275 (interpretation), in subsection (1), in the definition of “registered pharmacist”[^c18973261], after “Part 1” insert “ or 3 ”.
