## 105. Amendment of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006 [^c18973271], in section 206 (interpretation), in subsection (1), in the definition of “registered pharmacist”[^c18973281], after “Part 1” insert “ or 3 ”.
