# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 191

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 191. Amendment of the Medicines (Contact Lens Fluids and Other Substances) (Exemption from Licences) Order 1979

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Medicines (Contact Lens Fluids and Other Substances) (Exemption from Licences) Order 1979 [^c18974151], in article 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation), in paragraph (2), for the definition of “optician” substitute—

> “**optician**” means a person whose name is entered in the register of optometrists maintained under section 7(a) of the Opticians Act 1989, or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act.

.
