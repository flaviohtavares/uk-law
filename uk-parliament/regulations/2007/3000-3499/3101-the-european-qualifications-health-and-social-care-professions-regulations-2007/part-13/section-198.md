# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 198

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 198. Amendment of the Primary Care Trusts (Consultation on Establishment, Dissolution and Transfer of Staff) Regulations 1999

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Primary Care Trusts (Consultation on Establishment, Dissolution and Transfer of Staff) Regulations 1999 [^c18974231], in regulation 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation), in paragraph (2), in paragraph (d) of the definition of “health professional”, after “section 7” insert “ or 8B ”.
