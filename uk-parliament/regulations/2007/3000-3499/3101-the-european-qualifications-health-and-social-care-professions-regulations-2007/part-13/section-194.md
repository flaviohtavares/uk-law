# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 194

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 194. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 1986

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 1986 [^c18974181], in regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of “optician”, after “Opticians Act 1989” insert “ or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act, ”.
