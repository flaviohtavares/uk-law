# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 204

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. Amendment of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006 [^c18974301], in section 206 (interpretation), in subsection (1), in the definition of “optometrist”, after “Act 1989 (c.44)” insert “ , or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act, ”.
