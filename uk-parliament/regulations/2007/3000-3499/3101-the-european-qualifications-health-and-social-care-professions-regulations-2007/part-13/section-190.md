# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 190

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. Amendment of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 [^c18974141], in section 108 (interpretation and construction), in subsection (1), in the definition of “ophthalmic optician”, after “Opticians Act 1989” insert “ , or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act, ”.
