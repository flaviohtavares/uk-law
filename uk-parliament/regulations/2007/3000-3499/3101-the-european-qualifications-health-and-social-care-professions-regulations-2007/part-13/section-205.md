# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 205

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 [^c18974311], in regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definitions of “optometrist” and “register”, after “Opticians Act 1989” insert “ or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act ”.
