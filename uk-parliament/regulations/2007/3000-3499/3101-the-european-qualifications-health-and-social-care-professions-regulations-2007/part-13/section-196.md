# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 196

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 196. Amendment of the General Optical Council (Testing of Sight by Persons Training as Ophthalmic Opticians) Rules 1993

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the General Optical Council (Testing of Sight by Persons Training as Ophthalmic Opticians) Rules 1993 [^c18974201], in rule 4 (classes of persons training as optometrists), in paragraph (c)—

- (a) for “Regulation 6 of the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 1991” substitute “ the General Systems Regulations [^c18974211] ”; and
- (b) for “that regulation” substitute “ those Regulations ”.

## Annotations

[^c18974201]: Marginal citation: Scheduled to [S.I. 1994/70](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/70); relevant amendments were made by [S.I. 2005/848](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/848).

[^c18974211]: Marginal citation: The “General Systems Regulations” are defined in section 36 of the Opticians Act 1989.
