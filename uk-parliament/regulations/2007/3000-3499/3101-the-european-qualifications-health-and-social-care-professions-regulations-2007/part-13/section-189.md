# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 189

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Amendment of the National Health Service Act 1977

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service Act 1977 [^c18974121], in section 128 (interpretation and construction), in subsection (1), in the definition of “ophthalmic optician”, after “Opticians Act 1989” insert “ , or in the register of visiting optometrists from relevant European States maintained under section 8B(1)(a) of that Act [^c18974131], ”.

## Annotations

[^c18974121]: Marginal citation: [1977 c.49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/49). The definition of “ophthalmic optician” was substituted by [S.I. 2005/848](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/848), and is prospectively amended by the [Health Act 2006 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/28), [Schedule 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/28/schedule/8), [paragraph 24(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/28/paragraph/24/b). The National Health Service Act 1977 (“**the 1977 Act**”) was repealed by the [National Health Service (Consequential Provisions) Act 2006 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/43) (“**the 2006 Act**”). However, by virtue of section 8(4)(q) and (5) of the 2006 Act, the repeal of the definition of “ophthalmic optician” in the 1977 Act has not yet come into force.

[^c18974131]: Marginal citation: Section 8B was inserted by [S.I. 2007/3101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3101)
