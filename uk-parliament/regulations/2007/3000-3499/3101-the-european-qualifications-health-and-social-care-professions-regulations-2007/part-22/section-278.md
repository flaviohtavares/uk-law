# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 278

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 278. Amendment of the Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003 [^c18974651], after article 6 [^c18974661] add—

> **7. Annotations denoting visiting health professionals from relevant European States**
>
> The entries in the register are to include such annotation as the Council considers appropriate to denote that a registrant is a visiting health professional from a relevant European State [^c18974671].

.

## Annotations

[^c18974671]: Marginal citation: “Visiting health professional from a relevant European State” is defined in the Order as meaning a person registered in the register in exercise of entitlement under article 13A of the Order.
