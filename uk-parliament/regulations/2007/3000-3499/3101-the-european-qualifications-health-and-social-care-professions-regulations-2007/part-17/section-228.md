# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 228

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 228. Amendment of the General Chiropractic Council (Professional Indemnity Insurance) Rules 1999

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the General Chiropractic Council (Professional Indemnity Insurance) Rules 1999 [^c18974441], in rule 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) the existing provision shall be numbered as paragraph (1); and
- (b) after paragraph (1) add—

  > (2) These Rules do not apply in relation to temporarily registered chiropractors.

  .
