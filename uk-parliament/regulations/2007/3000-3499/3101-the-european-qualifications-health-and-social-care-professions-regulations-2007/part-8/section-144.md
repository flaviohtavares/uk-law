# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 144

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Amendment of regulation 13

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 13 [^c18973781] (other specialties), in paragraph (2)(a), for “outside the EEA has been accepted by another EEA State” substitute “ otherwise than in a relevant European State has been accepted by a relevant European State, other than the United Kingdom, ”.
