# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 116

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Amendment of section 19

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 19 [^c18973421] (regulations with respect to the register)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “The Council” substitute “ Subject to subsection (1A), the Council ”; and
- (b) after subsection (1) insert—

  > (1A) Regulations under this section shall not prescribe fees in respect of registration in the list mentioned in section 14(1A)(c).

  .
