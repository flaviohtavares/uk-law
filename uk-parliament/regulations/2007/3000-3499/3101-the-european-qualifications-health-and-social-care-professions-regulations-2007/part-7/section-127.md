# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 127

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Amendment of section 38

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 38 (prohibition on practice of dentistry by laymen), in subsection (1) [^c18973541], for “a registered dentist, a registered dental care professional or a visiting EEA practitioner entered in the list of such practitioners” substitute “ a registered dentist or a registered dental care professional ”.
