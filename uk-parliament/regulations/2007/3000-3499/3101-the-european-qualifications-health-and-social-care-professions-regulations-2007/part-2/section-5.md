# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 5

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendment of section 5

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 5 [^c18972551] (general functions of the Education Committee in relation to medical education in the United Kingdom)—

- (a) in subsection (2A), for “article 23 of Directive [93/16/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0016)” substitute “ article 24 of the Directive (basic medical training) ”;
- (b) omit subsection (2B); and
- (c) in subsection (4), for the definition of “Directive [93/16/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0016)” substitute—

  > “**the Directive**” means Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0036) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7th September 2005 on the recognition of professional qualifications (OJ No. L255, 30.09.2005, p.22) [^c18972561], and any reference in this Act to the Directive or to any provision of the Directive is a reference to the Directive, or to that provision of the Directive, as amended from time to time;

  .
