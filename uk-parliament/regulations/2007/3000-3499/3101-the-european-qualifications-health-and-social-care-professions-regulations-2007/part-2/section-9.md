# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2007, section 9

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Amendment of section 15A

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 15A [^c18972591] (provisional registration for EEA nationals)—

- (a) in the heading, after “EEA nationals” insert “ etc ”;
- (b) in subsections (1), (2) and (3)(a), for “an EEA State” substitute “ a relevant European State ”;
- (c) in subsection (3)(b), for “a right conferred by article 11 of Regulation [(EEC) No 1612/68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1968/1612), or any other enforceable Community right” substitute “ any enforceable Community right ”; and
- (d) in subsection (5), for “Article 23, paragraph 1(a), (b) and (c) of Directive [93/16/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0016)” substitute “ paragraph 3(a), (b) and (c) of article 24 of the Directive (basic medical training)& ”.
