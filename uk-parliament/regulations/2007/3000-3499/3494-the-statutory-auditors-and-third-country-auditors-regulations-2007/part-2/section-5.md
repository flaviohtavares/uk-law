# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2007, section 5

> Text as at 2016-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Appropriate qualifications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1219(1) of the Companies Act 2006 (appropriate qualifications)—

- (a) after paragraph (d) insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (e);
- (c) in paragraph (f), for “overseas” substitute “ third country ”.
