# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2007, section 32

> Text as at 2016-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Duties of registered third country auditors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) Section 1242 of the Companies Act 2006 (duties of registered third country auditors) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) after “third country auditor” insert “ who audits the accounts of a UK-traded non-EEA company ”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), omit“of traded non-Community companies”.

(3) In subsection (2)(a), for “third country audits” substitute “ audits of accounts of UK-traded non-EEA companies ”.
