## 16. Directions to comply with international obligations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 1254 of the Companies Act 2006 (directions to comply with international obligations) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(a) after “recognised qualifying body,” insert “ the Independent Supervisor ”.

(3) In subsection (3) after “given to” insert “ the Independent Supervisor or ”.
