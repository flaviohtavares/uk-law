# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2007, section 13

> Text as at 2016-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Delegation of functions to an existing body

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1253(5) of the Companies Act 2006 (delegation of functions to an existing body) for “paragraph 21, 22, 23(1) or 24(1) of Schedule 10” substitute “ paragraph 21 to 22B, 23(1) or 24(1) of Schedule 10 ”.
