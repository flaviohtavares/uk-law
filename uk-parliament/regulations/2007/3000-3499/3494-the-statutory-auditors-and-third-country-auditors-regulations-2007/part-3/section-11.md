# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2007, section 11

> Text as at 2016-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Reports of the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After section 1251 of the Companies Act 2006 (fees) insert—

> **Duty of Secretary of State to report on inspections**
>
> **1251A. Duty of the Secretary of State to report on inspections**
>
> The Secretary of State must, at least once in every calendar year, publish a report containing a summary of the results of inspections that are delivered to him—
>
> - (a) by the Independent Supervisor under section 1229(5A);
> - (b) by a recognised supervisory body under paragraph 13(9) of Schedule 10.
