# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2007, section 45

> Text as at 2016-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Revocations and savings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Company Auditors (Examinations) Regulations 1990 [^c19039181] are revoked.

(2) The Company Auditors (Examinations) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1990 [^c19039191] are revoked.

(3) The Companies Act 1989 (Register of Auditors and Information About Audit Firms) Regulations 1991 [^c19039201] are revoked.

(4) The Companies (1990 Order) (Register of Auditors and Information About Audit Firms) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 [^c19039211] are revoked.

(5) The European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) (First General System) Regulations 2005 [^c19039221] are revoked for the purposes of their application to the profession of company auditor, except for the purposes of their application in relation to auditors appointed for financial years beginning before 6th April 2008.
