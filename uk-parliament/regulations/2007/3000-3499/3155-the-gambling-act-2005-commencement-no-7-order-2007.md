# The Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3155 (C. 127)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007

Made

1st November 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 358(1) and (2) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007.

**2.** Sections 214 to 234 of the Gambling Act 2005 shall come into force on 1st December 2007.

Gerry Sutcliffe

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

1st November 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 214 to 234 of the Gambling Act 2005 on 1st December 2007. Those sections provide for the giving of temporary use notices to authorise premises to be used for providing facilities for gambling during the period specified in the notice.
