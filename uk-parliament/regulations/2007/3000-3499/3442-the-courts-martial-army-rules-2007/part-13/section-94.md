# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 2007, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Circumstances not provided for

In any circumstance not provided for by the Act or these Rules such course shall be adopted as appears to the judge advocate best calculated to do justice.
