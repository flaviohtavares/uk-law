# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 2007, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Notifying the court administration officer

(1) The prosecuting authority shall notify the court administration officer of any charge which he has preferred by sending to him a copy of the prosecution papers.

(2) On receipt of a copy of the prosecution papers from the prosecuting authority, the court administration officer shall send a copy of the prosecution papers to the Judge Advocate General.
