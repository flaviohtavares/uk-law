# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 2007, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Further evidence

With the leave of the judge advocate the prosecuting authority may call or recall a witness to give evidence on any matter raised by the accused in his defence which the prosecuting authority could not—

- (a) properly have dealt with before the accused disclosed his defence; or
- (b) reasonably have foreseen.
