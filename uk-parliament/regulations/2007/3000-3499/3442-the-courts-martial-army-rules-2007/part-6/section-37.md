# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 2007, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Preliminary hearing in open court

(1) A preliminary hearing not made under rule 38 shall, unless the judge advocate directs otherwise, take place before the judge advocate in open court.

(2) A preliminary hearing shall be a hearing before the judge advocate sitting alone.
