# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Aids to navigation

(1) In addition to their powers under section 201 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00012] (powers of harbour authorities as local lighthouse authorities) but subject to obtaining any necessary interest in or over land, the Authority may erect or place, alter, discontinue or remove aids to navigation in any place adjacent to the harbour.

(2) The Authority shall not exercise the powers of paragraph (1) without the approval of Trinity House.
