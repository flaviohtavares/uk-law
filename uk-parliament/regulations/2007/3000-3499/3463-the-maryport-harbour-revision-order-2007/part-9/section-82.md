# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Repeals

(1) The enactments specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 5 to this Order are repealed to the extent mentioned in column (3) of that Schedule.

(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of enactments by this Order the Authority may continue and maintain the existing works.
