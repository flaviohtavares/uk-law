# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Saving for Lord of the Manor

Nothing in this Order shall prejudice or derogate from any of the rights, privileges or powers of the Lord or Lady of the Manor of Ellenborough.
