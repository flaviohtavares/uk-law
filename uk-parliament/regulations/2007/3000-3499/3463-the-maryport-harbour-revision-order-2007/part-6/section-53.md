# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Refusal to pay charges for landing place

An officer of the Authority may prevent a vessel from using a landing place provided by the Authority, if the master of the vessel refuses to pay the charges for such use.
