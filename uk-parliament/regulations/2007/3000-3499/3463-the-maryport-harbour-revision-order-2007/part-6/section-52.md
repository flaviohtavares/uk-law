# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Harbourmaster may prevent sailing of vessels

The harbourmaster may prevent the removal or sailing from the harbour of any vessel until evidence has been produced to him of the payment of any charges payable in respect of the vessel, its passengers or the goods transported on it.
