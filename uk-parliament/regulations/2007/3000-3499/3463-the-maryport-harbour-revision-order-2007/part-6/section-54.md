# The Maryport Harbour Revision Order 2007, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Payment of charges on warehoused goods

Notwithstanding article 47, the owner or person having the charge of any goods warehoused, placed, stored or yarded in a warehouse, transit shed or area, store or yard of the Authority shall, before the removal of any of the goods from there and at such date or dates as shall be fixed by the Authority, pay such charges as shall be then due and payable on the goods.
