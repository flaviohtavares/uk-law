# The Public Records (Technology Strategy Board) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3466

public records

The Public Records (Technology Strategy Board) Order 2007

Made

12th December 2007

Laid before Parliament

19th December 2007

Coming into force

1st February 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of December 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by paragraph 3A of Schedule 1 to the Public Records Act 1958[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Public Records (Technology Strategy Board) Order 2007

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st February 2008.

## 2. Amendment to Schedule 1 to the Public Records Act 1958

(1) Schedule 1 to the Public Records Act 1958 (definition of public records) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 2 of the Table at the end of paragraph 3, at the appropriate place insert—

> Technology Strategy Board.

.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Part 2 of the Table at the end of paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the Public Records Act 1958 by adding an entry for the Technology Strategy Board, in order to make the administrative and departmental records of that body public records.
