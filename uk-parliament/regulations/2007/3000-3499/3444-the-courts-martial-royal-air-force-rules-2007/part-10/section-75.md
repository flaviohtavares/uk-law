# The Courts-Martial (Royal Air Force) Rules 2007, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Summing up

After the closing addresses, if any, the judge advocate shall direct the court upon the law relating to the case and summarise the evidence.
