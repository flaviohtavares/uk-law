# The Courts-Martial (Royal Air Force) Rules 2007, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Appointment of court officials

(1) The court administration officer may appoint at any time a person or persons to act as—

- (a) court recorder;
- (b) interpreter,

for the purposes of any proceedings before a court-martial (including a hearing before a judge advocate sitting alone).
