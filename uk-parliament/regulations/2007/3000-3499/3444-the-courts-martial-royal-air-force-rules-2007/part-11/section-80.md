# The Courts-Martial (Royal Air Force) Rules 2007, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Sentencing procedure after guilty plea

(1) This rule applies where the judge advocate has accepted a plea or pleas of guilty.

(2) Before presenting the information required under rule 82, the prosecuting authority shall address the court on the facts of the case.
