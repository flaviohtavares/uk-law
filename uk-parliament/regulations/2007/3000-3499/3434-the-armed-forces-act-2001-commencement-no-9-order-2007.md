# The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.9) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3434 (C. 146)

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.9) Order 2007

Made

*6th December 2007*

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2001[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.9) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 25 of the Armed Forces Act 2001 shall come into force on 1st January 2008.

Derek Twigg

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

6th December 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 25 of the Armed Forces Act 2001. Section 25 inserts new sections into the Army Act [1955 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/18), the Air Force Act [1955 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/19) and the Naval Discipline Act [1957 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1957/53). The new sections make provisions equivalent to that provided for in the civilian system by section 2 of the Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act [1965 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1965/25) for a judge advocate at a court martial to make an order compelling a person not subject to naval, military or air force law to attend court and give evidence.
