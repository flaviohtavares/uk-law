# The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3474

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES, ENGLAND

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) Order 2007

Made

12th December 2007

Coming into force

13th December 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of December 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) Order 2007 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) This Order applies in relation to the discharge of functions by fire and rescue authorities whose areas are in England only.

## 2. Appointment of inspector

Sir Kenneth John Knight is appointed an inspector for the purposes of section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Sir Kenneth John Knight as an inspector under section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
