# The Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3069 (C. 121)

northern ireland

The Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

Made

24th October 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(4) of the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 45 of the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 (Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice) shall come into force on 7<sup>th</sup> November 2007.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

24 October 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 7<sup>th</sup> November 2007 section 45 of the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007.
