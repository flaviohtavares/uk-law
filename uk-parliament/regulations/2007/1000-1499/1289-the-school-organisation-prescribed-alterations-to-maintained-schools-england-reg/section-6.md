# The School Organisation (Prescribed Alterations to Maintained Schools) (England) Regulations 2007, section 6

## 6. Consultation prior to these Regulations[^c-0f56f1fd898b9d0d250e0bd96d68f10b]

*Extent: E+W*

If before May 25<sup>th</sup> 2007 any action was taken which (had it been taken on or after that day) would to any extent have satisfied the requirements of paragraph 5 of Part 2 of Schedule 1, paragraph 27 of Part 2 of Schedule 3 and paragraph 27 of Part 2 of Schedule 5, those requirements are to that extent to be treated as satisfied.
