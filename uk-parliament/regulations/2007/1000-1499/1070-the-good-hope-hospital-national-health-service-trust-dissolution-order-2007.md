# The Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1070

National Health Service, England

The Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007

Made

29th March 2007

Coming into force

8th April 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 273(1) of, and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust following the completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 8<sup>th</sup> April 2007.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust established by the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] is dissolved, and accordingly that Order is revoked.

Signed by the Secretary of State for Health

Patricia Hewitt

Secretary of State for Health

Department of Health

29th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 8<sup>th</sup> April 2007 of the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust established on 4<sup>th</sup> November 1992.
