# The Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1032

COMMONS, ENGLAND

The Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007

Made

26th March 2007

Laid before Parliament

29th March 2007

Coming into force

1st June 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Commons Registration Act 1965[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2007.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.

## 2. Revocations

The following provisions of the Commons Registration (General) Regulations 1966[^f00003] are revoked—

- (a) regulation 32;
- (b) in regulation 34(1), the words “for searches and official certificates of search, and”;
- (c) regulation 35;
- (d) in Schedule 1, Form 21 and its duplicate; and
- (e) in Schedule 3, the first and second items in the table.

**3.** The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) Regulations 1982[^f00004];
- (b) the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) Regulations 1989[^f00005]; and
- (c) the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003[^f00006].

Barry Gardiner

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

26th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke with effect from 1<sup>st</sup> June 2007, in relation to England, provisions of the Commons Registration (General) Regulations 1966 relating to official searches of the commons registers and certificates of search. From 1<sup>st</sup> June 2007 it will instead be possible to search the commons registers by means of the Supplementary Enquiries of Local Authority Form CON29 Part II.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect of the change in the arrangements for searches of the commons registers was published for the Commons Act 2006 and copies are available on the Defra website (www.defra.gov.uk).
