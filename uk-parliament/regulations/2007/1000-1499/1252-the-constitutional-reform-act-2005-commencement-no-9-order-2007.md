# The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1252 (C. 54)

Lord chief justice of northern ireland

The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2007

Made

16th April 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 148 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2007.

## 2. Commencement of provisions of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005

The following provisions of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 shall come into force on 8<sup>th</sup> May 2007—

- (a) section 5(3) and 5(4) (representations to Parliament);
- (b) section 6 (representations to the Northern Ireland Assembly); and
- (c) section 11 (Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland), in so far as it inserts subsection (1B)(b) into section 12 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00002].

Falconer of Thoroton, C.

16th April 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Constitutional Reform Act [2005 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4) listed in Article 2. These relate to the role of the Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland in respect of making representations to Parliament, the Northern Ireland Assembly, the First Minister, deputy First Minister and Northern Ireland Ministers.
