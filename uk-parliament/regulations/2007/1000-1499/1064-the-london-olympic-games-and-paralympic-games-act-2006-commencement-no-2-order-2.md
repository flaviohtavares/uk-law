# The London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1064 (C. 44)

olympic games And paralympic games

sports grounds and sporting events

The London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007

Made

*27th* March 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 40(2) and (4) of the London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 2nd April 2007

The following provisions of the London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 shall come into force on 2nd April 2007—

- (a) paragraph 12 of Schedule 3,
- (b) paragraph 13 of Schedule 3, to the extent that it inserts section 8B(2) and (3) into the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995[^f00002], and
- (c) paragraph 14 of Schedule 3.

Richard Caborn

Minister of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

27th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the following provisions of the London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 into force on 2nd April 2007:

- paragraph 12 of Schedule 3, which inserts section 8A into the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (“1995 Act”),
- paragraph 13 of Schedule 3, to the extent that it inserts section 8B(2) and (3) into the 1995 Act, and
- paragraph 14 of Schedule 3, which inserts sections 12A and 12B into the 1995 Act.
