# The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1121 (C. 51)

JUDICIAL INDEPENDENCE

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2007

Made

29th March 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 148(3) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 4 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Guarantee of continued judicial independence: Northern Ireland) shall come into force on 8th May 2007.

David Hanson

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

29th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 4 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 on 8<sup>th</sup> May 2007.
