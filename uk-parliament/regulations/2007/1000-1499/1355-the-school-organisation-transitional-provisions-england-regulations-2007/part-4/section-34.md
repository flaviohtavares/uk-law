# The School Organisation (Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2007, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Withdrawal of Proposals

Proposals may be withdrawn by the governing body or local education authority which published the proposals provided that—

- (a) such proposals are withdrawn before any determination is made,
- (b) written notice is given to the local education authority in cases where the proposals have been made by the governing body, and in cases where the proposals have been referred to the adjudicator, to the adjudicator and the Secretary of State; and
- (c) written notice is placed at the entrance to the school or, if there is more than one main entrance, all of them.
