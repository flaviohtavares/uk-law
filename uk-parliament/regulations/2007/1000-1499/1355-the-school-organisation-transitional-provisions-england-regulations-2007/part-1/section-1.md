# The School Organisation (Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2007, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the School Organisation (Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 25th May 2007.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.
