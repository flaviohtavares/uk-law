# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1352

education, england

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.2) Order 2007

Made

2nd May 2007

Coming into force

3rd May 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of May 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2007 and comes into force on 3rd May 2007.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

(1) The persons named in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

(2) The person named in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order is appointed Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills with effect from 1st September 2007.

Christine Cook

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- David Jones

### PART 2 — Person appointed with effect from 1st September 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
