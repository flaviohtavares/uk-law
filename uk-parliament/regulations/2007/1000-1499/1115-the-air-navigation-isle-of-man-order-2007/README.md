EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the Isle of Man corresponding to certain provisions of the Air Navigation Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1970)) relating to the registration, marking, airworthiness, equipment, manning, operation, movement and safety of aircraft and the licensing and welfare of flight crew. The Order will facilitate the establishment of an Isle of Man Register of Aircraft.
