# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2007, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Approval of persons to furnish reports

In relation to any of its functions under any of the provisions of this Order the Department may approve a person as qualified to furnish reports to it and may accept such reports.
