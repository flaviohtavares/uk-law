# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2007, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Fatigue of crew — responsibilities of crew

A person shall not act as a member of the crew of an aircraft if he knows or suspects that he is suffering from, or, having regard to the circumstances of the flight to be undertaken, is likely to suffer from, such fatigue as may endanger the safety of the aircraft or of its occupants.
