# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) (England) Regulations 2007, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Information to be contained in a competition notice

(1) Competition notices inviting proposals for the establishment of a mainstream school falling within section 7(2) must specify the matters prescribed in Part 1 of Schedule 1 (in addition to the matters referred to in section 7(3)(a), (b) and (c)).

(2) Competition notices inviting proposals for the establishment of a special school falling within section 7(2) must specify the matters prescribed in Part 2 of Schedule 1 (in addition to the matters referred to in section 7(3)(a), (b) and (c)).
