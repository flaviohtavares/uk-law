# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) (England) Regulations 2007, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Prescribed interval after which competition notice may specify a date for the submission of proposals

For the purposes of section 7(3)(c), the prescribed interval is an interval of 4 months from the date of publication of the competition notice.
