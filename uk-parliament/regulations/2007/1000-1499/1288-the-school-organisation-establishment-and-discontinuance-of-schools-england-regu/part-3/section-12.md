# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) (England) Regulations 2007, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Information to be contained in proposals under section 11

Proposals under section 11(1) and 11(2) must contain the information specified in Part 1 of Schedule 3 in relation to mainstream schools, and in Part 2 of Schedule 3 in relation to special schools.
