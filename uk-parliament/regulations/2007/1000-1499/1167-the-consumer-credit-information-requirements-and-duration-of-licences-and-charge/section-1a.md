# The Consumer Credit (Information Requirements and Duration of Licences and Charges) Regulations 2007, section 1A

> Text as at 2014-09-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Application[^key-420fce6ee95208cc30129fca1ebf03e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following do not apply in relation to Northern Ireland—

- (a) regulation 4(aa) and (ba);
- (b) regulation 10A;
- (c) regulation 10B;
- (d) regulation 10C;
- (e) regulation 41A;
- (f) paragraph 3A of Part 1 of Schedule 1.
