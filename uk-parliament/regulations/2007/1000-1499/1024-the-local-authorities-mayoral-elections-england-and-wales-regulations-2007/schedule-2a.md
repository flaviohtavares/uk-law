# The Local Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (England and Wales) Regulations 2007, SCHEDULE2A

> Text as at 2024-12-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2A — Modification to a provision in the Representation of the People Act 1983 where a mayoral election is taken alone or together with a police and crime commissioner election[^c-fb8cbf236b1509088a9ef257feee68e6]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 3

### TABLE — The Representation of the People Act 1983

| *(1)*<br>*Provision* | *(2)*<br>*Modification* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 199B (translation of certain documents) | In subsection (6), insert after paragraph (b)—<br>“(ba) in the case of an election for a mayor under the 2000 Act, must have printed at the top of the list of candidates the words “Vote (x) for one candidate only” if there are only two candidates, or the words “Vote once (x) in column 1 for your first choice, and vote once (x) in column 2 for your second choice” if there are more than two candidates.” |
