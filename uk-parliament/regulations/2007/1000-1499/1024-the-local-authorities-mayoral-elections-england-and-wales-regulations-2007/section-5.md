# The Local Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (England and Wales) Regulations 2007, section 5

> Text as at 2024-12-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Questioning of mayoral election[^key-ffc92ce862d4b721c92bab499bcc9b7a]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of sections 9HE(6) and 44(4)[^key-a649c0d6e0c83464880ea61265042533] of the 2000 Act, Part 3 (legal proceedings) of the 1983 Act shall have effect in relation to the questioning of an election for the return of an elected mayor as it has effect in relation to the questioning of an election under the local government Act [^f00017].
