# The Local Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (England and Wales) Regulations 2007, section 4

> Text as at 2024-12-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Combination of polls[^key-2ab2b917d86653159220e6847eea7c99]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-f083e71e6be845c8382d9903ecfc0a97] Where the poll at a mayoral election is taken together with the poll at a relevant election or referendum[^f00016], the Mayoral Elections Rules shall be modified so as to have effect as set out in Schedule 3.

(2)[^M_F_e55a1070-ad3d-473f-8ee6-321801f15291] Where the poll at a mayoral election is taken together with a poll at a police and crime commissioner election and is not also taken together with a poll at any other relevant election or referendum, Schedule 3 has effect subject to the modifications set out in Schedule 3A.
