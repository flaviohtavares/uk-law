# The Exempt Charities (No. 2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 1364

CHARITIES

The Exempt Charities (No. 2) Order 2007

Made

2nd May 2007

Coming into force

3rd May 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of May 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities (No.2) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 3rd May 2007.

**2.** The Central School of Speech and Drama is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1993.

Christine Cook

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares The Central School of Speech and Drama to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 1993 with effect from 3rd May 2007. An exempt charity is not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except at the charity’s request.
