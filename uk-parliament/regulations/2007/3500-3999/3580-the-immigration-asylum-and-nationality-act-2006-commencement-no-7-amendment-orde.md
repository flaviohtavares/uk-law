# The Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Order amends [SI 2007/3138](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3138) made on 30th October 2007 and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2007 No. 3580 (C. 156)

immigration

The Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

18th December 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 62 of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) (Amendment) Order 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007

In article 3 of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007[^f00002] (commencement of provisions on 31st December 2007) for “31st December 2007” substitute “1st March 2008”.

Liam Byrne

Minister of State

Home Office

18 December 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2007. In consequence, sections 34, 35 and 39 of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 will be brought into force, and sections 31, 32, 36, 37 and 38 of that Act will be brought into force to the extent to which they are not already in force, on 1st March 2008 rather than on 31st December 2007.
