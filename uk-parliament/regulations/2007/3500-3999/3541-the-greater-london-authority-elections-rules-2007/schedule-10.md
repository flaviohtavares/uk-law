## SCHEDULE 10 — Schedule of Forms

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

### 1. Interpretation

In this Schedule—

- “**CMER**” means the Constituency Members Election Rules in Schedule 1 or 5;
- “**LMER**” means the London Members Election Rules in Schedule 2 or 6;
- “**MER**” means the Mayoral Election Rules in Schedule 3 or 7.

**2.** The forms in this Schedule may be adapted so far as circumstances require (and in particular, for the purposes of electronic counting)[^c-52582d86f9ab29cf14ffa0624df36305], but any adaptation made by the CRO must, where appropriate, be in compliance with any direction issued by the GLRO under rule 11.
