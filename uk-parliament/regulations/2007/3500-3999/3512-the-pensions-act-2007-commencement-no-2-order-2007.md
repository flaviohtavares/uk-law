# The Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 3512 (C.152)

Pensions

The Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007

Made

12th December 2007

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2) of the Pensions Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 18(1) to (3) of the Pensions Act 2007 (financial assistance scheme: increased levels of payment), is 14th December 2007.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Mike O’Brien

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

12th December 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 14th December 2007 section 18(1) to (3) of the Pensions Act [2007 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/22) which relates to increased payments by the Financial Assistance Scheme .
