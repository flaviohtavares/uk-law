# The Companies (EEA State) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 732

COMPANIES

The Companies (EEA State) Regulations 2007

Made

7th March 2007

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2007

Coming into force

9th March 2007

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the creation, operation, regulation or dissolution of companies and other forms of business organisation.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (EEA State) Regulations 2007.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 9th March 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Companies Act 1985

In section 744 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00004] (expressions used generally in that Act), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

## 3. Amendment of the Companies Act 2006

In section 1170 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00005] (meaning of “EEA State” and related expressions), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

## 4. Amendment of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986

In Article 2(3) of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00006] (general interpretation), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

## 5. Amendment of the Companies (Summary Financial Statement) Regulations 1995

In regulation 2(1) of the Companies (Summary Financial Statement) Regulations 1995[^f00007] (interpretation), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

## 6. Amendment of the Companies (Summary Financial Statement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

In regulation 2 of the Companies (Summary Financial Statement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00008] (interpretation), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

## 7. Amendment of the Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006

In regulation 2(1) of the Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006[^f00009] (interpretation), for the definition of “EEA State” substitute—

> - “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the definition of “EEA State” in companies legislation. The existing definitions do not include Bulgaria and Romania, which became Community member States on 1st January 2007. The new definitions refer to the definition of “EEA state” inserted into the Interpretation Act [1978 (c.30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30) by the Legislative and Regulatory Reform Act [2006 (c.51)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/51); this includes all Community member States. The definition is amended using powers under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act [1972 (c.68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) in order correctly to transpose Community legislation in relation to company law.

The main amendments are to section 744 of the Companies Act 1985 (the 1985 Act), Article 2 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 ([S.I. 1986/1032 (N.I. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1986/1032)) and section 1170 of the Companies Act [2006 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46). Section 1170 will be brought into force on 6th April 2007 if the Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2, Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2007 receives Parliamentary approval, at which point the other two definitions will be repealed.

These Regulations also substitute the same definition of “EEA State” in regulations that contain a definition in similar terms to that in section 744 of the 1985 Act.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
