# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 774 (L. 4)

FAMILY PROCEEDINGS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

8th March 2007

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2007

Coming into force

2nd April 2007

The Lord Chancellor after consulting the Lord Chief Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Part I of Schedule 11 of the Children Act 1989[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 2nd April 2007.

**2.** The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002] is amended in accordance with articles 3 and 4.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 2 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/1677](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1677)), which allocates care centres to local justice areas grouped according to region, and Schedule 3 to that Order, which designates certain county courts as adoption centres. The amendments make Wrexham County Court a care centre and an adoption centre with effect from 2nd April 2007, make Wales a separate Region and move some of the entries to the North West Region from the Wales Region.
