# The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Kenneth Macdonald) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 802

pensions

The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Kenneth Macdonald) Regulations 2007

Made

12th March 2007

Laid before Parliament

13th March 2007

Coming into force

9th April 2007

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Kenneth Macdonald) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 9<sup>th</sup> April 2007.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the 1971 Act” means the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

## 3. Pensions to which the 1971 Act will apply

The 1971 Act shall have effect in relation to any pensions payable under the pension schemes for Kenneth Macdonald (being schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00003]), as if they were pensions specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act.

Frank Roy

Dave Watts

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

12th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to any pensions payable under pension schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972 for Kenneth Macdonald, the Director of Public Prosecutions.
