# The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 621 (C. 27)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2007

Made

27th February 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 172(2) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 165 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 shall come into force on 6th April 2007.

Malcolm Wicks

Minister for Science and Innovation

Department of Trade and Industry

27th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 6th April 2007, section 165 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act [1994 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/33). This section inserts sections 107A and 198A into the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act [1988 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48).
