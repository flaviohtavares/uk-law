# The Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 695 (C. 29)

EMPLOYMENT

The Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007

Made

5th March 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 29(1) and (2) of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

The following provisions of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 come into force on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2007—

- (a) section 6(1) (prohibition of unlicensed activities) for purposes related to work falling within section 3(1)(b);
- (b) section 12 (offences: acting as a gangmaster, being in possession of false documents etc) for purposes related to work falling within section 3(1)(b);
- (c) section 13(1), (2) and (4) (offences: entering into arrangements with gangmasters) for purposes related to work falling within section 3(1)(b);
- (d) section 27 (exclusion of provisions relating to employment agencies and businesses) for purposes related to work falling within section 3(1)(b);
- (e) paragraph 20 of Schedule 2 for purposes related to work falling within section 3(1)(b).

Jeff Rooker

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

5th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 specified in article 2 of the Order.
