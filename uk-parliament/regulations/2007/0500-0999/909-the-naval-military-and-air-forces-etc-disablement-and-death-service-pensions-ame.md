# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 909

PENSIONS

The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2007

Made

21st March 2007

Laid before Parliament

28th March 2007

Coming into force

9th April 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of March 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty considers it expedient to amend the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006[^f00001].

ACCORDINGLY, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 12(1) and 24(3) of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling Her to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Naval, Military and Air Forces etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 9th April 2007.

(2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, and, except where the context otherwise requires, a reference to a numbered article, Part, Table or Schedule is a reference to the article, Part, Table or Schedule in the principal Order which bears that number.

## 2. Amendments to the principal Order and application

The amendments to the principal Order contained in Schedule 1 to this Order shall have effect.

Christine Cook

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE 1 — AMENDMENTS TO THE PRINCIPAL ORDER

Article 2

### 1. Amendment to article 12 (unemployability allowance)

In article 12(3)(e) for “52” substitute “104”.

### 2. Amendment to article 15 (allowance for lowered standard of occupation)

In article 15(1) after the words “lowered standard of occupation at” insert the words “a rate not exceeding”.

### 3. Amendment to article 23(2) (pensions to surviving spouses and surviving civil partners)

In article 23(2) for “£68.42” substitute “£70.88”.

### 4. Amendment of Schedule 1

In Schedule 1 (disablement due to service in the armed forces)—

(1) for the Table in Part II there shall be substituted the Table set out in Schedule 2 to this Order;

(2) for Tables 1 and 2 in Part III there shall respectively be substituted Tables 1 and 2 set out in Schedule 3 to this Order; and

(3) for the Table in Part IV there shall be substituted the Table set out in Schedule 4 to this Order.

### 5. Amendment of Schedule 2

In Schedule 2 (death due to service in the armed forces)—

(1) for Tables 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 of Part II there shall respectively be substituted the Tables set out in Schedule 5 to this Order; and

(2) for the Table in Part III there shall be substituted the Table set out in Schedule 6 to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 (“the principal Order”) which makes provision for pensions and other awards in respect of disablement or death due to service in the naval, military and air forces.

Paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 amends article 12(3)(e) of the principal Order by increasing the period between the termination of the original payment of unemployability allowance and the making of a new claim for unemployability allowance from 52 to 104 weeks for those persons who become engaged in remunerative work which then ceases, provided they meet the other criteria set out in article 12(3)(e).

Paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 corrects an omission in the principle Order which arose during the process of consolidating a number of earlier instruments into the principle Order.

Paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 varies the rate of supplementary pension payable to an entitled surviving spouse or surviving civil partner of a member whose service terminated before 31st March 1973.

Paragraphs 4 and 5 of Schedule 1 substitute Tables in Schedules 1 and 2 to the principal Order thereby varying the rates of retired pay, pensions, gratuities and allowances in respect of disablement or death due to service in the armed forces. In addition the rates set out in paragraphs 1(b)(i) and 1(b)(ii) of the Table at Schedule 4 of this Order, which replaces the Table at Part IV of Schedule 1 of the principal Order, have been revised to correct an error which arose during the process of consolidating a number of earlier instruments into the principal Order.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
