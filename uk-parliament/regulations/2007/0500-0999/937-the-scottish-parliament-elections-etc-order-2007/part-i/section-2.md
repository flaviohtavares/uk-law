# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2007, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders and Regulations are hereby revoked:–

- (a) the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002[^f00003];
- (b) paragraph 22 of the Schedule to the Health Act 1999 (Consequential Amendments) (Nursing and Midwifery) Order 2004[^f00004];
- (c) paragraph 2 of Schedule 10 to the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) Order 2005[^f00005],

as from the coming into force of this Order.
