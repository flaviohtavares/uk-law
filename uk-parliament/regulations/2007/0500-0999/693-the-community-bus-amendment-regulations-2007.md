# The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 693

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made

6th March 2007

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2007

Coming into force

1st April 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(1) and 60(1)(e) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and section 126(1)(c) and 134 of the Transport Act 1985[^f00002] and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00003].

He has consulted with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007.

## 2. Amendments

(1) The Community Bus Regulations 1986[^f00005] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4(1), for “£50” substitute “£53”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

S.J.Ladyman

Minister of State

Department for Transport

6th March 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee for a community bus permit granted under section 22 of the Transport Act 1985 from £50 to £53 (an increase of 6%). The fee was last increased on 30th September 2005.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the DVO Policy Unit of the Department for Transport, Zone 5/20, Southside, 105 Victoria Street, London SW1P 4DR. The regulatory impact assessment is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available along the instrument on the OPSI website at www.opsi.gov.uk.
