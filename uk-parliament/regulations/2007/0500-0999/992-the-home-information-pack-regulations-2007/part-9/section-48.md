# The Home Information Pack Regulations 2007, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Responsibility for proving purposes of disclosure

Where this Part refers to the purposes of a disclosure, it shall be the responsibility of the person seeking the disclosure to prove those purposes to the satisfaction of the person from whom disclosure is sought.
