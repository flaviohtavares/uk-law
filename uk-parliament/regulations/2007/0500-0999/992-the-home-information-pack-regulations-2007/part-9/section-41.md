# The Home Information Pack Regulations 2007, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Registration of home condition reports

Each home condition report entered onto the register—

- (a) shall be registered under a report reference number; and
- (b) shall not be altered once so registered.
