# The Home Information Pack Regulations 2007, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Copies of a home information pack

The copies of a home information pack or pack document provided or produced under section 156(1) or 167(1) of the 2004 Act must be—

- (a) true copies of the home information pack or pack document; or
- (b) where a pack document is an official copy, a true copy of it or another official copy.
