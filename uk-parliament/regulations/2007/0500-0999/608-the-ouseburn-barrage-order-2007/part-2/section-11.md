# The Ouseburn Barrage Order 2007, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Ouseburn not to be a reservoir

Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (2) of section 1 of the Reservoirs Act 1975[^f00015], no part of the Ouseburn as is impounded by the scheduled works shall, by virtue of the retention of water by means of the scheduled works, be taken to be a reservoir for the purposes of that Act.
