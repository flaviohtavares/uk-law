# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 615

education, england

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2007

Made

6th March 2007

Coming into force

7th March 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 6<sup>th</sup> day of March 2007

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2007 and comes into force on 7<sup>th</sup> March 2007.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Schools in England

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed Her Majesty’s Inspector of Schools in England.

Christine Cook

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Person Appointed

Article 2

Clare Melanie Walton

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the person named in the Schedule as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
