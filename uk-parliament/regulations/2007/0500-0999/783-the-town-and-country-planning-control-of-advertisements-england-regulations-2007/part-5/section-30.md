# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (England) Regulations 2007, section 30

> Text as at 2021-06-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Contravention of Regulations

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person displaying an advertisement in contravention of these Regulations shall be liable, on summary conviction of an offence under section 224(3) of the Act, to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale [^c18473171] and, in the case of a continuing offence, one tenth of level 4 on the standard scale for each day during which the offence continues after conviction.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to the Crown [^c18473181].
