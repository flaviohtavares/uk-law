# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (England) Regulations 2007, section 28

> Text as at 2021-06-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Extension of time limits

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may, in any particular case, extend the time within which anything is required to be done under these Regulations or within which any objection, representation or claim for compensation may be made.
