# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (England) Regulations 2007, section 23

> Text as at 2021-06-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Repayment of expense of removing prohibited advertisements

*Extent: E+W*

The time limit prescribed for the purpose of making a claim for compensation under section 223 of the Act for the recovery of expenses reasonably incurred is a period of 6 months from the completion of the works.
