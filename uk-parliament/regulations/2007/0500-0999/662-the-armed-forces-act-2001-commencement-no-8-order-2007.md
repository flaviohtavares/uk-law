# The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.8) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 662 (C. 28)

defence

The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.8) Order 2007

Made

*27th February* 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2001[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.8) Order 2007.

**2.** Section 21 of the Armed Forces Act 2001 shall come into force on 28th February 2007.

Derek Twigg

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

Date 27th February 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 21 of the Armed Forces Act 2001. Section 21 inserts new sections into the Army Act [1955 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/18), the Air Force Act [1955 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/19) and the Naval Discipline Act [1957 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1957/53). The new sections make provision equivalent to that provided for in the civilian system by sections 35 and 36 of the Criminal Justice Act [1988 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33), for the Attorney General to refer a case to the Courts-Martial Appeal Court where he considers that a sentence passed on a person by a court-martial was unduly lenient. This power is only exercisable in relation to those offences where the corresponding civil offence would be triable only on indictment.
