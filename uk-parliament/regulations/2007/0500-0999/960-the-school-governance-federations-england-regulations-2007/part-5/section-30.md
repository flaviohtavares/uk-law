# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2007, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Qualifications, tenure of office and instruments of government

Regulations 19 (*Notification of appointment*s) and 21 to 33 of, and Schedule 6 (*Qualifications and disqualifications*) to, the Constitution Regulations apply to the governing body of a federation and its members subject to the modifications in Schedule 7.
