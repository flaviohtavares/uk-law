# The Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Industrial Assurance and Friendly Societies) (Channel Islands) Order 1952

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1952 No. 165

RESERVE AND AUXILIARY FORCES

The Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Industrial Assurance and Friendly Societies) (Channel Islands) Order, 1952

Made

30th January 1952

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 30th day of January, 1952

Present

The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas by subsection (3) of section fifty-nine of the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Protection of Civil Interests) Act, 1951, it is provided that His Majesty may by Order in Council direct that the provisions of Part VI of that Act shall extend to the Channel Islands subject to such exceptions adaptations, and modifications, if any, as may be specified in the Order:

Now, therefore, His Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Industrial Assurance and Friendly Societies) (Channel Islands) Order, 1952.

**2.** Part VI of the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Protection of Civil Interests) Act, 1951, shall extend to the Channel Islands.

F. J. Fernau
