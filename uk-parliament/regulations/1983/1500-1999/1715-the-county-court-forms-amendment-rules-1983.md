# The County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1715 (L.27)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1983

Made

15th November 1983

Coming into Operation

12th December 1983

## Citation and interpretation

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1983.

(2) In these Rules a form referred to by number means the form so numbered in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001] and “**the main Schedule**” means that Schedule.

**2.** There shall be substituted in the main Schedule, for Forms N.12, N.13 and N.84 respectively, the forms contained in the Schedule to these Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982 so as to substitute new forms of the certificates of service and of the garnishee order to show cause.
