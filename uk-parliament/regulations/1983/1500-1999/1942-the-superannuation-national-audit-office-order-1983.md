# The Superannuation (National Audit Office) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1942

PENSIONS

The Superannuation (National Audit Office) Order 1983

Made

22nd December 1983

Laid before Parliament

23rd December 1983

Coming into Operation

1st January 1984

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) of the Superannuation Act 1972 and now vested in them[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (National Audit Office) Order 1983 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1984.

**2.** Employment as a member of the staff of the National Audit Office is hereby added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972.

Ian B. Lang

D.J.F. Hunt

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury

22nd December 1983

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The National Audit Act 1983 (c.44) sets up the National Audit Office on 1st January 1984. This Order adds employment in that office to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972, and thereby brings persons in such employment within the principal civil service pension scheme made under section 1 of the 1972 Act.
