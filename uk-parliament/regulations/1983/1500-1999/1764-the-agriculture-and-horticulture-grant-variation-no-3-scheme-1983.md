# The Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) (No. 3) Scheme 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1764

AGRICULTURE

HORTICULTURE

The Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) (No. 3) Scheme 1983

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

29th November 1983

Laid before Parliament

30th November 1983

Coming into Operation

1st December 1983

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 28 and 29 of the Agriculture Act 1970 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following scheme:—

## Citation and commencement

**1.** This scheme may be cited as the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) (No. 3) Scheme 1983 and shall come into operation on 1st December 1983.

## Application

**2.** This scheme (except paragraph 5(a) and (c) and the proviso to paragraph 8 below) shall not apply in relation to any claim for grant towards expenditure incurred for the purposes of the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant Scheme 1980[^f00001] (hereinafter called “**the principal scheme**”) before 1st December 1983.

## Variation of principal scheme

**3.** The principal scheme shall be further varied in accordance with paragraphs 4 to 8 of this scheme.

**4.** In regulation 2(1) thereof (interpretation)—

- (a) there shall be inserted immediately after the definition of “ECU” the following definition—

  > - **\`glasshouse'** means a fixed or mobile structure, used for the production of horticultural produce for sale, which is mounted on durable foundations, is not less than 1.67 metres high at the ridge, and of which more than half the total area of the sides, ends and roof is of glass or such other translucent material as will give a durability and transparency similar to that of glass;

  ;
- (b) there shall be inserted immediately after the definition of “less-favoured area” the following definition—

  > - **\`main framework'**, in relation to a building or structure, means the floor, outer walls and roof of that building or structure and any internal dividing walls which are integral to that building or structure;

**5.** In paragraph 5 thereof (restrictions on payment of grant)—

- (a) in subparagraph (2)(b) there shall be inserted after the words “on or after 30th November 1981” the words “relating to expenditure incurred before 1st December 1983”, and for the words “and in any case” there shall be substituted the word “or”;
- (b) there shall be inserted after subparagraph (2)(b) the following subparagraph—

  > - (bA) where that expenditure is incurred on or after 1st December 1983 and is in respect of any work, facility or transaction which is of a kind specified in any of paragraphs 1 to 5 of column 1 of the Schedule unless it appears to that Minister that, after completion of the works, facilities or transactions in respect of which the expenditure has been incurred, the number of pig places on the land on which there will be carried on the agricultural business in relation to which the expenditure has been incurred will not exceed 550, and in any of the above cases

  ;
- (c) in subparagraph (3) there shall be inserted after the date “30th November 1981” the words “and before 1st December 1983”;
- (d) there shall be inserted after subparagraph (3) the following subparagraph—

  > (3A) The appropriate Minister shall not pay grant under paragraph 3(1) towards expenditure in respect of any work, facility or transaction which is of a kind specified in any of paragraphs 1 to 5 of column 1 of the Schedule and is incurred on or after 1st December 1983 in connection with the breeding or keeping of dairy cows unless it appears to him that after completion of the works, facilities or transactions in respect of which that expenditure has been incurred not more than 40 dairy cows will be kept on the land on which there will be carried on the agricultural business in relation to which the expenditure has been incurred.

  .

**6.** In paragraph 7(1) thereof (restriction on payment of grant) there shall be inserted at the end the words “except to the extent that such excess expenditure or excess aggregate expenditure has been incurred in relation to any work, facility or transaction of a kind specified in paragraph 2(ii) or 16(ii) of column 1 of the Schedule and does not exceed the further sum of 50,000 ECU.”.

**7.** In paragraph 9 thereof (amounts of grant)—

- (a) in subparagraph (1)(a) the number “12(i)” shall be deleted;
- (b) in subparagraph (1)(b) for the words “paragraphs 6, 7(i), 7(ii), 8 to 11, 12(ii) and 12(iii)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraphs 6, 7(i), 7(ii) and 8 to 12”.

**8.** The Schedule to this scheme shall be substituted for the Schedule to the principal scheme (eligible works, facilities and transactions and rates of grant):

Provided that such substitution shall not affect the operation of the principal scheme in relation to expenditure incurred before 1st December 1983.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This scheme varies the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant Scheme 1980 (“the principal scheme”) and is also made in accordance with provisions of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1945/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1981/1945) and Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2180/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1981/2180) both restricting investment aids for pig production and Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1946/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1981/1946) restricting investment aids for milk production.

The main change made by the scheme is the substitution of a new Schedule of eligible works, transactions, facilities and rates of grant for the existing Schedule to the principal scheme in relation to claims for grant towards expenditure incurred on or after 1st December 1983. New rates of grant are specified for most items, and revised descriptions are introduced in respect of paragraphs 1(i) and (ii) (main framework of permanent buildings and structures not intended for horticultural production and bulk dry stores and silos), paragraph 2(i) (main framework of permanent buildings, unheated glasshouses and durable structures similar to glasshouses, intended for horticultural production), paragraph 2(ii) (heated glasshouses), paragraph 9 (pens, dips, stells or other facilities for sheltering livestock) and paragraph 16 (plant or equipment for preparation for market of horticultural produce and glasshouse heating systems). Consequential amendments are made to paragraph 9(1) of the principal scheme, and definitions of “glasshouse” and “main framework” are introduced into paragraph 2(1) (interpretation) of that scheme.

The restriction that expenditure on the breeding or keeping of pigs is eligible for grant only to the extent that it does not make possible an increase to more than 550 pig places (paragraph 5(2)(b) of the principal scheme as inserted by paragraph 4 of the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) Scheme 1981) is modified in relation to expenditure incurred on or after 1st December 1983 so that only expenditure on works of a kind specified in paragraphs 1 to 5 of column 1 of the Schedule is rendered ineligible if following such expenditure the number of pig places exceeds 550 (paragraph 5(a) and (b) of this scheme).

Grant towards expenditure on the breeding or keeping of dairy cows remains subject to the restriction that the expenditure must not result in an increase in the dairy herd to more than 40 cows (paragraph 5(3) of the principal scheme as inserted by paragraph 4 of the 1981 scheme) but is similarly modified, only expenditure on works of a kind specified in paragraphs 1 to 5 of column 1 of the Schedule incurred on or after 1st December 1983 being rendered ineligible by this restriction (paragraph 5(c) and (d) of this scheme).

Paragraph 7(1) of the principal scheme (restriction on grant) is also amended so that the maximum limit on expenditure which is eligible for grant is raised from 160,000 ECU to 210,000 ECU provided that the excess expenditure over 160,000 ECU has been incurred in relation to works of a kind specified in paragraph 2(ii) or 16(ii) of column 1 of the Schedule.
