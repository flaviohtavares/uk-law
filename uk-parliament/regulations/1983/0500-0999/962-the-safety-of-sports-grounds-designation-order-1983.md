# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 962

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1983

Made

4th July 1983

Laid before Parliament

13th July 1983

Coming into Operation

1st January 1984

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(1) the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, and after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1983 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1984.

**2.** The sports stadia specified in the Schedule to this Order (being stadia each of which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators) are hereby designated as stadia requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

Leon Brittan

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

4th July 1983

EXPLANATORY NOTE

By this Order the Secretary of State designates certain sports stadia as stadia requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.
