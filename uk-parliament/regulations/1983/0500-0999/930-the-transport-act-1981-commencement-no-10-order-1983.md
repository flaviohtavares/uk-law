# The Transport Act 1981 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 930 (C.25)

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Transport Act 1981 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1983

Made

30th June 1983

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by 18(3) of the Transport Act 1981, and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 1981 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1983.

**2.** The 2nd August 1983 is hereby appointed as the day on which paragraph 10 of Schedule 6 to the Transport Act 1981 comes into force.

Tom King

Secretary of State for Transport

30th June 1983

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 2nd August 1983 for the coming into force of paragraph 10 of Schedule 6 to the Transport Act 1981.
