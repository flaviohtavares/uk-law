# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Scheme may be cited as the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983, and shall come into operation on 21st July 1983.
