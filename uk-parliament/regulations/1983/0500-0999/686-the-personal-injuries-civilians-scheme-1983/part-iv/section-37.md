# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Awards to or in respect of ineligible members of the families of unemployable pensioners

Where a deceased person was at the date of his death in receipt of an allowance in respect of a child under Article 18(5)(d) by virtue of Article 18(5)(e) or in respect of a husband under Article 18(5)(a), a pension to or an allowance in respect of the child and a pension to the husband may be awarded at the rate and subject to the conditions which would have been appropriate under the foregoing provisions of this Part if the child or husband had been an eligible member of the family.
