# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Pension for disablement

A disabled person may be awarded a pension in respect of his disablement at the appropriate rate specified in Schedule 3, paragraph 1, where the degree of his disablement is 100 per cent. or, where the degree of his disablement is less than 100 per cent., at the rate which bears to the rate specified as aforesaid the same proportion as the degree of his disablement bears to 100 per cent.
