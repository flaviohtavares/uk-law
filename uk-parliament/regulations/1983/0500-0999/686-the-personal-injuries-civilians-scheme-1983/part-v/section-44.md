# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Exceptionally severe disablement allowance

The provisions of Article 15 shall apply to a person in receipt of a pension under Article 42 in all respects as if he were in receipt of a pension under Article 11.
