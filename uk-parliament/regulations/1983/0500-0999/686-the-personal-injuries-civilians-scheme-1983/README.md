EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme, which makes provision for payment of pensions and allowances to or in respect of civilians who were injured or killed in the 1939–45 War, consolidates the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1976 and subsequent amending Schemes which are set out in Schedule 6 and are revoked by Article 81.

No amendments other than of a drafting nature have been made.
