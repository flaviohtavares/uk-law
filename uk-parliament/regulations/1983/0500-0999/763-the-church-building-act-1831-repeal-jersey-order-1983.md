# The Church Building Act 1831 (Repeal) (Jersey) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 763

CHANNEL ISLANDS

The Church Building Act 1831 (Repeal) (Jersey) Order 1983

Made

18th May 1983

Coming into Operation

26th May 1983

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of May 1983

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 3(2) of the Statute Law (Repeals) Act 1974, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Church Building Act 1831 (Repeal) (Jersey) Order 1983 and shall come into operation on 26th May 1983.

**2.** The repeal of the Church Building Act 1831 by Part VII of the Schedule to the Statute Law (Repeals) Act 1974 shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey.

N.E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends to the Bailiwick of Jersey the repeal of the Church Building Act 1831.
