# The Water Act 1983 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1173 (C.32)

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Water Act 1983 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1983

Made

29th July 1983

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of their powers under section 11(5) of the Water Act 1983 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby order as follows:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Water Act 1983 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1983.

**2.** The provisions of the Water Act 1983 which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this order and which relate to the subject matter specified in column 2 thereof shall come into force on 10th August 1983.

Patrick Jenkin

Secretary of State for the Environment

27th July 1983

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

John Stradling Thomas

Minister of State for Wales

29th July 1983
