# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (No. 3) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1018

CLEAN AIR

The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (No. 3) Order 1983

Made

14th July 1983

Laid before Parliament

26th July 1983

Coming into Operation

17th August 1983

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11(4) of the Clean Air Act 1956 and now vested in him[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby orders as follows:—

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

(1) This order may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (No. 3) Order 1983 and shall come into operation on 17th August 1983.

(2) This order applies to England and Wales.

## 2. Class of fireplace exempted from section 11 of the Clean Air Act 1956

The class of fireplace described in Column (1) of the Schedule to this order shall, subject to the conditions specified in Column (2) thereof, be exempted from the provisions of section 11 of the Clean Air Act 1956 (which empowers a local authority to declare the whole or any part of their district to be a smoke control area).

Patrick Jenkin

Secretary of State for the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 11 of the Clean Air Act 1956 empowers local authorities to declare the whole or any part of their district to be a smoke control area in which the emission of smoke is generally prohibited.

This order, which applies to England and Wales, exempts a class of fireplace known as the Trianco ‘**Coal King**’ Boiler manufactured by Trianco Redfyre Limited, from the provisions of that section, subject to certain conditions as to the method of operation and the type of fuel to be used.
