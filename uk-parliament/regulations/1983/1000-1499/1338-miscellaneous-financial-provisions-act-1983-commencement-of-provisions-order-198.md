# Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983 (Commencement of Provisions) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 1338 (C. 38)

DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION

Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983 (Commencement of Provisions) Order 1983

Made

1st September 1983

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9(1) of the Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983 (Commencement of Provisions) Order 1983.

**2.** Sections 1 and 8 of, and Schedule 1 and 3 to, the Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983 shall come into force on 1st April 1984.

Patrick Jenkin

Secretary of State for the Environment

1st September 1983

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1984 sections 1 and 8 of, and Schedules 1 and 3 to, the Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1983. (By virtue of section 9(2) of the Act the remainder of the Act came into force on 13th July 1983.).

Section 1 and Schedule 1 provide for the establishment of the Development Commission in place of the Development Commissioners appointed under Part I of the Development and Road Improvement Funds Act 1909 (c. 47). Repeals, consequential on the provisions of the Act, are provided for in section 8 and Schedule 3.
