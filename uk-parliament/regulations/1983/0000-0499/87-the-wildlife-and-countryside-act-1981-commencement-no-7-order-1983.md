# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 87 (C.5)

WILDLIFE

The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1983

Made

27th January 1983

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England, the Secretary of State for Scotland as respects Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 74(3) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, hereby make the following order:

**1.** This order may be cited as the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1983.

**2.** Sections 32 and 41 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 shall come into force on 28th February 1983.

Tom King

Secretary of State for the Environment

24th January 1983

George Younger

Secretary of State for Scotland

25th January 1983

Nicholas Edwards

Secretary of State for Wales

27th January 1983

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order provides for the coming into force of those provisions of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 which have not been previously brought into force.

The relevant provisions, namely sections 32 and 41, come into force on 28th February 1983. Sections 32 and 41 relate to the duties of agriculture Ministers.
