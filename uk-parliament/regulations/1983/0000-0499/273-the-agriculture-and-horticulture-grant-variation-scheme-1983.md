# The Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) Scheme 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 273

AGRICULTURE

HORTICULTURE

The Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) Scheme 1983

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

1st March 1983

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 1983

Coming into Operation

1st April 1983

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 28 and 29 of the Agriculture Act 1970 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following Scheme:—

**1.** This Scheme may be cited as the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) Scheme 1983 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1983.

**2.** The Agriculture and Horticulture Grant Scheme 1980, as varied[^f00001], shall be further varied in accordance with the following paragraphs.

**3.** Immediately after paragraph 5 thereof (restrictions on payment of grant) there shall be inserted the following paragraph:—

> **5A.** The appropriate Minister shall not pay grant under paragraph 3(1) in relation to any expenditure incurred in replacing apple orchards or pear orchards to the extent that such expenditure was incurred before 8th November 1982.

**4.** Immediately after the entries numbered 13 in the Schedule thereto (eligible works, facilities and transactions and rates of grant) there shall be inserted the following entries in Columns 1, 2, 3 and 4 of that Schedule:—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme further varies the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant Scheme 1980 (as varied by the Agriculture and Horticulture Grant (Variation) Scheme 1981) by making provision for grant to be claimed under the Scheme towards the cost of replacing apple orchards or pear orchards (other than such orchards producing cider apples or perry pears) grubbed up after 7th November 1982 by an equal or smaller area of either apple orchards or pear orchards (depending on the type of orchard grubbed up) containing—

(i) apple trees or pear trees respectively of specified varieties (Cox's Orange Pippin apple, Bramley's Seedling apple and Spartan apple or Conference pear and Doyenne du Comice pear);

(ii) any other named varieties of apple trees or pear trees required for pollination (other than varieties producing cider apples or perry pears) not exceeding in number one third of the total of the specified varieties;

(iii) in the case of apple orchards, any number of crab apple trees or hybrid varieties of crab apple trees required for pollination.

Grant will be payable at the rate of 22.5 per cent towards the cost of replacements taking place after 7th November 1982.
