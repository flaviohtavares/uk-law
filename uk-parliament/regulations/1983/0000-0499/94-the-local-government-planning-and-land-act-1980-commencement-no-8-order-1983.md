# The Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1983

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1983 No. 94 (c.6)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1983

Made

26th January 1983

Laid before Parliament

10th February 1983

Coming into Operation

3rd March 1983

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 94(2) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1983 and shall come into operation on 3rd March 1983.

**2.** Part X of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 shall come into operation on 3rd March 1983 in the areas of the district councils specified in the Schedule to this order.

Nicholas Edwards

Secretary of State for Wales

26th January 1983

## SCHEDULE

- Alyn and Deeside
- Cardiff
- Newport
- Swansea
- Vale of Glamorgan
- Wrexham Maelor

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order brings Part X of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 into force in certain areas of Wales. The Schedule to this order specifies the district councils in whose areas Part X of the Act comes into operation and the order specifies the date from which it operates in those areas. Part X provides for registers to be kept of land owned by public bodies, being land which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, is not being used or not being sufficiently used for the performance of a public body's functions or of carrying on their undertaking. Part X also provides that the Secretary of State may direct a public body who own land on a register to take steps to dispose of it.
