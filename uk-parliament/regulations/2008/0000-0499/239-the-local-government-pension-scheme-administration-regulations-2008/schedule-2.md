# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, SCHEDULE 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — SCHEME EMPLOYERS

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 4(2) and (3)

### PART 1

**1.**[^c-d22788388c865bdb814f2ca4f2464cf8] The Commission for Local Administration in England.

**2.**[^c-d1cbb2dfdb893ac287bac587ff682c13] In England, a county council, a district council, a London borough council or the Common Council of the City of London.

**3.**[^c-6b419c5a259f3596ea3808ef34fd4ade] In Wales, a county council or a county borough council.

**4.**[^c-daafe59725d26987242b089cdb22c90f] A joint board, body or committee appointed under any Act or statutory order or statutory scheme, of which all the constituent authorities are councils of a description in paragraph 2 or 3 or a combination of such councils.

**5.**[^c-64a412632fb8acd0e88b7c4f6852b56a] A fire and rescue authority within the meaning of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00078].

**6.**[^c-2b49ac865384ad7f0825d09fc6ed4d71] A police authority within the meaning of the Police Act 1996[^f00079].

**7.**[^c-644bfda68aeea0d90f597f621458f097] A probation trust established under section 5 of the Offender Management Act 2007[^f00080].) or a National Probation Service local board

**8.**[^c-b50be0d43cd4eaa93fc6726d8afee29f] The Chichester Harbour Conservancy.

**9.**[^c-194b740f82fe01794b4ea4c27788a0cf] The Lee Valley Regional Park Authority.

**10.**[^c-207afc20a4c4d0f9042f7669fff243ba] A passenger transport authority.

**11.**[^c-f0176865fe997c86b8d5c74f63e1d8f5] The Broads Authority.

**12.**[^c-cc8ce247c296d3bccf331571386b6f28] A further education corporation.

**13.**[^c-a36fdb1ac78949edb44d65d126e0df80] A higher education corporation.

**14.**[^c-9bc666873c8608c8648ddf1d22079ae7] The London Pensions Fund Authority.

**15.**[^c-e858015f2fd716519eb63d81b044a068] The South Yorkshire Pensions Authority.

**16.**[^c-23933228f75319ca45644f6dc8d3ff52] The Environment Agency.

**17.**[^c-93d12710e647677ed1a1589a863d3563] A National Park Authority established under Part 3 of the Environment Act 1995[^f00081].

**18.**[^c-2298962fe45032e135690be98ed168d4] An Education Action Forum within the meaning of section 11 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00082].

**19.**[^c-754c0ef8085fc678b5fc6e872a00547d] The National College for School Leadership.

**20.**[^c-38df351b36d0b8f17a6285ee661f8103] The Standards Board for England.

**21.**[^c-ce2811f25f5dcf09107c1e1cdcea1a19] An Academy within the meaning of section 482 of the Education Act 1996[^f00083] or by virtue of section 67 of the Education Act 2002 (conversion of city academies into Academies)[^f00084].

**22.**[^c-2068f3cc459d0e711c3e56f35dda4bd8] A body set up by a local housing authority in exercise of powers under section 2 of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00085] as a housing management company to exercise management functions of the authority under an agreement approved by the Secretary of State under section 27 of the Housing Act 1985[^f00086].

**23.**[^c-74ad05ceeef2f883848fb5307b8c537a] The Valuation Tribunal Service for England established under section 105 of the Local Government Act 2003[^f00087] and the Valuation Tribunal Service for Wales established under regulation 5 of the Valuation Tribunals (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00088].

**24.**[^c-d7d68561ced4a50bba1c920c5962ec6f] A conservation board established under section 86 of the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000[^f00089].

**25.**[^c-5e7727cccd81b4dd0307c81038c60a73] Firebuy Limited established under Section 29 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

### PART 2

**1.**[^c-c3abb1e854f9ad6cf1e3c36f31fd2c74] The Board of Governors of the Museum of London;

**2.**[^c-ed9ce9f62cb0df82fc0541f2a190cdfa] A body (other than a body listed in Part 1 of this Schedule) which is—

- (a) a precepting authority (as defined in section 69 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00090]),
- (b) a levying body within the meaning of section 74 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 (levies)[^f00091], or
- (c) a body to which section 75 of that Act (special levies) applies.

**3.**[^c-446bcc979412560496267449596f48c3] A passenger transport executive.

**4.**[^c-6269c8083c9caf940f583407be102fa1] A designated institution which immediately before designation was assisted or maintained by a local education authority.

**5.**[^c-116c8f709e28f7c33ab6e44ca10e4fa8] A company under the control of a body listed in Part 1 of this Schedule where “under the control” has the same meaning as in section 68 or, as the case may be, section 73 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00092] (except that any direction given by the Secretary of State must be disregarded, and any references to a local authority treated as references to such a body).

**6.**[^c-b3b0a1f4dca0fe9543bcde4653d544d5] The Public Services Ombudsman for Wales.

**7.**[^c-3afc001bf20ecdf99b5ef9862d4b8b88] The Serious Organised Crime Agency.

**8.**[^c-28fac1a84e25f0f778c292c8bda53a5d] Transport for London.

**9.**[^c-9ba9df8eca6e1bb99856e53791722894] The London Development Agency.

**10.**[^c-0e4fda2a17f9aa8a6bb7807859d3925f] The Metropolitan Police Authority.

**11.**[^c-04e4cabe4b138f09807f185e33c6d1f9] The London Transport Users’ Committee.

**12.**[^c-28437c2370522d2679023dccdfe9f2d4] The Cultural Strategy Group for London.

**13.**[^c-3bc7592e76b2793a7bcaca6356b67048] The Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service.

**14.**[^c-658a3536b2171f16403a3ad8f514e20d] An urban development corporation.
