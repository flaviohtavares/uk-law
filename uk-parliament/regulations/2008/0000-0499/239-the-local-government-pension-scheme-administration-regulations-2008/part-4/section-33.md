# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, section 33

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Accounts and audit[^c-ca559cffaea083da6103d08a42253c8c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) After any of its pension funds has been audited, an administering authority must immediately send copies of the following to each body whose employees are active members—

- (a) a summary of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund; and
- (b) any report by the auditor.

(2) The pension input period for the purposes of section 238 of the Finance Act 2004[^f00034] is the year ending on 31st March 2009 and each year ending on 31st March after that year.
