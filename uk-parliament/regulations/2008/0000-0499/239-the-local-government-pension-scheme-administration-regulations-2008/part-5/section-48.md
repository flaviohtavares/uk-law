# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Pension increases under the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^c-bfaeee5f7822936308f1fb109c0cdcb3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00041] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) must be paid from the appropriate fund.
