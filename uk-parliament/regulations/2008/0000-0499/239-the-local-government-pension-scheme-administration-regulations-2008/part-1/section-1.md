# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^key-038a53e3b9df2cc07ca13840034d1c7b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st April 2008.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England and Wales[^f00002].
