# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, section 54

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Interpretation of Part[^c-7800bb7c8cc2405c72e6b7482eed74cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part a reference to the employing authority or the appropriate administering authority of a prospective member is a reference to the body that would be his employer or appropriate administering authority if he were to become an active member in the employment by virtue of which he would be eligible to join the Scheme.
