# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations 2008, section 87

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 87. Changes of fund and variable time employees[^c-07af6e9402d5dd5bd9db93f50f338679]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

An inter-fund transfer under regulation 86(2) in respect of a member who is a variable-time employee prior to the transfer and who remains a variable-time employee after the transfer shall be on the basis that his period of membership transfers on a day for day basis.
