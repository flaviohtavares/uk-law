## SCHEDULE — AMENDMENT OF THE EXPORT CONTROL (DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC OF CONGO) ORDER 2005

Article 2

**1.**[^key-c1860b278692745464e806d34303fa42] In article 1(2), in the definition of “the Regulation” after the words “13 June 2005” insert “as amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No 1377/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1377) of 26 November 2007[^f00003]”.

**2.**[^key-05911cb46b59a4e0ff420bcb6764a963] In article 3, before the words “Any person who” insert “Subject to article 3A,”.

**3.**[^key-87d6752b480dfd0dfb40896a36d467bc] After article 3, insert—

> **3A.** No person shall be guilty of an offence under paragraph (b) of article 3 where he did not know, or had no reason to suspect, that his actions would contravene the prohibition at article 2(b) of the Regulation.

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order implements in part Council Regulation [(EC) 1377/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1377) which amends Council Regulation [(EC) 889/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0889)[^f00004].

Council Regulation [(EC) 1377/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1377) was adopted pursuant to Council Common Position 2007/654/CFSP[^f00005] integrating the measures contained in UN Security Council Resolution 1771 (2007).

Council Regulation [(EC) 1377/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1377) also makes provision for recent developments in sanctions practice regarding the identification of competent authorities, liability for infringements and jurisdiction. It clarifies that the prohibition set out in Article 2(b) of Council Regulation [(EC) 889/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0889) (provision of financing or financial assistance related to military activities etc) shall not give rise to any liability, if the person concerned did not know, nor have any reasonable cause to suspect, that his actions would infringe the prohibition.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of business.
