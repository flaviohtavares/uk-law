# The A556 (M) Motorway (M6 to M56 Link) and Supplementary Connecting Roads Scheme 1996 (Revocation) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 232

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A556 (M) Motorway (M6 to M56 Link) and Supplementary Connecting Roads Scheme 1996 (Revocation) Order 2008

Made

30th January 2008

Coming into force

7th March 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16, 17 and 19 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and all other enabling powers:-

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A556 (M) Motorway (M6 to M56 Link) Supplementary Connecting Roads Scheme 1996 (Revocation) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 7th March 2008.

**2.** The A556 (M) Motorway (M6 to M56 Link) Supplementary Connecting Roads Scheme 1996[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Bagley

Divisional Director Highways Agency

30th January 2008
