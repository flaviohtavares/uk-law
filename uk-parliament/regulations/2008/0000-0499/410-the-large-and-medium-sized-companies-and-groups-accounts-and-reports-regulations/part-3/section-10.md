# The Large and Medium-sized Companies and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2008, section 10

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Directors' report

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-3880848768f6ba12e6b320fd8f82012c] The report which the directors of a company are required to prepare under section 415 of the 2006 Act (duty to prepare directors' report) must disclose the matters specified in Schedule 7 to these Regulations.

(2)[^c-00d5a9565ff3255a2bcf531e295e1791] In Schedule 7—

- Part 1 relates to matters of a general nature including political donations and expenditure,
- Part 2 relates to the acquisition by a company of its own shares or a charge on them,
- [^c-a7233f1afdbf1fcfb5e5ce9d3ba49133]...
- [^c-55003f516ec4c1bc87863a5ad366e4c3]...
- Part 6 relates to certain disclosures required by publicly traded companies, [^c-be7d00b069223a4f4a5c2b59bfddd5fd]...
- Part 7 relates to disclosures in relation to greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption and energy efficiency action by quoted companies,[^c-242ca5a3c5414f2758ef4e3bad74b719]
- Part 7A relates to disclosures in relation to greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption and energy efficiency action by unquoted companies,[^c-37a049c22dce7191553ac4a517d5f2ea] [^c-c6fa1b3931ed866bd8d6f08fb62f4ea1]...
- Part 8 relates to the statement of corporate governance arrangements[^c-89e110feecf2b236e7f57521eaa54690] , and[^c-7081ae389fb4ec708aca0b9d73153038]
- Part 9 relates to a company’s payment practices and performance in respect of payments made by the company to its suppliers.
