# The Large and Medium-sized Companies and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2008, section 14

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Review[^c-a5e40af26d912190983d2eeba08cbfe1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must from time to time—

- (a)[^c-cf9b61731821f8b1eb864a2cb9ad6ac4] carry out a review, respectively, of the provisions of these Regulations to which amendments have been made by—
  - (i) Part 6 of the Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016 (“the 2016 Regulations”), [^c-526edc2c516ae58af15b00ead4cfe0a7]...
  - (ii) Part 3 of the Companies (Miscellaneous Reporting) Regulations 2018, [^c-ef95e0f24672e8942f27d671570b86c0]...
  - (iii)[^c-0031f8c95a4728de042d743967062031] Part 2 of the Companies (Directors’ Report) and Limited Liability Partnerships (Energy and Carbon Report) Regulations 2018, and
  - (iv)[^c-c456cbc2e41257c6b2b1cd6f94a8fbd2] the Companies (Directors’ Report) (Payment Reporting) Regulations 2025, and
- (b) set out the conclusions of each review[^c-7d255eb6588da8b6d62cbaae3c09fe59] in a separate[^c-8ab0d92288366918e2cf2cc55965d248] report, and
- (c) publish the report.

(2) The report must, in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by those provisions,
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved,
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate, and
- (d) if those objectives remain appropriate, assess the extent to which they could be achieved in another way which involves less onerous regulatory provision.

(3)[^c-8a6f885f7745338697b5c0bd04d222f2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The first report under paragraph (1)(a)(i)[^c-c317ea917fffd88fca0496a928f1c345] must be published before the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date on which the 2016 Regulations come into force.

(4A)[^c-b1bf9fff96165e79907e734c68797eda] The first report under paragraph (1)(a)(ii) must be published before the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date on which Part 3 of the Companies (Miscellaneous Reporting) Regulations 2018 comes into force.

(4B)[^c-47b350ee72ae91eb5289ce67c2a93198] The first report under paragraph (1)(a)(iii) must be published before the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date on which the Companies (Directors’ Report) and Limited Liability Partnerships (Energy and Carbon Report) Regulations 2018 come into force.

(4C)[^c-ae8c580ddf7d9dc9a7766de09ab7341a] The first report under paragraph (1)(a)(iv) must be published on or before the 6th April 2029.

(5) Subsequent reports under paragraph (1)(a)(i) to (iv)[^c-05359fe9a7e477d01260dd29a02dda14][^c-87556bad6fc8ccf93750c77b2b7ebf8e] respectively must be published at intervals not exceeding 5 years.

(6) In this regulation, “regulatory provision” has the meaning given by section 32(4) of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015.
