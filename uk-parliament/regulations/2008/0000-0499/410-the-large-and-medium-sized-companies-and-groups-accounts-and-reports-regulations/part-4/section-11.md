# The Large and Medium-sized Companies and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2008, section 11

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Directors' remuneration report (quoted companies ...)[^key-2252b729ee0b828164b7ae76c985b89b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The remuneration report which the directors of a quoted company [^key-46fa931dd52e2c77a2334c61c09d44e4]... are required to prepare under section 420 of the 2006 Act (duty to prepare directors' remuneration report) must contain the information specified in Schedule 8 to these Regulations, and must comply with any requirement of that Schedule as to how information is to be set out in the report.

(1A)[^key-e4ffdbf12c62cd681ae631197cc2ff7c] The document setting out a revised directors’ remuneration policy in accordance with section 422A of the 2006 Act must contain the information specified in Schedule 8 to these Regulations, and must comply with any requirements in that Schedule as to how that information is to be set out.

(2)[^key-0491726f6788e3fde4ec1c229d5a7acb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of section 497 in Part 16 of the 2006 Act (auditor's report on auditable part of directors' remuneration report), “**the auditable part**” of a directors' remuneration report is the information set out in the report as identified in Part 5[^key-5f349e4a0fbd06504fc132bcd37db114] of Schedule 8 to these Regulations.
