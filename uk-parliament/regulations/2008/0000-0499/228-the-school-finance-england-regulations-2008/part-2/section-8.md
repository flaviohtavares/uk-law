# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2008, section 8

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Consultation[^c-4d52d0bdd5c80a83e2c1af395d6399c1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local education authority may make changes to the formulae they have used in the financial years beginning 1st April 2006 and 1st April 2007.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), a local education authority must consult their schools forum about any proposed changes under paragraph (1), in relation to the factors and criteria taken into account, and the methods, principles and rules adopted.

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply to changes made relating to matters referred to in regulation 21 (sixth form funding) or 23 (excluded pupils).
