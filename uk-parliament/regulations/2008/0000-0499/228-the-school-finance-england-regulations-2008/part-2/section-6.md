# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2008, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2008-02-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/228
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Exceptions[^key-c8237fc153d490df5e78305d4851a86f]

*Extent: E+W*

A local education authority’s LEA budget or schools budget must not include the following classes or descriptions of expenditure—

- (a) capital expenditure, other than
  - (i) CERA,
  - (ii) capital expenditure appropriated to the schools budget for the purpose of funding pay arrears due to staff whose salaries are met from the schools budget;
- (b) expenditure on capital financing, other than expenditure incurred
  - (i) on prudential borrowing, or
  - (ii) for the purpose of meeting the costs of financing the payment of pay arrears referred to in paragraph 6(a)(ii);
- (c) expenditure for the purposes of section 26 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00028] (arrangements for patrolling school crossings); and
- (d) expenditure met by the local education authority under section 51A of the 1998 Act[^f00029] (community purposes).
