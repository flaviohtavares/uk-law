# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2008, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2008-02-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/228

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Required content of schemes[^key-a4957c94918cc5b07df720d183fa373c]

*Extent: E+W*

A scheme prepared by a local education authority under section 48(1) of the 1998 Act[^f00034] must deal with the matters connected with the financing of schools maintained by the authority set out in Schedule 5.
