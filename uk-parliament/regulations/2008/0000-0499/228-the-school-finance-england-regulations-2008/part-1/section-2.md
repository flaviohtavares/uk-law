## 2. Revocation of previous Regulations[^key-e494c8f1ba3c3bf57836cb0d0a674d18]

*Extent: E+W*

The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) 2004[^f00024],The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget Regulations 2004[^f00025], and The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005[^f00026] are revoked on 1st April 2008.
