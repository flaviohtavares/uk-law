# The Immigration (Employment of Adults Subject to Immigration Control) (Maximum Penalty) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 132

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Employment of Adults Subject to Immigration Control) (Maximum Penalty) Order 2008

Made

22nd January 2008

Coming into force

29th February 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(2) of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006[^f00001].

In accordance with section 20(2) of that Act, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as The Immigration (Employment of Adults Subject to Immigration Control) (Maximum Penalty) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 29th February 2008.

## 2. Maximum Penalty

For the purposes of section 15(2) of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (employment of adults subject to immigration control: penalty notice) the prescribed maximum is £10,000.

Liam Byrne

Minister of State

Home Office

22nd January 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the maximum penalty which may be imposed by the Secretary of State under section 15(2) of the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 on an employer who acts contrary to that section in the employment of an adult subject to immigration control.
