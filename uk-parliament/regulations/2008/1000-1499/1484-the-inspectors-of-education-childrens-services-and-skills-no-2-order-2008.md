# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1484

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2008

Made

11th June 2008

Coming into force

12th June 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of June 2008

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2008 and comes into force on 12th June 2008.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council
