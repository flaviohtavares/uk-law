# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Gloucestershire) (Forest of Dean District) Designation Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1212

Road Traffic, England

The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Gloucestershire) (Forest of Dean District) Designation Order 2008

Made

28th April 2008

Laid before Parliament

1st May 2008

Coming into force

26th May 2008

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 8 and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 10 to the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001].

Gloucestershire County Council has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under these powers with respect to part of its area.

The Secretary of State has consulted the chief officer of the Gloucestershire Police in accordance with the requirements of paragraphs 8(3) of Schedule 8 and 3(4) of Schedule 10.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Gloucestershire) (Forest of Dean District) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 26th May 2008.

## 2. Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

The Secretary of State designates the area described in the Schedule as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and
- (b) a special enforcement area.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton

Minister of State

Department for Transport

28th April 2008

## SCHEDULE — Area designated as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area

Article 2

**1.** This Order applies to the Forest of Dean District except—

- (a) the M48 for its entire length where it passes through the district; and
- (b) the M50 for its entire length where it passes through the district including its on and off slip roads.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the area described in the Schedule as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant effect on the cost to business and the voluntary sector.
