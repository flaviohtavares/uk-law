# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Commencement of contract

The contract shall provide for services to be provided under it from any date that falls after 31st July 2008.
