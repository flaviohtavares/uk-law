# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Other contractual terms: additional services

Where a contract is for the provision of additional services, it must contain other terms that have the same effect as those specified in Schedule 2.
