# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Services: general

A contract for mandatory services must specify—

- (a) the address of each of the premises to be used by the contractor for the provision of such services; and
- (b) the hours during which services are normally to be provided at each of those premises.
