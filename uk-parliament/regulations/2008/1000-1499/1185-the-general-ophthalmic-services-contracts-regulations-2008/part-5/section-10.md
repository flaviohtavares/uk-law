# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. NHS contracts

In the case of a contractor who is to be regarded as a health service body pursuant to regulation 8, the contract must state that it is an NHS contract.
