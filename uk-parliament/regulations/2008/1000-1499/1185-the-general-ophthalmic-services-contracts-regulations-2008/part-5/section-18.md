# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Other contractual terms: mandatory services

Where a contract is for the provision of mandatory services, it must contain other terms which have the same effect as those specified in Schedule 1.
