# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Arrangements on termination[^c-4fc208eab8a8be6f37f811847b2ebfb3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A contract shall make suitable provision for arrangements on the termination of the contract including the consequences (whether financial or otherwise) of the contract ending.
