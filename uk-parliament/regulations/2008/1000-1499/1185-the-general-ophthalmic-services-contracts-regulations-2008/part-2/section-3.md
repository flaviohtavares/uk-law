# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Conditions: introductory

Subject to the provisions of any regulations made under section 43 of the Health Act 2006 (general ophthalmic services: transitional), a PCT may only enter into a contract if—

- (a) the conditions set out in regulation 4 are met, and
- (b) an application to enter into a contract, which complies with Schedule 3, has been made to that PCT.
