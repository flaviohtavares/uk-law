# The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Reasons

(1) Where a PCT is of the view that the conditions in regulation 4 for entering into a contract are not met, it shall notify the person intending to enter into the contract of—

- (a) that view and its reasons for that view; and
- (b) the person’s right of appeal under regulation 6.

(2) The PCT shall also notify of that view and its reasons for that view, a director, chief executive or secretary of a corporate body that is notified under paragraph (1), where its reason for the decision relates to that person or those persons.
