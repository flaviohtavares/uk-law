# The Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1158 (C. 51)

Cultural Objects

The Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008

Made

21st April 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 148(3) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of Part 6 of the Act, in so far as they apply in relation to Wales and in so far as they extend to Northern Ireland come into force on 22nd April 2008.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

21st April 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the fourth commencement order made under the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act [2007 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15). The Order brings into force Part 6 of that Act in so far as it applies in relation to Wales, and in so far as it extends to Northern Ireland.
