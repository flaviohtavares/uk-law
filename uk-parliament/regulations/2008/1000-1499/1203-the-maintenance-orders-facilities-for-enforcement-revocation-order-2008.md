# The Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Revocation) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1203

Maintenance Of Dependants

The Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Revocation) Order 2008

Made

7th May 2008

Coming into force

19th June 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 7th day of May 2008

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 19 of the Maintenance Orders Act 1958[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Revocation) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 19 June 2008.

## 2. Revocation

“Jersey” shall be omitted from the First Schedule to the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Order 1959[^f00002].

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Order 1959 insofar as it extends the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Act 1920 to Jersey.

Jersey is designated as a reciprocating country for the purposes of Part I of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972 by the Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders (Designation of Reciprocating Countries) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1202)), which comes into force on the same date as this Order.
