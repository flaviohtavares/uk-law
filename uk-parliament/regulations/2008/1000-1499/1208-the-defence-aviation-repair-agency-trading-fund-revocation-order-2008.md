# The Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1208

Government Trading Funds

The Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2008

Made

28th April 2008

Laid before the House of Commons

1st May 2008

Coming into force

23rd May 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 23rd May 2008.

## 2. Revocation

The Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund Order 2001[^f00002] is revoked.

Des Browne

Secretary of State for Defence

25th April 2008

We concur

Frank Roy

Alan Campbell

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

28th April 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund Order 2001 (“the 2001 Order”) as amended by the Defence Aviation Repair Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 2008 (“the 2008 Order”). As a result of the revocation, the trading fund established by the 2001 Order, and amended by the 2008 Order, ceases to exist.

The Electronics and Large Aircraft operations of the Defence Aviation Repair Agency (DARA) is financed by means of a new trading fund, the Defence Support Group (DSG), which was established under the Defence Support Group Trading Fund Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/563](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/563)). The assets and liabilities relating to those operations were appropriated to the new DSG Trading Fund.

The remaining operations of DARA, namely the Rotary Wing and Components business units, cease to be financed by means of a trading fund; those operations have been sold to Vector Aerospace International Ltd.
