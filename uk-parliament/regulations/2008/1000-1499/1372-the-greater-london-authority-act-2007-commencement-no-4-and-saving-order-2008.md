# The Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No.4 and Saving) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1372 (C. 61)

London Government

The Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No.4 and Saving) Order 2008

Made

19th May 2008

Coming into force

27th June 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 53(1), 59(7) and (9) of the Greater London Authority Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving) Order 2008.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 27th June 2008

Subject to article 3, section 30 of the Greater London Authority Act 2007 shall come into force on 27th June 2008.

## 3. Saving

Section 15 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004[^f00002] shall continue to apply, in the form in which it was in force immediately before 27th June 2008, in relation to any local development scheme submitted to the Secretary of State before that date.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Caroline Flint

Minister of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

19th May 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force section 30 of the Greater London Authority Act 2007 (local development schemes), on 27th June 2008.

Article 3 of this Order makes a saving for the purposes of local development schemes submitted to the Secretary of State before 27th June 2008.
