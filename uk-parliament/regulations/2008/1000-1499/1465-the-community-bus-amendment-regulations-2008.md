# The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1465

Public Passenger Transport

The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

5th June 2008

Laid before Parliament

12th June 2008

Coming into force

1st August 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(1)(a) and 60(1)(e) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and sections 126(1) and 134 of the Transport Act 1985[^f00002] and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00003].

Representative organisations have been consulted in accordance with section 61(2) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st August 2008.

## 2. Amendments

In regulation 4(1) of The Community Bus Regulations 1986[^f00005], for “£53” substitute “£55”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department for Transport

5th June 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee for a community bus permit granted under section 22 of the Transport Act 1985 from £53 to £55. This represents an increase of 3.8%. The fee was last increased on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007.

A full Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from: VOSA Corporate Office, Berkeley House, Croydon Street, Bristol, BS5 0DA. A copy of the Impact Assessment is also annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website at www.opsi.gov.uk.
