# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 45

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Saving for regulated rights, etc.[^key-2c4a32d16f08a0ecb251f219ceb25c53]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in this Order shall affect any estate, right or interest granted by Network Rail in respect of a railway facility which is subject to an access contract within the meaning of Part 1 of the Railways Act 1993[^f00026].
