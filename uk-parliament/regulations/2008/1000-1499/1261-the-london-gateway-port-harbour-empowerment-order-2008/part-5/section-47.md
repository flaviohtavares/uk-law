# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 47

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Conservation[^key-5686cbc882d8154a0a8a677b11725d18]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Harbour Authority shall have the power to dedicate or designate for conservation or ecological purposes any land in which the Harbour Authority has an interest or over which it has control.

(2) The Harbour Authority shall not dedicate or designate under this article any land that abuts the river without the prior approval of the PLA, as provided for in paragraph 17 of Schedule 8.
