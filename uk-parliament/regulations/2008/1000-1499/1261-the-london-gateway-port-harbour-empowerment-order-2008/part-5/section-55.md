# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 55

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. Harbour Authority not to be a local lighthouse authority[^c-a6cab3a5958849bbaeb9ca8d92ba2c10]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 193(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00030], nothing in this Order constitutes the Harbour Authority as a local lighthouse authority.
