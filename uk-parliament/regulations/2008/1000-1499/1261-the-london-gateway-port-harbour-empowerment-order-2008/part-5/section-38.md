# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 38

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Time when charges are payable and their recovery[^key-895b90cb8f09ee95a3d71b690be16bce]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Charges which the Harbour Authority may demand, take and recover in respect of vessels shall be payable on the entry to London Gateway Port of any vessel in respect of which the charges are payable.

(2) Once payable, those charges may be demanded, taken and recovered by such persons, at such places and such times and under such terms and conditions as the Harbour Authority may from time to time appoint.
