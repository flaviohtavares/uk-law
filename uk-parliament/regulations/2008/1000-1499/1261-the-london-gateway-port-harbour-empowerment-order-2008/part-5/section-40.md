# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 40

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Disputes concerning rates[^key-d289e01718cbe5dc277e681d5b8de327]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 46 (disputes concerning rates or charges occasioned by distress) of the 1847 Act as incorporated in this Order by article 3 (incorporation of the Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847) shall be read as though references to “a justice” were references to “a court of competent jurisdiction”.
