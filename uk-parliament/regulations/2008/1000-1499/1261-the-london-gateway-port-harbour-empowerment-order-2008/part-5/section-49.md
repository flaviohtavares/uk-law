# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 49

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. Arrangements with Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^key-95f633de3e8b55409d1b978ac5195714]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Harbour Authority and Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs may enter into any such agreement or arrangement as they think fit to provide for, or to facilitate, the assessment, collection or recovery of charges, including an agreement or arrangement as to the provision and maintenance of accommodation at the port premises.
