# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 36

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Power to enter vessels[^key-0e764cac79ef14d574ed60cc4a1d15c4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A duly authorised officer of the Harbour Authority may on producing, if so required, his authority, board a vessel within the area of jurisdiction to—

- (a) ascertain the charges payable on or in respect of the vessel or goods carried in the vessel; and
- (b) obtain any other information required for the assessment and collection of charges.

(2) Nothing in this article applies to any vessel that is under the control of any officer of the PLA.
