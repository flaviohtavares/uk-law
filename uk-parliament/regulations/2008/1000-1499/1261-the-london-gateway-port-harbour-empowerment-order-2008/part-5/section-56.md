# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 56

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Harbour Authority not to be a port health authority[^c-90932fe8564189f1b5e2dc7345fbcddc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 2 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00031], the Harbour Authority may not be designated as a port health authority.
