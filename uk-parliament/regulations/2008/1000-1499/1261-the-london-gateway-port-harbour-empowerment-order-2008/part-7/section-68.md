# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 68

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68. Local inquiries[^c-ab3aa9d8f7dfec7ef4b34a4acc5ff90b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may cause to be held such inquiries as she may consider necessary in connection with the exercise of any powers or duties conferred or imposed upon her and the giving of any consent or approval under this Order, and subsections (2) to (5) of section 250 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00032] shall apply to any such inquiry as if it were an inquiry held in pursuance of subsection (1) of that section and the Harbour Authority were a local authority.
