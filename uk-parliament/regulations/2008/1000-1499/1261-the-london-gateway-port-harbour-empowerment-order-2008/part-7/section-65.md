# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 65

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. No double recovery[^c-ce2697d1e581998edb8dc4e0a55b4346]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation shall not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
