# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, section 9

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Agreements entered into by the Company, etc.[^c-ae4e30e9e2c803e7d601531207f458c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any agreement or undertaking entered into by the Company before the coming into force of this Order in connection with the proposed exercise of its functions as Harbour Authority shall be binding upon the Harbour Authority notwithstanding that it was entered into by the Company before it was established as a Harbour Authority by article 8 (jurisdiction of the Harbour Authority).
