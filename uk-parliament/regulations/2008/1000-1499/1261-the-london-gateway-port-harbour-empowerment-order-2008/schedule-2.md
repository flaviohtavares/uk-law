# The London Gateway Port Harbour Empowerment Order 2008, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — RESTRICTIONS ON WORKS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 10(5)

**1.**[^key-8f94407806ca16026f91b5e6186c6d37] In this Schedule, “building” does not include any form of crane or flood lighting column or similar such structure.

### Containers and associated equipment

**2.**[^key-4f7d7691d886c70b9d6ccaa4a849e0fe] Within the port premises, containers may not be stacked above an elevation of 35 metres AOD.

**3.**[^key-f62b0c7ff7ef1582465230ff67b9028e] Within the port premises, gantry cranes used for stacking or otherwise handling containers may not be installed or used above an elevation of 48 metres AOD.

**4.**[^key-c58a31d553e0854b587c7c88fc96ca5a] Quayside container cranes may only be used in the area hatched brown on the planning restrictions plan and must not—

- (a) exceed 35 in number;
- (b) have an elevation (with boom up) exceeding 145 metres AOD; or
- (c) have an elevation (with boom down in operating condition) exceeding 100 metres AOD.

**5.**[^key-80d8b20bb88ad5dfad997447ef481c3d] Quayside equipment other than container cranes, such as general cargo cranes and oil products loading arms, may only be installed and operated in the area hatched brown on the planning restrictions plan and may not have an elevation exceeding 137 metres AOD.

### Other height restrictions

**6.**[^key-3197f18039f389b3fab4430482a9d3b2] Subject to paragraph 10, in the area washed in yellow on the planning restrictions plan, buildings may not be constructed above an elevation of 40 metres AOD.

**7.**[^key-df329d60311b47503a715d24005c31c5] In the area washed in blue on the planning restrictions plan, buildings may not be constructed above an elevation of 13 metres AOD except for one building to be used for port marine control purposes and its associated radio and radar masts and antennae, which may be constructed to a maximum elevation of 60 metres AOD.

**8.**[^key-265bcb25feee261656ae719c316c2342] Material dredged pursuant to article 13 (power to dredge) may only be stored or stockpiled in the area stippled on the planning restrictions plan and up to an elevation of 25 metres AOD.

**9.**[^key-1447bb9bc0bb548e9eadaa7245aadda3] Within the port premises, internal harbour security fencing shall not exceed 4 metres in height above ground level.

### Railways

**10.**[^key-02a77db106bdc585e2c82b2f45d5aa19] In the area cross-hatched on the planning restrictions plan, railway lines and associated railway plant, machinery, bridges and buildings may not be constructed above an elevation of 20 metres AOD.

**11.**[^key-55ac309027afa36c34e257f166d82a90] Only in the area cross-hatched on the planning restrictions plan may the following take place—

- (a) the construction, operation, re-laying, improvement and reconstruction of at grade and grade-separated crossings of rail and road; and
- (b) the relocation or reconstruction, for the purposes of the operation of the undertaking, of the works described in Work No. 10.

### Other construction restrictions

**12.**[^key-f8295552f598526b56a456d69c661da0] No flood lighting columns shall be constructed within 50 metres of the riverward boundaries of the port premises but this shall not prevent the use of lights on equipment referred to in paragraphs 4 and 5.

**13.**[^key-676dfb6da1343d916a1ef54dfec9f6e6] No more than five entry and exit complexes with associated gate facilities, barriers, fencing, inspection and security huts to serve the port premises are permitted.

**14.**[^key-b4fb5b03b2f5f69a3bb91437799247dc] No more than one multi-storey car storage facility is permitted with a maximum gross internal floor space of 425,000 square metres and a maximum height of 20 metres AOD.

**15.**[^key-3b9adff692e6a53baca4daef5f6668de] No more than one multi-storey car park is permitted with a maximum gross internal floor space of 20,000 square metres and a maximum height of 20 metres AOD.

### Activities permitted for construction or operational purposes only

**16.**[^key-3467a20cd1a974c3abe73743697d053f] Once constructed—

- (a) no part of the water frontage of the authorised works may be used other than for the berthing of vessels; and
- (b) no part of the water frontage of the authorised works between Coordinates Reference 1 and Coordinates Reference 7 as shown on Sheet No. 216 of the deposited plans may be used for the berthing of container vessels or roll-on roll-off ships.

**17.**[^key-fae79e7dbee25cc51a7041ea08df89b5] Construction plant, concrete batching plant, temporary grading, washing and weighing plant, temporary bridges, conveyor systems, haul roads, accesses, temporary buildings and offices and plant working spaces are only permitted to facilitate the construction or maintenance of the authorised works and for no other purpose and shall be removed once the use for which they were installed has ceased.

**18.**[^key-e8cf5ede1d6ca0cb069c689635ae5d67] Storage of fuels including diesel, petroleum or liquid petroleum gas within the port premises is only permitted for the purposes of the operation of the undertaking.

**19.**[^key-984fce6c78e509939ff7a4cd95f21639] The underground storage and pumping of storm, surface and waste water, and the construction of pump houses, foul sewage pumping stations and drainage systems is only permitted within the port premises to the extent that those activities and facilities are required for the purposes of the operation of the undertaking.

**20.**[^key-17a4e7e4ee04037b778d5b639ff61115] The construction and operation of electricity sub-stations is only permitted within the port premises to the extent required for the purposes of the operation of the undertaking.

**21.**[^key-8ef5ad3ad80bc1e6aaa8ba97a89a12d5] No signage is permitted within the port premises other than that which is required for the purposes of the operation of the undertaking. This may include, without limitation, directional signage, signage required for health and safety purposes as well as logos and branding on structures or apparatus.

**22.**[^key-2fbcf03f9ff7bc6ce3c1a62659c1a00e] No relocation, reconstruction or improvement of the existing bitumen and aviation fuel pipeline within the port premises is permitted other than for the purposes of the construction of the authorised works.

**23.**[^key-5a2fc1726a1a8a7a362daf382866a515] Subject to paragraph 24, the area of jurisdiction shall not be used for any purpose other than as a harbour for container handling together with ancillary cargo and other uses incidental to the functions of the Harbour Authority and such other uses as are authorised by this Order.

**24.**[^key-453164cf095b4124df06e50271c5479d] Paragraph 23 shall not preclude—

- (a) the permanent use of no more than 36 hectares of the area of jurisdiction for Ro-Ro use, including car import and export and ancillary uses;
- (b) the temporary use of the whole or part of the area of jurisdiction in excess of 36 hectares for Ro-Ro use, for separate periods for each part, or for the whole, of no more than five years in any one case;
- (c) the permanent use of facilities and land within the area of jurisdiction for the handling of oil and other fuel products; or
- (d) the use during construction of any part of the area of jurisdiction for the stockpiling, storage and handling of dredged materials by way of an aggregates wharf.

### Application of Town and Country Planning legislation

**25.**[^key-d1ef10664d0d7d566bd3b67e79d1186a] The provisions of this Schedule shall be deemed to be conditions subject to which a planning permission was granted under section 70 of the 1990 Act and, accordingly, they shall be subject to the provisions of that Act and all associated legislation and, in particular, they may be varied in their application to the authorised works in accordance with sections 73, 73A and 97 of the 1990 Act and the Town and Country Planning (General Development Procedure) Order 1995[^f00033].
