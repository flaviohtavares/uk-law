# The Home Information Pack (Amendment)(No. 2) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1266

Housing, England And Wales

The Home Information Pack (Amendment)(No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made

6th May 2008

Laid before Parliament

8th May 2008

Coming into force

1st June 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 163 and 250(2) of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001].

In accordance with section 250(3) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the Welsh Ministers[^f00002] in relation to residential properties in Wales.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Home Information Pack (Amendment)(No. 2) Regulations 2008.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1st June 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Home Information Pack (No.2) Regulations 2007

The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are amended by the substitution, for “1st June 2008”, of “1st January 2009” in the following provisions—

- (a) regulation 10A(1) (leasehold information included during a temporary period)[^f00004];
- (b) regulation 16(1)(a) (energy information unobtainable before or at the first point of marketing); and
- (c) regulation 34(2)(a) (first day marketing during a temporary period).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Caroline Flint

Minister of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

6th May 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007 (“the principal Regulations”). Under sections 155 to 159 of the Housing Act 2004, a home information pack must comply with the principal Regulations.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 10A(1) (leasehold information included during a temporary period), regulation 16(1)(a) (energy information unobtainable before or at the first point of marketing) and regulation 34(2)(a) (first day marketing during a temporary period) to extend until 1st January 2009 the exemptions set out in those regulations.
