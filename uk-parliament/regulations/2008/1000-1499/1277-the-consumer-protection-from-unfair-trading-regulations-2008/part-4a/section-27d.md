# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27D

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27D. What financial services are covered by this Part? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “product” does not include a service provided in the course of carrying on a regulated activity within the meaning of section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, other than a service to which paragraph (2) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) This paragraph applies to a service consisting of the provision of credit under an agreement which is a restricted-use credit agreement within paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of that term in article 60L(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) But paragraph (2) does not apply to an agreement under which the obligation of the borrower to repay is secured by a legal or equitable mortgage on land (other than timeshare accommodation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) In paragraph (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- “mortgage” includes a charge and (in Scotland) a heritable security; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- “timeshare accommodation” means overnight accommodation which is the subject of a timeshare contract within the meaning of the Timeshare, Holiday Products, Resale and Exchange Contracts Regulations 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) The fact that the supply of a product within regulation 2(1A) and (1B) may constitute an activity within article 39F (debt-collecting) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 does not prevent this Part from applying in relation to that supply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
