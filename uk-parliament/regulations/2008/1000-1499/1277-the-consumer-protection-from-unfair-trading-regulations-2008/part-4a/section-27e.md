# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27E

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27E. When does the right to unwind apply to a business to consumer contract? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer has the right to unwind in respect of a business to consumer contract if the consumer indicates to the trader that the consumer rejects the product, and does so— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) within the relevant period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) at a time when the product is capable of being rejected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) An indication under paragraph (1) may be something that the consumer says or does, but it must be clear. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) In paragraph (1)(a) “the relevant period” means the period of 90 days beginning with the later of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the day on which the consumer enters into the contract, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the relevant day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) In this Part “the relevant day” means the day on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the goods are first delivered, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the performance of the service begins, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) the digital content is first supplied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (d) the lease begins, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (e) the right is first exercisable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(as the case may be). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) But in the case of a mixed contract, “the relevant day” means the latest of the days mentioned in paragraph (4) that is relevant to the contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) In this Part “mixed contract” means a contract relating to a product which consists of any two or more of goods, a service, digital content, immoveable property or rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(7) For the purposes of this Part, where the consumer’s access to digital content on a device requires its transmission to the device under arrangements initiated by the trader, the day on which the digital content is first provided is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the day on which it reaches the device, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) if earlier, the day on which it reaches another trader chosen by the consumer to supply, under a contract with the consumer, a service by which digital content reaches the device. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), a product remains capable of being rejected only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the goods have not been fully consumed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the service has not been fully performed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) the digital content has not been fully consumed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (d) the lease has not expired, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (e) the right has not been fully exercised, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(as the case may be). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (8)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) goods have been fully consumed only if nothing is left of them, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) digital content has been fully consumed only if the digital content was available to the consumer for a fixed period and that period has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(10) A consumer does not have the right to unwind in respect of a business to consumer contract if the consumer has exercised the right to a discount in respect of that contract and the same prohibited practice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
