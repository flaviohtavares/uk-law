# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27H

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27H. How does the right to unwind work if payments are demanded which are not due? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer has the right to unwind in respect of a consumer payment for a product within regulation 2(1A) and (1B) if the consumer was not required to make all or part of the payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies, the consumer has the right to receive back from the trader— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the same amount of money as the consumer paid to the trader, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) in a case where the consumer was required to make part of the payment, an amount equal to the part of the payment the consumer was not required to make. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
