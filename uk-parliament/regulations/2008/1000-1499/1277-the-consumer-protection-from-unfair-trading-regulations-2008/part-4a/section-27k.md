# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27K

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27K. How can a consumer enforce the rights to redress? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer with a right to redress under this Part may bring a claim in civil proceedings to enforce that right. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) In Scotland, proceedings to enforce the right to unwind may be brought before the sheriff or in the Court of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) Paragraph (4) applies if in proceedings under this regulation the consumer establishes that the consumer has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the right to unwind, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the right to a discount, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) the right to damages. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) The court must make an order that gives effect to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) that right, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) any associated obligations of the consumer under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) The Limitation Act 1980 applies to a claim under this regulation in England and Wales as if it were an action founded on simple contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) The Limitation (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 applies to a claim under this regulation in Northern Ireland as if it were an action founded on simple contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
