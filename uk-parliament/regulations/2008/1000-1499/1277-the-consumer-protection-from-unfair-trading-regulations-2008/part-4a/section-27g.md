# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27G

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27G. How does the right to unwind work in the case of a consumer to business contract? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer who has a right to redress in respect of a consumer to business contract has the right to unwind in respect of that contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the consumer has the right to treat the contract as at an end so that the trader and the consumer are released from their obligations under it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the consumer has the right within paragraph (5) or (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) To treat the contract as at an end, the consumer must indicate to the trader that the contract is ended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) An indication under paragraph (3) may be something that the consumer says or does, but it must be clear. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) If the trader is able to return the goods to the consumer in the condition they were in when sold by the consumer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the consumer has a right to the return of the goods, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the consumer must repay to the trader the amount (if any) that the trader has paid for the goods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) If paragraph (5) does not apply, the consumer has a right to a payment from the trader of the amount (if any) by which the market price of the goods when the trader paid for them exceeds what the trader paid for them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
