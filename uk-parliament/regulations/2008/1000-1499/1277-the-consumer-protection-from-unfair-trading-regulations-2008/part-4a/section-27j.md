# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27J

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27J. How does the right to damages work? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject as follows, a consumer has the right to damages if the consumer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) has incurred financial loss which the consumer would not have incurred if the prohibited practice in question had not taken place, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) has suffered alarm, distress or physical inconvenience or discomfort which the consumer would not have suffered if the prohibited practice in question had not taken place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) The right to damages is the right to be paid damages by the trader for the loss or the alarm, distress or physical inconvenience or discomfort in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) The right to be paid damages for financial loss does not include the right to be paid damages in respect of the difference between the market price of a product and the amount payable for it under a contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) The right to be paid damages under this regulation is a right to be paid only damages in respect of loss that was reasonably foreseeable at the time of the prohibited practice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) A consumer does not have the right to damages if the trader proves that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the occurrence of the prohibited practice in question was due to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) a mistake, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] reliance on information supplied to the trader by another person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] the act or default of a person other than the trader, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] an accident, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (v) another cause beyond the trader’s control, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the trader took all reasonable precautions and exercised all due diligence to avoid the occurrence of the prohibited practice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
