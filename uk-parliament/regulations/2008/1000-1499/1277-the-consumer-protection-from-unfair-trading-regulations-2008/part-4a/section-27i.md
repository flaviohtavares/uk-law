# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27I

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27I. How does the right to a discount work? (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer has the right to a discount in respect of a business to consumer contract if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the consumer has made one or more payments for the product to the trader or one or more payments under the contract have not been made, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the consumer has not exercised the right to unwind in respect of the contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) If the consumer has made one or more payments, the consumer has the right to receive back from the trader the relevant percentage of the payment or payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) If one or more payments have not been made, the consumer has the right— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) to reduce by the relevant percentage as many of those payments as is appropriate having regard to the seriousness of the prohibited practice, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) in a case within paragraph (6), to reduce all of those payments by the relevant percentage. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) Subject to paragraph (6), the relevant percentage is as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) if the prohibited practice is more than minor, it is 25%, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) if the prohibited practice is significant, it is 50%, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) if the prohibited practice is serious, it is 75%, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (d) if the prohibited practice is very serious, it is 100%. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) The seriousness of the prohibited practice is to be assessed by reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the behaviour of the person who engaged in the practice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the impact of the practice on the consumer, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) the time that has elapsed since the prohibited practice took place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) Paragraph (4)[^key-2fec29483dbcca436724c0c35590bd5f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the amount payable for the product under the contract exceeds £5,000, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the market price of the product, at the time that the consumer entered into the contract, is lower than the amount payable for it under the contract, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) there is clear evidence of the difference between the market price of the product and the amount payable for it under the contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(7) In such a case, the relevant percentage is the percentage difference between the market price of the product and the amount payable for it under the contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(8) The application of this regulation does not affect any of the other rights and liabilities under the contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
