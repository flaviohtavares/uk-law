# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27L

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27L. How does this Part relate to the existing law? (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Part affects the ability of a consumer to make a claim under a rule of law or equity, or under an enactment, in respect of conduct constituting a prohibited practice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) But a consumer may not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) make a claim to be compensated under a rule of law or equity, or under an enactment, in respect of such conduct if the consumer has been compensated under this Part in respect of the conduct, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) make a claim to be compensated under this Part in respect of such conduct if the consumer has been compensated under a rule of law or equity, or under an enactment, in respect of the conduct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) In this regulation “enactment” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) an enactment contained in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, a Measure or Act of the National Assembly for Wales, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, an Act of the Scottish Parliament, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (d) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, Northern Ireland legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
