# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27A

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27A. When does a consumer have a right to redress? (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer has a right to redress under this Part if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the conditions in this regulation are met, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the conditions (if any) in the following provisions of this Part for the availability of that right are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) The first condition is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the consumer enters into a contract with a trader for the sale or supply of a product by the trader (a “business to consumer contract”), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the consumer enters into a contract with a trader for the sale of goods to the trader (a “consumer to business contract”), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) the consumer makes a payment to a trader for the supply of a product (a “consumer payment”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) Paragraph (2)(b) does not apply if, under the contract, the trader supplies or agrees to supply a product to the consumer as well as paying or agreeing to pay the consumer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) The second condition is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the trader engages in a prohibited practice in relation to the product, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) in a case where a consumer enters into a business to consumer contract for goods or digital content— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) a producer engages in a prohibited practice in relation to the goods or digital content, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] when the contract is entered into, the trader is aware of the commercial practice that constitutes the prohibited practice or could reasonably be expected to be aware of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) In paragraph (4)(b) “producer” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a manufacturer of the goods or digital content, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) an importer of the goods or digital content into the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] United Kingdom[^c-d5a748d35e2bafcdb9a2bb1d953a6fdd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) a person who purports to be a producer by placing the person’s name, trade mark or other distinctive sign on the goods or using it in connection with the digital content, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

and includes a producer acting personally or through another person acting in the producer’s name or on the producer’s behalf. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) The third condition is that the prohibited practice is a significant factor in the consumer’s decision to enter into the contract or make the payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
