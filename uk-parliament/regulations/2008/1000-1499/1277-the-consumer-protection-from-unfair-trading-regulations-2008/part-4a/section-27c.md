# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27C

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27C. What immoveable property is covered by this Part? (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “product” does not include immoveable property other than a relevant lease. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) In this regulation “relevant lease” in relation to England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] [^key-3064c4f6fe72c30eb4b286794ccae548]... means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) an assured tenancy within the meaning of Part 1 of the Housing Act 1988, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a lease under which accommodation is let as holiday accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) But none of the following are relevant leases for the purposes of paragraph (2)(a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a lease granted by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) a private registered provider of social housing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] a registered social landlord within the meaning of Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a lease of a dwelling-house or part of a dwelling-house— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) granted on payment of a premium calculated by reference to a percentage of the value of the dwelling-house or part or of the cost of providing it, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] under which the lessee (or the lessee’s personal representatives) will or may be entitled to a sum calculated by reference, directly or indirectly, to the value of the dwelling-house or part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) a lease granted to a person as a result of the exercise by a local housing authority within the meaning of the Housing Act 1996 of its functions under Part 7 (homelessness) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) In this regulation “relevant lease” in relation to Scotland means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) an assured tenancy within the meaning of Part 2 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a lease under which accommodation is let as holiday accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) In this regulation “relevant lease” in relation to Northern Ireland means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a private tenancy within the meaning of Article 3 of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a lease under which accommodation is let as holiday accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) But neither of the following are relevant leases for the purposes of paragraph (5)(a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a lease of a dwelling-house or part of a dwelling-house— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) granted on payment of a premium calculated by reference to a percentage of the value of the dwelling-house or part or of the cost of providing it, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] under which the lessee (or the lessee’s personal representatives) will or may be entitled to a sum calculated by reference, directly or indirectly, to the value of the dwelling-house or part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a private tenancy resulting from the exercise by the Northern Ireland Housing Executive of its functions under Part 2 (homelessness) of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(7)[^key-5bc704de62e479deeb30222729982171] In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]**relevant lease *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]**” in relation to Wales means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a standard contract, within the meaning given by the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 8 of that Act), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a lease under which accommodation is let as holiday accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(8) But none of the following are relevant leases for the purposes of paragraph (7)(a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) an introductory standard contract within the meaning given by the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 16 of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) a prohibited conduct standard contract within the meaning given by the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 116 of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) a supported standard contract within the meaning given by the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 143 of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (d) a lease under which the interest of the landlord belongs to Her Majesty in right of the Crown or to a government department or is held in trust for Her Majesty for the purposes of a government department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (e) a lease granted to a person as a result of the exercise by a local housing authority within the meaning of the Housing (Wales) Act 2014 of its functions under Part 2 (homelessness) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (f) a lease of a dwelling-house or part of a dwelling-house— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (i) granted on payment of a premium calculated by reference to a percentage of the value of the dwelling-house or part or of the cost of providing it, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27] under which the contract-holder (or the contract-holder’s personal representatives) will or may be entitled to a sum calculated by reference, directly or indirectly, to the value of the dwelling-house or part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
