# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27B

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27B. What does “prohibited practice” mean in this Part? (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-6c25cfaf34150ed64c8db26ec56de113] In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]**prohibited practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]**” means a commercial practice involving— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) a misleading action under section 225(4)(a) and section 226 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) an aggressive practice under section 225(4)(a) and section 228 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) Section 225(4)(a) of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 applies for the purposes of this Part as if for the definition of “transactional decision” in section 245 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 there were substituted[^key-1b1a3689df97d1c8ac3f1969ceb52b86] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]—

> - “transactional decision” means any decision taken by a consumer to enter into a contract with a trader for the sale or supply of a product by the trader, or for the sale of goods to the trader, or to make a payment to a trader for the supply of a product. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
