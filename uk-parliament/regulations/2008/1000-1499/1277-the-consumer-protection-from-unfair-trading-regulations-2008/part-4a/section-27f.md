# The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, section 27F

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27F. How does the right to unwind work in the case of a business to consumer contract? (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f2d06ad7be50f0b3d5f25e84870e6044][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27][^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a consumer has the right to unwind in respect of a business to consumer contract— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the contract comes to an end so that the consumer and the trader are released from their obligations under it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the trader has a duty to give the consumer a refund (subject as follows), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (c) if the contract was wholly or partly for the sale or supply of goods the consumer must make the goods available for collection by the trader. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(2) The consumer’s entitlement to a refund works as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(3) To the extent that the consumer paid money under the contract, the consumer is entitled to receive back the same amount of money (but see paragraphs (7) to (10)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(4) To the extent that the consumer transferred anything else under the contract, the consumer is entitled to receive back the same amount of what the consumer transferred, unless paragraph (5) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(5) To the extent that the consumer transferred under the contract something for which the same amount of the same thing cannot be substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the consumer is entitled to receive back in its original state whatever the consumer transferred, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) if it cannot be given back in its original state, the consumer is entitled to be paid its market price as at the time when the product was rejected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(6) There is no entitlement to a refund if none of paragraphs (3) to (5) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(7) The consumer’s entitlement to receive back the same amount of money as the consumer paid is qualified by paragraphs (8) to (10) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the contract was for the sale or supply of a product on a regular or continuous basis, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the period beginning with the relevant day and ending with the day on which the consumer rejected the product exceeds one month. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(8) In that case the consumer is only entitled to receive back the amount (if any) found by deducting the market price, when the consumer rejected the product, of the product supplied up to that time from the amount the consumer paid for it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(9) But paragraph (8) does not apply if it is not appropriate to apply that deduction having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

- (a) the behaviour of the person who engaged in the prohibited practice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
- (b) the impact of the practice on the consumer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]

(10) Where the product supplied up to the time when the consumer rejected it consists wholly or partly of goods, their market price is only to be taken into account under paragraph (8) to the extent that they have been consumed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5bd671bee5a2362a1f43e02fa5790a27]
