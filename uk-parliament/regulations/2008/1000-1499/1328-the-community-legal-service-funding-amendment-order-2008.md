# The Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1328

Legal Services Commission, England And Wales

The Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

20th May 2008

Laid before Parliament

20th May 2008

Coming into force

1st July 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(4) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001]. He has consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society in accordance with section 25(2) of that Act and has had regard to the matters specified in section 25(3) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2008 and comes into force on 1st July 2008.

(2) In this Order “the 2007 Order” means the Community Legal Service (Funding) Order 2007[^f00002].

## 2. Transitional provision

Where—

- (a) an application for funded services is made before 1st July 2008; or
- (b) a client is in receipt of Legal Help before 1st July 2008 and the funded services are extended on or after that date to include Help at Court or Legal Representation,

the 2007 Order shall continue to have effect as if this Order had not been made.

## 3. Amendments to the 2007 Order

In article 3(1) of the 2007 Order, in the definition of “Unified Contract”, for “the contract document, the schedule” substitute “the contract for signature, the schedules”.

**4.** For article 5(1)(a) of the 2007 Order substitute—

> - (a) after competitive tendering has taken place (whether or not as to price) and under which funded services specified in the contract (including services provided at a specified place or on behalf of a specified type of client) may be provided only by the successful tenderers;

.

**5.** For the Schedule to the 2007 Order substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Community Legal Service (Funding) Order 2007, which imposes conditions on the funding of services as part of the Community Legal Service, by limiting the powers of the Legal Services Commission to pay remuneration under contract for the provision of funded services. Article 5 of this Order substitutes a new Schedule containing revised fees and rates payable for funded services for matters begun on or after 1st July 2008. Most of the fees and rates have been increased; the others remain the same. Article 4 excludes from the application of the Schedule certain contracts which are the subject of competitive tendering. Article 3 corrects the definition of “Unified Contract”.

An impact assessment for this instrument has been prepared and is available from Civil Legal Aid Strategy Division, Ministry of Justice, 54 Victoria Street, London SW1E 6QW. It is also annexed to the explanatory memorandum for this instrument, which is available at http://www.opsi.gov.uk.
