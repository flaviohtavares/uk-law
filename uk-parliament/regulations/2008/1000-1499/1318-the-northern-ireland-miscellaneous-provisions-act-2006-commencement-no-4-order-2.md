# The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No.4) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1318 (C. 56)

Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No.4) Order 2008

Made

13th May 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(3) of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

(1) The provisions of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 6 (alteration of registers: pending elections);
- (b) paragraphs 1 to 6 and sub-paragraphs 7(2)(b) to 7(5) of Schedule 4 (minor and consequential amendments); and
- (c) section 30(1) (minor and consequential amendments etc) in so far as it relates to the provisions referred to in sub-paragraph (b).

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

13th May 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the day after the day on which it is made the provisions of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 set out in article 2.
