# The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1407 (C. 62)

Criminal Law

The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2008

Made

29th May 2008

The Secretary of State, being the relevant national authority under section 66(3)(b) of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 66(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement

Part 1, Chapter 2 (Alcohol Disorder Zones) (sections 15 to 20) of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 shall come into force on 5th June 2008.

Vernon Coaker

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

29th May 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th June 2008 Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006. This chapter consists of sections 15 to 20 which provide for the designation of alcohol disorder zones by local authorities.
