# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments etc) Order 2008, section 8

> Text as at 2021-03-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The repeal of provisions of Article 46A of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 [^c19199621] (execution of documents) does not affect the operation of that Article—

- (a) in relation to a liquidator of a company [^c19199631], or
- (b) as applied by the Foreign Companies (Execution of Documents) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 [^c19199641].

(2) That Article as so applied does not apply to companies registered in Great Britain to which section 44 of the Companies Act 2006 applies.
