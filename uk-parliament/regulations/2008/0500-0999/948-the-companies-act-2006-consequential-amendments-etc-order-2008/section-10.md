# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments etc) Order 2008, section 10

> Text as at 2021-03-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Saving for earlier consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The repeal of section 50 of the Companies Act 1989 [^c19199651] or Article 52 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 [^c19199661] (appointment etc of auditors: power to make consequential amendments) does not affect any amendments made by regulations under that section [^c19199671] or Article [^c19199681] that are in force immediately before 6th April 2008.
