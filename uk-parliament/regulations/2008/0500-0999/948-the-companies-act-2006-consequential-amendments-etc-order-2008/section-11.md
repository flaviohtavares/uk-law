# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments etc) Order 2008, section 11

> Text as at 2021-03-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Saving for limited liability partnerships

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Order affects any provision of the Companies Act 1985 or the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 as applied by the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001 [^c19199691] or the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 [^c19199701] to limited liability partnerships.
