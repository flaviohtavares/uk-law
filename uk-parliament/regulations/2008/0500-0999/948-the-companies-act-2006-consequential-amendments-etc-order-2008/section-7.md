# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments etc) Order 2008, section 7

> Text as at 2021-03-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Savings for provisions relating to execution of documents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The repeal of provisions of section 36A of the Companies Act 1985 [^c19199601] (execution of documents) does not affect the operation of that section as applied by the Foreign Companies (Execution of Documents) Regulations 1994 [^c19199611].

(2) That section as so applied does not apply to companies registered in Northern Ireland to which section 44 of the Companies Act 2006 applies.
