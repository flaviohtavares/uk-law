# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Penalty Charge Notices, Enforcement and Adjudication) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 78, 81, 82 and 89 of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^c-6026f62f030a3e6482d1db1834074aec]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Penalty Charge Notices, Enforcement and Adjudication) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 31st March 2008.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.

## 2. Amendments to the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Penalty Charge Notices, Enforcement and Adjudication) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^c-490a7295445543a86958217ef2055875]

(1) The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Penalty Charge Notices, Enforcement and Adjudication) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) At sub-paragraph 1(g) of the Schedule, for the words “on which the alleged contravention occurred” substitute the words “on which the notice is served.”

signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Paul Murphy

Secretary of State for Wales

27th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the [Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Penalty Charge Notices, Enforcement and Adjudication) (Wales) Regulations 2008/609](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/609).

Specifically these Regulations amend sub-paragraph 1(g) of the Schedule, removing the words “on which the alleged contravention occurred” and replacing them with the words “on which the notice is served.”

The reason for the amendment is to ensure a fair system, whereby all persons receiving a penalty charge notice have equal time to pay the charge
