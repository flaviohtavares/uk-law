# The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 610 (C. 23)

Representation Of The People

The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2008

Made

5th March 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 77(2) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No.6) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement of provisions of the Electoral Administration Act 2006

Section 63 (Regulation of loans: etc. Northern Ireland) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006 shall come into force on 10th March 2008.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

5th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 63 of the Electoral Administration Act [2006 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/22) on 10th March 2008.
