# The Tate Gallery Board (Additional Members) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 919

Museums And Galleries

The Tate Gallery Board (Additional Members) Order 2008

Made

20th March 2008

Coming into force

21st March 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 2(12)(a) of Schedule 2 to the Museums and Galleries Act 1992[^f00001](“the Act”):

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Tate Gallery Board (Additional Members) Order 2008 and comes into force on 21st March 2008.

## 2. Amendment

In paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Act, for “twelve” substitute “fourteen”.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

20th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Museums and Galleries Act 1992 so as to increase the number of members of the Tate Gallery Board from twelve to fourteen.
