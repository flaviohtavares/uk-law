# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Fees for inspections

(1) Unless regulation 44 or 48 applies, a fee in accordance with paragraphs 1 to 7 of Schedule 2 is payable for any inspection of a site made in connection with an application for, or during the currency of, a marketing authorization, a traditional herbal registration, a clinical trial authorisation, a manufacturing authorisation, a manufacturer’s licence or a wholesale dealer’s licence, except for an inspection for which a fee is payable under regulations 24 or 30.

(2) Unless regulation 28 or 29 applies, the fee in paragraph (1) is payable by the holder of or applicant for, as the case may be, the authorization, registration, authorisation or licence in relation to which the inspection is made.
