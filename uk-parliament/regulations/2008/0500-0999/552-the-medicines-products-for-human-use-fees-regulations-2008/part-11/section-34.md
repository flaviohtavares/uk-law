# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Fees for applications for membership and certificate

(1) The fee payable by an applicant for membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £120.

(2) The fee payable by an applicant for a certificate of membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £63.
