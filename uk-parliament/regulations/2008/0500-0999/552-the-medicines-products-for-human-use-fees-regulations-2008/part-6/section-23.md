# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Time for payment of fees under regulation 22

Unless regulation 44 applies, all sums payable by way of fees under regulation 22 (1) must be paid at the time when the proposed changes are submitted to the licensing authority.
