# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Payment of fees to Ministers

Any sums which under the provisions of these Regulations become payable by way of, or on account of, fees must be paid to one of the Ministers.
