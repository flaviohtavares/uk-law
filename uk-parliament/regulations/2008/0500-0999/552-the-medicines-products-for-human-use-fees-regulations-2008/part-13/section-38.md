# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Time for payment of capital fees in connection with applications or inspections

(1) Except where regulation 11, 17, 23 or 36 applies and subject to paragraph (2) and to regulations 39 and 44, all sums payable by way of capital fees under these Regulations in connection with any application must be paid at the time of the application.

(2) All sums payable by way of fees in respect of inspections made either in connection with an application for, or during the currency of, an authorization, licence or certificate must be paid within 14 days following receipt of written notice from the licensing authority requiring payment of those fees.
