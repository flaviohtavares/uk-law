# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Suspension of licences and certificates

Where any sum due by way of, or on account of, any fee or any part thereof payable under these Regulations remains unpaid by the holder of a product licence or a product licence of right, a manufacturer’s licence or a wholesale dealer’s licence, the licensing authority may serve a written notice on him requiring payment of the sum unpaid and, if after a period of one month from the date of service of such notice, or such longer period as the licensing authority may allow, the said sum remains unpaid, the licensing authority may forthwith suspend the licence until such sum has been paid.
