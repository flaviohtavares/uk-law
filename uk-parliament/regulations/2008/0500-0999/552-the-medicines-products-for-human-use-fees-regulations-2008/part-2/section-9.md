# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Fee for regulatory advice

Unless regulation 44 applies, the fee payable by the holder of a marketing authorization with whom the licensing authority holds a meeting in order to provide regulatory advice to that person, is £2,797.
