# The Medicines (Products for Human Use-Fees) Regulations 2008, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Time for payment of fees under regulations 4 to 10

Unless regulation 44 applies, all sums payable by way of fees under regulations 4 to 10 must be paid within 14 days following written notice from the licensing authority requiring payment of those fees.
