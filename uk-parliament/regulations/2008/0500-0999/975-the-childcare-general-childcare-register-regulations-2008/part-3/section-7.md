# The Childcare (General Childcare Register) Regulations 2008, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Matters to be considered by the Chief Inspector[^c-fec30c49b8d14c124f8402f9386530aa]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In exercising functions under Part 3 of the Act, the Chief Inspector[^f00005] is required to have regard to the requirements set out in Schedule 3.

**8.**[^c-d90e1fc410bd789159927a24ee152922] Any allegation that a later years provider to whom section 59 of the Act applies has failed to meet such of the requirements set out in Schedule 3 as are applicable to that provider, or to have regard to the matter specified in regulation 6(b), may be taken into account—

- (a) by the Chief Inspector in the exercise of functions under Part 3 of the Act, or
- (b) in any proceedings under that Part.
