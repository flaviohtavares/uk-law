# The Childcare (General Childcare Register) Regulations 2008, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Matters to be considered by the Chief Inspector

In exercising functions under Part 3 of the Act, the Chief Inspector[^f00005] is required to have regard to the requirements set out in Schedule 3.

**8.** Any allegation that a later years provider to whom section 59 of the Act applies has failed to meet such of the requirements set out in Schedule 3 as are applicable to that provider, or to have regard to the matter specified in regulation 6(b), may be taken into account—

- (a) by the Chief Inspector in the exercise of functions under Part 3 of the Act, or
- (b) in any proceedings under that Part.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: For the definition of “the Chief Inspector” see section 98(1) of the Childcare Act 2006, as amended by paragraph 117 of Schedule 14 to the Education and Inspections Act [2006 (c.40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40).
