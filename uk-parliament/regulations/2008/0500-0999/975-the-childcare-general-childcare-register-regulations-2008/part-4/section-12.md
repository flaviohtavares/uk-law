# The Childcare (General Childcare Register) Regulations 2008, section 12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Activities of persons registered in Part B of the general childcare register[^c-bd2fcf09631fc9932dfbae04982d33ea]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A person registered in Part B of the general childcare register must—

- (a) meet such of the requirements set out in Schedule 6 as are applicable to that person, and
- (b) in the provision of childcare have regard to the needs of each child relating to childcare.

**13.**[^c-973c032eafe069f437949a4ab3d5c95c] In exercising functions under Part 3 of the Act, the Chief Inspector is required to have regard to the requirements set out in Schedule 6.

**14.**[^c-dfe99a277d348db2b4f54b46000c8539] Any allegation that a person registered in Part B of the general childcare register has failed to meet such of the requirements set out in Schedule 6 as are applicable to that person, or to have regard to the matter specified in regulation 12(b), may be taken into account—

- (a) by the Chief Inspector in the exercise of functions under Part 3 of the Act, or
- (b) in any proceedings under that Part.
