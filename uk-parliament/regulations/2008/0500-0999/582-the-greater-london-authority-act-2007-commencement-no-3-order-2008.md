# The Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 582 (C.21)

London Government

The Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

Made

4th March 2008

Coming into force

6th April 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(7) and (9) of the Greater London Authority Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Greater London Authority Act 2007 (Commencement No.3) Order 2008.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 6th April 2008

The following provisions of the Greater London Authority Act 2007 shall come into force on 6th April 2008—

- (a) sections 31 to 36;
- (b) section 45;
- (c) sections 47 to 49;
- (d) section 51; and
- (e) section 57 and Schedule 2, so far as they are not yet in force and so far as they give effect to the repeals relating to the Museum of London Act 1986[^f00002].

Hazel Blears

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

4th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force on 6th April 2008, the following provisions of the Greater London Authority Act 2007, in so far as they are not already in force—

- sections 31 to 36 (planning);
- section 45 and sections 47 to 49 (Museum of London);
- section 51 (Mayor’s duty to exercise certain powers of appointment); and
- section 57 and Schedule 2 (consequential repeals in the Museum of London Act 1986).
