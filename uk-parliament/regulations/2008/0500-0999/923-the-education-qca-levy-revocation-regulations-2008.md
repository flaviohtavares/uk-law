# The Education (QCA Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 923

Education, England And Wales

Education, Northern Ireland

The Education (QCA Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2008

Made

25th March 2008

Laid before Parliament

8th April 2008

Coming into force

30th September 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 36 and 54(3) of the Education Act 1997[^f00001].

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (QCA Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 30th September 2008.

**2.** The Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002[^f00002] and the Education (QCA Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 [^f00003] are revoked.

David Lammy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Innovation, Universities and Skills

25th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002 and the regulations which amended them with effect from 30th September 2008.
