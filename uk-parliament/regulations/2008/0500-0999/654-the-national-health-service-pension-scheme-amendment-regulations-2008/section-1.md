# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 1

> Text as at 2008-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-e49689dd9b7eed32c4d484cf67fd4e6a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1st April 2008 and shall have effect from 1st April 2008, except for regulation 66(2) and (3)[^M_F_40cf6897-f444-4584-b75d-9d24e80f67e5], which shall have effect from 1st April 2003.

(3) In these Regulations—

- (a) an alpha-numerical reference to a regulation is a reference to a provision of the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995[^f00004] bearing that alpha-numeric;
- (b) an alphabetical reference to a Part is a reference to a Part of those Regulations bearing that letter.
