# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 34

> Text as at 2008-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Amendment of regulation H7[^c-3297c0f4e4e8a35280d810da783da668]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the heading to regulation H7 (dependent child not being maintained by surviving parent or spouse or civil partner of a parent), after “civil partner” insert “or nominated partner”.

(2) In regulation H7[^f00019] after “civil partner” (where three times occurring), insert “or nominated partner”.
