# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Cessation of reduction

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-444910144783d6326ef9f1095792041e] Any reduction imposed as a result of a failure determination which resulted from a failure to undertake work-related activity in accordance with the Employment and Support Allowance (Work-Related Activity) Regulations 2011 ceases to have effect if—

- (a)[^key-72173a489256f6f57ae33349d910d082] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the claimant subsequently ceases to be subject to a requirement to undertake work-related activity; or
- (c) the Secretary of State decides it is no longer appropriate to require the person to undertake work-related activity at that time.

(1A)[^key-cf5d235840c4e39c20872a9fc805ec0a] The Secretary of State must notify the person in writing—

- (a)[^key-e3c2bd5f533dd0cb5ea5d0bc895e094a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) of any decision under paragraph (1)(c).

(2) Any reduction imposed as a result of a failure determination which resulted from a failure to take part in a work-focused interview ceases to have effect if—

- (a)[^key-c8dca1b82cfee51a467005cc39adf14b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the claimant subsequently ceases to meet the requirements set out in regulation 54(2).
