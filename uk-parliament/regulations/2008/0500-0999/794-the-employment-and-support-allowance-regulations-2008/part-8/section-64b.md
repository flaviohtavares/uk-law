# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 64B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64B. Circumstances in which a claimant is to be treated as being in hardship[^c-9e7242575949065ccff474940882696e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A claimant is to be treated as being in hardship if the claimant’s partner, or a child or qualifying young person for whom the claimant or the claimant’s partner is responsible, is or will be in hardship unless a hardship payment is made.
