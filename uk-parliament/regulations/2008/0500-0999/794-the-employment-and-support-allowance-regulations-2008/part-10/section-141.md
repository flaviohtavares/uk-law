# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 141

## 141. Further disregard of student's income

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where any part of a student's income has already been taken into account for the purposes of assessing that student's entitlement to a grant, student loan or postgraduate[^c-9f2b8fbce09da7d0e478f692e0641099] [^c-64d8b6c8922d3ade8d288714db946e7d]... loan, the amount taken into account is to be disregarded in assessing that student's income.
