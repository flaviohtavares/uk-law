# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 100

## 100. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Chapter “**special account**” means, where a claimant was carrying on a commercial activity in respect of which assistance is received under the self-employment route, the account into which the gross receipts from that activity were payable during the period in respect of which such assistance was received.
