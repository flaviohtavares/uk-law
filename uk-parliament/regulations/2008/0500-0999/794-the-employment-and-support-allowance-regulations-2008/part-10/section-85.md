## 85. ... liable relative payments[^key-fefcfd743a141a685f9401ec8b717400]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 91 to 109, 111 to 117 and Chapter 10 of this Part do not apply to any payment which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter 8 of this Part (... liable relative payments[^key-f7e82bc43227830b0dad83896a2bf4bd]).
