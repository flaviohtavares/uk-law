# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 121. Disregard of payments treated as not relevant income

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Secretary of State treats any payment as not being relevant income for the purposes of section 74A of the Administration Act [^c1918974] (payment of benefit where maintenance payments collected by Secretary of State), that payment is to be disregarded in calculating a claimant's income.
