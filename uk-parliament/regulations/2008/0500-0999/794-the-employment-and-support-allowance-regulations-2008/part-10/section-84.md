# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 84. Income of participants in the self-employment route

*Extent: E+W+S*

Chapters 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 of this Part and regulations 132 to 137, 142 and 143 do not apply to any income which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter 5 of this Part (participants in the self-employment route).
