# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 143

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 143. Disregard of changes occurring during summer vacation

*Extent: E+W+S*

In calculating a student's income there is to be disregarded any change in the standard maintenance grant occurring in the recognised summer vacation appropriate to the student's course, if that vacation does not form part of the student's period of study, from the date on which the change occurred up to the end of that vacation.
