## 89. Calculation of income where pension payments, PPF periodic payments or councillor's allowance payable

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding the other provisions of this Part, regulation 94(1) and (6) is to apply for the purposes of calculating the amount of any pension payments, PPF periodic payments or councillor's allowance to which Chapter 2 of Part 9 (deductions from the contributory allowance) applies.
