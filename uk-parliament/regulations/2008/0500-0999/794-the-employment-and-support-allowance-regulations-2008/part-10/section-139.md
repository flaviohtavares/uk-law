# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 139

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 139. Treatment of fee loans

*Extent: E+W+S*

A loan for fees, known as a fee loan or a fee contribution loan, made pursuant to regulations made under Article 3 of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 [^c1919001], section 22 of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998 [^c1919002] or section 73(f) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 [^c1919003], is to be disregarded as income.
