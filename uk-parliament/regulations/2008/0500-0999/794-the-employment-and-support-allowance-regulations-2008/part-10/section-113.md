# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 113. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S*

Capital which a claimant possesses in the United Kingdom is to be calculated at its current market or surrender value less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10%; and
- (b) the amount of any incumbrance secured on it.
