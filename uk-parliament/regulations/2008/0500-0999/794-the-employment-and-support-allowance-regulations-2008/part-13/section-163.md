# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 163

## 163. Applicable amounts in urgent cases

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of calculating any entitlement to an income-related allowance under this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]

- (a) except in a case to which sub-paragraph (b) or (c) applies, a claimant's weekly applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (i)[^c-426339b42893b338c238d47aff21aa07] 90% of the amount applicable in respect of himself or herself or, if the claimant is a member of a couple or of a polygamous marriage, of the amount applicable in respect of both of them under paragraph 1(1), (2) or (3) of Schedule 4 or, as the case may be, the amount applicable in respect of them under regulation 68 (polygamous marriages); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 2 of Schedule 4 (premiums); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 4 of Schedule 4 (components); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d) (housing costs); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
- (b) in the case of a claimant to whom any paragraph of Schedule 5 (special cases) applies, the applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (i)[^c-6299997ba8e7f7aa442351d50f60051e] 90% of the amount applicable in column 2 of that Schedule in respect of the claimant and partner (if any); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 2 of Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 4 of Schedule 4; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
- (c) in the case of a claimant to whom regulation 162(2) applies, where that claimant is appealing to an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Social Security Act 1998, against a decision which embodies a determination that the claimant does not have limited capability for work, the applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (i)[^c-92f7e852e7877aa4f0998e464122ae09] 90% of the amount applicable in respect of himself or herself or, if the claimant is a member of a couple or of a polygamous marriage, of the amount applicable in respect of both of them under paragraph 1(1), (2) or (3) of Schedule 4 or, as the case may be, the amount applicable in respect of them under regulation 68 (polygamous marriages); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 2 of Schedule 4 (premiums); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
