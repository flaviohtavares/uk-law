# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 162

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 162. Urgent cases

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb] In a case to which this regulation applies a claimant's weekly applicable amount and that claimant's income and capital are to be calculated in accordance with the following provisions of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), this regulation applies to a claimant who is treated as possessing income under regulation 107(1) (notional income). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]

(3) This regulation is only to apply to a claimant to whom paragraph (2) applies, where the income that claimant is treated as possessing by virtue of regulation 107(1) is not readily available to that claimant; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]

- (a) the amount of an income-related allowance which would be payable but for this Part is less than the amount of an income-related allowance payable by virtue of the provisions of this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
- (b) the Secretary of State is satisfied that, unless the provisions of this Part are applied to the claimant, the claimant or the claimant's family will suffer hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-491db776d7671be1770af65d45dc2ddb]
