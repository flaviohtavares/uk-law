# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 78. Date from which a change in the rate of the payment takes effect

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a payment or councillor's allowance is already being made to a claimant and the rate of that payment or that allowance changes, the deduction at the new rate must take effect, calculated where appropriate in accordance with regulation 94(1) or (6), from the first day of the benefit week in which the new rate of the payment or councillor's allowance is paid.
