# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 72A

## 72A. Financial Assistance Scheme[^c-363c1fc0ae96b8d6ee7387ec6a7ce0d2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of sections 2(1)(c) and 3 of the Act (deductions from contributory allowance) pension payment is to include a Financial Assistance Scheme payment.

(2) In this regulation “Financial Assistance Scheme payment” means a payment made under the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005.
