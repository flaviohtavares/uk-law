# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 72. Permanent health insurance

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of sections 2(1)(c) and 3 of the Act (deductions from contributory allowance) pension payment is to include a permanent health insurance payment.

(2) In this regulation “**permanent health insurance payment**” means any periodical payment arranged by an employer under an insurance policy providing benefits in connection with physical or mental illness or disability, in relation to a former employee on the termination of that person's employment.
