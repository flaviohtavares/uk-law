## 67. Prescribed amounts

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulations 68, 69 [^c-1ff07c686a27e774c2b11434e26eb4ca]... (amounts in other cases, special cases [^c-1ff07c686a27e774c2b11434e26eb4ca]...), the amounts prescribed for the purposes of the calculation of the amount of an income-related allowance under section 4(2)(a) of the Act in relation to a claimant are such of the following amounts as may apply in the claimant's case—

- (a) an amount in respect of the claimant or, if the claimant is a member of a couple, an amount in respect of both of them determined in accordance with paragraph A1(1), (2) or (3) of Part A1 of Schedule 4[^c-f04cb12bb24864a5a6c88243bad07d1e] (amounts) as the case may be;
- (b) the amount of any premiums which may be applicable to the claimant determined in accordance with Parts 2 and 3 of that Schedule (premiums);
- (c) any amounts determined in accordance with Schedule 6 (housing costs) which may be applicable to the claimant in respect of housing costs as prescribed[^c-a6200c058cc7b03a2df6d495013ff45d] in that Schedule.

(2) Subject to regulation 69 (special cases) the amount prescribed for the purposes of the calculation of the amount of a claimant's contributory allowance under section 2(1)(a) of the Act is the amount determined in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Part 1 of[^c-0add2ae6c928f7ad3b6c2ff0a75c8fb7] Schedule 4 as may apply in the claimant's case.

(3) Subject to regulation 69, the amount of the work-related activity component and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2954dd4a30ae01d87613fdaea45f1e3c] support component are *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0fec36f454b383424e5c23cd29d06432] is prescribed in Part 4 of Schedule 4.
