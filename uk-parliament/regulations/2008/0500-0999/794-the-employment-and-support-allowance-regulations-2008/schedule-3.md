# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, SCHEDULE 3

## SCHEDULE 3 — ASSESSMENT OF WHETHER A CLAIMANT HAS LIMITED CAPABILITY FOR WORK-RELATED ACTIVITY[^c-d0f14a01d09b76f406fbb7cbeab850c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 34(1)

| Activity | Descriptors |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Mobilising unaided by another person with or without a walking stick, manual wheelchair or other aid if such aid is normally, or could reasonably be, worn or used.[^c-b3f72a14e40c0d3de3c2d109274fa179] | Cannot either:<br>- (a) mobilise more than 50 metres on level ground without stopping in order to avoid significant discomfort or exhaustion; or<br>- (b) repeatedly mobilise 50 metres within a reasonable timescale because of significant discomfort or exhaustion. |
| 2\. Transferring from one seated position to another. | Cannot move between one seated position and another seated position located next to one another without receiving physical assistance from another person. |
| 3\. Reaching. | Cannot raise either arm as if to put something in the top pocket of a coat or jacket. |
| 4\. Picking up and moving or transferring by the use of the upper body and arms (excluding standing, sitting, bending or kneeling and all other activities specified in this Schedule). | Cannot pick up and move a 0.5 litre carton full of liquid. |
| 5\. Manual dexterity. | Cannot press a button (such as a telephone keypad) with either hand or cannot turn the pages of a book with either hand.[^c-f7d958a45522429a55e4712eada495f1] |
| 6\. Making self understood through speaking, writing, typing, or other means which are normally, or could reasonably be, used, unaided by another person.[^c-285468042b5e53234936b5b6bee5ef56] | Cannot convey a simple message, such as the presence of a hazard. |
| 7\.<br>Understanding communication by:[^c-994bcc1a4c8e91029feb0ba0d57acd48]<br>- (i) verbal means (such as hearing or lip reading) alone,<br>- (ii) non-verbal means (such as reading 16 point print or Braille) alone, or<br>- (iii) a combination of (i) and (ii),<br>using any aid that is normally, or could reasonably be, used, unaided by another person. | Cannot understand a simple message, such as the location of a fire escape,[^c-d0517e03dcf24ab1f742f286bb32553a] due to sensory impairment[^c-e13d7d389feb5f16375b364b312360d4].... |
| 8\. Absence or loss of control whilst conscious leading to extensive evacuation of the bowel and/or voiding of the bladder, other than enuresis (bed-wetting), despite the wearing or use of any aids or adaptations which are normally, or could reasonably be, worn or used.[^c-3c16941718f44c46f1ecfbeb8a8af20f] | At least once a week experiences:<br>- (a) loss of control leading to extensive evacuation of the bowel and/or voiding of the bladder; or<br>- (b) substantial leakage of the contents of a collecting device<br>sufficient to require the individual to clean themselves and change clothing. |
| 9\. Learning tasks. | Cannot learn how to complete a simple task, such as setting an alarm clock, due to cognitive impairment or mental disorder. |
| 10\. Awareness of hazard. | Reduced awareness of everyday hazards, due to cognitive impairment or mental disorder, leads to a significant risk of:<br>- (a) injury to self or others; or<br>- (b) damage to property or possessions<br>such that the claimant requires[^c-db2c8a48956290d59b4000ef785a603d] supervision for the majority of the time to maintain safety. |
| 11\. Initiating and completing personal action (which means planning, organisation, problem solving, prioritising or switching tasks). | Cannot, due to impaired mental function, reliably initiate or complete at least 2 sequential personal actions. |
| 12\. Coping with change. | Cannot cope with any change, due to cognitive impairment or mental disorder, to the extent that day to day life cannot be managed. |
| 13\. Coping with social engagement, due to cognitive impairment or mental disorder. | Engagement in social contact is always precluded due to difficulty relating to others or significant distress experienced by the claimant[^c-a88cbc19b04d56f1392ec4b58b50b25b]. |
| 14\. Appropriateness of behaviour with other people, due to cognitive impairment or mental disorder. | Has, on a daily basis, uncontrollable episodes of aggressive or disinhibited behaviour that would be unreasonable in any workplace. |
| 15\. Conveying food or drink to the mouth. | - (a) Cannot convey food or drink to the claimant’s own mouth without receiving physical assistance from someone else;<br>- (b) Cannot convey food or drink to the claimant’s own mouth without repeatedly stopping or[^c-6019f6c7d4426e78b720774e58b2a439] experiencing breathlessness or severe discomfort;<br>- (c) Cannot convey food or drink to the claimant’s own mouth without receiving regular prompting given by someone else in the claimant’s physical presence; or<br>- (d) Owing to a severe disorder of mood or behaviour, fails to convey food or drink to the claimant’s own mouth without receiving:<br>  - (i) physical assistance from someone else; or<br>  - (ii) regular prompting given by someone else in the claimant’s presence. |
| 16\. Chewing or swallowing food or drink. | - (a) Cannot chew or swallow food or drink;<br>- (b) Cannot chew or swallow food or drink without repeatedly stopping, experiencing breathlessness or severe discomfort;<br>- (c) Cannot chew or swallow food or drink without repeatedly receiving regular prompting given by someone else in the claimant’s presence; or<br>- (d) Owing to a severe disorder of mood or behaviour, fails to:<br>  - (i) chew or swallow food or drink; or<br>  - (ii) chew or swallow food or drink without regular prompting given by someone else in the claimant’s presence. |
