# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/794

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Circumstances in which the condition that the claimant is not receiving education does not apply

*Extent: E+W+S*

Paragraph 6(1)(g) of Schedule 1 to the Act does not apply where the claimant is entitled to a disability living allowance , Scottish adult disability living allowance[^c-f0c71687e7a8d5ad1c4f79d630a1903d] , child disability payment[^c-4ba07f7a1e390aa07ef337c959103bef], armed forces independence payment[^c-038c2d6172466f62f868276d83587b31] ,[^c-b609f317489f4dba8428180d5ce9fe0e][^c-75bbc2e5f22bd5c8a095ffd9e05e8c75] personal independence payment or adult disability payment[^c-a1e42eda35956fa28c495a016b07b425].
