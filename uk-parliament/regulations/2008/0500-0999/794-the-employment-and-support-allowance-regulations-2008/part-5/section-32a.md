# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008, section 32A

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32A. Claimants to be treated as not having limited capability for work at the end of the period covered by medical evidence[^c-ab65f5205fa56318beac6cc6282aea94]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-55783edb25cb40c6ba038539d0bcc96a] Where the Secretary of State is satisfied that it is appropriate in the circumstances of the case [^c-af5025006d50b3e210f06533a22e3c6b]... a claimant may be treated as not having limited capability for work if—

- (a) the claimant has supplied medical evidence [^c-c83edb2762955534bef5de980e1f5865]...;
- (b) the period for which medical evidence was supplied has ended;
- (c) the Secretary of State has requested further medical evidence; and
- (d) the claimant has not, before whichever is the later of either the end of the period of 6 weeks beginning with the date of the Secretary of State’s request or the end of 6 weeks beginning with the day after the end of the period for which medical evidence was supplied—
  - (i) supplied further medical evidence, or
  - (ii) otherwise made contact with the Secretary of State to indicate that they wish to have the question of limited capability for work determined.

(2)[^c-141ae9f8588286c7f15af8b116a5ff8f] In this regulation “medical evidence” means evidence provided under regulation 2 or 5 of the Medical Evidence Regulations.
