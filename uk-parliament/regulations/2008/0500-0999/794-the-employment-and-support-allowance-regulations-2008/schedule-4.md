### PART 2 — Premiums

Regulations 67(1)(b) and 68(1)(c)

**2.** Except as provided in paragraph 4, the weekly premiums specified in Part 3 of this Schedule are, for the purposes of regulation 67(1)(b) and 68(1)(c), to be applicable to a claimant who satisfies the condition specified in paragraphs 5 to 8 in respect of that premium.

**3.** An enhanced disability premium in respect of a person is not applicable in addition to a pensioner premium.

**4.** (1) For the purposes of this Part of this Schedule, once a premium is applicable to a claimant under this Part, a person is to be treated as being in receipt of any benefit—

- (a) in the case of a benefit to which the Social Security (Overlapping Benefits) Regulations 1979 [^c1919030] applies, for any period during which, apart from the provisions of those Regulations, the person would be in receipt of that benefit; [^c-63091dcdf46d08ebcb24be4ffbe2956e]...
- (b) for any period spent by a person in undertaking a course of training or instruction provided or approved by the Secretary of State under section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973[^c1919031], or by Skills Development Scotland,[^c-9c126435580b758f4415991598e1f6fa] Scottish Enterprise or Highlands and Islands Enterprise under section 2 of the Enterprise and New Towns (Scotland) Act 1990 [^c1919032], or for any period during which the person is in receipt of a training allowance and[^c-f0d2f1cb6fde41bbd0beb9258577bb73]
- (c)[^c-f8e413459592078ddad2fbc247bfa78b] in the case of carer support payment, for any period during which, apart from regulation 16 of the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023, the person would be in receipt of that benefit.

(2)[^c-1e16ab86ad8a37132c5660a74952bbd3] For the purposes of the carer premium under paragraph 8, a claimant is to be treated as being in receipt of a carer’s allowance by virtue of sub-paragraph (1)(a) or carer support payment by virtue of sub-paragraph (1)(c)[^c-3ce3c830a6325c2c0eb743587932177f] only if and for so long as the person in respect of whose care the allowance or payment[^c-3c85e3f3cc5418ffc854cef97e6a9d2d] has been claimed remains in receipt of—

- (a) attendance allowance;
- (aa)[^c-2357d11792632e73e5a8da1bcdd7a6d8] pension age disability payment;
- (b) the care component of disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate prescribed in accordance with section 72(3) of the Contributions and Benefits Act; [^c-e865e7dbbd512050161e7b4eb4b8fdcc]...
- (ba)[^c-e18deb9e9cdf638da75cf701db8057c8] the care component of Scottish adult disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate in accordance with regulation 6(4) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025;
- (c) the daily living component of personal independence payment at the standard or enhanced rate in accordance with section 78(3) of the 2012 Act;[^c-5de6d1e2888cc15768ed023eb56a39e0] [^c-7612950e843bd261f79afefeeb81a550]...
- (ca)[^c-7eb41c4dbb575a2297d1c0241a4b845b] the daily living component of adult disability payment at the standard or enhanced rate in accordance with regulation 5 of the Disability Assistance for Working Age People (Scotland) Regulations 2022; or
- (d) armed forces independence payment.

#### 5. Pensioner premium

The condition is that the claimant or the claimant's partner has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit.

#### 6. Severe disability premium

(1) The condition is that the claimant is a severely disabled person.

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), a claimant is to be treated as being a severely disabled person if, and only if—

- (a) in the case of a single claimant, a lone parent, a person who has no partner and who is responsible for and a member of the same household as a young person[^c-e6abc5b89ec2a0f49f532805af4caef0] or a claimant who is treated as having no partner in consequence of sub-paragraph (3)—
  - (i) the claimant is in receipt of the care component, the daily living component[^c-7e5da8e58b08152e5836e144abf3eaab] , the daily living component of adult disability payment[^c-e9d613637295afccd0e7f5abbb782b27] , the care component of Scottish adult disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate in accordance with regulation 6(4) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025[^c-33ad964da2ee56dc77cb47d4c443c2dc] , armed forces independence payment[^c-ef7786278e6e5c7acaea9e2e2733bdf4] , attendance allowance or pension age disability payment[^c-66e8bd31f385ae15ec9ebdbfd24fd57d];
  - (ii) subject to sub-paragraph (4), the claimant has no non-dependants aged 18 or over normally residing with the claimant or with whom the claimant is normally residing; and
  - (iii) no person is entitled to, and in receipt of, a carer’s allowance[^c-4ada2b12877bf8510436d2752744895a] or carer support payment[^c-520c354b3d3220012653c5bbabe12dcc] or has an award of universal credit which includes the carer element in respect of caring for the claimant;
- (b) in the case of a claimant who has a partner—
  - (i) the claimant is in receipt of the care component, the daily living component[^c-54158a206cf5bdacbee3869870b0aba1] , the daily living component of adult disability payment[^c-cdcae531c7376c2c163057b75ec5acf3] , the care component of Scottish adult disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate in accordance with regulation 6(4) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025[^c-0fcbb19b63eadb3e6e5d5fbab04a2fa0] , armed forces independence payment[^c-ab743ea864b3239ce18e53ee77076ad1] , attendance allowance or pension age disability payment[^c-ae5ac3c04fc44aeead8c3a8c6881f1f0];
  - (ii) the claimant's partner is also in receipt of the care component, the daily living component[^c-c2d49c90b78759df1ab7538c43b91227] , the daily living component of adult disability payment[^c-dedad3a1c7450fca12d1526c224d5590] , the care component of Scottish adult disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate in accordance with regulation 6(4) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025[^c-384b0cf7e986c4c55c9cb290a63ccdea] , armed forces independence payment[^c-b29a067a18f705948acd089076009c23] , attendance allowance or pension age disability payment[^c-28ce6ebb727914a67b617ddbd713412f] or, if the claimant is a member of a polygamous marriage, all the partners of that marriage are in receipt of the care component, the daily living component , the daily living component of adult disability payment[^c-7f2051d7e7bb0da9a2b27264553e47d3] , armed forces independence payment , attendance allowance or pension age disability payment ; and
  - (iii) subject to sub-paragraph (4), the claimant has no non-dependants aged 18 or over normally residing with the claimant or with whom the claimant is normally residing,

  and, either a person is entitled to, and in receipt of, a carer's allowance or carer support payment[^c-6b14ec8c79c28084e0b398a76c8d077a] or has an award of universal credit which includes the carer element[^c-4e692938d0b5e690f9e904e5ef777363] in respect of caring for only one of the couple or, in the case of a polygamous marriage, for one or more but not all the partners of the marriage or, as the case may be, no person is entitled to, and in receipt of, such an allowance or payment[^c-388a45dd6ad9397ecd51eef2512d0d7f] or has such an award of universal credit[^c-7fca0d77395bcb8e1fc99d1dc7160867] in respect of caring for either member of the couple or any partner of the polygamous marriage.

(3) Where a claimant has a partner who does not satisfy the condition in sub-paragraph (2)(b)(ii) and that partner is blind or severely sight impaired or is treated as blind or severely sight impaired that partner is to be treated for the purposes of sub-paragraph (2) as if the partner were not a partner of the claimant.

(4) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (2)(a)(ii) and (b)(iii) no account is to be taken of—

- (a) a person receiving attendance allowance , pension age disability payment[^c-f6af26613f0a11591317133618ba258e] , the daily living component[^c-ba0d076cf297dec97a1b3da11293dfdc] , the daily living component of adult disability payment[^c-962f2d5f67378120c599c14a23cbbdc8] , the care component of Scottish adult disability living allowance at the highest or middle rate in accordance with regulation 6(4) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025[^c-300204068e490e16d6b5dd929cbbe2e5] , armed forces independence payment[^c-6195d2cbfa8babac18ed7e84f55644d8] or the care component;

- (c) a person who is blind or severely sight impaired or is treated as blind or severely sight impaired.

#### 8. Carer premium

(1) Subject to sub-paragraphs (2) , (4) and (5)[^c-ea82e9799b8c5720083d05ab8454d9a4], the condition is that the claimant or the claimant's partner is, or both of them are, entitled to a carer's allowance under section 70 of the Contributions and Benefits Act or carer support payment[^c-f882c683c34353674256c107adec9c89].

(2) Subject to sub-paragraph (5), where[^c-c93c7d3e43142e1968f59b74d88dcd67] a carer premium is awarded but—

- (a) the person in respect of whose care the carer's allowance or carer support payment[^c-1aacf00ce151f93bd658c45a941e677e] has been awarded dies; or
- (b) in any other case the person in respect of whom a carer premium has been awarded ceases to be entitled to a carer's allowance or carer support payment,

the condition for the award of the premium is to be treated as satisfied for a period of 8 weeks from the relevant date specified in sub-paragraph (3).

(3) The relevant date for the purposes of sub-paragraph (2) is—

- (a) where sub-paragraph (2)(a) applies, the Sunday following the death of the person in respect of whose care a carer's allowance or carer support payment[^c-e377c172c1c9fb2edfaf873027b748fe] has been awarded or the date of death if the death occurred on a Sunday; or
- (b) in any other case, the date on which the person who has been entitled to a carer's allowance or carer support payment ceases to be entitled to that allowance or payment[^c-f09e9c977cb38da5b7ef7da0781f588a].

(4) Subject to sub-paragraph (5), where[^c-350d41d1a17d053a5643aeeece62cf38] a person who has been entitled to a carer's allowance or carer support payment[^c-6f8583cb7ad28d3066fb7eea054620a4] ceases to be entitled to that allowance or payment[^c-6525ce1286dc9c4ac263924c73c07158] and makes a claim for an income-related allowance, the condition for the award of the carer premium is to be treated as satisfied for a period of 8 weeks from the date on which—

- (a) the person in respect of whose care the carer's allowance or carer support payment has been awarded dies; or
- (b) in any other case, the person who has been entitled to a carer's allowance or carer support payment ceased to be entitled to that allowance or payment.

(5)[^c-0de0d97d7c568c63eeb2e7768b8b6f23] For the purposes of sub-paragraphs (2)(a) and (4)(a), where the relevant premium has been awarded as a result of the claimant or the claimant’s partner being entitled to carer support payment, and the death occurred on or after 15th March 2026, the references to “8 weeks” are to be read as “12 weeks”.

#### 9. Persons in receipt of concessionary payments

For the purpose of determining whether a premium is applicable to a person under paragraphs 6, 7 and 8, any concessionary payment made to compensate that person for the non-payment of any benefit mentioned in those paragraphs is to be treated as if it were a payment of that benefit.

#### 10. Persons in receipt of benefit

For the purposes of this Part of this Schedule, a person is to be regarded as being in receipt of any benefit if, and only if, it is paid in respect of the person and is to be so regarded only for any period in respect of which that benefit is paid.
