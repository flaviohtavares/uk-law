EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends certain provisions of United Kingdom immigration and related legislation to the Isle of Man, with modifications as necessary. In particular, this Order extends provision contained in the following statutes—

- (a) the Immigration Act [1971 (c.77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/77);
- (b) the Immigration Act [1988 (c.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/14);
- (c) the Asylum and Immigration Act [1996 (c.49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/49);
- (d) the Immigration and Asylum Act [1999 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/33);
- (e) the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act [2002 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/41);
- (f) the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act [2004 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/19);
- (g) the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act [2006 (c.13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/13).

The Order revokes and replaces the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/2630](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/2630)) and Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/275](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/275)).
