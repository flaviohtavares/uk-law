## SCHEDULE 9 — The Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20

**1.**[^c-40218c5092d8ed76d5ebb2222fe73687] (1) Section 7 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) Omit subsection (2).

**2.**[^c-a286f99bade614fb36d8add5151b083f] (1) Section 11 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) Omit subsection (6).

**3.**[^c-c566891d88637ca4ce28802e4923194e] (1) Section 27 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) Omit subsection (2).

**4.**[^c-4350470df57f534aae000c06b1e380da] (1) Section 28 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) Omit subsection (4).

**5.**[^c-a10eeb44e70e0ff866c0777b12b0022a] (1) Section 50 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In subsection (2), for “Secretary of State” substitute “Council of Ministers”.

(3) Omit subsection (3).

**6.**[^c-1b7ba083728dd6036d4da85878181e7f] (1) Section 51 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In subsections (1), (2) and (3), for “Secretary of State” substitute “Governor”.

(3) In subsection (4), for “Consolidated Fund” substitute “General Revenue of the Isle of Man”.

**7.**[^c-9a939662e509df3b61480ad0dfcb5b7b] (1) Section 52 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “United Kingdom” substitute “Isle of Man”;
- (b) for paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) the Fees and Duties Act 1989 (an Act of Tynwald)[^f00152], and

  .

(3) At the end of (3)(c), (4)(a) and (5)(a) omit “and”.

(4) Omit subsections (2), (3)(d), (4)(b) and (5)(b).

(5) In subsection (6), for “United Kingdom” substitute “Isle of Man”.

**8.**[^c-c1f6a4a6741680c7de1b54feb05cfa31] (1) Section 54 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In subsection (2), for “Terrorism Act [2000 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11)” substitute “Anti-Terrorism and Crime Act 2003 (an Act of Tynwald)[^f00153]”.

**9.**[^c-4d26805478abc7269e137cb530a498ac] (1) Section 64 of the 2006 Act is modified as follows.

(2) Omit subsections (2)(c) and (3).
