# The Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 2008, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (308).** Text as at 2008-03-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/680
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-e2c6d6b2ff8bdeb0ada0a985058f4a98]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 2008.

(2) This Order shall come into force—

- (a) on 13th March 2008, for the purpose of enabling rules, regulations and orders to be made under any enactment which extends to the Isle of Man by virtue of this Order;
- (b) on 1st May 2008, for all other purposes.
