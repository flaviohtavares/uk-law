# The Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 2008, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Extension of the Criminal Justice Act 1982 to the Isle of Man

— Section 64 (person recommended by courts for deportation) of, and Schedule 10 (amendment of Schedule 3 to the 1971 Act) to, the Criminal Justice Act 1982[^f00012] shall extend to the Isle of Man.

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: [1982 c.48](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/48). Section 37 (the standard scale of fines for summary offences) of the Criminal Justice Act [1982 (c.48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/48), as amended by section 17(1) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1991 (c.53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/53), was extended to the Isle of Man with modifications by [S.I. 1992/2670](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2670).
