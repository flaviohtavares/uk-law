# The Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 2008, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1971 Act” means the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001];
- “the 1981 Act” means the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00002];
- “the 1988 Act” means the Immigration Act 1988[^f00003];
- “the 1996 Act” means the Asylum and Immigration Act 1996[^f00004];
- “the 1999 Act” means the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00005];
- “the 2002 Act” means the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00006];
- “the 2004 Act” means the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004[^f00007];
- “the 2006 Act” means the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006[^f00008].
