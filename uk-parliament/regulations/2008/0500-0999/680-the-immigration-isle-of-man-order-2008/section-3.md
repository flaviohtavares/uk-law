## 3. Revocation of existing Orders[^c-d01eb517fb7840e1197716bcff37e8d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1991[^f00009], and
- (b) the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1997[^f00010].
