# The Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 2008, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Revocation of existing Orders

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1991[^f00009], and
- (b) the Immigration (Isle of Man) Order 1997[^f00010].
