# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 3.L.1

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.L.1. Application of Chapter 3.L[^key-5a75d7fe3bf61d57fc23850805d2825d][^key-d8b7394b5516cee263fa1805f85422f4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter makes provision in relation to pensioner members of the 1995 Section who are eligible to be members of this Section of the Scheme in accordance with paragraph (1)(b)(ii) of regulation 3.B.1A (eligibility: transitional).

(2) A member of this Section of the Scheme to whom this Chapter applies is referred to as a “Waiting Period Joiner”.
