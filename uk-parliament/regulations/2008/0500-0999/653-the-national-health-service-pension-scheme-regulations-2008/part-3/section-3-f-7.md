# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 3.F.7

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.F.7. Effect of transfers-out[^c-d8b7394b5516cee263fa1805f85422f4]

*Extent: E+W*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a person’s rights under this Section of[^c-3f831fcfa04c7d1df0e638db1cb13ef2] the Scheme, those rights are extinguished.
