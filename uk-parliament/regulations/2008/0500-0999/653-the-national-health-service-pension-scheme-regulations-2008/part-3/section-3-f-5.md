# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 3.F.5

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.F.5. Ways in which transfer value payments may be applied[^c-d8b7394b5516cee263fa1805f85422f4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A deferred member[^c-f69913a0b0479071b355bdb34a7747d3] may only require the Secretary of State to apply the guaranteed cash equivalent transfer value payment in one or more of the ways permitted under section 95 of the 1993 Act.

(2)[^c-a84a76dbc4ca89301ebe612d87f025e0] In any other case, a member may only require the Secretary of State to apply the guaranteed equivalent transfer value payment in one or more of the ways permitted under section 101AE of the 1993 Act.

(3) The whole of the guaranteed cash equivalent transfer value payment must be applied, unless paragraph (4) applies.

(4) The benefits attributable to—

- (a) the member’s accrued rights to a guaranteed minimum pension, or
- (b) the member’s accrued rights attributable to service in contracted-out employment on or after 6th April 1997,

may be excluded from the guaranteed cash equivalent transfer value payment if section 96(2) of the 1993 Act applies (trustees or managers of certain receiving schemes or arrangements able and willing to accept a transfer payment only in respect of the member’s other rights).

(5) A transfer payment may only be made to—

- (a) a pension scheme that is registered under Chapter 2 of Part 4 of the 2004 Act, or
- (b) an arrangement that is a qualifying recognised overseas pension scheme for the purposes that Part (see section 169(2) of that Act).

(6)[^c-d1792456d6fd23ec828dd77d56745341] Paragraph (1) applies whether or not the deferred member is entitled to a guaranteed cash equivalent transfer value payment under Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of the 1993 Act.
