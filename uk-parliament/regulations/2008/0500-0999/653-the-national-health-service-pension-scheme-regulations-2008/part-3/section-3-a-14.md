# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 3.A.14

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.A.14. Exclusions and deductions from pensionable earnings—all practitioners[^key-d8b7394b5516cee263fa1805f85422f4]

*Extent: E+W*

Any sum that is withheld or otherwise recovered from a practitioner under the National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-25a2d0119dfe70d3ae31225a759f49a5][^f00100] will be excluded or deducted from the practitioner’s pensionable earnings in such manner and to such extent as the Secretary of State may approve. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-25a2d0119dfe70d3ae31225a759f49a5]
