> Text as at 2026-03-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.J.1. Appointment of Scheme administrator[^c-d8b7394b5516cee263fa1805f85422f4]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part and of Part 4 of the 2004 Act, the Scheme administrator is the NHS Business Services Authority (Awdurdod Gwasanaethau Busnes y GIG)[^f00132].
