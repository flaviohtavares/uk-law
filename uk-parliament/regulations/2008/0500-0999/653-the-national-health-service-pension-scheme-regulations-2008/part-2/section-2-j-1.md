# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 2.J.1

> Text as at 2026-03-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2.J.1. Appointment of Scheme administrator

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part and of Part 4 of the 2004 Act, the Scheme administrator is the NHS Business Services Authority (Awdurdod Gwasanaethau Busnes y GIG)[^f00063].

## Annotations

[^f00063]: Footnote: The Authority was established by [S.I. 2005/2414](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2414).
