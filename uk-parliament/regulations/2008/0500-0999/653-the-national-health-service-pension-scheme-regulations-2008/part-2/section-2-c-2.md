# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008, section 2.C.2

> Text as at 2026-03-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2.C.2. Contribution rate for members other than non-GP providers[^c-bc5337cd2c36d1867cbd77071e52fc6d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Contributions under regulation 2.C.1(1)(a) must be paid at the member’s contribution rate for the scheme year in question.

(2)[^c-d437a57cc7328ba6ee6544a23fa2c4d0] A member’s contribution rate for each scheme year from 2015-16[^c-a5d0e16440f6191be7f2c0c3d434d96f] is the percentage specified in column 2 of the following table in respect of the corresponding pensionable pay band specified in column 1 of that table into which the member’s pensionable pay falls.

Table:

Scheme Years from 2015-16[^c-876f9ec6cd406f0a054babfe852aee9d]

| *Column 1*<br>*Pensionable Pay Band* | *Column 2*<br>*Contribution Percentage Rate* |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £15,431 | 5% |
| £15,432 to £21,477 | 5.6% |
| £21,478 to £26,823 | 7.1% |
| £26,824 to £47,845 | 9.3% |
| £47,846 to £70,630 | 12.5% |
| £70,631 to £111,376 | 13.5% |
| £111,377 to any higher amount | 14.5% |

(2A)[^c-2fb8cfc947b26c56f50ad8357d7cf502] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Secretary of State shall, with the consent of the Treasury, determine the pensionable pay bands and contribution percentage rates specified in the tables set out in this regulation[^c-7118ba9834c79dce9ea08f95078dd5f9] in respect of each scheme year.

(4)[^c-17294cdfae7299460a3cad5f541337a1] Before determining those pensionable pay bands or contribution percentage rates, the Secretary of State must consider the advice of the Scheme actuary.
