> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.B.3. Cases with more than 10 years of officer service

*Extent: E+W*

Where a member has more than 10 years of pensionable service before first becoming a [^c-533f14b86f8fb44249a245dbf8c6fc15]... practitioner, the reference amount shall be the better of—

- (a) the amount determined by calculation method A, and
- (b) the base amount.
