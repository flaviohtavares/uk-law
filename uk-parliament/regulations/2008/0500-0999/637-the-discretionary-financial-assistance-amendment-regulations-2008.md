EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This instrument makes various amendments to the Discretionary Financial Assistance Regulations 2001 (“ *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]the Regulations *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]”). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]

Regulation 2 amends the references in regulation 3 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] the Regulations *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] in line with the Housing Benefit Regulations 2006 and the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006. It also adds a paragraph to regulation 3 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] the Regulations *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] precluding a discretionary housing payment being paid to meet a need for financial assistance which arises because of reductions in housing benefit to recover overpayments or reductions in council tax benefit to recover excess benefit. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]

Regulation 3 substitutes regulation 5 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] the Regulations *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9] so that discretionary housing payments may only be made in respect of a period where the person is or was entitled to housing benefit, council tax benefit or both. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6a9a0a7da72debf24b70b81f19eb1e9]
