# The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 621

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

6th March 2008

Laid before Parliament

6th March 2008

Coming into force

30th March 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 97(5) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 30<sup>th</sup> March 2008.

**2.** The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 2 for the words “31<sup>st</sup> March 2008”, substitute “31<sup>st</sup> March 2009”; and
- (b) in the Schedule, after the entries relating to the local justice areas[^f00003] in Cheshire: Halton, Warrington insert—

  > **Local Justice Areas in Cleveland:**
  >
  > - Hartlepool
  > - Langbaurgh East
  > - Teesside

  .

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Hanson

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

6th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/2198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2198)) (“the 2004 Order”) which specifies the local justice areas in which the provisions of Schedule 6 (Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work) to the Courts Act [2003 (c.39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39) are to be piloted and the period of the pilot. This Order extends the period of the pilot schemes provided for in the 2004 Order for a further year ending 31<sup>st</sup> March 2009. It also adds the following local justice areas in Cleveland to the pilot: Hartlepool, Langbaurgh East and Teesside.
