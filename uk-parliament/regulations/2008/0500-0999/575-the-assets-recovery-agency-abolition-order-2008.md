# The Assets Recovery Agency (Abolition) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 575

Proceeds Of Crime

The Assets Recovery Agency (Abolition) Order 2008

Made

4th March 2008

Coming into force

1st April 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 74(1) of the Serious Crime Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Assets Recovery Agency (Abolition) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1st April 2008.

**2.** The Assets Recovery Agency and the corporation sole that is its Director shall cease to exist on 1st April 2008.

Vernon Coaker

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

4th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the Assets Recovery Agency and the corporation sole that is its Director shall cease to exist on 1st April 2008.
