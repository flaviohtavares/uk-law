# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 727

National Health Service, England

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2008

Made

12th March 2008

Coming into force

31st March 2008

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 5 to that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2008.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Bradshaw

Minister of State,

Department of Health

8th March 2008

We consent,

Dave Watts

Alan Campbell

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, provided for in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established by the Secretary of State from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007 to 1<sup>st</sup> October 2007.
