# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 30

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Amount of the grant for fees for a course at a private institution[^c-7af6049125ef518b3b4934433729b929]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a private institution where regulation 29 does not apply is the lesser of—

- (a) £1,175; and
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year.

(2) In the case of a designated course at the University of Buckingham, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £2,975.
