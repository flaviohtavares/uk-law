# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 11

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Current system students[^key-a2e6c238d6fdd38a9675d30c2d98d791]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A current system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 2 of this Part.
