# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Requirement to enter a contract for a loan for fees[^c-5e2f976d695cc7e63f3acb71b9e6fa2c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

To receive a loan for fees payable under this Part, an eligible student must enter into a contract with the Secretary of State.
