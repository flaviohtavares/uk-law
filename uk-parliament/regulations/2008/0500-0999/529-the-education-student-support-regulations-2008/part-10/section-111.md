> This provision is not yet in force.

## 111. Overpayments of fee support[^key-0ad0b740e20f6874f2d4317fba8076b6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any overpayment of fee support is recoverable by the Secretary of State from the academic authority.
