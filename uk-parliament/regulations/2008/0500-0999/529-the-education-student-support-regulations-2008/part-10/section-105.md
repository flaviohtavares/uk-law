# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 105

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. General[^key-c65199bb154e3b2ad2c78cda7f9f54a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) —

- (a) fee loans and fee contribution loans are payable in accordance with regulation 108;
- (b) college fee loans are payable in accordance with regulation 109;
- (c) loans for living costs and long courses loans are payable in accordance with regulation 110;

(2) Regulations 106 and 107 apply in respect of the payment of all loans.
