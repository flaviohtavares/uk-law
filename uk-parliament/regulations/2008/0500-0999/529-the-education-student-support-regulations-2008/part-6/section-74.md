# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 74

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Students residing with parents[^c-72a9def531a4f90573728e255e2b0559]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where an eligible student resides at his parents’ home and the Secretary of State is satisfied that in all the circumstances his parents by reason of age, incapacity or otherwise cannot reasonably be expected to support him and that it would be appropriate for the amount of loan for living costs or long courses loan payable to a student in a category other than category A to apply in his case, the student must be treated as if he were not residing at his parents’ home for the purposes of this Part.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an eligible student who begins the specified designated course on or after 1st September 2004.
