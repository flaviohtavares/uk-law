# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 68

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Type 1 and type 2 teacher training students who are not 2008 cohort students[^key-a4cd5ea0045fcbb818e563ae4309f3f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This regulation applies to a current system student with full entitlement who is a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil, and who is not a 2008 cohort student.

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is —

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,580;
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,475;
- (c) for a student in category C, £5,510;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,625.

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,235;
- (b) for a student in category B, £5,895;
- (c) for a student in category C, £4,790;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,280.
