# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 78

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Deductions from loans for living costs[^key-bed7c388cee2c3420665290819775f30]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a current system student with full entitlement who is not a 2008 cohort student or an old system student with full entitlement may be made in accordance with Part 9.

(2) There is no deduction under Part 9 from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a 2008 cohort student or a student with reduced entitlement.
