# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 65

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. General[^c-165c9c3fffe53edc57904ea13af7a9e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The maximum amount of a loan for living costs in respect of an academic year is calculated as follows—

- (a) where the eligible student is a 2008 cohort student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 66;
- (b) where the eligible student is a current system student (other than a 2008 cohort student) with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 67 or 68;
- (c) where the eligible student is an old system student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 69;
- (d) where the eligible student is a student with reduced entitlement, in accordance with regulation 70.
