# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Qualifying conditions for the grant for travel – courses in medicine and dentistry[^key-ce1039567439e40bf80b78bbcf3291e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A grant is available to an eligible student attending a course in medicine or dentistry (a necessary part of which is a period of study by way of clinical training) in respect of the reasonable expenditure which he is obliged to incur in an academic year for the purpose of attending in connection with his course any hospital or other premises in the United Kingdom (not comprised in the institution) at which facilities for clinical training are provided other than expenditure incurred for the purpose of residential study away from the institution.
