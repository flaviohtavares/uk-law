# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 62

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amount of the higher education grant[^key-c88ab7558214006e860ab1792ec715fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The maximum amount of higher education grant available in respect of an academic year is £1,000.

(2) An old system student who qualifies for a higher education grant receives an amount as follows—

- (a) where the household income is £16,750 or less, he receives £1,000;
- (b) where the household income exceeds £16,750 and does not exceed £22,735, he receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/529) where—

  *M* is £1,000

  *A* is £1 for every complete £6.30 by which the household income exceeds £16,750; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £22,735, no higher education grant is payable.
