# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 41

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. General[^key-7507fac2358f3cd07609d47aca9c5a74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The grant for dependants consists of the following elements—

- (a) adult dependants’ grant;
- (b) childcare grant;
- (c) parents’ learning allowance.

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element and the amounts payable are set out in regulations 42 to 45.
