# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 49

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Amount of the grant for travel – courses in medicine and dentistry[^key-6ce052978e6bb100d8b43b1fb7ad3afc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The amount of grant payable under regulation 48 in respect of an academic year is equal to the reasonable expenditure that the Secretary of State determines the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purposes set out in that regulation less £295.
