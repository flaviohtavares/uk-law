# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Qualifying conditions for the disabled students’ allowance[^c-0d2a966bce566c837c9ac5d76987dcc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

An eligible student qualifies for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Secretary of State is satisfied that the student is obliged to incur in connection with his attendance on a designated course by reason of a disability to which he is subject.
