# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 99

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Order of application – 2008 cohort students[^c-e44b3d220e2ab62c079575ada62f3c29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The contribution or the remainder of the contribution, as the case may be, is to be applied as follows—

- (a) first, to reduce the amount of adult dependants’ grant calculated in accordance with regulation 45;
- (b) second, to reduce the childcare grant calculated in accordance with regulation 45;
- (c) third, to reduce the parents’ learning allowance calculated in accordance with regulation 45 (except the first £50 of the allowance);
- (d) fourth, to reduce the long courses loan calculated in accordance with Chapter 3 of Part 6;
- (e) fifth, to reduce the grant for travel calculated in accordance with Chapter 5 of Part 5.
