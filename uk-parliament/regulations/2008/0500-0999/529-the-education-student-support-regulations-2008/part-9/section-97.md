# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 97

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Application of contribution – current system students who are not 2008 cohort students[^key-ded8c7867c26eac4a5607eabdb67577d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the case of a current system student who is not a 2008 cohort student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 100.
