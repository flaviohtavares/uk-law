# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 82

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. General[^key-5b87b84055cd906bee77ae71bcdc55f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

An eligible student or person treated as an eligible student qualifies for a college fee loan in connection with his attendance on a qualifying course in accordance with this Part.
