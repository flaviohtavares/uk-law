# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2008, section 84

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Persons to be treated as eligible students[^c-21a7b8c27d44f9ce1de4281034704b4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A person is a “person treated as an eligible student” if—

- (a) none of the circumstances in regulation 4(3) applies to him; and
- (b) either—
  - (i) he would have fallen within paragraph 3, 6, 7, 10 or 11 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 if any requirement in those paragraphs to be ordinarily resident in England at a particular time had been a requirement to be ordinarily resident in Scotland at that time or if any requirement to be working or self-employed in England had been a requirement to be working or self-employed in Scotland; or
  - (ii) he is settled in the United Kingdom by virtue of a right of permanent residence and would have fallen within paragraph 8 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 if the requirement in that paragraph to be ordinarily resident in England at a particular time had been a requirement to be ordinarily resident in Scotland.
