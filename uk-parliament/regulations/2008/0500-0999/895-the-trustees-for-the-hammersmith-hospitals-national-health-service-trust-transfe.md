# The Trustees for the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 895

National Health Service, England

The Trustees for the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2008

Made

26th March 2008

Laid before Parliament

2nd April 2008

Coming into force

1st May 2008

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals, establishments and facilities of the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Trustees for the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2008.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trustees” means the trustees for the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003];
- “the new Trustees” means the trustees for the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00004];
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trustees and the new Trustees and signed on behalf of the old Trustees on 1<sup>st</sup> February 2008 and on behalf of the new Trustees on 5<sup>th</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> February 2008.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trustees to the new Trustees.

(2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference in relation to the trust property to the trustees of the trust property or to the old Trustees shall be construed as a reference to the new Trustees.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Bradshaw

Minister of State,

Department of Health

26th March 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the trustees for the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
