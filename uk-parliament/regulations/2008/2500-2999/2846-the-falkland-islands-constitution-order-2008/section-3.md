# The Falkland Islands Constitution Order 2008, section 3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Revocations[^c-3577e04dc8a51a2cb3e93c1b79f5a33a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Falkland Islands Constitution Order 1985[^f00003], the Falkland Islands Constitution (Amendment) Order 1997[^f00004], and the Falkland Islands Constitution (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1997[^f00005] are revoked with effect from the appointed day.
