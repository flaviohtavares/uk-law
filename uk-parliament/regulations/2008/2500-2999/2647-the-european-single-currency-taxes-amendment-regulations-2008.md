# The European Single Currency (Taxes) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2647

Corporation Tax

The European Single Currency (Taxes) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

7th October 2008

Laid before the House of Commons

7th October 2008

Coming into force

28th October 2008

The Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 163 of the Finance Act 1998[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the European Single Currency (Taxes) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 28th October 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the European Single Currency (Taxes) Regulations 1998

In regulation 6(1)(b) of the European Single Currency (Taxes) Regulations 1998[^f00002] substitute “under the I minus E basis” for “otherwise than under Case I of Schedule D”.

Steve McCabe

Dave Watts

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th October 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 6(1)(b) of the European Single Currency (Taxes) Regulations 1998 to make the terminology consistent with that used in primary legislation relating to the taxation of life assurance companies.

A full and final Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
