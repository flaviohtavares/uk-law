# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 48

> Text as at 2026-11-06.

## 48. Power to treat an application as a different type of application[^c-9d5694abc9620869e839835f0ddd3ab0]

The Upper[^c-6dba9b0392ca9701b226fc0de46212f8] Tribunal may treat an application for a decision to be corrected, set aside or reviewed, or for permission to appeal against a decision, as an application for any other one of those things.
