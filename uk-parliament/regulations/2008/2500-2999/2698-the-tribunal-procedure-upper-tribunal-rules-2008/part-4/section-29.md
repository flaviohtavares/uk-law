# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 29. Acknowledgment of service

(1) A person who is sent or provided with[^key-ab6a5a338be7439241c860b9af042794] a copy of an application for permission under rule 28(8) (application for permission to bring judicial review proceedings) or rule 28A(2)(a) (special provisions for[^key-42e8222ed06815e801fe81c36f42e115] immigration judicial review[^key-5626da20718f1c0e06c992ddd20acb01] proceedings) and wishes to take part in the proceedings must provide[^key-fc024d78d7361264b9bb6e356355cb1c] to the Upper Tribunal an acknowledgment of service so that it is received no later than 21 days after the date on which the Upper Tribunal sent, or in[^key-eae90af9e8bd780db3673ab1a44abd98] immigration judicial review proceedings the applicant provided, a copy of the application to that person.

(2) An acknowledgment of service under paragraph (1) must be in writing and state—

- (a) whether the person intends to support or[^key-726f21656fbe02df0bc899128a2c4ccf] oppose the application for permission;
- (b) their grounds for any support or[^key-ba45b97b363ec145f4e7432e13a16d98] opposition under sub-paragraph (a), or any other submission or information which they consider may assist the Upper Tribunal; and
- (c) the name and address of any other person not named in the application as a respondent or interested party whom the person providing the acknowledgment considers to be an interested party.

(2A)[^key-9c3d81c62271c9cacc84de383bb96342] In immigration judicial review[^key-0d7274d0c7ac01795e5699cd5255f0f8] proceedings, a person who provides an acknowledgement of service under paragraph (1) must also provide a copy to—

- (a) the applicant; and
- (b) any other person named in the application under rule 28(4)(a) or acknowledgement of service under paragraph (2)(c)

no later than the time specified in paragraph (1).

(3) A person who is provided with[^key-77f6b599774f5fd561cd99041f2a10e0] a copy of an application for permission under rule 28(8) or 28A(2)(a)[^key-c09758544af5b0dc4f631eac76298a23] but does not provide an acknowledgment of service to the Upper Tribunal[^key-41e265e3b618fbb754fa7c55297fd7fd] may not take part in the application for permission unless allowed to do so by the Upper Tribunal[^key-891595a365e901fd5f073333da53b5ea], but may take part in the subsequent proceedings if the application is successful.
