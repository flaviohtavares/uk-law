# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 28A

> Text as at 2026-11-06.

## 28A. Special provisions for immigration judicial review proceedings[^c-9eee4b5bc2ae219273156464c7cd69b5][^c-cbcf550666e88cbb49c187017471fda2]

(1) The Upper Tribunal must not accept an application for permission to bring immigration judicial review[^c-e6a42eecd26e372e8dceca4b9e0c31b4] proceedings unless it is either accompanied by any required fee or the Upper Tribunal accepts an undertaking that the fee will be paid.

(1A)[^c-7c6112a997d0f815c451b7131bde38c7] Where the Upper Tribunal accepts an application under paragraph (1), it must seal the application and send it to the applicant.

(2) Within 9 days after the date on which the Upper Tribunal sent the application to the applicant under paragraph (1A), the applicant must provide—[^c-db12116a9d69511ee6c69ad19c89bc93]

- (a) a copy of the sealed[^c-4d5c3b6c97c8dbea5af6fcd7cbc88682] application and any accompanying documents to each person named in the application as a respondent or an interested party; and
- (b) the Upper Tribunal with a written statement of when and how this was done.
