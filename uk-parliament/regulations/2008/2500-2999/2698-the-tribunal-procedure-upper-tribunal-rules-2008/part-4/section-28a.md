# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 28A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 28A. Special provisions for immigration judicial review proceedings[^key-9eee4b5bc2ae219273156464c7cd69b5][^key-cbcf550666e88cbb49c187017471fda2]

(1) The Upper Tribunal must not accept an application for permission to bring immigration judicial review[^key-e6a42eecd26e372e8dceca4b9e0c31b4] proceedings unless it is either accompanied by any required fee or the Upper Tribunal accepts an undertaking that the fee will be paid.

(2) Within 9 days of making an application referred to in paragraph (1), an applicant must provide—

- (a) a copy of the application and any accompanying documents to each person named in the application as a respondent or an interested party; and
- (b) the Upper Tribunal with a written statement of when and how this was done.
