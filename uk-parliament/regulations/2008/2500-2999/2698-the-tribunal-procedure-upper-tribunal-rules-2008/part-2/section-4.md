# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 4

> Text as at 2026-11-06.

## 4. Delegation to staff

(1) Staff appointed under section 40(1) of the 2007 Act (tribunal staff and services) or section 2(1) of the Courts Act 2003 (court officers, staff and services)[^c-90c5177334819cdbf536210f20c521fe] may, if authorised by[^c-bf473ff7d962a5644607f8fab11ef901] of the Senior President of Tribunals under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 5 to the 2007 Act[^c-cb320f6055e0e3e17e9153c810c1d022], carry out functions of a judicial nature permitted or required to be done by the Upper Tribunal.

(2)[^c-e7b500c45ceabd4b0c2bb638d66b17ae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Within 14 days after the date on which the Upper Tribunal sends notice of a decision made by a member of staff under paragraph (1) to a party, that party may apply in writing to the Upper Tribunal for that decision to be considered afresh by a judge.
