# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 26A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 26A. Cases transferred or referred to the Upper Tribunal, applications made directly to the Upper Tribunal , cases where an offence has been certified and proceedings without notice to a respondent[^key-1a52c1eb56ffb3b398b77226795af52a][^key-cb64106567fb2743e0f34a1c0d3d136b]

(1)[^key-cafa8cb0d975d0a3cef6506a271c78c3] Paragraphs (2) and (3) apply to—

- (a) a case transferred or referred to the Upper Tribunal from the First-tier Tribunal; [^key-83219f28f5c1e751d3e1508acccd7090]...
- (b) a case, other than an appeal or a case to which rule 26 (references under the Forfeiture Act 1982) applies, which is started by an application made directly to the Upper Tribunal ; or[^key-cc2b89975ce99172dcdfcab80bed31d0]
- (c)[^key-1ee0a79e381c0dd916570652c13b1170] a case where an offence has been certified to the Upper Tribunal.

(2) In a case to which this paragraph applies—

- (a) the Upper Tribunal must give directions as to the procedure to be followed in the consideration and disposal of the proceedings; [^key-c23617c1d5f66ae3ea1b0ef0846e700f]...
- (aa)[^key-35368997ebb0d463c5e71ab14b61907e] in a reference under section 325 or 326 of the Charities Act 2011[^key-3e4b9430c5a01ff9377722642deb3efc], the Upper Tribunal may give directions providing for an application to join the proceedings as a party and the time within which it may be made; and
- (b) the preceding rules in this Part will only apply to the proceedings to the extent provided for by such directions.

(3) If a case or matter to which this paragraph applies is to be determined without notice to or the involvement of a respondent—

- (a) any provision in these Rules requiring a document to be provided by or to a respondent; and
- (b) any other provision in these Rules permitting a respondent to participate in the proceedings

does not apply to that case or matter.

(4)[^key-7fbe202eb1d711c0c0d936e5bea4805b] Schedule 2 makes further provision for national security certificate appeals transferred to the Upper Tribunal.
