# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 36. Notice of hearings

(1) The Upper Tribunal must give each party entitled to attend a hearing reasonable notice of the time and place of the hearing (including any adjourned or postponed hearing) and any change to the time and place of the hearing.

(2) The period of notice under paragraph (1) must be at least 14 days except that—

- (a) in applications for permission to bring judicial review proceedings, the period of notice must be at least 2 working days; [^key-fd500f7d7b312b7264273542101c616a]... and[^key-0a2149518bb905f463b8e8096b19b4f9]
- (aa)[^key-a4f1e6eaa7c1bc6912f6b696bb49f10a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^key-a8a6b71abdd590c2e4b28076524f9cac]... the Upper Tribunal may give shorter notice—
  - (i) with the parties' consent; or
  - (ii) in urgent or exceptional cases.
