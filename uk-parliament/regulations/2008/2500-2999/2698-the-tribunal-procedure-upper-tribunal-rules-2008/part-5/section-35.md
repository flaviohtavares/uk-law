# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 35. Entitlement to attend a hearing

(1)[^key-080267966d00b9ad6c1a7a52312d2a06] Subject to rule 37(4) (exclusion of a person from a hearing), each party is entitled to attend a hearing.

(2)[^key-a8b651f6a96b089959d378c998b9a188] In a national security certificate appeal the relevant Minister is entitled to attend any hearing.
