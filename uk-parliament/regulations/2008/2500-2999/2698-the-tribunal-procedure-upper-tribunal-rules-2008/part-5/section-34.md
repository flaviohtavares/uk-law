# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 34. Decision with or without a hearing

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3)[^c-ac81b4c28f09ba362a4d1595ed525601], the Upper Tribunal may make any decision without a hearing.

(2) The Upper Tribunal must have regard to any view expressed by a party when deciding whether to hold a hearing to consider any matter, and the form of any such hearing.

(3)[^c-88e684d367a1ace63f38ad34c82c4590] In immigration judicial review proceedings, the Upper Tribunal must hold a hearing before making a decision which disposes of proceedings.

(4) Paragraph (3) does not affect the power of the Upper Tribunal to—

- (a) strike out a party’s case, pursuant to rule 8(1)(b) or 8(2);
- (b) consent to withdrawal, pursuant to rule 17;
- (c) determine an application for permission to bring judicial review proceedings, pursuant to rule 30; or
- (d) make a consent order disposing of proceedings, pursuant to rule 39,

without a hearing.
