# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 37A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 37A. Coronavirus temporary rule (recording of remote hearings)[^key-0c80473aacf2a20468cab63135fee621]

(1) In the circumstances set out in paragraph (3), the Upper Tribunal must direct that the hearing be recorded, if practicable.

(2) Where the Upper Tribunal has made a direction under paragraph (1), it may direct the manner in which the hearing must be recorded.

(3) The circumstances referred to in paragraph (1) are that the hearing, or part of it, is—

- (a) held in private under rule 37(2ZA); or
- (b) only treated as held in public by virtue of a media representative being able to access the proceedings remotely while they are taking place.

(4)[^key-eb8d18c007d2fc0e7b463de9104b7bc0]  On the application of any person, any recording made pursuant to a direction under paragraph (1) is to be accessed with the consent of the Upper Tribunal in such manner as the Upper Tribunal may direct.
