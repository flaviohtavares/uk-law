# The Tribunal Procedure (Upper Tribunal) Rules 2008, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2698

## 39. Consent orders

(1) The Upper Tribunal may, at the request of the parties but only if it considers it appropriate, make a consent order disposing of the proceedings and making such other appropriate provision as the parties have agreed.

(2) Notwithstanding any other provision of these Rules, the Upper[^c-e8b871e0e88d7fa9e958ac216798cf7f] Tribunal need not hold a hearing before making an order under paragraph (1)[^c-f7341fb703d231742db2c1e9e6f3a096]....
