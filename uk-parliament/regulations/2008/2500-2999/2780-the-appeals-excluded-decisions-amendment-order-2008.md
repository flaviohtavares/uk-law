# The Appeals (Excluded Decisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This statutory instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 2008/2707](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/2707) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that statutory instrument.

2008 No. 2780

Tribunals And Inquiries

The Appeals (Excluded Decisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

21st October 2008

Laid before Parliament

24th October 2008

Coming into force

3rd November 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11(5)(f) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Appeals (Excluded Decisions) (Amendment) Order 2008 and comes into force on 3rd November 2008.

## 2. Amendment to the Appeals (Excluded Decisions) Order 2008

In article 2(a) of the Appeals (Excluded Decisions) Order 2008[^f00002] for “section 102” substitute “sections 103 and 103A”.

By authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

21st October 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Appeals (Excluded Decisions) Order 2008. It inserts the correct references to the sections of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00003] which provide for appeal rights under that Act.
