# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions Order 2008, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2013-08-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2833

## 7. Appeal to Upper Tribunal from tribunals in Scotland[^c-4958611953daf5b80ec27d2f2645a753]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An appeal against a decision of the Pensions Appeal Tribunal in Scotland under section 5 of the Pensions Appeal Tribunals Act 1943[^f00006] (assessment decision) lies to the Upper Tribunal.
