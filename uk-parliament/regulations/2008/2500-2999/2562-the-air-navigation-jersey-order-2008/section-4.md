# The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2008, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application of the Air Navigation Order 2005

(1) The provisions of the Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00009] listed in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order apply, with the modifications specified in Part 2 of that Schedule, to Jersey.

(2) Any amendments made to the Air Navigation Order 2005 after the coming into force of this Order do not apply to Jersey.
