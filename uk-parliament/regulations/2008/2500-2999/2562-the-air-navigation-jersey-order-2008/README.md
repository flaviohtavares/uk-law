EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces the Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2000 ([S.I.2000/1346](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1346)), the Air Navigation (Amendment) (Jersey) Order 2000 ([S.I.2000/3246](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3246)) and the Air Navigation (Amendment No. 2) (Jersey) Order 2002 ([S.I.2002/1078](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1078)). It applies in Jersey, with the modifications specified in the Schedule to the Order, most of the provisions of the Air Navigation Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1970)), as amended up to the date of the making of this Order.
