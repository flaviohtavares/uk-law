# The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2008, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2008 and comes into force on the day on which the Civil Aviation (Jersey) Law 2008[^f00005] comes into force in its entirety.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Adopted by the States of Jersey on 13 May 2008 and being put forward to be sanctioned by Order of Her Majesty in Council at the same time as this Order.
