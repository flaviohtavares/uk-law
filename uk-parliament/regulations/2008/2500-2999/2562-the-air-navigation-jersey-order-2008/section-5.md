# The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2008, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Application of Interpretation (Jersey) Law 1954

The Interpretation (Jersey) Law 1954[^f00010] applies to this Order as if it were an enactment under Article 1(1) of that Law.
