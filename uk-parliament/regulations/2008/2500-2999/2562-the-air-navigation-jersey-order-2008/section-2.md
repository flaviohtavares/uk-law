# The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2008, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocation

The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2000[^f00006], the Air Navigation (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 2000[^f00007] and the Air Navigation (Jersey) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2002[^f00008] are revoked but the regulations and Orders made under those Orders remain in force as if they had been made under this Order.
