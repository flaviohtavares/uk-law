# The REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008, section 17

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Appearance of authorised persons before a court of summary jurisdiction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an authorised person is authorised in that behalf by—

- (a) the Health and Safety Executive;[^c-9f56fc40de68175221df3ce2cc2e0a9b]...
- (b) the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland; or[^c-4d495722ea51c9b9ace87a542171145d]
- (c) the Office for Nuclear Regulation.

that person may, although not of counsel or a solicitor, prosecute before a court of summary jurisdiction proceedings for an offence committed under regulation 11 or 13.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply in Scotland.
