> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Health and safety enforcement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f418f3d2bfd560c3a08a39cb0f9ab87a] This regulation applies where the Executive is jointly under an enforcement duty with any of the following enforcing authorities, whether or not any other enforcing authority is also under an enforcement duty in respect of the same listed REACH provision—

- (a) a local (health and safety) authority;
- (b) the Office of Rail Regulation *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-837e7e185dc7f8ed988110ba15acd492] the Office of Rail and Road *(Extent: E+W+S)* .

(2) Schedule 3 (health and safety enforcement) has effect.

(3) In circumstances not provided for under Schedule 3, the Executive must perform the joint duty on behalf of a local (health and safety) authority.

(4) The arrangements under Schedule 3 and the arrangement under paragraph (3) may be varied by an assignment made in accordance with regulation 3A or[^key-e204cd078c8b770b115b85284461ae99] an enforcement agreement made in accordance with regulation 5.
