# The REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Authorisations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10(2)

### SECTION 1 — The Environment Agency , the Natural Resources Body for Wales and the Scottish Environment Protection Agency[^M_F_801945b7-c74c-46a6-d156-cfb3925bbcfa]

**1.** In respect of the Environment Agency , the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-ed4fe5488a2149f97e12f3a231d7bf18] or the Scottish Environment Protection Agency, an authorisation made under section 108 of the Environment Act 1995[^f00071](powers of enforcing authorities and persons authorised by them).

### SECTION 2 — The Department of the Environment

**2.** In respect of the Department of the Environment, an authorisation made under—

- (a) article 72 of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00072](powers of enforcing authorities and persons authorised by them);
- (b) article 25 of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00073](powers of entry and inspection); or
- (c) regulation 8 of the Pollution, Prevention and Control Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00074](appointment of chief inspector and other inspectors).

### SECTION 3 — The Health and Safety Executive , the Office of Rail Regulation (affected by repeal, see note) the Office of Rail and Road , the Office for Nuclear Regulation and local (health and safety) authorities in Great Britain[^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec58961][^c-2938c110e9ee971c1930f1a372ace7ad][^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec5896][^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec58961][^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec58962][^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec58962][^c-1603055f84eb564232c380fe531eee68]

**3.** In respect of—

- (a) the Health and Safety Executive;[^c-6751b59f7fdf30857395eb65e14b4bb7]...
- (b) a local (health and safety) authority in Great Britain, [^c-f33fd5a3dbdbc1b8e7b5745459c6bcb6]...
- (c)[^c-4d454cda606982b786558b74852d47e3] the Office of Rail Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0fd9825713aa0e3bfe9ef3f458ec5896] the Office of Rail and Road ,or[^c-03f0eec672bc79bf40f1dbc994bff1a1]
- (d) the Office for Nuclear Regulation.

an authorisation made under section 19 of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974[^f00075](appointment of inspectors).

### SECTION 4 — The Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland and local (health and safety) authorities in Northern Ireland

**4.** In respect of—

- (a) the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland; or
- (b) a local (health and safety) authority in Northern Ireland,

an authorisation made under article 21 of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00076](appointment of inspectors).

### SECTION 5 — Local (consumer safety) authorities

**5.** In respect of—

- (a) a local (consumer safety) authority in Great Britain, an authorisation made under section 72 of the Weights and Measures Act 1985[^f00077](appointment of inspectors);
- (b) a local (consumer safety) authority in Northern Ireland, an authorisation made for the purposes of section 27(2) of the Consumer Protection Act 1987[^f00078](enforcement).

### SECTION 6 — The Secretary of State

**6.** In respect of the Secretary of State, an authorisation made under regulation 16 of the Offshore Chemicals Regulations 2002[^f00079](appointment of inspectors).
