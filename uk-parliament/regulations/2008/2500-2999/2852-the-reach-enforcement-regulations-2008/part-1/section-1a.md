# The REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008, section 1A

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Application[^key-1f5f02bc7b35f0eababde825a4fc81c6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations apply to—

- (a) England and Wales and Scotland; and
- (b) offshore installations in relevant waters, other than such waters within the seaward limits of the territorial sea adjacent to Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Application[^key-bc3f41773a11ba5f7308a0b9bddaf2d6]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations apply to Northern Ireland, including offshore installations in relevant waters within the seaward limits of the territorial sea adjacent to Northern Ireland.
