# The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2597 (C.114 )

JUDICIAL APPOINTMENTS AND DISCIPLINE

The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008

Made

2nd October 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 148 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement of section 8 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005

Section 8 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00002] shall come into force when this Order is made.

Jack Straw

Lord Chancellor

2nd October 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 8 of the Constitutional Reform Act [2005 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4) which provides for the appointment of a Head and Deputy Head of Criminal Justice in England and Wales.
