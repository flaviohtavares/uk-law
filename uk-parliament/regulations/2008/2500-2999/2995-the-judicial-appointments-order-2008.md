# The Judicial Appointments Order 2008

> Text as at 2023-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2008 No. 2995

Tribunals And Inquiries

Judicial Appointments And Discipline

The Judicial Appointments Order 2008

Made

17th November 2008

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001] and section 7(6A) and (6B) of the Social Security Act 1998[^f00002].

The Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales and the Judicial Appointments Commission in accordance with section 51(9) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, and in accordance with that subsection as it applies by virtue of section 7(6B) of the Social Security Act 1998.

A draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 51(14) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 and in accordance with section 80(4) of the Social Security Act 1998.

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-ea0db5fd127756b84021792ce2fc8d72]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Judicial Appointments Order 2008.

(2) This Order shall come into force—

- (a) for the purposes of article 7(b), on 30th November 2010; and
- (b) for all other purposes five days after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-b171db920356fcd5e4c7d379ffd869de]

In this Order—

- “the 1998 Act” means the Social Security Act 1998;
- “the 2007 Act” means the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007;
- “registered patent attorney” has the meaning assigned to it by section 275 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988;[^c-101b33d2bcdd75d30363eafdf46a93ac]
- “registered trade mark attorney” has the meaning assigned to it by section 83 of the Trade Marks Act 1994.[^c-6000fb4fa940e0483df321c26e2d3f72]

## 3. Specified qualifications under the 1998 Act[^c-6869f8402ce6190cb525ba85f7397bfc]

For the purposes of section 7(2)(a) of the 1998 Act—

- (a) the specified qualification is that the person is a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives; and
- (b) a person shall be taken first to become a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives when the person is admitted or enrolled as a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives.

## 4. Specified and relevant qualifications under the 2007 Act[^c-2b3e91a9b6fc86613dbbda5b961929f0]

Articles 5 and 6 are subject to articles 7 and 8.

## 5.[^M_I_7a8c5a9e-4b10-4db6-e51b-80d86bfd2b3c]

For the purposes of section 50(2) and (3) of the 2007 Act, a person holds a relevant qualification if the person is—

- (a) a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives;
- (b) a registered patent attorney[^c-de1096d5bd1649f035ea8ef26af79045]; or
- (c) a registered trade mark attorney[^c-057daec169e581a413126d470f57a579].

## 6.[^c-3bda5511ef03e022e09488b0b150c47a]

For the purposes of section 50 of the 2007 Act, a person shall be taken first to become—

- (a) a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives when the person is admitted or enrolled as a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives;
- (b) a registered patent attorney[^c-38cfb972db6c649af5a0e6da2890fa85] when the person’s name is entered on the register of patent attorneys[^c-380785af062a7f8a90bcfeb131d4e10c] in accordance with section 275 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^c-30dcd20e96be3befca91123892463d53]; or
- (c) a registered trade mark attorney[^c-9a3663933d53e3e8b4b96e43dc78535b] when the person’s name is entered on the register of trade mark attorneys[^c-807429c2392b5bfe59b94a79d3aa5f17] in accordance with section 83 of the Trade Marks Act 1994[^c-8821cffb3247005011208d3579eeb15b].

## 7. Specified offices under the 2007 Act[^c-8917d2d1e8388f22d2b0c3af467070f3]

A person who is a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives holds a relevant qualification for the purposes of section 50(2) and (3) of the 2007 Act in relation to the offices specified in—

- (a)[^c-0f009cd517876e07f62f20a8a09763d7] Part 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order; and
- (b)[^c-8917d2d1e8388f22d2b0c3af467070f3] Part 2 of Schedule 1 to this Order.

## 8.[^M_I_68546727-9fd3-495b-a51a-0895c6363765]

A person who is a registered patent attorney[^c-e59288ee1f2e9648aef6be383ef4fa47] or a registered trade mark attorney holds a relevant qualification for the purposes of section 50(2) and (3) of the 2007 Act in relation to the offices specified in Schedule 2 to this Order.

Jack Straw

Lord Chancellor

17th November 2008

## SCHEDULE 1 — Offices for which a Fellow of the Institute of Legal Executives holds a relevant qualification

Article 7

### PART 1[^c-95f41bcacc874bec4d2b4681aeda4676]

- Deputy district judge appointed under section 8(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00005],
- Deputy District Judge (Magistrates’ Courts) appointed under section 24(1) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00006],
- Adjudicator appointed under regulation 3(1) of the Road User Charging (Enforcement and Adjudication) (London) Regulations 2001[^f00007],
- [^c-6de200d981adb5a854d7f70a794c29f7]...
- Member of a panel of Employment Judges[^c-2e7688ed5d2757594412e51bd3ff0e5a] appointed under regulation 8(2)(a) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013[^c-f26766c5cdfb84b68fa27ab8c27c1c45],
- Judge of the First-tier Tribunal appointed under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007,
- Adjudicators appointed under regulation 16 of the Civil Enforcement of Road Traffic Contraventions (Approved Devices, Charging Guidelines and General Provisions) (England) Regulations 2022[^c-4e9ddb7deca1a638945b6aeedd41d9d5],
- Adjudicators appointed under regulation 16 of the Civil Enforcement of Road Traffic Contraventions (General Provisions) (Wales) Regulations 2013[^c-603ee03e528a43039eb38594b509131b].

### PART 2[^c-598b2877a2775c537f5cb0debc0eeaf5]

- District Judge appointed under section 6(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00011],
- District Judge (Magistrates’ Courts) appointed under section 22(1) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00012],
- Senior coroners, area coroners and assistant coroners appointed under section 23 of, and Part 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Coroners and Justice Act 2009,[^c-350a6eb23d04c318d4bff9eab8b47c05]
- Judge of the Upper Tribunal appointed under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 3 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007,[^c-7c8a80e5bb7c62601b30160d05330d57]
- Deputy Judge of the Upper Tribunal appointed under paragraph 7(1) of Schedule 3 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007,
- Recorder appointed under section 21(2) of the Courts Act 1971.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Offices for which a registered patent agent or a registered trade mark agent holds a relevant qualification[^c-be36eed9a1384cb1c1c3152fee14d6e7]

Article 8

[^c-be36eed9a1384cb1c1c3152fee14d6e7]

- Appointed person within the meaning of section 27A(1)(a) of the Registered Designs Act 1949[^c-ced19395657125c20d0b4c7dbe007155]
- Chairman or Deputy Chairman of the Copyright Tribunal appointed under section 145(2) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00013],
- Appointed person within the meaning of section 77(1) of the Trade Marks Act 1994[^f00014].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that Fellows of ILEX (Institute of Legal Executives) are eligible to be members of an appeal tribunal for the purposes of section 7 of the Social Security Act 1998.

This Order provides that Fellows of ILEX, registered patent agents and registered trade mark agents each hold a relevant qualification in relation to the judicial-appointment eligibility condition for the purposes of section 50 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act [2007 (c15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15) and in relation to the offices specified in Schedule 1 (Fellows of ILEX) and Schedule 2 (registered patent agents and registered trade mark agents).
