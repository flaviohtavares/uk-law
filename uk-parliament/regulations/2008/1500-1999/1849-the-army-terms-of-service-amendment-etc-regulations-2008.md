# The Army Terms of Service (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1849

Defence

The Army Terms of Service (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2008

Made

10th July 2008

Laid before Parliament

15th July 2008

Coming into force

6th August 2008

The Defence Council make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 2 of the Armed Forces Act 1966[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Army Terms of Service (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 6th August 2008.

## 2. Amendment to the Army Terms of Service Regulations 2007

(1) The Army Terms of Service Regulations 2007[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 11(2) (right to transfer to the reserve) after the words “date of enlistment” insert the words “or the person’s 18<sup>th</sup> birthday, whichever is the later”.

## 3. Revocation

The Army Terms of Service (Part-time Service in Northern Ireland) Regulations 1992[^f00003] are revoked.

## 4. Savings

The Army Terms of Service Regulations 2007 shall continue to apply without the amendment made by regulation 2 in relation to any person who enlisted in the regular army before 6th August 2008.

On behalf of the Defence Council

Derek Twigg

Richard Dannatt

Members of the Defence Council

10th July 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Army Terms of Service Regulations 2007 (“the 2007 Regulations”).

Regulation 2 provides for regulation 11(2) of the 2007 Regulations to be amended so that the calculation of the notice period, which a person who has enlisted in the regular army has to complete before exercising the right to be transferred to the reserve, begins with the date of the person’s enlistment or their 18<sup>th</sup> birthday, whichever is the later.

Regulation 3 revokes the Army Terms of Service (Part-time Service in Northern Ireland) Regulations 1992 consequent on the disbandment of the Northern Ireland resident battalions of the Royal Irish Regiment (to which they relate).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been carried out for this instrument as it has no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies.
