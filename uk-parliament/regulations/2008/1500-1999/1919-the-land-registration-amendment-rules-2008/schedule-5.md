# The Land Registration (Amendment) Rules 2008, SCHEDULE 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 5 — AMENDMENTS TO SCHEDULE 6 TO THE PRINCIPAL RULES

Rule 4(8)

### 1. Amendments to Part 3

In Part 3 of Schedule 6 to the principal rules—

- (a) in paragraph G, after “pending application” insert “or proposal by the registrar to alter the register”, and
- (b) in paragraph H, after “entry” insert “on the day list”.

### 2. Amendment to Part 4

In paragraph I of Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the principal rules, after “entry” insert “on the day list”.

### 3. Amendments to Part 5

In Part 5 of Schedule 6 to the principal rules—

- (a) in paragraph E, after “date” where it first appears insert “and time”, and
- (b) in paragraph F, after “Whether” insert “at the date and time of the official search certificate”.
