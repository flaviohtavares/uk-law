# The Land Registration (Amendment) Rules 2008, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Provision consequential upon revocation of rule 8(1)(i)

The registrar need not retain in the register any entry made under rule 8(1)(i) (name of the first proprietor of a registered estate with possessory title) or an equivalent entry made under the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00007].

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: The Land Registration Act [1925 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1925/21) was repealed by section 135 of, and Schedule 13 to, the Land Registration Act 2002 on 13 October 2003.
