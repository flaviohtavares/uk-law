## 68. Certificate of employment at a parliamentary election

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The form of certificate in Form F is prescribed for the purposes of rule 32(3) of the elections rules.

(2) The prescribed officer of police for those purposes is a member of the Police Service of Northern Ireland of or above the rank of chief inspector.
