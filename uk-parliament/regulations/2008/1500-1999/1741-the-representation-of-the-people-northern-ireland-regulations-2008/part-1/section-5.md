# The Representation of the People (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008, section 5

## 5. Communication of applications, notices etc.[^c-4153a1fac2da99f273bdcc8f6d159c47][^c-63e3f4d33999a03d69a4ea53ae8ad8a4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-a484ee14d4206d6f1b36243f053dafbf] The requirement in these Regulations that any application, attestation,[^c-482353d9f643bad3e80b8f62c06b2c82] notice, reminder,[^c-300b4bf34820636ef49cdd03cea5a8df] representation, objection or any other communication that is sent[^c-cc49b6711b3f88ddf21f618518896e46] should be in writing is satisfied where (apart from the usual meaning of that expression) the text of it—

- (a) is transmitted by electronic means;
- (b) is received in legible form; and
- (c) is capable of being used for subsequent reference.

(2)[^c-301fbc921270e95a2138fffb23cc7880] The reference to “text” in paragraph (1) includes a signature.
