## 23. Notification about registration as overseas elector

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the registration officer is satisfied that the declarant qualifies as an overseas elector under section 1A[^c-c70f5d1a020bb9982acb037bcfc1fe2f] of the 1985 Act, he shall so notify the declarant in writing[^c-33dc6bcb5ce6ccfa11626bb2599fa769].

(2) Where the registration officer rejects an application for registration in pursuance of an overseas elector’s declaration because—

- (a) in his opinion the declarant does not qualify as an overseas elector under section 1A[^c-0b6f6dabde90b023c901f81d6bc9c5c0] of the 1985 Act, or
- (b) the declaration does not satisfy the requirements of section 1C[^c-120d2d677389bd9a4c50a3050affd5d7] of the 1985 Act or regulation 20 [^c-0eaf98a7b0041f45cff8c205f6f37dd0]..., [^c-0b6f6dabde90b023c901f81d6bc9c5c0]...
- (c)[^c-0e30885c1636e0f78a0e169086d60c1b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the registration officer must notify the declarant in writing and set out their reasons for rejecting the application.[^c-dfd1861cc3d6655765515f95c00cc46a]

(3)[^c-e509ebb789d221eb2b5e886343379b8e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
