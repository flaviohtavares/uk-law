## 96. Supply of free copy of full register to the British Library

*Extent: N.I.*

The registration officer shall supply, free of charge on publication, one printed copy of—

- (a) any revised version of the register published under section 13(1) or (3) of the 1983 Act; and
- (b) any list of overseas electors,

to the British Library.
