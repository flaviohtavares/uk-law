# The Representation of the People (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1741
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Persons entitled to be present at proceedings on issue and receipt of postal ballot papers[^key-017b8621bd9cf83f2ecd014a22dd8dc1][^key-14a9e385dbf7d46a929f6c45cebc1d3a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of section 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D or 6E of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00064] no person may be present at the proceedings on the issue or receipt of postal ballot papers at a parliamentary election other than—

- (a) the returning officer and the returning officer’s clerks[^key-3b5faf2f688437308183b6f416a1a973];
- (b) a candidate;
- (c) an election agent or any person appointed by a candidate to attend in his election agent’s place; and
- (d) any agents appointed under regulation 73.

(2)[^key-344e8cc1d9f5a6c6c85942f034c79ddd] Sub-paragraphs (b), (c) and (d) of paragraph (1) do not apply to proceedings on issue or receipt of tendered postal ballot papers under rule 40ZA of the elections rules.
