## 84. Receipt of covering envelope by post[^c-cd8a1e4e39c1d9993651207515d94fd9][^c-81853bfc85b5112474a77427393420a2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-5b8290ae5065475fa89a24ab5e31d5b7] The returning officer shall, immediately on receipt by post[^c-04700b68668e158ed5a5028a9a4937bd] of a covering envelope before the close of the poll, place it unopened in a postal voters’ ballot box.

(2)[^c-7614ff3b43511fe1ba1a85a3c851ac05] This regulation does not apply to the receipt of a covering envelope for a tendered postal ballot paper.
