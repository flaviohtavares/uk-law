## 89. Sealing of receptacles[^key-838308ef3d99ca4ca9b561f88ddf5288]

*Extent: N.I.*

As soon as practicable after the completion of the procedure under regulation 88, the returning officer shall make up into separate packets the contents of—

- (a) the receptacle of rejected votes; and
- (b) the receptacle of declarations of identity,

and shall seal up such packets.
