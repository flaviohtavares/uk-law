## 81B. Alternative means of returning postal ballot paper[^c-d25e3178a2e053229c0075f589f1180a]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of rule 45(2)(a) of the election rules, a postal ballot paper—

- (a) may be returned to the returning officer by post or by hand, and
- (b) must be returned to the returning officer in a covering envelope.
