## 40. Representations regarding clerical errors[^key-77a451de75d542151a95ef11c91429f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 13BA(8)[^f00035] of the 1983 Act a representation may be made orally or in writing.

(2) Where a representation is made at a polling station to a presiding officer, the presiding officer must as soon as practicable communicate that representation to the registration officer.
