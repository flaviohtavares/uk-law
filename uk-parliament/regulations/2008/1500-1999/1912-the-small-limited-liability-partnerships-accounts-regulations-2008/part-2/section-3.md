# The Small Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Non-IAS individual accounts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 3 of the Small Companies Accounts Regulations applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **3. Non-IAS individual accounts**
>
> (1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation and regulation 5A,[^key-4f65f60daa32cc5d1f9073dd21f1bc27] non-IAS individual accounts under section 396 of the 2006 Act (non-IAS individual accounts) must comply with the provisions of Schedule 1 to the Small Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008 [^c19348951] as to the form and content of the balance sheet and profit and loss account, and additional information to be provided by way of notes to the accounts.
>
> (1A)[^key-a153666263b9f39459cd0c58c27b9b6b] Sections C (alternative accounting rules) and D (fair value accounting) in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations do not apply to an LLP which qualifies as a micro-entity in relation to a financial year (see sections 384A and 384B of the 2006 Act) and whose accounts for that year are prepared in accordance with the exemption permitted by—
>
> - (a) regulation 5A, or
> - (b) paragraph 1(1A) of Section A in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations.
>
> (2)[^key-1572a7da2cad08e0205c17954eb8c63b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (3) Accounts are treated as having complied with any provision of Schedule 1 to the Small Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008 if they comply instead with the corresponding provision of Schedule 1 to the Large and Medium-sized Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008 [^c19348971].

(2) The provisions of Schedule 1 to the Small Companies Accounts Regulations apply to LLPs, modified so that they are the provisions set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.
