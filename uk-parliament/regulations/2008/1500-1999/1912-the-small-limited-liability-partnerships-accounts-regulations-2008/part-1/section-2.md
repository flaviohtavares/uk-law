# The Small Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement and application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations come into force on 1st October 2008.

(2) They apply in relation to financial years beginning on or after 1st October 2008.

(3) They apply to LLPs which are subject to the small LLPs regime under Part 15 of the 2006 Act (see section 381 of that Act [^c19348941]) provisions of the Small Companies Accounts Regulations, with modifications.
