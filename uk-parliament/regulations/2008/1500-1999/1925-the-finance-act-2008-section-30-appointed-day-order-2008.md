# The Finance Act 2008, Section 30 (Appointed Day) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1925 (C. 88)

Corporation Tax

The Finance Act 2008, Section 30 (Appointed Day) Order 2008

Made

22nd July 2008

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2) of the Finance Act 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2008, Section 30 (Appointed Day) Order 2008.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the purposes of section 30 of the Finance Act 2008 (vaccine research relief: declaration about effect of relief) is 1st August 2008.

Claire Ward

Dave Watts

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

22nd July 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st August 2008 as the appointed day for the purposes of section 30 of the Finance Act 2008 (vaccine research relief: declaration about effect of relief). The amendments made by section 30(1) of that Act have effect by reference to the appointed day.
