# The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2008/1436 (C. 65)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1436) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2008 No. 1591 (C. 70)

Legal Services, England And Wales

The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

18th June 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 211(2) of the Legal Services Act 2007[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) Order 2008

(1) The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) Order 2008[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(d)(ii) for “, 113(b)(iii),” substitute—

> and 113(b)(iii);
>
> - (iii) in Part 3, paragraphs

.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

18th June 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects a drafting error in the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) Order 2007.
