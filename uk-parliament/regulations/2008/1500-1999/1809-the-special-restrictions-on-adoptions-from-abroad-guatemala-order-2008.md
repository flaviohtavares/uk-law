# The Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Abroad (Guatemala) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1809

Children And Young Persons, England And Wales

Children And Young Persons, Northern Ireland

The Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Abroad (Guatemala) Order 2008

Made

7th July 2008

Laid before Parliament

11th July 2008

Coming into force

1st August 2008

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(4) of the Children and Adoption Act 2006[^f00001]:

In accordance with section 9(5)(a) of that Act, he has consulted the Welsh Ministers[^f00002] and, in accordance with section 9(5)(b) of that Act, he has consulted the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety in Northern Ireland:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Abroad (Guatemala) Order 2008 and comes into force on 1st August 2008.

## 2. Declaration of special restrictions

Special restrictions are to apply for the time being in relation to the bringing of children into the United Kingdom from Guatemala in the cases mentioned in section 9(2) of the Children and Adoption Act 2006.

Kevin Brennan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Children, Schools and Families

7th July 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 9(4) of the Children and Adoption Act 2006 provides for the Secretary of State by order to declare that special restrictions are to apply for the time being to the bringing of children into the United Kingdom from a country or territory outside the British Islands in certain cases involving adoption. By article 2 of this Order, the Secretary of State makes such a declaration in relation to Guatemala.
