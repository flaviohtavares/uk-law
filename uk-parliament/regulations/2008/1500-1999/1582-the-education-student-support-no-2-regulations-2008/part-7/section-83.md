# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. General

An eligible student or person treated as an eligible student qualifies for a college fee loan in connection with the student’s attendance on a qualifying course in accordance with this Part.
