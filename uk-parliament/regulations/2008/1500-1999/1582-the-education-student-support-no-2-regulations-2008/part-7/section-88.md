# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 88

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Availability of college fee loans[^c-68b099ed3f9ecc63be6b5108b4e64347]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A college fee loan is available in respect of each standard academic year of the qualifying course and in respect of one academic year of the qualifying course that is not a standard academic year.

**89.**[^c-423d16b3ff160f637fc94d9f2a3bbfa8] Where a qualifying student is allowed to study the content of one standard academic year of the qualifying course over two or more academic years, for the purpose of determining whether a college fee loan is available to the qualifying student for those years, the first of such years of study is to be treated as a standard academic year and the following academic years of that kind are to be treated as academic years that are not standard academic years.

**90.**[^c-8d6d7cf4643eba52482eca41ae5babbd] For the purposes of this Part, “**standard academic year**” means an academic year of the qualifying course that would be taken by a person who does not repeat any part of the course and who enters the course at the same point as the qualifying student.
