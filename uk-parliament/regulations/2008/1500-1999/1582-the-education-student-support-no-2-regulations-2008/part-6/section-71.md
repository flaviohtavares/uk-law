# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Old system students with full entitlement[^c-1661a20e1464857736f81759b0ce3a5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,673;
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,643;
- (c) for a student in category C, £5,653;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,745.

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,319;
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,048;
- (c) for a student in category C, £4,915;
- (d) for a student in category D, £4,391.
