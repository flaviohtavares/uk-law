# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 113

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Overpayments of college fee loan[^c-54aee6698aeba9beee57abfa035bcd3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any overpayment of college fee loan is recoverable by the Secretary of State from the college or permanent private hall.
