# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Overpayments of fee support

Any overpayment of fee support is recoverable by the Secretary of State from the academic authority.
