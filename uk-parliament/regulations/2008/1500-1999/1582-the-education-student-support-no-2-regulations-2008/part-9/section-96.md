## 96. Application of contribution – 2008 cohort students[^c-c5068303f5e312f61c542bcae0c920bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the case of a 2008 cohort student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 99.
