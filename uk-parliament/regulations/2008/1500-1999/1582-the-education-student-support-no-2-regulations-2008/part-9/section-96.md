# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Application of contribution – 2008 cohort students

In the case of a 2008 cohort student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 99.
