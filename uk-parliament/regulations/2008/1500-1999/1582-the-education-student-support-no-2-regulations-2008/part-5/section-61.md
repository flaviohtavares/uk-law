# The Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amount of the special support grant – current system students who are not 2008 cohort students[^c-0fcdb0c469ccad15d262a61046b73e58]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The maximum amount of special support grant available to a current system student who is not a 2008 cohort student in respect of an academic year is—

- (a) in the case of a type 1 or type 3 teacher training student, £1,453;
- (b) in the case of a type 2 teacher training student, £2,906;
- (c) in the case of a current system student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student, £2,906.

(2) A type 1 teacher training student who is not a 2008 cohort student and who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £1,453;
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £27,819, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1582) where—
  - *M* is £1,453
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £5.86 by which the household income exceeds £18,360; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,819 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £646.

(3) A type 2 teacher training student who is not a 2008 cohort student and who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £2,906;
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £27,819, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1582) where—
  - *M* is £2,906
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £5.86 by which the household income exceeds £18,360; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,819 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £1,292.

(4) A type 3 teacher training student who is not a 2008 cohort student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £1,453;
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £39,333, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1582) where—
  - *M* is £1,453
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £5.86 by which the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £27,819
  - *B* is £1 for every complete £9.27 by which the household income exceeds £27,819 but does not exceed £39,333; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £39,333, no special support grant is payable.

(5) A current system student other than a 2008 cohort student or a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £2,906;
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £27,819, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1582) where—
  - *M* is £2,906
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £5.86 by which the household income exceeds £18,360;
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,819 but does not exceed £39,333, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1582) where—
  - *RM* is £1,292
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £9.27 by which the household income exceeds £27,819; and
- (d) where the household income exceeds £39,333, no special support grant is payable.
