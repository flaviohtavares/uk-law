# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 3

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “**1985 Act**” means the Companies Act 1985,
- “**1986 Order**” means the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, and
- “**LLP**” means a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000[^key-0468bc558ae39c4534fdee179b52e23f].

(2) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

- (a) any reference to a numbered Part, section or Schedule is to the Part, section or Schedule so numbered in the Companies Act 2006,
- (b) references in provisions applied to LLPs to other provisions of the Companies Act 2006 are to those provisions as applied to LLPs by these Regulations, and
- (c) references in provisions applied to LLPs to provisions of the Insolvency Act 1986 [^c19348651] or the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 [^c19348661] are to those provisions as applied to LLPs by the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001 [^c19348671] or the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 [^c19348681].
