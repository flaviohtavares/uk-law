# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 59

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Review[^key-f80df01fefa3e31e5a4e7ea51107a784]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must from time to time—

- (a)[^key-fe79c1df0502875ffc850bb1a499c96b] carry out a review, respectively, of the regulatory provision contained in these Regulations to which amendments have been made by—
  - (i) Part 2 of the Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016 (“the 2016 Regulations”),
  - (ii) Schedule 3 to the Statutory Auditors Regulations 2017, and
  - (iii) Part 3 of the Companies (Directors’ Report) and Limited Liability Partnerships (Energy and Carbon Report) Regulations 2018 (“the 2018 Regulations”),
- (b) set out the conclusions of each review[^key-f992296084ccf1206421f55d2124d614] in a separate[^key-942a97ef0f2bc394f8d6f2faeb7e908d] report, and
- (c) publish the report.

(1A)[^key-c17a8badd87983ed51245f4bdaa30b01] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Section 30(4) of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 requires that a report published under this regulation[^key-465f77b0182cf58b4d51c4e56ff9cdaf] must, in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by those provisions,
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved,
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate, and
- (d) if those objectives remain appropriate, assess the extent to which they could be achieved in another way which involves less onerous regulatory provision.

(3)[^key-7366785ea02d400d09fccc3c28686852] The first report under—

- (a) paragraph (1)(a)(i) and (ii) must be published before the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date on which the 2016 Regulations come into force;
- (b) paragraph (1)(a)(iii) must be published before the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date on which the 2018 Regulations come into force.

(4) Subsequent reports under this regulation must be published at intervals not exceeding 5 years.

(5) In this regulation, “regulatory provision” has the same meaning as in sections 28 to 32[^key-9fb1fe771fa5d848ed57fb7c578bf9de] of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 (see section 32 of that Act)[^key-693ec7debfab67d3f385ae1fe4d305ca].
