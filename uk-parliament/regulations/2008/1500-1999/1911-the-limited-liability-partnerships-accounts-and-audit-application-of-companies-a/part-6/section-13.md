# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 13

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Publication of annual accounts and reports[^c-18d4152eb73e1547c8cfd1352ed0d775]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 423 applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **423. Duty to circulate copies of annual accounts and reports[^c-0b7e0023e770c41d5001f3064b8acc86]**
>
> (1) Every LLP must send a copy of its annual accounts and reports[^c-839a37041ade3a3ff9a02e1ce77f78c1] for each financial year to—
>
> - (a) every member of the LLP, and
> - (b) every holder of the LLP's debentures,
>
> not later than the end of the period for filing accounts , the strategic report (if any)[^c-9411a5dffc2889a1b89a43b31755d800] , the auditor’s report on them and the energy and carbon report[^c-39e0b92b742984afbdbd564bef5a1173], or, if earlier, the date on which it actually delivers its accounts , the strategic report (if any) and the auditor's report on those accounts and that strategic report[^c-1095c52d05882de89bb23b549694743d] and the energy and carbon report (if any)[^c-b1bdad5378b381f6a19e872ee0add00c] to the registrar.
>
> (2) Copies need not be sent to a person for whom the LLP does not have a current address.
>
> (3) An LLP has a “current address” for a person if—
>
> - (a) an address has been notified to the LLP by the person as one at which documents may be sent to him, and
> - (b) the LLP has no reason to believe that documents sent to him at that address will not reach him.
>
> (4) Where copies are sent out over a period of days, references in this Act to the day on which copies are sent out shall be read as references to the last day of that period.
