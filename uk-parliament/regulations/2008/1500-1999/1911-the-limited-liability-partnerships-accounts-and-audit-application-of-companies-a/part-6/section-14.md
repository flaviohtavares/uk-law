# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 14

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Default in sending out copies of accounts and reports[^c-e47d76e47ab1e1eb1ed6786ea76937e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 425 applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **425. Default in sending out copies of accounts and reports : offences[^c-8cfc543e76a3d4c645fd2226a1c8e3ae]**
>
> (1) If default is made in complying with section 423, an offence is committed by—
>
> - (a) the LLP, and
> - (b) every member of the LLP who is in default.
>
> (2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—
>
> - (a) on conviction on indictment, to a fine;
> - (b) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum.
