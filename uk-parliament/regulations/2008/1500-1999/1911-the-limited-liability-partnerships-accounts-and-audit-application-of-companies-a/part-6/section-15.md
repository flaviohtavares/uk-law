# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 15

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Right of member or debenture holder to copies of accounts and reports[^key-8385b59dda3574da32da9d1713fe5583]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 431 applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **431. Right of member or debenture holder to copies of accounts and reports[^key-22889c24afc01cb2d0f46954743cd2a0]**
>
> (1) A member of, or holder of debentures of, an LLP is entitled to be provided, on demand and without charge, with a copy of—
>
> - (a) the LLP's last annual accounts, [^key-51d550a210bc0db2ebc292bcc148ff64]...
> - (aa)[^key-548e38b42a986e30c364e4bdabdecdcf] the last strategic report (if any),
> - (b) the auditor's report on those accounts (including the statement (where applicable) on that strategic report)[^key-2e0828ea17d730ef8545b505c7195a17] , and[^key-ea089924b3991f910de596371e141e1c]
> - (c)[^key-be30d52c9d6e356bb82c5403915fce51] the last energy and carbon report (if any).
>
> (2) The entitlement under this section is to a single copy of those documents, but that is in addition to any copy to which a person may be entitled under section 423.
>
> (3) If a demand made under this section is not complied with within seven days of receipt by the LLP, an offence is committed by—
>
> - (a) the LLP, and
> - (b) every member of the LLP who is in default.
>
> (4) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale and, for continued contravention, a daily default fine not exceeding one-tenth of level 3 on the standard scale.
