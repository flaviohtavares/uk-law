# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 57

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1292 applies to LLPs for the purposes of these Regulations[^key-7e3f3b5d8a57d977efadd3e5e683ae42], modified so that it reads as follows—

> **1292. Regulations and orders: supplementary**
>
> (1) Regulations under this Act may—
>
> - (a) make different provision for different cases or circumstances,
> - (b) include supplementary, incidental and consequential provision, and
> - (c) make transitional provision and savings.
>
> (2) Any provision that may be made by regulations under this Act subject to negative resolution procedure may be made by regulations subject to affirmative resolution procedure.
