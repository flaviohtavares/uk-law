# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 54

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Requirements of this Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1172 applies to LLPs for the purposes of these Regulations[^c-7e3f3b5d8a57d977efadd3e5e683ae42], modified so that it reads as follows—

> **1172. References to requirements of this Act**
>
> References in the provisions of this Act applied to LLPs to the requirements of this Act include the requirements of regulations and orders made under it.
