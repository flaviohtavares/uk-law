# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008, section 29

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Meaning of “annual accounts”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 471 applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **471. Meaning of “annual accounts” and related expressions**
>
> (1) In this Part an LLP's “**annual accounts**”, in relation to a financial year, means—
>
> - (a)[^key-1da4c366be52457bd0da27f442081539] any individual accounts prepared by the LLP for that year (see section 394), and
> - (b) any group accounts prepared by the LLP for that year (see section[^key-9a1056c273567d03df9b4461d3b35ac9] 399).
>
> This is subject to section 408 (option to omit individual profit and loss account from annual accounts where information given in notes to the individual balance sheet[^key-55fe0de6b775695f9ea96126e99c97fe]).
>
> (2)[^key-2ea7a328c4b750b4c4433ed47232e835] In this Part an LLP’s “annual accounts and reports” for a financial year are—
>
> - (a) its annual accounts,
> - (b) the strategic report (if any),
> - (c) the energy and carbon report (if any),
> - (d) the auditor’s report on those accounts and the strategic report (where this is covered by the auditor’s report), unless the LLP is exempt from audit.
