# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2008-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1518
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2008 No. 1518

Road Traffic, England

The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008

Made

11th June 2008

Laid before Parliament

13th June 2008

Coming into force

7th July 2008

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 8 and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 10 to the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001].

The Borough Council of Dudley has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under these powers with respect to the whole of its area.

The Secretary of State has consulted the chief constable of the West Midlands Police in accordance with the requirements of paragraphs 8(3) of Schedule 8 and 3(4) of Schedule 10.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-b6d423d77b2b81ddba4c3ee187841301]

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 7th July 2008.

## 2. Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area[^c-2639f6cd5f5f57ab25eeb736b39e33b8]

The Secretary of State designates The Borough Council of Dudley as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and
- (b) a special enforcement area.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton

Minister of State

Department for Transport

11th June 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the Borough Council of Dudley as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant effect on the cost to business and the voluntary sector.
