# The Immigration (Notices) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1819

Immigration

The Immigration (Notices) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2008

Made

8th July 2008

Laid before Parliament

10th July 2008

Coming into force

1st August 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Notices) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st August 2008.

## 2. Amendments to the Immigration (Notices) Regulations 2003

(1) The Immigration (Notices) Regulations 2003[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “immigration decision” after “section 82(2)” insert “and (3A)[^f00003]”.

(3) In regulation 5(1)(b) (contents of notice), for “or (j)” substitute “,(j) or (3A)”.

Liam Byrne

Minister of State

Home Office

8th July 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Immigration (Notices) Regulations 2003 to ensure that certain provisions about the notice, content and service of immigration decisions apply where the Secretary of State decides that the automatic deportation provisions of the UK Borders Act 2007 (section 32(5)) apply to a person.
