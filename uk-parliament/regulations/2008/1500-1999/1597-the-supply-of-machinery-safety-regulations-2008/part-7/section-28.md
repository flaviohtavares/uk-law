# The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1597

## 28. Time-limited derogation for impact machinery

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to—

- (a) portable cartridge-operated fixing machinery; or
- (b) other impact machinery,

before 29 June 2011, but shall have effect in relation to such machinery from that date.
