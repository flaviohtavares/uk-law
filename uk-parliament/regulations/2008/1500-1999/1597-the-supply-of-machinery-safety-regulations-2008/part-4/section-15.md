# The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1597
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 15. Protection of UK marking[^M_E_ec1a753c-509c-47b0-abb0-63054ccf8fac][^c-f77c2c24fe7a90594341230744b10686]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No person shall affix the UK[^c-c8e562ba9f256b9a3634cc39c9d4b18b] marking to machinery which does not comply with these Regulations.

(2) No person shall affix to machinery any marking, sign or inscription which is likely to mislead any other person as to the meaning or form of the UK marking (or both).

(3) Other markings may be affixed to machinery which bears the UK marking only if the visibility, legibility or meaning of the UK marking is not impaired as a result.

## 15. Protection of CE marking[^M_E_af0e6872-433e-4f4d-c6d0-3b1ff898d09e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person shall affix the CE marking to machinery which does not comply with these Regulations.

(2) No person shall affix to machinery any marking, sign or inscription which is likely to mislead any other person as to the meaning or form of the CE marking (or both).

(3) Other markings may be affixed to machinery which bears the CE marking only if the visibility, legibility or meaning of the CE marking is not impaired as a result.
