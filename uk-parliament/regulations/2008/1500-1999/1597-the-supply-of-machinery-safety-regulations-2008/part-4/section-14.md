# The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1597
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Machinery covered by more than one enactment[^M_E_d4df962d-016b-40c5-b3f2-4d8b59b7d382][^key-3964d2eff3a6f394e990f45d958c088b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where machinery falls within the scope of an[^M_F_27a3b719-a81f-4401-e2e4-b0a231a70190] enactment[^M_F_96491c46-a566-4dfd-e245-6c867fb933ef] other than these Regulations[^key-a499cf0b59592c728a2a59c7e772d3ef] (“ **the other enactment[^key-34e23f0f5ed354477fd26b03ac50c8fa]**”), the affixing of the UK[^key-eb2089239346f008182902a9cae6a328] marking indicates that the relevant requirements of the other enactment are also satisfied.

(2) Where—

- (a) the other enactment[^key-75a112b13746d1641c0bc5aa1fe912e0] includes a provision allowing the responsible person to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, the system to be applied;
- (b) the responsible person takes advantage of this option; and
- (c) the particulars of the other enactment which the responsible person has chosen to apply, [^key-5369a934cafcde19a3d2f0f98d62b3ff]... are given in the [^key-5bd9a4ed3ec5c069f2d8215742ddbe17]... declaration of conformity,

the affixing of the UK[^key-6b794f04ecec6b72770568043b78b451] marking indicates that the machinery conforms only to the provisions of the other enactment which the responsible person has chosen to apply.

## 14. Machinery covered by more than one Directive[^M_E_f0913390-c01e-41b1-87b4-4457c38f0eea]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where machinery falls within the scope of an[^M_F_4686805c-3b41-444c-be4f-23d563dd84bc] NI Protocol obligation[^key-c4887957aa0ece80f4a7e3dee1955f9e] other than the Directive (“**the other NI Protocol obligation[^key-d9e59ff97ee93e3f2c0146e326df4642]**”), the affixing of the CE marking indicates that the relevant requirements of the other NI Protocol obligation are also satisfied.

(2) Where—

- (a) the other NI Protocol obligation includes a provision allowing the responsible person to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, the system to be applied;
- (b) the responsible person takes advantage of this option; and
- (c) the particulars of the other NI Protocol obligation which the responsible person has chosen to apply, as published in the *Official Journal of the European Union*, are given in the EC declaration of conformity,

the affixing of the CE marking indicates that the machinery conforms only to the provisions of the other NI Protocol obligation which the responsible person has chosen to apply.
