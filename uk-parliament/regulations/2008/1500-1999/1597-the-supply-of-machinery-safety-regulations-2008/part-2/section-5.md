# The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1597
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 5. Disapplication where more specific ... safety rules apply[^M_E_6401bf93-d3e3-4102-a933-6bf034880832][^c-34e09cd3e2fa9ad561ed3bf2d8988939]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations do not apply to a product if, or to the extent that, other enactments (as they apply in Great Britain)[^M_F_cfedfde9-051e-4c87-e61b-144f3759c22d], which apply to it, make more specific provision than these Regulations[^M_F_dc9de9ff-e550-485f-9716-242b740f34ce] in connection with the hazards referred to in Annex I (Part 1 of Schedule 2).

## 5. Disapplication where more specific safety obligations apply[^M_E_b5958f81-821c-45c3-919f-40daa1342848][^c-ba7f57a315f14cc41f1d8ad7c83bcbdb]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to a product if, or to the extent that, paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies where any NI Protocol obligation, other than any obligation in relation to the Directive, applies to the product and makes more specific provision than the Directive in connection with the hazards referred to in Annex I (Part 1 of Schedule 2).
