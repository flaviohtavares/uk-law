# The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/1597

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Annex IV machinery not manufactured fully in accordance with designated standards or not fully covered by such standards[^M_E_14785ae6-7cc7-4c76-8801-38baee9acb6a][^key-8788cf9a029c7ac14e4347f7acd39d51]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to machinery—

- (a) which falls within a category which is referred to in Annex IV (Part 4 of Schedule 2); and
- (b) in respect of which any of the following conditions is satisfied—
  - (i) that the machinery is not manufactured in accordance with the designated[^key-228707580b0d1c971c1de598399105ad] standards which relate to it; or
  - (ii) that the machinery is only partly manufactured in accordance with the designated standards which relate to it; or
  - (iii) that the designated standards in accordance with which the machinery is manufactured do not cover all the applicable essential health and safety requirements; or
  - (iv) that no designated[^key-2995e9415a706c40738e8052fa5331be] standards exist for the machinery.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the responsible person shall follow one of the following conformity assessment procedures—

- (a) the Type-[^key-bfe16ccc78bb2cd0f5b04cf6ac4fc720] examination procedure prescribed in Annex IX (Part 9 of Schedule 2) and the internal checks on the manufacture of machinery prescribed in Annex VIII (Part 8 of Schedule 2), point 3; or
- (b) the full quality assurance procedure prescribed in Annex X (Part 10 of Schedule 2).

## 12. Annex IV machinery not manufactured fully in accordance with published harmonised standards or not fully covered by such standards[^M_E_4d32e011-d157-45d8-e3b4-b4aa056a76a6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to machinery—

- (a) which falls within a category which is referred to in Annex IV (Part 4 of Schedule 2); and
- (b) in respect of which any of the following conditions is satisfied—
  - (i) that the machinery is not manufactured in accordance with the published harmonised standards which relate to it; or
  - (ii) that the machinery is only partly manufactured in accordance with the published harmonised standards which relate to it; or
  - (iii) that the published harmonised standards in accordance with which the machinery is manufactured do not cover all the applicable essential health and safety requirements; or
  - (iv) that no harmonised standards exist for the machinery.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the responsible person shall follow one of the following conformity assessment procedures—

- (a) the EC type-examination procedure prescribed in Annex IX (Part 9 of Schedule 2) and the internal checks on the manufacture of machinery prescribed in Annex VIII (Part 8 of Schedule 2), point 3; or
- (b) the full quality assurance procedure prescribed in Annex X (Part 10 of Schedule 2).
