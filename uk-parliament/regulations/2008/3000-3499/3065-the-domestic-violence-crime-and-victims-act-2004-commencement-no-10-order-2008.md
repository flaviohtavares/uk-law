# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3065 (C. 131)

Criminal Law, Northern Ireland

The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008

Made

17th November 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 60 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2008.

**2.** Section 46 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Victims of mentally disordered persons) shall come into force on 14th December 2008.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

17th November 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 14th December 2008 section 46 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act [2004 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28).
