# The Rates of Child Benefit (Commencement) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3247

Social Security

The Rates of Child Benefit (Commencement) Order 2008

Made

18th December 2008

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 145(3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and section 141(3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002] and now vested in them[^f00003].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Rates of Child Benefit (Commencement) Order 2008.

## 2. Coming into force of new rates of child benefit

The different rates of child benefit prescribed in the Child Benefit (Rates) (Amendment) Regulations 2008[^f00004] shall come into force on 5th January 2009.

Tony Cunningham

Frank Roy

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

18th December 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that different rates of child benefit prescribed under the Child Benefit (Rates) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 shall come into force on 5th January 2009.
