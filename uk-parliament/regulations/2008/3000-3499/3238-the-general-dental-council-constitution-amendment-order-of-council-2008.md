# The General Dental Council (Constitution) (Amendment) Order of Council 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3238

Health Care And Associated Professions

Dentists

Professions Complementary To Dentistry

The General Dental Council (Constitution) (Amendment) Order of Council 2008

Made

16th December 2008

Laid before Parliament

17th December 2008

Coming into force

19th January 2009

Their Lordships make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2A) and (2B) of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the General Dental Council (Constitution) (Amendment) Order of Council 2008 and comes into force on 19th January 2009.

## 2. Amendment of article 4 of the General Dental Council (Constitution) Order of Council 2006

In article 4 of the General Dental Council (Constitution) Order of Council 2006[^f00002] (tenure of office of members of the Council), for paragraph (2) substitute the following paragraph—

> (2) The term of office of a member—
>
> - (a) whose term of office—
>   - (i) prior to the coming into force of the General Dental Council (Constitution) (Amendment) Order of Council 2007[^f00003], would have expired at the end of 9th April 2008, and
>   - (ii) thereafter but prior to the coming into force of this paragraph, would have expired at the end of 9th April 2009; or
> - (b) appointed on or after 9th April 2008 whose term of office, prior to the coming into force of this paragraph, would have expired at the end of 9th April 2009,
>
> shall expire at the end of 9th October 2009.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision with regard to the constitution of the General Dental Council. Its effect is to extend the terms of office of the members of the Council which were due to expire at the end of 9th April 2009. They will instead expire at the end of 9th October 2009.
