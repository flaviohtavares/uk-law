# The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3204 (C. 145)

Children And Young Persons, England

Protection Of Vulnerable Adults, England

The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2008

Made

16th December 2008

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 61(5) and 65 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2008.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on the 17th December 2008

17th December 2008 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act for the purposes of making regulations[^f00002]—

- (a) section 63(1) (amendments and repeals) in so far as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 9 set out in paragraphs (b) and (c);
- (b)
  - (i) paragraph 5(2), (3), (6) and (7), in relation to England;
  - (ii) paragraph 5(4);
  - (iii) paragraph 5(1), in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraphs (i) and (ii);
  - (iv) paragraph 6(2), (3) and (6), in relation to England;
  - (v) paragraph 6(4);
  - (vi) paragraph 6(1), in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraphs (iv) and (v);
  - (vii) paragraph 7, in relation to England;
- (c) paragraph 2, in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in paragraph (b).

Delyth Morgan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Children, Schools and Families

16th December 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the fourth Commencement Order made under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (“the Act”). Article 2 brings into force the provisions of the Act which amend sections 15 and 15A of, and Schedule 2 to, the Teaching and Higher Education Act [1998 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/30) in so far as they relate to England and for the purposes of making regulations under sections 15 and 15A.
