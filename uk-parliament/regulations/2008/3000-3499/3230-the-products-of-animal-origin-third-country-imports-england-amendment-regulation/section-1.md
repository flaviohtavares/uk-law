## 1. Title, application and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2008;
- (b) apply in England; and
- (c) come into force on 15th January 2009.
