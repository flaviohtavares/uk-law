# The UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3136 (C. 135)

Immigration

The UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008

Made

5th December 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 59(2) and (3)(a) of the UK Borders Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the UK Borders Act 2007 shall come into force on 6<sup>th</sup> January 2009 —

- (a) section 21 (children);
- (b) for all remaining purposes, section 51 (Border and Immigration Inspectorate: plans); and
- (c) for all remaining purposes, sections 52 and 53 (Border and Immigration Inspectorate: relationship with other bodies).

Phil Woolas

Minister of State

Home Office

5th December 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6<sup>th</sup> January 2009 section 21 of the UK Borders Act 2007 which concerns the issuing of a code of practice, designed to ensure that in exercising functions, the Border and Immigration Agency takes appropriate steps to ensure that while children are in the UK, they are safe from harm. On the same date, this Order also brings into force sections 51, 52 and 53 of the Act for all remaining purposes. These provisions concern the Chief Inspector of the Border and Immigration Agency.
