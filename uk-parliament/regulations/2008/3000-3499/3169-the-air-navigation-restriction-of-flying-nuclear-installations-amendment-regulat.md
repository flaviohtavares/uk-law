# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3169

Civil Aviation

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

11th December 2008

Coming into force

16th January 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport deems it necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in the vicinity of certain nuclear installations and, in exercise of the powers conferred under article 96 of the Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 16th January 2009.

**2.** The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) Regulations 2007[^f00002] are amended as follows.

**3.** In Schedule 2, in column 3 of the entry for Rosyth, for “560121N 0032709W” substitute “560147N 0032703W”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

11th December 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make a small change to the position of the restricted area over the nuclear installation at Rosyth. This is in order to re-centre the restricted zone over the one remaining nuclear facility at Rosyth Dockyard. The change will be notified in the United Kingdom Aeronautical Information Publication.
