# The Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2097

Road Traffic

The Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

28th July 2008

Laid before Parliament

5th August 2008

Coming into force

1st October 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 165B of the Road Traffic Act 1988 [^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

In accordance with section 195(2)[^f00002] of that Act, she has consulted with such representative organisations as she thinks fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st October 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) Regulations 2005

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) Regulations 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following regulations.

(2) In relation to vehicles removed prior to 1 October 2008, nothing in these Regulations shall have effect and the provisions mentioned in paragraph (1) shall continue to have effect.

**3.** In regulation 2 the following definitions are inserted in the appropriate alphabetical place—

> - “laden” means that the vehicle is carrying a load;
> - “load” means anything other than—
>   - (a) the body and all parts of the vehicle which are necessary to or ordinarily used with the vehicle when working on a road;
>   - (b) any water, fuel or accumulators used for the purpose of the supply of power for the propulsion of the vehicle;
>   - (c) the driver, any passengers and their personal effects;
>   - (d) a crane, works truck as defined in regulation 3(2) of the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986[^f00004], or other special appliance or apparatus which is a permanent or essentially permanent fixture of the vehicle; and
>   - (e) any containers or other equipment intended or adapted for the purpose of holding or carrying a load by the vehicle;
> - “MAM” means maximum authorised mass as defined in regulation 3(1) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999 [^f00005];
> - “off road” means that no part of the vehicle is in contact with the road;
> - “on road” means that any part of the vehicle is in contact with the road;
> - “road” means any length of highway or of any other road to which the public has access, and includes bridges over which a road passes;
> - “substantially damaged” means such damage to a vehicle that in the reasonable opinion of a constable it cannot be driven safely on the road;
> - “two wheeled vehicle” means a mechanically propelled vehicle constructed or adapted to have two wheels or less and the MAM of which does not exceed 3.5 tonnes;
> - “unladen” means that the vehicle is not carrying a load;

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic Act 1988 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) Regulations 2005 by increasing the charges that apply in respect of the removal and retention of vehicles seized by the police under section 165A of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (power to seize vehicles driven without licence or insurance). The charges were previously £105 for removal and £12 for storage per 24 hour period or part thereof.
