# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2008

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2008-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2008/2343
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

2008 No. 2343

Clean Air, England

The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2008

Made

1st September 2008

Laid before Parliament

5th September 2008

Coming into force

1st October 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21 of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is satisfied that the fireplaces exempted by this Order can be used for burning fuel other than authorised fuels[^f00002] without producing any smoke or substantial quantity of smoke.

## 1. Citation, application and commencement[^key-b2eff5b69ad2308ea05f2b147ba345e0]

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2008;
- (b) applies in England; and
- (c) comes into force on 1st October 2008.

## 2. Fireplaces exempted from the provisions of section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993[^key-7da81bffd97529eb2db68df8b72d331f]

The fireplaces listed in the Schedule are exempt from the provisions of section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (prohibition of emission of smoke in smoke control area), subject to the conditions listed in the Schedule.

## 3. Revocation[^key-22197f49996e47632773bdc241c358b2]

The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order 2008[^f00003] is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces with amendments the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/515](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/515)). It exempts the fireplaces listed in the Schedule from the provisions of section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (prohibition of smoke emissions in smoke control area), subject to the conditions set out in the Schedule. Several additional fireplaces have been exempted.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as no impact on business, the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
