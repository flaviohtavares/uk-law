# The Consumer Credit Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2008/831 (C. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/831) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2008 No. 2444 (C.105)

Consumer Credit

The Consumer Credit Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

15th September 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 71(2) of the Consumer Credit Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Consumer Credit Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008.

## 2. Amendment of Order

(1) The Consumer Credit Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2008[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(2) for “1st October 2008” substitute “31st October 2008”.

(3) In the heading to Schedule 3 for “1ST OCTOBER 2008” substitute “31ST OCTOBER 2008”.

Gareth Thomas

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Trade and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Enterprise & Regulatory Reform

15th September 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Consumer Credit Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2008 (the Original Order) to correct the date the provisions in Schedule 3 are being brought into force.

The Order is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of the Original Order.
