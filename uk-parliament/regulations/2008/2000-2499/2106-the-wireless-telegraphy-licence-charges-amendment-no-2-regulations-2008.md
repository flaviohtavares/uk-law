# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2106

Electronic Communications

The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made

5th August 2008

Coming into force

29th August 2008

The Office of Communications (“OFCOM”) make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12, 13(2) and 122(7) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006[^f00001], (“the Act”).

Before making these Regulations OFCOM have given notice of their proposal to do so in accordance with section 122(4)(a) of the Act, published notice of their proposal in accordance with section 122(4)(b) of the Act, and have considered the representations made to them before the time specified in the notice in accordance with section 122(4)(c) of the Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 29th August 2008.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) Regulations 2005[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) Regulations 2005

The principal Regulations shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 3

In regulation 3 (interpretation) in paragraph (1)—

- (a) after the definition of “earth station” insert the following definition—

  > “exclusive basis” means authorised use of a channel which is not shared with any other licensee;

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “fixed mobile station” insert the following definitions—

  > “high population area” means such part of the British Islands being an area listed in Schedule 9 and identified by reference to a grid square of the 2<sup>nd</sup> series of Landranger maps published by the Ordnance Survey which is considered by OFCOM to have a high proportion of the population;
  >
  > “high usage bands” mean any of UHF Band I, UHF Band II and the VHF High Band;

  ;
- (c) after the definition of “hub” insert the following definition—

  > large coverage area” means an area within which wireless telegraphy is authorised under a licence condition permitting—
  >
  > - (a) effective radiated power of transmission greater than 5 Watts where antenna height is greater than 10 metres;
  > - (b) effective radiated power of transmission less than or equal to 5 Watts where antenna height is greater than 30 metres; or
  > - (c) transmission within a circular area which has a radius greater than 15 kilometres but less than or equal to 30 kilometres;

  ;
- (d) after the definition of “licensee” insert the following definitions—

  > “low population area” means such part of the British Islands being an area listed in Schedule 9 and identified by reference to a grid square of the 2<sup>nd</sup> series of Landranger maps published by the Ordnance Survey which is considered by OFCOM to have a low proportion of the population;
  >
  > “low usage bands” mean the 133 kHz – 147 kHz band, the 26.225 MHz band, the 49.49375 MHz band, VHF Band I and VHF Low Band;
  >
  > “medium coverage area” means an area within which wireless telegraphy is authorised under a licence condition permitting—
  >
  > - (a) effective radiated power of transmission less than or equal to 5 Watts where antenna height is greater than 10 metres and less than or equal to 30 metres;
  > - (b) effective radiated power of transmission greater than 5 Watts where antenna height is less than or equal to 10 metres; or
  > - (c) transmission within a circular area which has a radius greater than 3 kilometres but less than or equal to 15 kilometres;
  >
  > “medium population area” means such part of the British Islands being an area listed in Schedule 9 and identified by reference to a grid square of the 2<sup>nd</sup> series of Landranger maps published by the Ordnance Survey which is considered by OFCOM to have a medium proportion of the population;
  >
  > “medium usage bands” mean VHF Band III and the VHF Mid Band;

  ;
- (e) after the definition of “prescribed time” insert the following definition—

  > “shared basis” means authorised use of a channel which is shared with another licensee;
- (f) after the definition of “slot” insert the following definition—

  > “small coverage area” means an area within which wireless telegraphy is authorised under a licence condition permitting—
  >
  > - (a) effective radiated power of transmissions less than or equal to 5 Watts where antenna height is less than or equal to 10 metres; or
  > - (b) transmission within a circular area which is less than or equal to 3 kilometres;

  ; and
- (g) after the definition of “UHF Band II” insert the following definitions—

  > “VHF Band I” means that part of the radio frequency spectrum between 55.75 MHz and 68.0 MHz;
  >
  > “VHF Band III” means that part of the radio frequency spectrum between 177.20625 MHz and 207.49375 MHz;

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1378](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1378) as amended by [S.I. 2006/2894](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2894), [S.I. 2007/2326](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2326) and [S.I. 2008/139](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/139)) (the “principal Regulations”).

The Regulations introduce charges for five new classes of wireless telegraphy licence. These are the Business Radio (Simple UK) class, Business Radio (Suppliers Light) class, the Business Radio (Simple Site) class, the Business Radio (Area Defined) class and the Business Area (Technically Assigned) class. The Regulations also remove from the principal Regulations the Business Radio (Standard) class.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that these Regulations will have on the costs to business is available to the public from the OFCOM Library at Riverside House, 2A Southwark Bridge Road, London SE1 9HA (Tel: 020 7981 3000) of on the OFCOM’s website at www.ofcom.org.uk. Copies of this assessment have also been placed in the libraries of the Houses of Parliament.
