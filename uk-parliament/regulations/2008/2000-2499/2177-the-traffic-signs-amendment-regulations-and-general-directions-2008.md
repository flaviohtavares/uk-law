# The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations and General Directions 2008

> Text as at 2016-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2008 No. 2177

Road Traffic

The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations and General Directions 2008

Made

12th August 2008

Laid before Parliament

18th August 2008

Coming into force

15th September 2008

The Regulations contained in Part 1 of this Instrument are made in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 64(1), (2) and (3) and 85(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has consulted with representative organisations in relation to those Regulations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act.

The General Directions contained in Part 2 of this Instrument are given in exercise of the powers conferred by section 65(1) of that Act[^f00002].

Accordingly the Secretary of State makes the Regulations and gives the General Directions contained respectively in Parts 1 and 2 of this Instrument.

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

(This note is not part of the Instrument) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

This Instrument makes a number of amendments to the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Part 1 is the Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations 2008 which amend the Traffic Signs Regulations 2002 (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]**the Principal Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]**”). Regulation 3 adds a new sign to Schedule 4 of the Principal Regulations that is similar to the sign in diagram 880 in that Schedule but containing a national speed limit roundel. Regulation 4 amends cross-references from the Principal Regulations to the Traffic Signs General Directions 2002 (“the Principal Directions”) to reflect the amendments made by Part 2 of this Instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Part 2 is the Traffic Signs (Amendment) General Directions 2008 which amend the Principal Directions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Direction 3 amends direction 8(1) of the Principal Directions to permit the sign shown in diagram 675 (end of a 20mph zone and notification of new speed limit) to be used to indicate the point where a speed limit (other than a national speed limit) begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Direction 4 amends direction 10(2) of the Principal Directions so that a sign shown in diagram 671 indicating the national speed limit is not required where a sign shown in diagram 675 is displayed as varied by the permitted variation shown in item 4 of the table below that diagram. The permitted variation is to replace the 30mph speed limit roundel with a national speed limit roundel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Direction 5 amends direction 11 of the Principal Directions so that an additional sign shown in diagrams 670 or 671 may be placed on each approach to an enforcement camera, even if this affects the regular spacing of repeater signs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Direction 6 extends direction 18 of the Principal Directions to enable road markings indicating the speed limit to be placed in conjunction with the diagram 880 sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]

Direction 7 amends direction 32 of the Principal Directions to clarify its applicability to both fixed and mobile enforcement cameras and to the new diagram 880.1 sign (which may only be placed on unlit roads subject to a national speed limit). An additional diagram 880 or 880.1 sign is also now permitted on each approach to an enforcement camera. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2926011d0428e65a72069f88b6420456]
