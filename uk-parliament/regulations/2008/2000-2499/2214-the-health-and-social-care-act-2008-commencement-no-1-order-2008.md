# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.1) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2214 (C. 100)

Health Care And Associated Professions

The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.1) Order 2008

Made

15th August 2008

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 170(3) and (4) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.1) Order 2008.

## 2. Appointed day for provisions relating to the Appointments Commission

25th August 2008 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008—

- (a) section 127 (which gives effect to Schedule 10) in so far as it gives effect to the provisions referred to in paragraphs (b) and (c);
- (b) paragraph 20 of Schedule 10 (amendments to the Health Act 2006 relating to directions given by the Secretary of State to the Appointments Commission); and
- (c) paragraph 21 of Schedule 10 in so far as it relates to section 60(3)(a) of the Health Act 2006 (amendments relating to directions given by the Privy Council to the Appointments Commission).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ann Keen

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

15th August 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force specified provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 relating to the Appointments Commission. The provisions listed in Article 2, which relate to the power to give directions to the Appointments Commission, are brought into force on 25th August 2008. These provisions include new powers to make directions in respect of the chair and certain non-executive members of the Council for Healthcare Regulatory Excellence.
