# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1st October 2008.

(3) Save as provided in paragraphs (4) to (7), these Regulations shall have effect from 1st October 2008.

(4) Regulation 3(h) and (j) shall have effect from 1st April 2004.

(5) The following regulations shall have effect from 14th December 2007—

- (a) regulation 3(b);
- (b) regulation 6.
