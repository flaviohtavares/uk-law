# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Interpretation of Part 3

In this Part an alphanumerical reference to a regulation is a reference to a provision of the National Health Service (Injury Benefits) Regulations 1995[^f00027] bearing that designation.
