# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Amendment of regulation T6

In regulation T6(1A) (loss of rights to benefits)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “widower” insert “, surviving nominated partner”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c)—
  - (i) after “notice” insert “or nomination”;
  - (ii) for “F5(3A)” substitute “F5”.
