# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Amendment of regulation 2.J.14

In regulation 2.J.14 (employing authority record keeping and contribution estimates)—

- (a) in the heading to that regulation, after “employing authority” insert “and certain member”; and
- (b) in paragraph (1), for “the host” substitute “each relevant host”.
