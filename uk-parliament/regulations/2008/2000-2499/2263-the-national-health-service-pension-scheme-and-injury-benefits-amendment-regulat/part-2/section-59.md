# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Amendment of regulation 3.A.8

In regulation 3.A.8 (pensionable earnings—breaks in service)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a), for “regulation 3.C.1” substitute “regulations 3.C.1 and 3.C.2”;
- (b) in paragraph (8), for “(3)(a) or (4)” substitute “(5)(b) or (7)”;
- (c) after paragraph (9) insert—

  > (10) For the purposes of making contributions to the Scheme under regulations 3.C.1 and 3.C.3, during any period of absence which counts as pensionable service under regulation 3.A.4(3) or (3A), amounts equal to the rate of the member’s pensionable earnings calculated as described in paragraph (3)(a) or (b) of this regulation will be treated as pensionable earnings.
