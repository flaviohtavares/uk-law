# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Amendment of regulation 2.D.10

In regulation 2.D.10 (early retirement on ill-health (deferred members))—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), before “incapable” insert “permanently”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a)(ii), for “impairment” substitute “infirmity”.
