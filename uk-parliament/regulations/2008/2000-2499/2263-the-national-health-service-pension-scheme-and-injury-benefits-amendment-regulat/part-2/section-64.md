# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Amendment of regulation 3.C.3

In regulation 3.C.3 (contributions by employing authorities: general), for paragraph (2) substitute—

> (2) In specifying such a rate, the Secretary of State must—
>
> - (a) obtain the consent of the Treasury, and
> - (b) take account of the advice of the Scheme actuary and the cost of providing for any increase in pensions under the Scheme as a result of orders made under the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00023] and section 59 of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975[^f00024].

.
