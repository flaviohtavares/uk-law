# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Amendment of regulation 2.B.2

In regulation 2.B.2 (restrictions on eligibility: general)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for “parital” substitute “partial”;
- (b) for paragraph (2)(b) substitute—

  > - (b) became a pensioner member of that Scheme on or after that date, or
  > - (c) is a deferred member of that Scheme, but is not a deferred member—
  >   - (i) to whom regulation 2.B.1(5)(e) or (f) applies, and
  >   - (ii) in respect of whom permission of the Secretary of State to rejoin the NHS Pension Scheme 1995 has not been granted pursuant to regulation B2(3) of that Scheme.
