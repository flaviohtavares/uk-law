# The National Health Service Pension Scheme and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2008, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Amendment of regulation 3.F.8

In regulation 3.F.8 (right to apply for acceptance of transfer value payment from another scheme)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a), after “scheme” insert “other than a corresponding health service scheme”;
- (b) for paragraph (2)(d) substitute—

  > - (d) a corresponding 1995 scheme, and
  > - (e) a corresponding 2008 scheme.

  ;
- (c) after paragraph (2) insert—

  > (2A) a member who makes an application for a transfer value to be accepted by the Secretary of State in respect of his rights under a corresponding 2008 scheme may not also make an application for a transfer value to be accepted in respect of his rights under a corresponding 1995 scheme.

  ;
- (d) in paragraph (4)(a)(ii), for “purposes of that Act” substitute “purposes of the 2004 Act”.
