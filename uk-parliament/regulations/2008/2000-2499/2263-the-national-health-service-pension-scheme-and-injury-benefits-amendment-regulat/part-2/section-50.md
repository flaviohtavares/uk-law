## 50. Amendment of regulation 2.J.7

In regulation 2.J.7(5)(c) (forfeiture of rights to benefits)—

- (a) after “2.E.21(3)” insert “or (10)”;
- (b) for “lup sum” substitute “lump sum or pension”.
