# The Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] (“the 1972 Act”) and by sections 85(1)(a) and (b) and (3) and 86(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00002] (“the 1995 Act”).

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the 1972 Act in relation to measures relating to the safety of ships and the heath and safety of persons on them.

In accordance with section 86(4) of the 1995 Act the Secretary of State has consulted the persons referred to in that section.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 8th September 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment) Regulations 2006

In regulation 11(2) of the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment) Regulations 2006[^f00004], after “lifting equipment” insert “(other than an accessory for lifting)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment) Regulations 2006 (“the 2006 Regulations”). The 2006 Regulations, including the provisions amended by these Regulations, implement the United Kingdom’s obligations under Council Directive [89/655/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0655) (OJ L393, 30.12.89, p 13) as amended by Council Directive [95/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0063) (OJ L335, 30.12.95, p 28) concerning the minimum safety and health requirements for the use of work equipment by workers at work (second individual Directive within the meaning of Article 16(1) of Directive [89/391/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0391)). They provide that the requirement in regulation 11(2), that the ship’s lifting equipment is not used unless it has been suitably tested by a competent person within the preceding five years, does not apply to accessories for lifting as defined in regulation 2(1) of the 2006 Regulations.

The Regulations are made under the powers in the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 except in respect of their application to Government ships where the power is provided by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced as the purpose of this instrument is to bring the impact of the 2006 Regulations into line with that anticipated on the making of those Regulations, for which a regulatory impact assessment was produced.
