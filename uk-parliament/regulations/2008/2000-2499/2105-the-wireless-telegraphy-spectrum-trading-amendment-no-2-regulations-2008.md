# The Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2105

Electronic Communications

The Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made

5th August 2008

Coming into force

29th August 2008

The Office of Communications (“OFCOM”) make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(1) and (3) and section 122(7) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006[^f00001] (“the Act”).

Before making the Regulations OFCOM have given notice of their proposal to do so in accordance with section 122(4)(a) of the Act, published notice of their proposal in accordance with section 122(4)(b) of the Act and have considered the representations made to them before the time specified in the notice in accordance with section 122(4)(c) of the Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 29th August 2008**.**

## 2. Amendment of the Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) Regulations 2004

The Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) Regulations 2004[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with these Regulations.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 4

In regulation 4(3) after “Parts 1 to 6 of the Schedule” insert “and Parts 8 and 9 of the Schedule”.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 6

In regulation 6(a)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (v) delete “or”;
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (vi) delete “and”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (vi) add two new sub-paragraphs as follows—

  > - (vii) part frequency channels which have in each case a bandwidth of 6.25 kHz or a multiple thereof and the same start or end frequency limit as a licensed frequency channel under one of the licences of a class specified in Column 1 of Part 8 of the Schedule which apply to a station or apparatus operating within any of the frequency bands specified in Column 2 of the same Part; or
  > - (viii) a geographical area being a fifty kilometre by fifty kilometre square part of the total geographical area in which the holder is authorised to establish, install and use radio transmitting and receiving stations or apparatus under one of the licences specified in Column 1 of Part 8 of the Schedule which apply to a station or apparatus operating within any of the frequency bands specified in Column 2 of the same Part; and

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Wireless Telegraphy (Spectrum Trading) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3154](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3154) as amended by [S.I. 2006/339](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/339), [S.I. 2006/1807](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1807), [S.I. 2007/380](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/380), [S.I. 2007/3387](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3387) and [S.I. 2008/688](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/688)) (the “principal Regulations”)

These Regulations amend the principal Regulations to authorise the transfer of certain rights and obligations arising by virtue of five new classes of wireless telegraphy licence. These new classes are the Business Radio (Simple UK) class, the Business Radio (Suppliers Light) class, the Business Radio (Simple Site) class, the Business Radio (Area Defined) class and the Business Radio (Technically Assigned) class.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that these Regulations will have on the costs to business is available to the public from OFCOM at Riverside House, 2a Southwark Bridge Road, London SE1 9HA (Tel: 0207 7981 3000) or on the OFCOM internet website at www.ofcom.org.uk. Copies of the report have also been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
