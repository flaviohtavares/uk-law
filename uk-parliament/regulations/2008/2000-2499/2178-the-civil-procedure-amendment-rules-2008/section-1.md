# The Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2008, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2008.

(2) Except as provided in paragraphs (3) and (4), these Rules come into force on 1st October 2008.

(3) Rules 6(a), 7 and 38 of these Rules and Schedule 2 to these Rules come into force—

- (a) in so far as they relate to European orders for payment made under Regulation [(EC) No 1896/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1896) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 December 2006 creating a European order for payment procedure[^f00002], on 12th December 2008; and
- (b) in so far as they relate to the European small claims procedure under Regulation [(EC) No 861/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0861) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 July 2007 establishing a European small claims procedure[^f00003], on 1st January 2009.

(4) Rules 37(b), (c) and (d) of these Rules come into force on 12th December 2008.

**2.** In these Rules—

- (a) a reference to a Part or rule by number alone means the Part or rule so numbered in the Civil Procedure Rules 1998[^f00004];
- (b) a reference to an Order by number and prefixed “**RSC**” means the RSC Order so numbered in Schedule 1 to those Rules; and
- (c) a reference to an Order by number and prefixed “**CCR**” means the CCR Order so numbered in Schedule 2 to those Rules.
