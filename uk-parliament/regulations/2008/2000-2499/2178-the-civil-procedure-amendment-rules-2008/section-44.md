# The Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2008, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Transitional Provision

The amendments made by rule 26 of these Rules to rule 52.3 do not apply to proceedings where the notice of appeal was filed before 1st October 2008 and rule 52.3 in force immediately before that date will continue to apply to those proceedings as if that rule had not been amended.
