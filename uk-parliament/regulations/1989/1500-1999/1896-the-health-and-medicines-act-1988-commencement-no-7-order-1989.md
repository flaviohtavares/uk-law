# The Health and Medicines Act 1988 (Commencement No.7) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1896 (C.60)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Health and Medicines Act 1988 (Commencement No.7) Order 1989

Made

14th October 1989

In exercise of powers conferred by section 26(1) of the Health and Medicines Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Medicines Act 1988 (Commencement No.7) Order 1989.

## 2. Appointed day

15th October is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Health and Medicines Act 1988:—

- (a) in section 8 (retirement of practitioners)—
  - (i) paragraph (a) in subsection (1) except for the words “or section 19 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978”[^f00002];
  - (ii) the words “(a) or” in subsection (2) except so far as they have effect for the purposes of any list maintained under section 19 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978;
- (b) section 25(1) (consequential amendments) so far as it relates to paragraph 3 of Schedule 2, and to paragraph 1 of that Schedule to the extent that it has effect for the purposes of that paragraph.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

14th October 1989

This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in England and Wales on 15th October 1989 the provisions of the Health and Medicines Act 1988 relating to the retirement of medical practitioners.
