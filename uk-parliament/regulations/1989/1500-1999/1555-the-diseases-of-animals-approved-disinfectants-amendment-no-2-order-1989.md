# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1555

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989

Made

29th August 1989

Coming into force

31st August 1989

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b) and (c) and (2), 23(f) and (g) and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby order as follows:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 31st August 1989.Amendment

**2.** At the end of Schedule 1 to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be added the entries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 29th August 1989.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food
