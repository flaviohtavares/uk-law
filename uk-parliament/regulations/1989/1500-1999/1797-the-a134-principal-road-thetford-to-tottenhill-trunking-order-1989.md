# The A134 Principal Road (Thetford to Tottenhill Trunking) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1797

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A134 Principal Road (Thetford to Tottenhill Trunking) Order 1989

Made

29th September 1989

Coming into force

1st November 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The length of highway described in the schedule to this Order and indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** In this Order“the deposited plan” means a plan numbered HA10/2E/307 marked“The A134 Principal Road (Thetford to Tottenhill Trunking) Order 1989” signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.

This Order shall come into force on 1st November 1989 and may be cited as the A134 Principal Road (Thetford to Tottenhill Trunking) Order 1989.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Alan Whitfield

Regional Director Eastern Region

Department of Transport

29th September 1989

## SCHEDULE — EXISTING HIGHWAY TO BECOME A TRUNK ROAD

The length of existing highway to become a trunk road is the A134 Principal Road from (and including) a point 1,750 metres north as measured along the route of the highway from its junction with Croxton Road, Thetford in the County of Norfolk, northwards to its junction with the A10 Trunk Road, at Tottenhill in the County of Norfolk (a distance of approximately 38 kilometres, measured along the route of that length of highway).
