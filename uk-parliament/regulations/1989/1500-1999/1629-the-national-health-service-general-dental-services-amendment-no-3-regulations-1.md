# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment (No. 3) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1629

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment (No. 3) Regulations 1989

Made

7th September 1989

Laid before Parliament

8th September 1989

Coming into force

1st October 1989

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 35(1), 36(1), 126(1) and 128(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment (No. 3) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st October 1989.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

In regulation 2(1) of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1973[^f00002] (interpretation) in the definition“dental officer” there are substituted for the words“Department of Health and Social Security” the words“Board[^f00003], of the Department of Health”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

David Mellor

Minister of State,

Department of Health

7th September 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1973 which provide for the arrangements under which dentists provide dental services under the National Health Service in England and Wales.

Regulation 2 amends the definition of“dental officer” to include a dental officer employed by the Dental Practice Board.
