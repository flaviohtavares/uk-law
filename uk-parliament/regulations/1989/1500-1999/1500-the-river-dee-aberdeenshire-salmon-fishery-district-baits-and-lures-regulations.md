# The River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1500 (S.127)

RIVER, SCOTLAND

SALMON AND FRESHWATER FISHERIES

The River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1989

Made

15th August 1989

Coming into force

1st November 1989

The Secretary of State, on application to him by the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) District Salmon Fishery Board, having carried out all necessary consultations and complied with all necessary procedures as required by paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 of Schedule 1 to the Salmon Act 1986( [^f00001]) as applied by section 8(5) of that Act (there being no objections or representations duly made with respect to the proposals in the application), in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 8 of that Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st November 1989.

## 2. Specification of baits and lures and area of application

Natural prawns and shrimps (whether fresh or preserved, dyed or natural colour) and any part thereof are, in relation to the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) salmon fishery district, specified for the purposes of the definition of “rod and line” in section 24(1) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Protection) (Scotland) Act 1951( [^f00002]).

L. V. McEwan

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Department of Agriculture and Fisheries for Scotland

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

15th August 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify natural prawns and shrimps and any part of them for the purposes of the definition of “rod and line” in section 24(1) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Protection) (Scotland) Act 1951. They apply throughout the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) salmon fishery district for the whole of each year. The effect is to prohibit the use of natural prawns and shrimps (or any part of them) as bait when fishing by rod and line for salmon and sea trout.
