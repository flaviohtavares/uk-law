# The Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973 (Continuation) (Jersey) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1998

CONTROL OF FUEL AND ELECTRICITY

The Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973 (Continuation) (Jersey) Order 1989

Made

1st November 1989

Coming into force

30th November 1989

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 1st day of November 1989

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 10(3) of the Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973 (Continuation) (Jersey) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 30th November 1989.

**2.** Sections 1 to 8 of the Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973, as it has effect in the Bailiwick of Jersey, shall continue in force for a period of one year beyond 30th November 1989, being the date on which they would otherwise expire.

G.I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues provisions of the Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973 in force in the Bailiwick of Jersey for one year from 30th November 1989 (being the date to which those provisions were continued in force by the Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973 (Continuation) (Jersey) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/1848](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1848))).
