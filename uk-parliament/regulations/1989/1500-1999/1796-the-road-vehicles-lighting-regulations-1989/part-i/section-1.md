# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, section 1

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Commencement, citation and revocations[^key-57ae2acaa8234156df5b2a5c915363c4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st November 1989.

(2) The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1984[^f00002] and the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment) Regulations 1987[^f00003] are hereby revoked.
