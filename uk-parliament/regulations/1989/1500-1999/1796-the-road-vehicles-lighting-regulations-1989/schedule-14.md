# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, SCHEDULE 14

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 14

*Extent: E+W+S*

### PART I — Requirements relating to Obligatory Reversing Lamps[^key-4c68c5e2452f2c00a43cd436312f471d]

Regulations 18, 20

Obligatory reversing lamps must comply with the requirements of paragraph 6.4 of ECE Regulation 48.

### PART II — requirements relating to optional reversing lamps

(Regulation 20)

| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.**[^M_I_b0131e5a-6728-4c28-84be-c9de58f7797e] Number: | Not more than two but if the vehicle has an overall length which exceeds 6 m and is—[^key-82965e9176b8c24aa8d05a4cd13e3847]<br>- (a) a bus; or<br>- (b) a vehicle which is not a passenger vehicle,<br>four may be fitted |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.**[^M_I_cdaba9b3-69b3-4382-b6a5-5be868347ad3] Position: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.**[^M_I_7b81cc23-4ea7-4fea-efb3-60bb1288bf17] Angles of visibility: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.**[^M_I_09006cd6-3b36-4a9d-99b6-6e4f22809b7b] Alignment: | To the rear but if, in accordance with paragraph 1, four are fitted the configuration shall be—[^key-ebbf542f4015cca23fc7e6428d6b5787]<br>- (a) four to the rear; or<br>- (b) two to the rear and one on each side |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.**[^M_I_61d6c875-983e-4a9c-a6ee-d2c8406ae18b] Markings–<br>(a) A motor vehicle first used on or after 1st April 1986 and a trailer manufactured on or after 1st October 1985: | An approval mark |
| (b) A motor vehicle first used before 1st April 1986 and a trailer manufactured before 1st October 1985: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.**[^M_I_cc5be23b-9e05-44c5-9d39-a135611119a0] Size of illuminated area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.**[^M_I_a7f12eb2-97c0-4b71-bffa-55a9e417ecd7] Colour: | White |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**8.**[^M_I_4b7ab18f-771d-406a-9b50-602ccd0fa650] Wattage–<br>(a) A reversing lamp bearing an approval mark: | No requirement |
| (b) A reversing lamp not bearing an approval mark: | The total wattage of any one reversing lamp shall not exceed 24 watts |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.**[^M_I_d74a3d02-370d-4949-c286-813dbe8bc888] Intensity: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**10.**[^M_I_f37fcd9d-cfe2-4298-9e25-42561d13f880] Electrical connections: | No requirement except that if, in accordance with paragraph 4, reversing lamps have been fitted to the side, the following requirements shall apply in relation to those lamps—[^key-9ed30aa362bdb8bdbb6e63487715b308]<br>- (a) it shall not be possible for the reversing lamps to be illuminated unless the front and rear position lamps of the vehicle are illuminated at the same time; and<br>- (b) the reversing lamps shall be switched on and off by the manual operation of one switch which shall have no other function; but<br>- (c) the reversing lamps shall switch off automatically if the vehicle is moving forward at a speed of 10km/h or more |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**11.**[^M_I_707b4584-6fcd-4867-c3ca-bf97ab026498] Tell-tale–<br>(a) A motor vehicle first used on or after 1st July 1954, provided that the electrical connections are such that any[^key-a84a60084c5a4424b46ad92df7692e46] reversing lamp or lamps fitted to the rear[^key-20ec1cdb1da922e103cf048701631584] cannot be illuminated other than automatically by the selection of the reverse gear of the vehicle: | No requirement |
| (b) Any other motor vehicle first used on or after 1st July 1954: | A circuit-closed tell-tale shall be fitted |
| (c) A motor vehicle first used before 1st July 1954: | No requirement |
| (d) Any vehicle which is not a motor vehicle: | No requirement |

**12.**[^key-b424f352d23099800b0b54128ba28919] Definitions–

- In this Schedule “approval mark” means either–
  - (a) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 5 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 21 of Schedule 4 to those Regulations; or
  - (b) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 4 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 23 or 23A of Schedule 2 to those Regulations.
