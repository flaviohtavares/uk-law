# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, SCHEDULE 9

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 9

*Extent: E+W+S*

(Regulations 18, 20 and 22)

### PART I — Requirements relating to obligatory side marker lamps and to optional side marker lamps to the extent specified in part ii

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.**[^M_I_7bc78f84-5a7d-4eda-eaf0-12916910e8fe] Number–<br>(a) A vehicle not covered by sub-paragraph (b) which is–<br>- (i) a motor vehicle first used on or after 1st April 1991 or a trailer manufactured on or after 1st October 1990: | On each side: two and as many more as are sufficient to satisfy the requirements of paragraph 2(a) |
| - (ii) a trailer manufactured before 1st October 1990: | One on each side |
| (b) Long vehicles and vehicle combinations to which regulation 22 applies: | The numbers required by regulation 22 |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.**[^M_I_43874048-01cd-4f38-d2aa-d3458239dc92] Position–<br>(a) Longitudinal–<br>- (i) A vehicle of a type mentioned in sub-paragraph 1(a)(i)–<br>  - (A) Maximum distance from the front of the vehicle, including any drawbar, in respect of the foremost side marker lamp on each side: | 4 m |
| - (B) Maximum distance from the rear of the vehicle in respect of the rearmost side marker lamp on each side: | 1 m |
| - (C) Maximum separation distance between the light-emitting surfaces of adjacent side marker lamps on the same side of the vehicle: | 3 m or, if this is not practicable, 4 m |
| - (ii) A vehicle of a type mentioned in sub-paragraph 1(a)(ii): | Such that no part of the light-emitting surface is forward of, or more than 1530 mm to the rear of, the centre point of the overall length of the trailer |
| - (iii) Long vehicles and vehicle combinations, including any loads, to which regulation 22 applies: | As specified in regulation 22 |
| (b) Lateral: | No requirement |
| (c) Vertical–<br>- (i) Maximum height above the ground: | 2300 mm |
| - (ii) Minimum height above the ground: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.**[^M_I_51b023f2-24df-4b23-81ed-f88fd72562e6] Angles of visibility–<br>(a) Horizontal: | 45° to the left and to the right when viewed in a direction at right angles to the longitudinal axis of the vehicle |
| (b) Vertical: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.**[^M_I_a9f8d92e-c8e2-415c-e6ad-ad917b730305] Alignment: | To the side |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.**[^M_I_81778ae8-b998-4ca7-b254-841f7229bd0e] Markings: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.**[^M_I_a62fb048-3700-4225-92ce-2deffe60b967] Size of illuminated area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.**[^M_I_2fd1ea93-16c6-4d27-8e46-a1cafb71aa6f] Colour: | Amber<br>or, if within 1 m of the rear of the vehicle it may be red<br>or, if the vehicle is a trailer manufactured before 1st October 1990, it may be white when viewed from the front and red when viewed from the rear |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**8.**[^M_I_bc31ee3f-8e22-44f7-8526-3b12295182f4] Wattage: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.**[^M_I_1702810d-5848-4a45-9e0f-84d2eeb45bb7] Intensity: | Visible from a reasonable distance |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**10.**[^M_I_45e2ce90-64f0-4779-8051-2f53278ecacb] Electrical connections: | No individual requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**11.**[^M_I_03babdb1-070c-40e9-d051-d82354a91be7] Tell-tale: | No requirement |

### PART II — Requirements relating to optional side marker lamps[^c-e10a8aea7bee6f1775d19460689d243a]

Any number may be fitted and the only requirement prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted is that specified in paragraph 7 of Part I.
