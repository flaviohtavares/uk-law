# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, section 18

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Obligatory lamps, reflectors, rear markings and devices[^c-bb7156e6122f031b277e7c5f18d2a8d2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Save as provided in the foregoing provisions of these Regulations , in regulation 20A(9)[^c-f643f05b8f704833e37799d866edae90] and in paragraphs (2),[^c-a4dded3adec07820e6b21b2bce7febcc] (2ZA),[^c-3b07e6a53defaf125c478653b948e0b0] (2A) and (2B), every vehicle of a class specified in a Table in Schedule 1 shall be fitted with lamps, reflectors, rear markings and devices which–

- (a) are of a type specified in column 1 of that Table, and
- (b) comply with the relevant installation, alignment and performance requirements set out in the Schedule or Part of a Schedule shown against that type in column 2 of that Table.

(2) The requirements specified in paragraph (1) do not apply in respect of a lamp, reflector, rear marking or device of a type specified in column 1 of a Table in the case of a vehicle shown against it in column 3 of that Table.

(2ZA)[^c-145c39e20c7232ccba7aa37846b9ca46] The requirements specified in paragraph (1) do not apply in the case of a headlamp cleaning device fitted to a lamp, other than a gas discharge headlamp or an adaptive front lighting system.

(2A)[^c-26f72e794b283015a097dc5ca016cebd] The requirements specified in paragraph 5(c) and (ca) of Schedule 2 shall not apply in the case of a front position lamp capable of emitting a flashing light which is fitted to—

- (i) a pedal cycle; or
- (ii) a trailer drawn by, or a sidecar attached to, a pedal cycle,

unless the lamp is also capable of emitting a steady light.

(2B) The requirements specified in paragraph 5(d) and (e) of Schedule 10 shall not apply in the case of a rear position lamp capable of emitting a flashing light which is fitted to—

- (i) a pedal cycle; or
- (ii) a trailer drawn by, or a sidecar attached to, a pedal cycle,

unless the lamp is also capable of emitting a steady light.

(3) The requirements specified in paragraph (1) apply without prejudice to any additional requirements specified in regulations 20 , 20A[^c-b1fcd889febd0a473225beb9b66ea041] and 21.

(4) The Schedules referred to in the Tables in Schedule 1 are Schedules 2 to 21 , and 24[^c-894764594342dc74643c5078f13ae61d].
