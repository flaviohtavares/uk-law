# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, SCHEDULE 21

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 21

*Extent: E+W+S*

(Regulations 18 and 20)

### PART I — Requirements relating to obligatory front retroreflectors and to optional front retro reflectors to the extent specified in part ii

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.**[^M_I_c0d8aef4-7c31-4c45-a48e-32f61a6730ae] Number: | Two |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.**[^M_I_5cc1c973-0a8c-4555-a403-4d1e389f647f] Position–<br>(a) Longitudinal: | No requirement |
| (b) Lateral–<br>- (i) Maximum distance from the side of the trailer: | 150 mm |
| - (ii) Minimum separation distance between a pair of front reflectors: | 600 mm or, if the overall width of the trailer is less than 1400 mm, 400 mm |
| (c) Vertical–<br>- (i) Maximum height above the ground: | 900 mm or, if the structure of the trailer makes this impracticable, 1500 mm |
| - (ii) Minimum height above the ground: | 250 mm[^key-5056a6cc43e4c8c4f4c14d32cf35f5f9] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.**[^M_I_dff9b78b-4110-486e-e575-f584421c625a] Angles of visibility–<br>(a) Horizontal: | 30° outwards and 5° inwards |
| (b) Vertical–<br>- (i) Any case not covered by sub-paragraph (ii): | 10°[^key-421723bca031868153fdcdbcae68351b] above and below the horizontal |
| - (ii) Where the highest point of the reflecting area is less than 750 mm above the ground: | 10° above and 5° below the horizontal |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.**[^M_I_dae8b230-0611-4fb8-b8e4-50b421d04d1d] Alignment: | To the front |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.**[^M_I_f896c9ab-7912-4601-8ab0-f7486140dd23] Markings: | An approval mark |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.**[^M_I_869e54c5-6235-4cfd-fd04-39de794162ab] Size of reflecting area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.**[^M_I_f942b334-9afb-43b4-ddf9-5249ca8625e4] Colour: | White |

**8.**[^key-35f2ccc1cdde7d48719ea7e677b9a479] Other requirements–

(a) Where two front reflectors are required to be fitted they shall form a pair.

(b) Triangular shaped retro reflectors shall not be fitted to the front of any trailer.

**9.**[^key-57bad70a082a37487850c156b39779bc] Definitions–

- In this Schedule– “approval mark” means either–
  - (a) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 4 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 3 or 3A or 3B of Schedule 2 to those Regulations; or
  - (b) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 5 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulation and shown at item 4 of Schedule 4 to those Regulations

### PART II — Requirements relating to optional front retro reflectors[^key-e432859b83979ab959de2d8640990d4f]

Any number may be fitted and the only requirements prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted are that specified in paragraph 8(b) of Part I and that the colour shall not be red.
