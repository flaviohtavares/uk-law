## SCHEDULE 6 — requirements relating to optional front fog lamps

*Extent: E+W+S*

(Regulation 20)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.**[^M_I_a9d8a195-662a-4583-95cf-dd8b0c09fd36] Number–<br>(a) Any vehicle not covered by sub-paragraph (b): | No requirement |
| (b) A motor vehicle, other than a motor bicycle or motor bicycle combination, first used on or after 1st April 1991: | Not more than two |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.**[^M_I_5b32f9b6-8140-4891-de99-59b9c1b3c2b7] Position–<br>(a) Longitudinal: | No requirement |
| (b) Lateral–<br>- (i) Where a pair of front fog lamps is used in conditions of seriously reduced visibility in place of the obligatory dipped beam headlamps–<br>  - Maximum distance from side of vehicle: | 400 mm |
| - (ii) in all other cases: | No requirement |
| (c) Vertical–<br>- (i) Maximum height above the ground–<br>  - (A) Any vehicle not covered by sub-paragraph (B): | 1200 mm |
| - (B) An agricultural vehicle, a road clearance vehicle, an aerodrome fire tender, an aerodrome runway sweeper, an industrial tractor, engineering plant and a home forces' vehicle: | No requirement |
| - (ii) Minimum height above the ground: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.**[^M_I_cfc86974-42af-4bdd-9b8b-969a1f26f5d0] Angles of visibility: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.**[^M_I_617a0f75-5546-4b83-b445-daa1a31a8327] Alignment: | To the front and so aimed that the upper edge of the beam is, as near as practicable, 3 per cent below the horizontal when the vehicle is at its kerbside weight and has a weight of 75 kg on the driver’s seat |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.**[^M_I_df218a2e-aaaa-4a8b-b2f4-4664752fedb5] Markings–<br>(a) A vehicle first used on or after 1st April 1986: | An approval mark |
| (b) A vehicle first used before 1st April 1986: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.**[^M_I_1a6a71e9-965c-4878-df19-f1940c460d28] Size of illuminated area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.**[^M_I_158a9ff0-aeff-4c7a-f902-e4321f81116b] Colour: | White or yellow |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**8.**[^M_I_3df27efa-bdaf-4413-e827-092490bf48cd] Wattage: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.**[^M_I_94d47d63-974d-4a99-8b35-d8f785ac4d4f] Intensity: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**10.**[^M_I_5ccfa8b4-aca6-4c2d-dacd-7df23c53836f] Electrical connections: | No individual requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**11.**[^M_I_c91f8286-aebd-49de-e37c-bfad3e4f702d] Tell-tale: | No requirement |

**12.**[^key-32d980f889948fd71b1e8fc85854d4f1] Other requirements–

- Every front fog lamp shall be so constructed that the direction of the beam of light emitted therefrom can be adjusted whilst the vehicle is stationary.

**13.**[^key-6fcecbc840e362292e2366b8dcafff99] Definitions–

- In this Schedule “appproval mark” means either–
  - (a) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 5 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 19 of Schedule 4 to those Regulations; or
  - (b) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 4 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 19 or 19A of Schedule 2 of those Regulations.
