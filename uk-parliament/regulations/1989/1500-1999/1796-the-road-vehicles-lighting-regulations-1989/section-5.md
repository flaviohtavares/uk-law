# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, section 5

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**5.** The definition of “Community Directive [76/756/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0756), as amended” in regulation 3 sets out details of this Directive and the amendments. Copies of the Directives can be obtained from HM Stationery Office.
