# The Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989, SCHEDULE 20

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 20

*Extent: E+W+S*

(Regulation 18 and 20)

### PART I — Requirements relating to obligatory pedal retro reflectors and optional pedal retro reflectors to the extent specified in part ii

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.**[^M_I_e1e4d7f8-aa57-46a6-e5b7-c9bd3c97ebce] Number: | Two reflectors on each pedal |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.**[^M_I_2744a814-d6b8-4c10-db9f-1a0b9725fb94] Position–<br>(a) Longitudinal: | On the leading edge and the trailing edge of each pedal |
| (b) Lateral: | No requirement |
| (c) Vertical: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.**[^M_I_2e302a99-04d4-4b84-e15a-598c5b114868] Angles of visibility: | Such that the reflector on the leading edge of each pedal is plainly visible to the front and the reflector on the trailing edge of each pedal is plainly visible to the rear |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.**[^M_I_02e9c52b-6773-4600-a0f9-3a7f947eca54] Markings: | A British Standard mark |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.**[^M_I_bebfc262-ca69-4f84-ed12-f4e713a70203] Size of reflecting area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.**[^M_I_8d7fd0bb-dc09-4292-90bb-643e42898c88] Colour: | Amber |

**7.**[^c-f79e8e92637ddf53a8d0d04c30951ea5] Definitions–

- In this Schedule “British Standard mark” means the specification for photometric and physical requirements of reflective devices published by the British Standards Institution under the reference BS 6102: Part 2: 1982, namely “BS 6102/2”.

### PART II — Requirements relating to optional pedal retro reflectors[^c-8eed8e36ba6f51dcdf280b7d7c62d846]

Any number may be fitted and the only requirement prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted is that specified in paragraph 6 of Part I.
