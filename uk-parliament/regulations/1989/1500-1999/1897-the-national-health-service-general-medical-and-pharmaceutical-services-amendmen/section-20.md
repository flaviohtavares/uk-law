# The National Health Service (General Medical and Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1989, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Insertion of regulations 33A and 33B in the principal Regulations

After regulation 33 of the principal Regulations there shall be inserted the following new regulations:–

> **33A. Appointment of medical adviser**
>
> The Committee shall appoint a doctor to assist it in the exercise of its functions pursuant to paragraph 39A of the terms of service for doctors (inquiries about prescriptions and referrals).
>
> **33B. Guidance to doctors**
>
> A Committee may issue guidance to doctors whose names are included in its medical list to assist them in assessing in accordance with paragraph 24A of the terms of service the qualifications, experience and competence of any employee, or prospective employee, provided that such guidance has regard as appropriate to standards adopted by any appropriate national regulatory body for a profession or occupation or similar body.
