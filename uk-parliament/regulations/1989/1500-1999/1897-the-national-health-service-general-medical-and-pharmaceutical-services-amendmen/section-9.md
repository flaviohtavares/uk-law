# The National Health Service (General Medical and Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1989, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Insertion of regulation 6A in the principal Regulations

After regulation 6 of the principal Regulations there shall be inserted the following regulation:–

> **6A. Amendment of the Local Directory**
>
> (1) A doctor shall, unless it is impractical for him to do so, notify the Committee within 28 days of any occurrence requiring a change in the information recorded about him in the Local Directory.
>
> (2) The Committee shall, in the event of a notification pursuant to paragraph (1), make any necessary amendment to the Local Directory.
