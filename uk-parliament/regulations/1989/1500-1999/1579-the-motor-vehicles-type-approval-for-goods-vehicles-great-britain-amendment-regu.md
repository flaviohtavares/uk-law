# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1579

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

31st August 1989

Laid before Parliament

8th September 1989

Coming into force

1st October 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54(1) and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on1st October 1989.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendments relating to exhaust emissions

At the end of regulation 4 there shall be added the following paragraph:—

> (14) Schedule 1B shall have effect for the purpose of specifying, in relation to certain vehicles, dates which are to be read as if they appeared in columns (5) and (6) of item 2B, and column (5) of items 2C and 2D in Schedule 1.

.

**4.** For items 2, 2A and 2B in Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the items numbered 2, 2A, 2B, 2C and 2D as set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

**5.** A Schedule shall be inserted after Schedule 1A in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## 6. Supplementary provisions

In Schedule 1, in column (2) of items 3, 3A and 3B, for the words “Exhaust emissions” there shall be substituted the words “Smoke emissions”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Transport

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982.

Previously only vehicles with petrol engines were subject to type approval requirements relating to the content of exhaust emissions (as opposed to the density of smoke). These Regulations substitute five new items in Schedule 1 of the 1982 Regulations for four previous items. The new items re-enact the existing requirements for vehicles with petrol engines. They also introduce new alternative requirements for vehicles with petrol engines and new requirements for vehicles with diesel engines. The Table below sets out the derivation of the new items in so far as they re-enact previous items and the instruments referred to in the items. It also identifies the requirements that are new.

| *Instrument referred to in item* | *Previous Schedule Item* | *New Schedule Item* | *Extent that requirements are new* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) as last amended by [78/665/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0665), or ECE Regulation 15.03. | 2 | 2 | — |
| The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986. | 2A | 2A | — |
| Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) as last amended by [83/351/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0351); and ECE Regulation 15.04. | 2 | 2B | New so far as the requirement applies to vehicles with diesel engines. |
| Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) as last amended by [88/76/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0076). | 2B | 2C | New so far as the requirement applies to vehicles with diesel engines. |
| Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077). | — | 2D | New for vehicles with diesel engines and does not apply to vehicles with petrol engines. |

The application dates for the new alternative requirements, in so far as they are not set out in the items, are determined in accordance with the new Schedule 1B which is added by regulation 5. The dates of cessation of the existing requirements and some of the new requirements, in so far as they are not set out in the items, are also determined in accordance with that Schedule.

Regulation 6 changes the name of the subject matter of items 3, 3A and 3B inSchedule 1 from “exhaust emission” to “smoke emissions”. These items relate only to vehicles with diesel engines and the change is made to avoid confusion with the exhaust emission requirements set out in items 2, 2A, 2B, 2C and 2D.

Copies of the EEC Directives and ECE Regulations (and amendments) referred to in these Regulations can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
