# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 20) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1563

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 20) Order 1989

Made

16th August 1989

Coming into force

11th September 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:-

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 20) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 11th September 1989.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, Texaco North Sea UK Company, whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is situated at 1 Knightsbridge Green, London SW1X 7QJ, is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-line described in the Schedule hereto.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State

Department of Energy

16th August 1989
