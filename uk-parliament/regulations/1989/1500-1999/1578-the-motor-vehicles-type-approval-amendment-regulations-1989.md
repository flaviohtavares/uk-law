# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1578

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

1st September 1989

Laid before Parliament

8th September 1989

Coming into force

1st October 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st October 1989.

**2.** (1) Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980[^f00003] is hereby amended as follows.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980 so that

- (a) the type approval requirements relating to the installation of lighting and light signalling devices on motor vehicles and their trailers are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [76/756/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0756) as amended by Commission Directives [80/233/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0233) and [82/244/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0244), Council Directive [83/276/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0276), and Commission Directives [84/8/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0008) as corrected and [89/278/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0278), as corrected;
- (b) the type approval requirements, relating to direction indicator lamps for motor vehicles and their trailers are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [76/759/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0759) as amended by Commission Directive [89/277/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0277), as corrected; and
- (c) the type approval requirements relating to the lateral protection (side guards) of certain motor vehicles and their trailers are the requirements of Council Directive [89/297/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0297).

**2.** Copies of the Council Directives and Commission Directives referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
