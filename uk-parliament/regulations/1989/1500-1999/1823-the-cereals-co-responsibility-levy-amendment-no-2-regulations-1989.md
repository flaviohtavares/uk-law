# The Cereals Co-responsibility Levy (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1823

AGRICULTURE

The Cereals Co-responsibility Levy (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989

Made

5th October 1989

Laid before Parliament

9th October 1989

Coming into force

30th October 1989

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Economic Community, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Cereals Co-responsibility Levy (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 30th October 1989.

## 2. Amendment of the Cereals Co-responsibility Levy Regulations 1988

The Cereals Co-responsibility Levy Regulations 1988[^f00003] are hereby further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

**3.** In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) for the definition of “the Commission Regulation” there shall be substituted the following definition— ““the Commission Regulation” means Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 1432/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/1432) laying down detailed rules for applying the co-responsibility levy in the cereals sector[^f00004], as amended by Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2324/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/2324)[^f00005], Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 3858/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/3858)[^f00006] and Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2712/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/2712)[^f00007];”.

**4.** For regulation 10 (recovery of cereals co-responsibility levy) there shall be substituted the following regulation—

> **10.** (1) Interest payable in accordance with Article 4(4) of the Commission Regulation in respect of any delay in a payment of cereals co-responsibility levy shall be calculated at the rate of one percentage point above the sterling three month London interbank offered rate on—
>
> - (a) the day on which that payment became due; or
> - (b) where that day falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the next working day.
>
> (2) Cereals co-responsibility levy and any interest thereon due from any person shall be recoverable by the Board as a civil debt.

.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 4th October 1989.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Sanderson of Bowden

Minister of State, Scottish Office

5th October 1989

This note is not part of the Regulations)

- (a) amend the definition of “the Commission Regulation” so as to refer to its amendment by Commission Regulation 2712/89 (regulation 3);
- (b) amend regulation 10 so as to specify a rate of interest for the United Kingdom and to provide for its recovery by the Intervention Board for Agricultural Produce (regulation 4).
