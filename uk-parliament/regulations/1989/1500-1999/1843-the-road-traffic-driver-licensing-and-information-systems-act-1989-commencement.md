# The Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the S.I. of the same number issued on 13th October and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1989 No. 1843 (C.58)

ROAD TRAFFIC

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989

Made

6th October 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(2) of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989[^f00001] (“the Act”), and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989.

**2.** The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 8th November 1989.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

6th October 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th November 1989 the provisions of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 specified in the Schedule to the Order. Such provisions have particular relevance to the driving of public service vehicles by persons who are not licensed to drive such vehicles under section 22 of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act [1981 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/14) but who have made an application for the grant of a licence and who have passed the appropriate test of competence to drive.
