# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

10th October 1989

Laid before Parliament

18th October 1989

Coming into force

8th November 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1), (2) and (5) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], as read with section 43 of that Act, and all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and introduction

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 8th November 1989.

**2.** (1) The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986[^f00002]shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

(2) The Secretary of State is satisfied that—

- (a) it is requisite that the provisions of regulations 4 to 9 shall apply as from8th November 1989 to vehicles registered under the Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971[^f00003]before the expiration of one year from the making of these Regulations; and
- (b) notwithstanding that the said provisions will then apply to those vehicles, no undue hardship or inconvenience will be caused thereby.

## 3. Amendments relating to Interpretation

In the Table in paragraph (2) of regulation 3—

- (a) the meaning specified in column 2 for the expression “Community Directive, followed by a number” shall be amended by the substitution for the words “the reference to it means” of the words “the reference to the amending Directive includes a reference to”; and
- (b) the meaning specified in column 2 for the expression “the Community Recording Equipment Regulation” shall be substituted by the following—

  > Council Regulation [(EEC) 3821/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1985/3821) of 20th December 1985 on recording equipment in road transport[^f00004], as read with the Community Drivers' Hours and Recording Equipment (Exemptions and Supplementary Provisions) Regulations 1986[^f00005]

  .

## 4. Amendment relating to compliance with Community Directives and ECE Regulations

In regulation 6(2) the word “or” immediately following sub-paragraph (b) shall be omitted and after sub-paragraph (c) there shall be added the following words—

> - ; or
>   - (a) a sound level measurement certificate issued by the Secretary of State under regulation 4 of the Motorcycles (Sound Level Measurement Certificates) Regulations 1980[^f00006];

.

This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 (“**the principal regulations**”).

The principal amendments are to regulation 57 (noise limits—motorcycles), which is amended in two respects.

**1.** Compliance with the sound level limits permitted by E.E.C. Council Directive 87/56 at its first stage of implementation will be compulsory for motorcycles first used on or after 1st April 1991.

**2.** Previously a vehicle was treated as complying with the principal regulations if a sound level measuring certificate (“a certificate”) was issued in relation to the vehicle by reason of the vehicle’s conforming with the requirements of E.E.C Council Directive 78/1015. By virtue of these Regulations a vehicle first used not later than31st March 1991 may be treated as complying with the principal regulations if a certificate has been issued in relation to the vehicle by reason of the vehicle’s conforming with either the requirements of Directive 78/1015 or 87/56. A vehicle first used on or after 1st April 1991 however may only be treated as complying with the principal regulations if a certificate has been issued in relation to the vehicle by reason of the vehicle’s conforming with the requirements of Directive 87/56. In this connection the omission of the sound level measuring certificate from regulation 6 in the principal regulations is corrected.

There are also provisions to clarify the meaning given in the principal regulations for “Community Directive, followed by a number”, to update the meaning given in the principal regulations for “the Community Recording Equipment Regulation”, and to effect consequential amendments.
