# The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of errors in [S.I. 1989/1319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1319) and is being issued free to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1989 No. 1541

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989

Made

23rd August 1989

Laid before Parliament

25th August 1989

Coming into force

31st August 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 218(1)(a), (2), (3) and (6) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 31st August 1989.

**2.** The Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989[^f00002] shall be amended as follows–

- (a) For paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the following paragraph–

  > **2.** Any prohibition or restriction on a person’s employment in force immediately before 1st September 1989 which–
  >
  > - (a) is contained in a direction under regulation 10 of the 1982 Regulations, or
  > - (b) has effect, by virtue of paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to those Regulations, as if so contained
  >
  > shall have effect as if it were contained in a direction under regulation 10(2) of these Regulations and, accordingly, regulation 10(6) shall apply in relation thereto.
- (b) In paragraph 5(3)(a)(ii) of Schedule 4 after the words “in more than one school” there shall be added the words “maintained by the same local education authority”.
- (c) In paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 the word “if” shall be deleted where it first appears and inserted at the beginning of sub-paragraph (a).
- (d) The last sentence of paragraph 2(3) of Schedule 5 shall be deleted.

Angela Rumbold

Minister of State,

Department of Education and Science

23rd August 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These amending Regulations make 4 corrections to the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989. Paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 is amended so that it does not re-impose a bar on the employment of a teacher imposed before 8th April 1982 but subsequently withdrawn. Paragraph 5(3)(a)(ii) of Schedule 4 is amended by the addition to the statement required under paragraph 5(3)(a) of a requirement to state that the schools at which the teacher may be employed are maintained by the same local education authority. A drafting amendment has been made to paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5. The final sentence of paragraph 2(3) of Schedule 5 has been deleted as inconsistent with the requirement for written notification of qualified teacher status under paragraph 1(1)(b) of that Schedule.
