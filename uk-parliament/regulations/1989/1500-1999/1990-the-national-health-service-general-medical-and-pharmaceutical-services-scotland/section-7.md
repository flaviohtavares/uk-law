# The National Health Service (General Medical and Pharmaceutical Services) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1989, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment to regulation 6 (application for inclusion in medical list)

In paragraph (1) of regulation 6 (application for inclusion in medical list) of the principal Regulations, for the words “in the form set out” there shall be substituted the words “in writing which shall include the information and undertakings specified”.
