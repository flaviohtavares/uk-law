# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 778 (S.79)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989

Made

4th May 1989

Coming into force

12th June 1989

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989 and shall come into force on 12th June 1989.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

In Chapter IV of the Table of Fees in rule 347 of the Rules of the Court of Session[^f00002], in the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the figures specified in column 2, substitute the figures specified in column 3 of that table:—
