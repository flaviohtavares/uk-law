# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1989, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Official statements in respect of imported trees, wood, isolated bark etc.

Any official statement required to be made in accordance with column 3 of Schedule 3 in respect of any tree, wood, isolated bark, soil or growing medium shall be deemed to have been made by the issue of a phytosanitary certificate to accompany that tree, wood, isolated bark, soil or growing medium in accordance with this Order.
