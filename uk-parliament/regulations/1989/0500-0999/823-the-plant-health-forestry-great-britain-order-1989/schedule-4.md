# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1989, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4 — EXPORTS TO MEMBER STATES

Article 11

### PART I — TREES, WOOD, ISOLATED BARK, SOIL AND GROWING MEDIUM TO BE ACCOMPANIED BY A PHYTOSANITARY CERTIFICATE OR A REFORWARDING PHYTOSANITARY CERTIFICATE ISSUED IN GREAT BRITAIN WHEN EXPORTED OR RE-EXPORTED TO A MEMBER STATE

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>Item</em></th><th><em>Description of trees, wood, isolated bark, soil and growing medium</em></th></tr>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1.</td><td>Trees, planted or intended for planting, other than seeds.</td></tr>
<tr><td>2.</td><td>Wood of the following genera or species–<br>(a) <em>Acer saccharum</em> grown in the United States of America;<br>(b) <em>Castanea</em>;<br>(c) <em>Coniferae</em> grown in a country or part of a country not in Europe except where the wood has been kiln-dried in accordance with the specification set out in paragraph 2, column 3 of item 1 of Part II of Schedule 3;<br>(d) <em>Platanus</em> grown in the United States of America and other countries where<em>Ceratocystis fimbriata f.sp. platani</em> is known to occur;<br>(e) <em>Populus</em> grown in any country of the American continent;<br>(f) <em>Quercus</em>.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3.</td><td>Isolated bark of<em>Quercus</em>, other than<em>Quercus suber</em>, grown in countries other than Romania, the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics or a country in North America.</td></tr>
<tr><td>4.</td><td>(a) Soil and growing medium not attached to or associated with trees, other than that composed entirely of inorganic substances or unused peat; and<br>(b) Soil and growing medium–<br>(i) attached to or associated with trees; and<br>(ii) originating in a country outside the Euro-Mediterranean area.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
