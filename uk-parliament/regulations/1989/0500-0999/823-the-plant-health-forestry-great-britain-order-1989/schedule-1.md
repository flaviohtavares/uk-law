# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1989, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — TREE PESTS THE LANDING OF WHICH IS PROHIBITED

Articles 3 and 4

### PART I — live organisms of the animal kingdom at all stages of their development

**1.** *Acleris variana*(Fern.), the Blackheaded Budworm.

**2.** *Arrhenodes minutus*(Drury), the Oak Timberworm.

**3.** *Bursaphelenchus xylophilus*(Steiner and Buhrer) Nickle, the Pine Wood Nematode.

**4.** *Dendroctonus micans*(Kugelann), the Great Spruce Bark Beetle.

**5.** *Hylurgopinus rufipes*(Eichhoff), the American Dark Elm Bark Beetle.

**6.** *Ips amitinus*(Eichhoff), the Smaller Eight-Toothed Spruce Bark Beetle.

**7.** *Ips duplicatus*(Sahlberg), the Northern Spruce Bark Beetle.

**8.** *Ips typographus*(L.), the Larger Eight-Toothed Spruce Bark Beetle.

**9.** *Pissodes* spp. (non-European), Weevils.

**10.** *Pseudopityophthorus minutissimus*(Zimmerman), Oak Bark Beetles.

**11.** *Pseudopityophthorus pruinosus*(Eichhoff), Oak Bark Beetles.

**12.** *Scaphoideus luteolus* Van Duzee, the White-banded Elm Leaf Hopper.

**13.** *Scolytidae*(non-European) (Bark Beetles) of conifers (*Coniferae*).

### PART II — BACTERIA

*Xanthomonas populi*(Ridé) Ridé and Ridé, (*Aplanobacter populi* Ridé), the cause of Bacterial Canker of poplar.

### PART III — CRYPTOGAMIA

**1.** *Atropellis* spp., the cause of Atropellis Canker of pine.

**2.** *Ceratocystis coerulescens*(Münch) Bak., (non-European spp.), the cause of Sapstreak of maple.

**3.** *Ceratocystis fagacearum*(Bretz) Hunt, the cause of Oak Wilt.

**4.** *Ceratocystis fimbriata*(Ell. and Halst.) Davidson*f. sp. platani,* Walt., the cause of Canker Stain of plane.

**5.** *Cercoseptoria pini-densiflorae*(Hori and Nambu) Deighton, (*Cercospora pini-densiflorae*), the cause of Needle Blight of pine.

**6.** *Chrysomyxa arctostaphyli* Diet., the cause of Spruce Witches Broom Rust.

**7.** *Cronartium* spp. (non-European), the cause of Conifer Rust.

**8.** *Cronartium quercuum*(Berk.) Miyabe ex Shirai, the cause of Eastern Pine Oak Gall Rust.

**9.** *Cryphonectria parasitica*(Murrill) Barr. (*Endothia parasitica*(Murrill) Anderson and Anderson), the cause of Sweet Chestnut Blight.

**10.** *Endocronartium* spp. (non-European), (*Peridermium*), the cause of Conifer Rust.

**11.** *Guignardia laricina*(Saw.) Yamamoto & K. Ito, the cause of Shoot Blight of larch.

**12.** *Hypoxylon pruinatum*(Klotzsch) Cke., (*Hypoxylon mammatum*(Wahl.) Mill.), the cause of Hypoxylon Canker of poplar.

**13.** *Inonotus weirii*(Murrill) Kotlaba and Pouzar (*Phellinus weirii*(Murr.) Gilb.) (*Poria weirii* Murr.) (Murr.), the cause of Poria Root Rot.

**14.** *Melampsora farlowii*(Arthur) Davis, the cause of Hemlock Rust.

**15.** *Melampsora medusae* Thüm. (*M. albertensis* Arthur), the cause of Conifer-aspen Rust.

**16.** *Mycosphaerella larici-leptolepis*(K. Ito et al), the cause of Needle Cast of larch.

**17.** *Mycosphaerella populorum* G. E. Thomp. (*Septoria musiva* Peck), the cause of Septoria Canker of poplar.

**18.** *Ophiostoma roboris* Georg. et Teod., associated with Vascular Mycosis of oak.

**19.** *Scirrhia acicola*(Dearn.) Siggers, the cause of Brown Spot Needle Blight of pine.

**20.** *Scirrhia pini* Funk and Parker (*Dothistroma pini* Hulbary), the cause of Needle Blight of pine.

### PART IV — VIRUSES AND MYCOPLASMAS

Elm phloem necrosis.

### PART V — PHANEROGAMS

*Arceuthobium* spp. (non-European), the dwarf mistletoes.
