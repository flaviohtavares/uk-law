## SCHEDULE 3 — CONDITIONS SUBJECT TO WHICH TREES, WOOD, ISOLATED BARK, SOIL AND GROWING MEDIUM MAY BE LANDED UNLESS OTHERWISE PROHIBITED UNDER ARTICLE 3
