# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1989, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1989, shall apply to Great Britain and shall come into force on 8th June 1989.
