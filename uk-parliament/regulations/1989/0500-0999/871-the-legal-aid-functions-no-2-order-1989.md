# The Legal Aid (Functions) (No. 2) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 871

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Aid (Functions) (No. 2) Order 1989

Made

19th May 1989

Coming into force

19th May 1989

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 3(4) of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which has, in accordance with section 36(2)(b) of that Act, been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Legal Aid (Functions) (No. 2) Order 1989 and shall come into force on the day on which it is made.

## 2. Determination of costs of representation in care proceedings

The general function conferred on the Legal Aid Board by section 3(2) of the Legal Aid Act 1988 shall include determination of the costs of representation for the purposes of the proceedings mentioned in subsection (4)(c) of that section, and all matters relating to such determination.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C*

Dated 19th May 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confers on the Legal Aid Board the function of determining the costs of representation in care proceedings in juvenile courts and the Crown Court.
