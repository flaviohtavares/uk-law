# The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 951

LAND CHARGES

The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1989

Made

7th June 1989

Laid before Parliament

8th June 1989

Coming into force

1st July 1989

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14 of the Local Land Charges Act 1975[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the treasury as to fees, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1989 and shall come into force on 1st July 1989.

**2.** For Schedule 3 to the Local Land Charges Rules 1977[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following Schedule:

**3.** The Local Land Charges (Amendment Rules 1987[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 5th June 1989

We concur,

Stephen Dorrell;

Alan Howarth.

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury.

Dated 7th June 1989

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules increase the fees payable in connection with the registration of local land charges by 7.5 per cent.
