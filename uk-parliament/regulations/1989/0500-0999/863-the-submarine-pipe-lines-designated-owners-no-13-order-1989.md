# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 13) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 863

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 13) Order 1989

Made

11th May 1989

Coming into force

12th June 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 13) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 12th June 1989.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

11th May 1989
