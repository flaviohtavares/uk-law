# The Community Charges (Notification of Deaths) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 735 (S.77)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Community Charges (Notification of Deaths) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1989

Made

24th April 1989

Laid before Parliament

9th May 1989

Coming into force

30th May 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 28B(1), (3) and (4) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Charges (Notification of Deaths) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 30th May 1989.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Community Charges (Notification of Deaths) (Scotland) Regulations 1988([^f00002]).

## 3. Amendment of definition of registrar

In regulation 2 of the principal Regulations for the words “the district registrar for each registration district” there shall be substituted “, in respect of a death registered in any registration district, the district registrar for that district” .

Michael B Forsyth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th April 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Community Charges (Notification of Deaths) (Scotland) Regulations 1988 prescribe the particulars of death of persons over the age of 16 years usually resident in the United Kingdom which the district registrar must furnish to the appropriate Community Charges Registration Officer.

These Regulations amend the definition of registrar contained in the 1988 Regulations to require notification only by the registrar of the district in which the death was registered.
