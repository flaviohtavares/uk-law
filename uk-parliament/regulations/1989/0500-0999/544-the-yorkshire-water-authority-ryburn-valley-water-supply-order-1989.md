# The Yorkshire Water Authority (Ryburn Valley Water Supply) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 544

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Yorkshire Water Authority (Ryburn Valley Water Supply) Order 1989

Made

22nd March 1989

Coming into force

23rd March 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment, on the application of the Yorkshire Water Authority, being statutory water undertakers[^f00001], and in exercise of powers conferred by sections 23, 32(1) and 59(1)[^f00002] of the Water Act 1945[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Yorkshire Water Authority (Ryburn Valley Water Supply) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 23rd March 1989.

(2) The Yorkshire Water Authority Orders 1974 to 1988 and this Order may be cited together as the Yorkshire Water Authority Orders 1974 to 1989.

(3) In this Order–

- “the Authority” means the Yorkshire Water Authority;
- “the authorised works” means the two water storage tanks each with an inlet main and an outlet main, and an access track, shown on the deposited plan;
- “the deposited plan” means the plan prepared in duplicate, signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment and marked“Plan referred to in the Yorkshire Water Authority (Ryburn Valley Water Supply) Order 1989”, one duplicate of which is deposited and available for inspection at the principal office of the Authority at West Riding House, Albion Street, Leeds LS1 5AA, and the other at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- “the Third Schedule” means the Third Schedule to the Water Act 1945;
- “the undertaking” means the water undertaking of the Authority as for the time being authorised by any enactment.

## 2. Power to acquire land and construct and maintain works

(1) The Authority may construct and maintain the authorised works on the land described in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) For the purpose of carrying out the authorised works the Authority are hereby authorised to acquire compulsorily the land described in Schedule 1 to this Order.

## 3. Works to form part of the undertaking

The authorised works shall for all purposes form part of the undertaking.

## 4. Incorporation of enactments

(1) The provisions of the Third Schedule specified in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Order shall apply to the undertaking, subject to the modifications set out in column (2) thereof.

(2) For the purposes of section 13 of the Third Schedule as so applied, the prescribed distance shall be two hundred metres.

(3) Part I of the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965[^f00005] shall apply to this order subject to the following exceptions and modifications:–

- (a) omit section 27 (acquiring authority to make good deficiencies in rates);
- (b) in Schedule 3 (alternative procedure for obtaining right of entry), omit paragraph 3(3).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

J. A. L. Gunn

An Under Secretary in the

Department of the Environment

22nd March 1989

## SCHEDULE 1 — DESCRIPTION OF LAND

**1.** (1) Land at Wholestone Quarry, Saddleworth Road, Barkisland in the District of Calderdale in the County of West Yorkshire shown edged pink on the deposited plan, having an area of 2.05 acres or thereabouts and forming part of enclosure number 1637 on the Ordnance Survey 1:2500 map sheet reference SE 0518 (1962 edition).
