# Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 777 (S.78)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989

Made

4th May 1989

Laid before Parliament

16th May 1989

Coming into force

12th June 1989

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1989 and shall come into force on 12th June 1989.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.
