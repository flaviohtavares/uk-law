# The Liverpool–Leeds–Hull Trunk Road (Howden Bypass) (Revocation) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 734

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Liverpool–Leeds–Hull Trunk Road (Howden Bypass) (Revocation) Order 1989

Made

17th April 1989

Coming into force

19th May 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The Liverpool–Leeds–Hull Trunk Road (Howden Bypass) Order 1938[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 19th May 1989 and may be cited as the Liverpool–Leeds–Hull Trunk Road (Howden Bypass) (Revocation) Order 1989.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

J F Ballard

Regional Director Yorkshire and Humberside Region

Department of Transport

17th April 1989
