# The A282 Trunk Road Dartford–Thurrock Approach Roads London Road (Slip Road) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 817

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A282 Trunk Road Dartford–Thurrock Approach Roads London Road (Slip Road) Order 1989

Made

5th May 1989

Coming into force

15th June 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him [^f00002], and of all otherenabling powers:

**1.** The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order (“the new trunk road”) shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** In this Order:—

(i) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered RSE/A282/65/2/1 marked “The A282 Trunk Road Dartford–Thurrock Approach Roads London Road (Slip Road) Order 1989”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House,43 Marsham Street, London SW1 3PY;

(ii) “the trunk road” means the Dartford–Thurrock Trunk Road (A282).

**4.** This Order shall come into force on 15th June 1989 and may be cited as the A282 Trunk Road Dartford–Thurrock Approach Roads London Road (Slip Road) Order 1989.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

D. Gruffydd Jones

Regional Director South East Region

Department of Transport

5th May 1989

## SCHEDULE — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is southwards from a junction with London Road to a point where it joins the trunk road and is given the reference number 1 on the deposited plan.
