# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1989, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Directions

Any power conferred by these Regulations to give a direction includes power to cancel or vary the direction by a subsequent direction.
