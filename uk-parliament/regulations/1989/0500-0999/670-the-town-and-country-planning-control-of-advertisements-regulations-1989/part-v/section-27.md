# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1989, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Statutory Instruments revoked

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1984[^f00009], the Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Amendment) Regulations 1987[^f00010] and the Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1987[^f00011] are hereby revoked.
