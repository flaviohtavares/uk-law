# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1989, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Requirement for consent

Except as provided by regulation 3(2) and regulation 19(1)(9), no advertisement may be displayed without consent granted by the local planning authority or by the Secretary of State on an application in that behalf (referred to in these Regulations as “express consent”), or granted by regulation 6 below (referred to in these Regulations as “deemed consent”).
