# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1989, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Deemed consent for the display of advertisements

(1) Subject to regulations 7 and 8 below, deemed consent is hereby granted for the display of an advertisement falling within any class specified in Part I of Schedule 3, subject–

- (a) to any conditions and limitations specified in that Part in relation to that class; and
- (b) to the standard conditions.

(2) Part II of Schedule 3 applies for the interpretation of that Schedule.
