# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 7) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 755

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 7) Order 1989

Made

28th April 1989

Coming into force

1st June 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 7) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st June 1989.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

28th April 1989

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### PART I — DESIGNATED OWNERS

- Esso Exploration and Production UK Limited, whose registered office is at Esso House, Victoria Street, London SW1E 5JW.
- Shell U.K. Limited, whose registered office is at Shell-Mex House, Strand, London WC2R 0DX.
