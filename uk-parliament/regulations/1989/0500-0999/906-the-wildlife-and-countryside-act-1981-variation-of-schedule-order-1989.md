# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 906

WILDLIFE

The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule) Order 1989

Made

25th May 1989

Laid before Parliament

7th June 1989

Coming into force

28th June 1989

The Secretary of State –

(1) having received (under section 22(3) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^f00001] (“the Act”) a representation from the Nature Conservancy Council;

(2) having given (under section 26(4) of the Act) any local authority affected and any other person affected an opportunity to submit objections or representations with respect to the subject matter of this Order;

(3) being of the opinion that the animals referred to in article 2 below are in danger of extinction in Great Britain or likely to become so endangered unless conservation measures are taken;

in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(3) of the Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 28th June 1989.

## 2. Variation of Schedule 5

25th May 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies throughout Great Britain, varies Schedule 5 to the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 which lists animals protected under that Act.

Article 2 adds 22 butterfly species to Schedule 5, conferring protection in respect of the sale of those species.
