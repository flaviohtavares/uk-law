# The Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 328

INDUSTRIAL ASSURANCE

The Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1989

Made

3rd March 1989

Laid before Parliament

6th March 1989

Coming into force

1st April 1989

The Industrial Assurance Commissioner, with the approval of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 43 of the Industrial Assurance Act 1923[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

**2.** In the Regulations “the Act” means the Industrial Assurance Act 1923.

**3.** The fees set out in the Schedule hereto shall be payable in respect of the matters set out therein.

**4.** The Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1988[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

J. M. Bridgeman

Industrial Assurance Commissioner

28th February 1989

We approve these Regulations,

Kenneth Carlisle

Alan Howarth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

3rd March 1989

These Regulations supersede the Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1988. They generally increase by about 6% the fees payable in connection with the exercise by the Industrial Assurance Commissioner of his functions under the Industrial Assurance Act 1923.
