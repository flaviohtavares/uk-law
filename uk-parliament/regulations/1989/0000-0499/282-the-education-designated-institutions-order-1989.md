# The Education (Designated Institutions) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 282

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Designated Institutions) Order 1989

Made

1st March 1989

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1989

Coming into force

1st April 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 129(1) and 130(1)(a) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

**2.** The institutions which are specified in the Schedule to this Order, being institutions which appear to the Secretary of State to fall within subsection (2) or (3) of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, are hereby designated as institutions eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Polytechnics and Colleges Funding Council.

**3.** Section 130 of that Act shall apply to those institutions specified in Part I of that Schedule.

1st March 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates as from 1st April 1989 certain educational institutions as institutions eligible to receive funds from the Polytechnics and Colleges Funding Council. In respect of those which prior to that date are assisted by a local education authority section 130 of the Education Reform Act 1988 is applied so as to effect a transfer of relevant property, rights and liabilities from the authority to the institution on that date.
