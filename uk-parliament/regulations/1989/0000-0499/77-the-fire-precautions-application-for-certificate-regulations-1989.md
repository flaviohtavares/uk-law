# The Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 77

FIRE PRECAUTIONS

The Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1989

Made

20th January 1989

Laid before Parliament

27th January 1989

Coming into force

1st April 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5(1) and 43(1) of the Fire Precautions Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

**2.** An application for a fire certificate in respect of any premises shall be in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form to the like effect.

**3.** The Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1976[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

20th January 1989

## SCHEDULE — FIRE PRECAUTIONS ACT 1971, s.5

Regulation 2

APPLICATION FOR A FIRE CERTIFICATE

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/77/images/uksi_19890077_en_001) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/77/images/uksi_19890077_en_002) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/77/images/uksi_19890077_en_003)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations supersede the Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1976 and prescribe a new form of application for a fire certificate under the Fire Precautions Act 1971. The new form in the Schedule to these Regulations makes changes in the information required by paragraph 3 of the form as to the number of persons at work in the premises to which the application relates and requires details to be given in paragraph 8 of the fire-fighting equipment available for use in the premises.
