# The North East of Birmingham–Nottingham Trunk Road (Breedon-On-The-Hill Bypass) Order 1957 Revocation Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 202

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North East of Birmingham–Nottingham Trunk Road (Breedon-On-The-Hill Bypass) Order 1957 Revocation Order 1989

Made

14th February 1989

Coming into force

3rd March 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order shall come into force on 3rd March 1989, and may be cited as the North East of Birmingham–Nottingham Trunk Road (Breedon-On-The-Hill Bypass) Order 1957 Revocation Order 1989.

**2.** The North East of Birmingham–Nottingham Trunk Road (Breedon-On-The-Hill Bypass) Order 1957[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

P. M. Hewitt

Regional Director East Midlands Region

Department of Transport

14th February 1989
