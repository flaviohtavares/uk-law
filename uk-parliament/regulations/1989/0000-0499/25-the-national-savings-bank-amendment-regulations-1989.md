# The National Savings Bank (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 25

SAVINGS BANKS

The National Savings Bank (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

10th January 1989

Laid before Parliament

13th January 1989

Coming into force

1st March 1989

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(1) of the National Savings Bank Act 1971[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the National Savings Bank (Amendment) Regulations 1989, and shall come into force on 1st March 1989.

**2.** The National Savings Bank Regulations 1972[^f00002] shall be amended by deleting sub-paragraph (b) in regulation 22(1).

David Lightbown

David Maclean

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

10th January 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which amend the National Savings Bank Regulations 1972, abolish the “Paybill” facility whereby a depositor was able to withdraw up to £250 for the purpose of making immediate payments through the Post Office.
