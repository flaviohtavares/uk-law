# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 69 (S.10)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1988

Approved by the House of Commons

Made

28th November 1988

Laid before the House of Commons

10th November 1988

Coming into force

12th January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987( [^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be concerned and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

## 2. Revenue support grants for 1989-90

The amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the financial year 1989-90 shall be the amount determined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite to the name of that local authority.

Malcolm Rifkind

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

22nd November 1988

We consent,

David Maclean

Tony Durant

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

28th November 1988
