# The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1 (C. 1)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1989

Made

3rd January 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 171(1) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1989.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 shall come into force on 1st February 1989–

- (a) sections 35 and 36 (reviews of sentencing);
- (b) Schedule 3 (reviews of sentencing – supplementary);
- (c) section 170(1) (minor and consequential amendments) to the extent necessary to bring into force the provisions of Schedule 15 specified in the following paragraph; and
- (d) so much of Schedule 15 (minor and consequential amendments) as amends the following enactments–
  - (i) the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act [1978 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/23);
  - (ii) section 44 of the Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Act [1980 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/47); and
  - (iii) the Supreme Court Act [1981 (c. 54)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/54).

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

3rd January 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st February 1989 in England and Wales and Northern Ireland sections 35 and 36 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Criminal Justice Act 1988 and associated amendments. These provisions empower the Court of Appeal on a reference from the Attorney General to review for undue leniency the sentencing of persons convicted in the Crown Court of certain offences, to quash any sentence passed by the Crown Court, and to pass an appropriate sentence in its place.
