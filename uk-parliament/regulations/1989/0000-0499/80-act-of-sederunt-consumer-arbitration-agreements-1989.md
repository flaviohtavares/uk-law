# Act of Sederunt (Consumer Arbitration Agreements) 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 80 (S.11)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Consumer Arbitration Agreements) 1989

Made

19th January 1989

Coming into force

1st February 1989

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Consumer Arbitration Agreements) 1989 and shall come into force on 1st February 1989.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

Applications under section 7(1) of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988

**2.** An application to the sheriff under section 7(1) of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988([^f00002]) shall be made by summary application.

Emslie Lord

President, IPD Edinburgh

19th January 1989

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt provides that any application to the sheriff under section 7(1) of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 shall be made by summary application.
