# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Procedure on refusal of amendment

Where an Area Director refuses an application for the amendment of a certificate, he shall notify the assisted person’s solicitor in writing, stating his reasons for so doing.
