# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Reasons to be given for refusing authority

If an Area Director refuses an application for authority made under regulation 59 or 61, he shall give written reasons for his decision.
