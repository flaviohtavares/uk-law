# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Notice to trustee in bankruptcy, etc.

(1) Where moneys become payable under regulation 87(b), the solicitor or the Board, as the case may be, shall send to the trustee in bankruptcy, the trustee or assignee of the deed of arrangement or the liquidator of the company in liquidation, as the case may be, notice that a certificate has been issued to the assisted person.

(2) A notice sent under paragraph (1) shall operate as a request by the assisted person for payment of the moneys payable under regulation 87(b) to the assisted person’s solicitor or the Board, as the case may be, and shall be a sufficient authority for that purpose.
