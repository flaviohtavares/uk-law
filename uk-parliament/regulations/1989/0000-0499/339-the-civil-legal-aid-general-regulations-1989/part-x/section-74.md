# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Effect of revocation or discharge

(1) An Area Director may terminate a certificate by revoking or discharging it under this Part of these Regulations.

(2) Subject to this Part of these Regulations, a person whose certificate is revoked shall be deemed never to have been an assisted person in relation to those proceedings except for the purposes of section 18 of the Act; and a person whose certificate is discharged shall, from the date of the discharge, cease to be an assisted person in the proceedings to which the certificate related.
