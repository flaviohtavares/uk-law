# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 134

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 134. Time and form of application

(1) An application for an order under section 18 of the Act may be made at any time and in any manner in which an application for an order for costs might be made in respect of the same proceedings if none of the parties were receiving legal aid.

(2) Any proceedings in respect of which a separate certificate could properly be issued shall be treated as separate proceedings for the purposes of section 18 of the Act.
