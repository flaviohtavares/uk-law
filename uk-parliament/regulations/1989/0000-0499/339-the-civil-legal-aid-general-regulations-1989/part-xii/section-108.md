# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 108

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 108. Failure to apply for taxation

Where, in any proceedings to which a former assisted person was a party, an order or agreement was made for the payment to him of costs and he has failed to ask for the costs to be taxed or his certificate is discharged before taxation, the Board may authorise the making of the application for taxation on his behalf and the costs of the application and of taxation shall be deemed to be costs in the proceedings to which the certificate related.
