# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Costs of applications, reports, etc., under these Regulations

Costs incurred by reason of any application made under Part VIII, and of any report made by an assisted person’s solicitor under Part IX, of these Regulations shall be taxed on the standard basis and costs incurred by reason of regulation 25, 50, 54, 82 or 124 shall be costs in the cause.
