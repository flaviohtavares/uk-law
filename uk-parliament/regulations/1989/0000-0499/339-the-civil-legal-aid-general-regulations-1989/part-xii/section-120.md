# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Costs to be paid out of the fund

Any proceedings under regulations 113 to 119 shall be deemed to be proceedings to which the assisted person’s certificate relates, whether or not it has been discharged or revoked, and the costs of such proceedings shall be paid out of the fund.
