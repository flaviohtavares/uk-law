# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 114. Application to judge to review taxation

Where the assisted person or his solicitor, as the case may be, is dissatisfied with the decision of the taxing officer on any matter to which objection has been taken under regulation 113, the solicitor shall apply to the Board for authority to have the taxation reviewed; and, if the Board gives authority (but not otherwise), the solicitor may apply (or instruct counsel to apply) to a judge to review the taxation in accordance with rules of court.
