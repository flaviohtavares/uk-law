# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Duty to report death, etc., of assisted person

A solicitor who has acted or is acting for an assisted person shall, on becoming aware that the assisted person,–

- (a) has died; or
- (b) has had a bankruptcy order made against him, report that fact to the Area Director.
