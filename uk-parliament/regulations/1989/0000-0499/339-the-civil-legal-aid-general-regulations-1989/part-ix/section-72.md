# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Duty to report completion of case

A solicitor shall report forthwith to the Area Director either–

- (a) upon the completion of the case if he has completed the work authorised by the certificate, or
- (b) if, for any reason, he is unable to complete the work.
