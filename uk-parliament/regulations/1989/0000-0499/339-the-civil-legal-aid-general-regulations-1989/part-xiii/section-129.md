# The Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989, section 129

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 129. Order for costs

The court may direct–

- (a) that payment under the order for costs shall be limited to such amount, payable in instalments or otherwise (including an amount to be determined on taxation), as the court thinks reasonable having regard to all the circumstances; or
- (b) where the court thinks it reasonable that no payment should be made immediately or that the assisted person should have no liability for payment, that payment under the order for costs be suspended either until such date as the court may determine or indefinitely.
