# The Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1989, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 5th April 1989.

(2) In these Regulations, “Part V” means Part V of the Housing Act 1985.
