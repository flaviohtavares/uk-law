# The Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1989, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** The Local Government Reorganisation (Preservation of Right to Buy) Order 1986[^f00003] shall not apply in any case where the qualifying disposal, within the meaning of article 3 of that Order, is made on or after the coming into force of these Regulations.
