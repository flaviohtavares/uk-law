# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1989, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Fat content of milk

At the request of a producer to whom the second indented paragraph of the second paragraph of Article 12(1) of the Commission Regulation applies, the Minister shall decide, for the purposes of any levy, that the fat content which shall be considered as representative of the milk delivered to a purchaser by that producer shall be determined in accordance with that paragraph.

**6.** New arbitration procedures have been introduced which are similar to those which operate under the agricultural holdings legislation, and contain in particular new statutory time limits within which certain steps in the arbitration must be taken (Schedule 4, 5 and 6).
