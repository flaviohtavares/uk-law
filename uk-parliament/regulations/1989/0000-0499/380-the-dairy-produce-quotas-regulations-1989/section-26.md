# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1989, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Inspection of entries in the Minister’s registers

The Minister shall permit, during reasonable working hours, inspection of any entry relating to–

- (a) a specific holding in the registers referred to in regulation 25(1)(b)(i) and 25(2)(b)(i) by any person who is the direct seller or producer in relation to, or gives the Minister a statement in writing that he has an interest in, that holding, and
- (b) a specific purchaser in either register referred to in regulation 25(3)(b) by the purchaser,

and shall, on payment of a reasonable charge, forward a copy of that entry to any such person who requests it.
