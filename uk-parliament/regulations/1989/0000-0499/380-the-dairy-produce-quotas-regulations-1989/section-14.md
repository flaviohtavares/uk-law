# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1989, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Quota exchange

For the purposes of Article 5(5) and (6) of the Commission Regulation (which deals with changes from direct sales to wholesale delivery and vice versa) any person in a region may exchange direct sales quota for wholesale quota with any other person with a holding in the same region on such terms as those producers and the Minister may agree having regard to the provisions of the Community legislation.
