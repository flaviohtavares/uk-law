# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1989, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Northern Ireland wholesale provision

The Minister may add to any exceptional hardship provision or small producers supplementary development provision from wholesale quota in any running regional wholesale reserve in Northern Ireland, in accordance with Schedule 9.
