# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 362 (S.38)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1989

Made

7th March 1989

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1989

Coming into force

1st April 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1989, shall extend to Scotland only and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

**2.** The provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to fees charged in respect of the matters mentioned in column 2 of that Schedule) shall have effect as if, for the references to the sums specified in those provisions (for which the sums mentioned in column 3 of that Schedule were substituted by the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 1988[^f00002]), there were substituted references to the sums specified in column 4 of that Schedule.

**3.** The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 1988 is revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th March 1989
