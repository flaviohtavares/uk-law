# The Public Trustee (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 437

TRUSTEES

The Public Trustee (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1989

Made

9th March 1989

Coming into force

1st April 1989

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 9 of the Public Trustee Act 1906[^f00001], and with the sanction of the Lord Chancellor, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Trustee (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

**2.** In article 20 of the Public Trustee (Fees) Order 1985 [^f00002]; the figure “1” shall be substituted for the figure “1 2/13” in paragraph (3).

Alan Howarth

Stephen Dorrell

Two of the Lords Commmissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 9th March 1989

I hereby signify my sanction

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 9th March 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Public Trustee (Fees) Order 1985 by adjusting the withdrawal fee rate fixed by that Order.
