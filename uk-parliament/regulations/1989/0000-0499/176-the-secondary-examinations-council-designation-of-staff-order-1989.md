# The Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 176

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1989

Made

9th February 1989

Laid before Parliament

14th February 1989

Coming into force

7th March 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 15(3) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1989, and shall come into force on 7th March 1989.

## 2. Designation of staff to be transferred from existing body

The employees of the Secondary Examinations Council who are named below are designated as respects the School Examinations and Assessment Council:

- Browne, Frances Claire
- Scruton, Paul
- Wilson, Patricia Ann.

Kenneth Baker

Secretary of State for Education and Science

9th February 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order concerns certain staff of the Secondary Examinations Council. The Council is to be wound up in consequence of the establishment on 15th August 1988 (under section 14 of the Education Reform Act 1988) of the Curriculum Council for Wales, the National Curriculum Council, and the School Examinations and Assessment Council. Under section 15 of that Act, the Secretary of State may designate (either individually or by class or description) employees of the School Examinations Council for automatic transfer to the new Councils. Where an individual is so designated there is no termination of, or change in the terms of, his contract of employment. This Order lists those staff so designated as respects the School Examinations and Assessment Council in addition to those already designated by the School Curriculum Development Committee and Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/2171](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/2171)).
