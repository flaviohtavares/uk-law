# The Board of Inquiry (Air Force) (Amendment) Rules 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 257

DEFENCE

The Board of Inquiry (Air Force) (Amendment) Rules 1989

Made

23rd February 1989

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 1989

Coming into force

31st March 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 135 of the Air Force Act 1955[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:–

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Board of Inquiry (Air Force) (Amendment) Rules 1989 and shall come into force on 31st March 1989.

## 2. Amendment to the Principal Rules

The Board of Inquiry (Air Force) Rules 1956[^f00002] shall be amended in rule 13 by the deletion in paragraph (3)(a) of the words

“and states as the ground of his objection either that he has no religious belief or that the taking of an oath is contrary to his religious belief,”.

George Younger

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

23rd February 1989

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules further amend the Board of Inquiry (Air Force) Rules 1956, by removing the condition that a witness before a board who objects to taking an oath should, before being permitted to affirm, state as the ground of his objection that he has no religious belief, or that the taking of an oath is contrary to his religious belief.
