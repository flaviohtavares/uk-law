# The Income Tax (Building Societies) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 36

INCOME TAX

The Income Tax (Building Societies) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

12th January 1989

Laid before the House of Commons

13th January 1989

Coming into force

6th February 1989

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 476(1) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Building Societies) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 6th February 1989.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations the “1986 Regulations” means the Income Tax (Building Societies) Regulations 1986[^f00002].

## 3. Amendments to the 1986 Regulations

In Regulation 2(1) of the 1986 Regulations—

- (a) after the definition of “exempt pension fund” there shall be inserted—

  > - “general client account deposit” means a deposit, held by a building society in a client account (other than one which is identified by the building society as one in which sums are held only for one or more particular clients of the person whose account it is) in respect of which that person is required by provision made under any enactment to make payments representing interest to some or all of the clients for whom, or on whose account, he received the sums deposited in the account;

  ; and
- (b) in the definition of “qualifying time deposit” in paragraph (b) for the words “before the end of the period of twelve months” there shall be substituted “not later than the end of the period of five years”.

**4.** In Regulation 6(1) of the 1986 Regulations—

- (a) after paragraph (g) there shall be inserted—

  > - (gg) a payment of interest made to a building society on a loan made by a building society;

  ; and
- (b) after paragraph (h) there shall be inserted—

  > - (hh) a payment in respect of a general client account deposit;

  .

A. J. G. Isaac

T. J. Painter

Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue

12th January 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 6th February 1989, further amend the Income Tax (Building Societies) Regulations 1986 (“the 1986 Regulations”).

Regulation 1 provides for citation and commencement and regulation 2 for interpretation.

Regulation 3 amends Regulation 2(1) of the 1986 Regulation in two respects: paragraph (a) inserts a definition of “general client account deposit” and paragraph (b) amends the definition of “qualifying time deposit” by increasing the maximum specified period before the expiration of which the loan must be repaid from one of less than twelve months beginning on the date the deposit is made to one of five years beginning on that date.

Regulation 4 amends Regulation 6(1) of the 1986 Regulations (gross payments) so that a payment of interest made to a building society by a building society on a loan made by a building society may be a gross payment and so that a gross payment may be made in respect of a general client account deposit.
