# The Levens Bridge–Carlisle Trunk Road (A596 Wigton Bypass) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1440

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Levens Bridge–Carlisle Trunk Road (A596 Wigton Bypass) Order 1989

Made

25th July 1989

Coming into force

25th August 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him [^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** The Secretary of State directs as respects any part of a highway which crosses the route of the new trunk road that–

- (a) where the highway is a highway maintainable at the public expense by a local highway authority, the part in question shall be maintained by that authority, and
- (b) where the highway is not a highway so maintainable and is not maintainable under a special enactment or by reason of tenure, enclosure or prescription, the Secretary of State shall be under no duty to maintain the part in question.

until, in either case, a date to be specified in a notice given by the Secretary of State to the highway authority for that highway. The date specified will not be later than the date on which the relevant route is opened for the purpose of through traffic.

**4.** In this Order:(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

(2)

- (i) “the deposited plan”means the plan numbered HA10/1 CNW 267 marked“The Levens Bridge–Carlisle Trunk Road (A596 Wigton Bypass) Order 1989” signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (ii) “the new trunk road”means the highway mentioned in article 1 of this Order;
- (iii) “the trunk road”means the Levens Bridge–Carlisle Trunk Road (A596).

**5.** This Order shall come into force on 25th August 1989 and may be cited as the Levens Bridge–Carlisle Trunk Road (A596 Wigton Bypass) Order 1989.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

D. C. Renshaw

Regional Director North West Region

Department of Transport

25th July 1989

## SCHEDULE — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is at Wigton in the County of Cumbria about 3.69 kilometres in length starting at a point on the trunk road approximately 70 metres west of Church Rigg and then going north eastwards, north of and alongside the railway, to rejoin the trunk road approximately 110 metres north east of its junction with the U2066 at Smithfield Bridge.
