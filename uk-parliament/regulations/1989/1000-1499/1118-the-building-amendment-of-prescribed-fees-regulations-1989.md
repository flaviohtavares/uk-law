# The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1118

BUILDING AND BUILDINGS

The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1989

Made

4th July 1989

Laid before Parliament

11th July 1989

Coming into force

1st September 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 1, 3, 16(10), 34, 35, 50(3) and 126[^f00001] of, and paragraphs 2, 5 and 10(c) of Schedule 1 to, the Building Act 1984[^f00002], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

## 3. Transitional Provisions

These Regulations shall not affect the application of the Building (Prescribed Fees etc) Regulations 1985 in relation to work in respect of which–

(1) a building notice was given or full plans were deposited (under regulation 11 of the Building Regulations 1985[^f00004]; or

(2) an initial notice or a public body’s notice was given (under section 47(1) or 54(1) of the Building Act 1984), before 1st September 1989, notwithstanding that on or after that date a notice is or has been given, or plans are or have been deposited, in respect of substantially the same work.

## 4. Revocations

The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1988[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

Nicholas Ridley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

4th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Building (Prescribed Fees etc.) Regulations 1985 so as to increase the flat rate fees payable to a local authority.

(1) by approximately 10% in respect of the erection of small domestic buildings and connected work (Schedule 1 to the 1985 Regulations), and

(2) by approximately 30% in respect of the erection of certain garages, carports and the making of small extensions and alterations to domestic buildings (Schedule 2 to the 1985 Regulations).

Transitional provisions are contained in regulation 3. Regulation 4 revokes the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1988 which are superseded by these Regulations.
