# The Atomic Energy Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1317 (C.46)

ATOMIC ENERGY AND RADIOACTIVE SUBSTANCES

The Atomic Energy Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made

22nd July 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 7(2) of the Atomic Energy Act 1989[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Atomic Energy Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

**2.** The Atomic Energy Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

Michael Spicer

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Energy

22nd July 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Atomic Energy Act 1989 into force on 1st September 1989.
