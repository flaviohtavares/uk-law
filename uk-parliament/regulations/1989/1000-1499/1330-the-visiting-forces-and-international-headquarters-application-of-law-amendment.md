# The Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) (Amendment) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1330

DEFENCE

The Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) (Amendment) Order 1989

Made

2nd August 1989

Coming into force

2nd August 1989

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of August 1989

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has in accordance with section 8(6) of the Visiting Forces Act 1952[^f00001] been laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of the said section 8, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) (Amendment) Order 1989, and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** In article 3 (3) of the Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) Order 1965[^f00002] (which applies that Order to the visiting forces mentioned therein) for “Italy and the Federal Republic of Germany” there shall be substituted “Italy, the Federal Republic of Germany and Spain”.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) Order 1965, which provides for the application to visiting forces and international headquarters of law applicable to home forces, by adding to the visiting forces mentioned in article 3(3) of that Order those of Spain.
