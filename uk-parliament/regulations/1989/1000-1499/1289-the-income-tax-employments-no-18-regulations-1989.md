# The Income Tax (Employments) (No. 18) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1289

INCOME TAX

The Income Tax (Employments) (No. 18) Regulations 1989

Made

28th July 1989

Laid before the House of Commons

28th July 1989

Coming into force

18th August 1989

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 203 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Employments) (No. 18) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 18th August 1989.

## 2. Amendment to the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1973

In regulation 29A(4) of the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1973[^f00002] for the words “has the same meaning as in section 89 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 as from time to time increased or decreased by the Treasury in accordance with that section for the purposes of section 86 of that Act” there shall be substituted–

- “means the rate applicable under section 178 of the Finance Act 1989[^f00003] for the purposes of section 86 of the Taxes Management Act 1970[^f00004]”.

A. J. G. Isaac

L. J. H. Beighton

Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue

28th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 18th August 1989, amend regulation 29A(4) of the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1973 by substituting a reference to “the rate applicable under section 178 of the Finance Act 1989 for the purposes of section 86 of the Taxes Management Act 1970”.

The amendment is consequential upon the coming into force on 18th August 1989 of section 178 of the Finance Act 1989 and the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/1297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1297)) and of the repeal of section 89 of the Taxes Management Act 1970.
