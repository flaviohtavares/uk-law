# The Central Regional Council (Denny and Dunipace Water Supply Confirmation Act 1888) Repeal Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1498 (S.125)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Central Regional Council (Denny and Dunipace Water Supply Confirmation Act 1888) Repeal Order 1989

Made

15th August 1989

Coming into force

22nd August 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 107(1)(b) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980( [^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Central Regional Council, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Central Regional Council (Denny and Dunipace Water Supply Confirmation Act 1888) Repeal Order 1989 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1989.

**2.** The Denny and Dunipace Water Supply Confirmation Act 1888( [^f00002]) is hereby repealed.

**3.** The Central Regional Council shall at all times after the expiration of six months from the date on which this Order comes into force keep a copy thereof at the office of the Chief Executive of the Council.

Alasdair C Paton

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office, Scottish Development

Department

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

15th August 1989
