# The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in S.I. [1989/955](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/955) (c.25) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1989 No. 1032 (C.27)

COPYRIGHT

DESIGNS

RIGHTS IN PERFORMANCES

The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1989

Made

20th June 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 305(3) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001] (“the Act”), hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1989.

**2.** The Copyright, Designs and Patents Acts 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1989[^f00002] is amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Trade and Industry

20th June 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1989 by substituting for the Schedule thereto a new Schedule specifying the provisions of the Act brought into force for the purpose only of enabling subordinate legislation thereunder to be made to come into force on 1st August 1989. The majority of the provisions of the Act (including those mentioned in the Schedule substituted by this Order) come into force on that date by virtue of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989 (S.I. [1989/816](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/816) (c.21)).
