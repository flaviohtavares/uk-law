# The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1303 (C. 45)

DESIGNS

PATENTS

TRADE MARKS

The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1989

Made

27th July 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 305(3) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1989.

**2.** Section 304(4) and (6) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (“the 1988 Act”) shall come into force on 28th July 1989.

**3.** In article 2 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989[^f00002] there shall be added to the list of the provisions of Part VII of the 1988 Act which do not come into force on 1st August 1989 a reference to section 304(4) and (6).

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

27th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1989 provides for the majority of the provisions of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (including section 304(4) and (6)) to come into force on 1st August 1989. This Order amends the earlier Order and brings section 304(4) and (6) into force on 28th July 1989.
