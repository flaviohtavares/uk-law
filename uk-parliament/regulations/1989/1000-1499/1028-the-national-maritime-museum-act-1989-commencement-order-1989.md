# The National Maritime Museum Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1028 (C.26)

MUSEUMS AND GALLERIES

The National Maritime Museum Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made

19th June 1989

The Lord President of the Council, in exercise of his powers under section 3(3) of the National Maritime Museum Act 1989[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Maritime Museum Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

**2.** The National Maritime Museum Act 1989 shall come into force on 7th July 1989.

John Wakeham

Lord President of the Council

19th June 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 7th July 1989 all the provisions of the National Maritime Museum Act 1989.
