# The Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No.1) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1318 (C.47)

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No.1) Order 1989

Made

31st July 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No.1) Order 1989.

**2.** The Representation of the People Act 1989, except sections 1 to 4, shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

31st July 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st September 1989 all of the provisions of the Representation of the People Act 1989, except sections 1 to 4.
