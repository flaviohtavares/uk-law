# The Scholarships and Other Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1278

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Scholarships and Other Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

25th July 1989

Laid before Parliament

28th July 1989

Coming into force

1st September 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 81 of the Education Act 1944[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scholarships and Other Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

## 2. Amendment of the Scholarships and Other Benefits Regulations 1977

(1) In the definition of “school” in regulation 2(1) of the Scholarships and Other Benefits Regulations 1977[^f00002], for the words “the Education Acts 1944 to 1976” there shall be substituted the words “the Education Acts 1944 to 1988”.

(2) In regulation 4(a) of those Regulations, for the words “or special schools” there shall be substituted the words “, grant-maintained schools or special schools”.

Kenneth Baker

Secretary of State for Education and Science

21st July 1989

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

25th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 4(a) of the Scholarships and Other Benefits Regulations 1977 (which permits local education authorities to defray the expenses of pupils taking part in school activities) by inserting a reference to grant-maintained schools. They also update the definition of “school” in those Regulations to refer to the Education Acts 1944 to 1988.
