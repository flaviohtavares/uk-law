## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c5501b650381c7a280058a12ca218ebd][^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(1) In these Regulations– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

- “agriculture” means the growing of all types of commercial food crops, including for stock-rearing purposes, and cognate words shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “agricultural unit” means an area of agricultural land used for a single agricultural purpose, not exceeding 5 hectares; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “dedicated site” means an area of agricultural land which on 17th June 1986 was dedicated to the disposal of sludge but on which commercial food crops were being grown exclusively for animal consumption; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “the operative date” means 1st September 1989; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “sludge” means residual sludge from sewage plants treating domestic or urban waste waters and from other sewage plants treating waste waters of a composition similar to domestic and urban waste waters; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “septic tank sludge” means residual sludge from septic tanks and other similar installations for the treatment of sewage; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “sludge producer” means any person who manages a plant at which sludge is produced for disposal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “the sludge table” means the table set out in Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “the soil table” means the table set out in Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “treated sludge” means sludge or septic tank sludge which has undergone biological, chemical or heat treatment, long-term storage or any other appropriate process so as significantly to reduce its fermentability and the health hazards resulting from its use, and“untreated sludge” shall be construed accordingly; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- “use” means spreading on the soil or any other application on or in the soil, and“used” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(2) In these Regulations, references to a numbered regulation or Schedule are references to the regulation or Schedule bearing that number in these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

## 3. Prohibition on use or supply of sludge (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ee1024346228c8591f8a9dbff4fe78d7][^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(1) No person shall cause or knowingly permit sludge to be used on agricultural land unless the following requirements are fulfilled; and no person shall supply sludge for use on agricultural land if he knows or has reason to believe that the requirements of paragraph (6) below will not be fulfilled when the sludge is so used, or that the precautions set out in regulation 4 will not be observed after such use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(2) The sludge shall be tested in accordance with Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(3) The soil on the land shall be tested or assessed in accordance with Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(4) Unless the land is a dedicated site:– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

- (a) the average annual rate of addition to the land by means of the sludge of any of the elements listed in column (1) of the sludge table shall not exceed the limit (in kilograms per hectare per year) specified in column (2) thereof; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- (b) the concentration in the soil of any of the elements listed in column (1) of the soil table shall not exceed the limit specified in column (2) thereof; and where that limit is not exceeded at the time of the use, it shall not be exceeded by reason of the use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(5) The pH value of the soil shall not be less than 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(6) No fruit or vegetable crops, other than fruit trees, shall be growing or being harvested in the soil at the time of the use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(7) The sludge shall be used in such a way that account is taken of the nutrient needs of the plants and that the quality of the soil and of the surface and ground water is not impaired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

## 5. Information to be supplied to sludge producer (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6591a24a3dfaa87e5cca3f1b5b0eedf4][^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

## 6. Register to be kept by sludge producer (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-10066a8825116cc6607ecfed0ec5abaa][^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

(1) Every sludge producer shall prepare and maintain a register containing the following particulars:– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

- (a) the total quantity of sludge produced in any year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- (b) in relation to sludge supplied for the purpose of use in agriculture in any year:– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
  - (i) the total quantity of sludge supplied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] the composition and properties of that sludge as determined in accordance with Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] the quantities of treated sludge supplied, and the type of treatment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] the names and addresses of the persons to whom the sludge was supplied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
  - (v) the address and area of each agricultural unit on which sludge has been used, the quantity of sludge used thereon, and the amount of each of the elements listed in the sludge table which have been added thereto; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- (c) a copy of every analysis or assessment made under Schedule 2, or in accordance with advice given for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] regulation 8(4)[^key-be3177012d42e76adfb9a8941bddfa52] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe], relating to the soil of an agricultural unit on which sludge has been used; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
- (d) a copy of any advice issued for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] regulation 8(3)(a)[^key-a3d171dfcdd2323a0a27d143561bd638] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe].

(2) In this regulation“year” means the period from the operative date to December 31st 1989, and thereafter the period of twelve months commencing on January 1st. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

## 8. Dedicated sites (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4fba8e94833fad60e1fa6863847159a7][^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

- (i) in England, the Minister of Agriculture Fisheries and Food *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe] and the Food Standards Agency *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-fb3f0ded00b17c2f061073bc07605627] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe];

Regulation 3 prohibits sludge from sewage plants from being used in agriculture unless specified requirements are fulfilled. They include the testing of the sludge and the soil (Schedules 1 and 2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

Regulation 4 specifies precautions which must be taken after sludge from sewage plants or septic tanks is used on agricultural land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

Regulation 5 requires the occupier of land on which sludge has been used to provide the sludge producer with information about the land and the sludge used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

Regulations 6 and 7 require every sludge producer to maintain a register of the quantities of sludge produced and supplied for use in agriculture, including details of the amount of sludge used on each agricultural unit and the results of analysis of the sludge and the soil. The register is to be available to the Secretary of State for inspection, together with such information or facilities as he may reasonably require, including facilities for analysing the sludge or soil. The sludge producer is required to provide persons he supplies with the results of analysis of the sludge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

Regulation 8 makes special provision for dedicated sites, which on 17th June 1986 (the date of notification of the Directive) were dedicated to the disposal of sludge but on which commercial food crops were being grown exclusively for animal consumption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]

Regulation 9 provides an offence of contravening the Regulations, which carries a maximum fine on summary conviction of level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8bf0219c106a53369f527384360ffbe]
