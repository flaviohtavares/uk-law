# The Education (School Curriculum and Related Information) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1136

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Curriculum and Related Information) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

5th July 1989

Laid before Parliament

7th July 1989

Coming into force

31st July 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 8(5) and (7) of the Education Act 1980[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Curriculum and Related Information) (Amendment) Regulations 1989, and shall come into force on 31st July 1989.

**2.** For sub-paragraph (a) of regulation 1(1) of the Education (School Curriculum and Related Information) Regulations 1989[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraphs–

> - (a) except for regulation 3, regulations 8 and 10 insofar as they apply to pupils with statements of special educational needs, and regulations 11 and 12, on 1st August 1989;
>   - (aa) as regards regulation 3, on 1st January 1990;

.

Kenneth Baker

Secretary of State for Education and Science

5th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (School Curriculum and Related Information) Regulations 1989 so as to postpone the commencement of amendments to the Education (School Information) Regulations 1981 (S.I. [1981/630](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/630)) from 1st August 1989 until 1st January 1990.
