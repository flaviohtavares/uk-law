# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, section 57

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Rounding of fractions[^c-ad421681bf2596abad1afe86db0575a5][^c-c3487bf9f232dfd10e60010492650745]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any amount determined under these Regulations including any amount to which the members of a couple or of a polygamous marriage may be entitled may, if it is appropriate, be rounded to the nearest whole penny by disregarding any amount less than half a penny and treating any amount of half a penny or more as a whole penny.
