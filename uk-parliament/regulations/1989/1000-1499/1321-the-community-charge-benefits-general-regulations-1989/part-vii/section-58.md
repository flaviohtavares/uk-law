# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, section 58

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Increases of weekly amounts for exceptional circumstances[^key-46b037ba5e57c83db9cdcaa42b1e1deb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The appropriate authority may modify the provisions of Part VI of these Regulations (amount of benefit) so that, if it considers the circumstances of a person for whom a claim for community charge benefit has been made to be exceptional, the amount of any community charge benefit to be allowed or paid in his case[^key-2b6bda5f45f52583f6d764c907ee28ab] may be increased to an extent which does not cause the total community charge benefit to be allowed or paid to him in any week to exceed the amount of his maximum community charge benefit, calculated in respect of a day in accordance with regulation 46 (maximum community charge benefit), multiplied by 7.[^key-9e92c55d901572274c2a692c8b2079a3]
