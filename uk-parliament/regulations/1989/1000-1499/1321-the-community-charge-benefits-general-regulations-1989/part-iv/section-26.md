# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, section 26

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Modifications in respect of child and young person[^key-015473f13decceefeeb5272b9ed62a4d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the income of a child or young person , other than income consisting of any payment of maintenance whether under a court order or not,[^key-6285184d5cff242f9524e4b52b020a04] calculated in accordance with the foregoing provisions of this Part exceeds the amount included under Schedule 1 in the calculation of the claimant’s applicable amount for that child or young person by way of the personal allowance and disabled child premium, if any, the excess shall not be treated as income of the claimant.

(2) Where the capital of a child or young person, if calculated in accordance with Chapter VI in like manner as for the claimant, except where otherwise provided, would exceed £3000, any income of that child or young person shall not be treated as income of the claimant.

(3) In calculating the net earnings or net profit of a child or young person there shall be disregarded (in addition to any sum which falls to be disregarded under paragraphs 11 and 12) any sum specified in paragraphs 13 and 14 of Schedule 2.

(4) Any income of a child or young person which is to be disregarded under Schedule 3 shall be disregarded in such manner as to produce the result most favourable to the claimant.
