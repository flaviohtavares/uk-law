# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, section 29

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^key-4f8e804a2c3cc176eb009a618635cd7a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant’s family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
