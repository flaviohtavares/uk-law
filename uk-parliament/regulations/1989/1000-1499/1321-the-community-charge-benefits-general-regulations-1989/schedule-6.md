# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — CONSTITUTION OF REVIEW BOARDS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 69(3)

**1.**[^c-f91e4fd1cf9c901b560e5f228b45489e] A Review Board appointed by an authority listed in column (1) of the Table below shall consist of not less than three of the persons specified in relation to that authority in column (2) of that Table.

**Table**

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
<tr><th><em>Authority</em></th><th><em>Composition of Board</em></th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. A charging authority other than the Common Council of the City of London.</td><td>1. Councillors of that Authority.</td></tr>
<tr><td>2. The Common Council of the City of London.</td><td>2. Persons who are mayor, aldermen or common councilmen.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3. In Scotland, a levying authority or district council.</td><td>3. Councillors of that authority or council.</td></tr>
<tr><td>4. In Scotland, a Development Corporation.</td><td>4. Members of a Development Corporation established for the purposes of a new town by order made, or having effect as if made, under section 2(1) of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968.</td></tr>
<tr><td>5. Scottish Homes.</td><td>5. Members of the Scottish Homes.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^f00108]

**2.**[^c-9051dc9874b1f9481d6adc02a5d4c7d1] The members of a Review Board shall appoint one of their number to be the Chairman.
