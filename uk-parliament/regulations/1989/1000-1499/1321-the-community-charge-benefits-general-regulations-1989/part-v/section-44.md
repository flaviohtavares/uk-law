# The Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989, section 44

> Text as at 1993-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Income treated as capital[^key-0b0e3ea786f1a0f4d7084ca0b20f522c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any amount by way of a refund of tax deducted from a student’s covenant income shall be treated as capital.
