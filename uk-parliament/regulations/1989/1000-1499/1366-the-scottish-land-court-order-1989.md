# The Scottish Land Court Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1366 (S.114)

SCOTTISH LAND COURT

The Scottish Land Court Order 1989

Made

26th July 1989

Laid before Parliament

11th August 1989

Coming into force

1st September 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(3)(h) of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Scottish Land Court Order 1989, and shall come into force on 1st September 1989

**2.** The Scottish Land Court is hereby specified as a Scottish Court for the purposes of section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

26th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the Scottish Land Court as a Scottish Court for the purposes of section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895. In effect this transfers the power to set fees in the Scottish Land Court from the Land Court members to the Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury.
