# The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Extension of Relevant Period) (No. 2) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1232

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Extension of Relevant Period) (No. 2) Order 1989

Made

19th July 1989

Coming into force

4th August 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Telecommunications Act 1984 (Extension of Relevant Period) (No. 2) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 4th August 1989.

**2.** The relevant period for the purposes of paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the Telecommunications Act 1984 shall be eight years beginning with 5th August 1984.

Robert Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

19th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

Paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the Telecommunications Act 1984 (“the Act”) provides that licences to run telecommunication systems granted, or having effect as if granted, under section 15(1) of the British Telecommunications Act [1981 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/38) shall have effect as if granted under section 7 of the Act and shall continue in force until the end of the relevant period specified in that paragraph, unless before the end of the relevant period such a licence has either been revoked or has expired. Paragraph 1(7) provides that the relevant period shall be the period of two years beginning with the appointed day referred to in section 2 of the Act, or such other period beginning with that day as the Secretary of State may by order prescribe. The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Appointed Day) (No. 2) Order 1984 (S.I. [1984/876](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/876)) provided that the appointed day should be 5th August 1984.

The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Extension of Relevant Period) Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1275](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1275)) had the effect of extending the relevant period by three years.

This Order has the effect of extending the relevant period by a further three years so as to expire on 4th August 1992.
