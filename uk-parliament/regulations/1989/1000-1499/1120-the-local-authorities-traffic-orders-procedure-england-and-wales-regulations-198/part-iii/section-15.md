# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Commencement date of order

No date on which an order, or a provision of an order, comes into force shall be earlier than the date on which the authority intend the notice of the making of the order to be published in a local newspaper under regulation 16.
