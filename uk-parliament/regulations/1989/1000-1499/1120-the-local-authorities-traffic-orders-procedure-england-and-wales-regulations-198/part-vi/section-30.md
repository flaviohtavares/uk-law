# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**30.** (1) In this Part of the Regulations, “old enactment” means an enactment referred to in regulation 1(2) (revocations).

(2) Where a notice of a proposal to make an order has been published in the London Gazette before the coming into force of these Regulations pursuant to the requirements of an old enactment, the old enactment shall continue to apply to the order and nothing in these Regulations affects that enactment.

(3) Nothing in these Regulations shall be taken as prejudicing the operation of sections 16 and 17 of the Interpretation Act 1978([^f00010]) (which relates to the effect of repeals).
