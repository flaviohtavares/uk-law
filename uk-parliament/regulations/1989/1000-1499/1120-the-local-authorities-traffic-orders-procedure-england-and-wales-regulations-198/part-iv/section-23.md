# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Orders under section 30 of the 1984 Act (playgrounds in London)

Regulation 8 shall not apply to an order under section 30 of the 1984 Act (prohibition of traffic on roads to be used as playgrounds in Greater London).
