# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Consent of the Secretary of State

Where an order is one which under Part II of Schedule 9 to the 1984 Act requires the consent of the Secretary of State, the order making authority’s application to the Secretary of State for such consent shall be accompanied by copies of such of the documents specified in Schedule 4 as may be applicable.
