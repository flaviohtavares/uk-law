# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Consideration of objections

Before making an order the order making authority shall consider all objections duly made in accordance with regulation 7 or 29 and not withdrawn and also the report and recommendations (if any) of the person holding any public inquiry in connection with the order.
