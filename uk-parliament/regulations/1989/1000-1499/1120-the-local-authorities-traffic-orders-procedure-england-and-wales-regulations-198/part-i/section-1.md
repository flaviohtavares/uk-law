# The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, Commencement and Revocations

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 28th July 1989.

(2) The Greater London Street Playground (Procedures) Regulations 1972[^f00003], the Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1986[^f00004], the Control of Parking in Goods Vehicle Loading Areas Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1986[^f00005] and the London Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) Regulations 1986[^f00006] are hereby revoked.

(3) Paragraph (2) has effect subject to Part VI of these Regulations (transitional provisions).
