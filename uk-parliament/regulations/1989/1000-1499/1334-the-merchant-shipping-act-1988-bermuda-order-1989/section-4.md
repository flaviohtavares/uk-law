# The Merchant Shipping Act 1988 (Bermuda) Order 1989, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** Schedule 6 to the Merchant Shipping Act 1988 in so far as it relates to the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 shall extend to Bermuda as part of the law thereof.
