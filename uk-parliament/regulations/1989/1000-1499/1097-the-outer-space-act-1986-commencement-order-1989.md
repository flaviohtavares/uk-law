# The Outer Space Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1097 (C.33)

OUTER SPACE

The Outer Space Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made

29th June 1989

Coming into force

31st July 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Outer Space Act 1986[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Outer Space Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1989.

**2.** The day appointed for the coming into force of the Outer Space Act 1986 shall be 31st July 1989.

Robert Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

29th June 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Outer Space Act 1986 into force on the 31st July 1989.
