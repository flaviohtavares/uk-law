# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) (Revocation) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 1228

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) (Revocation) Regulations 1989

Made

12th July 1989

Coming into force

Forthwith

Whereas the Secretary of State for Transport, for a reason affecting the public interest, deemed it necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in the vicinity of Molesworth Aerodrome; and

Whereas because of changed circumstances at Molesworth Aerodrome the Secretary of State for Transport no longer deems it necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in that area:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by article 69 of the Air Navigation Order 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) (Revocation) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

R E Clarke

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

12th July 1989

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the restrictions on flying in specified airspace around Molesworth Aerodrome.
