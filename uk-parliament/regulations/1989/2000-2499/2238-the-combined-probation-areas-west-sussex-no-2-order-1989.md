# The Combined Probation Areas (West Sussex) (No.2) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2238

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (West Sussex) (No.2) Order 1989

Made

30th November 1989

Coming into force

1st December 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultations required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (West Sussex) (No.2) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st December 1989.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the West Sussex Probation Committee[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting in column 3–

- (a) “3” for “5” in the entry in respect of the petty sessional division of Chichester and District; and
- (b) “3” for “2” in the entry in respect of the petty sessional division of Mid-Sussex.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th November 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 so as to change the number of justices appointed to the West Sussex Probation Committee for some of the petty sessional divisions in West Sussex. The petty sessional division of Chichester and District will appoint 3 justices rather than 5 and Mid-Sussex will appoint 3 rather than 2.

The effect of the Order is to reduce the number of justices on the Committee from 19 to 18.
