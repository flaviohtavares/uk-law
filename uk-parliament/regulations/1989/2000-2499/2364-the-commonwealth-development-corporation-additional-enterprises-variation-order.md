# The Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) (Variation) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2364

OVERSEAS DEVELOPMENT AND CO-OPERATION

The Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) (Variation) Order 1989

Made

7th December 1989

Laid before Parliament

18th December 1989

Coming into force

8th January 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by subsections 3(3) and 3(4) of the Commonwealth Development Corporation Act 1978[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) (Variation) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 8th January 1990.

**2.** The Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987[^f00003] shall be varied by the addition to the classes of enterprises specified in article 2 thereof, of the classes of enterprises specified below—

enterprises for merchanting, wholesaling and stockholding; for the provision or improvement of factories and industrial, commercial and retail premises; and for the promotion of tourism.

**3.** This Order and the Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987 may together be cited as the Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Orders 1987 to 1989.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal

Foreign and Commonwealth Office Secretaries of State

7th December 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds enterprises for merchanting, wholesaling and stockholding, for the provision or improvement of factories, and industrial, commercial and retail premises, and for the promotion of tourism to the classes of enterprises to which the powers of the Corporation, under the Commonwealth Development Corporation Acts 1978 to 1986, relate. It varies article 2 of the Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987.
