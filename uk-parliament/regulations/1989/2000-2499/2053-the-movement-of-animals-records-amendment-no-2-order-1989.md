# The Movement of Animals (Records) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2053

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Movement of Animals (Records) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989

Made

2nd November 1989

Coming into force

23rd November 1989

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 72 and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Movement of Animals (Records) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 23rd November 1989.

## 2. Amendment

In article 2(3) (interpretation) of the Movement of Animals (Records) Order 1960[^f00002] after the words “article 16(1) of the Tuberculosis (England and Wales) Order 1984” there shall be inserted the words “or article 16(1) of the Tuberculosis (Scotland) Order 1984[^f00003], as appropriate,”.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 5th October 1989.

(L.S.)

John Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Sanderson of Bowden

Minister of State, Scottish Office

10th October 1989

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

2nd November 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Movement of Animals (Records) Order 1960. It provides that the references in the required form of movement record to an ear-mark or an ear-tag applied to bovine animals shall include a reference to any mark or other means of identification applied for the purposes of the Tuberculosis (Scotland) Order 1984 as well as to any mark or other means of identification applied for the purposes of the Tuberculosis (England and Wales) Order 1984 (S.I. [1924/1943](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1924/1943)) (article 2).
