# The Air Navigation Order 1989, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Exemption from Order

The Authority may exempt from any of the provisions of this Order (other than articles 67, 88, 89, 92, 93 or 105 thereof) or any regulations made thereunder, any aircraft or persons or classes of aircraft or persons, either absolutely or subject to such conditions as it thinks fit.
