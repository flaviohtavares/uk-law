# The Air Navigation Order 1989, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Wearing of survival suits by crew

Each member of the crew of an aircraft registered in the United Kingdom shall wear a survival suit if such a suit is required by article 13 of this Order to be carried.
