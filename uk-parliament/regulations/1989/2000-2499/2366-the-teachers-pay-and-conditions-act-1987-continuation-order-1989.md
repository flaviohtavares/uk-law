# The Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987 (Continuation) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2366

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987 (Continuation) Order 1989

Made

14th December 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6(2) of the Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which has been laid before Parliament pursuant to section 6(4) of that Act and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987 (Continuation) Order 1989.

**2.** The Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987 shall not expire on 31st March 1990 but shall continue in force for a further year.

John MacGregor

Secretary of State for Education and Science

14th December 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 6 of the Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1987 provides that that Act shall expire on 31st March 1990 unless continued in force by an order made by the Secretary of State. This Order continues that Act in force for a further year.
