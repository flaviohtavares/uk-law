# The Local Statutory Provisions (Postponement of Repeal) (Scotland) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2223 (S.146)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Statutory Provisions (Postponement of Repeal) (Scotland) Order 1989

Made

28th November 1989

Laid before Parliament

8th December 1989

Coming into force

31st December 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 225(6)(b) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973( [^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Statutory Provisions (Postponement of Repeal) (Scotland) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 31st December 1989.

**2.** In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973;
- “local statutory provision” means any local statutory provision to which section 225(6) of the Act applies.

**3.** The date on which all local statutory provisions applying to the whole or any part of the local government areas specified in the Schedule to this Order, so far as they apply, are to cease to have effect under section 225(6) of the Act is hereby postponed to 31st December 1990.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th November 1989

## SCHEDULE

Article 3

- Strathclyde Region
- City of Dundee District
- City of Edinburgh District

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 225(6) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 as amended provides that all local statutory provisions to which the subsection applies shall cease to have effect at the end of 1984. Previous Orders (S.I. [1984/1926](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/1926), [1985/1629](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/1629), [1986/2034](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2034), [1987/2090](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2090) and [1988/2106](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/2106)) have postponed until the end of 1989 the date on which such local statutory provisions applying to the local government areas or parts thereof listed in the Schedules to those Orders were to cease to have effect. This Order further postpones that date until the end of 1990 in respect of those local government areas listed in the Schedule to this Order.
