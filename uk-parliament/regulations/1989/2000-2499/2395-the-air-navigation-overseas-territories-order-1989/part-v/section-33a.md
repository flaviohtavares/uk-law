# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 1989, section 33A

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33A. Wearing of survival suits by crew

Each member of the crew of an aircraft registered in the Territory shall wear a survival suit if such a suit is required by Article 13 of this Order to be carried.
