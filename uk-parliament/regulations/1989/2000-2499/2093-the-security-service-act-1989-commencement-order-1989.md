# The Security Service Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2093 (C.64)

SECURITY SERVICE

The Security Service Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made

10th November 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 7(2) of the Security Service Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Security Service Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

**2.** The Security Service Act 1989 shall come into force on 18th December 1989.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

10th November 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 18th December 1989 all the provisions of the Security Service Act 1989.
