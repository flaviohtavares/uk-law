# The Finance Act 1985 (Serious Misdeclaration and Interest on Tax) (Appointed Days) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2270 (C.69)

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Finance Act 1985 (Serious Misdeclaration and Interest on Tax) (Appointed Days) Order 1989

Made

4th December 1989

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 14(8) and 18(10) of the Finance Act 1985[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1985 (Serious Misdeclaration and Interest on Tax) (Appointed Days) Order 1989.

**2.** Section 14 of the Finance Act 1985 shall come into operation on 31st March 1990.

**3.** Section 18 of the Finance Act 1985 shall come into operation on 1st April 1990.

John Taylor

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th December 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 14 and 18 of the Finance Act 1985. Both sections will apply to all prescribed accounting periods which begin on, or after, 1st April 1990.
