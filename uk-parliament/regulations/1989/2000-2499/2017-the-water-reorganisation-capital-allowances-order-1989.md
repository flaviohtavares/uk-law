# The Water Reorganisation (Capital Allowances) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2017

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Water Reorganisation (Capital Allowances) Order 1989

Made

1st November 1989

Coming into force

15th November 1989

The Secretary of State for Wales, in relation to the successor company nominated by him, and the Secretary of State for the Environment, in relation to the other successor companies, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 95(1) and (2) of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby make the following Order:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Water Reorganisation (Capital Allowances) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 15th November 1989.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Water Act 1989.

## 2. Writing-down allowances – industrial buildings and structures

(1) The amount specified in column (2) of Part I or Part II of Schedule 1 to this Order in relation to a successor company (being a company wholly owned by the Crown) shall be taken, for the purposes of subsection (3) of section 2 of the Capital Allowances Act 1968[^f00002] (writing-down allowances), to be the residue on the transfer date of expenditure in relation to which the property vested in that company in accordance with a scheme under Schedule 2 to the Act and described in column (1) of the said Part I or Part II is a relevant interest for the purposes of the said section 2.

(2) The period specified in column (3) of Part I or Part II of Schedule 1 in relation to a successor company shall be treated, for the purposes of the said section 2, and in relation to the property vested and described as mentioned in paragraph (1), as the part of the period of 25 or, as the case may be, 50 years which is unexpired on the transfer date.

## 3. Writing-down allowances – machinery and plant

For the purposes of section 95(2) of the Act, each successor company (being a company wholly owned by the Crown) shall be treated as having incurred on the transfer date capital expenditure of the amount specified in relation to that company in column (2) of Schedule 2 to this Order in respect of the deemed acquisition for the purposes for which they are used by that company on and after that date of the motor cars, plant and machinery vested in it in accordance with a scheme under Schedule 2 to the Act and described in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Order.

Section 2(3) of the Capital Allowances Act 1968 provides for the calculation of the writing-down allowance in respect of a period which ends after the sale of the relevant interest in an industrial building or structure by reference to the residue of certain expenditure. The writing-down allowance for a chargeable period is the residue reduced in the proportion which the length of the chargeable period bears to the unexpired part, at the date of sale, of the period of 25 or, in certain cases, 50 years beginning with the date on which the building or structure was first used.

Article 2(1) of this Order and Schedule 1 specify for the purposes of section 2 of the 1968 Act the amount that is to be taken as the residue in relation to industrial buildings and structures which vested on the transfer date (1st September 1989), in accordance with schemes made under Schedule 2 to the Water Act 1989, in companies which succeeded the water authorities.

Article 2(2) and Schedule 1 specify in relation to those buildings and structures the period which is to be treated as the unexpired part of the period of 25 or, as the case may be, 50 years.

Article 3 of the Order and Schedule 2 specify the amount of capital expenditure that the successor companies are deemed to have incurred on the transfer date in respect of machinery and plant which vested in them in accordance with Schedule 2 schemes. Those amounts are relevant for the purpose of calculating the capital allowances in respect of machinery and plant for which Chapter I of Part III of the Finance Act 1971 provides.
