# The East Surrey Water (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2101

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The East Surrey Water (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1989

Made

14th November 1989

Laid before Parliament

20th November 1989

Coming into force

11th December 1989

Whereas provision is made for the constitution and regulation of the East Surrey Water Company (“the Company”) in local statutory provisions (“the local statutory provisions”):

And whereas the Company holds an appointment as a water undertaker under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] (“the Act”):

And whereas the local statutory provisions would, if the Company became a registered water company[^f00002], have effect in accordance with paragraph 5 of Schedule 21 to the Companies Act 1985[^f00003]:

And whereas the Company has by a special resolution passed on 10th October 1989 (“the special resolution”) proposed that provision contained in a memorandum and articles described in Schedule 1 to this Order (“the memorandum and articles”), copies of which have been certified as such by the Secretary of State, shall have effect in substitution for the local statutory provisions (“the proposal”):

And whereas it appears to the Secretary of State that the requirements set out in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of section 101(3) of the Act are satisfied as respects the proposal:

And whereas it appears to the Secretary of State appropriate in consequence of his approval of the proposal by this Order that certain provisions of the East Surrey Water Acts and Orders 1862 to 1987 be repealed:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 101(2), (6) and (7) of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the East Surrey Water (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 11th December 1989.

(2) In this Order –

- “the 1845 Act” means the Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845[^f00004]; and
- “the 1863 Act” means the Companies Clauses Act 1863[^f00005].

## 2. Approval of proposal contained in the special resolution

The proposal contained in the special resolution is hereby approved for the purposes of section 101(2) of the Act.

## 3. Cessation of local statutory provisions

The date on which the local statutory provisions shall cease to have effect and the memorandum and articles shall come into force is 11th December 1989.

## 4. Consequential provision

The provisions of the East Surrey Water Acts and Orders 1862 to 1987 mentioned in Schedule 2 hereto shall be repealed to the extent indicated in that Schedule with effect from the date mentioned in article 3.

Chris Patten

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

14th November 1989

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 101(2) of the Water Act 1989 provides for the replacement of provisions relating to the constitution and regulation of a statutory water company and contained in its local Acts and Orders by provisions contained in a memorandum and articles of association which have been approved by special resolution of the members of the company. The replacement provisions have effect only if they have been approved by the Secretary of State and on such date as he determines.

This Order approves a proposal by the East Surrey Water Company that a memorandum and articles of association should have effect in substitution for provisions contained in the East Surrey Water Acts and Orders 1862 to 1987 (article 2). It also provides that the date on which those provisions cease to have effect is 11th December 1989 (article 3) and for the repeal of the superseded provisions and for consequential repeals (article 4).

This Order is associated with the company’s proposal to convert to the status of a registered company under the Companies Act 1985.
