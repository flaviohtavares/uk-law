# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2310 (S.152)

HOUSING, SCOTLAND

The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1989

Made

7th December 1989

Laid before Parliament

12th December 1989

Coming into force

26th January 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 26th January 1990.

## 2. General fund contribution limit

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which each of the local authorities listed in Column 1 of the Schedule hereto may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 1990-91 shall not exceed the amount set out in relation to that authority in Column 2 thereof.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh
