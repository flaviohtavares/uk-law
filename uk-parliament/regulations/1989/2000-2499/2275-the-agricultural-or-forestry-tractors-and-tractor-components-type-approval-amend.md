# The Agricultural or Forestry Tractors and Tractor Components (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2275

TRACTORS

The Agricultural or Forestry Tractors and Tractor Components (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made

3rd December 1989

Laid before Parliament

6th December 1989

Coming into force

31st December 1989

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to any type approval scheme for agricultural or forestry tractors and their components, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:–

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Agricultural or Forestry Tractors and Tractor Components (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 31st December 1989.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Agricultural or Forestry Tractors and Tractor Components (Type Approval) Regulations 1988[^f00003].

## 3. Cancellation or suspension of type approval certificates where tractors or tractor components are not manufactured in conformity with type approval requirements or component type approval requirements

For regulation 13 of the principal Regulations there shall be substituted–

> **13.** (1) If the Minister–
>
> - (a) after examination of at least two tractors or tractor components, being tractors or tractor components of a particular type in respect of which a type approval certificate or a component type approval certificate is in force and, in the case of tractors, in respect of each of which a certificate of conformity is in force and, in the case of tractor components, each of which bears a component type approval mark, is satisfied that each of those tractors or tractor components has not been manufactured so as to conform with the relevant aspects of design, construction, equipment or marking, or
> - (b) is notified by the competent authority of any other member State that any such tractor or tractor component as may be specified by that authority has not been manufactured so as to conform with the relevant aspects of design, construction, equipment or marking, after such examination as aforesaid, and the Minister is similarly so satisfied,
>
> he shall give notice of that fact to the manufacturer specifying–
>
> - (i) the particular respects in which the tractors or tractor components in question have been found (either by the Minister, or, as the case may be, the said competent authority) not so to conform,
> - (ii) the steps that shall be taken by the manufacturer to ensure that tractors or tractor components of that type are manufactured by him so as to conform with the said aspects and the period within which those steps shall be taken, and
> - (iii) that unless those steps are taken within that period the type approval certificate or component type approval certificate may be cancelled or suspended,
>
> - and if at any time after the expiration of that period, the Minister (whether or not after any, or any further, examination of any such tractor or tractor components) is not satisfied that all the steps so specified have been taken, he may by notice under regulation 15 cancel or suspend the type approval certificate or component type approval certificate.
>
> (2) A copy of any notice given to a manufacturer under paragraph (1) of this regulation shall, within a period of one month from the date on which it is given, be sent by the Minister to the competent authority of every other member State.

.

## 4. Notice of cancellation, suspension or modification of type approval certificates or component type approval certificates

In regulation 15(1)(a) of the principal Regulations after the word “issued,” there shall be inserted–

“setting out the remedies available to the manufacturer under regulation 16 and of the time limits allowed for the exercise of such remedies,”.

## 5. List of Community Directives

For Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations there shall be substituted the provisions contained in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply throughout the United Kingdom and come into force on 31st December 1989, amend the Agricultural or Forestry Tractors and Tractor Components (Type Approval) Regulations 1988 (the principal Regulations). They implement Commission Directive [88/465/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0465) (OJ No. L228, 17.8.88, p.31) and Council Directive [89/173/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0173) (OJ No. L67, 10.3.89, p.1) and make certain other amendments to the principal Regulations.

A printing error in Regulation 13 of the principal Regulations is corrected to make clear that the Minister must notify the manufacturer where the facts are as in either paragraph (1)(a) or (1)(b) of that regulation (Regulation 3).

Regulation 15 of the principal Regulations is amended to provide that the Minister must give notice of the remedies available to the manufacturer and of the time limits for their exercise (Regulation 4).

A new Schedule 2 is substituted which–

- (a) adds a reference to Commission Directive [88/465/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0465) in the entry relating to drivers' seats;
- (b) adds Council Directive [89/173/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0173) relating to dimensions and towable masses, speed governors and protection of drive components, projections and wheels, windscreens and other glazing, mechanical couplings, location and fixing of statutory plates and inscriptions, and brake control; and
- (c) makes additions to the listed Community Directives.
