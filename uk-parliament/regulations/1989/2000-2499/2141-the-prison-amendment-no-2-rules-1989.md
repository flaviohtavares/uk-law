# The Prison (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1989

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1989 No. 2141

PRISONS

The Prison (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1989

Made

20th November 1989

Laid before Parliament

27th November 1989

Coming into force

18th December 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 47 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], I hereby make the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1989 and shall come into force on 18th December 1989.

**2.** The Prison Rules (1964)[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in rule 43(2)[^f00003] (removal from association) –
  - (i) for the words “24 hours” there shall be substituted the words “3 days”;
  - (ii) the words “by virtue of a sentence of detention in a young offender institution” shall be omitted;
- (b) in rule 50(2)[^f00004] (governor’s punishments) there shall be added at the end the words, “but, in the case of forfeiture of remission of sentence, the total period forfeited shall not exceed 28 days.”.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

20th November 1989

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Prison Rules 1964, as amended, in two respects.

Rule 2(a) extends the period during which a prisoner can be removed from association with other prisoners without the authority of a member of the board of visitors or of the Secretary of State from 24 hours to three days. It also amends the position with respect to persons aged under 21 who are removed from association with such authority.

Rule 2(b) limits to 28 days the total period of forfeiture of remission of sentence where the governor punishes a prisoner for the commission of more than one offence against discipline arising out of an incident.
