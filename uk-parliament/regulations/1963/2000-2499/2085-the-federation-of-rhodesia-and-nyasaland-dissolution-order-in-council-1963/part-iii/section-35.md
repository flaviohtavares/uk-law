# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Procedure of Higher Authority

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part of this Order and of any other law, the Higher Authority shall determine its own procedure.

(2) No decision of the Higher Authority shall have effect unless it is unanimous.
