# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Interpretation

In this Part of this Order, except where the context otherwise requires—

- “**the Corporation**” means the Central African Power Corporation constituted by section 36;
- “**the Higher Authority**” means the Higher Authority constituted by section 33;
- “**the Territories**” means Southern Rhodesia and Northern Rhodesia, and “**Territory**” shall be construed accordingly;
- “**undertaking**” means any undertaking which generates, imports and either additionally or alternatively transmits, distributes, or supplies electricity.
