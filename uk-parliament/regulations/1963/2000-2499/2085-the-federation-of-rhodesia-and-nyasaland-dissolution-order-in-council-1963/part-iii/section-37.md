# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Member-ship of Corporation

(1) The Corporation shall consist of a Chairman, who shall be appointed on the first occasion by the Governments of Northern and Southern Rhodesia jointly and thereafter by the Higher Authority, and seven other members of whom three shall be appointed by the Government of Northern Rhodesia, three by the Government of Southern Rhodesia and one, who shall be a person acceptable to the Common-wealth Development Corporation, by the Higher Authority.

(2) The Governments of the Territories shall each designate one of the three members appointed by them to be Deputy Chairmen of the Corporation.

(3) Each member of the Corporation shall be paid out of the funds of the Corporation such remuneration and allowances, if any, and, subject to the provisions of this Part of this Order, shall have such other conditions of service, as the Higher Authority may determine.
