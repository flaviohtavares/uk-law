# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Regulations to adapt existing law

The Governor of the Bank of Rhodesia and Nyasaland, with the concurrence of the three Ministers named in section 68, may, by regulations made before the dissolution of the Federation, make with effect from the dissolution such modifications or adaptations in the Acts specified in section 68 as appear necessary or expedient in consequence of the provisions of this Order.
