# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Capital of Bank

The capital stock of the Bank of Rhodesia and Nyasaland held by the Federal Government shall on the dissolution of the Federation be held by the Liquidating Agency or, in the event of the Liquidating Agency ceasing to exist before the date upon which the Bank of Rhodesia and Nyasaland ceases to exist, by some other person or body appointed by the Committee of Ministers.
