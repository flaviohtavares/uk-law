# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Power of Legislature of Territory to amend sections 71 and 72

The provisions of sections 71 and 72 may, as respects Southern Rhodesia or Northern Rhodesia, be amended or revoked by a law of the Legislature of the Territory.
