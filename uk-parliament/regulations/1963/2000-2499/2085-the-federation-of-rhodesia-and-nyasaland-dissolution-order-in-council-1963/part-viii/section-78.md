# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963.

(2) Save as provided in subsection (3), this Order shall come into operation immediately before 1st January 1964.

(3) Sections 2 (except subsection (1)) and 17(2), the provisions of Part III except sections 42, 43 and 45, the provisions of Part IV except sections 48(2), 56, 57 and 59, and section 69 shall come into operation on 21st December 1963.
