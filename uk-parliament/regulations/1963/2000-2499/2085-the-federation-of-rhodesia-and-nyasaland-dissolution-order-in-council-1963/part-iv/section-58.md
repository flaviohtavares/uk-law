# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Power of Corporation to execute certain instruments

Notwithstanding any other provisions in this Order, the Corporation may, before 1st January 1964, enter into any agreement, execute any instrument or do any other thing which it deems necessary to enable it to assume its functions on that date.
