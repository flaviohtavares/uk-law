# The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (Dissolution) Order in Council 1963, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Pending legal proceedings

(1) In any legal proceedings pending on the dissolution of the Federation there shall be made such substitution of one party for another as may be necessary to take account of any transfer by this Order of functions, rights, liabilities or obligations.

(2) Any appeal brought after the dissolution of the Federation against a decision given in any legal proceedings before the dissolution of the Federation may be brought by or against the party who should, by virtue of subsection (1), have been the appellant or respondent if the proceedings had continued after the dissolution of the Federation.
