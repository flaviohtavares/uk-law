# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**14.** (1) Subject to paragraph (2) of this regulation, linear measures shall be stamped near one end or, in the case of sub-divided measures, near the beginning of the scale on each graduated side.

(2) In the case of linked measures and riband and tape measures, the stamp may be placed on a metal label or disc permanently attached to the measure or on the handle thereof.
