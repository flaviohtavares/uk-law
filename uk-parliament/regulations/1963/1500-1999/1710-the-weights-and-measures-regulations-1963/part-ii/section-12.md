# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**12.** Linked measures and riband or tape measures shall be tested when subjected to a tension or pull as follows:—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| (*a*) Riband or tape measures made of material other than metal | 2 pounds; |
| (*b*) Riband or tape measures made of metal | 10 pounds; |
| (*c*) Linked measures | 15 pounds; |

and the measures under test shall be supported throughout its whole length on a plane and even base.
