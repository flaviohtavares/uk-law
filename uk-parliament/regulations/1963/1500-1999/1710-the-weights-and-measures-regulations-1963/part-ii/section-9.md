# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**9.** (1) Linear measures shall be made of steel, brass, aluminium alloys, ivory, laminated bakelite, reinforced fibreglass, hard wood or woven tape, or of any other material approved by the Board.

(2) Such measures of a maximum purported value of 2 feet or more and made of wood shall have both ends tipped with metal and the tips shall be riveted through the wood.
