# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**53.** The inspector shall not test any weight unless his testing equipment is—

- (a) balanced in true equipoise;
- (b) free from any influences likely in his opinion to affect its accuracy.
