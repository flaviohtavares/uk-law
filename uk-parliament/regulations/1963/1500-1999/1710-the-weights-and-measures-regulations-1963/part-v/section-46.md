# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**46.** (1) Weights shall have their purported values conspicuously, legibly and durably marked thereon, either in full or by means of one of the following abbreviations only:—

- lb
- oz
- dr
- gr
- oz.tr
- oz.apoth
- dwt
- kg
- kilog
- kilogram
- gram
- g
- milligram
- mg
- C.M.:

provided that—

- (a) during a period of 5 years from the date of the coming into operation of these Regulations, metric weights which were first stamped prior to the said date may be marked with one of the following abbreviations:—
  - grm
  - dg
  - cg
- (b) apothecaries weights may be marked by means of one of the following symbols:—

  iv (4 drachms) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000002) ij (2 drachms) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000003) i (1 drachm) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000004) ij (2 scruples) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000005) *fs* (1½ scruples) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000006) i (1 scruple) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000007) *fs* (½ scruples) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1963/1710/images/go0000008)

(2) If the maker's name is stated on any weight, it shall be in letters not exceeding half the size of the letters or numerals indicating the purported value of that weight.
