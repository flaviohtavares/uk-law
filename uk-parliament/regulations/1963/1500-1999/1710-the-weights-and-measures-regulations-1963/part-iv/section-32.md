# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**32.** Dry capacity measures of a maximum purported value not exceeding 1 bushel (8 gallons) shall be of cylindrical shape, with the internal diameter approximately equal either to the depth or to twice the depth, the difference between the internal diameter and the depth being not greater than 5 per cent. of the depth or twice the depth respectively.
