# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**112.** If a movable hutch, barrow, frame or bucket is used instead of an ordinary platform on platform weighing machines and weighbridges, it shall form an essential part of the instrument without which the instrument cannot be balanced.
