# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**122.** (1) On platform weighing machines and weighbridges fitted with dials, the stamp shall be placed on the plug or stud provided for that purpose on the housing of the instrument.

(2) On platform weighing machines and weighbridges not fitted with dials, the stamp shall be placed on the said plug or stud in a conspicuous position either on the shoulder or on the opposite end of the steelyard indicator.
