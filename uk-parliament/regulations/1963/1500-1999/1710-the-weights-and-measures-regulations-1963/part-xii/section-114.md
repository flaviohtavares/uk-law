# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**114.** In the case of small portable platform weighing machines for use in the weighing of coal and commonly known as bob-up weighing machines, the counterpoises shall not be threaded on to a pin rigidly attached to one end of the main lever, but shall either be used in a tray or pan suspended from a knife-edge bearing or be placed on a loose shackle.
