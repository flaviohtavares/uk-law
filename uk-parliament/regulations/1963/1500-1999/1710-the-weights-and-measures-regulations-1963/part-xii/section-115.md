# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**115.** The balancing arrangement for platform weighing machines and weightbridges to compensate for daily wear and tear shall have a range not exceeding one-half per cent. of the capacity of the instrument and not less than one-eighth per cent. in each direction, and it shall be securely attached to the instrument and actuated by a detachable key.
