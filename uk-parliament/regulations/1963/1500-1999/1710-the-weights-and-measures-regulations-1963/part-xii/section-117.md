# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**117.** Platform weighing machines and weighbridges for use in weighing in units of both the imperial system and the metric system shall bear a clear inscription to that effect.
