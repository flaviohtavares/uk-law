# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**72.** No beam scale shall—

- (a) if constructed with swan-neck ends, be of a capacity of 7 pounds or less or have a beam under 16 inches in length;
- (b) be fitted with loaded weight pans;
- (c) if of a capacity of less than 2 hundredweight, be fitted with wooden scale boards.
