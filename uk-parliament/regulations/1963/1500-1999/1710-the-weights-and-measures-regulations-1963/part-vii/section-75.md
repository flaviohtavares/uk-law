# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**75.** All beam scales shall be indelibly marked either with the inscription “Class B” or with the inscription “Class C”.
