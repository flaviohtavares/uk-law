# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**19.** No liquid capacity measure shall be so constructed that—

- (a) it has a false bottom; or
- (b) it does not completely empty when titled to an angle of 120 degrees from the vertical.
