# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**28.** (1) Liquid capacity measures of maximum purported values between 8 gallons and one-sixth of a gill inclusive, shall be tested by transferring water from the local or working standard into the measure under test.

(2) Measures—

- (a) with a lip or rim, shall be tested to the bottom of the lip or rim;
- (b) on which the purported value is defined by a line, shall be tested to the top of the line by taking the level of the water at the bottom of the meniscus.
