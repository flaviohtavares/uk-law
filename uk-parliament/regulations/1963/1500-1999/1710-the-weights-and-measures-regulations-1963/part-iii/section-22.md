# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**22.** Dipping measures made of metal of a maximum purported value not exceeding half a gallon for use for the sale of milk shall be of circular or elliptical section with vertical sides, and the height shall not be more or less by 10 per cent. than 1½ times the mean diameter of the section.
