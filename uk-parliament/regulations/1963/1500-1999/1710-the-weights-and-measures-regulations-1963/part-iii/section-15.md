# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**15.** Liquid capacity measures shall be made of aluminium alloys, copper, copper alloys, earthenware, enamelled-metal, glass, nickel alloys, plated, tinned or galvanised iron or steel, stainless steel, tin alloys, urea formaldehyde plastic or vulcanite, or of any other material approved by the Board.
