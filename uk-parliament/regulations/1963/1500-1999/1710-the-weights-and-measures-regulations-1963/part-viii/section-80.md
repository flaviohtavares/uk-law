# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**80.** (1) Where the beam of a counter machine has two side members, they shall be connected together by not less than two cross bars; and the supports for the pans shall be of suitably rigid structure, such as crosses strengthened by straps.

(2) The centre forks of counter machines shall be so fixed that they cannot twist or get out of place.
