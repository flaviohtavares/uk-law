# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**95.** Spring balances may be tested for efficiency or ability to recover by leaving on them for a period not exceeding 24 hours, a load equal to the capacity of the instrument; and then, after the expiration of a further period of 4 hours, by testing for accuracy.
