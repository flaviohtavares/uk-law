# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**91.** Where spring balances are provided with an adjustable pointer, the range of adjustment shall not exceed 1 per cent, of the capacity of the instrument, except that in the case of spring balances for use at a coal mine it shall not exceed 2 per cent. of the said capacity.
