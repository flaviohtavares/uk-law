# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**89.** Where the graduations commence at any point of the scale or dial other than at the zero indication, the position of the pointer when there is no load shall be clearly indicated by a zero mark.
