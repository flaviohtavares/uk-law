# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**90.** Spring balances of capacities between 1 pound and 100 pounds inclusive shall only be made in capacities corresponding to those specified in the first column of the table contained in regulation 88.
