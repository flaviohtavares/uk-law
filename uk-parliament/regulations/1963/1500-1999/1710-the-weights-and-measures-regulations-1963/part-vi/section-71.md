# The Weights and Measures Regulations 1963, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**71.** In testing weighing instruments fitted with a price computing mechanism, the inspector shall in addition to testing at each numbered graduation satisfy himself that—

- (a) they indicate the price correctly; and
- (b) they comply with the requirements of these Regulations in so far as they are applicable to the particular type, class or description of weighing instrument concerned.
