# The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010 (Amendment) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2010/2317 (C. 112)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2317) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2010 No. 2337 (C. 113)

Equality

The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010 (Amendment) Order 2010

Made

22nd September 2010

Coming into force

1st October 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 207(4) and 216(3) of the Equality Act 2010[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010 (Amendment) Order 2010.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st October 2010 at the same time as the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of transitory provisions relating to ships and hovercraft

After paragraph (4) of article 11 of the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010, insert—

> (5) Despite their amendment by article 24 and Schedule 8, the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004[^f00003] continue to apply in the form immediately before their amendment by those provisions, so far as they relate to work on ships, work on hovercraft and seafarers, until regulations under section 81 of the 2010 Act come into force.

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under the Equality Act [2010 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15) (“the Act”). It amends the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 4, Savings, Consequential, Transitional, Transitory and Incidental Provisions and Revocation) Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2317 (C. 112)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2317)) (“Commencement Order No. 4”). Commencement Order No. 4 brings the majority of the Act into force on 1st October 2010 and makes various savings, consequential, transitional, transitory and incidental provisions and a revocation. These savings and other provisions are made as a result of the repeal and revocation of previous discrimination legislation and its replacement by the Act, and of the lapse of previous subordinate legislation because of the repeal of previous discrimination legislation by that Act.

This Order inserts a provision as new paragraph (5) of article 11, which saves the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1861](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1861)) in the form in which they stand immediately prior to their amendment by article 24 of, and Schedule 8 to, Commencement Order No. 4. These are saved only so far as they relate to work on ships, work on hovercraft and seafarers, and only until regulations under section 81 of the Act come into force.
