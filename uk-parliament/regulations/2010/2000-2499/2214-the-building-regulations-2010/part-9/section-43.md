## 43. Pressure testing[^c-17c3358a87d1d69cfcd3e16427ec1957][^c-994c7301a6427554d62da43578bc0f0b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to the erection of a building in relation to which paragraph L1(a)(i) of Schedule 1 imposes a requirement.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the person carrying out the work shall, for the purpose of ensuring compliance with regulation 26 [^c-7cbd68eb5fce595ff54511a4ffbe6155]and regulation 26A[^c-a862bd8d4dc11aabb9f7622c6e802f23] and paragraph L1(a)(i) of Schedule 1—

- (a) ensure that—
  - (i) pressure testing is carried out in such circumstances as are approved by the Secretary of State; and
  - (ii) the testing is carried out in accordance with a procedure approved by the Secretary of State; and
- (b) subject to paragraph (5), give notice of the results of the testing to the [^c-5425aa964a6c85c220654d13c495688a]building control authority *(Extent: E)*[^c-a78f078790471b80d14ed02a0ae2456d].

(3) The notice referred to in paragraph (2)(b) shall—

- (a) record the results and the data upon which they are based in a manner approved by the Secretary of State; and
- (b) be given to the [^c-3a2cf8cce8cb7751ba7e6017e5da118b]building control authority *(Extent: E)*[^c-57113565215a7a801bfc6529a3a43b3d] not later than seven days after the final test is carried out.

(4) A [^c-c1ba8fc5809fb54c36ea66dcb0f943a0]building control authority *(Extent: E)*[^c-f2c9a626069f8038df3edca072904fd4] are authorised to accept, as evidence that the requirements of paragraph (2)(a)(ii) have been satisfied, a certificate to that effect by a person who is registered by [^c-98668e71536c5dbb678584619c3cf017][^c-56305837f0f2ce9afe7db2093b6e7aa8]... [^c-eebb3c7a2b4abf11a01c097eb8657063]Elmhurst Energy Systems Limited *(Extent: E)*[^c-c4ebce0b6087e166e031b9cf4e0ab2e6] [^c-a28f392f5747e0e30ae70da43e246d6b]or the Air Tightness Testing and Measurement Association[^c-c78ef5e9a3f0cdad0949337e5f33cbed] in respect of pressure testing for the air tightness of buildings.

(5) Where such a certificate contains the information required by paragraph (3)(a), paragraph (2)(b) does not apply.

*Extent: E*

(6)[^c-d02b6a6ef674ace4424ca92a2e5d928b] Where the regulator is the building control authority by virtue of section 91ZB of the Act (the regulator: building control authority for other work), it must send a copy of each notice or certificate it receives under this regulation in relation to a building to the local authority for the area in which the building is situated.

*Extent: W*

(6)[^c-1627ef38f164eaa3fa450b8b5001d3a2] Where the building control authority is a designated local authority under section 91ZD of the Act, it must send a copy of each notice or certificate it receives under this regulation in relation to a building to the local authority for the area in which the building is situated.
