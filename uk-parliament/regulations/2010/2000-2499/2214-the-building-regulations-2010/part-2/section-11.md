## 11. Power to dispense with or relax requirements

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), the power under section 8(1) of the Act to dispense with or relax any requirement contained in these Regulations shall be exercisable by the local authority.

(2) Any notification by the [^key-7b39a0ba8147cd2bf2e0fd32acea618a]building control authority *(Extent: E)*[^key-e3b2e9dc78f31a75b4a98f40ba2ff170] to an applicant that they have refused the applicant's application to dispense with or relax any requirement of these Regulations shall inform the applicant of the effect of section 39(1) and (3) of the Act (appeal against refusal etc. to relax building regulations).

(3)[^key-d6967bb596dcdee7495698cd12bc3ce9][^key-dea42d2641e8aa17e7cb41c62a62edcf] Sub-sections (1) to (5) of section 8 of the Act (relaxation of building regulations) do not apply to regulations 23(1)(a), 25A, 25B, [^key-d8cae4f7e759357d76d591f23ff8b84a]and[^key-14ba212fddef45729865363727356dc3] 26 [^key-632535902709999dfd70bc77298010bf][^key-43165777b508d866ae0bf2c240a1f15f]... [^key-26b64156bd89450956e24af4ad64b66e]and paragraph R1[^key-3d9e5fcedce1ded8c36cb287c5cd1e33] (in-building physical infrastructure for high-speed electronic communications networks) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91713c4cf19cafa2e0f169e2c5d06e4b] of Schedule 1.
