## 8. Limitation on requirements

*Extent: E+W*

Parts A to D, F to K, [^c-01411e5518143e556667348755d03545], N[^c-82c553711ca10de8ac0d23a275296fea] and P (except for paragraphs G2, H2 and J7) of Schedule 1 shall not require anything to be done except for the purpose of securing reasonable standards of health and safety for persons in or about buildings (and any others who may be affected by buildings, or matters connected with buildings).
