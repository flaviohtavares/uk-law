## 34. Application of building regulations to educational buildings, buildings of statutory undertakers and Crown Buildings[^c-ae2af8b9455733dd625882e09e4aa3d0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations 11(3),[^c-a41f55912cbbae46babd86b056833054] 21, 23(1)(a), [^c-1953e9021cf1638651d39ff43053d0c2]25[^c-0287eaa43ff510ff8c7c1ca0bee58e92] 25(a), 25A, 25B, 26 [^c-7232ec7bdfed06e33db6ecd105f0720c][^c-5cb7b49f182bfe2be9eea016d990a7bf]... and 35(1) apply (in so far as applicable by virtue of regulation 21) to—

- (a) educational buildings and buildings of statutory undertakers (notwithstanding section 4(1) of the Act);
- (b) Crown buildings; and
- (c) building work carried out or proposed to be carried out by Crown authorities.

(2) In this regulation “educational buildings and buildings of statutory undertakers” means buildings which fall within paragraphs (a), (b) or (c) of section 4(1) of the Act.
