# The Building Regulations 2010, section 25C

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25C. New buildings: minimum energy performance requirements[^c-bf775cabe40bd982e0e9dd3a002b97ec][^c-b879a8ba96781e65ff74200c439f5ef4]

*Extent: E+W*

Minimum energy performance requirements [^c-b879a8ba96781e65ff74200c439f5ef4]must[^c-52e63d41d6e346eb9bd6af5a025fd211] be approved by the Welsh Ministers, [^c-b879a8ba96781e65ff74200c439f5ef4]calculated and expressed[^c-0130ea6d985b534ab0e75d223b7882a1] in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24, for—

- (a) new buildings [^c-4436ac2c4958ebad215a2ad8b01e49d9]..., in the form of target primary energy [^c-6aba41c6f971b002643a5576aa495aef]... rates; and
- (b) when a dwelling is erected[^c-b9e78794c3c3b49049efbcf4e1cafb4f], in the form of target fabric performance values.
