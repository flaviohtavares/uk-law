# The Building Regulations 2010, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2214
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Minimum energy performance requirements for new buildings[^M_E_353971a2-c759-4289-9ab4-6ddeefc3e467][^c-55ba0018e7f874000e3d0ca7aa542a57]

*Extent: E*

Minimum energy performance requirements shall be approved by the Secretary of State, calculated and expressed[^c-5070af9b78e89087e9abf7c18b5a133d] in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24, for—

- (a) new buildings (which shall include new dwellings), in the form of target CO<sub>2</sub> emission rates; [^c-1980e09a539386b5cf2c8e3b14f16416]...
- (b) new dwellings, in the form of target fabric energy efficiency rates ; and[^c-2497a46d0fe88be63f9ef914f94ee757]
- (c) new buildings in the form of target primary energy rates.

## 25. Minimum energy performance requirements for new buildings[^M_E_c44adc78-2ab9-4c22-fdd3-0da0349471a0][^V_W_f7fe0faf-5478-ccda-f4cb-5b422fcc2074]

*Extent: W*

The Welsh Ministers shall approve minimum energy performance requirements for new buildings, in the form of target CO² emission rates, which shall be calculated and expressed in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24.
