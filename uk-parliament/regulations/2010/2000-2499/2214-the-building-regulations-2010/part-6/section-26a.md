# The Building Regulations 2010, section 26A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2214
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26A. Fabric energy efficiency rates[^M_E_ecd6dd63-8b58-4b5a-a2b3-ef0008d12259][^key-b2325fa0c1ae80bf26870a82fb4376a2][^key-b44f0cc805d4deead225dcceac6e74e6]

*Extent: E*

*Extent: E+W*

Where a dwelling is erected, it shall not exceed the target fabric energy efficiency rate for the dwelling that has been approved pursuant to regulation 25, applying the methodology of calculation and expression of the energy performance of buildings approved pursuant to regulation 24[^key-dc50d0d383582099724bf25e5edb78f2].

## 26A. Primary energy ... rates for new buildings[^M_E_6f0eb01a-ba35-4713-9f90-d95778ad1168][^key-412f7fc8b97df482dfae8860b421154a][^key-bd033d03acfd5c84379733eb4bddcf77]

*Extent: W*

*Extent: E+W*

Where a building [^key-c891a43a69bca28ddfcdaaf2254d848e]... is erected, it must not exceed the target primary energy [^key-1dbc5de05633f1afab509a270a431600]... rate for the building which has been approved pursuant to regulation 25C(a), applying the methodology of calculation and expression of the energy performance of buildings approved pursuant to regulation 24.
