## 26B. Fabric performance values for new dwellings[^c-048da3356c542487563b851a552e2ed2][^c-78cf9a8ac29f2836388c535b2f7474d9]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a dwelling is erected, it must not exceed the target fabric performance values for the dwelling which have been approved pursuant to regulation 25C(b) [^c-78cf9a8ac29f2836388c535b2f7474d9], applying the methodology of calculation and expression of the energy performance of buildings approved pursuant to regulation 24[^c-5e5082d79c7e2043423ba4d193cb938a].
