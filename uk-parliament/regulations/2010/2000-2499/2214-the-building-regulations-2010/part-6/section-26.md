## 26. CO<sub>2</sub> emission rates for new buildings

*Extent: E+W*

Where a building is erected, it shall not exceed the target CO<sub>2</sub> emission rate for the building that has been approved pursuant to regulation 25 [^key-7440518bf80906a18bfb94a48f49cfd4], applying the methodology of calculation and expression of the energy performance of buildings approved pursuant to regulation 24[^key-bc681c1a8913e175bbfd528c92a79ded].
