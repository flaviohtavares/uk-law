## 54. Revocations and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-bedde9a190aac9ea6858d1736a04947a] The Regulations specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 5 are revoked to the extent specified in relation to each in the third column of that table. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bedde9a190aac9ea6858d1736a04947a]

(2)[^c-c2b6a53cc6b7b5489fc23ba75966c0db] Schedule 6, which contains consequential amendments to the Building (Local Authority Charges) Regulations 2010 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2b6a53cc6b7b5489fc23ba75966c0db][^c20435551] and the Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) (England and Wales) Regulations 2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2b6a53cc6b7b5489fc23ba75966c0db][^c20435561], has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2b6a53cc6b7b5489fc23ba75966c0db]
