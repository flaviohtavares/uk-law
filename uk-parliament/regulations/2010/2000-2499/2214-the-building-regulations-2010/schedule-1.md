## SCHEDULE 1 — Requirements

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 4 and 6

PART A STRUCTURE

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th>Requirement</th><th>Limits on application</th></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Loading</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>A1</strong>.—(1) The building shall be constructed so that the combined dead, imposed and wind loads are sustained and transmitted by it to the ground—<br>(a) safely; and<br>(b) without causing such deflection or deformation of any part of the building, or such movement of the ground, as will impair the stability of any part of another building.<br>(2) In assessing whether a building complies with sub-paragraph (1) regard shall be had to the imposed and wind loads to which it is likely to be subjected in the ordinary course of its use for the purpose for which it is intended.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Ground Movement</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>A2</strong>. The building shall be constructed so that ground movement caused by—<br>(a) swelling, shrinkage or freezing of the subsoil; or<br>(b) land-slip or subsidence (other than subsidence arising from shrinkage), in so far as the risk can be reasonably foreseen,<br>will not impair the stability of any part of the building.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Disproportionate collapse</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>A3</strong>. The building shall be constructed so that in the event of an accident the building will not suffer collapse to an extent disproportionate to the cause.</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

PART B FIRE SAFETY

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Means of warning and escape</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B1</strong>. The building shall be designed and constructed so that there are appropriate provisions for the early warning of fire, and appropriate means of escape in case of fire from the building to a place of safety outside the building capable of being safely and effectively used at all material times.</td><td>Requirement B1 does not apply to any prison provided under section 33 of the Prison Act 1952 (power to provide prisons etc).</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Internal fire spread (linings)</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B2</strong>.—(1) To inhibit the spread of fire within the building, the internal linings shall—<br>(a) adequately resist the spread of flame over their surfaces; and<br>(b) have, if ignited, either a rate of heat release or a rate of fire growth, which is reasonable in the circumstances.<br>(2) In this paragraph “internal linings” means the materials or products used in lining any partition, wall, ceiling or other internal structure.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Internal fire spread (structure)</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B3</strong>.—(1) The building shall be designed and constructed so that, in the event of fire, its stability will be maintained for a reasonable period.<br>(2) A wall common to two or more buildings shall be designed and constructed so that it adequately resists the spread of fire between those buildings. For the purposes of this sub-paragraph a house in a terrace and a semi-detached house are each to be treated as a separate building.<br>(3) Where reasonably necessary to inhibit the spread of fire within the building, measures shall be taken, to an extent appropriate to the size and intended use of the building, comprising either or both of the following—<br>(a) sub-division of the building with fire-resisting construction;<br>(b) installation of suitable automatic fire suppression systems.<br>(4) The building shall be designed and constructed so that the unseen spread of fire and smoke within concealed spaces in its structure and fabric is inhibited.</td><td>Requirement B3(3) does not apply to material alterations to any prison provided under section 33 of the Prison Act 1952.</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>External Fire Spread</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B4</strong>.—(1) The external walls of the building shall adequately resist the spread of fire over the walls and from one building to another, having regard to the height, use and position of the building.<br>(2) The roof of the building shall adequately resist the spread of fire over the roof and from one building to another, having regard to the use and position of the building.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Access and facilities for the fire service</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B5</strong>.—(1) The building shall be designed and constructed so as to provide reasonable facilities to assist fire fighters in the protection of life.<br>(2) Reasonable provision shall be made within the site of the building to enable fire appliances to gain access to the building.</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

PART C SITE PREPARATION AND RESISTANCE TO CONTAMINANTS AND MOISTURE

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Preparation of site and resistance to contaminants</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>C1</strong>.—(1) The ground to be covered by the building shall be reasonably free from any material that might damage the building or affect its stability, including vegetable matter, topsoil and pre-existing foundations.<br>(2) Reasonable precautions shall be taken to avoid danger to health and safety caused by contaminants on or in the ground covered, or to be covered by the building and any land associated with the building.<br>(3) Adequate sub-soil drainage shall be provided, if it is needed to avoid—<br>(a) the passage of ground moisture to the interior of the building;<br>(b) damage to the building, including damage through the transport of water-borne contaminants to the foundations of the building.<br>(4) For the purposes of this requirement, “contaminant” means any substance which is or may become harmful to persons or buildings including substances which are corrosive, explosive flammable, radioactive or toxic.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Resistance to moisture</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>C2</strong>. The walls, floors and roof of the building shall adequately protect the building and people who use the building from harmful effects caused by—<br>(a) ground moisture;<br>(b) precipitation including wind-driven spray;<br>(c) interstitial and surface condensation; and<br>(d) spillage of water from or associated with sanitary fittings or fixed appliances.</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

PART E RESISTANCE TO THE PASSAGE OF SOUND

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Protection against sound from other parts of the building and adjoining buildings</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>E1</strong>. Dwelling-houses, flats and rooms for residential purposes shall be designed and constructed in such a way that they provide reasonable resistance to sound from other parts of the same building and from adjoining buildings.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Protection against sound within a dwelling-house etc</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>E2</strong>. Dwelling-houses, flats and rooms for residential purposes shall be designed and constructed in such a way that—<br>(a) internal walls between a bedroom or a room containing a water closet, and other rooms; and<br>(b) internal floors,<br>provide reasonable resistance to sound.</td><td>Requirement E2 does not apply to—<br>(a) an internal wall which contains a door;<br>(b) an internal wall which separates an ensuite toilet from the associated bedroom;<br>(c) existing walls and floors in a building which is subject to a material change of use.</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Reverberation in common internal parts of buildings containing flats or rooms for residential purposes</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>E3</strong>. The common internal parts of buildings which contain flats or rooms for residential purposes shall be designed and constructed in such a way as to prevent more reverberation around the common parts than is reasonable.</td><td>Requirement E3 only applies to corridors, stairwells, hallways and entrance halls which give access to the flat or room for residential purposes.</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Acoustic conditions in schools</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>E4</strong>.—(1) Each room or other space in a school building shall be designed and constructed in such a way that it has the acoustic conditions and the insulation against disturbance by noise appropriate to its intended use.<br>(2) For the purposes of this Part—<br>“school” has the same meaning as in section 4 of the Education Act 1996; and<br>“school building” means any building forming a school or part of a school.</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

PART G SANITATION, HOT WATER SAFETY AND WATER EFFICIENCY

<table>
<tbody>

<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Hot water supply and systems</strong></th></tr>

<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Kitchens and food preparation areas</strong></td></tr>

PART H DRAINAGE AND WASTE DISPOSAL

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Foul water drainage</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H1</strong>.—(1) An adequate system of drainage shall be provided to carry foul water from appliances within the building to one of the following, listed in order of priority—<br>(a) a public sewer; or where that is not reasonably practicable,<br>(b) a private sewer communicating with a public sewer; or where that is not reasonably practicable,<br>(c) either a septic tank which has an appropriate form of secondary treatment or another wastewater treatment system; or, where that is not reasonably practicable,<br>(d) a cesspool.<br>(2) In this Part “foul water” means waste water which comprises or includes—<br>(a) waste from a sanitary convenience, bidet or appliance used for washing receptacles for foul waste; or<br>(b) water which has been used for food preparation, cooking or washing.</td><td>Requirement H1 does not apply to the diversion of water which has been used for personal washing or for the washing of clothes, linen or other articles to collection systems for reuse.</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Wastewater treatment systems and cesspools</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H2</strong>.—(1) Any septic tank and its form of secondary treatment, other wastewater treatment system or cesspool, shall be so sited and constructed that—<br>(a) it is not prejudicial to the health of any person;<br>(b) it will not contaminate any watercourse, underground water or water supply;<br>(c) there are adequate means of access for emptying and maintenance; and<br>(d) where relevant, it will function to a sufficient standard for the protection of health in the event of a power failure<br>(2) Any septic tank or holding tank which is part of a wastewater treatment system or cesspool shall be—<br>(a) of adequate capacity;<br>(b) so constructed that it is impermeable to liquids; and<br>(c) adequately ventilated.<br>(3) Where a foul water drainage system from a building discharges to a septic tank, wastewater treatment system or cesspool, a durable notice shall be affixed in a suitable place in the building containing information on any continuing maintenance required to avoid risks to health.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Rainwater drainage</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H3</strong>.—(1) Adequate provision shall be made for rainwater to be carried from the roof of the building.<br>(2) Paved areas around the building shall be so constructed as to be adequately drained.<br>(3) Rainwater from a system provided pursuant to sub-paragraphs (1) or (2) shall discharge to one of the following, listed in order of priority—<br>(a) an adequate soakaway or some other adequate infiltration system; or, where that is not reasonably practicable,<br>(b) a watercourse; or, where that is not reasonably practicable,<br>(c) a sewer.</td><td>Requirement H3(2) applies only to paved areas—<br>(a) which provide access to the building pursuant to requirement M1 (access and use), or <em>(affected by repeal, see note)</em> M1 (access to and use of buildings other than dwellings), requirement M2 (access to extensions to buildings other than dwellings) , or requirement M4(1), (2) or (3) (access to and use of dwellings);<br>(b) which provide access to or from a place of storage pursuant to requirement H6(2) (solid waste storage); or<br>(c) in any passage giving access to the building, where this is intended to be used in common by the occupiers of one or more other buildings<br>Requirement H3(3) does not apply to the gathering of rainwater for reuse</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Building over sewers</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H4</strong>.—(1) The erection or extension of a building or work involving the underpinning of a building shall be carried out in a way that is not detrimental to the building or building extension or to the continued maintenance of the drain, sewer or disposal main.<br>(2) In this paragraph “disposal main” means any pipe, tunnel or conduit used for the conveyance of effluent to or from a sewage disposal works, which is not a public sewer.<br>(3) In this paragraph and paragraph H5 “map of sewers” means any records kept by a sewerage undertaker under section 199 of the Water Industry Act 1991.</td><td>Requirement H4 applies only to work carried out—<br>(a) over a drain, sewer or disposal main which is shown on any map of sewers; or<br>(b) on any site or in such a manner as may result in interference with the use of, or obstruction of the access of any person to, any drain, sewer or disposal main which is shown on any map of sewers.</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Separate systems of drainage</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H5</strong>. Any system for discharging water to a sewer which is provided pursuant to paragraph H3 shall be separate from that provided for the conveyance of foul water from the building.</td><td>Requirement H5 applies only to a system provided in connection with the erection or extension of a building where it is reasonably practicable for the system to discharge directly or indirectly to a sewer for the separate conveyance of surface water which is—<br>(a) shown on a map of sewers; or<br>(b) under construction either by the sewerage undertaker or by some other person (where the sewer is the subject of an agreement to make a declaration of vesting pursuant to section 104 of the Water Industry Act 1991).</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Solid waste storages</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>H6</strong>.—(1) Adequate provision shall be made for storage of solid waste.<br>(2) Adequate means of access shall be provided—<br>(a) for people in the building to the place of storage; and<br>(b) from the place of storage to a collection point (where one has been specified by the waste collection authority under section 46 (household waste) or section 47 (commercial waste) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990) or to a street (where no collection point has been specified).</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Protection against pollution</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>J7</strong>. Oil storage tanks and the pipes connecting them to combustion appliances shall—<br>(a) be so constructed and protected as to reduce to a reasonable level the risk of the oil escaping and causing pollution; and<br>(b) have affixed in a prominent position a durable notice containing information on how to respond to an oil escape so as to reduce to a reasonable level the risk of pollution.</td><td>Requirement J7 applies only to fixed oil storage tanks with capacities of 3,500 litres or less, and connecting pipes, which are—<br>(a) located outside the building; and<br>(b) serve fixed combustion appliances (including incinerators) in a building used wholly or mainly as a private dwelling,<br>but does not apply to buried systems.</td></tr>
