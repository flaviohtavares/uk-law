# The Building Regulations 2010, section 44B

## 44B. Exemptions from paragraph R1 of Schedule 1[^key-2766d8f94a11b1ba983254dda3add6df]

*Extent: E+W*

The requirements of paragraph R1 (in-building physical infrastructure for high-speed electronic communications networks) of Schedule 1 do not apply to the following types of building or building work—

- (a) buildings which are—
  - (i) listed in accordance with section 1 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4d3dc962ae5adee5ce0433e097789ed4] section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 *(Extent: W)*, [^key-d773db77bff47084a2e4d6a0fa981779]or[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b]
  - (ii) in a conservation area designated in accordance with section 69 of that Act *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed33618e5f18bc9d56b8b845defc2ca9] section 158 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 *(Extent: W)*, [^key-732a8203b27731ae222efe156183237f]or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b] included in the schedule of monuments maintained under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b] section 1 of the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2a54c134a8a1ca7db55aa7b56199163e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b] section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-87f45f7be8636e859de45b2554aed03b],

  where compliance with paragraph R1 of Schedule 1 would unacceptably alter their character or appearance;
- (b) buildings—
  - (i) occupied by the Ministry of Defence or the armed forces of the Crown, or
  - (ii) otherwise occupied for purposes connected to national security;
- (c) buildings situated in isolated areas where the prospect of high-speed connection is considered too remote to justify equipping the building with high-speed ready in-building physical infrastructure or an access point;
- (d) major renovation works in cases in which the cost of compliance with paragraph R1 of Schedule 1 would be disproportionate to the benefit gained.
