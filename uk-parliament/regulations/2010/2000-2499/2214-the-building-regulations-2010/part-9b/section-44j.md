## 44J (Extent: E). Minimum standards of an electric vehicle charge point (Extent: E)[^c-a7aee3d2dff622cef3a5c06fa3d93d82]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E)) For the purposes of this Part and Part S of Schedule 1, an electric vehicle charge point must meet the following minimum standards. *(Extent: E)*

(2 (Extent: E)) It must be capable of providing a reasonable power output for each parking space for which it is intended to be used. *(Extent: E)*

(3 (Extent: E)) It must be run on a dedicated circuit. *(Extent: E)*

(4 (Extent: E)) It must be compatible with all vehicles which may require access to it. *(Extent: E)*
