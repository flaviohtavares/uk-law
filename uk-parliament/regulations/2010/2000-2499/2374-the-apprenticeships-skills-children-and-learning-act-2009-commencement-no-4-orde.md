# The Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010

Made

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 269(4) of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 30th September 2010

Sections 23 to 27 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 come into force on 30th September 2010.

## 3. Provision coming into force on 28th February 2011

Section 202(3) of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 comes into force on 28th February 2011.

Nick Gibb

28th September 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the 30th September 2010 sections 23 to 27 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (“the Act”). Sections 23 to 27 make provisions relating to the preparation, modification and content of the specification for apprenticeship standards for England. Apprenticeship frameworks must conform to the requirements of the specification of apprenticeship standards for England if they are to be issued by the English issuing authority as recognised English frameworks.

This Order also brings into force on 28th February 2011 section 202(3) of the Act which inserts a new section 47ZA into the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. Section 47ZA applies where a local authority propose to allocate money to a childcare provider (other than a maintained school), for the purposes of discharging the authority’s duty to secure sufficient childcare free of charge. The table in the note below lists the provisions brought into force by previous orders and footnotes the order which amended a previous order.
