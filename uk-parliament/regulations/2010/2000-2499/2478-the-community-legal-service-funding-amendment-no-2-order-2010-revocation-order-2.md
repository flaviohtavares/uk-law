# The Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2010 (Revocation) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2478

Legal Services Commission, England And Wales

The Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2010 (Revocation) Order 2010

Made

11th October 2010

Laid before Parliament

12th October 2010

Coming into force

13th October 2010

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(4) and 25(8A) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001].

He has consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society in accordance with section 25(2) of that Act and has had regard to the matters specified in section 25(3) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2010 (Revocation) Order 2010 and comes into force on 13th October 2010.

## 2. Revocation

The Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2010[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor.

J Djanogly

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

11th October 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Community Legal Service (Funding) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/1109](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1109)).
