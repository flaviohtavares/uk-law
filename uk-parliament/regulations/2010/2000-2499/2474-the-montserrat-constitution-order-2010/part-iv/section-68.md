# The Montserrat Constitution Order 2010, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Recalling dissolved Legislative Assembly in case of emergency

If, between a dissolution of the Legislative Assembly and the next ensuing general election, an emergency arises of such a nature that, in the opinion of the Governor, it is necessary for the Assembly to be recalled, the Governor may, acting after consultation with the Premier, summon the Assembly that has been dissolved, and that Assembly shall thereupon be deemed (except for the purposes of section 69) not to have been dissolved, but shall be deemed (except as aforesaid) to be dissolved on the date on which the next ensuing general election is held.
