# The Montserrat Constitution Order 2010, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Privileges, immunities and powers of Legislative Assembly

The Legislature may by law determine and regulate the privileges, immunities and powers of the Legislative Assembly and its members, but no such privileges, immunities or powers shall exceed those of the Commons House of Parliament of the United Kingdom or of its members.
