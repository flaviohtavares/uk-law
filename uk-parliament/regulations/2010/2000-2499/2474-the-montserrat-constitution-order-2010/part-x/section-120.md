# The Montserrat Constitution Order 2010, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Transitional provision concerning Director of Public Prosecutions

Until a person is appointed to the office of Director of Public Prosecutions in accordance with this Constitution, the Attorney-General shall perform the functions of that office.
