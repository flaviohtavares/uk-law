# The Montserrat Constitution Order 2010, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Re-election or reappointment

Any person who has vacated his or her seat in the Legislative Assembly or who has vacated any office constituted by or under this Constitution may, if qualified, again be elected to the Assembly or appointed to that office, as the case may be, from time to time.
