# The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No.3) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2191 (C.109)

Equality

The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No.3) Order 2010

Made

2nd September 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 207(4)(a) and 216(3) of the Equality Act 2010[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No.3) Order 2010.

## 2. Provisions conferring power to make subordinate legislation etc

Section 96(9)(b) of the Equality Act 2010 comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made for the purpose of enabling regulations to be made.

Theresa May

Secretary of State for the Home Department

2nd September 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 96(9)(b) of the Equality Act [2010 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15) for the purpose of making regulations prescribing the manner in which the appropriate regulator must publish specific matters which are not subject to the duty to make reasonable adjustments.
