# The Anti-Slavery Day Act 2010 (Specified Date) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2325

Human Rights, England And Wales

The Anti-Slavery Day Act 2010 (Specified Date) Order 2010

Made

21st September 2010

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 1(1) of the Anti-Slavery Day Act 2010[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Anti-Slavery Day Act 2010 (Specified Date) Order 2010 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Specified Date

The date specified to be observed each year as Anti-Slavery Day is 18th October.

Damian Green

Minister of State

Home Office

21st September 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Anti-Slavery Day Act 2010 introduced a national day to raise awareness of the need to combat slavery, human trafficking and exploitation.

This Order specifies that 18th October shall be observed each year as Anti-Slavery Day.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
