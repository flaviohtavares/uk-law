# The Norfolk Community Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2010 No. 2466

National Health Service, England

The Norfolk Community Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010

Made

7th October 2010

Coming into force

1st November 2010

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 272(7) and (8) of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-84dca874a4b57a51edf6bb98990b51f7]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Norfolk Community Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st November 2010.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service Act 2006;
- “community health services” means any services—[^c-67eced4efc7ed543f96e6cb1eec67ca3]
  - (a) whose provision must be arranged by an integrated care board[^c-a18556bc6500fe82e670cddf0d66982d] under section 3(1)(g) or (i)[^c-9431f1357908b845b257f7b068e44f8b] of the Act;
  - (b) which are of a kind mentioned in section 3(1)(g) or (i)[^c-17356d75d3ad1aff40584743d7aa125c] of the Act, but—
    - (i) whose provision must be arranged by NHS England[^c-1e9a0552f9dc85b6794301ea7d596380] under regulations made under section 3B of the Act[^f00003]; or
    - (ii) which may be provided by the Secretary of State or a local authority under section 2B of the Act;
  - (c) which a local authority must provide under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act;
  - (d) which a local authority may provide under paragraph 2 or 7A of Schedule 1 to the Act; or
  - (e) which the Secretary of State must provide under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Act;
- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by article 2.

## 2. Establishment and name of trust[^c-9ac39245d85d621edf24ac0342e1a7a2]

There is established a National Health Service trust which shall be called the East of England[^c-bb175abfcaf780ff187378cc21ae7cd5] Community Health and Care National Health Service Trust.

## 3. Functions of the trust[^c-be3fbd089cb05cbd485f1a7b36b43d2d][^c-c089bc24596de650976f496a4ee985c5]

The trust’s functions are to provide goods and services for the purposes of the health service.

## 4. Directors of the trust[^c-ace64a544fc8078f27bc99415e2960aa]

(1)[^c-4f7347b98ecfc6dd69ac2a69fe28e462] The trust must have, in addition to the chairman, 5 executives and 6 non-executive directors.

(2) If the Secretary of State suspends the chairman, or a non-executive director of the trust, the number of non-executive directors of the trust shall increase during the period of suspension by the number of persons so suspended.

## 5. Accounting date[^c-cc8f281f4c2d17edef2a81ed800e08c2][^c-5052d3b439de0e729003574bd225689e]

The accounting date of the trust is 31st March.

## 6. Transfer of staff to the trust[^c-1f13a23ee86ef375d31a375f0c40c8fe]

The Norfolk Primary Care Trust[^f00004] has made a scheme for the purposes of paragraph 8(1)(b) of Schedule 4 to the Act for the transfer of staff to the trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Simon Burns

Minister of State,

Department of Health

7th October 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a National Health Service trust called the Norfolk Community Health and Care National Health Service Trust.

The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3), the number of executive and non-executive directors of the trust (article 4), the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions), and the accounting date of the trust (article 5). This Order also refers to the scheme made by the Norfolk Primary Care trust for the transfer of certain of its staff to the trust (article 6).
