# The Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) (Amendment) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1942 (C. 103)

Charities, England And Wales

The Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) (Amendment) Order 2010

Made

26th July 2010

The Minister for the Cabinet Office makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 78(6) and 79(2) and (3) of the Charities Act 2006[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) (Amendment) Order 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) Order 2008

(1) The Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) Order 2008[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Articles 1, 6, 11, 19, 24 and 26 for “1st September 2010”, in each place, substitute “1st September 2011”.

Nick Hurd

Parliamentary Secretary

Cabinet Office

26th July 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the transitional and transitory provisions and savings made in the Charities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional and Transitory Provisions and Savings) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/3267 (C.150)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3267), amended by [S.I. 2009/2648 (C.118)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2648)). The effect is that, notwithstanding their removal from Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 2003 with effect from 31 January 2009, the following will continue to be treated as if they were exempt charities until 1st September 2011, instead of 1st September 2010:

- (a) bodies to which section 23(1)(a) or (b) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 applies; and
- (b) institutions to which section 23(2) of that Act applies.
