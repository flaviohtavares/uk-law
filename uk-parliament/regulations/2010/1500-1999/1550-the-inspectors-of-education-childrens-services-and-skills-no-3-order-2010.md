# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1550

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2010

Made

9th June 2010

Coming into force

10th June 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of June 2010

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2010 and comes into force on 10th June 2010.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council
