# The Midland Metro Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1722

Transport And Works, England

Transport, England

The Midland Metro Order 2010

Made

29th June 2010

Coming into force

20th July 2010

An application has been made to the Secretary of State in accordance with the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006[^f00001] for an Order under sections 1 and 5 of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00002] (“the 1992 Act”).

The Secretary of State has determined to make an Order giving effect to the proposals comprised in the application without modifications.

Notice of the Secretary of State’s determination was published in the London Gazette on 25th June 2010.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 5 of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the 1992 Act, makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Midland Metro Order 2010 and shall come into force on 20th July 2010.

## 2. Modification of Midland Metro Act 1989

(1) Subsection (1) of section 9 (requirements applicable to tramways) of the Midland Metro Act 1989[^f00003], including that provision as applied by any of the other Midland Metro Acts specified in paragraph (2), shall cease to have effect.

(2) The other Midland Metro Acts specified are:

- The Midland Metro Act 1992[^f00004];
- The Midland Metro (No.2) Act 1992[^f00005];
- The Midland Metro Act 1993[^f00006]; and
- The Midland Metro (No.2) Act 1993[^f00007].

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Ellis Harvey

Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

Department for Transport

29th June 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order repeals in relation to the existing Midland Metro light rail system a statutory requirement relating to the minimum distance between passing carriages in order to achieve consistency with current guidance.
