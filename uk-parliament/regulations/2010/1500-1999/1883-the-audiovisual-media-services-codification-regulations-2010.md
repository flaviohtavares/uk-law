# The Audiovisual Media Services (Codification) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1883

Electronic Communications

The Audiovisual Media Services (Codification) Regulations 2010

Made

20th July 2010

Laid before Parliament

23rd July 2010

Coming into force

18th August 2010

These Regulations are made by the Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to information society services[^f00002] and measures relating to television broadcasting[^f00003], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Audiovisual Media Services (Codification) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 18th August 2010.

## 2. Amendments to the Broadcasting Act 1990

In section 177(6) of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00004] for “Council Directive [89/552/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0552)” substitute “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive”.

**3.** In section 202[^f00005] of that Act—

- (a) after the definition of “advertising agent” insert—

  > - “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive” means Directive 2010/13/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the provision of audiovisual media services

  ;
- (b) omit subsection (4A).

## 4. Amendments to the Communications Act 2003

In section 335A(1)(a) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00006] for “3” substitute “4”.

**5.** In section 405(1)[^f00007] of that Act for the definition of “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive” substitute—

> - “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive” means Directive 2010/13/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the provision of audiovisual media services

.

## 6. Amendment to the Gambling Act 2005

In section 333(5)(a) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00008] for “89/552/EEC on the coordination of provisions concerning television broadcasting” substitute “2010/13/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the provision of audiovisual media services”.

## 7. Amendment to the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006

In section 115(1) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006[^f00009] for the definition of “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive” substitute—

> - “the Audiovisual Media Services Directive” means Directive 2010/13/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the provision of audiovisual media services

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend specified legislation to reflect the codification of the EU Directives in the field of audiovisual media services.

Directive 2010/13/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the provision of audiovisual media services (Audiovisual Media Services Directive)[^f00010] repeals Council Directive [89/552/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0552) on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in Member States concerning the pursuit of television broadcasting activities[^f00011]. Directive [89/552/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0552) was amended by Directive [97/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0036) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00012] and Directive [2007/65/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0065) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00013].

Regulations 2 and 3 amend the Broadcasting Act 1990. Regulations 4 and 5 amend the Communications Act 2003. Regulations 6 amends the Gambling Act 2005 and regulation 7 the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
