# The Ecclesiastical Exemption (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (England) (Amendment) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1806

Town And Country Planning, England

The Ecclesiastical Exemption (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (England) (Amendment) Order 2010

Made

13th July 2010

Laid before Parliament

14th July 2010

Coming into force

1st October 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 60(5) and (6), 75(7) and (8) and 93(6) and (7) of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Exemption (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (England) (Amendment) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st October 2010.

## 2. Amendment

In Schedule 2 of the Ecclesiastical Exemption (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (England) Order 2010[^f00002], before “North Western” insert “Northern”.

John Penrose

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

13th July 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Ecclesiastical Exemption (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (England) Order 2010 ([SI 2010/1176](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1176)) (“the Principal Order”) which applies in respect of buildings situated in England.

Article 7(1)(d) of the Principal Order retains the ecclesiastical exemption for church buildings owned by or held in trust for the purposes of the United Reformed Church which are situated within one of the Provinces set out in Schedule 2 of that Order and held on trusts set out in Part I of Schedule 2 to the United Reformed Church Act [1972 (c.18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/18) or Part I of Schedule 2 to the United Reformed Church Act [1981 (c.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/14).

This Order amends Schedule 2 of the Principal Order by inserting the ‘Northern’ Province of the United Reformed Church to that Schedule.
