# The Education and Inspections Act 2006 and Education and Skills Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) (England) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1939

Education, England

The Education and Inspections Act 2006 and Education and Skills Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) (England) Regulations 2010

Made

29th July 2010

Laid before Parliament

4th August 2010

Coming into force

1st September 2010

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 183 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001] and section 170 of the Education and Skills Act 2008[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education and Inspections Act 2006 and Education and Skills Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) (England) Regulations 2010 and comes into force on the 1st September 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996

In regulation 57(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00003] for the definition of “the Connexions Service” substitute—

> - “the Connexions Service” means a service made available by a local authority in accordance with section 68 of the Education and Skills Act 2008;

.

## 3. Amendment of the Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001[^f00004] for the definition of “Connexions Service” substitute—

> - “Connexions Service” means a service made available by a local authority in accordance with section 68 of the Education and Skills Act 2008;

.

## 4. Amendment of the Contracting Out (Local Education Authority Functions) (England) Order 2002

In Schedule 3 of the Contracting Out (Local Education Authority Functions) England Order 2002[^f00005] omit paragraphs (n) and (o).

## 5. Amendment of the School Companies Regulations 2002

In regulation 2(2)(a) of the School Companies Regulations 2002[^f00006] for “section 65 or 83 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000” substitute “section 83 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000 or section 128 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006”.

## 6. Amendment of the School Companies (Private Finance Initiative Companies) Regulations 2002

In regulation 2(2)(a) of the School Companies (Private Finance Initiative Companies) Regulations 2002[^f00007] for “section 65 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000” substitute “section 128 of the Education and Skills Act 2006”.

## 7. Amendment of the Children Act 2004 (Children’s Services) Regulations 2005

In regulation 2(1)(r) of the Children Act 2004 (Children’s Services) Regulations 2005[^f00008] for “section 118(1)(c) of the Learning and Skills Act 2000 (inspection of services to encourage, etc effective participation by young persons in education or training)” substitute “section 75 of the Education and Skills Act 2008 (inspection)”.

## 8. Amendment of the Children Act 2004 (Joint Area Reviews) Regulations 2005

(1) The Schedule to Children Act 2004 (Joint Area Reviews) Regulations 2005[^f00009] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 omit the definitions of “the 2000 Act” and “the ALI”.

(3) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1) for “section 114(1) of the 2000 Act (provision of services encouraging participation by young persons in education or training)” substitute “section 68 (support services: provision by local authorities) of the Education and Skills Act 2008”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (2)(a) for “section 118(7) of the 2000 Act” substitute “section 75(7) of the Education and Skills Act 2008”.

## 9. Amendment of the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2006

In Schedule 1, Part 1, to the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00010] omit paragraph 15.

## 10. Amendment of the Education (Information About Children in Alternative Provision) (England) Regulations 2007

(1) The Education (Information About Children in Alternative Provision) (England) Regulations 2007[^f00011] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 8(k)—

- (a) for “the Secretary of State” substitute “a local authority”;
- (b) for “section 114(2) of the Learning and Skills Act 2000” substitute “section 68(3)(b) of the Education and Skills Act 2008”.

## 11. Amendment of the School Finance (England) Regulations 2008

In paragraph 19 of Schedule 1 to the School Finance (England) Regulations 2008[^f00012] for “under section 116 of the 2000 Act” substitute “under section 68 of the Education and Skills Act 2008”.

## 12. Amendment of the Education (Budget Statements) (England) Regulations 2008

In regulation 2 of the Education (Budget Statements) (England) Regulations 2008[^f00013] in the definition of “youth work” for “section 114 of the 2000 Act” substitute “section 68 of the Education and Skills Act 2008”.

Nick Gibb

Minister of State for Schools

Department for Education

29th July 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under the Education and Inspections Act 2006 and the Education and Skills Act 2008.

The Regulations make consequential amendments to subordinate legislation necessary as a consequence of the repealing of sections of the Learning and Skills Act 2000[^f00014] by the two Acts mentioned above.
