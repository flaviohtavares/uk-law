# The Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1726 (C. 90)

Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010

Made

2nd July 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(3) of the Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010.

**2.** Sections 1 and 2 of the Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010 shall come into force on the 5th July 2010.

Owen Paterson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

2nd July 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th July 2010 sections 1 and 2 of the Northern Ireland Assembly Members Act 2010. Section 3 of the Act came into force on Royal Assent.
