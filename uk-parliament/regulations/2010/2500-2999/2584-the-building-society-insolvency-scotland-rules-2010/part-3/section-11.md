# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 11

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Appointment of provisional building society liquidator[^c-9a32d6c6ca59a2baabc560183756fb12]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.1 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) For rule 4.1(1) substitute—

> (1) An application to the court for the appointment of a provisional building society liquidator under section 135 of the Insolvency Act may be made—
>
> - (a) by the Bank of England; or
> - (b) by the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3], with the consent of the Bank of England.

.
