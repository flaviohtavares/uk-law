# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 105

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 105. Definition of “proxy”[^c-459d8dfeee042a11520284f04f466867]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 7.14 of the 1986 Rules[^f00048].

(2) In rule 7.14(4) for “chairman of the meeting” substitute “chair of the meeting or the building society liquidator”.
