## 6. Application for building society insolvency order[^c-7fa3673b2d793449955f21b8378db257]

*Extent: S*

An application for a building society insolvency order under section 95 of the Banking Act shall be in accordance with Part 10 of Chapter 74 of the Rules of the Court of Session.
