## 5. Overview[^c-dad5efc4132f46d6f5469aea203d0af9]

*Extent: S*

The purpose of these Rules is to provide a procedure for the appointment of a building society liquidator and the operation of building society insolvency under Part 2 of the Banking Act in Scotland.
