# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 87

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 87. FSA’s directions under section 116 of the Banking Act[^key-0e7842cac2d8941a9fec722f4156c2fb]

*Extent: S*

Where the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3] gives a direction under section 116 of the Banking Act (application by an interested person for postponement of dissolution) the appropriate regulator shall send a copy of the direction to that applicant for it[^f00040].
