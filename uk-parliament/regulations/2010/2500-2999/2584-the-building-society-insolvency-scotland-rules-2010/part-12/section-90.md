## 90. Dissolution after winding up[^key-ee1b785f8d5a748fe5f0c9ff177aae18]

*Extent: S*

Apply rule 4.77 of the 1986 Rules. Ignore the reference to section 204 and for “registrar of companies” substitute “Financial Conduct Authority”[^key-108a7b85ba23ea6cbfa7992a457be5cd].
