## 53. Loss of qualification as insolvency practitioner[^key-298ae9292395acde47e860e22168a4b8]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.37 paragraphs (1) and (2)[^f00028] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In rule 4.37(2) after “Accountant in Bankruptcy” insert “and the Bank of England”.
