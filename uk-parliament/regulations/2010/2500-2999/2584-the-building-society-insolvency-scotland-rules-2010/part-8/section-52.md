# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 52

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Building society liquidator deceased[^key-5442bdd2cb98dc8d7851c103fb86b54b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.36 of the 1986 Rules[^f00027].

(2) In rule 4.36(1) after “court” insert “the Bank of England and liquidation committee”.
