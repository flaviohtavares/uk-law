# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 33

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 33. Appointment to be advertised and registered[^key-9479ea4be9ac30143cc42196ddec585a]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the building society liquidator is appointed by a meeting of the creditors under rule 32 or by the Bank of England under rule 51.

(2) The building society liquidator shall, after receiving the certificate of appointment, give notice of the appointment in such newspapers as the building society liquidator thinks most appropriate for ensuring that it comes to the notice of the building society’s creditors and contributories.

(3) The expense of giving notice under this rule shall be borne in the first instance by the building society liquidator and such expense shall be treated as an expense of the building society insolvency.

(4) The building society liquidator shall immediately notify the appointment to the court, the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3] and the Accountant in Bankruptcy.
