# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 56

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56. Power of court to set aside certain transactions[^c-bdbca25c75f80125f9bd230565c72ac6]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.38 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In rule 4.38(2) of that Rule—

- (a) leave out “either”;
- (b) leave out “or” at the end of sub-paragraph (a); and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

  > , or (c) it is shown to the court’s satisfaction that the transaction was entered into by the building society liquidator for the purpose of achieving Objective 1.

  .
