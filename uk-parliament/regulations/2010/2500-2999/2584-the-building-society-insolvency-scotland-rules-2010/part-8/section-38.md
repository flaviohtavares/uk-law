# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 38

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 38. Procedure on building society liquidator’s removal[^key-ec15a3315ac4a4b84219aa9930bd3fb1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.24 of the 1986 Rules[^f00023].

(2) At the end of rule 4.24(1)(a) and (b) after “Accountant in Bankruptcy” where it appears insert “the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3] and the Bank of England.”.
