# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 39

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Release of building society liquidator on removal[^key-d1075350a0225de361147504514e5433]

*Extent: S*

Where the building society liquidator has been removed by a creditors’ meeting which has not resolved against the building society liquidator’s release, the fact of that release shall be stated in the certificate of removal before a copy of it is sent, in accordance with rule 38, to the court, the Accountant in Bankruptcy, the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3] and the Bank of England.
