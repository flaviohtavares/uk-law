## 23. Meaning of “contributories”[^key-43ee5c4d23392946d3fa6a14797359bd]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of this Part, “**contributories**” does not include the borrowing members of the society as defined at paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 2 to the Building Societies Act.
