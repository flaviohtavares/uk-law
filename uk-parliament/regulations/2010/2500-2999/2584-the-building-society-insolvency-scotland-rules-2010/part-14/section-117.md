# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 117

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 117. Right to list of creditors and copy documents[^key-e67a1617918751776b6cb8073bc7f783]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 7.26 of the 1986 Rules[^f00054].

(2) For rule 7.26(1) substitute “Paragraph (2) applies to building society insolvency proceedings.”.

(3) Ignore rule 7.26(2A) where it first occurs referring to member State liquidator.
