# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 123

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 123. Sederunt book[^c-ef1da6ce041d7c63b063b5f33e7d4d11]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 7.33 of the 1986 Rules[^f00058].

(2) In rule 7.33 for the words “the Act or of the Rules”, substitute “the Act as applied by the 2009 Act and these Rules”.
