# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 112

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 112. Service outside the United Kingdom[^key-5fb1f6336d4008dbeaf6a626844a1a43]

*Extent: S*

Where, for the purposes of building society insolvency proceedings, there is a requirement to effect service on a person outwith the United Kingdom, the court may, on application, order service to be effected in such manner as it thinks fit.
