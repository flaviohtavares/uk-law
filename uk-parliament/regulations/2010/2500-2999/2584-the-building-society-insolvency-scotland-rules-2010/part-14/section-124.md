## 124. Disposal of building society’s books, papers and other records[^key-b91a13949731b708b529e5cefe062497]

*Extent: S*

Apply rule 7.34(1) and (3) of the 1986 Rules[^f00059]. In paragraph (3), ignore subparagraphs (b) and (c).
