# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 17

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Notice requiring statement of affairs[^key-82987d5febf511b27512d1e13a822423]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.7 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) Insert new rule 4.7(5)–

> (5) The building society liquidator shall, at the same time as sending a notice under paragraph (3), send a copy of such notice to the appropriate regulator[^M_F_e8621171-61d1-4c2d-ac9f-210d5e4947c3].
