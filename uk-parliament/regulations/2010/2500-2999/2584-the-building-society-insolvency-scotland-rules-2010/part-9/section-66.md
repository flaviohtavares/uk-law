# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 66

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 66. Committee members’ representatives[^c-0d6b50d49b5f954ba74f7213ecc6d931]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.48 of the 1986 Rules[^f00032].

(2) In paragraph (2), omit from “or authorisation” to “Act”.

## Annotations

[^c-0d6b50d49b5f954ba74f7213ecc6d931]: Commencement: Rule 66 in force at 15.11.2010, see [rule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2584/rule/1)

[^f00032]: Footnote: Rule 4.48 was amended by paragraph 26 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921). Rule 4.48 of the 1986 Rules refers to section 375 of the Companies Act [1985 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6). That section was replaced by section 323 of the Companies Act [2006 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46) with effect from 1st October 2007.
