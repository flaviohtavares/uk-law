# The Building Society Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2010, section 60

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 60. Formalities of establishment[^c-fb02687ac0eb68e803a8adddeebca5d1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Apply rule 4.42 of the 1986 Rules[^f00030].

(2) In paragraph (3) omit “or authorisation” and from “or any representative” to “Act”.

## Annotations

[^c-fb02687ac0eb68e803a8adddeebca5d1]: Commencement: Rule 60 in force at 15.11.2010, see [rule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2584/rule/1)
