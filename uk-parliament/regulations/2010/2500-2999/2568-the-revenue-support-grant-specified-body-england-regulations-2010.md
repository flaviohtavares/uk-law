# The Revenue Support Grant (Specified Body) (England) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2568

Local Government, England

The Revenue Support Grant (Specified Body) (England) Regulations 2010

Made

19th October 2010

Laid before Parliament

25th October 2010

Coming into force

15th November 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(4) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001] and after consulting in accordance with section 76(5) of that Act, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Body) (England) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 15th November 2010.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.

## 2. Revocation

The instruments listed in the Schedule to these Regulations are revoked.

## 3. Specified body

In relation to any financial year beginning on or after 1st April 2011, the Improvement and Development Agency for Local Government (company registration number 3675577) is specified for the purposes of section 76(4) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Sections 78 and 79 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 provide for revenue support grant to be payable to any body which provides services for local authorities and which is specified by the Secretary of State in regulations.

These Regulations revoke and replace the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1992 and the instruments which amended those Regulations. In relation to any financial beginning on or after 1st April 2011, these Regulations specify the Improvement and Development Agency for Local Government as the sole body to which revenue support grant must be paid.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
