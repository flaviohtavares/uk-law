# The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 2010, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2977
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 2010.

(2) This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) This Order does not apply to an election to fill a casual vacancy in a district council that occurred (within the meaning of section 11(5) of the 1962 Act [^c20634801]) on or before the day on which this Order comes into force.
