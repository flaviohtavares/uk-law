# The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 2010, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2977

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 2 to the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 [^c20634871], the entry in respect of rule 33 of Schedule 5 to the 1962 Act is revoked.

(2) In the Postponement of Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 [^c20634881], article 2(3) (which provides that section 11(1A) of the 1962 Act does not apply to the local general election in 2011) is revoked.
