# The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 2010, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2977
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Order—

- “**the 1962 Act**” means the Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962 [^c20634811];
- “**Local Elections Rules**” means the rules in Schedule 5 to the 1962 Act [^c20634821]; and
- “**the 1985 Order**” means the Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 1985 [^c20634831].

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^c20634841] applies to Article 1 and this Article and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.
