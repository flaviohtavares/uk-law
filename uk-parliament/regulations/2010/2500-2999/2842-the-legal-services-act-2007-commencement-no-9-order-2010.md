# The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2842 (C. 127)

Legal Services, England And Wales

The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2010

Made

25th November 2010

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 211(2) of the Legal Services Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

Sections 80 and 81 of the Legal Services Act 2007 come into force on 30th November 2010.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

J Djanogly

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

25th November 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the ninth commencement order made under the Legal Services Act [2007 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29) (“the 2007 Act”).

Article 2 commences sections 80 and 81 of the 2007 Act on 30th November 2010. Section 80 enables the Lord Chancellor, on the recommendation of the Legal Services Board, to make an order either establishing new appellate bodies for the purposes of appeals under Part 5 of the 2007 Act (alternative business structures) or making provision to enable existing bodies to hear such appeals. Section 81 sets out procedural requirements about recommendations by the Legal Services Board for an order under section 80.
