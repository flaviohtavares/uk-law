# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 192

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 192. Use of reports[^c-17c54371182e0f81fa2123aa98b6bc67]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Unless the application involves other parties, or the court orders otherwise, a report may be filed in court instead of a witness statement by—

- (a) the building society liquidator,
- (b) the provisional building society liquidator, or
- (c) the special manager.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of a witness statement, the report shall be treated for the purposes of rule 191, and any hearing before the court, as if it were a witness statement.
