> Text as at 2013-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 200. Title of proceedings[^c-e67dc0cef88922b6e8de8e6c37c0a7d6]

*Extent: E+W*

Every proceeding under Part 2 of the Banking Act shall, with any necessary additions, be titled “IN THE MATTER OF …………….. (naming the building society to which the proceedings relate) AND IN THE MATTER OF THE BANKING ACT 2009”.
