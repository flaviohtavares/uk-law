# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 222

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 222. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies[^key-b5388ee0c4838f662d05fb2035742bf2]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in a building society insolvency, the court makes an order staying any action, execution or legal process against the property of the building society, service of the order may be effected by delivering a sealed copy to the address for service of the claimant or other person having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
