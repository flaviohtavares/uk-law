# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 225

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 225. Further information and disclosure[^c-384b0594ff7e1c667af2b629e25fc0c6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 7.60 of the 1986 Rules[^f00079].

(2) After paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) Before the passing of a full payment resolution the court shall only grant an order on an application under paragraph (1)(b) if satisfied that granting the order is unlikely to prejudice the achievement of Objective 1.

.
