# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 210

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 210. Application for costs[^c-424175ede814a4dd44dc29439a8fdc91]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 7.40 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1) for “insolvency” substitute “building society insolvency”.

(3) In paragraph (2), leave out the words from “, and, in winding up” to the end.

(4) In paragraph (3), leave out “and, where appropriate, the official receiver”.

(5) Ignore paragraph (3A)[^f00074].
