## 213. Introductory[^c-db12e60f6e312a3e4d9d7f5df91eefc4]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 7.43 of the 1986 Rules[^f00076]. In paragraph (1), for (a), substitute “ by reason of being a protected person within the meaning of Part 21 of the CPR or”.
