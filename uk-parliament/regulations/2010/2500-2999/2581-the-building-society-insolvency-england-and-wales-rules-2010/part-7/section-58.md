# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 58

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Meaning of “prove”[^key-640a1b83334ab0203d1ca2d694d2e546]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.73 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (5), for “or a Government Department” substitute “, a Government Department or the FSCS”.

(3) Ignore paragraphs (2) and (8)[^f00024].
