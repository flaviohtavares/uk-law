# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 155

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 155. Primacy of Objective 1[^c-f1f2d2bd8ef6c660245239c56a83fd09]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the building society liquidator considers that the carrying out of a duty imposed by a rule in this Part would prejudice the achievement of Objective 1, the building society liquidator shall postpone the carrying out of that duty until the building society liquidator considers that the carrying out of the duty would no longer be likely to prejudice the achievement of that Objective.
