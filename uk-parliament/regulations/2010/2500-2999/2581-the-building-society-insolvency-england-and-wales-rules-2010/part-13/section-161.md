# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 161

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 161. Costs not to fall on building society liquidator[^key-c723ab4425003b385f695c7e7675825f]

*Extent: E+W*

The building society liquidator is not personally liable for any costs incurred by a person in respect of an application to set aside or vary the building society liquidator’s act or decision in settling the list of contributories, or varying or adding to the list, unless the court makes an order to that effect.
