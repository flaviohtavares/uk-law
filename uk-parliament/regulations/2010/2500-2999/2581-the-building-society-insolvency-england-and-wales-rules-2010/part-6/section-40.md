# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 40

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Meaning of “contributories”[^c-adb89d2bad2fe2b9b33a9c43860f0945]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part, “**contributories**” does not include the borrowing members of the society (see paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 2 to the Building Societies Act).
