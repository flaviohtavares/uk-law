# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 45

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Requisitioned meetings: general[^c-101eeb485f0d7015487692a125bb583f]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 4.57 of the 1986 Rules. In rule 4.57(4)(a) for “the amount for which they may vote at any meeting” substitute “the value of the shares held by them in the society”.
