> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Admission and rejection of proof (creditors’ meetings)[^c-185aae357d4654a0d5ad4066d3809009]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 4.70 of the 1986 Rules. For paragraph (5) substitute—

> (5) The chair is not personally liable for costs incurred by any person in respect of an application under this rule unless the court makes an order to that effect.
