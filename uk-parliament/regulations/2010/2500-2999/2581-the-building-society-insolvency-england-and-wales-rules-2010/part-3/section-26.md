# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 26

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Termination of appointment[^c-b192ee758788249dd54bbd1ec7cf7472]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.31 of the 1986 Rules[^f00015].

(2) After paragraph (2) insert—

> (2A) On the making of a building society insolvency order the appointment of the provisional building society liquidator shall terminate.

.
