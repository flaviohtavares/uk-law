> Text as at 2013-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 138. General qualification on powers[^c-4212ed7b21d6e9326c46fc3833234e2d]

*Extent: E+W*

In exercising any power conferred on the building society liquidator by this Part before a full payment resolution has been passed, the building society liquidator shall exercise it consistently with Objective 1.
