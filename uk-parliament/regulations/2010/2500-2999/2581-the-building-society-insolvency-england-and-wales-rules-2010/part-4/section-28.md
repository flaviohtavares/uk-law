# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 28

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Verification and filing[^c-66b3a4060352da5659bb0c68c784de51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.33 of the 1986 Rules. For “official receiver” substitute “building society liquidator”.

(2) For paragraph (6), substitute—

> (6) The building society liquidator shall file the statement of affairs in court and shall send a copy of it to the appropriate regulator[^M_F_7232384c-0ea1-45ab-a4ac-6780835287f8].

.

(3) Ignore paragraph (7).
