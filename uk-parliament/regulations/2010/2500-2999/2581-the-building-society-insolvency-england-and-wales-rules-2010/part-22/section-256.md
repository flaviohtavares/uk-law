# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 256

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 256. Quorum at meeting of creditors or contributories[^key-52ec8072e9105a4668c9a3340dc5453c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 12.4A of the 1986 Rules[^f00094].

(2) For paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) For the purposes of this rule, the reference to the creditor or contributories necessary to constitute a quorum is to those persons present or represented by proxy by any person (including the chair).
