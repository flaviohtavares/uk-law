> Extent: England, Wales.

## 181. Procedure following appeal under section 116 of the Banking Act[^key-2727f072346e36d809a7b2c2b0461021]

*Extent: E+W*

Following an appeal under section 116(2) of the Banking Act (against a decision of the Financial Conduct Authority or the Prudential Regulation Authority[^key-3b2a55373554bc4ff95e0dcaaf011b29] under that section) the court shall send two sealed copies of its order to the person in whose favour the appeal was determined, and that person shall send one of the copies to the regulator in question[^key-d49d9bd8c2f97debc85ae976d87bdb21].
