# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 180

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 180. Appropriate regulator’s directions under section 116 of the Banking Act[^c-0c3f7881567fe382486e30f61d62015d][^M_F_7232384c-0ea1-45ab-a4ac-6780835287f8]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the appropriate regulator gives a direction under section 116 of the Banking Act (application by an interested person for postponement of dissolution) the appropriate regulator shall send a copy of the direction to that applicant[^f00064].
