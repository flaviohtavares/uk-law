> Text as at 2013-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 282. “The appropriate fee”[^c-6b8a8e1668d4651ad64de95c98a9e6f7]

*Extent: E+W*

”The appropriate fee” means 15 pence per A4 or A5 page, and 30 pence per A3 page.
