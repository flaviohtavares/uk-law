# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 279

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 279. Notice to joint building society liquidators[^c-0849fc2153ab99ac29b93db8d9783bcd]

*Extent: E+W*

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the building society liquidator, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
