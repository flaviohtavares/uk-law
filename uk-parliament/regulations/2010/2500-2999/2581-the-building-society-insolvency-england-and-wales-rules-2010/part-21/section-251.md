## 251. Debt payable at future time[^c-3914f647705051ba985df480efd1f734]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 11.13 of the 1986 Rules[^f00090]. For paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) In paragraph (2), “**relevant date**” means the date of the commencement of building society insolvency.
