# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 239

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 239. Preliminary[^key-ef27a5c768f6a277609462c66e7722c3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The rules in this Part relate to the declaration and payment of dividends in a building society insolvency.

(2) In this Part—

- “**creditors**” means those creditors of the building society of whom the building society liquidator is aware, or who are identified in the building society’s statement of affairs, and
- “**the insolvent**” means the building society.
