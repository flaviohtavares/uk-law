# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 244

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 244. Notice of declaration[^c-59498971ebf3b5c206cc9f2fa52bcd0c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 11.6 of the 1986 Rules[^f00087].

(2) Ignore paragraph (1)(b)[^f00088].

(3) In paragraph (2), after (b), add—

> - (ba) expenses incurred by the building society liquidator in the achievement of Objective 1 under section 99 of the Banking Act.

.
