# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 113

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Power of court to set aside certain transactions[^c-fea75f48fe9415249425d98ad4d0ec32]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.149 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) leave out “either”, and
- (b) leave out “or” at the end of sub–paragraph (a), and after sub–paragraph (b) insert “, or” and—

  > - (c) it is shown to the court’s satisfaction that the transaction was entered into by the building society liquidator for the purpose of achieving Objective 1.

  .
