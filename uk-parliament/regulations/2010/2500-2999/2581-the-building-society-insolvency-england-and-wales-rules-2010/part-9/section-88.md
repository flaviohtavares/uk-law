# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 88

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Creditors’ meeting to be notified of the building society liquidator’s resignation[^key-44c1f1b07d026a04c4629b878c76b0e2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.108 of the 1986 Rules[^f00037].

(2) For paragraph (1), substitute—

> (1) Before resigning office, the building society liquidator must obtain the consent of the Bank of England and must call a meeting of creditors to notify them of this.
>
> (1A) The notice summoning the meeting shall indicate that this is the purpose, or one of the purposes, of the meeting and shall draw the attention of the creditors to rule 96 with respect to the building society liquidator’s release.
>
> (1B) The notice in paragraph (1A) shall enclose a copy of the Bank of England’s consent.

.

(3) For paragraph (2) substitute—

> (2) Copies of the notice and of the account mentioned in paragraph (3) shall be sent to the court, the Bank of England and the appropriate regulator[^M_F_7232384c-0ea1-45ab-a4ac-6780835287f8].

.
