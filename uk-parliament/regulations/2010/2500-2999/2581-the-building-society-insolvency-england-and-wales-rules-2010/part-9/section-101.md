# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 101

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Building society liquidator’s remuneration where he realises assets on behalf of chargeholder[^c-e13bbc0cb622bdbdf7485ca8203f036d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.127B[^f00043] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for the words from “liquidator” to “and” substitute “building society liquidator”.

(3) In paragraphs (2) and (3), after “Schedule 6” add “to the 1986 Rules as applied by the Schedule to the 2010 Rules”.
