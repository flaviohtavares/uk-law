# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 122

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 122. Quorum[^c-41653c364ccd0dbbcd7d44aedc85ebbd]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to rule 4.171(6A) of the 1986 Rules as inserted by rule 134(3) of these rules, a meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least two members are present or represented.
