# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 134

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 134. Composition of committee when creditors paid in full[^c-a0bfa210f196a7021ebf7474e9ebf375]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.171 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (6)—

- (a) at the beginning, insert “Subject to paragraph (6A) below”;
- (b) after “under paragraph (1)” insert “or since the FSCS has ceased to be on the committee”;
- (c) at the end, insert “subject to paragraph (6A) below”.

(3) After paragraph (6) insert—

> (6A) The liquidation committee continues to exist and can act so long as it consists of at least the FSCS.
