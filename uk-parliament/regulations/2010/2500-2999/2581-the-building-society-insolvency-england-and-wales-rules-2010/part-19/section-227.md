# The Building Society Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2010, section 227

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 227. Definition of “proxy”[^c-3768da51866e35f165a98b7e071a0355]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 8.1 of the 1986 Rules[^f00080].

(2) In paragraph (2) leave out “company”.

(3) In paragraph (4), for “chairman of the meeting” to “official receiver” substitute “chair of the meeting or the building society liquidator”.
