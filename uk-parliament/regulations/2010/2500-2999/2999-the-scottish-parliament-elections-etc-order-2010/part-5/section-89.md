# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2015-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2999

## 89. Advertisements[^key-0e3e89907df2fdf591d2aababd9df8a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00055] shall have effect in relation to the display on any site in Scotland of an advertisement relating specifically to a Scottish parliamentary election as they have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a parliamentary election.
