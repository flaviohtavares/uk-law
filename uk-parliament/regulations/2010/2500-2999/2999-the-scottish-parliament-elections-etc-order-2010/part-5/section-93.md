# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2015-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2999
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Premises used for election purposes[^c-bee3be8bbd2e7aeaac92ec66d1712617]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 98 of the 1983 Act[^f00057] (premises not affected for rates) shall have effect as if the reference to public meetings in furtherance of a person’s candidature at an election included a reference to public meetings promoting a particular result at a Scottish parliamentary election.

## Annotations

[^c-bee3be8bbd2e7aeaac92ec66d1712617]: Commencement: Art. 93 in force at 30.12.2010, see [art. 1(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2999/article/1/1)

[^f00057]: Footnote: Section 98 was repealed for England and Wales by the Local Government Finance Act [1988 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41), section 149 and Schedule 13, Part 1, but that repeal does not extend to Scotland.
