# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 75

## 75. Prohibition of paid canvassers[^key-28701fcc4cada2b4b3865ed3f7da513c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person (“P”) is, either before, during or after a Scottish parliamentary election, engaged or employed for payment or promise of payment as a canvasser for the purpose of promoting or procuring a particular result at the election both P and the person so engaging or employing P shall be guilty of illegal employment.
