# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 70

## 70. Illegal canvassing by police officers[^key-cc42c5cc9d1d47ba4946169e065b34ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No member of a police force shall, at a Scottish parliamentary election, by word, message, writing or in any other manner, endeavour to persuade any person to give, or dissuade any person from giving, that person’s vote, whether as an elector or as proxy, for the return of any constituency or regional member where the constituency or region falls wholly or partly within the police area for which that police force is maintained.

(2) A person acting in contravention of paragraph (1) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale; but nothing in that paragraph shall subject a member of a police force to any penalty for anything done in the discharge of that person’s duty as a member of the force.
