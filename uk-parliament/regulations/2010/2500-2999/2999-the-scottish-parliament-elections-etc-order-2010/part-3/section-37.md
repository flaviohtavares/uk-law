# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 37

## 37. Expenses of constituency candidate[^c-8299a874083515c2327f1aa2ed1d9fc4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, sums paid and expenses incurred by a candidate for return as a constituency member in respect of whom the CRO has received a certificate issued by a nominating officer of a registered party under rule 7 of the Scottish Parliamentary Election Rules (authorisation to use a registered party’s name and description) are not to be regarded as having been paid or incurred by that party.
