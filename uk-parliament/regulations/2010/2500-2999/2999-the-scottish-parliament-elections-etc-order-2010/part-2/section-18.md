# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2015-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2999

## 18. Payment by and to returning officers[^key-e71c759ba4c29f74af162661e38f622f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 29 of the 1983 Act[^f00027] (payments by and to returning officers) shall apply for the purposes of a Scottish parliamentary election subject to the following modifications—

- (a) any reference to a parliamentary election shall be construed as a reference to a Scottish parliamentary election;
- (b) any reference to a returning officer shall be construed as a reference to a CRO and a RRO;
- (ba)[^key-a9bc443c55af7be168a8e10566f9e43e] any reference to the Secretary of State shall be construed as a reference to the Scottish Ministers;
- (bb) any reference to the Consolidated Fund shall be construed as a reference to the Scottish Consolidated Fund;
- (c) subsections (1) and (2) (expenses in connection with the writ for a parliamentary election) shall be disregarded;
- (d) the reference in subsection (5) to “section 30 below” (taxation of returning officer’s account) shall be construed as a reference to article 19;
- (e) in subsection (8) (form and submission of returning officer’s accounts), for “Regulations may” substitute “The Secretary of State may by regulations”;
- (f) after subsection (8) insert—

  > (8A) Any order made under subsection (3) and any regulations made under subsection (8) may make different provision in relation to different cases or in relation to different classes of case.

  ;
- (g)[^key-c776dc045ccb43a9dc2b7392ebb87fd4] subsection (9) (Treasury consent) shall be disregarded.
