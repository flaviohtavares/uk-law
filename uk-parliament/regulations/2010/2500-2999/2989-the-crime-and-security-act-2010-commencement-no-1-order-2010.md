# The Crime and Security Act 2010 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2989 (C. 133)

Police, England And Wales

The Crime and Security Act 2010 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2010

Made

14th December 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(1) of the Crime and Security Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Crime and Security Act 2010 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2010.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Crime and Security Act 2010.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 31st January 2011

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following sections of the Act is 31st January 2011:

- (a) section 37 (application for variation or discharge of injunction);
- (b) section 38 (power of court to remand).

James Brokenshire

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

14th December 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 37 and section 38 of the Crime and Security Act 2010 into force on 31st January 2011. Section 37 amends section 42 of the Policing and Crime Act 2009 and section 38 amends Schedule 5 to that Act, both of which are also being brought into force on 31st January 2011.
