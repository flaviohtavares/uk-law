# The National Assembly for Wales Referendum (Assembly Act Provisions) (Referendum Question, Date of Referendum Etc.) Order 2010, section 16

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Explanatory material[^c-057c99694c65b58ad3dc62079b0ca65a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission must take such steps, as they think appropriate, to promote public awareness in Wales about the referendum, the subject matter of the referendum and how to vote in the referendum.
