# The National Assembly for Wales Referendum (Assembly Act Provisions) (Referendum Question, Date of Referendum Etc.) Order 2010, section 5

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Referendum period[^key-c2363dc9d331b63b37fb3e17d4d657b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of Part 7 of the 2000 Act, the referendum period for the referendum begins with the date on which this Order comes into force and ends with the date of the poll at the referendum.
