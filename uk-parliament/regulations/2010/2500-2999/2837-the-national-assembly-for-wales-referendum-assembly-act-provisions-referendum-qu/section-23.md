# The National Assembly for Wales Referendum (Assembly Act Provisions) (Referendum Question, Date of Referendum Etc.) Order 2010, section 23

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Advertisements[^key-56d724c70fc79503f51ed02049ffe0db]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992[^f00021] apply in relation to the display on any site in Wales of an advertisement relating specifically to the referendum as they apply in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a pending election to which those Regulations apply.
