# The National Assembly for Wales Referendum (Assembly Act Provisions) (Referendum Question, Date of Referendum Etc.) Order 2010, section 3

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Referendum and date of poll[^c-9350cab6232d3c66de4c3c6a292c5f77]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A referendum is to be held throughout Wales about whether the Assembly Act provisions should come into force[^f00016].

(2) The poll at the referendum must be held on 3rd March 2011.
