# The Harbour School (Amendment) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2804

Education, England

The Harbour School (Amendment) Order 2010

Made

16th November 2010

Laid before Parliament

25th November 2010

Coming into force

15th December 2010

The Council of the City of Portsmouth (“the authority”), being a qualifying body for the purposes of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“the Act”), have, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Act, consulted such persons as appear to them to be appropriate;

It is the opinion of the Secretary of State for Education pursuant to section 1(1)(a) of the Act, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2), that the extension of the period for which the Harbour School Order 2007[^f00002] has effect may contribute to the raising of educational standards in England;

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1) and 3(1)(a)(iii) of the Act, on the application of the authority, makes the following Order[^f00003]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Harbour School (Amendment) Order 2010 and comes into force on 15th December 2010.

## 2. Amendment

In article 10 of the Harbour School Order 2007 (time period) for “16th December 2010” substitute “16th December 2013”.

Jonathan Hill

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

16th November 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under sections 2 and 3 of the Education Act 2002 and amends the Harbour School Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”) to provide that the 2007 Order is extended and will cease to have effect on 16th December 2013.
