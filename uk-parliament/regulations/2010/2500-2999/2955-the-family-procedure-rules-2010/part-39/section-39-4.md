## 39.4. Where to apply[^c-6f18a98ebdd7ac47a0b62b3a2b8cf834]

*Extent: E+W*

An application for an attachment of earnings order to which this Chapter applies must be sent to the family court.

( His Majesty’s[^c-c4b32da272cad3e1c80e04ed8ef58e29] Courts and Tribunals Service publishes information to identify the appropriate location to which an application for an attachment of earnings order should be sent.)
