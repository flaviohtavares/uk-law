## 39.5. Application for an attachment of earnings order[^key-6f18a98ebdd7ac47a0b62b3a2b8cf834]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an application is made for an attachment of earnings order on the making of the maintenance order or of an order varying the maintenance order, the remainder of this rule and rule 39.6 do not apply.

(2) A creditor who wishes to apply for an attachment of earnings order must file—

- (a) an application in accordance with rule 33.3(1); and
- (b) a copy of the sealed <sup>(GL)</sup> maintenance order.

(3) When the documents mentioned in paragraph (2) are filed with the court, the court officer must fix a day for the hearing of the application.
