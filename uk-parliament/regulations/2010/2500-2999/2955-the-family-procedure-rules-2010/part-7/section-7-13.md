## 7.13. References to respondents[^key-ded58967ac7753f9db74f558aedf6c5f]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a respondent makes an application for a matrimonial order or a civil partnership order, unless the context otherwise requires, the rules in this Part shall apply with necessary modifications as if the reference to a respondent is a reference to the applicant in the other party’s application for a matrimonial order or a civil partnership order.
