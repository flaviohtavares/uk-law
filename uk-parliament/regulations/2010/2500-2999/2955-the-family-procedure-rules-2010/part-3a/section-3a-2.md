# The Family Procedure Rules 2010, section 3A.2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2955

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3A.2. Application of provisions in this Part[^c-c31fab2c436238e66887a59bc9717311]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Rule 3A.4 does not apply to a party who is a child.

(2) Rules 3A.3 to 3A.5 do not apply to a party who is a protected party.

(3)[^c-86aa30b90620f5769674c35715f2fa63] Rules 3A.3 to 3A.5 do not apply to a party or witness who—

- (a) falls within the assumption set out at rule 3A.2A(1); and
- (b) has not made a request of a kind referred to in rule 3A.2A(2).
