# The Family Procedure Rules 2010, section 38.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2955
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38.1. Scope and interpretation[^c-78b0c120027e9a299f4da77935234708]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part contains rules about the [^c-ad4086dc7d664f8183afef2842919d38]... recognition and enforcement of incoming[^c-1db51976c3f22e0cfb92771a2861d257] protection measures [^c-2ff43f2d8e3e9f1ca0aa7a272e97b9e3]....

(2) In this Part—

- [^c-015a69643dee96355c0f76d920fb19ba]...
- [^c-3c6a184de5aa989c07d821c61dc2c9c9]...
- “Article 11 notice” means the notification required by Article 11 of the Protection Measures Regulation;
- “Article 14 certificate” means a certificate issued under Article 14 of the Protection Measures Regulation;
- [^c-360866def0945fe4c51f70eddccbb7aa]...
- “person causing the risk” has the meaning given to it in the Protection Measures Regulation; and
- “protected person” has the meaning given to it in the Protection Measures Regulation.
