## 40.10. Interpretation[^c-3dbb655a015a8798d9ff7ba0628b5784]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter, “stop order” means an order of the High Court not to take, in relation to securities specified in the order, any of the steps listed in section 5(5) of the 1979 Act.
