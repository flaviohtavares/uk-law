## 40.17. Amendment of a stop notice[^c-3dbb655a015a8798d9ff7ba0628b5784]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any securities are incorrectly described in a stop notice which has been obtained and served in accordance with rule 40.15, the applicant may request an amended stop notice in accordance with that rule.

(2) The amended stop notice takes effect when it is served.
