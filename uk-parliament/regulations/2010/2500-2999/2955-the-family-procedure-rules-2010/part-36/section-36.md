# The Family Procedure Rules 2010, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2955
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36.1. Transitional provisions

*Extent: E+W*

Practice Direction 36A shall make provision for the extent to which these rules shall apply to proceedings started before the day on which they come into force.

## 36.2. Pilot schemes

*Extent: E+W*

Practice directions may modify or disapply any provision of these rules—

- (a) for specified periods; and
- (b) in relation to proceedings in specified courts,

during the operation of pilot schemes for assessing the use of new practices and procedures in connection with proceedings.
