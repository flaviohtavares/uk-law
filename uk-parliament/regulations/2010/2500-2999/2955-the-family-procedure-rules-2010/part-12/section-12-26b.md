## 12.26B. Disapplication of rule 4.1(3)(a) court’s power to extend or shorten the time for compliance with a rule[^c-67f1b955f8dc6a46e9656c48df483cb8]

*Extent: E+W*

Rule 4.1(3)(a) does not apply to any period that is for the time being allowed under section 32(1)(a)(ii) of the 1989 Act.
