## 29.18. Application for change of area[^key-0bd6faf9a0ce4d6c17b7b465ca7d6091]

*Extent: E+W*

The Part 18 procedure applies to an application to the family court for existing proceedings to be heard in a different Designated Family Judge area.

(His Majesty’s[^key-dcbd28d409804763284a5ef44454ab28] Courts and Tribunals Service publishes information to enable Designated Family Judge areas to be identified.)
