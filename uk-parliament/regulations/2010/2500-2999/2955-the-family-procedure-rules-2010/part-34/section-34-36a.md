## 34.36A. Directions as to stays, documents and translations[^key-43340d4b3ceeafecda494295544be0d2]

*Extent: E+W*

At any stage in proceedings for registration of a maintenance order under this Section of this Chapter, the court may give directions about the conduct of the proceedings, including—

- (a) staying of proceedings in accordance with—
  - (i)[^key-1f8d3d2746fc5967b006a1cf88548abc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii)[^key-1f8d3d2746fc5967b006a1cf88548abc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^key-1f8d3d2746fc5967b006a1cf88548abc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iv)[^key-1f8d3d2746fc5967b006a1cf88548abc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (v)[^key-2b1e10b524ea08a56066885205390fb9] Article 30(6) of the 2007 Hague Convention;
- (b) the provision of documents in accordance with—
  - (i)[^key-48d96ac90dd9c1d20933ddad2df2510e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii)[^key-48d96ac90dd9c1d20933ddad2df2510e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^key-48d96ac90dd9c1d20933ddad2df2510e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iv)[^key-48d96ac90dd9c1d20933ddad2df2510e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (v)[^key-646fa5ec7b5600a24c776ebf7f5011ef] Article 25 or 30 of the 2007 Hague Convention;
- (c) the provision of translations in accordance with—
  - (i)[^key-8a82cb1c3266edd759c78257ed55db28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii)[^key-8a82cb1c3266edd759c78257ed55db28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^key-8a82cb1c3266edd759c78257ed55db28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iv)[^key-8a82cb1c3266edd759c78257ed55db28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (v)[^key-93de9a8329446517ef8edb8a4a0a4888] in relation to an application under this Section relating to the 2007 Hague Convention, without prejudice to Article 44 of that Convention.
