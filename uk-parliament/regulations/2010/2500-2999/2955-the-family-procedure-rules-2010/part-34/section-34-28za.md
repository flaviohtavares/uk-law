## 34.28ZA. Notification of proceedings in a Hague Convention Country or in the United States of America[^c-d290d4a2f8200648315613d026b91722]

*Extent: E+W*

Practice Direction 34E applies where the court officer receives from the Lord Chancellor notice of the institution of proceedings, including notice of the substance of a claim, in a Hague Convention Country or in the United States of America in relation to the making, variation or revocation of a maintenance order.
