## 34.40. Enforcement of orders of the High Court or the family court[^key-54ba85089d6ee5d1e9045f09a91b2366][^key-022fd31db12b6090cfae18da5216a572]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where a person wishes to enforce a maintenance order obtained in the High Court or the family court[^key-3fae082d33d66fc3e2ab7d6670072865] in [^key-534fcc6f788b2c72cd45d3687773e3c2]... a State bound by the 2007 Hague Convention [^key-75bac1b67c7f408a8182fd7b1afb2966]....

(2) Subject to the requirements of Practice Direction 34A, rules 74.12 (application for a certified copy of a judgment) and 74.13 (evidence in support) of the CPR apply in relation to—

- (a)[^key-62174f5ed380c9053a2c5c4cbf3d628c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) an application for a certified copy of a judgment and a certificate giving particulars relating to the judgment and the proceedings in which it was given.
