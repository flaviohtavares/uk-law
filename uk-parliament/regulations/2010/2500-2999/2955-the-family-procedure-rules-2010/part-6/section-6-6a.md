# The Family Procedure Rules 2010, section 6.6A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2955

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.6A. Time for serving an application by the applicant[^c-f1f3516c77c520c42c0d7fb6a66583f5]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the applicant serves the application, the applicant must complete the step required by the following table in relation to the method of service chosen before 12.00 midnight on the day 28 days after the date of issue of the application.

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Method of service</th><th colspan="2">Step required</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td colspan="2">First class post, document exchange or other service which provides for delivery on the next business day</td><td>Posting, leaving with, delivering to or collection by the relevant service provider</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2">Personal service under rule 6.7, by someone other than the applicant personally</td><td>Leaving it with the person to be served</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2">Email service under rule 6.7A</td><td>Sending the application by e-mail and sending the notice required by rule 6.7A(2) by posting, leaving with, delivering to or collection by the relevant service provider</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
