## 3.6. Applications to which the MIAM requirement applies[^c-e91d532a33b448779354909a88339e3e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The MIAM requirement applies to any application to initiate the proceedings specified in paragraph (2), unless a MIAM exemption [^c-b81bb99a76031b8ea26c531795fe7315]... applies.

(2) The specified proceedings are—

- (a) the private law proceedings relating to children specified in Practice Direction 3A; and
- (b) the proceedings for a financial remedy specified in Practice Direction 3A.
