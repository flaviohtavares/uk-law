## 3.9. Conduct of MIAMs[^c-e91d532a33b448779354909a88339e3e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Only an authorised family mediator may conduct a MIAM.

(2) At the MIAM, the authorised family mediator must—

- (a) provide information about the principles, process and different models of mediation, and information about other methods of non-court dispute resolution;
- (b)[^c-156b209480519913e29359fc37be6a48] consider and explain the potential benefits of mediation and other methods of non-court dispute resolution as a means of resolving the dispute;
- (c) assess whether there has been, or is a risk of, domestic abuse;[^c-b0c7d73a7f08e8087c035b712ec4bb0e]
- (d) assess whether there has been, or is a risk of, harm by a prospective party to a child that would be a subject of the application;[^c-147012ddd992375bce4472509ed97816]
- (e)[^c-b19f4749fa017441cec41764b9fd95b3] indicate to those attending the MIAM which form, or forms, of non-court dispute resolution may be most suitable as a means of resolving the dispute, and why; and
- (f) where sub-paragraph (e) applies, provide information to those attending the MIAM about how to proceed with the form, or forms, of non-court dispute resolution in question.
