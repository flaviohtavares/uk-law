# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010, section 14

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Offences and penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for any person to contravene, or cause or permit another person to contravene, any of regulations 3 to 5, 8 or 9.

(2) Any person guilty of an offence under paragraph (1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
