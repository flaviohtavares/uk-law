# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010, SCHEDULE 1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Declaration of Conformity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 3(2)

**1.** A person who makes a declaration of conformity declares that an energy-related product to which the declaration relates complies with the applicable implementing measure.

**2.** A declaration of conformity—

- (a) must be in writing; and
- (b) may be in respect of more than one energy-related product.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**3.**[^M_E_41490da5-492f-4986-a84e-c39b1ec27e37] A declaration of conformity must include—

- (a) the name and address of the person making it;
- (b) a description of the product sufficient for unambiguous identification;
- (c) where appropriate—
  - (i) the references of the designated[^M_F_0b071d6a-d573-4c38-faea-599aa0265019] standards applied;
  - (ii) the other technical standards and specifications used;
  - (iii)[^M_F_74d88399-cc06-4c7d-a45b-9f3ca959d37e] the reference to other legislation, not referred to in paragraph 4, providing for the affixing of the UK marking; and
- (d) the identification and signature of the individual empowered to bind the person making the declaration.

*Extent: N.I.*

**3.**[^M_E_effeec0f-7ea0-49cc-9db8-6e277b7de59b] A declaration of conformity must include—

- (a) the name and address of the person making it;
- (b) a description of the product sufficient for unambiguous identification;
- (c) where appropriate—
  - (i) the references of the harmonised standards applied;
  - (ii) the other technical standards and specifications used;
  - (iii) the reference to other EU[^V_NI_3bf10eeb-c207-8f58-079b-9070e2574346] legislation, not referred to in paragraph 4, providing for the affixing of the CE mark that is applied; and
- (d) the identification and signature of the individual empowered to bind the person making the declaration.

*Extent: E+W+S*
