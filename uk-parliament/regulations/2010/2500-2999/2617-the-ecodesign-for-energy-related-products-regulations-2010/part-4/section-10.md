# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010, section 10

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Notifying authority, competent authority and market surveillance authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d2e8182e68e8d61699b8366fec47465d] For the purposes of Article R 2(8) and R 2(9) of Annex I to the Marketing Decision the competent national authority is the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2e8182e68e8d61699b8366fec47465d]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-364589a0cc0675e64baac72ca305990c] For the purposes of Article R(14) to R(28) of Annex I to the Marketing Decision the notifying authority is the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-364589a0cc0675e64baac72ca305990c]

(3) For the purposes of Article 2(18) of RAMS the market surveillance authority is the Secretary of State.
