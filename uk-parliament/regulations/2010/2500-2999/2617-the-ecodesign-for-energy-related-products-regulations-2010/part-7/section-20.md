## 20. Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are revoked—

- (a) the Ecodesign for Energy-Using Products Regulations 2007[^f00007]; and
- (b) the Ecodesign for Energy-Using Products (Amendment) Regulations 2009[^f00008].
