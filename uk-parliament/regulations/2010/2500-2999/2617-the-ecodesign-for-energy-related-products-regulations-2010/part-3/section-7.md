# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010, section 7

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_39c515ce-1bf6-4101-8e78-fe53aa6fa79b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Unless the contrary is proved, where—

- (a) an energy-related product; or
- (b) the packaging or documentation that accompanies that product,

bears the UK[^c-a2272576f50c58c0210110f89d991986] marking, the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure.

(2)[^c-9347d3f80e5bcfc0e308c61476d43de4] Unless the contrary is proved, where designated standards have been applied to an energy-related product, the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure to the extent that the designated standards relate to the requirements of that measure.

(3) Unless the contrary is proved, where an energy-related product has been awarded a community eco-label before IP completion day[^c-c8b7af431956d9c6a19513de0c262b4b], the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure to the extent that the Community eco-label relates to the requirements of that measure.

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3) “Community eco-label” means a label that meets the requirements of Regulation [(EC) No 1980/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1980) of the European Parliament and of the Council on a revised Community eco-label award scheme[^f00006].

## 7. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_9d983d0e-9bbb-43bd-bff2-291e6d4b5620]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Unless the contrary is proved, where—

- (a) an energy-related product; or
- (b) the packaging or documentation that accompanies that product,

bears the CE marking, the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure.

(2) Unless the contrary is proved, where—

- (a) harmonised standards have been applied to an energy-related product; and
- (b) the reference numbers of those standards have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union,

the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure to the extent that the harmonised standards relate to the requirements of that measure.

(3) Unless the contrary is proved, where an energy-related product has been awarded a community eco-label, the product is presumed to comply with the applicable implementing measure to the extent that the Community eco-label relates to the requirements of that measure.

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3) “Community eco-label” means a label that meets the requirements of Regulation [(EC) No 1980/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1980) of the European Parliament and of the Council on a revised Community eco-label award scheme.
