# The Tax Avoidance Schemes (Penalty) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2743

Taxes

The Tax Avoidance Schemes (Penalty) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made

15th November 2010

Coming into force

1st January 2011

A draft of these Regulations was laid before, and approved by, the House of Commons under section 98C(2F)(c) of the Taxes Management Act 1970[^f00001].

Accordingly the Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 98C(2C)(a) of the Taxes Management Act 1970.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Tax Avoidance Schemes (Penalty) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st January 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Tax Avoidance Schemes (Penalty) Regulations 2007

The Tax Avoidance Schemes (Penalty) Regulations 2007[^f00002] are amended as follows.

**3.** For regulation 3 substitute—

> **3.** For the purposes of section 98C(2A) of TMA 1970, the prescribed sum is £5,000.

**4.** For regulation 4 substitute—

> **4.** For the purposes of section 98C(2B) of TMA 1970, the prescribed sum is £5,000.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the amount of the two daily penalties imposed by section 98C of the Taxes Management Act [1970 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9) in certain circumstances. Section 98C penalises the failure to comply with the notification requirements of Part 7 of the Finance Act [2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12). Part 7 of the Finance Act 2004 was amended by the Finance Act [2007 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/11) to create new obligations on promoters to provide information in respect of tax avoidance schemes. The initial penalty under section 98C(1)(a) was amended by the Finance Act [2010 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/13) to include a daily penalty. The daily penalties are increased where there is a failure to provide information following the making of an order under sections 306A and 314A of the Finance Act 2004.

A full Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available at http://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/20091222074811/http://hmrc.gov.uk/budget2010/strength-revise-dotas-ia-5295.pdf.
