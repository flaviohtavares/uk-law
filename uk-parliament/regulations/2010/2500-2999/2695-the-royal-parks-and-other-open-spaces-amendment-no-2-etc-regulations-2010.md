# The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) (No.2) etc. Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2695

Open Spaces

The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) (No.2) etc. Regulations 2010

Made

1st November 2010

Coming into force in accordance with Regulation 1

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(1) of the Parks Regulation (Amendment) Act 1926[^f00001] and by section 62 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002].

A draft of these Regulations has been laid before Parliament for a period of forty days pursuant to section 6 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946, and that period has expired without either House resolving that the Regulations be not made.

## 1. Citation, Commencement and Interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) (No.2) etc. Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they were made.

## 2. Amendment of The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) etc. Regulations 2010

Regulations 1(2) and 5 to 8 of The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) etc Regulations 2010[^f00003] are revoked.

John Penrose

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

1st November 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke those provisions of The Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces (Amendment) etc. Regulations 2010 which relate to the introduction of car parking charges in Bushy Park and Richmond Park with the effect that no such charges will apply.
