# The Value Added Tax (Construction of Buildings) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 486

Value Added Tax

The Value Added Tax (Construction of Buildings) Order 2010

Made

1st March 2010

Laid before the House of Commons

1st March 2010

Coming into force

1st April 2010

The Treasury make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(4) and 96(9) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Construction of Buildings) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Amendment of Group 5 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994

(1) In Note (21)[^f00002] of Group 5 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (construction of buildings, etc)—

- (a) before paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) a private registered provider of social housing,

  [^f00003],
- (b) in paragraph (a), after “1996” insert “(Welsh registered social landlords)”, and
- (c) for paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) a registered social landlord within the meaning of the [Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2001/10) (Scottish registered social landlords), or

  .

(2) Until the coming into force of provision defining “private registered provider of social housing” in enactments and instruments generally, that expression in Note (21) of Group 5 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 means persons listed in the register of providers of social housing maintained under Chapter 3 of Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008[^f00004] who are not local authorities within the meaning of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00005].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Note (21) of Group 5 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23), with effect from 1st April 2010.

By virtue of item 3 of Group 5, certain supplies to relevant housing associations (“RHAs”) are zero rated for the purposes of value added tax. The definition of RHAs, for the purposes of item 3, is in Note (21) of Group 5 and includes registered social landlords within the meaning of Part 1 of the Housing Act [1996 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52). The system of registered social landlords under that Part was established in England and Wales. Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act [2008 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/17) replaces the system in England and preserves it in Wales. Article 2(1)(a) inserts Note (21)(za) so that the definition of RHAs includes private registered providers of social housing under the new system for England. Article 2(1)(b) of the Order amends Note (21)(a) to indicate that the system under Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996 continues in Wales. In relation to Scotland, article 2(1)(c) amends Note (21)(b) to refer to registered social landlords under the Housing (Scotland) Act [2001 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2001/10). Article 2(2) defines “private registered provider of social housing” until the coming into force of provision defining that expression in enactments and instruments generally.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
