# The Railways (Public Service Obligations) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 402

Transport

The Railways (Public Service Obligations) Regulations 2010

Made

18th February 2010

Laid before Parliament

24th February 2010

Coming into force

18th March 2010

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001].

He is designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to measures relating to railways and railway transport.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Railways (Public Service Obligations) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 18th March 2010.

## 2. Amendment of section 136 of the Railways Act 1993

(1) Section 136 of the Railways Act 1993[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) After subsection (2A) insert —

> (2B) Subsections (2) and (2A) cease to have effect on 3rd December 2012.

.

(3) In subsection (11), for the definition of “the public service obligations regulations” substitute—

> “**the public service obligations regulations**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) save that before 3rd December 2012 it means—
>
> - (a) in respect of the carriage of goods by railway, [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 1191/69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1969/1191) as amended by [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 1893/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/1893); and
> - (b) in respect of the carriage of passengers by railway, Regulation [(EC) No. 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370);

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Sadiq Khan

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make changes to section 136 of the Railways Act 1993 in consequence of Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) of the European Parliament and of the Council (O.J. L315, 3.12.07, p.1).

Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) replaces and repeals [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 1191/69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1969/1191) (O.J. L156, 28.6.1969, p.1) in relation to the carriage of passengers by railway. In relation to the carriage of goods by railway, Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) provides that, whilst Regulation [(EEC) No. 1191/69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1969/1191) is repealed, its provisions will continue to apply until the end of 2nd December 2012.

Regulation 2(2) inserts subsection (2B) into section 136. Subsection (2B) provides that subsections (2) and (2A) (which relate to the carriage of goods by railway) cease to have effect on 3rd December 2012..

Regulation 2(3) amends section 136(11) to provide that a reference to the “**public service obligations regulations**” is a reference to Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) save that, before 3rd December 2012, in relation to the carriage of goods by railway, it is a reference to [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 1191/69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1969/1191) as amended by [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 1893/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/1893).

The European Union Regulations referred to in these Regulations may be downloaded without charge from the European Union Law Website (EURLEX):

http://eur-lex.europa.eu/en/index.htm

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the costs of business, the public sector, third sector organisations, regulators or consumers is foreseen.
