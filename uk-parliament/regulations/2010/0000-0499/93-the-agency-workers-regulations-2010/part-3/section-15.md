# The Agency Workers Regulations 2010, section 15

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Restrictions on contracting out

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 203 [^c20081041] of the 1996 Act (restrictions on contracting out) shall apply in relation to these Regulations as if they were contained in that Act.

## Annotations

[^c20081041]: Marginal citation: Section 203 was amended by the [Employment Rights (Dispute Resolution) Act 1998 (c.8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/8) and by the [Employment Relations Act 1999 (c.26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/26), [section 44](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/26/section/44) and Schedule 9.
