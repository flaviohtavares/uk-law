# The Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 37 (C. 6)

Perpetuities And Accumulations, England And Wales

The Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010

Made

5th January 2010

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 22(2) of the Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

Sections 1 to 21 of and the Schedule to the Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 2009 shall come into force on 6th April 2010.

Signed on the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

5th January 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 1 to 21 of and the Schedule to the Perpetuities and Accumulations Act [2009 (c.18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/18) on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2010. The rest of the Act, namely sections 22 to 24, came into force on Royal Assent.
