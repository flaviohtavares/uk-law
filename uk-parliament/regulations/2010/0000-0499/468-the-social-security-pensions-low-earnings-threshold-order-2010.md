# The Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 468

Social Security

The Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2010

Made

23rd February 2010

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2010

Coming into force

6th April 2010

In accordance with section 148A (revaluation of low earnings threshold) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001], the Secretary of State has reviewed the general level of earnings obtaining in Great Britain.

It appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of earnings has increased during the review period.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 148A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 6th April 2010.

## 2. Low earnings threshold

For the purposes of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002], it is directed that the low earnings threshold[^f00003] for the tax years following the tax year 2009 – 2010 shall be £14,100.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Angela Eagle

Minster of State

Department for Work and Pensions

23rd February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made following a review by the Secretary of State under section 148A (revaluation of low earnings threshold) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) of the general level of earnings in Great Britain with a view to determining whether, and if so by how much, the amount of the low earnings threshold for the purposes of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) should be increased for future tax years.

The low earnings threshold is the amount by reference to which the three surplus earnings bands are determined for the purpose of calculating the additional pension (the state second pension) in a state retirement pension.

As a result of that review, it appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of such earnings during the period from 1st October 2008 to 30th September 2009 has increased by 1.2%.

This Order directs that the low earnings threshold for the tax years following 2009 – 2010 shall be £14,100. The threshold for the tax year 2009 – 2010 was directed to be £13,900 by virtue of the Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/610](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/610)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no new impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
