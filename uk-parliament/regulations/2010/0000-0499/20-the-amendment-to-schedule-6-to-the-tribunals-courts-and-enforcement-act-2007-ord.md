# The Amendment to Schedule 6 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 20

Tribunals And Inquiries

The Amendment to Schedule 6 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 Order 2010

Made

6th January 2010

Coming into force

14th February 2010

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 37(1)(a) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001].

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 49(5) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Amendment to Schedule 6 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 Order 2010 and comes into force on 14th February 2010.

## 2. Addition to the list of tribunals in Schedule 6

In Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (tribunals for the purposes of section 30), insert the following entry at the appropriate place—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Asylum and Immigration Tribunal | Section 81 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00002] |

.

By the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Ministry of Justice

6th January 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Part 4 of Schedule 6 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 lists the Tribunals that are Tribunals for the purposes of section 30 of the 2007 Act. Section 30 of the 2007 Act gives the Lord Chancellor the power to, by Order, transfer the functions of those Tribunals to the First-tier Tribunal and the Upper Tribunal. Section 37(1)(a) of the 2007 Act allows the Lord Chancellor to, by Order, add a Tribunal to that list. This Order adds the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal to the list of Tribunals in Part 4.
