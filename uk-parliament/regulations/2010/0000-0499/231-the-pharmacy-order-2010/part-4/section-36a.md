## 36A. Additional requirements for specified state professionals[^c-367a615bb6a9e585d2d9aada7f6363ac]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any fee prescribed by the Council under article 36(1) in connection with the making of an entry in the register relating to a specified state professional must be—

- (a) reasonable and proportionate to the cost of dealing with such an applicant’s application;
- (b) transparent, and made public in advance; and
- (c) payable by electronic means through the Council’s website.
