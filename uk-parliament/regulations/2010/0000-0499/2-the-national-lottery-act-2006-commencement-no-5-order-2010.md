# The National Lottery Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 2 (C. 1)

National Lottery

The National Lottery Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2010

Made

5th January 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22 of the National Lottery Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This order may be cited as the National Lottery Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement

Section 5 of the National Lottery Act 2006 shall come into force on 6th January 2010.

Siôn Simon

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

5th January 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 5 of the National Lottery Act 2006, which inserts into the National Lottery etc. Act [1993 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/39) a new Section 7A (Annual Fee), a provision for the charging of annual licence fees, and a provision for a licence to be revoked for failure to pay an annual fee.
