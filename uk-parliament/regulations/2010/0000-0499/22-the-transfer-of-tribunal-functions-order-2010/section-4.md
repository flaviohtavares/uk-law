# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions Order 2010, section 4

> Text as at 2018-05-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Persons becoming judges and other members of the First-tier Tribunal and the Upper Tribunal[^key-2ed44af8c65047fdd088d5b20e9af59d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who, immediately before this Order comes into force, holds an office listed in a part of Schedule 1, is to hold the office or offices set out in the corresponding entry in the table below.

| Part of Schedule 1 | Office or offices |
| --- | --- |
| Part 1 | Transferred-in judge of the Upper Tribunal |
| Part 2 | Transferred-in other member of the Upper Tribunal |
| Part 3 | Deputy judge of the Upper Tribunal and transferred in judge of the First-tier Tribunal |
| Part 4 | Transferred-in judge of the First-tier Tribunal |
| Part 5 | Transferred-in other member of the First-tier Tribunal |
