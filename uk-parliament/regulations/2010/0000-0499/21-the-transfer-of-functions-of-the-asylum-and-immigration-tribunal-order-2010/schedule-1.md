# The Transfer of Functions of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal Order 2010, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2013-08-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Consequential provisions – primary legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 5(1)

### 1. Immigration Act 1971[^key-0d61b0723c9468b6babc4fc8a8e6caf5]

Schedule 2 to the Immigration Act 1971[^f00006] (administrative provisions as to control on entry etc.) is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-4263b65e869a3200565b61437c18618f] In paragraphs 22(1A), (2), and (3), 23(1) and (2), 24(2)(a) and (3), 29(2), (3) and (6) and 33(3) for “the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal”, in each place, substitute “the First-tier Tribunal”.

**3.**[^key-02fb7976f87b142d3a1222cb6a1f4780] For paragraph 25 substitute—

> **25.** Tribunal Procedure Rules may make provision with respect to applications to the First-tier Tribunal under paragraphs 22 to 24 and matters arising out of such applications.

.

### 4. House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975[^key-609be0e6f80716fda41b690d70dfe957]

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975[^f00007] (bodies of which all members are disqualified) omit the entry relating to the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal.

### 5. Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975[^key-a20f21cb680e1c7ccb9edf9a3d383b59]

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975[^f00008] (bodies of which all members are disqualified) omit the entry relating to the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal.

### 6. Race Relations Act 1976[^key-345d78d44d1386b5bad53459b8592274]

In section 57A(5) of the Race Relations Act 1976[^f00009] (claims under section 19B in immigration cases)—

- (a) in the definition of “immigration appellate body”—
  - (i) for “the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal,” substitute “the First-tier Tribunal,”; and
  - (ii) after “the Special Immigration Appeals Commission,” insert “the Upper Tribunal,”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) of the definition of “relevant decision” for “or Part 5 of the 2002 Act” substitute “, Part 5 of the 2002 Act or section 11 or 13 of the 2007 Act”;
- (c) in the definition of “relevant immigration proceedings” for “or Part 5 of the 2002 Act” substitute “, Part 5 of the 2002 Act or section 11 or 13 of the 2007 Act”; and
- (d) after the definition of “the 2002 Act” insert—

  > - “the 2007 Act” means the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007;

  .

### 7. British Nationality Act 1981[^key-702557743afe1466914fb35bd82b66bc]

In section 40A of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00010] (deprivation of citizenship: appeal)—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal” substitute “the First-tier Tribunal”; and
- (b) omit subsection (3)(b).

### 8. Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^key-c92964e7de01ef87077054d53bc971b8]

### 9. Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^key-d3330a312cdbd87fa6d946f6b494d18d]

In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00012] (tribunals under direct supervision of Council) omit the entry at paragraph 22 relating to immigration and asylum.

The Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993[^f00013] is amended as follows.

**12.**[^key-91a9d36d45a47e747463dd77260465c5] In Part 2 of Schedule 1 (other offices whose holders are appointed by the Lord Chancellor) omit the entry relating to the President or other member of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal.

**13.**[^key-22d5fb7891c73d0437c630e0f879992f] In Schedule 5 (retirement provisions: the relevant offices) omit the entry relating to the President or other member of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal.

### 14. Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act 1997[^key-a457f20695df76cbda47b2c08f6137e5]

The Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act 1997[^f00014] is amended as follows.

**15.**[^key-e74b4b3a2c863ead2bb22e331ae2c659] In section 5(2A) (procedure in relation to jurisdiction under sections 2 and 3) for the words from “rules under section 106” to the end substitute “Tribunal Procedure Rules”.

**16.**[^key-c2e17d145e08680e60ebf242676c6442] In paragraph 5(b) of Schedule 1 (proceedings) for “a legally qualified member of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal” substitute “a judge of the First-tier Tribunal, or of the Upper Tribunal, who is assigned to a chamber with responsibility for immigration and asylum matters”.

**17.**[^key-729af3b2f44c3c54ec4e4cc0d8920541] In paragraphs 1(3)(a) and (b) and (4)(a) and (b) and 2(2)(a) and (b) and (3)(a) and (b) of Schedule 3 (bail: modifications of Schedule 2 to the Immigration Act 1971) for “the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal” substitute “the First-tier Tribunal”.

### 18. Access to Justice Act 1999[^key-4f831b5384bc1a223d1a21db987966e7]

In paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00015] (community legal services: excluded services)—

- (a) after paragraph (g) insert—

  > - (gza) the First-tier Tribunal under—
  >   - (i) Schedule 2 to the Immigration Act 1971,
  >   - (ii) section 40A of the British Nationality Act 1981,
  >   - (iii) Part 5 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, or
  >   - (iv) regulation 26 of the Immigration (European Economic Area) Regulations 2006,

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (ga) after “paragraph (g)” insert “or (gza)”; and
- (c) omit paragraph (h).

.

The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00017] is amended as follows.

**21.**[^key-aa8a66b25ad4f85249a955862fe2e55d] In section 72 (serious criminal) after subsection (10) insert—

> (10A) Subsection (10) also applies in relation to the Upper Tribunal when it acts under section 12(2)(b)(ii) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

.

**22.**[^key-949989a3e0d77e21f6f2b6c41d47f2d3] For section 81 (the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal) substitute—

> **81. Meaning of “the Tribunal”**
>
> In this Part “the Tribunal” means the First-tier Tribunal.

.

**23.**[^key-bd7c094aef399374860f8629825c63b1] In section 85A (matters to be considered: new evidence: exceptions) at the end insert—

> (5) Tribunal Procedure Rules may make provision, for the purposes of subsection (4)(a), about the circumstances in which evidence is to be treated, or not treated, as submitted in support of, and at the time of making, an application.

.

**24.**[^key-67b4c56cad87f6a7c9a4ac4cbc8041ff] In section 87 (successful appeal: direction)—

- (a) for subsection (3) substitute—

  > (3) But a direction under this section shall not have effect while—
  >
  > - (a) an application for permission to appeal under section 11 or 13 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 could be made or is awaiting determination,
  > - (b) permission to appeal to the Upper Tribunal or a court under either of those sections has been granted and the appeal is awaiting determination, or
  > - (c) an appeal has been remitted under section 12 or 14 of that Act and is awaiting determination.

  ; and
- (b) in subsection (4) for “103A” substitute “11 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007”.

**25.**[^key-6d5d8f7773708636229c1dfbe51c70dd] Omit sections 103A to 103E (procedure).

**26.**[^key-6a8cb4c6de7a7876eb7a48c996d8dddc] In section 104 (pending appeal)—

- (a) for subsection (2) substitute—

  > (2) An appeal under section 82(1) is not finally determined for the purpose of subsection (1)(b) while—
  >
  > - (a) an application for permission to appeal under section 11 or 13 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 could be made or is awaiting determination,
  > - (b) permission to appeal under either of those sections has been granted and the appeal is awaiting determination, or
  > - (c) an appeal has been remitted under section 12 or 14 of that Act and is awaiting determination.

  ; and
- (b) in subsections (4B)(b) and (4C) for “any relevant procedure rules (which may include provision about timing)” substitute “Tribunal Procedure Rules”.

**27.**[^key-36d2291b3f5284c8a7918ecc2d0253d7] In section 106 (rules)—

- (a) omit subsections (1), (1A) and (2);
- (b) for subsection (3) substitute—

  > (3) In the case of an appeal under section 82, 83 or 83A or by virtue of section 109, Tribunal Procedure Rules may enable the Tribunal to certify that the appeal had no merit (and shall make provision for the consequences of the issue of a certificate).

  ; and
- (c) in subsection (4) for “rules under subsection (1)” substitute “Tribunal Procedure Rules in connection with proceedings under section 82, 83 or 83A or by virtue of section 109”.

**28.**[^key-978881753cc048a86eeb993e97d24d3f] In section 107 (practice directions)—

- (a) omit subsections (1), (1A) and (4) to (7);
- (b) for subsection (3) substitute—

  > (3) In the case of proceedings under section 82, 83 or 83A or by virtue of section 109, or proceedings in the Upper Tribunal arising out of such proceedings, practice directions under section 23 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007—
  >
  > - (a) may require the Tribunal to treat a specified decision of the Tribunal or Upper Tribunal as authoritative in respect of a particular matter; and
  > - (b) may require the Upper Tribunal to treat a specified decision of the Tribunal or Upper Tribunal as authoritative in respect of a particular matter.

  ; and
- (c) after that subsection insert—

  > (3A) In subsection (3) the reference to a decision of the Tribunal includes—
  >
  > - (a) a decision of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal, and
  > - (b) a decision of the Immigration Appeal Tribunal.

  .

**29.**[^key-51af96252698f498b628a1fcd4c4d152] In section 112 (regulations, &c.)—

- (a) in subsection (2) omit “and rules” and “, other than regulations under section 103D(4),”;
- (b) in subsection (3) omit “and rules”; and
- (c) omit subsections (6) and (7).

**30.**[^key-65cf58c0b6681f1e9e1bea29bebf6c99] Omit Schedule 4 (the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal).
