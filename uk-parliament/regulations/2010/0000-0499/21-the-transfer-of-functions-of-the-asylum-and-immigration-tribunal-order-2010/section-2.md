# The Transfer of Functions of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal Order 2010, section 2

> Text as at 2013-08-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Transfer of functions and abolition of tribunal[^c-a7fd1a8fc9edeb5a49951c0a395eda10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The functions of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal are transferred to the First-tier Tribunal.

(2) The Asylum and Immigration Tribunal is abolished.
