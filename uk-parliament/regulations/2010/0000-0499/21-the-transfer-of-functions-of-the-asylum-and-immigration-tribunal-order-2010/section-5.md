# The Transfer of Functions of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal Order 2010, section 5

> Text as at 2013-08-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Consequential and transitional provisions[^c-6e93c6b8d0bcbd185f4cb78903bc096d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 contains consequential amendments to primary legislation as a consequence of the transfers effected by this Order.

(2) Schedule 2 contains consequential amendments to secondary legislation as a consequence of the transfers effected by this Order.

(3) Schedule 3 contains repeals and revocations as a consequence of the amendments in Schedules 1 and 2.

(4) Schedule 4 contains transitional and saving provisions.
