# The Transfer of Functions of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal Order 2010, section 4

> Text as at 2013-08-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Transfer of Rules[^key-5f3c82ba9df0eb1437f7a1e00471a275]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Procedure) Rules 2005[^f00004] and the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Procedure) (Fast-track) Rules 2005[^f00005] have effect as if they were Tribunal Procedure Rules.
