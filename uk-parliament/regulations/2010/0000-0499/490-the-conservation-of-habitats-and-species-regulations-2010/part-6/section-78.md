# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 78

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 78. Local development orders[^key-41d0cac1db44e038b7b3df695ed18180]

A local development order may not grant planning permission for development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-41d0cac1db44e038b7b3df695ed18180]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site or a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-41d0cac1db44e038b7b3df695ed18180]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-41d0cac1db44e038b7b3df695ed18180]
