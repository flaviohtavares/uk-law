# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 78A

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 78A. Neighbourhood development orders[^c-d7c97e83c9d327d1feb802889858bd02][^c-b33e6b6186937888ec25c5280e230fc5]

A neighbourhood development order may not grant planning permission for development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d7c97e83c9d327d1feb802889858bd02]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site or a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d7c97e83c9d327d1feb802889858bd02]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d7c97e83c9d327d1feb802889858bd02]
