# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 80

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 80. Enterprise zones[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]

(1) Where an order designating an enterprise zone is made, or where a modified enterprise zone scheme is approved, that order or scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]

(2) Where such an order was made or such a scheme was approved before 1st April 2010, that order or scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development of a kind mentioned in paragraph (1), whether or not the development authorised by the permission has been begun. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]

(3) Where an order designating an enterprise zone is made, or where a modified enterprise zone scheme is approved, that order or scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development which is likely to have a significant effect on a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]

(4) Where such an order was made or such a scheme was approved on or after 21st August 2007 and before 1st April 2010, that order or scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development of a kind mentioned in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0ec3a78c310d71e05ffb046ce7a1aabc]
