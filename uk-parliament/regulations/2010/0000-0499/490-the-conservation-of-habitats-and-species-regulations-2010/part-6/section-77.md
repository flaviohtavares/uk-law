# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 77

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 77. Special development orders[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]

(1) A special development order may not grant planning permission for development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]

(2) A special development order made before 1st April 2010 is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development of a kind mentioned in paragraph (1), whether or not the development authorised by the permission has been begun. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]

(3) Nothing in this regulation affects anything done pursuant to a special development order before 1st April 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]

(4) A special development order may not grant planning permission for development which is likely to have a significant effect on a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de13dd19ad012e3ed7eb522a0e9a5cca]
