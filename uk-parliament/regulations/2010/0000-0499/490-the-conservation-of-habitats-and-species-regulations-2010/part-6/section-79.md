# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 79

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 79. Simplified planning zones[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]

(1) Where a simplified planning zone scheme is adopted or approved, that scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]

(2) A simplified planning zone scheme in force before 1st April 2010 is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development of a kind mentioned in paragraph (1), whether or not the development authorised by the permission has been begun. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]

(3) Where a simplified planning zone scheme is adopted or approved, that scheme is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development which is likely to have a significant effect on a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]

(4) A simplified planning zone scheme adopted or approved on or after 21st August 2007 and before 1st April 2010 is not to be taken to grant planning permission for development of a kind mentioned in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e22e350c0102371e2136576f0d78285]
