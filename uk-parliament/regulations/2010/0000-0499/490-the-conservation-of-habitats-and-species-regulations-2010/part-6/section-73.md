# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, section 73

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 73. General development orders[^key-6d12a0e2f08b1d713b8dac3b405767e4][^key-dab0648863183efa38955f58910a929c][^key-1bb4736bd793c4c1ffd64f37c2aa7e09][^key-1016523b5229c5961e67db14ce08b6f0][^key-2eb09709950b3c0147cc0457f1e051da][^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

(1) It is a condition of any planning permission granted by a general development order, whether made before or after 1st April 2010, that development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site or a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

must not be begun until the developer has received written notification of the approval of the local planning authority under regulation 75 (approval of local planning authority). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

(2) It is a condition of any planning permission granted by a general development order made before 1st April 2010 that development which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]
- (b) is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]
- (c) was begun but not completed before that date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

must not be continued until the developer has received written notification of the approval of the local planning authority under regulation 75. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]

(3) Nothing in this regulation affects anything done pursuant to any such planning permission before 1st April 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e99a5d3e22416837dd66f39e2661133]
