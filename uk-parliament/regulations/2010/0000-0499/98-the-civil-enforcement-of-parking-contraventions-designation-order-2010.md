# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions Designation Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 98

Road Traffic, England

The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions Designation Order 2010

Made

28th January 2010

Laid before Parliament

4th February 2010

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

This order is made in exercise of powers conferred by section 89(3) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] and by paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 8, and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 10, to that Act.

The Cheshire East Borough Council[^f00002] has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made, in exercise of those powers, in respect of part of that council’s area.

The Cornwall Council[^f00003] has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made, in exercise of those powers, in respect of the whole of that council’s area.

The Lancashire County Council has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made, in exercise of those powers, in respect of part of that council’s area.

The North Lincolnshire Borough Council[^f00004] has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made, in exercise of those powers, in respect of part of that council’s area.

In accordance with the requirements of paragraph 8(3) of that Schedule 8 and paragraph 3(4) of that Schedule 10, the Secretary of State has consulted the Chief Constable of Cheshire Constabulary, the Chief Constable of the Devon and Cornwall Constabulary, the Chief Constable of the Humberside Police and the Chief Constable of Lancashire Constabulary.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State[^f00005] makes this order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions Designation Order 2010.

(2) Except for articles 2(c) and 3(2)(b), this order shall come into force on 1st March 2010.

(3) Articles 2(c) and 3(2)(b) shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Revocation of previous orders

The following orders are revoked—

- (a) the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Lancashire) Order 2004[^f00006];
- (b) the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Cheshire) (Borough of Crewe and Nantwich) Order 2007[^f00007];
- (c) the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Cornwall) Designation Order 2008[^f00008]; and
- (d) the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Cheshire) (Borough of Macclesfield) Designation Order 2008[^f00009].

## 3. Designation of civil enforcement areas and special enforcement areas under the Traffic Management Act 2004

(1) The Secretary of State designates each of the areas mentioned in paragraph (2) as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and
- (b) a special enforcement area.

(2) Those areas are—

- (a) the part of the area of the Cheshire East Borough Council specified in Schedule 1;
- (b) the whole of the area of The Cornwall Council;
- (c) the part of the area of The Lancashire County Council specified in Schedule 2; and
- (d) the part of the area of The North Lincolnshire Borough Council specified in Schedule 3.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the order)

This order designates part of the area of each of the following councils as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and as a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004—

- the Cheshire East Borough Council;
- The Lancashire County Council; and
- The North Lincolnshire Borough Council.

The order designates the whole of the area of The Cornwall Council as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and as a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.

The order revokes the following orders—

- the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Lancashire) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/2028](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2028));
- the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Cheshire) (Borough of Crewe and Nantwich) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/3566](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3566));
- the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Cornwall) Designation Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1055](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1055)); and
- the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Cheshire) (Borough of Macclesfield) Designation Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1084](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1084)).

An impact assessment has not been produced as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
