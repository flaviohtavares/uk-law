# The National Health Service (Strategic Health Authorities: Further Duty to Involve Users) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 423

National Health Service, England

The National Health Service (Strategic Health Authorities: Further Duty to Involve Users) Regulations 2010

Made

22nd February 2010

Laid before Parliament

26th February 2010

Coming into force

1st April 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 242A and 272(7) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Strategic Health Authorities: Further Duty to Involve Users) Regulations 2010.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Further duty to involve users

Each Strategic Health Authority must make arrangements which secure that health service users are, directly or through representatives, involved (whether by being consulted, or provided with information, or in other ways) in the matters specified in regulation 3.

## 3. Matters to which the duty to involve users applies

(1) The matters referred to in regulation 2 are the preparation and review of plans made by or on behalf of a Strategic Health Authority which set out strategies for—

- (a) improving the health of people in the area of the Authority; or
- (b) the provision of health services in the area of the Authority which, if implemented, would have an impact on—
  - (i) the manner in which services are delivered to health service users, or
  - (ii) the range of services available to those users.

(2) The plans referred to in this regulation include plans made by or on behalf of the Strategic Health Authority jointly with another person.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations place a duty on each Strategic Health Authority to make arrangements to further involve users pursuant to section 242A of the National Health Service Act [2006 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41). This duty is in addition to the duty to involve users under section 242(1B) of that Act.

Regulations 2 and 3 require each Strategic Health Authority to involve users in the preparation and review of plans made by or on behalf of the Authority, including plans made jointly with another person, which set out strategies for improving the health of the people in its area or for the provision of services within its area. The duty applies to plans about the provision of services only where those plans would have an impact on the range of services or the manner in which they are delivered.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
