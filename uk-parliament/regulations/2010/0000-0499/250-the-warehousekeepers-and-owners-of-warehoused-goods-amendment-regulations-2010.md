# The Warehousekeepers and Owners of Warehoused Goods (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 250

Excise

The Warehousekeepers and Owners of Warehoused Goods (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made

4th February 2010

Laid before Parliament

8th February 2010

Coming into force

2nd March 2010

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 93(1) of the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979[^f00001], make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Warehousekeepers and Owners of Warehoused Goods (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 2nd March 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Warehousekeepers and Owners of Warehoused Goods Regulations 1999[^f00002]

In regulation 3(1) omit the words “this Part of”.

Mike Eland

Lesley Strathie

Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs

4th February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

These Regulations amend the Warehousekeepers and Owners of Warehoused Goods Regulations 1999 by omitting from Regulation 3(1) the words “this Part of”. This amendment provides that all the provisions of the Regulations may be relied upon for the approval of occupiers of excise warehouses.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as a negligible impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
