# The General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010 (Commencement No.1) Order of Council 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 478 (C. 34)

Health Care And Associated Professions

Doctors

The General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010 (Commencement No.1) Order of Council 2010

Made

24th February 2010

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 24th day of February 2010

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Their Lordships make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by article 1(4) of the General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010 (Commencement No.1) Order of Council 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

1st April 2010 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010—

- (a) article 3;
- (b) article 4 and Schedule 1, except in relation to paragraph 12 of that Schedule; and
- (c) article 7 and Schedule 3, except in relation to paragraph 5 of that Schedule.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2010, the provisions of the General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2010 ([S.I.2010/234](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/234)) relating to the abolition of the Postgraduate Medical Education and Training Board and the transfer of its functions to the General Medical Council. It also brings into force consequential amendments to legislation arising from the transfer of those functions.
