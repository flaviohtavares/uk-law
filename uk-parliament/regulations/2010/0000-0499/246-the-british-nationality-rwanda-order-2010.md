# The British Nationality (Rwanda) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 246

British Nationality

The British Nationality (Rwanda) Order 2010

Made

10th February 2010

Laid before Parliament

17th February 2010

Coming into force

10th March 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of February 2010

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred on Her by section 37(2) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00001], by and with the advice of her Privy Council, orders as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the British Nationality (Rwanda) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 10th March 2010.

**2.** In Schedule 3[^f00002] to the British Nationality Act 1981 (countries whose citizens are Commonwealth citizens), insert “Rwanda” at the appropriate place.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds Rwanda to Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act 1981 with the result that citizens of Rwanda shall have the status of Commonwealth citizens.
