The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 87(1)(a) of the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-aed8ffa7f9cab351f91e8bab63fa6dbd]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on 26th February 2010.

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Scotland.

## 2. Prescribed police stations[^c-0acd4ae6549aaeb957bf99ffa95864f2]

The police stations listed in Schedule 1 (in England and Wales) and Schedule 2 (in Northern Ireland) to these Regulations are hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 87(1)(a) of the Sexual Offences Act 2003.

## 3. Revocation[^c-29293e38560606b18f97a76ac8728b54]

The Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply to England, Wales and Northern Ireland. These Regulations revoke the Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/722](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/722)). The Sexual Offences Act 2003 (“the 2003 Act”) provides that relevant offenders are subject to requirements to notify certain personal details to the police. Section 87(1) of the 2003 Act provides for such notifications to be given by attending any police station in a person’s local police area which is prescribed in regulations. These Regulations prescribe those police stations. For the purposes of section 87 of the 2003 Act, “local police area” is defined in section 88(3) of the 2003 Act.
