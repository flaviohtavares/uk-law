# The Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2010, section 3

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Extent[^c-0dd666e099f5659f5102e7f3140ac5aa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an amendment or repeal made by this Order has the same extent as the enactment to which it relates.

(2) The amendments and repeals in respect of the following enactments extend to England and Wales only—

- (a) sections 2B, 69 and 69A and Part 3 of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00004];
- (b) paragraph 42 (and the italic heading before it) of Schedule 5 to the Housing and Planning Act 1986[^f00005];
- (c) section 57 of, and paragraphs 28, 29, 31(3) and 33 of Schedule 6 to, the Housing Act 1988[^f00006];
- (d) paragraphs 27(2) and (4), 28, 29, 31, 33, 35(3), 36, 37, 53(3), (4), and (5) and 66 of Schedule 16 to the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00007];
- (e) paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 2 to the Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004[^f00008];
- (f) paragraphs 3 and 11 of Schedule 11 to the Housing Act 2004[^f00009]; and
- (g) paragraph 23 of Schedule 8 and paragraph 1(2)(d) of Schedule 9 to the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007[^f00010].
