# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 750

Public Health, England And Wales

The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2010

Made

9th March 2010

Coming into force

6th April 2010

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 167(1)(b) and (3) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001].

A draft of this instrument has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament pursuant to section 162(3)(g) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent.

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 6th April 2010.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

## 2. Amendment to Water Industry Act 1991

In Schedule 4A to the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00002] (premises that are not to be disconnected for non-payment of charges), for paragraph 4 substitute the following paragraph—

> **4.** A hospital as defined by section 275 of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00003] in relation to England or section 206 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00004] in relation to Wales.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Gillian Merron

Minister of State,

Department of Health

9th March 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Water Industry Act 1991 as a consequence of the repeal of section 11 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 by section 130 of the Health and Social Care Act 2008.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private and voluntary sectors is foreseen.
