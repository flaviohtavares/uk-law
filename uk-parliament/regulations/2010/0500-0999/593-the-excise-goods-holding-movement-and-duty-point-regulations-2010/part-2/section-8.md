# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 8

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Goods released for consumption in the United Kingdom-persons liable to pay[^key-49714b433340c8b737f2cf31c42cadd5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 9, the person liable to pay the duty when excise goods are released for consumption by virtue of regulation 6(1)(a) (excise goods leaving a duty suspension arrangement) is the authorised warehousekeeper [^key-97ac83fbb87d19f61af4e08e2bd662b3]... or any other person releasing the excise goods or on whose behalf the excise goods are released from the duty suspension arrangement.

(2) In the case of an irregular departure from a tax warehouse any other person involved in that departure is jointly and severally liable to pay the duty with the persons specified in paragraph (1).
