# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 5

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Goods released for consumption in the United Kingdom-excise duty point[^key-731adf240def8475f05dd87039387fe0][^key-b97c8e074bb65843098426ce4506bde9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulations 7(2) and 7A[^key-3b7b22251b4f7559c2b40444a3cba99e], there is an excise duty point at the time when excise goods are released for consumption in the United Kingdom.
