## 3. Interpretation[^M_C_c779ac51-3328-4da0-d0b8-d86a470811ba][^M_C_def5dc61-f7f4-46f9-e89d-ef69479fbe2c]

(1) In these Regulations—

- [^key-54ae48a8e966d89d44e6e386455bfed5]...
- “[^key-e9f9bc46d5d80889292ee004127c14bf]**alcoholic product**” has the meaning given in section 44 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023;
- [^key-09a866e747aaf7666ec24a4af164657a]...
- “**authorised warehousekeeper**” means–
  - (a) [^key-c0d4ede89df3b95ae8e565bb4c4d009d]...
    - (i) the occupier of an excise warehouse who is approved in accordance with regulations made under section 93(1) of CEMA 1979;
    - (ii) a person approved under section 82 (approval requirement: producers) of the F(No. 2)A 2023[^key-aac7cbbf512b74b9a1170167aaac2a5b];
    - (iii) [^key-64660d3f410f11bd5e9ddb2bf6f87339]...
    - (iv) [^key-64660d3f410f11bd5e9ddb2bf6f87339]...
    - (v) the occupier of premises registered for the manufacture or safe storage of tobacco products in accordance with regulations made under section 7(1)(b) or (ba) of TPDA 1979 [^c20164791]; [^key-575dfd9018b3620d6c361ad2ea62ef6b]...
    - (vi) any premises in respect of which a person is approved under regulation 4 of the VP(PDSC)R 2026;[^key-561503f5b2075dd931ee62c83940159d]
- “**business day**” means any day except—
  - (a) Saturday, Sunday, Good Friday or Christmas Day [^c20164801];
  - (b) a bank holiday [^c20164811] under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 [^c20164821];
  - (c) a day appointed by Royal proclamation as a public fast or thanksgiving day [^c20164831];
  - (d) a day declared by an order under section 2 of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 to be a non-business day [^c20164841];
- “**CEMA 1979**” means the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979;
- “[^key-052aa7831a819307e55c4186961dfcfd]**change of destination message**” means the message submitted by the consignor of excise goods using the UK computerised system notifying the Commissioners of a change of destination of those goods;
- “**the Commissioners**” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- [^key-516b407eb1c5a26547f416ab34191b98]...
- “**customs and excise Acts**” has the meaning given in section 1(1) of CEMA 1979;
- [^key-c18e16c247284c41ffcf0205a7d4a24d]...
- “[^key-13d1d18bb82681759f0cb9a6794721f4]**customs suspensive procedure or arrangement**” means—
  - (a) any one of the special Customs procedures provided for in Part 1 of the TCTA 2018 (other than an authorised use procedure); and
  - (b) the situation between the goods being presented to the Commissioners upon their entry into the United Kingdom in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the TCTA 2018 and the earlier of—
    - (i) the end of the period of 90 days beginning with the day on which the goods are so presented, and
    - (ii) a Customs declaration under Schedule 1 to that Act being accepted in respect of the goods;
- [^key-19010e64676da25f0eea0da2eb3310d6]...
- [^key-ef523734546d50a4ece0551161d261fe]...
- “**duty**” means any excise duty;
- “**duty deferment arrangement**” means any provision made by or under the customs and excise Acts that permits the payment of excise duty to be deferred;
- “**duty suspension arrangement**” means a tax arrangement applied to the production, processing, holding or movement of excise goods not covered by a customs suspensive procedure or arrangement, excise duty being suspended;
- “[^key-6097770cb86d07fa06539316f9dd1307]**electronic administrative document**” means the message submitted by the consignor to the Commissioners using the UK computerised system under cover of which a movement of excise goods takes place under a duty suspension arrangement;
- “[^key-c2b5bd00ac869df8f58898934d815e0e]**EMCS requirements**” means the requirements specified in the notice published by the Commissioners in accordance with regulation 56(2) and—
  - (a) in the case of a draft electronic administrative document and an electronic administrative document, the respective requirements set out in paragraphs (2A) and (9) of regulation 57;
  - (b) in the case of a fallback accompanying document, the requirements set out in paragraphs (2)(b) and (2A) of regulation 60;
  - (c) in the case of a fallback report of receipt, the requirements set out in paragraphs (1) and (1A) of regulation 61;
  - (d) in the case of a report of receipt, the requirements set out in regulation 59(1);
  - (e) in the case of a report of export, the requirements set out in regulation 59A(3);
- “[^key-64064ab8d627716595b294e96cdc75df]**energy products**” means hydrocarbon oil, biodiesel, bioethanol, aqua methanol, road fuel gas, fuel substitute or any mixture of such products charged to excise duty or treated as such under sections 6 to 8, 20AAA, 20AAD and 23C of the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979, or pursuant to an order made under section 10 of the Finance Act 1993;
- [^key-d6e7c481b31bebc0169a479bd1c23028]...
- [^key-928a6ed93ddc79e216c79b37b795cdde]...
- “[^key-e7e66040e48c9eedba1aa51740d823ec]**excise duty**” has the meaning given to it in section 49 of the TCTA 2018;
- “[^key-6b3083f54c132d653becdffc8da149cd]**excise goods**” means goods which are chargeable with excise duty and goods which are treated as chargeable with excise duty under section 23C of the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979;
- [^key-b112823fcfe427d162d057599ff43b0e]...
- [^key-2154e23ff2e1fc39fe50843b778ca7f3]...
- “[^key-3a3a587d69885b3b5b1ed1f06db45746]**FA 2026**” means the Finance Act 2026;
- “**fallback accompanying document**” means the paper document referred to in regulation 60(2)(b)[^key-8a6f54fa768fcd97da7028c0932443ed];
- [^key-3b11f5d6fba8e6faa1bda1514703ea31]...
- “**fallback report of receipt**” means the paper document referred to in regulation 61(1)[^key-45c45cc1e8a3ca2fb81fe47ee81f6082];
- “[^key-c7d493da21d2113ee53134bfd3565b07]**F(No. 2)A 2023**” means the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023;
- “[^key-8e6c603ccd00051d2882b2f36f6b96f0]**irregularity**” means a situation occurring in the course of a movement of excise goods under a duty suspension arrangement, other than a relevant event referred to in regulation 21, as a result of which the movement, or a part of that movement, has not ended in accordance with regulation 3(3)(b);
- [^key-aa469f288a134ad77097db2358435f1d]...
- “Northern Ireland Regulations 2020” mean the Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010 as modified and applied in relation to excise goods in Northern Ireland by the Excise Duties (Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020;[^key-ccca243ab5fe0d18086cd824026bf3c7]
- [^key-a50a89fd99f17da5273614ec2158bca5]...

- “**report of receipt**” means a report submitted by the consignee of excise goods using the UK computerised system to the Commissioners[^key-a6b29e510e5e0beccfb4f36661b54b29] confirming that the goods have been received;
- [^key-7f0f132ddcabeca1970681d80db24848]...
- “**tax warehouse**” means –
  - (a) [^key-9bdaba2a5166507dca18f9fc3baeac1b]...
    - (i) an excise warehouse [^c20164921];
    - (ii) any premises in respect of which a person is approved under section 82 of the F(No.2)A 2023[^key-f22d7d64b282caceb950e8dc591633b8];
    - (iii) [^key-68243146003d1d83ab16603dc1b66976]...

- [^key-d35b1601b50b7aa8d282ee10583d7177]...
- “**tobacco products**” has the meaning given in section 1(1) of TPDA 1979;
- “**TPDA 1979**” means the Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979;
- “**transporter**” means the person carrying out the first transportation of excise goods in a movement of such goods;
- “[^key-1fd9e12800e6e86b4259f6618bb34e85]**the UCC**” means [Regulation (EU) No 952/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0952) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code;

- [^key-65278e9a25dc452abe947ac6afa93c22]...
- “**unique administrative reference code**” means a code assigned to an electronic administrative document;
- “**vaping product**” has the meaning given by section 116(1) of FA 2026;
- “**VP(PDSC)R 2026**” means the Vaping Products (Production, Duty Stamps and Commencement) Regulations 2026.

(2)[^key-47f337cc76295574ee8c139b04e532d1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of these Regulations a movement of excise goods under a duty suspension arrangement—

- (a)[^key-6f649817a6f9f9024465c8cfe063ab19] starts when—
  - (i) the goods leave a tax warehouse (where the consignor is an authorised warehousekeeper); or
  - (ii) a declaration for the free-circulation procedure or an authorised use procedure is accepted in accordance with Schedule 1 to the TCTA 2018 (where the consignor is a registered consignor), and
- (b) ends-
  - (i) except where sub-paragraph (ii) applies, when the consignee takes delivery of the goods, or
  - (ii) in a case where the goods were dispatched to a place from where they will leave the territory of the United Kingdom[^key-a12a0a8390aaefb84425bf0c0f2e728b], when they have so left.
