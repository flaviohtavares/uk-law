# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 56A

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56A. Northern Ireland – special provisions[^M_F_1732fc1c-0b7a-4095-ebb0-246d4abcc043]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except where paragraph (1A) applies,[^key-869bdc7e48c38efca0ae52fe557e8dfb] Parts 8 and 9 do not apply to the movement of excise goods under duty suspension where—

- (a) the goods have been dispatched from an EU Member State in duty suspension to a tax warehouse in Northern Ireland travelling via Great Britain;
- (b) the movement ends in a tax warehouse in Northern Ireland.

(1A)[^key-91ebb1a3d04edc631a79b8bb75ed2f8d] This paragraph applies where the movement is of vaping products.

(2) In the case of a movement of goods from Great Britain to Northern Ireland to which Part 8 or 9 applies the consignor must be a NI registered consignor under the Northern Ireland Regulations 2020.
