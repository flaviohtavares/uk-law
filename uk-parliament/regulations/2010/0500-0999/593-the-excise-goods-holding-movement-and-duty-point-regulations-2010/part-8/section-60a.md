# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 60A

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60A. Procedure for amending destination when UK computerised system unavailable[^c-7b24f97db7d82cbd7d26efbdff616551][^c-cef1cd55e211cdf35d69738a48a8ee2e][^c-087d57b91f39c50015d86affd5c7ac93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If, in accordance with regulation 58(3), the destination shown on the electronic administrative document is amended, the consignor must, before the destination is amended, notify the Commissioners of the new destination.

(2) As soon as the UK[^c-2860c279744e7457939799dc63a0e53d] computerised system is restored, the consignor must notify the Commissioners of the new destination using the UK computerised system.

(3)[^c-4b8f1c70bb13914d3d3698afcdb2bbac] The consignor must ensure that the information notified to the Commissioners in accordance with paragraph (1) is in the form of data elements, expressed in the same manner as the change of destination message referred to in regulation 58(5).
