## 80. Irregularities[^c-2b848454cc24f933252c62f316ec0945][^c-d89718dd6b01668636a118879702350a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) excise goods are moved under a duty suspension arrangement; and
- (b) in relation to those goods and that movement, there is an irregularity [^c-22ac227e9b530b3ff856479de09334b2]....

(2) Where an irregularity occurs [^c-41edd10256195c356fb028199aa0a08e]..., the excise goods are released for consumption [^c-41edd10256195c356fb028199aa0a08e]... at the time of the irregularity or, where it is not possible to establish when the irregularity occurred, the time when the irregularity is detected or first comes to the attention of the Commissioners.
