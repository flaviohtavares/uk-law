# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 30

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Approval and Registration[^c-344ce8bbae9e10c382f682756e0ec09a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 100G of CEMA 1979 the Commissioners may approve revenue traders who wish to only dispatch excise goods under duty suspension arrangements upon a declaration for the free-circulation procedure or an authorised use procedure being accepted[^c-aacf0873de5d19207dd7762cee20937b] in accordance with Part 1 of the TCTA 2018[^c-827fb5fbfe211dafde33f2f94e930216] and register them as excise dealers and shippers in accordance with section 100G(2) of CEMA 1979.

(2) A revenue trader who has been so approved and registered shall be known as a [^c-df152e2798a1e96e6c804358d8bb8a48]... registered consignor.
