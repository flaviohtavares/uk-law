# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 33

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Conditions and restrictions[^c-303358cd7ea324361e76b37d882a0b57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The approval and registration of [^c-51835833d1c94d930bb547e90720b587]... registered consignors, in addition to any conditions or restrictions imposed on them by the Commissioners under section 100G(4) of the Act, is subject to the conditions and restrictions prescribed in a notice published by the Commissioners.
