# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 34

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Holding of excise goods under duty suspension arrangements[^c-36b17cb7a39e233710faa3f0889f7a4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Excise goods may be deposited and kept under duty suspension arrangements only in a tax warehouse.
