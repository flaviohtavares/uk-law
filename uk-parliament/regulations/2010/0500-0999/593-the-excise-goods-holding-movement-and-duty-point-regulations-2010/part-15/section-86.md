# The Excise Goods (Holding, Movement and Duty Point) Regulations 2010, section 86

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. General conditions and restrictions[^key-4e9539563c51405b343cd47605ee42aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners may in a notice published by them—

- (a) impose on authorised warehousekeepers [^key-3c296ad0963239a8018c1306030daa5e]... conditions and restrictions subject to which excise goods to which these Regulations apply may be deposited in or removed from excise warehouses;
- (b)[^key-fc5dbc67845ac413f7c101854cc428ee] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) prescribe conditions and restrictions subject to which excise goods to which these Regulations apply and in respect of which [^key-d5eb2985f865409e99f5885e956663b5]... excise duty has not been paid may be dispatched by [^key-dba445e60ca6d675142ed2feaf6cee11]... registered consignors;
- (d) impose on transporters and on persons undertaking the carriage of excise goods requirements concerning the keeping and preserving of the documents that are required by these Regulations to accompany the goods.
