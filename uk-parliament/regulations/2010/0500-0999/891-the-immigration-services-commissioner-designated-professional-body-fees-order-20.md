# The Immigration Services Commissioner (Designated Professional Body) (Fees) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

22nd March 2010

Laid before Parliament

23rd March 2010

Coming into force

21st April 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 86 (10) and (12) and section 166 (3) (b) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Immigration Services Commissioner (Designated Professional Body) (Fees) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 21st April 2010.

## 2. Fees

(1) The fee to be paid to the Commissioner by each designated professional body for the year 1st April 2009 to 31st March 2010 shall be the sum specified in the Schedule to this Order.

(2) The fee shall be paid on or before 30th April 2010.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the fee to be paid by the designated professional bodies to the Immigration Services Commissioner (“Commissioner”) for the purpose of meeting the costs incurred by her in discharging her functions under Part 5 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.

The level of the fee varies according to the designated professional body concerned. The different level of fee is designed to reflect the cost of the Commissioner’s regulatory activity referable to each particular body.

The fees specified in the Schedule to this Order are for the year 1st April 2009 to 31st March 2010 but reflect the referable costs incurred in the course of the Commissioner’s regulatory activity in the preceding year 1st April 2008 to 31st March 2009.
