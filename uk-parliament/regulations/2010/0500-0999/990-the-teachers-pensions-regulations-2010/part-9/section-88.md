# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 88

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 88. Introduction

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Part makes provision for the payment of—

- (a) adult pensions;
- (b) additional pensions for surviving adults;
- (c) child pensions.
