# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 92

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 92. Meaning of “surviving adult”

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Regulations the “**surviving adult**” of a person who has died means the deceased's widow, widower, surviving civil partner, surviving qualifying partner[^key-f7871870d63f4b5cb5c76d32154262d1] or surviving nominated beneficiary.
