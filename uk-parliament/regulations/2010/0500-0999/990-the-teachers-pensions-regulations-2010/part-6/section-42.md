# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 42

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Reckonable service: exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person's reckonable service does not include—

- (a) any contributions refund period;
- (b) any period in respect of which a transfer value has been paid;
- (c) any period in respect of which a cash equivalent has been paid;
- (d) any period which would, apart from this paragraph, result in more than 45 years counting as the person's reckonable service;
- (e) any period in respect of which a short-service annuity is payable.
