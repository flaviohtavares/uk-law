# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 14D

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14D. Meaning of “transferred services”[^key-24b4a48e0293be1c22d775da0e3d92bd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

“Transferred services” means services specified in the description of employment in the Participation Agreement.
