# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 14C

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14C. Meaning of “fair deal transfer date”[^key-24b4a48e0293be1c22d775da0e3d92bd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

“Fair deal transfer date” means the date on which transferred services are transferred between a contracting scheme employer and an accepted employer.
