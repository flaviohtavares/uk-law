# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 18

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Teachers' ordinary contributions

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person in pensionable employment must pay contributions at a percentage rate, determined in accordance with paragraph 2[^key-ac44a29451dd001966c04a7db0b6aef1] of Schedule 3 (employees' contributions), of the person's contributable salary for the time being.

(2) This regulation does not apply to a person who has reckonable service of more than 45 years.
