# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 55

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. Retirement benefits qualification service

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The retirement benefits qualification service of a person (T) is the total of the following—

- (a) any period spent by T in pensionable employment;
- (b) any period counting as reckonable service under regulation 41(1)(c),(d),(e) or (f) (reckonable service);
- (c) any period of employment in respect of which the Secretary of State has accepted a transfer value;
- (d) any period of comparable British service prior to 1st January 2007, or comparable United Kingdom service up to and including 31st March 2015[^c-a3bbb3c049b3304951980068fe7a70c8];
- (e) any period of admitted service;
- (f) any period of specified country service;
- (g) any period of employment which ended on or before 31st March 1975[^c-038c7114801d3098cd51fda7acd5e177] which is pensionable for the purposes of the Universities Superannuation Scheme [^c20237811];
- (h) where T was given, but did not take, the opportunity to transfer T's past service pension rights under regulation 36A[^c-23be428a506f18859efcc19fff2bedc9], any period of employment which would have counted as T's retirement benefits qualification service under sub-paragraph (c) if the transfer value in respect of such rights had been accepted by the Secretary of State.

(2) But T's retirement benefits qualification service does not include—

- (a) any contributions refund period;
- (b) any period in respect of which a transfer value has been paid;
- (c) any period in respect of which a cash equivalent has been paid.
