# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 45

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Introduction

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Part makes provision for the payment of—

- (a) phased retirement benefits;
- (b) retirement benefits;
- (c) total incapacity benefits;
- (d) additional pensions;
- (e) admitted service benefits;
- (f) short-service annuities;
- (g) short-service serious ill-health grants.
