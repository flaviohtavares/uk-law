# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 51

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Meaning of “UK transferee with NPA 60 rights, etc.”

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person (P) is a UK transferee with NPA 60 rights if—

- (a) P enters pensionable employment on or before 31st March 2015[^key-e92bf1cd61ee49988580e30f2100284c] other than after a disqualifying break,
- (b) P was in comparable United Kingdom service at any time on or before 31st December 2006[^key-754037033ca62d1532d0d21b86b09bc8], and
- (c) no transfer value or cash equivalent is paid in respect of that comparable United Kingdom service by a comparable United Kingdom scheme other than to the Secretary of State under Part 4, Chapter 3, Section 3[^key-2cc31225d1bc65667083dc4a208f0110] or to another comparable United Kingdom scheme, and
- (d) P's contributions in respect of P's comparable United Kingdom service are not repaid.

(2) A person (P) is a UK transferee with NPA 60 rights and NPA 65 rights if—

- (a) P enters pensionable employment on or before 31st March 2015[^key-73349e602272ea8da31d98027ce871b2] after a disqualifying break,
- (b) P was in comparable United Kingdom service at any time on or before 31st December 2006[^key-3dba630bde14db4f95a6ad0f2233ad89], and
- (c) a transfer value is paid in respect of that comparable United Kingdom service by a comparable United Kingdom scheme to the Secretary of State under Part 4, Chapter 3, Section 3[^key-1c76cb94110cc0edb105e251737688ab].
