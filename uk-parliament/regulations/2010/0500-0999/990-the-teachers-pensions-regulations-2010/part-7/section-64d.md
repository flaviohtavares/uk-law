# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 64D

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64D. Recovery of overpaid pension[^c-7dab7498a4c720d7dd3a3a72c615122f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If the abatable pension paid in any tax year has exceeded the amount which should have been paid by virtue of regulations 64B and 64C, the difference between the amount which has been paid and the amount which should have been paid is an overpayment to which paragraph (2) applies.

(2) Where this paragraph applies, the overpayment is recoverable—

- (a) under regulation 114,
- (b) by a reduction in the abatable pension paid in any subsequent tax year, or
- (c) by a combination of (a) and (b).
