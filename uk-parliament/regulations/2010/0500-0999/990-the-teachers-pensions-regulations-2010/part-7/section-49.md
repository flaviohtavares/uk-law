# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 49

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. “Pre-2007 entrant”: meaning and normal pension age

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person (P) is a pre-2007 entrant if—

- (a) P was in pensionable employment at any time before 1st January 2007, is a UK transferee with NPA 60 rights or is a public sector TUPE transferee with NPA 60 rights, and
- (b) P is not a person with mixed service or a 2007 or later entrant.

(2) A pre-2007 entrant has a normal pension age of 60 in relation to all of the person's reckonable service.
