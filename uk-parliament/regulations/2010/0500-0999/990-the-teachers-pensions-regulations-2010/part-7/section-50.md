# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 50

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Meaning of “NPA 60 reckonable service” and “NPA 65 reckonable service”

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Regulations—

- (a) a reference to a person's “**NPA 60 reckonable service**” is a reference to the reckonable service in relation to which the person has a normal pension age of 60;
- (b) a reference to a person's “**NPA 65 reckonable service**” is a reference to the reckonable service in relation to which the person has a normal pension age of 65.
