# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 35G

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35G. Restrictions on the acceptance of a transfer value[^c-fa696061dd48909a370bd570e6b4c578]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A transfer value payment in respect of a person (P) may not be accepted under this Section if, before P's application for a transfer value payment is received, P is in receipt of—

- (a) retirement benefits under this scheme,
- (b) a short-service serious ill-health grant, or
- (c) a short-service annuity.
