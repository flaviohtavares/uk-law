# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 35A

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35A. Application of this Section[^c-fa696061dd48909a370bd570e6b4c578]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Section applies if a person ceases to be in pensionable employment and has become subject to another registered pension scheme.

(2) This Section only applies to a person who ceases to be in pensionable employment and becomes subject to a personal pension scheme if the person was in pensionable employment after 31st December 1985.
