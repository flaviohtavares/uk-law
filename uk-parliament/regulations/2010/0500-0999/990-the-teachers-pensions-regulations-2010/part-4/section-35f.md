# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 35F

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35F. Receipt under this Section[^c-fa696061dd48909a370bd570e6b4c578]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A transfer value may be accepted under this Section in respect of a person (P) who enters pensionable employment if—

- (a) P makes a written application to the Secretary of State within 12 months from the date on which P enters pensionable employment, and
- (b) P's application is made before P attains the normal pension age.
