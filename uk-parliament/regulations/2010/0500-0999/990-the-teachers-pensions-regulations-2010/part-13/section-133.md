# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 133

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 133. Extension of time

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may in any particular case extend, or treat as extended, the time within which anything is required or authorised to be done under these Regulations.
