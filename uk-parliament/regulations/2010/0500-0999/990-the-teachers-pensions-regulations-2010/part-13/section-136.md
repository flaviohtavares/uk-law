# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 136

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 136. Meaning of payment being “initiated”

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purpose of these Regulations payment of a pension is “initiated” on the date on which the initial payment of the pension is made under regulation 109(2) (monthly payments) or 110(2) quarterly payments.
