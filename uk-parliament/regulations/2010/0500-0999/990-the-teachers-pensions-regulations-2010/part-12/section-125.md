# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 2010, section 125

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 125. Teachers' Pension Budgeting and Valuation Account

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An account in a form approved by the Treasury is to be prepared by the Secretary of State for every financial year starting with the financial year ending 31st March 2011.

(2) The account shall be open to examination by the Comptroller and Auditor General.
