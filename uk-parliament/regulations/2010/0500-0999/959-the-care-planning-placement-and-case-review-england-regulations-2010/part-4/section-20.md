# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 20

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Support for P

*Extent: E+W*

Where C is placed, or is to be placed, with P, the responsible authority must provide such services and support to P as appear to them to be necessary to safeguard and promote C's welfare and must record details of such services and support in C's care plan.
