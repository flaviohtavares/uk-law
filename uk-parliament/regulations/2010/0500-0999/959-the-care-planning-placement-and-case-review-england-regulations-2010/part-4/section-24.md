# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 24

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Temporary approval of relative, friend or other person connected with C (affected by repeal, see note) Temporary approval[^c-9bb0241b5a832eb1e8374c894de20286]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f] Where the responsible authority is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f]

- (a) the most appropriate placement for C is with a connected person, notwithstanding that the connected person is not approved as a local authority foster parent, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f]
- (b) it is necessary for C to be placed with the connected person before the connected person's suitability to be a local authority foster parent has been assessed in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f] the Fostering Services Regulations[^c-8b17790b470387f0b2c60b7fee27066c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f],

they may approve that person as a local authority foster parent for a temporary period not exceeding 16 weeks (“temporary approval”) provided that they first comply with the requirements of paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-edcbb8c5dd804055fb624bf91f1f532f]

(1)  Where the responsible authority is satisfied that the most appropriate placement for C is with a person who has not yet been approved as a local authority foster parent they may approve that person as a local authority foster parent for a temporary period not exceeding 24 weeks (“temporary approval”) provided that they first comply with the requirements of paragraph (2).

(2) Before making a placement under paragraph (1), the responsible authority must—

- (a) assess the suitability of the connected *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9db2a18576febd546642538e86355450] person to care for C, including the suitability of—
  - (i) the proposed accommodation, and
  - (ii) all other persons aged 18 and over who are members of the household in which it is proposed that C will live,

taking into account all the matters set out in Schedule 4,

- (b) consider whether, in all the circumstances and taking into account the services to be provided by the responsible authority, the proposed arrangements will safeguard and promote C's welfare and meet C's needs set out in the care plan, and
- (c) make immediate arrangements for the suitability of the connected *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9db2a18576febd546642538e86355450] person to be a local authority foster parent to be assessed in accordance with the Fostering Services Regulations[^c-9f44dfa40f0d352dc9de8d8b26383dee] (“the full assessment process”) before the temporary approval expires.

(3) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3fb8ea3ee10d61b18277f8c7ef7df04]**connected person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3fb8ea3ee10d61b18277f8c7ef7df04]**” means a relative, friend or other person connected with C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3fb8ea3ee10d61b18277f8c7ef7df04]
