# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 18

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Decision to place a child with P

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The decision to place C with P must not be put into effect until it has been approved by a nominated officer, and the responsible authority have prepared a placement plan for C *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aea39fe4dcb59c2b464c3652024c64e0].

(2) Before approving a decision under paragraph (1), the nominated officer must be satisfied that-

- (a) the requirements of regulation 9(1)(b)(i) have been complied with,
- (b) the requirements of regulation 17 have been complied with,
- (c) the placement will safeguard and promote C's welfare, and
- (d) the IRO has been consulted.
