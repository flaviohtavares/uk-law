# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 27

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. General duties of the responsible authority when placing a child in other arrangements

*Extent: E+W*

Before placing C in accommodation in accordance with other arrangements[^c-b29784a9460f94b13935b7229b732d50] under section 22C(6)(d), the responsible authority must—

- (a)[^c-c46e2cb5ec6ff097a8bb2399dbbd849d] be satisfied that the accommodation is suitable for C and—
  - (i) where that accommodation is specified in regulation 27C(1)(a), must have regard to the matters set out in Schedule 6, and
  - (ii) where that accommodation is specified in regulation 27A or 27C(1)(b), must have regard to the matters set out in paragraph 2(a) of Schedule 6,
- (b) unless it is not reasonably practicable, arrange for C to visit the accommodation, and
- (c) inform the IRO.
