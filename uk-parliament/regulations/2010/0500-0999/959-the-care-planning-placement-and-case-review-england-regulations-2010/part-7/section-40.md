# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 40

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Meaning of eligible child

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 19B(2)(b) of Schedule 2 to the 1989 Act (*meaning of eligible child*), the prescribed period is 13 weeks and the prescribed age is 14.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph 19B(3)(b) of that Schedule, if C is a child to whom regulation 48 applies, C is not an eligible child despite falling within paragraph 19B(2) of that Schedule.
