# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 41

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. General duties

*Extent: E+W*

If C is an eligible child, the responsible authority must —

- (a) assess C's needs in accordance with regulation 42, and
- (b) prepare C's pathway plan, in accordance with regulation 43.
