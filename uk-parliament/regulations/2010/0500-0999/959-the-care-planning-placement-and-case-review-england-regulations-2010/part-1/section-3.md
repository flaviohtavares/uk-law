# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 3

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations do not apply in relation to any child who is looked after by a local authority and who has been placed for adoption under the Adoption and Children Act 2002 [^c20224531] unless the child falls within regulation 47A[^key-8fc2a0d7b55cefd5489391ada2134ec1].
