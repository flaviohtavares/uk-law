# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 36

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. The role of the IRO

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The IRO must—

- (a) so far as reasonably practicable, attend any meeting held as part of the review (“the review meeting”) and, if attending the review meeting, chair it,
- (b) speak to C in private about the matters to be considered at the review unless C, being of sufficient understanding to do so, refuses or the IRO considers it inappropriate having regard to C's age and understanding,
- (c) ensure that, so far as reasonably practicable, the wishes and feelings of C's parents, or any person who is not C's parent but who has parental responsibility for C, and the views of the appropriate person,[^c-9165f4d34099a91ffa98b618f9282437] have been ascertained and taken into account, and
- (d) ensure that the review is conducted in accordance with this Part and in particular—
  - (i) that the persons responsible for implementing any decision taken in consequence of the review are identified, and
  - (ii) that any failure to review the case in accordance with this Part or to take proper steps to implement decisions taken in consequence of the review are brought to the attention of an officer at an appropriate level of seniority within the responsible authority.

(2)[^c-3b737e6af40158abd8d1e2cd1514fedf] The IRO may, if not satisfied that sufficient information has been provided by the responsible authority to enable proper consideration of any of the matters in Schedule 7, adjourn the review meeting once for not more than 20 working days, and no proposal considered in the course of the review may be implemented until the review has been completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b737e6af40158abd8d1e2cd1514fedf]

(2) The IRO may adjourn the review meeting for not more than 20 working days, and no proposal considered in the course of the meeting may be implemented until the review has been completed.
