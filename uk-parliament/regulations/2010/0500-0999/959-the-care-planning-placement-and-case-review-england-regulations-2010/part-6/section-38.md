# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 38

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Records of reviews

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible authority must ensure that a written record of the review is prepared, and that the information obtained in the course of the review, details of proceedings at the review meeting, and any decisions made in the course, or as a result, of the review are included in C's case record.
