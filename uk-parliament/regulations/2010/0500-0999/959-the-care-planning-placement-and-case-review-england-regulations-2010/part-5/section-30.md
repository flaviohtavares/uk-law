# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 30

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Consequences of visits

*Extent: E+W*

Where, as the result of a visit carried out in accordance with this Part, R's assessment is that C's welfare is not adequately safeguarded and promoted by the placement, the responsible authority must review C's case in accordance with Part 6.
