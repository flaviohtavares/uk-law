# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 29

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Conduct of visits

*Extent: E+W*

On each visit, R must speak to C in private unless—

- (a) C, being of sufficient age and understanding to do so, refuses,
- (b) R considers it inappropriate to do so, having regard to C's age and understanding, or
- (c) R is unable to do so.
