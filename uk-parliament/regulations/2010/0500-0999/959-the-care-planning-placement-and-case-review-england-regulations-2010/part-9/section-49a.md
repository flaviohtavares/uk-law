# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010, section 49A

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49A. Transfer of case records[^c-92b08807928c4b78ac75155209b2641b]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible authority may transfer a copy of C’s case record (or part of that record) to an adoption agency when it considers this to be in the interests of C, and a written record shall be kept of any such transfer.
