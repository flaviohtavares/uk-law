# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Devolution of Policing and Justice Functions) Order 2010, section 27

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Repeals of earlier amendments of Schedule 3 to the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^key-2708339dc177f1da4438b3e7ec2ce211]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 24(4) of Schedule 6 to the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00036] (which amended paragraph 11 of Schedule 3 to the Northern Ireland Act 1998) is omitted.

(2) The following provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00037] are omitted—

- (a) section 83 (which inserted paragraphs 9(h), 9A and 15A into Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act);
- (b) paragraph 65(3) of Schedule 12 (which amended paragraph 9 of Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act).

(3) Section 58(3) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00038] (which inserted paragraph 14A into Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act) is omitted.

(4) Paragraph 13(3) of Schedule 4 to the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006[^f00039] (which inserted paragraph 11A into Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act) is omitted.
