# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Devolution of Policing and Justice Functions) Order 2010, section 20

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20.[^c-6ef6d64bfd8088a9146ecda81f381c6d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to article 19(13), articles 18 and 19 operate so as to transfer property and liabilities—

- (a) whether or not they would otherwise be capable of being transferred,
- (b) without any instrument or other formality being required, and
- (c) irrespective of any kind of requirement for consent that would otherwise apply.
