# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Devolution of Policing and Justice Functions) Order 2010, section 6

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Functions relating to the treatment of offenders[^c-e37d93c294c9ecf44217bd8721a50522]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 (which makes amendments relating to prisons) has effect.

(2) Schedule 5 (which makes amendments relating to the release of prisoners on licence) has effect.

(3) Schedule 6 (which makes amendments relating to miscarriages of justice and the Royal prerogative of mercy) has effect.

(4) Schedule 7 (which amends the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00003]) has effect.

(5) Schedule 8 (which amends the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984[^f00004] and the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997[^f00005]) has effect.
