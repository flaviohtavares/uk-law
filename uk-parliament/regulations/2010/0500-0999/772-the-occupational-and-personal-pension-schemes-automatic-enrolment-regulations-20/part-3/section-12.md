# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 12

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12. Automatic re-enrolment dates

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs [^c-bac8b84104b5f71004f048af623a75b9]... (3) and (4), the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5 (automatic re-enrolment) of the Act—

- (a) is the date chosen at the discretion of the employer, within a period beginning 3 months before, and ending at the end of the period of 3 months beginning with,[^c-e921bb77a2e92289eb86965f9a3910bc] the third anniversary of the staging date; and
- (b) thereafter, is the date chosen at the discretion of the employer, within a period beginning 3 months before, and ending at the end of the period of 3 months beginning with, the third anniversary of the date chosen for the previous automatic re-enrolment date.

(2)[^c-f3c142bf2fd188d4d14cfdf49624fdfd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In a case under section 6(4) of the Act, the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5 is the day after the day on which the jobholder ceases to be an active member of the scheme[^c-80af42a85a8b51ff7425a3d7c86f4fa8]

(4) In a case under section 6(5) of the Act, the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5 is the first day on which all the requirements of section 1(1) (jobholders) of the Act are met (so that the person is a jobholder from that date).
