# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 44

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Description of an occupational pension scheme with its main administration outside the United Kingdom[^c-f70c717acc96668449b30ab61459a65e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A pension scheme that has its main administration outside the United Kingdom[^c-f1e50b200d64816b1c66d96ad7bcd163] is an occupational pension scheme for the purposes of section 18(c) (occupational pension schemes) of the Act if it is an occupational pension scheme within the meaning of section 1(1) of the 1993 Act.
