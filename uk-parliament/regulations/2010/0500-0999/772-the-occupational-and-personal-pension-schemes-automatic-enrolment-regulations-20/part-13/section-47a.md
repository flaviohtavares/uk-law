# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 47A

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47A. Meaning of “provider”[^key-517bcedc3cd0fde286e0aed30e2751c7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of the definition of “provider” in section 99 of the Act (interpretation of Part) a provider of a personal pension scheme to which section 26 of the Act (quality requirement: UK personal pension schemes) does not apply is a person whose normal business includes the provision of personal pensions.
