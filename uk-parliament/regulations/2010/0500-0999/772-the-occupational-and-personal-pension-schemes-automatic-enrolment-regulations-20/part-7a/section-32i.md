# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 32I

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32I. Contributions under alternative requirements during transitional periods[^c-021bf0aea326ae513867e96a32ed0baf]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) During the first transitional period referred to in section 29(1) and the second transitional period referred to in section 29(3) of the Act[^c-02fc433556433c9b94b1466cdc893396]... there are substituted for the percentages referred to in the provisions in the first column of the table below the percentage shown in the corresponding entry for the transitional period in question.

| Provision of Regulations | 1st Transitional Period | 2nd Transitional Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulations 32E(2)(b) and 32F(2)(b)(ii)<br>(first set of requirements – employer) | 2% | 3% |
| Regulations 32E(2)(c) and 32F(3)(b)<br>(first set of requirements –<br>total contributions) | 3% | 6% |
| Regulations 32E(3)(a)(ii) and 32F(4)(b)(ii)<br>(second set of requirements – employer) | 1% | 2% |
| Regulations 32E(3)(a)(iii) and 32F(5)(b)<br>(second set of requirements – total contributions) | 2% | 5% |
| Regulations 32E(4)(b) and 32F(6)(b)(ii)<br>(third set of requirements – employer) | 1% | 2% |
| Regulations 32E(4)(c) and 32F(7)(b)<br>(third set of requirements – total contributions) | 2% | 5% |
