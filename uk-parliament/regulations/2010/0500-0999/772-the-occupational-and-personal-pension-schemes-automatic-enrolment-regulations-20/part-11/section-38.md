# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 38

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Appropriate age[^key-7bb9161434eafc625e48cb8550c704b9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of making a relevant determination, the appropriate age prescribed for a member whose pensionable age is over 65 is the age at which the member attains pensionable age.

(2) In this regulation, “relevant determination” means a determination under section 22 (test scheme standard) of the Act as to whether a scheme satisfies the test scheme standard in relation to a jobholder.
