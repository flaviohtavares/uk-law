# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010, section 52

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Persons working on vessels[^c-713166f0782ca61781dfca641063e1e6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The relevant provisions apply, without modification, in relation to a person employed or engaged in any capacity on board a ship as if such a person were a worker for the purposes of those provisions.
