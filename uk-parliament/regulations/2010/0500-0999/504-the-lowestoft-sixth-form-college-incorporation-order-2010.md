# The Lowestoft Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 504

Education, England

The Lowestoft Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2010

Made

28th February 2010

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2010

Coming into force

23rd March 2010

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a) and 17 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”).

In accordance with section 51(3) of the Act, the Secretary of State has published a draft of the proposed order by the time and in the manner prescribed by regulation 6 of the Education (Publication of Draft Proposals and Orders) (Further Education Corporations) (England) Regulations 2001[^f00002].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Lowestoft Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2010 and comes into force on 23rd March 2010.

**2.** There is established as from 23rd March 2010 a body corporate called “the Lowestoft Sixth Form College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

**3.** The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order is 23rd March 2010, and the corporation shall conduct the Lowestoft Sixth Form College from that date.

Kevin Brennan

Minister for Further Education, Skills Apprenticeships and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

28th February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called “the Lowestoft Sixth Form College” under section 16(1)(a) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 23rd March 2010 as the operative date for the purposes of Part I of that Act, being the date from which the further education corporation shall operate the college.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no wider impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
