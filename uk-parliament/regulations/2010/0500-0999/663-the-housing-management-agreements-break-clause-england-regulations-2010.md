# The Housing Management Agreements (Break Clause)(England) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 663

Housing, England

The Housing Management Agreements (Break Clause)(England) Regulations 2010

Made

8th March 2010

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2010

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(4) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Management Agreements (Break Clause)(England) Regulations 2010.

(2) These Regulations come into force immediately after section 111 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008[^f00002] comes into force (“the commencement date”).

(3) These Regulations apply to local housing authorities in England.

## 2. Break clause in housing management agreements

(1) This regulation applies to a management agreement[^f00003] which is entered into on or after the commencement date.

(2) A management agreement to which this regulation applies must provide that where a requirement is imposed under section 247(2) or 249(2) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (requirement to put management out to tender or transfer management)—

- (a) the authority and the manager must make arrangements for the transfer of management functions from the manager, and
- (b) the authority and the manager must determine the agreement,

so far as necessary to ensure that the agreement does not continue to have effect as respects management functions which are the subject of the requirement.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 27 of the Housing Act [1985 (c.68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/68) enables a local housing authority to delegate management of its housing to a manager through a management agreement. Local housing authorities who provide or intend to provide social housing will be subject to regulation by the Regulator of Social Housing under Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act [2008 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/17) when that regime is commenced. The Regulator of Social Housing’s enforcement powers include the power to impose a requirement to put the management of social housing out to tender or to require transfer of management functions to a specified provider. These Regulations require that any management agreement entered into on or after the date on which Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 is commenced, must include break clause allowing the agreement to be determined and the management functions transferred in circumstances where a management requirement is imposed by the regulator.
