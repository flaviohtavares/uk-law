## 62A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Reporting by local councils[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f][^key-51f606f9777551895e84190f6e941093]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] A local council must prepare a report for any financial year (“the reported year”) in which it receives CIL receipts. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] The report must include— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the total CIL receipts for the reported year; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the total CIL expenditure for the reported year; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] summary of CIL expenditure during the reported year including— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the items to which CIL has been applied; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the amount of CIL expenditure on each item; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] details of any notices received in accordance with regulation 59E, including— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the total value of CIL receipts subject to notices served in accordance with regulation 59E during the reported year; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the total value of CIL receipts subject to a notice served in accordance with regulation 59E in any year that has not been paid to the relevant charging authority by the end of the reported year. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
- (e (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] the total amount of— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] CIL receipts for the reported year retained at the end of the reported year; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] CIL receipts from previous years retained at the end of the reported year. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] The local council must— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] publish the report— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] on its website; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] on the website of the charging authority for the area if the local council does not have a website; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
  - (iii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] within its area as it considers appropriate if neither the local council nor the charging authority have a website, or the charging authority refuses to put the report on its website in accordance with paragraph (ii); and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f] send a copy of the report to the charging authority from which it received CIL receipts, no later than 31st December following the reported year, unless the report is, or is to be, published on the charging authority’s website. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-870915ae9361541c53e7189edcf4049f]
