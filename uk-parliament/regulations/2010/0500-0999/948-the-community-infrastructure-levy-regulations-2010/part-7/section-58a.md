# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 58A

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58A. Interpretation of Part 7[^c-52b3c46a0242d8c2a82463f6e2059ed5]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “acquired land” and “relevant purpose” have the same meaning as in regulation 73 (payment in kind); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bb0a0cc1ed5454e2176c3f6b7a69da04]
- “CIL expenditure” includes— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
  - (a) the value of any acquired land on which development (within the meaning in TCPA 1990) consistent with a relevant purpose has been commenced or completed, and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
  - (b) CIL receipts transferred by a charging authority to another person to spend on infrastructure (including money transferred to such a person which it has not yet spent); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
- “CIL receipts” means— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
  - (a) for a charging authority— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
    - (i) CIL collected by that authority (including the value of any acquired land), but does not include CIL collected on behalf of the charging authority by another public authority but which that authority has not yet paid to the charging authority; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
    - (ii) CIL recovered by that authority in accordance with regulation 59D, but does not include CIL not yet paid to the charging authority by the local council; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
  - (b) for a local council, CIL passed to it under regulations 59(4), 59A(2) or 59B, but does not include funds not yet paid to the local council by the charging authority in accordance with regulation 59D. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
- “dwelling” has the meaning given in section 3 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992;
- “ *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb][formula: IA definition](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/948) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]” means the index figure for the year in which CIL is passed to the local council; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
- “index figure” has the same meaning as in *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb] regulation 40(6)[^c-4b22a45cdb03a07b7ef9424db6915cbc] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb] (calculation of chargeable amount); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f07fc7aac93b22b1341186d4419cecb]
- “local council” means—
  - (a) in England, a parish council;
  - (b) in Wales, a community council; and
- “neighbourhood development plan” has the same meaning as in section 38A of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004.
