# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 80

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Surcharge for failure to assume liability[^c-1c661871719b5218555e4d795b46c8e4]

*Extent: E+W*

A collecting authority may impose a surcharge of £50 on each person liable to pay CIL in respect of a chargeable development if—

- (a) nobody has assumed liability to pay CIL in respect of the chargeable development; and
- (b) the chargeable development has been commenced.
