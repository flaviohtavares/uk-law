# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 96

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96. Liability orders: reminder notice[^c-58fb96b112b75aac62738c8d8c9760cc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before a collecting authority applies for a liability order it must serve on the person against whom the application is to be made a notice (“reminder notice”) which must state every amount in respect of which the authority is to make the application.

(2) A reminder notice may be served in respect of an amount at any time after it has become due.
