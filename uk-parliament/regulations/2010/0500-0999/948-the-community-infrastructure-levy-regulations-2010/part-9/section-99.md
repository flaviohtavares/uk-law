## 99 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Appeals in connection with distress[^c-1b9f689cb4a0eab8baf127822714caa5][^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] A person aggrieved by the levy of, or an attempt to levy, a distress may appeal to a magistrates’ court. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] The appeal must be instituted by making a complaint to a justice of the peace, and requesting the issue of a summons directed to the authority which levied or attempted to levy the distress to appear before the court to answer to the matter by which the person is aggrieved. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] If the court is satisfied that a levy was irregular, it may— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] order the goods distrained to be discharged if they are in the possession of the authority; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] by order award compensation in respect of any goods distrained and sold. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] The amount of compensation that may be awarded under paragraph (3)(b) is an amount equal to the amount which, in the opinion of the court, would be awarded by special damages in respect of the goods if proceedings were brought in trespass or otherwise in connection with the irregularity under regulation *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] 98(11) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962] If the court is satisfied that an attempted levy was irregular, it may by order require the authority to desist from levying in the manner giving rise to the irregularity. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8445bc6de4703912e1c8d09c4bea0962]
