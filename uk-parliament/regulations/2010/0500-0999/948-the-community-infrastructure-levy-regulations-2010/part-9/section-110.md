## 110. Offence for supplying false information[^c-5dafae26e6cb991eb11cb0730370b65d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person, knowingly or recklessly, to supply information which is false or misleading in a material respect to a charging authority or[^c-10a0647492fef7d054df291e890646a7] a collecting authority in response to a requirement under these Regulations.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this regulation is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine[^c-6a07c99a109cc0234c9675bbd2af613c]; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine, or both.
