# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 5

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Meaning of “planning permission”[^c-10c38ee588d66d03eca22be95b2f71b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of Part 11 of PA 2008, “planning permission” means—

- (a) planning permission granted by a local planning authority under section 70, 73 or 73A of TCPA 1990[^f00007];
- (b) planning permission granted by the Secretary of State under the provisions mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) as applied by sections 76A(10), 76C(1),[^c-9883d18364cffe75e7b6f3b4a2faca44] 77(4) and 79(4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a22a12c20217af3e1217f7e096ffaee5] , 79(4) and 293H(1) of TCPA 1990[^f00008] (including permission so granted by a person appointed by the Secretary of State in accordance with section 76D(1) or 293I(1) of TCPA 1990 or[^c-65738f8b0dd96e7b5c95437dca2dac58] regulations made under Schedule 6 to TCPA 1990);
- (c) planning permission granted or modified under section 177(1) of TCPA 1990[^f00009] (grant or modification of planning permission on appeals against enforcement notices);
- (d) modification of a planning permission under section 97 or 100 of TCPA 1990[^f00010];
- (e) planning permission granted by an order made under section 102 or 104 of TCPA 1990[^f00011] (orders requiring discontinuance of use or alteration or removal of buildings or works);
- (f) development consent granted by an order made under section 114(1)(a) of PA 2008; or
- (g) a general consent.

(2) But planning permission does not include planning permission granted for a limited period.

(3) In paragraph (1)(g) “general consent” means—

- (a) planning permission granted—
  - (i) by a development order made under section 59 of TCPA 1990,
  - (ii) by a local development order adopted under section 61A of TCPA 1990[^f00012],
  - (iia)[^c-76a8994ceb312011d0ecb8a8cdafde38] by a neighbourhood development order made under section 61E or 61Q (community right to build orders)[^c-64112a5ce7a205fbc42459cbeeefce0e] of TCPA 1990,
  - (iii) by a simplified planning zone scheme within the meaning of sections 82 and 83[^f00013] of TCPA 1990,
  - (iv) in accordance with section 90 of TCPA 1990[^f00014] (development with government authorisation), or
  - (v) by an enterprise zone scheme adopted under Schedule 32 to the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00015]; or
- (b) development authorised by an Act of Parliament or an order approved by both Houses of Parliament which designates specifically the nature of the development authorised and the land on which it may be carried out.
