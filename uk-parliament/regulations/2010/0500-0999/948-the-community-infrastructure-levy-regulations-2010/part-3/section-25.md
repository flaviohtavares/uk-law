# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 25

## 25. Approval and publication of a charging schedule[^key-0f7d9ff148ca2a9a10b7bc53f0c7a94f]

*Extent: E+W*

As soon as practicable after the charging authority approves a charging schedule in accordance with section 213 of PA 2008 it must—

- (a) publish the charging schedule , together with any report made under section 213(3B) of PA 2008,[^key-8b225dd4bbd7de301d8b5a6e6f5461f5] on its website;
- (b) make the charging schedule , together with any report made under section 213(3B) of PA 2008,[^key-a9ba20a7f48892ae25cba9a8531431d6] available for inspection at the places at which the documents mentioned in regulation 16(1)(a) were made available;
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a13318898eb9ed5b2e0017d179e36be7] give notice by local advertisement of the approval of the charging schedule, that a copy of the charging schedule is available for inspection, and of the places at which it can be inspected; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a13318898eb9ed5b2e0017d179e36be7]
- (d) give notice to those persons who requested to be notified of the approval of the charging schedule that it has been so approved; and
- (e) send a copy of the charging schedule to each of the relevant consenting authorities.
