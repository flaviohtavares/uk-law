# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/948
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Joint examinations[^c-aa34b6640a9e7905483f56b67b08930f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Two or more charging schedules may be examined as part of the same examination if the charging authorities who prepared the draft charging schedules all agree.

(2) Examination of a charging schedule may be carried out jointly with—

- (a) an examination of a local plan or supplementary plan[^c-8ed094acd5057216415c6491828a9754] under section 15D or section 15DB of PCPA 2004[^c-58cc6aa9db8758071a6f932659776bb2]; or
- (b) an examination of a local development plan under section 64 of PCPA 2004 (independent examination).

(3) In relation to Greater London, examination of a charging schedule prepared by the Mayor may be carried out jointly with an examination in public of the spatial development strategy under section 338 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00021] (examination in public).

(4) Where a joint examination is carried out under paragraph (3), any other charging schedule prepared by a London borough council or MDC[^c-766e6a5c889cbabb1f8de78886207282] may be examined as part of the same examination.

(5) The charging authority and Secretary of State must agree to a joint examination under paragraph (2)(a) or (3).

(6) The charging authority and the Welsh Ministers must agree to a joint examination under paragraph (2)(b).

(7) A joint examination under paragraph (2) may only be carried out in relation to one or more charging schedules and one local plan, one supplementary plan[^c-e0d6b0238641f455891018c495ea7644] or one local development plan (as the case may be).
