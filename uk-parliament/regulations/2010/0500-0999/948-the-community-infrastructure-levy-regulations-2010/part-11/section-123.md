## 123 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Further limitations on use of planning obligations[^key-bbac355c3f2f185e8900789633023547][^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] This regulation applies where a relevant determination is made which results in planning permission being granted for development. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] A planning obligation may not constitute a reason for granting planning permission for the development to the extent that the obligation provides for the funding or provision of relevant infrastructure *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] (including, subject to paragraph (2B), through requiring a highway agreement to be entered into)[^key-1d20eea7e702732b9470e3b2572c471c] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

(2A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cbac8cd9cc881cd5e0e8060946a3a00a][^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] Subject to paragraph (2B) a condition falling within either of the following descriptions may not be imposed on the grant of planning permission— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] a condition that requires a highway agreement for the funding or provision of relevant infrastructure to be entered into; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] a condition that prevents or restricts the carrying out of development until a highway agreement for the funding or provision of relevant infrastructure has been entered into. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

(2B (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] Paragraphs (2) and (2A) do not apply in relation to highway agreements to be entered into with— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] the Minister, for the purposes of section 1(1) of the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] 1980 Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] [^key-112591277cefd5987563cb180a37d82c]... *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] Transport for London *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] ; or[^key-f25fccbe99f060fd41311600378e3989] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] a strategic highways company for the time being appointed under Part 1 of the Infrastructure Act 2015 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] Other than through requiring a highway agreement to be entered into, a planning obligation[^key-b56132180ef59048c1890c79271b924b] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] (“obligation A”) may not constitute a reason for granting planning permission to the extent that— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] obligation A provides for the funding or provision of an infrastructure project or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] provides for the funding or provision of a[^key-84e4ae5843ca7588e2824723ed21cfbf] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] type of infrastructure; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] five or more separate planning obligations that— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] relate to planning permissions granted for development within the area of the charging authority; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] which provide for the funding or provision of that project *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] or provide for the funding or provision of that[^key-6105ca24820525674bcf2e0a4265f47e] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] type of infrastructure, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

  have been entered into *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] on or after 6th April 2010[^key-814969136bbbffcc61016a82aef46a7e] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] In this regulation— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- “the[^key-0e1d6eb8dbfb601bd0ba16e4a840ca67] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] 1980 Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]” means the Highways Act 1980; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- “funding” in relation to the funding of infrastructure, means the provision of that infrastructure by way of funding; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

  - (a) under section 70, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] 73,[^key-8741d27fa8b12c07d0b72b21b4f24aa8] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] 76A or 77 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] TCPA 1990 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] of an application for planning permission *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] [^key-846949bdfa46edc89a03d6e6aa370508]..., or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]

- “highway agreement” means an agreement under section 278 of the[^key-f3fac83805cda048cd6dc84b0876df8b] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] 1980 Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
- “planning obligation” means a planning obligation under section 106 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] TCPA 1990 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb] and includes a proposed planning obligation but does not include a planning obligation that relates to or is connected with the funding or provision of scheduled works within the meaning of Schedule 1 to the Crossrail Act 2008; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fd96c7524a952e0878e2edf3068107bb]
