# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 121

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 121. Costs[^c-8e66510c77cdb03a1f55adcbe1495d78]

*Extent: E+W*

The appointed person may make orders as to the costs of the parties to the appeal and as to the parties by whom such costs are to be paid.
