## 79. Use of information by collecting authority[^c-d7c020f60ea27359aa2d3726e69eedb5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In carrying out its functions under these Regulations a collecting authority may use information obtained under any other enactment provided it does not fall within paragraph (2).

(2) Information falls within this paragraph if—

- (a) it was obtained by a committee of the authority in its capacity as a police authority; or
- (b) it was obtained by the authority in its capacity as an employer.
