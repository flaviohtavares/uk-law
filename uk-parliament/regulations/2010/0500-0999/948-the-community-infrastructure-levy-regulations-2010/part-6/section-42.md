# The Community Infrastructure Levy Regulations 2010, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/948
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Exemption for minor development[^c-de2ef8611790310af4ecc4a39d97d299]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Liability to CIL does not arise in respect of a [^c-10f4201d3339fe225385157a8579eb6c]... development if, on completion of that development, the gross internal area of new build on the relevant land will be less than 100 square metres.

(2) But paragraph (1) does not apply where the [^c-e56f55b1b91ef66f74e0c65fe2447fc7]... development will comprise one or more dwellings.

(3) In paragraph (1) “new build” means that part of the [^c-294f54dc3f2d78b5d324da539fbccfaf]... development which will comprise new buildings and enlargements to existing buildings.
