EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Volatile Organic Compounds in Paints, Varnishes and Vehicle Refinishing Products Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2773](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2773)) which implement Directive [2004/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0042) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain paints, varnishes and vehicle refinishing products and amending Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013).

The amendments are to implement amendments to Directive 2004/42 made by Directive [2008/112/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0112) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 amending Council Directive [76/768/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0768), [88/378/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0378), [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013) and Directives [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0053), [2002/96/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0096) and [2004/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0042) of the European Parliament and of the Council in order to adapt them to Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures. The aim of Directive [2008/112/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0112) is to incorporate into Community legislation, criteria for the classification and labelling of substances and mixtures provided for by the Globally Harmonised System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals.

A full regulatory impact assessment was produced in relation to the Volatile Organic Compounds in Paints, Varnishes and Vehicle Refinishing Products Regulations 2005 and a further impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no further impact on business, the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
