# The UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 606 (C. 41)

Immigration

The UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2010

Made

4th March 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(2) and (3)(a) of the UK Borders Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the UK Borders Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement

Section 24 (seizure of cash) of the UK Borders Act 2007 shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2010.

Phil Woolas

Minister of State

Home Office

4th March 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 24 (seizure of cash) of the UK Borders Act 2007 on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2010. Section 24 (seizure of cash) applies Chapter 3 of Part 5 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (recovery of cash) to an immigration officer as it applies in relation to a constable.
