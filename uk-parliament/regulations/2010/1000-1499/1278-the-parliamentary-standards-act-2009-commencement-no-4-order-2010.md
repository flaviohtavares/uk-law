# The Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1278 (C. 80)

Parliament

The Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010

Made

15th April 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(3) of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement

Section 7 (information and guidance)[^f00002] of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 comes into force on 7th May 2010.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Justice

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

15th April 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 7th May 2010 section 7 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 which, as amended by the Constitutional Reform and Governance Act [2010 (c.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/25), makes provision about the general duties of the Independent Parliamentary Standards Authority.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
