# The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Provision of Information with Consent) Regulations 2009 (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [SI 2009/2575](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2575) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(6)(a), (7)(b), 40(4) and (7) of the Identity Cards Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Identity Cards Act 2006 (Provision of Information with Consent) Regulations 2009 (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on 29<sup> </sup>April 2010.

## 2. Amendments to the Identity Cards Act 2006 (Provision of Information with Consent) Regulations 2009

The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Provision of Information with Consent) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 1(2) (Citation, commencement and interpretation)

- (a) in the definition of “aerodrome” insert “in” after “it”;
- (b) omit the definition of “airside pass”;
- (c) after the definition of “consent” insert ““the Identity and Passport Service” means the Secretary of State acting through the Identity and Passport Service;”; and
- (d) after the definition of “grant of approval” insert “”manager” has the meaning given to it in section 38(1) of the Aviation Security Act 1982[^f00003];”.

**4.** In regulation 3 (Provision of consent), after sub-paragraph (b) omit “and” and after sub-paragraph (c) add

> - and
>   - (a) subject to regulation 4, a person who is accredited by the Identity and Passport Service to be provided with information from passport records under the Passport Validation Service,

.

**5.** In regulation 4(1) (Validation of identity card details), after “application with consent” add “for the purposes of section 12(1)(a) and (b) and 12(6) of the 2006 Act”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Provision of Information with Consent) Regulations 2009 were made on 23 September 2009 and came into force on 20 October 2009. There were some small errors in regulation 1(2) in relation to definitions. Those errors are corrected in these Regulations. Regulation 4 adds a person who is accredited by the Identity and Passport Service to be provided with information from passport records under the Passport Validation Service to the list of persons who may make an application under section 12(1)(a) and (b) and 12(6). Regulation 5 makes it clear that applications made by such persons are made under those sections only.
