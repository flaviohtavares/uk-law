# The Representation of the People (Timing of the Canvass) (Northern Ireland) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1152

Representation Of The People, Northern Ireland

The Representation of the People (Timing of the Canvass) (Northern Ireland) Order 2010

Made

31st March 2010

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

In accordance with section 10ZA(5) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] (“the 1983 Act”) the Chief Electoral Officer for Northern Ireland, on 9th October 2009, made a recommendation to the Secretary of State against a canvass being conducted in the year 2010 for the purpose of meeting the relevant registration objectives[^f00002].

Having considered that recommendation in accordance with section 10ZA(5)(b) of the 1983 Act, the Secretary of State is satisfied that the public interest does not require a canvass to be conducted for that purpose.

A draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 10ZA(7) of the 1983 Act.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 10ZA(1) of the 1983 Act makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People (Timing of the Canvass) (Northern Ireland) Order 2010 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it was made.

## 2. Timing of canvass

The Chief Electoral Officer for Northern Ireland shall not, under section 10ZA(1)(a) of the Representation of the People Act 1983, be required to conduct a canvass in the year 2010.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

31st March 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of section 10ZA of the Representation of the People Act 1983 (“the 1983 Act”), the Chief Electoral Officer for Northern Ireland is required to conduct a canvass in Northern Ireland in the year 2010. However, if, on or before 15 April 2010, the Chief Electoral Officer makes a recommendation to the Secretary of State against a canvass being conducted in 2010 for the purpose of meeting the relevant registration objectives (which are set out in section 10ZB of the 1983 Act), the Secretary of State can, by Order, make provision that that requirement shall not apply. Before doing so the Secretary of State must be satisfied, having considered the recommendation, that the public interest does not require a canvass to be conducted for that purpose.

In October 2009 the Secretary of State received such a recommendation from the Chief Electoral Officer and, having considered it, was satisfied that the public interest did not require a canvass to be conducted for the purpose of meeting the relevant registration objectives. This Order therefore provides that the Chief Electoral Officer need not conduct a canvass in 2010.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
