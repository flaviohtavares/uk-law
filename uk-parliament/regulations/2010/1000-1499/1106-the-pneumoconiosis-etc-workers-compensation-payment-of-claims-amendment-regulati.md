# The Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1106

Social Security

The Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made

30th March 2010

Coming into force

1st April 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1), (2) and (4) and 7(2) of the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act 1979[^f00001].

In accordance with section 7(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

(2)  These Regulations shall apply in relation to any case in which a person first fulfils the conditions of entitlement to a payment under the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act 1979 on or after the day on which they come into force.

## 2. Amendment of the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) Regulations 1988

In the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) Regulations 1988[^f00002]—

- (a) in regulations 5(1) and 8 (minimum amounts payable to dependant), for “£2,609” substitute “£2,649”;
- (b) in regulation 6(1)(a) (payment where pneumoconiosis is accompanied by tuberculosis), for “£5,397” (in both places) substitute “£5,478”; and
- (c) for the Schedule to those Regulations substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Under the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act [1979 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/41) (“the Act”) lump sum payments may be made to certain persons disabled by a disease to which the Act applies, or to dependants of persons who were so disabled before they died.

These Regulations amend the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) Regulations 1988 ([S.I. 1988/668](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/668)). Regulation 2(c) of these Regulations substitutes a new Schedule into the Act so as to increase amounts payable under it. The increase in each case is 1.5% rounded up or down to the nearest £1 as appropriate.

Payments under Parts A to C of Table 2 of the substituted Schedule, which prescribe the rates payable to dependants, have been increased by up to a further £5,000 to partially equalise payments to those disabled by the diseases to which the Act applies and payments to their dependants.

The diseases to which the Act applies are pneumoconiosis, byssinosis, diffuse mesothelioma, primary carcinoma of the lung (where accompanied by asbestosis or diffuse pleural thickening) and diffuse pleural thickening.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
