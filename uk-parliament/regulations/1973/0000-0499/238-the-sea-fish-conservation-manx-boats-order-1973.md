# The Sea Fish (Conservation) (Manx Boats) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 238

SEA FISHERIES

BOATS AND METHODS OF FISHING

The Sea Fish (Conservation) (Manx Boats) Order 1973

Made

16th February 1973

Coming into Operation

1st April 1973

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of February 1973

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 24(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sea Fish (Conservation) (Manx Boats) Order 1973 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1973.

**2.** Section 3 of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 (which relates to the regulation of fishing nets and other fishing gear) shall apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in the Isle of Man as it applies in relation to British fishing boats registered in the United Kingdom.

**3.** Section 5 of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 (which confers power to restrict fishing for sea fish) shall apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in the Isle of Man as it applies in relation to British fishing boats registered in the United Kingdom.

W.G. Agnew

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies sections 3 and 5 of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 to British fishing boats registered in the Isle of Man.
