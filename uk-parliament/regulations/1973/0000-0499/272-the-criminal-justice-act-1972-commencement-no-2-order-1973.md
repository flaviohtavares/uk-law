# The Criminal Justice Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 272 (c.7)

CRIMINAL PROCEDURE, ENGLAND AND WALES CRIMINAL PROCEDURE, SCOTLAND

The Criminal Justice Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1973

Made

21st February 1973

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 66(6) of the Criminal Justice Act 1972, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1973.

**2.** The provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1972 specified in the Schedules to this Order shall come into force on the dates mentioned in the headings of those Schedules.

Robert Carr

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

21st February 1973
