# The Metropolitan Districts (Names) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 137

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Metropolitan Districts (Names) Order 1973

Made

31st January 1973

Coming into Operation

1st April 1973

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1(3) of the Local Government Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

## 1. Title and commencement

This order may be cited as the Metropolitan Districts (Names) Order 1973 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1973.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

(2) In this order “**identification letter**” in relation to any metropolitan district in a metropolitan county named in column (1) of the Schedule to this order means the letter by which such district is identified in column (2) of Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government Act 1972.

## 3. Names of metropolitan districts

In the metropolitan counties named in column (1) of the Schedule to this order each metropolitan district identified by its identification letter in column (2) shall bear the name specified in respect of the district in column (3).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

C. J. Pearce

An Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment

31st January 1973
