# The Sea Fisheries (Channel Islands Boats) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1320

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fisheries (Channel Islands Boats) Order 1973

Made

27th July 1973

Coming into Operation

1st October 1973

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of July 1973

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 21(1)(b) of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (Channel Islands Boats) Order 1973 and shall come into operation on 1st October 1973.

**2.** The provisions of sections 5 to 14 of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968 shall apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in any of the Channel Islands as they apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in the United Kingdom.

W.G. Agnew

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies certain provisions of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968, which regulate sea fishing operations, and certain ancillary provisions, to British fishing boats registered in any of the Channel Islands as they apply in relation to such boats registered in the United Kingdom.
