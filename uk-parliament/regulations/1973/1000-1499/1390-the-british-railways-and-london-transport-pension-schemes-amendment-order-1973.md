# The British Railways and London Transport Pension Schemes (Amendment) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1390

TRANSPORT

PENSIONS AND COMPENSATION

The British Railways and London Transport Pension Schemes (Amendment) Order 1973

Made

6th August 1973

Laid before Parliament

17th August 1973

Coming into Operation

14th September 1973

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of powers conferred by section 74 of the Transport Act 1962 and section 18 of the Transport (London) Act 1969 and now vested in him[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers and, so far as regards Article 3 of this Order, on the application of the London Transport Executive made with the approval of the Greater London Council, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the British Railways and London Transport Pension Schemes (Amendment) Order 1973 and shall come into operation on the 14th September 1973.

**2.** The British Transport (Amalgamation of Railways' Pension Funds) (No. 2) Order 1970[^f00002] shall have effect as if in paragraph (4) of Article 6 thereof (which relates to membership of the Committee of the Railway Clearing System Superannuation Fund) there were substituted for the words “three years” the words “six years”.

**3.** The London Transport (Alteration of Pension Schemes) Order 1970[^f00003] shall have effect as if in paragraph (4) of Article 3 thereof (which relates to membership of certain committees, including the Committee of the Railway Clearing System Superannuation Fund) there were substituted for the words “three years” the words “six years”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

John Peyton

Minister for Transport Industries

Department of the Environment

6th August 1973

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Article 6(4) of the British Transport (Amalgamation of Railways' Pension Funds) (No. 2) Order 1970 and Article 3(4) of the London Transport (Alteration of Pension Schemes) Order 1970 so as to enable persons who, in consequence of becoming members of the New Section of the British Railways Superannuation Fund or, as the case may be, of the London Transport 1970 Superannuation Fund, cease to be members of the Railway Clearing System Superannuation Fund, to remain as members of the Committee of that fund for a period of six years (instead of three years) from the date of the cessation.
