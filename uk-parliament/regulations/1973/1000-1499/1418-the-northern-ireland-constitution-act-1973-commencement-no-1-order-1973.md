# The Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1418 (C.37)

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1973

Made

8th August 1973

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 43(6) of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1973.

**2.** Part III of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 shall come into operation on 1st September 1973.

W.S.I. Whitelaw

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretary of State

Northern Ireland Office

8th August 1973

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings Part III of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Prevention of Religious and Political Discrimination) into force on 1st September 1973.
