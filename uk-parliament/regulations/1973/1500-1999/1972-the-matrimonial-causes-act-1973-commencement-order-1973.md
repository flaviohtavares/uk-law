# The Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1972 (C.59)

MATRIMONIAL CAUSES

The Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

Made

26th November 1973

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 55(2) of the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of Parliament.

**2.** The Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 shall come into operation on 1st January 1974.

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C

Dated 26th November 1973

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 into operation on 1st January 1974.
