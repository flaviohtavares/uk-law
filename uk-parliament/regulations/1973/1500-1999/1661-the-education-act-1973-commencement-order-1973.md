# The Education Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1661 (C.51)

CHARITIES

The Education Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

Made

28th September 1973

The Secretary of State for Education and Science and the Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 1(5) of the Education Act 1973, hereby order as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973.

**2.** Section 1(1)(a) of, and Part III of schedule 2 to, the Education Act 1973 shall come into force on 1st February 1974.

Given under the Official Seal of the Secretary of State for Education and Science on 26th September 1973.

Margaret H. Thatcher

Secretary of State for Education and Science

Given under my hand on 28th September 1973.

Peter Thomas

Secretary of State for Wales

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st February 1974 as the day on which the functions of the Secretary of State for Education and Science and the Secretary of State for Wales under the Charities Act 1960 (c.58) cease to be exercisable by them.
