# The International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1617 (C.48)

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

Made

21st September 1973

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 hereby orders as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973.

**2.** The International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 shall come into force on 1st October 1973.

Limerick

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Trade

Department of Trade and Industry

21st September 1973

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1973 the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973.
