# The Medicines Act 1968 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1973

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1973 No. 1529 (C.44)

MEDICINES

The Medicines Act 1968 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1973

Made

29th August 1973

The Secretaries of State respectively concerned with health in England and Wales, the Secretary of State concerned with health and with agriculture in Scotland, the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food acting jointly, in exercise of powers conferred by section 136(3) of the Medicines Act 1968 and now vested in them[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the following order, hereby make the following order:—

**1.** The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of section 90(2) of the Medicines Act 1968 shall be 1st January 1974.

**2.** This order may be cited as the Medicines Act 1968 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1973.

Keith Joseph

Secretary of State for Social Services

9th August 1973

Peter Thomas

Secretary of State for Wales

10th August 1973

Gordon Campbell

Secretary of State for Scotland

29th August 1973

W.S.I. Whitelaw

Secretary of State for Northern Ireland

24th August 1973

In witness whereof the official seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 17th August 1973.

Joseph Godber

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st January 1974 section 90(2) of the Medicines Act 1968, which contains prohibitions relating to false or misleading descriptions on containers or packages of medicated animal feeding stuffs.
