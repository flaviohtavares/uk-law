# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 2922

Education, Wales

The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2014

Made

5th November 2014

Coming into force

6th November 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 5th day of November 2014

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(2) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2014 and comes into force on 6th November 2014.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

- Mamta Chandulal Arnott
- Anthony John Bate
- Dyfrig Wyn Ellis
- Michelle Gosney
- Richard Owen Lloyd
- Steven William Pringle
- Andrew James Thorne
- Elwyn Vaughan Williams

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales, or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.
