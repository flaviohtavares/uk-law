# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 2921

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2014

Made

5th November 2014

Coming into force

6th November 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 5th day of November 2014

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2014 and comes into force on 6th November 2014.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
