## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-19169406ce4a0b6c87b4f7f0eea009d4]

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations may be cited as the Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014 and come into force immediately after sections 14(5) and 17(7) of the Act are both fully in force.
