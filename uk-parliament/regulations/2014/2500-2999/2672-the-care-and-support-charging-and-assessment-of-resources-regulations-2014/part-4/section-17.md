# The Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/2672
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Notional income[^c-71983a007cc16dd26b22f7c6482f0840]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The adult is to be treated as possessing income of which the adult has deprived themselves for the purpose of decreasing the amount they may be liable to pay towards the cost of meeting their needs for care and support, or their needs for support.

(2) The adult is to be treated as possessing any income which would be treated as income possessed by a claimant of income support under regulation 42(2) to (4A) of the Income Support Regulations (notional income)[^f00024].

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), the adult is to be treated as possessing any income paid or due to be paid to a local authority by a third party pursuant to an agreement between the local authority and the third party made in connection with the liability of the adult to pay towards the cost of accommodation provided for the adult under the Act.

(4) The adult is not to be treated as possessing any voluntary payment of income made by a third party to a local authority for the purpose of discharging any arrears of the payments required by the authority from the adult for accommodation provided under the Act.
