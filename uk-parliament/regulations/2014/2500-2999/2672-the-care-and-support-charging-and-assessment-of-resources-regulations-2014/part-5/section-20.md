## 20. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom[^key-c5f5dcb578453a4d148018fca83b9a7b]

*Extent: E+W*

Capital which the adult possesses in the United Kingdom is to be calculated at its current market or surrender value (whichever is the higher), less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10%; and
- (b) the amount of any encumbrance secured on it.
