## 21. Calculation of capital outside the United Kingdom[^key-ddaeeb0ac5f98d7f949f5b67f56d3adb]

*Extent: E+W*

Capital which the adult possesses outside of the United Kingdom shall be calculated in accordance with the method set out in regulation 50 of the Income Support Regulations (calculation of capital outside the United Kingdom).
