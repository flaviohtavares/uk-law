> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Financial assessment[^c-eba50dffce06cb38a0c6e370e09754f1]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must carry out a financial assessment of the adult under section 17(1), (3) or (4) of the Act in accordance with the provisions of Parts 3 to 5 of these Regulations.
