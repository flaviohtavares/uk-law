## 4. Adults to whom services are to be provided free of charge[^c-367381e1d5c51b4549871017f06d1dfb]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must not make a charge for meeting needs under section 14(1) of the Act where the care and support is provided to an adult, under section 18, 19 or 20 of the Act, suffering from variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease.
