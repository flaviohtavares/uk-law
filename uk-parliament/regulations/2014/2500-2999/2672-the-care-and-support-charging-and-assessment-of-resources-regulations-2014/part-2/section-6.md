# The Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014, section 6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Personal expenses allowance for residents or temporary residents provided with accommodation in a care home[^key-b1aef70959586ba41be7d526858e8f54]

*Extent: E+W*

The amount specified for the purposes of section 14(7) of the Act[^f00015] in relation to a resident or temporary resident provided with accommodation in a care home is £31.80[^key-1ca92e6e2197d00604e5e977c1d44f9b] each week.
