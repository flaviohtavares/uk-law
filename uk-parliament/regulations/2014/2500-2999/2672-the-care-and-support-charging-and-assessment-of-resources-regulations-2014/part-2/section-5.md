# The Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014, section 5

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Costs of putting in place arrangements to meet needs[^c-c98288cbca933eab853f64f602af10e6]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a local authority is meeting needs because Condition 2 in section 18, or Condition 2 or 4 in section 20, of the Act is met, the charge the authority may make under section 14(1)(b) of the Act may only cover the cost that the authority incurs in putting in place the arrangements for meeting those needs.
