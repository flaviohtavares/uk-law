# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 27

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Provision against danger to navigation[^key-72604c6b99f491c8120e9f07878e0cca]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In case of damage to, or destruction or decay of, a tidal work or any part of it, the undertaker must as soon as reasonably practicable notify the Conservancy Authority and Trinity House and must lay down such buoys, exhibit such lights and take such other steps for preventing danger to navigation as Trinity House and the Conservancy Authority or, failing agreement between them, the Conservancy Authority may from time to time direct.

(2) If the undertaker fails to notify the Conservancy Authority or Trinity House as required by this article or to comply in any respect with any requirements of a direction given under this article, it is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
