# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 44

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Deemed marine licence[^key-d32d14fbd96d0840eccfe43cbe738d53]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker is deemed to be granted a licence under Part 4 (marine licences) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00030] to carry out the works described in Schedule 8 (deemed marine licence), subject to the provisions set out in that Schedule, which are to be treated as licence conditions.
