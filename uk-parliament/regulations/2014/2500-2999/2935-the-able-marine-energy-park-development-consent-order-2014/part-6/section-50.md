# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 50

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-c097b151205d7dc0c042ac1ee525b316]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order within the area of jurisdiction is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act).
