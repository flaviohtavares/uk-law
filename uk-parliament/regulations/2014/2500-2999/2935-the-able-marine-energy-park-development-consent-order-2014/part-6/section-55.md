# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 55

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. Certification of plans etc[^key-55c5b7992e583db9bb2c5bf21517ac9f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, must submit to the Secretary of State copies of—

- (a) the book of reference;
- (b) the design drawings;
- (c) the ecology plans;
- (d) the land plans;
- (e) the rights of way plans;
- (f) the environmental statement as defined in paragraph 1 of Schedule 11 (requirements); and
- (g) the works plans,

for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
