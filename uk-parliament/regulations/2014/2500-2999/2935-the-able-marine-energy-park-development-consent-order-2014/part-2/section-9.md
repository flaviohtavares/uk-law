# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Agreements entered into by the Company etc.[^key-0b3b1d3bc06df9adadeabc131ac24a5a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any agreement or undertaking entered into by the Company before the coming into force of this Order in connection with the proposed exercise of its functions as Harbour Authority is binding upon the Harbour Authority regardless of the fact that it was entered into by the Company before it was established as the Harbour Authority by article 8 (jurisdiction of the Harbour Authority) unless any provision of the agreement or undertaking would be outside the statutory functions of the Harbour Authority.
