# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 32

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-114b03980fdbb396a4ea939a23c8436b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00026] (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
