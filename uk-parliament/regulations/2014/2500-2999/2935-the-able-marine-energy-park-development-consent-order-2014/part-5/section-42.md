# The Able Marine Energy Park Development Consent Order 2014, section 42

> Text as at 2022-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Statutory undertakers[^c-e81f1c30ca4f6a84cca1001d21a12618]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may only extinguish the rights of, remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers if such extinguishment, removal or repositioning is necessary for carrying out the authorised development.
