# The Walney Extension Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 32

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Operational land for the purposes of the 1990 Act[^key-5ec69abe2b896d8efee0d41ba67901dd]

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order shall be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act).
