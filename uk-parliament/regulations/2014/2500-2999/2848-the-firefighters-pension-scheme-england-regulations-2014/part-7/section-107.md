# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 107

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-c3f202076b78f7694c9aa1994d0edc11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under these Regulations are subject to the reduction of the relevant amount calculated in accordance with regulation 54 (establishment of pension credit member’s account).
