# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 106

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Annual rate of pension credit member’s pension[^c-16bed882533328a1a5aa12a32373bc3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The annual rate of a pension credit member’s pension is calculated by—

- (a) taking the amount of credited pension specified in the pension credit member’s account; and
- (b) subtracting the commutation amount (if any) specified in that account in relation to that amount.
