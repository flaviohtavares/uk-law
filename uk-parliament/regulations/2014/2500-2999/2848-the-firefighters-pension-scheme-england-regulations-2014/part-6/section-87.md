# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 87

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Eligible child’s pension payable on death of deferred member[^key-7d11def53b8370f6001cc2014d96f7f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to an eligible child of a member who dies if, at the date of death, the member is a deferred member.

(2) The annual rate of an eligible child’s pension is an amount equal to the specified proportion of the sum of the provisional amount of deferred pension specified in the deferred member’s account and the amount of the added pension (if any) specified in the added pension account.
