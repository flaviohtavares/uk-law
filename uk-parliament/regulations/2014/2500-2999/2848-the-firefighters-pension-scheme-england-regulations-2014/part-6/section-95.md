# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 95

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Person to whom lump sum death benefit payable[^key-a410bb55e07b926a0690762a0afe8db4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager may, at its absolute discretion, pay any lump sum death benefit payable under this Chapter to or for the benefit of the member’s nominee, personal representatives or any person appearing to the scheme manager to have been a relative or dependent of the member.
