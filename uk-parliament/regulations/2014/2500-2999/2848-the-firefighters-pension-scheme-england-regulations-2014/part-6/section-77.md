# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 77

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Meaning of “initial period”[^key-307837a2c5ce798ba7ad0eeed7e64d52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of these Regulations[^key-8a7b49444ca481014921f86211098a8a], the “initial period” is the period of 13 weeks commencing on the day after the death of the member during which a bereavement pension may be payable to any surviving partner or eligible child.
