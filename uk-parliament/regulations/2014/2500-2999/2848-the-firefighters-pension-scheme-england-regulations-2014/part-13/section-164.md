# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 164

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Late payment of retirement index adjustment[^key-6dd48a9b2f899195676e0f9d4921b14b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment to be paid before the end of the last active scheme year.
