# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 187

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 187. Duty to have regard to guidance[^c-5ffb4e37b97c36a739b1e6d916faba3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A scheme manager must have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this Part.
