# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 177

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 177. Scheme administrator for the purposes of the Finance Act 2004[^key-e39bde52727dd1ca26c2fdcbdf5e49a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all liabilities and responsibilities connected with the functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004 which the scheme manager assumes as sub-scheme administrator under regulation 3 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Registered Pension Schemes (Splitting of Schemes) Regulations 2006[^f00068].
