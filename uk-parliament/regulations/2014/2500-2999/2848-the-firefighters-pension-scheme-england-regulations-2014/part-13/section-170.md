# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 170

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Limitation on assignment of benefits[^c-ba24ed0833fddfbd526133a6b457d78f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An assignment of an award under these Regulations is void to the extent that it is in favour of a person other than a dependant of the person entitled to the award.
