# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 22

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Description of pension[^c-985a250d9ae8d3847d270093ed31d079]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of this Part, description of pension means any of the following—

- (a) earned pension;
- (b) transferred pension;
- (c) club transfer earned pension;
- (d) added pension.
