# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 21

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Pension credit member[^c-ee20308876e885b96c3968eeb735025d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is a pension credit member of this scheme if that person has been given a pension credit in the scheme as a consequence of a pension debit created under section 29 of the WRPA 1999[^c-9f9fc4c770c4bdbfbfd0022ce20e25be] in relation to a member of this scheme.
