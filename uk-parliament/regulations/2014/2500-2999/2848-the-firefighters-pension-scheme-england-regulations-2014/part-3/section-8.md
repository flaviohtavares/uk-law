# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 8

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Service in two or more scheme employments[^key-a29a63b1d264b5741bc3c6101ec77c55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person is in service in two or more scheme employments, regulation 7 (eligible persons) applies separately in relation to each employment.
