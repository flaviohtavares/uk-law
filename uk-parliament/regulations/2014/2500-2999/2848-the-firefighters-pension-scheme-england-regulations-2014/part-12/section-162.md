# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 162

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Notices etc[^key-fd041415dabf03b58a5435cd18259d63]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any notice, information or document which an appellant (P) is entitled to receive for any purpose of regulations 154 (appeals against determinations based on medical evidence) to 161 (expenses of each party) is, unless the contrary is proved, to be treated as having been received by P if it was posted in a letter addressed to P at P’s last known place of residence.
