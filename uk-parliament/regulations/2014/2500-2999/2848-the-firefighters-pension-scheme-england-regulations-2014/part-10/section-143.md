# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 143

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Amount of transferred pension[^c-91c974fd6f058c6aebd0f62ca5e100e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to—

- (a) any transfer payment received in relation to P from another pension scheme that is not a club scheme; and
- (b) any transfer payment in respect of added pension received in relation to P from another club scheme.

(2) For the scheme year in which the transfer date falls, the amount of transferred pension P is entitled to count in respect of the transfer payment is—

- (a) the amount specified in the transfer statement; or
- (b) if such a statement is not provided or is not calculated in accordance with actuarial guidance, an amount calculated by the scheme manager in accordance with actuarial guidance.
