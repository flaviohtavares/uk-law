# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 147

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Request to confirm details on certificate[^key-5cf21b26a3e0f6ada7eded61d37e9769]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a member has been provided with a certificate under regulation 146 (requirement for scheme manager to provide a certificate) and is dissatisfied with the information stated on the certificate, that member may within the period of three months commencing on the date on which the certificate was received, request the scheme manager who provided the certificate to confirm the accuracy of the information contained in it or to provide an amended certificate.
