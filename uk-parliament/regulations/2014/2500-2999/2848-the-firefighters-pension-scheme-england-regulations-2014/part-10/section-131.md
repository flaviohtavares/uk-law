# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (England) Regulations 2014, section 131

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Application of Part[^c-6b3765e1bc805f7f6b7fd5c1b26262b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 4 of Part 4 of PSA 1993 (transfer values) and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 5 of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds)[^f00050] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
