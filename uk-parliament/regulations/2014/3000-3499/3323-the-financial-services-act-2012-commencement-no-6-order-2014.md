# The Financial Services Act 2012 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 3323 (C. 156)

Banks And Banking

The Financial Services Act 2012 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014

Made

17th December 2014

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122(3) and (4) of the Financial Services Act 2012[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Financial Services Act 2012 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014.

## 2. Provisions of the Financial Services Act 2012 coming into force on 1st January 2015

1st January 2015 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sections 96(1), (2), (5) and (6), 98 and 99 of the Financial Services Act 2012 for all purposes.

Mark Lancaster

Gavin Barwell

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

17th December 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force subsections (1), (2), (5) and (6) of section 96, and sections 98 and 99 of the Financial Services Act [2012 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/21) (“the Act”), which amend the Banking Act 2009, on 1st January 2015. This is the sixth commencement order to be made under the Act.

This Order will have no effect on the costs of business and the voluntary sector independent of the provision this Order brings into force. For that reason, no impact assessment has been produced for this instrument.
