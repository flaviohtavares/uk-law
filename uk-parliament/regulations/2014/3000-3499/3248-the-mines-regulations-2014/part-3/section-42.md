> Text as at 2020-03-02.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Transport rules[^c-9392a79406be3363bf180239c455dcf1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Rules required by regulation 12 must include rules for the purpose of ensuring the safe operation of vehicles, conveyors and traffic routes.
