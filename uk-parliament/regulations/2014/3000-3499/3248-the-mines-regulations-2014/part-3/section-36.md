# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 36

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Shaft construction and modification[^key-e03db1775aba5d738cd49a7e48339816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure that, so far as is reasonably practicable, the construction and modification of every shaft is planned, designed and carried out so as to be safe and without the risk of injury to any person.
