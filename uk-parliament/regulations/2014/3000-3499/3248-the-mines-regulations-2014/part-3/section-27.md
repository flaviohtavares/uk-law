# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 27

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Application of regulations 28 to 31[^key-37fe1f616c19e002541c36af6fd806bc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 28 to 31 apply to mines at which explosives are transported, stored, handled or used.
