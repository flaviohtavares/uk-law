> Text as at 2020-03-02.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Duties relating to explosives in mines with explosive atmospheres[^c-f6786431ba488db60c62882f865e84ef]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In mines in which places have been classified as hazardous under regulation 7(1) of DSEAR, the mine operator must ensure that—

- (a) the only explosives that are used are suitable for use in such places; and
- (b) before a shot is charged or fired, suitable tests for the presence of flammable gases are carried out.
