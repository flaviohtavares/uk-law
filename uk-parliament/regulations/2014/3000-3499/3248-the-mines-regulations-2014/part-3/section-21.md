# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 21

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21. Application of regulations 22 and 23[^key-cf0eae4a9b6c747606adeb3437377389]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 22 and 23 apply below ground in mines in which places have been classified as hazardous under regulation 7(1) of DSEAR.
