# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 64

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Records of substances tipped[^c-3cbe940e3a166bb273b6b74b5ebaa693]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure that sufficient records are kept of the nature, quantity and location of all substances accumulated or deposited at a notifiable tip to enable an accurate assessment of the stability of that tip to be made.
