# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Abandoned tips[^c-3e6654f5b300b2159b743ca3e0f6f8aa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulation 4(8), from the date of a notice of abandonment of a mine any tip that is deemed to form part of that mine (in this regulation referred to as an “abandoned tip”) ceases to be a tip to which these Regulations apply.

(2) Despite paragraph (1), regulation 68 continues to apply in relation to every report or record made in relation to an abandoned tip.
