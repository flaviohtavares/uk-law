# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 60

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60. General duty to ensure safety of tips[^key-473e8a9101aae5a36529fdc03a5fb131]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure that tips are designed, constructed, operated and maintained so as to ensure that—

- (a) instability; or
- (b) movement,

which is likely to give rise to a risk to the health and safety of any person is avoided.
