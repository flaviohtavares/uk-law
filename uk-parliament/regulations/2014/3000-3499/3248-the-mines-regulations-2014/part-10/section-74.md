# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 74

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 74. Repeals and revocations[^key-752eeafaca077bf910c818bc4a7b27f6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The provisions of the Acts specified in column 1 of Schedule 3 are repealed to the extent set out in the corresponding entry in column 2.

(2) The instruments specified in Part 1 of Schedule 4 are revoked in full.

(3) The instruments specified in column 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 4 are revoked to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in column 2.
