# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 72

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 72. Transitional provisions: tips[^key-e79ab961ee7c29bfa130696631814ac0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A mine operator must carry out by 5th April 2016 the first appraisal of a tip that, immediately before the commencement date, was not classified within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of the 1971 Regulations.

(2) Where, before the commencement date, a report has been obtained in accordance with regulation 9(2)(a), 12(1) or 18(1) of the 1971 Regulations and is less than two years old, that report—

- (a) must be treated as a geotechnical assessment for the purpose of regulation 62; and
- (b) remains valid for two years from the date on which it was made.
