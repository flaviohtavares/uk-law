# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 69

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Plans of abandoned mines[^c-b22cad6cafbe940fb0d123d2c5c70b12]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the mine operator gives a notice of abandonment, the mine operator must, within three months of the date on which the mine is abandoned or the tip is closed, send to the Executive, or a body approved by it, an accurate plan of that mine or tip (as the case may be).

(2) Where a plan has been sent to the Executive or a body approved by it under paragraph (1), that plan must be retained by the Executive or that body in accordance with arrangements approved by the Executive.
