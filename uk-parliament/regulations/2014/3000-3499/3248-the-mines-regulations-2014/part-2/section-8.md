# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 8

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Co-operation[^key-9a5148b92ca1639986767a5119f7580b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every employer of persons at work at a mine and every person at work at a mine must co-operate with the mine operator to the extent necessary to enable the mine operator to comply with the relevant statutory provisions.
