# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 16

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Record of persons below ground[^c-de6334f39616ae293cee8737b98cdee1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure that a record is kept of every person below ground at the mine.
