# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 51

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Auxiliary equipment[^c-75e5742c988316b704644010e90d5f40]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure that suitable auxiliary equipment is available for use when any equipment that is required to be used by persons to leave the mine becomes unavailable or breaks down.
