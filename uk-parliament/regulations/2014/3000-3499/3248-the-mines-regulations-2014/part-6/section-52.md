# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 52

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Intake airways[^c-aa76f95c920820d3659e1476b6366c6b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The mine operator must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that the intake airways for the mine are constructed of suitable fire resistant materials.
