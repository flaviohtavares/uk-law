# The Mines Regulations 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2020-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-68be086e83b0c965dbb0cca34e596326]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mines Regulations 2014.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 6th April 2015.

(3) These Regulations apply outside Great Britain as sections 1 to 59 and 80 to 82 of the 1974 Act apply by virtue of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application outside Great Britain) Order 2013[^f00007].
