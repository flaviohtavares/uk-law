> Text as at 2017-12-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Power to operate and use the authorised works[^c-31f453abcf4c48f361243e0aa909304a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

LUL may operate and use the authorised works as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of passengers.
