# The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 3192 (C. 142)

Criminal Law

The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014

Made

2nd December 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 94 of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2014.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 3rd December 2014

The following provisions of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 come into force on 3rd December 2014—

- (a) Section 90 (freezing of terrorist property);
- (b) Schedule 4 (terrorist property: freezing orders).

James Brokenshire

Minister of State

Home Office

2nd December 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the sixth[^f00002] commencement order made under the Crime (International Co-operation) Act [2003 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/32). It brings into force section 90 of, and Schedule 4 to, that Act.

Schedule 4 amends the Terrorism Act [2000 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11) to implement the Framework Decision[^f00003] on the execution in the European Union of orders freezing property or evidence adopted by the Council of the European Union on 22nd July 2003 in relation to terrorist property.
