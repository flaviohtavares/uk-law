# The Access to the Countryside (Coastal Margin) (Sea Palling to Weybourne) Order 2014

> Text as at 2014-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2014 No. 3128

Countryside, England

The Access to the Countryside (Coastal Margin) (Sea Palling to Weybourne) Order 2014

Made

23rd November 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A(10) of the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Access to the Countryside (Coastal Margin) (Sea Palling to Weybourne) Order 2014.

## 2. End of access preparation period

(1) In relation to land which is coastal margin as a result of the relevant approval, the day appointed as the day on which the access preparation period[^f00002] ends is 11th December 2014.

(2) For the purposes of this article–

- (a) “the relevant approval” means the approval (under section 52(1) of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949[^f00003]) by the Secretary of State[^f00004] of the relevant report, on 7th April 2014; and
- (b) “the relevant report” means the report entitled “Coastal Access Sea Palling to Weybourne” relating to a long-distance route in Norfolk, from Sea Palling to Weybourne, submitted by Natural England to the Secretary of State (under sections 51 and 55A[^f00005] of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949) on 22nd July 2013.

Dan Rogerson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

23rd November 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 3A(10) of the Countryside and Rights of Way Act [2000 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/37). It appoints the day on which the access preparation period ends in relation to land which is coastal margin as a result of the approval by the Secretary of State of Natural England’s proposals contained in a report under sections 51 and 55A of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act [1949 (c. 97)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1949/97) (“coastal access report”). The coastal access report in question relates to the long-distance route in Norfolk which runs from Sea Palling to Weybourne.

On 22nd July 2013 Natural England submitted to the Secretary of State its coastal access report (entitled *Coastal Access Sea Palling to Weybourne*) setting out the proposals for a long-distance route from Sea Palling to Weybourne. A copy of this report is available online at www.gov.uk, or may be obtained by writing to Coastal Access Delivery Team - Norfolk, Natural England, Eastbrook, Shaftesbury Road, Cambridge CB2 8DR. The Secretary of State approved this report on 7th April 2014. A copy of the Secretary of State’s notice of approval is available online at www.gov.uk, or may be obtained by writing to DEFRA, Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 2AL.

A full impact assessment has not been prepared in respect of this Order because the assumptions underlying the Order were reflected in the impact assessment which was prepared for the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009. It may be found online at www.gov.uk, or may be obtained by writing to DEFRA, Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 2AL.
