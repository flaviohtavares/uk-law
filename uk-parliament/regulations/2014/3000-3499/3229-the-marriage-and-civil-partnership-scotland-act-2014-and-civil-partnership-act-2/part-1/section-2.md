# The Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 and Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2014, section 2

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-d5247dfc2e72c5f89bf1044e90068ffa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- “the 1977 Act” means the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977[^f00003];
- “the 1992 Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00004];
- “the 2004 Act” means the Gender Recognition Act 2004[^f00005];
- “the 2006 Act” means the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00006];
- “the 2013 Act” means the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013[^f00007];
- “the 2014 Act” means the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00008].
