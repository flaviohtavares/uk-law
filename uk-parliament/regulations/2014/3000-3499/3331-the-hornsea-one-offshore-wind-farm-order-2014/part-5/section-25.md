# The Hornsea One Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 25

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Statutory undertakers[^c-d18aa26e230a227c44980bc217d5aa1e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 12 (Protective Provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) exercise the powers conferred by articles 15 (compulsory acquisition of land) and 16 (compulsory acquisition of rights) in relation to so much of any land referred to in those articles as belongs to statutory undertakers;
- (b) extinguish the rights of, remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers over or within the Order land referred to in those articles.
