# The Hornsea One Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-45223ef64b09fa1f2c304dc4121431cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land shown numbered 404 on the land plans as is required for the authorised project or to facilitate, or is incidental, to it.
