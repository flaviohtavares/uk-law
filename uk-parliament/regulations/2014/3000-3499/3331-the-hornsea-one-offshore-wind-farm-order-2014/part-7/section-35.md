# The Hornsea One Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 35

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Deemed marine licences under the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^key-475e055459894ca8401b0962d996d1c5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The person who is the licence-holder for the purpose of each of Schedules 8 to 11 is deemed to be granted under Part 4 of the 2009 Act the deemed marine licence set out in the relevant Schedule, subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule.
