# The Hornsea One Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 32

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Procedure in relation to approvals etc under requirements[^key-ac78aa9736e6e256a1fb25c157bc66ea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where an application is made to the relevant planning authority or authorities for any consent, agreement or approval required by a requirement, the following provisions apply, so far as they relate to a consent, agreement or approval of a local planning authority required by a condition imposed on a grant of planning permission as if the requirement was a condition imposed on the grant of planning permission—

- (a) sections 78 and 79 of the 1990 Act (right of appeal in relation to planning decisions);
- (b) any orders, rules or regulations which make provision in relation to appeals under section 78 of the 1990 Act,

insofar as those provisions are not inconsistent with the Infrastructure Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment Regulations) 2009[^f00024] and any orders, rules or regulations made under the 2008 Act.
