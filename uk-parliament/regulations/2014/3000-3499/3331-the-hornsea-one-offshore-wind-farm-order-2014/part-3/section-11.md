# The Hornsea One Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Highway improvements[^key-cbd6800102072c2221f844421a8010b1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may carry out highway improvements to the plot marked 106 on the land plans.

(2) The highway improvements should be carried out in accordance with plans approved by the relevant highway authority, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld.

(3) If the relevant highway authority fails to notify the undertaker of its decision on whether to give approval within 28 days of receiving plans for approval that highway authority is deemed to have given approval.
