# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 57

## 57. Notice of matters which could necessitate an amendment of a plan

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The PRA and the FCA must notify the Bank without delay of any change of circumstances or other matter coming to their attention which could necessitate an amendment of a resolution plan or group resolution plan.
