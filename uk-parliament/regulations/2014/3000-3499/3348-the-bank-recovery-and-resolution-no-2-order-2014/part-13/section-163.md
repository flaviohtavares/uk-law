# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 163

## 163. Application and interpretation of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where, in relation to a relevant group—

- (a) the Bank has made a resolution instrument under section 12A of the Banking Act 2009 in respect of a single group entity which is not an institution (“the relevant entity”); and
- (b) the management body of the relevant entity or the[^key-8d00c0659ff75b624d0a9ff45a759d8f] resolution administrator submits a to the Bank for assessment [^key-e945886855b69a3bd94a1fcde40878bc]....

(2) In this Chapter “**business reorganisation plan**” has the meaning given in section 48H of the Banking Act 2009, as applied by section 81BA of that Act[^key-a6b262961e84bd25431c5ab5cc55245a] and “**resolution administrator**” has the[^key-feaba94839945a5716827a708b460d65] same meaning for the relevant entity as it has[^key-4e656828ea6b8389249402bd87b6d4cb] for an institution in Chapter 1.
