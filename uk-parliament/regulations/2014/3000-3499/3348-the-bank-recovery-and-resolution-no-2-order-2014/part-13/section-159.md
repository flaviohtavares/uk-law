# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 159

## 159. Application and interpretation of Chapter 1

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where—

- (a) an institution is authorised by the PRA or FCA and is not part of a group subject to supervision on a consolidated basis in accordance with the capital requirements regulation and CRR rules[^c-9c03cdeb69dfca34d312fdbf2a99c60a];
- (b) the Bank has made a resolution instrument under section 12A of the Banking Act 2009 [^c22308101] (bail-in option) in respect of the institution; and
- (c) the management body of the institution or the[^c-a46657fbc8a45628ee6d8f46b93d237f] resolution administrator submits a business reorganisation plan to the Bank for assessment in accordance with section 48H of the Banking Act 2009[^c-96207936a3a0bd1efc9eedb0fa29dc0a] (business reorganisation plan).

(2) In this Chapter—

- [^c-8f92e9c3610d7a25a55f64780205c26a]...
- [^c-8f92e9c3610d7a25a55f64780205c26a]...
- “**resolution administrator**” means the individual or body corporate appointed by the Bank under section 62B of the Banking Act 2009 [^c22308111] as the resolution administrator of the institution.
