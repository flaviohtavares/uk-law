# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 160

## 160. Assessment of business reorganisation plan

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Bank must assess the business reorganisation plan jointly with the appropriate regulator within one month beginning with the date on which it receives the plan.
