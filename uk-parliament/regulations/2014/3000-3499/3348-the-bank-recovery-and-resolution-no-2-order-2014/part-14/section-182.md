# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 182

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Matters to be notified by the regulator to the Bank

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The regulator must notify the Bank if—

- (a) an undertaking notifies the regulator that the undertaking is failing or likely to fail (within the meaning given in section 7(5C) of the Banking Act 2009[^c-7a02165ac99fc4e9a53f3f82acec1aba]); or
- (b) the regulator requires an undertaking to take crisis prevention measures [^c22308171] or a supervisory measure[^c-5903d3a127dbd44c7c0eacc4b75bdf81].
