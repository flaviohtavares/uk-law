# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 184

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 184. Duty to send copy of share transfer instrument etc to members and creditors of institution

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where, in respect of an undertaking—

- (a) the Bank has applied one or more of the resolution tools; or
- (b) the Treasury have made a share transfer order for the purpose of taking the undertaking into temporary public ownership.

(2) Except where securities issued by the undertaking have been admitted to trading on a regulated market (within the meaning given in section 103(1) of FSMA), the Bank must send a copy of any property transfer instrument, resolution instrument, share transfer instrument, share transfer order or third-country instrument made in respect of the undertaking to the members and creditors of the undertaking who are known to the Bank.

(3) In this article—

- “**member**” includes—
  - (a) a shareholder of a company;
  - (b) a member of a limited liability partnership; and
  - (c) a shareholding or borrowing member of a building society established under the Building Societies Act 1986 [^c22308191] (“shareholding member” and “borrowing member” have the meaning given in paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 2 to that Act);
- “**property transfer instrument**” means a property transfer instrument (within the meaning given by section 33 [^c22308201]) made under section 11 (private sector purchaser), section 41A [^c22308211] (transfer of property subsequent to resolution instrument), section 42 [^c22308221] (supplemental instruments), section 42A [^c22308231] (private sector purchaser: reverse property transfer), section 43 [^c22308241] (onward transfer), section 44 [^c22308251] (resolution company: reverse property transfer) or section 44A [^c22308261] (bail-in: reverse property transfer);
- “**resolution instrument**” means a resolution instrument made under section 12A (bail-in option), section 48U (supplemental resolution instruments), section 48V (onward transfer) or section 48W (reverse transfer) [^c22308271];
- “**share transfer instrument**” means a share transfer instrument (within the meaning given by section 15) made under section 11, section 26 [^c22308281] (supplemental instruments), section 26A [^c22308291] (private sector purchaser: reverse share transfer), section 30 [^c22308301] (resolution company: share transfers) or section 31 [^c22308311] (resolution company: reverse share transfer);
- “**share transfer order**” means a share transfer order (within the meaning given by section 16) made by the Treasury under section 13 [^c22308321] (temporary public ownership), section 27 [^c22308331] (supplemental orders), section 28 [^c22308341] (onward transfer) or section 29 [^c22308351] (reverse share transfer); and
- “**third-country instrument**” has the meaning given in section 89I(4) [^c22308361].

(4) In paragraph (3) each reference to a section is a reference to a section of the Banking Act 2009.
