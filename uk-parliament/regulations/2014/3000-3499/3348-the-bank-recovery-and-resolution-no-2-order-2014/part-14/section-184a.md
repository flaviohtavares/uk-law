# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 184A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 184A. Notifications under articles 182, 183 and 184[^key-f64601f2bdb5120f7b1340ec51bbc79e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The PRA and the FCA may each make technical standards specifying the procedures for, and contents of notifications under article 182 or 183(1) in circumstances where it is the regulator.

(2) The Bank may make technical standards specifying—

- (a) the procedures for, and contents of notifications under article 183(2); or
- (b) the procedures for sending documents under article 184.
