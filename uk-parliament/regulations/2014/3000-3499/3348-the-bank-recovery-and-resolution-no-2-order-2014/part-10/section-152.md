## 152. Determination that Case 2, 3, 4 or 5 is satisfied

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where, in the Bank's assessment, there is no reasonable prospect that any alternative measure which is available and could feasibly be taken would, within a reasonable time, avoid the need for the determination referred to in a Case 2, 4 or 5 notice or a Case 3 notice.

(2) [^key-f9eee486a49d0e3ec944b41d8e19db2f]... The Bank must decide whether to make the determination referred to in the notice.
