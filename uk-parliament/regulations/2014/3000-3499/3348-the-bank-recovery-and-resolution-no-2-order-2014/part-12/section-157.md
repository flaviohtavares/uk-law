# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 157

## 157. Application and interpretation of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies where the Bank has decided to apply the stabilisation option referred to in paragraph (c) of section 1(3) (the bail-in option) in relation to liabilities arising from a derivative contract.

(2) In this Part each reference to a section is a reference to a section of the Banking Act 2009.
