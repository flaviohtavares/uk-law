## 61. Application and interpretation of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where the Bank—

- (a) [^key-f090b9f8cdf7d9790b493aae89d7df2c]... draws up a group resolution plan in accordance with Chapter 2 of Part 5 or reviews a plan drawn up in accordance with that Chapter[^key-f090b9f8cdf7d9790b493aae89d7df2c]...
- (b)[^key-3a54700d2878a5e4530267c97adc47dd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In this Chapter “**assessment of group resolvability**” means an assessment of the extent to which it would be feasible and credible to apply the resolution tools or exercise resolution powers in respect of resolution entities, or take[^key-be78aaf8f52a0e57fcca18515fdd5267] insolvency proceedings in respect of group entities while avoiding to the maximum extent possible any significant adverse effect on the financial system of the United Kingdom[^key-0ab4973da8315ce6bc7e9e6c4ae1fd56] or the continuity of the critical functions of group entities.
