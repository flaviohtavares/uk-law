# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 216

## 216. Interpretation of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “[^key-cb34c664241c5d2218787927d7c26402]**applying the public equity support tool**” means participating in the recapitalisation of an institution or an entity by providing capital to the institution or entity in exchange for Common Equity Tier 1 instruments, Additional Tier 1 instruments or Tier 2 instruments;
- “**Common Equity Tier 1 instruments**”, “**Additional Tier 1 instruments**” and “**Tier 2 instruments**” have the meanings given in section 3(1) of the Banking Act 2009;
- “**the use of resolution tools, powers and mechanisms**” means—
  - (a) the exercise by the Bank or the Treasury of a stabilisation power (within the meaning given in section 1(4) of the Banking Act 2009);
  - (b) the making by the Bank of a mandatory reduction instrument (within the meaning given in section 6B of that Act [^c22308551]); or
  - (c) the exercise by the Treasury of its powers under section 228 of the Banking Act 2009, subject to the requirements of the capital requirements regulation[^key-32051e40489e34a5aee5c46c0425f8e9] and CRR rules[^key-ce1dd71fb68965ef4b97e86d035b0542], where the Treasury is applying the public equity support tool; and
- “**UK-registered company**” has the meaning given in section 1158 of the Companies Act 2006 [^c22308561] (meaning of UK-registered company).

(2)[^key-8656159f3522fa0af7d03ce0c667a6ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of this Part a company is a company under resolution if it is a UK-registered company which is subject to the use of resolution tools, powers and mechanisms.

(4) But such a company is not a company under resolution if—

- (a) it has ceased to be subject to the exercise of a stabilisation power or the application of the public equity support tool; and
- (b) the results which are to be achieved by an instrument made in respect of the company under Part 1 of the Banking Act 2009 have been achieved.
