# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Obligation to provide annual report

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The directors of the UK group entity which has entered into an authorised agreement must prepare an annual report on the performance of the agreement and the implementation of any decision taken pursuant to it.

(2) The directors must deliver a copy of the annual report to every member of the entity, electronically or by other means, no later than the first and each subsequent anniversary of the date on which the entity enters into the agreement.
