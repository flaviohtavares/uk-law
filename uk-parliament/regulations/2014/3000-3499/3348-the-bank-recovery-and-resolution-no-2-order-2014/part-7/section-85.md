# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348

## 85. Duty to transmit a copy of application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate regulator must send a copy of the application or, where paragraph (2) has effect in relation to any information, of the application without that information, without delay to any[^c-27d359fd478e2e619bdf5745f0f5436a] relevant competent authority.

(2) This article does not require any information contained in the application to be disclosed if its disclosure would be contrary to section 348 of FSMA.
