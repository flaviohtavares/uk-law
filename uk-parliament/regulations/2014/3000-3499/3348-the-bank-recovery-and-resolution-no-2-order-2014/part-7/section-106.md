# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 106

## 106. Reciprocal support

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the PRA or FCA agrees the provision, with or without restrictions, of the financial support to which a relevant notice refers, the notifying group entity may agree with the intended recipient of that support to receive financial support from the intended recipient.
