# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 98A

## 98A. Publication of information concerning group financial support agreements[^c-b8eba941f72fc821a5ed2212d7490aef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The PRA and the FCA may each make technical standards relating to the form and content of any description of entry into a group financial support agreement which the directors of a UK group entity are required to publish by rules made by the PRA or the FCA under Part 9A of FSMA.
