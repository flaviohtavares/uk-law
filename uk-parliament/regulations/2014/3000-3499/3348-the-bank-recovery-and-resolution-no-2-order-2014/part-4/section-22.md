# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Revision of plan

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate regulator—

- (a) must notify a UK parent undertaking[^c-54f7c694b252fa0b55e3e9a58e856691] if the group recovery plan is found on assessment to contain an impediment; and
- (b) may not require a UK parent undertaking to revise the plan without giving it an opportunity to state its opinion on that requirement.

(2) If the appropriate regulator requires a UK parent undertaking[^c-b8165fc0133a5038ee7d4d0a54ba8c17] to revise the plan, it must allow the undertaking[^c-33d6894670dca938e352ac034968811f] two months, which it may on application by the undertaking extend to three months, to prepare a plan which demonstrates that the impediment has been addressed.
