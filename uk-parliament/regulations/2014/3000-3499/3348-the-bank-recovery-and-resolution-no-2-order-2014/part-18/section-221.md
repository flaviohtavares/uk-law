# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 221

## 221. Investment firms to be treated as financial institutions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An investment firm within the meaning given in section 258A of the Banking Act 2009 [^c22308671] (“investment firm”) is to be treated as a financial institution for the purposes of section 228 (Consolidated Fund) and 229 (National Loans Fund) of that Act.
