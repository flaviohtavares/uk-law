# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 138

## 138. Review of minimum requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank must review the minimum requirements for group institutions when, in accordance with Chapter 4 of Part 5, it reviews the group resolution plan.

(2) Articles 134 and 135[^c-ea0b09d9ac31124d1ddee753763cd07c] apply for the purpose of the review, but have effect for that purpose as if each reference to determining (or the determination of) a minimum requirement were a reference to re-determining (or the re-determination of) the requirement on review.
