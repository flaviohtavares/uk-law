# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 125

## 125. Application and interpretation of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where the PRA or FCA is the consolidating supervisor in relation to a relevant group.

(2) In this Chapter—

- [^c-d3dac33afcaaea67f0b3ea6f97aea34b]...
- “**group entity**” includes an undertaking which is—
  - (a) a parent undertaking of the UK[^c-fa270ed632b181d202f003d8d27669c4] parent undertaking; and
  - (b) a mixed activity holding company which has at least one subsidiary which—
    - (i) is an institution; and
    - (ii) is not a subsidiary of a financial holding company which is also a subsidiary of the mixed activity holding company;
- “**group institution**” means—
  - (a) the UK[^c-1c14f0b5b76c2c86620b966a0c4841e3] parent undertaking, if it is a relevant institution;
  - (b) a group subsidiary which is a relevant institution;
  - (c) a group entity, other than an institution, which is—
    - (i) required under article 139 [^c-fb40d21d2e3381dd3c7697fcdcf10328]... to maintain a minimum requirement for own funds and eligible liabilities; [^c-fb40d21d2e3381dd3c7697fcdcf10328]...
    - (ii) [^c-2c77a51f262e9846ceceb4ef5387953a]...
  - (d) where the group resolution plan does not provide for the separate resolution [^c22307941] of a subsidiary set up in a third country, that subsidiary if it would be a relevant institution if it were set up in the UK[^c-d61e8f10be5795dae0ee7b1d7637ddc9];
- “**minimum consolidated requirement**” means the requirement for a minimum level of own funds and eligible liabilities of the group institutions , including any transitional minimum consolidated requirement of the group institutions in accordance with article 126(2C)[^c-ec395099b1ba661b29eed7818c4bc2eb] [^c-957d2b52d6607e4f3d12e8ecbc0ce767]...;
- “**minimum requirement**”, in relation to a group institution, means a minimum requirement for own funds and eligible liabilities , including any transitional minimum requirement in accordance with article 135(2C)[^c-3392fa68a0af15ddb06006741ba7fff3] [^c-417d126e4ee0033bedf901f98e17d24a]...; and
- “netting arrangement”—
  - (a) in relation to an institution authorised by the PRA or FCA, means a title transfer collateral arrangement, set-off arrangement or netting arrangement (within the meaning given by section 48(1)(b), (c) and (d) of the Banking Act 2009);
  - (b) [^c-b3efcc5bb6e02c2bfa78a991eb61e9b0]...

(3) “Relevant institution”, in the definition of “group institution”, means an institution which—

- (a) if authorised by the PRA or FCA, is not a mortgage credit institution within the meaning given in Chapter 1; and
- (b) if set up in a country other than the United Kingdom, does not meet criteria which are equivalent in that country to the criteria set out in article 121(2).
