# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 129

## 129. Review of minimum consolidated requirement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank must review the minimum consolidated requirement for each resolution group[^c-abd8ea82a53db815d524929ba0678c97] when, in accordance with Chapter 4 of Part 5, it reviews the group resolution plan.

(2) Article 126 applies[^c-8714ab18baea8f754d82d8edc6e0cb9e] for the purpose of the review, but have effect for that purpose as if each reference to determining (or the determination of) the minimum consolidated requirement were a reference to re-determining (or the re-determination of) the requirement on review.
