## 122. Duties of the Bank in relation to minimum requirement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank must exercise the powers conferred by section 3A of the Banking Act 2009 [^c22307931] (removal of impediments to the exercise of stabilisation powers etc)—

- (a) to ensure that a relevant institution is required at all times to maintain a minimum requirement for own funds and eligible liabilities [^key-45f48bd2986f9c67e31bc9c6285217eb]...; and
- (b) with the object of ensuring that at all times the institution meets the minimum requirement specified in a direction given for that purpose.
