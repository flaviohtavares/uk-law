# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Notice that institution meets the conditions for early intervention

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-3b4f27eb5859c31db048067979f81324] The appropriate regulator must notify the Bank without delay if it determines that a relevant institution meets the conditions for early intervention or the appointment of a temporary manager[^c-81771199feade1d2de08253b53b62a5e].

(2)[^c-40758a66a462268e67d2fd7ab01a9a89] The PRA and the FCA may each make technical standards relating to the circumstances in which a relevant institution may be taken as meeting the conditions for early intervention.
