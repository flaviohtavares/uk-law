## 110. Application and interpretation of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where the PRA or FCA is the consolidating supervisor in relation to a relevant group.

(2) In this Chapter—

- “measure for early intervention”—
  - (a) [^key-6bc647635e7c230abea87e1320e1c606]... has the same meaning as in Chapter 1;
  - (b) [^key-67c4c336618ee68a62eb5cb7155aef67]...
- [^key-514c1f8c31eee1668abee6379f5dd46a]...
- temporary manager” means—[^key-b73fda0e8e1cf247148e277d7f8ae316]
  - (a) in relation to a UK group entity, a temporary manager appointed by the appropriate regulator under section 71C of FSMA;
  - (b) [^key-9b12b2afae5ccf5b55573feb7836f1af]...
- “**UK group entity**” means—
  - (a) the UK[^key-ec3120c85bf0198f25122fe74ad609e3] parent undertaking[^key-6d5d80432b35a1119d3bfaccbaa4968a]...;
  - (b) a group subsidiary which is an institution authorised by the PRA or FCA.
