# The Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/3348
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Interpretation of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**Common Equity Tier 1 instruments**” has the meaning given in section 3(1) of the Banking Act 2009 [^c22308051]; and
- “[^c-6f75682fd17e20b72ab2b8af7d08bd9b]**relevant capital instruments**” has the meaning given in section 3(1) of the Banking Act 2009;
- “**UK entity**” means—
  - (a) an institution which is authorised by the PRA or FCA and is not part of a group subject to supervision on a consolidated basis in accordance with the capital requirements regulation and CRR rules[^c-3cc540de79e1635522dd8ec99e087897]; or
  - (b) in relation to a relevant group, a group entity set up in the United Kingdom.
