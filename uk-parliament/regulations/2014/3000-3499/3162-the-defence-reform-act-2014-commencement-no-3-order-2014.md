# The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 3162 (C. 139)

Defence

The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014

Made

27th November 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 50(1) and (2) of the Defence Reform Act 2014[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014.

**2.** In this Order “the Act” means the Defence Reform Act 2014.

## 3. Commencement

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 5 December 2014—

- (a) sections 14 to 18;
- (b) section 19(2), (3), (5) and (6);
- (c) sections 20 to 36;
- (d) sections 38 and 39;
- (e) section 43;
- (f) paragraph 10(2) to (6) of Schedule 4;
- (g) Schedule 5, with the exception of paragraph 2(5) and paragraph 6(4).

Philip Dunne

Minister for Defence Equipment, Support and Technology

Ministry of Defence

27th November 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of Part 2 of the Defence Reform Act [2014 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/20) relating to single source contracting.
