# The Business Rate Supplements Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 3200 (C. 143)

Local Government, England

The Business Rate Supplements Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2014

Made

1st December 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(2) of the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and application

This Order, which applies in relation to England only, may be cited as the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2014.

## 2. Commencement of the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009

Section 16(5) of, and Schedule 2 to, the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009 come into force, in so far as those provisions are not already in force, on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Penny Mordaunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

1st December 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on the day after the day on which this Order is made, section 16(5) of, and Schedule 2 to, the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009 in so far as those provisions are not already in force. Those provisions provide for a new type of business improvement district to be funded by a levy.

This Order completes the commencement of the Business Rate Supplements Act 2009.
