# The Single Source Contract Regulations 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2026-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Single Source Contract Regulations 2014 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.
