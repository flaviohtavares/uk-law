# The Single Source Contract Regulations 2014, section 4

> Text as at 2026-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Meaning of “contract completion date” and “component completion date”[^key-1c6e035f4eb7cc80078d7b89bf32de8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The “**contract completion date**”, in relation to a contract, means—

- (a)[^key-56266c80abd101400af6296fff5020de] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^key-9b7a785c2d32e9963b6bf66dd224734a]... the date on which the contractor completes all obligations which entitle it to final payment under the contract;
- (c) if the contract is terminated before the date described in sub-paragraph (1)(b)[^key-d5356df59c6c8272aea6881031ec4f28], the date on which the contract is terminated.

(1A)[^key-51caee7ab0df6f48d616992b9c275363] The “**component completion date**”, in relation to a component of a contract, means—

- (a) the date on which the contractor completes all obligations under that component which entitle it to final payment in respect of that component;
- (b) if that component is terminated, but any other part of the contract is not terminated, before the date described in sub-paragraph (a), the date on which the component is terminated.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b) and (1A)(a)[^key-39bacdb979bc3647f4523092d214e958], final payment under a contract or in respect of a component[^key-a561a290e9f72da188f2aa0c926e4f5a] does not include—

- (a) any payment relating to the effect of specified indices or rates (regulation 10(5) or (10));
- (b) any TCIF adjustment;
- (c) any payment under regulation 16 (final price adjustment); or
- (d) any amount which is determined by the SSRO to be payable.
