# The Single Source Contract Regulations 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2026-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Target cost incentive fee (“TCIF”) adjustment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The circumstances specified for the purposes of section 16(1) are that—

- (a) the qualifying defence contract—
  - (i) contains provision to the effect that the total price payable under the contract or component[^key-82a98c98f642095b5db4c04715a24917] is subject to adjustment in accordance with section 16(1)(b); and
  - (ii) specifies the matters which must be taken into account in determining the amount of that adjustment; and
- (b) the price payable under the contract or component[^key-1aa769955111e1fb534b7e2e8f0f9e60] was determined using the target pricing method.
