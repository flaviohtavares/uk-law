# The Single Source Contract Regulations 2014, section 66

> Text as at 2026-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Price adjustments for qualifying sub-contracts[^key-f3fb937cd83746b27087caf6a9f30618]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the amount of an adjustment to the contract price of a qualifying sub-contract is determined by the SSRO under regulation 16(7) or calculated under regulation 17—

- (a) the contract price is not to be adjusted by that amount; and
- (b) a payment for that amount must be made by the Secretary of State to the sub-contractor or by the sub-contractor to the Secretary of State (whichever is appropriate).
