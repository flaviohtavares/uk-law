# The Single Source Contract Regulations 2014, section 57

> Text as at 2026-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**contracting authority**” means the party which is, or would be, liable to pay the contract price under a qualifying sub-contract.
