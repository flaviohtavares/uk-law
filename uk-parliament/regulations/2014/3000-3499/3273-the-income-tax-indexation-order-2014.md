# The Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 3273

Income Tax

The Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2014

Made

10th December 2014

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(6) of the Income Tax Act 2007[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2014.

(2) In this Order references to sections are references to sections of the Income Tax Act 2007.

## 2. Indexation of allowances for the tax year 2015-16

For the tax year 2015-16—

- (a) the amount specified in section 38(1)[^f00002] (blind person’s allowance) is replaced with “£2,260”;
- (b) the amount specified in section 43[^f00003] (tax reductions for married couples and civil partners: the minimum amount) is replaced with “£3,180”;
- (c) the amount specified in section 45(3)(a)[^f00004] (married couple’s allowance: marriages before 5th December 2005) is replaced with “£8,275”;
- (d) the amount specified in section 46(3)(a)[^f00005] (married couple’s allowance: marriages and civil partnerships on or after 5th December 2005) is replaced with “£8,275”;
- (e) the amounts specified in sections 37(2), 45(4) and 46(4)[^f00006] (adjusted net income limit) is replaced with “£27,400”.

Alun Cairns

Mark Lancaster

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

10th December 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to the indexation requirement in section 57 of the Income Tax Act [2007 (c. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3) (“the Act”). Section 57 provides that, if the consumer prices index has risen, corresponding increases are to be made to certain allowances and limits. These increases have effect for the 2015-16 tax year.

Article 2 increases the amount of the blind person’s allowance and the minimum and maximum amounts of married couple’s allowance. It also increases the income limits for married couple’s allowance and the personal allowance for individuals born before 5th April 1938.

This Order does not make provision under section 21 of the Act (modifying the amounts of the basic rate limit and starting rate limit for savings) or under section 57(1)(a) of the Act (increasing the personal allowance for individuals born after 5th April 1938) as the requirement to do so was disapplied for the 2015-16 tax year by sections 2(2) and 3(3) of the Finance Act [2014 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26).

This Order also does not make provision, under section 57 of the Act, dealing with section 36 of the Act. Section 36 was repealed, with effect from the 2015-16 tax year, by section 2(5) of the Finance Act 2014.

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for this instrument as it gives effect to previously announced policy and relates to a predetermined indexation formula.
