# The Agriculture (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2014

> Text as at 2014-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2014 No. 1902

Agriculture

The Agriculture (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2014

Made

15th July 2014

Laid before Parliament

21st July 2014

Coming into force

20th August 2014

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union[^f00002] and makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 20th August 2014.

(2) The revocations made by regulation 2 and the Schedule have the same extent as the enactments revoked.

## 2. Revocations

The Regulations listed in the Schedule are revoked.

George Eustice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs
