# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 23

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Other circumstances in which a local authority must secure a re-assessment

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must secure a re-assessment of a child or young person's EHC Plan where it receives a request to do so from the responsible commissioning body for that child or young person.
