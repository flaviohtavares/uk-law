# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 40

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Training, qualifications and experience of mediators

*Extent: E+W*

Mediators must have sufficient knowledge of the legislation relating to special educational needs, health and social care to be able to conduct the mediation.
