# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 26

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Securing a re-assessment of educational, health care and social care provision

*Extent: E+W*

(1) When securing a re-assessment of educational, health care and social care provision in a child or young person's EHC plan a local authority must comply with the requirements of regulations 6 and 7.

(2) Regulations 8 and 9 also apply to re-assessments.
