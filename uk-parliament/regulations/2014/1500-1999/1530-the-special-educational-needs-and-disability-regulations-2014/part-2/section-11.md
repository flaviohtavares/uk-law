# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Preparation of EHC plans

*Extent: E+W*

When preparing a child or young person's EHC Plan a local authority must—

- (a) take into account the evidence received when securing the EHC needs assessment; and
- (b) consider how best to achieve the outcomes to be sought for the child or young person.
