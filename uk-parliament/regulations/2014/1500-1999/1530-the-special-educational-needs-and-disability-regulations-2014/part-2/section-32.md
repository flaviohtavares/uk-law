# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 32

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Information to be included in notices sent by a local authority

*Extent: E+W*

Where a notice sent by a local authority must include the information set out in this regulation, that information is—

- (a) the right of the child's parent or young person to request mediation under section 53 or 54 of the Act;
- (b) the requirement to obtain a certificate in accordance with section 55(4) or (5) (“a mediation certificate”) before any appeal can be made to the First-tier Tribunal [^c22119171];
- (c) contact details for the mediation adviser that the child's parent or young person should contact to obtain that certificate;
- (d) the timescales for requesting mediation;
- (e) the requirement to inform the local authority—
  - (i) if the parent or young person wishes to pursue mediation,
  - (ii) the mediation issues [^c22119181], and
  - (iii) where the mediation issues are or include the fact that no health care provision, or no health care provision, of a particular kind is specified in the EHC plan, the health care provision that the child's parent or young person wishes to be specified in the EHC plan;
- (f) contact details for any person acting on behalf of the local authority whom the child's parent or young person should contact if they wish to pursue mediation.

## Annotations

[^c22119171]: Marginal citation: The First-tier Tribunal was established under section 3 of the [Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15)

[^c22119181]: Marginal citation: Mediation issues has the same meaning as in section 53(1)(b) of the Act
