# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 58

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Types of special post-16 institution that may be approved

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may approve a special post-16 institution under section 41 of the Act where it is not—

- (a) an institution within the further education sector;
- (b) a 16 to19 Academy [^c22119261];
- (c) maintained by a local authority.

## Annotations

[^c22119261]: Marginal citation: 16 to19 Academy has the same meaning as section 1B of the Academies Act 2010. Section 1B was inserted by section 53(7) of the 2011 Act
