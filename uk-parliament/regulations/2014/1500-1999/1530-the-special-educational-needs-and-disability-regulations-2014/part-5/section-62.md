# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 62

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Publication of list of approved institutions

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State must publish a list of all institutions that have been approved, and have not had that approval withdrawn, on the Internet.
