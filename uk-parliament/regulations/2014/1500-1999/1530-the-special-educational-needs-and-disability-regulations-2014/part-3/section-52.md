# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 52

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Manner of publication of report

*Extent: E+W*

The governing body or proprietor of the school must publish on the school's website its report containing SEN information.
