# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 57

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Manner of publication

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must—

- (a) publish its local offer by placing it on their website;
- (b) publish its arrangements for enabling—
  - (i) people without access to the Internet; and
  - (ii) different groups, including people with special educational needs or a disability,

  to obtain a copy of the offer; and
- (c) publish how those people identified in paragraph (b) can provide comments on the local offer.
