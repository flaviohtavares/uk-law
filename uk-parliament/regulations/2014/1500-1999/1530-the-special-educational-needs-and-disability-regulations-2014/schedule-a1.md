## SCHEDULE A1 — Providers of the National Professional Qualification for Special Educational Needs Co-ordinators[^key-cfc016e03cb3b9dc9a82f99f073213a0]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 49(9)

**1.** The providers of the National Professional Qualification for Special Educational Needs Co-ordinators are—

- (a) Ambition Institute (registered charity number 1146924, company number 07984030);
- (b) Best Practice Network Ltd (company number 04472661);
- (c) the National Society (Church of England and Church in Wales) for the Promotion of Education (registered charity number 313070);
- (d) the School-Led Development Trust (registered charity number 1202423, company number 13429740);
- (e) Teach First (registered charity number 1098294, company number 4478840);
- (f) UCL Consultants Limited (company number 03332258).
