# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, section 65

> Text as at 2024-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Mental Capacity Act 2005

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 63 and 64 have effect in spite of section 27(1)(g) of the Mental Capacity Act 2005 [^c22119291].

## Annotations

[^c22119291]: Marginal citation: [2005 c.9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9) Section 27(1)(g) does not permit decisions on discharging parental responsibilities in matters not relating to a child's property to be made on a person's behalf.
