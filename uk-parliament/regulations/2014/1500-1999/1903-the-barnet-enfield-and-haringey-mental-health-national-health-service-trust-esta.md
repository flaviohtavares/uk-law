# The Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1903

National Health Service, England

The Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2014

Made

17th July 2014

Coming into force

1st August 2014

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and 273(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

In accordance with section 25(3) of that Act, the prescribed consultation has been completed[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st August 2014.

(2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of article 4 of the Establishment Order

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust), for “5 non-executive directors” substitute “7 non-executive directors”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health

17th July 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001, which established the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 5 to 7.
