# The Petroleum Licensing (Exploration and Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 2014, section 1A

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Application[^c-9fc0d0ec8b3d99ede1ccc04ce5c64019]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except for regulations 1(2) and 2(1A) and Schedules 2A and 3A, these Regulations do not apply in relation to a landward petroleum exploration licence or a petroleum exploration and development licence in respect of an area within the Scottish onshore area.

(1A)[^c-565771041a5ffd5ed6edb63147996249] Except for regulations 1(2) and 2(1A) and Schedules 2B and 3B, these Regulations do not apply in relation to a landward petroleum exploration licence or a petroleum exploration and development licence in respect of an area within the Welsh onshore area.

(2) In this regulation, “**Scottish onshore area**” has the meaning given in section 8A(3) of the Petroleum Act 1998 and “Welsh onshore area” has the meaning given in section 8A(5) of that Act[^c-669890da01c516baa627b3cd34d8465a].
