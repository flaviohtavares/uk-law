# The Petroleum Licensing (Exploration and Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-f874b9b9a7563d5d4961185c367dbfd7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Petroleum Licensing (Exploration and Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 17th July 2014.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “[^key-ac4c2d2b9b95b727e027cdae1830eabd]**landward area**” means an area of Great Britain that is within the baselines established by any Order in Council under section 1(1)(b) of the Territorial Sea Act 1987 (extension of territorial sea);
- “landward petroleum exploration licence” means a licence to search for petroleum in a landward area; and
- “petroleum exploration and development licence” means a licence to search and bore for, and get, petroleum in a landward area.
