# The Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment No. 2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2013/2302](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2302) and is being issued free of charge to all known purchasers of that Statutory Instrument and of [S.I. 2014/513](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/513) which was also affected by the defect.

The Lord Chancellor, with the consent and sanction of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001], and sections 414 and 415 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00002].

In accordance with section 92(5) and (6) of the Courts Act 2003 the Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice and the Civil Justice Council.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment No.2) Order 2014 and comes into force on 4th August 2014.

## 2. Amendments of the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008

The Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008[^f00003] is amended as follows:

**3.** In Schedule 1 (fees to be taken)—

- (a) at fee 3.5, in column 2 (amount of fee), substitute £280 for £160;
- (b) at fee 8.1(a), in column 2 (amount of fee), substitute £70 for £100.

**4.** In Schedule 2 (remissions and part remissions), in sub-paragraph (c) of the definition of “excluded benefits”—

- (a) after “2002,” omit “or”;
- (b) after “section 12B(1) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968”, insert “or the Social Care (Self-directed Support) (Scotland) Act 2013[^f00004]”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the [Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008 (S.I 2008/1053)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1053).

Fee 3.5 (applications under the Companies Act [1985 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6), the Companies Act [2006 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46) and Insolvency Act [1986 (c.45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/45)) has been increased from £160 to £280.

Fee 8.1 (a) (County Court enforcement in cases other than County Court Business Centre cases) has been reduced from £100 to £70.

In Schedule 2, the definition of benefits that are excluded when determining whether to grant a fee remission has been corrected to include benefits based upon the Social Care (Self-directed Support) (Scotland) Act [2013 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2013/1).
