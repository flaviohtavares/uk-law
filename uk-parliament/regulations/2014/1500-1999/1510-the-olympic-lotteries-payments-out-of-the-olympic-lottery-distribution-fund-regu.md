# The Olympic Lotteries (Payments out of the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund) Regulations 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1510

Betting, Gaming And Lotteries

The Olympic Lotteries (Payments out of the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund) Regulations 2014

Made

5th June 2014

Laid before Parliament

10th June 2014

Coming into force

22nd July 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 26(2)(c) and (4)(a) and 36(2)(a) of the Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Olympic Lotteries (Payments out of the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund) Regulations 2014 and shall come into force on 22nd July 2014.

## 2. Payment into the National Lottery Distribution Fund

(1) The Secretary of State may make a payment of £79,000,000 from the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund into the National Lottery Distribution Fund on or after 22nd July 2014.

(2) Any payment made under paragraph (1) is to be treated as having been paid into the National Lottery Distribution Fund by virtue of section 5(6)[^f00002] of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00003] (general payments into fund).

Sajid Javid

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

5th June 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations permit the Secretary of State to make a payment of £79,000,000 from the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund into the National Lottery Distribution Fund, on or after 22nd July 2014.

Article 2(1) allows the payment. Article 2(2) specifies that the payment is to be treated as having been paid into the National Lottery Distribution Fund by virtue of section 5(6) of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993. This results in the payment being apportioned within the National Lottery Distribution Fund as provided in section 22 of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared and is available alongside this instrument on the website, www.legislation.gov.uk.
