# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Evidence packs

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 33A, a[^key-5d7d8aa0e147bb90564a30039b214469] responsible undertaking must maintain a written record in relation to each ESOS assessment carried out by it (the “evidence pack”) which includes—

- (a) records of any data used for the purposes of—
  - (i) the calculation of total energy consumption under Chapter 2 of this Part,
  - (ii) the identification of areas of significant energy consumption under regulation 25,
  - (iii) the energy audit under Chapter 3 of this Part, including in particular the identification of energy saving opportunities under regulation 27,
  - (iv)[^key-c664d5568111fb627a7c518e6410e9bc] calculations related to organisational purposes under Chapter 2A of this Part,
  - (v) conversion of total energy consumption or significant energy consumption into kWh units under regulation 25A,
  - (vi) the preparation of any ESOS report under regulation 27A,
  - (vii) the calculation of estimated energy savings since the previous compliance date under regulation 27D,
  - (viii) any ESOS action plan under regulation 34A,
  - (ix) any ESOS progress update under regulation 34B,
- (b) evidence of the certification of any certified energy management system, [^key-291de8a20d2c68ef1834924c2242848f]..., relied on by the participant in accordance with regulation 33(2) or (3)[^key-64b8b3f14387a96e5ba3837d67b21976],
- (c) any agreement made in accordance with regulation 19(3), or paragraph 2, 4, 6, 7 , 7A, 7B[^key-b0464a1c92f0d5868788ed33cd8c6a3d] or 11 of Schedule 2,
- (d) the notification given by the lead assessor under regulation 21(2)(b), [^key-f57231bba61a8c3f26e516ac0c62399b]...
- (e) any information recorded in accordance with regulation 21A(1), 27(6)(b), 34A(5)(b) or 34B(5)(b)[^key-d47014de51d38c7510c9abfa4fd711b9],
- (f)[^key-58062571c1f9f3277562de6c18a69242] any ESOS report,
- (g) the information notified to the scheme administrator in accordance with regulation 29,
- (h) any ESOS action plan, [^key-c43d06df797404dc1426ad74a4638822]...
- (i) any ESOS progress update,
- (j)[^key-6ba6a0e90d6d8d264739987e6a96f225] details of the methods and data used for the purposes of making any estimate in accordance with regulation 21A(1), and
- (k) any ESOS action plan review.

(2)[^key-3179ac0d0b6f58df6580c450f2edd8aa] The evidence pack must be kept—

- (a) in relation to the initial compliance period, until at least 5th June 2024, and
- (b) in relation to any other compliance period, for at least two subsequent compliance periods following the compliance period to which it relates.
