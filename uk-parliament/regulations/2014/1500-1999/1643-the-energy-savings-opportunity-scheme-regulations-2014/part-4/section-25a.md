# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 25A

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25A. Conversion into kWh units[^key-5f860ceadec4c4dace2d92c6512f1e93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a participant’s total energy consumption, or, if applicable, significant energy consumption, as calculated in accordance with this Chapter, is not measured in kWh, the responsible undertaking must convert the participant’s total energy consumption, or significant energy consumption into kWh.

(2) The conversion under paragraph (1) must—

- (a) where reasonably practicable, be based on verifiable data, or
- (b) otherwise, be based on a reasonable estimate of the amount of energy consumed in kWh.
