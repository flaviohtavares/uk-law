# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Provision of information to the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme administrator must provide to the Secretary of State any information held by it by virtue of these Regulations as is requested.
