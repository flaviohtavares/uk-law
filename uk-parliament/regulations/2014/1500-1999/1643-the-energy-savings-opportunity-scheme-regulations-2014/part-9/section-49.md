# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 49

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Effect of an appeal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The bringing of an appeal suspends the determination, enforcement notice or penalty notice (as the case may be) being appealed taking effect pending determination of the appeal.
