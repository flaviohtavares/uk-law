# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 51

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Service of documents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any determination or notice required to be served on a responsible undertaking, may be served by—

- (a) delivering or sending it to, or leaving it at—
  - (i) the responsible undertaking's registered office (where applicable),
  - (ii) the responsible undertaking's principal place of activity, or
  - (iii) another address in the United Kingdom specified by the responsible undertaking as its address for service, or
- (b) sending it by electronic means to the email address provided by the responsible undertaking pursuant to regulation 29(1)(a) and the second row of Table A in[^key-0762c3eb76b3d4e3c5e68d4c94f5f0f6] Schedule 3.
