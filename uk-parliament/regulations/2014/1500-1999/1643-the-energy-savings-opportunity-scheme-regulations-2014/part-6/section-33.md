# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 33

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Compliance with ISO 50001

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in any case where a participant's energy management system is certified, within the relevant period[^key-3b798419836d1657c15a0ea867ff0aa1], as being in compliance with ISO 50001 (a “certified energy management system”), and that certification remains valid on the compliance date.

(2) Except where paragraph (3) applies, the[^M_F_de104d81-779a-4e3a-9e7e-8cf73f33a053] participant is deemed to have complied with Chapter 3 of Part 4 in relation to any part of[^M_F_63705b2d-8de0-4c65-c1af-8eb81ce99087] its energy consumption which falls under the certified energy management system.

(2A)[^key-361c4dc6f3ed499088ffa92f5e4d70cc] Paragraph 3 applies where all of the participant’s—

- (a) total energy consumption, or
- (b) significant energy consumption

falls under the certified energy management system.

(3) Where the conditions in paragraph (2A) are met[^key-373883855826aaf076e1d6349313f7c1], the participant is deemed to have complied with

- (a)[^key-1fb342c136d65581a5ee3cc7d7d52cb3][^key-072f5269a0f262e13e710b8e109b5fba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) regulation 21, and
- (c) Chapters 3 and 3A[^key-214946166f47fe1ce8a334ec8624ad85] of Part 4.

(4) In this regulation—

- (a) “**certified**” means certified by a body that is accredited, for the purpose of certifying compliance with ISO 50001, by at least one of the following—
  - (i) a member of the International Accreditation Forum,
  - (ii) a national accreditation body of a Member State[^key-6fa64989dc473453f55aab5c7f23d71a],
  - (iii)[^key-524bc6b49214cf26367b107396723c3d] the national accreditation body of the United Kingdom appointed in accordance with Article 4(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/765) of the European Parliament and of the Council,
- (b) “**energy management system**” has the meaning given in Article 2(11) of the Directive,
- (c) “**ISO 50001**” means the international standard “*50001:2011 Energy management systems – Requirements with guidance for use*”[^c22147191] or the international standard “[^key-b3452c5b643bf3a4a7cf1c3ec70cf0b4]*50001:2018 Energy management systems – Requirements with guidance for use*”, [^key-f9931b0c22490e0c64832bb61bf1fe03]...
- (d) “**national accreditation body of a Member State[^key-eb457b35ee305908b5720fbeb7f1a11d]** ” means a national accreditation body of a Member State within the meaning of Article 2(11) of Regulation (EC) no 765/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^c22147201] as it has effect in EU law[^key-d60faf23fa4a0e34bde262f7adcace15],
- (e)[^key-d5665583435fed135a907011a0957489] “**relevant period**” means—
  - (i) in the case of the compliance period beginning on 6th December 2019, the period beginning on that date and ending on the compliance date,
  - (ii) in any other case, the compliance period.
