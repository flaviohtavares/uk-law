# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Other information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A responsible undertaking must notify the scheme administrator if it becomes aware that it is in breach of any requirement of these Regulations.

(2) A compliance body may take into account any information—

- (a) provided to it in accordance with paragraph (1), or
- (b) held by it, or provided to it, by virtue of any other provision in these Regulations or any other legislative provision,

in determining whether a responsible undertaking has complied with these Regulations.
