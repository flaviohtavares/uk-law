> Text as at 2026-07-22.

## 29. Notification of compliance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-390eb81b16e7f19153c56b0d8dfdae48] A responsible undertaking must notify the scheme administrator using the Notification System whether the participant has complied with Part 4, (or, as the case may be, Part 6) in relation to a compliance period by providing—

- (a)[^key-4f6614f032a8bca41b16218992c4e8f4] where the responsible undertaking has conducted an energy audit under Chapter 3 of Part 4, the information specified in column 2 of tables A, C and E, F, G , H and J[^key-3ffaf0fd3c76bcc569d268a50aeffc19] in Schedule 3,
- (aa) where the participant is deemed to have complied with Chapter 3 of Part 4 by virtue of regulation 33(2)[^key-897a3406d3413b5cba5b17f71916f58b]—
  - (i) to the extent not already provided under sub-paragraph (a), the information specified in column 2 of tables A, C, E, F, G and J[^key-381764c079ee4c232f90f79078783339] in Schedule 3,
  - (ii)[^key-a2f33192e937d55d7bf5ef56b0ac0800] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii) the information specified in column 2 of Table I in Schedule 3,
- (ab) where the responsible undertaking is one of two or more relevant undertakings complying with the Scheme as one participant, the information specified in column 2 of Table B in Schedule 3,
- (ac) where a lead assessor is required to be appointed under regulation 21, the information specified in column 2 of Table D in Schedule 3, [^key-f3b553c7b2c70c89fcac92e6e9782219]...
- (ad)[^key-68f970ae470f244b79e25723d3c36d02] where the participant is deemed, by virtue of regulation 33(3), to have complied with regulations 21 and 26 and Chapters 3 and 3A of Part 4, the information specified in column 2 of Tables A, C, F, G, J and K in Schedule 3,
- (ae) where the participant is deemed, by virtue of regulation 33A, to have complied with regulations 20, 21, 21A(2)(b) and Chapters 2A to 4 of Part 4, the information specified in column 2 of Tables A and C in Schedule 3,
- (af) where the participant is one to which regulation 17(1)(b) applies, the information specified in column 2 of Table B in Schedule 3, and
- (b) the confirmation required by regulation 31

after the qualification date, and by no later than the compliance date, for that compliance period.
