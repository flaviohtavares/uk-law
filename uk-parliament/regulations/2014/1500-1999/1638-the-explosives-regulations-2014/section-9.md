# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**9.** As under the 1991 Regulations, there is provision made (in regulation 32) for restrictions on the employment of a “prohibited person” where the employee would handle or have control of a “relevant explosive” or any “restricted substance”. These terms are defined in regulation 2(1), with an updated definition of “prohibited person”.
