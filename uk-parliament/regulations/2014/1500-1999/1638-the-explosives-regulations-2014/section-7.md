# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 7

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**7.** The definition of “explosive” and related terms, such as “relevant explosive”, are included in regulation 2(1). The document called the “United Nations Recommendations” referred to in the definition of “explosive”, and the Manual of Tests and Criteria, fifth revised edition, which supports those Recommendations, can be downloaded free from the website of the United Nations at www.unece.org/trans/danger/publi/dg\_publications.html.
