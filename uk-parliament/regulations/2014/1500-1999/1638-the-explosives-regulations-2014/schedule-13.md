# The Explosives Regulations 2014, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2024-10-01.

## SCHEDULE 13 — AMENDMENTS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 48(1)

### PART 1 — AMENDMENTS TO PRIMARY LEGISLATION

#### 1. Explosives Act 1875

(1) The Explosives Act 1875 [^c22151111] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit sections 23 and 61.

(3) In section 74 (seizure and detention of explosives liable to forfeiture) —

- (a) in the opening words, omit “any inspector appointed by the Health and Safety Executive under section 19 of the 1974 Act, or”;
- (b) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) for the words from “he is an inspector” to “a justice”, substitute “ he is authorised by an order from a justice ”; and
  - (ii) omit “or of an inspector appointed by the Health and Safety Executive under section 19 of the 1974 Act,”; and
- (c) after subsection (6), omit the words from “In this section” to the end of the section.

#### 2. London Building Act 1930

In section 143 of the London Building Act 1930 [^c22151121] (regulations for building near dangerous business), in subsection (4)(a), for “the Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005” substitute “ the Explosives Regulations 2014 ”.

#### 3. Fireworks Act 1951

The Fireworks Act 1951 [^c22151131] is repealed.

#### 4. Customs and Excise Management Act 1979

In section 75(1) of the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 [^c22151141] (explosives), for “the Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005” substitute “ the Explosives Regulations 2014 ”.

#### 5. Isle of Man Act 1979

In section 8(2) of the Isle of Man Act 1979 [^c22151151] (removal of goods from Isle of Man to United Kingdom)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (b) insert “ or ”; and
- (b) for paragraphs (c) and (d) substitute —

  > - (c) any explosives the importation of which into the United Kingdom is prohibited by regulation 29 of the Explosives Regulations 2014.

  .

#### 6. Environmental Protection Act 1990

In section 142(7) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 [^c22151161] (powers to obtain information about potentially hazardous substances), for “the Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005” substitute “ the Explosives Regulations 2014 ”.

#### 7. Fireworks Act 2003

In section 14(2) of the Fireworks Act 2003 [^c22151171] (prohibition of supply etc. of other explosives), for “the Explosives Act 1875 (c. 17)” substitute “ the Explosives Regulations 2014 ”.

#### 8. Energy Act 2013

(1) The Energy Act 2013 [^c22151181] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 12 (minor and consequential amendments relating to Part 3), omit paragraphs 50 to 52.
