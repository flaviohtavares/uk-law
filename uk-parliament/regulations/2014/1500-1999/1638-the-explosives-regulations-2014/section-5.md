# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 5

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

**5.** [^c22151401]The provisions in regulation 38 and Schedule 8 that implement the Montreal Convention were notified in draft to the European Commission under the provisions of the Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations .
