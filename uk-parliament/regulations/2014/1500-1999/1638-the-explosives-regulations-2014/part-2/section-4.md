# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 4

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Authorisation to acquire or acquire and keep explosives

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An explosives certificate is a certificate certifying that the person to whom it is issued is a fit person—

- (a) to acquire explosives; or
- (b) to acquire and keep explosives,

in accordance with the terms of the explosives certificate.

(2) An explosives certificate must be in a form approved for the time being for the purposes of this regulation by the Executive.
