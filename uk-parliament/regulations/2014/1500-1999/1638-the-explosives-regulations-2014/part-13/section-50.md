# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 50

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Information identifying importer[^c-56624b264767c0fec16781cfb5f3d4ef][^c-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before placing a civil explosive on the market, an importer must indicate on the civil explosive—

- (a) any of—
  - (i) the name;
  - (ii) registered trade name; or
  - (iii) registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(1A)[^c-f1211f430fa081cf539d4e378542644f] Paragraph (1) does not apply where the importer has imported the civil explosive from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-b005bfc475c4a714c7160923c6aaefce] beginning with IP completion day, and before placing the civil explosive on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1) in a document accompanying the civil explosive.

(2) The information specified in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and a relevant authority[^c-78a7870f81c8a633d807b3930e569931].

(3) Where it is not possible to indicate the information specified in paragraph (1) on the civil explosive, the importer must indicate that information—

- (a) on the packaging; or
- (b) in a document accompanying the civil explosive.
