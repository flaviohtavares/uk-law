# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 52

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^key-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06][^key-52dad97fc7c464a24c1b4b9c0b16de59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the civil explosive is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the [^key-3ec72a25c4bf71cdb52a397520a46a5c]... declaration of conformity at the disposal of the market surveillance authority; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation can be made available to that authority, upon request.
