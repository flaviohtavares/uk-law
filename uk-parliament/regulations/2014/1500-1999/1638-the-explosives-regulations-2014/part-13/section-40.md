# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 40

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Technical documentation and conformity assessment[^key-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before placing a civil explosive on the market, or using it for their own purposes, a manufacturer must, in respect of that civil explosive—

- (a) have a relevant conformity assessment procedure carried out; and
- (b) draw up the technical documentation referred to —
  - (i) for a civil explosive in respect of which the conformity assessment procedure in regulation 66(2)(a)[^key-a007cbd3573cc2abe06c0996e0a17e2b] is being carried out, in paragraph 2(2)(c) of Part 1 (Module B) of Schedule 17[^key-14013f5af0ba724aedecbbee7af43f6e];
  - (ii) for a civil explosive in respect of which the conformity assessment procedure in regulation 66(2)(b)[^key-d6673593a048fd6834579d3656d5e177] is being carried out, in paragraph 46 of Part 6 (Module G) of Schedule 17[^key-5354efe176f199c40c3cc8ef90e497aa].
