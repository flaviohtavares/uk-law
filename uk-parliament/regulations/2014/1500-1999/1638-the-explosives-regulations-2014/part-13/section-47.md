# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 47

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Prohibition on placing on the market civil explosives which are not in conformity[^c-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must not place a civil explosive on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential safety requirements.
