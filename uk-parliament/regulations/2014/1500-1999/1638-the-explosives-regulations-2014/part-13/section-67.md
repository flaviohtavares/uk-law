# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Declaration of conformity[^c-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06][^c-27c6085d368b1eb5346c3fbecf95968b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The [^c-245e789e8e6485c6b444eca948b1ee7b]... declaration of conformity for a civil explosive must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the civil explosive;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Schedule 17,[^c-a72dda777d7e51c0764d3e3c39b398a0] for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the civil explosive; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 18.[^c-8d2f52f4634aac7d2160499e157cfd45]
