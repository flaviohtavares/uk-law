# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 65

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Presumption of conformity[^key-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A civil explosive which is in conformity with a designated[^key-993f6289b00be7b8d407f70fe3a9b1dd] standard, or part of such a standard, [^key-e16f42f68bf2ea69394c3025ef9daf6b]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential safety requirements covered by that standard, or that part of that standard.

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
