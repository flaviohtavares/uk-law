# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 58

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Duty to take action in respect of civil explosives made available on the market which are not in conformity[^c-858488a89cb65a91a8398020468dfb06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A distributor who considers, or has reason to believe, that a civil explosive which the distributor has made available on the market is not in conformity with this Part must make sure that the necessary corrective measures are taken to—

- (a) bring that civil explosive into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the civil explosive; or
- (c) recall the civil explosive.

(2) Where the civil explosive presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority[^c-267175369a0f08844eb43a18822925a1]... of that risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the civil explosive is considered not to be in conformity with this Part; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
