# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 2

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**2.** The Regulations implement as regards Great Britain, Council Directive [93/15/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0015) on the harmonization of the provisions relating to the placing on the market and supervision of explosives for civil uses (OJ No. L121, 15.5.93, p.20) as amended by Regulation [(EC) No. 1882/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1882) of the European Parliament and the Council (OJ No. L 284, 31.10.2003, p.1), Regulation [(EC) No. 219/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0219) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ No. L 87, 31.3.2009, p.109) and Regulation (EU) No. 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ No. L 316, 14.11. 2012, p. 12). Regulations 8 and 39 to 42, 43 and 44 in part, and 45 are for implementing these European provisions.
