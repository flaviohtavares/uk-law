# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 80

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Enforcement in relation to regulation 8 and Part 13, market surveillance and further matters[^c-4a1fbedd8db826ce0a12a9ef062bd2d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 12, which makes provision as to—

- (a) enforcement in relation to regulation 8 and Part 13;
- (b) market surveillance in relation to that Part;
- (c) compliance, withdrawal and recall notices; and
- (d) a defence of due diligence, appeals against notices and further provisions in relation to enforcement;

has effect.
