# The Explosives Regulations 2014, section 3

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**3.** The Regulations implement, as regards Great Britain, Commission Directive [2008/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0043) setting up, pursuant to Council Directive [93/15/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0015), a system for the identification and traceability of explosives for civil uses (OJ No. L 94, 5.4.2008, p.8) (“**the 2008 Directive**”) as amended by Commission Directive 2012/4/EU (OJ No. L 50, 23.2.12, p.18). Regulations 33, 34, 36 and 43 and 44 in part, are for implementing these European provisions.
