# The Rampion Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2025-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Deemed marine licences under the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^c-6899c5fead2eacd2873e7522f7978242]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The deemed array marine licence and the deemed export cables marine licence are deemed to be granted to the undertaker under Part 4 of Chapter 1 of the 2009 Act, subject to the conditions set out in Part 2 of Schedule 13 (deemed licence under Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 – array) and Part 2 of Schedule 14 (deemed licence under Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 – export cables) respectively.
