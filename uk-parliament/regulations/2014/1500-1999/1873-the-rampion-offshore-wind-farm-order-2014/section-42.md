# The Rampion Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 42

> Text as at 2025-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Arbitration[^key-3baceeb4230b77f21d47b4c47936005d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any difference or dispute under any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for, shall be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties, or failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
