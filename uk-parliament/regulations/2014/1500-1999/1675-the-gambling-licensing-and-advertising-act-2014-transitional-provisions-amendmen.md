# The Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2014/1641](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1641) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2014 No. 1675

Betting, Gaming And Lotteries

Made

26th June 2014

Laid before Parliament

27th June 2014

Coming into force

16th July 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(4), (6) and (7) of the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014[^f00001].

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2014 and it comes into force on 16th July 2014.

## 2. Amendment to the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (Transitional Provisions) Order 2014

In article 2(1)(d) of the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (Transitional Provisions) Order 2014[^f00002], after “Gibraltar,” insert “the Isle of Man,”.

Helen Grant

Minister for Sport and Equalities

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

26th June 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the definition of “relevant state” in article 2(1)(d) of the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (Transitional Provisions) Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/1641](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1641)) (“the 2014 Order”) so as to include a reference to the Isle of Man which was omitted in error.

The 2014 Order provides for the transitional arrangements that will apply in respect of advance applications made under the Gambling Act 2005 for, or to vary, a remote operating licence in anticipation of the coming into force of the new licensing requirement in subsections 1(1)( and (2) of the Gambling (Licensing and Advertising) Act 2014 (“the 2014 Act”).

A full impact assessment of the effect that the 2014 Act and 2014 Order will have on the costs of business is available from the Department for Culture, Media and Sport, 100 Parliament Street, London SW1A 2BQ. This Order does not affect the costs as set out in that impact assessment.
