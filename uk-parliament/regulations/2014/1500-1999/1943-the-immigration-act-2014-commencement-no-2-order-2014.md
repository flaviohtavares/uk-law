# The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1943 (C. 89)

Immigration

The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

Made

22nd July 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75(3) of the Immigration Act 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014.

## 2. Commencement of provision

The date appointed for the coming into force of section 40 of the Immigration Act 2014 (prohibition on opening current accounts for disqualified persons) is 12th December 2014.

James Brokenshire

Minister of State

Home Office

22nd July 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the second commencement order made under the Immigration Act 2014 (“the Act”). It brings into force section 40 of the Act on 12th December 2014.
