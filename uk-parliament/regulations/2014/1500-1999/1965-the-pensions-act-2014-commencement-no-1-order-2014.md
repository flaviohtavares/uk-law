# The Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1965 (C. 92)

Public Service Pensions

The Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014

Made

22nd July 2014

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 56(6) of the Pensions Act 2014[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 23rd July 2014

23rd July 2014 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 52 of the Pensions Act 2014 (public service pension schemes: transitional arrangements) for all purposes.

Signed

David Evennett

John Penrose

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

22nd July 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 52 of the Pensions Act [2014 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/19) on 23rd July 2014. That section amends section 18 of the Public Service Pensions Act [2013 (c.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/25) which relates to restriction of existing pension schemes and transitional arrangements. This is the first commencement order to be made under the Pensions Act 2014.

The Order will have no effect on the costs of business or the voluntary sector. For that reason no impact assessment has been produced.
