> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27I. Disposal of pig products that have been traced in England[^key-2db1384982de3a661bd5c58a54bf0b23]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State must—

- (a) dispose of any pig products in England which have been traced in accordance with this Part; or
- (b) require their disposal by notice given to the person in possession of the pig products.
