> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21A. Removal of feral pig control zones in England[^key-9ca006619d9e504bf79333386a5d837d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State must keep in place a feral pig control zone until—

- (a) a restricted zone 2 is declared covering all or part of the area covered by the feral pig control zone; or
- (b) the Secretary of State considers, on the basis of a risk assessment, it is appropriate for the zone to be removed.

(2) Where sub-paragraph (a) or (b) of paragraph (1) applies, the Secretary of State must revoke the zone.
