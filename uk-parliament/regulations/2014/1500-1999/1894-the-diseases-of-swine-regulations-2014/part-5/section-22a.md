# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 22A

> Text as at 2026-04-29.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22A. Emergency vaccination of feral pigs in England against classical swine fever and African swine fever[^c-bc20a8601555a8cea5cd4593a6e1c953]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Chief Veterinary Officer concludes that classical swine fever or African swine fever is present in a feral pig in England, and the epidemiological data available indicates there is a risk of the spread of that disease, the Secretary of State may—

- (a) introduce emergency vaccination of feral pigs in a specified area; and
- (b) impose such conditions and restrictions in the specified area as the Secretary of State considers are necessary in order to improve the effectiveness of the vaccination campaign.

(2) An occupier must comply with any conditions or restrictions imposed under paragraph (1)(b).
