# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 27D

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27D. Area controls following confirmation of swine vesicular disease in pigs in England[^key-2db1384982de3a661bd5c58a54bf0b23]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Following confirmation that swine vesicular disease is present in a pig in England, the Secretary of State may, following a risk assessment, decide—

- (a) not to declare any zone under these Regulations;
- (b) to declare a protection zone only or a surveillance zone only;
- (c) to declare a protection zone or a surveillance zone smaller in size than required by regulation 23(4);
- (d) to declare a protection zone or a surveillance zone shorter in duration than required by regulation 25A;
- (e) to disapply some or all of the measures in any or all of the following—
  - (i) Schedule 1A;
  - (ii) Schedule 3A;
  - (iii) Schedule 4A.
