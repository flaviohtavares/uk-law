> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Powers of inspectors in case of default[^c-019d11b27f0364a91f53ea2f44dc31e3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

If any person fails to comply with a requirement in or under these Regulations, an inspector may take such steps as that inspector considers necessary, including seizure of things, to ensure the requirement is met at the expense of that person.
