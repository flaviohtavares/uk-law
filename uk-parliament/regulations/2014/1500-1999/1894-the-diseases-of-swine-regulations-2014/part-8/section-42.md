# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 42

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Exceptional circumstances[^key-131215b95c9d44397c941844a37df471]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A veterinary inspector may for the purpose of ensuring the health or welfare of any pig in exceptional circumstances—

- (a) license a person to carry out any action that is otherwise prohibited under these Regulations; or
- (b) exempt a person, by notice, from any requirement under these Regulations.
