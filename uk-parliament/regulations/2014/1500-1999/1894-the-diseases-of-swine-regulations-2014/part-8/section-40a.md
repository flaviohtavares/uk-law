> Text as at 2026-04-29.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40A. Disapplication of measures in England to those executing these Regulations[^c-54eb4f643f3a3c388a9ea4b7cc5876ea]

*Extent: E+W*

Any requirement or prohibition under these Regulations restricting the movement of any person, or the use of any thing, in England does not apply to any of the following in their execution of the Regulations—

- (a) the Secretary of State;
- (b) a local authority;
- (c) the police;
- (d) a person authorised by the Secretary of State or the local authority to execute these Regulations.
