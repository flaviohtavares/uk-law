# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 24A

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24A. Additional measures etc. applicable in England[^key-e35965bf330fcb3fe80b8b2037e76a26]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may apply, in England, any other measure the Secretary of State considers necessary to reduce the spread of disease in respect of the whole or any part of any zone declared by the Secretary of State under these Regulations.
