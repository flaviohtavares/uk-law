# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Premises straddling a protection or a surveillance zone[^key-9f7f224af9e8e0d2396e95b3fd1a8fdb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Premises which are partly inside a protection zone and partly inside a surveillance zone must be deemed to be wholly inside the protection zone.

(2) Premises which are partly inside and partly outside the outer boundary of a surveillance zone must be deemed to be wholly inside that zone.
