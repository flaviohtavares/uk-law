# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 2

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-7bc6cabd422998cf08ea2040fed89093]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “carcase” , other than in England, *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-63da880412b967168cf8e02d795075d9] means a carcase or part of a carcase of a pig, but does not include a sample taken from a carcase;
- “Chief Veterinary Officer” means the Chief Veterinary Officer for England, Scotland or Wales dependent upon where disease is present;
- “contact premises” means premises that are designated as contact premises under regulation 8(1);
- “Council [Directive 92/119/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0119)” means Council [Directive 92/119/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0119) introducing general Community measures for the control of certain animal diseases and specific measures relating to swine vesicular disease as amended from time to time;
- “Council [Directive 2001/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0089)” means Council [Directive 2001/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0089) on Community measures for the control of classical swine fever as amended from time to time;
- “Council [Directive 2002/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0060)” means Council [Directive 2002/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0060) laying down specific provisions for the control of African swine fever as amended from time to time;
- “designated slaughterhouse” means a slaughterhouse designated by the appropriate authority under the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (England) Regulations 2008[^f00007], the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Scotland) Order 2008[^f00008] or the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00009];
- “Diagnostic Manual” refers to the Diagnostic Manual contained in—
  - (a) Commission [Decision 2000/428/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0428) establishing diagnostic procedures, sampling methods and criteria for the evaluation of the results of laboratory tests for the confirmation and differential diagnosis of swine vesicular disease[^f00010],
  - (b) Commission [Decision 2002/106/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0106) approving a Diagnostic Manual establishing diagnostic procedures, sampling methods and criteria for evaluation of the laboratory tests for the confirmation of classical swine fever[^f00011],
  - (c) Commission [Decision 2003/422/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0422) approving an African swine fever Diagnostic Manual[^f00012];
- “disease” in these Regulations, unless the context refers to only one or more of these diseases, means any of the following diseases of pig (and includes the virus of any such disease)—
  - (a) African swine fever;
  - (b) classical swine fever (otherwise known as hog cholera); and
  - (c) swine vesicular disease;
- “equipment”, except in regulation 34(9)(a), means any equipment that has been in contact with pigs or faeces, blood or other bodily fluids from a pig;
- “feral pig” means any non-captive pig which is living in the wild;
- “infected premises” means premises that have been designated as infected premises under regulation 10 or regulation 10A *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-ef98afc487d9ac044ee63fc03f52ecc1];
- “inspector” means a person appointed to be an inspector for the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00013] by an appropriate authority or by a local authority, and, when used in relation to an officer of the appropriate authority, includes a veterinary inspector;
- “keeper” means any person who owns or is responsible for a pig, whether on a permanent or temporary basis;
- “ *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-a7f842e4444c012ffa014cdc3e4038c1]**licensing officer *(Extent: E+W)***” means— *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (a) a veterinary inspector; or *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (b) an inspector or officer of the Secretary of State acting under the instructions of a veterinary inspector; *(Extent: E+W)*
- “livestock” means any hoofed animals other than equidae;
- “local authority” means—
  - (a) in England—
    - (i) where there is an authority which is the sole principal council for its local government area (“a unitary authority”), that authority;
    - (ii) where there is not a unitary authority—
      - (aa) [^c-96a0cae28ba93e92b734ad2273fdfd4e]...
      - (bb) in a non-metropolitan county, the county council;
      - (cc) in each London borough, the council of that borough; or
      - (dd) in the City of London, the Common Council;
  - (b) in Wales, a county council or a county borough council;
  - (c) in Scotland, a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00014];
- “marker vaccine” means a vaccine which results in a clear differentiation between a vaccinated pig and a diseased pig;
- “occupier” means any person having day-to-day responsibility for the premises or for any pigs on the premises;
- “pig” means any animal of the *Suidae* family;
- “premises” includes any land, building or other place;
- “ *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-bc40982ccf4786a55fb097a32d552c06]**Restricted Zone *(Extent: E+W)***” means a restricted zone 1, a restricted zone 2 or a restricted zone 3; *(Extent: E+W)*
- “ *(Extent: E+W)***restricted zone 1 *(Extent: E+W)***” means a restricted zone 1 declared under regulation 27A; *(Extent: E+W)*
- “ *(Extent: E+W)***restricted zone 2 *(Extent: E+W)***” means a restricted zone 2 declared under regulation 27A; *(Extent: E+W)*
- “ *(Extent: E+W)***restricted zone 3 *(Extent: E+W)***” means a restricted zone 3 declared under regulation 27A; *(Extent: E+W)*
- “sentinel pig” means a pig introduced to infected premises after cleansing and disinfection in accordance with regulation 18 for surveillance purposes;
- “seropositive pig” means a pig identified as having antibodies against swine vesicular disease, African swine fever or classical swine fever;
- “suspect premises” means premises that are designated as suspect premises under regulation 7(2);
- “vectors” means ticks of the species *Ornithodorus erraticus* or any other tick or insect species specified by the appropriate authority;
- “veterinary inspector” means a veterinary inspector appointed by an appropriate authority for the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981.

(2) In these Regulations—

- (a) “appropriate authority” means—
  - (i) the Secretary of State where the functions are exercised in England;
  - (ii) the Scottish Ministers where the functions are exercised in Scotland; and
  - (iii) the Welsh Ministers where the functions are exercised in Wales;
- (b) terms used in the European Union instruments have the same meaning as in those instruments.

(3) In paragraph (2)(b) “the European Union instruments” means—

- (a) Council [Directive 92/119/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0119);
- (b) Council [Directive 2001/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0089);
- (c) Council [Directive 2002/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0060);
- (d) Commission [Decision 2000/428/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0428) establishing diagnostic procedures, sampling methods and criteria for the evaluation of the results of laboratory tests for the confirmation and differential diagnosis of swine vesicular disease;
- (e) Commission [Decision 2002/106/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0106) approving a Diagnostic Manual establishing diagnostic procedures, sampling methods and criteria for evaluation of the laboratory tests for the confirmation of classical swine fever; and
- (f) Commission [Decision 2003/422/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0422) approving an African swine fever Diagnostic Manual.

*Extent: E+W*

(4)[^c-978f56408266988b2775045b2a89ca12] Paragraphs (2)(b) and (3) do not apply in relation to England.
