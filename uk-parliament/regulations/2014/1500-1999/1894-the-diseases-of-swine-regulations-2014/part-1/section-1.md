# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, extent and commencement[^c-942f209a85dd68eb76311622248601a8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014;
- (b) extend to Great Britain; and
- (c) come into force on 14th August 2014.
