## Schedule 1A — Restriction of movement of pig products from premises that are later designated as infected premises under regulation 10A[^c-346a45588e2235315a6d8f98cebe7893]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 10A(3)

### 1. Restriction of movement of pig products from premises that are later designated as infected premises under regulation 10A

(1) A person must not, except in accordance with a licence granted under sub-paragraph (2), move from premises in England any pig products that have been—

- (a) moved from other premises that, after that move, are designated under regulation 10A as infected premises by a designation which has been notified to that person; or
- (b) been obtained from pigs that have been moved from other premises that, after that move, are designated under regulation 10A as infected premises by a designation which has been notified to that person.

(2) A licensing officer may licence the movement of such pig products for the purposes of immediate disposal.

### 2. Exceptions

Paragraph 1 does not apply to pig products that—

- (a) have been—
  - (i) produced at least 15 days before the estimated earliest date of infection for the infected premises; and
  - (ii) obtained, cut, transported and stored separately from pig products produced after that date;
- (b) have undergone treatment in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin Regulations; or
- (c) have been moved from the establishment at which the meat from which they were obtained was first processed.
