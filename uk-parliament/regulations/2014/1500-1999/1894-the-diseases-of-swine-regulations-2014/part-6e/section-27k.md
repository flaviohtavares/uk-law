# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 27K

> Text as at 2026-04-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27K. General restriction on the movement of domestic pigs and pig products in England to prevent the spread of African swine fever[^c-2db1384982de3a661bd5c58a54bf0b23]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may, on the basis of a risk assessment, prohibit the movement of any or all of the following in England if the Secretary of State considers it necessary to prevent the spread of African swine fever—

- (a) domestic pigs;
- (b) pig products;
- (c) any thing liable to transmit African swine fever.
