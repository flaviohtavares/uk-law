# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Killing pigs on infected premises[^key-e0a1a058b73c5139a0e93637e882e7de]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When premises are designated as infected premises the appropriate authority must arrange for the killing of all pigs on the premises unless regulation 12 applies.
