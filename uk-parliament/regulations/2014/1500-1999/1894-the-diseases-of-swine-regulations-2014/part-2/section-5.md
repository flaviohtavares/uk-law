# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 5

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Notification requirements[^key-8f3dcd4642763c3f468660fab1cb673d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any person who suspects that a domestic or feral pig or carcase is infected with disease must immediately notify the appropriate authority.

(2) Any person who examines a sample taken from a pig or a carcase and who—

- (a) suspects that the pig or carcase is infected with disease, or
- (b) detects evidence of antibodies to, or antigens of, disease,

must immediately notify the appropriate authority.
