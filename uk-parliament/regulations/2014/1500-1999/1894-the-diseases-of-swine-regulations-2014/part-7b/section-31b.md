## 31B. Cleansing and disinfection of premises etc. in England[^c-2e34b9a818969462be2cf1bbff55271b]

*Extent: E+W*

A person required by or under these Regulations to cleanse and disinfect any premises, or any thing, in England must do so in accordance with Schedule 4A.
