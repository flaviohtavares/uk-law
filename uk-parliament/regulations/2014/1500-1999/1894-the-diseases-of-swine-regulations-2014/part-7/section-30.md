# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 30

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Pigs vaccinated against classical swine fever or African swine fever (Extent: E+W)[^key-ef3abc7935ec2befbf01eafa0e368b36][^key-27f9817e6049cfe8af71e8a15d1eb795]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to pigs vaccinated against classical swine fever or, in England, classical swine fever or African swine fever *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-c0edb3190b0765ffdd5e869dcf4a44a5].

(2) A keeper who has had a pig vaccinated in an emergency vaccination zone or under the authority of a licence granted by a veterinary inspector or, in the case of a pig vaccinated in England, a licensing officer *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-b7a3953fee66aba14f940d946d25c607] must—

- (a) ensure that it is identified in accordance with instructions from a veterinary inspector; and
- (b) keep a record that it has been vaccinated.

(3) The keeper must only have a vaccinated pig slaughtered at a designated slaughterhouse.

(4) Following slaughter of a vaccinated pig the carcase must—

- (a) be disposed of in accordance with the Animal By-Products (Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2013[^f00029], the Animal By-Products (Enforcement) (Scotland) Regulations 2013[^f00030] or the Animal By-Products (Enforcement) (Wales) Regulations 2014[^f00031]; or
- (b) have a stamp applied and be treated under the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (England) Regulations 2008[^f00032], the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Scotland) Order 2008[^f00033] or the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00034].

(5) Where a marker vaccine has been used on a pig, the meat derived from that pig may be exempted by the appropriate authority from the requirement to be treated as restricted meat under the appropriate legislation listed in paragraph (4)(b).
