# The Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014, section 28

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Prohibition on vaccination except in certain cases[^c-7260d78e7e016ac4ee50ae7fbc0d292f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person must not vaccinate a domestic pig (other than a domestic pig in England) *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-d8ec943ce041b6bceb76eae53816f58e] against disease other than in accordance with this Part or under the authority of a licence granted by a veterinary inspector.

*Extent: E+W*

(1A)[^c-112079e1490257bb16f5d0893c7cabcd] A person must not vaccinate a domestic pig in England against disease other than in accordance with this Part or under the authority of a licence granted by a licensing officer.

(2) A person must not vaccinate a feral pig against disease other than in accordance with regulation 22.
