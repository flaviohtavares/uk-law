# The Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1597

National Health Service, England

The Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2014

Made

17th June 2014

Coming into force

1st July 2014

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 28 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust, following the consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st July 2014.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

(1) The Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

(2) The Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

17th June 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st July 2014 of the Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust established on 1st April 1999 and revokes the Barnet and Chase Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999 that established the trust.
