# The East Anglia ONE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 4

> Text as at 2021-09-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Power to construct and maintain authorised project[^key-0124edcf6a3ddd52f317e9d9eaeded93]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertakers may, at any time construct and maintain, and maintain from time to time, the authorised project, except to the extent that this Order or an agreement made under this Order provides otherwise.
