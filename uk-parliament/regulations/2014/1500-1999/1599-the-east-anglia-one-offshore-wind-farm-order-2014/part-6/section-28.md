# The East Anglia ONE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 28

> Text as at 2021-09-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Operation of generating station[^key-3fe257c821058a9c08cddc3e312fcfd9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertakers is hereby authorised to operate the generating station comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertakers of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence under any other legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of an electricity generating station.
