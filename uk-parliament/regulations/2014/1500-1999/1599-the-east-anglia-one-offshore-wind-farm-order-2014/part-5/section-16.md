# The East Anglia ONE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 16

> Text as at 2021-09-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Time limit for exercise of authority to acquire land compulsorily[^key-e9526019cd94299f2d315d868240841d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) After the end of the period of 5 years beginning on the day on which this Order is made—

- (a) no notice to treat shall be served under Part 1 of the 1965 Act; and
- (b) no declaration shall be executed under section 4 of the Compulsory Purchase (Vesting Declarations) Act 1981 as applied by article 19 (application of the Compulsory Purchase (Vesting Declarations) Act 1981)[^f00019].

(2) The authority conferred by article 23 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised project) shall cease at the end of the period referred to in paragraph (1), except that nothing in this paragraph prevents the undertakers remaining in possession of land after the end of that period, if the land was entered and possession was taken before the end of that period.
