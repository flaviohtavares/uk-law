# The East Anglia ONE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2014, section 26

> Text as at 2021-09-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Statutory undertakers[^key-533b3f32473b547462e94d2c83a883cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 9 (Protective provisions) the undertakers may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily, or acquire new rights or impose restrictive covenants over, the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans within the limits to the land to be acquired and described in the book of reference; and
- (b) extinguish the rights of, remove, relocate the rights of or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers over or within the Order land.
