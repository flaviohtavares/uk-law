> Text as at 2021-09-25.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-84007bc64f21f0d0127140a6980410d1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertakers may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised project or to facilitate, or is incidental, to it.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 17 (compulsory acquisition of rights) and article 23 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised project).
