# The Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1882

Defence

The Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2014

Made

16th July 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of July 2014

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

A draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

Accordingly, Her Majesty, on the advice of Her Privy Council and in exercise of the power conferred by section 382(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2014.

**2.** The Armed Forces Act 2006 shall, instead of expiring at the end of 2nd November 2014, expire at the end of 2nd November 2015.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 382 of the Armed Forces Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”) provides that the 2006 Act will expire at the end of one year beginning with the day on which the Armed Forces Act 2011 (“the 2011 Act”) was passed, unless continued in force by Order in Council in accordance with that section. The 2011 Act was passed on 3rd November 2011. The Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/1750](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1750)) and the Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2603](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2603)) each provided for the continuation of the 2006 Act for a further period of 12 months. This Order provides for the continuation of the 2006 Act so that it will expire at the end of 2nd November 2015.
