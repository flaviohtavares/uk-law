# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 53K

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53K. Interpretation of Part 10B[^c-317947cde359e390675327c291054563]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “abandonment security” has the meaning given in regulation 53N(1);
- “relevant period” has the meaning given in regulation 53L(3);
- “wages” has the same meaning as in Part 10 (see regulation 53).
