# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 53M

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53M. Abandonment security requirement applicable to all ships[^key-317947cde359e390675327c291054563]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A ship must not be operated unless—

- (a) in the case of a ship in a category described in regulation 3(1)(a) or (b), an abandonment security is in force in relation to the ship; or
- (b) in the case of a ship in the category described in regulation 3(3), financial security is in place for the ship in accordance with paragraph 3 of standard A2.5.2 of the MLC (financial security for abandoned seafarers).

(2) A breach of paragraph (1) is an offence by the shipowner.
