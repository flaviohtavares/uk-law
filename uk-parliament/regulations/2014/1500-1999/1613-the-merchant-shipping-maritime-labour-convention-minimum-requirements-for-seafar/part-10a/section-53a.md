# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 53A

> Text as at 2026-07-09.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53A. Interpretation of Part 10A[^key-b2476b875e9b569c760afecc0f36919d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “shipowner’s security” has the meaning given in regulation 53C;
- “shipowner’s security document” has the meaning given in regulation 53G(6).
