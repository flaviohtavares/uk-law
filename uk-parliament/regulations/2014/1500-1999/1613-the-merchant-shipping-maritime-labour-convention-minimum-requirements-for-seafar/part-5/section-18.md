# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 18

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Part 5 requirements for non-United Kingdom ships with MLC documentation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A ship must not be operated unless the shipowner complies with the requirements in paragraph 2 of Standard A2.2 (wages).

(2) A breach of paragraph (1) is an offence by the shipowner.
