# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Part 6 requirements for non-United Kingdom ships with MLC documentation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A shipowner must make such provision as is necessary for the repatriation of a seafarer as soon as is practicable in the circumstances described in paragraph 1 of Standard A2.5.1[^c-57d768c24c4b0dbfd9a5d9dc7c67948c] of the MLC, subject to any national provisions which have been adopted by the State whose flag the ship is entitled to fly pursuant to paragraph 2 of Standard A2.5.1.

(2) A breach of paragraph (1) is an offence by the shipowner.

(3) A shipowner must comply with –

- (a) the prohibitions in paragraph 3 of Standard A2.5.1[^c-a3278cca75de23568be7071eb703fbec], whether or not those prohibitions apply in the State whose flag the ship is entitled to fly; and
- (b) the requirement in paragraph 9 of Standard A2.5.1[^c-47027632a23c63d4b2781f05f2af5e24], whether or not the State whose flag the ship is entitled to fly has imposed that requirement in its national laws or otherwise.

(4) A breach of paragraph (3) is an offence by the shipowner.
