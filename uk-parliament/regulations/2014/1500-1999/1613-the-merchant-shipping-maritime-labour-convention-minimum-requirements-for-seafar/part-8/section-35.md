# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 35

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Organisation and equipment of the catering department

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The shipowner and the master of a ship must ensure that—

- (a) food and drinking water which are provided for seafarers are stored and handled; and
- (b) the catering department is organised and equipped,

in accordance with the requirements set out in Merchant Shipping Notice 1845(M).

(2) A breach of paragraph (1) is an offence by the shipowner and the master of the ship.
