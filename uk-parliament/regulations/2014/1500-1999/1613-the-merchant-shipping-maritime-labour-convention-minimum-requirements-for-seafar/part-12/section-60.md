# The Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Minimum Requirements for Seafarers etc.) Regulations 2014, section 60

> Text as at 2026-07-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Defence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In any proceedings for an offence under these Regulations (other than an offence under regulation 10(5)) it is a defence for the person charged to show that all reasonable steps had been taken by that person to ensure compliance with the provision concerned.
