# The Plymouth College of Art (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1507

Education, England

The Plymouth College of Art (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2014

Made

9th June 2014

Laid before Parliament

10th June 2014

Coming into force

4th July 2014

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

In accordance with section 122A(1) of that Act, it appears to the Secretary of State that the full-time equivalent enrolment number of the institution conducted by Plymouth College of Art[^f00002] for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Plymouth College of Art (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2014 and comes into force on 4th July 2014.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England.

## 2. Transfer to the higher education sector

On 4th July 2014 Plymouth College of Art is transferred to the higher education sector.

David Willetts

Minister of State for Universities and Science

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

9th June 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988, provides for the transfer of Plymouth College of Art from the further education sector to the higher education sector on 4th July 2014.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order because it has no impact on businesses or civil society organisations. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
