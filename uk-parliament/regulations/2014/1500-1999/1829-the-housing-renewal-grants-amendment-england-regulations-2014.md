# The Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1829

Housing, England

The Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2014

Made

10th July 2014

Laid before Parliament

16th July 2014

Coming into force

13th August 2014

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(3), 30 and 146(1) and (2) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001], and with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2014 and shall come into force on 13th August 2014.

## 2. Application

 These Regulations apply to applications for grants which fall to be approved on or after 13th August by local housing authorities in England.

## 3. Amendment of the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996

The Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996[^f00002] are amended as follows:

(1) In regulation 19(9B)(a)(iv) after “…section 35” insert “or 35B”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Regulation 3(1) of these Regulations amends regulation 19(9B)(a)(vi) of the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2367](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2367)) to incorporate a reference to section 35B of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4). Section 35B provides for maternity allowance for woman who participate in the work of their self-employed spouse or civil partner, which participation is not remunerated. Maternity allowance falls to be considered as relevant income when awarding a grant under the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996.

Section 35B was inserted into the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 by the Social Security (Maternity Allowance) (Participating Wife or Civil Partner or Self-Employed Earner) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/606)) which implemented Directive 2010/41/EU (on the application of the principle of equal treatment between men and women engaged in an activity in a self/-employed capacity and repealing Council [Directive 86/613/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1986/0613)). (OJ L 180 15.7.2010 p.1).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
