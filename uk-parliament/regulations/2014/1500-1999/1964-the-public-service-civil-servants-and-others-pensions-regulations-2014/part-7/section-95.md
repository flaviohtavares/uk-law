# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 95

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^key-c419709a979c2b4f39b3f83e3dc58b66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Part are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 (reduction of benefit) of WRPA 1999.
