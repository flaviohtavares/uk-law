> Text as at 2025-11-17.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Scheme manager[^key-86e7842854b7e90434bf0065004aebcb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Minister is the scheme manager for this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it[^f00040].

(2) The scheme manager is responsible for managing and administering this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it.
