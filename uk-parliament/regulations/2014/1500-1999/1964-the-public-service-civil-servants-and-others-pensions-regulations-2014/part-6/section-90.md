# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 90

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Adjustment of allocated benefit (members who have reached the age of 75)[^key-66787ade3553c75f79f3e90add026436]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary of an allocation election may be adjusted in a manner determined by the scheme manager if—

- (a) the member who made the allocation dies after reaching the age of 75; and
- (b) on the death of the member, the amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary does not qualify as a dependants’ scheme pension under section 167 (the pension death benefit rules) of FA 2004[^f00060].

(2) In this regulation, “allocated pension” has the meaning given in regulation 89(1)(b).
