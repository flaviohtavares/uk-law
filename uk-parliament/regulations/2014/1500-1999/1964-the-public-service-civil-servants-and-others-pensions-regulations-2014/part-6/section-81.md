# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 81

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Exercising an option under this Chapter[^c-def33eb7913e4247a4ee6f315bddbee9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by notice to the scheme manager in any form the scheme manager requires.

(2) In this Chapter, “option notice” means a notice by which a member exercises an option under this Chapter.
