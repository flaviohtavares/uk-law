# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 72

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Meaning of “lower tier payment threshold”[^c-c2decc4230a19ae899eafdd3034ea093]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An active member of this scheme meets the lower tier threshold for payment of an ill-health pension (“lower tier payment threshold”) if—

- (a) the member’s breakdown in health involves incapacity for employment; or
- (b) the member is partially retired, the member’s breakdown in health involves total incapacity for employment.
