# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Entitlement to partial retirement pension or full retirement pension[^key-7141f61d20222549600f8e4c36a8c3d2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An active member of this scheme (P) who exercises the partial retirement option is entitled to the immediate payment for life of—

- (a) if P claims payment of part only of P’s accrued pensions, a partial retirement pension calculated in accordance with regulation 55; or
- (b) if P claims payment of the whole of P’s accrued pensions, a full retirement pension calculated in accordance with regulation 52.
