# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 116

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Payment of pensions under this Part[^key-9c2f6170f7527e49f008c99bd3fc10d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A surviving adult’s pension or eligible child’s pension is payable from the day after the date of the member’s death.

(2) Unless the scheme manager directs otherwise, an eligible child’s pension payable in respect of an eligible child aged under 18 must be paid—

- (a) if the child is in the care of the member’s surviving adult, to the surviving adult; and
- (b) in any other case, to the child’s guardian.
