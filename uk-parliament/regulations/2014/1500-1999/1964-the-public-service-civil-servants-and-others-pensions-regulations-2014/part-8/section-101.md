# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 101

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Meaning of “surviving partner”[^key-bb542271acc7ce74d9ae3186fdc6bc87]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (P) is a surviving partner of a deceased member of this scheme if P satisfies the scheme manager that immediately before the member’s death—

- (a) P and the member were cohabiting as partners in an exclusive, committed long-term relationship;
- (b) P and the member were not prevented from entering into a marriage or a civil partnership; and
- (c) either P was financially dependent on the member, or P and the member were financially interdependent.
