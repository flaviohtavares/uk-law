# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 112

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Meaning of “eligible child’s pension”[^c-f66babf34eb8b93ffec4703f8500f155]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, “eligible child’s pension” means any of the following pensions payable under this Part—

- (a) a child’s earned pension;
- (b) a child’s lower tier earned pension;
- (c) a child’s upper tier top up earned pension;
- (d) a child’s added pension.
