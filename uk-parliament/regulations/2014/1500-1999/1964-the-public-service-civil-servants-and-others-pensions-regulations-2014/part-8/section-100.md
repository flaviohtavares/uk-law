# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 100

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Surviving adults[^key-ca5527da920f6129aa71a593112e954f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “surviving adult”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means the member’s surviving spouse, surviving civil partner or surviving [^key-0909e3795389d74c2f7868ed7b141ad5]... partner;
- “surviving civil partner”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was in a civil partnership with the member at the date of the member’s death; and
- “surviving spouse”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was married to the member at the date of the member’s death.
