# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 126

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Recovery of payments[^key-152f0978e08749eba3e6038d7f40daf7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager may recover a lump sum death benefit paid to any person if the person’s nomination is subsequently found to be invalid.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not affect any other right the scheme manager has to recover a payment or an overpayment.
