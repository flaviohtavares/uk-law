# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 138

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Deduction of payments for extra pension under Schedule 1[^c-89219d0cd7fda54d7e8c78f117de9e2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 1 has effect for the purpose of enabling deductions of payments for—

- (a) added pension;
- (b) an effective pension age; and
- (c) an enhanced effective pension age.
