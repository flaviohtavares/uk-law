# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Service in 2 or more scheme employments[^c-e4c17f0a24a5d1a4c227eaab91b0f229]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person is in service in 2 or more scheme employments, regulation 14 applies separately in relation to each employment.
