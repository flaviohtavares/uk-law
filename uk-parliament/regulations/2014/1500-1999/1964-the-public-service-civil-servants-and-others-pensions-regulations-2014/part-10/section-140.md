# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 140

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Application of Part[^c-1f71491c7067f6b3d09d751a1c07e94b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA[^c-d4fc1368d9a2be4c72f6417c084bf8c6] of PSA 1993 (transfer values) and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 2[^c-b2c56553af1e1f37236b4c9266e7eba1] of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds)[^f00074] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
