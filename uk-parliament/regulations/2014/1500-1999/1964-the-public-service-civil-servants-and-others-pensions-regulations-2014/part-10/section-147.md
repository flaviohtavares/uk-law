# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 147

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Effect of a transfer out[^c-ac64df3afbdf041770c12c6e05ff13c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a transfer payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
