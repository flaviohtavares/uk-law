# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 162

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Late payment of retirement index adjustment[^key-8cd0eb3c0a544ae96d0e760d62bb759f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment to be paid before the end of the last active scheme year.
