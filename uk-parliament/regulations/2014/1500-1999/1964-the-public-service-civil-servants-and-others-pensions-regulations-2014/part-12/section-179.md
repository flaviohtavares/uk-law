# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 179

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^c-1f993d6962dd29ebf5d440bf538ccc75]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must provide benefit information statements to each active member of this scheme in accordance with—

- (a) section 14 of the Act (information about benefits); and
- (b) directions made under that section (“Treasury directions”).
