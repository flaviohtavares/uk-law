# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 170

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Scheme manager to be scheme administrator for purposes of Part 4 of Finance Act 2004[^c-b2523b92475ab7201d75d9bb9c90ead4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004 (pension schemes etc).
