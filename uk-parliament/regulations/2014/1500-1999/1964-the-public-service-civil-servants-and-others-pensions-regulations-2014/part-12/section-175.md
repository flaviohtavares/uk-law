# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 175

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 175. General prohibition on unauthorised payments[^c-8ec7c5383b007f55227dbc1219aa17bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of FA 2004 (pension schemes etc.) (see section 160(5) of that Act).
