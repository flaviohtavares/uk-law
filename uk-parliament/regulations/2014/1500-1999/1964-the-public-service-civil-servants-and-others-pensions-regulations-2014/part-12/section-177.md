# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations 2014, section 177

> Text as at 2025-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 177. Determination of questions[^key-03a409abb7df3490ce8e554adb73f575]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except as otherwise provided by these Regulations, any question arising under this scheme is to be determined by the scheme manager, whose decision on it is final.
