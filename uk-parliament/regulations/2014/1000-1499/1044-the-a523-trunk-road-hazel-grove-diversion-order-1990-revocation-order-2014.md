# The A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion Order) 1990 (Revocation) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1044

Highways, England

The A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion Order) 1990 (Revocation) Order 2014

Made

8th April 2014

Coming into force

30th April 2014

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and[^f00003]:-

**1.** This Scheme may be cited as the A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion) Order 1990 (Revocation) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 30th April 2014.

**2.** The A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion Order) 1990 is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Alan Shepherd

NDD NW Divisional Director

Highways Agency

8th April 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the formerly made Order 1990.
