# The Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1134 (C. 47)

Children And Young Persons

Family Proceedings

The Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2014

Made

30th April 2014

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 139(6) and (7) of the Children and Families Act 2014[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

(1) The Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) After article 4 insert —

> **4A. Provision coming into force on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2014**
>
> Section 78 comes into force on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2014

.

(3) In article 5, omit paragraph (c).

Edward Timpson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

30th April 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014 to change the date of coming into force of section 78 of that Act from 13th May 2014 to 1st May 2014.
