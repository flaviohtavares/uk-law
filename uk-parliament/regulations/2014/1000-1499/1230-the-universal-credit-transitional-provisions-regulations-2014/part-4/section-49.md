## 49. Meaning of “migration day”[^c-90e3de9c82961a5746b71bf6175c0696]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“Migration day”, in relation to a qualifying claim, means the day before the first day on which the claimant is entitled to universal credit in connection with that claim.
