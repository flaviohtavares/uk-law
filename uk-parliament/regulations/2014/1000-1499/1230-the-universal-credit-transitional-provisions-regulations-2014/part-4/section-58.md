# The Universal Credit (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2014, section 58

## 58. Qualifying claim – Secretary of State may set later commencement day[^c-90e3de9c82961a5746b71bf6175c0696]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the Secretary of State decides a qualifying claim, and it is not a case where the award is to commence before the date of claim by virtue of regulation 46(3) [^c-66a68fc3884d3bf7c4a76bff44d34db8]... (claim made by the final deadline) or regulation 26 of the Claims and Payments Regulations (time within which a claim for universal credit is to be made) as modified by regulation 15 of these Regulations, the Secretary of State may determine a day on which the award of universal credit is to commence that is after, but no more than one month after, the date of claim.
