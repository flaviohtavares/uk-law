# The Universal Credit (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2014, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2014/1230

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Exclusion of entitlement to certain benefits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), a claimant is not entitled to—

- (a) income support;
- (b) housing benefit;
- (c) a tax credit; or
- (d) state pension credit [^key-cd78a2751c77f6f211f9f85aa352f358]... [^c2210275],

in respect of any period when the claimant is entitled to universal credit [^c2210276].

(2) Entitlement to universal credit does not preclude the claimant from entitlement—

- (a)[^key-47256eed311644e4759655c7918f6ccd] to housing benefit in respect of specified accommodation or temporary accommodation;
- (ab) to housing benefit or income support where regulation 8(2A) or 46(1)[^key-3f56b5d1a12e2445d746002142540a0c] applies; [^key-b33fbc31d05a7e25edb18333e603e84d]...
- (b) during the first assessment period for universal credit, where the claimant is a new claimant partner, to—
  - (i) income support, where an award to which the new claimant partner is entitled terminates, in accordance with regulation 7(4), after the first date of entitlement to universal credit;
  - (ii) housing benefit, where regulation 7(5)(b) or (c)[^key-3d9f3fb85cc2f3e0255fd17f9fe1904b] applies and an award of housing benefit to which the new claimant partner is entitled terminates after the first date of entitlement to universal credit; [^key-8f925914ed6012ff367632ea2d9a6ab5]...
  - (iii) a tax credit, where an award to which the new claimant partner is entitled terminates, in accordance with the 2002 Act, after the first date of entitlement to universal credit; or[^key-2339b10b8af81f407fb40ef771c7a3dc]
  - (iv) state pension credit, where an award to which the new claimant partner is entitled terminates after the first date of entitlement to universal credit; or
- (c) during the last assessment period for universal credit, where the claimant reaches the qualifying age for state pension credit and paragraph 26 of Schedule 1 to the Decisions and Appeals Regulations applies, to housing benefit or state pension credit from the date the claimant reaches that age
