# The Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1369

Ecclesiastical Law, England

The Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 2014

Made

19th May 2014

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of their powers conferred by section 21(2) of the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 2014.

(2) In this Order “the Measure” means the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014.

## 2. Commencement

(1) All the provisions of the Measure which have not yet been commenced, other than those specified in paragraph (2), come into force on the day on which this Order is made.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 2;
- (b) section 7;
- (c) section 12;
- (d) paragraphs 7 and 9, and sub-paragraphs (4), (5) and (12) of paragraph 19 of Schedule 2.

\+Justin Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the majority of the provisions of the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2014, which makes amendments to a wide range of Church Measures and other legislation. The remaining provisions will be brought into force at a later date.
