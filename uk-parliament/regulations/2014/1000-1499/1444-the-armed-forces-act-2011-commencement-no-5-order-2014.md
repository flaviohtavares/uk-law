# The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1444 (C. 54)

Defence

The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014

Made

1st June 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(3) of the Armed Forces Act 2011[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014.

**2.** In this Order “the Act” means the Armed Forces Act 2011.

## 3. Commencement

Section 4 of the Act comes into force on 4th June 2014.

Anna Soubry

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

1st June 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 4 of the Armed Forces Act [2011 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/18) on 4th June 2014.
