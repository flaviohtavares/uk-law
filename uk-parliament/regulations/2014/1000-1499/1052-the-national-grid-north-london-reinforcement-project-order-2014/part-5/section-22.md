> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-7d30c35ad35799b4bf4f4ce69d751060]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 9 (land to be acquired compulsorily) as is required for the construction, operation and maintenance of the authorised development or is incidental to the authorised development or necessary to facilitate it or is required as replacement land.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 25 (compulsory acquisition of rights) and article 31 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
