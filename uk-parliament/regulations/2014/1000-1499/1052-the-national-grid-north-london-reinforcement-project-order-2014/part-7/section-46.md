# The National Grid (North London Reinforcement Project) Order 2014, section 46

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Arbitration[^c-ff11060cb06d64968b97fb54e21f26ee]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Except where otherwise expressly provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed between the parties, any difference under any provision of this Order shall be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
