> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 3. Application and modification of legislative provisions[^key-cc0189cf2c20280c581379db8b33b5f2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the modifications set out in Schedule 10 the enactments for the time being in force with respect to compensation for compulsory purchase of land shall apply in the case of the compulsory acquisition under this Order of a right by the creation of a new right as they apply with respect to compensation for the compulsory purchase of land and interests in land.
