# The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 792

National Health Service, England

The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2014

Made

20th March 2014

Coming into force

31st March 2014

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27 of, and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2014 and comes into force on 31st March 2014.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trust

The originating capital of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust[^f00002] is £72,544,000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

17th March 2014

We consent,

Sam Gyimah

David Evennett

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust.
