# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-54365463fff4f99bbe0d398fe5a297d5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014 and comes into force on 22nd April 2014.

(2) In this Order “the 1972 Act” means the Maintenance Enforcement (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972[^f00002].
