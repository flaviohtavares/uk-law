# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**67.**[^c-4508c17d1f693ef6d4b166b2f52bb9f1] In section 8—

- (a) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) for “a registered order” substitute “an order registered in a court in Northern Ireland”; and
  - (ii) for “appropriate officer of the registering” substitute “clerk of that”;
- (b) omit subsection (3A);
- (c) omit subsections (4) to (4B); and
- (d) in subsection (5), for “The magistrates’ court” substitute “A magistrates’ court in Northern Ireland”.
