# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 118

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Amendments to the Recovery of Maintenance (United States of America) Order 2007[^key-d19d0fea01363673db1b64d25d2e37a0]

*Extent: E+W*

In article 3 of the Recovery of Maintenance (United States of America) Order 2007[^f00034], in the inserted section 26(3A) of the 1972 Act, for “a justice of the peace” substitute “a judge of the family court”.
