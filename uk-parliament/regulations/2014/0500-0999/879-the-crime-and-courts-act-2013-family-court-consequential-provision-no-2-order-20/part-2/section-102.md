# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 102

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**102.**[^key-40107cfc422b0eeb3fbd839cb2d4644f] References in the Justices of the Peace (Training and Development Committee) Rules 2007 as amended by this Order to a family panel or to a combined family panel are to be read to include such a panel established in accordance with the Family Proceedings Courts (Constitution of Committees and Right to Preside Rules 2007[^f00032] prior to the revocation of the latter Rules by this Order.
