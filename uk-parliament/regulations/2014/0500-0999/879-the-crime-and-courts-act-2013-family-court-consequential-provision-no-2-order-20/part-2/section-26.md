# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 26

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Amendment to the Magistrates’ Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1992[^key-7f7687380991363c4efd277e3e8e3c71]

*Extent: E+W*

In the definition of “court” in rule 13(2) of the Magistrates’ Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1992[^f00011], omit “, but does not include a family proceedings court”.
