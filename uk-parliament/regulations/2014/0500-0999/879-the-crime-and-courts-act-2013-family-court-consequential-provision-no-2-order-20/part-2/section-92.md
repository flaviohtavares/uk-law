## 92. Amendments to the Fines Collection Regulations 2006[^c-0a07784878c01500f3e8a1eadfe8f5f8]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 30 of the Fines Collection Regulations 2006[^f00028]—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (i), omit “and”; and
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (ii);
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (ii), omit “or 11”; and
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (iii), omit paragraph (aa);
- (c) in paragraph (g)(i), omit “the first occurrence of”;
- (d) omit paragraph (i); and
- (e) omit paragraph (j)(i).
