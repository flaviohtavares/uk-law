# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 133

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Amendments to the Court Funds Rules 2011[^c-230903432161d7213b721939cdaa940a]

*Extent: E+W*

In rule 2(b) of the Court Funds Rules 2011[^f00040]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (iii), omit “or”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (iv), for “.” substitute “; or”; and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (iv), insert—

  > - (v) the family court.

  .
