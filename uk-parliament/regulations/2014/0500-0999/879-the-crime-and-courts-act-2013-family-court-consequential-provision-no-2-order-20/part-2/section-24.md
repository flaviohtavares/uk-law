# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 24

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Amendments to the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991[^c-2e5fe707203acd1e45671c7d7cb0a133]

*Extent: E+W*

In the notes to the forms C(PRA1), C(PRA2) and C(PRA3) in the Schedule to the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991[^f00009]—

- (a) for “your local family proceedings court, or county court” substitute “a local office of the family court”; and
- (b) for “a local family proceedings court, or county court,” substitute “a local office of the family court”.
