# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 129

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**129.**[^c-1d7173af764f89fa5a4f37263a6ce3c6] In paragraph 6—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(a), for “a magistrates’ court” substitute “the family court”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (2), after “transmitted to” insert “the family court,”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (7)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a)—
    - (aa) for “a magistrates’ court in England and Wales” substitute “the family court”; and
    - (bb) omit the words after “that court”; and
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), for “section 150(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980” substitute “section 1(10) of the Maintenance Enforcement Act 1991”; and
- (d) in sub-paragraph (10)—
  - (i) for “a magistrates’ court in England and Wales or Northern Ireland” substitute “the family court or, in Northern Ireland, a magistrates’ court”; and
  - (ii) for “designated officer” substitute “court officer”.
