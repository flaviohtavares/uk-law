# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 121

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 121. Amendment to the Child Support Information Regulations 2008[^key-acf1f70070b55ac1d7bf9dbacbe41a43]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 6(2)(a) of the Child Support Information Regulations 2008[^f00036], for paragraphs (ii) and (iii) substitute—

> - (ii) in relation to the family court, a judge of that court;

.
