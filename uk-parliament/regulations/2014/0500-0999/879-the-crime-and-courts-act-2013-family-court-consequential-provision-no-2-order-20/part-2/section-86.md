# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 86

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Amendment to the Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service (Reviewed Case Referral) Regulations 2004[^key-5a0e0b3f1bd30b2df5f1763dbce100a2]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2 of the Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service (Reviewed Case Referral) Regulations 2004[^f00025], in the definition of “court” for “, a county court or a magistrates’ court” substitute “or the family court”.
