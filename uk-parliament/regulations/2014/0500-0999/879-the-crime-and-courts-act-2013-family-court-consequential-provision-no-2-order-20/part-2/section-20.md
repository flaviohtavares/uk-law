# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 20

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**20.**[^c-c1b768fd4d93b962c25f2ec7e011558b] After rule 101A insert—

> **101B. Direction to a local authority under section 447(2) and (3) of the Education Act 1996**
>
> (1) For the purposes of section 447(2) and (3) of the Education Act 1996, a direction by a magistrates’ court to a local authority to apply for an education supervision order must be in writing.
>
> (2) Where, following such a direction, a local authority informs the court that they have decided not to apply for an education supervision order, they must do so in writing.
>
> (3) In this rule, “education supervision order” has the meaning assigned to it by section 36(2) of the Children Act 1989.
