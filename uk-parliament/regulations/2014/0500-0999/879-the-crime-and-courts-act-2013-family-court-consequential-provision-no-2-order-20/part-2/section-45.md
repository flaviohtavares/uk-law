## 45. Amendments to the Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders (Republic of Ireland) Order 1993[^c-a859a9451cb88035657a0596768ac6f3]

*Extent: E+W*

Insofar as it remains in force for the purposes specified in paragraph 28(1) and (2) of Schedule 7 to the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Maintenance) Regulations 2011[^f00014], the Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders (Republic of Ireland) Order 1993[^f00015] is amended as follows.
