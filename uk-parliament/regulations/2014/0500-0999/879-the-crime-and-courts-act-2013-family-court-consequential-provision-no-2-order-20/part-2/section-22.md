# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 22

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Amendments to the Insolvency Rules 1986[^c-d76836431f42e23e7f0815134d3a20a1]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00007] in the definition of “fine” and “family proceedings”—

- (a) in rule 5A.2; and
- (b) in rule 12.3(2);

for “which applies the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 and” substitute “which refers to proceedings in the family court and to the meaning of “family proceedings” in”.
