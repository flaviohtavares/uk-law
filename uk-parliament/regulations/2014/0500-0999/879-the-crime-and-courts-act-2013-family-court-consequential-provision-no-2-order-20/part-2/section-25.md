# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 25

> Text as at 2014-04-22.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Amendment to the Child Support (Collection and Enforcement) Regulations 1992[^key-2c5ad68745b83f004d7e496a0b81ab2d]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 25AB(1) of the Child Support (Collection and Enforcement) Regulations 1992[^f00010], for “a county court” substitute “the family court”.
