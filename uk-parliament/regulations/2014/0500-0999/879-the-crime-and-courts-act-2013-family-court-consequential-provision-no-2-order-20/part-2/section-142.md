# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 142

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**142.**[^key-90e4d70b0268fedbf656653280d899df] In paragraph 2—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1), in the definition of “maintenance decision” for “a magistrates’ court maintenance order within the meaning of section 150(1) of the 1980 Act” substitute “a maintenance order enforceable in the family court”; and
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (2)(c).
