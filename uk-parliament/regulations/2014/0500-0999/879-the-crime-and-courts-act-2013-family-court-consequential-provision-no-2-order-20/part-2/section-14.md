# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 14

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Amendments to the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971[^key-33635a8144c9b35354e10ec0d7bd4672]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 1 to the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971[^f00005]—

- (a) in Form 1 (Direction Form), in Part I, for “Proper Officer of the Court” substitute “court officer”; and
- (b) in Form 2 (Report by Tester), in the heading to the form, for “(Designated Officer)…. Magistrates’ Court”, substitute “The family court sitting at…….”.
