# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 74

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 74. Amendment to the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^key-85be3fda82fc19b0443e4fbeec446250]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 169(3) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00018], for “within the meaning of section 150(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980” substitute “made by the family court”.
