# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 27

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Amendment to the Child Support (Maintenance Arrangements and Jurisdiction) Regulations 1992[^c-237fe5cb3c9629195dd2177aae167a69]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulations 5(4)(a) and 6(2)(a) of the Child Support (Maintenance Arrangements and Jurisdiction) Regulations 1992[^f00012], for paragraphs (ii) and (iii) substitute—

> - (ii) in relation to the family court, a court officer;

.
