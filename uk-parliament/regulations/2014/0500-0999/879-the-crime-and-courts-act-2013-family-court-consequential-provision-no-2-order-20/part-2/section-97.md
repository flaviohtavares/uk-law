# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 97

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**97.**[^key-bf670d8fe0c9940ae98b676c61ac6ad2] In rule 35—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (b) for “family proceedings court” substitute “family court”; and
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (d) for “a family proceedings court” substitute “the family court”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (3) and (4), for the words after “presiding” substitute “in the family court in accordance with provision made in rules under section 31D of the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984[^f00031].”.
