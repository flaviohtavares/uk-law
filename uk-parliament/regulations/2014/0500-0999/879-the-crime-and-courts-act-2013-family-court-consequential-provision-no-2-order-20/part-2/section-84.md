# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Family Court: Consequential Provision) (No.2) Order 2014, section 84

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**84.**[^c-55569fa03ec3f2efeeb89b87af8251d0] In paragraph 3—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (5), substitute—

  > (5) A maintenance order which by virtue of Article 39 is enforceable by a magistrates’ court in England and Wales shall—
  >
  > - (a) be transmitted by the Lord Chancellor to the family court; and
  > - (b) be enforceable in the family court in the same manner as a maintenance order made by that court.

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (7), omit “England and Wales or”; and
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (8)(a).
