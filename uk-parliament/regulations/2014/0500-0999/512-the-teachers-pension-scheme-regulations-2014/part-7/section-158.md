# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 158

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 158. Death grant: death of pension credit member before pension becomes payable

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A death grant may be paid[^c-f6f7fd2f41aa1a2c241534cc59e99db0] under this regulation on the death of a pension credit member (D) who dies before a pension credit retirement pension becomes payable to D under regulation 155.

(2) The amount of the death grant is found by—

- (a) taking the amount of credited pension which would have become payable to D at the date of D's death if D had reached normal pension age; and
- (b) multiplying that amount by 2.25.
