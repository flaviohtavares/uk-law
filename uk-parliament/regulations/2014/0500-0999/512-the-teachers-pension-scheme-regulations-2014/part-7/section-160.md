# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 160

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 160. Payment of death grant

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-20aaee66ff05fd5951b3e3706c509698] On the death of a pension credit member, where a death grant is to be paid, the payment must be made[^c-0dac8c675aed978bf11beb605f1c6804]—

- (a) to the death grant beneficiary;
- (b) if there is more than one death grant beneficiary, to those beneficiaries in the shares determined in accordance with regulation 157(4) and (5); or
- (c) if there is no death grant beneficiary—
  - (i) to the member's surviving spouse , surviving civil partner or surviving qualifying partner[^c-a1d1ae558d5a82c338f81547fef71654]; or,
  - (ii) if there is no such person, to the member's personal representatives as part of the member's estate.

(2)[^c-d941ec8500fb6195e352a8968116ec4b] In paragraph (1)(c)(i) “surviving qualifying partner” has the meaning given by regulation 135.
