# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 153

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 153. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations—

- “**pension credit**” means a credit under section 29(1)(b) of the WRPA 1999 as against the scheme manager as the person responsible for this scheme;
- “**pension credit member**” means a person entitled to a pension credit;
- “**pension credit retirement pension**” means a pension payable under regulation 155(1);
- “**pension debit member**” means, in relation to a pension credit member, the person whose rights under these Regulations become subject to a debit under section 29(1)(a) of WRPA 1999 when the pension credit member becomes entitled to a pension credit.
