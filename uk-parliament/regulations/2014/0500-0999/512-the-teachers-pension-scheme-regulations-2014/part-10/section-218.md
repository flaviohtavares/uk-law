# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 218

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 218. Accepting a bulk transfer payment

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The scheme manager must accept a transfer payment from another occupational pension scheme in respect of each person to whom this Chapter applies as part of a bulk transfer payment if it is offered.

(2) In this Chapter, “**bulk transfer payment**” means a single transfer payment in respect of all persons to whom this Chapter applies.
