# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 210

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 210. Application for payment of a club transfer value

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person (P) who is provided with a club transfer statement of entitlement may apply for a club transfer value to be paid to another public service pension scheme (“**the receiving scheme**”).

(2) The application must—

- (a) be made within 12 months after the day on which P becomes subject to the receiving scheme;
- (b) be by notice to the scheme manager;
- (c) specify the pension scheme to which the club transfer value is to be paid; and
- (d) meet any other conditions the scheme manager requires.
