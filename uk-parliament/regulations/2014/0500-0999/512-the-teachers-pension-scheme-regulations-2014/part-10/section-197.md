# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 197

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 197. Application of Part

*Extent: E+W*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 4 of Part 4 of PSA 1993 (transfer values); and
- (b) is without prejudice to that Chapter or Chapter 5 of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds).
