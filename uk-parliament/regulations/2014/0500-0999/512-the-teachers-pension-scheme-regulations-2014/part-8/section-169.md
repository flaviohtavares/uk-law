# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 169

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 169. Amount of lump sum payable under regulation 168

*Extent: E+W*

The amount of a lump sum payable to a person (P) under regulation 168 must—

- (a) be a multiple of £12; and
- (b) not exceed P's permitted maximum.
