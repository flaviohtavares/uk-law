# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 166

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 166. Apportionment Act 1870 not to apply

*Extent: E+W*

The Apportionment Act 1870 [^c22421181] being inconsistent with regulations 164 (monthly payment of pension) and 165 (quarterly payment of pension) does not apply to benefits under these Regulations.
