# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 167

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 167. Member declaration

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The scheme manager may not pay a member a lump sum under this Chapter unless the member declares in writing that, on payment of the lump sum, paragraph 3A of Schedule 29 to FA 2004 [^c22421191] would not apply.

(2) The declaration must be—

- (a) signed by the member;
- (b) in a form specified by the scheme manager; and
- (c) provided by a date determined by the scheme manager.
