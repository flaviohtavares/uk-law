# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 188

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 188. Meaning of “entitlement day” (repayment of balance of contributions)

*Extent: E+W*

The entitlement day for repayment of the balance of contributions is one month after the last day of pensionable service.
