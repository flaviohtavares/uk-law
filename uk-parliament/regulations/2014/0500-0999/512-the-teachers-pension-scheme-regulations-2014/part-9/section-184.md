# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 184

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 184. Pensionable earnings for a pay period

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, “**pensionable earnings**”, in relation to a pay period, has the following meaning.

(2) For any pay period falling within a period of sick leave in which the pensionable earnings of a member (P) are reduced, P's pensionable earnings are the reduced amount of pensionable earnings paid to P.

(3) For a pay period falling within a period in which an election under regulation 26 has effect or is taken to have effect, P's pensionable earnings are an amount equal to P's notional salary.

(4) For a pay period falling within a period in which P is on maternity leave, adoption leave, shared parental leave[^c-46379eea64dfa9474955cb2affb7e47b] , parental bereavement leave[^c-9e8d38b0019d9d6722d0a0bae1a8fb5c] , neonatal care leave[^c-0b08e18637386fd690f4ddb3b2c72504] or paternity leave, P's pensionable earnings include any statutory pay paid to P.
