# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 7

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Scheme manager

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State is the scheme manager for this scheme and any connected scheme.

(2) The scheme manager is responsible for managing this scheme and any connected scheme.

(3)[^c-a6ebde89c1d32d006e63636254fd1165] The responsible authority may delegate any functions of the scheme manager under these Regulations, including this power to delegate.
