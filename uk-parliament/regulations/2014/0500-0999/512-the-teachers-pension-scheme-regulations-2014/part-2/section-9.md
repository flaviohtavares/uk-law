# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Membership of pension board

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph 2, the scheme manager may determine—

- (a) the membership of the Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board;
- (b) the manner in which[^c-11c6f640a2e478a5d8d38b580dbf35f0] the Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board members may be appointed and removed; and
- (c) the terms of appointment of the Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board members.

(2) The Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board is to include equal numbers representing the employers [^c22421051] and members.
