# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 39

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Pensionable earnings for the purpose of accruing benefits

*Extent: E+W*

For the purpose of calculating a member's (P's) amount of earned pension for a pay period—

- (a) if P's pensionable earnings are reduced during sick leave, carer’s leave,[^c-fedfed1039153485766c7807e1090313] adoption leave, maternity leave, parental leave, shared parental leave[^c-7306c76d1f71e4301e935a736adfb431] , parental bereavement leave[^c-301aff115a681f6509d7828e8a807acb] , neonatal care leave[^c-5887c4f91cdfef70f9a1c9a0f5442f75] or paternity leave [^c-041aea4e04990e07b9cbbdf1797b4d7b]..., P's pensionable earnings for that period include P's notional pensionable earnings; and
- (b) if an election under regulation 26 has effect or is taken to have effect for that period, are an amount equal to P's notional salary.
