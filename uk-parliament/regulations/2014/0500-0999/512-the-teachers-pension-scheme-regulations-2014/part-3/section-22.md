# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 22

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Payment of retirement benefits to dual capacity members

*Extent: E+W*

Retirement benefits under this scheme are payable to a dual capacity member as if the member were 2 or more members and the amounts payable are determined accordingly [^c22421081].

## Annotations

[^c22421081]: Marginal citation: The result is that 2 or more pensions or lump sums may be payable in respect of the one member.
