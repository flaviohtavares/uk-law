# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 128

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 128. Meaning of “dies out of service”

*Extent: E+W*

A member (D) dies out of service for the purpose of survivor's benefits if—

- (a) D does not die in service; or
- (b) D does not die as a pensioner member.
