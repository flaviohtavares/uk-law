# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Deferred member's account established after ill-health pension ceases to be payable

*Extent: E+W*

If an ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 114 or 118 and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member's account must be established; and
- (b) that account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of pension payable immediately before the ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
