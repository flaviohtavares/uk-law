# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 42

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**the leaving year**” means the financial year in which the relevant last day falls;
- “**the relevant last day**” means—
  - (a) for a member who has made a phased retirement application, the day before the entitlement day for a phased retirement pension; and
  - (b) otherwise, the last day of pensionable service.
