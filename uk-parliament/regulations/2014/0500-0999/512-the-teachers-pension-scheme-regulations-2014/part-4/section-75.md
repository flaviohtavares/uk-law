# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 75

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Meaning of “conversion amount”

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations, “**conversion amount**”, in relation to any pension converted in part to a lump sum under regulation 168, means the amount of pension converted to a lump sum.
