# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 72

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Meaning of “phased retirement additional pension”

*Extent: E+W*

The amount of phased retirement additional pension is the sum of—

- (a) the amount of accrued additional pension calculated under regulation 47(3); and
- (b) the leaver PIA index adjustment for that amount.
