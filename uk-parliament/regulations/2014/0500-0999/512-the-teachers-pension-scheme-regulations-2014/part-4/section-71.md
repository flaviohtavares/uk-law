# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 71

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Meaning of “phased retirement earned pension”

*Extent: E+W*

The amount of phased retirement earned pension is the sum of—

- (a) the amount of accrued earned pension calculated under regulation 47(2); and
- (b) the leaver index adjustment for that amount.
