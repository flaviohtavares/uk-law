# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 63

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Account to specify amount of accrued earned pension and leaver index adjustment

*Extent: E+W*

The deferred member's account must specify—

- (a) the amount of accrued earned pension calculated under regulation 46(3); and
- (b) the leaver index adjustment for that amount.
