# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 123

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. Meaning of “entitlement day” (short-service annuity)

*Extent: E+W*

The entitlement day for a short-service annuity is the date specified in an application under regulation 162 for payment of the annuity, which must be no earlier than 6 weeks after the day on which the application is made.
