# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 99

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. Interpretation of “pensionable service”

*Extent: E+W*

For the purpose of this Chapter, a person in respect of whom an election under regulation 26 has effect is not treated as being in pensionable service.
