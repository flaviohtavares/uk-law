# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 106

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 106. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**ill-health application**” means an application under regulation 162 for payment of—
  - (a) an ill-health pension; and
  - (b) if applicable, a total incapacity pension; and
- “**stepped down employment**” has the meaning given in regulation 117.
