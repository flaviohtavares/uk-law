# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 90

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 90. The reduced annual rate condition

*Extent: E+W*

A person (P) meets the reduced annual rate condition if—

- (a) P is in one or more eligible employments;
- (b) the terms of employment change and as a result there is a reduction in the annual rate of P's pensionable earnings; and
- (c) the reduced annual rate is not more than 80% of the average annual rate of P's pensionable earnings for the 12 months[^c-a9f3fafc983a824772be90932393043a] of pensionable service immediately before the reduction.
