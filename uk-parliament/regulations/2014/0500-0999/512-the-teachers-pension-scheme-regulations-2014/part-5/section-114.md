# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 114

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 114. When ill-health pension ceases to be payable

*Extent: E+W*

An ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P), unless P has reached normal pension age, on the earlier of the following dates—

- (a) the date on which P re-enters eligible employment;
- (b) the date on which P engages in any work as a teacher which is not an eligible employment.
