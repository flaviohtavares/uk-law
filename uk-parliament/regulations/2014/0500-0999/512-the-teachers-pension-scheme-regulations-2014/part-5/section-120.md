# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations 2014, section 120

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Meaning of “entitlement day” (short-service serious ill-health grant)

*Extent: E+W*

The entitlement day for a short-service serious ill-health grant is the day after a person (P) leaves all eligible employment because P is incapacitated.
