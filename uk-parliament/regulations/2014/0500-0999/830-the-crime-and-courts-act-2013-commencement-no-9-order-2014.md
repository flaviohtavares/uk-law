# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 830 (C. 34)

Enforcement, England And Wales

The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2014

Made

24th March 2014

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 61(3) of the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2014.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 6th April 2014

Section 25 of the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Enforcement by taking control of goods), so far as not already in force, comes into force on 6th April 2014.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Edward Faulks

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

24th March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the ninth commencement order made under the Crime and Courts Act [2013 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/22) (“the Act”). It brings into force section 25 of the Act.

Section 25 is brought into force to coincide with the coming into force of the provisions in sections 62 to 90 of and Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00002] which it amends, which make provision for the replacement of various powers of enforcement by way of execution and distraint by a procedure known as taking control of goods, and for the replacement of distress for rent by a power (to be known as commercial rent arrears recovery, or CRAR) to use the taking control of goods procedure.
