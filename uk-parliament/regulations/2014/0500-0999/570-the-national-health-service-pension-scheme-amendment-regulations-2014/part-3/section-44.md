# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Amendment of regulation 3.C.5

For sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (19A)[^f00031] of regulation 3.C.5 (payment of contributions), substitute—

> - (b) by deduction from any benefit payable to, or in respect of, the member where the Secretary of State has notified the member of an intention to do so.

.
