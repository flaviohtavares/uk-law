# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Amendment of regulation 2.C.7

(1) Regulation 2.C.7 (guarantees, indemnities and bonds) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “fails to pay contributions” insert “or has previously failed to do so”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (b)(iv), insert—

  > - (v) an Independent Provider.

  .

(3) In paragraph (2), after “liabilities of the authority”, insert “(or such liabilities as are specified by the Secretary of State)”.
