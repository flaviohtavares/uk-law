# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Amendment of regulation 1.B.3

In paragraph (3A) of regulation 1.B.3 (provision of information relevant for tax purposes)[^f00017]—

- (a) after “to the 2011 Act” insert “or paragraph 1 of Schedule 22 to the Finance Act 2013”;
- (b) after “Regulations 2011” insert “or the Registered Pension Schemes and Relieved Non-UK Pension Schemes (Lifetime Allowance Transitional Protection) (Notification) Regulations 2013[^f00018]”.
