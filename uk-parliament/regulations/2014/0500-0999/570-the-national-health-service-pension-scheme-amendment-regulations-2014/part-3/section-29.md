# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Amendment of regulation 2.D.6

In sub-paragraph (c) of paragraph (1) of regulation 2.D.6 (increase in pensionable pay following exercise of option under regulation 2.D.5), for “less than 90 per cent” substitute “90 per cent or less”.
