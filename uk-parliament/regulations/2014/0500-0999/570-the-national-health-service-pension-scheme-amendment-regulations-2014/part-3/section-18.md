# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Interpretation of Part 3

In this Part, an alphanumerical reference to a regulation is a reference to a provision of the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008[^f00016] bearing that designation.
