# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendment of regulation 2.C.1

For sub-paragraph (b)[^f00023] of paragraph (6) of regulation 2.C.1 (contributions by members), substitute—

> - (b) by deduction from any benefit payable to, or in respect of, the member where the Secretary of State has notified the member of an intention to do so.

.
