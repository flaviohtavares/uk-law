# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Amendment of regulation 3.A.9

In paragraph (3) of regulation 3.A.9 (calculating pensionable earnings of medical practitioners in partnership), for “registered medical practitioner” substitute “type 1 medical practitioner”.
