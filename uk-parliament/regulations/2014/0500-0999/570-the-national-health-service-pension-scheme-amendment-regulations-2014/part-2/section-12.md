# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Amendment of regulation T2A

(1) Regulation T2A (deduction of tax: further provisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (8A)[^f00009]—

- (a) after “to the 2011 Act” insert “or paragraph 1 of Schedule 22 to the Finance Act 2013”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), after “Regulations 2011” insert “or the Registered Pension Schemes and Relieved Non-UK Pension Schemes (Lifetime Allowance Transitional Protection) (Notification) Regulations 2013[^f00010]”.

(3) In paragraph (9), after “paragraph (8)” insert “or (8A)”.

(4) In paragraph (10), after “paragraph (8)” insert “or (8A)”.
