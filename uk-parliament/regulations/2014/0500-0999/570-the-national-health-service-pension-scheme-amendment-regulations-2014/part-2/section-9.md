# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of regulation E3A

After sub-paragraph (c) of paragraph (2) of regulation E3A (early retirement pension (termination of employment by employing authority))[^f00006], insert—

> - (ca) where the member’s employing authority is an Independent Provider, that the Secretary of State has received the additional contributions referred to in regulation D2 in respect of that member;

.
