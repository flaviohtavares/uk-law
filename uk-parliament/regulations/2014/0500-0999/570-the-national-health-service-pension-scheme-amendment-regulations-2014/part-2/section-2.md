# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation of Part 2

In this Part, an alphanumerical reference to a regulation or a reference to a Schedule is a reference to a provision of the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995[^f00002] bearing that designation.
