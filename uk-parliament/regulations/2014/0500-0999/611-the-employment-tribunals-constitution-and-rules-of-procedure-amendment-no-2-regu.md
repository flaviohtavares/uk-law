# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 611

Employment Tribunals

The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2014

Made

12th March 2014

Laid before Parliament

14th March 2014

Coming into force

6th April 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7(1) and (3)(j), and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 6th April 2014.

## 2. Amendment to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013

In regulation 14 of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013[^f00002] omit paragraph (2).

Jenny Willott

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

12th March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013. The Regulations remove the requirement for the Ministry of Justice to delete Employment Tribunal judgments from its records after six years.

A full impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
