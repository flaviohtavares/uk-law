# The HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 (Commencement No.2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 797 (C. 31)

Road Traffic

The HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 (Commencement No.2) Order 2014

Made

24th March 2014

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(1) of the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 (Commencement No.2) Order 2014.

## 2. Commencement of Provisions

Insofar as not already in force, the remainder of the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 comes into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2014.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Goodwill

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

24th March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Insofar as not already in force, this Order commences the remaining provisions of the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013.
