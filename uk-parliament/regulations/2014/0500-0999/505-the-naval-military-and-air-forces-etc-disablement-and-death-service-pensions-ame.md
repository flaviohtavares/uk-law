# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 505

Pensions

The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2014

Made

5th March 2014

Laid before Parliament

12th March 2014

Coming into force

*7*th April 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 5th day of March 2014

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty considers it expedient to amend the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006[^f00001].

ACCORDINGLY, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 12(1) and 24(3) of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling Her to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 7th April 2014.

## 2. Amendment to the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006

(1) The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 (“the principal Order”) is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1 to the principal Order—

- (a) for the Table in Part 2 substitute the Table set out in Schedule 1 to this Order;
- (b) for Tables 1 and 2 in Part 3 substitute Tables 1 and 2 set out in Schedule 2 to this Order;

  and
- (c) for the Table in Part 4 substitute the Table set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.

(3) In Schedule 2 to the principal Order—

- (a) for Tables 1 to 7 of Part 2 substitute Tables 1 to 7 set out in Schedule 4 to this Order; and
- (b) for the Table in Part 3 substitute the Table set out in Schedule 5 to this Order.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 which makes provision for pensions and other awards in respect of disablement or death due to service before 6th April 2005 in the naval, military and air forces.

Article 2 substitutes Tables in Schedules 1 and 2 thereby varying the rates of retired pay, pensions, gratuities and allowances in respect of disablement or death due to service in the armed forces.
