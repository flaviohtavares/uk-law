> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Notices, notifications and applications[^key-a2553bbba0e18a4c196b0a8142bc3c49]

*Extent: E+W+S*

All notices, notifications and applications under these Regulations—

- (a) must be in writing; and
- (b) may be transmitted by electronic means.
