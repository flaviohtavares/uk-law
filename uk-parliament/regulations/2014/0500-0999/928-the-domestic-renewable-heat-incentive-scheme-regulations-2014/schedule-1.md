# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Standards relevant to plants

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 4 to 6

**1.**[^c-19c287e985dcba8a340fc36a95692ec2] (1) This Schedule specifies standards for plants.

(2) The standards for biomass boilers are: BS EN 303-5:2012, BS EN 16510-1:2018,[^c-b798b89c7c356a2d16cae1565f6237dd] EN 303-5:2012[^f00026], EN 12809:2001+A1:2004[^f00027] or EN 303-5:1999[^f00028].

(3) The standard for biomass stoves is EN 14785:2006[^f00029].

(4) The standards for heat pumps are—

- (za)[^c-e3680b7f7a683a6c26ca7f652f992f7d] BS EN 14511-1:2018, BS EN 14511-2:2018, BS EN 14511-3:2018 and BS EN 14511-4:2018;
- (a) EN 14511-1: 2013[^f00030], EN 14511-2: 2013[^f00031], EN 14511-3:2013[^f00032] and EN 14511-4: 2013[^f00033];
- (b) EN 14511-1: 2011[^f00034], EN 14511-2: 2011[^f00035], EN 14511-3: 2011[^f00036] and EN 14511-4: 2011[^f00037];
- (c) EN 14511-1: 2007[^f00038], EN 14511-2: 2007[^f00039], EN 14511-3: 2007[^f00040] and EN 14511-4: 2007[^f00041]; or
- (d) EN 14511-1: 2004[^f00042], EN 14511-2: 2004[^f00043], EN 14511-3: 2004[^f00044] and EN 14511-4: 2004[^f00045].

(5) The standards for solar thermal plants are—

- (a) BS EN ISO 9806:2017,[^c-ce41e41dd54afd130384b09370b8e229] EN 12975-1:2006+A1:2010[^f00046] and EN 12975-2:2006[^f00047];
- (b) BS EN ISO 9806:2017,[^c-a0fbf01161de6b65fd6ef71b44d5c340] EN 12975-1:2006+A1:2010[^f00048] and EN ISO 9806:2013[^f00049]; or
- (c) BS EN 12976-1:2017, BS EN 12976-2:2017, BS EN 12976-2:2019,[^c-376ad45fc1d9fe45144625723fab0691] EN 12976-1:2006[^f00050] and EN 12976-2:2006[^f00051].
