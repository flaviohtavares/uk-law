# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 47A

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47A. Replacement parts[^c-2849ebbc666cf78c5cb794a217f0decb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A replacement part must use the same source of energy as the original part.

(2) In this regulation, “**replacement part**” means any part of an accredited domestic plant which—

- (a) uses a source of energy; and
- (b) is replaced during repair work.
