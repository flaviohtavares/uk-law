# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 6

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Requirements for solar thermal plants[^c-042a03138e1f4f43d15c0ead384855e1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the plant is a solar thermal plant, the applicable requirements referred to in regulation 3(a) are that it—

- (a)[^c-a576199158cddc7090bd734a9a24ec69] is designed and installed to provide heating—
  - (i) solely to a single eligible property, or to both a single eligible property and any related property; and
  - (ii) solely for an eligible purpose, using liquid as a medium for delivering that heat;
- (b) meets the requirements set out in whichever of the standards for solar thermal plants specified in paragraph 1(5)(a), (b) and (c)[^c-bf328a37dbfa629343f8077f5d6fab3b] of Schedule 1 are relevant to the plant and are applicable on the plant’s first commissioning date;
- (c) cannot also be used to generate electricity.
