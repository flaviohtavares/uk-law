# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Plants used to generate heat before the first commissioning date[^key-28aa7c9ebd411d4b3549e34d94711794]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The requirements referred to in regulation 3(b) are that no part of the plant which generates heat, other than any of the components listed in paragraph (2), was used before the plant’s first commissioning date.

(2) The components referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a)[^key-dadcc8ff3b529f94b80967878de2b517] components, apart from solar thermal collectors (liquid filled flat plate or evacuated tubes), which solely generate heat for the purpose of heating domestic hot water;
- (b) supplementary electric heaters; and
- (c) circulation pumps.
