# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Requirement that plant is not an accredited RHI installation[^c-6360974018cf0b5e12f88fb8a3b065d4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The requirements referred to in regulation 3(b) are that the plant is not, and has not been at any time, an accredited RHI installation.
