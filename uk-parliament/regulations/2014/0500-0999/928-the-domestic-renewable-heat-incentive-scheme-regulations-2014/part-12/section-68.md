# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 68

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68. Duty to publish guidance[^c-5fe752df05ea88d60dced659dfedd92d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Authority must publish procedural guidance to participants and RIs[^c-145e5ac175f408739345d2e6c093eebc] in connection with the administration of the domestic RHI scheme.
