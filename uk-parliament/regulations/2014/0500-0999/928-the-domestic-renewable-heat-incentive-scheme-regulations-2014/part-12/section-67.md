# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 67

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Duty to maintain a central register[^c-c9ae063e21218dc98088e079fe255037]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Authority must maintain a register of—

- (a) all accredited domestic plants;
- (b) all registered metering and monitoring agreements ;[^c-f7cad33a0daf5d707c7a8be851069ae6]
- (c) all RIs.
