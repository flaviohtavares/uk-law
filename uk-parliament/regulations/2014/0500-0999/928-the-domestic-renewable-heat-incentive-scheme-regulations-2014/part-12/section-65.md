# The Domestic Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2014, section 65

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Provision of information to the Authority[^key-bf49432487f0a65be9ec55a789bf112c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Authority requests any information or declarations from an applicant, participant, investor or RI[^key-9b99cfbfd446ddc9317bc11b313bb598] under these Regulations, or an applicant, participant, investor or RI[^key-ac9571e2ab17537f17cc640fffe49637] is otherwise required to provide any information or declarations—

- (a) that information and those declarations must be provided in such manner and form as the Authority may reasonably request and must be accurate to the best of that person’s[^key-47f311cdf08bc609b80cd132ab09fb4a] knowledge and belief; and
- (b) the costs of providing the information are to be borne by that person[^key-51f8988c19d617d657514bdd16b2f45f].
