# The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) (Amendment) Order 2014

> Text as at 2014-03-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

Agricultural Employment, England

Landlord And Tenant, England

Terms And Conditions Of Employment, England

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) (Amendment) Order 2014

Made

26th March 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 103(3) and (4) of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) (Amendment) Order 2014.

## 2. Amendments of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 3 (provisions coming into force on 31st March 2014)—
  - (i) omit “sections 13 and 14 of, and”,
  - (ii) after “Schedule 1 to” omit the comma;
- (b) after article 3 insert—

  > **3A. Provisions coming into force on 30th September 2014**
  >
  > Section 72(4) of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 20 to, the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 so far as relating to sections 13 and 14 of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948 come into force in relation to England on 30th September 2014.

  .

George Eustice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

26th March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2979 (C. 122)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2979)).

Article 2 amends the provision which was to bring into force on 31st March 2014 in relation to England the repeal of provisions in the Agricultural Wages Act 1948 (c. 47 (11 &12 Geo 6)) relating to the laying of annual reports to Parliament and expenses of or authorised by the Minister. The effect of this amendment is to defer this repeal until 30th September 2014 except in relation to the provision for payment of members of the Agricultural Wages Board which is unaffected by this amendment.
