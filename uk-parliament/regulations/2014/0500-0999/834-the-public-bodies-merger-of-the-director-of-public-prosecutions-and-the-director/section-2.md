# The Public Bodies (Merger of the Director of Public Prosecutions and the Director of Revenue and Customs Prosecutions) Order 2014, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the CPS” means the Crown Prosecution Service;
- “the CRCA 2005” means the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005[^f00002];
- “the DPP” means the Director of Public Prosecutions;
- “the DRCP” means the Director of Revenue and Customs Prosecutions;
- “the POA 1985” means the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985[^f00003];
- “the RCPO” means the Revenue and Customs Prosecutions Office.
