# The Public Bodies (Merger of the Director of Public Prosecutions and the Director of Revenue and Customs Prosecutions) Order 2014, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Assignment of functions

An assignment of a function to the DRCP by order under section 31(1) of the Borders, Citizenship and Immigration Act 2009[^f00007] (power to assign prosecution functions relating to criminal investigations by designated customs officials etc) is to be treated after the coming into force of this Order as if it were an assignment to the DPP under section 3(2)(g)[^f00008] of the POA 1985.
