# The Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees (Amendment) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 878 (L. 21)

Tribunals And Inquiries, England And Wales

The Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees (Amendment) Order 2014

Made

27th March 2014

Laid before Parliament

1st April 2014

Coming into force

22nd April 2014

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001], with the consent of the Treasury and having consulted in accordance with section 42(5) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees (Amendment) Order 2014 and comes into force on 22nd April 2014.

## 2. Amendment to the Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees Order 2011

(1) The Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees Order 2011[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In the table in Schedule 1 (fees to be take in immigration judicial review proceedings)—

- (a) at fee 1.1 in the right hand column, for “£60” substitute “£140”;
- (b) at fee 1.1(a) in the right hand column, for “£215” substitute “£350”;
- (c) in the note to fee 1.1(a) for “fee 1.2 is not payable.” substitute “the amount payable under fee 1.2 is £350.”;
- (d) at fee 1.2 in the right hand column, for “£215” substitute “£700”;
- (e) at fee 1.3 in the right hand column, for “£60” substitute “£140”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2344](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2344)).

It increases the fees payable on starting judicial review proceedings in the Upper Tribunal.

If the fee has been paid for a request to reconsider at a hearing a decision on permission to bring a judicial review and permission is subsequently granted at a hearing, only half of the judicial review fee is then payable.

A full impact assessment of the effects of these fee increases can be found at https://consult.justice.gov.uk/digital-communications/court-fees-proposals-for-reform.
