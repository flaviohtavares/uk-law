# The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 2370 (C. 101)

Defence

The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

Made

3rd September 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 50(1) and (2) of the Defence Reform Act 2014[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014.

**2.** In this Order “the Act” means the Defence Reform Act 2014.

## 3. Commencement

Section 46 of the Act and Schedule 7 to the Act come into force on 5th September 2014.

**4.** The following provisions of the Act come into force on 1st October 2014—

- (a) section 44;
- (b) section 45 and Schedule 6;
- (c) section 47; and
- (d) section 48.

Julian Brazier

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

3rd September 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Defence Reform Act [2014 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/20) relating to reserve forces. Section 46 and Schedule 7 are brought into force on 5th September 2014. The provisions specified in Article 4 are brought into force on 1st October 2014.
