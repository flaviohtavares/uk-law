# The Special Educational Needs (Code of Practice) (Appointed Day) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 2254

Education, England

The Special Educational Needs (Code of Practice) (Appointed Day) Order 2014

Made

22nd August 2014

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by sections 78(6) of the Children and Families Act 2014[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Special Educational Needs (Code of Practice) (Appointed Day) Order 2014.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the “Special Educational Needs and Disability Code of Practice: 0 to 25 years” is 1st September 2014.

Edward Timpson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

22nd August 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st September 2014 as the day on which the Special Educational Needs and Disability Code of Practice: 0 to 25 years comes into force. The Special Educational Needs and Disability Code of Practice is issued by the Secretary of State under section 77 of the Children and Families Act 2014 and provides guidance on the exercise of functions under Part 3 of that Act.

The Code of Practice is available at www.gov.uk/government/publications/send-code-of-practice-0-to-25 .

No impact assessment has been prepared for this Order as there is no impact on the private and voluntary sector.
