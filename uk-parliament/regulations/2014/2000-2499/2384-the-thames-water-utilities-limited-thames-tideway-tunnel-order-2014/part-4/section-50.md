# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 50

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Deemed marine licence[^c-ca49b8a43e2b2613d54416f5661b5810]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker is granted a deemed marine licence under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00032] to carry out the activities specified in Part 1 of Schedule 15 (*deemed marine licence*), subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule 15.
