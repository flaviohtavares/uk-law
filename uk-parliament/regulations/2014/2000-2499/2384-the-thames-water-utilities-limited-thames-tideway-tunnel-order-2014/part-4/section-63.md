# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 63

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Arbitration[^key-9d3d27d8269928ab014283c3b84381c6]

*Extent: E+W*

Any difference under any provision of this Order (other than a difference which falls to be determined by the tribunal) shall, unless otherwise provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed between the parties, be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by—

- (a) in the case of matter pertaining to land and surveying of such land, the President of the Royal Institute of Chartered Surveyors;
- (b) in the case of matters pertaining to archaeology, the President of the Institute of Field Archaeologists;
- (c) in the case of matters of legal interpretation, the President of the Law Society;
- (d) in the case of all other matters, the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
