# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 55

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Removal of consent requirements[^c-703827d9c6134718b7e886e8a761deb1]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 18 *(removal of consent requirements)* to this Order, which makes provision for the removal of consent requirements under Section 150 of the 2008 Act in relation to the provisions of this Order and otherwise for the purposes of this Order, shall have effect.
