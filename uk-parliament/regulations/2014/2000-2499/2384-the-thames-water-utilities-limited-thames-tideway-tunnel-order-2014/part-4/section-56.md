## 56. Application, disapplication and modification of legislative provisions[^key-f1b855c0c591331de3782bb0caa086c1]

*Extent: E+W*

Part 1 of Schedule 19 *(miscellaneous controls)* to this Order, which makes provision applying, modifying and excluding statutory provisions which relate to matters for which provision may be made by this Order, has effect.
