# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 61

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Certification of plans etc[^key-ba844ec9b8473adb1d2fbaa18efdac0b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit to the Secretary of State copies of—

- (a) the land plans;
- (b) the access plans;
- (c) the works plans and sections;
- (d) the approved plans;
- (e) the book of reference; and
- (f) the environmental statement,

for certification that they are true copies of the plans or documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
