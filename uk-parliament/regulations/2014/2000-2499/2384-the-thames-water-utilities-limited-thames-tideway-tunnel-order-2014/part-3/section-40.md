# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 40

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-5faab90354046e0a946ab3b810185f13]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00027] (*minerals*) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
