# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Thames Tideway Tunnel) Order 2014, section 8

> Text as at 2022-11-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Benefit of Order[^key-f07b5552715b81d72498132f8e5a5be2]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to article 9 (transfer of benefit of Order), the provisions of this Order shall have effect solely for the benefit of Thames Water Utilities Limited (save where the context requires otherwise) and any successor under a special administration order or otherwise.
