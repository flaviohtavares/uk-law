## 5. Maintenance of authorised project[^c-18cb8ead0a2dae8230fea1f155d413c7]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised project, except to the extent that this Order provides otherwise, and for the avoidance of doubt the power of maintenance provided by this article does not constitute the grant of development consent for any development not authorised by article 3 (development consent etc. granted by this order).
