# The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 7 and Amendment) Order 2014

> Text as at 2014-09-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2014 No. 2481

Agricultural Employment, England

Landlord And Tenant, England

Terms And Conditions Of Employment, England

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 7 and Amendment) Order 2014

Made

14th September 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 100 and 103(3) and (4) of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 7 and Amendment) Order 2014.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on the date after the day on which this Order is made

Section 72(4) of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 20 to, the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (consequential provision) so far as relating to the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00002] come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## 3. Amendment of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2013

In article 4(1) of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2013[^f00003], omit sub-paragraph (b).

## 4. Amendment of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013

For article 3A of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013[^f00004] substitute—

> **3A. Provisions coming into force on 31st December 2014**
>
> Section 72(4) of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 20 to, the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 so far as relating to sections 13 and 14 of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948[^f00005] come into force in relation to England on 31st December 2014.

.

George Eustice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the seventh Commencement Order made under the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act [2013 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/24) (“the Act”).

This Order brings into force a consequential amendment made by the Act. In addition, it amends the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1455 (C. 55)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1455)) (“CO No. 1”) and the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2979 (C. 122)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2979)) (“CO No. 4”), both of which have been the subject of previous amendments.

Article 2 brings into force provisions of the Act which amend the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012 ([S.S.I. 2012/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/88)).

Article 3 makes an amendment to CO No. 1 to revoke the saving of the Agricultural Wages (England and Wales) Order 2012 in relation to Wales. This is now unnecessary as a result of the saving made by section 12(1) of the Agricultural Sector (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw 6).

Article 4 makes an amendment to CO No. 4 regarding a provision which was to bring into force on 30th September 2014 in relation to England the repeal of provisions of the Agricultural Wages Act [1948 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1948/47) relating to the making of annual reports to Parliament and expenses of, or authorised by, the Minister. The amendment defers this repeal until 31st December 2014.
