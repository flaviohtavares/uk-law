# The Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014, section 104

> Text as at 2026-06-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Effect of transfers out[^c-ba6c266177f9151817ed8ab5a231fa8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
