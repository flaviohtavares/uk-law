> Text as at 2026-06-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Establishment of an active member’s account[^key-af1285ef2be3d71f30e75a332288ad91]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A pension account must be established for each active member (“the active member’s account”) from the person’s first day.
