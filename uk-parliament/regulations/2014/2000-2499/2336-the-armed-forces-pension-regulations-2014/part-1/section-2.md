# The Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014, section 2

> Text as at 2026-06-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Establishment of scheme[^key-26eb797a9f74abab113f250458999438]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c4c0dd787766aa2ac2372eac79b629bf] These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits in respect of a person’s service in the armed forces.

(2)[^key-24005f966d801a2618f5accc6bc8c289] The Schedule (remediable service) makes provision about remediable service.
