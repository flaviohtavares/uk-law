# The Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014, section 50

> Text as at 2026-06-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^key-edc6df5fb0d2aedc584998d0bfd18b8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this scheme are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 of the WRPA 1999.
