> Text as at 2026-06-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^c-82fe4993fd93c3d0834dc2136c9f05b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager must provide an annual benefit information statement to each active member of this scheme in accordance with Treasury directions made under section 14 of the Act (information about benefits).
