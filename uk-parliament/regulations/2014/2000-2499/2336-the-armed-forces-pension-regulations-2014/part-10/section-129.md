# The Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014, section 129

> Text as at 2026-06-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. General prohibition on unauthorised payments[^c-fe2c47dc794dd8a3802e2bf181f57ec4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of the FA 2004 (see section 160(5) of that Act).
