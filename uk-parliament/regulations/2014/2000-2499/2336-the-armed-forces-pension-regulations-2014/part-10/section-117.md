> Text as at 2026-06-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Scheme administrator for purposes of the Finance Act 2004[^key-6d712f79a68cce1cc36cbb2b9bc1f4c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on a scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of the FA 2004.

(2) In this regulation ‘scheme administrator’ has the meaning given in Part 4 of the FA 2004.
