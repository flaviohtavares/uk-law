> Text as at 2026-06-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Late payment of retirement index adjustment[^key-d855d85d318f68569c1dd3b03f2c3864]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment that is payable in respect of the last active scheme year to be paid before the end of that year.
