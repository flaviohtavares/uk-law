# The Control of Noise (Code of Practice on Noise from Audible Intruder Alarms) (Revocation) (England) Order 2014

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

2014 No. 2123

Public Health, England

The Control of Noise (Code of Practice on Noise from Audible Intruder Alarms) (Revocation) (England) Order 2014

Made

29th July 2014

Laid before Parliament

7th August 2014

Coming into force

1st October 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 71(3) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation, application and commencement

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Control of Noise (Code of Practice on Noise from Audible Intruder Alarms) (Revocation) (England) Order 2014;
- (b) applies in England; and
- (c) comes into force on 1st October 2014.

## 2. Revocation

The Control of Noise (Code of Practice on Noise from Audible Intruder Alarms) Order 1981[^f00002] is revoked.

Dan Rogerson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

29th July 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Control of Noise (Code of Practice on Noise from Audible Intruder Alarms) Order 1981 ([S.I. 1981/1829](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/1829)) in relation to England.

An impact assessment has not been produced as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
