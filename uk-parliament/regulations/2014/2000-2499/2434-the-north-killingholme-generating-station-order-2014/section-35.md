# The North Killingholme (Generating Station) Order 2014, section 35

> Text as at 2026-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Arbitration[^c-aec7162f1823f1d18b45ed323ab02942]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any difference under provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for, shall be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
