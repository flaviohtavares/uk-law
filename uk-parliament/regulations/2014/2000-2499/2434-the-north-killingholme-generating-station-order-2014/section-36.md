# The North Killingholme (Generating Station) Order 2014, section 36

> Text as at 2026-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Deemed marine licence[^key-4e0e797dce98223bd489b53e00da897d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker is deemed to be granted a licence under section 66 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00015] to carry out the works described in Schedule 7, subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 3 of that Schedule.
