# The Closure of Prisons (No. 2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 2340

Prisons, England And Wales

The Closure of Prisons (No. 2) Order 2014

Made

28th August 2014

Laid before Parliament

3rd September 2014

Coming into force

28th September 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(1) of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (No. 2) Order 2014 and comes into force on 28th September 2014.

## 2. Closure of prison

HM Prison The Verne, in Dorset, is closed.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Andrew Selous

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

28th August 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

HM Prison The Verne was closed on 31st January 2014 by [S.I. 2014/3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3). On 18th March 2014, a declaration was made under section 33(2)(a) of the Prison Act [1952 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1952/52) to re-open The Verne as a prison. This Order provides for its closure on 28th September 2014.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
