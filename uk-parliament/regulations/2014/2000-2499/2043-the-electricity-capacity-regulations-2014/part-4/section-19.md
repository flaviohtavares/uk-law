# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 19

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Regulations 16 and 17: interpretation[^c-484e93f657259db6d313bd07ee10a8e6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulations 16 and 17[^c-295c2127afba58905b377806bfaefbc8], “**delivery period**” means the delivery year or period of delivery years for which a capacity obligation would be awarded in respect of a CMU (“CMU i”) if a bid in respect of CMU i were accepted at the capacity auction for which the applicant is applying for prequalification.
