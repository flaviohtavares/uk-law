# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 32

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Termination fee rates

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation—

- “TFx[^key-651bcd76628ea11a24b038ad73df81e4]<sub>rate</sub>” (for any value of x from x = 1 to x = 9[^key-df67bf0c1c5ddb929aaaa007a7befff4]) means the rate at which a termination fee is payable by a capacity provider if—
  - (a) a capacity agreement is terminated on a ground specified in capacity market rules; and
  - (b) capacity market rules specify that TFx[^key-bac5a2bc8b1a7261a6db5c619b986d95] is payable in the event of the capacity agreement being terminated on that ground;
- [^key-779c43dfaf5ba30cafa65499e0abfa85]...[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2043/images/uksi_20142043_en_002)

(2)[^key-774580af6751649c9f595aa30de70936] Those rates are as follows—

- TF1<sub>rate</sub> is £5,000/MW;
- TF2<sub>rate</sub> is £25,000/MW;
- TF3<sub>rate</sub> is £10,000/MW;
- TF4<sub>rate</sub> is £15,000/MW;
- TF5<sub>rate</sub> is £35,000/MW

- TF6[^key-ace8509688aa4dc7b4be4df4a7aced8f]<sub>rate</sub> is £6,500/MW;
- TF7<sub>rate</sub> is £13,000/MW;
- TF8<sub>rate</sub> is £19,500/MW;
- TF9<sub>rate</sub> is £45,500/MW.

(4) References in this regulation to a rate expressed as £/MW are to that amount in pounds per MW of de-rated capacity [^key-1f9e4df45ebb2c8765466d8ae2041090]... for which the capacity agreement is issued, as specified in the capacity market register.

(5)[^key-560aa4af0ac07e3202dbf62143e0ede6] This regulation applies to the termination of a transferred part as it applies to the termination of a capacity agreement; but in that case, the reference in paragraph (4) to the de-rated capacity [^key-1f9e4df45ebb2c8765466d8ae2041090]... for which the capacity agreement is issued is to be construed as a reference to the capacity obligation comprised in the transferred part.
