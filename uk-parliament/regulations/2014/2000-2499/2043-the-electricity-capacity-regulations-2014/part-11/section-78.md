# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 78

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 78. Objectives[^M_I_dd0f0e40-125b-4590-a42e-740b20cfd320]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Authority must when making capacity market rules, in addition to having regard to its principal objective and general duties, have regard to the following objectives—

- (a) promoting investment in capacity to ensure security of electricity supply;
- (b) facilitating the efficient operation and administration of the capacity market;
- (c) ensuring the compatibility of capacity market rules with other subordinate legislation under Part 2 of the Act.
