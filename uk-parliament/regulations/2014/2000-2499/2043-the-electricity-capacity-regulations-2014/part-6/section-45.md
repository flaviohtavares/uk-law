# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 45

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. General

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Chapter—

- “**draw down**” has the same meaning as it has in Part 7;
- “**in default**” means a failure to pay in full an invoiced amount by the end of[^c-ca2ebbd9a9dcdd1ff45c97540e7d1e35] the payment due date;
- “**invoiced amount**” means the total amount payable by a payer as stated in the invoice issued to that payer under regulation 41, 43 [^c-d85ac64f4ddd05550418097bd788c275]...[^c-447db6387fe40bcad5d8a2a83d2f9445] or 49A;
- “**payer**” , subject to regulation 47(7),[^c-4325780a37f67c4c363648d8b21662a6] means a person to whom an invoice is issued under regulation 41, 43 [^c-97115d02e7eacdc3cabd9f3575ffef39]...[^c-79720865fffca15c34ec5d92e1027c31] or 49A;
- “**payment due date**” means the day specified in an invoice in accordance with regulation 38(2) as the date by which it is to be paid.
