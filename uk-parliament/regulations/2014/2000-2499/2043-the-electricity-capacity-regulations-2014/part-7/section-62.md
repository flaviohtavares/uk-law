# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 62

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Effect of non-compliance

*Extent: E+W+S*

A may not bid in a capacity auction in respect of a CMU for which applicant credit cover is required, if A has not complied with this Part in relation to the provision of applicant credit cover in respect of that CMU.
