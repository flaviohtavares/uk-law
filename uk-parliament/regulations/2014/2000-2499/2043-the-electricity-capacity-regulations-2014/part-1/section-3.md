# The Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014, section 3

> Text as at 2026-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. “Providing electricity”; “reducing demand for electricity”; “electricity supplier”

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of section 27 of the Act—

- (a) “**providing electricity**” means providing any metered electrical output by a generating unit or through an electricity interconnector[^key-996847682ecb9cf0a85c2381ae51d1a3], and includes, in particular, providing such output by a generating unit which forms part of a storage facility; and
- (b) “**reducing demand for electricity**” means—
  - (i) providing demand side response; or
  - (ii) permanent electricity demand reduction.

(2) For the purposes of [^key-78288ed6174189ebce55cd95d32930da]... these Regulations, “**electricity supplier**” means a person supplying electricity to premises[^key-0f0c5702f088762c63b38c6e7a7f3226] in Great Britain under a licence granted or treated as granted under section 6(1)(d) of EA 1989 [^c22186141].
