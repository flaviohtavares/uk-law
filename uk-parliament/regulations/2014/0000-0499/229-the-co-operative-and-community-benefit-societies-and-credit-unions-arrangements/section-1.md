# The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions (Arrangements, Reconstructions and Administration) Order 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions (Arrangements, Reconstructions and Administration) Order 2014[^key-c94b8d0990ab05650789878ad9187b58], and comes into force on 6th April 2014.

(2) In this Order—

- [^key-c04147d843fbf777550ed66020c6ec26]...
- [^key-c04147d843fbf777550ed66020c6ec26]...
- “**the 1986 Act**” means the Insolvency Act 1986 [^c22008771] (see also paragraph (5))[^key-303dc390b8544f6a61c0478fe903f33c];
- “**the 2006 Act**” means the Companies Act 2006 [^c22008781];
- “the[^key-ef18c109681ed344dc3d84a8c42a4cf2] 2014 Act” means the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014;
- “the 2015 Act” means the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015;[^key-1ea33bde4cfd743b760c4dd5a6565354]
- “**authorised person**” has the meaning given in section 31(2) of FSMA;
- “**authorised deposit taker**” has the meaning given in section 359(4) of FSMA [^c22008791];
- [^key-9ab499aae5032b79f054e17921478b7d]...
- “**deposit**” has the meaning given by article 5 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 [^c22008801];
- [^key-9ab499aae5032b79f054e17921478b7d]...
- “**FSMA**” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c22008811];
- “**member**”, in relation to a relevant society, means a person whose name is entered as a member in the register kept by the society in accordance with section 30(1) of the[^key-273ec36e88899f02c6025196618dec38] 2014 Act;
- [^key-9ab499aae5032b79f054e17921478b7d]...
- “relevant CCBS” means a co-operative society or community benefit society, that is registered under the 2014 Act but not a society that is—[^key-9537b85ceb838efc480ca221633c7238]
  - (a) registered as a social landlord under Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996 or Part 2 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010; or
  - (b) a credit union within the meaning of section 31(1) of the Credit Unions Act 1979;
- “**relevant person**” has the meaning given in section 213(9)(a) of FSMA [^c22008831]; and
- “**relevant society**” means a registered society which[^key-ae7d042685fa8febc36dfe61b0b1e853] is not—
  - (a) a private registered provider of social housing; or
  - (b) registered as a social landlord under Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996 [^c22008841] or under Part 2 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010 [^c22008851].

(3) The definition of “**authorised deposit taker**” is to be construed in accordance with—

- (a) section 22 of, and Schedule 2 to, FSMA [^c22008861]; and
- (b) any relevant order under section 22 [^c22008871].

(4) For the purposes of this Order a relevant society is “in administration” while the appointment of an administrator of the society under Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act [^c22008881] has effect.

(5)[^key-842bcc1b00f53e3c073fcca4cfa668b6] In this Order a reference to the 1986 Act is to the 1986 Act without the amendments made by section 1 of, and Schedules 1 to 3 to, the Corporate Insolvency and Governance Act 2020.
