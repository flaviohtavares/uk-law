# The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions (Arrangements, Reconstructions and Administration) Order 2014, section 8

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Application of section 361 of FSMA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 361 of FSMA (administrator's duty to report to FCA and PRA) [^c22008971] applies in relation to a relevant society, and for that purpose a reference to a company includes a reference to a relevant society.

## Annotations

[^c22008971]: Marginal citation: Section 361 was substituted by the Enterprise Act 2002, Schedule 17, paragraphs 53 and 56; and was amended by the Financial Services Act 2012, Schedule 14, paragraphs 1 and 7. There are other amendments not relevant to this Order.
