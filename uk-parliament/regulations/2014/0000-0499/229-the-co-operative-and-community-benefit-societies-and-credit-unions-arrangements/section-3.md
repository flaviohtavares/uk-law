# The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions (Arrangements, Reconstructions and Administration) Order 2014, section 3

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Application of section 176A of the 1986 Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 176A of the 1986 Act (share of assets for unsecured creditors) [^c22008901] applies in relation to a relevant society which is in administration, and for that purpose—

- (a) a reference to a company includes a reference to a relevant society;
- (b) a reference to a receiver is to be ignored; and
- (c) in subsection (4)(b) [^c22008911] the reference to Part 26 of the 2006 Act is a reference to that Part as applied in relation to a relevant society.
