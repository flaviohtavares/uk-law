# The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions (Arrangements, Reconstructions and Administration) Order 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Modified application of section 126 of the 2014 Act[^c-32a265c75dab5bff37ba374884a5b186]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 126 of the 2014 Act (dissolution etc to occur only after society’s property dealt with) has effect in relation to a relevant society which is in administration as if in subsection (3) the reference to the liquidator included a reference to the administrator.
