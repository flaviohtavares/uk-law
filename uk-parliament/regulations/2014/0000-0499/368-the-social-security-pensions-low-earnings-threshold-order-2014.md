# The Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 368

Social Security

The Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2014

Made

19th February 2014

Laid before Parliament

25th February 2014

Coming into force

6th April 2014

In accordance with section 148A(1) (revaluation of low earnings threshold) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001], the Secretary of State has reviewed the general level of earnings obtaining in Great Britain.

It appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of earnings has increased during the review period.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 148A(3) to (5) and 189(1), (4) and (5) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 6th April 2014.

## 2. Low earnings threshold

For the purposes of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00003], it is directed that the low earnings threshold[^f00004] for the tax years following the tax year 2013-2014 shall be £15,100.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Steve Webb

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

19th February 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The low earnings threshold is the amount by reference to which the surplus earnings bands are determined for the purpose of calculating the additional pension (the state second pension) in a state retirement pension.

This Order is made following a review by the Secretary of State under section 148A (revaluation of low earnings threshold) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) of the general level of earnings in Great Britain with a view to determining whether, and if so by how much, the amount of the low earnings threshold for the purposes of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) should be increased for future tax years.

As a result of that review, it appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of earnings during the period from 1st October 2012 to 30th September 2013 has increased by 0.9%.

This Order directs that the low earnings threshold for the tax years following 2013 – 2014 shall be £15,100. The threshold for the tax year 2013 – 2014 was directed to be £15,000 by virtue of the Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/528](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/528)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations.
