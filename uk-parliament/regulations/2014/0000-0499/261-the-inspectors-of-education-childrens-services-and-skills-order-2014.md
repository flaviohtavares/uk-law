# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 261

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2014

Made

11th February 2014

Coming into force

12th February 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 2014

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2014 and comes into force on 12th February 2014.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

(1) The persons named in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

(2) The person named in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order is appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills with effect from 1st April 2014

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
