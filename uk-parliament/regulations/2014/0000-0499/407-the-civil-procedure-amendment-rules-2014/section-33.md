# The Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2014, section 33

> Text as at 2014-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**33.**[^c-a5a1f2fc021f368e4b4adab4b2e6f218] In Part 73—

- (a) in rule 73.3, in subparagraph (2)—
  - (i) after “issued in the court” insert “or County Court hearing centre”; and
  - (ii) in subparagraph (e), for the words from “Northampton” to “designated money claim”, substitute “the County Court Money Claims Centre”; and
- (b) in rule 73.10, in the words in parentheses which follow paragraph (2), for “county court’s” substitute “County Court’s”.
