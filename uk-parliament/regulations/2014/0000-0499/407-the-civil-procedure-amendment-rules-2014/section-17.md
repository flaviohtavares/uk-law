# The Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2014, section 17

> Text as at 2014-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**17.**[^key-bab894621dab3f30e1c731bf03ee9c2a] In rule 42.1—

- (a) before “Where the address”, insert “(1)”;
- (b) for “his” substitute “that party’s”; and
- (c) after what will be paragraph (1), insert—

  > (2) For the purposes of this Part, “**solicitor**” has the meaning set out in rule 6.2(d).
