# The Energy Act 2013 (Office for Nuclear Regulation) (Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2014, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Regulations to be treated as regulations under the 2013 Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 1 which—

- (a) makes provision for existing regulations to be treated as regulations under the 2013 Act, and
- (b) makes transitional modifications to the application of the 2013 Act in consequence of the provisions mentioned in sub-paragraph (a),

has effect.
