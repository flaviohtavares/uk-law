# The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 183 (C. 6)

Co-operative Societies

Community Benefit Societies

Credit Unions

The Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

Made

30th January 2014

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(2) of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 6th April 2014

Section 3 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010 comes into force on 6th April 2014.

## 3. Provision coming into force on 1st August 2014

Section 1 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act 2010 comes into force on 1st August 2014.

David Evennett

Karen Bradley

30th January 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 3 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies and Credit Unions Act [2010 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/7) (the “2010 Act”) into force on 6th April 2014 and section 1 of the 2010 Act into force on 1st August 2014. Section 1 requires that in future societies to be registered under section 1 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act [1965 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1965/12) must be either registered as co-operative societies or as community benefit societies. Section 3 applies the Company Directors Disqualification Act [1986 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/46) to societies registered or deemed to be registered under the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965.
