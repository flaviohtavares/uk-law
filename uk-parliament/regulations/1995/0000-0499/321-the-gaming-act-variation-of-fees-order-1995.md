# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 321

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1995

Made

13th February 1995

Laid before Parliament

20th February 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of the powers conferred on him by sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

(2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

**2.** The provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to fees charged in relation to the matters mentioned in column 2 of that Schedule) shall have effect as if, for the references to the sums specified in those provisions (for which the sums mentioned in column 3 of that Schedule were substituted by the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1992[^f00002]), there were substituted references to the sums specified in column 4 of that Schedule.

**3.** The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1992[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

13th February 1995
