# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Clun) Designation (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 190

AGRICULTURE

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Clun) Designation (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

26th January 1995

Laid before Parliament

7th February 1995

Coming into force

28th February 1995

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food has by order designated an area in the vicinity of Clun as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Clun) Designation (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 28th February 1995.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Clun) Designation Order 1993[^f00003].

## 3. Amendment of the principal Order

The principal Order shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

**4.** In article 2, paragraph 1 of the principal Order, the definition of “managed woodland” shall be deleted.

## 5. Rates of payment under agreement

In article 6 of the principal Order—

(1) in paragraph (1), for the amount “£8”, there shall be substituted the amount “£10”;

- (2) in paragraph (2)—
- (a) the figure “6”, where it first appears, shall be deleted;
- (b) in the table—
  - (i) in the left hand column, the words “Schedule 6” shall be deleted;
  - (ii) in the right hand column, on the corresponding line, the figure “25” shall be deleted;

(3) for paragraph (3), there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> (3) Where an agreement includes a conservation plan, the Minister shall also make payments in respect of the operations included in the plan, subject to a maximum of £20,000 for each agreement.

.

## 6. Amendment to Schedules

(1) Schedule 6 to the principal Order shall be deleted.

(2) In Schedule 8, paragraph 1 to the principal Order—

- (a) the word “traditional”, where it first appears, shall be deleted;
- (b) the words “methods and” shall be deleted.

## 7. Saving

(1) Article 5(1) of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1995.

(2) Articles 4, 5(2) and 6(1) of this Order shall not apply in relation to any agreement made on or before the date on which this Order comes into force.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Clun) Designation Order 1993 (“the principal Order”) which designated an area in the vicinity of Clun as an environmentally sensitive area.

Subject to a saving provision, the Order amends the rates of payments to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 (articles 5(1), 5(3) and 7(1)).

Article 5(2) deletes the references, in article 6(2) of the principal Order, to Schedule 6 of that Order and to the rate of the additional payment to be made where an agreement includes provisions relating to managed woodland in accordance with that Schedule. Accordingly, the definition of “managed woodland” in article 2(1) of the principal Order, and Schedule 6 to the principal Order (ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS— V (MANAGED WOODLAND)) are deleted (articles 4 and 6(1)). These amendments do not apply in relation to agreements made on or before the date on which this Order comes into force (article 7(2)).

The Order makes an amendment to the list of operations, in Schedule 8 to the principal Order, that may be included a “conservation plan” (as defined in article 2(1) of the principal Order) (article 6(2)).
