These Regulations implement Commission Directive [94/15/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0015) (OJ No. L 103, 22.4.94, p.20) adapting to technical progress for the first time Council Directive [90/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0220) on the deliberate release into the environment of genetically modified organisms by amending the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 1992 ([SI 1992/3280](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3280)). Regulation 3 and the Schedule make separate provision for information required in relation to applications to release or market higher plants and that required in relation to applications to release or market other organisms. Regulation 4 amends the provisions relating to applications for consent to release in order to take account of Commission Decision [94/730/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0730) (OJ No. L292, 22.11.94, p.31).

In addition, these Regulations amend the 1992 Regulations with regard to—

- (a) the requirements for advertising applications for consent to release organisms (regulation 5);
- (b) the cases and circumstances in which a marketing consent is required (regulation 6);
- (c) the provisions relating to keeping the public register (regulation 7).
