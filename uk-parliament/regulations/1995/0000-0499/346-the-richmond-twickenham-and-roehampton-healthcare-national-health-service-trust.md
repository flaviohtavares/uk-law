# The Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 346

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

1st February 1995

Laid before Parliament

22nd February 1995

Coming into force

15th March 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities resulting from the establishment of the Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the Special Trustees for Westminster and Roehampton Hospitals and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 15th March 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Special Trustees” means the Special Trustees for Westminster and Roehampton Hospitals;
- “the Trust” means the Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Special Trustees and the Trust on 29th September 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Special Trustees and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 15th March 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Special Trustees to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

15th February 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Richmond, Twickenham and Roehampton Healthcare National Health Service Trust on 15th March 1995 trust property formerly held by the Special Trustees for Westminster and Roehampton Hospitals. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
