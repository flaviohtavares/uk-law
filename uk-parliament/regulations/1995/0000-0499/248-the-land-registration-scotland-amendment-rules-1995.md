# The Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 248 (S.9)

LAND REGISTRATION, SCOTLAND

The Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1995

Made

1st February 1995

Laid before Parliament

15th February 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(1) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the Lord President of the Court of Session, hereby makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

## 2. Amendment of the Land Registration (Scotland) Rules 1980

For Schedule A to the Land Registration (Scotland) Rules 1980[^f00002](forms to be used in connection with registration) there shall be substituted the Schedule A set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

## 3. Revocation

The Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988[^f00003]are revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House Edinburgh

1st February 1995

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules substitute for Schedule A to the Land Registration (Scotland) Rules 1980 a new Schedule setting out amended forms to be used in connection with registration.

The layout of forms 1 to 5 and 8 to 15 has been altered to allow them to be read by computer in anticipation of the introduction of new technology at the Registers of Scotland Executive Agency. Changes have also been made to forms 1 to 15 to take account of legislative changes, including changes in fees, since the forms were last revised, to highlight the requirement, in certain cases involving first registration, of a plan identifying the relevant property on the Ordnance Map and to make other minor administrative improvements.
