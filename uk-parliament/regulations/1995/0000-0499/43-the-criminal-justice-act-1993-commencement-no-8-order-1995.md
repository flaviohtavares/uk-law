# The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 43 (c.3)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1995

Made

11th January 1995

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 78(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 1993[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1995.

**2.** The provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (“the 1993 Act”) referred to in the left–hand column of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters described in the right–hand column of the Schedule) shall come into force on 3rd February 1995.

**3.** (1) So far as relating to the following provisions of the 1993 Act, namely, sections 27 and 28, this Order extends to England and Wales only.

(2) So far as relating to section 24(12) to (15), this Order extends to Scotland only.

(3) So far as relating to the following provisions of the 1993 Act, namely—

- (a) sections 36 to 43; and
- (b) paragraph 3 and paragraph 17(2) to (3) and (6) to (7) of Schedule 5 and Schedule 6 so far as it relates to the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991[^f00002],

this Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

11th January 1995

The sixth Order brought into force certain provisions of that Act on 1st April 1994 for, variously, the United Kingdom, Great Britain, England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The seventh Order brought into force section 72 of that Act on 22nd August 1994 for the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man.
