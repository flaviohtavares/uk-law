# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section M6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## M6. Transfers in respect of more than one member

(1) If two or more members leave pensionable employment, join another occupational pension scheme and exercise a right to transfer to that scheme under regulation M1 (member’s right to transfer or buy-out), the Secretary of State may, after consulting the Government Actuary, make a single transfer payment to that scheme in respect of those members.

(2) The Secretary of State will calculate the amount of any transfer payment under this regulation after taking advice from the Government Actuary.
