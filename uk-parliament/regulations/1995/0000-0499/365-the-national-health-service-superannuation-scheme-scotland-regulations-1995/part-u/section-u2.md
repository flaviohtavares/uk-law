# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section U2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## U2. Determination of questions

The Secretary of State will determine any question concerning any person’s rights or liabilities under these Regulations and his decision shall be final.
