# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section T1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## T1. Claims for benefits

A person claiming to be entitled to benefits under these Regulations shall make a claim in writing to the Secretary of State and shall provide such evidence of entitlement as the Secretary of State may require.
