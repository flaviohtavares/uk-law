# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section T2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## T2. Deduction of tax

The Secretary of State shall be entitled to deduct from any payment under the scheme any tax for which he may be liable in respect of it.
