# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section K3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## K3. Late retirement

If a member’s pension is postponed for more than 7 weeks after state pension age, or is reduced or suspended after it becomes payable, the member’s guaranteed minimum pension as increased under section 109 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (annual increases of guaranteed minimum pensions) will be increased by 1/7th per cent for each complete 7 days of postponement.
