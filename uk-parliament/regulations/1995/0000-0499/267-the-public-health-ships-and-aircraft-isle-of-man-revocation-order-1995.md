# The Public Health (Ships and Aircraft) (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 267

PUBLIC HEALTH

The Public Health (Ships and Aircraft) (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

Made

8th February 1995

Coming into force

1st March 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of February 1995

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 76 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Health (Ships and Aircraft) (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st March 1995.

**2.** The Public Health (Aircraft) (Isle of Man) Order 1982[^f00002] and the Public Health (Ships) (Isle of Man) Order 1982[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Public Health (Aircraft) (Isle of Man) Order 1982 and the Public Health (Ships) (Isle of Man) Order 1982, which extended to the Isle of Man, subject to exceptions and modifications, the Public Health (Aircraft) Regulations 1979 (S.I. [1979/1434](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/1434)) and the Public Health (Ships) Regulations 1979 (S.I. [1979/1435](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/1435)). Those Regulations have been superseded by regulations made under the Public Health Act 1990 (an Act of Tynwald).
