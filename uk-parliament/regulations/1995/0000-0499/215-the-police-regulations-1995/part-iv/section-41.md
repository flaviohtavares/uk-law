# The Police Regulations 1995, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. London weighting

The annual pay of a member of the City of London or metropolitan police force shall be increased by £1,365, but any allowance under these Regulations calculated by reference to a member’s pay, shall be calculated as if this regulation had not been made.
