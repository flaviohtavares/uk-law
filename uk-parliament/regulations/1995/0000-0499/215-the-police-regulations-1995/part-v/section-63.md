# The Police Regulations 1995, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Allowances in respect of periods of suspension

This Part of these Regulations shall have effect in relation to a member of a police force suspended under the Discipline Regulations, subject to the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 7.
