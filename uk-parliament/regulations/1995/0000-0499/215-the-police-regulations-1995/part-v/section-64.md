# The Police Regulations 1995, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. University scholars

This Part of these Regulations shall have effect in relation to a university scholar subject to the provisions of paragraph 4 of Schedule 5.
