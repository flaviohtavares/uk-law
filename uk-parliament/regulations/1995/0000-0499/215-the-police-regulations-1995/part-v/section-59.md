# The Police Regulations 1995, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Allowance in respect of medical charges

(1) A member of a police force, if the charges are incurred by reason of an injury received without his default in the execution of his duty as a constable, shall be reimbursed any charges incurred in his case under section 77, 78 or 79 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00032](which sections relate to charges for certain drugs, medicines and appliances and for dental treatment).

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) “injury”and “injury received in the execution of duty”have the same meanings as they have in the Police Pensions Regulations.
