# The Police Regulations 1995, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Advances to cover expenses when away on duty

Where a member of a police force of or below the rank of chief inspector is required to do duty away from his usual place of duty he shall, if he so requests, be given an advance to cover, as far as practicable, any expenses which he will probably incur.
