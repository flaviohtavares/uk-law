# The Police Regulations 1995, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. London allowance

A member of the City of London or metropolitan police force shall be paid a London allowance at the rate of £1,011 a year.
