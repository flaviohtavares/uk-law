# The Police Regulations 1995, SCHEDULE 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 10 — DOG HANDLER'S ALLOWANCE

Regulation 58

**1.** (1) Subject as hereinafter provided, a dog handler’s allowance shall be payable—

- (a) in the case of a constable, at the annual rate of £957;
- (b) in any other case, at the annual rate of £1,308.

(2) Where the member keeps and cares for at his home more than one dog owned by the police authority, the annual rate of the allowance shall be multiplied by the number of such dogs so kept and cared for.
