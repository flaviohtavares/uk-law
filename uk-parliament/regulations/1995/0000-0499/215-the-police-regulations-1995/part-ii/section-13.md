# The Police Regulations 1995, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Appointment of chief constable

Without prejudice to section 4(2) of the 1964 Act or, on or after 1st April 1995, section 5A(1) of that Act[^f00017]and regulations 11 and 12, no person shall be appointed to the office of chief constable in a police force unless he has at least 2 years' experience in some other force in the rank of superintendent or a higher rank.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: Section 5A(1) was inserted by section 5 of the Police and Magistrates' Courts Act [1994 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/29).
