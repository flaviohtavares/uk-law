# The Police Regulations 1995, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Revocations and savings

(1) The Regulations specified in Part I of Schedule 12 are revoked to the extent specified.

(2) The revocations have effect subject to the savings in Part II of Schedule 12.
