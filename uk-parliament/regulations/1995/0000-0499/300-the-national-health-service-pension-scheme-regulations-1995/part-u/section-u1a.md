## U1A. Determinations by medical practitioners[^key-f77125ac8ef835f6366e1eea33303f4f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-1d7bfd4eeaac49072cd454f4ddd1b4a8] The Secretary of State may make arrangements for his functions under these Regulations in relation to a decision whether a person is—

- (a) permanently incapable of efficiently discharging the duties of his employment because of physical or mental infirmity for the purposes of regulation E2 or E2A[^key-7dc2c0225b09f3053e3e96286b198148];
- (b) incapable of earning a living because of physical or mental infirmity for the purposes of regulation G8(2) [^key-ee70a99054351bb634c3a9a96ce8aa34]...[^key-0509561f3932fecf47f87fafcbc0a70a] or regulation G15(2)(a);
- (c) incapable of earning a living because of permanent physical or mental infirmity for the purposes of regulation H1(6);
- (d) in good health for the purposes of regulation J1(6);
- (e) suffering from mental or physical infirmity that makes him permanently incapable of efficiently discharging the duties of employment or suffering from mental or physical infirmity that makes him permanently incapable of engaging in regular employment for the purposes of regulation L1(3)(a) and (b) respectively,
- (f)[^key-bba3600c21c45ca973dc9502c4d86fc4] permanently incapable of regular employment because of physical or mental infirmity for the purposes of regulation E2A(2)(b)(ii),
- (g)[^key-8ac3ac756de718ffbc7155f4a4911aba] expected to live for less than one year for the purposes of the lump sum rule referred to in regulations E1(4)(b) , E2A(7)(b) and L1(5)(b)[^key-75273366a663a1ecda8dbfcb58c9fccd],
- (h)[^key-dda13425c2b56623ced4b8264faefd39] able to satisfy the severe ill health condition for the purposes of subsection (3)(a) of section 229 of the 2004 Act (total pension input amount),

to be discharged by—[^key-c531c4569a6701f8485b051b1a9952f1]

- (i) a registered medical practitioner (whether practising on his own or as part of a group); or
- (ii) a body corporate or unincorporate employing such practitioners (whether under a contract of service or for services),

approved by the Secretary of State to act on his behalf.

(2)[^key-e7b79fcf79858b083c2ad720cda48ba6] In relation to the decisions referred to in paragraph (1), the Secretary of State may require any person referred to in that paragraph to submit to a medical examination by a registered medical practitioner selected by the Secretary of State, and in that event, the Secretary of State shall—

- (a) also offer the person an opportunity of submitting a report from that person’s own medical adviser as a result of an examination by such an adviser, and
- (b) take that report into consideration together with the report of the medical practitioner selected by the Secretary of State.
