# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section K3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/300
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## K3. Late retirement

*Extent: E+W+S*

If a member’s pension is postponed for more than 7 weeks after guaranteed minimum pension age[^key-057c552f5267bb87a21294967b80d021], or is reduced or suspended after it becomes payable, the member’s guaranteed minimum pension, as increased under section 109 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (annual increases of guaranteed minimum pensions), will be increased by 1/7th per cent. for each complete 7 days of postponement.
