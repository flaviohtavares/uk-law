# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section K1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## K1. Contracting-out conditions to be overriding[^c-e70f9917567c5614bdab5f7dd5c4de74]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Section of the Scheme[^M_F_51d3ac99-7d0e-48ad-dd51-c079ff8d7ef1] will be administered in conformity with the contracting-out conditions and, with the exception of the circumstances specified in paragraph (2), regulations K2 to K7 override any inconsistent provisions of these regulations.

(2) The circumstances referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) if a trivial pension is commuted in accordance with regulation T7; or
- (b) if a pension is commuted in accordance with regulations E2(10), E2A(10) or L1(5) (pensions on early retirement due to ill health and preserved pensions); or
- (c)[^c-cd406a0772d1a32ff49a442b5cddbc0a] if a pension is forfeited for the reasons specified in paragraph (1)(b) or (c) or paragraph (1A) of regulation T6.

(3) In this Part—

- (a)[^c-9a2ffbc77ab76bd66c05291f155db5ae] “contracting-out conditions” means—
  - (i) the conditions specified in section 12E of the 1993 Act; and
  - (ii) the conditions or requirements that apply in respect of a member’s section 9(2B) rights;
- (b) “guaranteed minimum” means the guaranteed minimum as defined in sections 13 to 17 of the 1993 Act;
- (c) “guaranteed minimum pension” is a pension calculated in accordance with the guaranteed minimum requirements;
- (ca)[^c-56766dfb3282460aa225193a2047a141] “guaranteed minimum pension age” means age 65 in the case of a man or age 60 in the case of a woman;
- (d) “protected rights” has the same meaning as in the 1993 Act.
