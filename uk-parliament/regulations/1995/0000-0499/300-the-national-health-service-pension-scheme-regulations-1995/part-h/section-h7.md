# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section H7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/300
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## H7. Dependent child not being maintained by surviving parent or spouse or civil partner or scheme partner of a parent[^c-f61c9a333ac94cdc5e4cb9f883a955de][^c-a89e032fa4d08e30f1ada2044c87b65f][^c-3e862cdb9d49d7decad3b7e334176275]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If a member dies leaving a dependent child and there is a surviving parent (or spouse or civil partner[^c-6901112ea90e325ccfd867d0ec085405] or[^c-2b9925dd3b606e865512eb016b097060] scheme partner[^c-95153d6e132cbb72f3dd5f828c59340b] of a parent) but that dependent child is not being maintained by the surviving parent (or spouse or civil partner or scheme partner of a parent), the Secretary of State may increase the amount of the child allowance that would otherwise be payable under these Regulations.

(2) The allowance may, at the Secretary of State’s discretion, be increased up to an amount equal to the amount that would have been payable if there were no surviving parent (or spouse or civil partner[^c-31b2eee08db4119acadc5c2109c72b04] or scheme partner[^c-52cdad64243ed6715fdd0402d4f3a04d] of a parent).
