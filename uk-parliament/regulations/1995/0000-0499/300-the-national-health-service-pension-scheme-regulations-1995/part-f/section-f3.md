# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section F3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## F3. Member dies with preserved pension

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If a member leaves pensionable employment with a preserved pension under regulation L1 and dies before his pension under this Section of[^M_F_b49da709-4fe4-431a-c09d-7f09fe76df25] the scheme becomes payable, a lump sum on death shall be payable in accordance with regulation F5.

(2) The lump sum will be equal to 3 times the yearly rate of the member’s preserved pension, calculated as described in regulation L1.
