# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section R12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/300
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## R12. Pension sharing on divorce or nullity of marriage or, dissolution or nullity of civil partnership[^c-a4d0d850b799d94bbd7156818ef80a70][^c-f509174da46ca3e9bd9f004f092336de]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 2A shall have effect in relation to—

- (a) pension credit rights; and
- (b) pension credit benefit payable,

under this Section of[^M_F_569653b0-2096-4fb1-b042-ac2d0516f9e0] the scheme.

(2) Except as provided for in this regulation and in Schedule 2A, Parts B to V of these Regulations shall not apply to a person entitled to a pension credit or to a pension credit member.
