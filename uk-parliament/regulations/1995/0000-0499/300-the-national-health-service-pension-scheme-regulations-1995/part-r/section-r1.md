# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section R1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/300

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## R1. Practitioners and specialist trainees in general practice[^c-ed0b9b8f45de394005bde50275f43e38]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations apply to members who are or have been practitioners as if they were officers employed by the relevant Local Health Board[^c-476b7a641e5b7267459df8f50b50274a] or[^c-b7d42fb9fcc3a37e9c8271d0e3cb69cc] NHS England[^c-1f99af0a219aaeae90c0d0c538ae7b59], but with the modifications described in Schedule 2.

(2) These Regulations apply to members who are or have been specialist trainees in general practice[^c-e1540b8d57d44c33a7a03f233eb2a89b] as if they were whole–time officers employed by the relevant employing authority[^c-4b014964e7b1bc8dacc2f96164841db7].

(3)[^c-dd78c4183559ba93e170d4084a18dec9] These Regulations apply from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2004 to a non-GP provider as if they were a whole-time officer employed by [^c-a64e5030e6346d5d917f354ec42e409f]... NHS England[^c-ac0249c2f103a7994dd045b424e59521] or relevant Local Health Board with the modifications described in paragraphs 3, 4, 5, 10 and 23 of Schedule 2[^c-5024f24c90f1ffd4ab2f3daab2d994aa] and Schedule 2B[^c-986c27405cb3926753143127bd08366e].
