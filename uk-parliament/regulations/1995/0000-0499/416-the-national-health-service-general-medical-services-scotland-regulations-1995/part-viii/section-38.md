# The National Health Service (General Medical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Guidance to doctors

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) a Board may issue guidance to doctors whose names are included in its medical list to assist them in assessing, in accordance with paragraph 22 of Schedule 1, the qualifications, experience and competence of any employee or prospective employee.

(2) Any guidance issued under paragraph (1) shall—

- (a) be issued only after consultation with the Area Medical Committee, and
- (b) have regard to standards adopted either by an appropriate national regulatory body for a profession or occupation or by a similar body.
