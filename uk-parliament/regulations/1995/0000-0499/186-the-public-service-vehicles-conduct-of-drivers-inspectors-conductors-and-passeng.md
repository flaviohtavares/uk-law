# The Public Service Vehicles (Conduct of Drivers, Inspectors, Conductors and Passengers) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 186

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Public Service Vehicles (Conduct of Drivers, Inspectors, Conductors and Passengers) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

30th January 1995

Laid before Parliament

7th February 1995

Coming into force

1st March 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 60(2) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conduct of Drivers, Inspectors, Conductors and Passengers) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st March 1995.

**2.** (1) The Public Service Vehicles (Conduct of Drivers, Inspectors, Conductors and Passengers) Regulations 1990[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 6(1), for sub-paragraph (g) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

> - (g) speak to the driver whilst the vehicle is in motion except—
>   - (i) in an emergency;
>   - (ii) for reasons of safety; or
>   - (iii) to give directions as to the stopping of the vehicle;.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Steven Norris

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

30th January 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conduct of Drivers, Inspectors, Conductors and Passengers) Regulations 1990 so that passengers on a public service vehicle are not prohibited by those Regulations from speaking to the driver when the vehicle is not in motion.
