# The Apple Orchard Grubbing Up (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 40

AGRICULTURE

The Apple Orchard Grubbing Up (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

11th January 1995

Laid before Parliament

12th January 1995

Coming into force

13th January 1995

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Apple Orchard Grubbing Up (Amendment) Regulations 1995, shall apply throughout Great Britain and shall come into force on 13th January 1995.

## 2. Amendment to the Apple Orchard Grubbing Up Regulations

In the definition of “the Commission Regulation” in regulation 2(1) of the Apple Orchard Grubbing Up Regulations 1991[^f00003], the words “and as read with Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 3149/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/3149)[^f00004]” shall be inserted at the end.

Michael Jack

Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

11th January 1995

Hector Monro

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

11th January 1995

(This note it not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Apple Orchard Grubbing Up Regulations 1991, by amending the definition of “the Commission Regulation” to take account of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 3149/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/3149) (OJ No. L 332, 22.12.94, p. 29). This Commission Regulation derogates from Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2604/90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1990/2604) (OJ No. L 245, 8.9.90, p. 23, amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2264/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/2264) (OJ No. L 246, 21.9.94, p. 2)), by permitting applications for the payment of premium for the grubbing up of apple orchards, payable by virtue of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1200/90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1990/1200) (OJ No. L 119, 11.5.90, p. 63, last amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1890/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/1890) (OJ No. L 197, 30.7.94, p. 41)), to be made during the month of January 1995.

- The amendment to the Apple Orchard Grubbing Up Regulations 1991 ensures that, from the coming into force of the amendment, applications for the grubbing up premium made pursuant to the derogation set out in Commission Regulation 3149/94 will be subject to the provisions of the Apple Orchard Grubbing Up Regulations 1991.
