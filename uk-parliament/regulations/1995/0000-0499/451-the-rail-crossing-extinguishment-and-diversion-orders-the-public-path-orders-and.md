# The Rail Crossing Extinguishment and Diversion Orders, the Public Path Orders and the Definitive Maps and Statements (Amendment) Regulations 1995

[1993/11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/11) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those Statutory Instruments.

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 26(5), 118(3), 118A(7), 119(7), 119A(10), 120(3A) of, and paragraph 1(3)(b)(iv) of Schedule 6 to, the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], sections 53(6), 54(1) and paragraph 3(2)(b)(iv) of Schedule 15 to the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^f00002] and sections 259(4), 333 and paragraphs 1(2)(b)(v) and 7 of Schedule 14 to the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rail Crossing Extinguishment and Diversion Orders, the Public Path Orders and the Definitive Maps and Statements (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 29th March 1995.

## 2. Amendment of the Rail Crossing Extinguishment and Diversion Orders Regulations 1993

The Rail Crossing Extinguishment and Diversion Orders Regulations 1993[^f00004] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 for “in the form in Part I of Schedule 1” there shall be substituted “in the form set out in Form 1 in Schedule 1”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) of regulation 2 for “in the form in Part 2 of Schedule 1” there shall be substituted “in the form set out in Form 2 in Schedule 1”; and
- (c) for the list of areas and persons in Schedule 4 there shall be substituted the list set out in the Schedule hereto.

## 3. Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Public Path Orders) Regulations 1993

The Town and Country Planning (Public Path Orders) Regulations 1993[^f00005] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in Form 2 in Schedule 2 for the two references to “paragraph \[7\] \[8\] of Schedule 14” there shall be substituted in each case “paragraph 7 of Schedule 14”; and
- (b) for the list of areas and persons in Schedule 3 there shall be substituted the list set out in the Schedule hereto.

## 4. Amendment of the Public Path Orders Regulations 1993

The Public Path Orders Regulations 1993[^f00006] shall be amended as follows—

These Regulations make minor amendments to the Rail Crossing Extinguishment and Diversion Orders Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/9)), The Town and Country Planning (Public ath Orders) Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/10)), the Public Path Orders Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/11)) and the Wildlife and Countryside (Definitive Maps and Statements) Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/12)).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 2 of S.I. [1993/9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/9) so that it refers to Forms 1 and 2 in Schedule 1 rather than Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 1.

Regulation 3 amends Form 2 in Schedule 2 to S.I. [1993/10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/10) to omit the references to paragraph 8 of Schedule 14 to the Town and Country Planning Act 1990.

Regulation 4 substitutes a new regulation 2(1), (2) and Schedule 1 to S.I. [1993/11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/11). References to the form of an acquisition extinguishment order are omitted and minor drafting changes are made.

Regulations 2 to 5 also amend the areas in relation to which persons are to be served with notice of orders.
