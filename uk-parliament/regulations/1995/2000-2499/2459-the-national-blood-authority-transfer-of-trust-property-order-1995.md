# The National Blood Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2459

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Blood Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

21st September 1995

Laid before Parliament

28th September 1995

Coming into force

20th October 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of establishments and facilities formerly managed by the South Western Regional Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the National Blood Authority[^f00002], having consulted the South and West Regional Health Authority[^f00003] and the National Blood Authority in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Blood Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 20th October 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the first Authority” means the South and West Regional Health Authority;
- “the second Authority” means the National Blood Authority;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the South Western Regional Health Authority and the second Authority on 19th January 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the South Western Regional Health Authority and the second Authority.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 20th October 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the first Authority to the second Authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

21st September 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the National Blood Authority on 20th October 1995 trust property formerly held by the South and West Regional Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, Corporate Affairs — Operational Policy Unit 2, Room 313, Eileen House, 80—94 Newington Causeway, London SE1 6EF.
