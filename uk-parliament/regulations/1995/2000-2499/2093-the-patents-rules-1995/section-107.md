# The Patents Rules 1995, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Supporting statements or evidence

(1) Where by virtue of any of the rules mentioned in paragraph (2) of this rule, any notice or application is required to be supported by a statement or evidence, such a statement or evidence shall be filed on, or within fourteen days after, the date on which the notice is given or the application is made.

(2) The rules referred to in paragraph (1) above are rules 40(3), 41(1), 43(3), 64(1)(b), 65(2), 71(2), 78(3) and 91(5).
