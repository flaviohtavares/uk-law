# The Patents Rules 1995, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Fees to be paid in sterling

All fees payable to the Patent Office under the said Treaty in respect of international applications shall be paid in sterling.
