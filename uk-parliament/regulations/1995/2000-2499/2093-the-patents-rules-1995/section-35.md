# The Patents Rules 1995, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35.  Amendment of request for grant

Subject to rule 45(3), an application for amendment of the request for the grant of a patent shall—

- (a) be made on Patents Form 11/77;
- (b) clearly identify the proposed amendment; and

(c) state the reasons for it.
