# The Fire Services (Appointments and Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2109

FIRE SERVICES

The Fire Services (Appointments and Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

11th August 1995

Laid before Parliament

11th August 1995

Coming into force

1st September 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00001], and after consultation with the Central Fire Brigades Advisory Council, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Fire Services (Appointments and Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st September 1995.

**2.** The Fire Services (Appointments and Promotion) Regulations 1978[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the words “Drill Book”, in paragraph (a) of Part II of Schedules 1 and 2, the words “Training Manual”.

Blatch

Minister of State

Home Office

11th August 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Fire Services (Appointments and Promotion) Regulations 1978 by substituting for the references to the Fire Service Drill Book, in the provisions relating to the conduct and organisation of drills as part of the practical examinations for promotion, references to the Fire Service Training Manual (which is replacing the Fire Service Drill Book).
