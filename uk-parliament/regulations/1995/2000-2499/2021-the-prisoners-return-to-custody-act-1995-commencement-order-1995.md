# The Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2021 (C.41)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

POLICE

The Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made

28th July 1995

In the exercise of the powers conferred upon my by section 3(2) of the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

**2.** The Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 shall come into force on 5th September 1995.

Home Office

Mishael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

28th July 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th September 1995 the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995.
