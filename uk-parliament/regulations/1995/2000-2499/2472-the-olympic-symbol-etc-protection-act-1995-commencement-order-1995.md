# The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2472 (C. 48)

TRADE MARKS

COMMERCIAL PROPERTY

The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made

19th September 1995

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 19(2) of the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

**2.** The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 shall come into force on 20th September 1995.

Virginia Bottomley

Secretary of State for National Heritage

19th September 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 into force.
