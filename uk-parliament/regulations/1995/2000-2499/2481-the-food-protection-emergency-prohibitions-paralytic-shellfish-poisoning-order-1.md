# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1995 and (Nos.4 and 5) Orders 1995 Revocation Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2481

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1995 and (Nos.4 and 5) Orders 1995 Revocation Order 1995

Laid before Parliament

Made

20th September 1995

Coming into force with article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1995 and (Nos.4 and 5) Orders 1995 Revocation Order 1995 and shall come into force at 17.00 hours on 20th September 1995.

## 2. Revocation

The following Orders are revoked:—

- (a) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1995[^f00002];
- (b) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.4) Order 1995[^f00003];
- (c) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.5) Order 1995[^f00004].

A J Matheson

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

20th September 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Orders 1995 listed in article 2 of the Order (“the principal Orders”) (insofar as they remain in force). The principal Orders prohibited—

- (a) fishing for or taking shellfish within the areas designated in the Schedules to those Orders (article 4);
- (b) movement of such shellfish out of those areas (article 5); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such shellfish from the designated areas within the United Kingdom (article 6).

The effect of this Order is to remove completely the prohibition from fishing for, taking, moving, landing, using in food production and supplying the shellfish affected by the principal Orders.
