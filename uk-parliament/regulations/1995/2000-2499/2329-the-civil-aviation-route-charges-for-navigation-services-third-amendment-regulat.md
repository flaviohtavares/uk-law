# The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2329

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

6th September 1995

Laid before Parliament

7th September 1995

Coming into force

1st October 1995

Whereas in pursuance of tariffs approved under the Eurocontrol Convention[^f00001] and under the Multilateral Agreement relating to Route Charges concluded at Brussels on 12th February 1981[^f00001] (being international agreements to which the United Kingdom is a party), the Secretary of State for Transport has determined rates of charges, as specified in the following Regulations, payable to Eurocontrol in respect of navigation services provided for aircraft:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 73(1)(a), (1A)(a) and (6) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st October 1995.

**2.** The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) Regulations 1994[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:

These Regulations further amend the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) Regulations 1994 (“the Principal Regulations”) as follows:

The unit rate in ECUs for Norway specified in Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is reduced from 54.16 to 41.02 (regulation 2(a)).

In consequence new transatlantic charges specified in Schedule 3 to the Principal Regulations are introduced (regulation 2(b)).

The amount of the charges in ECUs set out in Schedule 3 to the Principal Regulations are calculated by reference to the costs of provision of en-route navigation services in the participating countries in the Eurocontrol charges system, the amount of traffic using each country’s airspace and the relationship of each country’s currency to the ECU over a period agreed by Ministers of the participating countries. The interaction of these elements varies in each country. In calculating the revised charges the average of the exchange rates between the ECU and the currencies of the participating countries obtaining in the month of July 1994 have been used.

Out of the 158 aerodromes listed in Schedule 3, the transatlantic charges have been decreased in relation to 15 of those aerodromes. The decreases range from 0.1% in the case of Frankfurt and Munich in Zone II to 20.7% in the case of Stockholm in Zone II.
