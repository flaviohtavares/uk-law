# The Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2328

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

6th September 1995

Laid before Parliament

7th September 1995

Coming into force

1st October 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st October 1995.

**2.** (1) Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992 and are made in consequence of Commission Directives [94/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0053), [94/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0068), [94/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0078) and Directive [94/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0020) which was made by the European Parliament and the Council. These Regulations also correct an error in item 39 of Schedule 1.

Directive [94/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0053) further amends Council Directive [78/316/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0316) relating to the interior fittings of motor vehicles (identification of controls, tell-tales and indicators).

Directive [94/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0068) amends Council Directive [78/318/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0318) relating to the wiper and washer systems of motor vehicles.

Directive [94/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0078) amends Council Directive [78/549/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0549) relating to the wheel guards of motor vehicles.

Directive [94/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0020) relates to mechanical coupling devices of motor vehicles and their trailers and their attachment to those vehicles.

Copies of EEC and EC Directives which are relevant to these Regulations can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. The details of the Directives are set out below.
