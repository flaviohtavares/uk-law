# The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 563

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1995

Made

6th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

8th March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by sections 8(1)(b) and (1A)(c) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

## 2. Abolition of Authorities

The District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby abolished.

## 3. Establishment and names of new Authorities

For each of the districts determined by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) Order 1995[^f00002] and specified in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Order there is established a District Health Authority which shall be known by the name specified in column (2) of that Schedule in relation to that district.

## 4. Amendment of the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990

In consequence of articles 2 and 3 of this Order the National Health Service (District Authorities) Order 1990[^f00003] shall be amended by—

- (a) the omission from Part 5 of Schedule 1 of the entries in columns (1) and (2) relating to Chichester District, East Surrey District, Mid Downs District, Mid Surrey District, North West Surrey District, South West Surrey District and Worthing District, and the insertion, immediately after the entries in that Part relating to—
  - (i) Croydon District, of the following entries in columns (1) and (2) respectively:—

  > | South Cheshire | South Cheshire Health Authority |

This Order abolishes on 1st April 1995 the District Health Authorities named in Schedule 1 (article 2) and establishes four new District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 2 in relation to the districts for which they are established (article 3). The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990 is amended to reflect these changes (article 4).

The changes made to District Health Authorities in this Order are reflected in changes to the districts of the abolished authorities made by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) Order 1995.
