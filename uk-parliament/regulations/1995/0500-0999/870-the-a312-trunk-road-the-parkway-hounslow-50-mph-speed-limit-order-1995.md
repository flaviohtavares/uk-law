# The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (50 mph Speed Limit) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 870

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (50 mph Speed Limit) Order 1995

Made

20th March 1995

Coming into force

31st March 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(1)(a) and (2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (50 mph Speed Limit) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 31st March 1995.

**2.** In this Order “The Parkway” means the A312 trunk road in the London Borough of Hounslow known as The Parkway—

- (i) between its junctions with the A30 trunk road and the southern side of the A4 trunk road; and
- (ii) between its junction with the northern side of the A4 trunk road and the northern edge of the circulatory carriageway of its interchange with the M4 motorway.

**3.** No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 50 miles per hour on The Parkway.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P J Bevis

A Grade 6 Officer Highways Agency, London Office

20th March 1995
