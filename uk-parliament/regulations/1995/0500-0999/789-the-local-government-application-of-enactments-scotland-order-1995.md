# The Local Government (Application of Enactments) (Scotland) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 789 (S.71)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Government (Application of Enactments) (Scotland) Order 1995

Made

14th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

16th March 1995

Coming into force

6th April 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 181(1) and (2) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Application of Enactments) (Scotland) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 6th April 1995.

(2) In this Order, “new local authority” means a council in respect of which an ordinary election of councillors is to take place on 6th April 1995 in terms of section 5(2) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.

## 2. Application of enactments

In the period prior to 1st April 1996, the provisions specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall apply to a new local authority as they apply to a local authority subject, where specified, to the modifications in column 2 of the Schedule.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Order)

In consequence of local government reorganisation in Scotland, new mainland councils will be elected on 6th April 1995. Those councils will take over local authority functions at 1st April 1996.

This Order applies certain general and procedural provisions of the Local Government (Scotland) Act [1973 (c. 65)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/65) and of certain other enactments relating to local authorities which would not otherwise apply, in the period prior to 1st April 1996, to those new councils, as they apply to existing councils.
