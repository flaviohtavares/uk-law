# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1995/551](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/551) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1995 No. 737

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1995

Made

13th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

14th March 1995

Coming into force

31st March 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1), (2) and (5) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 31st March 1995.

**2.** (1) The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations 1995[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (2) of regulation 3 there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> (2) In paragraph (1), the words “except sub-paragraph (b)(ii)” shall be omitted, for the words “the trailer” there shall be substituted the words “a trailer” and after the words “1983 or” in the proviso there shall be inserted the word “which”.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Steven Norris

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

13th March 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations 1995. They make no changes of substance.
