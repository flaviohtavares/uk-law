# The Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Recovery of costs

The Minister shall be entitled to recover on demand from a producer the costs of carrying out any inspection specified in paragraph 3(b) of Schedule 7.
