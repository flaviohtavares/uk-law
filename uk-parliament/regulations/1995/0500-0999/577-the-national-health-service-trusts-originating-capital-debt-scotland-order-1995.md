# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Scotland) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 577 (S.46)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Scotland) Order 1995

Made

28th February 1995

Coming into force

14th March 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12E(1) and (4) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Scotland) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 14th March 1995.

(2) In this Order “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. Originating capital debt of NHS trusts

(1) The originating capital debt of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified opposite it in column 2 of the Schedule.

(2) Each originating capital debt specified in column 2 of the Schedule shall be divided between an initial loan of an amount specified opposite it in column 3 of the Schedule and public dividend capital of an amount specified opposite it in column 4 of the Schedule.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital debt provided for in section 12E of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 of NHS trusts established under that Act with an operational date of 1st April 1994 or, in the case of Central Scotland Healthcare National Health Service Trust, 1st October 1994. It provides also for the splitting of the originating capital debts into loan and public dividend capital.
