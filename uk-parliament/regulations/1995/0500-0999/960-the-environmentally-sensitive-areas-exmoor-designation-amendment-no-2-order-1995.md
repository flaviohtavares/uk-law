# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 1995/195](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/195) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1995 No. 960

AGRICULTURE

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1995

Made

30th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

31st March 1995

Coming into force

21st April 1995

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food has by order designated an area on Exmoor as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 21st April 1995.

## 2. Amendment

Article 6(3) of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation Order 1993[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the words “at the rate per annum of £2.40 for each 10 metres of hedgerow”, the words “at the rate per annum of 24p for each metre of hedgerow”.

## 3. Saving

Article 2 of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1995.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation Order 1993 (“the principal Order”) which designated an area on Exmoor as an environmentally sensitive area.

Subject to saving provision, this Order amends the rates of payment to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 which includes the additional provisions for the management of stockproof hedgerows by traditional means, specified in Schedule 4 to the principal Order.
