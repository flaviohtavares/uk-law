# The Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 934

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

29th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

5th April 1995

Coming into force

26th April 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Herefordshire Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, comencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 26th April 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Herefordshire Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 12th December 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 26th April 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

29th March 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Hereford Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 26th April 1995 trust property formerly held by the Herefordshire Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
