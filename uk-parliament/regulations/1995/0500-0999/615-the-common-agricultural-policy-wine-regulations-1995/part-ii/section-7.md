# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1995, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Exemption relating to planting and other prohibitions

The provisions of Article 13(1) of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2389/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/2389)[^f00017], as amended, relating to the prohibition of the planting, field grafting and double–grafting of vine varieties not listed in the classification referred to in Article 1 of that Regulation shall not apply where the purpose of the planting is one of the purposes set out in the first sub–paragraph of Article 13(2) thereof.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: OJ No. L232, 9.8.89, p.1, the amendment to which is specified at item 34 in column 1 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations; it is not relevant to Article 13(1) of the Regulation.
