# The United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 801

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1995

Made

17th March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust established by the Leeds General Infirmary and Associated Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order and the Leeds General Infirmary and Associated Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1991[^f00006] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

17th March 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 1995 of the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust established on 21st December 1990.
