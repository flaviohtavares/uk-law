# The Fish Health (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Fish Health (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

22nd March 1995

Laid before Parliament

27th March 1995

Coming into force

17th April 1995

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

—These Regulations may be cited as the Fish Health (Amendment) Regulations 1995, and shall come into force on 17th April 1995.

## 2. Amendment of the Fish Health Regulations 1992

(1) The Fish Health Regulations 1992[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of this regulation.

(2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 14 (powers of veterinary inspector) for the words “Subject to regulation 9 of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1992” there shall be substituted—

> Subject to regulation 10 of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1993

[^f00004].

(3) For Schedule 2 there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

(4) For Schedule 3 there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

(5) In Part I of Schedule 7 there shall be inserted the following new provisions after the entry relating to “Commission Decision [93/169/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0169)”—

These Regulations amend the Fish Health Regulations 1992 (“the principal Regulations”) so as to implement Commission Decisions [948/817/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/19948/0817) (OJ No. L337, 24.12.94, p 88) and [94/865/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0865) (OJ No. L352, 31.12.94, p.75).

The Regulations:

- (a) provide for the substitution of Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations;
- (b) provide for the substitution of Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations;
- (c) provide for the amendment of Schedule 7 to the principal Regulations;
- (d) Update the reference in regulation 14(1) of the principal Regulations to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1992.
