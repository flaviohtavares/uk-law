# The Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 752

INTELLIGENCE SERVICES

The Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) Order 1995

Made

15th March 1995

Coming into force

5th April 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of March 1995

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(4) of the Intelligence Services Act 1994[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 5th April 1995.

**2.** Subsection (1) of section 5 and subsection (1) of section 11 of the Intelligence Services Act 1994 shall extend to the colonies specified in the Schedule hereto as part of the law thereof.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

- Anguilla
- British Antarctic Territory
- British Indian Ocean Territory
- Cayman Islands
- Falkland Islands
- Gibraltar
- Hong Kong
- Montserrat
- Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno Islands
- St Helena and its dependencies
- South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands
- Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia
- Turks and Caicos Islands
- Virgin Islands

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order directs that section 5(1) (warrants) and section 11(1) (interpretation) of the Intelligence Services Act 1994 shall extend to the colonies specified in the Schedule.
