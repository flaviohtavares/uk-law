# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 951

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

30th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

31st March 1995

Coming into force

24th April 1995

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], having had regard to the matters specified in section 34(9) and having consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 24th April 1995.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations a reference to any regulation by number alone means the regulation so numbered in the Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) Regulations 1989[^f00002].

## 3. Transitional provisions

These Regulations shall apply for the purposes of work done on or after 24th April 1995 to which the Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) Regulations 1989 apply.

## 4. Amendments to the Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) Regulations 1989

Regulation 2 shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) after the definition of “business day” there shall be inserted the following definition:—

  > “contract” means a contract entered into by the Board with other persons or bodies pursuant to its powers under section 4 of the 1988 Act;

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “duty solicitor scheme” there shall be inserted the following definition:—

  > “franchisee” means a person or body (other than the Board) acting under the terms of a franchising contract;

  .

**5.** Regulation 5(2) shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) for the word “basic” wherever it occurs there shall be substituted the word “prescribed”;
- (b) in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), after “paragraph 1(1)(a)” there shall in inserted “(or paragraph 1A in the case of work done by a franchisee)”.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 29th March 1995

We consent

Timothy Wood

Timothy Kirkhope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 30th March 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance (Duty Solicitor) (Remuneration) Regulations 1989. They provide increased rates of remuneration for work done on or after 24th April 1995 and introduce a higher rate for franchisees.
