# The Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to the financial year beginning on that date.
