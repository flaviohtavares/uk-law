# The Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1995, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Determination of an amount as a percentage addition

(1) Subject to regulations 11 and 12, the funding authority shall determine an amount which is equal to the relevant percentage of the amount determined in respect of the school in accordance with, as the case may be, regulation 6, 7, 8 or 9.

(2) In these Regulations the relevant percentage is the percentage determined in accordance with Schedule 1.
