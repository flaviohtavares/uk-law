(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughof Lambeth) Order 1994 (“the principal order”) so as to designate thewhole of the London borough of Lambeth (excluding the roads specified in theSchedule) as a special parking area for the purposes of Part II of the RoadTraffic Act 1991 with effect from 3rd April 1995. The new area subsumes thespecial parking areas at Brixton and North Lambeth designated by the principalorder.
