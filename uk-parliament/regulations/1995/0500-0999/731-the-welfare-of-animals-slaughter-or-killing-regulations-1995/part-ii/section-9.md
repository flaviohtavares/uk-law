## 9 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). The slaughter of animals in slaughterhouses and knackers' yards (affected by repeal, see note) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fff174c11aa234bf6f78c98ad8e829d3][^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

Where any soliped, ruminant, pig, rabbit or bird is brought into a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard for slaughter, that animal shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] moved and lairaged in accordance with Schedule 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] restrained in accordance with Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] subject to regulation 22, stunned before slaughter in accordance with Parts I and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] of Schedule 5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7f24afcfd63f2e7102de37d71ac3f7f] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
