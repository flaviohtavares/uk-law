(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Any person engaged in the bleeding or pithing of any animal which has been stunned shall ensure that the animal is bled or pithed without delay after it has been stunned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f6981f80a88e11480b5b5946e4cb590d][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] (1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, any person engaged in the bleeding of any animal that has been stunned shall ensure that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the bleeding is rapid, profuse and complete; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the bleeding is completed before the animal regains consciousness; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the bleeding is carried out by severing at least one of the carotid arteries or the vessels from which they arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, after severance of at least one of the carotid arteries or the vessels from which they arise of any animal that has been stunned before bleeding, no person shall cause or permit any further dressing procedure or any electrical stimulation to be performed on the animal before the bleeding has ended and in any event not before the expiry of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of a turkey or goose, a period of not less than 2 minutes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of any other bird, a period of not less than 90 seconds; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of bovine animals, a period of not less than 30 seconds; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of sheep, goats, pigs and deer, a period of not less than 20 seconds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) above shall not apply to any animal which has been pithed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-c4abb34db5667b932771083604edc2dd][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] (1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Where one person is responsible for the stunning and pithing, or for the stunning, shackling, hoisting and bleeding, of animals other than birds or rabbits, or for some of those operations, such operations must be carried out by him consecutively in respect of one animal before being so carried out by him in respect of another animal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Manual back up of automatic machinery (affected by repeal, see note) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-406f0b4583a568e7c8737a914db97a83][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The occupier of a slaughterhouse shall ensure that no bird is slaughtered by means of automatic neck cutters unless, whenever the neck cutters are operated, a person is present who is able to ascertain whether or not the neck cutters have effectively severed at least one of the carotid arteries or the vessels from which they arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] In the event of the machinery not being effective in severing at least one of the carotid arteries or the vessels from which they arise, the occupier of the slaughterhouse in which the neck cutters are situated shall ensure that the bird is slaughtered or killed immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-82c03900521f378ae765cabadeae9b50] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
