# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 8 (affected by repeal, see note) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS FOR THE SLAUGHTER OR KILLING OF HORSES IN SLAUGHTERHOUSES AND KNACKERS' YARDS (affected by repeal, see note) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 12 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-8a8f7ec41c1c2af8db2b4708ceae59d5][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to paragraph 3 below, no person shall slaughter or kill, or cause or permit to be slaughtered or killed, any horse in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard except in a room or a bay which has been provided for the slaughter or killing of horses by the occupier of the slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard in accordance with paragraph 2(e) of Schedule 2 (which relates to additional provisions for horses). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-863db8ad363e2ab05fb81baaf6a31fee][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to paragraph 3 below, no person shall slaughter or kill, or cause or permit to be slaughtered or killed, any horse in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in a room or bay in which there are the remains of a horse or other animal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] within sight of any other horse. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-60898e30b56cda91f7915833c1ba0b08][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall not apply in the case of a horse slaughtered or killed in accordance with paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 (which relates to emergency slaughter and killing). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2931e71d8b64b0ec73696b56bef1c6a0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
