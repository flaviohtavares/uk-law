# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Title, commencement and extent (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3b455fd211f2676d15afd3f540bfb793][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to paragraph (3) below, these Regulations extend to Great Britain. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Paragraph 2(3) and (4) of Schedule 6 extends to England and Wales. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
