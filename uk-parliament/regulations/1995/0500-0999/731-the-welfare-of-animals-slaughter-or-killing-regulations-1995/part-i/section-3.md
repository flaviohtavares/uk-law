# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Application and exemptions (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dbea9546383e8f360239f085a14d9827][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] These Regulations apply to the movement, lairaging, restraint, stunning, slaughter and killing of animals bred or kept for the production of meat, skin, fur or other products, to methods of killing animals for the purpose of disease control and to the killing of surplus chicks and embryos in hatchery waste. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] These Regulations do not apply to any act lawfully done under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^f00003]. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] These Regulations do not apply to any animal which is killed during a sporting event. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] These Regulations do not apply to the killing of wild game. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
