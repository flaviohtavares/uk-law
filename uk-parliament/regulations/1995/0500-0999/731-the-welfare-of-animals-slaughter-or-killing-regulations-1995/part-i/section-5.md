# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Safeguarding the welfare of animals (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dee184b5b8aea9d6675b8f5d1a42737a][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The occupier of a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard shall ensure that at all times when there are live animals on the premises a person (whether or not himself) is available who is competent, and who has authority to take whatever action may be necessary to safeguard the welfare of the animals in accordance with these Regulations. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
