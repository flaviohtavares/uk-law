# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Animal welfare legislation and codes (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8c5a98050623dee74c450cff35544b03][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The occupier of a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard shall ensure that any person who is involved in any of the activities governed by these Regulations— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] is acquainted with the provisions of the legislation, and of any welfare codes, relevant to the operations that that person carries out; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] has access to a copy of any such welfare code at the slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] has received instruction and guidance on the requirements of such legislation and any such welfare code; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0f87fdba1c064725f9eda90f9fd51ab3][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] In this regulation “welfare code” means any current code issued under regulation 7. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
