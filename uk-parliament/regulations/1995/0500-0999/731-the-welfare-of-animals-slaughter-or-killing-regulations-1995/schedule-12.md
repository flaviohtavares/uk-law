#### 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Interpretation (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cb45754576ce4b7ca01a90188e274db0][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

In this Schedule— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] “animal” means any sheep, goat or bovine animal; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] “bovine animal” means— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in sub-paragraph (a) above and paragraph 7(b) below, any ox, bullock, cow, heifer, steer or calf; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] elsewhere, any ox, bullock, cow, heifer or steer or any calf which is too large to be restrained manually for slaughter on a cradle or table; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] “bird” means any turkey, domestic fowl, guinea-fowl, duck, goose or quail. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Slaughter by a religious method (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0705e104c026372b54eb4b13b29f2323][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

In this Schedule references to slaughter by a religious method are references to slaughter without the infliction of unnecessary suffering— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] by the Jewish method for the food of Jews by a Jew who holds a licence in accordance with Schedule 1 (which relates to the licensing of slaughtermen) and who is duly licensed— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in England and Wales by the Rabbinical Commission referred to in Part IV of this Schedule; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in Scotland by the Chief Rabbi; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] by the Muslim method for the food of Muslims by a Muslim who holds a licence in accordance with Schedule 1. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART II (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — PROVISIONS RELATING TO THE SLAUGHTER OF ANIMALS BY A RELIGIOUS METHOD (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Slaughter of bovine animals by a religious method (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2eef0e90fcaa39f5b45d064d2f68a30f][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, no person shall slaughter, or cause or permit to be slaughtered, any bovine animal in a slaughterhouse by a religious method unless the animal is in an upright position in a restraining pen which has been approved by the Minister and which the Minister is satisfied has been installed in such a manner as to ensure that it will operate efficiently. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The Minister may, for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1) above, give his approval to a restraining pen, but he shall not give any such approval unless he is satisfied that the pen is of such a size and design, and is able to be so operated, as to protect a bovine animal from any avoidable pain, suffering, agitation, injuries or contusions while confined in it or while entering it and, in particular, unless he is satisfied that the pen— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] contains an effective means of restraining any bovine animal confined in it (including a suitable head restraint for that purpose); and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] contains means of support which will take the weight of the animal during and following slaughter in it. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) above shall not apply where the bovine animal is stunned, or stunned and pithed, before slaughter, but in such cases any bovine animal shall be restrained before stunning in accordance with paragraph 3(a) of Schedule 4. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Use and maintenance of restraining pens (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-36de6df6e19c239d052beb4d4dde624b][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The occupier of a slaughterhouse in which there is a restraining pen shall ensure that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the means of restraining and supporting a bovine animal confined in such a pen described in paragraph 3(2)(a) and (b) above are used in respect of any bovine animal confined in it; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the pen is kept in good working order; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] if it has been modified after it has been approved by the Minister, the pen is not used again until a fresh approval is given by the Minister. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Handling of animals during slaughter (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-64179cf5184120e1db2b2ec494959850][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The occupier of a slaughterhouse where animals are slaughtered by a religious method and any person engaged in the slaughter of any animal by a religious method shall ensure that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] no bovine animal is placed in a restraining pen unless the person who is to carry out the slaughter is ready to make the incision immediately the bovine animal is placed in the pen; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] no animal is shackled or hoisted until the appropriate period referred to in paragraph 7 below has elapsed and unless the animal is unconscious; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any sheep or goat, or any calf which is small enough to be restrained manually on a cradle or table, is slaughtered only on a cradle or table and that only one such animal is placed on the cradle or table at any one time; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where the slaughter is carried out in a slaughterhouse, a captive bolt instrument is kept close to the restraining pen, cradle or table for use in case of emergency and is immediately used where the animal is subjected to any avoidable pain, suffering or agitation or has any injuries or contusions. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Method of slaughter (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67457a89f69877569ac64179e8726613][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

Any person who slaughters by a religious method any animal which has not been stunned before bleeding shall— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] before each animal is slaughtered, inspect the knife to be used and ensure that it is not used unless it is undamaged and of sufficient size and sharpness to be capable of being used to slaughter the animal in the manner described in sub-paragraph (b) below; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] ensure that each animal is slaughtered by the severance, by rapid, uninterrupted movements of a knife, of both its carotid arteries and both its jugular veins. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 7 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Handling animals after slaughter by a religious method (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4e2b25bfeb2c15168196875566806f9c][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The occupier of a slaughterhouse in which animals are slaughtered by a religious method and any person engaged in the slaughter by a religious method of any animal shall ensure that where the animal has not been stunned, or stunned and pithed, before bleeding it is not moved until it is unconscious and in any event not before the expiry of— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of any sheep or any goat, a period of not less than 20 seconds; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of any bovine animal, a period of not less than 30 seconds, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

after it has been slaughtered in the manner described in paragraph 6 above. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 8 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Prohibition against slaughter by a religious method elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-47a6d36c287ed194e782fdb6916800f1][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART III (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — PROVISIONS RELATING TO THE SLAUGHTER OF BIRDS BY A RELIGIOUS METHOD (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 9 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Slaughter of birds by a religious method (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-44dc36f71b1142945c06e23a1b48504d][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

Any person who slaughters by a religious method any bird which has not been stunned before slaughter shall ensure that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] each bird is slaughtered by the severance, by rapid and uninterrupted movements of a knife, of its carotid arteries; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the knife to be used for the slaughtering of the birds is undamaged and of sufficient size and sharpness to enable each bird to be slaughtered in the manner described in sub-paragraph (a) above. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 10 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Handling birds after slaughter by a religious method (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cde3ffe5f1d6d696318ba987b684e2a6][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The occupier of a slaughterhouse in which birds are slaughtered by a religious method and any person engaged in the slaughter of any bird by a religious method shall ensure that, where the bird has not been stunned before bleeding, no further dressing procedure or any electrical stimulation is performed on the bird before it is unconscious and in any event not before the expiry of— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of a turkey or goose, a period of not less than 2 minutes; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] in the case of any other bird, a period of not less than 90 seconds, after it has been slaughtered in the manner described in paragraph 9 above. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
