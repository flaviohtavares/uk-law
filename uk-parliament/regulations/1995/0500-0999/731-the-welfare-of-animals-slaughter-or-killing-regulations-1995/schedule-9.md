### 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Interpretation (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e2f2842d272af2da1c3c8b528276f7fe][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Permitted methods of slaughtering or killing animals for the purpose of disease control (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2912d3d2bd6807cffa787056bea8779a][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall slaughter or kill any animal for the purpose of disease control, or cause or permit any animal to be slaughtered or killed for that purpose, except by one of the following methods— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] free bullet; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] electrocution; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] exposure to carbon dioxide or to a lethal concentration of other gases or gas mixtures; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] for *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] [^c-39ba4662a7b62ab4f11ac7f0f6a186d4]... *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] birds, dislocation of the neck; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] captive bolt, provided that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the animal is either pithed or the blood vessels in its neck are severed without delay afterwards and in any event before the animal regains consciousness; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] apart from the requirements in (i) above, nothing more is done to the animal before it has been ascertained that the animal is dead; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (f (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] lethal injection of— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] a drug with anaesthetic properties which causes rapid loss of consciousness followed by death; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any other compound if preceded by the induction of anaesthesia *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] ; or *(Extent: S)*[^M_F_9fbd6f1b-8357-4736-c47a-272097e561cb] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (g (Extent: E) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8526792fc7c49b4149f9fe4fd7d541d8][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] for birds, a pneumatic or cartridge operated percussive device producing immediate death, provided that nothing more is done to a bird before it has been ascertained that the bird is dead *(Extent: E)* *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (h (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing for the purpose of disease control by electrocution (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0f861e15d22da744553ddebc028be22f][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill any animal for the purposes of disease control by electrocution, or cause or permit any animal to be so killed, unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the strength and duration of the current used are sufficient to kill immediately an animal of that species; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the animal has been stunned in accordance with paragraph 5, or with paragraphs 8 and 9, or with paragraphs 10 and 11 of Schedule 5. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for stunning by use of a captive bolt instrument (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ae6b3f4c3e31b3675222695842dea2d7][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, a captive bolt instrument to stun any animal prior to its slaughter or killing for the purpose of disease control unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, the instrument is positioned so as to ensure that the projectile enters the cerebral cortex; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the instrument is applied in the proper position and the correct strength of cartridge or other propellant is used, in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions, to produce an effective stun. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] No person shall shoot, or cause or permit to be shot, any bovine animal in the back of the head. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] No person shall shoot, or cause or permit to be shot, any sheep or goat in the back of its head, unless the presence of horns prevents use of the top or the front of its head, in which case it may be shot in the back of the head provided that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the shot is placed immediately behind the base of the horns and aimed towards the mouth; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] bleeding is commenced within 15 seconds of shooting or the sheep or goat is killed within 15 seconds of shooting in accordance with Part III of Schedule 5. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-cc4555e13e289de31150389500eaaf58][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

Any person who uses a captive bolt instrument shall check that the bolt is retracted to its full extent after each shot and if it is not so retracted shall ensure that the instrument is not used again until it has been repaired. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
