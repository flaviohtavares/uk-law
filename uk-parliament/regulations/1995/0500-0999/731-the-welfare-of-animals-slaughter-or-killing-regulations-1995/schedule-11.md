# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, SCHEDULE 11

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 11 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — KILLING OF SURPLUS CHICKS AND EMBRYOS IN HATCHERY WASTE (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 19 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Permitted methods of killing surplus chicks (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8a6518385ce18182a0e660a11981ab0f][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any surplus chick except by one of the following methods— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] use of a mechanical apparatus producing immediate death; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] exposure to gas mixtures; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] dislocation of the neck. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing surplus chicks by use of a mechanical apparatus producing immediate death (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9be2f4c457832cf982916e98a0f04622][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, any mechanical apparatus to kill any surplus chick unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the apparatus contains rapidly rotating mechanically operated killing blades or projections; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the capacity of the apparatus is sufficient to ensure that every chick is killed immediately. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing surplus chicks by exposure to gas mixtures (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ea9ae3094fd4867848fb03e637be2847][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any surplus chick by exposure to a gas mixture unless the chick is placed in the gas mixture and it remains in the gas mixture until it is dead. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] In this paragraph “gas mixture” means— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] an atmosphere with the highest obtainable concentration of carbon dioxide, supplied by a source of 100% carbon dioxide; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] a maximum of 2% oxygen by volume and 90% argon (or other inert gas) by volume in atmospheric air; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: E) (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e6ebd4713917874262d72b27cface51][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] a maximum of 30% carbon dioxide by volume and a minimum of 60% argon (or other inert gas) by volume in atmospheric air, with no more than 2% residual oxygen. *(Extent: E)* *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific reqirements for killing surplus chicks by dislocation of the neck (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f66d6decf4b3842275187d4548013af6][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any surplus chick by dislocation of the neck unless the dislocation is accompanied by the severance of the spinal cord and blood vessels in the chick’s neck. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Permitted method of killing embryos in hatchery waste (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-72e48fc5fadd20154dfa737b654aeef3][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any embryo in hatchery waste, except by use of a mechanical apparatus producing immediate death. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing embryos in hatchery waste by use of a mechanical apparatus producing immediate death (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bc290f52e71b8ec31dcbb71da9c7156b][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, any mechanical apparatus to kill any embryo in hatchery waste unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the apparatus contains rapidly rotating mechanically operated killing blades or projections; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the capacity of the apparatus is sufficient to ensure that every embryo is killed immediately. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
