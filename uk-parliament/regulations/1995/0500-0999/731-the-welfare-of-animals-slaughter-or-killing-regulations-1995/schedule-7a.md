## SCHEDULE 7A (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — KILLING OF BIRDS BY EXPOSURE TO GAS MIXTURES ELSEWHERE THAN IN A SLAUGHTERHOUSE (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-60f6f2846ff7f4d940a3f24946bb5583][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART I (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — Interpretation (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] In this Schedule– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- “bird” means any domestic fowl, turkey, pheasant, quail, partridge, goose, duck or guinea fowl; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “bird shed” means a building designed and constructed to house birds that has been sealed so as to be capable of containing the gas mixture specified in combination 1 in the table in Part V; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “breeder hen” means a domestic fowl which has reached laying maturity and is kept for production of eggs intended for hatching; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “chamber” means a bird shed or gas container in which birds are killed by exposure to a gas mixture; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “end of lay hen” means a laying hen no longer required for the production of eggs; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “end of life breeder hen” means a breeder hen no longer required for the production of hatching eggs; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “gas container” means a receptacle capable of containing the gas mixtures specified in combinations 2 or 3 in the table in Part V which meets the requirements specified in paragraph 5; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “gas mixture” means any of the mixtures of gases specified in the second column of the table in Part V; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- “laying hen” means a domestic fowl which has reached laying maturity and is kept for production of eggs not intended for hatching. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART II (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — End of lay hens and end of life breeder hens (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] (1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Subject to sub-paragraphs (2) and (3), end of lay hens and end of life breeder hens may be killed on premises where they have been kept as laying or breeder hens by exposure to gas mixtures in accordance with Part V of this Schedule. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The owner of the end of lay hens or the end of life breeder hens must give the Scottish Ministers a minimum of five working days' notice specifying– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the owner’s name and address; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the address of the premises at which the killings will take place; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the date on which the killings will take place; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] which of the combinations of gas mixture and chamber listed in the table in Part V of this Schedule is intended to be used for the killings; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the number and age of hens being killed and whether kept as laying or breeder hens. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Part IV of this Schedule applies to the killing of end of lay hens and end of life breeder hens permitted by this paragraph. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART III (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — Killing where there is a restriction on movement (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

**3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] (1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The Scottish Ministers may permit the killing of birds elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse by exposure to gas mixtures in accordance with Part V of this Schedule if– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] an event occurs in Great Britain that makes the movement of birds from the premises where they have been kept to a slaughterhouse impossible or impracticable; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] it is foreseeable that the welfare of the birds will be compromised as a result of the restrictions on their movement. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] A permit issued under this paragraph– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] must be in writing; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] may be general or specific; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] may include such conditions as are specified in the permit; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] may at any time be amended, suspended or revoked by notice in writing. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] A general permit and any amendment, suspension or revocation of it shall be published in such manner as the Scottish Ministers see fit. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] A specific permit and any amendment, suspension or revocation of it shall be served on the individual to whom the permit was granted. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Where the Scottish Ministers have granted a permit under this paragraph– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the occupier of the premises where the birds are kept must take all reasonable measures to avoid welfare problems in birds affected by restrictions on their movement; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the killing of the birds by exposure to gas mixtures in accordance with this Schedule may only take place where, following an inspection by a veterinary surgeon, the veterinary surgeon has confirmed that the welfare of the birds affected by restrictions on their movement will be gravely compromised within 7 days from the date of the inspection. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Part IV of this Schedule applies to the killing of birds permitted by this paragraph. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### PART IV (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — The chamber (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Chamber operator (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person may operate a chamber consisting of a bird shed except under the direct supervision of a veterinary surgeon. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Construction of the chamber (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The operator of a chamber used to kill birds must ensure that the chamber is– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] designed, adapted, constructed, and maintained so as to avoid injury to any bird; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] fitted with an apparatus that can deliver the relevant gas mixture to the chamber in accordance with the combinations listed in the table in Part V of this Schedule; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] fitted with devices which– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] measure and display the maximum concentration by volume of oxygen in the gas mixture specified in combination 3 in that table or the minimum concentration by volume of carbon dioxide, in the gas mixture specified in combination 1 and 2 of that table, as a percentage at the point of minimum concentration in the chamber; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where combination 3 in that table is used, give clearly visible or audible warning signals where the final concentration by volume of oxygen rises above 5% for more than 30 seconds; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (iii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where combination 1 or 2 in that table is used, give clearly visible or audible warning signals where the final concentration by volume of carbon dioxide falls below 45% for more than 30 seconds; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] fitted with a means of visually monitoring birds in the chamber. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

#### 6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Operation of the chamber (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

The operator of the chamber must ensure that– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] every person engaged in the killing is instructed as to the method of operation of the chamber; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] birds are– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] rapidly rendered insensible to pain or distress; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] exposed to the gas mixtures mentioned in the second column of the table in Part V long enough to ensure they are killed and in any event for a period of– *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
    - (aa (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where combination 1 in that table is used, not less than 5 minutes; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
    - (bb (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where combination 2 in that table is used, not less than 2 minutes; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
    - (cc (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where combination 3 in that table is used, not less than 90 seconds; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] where the visible or audible warning signals provided for in paragraph 5(c)(ii) or 5(c)(iii) are activated, more of the gas mixture is immediately supplied to the chamber until the required concentrations are achieved; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] after exposure to a gas mixture nothing more is done to any bird until it is ascertained that it is dead. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
