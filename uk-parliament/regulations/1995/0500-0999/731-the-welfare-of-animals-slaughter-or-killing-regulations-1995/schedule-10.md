# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, SCHEDULE 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 10 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note) — METHODS OF KILLING FOX AND MINK (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 18 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Application (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9f371d1ca53b79724f9743bf4e6f3e92][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

This Schedule shall apply only to the killing of fox and mink which are farmed for their fur. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Killing fox which is farmed for its fur (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7f333b57f6a50e411a57334af049badc][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any fox which is farmed for its fur, except by administering to the fox a lethal injection of a drug which has anaesthetic properties and which causes a rapid loss of consciousness followed by death. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Killing mink which is farmed for its fur (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-eaca0a438477199163b922e20d48f4fd][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any mink which is farmed for its fur, except by one of the following methods— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] a lethal injection of a drug with anaesthetic properties; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] exposure to carbon monoxide; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] exposure to carbon dioxide. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing mink by a lethal injection of a drug with anaesthetic properties (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bc933c71a9d3041b9747d781f5d76a6b][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any mink which is farmed for its fur except by administering to the mink a lethal injection of a drug which has anaesthetic properties and which causes a rapid loss of consciousness followed by death. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing mink by exposure to carbon monoxide (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ce4a922bdbcdedb25c109a158884fe2b][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any mink by exposure to carbon monoxide unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the chamber in which the mink is exposed to the gas is designed, constructed and maintained in such a way as to— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] avoid injury to the mink; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] allow the mink to be supervised; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the mink is introduced into the chamber only after the chamber has been filled with a concentration of carbon monoxide of at least 1% by volume, supplied by a source of 100% carbon monoxide; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] gas produced by an engine specially adapted for that purpose is used only if— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the gas used has been suitably cooled and sufficiently filtered and is free from any irritant matter or irritant gas; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] no mink is placed in the chamber until the concentration of carbon monoxide has reached at least 1% by volume; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the gas induces deep general anaesthesia when inhaled and then causes certain death; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the mink remains in the chamber until it is dead. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

### 6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific requirements for killing mink by exposure to carbon dioxide (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0a8ecb77a69ea2117968577920ce01d8][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

No person shall kill, or cause or permit to be killed, any mink by exposure to carbon dioxide unless— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the chamber in which the mink is exposed to the gas is designed, constructed and maintained in such a way as to— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] avoid injury to the mink; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] allow the mink to be supervised; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the mink is introduced into the chamber only when the atmosphere contains the highest possible concentration of carbon dioxide supplied by a source of 100% carbon dioxide; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the gas induces deep general anaesthesia when inhaled and then causes certain death; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] the mink remains in the chamber until it is dead. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
