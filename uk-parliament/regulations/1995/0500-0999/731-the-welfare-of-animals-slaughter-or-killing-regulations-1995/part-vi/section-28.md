## 28 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Repeals, revocations and amendments (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4fe6bcee51ed413604b8f92ddc59bae0][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The enactments mentioned in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 13 are hereby repealed to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The enactments and regulations mentioned in Schedule 14 to these Regulations shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] The regulations mentioned in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 15 are hereby revoked. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
