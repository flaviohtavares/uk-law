# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 18

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). The killing of fox and mink which are farmed for their fur (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1eecc664f1dab22e1221489fc0035e7d][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The killing of any fox or mink which is farmed for its fur shall be in accordance with Schedule 10. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
