# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Birds awaiting slaughter at place of purchase (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8447ebc04afb2d258a7e8d728f282d42][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The occupier or person in charge of any premises at which birds are offered or exposed for sale prior to being slaughtered there shall ensure that, on arrival at the premises, the birds are forthwith— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] placed in accommodation in which they are able, without difficulty, to stand upright, turn around and stretch their wings; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] provided with a sufficient supply of wholesome food and clean drinking water. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
