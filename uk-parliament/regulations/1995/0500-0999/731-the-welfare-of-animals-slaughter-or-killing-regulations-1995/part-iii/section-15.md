## 15. The killing of animals elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard[^M_E_mo55s8f0-b277-zzrk-miga-67c1e0c44f7e6][^key-9b31f4cca43f1cb29c48da9dcbf100a2]

*Extent: S*

Subject to regulations 16 to 19, where any soliped, ruminant, pig, rabbit or bird is killed elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard, it shall be killed in accordance with Part I of Schedule 5 and paragraphs 13(a), (b), (c) and (e) and 14 of Part III of Schedule 5.
