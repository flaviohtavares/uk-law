# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). The slaughter of animals elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9f8ffad1bb98e0fb460f09cc794f5511][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to regulations 16 and 17, where any soliped, ruminant, pig, rabbit or bird is slaughtered elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard, that animal shall be— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] restrained in accordance with Schedule 4; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] [^key-d6df07f344998791b2c943f6004f351b]... stunned before slaughter in accordance with Parts I and II of Schedule 5; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] bled or pithed in accordance with Schedule 6. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
