# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 16

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). The slaughter or killing of animals elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard for private consumption (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b54dd5647d8c11131b6cbe4d69fefe5b][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 14 and 15 shall not apply in respect of any bird, rabbit, pig, sheep or goat which is slaughtered or killed by its owner for his private consumption, provided that— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] regulation 4(1) is complied with; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any such animal that is slaughtered is stunned before slaughter in accordance with Parts I and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] II *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] of Schedule 5. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
