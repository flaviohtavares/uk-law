# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 23

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Powers of authorised persons (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6730a8d7746e90e3767e215b8ba9dc7c][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] An authorised person, upon producing, if required to do so, some duly authenticated document showing his authority, may at any time enter— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any land or premises, other than premises used wholly or mainly as a dwelling, where he reasonably suspects that any activity which is governed by these Regulations is, or has been, carried on, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

for the purpose of ascertaining whether there is or has been any contravention of these Regulations. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] An authorised person shall have power to carry out all checks and examinations necessary for the enforcement of these Regulations. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (2) above, an authorised person may in particular, where he has a reasonable suspicion that there is or has been a contravention of these Regulations— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] take samples (and, if necessary, send the samples for laboratory testing) from any animal, carcase or part of a carcase; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] take away any carcase or part of a carcase (and, if necessary, send it for laboratory testing); *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] require the production of any relevant record or document; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] inspect any relevant record or document; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] take copies of any relevant record or document; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (f (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] take away any relevant record or document. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] An authorised person may take with him into the slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard or onto the premises or land such other persons as he considers necessary *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] [^key-b7343a949eb8d038ddcb43a17ca8ffc1]... *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
