# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 24

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Obstruction (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0eba384a9ef39e908c27208ce5b9c455][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] No person shall— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] intentionally obstruct any person acting in the execution of these Regulations; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] without reasonable cause, fail to give to any person acting in the execution of these Regulations any assistance or information which that person may reasonably require of him for the purposes of his functions under these Regulations; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] furnish to any person acting in the execution of these Regulations any information which he knows to be false or misleading. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Nothing in paragraph (1)(b) above shall be construed as requiring any person to answer any question or give any information if to do so might incriminate him. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
