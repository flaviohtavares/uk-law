# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 27

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Defence (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-209791f4e4da19d1cf99ed08aa6f5ca3][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who contravenes any provision in these Regulations shall not be guilty of an offence in respect of such contravention if he proves that by reason of accident or other emergency the contravention was necessary for preventing injury or suffering to any person or animal. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
