# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 25

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences by bodies corporate (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5b21c0945f9cd50666daac0e55eb726d][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Where a body corporate is guilty of an offence under these Regulations and the offence is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body corporate; or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] any person who was purporting to act in any such capacity, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

he as well as the body corporate shall be guilty of the offence and be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] For the purposes of paragraph (1) above, “director”, in relation to a body corporate whose affairs are managed by its members, means a member of the body corporate. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Where an offence under these Regulations is committed by a Scottish partnership and is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of, a partner, he as well as the partnership shall be guilty of the offence and be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
