# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995, section 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences and penalties (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6fcf493fb1c687edd4dae6b7c7a492d9][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311][^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Any person who contravenes any provision of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Any person guilty of an offence by virtue of regulation 4(2) or (3) or 24(1)(a) or (b) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] A person guilty of an offence by virtue of regulation 24(1)(c) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] A person guilty of any other offence under these Regulations shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or both. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]

(5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311] Paragraph (1) above shall not apply to anything done or omitted by the Minister or an authorised veterinary surgeon in exercise of functions conferred by these Regulations. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29e8ef3a5d57f823261f46df805d0311]
