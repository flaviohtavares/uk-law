# The Central Regional Council (Prohibition of Swimming, Bathing etc. in Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 538 (S.38)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Central Regional Council (Prohibition of Swimming, Bathing etc. in Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1995

Made

2nd March 1995

Coming into force

5th March 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Central Regional Council (Prohibition of Swimming, Bathing etc. in Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1995 and shall come into force on 5th March 1995.

**2.** The period during which the Central Regional Council (Prohibition of Swimming, Bathing etc. in Reservoirs) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 6th March 1996.

T D Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department,

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

2nd March 1995
