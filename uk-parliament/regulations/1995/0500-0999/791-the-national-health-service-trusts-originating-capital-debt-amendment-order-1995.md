# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Amendment Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1995/407](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/407) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1995 No. 791

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Amendment Order 1995

Made

14th March 1995

Coming into force

15th March 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 9(1) and (4) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Amendment Order 1995, and shall come into force on 15th March 1995.

(2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1995[^f00004].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

In the Schedule to the principal Order, for the amounts specified in columns (2), (3) and (4) in relation to the North Durham Acute Hospitals NHS Trust there shall be substituted the following amounts—

- (a) in column (2) (originating capital debt), £21,635,880;
- (b) in column (3) (initial loan), £10,817,940; and
- (c) in column (4) (public dividend capital), £10,817,940.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

14th March 1995

We consent

Derek Conway

Timothy Kirkhope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty\`s Treasury

14th March 1995

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1995 (“the principal Order”) which specifies the amounts of originating capital debt, initial loan and public dividend capital in relation to National Health Service trusts whose operational date was 1st April 1994. The amendments made by this Order correct errors in the amounts specified, in the Schedule to the principal Order, in relation to the North Durham Acute Hospitals NHS Trust.
