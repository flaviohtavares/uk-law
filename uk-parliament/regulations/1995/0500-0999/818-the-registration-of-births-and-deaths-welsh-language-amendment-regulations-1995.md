# The Registration of Births and Deaths (Welsh Language) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 818

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES ETC.

ENGLAND AND WALES

The Registration of Births and Deaths (Welsh Language) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

17th March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Registrar General in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5, 9(5), 10(1), 10A(1), 11(1)(b) and 39 of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953[^f00001] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the approval[^f00003] of the Secretary of State for Wales[^f00004], hereby makes the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births and Deaths (Welsh Language) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Registration of Births and Deaths (Welsh Language) Regulations 1987[^f00005].

## 2. Substitution of forms 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 9 in Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations

For forms 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 9 in Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations (prescribed forms)[^f00006] there shall be substituted respectively the forms 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 9 in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Revocation of regulation 9 of the principal Regulations

Regulation 9 of the principal Regulations (discrepancies) is hereby revoked.

Given under my hand on

P. J. Wormald

Registrar General

15th March 1995

I approve,

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make minor amendments to the Registration of Births and Deaths (Welsh Language) Regulations 1987.

The forms prescribed for the registration of live births and still-births and their associated declarations, the declaration and statement for the re-registration of a birth and the form of certificate of registration of birth are amended so as to delete any reference to an NHS number, to insert a specific reference to the occupation of the mother and to make other minor changes in layout. These amendments, amongst other changes, implement proposals in paragraphs 4.5 and 4.21 of the White Paper “Registration: proposals for change” (Cm 939) published in January 1990.

Regulation 3 revokes the provision in the 1987 Regulations that in the case of any discrepancy between an English text and a Welsh text permitted by the Regulations, the English text shall prevail.

These Regulations impose no costs on business.

Nid yw'r Rheoliadau hyn yn gosod unrhyw gost ar fusnes.
