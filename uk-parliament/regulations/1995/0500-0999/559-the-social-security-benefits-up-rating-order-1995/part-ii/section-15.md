# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1995, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995[^f00029], in paragraph (2) of regulation 18—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for the sum of £12.15 there shall be substituted the sum of £12.40;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £7.60 there shall be substituted the sum of £7.80; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for the sum of £3.80 there shall be substituted the sum of £3.90.
