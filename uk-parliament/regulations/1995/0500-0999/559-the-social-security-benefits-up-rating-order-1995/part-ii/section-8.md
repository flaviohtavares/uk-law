# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1995, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Earnings Limits

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) the sum specified in paragraph (a) is £125 and the sums specified in paragraph (b) are £16 and £125 respectively.
