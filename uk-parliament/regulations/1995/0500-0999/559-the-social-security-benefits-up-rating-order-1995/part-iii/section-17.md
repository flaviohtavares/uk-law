# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1995, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Disability Working Allowance

In the Disability Working Allowance Regulations—

- (a) in regulation 51(4) (determination of appropriate maximum disability working allowance), the capital sum prescribed is £3,000;
- (b) in regulation 51(4), (5) and (6), the amount specified for the allowance in respect of a child or young person is NIL;
- (c) in regulation 52(1) (applicable amount)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for the sum of £43.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £54.75;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £71.70 there shall be substituted the sum of £73.00; and
- (d) in paragraph 19(b)[^f00031] of Schedule 3 (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings), for the sum of £8.60 there shall be substituted the sum of £9.20; and
- (e) in Schedule 5 (determination of appropriate maximum disability working allowance) the sums prescribed shall be increased so as to have effect as set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.
