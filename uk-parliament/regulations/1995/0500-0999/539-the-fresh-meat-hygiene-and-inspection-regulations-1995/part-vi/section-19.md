# The Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Records of Inspections

(1) The Minister shall keep in respect of individual licensed premises, where appropriate, a record, for the purpose of compliance with the provisions of Council Directive [91/497/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0497), of the results of—

- (a) ante-mortem health inspections; and
- (b) post-mortem health inspections.

(2) The Minister shall retain the record of such an inspection until the end of the period of one year commencing with the date of the inspection to which it relates.
