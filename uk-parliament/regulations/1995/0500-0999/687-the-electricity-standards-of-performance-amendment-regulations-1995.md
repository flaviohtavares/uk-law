# The Electricity (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 687

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

9th March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Director General of Electricity Supply, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 39 and 60 of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001], after consultation with public electricity suppliers and with persons and bodies representative of persons likely to be affected by the exercise of the powers, after considering the results of research to discover the views of a representative sample of persons likely to be affected, and with the consent of the Secretary of State for Trade and Industry (as respects England and Wales) and the Secretary of State for Scotland (as respects Scotland) in accordance with section 39(1)(a) of the Electricity Act 1989, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Electricity (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 1995, and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

## 2. Amendment of the Electricity (Standards of Performance) Regulations 1993

Schedules 2 to 4 to the Electricity (Standards of Performance) Regulations 1993[^f00002] (“the Principal Regulations”) are revoked and substituted by Schedules 2 to 4 in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

**3.** Schedules 6 to 14 to the Principal Regulations are revoked and substituted by Schedules 6 to 14 in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

S. C. Littlechild,

The Director General of Electricity Supply

8th March 1995

We consent

Richard Page,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Industry and Energy,

Department of Trade and Industry

8th March 1995

George Kynoch,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Scottish Office

9th March 1995

- Schedules 8 and 9 contain a revised period in respect of the matter referred to in regulation 5(2)(a) of the Principal Regulations;

Schedules 1 and 5 to the Principal Regulations have not been amended.
