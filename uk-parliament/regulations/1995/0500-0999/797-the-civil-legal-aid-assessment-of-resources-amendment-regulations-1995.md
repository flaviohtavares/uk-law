# The Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 797

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

16th March 1995

Laid before Parliament

17th March 1995

Coming into force

10th April 1995

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 15(1), 16, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and transitional provisions

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 10th April 1995.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to applications for legal aid made on or after 10th April 1995 and applications made before that date shall be treated as if these Regulations had not come into force.

## 2. Amendments to the Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1989

Regulation 4 of the Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1989[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) in paragraph (2), “£7,187” shall be substituted for “£7,060”;
- (b) in paragraph (3), “£7,920” shall be substituted for “£7,780”;
- (c) in paragraph (4), “£2,425” shall be substituted for “£2,382”.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 14th March 1995

We consent,

Andrew Mitchell

Derek Conway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 16th March 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1989. The income limit for non–contributory civil legal aid is increased from £2,382 to £2,425. The upper income limit is increased from £7,060 to £7,187; for personal injury cases the upper income limit is increased from £7,780 to £7,920.
