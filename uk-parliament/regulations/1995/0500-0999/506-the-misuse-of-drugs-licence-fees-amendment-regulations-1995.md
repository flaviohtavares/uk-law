# The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 506

DANGEROUS DRUGS

The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

28th February 1995

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of sections 30, 31 and 37(1) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001], and after consultation with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

**2.** Regulation 3(1) of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£450” there shall be substituted “£462”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “ £300” there shall be substituted “£308”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for “ £150” there shall be substituted “£154”;
- (d) in sub-paragraph (d), for “ £150” there shall be substituted “£154”; and
- (e) in sub-paragraph (e), for “ £75” there shall be substituted “£77”.

M. Forsyth

Minister of State

Home Office

28th February 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 30 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 provides that a licence issued by the Secretary of State for the purposes of that Act or of Regulations made under it may be issued subject to the condition of the payment of a fee. The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 1986, as amended, prescribe the fee payable in relation to a licence to produce, supply or offer to supply or possess controlled drugs; the present Regulations further amend those Regulations so as to increase the fees there prescribed.
