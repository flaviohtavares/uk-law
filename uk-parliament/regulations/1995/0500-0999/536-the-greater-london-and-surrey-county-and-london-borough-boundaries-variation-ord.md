# The Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) (Variation) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1994/331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/331) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1995 No. 536

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

CHANGES IN LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREAS

The Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) (Variation) Order 1995

Made

1st March 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2), 69 and 266 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having complied with the procedures laid down in section 69(2) and (3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order, which varies supplementary provisions contained in the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994[^f00002], an order made by him pursuant to his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Act on 15th February 1994:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) (Variation) Order 1995, and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

## 2. Variation in respect of the Metropolitan Police District

(1) The Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994 is varied as provided in paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In articles 3(1) and (2), 4(1) and 6(1) the words “shall cease to form part of the Metropolitan Police District and” are omitted.

(3) In articles 3(3), 4(2) and 6(2) and (3)—

- (a) after sub–paragraph (c) the words “and to” are inserted;
- (b) sub–paragraph (e), and the words “and to” immediately before that sub– paragraph, are omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

1st March 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects defects in the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994 ([S.I. 1994/331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/331)). That Order purported to transfer to the Metropolitan Police District areas already in that District, and wrongly provided that certain areas should cease to be in the Metropolitan Police District.
