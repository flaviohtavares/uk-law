The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 45(1), 57(1) and 58(1) of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 57(12) of that Act and with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—[^key-631d09fa68d7e4baf43e92eb48d0f3a3]

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-2bae634214a50a6223bd3bcdc7579499]

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Fees) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-f98315e1c37efdf68f76da9184ce19c8]

(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, any reference to—

- (a) a numbered section is a reference to the section bearing that number in the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995,
- (b) a numbered regulation is a reference to the regulation bearing that number in these Regulations,
- (c) a numbered paragraph is a reference to the paragraph bearing that number in the regulation in which the reference appears, and
- (d)[^key-b2bab04c89b872b61ceb9368bf41416f] a numbered column or numbered fee is a reference to the column or fee bearing that number in the Schedule to the Regulations.

(2)[^key-5eea883cb2709bc5e1adbdbca25b0283] In these Regulations—

- “application for a licence”, “application for a variation of a licence”, “application” and “licence” have the same meaning as in the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995;
- “[^key-d3e52f0037a626916c1fa99ccbf67d49]**light goods vehicle**” has the meaning given in section 58(1);

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations replace the provisions in regulation 35 of the Goods Vehicles (Operators' Licences, Qualifications and Fees) Regulations 1984 ([SI 1984/176](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/176)) as amended which have been revoked by the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/2869](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2869)), and impose a new fee structure.

**2.** Regulation 3 and the Schedule set out what fees shall be payable and when those fees shall become due. Regulation 3(8) enables charges to be made by the traffic commissioner in accordance with an administrative arrangement made with the licence-holder.

**3.** Regulation 4 provides for unused vehicle fees covering 12 month periods to be refunded in specified circumstances. These cover surrender, suspension, curtailment or revocation for disciplinary reasons, as well as the voluntary reduction by the licence-holder of the number of specified vehicles by 10 or more or by 20 per cent.

**4.** Regulation 5 provides for a licence holder to transfer vehicle fees paid from a vehicle which is removed from the licence to one which is added to it.
