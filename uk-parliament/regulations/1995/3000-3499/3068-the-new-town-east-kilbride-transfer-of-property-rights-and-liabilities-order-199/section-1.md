# The New Town (East Kilbride) (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the New Town (East Kilbride) (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 31st December 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the council” means East Kilbride District Council being a council constituted in accordance with Part I of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00002];
- “development corporation” means the East Kilbride Development Corporation established by the New Town (East Kilbride) Development Corporation Order 1947[^f00003];
- “enactment” includes an order, regulation, rule or other instrument having effect by virtue of an Act;
- “recorded GRS (Lanark)” means recorded in the Division of the General Register of Sasines for the County of Lanark.

(3) A reference in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order to a “plan” followed by a number is a reference to the plan of that number annexed to the Schedule to this Order.
