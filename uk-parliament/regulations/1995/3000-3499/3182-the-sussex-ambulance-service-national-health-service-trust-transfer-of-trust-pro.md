# The Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3182

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

7th December 1995

Laid before Parliament

15th December 1995

Coming into force

8th January 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Worthing District Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the West Sussex Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 8th January 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the West Sussex Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 31st August 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 8th January 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

7th December 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Sussex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust on 8th January 1996 trust property formerly held by the West Sussex Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
