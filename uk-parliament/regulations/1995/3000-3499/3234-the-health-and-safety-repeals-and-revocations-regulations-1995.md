# The Health and Safety (Repeals and Revocations) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3234

HEALTH AND SAFETY

The Health and Safety (Repeals and Revocations) Regulations 1995

Made

11th December 1995

Coming into force

All regulations except regulation 2(3)

1st January 1996

Regulation 2(3)

1st January 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (1) of section 37 of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted the Health and Safety Commission (“the Commission”) in accordance with subsection (2) of that section, and having in accordance with subsection (3) of that section required the Commission to consult such persons as it considered appropriate for the purpose of deciding how it should respond to the consultation by the Secretary of State, and a draft of the following Regulations having been laid before Parliament in accordance with subsection (7) of that section and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament, hereby makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety (Repeals and Revocations) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996 except regulation 2(3), which shall come into force on 1st January 1997.

## 2. Repeals and revocations

(1) The enactments specified in column 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 shall be repealed to the extent specified in column 3 of that Part.

(2) The instruments specified in column 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 shall be revoked.

(3) The instruments specified in Schedule 2 shall be revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

11th December 1995
