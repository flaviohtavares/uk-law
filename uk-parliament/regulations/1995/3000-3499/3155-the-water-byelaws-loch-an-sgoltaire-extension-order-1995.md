# The Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3155 (S.230)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1995

Made

4th December 1995

Coming into force

10th December 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1995 and shall come into force on 10th December 1995.

**2.** The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch an Sgoltaire) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 11th December 1996.

T D Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, The Scottish Office

The Scottish Office Agriculture,

Environment and Fisheries Department,

Victoria Quay,

Edinburgh

4th December 1995
