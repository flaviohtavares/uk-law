# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3094 (S.225)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1995

Made

28th November 1995

Coming into force

1st January 1996

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001], section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to the provisions of the following sub-paragraphs, for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994[^f00003] substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

(2) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994 as substituted by sub-paragraph (1) above shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

(3) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994 having effect before the substitution under sub-paragraph (1) above shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

Hope of Craighead

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh,

28th November 1995

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994 (S.I. [1994/391](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/391)) by increasing the fees payable to Messengers-at-Arms by about 3.3%. The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) (No.2) 1994 (S.I. [1994/3268](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3268)).
