# Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3069 (S.223)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 1995

Made

24th November 1995

Coming into force

18th December 1995

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 1995 and shall come into force on 18th December 1995.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Lands Valuation Appeal Court

The judges named for the purposes of appeals under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879 shall be as follows:—

- The Honourable Lord Clyde,
- The Honourable Lord Cullen,
- The Honourable Lord Prosser,
- The Honourable Lord Milligan,
- The Honourable Lord Gill.

Hope of Craighead

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh,

24th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints the judges who may hear appeals under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879.
