# The Adopted Children Register and Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3158 (S.233)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Adopted Children Register and Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1995

Made

29th November 1995

Laid before Parliament

6th December 1995

Coming into force

1st January 1996

The Registrar General for Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the Adoption (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and with the approval of the Secretary of State, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Adopted Children Register and Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

## 2. Form of entry in the Adopted Children Register

The entry to be made in the Adopted Children Register pursuant to a direction contained in an adoption order shall be in the form set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

## 3. Form of entry in the Parental Order Register

The entry to be made in the Parental Order Register pursuant to a direction contained in a parental order shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## 4. Revocations

(1) The Adopted Children Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1991[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

(2) The Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1994[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify revised forms of the entries which are to be made on and after 1st January 1996 in—

- (a) the Adopted Children Register maintained by the Registrar General for Scotland; and
- (b) the Parental Order Register which is maintained by the Registrar General for Scotland in consequence of the Parental Orders (Human Fertilisation and Embryology) (Scotland) Regulations 1994 (S.I. [1994/2804](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2804)).

The entries will be made following a direction in an adoption order or a parental order as the case may be.

The revised forms of entry make minor changes to the style and layout of the forms of entry specified in the Adopted Children Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1991 and the Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1994 which previously specified the forms of entry and are revoked by these Regulations.
