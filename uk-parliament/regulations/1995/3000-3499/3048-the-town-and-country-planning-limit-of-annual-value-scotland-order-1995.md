# The Town and Country Planning (Limit of Annual Value) (Scotland) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3048 (S.221)

ACQUISITION OF LAND

The Town and Country Planning (Limit of Annual Value) (Scotland) Order 1995

Made

14th November 1995

Laid before Parliament

30th November 1995

Coming into force

1st January 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 181(4)(a) and 273(3) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Limit of Annual Value) (Scotland) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

## 2. Limit of Annual Value

The amount prescribed for the purposes of section 181(4)(a) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 is £21,500.

## 3. Revocation

The Town and Country Planning (Limit of Annual Value) (Scotland) Order 1985[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

14th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes £21,500 as the limit of annual value for the purposes of section 181 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 (which relates to the circumstances in which authorities may be obliged to purchase interests of owner-occupiers affected by planning proposals) and replaces the limit of £12,000 prescribed by the Town and Country Planning (Limit of Annual Value) (Scotland) Order 1985.
