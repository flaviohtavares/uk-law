# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Supervision of Parking) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3108

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Supervision of Parking) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 1995

Made

30th November 1995

Laid before Parliament

8th December 1995

Coming into force

31st December 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 6(3) and 15(6) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Supervision of Parking) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 31st December 1995.

## 2. Supervision of parking

(1) For regulation 2(1)(cc) of the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Supervision of Parking, Management of Vehicles and Security Work) (England) Regulations 1994[^f00002] there is substituted—

> - (cc) on or after 1st April 1997 by a county council or non-metropolitan district council which is not subject to a structural change; or

.

(2) Regulation 4 of the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 1995[^f00003] is amended as follows—

- (a) paragraph (a) is revoked; and
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “regulation 2(d)(ii)” there is substituted “paragraph (1)(d)(ii)”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

30th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Supervision of Parking, Management of Vehicles and Security Work) (England) Regulations 1994. The effect is to alter the date from which the compulsory competitive tendering requirements of the Local Government Act 1988 in respect of the supervision of parking apply to county councils and non-metropolitan district councils which are not subject to a structural change pursuant to local government reorganisation. The alteration substitutes for 1st October 1997, in relation to such county councils, a date of 1st April 1997 and, in relation to such non-metropolitan district councils, substitutes for 1st January 1996 a date of 1st April 1997. The amendment made replaces that made in relation to the supervision of parking by the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Competition) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and, in consequence, revokes regulation 4(a) of those Regulations. Regulation 4(b) of those Regulations is substituted, in order to correct a minor error in that instrument.
