# The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3340 (C.79)

ENERGY CONSERVATION

ENGLAND

The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 1995

Made

29th December 1995

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(2) and (3) of the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 1995.

## 2. Commencement

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

(2) Sections 3(1) and 4(1) and (2) of that Act shall come into force on 15th January 1996.

## 3. Application

This Order shall apply in relation only to energy conservation authorities whose areas are in England.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Jones

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

29th December 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force in England the following provisions of the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995—

- on 15th January 1996, sections 3(1) and 4(1)-(2), and
- on 1st April 1996, the remainder of the Act.

As regards England, there remain no further provisions of the Act to be brought into force.

The following provisions of the Act are brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order:
