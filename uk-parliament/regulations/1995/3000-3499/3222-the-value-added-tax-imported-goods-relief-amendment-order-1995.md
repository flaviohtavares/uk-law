# The Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 3222

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

6th December 1995

Laid before the House of Commons

11th December 1995

Coming into force

1st January 1996

Whereas Council Directive [83/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0181)[^f00001] determining the scope of Article 14(1)(d) of Council Directive [77/388/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0388)[^f00002] as regards exemption from VAT on final importation of certain goods, permitted exemptions on imports of certain goods of a total value not exceeding 22 ECU:

Whereas Council Directive [88/331/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0331)[^f00003] amended Council Directive [83/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0181) so as to require exemption from VAT on final importation of certain goods of a total value not exceeding 10 ECU and to permit such exemption in respect of such goods of a total value exceeding 10 ECU but not exeeding 22 ECU:

Whereas in conformity with Articles 22, 23 and 90 of Council Directive [83/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0181) the Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief Order 1984[^f00004] made provision for relief from VAT on imports of certain goods of a total value not exceeding £15:

Whereas it appears to the Treasury expedient that relief from VAT on imports of certain goods of a greater total value should be permitted:

Now, therefore, the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury, by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 37(1) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00005] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

**2.** In Group 8, Item No. 8 of Schedule 2 to the Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief Order 1984 for “£15” there shall be substituted “£18”.

Derek Conway

Bowen Wells

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

6th December 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Value Added Tax (Imported Goods) Relief Order 1984 (S.I. No. [1984/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/746)) which provides for relief from VAT on final importations of certain goods. It increases the maximum value for such relief on consignments of low value from £15 to £18.
