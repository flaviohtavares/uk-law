# The National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1645

NATIONAL LOTTERY

The National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) Order 1995

Made

25th June 1995

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1995

Coming into force

26th July 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to and section 60 of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and coming into force

This Order may be cited as the National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 26th July 1995.

## 2. Increase in membership

Paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 shall have effect with the substitution of the words “a chairman and twenty-one other members,” for the words “a chairman and sixteen other members,”.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

25th June 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the number of members to be appointed to the National Lottery Charities Board by the Secretary of State under paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the National Lottery etc. Act 1993.
