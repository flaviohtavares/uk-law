# The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Derek Compton Lewis) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1681

PENSIONS

The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Derek Compton Lewis) Regulations 1995

Made

3rd July 1995

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1995

Coming into force

25th July 1995

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for Derek Compton Lewis) Regulations 1995, and shall come into force on 25th July 1995.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations—

- “the 1971 Act” means the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971;
- “pension” means a pension within the meaning of the 1971 Act.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations the time when a pension “begins” is that stated in section 8(2) of the 1971 Act[^f00003].

## 3. Pensions to which the 1971 Act will apply

The 1971 Act shall have effect in relation to any pensions payable under the Pension Schemes for Mr Derek Compton Lewis (being schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00004]) as if they were pensions specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act.

Andrew Mitchell

Derek Conway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

3rd July 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to any pensions payable under the Pension Schemes for Mr Derek Compton Lewis (being superannuation schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972).
