# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**12.** An air traffic controller’s licence shall not be granted to a person under the age of 20 years (article 81(1)).
