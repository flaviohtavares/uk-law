# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. Competent authority for technical harmonisation

The Civil Aviation Authority shall be the authority responsible in the United Kingdom for carrying out the functions of the Authority under JAR-145.
