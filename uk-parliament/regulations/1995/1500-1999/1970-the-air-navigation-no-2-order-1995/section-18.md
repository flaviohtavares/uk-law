# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**18.** The purposes for which an aircraft may fly in accordance with A or B Conditions are extended and clarified (Schedule 2).
