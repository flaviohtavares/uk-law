# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**5.** The Civil Aviation Authority must issue a certificate of validation for any licence issued by the competent authority of a Member State of the European Community based on requirements equivalent to those for the equivalent licence granted by the Authority (article 23).
