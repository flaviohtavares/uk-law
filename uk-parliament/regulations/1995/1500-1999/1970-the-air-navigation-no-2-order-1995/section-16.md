# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**16.** Valuable consideration passing wholly within a group of companies is disregarded for the purpose of considering whether a flight is for the purpose of public transport (article 119(5)).
