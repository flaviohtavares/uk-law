# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** When flying over an area specified in one Order, suitable navigational equipment must be carried but this equipment is not required to be approved by the Civil Aviation Authority (article 20(4)).
