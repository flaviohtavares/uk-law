# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Records to be kept

The operator of a public transport aircraft registered in the United Kingdom shall, in respect of any flight by that aircraft during which it may fly at an altitude of more than 49,000 feet keep a record in a manner prescribed of the total dose of cosmic radiation to which the aircraft is exposed during the flight together with the names of the members of the crew of the aircraft during the flight.
