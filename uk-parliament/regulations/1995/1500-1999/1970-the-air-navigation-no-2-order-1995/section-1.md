# The Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** The requirement for an aircraft to be maintained in accordance with the Order and to have a certificate of release to service issued in accordance with those requirements does not apply to an aircraft which is required to be maintained in accordance with JAR-145. JAR-145 is a joint aviation requirement annexed to Council Regulation [(EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3922) on the harmonisation of technical requirements and administrative procedures in the field of civil aviation. JAR-145 applies to aircraft flying for the purpose of commercial transport (articles 10, 11, 12 and 13).
