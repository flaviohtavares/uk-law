# The Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1556

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1995

Made

19th May 1995

Whereas Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig holds an appointment under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001] as a water undertaker ( “the Company”):

And whereas the company has, pursuant to section 182(1)(a) of that Act, submitted to the Secretary of State for his approval a Code of Practice[^f00002] with respect to the exercise by the Company of powers conferred by or by virtue of sections 159 and 161(2) of that Act ( “the Code of Practice”):

And whereas the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to approve the Code of Practice for the purpose of promoting what appears to him to be desirable practices with regard to the exercise by the Company of those powers:

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers under section 182(2) of the Water Industry Act 1991, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1995.

## 2. Code of Practice

The Code of Practice prepared by the Company and certified as such by the Secretary of State is hereby approved.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

G C G Craig

An Under Secretary in the Welsh Office

19th May 1995
