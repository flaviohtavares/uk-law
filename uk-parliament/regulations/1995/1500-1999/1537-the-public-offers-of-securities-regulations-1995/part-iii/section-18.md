# The Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Penalties

Where these Regulations amend, extend the application of, or modify the effect of, a provision contained in Part IV of the Act and thereby create a new criminal offence which, but for this regulation, would be punishable to a greater extent than is permitted under paragraph 1(1)(d) of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972[^f00013], the maximum punishment for the offence shall be the maximum permitted under that paragraph at the time the offence was committed, on conviction on indictment or on summary conviction, as the case may be.
