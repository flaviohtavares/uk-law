# The Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 19th June 1995.

(2) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland.
