# The Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Revocation

The Companies Act 1985 (Mutual Recognition of Prospectuses) Regulations 1991[^f00015] and the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (Mutual Recognition of Prospectuses) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00016] are hereby revoked.
