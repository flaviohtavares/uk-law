# The Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Designations

For the purposes of Articles 11, 12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22 of Council Directive No. [89/298/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0298)[^f00014] the Treasury may designate such bodies as they think fit and they shall give notice in such manner as they think appropriate of the designations they have made.
