# The Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Registration of documents by the registrar of companies

For the purposes of the provisions mentioned in section 735B of the Companies Act 1985[^f00017], regulations 4(2) and 10(1) shall be regarded as provisions of the Companies Acts.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: [1985 c. 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6): section 735B was inserted by section 127(5) of the Companies Act [1989 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/40).
