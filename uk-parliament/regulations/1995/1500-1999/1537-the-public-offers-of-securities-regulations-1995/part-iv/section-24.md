> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendments to regulations made under the Banking Act 1987

Schedule 5 to these Regulations shall have effect to amend regulations made under the Banking Act 1987[^f00018].
