# The Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1540

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

14th June 1995

Laid before Parliament

22nd June 1995

Coming into force

17th July 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(7) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 17th July 1995.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the principal order” means the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) Order 1987[^f00002]; and
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to the principal order.

## 3. Amendment of the principal order

(1) The principal order shall be amended in accordance with articles 4, 5, 6 and 7 below.

## 4. Bodies ceasing to be designated

Items 32, 33, 61, 70 and 71 of the Schedule shall be omitted.

This Order amends the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) Order 1987. Five bodies cease to be designated under section 19 of the Transport Act 1985, six bodies are newly designated, the current names and addresses of three bodies are substituted for those given in the 1987 Order and the current addresses of twelve designated bodies are so substituted.
