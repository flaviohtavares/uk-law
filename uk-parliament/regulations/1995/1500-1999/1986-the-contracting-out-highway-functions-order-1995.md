Made

Coming into force

25th July 1995

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament under section 77(2) of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994[^f00001],

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 69[^f00002] and 77(1)(b) of that Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-b482467e91aaf1b4c202616c3ef3baa9]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Highway Functions) Order 1995 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.
