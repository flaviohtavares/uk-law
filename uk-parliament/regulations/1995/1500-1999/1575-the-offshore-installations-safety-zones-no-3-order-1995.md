# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1575

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 1995

Made

21st June 1995

Coming into force

22nd June 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

**1.** This order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 22nd June 1995.

**2.** The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 2) Order 1995 1995 [^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State

Phillip Oppenheim

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

21st June 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order revokes the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 2) Order 1995.
