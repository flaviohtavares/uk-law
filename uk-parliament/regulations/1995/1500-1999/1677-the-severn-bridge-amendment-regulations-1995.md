# The Severn Bridge (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1677

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Severn Bridge (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

28th June 1995

Laid before Parliament

11th July 1995

Coming into force

1st August 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 21(1) of the Severn Bridges Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Severn Bridge (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st August 1995.

**2.** Regulation 12(6) of the Severn Bridge Regulations 1993[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

(a) In sub-paragraph (a) for “£80” there shall be substituted “£100” and for “£40” there shall be substituted “£50”;

(b) In sub-paragraph (b) for “£35” there shall be substituted “£40” and for “£17.50” there shall be substituted “£20”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Watts

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

28th June 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 12(6) of the Severn Bridge Regulations 1993 by increasing the rates of charge payable for the removal, repair, refuelling or adjustment of vehicles. The rates were last set by the Severn Bridge (Amendment) Regulations 1994, which came into force on 1st August 1994.
