# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1649

FAMILY LAW

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

28th June 1995

Laid before Parliament

29th June 1995

Coming into force

2nd October 1995

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Part I of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 2nd October 1995.

**2.** In Schedule 1 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002] (“the 1991 Order”), under the heading “**Wales and Chester Circuit**”, after “Newport (Gwent) County Court” there shall be inserted “Pontypridd County Court”.

**3.** In column (ii) of Schedule 2 to the 1991 Order, for “Merthyr Tydfil County Court” wherever it appears, there shall be substituted “Pontypridd County Court”.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 28th June 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 by nominating Pontypridd County Court as a Family Hearing Centre and by listing it as a care centre for the purposes of the Order in place of Merthyr Tydfil County Court.
