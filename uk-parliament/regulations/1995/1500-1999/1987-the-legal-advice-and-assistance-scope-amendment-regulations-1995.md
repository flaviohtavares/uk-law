# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1987

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

21st July 1995

Coming into force

22nd July 1995

Whereas a draft of these Regulations has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

## 2. Transitional provisions

These Regulations shall not apply in relation to proceedings in which a magistrates' court has begun to inquire into a case as examining justices before the day on which section 44 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00002] comes into force, and the Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) Regulations 1989[^f00003] shall apply to such proceedings as if these Regulations had not come into force.

## 3. Amendment to the Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) Regulations 1989

In regulation 7(3) of the Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) Regulations 1989, for “committal proceedings” there shall be substituted “the hearing of oral representa-tions supporting or opposing an application for dismissal under section 6 of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980[^f00004]”.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 21st July 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 44 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 abolishes committal for trial and substitutes a new procedure of transfer for trial. These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance (Scope) Regulations 1989 by substituting a reference to an oral hearing under the new procedure for the reference to committal proceedings in the regulation excluding such proceedings from the scope of ABWOR.
