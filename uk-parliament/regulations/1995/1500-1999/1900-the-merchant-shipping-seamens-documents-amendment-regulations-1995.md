# The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Documents) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1900

MERCHANT SHIPPING

MASTERS AND SEAMEN

The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Documents) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

18th July 1995

Laid before Parliament

26th July 1995

Coming into force

1st September 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, after consulting with the organisations referred to in section 99(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1970[^f00001] (hereinafter called “the Act”), in exercise of powers conferred by section 71 of the Act and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Documents) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st September 1995.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Documents) Regulations 1987[^f00003] shall be amended by omitting, from Schedule 3, entry number 11 (National insurance number).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Goschen

Parliamentary Under Secretary for State,

Department of Transport

18th July 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Documents) Regulations 1987, to remove the requirement that a discharge book (and an application for a discharge book) contain the seaman’s National Insurance number.
