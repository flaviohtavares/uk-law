# The Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2945 (C.64)

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made

10th November 1995

In exercise of the power conferred on me by section 2 of the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

**2.** The Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 shall come into force on 17th November 1995.

P. B. B. Mayhew

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

10th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 17th November 1995 the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995.
