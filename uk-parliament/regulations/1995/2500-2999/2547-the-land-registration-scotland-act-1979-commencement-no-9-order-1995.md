# The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.9) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2547 (C.50) (S.184)

LAND REGISTRATION, SCOTLAND

The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.9) Order 1995

Made

25th September 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 30(2) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.9) Order 1995.

**2.** Sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 shall come into force on 1st April 1996 in the areas, for the purpose of registration of writs, of the County of Aberdeen and the County of Kincardine.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th September 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1996 in the areas of the County of Aberdeen and the County of Kincardine section 2(1) and (2) (which provides for the circumstances in which an interest in land shall be registrable) and section 3(3) (which provides that certain persons are to obtain a real right only by registration) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 have been brought into force by commencement orders made before the date of this Order in relation to the following areas:—
