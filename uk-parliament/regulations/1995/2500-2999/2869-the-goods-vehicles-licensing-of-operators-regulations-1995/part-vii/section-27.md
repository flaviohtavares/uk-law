# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 27

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Issue of copies of licences and discs[^key-eeb3371101ef84850032a49a5520c75f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If a licence or disc has been lost, destroyed or defaced, the person to whom it was issued shall forthwith notify in writing a traffic commissioner[^key-2b896233304401f84bdc82ec7343dd7a].

(2) If—

- (a) a traffic commissioner[^key-614dd5549992c3d4f1f1ab9fd7968949] is satisfied that a licence or disc has been lost, destroyed or defaced; and
- (b) in the case of a licence or disc which has been defaced, it is surrendered to a traffic commissioner,

a traffic commissioner shall issue a copy (so marked) which shall have effect as the original licence or disc.

(3) Where a licence or disc has been lost and after a copy has been issued the lost licence or disc is found by or comes into the possession of the licence-holder he shall forthwith return the original licence or disc to a traffic commissioner[^key-09825eec9116307a3c0f4eb3a7e1d053].
