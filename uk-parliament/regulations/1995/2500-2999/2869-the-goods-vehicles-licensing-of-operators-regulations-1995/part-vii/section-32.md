# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 32

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Offences[^key-6e6ebe91891e175c11ca9486863edc6c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any contravention of, or failure to comply with, a provision in regulations 23(3), 23(4), 25, 26, 27(1), 27(3), 28(1), 28(2), 28(3), 28(4) or 30(6), is hereby declared to be an offence and for the purposes of section 57(9) any provision mentioned above shall be regarded as made under the 1995 Act.
