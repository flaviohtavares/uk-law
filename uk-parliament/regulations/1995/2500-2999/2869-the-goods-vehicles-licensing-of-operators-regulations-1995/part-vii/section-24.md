# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 24

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Temporary addition of a motor vehicle[^c-cea3794791d571d142094449bb2ca7ef]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where—

- (a) a motor vehicle specified in an operator’s licence (“the specified vehicle") has been rendered unfit for service, or withdrawn from service for overhaul or repair, and the licence-holder informs a traffic commissioner[^c-1a411307bd836d4b01b28799fe51ed8e] of his desire to have a variation of the licence specifying another motor vehicle in its place (“the additional vehicle"); or
- (b) the specified vehicle has been rendered fit for service again, and the licence-holder informs a traffic commissioner of his desire to have a variation of the licence whereby the additional vehicle will cease to be specified on the licence,

the provisions of regulations 4 and 5 shall not apply.
