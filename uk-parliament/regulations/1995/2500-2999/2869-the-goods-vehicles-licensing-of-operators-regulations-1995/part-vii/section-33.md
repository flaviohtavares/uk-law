# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 33

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Classes of vehicle for which a licence is not required[^key-34a1bb44cb20fe35157fa1af70b55024]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The clasess of vehicle specified under section 2(2)(d) as those to which section 2(1) does not apply are the classes mentioned in Parts 1 and 3[^key-6018ef3685bb91c27f3d95dd884f8b31] of Schedule 3.

(2) The relevant plated weight of a goods vehicle, for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the 1995 Act (meaning of “small goods vehicle") is the gross weight not to be exceeded in Great Britain of the vehicle as shown on a Ministry plate as defined in column 2 of the Table in regulation 3(2) of the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 or, if no such plate has been issued in respect of that vehicle, the maximum gross weight of the vehicle as shown on a plate affixed to the vehicle by virtue of regulation 66 of those Regulations.
