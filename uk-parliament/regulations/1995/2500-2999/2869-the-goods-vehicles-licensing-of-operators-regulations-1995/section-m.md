# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section m

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**m)** regulation 36 interprets “relevant weight" for the purpose of determining whether a goods vehicle’s weight exceeds a weight restriction specified in an operator’s licence.
