# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Notice of application for heavy goods vehicle licence or variation of heavy goods vehicle licence[^c-9d837620a9d63b36aac62ad9c8224be4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 7(2)

**1.**[^c-803f55f76410f72f02df993e6bae4bb8] Information to be given in Notice of Application—

- (a) Name of applicant.
- (b) Trading name, if any.
- (c) Address for receipt of correspondence.
- (d) Whether the application is in respect of a new licence, or the variation of a licence.
- (e) The place or places proposed to be used as an operating centre or centres (including, if available, the postal address or addresses).
- (f) The number of heavy goods vehicles[^c-77ef6aeb1f88767c9c1f55d8f8e0348c] proposed to be kept at each operating centre or centres.
- (g) The number of heavy goods vehicles[^c-71c033cf62d97d4224f0933c8fed0d92] now kept, if different.
- (h) In respect of an existing licence, details of any proposed changes to or removal of existing conditions or undertakings affecting an operating centre.

**2.**[^c-1fe7660531c648c01462ab0f33918379] Every notice shall contain the following wording:

> Owners or occupiers of land (including buildings) near the operating centre(s) who believe that their use or enjoyment of that land would be affected, should make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at \[[^c-7a73caba60dc784c3d1d4fd23216154c]*specify the address*\] stating their reasons, within 21 days of this notice. Representors must at the same time send a copy of their representations to the applicant at the address given at the top of this notice. A Guide to making representations is available from \[*specify the address*\].
