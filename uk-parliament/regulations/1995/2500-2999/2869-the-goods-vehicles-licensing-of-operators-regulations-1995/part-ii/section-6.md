## 6. Dispensations as to applications[^c-0bce1f1fd8ce9c52b2eee622438a9b95]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A traffic commissioner[^c-2dd1ec3e66b90ecd1963b36d273a24e5] may consider an application notwithstanding that the requirement specified in regulation 5 has not been complied with.
