# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 5

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Time of applications[^c-2ac06da04d61878a9d26258bc557d506]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every application shall be sent to a traffic commissioner[^c-f764489aaf6225d78909f793baf41405] so as to reach him not less than 9 weeks before the time at which the applicant desires the licence or variation applied for to take effect.
