# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Regulations 1995, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

**8.** Part VI (regulations 21 and 22) prescribes matters relating to the content, publication and availability of statements (known as “Applications and Decisions") by a traffic commissioner, and relating to the provision of reasons for his decisions.
