# The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2621

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

5th October 1995

Laid before Parliament

23rd October 1995

Coming into force

24th October 1995

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1A(1) and (2) of the Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 1987[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 24th October 1995.

**2.** For the Schedule to the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

Home Office

Ann Widdecombe

Minister of State

5th October 1995

## SCHEDULE — COUNTRIES OR TERRITORIES WHOSE NATIONALS OR CITIZENS NEED A VISA FOR PASSING THROUGH THE UNITED KINGDOM

Article 2

- Afghanistan
- People’s Republic of China
- Ghana
- Iran
- Iraq
- Libya
- Nigeria
- Somalia
- Sri Lanka
- Turkey
- Uganda
- Zaire

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993 by adding China, Ghana and Nigeria to, and removing Lebanon from, the list of countries or territories whose nationals or citizens need a visa for passing through the United Kingdom.
