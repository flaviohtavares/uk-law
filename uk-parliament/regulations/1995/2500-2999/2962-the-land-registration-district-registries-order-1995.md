# The Land Registration (District Registries) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2962

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration (District Registries) Order 1995

Made

15th November 1995

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 132(1) of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00001], and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This order, which supersedes the Land Registration (District Registries) Order 1991[^f00002], may be cited as the Land Registration (District Registries) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

(2) In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “district land registry” means a district registry of the Land Registry within the meaning of section 132 of the Land Registration Act 1925;
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. District Land Registries

The districts of the Gloucester, Plymouth, Portsmouth and Weymouth District Land Registries are altered so that the district of each of the district land registries specified in column 1 of the Schedule comprises the administrative areas specified opposite its name in column 2 of the Schedule.

We concur,

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 6th of November 1995

Simon Burns

David Willets

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 15th November 1995

This Order, which supersedes the Land Registration (District Registries) Order 1991, alters the areas served by the Gloucester, Plymouth, Portsmouth and Weymouth District Land Registries by transferring responsibility for the registration of titles:—

- (a) in South Gloucestershire and Bristol from the Plymouth to the Gloucester District Land Registry;
- (b) in Wiltshire from the Gloucester to the Weymouth District Land Registry;
- (c) in West Sussex from the Weymouth to the Portsmouth District Land Registry.

The districts of the remaining district registries at Birkenhead, Coventry, Croydon, Durham, Harrow, Kingston upon Hull, Leicester, Lytham, Nottingham, Peterborough, Stevenage, Swansea, Telford, Tunbridge Wells and York are unaltered.
