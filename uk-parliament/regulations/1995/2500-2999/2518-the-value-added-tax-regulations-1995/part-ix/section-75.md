# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 75

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Change in VAT[^c-93550b3e6836174f4c7464892dd4700a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where pursuant to any enactment there is a change in the VAT charged on any supply, including a change to or from no VAT being charged on such supply, a retailer using any scheme shall take such steps relating to that scheme as are directed in any notice applicable to him or as may be agreed between him and the Commissioners.
