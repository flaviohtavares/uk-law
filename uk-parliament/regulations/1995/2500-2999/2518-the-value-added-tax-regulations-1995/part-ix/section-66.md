# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Interpretation of Part IX[^key-baa217409aa1746c302907545ef320e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “flat-rate trader” has the meaning given in regulation 55A;[^key-c7e65b9985a9a104c9601fdd80e5dd1c]
- “notice” means any notice or leaflet published by the Commissioners pursuant to this Part;
- “scheme” means a method as referred to in regulation 67.
