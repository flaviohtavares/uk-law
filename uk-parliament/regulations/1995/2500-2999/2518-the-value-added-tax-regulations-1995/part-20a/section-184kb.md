# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 184KB

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**184KB.**[^key-74337c76ddd59b8964239dd5ddaefe75] For regulation 173X read—

> **173X.** (1) Where—
>
> - (a) any amount has been paid to any person by way of interest under regulation 173W, but
> - (b) that person was not entitled to that amount under that regulation,
>
> the amount to which the person was not entitled shall be treated as if it were an amount of repayment interest recoverable as late payment interest under paragraph 6 of Schedule 54A to the Finance Act 2009 (certain amounts of repayment interest recoverable as late payment interest).
>
> (2) For the purpose of this regulation the requirement to meet Conditions A to C in paragraph 7 of Schedule 54A is to be treated as met.
