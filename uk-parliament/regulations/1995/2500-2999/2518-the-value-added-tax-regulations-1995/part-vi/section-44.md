# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 44

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Interpretation of Part VI[^c-10b2f4d806627516f79561bc68ef6756]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “body corporate” means a body corporate which is under a duty to make payments on account by virtue of the Value Added Tax (Payments on Account) Order 1993[^f00008] and “relevant division” means a division of a body corporate by reference to the business of which that body corporate is under such a duty;
- “payments on account” has the same meaning as in the Value Added Tax (Payments on Account) Order 1993.
