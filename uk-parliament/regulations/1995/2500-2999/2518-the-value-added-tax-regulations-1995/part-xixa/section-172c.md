# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 172C

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172C. Interpretation of Part XIXA[^key-30eb849ea500c2377d02d94378419ee4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any expression used in this Part to which a meaning is given in Part XIX of these Regulations shall, unless the contrary intention appears, have the same meaning in this Part as it has in that Part.
