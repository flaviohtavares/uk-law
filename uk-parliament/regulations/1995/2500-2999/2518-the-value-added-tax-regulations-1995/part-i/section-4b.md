# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 4B

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**4B.**[^key-c79058e46800932b66f892dfec3ece7b] (1) A specified communication is—

- (a) an application under section 43B(1), (2)(d) or (3) of the Act (Groups: applications);
- (b) a notification under regulation 5(1), (2) or (3) (registration and notification);
- (c) an application under regulation 6(1)(d) (transfer of a going concern);
- (d) a notification under regulation 10(1) or (4) (VAT representatives);
- (e) an application under regulation 52(1) (annual accounting scheme: eligibility);
- (f) a notification under regulation 54(2) (annual accounting scheme: termination);
- (g) a notification under regulation 55(1)(d) (annual accounting scheme: termination);
- (h) a notification under regulation 55B(1)(a) (flat-rate scheme for small businesses: notification of desire to join the scheme); [^key-c461673b401cba4293c58fef6a9e9305]...
- (i) a notification under regulation 55Q(1)(e) (flat-rate scheme for small businesses: notification of decision to withdraw from the scheme); [^key-dccc98bbdf20120cfc75ff22094d0504]...[^key-d8c85135be713717a018e57ad5070a9b]
- (j) a notification under paragraph (3) , (3A) or (4)[^key-1ef9e13bfc2a90389c63e7c34a0f4fee] of regulation 148A (notification of the arrival in the United Kingdom of motorised land vehicles and payment of VAT); and[^key-1d6558919faf9c117ba5679cdd29c024]
- (k) claim under regulation 201 (claim for refund of VAT made pursuant to section 35 of, or paragraph 18A of Schedule 9ZA to, the Act.
