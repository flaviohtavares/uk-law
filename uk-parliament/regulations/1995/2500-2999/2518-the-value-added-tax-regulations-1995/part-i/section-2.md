# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 2

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation—general[^c-7761ea5de4205a964978d8783a0ff37b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) In these Regulations unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Act” means the Value Added Tax Act 1994 and any reference to a Schedule to the Act includes a reference to a Schedule as amended from time to time by Order of the Treasury;
- “alphabetical code” means the alphabetical prefix as set out below which shall be used to identify the[^c-8327a9c5ed88c7adb936a041ed4f4f4e] relevant territory[^c-56d65bfa4e6b4bf77aa099de9af02a89]—
  - Austria — AT
  - Belgium — BE
  - Bulgaria — BG[^c-d3f4058f16037fcf0394859f35465618]
  - Cyprus — CY
  - Czech Republic — CZ
  - Denmark — DK
  - Estonia — EE
  - Finland — FI
  - France — FR
  - Germany — DE
  - Greece — EL
  - Hungary — HU
  - Ireland — IE
  - Italy — IT
  - Latvia — LV
  - Lithuania — LT
  - Luxembourg — LU
  - Malta — MT
  - Netherlands — NL
  - Poland — PL
  - Portugal — PT
  - Romania — RO[^c-32602bd6fa7711147411b12b9b07cd0e]
  - Slovakia — SK
  - Slovenia — SI
  - Spain — ES
  - Sweden — SE
  - United Kingdom — XI[^c-bb47d853d2e3100cf400fa5ac58fc8aa]
- “Collector” includes Deputy Collector and Assistant Collector;
- “the Community” means the European Community;
- “continental shelf” means a designated area within the meaning of the Continental Shelf Act 1964[^f00002];
- “Controller” means the Controller, Customs and Excise Value Added Tax Central Unit;
- [^c-e1e4be29a0c63379bcd575f4979c200d]...
- “fiscal or other warehousing regime” means “fiscal warehousing regime or warehousing regime”[^c-7a0c1a87519a08d400d1540de74ad939] , and “[^c-05aa7f47a0d502cc467ba4da44adee9d]**Northern Ireland fiscal or other Northern Ireland warehousing regime**” means “Northern Ireland fiscal warehousing regime or Northern Ireland warehousing regime”;
- “[^c-5eada4ffe11124e574d01548496d0904]**identified for the purposes of VAT in Northern Ireland**” has the meaning given by paragraph 7 of Schedule 9ZA to the Act;
- “Northern Ireland fiscal warehouse” and “Northern Ireland fiscal warehousing regime” have the meanings given by sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) respectively of paragraph 25 (supplementary provision) of Schedule 9ZB to the Act;
- “Northern Ireland warehouse” and “Northern Ireland warehousing regime” have the meanings given by sub-paragraphs (11) and (12) respectively of paragraph 16 (place and time of supply: Northern Ireland warehouses) of Schedule 9ZB to the Act;
- [^c-e1e4be29a0c63379bcd575f4979c200d]...
- [^c-e1e4be29a0c63379bcd575f4979c200d]...
- “prescribed accounting period”, subject to regulation 99(1), means a period such as is referred to in regulation 25;
- “proper officer” means the person appointed or authorised by the Commissioners to act in respect of any matter in the course of his duties;
- [^c-34044a5506da176e81fc433500b085da]...
- “registered person” means a person registered by the Commissioners under Schedule 1,[^c-1c6e689aa66f756f0a6fc155b2795ac6] 1A,[^c-5a9bd4a5ae81cfc0a236ef7e0919cc0c] 3A, Part 8 or 9 of Schedule 9ZA or Schedule[^c-1375ad2c002e68ec87a3b3e19f873079] or 9ZC[^c-1a395341766bb84b38edfa74a28e7489] to the Act;
- “registration number” means the number allocated by the Commissioners to a taxable person in the certificate of registration issued to him;
- “[^c-a3fc17ed9c5f8121c8bfe0000d24b48c]**relevant territory**” means, except where otherwise provided, a member State or the United Kingdom;
- “return” means a return which is required to be made in accordance with regulation 25;
- “specified date” means the date specified in a person’s application for registration for the purpose of VAT as that on which he expects to make his first taxable supply.
- [^c-e1e4be29a0c63379bcd575f4979c200d]...

(2) A reference in these Regulations to “this Part” is a reference to the Part of these Regulations in which that reference is made.

(3) In these Regulations any reference to a form specified in a notice published by the Commissioners[^c-ea40aa6fd791113973a818dc808799f6] shall include a reference to a form which the Commissioners are satisfied is a form to the like effect.

(4)[^c-01eb43115fb02742e0c7baff04ee64da] A reference in these Regulations to “another member State” is to be read as a reference to “a member State”, and “other member State” and “other member States” are to be interpreted accordingly.
