# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 145S

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145S. Fiscal warehousing certificates[^key-b0c2d0f703543376740abee55bf98151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The certificate referred to in paragraph 19(1)(d) of Schedule 9ZB to the Act (certificate relating to acquisitions in or intended for fiscal warehousing) and the certificate referred to in paragraph 19(3)(d) of Schedule 9ZB to the Act (supplies of goods intended for fiscal warehousing) must contain the information indicated in the form specified in a notice published by the Commissioners;

(2) A certificate prepared under paragraph 19(1)(d) of Schedule 9ZB by an acquirer who is not a taxable person must be kept by that person for a period of six years commencing on the day the certificate is prepared; and the person must produce it to a proper officer when that officer requests the person to do so.
