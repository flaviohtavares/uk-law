# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 43D

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43D. Repayments to the Commissioners (affected by repeal, see note)Notifications and repayments to the Commissioners[^key-2a14aad12a600985c9114a620061f4ed][^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836][^key-81ddd63c5421647fcec17b64997df8e7][^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The claimant shall, without prior demand, make any repayment to the Commissioners that he is required to make by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836] regulation 43C(d) and (e)[^key-e7cb197ca426922ecb79ad2f87707732] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836] above within 14 days of the expiration of the period of 90 days referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836] regulation 43C(a)[^key-429e240fed09b2208f4909c8f504b467] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836] above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7bd8f49790c6b625c87617d908327836]

The claimant shall give any notification to the Commissioners that he is required to give by virtue of regulation 43C(d) above and, without any prior demand, make any repayment to the Commissioners that he is required to make by virtue of regulation 43C(da) and (e) above within 14 days of the expiration of the 90 days referred to in regulation 43C(a) above.
