# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 43H

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43H. Reimbursement arrangements made before 11th February 1998[^c-2a14aad12a600985c9114a620061f4ed][^c-81ddd63c5421647fcec17b64997df8e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Reimbursement arrangements made by a claimant before 11th February 1998 shall not be disregarded for the purposes of section 80(3) of the Act if, not later than 11th March 1998— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f]

- (a) he includes in those arrangements (if they are not already included) the provisions described in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f] regulation 43C[^c-880a4f0bee44abd57d00750ef145819a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f] above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f]
- (b) gives the undertakings described in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f] regulation 43G[^c-635cc3d73147893a69184229080626c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f] above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a0e4f845692cf672139e37101912f53f]
