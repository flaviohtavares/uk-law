# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 43B

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43B. Reimbursement arrangements—general[^key-2a14aad12a600985c9114a620061f4ed][^key-81ddd63c5421647fcec17b64997df8e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Without prejudice to regulation 43H[^key-f606b3ab7aea5d0a6df4ad39986c56ee] below, for the purposes of section 80(3) of the Act (defence by the Commissioners that repayment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-477041a20dd26fd30ba0e924692f5989] crediting by them of an amount claimed would unjustly enrich the claimant) reimbursement arrangements made by a claimant shall be disregarded except where they—

- (a) include the provisions described in regulation 43C[^key-b58ba9dee4caec7cb264fd7a289c5b7b] below; and
- (b) are supported by the undertakings described in regulation 43G[^key-86852871dd5587994cbc727a3d1bb16e] below.
