# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 155

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Supplies of new means of transport to persons departing Northern Ireland for a member State[^key-2f274a4fd3057ffed7ac36e0ae49c77d][^key-8da3364fca8c86d1b755556ad4efdd78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners may, on application by a person who is not taxable in a member State[^key-e485f1ef987f3413cdce85748ffd225e] and who intends—

- (a) to purchase a new means of transport in Northern Ireland[^key-2eb72015b1692720b05bf107edafbdad], and
- (b) to remove that new means of transport from Northern Ireland[^key-74dea722eefc6ba0f481261c94955b21] to a member State,

permit that person to purchase a new means of transport without payment of VAT, for subsequent removal to a member State within 2 months of the date of supply and its supply, subject to such conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.
