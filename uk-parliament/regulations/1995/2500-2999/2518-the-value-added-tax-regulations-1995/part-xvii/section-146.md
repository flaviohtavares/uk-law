# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 146

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Interpretation of Part XVII[^c-785dc0ae3777784a5e7057a9218ad1f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “claim” means a claim for a refund of VAT made pursuant to paragraph 19 of Schedule 9ZA to[^c-9187c4a9ec8cd214377f13ca4aceea94] the Act and “claimant” shall be construed accordingly;
- “competent authority” means an authority having powers under the laws in force in a relevant territory[^c-d96e02afd5d2ed5f592d6602a92415e4] to register a vehicle for road use in that territory[^c-0aa5fdbf23768edd750ba1fe0f4ec07f];
- “first entry into service” in relation to a new means of transport means the time determined in relation to that means of transport under regulation 147;
- “[^c-4bb96ce43dfbcf724dcaf78c13c88ab6]**new means of transport**” has the meaning given by paragraph 83 of Schedule 9ZA to the Act;
- “registration” means registration for road use in a member State corresponding in relation to that member State to registration in accordance with the Vehicles Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00034];
- “[^c-cc0436fc5e25143a9f35421fa71aa0ae]**relevant territory**” means a member State or Northern Ireland.
