# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 128

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Export of freight containers[^key-e5e15dfc92fbc82e3755ee80424206d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the Commissioners are satisfied that a container is to be exported to a place outside Great Britain[^key-fad116aa5fa8888177cdc6028c7d5564], its supply, subject to such conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.
