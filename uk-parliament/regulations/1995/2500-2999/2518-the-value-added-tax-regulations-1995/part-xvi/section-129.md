# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 129

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Supplies to overseas persons[^c-f7447f9023e3a904540e60c329aea1d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) Where the Commissioners are satisfied that—

- (a) goods intended for export to a place outside Great Britain[^c-5aeb3836e5f01fd8c2a3cbdbe032241b] other than Northern Ireland[^c-e9ba557b4351119d5dc18a0a8b6869d0] have been supplied at a time when they were located in Great Britain[^c-6248cd55d0d3529b89039d2ca6049c1c] [^c-239643756888bc95bc38772e04a4d657]... to—
  - (i) a person not resident in Great Britain,
  - (ii) a trader who has no business establishment in Great Britain from which taxable supplies are made, or
  - (iii) an overseas authority, [^c-7eded697dd0ab7b5bfe77ba39fa3c076]...
- (b) the goods were exported to a place outside Great Britain other than Northern Ireland[^c-6b23bbcc7efe9397be747dc5bdd822e9], and[^c-4f27c42770aef2b040b527217cc6c630]
- (c)[^c-d8e03fe6b3971254be76fd5307f0deb5] the goods are not personal gifts on export as defined in regulation 2 of the Customs (Export) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, and[^c-43305fb9778bc89afc650fa2d45eb12c]
- (d)[^c-2c83a0275427c1dd48a6c652d281a240] any conditions that may be specified by the Commissioners in a notice published by them have been met,

the supply, subject to such other[^c-d3be876436bc96625d29ff27adfc9a99] conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.

(2)[^c-9d52ee6404f2111be1d20bf8a065595b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
