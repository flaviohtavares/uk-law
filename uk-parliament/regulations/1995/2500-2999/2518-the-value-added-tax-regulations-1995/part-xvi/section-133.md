# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 133

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**133.**[^key-8cd521d88ed4361f7643718f301d64e0] (1)[^key-f2d4faece8e2bcf7b8891ddaf540d281] The Commissioners may, on application by any person who intends to depart from Great Britain[^key-9b294c00e3fd921e8455e9e69dfad539] within 9 months and remain outside Great Britain and Northern Ireland[^key-c873f703b24ab1a9491259fe8a2db33a] for a period of at least 6 months, permit him within 6 months of his intended departure to purchase, from a registered person, a [^M_F_11cff01c-d792-4196-f646-5ed8f90d37bf]... motor vehicle located in Great Britain at the time of its purchase[^key-8cf2cfe10cfe1bcab5d4152710092d62] without payment of VAT, for subsequent export, and its supply, subject to such conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.

(2)[^key-cd945c4070d1e0a7fc9e9cc0f9ed9307] The conditions that the Commissioners may impose under paragraph (1) may be specified in a notice published by them.
