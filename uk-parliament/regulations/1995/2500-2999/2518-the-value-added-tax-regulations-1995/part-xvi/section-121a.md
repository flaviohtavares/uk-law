# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 121A

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**121A.**[^c-84c539f9a7dd6bae1ef7c97c0db375cc] In the Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, Part 10 (guarantees), in regulation 98(1), regard there being a third sub-paragraph as follows—

> - (c) in relation to the VAT chargeable on the importation of goods into Great Britain, from any territory not including Northern Ireland[^c-ff61893efe1f7d5382f62881e33eafb9], the specified amount may be nil where in the opinion of an HMRC officer there is no risk to the payment.
