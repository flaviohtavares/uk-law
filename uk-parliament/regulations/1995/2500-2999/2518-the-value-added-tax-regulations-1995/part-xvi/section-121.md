# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 121

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121. Adaptations[^key-487f63bdad659602d3cb5dcbd09538fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provision made by the following enactments shall apply to importations[^key-2d4920a8b95097f1ab004402072a86d1], as mentioned in section 16(1) of the Act, subject to the adaptations prescribed by this regulation.

(2)[^key-22a04dab98c305414e85bd89a19a24f1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Section 129 of the Finance Act 1999 (recovery of certain amounts by the Commissioners) shall be regarded as providing for the recovery of a repayment of any relevant VAT (import VAT).
