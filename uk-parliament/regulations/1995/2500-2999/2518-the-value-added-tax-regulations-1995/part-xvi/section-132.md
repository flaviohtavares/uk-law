# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 132

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132. Supplies to persons departing from Great Britain[^c-eb5926ca244491ca015c039ae83a91ba][^c-5e07c89c24e6feffbe5ef4b9b0749ddb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-2ea2b60ced66c88e86c5fd0777c30a74] The Commissioners may, on application by an overseas visitor who intends to depart from Great Britain[^c-e4b40bc86bfd535ca4e9ac5f88053326] within 15 months and remain outside Great Britain and Northern Ireland[^c-5306487d16abfc949a514e13e3a9b22d] for a period of at least 6 months, permit him within 12 months of his intended departure to purchase, from a registered person, a [^M_F_0cac8ed5-7c8f-43ed-94f8-b4c0b1729f5b]... motor vehicle located in Great Britain at the time of its purchase[^c-ebd2f86873ab80707f9e625ad24a97fc] without payment of VAT, for subsequent export, and its supply, subject to such conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.

(2)[^c-c7d6e874a78b17a1f50cb5e4efd50b4e] The conditions that the Commissioners may impose under paragraph (1) may be specified in a notice published by them.
