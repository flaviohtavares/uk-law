# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 17

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Paragraph 6(9) of Schedule 9ZA supplies to persons belonging in other member States[^c-c9e4c29321b5df317bc2627631ec7a6f][^c-ac9374d6c7acef7b30b07123a48d1815]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) Where a registered person makes a supply such as is mentioned in paragraph 6(9) of Schedule 9ZA to[^c-4a05cd81dc79e50497cfa6ec986ca039] the Act he shall provide the person supplied with an invoice in respect of that supply.

(2)[^c-2e184d3b5a0ae048f01593d770815c04] An invoice provided under this regulation shall comply with the requirements of regulations 13 and 14.
