# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 16

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Retailers' invoices[^key-6e6f900b1aa7469d15c2e81d62085d2c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, a registered person who is a retailer shall not be required to provide a VAT invoice, except that he shall provide such an invoice at the request of a customer who is a taxable person in respect of any supply to him; but, in that event, if, but only if, the consideration for the supply does not exceed £250[^key-d20a31390330461d02540bd3cd85677d] and , where the retailer is identified for the purposes of VAT in Northern Ireland,[^key-c7242bd2b9bb499d2f6556d1345a92e6] the supply is other than to a person in another member State, the VAT invoice need contain only the following particulars—

- (a) the name, address and registration number of the retailer,
- (b) the time of the supply,
- (c) a description sufficient to identify the goods or services supplied,
- (d) the total amount payable including VAT, and
- (e) for each rate of VAT chargeable, the gross amount payable including VAT, and the VAT rate applicable.

(2) Where a registered person provides an invoice in accordance with this regulation, the invoice shall not contain any reference to any exempt supply.
