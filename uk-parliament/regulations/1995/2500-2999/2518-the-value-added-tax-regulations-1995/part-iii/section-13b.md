# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 13B

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**13B.**[^c-3be30cdbf356ed46346bc69aa32a211d][^c-b8194c67b5ada9f6a4720c93ca64af8a] Where a VAT invoice or part of a VAT invoice is in a language other than English the Commissioners may, by notice in writing, require that an English translation of the invoice is provided to them by a person who has received such an invoice in the United Kingdom within 30 days of the date of the notice.
