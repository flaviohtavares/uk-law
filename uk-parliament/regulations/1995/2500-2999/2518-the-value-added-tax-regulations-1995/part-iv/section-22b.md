# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 22B

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**22B.**[^c-1c6cdc92689d41c706d15a33d4fef698] (1) Where statements are to be submitted under regulation 22 and[^c-f2bf93865ee35f5526c75d06319e1ee4] 22ZZA[^c-3442cc2d887ac0bb7e01e027657754a0] in respect of periods ending on the same day, the statements may be submitted on a single form.

(2) A taxable person may submit a statement under regulation 22 or 22ZZA[^c-1fbaaa7b152592a2a7be2b9b0ac62ed4] on paper or on-line using an electronic portal provided by the Commissioners for that purpose.

(3) A taxable person who is required to submit a statement under regulation 22 or 22ZZA[^c-eefac2eba74214a5fafacf29c52ca81c] must do so—

- (a) where the statement is submitted on-line, not later than 21 days from the end of the period to which the statement relates,
- (b) in every other case, not later than 14 days from the end of the period to which the statement relates.
