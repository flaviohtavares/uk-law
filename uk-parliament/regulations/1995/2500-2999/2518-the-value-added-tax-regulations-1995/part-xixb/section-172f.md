# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 172F

> Text as at 2026-07-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172F. Application[^c-12b3ddc9e3df0471c0d476f47d2d5398]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies where the supply in relation to which a person has claimed credit for input tax was made on or after 1st January 2003.
