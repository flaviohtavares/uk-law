# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 172G

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172G. Interpretation[^key-12b3ddc9e3df0471c0d476f47d2d5398]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “relevant period” means 6 months following—
  - (i) the date of the supply, or
  - (ii) if later, the date on which the consideration for the supply, or (as the case may be) the unpaid part of it, became payable.
