# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 172J

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172J. Attribution of payments[^key-12b3ddc9e3df0471c0d476f47d2d5398]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The rules on the attribution of payments in regulation 170 and, as the case may be, 170A(5)[^key-0a1f9fc3a5ccecea2cf38ae283e8eb1e] above shall apply for determining whether anything paid is to be taken as paid by way of consideration for a particular supply.
