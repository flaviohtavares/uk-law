# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 43

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Goods removed from warehousing regime[^key-efa3d5b47fc452895d6bd1409ac9d96e][^key-d330ceeec4f024a3db3033a963629c48][^key-8ced83565c553e6653cecaf0abf892ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) This regulation applies to a registered person who is an approved person within the meaning of the Excise Duties (Deferred Payment) Regulations 1992[^f00007] in respect of goods which are at a specified warehouse or Northern Ireland warehouse[^key-9aa3548b7b529af26e58abef4a3555cb].

(2)[^key-ebda4dc640e99ff8fc2ab198725cdea2] Where a person to whom this regulation applies is—

- (a) the person who is liable under section 18(4)(b) of , or paragraph 16(8) of Schedule 9ZB to,[^key-c1cf5e016bb8e49c3d8dfb8b34afa65b] the Act to pay VAT on a supply of goods while the goods are subject to a warehousing regime or Northern Ireland warehousing regime, as the case may be[^key-fdc76d68adf91f73fa3c772e4247ed4c], or
- (b) liable under section 18D(2) of the Act to pay VAT on a supply of services to which section 18C(3) of the Act applies (specified services performed on or in relation to goods which are subject to a warehousing regime),

he may pay that VAT at or before the relevant time determined in accordance with paragraph (3) below instead of at the time provided for by sections 18(4)(b) or 18D(2)(a) of, or paragraph 16(8) of Schedule 9ZB to,[^key-b8f74daf6aaffe7544bd76e2734c57d7] the Act.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2) above the relevant time means—

- (a) in relation to hydrocarbon oils, the 15th day of the month immediately following the month in which the hydrocarbon oils were removed from the warehousing regime;
- (b) in relation to any other goods subject to a duty of excise, the day (payment day) on which the registered person is required to pay the excise duty on the goods in accordance with regulation 5 of the Excise Duties (Deferred Payment) Regulations 1992.

(4) Where any goods of a kind chargeable to a duty of excise qualify for any relief of that duty, that relief shall be disregarded for the purposes of determining the relevant time under paragraph (3) above.
