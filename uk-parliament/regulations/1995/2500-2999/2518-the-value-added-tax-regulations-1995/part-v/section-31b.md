# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 31B

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**31B.**[^c-759b74d82b8780fdb2732631520211b9] Where a person receives a supply of a description falling within article 31A(1) above that person shall retain the purchase invoice in relation to that supply for a minimum period of 6 years.
