# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 31C

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**31C.**[^key-759b74d82b8780fdb2732631520211b9] Paragraph 10(2) of Schedule 11 to the Act shall apply in relation to supplies of a description falling within items 1 and 2 of Group 15 of Schedule 9 to the Act as it applies in relation to the supply of goods under taxable supplies.
