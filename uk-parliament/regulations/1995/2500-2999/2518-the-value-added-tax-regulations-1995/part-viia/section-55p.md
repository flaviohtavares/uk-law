# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 55P

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55P. Termination by the Commissioners[^c-ee82af7d77e229b34afc5529565091a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners may terminate the authorisation of a flat-rate trader at any time if—

- (a) they consider it necessary to do so for the protection of the revenue, or
- (b) a false statement was made by, or on behalf of, him in relation to his application for authorisation.
