# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 96

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Interpretation of Part XII[^c-90ac6966663a63166abdc4e7f98f06d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “relevant transaction”, in relation to any acquisition of goods from another member State, and “relevant time” in relation to any such acquisition, have the meanings given in paragraph 13 of Schedule 9ZA[^c-5a5ad6c5eca3d472047ee74713ec215e] to the Act.
