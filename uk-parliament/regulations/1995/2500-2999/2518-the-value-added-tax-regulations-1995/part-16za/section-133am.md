# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 133AM

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133AM. Export of freight containers from Northern Ireland[^c-19dd10e2bce114d33538c01fec5b12a7][^c-af00d1430eb3e0760145692a10b852cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the Commissioners are satisfied that a container is to be exported, its supply, subject to such conditions as they may impose, shall be zero-rated.
