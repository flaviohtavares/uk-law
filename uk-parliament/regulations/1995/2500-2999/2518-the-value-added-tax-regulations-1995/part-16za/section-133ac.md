# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 133AC

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133AC. Regulations excepted[^key-19dd10e2bce114d33538c01fec5b12a7][^key-af00d1430eb3e0760145692a10b852cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provision made by or under the following subordinate legislation shall be excepted from applying to importations as mentioned in section 16(1) of the Act—

- (a) regulations 16(4) and (5) and 19(1)(b) of the Excise Warehousing (Etc.) Regulations 1988 (certain removals from warehouse);
- (b) any regulations made under section 197(2)(f) of the Finance Act 1996 (rate of interest on overdue customs duty and on repayments of amounts paid by way of customs duty).
