# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 56

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Interpretation of Part VIII[^key-b89c4c8e80f0517ea5fc7f93ead29e67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “money” means banknotes or coins;
- “notice” means any notice published pursuant to this Part.
