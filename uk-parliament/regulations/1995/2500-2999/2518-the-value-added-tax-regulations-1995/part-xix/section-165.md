# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 165

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 165. Interpretation of Part XIX[^c-987f27307af3bf281cd0f08bd61d1571][^c-be57a27e8154cb1a0f84d5ddd8881765][^c-8c8efcf65a8c056865a8de5309c1b9bf][^c-be57a27e8154cb1a0f84d5ddd8881765]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “claim” means a claim in accordance with regulations 166 [^c-6d6aa69a0cd4cdaa19f9b3ba1f3516ad]... and 167 for a refund of VAT to which a person is entitled by virtue of section 36 of the Act and “claimant” shall be construed accordingly;
- “payment” means any payment or part-payment which is made by any person [^c-51dc1fb4f9bc169f4d0471202b8609c2]... by way of consideration for a supply regardless of whether such payment extinguishes the purchaser’s debt to the claimant or not;
- “purchaser” means a person to whom the claimant made a relevant supply;
- “refunds for bad debts account” has the meaning given in regulation 168;
- “relevant supply” means any taxable supply upon which a claim is based;
- “return” means the return which the claimant is required to make in accordance with regulation 25 [^c-85016f37d4b83c6369ae946ba0ca3317]...;
- “security” means—
  - (a) in relation to England, Wales and Northern Ireland, any mortgage, charge, lien or other security, and
  - (b) in relation to Scotland, any security (whether heritable or moveable), any floating charge and any right of lien or preference and right of retention (other than a right of compensation or set-off).
- [^c-6aa82e6bc0cdfb3fc0e99470ccebffca]...
