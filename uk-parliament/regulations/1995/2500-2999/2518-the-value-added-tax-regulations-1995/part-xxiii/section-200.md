# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 200

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Interpretation of Part XXIII[^key-bbbda7cbfad377e8b5654f9de75839a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “claim” means a claim for refund of VAT made pursuant to section 35 of , or paragraph 18A of Schedule 9ZA to,[^key-065be38f6bb69e1ab3f739435dcfdb31] the Act, and “claimant” shall be construed accordingly;
- “relevant building” means a building in respect of which a claimant makes a claim.
