# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 7

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Notice by partnership[^c-904599d874af683cfa877224da94e8ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—

(1) Where any notice is required to be given for the purposes of the Act or these Regulations by a partnership, it shall be the joint and several liability of all the partners to give such notice, provided that a notice given by one partner shall be a sufficient compliance with any such requirement.

(2) Where, in Scotland, a body of persons carrying on a business which includes the making of taxable supplies is a partnership required to be registered, any notice shall be given and signed in the manner indicated in section 6 of the Partnership Act 1890[^f00003].
