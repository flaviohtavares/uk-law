# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 145E

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145E. Fiscal warehousing regimes[^c-6f448ec98873fbec9dfeb11664bd92a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Upon any eligible goods entering a fiscal warehouse the relevant fiscal warehousekeeper shall record their entry in his relevant fiscal warehousing record.

(2) Eligible goods shall only be subject to or in a fiscal warehousing regime at any time—

- (a) while they are allocated to that regime in the relevant fiscal warehousing record;
- (b) while they are not identified in that record as having been transferred; or,
- (c) prior to their removal from that regime.
