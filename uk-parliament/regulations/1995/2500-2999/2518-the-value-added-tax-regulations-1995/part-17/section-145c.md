# The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995, section 145C

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145C. Certificates connected with services in fiscal or other warehousing regimes[^c-6f448ec98873fbec9dfeb11664bd92a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The certificate referred to in section 18C(1)(c) (certificate required for the zero-rating of certain services performed on or in relation to goods while those goods are subject to a fiscal or other warehousing regime) shall contain the information indicated in the form specified in a notice published by the Commissioners[^c-2f7d3d8fefe832bda85cccb3d428357f].
