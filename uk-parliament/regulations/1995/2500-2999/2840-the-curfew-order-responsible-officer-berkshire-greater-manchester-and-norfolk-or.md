# The Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (Berkshire, Greater Manchester and Norfolk) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2840

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (Berkshire, Greater Manchester and Norfolk) Order 1995

Made

1st November 1995

Coming into force

6th November 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 12(4) and 30(1)(b) of the Criminal Justice Act 1991,[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (Berkshire, Greater Manchester and Norfolk) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 6th November 1995.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “place of curfew” means a place specified by a curfew order as a place at which a person is required by the order to remain for the curfew periods.

## 3. Responsible Officer in Berkshire or Greater Manchester

Where a curfew order specifies a place in Berkshire or Greater Manchester as a place of curfew the responsible officer shall be an employee of Securicor Custodial Services Limited of Sutton Park, 15 Carshalton Road, Sutton, Surrey SM1 4LD.

## 4. Responsible Officer in Norfolk

Where a curfew order specifies a place in Norfolk as a place of curfew the responsible officer shall be an employee of Geografix Limited, Hurricane Way, Norwich NR6 6EW.

## 5. Revocation

The Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (City of Manchester, Reading and Norfolk) Order 1995[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Blatch

Minister of State

Home Office

1st November 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order describes the persons who are to be made responsible, by a curfew order relating to a place of curfew in Berkshire, Greater Manchester or Norfolk, for monitoring an offender’s whereabouts during the curfew periods.

The Order also revokes the Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (City of Manchester, Reading and Norfolk) Order 1995 (S.I. [1995/1379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1379)).
