# The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Noise and vibration caused by aircraft on aerodromes

The conditions under which noise and vibration may be caused by aircraft (including military aircraft) on Government aerodromes, licensed aerodromes or on aerodromes at which the manufacture, repair or maintenance of aircraft is carried out by persons carrying on business as manufacturers or repairers of aircraft shall be as specified in Regulation 12 in Schedule 15 to this Order.
