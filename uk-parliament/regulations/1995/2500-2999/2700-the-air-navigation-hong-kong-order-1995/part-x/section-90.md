# The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Enforcement of directions

Any person who without reasonable excuse fails to comply with any direction given to him under any provision of this Order or any regulations made thereunder shall be deemed for the purpose of this Order to have contravened that provision.
