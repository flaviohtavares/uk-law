# The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Application of Order to British-controlled aircraft not registered in Hong Kong

The Governor may direct that such of the provisions of this Order and of any regulations made or having effect thereunder as may be specified in the direction shall have effect as if reference in those provisions to aircraft registered in Hong Kong included references to the aircraft specified in the direction, being an aircraft not so registered but for the time being under the management of a person who, or of persons each of whom, is qualified to hold a legal or beneficial interest by way of ownership in an aircraft registered in Hong Kong.
