# The Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2928

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

9th November 1995

Laid before Parliament

21st November 1995

Coming into force

1st January 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(3) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

**2.** In regulation 10(3) of the Immigration (Registration with Police) Regulations 1972[^f00002] (which relates to the fee paid for the issue of registration certificates), for the figure “£30” there shall be substituted the figure “£34”.

Timothy Kirkhope

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

9th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee payable when aliens are required to register with the police from £30 to £34.
