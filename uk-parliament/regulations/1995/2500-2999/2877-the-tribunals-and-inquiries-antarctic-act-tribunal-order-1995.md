# The Tribunals And Inquiries (Antarctic Act Tribunal) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2877

TRIBUNALS AND INQUIRIES

The Tribunals And Inquiries (Antarctic Act Tribunal) Order 1995

Made

6th November 1995

Laid before Parliament

8th November 1995

Coming into force

1st December 1995

The Lord Chancellor and the Lord Advocate, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 13(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00001], hereby make the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Tribunals and Inquiries (Antarctic Act Tribunal) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st December 1995.

**2.** In Schedule 1 to the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992, after paragraph 2, there shall be inserted—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Antarctica | **2A.** The Tribunal established under Regulation 11 of the Antarctic Regulations 1995[^f00002]. |

.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

2nd November 1995

Rodger of Earlsferry,

Lord Advocate

6th November 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings under the supervision of the Council on Tribunals the Antarctic Act Tribunal established by the Antarctic Regulations 1995.
