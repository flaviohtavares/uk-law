# The Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2691

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

12th October 1995

Laid before Parliament

19th October 1995

Coming into force

10th November 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabing him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Crewe Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the South Cheshire Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 10th November 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the South Cheshire Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the South and East Cheshire Health Authority and the Trust on 23rd December 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of that Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 10th November 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

12th October 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Cheshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust on 10th November 1995 trust property formerly held by the South Cheshire Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
