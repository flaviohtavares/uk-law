# The Employment Code of Practice (Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 2729

TRADE UNIONS

The Employment Code of Practice (Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers) Order 1995

Made

17th October 1995

Laid before Parliament

18th October 1995

Coming into force

17th November 1995

Whereas —

(1) under section 203(2) of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00001] (“the 1992 Act”) the Secretary of State may from time to time revise the whole or any part of a Code of Practice issued by him;

(2) in pursuance of section 203(2) of the 1992 Act the Secretary of State proposed to revise the whole of the Code of Practice on Trade Union Ballots on Industrial Action (1st Revision) which came into effect on 20th May 1991[^f00002];

(3) in pursuance of section 204(1) of the 1992 Act, the Secretary of State, after consultation with the Advisory Conciliation and Arbitration Service, prepared and published in draft a revised Code of Practice on Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers (“the draft Code”);

(5) in pursuance of section 204(1) of the 1992 Act, the Secretary of State considered representations made to him about the draft Code;

(6) in pursuance of section 204(2) the Secretary of State laid the draft Code before both Houses of Parliament, the draft Code was approved by resolution of each House of Parliament and the Secretary of State is issuing the Code of Practice on Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers in the form of that draft;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 204(3) of the 1992 Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Employment Code of Practice (Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers) Order 1995 and comes into force on 17th November 1995.

## 2. Appointed date for revised code of practice

The day appointed for the coming into effect of the revised Code of Practice on Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers issued by the Secretary of State pursuant to section 203 of the 1992 Act (the draft of which was laid before both Houses of Parliament on 11th May 1995 and approved by resolutions of the House of Commons on 15th June 1995 and the House of Lords on 29th June 1995) is 17th November 1995.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Jonathan Evans

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competition and Consumer Affairs,

Department of Trade and Industry

17th October 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 17th November 1995 as the day upon which the revised Code of Practice on Industrial Action Ballots and Notice to Employers (issued by the Secretary of State under section 203 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992) comes into effect. The revised Code replaces the whole of the Code of Practice on Trade Union Ballots on Industrial Action (1st Revision) which came into effect on 20th May 1991.
