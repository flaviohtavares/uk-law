# The European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1298

CINEMAS AND FILMS

The European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) Order 1995

Made

17th May 1995

Coming into force

7th June 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, 17th day of May 1995

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 7th June 1995.

**2.** The Schedule to the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production Order 1994[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting at the apppropriate place in alphabetical order the word “Slovakia”.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production Order 1994 (“the Principal Order”) provides that films made in accordance with the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Cm 2495) are to be treated as British films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985. For the Convention to apply where there are only two co-producers one must be established in the United Kingdom and the other in one of the countries set out in the Schedule to the Principal Order. For the Convention to apply where there are three or more co-producers one must be established in the United Kingdom and at least two others in different countries set out in the Schedule to the Principal Order.

This Order amends the Schedule to the Principal Order by adding Slovakia to the countries set out therein.

In respect of a film which is a British film fiscal benefits may accrue under the Capital Allowance Act [1990 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/1) and the Finance (No. 2) Act [1992 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/48).
