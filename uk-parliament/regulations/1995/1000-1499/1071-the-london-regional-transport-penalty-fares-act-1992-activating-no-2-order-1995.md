# The London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992 (Activating No. 2) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1071

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992 (Activating No. 2) Order 1995

Made

12th April 1995

Coming into force

30th April 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, at the request of London Regional Transport and in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(4) of the London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992 (Activating No. 2) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 30th April 1995.

## 2. Activation of penalty fare provisions

Sections 4 and 6 to 9 of the London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992 shall have effect, as from 30th April 1995, with respect to any bus service to which that Act applies by virtue of section 3(1) thereof.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Goschen

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Transport

12th April 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th April 1995 the provisions of the London Regional Transport (Penalty Fares) Act 1992 as to the charging of penalty fares on bus services to which, by virtue of section 3(1) thereof, that Act applies. The bus services concerned are services provided under an agreement with London Regional Transport, if the agreement has been entered into under section 3(2) of the London Regional Transport Act 1984 and if it provides that the 1992 Act is to apply to services provided under it.
