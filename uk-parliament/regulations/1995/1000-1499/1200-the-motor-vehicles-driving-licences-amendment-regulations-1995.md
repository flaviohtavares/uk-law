# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1200

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

1st May 1995

Laid before Parliament

3rd May 1995

Coming into force

29th May 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 89(3)[^f00001] and (4)(b)[^f00002], 105(1), (3) and (4), 108(1)[^f00003] and 192(1)[^f00004] of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00005], after consulting with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 29th May 1995.

**2.** The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1987[^f00006] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4 below.

**3.** (1) Regulation 19[^f00007] (Fees in respect of tests) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£35” there shall be substituted “£36”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£27.50” there shall be substituted “£28.50”.

(3) In paragraph (3A)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£46.50” there shall be substituted “£47.50”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£37.50” there shall be substituted “£38.50”.

(4) In paragraph (5), for “£27.50” there shall be substituted “£28.50”.

**4.** (1) Regulation 19A[^f00008] (Fees in respect of extended tests) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£70” there shall be substituted “£72”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£55” there shall be substituted “£57”.

(3) In paragraph (4)(a) for “£92” there shall be substituted “£95”.

**5.** Regulations 3 and 4 above shall not apply in the case of an application for an appointment for a test duly made before 29th May 1995.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Steven Norris

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

28th April 1995

We approve the making of these Regulations

Timothy Kirkhope

Derek Conway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

1st May 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulations 19 and 19A of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1987 so as to increase, for test applications made on or after 29th May 1995, the fees for ordinary driving tests and London taxi drivers' tests and some of the fees for extended driving tests ordered by the court under section 36 of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988.
