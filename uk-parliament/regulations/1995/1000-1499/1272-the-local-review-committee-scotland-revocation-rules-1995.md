# The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Revocation Rules 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1272 (S.98)

PRISONS

The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Revocation Rules 1995

Made

9th May 1995

Laid before Parliament

11th May 1995

Coming

1st June 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 18(5) of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Revocation Rules 1995 and shall come into force on 1st June 1995.

**2.** Rule 8 of the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

**3.** The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967 and the Rules specified in the Schedule to these Rules are revoked with effect from 1st September 1995.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

9th May 1995
