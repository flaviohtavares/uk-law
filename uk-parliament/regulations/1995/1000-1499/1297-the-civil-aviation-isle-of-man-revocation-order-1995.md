# The Civil Aviation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1297

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

Made

17th May 1995

Coming into force

22nd June 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of May 1995

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 9 of the Carriage by Air Act 1961[^f00001], section 5(1) of the Carriage by Air (Supplementary Provisions) Act 1962[^f00002], section 8 of the Tokyo Convention Act 1967[^f00003], section 108 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00004] and section 39 of the Aviation Security Act 1982[^f00005], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 22nd June 1995.

**2.** The Orders specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

This Order revokes the Orders in Council listed in the Schedule, which extended to the Isle of Man, subject to exceptions and modifications, various Acts of Parliament relating to civil aviation, now repealed and replaced by the Civil Aviation Act 1982 and the Aviation Security Act 1982. They have been superseded by legislation made by virtue of the Airports and Civil Aviation Act 1987 (an Act of Tynwald).
