# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1015

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1995

Made

5th April 1995

Coming into force

26th April 1995

The review body appointed under section 1(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] having made a report following the reference to them of certain matters, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act and after consulting, in accordance with section 2(1) and (2) of the Act, such associations of local edcuation authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of voluntary and grant–maintained schools, such governors of grant–maintained special schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to her to be concerned, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 26th April 1995.

## 2. Application and effect

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in sections 5(1) and (2) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991) in England and Wales, and gives effect without any material modification to recommendations of the review body.
