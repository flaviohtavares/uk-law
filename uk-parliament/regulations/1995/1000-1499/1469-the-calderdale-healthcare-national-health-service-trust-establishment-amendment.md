# The Calderdale Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1469

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Calderdale Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1995

Made

7th June 1995

Coming into force

6th July 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Calderdale Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1995 and shall come into force on 6th July 1995.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In Article 3(2)(a) of the Calderdale Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] for the words “Royal Halifax Infirmary, Free School Lane, Halifax, West Yorkshire HX1 2YP.” there shall be substituted the words “Halifax General Hospital, Salterhebble, Halifax HX3 OPW and associated hospitals;”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

7th June 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Calderdale Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 to alter the name and address of the hospital at which accommodation and services (which the NHS trust established by that Order is to own and manage) are to be provided.
