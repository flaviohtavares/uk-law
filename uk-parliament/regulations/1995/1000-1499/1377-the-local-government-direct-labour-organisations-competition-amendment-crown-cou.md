# The Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Competition) (Amendment) (Crown Courts) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1377

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Competition) (Amendment) (Crown Courts) Regulations 1995

Made

24th May 1995

Laid before Parliament

5th June 1995

Coming into force

26th June 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9(3) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Competition) (Amendment) (Crown Courts) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 26th June 1995.

## 2. Functional work: Crown Court premises

In the Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Competition) Regulations 1989[^f00002] there is added at the end of paragraph (1) of regulation 9—

> - (d) work undertaken for the purposes of a Crown Court.

.

Authorised by the Secretary of State to sign in that behalf

Robert Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of the Environment

24th May 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Part III of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 requires local authorities, before undertaking construction or maintenance work through their direct labour organisations, to have satisfied certain requirements as respects competitive tendering. These regulations provide for work undertaken for the purposes of a Crown Court to be exempt from such requirements.
