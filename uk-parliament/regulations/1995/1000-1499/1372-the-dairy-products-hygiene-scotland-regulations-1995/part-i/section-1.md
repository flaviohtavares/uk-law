# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 15th June 1995.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to Scotland.
