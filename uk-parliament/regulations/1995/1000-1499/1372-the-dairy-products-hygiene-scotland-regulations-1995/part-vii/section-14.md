# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Dairy farms

Any production holding which is both licensed as a dairy farm immediately before the coming into force of these Regulations pursuant to the Milk and Dairies (Scotland) Regulations 1990[^f00013] and is in operation as such on the date before the date these Regulations come into force, shall be deemed to be premises licensed under and subject to the provisions of these Regulations as a production holding.
