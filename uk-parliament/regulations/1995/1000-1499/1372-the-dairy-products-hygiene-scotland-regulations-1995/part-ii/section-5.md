# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. List of production holdings

(1) The food authority shall allot a licence number to each licensed production holding including any premises deemed to be licensed in accordance with regulation 14, and keep a list of such production holdings.

(2) Any licensed production holding which ceases to be used as a production holding shall be removed from the list kept by the food authority pursuant to paragraph (1) above.
