# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Information to be provided by food authorities

Every food authority shall provide the Secretary of State with such information relating to the execution of its duties under these Regulations as he may from time to time require.
