# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. List of dairy establishments

(1) The food authority shall allot an approval number to each dairy establishment which it has approved and shall keep a list of those dairy establishments.

(2) Any approved dairy establishment which ceases to be used as a dairy establishment shall be removed from the list kept by the food authority pursuant to paragraph (1) above.
