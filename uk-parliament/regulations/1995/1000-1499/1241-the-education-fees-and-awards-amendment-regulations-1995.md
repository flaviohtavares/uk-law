# The Education (Fees and Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1241

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Fees and Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

3rd May 1995

Laid before Parliament

9th May 1995

Coming into force

1st June 1995

The Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 1 and 2 of the Education (Fees and Awards) Act 1983[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Fees and Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st June 1995.

**2.** Regulation 4 of The Education (Fees and Awards) Regulations 1994[^f00002] is amended—

- (a) in paragraphs (1) and (2) by omitting the words “full-time or sandwich” in both places where they occur, and
- (b) in paragraph (3) by omitting the definitions of “full-time course” and “sandwich course”.

**3.** The above amendments shall not apply as respects the charging of relevant fees in respect of students attending a course which begins before 1st September 1995.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

3rd May 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Education (Fees and Awards) Regulations 1994 and come into force on 1st June 1995.

Part II of the 1994 Regulations is amended so that it applies not only to full-time and sandwich courses but also to part-time courses.
