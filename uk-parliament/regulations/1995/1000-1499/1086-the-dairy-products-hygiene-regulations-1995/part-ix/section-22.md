# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1995, section 22

## 22. Imports from third countries and EEA States[^c-491ae4704d74def5a6e7d44c156748e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No person shall import into England and Wales for sale for human consumption from a third country any cows' milk or cream.

(2) No person shall import into England and Wales for sale for human consumption from a third country any other dairy products except in accordance with the following paragraphs of this regulation.

(3) In the case of dairy products produced or manufactured in another EEA State, they shall comply with the legislation having effect in that EEA State which implements Council Directive 92/46.

(4) In the case of dairy products other than cows' milk or cream manufactured in any third country, they shall be accompanied by a health certificate or certificate as specified by Article 23(2)(b) or 25(1) of Council Directive 92/46.

(5) In paragraph (3) above, “the legislation” shall include any administrative provisions enacted in the EEA State referred to in that paragraph to comply with Council Directive 92/46.
