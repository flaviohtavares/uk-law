## 23. Revocations[^c-3fa0b0f61a54fb9b88b585b826e148ee]

*Extent: E+W*

The Regulations specified in Schedule 12 to these Regulations are revoked to the extent specified in Column 3 of that Schedule from the date these Regulations come into force.
