## 10. Methods of analysis[^c-571f08f2dbfec354775a4ef8086b2fb1]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of these Regulations the methods of analysis and testing of dairy products specified in Schedule 11 shall be used in relation to the requirements referred to in that Schedule.
