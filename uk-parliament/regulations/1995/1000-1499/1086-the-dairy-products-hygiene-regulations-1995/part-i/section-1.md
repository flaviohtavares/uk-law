# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 1995-05-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/1086

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title and commencement[^c-05545628f9ce39d468f5283a7085ecb2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Dairy Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 9th May 1995.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to England and Wales.
