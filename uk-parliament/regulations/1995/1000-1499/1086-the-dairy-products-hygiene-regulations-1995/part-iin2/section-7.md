## 7. List of dairy establishments[^c-11f480bd332c03a14e090e9fb3d26756]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The approving authority shall allot an approval number to each dairy establishment which that authority has approved and shall keep a list of those dairy establishments.

(2) Any approved dairy establishment which ceases to be used as a dairy establishment shall be removed from the list kept by the approving authority pursuant to paragraph (1) above.
