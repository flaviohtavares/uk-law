# The Dairy Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1995, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Information to be provided by approving authorities

Every approving authority shall provide the Minister with such information relating to the execution of its duties under these Regulations as the Minister may from time to time require.
