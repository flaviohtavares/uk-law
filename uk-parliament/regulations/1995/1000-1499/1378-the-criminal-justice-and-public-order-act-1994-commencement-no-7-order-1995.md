# The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1378 (C.29)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1995

Made

24th May 1995

In exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 172(2) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1995.

**2.** Section 19 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (extension of kinds of secure accommodation for certain young persons) shall come into force on 30th May 1995.

Virginia Bottomley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Department of Health

24th May 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th May 1995 section 19 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (extension of kinds of secure accommodation).
