# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1296

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995

Made

17th May 1995

Laid before Parliament

31st May 1995

Coming into force

22nd June 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of May 1995

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by sections 60, 61, 77(1) and 102(2) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001] and all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 22nd June 1995.

**2.** The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 1979[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 1979, which extended to the Isle of Man, subject to exceptions, adaptations and modifications, the Air Navigation Order 1976 (S.I. [1976/1783](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/1783)). The 1979 Order has been superseded by legislation made by virtue of the Airports and Civil Aviation Act 1987 (an Act of Tynwald).
