# The North Yorkshire Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1127

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North Yorkshire Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made

19th April 1995

Laid before Parliament

26th April 1995

Coming into force

17th May 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to changes in the areas and functions of health authorities arising out of the abolition of Airedale District, the variation of Bradford District and the determination of North Yorkshire District[^f00002], having consulted the Bradford Health Authority and the North Yorkshire Health Authority in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North Yorkshire Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 17th May 1995.

(2) In this Order—

- “the first Authority” means the Bradford Health Authority;
- “the second Authority” means the North Yorkshire Health Authority;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the first Authority and the second Authority on 1stAugust 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the first Authority and the second Authority.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 17th May 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the first Authority to the second Authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

19th April 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the North Yorkshire Health Authority on 17th May 1995 trust property formerly held by the Bradford Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
