# The Air Navigation Order 1995, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocation

The following Orders are hereby revoked, that is to say:

- the Air Navigation Order 1989[^f00004];
- the Air Navigation (Amendment) Order 1990[^f00005];
- the Air Navigation (Second Amendment) Order 1991[^f00006];
- the Air Navigation (Third Amendment) Order 1993[^f00007];
- the Air Navigation (Fourth Amendment) Order 1993[^f00008];
- the Air Navigation (Fifth Amendment) Order 1993[^f00009].
