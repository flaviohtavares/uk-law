# The North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) (No. 2) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1121

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) (No. 2) Order 1995

Made

18th April 1995

Laid before Parliament

27th April 1995

Coming into force

19th May 1995

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Clwyd Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) (No. 2) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 19th May 1995.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means the Clwyd Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 1st February 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 19th May 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

18th April 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust on 19th May 1995 trust property formerly held by the Clwyd Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from Welsh Office, HFM3, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
