## SCHEDULE B5 — MISCELLANEOUS EXCEPTIONS TO ELIGIBILITY UNDER REGULATION B1

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation B9.

**1.**[^c-6fe5b69734d3057b475d10010044639a] A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme if he is—

- (a) a chaplain to whom the Church of England Pensions Regulations 1988[^f00137] apply;
- (b) an employee in respect of whom contributions are made to the Merchant Navy Officers Pension Fund;
- (c) a person who is entitled as mentioned in section 28 of the Kingston upon Hull Act 1984[^f00138] (superannuation of telephone employees).

**2.**[^c-5aa47ef24c2c1cc2f4bb112e035d6102] A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of being an employee of a magistrates' courts committee if he gave notice under the proviso to section 20(1) of the Act of 1937 electing that that Act should not apply to his clerkship.

**3.**[^c-34058aaa77eaa5455a2e2011d386d0aa] A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of being an employee of a probation committee if he gave notice in accordance with Article 1 of the Probation Officers (Superannuation) Order 1948[^f00139].

**4.**[^c-b4df9f364116d30e34240b9067744c25] A person appointed under section 9(2) of the Museum of London Act 1965[^f00140] to act as secretary or treasurer of the Board of Governors of the Museum of London is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of the Board being specified in Part II of Schedule B1.

**5.**[^c-ff692f3da32529462cecfc444dadcfe5] A person who is (or is deemed to be) a variable-time employee of a body mentioned in paragraph 3, 4 or 5 of Part II of Schedule B1 is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme in respect of that employment.

**6.**[^c-e95ad66edb09be5b37b97a897497d68e] A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme in respect of his part-time employment as a member of a fire brigade maintained in pursuance of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00141] on terms under which he is or may be required to engage in fire fighting.

**7.**[^c-40af289be3ed5403da921397748322ec] A person who is an employee of a body mentioned in paragraph 6 of Part II of Schedule B1 is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of that paragraph unless—

- (a) immediately before the start of his employment with the company there mentioned he was employed by a body specified in part I of that Schedule; and
- (b) he is not an admission agreement employee.
