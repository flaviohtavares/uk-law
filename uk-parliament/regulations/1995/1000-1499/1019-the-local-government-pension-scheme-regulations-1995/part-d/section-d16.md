# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section D16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D16. Reduction of retirement pensions of certain former teachers.

The provisions of Schedule D6 shall have effect as respects the reduction in pensions of certain former teachers.
