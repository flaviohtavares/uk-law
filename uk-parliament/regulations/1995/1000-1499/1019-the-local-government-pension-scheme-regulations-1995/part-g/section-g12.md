# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section G12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G12. Adjustments to children’s pensions for certain re-employed pensioners etc.

The provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part IV of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) and to paragraph 3 of Schedule F1 (women dying before 6th April 1988).
