# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section G9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G9. Long-term pensions: increase of period of membership in certain cases

Unless the retirement pension determined under regulation G3(4), G5(3) or G6(3) would be greater, the retirement pension of a deceased person shall be taken to be that to which he would have been entitled if he were entitled to count the lesser of—

- (a) 10 years of membership; and
- (b) the period of membership which he would have been entitled to count if he had continued to be a member until his 65th birthday.
