# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section G4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (244).** Text as at 1995-05-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1995/1019
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## G4. Member’s children’s short-term pension[^c-c87e40737b73d10fb754dd1c3b9b6295]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulation G8, where a member dies leaving one or more eligible children, they are entitled to or to the benefit of a children’s short-term pension for 6 months after the death or, if less, until they cease to be children.

(2) Subject to regulation G8, the annual rate of a children’s short-term pension is the rate at which a surviving spouse’s short-term pension would have been payable by virtue of regulation F1.
