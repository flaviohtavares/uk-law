# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section A1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## A1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 2nd May 1995 (and in these regulations that date is referred to as “the commencement date”).
