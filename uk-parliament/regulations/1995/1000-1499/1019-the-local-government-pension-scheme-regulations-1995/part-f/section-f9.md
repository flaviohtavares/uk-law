# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section F9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F9. Widowers: elections for added years etc.

Schedule F1 shall have effect for the purpose of making provision as to elections by women members affecting their widowers' pensions.
