# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section C27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C27. Limitation of payments

Schedule C5 has effect for the limitation, in certain circumstances, of payments under this Part and benefits under other Parts of these regulations.
