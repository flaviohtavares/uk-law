# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section C16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C16. Further provisions as to making of payments under regulations C13 and C14

(1) A member shall specify in a notice of election to make additional periodical payments under regulation C13 or C14 the birthday (“the specified birthday”) up to which additional payments are to be paid, which may be—

- (a) his 65th birthday, or
- (b) any earlier birthday which is or is after his NRD.

(2) Such additional periodical payments are to be paid, at such intervals as the appropriate administering authority may determine, from the member’s next birthday after the date of the election.
