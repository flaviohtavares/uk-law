# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section C17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C17. No elections to make payments after 64

A person may not make an election under regulation C9, C13 or C14 if he has attained the age of 64.
