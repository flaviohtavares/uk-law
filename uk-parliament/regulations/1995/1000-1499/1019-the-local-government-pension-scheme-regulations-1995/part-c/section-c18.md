# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section C18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C18. Notice to discontinue payments

Payment in accordance with regulation C9(2) or C16(2) may be discontinued if the member notifies the appropriate administering authority and the employing authority in writing that he wishes it to be discontinued.
