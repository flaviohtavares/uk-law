# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section K10

## K10. General provisions about payment of transfer values[^c-6514625f88f2b2ab1c2b3c0e3b198fd6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The transfer value under regulation K2 shall be paid to the scheme managers by the fund authority out of their pension fund.

(2) The fund authority shall provide the scheme managers and the person to whom the transfer value relates with a written notice showing how it was calculated.

(3) A notice provided under paragraph (2) shall include a statement of the kind required by regulation J4(2)(c).
