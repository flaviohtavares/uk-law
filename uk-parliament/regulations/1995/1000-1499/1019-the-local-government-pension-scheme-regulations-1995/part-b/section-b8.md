# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section B8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B8. Ineligibility of employees eligible to join other statutory schemes

(1) A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of any employment which also entitles him to belong to another occupational pension scheme provided by or under an enactment.

(2) The reference in paragraph (1) to an enactment—

- (a) includes an enactment in a local Act, but
- (b) excludes section 7 of the Superannuation Act 1972.
