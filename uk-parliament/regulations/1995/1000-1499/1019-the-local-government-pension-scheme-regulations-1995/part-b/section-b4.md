# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section B4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B4. Certain office-holders etc. to be treated as employees of LGPS employers

Schedule B2 shall have effect for the purpose of deeming certain persons to be employees of LGPS employers and, unless the context otherwise requires, references in these regulations to employment by or under such employers, and all related expressions, shall be construed accordingly.
