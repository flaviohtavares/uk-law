# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section H2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H2. National Insurance

Where, but for the revocation of the 1974 regulations, the amount of a benefit would have fallen to be reduced under Part F of those regulations, the amount shall be reduced as if that Part had not been revoked.
