# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section H3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H3. Benefits not assignable

Every benefit to which a person is entitled under these regulations—

- (a) is payable to or in trust for him, and
- (b) is not assignable or chargeable with that or any other person’s debts or other liabilities.
