# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section L2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L2. Transfer of existing pension funds

Where any superannuation fund to which regulation L1 applies is vested in any body by virtue of an order made under the Local Government Act 1992[^f00052], it shall be maintained by that body for the purposes of these regulations.
