# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section L9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L9. Accounts and audit

As soon as practicable after any audit of their pension fund an administering authority shall send each body whose employees contribute to the fund copies—

- (a) of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund, and
- (b) of any report by the auditor.
