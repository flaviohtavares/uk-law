# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section L1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L1. Continuation of existing superannuation funds

The superannuation funds maintained immediately before the commencement date under or for the purposes of the 1986 regulations shall, subject to the provisions of these regulations, continue to be maintained for the purposes of these regulations by the bodies then responsible for maintaining them (and those funds are referred to in these regulations as “pension funds”).
