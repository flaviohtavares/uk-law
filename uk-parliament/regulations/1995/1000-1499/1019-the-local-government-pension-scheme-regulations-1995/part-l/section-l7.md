# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section L7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L7. Use of fund money by administering authority

An administering authority shall pay interest to the fund on the total from day to day of any fund money used by them and for the time being not repaid, at a rate no lower than the lowest rate at which that amount could have been borrowed by them at arms' length at 7 days' notice (otherwise than by way of overdraft from a bank).
