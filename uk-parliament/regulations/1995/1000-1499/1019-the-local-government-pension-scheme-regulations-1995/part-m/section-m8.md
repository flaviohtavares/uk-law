## M8. Transitional and transitory provisions[^c-01e356d2a25e834bcbac49518634428f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule M4 has effect for the purpose of making transitional provision and savings consequential on the making of these regulations.

(2) Nothing in that Schedule affects the general operation of sections 16 and 17 of the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00075] (as applied by section 23 of that Act).
