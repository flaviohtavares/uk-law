# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section M1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## M1. Local government reorganisation

Schedule M1 has effect as respects the application of these regulations to certain employees who have been affected by the operation of the Local Government Acts and other reorganisations effected by or under statutes.
