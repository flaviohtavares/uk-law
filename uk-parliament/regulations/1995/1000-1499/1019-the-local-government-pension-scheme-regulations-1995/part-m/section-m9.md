# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section M9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## M9. Revocations

The subordinate legislation specified in Schedule M5 is revoked to the extent specified in the third column (but subject to the provisions in Schedule C6 and the savings in Schedule M4).
