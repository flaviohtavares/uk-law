# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section M11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## M11. Applications to Isles of Scilly

These regulations apply to the Isles of Scilly as if the Isles of Scilly were a district in the county of Cornwall and the Isles of Scilly were the council of that district.
