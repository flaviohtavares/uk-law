# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section E7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E7. Part-timers' pensionable remuneration for certain purposes of this Part

Paragraph 7 of Schedule D1 (by virtue of which a member is, in respect of any period of part-time local government employment, to be treated as having received the remuneration which would have been paid in respect of a single comparable whole-time employment) does not apply to the application of regulation D1 and that Schedule in ascertaining the deceased’s pensionable remuneration for the purposes of regulation E1, E3(2)(a) or (3)(b) or E4(2)(ii).
