# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1995, section E6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E6. Adjustments to death grants for certain re-employed pensioners

The provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part III of Schedule D5 (separate benefits of certain persons re-employed by LGPS employers) and Part IV of that Schedule (death in further employment of such employees).
