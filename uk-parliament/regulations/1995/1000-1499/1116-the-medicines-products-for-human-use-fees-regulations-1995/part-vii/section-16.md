# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1995, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Time for payment of capital fees in connection with applications or inspections

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and to regulations 17 and 19, all sums payable by way of capital fees under these Regulations in connection with any application shall be payable at the time of the application.

(2) All sums payable by way of fees in respect of inspections made either in connection with an application for, or during the currency of, an authorization, licence or certificate shall become payable within 14 days following written notice from the licensing authority requiring payment of those fees.
