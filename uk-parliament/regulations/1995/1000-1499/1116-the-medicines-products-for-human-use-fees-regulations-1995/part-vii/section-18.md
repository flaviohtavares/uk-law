# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1995, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Time for payment of periodic fees

All periodic fees shall be payable on the first day of the fee period to which they relate.
