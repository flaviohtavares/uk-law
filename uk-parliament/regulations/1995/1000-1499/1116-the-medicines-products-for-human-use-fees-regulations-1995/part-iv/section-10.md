# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1995, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Renewals of clinical trial certificates

Subject to regulations 19 and 23, in connection with an application under section 38(2) of the Act for renewal of a clinical trial certificate, there shall be payable by the applicant a fee of £2,405.
