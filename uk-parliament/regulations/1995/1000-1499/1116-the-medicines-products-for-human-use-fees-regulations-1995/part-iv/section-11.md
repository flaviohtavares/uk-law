# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1995, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Renewals of certain manufacturer’s licences

(1) Subject to regulation 23, the fee payable in connection with an application for renewal of a manufacturer’s licence which is limited solely to the manufacture or assembly of medicinal products the sale or supply of which does not require a marketing authorization or a product licence and to which article 2(2)(i)(e) (exemptions for certain special manufactured products) of the Medicines (Exemption from Licences) (Special and Transitional Cases) Order 1971[^f00008] applies, shall be £85.

(2) In respect of any inspection made in connection with an application referred to in paragraph (1), the fee payable shall be that prescribed in paragraph 2(d) of Schedule 2.
