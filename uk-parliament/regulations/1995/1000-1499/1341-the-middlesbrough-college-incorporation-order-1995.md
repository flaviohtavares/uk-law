# The Middlesbrough College (Incorporation) Order 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1341

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Middlesbrough College (Incorporation) Order 1995

Made

22nd May 1995

Laid before Parliament

23rd May 1995

Coming into force

15th June 1995

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education has received a proposal from the Further Education Funding Council, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the establishment of a body corporate under section 16(1)(a) of the Act;

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Middlesbrough College (Incorporation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 15th June 1995 (“the Commencement Date”).

**2.** On the Commencement Date there shall be established a body corporate called “Middlesbrough College” for the purpose of establishing and conducting from the operative date a new educational institution of that name (to be formed by the merger of Acklam Sixth Form College and Kirby College of Further Education).

**3.** The “operative date” in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st August 1995, and the corporation shall conduct Middlesbrough College from that date.

Tim Boswell

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

22nd May 1995

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of establishing and conducting Middlesbrough College and appoints 1st August 1995 as the “operative date” for the purposes of Part I of that Act.
