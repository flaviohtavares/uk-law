# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995, and shall come into force on 30th June 1995.

**1.** These Regulations, which apply to Great Britain and come into force on 30th June 1995, consolidate with amendments the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1991 as amended, and implement the European Community directives listed at the end of this note.
