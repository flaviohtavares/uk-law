# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Revocation

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1991[^f00015], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00016], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1994[^f00017] and the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1994[^f00018] are hereby revoked.
