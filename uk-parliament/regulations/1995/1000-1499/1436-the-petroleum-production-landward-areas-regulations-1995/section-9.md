# The Petroleum (Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 1995, section 9

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Form of licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dbb4d2595b408032c6f0e90758006119][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every licence shall incorporate the model clauses respectively prescribed by the next following paragraph for the kind of licence to which that licence belongs unless the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^key-9033e795664372aca50644046a1144d2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] thinks fit to modify or exclude, in any particular case, the clauses so prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(2) The clauses prescribed for incorporation in licences of the following kinds are those set out in the respective Schedules to these Regulations, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (a) for incorporation in petroleum exploration and development licences, the clauses set out in Schedule 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (b) for incorporation in supplementary seismic survey licences, the clauses set out in Schedule 4; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (c) for incorporation in methane drainage licences, the clauses set out in Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
