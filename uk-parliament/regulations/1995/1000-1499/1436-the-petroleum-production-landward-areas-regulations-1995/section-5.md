# The Petroleum (Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 1995, section 5

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Applications for licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9791d44774e029d17c8b5aaa7e9e0763][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every application for a licence shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (a) be made in writing and sent to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] the OGA, Aberdeen AB10[^c-409724cc9464b82e84a40d0bad64199f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (b) be accompanied by the appropriate fee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] , as set out in the Oil and Gas Authority (Fees) Regulations 2016[^c-e98c140f6fa315e9365ca1a85c08c5e6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2].

(2) Every application for a licence which relates to an area bounded by any of the lines specified in Schedule 1 to these Regulations shall be accompanied by two copies of an Ordnance Survey map on a scale of 1:25,000, or such other map or chart as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^c-7c42e241c3403d626ddf69f71bfda04f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] may allow, upon which the boundaries of the area in relation to which the licence is sought are clearly defined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(3) An application for a licence other than a petroleum exploration and development licence may be made at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
