## SCHEDULE 5 (affected by repeal, see note) — MODEL CLAUSES FOR METHANE DRAINAGE LICENCES (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

### 1. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b12e8a88e44c29f88c2feef69ad467f4][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(1) In the following clauses the following expressions have the meanings hereby respectively assigned to them, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- “the Act” means the Petroleum (Production) Act 1934; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “the licensed area” means the area in which the Licensee may exercise the rights granted by this licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “the Licensee” means the person or persons to whom this licence is granted, his personal representatives and any person or persons to whom the rights conferred by this licence may lawfully have been assigned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “mine” has the same meaning as in the Mines and Quarries Act 1954; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “the OGA” means the Oil and Gas Authority.[^key-3e86a8af22f91aa7aed9e392e0a8af11] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(2) Any obligations which are to be observed and performed by the Licensee shall at any time at which the Licensee is more than one person be joint and several obligations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

**2.**[^key-f303cbbdeb437b0409ed2eb305acfd2d] In consideration of the payments hereinafter provided and the performance and observance by the Licensee of all the terms and conditions hereof, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^key-34a90ac8858e28a5a8a9d46c849936b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] in exercise of the powers conferred by the Act, hereby grants to the Licensee LICENCE AND LIBERTY during the continuance of this licence and subject to the provisions hereof to get natural gas in the course of operations for making and keeping safe mines whether or not disused within ALL THOSE lands having a superficial area of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] or thereabouts, situate in the County (Counties) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] and more particularly delineated and described on the Ordnance Survey map annexed hereto and thereon edged red. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

Without prejudice to any obligation or liability imposed by or incurred under the terms and conditions hereof the Licensee may at any time during the term hereby granted determine this licence by giving to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^key-820a2c0ca776e3393509dcc33687a5af] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] not less than one month’s previous notice in writing to that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

### 5. Payment of consideration for licence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b74a14d2f97ac958c7e694ff3d67c2ac][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(1) The Licensee shall pay to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^key-d44c21455a9242586e3507117ae37de0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] during the term of this licence the consideration for the grant of this licence specified in the Schedule to this licence at the times and in the manner so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(2) The Licensee shall not by reason of the determination of this licence be entitled to be repaid or allowed any part of any sum payable to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] pursuant to this licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA[^key-d6fd16af4813de0042f08d46787f86e9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] shall nevertheless be entitled at any time to make use of any information received from the Licensee for the purpose of preparing and publishing such returns and reports as may be required of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] OGA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] by law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

### 9. Notice of commencement and termination of operations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0f89e09d9439a0d33d83654c2f667577][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (b) whether such mine is a disused mine or not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
