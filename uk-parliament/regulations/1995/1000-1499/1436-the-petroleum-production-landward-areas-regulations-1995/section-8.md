# The Petroleum (Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 1995, section 8

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Applications for supplementary seismic survey licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94b5109fe15a01060bd41dea92dd5b7c][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application for a supplementary seismic survey licence shall relate to a clearly defined area, no part of which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (a) subject to a principal licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (b) more than one kilometre from the boundary of the area to which a principal licence held by the applicant relates; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (c) below the low water line. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(2) Every such application shall be accompanied by two copies of a programme in accordance with which the applicant proposes to undertake seismic surveys in both the area to which the principal licence relates and the area to which the supplementary seismic survey licence is to relate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
