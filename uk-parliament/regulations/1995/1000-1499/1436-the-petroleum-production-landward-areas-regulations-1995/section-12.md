# The Petroleum (Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 1995, section 12

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Review (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2ff661d167a57965ca25b832253b7e9e][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (c) publish the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(2) The report must in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which they could be achieved in a less burdensome way. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(3) The first report under this regulation must be published before the end of the period of five years beginning with the day on which this regulation comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

(4) Subsequent reports under this regulation must be published at intervals not exceeding five years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
