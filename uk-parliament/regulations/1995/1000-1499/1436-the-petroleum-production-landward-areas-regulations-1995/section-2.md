# The Petroleum (Production) (Landward Areas) Regulations 1995, section 2

> Text as at 2024-01-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-51e28b237334934177130f88d705ea18][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2][^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, the following expressions have the meanings hereby respectively assigned to them, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]

- “the Act” means the Petroleum (Production) Act 1934; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “blocks” has the meaning assigned thereto in regulation 7(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “landward areas” has the meaning assigned thereto in regulation 3 and “seaward areas” (when used in Schedule 1 to these Regulations) means areas on the seaward side of the lines there referred to; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “low water line” has the same meaning as it has in Schedule 1 to these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “methane drainage licence” means a licence to get natural gas in the course of operations for making and keeping safe mines whether or not disused; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “Official Journal” means the Official Journal of the European Communities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “the OGA” means the Oil and Gas Authority;[^c-5f03dadb58c6f8edb358a03d8f17f6db] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “petroleum exploration and development licence” means a licence granted pursuant to these Regulations to search and bore for and get petroleum in a landward area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “principal licence” means a licence of any description under the Act other than a methane drainage licence or a supplementary seismic survey licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “[^c-6093423bb989d7b551d1b579954ad0b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]**Scottish onshore area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]**” has the meaning given by section 8A of the Petroleum Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “supplementary seismic survey licence” means a licence to search for petroleum by undertaking seismic surveys in an area adjacent to an area to which a principal licence relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2] [^c-a7c411be6d4542970437890d2a1fdbbb]...
- “Welsh onshore area” has the meaning given by section 8A of the Petroleum Act 1998; and[^c-dcab958ba2b6492a9921e47ff3d5fb6f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
- “work programme” means a scheme of prospecting including any geological survey by any physical or chemical means and any test drilling. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cd3b46a11fde6ac5710a965753364bb2]
