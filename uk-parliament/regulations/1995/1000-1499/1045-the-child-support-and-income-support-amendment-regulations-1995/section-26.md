# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 26

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Substitution of regulation 2 of the Maintenance Arrangements and Jurisdiction Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

For regulation 2 of the Maintenance Arrangements and Jurisdiction Regulations (prescription of enactments for the purposes of Section 8(11) of the Act), there shall be substituted the following regulation—

> **2. Prescription of enactments for the purposes of section 8(11) of the Act**
>
> The following enactments are prescribed for the purposes of section 8(11)(f) of the Act—
>
> - (a) the Conjugal Rights (Scotland) Amendment Act 1861 [^c1795501];
> - (b) the Court of Session Act 1868 [^c1795502];
> - (c) the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907 [^c1795503];
> - (d) the Guardianship of Infants Act 1925 [^c1795504];
> - (e) the Illegitimate Children (Scotland) Act 1930 [^c1795505];
> - (f) the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1932 [^c1795506];
> - (g) the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1937 [^c1795507];
> - (h) the Custody of Children (Scotland) Act 1939 [^c1795508];
> - (i) the National Assistance Act 1948 [^c1795509];
> - (j) the Affiliation Orders Act 1952 [^c1795510];
> - (k) the Affiliation Proceedings Act 1957 [^c1795511];
> - (l) the Matrimonial Proceedings (Children) Act 1958 [^c1795512];
> - (m) the Guardianship of Minors Act 1971 [^c1795513];
> - (n) the Guardianship Act 1973 [^c1795514];
> - (o) the Children Act 1975 [^c1795515];
> - (p) the Supplementary Benefits Act 1976 [^c1795516];
> - (q) the Social Security Act 1986 [^c1795517];
> - (r) the Social Security Administration Act 1992 [^c1795518].

.
