# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 57

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Insertion of Schedules 3A and 3B into the Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

After Schedule 3 to the Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases Regulations there shall be inserted, as Schedules 3A and 3B, the Schedules set out in Schedules 1 and 2 respectively to these Regulations.
