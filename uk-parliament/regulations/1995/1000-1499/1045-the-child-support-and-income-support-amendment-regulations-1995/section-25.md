# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 25

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Amendment of regulation 1 of the Maintenance Arrangement and Jurisdiction Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

In paragraph (2) of regulation 1 of the Maintenance Arrangements and Jurisdiction Regulations, after the definition of “the Act” there shall be inserted the following definition—

> “Maintenance Assessment Procedure Regulations” means the Child Support (Maintenance Assessment Procedure) Regulations 1992 [^c1795500];

.
