> Text as at 2014-01-01.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 2 of the Appeals Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

After paragraph (2) of regulation 2 of the Appeals Regulation (Service of notices or documents) there shall be added the following paragraph —

> (3) The provisions of paragraph (2) shall apply to a summons or a citation issued under regulation 10.
