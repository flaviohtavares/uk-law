# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 40

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Amendment of regulation 42 of the Maintenance Assessment Procedure Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

In paragraph (5) of regulation 42 of the Maintenance Assessment Procedure Regulations (review of a reduced benefit direction), after the words “were given” there shall be added the words “ or the Secretary of State or a child support officer becomes aware of a question of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2A) or (2B) ”.
