# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 15

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Amendment of regulation 9 of the Collection and Enforcement Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

For paragraphs (d) and (e) of regulation 9 of the Collection and Enforcement Regulations [^c1795495], there shall be substituted the following paragraphs—

> - (d) the normal deduction rate or rates and the date upon which each is to take effect;
> - (e) the protected earnings rate;

.

## Annotations
