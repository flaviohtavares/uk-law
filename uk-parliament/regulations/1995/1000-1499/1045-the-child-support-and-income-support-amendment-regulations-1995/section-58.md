# The Child Support and Income Support (Amendment) Regulations 1995, section 58

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Amendment of Schedule 4 to the Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases Regulations—

*Extent: E+W+S*

In paragraph (a) of Schedule 4 to the Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases Regulations—

- (a) for sub-paragraphs (i) to (iii), there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraphs—

  > - (i) incapacity benefit under section 30A [^c1795537];
  > - (ii) long-term incapacity benefit for widows under section 40;
  > - (iii) long-term incapacity benefit for widowers under section 41;

  ; and
- (b) sub-paragraph (v) shall be omitted.

## Annotations

[^c1795537]: Marginal citation: [Section 30A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1045/section/30A) was inserted by section 1of, and sections 40 and 41 substituted by paragraph s 8 and 9 of Schedule 1 to, the [Social Security (Incapacity for Work) Act 1994 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/18).
