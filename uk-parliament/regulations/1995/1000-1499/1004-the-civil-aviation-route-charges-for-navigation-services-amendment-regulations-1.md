# The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1004

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made

5th April 1995

Laid before Parliament

6th April 1995

Coming into force

1st May 1995

Whereas in pursuance of tariffs approved under the Eurocontrol Convention[^f00001] and under the Multilateral Agreement relating to Route Charges concluded at Brussels on 12th February 1981[^f00001] (being international agreements to which the United Kingdom is a party), the Secretary of State for Transport has determined rates of charges, as specified in the following Regulations, payable to Eurocontrol in respect of navigation services provided for aircraft:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of his powers under section 73(1)(a), (1A)(a), and (6) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st May 1995.

**2.** The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) Regulations 1994[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) For regulation 9(vii) there shall be substituted the following regulation—

  > - (vii) flights made exclusively for the purpose of the instruction or testing of flight crew within the specified airspace of the United Kingdom;
- (b) For Schedule 2 thereof there shall be substituted—

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) Regulations 1994 as follows:

The exemption from the Regulations for flights made exclusively for the purpose of the instruction or testing of flight crew is restricted to such flights made within the specified airspace of the United Kingdom (regulation 2(a)).

The unit rate in ECUs set out in Schedule 2 of the principal Regulations is reduced in the case of Austria by 3.8% and in the case of the United Kingdom by 5.8%. The unit rate in the case of Turkey is increased by 7.2% (regulation 2(b)).

In consequence of the adjustment to the unit rates of Austria, Turkey and the United Kingdom new transatlantic charges are introduced (regulation 2(c)).

The unit rates in ECUs set out in regulation 2(b) and the amount of the charges in ECUs set out in regulation 2(c) (for transatlantic flights) are calculated by reference to the costs of provision of en-route navigation services in the participating countries in the Eurocontrol charges system, the amount of traffic using each country’s airspace and the relationship of each country’s currency to the ECU over a period agreed by Ministers of the participating countries. The interaction of these elements varies in each country. In calculating the revised charges the average of the exchange rates between the ECU and the currencies of the participating countries obtaining in the month of July 1994 have been used.
