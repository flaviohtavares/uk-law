# The Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) (Suspension No. 2) Regulations 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 1151

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) (Suspension No. 2) Regulations 1995

Made

25th April 1995

Laid before Parliament

26th April 1995

Coming into force

25th April 1995

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the control of the export of goods and measures relating to trade in dual-use goods, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) (Suspension No. 2) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** Regulation 3 of the Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) (Suspension) Regulations 1995[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**3.** The Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) Regulations 1995[^f00004] shall come into force for all purposes for which they are not yet in force on 1st July 1995.

Ian Taylor

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Trade and Technology,

Department of Trade and Industry

25th April 1995

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) (Suspension) Regulations 1995 provided that most provisions of the Dual-Use and Related Goods (Export Control) Regulations (“the principal Regulations”) should come into force on the day on which Council Regulation [(EC) No. 3381/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/3381) (not yet published) was published in the Official Journal of the European Communities.

These Regulations now provide that the principal Regulations will come into force on 1st July 1995, the date fixed by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 837/95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1995/0837) (O.J. L 90, 21.4.95, p.1) as the date from which Regulation 3381/94 will apply.
