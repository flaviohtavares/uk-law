## FIRST SCHEDULE (affected by repeal, see note) — THE COAL AND OTHER MINES (HORSES) REGULATIONS, 1956, HAVING EFFECT AS IF MADE UNDER SECTION ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE OF THE MINES AND QUARRIES ACT, 1954 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9][^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**1.** These regulations shall apply to every mine of coal, stratified ironstone, shale or fireclay, and in these regulations “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**mine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9] such a mine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Age and health of horses employed below ground (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**2.** No horse shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) be taken below ground at a mine unless it has attained the age of four years; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) be taken or allowed to be below ground at a mine if it is blind. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**3.** No horse shall be taken below ground at a mine unless as shortly as practicable before being so taken it has been subjected to a test for the purpose of ascertaining whether it is suffering from glanders, and has been certified to be free therefrom, by a duly registered veterinary surgeon (in these regulations referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**veterinary surgeon *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**”).

**4.** (1) It shall be the duty of the manager of every mine to ensure that every horse employed below ground at that mine is, at intervals not exceeding twelve months, examined by a veterinary surgeon. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) A report of every such examination (including a certificate as to the fitness or permanent or temporary unfitness of the horse concerned for work or for work below ground at the mine, as the case may be) shall forthwith be recorded in the book in which the record relating to that horse is required to be kept in pursuance of paragraph (1) of regulation ten of these regulations, and signed by the veterinary surgeon who made the examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(3) Any horse certified as aforesaid to be permanently unfit for work or for work below ground at a mine shall (if below ground) forthwith be brought to the surface. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(4) No horse certified as aforesaid to be permanently or temporarily unfit for work shall be disposed of alive otherwise than for immediate destruction or to a home of rest maintained for the reception of animals not then fit for work or to a responsible person not carrying on the business of dealing in horses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(5) Where a horse, having been certified as aforesaid to be permanently unfit for work or for work below ground at a mine, is destroyed or otherwise disposed of, a statement of the manner of that disposal shall be recorded forthwith by a person appointed for the purpose by the manager in the book in which the record relating to that horse was last required to be kept in pursuance of paragraph (1) of regulation ten of these regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Carriage of horses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**5.** No horse shall be carried through any part of a mine on a vehicle except when it is impracticable or dangerous for it to walk because of an accident in that mine, or when the horse has been injured or is ill: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

Provided that nothing in this regulation shall restrict the carrying of any horse by means of winding or haulage apparatus through a shaft or staple-pit or on an incline, being an incline too steep for that horse to walk thereon with safety. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Prohibition of riding on horses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**6.** No person shall ride on a horse below ground at a mine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Periods of employment of horses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**7.** (1) No horse shall be allowed to be out of its stable for the purpose of work during any period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) of twenty-four hours, during more than two shifts; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) of forty-eight hours, during more than three shifts; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) of seven days, during more than seven shifts or for more than forty-eight hours in the aggregate: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

  Provided that nothing in this regulation shall restrict the working of any horse where necessary through unforeseen circumstances for the purpose of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

  - (i) saving life; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9] remedying the effect of an accident; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9] avoiding serious interference with ordinary work in the mine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) It shall be the duty of the appropriate horse-keeper to ensure compliance with the provisions of the last preceding paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(3) For the purposes of this regulation the period of work on any day (excluding any overtime) of any person under whose charge a horse is when out of its stable shall be regarded as a shift. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Appointment and duties of horse-keepers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**8.** The manager of every mine shall make and secure the efficient carrying out of arrangements whereby a competent person appointed for the purpose by the manager (in these regulations referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**horse-keeper *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]**”) has the care of each horse employed below ground at the mine when it is in a stable at that mine, and without prejudice to the generality of that requirement the number of horse-keepers so appointed shall not be less than one for each fifteen horses, and one for any number of horses not thus taken into account, in the total number of horses employed below ground at that mine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**9.** Every horse-keeper shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) ensure that no horse for the time being under his care leaves its stable, for the purpose of work, in an unfit condition, improperly shod or wearing harness otherwise than of a proper fit and in good condition and having a guard for the eyes; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) forthwith after the return to its stable of any horse for the time being under his care, examine that horse and its harness, attend to any injury to that horse, and clean and groom it or cause it to be cleaned and groomed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**10.** (1) In the case of every mine at which any horse is employed, there shall be kept, in a book provided for the purpose by the owner of the mine, a record of each horse for the time being so employed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) Every horse-keeper shall, with respect to every horse for the time being under his care, make a full and accurate report every day in the book provided in pursuance of the last preceding paragraph, stating in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) the condition of the horse; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) the name of every person (other than the horse-keeper) who has been in charge of it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) in any case in which it has been taken from its stable, the times at which it was taken therefrom and returned thereto; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (d) the nature and period of any working of that horse for any purpose specified in the proviso to paragraph (1) of regulation seven of these regulations, being working which, but for the operation of that proviso, would be prohibited by that regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (e) the nature of any report made to or by him in pursuance of paragraph (2) of regulation fourteen or paragraph (1) of regulation fifteen (as the case may be) of these regulations, and the nature of any action known by him to have been taken as a consequence of that report; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (f) any other information for which provision is made in that book. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Construction, equipment and use of stables (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**11.** (1) The manager shall ensure that no stable below ground at a mine is used to house any horse unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) no part of it is in a road on which any person travels to or from his working place, vehicles (other than vehicles used for the servicing of the stable) run or conveyors are operated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) it is provided with suitable and sufficient artificial lighting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) it is ventilated by air which has not ventilated any working face; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (d) there is provided, at its return end, one loose box, of adequate size to house the largest horse for the time being in that stable, for each twenty-five horses, and one such loose box for any number of horses not thus taken into account, in the total number of horses for the time being housed in that stable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (e) there are provided one or more drinking troughs, so constructed as to be easily drained and cleaned, being adequate in aggregate length and capacity for the horses for the time being housed in that stable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (f) the floor throughout is paved or of concrete; and suitable drainage is provided for every stall and loose box therein; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (g) every roof, wall and partition (other than a roof, wall or partition which has been painted or is made of slate, tiles, glazed brick or iron) has been limewashed before being so used and at intervals not exceeding three months during such use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) The manager shall ensure that no stall below ground at a mine is used— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) to house more than one horse; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) to house any horse, unless it is fitted with a manager. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(3) The manager shall make and secure the efficient carrying out of arrangements to ensure that every horse below ground at the mine is provided with accommodation in a stable and, except when in a loose box, in a stall of adequate size for that horse. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**12.** The manager of every mine at which any horses are employed below ground shall make and secure the carrying out of efficient arrangements to ensure that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) every stable in use is cleaned daily, and kept in a sanitary condition; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) an adequate supply of clean straw or other suitable bedding is provided for the use of every horse in a stable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) there is provided daily, and so far as practicable protected from dust and other deleterious substances before it is consumed, an adequate supply of wholesome food and wholesome drinking water for every horse for consumption while the horse is in its stable and while it is out of its stable for the purpose of working; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (d) every receptacle for such food and water is so far as practicable so constructed as to protect the contents from dust and other deleterious substances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (e) an adequate supply of medicines, ointments and dressings of a kind suitable for horses, and suitable and sufficient apparatus for the destruction of horses requiring to be destroyed, are provided and kept readily available for use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

### Duties of persons in charge of horses outside the stables (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**13.** (1) The manager shall make and secure the efficient carrying out of arrangements to ensure that every horse below ground at the mine is, when out of its stable, under the charge of a person appointed for that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) Every person appointed in pursuance of the last preceding paragraph, when in charge of a horse out of its stable, shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) be responsible for that horse throughout the period from his taking charge of it until he returns it to a horse-keeper at its stable or otherwise disposes of it in accordance with an order given to him by an official of the mine or a horse-keeper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) when driving it, do so carefully; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) comply with any instructions relating to the horse given to him by an official of the mine or a horse-keeper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (d) in every case in which the horse is out of its stable for a period exceeding four hours, feed and water it, or cause it to be fed and watered during that period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (e) unless otherwise ordered by an official of the mine or a horse-keeper, return it not later than the end of his shift to a horse-keeper at its stable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (f) unless the horse is properly secured, remain with it throughout the period during which it is in his charge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

**14.** (1) No person shall at any time work a horse below ground at a mine if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) it is unfit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) it is not properly shod; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) it is wearing harness which does not fit properly or is not in good condition, or is without a guard for the eyes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

(2) In any case in which a person, other than a horse-keeper, for the time being in charge of a horse below ground at a mine has reason to believe that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

- (a) the horse is unfit, is injured, or shows any symptoms of overwork; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (b) the horse is not properly shod; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (c) the horse is wearing harness which does not fit properly or which is not in good condition, or is without a guard for the eyes; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (d) there is any inadequacy in the supply of food or water for the horse at or near any place where it is working; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]
- (e) the horse or its harness has rubbed against the roof or sides of a road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c843e2ba6a6b516c62bc8e5f972ab2b9]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order is made under the Mines and Quarries Act, 1954,s. 190, which empowers the Minister of Fuel and Power to re-enact (to the extent to which they could be enacted in regulations made under the Act and subject to modifications of the kind specified in that section) the provisions of regulations and enactments which will cease to have effect at the commencement of the Act.

The provisions set out as regulations in the first schedule to this order apply to mines of coal, stratified ironstone, shale and fireclay, and relate to the employment of horses in such mines. The regulations prohibit the taking below ground of any horse unless it is at least four years old and is certified by a veterinary surgeon to be free from glanders, and no blind horse may be taken or allowed to be below ground. Provision is made to ensure that every horse is periodically examined by a veterinary surgeon, and unfit horses must be dealt with as specified in reg. 4. The regulations also restrict the carrying of horses on vehicles below ground; prohibit persons from riding on them; lay down maximum periods of work; require the appointment and specify the duties of horse-keepers; and make provision with regard to the construction, equipment and use of stables and the general welfare and care of the horses not only when they are in their stables but also when they are at work. These regulations comprise, and will at the commencement of the Act replace, provisions of the regulations and enactments specified in the second schedule to the order.
