# The Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2108 (C. 63)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1991

Made

19th September 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 32(2) of the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1991.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 shall come into force on 23rd September 1991:

- Part III (drug trafficking money imported or exported in cash), so far as not already in force;
- Section 30(2) (payment of forfeited sums into the Consolidated Fund).

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

19th September 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force Part III of the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 (which provides for the seizure and forfeiture of drug trafficking money imported or exported in cash), so far as not already in force, and section 30(payment of forfeited sums into the Consolidated Fund).

The other provisions of the 1990 Act are already in force by virtue of S.I. [1991/1072](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1072).
