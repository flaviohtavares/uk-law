# The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Aylesbury to West of Tring) Detrunking Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2281

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Aylesbury to West of Tring) Detrunking Order 1991

Made

25th September 1991

Coming into force

31st October 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** The length of the Trunk Road described in the Schedule to this Order and shown by a broken black line on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road, and shall be classified as a classified road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Buckinghamshire that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

**2.** In this Order—

(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway:

(2)

- (i) “classified road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is not a principal road for the purposes of enactments or instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads but is a classified road for the purpose of every enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State and which does not specififically refer to their classification as principal roads;
- (ii) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/2E/316 marked “The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Aylesbury to West of Tring) Detrunking Order 1991”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (iiii) “the new trunk road” means the trunk road which the Secretary of State is authorised to construct in pursuance of the A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (Aston Clinton Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1991[^f00003]; and
- (iv) “the Trunk Road” means the London–Birmingham Trunk Road (A41).

**3.** This Order shall come into force on 31st October 1991 and may be cited as the A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Aylesbury to West of Tring) Detrunking Order 1991.

Signed by authority of the

Peter Emms

Regional Director Eastern Region

Department of Transport

25th September 1991

## SCHEDULE

LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE TRUNK ROAD The length of the Trunk Road ceasing to be a trunk road is from a point 1060 metres southeast of its junction with New Road, Weston Turville, to, and including, the Tring East Interchange.
