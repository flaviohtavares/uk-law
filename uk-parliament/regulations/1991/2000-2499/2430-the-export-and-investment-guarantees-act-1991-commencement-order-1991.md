# The Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2430 (C. 71)

EXPORT AND INVESTMENT GUARANTEES

The Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made

22nd October 1991

The Secretary of State, in the exercise of his powers under Section 15(6) of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

**2.** The provisions of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 shall come into force on 23rd October 1991.

Timothy Sainsbury

Minister for Trade,

Department of Trade and Industry

22nd October 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 23rd October 1991 the whole of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991.
