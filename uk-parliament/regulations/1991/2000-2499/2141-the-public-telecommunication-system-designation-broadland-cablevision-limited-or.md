# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Broadland Cablevision Limited) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2141

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Broadland Cablevision Limited) Order 1991

Made

18th September 1991

Laid before Parliament

26th September 1991

Coming into force

24th October 1991

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Broadland Cablevision Limited on17th January 1991 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation(Broadland Cablevision Limited) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 24th October 1991.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

John Redwood

Minister of State,

Department of Tradeand Industry

18th September 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Broadland Cablevision Limited on 17th January 1991a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the eastern parts ofthe Districts of Great Yarmouth and Waveney. A copy of that licence was laid before Parliament on 26th September 1991. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Broadland Cablevision Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 24th October 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
