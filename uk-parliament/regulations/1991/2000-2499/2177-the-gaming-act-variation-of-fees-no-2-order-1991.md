# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2177

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991

Made

30th September 1991

Laid before Parliament

7th October 1991

Coming into force

2nd December 1991

In pursuance of sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

(2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

**2.** Section 48(3)(h) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00002] (under which the fee to be charged for the grant or renewal of a permit under section 34 of that Act is £25) shall have effect with the substitution, for the sum referred to therein, of a reference to £32.

**3.** The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th September 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from £25 to £32 the fee to be charged in England and Wales under the Gaming Act 1968 for the grant or renewal of a permit under section 34 of that Act for premises where gaming machines are used.
