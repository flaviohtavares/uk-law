# The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2240

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991

Made

7th October 1991

Laid before Parliament

10th October 1991

Coming into force

1st November 1991

The Secretary of State for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 218(5)(c) and (6)(c) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Teachers) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st November 1991.

## 2. Amendment

Regulation 7 of the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989[^f00002] shall be amended as follows by the insertion after paragraph (b) of the following—

> - (c) by the governing body of a school as workers with children or young persons;

.

Kenneth Clarke

Secretary of State for Education and Science

7th October 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989: the definition of “relevant employment” in regulation 7 is amended to cover the employment by the governing bodies of schools of workers with children and young persons.
