# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.8) Order 1991 Revocation Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2126 (S. 175)

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.8) Order 1991 Revocation Order 1991

Made

20th September 1991

Laid before Parliament

23rd September 1991

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and (2) and 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.8) Order 1991 Revocation Order 1991 and shall come into force at 17.00 hours on 20th September 1991.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.8) Order 1991[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

E. C. Davison

Assistant Secretary Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

20th September 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.8) Order 1991 which prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking velvet crabs within the area designated in the Schedule to that Order (Article 4),
- (b) movement of velvet crabs out of that area (Article 5),
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, velvet crabs from the designated area within the United Kingdom (Article 6).
