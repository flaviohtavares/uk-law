# The Value Added Tax Act 1983 (Interest on Overpayments etc) (Prescribed Rate) (No. 2) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2282

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax Act 1983 (Interest on Overpayments etc) (Prescribed Rate) (No. 2) Order 1991

Made

14th October 1991

Laid before the House of Commons

15th October 1991

Coming into force

16th October 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 38A(3) of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax Act 1983 (Interest on Overpayments etc) (Prescribed Rate) (No. 2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 16th October 1991.

**2.** For the purposes of section 38A of the Value Added Tax Act 1983 the prescribed rate shall be 10.25 per cent.

Sydney Chapman

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

14th October 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reduces from 12 per cent to 10.25 per cent the prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of section 38A of the Value Added Tax Act 1983 (interest in certain cases of official error).
