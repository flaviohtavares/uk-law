# The Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2488 (C.72)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made

31st October 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9(2) of the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

**2.** The Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

31st October 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1992 the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991.
