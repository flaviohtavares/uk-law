# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2499 (S.199)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991

Made

31st October 1991

Laid before Parliament

11th November 1991

Coming into force

2nd December 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** –

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

(2) This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

**2.** Section 48(3)(h) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00002] (under which the fee to be charged for the grant or renewal of a permit under section 34 of that Act is £25) shall have effect with the substitution, for the sum referred to therein, of a reference to £32.

**3.** The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

31st October 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, increases from £25 to £32 the fee to be charged under the Gaming Act 1968 for the grant or renewal of a permit under section 34 of that Act.
