# The River Lunan Catchment Area Protection (Renewal) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2236 (S. 186)

RIVER, SCOTLAND

SALMON AND FRESHWATER FISHERIES

The River Lunan Catchment Area Protection (Renewal) Order 1991

Made

7th October 1991

Coming into force

8th October 1991

The Secretary of State, having received no complaints concerning the implementation of proposals, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(7) of the Freshwater and Salmon Fisheries (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the River Lunan Catchment Area Protection (Renewal) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 8th October 1991.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order —

- “the 1983 Order” means the River Lunan Catchment Area Protection Order 1983[^f00002];
- “the 1988 Order” means the River Lunan Catchment Area Protection (Renewal) Order 1988[^f00003].

## 3. Renewal of Order

The 1988 Order is hereby renewed.

## 4. Prohibition

During the period from 8th October 1991 to 7th October 1994, both dates inclusive, fishing for or taking freshwater fish in the inland waters in the area prescribed in article 4 of the 1983 Order, without legal right or without written permission from a person having such a right, is hereby prohibited.

T. J. Kelly

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

7th October 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order renews for the third time the prohibition contained in article 3 of the River Lunan Catchment Area Protection Order 1983, which prohibited fishing for or taking freshwater fish in inland waters in the area prescribed in that Order without legal right or without written permission from someone having such a right. The effect of the renewal is that the prohibition has effect from 8th October 1991 to 7th October 1994.

The prescribed area referred to is that part of the catchment area of the River Lunan lying within Tayside Region. The area is illustrated on a map attached to the 1983 Order and is defined by reference to a detailed large scale map which may be inspected at the principal office of the Scottish Office Agriculture and Fisheries Department in Edinburgh, on any working day between 10.00 am and 4.00 pm.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the boundaries of the prescribed area.
