# The Fertilisers Regulations 1991, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Limits of variation[^c-7a33dda06d17d953d69afb8cd5e86632]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of section 74, the limits of variation in relation to any misstatement as to the nature, substance or quality of any material specified in the second column of the table in Schedule 1 shall, subject to the provisions of that Schedule, be the corresponding limits in relation to that material set out in the fifth and, as the case may be, in the sixth column of the said table.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**6.** The Regulations come into force on 1st November 1991, but subject to regulation 1(2) in the case of material, not designated as an EEC fertiliser, sold or offered for sale loose or in large containers before 1st June 1992 or in small containers before 1st February 1993.
