# The Fertilisers Regulations 1991, section 2B

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2B. Scope: “EC fertilisers”[^c-ad0af8cad58cc571ef6f1d90dcd4421e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations do not apply to fertilisers designated as “EC fertilisers” which comply with the requirements set out in Regulation [(EC) No 2003/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/2003) as it has effect in EU law as amended from time to time, where those fertilisers are placed on the market before the end of the period of two years beginning with IP completion day.
