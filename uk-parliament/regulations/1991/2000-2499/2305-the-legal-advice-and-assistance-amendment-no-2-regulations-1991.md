# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2305

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

Made

17th October 1991

Laid before Parliament

21st October 1991

Coming into force

11th November 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 9, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], having had regard to the matters specified in section 34(9) and consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and transitional provisions

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 11th November 1991.

(2) Regulations 3 and 4 shall apply to any assessment or review of a claim for costs made in respect of work done on or after 11th November 1991 and costs payable in respect of work done before that date shall be assessed or reviewed as if these Regulations had not been made.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations a regulation or Schedule referred to by number means a regulation or Schedule so numbered in the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989

In regulation 30—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for “paragraph (2)” there shall be substituted “paragraphs (2) and (3)”;
- (b) after paragraph (2) there shall be inserted the following new paragraph—

  > - (3) Where the claim is in respect of ABWOR to which Part III of the Act applies by virtue of regulation 9(a) of the Scope Regulations, paragraph (1) shall apply as if Schedule 6 to these Regulations were substituted for Schedule 1, Part I, paragraph 1(1)(a) to the Costs Regulations.

  .

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989 by increasing the rate of remuneration in respect of advice by way of representation (ABWOR) in respect of proceedings before Mental Health Review Tribunals.
