# The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2309 (S. 191)

AGRICULTURE

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1991

Made

16th October 1991

Laid before Parliament

21st October 1991

Coming into force

11th November 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 to the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Application, citation and commencement

This Order, which applies to tenancies in Scotland, may be cited as the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1991 and shall come into force on 11th November 1991.

## 2. Amendment of principal order

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Revocation

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1987[^f00003], the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1988[^f00004] and the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1990[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

Strathclyde

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Order 1986, which prescribes the “standard yield per hectare” and the “average yield per hectare” to be taken into account in determining the “standard yield” for the purposes of calculating the payment which a tenant in Scotland is entitled, by Schedule 2 to the Agriculture Act 1986, to obtain from his landlord in respect of milk quota on the termination of a lease of a tenancy.

This Order substitutes for the Schedule to that Order a new Schedule, in which the “standard yield per hectare” prescribed by article 3 of that Order is revised.

This Order also revokes the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1987, the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1988 and the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1990.
