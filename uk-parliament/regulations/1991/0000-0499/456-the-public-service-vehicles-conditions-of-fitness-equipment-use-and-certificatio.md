# The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 456

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

28th February 1991

Laid before Parliament

7th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10(1), 52(1)(a) and 60 of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and by the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

**2.** The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

**3.** In the Regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the amounts specified in column 2 of the Table there shall be substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of the Table.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981 as follows.

- (a) The fee payable for a certificate of initial fitness is increased from £123.00 to £130.50, both on the first application, and on any subsequent application when the test which that application will require includes a test of stability under Regulation 6. The fee payable for the certificate on any other subsequent application is increased from £14.75 to £15.60.
- (b) The fees payable for type approvals are increased from £710.00, £67.00, £352.00 and £1520.00 to respectively £753.00, £71.00, £373.00 and £1611.00.
- (c) The fee payable for the issue of a duplicate certificate is increased from £8.25 to £8.80.
