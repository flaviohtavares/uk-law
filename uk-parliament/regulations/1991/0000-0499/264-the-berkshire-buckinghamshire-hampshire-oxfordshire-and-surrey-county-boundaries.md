# The Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hampshire, Oxfordshire and Surrey (County Boundaries) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 264

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

CHANGES IN LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREAS

The Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hampshire, Oxfordshire and Surrey (County Boundaries) Order 1991

Made

18th February 1991

Laid before Parliament

20th February 1991

Coming into force

for the purposes referred to in article 1(2)

15th March 1991

for all other purposes

1st April 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order which gives effect to proposals submitted to him under sections 47, 48(1) and 5 1 (1) of that Act, more than six weeks before the making of this Order, by the Local Government Boundary Commission for England—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hampshire, Oxfordshire and Surrey (County Boundaries) Order 1991.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 15th March 1991 for any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) (coming into operation of order) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976[^f00002].

(3) For all other purposes this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, “boundary map” means one of the 29 maps prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Maps of the Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hampshire, Oxfordshire and Surrey (County Boundaries) Order 1991” duly numbered for reference, and deposited in accordance with regulation 5 of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976.

## 3. Transfers between local government areas

The areas marked as indicated in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order on the boundary map indicated in column I of that Schedule shall be transferred—

- (a) from one county to another as specified in the heading to each Part of that Schedule;
- (b) from the district specified in column 3 of that Schedule;
- (c) from the parish (if any is named) specified in column 4 of that Schedule;
- (d) to the district specified in column 5 of that Schedule;
- (e) to the ward specified in column 6 of that Schedule (being a ward of the district to which the area is transferred);
- (f) to the parish (if any is named) specified in column 7 of that Schedule;
- (g) to the ward (if any is named) specified in column 8 of that Schedule (being a ward of the parish to which the area is transferred);
- (h) to the electoral division specified in column 9 of that Schedule (being an electoral division of the county to which the area is transferred);
- (i) to the petty sessional division (if any is named) specified in column 10 of that Schedule; and
- (j) to the coroner’s district specified in column 11 of that Schedule.

## 4. Town and country planning

Any provision of a development plan applying to an area transferred by this Order shall continue to apply thereto notwithstanding the transfer; and after such transfer, any such provision shall, in so far as it so applies, be for all purposes deemed to be a provision of a corresponding plan of the authority which becomes the appropriate local planning authority for the transferred area as a result of the transfer.

## 5. Retained property

Numbers 1 and 2 London Road, Ascot which are transferred by this Order to the district of the Royal Borough of Windsor and Maidenhead in Berkshire are hereby specified in relatinon specified in relation to regulation 62(1)(b) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Emily Blatch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

This Order transfers areas between Berkshire and the counties of Oxfordshire, Buckinghamshire, Surrey and Hampshire and makes consequential changes to disticts, parishes, local government electoral areas and coroners' districts.

The map forming part of this note gives a general guide to the areas affected, Prints of the detailed boundary maps (described in article 2) may be inspected at all reasonable times at the offices of the district councils whose areas are affected by this Order, and at the office of the Secretary of State for the Enviroment, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB.

The areas transferred have approximately the area and population shown below:

| *Counties* | *Area (hectares)* | *Population* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oxfordshire to Berkshire | 9 | 0 |
| Berkshire to Oxfordshire | 7 | 11 |
| Buckinghamshire to Berkshire | 10 | 0 |
| Berkshire to Buckinghamshire | 22 | 65 |
| Surrey to Berkshire | 195 | 2,019 |
| Berkshire to Surrey | 72 | 55 |
| Hampshire to Berkshire | 26 | 317 |
| Berkshire to Hampshire | 44 | 1,287 |

The local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976 contain general provisions on the effect of orders such as this one. Article 5 of this Order prevents ownership of the properties described in that article being transferred under those Regulations.
