# The Buckinghamshire County Council Downhead Park to Willen Park (Canal Bridge) Scheme 1989 Confirmation Instrument 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No.37

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Buckinghamshire County Council Downhead Park to Willen Park (Canal Bridge) Scheme 1989 Confirmation Instrument 1991

Made

11th January 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The Buckinghamshire County Council Downhead Park to Willen Park (Canal Bridge) Scheme 1989 is hereby confirmed with modifications.

**2.** The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY; and at the offices of the County Council of Buckinghamshire, County Hall, Aylesbury, Bucks, HP20 1UA.

**3.** The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**4.** This Instrument may be cited as the Buckinghamshire County Council Downhead Park to Willen Park (Canal Bridge) Scheme 1989 Confirmation Instrument 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J.A. Owen

Regional Director, Northern Region,

Department of Transport

11th January 1991

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE INSTRUMENT

THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF BUCKINGHAMSHIRE (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under section 106 of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the Grand Union Canal between Downhead Park and Willen Park in the Borough of Milton Keynes in the County of Buckinghamshire the bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct between Stanway Crescent Downhead Park and Hammond Crescent Willen Park in the Borough of Milton Keynes in the said County.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as The Buckinghamshire County Council Downhead Park to Willen Park (Canal Bridge) Scheme 1989.

GIVEN under the Common Seal of the Council this twenty first day of June 1989
