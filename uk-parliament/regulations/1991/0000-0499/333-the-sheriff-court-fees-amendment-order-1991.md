# The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 333 (S. 32)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1991

Made

20th February 1991

Laid before Parliament

6th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

**2.** For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1990[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th February 1991

We concur,

Gregory Knight

I. C. Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th February 1991
