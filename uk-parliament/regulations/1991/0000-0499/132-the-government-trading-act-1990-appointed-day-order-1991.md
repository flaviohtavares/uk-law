# The Government Trading Act 1990 (Appointed Day) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 132 (C.4)

BORROWING CONTROL

The Government Trading Act 1990 (Appointed Day) Order 1991

Made

28th January 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 4(3) of the Government Trading Act 1990[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Government Trading Act 1990 (Appointed Day) Order 1991.

**2.** Section 4 of the Government Trading Act 1990 (which relates to the repeal of the Borrowing (Control and Guarantees) Act 1946[^f00002]) shall come into force on 11th February 1991.

Gregory Knight

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

28th January 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)
