# The Whitehaven Harbour Revision Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 238

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Whitehaven Harbour Revision Order 1991

Made

7th February 1991

Coming into force

8th February 1991

Whereas the making of this Order is not opposed:

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Transport (being the appropriate Minister under section 14(7) of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001] for the purpose of making this Order) in exercise of the powers conferred by the said section 14 and now vested in him[^f00002]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and on the application of the Whitehaven Harbour Commissioners, hereby make the following Order:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Whitehaven Harbour Revision Order 1991 and shall come into force on 8th February 1991.

(2) The Whitehaven Town and Harbour Acts and Order 1708 to 1981 and this Order may be cited together as the Whitehaven Town and Harbour Acts and Orders 1708 to 1991.

## 2. Amendment of article 13 of Scheme of 1894

Paragraph (a) of article 13 of the Borough and Harbour of Whitehaven Scheme 1894[^f00003](which precludes any woman being appointed or elected as a Harbour Commissioner) shall cease to have effect.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

H. B. Wenban-Smith

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

7th February 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes the restriction contained in a Scheme of 1894 upon women being appointed or elected as Commissioners of Whitehaven Harbour.
