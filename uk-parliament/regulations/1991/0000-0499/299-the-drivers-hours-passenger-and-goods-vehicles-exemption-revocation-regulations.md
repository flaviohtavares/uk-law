# The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 299

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1991

Made

22nd February 1991

Laid before Parliament

22nd February 1991

Coming into force

25th February 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 96(10) of the Transport Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002]and of all other enabling powers, after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 101(6) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 25th February 1991.

**2.** The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) Regulations 1991[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister for Public Transport,

Department of Transport

22 February 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) Regulations 1991.
