# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 323 (S.28)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1991

Approved by the House of Commons

Made

28th January 1991

Laid before the House of Commons

30th January 1991

Coming into force

14th February 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 1(1) and (2) of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be appropriate in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of the said Schedule[^f00001], and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1991 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

## 2. Revenue support grants for 1991-92

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1991-92 are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the financial year 1991-92 shall be the amount determined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

Ian Lang

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th January 1991

We consent,
