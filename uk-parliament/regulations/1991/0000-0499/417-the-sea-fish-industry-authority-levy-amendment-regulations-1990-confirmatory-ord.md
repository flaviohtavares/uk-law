# The Sea Fish Industry Authority (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 Confirmatory Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 417

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fish Industry Authority (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 Confirmatory Order 1991

Made

1st March 1991

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

Whereas the Sea Fish Industry Authority (hereinafter referred to as “**the Authority**”) has made the Sea Fish Industry Authority (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 (hereinafter referred to as “**the Regulations**”);

And whereas the Authority has transmitted to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fish industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland (hereinafter referred to as “**the Ministers**”) the objections to the Regulations which have been duly made to the Authority and have not been withdrawn and the Ministers have considered such objections;

And whereas the Ministers, after consultation with the Authority, have considered it desirable to make modifications in the Regulations and have caused notice of those modifications to be published in such manner as they thought best adapted for informing persons affected;

Now, therefore, the Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 4(2) and (10) and 14(1) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00001] and of all their other enabling powers, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fish Industry Authority (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 Confirmatory Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

## 2. Modification and confirmation

The Regulations, having been modified by the Ministers, are hereby confirmed and the Regulations as modified are set out in the Schedule to this Order.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 27th February 1991.

## SCHEDULE — THE SEA FISH INDUSTRY AUTHORITY (LEVY) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 1990, AS MODIFIED

Article 2

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confirms, after modification, Regulations made by the Sea Fish Industry Authority which amend the Sea Fish Industry Authority (Levy) Regulations 1988. The amending Regulations change most of the rates of the levy imposed by that Authority in respect of sea fish and sea fish products for the purpose of financing its activities. The old rates of levy are shown in italics in the revised Schedule to the new Regulations.
