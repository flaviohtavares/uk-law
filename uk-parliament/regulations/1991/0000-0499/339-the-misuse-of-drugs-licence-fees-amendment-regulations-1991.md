# The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 339

DANGEROUS DRUGS

The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

26th February 1991

Laid before Parliament

6th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

In pursuance of sections 30, 31 and 37(1) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001], and after consultation with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs, I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

**2.** Regulation 3(1) of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00002] shall be amended as follows–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£342” there shall be substituted “£378”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£228” there shall be substituted “£252”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for “£114” there shall be substituted “£126”;
- (d) in sub-paragraph (d), for “£114” there shall be substituted “£126”; and
- (e) in sub-paragraph (e), for “£57” there shall be substituted “£63”.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

26th February 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 30 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 provides that a licence issued by the Secretary of State for the purposes of that Act or of Regulations made under it may be issued subject to the condition of payment of a fee. The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 1986, as amended, prescribe the fee payable in relation to a licence to produce, supply, offer to supply or possess controlled drugs; the present Regulations further amend those Regulations so as to increase the fees there prescribed by approximately 10 per cent.
