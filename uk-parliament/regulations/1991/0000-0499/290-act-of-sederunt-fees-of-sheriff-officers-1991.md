# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 290 (S.26)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1991

Made

19th February 1991

Laid before Parliament

4th March 1991

Coming into force

25th March 1991

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1991 and shall come into force on 25th March 1991.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs of this rule, for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990[^f00003], substitute the table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

(2) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 as substituted by paragraph (1) above shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederant comes into force.

(3) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 having effect before the substitution under paragraph (1) above shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, IPD

Edinburgh

19th February 1991

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 by increasing the fees payable to Sheriff Officers by about 14%. The last increase was in the ACt of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1989 (S.I. [1989/1018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1018)).
