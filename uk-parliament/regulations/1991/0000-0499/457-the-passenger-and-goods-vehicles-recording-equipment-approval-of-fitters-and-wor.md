# The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 457

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

4th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

7th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

**2.** In Regulation 3 of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00003] for “£230” there shall be substituted “£244” and for “£93” there shall be substituted “£99”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

28th February 1991

We consent to the making of the Regulations

Sydney Chapman

Gregory Knight

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986 to increase the fees for the approval of fitters or workshops for the installation or repair of recording equipment (Tachographs). The fees were last increased in December 1989.

The fee for the issue of an approval is increased from £230 to £244 and for the renewal of an approval from £93 to £99.
