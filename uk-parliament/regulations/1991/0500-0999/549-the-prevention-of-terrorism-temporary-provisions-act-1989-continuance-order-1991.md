# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (Continuance) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 549

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (Continuance) Order 1991

Made

7th March 1991

Coming into force

22nd March 1991

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 27(6)(a) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (Continuance) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 22nd March 1991.

(2) In this Order “the 1989 Act” means the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989.

**2.** This Order applies to the provisions of Parts I to V and section 27(6)(c) of the 1989 Act except the provisions of Parts III and V so far as they have effect in Northern Ireland and relate to proscribed organisations for the purposes of section 21 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1978[^f00002] or offences or orders under that section.

**3.** The provisions of the 1989 Act to which this Order applies shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beginning with 22nd March 1991.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

7th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues in force, with exceptions, the provisions of Parts I to V and section 27(6)(c) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (“the 1989 Act”) for a period of 12 months from 22nd March 1991. The exceptions are the provisions of Parts III and V of the 1989 Act so far as they have effect in Northern Ireland and relate to proscribed organisations for the purposes of section 21 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1978 (“the 1978 Act”) or offences or orders under that section. Separate provision is made for the continuance in force of those provisions under section 33(3) of the 1978 Act by virtue of section 27(11) of the 1989 Act.
