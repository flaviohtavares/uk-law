# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 608 (C.14)

LEGAL SERVICES

LEGAL PROFESSION

ARBITRATION

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

OATHS

The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1991

Made

11th March 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1991.

**2.** The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 11th March 1991
