# The (A696) Ponteland Road Roundabout (Trunking) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 696

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The (A696) Ponteland Road Roundabout (Trunking) Order 1991

Made

27th February 1991

Coming into force

15th April 1991

The Secretary of State of Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The roundabout at Ponteland Road at the junction with the A1 trunk road Newcastle Western Bypass in the City of Newcastle upon Tyne and shown by black vertical stripes on the deposited plan shall become trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** In this Order “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/2-1N228, marked “The (A696) Ponteland Road Roundabout (Trunking) Order 1991”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.

**3.** This Order shall come into force on 15th April 1991 and may be cited as the (A696) Ponteland Road Roundabout (Trunking) Order 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J A Owen

Regional Director, Northern Region,

Department of Transport

27th February 1991
