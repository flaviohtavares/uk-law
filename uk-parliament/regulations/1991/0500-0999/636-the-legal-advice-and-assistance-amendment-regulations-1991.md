# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 636

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

11th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

18th March 1991

Coming into force

8th April 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 9, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 8th April 1991.

**2.** In these Regulations a regulation or Schedule referred to by number means a regulation or Schedule so numbered in the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989[^f00002].

**3.** In paragraph (1) of regulation 30 the words after “Part I,” shall be deleted and there shall be inserted the words “paragraph 1(1)(a) to the Costs Regulations as if the work done was work to which those provisions applied, save that paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 1, Part I shall not apply.”.

**4.** In the table in Schedule 3 for the figure “£64” there shall be substituted the figure “£70” and for the figure “£72” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the figure “£74”.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 8th March 1991

We consent,

Thomas Sackville

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 11th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989 by excluding from the assessment of costs under the Regulations the provisions of the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (Costs) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/343](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/343)) which allow for reduction and enhancement of the prescribed rates and by amending the scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance.
