# The Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 657

SEEDS

Made

8th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

22nd March 1991

Coming into force

12th April 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 16(1), (1A)(e) and (8) and 36 of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to them to be concerned in accordance with section 16(1) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 12th April 1991.

## 2. Amendment of the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) Regulations 1990

(1) The Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) Regulations 1990[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of this regulation.

(2) In regulation 3 (payment of fees)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) for the words “Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) below” there shall be substituted the words “Subject to paragraph (2) below”;
  - (ii) sub-paragraph (c) shall be omitted;
  - (iii) for sub-paragraph (d) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

    > - (d) a renewal fee, being the new fee set out in the second column of Part III of the Schedule in relation to a year of retention of a plant variety in a National List opposite the reference in the first column of the said Part III to the plant variety of the kind retained in the National List, such fee being payable, subject to regulation 4(3) below, before the beginning of the year of retention in the National List;

    ;
  - (iv) in sub-paragraph (e) “Part IV” shall be substituted for “Part V” in each of the three places in which it occurs;
- (b) paragraph (3) shall be omitted.

(3) In paragraph (2) of regulation 4 (renewal fees) “Part III” shall be substituted for “Part IV”.

(4) For the Schedule there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) Regulations 1990 by prescribing revised fees in respect of matters arising under the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) Regulations 1982 (S.I. [1982/844](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/844)) (as amended) (regulation 2(4)).

The matters in respect of which the revised fees are prescribed are listed in the Schedule to these Regulations, and the fees which were chargeable before these Regulations came into force are printed in italics beside the revised fees.

These Regulations also amend the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Fees) Regulations 1990 so as to—

- (a) remove the requirement that a fee be paid in respect of trials for value for cultivation and use of a plant variety on application for entry in a National List (regulation 2(2)(a)(i) and (ii) and 2(2)(b));
- (b) require the payment of the same renewal fee for each year of the retention of a plant variety in the National List (regulation 2(2)(a)(iii).
