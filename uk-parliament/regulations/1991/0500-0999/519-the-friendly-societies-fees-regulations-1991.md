# The Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 519

FRIENDLY SOCIETIES

The Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1991

Made

7th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

8th March 1991

Coming into force

1st April 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 104(1) of the Friendly Societies Act 1974[^f00001] and by article 2 of the Friendly Societies (Fees) Order 1971[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations supersede the Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1990. They generally increase by about 8% the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted and for the inspection of documents under the Friendly Societies Act 1974.
