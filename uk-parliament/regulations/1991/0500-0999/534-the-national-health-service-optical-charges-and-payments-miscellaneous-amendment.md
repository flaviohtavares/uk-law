The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 26(1), (1C), (1E) and (2), 70(1), 73(a) and (c), 74(a) and (c), 105(7) and 108(1) of, and paragraphs 2 and 2A of Schedule 11 to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) (Scotland) Regulations 1989[^f00002].

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) (Scotland) Regulations 1989 (“the principal Regulations”) which provide for payments to be made, by means of a voucher system, in respect of costs incurred by certain categories of persons in connection with the supply and replacement of optical appliances, and make an associated amendment to the Regulations which make provision for general ophthalmic services (ie. sight testing services).

Regulation 2 amends the definition of “complex applicance” to include an optical appliance at least one lens of which is a prism-controlled bifocal lens (and regulation 19 similarly amends the definition of “complex applicance” in the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1986), and regulation 18(1)(b)(i) makes a consequential amendment to the specifications of appliances for which vouchers may be used. Regulation 18(1)(a) and (b)(ii) and (iii) clarify the specifications of appliances to which vouchers with letter codes C and F apply.

These Regulations amend Schedule I to, and substitute a new Schedule 2 in, the principal Regulations to increase by an average of 6.00% (11.5% in the case of contact lenses) the value of vouchers issued in respect of the cost of the supply and replacement of glasses and contact lenses (regulation 18(2)) and of the repair and replacement of appliances (regulation 18(3) and the Schedule). Regulation 16 makes consequential amendments to regulation 20 of the principal Regulations (redemption value of voucher replacement or repair). Regulation 18(4) increases the additional values for vouchers in respect of prisms, tints, photochromic lenses and special categories of appliances.

These Regulations also make amendments in consequence of the coming into force of powers enabling provisions of NHS services from lst April 1991 by NHS trusts under the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 as amended by the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990.

Regulation 2 substitutes the definition of “responsible Board” and amends the definition of “voucher” to make provision in respect of vouchers issued and completed by an NHS trust, and further amendments are made in the principal Regulations by regulations 3 to 15, 17 and 18(4)(f)(i) and (4)(g)(i) so that charges and payments may be made, and vouchers issued, in respect of the supply of optical appliances by NHS trusts.

Regulation 20 makes similar consequential amendments in the National Health Service (Charges for Appliances) (Scotland) Regulations 1974.
