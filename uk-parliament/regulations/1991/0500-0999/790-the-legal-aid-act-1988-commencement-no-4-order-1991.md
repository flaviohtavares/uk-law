# The Legal Aid Act 1988 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 790 (C.17)

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Aid Act 1988 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1991

Made

21st March 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him bysection 47(2) of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Legal Aid Act 1988 (Commencement No.4) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st May 1991.

(2) Sections 29 and 30(3) of the Legal Aid Act 1988 shall come intoforce on 1st May 1991.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

21st March 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 29 and 30(3) of the Legal AidAct 1988 (representation in contempt proceedings and application ofsection 25 to contempt proceedings).
