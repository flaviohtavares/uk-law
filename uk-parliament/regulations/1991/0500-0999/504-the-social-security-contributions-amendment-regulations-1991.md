# The Social Security (Contributions) Amendment Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 504

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Contributions) Amendment Regulations 1991

Made

5th March 1991

Coming into force

6th April 1991

Whereas a draft of the following Regulations was laid before Parliament in accordance with the provisions of section 167(1) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00001] and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Social Security, in conjunction with the Treasury[^f00002], in exercise of powers conferred by sections 129(1) and 166(2) of, and Schedule 20 to, the Social Security Act 1975[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals to make these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00004], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Contributions) Amendment Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 6th April 1991.

## 2. Amendment to Regulations

In regulation 98 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979[^f00005] (modification of provisions of the Social Security Act 1975 in relation to share fishermen), in paragraph (c) (weekly rate of any Class 2 contributions payable by share fishermen) for the sum of “£6.15” there shall be substituted the sum of “£6.20”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Nicholas Scott

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

1st March 1991

We consent,

Sydney Chapman

Irvine Patrick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

5th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend regulation 98 (which relates to share fishermen) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979. They amend paragraph (c) in that regulation by increasing the special rate of Class 2 contributions payable by share fishermen from £6.15 to £6.20.
