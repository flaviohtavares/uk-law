# The King’s Lynn-Newark Trunk Road (Leadenham By-Pass and Slip Roads) (Revocation) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 799

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The King’s Lynn-Newark Trunk Road (Leadenham By-Pass and Slip Roads) (Revocation) Order 1991

Made

20th March 1991

Coming into force

4th April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The King’s Lynn-Newark Trunk Road (Leadenham By-Pass and Slip Roads) Order 1973[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 4th April 1991 and may be cited as the King’s Lynn-Newark Trunk Road (Leadenham By-Pass and Slip Roads) (Revocation) Order 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D.J. Morrison

Regional Director, East Midlands Region,

Department of Transport

20th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes Order made under sections 7 and 44 of the Highways Act [1959 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1959/25), section 27 of the Local Government Act [1966 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1966/42) and section 68 of the Highways Act [1971 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/41).
