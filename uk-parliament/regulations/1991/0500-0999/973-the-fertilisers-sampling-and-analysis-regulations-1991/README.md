The Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1991

Made

27th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

29th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply throughout Great Britain, consolidate and supersede the Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1978 ([SI. 1978/1108](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/1108)) and the Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1980 ([SI. 1980/1130](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1980/1130)). They implement the Directives listed in paragraph 2 below.
