# The Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1991, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Methods of sending part of a sample

Any part of a sample required to be sent to any person in pursuance of subsection (1)(b) or (2) of section 77 shall be sent by registered post or by the recorded delivery service or delivered or given by hand.

**4.** The principal changes from the superseded Regulations are the introduction of sampling procedures for fluid fertilisers (in Schedule 1) and of methods of analysis for straight ammonium nitrate fertiliser (in Schedule 2).
