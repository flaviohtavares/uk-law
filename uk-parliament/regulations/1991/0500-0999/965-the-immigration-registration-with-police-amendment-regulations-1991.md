# The Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 965

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

8th April 1991

Laid before Parliament

16th April 1991

Coming into force

1st June 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 4(3) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st June 1991.

**2.** In regulation 10(3) of the Immigration (Registration with Police) Regulations 1972[^f00002] (which relates to the fee to be paid for the issue of registration certificates), for the figure “£36” there shall be substituted the figure “£27.50”.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

8th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations reduce the fee payable when aliens are required to register with the police from £36 to £27.50. This reduction reflects a recent reassessment of the cost of the issue of registration certificates.
