# The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 996

DIPLOMATIC SERVICE

The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1991

Made

16th April 1991

Coming into force

16th May 1991

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 16th day of April 1991

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred upon Her by section 1(1) of the Consular Fees Act 1980[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order shall come into force on 16th May 1991 and may be cited as the Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1991.

**2.** Part II of the table of consular fees in the Schedule annexed to the Consular Fees Order 1989[^f00002] is amended as follows–

under Fee 22 delete subparagraph (1)(b)(iii) and replace it with the following words

> - (iii) for multiple entries, valid for six months from date of issue 30.00

.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Consular Fees Order 1989 as amended by the Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1990 so as to replace the two entry visa with a new short term multiple entry visa valid for six months. The fee remains the same at £30.
