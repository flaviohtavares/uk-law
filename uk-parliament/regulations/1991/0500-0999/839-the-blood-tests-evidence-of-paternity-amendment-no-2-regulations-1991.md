# The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 839

EVIDENCE

The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

Made

27th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

9th April 1991

Coming into force

1st May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 22 of the Family Law Reform Act 1969[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st May 1991.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**3.** The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1991[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

27th March 1991

These Regulations increase the charges which may he made by blood samplers under the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971 in respect of blood tests carried out for the purpose of determining paternity in civil proceedings. These were last increased in February 1991 (S.I. [1991/12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/12)).
