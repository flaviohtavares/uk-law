# The State Awards (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 831

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The State Awards (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

25th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

27th March 1991

Coming into force

17th April 1991

The Secretary of State for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(b) and (c) and 4(2) and (3) of the Education Act 1962[^f00001] (“the Act”) hereby makes the following regulations:–

## 1. Citation, Commencement and Extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the State Awards (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 17th April 1991.

(2) Save in so far as these regulations are made in pursuance of section 3(b) of the Act (awards in respect of post graduate and comparable courses) they shall not extend to Wales so as to apply in respect of persons ordinarily resident therein.

## 2. Amendment

In paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the State Awards Regulations 1978[^f00002] the definition of “relevant territory” shall be amended by the substitution for the words “and (as regards any period prior to their accession to that Community) the Kingdom of Spain and the Portuguese Republic” the words “and (as respects any period prior to the unification of the Federal Republic of Germany) the former German Democratic Republic.”

Kenneth Clarke

Secretary of State for Education and Science

25th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the State Awards Regulations 1978 so that for the purpose of determining eligibility for an award residence in the former German Democratic Republic prior to the unification of the Federal Republic of Germany is treated as residence in the European Community.
