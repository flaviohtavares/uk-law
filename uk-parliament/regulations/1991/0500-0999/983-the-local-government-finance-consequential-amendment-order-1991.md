# The Local Government Finance (Consequential Amendment) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 983

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

RATING AND VALUATION

The Local Government Finance (Consequential Amendment) Order 1991

Made

12th April 1991

Laid before Parliament

16th April 1991

Coming into force

7th May 1991

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, andthe Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of thepowers conferred on them by section 147(1) and (2) of the LocalGovernment Finance Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf,hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance(Consequential Amendment) Order 1991, and shall come into force on 7thMay 1991.

## 2. Consequential amendment

Section 109(6) of the Land Drainage Act 1976[^f00002] is amended by the omission of the words“or the rates upon which they are to be charged”.

Michael Heseltine

Secretary of State for the Environment

12th April 1991

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

12th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order amends subsection (6) of section 109 of theLand Drainage Act 1976. The words omitted from that subsection relate tothe rating system which was superseded by the provisions of the LocalGovernment Finance Act 1988.
