# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 871

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

28th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

9th April 1991

Coming into force

13th May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 14(2) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain, I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 13th May 1991.

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Scotland.

**2.** Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the figure “£5”, in both places where it occurs, the figure “£5.70”.

**3.** The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1989[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

28th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming on bingo club premises in England and Wales from £5.00 to £5.70.
