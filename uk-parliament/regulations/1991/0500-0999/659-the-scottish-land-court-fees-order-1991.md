# The Scottish Land Court (Fees) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 659 (S.69)

SCOTTISH LAND COURT

The Scottish Land Court (Fees) Order 1991

Made

11th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

18th March 1991

Coming into force

8th April 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Scottish Land Court (Fees) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 8th April 1991.

**2.** (1) This Order shall regulate the fees payable in respect of proceedings before the Scottish Land Court.

(2) The fees regulated by this Order shall be payable to the Principal Clerk of the Scottish Land Court or any officer acting for him.

**3.** The fees payable in respect of the matters specified in column 1 of the table of fees in the Schedule to this Order shall be the fees specified in relation to those matters in column 2 of that table (the fees formerly payable being shown in column 3 of that table).

**4.** The Scottish Land Court (Fees) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th March 1991

We concur,

I. C. Patnick

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which replaces the Scottish Land Court (Fees) Order 1990, prescribes fees payable in respect of applications to the Scottish Land Court and, in certain cases, increases the fees which were payable under the 1990 Order. A new fee is introduced at paragraph (1)(g)(ii) of the Table of Fees.
