# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1991, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Statutory Sick Pay

In section 7(1) of the Social Security and Housing Benefits Act 1982[^f00022] (relationship between rates of payment of statutory sick pay and employees' normal weekly earnings) the sums specified in paragraph (a) are £52.50 and £125.00 respectively and the sum specified in paragraph (b) is £39.25.

## Annotations

[^f00022]: Footnote: [1982 c. 24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/24); section 7(1) was substituted by regulation 2 of the Statutory Sick Pay (Rate of Payment) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/257](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/257)).
