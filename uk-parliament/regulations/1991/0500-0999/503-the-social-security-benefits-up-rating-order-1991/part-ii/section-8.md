# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1991, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Sum specified for child benefit

In regulation 2 of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations 1976[^f00021] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), the sum specified is £7.25; and
- (b) in paragaph (2), the sum specified is £5.60.
