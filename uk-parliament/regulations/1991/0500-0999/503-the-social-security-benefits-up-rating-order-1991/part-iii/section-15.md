# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1991, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Income Support Transitional Protection

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations 1987[^f00027] shall be increased by 8.1 per cent. of the amount apart from this Order.

## Annotations

[^f00027]: Footnote: S.I. [1987/1969](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1969); additions to income support may be included in the Order under section 63 of the Social Security Act 1986 by virtue of the modification made by regulation 2 of S.I. [1988/961](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/961).
