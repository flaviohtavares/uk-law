## SCHEDULE 4 — PART IV OF SCHEDULE 2 TO THE INCOME SUPPORT REGULATIONS AS AMENDED BY THIS ORDER

Article 14(5)

> **PART IV — WEEKLY AMOUNTS OF PREMIUMS SPECIFIED IN PART III**
>
> | Premium | Amount |
> | --- | --- |
> | **15.** —<br>(1) Lone parent premium. | (1) £4.45 |
> | (2) [^f00046] Pensioner premium for persons aged under 75—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9(a); | (2)<br>- (a) £13.75; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9(b). | - (b) £20.90. |
> | (2A) Pensioner premium for persons aged 75 and over—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(a); | (2A)<br>- (a) £15.55; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(b). | - (b) £23.35. |
> | (3) Higher Pensioner Premium—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(1)(a) or (b); | (3)<br>- (a) £18.45; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(2)(a) or (b). | - (b) £26.20. |
> | (4) Disability Premium—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(a); | (4)<br>- (a) £16.65; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(b). | - (b) £23.90. |
> | (5) Severe Disability Premium—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 13(2)(a); | (5)<br>- (a) £31.25; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 13(2)(b)—<br>  - (i) if there is someone in receipt of an invalid care allowance or if he or any partner satisfies that condition only by virtue of paragraph 13(3A)[^f00047]; | - (b)<br>  - (i) £31.25; |
> | - (ii) if no-one is in receipt of such an allowance. | - (ii) £62.50. |
> | (6) Disabled Child Premium. | (6) £16.65 in respect of each child or young person in respect of whom the conditions specified in paragraph 14 are satisfied. |
> | (7) Carer Premium[^f00048] | (7) £10.80 in respect of each person who satisfied the condition specified in paragraph 14ZA. |
