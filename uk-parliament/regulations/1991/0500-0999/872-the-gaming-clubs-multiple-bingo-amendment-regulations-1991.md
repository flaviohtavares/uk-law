# The Gaming Clubs (Multiple Bingo) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 872

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Multiple Bingo) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

28th March 1991

Laid before Parliament

9th April 1991

Coming into force

13th May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(1) of theGaming (Bingo) Act 1985[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for GreatBritain, I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Multiple Bingo)(Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 13th May1991.

**2.** In regulation 2 of the Gaming Clubs (Multiple Bingo) Regulations1986[^f00002], after the word“shall”, there shall be inserted the words“on a Saturday be two and in any other such period shall”.

Home Office

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

28th March 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Gaming Clubs (Multiple Bingo)Regulations 1986 to increase from one to two the number of games ofmultiple bingo that may be played on any licensed bingo club premises ona Saturday.
