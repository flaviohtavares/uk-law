# The Employment Protection Code of Practice (Time Off) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 968

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Employment Protection Code of Practice (Time Off) Order 1991

Made

9th April 1991

Laid before Parliament

18th April 1991

Coming into force

13th May 1991

Whereas—

(1) in pursuance of section 6(1), (2)(b) and (5) of the EmploymentProtection Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as“the Act”) the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service (hereinafterreferred to as“the Service”) issued a Code of Practice on time off for trade union duties andactivities which came into effect on 1st April 1978;

(2) section 6(10) of the Act provides that the Service may from time totime revise the whole or any part of a Code of Practice issued underthat section and issue the revised Code, and that section 6(3) to (9)shall apply (with appropriate modifications) in relation to such arevised Code;

(3) in pursuance of section 6(3) and (10) of the Act the Service hasrevised the Code of Practice on time off for trade union duties andactivities, has published a draft of the revised Code of Practice andhas considered representations made to it about the draft;

(4) in pursuance of section 6(4) and (10) of the Act the Service hastransmitted the draft revised Code of Practice to the Secretary ofState, who has laid it before both Houses of Parliament;

(5) in pursuance of section 6(5) and (10) of the Act the draft revisedCode of Practice has been approved by resolution of each House ofParliament and the Service is issuing the revised Code of Practice inthe form of the draft;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powerconferred on him by sections 6(5) and (10) and 123 of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Employment Protection Code ofPractice (Time Off) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 13th May1991.

## 2. Appointed day for revised Code of Practice

The revised Code of Practice on time off for trade union duties andactivities issued by the Service under section 6(5) and (10) of the Actshall come into effect on 13th May 1991.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

9th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 13th May 1991 as the day upon which the revisedCode of Practice on time off for trade union duties and activities whichis being issued by the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Serviceunder section 6 of the Employment Protection Act 1975 will come intoeffect.
