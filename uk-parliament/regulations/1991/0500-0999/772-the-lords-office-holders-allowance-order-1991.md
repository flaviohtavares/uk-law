# The Lords Office-holders Allowance Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 772

PARLIAMENT

The Lords Office-holders Allowance Order 1991

Made

20th March 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 20th day of March 1991

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 5(2) (b) and (3) of the Ministerial and other Pensions and Salaries Act 1991[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Lords Office-holders Allowance Order 1991.

## 2. Amount of allowance

The amount of the allowance payable under section 5 of the Ministerial and other Pensions and Salaries Act 1991 for the year beginning with 1st August 1990 and each subsequent year beginning with 1st August shall be equal to 220 times the maximum daily amount which, under any Resolution of the House of Lords, is recoverable in that year by a member of that House, other than one to whom that section applies, in respect of his expenses in staying overnight away from his main or only residence.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the way in which the allowance payable under section 5 of the Ministerial and other Pensions and Salaries Act 1991 is to be calculated for the year beginning 1st August 1990 and each subsequent year.
