# The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1874 (C. 51)

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

Made

15th August 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(5) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991.

**2.** Sections 1, 4, 5 and subsections (1), (2), (4) and (5) of section 6 of, and Schedule 1 to, the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 shall come into force on 22nd August 1991.

Michael Fallon

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Education and Science

15th August 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd August 1991 all the provisions of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 except section 2 (which relates to the making of pay and conditions orders), section 3 (which makes special provisions as to grantmaintained schools) and subsection (3) of section 6 and Schedule 2 (which repeal the Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act [1987 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/1) and related provisions of the Education Reform Act [1988 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/40)).
