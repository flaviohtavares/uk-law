# The Football (Offences) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1564 (C.41)

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Football (Offences) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made

9th July 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 6(2) of the Football (Offences) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Football (Offences) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

**2.** The Football (Offences) Act 1991 shall come into force on 10th August 1991.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

9th July 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Football (Offences) Act 1991 into force on 10th August 1991.
