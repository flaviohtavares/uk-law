# Children’s Homes Regulations 1991, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Access by guardians ad litem to records and register

(1) Each voluntary organisation, where they are not acting as an authorised person[^f00007], and every person carrying on a registered children’s home shall provide a guardian ad litem of a child—

- (a) such access as may be required to—
  - (i) records in so far as they relate to the child maintained in accordance with these Regulations; and
  - (ii) the information from such records held in whatever form (such as by means of computer).
- (b) such copies of the records as he may require.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: Section 42 of the Act as amended by paragraph 18 of Schedule 16 to the Court and Legal Services Act [1990 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/41) provides for guardians ad litem to have right of access to local authority and authorised persons' records. “Authorised person” is defined in section 31 of the Children Act 1989.
