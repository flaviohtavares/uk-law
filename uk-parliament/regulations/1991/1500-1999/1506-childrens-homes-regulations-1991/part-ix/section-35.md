# Children’s Homes Regulations 1991, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Revocation of Regulations

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Administration of Children’s Homes Regulations 1951[^f00012] ;
- (b) the Community Homes Regulations 1972[^f00013] ;
- (c) the Children’s Homes (Control and Discipline) Regulations 1990[^f00014] .
