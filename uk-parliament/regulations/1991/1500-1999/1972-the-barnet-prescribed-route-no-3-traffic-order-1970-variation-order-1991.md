# The Barnet (Prescribed Route) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1970 (Variation) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1972

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Barnet (Prescribed Route) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1970 (Variation) Order 1991

Made

27th August 1991

Coming into force

31st August 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department in accordance with section 7(6) of the said Act of 1984, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Barnet (Prescribed Route) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1970 (Variation) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 31st August 1991.

**2.** The Barnet (Prescribed Route) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1970[^f00002] shall be varied by omitting therefrom article 4 of that Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

S. Chatterjee

Director (Transport), London Regional Office of the

Department of Transport

27th August 1991
