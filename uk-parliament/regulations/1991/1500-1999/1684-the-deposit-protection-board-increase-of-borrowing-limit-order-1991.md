# The Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1684

BANKS AND BANKING

The Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) Order 1991

Made

24th July 1991

Laid before Parliament

25th July 1991

Coming into force

1st August 1991

The Treasury in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 64 of the Banking Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with the Deposit Protection Board, hereby order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st August 1991.

## 2. Increase of borrowing limit

The sum of £125 million is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 64(1) of the Banking Act 1987 (borrowing powers of the Deposit Protection Board).

## 3. Revocation of earlier instrument

The Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) (No. 2) Order 1990[^f00002]is hereby revoked.

Gregory Knight

Sydney Chapman

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury.

24th July 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a limit of £125 million for the purposes of the borrowing power of the Deposit Protection Board contained in section 64(1) of the Banking Act 1987. The limit replaces the limit of £50 million prescribed by the Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) (No. 2) Order 1990, which is revoked.
