# The Financial Services Act 1986 (Schedule 1 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Exemption) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1516

FINANCIAL SERVICES

The Financial Services Act 1986 (Schedule 1 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Exemption) Order 1991

Made

29th June 1991

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1991

Coming into force

1st August 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 2, 46 and 75(9) of the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Financial Services Act 1986 (Schedule 1 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Exemption) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st August 1991.

## 2. Amendment to Schedule 1 of Financial Services Act 1986

Paragraph 35(f)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution of the words “being arrangements which are” for the words “and which is”.

## 3. Exemption

The body known as “Scottish Enterprise” established by the Enterprise and New Towns (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00003] shall be an exempted person.

John Redwood

Minister of State

Department of Trade and Industry

29th June 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order makes a drafting amendment to paragraph 35(f) of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services Act 1986. It also makes Scottish Enterprise an exempted person for the purposes of the Financial Services Act 1986.
