# The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1628

DESIGNS

The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1991

Made

16th July 1991

Laid before Parliament

18th July 1991

Coming into force

12th August 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 36, 40 and 44(1) of the Registered Designs Act 1949[^f00001], of the power conferred upon him by the Department of Trade and Industry (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the Council on Tribunals pursuant to section 10(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00003] and with the consent of the Treasury pursuant to the said section 40, hereby makes the following Rules:–

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1991 and shall come into force on 12th August 1991.

(2) The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1990[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

**2.** These Rules shall be construed as one with the Registered Designs Rules 1989[^f00005].

**3.** The fees to be paid in respect of any matters arising under the Registered Designs Act 1949 shall be those specified in the Schedule to these Rules; and in any case where a form specified in the Schedule as the corresponding form in relation to any matter is required by the Registered Designs Rules 1989 to be used, that form or any alternative form accepted by the registrar under rule 4(2) of those Rules shall be accompanied by the fee, if any, specified in respect of that matter.

Reay

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Trade and Industry

15th July 1991

We consent to the making of these Rules.

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules revoke and replace the Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1990. As a result of rationalisation of the general fee structure, fees in respect of some matters have been abolished and in respect of other matters have been increased with effect from 12th August 1991. It is estimated that the total amount of fees collected per annum at the new levels will be 7½ per cent greater than the amount collected per annum at existing levels.
