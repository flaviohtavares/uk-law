# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1883 (C.52)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

LEGAL PROFESSION

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1991

Made

17th August 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1991.

**2.** Sections 4 and 112 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 shall come into force on 1st October 1991.

**3.** The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 14th October 1991.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 17th August 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1991 the provisions in sections 4 and 112 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 relating to costs. In addition, this Order brings into force, on 14th October 1991, provisions in the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 relating to the Children Act 1989 and to foreign lawyers registering with the Law Society for various purposes.
