# The Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1917

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1991

Made

22nd August 1991

Coming into force

2nd September 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd September 1991.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Hampshire Probation Committee shall be amended by–

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Alton and Petersfield; and
- (b) inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “Alton and Petersfield” and corresponding to this in column 3 “2”.

**3.** The members of the Hampshire Probation Committee appointed before 2nd September 1991 by the justices for the petty sessional divisions of Alton and Petersfield shall continue in office as if they had been appointed by the justices for the new petty sessional division of Alton and Petersfield.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

22nd August 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessional divisions of Alton and Petersfield into the new division of Alton and Petersfield effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Hampshire) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/1828](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1828)) which comes into force on 2nd September 1991.
