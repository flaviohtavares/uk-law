# The Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1519 (C.40)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

Made

2nd July 1991

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of powers conferred by section 15(2) of the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 3 of the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act 1991 (Disability Living Allowance Advisory Board)—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations expressed to come into force on 12th August 1991, is 3rd July 1991; and
- (b) for all other purposes, is 12th August 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Nicholas Scott

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

2nd July 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force of section 3 of the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act [1991 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/21), which relates to the Disability Living Allowance Advisory Board, on the following dates—

3rd July 1991 for the purpose only of authorising the making of Regulations; and

12th August 1991 for all other purposes.
