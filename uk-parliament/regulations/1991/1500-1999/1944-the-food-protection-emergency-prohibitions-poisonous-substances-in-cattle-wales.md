# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1944

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 1991

Made

29th August 1991

Laid before Parliament

30th August 1991

Coming into force

30th August 1991

Whereas the Secretary of State was of the opinion, as mentioned in section 1(1)(a) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], that there existed or might have existed circumstances which were likely to create a hazard to human health through human consumption of food;

And whereas the Secretary of State was of the opinion, as mentioned in section 1(1)(b) of the said Act, that in consequence food which was or might have been in the future in the area described in the Schedule to the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) Order 1991[^f00002] or which was derived or might have been in the future derived from cattle in that area was, or might have been, or might have become unsuitable for human consumption;

And whereas the Secretary of State is now of the opinion that no such circumstances exist which are likely to create a hazard to human health through human consumption of food and that in consequence such food is not and will not be or become unsuitable for human consumption;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) and (3) of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 30th August 1991.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) Order 1991 is hereby revoked.

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

29th August 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Poisonous Substances in Cattle) (Wales) Order 1991 which contained emergency prohibitions on various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food derived from cattle which might have ingested polychlorinated biphenyls or other poisonous substances.
