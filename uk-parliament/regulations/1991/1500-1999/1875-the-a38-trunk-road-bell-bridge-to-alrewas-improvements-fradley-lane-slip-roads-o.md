# The A38 Trunk Road (Bell Bridge to Alrewas Improvements) (Fradley Lane Slip Roads) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1875

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A38 Trunk Road (Bell Bridge to Alrewas Improvements) (Fradley Lane Slip Roads) Order 1991

Made

26th July 1991

Coming into force

6th September 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The new highways which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the routes described in the Schedule to this Order, which connect the A38 trunk road with other highways at the place stated in that Schedule (the highways along these routes being in this Order referred to as “the slip roads”), shall become trunk roads as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of each of the new trunk roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** In this Order, “the deposited plan” means the plan marked “The A38 Trunk Road (Bell Bridge to Alrewas Improvements) (Fradley Lane Slip Roads) Order 1991”, signed by authority of the Secretary of the State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.

**4.** This Order shall come into force on 6th September 1991 and may be cited as the A38 Trunk Road (Bell Bridge to Alrewas Improvements) (Fradley Lane Slip Roads) Order 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D R Ritchie

Regional Director, West Midlands Region,

Department of Transport

26th July 1991

## SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE SLIP ROADS

*The routes of the slip roads are the following:–*

*Junction with Northbound Carriageway of A38 at Fradley Lane*

Two routes to connect the northbound carriageway of the A38 Trunk Road with a new highway to be constructed by the Secretary of State pursuant to the A38 Trunk Road (Bell Bridge to Alrewas Improvements) Side Roads Order 1991, which new highway will link with Fradley Lane and Fine Lane, between Bell Bridge and Alrewas in the County of Staffordshire (the slip roads along these routes being given the reference numbers 1 and 2 on the deposited plan).
