# The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1528 (S. 141)

BUILDING AND BUILDINGS

The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1991

Made

1st July 1991

Laid before Parliament

15th July 1991

Coming into force

1st September 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 20, 24(1)(b) and 29(1) of the Building (Scotland) Act 1959[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st September 1991.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 1981[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule 2 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**3.** The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988[^f00003] and The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1990[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st July 1991
