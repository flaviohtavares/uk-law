# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1971

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991

Made

3rd September 1991

Laid before Parliament

10th September 1991

Coming into force

1st October 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54(1) and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st October 1991.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendments to regulation 2 (Interpretation)

–

(1) For the heading preceding regulation 2 there shall be substituted–

> General Interpretation

.

(2) Regulation 2(2) shall be omitted.

(3) After regulation 2, there shall be inserted–

> **2A. Interpretation of instruments referred to in Schedule 1**
>
> The instruments referred to in column 3 of Schedule 1 shall, for the purposes of these Regulations, have effect as if–
>
> - (a) a reference to a vehicle in category M1 were a reference to a vehicle described as M1 in Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) of 6th February 1970; and
> - (b) a reference to a vehicle in category L5 were a reference to a vehicle described as L5 in ECE Regulation 78 of 15th October 1988.

.

## 4. Amendments to type approval requirements in respect of brakes

In Schedule 1, for items 13E, 13F and 13G there shall be substituted the items numbered 13E, 13F and 13G set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984.

Regulation 3 introduces a new regulation 2A entitled “Interpretation of Instruments referred to in Schedule 1”.

Regulation 4 revises the type approval requirements relating to brakes so as to take account of amendments to both ECE Regulation 13 and Council Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320) and to introduce, as an alternative, the requirements of ECE Regulation 78 as amended on 28th November 1990 in relation to category L5 vehicles.

Copies of the ECE Regulations (and amendments) and of the EEC Directives (and amendments) referred to in these Regulations and in this note can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
