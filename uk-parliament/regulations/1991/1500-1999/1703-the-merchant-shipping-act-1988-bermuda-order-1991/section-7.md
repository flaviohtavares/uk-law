# The Merchant Shipping Act 1988 (Bermuda) Order 1991, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**7.** Any reference in this Order to the Merchant Shipping Acts shall be construed as a reference to those Acts as they extend to or apply in Bermuda, and shall include reference to the Bermuda Merchant Shipping Act 1979[^f00005], and any reference to a particular Merchant Shipping Act or to provisions thereof shall be construed as a reference to that Act or to those provisions as it extends or they extend to Bermuda as amended by the said Bermuda Merchant Shipping Act.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Act No. 16 of 1979 of Bermuda.
