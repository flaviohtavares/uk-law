# The Merchant Shipping Act 1988 (Bermuda) Order 1991, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** Subject to the modifications specified in Article 7 of and in Schedule 1 to this Order, Schedule 5 to the Merchant Shipping Act 1988 in so far as it relates to the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00002], and the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) Act 1971[^f00003] shall extend to Bermuda as part of the law thereof.
