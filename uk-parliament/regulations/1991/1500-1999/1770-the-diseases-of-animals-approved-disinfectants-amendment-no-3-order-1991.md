# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1770

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1991

Made

25th July 1991

Coming into force

16th August 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b) and (c) and (2), 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 16th August 1991.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order the “principal Order” means the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment of principal Order

(1) At the end of Schedule 1 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be added the entries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) The entries listed in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be deleted from Schedule 1 to the principal Order.

(3) For the provisions of Schedule 2 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.

(4) In article 6(2) of the principal Order (which specifies the date until which disinfectants subject to transitional provisions remain approved) for the words “30th June 1991” there shall be substituted the words “31st December 1991”.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 23rd July 1991.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

This Order further amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 by adding to the list of approved disinfectants in that Order those newly approved disinfectants which are mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Order (article 3(1)) and by deleting from that list the disinfectants mentioned in Schedule 2 (article 3(2)). The disinfectants deleted may, however, continue to be used as approved disinfectants until 31st December 1991 (article 3(3) and (4)).
