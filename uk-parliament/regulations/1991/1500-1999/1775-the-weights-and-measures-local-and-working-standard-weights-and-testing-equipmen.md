# The Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1775

WEIGHTS AND MEASURES

The Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

26th July 1991

Laid before Parliament

5th August 1991

Coming into force

2nd September 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 5(9) and 86(1) of the Weights and Measures Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd September 1991.

**2.** The Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) Regulations 1986([^f00002]) are hereby amended in Schedule 2 by substituting for Table 6 the following Table:

John Redwood

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

26th July 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2, Table 6 of the Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) Regulations 1986 in which are prescribed the limits of error for test weights used by inspectors of weights and measures. The amendments made prescribe the limits of error for weights of 56 lb down to 14 lb and of 20 kg down to 5 kg.
