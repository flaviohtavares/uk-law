# The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1700

EXTRADITION

The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991

Made

24th July 1991

Laid before Parliament

1st August 1991

Coming into force

22nd August 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of July 1991

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 5(1) of the Extradition Act 1989[^f00001] or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1991.

**2.** The countries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby designated for the purposes of section 1(2) of the Extradition Act 1989.

**3.** The Orders listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby revoked.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council
