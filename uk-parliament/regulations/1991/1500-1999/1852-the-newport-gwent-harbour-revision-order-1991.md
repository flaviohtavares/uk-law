# The Newport (Gwent) Harbour Revision Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1852

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Newport (Gwent) Harbour Revision Order 1991

Made

7th August 1991

Coming into force

8th August 1991

Whereas the making of this Order is not opposed;

Now therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport (being the appropriate Minister under section 14(7) of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001] for the purpose of making this Order), in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the said Act and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and on the application of the Newport (Gwent) Harbour Commissioners, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Newport (Gwent) Harbour Revision Order 1991 and shall come into force on 8th August 1991.

## 2. Repeal

The provisions of the Newport (Monmouthshire) Harbour Act 1836[^f00003] listed in the Schedule to this Order are hereby repealed.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

H. B. Wenban-Smith

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

7th August 1991

This Order provides for the repeal of certain sections of the Newport (Monmouthshire) Act 1836 mainly relating to the rates or duties or charges payable by users of Newport Harbour, including provisions which confer exemptions from rates or duties or charges on vessels returning to the harbour during the same voyage or making over thirty voyages within a specified 12 month period.

The Applicants for the Order are the Newport (Gwent) Harbour Commissioners.
