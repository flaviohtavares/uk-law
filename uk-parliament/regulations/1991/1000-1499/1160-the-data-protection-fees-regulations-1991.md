# The Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1160

DATA PROTECTION

The Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 1991

Made

9th May 1991

Laid before Parliament

10th May 1991

Coming into force

1st June 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 6(7), 8(5),40(7) and 41 of the Data Protection Act 1984[^f00001] and by the Data Protection Registration Fee Order 1991[^f00002] and after consultation with the Registrar and with theapproval of the Treasury in accordance with the said section 40(7), Ihereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Data Protection (Fees)Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st June 1991.

**2.** The fee payable under section 6(7) of the Data Protection Act 1984on an application for registration and under section 8(5) of that Act onan application for the renewal of registration shall be £75.

**3.** The Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 1988[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

8th May 1991

We approve of these Regulations

Sydney Chapman

Greg Knight

Two of the Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th May 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase to £75 from £56 the feewhich is to accompany an application to the Data Protection Registrarfor registration or for renewal of registration under the DataProtection Act 1984.
