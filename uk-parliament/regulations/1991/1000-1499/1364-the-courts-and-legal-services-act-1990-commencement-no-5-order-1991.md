# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1364 (C.37)

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

LEGAL SERVICES

LEGAL PROFESSION

NOTARIES PUBLIC

The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1991

Made

11th June 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1991.

**2.** The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st July 1991.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 11th June 1991
