# The Representation of the People Act 1990 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1244 (C.33)

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The Representation of the People Act 1990 (Commencement No. 1)Order 1991

Made

22nd May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 2(2) of theRepresentation of the People Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1990(Commencement No. 1) Order 1991.

**2.** The Representation of the People Act 1990 shall come into force inGreat Britain on 10th June 1991.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

22nd May 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the Representation of the PeopleAct 1990 into force in Great Britain on 10th June 1991.
