# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Telecommunications Network Limited) (Bromley) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1045

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Telecommunications Network Limited) (Bromley) Order 1991

Made

22nd April 1991

Laid before Parliament

25th April 1991

Coming into force

23rd May 1991

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to TelecommunicationsNetwork Limited on 7th February 1991 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of thetelecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the followingOrder:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication SystemDesignation (Telecommunications Network Limited) (Bromley) Order 1991and shall come into force on 23rd May 1991.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as apublic telecommunication system.

John Redwood

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

22nd April 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Telecommunications NetworkLimited on 7th February 1991 a licence under section 7 of theTelecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systemsspecified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the London Boroughof Bromley. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 25thApril 1991. This Order designates those telecommunication systems aspublic telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section9(3) of that Act, Telecommunications Network Limited will be a publictelecommunications operator in respect of its Bromley telecommunicationsystems when the Order comes into force on 23rd May 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office ofTelecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
