# The International Organisations (Tax Exempt Securities) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1202

INCOME TAX

TAXES

The International Organisations (Tax Exempt Securities) Order 1991

Made

16th May 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 126(1) of the Finance Act 1984[^f00001] and section 324(1) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00002], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the International Organisations (Tax Exempt Securities) Order 1991.

**2.** The following international organisation is designated for the purposes of section 126 of the Finance Act 1984 and section 324 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988–

- The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development

Thomas Sackville

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

16th May 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the limited exemptions from income tax, capital gains tax, inheritance tax, stamp duty and stamp duty reserve tax contained in section 324 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 and in section 126 of the Finance Act 1984 (as amended) to be given in respect of securities issued by the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, an international organisation of which the United Kingdom is a member. These exemptions are conferred in accordance with Article 53(9) and (10) of the Agreement establishing the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (Cmnd. 1116). The Agreement was signed by the United Kingdom in Paris on 29th May 1990 and entered into force for the United Kingdom on 28th March 1991.
