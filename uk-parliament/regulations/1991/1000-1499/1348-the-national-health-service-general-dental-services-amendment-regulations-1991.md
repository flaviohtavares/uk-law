# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1348

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment Regulations 1991

Made

9th June 1991

Laid before Parliament

10th June 1991

Coming into force

1st July 1991

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 35(1), 36(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Amendment Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st July 1991.

## 2. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1973

(1) The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1973[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs of this regulation.

(2) In paragraph 16 (2) of Schedule 1 (terms of service for dentists)—

- (a) after head (m) there is inserted the following head:—

  > - (mm) any treatment immediately necessary as a result of trauma;

  ;
- (b) in head (n) after “(f)” there is added “and (mm)”;
- (c) in head (o) for the words “and (k)” there are substituted the words “, (k) and (mm)”;
- (d) in head (p) after “(k)” there is added “and (mm)”; and
- (e) in head (q) for the words “and (m)” there are substituted the words “, (m) and (mm)”.

(3) In Part II of Schedule 1C (prior approval treatment)—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (course of care and treatment) for “£500” there is substituted “£600”; and
- (b) in paragraph 2 (orthodontic care and treatment) for “£500” there is substituted “£600”.

## 3. Application of these Regulations

The amendments made by regulation 2(3) of these Regulations apply only in relation to a course of care and treatment, or to any orthodontic care and treatment, commenced on or after 1st July 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Hooper

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

9th June 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1973 (“the principal Regulations”) which regulate the terms on which general dental services are provided under the National Health Service Act 1977.

Regulation 2(2) adds a further item to the list of items of treatment which a dentist can provide as occasional treatment.

Regulation 2(3) amends Schedule 1C to the principal Regulations to increase from £500 to £600 the maximum cost of a course of careand treatment or any orthodontic careand treatment which a dentist may provide without first seeking the prior approval of the Dental Practice Board. These amendments apply only in relation to careand treatment commenced on or after 1st July 1991 (regulation 3).
