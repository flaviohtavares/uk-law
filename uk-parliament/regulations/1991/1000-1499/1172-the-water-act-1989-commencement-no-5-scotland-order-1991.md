# The Water Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) (Scotland) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1172 (C. 30) (S. 116)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) (Scotland) Order 1991

Made

3rd May 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 194(4) of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Water Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) (Scotland) Order 1991.

## 2. Commencement of provisions on 31st May 1991

The following provisions of the Water Act 1989 shall come into force in Scotland on 31st May 1991:–

- Schedule 23, paragraph 4, so far as relating to section 33 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00002], and section

169 so far as it gives effect to that paragraph of that Schedule in relation to the said section 33.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd May 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 31st May 1991 the provision in Schedule 23 to the Water Act 1989 which substitutes section 33 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974. Section 33, which now extends to Scotland only, deals with control of sanitary appliances on vessels.
