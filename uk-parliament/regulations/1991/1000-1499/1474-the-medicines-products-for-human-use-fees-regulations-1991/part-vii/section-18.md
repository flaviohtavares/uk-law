# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1991, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Time for payment of periodic fees

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), all periodic fees shall be payable on the first day of the licence fee period to which they relate.

(2) Periodic fees payable in respect of the licence fee period beginning with the date of coming into force of these Regulations, shall be payable within 28 days of receipt of a written notice given by the licensing authority requiring payment of such fees.
