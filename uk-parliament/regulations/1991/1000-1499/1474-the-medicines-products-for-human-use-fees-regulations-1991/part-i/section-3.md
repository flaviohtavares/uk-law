# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1991, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Fees payable in connection with applications and inspections

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of a capital fee payable in connection with an application is that payable in accordance with these Regulations as in force when the application is made.

(2) The amount of a fee payable in respect of an inspection is that payable in accordance with these Regulations as in force when the inspection is made.
