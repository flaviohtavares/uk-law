# The Medicines (Products for Human Use — Fees) Regulations 1991, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Renewals of certain manufacturers' licences

(1) Subject to regulations 12 and 23, the fee payable in connection with an application for renewal of a manufacturer’s licence which is limited solely to the manufacture or assembly of medicinal products, the sale or supply of which do not require a product licence and to which Article 2(2)(i)(e) of the Medicines (Exemption from Licences) (Special and Transitional Cases) Order 1971[^f00005] applies shall be £80.

(2) In respect of any inspection made in connection with an application referred to in paragraph (1), the fee payable shall be that prescribed in paragraph 2(d) of Schedule 2.
