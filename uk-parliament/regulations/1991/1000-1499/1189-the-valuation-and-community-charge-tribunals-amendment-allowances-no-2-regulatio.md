# The Valuation and Community Charge Tribunals (Amendment) (Allowances) (No.2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1189

COMMUNITY CHARGES, ENGLAND AND WALES

RATING AND VALUATION

The Valuation and Community Charge Tribunals (Amendment)(Allowances) (No.2) Regulations 1991

Made

10th May 1991

Laid before Parliament

21st May 1991

Coming into force

11th June 1991

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, andthe Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of thepowers conferred on them by section 146(6) of, and paragraphs 1 and5(1)(p) of Schedule 11 to, the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf,hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation and Community ChargeTribunals (Amendment) (Allowances) (No.2) Regulations 1991 and shallcome into force on 11th June 1991 (“the operative date”).

**2.** Each provision of Schedule 2 to the Valuation and Community ChargeTribunals Regulations 1989[^f00002] mentioned in the Table below shall be amended inrelation to approved duties (within the meaning of that Schedule)performed on or after the operative date by substituting, for the amountmentioned in the second column of the Table (“the original amount”), the amount mentioned in the third column in relation to thatprovision, wherever in that provision the original amount is mentioned.

**TABLE**

| *Provision* | *Original Amount* | *Substituted Amount* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paragraph 1, Table A | £17.65 | £19.25 |
|  | £35.30 | £38.50 |
| Paragraph 5 | 27.3p | 32.1p |

Michael Heseltine

Secretary of State for the Environment

9th May 1991

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

10th May 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the amounts of financial loss allowanceand certain travelling allowances prescribed in relation to theperformance of approved duties by members of valuation and communitycharge tribunals.
