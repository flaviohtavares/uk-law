# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Large Goods and Passenger-Carrying Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1122

ROAD TRAFFIC

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Large Goods andPassenger-Carrying Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

Made

1st May 1991

Laid before Parliament

9th May 1991

Coming into force

3rd June 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powersconferred by sections 89(3) and (4)(b), 105(1) and (4) and 108(1)[^f00001]of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and afterconsultation with representative organisations in accordance withsection 195(2) of that Act, and with the approval of the Treasury,hereby makes the following Regulations.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (DrivingLicences) (Large Goods and Passenger-Carrying Vehicles) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 3rd June 1991.

**2.** (1) The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Large Goods andPassenger-Carrying Vehicles) Regulations 1990[^f00003]shall be amended in accordance with the followingprovision of this regulation.

(2) In regulation 15 (Applications for tests) for paragraph (2) thereshall be substituted the following paragraph—

> (2) An applicant for such an appointment shall, when making theapplication, specify the category of vehicle in respect of which hedesires to take the test and pay—
>
> - (a) £45 if the application for the test is made before 3rd June1991;
> - (b) £48 if the application is made on or after that date and thetest is to be conducted on a day other than a Saturday;
> - (c) £75 if the application is made on or after that dateand thetest is to be conducted on a Saturday.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

We approve the making of these Regulations

Thomas Sackville

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

1st May 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations

These Regulations amend regulation 15 of the Motor Vehicles(Driving Licences) (Large Goods and Passenger-Carrying Vehicles)Regulations 1990 so as to—

- (a) increase the fee payable for a test to drive a large goods orpassenger-carrying vehicle, and
- (b) introduce a premium fee for a driving test taken on a Saturday.

The fee for a test to drive an LGV or PCV which is taken on aweek-day is increased from £45 to £48. The fee for the sametest, if taken on a Saturday, is £75.
