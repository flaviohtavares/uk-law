# The Pilotage Act 1987 (Abolition of Pilotage Commission: Appointed Day) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1028

PILOTAGE

The Pilotage Act 1987 (Abolition of Pilotage Commission: AppointedDay) Order 1991

Made

18th April 1991

Laid before Parliament

23rd April 1991

Coming into force

29th April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 26(1) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf,hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Pilotage Act 1987 (Abolition ofPilotage Commission: Appointed Day) Order 1991 and shall come into forceon 29th April 1991.

**2.** The day appointed for the Pilotage Commission ceasing to exist is30th April 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

18th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 30th April 1991 as the day on which the Pilotage Commission ceases to exist.
