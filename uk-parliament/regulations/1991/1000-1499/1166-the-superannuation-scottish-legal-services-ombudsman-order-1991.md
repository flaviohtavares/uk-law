# The Superannuation (Scottish Legal Services Ombudsman) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1166

PENSIONS

The Superannuation (Scottish Legal Services Ombudsman) Order 1991

Made

9th May 1991

Laid before Parliament

10th May 1991

Coming into force

1st June 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) ofthe Superannuation Act 1972[^f00001]and now vested in them[^f00002], hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Scottish LegalServices Ombudsman) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st June1991.

**2.** The office of the Scottish legal services ombudsman is hereby addedto the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972.

**3.** Employment by the Scottish legal services ombudsman is hereby addedto the employments listed in that Schedule.

Thomas Sackville

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th May 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the office of the Scottish legal services ombudsmanand the employment of his staff to the offices and employments listed inSchedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 so that the Principal CivilService Pension Scheme and the Civil Service Additional VoluntaryContribution Scheme made under section 1 of that Act may apply to them.Provision for the appointment of the Scottish legal services ombudsmanand his staff was made by section 34 of the Law Reform (MiscellaneousProvisions) (Scotland) Act [1990 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/40) and Schedule 3 thereto.
