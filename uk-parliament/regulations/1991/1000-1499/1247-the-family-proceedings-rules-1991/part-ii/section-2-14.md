# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 2.14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.14. Filing and amendment of pleadings after directions for trial

No pleading shall be filed or amended without leave after directions for trial have been given.
