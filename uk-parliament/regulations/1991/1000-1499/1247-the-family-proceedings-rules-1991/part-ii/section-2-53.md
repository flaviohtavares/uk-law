# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 2.53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.53. Application by petitioner or respondent for ancillary relief

(1) Any application by a petitioner, or by a respondent who files an answer claiming relief, for—

- (a) an order for maintenance pending suit,
- (b) a financial provision order,
- (c) a property adjustment order,

shall be made in the petition or answer, as the case may be.

(2) Notwithstanding anything in paragraph (1), an application for ancillary relief which should have been made in the petition or answer may be made subsequently—

- (a) by leave of the court, either by notice in Form M11 or at the trial, or
- (b) where the parties are agreed upon the terms of the proposed order, without leave by notice in Form M11.

(3) An application by a petitioner or respondent for ancillary relief, not being an application which is required to be made in the petition or answer, shall be made by notice in Form M11.
