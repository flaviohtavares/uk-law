# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 2.55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.55. Application in Form Mll or M12

Where an application for ancillary relief is made by notice in Form M11 or an application under rule 2.45 is made by notice in Form M12 the notice shall be filed—

- (a) if the case is pending in a divorce county court, in that court, or
- (b) if the cause is pending in the High Court, in the registry in which it is proceeding,

and within four days after filing the notice the applicant shall serve a copy on the respondent to the application.
