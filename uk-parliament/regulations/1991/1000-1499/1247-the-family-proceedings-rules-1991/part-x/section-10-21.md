# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 10.21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.21. Disclosure of addresses

(1) Nothing in these rules shall be construed as requiring any party to reveal the address of their private residence (or that of any child) save by order of the court.

(2) Where a party declines to reveal an address in reliance upon paragraph (1) above, he shall give notice of that address to the court 'n Form CHA59 and that address shall not be revealed to any person save by order of the court.
