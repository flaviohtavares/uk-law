# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 10.18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.18. No notice of intention to proceed after year’s delay

RSC Order 3, rule 6 (which requires a party to give notice of intention to proceed after a year’s delay) shall not apply to any proceedings pending in the High Court.
