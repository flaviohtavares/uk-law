# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 10.19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.19. Filing of documents at place of hearing etc.

Where the file of any family proceedings has been sent from one divorce county court or registry to another for the purpose of a hearing or for some other purpose, any document needed for that purpose and required to be filed shall be filed in the other court or registry.
