# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 10.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.16. Copies of decrees and orders

(1) A copy of every decree shall be sent by the proper officer to every party to the cause.

(2) A sealed or other copy of a decree or order made in open court shall be issued to any person requiring it on payment of the prescribed fee.
