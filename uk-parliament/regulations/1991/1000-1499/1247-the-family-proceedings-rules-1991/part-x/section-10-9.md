# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 10.9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.9. Mode of making applications

Except where these rules, or any rules applied by these rules, otherwise provide, every application in family proceedings—

- (a) shall be made to a district judge;
- (b) shall, if the proceedings are pending in the High Court, be made by summons or, if the proceedings are pending in a divorce county court, be made in accordance with CCR Order 13, rule I (which deals with applications in the course of proceedings).
