# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 4.24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.24. Notification of consent

Consent for the purposes of–

- (a) section 16(3),
- (b) section 33(7), or
- (c) paragraph 19(3)(c) or (d) of Schedule 2,

shall be given either–

- (i) orally in court, or
- (ii) in writing to the court signed by the person giving his consent.
