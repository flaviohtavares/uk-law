# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 4.20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.20. Oral evidence

The court or the proper officer shall keep a note of the substance of the oral evidence given at a hearing of, or directions appointment in, proceedings to which this Part applies.
