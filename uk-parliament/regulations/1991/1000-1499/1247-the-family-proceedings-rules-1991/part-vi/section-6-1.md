# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 6.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.1. Interpretation

In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires–

- (a) “the Act” means the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00045] and words or expressions bear the same meaning as in that Act;
- (b) “the Hague Convention” means the convention defined in section 1(1) of the Act and “**the European Convention**” means the convention defined in section 12(1) of the Act.
