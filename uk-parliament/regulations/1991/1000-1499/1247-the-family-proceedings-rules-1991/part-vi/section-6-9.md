# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 6.9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.9. Dispensing with service

The court may dispense with service of any summons (whether originating or ordinary) in any proceedings under the Act.
