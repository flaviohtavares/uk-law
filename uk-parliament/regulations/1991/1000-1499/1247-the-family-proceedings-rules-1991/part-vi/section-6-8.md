# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 6.8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.8. Hearing

Any application under the Act (other than an application (a) to join a defendant, (b) to dispense with service or extend the time for acknowledging service, or (c) for the transfer of proceedings) shall be heard and determined by a judge and shall be dealt with in chambers unless the court otherwise directs.
