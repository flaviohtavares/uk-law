# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 6.13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.13. Interim directions

An application for interim directions under section 5 or section 19 of the Act may where the case is one of urgency be made ex parte on affidavit but shall otherwise be made by summons.
