# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 6.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.10. Adjournment of summons

The hearing of the originating summons under which an application under the Hague Convention or the European Convention is made may be adjourned for a period not exceeding 21 days at any one time.
