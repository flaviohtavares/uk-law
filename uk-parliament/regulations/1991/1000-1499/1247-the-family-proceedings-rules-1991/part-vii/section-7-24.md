# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.24. Registration in a magistrates' court of an order registered in the High Court

On receipt of notice that a maintenance order registered in the High Court in accordance with section 17(4) of the Act of 1950 has been registered in a magistrates' court in accordance with section 2(5) of the Act of 1958, the proper officer shall cause particulars of the registration to be entered in Part II of the register.
