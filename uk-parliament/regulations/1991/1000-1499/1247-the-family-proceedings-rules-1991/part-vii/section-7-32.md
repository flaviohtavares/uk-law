# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.32. Certification of evidence given on provisional order

Where the court makes a provisional order under section 5 of the Act of 1972[^f00071], the document required by subsection (4) of that section to set out or summarise the evidence given in the proceedings shall be authenticated by a certificate signed by the district judge.

## Annotations

[^f00071]: Footnote: Section 5 was amended by section 54 of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act [1978 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/22).
