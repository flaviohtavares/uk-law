# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.22. Application and interpretation of rules 7.22 to 7.29

Section 21 of the Act of 1958[^f00066] shall apply to the interpretation of this rule and rules 7.23 to 7.29 as it applies to the interpretation of that Act; and in those rules—

- “**cause book**” includes cause card; and
- “**the register**” means any register kept for the purposes of the Act of 1958.
