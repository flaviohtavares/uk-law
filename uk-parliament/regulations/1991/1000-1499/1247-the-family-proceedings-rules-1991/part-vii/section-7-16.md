# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.16. Interpretation

In this chapter—

- “the Act of 1920” means the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Act 1920[^f00056];
- “the Act of 1950” means the Maintenance Orders Act 1950[^f00057];
- “the Act of 1958” means the Maintenance Orders Act 1958[^f00058];
- “the Act of 1965” means the Matrimonial Causes Act 1965[^f00059];
- “the Act of 1971” means the Attachment of Earnings Act 1971[^f00060];
- “the Act of 1972” means the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972[^f00061];
- “**English maintenance order**” means a maintenance order made in the High Court.
