# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.12. Interim directions

(1) An application for interim directions under section 29 of the Act of 1986 may be heard and determined by a district judge.

(2) The parties to the proceedings for enforcement and, if he is not a party thereto, the applicant for the Part I order, shall be made parties to the application.
