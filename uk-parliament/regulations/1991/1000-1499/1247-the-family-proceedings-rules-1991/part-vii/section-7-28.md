# The Family Proceedings Rules 1991, section 7.28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.28. Appeal from variation etc of order by magistrates' court

An appeal to the High Court under section 4(7) of the Act of 1958[^f00067] shall be heard and determined by a Divisional Court of the Family Division, and rule 8.2 shall apply as it applies in relation to an appeal from a magistrates' court under the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978[^f00068].

## Annotations

[^f00067]: Footnote: Section 4(7) was amended by the Administration of Justice Act [1970 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/31), section 32 and Schedule 5, Part IV.
