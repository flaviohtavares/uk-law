# The Merchant Shipping (Categorisation of Registries of Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1469

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Categorisation of Registries of Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 1991

Made

26th June 1991

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1991

Coming into force

25th July 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of June 1991

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 11 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Categorisation of Registries of Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 25th July 1991.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Categorisation of Registries of Overseas Territories) Order 1991[^f00002] is hereby amended by inserting in the Schedule “Cayman Islands” after “Bermuda” in column 4 of category 1 (Unlimited tonnage and type).

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds Cayman Islands to the category of overseas territories in which there is no restriction as to the ships which may be registered there.
