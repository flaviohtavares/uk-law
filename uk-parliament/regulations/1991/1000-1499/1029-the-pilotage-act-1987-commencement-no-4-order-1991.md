# The Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 1029 (C. 26)

PILOTAGE

The Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1991

Made

18th April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powersconferred upon him by section 33(2) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] (“the Act”), hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No.4) Order 1991.

**2.** The provisions of the Act not already in force[^f00002]shall come into force on 30th April 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeeman

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

18th April 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th April 1991 the remaining provisions of the Pilotage Act 1987 — namely the repeal of provisions in the Pilotage Act [1983 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/21) which related to the continued existence of the Pilotage Commission pending its abolition (the date for which has now been appointed for April 30th 1991, the date also appointed by this Order).

The Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1306](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1306)), the Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/2138](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2138))and the Pilotage Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/1137](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1137)) brought into force all other provisions of the Act.
