# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (89).** Text as at 2001-04-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1991/2887
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Declaration by claimant[^key-c7f13ecdd0f2440f7836e03cc76b80fa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

On an initial claim, a declaration by the claimant that he has a physical or mental disability which puts him at a disadvantage in getting a job is not conclusive that for the purposes of section 20(6A)(b) of the Act he has a disability, where—

- (a) the claim itself contains contrary indications, or
- (b) the appropriate officer[^key-2eb9da86240f2f7897ce92b0929e35ba] has before him other evidence which contradicts that declaration or[^key-0fa409bb56adca2991ae7cb33228fa65]
- (c) the claim relates to section 129(2C) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (fast-track procedure).
