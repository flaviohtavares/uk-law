## SCHEDULE 1 — DISABILITY WHICH PUTS A PERSON AT A DISADVANTAGE IN GETTING A JOB

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 3

### PART I

**1.**[^c-7e9027bccf6d34f4443f89b0366f6975] When standing he cannot keep his balance unless he continually holds onto something.

**2.**[^c-e86e93ac864667ad1b0db6c0458ddf46] Using any crutches, walking frame, walking stick, prosthesis or similar walking aid which he habitually uses, he cannot walk a continuous distance of 100 metres along level ground without stopping or without suffering severe pain.

**3.**[^c-3ab2ce92c323aa6207c189b3c727633a] He can use neither of his hands behind his back as in the process of putting on a jacket or of tucking a shirt into trousers.

**4.**[^c-5d63dfd60c98e6e2c892abde3f44fcd9] He can extend neither of his arms in front of him so as to shake hands with another person without difficulty.

**5.**[^c-8296cfcc0ca781ce594e64995f2e1181] He can put neither of his hands up to his head without difficulty so as to put on a hat.

**6.**[^c-687659542d81619d033d5006e29a34b8] Due to lack of manual dexterity he cannot , with one hand, pick up[^c-bf94bba7c771c616315adf332fd348cc] a coin which is not more than 2P centimetres in diameter.

**7.**[^c-ac25825ed6f9f708cc8756e564fa9775] He is not able to use his hands or arms to pick up a full jug of 1 litre capacity and pour from it into a cup, without difficulty.

**8.**[^c-f2b7f27aa3c31c14dbd2b08b53a0a27e] He can turn neither of his hands sideways through 180°.

**9.**[^c-9e336f3a3607945ef6afd6e335a61c01] He is registered as blind or registered as partially sighted in a register compiled by a local authority under section 29(4)(g) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00078] (welfare services) or, in Scotland, has been certified as blind or as partially sighted and in consequence registered as blind or partially sighted in a register maintained by or on behalf of a regional or island council.

**10.**[^c-f6015c319d2176b7f06e4d990e6197d5] He cannot see to read 16 point print at a distance greater than 20 centimetres, if appropriate, wearing the glasses he normally uses.

**11.**[^c-d680fc5800c56d95854d0335e6e75ad9] He cannot hear a telephone ring when he is in the same room as the telephone, if appropriate, using a hearing aid he normally uses.

**12.**[^c-949eabe11d160ec7d8d44dd3cb613f4a] In a quiet room he has difficulty in hearing what someone talking in a loud voice at a distance of 2 metres says, if appropriate, using a hearing aid he normally uses.

**13.**[^c-e89fea974b3abb5eb0be9e2ff416b18e] People who know him well have difficulty in understanding what he says.

**14.**[^c-4c58813f1054c9202ae5d2992ff1c2cb] When a person he knows well speaks to him, he has difficulty in understanding what that person says.

**15.**[^c-de9dc5153003f1497d474b52aa65fb03] At least once a year during waking hours he is in a coma or has a fit in which he loses consciousness.

**16.**[^c-66f9a5c68fed2a9e8b93aab13eb517d8] He has a mental illness for which he receives regular treatment under the supervision of a medically qualified person.

**17.**[^c-16dba21e3e29d50feb636d35d3298f3b] Due to mental disability he is often confused or forgetful.

**18.**[^c-e64cff1b37840464ca889777767147f1] He cannot do the simplest addition and subtraction.

**19.**[^c-3f42f8e008afa18bcdf61efb711f1f96] Due to mental disability he strikes people or damages property or is unable to form normal social relationships.

**20.**[^c-317fffad814878300baaaac615d83690] He cannot normally sustain an 8 hour working day or a 5 day working week due to a medical condition or intermittent or continuous severe pain.

### PART II

**21.**[^c-204abc1194e83fc9aa45a34fc561257f] Subject to paragraph 24, there is payable to him—

- (a) the highest or middle rate of the care component of disability living allowance,
- (b) the higher rate of the mobility component of disability living allowance,
- (c) an attendance allowance under section 35 of the Social Security Act,
- (d) disablement benefit where the extent of the disablement is assessed at not less than 80 per cent. in accordance with section 57 of and Schedule 8 to the Social Security Act,
- (e) a war pension in respect of which the degree of disablement is certified at not less than 80 per cent.; and for the purposes of this sub-paragraph “war pension” means a war pension in accordance with section 25(4) of the Social Security Act 1989[^f00079] ,
- (f) mobility supplement, or
- (g) a benefit corresponding to a benefit mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a)—(f), under any enactment having effect in Northern Ireland.

**22.**[^c-a4b703fe78693184b7cab6d77ce568e3] Subject to paragraph 24, for one or more of the 182[^c-215b94476563bf0c16d26ec4779a1ec2] days immediately preceding the date when the initial claim for disabled person’s tax credit[^c-4e17939b3fcd16d830fe255a27526035] was made or treated as made, there was payable to him severe disablement allowance or a corresponding benefit under any enactment having effect in Northern Ireland.

**23.**[^c-ad4c573eb5d223b25b3580015badc82a] Subject to paragraph 24, he has an invalid carriage or other vehicle provided by the Secretary of State under section 5(2)(a) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00080] and Schedule 2 to that Act or under section 46 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00081] or provided under Article 30(1) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00082] .

**24.**[^c-62a15dd9d83c7f4b138c8c302d27e333] Paragraphs 21—23 are subject to the condition that no evidence is before the appropriate officer[^c-907ce125f704c430d74ee32237e76703] which gives him reasonable grounds for believing that in respect of an initial claim, none of the paragraphs in Part I or Part III of this Schedule apply to the claimant and in respect of a repeat claim, none of the paragraphs in Part I apply to the claimant.

### PART III

**25.**[^c-675dc018eb04f7155cf6e8dfb926afc7] As a result of an illness or accident he is undergoing a period of habilitation or rehabilitation.
