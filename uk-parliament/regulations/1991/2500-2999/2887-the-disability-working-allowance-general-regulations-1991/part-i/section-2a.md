# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 2A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (89).** Text as at 2001-04-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1991/2887
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2A. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Administration Act[^key-9de4590ddf2052e31c37190e2e89dec7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act (requirement to state national insurance number) shall not apply—

- (a) to a child or young person in respect of whom disabled person’s tax credit[^key-ac4c0f9bfbb4ca0bd1969f35a71340cf] is claimed;
- (b) to any claim for disabled person’s tax credit made or treated as made before 9th February 1998;
- (c) to a partner in respect of whom a claim for disabled person’s tax credit is made or treated as made before 5th October 1998.
