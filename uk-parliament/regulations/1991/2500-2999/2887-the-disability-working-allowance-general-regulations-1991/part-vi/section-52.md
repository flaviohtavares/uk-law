# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2001-11-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1991/2887
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Applicable amount[^c-e3d9b738e6713cc17392be73da080325][^c-5de1581e9332246cd37552002bdfe1d1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The applicable amount for the purposes of section 20(6A) of the Act (conditions of entitlement to disabled person’s tax credit[^c-5d4d1ff000c4df853add6e3ca0f7d3d9]) shall, in the case of a claimant who is—

- (a) single, be £72.25[^c-6a9e4d97a899ae7a9e1bf9e449f0bf73] per week;
- (b) a member of a married or unmarried couple, or a lone parent, be £92.90[^c-c62587bfb8d9de18574ad60d0ef111e9] per week.

(2) For the purposes of section 20(6D) of the Act[^f00075] (date on which applicable amount is to be determined) the prescribed date is the date on which the period under section 20(6F) of the Act (period of award) begins.
