# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 53

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Entitlement to disability working allowance where income exceeds the applicable amount[^c-b389d8aaebe9f97beba51de99f5bde2f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The prescribed percentage for the purpose of section 21(3B) of the Act[^f00076] (percentage of excess of income over applicable amount which is deducted from maximum disabled person’s tax credit[^c-cca8b7079a8904db62e055364031bbb6]) shall be 55[^c-e754fc3a6334f9a2a9b87c2a268d8dbc] per cent..
