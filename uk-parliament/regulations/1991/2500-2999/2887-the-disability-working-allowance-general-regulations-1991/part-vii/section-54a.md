# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 54A

## 54A. Surrendering an award following birth or adoption etc. of child[^c-1437d94b20127a73c5d74eb16b8eb2da]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An existing award of disabled person’s tax credit shall cease to have effect if the claimant or partner elects, by notice to the Board, to surrender it following the birth of a child, the adoption of a child or young person or the granting of a parental order for a surrogate child.

(2) The award shall terminate with effect from—

- (a) the day on which the notice is given to the Board, if that day is a Monday, or
- (b) the Monday following the day on which the notice is given to the Board, if that day is other than a Monday.
