# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2001-08-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1991/2887
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Disregard of contribution[^c-c120827177baa7c5171b02f01a32c0f3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the claimant or his partner is a student and , for the purposes of assessing a contribution to the student’s grant[^c-48831bfdc9279f29dd5434b5cd4ab9b4] or student loan[^c-0b8f3e01382b1d1d0de353aad06cee82], the other partner’s income has been taken into account, an amount equal to that contribution shall be disregarded for the purposes of assessing that other partner’s income.
