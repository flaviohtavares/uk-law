# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991, section 57

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Prescribed circumstances for entitlement to disability working allowance[^c-bc5e1ca5011d19f2967767fd4174a81b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of section 20(6A)(d) of the Act (prescribed circumstances) where a claimant or a member of his family is entitled to working families' tax credit[^c-1b5c5fee7030bf74917cf46571d72bc6], he is entitled to disability working allowance, if—

- (a) at the date of the claim for disabled person’s tax credit[^c-166dec337e47e9790e172636849442bc] the award of working families' tax credit for him or a member of his family will expire within 28 days; and
- (b) the claimant is or would be otherwise entitled to disabled person’s tax credit by virtue of these Regulations; and
- (c) the claim for disabled person’s tax credit is made in respect of a period which commences immediately after the expiry of the award of working families' tax credit.
