# The Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2685

HOUSING, ENGLAND ANDWALES

The Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1991

Made

28th November 1991

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1991

Coming into force

30th December 1991

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 104(2A), 111(a), 112 and 114(1)([^f00001]) of the Housing Act 1988([^f00002]) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 30th December 1991.

**2.** In the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) Regulations 1990[^f00003], in regulation 3(3) (payment by instalments) for “11.95 per cent.” there is substituted “11.50 per cent.”.

**3.** Regulation 2 above does not apply in respect of any period before 30th December 1991 notwithstanding that interest included in the amount of an instalment is paid after 29th December 1991.

Michael Heseltine

Secretary of State for the Environment

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Part IV (that is, sections 93 to 114) of the Housing Act 1988 (Change of Landlord: secure tenants) confers the right to acquire the fee simple estate in buildings and other property from a public sector landlord. Where, in the exercise of that right, it is determined that the property to which the acquisition relates would not realise any price, section 99(3) of that Act provides that there is a disposal cost attributable to that property. Section 104(2A) provides for such a disposal cost to be paid by instalments, which may include interest, in prescribed cases.

Regulation 3 of the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) Regulations 1990 prescribes the cases in which a disposal cost may be paid by instalments and makes provision as to payment. That regulation, as amended by S.I. [1990/2219](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2219) and S.I. [1991/1899](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1899), provides that the amount of each instalment shall include interest at 11.95 per cent. on the outstanding amount.

Regulation 2 of these Regulations decreases the rate of interest to 11.50 per cent., and regulation 3 provides that the new rate of interest does not apply in respect of any period before 30th December 1991.
