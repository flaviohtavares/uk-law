# The Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) (Variation) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2565

SEA FISHERIES

CONSERVATION OF SEA FISH

The Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) (Variation) Order 1991

Made

11th November 1991

Laid before Parliament

18th November 1991

Coming into force

9th December 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(1), (2) and (3), 15(3), 20(1) and 22(2)(a) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation with the Secretary of State for Transport, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) (Variation) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 9th December 1991.

## 2. Variation of the Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) Order 1990

The Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) Order 1990[^f00002] shall be varied–

- (a) by omitting paragraph (g) in article 4 thereof (exceptions from prohibition on landing);
- (b) by omitting Schedule 2 thereto (statistical divisions of ICES VII and descriptions of sea fish caught therein to which exception from landing prohibition applies).

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 4th November 1991.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Sea Fishing (Specified Western Waters) (Restrictions on Landing) Order 1990 “the principal Order” which prohibits, with exceptions, the landing in the United Kingdom of any sea fish (other than salmon or migratory trout) caught in ICES sub-area VII or VIII.

This Order removes the provision in the principal Order which permitted the landing of a quantity of one or more of the descriptions of sea fish referred to in an entry in Schedule 2 to that Order caught in specified areas otherwise than under the authority of a licence as a by-catch not exceeding 10 per cent. by weight of the landed catch in the course of lawful fishing for another description of sea fish (article 2).
