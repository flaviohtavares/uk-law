# The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No.2605

MEDICINES

The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 1991

Made

15th November 1991

Laid before Parliament

22nd November 1991

Coming into force

1st January 1992

The Secretaries of State respectively concerned with health in England, in Wales and in Scotland and the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, acting jointly as the Health Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by sections 1(1)(a), 75(1), 76(1), (2) and (6), 129(5) and 132(1) of the Medicines Act 1968[^f00001] and vested in them[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting, pursuant to section 129(6) of that Act, such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by these Regulations, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations, which may be cited as the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 1991, shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

## 2. Amendment of Regulation 3 of the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973

Regulation 3 of the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:–

- (a) in paragraph (1) (fee for registration of premises), for “£104” there shall be substituted “£113” and for “£54” (fee where premises are in Northern Ireland) there shall be substituted “£58”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) (retention fee), for “£67” there shall be substituted “£73” and for “£49” (fee where premises are in Northern Ireland) there shall be substituted “£53”; and
- (c) in paragraph (3) (additional sum by way of penalty), for “£215” there shall be substituted “£233” and for “£154” (sum where premises are in Northern Ireland) there shall be substituted “£166”.

## 3. Revocation

The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees)Amendment Regulations 1990[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973“the principal regulations”. They increase the fee for registration of premises at which a retail pharmacy business is, or is to be, carried on, the subsequent annual fees (retention fees) and the penalty (payable in circumstances specified in section 76(2) of the Medicines Act 1968) for failure to pay retention fees.

These Regulations also revoke the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 1990 which increased the fees in the principal regulations, and whose effect is spent on the coming into force of these Regulations.
