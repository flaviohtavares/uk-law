# The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2500 (C.73)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

Made

4th November 1991

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 8(2) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991.

(2) This Order shall extend to Northern Ireland only.

**2.** Section 8(6) and (7) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 shall come into force on 11th November 1991.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

4th November 1991

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in Northern Ireland on 11th November 1991 section 8(6) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (which provides for an Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act [1974 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/28) made only for purposes corresponding to those of the 1991 Act to be subject to annulment by resolution of either House instead of affirmative procedure).
