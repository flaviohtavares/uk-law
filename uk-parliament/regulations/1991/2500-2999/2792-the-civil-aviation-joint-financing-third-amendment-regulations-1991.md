# The Civil Aviation (Joint Financing) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2792

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Joint Financing) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made

10th December 1991

Laid before Parliament

11th December 1991

Coming into force

1st January 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers under section 73(1)(a) and (6)(a) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Joint Financing) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

**2.** Regulation 4(1) of the Civil Aviation (Joint Financing) Regulations 1988[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting respectively for the sums £25.45, £7.48 and £17.97 the sums £28.96, £8.72 and £20.24.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Patrick McLoughlin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

4th December 1991

We consent to the makingof these Regulations.

Gregory Knight

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

10th December 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Joint Financing) Regulations 1988, as follows—

The charge payable by operators of aircraft to the Civil Aviation Authority in respect of crossings between Europe and North America is increased in consequence of a decisionof the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation. The element of the charge payable in respect of air navigation services provided by the Government of Denmark is increased from £7.48 to £8.72. The element of the charge payable in respect of air navigation services provided by the Government of Iceland is increased from £17.97 to £20.24. The total charge is thus increased from £25.45 to £28.96.

The charges are payable in pursuance of the Agreements on the Joint Financing of certain Air Navigation Services respectively in Greenland and the Faroe Islands and in Iceland, opened for signature in Geneva on 25th September 1956 (Cmnd. Nos. 677 and 678) as amended by the Protocols opened for signature at Montreal on 3rd November 1982 (Cmnd. Nos. 8844 and 8845).

The charges are required by the principal Regulations to be remitted to the Governments of Denmark and Iceland subject to the deduction of a fee not exceeding 5 per cent. for the CAA’s expenses in billing and collection.
