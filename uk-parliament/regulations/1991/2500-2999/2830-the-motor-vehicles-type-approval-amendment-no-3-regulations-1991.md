# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2830

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991

Made

13th December 1991

Laid before Parliament

16th December 1991

Coming into force

1st January 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

**2.** (1) Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980[^f00003] is hereby amended as follows.

(2) In column 2, in item 11A, there is added–

> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | or 88/77/EEC as amended by 91/542/EEC | 1 October 1991 | O.J. L295, 25.10.91, p. 1 |

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

13th December 1991

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regula tions 1980 so that the type approval requirements relating to the measures to be taken against the emission of gaseous and particulate pollutants from diesel engines for use in vehicles are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077) as amended by Council Directive [91/542/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0542).

**2.** Copies of the Directives referred to in these Regulations and this note may be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. Details of the Directives are set out in the Table below:
