# The Public Works Contracts Regulations 1991, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Supplementary information

Subject to regulation 18, the contracting authority may require a contractor to provide information supplementing the information provided in accordance with regulations 14, 15 and 16 or to clarify that information, provided that the information so required relates to the matters specified in regulations 14, 15 and 16.
