# The Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Other amendments

Part VII of the 1985 Act shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

**7.** The 1985 Act shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 3 to these Regulations, which are consequential on the amendments made by the foregoing Regulations.

**8.** In section 32 of the Housing Subsidies Act 1967[^f00005] (Interpretation), in the definition of “insurance company”, the words “Schedule 9A” shall be substituted for the words “Schedule 9”.

**6.** Regulation 9 sets out a transitional provision whereby a company may prepare accounts in accordance with the unamended Part VII, as that Part applies to banking companies and groups, rather than under the amended provisions for a financial year commencing prior to 23rd December 1992.
