# The Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Insertion of new Schedule

(1) Parts I and II of Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act[^f00004] shall form a new Schedule to that Act numbered “Schedule 9A” and entitled “Special Provisions For Insurance Companies And Groups”, which shall be inserted after Schedule 9.

(2) Part III of Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act is repealed.

(3) The following is substituted for the heading of Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act: “Special Provisions For Banking Companies And Groups”.

(4) Parts I to III of Schedule 1 to these Regulations (Form and content of accounts of banking companies and groups) are inserted into the 1985 Act as Parts I to III of Schedule 9 to that Act.

**5.** Regulations 6 to 8 make further and consequential amendments.

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act has been amended by the Financial Services Act [1986 (c. 60)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/60) and by section 18 of, and Schedule 7 to, the 1989 Act.
