# The Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** Regulation 4 also amends section 255C of the 1985 Act, which sets out special rules on the content of the directors' report and presently applies to banking and insurance companies and groups, so that for the future it will only apply to insurance companies and groups.
