# The Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made. 2. In these Regulations “the 1985 Act” means the Companies Act 1985 and “the 1989 Act” means the Companies Act 1989.

**1.** These Regulations implement Council Directive [86/635/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1986/0635) Official Journal N.O. L372 of 31.12.1986, pages 1–17 on the Annual Accounts and Consolidated Accounts of Banks and Other Financial Institutions, in so far as that Directive is applicable to bodies corporate to which Part VII of the Companies Act l985 (“the l985 Act”) applies.
