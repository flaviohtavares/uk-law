# The Companies Act 1985 (Bank Accounts) Regulations 1991, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — FURTHER AMENDMENTS TO PART VII OF THE 1985 ACT

Regulation 6.

**1.** Section 254(3) of the 1985 Act[^f00007] is amended by the insertion of the words “if the company is a banking company or the parent company of a banking group or” after the word “apply” therein.

**2.** Section 260(6)[^f00008] of the 1985 Act is amended by the insertion of the words “and in Chapter I of Part I of Schedule 9” after the words “Schedule 4”.

**3.** The index of defined expressions set out in section 262A of the 1985 Act[^f00009] is amended:

- (a) by the substitution, against the entries relating to the following expressions, of the following new references in place of those already existing:

**4.** Parts I and II of Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act, formed into a new Schedule by Regulation 5 of these Regulations and numbered Schedule 9A, shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 17(2) of Part I, the words from “except in so far as” to the end shall be omitted;
- (b) paragraph 27 of Part I is repealed;
- (c) in paragraph 28A[^f00010] of Part I, the words “27 or” shall be omitted;
- (d) the words “Banking or” shall be omitted from the heading to Part II;
- (e) the following shall be substituted for paragraph 1 of Part II:

  > **1.** An undertaking (other than one carrying on insurance business) whose activities are a direct extension of or ancillary to insurance business shall not be excluded from consolidation under section 229(4) (exclusion of undertakings whose activities are different from those of the undertakings consolidated).

  ;
- (f) in paragraph 5 of Part II, the word “an” shall be substituted for the words “a banking or” in sub-paragraph (1) and the words “27 or” in that sub-paragraph and “27(4)” in subparagraph (2) shall be omitted; and
- (g) paragraph 6 of Part II is repealed.

**5.** Schedule 10 to the 1985 Act (Directors' Report where Accounts Prepared in Accordance with Special Provisions for Banking or Insurance Companies or Groups)[^f00011] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) the words “banking or” shall be omitted from the heading to the Schedule and also from paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(1) where they occur; and
- (b) the words “(other than banking …Schedule 9)” in paragraph 2(2) shall be omitted.
