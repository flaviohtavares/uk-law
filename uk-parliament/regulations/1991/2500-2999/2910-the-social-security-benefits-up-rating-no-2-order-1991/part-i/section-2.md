# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 1991, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the 1975 Act” means the Social Security Act 1975[^f00003];
- “the 1986 Act” means the Social Security Act 1986;
- “the Pensions Act” means the Social Security Pensions Act 1975[^f00004];
- “the Community Charge Benefits Regulations” means the Community Charge Benefits (General) Regulations 1989[^f00005];
- “the Family Credit Regulations” means the Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00006];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regula-tions 1987[^f00007]; and
- “the Housing Benefit Regulations” means the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00008].
