# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 1991, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. The Relevant Sum for Income Support

In section 23(5)(b) of the 1986 Act[^f00028] (trade disputes; the relevant sum) the substituted sum is £22.50.

## Annotations

[^f00028]: Footnote: Stated in accordance with the Social Security Act [1986 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/50), section 23(7).
