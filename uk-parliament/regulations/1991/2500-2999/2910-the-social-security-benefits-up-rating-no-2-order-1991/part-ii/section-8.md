# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 1991, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Sum specified for child benefit

In regulation 2 of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations 1976[^f00021] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1), the sum specified is £9.65;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (1), the sum specified is £7.80; and
- (c) in paragraph 2, the sum specified is £5.85.
