# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 1991, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Earnings Limits

In section 41(2B) of the 1975 Act[^f00024] (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) for the sums £110, £14 and £110 there shall be substituted the sums of £115, £15 and £115 respectively.

## Annotations

[^f00024]: Footnote: Section 41(2B) was inserted by the Health and Social Security Act [1984 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/48), section 13, Schedule 5, paragraph 3(c).
