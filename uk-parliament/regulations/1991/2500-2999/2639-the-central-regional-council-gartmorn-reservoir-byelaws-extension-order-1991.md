# The Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2639 (S.207)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1991

Made

20th November 1991

Coming into force

25th November 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1991 and shall come into force on 25th November 1991.

**2.** The period during which the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws 1981 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 29th February 1992.

T.D. Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department,

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

20th November 1991
