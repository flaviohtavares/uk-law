# The Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 2862 (C. 88) (S. 221)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1991

Made

19th December 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 75(2) of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1991.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “the Act” means the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990.

## 3. Commencement

The provisions of the Act which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall, insofar as they are not then in force, come into force on 31st December 1991, but, where a particular purpose is specified in column 2 of that Schedule in relation to any such provision, that provision shall come into force on that day only for that purpose.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th December 1991

## SCHEDULE — The provisions of the Act which come into force on 31st December 1991

Article 3

| *Column 1* | *Column 2* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 74, In schedule 8, part 1 | Only for the purpose of bringing into force Part I of Schedule 8. |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 31st December 1991 as the date for the coming into force of the provisions of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 which are specified in the Schedule to the Order insofar as these are not already in force. The provisions so specified contain amendments to the Licensing (Scotland) Act [1976 (c. 66)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/66).

(This note is not part of the Order)
