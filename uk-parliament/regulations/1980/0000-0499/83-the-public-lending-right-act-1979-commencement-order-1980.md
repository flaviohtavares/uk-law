# The Public Lending Right Act 1979 (Commencement) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 83 (C. 5)

LIBRARIES

The Public Lending Right Act 1979 (Commencement) Order 1980

Made

22nd January 1980

Laid before Parliament

31st January 1980

Coming into Operation

21st February 1980

In exercise of the power conferred by section 5(3) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979 and vested in me[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Act 1979 (Commencement) Order 1980 and shall come into operation on 21st February 1980.

**2.** The Public Lending Right Act 1979 shall come into force on 1st March 1980.

Norman St. John-Stevas

Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster

22nd January 1980

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Public Lending Right Act 1979 into force on 1st March 1980.
