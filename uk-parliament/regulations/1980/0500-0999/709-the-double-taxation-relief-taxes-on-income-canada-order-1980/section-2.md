# The Double Taxation Relief (Taxes on Income) (Canada) Order 1980, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**2.** It is hereby declared—

- (a) that the arrangements specified in the Convention set out in the Schedule to this Order have been made with the Government of Canada with a view to affording relief from double taxation in relation to income tax, corporation tax or capital gains tax and taxes of a similar character imposed by the laws of Canada; and
- (b) that it is expedient that those arrangements should have effect.
