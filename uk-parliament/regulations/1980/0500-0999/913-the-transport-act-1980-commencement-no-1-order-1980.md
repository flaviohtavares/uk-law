# The Transport Act 1980 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 913 (C.36)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Transport Act 1980 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1980

Made

2nd July 1980

The Minister of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 70(5) and (6) of the Transport Act 1980, (hereinafter referred to as “**the 1980 Act**” ) and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 1980 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1980.

**2.** The provisions of—

- (a) the sections of the 1980 Act specified in column (1) of Part I of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the subject matter specified in column (2) of that Schedule in respect of those sections),
- (b) the paragraphs of Schedule 5 to the 1980 Act specified in column (1) of Part II of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the sections of the 1960 Act[^f00001] specified in column (2) of that Schedule in respect of those paragraphs), and
- (c) the repeals specified in Part I of Schedule 9 to the 1980 Act relating to provisions of the enactments specified in Part III of the Schedule to this Order

shall come into operation on 31st July 1980.

**3.** The reference in section 27(1) of the 1980 Act to a standard licence shall, from the date on which this Order is made until the date on which this Article is revoked, be construed as a reference to a standard licence as defined in Regulation 2(1) of the Public Service Vehicle Operators (Qualifications) Regulations 1977.

**4.** The reference in section 36 of the 1980 Act to a public service vehicle shall, from the date on which this Order is made until the date on which this Article is revoked, be construed as a reference to a public service vehicle as defined in section 117 of the 1960 Act.

**5.** The reference in section 121(4)(b) and (c) of the 1960 Act, as substituted by section 39 of the 1980 Act, to a panel of persons shall, until panels are constituted under the said section 121(4)(b) and (c) as so substituted and in accordance with regulations made under section 121(5) of the 1960 Act (as substituted by section 39 of the 1980 Act), be construed as references to panels constituted respectively under section 121(4)(b) and (c) of the 1960 Act in its original form and in accordance with the Traffic Commissioners (Nominations to Panels) Regulations 1962.

**6.** All appointments of traffic commissioners due to end on 14th November 1980 are hereby extended to 31st March 1981 and the terms of service of such commissioners which apply as respects the period immediately prior to 14th November 1980 shall continue to apply from that date until 31st March 1981.

**7.** The reference in paragraph 6(2) of Part I of Schedule 5 to the 1980 Act to the holder of the PSV operator's licence under which the vehicle is being used shall, from the date on which this Order is made until the date on which this Article is revoked, be construed as a reference to the holder of the public service vehicle licence granted under section 127 of the 1960 Act relating to the vehicle in relation to which the inspector is exercising his authority.

**8.** In this Order, and in any of the provisions of the 1980 Act which are mentioned in Article 2 above, a reference to “**the 1960 Act**” shall be construed in accordance with section 44(1) of the 1980 Act.

Norman Fowler

Minister of Transport

2nd July 1980

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 31st July 1980 the provisions of the Transport Act 1980 which are specified in Parts I, II and III of the Schedule to this Order. Those provisions relate to—

- (a) applications by a person who wishes to carry on a road passenger transport business or to be the transport manager of such a business for a certificate as to his repute and professional competence and, where relevant, his financial standing;
- (b) the abolition of the requirement for licences to enable persons to act as conductors of public service vehicles;
- (c) the appointment of traffic commissioners and the constitution of panels of nominees for appointment;
- (d) the prohibition of certain roof signs on certain vehicles;
- (e) minor and consequential amendments and repeals.
