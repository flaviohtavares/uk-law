# The Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 1753

INDUSTRIAL TRAINING

The Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1980

Made

17th November 1980

Coming into Operation

31st December 1980

The Secretary of State, on the recommendation of the Manpower Services Commission, and in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9A(2) of the Industrial Training Act 1964[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1980 and shall come into operation on 31st December 1980.

## 2. Transfer of Establishments' Activities

The activities carried on at each establishment specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, being an establishment of an employer in the industry of an industrial training board, shall instead of being included for the purposes of the Act in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 2 of that Schedule, be included for those purposes in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Jim Lester

Joint Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Employment

17th November 1980

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order comes into operation on 31st December 1980. It transfers the activities of the establishments specified in column 1 of the Schedule to the Order from the industry of the industrial training board specified in column 2 of the Schedule. The activities of the establishments so specified are transferred to the industry of the industrial training board specified in column 3 of the Schedule.
