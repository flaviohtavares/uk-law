# The Transfer of Functions (Passenger Car Fuel Consumption Tests) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 1719

MINISTERS OF THE CROWN

The Transfer of Functions (Passenger Car Fuel Consumption Tests) Order 1980

Made

11th November 1980

Laid before Parliament

19th November 1980

Coming into Operation

11th December 1980

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of November 1980

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1 of the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transfer of Functions (Passenger Car Fuel Consumption Tests) Order 1980.

(2) This Order comes into operation on 11th December 1980.

## 2. Transfer of Functions

The functions of the Secretary of State under section 15 of the Energy Act 1976 and the Passenger Car Fuel Consumption Order 1977 are hereby transferred to the Minister of Transport.

## 3. Supplementary

(1) This Order shall not affect the validity of anything done by or in relation to the Secretary of State before the coming into operation of this Order, and anything which at the coming into operation of this Order is in process of being done by or in relation to the Secretary of State (including in particular any legal proceedings to which he is a party) may, if it relates to any functions transferred by this Order, be continued by or in relation to the Minister of Transport.

(2) Any requirement or determination made, approval, exemption, direction or authorisation given, certificate furnished, result published or other thing whatsoever done by or on behalf of the Secretary of State in connection with any functions transferred by this Order shall, if in force at the commencement of this Order, have effect as if made, given, furnished, published or done by or, as the case may be, on behalf of the Minister of Transport so far as that is required for continuing its effect after the commencement of this Order.

(3) Any enactment or other instrument passed or made before the coming into operation of this Order shall have effect, so far as may be necessary for the purpose or in consequence of the transfer of functions effected by this Order, as if any reference to the Secretary of State or the Secretary of State for Energy or an officer of his were or included a reference to the Minister of Transport or an officer of his as the case may be.

N. E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the Minister of Transport the functions of the Secretary of State under section 15 of the Energy Act 1976 and the Passenger Car Fuel Consumption Order 1977.
