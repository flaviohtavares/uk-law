# The Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 1632 (c.69)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1980

Made

29th October 1980

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65(7) of the Criminal Law Act 1977, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1980.

(2) In the case of any provision specified in the Schedule to this Order which (by virtue of sections 63(2) and 65(10) of the Criminal Law Act 1977) extends to Scotland nothing in this Order shall bring the provision into force in its application to Scotland.

**2.** The provisions of the Criminal Law Act 1977 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force on 1st December 1980.

W. S. I. Whitelaw

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

29th October 1980

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st December 1980 the provisions of the Criminal Law Act 1977 set out in the Schedule to the Order. It does not apply in Scotland.

Other Orders bringing into force provisions of that Act were made by S.I. 1977/1365, 1426, 1682, 1744 (for Scotland), 1978/712, 900 (for Scotland) and 1980/487, 587 (for Scotland).
