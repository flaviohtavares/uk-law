# The British Aerospace (Appointed Day) Order 1980

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1980 No. 1988

AIRCRAFT AND SHIPBUILDING INDUSTRIES

The British Aerospace (Appointed Day) Order 1980

Made

16th December 1980

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14(1) of the British Aerospace Act 1980, and after consulting British Aerospace and the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the British Aerospace (Appointed Day) Order 1980.

**2.** The appointed day for the purposes of the British Aerospace Act 1980 is 1st January 1981.

Adam Butler

Minister of State

Department of Industry

16th December 1980

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides that the appointed day for the purposes of the British Aerospace Act 1980 is 1st January 1981.
