## 35 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Establishing compliance with the essential requirements – non-prescribed measuring instruments (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7d9c3566a78c6ecf59315f78381454e7][^c-7d9c3566a78c6ecf59315f78381454e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer may demonstrate compliance with the essential requirements in respect of a non-prescribed measuring instrument in the same manner as a regulated measuring instrument and the requirements of Part 4 accordingly apply. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d9c3566a78c6ecf59315f78381454e7]
