# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Penalties for offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under any provision of these Regulations is liable, on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales to a fine; and
- (b) in Scotland or Northern Ireland to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
