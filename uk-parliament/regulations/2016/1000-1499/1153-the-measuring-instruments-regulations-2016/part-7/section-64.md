## 64. EU safeguard procedure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] Where another *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] relevant state[^c-26dd2948c39a733f427d63be6dd88391] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] has initiated the procedure under Article 42 of the Directive, the Market surveillance authority must without delay, inform the Commission and the other *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] relevant states[^c-ad2c2ffcf4bec43c2135b3277455b69d] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] of— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] any measures taken by a competent authority in respect of the regulated measuring instrument; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] any additional information which the market surveillance authority has at its disposal relating to the lack of conformity of the regulated measuring *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] instrument.[^c-47fdd8d518aa26e81966b44ef5a55339] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d7bfd54d8bf1816556a4482db1bacb50][^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] Where a measure taken by another *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] relevant[^c-35781a3cebfabec8aa98d121ec930bea] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] state in respect of a regulated measuring instrument is considered justified under Article 42 of the Directive, the market surveillance authority must ensure that appropriate measures to withdraw the instrument are taken in respect of the regulated measuring instrument without delay. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38] If, pursuant to Article 43 of the Directive, the Commission considers a direction given pursuant to regulation 63(4) is unjustified, the market surveillance authority must forthwith withdraw it and notify other enforcement authorities and economic operators affected accordingly. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-421073c70d4a45674d63671a25441f38]
