# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 37. Methods of establishing conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_d4e8af49-162b-4486-c17a-fc25788cfc3b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Conformity with the essential requirements may be established in relation to a regulated[^c-66a8a03f0d4df736bd210b143658ce2d] measuring instrument—

- (a) through conformity with designated[^c-a97bd543db969fe766a1a179f5bca3ea] standards (or parts of those standards) covering the essential requirements [^c-77932531466e43256434995b81b23692]...;
- (b) through conformity with parts of normative documents which cover the essential requirements where the parts of the normative documents have been included in a list published by the Secretary of State[^c-572b9ec04113ab3bfc489e1f6f7a3819]; or
- (c) through the use by the manufacturer of any other technical solution that complies with the essential requirements.

## 37. Methods of establishing conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_539d5158-80a2-47b5-aac4-c62d935bc767]

*Extent: N.I.*

Conformity with the essential requirements may be established in relation to a measuring instrument—

- (a) through conformity with harmonised standards (or parts of those standards) covering the essential requirements where the harmonised standards have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union;
- (b) through conformity with parts of normative documents which cover the essential requirements where the parts of the normative documents have been included in a list published in the Official Journal of the European Union; or
- (c) through the use by the manufacturer of any other technical solution that complies with the essential requirements.
