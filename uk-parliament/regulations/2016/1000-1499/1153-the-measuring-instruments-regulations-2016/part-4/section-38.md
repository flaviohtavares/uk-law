# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Presumptions of conformity of regulated measuring instruments[^M_E_f6d7096b-272b-47e2-e5bf-c173dc7f5fa4][^key-2a85131108e0ea5af1ba8b32ac5db27a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulated[^key-62e2338239ce6c3a9c7baee39e98a38c] measuring instruments which are in conformity with designated[^key-05619e3158d2a2e640a8636c4b518d61] standards (or parts of those standards) of a kind mentioned in regulation 37(a), are to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those standards (or parts of those standards).

(2) Regulated[^key-725a17e7e517e11aefeab5bad5fd3e86] measuring instruments which are in conformity with parts of normative documents of a kind mentioned in regulation 37(b), are to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those parts of normative documents.

(3) To benefit from a presumption of conformity under paragraphs (1) or (2), the manufacturer must correctly apply solutions mentioned in the relevant designated[^key-8824aa9c5f21fd955dce3764fed9a269] standards or in the normative documents.

(4) Compliance with the appropriate tests mentioned in regulation 45(1)(i) is to be presumed if the corresponding test programme has been performed in accordance with the documents mentioned in paragraphs (1) and (2) and if the test results ensure compliance with the essential requirements.

## 38. Presumptions of conformity of measuring instruments[^M_E_0edb28b5-0b5c-4ef1-9486-6412dd8ce0f6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Measuring instruments which are in conformity with harmonised standards (or parts of those standards) of a kind mentioned in regulation 37(a), are to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those standards (or parts of those standards).

(2) Measuring instruments which are in conformity with parts of normative documents of a kind mentioned in regulation 37(b), are to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those parts of normative documents.

(3) To benefit from a presumption of conformity under paragraphs (1) or (2), the manufacturer must correctly apply solutions mentioned in the relevant harmonised standards or in the normative documents.

(4) Compliance with the appropriate tests mentioned in regulation 45(1)(i) is to be presumed if the corresponding test programme has been performed in accordance with the documents mentioned in paragraphs (1) and (2) and if the test results ensure compliance with the essential requirements.
