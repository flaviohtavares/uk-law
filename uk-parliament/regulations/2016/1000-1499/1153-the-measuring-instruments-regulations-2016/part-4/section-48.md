# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Regulated measuring instruments that require more than one declaration of conformity[^M_E_56da0214-9ca9-4500-aa89-37fd3b86fb06][^c-718056cde2401e75e0e064e5fa6e64e2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a regulated measuring instrument is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.

## 48. Measuring instruments that require more than one declaration of conformity[^M_E_f9a58b79-9ab2-44c6-fbf6-6a73c8af55d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a measuring instrument is subject to an NI Protocol obligation[^c-13824eb6b69af3afd3f88cea9eb639e5] for an EU declaration of conformity otherwise than by virtue of these Regulations.

(2) Where this regulation applies, a single EU declaration of conformity must be drawn up covering all applicable requirements which identifies the relevant[^c-7c8a68a3def08f6f3103dc7ac0a2634e] Union acts concerned including their publication references.
