> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>

## 46. Application of Chapter[^M_E_116c6944-f682-4caa-e58e-2ec5fe1f9a6e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Chapter applies in relation to [^c-026a7782cb695ee088495f0cc0d789b6]... declarations of conformity made in relation to a regulated[^c-fba450125edde431caddeebcd366f485] measuring instrument for the purposes of these Regulations.

## 46. Application of Chapter[^M_E_27329061-ff3d-4b9f-8f99-75671fef7910]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies in relation to EU declarations of conformity made in relation to a measuring instrument for the purposes of these Regulations.
