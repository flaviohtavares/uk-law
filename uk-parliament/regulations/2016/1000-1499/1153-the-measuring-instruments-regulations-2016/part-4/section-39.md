# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Conformity assessment procedures[^M_E_ec43b7d1-cd0a-45c9-af02-ca16e1b83f6a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Conformity assessment of a regulated[^key-5369f6585398509f20af2339a752892a] measuring instrument with the essential requirements must be established by the application at the choice of the manufacturer, of one of the conformity assessment procedures listed as applicable in relation to the regulated measuring instrument in Schedule 1C to 1J[^key-446e64849912c714a79c855f1746abc2].

(2) An approved[^key-a9307f14a06d9824c42d945f88ef0ebc] body must carry out the conformity assessment procedure selected by the manufacturer in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 4.

(3) The documents and correspondence relating to the conformity assessment procedures referred to in this regulation which are carried out in the United Kingdom must be drawn up in English.

## 39. Conformity assessment procedures[^M_E_8b1642c9-a362-4e39-87ba-a22a88f4c63d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Conformity assessment of a measuring instrument with the essential requirements must be established by the application at the choice of the manufacturer, of one of the conformity assessment procedures listed as applicable in relation to the measuring instrument in Schedule 1.

(2) A notified body must carry out the conformity assessment procedure selected by the manufacturer in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 4.

(3) The documents and correspondence relating to the conformity assessment procedures referred to in this regulation which are carried out in the United Kingdom must be drawn up in English.
