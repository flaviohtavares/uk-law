# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 50. Conformity with ... requirements to be indicated by the UK marking[^M_E_62417086-51d0-49a7-9bc0-e72d3314a987][^c-ae40e6c31eb30ed3bf32c55066f013e2][^c-460a24e968a26467509b42145efa5f85]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The conformity of a measuring instrument with the requirements of these Regulations must be indicated by the presence on it of the UK[^c-c217c19850895b576d5861884c3d7c21] marking and the M marking.

## 50. Conformity with Directive requirements to be indicated by the CE marking[^M_E_292a7196-0992-424f-cb3c-b8d8f30aa736]

*Extent: N.I.*

The conformity of a measuring instrument with the requirements of these Regulations must be indicated by the presence on it of the CE marking and the M marking.
