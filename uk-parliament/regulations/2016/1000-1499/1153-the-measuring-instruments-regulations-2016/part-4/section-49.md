# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 49. Responsibility of manufacturer that draws up declaration of conformity[^M_E_36662b53-a89b-47cf-93c6-613f8853d351]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A manufacturer, who draws up a[^c-017298909ba58e8e4db00884d5f6714c] declaration of conformity in relation to a regulated[^c-0d391476c5afcbfe7972345096e64169] measuring instrument, is responsible for compliance of the measuring instrument with the requirements of these Regulations.

## 49. Responsibility of manufacturer that draws up declaration of conformity[^M_E_8fcd779a-7e00-4b28-a641-63e60a02cccc]

*Extent: N.I.*

A manufacturer, who draws up an EU declaration of conformity in relation to a measuring instrument, is responsible for compliance of the measuring instrument with the requirements of these Regulations.
