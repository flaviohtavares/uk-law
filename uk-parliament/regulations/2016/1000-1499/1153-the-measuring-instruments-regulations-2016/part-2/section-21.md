# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Duty of importers to ensure proper conditions of storage and transport

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must, in respect of regulated measuring instruments under the importer's responsibility ensure that the conditions of their storage or transport are not such as to jeopardise their continuing compliance with the essential requirements.
