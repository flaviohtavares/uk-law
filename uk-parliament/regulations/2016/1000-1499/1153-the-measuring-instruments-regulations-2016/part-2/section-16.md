## 16. Introductory[^M_E_a5df87c0-97dd-44bf-f82c-6f024970dcef]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Chapter applies to the placing on the market or the putting into use of a regulated measuring instrument from a country outside the [^c-d2483c7fbc658083365978f607838ef3]... United Kingdom.

## 16. Introductory[^M_E_aaf4aa4b-d4fb-40f2-e608-0957dee45afa]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies to the placing on the market or the putting into use of a regulated measuring instrument from a country outside the relevant market[^c-d37d7ef8bab4c89b2aa2ee9c8f931f8b] that is imported into Northern Ireland[^c-9107002081355b14c141970eacb7c020].
