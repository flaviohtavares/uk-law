# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 19. Requirements to mark importers' details on regulated measuring instruments[^M_E_75f6e5ed-a653-4556-f511-3d7b4ead5baa][^c-22f70420789e6fe20f309524256a6578]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must indicate on regulated measuring instruments imported by that importer, the importer's name, registered trade name or trademark and the postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2)[^c-329e46affd1e12437103b0acb1f49df9] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) the regulated measuring instrument it too small or too sensitive a composition to allow it to bear the information required by paragraph (1); or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the regulated measuring instrument from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-e693ac384f6f7c15c3707a6e76e2240a] beginning with IP completion day, and
- (b) before placing the regulated measuring instrument on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) where sub-paragraph (a)(i) applies, on any packaging in which the instrument is supplied and in any accompanying documents;
  - (ii) where sub-paragraph (a)(ii) applies, in a document accompanying the instrument.

(3)[^c-b629e3402b16e8befa727a22b09d609c] Any contact details required by this regulation must be clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 19. Requirements to mark importers' details on regulated measuring instruments[^M_E_b8b9d01e-b443-48f3-a2f0-2a53fa4b850f][^V_NI_8e8af94a-dc3e-6322-1b11-c8df2e57c1d3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must indicate on regulated measuring instruments imported by that importer, the importer's name, registered trade name or trademark and the postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) Where a regulated measuring instrument is too small or of too sensitive a composition to allow it to bear the information required by paragraph (1), such information must be marked on any packaging in which the instrument is supplied and on any accompanying documents.

(3) Any contact details required by this regulation must be in a language easily understood by end-users and market surveillance authorities and, in the case of regulated measuring instruments made available in Northern Ireland[^c-4d6f9445a3ddfac79c72fc69fb38571f], they must be in English.
