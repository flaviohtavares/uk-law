# The Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1153
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 58. Register of approved bodies[^M_E_382f046f-4f9e-4bb7-a268-bb913f6815c8][^c-819bc0dbe42933501fb9b560b0fdcea2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State must—

- (a) assign an approved body identification number to each approved body; and
- (b) compile and maintain a register of—
  - (i) approved bodies;
  - (ii) their approved body identification numbers;
  - (iii) the activities for which they have been approved; and
  - (iv) any restrictions on those activities.

(2) The register referred to in paragraph (1) must be made publicly available.

## 58. Monitoring[^M_E_8874a253-63e2-4e6d-8d49-bf2542eb4e42]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must monitor each notified body with a view to verifying that the notified body—

- (a) continues to meet the notified body requirements;
- (b) meets any conditions set in accordance with regulation 55(6)(b); and
- (c) carries out its functions in accordance with these Regulations.

(2) The Secretary of State must inform the Commission of the United Kingdom's procedures for the monitoring of notified bodies, and any changes to those procedures.
