# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Amendment of article 41[^c-b026dcfbe931c2eb0ba4ddf88db18b7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 41 (powers of relevant court on appeal from Appeals Committee) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4)(b), after “against” insert “or, in the case of an appeal against a decision to send an alert about a person, direct that the alert be withdrawn or amended”.

(3) In paragraph (4)(c), omit the “or” at the end.

(4) After paragraph (4)(c), insert—

> - (ca) with respect to an appeal under article 40(1A), enter any decision which could have been taken by the Council; or

.
