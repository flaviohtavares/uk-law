# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 32

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Amendment of article 23[^c-c6a09569a44568f3c7c7eb2e19e11734]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 23 (form, manner and content of applications for entry or for renewal of an entry in the register: pharmacists and pharmacy technicians) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(c)(iv)(bb), omit the “and” at the end.

(3) After paragraph (1)(c)(v), insert—

> - (vi) if the applicant is an exempt person, in the event of justified doubts, confirmation from the competent authority of a relevant European State that the applicant is not suspended or prohibited from practising as a pharmacist or pharmacy technician as a result of serious professional misconduct or conviction of criminal offences relating to the pursuit of any of the applicant’s professional activities; and

.
