# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Insertion of article 33A[^key-4d61f4a97558e59fa3c9a4c4cd4adf0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After article 33 (visiting pharmacists and pharmacy technicians from relevant European States), insert—

> **33A. European professional card**
>
> (1) Schedule 2A ([Directive 2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0036): European professional card) has effect.
>
> (2) The Council may charge a reasonable fee to cover the costs of processing an application for or in relation to a European professional card under Schedule 2A.

.
