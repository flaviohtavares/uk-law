# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 34

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Amendment of article 39[^c-c55e784c247a82944c251e0e4d7f76ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 39 (appealable decisions)[^f00027] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(r), omit the “and” at the end.

(3) After paragraph (1)(s), insert—

> - (t) a decision of the Council under regulation 67 of the General Systems Regulations to send an alert about a person; and
> - (u) a decision of the Council, under Schedule 2A, or under Part 4 of the General Systems Regulations, to revoke, or not to issue, extend or vary, a European professional card.

.

(4) After paragraph (1), insert—

> (1A) A failure by the Council to make a decision in relation to a European professional card within the time limit under paragraph 10(4) or 12(4) of Schedule 2C or regulation 51(1) or 56(1) of the General Systems Regulations is also appealable for the purposes of article 40.

.
