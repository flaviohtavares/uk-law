# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 27

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Amendment of regulation 2[^c-8eab60d6659a54d570f1ef3a1079a34f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 2 (manner and content of applications by exempt persons for registration in the register of pharmaceutical chemists) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), for “(6)” substitute “(6A)”.

(3) After paragraph (6), insert—

> (6A) In the event of justified doubts, the registrar shall accept a certificate issued by a competent authority in E’s attesting State which confirms that E is not suspended or prohibited from practising as a pharmacist as a result of serious professional misconduct or conviction of criminal offences relating to the pursuit of any of E’s professional activities.

.

(4) In paragraph (7), for “(6)” substitute “(6A)”.

(5) In paragraph (8), for “, (4), (5) or (6)” substitute “to (6A)”.
