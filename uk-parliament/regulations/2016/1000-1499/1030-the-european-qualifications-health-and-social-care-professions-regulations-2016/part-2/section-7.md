## 7. Amendment of section 19A[^key-c574934d9005814d23c43dde01a0ca96]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 19A (full registration of EEA nationals etc by virtue of overseas qualifications accepted by a relevant European State other than the United Kingdom)[^f00008] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (a), for “3(9)(e)” substitute “3(8)(e)”;

(3) In paragraph (b)—

- (a) for “20 to 26” substitute “27 to 34”;
- (b) for “3(4)” substitute “3(5)”.
