# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 84

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Amendment of rule 20[^key-15adb3c53e906beddd2c165a3faf5bd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Rule 20 (period during which an appeal may be made)[^f00065] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (a)—

- (a) for “or (c)” substitute “, (c) or (e) to (h)”;
- (b) omit the “or” at the end.

(3) In paragraph (b), at the end, insert “; or”.

(4) After paragraph (b), insert—

> - (c) where the appeal is in relation to a failure to issue a decision in relation to a European professional card within the applicable time limit, as referred to in sub-paragraph (h) of that article, before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the date by which the decision was required to have been sent.

.
