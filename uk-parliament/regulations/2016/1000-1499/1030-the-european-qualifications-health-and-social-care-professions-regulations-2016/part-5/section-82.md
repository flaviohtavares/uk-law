# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 82

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Introductory[^key-742bd2eaa81ab0dcf258243e216faa42]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Nursing and Midwifery (Education, Registration and Registration Appeals) Rules 2004, which are set out in the Schedule to the Nursing and Midwifery Council (Education, Registration and Registration Appeals) Rules Order of Council 2004[^f00063], are amended in accordance with this Chapter.
