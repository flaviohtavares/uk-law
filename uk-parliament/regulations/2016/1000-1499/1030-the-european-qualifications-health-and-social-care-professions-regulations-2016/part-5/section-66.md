# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 66

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Insertion of article 39B[^c-64872fcf3e2a33d7ae0b2f472e9afeb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After article 39A, insert—

> **39B. European professional card**
>
> (1) Schedule 2B of this Order ([Directive 2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0036): European professional card) has effect.
>
> (2) The Council may charge a reasonable fee to cover the costs of processing an application for or in relation to a European professional card under Schedule 2B.
