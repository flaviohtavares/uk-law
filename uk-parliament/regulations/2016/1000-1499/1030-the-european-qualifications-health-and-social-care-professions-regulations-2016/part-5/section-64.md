# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 64

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Amendment of article 38[^key-89c008264bbb1b5a2fc13c3b5551c0ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 38 (appeals)[^f00048] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (1A)(b).

(3) In paragraph (3)(b), after “against” insert “or, in the case of an appeal under article 37(1)(f), direct that the alert be withdrawn or amended”.

(4) In paragraph (3)(c), omit the “or” at the end.

(5) After paragraph (3)(c), insert—

> - (ca) with respect to an appeal under paragraph (1)(h), enter any decision which could have been taken by the Council; or

.
