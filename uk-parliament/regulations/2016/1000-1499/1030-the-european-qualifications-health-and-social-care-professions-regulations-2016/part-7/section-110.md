# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 110

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Amendment of section 71[^key-935043e9d47ccfce3000ab1bd958177f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 71(rules)[^f00089] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3A), for “European part” substitute “European social worker part or visiting European social care manager part”.
