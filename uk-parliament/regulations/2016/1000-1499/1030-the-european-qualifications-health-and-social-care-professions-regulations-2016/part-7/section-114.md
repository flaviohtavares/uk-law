# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 114

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Amendment of section 15[^c-5254b898f1f0501494c509e869a8391f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 15 (appeals to the Care Tribunal)[^f00093] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)(b), after “decision” insert “or, in the case of an appeal from a decision falling within subsection (3)(c), direct that the alert be withdrawn or amended”.

(3) In subsection (3)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), omit the “and” at the end;
- (b) in paragraph (b), at the end, insert “, and”;
- (c) after paragraph (b), insert—

  > - (c) a decision under regulation 67 of the General Systems Regulations to send an alert about a person.

  .
