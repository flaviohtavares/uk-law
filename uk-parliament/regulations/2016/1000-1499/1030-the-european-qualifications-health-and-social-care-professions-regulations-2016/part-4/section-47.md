## 47. Amendment of section 15[^c-9e2d56cdbf3e1de5e346fc0befdb0066]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 15 (qualification for registration in the dentists register) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(ba)(i), for “3(9)(a) or (e)” substitute “3(8)(a) or (e)”.

(3) In subsection (1)(ba)(ii)—

- (a) for “20 to 26” substitute “27 to 34”;
- (b) for “3(4)” substitute “3(5)”.
