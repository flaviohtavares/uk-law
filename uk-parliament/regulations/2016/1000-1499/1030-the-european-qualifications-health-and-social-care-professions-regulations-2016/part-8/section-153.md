# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 153

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Amendment of rule 4[^key-ba316d35aad2be09bcc3be5be6175910]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Rule 4 (applications for registration)[^f00105] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4)(a)(ii), omit the “or” at the end.

(3) After paragraph (4)(a)(ii), insert—

> - (iia) where the applicant is relying on article 12(1)(ba) of the Order, evidence that the applicant has obtained a European professional card; or

.
