# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 152

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Introductory[^c-ecd418d60cd9d5f4d7ef4fe8b2996c51]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Health and Care Professions Council (Registration and Fees) Rules 2003, which are set out in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Registration and Fees) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00104], are amended in accordance with this Chapter.
