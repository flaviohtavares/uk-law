# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 150

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Insertion of article 49[^key-a39735f23428bda612f96e70d82bc906]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After article 48 (supplementary and transitional provisions and extent), insert—

> **49. Review**
>
> By 1st November 2021, and subsequently at intervals not exceeding 5 years from that date, in accordance with section 30 of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, the Secretary of State must—
>
> - (a) carry out a review of the amendments made to this Order by the European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016;
> - (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and
> - (c) publish the report.

.
