# The European Qualifications (Health and Social Care Professions) Regulations 2016, section 93

> Text as at 2016-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Amendment of section 29A[^key-51675fe2779009712a112362ad3142e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 29A (appeals against decisions of the General Council)[^f00074] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2), after paragraph (b), insert—

> - (c) a decision of the General Council under regulation 67 of the General Systems Regulations to send an alert about a person.

.

(3) In subsection (3)(b), after “against” insert “or, in the case of an appeal from a decision falling within subsection (2)(c), direct that the alert be withdrawn or amended”.
