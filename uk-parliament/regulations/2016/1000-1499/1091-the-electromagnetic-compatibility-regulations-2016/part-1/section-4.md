# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 4. Application of safety legislation[^M_E_7a52796a-6b37-4276-bbd4-7a7ed87db325]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of any enactment[^c-51fb12956c0b973e1ca48787bfbbcf71] regulating the safety of equipment.

## 4. Application of safety legislation

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of EU or national legislation regulating the safety of equipment.
