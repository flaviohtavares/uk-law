# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 6. Making available or putting into service[^M_E_90c8f189-51a4-43bb-f499-97c3d04f4cea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these Regulations prevents the making available on the market, or the putting into service in the United Kingdom, of equipment which is in conformity with Part 2 when the equipment is properly installed, maintained and used for its intended purpose.

## 6. Making available or putting into service

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations prevents the making available on the market, or the putting into service in Northern Ireland[^c-1770c81af491ab2457223c4371f1f839], of equipment which is in conformity with Part 2 when the equipment is properly installed, maintained and used for its intended purpose.
