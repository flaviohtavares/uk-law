# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 39. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_23308d68-a9f9-4037-b6a5-b49cb6816218]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Equipment which is in conformity with a designated[^c-aba5c6ed03dc373e2fd91a56cabe8ef8] standard (or part of such a standard) [^c-10553197e5b8dd31c1c32fdf0b8ca605]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 39. Presumption of conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Equipment which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
