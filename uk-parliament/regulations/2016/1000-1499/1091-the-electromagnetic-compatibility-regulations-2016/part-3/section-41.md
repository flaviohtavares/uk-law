# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Declaration of conformity[^M_E_acac572c-0557-480a-f945-c05c9d62a076][^key-081e494831bb1d8f2ee5233a8b7f0d87]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^key-47213b0ef56b0252a4fb2019e66480c7]... declaration of conformity for apparatus must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the apparatus;
- (b) contain the elements of the relevant conformity assessment procedure or procedures followed in respect of the apparatus; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 4.

## 41. EU declaration of conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity for apparatus must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the apparatus;
- (b) contain the elements of the relevant conformity assessment procedure or procedures followed in respect of the apparatus; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 4.
