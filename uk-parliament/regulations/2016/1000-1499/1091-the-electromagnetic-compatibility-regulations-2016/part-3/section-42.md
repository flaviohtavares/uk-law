# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 42. UK marking[^M_E_a01f0ea5-daa7-4d2f-8f4c-8a369dfeefb0][^c-94ec936a9fdd6deb2e61e73ca94224b4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-bbb52844a2c6c3501a80e22bfe4dab96] The UK marking must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly—

- (a) to the apparatus;
- (b) to its data plate; or
- (c) where paragraph (1A) applies, to—
  - (i) a label affixed to the apparatus or its data plate; or
  - (ii) to a document accompanying the apparatus.

(1A)[^c-bbc30701141635663475edb35cab5d2a] For a period of seven years[^c-022f5b223a753e2498404c15c177655a] beginning with IP completion day, the UK marking may be affixed to—

- (a) a label affixed to the apparatus or its data plate; or
- (b) a document accompanying the apparatus.

(2) Where paragraph (1A) does not apply and[^c-903d779473a5c198046e7b05c899f499] it is not possible or warranted, on account of the nature of the apparatus, to affix the UK[^c-508f900c674dd3dd49a2384c75487695] marking in accordance with paragraph (1), the CE marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the packaging; and
- (b) the accompanying documents.

## 42. CE marking[^M_E_d8d965a3-4087-437e-eb27-ebd9c763dd1d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The CE marking must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly to the apparatus or to its data plate.

(2) Where it is not possible or warranted, on account of the nature of the apparatus, to affix the CE marking in accordance with paragraph (1), the CE marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the packaging; and
- (b) the accompanying documents.
