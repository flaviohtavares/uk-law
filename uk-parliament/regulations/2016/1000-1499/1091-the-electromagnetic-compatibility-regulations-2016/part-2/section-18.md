# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Requirements that must be satisfied before an importer places apparatus on the market[^M_E_10038202-391d-4339-8f0c-9bd933d1b8d4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before placing apparatus on the market an importer must ensure that—

- (a) a relevant conformity assessment has been carried out by the manufacturer;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c) the apparatus—
  - (i) bears the UK[^key-b87661cd54404b7300b932411e0dacf6] marking; and
  - (ii) is accompanied by the required documents; and
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 13 (information identifying manufacturer).

(2) In paragraph (1)(c)(ii) “**required documents**” means any documents that are required to be provided pursuant to regulation 13(2).

## 18. Requirements that must be satisfied before an importer places apparatus on the market

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before placing apparatus on the market an importer must ensure that—

- (a) a relevant conformity assessment has been carried out by the manufacturer;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c) the apparatus—
  - (i) bears the CE marking; and
  - (ii) is accompanied by the required documents; and
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 13 (information identifying manufacturer).

(2) In paragraph (1)(c)(ii) “**required documents**” means any documents that are required to be provided pursuant to regulation 13(2).
