# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Information identifying importer[^c-8fa56a7a8f160420e1018b91803e72c5][^M_E_685f0f8d-5495-4571-8e7e-023268d4dac8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must, before placing apparatus on the market, ensure that the following appear on the apparatus [^c-217f554369146c0fc9530cf7e36c34e6]...—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(1A)[^c-10e418746e62fdb2115087173bbb6a1a] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) it is not possible to set out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the packaging of the apparatus or on the apparatus, or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the apparatus from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-907d66dd16e24988ddfd2b78900adf06] beginning with IP completion day, and
- (b) before placing the apparatus on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1) in a document accompanying the apparatus.

(2) The information specified in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and the enforcing authority[^c-fcf6b962f05f17f1711ce530fec613d7].

## 20. Information identifying importer

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must, before placing apparatus on the market, ensure that the following appear on the apparatus or, where that is not possible, on the packaging of the apparatus or in a document accompanying the apparatus—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) The information specified in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and the competent national authority in the relevant state[^c-6b660901110723ab32000f81ee6f666e] in which it is to be made available.
