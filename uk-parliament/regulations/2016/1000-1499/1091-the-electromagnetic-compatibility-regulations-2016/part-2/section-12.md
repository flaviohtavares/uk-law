# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_8d85e549-b9fc-45c6-ea26-ff15fc5f888d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer of apparatus which is manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing apparatus on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any apparatus will be in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in the design or characteristics; and
- (b) any change in a designated[^c-fd58e52e3ea0f5ba9892632b5d00af88] standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the [^c-45fabe35cbd2274cc55233492db34ab0]... declaration of conformity was drawn up.

## 12. Compliance procedures for series production

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer of apparatus which is manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing apparatus on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any apparatus will be in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in the design or characteristics; and
- (b) any change in a harmonised standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the EU declaration of conformity was drawn up.
