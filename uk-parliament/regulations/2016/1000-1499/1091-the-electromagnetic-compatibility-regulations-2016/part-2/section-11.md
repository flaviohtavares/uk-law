## 11. Retention of technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^key-8d6a9643cd02e4e6d216643f2797e07e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d60d396490ade4eb11e5dd21a6419e5a] declaration of conformity (as referred to in regulation 41) drawn up in respect of the apparatus for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the apparatus is placed on the market.
