## 24. Retention of technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^key-223c3019fd82bb235a49b62305ed1a73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must keep the technical documentation and the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-333bad21a90e276297c98c2fe789fc76] declaration of conformity (as referred to in regulation 41) drawn up in respect of the apparatus for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the apparatus is placed on the market.
