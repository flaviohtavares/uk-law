# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 23. Importer's duty to take action in respect of apparatus placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_ee2274d0-5c19-44c3-95ed-107581c5e584]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer who considers or has reason to believe that apparatus that the importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the apparatus into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the apparatus; or
- (c) recall the apparatus.

(2) Where the apparatus presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^c-49a4564022700013ccf24a86c6d7b278]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the apparatus is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 23. Importer's duty to take action in respect of apparatus placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer who considers or has reason to believe that apparatus that the importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the apparatus into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the apparatus; or
- (c) recall the apparatus.

(2) Where the apparatus presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent authorities of any relevant state[^c-110b5569a84713ba5f9938d37f65f375] in which the importer has made the apparatus available on the market of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the apparatus is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
