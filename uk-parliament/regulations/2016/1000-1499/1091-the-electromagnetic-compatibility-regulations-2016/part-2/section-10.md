# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_208d4e5e-96b3-4a9c-9487-5e46f9d91d49][^key-b0d5766ccd81e3a2a8bc12b602d12cc8][^key-65caba50e3c3ddaa93e372b0654f16f0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the conformity of apparatus with the essential requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the apparatus on the market—

- (a) draw up a[^key-29d78c339ad26d7f057dfe1e739d6f6b] declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 41 [^key-f874d368096265bb5175ed37487fef93]...; and
- (b) affix the UK[^key-7a0681d4343b5f44d6a263c27e260a09] marking in accordance with regulation 42 (UK marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the [^key-3647f9c6198a7adc157274976195766c]... declaration of conformity up-to-date.

(3)[^key-5da71fb240c46199d21ab9ef9450d639] Where apparatus is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment concerned by its title.

## 10. EU declaration of conformity and CE marking

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the conformity of apparatus with the essential requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the apparatus on the market—

- (a) draw up an EU declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 41 (EU declaration of conformity); and
- (b) affix the CE marking in accordance with regulation 42 (CE marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the EU declaration of conformity up-to-date.

(3) Where apparatus is subject to more than one NI Protocol obligation[^key-198bf06d4651e485cc75fb04b6bd922a] requiring a declaration of conformity to be drawn up, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity, which—

- (a) identifies the relevant[^key-9449d787af73ece767fbfd4d7f732b21] EU instruments; and
- (b) includes references to the publication of the relevant[^key-d190547d6001bbd721a45c798aabd6ea] EU instruments in the Official Journal.
