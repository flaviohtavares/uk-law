# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Duty to ensure apparatus complies with the essential requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before placing apparatus on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the essential requirements.
