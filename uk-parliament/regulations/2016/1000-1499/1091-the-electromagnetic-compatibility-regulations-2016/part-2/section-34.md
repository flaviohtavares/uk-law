## 34. Translation of EU declaration of conformity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] Before placing apparatus on the market or making apparatus available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] relevant state[^c-1886ba8326457b4d88a6f303b2355ac3] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] in which it is to be placed on the market or made available on the market. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] Where the apparatus is to be placed on the market or made available on the market in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1] Northern Ireland[^c-ff5d6398b7da5082862f339cdadae9d9] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1], the language referred to in paragraph (1) is English. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b890d931aedb22c857b048cf20145ec1]
