# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 60

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Restrictive measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2020-12-31).**

When enforcing these Regulations, an enforcing authority must comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS in relation to any measure to—

- (a) prohibit or restrict apparatus being made available on the market;
- (b) withdraw apparatus; or
- (c) recall apparatus.
