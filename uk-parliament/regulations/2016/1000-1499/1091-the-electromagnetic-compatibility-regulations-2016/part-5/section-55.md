# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 55

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Exercise of enforcement powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2020-12-31).**

When enforcing these Regulations, the enforcing authority must exercise its powers in a manner which is consistent with—

- (a) regulation 56 (evaluation of apparatus presenting a risk);
- (b) regulation 57 (enforcement action in respect of apparatus that is not in conformity and which present a risk);
- (c) regulation 58 ( *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_86a93b3b-373a-45ad-f21d-a7f32f11e626]EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_86a93b3b-373a-45ad-f21d-a7f32f11e626] safeguard procedure); *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_86a93b3b-373a-45ad-f21d-a7f32f11e626]
- (d) regulation 59 (enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance);
- (e) regulation 60 (restrictive measures).
