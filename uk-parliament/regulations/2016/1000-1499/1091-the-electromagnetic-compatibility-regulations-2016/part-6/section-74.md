# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 74

## 74. Transitional provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations prevents the making available on the market or the putting into service of equipment which—

- (a) is in conformity with the requirements of Directive [2004/108/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0108) on the approximation of laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to electromagnetic compatibility [^V_NI_a1593702-8b22-933d-0766-738194fd4734]; and
- (b) is placed on the market or put into service before the commencement date.
