# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1091
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Revocations and savings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the 2006 Regulations are revoked.

(2) The 2006 Regulations continue to apply, subject to the modifications in paragraph (2A), *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-1f6ccf28d06d2ab2944291d97114dcff] as if they had not been revoked, to equipment placed on the market or put into service before the commencement date.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(2A)[^c-778f47195cde729ddb8c213b28f052de] The modifications referred to in paragraph (2) are that in the Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2006—

- (a) any reference to “the Community” is to be read as including the United Kingdom;
- (b) any reference to a “member State” is to be read as including the United Kingdom.

(3) Accordingly, despite the repeals in regulation 76(4), the entries in paragraphs 10, 19(7)(a), 25(7) and 30(1) of Schedule 5 to the Consumer Rights Act 2015 [^c23181421] relating to the 2006 Regulations are to continue to have effect in relation to equipment placed on the market or put into service before the commencement date.

(4) The Electromagnetic Compatibility (Amendment) Regulations 2006 [^c23181431] are revoked.

(5) Nothing in these Regulations is to be construed as preventing the taking of any action in respect of any equipment under the provisions of any other enactment.
