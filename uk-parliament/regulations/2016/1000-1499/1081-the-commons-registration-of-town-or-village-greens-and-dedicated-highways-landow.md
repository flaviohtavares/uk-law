# The Commons (Registration of Town or Village Greens) and Dedicated Highways (Landowner Statements and Declarations) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

Made

9th November 2016

Laid before Parliament

10th November 2016

Coming into force

1st December 2016

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(6C) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and section 15A(6) of the Commons Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commons (Registration of Town or Village Greens) and Dedicated Highways (Landowner Statements and Declarations) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 1st December 2016.

(2) These Regulations apply to any application to which regulation 2(1) of the 2013 Regulations applies made on or after the date on which these Regulations come into force.

(3) In these Regulations, “the 2013 Regulations” means the Commons (Registration of Town or Village Greens) and Dedicated Highways (Landowner Statements and Declarations) (England) Regulations 2013[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the Commons (Registration of Town or Village Greens) and Dedicated Highways (Landowner Statements and Declarations) (England) Regulations 2013

In regulation 4(2)(c) of the 2013 Regulations, at the beginning insert “in respect of an application to which regulation 2(1)(c) applies,”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Commons (Registration of Town or Village Greens) and Dedicated Highways (Landowner Statements and Declarations) (England) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1774](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1774)) to remove the requirement on an appropriate authority to post notice of an application to deposit a statement and map, or to lodge a declaration, under section 31(6) of the Highways Act [1980 (c. 66)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/66) in an obvious or conspicuous place on the land to which the application relates.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
