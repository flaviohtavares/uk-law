# The Rent Officers (Housing Benefit and Universal Credit Functions) (Local Housing Allowance Amendments) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

6th December 2016

Laid before Parliament

12th December 2016

Coming into force

23rd January 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1) and (6) of the Housing Act 1996[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Rent Officers (Housing Benefit and Universal Credit Functions) (Local Housing Allowance Amendments) Order 2016 and comes into force on 23rd January 2017.

## 2. Amendment of the Rent Officers (Housing Benefit Functions) Order 1997

(2) In article 4B, after paragraph (1A) (broad rental market area determinations)[^f00003], insert—

> (1B) The power in paragraph (1A) is not limited by paragraph 2(2A) or (2B) of Schedule 3B.

## 3. Amendment of the Rent Officers (Housing Benefit Functions) (Scotland) Order 1997

(1) The Rent Officers (Housing Benefit Functions) (Scotland) Order 1997[^f00005] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 4B, after paragraph (1A) (broad rental market area determinations)[^f00006], insert—

> (1B) The power in paragraph (1A) is not limited by paragraph 2(2A) or (2B) of Schedule 3B.

.

(3) In paragraph 2 of Schedule 3B (local housing allowance determinations)[^f00007]—

- (a) before sub-paragraph (3) insert—

  > (2A) For all broad rental market areas other than Lothian, the local housing allowance for any category of dwelling is the lower of the amounts set out in sub-paragraph (3)(a) and (b).
  >
  > (2B) For the Lothian broad rental market area, the local housing allowance—
  >
  > - (a) for the category of dwelling defined in paragraph 1(1)(b) of this Schedule (one bedroom, exclusive use), is the lower of the amounts set out in sub-paragraph (3A)(a) and (b);
  > - (b) for all other categories of dwelling, is the lower of the amounts set out in sub-paragraph (3)(a) and (b).

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (3)—
  - (i) for the words before paragraph (a) substitute “The amounts referred to in sub-paragraphs (2A) and (2B)(b) are—”;
  - (ii) at the end of paragraph (a), for “or” substitute “and”;
  - (iii) in paragraph (b), omit “, where that rent is lower than the allowance referred to in paragraph (a)”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (3) insert—

  > (3A) The amounts referred to in (2B)(a) are—
  >
  > - (a) the local housing allowance determined for that category of dwelling on 30th January 2015 (or, where the determination is amended under article 7A(4) (errors), the allowance provided for in the amended determination), plus 3%; and
  > - (b) £260.64.

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Rent Officers (Housing Benefit Functions) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1984)), the Rent Officers (Housing Benefit Functions) (Scotland) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1995)) and the Rent Officers (Universal Credit Functions) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/382](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/382)) (“the Orders”) to make changes to the manner in which a local housing allowance is determined.

Articles 2, 3 and 4 amend the Orders to provide that, for dwellings in specified categories in specified broad rental market areas, the local housing allowance is as currently determined but with an uplift of 3%. These articles further provide that for these dwellings, there is a maximum local housing allowance.

Further, article 4 amends [S.I. 2013/382](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/382) to provide that, when making a housing payment determination in respect of accommodation, a rent officer may have regard to certain local authority areas other than those adjoining the local authority area in which that accommodation is situated.

An impact assessment has not been provided for this instrument as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations.
