# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89.[^M_E_ad36fac8-a566-4911-f985-abf06a2ab369]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of these Regulations, a certificate issued, or approval granted, by a notified body, recognised third party organisation or user inspectorate under the 1999 Regulations, or any enactment of another member State which implemented Directive [97/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/23) [^c23178281], is to be treated as a certificate issued or approval granted under Annex II to the Directive (as amended from time to time).

## 89.[^M_E_a307c263-7160-4ccb-c9be-6196e9440359]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of these Regulations, a certificate issued, or approval granted, by a notified body, recognised third party organisation or user inspectorate under the 1999 Regulations, or any enactment of another relevant state[^key-a5708d580b0d635b82b056a53e975d35] which implemented Directive [97/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/23) [^V_NI_ff3efd71-2de7-3a71-0806-211f0674e115], is to be treated as a certificate issued or approval granted under Annex II to the Directive (as amended from time to time).
