# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Requirement for pressure equipment and assemblies to comply with sound engineering practice[^M_E_e747e759-5889-4010-e15f-4b4c1513cebc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to pressure equipment and assemblies to which these Regulations apply and which are below or equal to the limits set out in regulations 6(a) to (c) or regulation 7, as applicable.

(2) Pressure equipment and assemblies to which this regulation applies must be—

- (a) designed and manufactured in accordance with sound engineering practice[^c-d9d88ef8c0471a626699fcca1e124cf7] in order to ensure safe use; and
- (b) accompanied by adequate instructions for use.

(3)[^c-3fa829b15521e7bbd237d6464c7df1e4] Pressure equipment and assemblies to which this regulation applies must not bear the UK marking referred to in regulation 49 unless required to do so by other applicable UK legislation.

(4) In this regulation, “**safe**” means that the pressure equipment or assembly, when properly installed and maintained and used for its intended purpose, is not liable to endanger the health or safety of persons and, where applicable, domestic animals or property.

## 8. Requirement for pressure equipment and assemblies to comply with sound engineering practice[^M_E_aa11d2b8-3d37-46e3-b0cd-ed37e001b336]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to pressure equipment and assemblies to which these Regulations apply and which are below or equal to the limits set out in regulations 6(a) to (c) or regulation 7, as applicable.

(2) Pressure equipment and assemblies to which this regulation applies must be—

- (a) designed and manufactured in accordance with the sound engineering practice of a relevant state[^c-766f5d9cfc28c1a3a0767b18af9c591a] in order to ensure safe use; and
- (b) accompanied by adequate instructions for use.

(3) Pressure equipment or assemblies to which this regulation applies must not bear the CE marking referred to in Regulation 49 unless required to do so by other applicable EU legislation.

(4) In this regulation, “**safe**” means that the pressure equipment or assembly, when properly installed and maintained and used for its intended purpose, is not liable to endanger the health or safety of persons and, where applicable, domestic animals or property.
