# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 47.[^M_E_24265bda-72c6-4cdc-c8e8-7bb3314cbec8][^c-9f2bdf04e8e77b55b1ec8df328a51eb0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The records and correspondence relating to conformity assessment must be clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 47.[^M_E_d54cd826-b944-487c-8fb3-531ef71b7784]

*Extent: N.I.*

The records and correspondence relating to conformity assessment must be in an official language of the relevant state[^c-6d50c6bc07ac74c1904acc0b925d1332] where the body responsible for carrying out such conformity assessment procedures is established, or in a language accepted by that body.
