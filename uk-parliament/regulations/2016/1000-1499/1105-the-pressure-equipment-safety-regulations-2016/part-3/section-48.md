# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 48. ... Declaration of conformity[^M_E_96ef59fa-e47a-4577-a24c-82b495d07b9b][^c-a701f056d2558ec8df74288bd08ac058]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^c-a3cf149094d5b45be122e985f7de1c47]... declaration of conformity in respect of pressure equipment or an assembly must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of pressure equipment;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Schedule 1A to these Regulations[^c-3c550fc348161bed22c821ff097d3801] for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the pressure equipment or assembly; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 11.

## 48. EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_4496d01c-379f-4b4b-917e-13af5038856a]

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity in respect of pressure equipment or an assembly must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of pressure equipment;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Annex III to the Directive (as amended from time to time) for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the pressure equipment or assembly; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 11.
