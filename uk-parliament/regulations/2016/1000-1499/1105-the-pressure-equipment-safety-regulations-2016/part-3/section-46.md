# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 41 to 45 do not apply to pressure equipment items and assemblies which are made available on the market or put into service solely in the interests of experimentation.
