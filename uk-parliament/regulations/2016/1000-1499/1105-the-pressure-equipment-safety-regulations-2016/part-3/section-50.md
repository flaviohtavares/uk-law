# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). European approval for materials (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60][^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] For the purpose of this regulation, an issuing body is a body which has been notified under regulation 55 (notification) specifically in relation to the activity of issuing European approval for materials. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] European approval for materials must be issued, at the request of one or more manufacturers of materials or equipment, by an issuing body. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] The issuing body must determine and perform, or arrange for the performance of, the appropriate inspections and tests to certify the conformity of the types of material with the corresponding requirements of these Regulations. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] In the case of materials recognised as being safe to use before 29 November 1999, the issuing body must take account of the existing data when certifying such conformity. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] Prior to issuing a European approval for materials, the issuing body must inform the other *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] relevant states[^c-5e7d66ea00ec90b9b9a6b666da5b1089] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] and the Commission by sending them the appropriate information. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] Where, within three months of being informed by the issuing body, a *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] relevant state[^c-3db52510589f324ee24e80a2f04a36aa] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] or the Commission provides comments with reasons, the issuing body must take those comments into account before issuing the European approval for materials. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] A copy of the European approval for materials must be sent to the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] relevant states[^c-624021905ffd08bbae6da7e95a95c080] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60], the bodies notified under regulation 55 and the Commission. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(8 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] The issuing body must withdraw its approval if it finds that it should not have been issued or that the type of materials is covered by a harmonised standard. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]

(9 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] If an issuing body withdraws approval for materials under paragraph 8, it must immediately inform the other *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] relevant states[^c-9b014d67635b25ac7f535b45a1721306] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60] and the bodies notified under regulation 55 of that withdrawal. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6809c2fac36df412fdb836f2c3b2ca60]
