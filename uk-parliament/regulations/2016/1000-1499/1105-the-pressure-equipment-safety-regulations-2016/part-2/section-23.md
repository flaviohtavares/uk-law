# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 23. Information identifying importer[^M_E_8b89d9d7-3cfc-48df-c5a8-a1b1b37d9886][^c-8a077072b7f3dc740c8a1d561a750482]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before placing pressure equipment or an assembly on the market, an importer must indicate on the pressure equipment or assembly—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) The information specified in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other users and the market surveillance authority [^c-35288ddf5da26be8454767732fbd1002]....

(3)[^c-7af96550ea2ca1ec533a01496ee0d123] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) it is not possible to set out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the pressure equipment or assembly, or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the pressure equipment or assembly from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-a59fc7fa0ed7e2d815ee55238cb527dc] beginning with IP completion day, and
- (b) before placing the pressure equipment or assembly on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the packaging of the pressure equipment or assembly or in a document accompanying the pressure equipment or assembly.

## 23. Information identifying importer[^M_E_9d5d785d-2391-4d24-a77f-c16357957a4a][^V_NI_c9827ce0-79f2-f6d0-4add-fef21bb7d2c9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before placing pressure equipment or an assembly on the market, an importer must indicate on the pressure equipment or assembly—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) The information specified in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other users and the market surveillance authority in the relevant state[^c-8394dfb2e472c25f9d02655618cb152f] in which it is to be made available to such users.

(3) Where it is not possible to indicate the information specified in paragraph (1) on the pressure equipment or assembly[^V_NI_3b49461b-7c92-ae19-179d-9dd441870e2a], the importer must indicate that information—

- (a) on the packaging; or
- (b) in a document accompanying the pressure equipment or assembly.
