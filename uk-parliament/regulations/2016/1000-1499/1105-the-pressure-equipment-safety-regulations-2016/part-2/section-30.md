# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Duty to act with due care

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When making pressure equipment or an assembly available on the market, a distributor must act with due care to ensure that it is in conformity with Part 2.
