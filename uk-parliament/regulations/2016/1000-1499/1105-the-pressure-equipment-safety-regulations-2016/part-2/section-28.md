# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 28. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_aecf717e-35da-4cdc-c6c7-bc2fc168a3d6][^c-f833387e3774b64a2746359847a5569a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which pressure equipment or an assembly is placed on the market, keep and, upon request, make available to an enforcing authority the following in relation to the pressure equipment or assembly—

- (a) a copy of the [^c-4ada3e88c577dd9b4859680a382b21bc]... declaration of conformity; and
- (b) the technical documentation.

## 28. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_946f0288-4b92-4731-e1cb-0f929011381d]

*Extent: N.I.*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which pressure equipment or an assembly is placed on the market, keep and, upon request, make available to an enforcing authority the following in relation to the pressure equipment or assembly—

- (a) a copy of the EU declaration of conformity; and
- (b) the technical documentation.
