# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 17. Duty to take action in respect of pressure equipment or assemblies placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_8a90149f-ecf6-4438-f99f-60118aac6481]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer who considers, or has reason to believe, that pressure equipment or an assembly which that manufacturer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the pressure equipment or assembly into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pressure equipment or assembly; or
- (c) recall the pressure equipment or assembly.

(2) Where pressure equipment or an assembly presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority[^c-a4c7a142a34b3bff5b7bce014323c460]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pressure equipment or assembly is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 17. Duty to take action in respect of pressure equipment or assemblies placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_ef8fec89-cd3c-41dd-c553-556233229424]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer who considers, or has reason to believe, that pressure equipment or an assembly which that manufacturer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the pressure equipment or assembly into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pressure equipment or assembly; or
- (c) recall the pressure equipment or assembly.

(2) Where pressure equipment or an assembly presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority, and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^c-5e0e6d3ae6233683fdb88f7ea717885e] in which the manufacturer made the pressure equipment or assembly available on the market, of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pressure equipment or assembly is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
