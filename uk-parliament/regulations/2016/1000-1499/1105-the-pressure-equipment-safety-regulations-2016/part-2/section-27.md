# The Pressure Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1105
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of pressure equipment or assemblies placed on the market considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_7cf9b2fb-e0f2-4fdf-978a-e31fbb6bf0b0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that pressure equipment or an assembly which that importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring that pressure equipment or assembly into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pressure equipment or assembly; or
- (c) recall the pressure equipment or assembly.

(2) Where the pressure equipment or assembly presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities [^c-0745c234f38aec9ac35929b3cf420938]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pressure equipment or assembly is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of pressure equipment or assemblies placed on the market considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_621ee534-d5c5-4669-b511-a9a0ce6c40bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that pressure equipment or an assembly which that importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring that pressure equipment or assembly into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pressure equipment or assembly; or
- (c) recall the pressure equipment or assembly.

(2) Where the pressure equipment or assembly presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^c-412bf334a9f694b466bd8bd689f0f299] in which the importer made the pressure equipment or assembly available on the market of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pressure equipment or assembly is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
