## 37. Translation of ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_c85842f3-451c-44e7-c6b8-aeee26437397][^c-2e275cce6775d2cc8c402230c8938033]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before making pressure equipment or an assembly available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the [^c-e813d8cab85fa164baaed14072646cc4]... declaration of conformity is in English[^c-dfa33098c2136fa50b4af7dfa5bba177].

(2)[^c-d802ccfa2ef83469c54714ae201b75a1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 37. Translation of EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_7bef0bac-6b8c-4975-d02f-f812341fd4b3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making pressure equipment or an assembly available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the EU declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the relevant state[^c-436f101ca47cd1469339f28ea8c7d26c] in which it is to be made available on the market.

(2) Where the pressure equipment or assembly is to be made available on the market in Northern Ireland[^c-282371e66e41c919904295684aee7369], the language required is English.
