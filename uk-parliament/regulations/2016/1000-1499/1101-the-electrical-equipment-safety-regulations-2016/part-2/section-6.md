# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 6

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_66a093ca-5dd1-492c-a2ed-de5a42e62ff6][^c-e016cf4ffff3b458618d3d2f03ad6de4][^c-26054bf62ae14a96a2e198fa7af52689]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the conformity of electrical equipment with the principal elements of the safety objectives has been demonstrated by the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 2, a manufacturer must, before placing that electrical equipment on the market—

- (a) draw up a declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 38 ([^c-ca41cf6fc5f6c2380c40527a97edf9f6]... declaration of conformity); and
- (b) affix the UK[^c-4faa241946fa546d18c8b71f0f279e19] marking in accordance with regulation 39 (UK marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the [^c-6d79b75347f94172d952962369792472]... declaration of conformity up to date.

(3)[^c-cf911b5347a3de112a05d198ba42b354] Where electrical equipment is subject to more than one enactment requiring a declaration of conformity to be drawn up, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.

## 6. EU declaration of conformity and CE marking[^M_E_98b6f792-5018-4a81-f037-ab10ac86a1bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the conformity of electrical equipment with the principal elements of the safety objectives has been demonstrated by the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 2, a manufacturer must, before placing that electrical equipment on the market—

- (a) draw up a declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 38 (EU declaration of conformity); and
- (b) affix the CE marking in accordance with regulation 39 (CE marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the EU declaration of conformity up to date.

(3) Where electrical equipment is subject to more than one NI Protocol obligation[^V_NI_8cad4ec9-11b1-ffdf-699a-d2dae2c8f7c3] requiring a declaration of conformity to be drawn up, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which—

- (a) identifies the relevant[^V_NI_90cf0829-1f25-6840-8e30-91a974582b7f] EU instruments; and
- (b) includes references to the publication of those EU instruments in the Official Journal.

(4)[^V_NI_1beec0b7-2810-5337-ec77-0ac9674fab0c] In paragraph (3) “**NI Protocol obligation**” means any obligation created or arising by or under the Protocol on Ireland/ Northern Ireland in the EU withdrawal agreement, whether or not an obligation to which section 7A(2) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 applies.
