# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 29

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 29. Duty to take action in respect of electrical equipment made available on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_4fdd708f-cd87-49ff-af37-f86d737b55a4][^c-d711a83eb008d7e5b1b15a32d08d9057]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A distributor who considers, or has reason to believe, that electrical equipment which that distributor has made available[^c-dc5761f8b9e88f190ca1dab625dff710] on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must ensure that the necessary corrective measures are taken to—

- (a) bring that electrical equipment into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the electrical equipment; or
- (c) recall the electrical equipment.

(2) Where the electrical equipment presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^c-61fe1572a0eade8edca887534815a6b5]... of that risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the electrical equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 29. Duty to take action in respect of electrical equipment made available on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_fe3aab65-56c5-41b6-b4f5-db55aa7f3198][^V_NI_284c8fd9-db76-f961-cd11-2fcef242b68a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A distributor who considers, or has reason to believe, that electrical equipment which that distributor has made available[^V_NI_2d561423-8cb7-95b4-ca86-13862260d95d] on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must ensure that the necessary corrective measures are taken to—

- (a) bring that electrical equipment into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the electrical equipment; or
- (c) recall the electrical equipment.

(2) Where the electrical equipment presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of the other relevant states[^V_NI_c716d29c-6d91-a6bc-ee0d-edca365757f2] in which the distributor has made the electrical equipment available on the market, of that risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the electrical equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
