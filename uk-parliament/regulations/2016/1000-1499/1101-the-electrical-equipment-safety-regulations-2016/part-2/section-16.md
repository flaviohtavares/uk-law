# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 16

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places electrical equipment on the market[^M_E_d0e828e3-4e6c-4b75-cee0-e6808a189c2d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing electrical equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 2 has been carried out;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c) the electrical equipment bears the UK[^c-1529109390e99e1881d70b754a5d9511] marking; and
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment).

## 16. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places electrical equipment on the market[^M_E_766a96b4-47e5-467c-cf3c-3e73331ccd14]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing electrical equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 2 has been carried out;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c) the electrical equipment bears the CE marking; and
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment).
