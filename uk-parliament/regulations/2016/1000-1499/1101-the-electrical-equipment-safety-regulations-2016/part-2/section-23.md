# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 23

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Duty to take action in respect of electrical equipment placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_fddacc46-e830-4d90-9825-96316378583a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that electrical equipment which that importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring that electrical equipment into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the electrical equipment; or
- (c) recall the electrical equipment.

(2) Where the electrical equipment presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^key-944fcdae7683b79dc450660d1f44d838]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the electrical equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 23. Duty to take action in respect of electrical equipment placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_5b6c34c4-48ed-4df4-b680-46796661a4bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that electrical equipment which that importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring that electrical equipment into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the electrical equipment; or
- (c) recall the electrical equipment.

(2) Where the electrical equipment presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^V_NI_c172c468-cacf-b690-265b-cab315066c2b] in which the manufacturer made the electrical equipment available on the market of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the electrical equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
