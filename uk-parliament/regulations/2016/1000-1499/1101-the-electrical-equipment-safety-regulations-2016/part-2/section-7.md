# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 7

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Retention of technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^c-ac7cddf1e5c22dc66af7d7d488c29d4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2f34935cfc9657d96fbe67ec6add1418] declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of electrical equipment and make them available for inspection by the market surveillance authority for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the electrical equipment is placed on the market.
