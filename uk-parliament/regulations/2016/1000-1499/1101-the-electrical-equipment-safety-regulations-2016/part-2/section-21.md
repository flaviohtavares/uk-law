# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 21

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Retention of technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^key-07cf5d7d3babed51e9183cecb0640131]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which electrical equipment is placed on the market, keep and, upon request, make available to an enforcing authority the following in relation to the electrical equipment—

- (a) a copy of the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a825844e4e42d126a1e78ce1af2db565] declaration of conformity; and
- (b) the technical documentation.
