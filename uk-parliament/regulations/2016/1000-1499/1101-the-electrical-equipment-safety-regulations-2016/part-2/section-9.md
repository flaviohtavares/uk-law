# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 9

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_f66d1473-df63-4c51-f5a2-bedd800d856f][^c-293182e0673ef41a4a5b3ca981c0ed77]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When placing electrical equipment on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 9. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_f1299772-dac2-4de6-835c-bc84c5b6ae11]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing electrical equipment on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users.

(2) Such instructions and safety information must be clear, understandable and intelligible.

(3) Where the electrical equipment is placed on the market in Northern Ireland[^c-9456e459e0b059298bddd60d011d3cce], the language referred to in paragraph (1) must be English.
