# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 26

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes electrical equipment available on the market[^M_E_5e859bf5-3c67-4dfd-9bb8-7e2ae5859090]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before making electrical equipment available on the market, the distributor must verify that—

- (a) the electrical equipment—
  - (i) bears the UK[^c-f3f45b199c6b03d01ad9222b3b228f3c] marking;
  - (ii) is accompanied by the required documents;
  - (iii) is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and easily understandable English;[^c-46e07b8e2673dc3637e6c7f07e96e5d9]
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment);
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements of regulation 18 (information identifying importer).

(2) In paragraph (1)(a)(ii), “**required documents**” means the documents that the manufacturer or importer is required to provide with the electrical equipment pursuant to—

- (a) regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment);
- (b) regulation 9 or 19 (instructions and safety information); and
- (c) regulation 18 (information identifying importer).

## 26. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes electrical equipment available on the market[^M_E_d5218828-5792-4f7f-ea81-f74656a1e0fd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making electrical equipment available on the market, the distributor must verify that—

- (a) the electrical equipment—
  - (i) bears the CE marking;
  - (ii) is accompanied by the required documents;
  - (iii) is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users in the relevant state[^V_NI_62058040-b498-229d-ca5c-c343117591ef] in which the electrical equipment is to be made available on the market;
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements of regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment);
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements of regulation 18 (information identifying importer).

(2) In paragraph (1)(a)(ii), “**required documents**” means the documents that the manufacturer or importer is required to provide with the electrical equipment pursuant to—

- (a) regulation 8 (labelling of electrical equipment);
- (b) regulation 9 or 19 (instructions and safety information); and
- (c) regulation 18 (information identifying importer).

(3) Where the electrical equipment is to be made available on the market in Northern Ireland[^V_NI_ef8b8bb8-6159-b888-d8bc-9098a1a28d67], the language referred to in paragraph (1)(a)(iii) must be English.
