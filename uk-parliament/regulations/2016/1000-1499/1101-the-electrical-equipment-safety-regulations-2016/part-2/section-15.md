# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 15

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Prohibition on placing on the market electrical equipment which is not in conformity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must not place electrical equipment on the market unless it is in conformity with Part 2.
