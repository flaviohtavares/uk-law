# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 19

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 19. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_eb93f1d1-f607-4748-beb0-fba2330862a7][^c-b5fa097345272e7bbe872d824cf04b47]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When placing electrical equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 19. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_7ca44ed6-2916-4042-ca85-6d469bee476f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing electrical equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users.

(2) Where the electrical equipment is placed on the market in Northern Ireland[^V_NI_5040c285-85ca-eb32-c613-6b7251bd223f], the language referred to in paragraph (1) must be English.
