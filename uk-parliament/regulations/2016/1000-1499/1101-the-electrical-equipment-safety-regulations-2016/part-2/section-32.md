# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 32

> Text as at 2024-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Translation of declaration of conformity[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] Before making electrical equipment available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] relevant state[^key-a27f3c2c3fac14699443eb19268432e0] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] in which it is to be made available on the market. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] Where the electrical equipment is to be made available on the market in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295] Northern Ireland[^key-4bcae457635434dad2dd4e88fe4c6bef] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295], the language required is English. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-63c0281d08ebd887dad12db3964d5295]
