# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 50

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of regulations 4 to 12, 13(4), 15 to 23, 24(4)[^c-429f4195243b772419f6ee1034bac4d5], 25 to 29, 30(3) or 33 to 35.

(2) It is an offence for any person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of a withdrawal or recall notice served on that person by an enforcing authority under these Regulations.
