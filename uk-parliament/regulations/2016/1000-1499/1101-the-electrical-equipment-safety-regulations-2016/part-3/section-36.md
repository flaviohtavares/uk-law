# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Presumption of conformity on the basis of designated standards[^M_E_905bdab0-d3c6-4633-e810-e7655f3379b4][^key-4a7d7723f6abc5890962ec729159864c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Electrical equipment which is in conformity with a designated[^key-9fa958bf90e4d9d21df7c2f7105a3275] standard (or part of such a standard) [^key-90586ca0fd0b44f70891e3794095c8ae]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the principal elements of the safety objectives covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 36. Presumption of conformity on the basis of harmonised standards[^M_E_749f1328-54a5-4de2-858e-ac48ad6ee7f3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Electrical equipment which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal is to be presumed to be in conformity with the principal elements of the safety objectives covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
