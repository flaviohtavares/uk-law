# The Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 38

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Declaration of conformity[^M_E_e3d76a3c-430c-4bd2-c0be-59060102c410][^key-769b6351c4b8c73da24c5edf028e2b7d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^key-d96a957130610268f1a3b69bbc64c064]... declaration of conformity for electrical equipment must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the principal elements of the safety objectives has been demonstrated in respect of the electrical equipment;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 8; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in Schedule 2 for Module A Internal production control.

## 38. EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_cac59188-7ec5-40e0-8293-e5eca53daf02]

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity for electrical equipment must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the principal elements of the safety objectives has been demonstrated in respect of the electrical equipment;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 8; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in Schedule 2 for Module A Internal production control.
