# The Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016, section 7.108A

> Text as at 2026-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7.108A. Priority of moratorium debts in subsequent winding up[^c-e1dfd5b5f6475b470bbb3106f04eff83]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where section 174A applies the moratorium debts and priority pre-moratorium debts mentioned in subsection (2)(b) of that section are payable in the following order of priority—

- (a) amounts payable in respect of goods or services supplied during the moratorium under a contract where, but for section 233B(3) or (4), the supplier would not have had to make that supply,
- (b) wages or salary arising under contract of employment,
- (c) other debts or other liabilities apart from the monitor’s remuneration or expenses, and
- (d) the monitor’s remuneration or expenses.
