# The Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016, SCHEDULE 11

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 11 — Determination of insolvency office-holder's remuneration

*Extent: E+W*

Rule 18.22

This table sets out the realisation and distribution scales for determining the remuneration of trustees and liquidators.

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong><em>The realisation scale</em></strong></th></tr>
<tr><td>on the first £5,000</td><td>20%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on the next £5,000</td><td>15%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on the next £90,000</td><td>10%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on all further sums realised</td><td>5%</td></tr>
<tr><td></td><td></td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong><em>The distribution scale</em></strong></th></tr>
<tr><td>on the first £5,000</td><td>10%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on the next £5,000</td><td>7.5%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on the next £90,000</td><td>5%</td></tr>
<tr><td>on all further sums distributed</td><td>2.5%.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
