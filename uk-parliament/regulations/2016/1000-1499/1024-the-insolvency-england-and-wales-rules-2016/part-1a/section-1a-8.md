# The Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016, section 1A.8

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1A.8. Moratorium application (sections A4 and A5)[^key-02a8ddbbe79f9845ba59d3ad2c51c222]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for a moratorium under sections A4 or A5 must—

- (a) specify the date on which the application is filed, and
- (b) be accompanied by the relevant documents (as to which see section A6).

(2) The application must be endorsed by the court with the date and time of filing.
