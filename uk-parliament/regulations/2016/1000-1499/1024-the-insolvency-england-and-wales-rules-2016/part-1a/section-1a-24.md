# The Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016, section 1A.24

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1A.24. Debts that are to be disregarded for the purposes of section A38(1)(d) of the Act[^key-02a8ddbbe79f9845ba59d3ad2c51c222]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of deciding whether to bring a moratorium to an end under section A38(1)(d) the monitor must disregard—

- (a) any debts that the monitor has reasonable grounds for thinking are likely to be—
  - (i) paid, or
  - (ii) compounded to the satisfaction of the creditor,

  within five business days of the decision, and
- (b) any debts in respect of which the creditor has agreed to defer payment until a time that is later than the decision.
