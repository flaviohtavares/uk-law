> Text as at 2026-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1A.16. Directions[^key-02a8ddbbe79f9845ba59d3ad2c51c222]

*Extent: E+W*

The court may at any time give such directions as it thinks just as to service of the notice of extension on or to any person.
