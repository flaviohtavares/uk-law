# The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1104

Infrastructure Planning

The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2016

Made

16th November 2016

Coming into force

17th November 2016

The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016[^f00001] (the “Order”), which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (the “Act”), contained correctable errors within the meaning of the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received, before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of that Schedule, a written request from SMart Wind Limited to correct errors and omissions in the Order. In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule, the Secretary of State informed East Lindsey District Council, North East Lincolnshire Council, North Lincolnshire Council, West Lindsey District Council and Lincolnshire County Council that the request had been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(4) and (8) of that Schedule, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2016.

(2) This Order comes into force on 17th November 2016.

## 2. Corrections

The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made; and
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Giles Scott

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Coal Liabilities

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

16th November 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from SMart Wind Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
