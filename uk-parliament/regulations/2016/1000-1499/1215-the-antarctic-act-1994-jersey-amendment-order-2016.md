# The Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1215

Antarctica

The Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 2016

Made

14th December 2016

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2016

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by sections 34(2) and (3) of the Antarctic Act 1994[^f00001], and 18(2)(b) of the Antarctic Act 2013[^f00002] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 2016 and shall come into force on the seventh day after it is registered by the Royal Court of Jersey.

## 2. Amendment of the Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) Order 1995

(1) The Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) Order 1995[^f00003] is amended as provided in paragraph (2).

(2) In article 2, after “the Antarctic Act 1994” insert “as amended by sections 14, 15 and 16 of the Antarctic Act 2013”.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Antarctic Act 1994 (Jersey) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/1034](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1034)), which extended the Antarctic Act [1994 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/15) to Jersey with certain modifications, to extend the amendments made to that Act by Part 2 of the Antarctic Act [2013 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/15) to Jersey.
