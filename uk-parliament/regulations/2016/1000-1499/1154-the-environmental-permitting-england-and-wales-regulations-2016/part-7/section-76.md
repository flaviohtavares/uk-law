# The Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016, section 76

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Amendment of the Transfrontier Shipment of Waste Regulations 2007

*Extent: E+W*

For regulation 16 of the Transfrontier Shipment of Waste Regulations 2007 [^c23192871], substitute—

> **16. The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011**
>
> The reference to a waste management plan in regulation 7 of the Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011 [^c23192881] includes a waste management plan made under this Part.
