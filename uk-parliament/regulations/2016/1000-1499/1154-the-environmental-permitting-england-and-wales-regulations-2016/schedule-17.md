## SCHEDULE 17 — Titanium dioxide: Industrial Emissions Directive

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 35(1)

### 1. Application

This Schedule applies in relation to every installation in which titanium dioxide is produced.

### 3. Exercise of relevant functions

The regulator must exercise its relevant functions so as to ensure compliance with the following provisions of the Industrial Emissions Directive—

- (a) Article 67;
- (b) Article 68;
- (c) Article 69;
- (d) Article 70.
