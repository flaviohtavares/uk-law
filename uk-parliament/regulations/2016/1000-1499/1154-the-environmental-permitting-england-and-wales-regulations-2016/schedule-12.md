## SCHEDULE 12 — Waste electrical and electronic equipment

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 35(1)

### 1. Application

This Schedule applies in relation to WEEE which is within the scope of the WEEE Directive by virtue of Article 2 of that Directive.
