# The Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016, section 35

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Specific provisions applying to environmental permits

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedules 7 to 25B[^key-0ec5a59b2a46537cce43a83cc45bd81d] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-49528f999252394af2ffdc57cd0b9c92]25C *(Extent: E)* have effect.

(2) To the extent that the operation of a regulated facility of a description or class mentioned in any of Schedules 7 to 25B *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-59026578d2ea7d4f3b5ee2087d34e700]25C *(Extent: E)* requires an environmental permit, the requirements of that Schedule apply in relation to that regulated facility.
