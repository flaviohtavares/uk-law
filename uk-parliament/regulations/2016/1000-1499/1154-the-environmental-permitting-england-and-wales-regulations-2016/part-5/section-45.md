## 45. Interpretation of this Part

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**final confidentiality decision**” means—
  - (a) a determination under regulation 50, or
  - (b) the determination or withdrawal of an appeal in relation to a determination under regulation 50;
- “**the information subject**” means the person to whom information relates;
- “**objection notice**” means a notice given under regulation 48(1)(b).
