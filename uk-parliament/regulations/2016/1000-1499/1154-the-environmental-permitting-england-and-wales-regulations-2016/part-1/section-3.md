# The Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (74).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1154

## 3. Interpretation: Directives

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations—

- “**the Asbestos Directive**” means Council Directive [87/217/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1987/217) on the prevention and reduction of environmental pollution by asbestos [^c23192631], as read in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 1A[^c-c3868b56f4da995c772a85256e4d414d];
- “the Basic Safety Standards Directive” means Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom laying down basic safety standards for the protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation[^c-dc168dd961a791848ad2466a0bc09648], as read in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 1A[^c-29b3377ddec57c7be90e7ba9b6928859];
- “**the Batteries Directive**” means Directive [2006/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/66) of the European Parliament and of the Council on batteries and accumulators and waste batteries and accumulators [^c23192651] , as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/849[^c-e5e156e1fd7d9c151dea15e665943e6a], as read in accordance with paragraph 3 of Schedule 1A[^c-6b51dd8557320765d89b0be7ae6e444f];
- “**the End-of-Life Vehicles Directive**” means Directive [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/53) of the European Parliament and of the Council on end-of-life vehicles [^c23192661] , as last amended by Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2020/363[^c-f520052cc3716f549374062b4c011d00], as read in accordance with paragraph 4 of Schedule 1A[^c-80a4dfa0fbc17dac79d1bff3f195f48c];
- “**the Energy Efficiency Directive**” means Directive 2012/27/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on energy efficiency [^c23192671] , as read in accordance with paragraph 5 of Schedule 1A[^c-dc769f79e6a296fefa5495b8f3bc70ca];
- [^c-a43021b6f67f2a92d6926b6275958568]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-549036850afbd35cd6ee910938e0d3c9]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**the Industrial Emissions Directive**” means Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control) [^c23192701] , as read in accordance with paragraph 6 of Schedule 1A[^c-b5f96c3c7b2279538a197d23d5c50ed0];
- “**the Landfill Directive**” means Council Directive [1999/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/31) on the landfill of waste [^c23192711] , as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/850[^c-6648346fa124916c836cf6a8808f29bd], as read with Council Decision [2003/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2003/33) establishing criteria and procedures for the acceptance of waste at landfills pursuant to Article 16 of, and Annex II to, Directive [1999/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/31) [^c23192721], and in accordance with paragraph 7 of Schedule 1A[^c-11ef0179e6d758c1b61b0935262d4b27];
- “the Medium Combustion Plant Directive” means[^c-3c169fce806a6230ce41ee7422ee3df2] [Directive 2015/2193/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2015/2193) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from medium combustion plants, as read in accordance with paragraph 8 of Schedule 1A[^c-fe4faaf9d96f518d046e913c1fdddf57];
- “**the Mining Waste Directive**” means Directive [2006/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/21) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the management of waste from extractive industries [^c23192731], as read in accordance with paragraph 9 of Schedule 1A[^c-3255cf6b46b03fa4a685fe97dc191d24];
- “**PVR I**” means European Parliament and Council Directive [94/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1994/63) on the control of volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions resulting from the storage of petrol and its distribution from terminals to service stations [^c23192741], as read in accordance with paragraph 10 of Schedule 1A[^c-51a30e779e8a435256a3e9aa6ea57329];
- “**PVR II**” means Directive [2009/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/126) of the European Parliament and of the Council on Stage II petrol vapour recovery during refuelling of motor vehicles at service stations [^c23192751], as read in accordance with paragraph 11 of Schedule 1A[^c-72a52d6a0e80b5a3ef24d721abb8985a];
- “**the Waste Framework Directive**” means Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste , as last amended by[^c-415a9e2de63795a53818d3c7eb4d7d76] Directive (EU) 2018/851[^c-445d73a1eeae16982c31d40b02aaeb3f], and as read in accordance with paragraph 12 of Schedule 1A[^c-e49c25e0a9e191f9ec44b03b982447fc];
- “**the Water Framework Directive**” means Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/60) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy [^c23192771], as read in accordance with paragraph 13 of Schedule 1A[^c-94d1e302c870254f2b35146a5bb94dd8];
- “**the WEEE Directive**” means Directive 2012/19/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) [^c23192781] , as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/849[^c-1154dd7abe8bd00da26bbf46855cfd2b] , as read in accordance with paragraph 14 of Schedule 1A[^c-55dea79f2eb540373bc8fcad6858aa3b].
