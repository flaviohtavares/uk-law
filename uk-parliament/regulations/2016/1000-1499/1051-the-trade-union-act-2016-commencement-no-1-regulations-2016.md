# The Trade Union Act 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1051

Trade Unions

The Trade Union Act 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2016

Made

1st November 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25 of the Trade Union Act 2016[^f00001] makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Union Act 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the 2016 Act” means the Trade Union Act 2016; and
- “the 1992 Act” means the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00002].

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 3rd November 2016

The following provisions of the 2016 Act come into force on 3rd November 2016—

- (a) section 1 (meaning of “the 1992 Act”);
- (b) section 4 (review of electronic balloting).

Margot James

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Small Business, Consumers and Corporate Responsibility

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

1st November 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions of the Trade Union Act [2016 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/15) (“the 2016 Act”). These are the first commencement regulations made under the 2016 Act. Certain provisions came into force on the passing of the 2016 Act.

Regulation 3 commences two provisions of the 2016 Act that:

- provide for references to the 1992 Act to mean the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992; and
- provide for a review into electronic balloting.
