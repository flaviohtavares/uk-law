# The National Police Records (Recordable Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an omission in [SI 2001/1139](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1139) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

Made

17th October 2016

Laid before Parliament

20th October 2016

Coming into force

14th November 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(4) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 14th November 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000

(1) The Schedule to the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 3 insert—

> **3A.** section 31(1)(c) of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00003] (offence of racially or religiously aggravated harassment, alarm or distress);

.

Brandon Lewis

Minister of State

Home Office

17th October 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000 to make an offence contrary to section 31(1)(c) of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (racially or religiously aggravated harassment, alarm or distress) a recordable offence.
