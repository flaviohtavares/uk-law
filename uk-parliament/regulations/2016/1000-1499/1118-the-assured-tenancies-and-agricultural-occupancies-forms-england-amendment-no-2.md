# The Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1118

Landlord And Tenant, England

The Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2016

Made

17th November 2016

Coming into force

1st December 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(3), and 45(1) and (5) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 1st December 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) Regulations 2015

(1) The Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) Regulations 2015[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) For Form No. 3 in the Schedule to those Regulations substitute the Form No. 3 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (England) Regulations 2015 ([SI 2015/620](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/620)) (“the principal regulations”).

Regulation 2 substitutes a new Form No. 3 for the equivalent form prescribed in the principal regulations.

Amendments have been made to Form No. 3 to reflect the new ground for possession under section 8 of the Housing Act 1988, ground 7B, which was introduced by section 41 of the Immigration Act 2016.
