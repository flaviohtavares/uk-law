# The Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1213

Ecclesiastical Law

The Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2016

Made

12th December 2016

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12 of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2016.

## 2. Commencement

All the provisions of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 which are not already in force shall come into force on 1st January 2017.

\+Justin Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

12th December 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 2017 all the provisions of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 which are not already in force.
