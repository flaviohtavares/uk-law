# The Transport for Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Trafford Park Extension) Order 2016, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. As to Trafford Park railways

(1) In constructing the authorised works, the undertaker may remove any track or other apparatus within the limits of deviation.

(2) Nothing in the Trafford Park Railway enactments is to be taken to permit any railway to be carried across or to permit any other interference with the construction or operation of the transit system authorised by this Order without the consent of the undertaker, but such consent must not be unreasonably withheld.

(3) In this article, the Trafford Park Railway enactments means the West Manchester Light Railway Orders 1899 to 1906 made under the Light Railways Act 1896[^f00021], the Trafford Park Act 1904[^f00022], the Trafford Park Act 1922[^f00023], the Trafford Park Railway Order 1995[^f00024] and the Trafford Park Railway Order 2000[^f00025].
