# The Transport for Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Trafford Park Extension) Order 2016, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Disapplication of the Commons Act 2006

No land within the limits of land to be acquired or used may be registered in accordance with paragraphs 2 to 4 of Schedule 2 (non-registration or mistaken registration under the 1965 Act) to the Commons Act 2006[^f00020].
