# The Transport for Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Trafford Park Extension) Order 2016, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application of enactments relating to railways

(1) The provisions of the Railway Regulation Acts 1840 to 1893[^f00013] do not apply in relation to the authorised transit system.

(2) The provisions of the Highways (Railway Crossings) Act 1839[^f00014] do not apply in relation to the authorised transit system.
