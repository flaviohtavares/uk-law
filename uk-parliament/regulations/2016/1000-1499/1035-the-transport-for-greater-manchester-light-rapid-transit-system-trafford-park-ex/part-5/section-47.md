# The Transport for Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Trafford Park Extension) Order 2016, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Protection of navigation

The provisions of Schedule 9 (protection of navigation) have effect for the protection of the Manchester Ship Canal Company Limited and the Bridgewater Canal Company Limited.
