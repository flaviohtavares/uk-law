# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Lifts where risks are wholly or partly covered by other enactments[^M_E_0c8a019c-359e-402a-8105-2fc24af2fd66][^key-02c1a92012357a754844c64320c95c99]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations do not apply to a lift or a safety component for lifts insofar as and to the extent that the essential health and safety requirements relate to risks wholly or partly covered by other specific enactments[^key-6cdaec30a4e8ce72481c07fb038661f8] applicable to that lift or safety component.

## 5. Lifts where risks are wholly or partly covered by other NI Protocol obligation[^M_E_1a9fb01f-2fca-4544-f553-d020bbb9757f][^key-44d1326dc121b38874940ccd6b25a7ac]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations do not apply to a lift or a safety component for lifts insofar as and to the extent that the essential health and safety requirements relate to risks wholly or partly covered by other specific NI Protocol obligation[^key-99d3debe3736253dad5b178bb19fff82] applicable to that lift or safety component.
