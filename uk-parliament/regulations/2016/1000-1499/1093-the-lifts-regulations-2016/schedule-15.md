# The Lifts Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 15

## SCHEDULE 15 (Extent: E+W+S) — CONFORMITY BASED ON UNIT VERIFICATION FOR LIFTS (Annex VIII to the Directive) (Extent: E+W+S)[^key-bab6ba6bb63318b273db6356bd96a3eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 47 and 50 *(Extent: E+W+S)*

### MODULE G (Extent: E+W+S)

**1 (Extent: E+W+S).** Conformity based on unit verification is the conformity assessment procedure whereby an approved body assesses whether a lift complies with the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 2 (Extent: E+W+S). Obligations of the installer (Extent: E+W+S)

**2 (Extent: E+W+S).1.** The installer shall take all measures necessary so that the manufacturing process and its monitoring ensure conformity of the lift with the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**2 (Extent: E+W+S).2.** The installer shall apply to a single approved body of his choice for unit verification. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The application shall contain: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the name and address of the installer, and if the application is lodged by the authorised representative, his name and address as well; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the location where the lift is installed; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) a written declaration to the effect that a similar application has not been lodged with another approved body; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) the technical documentation. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).** The technical documentation shall allow an assessment of the conformity of the lift with the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The technical documentation shall contain at least the following elements: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a description of the lift; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) design and manufacturing drawings and diagrams; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) explanations necessary for the understanding of those drawings and diagrams and of the operation of the lift; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) a list of the essential health and safety requirements taken into consideration; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (e (Extent: E+W+S)) a list of the designated standards applied in full or in part and, where those designated standards have not been applied, descriptions of the solutions adopted to meet the essential health and safety requirements of these Regulations, including a list of other relevant technical specifications applied. In the event of partly applied designated standards, the technical documentation shall specify the parts which have been applied; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (f (Extent: E+W+S)) a copy of the Type examination certificates of the safety components for lifts incorporated in the lift; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (g (Extent: E+W+S)) results of design calculations performed by or for the installer; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (h (Extent: E+W+S)) test reports; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (i (Extent: E+W+S)) a copy of the instructions referred to in point 7.2 of Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 4 (Extent: E+W+S). Verification (Extent: E+W+S)

The approved body chosen by the installer shall examine the technical documentation and the lift and carry out the appropriate tests as set out in the relevant designated standard(s), or equivalent tests, to check its conformity with the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. The tests shall include at least the tests referred to in point 3.3 of Schedule 12. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

If the lift meets the essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1 the approved body shall issue a certificate of conformity relating to the tests carried out. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The approved body shall fill in the corresponding pages of the logbook referred to in point 7.2 of Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

If the approved body refuses to issue the certificate of conformity, it shall state in detail its reasons for refusal and indicate the necessary corrective measures to be taken. When the installer reapplies for unit verification he shall apply to the same approved body. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

On request, the approved body shall provide the Secretary of State with a copy of the certificate of conformity. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 5 (Extent: E+W+S). UK marking and Declaration of conformity (Extent: E+W+S)

**5 (Extent: E+W+S).1.** The installer shall affix the UK marking in the car of each lift which satisfies the essential health and safety requirements of these Regulations, and, under the responsibility of the approved body referred to in point 2.2, the latter's identification number adjacent to the UK marking in the car of each lift. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**5 (Extent: E+W+S).2** The installer shall draw up a written declaration of conformity for each lift and keep a copy of the Declaration of conformity at the disposal of the enforcing authorities for 10 years after the placing on the market of the lift. A copy of the declaration of conformity shall be made available to the relevant authorities upon request. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**6 (Extent: E+W+S).** The installer shall keep with the technical documentation a copy of the certificate of conformity at the disposal of the enforcing authorities for 10 years from the date on which the lift is placed on the market. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 7 (Extent: E+W+S). Authorised representative (Extent: E+W+S)

The installer's obligations set out in points 2.2 and 6 may be fulfilled by his authorised representative, on his behalf and under his responsibility, provided that they are specified in the mandate. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
