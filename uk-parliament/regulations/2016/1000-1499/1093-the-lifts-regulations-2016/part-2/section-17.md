# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_5834c117-a87c-4d4b-cd40-0c17bbe42c9f][^key-f92d85c975c9ad5f4198ccb0f1249c6b][^key-738fcc8085c3f06c706be2f3f07a400d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the conformity of a safety component for lifts with the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, before placing the safety component for lifts on the market, the manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up a declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 49 ([^key-f77c05cba4520222a33e9641148319e8]...declaration of conformity);
- (b) ensure that it accompanies the safety component for lifts; and
- (c) affix the UK[^key-e5429b24a927da882a5ee3a0e2dd6988] marking in accordance with regulation 50 (UK marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the [^key-cc7b3e95468cca8ea2f519db700f1ac0]... declaration of conformity up-to-date.

(3)[^key-4262e9f36e5708fc0ead30852500ffd8] Where a safety component for lifts is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.

## 17. EU declaration of conformity and CE marking[^M_E_1071695c-63c2-4381-9d64-93f806898d13]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the conformity of a safety component for lifts with the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, before placing the safety component for lifts on the market, the manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up a declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 49 (EU declaration of conformity);
- (b) ensure that it accompanies the safety component for lifts; and
- (c) affix the CE marking in accordance with regulation 50 (CE marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the EU declaration of conformity up-to-date.

(3) Where a safety component for lifts is subject to more than one NI Protocol obligation[^key-9d390ada17f11ecc29630913608cc431] requiring a declaration of conformity to be drawn up, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity, which—

- (a) identifies the EU instruments; and
- (b) includes references to the publication of those relevant[^key-0636515c2a83fad8ac2f61af0e07f77c] EU instruments in the Official Journal.
