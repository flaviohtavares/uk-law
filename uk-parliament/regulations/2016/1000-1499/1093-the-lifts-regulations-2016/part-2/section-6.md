# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Design, manufacture, installation and testing in accordance with essential health and safety requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before placing a lift on the market, an installer must ensure that it has been designed, manufactured, installed and tested in accordance with the essential health and safety requirements.
