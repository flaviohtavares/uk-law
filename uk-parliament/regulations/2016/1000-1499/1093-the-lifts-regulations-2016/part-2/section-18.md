# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 18. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_8cdce2a5-ddf3-4603-9e12-a74b02d79491][^c-0c1d5398437b77491622d5e2072fe025]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation, the [^c-14324dcc5dbb1e6774b46a905fbf6ac1]... declaration of conformity and, where appropriate, any approval decision, drawn up in respect of a safety component for lifts for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the safety component for lifts is placed on the market.

## 18. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_43955dac-6907-4e66-c8c3-248edae7c46e]

*Extent: N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation, the EU declaration of conformity and, where appropriate, any approval decision, drawn up in respect of a safety component for lifts for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the safety component for lifts is placed on the market.
