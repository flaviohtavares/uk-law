# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Duty to act with due care

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When making a safety component for lifts available on the market, a distributor must act with due care to ensure that it is in conformity with Part 2.
