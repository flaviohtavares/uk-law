## 43 (Extent: EWS) (affected by repeal, see note). Translation of declaration of conformity (Extent: EWS) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1][^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1]

(1 (Extent: EWS) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] Before placing a lift on the market or making a safety component for lifts available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] EU *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] relevant state[^c-61c7980c8734e23205ba85a3ff7dda76] *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] in which the lift is to be placed on the market or the safety component for lifts is to be made available on the market. *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1]

(2 (Extent: EWS) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] Where the lift is to be placed on the market or the safety component for lifts is to be made available on the market in *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1] Northern Ireland[^c-9736c4925b1d17513cc0ecdba397f00f] *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1], the language required is English. *(Extent: EWS)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18198b6a7c38447bb70ae6ec4d770ba1]
