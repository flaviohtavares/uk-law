# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Duty to take action in respect of lifts placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_a8d3fc20-627d-477e-f4fa-c4b6cfa0f734]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An installer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a lift which that installer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2, must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to bring that lift into conformity.

(2) Where the lift presents a risk, the installer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^c-6f07cb57acc961fb6f9a111054d54d12]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the lift is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 12. Duty to take action in respect of lifts placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_6f7ade38-1e09-40eb-884b-f0e913ab3aea]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An installer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a lift which that installer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2, must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to bring that lift into conformity.

(2) Where the lift presents a risk, the installer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority, and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^c-8385f2af985510318100eea575449a4e] in which the installer placed the lift on the market, of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the lift is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
