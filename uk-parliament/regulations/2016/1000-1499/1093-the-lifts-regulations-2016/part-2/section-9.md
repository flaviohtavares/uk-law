# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_ca8b1a6e-e649-4faa-fda2-8d89daf9bd8b][^c-8319da8b5834bd6b62e0a0486b4fa2a0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An installer must keep the technical documentation, the [^c-fc8b644cb0b39023f1a1c66110ba6d80]... declaration of conformity and, where applicable, any approval decision, drawn up in respect of a lift for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the lift is placed on the market.

## 9. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_f1be14c3-a725-4dd2-c005-bb2715c1f597]

*Extent: N.I.*

An installer must keep the technical documentation, the EU declaration of conformity and, where applicable, any approval decision, drawn up in respect of a lift for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the lift is placed on the market.
