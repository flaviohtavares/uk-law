# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 33. Retention of technical documentation and ...declaration of conformity[^M_E_ab3a7d81-c37d-4142-d4a3-957377325b42][^c-b392fd8d1ba3fa47512941f4d914b930]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the safety component for lifts is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the [^c-a733113ad426f97934ecf95553612d82]... declaration of conformity and, where applicable, any approval decision, at the disposal of enforcing authorities; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation can be made available to enforcing authorities, upon request.

## 33. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_75f30998-ed20-495f-f8d0-804a385f6cf2]

*Extent: N.I.*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the safety component for lifts is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the EU declaration of conformity and, where applicable, any approval decision, at the disposal of enforcing authorities; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation can be made available to enforcing authorities, upon request.
