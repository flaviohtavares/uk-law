# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Prohibition on placing on the market a safety component for lifts which is not in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must not place a safety component for lifts on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements.
