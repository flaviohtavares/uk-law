# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Evaluation of lifts or safety components for lifts presenting a risk

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe that a lift or safety component for lifts presents a risk, the market surveillance authority must carry out an evaluation in relation to the lift or the safety component for lifts covering the relevant requirements of Part 2 applying in respect of that lift or safety component for lifts.

(2) Where an enforcing authority other than the market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe that a lift or safety component for lifts presents a risk, that enforcing authority must carry out an evaluation in relation to the lift or the safety component for lifts covering the relevant requirements of Part 2 applying in respect of that lift or safety component for lifts.
