# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Restrictive measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, an enforcing authority must comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS in relation to any measure to—

- (a) in relation to a lift—
  - (i) prohibit or restrict a lift being placed on the market;
  - (ii) prohibit or restrict the use of a lift; or
  - (iii) recall a lift;
- (b) in relation to a safety component for lifts—
  - (i) prohibit the safety component for lifts being made available on the market;
  - (ii) withdraw a safety component for lifts; or
  - (iii) recall a safety component for lifts.
