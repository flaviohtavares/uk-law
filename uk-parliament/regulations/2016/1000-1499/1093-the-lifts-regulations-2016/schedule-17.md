# The Lifts Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 17 (Extent: E+W+S) — CONFORMITY TO TYPE BASED ON PRODUCT QUALITY ASSURANCE FOR LIFTS (Annex X to the Directive) (Extent: E+W+S)[^c-bab6ba6bb63318b273db6356bd96a3eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 47 *(Extent: E+W+S)*

### MODULE E (Extent: E+W+S)

**1 (Extent: E+W+S).** Conformity to type based on product quality assurance is the part of the conformity assessment procedure whereby an approved body assesses the product quality system of an installer to ensure that the lifts are in conformity with the approved type as described in the Type examination certificate or with a lift designed and manufactured under a full quality system approved in accordance with Schedule 18, and satisfy the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 2 (Extent: E+W+S). Obligations of the installer (Extent: E+W+S)

The installer shall operate an approved quality system for final inspection and testing of the lift as specified in point 3, and shall be subject to surveillance as specified in point 4. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 3 (Extent: E+W+S). Quality system (Extent: E+W+S)

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).1.** The installer shall lodge an application for assessment of his quality system for the lifts concerned with a single approved body of his choice. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The application shall include: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the name and address of the installer, and if the application is lodged by the authorised representative, his name and address as well; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) all relevant information on the lifts to be installed; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) the documentation on the quality system; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) the technical documentation of the lifts to be installed; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (e (Extent: E+W+S)) a written declaration that the same application has not been lodged with any other approved body. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).2.** Under the quality system, each lift shall be examined and appropriate tests as set out in the relevant designated standards or equivalent tests shall be carried out in order to ensure its conformity with the applicable essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

All the elements, requirements and provisions adopted by the installer shall be documented in a systematic and orderly manner in the form of written policies, procedures and instructions. This quality system documentation shall permit a consistent interpretation of the quality programmes, plans, manuals and quality records. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

It shall contain in particular an adequate description of: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the quality objectives; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the organisational structure, responsibilities and powers of the management with regard to product quality; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) the examinations and tests that will be carried out before placing on the market, including at least the tests laid down in point 3.3 of Schedule 12; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) the means of monitoring the effective operation of the quality system; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (e (Extent: E+W+S)) the quality records, such as inspection reports and test data, calibration data, reports on the qualifications of the personnel concerned. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).3.** The approved body shall assess the quality system to determine whether it satisfies the requirements referred to in point 3.2. It shall presume conformity with those requirements in respect of the elements of the quality systems that comply with the corresponding specifications of the relevant designated standard. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The auditing team shall have at least one member with experience of assessment in the lift technology concerned and knowledge of the essential health and safety requirements set out in Schedule 1. The audit shall include an assessment visit to the premises of the installer and a visit to the installation site. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The decision shall be notified to the installer. The notification shall contain the conclusions of the audit and the reasoned assessment decision. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).4.** The installer shall undertake to fulfil the obligations arising from the quality system as approved and to maintain it so that it remains adequate and efficient. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).4.1.** The installer shall keep the approved body which has approved the quality system informed of any intended change to the system. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**3 (Extent: E+W+S).4.2.** The approved body shall assess the modifications proposed and decide whether the modified quality system will continue to satisfy the requirements referred to in point 3.2 or whether a reassessment is necessary. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

It shall notify its decision to the installer or, where appropriate, to his authorised representative. The notification shall contain the conclusions of the assessment and the reasoned assessment decision. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

The approved body shall affix, or cause to be affixed, its identification number adjacent to the UK marking in accordance with regulation 50. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 4 (Extent: E+W+S). Surveillance under the responsibility of the approved body (Extent: E+W+S)

**4 (Extent: E+W+S).1.** The purpose of surveillance is to make sure that the installer duly fulfils the obligations arising out of the approved quality system. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**4 (Extent: E+W+S).2.** The installer shall, for assessment purposes, allow the approved body access to the installation, inspection and testing locations, and shall provide it with all necessary information, in particular: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the quality system documentation; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the technical documentation; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) the quality records, such as inspection reports and test data, calibration data, reports on the qualifications of the personnel concerned, etc. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**4 (Extent: E+W+S).3.** The approved body shall periodically carry out audits to ensure that the installer maintains and applies the quality system and shall provide the installer with an audit report. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**4 (Extent: E+W+S).4.** Additionally, the approved body may pay unexpected visits to the lift installation sites. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

At the time of such visits, the approved body may, where necessary, carry out tests or have them carried out in order to check the proper functioning of the quality system and of the lift. It shall provide the installer with a visit report and, if tests have been carried out, with a test report. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**5 (Extent: E+W+S).** The installer shall, for 10 years after the last lift has been placed on the market, keep at the disposal of the enforcing authorities: *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the documentation referred to in point 3.1(c); *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the technical documentation referred to in point 3.1(d); *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) the information relating to the changes referred to in point 3.4.1; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) the decisions and reports from the approved body which are referred to in the second paragraph of point 3.4.2 and in points 4.3 and 4.4. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**6 (Extent: E+W+S).** Each approved body shall inform the Secretary of State of quality system approval decision(s) issued or withdrawn, and shall, periodically or upon request, make available to the Secretary of State the list of approval decisions, refused, suspended or otherwise restricted. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

Each approved body shall inform the other approved bodies of quality system approval decision(s) which it has refused, suspended or withdrawn and, upon request, of approval decision(s) which it has issued. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

On request, the approved body shall provide the Secretary of State with a copy of the quality system approval decision(s) issued. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 7 (Extent: E+W+S). UK marking and declaration of conformity (Extent: E+W+S)

**7 (Extent: E+W+S).1.** The installer shall affix the UK marking in the car of each lift which satisfies the essential health and safety requirements of these Regulations, and, under the responsibility of the approved body referred to in point 3.1, the latter's identification number adjacent to the UK marking in the car of each lift. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

**7 (Extent: E+W+S).2.** The installer shall draw up a written Declaration of conformity for each lift and keep a copy of the Declaration of conformity at the disposal of the enforcing authorities for 10 years after the placing on the market of the lift. A copy of the Declaration of conformity shall be made available to the relevant authorities upon request. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

#### 8 (Extent: E+W+S). Authorised representative (Extent: E+W+S)

The installer's obligations set out in points 3.1, 3.4.1, 5 and 7 may be fulfilled by his authorised representative, on his behalf and under his responsibility, provided that they are specified in the mandate. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
