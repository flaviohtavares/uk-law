# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_4b299090-82f5-4a18-cfa5-47ad57ba7247]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A lift or a safety component for lifts which is in conformity with a designated[^c-634bf4af68da891172e7b242880de053] standard (or part of such a standard) [^c-245837b2a151cfc5842ddda9ebaae2d9]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 46. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_44ec76ac-4041-4824-dc74-e39dc29a168a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A lift or a safety component for lifts which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
