# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Conformity assessment procedures for safety components for lifts[^M_E_c348fac7-63a7-471e-d98b-828f277186c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the assessment of conformity of a safety component for lifts, the manufacturer must carry out one of the following procedures—

- (a) the model of the safety component for lifts must be submitted for Type[^c-3ae01b6a27520eab19b0a873d2ee1fb0] examination set out in Part A of Schedule 11[^c-8c8742f67cca3bfdcce805107edb4fa6] and the conformity to type must be ensured with random checking of the safety component for lifts set out in Schedule 16[^c-2fe54ff913dd5f4d67b708f97014d359];
- (b) the model of the safety component for lifts must be submitted for Type examination set out in Part A of Schedule 11[^c-ca85283fe4b8f316bf6b846566f116f8] and be subject to conformity to type based on product quality assurance in accordance with Schedule 13[^c-f063cfa7402fc092e2e4551145b476c2];
- (c) conformity based on full quality assurance set out in Schedule 14[^c-5e40efe13c485762410766037515b53a].

## 48. Conformity assessment procedures for safety components for lifts[^M_E_6a1acfcf-4c6f-4f32-bec1-a7f18163c0a1]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the assessment of conformity of a safety component for lifts, the manufacturer must carry out one of the following procedures—

- (a) the model of the safety component for lifts must be submitted for EU type examination set out in Part A of Annex IV to the Directive (as amended from time to time) and the conformity to type must be ensured with random checking of the safety component for lifts set out in Annex IX to the Directive (as amended from time to time);
- (b) the model of the safety component for lifts must be submitted for EU type examination set out in Part A of Annex IV to the Directive (as amended from time to time) and be subject to conformity to type based on product quality assurance in accordance with Annex VI to the Directive (as amended from time to time);
- (c) conformity based on full quality assurance set out in Annex VII to the Directive (as amended from time to time).
