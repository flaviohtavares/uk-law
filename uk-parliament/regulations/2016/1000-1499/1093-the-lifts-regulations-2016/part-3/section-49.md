# The Lifts Regulations 2016, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1093
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Declaration of conformity[^M_E_1d673095-576c-4be6-84a2-0668bf5fce47][^c-e5bcf0ffb7058977c4ad039f4dd1665c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^c-3d47115cbde62864d64b17fb45804caf]...declaration of conformity for a lift or a safety component for lifts must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the lift or the safety component for lifts;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Schedules 12 to 19[^c-f700039afd8eee64d29e0d65babecd1b] for the relevant conformity assessment procedure carried out in respect of the lift or the safety component for lifts;
- (c) have the model structure set out—
  - (i) in relation to lifts, in Part 1 of Schedule 5 ([^c-3d47115cbde62864d64b17fb45804caf]... declaration of conformity);
  - (ii) in relation to safety components for lifts, in Part 2 of Schedule 5.

## 49. EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_0abd825e-1a20-4cc4-fba8-9084cd6b203a]

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity for a lift or a safety component for lifts must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the lift or the safety component for lifts;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Annexes V to XII to the Directive (as amended from time to time) for the relevant conformity assessment procedure carried out in respect of the lift or the safety component for lifts;
- (c) have the model structure set out—
  - (i) in relation to lifts, in Part 1 of Schedule 5 (EU declaration of conformity);
  - (ii) in relation to safety components for lifts, in Part 2 of Schedule 5.
