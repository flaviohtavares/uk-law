# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Design and manufacture in accordance with essential safety requirements and sound engineering practice[^M_E_cbd26ed9-4e70-4e2a-bd8b-4c22251c21fe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before placing a category A vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that the vessel has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the essential safety requirements.

(2) Before placing a category B vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that the vessel has been designed and manufactured in accordance with sound engineering practice[^c-b2a4a7466f6285ab13e8c02a547dfe0c].

## 4. Design and manufacture in accordance with essential safety requirements and sound engineering practice[^M_E_38bd9a31-d8f6-451e-9075-816a4f4d8742]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before placing a category A vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that the vessel has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the essential safety requirements.

(2) Before placing a category B vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that the vessel has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the sound engineering practice of a relevant state[^c-3cd9ab9a2c80842e07a260561f009154].
