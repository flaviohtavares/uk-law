# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes available on the market a category A vessel[^M_E_972043c6-3282-4c96-b01e-60222c0cd2f6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before making a category A vessel available on the market, a distributor must ensure that—

- (a) the vessel—
  - (i) bears the UK[^c-2fe67f1f72ba2d951828b0105bec1201] marking and the inscriptions; and
  - (ii)[^c-57247deb7d24fc07396c1c4d769a78f8] is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English;
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels); and
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements in regulation 21 (information identifying importer).

## 29. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes available on the market a category A vessel[^M_E_134595ec-a5b5-4a65-d878-9aae791c8f26]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before making a category A vessel available on the market, a distributor must ensure that—

- (a) the vessel—
  - (i) bears the CE marking and the inscriptions; and
  - (ii) is accompanied by the instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by end-users in the relevant state[^c-57b8b6145fe4f9ced3ba9a32e02cd6ab] in which the vessel is to be made available on the market;
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels); and
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements in regulation 21 (information identifying importer).
