# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 21

## 21. Information identifying importer[^M_E_3347b69f-5521-472e-c808-8213ff614f65][^c-8004c5bddf11567a67875f76ca205177]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before placing a vessel on the market, an importer must indicate on the vessel [^c-08668b739a11c84d13c71e7bad907745]...—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(1A)[^c-2c75b5db63a32215cbfd7e1582be5f35] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) it is not possible to set out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the vessel, or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the vessel from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-f2b296877844d84bfbefdeb3c553b869] beginning with IP completion day, and
- (b) before placing the vessel on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1) in a document accompanying the vessel.

(2) The contact details referred to in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and the enforcing authority[^c-90f4516f6c5496a2399918ce9fda5cc7].

## 21. Information identifying importer[^M_E_07290e48-bd64-4bbb-a086-db7fc671acb0][^V_NI_b170fda6-c209-b50c-b946-cd145773309f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before placing a vessel on the market, an importer must indicate on the vessel or, where that is not possible, in a document accompanying the vessel—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the importer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) The contact details referred to in paragraph (1) must be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and the competent national authority in the relevant state[^c-3507612fbcd3d0040164329a0dc8b9e7] in which it is to be made available to such end-users.
