# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092

## 32. Storage and transport conditions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a distributor has responsibility for a Category A vessel, the distributor must ensure that the conditions under which it is stored or transported do not jeopardise its conformity with the essential safety requirements.
