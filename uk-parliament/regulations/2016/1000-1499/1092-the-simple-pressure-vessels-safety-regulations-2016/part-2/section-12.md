# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Provision of instructions and safety information[^M_E_706f85bc-845d-44df-cd52-88f9631716e3][^key-120838ae454752021cd4e0af3cede356]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When placing a vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that a vessel is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 12. Provision of instructions and safety information[^M_E_edc94612-fc44-4e40-88c4-95530eef3a6b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing a vessel on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that a vessel is accompanied by the instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by end-users in the relevant state[^key-21f553cdd01f53c89c232e2b90131c79] in which it is to be made available on the market.

(2) The instructions and safety information referred to in paragraph (1) must be clear, understandable and intelligible.

(3) Where the relevant state[^key-7b07669b406a3098c4be386cf6d5dc54] referred to in paragraph (1) is Northern Ireland[^key-8788411df6cf37083b862cb195998918], the language referred to in that paragraph must be English.
