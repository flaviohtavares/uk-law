# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Retention by manufacturer of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_d59c6fd9-21f6-41d3-bffd-a7f94d0e21b8][^key-7ee6ab33abc6834fd7f6c4149045e310]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the [^key-fdc8bec4a139975e00b2c9d588b165fe]... declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a category A vessel and make it available for inspection by the enforcing authorities for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the vessel was placed on the market.

## 8. Retention by manufacturer of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_290dcfdb-a0ea-4cd3-f75d-1823acc38327]

*Extent: N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a category A vessel and make it available for inspection by the enforcing authorities for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the vessel was placed on the market.
