# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Duty of importer to take action in respect of vessels placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_b8bc0b06-02ba-4be0-9f0e-d09b806a9a4e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a vessel which the importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the vessel into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the vessel; or
- (c) recall the vessel.

(2) Where the vessel presents a risk to the health or safety of persons, to domestic animals or to property, an importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority of the risk, [^c-1d0895696d83bd8d6115478828572b35]... giving details, in particular, of—

- (a) the respect in which the vessel is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 25. Duty of importer to take action in respect of vessels placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_a0c580b8-62c4-4869-fd7a-509d94d4a3a4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a vessel which the importer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the vessel into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the vessel; or
- (c) recall the vessel.

(2) Where the vessel presents a risk to the health or safety of persons, to domestic animals or to property, an importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority of the risk, and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^c-1015304a98d7b8642b59e52ab8bd28d7] in which the importer made the vessel available on the market, giving details, in particular, of—

- (a) the respect in which the vessel is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
