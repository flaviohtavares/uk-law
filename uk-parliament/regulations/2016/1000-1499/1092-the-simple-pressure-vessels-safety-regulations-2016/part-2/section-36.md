## 36. Translation of EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_d724bacd-b863-45f1-ef5e-143bd6b2572f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making a Category A vessel available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the EU declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the relevant state[^key-556128ed9c0b3f2d80562c35622168c4] in which it is to be made available on the market.

(2) Where the category A vessel is to be made available on the market in Northern Ireland[^key-6f3587656e02c333a265c168d32b24f0], the language required is English.
