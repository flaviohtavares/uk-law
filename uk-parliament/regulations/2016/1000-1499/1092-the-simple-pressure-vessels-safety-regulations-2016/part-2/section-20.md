# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 20. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places a category B vessel on the marke t[^M_E_a46e783e-48a0-4719-ce10-e8cda915b93f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing a category B vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) it has been designed and manufactured in accordance with sound engineering practice[^c-af5a0e44e807fac75658e23c2eb7c6ce];
- (b) it bears the inscriptions; and
- (c) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels).

## 20. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places a category B vessel on the market[^M_E_6010e037-8634-4559-bce7-ebbcc44cb61b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing a category B vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) it has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the sound engineering practice in a relevant state[^c-2450598ab47b6ff61879fddca789870b];
- (b) it bears the inscriptions; and
- (c) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels).
