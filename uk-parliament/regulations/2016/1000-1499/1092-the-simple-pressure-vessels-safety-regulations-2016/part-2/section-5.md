# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Technical documentation and conformity assessment for category A vessels

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before placing a category A vessel on the market, a manufacturer must–

- (a) draw up the technical documentation in respect of that vessel; and
- (b) carry out a relevant conformity assessment procedure in respect of the vessel or have such a procedure carried out.
