# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 22. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_4c0e033c-be80-4abe-c830-6a0c12169fbd][^c-17f1755d2e24b2f8ae67f42c0c130551]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When placing a vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that the vessel is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English.

## 22. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_4232560f-246e-4500-b1cf-84d0ba4e94a1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing a vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that the vessel is accompanied by the instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by end-users in the relevant state[^c-77b5a1289c5e5d0a15a52f9b4b8989eb] in which it is to be made available.

(2) The instructions and safety information referred to in paragraph (1) must be clear and understandable.

(3) Where the relevant state[^c-9ff2665965af6254c6a15e9dbbd60b1b] referred to in paragraph (1) is Northern Ireland[^c-e15399f218037108c88261ace65424ca], the language referred to in that paragraph must be English.
