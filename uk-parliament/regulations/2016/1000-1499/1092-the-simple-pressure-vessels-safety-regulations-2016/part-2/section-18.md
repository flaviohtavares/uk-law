# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places a category A vessel on the market[^M_E_4a6e6cd8-9569-4e60-a17f-f4043dec2dc9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing a category A vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) the relevant conformity assessment procedure has been carried out;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c)[^key-51406a41df093401205b80843c2fd204] the UK marking and the inscriptions have been affixed in accordance with regulation 6(1)(b) to (d);
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels).

## 18. Requirements which must be satisfied before an importer places a category A vessel on the market[^M_E_4ba5af84-3c1d-4da7-fdc3-5fe172089ec6]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing a category A vessel on the market, an importer must ensure that—

- (a) the relevant conformity assessment procedure has been carried out;
- (b) the manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation;
- (c) the vessel bears the CE marking and the inscriptions; and;
- (d) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels).
