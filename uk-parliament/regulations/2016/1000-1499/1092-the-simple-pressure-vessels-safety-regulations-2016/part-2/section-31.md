## 31. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes a category B vessel available on the market[^M_E_20af1c69-1b63-43f7-aaad-d0e67d8af6f6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before making a category B vessel available on the market, a distributor must verify that—

- (a) the vessel—
  - (i) bears the inscriptions;
  - (ii)[^c-0031222608972fc69f9f9e4dd05bb01c] is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English;
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels); and
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements in regulation 21 (information identifying importer).

## 31. Requirements which must be satisfied before a distributor makes a category B vessel available on the market[^M_E_488854b4-f5ba-408e-ac1a-85ab5c14fde4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making a category B vessel available on the market, a distributor must verify that—

- (a) the vessel—
  - (i) bears the inscriptions;
  - (ii) is accompanied by the instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by end-users in the relevant state[^c-a1ccacf989ea345b692db906a5802fe7] in which the vessel is to be made available on the market;
- (b) the manufacturer has complied with the requirements set out in regulation 11 (labelling of vessels); and
- (c) the importer has complied with the requirements in regulation 21 (information identifying importer).

(2) Where the relevant state[^c-9a25a98a4cec548bdfbb7b97ccc3c713] referred to in paragraph (1) is Northern Ireland[^c-27d40b5c50ba6bc9b330920a6882aace], the language referred to in paragraph (1) must be English.
