# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Transitional provision[^M_E_e0c6693b-6b15-4ba6-e8d3-1fac4ebff4b2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A certificate issued by a United Kingdom approved body under regulations 10 (EC certificate of adequacy), 11 (EC type-examination certificate), 12(7) (EC verification) and 13 (EC certificate of conformity) of the 1991 Regulations, or under the corresponding provisions of a[^key-fe51cbd1bc0db03755312430482e8f33] Member State's enactment implementing the 2009 Directive, is valid under these Regulations.

(2) In this regulation, “**2009 Directive**” means Directive [2009/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0105) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009 relating to simple pressure vessels [^c23182301].

## 76. Transitional provision[^M_E_4481a3e8-18f7-4d4d-f125-7802a68ce29a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A certificate issued by a United Kingdom approved body under regulations 10 (EC certificate of adequacy), 11 (EC type-examination certificate), 12(7) (EC verification) and 13 (EC certificate of conformity) of the 1991 Regulations, or under the corresponding provisions of another relevant state’s[^key-b1dfeb165e73b9ef5b98f60592156c9a] enactment implementing the 2009 Directive, is valid under these Regulations.

(2) In this regulation, “**2009 Directive**” means Directive [2009/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0105) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009 relating to simple pressure vessels [^V_NI_3c1bca90-be5a-91d5-9fd6-c42dfae64d1a].
