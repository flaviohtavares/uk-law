# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Declaration of conformity[^M_E_6760043f-68ba-4bc8-a4cc-622185d95e7d][^key-00994806c6ef6e25a803327849229b85]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^key-c2948f210ba2a2f23de19e2b07fddc75]... declaration of conformity in respect of a category A vessel must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the vessel;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 3; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in Schedule 2 for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the vessel.

## 43. EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_f8e3f2e5-b7c3-4f52-fce4-8a1defeae8a0]

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity in respect of a category A vessel must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the vessel;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 3; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in Schedule 2 for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the vessel.
