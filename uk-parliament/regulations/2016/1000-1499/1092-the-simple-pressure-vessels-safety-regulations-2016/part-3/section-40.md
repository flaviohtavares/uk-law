# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Conformity assessment procedures prior to manufacture[^M_E_0ab3090a-6ef4-470e-f800-835f296319e5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Prior to the manufacture of a category A vessel, a Type[^c-61f43de03d69d754768e99c652768327] examination (Module B), as described in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2, must be carried out in respect of the technical design of the vessel.

(2) Where a vessel is to be manufactured in accordance with a designated[^c-318c9ba0decdfc969b0694cd5b06bd10] standard (or part of such standard) referred to in regulation 39, (presumption of conformity of category A vessels) the manufacturer must choose one of the following forms of examination—

- (a) an examination of the technical documentation and supporting evidence in respect of the vessel without an examination of a specimen vessel (Module B – design type); or
- (b) an examination of the technical documentation and supporting evidence in respect of the vessel, with an examination of a prototype, representative of the production envisaged, of the complete vessel (Module B – production type).

(3) Where a vessel is not to be manufactured, or is to be manufactured only partly, in accordance with a designated[^c-275def05105cba544ebf0973fee81b78] standard (or part of such standard) referred to in regulation 39, the examination must be an examination of the type referred to in paragraph (2)(b).

(4) Paragraphs 2 to 9 of Schedule 2 make provision in respect of a Type[^c-acfcbb43247517b9623c25ed78c34140] examination (Module B) and related matters.

## 40. Conformity assessment procedures prior to manufacture[^M_E_c295e6f2-fe8e-4498-c518-c1df6d2eb5aa]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Prior to the manufacture of a category A vessel, an EU-type examination (Module B), as described in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2, must be carried out in respect of the technical design of the vessel.

(2) Where a vessel is to be manufactured in accordance with a harmonised standard (or part of such standard) referred to in regulation 39, (presumption of conformity of category A vessels) the manufacturer must choose one of the following forms of examination—

- (a) an examination of the technical documentation and supporting evidence in respect of the vessel without an examination of a specimen vessel (Module B – design type); or
- (b) an examination of the technical documentation and supporting evidence in respect of the vessel, with an examination of a prototype, representative of the production envisaged, of the complete vessel (Module B – production type).

(3) Where a vessel is not to be manufactured, or is to be manufactured only partly, in accordance with a harmonised standard (or part of such standard) referred to in regulation 39, the examination must be an examination of the type referred to in paragraph (2)(b).

(4) Paragraphs 2 to 9 of Schedule 2 make provision in respect of an EU-type examination (Module B) and related matters.
