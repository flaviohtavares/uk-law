# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Presumption of conformity of category A vessels[^M_E_26b3f1df-a020-4c14-f189-96e3e8db7c6b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A category A vessel which is in conformity with a designated[^key-d71af1921a1c4932ee2e533e5a56b28f] standard (or part of such a standard) [^key-ce3d70f73e0560a8f15889ca62ddd1f3]...*,* is presumed to be in conformity with the essential safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 39. Presumption of conformity of category A vessels[^M_E_25e1f38c-5ce7-468a-833b-592a9f6fd073]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A category A vessel which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal, is presumed to be in conformity with the essential safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
