# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Records and correspondence language requirements[^M_E_6ff65fb7-49fd-47fe-e59c-0b4074f50319]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The records and correspondence relating to the conformity assessment procedures referred to in regulations 40 (conformity assessment procedures prior to manufacture) and 41 (conformity assessment procedures prior to placing a vessel on the market) must be in English[^c-34c7dafadd3efa26ba2dd3ce98278699] or in a language acceptable to the approved body[^c-362f573dabe5ad564ee1bd86f3b30c11].

## 42. Records and correspondence language requirements[^M_E_6016b640-e24d-419d-e7e1-1e7dcdacfd12]

*Extent: N.I.*

The records and correspondence relating to the conformity assessment procedures referred to in regulations 40 (conformity assessment procedures prior to manufacture) and 41 (conformity assessment procedures prior to placing a vessel on the market) must be in an official language of the relevant state[^c-9f4cc58ffdd10e4c072252665924731c] in which the notified body is established or in a language acceptable to that body.
