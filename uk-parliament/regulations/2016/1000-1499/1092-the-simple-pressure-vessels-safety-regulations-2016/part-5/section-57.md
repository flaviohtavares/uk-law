# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 57

## 57. Exercise of enforcement powers[^M_E_8c34f85c-676a-494b-c75a-3626621851a9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When enforcing these Regulations or RAMS (in its application to vessels), the enforcing authority must exercise its powers in a manner which is consistent with—

- (a) regulation 58 (evaluation of vessels presenting a risk);
- (b) regulation 59 (enforcement action in respect of vessels which are not in conformity);
- (c)[^c-e96a63363398668740b6d08a6599b2c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) regulation 61 (enforcement action in respect of vessels which are in conformity but which present a risk);
- (e) regulation 62 (enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance); and
- (f) regulation 63 (restrictive measures).

## 57. Exercise of enforcement powers[^M_E_e9131d85-2715-47e7-8352-a369422d1438]

*Extent: N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations or RAMS (in its application to vessels), the enforcing authority must exercise its powers in a manner which is consistent with—

- (a) regulation 58 (evaluation of vessels presenting a risk);
- (b) regulation 59 (enforcement action in respect of vessels which are not in conformity);
- (c) regulation 60 (EU safeguard procedure);
- (d) regulation 61 (enforcement action in respect of vessels which are in conformity but which present a risk);
- (e) regulation 62 (enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance); and
- (f) regulation 63 (restrictive measures).
