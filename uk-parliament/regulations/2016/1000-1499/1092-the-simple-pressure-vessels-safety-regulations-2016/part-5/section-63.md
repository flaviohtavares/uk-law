# The Simple Pressure Vessels (Safety) Regulations 2016, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1092
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Restrictive measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, an enforcing authority must comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS (as amended from time to time) in relation to any measure to—

- (a) prohibit or restrict a vessel being made available on the market;
- (b) withdraw a vessel; or
- (c) recall a vessel.
