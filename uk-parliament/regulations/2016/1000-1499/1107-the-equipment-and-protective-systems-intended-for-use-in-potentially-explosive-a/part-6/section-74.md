# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1107
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 74. Consequential Amendments[^c-edf5f1bee8bb0c69aa330ecd2ada87ce]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to the Dangerous Substances and Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2002[^f00016] for “the Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 1996” substitute “the Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016”.

(2) In relation to a product placed on the market before the commencement date, the amendments in paragraph (1) do not apply.
