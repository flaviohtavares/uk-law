# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 15

## 15. Duty to take action in respect of a product placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^c-e77d09526ff3e86041562b66db53ef19]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a product which the manufacturer has placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2, must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the product into conformity,
- (b) withdraw the product, or
- (c) recall the product.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority[^c-bbe7884e8ab730cbd1a1a6c9fc68456b]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the product is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2, and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
