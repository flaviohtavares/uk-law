# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 20

## 20. Prohibition on placing on the market products considered not to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements[^key-e6d9cc53fb2352808bc552cfb9e22fd7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where an importer considers, or has reason to believe, that a product is not in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements, the importer must not place the product on the market.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the importer must inform the manufacturer and the market surveillance authority of that risk.
