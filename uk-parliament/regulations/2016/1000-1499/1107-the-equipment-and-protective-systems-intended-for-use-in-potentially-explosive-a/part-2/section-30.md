## 30. Storage and transport[^key-0c60a7989f828a87fcfe2141bcda9db2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Each distributor must ensure that, whilst a product is under that distributor’s responsibility, its storage or transport conditions do not jeopardise its conformity with the essential health and safety requirements.
