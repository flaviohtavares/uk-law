## 11. Labelling and packaging of products[^key-64c3827f68e05c45806b911140e5e8cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before placing a product on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it bears a type, batch or serial number or other element allowing its identification.

(2) If the size or nature of the product does not provide sufficient space for the labelling requirements in paragraph (1), the manufacturer must ensure that the information is provided on the packaging or in a document accompanying the product.
