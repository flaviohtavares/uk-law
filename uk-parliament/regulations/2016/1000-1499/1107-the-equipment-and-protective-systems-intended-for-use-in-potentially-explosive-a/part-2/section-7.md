# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1107
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 7. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^key-b2a00433bd6bb65c2f1e38a892130416][^key-661985a5879310c098ce620a36f4be8f][^key-3c8c1e8f0fb1542a2aaeca104450fd9c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Save for where a product is a component, where the conformity of a product with the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the product on the market—

- (a) draw up a declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 40 ([^key-3142296a468c781e131d820508205583]... declaration of conformity), and
- (b) affix the UK[^key-f0aa3d1ee533ef07ac15e6cb8e1ae867] Marking in accordance with regulation 41 ( UK Marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the [^key-c0e61087a37e783a92dd6b8333727c17]... declaration of conformity up-to-date.

(3) Where the conformity of a component with the essential health and safety requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the component on the market, draw up a written attestation of conformity in accordance with regulation 39(3) (conformity assessment procedures).

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), before placing a product on the market, the manufacturer must ensure that each product is accompanied by a copy of the [^key-7f03777f067fe4537530ae168e703109]... declaration of conformity or attestation of conformity as appropriate.

(5) Where a large number of products are delivered to a single user, the batch or consignment may be accompanied by a single copy of the [^key-fe9ec4955a20952b7febead34ecd244e]... declaration or attestation of conformity as appropriate.

(6)[^key-5d6b7f54a6b4b903b4c0c1642b83620b] Where a product is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.
