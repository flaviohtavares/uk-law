## 23. Storage and transport[^key-037a6ed2f09cc07474ed50618ee6ff57]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Each importer must ensure that, whilst a product is under that importer’s responsibility, its storage or transport conditions do not jeopardise its conformity with the essential health and safety requirements.
