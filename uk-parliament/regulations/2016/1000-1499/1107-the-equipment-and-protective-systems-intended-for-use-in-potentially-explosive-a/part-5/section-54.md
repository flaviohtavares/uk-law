## 54. Exercise of enforcement powers[^key-9ebaee5ea1d0b4737f373422f3d4d840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When enforcing these Regulations, the market surveillance authority must exercise its powers in a manner which is consistent with—

- (a) regulation 55 (evaluation of a product presenting a risk);
- (b) regulation 56 (enforcement action in respect of products which are not in conformity and which present a risk);
- (c)[^key-8674e63e166b7f01e3c5db47460ed83e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) regulation 58 (enforcement action in respect of products which are in conformity, but present a risk);
- (e) regulation 59 (enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance);
- (f) regulation 60 (restrictive measures).
