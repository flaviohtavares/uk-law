# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1107
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Enforcement action in respect of products which are in conformity, but present a risk[^c-0f4b3f16b06e0fa9b81fef24baaca23f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where, having carried out an evaluation under regulation 55, the market surveillance authority finds that although a product is in conformity with Part 2, it presents a risk, the market surveillance authority must require a relevant economic operator to take appropriate measures to—

- (a) ensure that the product concerned, when placed on the market, no longer presents a risk,
- (b) withdraw the product within a prescribed period, or
- (c) recall the product within a prescribed period.

(2) Where the market surveillance authority takes measures under paragraph (1), it must notify the Secretary of State immediately.

(3) Where the Secretary of State receives a notice under paragraph (2), the Secretary of State must notify the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland[^c-6a77da933bc92f41cdc98aeb40afa34f] immediately.

(4) The notices referred to in paragraphs (2) and (3) must include details about the product and, in particular—

- (a) the data necessary for the identification of the product concerned;
- (b) the origin and the supply chain of the product;
- (c) the nature of the risk involved;
- (d) the nature and duration of the measures taken by the market surveillance authority.

(5) In this regulation, “prescribed period” means a period which is—

- (a) prescribed by the market surveillance authority;
- (b) reasonable and commensurate with the nature of the risk presented by the product.
