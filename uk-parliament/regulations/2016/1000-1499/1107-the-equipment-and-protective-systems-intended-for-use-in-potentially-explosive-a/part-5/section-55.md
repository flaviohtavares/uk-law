## 55. Evaluation of a product presenting a risk[^c-0d819ed485c94ff19041ce34cb400ee2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe that a product presents a risk, the market surveillance authority must carry out an evaluation in relation to the product covering the relevant requirements of Part 2.
