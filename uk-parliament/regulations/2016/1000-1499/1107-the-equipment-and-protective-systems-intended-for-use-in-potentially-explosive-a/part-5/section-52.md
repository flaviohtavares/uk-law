## 52. Enforcement[^c-d623fe12afc934ee7a599ee87c969e55]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The market surveillance authority must enforce these Regulations and RAMS in its application to a product.

(2) In Scotland, only the Lord Advocate may prosecute an offence under these Regulations.
