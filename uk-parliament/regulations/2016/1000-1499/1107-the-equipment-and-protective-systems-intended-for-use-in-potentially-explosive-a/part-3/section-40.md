# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 40

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. ... Declaration of conformity[^c-6e36f3820403232d20805b0788e8fcec][^c-ffebebe6bc2a3006772fbc79e5ed847e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^c-998b9616de8b8feed75a7d13a9a7e727]... declaration of conformity for a product must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential health and safety requirements have been demonstrated in respect of the product;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 6;
- (c) contain the elements specified in Schedule 3A to these Regulations[^c-e69061690bf616369ff8f68bd272702c] for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the product.
