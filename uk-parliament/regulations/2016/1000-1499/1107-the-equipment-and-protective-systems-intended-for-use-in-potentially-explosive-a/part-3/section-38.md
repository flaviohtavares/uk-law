# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1107
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Presumption of conformity[^c-fbffe43efcf78bcdf6ec8331e8014b11]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A product which is in conformity with a designated[^c-a28050b2ea9a2ce5c0fbddd8466bebee] standard (or part of such a standard) [^c-baf07d0c0bf24edecc0409c23c613030]... is presumed to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
