# The Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 1046

Infrastructure Planning

The Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2016

Made

1st November 2016

Coming into force

22nd November 2016

The Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2016 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

Before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act .

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Stafford Borough Council and Staffordshire County Council that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2016 and comes into force on 22nd November 2016.

## 2. Corrections

The Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2016 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Giles Scott

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Coal Liabilities

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

1st November 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors and omissions identified in the Meaford Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2016, a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request from the applicant under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
