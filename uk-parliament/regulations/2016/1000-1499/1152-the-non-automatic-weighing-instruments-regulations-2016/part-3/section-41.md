# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Regulated instruments that require more than one declaration of conformity[^M_E_b468bda2-c7b2-4ebf-ef6e-5a595379702e][^key-62722840c2ccb10b77621344e8174aba]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a non-automatic weighing instrument is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.

## 41. Regulated instruments that require more than one declaration of conformity[^M_E_2f1f73b6-19d3-4cdf-c40a-2df4944c9891]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument is subject to an NI Protocol obligation[^key-5666eb344c7005bb57031d2b3d63a1bd] for an EU declaration of conformity otherwise than by virtue of these Regulations.

(2) Where this regulation applies, a single EU declaration of conformity must be drawn up covering all applicable requirements which identifies the relevant[^key-069c212096b5a39d880fc8ff63732eaa] Union acts concerned including their publication references.
