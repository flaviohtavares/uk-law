# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Methods of establishing conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_0cfd6530-7327-482b-83d2-fa5c325dd1cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Conformity with the essential requirements may be established in relation to a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument—

- (a) through conformity with designated[^key-986d55e007bd4c1e8fe8b6da65981c4a] standards (or parts of those standards) covering the essential requirements [^M_F_cacce5ab-d23a-4a8f-8a90-a8aae9bad259]...; or
- (b) through the use by the manufacturer of any other technical solution that complies with the essential requirements.

## 34. Methods of establishing conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_b15cfc8c-bcbc-4c58-d4b8-ebf60e5b60e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Conformity with the essential requirements may be established in relation to a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument—

- (a) through conformity with harmonised standards (or parts of those standards) covering the essential requirements where the harmonised standards have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union; or
- (b) through the use by the manufacturer of any other technical solution that complies with the essential requirements.
