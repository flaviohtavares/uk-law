# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Form and contents of ... declaration of conformity etc.[^M_E_737645f4-ab6f-49b0-a370-d3625102ce06][^c-07e2d98c642ff5268d15661d209fc47e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The [^c-302442a5c443edf4332fdff8cda34462]... declaration of conformity must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential requirements has been demonstrated in relation to the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Schedule 9[^c-fbfb45850ad744a2052c7fd7e55c67df]; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in the relevant modules set out in Schedule 7[^c-9aa3621d95f4fbc24420409927e64c6e] and must be updated when appropriate.

(2) Where a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument is placed or made available on the market in the United Kingdom, the [^c-39d0a1c75e95fd84a71ee298bb971072]... declaration of conformity in relation to the instrument must be in English.

## 40. Form and contents of EU declaration of conformity etc.[^M_E_8df42e45-0b30-4738-e6cb-9a53597c2f8c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The EU declaration of conformity must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential requirements has been demonstrated in relation to the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument;
- (b) have the model structure set out in Annex IV to the Directive; and
- (c) contain the elements specified in the relevant modules set out in Annex II to the Directive and must be updated when appropriate.

(2) Where a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument is placed or made available on the market in Northern Ireland[^c-adc90c60c52845ac025cef0c6ec26ed1], the EU declaration of conformity in relation to the instrument must be in English.
