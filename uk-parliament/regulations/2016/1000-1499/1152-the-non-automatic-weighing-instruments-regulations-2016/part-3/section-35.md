# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Presumptions of conformity of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_ba11af2c-95f5-4761-e300-0cb4b80e8acb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulated non-automatic weighing instruments which are in conformity with designated[^c-3292dfe1e4d87cf3978dbc4f259af21b] standards (or parts of those standards) shall be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those standards (or parts of those standards).

## 35. Presumptions of conformity of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_693dbbbf-e609-418c-81ae-3900d0e5e564]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulated non-automatic weighing instruments which are in conformity with harmonised standards (or parts of those standards) shall be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by those standards (or parts of those standards).
