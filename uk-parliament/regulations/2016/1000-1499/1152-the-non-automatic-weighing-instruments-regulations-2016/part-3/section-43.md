# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Conformity with ... requirements to be indicated by the UK marking[^M_E_ada8ada7-9c91-477a-d1ec-abc9b6a35c70][^c-9f3a5ae2a23649ff71229d1dda230e41][^c-1f3a4324ef38db3bb70472e47a41e9f3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The conformity of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument with the requirements of these Regulations must be indicated by the presence on it of the UK[^c-74fb378d6ddbe51df64d720393708e65] marking and the M marking.

## 43. Conformity with Directive requirements to be indicated by the CE marking[^M_E_e66739c7-4792-4447-d88e-a56f1d341e41]

*Extent: N.I.*

The conformity of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument with the requirements of these Regulations must be indicated by the presence on it of the CE marking and the M marking.
