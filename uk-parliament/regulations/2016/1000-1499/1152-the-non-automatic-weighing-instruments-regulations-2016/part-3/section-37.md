## 37 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Subsidiaries and contractors (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99][^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] This regulation applies where— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] a notified body subcontracts specific conformity assessment activities, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] has such activities carried out by a subsidiary. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] The activities are only to be treated as having been carried out by a notified body for the purposes of regulation 36 (conformity assessment procedures) where the conditions in paragraphs (3) and (4) are met. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] The notified body must— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] ensure that the subcontractor or subsidiary meets the notified body requirements; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] inform the Secretary of State accordingly. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] The notified body must have obtained the agreement of the client to the use of a subcontractor or subsidiary. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] Where a notified body subcontracts specific conformity assessment activities, or has such activities carried out by a subsidiary, the notified body must for a period of at least 10 years beginning on the day after the activities are carried out, keep at the disposal of the Secretary of State the documentation concerning— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] the assessment of the qualifications of the subcontractor or the subsidiary; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] the conformity assessment activities carried out by the subcontractor or subsidiary. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99] When monitoring a notified body in accordance with regulation 52 (monitoring), the Secretary of State must treat the notified body as responsible for the tasks performed by a subcontractor or subsidiary, wherever the subcontractor or subsidiary is established. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58c84f7411ebefa8f330e4a7e4ceeb99]
