# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 53. Register of approved bodies[^M_E_66afe078-4786-41c7-d90c-0878c4f1e42f][^c-6b0a91f0fbfd2f7a675ec012ad9a4534]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State must—

- (a) assign an approved body identification number to each approved body; and
- (b) compile and maintain a register of—
  - (i) approved bodies;
  - (ii) their approved body identification numbers;
  - (iii) the activities for which they have been approved; and
  - (iv) any restrictions on those activities.

(2) The register referred to in paragraph (1) must be made publicly available.

## 53. Delegation to the United Kingdom Accreditation Service[^M_E_1dae0906-be3a-4647-cf49-1e4dcfb8de2d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may authorise the United Kingdom Accreditation Service to carry out the following activities on behalf of the Secretary of State—

- (a) assessing whether a conformity assessment body meets the notified body requirements; and
- (b) monitoring notified bodies.

(2) Where the Secretary of State authorises the United Kingdom Accreditation Service pursuant to paragraph (1), the Secretary of State remains fully responsible for anything done pursuant to that authorisation.
