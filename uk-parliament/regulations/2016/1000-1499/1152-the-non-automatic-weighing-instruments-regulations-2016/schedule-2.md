# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 2

## SCHEDULE 2 — OPERATIONAL OBLIGATIONS OF NOTIFIED (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)APPROVED (Extent: E+W+S) BODIES[^M_F_daded585-8374-415e-ff73-0611190f0d9f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 36(4)

**1.** Conformity assessment must be carried out in a proportionate manner, avoiding unnecessary burdens for economic operators.

**2.** Conformity assessment bodies must perform their activities taking due account of—

- (a) the size of an undertaking;
- (b) the sector in which it operates and its structure;
- (c) the degree of complexity of the of the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument technology in question; and
- (d) the mass or serial nature of the production process,

but respecting the degree of rigour and the level of protection required for compliance of the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument with these Regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**3.**[^M_E_823b9f7c-660d-490a-ca79-f71cece4c302] Where an approved[^key-c18b72b16340ced7b3e70398cb5443b2] body finds that the essential requirements have not been met by a manufacturer, it—

- (a) must require that manufacturer to take appropriate corrective measures; and
- (b) must not issue a certificate of conformity.

*Extent: N.I.*

**3.**[^M_E_a190c25f-7a6a-4b34-f80b-e530bcb0f156] Where a notified body finds that the essential requirements have not been met by a manufacturer, it—

- (a) must require that manufacturer to take appropriate corrective measures; and
- (b) must not issue a certificate of conformity.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**4.**[^M_E_51b7219d-33ee-450c-c7cc-b6ec4b4a2937] Where in the course of the monitoring of conformity following the issue of a certificate, an approved[^key-83fe710d29358e24e9cb3d0c1007cd78] body finds that a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument no longer complies, it must require the manufacturer to take appropriate corrective measures and must suspend or withdraw the certificate if necessary.

*Extent: N.I.*

**4.**[^M_E_722a4c35-8c0e-45c2-fa71-647931926054] Where in the course of the monitoring of conformity following the issue of a certificate, a notified body finds that a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument no longer complies, it must require the manufacturer to take appropriate corrective measures and must suspend or withdraw the certificate if necessary.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**5.**[^M_E_eea69b6b-ab22-428e-b3a7-d058dbb292f9] Where corrective measured are not taken or do not have the required effect, the approved[^key-8f2566b4f09eb47a17f33e43fe0bb33e] body must restrict, suspend or withdraw any certificates, as appropriate.

*Extent: N.I.*

**5.**[^M_E_66a03599-fbe5-4b83-ea07-f9073303f23f] Where corrective measured are not taken or do not have the required effect, the notified body must restrict, suspend or withdraw any certificates, as appropriate.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**6.**[^M_E_f2055001-ae3b-4d1d-a94a-9ba57950c95a] Where a person is aggrieved at a decision taken by an approved[^key-1b8d78708886dbd09a1892a6fa90626f] body in relation to the conformity assessment of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument, the approved body must have appropriate arrangements for the review of that decision by a person who was not involved in the taking of that decision.

*Extent: N.I.*

**6.**[^M_E_63b7ea75-aa18-4b88-cb18-883a60f7bbb1] Where a person is aggrieved at a decision taken by a notified body in relation to the conformity assessment of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument, the notified body must have appropriate arrangements for the review of that decision by a person who was not involved in the taking of that decision.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**7.**[^M_E_84edaeea-5721-43fa-a6b5-9ac93df92a3a] Approved bodies must inform the Secretary of State[^key-046f74cb32b0f797ed5b88195cff8c49] of the following—

- (a) any refusal, restriction, suspension or withdrawal of a certificate;
- (b) any circumstances affecting the scope of or conditions for approval[^key-2b5dcbb6d0fe0711505fc391015e2a42];
- (c) any request for information which they have received from market surveillance authorities regarding conformity assessment; and
- (d) on request, conformity assessment activities performed within the scope of their notification and any other activity performed, including cross-border activities and subcontracting.

*Extent: N.I.*

**7.**[^M_E_cb44d784-d6a4-4ae0-bc70-d34406966c10] Notified bodies must inform the notifying authority of the following—

- (a) any refusal, restriction, suspension or withdrawal of a certificate;
- (b) any circumstances affecting the scope of or conditions for notification;
- (c) any request for information which they have received from market surveillance authorities regarding conformity assessment; and
- (d) on request, conformity assessment activities performed within the scope of their notification and any other activity performed, including cross-border activities and subcontracting.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**8.**[^M_E_3a74da06-046a-4c28-bbcd-28196f0441c7] Approved bodies must provide other bodies approved under these Regulations[^key-16c93f541d670658739a698d7a1d178c] carrying out similar conformity assessment activities covering the same regulated non-automatic weighing instruments with relevant information on issues relating to negative and, on request positive conformity assessment results.

*Extent: N.I.*

**8.**[^M_E_7cb912f3-c816-4494-e944-0c50e7d1479e] Notified bodies must provide other bodies notified under this Directive carrying out similar conformity assessment activities covering the same regulated non-automatic weighing instruments with relevant information on issues relating to negative and, on request positive conformity assessment results.

- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-684c81f628842140293adbc818acf7b2] ensure attendance of that representative at meetings of the group. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-684c81f628842140293adbc818acf7b2]
