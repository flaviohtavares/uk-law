# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Importers' duty to ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments are accompanied by relevant documentation.[^M_E_3a4afa7c-3827-4adc-f807-47f7065640a3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments imported by the importer are accompanied by instructions and information which are clear, legible and in easily understandable English[^key-bbeeb3b98d7fe50cd66aef3e93e1a37e].

(2)[^key-e38dbbfe772016b554788080c7d007d5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 19. Importers' duty to ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments are accompanied by relevant documentation.[^M_E_0949e3ae-986f-4a55-e36a-50a60c4f4cc3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments imported by the importer are accompanied by instructions and information in a language easily understood by end users

(2) Where end users are in Northern Ireland[^key-2f323ef850eab1b0b6d4d55f9b006c2d], the instructions and information referred to in paragraph (1) must be in English.
