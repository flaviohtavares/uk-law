# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Duty of distributors to ensure proper conditions of storage and transport

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A distributor must, in respect of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments under that distributor's responsibility, ensure that the conditions of their storage or transport are not such as to jeopardise their continuing compliance with the essential requirements.
