# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Action to be taken by distributors where regulated non-automatic weighing instruments placed on the market by them are not in conformity with essential requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a distributor considers, or has reason to believe, that a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument made available[^key-760ea8f14c388dd7d98bfb3ef91a0349] on the market by that distributor is not in conformity with the requirements of these Regulations.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the distributor must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to bring the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument into conformity, or withdraw or recall it, if appropriate.

(3) Where the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the competent authority to that effect, giving details, in particular, of the non-compliance of the instrument and of the corrective measures taken by that distributor.
