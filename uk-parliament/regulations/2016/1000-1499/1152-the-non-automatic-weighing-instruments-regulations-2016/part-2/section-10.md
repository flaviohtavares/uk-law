# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Manufacturers to mark contact details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_6d771946-9e79-476a-db00-801c3f82d17d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer must indicate on every regulated non-automatic weighing instruments manufactured by that manufacturer, the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or registered trade mark and the postal address at which the manufacturer can be contacted.

(2) The address required by these Regulations must indicate a single point at which the manufacturer can be contacted.

(3) The contact details required by this regulation must be clear, legible and in easily understandable[^c-f7e0e4265f1fb124af92523ee595eccd] English.

## 10. Manufacturers to mark contact details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_c4268810-2075-4a97-e401-79e5427eb91f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer must indicate on every regulated non-automatic weighing instruments manufactured by that manufacturer, the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or registered trade mark and the postal address at which the manufacturer can be contacted.

(2) The address required by these Regulations must indicate a single point at which the manufacturer can be contacted.

(3) The contact details required by this regulation must be in a language that is easily understood by end-users and market surveillance authorities and in the case of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments made available in Northern Ireland[^c-a182d22e4806010cc738fc5e6eb4b8d3], they must be in English.
