# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Requirements to mark importers' details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_9522310f-10f0-4d2c-8995-5aadb8c54867][^key-ba0446179a88e528f401d58629389d2a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must indicate on any regulated non-automatic weighing instrument imported by that importer, the importer's name, registered trade name or trademark, and the postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2)[^key-a12f24fa1789c1a1e15f74378d30f894] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) the importer would have to open the packaging in order to indicate the information on the instrument; or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the instrument from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^key-639a21e39b2a11da18fbebfd789d6ce1] beginning with IP completion day, and
- (b) before placing the instrument on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) where sub-paragraph (a)(i) applies, on the packaging and in a document accompanying the instrument;
  - (ii) where sub-paragraph (a)(ii) applies, in a document accompanying the instrument.

(3) The contact details required by this regulation must be clear, legible and in easily understandable[^key-61806bed2241a036beb56d6ca0f19c48] English.

## 18. Requirements to mark importers' details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_21f8e3ec-b28f-4655-d989-ef37599c0df3][^V_NI_8daa6ad3-aec8-32c5-09a3-ccf679e6df06]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must indicate on any regulated non-automatic weighing instrument imported by that importer, the importer's name, registered trade name or trademark, and the postal address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) Where this would require the packaging to be opened, those indications may be given on the packaging and in a document accompanying the instrument.

(3) The contact details required by this regulation must be in a language that is easily understood by end-users and market surveillance authorities, and in the case of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments made available in Northern Ireland[^key-f4317cc315440cbabf165b405a6b3839], they must be in English.
