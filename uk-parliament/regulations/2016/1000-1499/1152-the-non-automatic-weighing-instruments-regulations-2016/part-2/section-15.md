# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 15. Introductory[^M_E_02c374b3-9d95-41ec-fe4f-76b35892de57]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Chapter applies to the placing on the market of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument that is imported into the United Kingdom from a country outside the United Kingdom[^c-345f15f4874462287db58f984464db4d].

## 15. Introductory[^M_E_a95e2b5d-16b1-4a3f-8816-fb452dd77969]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies to the placing on the market of a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument that is imported into Northern Ireland[^c-4000c2e30b8b5f307d1cc75f70dba79d] from a country outside the relevant market[^c-0d3d68ed28818388ec95ffc8a4f61d82].
