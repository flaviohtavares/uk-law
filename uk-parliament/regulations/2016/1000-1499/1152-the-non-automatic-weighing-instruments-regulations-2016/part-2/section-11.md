# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 11. Documentation to accompany regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_c3b1fb1b-475d-4275-8f13-1ae3fafc1dc4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer must ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments manufactured by that manufacturer are accompanied by instructions and information easily understood by end-users.

(2)[^c-6d1ddd961356eb26618601b8b859513d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Such instructions and information and any labelling relating to a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument must be clear, legible and in easily understandable English[^c-9ff26de436aaa15df5ba69261b601a1d].

## 11. Documentation to accompany regulated non-automatic weighing instruments[^M_E_0e7cb58c-33c3-42bf-b451-7689493ff22c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer must ensure that regulated non-automatic weighing instruments manufactured by that manufacturer are accompanied by instructions and information easily understood by end-users.

(2) Where end-users are in Northern Ireland[^c-f75570887e5fbc617ad021acb814739d], those instructions and information must be in English.

(3) Such instructions and information and any labelling relating to a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument must be clear, understandable and intelligible.
