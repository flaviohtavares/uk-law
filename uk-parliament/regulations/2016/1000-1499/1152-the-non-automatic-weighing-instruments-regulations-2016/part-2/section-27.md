# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Distributors – verification obligations[^M_E_789547b2-59ac-4b4f-c57f-ff537851554e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The distributor must verify that the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument bears the UK[^key-b66a7d5738d6df5b8022e81fa00ce23b] marking and the M marking.

(2) The distributor must verify that the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument, it is accompanied by instructions and information which are clear, legible and in easily understandable English[^key-f37ba111451784c254f9dc5c77940284].

(3)[^key-e05c2ae91b1966fce28de05f969eef49] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The distributor must verify that the manufacturer and the importer have complied with the requirements set out in regulation 9 (manufacturers' obligations in relation to the marking of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments with serial numbers etc.), regulation 10 (manufacturers to mark contact details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments) and regulation 18 (requirements to mark importers' details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments).

## 27. Distributors – verification obligations[^M_E_502be4c1-79d1-4373-ee52-26809443f1a0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The distributor must verify that the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument bears the CE marking and the M marking.

(2) The distributor must verify that the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument, it is accompanied by instructions and information easily understood by end users.

(3) Instructions and information supplied in accordance with this regulation must be in a language that is easily understood by end users and where those users are in Northern Ireland[^key-6d81f4f9b441e95489e398dbc6a91fe0] must be in English.

(4) The distributor must verify that the manufacturer and the importer have complied with the requirements set out in regulation 9 (manufacturers' obligations in relation to the marking of regulated non-automatic weighing instruments with serial numbers etc.), regulation 10 (manufacturers to mark contact details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments) and regulation 18 (requirements to mark importers' details on regulated non-automatic weighing instruments).
