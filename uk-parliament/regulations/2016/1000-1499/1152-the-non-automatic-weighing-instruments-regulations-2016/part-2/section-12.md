# The Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/1152
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 12. Action to be taken where regulated non-automatic weighing instruments placed on the market are not in conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_14ff5958-39a3-444d-cc69-b51e496f7241]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies where a manufacturer considers or has reason to believe that a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument placed on the market by that manufacturer is not in conformity with the requirements of these Regulations.

(2) The manufacturer must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to bring the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument into conformity, or withdraw or recall it, if appropriate.

(3) Where the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the competent authority[^c-33028f1b42bc2a833eb7370eb0f1b962] to that effect giving details, in particular, of the non-compliance and of any corrective measures taken.

## 12. Action to be taken where regulated non-automatic weighing instruments placed on the market are not in conformity with the essential requirements[^M_E_5dd1f157-25ca-48cb-d876-778ccbb18627]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a manufacturer considers or has reason to believe that a regulated non-automatic weighing instrument placed on the market by that manufacturer is not in conformity with the requirements of these Regulations.

(2) The manufacturer must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to bring the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument into conformity, or withdraw or recall it, if appropriate.

(3) Where the regulated non-automatic weighing instrument presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the competent national authorities of the relevant[^c-1277e69c59445ea9c33c29b6cd566764] states in which the instrument has been made available on the market to that effect giving details, in particular, of the non-compliance and of any corrective measures taken.
