# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2016, section 12

> Text as at 2025-05-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Contractual terms restricting choice of auditor[^c-d36b241687d18e2a919773173993ee71]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to any term in a contract which, in relation to the conduct of a statutory audit of an audited person other than a person which is a public interest entity, provides for the restriction of the audited person’s choice of statutory auditor to certain categories or lists of statutory auditors.

(2) A term to which this regulation applies has no effect.
