# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2016, section 2

> Text as at 2025-05-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Companies Act 2006 [^c23154821];
- “appropriate qualification”, “audited person”, “equivalent third country”,[^key-cdc19c42d334450c7372f2324c0d6a43] [^key-0aaf1744b1492dcbe7c7487a3bbb5ab9]... [^key-0aaf1744b1492dcbe7c7487a3bbb5ab9]... “firm”, “group”, “**parent undertaking**”, “regulated market”,[^key-841d4d084668dfed6bc0b725ea5d8c7a] “**statutory audit**”, “**statutory auditor**”, “**statutory audit work**”, “third country”, “third country auditor”, “transferable securities”, “transitional third country” and “UK regulated market”[^key-c857f17c2db94b401cea6d14fd98183a] have the same meaning as in Part 42 of the Act;
- “[^key-8ddccc7b14cf1b4827eef549db7bf2e1]**audit committee**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 2 of Part 16 of the Act;
- “**Audit Directive**” means Directive [2006/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0043) of the European Parliament and of the Council on statutory audits of annual accounts and consolidated accounts, amending Council Directives [78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0660) and [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0349) and repealing Council Directive [84/253/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0253) [^c23154831];
- “**Audit Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) 537/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 on specific requirements regarding statutory audit of public-interest entities and repealing Commission Decision [2005/909/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0909) [^c23154841];
- “**audit report**” means the report required in accordance with the audit reporting requirements;
- “**audit reporting requirements**” means the requirements of—
  - (a) sections 495 to 498A [^c23154851] and 503 to 506 of the Act,
  - (b) sections 495, 496,[^key-a87d888e1bf0f1c32babb7d18a481064] 498 and 503 to 506 of the Act as applied to an LLP by the Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008 (and “**LLP**” has the meaning given by regulation 3 of those Regulations),
  - (c) sections 495, 496 and 498 of the Act as applied to the auditor of a qualifying partnership, and sections 503 to 506 of that Act as applied in relation to the auditors' report for a qualifying partnership, by the Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008 [^c23154861] (“**qualifying partnership**” has the meaning given by regulation 3 of those Regulations),
  - (d) sections 495 to 498A and 503 to 506[^key-747d92f252688755a82c6a77d83a4fe9] of the Act as applied to an unregistered company by the Unregistered Companies Regulations 2009 [^c23154871] (“**unregistered company**” has the meaning given by regulation 2 of those Regulations),
  - (e) [^key-5686b750a0790cda8fa612966aa296e9]...
  - (f) sections 495 , 496[^key-79bfaf8935bd93a61f842bf8262ea2c7] and 498 of the Act as applied to an auditor of an insurance undertaking and sections 503 to 506 of the Act as applied in relation to the auditor's report for an insurance undertaking, by the Insurance Accounts Directive (Miscellaneous Insurance Undertakings) Regulations 2008 [^c23154891] (“**insurance undertaking**” has the meaning given by regulation 2 of those Regulations),
  - (g) sections 78 to 79 of the Building Societies Act 1986 [^c23154901],
  - (h) sections 73 to 74C of the Friendly Societies Act 1992 [^c23154911], in relation to the audit of a friendly society which is subject to special requirements under that Act (see section 78A(3) of that Act)[^key-7c19addc65fb5c8a80c88ae44eec1f1a],
  - (i) regulations 3, 10 to 14 and 22 to 26 of the Insurance Accounts Directive (Lloyd's Syndicate and Aggregate Accounts) Regulations 2008 [^c23154931],
  - (j) Articles 10 and 11 of the Audit Regulation;
- “**competent authority**” means the Financial Reporting Council Limited [^c23154941];
- “**financial year**” has the same meaning as in section 390 of the Act;
- “**group auditor**” means the statutory auditor appointed to audit the consolidated accounts of a group and “**group audit**” shall be construed accordingly;
- “[^key-b60d48372c0995776fa6d537474899da]**international auditing standards**” means International Standards on Auditing, International Standard on Quality Control and other related Standards issued from time to time by the International Federation of Accountants through the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board, in so far as they are relevant to the statutory audit;
- “**network**” means an association of persons other than a firm co-operating in audit work by way of—
  - (a) profit-sharing,
  - (b) cost-sharing,
  - (c) common ownership, control or management,
  - (d) common quality control policies and procedures,
  - (e) common business strategy, or
  - (f) use of a common name;
- “**personal data**” has the same meaning as in section 1(1) of the Data Protection Act 1998 [^c23154951];
- “**public interest entity**” means—
  - (a) an issuer whose transferable securities are admitted to trading on a UK regulated market[^key-74766cfbb5f9b5768bbea1628b8f1080],
  - (b) a credit institution within the meaning given by Article 4(1)(1) of Regulation (EU) No. 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^c23154961], which is a CRR firm within the meaning of Article 4(1)(2A) of that Regulation[^key-0e266bd7c49e4c3a8689937fd6a5340b] [^c23154971], or
  - (c) a person who would be an insurance undertaking as defined in Article 2(1) of Council Directive[^key-4a5de609993a93cbca4dc729b6a7127f] [91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/674) of 19 December 1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the annual accounts of insurance undertakings as that Article had effect immediately before IP completion day, were the United Kingdom a member State; ;
- “**issuer**” [^key-c950ce17292a0646642b2d5f191028ef]... has[^key-b8dee8d6ec4754034ce02b9fa82d3703] the same meaning as in Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23154991];
- “**recognised supervisory body**” means a supervisory body, within the meaning in section 1217(1) of the Act, recognised in accordance with Schedule 10 of that Act (and “**member**”, in relation to a recognised supervisory body, has the meaning given by section 1217(2) of that Act);
- “**sanction**” means any measure taken by the competent authority under regulation 5;
- “[^key-ba39921cec2489987024c26ae985cda3]**third country competent authority**” means a body established in a third country exercising functions related to the regulation or oversight of auditors;
- “**UK-adopted international standards**” means the international auditing standards adopted by the competent authority in accordance with regulation 4(3)(za).
- [^key-a17b080cc763502152debfea1fb21c77]...
