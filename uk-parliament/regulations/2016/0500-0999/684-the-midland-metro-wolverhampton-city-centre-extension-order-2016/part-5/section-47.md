> Text as at 2017-12-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Saving for highway authorities[^c-4813b393266775e5331ab894b29ae22a]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Order affects any power of a highway authority to widen, alter, divert or improve any highway along which the authorised tramway is constructed.
