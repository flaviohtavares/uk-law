# The Midland Metro (Wolverhampton City Centre Extension) Order 2016, section 44

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44. Planning permission[^key-8a610cb9bf549322d479c971c2fd47d4]

*Extent: E+W*

Planning permission which is deemed by a direction under section 90(2A)[^f00043] (development with government authorisation) of the 1990 Act to be granted in relation to the authorised works is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act) of that Act.
