# The Midland Metro (Wolverhampton City Centre Extension) Order 2016, section 41

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Power to charge fares[^c-6eb10a43dbad0fef186abcfcb4608922]

*Extent: E+W*

The Authority may demand, take and recover or waive such charges for carrying passengers or goods on tramcars using the authorised tramway or for any other services or facilities provided in connection with the operation of the authorised tramway, as it thinks fit.
