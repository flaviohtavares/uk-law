# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 28

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Amendments to Part 1 (introduction)[^c-9483315cd18234c021823f709a2c08b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 1(2) (interpretation), for the definition of “LLP” substitute—

> - “LLP” means a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000[^f00042];

.
