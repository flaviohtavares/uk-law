# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 29

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Amendments to Part 2 (form and content of individual accounts)[^c-c67baa108cdc3f12091cc9aff50d5608]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 3(1) (non-IAS individual accounts), in the modified regulation 3 of the Small Companies Accounts Regulations which is applied to LLPs—

- (a) at the beginning of paragraph (1) insert “Subject to the following provisions of this regulation and regulation 5A,”;
- (b) after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) Sections C (alternative accounting rules) and D (fair value accounting) in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations do not apply to an LLP which qualifies as a micro-entity in relation to a financial year (see sections 384A and 384B of the 2006 Act) and whose accounts for that year are prepared in accordance with the exemption permitted by—
  >
  > - (a) regulation 5A, or
  > - (b) paragraph 1(1A) of Section A in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

  ; and
- (c) omit paragraph (2).
