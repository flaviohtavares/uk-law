# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 45

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Amendments to Schedule 5 (general interpretation)[^key-50170184d737e76382312fd9c31f6e8f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 5 (general interpretation)—

- (a) for paragraph 2(1) (definitions of financial instruments) substitute—

  > (1) The expressions listed in sub-paragraph (2) have the same meaning as they have in [Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc. of certain types of undertakings[^f00043].

  ;
- (b) in paragraph 8(5) (participating interests)—
  - (i) before “Part 1 of Schedule 1” insert “Section B of”; and
  - (ii) omit “and Part 1 of Schedule 3”; and
- (c) in paragraph 12(3) (staff costs), for “the LLP’s profit and loss account” substitute “the profit and loss account Format 2 in Section B of Part 1 of Schedule 1”.
