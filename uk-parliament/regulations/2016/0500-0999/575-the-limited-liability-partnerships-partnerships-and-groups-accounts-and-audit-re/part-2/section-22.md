# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 22

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Exemption from audit: qualifying subsidiaries[^key-6201740e101c077cac8cbe18177a223c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 34A[^f00037] (exemption from audit: qualifying subsidiaries) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 479A(2)(c) (subsidiary LLPs: conditions for exemption from audit), for sub-paragraph (i) substitute—

> - (i) the provisions of [Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc. of certain types of undertakings, or

.

(3) In section 479B (LLPs excluded from the subsidiary audit exemption), before paragraph (a) insert—

> - (za) a traded LLP as defined in section 474(1),

.
