> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Filing obligations of large LLPs[^key-41c4f3ee1ce5856e1c16ac9eca4617cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 19 (filing obligations of large LLPs) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 446 (filing obligations of large LLPs), at the end of subsection (2) insert—

> This does not apply if the LLP is exempt from audit and the members have taken advantage of that exemption.

.
