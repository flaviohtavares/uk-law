# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 15

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Exemption for dormant subsidiary LLPs[^key-5f45318943ca4c372a233def5094d00c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 19A[^f00031] (exemption for dormant subsidiary LLPs) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 448A (dormant subsidiary LLPs exempt from obligation to file accounts), for subsection (2)(c)(i) substitute—

> - (i) the provisions of [Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc. of certain types of undertakings[^f00032], or

.

(3) In section 448B (LLPs excluded from the dormant subsidiaries exemption), before paragraph (a) insert—

> - (za) a traded LLP,

.
