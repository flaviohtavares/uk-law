## 19. Meaning of “annual accounts”[^c-98a3d8b315dff14f484052c6f9d5b3b6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 29 (meaning of “annual accounts”) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 471(1)[^f00033] (meaning of “annual accounts” and related expressions), for “given in group accounts” substitute “given in notes to the individual balance sheet”.
