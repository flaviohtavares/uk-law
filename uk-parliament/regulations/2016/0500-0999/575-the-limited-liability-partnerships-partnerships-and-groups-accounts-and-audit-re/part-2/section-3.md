# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 3

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Amendments to the Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008[^key-982fc26b3b8c2a9de7ce4cdd3b802641]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The 2008 Regulations are amended in accordance with this Part.

(2) Any reference in this Part to a numbered section is a reference to the section so numbered in the Act as applied to LLPs by the 2008 Regulations.
