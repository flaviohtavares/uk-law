# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 20

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Notes to the accounts[^key-6d8acbccfd93d5d0f8616e4aee889632]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 30 (notes to the accounts) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 472 (notes to the accounts)—

- (a) omit subsection (1); and
- (b) before subsection (2) insert—

  > (1A) In the case of an LLP which qualifies as a micro-entity in relation to a financial year (see sections 384A and 384B), the notes to the accounts for that year required by regulation 5A[^f00034] of, and paragraph 55 of Part 3 of Schedule 1[^f00035] to, the Small Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1912](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1912)) must be included at the foot of the balance sheet.

  .
