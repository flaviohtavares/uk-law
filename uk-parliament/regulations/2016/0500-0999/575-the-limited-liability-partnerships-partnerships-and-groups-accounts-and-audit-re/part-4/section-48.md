# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 48

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Amendment to Part 1 (introduction)[^c-86db135b639b59f9f0e736060e263c78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 1(2) (interpretation), for the definition of “LLP” substitute—

> “LLP” means a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000[^f00046].
