# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 59

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Amendments to Part 3 of Schedule 2 (LLP required to prepare group accounts: information on related undertakings required whether preparing non-IAS or IAS accounts)[^key-58f2bffb99d0ebfb23f8e10fd34b2c82]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Part 3 of Schedule 2 (LLP required to prepare group accounts: information on related undertakings required whether preparing non-IAS or IAS accounts)—

- (a) for paragraph 16(1)(b) (joint ventures) substitute—

  > - (b) the address of the undertaking’s registered office (whether in or outside the United Kingdom),

  ; and
- (b) for paragraph 17(3)(b) (associated undertakings) substitute—

  > - (b) the address of the undertaking’s registered office (whether in or outside the United Kingdom).

  .
