# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 61

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendments to Schedule 4 (general interpretation)[^key-7fd0d9accd0555b2749535ea9dfdd3df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 4 (definitions of financial instruments), for the words from “Council [Directive 78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0660)” to the end, substitute “[Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc. of certain types of undertakings[^f00047] and Council [Directive 91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0674) of 19 December 1991 on the annual accounts and consolidated accounts of insurance undertakings[^f00048].”.
