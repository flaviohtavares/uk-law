# The Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016, section 1

> Text as at 2016-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and interpretation[^c-83bcc3770dd7918e68c3c584c8a905b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Limited Liability Partnerships, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2016.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the Act” means the Companies Act 2006[^f00007];
- “the Large and Medium-sized Companies Accounts Regulations” means the Large and Medium-sized Companies and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2008[^f00008];
- “the Small Companies Accounts Regulations” means the Small Companies and Groups (Accounts and Directors’ Report) Regulations 2008[^f00009];
- “the 2008 Regulations” means the Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008[^f00010]; and
- “LLP” means a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000.
