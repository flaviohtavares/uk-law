# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.3) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 995

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No.3) Order 2016

Made

12th October 2016

Coming into force

13th October 2016

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of October 2016

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2016 and comes into force on 13th October 2016.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
