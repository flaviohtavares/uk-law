# The North Wales Wind Farms Connection Order 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2017-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Disapplication of legislative provisions[^key-e2429ba57b69209665c7aa97ea8be369]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 23 of the Land Drainage Act 1991[^f00020] does not apply in relation to the construction of works carried out for the purpose of, or in connection with, the construction or maintenance of the authorised development.
