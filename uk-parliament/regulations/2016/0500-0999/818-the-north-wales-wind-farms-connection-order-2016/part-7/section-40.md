# The North Wales Wind Farms Connection Order 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2017-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Arbitration[^c-5a0c1842a3b51f233a84fce618ffdd9a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 38 (procedure in relation to certain approvals etc.) and except where otherwise provided for in this Order any difference under any provision of this Order, is to be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
