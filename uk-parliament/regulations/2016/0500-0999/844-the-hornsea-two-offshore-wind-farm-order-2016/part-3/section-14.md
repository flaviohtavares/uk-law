> Text as at 2018-05-08.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Highway improvements[^c-d1627df3a7d07f6c611e0b46d885f070]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may carry out highway improvements to the plot marked 133 on the land plans.

(2) The highway improvements must be carried out in accordance with plans approved by the highway authority, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld.

(3) If the highway authority fails to notify the undertaker of its decision within 28 days of receiving plans for approval under paragraph (2), the highway authority is deemed to have given approval.
