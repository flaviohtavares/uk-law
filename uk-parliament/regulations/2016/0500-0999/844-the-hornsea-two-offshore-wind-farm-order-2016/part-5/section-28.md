# The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016, section 28

> Text as at 2018-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Statutory undertakers[^key-392d04a0d20e7ecf1940f34d1fcae34f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to Schedule 12 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) exercise the powers conferred by articles 18 and 19 in relation to so much of any land referred to in those articles as belongs to statutory undertakers; and
- (b) extinguish the rights of, remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers over or within the Order land referred to in those articles.
