# The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2018-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Deemed marine licences under Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^key-1dcca7687b1a31d8fbdbe2684a02d568]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 8 to 11 are deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensed activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the Conditions set out in Part 2, of each licence.
