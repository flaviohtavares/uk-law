# The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016, section 32

> Text as at 2018-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Operational land for purposes of 1990 Act[^c-bef17ae9761d2d0ef0f2f76f18804abb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order must be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act).
