# The Hornsea Two Offshore Wind Farm Order 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2018-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Certification of plans, etc.[^c-c9e804f3c13ce8aaf9994a70370cf9cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after this Order is made, submit to the Secretary of State copies of the documents listed in Schedule 13 (documents to be certified) for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
