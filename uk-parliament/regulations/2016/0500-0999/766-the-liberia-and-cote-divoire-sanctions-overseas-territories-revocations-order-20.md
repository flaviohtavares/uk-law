# The Liberia and Côte d’Ivoire (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 766

Overseas Territories

The Liberia and Côte d’Ivoire (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2016

Made

13th July 2016

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2016

Coming into force

10th August 2016

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of July 2016

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Under article 41 of the Charter of the United Nations, the Security Council of the United Nations has, by resolutions adopted on 28th April 2016 and 25th May 2016, terminated the measures adopted in respect of Côte d’Ivoire and Liberia respectively:

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1 of the United Nations Act 1946[^f00001], section 112 of the Saint Helena Act 1833[^f00002], the British Settlements Acts 1887 and 1945[^f00003], and all of the other powers enabling Her to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Liberia and Côte d’Ivoire (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2016 and comes into force on 10th August 2016.

(2) This Order extends to the territories listed in the Schedule.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders are revoked:

- (a) the Côte d’Ivoire (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2012[^f00004]; and
- (b) the Liberia (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2015[^f00005].

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Territories to which this Order extends

Article 1(2)

- Anguilla
- British Antarctic Territory
- British Indian Ocean Territory
- Cayman Islands
- Falkland Islands
- Montserrat
- Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno Islands
- St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha
- South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands
- The Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia in the Island of Cyprus
- Turks and Caicos Islands
- Virgin Islands

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Côte d’Ivoire (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/3067](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/3067)) and the Liberia (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1899](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1899)), which gave effect in specified Overseas Territories to sanctions adopted by the United Nations Security Council and the European Union against Côte d’Ivoire and Liberia respectively. The Order reflects the termination of those sanctions by the United Nations Security Council and the European Union.
