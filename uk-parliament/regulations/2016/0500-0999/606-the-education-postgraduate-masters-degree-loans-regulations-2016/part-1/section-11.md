# The Education (Postgraduate Master's Degree Loans) Regulations 2016, section 11

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Requirement to enter into a contract for a postgraduate master's degree loan

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) To receive a postgraduate master's degree loan a student must enter into a contract with the Secretary of State.

(2) Where the Secretary of State requires a contract to be signed by a student, an electronic signature in such form as the Secretary of State may specify satisfies such a requirement.
