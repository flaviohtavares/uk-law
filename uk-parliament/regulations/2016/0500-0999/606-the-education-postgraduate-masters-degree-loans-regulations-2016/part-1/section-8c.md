# The Education (Postgraduate Master's Degree Loans) Regulations 2016, section 8C

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8C. Restriction of designation of courses taught or delivered by franchised providers[^key-8ce8f101023d5495af75f4a1f083bbbf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to courses beginning on or after 1st September 2028.

(2) A course which is taught or delivered by a franchised provider on behalf of a lead provider in England will not be a designated course unless that franchised provider is a qualifying franchised provider.
