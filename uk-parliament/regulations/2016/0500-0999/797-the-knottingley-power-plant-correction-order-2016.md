# The Knottingley Power Plant (Correction) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2016 No. 797

Infrastructure Planning

The Knottingley Power Plant (Correction) Order 2016

Made

21st July 2016

Coming into force

22nd July 2016

The Knottingley Power Plant Order 2015[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors and omissions within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

Before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors in the Knottingley Power Plant Order 2015 under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

The Secretary of State has informed North Yorkshire County Council, Selby District Council and Wakefield Metropolitan District Council, the local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the Knottingley Power Plant Station Order 2015 relates is situated, that the request has been received in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Knottingley Power Plant (Correction) Order 2016 and comes into force on 22nd July 2016.

## 2. Corrections

(1) The Knottingley Power Plant Order 2015 is to be corrected as provided for in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) the first column sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) the second column sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) the third column sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Giles Scott

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Coal Liabilities

Department of Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors and omissions identified in the Knottingley Power Plant Order 2015, a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request from the applicant under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 of that Act.
