> Extent: Scotland.

## 62. Notice of intention to resign[^key-8add3377a3756c58cde9a6e501d7bd4f]

*Extent: S*

The postal administrator must give to the persons specified below at least 5 business days’ notice in Form PA20(S) of the postal administrator’s intention to resign, or to apply for the court’s leave to do so—

- (a) the Secretary of State;
- (b) OFCOM;
- (c) if there is a continuing postal administrator of the company, [^key-45fd7f46502de1b032f308f27529dba3]... the continuing postal administrator; and
- (d) if there is no such postal administrator, [^key-7474a52eb71f3edfc2f32a8cecd8f309]... the company and its creditors.
