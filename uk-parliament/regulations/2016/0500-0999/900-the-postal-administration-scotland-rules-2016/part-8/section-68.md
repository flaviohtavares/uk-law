## 68. Joint or concurrent appointments[^key-60078f977ca6514d5b54ed0e74b638d5]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a person is appointed in accordance with paragraph 103 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act to act as postal administrator jointly or concurrently with the persons then acting, the same provisions shall apply, subject to this Rule and to such other modification as may be necessary, in respect of the making of this appointment as in the case of the original appointment of an administrator.

(2) The additional postal administrator shall send notice of the appointment in Form PA23(S) to the registrar of companies.
