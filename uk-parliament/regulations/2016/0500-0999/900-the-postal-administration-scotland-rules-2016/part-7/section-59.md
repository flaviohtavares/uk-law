## 59. Moving from postal administration to dissolution[^key-72e8cc9c3e6c1dfc802cf4ff0edc783e]

*Extent: S*

(1) The notice required by paragraph 84(1) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act shall be in Form PA18(S) and shall be accompanied by a final progress report.

(2) As soon as reasonably practicable a copy of the notice and accompanying documents shall be sent to—

- (a) all those who received notice of the postal administrator’s appointment;
- (b) where the Secretary of State did not receive notice of the postal administrator’s appointment, the Secretary of State; and
- (c) where OFCOM did not receive notice of the postal administrator’s appointment, to OFCOM.

(3) Where the court makes an order under paragraph 84(7) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act it shall, where the applicant is not the postal administrator, give a copy of the order to the postal administrator.

(4) The notice required by paragraph 84(8) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act shall be in Form PA19(S).

(5) OFCOM must notify the Secretary of State before directing the postal administrator to send a notice of moving from postal administration to dissolution to the registrar of companies.
