# The Postal Administration (Scotland) Rules 2016, section 82

> Text as at 2017-04-13.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 82. Notice by and to representatives[^c-4fb65199fa376bc532e35582056c5aa6]

*Extent: S*

Where under the 1986 Act or these Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given, sent or delivered by a person (“the sender”) to another (“the recipient”), it may be given, sent or delivered by any person duly authorised by the sender to do so to any person duly authorised by the recipient to receive or accept it.
