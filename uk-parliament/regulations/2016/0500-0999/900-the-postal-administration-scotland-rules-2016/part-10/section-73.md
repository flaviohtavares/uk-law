# The Postal Administration (Scotland) Rules 2016, section 73

> Text as at 2017-04-13.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 73. Form of proxy[^key-9ed99888f7a7b2ccca92682f3d2fd7e7]

*Extent: S*

(1) With every notice summoning a meeting of creditors or of the company in postal administration proceedings there shall be sent out forms of proxy.

(2) A form of proxy shall not be sent out with the name or description of any person inserted in it.

(3) A proxy shall be in the form sent out with the notice summoning the meeting or in a form substantially to the same effect.

(4) A form of proxy shall be filled out and signed by the principal, or by some person acting under the authority of the principal and, where it is signed by someone other than the principal, the nature of that person’s authority shall be stated on the form.
