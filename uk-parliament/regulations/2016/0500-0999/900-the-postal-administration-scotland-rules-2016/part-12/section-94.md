> Text as at 2017-04-13.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 94. Punishment of offences[^key-8f0b1e548fd427c4ab09d4abfe1ce300]

*Extent: S*

Section 431 (summary proceedings) of the 1986 Act[^f00024], as it applies to Scotland, has effect in relation to offences under these Rules as to offences under the 1986 Act.
