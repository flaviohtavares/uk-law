## 90. Evidence of proceedings at meetings[^c-ecc450af16d83924160e0984575266a7]

*Extent: S*

A report of proceedings at a meeting of the company or of the creditors in a postal administration, signed by the chair of that meeting, shall be deemed, unless the contrary is shown, to be sufficient evidence of the matters contained in that report.
