## 95. Forms for use in postal administration proceedings[^key-0def04a14514c9a1cf0df855546d4147]

*Extent: S*

(1) The forms contained in the Schedule to these Rules shall be used in, and in connection with, postal administration proceedings.

(2) The forms shall be used with such variations, if any, as the circumstances may require.
