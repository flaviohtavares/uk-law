# The Postal Administration (Scotland) Rules 2016, section 101

> Text as at 2017-04-13.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 101. Omission of unobtainable information[^c-3ccae311b3a3eb3f33f42ee3b5f06615]

*Extent: S*

Information required under Rules 98[^M_F_d57468dd-bec6-423b-c598-ab469cfcd903] and 99 to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
