## 89. Validity of proceedings[^key-a858b285f6c659eee46ca48f17d03f1b]

*Extent: S*

Where in accordance with the 1986 Act or these Rules a meeting of creditors or other persons is summoned by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly summoned and held, notwithstanding that not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
