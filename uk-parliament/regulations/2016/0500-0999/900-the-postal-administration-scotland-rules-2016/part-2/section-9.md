## 9. Notice of dismissal of application for a postal administration order[^key-fcbb734e53aa3b218ec177bd6df14dad]

*Extent: S*

If the court dismisses the petition the petitioner shall as soon as reasonably practicable send notice of the court’s order dismissing the petition to all those to whom the petition was notified under Rule 7[^key-3b70fec12261cc8bab1e1c216a3de2e8] in Form PA3(S).
