## 6. Statement of proposed postal administrator[^key-e9c506056a65dd164e05f1ca71ae4594]

*Extent: S*

There shall be lodged together with the petition a statement of the proposed postal administrator, in the form required by Form PA1(S), stating—

- (a) that the proposed postal administrator consents to accept appointment as postal administrator of the company; and
- (b) details of any prior professional relationship that the proposed postal administrator has had with the company.
