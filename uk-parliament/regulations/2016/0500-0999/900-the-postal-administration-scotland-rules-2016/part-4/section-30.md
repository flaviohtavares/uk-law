## 30. Entitlement to vote (creditors and members)[^key-a47182e50fd577ae3b90d2811a2e26fa]

*Extent: S*

(1) Part 5 (claims in postal administration) (except Rule 38(2) and (3)[^key-56714227cc94da79f38bbc499901b68d]) applies for the purpose of determining a creditor’s entitlement to vote at any creditors’ meeting in a postal administration.

(2) Members of a company at their meetings shall vote according to the rights attaching to their shares in accordance with the articles of association.

(3) The reference in paragraph (2) to a member’s share shall include any other interests which the member may have as a member of the company.
