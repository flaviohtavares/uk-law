## 39. Deemed consent under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^key-f61650bba36159f653b7ed86c6456b47]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licence set out in Schedule 9 is deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensable marine activities (as defined in section 66 of the 2009 Act) set out in Part 2, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 3, of the licence
