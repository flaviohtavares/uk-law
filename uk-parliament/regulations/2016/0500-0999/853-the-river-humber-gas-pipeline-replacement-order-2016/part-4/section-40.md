# The River Humber Gas Pipeline Replacement Order 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-761276a4fa2d3e9df4b3d1e882f9d73d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act).
