# The River Humber Gas Pipeline Replacement Order 2016, section 46

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Arbitration[^c-5164306b656e61824e37f5a0035b2a26]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any difference under any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for (including determination by the tribunal) or unless otherwise agreed between the parties, must be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
