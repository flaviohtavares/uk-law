# The River Humber Gas Pipeline Replacement Order 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Statutory undertakers[^key-5d6c0f93503ad7d760503c8dc7dcd1d1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of Schedule 10 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily the land belonging to statutory undertakers within the Order limits as shown on the land plans and described in the book of reference;
- (b) extinguish the rights of, remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers where such apparatus is anywhere within the Order limits; and
- (c) acquire compulsorily the new rights over land belonging to statutory undertakers within the Order limits described in the book of reference and shown on the land plans.
