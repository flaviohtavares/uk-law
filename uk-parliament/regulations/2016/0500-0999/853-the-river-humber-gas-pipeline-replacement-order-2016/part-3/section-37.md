# The River Humber Gas Pipeline Replacement Order 2016, section 37

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Incorporation of the mineral code[^c-4079fe72ea998e0b53572f1dc3efa675]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00025] (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”; and
- (c) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
