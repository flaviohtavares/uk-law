# The River Humber Gas Pipeline Replacement Order 2016, section 31

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. No double recovery[^c-ccf82fbcfc9ecdaea4c3ca83865c4384]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions under this Order.
