# The Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 956 (C. 67)

Housing, England

Acquisition Of Land, England And Wales

The Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2016

Made

27th September 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 216(3) of the Housing and Planning Act 2016[^f00001] makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2016

The following provisions of the Housing and Planning Act 2016 come into force on 1st October 2016—

- (a) sections 80 to 91 (rents for high income social tenants); and
- (b) the following provisions of Schedule 15 and section 183 (notice of general vesting declaration procedure) so far as it relates to those provisions–
  - (i) paragraphs 1, 2(1) and 2(2) for the purpose only of exercising the power to prescribe by regulations the statement referred to in section 15(4)(e) of the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00002] and the form referred to in section 15(4)(f) of that Act;
  - (ii) paragraphs 1, 3(1) and 3(2) for the purpose only of exercising the power to prescribe by regulations the statement referred to in paragraph 6(4)(e) of Schedule 1 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 and the form referred to in paragraph 6(4)(f) of Schedule 1 to that Act; and
  - (iii) paragraph 8.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Gavin Barwell

Minister of State

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force various provisions of the Housing and Planning Act [2016 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/22) (“the Act”) on 1st October 2016.

Regulation 2(a) brings into force Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the Act which makes provision relating to rents for high income social tenants of local housing authorities (sections 80 to 87) and private registered providers of social housing (sections 89 and 90). Section 91 provides for the interpretation of Chapter 3 of Part 4.

Regulation 2(b) brings into force section 183 of and paragraphs 1, 2(1) and 2(2) and 3(1) and 3(2) of Schedule 15 to, the Act for the purposes of prescribing statements and forms under section 15(4)(e) and (f) of, and paragraph 6(4)(e) and (f) of Schedule 1 to, the Acquisition of Land Act [1981(c.67)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/67). Regulation 2(b) also commences paragraph 8 of Schedule 15, which enables the Secretary of State to make regulations to amend legislation on compulsory purchase for the purpose of making amendments which correspond to those made by that Schedule.
