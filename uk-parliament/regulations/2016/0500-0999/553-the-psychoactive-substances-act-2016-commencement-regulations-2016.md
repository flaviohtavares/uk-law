# The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Commencement) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 553 (C. 36)

Dangerous Drugs

The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Commencement) Regulations 2016

Made

3rd May 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) of the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Commencement) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Commencement

The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 comes into force on 26th May 2016 in so far as it is not already in force.

Karen Bradley

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

3rd May 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring the provisions of the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 into force on 26th May 2016 in so far as they are not already in force. Sections 59, 61, 62, 63 and all enabling powers came into force on Royal Assent.
