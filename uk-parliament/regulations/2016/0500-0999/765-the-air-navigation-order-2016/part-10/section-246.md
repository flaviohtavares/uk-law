# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 246

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246. Stowaways

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not secrete themself for the purpose of being carried in an aircraft without the consent of either the operator or the pilot in command or of any other person entitled to give consent to being carried in the aircraft.
