# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 265G

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 265G. Fixed penalty offences[^key-e549c5702de2908e9afd739af27afbac][^key-f119a03c98b4d1313284dd2ca06e4c52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the case of an offence under any provision of this Order which is prescribed under paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 10 to the Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft Act 2021, this Order has effect subject to Schedule 10 to that Act (fixed penalties for certain offences relating to unmanned aircraft).
