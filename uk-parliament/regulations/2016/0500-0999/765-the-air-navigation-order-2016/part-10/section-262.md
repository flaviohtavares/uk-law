# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 262

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Access and inspection for airworthiness purposes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CAA may cause such inspections, investigations, tests, experiments and flight trials to be made as it deems necessary for the purposes of Part 4 of this Order or for the purposes of Part 21, Part 145 or Part M.

(2) Any person authorised to do so by the CAA may at any reasonable time inspect any part of, or material intended to be incorporated in or used in the manufacture of any part of, an aircraft or its equipment or any documents relating to the aircraft and may for that purpose go onto any aerodrome or enter any aircraft factory.
