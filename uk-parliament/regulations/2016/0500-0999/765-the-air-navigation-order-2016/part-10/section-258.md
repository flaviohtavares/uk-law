# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 258

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 258. Grounded aircraft not to fly

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An aircraft which has been grounded in accordance with paragraph ARO.RAMP.140 of Part-ARO and which has not subsequently been permitted to fly must not be flown.
