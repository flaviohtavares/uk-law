# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 268

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 268. Approval of persons to supply reports

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to any of its functions under any of the provisions of this Order the CAA may approve a person as qualified to supply reports to it and may accept such reports.
