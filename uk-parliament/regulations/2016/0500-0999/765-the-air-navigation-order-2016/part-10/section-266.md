# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 266

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 266. Exemption from Order

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The CAA may exempt from any of the provisions of this Order (other than articles 179, 230, 247, 250, 251, 252, 255, and 267) or any regulations made under this Order, any aircraft or persons or classes of aircraft or persons, subject to such conditions it deems appropriate.
