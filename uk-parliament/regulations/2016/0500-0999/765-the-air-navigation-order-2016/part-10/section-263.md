# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 263

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 263. Obstruction of persons

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not intentionally obstruct or impede any person who is exercising a power or performing a duty under this Order or under a Safety[^key-81c139d8d5df80bd4de72688bfe1c430] Regulation.
