# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 50

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Validity of the national airworthiness review certificate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A national airworthiness review certificate becomes invalid if—

- (a) suspended or revoked;
- (b) the certificate of airworthiness is suspended or revoked; or
- (c) the aircraft ceases to be registered in the United Kingdom.
