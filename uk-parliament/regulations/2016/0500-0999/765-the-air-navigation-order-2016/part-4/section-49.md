# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 49

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Requirement for and validity of a national airworthiness review certificate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft with a national certificate of airworthiness must not fly unless it has a valid national airworthiness review certificate.

(2) Subject to articles [^c-cc91bb7a34bd409c5f604e958d462c6c]... 50, 56, 57, 58 and 253, a national airworthiness review certificate is valid for one year.
