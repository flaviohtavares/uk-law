# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 16

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Public transport and commercial operations – exceptions – glider towing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A flight for the purpose of glider towing is a non-commercial flight if it meets the criteria and conditions relating to sailplane towing described in article 6(4a)(c) of the [^c-a07efb59c46a5aafa9bf1b0af58d479f]... Air Operations Regulation.
