# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 12

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Public transport and commercial operations – exceptions – charity flights

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A flight is a non-commercial flight if there has been no valuable consideration or[^c-1840a8074223f91d5167dd1ab5b949a9] the only valuable consideration given or promised for the flight [^c-d1022ee98f9df9673a5a0db81adf8b24]... is one or more of the following—

- (a) valuable consideration given or promised to a registered charity which is not the operator of the aircraft; or
- (b) valuable consideration for which the CAA has otherwise given permission.
