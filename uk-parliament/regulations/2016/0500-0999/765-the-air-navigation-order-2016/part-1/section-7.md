# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 7

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Meaning of “commercial operation”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Order, “**commercial operation**” means any operation of an aircraft other than[^key-9e54d8c659d4c20e39dc653bc72c1de8] for public transport—

- (a) which is available to the public; or
- (b)[^key-2c26376c4421178de6cb98ec5890d492] which, when not made available to the public, is performed under a contract between an operator and a customer, where the latter has no control over the operator,

in return for remuneration or other valuable consideration.
