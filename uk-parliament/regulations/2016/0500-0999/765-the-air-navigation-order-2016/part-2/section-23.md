# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 23

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Exceptions from application of provisions of the Order for certain classes of aircraft

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to—

- (a) any small balloon;
- (b) any kite weighing not more than 2kg;
- (c)[^key-67b109af48c07ddb479f6a177c12d158] any unmanned aircraft other than an unmanned aircraft subject to certification;
- (d) any parachute including a parascending parachute.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4)[^key-efadc817c8e035553afe9725806c5169], nothing in this Order applies to or in relation to an aircraft to which this article applies.

(3) Articles 2, 91, 92, 93,[^key-b4cfedbd395389681f12791e0dd67da8] [^key-032b6e0ccfccbdf199bdf327d2cc70ac]... 94A, 94B,[^key-2fbbf66810b37447cfc33d87deadfc86] 94BA,[^key-2a8a63589471144a37887acfb3cc6536] [^key-df6759704799691b3509ac4ad5e3b43c]... 239, 241 and 257 (except 257(2)(a)) apply to or in relation to an aircraft to which this article applies, and articles 253, 265, 266 and 269 apply[^key-be1b31d28462b63631a1a25b1c728dea] in relation to those articles.

(4)[^key-cfe7486e815a1fc1e5461fe785bda4ef] Articles [^key-cab81a861f1125c332619e8bbb55693e]... 265E and 265F apply to or in relation to unmanned aircraft to which this article applies, and article 265(3) applies in relation to those articles.
