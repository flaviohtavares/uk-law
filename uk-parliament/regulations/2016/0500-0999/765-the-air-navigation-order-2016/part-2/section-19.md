# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 19

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Application of the Order to British-controlled aircraft registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CAA may direct that such of the provisions of this Order and of any regulations made or having effect under this Order as may be specified in the direction have effect as if reference in those provisions to aircraft registered in the United Kingdom included references to the aircraft specified in the direction.

(2) A direction under paragraph (1) may only specify an aircraft registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom but for the time being under the management of a person who, or of persons each of whom, is qualified to hold a legal or beneficial interest by way of ownership in an aircraft registered in the United Kingdom.
