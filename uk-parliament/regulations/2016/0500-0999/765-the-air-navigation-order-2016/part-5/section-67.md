# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Flight crew required by aircraft registered in the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft registered in the United Kingdom must carry a flight crew adequate in number and description to ensure the safety of the aircraft.

(2) An aircraft—

- (a) which has a flight manual, must carry a flight crew of at least the number and description specified in that flight manual;
- (b) which does not currently have a flight manual but has done in the past, must carry a flight crew of at least the number and description specified in that flight manual.
