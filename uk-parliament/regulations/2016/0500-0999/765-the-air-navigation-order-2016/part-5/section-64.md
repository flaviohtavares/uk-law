# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 64

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Meaning of “non-Part-21 aircraft” in this Part[^c-cd78812709161753e8cb87d47bccff0f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, “**non-Part-21[^c-ce2aaf72b2efea53ef18106caf7a458d] aircraft**” means an aircraft which, when conducting the flight or operation, is not subject to any of Annexes III to VIII of the [^c-b05ae57f84912e7b9c309f4ad23407ef]... Air Operations Regulation.
