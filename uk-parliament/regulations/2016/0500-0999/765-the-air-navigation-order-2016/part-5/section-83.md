# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 83

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Height keeping performance – aircraft registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom must not fly in Reduced Vertical Separation Minimum airspace in the United Kingdom unless—

- (a) it complies with paragraph (2); or
- (b) it is otherwise authorised by the appropriate air traffic control unit.

(2) An aircraft complies with this paragraph if it is registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom and—

- (a) it is so equipped with height keeping systems as to comply with the law of the country in which the aircraft is registered in so far as that law requires it to be so equipped when flying in any designated airspace; and
- (b) the equipment is capable of being operated so as to enable the aircraft to maintain the height keeping performance notified for the airspace in which the aircraft is flying, and it is so operated.
