# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 65

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Application of Chapters 2 and 7

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapters 2 (other than articles 77(1) and 79)[^c-5f69e923966352322a4709bd13967b82] and 7 apply only to non-Part-21[^c-65eca98770d12bda27dbaee4469afeb2] aircraft.
