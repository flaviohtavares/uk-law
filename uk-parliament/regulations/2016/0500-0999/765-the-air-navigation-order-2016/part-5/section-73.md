# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 73

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Passenger briefings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The pilot in command must ensure that before or, where appropriate, during the flight, passengers are given a briefing on emergency equipment and procedures.

(2) This article does not apply to the pilot in command of an aircraft registered in the United Kingdom in relation to a flight under and in accordance with the terms of a police air operator's certificate.
