# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 118

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Wearing of survival suits by crew

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) does not apply to any member of the crew of an aircraft flying under and in accordance with the terms of a police air operator's certificate.

(2) Each member of the crew of an aircraft registered in the United Kingdom must wear a survival suit if such a suit is required to be carried by article 119 and Part 1 of Schedule 6.
