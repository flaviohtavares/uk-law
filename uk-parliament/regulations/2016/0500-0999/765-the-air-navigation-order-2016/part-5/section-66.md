# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 66

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Flight crew required by aircraft registered elsewhere than in the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to an aircraft registered in a country other than the United Kingdom.

(2) An aircraft must not fly unless it carries a flight crew of the number and description required by the law of the country in which it is registered.
