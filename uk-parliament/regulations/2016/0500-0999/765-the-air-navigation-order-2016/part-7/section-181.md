# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 181

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Duty of person in charge to be satisfied as to competence of controllers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of an approval granted under article 180 must not permit any person to act as an air traffic controller or a student air traffic controller in the provision of the service under the approval unless—

- (a) that person holds an appropriate licence; and
- (b) the approval holder is satisfied that the person is competent to perform the duties of an air traffic controller or a student air traffic controller.
