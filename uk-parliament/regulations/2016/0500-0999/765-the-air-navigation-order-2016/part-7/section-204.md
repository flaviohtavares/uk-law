# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 204

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. Approval of courses, persons, examinations and simulators

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Without prejudice to any other provision of this Order the CAA may, for the purposes of this Chapter, approve—

- (a) any course of training or instruction;
- (b) a person to conduct such examinations, assessments or tests as it may specify; and
- (c) any examinations, assessments or tests.
