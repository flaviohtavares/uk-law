## 198. Meaning of terms used in this Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Terms used in this Chapter[^c-66807ba0ffecd4ce0b485fd1325e3adb] have the same meaning as equivalent terms used in the [^c-bc33a3da69ef36b83ab813b5cb3a9009]... Air Traffic Controller Licensing Regulation.
