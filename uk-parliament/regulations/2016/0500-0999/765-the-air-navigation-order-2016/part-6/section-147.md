# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 147

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Flight crew licence requirement – exception for non-Part-21 aircraft where CAA permission granted[^key-fac8d9facd217f285bb76d144884aa0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Order prohibits the holder of a pilot's licence from acting as pilot of a non-Part-21[^key-36103ace3550bbde53b72c0200aa50dc] aircraft certificated for single pilot operation if the holder is testing any person for the purposes of articles 152(1) and (8), 154(2), 155(2), 156 or 157 with the permission of the CAA.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies even though—

- (a) the type of aircraft in which the test is conducted is not specified in an aircraft rating included in the licence; or
- (b) the licence or personal flying log book does not include a valid certificate of test, experience or revalidation for the type of aircraft.
