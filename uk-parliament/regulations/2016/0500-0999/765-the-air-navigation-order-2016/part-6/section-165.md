> Text as at 2026-05-01.

## 165. Occupational health medical practitioners

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph MED.D.040(b) of Part-MED an “occupational health medical practitioner” is any doctor listed in the Specialist Register of the General Medical Council as having specialist registration in occupational medicine.
