# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 146

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Flight crew licence requirement – exception for non-Part-21 gliders[^c-8a56358f05bc026c8588d52122226796]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person may act as the pilot in command or co-pilot of a non-Part-21[^c-b27358826b53d390c3ed7f52202cae5a] glider without being the holder of an appropriate licence if the flight is not for the purpose of public transport.
