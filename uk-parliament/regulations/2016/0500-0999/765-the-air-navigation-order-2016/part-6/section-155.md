# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 155

> Text as at 2026-05-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Maintenance of privileges of aircraft ratings for United Kingdom aeroplane and helicopter licences specified in Chapter 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 8[^c-0cee227ab352610ff3f84ef189786671]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to United Kingdom aeroplane and helicopter licences specified in Chapter 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 8[^c-084d883438173b484f01df6ef052f784].

(2) The holder of a licence to which this article applies is not entitled to exercise the privileges of an aircraft rating specified in Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 8 which is included in the licence on a flight unless—

- (a) the licence has a certificate of revalidation for the rating;
- (b) the certificate is appropriate, issued and valid in accordance with Chapter 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 8; and[^c-602ddfadae3c9676a2e09a0128e177eb]
- (c)[^c-4356d38fb1f7e9bfdde569e1a6ca4813] the holder has undertaken the differences training specified in paragraph FCL 710 of Part-FCL and detailed information about that differences training has been entered in the holder’s personal flying log by the instructor who provided the training.
