# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 145

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Flight crew licence requirement – exception for members of HM Forces

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person may act as a member of the flight crew of an aircraft registered in the United Kingdom without being the holder of an appropriate licence if, in so doing, the person is acting in the course of his or her duty as a member of any of Her Majesty's naval, military or air forces.
