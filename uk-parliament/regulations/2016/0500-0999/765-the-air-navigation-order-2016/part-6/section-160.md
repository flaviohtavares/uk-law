# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 160

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. Requirement for a medical certificate for a United Kingdom licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to any licence granted under article 152.

(2) Subject to article 163, the holder of a licence to which this article applies is not entitled to perform any of the functions to which the licence relates unless—

- (a) the licence includes a valid medical certificate issued under article 161; or
- (b) the holder of the licence has a valid medical certificate issued under Part-MED that is not a Light Aircraft Pilot Licence medical certificate.
