# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 138

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Appropriate licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An appropriate licence for the purposes of this Part and Schedule 8 means a licence which entitles the holder to perform the functions being undertaken in relation to the aircraft concerned on the particular flight.
