# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 159

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 159. Maintenance of privileges of Flight Navigators' Licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of a United Kingdom Flight Navigator's Licence is not entitled to exercise the privileges of the licence on a flight to which article 111 applies unless—

- (a) the licence has a certificate of revalidation; and
- (b) the certificate is appropriate, issued and valid in accordance with Chapter 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 8.
