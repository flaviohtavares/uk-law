> Text as at 2026-05-01.

## 169. Validation of licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-2ad4a3dfab905db771d112ffaa4a14fe]... The CAA may issue a certificate of validation rendering valid for the purposes of this Order any flight crew licence granted under the law of any country other than that of the United Kingdom.
