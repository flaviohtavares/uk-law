# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 158

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Maintenance of privileges of Flight Engineers' Licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of a United Kingdom Flight Engineer's Licence is not entitled to exercise the privileges of an aircraft rating contained in the licence on a flight unless—

- (a) the licence has a certificate of revalidation for the rating; and
- (b) the certificate is appropriate, issued and valid in accordance with Chapter 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 8.
