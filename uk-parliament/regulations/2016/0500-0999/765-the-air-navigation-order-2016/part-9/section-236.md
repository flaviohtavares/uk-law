# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 236

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 236. Production of air traffic service equipment documents and records

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of an approval under article 205 or 206 must, within a reasonable time after being requested to do so by an authorised person, cause to be produced to that person any documents and records relating to any air traffic service equipment used or intended to be used in connection with the provision of a service to an aircraft.
