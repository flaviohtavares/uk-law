# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 237

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 237. Power to inspect and copy documents and records

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An authorised person has the power to inspect and copy any certificate, licence, log, declaration, document or record which the authorised person has the power under this Order, under any regulations made under this Order, [^c-b22009a6f2842d06b704539a6cfdb78e]... or under a Safety[^c-3b9dd7bd0a23f8f5fc6fc3906b9298d9] Regulation to require to be produced.
