# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 231

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 231. Use of flight recording systems

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On a flight on which a flight data recorder, a cockpit voice recorder or a combined cockpit voice recorder and flight data recorder is required by paragraph 4(4) or (5)[^key-34a802cf418304d5b2c00bb67eea1a78] of Part 1 of Schedule 6 to be carried in an aeroplane, the recorder must always be in use from the beginning of the take-off run to the end of the landing run.

(2) On any flight on which a cockpit voice recorder, a flight data recorder or a combined cockpit voice recorder and flight data recorder is required by paragraph 4(13)[^key-7f731f12f69755460e33fd0aad000b2d] of Part 1 of Schedule 6 to be carried in a helicopter, the recorder must always be in use from the time the rotors first turn for the purpose of taking off until the rotors are next stopped.
