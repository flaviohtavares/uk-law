# The Air Navigation Order 2016, section 30

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Aircraft subject to an international interest

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to an aircraft—

- (a) which is the subject of a registered international interest within the meaning of the 2015 Regulations, and
- (b) in respect of which an irrevocable de-registration and export request authorisation is in force.

(2) The CAA may cancel the registration of an aircraft to which this article applies under article 28(3) only in pursuance of an application made by a person who is recorded, in accordance with regulation 22 of the 2015 Regulations, as the authorised party under an irrevocable de-registration and export request authorisation in respect of the aircraft.

(3) Expressions used in this article have the same meaning as in the 2015 Regulations.
