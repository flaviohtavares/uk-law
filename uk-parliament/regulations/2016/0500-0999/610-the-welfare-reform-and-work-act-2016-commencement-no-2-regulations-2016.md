# The Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 610 (C. 46)

Social Security

The Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016

Made

26th May 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 36(6) of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Commencement

Section 10 (benefit cap: Social Security Advisory Committee) of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 comes into force on 9th June 2016.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Freud

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

26th May 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations appoint 9th June 2016 as the day for the coming into force of section 10 (benefit cap: Social Security Advisory Committee) of the Welfare Reform and Work Act [2016 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/7).
