# The Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 550 (C. 34)

Insurance

The Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2016

Made

28th April 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following order in exercise of the power conferred by section 21(2) of the Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2016.

## 2. Act coming into force on the 1st August 2016

The Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010, so far as not already in force, comes into force on 1st August 2016.

Edward Faulks

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

28th April 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences the Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010 so far as that Act has not already been commenced. Section 19 of the Act is already in force, having been substituted for the original section 19 by section 19 of the Insurance Act [2015 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/4), which came into force two months after the passing of the Insurance Act 2015. This Order accordingly commences the rest of the Third Parties (Rights against Insurers) Act 2010, so that the entire Act is now commenced.
