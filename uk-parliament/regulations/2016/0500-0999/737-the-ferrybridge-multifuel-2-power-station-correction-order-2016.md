# The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station (Correction) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 737

Infrastructure Planning

The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station (Correction) Order 2016

Made

12th July 2016

Coming into force

13th July 2016

The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station Order 2015 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

Before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from Multifuel Energy Limited for the correction of errors in the Order under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Wakefield Metropolitan District Council, as the relevant local planning authority (as defined in paragraph 4 of Schedule 4 to the Act), that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station (Correction) Order 2016.

(2) This Order comes into force on 13th July 2016.

## 2. Corrections

The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station Order 2015 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted or inserted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Coal Liabilities

Department of Energy and Climate Change

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors identified in the Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station Order 2015, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request from Multifuel Energy Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
