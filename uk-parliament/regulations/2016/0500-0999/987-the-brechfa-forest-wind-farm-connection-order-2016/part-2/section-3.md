# The Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection Order 2016, section 3

> Text as at 2017-03-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent granted by the Order[^c-6b3ab26646a0820bcbf5a8f13e4f7d34]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, and to the Requirements in Schedule 3, the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development in Schedule 1 to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) The undertaker may install, and keep installed, the above ground electric lines and the underground cables included in the authorised development.

(3) The undertaker may use the electric line and any other elements of the authorised development as part of the electricity distribution system in Wales.

(4) Subject to article 5 (limits of deviation), the authorised development must be constructed and installed in the lines and situations shown on the works plans and in accordance with the levels shown on the design drawings.
