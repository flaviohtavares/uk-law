# The Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection Order 2016, section 21

> Text as at 2017-03-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-dbaf94d7df8ee46c824960e75ee1e538]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00019] (minerals) are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” there is substituted “the undertaker”.
