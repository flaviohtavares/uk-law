# The Education and Adoption Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 866 (C. 61)

Education, England And Wales

The Education and Adoption Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016

Made

1st September 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 19(2) of the Education and Adoption Act 2016[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Education and Adoption Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 5th September 2016

Section 1 (coasting schools) of the Education and Adoption Act 2016 comes into force, for the purposes of making regulations, on 5th September 2016.

John Nash

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

1st September 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the second commencement Regulations made under the Education and Adoption Act 2016 (“the Act”).

These Regulations appoint 5th September 2016 as the day for the coming into force of section 1 of the Act, for the purposes of making regulations. This will enable the Secretary of State to make regulations which define “coasting” in relation to a school.
