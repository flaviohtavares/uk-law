# The M4 Motorway (Junctions 3 to 12) (Smart Motorway) Development Consent Order 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-717cfb7b5fdb2741bd0bcfe5aec97b66]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00030] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “minerals” substitute “minerals and other substances”.
