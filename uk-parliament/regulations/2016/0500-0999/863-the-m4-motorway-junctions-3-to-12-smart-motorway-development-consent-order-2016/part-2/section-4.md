# The M4 Motorway (Junctions 3 to 12) (Smart Motorway) Development Consent Order 2016, section 4

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised development[^c-0f5fa0327f6f4eda8c852e3ad2f4d7d8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Without limitation on the scope of section 41 (duty to maintain highways maintainable at the public expense) of the 1980 Act and section 1 (appointment of strategic highways companies) of the Infrastructure Act 2015[^f00016] the undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order or an agreement made under this Order or the 1980 Act, provides otherwise.
