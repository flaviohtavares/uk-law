# The M4 Motorway (Junctions 3 to 12) (Smart Motorway) Development Consent Order 2016, section 6

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Power to deviate[^key-6df9559daf33e127660597ffae12b141]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In carrying out the scheduled works the undertaker may—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the works plans within the Order limits in respect of any linear work and in respect of any non-linear work within the Non-linear Work Boundary for that work;
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown or noted on the engineering drawings and sections, to a maximum of 0.5 metres upwards or downwards; and
- (c) deviate vertically from the parapet heights stated on the engineering drawings and sections, to a maximum of 0.5 metres upwards.
