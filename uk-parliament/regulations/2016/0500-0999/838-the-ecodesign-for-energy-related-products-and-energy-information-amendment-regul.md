# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 838

Energy Conservation

The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2016

Made

9th August 2016

Laid before Parliament

10th August 2016

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the environmental aspects of product design and makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 1st September 2016.

(3) Regulation 2(3) comes into force on 1st January 2018.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2617](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2617)) (“the 2010 Regulations”) and the Energy Information Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1524](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1524)) (“the 2011 Regulations”) and partially implement Commission Regulations (EU) Nos [2015/1095](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1095) and [2015/1188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1188), and Commission Delegated Regulations (EU) Nos 2015/1094, 2015/1187 and [2015/1186](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1186) as follows—

- (a) regulation 2 adds two new entries to the table in paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (declaration of conformity) to the 2010 Regulations—
  - (i) from 1st September 2016, for professional refrigerated storage cabinets, blast cabinets, condensing units and process chillers;
  - (ii) from 1st January 2018, for local space heaters; and
- (b) regulation 3 adds three new entries to the table in Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the 2011 Regulations to apply from the dates referred to in regulation 3 for—
  - (i) professional refrigerated storage cabinets;
  - (ii) solid fuel boilers and packages of a solid fuel boiler, supplementary heaters, temperature controls and solar devices;
  - (iii) local space heaters.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
