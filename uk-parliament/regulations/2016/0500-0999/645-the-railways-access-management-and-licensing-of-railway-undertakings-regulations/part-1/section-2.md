# The Railways (Access, Management and Licensing of Railway Undertakings) Regulations 2016, section 2

> Text as at 2024-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Amendments and revocations

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) the Railways Infrastructure (Access and Management) Regulations 2005 [^c23153931];
- (b) the Railways Infrastructure (Access and Management) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 [^c23153941]; and
- (c) the Railways Infrastructure (Access and Management) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 [^c23153951].

(2) Schedule 1 (amendments) has effect.
