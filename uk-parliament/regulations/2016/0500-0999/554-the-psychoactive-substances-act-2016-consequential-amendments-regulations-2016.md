# The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 554

Dangerous Drugs

The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016

Made

3rd May 2016

Laid before Parliament

5th May 2016

Coming into force

26th May 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) and (2)(c) of the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 26th May 2016.

## 2. Amendment to the Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) Order 1999

(1) The Visiting Forces and International Headquarters (Application of Law) Order 1999[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 5 (enactments extending general Crown privilege to visiting forces etc.), after the entry relating to the Sunday Trading Act 1994, insert—

> The Psychoactive Substances Act [2016 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/2).

.

## 3. Amendment to the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Criteria and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2009

(1) The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Criteria and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2009[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1 of the table in paragraph 2 of the Schedule (prescribed criteria – automatic inclusion in the children’s barred list with the right to make representations) insert the following entry in the appropriate place—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Psychoactive Substances Act 2016, section 5 | The person to whom the psychoactive substance was supplied or offered to be supplied was a child |

.

(3) In Part 1 of the table in paragraph 4 of the Schedule (prescribed criteria – automatic inclusion in the adults’ barred list with the right to make representations) insert the following entry in the appropriate place—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Psychoactive Substances Act 2016, section 5 | The person to whom the psychoactive substance was supplied or offered to be supplied was a child |

.

Karen Bradley

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

3rd May 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to secondary legislation in consequence of the Psychoactive Substances Act [2016 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/2) (the Act).
