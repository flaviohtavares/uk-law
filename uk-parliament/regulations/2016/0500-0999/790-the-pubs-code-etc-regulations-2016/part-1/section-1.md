# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 1

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-cb2e1583951f63b5655039e55f471488]

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016;
- (b) come into force on the day after the day on which they are made; and
- (c) extend to England and Wales.
