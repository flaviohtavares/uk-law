# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 54

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Short agreements[^key-c79b96df57bb7a9ec99edcda7812604b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a pub-owning business and a tied pub tenant—

- (a) enter into a short agreement (whether before, on or after the commencement date); and
- (b) that agreement, together with all other short agreements (if any) previously entered into between the pub-owning business and the tied pub tenant entitle the tenant to occupy the tied pub for a total period of less than 12 months,

regulations 9 to 13 and Parts 3 to 10 of these Regulations do not apply to the dealings of the pub-owning business and the tenant in connection with that agreement.
