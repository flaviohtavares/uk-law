# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Documents to which the independent assessor must have regard

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 37

**1.**[^key-adc31643cbb19cab3fd927e6e387acd6] Documents held by the tied pub tenant or the pub-owning business which provide evidence of the tied pub’s level of trading in the last 3 years.

**2.**[^key-88afafb95dfebe7d75bd038bbafa6e2d] Documents held by the tied pub tenant or the pub-owning business which present a reasonable forecast of the tied pub’s level of trading for the next 3 years.

**3.**[^key-b529e77f6a6fe2fd56d6a1dab37f38ac] For the purposes of paragraph 2, a “reasonable forecast” is a forecast which—

- (a) is based on an assessment of the level of trading at the tied pub if it were operated by a reasonably efficient tenant; and
- (b) may be based on an assessment of the pub’s level of trading if it were not subject to a product or service tie.

**4.**[^key-d71b9334c9f45d0db544070d93dba65f] Documents held by the tied pub tenant or the pub-owning business which describe any special commercial or financial advantages provided to the tied pub tenant under the terms of the tenancy or licence.
