# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 53

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Extended protection to last until a rent assessment is concluded[^key-9b0ddb0a1a885ffcacc04df28aae2f85]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 69(5)(b) of SBEEA 2015, a rent assessment or assessment of money payable in lieu of rent is concluded when the tenant and the landlord who is no longer a pub-owning business by virtue of section 69(1) of the SBEEA 2015[^key-a844612918b943b32263958c04ad051f] agree the new rent in writing under regulation 22(2)[^key-f6b825132e75b754be0e806c0c1cd549].
