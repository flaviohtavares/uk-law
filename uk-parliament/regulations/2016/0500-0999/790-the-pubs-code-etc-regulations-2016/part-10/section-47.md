# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 47

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Gaming machines[^c-2de6515a12de235f7251a752d0652f1a]

*Extent: E+W*

A pub-owning business—

- (a) must not enter into a new tenancy or licence; and
- (b) must not renew a tenancy or a licence,

which requires a tied pub tenant to purchase or rent gaming machines.
