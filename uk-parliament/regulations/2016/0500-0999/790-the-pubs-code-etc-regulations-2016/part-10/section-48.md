> Text as at 2022-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Blank template for profit and loss account[^key-37e93b05d3ff75745ed9539196282c7f]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the tied pub tenant so requests, the pub-owning business must provide to the tenant a blank template for completing the tied pub’s profit and loss account.
