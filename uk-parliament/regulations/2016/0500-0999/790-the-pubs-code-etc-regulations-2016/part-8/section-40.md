# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Disputes about rent etc payable during MRO procedure[^key-8e37e0f3f288b2279d1fffc2ef1d2eeb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the tied pub tenant and the pub-owning business disagree as to whether an amount has become recoverable by the pub-owning business at the end of the MRO procedure as a result of regulation 28(3), either of them may refer the matter to the Adjudicator.
