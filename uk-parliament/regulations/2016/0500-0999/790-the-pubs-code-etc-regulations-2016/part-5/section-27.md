> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. A rent assessment or an assessment of money payable in lieu of rent[^key-28606ff0d41b04a88af570f8b4d844a4]

*Extent: E+W*

The event specified in this regulation is that the tied pub tenant receives a rent assessment proposal sent by the pub-owning business under regulation 20(1) in respect of the tenancy or licence.
