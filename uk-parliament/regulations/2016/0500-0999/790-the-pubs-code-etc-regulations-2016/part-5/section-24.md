# The Pubs Code etc. Regulations 2016, section 24

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. A significant increase in the price of a product or service[^c-eb1e8556d89d9027835d228835ae6c5d]

*Extent: E+W*

The event specified in this regulation is that the tied pub tenant receives notification of a significant increase in the price at which a product or service which is subject to a product or service tie is supplied to the tied pub tenant.
