## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-6cfe9bcd739d84385a9fb521b71159f8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate or is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 23 (compulsory acquisition of rights) and paragraph (8) of article 30 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
