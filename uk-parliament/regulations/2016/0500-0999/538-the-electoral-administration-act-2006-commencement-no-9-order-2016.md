# The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 538 (C. 33)

Representation Of The People

The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2016

Made

26th April 2016

The Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 77(2) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

Section 59(4) and (5) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006 (reporting donations to holders of certain elective offices) comes into force on 27th April 2016, so far as it is not already in force.

John Penrose

Minister for Constitutional Reform

Cabinet Office

26th April 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 59(4) and (5) of the Electoral Administration Act [2006 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/22) on 27th April 2016, so far as it is not already in force. Those subsections ensure that before the Secretary of State makes an order commencing section 59 of the 2006 Act as it applies to the holders of a relevant elective office (as defined by paragraph 1(8) of Schedule 7 to the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act [2000 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41)), he or she must have been informed by the Electoral Commission that the Commission is satisfied that it will receive sufficient information about donations to those office-holders. Information will be sufficient, broadly speaking, if it corresponds to that required to be reported under Schedule 7 to the 2000 Act.
