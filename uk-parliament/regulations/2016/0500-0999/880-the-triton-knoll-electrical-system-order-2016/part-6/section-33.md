# The Triton Knoll Electrical System Order 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Operational land for purposes of Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^c-54a334f819ffc89bd097c0ab93086e29]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order must be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)<sup> </sup>of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act).
