# The Triton Knoll Electrical System Order 2016, section 11

> Text as at 2017-11-30.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Agreements with a highway authority[^c-e3465a1a50ac53dd81f911c484ac4e97]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A highway authority and the undertaker may enter into agreements with respect to—

- (a) any temporary stopping up, alteration or diversion of a street authorised by this Order; or
- (b) the carrying out in the street of any of the works referred to in article 8(1) (street works).

(2) An agreement referred to in paragraph (1) may, without limiting that paragraph,—

- (a) provide for the highway authority to carry out any function under this Order which relates to the street in question;
- (b) include an agreement between the undertaker and highway authority specifying a reasonable time for the completion of the works; and
- (c) contain such terms as to payment and otherwise as the parties consider appropriate.
