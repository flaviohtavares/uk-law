# The Triton Knoll Electrical System Order 2016, section 28

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. No double recovery[^c-6366658123d03531e012e7723e228995]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
