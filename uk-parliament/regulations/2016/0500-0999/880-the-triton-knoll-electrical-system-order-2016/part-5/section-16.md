# The Triton Knoll Electrical System Order 2016, section 16

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^c-22892b2de0e97967927fc1a1afaf912b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 (minerals) are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”.
