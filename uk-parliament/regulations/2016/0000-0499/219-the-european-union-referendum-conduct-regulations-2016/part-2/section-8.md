# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 8

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Notice of referendum[^c-1485074c722e66f53df7bedc1943570a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each counting officer must give public notice of the referendum stating—

- (a) the date of the poll;
- (b) the date by which—
  - (i) applications to vote by post or by proxy, and
  - (ii) other applications and notices about postal or proxy voting,

  must reach the registration officer in order that they may be effective for the referendum.
