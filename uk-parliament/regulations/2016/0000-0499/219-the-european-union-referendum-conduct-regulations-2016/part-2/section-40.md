# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Refusal to deliver ballot paper[^c-44b54877ac1b094a68cd19e08d4d2558]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision by a presiding officer taken under paragraph (2) of regulation 32, including that paragraph as applied by regulation 35, 36 or 37, is final (except that it is subject to review in proceedings brought by an application for judicial review).
