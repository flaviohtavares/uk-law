# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 54

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Declaration of referendum result by Chief Counting Officer[^c-bd3d1e888d1b3cb780bfdfd4023a2f5e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After making the certification under section 128(6) of the 2000 Act, the Chief Counting Officer must—

- (a) immediately make a declaration of the matters certified under that provision;
- (b) as soon as practicable give public notice of the matters certified together with the number of rejected ballot papers under each head shown in the statements of rejected ballot papers.
