# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 12

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. The official mark[^c-dab94d7d9eb2b4995518ab96e8d615e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every ballot paper must contain an appropriate security marking (referred to in these Regulations as the “official mark”).

(2) The official mark must be kept secret.

(3) The counting officer may use a different official mark for different purposes.
