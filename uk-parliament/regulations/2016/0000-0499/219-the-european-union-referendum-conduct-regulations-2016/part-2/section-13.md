# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 13

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Prohibition of disclosure of vote[^c-964f115814e194d145a1192bf9267a0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who has voted in the referendum may not be required, in any legal proceeding to question the referendum, to state for which answer he or she voted.
