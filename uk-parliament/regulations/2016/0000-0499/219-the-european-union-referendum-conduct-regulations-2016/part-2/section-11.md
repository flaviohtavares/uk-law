# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 11

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Corresponding number list[^c-91fc2cb95b22924171db6c0c1b3b0c39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The counting officer must prepare a list containing the numbers and other unique identifying marks of all of the ballot papers to be—

- (a) issued by the officer in pursuance of regulation 17(1), or
- (b) provided by the officer in pursuance of regulation 22(1).

(2) The list must be in the form set out in Form 2 in Schedule 4.
