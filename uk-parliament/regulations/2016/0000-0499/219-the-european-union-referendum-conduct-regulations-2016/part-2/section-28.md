# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 28

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Sealing of ballot boxes[^c-d287acb64192423a664e52cb9fdecf4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Immediately before the commencement of the poll, the presiding officer must show anyone present in the polling station that the ballot box is empty.

(2) The presiding officer must then—

- (a) lock the box (if it has a lock) and place his or her seal on it in a manner that prevents it from being opened without breaking the seal, and
- (b) place the box in his or her view for the receipt of ballot papers, and keep it so locked (if it has a lock) and sealed.
