# The European Union Referendum (Conduct) Regulations 2016, section 59

> Text as at 2016-06-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Overview and interpretation[^c-156a395d6b0054cdc079ee90f93663a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter of this Part contains provision about voting in the referendum in England and Wales and Scotland.

(2) In this Chapter of this Part[^f00036]—

- “anonymous entry”, in relation to a register of electors, is to be read in accordance with section 9B of the 1983 Act[^f00037];
- “peer” means a peer who is a member of the House of Lords;
- “relevant register” means—
  - (a) a register of parliamentary electors, or
  - (b) in relation to a peer—
    - (i) a register of local government electors, or
    - (ii) a register of peers maintained under section 3 of the Representation of the People Act 1985[^f00038] (peers resident outside the United Kingdom);
- “the relevant regulations”—
  - (a) in relation to England and Wales, means the Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00039] as applied by Part 1 of Schedule 3;
  - (b) in relation to Scotland, means the Representation of the People (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00040] as so applied;
- “the RPA 2000” means the Representation of the People Act 2000[^f00041].
