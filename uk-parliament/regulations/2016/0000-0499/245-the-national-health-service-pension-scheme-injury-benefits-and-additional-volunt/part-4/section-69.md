# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Amendment of regulation 115

For sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (8) of regulation 115 (amount of pension: survivor of active member), substitute—

> - (b) sub-paragraph (a) does not apply if—
>   - (i) the Secretary of State’s liability to provide a guaranteed minimum pension in respect of the surviving adult dependent is discharged by the payment of a contributions equivalent premium under section 55(2) of the 1993 Act, or
>   - (ii) a surviving widow, widower or surviving civil partner has been convicted of an offence specified in paragraph 12 of Schedule 3[^f00030] and the Secretary of State directed, as a consequence of that conviction, that the person’s rights to a payment in respect of the member’s death is forfeit.

.

## Annotations

[^f00030]: Footnote: Paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 is concerned with the forfeiture of rights to benefits.
