# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Amendment of regulation 118

(1) Regulation 118 (recent leavers) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2), for “recent leaver’s” substitute “surviving spouse’s or surviving civil partner’s”.

(3) For paragraph (3), substitute—

> (3) Paragraph (2) does not apply if—
>
> - (a) the Secretary of State’s liability to provide a guaranteed minimum pension in respect of the surviving spouse or civil partner is discharged by the payment of a contributions equivalent premium under section 55(2) of the 1993 Act, or
> - (b) a surviving widow, widower or surviving civil partner has been convicted of an offence specified in paragraph 12 of Schedule 3[^f00031] and the Secretary of State directed, as a consequence of that conviction, that the person’s rights to a payment in respect of the member’s death is forfeit.

.

## Annotations

[^f00031]: Footnote: Paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 is concerned with the forfeiture of rights to benefits.
