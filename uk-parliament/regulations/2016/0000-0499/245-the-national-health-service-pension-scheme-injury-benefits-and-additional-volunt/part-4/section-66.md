# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Amendment of regulation 92

In the definition of “E” in paragraph (3) of regulation 92 (annual rate of ill-health pension), for “M’s” substitute “the day before M reaches”.
