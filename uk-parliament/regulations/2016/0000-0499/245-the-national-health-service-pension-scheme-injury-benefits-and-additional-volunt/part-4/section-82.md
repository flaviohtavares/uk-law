# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Amendment of regulation 154

Paragraph (3) of regulation 154 (IP guarantees) is amended as follows—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “an independent provider” substitute “that independent provider”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), after “this Part,” insert “that independent provider”.
