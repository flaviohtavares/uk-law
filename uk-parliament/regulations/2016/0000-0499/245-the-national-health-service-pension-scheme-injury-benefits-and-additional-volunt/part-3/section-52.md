# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Amendment of regulation 3.F.5

(1) Regulation 3.F.5 (ways in which transfer value payments may be applied) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)[^f00026], for “If Chapter 4 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act applies to a member, that member” substitute “A deferred member”.

(3) After paragraph (5), add—

> (6) Paragraph (1) applies whether or not the deferred member is entitled to a guaranteed cash equivalent transfer value payment under Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of the 1993 Act.

.
