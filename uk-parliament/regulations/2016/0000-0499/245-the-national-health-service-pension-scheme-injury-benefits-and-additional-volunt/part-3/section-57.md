## 57. Amendment of regulation 3.J.14

(1) Regulation 3.J.14 (employing authority and certain member record keeping and contribution estimates) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (c) of paragraph (4), after “parental leave payments” insert “, shared parental leave payments”.

(3) In sub-paragraph (e) of paragraph (5), after “parental leave payments” insert “, shared parental leave payments”.
