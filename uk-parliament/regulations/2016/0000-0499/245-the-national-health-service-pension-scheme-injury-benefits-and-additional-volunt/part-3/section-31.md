# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Amendment of regulation 2.F.1

(1) Regulation 2.F.1 (introduction: rights to transfer value payment) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), for “Chapter 4 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act (transfer values)” substitute “Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of the 1993 Act (transfer rights: general)”.

(3) In paragraph (2), for “Chapter 5” substitute “Chapter 2”.

(4) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)[^f00018], for “Chapter 4” substitute “Chapter 1”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “Chapter 5” substitute “Chapter 2”.

## Annotations

[^f00018]: Footnote: Sub-paragraph (a) was last amended by regulations 17 and 31 of [S.I. 2015/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/96).
