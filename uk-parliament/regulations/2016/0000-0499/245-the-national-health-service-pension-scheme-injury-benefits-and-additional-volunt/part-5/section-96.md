# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Amendment of regulation 40

(1) The table in paragraph (1) of regulation 40 (death in service) is amended as follows.

(2) In column 2, for “2015 Scheme” (eight times) substitute “new scheme”.

(3) In Part 2[^f00036] of that table, adjacent to the entry in column 1 referring to an original entitlement to a pension under regulation 2.E.12(3)(a), in column 2 for “37.5% of the pension” substitute “the appropriate proportion”.

## Annotations

[^f00036]: Footnote: Part 2 of the table is concerned with entitlement derived from the 2008 Section as set out in [S.I. 2008/653](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/653).
