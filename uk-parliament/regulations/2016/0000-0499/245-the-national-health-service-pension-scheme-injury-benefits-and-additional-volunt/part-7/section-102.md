# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Amendment of regulation 11

In regulation 11 (retirement and dependants benefits)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after sub-paragraph (d) add—

  > - (e) a lifetime allowance excess lump sum referred to in section 166(1)(g) of the 2004 Act[^f00039].

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (11), omit “, subject to any limit imposed by regulation 13,”.

## Annotations

[^f00039]: Footnote: Section 166(1) of the Finance Act [2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12) provides for the “lump sum rule”. Under that rule, no lump sum may be paid by a registered pension scheme to a member unless it is a lump sum specified in paragraphs (a) to (h) of section 166(1).
