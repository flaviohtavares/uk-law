# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of regulation P1

In paragraphs (1) and (4)(b) of regulation P1 (absence because of illness or injury or certain types of leave)[^f00009], for “or parental leave” substitute “, parental leave or shared parental leave”.
