# The Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) (Amendment) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 432

Education, England

The Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) (Amendment) Order 2016

Made

23rd March 2016

Laid before Parliament

30th March 2016

Coming into force

1st September 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 85(3A)(b) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) (Amendment) Order 2016 and comes into force on 1st September 2016.

## 2. Amendment to the Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) Order 2007

In article 2(a) of the Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) Order 2007[^f00002] (specification of sets of qualifications), for “Science GCSE and Additional Science GCSE” substitute “Combined Science GCSE”.

Nick Gibb

Minister of State

Department for Education

23rd March 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 2 of the Education (National Curriculum) (Science at Key Stage 4) (England) Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”) to remove the reference to Science GCSE and Additional Science GCSE and substitute a reference to Combined Science GCSE.

A full impact assessment has not been provided for this instrument as no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations is foreseen.
