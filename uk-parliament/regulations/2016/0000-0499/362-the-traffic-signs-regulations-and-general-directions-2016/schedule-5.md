## SCHEDULE 5 — Signs to indicate parking places and areas subject to parking controls

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 3, Direction 3

### PART 1 — Parking place signs and plates

**1.**[^c-54e1be11fbf5679fd3fae662a4d1b4bf] A sign for indicating a parking place (a “parking place sign”) must be square and of the size, colour and type shown in the upper part of the diagram above the sign table in this Part.

**2.**[^c-89dab337d72411b793b236140be24574] A restriction of a description in column 2 of items 2 to 6 of the sign table in this Part must be conveyed by a plate of the size, colour and type provided for in the diagram in column 3 at the item in question.

### PART 2 — Entrance to a zone or area (other than a pedestrian, or a pedestrian and cycle, zone) where parking controls apply

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/362/images/uksi_20160362_en_152)

**3.**[^c-db97380869a6a4a99f504b8cb1fe0a25] The provisions as to size, colour and type in this Part apply to a sign for conveying information, a restriction or a prohibition of a description in column 2 of an item in the sign table in Part 3.

**4.**[^c-93c6f491117ea2c03815a674fd2c349c] The sign must be of the shape shown in the diagram at the beginning of this Part with a white background and black lines as shown (except that the bottom panel may or must be omitted – see paragraph 5).

**5.**[^c-0f7c6d1c3da6901f9021725ed6ea9cdf] The upper panel must contain—

- (a) the symbol or symbols shown in column 3 of the item; and
- (b) a legend shown in column 4 of the item, with the height and placing of the legend complying with column 5.

**6.**[^c-0af5299d2687a1dfbd51df03ad0eea82] The upper panel may be varied in accordance with column 6 of the item.

**7.**[^c-50890f1bff62b1332ddb149e4c022dd1] Where provided for in column 7 of the item, a legend specified at column 2, or column 2 when read with column 4, in the sign table in Part 4 may be included in the lower panel and, where no legend is included, the lower panel must be omitted.

**8.**[^c-f3c07b54f712067fdcef146a8a01815b] A legend or symbol included in the lower panel must comply with the requirements as to size in column 3 of the item in the Part 4 sign table in which the legend or symbol is specified.

### PART 5 — The Schedule 5 General Direction

**1.**[^c-ccb65340b61f8529e4bcc82ece6584da] (1) The signs provided for in this Schedule must only be placed on or near a road to indicate the effect of an Act, order, regulation, bylaw, resolution or notice which prohibits or restricts the use of the road by traffic.

(2) This does not apply to a parking place sign provided for at paragraph 1 of Part 1 of this Schedule when placed without an associated plate.
