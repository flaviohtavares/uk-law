## SCHEDULE 10 — Signs for speed limits

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 3, Direction 3

### PART 1 — Provisions applying to signs in Part 2

**1.**[^c-1a318d9aba7ad74238e79e35cc968236] Information or a restriction of a description in column 2 of an item in the Part 2 sign table must be conveyed by a sign which—

- (a) is of the colour and type; and
- (b) complies with the requirements as to size,

  shown in the diagram in column 3 of the item, or that diagram as varied in accordance with column 4.

**2.**[^c-acca38274ce9f95e0d1dcea341fed4b2] A provision of Part 3 applies to a sign provided for in the Part 2 sign table where indicated in column 5 of that table (by reference to a paragraph number in Part 3).

**3.**[^c-4d1589caa78840bcc18d647870a47208] A Schedule 10 General Direction applies to a sign where indicated in column 6 of the Part 2 sign table (by reference to a number equivalent to the Schedule 10 General Direction in Part 4).

### PART 3 — Provisions applying to signs in Part 2

**1.**[^c-2f531aac90153041ea604981c96f8f77] Where the sign is a variable message sign which displays the numeral by means of light-emitting characters or symbols in such a way that the sign is only visible when associated equipment detects the presence of a vehicle exceeding the speed limit indicated by the sign, the variable message sign may display, beneath the sign displayed on it, the legend “SLOW DOWN” in characters having a height not less than one quarter of the height of the displayed sign.

**2.**[^c-528152427e18f213ef9ff92f2823a951] Where the sign is a terminal sign and is erected on a trunk or principal road within 50 metres of any lamp lit by electricity that has been placed there in order to provide illumination to users of the road, the illumination requirements for the sign are—

- (a) where the lamp is illuminated throughout the hours of darkness, the sign must be illuminated by internal or external lighting for so long as the lamp is illuminated and may also be reflectorised; or
- (b) where the lamp is not illuminated throughout the hours of darkness–—
  - (i) the sign must be illuminated by internal or external lighting for so long as the lamp is illuminated and must also be reflectorised; or
  - (ii) the sign must be illuminated throughout the hours of darkness by internal or external lighting and may also be reflectorised.

**3.**[^c-438aaf3f7a3b152fdf1a8e70920da908] Two or more signs placed at, or near, the same point for the same purpose must, if they are terminal signs, be illuminated by the same method.

**4.**[^c-c5c8198d0eec7da54cebfcbd133c3f9d] The road marking must be reflectorised.

### PART 4 — The Schedule 10 General Directions

**1.**[^c-eec1c3102aa1730856e8d41537fe1906] (1) The sign provided for at item 5 of the sign table in Part 2 (“the diagram 674 sign”) may only be placed where no part of the road to which the speed limit applies is more than 50 metres from a traffic calming feature.

(2) The restriction in sub-paragraph (1) does not apply in respect of a road which is a cul-de-sac that is less than 80 metres long.

(3) Each of the following is a traffic calming feature—

- (a) a road hump constructed pursuant to section 90A of the Highways Act 1980[^f00082] (“the 1980 Act”) or section 36 of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00083] and in accordance with the Highways (Road Humps) Regulations 1999[^f00084] or the Road Humps (Scotland) Regulations 1998[^f00085];
- (b) traffic calming works constructed in accordance with section 90G of the 1980 Act[^f00086] or section 39A of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00087] and in accordance with the Highways (Traffic Calming) Regulations 1999[^f00088] or the Roads (Traffic Calming) (Scotland) Regulations 1994[^f00089];
- (c) a refuge for pedestrians which was constructed pursuant to section 68 of the 1980 Act, or section 27(c) of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984, after 15th June 1999 and is constructed so as to encourage a reduction in the speed of traffic using the carriageway;
- (d) a variation of the relative widths of the carriageway or of any footway pursuant to section 75 of the 1980 Act, or section 1(1) or 2(1) of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984, which—
  - (i) was carried out after 15th June 1999 for the purpose of encouraging a reduction in the speed of traffic using the carriageway; and
  - (ii) had the effect of reducing the width of the carriageway;
- (e) a horizontal bend in the carriageway through which all vehicular traffic has to change direction by no less than 70 degrees within a distance of 32 metres as measured at the inner kerb radius;
- (f) a sign provided for at item 1 of the Part 2 sign table varied to “20”; or
- (g) a road marking provided for at item 9 of that table varied to “20”.

(4) At least one traffic calming feature as defined in [^c-58e390f01a65161a127972ee21f1b16d]sub-paragraph (3)(a) to (e)[^c-545464712586009af602d30805856cf4] must be placed within the zone indicated by the diagram 674 sign.

(5) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), the distance of 50 metres is to be measured along roads to which the speed limit indicated by the diagram 674 sign applies.

**2.**[^c-6ccad5f20c658d4bc10dde0a7ecaf878] This sign must not be placed as a repeater sign where—

- (a) in relation to England, the road is subject to a maximum speed limit of 30 mph and has a system of carriageway lighting; or
- (b) in relation to Wales, the road is subject to a maximum speed limit of 20 mph and has a system of carriageway lighting.

**3.**[^c-b3daa04238c0f5fa702f1f271b48eae8] The sign must not be placed as a repeater sign—

- (a) on a motorway; or
- (b) where a road, other than a motorway, does not have a system of carriageway lighting.

**4.**[^c-1cc9e7974bac9efdfd1c07571c7bee53] The sign—

- (a) must only be placed to indicate the effect of an Act, order, regulation, bylaw, resolution or notice which restricts the use of the road by traffic; and
- (b) when placed as a terminal sign, must be placed as near as practicable to the point that the speed limit begins or ends.

**5.**[^c-6703fee4d24cb8906812049a20da6d0c] If the road marking is placed to indicate where a maximum speed limit commences, it must be placed in conjunction with an upright sign provided for at item 1, 5, 6 or 8 of the Part 2 sign table.

**6.**[^c-cd32c88b02aaccf41f1f4eab94a86297] (1) Sub-paragraph (2) applies where the backing board for the sign is yellow.

(2) When placed on the same backing board as the sign provided for at item 81 of the sign table in Part 2 of Schedule 11, the backing board may be of any shape.
