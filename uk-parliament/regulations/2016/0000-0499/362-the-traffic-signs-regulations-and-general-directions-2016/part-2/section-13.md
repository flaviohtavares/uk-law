# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/362
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13. Saving[^c-030156e59b50d9dc29589a8bc7126b5c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This general direction applies to a sign that is in place immediately before the coming into force of these General Directions which does not, but for this general direction, comply with these General Directions.

(2) For so long as the way in which it is placed would have conformed with the Pelican and Puffin Pedestrian Crossings General Directions 1997[^f00022] or the Traffic Signs General Directions 2002[^f00023], it is to be treated as having been placed in conformity with these General Directions.
