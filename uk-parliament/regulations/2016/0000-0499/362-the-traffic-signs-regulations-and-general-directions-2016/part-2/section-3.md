## 3. Application of general directions[^c-e8dc041a315c509bc18d318766ee4c49]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The general directions in the final Part of each of Schedules 2 to 15 apply to the signs provided for in that Schedule to the extent indicated in the Schedule.
