## 11. Special directions[^key-589747c47541d837c298019993583dc3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these General Directions limits the power of the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the Welsh Ministers, by special direction under section 65(2) of the 1984 Act[^f00021], to dispense with, add to or modify any of the requirements of these General Directions in their application to any particular case.
