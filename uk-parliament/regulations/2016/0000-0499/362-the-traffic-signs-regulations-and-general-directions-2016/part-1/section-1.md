# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/362
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-5a58d93b67b9aca9adea17becb5964e1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Part of this Instrument—

- (a) may be cited—
  - (i) as the Traffic Signs Regulations 2016, and
  - (ii) together with Part 2, as the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016; and
- (b) comes into force on 22nd April 2016.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations extend to the whole of Great Britain.

(3) Provision about the school crossing patrol sign extends only to England and Wales[^f00011].
