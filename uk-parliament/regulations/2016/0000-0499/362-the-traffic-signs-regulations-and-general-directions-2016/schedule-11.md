## SCHEDULE 11 — Signs that give information, are advisory or guide traffic

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 3, Direction 3

### PART 1 — Provisions applying to signs in Part 2

**1.**[^key-373e7caa8721a3380ed3e63fa9db0ca3] Except for item 75, a warning or information of a description in column 2 of an item in the Part 2 sign table must be conveyed by a sign which is—

- (a) of the colour and type; and
- (b) complies with the requirements as to size,

shown in the diagram in column 3 of the item, or that diagram as varied in accordance with column 4 (by reference to paragraphs in Part 5).

**2.**[^key-6fe22aecbfe0cec5cd5a353a15b8538f] In respect of item 75 (traffic mirror), the mirror must be as specified in column 3.

**3.**[^key-5c971243ff0488416d3a9d2c49f0b502] A paragraph of Part 6, specified by reference to a number in column 5 of an item in the Part 2 sign table, applies to the sign provided for at that item.

**4.**[^key-4fcde4ca4bdd81d5e36fc165da70f3a7] A number in column 6 of an item in the Part 2 sign table indicates that the Schedule 11 General Direction bearing that number applies to the sign provided for at that item.
