# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2016, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-925cfe9dc972bd9c66efa7086bb5e9bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “LLP” means a limited liability partnership incorporated under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000;
- “principal Regulations” means the Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009[^f00002]; and
- “PSC Regulations” means the Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016[^f00003].
