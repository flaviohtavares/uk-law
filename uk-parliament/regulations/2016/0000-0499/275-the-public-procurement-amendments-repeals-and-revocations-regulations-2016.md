The Minister for the Cabinet Office is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to public procurement[^f00002] and in relation to the combating of late payment in commercial transactions[^f00003].

The Minister for the Cabinet Office makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, as read with paragraph 1A[^f00004] of Schedule 2 to, that Act.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of that Act and it appears to the Minister for the Cabinet Office that it is expedient for certain references to EU instruments to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

## 5. Transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6f67ada49e6a6db1193d4213a78d1828][^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(1) Nothing in these Regulations affects any contract award procedure or design contest commenced before 18th April 2016. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(2) For that purpose, a contract award procedure or design contest has been commenced before 18th April 2016 if, before that date— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

- (a) a contract notice has been sent to the Official Journal in accordance with the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd][^f00005] in order to invite offers or requests to be selected to tender for, or to negotiate in respect of, a proposed public contract, framework agreement or dynamic purchasing system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (b) the contracting authority has had published any form of advertisement seeking offers or expressions of interest in a proposed public contract, framework agreement or dynamic purchasing system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (c) the contracting authority has contacted any economic operator in order to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
  - (i) seek expressions of interest or offers in respect of a proposed public contract, framework agreement or dynamic purchasing system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd] respond to an unsolicited expression of interest or offer received from that economic operator in relation to a proposed public contract, framework agreement or dynamic purchasing system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (d) a contest notice has been sent to the Official Journal in accordance with the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 for the purpose of regulation 79 of those Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(3) Nothing in these Regulations affects the award of a specific contract based on a framework agreement where the framework agreement was concluded— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

- (a) before 18th April 2016; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (b) on or after that date following a contract award procedure which, by virtue of paragraph (1), was not affected by these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(4) Nothing in these Regulations affects the award of a specific contract under a dynamic purchasing system where the system was established— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

- (a) before 18th April 2016; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (b) on or after that date following a contract award procedure which, by virtue of paragraph (1), was not affected by these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(5) Nothing in these Regulations affects a contract awarded— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

- (a) before 18th April 2016; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]
- (b) on or after that date but where the award itself was not, by virtue of paragraphs (1) to (4), affected by these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(6) In this regulation, “contracting authority”, “design contests”, “dynamic purchasing system”, “economic operator”, “framework agreement”, “Official Journal” and “public contract” have the same meanings as in the Public Contracts Regulations 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

(7) In so far as this regulation relates to contracts affected by the amendments and modifications made by paragraphs 48 to 50 of Schedule 2— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

- (a) the reference in regulation 5(2)(a) to the “Public Contracts Regulations 2015” is a reference to the Utilities Contracts Regulations 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd][^f00006];
- (b) the references in regulation 5(2)(b) and (c) to the “contracting authority” are references to a “licenced infrastructure provider” or an “incumbent undertaker” (as the case may be) within the meaning of the Water Industry (Specified Infrastructure Projects) (English Undertakers) Regulations 2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd][^f00007];
- (c) the references in regulation 5(2) to a “proposed public contract” are references to a “proposed contract”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68078d6a794b04960fe4ec3b402d7bcd]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make miscellaneous amendments to Acts of Parliament and statutory instruments that are consequential on the coming into force of the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 (which transpose [Directive 2014/24/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0024) of the European Parliament and of the Council on public procurement (OJ No L 94, 28.3.2014, p63)), the Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016 (which transpose [Directive 2014/25/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0025) of the European Parliament and of the Council on procurement by entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors (OJ No L 94, 28.3.2014, p243)) and the Concession Contracts Regulations 2016 (which transpose [Directive 2014/23/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0023) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the award of concession contracts (OJ No L 94, 28.3.2104)). Schedule 3 to these Regulations contains consequential revocations and repeals.

These Regulations also make a corrective amendment to the Defence and Security Public Contracts Regulations 2011 and miscellaneous amendments to the Public Contracts Regulations 2015, including some corrective amendments and amendments to transpose Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1986 establishing standard forms for the publication of notices in the field of public procurement (OJ No L 296, 12.11.2015, p1). This Commission Implementing Regulation sets out the required standard format for notices sent to the Publications Office of the European Union for publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
