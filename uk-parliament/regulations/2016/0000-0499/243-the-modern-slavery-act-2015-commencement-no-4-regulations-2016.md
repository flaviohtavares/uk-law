# The Modern Slavery Act 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 243 (C. 14)

Modern Slavery

The Modern Slavery Act 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2016

Made

25th February 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) of the Modern Slavery Act 2015[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Modern Slavery Act 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2016.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 17th March 2016

The following provisions of Schedule 5 to the Modern Slavery Act 2015 (minor and consequential amendments) come into force on 17th March 2016—

- (a) paragraph 11;
- (b) paragraph 15; and
- (c) paragraph 27(3).

Karen Bradley

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

25th February 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the fourth commencement regulations made under the Modern Slavery Act [2015 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30).

Regulation 2 brings into force consequential amendments to the Administration of Justice Act [1970 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/31), the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29) and the Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act [2013 (c. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3).
