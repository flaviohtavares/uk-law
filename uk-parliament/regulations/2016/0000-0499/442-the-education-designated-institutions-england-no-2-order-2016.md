# The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 442

Education, England

The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2016

Made

24th March 2016

Laid before Parliament

29th March 2016

Coming into force

19th April 2016

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2016 and comes into force on 19th April 2016.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England.

## 2. Designation of institution

University Campus Suffolk, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

Joseph Johnson

Minister of State for Universities and Science

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

24th March 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates University Campus Suffolk as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

An impact assessment has not been produced for the Order because it has no impact on businesses, civil society organisations or the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
