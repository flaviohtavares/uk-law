# The Thorpe Marsh Gas Pipeline Order 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. No double recovery[^c-69166469f8ca557b330a6eeadbf37c31]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and any other enactment, any contract, any rule of law or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
