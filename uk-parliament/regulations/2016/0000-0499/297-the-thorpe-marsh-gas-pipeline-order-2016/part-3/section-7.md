## 7. Benefit of Order[^key-55b2b1f12e6cccca28a6e304142fcb70]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 8 (consent to transfer benefit of Order), the provisions of this Order are to have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.
