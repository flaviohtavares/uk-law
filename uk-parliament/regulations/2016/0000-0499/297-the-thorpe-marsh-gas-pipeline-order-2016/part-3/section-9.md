> Text as at 2017-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Application and modification of the 1997 Regulations[^c-9881c9f86478813ec97b3ed8b1cfdc88]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 6(1) of the 1997 Regulations is modified so as to read for the purposes of this Order only as if there were inserted after sub-paragraph (j) the following—

> ; or
>
> - (k) for carrying out development which has been authorised by an order granting development consent pursuant to the Planning Act 2008.

.
