> Text as at 2017-11-30.

## 15. Access to works[^c-56f0525777a71a1662b643d9ae380397]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may, for the purposes of the authorised development, form and lay out means of access, or improve existing means of access, in the locations specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 4 (access to works).
