# The East Midlands Gateway Rail Freight Interchange and Highway Order 2016, section 27

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-cb93ccb2419b55f0a1ce13e63151f0cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00033] (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
