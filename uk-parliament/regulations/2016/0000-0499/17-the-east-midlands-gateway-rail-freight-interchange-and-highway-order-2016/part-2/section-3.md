# The East Midlands Gateway Rail Freight Interchange and Highway Order 2016, section 3

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent granted by the Order[^key-b8e1df9e4dd260a870a7f945738face6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development to be carried out subject to the provisions of the Order within the Order limits and subject to the requirements.

(2) Nothing in this Order prevents the carrying out of archaeological investigations (under requirement 13(1)), investigations for the purpose of assessing ground conditions, remedial work in respect of any contamination or erection of any temporary means of enclosure and the temporary display of site notices or advertisement immediately upon this Order coming into force.
