# The East Midlands Gateway Rail Freight Interchange and Highway Order 2016, section 6

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-b552f0dc5b4be6668b0137cb12184e0e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order provides otherwise.
