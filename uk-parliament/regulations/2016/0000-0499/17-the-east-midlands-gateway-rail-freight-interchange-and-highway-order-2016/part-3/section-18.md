> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Amendments to traffic regulation orders[^c-6cf819e8abee3f54e3fb1a5103a99450]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The orders referred to in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 9 (amendments to existing orders) are revoked or amended as set out in column (3) of Schedule 9 upon the event listed in column (4) of Schedule 9 occurring.
