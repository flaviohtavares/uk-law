# The East Midlands Gateway Rail Freight Interchange and Highway Order 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Defence to proceedings in respect of statutory nuisance[^c-36d2b8e6da43954dd6e4405f09a0e23c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where proceedings are brought under section 82(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00036] (summary proceedings by persons aggrieved by statutory nuisance) in relation to a nuisance falling within paragraph (g) of section 79(1) of that Act (noise emitted from premises so as to be prejudicial to health or a nuisance) no order may be made, and no fine may be imposed, under section 82(2)[^f00037] of that Act if—

- (a) the defendant shows that the nuisance—
  - (i) relates to premises used by the undertaker for the purposes of or in connection with the construction or maintenance of the authorised development and that the nuisance is attributable to the carrying out of the authorised development in accordance with a notice served under section 60 (control of noise on construction site), or a consent given under section 61 (prior consent for work on construction site) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00038]; or
  - (ii) is a consequence of the construction or maintenance of the authorised development and that it cannot reasonably be avoided; or
- (b) the defendant shows that the nuisance is a consequence of the use of the authorised development and that it cannot be reasonably avoided.

(2) Section 61(9) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974, does not apply where the consent relates to the use of the premises by the undertaker for the purposes of or in connection with the construction or maintenance of the authorised development.
