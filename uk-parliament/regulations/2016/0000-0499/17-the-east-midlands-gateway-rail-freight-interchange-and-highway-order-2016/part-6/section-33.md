# The East Midlands Gateway Rail Freight Interchange and Highway Order 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Operation and use of railways[^key-b544c2f22f0bb3ba016bf19f7f1eb330]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may operate and use the railway comprised in the authorised development and any other elements of the authorised development as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of goods.

(2) Nothing in this Order, or in any enactment incorporated with or applied by this Order, prejudices or affects the operation of Part 1 (the provision of railway services) of the Railways Act 1993[^f00035].
