# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amendment of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 8(1) [^c23108421] of the Human Medicines Regulations (general interpretation), in the definition of “Fees Regulations” for “the Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2013” substitute “ the Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016 ”.
