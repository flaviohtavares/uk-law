# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 41

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Fees for applications for membership and certificates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee payable by an applicant for membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £141[^c-6d4eb00e0b370be833774623bf8cb284].

(2) The fee payable by an applicant for a certificate of membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £75[^c-b8079eb41db1c83b672c76be175d54e2].
