# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 31

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Fees for inspections of pharmacovigilance service providers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an inspection is made of a pharmacovigilance service provider and that inspection is not related to anything done under regulation 30(1)(a), a fee is payable in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 3.

(2) The fee in paragraph (1) is payable by the pharmacovigilance service provider who is the subject of an inspection.

(3) In this regulation a “**pharmacovigilance service provider**” means a provider of pharmacovigilance services to a marketing authorisation holder.
