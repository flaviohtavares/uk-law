# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 37

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Amount, and time for payment, of inspection fees in respect of an application for a broker's registration or an active substance registration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) All sums payable by way of fees in respect of any assessment or inspection of a site in connection with an application for a broker's registration or an active substance registration under regulation 30(1) must be paid in advance of an application or at the time the application is made.

(2) The inspection fee payable as a consequence of paragraph (1) shall be—

- (a) in relation to broker's registrations, the amount specified in paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 3;
- (b) in relation to active substance registrations, the amount specified in paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 3.
