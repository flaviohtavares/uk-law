# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Fees for applications for certificates and copy certificates by exporters of medicinal products

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee payable by an applicant for a certificate issued under regulation 31 (certification of manufacturer's licence) of the Human Medicines Regulations, is—

- (a) £182[^key-d21294273ab9724f067f6b87ccc4779a], if the applicant requests the certificate to be issued within 24 hours of receipt of the application; and
- (b) £82[^key-02a3660d884fc52b438307b71f9f04b6] in any other case.

(2) The fee in paragraph (1)(a) and (b) is for three identical signed certificates.

(3) The fee payable by the applicant for a certified copy of the certificate referred to in paragraph (1) is £37[^key-5665a0ff2ba8dd205d4f5567de61fef1].
