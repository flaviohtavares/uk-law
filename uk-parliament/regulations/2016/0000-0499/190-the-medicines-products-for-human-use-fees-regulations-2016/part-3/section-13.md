# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 13

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Fee for application to be included on the list of online sellers of medicines

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The fee payable by an applicant to be included on the list of online sellers of medicines is the fee prescribed for that application in Part 2 of Schedule 2.
