# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 27

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Fees for renewals of certain manufacturer's licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee payable by the applicant for an application to renew a manufacturer's licence which falls within the description in paragraph (2) is £214[^key-0661b3c556bd6f416bbc01eccde6a591].

(2) The licence referred to in paragraph (1) is one—

- (a) which is solely for the manufacture of medicinal products the sale or supply of which does not require a marketing authorisation or a product licence; and
- (b) to which regulation 169 (mixing of general sale medicinal products) of the Human Medicines Regulations applies.

(3) Where an inspection of a site is made in connection with the application referred to in paragraph (1) an inspection fee of £325[^key-1a5eed2679f36e58dde20075f3ac49f7] is also payable by the applicant.
