# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 19EA

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19EA. Fee for assessment of clinical trial annual safety reports[^c-2664b142a4f37489d1d98aa5c03861ab][^c-407e3344a00ee603e6097de77073b58b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The fee payable by a sponsor who submits an annual safety report to the licensing authority under regulation 35(1)(b) of the Clinical Trials Regulations is £343[^c-61cd0c080398730bce9b6ee441943bf6].
