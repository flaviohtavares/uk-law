# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 19G

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19G. Time for payment of fees under regulations 19A to 19F[^key-407e3344a00ee603e6097de77073b58b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All sums payable by way of fees under regulations 19A to 19F are payable on invoice.
