# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 19A

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19A. Fees for certification of plasma master files[^c-407e3344a00ee603e6097de77073b58b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee payable by a person who submits a plasma master file to the licensing authority for scientific and technical evaluation in accordance with paragraph 1.1(c), second indent, of Part III of Annex I to the 2001 Directive, is £11,126[^c-397f2d12d768866b132bc442405485f1].

(2) The fee payable by a person who submits a plasma master file to the licensing authority for re-certification in accordance with paragraph 1.1(c), third indent, of Part III of Annex I to the 2001 Directive is—

- (a) £419[^c-c11e521e8d75e8457a1a13d1da7d95b0], where there are no changes to the plasma master file other than an update to epidemiological data; or
- (b) £1,593[^c-81bc77427824bd8754505553921dd410], in any other case.
