# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 20

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Fees for modifications to clinical trial authorisations[^c-32da0d138fa719c00328ffa22280da05]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who sends a modification request under regulation 22 (modifications[^c-e35a1744010d98d4cb4cef2c449a8f9e] by the sponsor) of the Clinical Trials Regulations relating to amendment of the protocol or the dossier related to a request for authorisation in accordance with paragraphs 2 or 5(a) of Part A1 of Schedule 3 (request for approval)[^c-6ffb231ae237abe9d4d0deb97e56a7ba] to those Regulations must pay the fees mentioned in paragraph (2).

(2) The fees referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the fee prescribed in paragraph 49 of Schedule 2 in connection with that amendment; and
- (b) in respect of an inspection of a site made in connection with the application, the fee payable in accordance with regulations 30, 32, 33, 35 and 37.
