# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 45

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Fees for applications made at the invitation of the licensing authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No fee shall be payable under this Part in connection with an application for the grant or variation of a certificate of registration under Part 6 (certification of homoeopathic medicinal products) of the Human Medicines Regulations where the application is made at the specific request of the licensing authority.
