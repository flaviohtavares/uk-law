# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 47

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Fees for variations of certificates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee payable by an applicant in connection with an application for an administrative variation of a certificate of registration shall be—

- (a) where more than one application for an administrative variation is made at the same time by the same applicant and the applications are for identical variations—
  - (i) in respect of the first application considered by the licensing authority, a fee of £149[^c-15cede2eb8f4cdabcbb44878a57d8781], and
  - (ii) in respect of each other application so considered, a fee of £75[^c-5f390b5c712e80cbfda209a360fd7200];
- (b) in any other case, a fee of £149[^c-6eab6c98fa3ed1a899f80c703b0470c2].

(2) The fee payable by an applicant in connection with an application for a standard variation of a certificate of registration shall be—

- (a) where more than one application for a standard variation is made at the same time by the same applicant and the applications are for identical variations—
  - (i) in respect of the first application considered by the licensing authority, a fee of £296[^c-18cd7b7e74719ba10c346c9b231b8a9f];
  - (ii) in respect of each other application so considered, where further medical, technical or scientific assessment is required, a fee of £296[^c-39c8c875f7010bfe14f8f0c84e50edc2];
  - (iii) in respect of the second to thirtieth applications so considered, where no further medical, technical or scientific assessment is required, a fee of £149[^c-7fb99618cbe144262b178dca2de71dd3];
  - (iv) in respect of each other application so considered, where no further medical, technical or scientific assessment is required, a fee of £75[^c-82673e317affe7cc84a8f8c85191c2aa];
- (b) in any other case, a fee of £296[^c-89083df17dc8cc47a9c8e1fb6df7db05].
