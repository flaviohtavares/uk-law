# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 17

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Fees for applications for regulatory assistance under the mutual recognition procedure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The fee payable by an applicant to the licensing authority for regulatory assistance in connection with obtaining recognition according to the procedure laid down in Title III, Chapter 4 of the 2001 Directive of a single United Kingdom marketing authorisation in another EEA State or in other EEA States, is the fee prescribed in Part 3 of Schedule 2 in connection with the application or set of applications.
