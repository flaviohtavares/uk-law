# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Time for payment of fees under regulations 3A to 8[^c-d0d0a2d9f36cb0bf35bdef9803105b8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All sums payable by way of fees under regulations 3A to 8 must be paid within a period of 14 days, commencing on the date of the written notice issued by the licensing authority requiring payment of those fees.
