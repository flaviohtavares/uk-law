# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 8

> Text as at 2026-04-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Fee for advice on labelling or leaflets

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The fee payable by the holder of one or more marketing authorisations with whom the licensing authority holds a meeting in order to provide advice on proposed changes to the labelling or the package leaflets of the medicinal products to which those authorisations relate, is £3,757[^c-6bef36270463cf58447756af44675a5b].
