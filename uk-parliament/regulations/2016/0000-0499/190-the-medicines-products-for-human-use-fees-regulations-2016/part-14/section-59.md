# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 59

> Text as at 2026-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Civil proceedings to recover unpaid fees

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All unpaid sums due by way of, or on account of, any fees payable under these Regulations shall be recoverable as debts due to the Crown.
