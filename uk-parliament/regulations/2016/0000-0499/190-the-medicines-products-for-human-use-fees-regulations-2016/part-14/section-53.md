# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 53

> Text as at 2026-04-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Time for payment of periodic fees

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All periodic fees must be paid by the first day of the fee period to which they relate, unless otherwise specified in these Regulations.
