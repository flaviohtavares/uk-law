# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2016, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Time for payment of fees under regulation 25

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All sums payable by way of fees under regulation 25(1) must be paid by the time that the proposed changes are submitted to the licensing authority.
