# The National Grid (Hinkley Point C Connection Project) Order 2016, section 35

> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 35. Deemed Marine Licence

The undertaker is deemed to have been granted the licence under Part 4 of the 2009 Act contained in Schedule 9 to this Order, to carry out the works and make the deposits described in that licence and subject to the licence conditions which are deemed to have been attached to the licence by the Secretary of State under Part 4 of the 2009 Act.
