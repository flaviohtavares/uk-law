# The National Grid (Hinkley Point C Connection Project) Order 2016, section 48

> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 48. No double recovery

Compensation must not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
