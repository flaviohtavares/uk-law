# The National Grid (Hinkley Point C Connection Project) Order 2016, section 20

> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00018] (minerals) are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” there is substituted “the undertaker”.

## Annotations

[^f00018]: Footnote: [1981 c. 67](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/67). Sub-paragraph (5) of paragraph 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 was amended by section 67 of, and paragraph 27(3) of Schedule 9 to, the Coal Industry Act [1994 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/21) and paragraph 8 of Part 3 of Schedule was amended by section 46 of the Criminal Justice Act [1982 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/48). There are other amendments to the 1981 Act which are not relevant to this Order.
