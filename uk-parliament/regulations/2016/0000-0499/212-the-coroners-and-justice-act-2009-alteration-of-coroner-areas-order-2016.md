# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

22nd February 2016

Laid before Parliament

25th February 2016

Coming into force

1st April 2016

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001], and after consulting in accordance with that paragraph, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2016 and comes into force on 1st April 2016.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “coroner area” means a coroner area constituted—
  - (a) where the coroner area was constituted by the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2013[^f00002], by that Order, or
  - (b) in any other case, by the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013[^f00003];
- “altered coroner area” means a new coroner area resulting from the combining of coroner areas as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Altered coroner areas

Coroner areas listed in the first column of the table in the Schedule to this Order are combined into the altered coroner area listed in the corresponding entry in the second column.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroners Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1625](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1625)), which came into force on 25th July 2013, constituted the former coroners’ districts under the Coroners Act [1988 (c.13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/13) as corresponding coroner areas under the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25).

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1626](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1626)), which came into force on 26th July 2013, amalgamated some of those coroner areas into new coroner areas.

This Order combines six coroner areas into three new coroner areas.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
