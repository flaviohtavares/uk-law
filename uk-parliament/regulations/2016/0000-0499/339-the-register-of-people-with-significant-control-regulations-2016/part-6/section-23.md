# The Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/339
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Permitted disclosure of usual residential address information by the registrar to credit reference agencies[^c-2d6f6003c7ac4d4a472a68274357d019]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The conditions specified for the disclosure of information within section 790ZF(2) of the Act by the registrar to a credit reference agency [^c23150941] in accordance with section 243 of the Act (as applied by section 790ZF of the Act) are listed in Part 2 of Schedule 4.

(2) The registrar may rely on a statement delivered to the registrar by a credit reference agency under Part 2 of Schedule 4 as sufficient evidence of the matters stated in it.
