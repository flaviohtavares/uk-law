# The Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/339
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Duration of a determination made under regulation 25, 26 or 27[^key-2d6f6003c7ac4d4a472a68274357d019]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A determination made under regulation 25(5), 26(7) or 27(7) that an application is successful continues to have effect until—

- (a) either—
  - (i) the person to whom the determination relates; or
  - (ii) that person's personal representative,

  notifies the registrar in writing that he or she wishes the determination to cease to have effect; or
- (b) the registrar revokes the determination under regulation 32.
