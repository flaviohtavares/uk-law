## 4. Legal entities which are subject to their own disclosure requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A legal entity [^c23150811] (other than one to which section 790C(7)(c) of the Act applies) is specified for the purpose of section 790C(7)(d) of the Act if it has voting shares admitted to trading—

- (a)[^c-12a78667208764665de32adce54924de] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) on a market listed in Schedule 1.
