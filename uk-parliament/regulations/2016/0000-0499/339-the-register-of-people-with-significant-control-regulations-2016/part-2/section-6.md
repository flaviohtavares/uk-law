# The Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016, section 6

## 6. Fee for a copy of a company's PSC register[^key-aad6d7f9d171e6be8a1c60bb8ed60ac8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fee prescribed for the purpose of section 790O(2) of the Act is £12.

(2) That fee applies to any single request for a copy of a company's PSC register [^c23150821], or any part of it, regardless of how many parts are required to be copied.
