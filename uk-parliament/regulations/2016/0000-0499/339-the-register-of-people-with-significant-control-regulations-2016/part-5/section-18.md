# The Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016, section 18

## 18. Content of a warning notice[^c-2d6f6003c7ac4d4a472a68274357d019]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A warning notice [^c23150911] given under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1B to the Act must—

- (a) specify the date on which the warning notice is given;
- (b) be accompanied by a copy of the notice given under section 790D , 790DA, 790E or 790EA[^c-c4d1f7199f3449ea55ce1d276634d8a1] of the Act to which the warning notice relates;
- (c) identify the addressee's relevant interest in the company by reference to the shares or right in question;
- (d) state that the company will consider reasons provided to it as to why the addressee failed to comply with the notice given under section 790D , 790DA, 790E or 790EA[^c-5cde1da1378e9f44c4cb0bf1d7c7fd71] of the Act;
- (e) explain the effect of a restrictions notice; and
- (f) state that, by virtue of a restrictions notice, certain acts or failures to act may constitute an offence.
