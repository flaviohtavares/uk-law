# The Register of People with Significant Control Regulations 2016, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2016/339
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Failure to comply with a section 790D , 790DA, 790E or 790EA notice: valid reason[^key-2d6f6003c7ac4d4a472a68274357d019][^key-18a6a0e86941f1a5f7ef18b3b7560b9e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company must take into account any incapacity of the addressee of a notice given under section 790D , 790DA, 790E or 790EA[^key-904ef73cc829d247c5232a43334e35d5] of the Act in deciding what counts as a “valid reason” sufficient to justify the addressee's failure to comply with the notice.
