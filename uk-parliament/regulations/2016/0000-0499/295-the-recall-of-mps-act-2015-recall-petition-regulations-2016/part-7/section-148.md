# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 148

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Publication of documents[^key-d6ce884d8650d96e9374990e8096feed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any failure to publish a document in accordance with these Regulations does not invalidate the document, but this provision does not relieve any person from any penalty for such a failure.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), where a document is made available for inspection, any person may make a copy (whether in handwriting or by other means) of the whole or any part of such a document.

(3) Paragraph (2) is subject to any restriction as to the making of copies which is imposed by—

- (a) regulation 80(6); and
- (b) paragraph 3(7) of Schedule 3.
