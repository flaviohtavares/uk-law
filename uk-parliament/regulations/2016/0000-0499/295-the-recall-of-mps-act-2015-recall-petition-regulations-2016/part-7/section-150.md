# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 150

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Modification of section 6C PPERA 2000[^key-e517a18ac62878eab157e54c2bf89b27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 6C of PPERA 2000 (accredited observers: individuals) is to apply for the purposes of attending the count of a petition under regulation 39 (the count) as it applies for the purposes of attending the proceedings for the counting of votes under that section.
