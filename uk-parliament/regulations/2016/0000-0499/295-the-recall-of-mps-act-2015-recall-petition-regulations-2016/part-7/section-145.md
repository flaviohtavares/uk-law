# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 145

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Time[^c-8d08274c3244a2b90faeb39a63eef921]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In computing any period of time for the purposes of these Regulations any day which is not a working day is to be disregarded[^f00044].
