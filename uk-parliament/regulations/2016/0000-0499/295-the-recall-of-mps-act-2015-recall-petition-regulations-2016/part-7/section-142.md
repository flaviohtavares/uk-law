# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 142

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Communication of applications, notices etc[^c-4967360af43152145d770100c225ec35]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The requirement in these Regulations that any application, notice, representation or objection should be in writing is satisfied where (apart from the usual meaning of that expression) the text of it—

- (a) is transmitted by electronic means;
- (b) is received in legible form; and
- (c) is capable of being used for subsequent reference.
