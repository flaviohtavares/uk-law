# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 151

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Fee for the copy of the return or declaration or any other document[^c-56c4b6b6f8dce7be9e866525948373c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph 9 of Schedule 5 to the Act (inspection of returns and accompanying documents) the prescribed fee under subparagraph (c) is to be 20p for each side of paper supplied under that subparagraph.
