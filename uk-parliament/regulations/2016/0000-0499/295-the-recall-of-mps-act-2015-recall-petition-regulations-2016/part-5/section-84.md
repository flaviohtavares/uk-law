## 84. Form of declaration of identity: Northern Ireland[^c-b8f340dba860bbbd5686d1ce078007c5]

*Extent: N.I.*

The form of the declaration of identity to be used in Northern Ireland for the purposes of a petition must be in the form of Form M in Schedule 2.
