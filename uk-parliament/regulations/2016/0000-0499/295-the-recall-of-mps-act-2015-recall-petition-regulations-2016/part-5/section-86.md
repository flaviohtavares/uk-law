# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 86

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Time when postal signing packs are to be issued[^c-c035c2cf32b6cb8849bffbb81f39ad0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A petition officer must issue the postal signing packs as soon as reasonably practicable and, in any event—

- (a) between one and three working days before the designated day[^f00042];
- (b) on the tenth and twentieth working day after the designated day; and
- (c) by the eighth working day before the last day of the signing period.

## Annotations

[^c-c035c2cf32b6cb8849bffbb81f39ad0a]: Commencement: Reg. 86 in force at 4.3.2016, see [reg. 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/295/regulation/1)

[^f00042]: Footnote: See section 7(1) of the Act; “designated day” means the day from which the petition is made available for signing.
