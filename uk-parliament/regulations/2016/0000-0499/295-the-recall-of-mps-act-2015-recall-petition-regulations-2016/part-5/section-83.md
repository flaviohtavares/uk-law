## 83. Form of postal petition statement: England and Wales and Scotland[^key-b41c2e43f82f1140ba21c9b848d3933b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The form of the postal petition statement to be used in England and Wales and Scotland for the purposes of a petition must be in the form of Form K in Schedule 2.
