# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 102

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Receipt of covering envelopes[^key-6e30351c4ac3acad3a6e9718a97c59dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The petition officer must, immediately on receipt by post[^key-17662dfab4f020df6d674924b5f6a5a8] of a covering envelope (or an envelope which is stated to include a postal signing sheet) before the end of the signing period, place it unopened in a postal signers’ ballot box.

(2) Where an envelope, other than a covering envelope issued by the petition officer—

- (a) has been opened, and
- (b) contains a signing sheet envelope, postal petition statement or declaration of identity (as the case may be), or signing sheet,

the first-mentioned envelope, together with its contents, must be placed in a postal signers’ ballot box.
