# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 88

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Refusal to issue postal signing sheet[^key-c93bb917e52a875fc8b71f6cd7bcabc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the petition officer is satisfied that two or more entries in the postal signers list, list of proxies, or the proxy postal signers list, or an entry in two or more of those lists, relates to the same elector, the officer must not issue more than one postal signing sheet in respect of that elector at any one petition.
