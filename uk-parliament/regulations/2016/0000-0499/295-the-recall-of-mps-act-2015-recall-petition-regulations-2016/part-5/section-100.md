# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 100

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Persons entitled to be present at proceedings on receipt of postal signing sheet[^c-69ef7c7cb94e55e79dd0c3ff27c67b92]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No person may be present at the proceedings on the receipt of postal signing sheets other than—

- (a) the petition officer;
- (b) any assistants of the petition officer;
- (c) any petition clerk;
- (d) any representative of the Electoral Commission.
