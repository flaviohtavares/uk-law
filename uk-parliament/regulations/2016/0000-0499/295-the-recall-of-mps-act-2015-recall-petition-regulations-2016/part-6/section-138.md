# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 138

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Interference with notices[^key-891e77c53dd0dbd4b43a596a04a5fa67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person who without lawful authority destroys, mutilates, defaces or removes—

- (a) any notice published by the petition officer in connection with their duties as such, or
- (b) any copies of a document which have been made available for inspection in pursuance of those duties,

is liable on a summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
