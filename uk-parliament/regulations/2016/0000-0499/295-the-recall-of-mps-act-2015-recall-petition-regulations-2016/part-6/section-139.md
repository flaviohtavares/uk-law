> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139. Corrupt and illegal practices[^key-816106f4052c37f1734832434dea49f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 25 of Schedule 3 to the Act (corrupt and illegal practices) applies to an offence under this Part that is a corrupt or illegal practice as if that offence were such an offence under that Schedule.
