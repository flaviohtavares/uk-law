# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 128

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Illegal canvassing by police officers[^c-145a47e141c552b3149365f03e257372]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member of a police force must not by word, message, writing or in any other manner, endeavour to persuade any person to sign a petition held in relation to a constituency wholly or partly within the police area of the force of which that person is a member, or dissuade any person from signing such a petition, whether as an elector or as proxy.

(2) A person who contravenes paragraph (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale, but nothing in that paragraph shall subject a member of a police force to any penalty for anything done in the discharge of their duty as a member of the force.

(3) In this regulation, references to a member of a police force and to a police area are to be taken in relation to Northern Ireland as references to a member of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and to Northern Ireland.
