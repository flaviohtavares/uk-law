# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 49

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Interpretation[^c-9da6696caab70827213b302c05c4bfb4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- (a) “Schedule 4” means Schedule 4 to the 2000 Act (absent voting in Great Britain);
- (b) one person is “related” to another if he or she is the spouse or civil partner, parent, grandparent, brother, sister, child or grandchild of the other.
