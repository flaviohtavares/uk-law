# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 67

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Additional provision concerning the requirement that an application for absent signing must be signed by the applicant: England and Wales and Scotland[^key-3a15f2018c4e31732a296347bb6767e8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The registration officer must be satisfied—

- (a) that an application under Chapter 1 of this Part meets any requirements that it has been signed by the applicant and states his or her date of birth by referring to any signature and date of birth previously provided by the applicant to the registration officer; and
- (b) as to whether the applicant is unable to provide a signature or a consistent signature due to any disability or inability to read and write.
