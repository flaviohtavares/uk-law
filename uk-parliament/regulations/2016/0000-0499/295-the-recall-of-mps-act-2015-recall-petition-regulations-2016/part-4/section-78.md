# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 78

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Cancellation of proxy appointment[^key-fcfe8247f9cdfd4b3be52b21f89d8e13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the appointment of a proxy is cancelled by notice given to the registration officer under regulation 57(9) or ceases to be in force under regulation 57(10), the petition officer must—

- (a) notify the person whose appointment as proxy has been cancelled, expired or is no longer in force, unless the registration officer has previously been notified by that person that they no longer wish to act as proxy; and
- (b) remove that person’s name from the record kept under regulation 51(4) or regulation 52(5) (as the case may be).
