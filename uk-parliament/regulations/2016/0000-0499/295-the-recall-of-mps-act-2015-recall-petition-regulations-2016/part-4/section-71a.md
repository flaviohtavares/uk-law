# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 71A

## 71A (Extent: EWS). Grounds relating to identification: England and Wales and Scotland (Extent: EWS)[^c-2132779ff77d460056b136dd32307cbe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: EWS)) For the purposes of this Part, an application is made on grounds relating to identification if it is made because the following apply to B— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) condition 1, 2 or 3, and *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) except where B has an anonymous entry in the register, condition 4. *(Extent: EWS)*

(2 (Extent: EWS)) In this regulation, “ *(Extent: EWS)***B *(Extent: EWS)***” means— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) the applicant, where— *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (i (Extent: EWS)) the application is made under regulation 53(2), and *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (ii (Extent: EWS)) regulation 63A(2) does not apply to the application made under regulation 57(7) which is included in that application under regulation 53(2); *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) otherwise, the person already appointed as the applicant’s proxy at the time the application is made. *(Extent: EWS)*

(3 (Extent: EWS)) Condition 1 is that— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) immediately before the deadline, B has a specified document which B intends to use to sign the petition in person, and *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) after that deadline, that document is— *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (i (Extent: EWS)) lost, stolen, destroyed or damaged so as to be no longer usable as a specified document for the purposes of that petition, or *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (ii (Extent: EWS)) sent by B to another person to prove B’s identity, and B considers it is unlikely to be returned to B on or before the last day of the signing period at that petition. *(Extent: EWS)*

(4 (Extent: EWS)) Condition 2 is that— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) at a time during the period of 3 months ending with the deadline, B has applied for a specified document, *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) immediately before the deadline, B has not received that document, and *(Extent: EWS)*
- (c (Extent: EWS)) the application for the specified document has not been refused or withdrawn. *(Extent: EWS)*

(5 (Extent: EWS)) Condition 3 is that— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) B has an anonymous entry in the register, and *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) either— *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (i (Extent: EWS)) B has not been issued with an anonymous elector’s document, or *(Extent: EWS)*
  - (ii (Extent: EWS)) B has been issued with an anonymous elector’s document, and after the deadline B is allocated an electoral number which is different from the one shown on that document other than as a result of an application under section 9B of the 1983 Act (anonymous registration). *(Extent: EWS)*

(6 (Extent: EWS)) Condition 4 is that B does not have access to another specified document. *(Extent: EWS)*

(7 (Extent: EWS)) In this regulation— *(Extent: EWS)*

- (a (Extent: EWS)) the “ *(Extent: EWS)***deadline *(Extent: EWS)***” means 5pm on the sixth day before the last day of the signing period at the petition to which the application relates; *(Extent: EWS)*
- (b (Extent: EWS)) “ *(Extent: EWS)***specified document *(Extent: EWS)***” has the meaning given in regulation 27(1I). *(Extent: EWS)*
