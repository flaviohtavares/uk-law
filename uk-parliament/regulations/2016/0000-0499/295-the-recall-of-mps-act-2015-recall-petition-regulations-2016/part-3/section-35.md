# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 35

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Correction of errors during the signing period[^c-e98e23883f032c95af481377f76f3280]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The petition clerk must keep a list of persons to whom signing sheets are delivered in consequence of any alteration to the register of parliamentary electors made by virtue of section 13BC(6) of the 1983 Act which takes effect during the signing period (“list of alterations to the register”).
