# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 16

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Allotment of petition signing places[^key-3ff173b884896e85789394f5d40d7f9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The petition officer must allot a petition signing place to each person entitled to sign the petition in such manner as appears to the petition officer to be most convenient for that person (“the allotted petition signing place”).
