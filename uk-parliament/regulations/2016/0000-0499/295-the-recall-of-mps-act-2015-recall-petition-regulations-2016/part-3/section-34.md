# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 34

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Reporting discrepancies[^key-80cb8d70a7829baa3efb8a5a72f00e94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the petition clerk identifies any discrepancies between the number of signing sheets removed from the ballot box and the number of signing sheets delivered by the petition clerk for that day the petition clerk must immediately report the discrepancy to the petition officer.
