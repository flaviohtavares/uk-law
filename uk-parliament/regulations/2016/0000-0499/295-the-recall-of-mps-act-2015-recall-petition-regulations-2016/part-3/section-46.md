# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 46

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Retention and disposal of documents: England and Wales and Northern Ireland[^c-4b22cda41b0bee6561fe21def0690a96][^c-0639111704a87cf3812a41bb0a2b58c9]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c-9d2811e0b58a5f2895e72775567f342c] The registration officer must retain for a year all documents relating to a petition forwarded to them in pursuance of these Regulations by a petition officer, and then, unless otherwise directed by order of the House of Commons or any court, must cause them to be destroyed.

(2)[^c-eba710bd336947a35318db4f3e215dc9] The petition officer must retain for a year the documents referred to in regulation 44(2) (delivery of documents to the registration officer: Northern Ireland) and 110A (list of rejected postal petition documents: Northern Ireland)[^c-6d8d1a947996e3ade36b4231aa4e9f65] and then, unless otherwise directed by order of the House of Commons or any court, must cause them to be destroyed.
