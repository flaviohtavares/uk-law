# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 41

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Decisions on signing sheets[^c-cd3d1c18e8480d75e400e1552e47d7a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The decision of the petition officer on any question arising in respect of any signing sheet may be subject to review on a recall petition complaint but, subject to that, is final and may not be questioned in any proceedings whatsoever.
