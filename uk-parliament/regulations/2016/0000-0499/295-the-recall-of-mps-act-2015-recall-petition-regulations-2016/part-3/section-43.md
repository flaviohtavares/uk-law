# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 43

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Sealing up of signing sheets[^key-a885e99813470212f33522af4498718c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On the completion of the counting of the signing sheets, the petition officer must seal up in separate packets the counted and rejected signing sheets.

(2) The petition officer must not open the sealed packets of—

- (a) tendered signing sheets;
- (b) the completed corresponding number lists;
- (ba)[^key-040d87c49d5fa9cc4de9987cb9d4b766] in the case of a petition held in England, Wales or Scotland, the completed signing sheet refusal list;
- (c) certificates as to employment on duty during the petition period; or
- (d) marked copies of the register (including any marked copy notices issued under section 13BC(6) of the 1983 Act) and lists of proxies.
