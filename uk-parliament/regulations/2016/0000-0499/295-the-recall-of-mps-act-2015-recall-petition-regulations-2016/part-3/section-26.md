# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 26

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Challenge of person signing the petition[^key-ca2b78ce3ad61a6c66bd3f39ed02a1a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is entitled to sign the petition must not be prevented from doing so unless the person is arrested on the grounds that the person is suspected of committing or of being about to commit an offence of personation.
