# The Recall of MPs Act 2015 (Recall Petition) Regulations 2016, section 15

> Text as at 2025-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Availability for signing the petition[^key-19182bfc3d2eaae1186c451bdf8aa2c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The petition officer must ensure that the petition is available for signing at each petition signing place—

- (a) on each working day throughout the signing period[^f00016]; and
- (b) between the hours of 9am and 5pm on each of those days.

(2) In addition to the requirements set out in paragraph (1), the petition officer must make reasonable provision for the availability of the petition for signing at other times during the signing period.
