# The Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 345 (C. 19)

Legal Services, England And Wales

The Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2016

Made

10th March 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 151(1) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2016.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 1986 Act” means the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00002];
- “the 2012 Act” means the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012;
- “company” means a company within the meaning of section 1 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00003] or a company which may be wound up under Part V of the 1986 Act; and
- “proceedings” has the same meaning as in section 58A(4) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00004].

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 6th April 2016

Section 44[^f00005] (conditional fee agreements: success fees) and section 46[^f00006] (recovery of insurance premiums) of the 2012 Act come into force on 6th April 2016 in relation to—

- (a) proceedings in England and Wales brought by a person acting in the capacity of—
  - (i) a liquidator of a company which is being wound up in England and Wales or Scotland under Parts IV or V of the 1986 Act; or
  - (ii) a trustee of a bankrupt’s estate under Part IX of the 1986 Act;
- (b) proceedings brought by a person acting in the capacity of an administrator appointed pursuant to the provisions of Part II of the 1986 Act;
- (c) proceedings in England and Wales brought by a company which is being wound up in England and Wales or Scotland under Parts IV or V of the 1986 Act; and
- (d) proceedings brought by a company which has entered administration under Part II of the 1986 Act.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Edward Faulks

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

10th March 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the twelfth commencement order made under the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c.10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10) (“the 2012 Act”).

Article 2 brings into force, on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2016, section 44 (conditional fee agreements: success fees) and section 46 (recovery of insurance premiums by way of costs) of the 2012 Act in relation to insolvency proceedings. Insolvency proceedings were previously excluded from the commencement of sections 44 and 46 when those provisions were commenced by the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 5 and Saving Provision) Order 2013 ([SI 2013/77](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/77)).

The transitional provisions in sections 44(6) and 46(3) of the 2012 Act have effect in respect of the provisions commenced by this Order.
