# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**2.** The Directive repeals and replaces Council [Directive 93/15/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0015) on the harmonisation of the provisions relating to the placing on the market and supervision of explosives for civil uses[^f00017] (“the repealed Directive”) as amended by Regulation [(EC) No 1882/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1882) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29th September 2003[^f00018], Regulation [(EC) No 219/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0219) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11th March 2009[^f00019] and Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25th October 2012[^f00020].
