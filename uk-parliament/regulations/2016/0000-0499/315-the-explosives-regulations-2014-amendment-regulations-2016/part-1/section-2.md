# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Application outside Great Britain and extent

(1) These Regulations apply outside Great Britain as sections 1 to 59 and 80 to 82 of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974[^f00003] apply by virtue of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application outside Great Britain) Order 2013[^f00004].

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Northern Ireland.
