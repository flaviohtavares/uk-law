# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** The repealed Directive was first implemented as respects Great Britain by the Placing on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations 1993[^f00021] (“the 1993 Regulations”). Those Regulations were revoked by the consolidating Explosives Regulations 2014[^f00022] (“the 2014 Regulations”), which included provisions replacing the 1993 Regulations in Part 13 of the 2014 Regulations. Part 13 is now substituted by these Regulations and is for implementing the Directive.
