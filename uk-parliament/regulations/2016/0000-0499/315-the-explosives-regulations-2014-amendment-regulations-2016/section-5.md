# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**5.** The substituted Part 13 makes provision in relation to “economic operators” (for which a definition is added to regulation 2(1) of the 2014 Regulations), conformity assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies. Duties are imposed on manufacturers, importers and distributors of civil explosives. Provision is made as to the appointment in writing of authorised representatives by manufacturers who may then perform certain tasks on the manufacturer’s behalf. For manufacturers the duties include ensuring that a civil explosive has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the essential safety requirements set out in Schedule 9 to the 2014 Regulations, having a relevant conformity assessment procedure carried out before the civil explosive is placed on the market and affixing the CE marking.
