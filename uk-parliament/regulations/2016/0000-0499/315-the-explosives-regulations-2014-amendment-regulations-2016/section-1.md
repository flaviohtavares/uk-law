# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** These Regulations implement, as regards Great Britain, [Directive 2014/28/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0028) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the harmonisation of the laws of the Member States relating to the making available on the market and supervision of explosives for civil uses (recast) (“the Directive”).
