# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**7.** The obligations on distributors include acting with due care to ensure that civil explosives are in conformity with Part 13 and checking that the civil explosives bear the CE marking.
