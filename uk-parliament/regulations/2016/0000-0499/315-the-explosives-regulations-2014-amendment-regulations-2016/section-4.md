# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** Part 2 of these Regulations includes the amendments to the 2014 Regulations. The main amendments are the definitions added to regulation 2 of those Regulations, including a revised definition of “civil explosive” (regulation 4), the substituted Part 13 (regulation 7) and the related substitution of Schedule 12 for enforcement and market surveillance (regulation 13) and the addition of two Schedules concerning notified bodies (regulation 14).
