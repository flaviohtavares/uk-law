# The Explosives Regulations 2014 (Amendment) Regulations 2016, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Consequential amendment

In regulation 33(2)(e)(ii) of the Dangerous Substances in Harbour Areas Regulations 1987[^f00013] (application of Part IX), for “regulation 47(1)” substitute “regulation 82(1)”.
