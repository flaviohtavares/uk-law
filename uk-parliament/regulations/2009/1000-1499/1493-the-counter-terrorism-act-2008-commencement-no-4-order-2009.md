# The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1493 (C. 75)

Prevention And Suppression Of Terrorism

The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2009

Made

16th June 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(5) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2009.

**2.** The following provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 shall come into force on 1st October 2009—

- (a) sections 40 to 61 (notification requirements);
- (b) Schedule 4 (notification orders);
- (c) Schedule 5 (foreign travel restriction orders); and
- (d) Schedule 6 (notification requirements: application to service offences).

David Hanson

Minister of State

Home Office

16th June 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st October 2009 the provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 listed in article 2.
