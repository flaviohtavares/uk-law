# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 57

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Application and interpretation of Part 12

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of this Part apply in relation to any proceedings in which an issue of fact falls to be determined, unless otherwise stated.

(2) In relation to any proceedings other than trial proceedings, references in this Part to a defendant are to be read as references to a person to whom the proceedings relate.
