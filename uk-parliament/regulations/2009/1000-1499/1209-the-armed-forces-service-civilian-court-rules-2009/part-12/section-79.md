# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 79

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Evidence given in private

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A special measures direction may not provide for the exclusion of persons under section 25 of the 1999 Act unless—

- (a)[^key-ccc1e247f24e65f8f83b3785c8d30817] the proceedings relate to—
  - (i) a sexual offence,
  - (ii) a modern slavery offence, or
  - (iii) any other offence where it is alleged that the behaviour of the defendant amounted to domestic abuse; or
- (b) it appears to the court that there are reasonable grounds for believing that any person other than a defendant has sought, or will seek, to intimidate the witness in connection with testifying in the proceedings.
