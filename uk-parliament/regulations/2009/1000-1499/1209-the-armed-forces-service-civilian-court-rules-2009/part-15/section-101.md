# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 101

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Breach of requirements: application for summons or warrant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application by the responsible officer for a summons or a warrant under paragraph 8A of Schedule 10 to the Sentencing Code (as inserted by paragraph 5 of Schedule 6A to the Act)[^key-930abd64027f95e9d0f8ccf341bf5c9c] shall be made in writing to the court administration officer, specifying—

- (a) the requirement of the order with which the offender is alleged to have failed to comply;
- (b) the respect in which, and the date on which (or the dates between which) he is alleged to have failed to comply with that requirement;
- (c) whether he has within the previous twelve months been given a warning under paragraph 6 of Schedule 10 to the Sentencing Code[^key-16a3fbd345cf588a05a72d2aef5b3d2d] in respect of the order, and if so when and in what terms; and
- (d) any grounds on which, to the responsible officer's knowledge, the offender is likely to rely as constituting a reasonable excuse for the alleged failure to comply.

(2) The court administration officer shall forward the application to the Judge Advocate General.

(3) The Judge Advocate General may—

- (a) issue a summons under paragraph 8A of Schedule 10 to the Sentencing Code[^key-4f25d68a1046263d3fbee6d263a6d85b] requiring the offender to appear before the court for proceedings under paragraph 11[^key-054b8ed18b272f7787df056f6609af21] of that Schedule;
- (b) issue a warrant under paragraph 8A[^key-6edde48f6f890bd0a0791096fe195131] of that Schedule for the offender's arrest;
- (c) dismiss the application without a hearing; or
- (d) direct a hearing of the application.

(4) If the Judge Advocate General directs a hearing of the application, the court administration officer shall notify the responsible officer of the time and place appointed for the hearing.
