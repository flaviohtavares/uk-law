# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 85

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Opening address

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before the Director adduces any evidence in trial proceedings—

- (a) the Director, and
- (b) with leave of the court, any defendant,

may make an opening address.
