# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 90

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Finding of not guilty before conclusion of the defence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At any time after the close of the case for the prosecution the court may find a defendant not guilty of a charge.

(2) The court may not make a finding under this rule unless it has invited the Director to address the court as to whether such a finding should be made.
