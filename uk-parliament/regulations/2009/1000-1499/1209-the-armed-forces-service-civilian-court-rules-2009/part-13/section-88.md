# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 88

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Submission of no case to answer

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At the close of the case for the prosecution a defendant may submit, in respect of any charge, that the Director has failed to establish a case for him to answer.

(2) If such a submission is allowed, the court shall find the defendant not guilty of the charge.
