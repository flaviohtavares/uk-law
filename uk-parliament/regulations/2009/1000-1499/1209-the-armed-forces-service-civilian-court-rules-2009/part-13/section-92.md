# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 92

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Announcement of findings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The finding of the court on each charge shall be announced in open court; and the judge advocate shall sign a record of the findings.

(2) Where no finding is recorded on a charge, the court may direct that the charge is to lie on the file, not to be proceeded with without the leave of the court or the Court Martial.
