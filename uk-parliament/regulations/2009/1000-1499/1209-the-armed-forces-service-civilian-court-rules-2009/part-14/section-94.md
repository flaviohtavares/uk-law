# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 94

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Dispute on facts after plea of guilty

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where, after the court has recorded a plea of guilty in respect of any charge, there are disputed facts in the case, the court may direct that any issue of fact be tried by the court.

(2) The finding of the court shall be announced in open court.
