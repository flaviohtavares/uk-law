# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 19

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Proceedings in absence of defendant etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Proceedings may be held in the absence of any person to whom they relate, if the court so directs.

(2) This rule does not permit a defendant to be arraigned in his absence.

(3)[^key-151ae6f44170f75d45efb805f321a3ce] The court may not impose a driving disqualification order in the absence of an offender, unless the court is satisfied that the offender was informed prior to the hearing that the court was considering disqualification.
