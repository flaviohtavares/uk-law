# The Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009, section 99E

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99E. Announcement of varied sentence[^key-ad473a5694dcb2fae3b714b92603f878]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the court varies the original sentence, rule 99 (pronouncement of sentence) and sections 252 (duty to give reasons and explain sentence) and 253(2) (duties in complying with section 252) of the Act apply as they apply to the passing of a sentence.
