# The Nottingham Express Transit System Order 2009, section 69

> Text as at 2011-07-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Saving for highway authorities[^c-fd10303f13bfe3826ae3013af2824faa]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Order shall affect any power of a highway authority to widen, alter, divert or improve any highway along which the authorised street tramway is laid.
