# The Nottingham Express Transit System Order 2009, section 15

> Text as at 2011-07-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Construction of bridges and tunnels[^key-63b6846d9e07c0a516c7bacd3b6dd0f8]

*Extent: E+W*

Any bridge or tunnel to be constructed under this Order for carrying a highway over or under the authorised tramroad shall be constructed in accordance with plans and specifications approved by the highway authority, but such approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.
