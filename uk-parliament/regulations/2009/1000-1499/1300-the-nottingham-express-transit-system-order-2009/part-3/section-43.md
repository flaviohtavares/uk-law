# The Nottingham Express Transit System Order 2009, section 43

> Text as at 2011-07-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Open space in the Borough of Broxtowe[^c-1418c0b020db84dc64d02d7d23ea2326]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Broxtowe open space and the County open space shall not vest in the promoter until the promoter has acquired the Broxtowe exchange land and, upon a request made by the promoter, Broxtowe Borough Council has certified that a scheme for the provision of the Broxtowe exchange land as open space has been implemented to its satisfaction.

(2) Upon the requirements of paragraph (1) being satisfied, the Broxtowe exchange land shall vest in the County Council subject to the like rights, trusts and incidents as attached to the Broxtowe open space and the County open space and, on the vesting of the Broxtowe exchange land, the Broxtowe open space and the County open space shall each be discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which they were previously subject.

(3) In this article—

- (a) “the Broxtowe exchange land” means the land in the Borough of Broxtowe shown numbered 14 on the works and land plans and hatched green on the open space and exchange land plans;
- (b) “the Broxtowe open space” means the open space comprised in land in the Borough of Broxtowe shown numbered 26, 32, 33, 37, 42, 66, 262, 504, 507, 510 and 511 on the works and land plans and hatched red on the open space and exchange land plans; and
- (c) “the County open space” means the open space comprised in land in the Borough of Broxtowe shown numbered 67 on the works and land plans and hatched blue on the open space and exchange land plans.
