# The Nottingham Express Transit System Order 2009, section 64

> Text as at 2011-07-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Notice of penalty fare provisions[^c-730e8968bc5e73eedecda63ade5375e5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It shall be the duty of the promoter to secure that a warning notice meeting the requirements of paragraph (2) is posted—

- (a) at every tramway stop, in such a position as to be readily visible to prospective passengers; and
- (b) in every tramcar, in such a position as to be readily visible to passengers travelling on that tramcar.

(2) A warning notice posted pursuant to paragraph (1) shall (however expressed) indicate the circumstances (as provided in article 61 (penalty fares)) in which passengers may be liable to pay a penalty fare and state the amount of the penalty fare.
