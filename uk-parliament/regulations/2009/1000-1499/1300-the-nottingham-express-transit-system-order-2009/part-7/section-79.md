# The Nottingham Express Transit System Order 2009, section 79

> Text as at 2011-07-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 79. Certification of plans, etc.[^c-7d31082cfdaf0ba894620673d73dbefa]

*Extent: E+W*

The promoter shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the works and land plans, the open space and exchange land plans and the traffic regulation and rights of way plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference, the works and land plans, the open space and exchange land plans and the traffic regulation and rights of way plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
