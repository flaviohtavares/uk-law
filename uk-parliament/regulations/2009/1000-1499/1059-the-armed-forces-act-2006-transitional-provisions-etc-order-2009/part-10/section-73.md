# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 73

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Powers where review completed before commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where—

- (a) before commencement, a review of a finding or punishment was carried out under section 115 of AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or section 71B of NDA 1957;
- (b) the finding or punishment was not quashed on the review; and
- (c) by commencement, no reference has been made under subsection (5A) or (5B) of section 115 of AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or of section 71B of NDA 1957.

(2) Subsections (3) to (7) of section 152 of AFA 2006 shall apply (with the modifications made by article 72) as if the review had been carried out under that section.

(3) Where, immediately before commencement, leave for a reference has been granted under subsection (5A) or (5B) of section 115 of AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or of section 71B of NDA 1957 but the reference has not been made, the leave has effect after commencement as leave granted by the Summary Appeal Court under section 152(4) or (7) of AFA 2006.
