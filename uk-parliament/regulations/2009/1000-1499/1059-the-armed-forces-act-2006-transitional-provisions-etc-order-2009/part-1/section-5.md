# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 5

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. “Service offence” in AFA 2006 not to include offences committed before commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 50(2) of AFA 2006 (definition of “service offence”), references in paragraphs (g) to (i) to an offence do not include one committed before commencement.
