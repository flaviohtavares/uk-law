# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 4

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Offences triable by civilian court

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Insofar as an offence under an enactment repealed by AFA 2006 is triable by a civilian court—

- (a) nothing in this Order affects the application of section 16 of the Interpretation Act 1978 [^c19669341] (general savings) in relation to the offence;
- (b) subject to paragraph (2), nothing in this Order applies in relation to the offence.

(2) Paragraph (1)(b) does not affect the application of articles 154 and 199 to 201.
