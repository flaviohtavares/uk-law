# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 189

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in this Part to a person's enlistment before commencement are to—

- (a) his enlistment in the regular army, the Royal Marines or the regular air force before commencement, or
- (b) his entry for service in the Royal Navy before commencement,

and references to persons enlisted before commencement are to be read accordingly.
