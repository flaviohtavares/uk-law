# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 195

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Term of compulsory service

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 13(7) of RFA 1996 (definition of “term of compulsory service”) the reference to a requirement imposed under AFA 2006 includes a requirement imposed by or under AA 1955, AFA 1955 or AFA 1966.
