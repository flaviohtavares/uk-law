# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 193

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. Discharge etc from the regular forces and transfer to the reserve forces

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 331(3) of AFA 2006 (power to make regulations conferring on a warrant officer a right to be discharged following his reduction in rank or rate) references to a warrant officer's reduction in rank or rate include a reduction imposed before commencement.

(2) Section 331(4) of that Act (power to make regulations enabling a person's discharge or transfer to the reserve forces to be postponed) is to be read as including power to make provision as to the effect of—

- (a) a person's retention in service by virtue of section 9 of AA 1955 or AFA 1955, paragraph 4A of Schedule 7 to AA 1955 or section 4 of AFA 1966;
- (b) a declaration made by a person under section 9(6) of AA 1955 or AFA 1955, paragraph 4A(6) of Schedule 7 to AA 1955 or section 4(6) of AFA 1966; and
- (c) any notice given by a person in accordance with such a declaration.
