# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 194

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 194. Membership of reserve force

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 25 of Schedule 14 to AFA 2006 (amendment of section 2(2)(a) of RFA 1996) does not prevent a person who, immediately before commencement, was a member of a reserve force from continuing to be a member of that force.
