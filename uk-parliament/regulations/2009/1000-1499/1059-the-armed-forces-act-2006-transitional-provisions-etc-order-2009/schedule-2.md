Article 206

### 1. Definitions

In this Schedule—

- “**the 2005 Order**” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Sentencing) (Transitory Provisions) Order 2005 [^c19670461];
- “**CJA 2003**” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003 [^c19670471];
- “**CJCSA 2000**” means the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 [^c19670481];
- “**PCC(S)A 2000**” means the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 [^c19670491].

### 8. Custody for life where custodial sentence would have been required by SDAs

(1) This paragraph applies until the coming into force of section 61 of CJCSA 2000, where—

- (a) article 89 (sentences required by SDAs) applies; and
- (b) the offender is aged under 21 on conviction.

(2) Where this paragraph applies, the references in article 89(2) and (4) to a sentence of life imprisonment are to be read as to a sentence of custody for life under section 272(2)(a) of the Sentencing Code, as applied by section 210A of AFA 2006[^key-ff5ab227bde3e7790e8fb1d73c754bfe].

### 17. Notification requirements under Counter-Terrorism Act 2008

In each of the following provisions of Schedule 6 to the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 [^c19670501] (application of notification requirements to service offences), the reference to imprisonment includes detention in a young offender institution—

- (a) paragraph 5(1)(a)(ii);
- (b) paragraph 7(2)(b) and (3).

### 18. The Supreme Court

(1) In relation to any time before the coming into force of section 23 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 [^c19670511]—

- (a) references to the Supreme Court in AFA 2006 and article 92 are to be read as to the House of Lords; and
- (b) references to the Supreme Court inserted by AFA 2006 in any other enactment are to be read as to the House of Lords.

(2) In relation to any time before the coming into force of section 59(2) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005, references in AFA 2006 to the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland are to be read as to the Supreme Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland.
