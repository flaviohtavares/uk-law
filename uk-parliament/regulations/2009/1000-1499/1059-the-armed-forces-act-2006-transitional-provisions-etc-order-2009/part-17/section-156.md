# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 156

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Review of stoppages and SDA compensation order, etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 177 of AFA 2006 (review of service compensation order), references to a service compensation order include—

- (a) stoppages under AA 1955, AFA 1955 or NDA 1957; and
- (b) a compensation order under paragraph 11 of Schedule 5A to AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or of Schedule 4A to NDA 1957.
