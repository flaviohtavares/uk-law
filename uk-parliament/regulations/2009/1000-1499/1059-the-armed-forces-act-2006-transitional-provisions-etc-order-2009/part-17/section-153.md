# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 153

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Power of service policeman to arrest person unlawfully at large

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 303(1) of AFA 2006 (power of service policeman to arrest person sentenced to service detention who is unlawfully at large), the reference to service detention includes an SDA sentence of service detention.

(2) Section 119(5) of AA 1955 and AFA 1955 (provision corresponding to section 303(2) of AFA 2006) apply for the purposes of section 303(1) of AFA 2006.

(3) Section 88(2) of NDA 1957 (provision corresponding to section 303(2) of AFA 2006) applies for the purposes of section 303(1) of AFA 2006, but as if—

- (a) the reference to civil custody were omitted; and
- (b) in the definition of “the appropriate rules” in section 88(3) of NDA 1957, paragraph (c) were omitted.
