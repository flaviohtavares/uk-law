# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 170

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. CSO made by any SDA court: conviction of further offence by Service Civilian Court

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where a person in whose case a community supervision order has been made is convicted by the Service Civilian Court of an offence committed during the supervision period (including an offence under paragraph 4(6) of Schedule 5A to AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or of Schedule 4A to NDA 1957).

(2) The Service Civilian Court may deal with the offender for the offence for which the order was made in any way in which it could deal with the offender if it had just convicted the offender of that offence.

(3) It is to be assumed for the purposes of paragraph (2) that the offence is one the Service Civilian Court would have jurisdiction to try.
