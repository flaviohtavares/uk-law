# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 149

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 149. Duty to receive and detain prisoners

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 299 of AFA 2006 (duty to receive prisoners), the reference to rules under section 300 of that Act includes Imprisonment and Detention Rules within the meaning of section 122 of AA 1955.
