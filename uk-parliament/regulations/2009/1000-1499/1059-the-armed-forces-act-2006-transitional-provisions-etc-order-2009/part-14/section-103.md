# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 103

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Increase in sentence for racial or religious aggravation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 240(3) of AFA 2006 (offences to which section 240 does not apply)—

- (a) the reference to an offence under section 42 of that Act includes an SDA civil offence;
- (b) the reference to the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales includes the corresponding civil offence.
