# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 180

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. Arrest by civilian police under warrant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 313 of AFA 2006 (arrest by civilian police under warrant of judge advocate) is modified as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), the reference to a service offence includes an SDA offence.

(3) In subsections (3) and (4), references to a warrant issued under that section include a warrant issued under section 190A of AA 1955 or AFA 1955 or section 103 of NDA 1957.
