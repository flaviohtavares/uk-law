# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 133

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Maximum fine for SDA civil offence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 284(2) of AFA 2006 (maximum fine by Service Civilian Court for criminal conduct offence)—

- (a) the reference to an offence under section 42 of that Act includes an SDA civil offence;
- (b) the reference to the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales includes the corresponding civil offence.
