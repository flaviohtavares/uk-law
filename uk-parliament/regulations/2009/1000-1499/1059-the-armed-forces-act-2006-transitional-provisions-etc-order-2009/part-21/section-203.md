# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 203

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Protection of children of service families

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 20(6)(b)(iii) of AFA 1991 (return of child to person other than parent etc), the reference to the agreement of a judge advocate includes an agreement given before commencement by the officer having jurisdiction.
