# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 202

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 202. Affidavits and declarations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 204(2) of AA 1955 or (as the case may be) AFA 1955 (admissibility of document pursuant to affidavit or declaration) shall continue to apply in relation to an affidavit or declaration taken in pursuance of the power conferred by section 204(1) of that Act, notwithstanding its repeal by AFA 2006.
