# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 90

> Text as at 2024-04-17.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Service restraining orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 230(3) of AFA 2006 (remission by Appeal Court of case to Court Martial), the reference to an appeal against conviction includes such an appeal brought from a court-martial.
