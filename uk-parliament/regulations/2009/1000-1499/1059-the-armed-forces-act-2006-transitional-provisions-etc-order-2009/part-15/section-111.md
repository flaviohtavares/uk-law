> Text as at 2024-04-17.

## 111. Quashing of conviction by court-martial

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 12(1)(a) of CMAA 1968 (duty to allow appeal against unsafe conviction), the reference to the Court Martial includes a court-martial.
