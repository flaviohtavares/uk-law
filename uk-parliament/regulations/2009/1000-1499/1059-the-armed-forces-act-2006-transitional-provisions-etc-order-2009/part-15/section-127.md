# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 127

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Duties of registrar with respect to appeals etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to an appeal or application for leave to appeal from a court-martial, section 50(1) of CMAA 1968 (duties of registrar with respect to appeals etc) applies without the amendment made by AFA 2006.
