# The Armed Forces Act 2006 (Transitional Provisions etc) Order 2009, section 116

> Text as at 2024-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Effect of appeal, or application for leave to appeal, against sentence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 16A(1) of CMAA 1968 (appeal, or application for leave to appeal, against one sentence treated as appeal or application in respect of other sentences) applies where a court-martial passed two or more sentences on a person on the same occasion.
