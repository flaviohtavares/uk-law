[^key-599eabef70dd7d1cc29b62759a2cf52d]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the [Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) Regulations 1997 (S.I. 1997/1331)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1331) to reflect the codification of Council Directive [78/659/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0659) and its amendments into a single text now contained in Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as no impact on business or the voluntary sector is foreseen.
