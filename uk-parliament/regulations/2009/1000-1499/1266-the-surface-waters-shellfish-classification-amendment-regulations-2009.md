[^c-64c118847d73133eadda28c67fd38220]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the [Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations 1997 (S.I. 1997/1332)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1332) to reflect the codification of Council Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923) and its amendments into a single text now contained in Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as no impact on business or the voluntary sector is foreseen.
