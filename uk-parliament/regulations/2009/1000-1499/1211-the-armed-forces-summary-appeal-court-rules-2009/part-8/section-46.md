# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 46

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Outline of respondent's case

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A judge advocate may direct the Director to serve on each appellant and the court administration officer, before any preliminary proceedings, an outline of the respondent's case.
