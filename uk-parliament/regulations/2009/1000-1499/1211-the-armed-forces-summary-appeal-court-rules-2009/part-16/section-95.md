# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 95

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Transitory provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Until paragraph 5 of Schedule 11 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 [^c19687941] comes into force, the reference in rule 41(2)(c) to the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland is to be read as a reference to the Supreme Court of Northern Ireland.
