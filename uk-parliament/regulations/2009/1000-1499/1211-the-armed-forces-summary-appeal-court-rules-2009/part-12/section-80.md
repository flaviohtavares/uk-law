> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. The case for the appellant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to rule 77(2), where an appellant gives evidence, he must do so before calling any other witness.
