# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 88

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Pronouncement of punishment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The punishment shall be recorded in writing, dated and signed by the judge advocate.

(2) The judge advocate shall pronounce punishment and make the statement of reasons, and give the explanation, required by section 252(1).

(3) In this rule “**punishment**” has the same meaning as “**sentence**” in section 252.
