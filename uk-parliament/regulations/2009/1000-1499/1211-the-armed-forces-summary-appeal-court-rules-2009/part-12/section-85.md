# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 85

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Pre-sentence report and previous convictions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the court administration officer has arranged for a pre-sentence report to be prepared in advance of the proceedings, he shall serve a copy on the Director and the appellant before the time appointed for the proceedings.

(2) Where the Director has obtained a record of the appellant's previous convictions in advance of the proceedings, he shall serve a copy on the appellant and the court administration officer before the time appointed for the proceedings.
