# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 79

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Submission of no case to answer

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At the close of the case for the respondent an appellant may submit, in respect of any charge, that the Director has failed to establish a case for him to answer.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of rule 40, the judge advocate shall hear and rule on such a submission in the absence of the lay members.

(3) If such a submission is allowed, the judge advocate shall direct the court to quash the finding.
