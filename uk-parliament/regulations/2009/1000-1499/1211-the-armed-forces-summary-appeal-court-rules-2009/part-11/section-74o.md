# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 74O

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74O. Warning to lay members[^key-4ae190d2f805d762602551f8d5f7d7ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where in proceedings with lay members evidence has been given in accordance with a special measures direction, the judge advocate must give the lay members such warning (if any) as he considers necessary to ensure that the fact that the direction was given in relation to the witness does not prejudice the appellant.
