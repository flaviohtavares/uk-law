# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 74F

> Text as at 2023-11-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74F. Witnesses eligible for assistance on grounds of fear or distress about testifying[^key-4ae190d2f805d762602551f8d5f7d7ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A witness (other than an appellant) is eligible for assistance by virtue of this rule if the judge advocate is satisfied that the quality of evidence given by the witness is likely to be diminished by reason of fear or distress on the part of the witness in connection with testifying in the proceedings.
