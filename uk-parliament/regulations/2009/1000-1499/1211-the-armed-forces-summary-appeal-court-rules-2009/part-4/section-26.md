# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 26

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Proceedings in absence of appellant etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Proceedings may be held in the absence of any person to whom they relate, if the judge advocate so directs.
