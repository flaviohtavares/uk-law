# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 21

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. The court administration officer

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The court administration officer must exercise his functions (other than that of specifying the lay members for any proceedings) subject to any direction given by a judge advocate.

(2) The court administration officer may delegate any of his functions to a member of the Military Court Service.
