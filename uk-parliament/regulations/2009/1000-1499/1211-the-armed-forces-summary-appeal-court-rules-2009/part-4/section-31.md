# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 31

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Exhibits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any exhibit admitted in evidence must be marked sequentially with either a number or a letter.

(2) Each exhibit, or a label attached to it, must be signed by or on behalf of the judge advocate.

(3) Each exhibit must be retained with the record of proceedings, unless the judge advocate otherwise directs.
