# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 40

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Judge advocate's power to direct lay members to withdraw

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In any proceedings with lay members, the judge advocate may direct the lay members to withdraw while he hears submissions or gives a ruling on any question of law, practice or procedure.
