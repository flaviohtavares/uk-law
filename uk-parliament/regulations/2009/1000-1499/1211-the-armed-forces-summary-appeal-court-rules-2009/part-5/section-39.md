# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 39

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Swearing of lay members

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At the commencement of any proceedings to which rule 37 applies, an oath shall be administered—

- (a) subject to that rule, to each of the lay members; and
- (b) to any person in attendance for instruction.
