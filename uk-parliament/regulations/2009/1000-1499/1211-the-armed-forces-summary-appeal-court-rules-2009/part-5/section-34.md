# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 34

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Proceedings without lay members

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For proceedings to which this rule applies, there shall be no lay members.

(2) This rule applies to preliminary proceedings.

(3) This rule applies to ancillary proceedings.

(4)[^key-64581b7085ffdebd2e322663cc44489f] This rule applies to variation proceedings if a direction is given under rule 88E.
