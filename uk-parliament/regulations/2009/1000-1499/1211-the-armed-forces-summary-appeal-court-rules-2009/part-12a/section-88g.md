> Text as at 2023-11-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88G. Decision on variation of punishment[^key-983f1a934bf9a85f10973b5c194f3240]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the case of an equality of votes on whether the SAC punishment should be varied, and if so how, the judge advocate has a casting vote.
