# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 88H

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88H. Announcement of varied punishment[^c-983f1a934bf9a85f10973b5c194f3240]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the court varies the SAC punishment, rule 88 (pronouncement of punishment) and sections 252 (duty to give reasons and explain sentence) and 253(2) (duties in complying with section 252) of the Act apply as they apply to pronouncement of punishment.
