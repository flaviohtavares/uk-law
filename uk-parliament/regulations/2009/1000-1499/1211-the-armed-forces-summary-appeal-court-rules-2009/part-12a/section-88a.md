# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 88A

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88A. Application of Part 12A[^key-983f1a934bf9a85f10973b5c194f3240]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies where the court has, on or after 13th November 2023, awarded a punishment in appeal proceedings or confirmed in appeal proceedings a punishment awarded by a commanding officer at summary hearing.
