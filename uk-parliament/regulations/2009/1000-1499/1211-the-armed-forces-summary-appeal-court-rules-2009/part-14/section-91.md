# The Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009, section 91

> Text as at 2023-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Withholding of matter from the public in proceedings before the court

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The court may give leave for any name or other matter given in evidence in proceedings to be withheld from the public.
