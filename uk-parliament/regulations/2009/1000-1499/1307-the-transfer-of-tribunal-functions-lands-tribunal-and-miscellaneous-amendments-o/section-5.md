# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions (Lands Tribunal and Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2009, section 5

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Consequential and transitional provisions[^key-045cca75ea4d34824863c124369c3802]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedules 1 to 3 contain amendments to legislation as a consequence of the transfers effected by this Order, by the Transfer of Tribunal Functions Order 2008[^f00003] and by the Transfer of Tribunal Functions and Revenue and Customs Appeals Order 2009[^f00004].

(2) Schedule 1 contains amendments to primary legislation.

(3)[^key-0281e872a32e80bfc48bbf97af603c4f] Schedule 2 contains amendments to secondary legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0281e872a32e80bfc48bbf97af603c4f]

(4) Schedule 3 contains amendments to Church of England measures.

(5) Schedule 4 contains repeals and revocations as a consequence of the amendments in Schedules 1 and 2.

(6) Schedule 5 contains transitional and saving provisions.
