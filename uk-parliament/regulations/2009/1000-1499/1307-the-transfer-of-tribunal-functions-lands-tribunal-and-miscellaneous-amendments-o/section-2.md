# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions (Lands Tribunal and Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2009, section 2

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Transfer of functions and abolition of the Lands Tribunal[^key-8be30a37b95ffeb3167b74920c3750f4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The functions of the Lands Tribunal are transferred to the Upper Tribunal.

(2) The Lands Tribunal is abolished.
