# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions (Lands Tribunal and Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2009, section 6

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Amendment of local and private Acts, and of Transport and Works Act Orders[^key-27d8bd68cbaa0a83de153213eaa097e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any reference to the Lands Tribunal in a local or private Act is to be read as a reference to the Upper Tribunal.

(2) Any reference to the Lands Tribunal in an order made under section 1 or 3 of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00005] is to be read as a reference to the Upper Tribunal.

(3) In paragraphs (1) and (2), a reference to the Lands Tribunal does not include a reference to the Lands Tribunal for Scotland or the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland.
