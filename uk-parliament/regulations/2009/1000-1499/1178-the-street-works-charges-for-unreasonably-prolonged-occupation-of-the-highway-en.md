# The Street Works (Charges for Unreasonably Prolonged Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 2009/303](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/303) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2009 No. 1178

Highways, England

The Street Works (Charges for Unreasonably Prolonged Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

6th May 2009

Laid before Parliament

11th May 2009

Coming into force

1st June 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 74, 97 and 104(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Street Works (Charges for Unreasonably Prolonged Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 1st June 2009.

(2) These Regulations apply to England only.

## 2. Amendment

(1) Regulation 15(5) of the Street Works (Charges for Unreasonably Prolonged Occupation of the Highway) (England) Regulations 2009[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit the words “from and including 1<sup>st</sup> April 2009”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Paul Clark

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

6th May 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Street Works (Charges for Unreasonably Prolonged Occupation of the Highway) (England) Regulations 2009 to remove the provision contained in regulation 15(5) indicating 1<sup>st</sup> April 2009 as the date from which all notices required to be given under those Regulations are to be given using electronic communications. This clarifies that the requirement had effect from the date on which those Regulations came in to force, namely the 6<sup>th</sup> April 2009.
