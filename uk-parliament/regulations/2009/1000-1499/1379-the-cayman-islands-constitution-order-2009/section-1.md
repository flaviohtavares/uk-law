# The Cayman Islands Constitution Order 2009, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Cayman Islands Constitution Order 2009.

(2) This Order shall come into force on such day as the Governor, acting in his or her discretion, shall appoint by proclamation published in a Government Notice.
