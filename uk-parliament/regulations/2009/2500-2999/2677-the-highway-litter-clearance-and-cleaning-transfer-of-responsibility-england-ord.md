# The Highway Litter Clearance and Cleaning (Transfer of Responsibility) (England) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2677

Environmental Protection

The Highway Litter Clearance and Cleaning (Transfer of Responsibility) (England) Order 2009

Made

2nd October 2009

Laid before Parliament

7th October 2009

Coming into force

30th October 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 86(11) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001].

In accordance with paragraphs (b) and (c) of that subsection of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted with the local authorities of Dartford, Elmbridge, Guildford, Havering, Hertsmere, Hillingdon, Reigate and Banstead, Runnymede, Sevenoaks, South Buckinghamshire, Spelthorne, St. Albans, Swale, Thurrock and Windsor and Maidenhead and it appears to the Secretary of State to be necessary or expedient for the following Order to be made in order to prevent or minimise interference with the passage or with the safety of traffic along the highway.

## 1. Citation application and commencement

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Highway Litter Clearance and Cleaning (Transfer of Responsibility) (England) Order 2009;
- (b) applies in England; and
- (c) comes into force on 30th October 2009.

## 2. Transfer of Responsibilities to the Secretary of State

On the date on which this Order comes into force, there shall be transferred to the Secretary of State for Transport from each local authority listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order the responsibility for the discharge of the duties imposed by section 89 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 as respects the part or parts of the highway or highways specified in relation to that local authority in column 2 of that Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 89 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 places a duty on each local authority to ensure that land comprised in the highways maintainable at the public expense within its area (other than motorways) are so far as is practicable, kept clean and clear of litter and refuse. Section 86(11) empowers the Secretary of State to transfer responsibility for the discharge of these duties as respects all or any part of such a highway from the local authority to the highway authority for that highway.

This Order transfers responsibility in respect of those parts of the trunk roads listed in the Schedule from the local authorities who would otherwise be responsible, to the Secretary of State for Transport who is the highway authority for them. The transfer becomes effective on 30th October 2009.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the costs of the private, public or voluntary sector is foreseen.

The Explanatory Memorandum for this Order is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website (www.opsi.gov.uk).
