# The A421 Trunk Road (A421/A600 Junction Westbound On Slip Road) Detrunking Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2716

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A421 Trunk Road (A421/A600 Junction Westbound On Slip Road) Detrunking Order 2009

Made

6th October 2009

Coming into force

18th December 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A421 Trunk Road (A421/A600 Junction Westbound On Slip Road) Detrunking Order 2009 and shall come into force on 18th December 2009.

**2.** In this Order—

- (a) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State;
- (b) “the trunk road” means the A421 Trunk Road.

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies Bedford Borough Council that they will become responsible for that length of road.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

G Drake

A Divisional Director in the Highways Agency

6th October 2009

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is situated between the eastern end of the slip road where it meets the roundabout joining the A600 and the A421 westbound and terminating after 130 metres on the A421, within the Borough of Bedford and shown by broad black dashes on the plan numbered HA/10/NOD/003, marked “The A421 Trunk Road (A421/A600 Junction Westbound On Slip Road) Detrunking Order 2009” signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Information Management Division, Ashdown House, Sedlescombe Road North, St Leonards On Sea, East Sussex TN37 7GA.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reclassifies a new dedicated westbound on slip road at the A421/A600 junction at Shortstown, Bedford. The new dedicated on slip road will merge with the existing on slip road at a point approximately 120 metres from the junction roundabout. Once constructed, a 130 metre section of the widened on slip road from the roundabout at the eastern end of the westbound on slip road will be detrunked and responsibility for it will pass to the local highway authority.

The dedicated westbound on slip road is proposed to enable development of land on part of the old RAF Cardington site by increasing capacity at this junction.
