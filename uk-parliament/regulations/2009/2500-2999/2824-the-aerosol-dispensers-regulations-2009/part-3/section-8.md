# The Aerosol Dispensers Regulations 2009, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Transitional provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations 4 and 5(a) do not prohibit the supply of, or possession for supply of, an aerosol dispenser which, before 29th April 2010, was marked with the reversed epsilon marking in accordance with the provisions of the Aerosol Dispensers (EEC Requirements) Regulations 1977 [^c19921921].

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply after 28th October 2010.
