# The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2573 (C. 110)

Infrastructure Planning

The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

Made

21st September 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 241(8) of the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Planning Act 2008.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2009

Paragraphs 24 to 27 of Schedule 1 to the Act, so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 1st October 2009.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Shahid Malik

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

21st September 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, on 1st October 2009, paragraphs 24 to 27 of Schedule 1 to the Planning Act 2008. The effect of this, together with section 240(6) of the Planning Act 2008, is that those paragraphs, which make amendments to various enactments so as to insert references to the Infrastructure Planning Commission, will be in force for the United Kingdom.
