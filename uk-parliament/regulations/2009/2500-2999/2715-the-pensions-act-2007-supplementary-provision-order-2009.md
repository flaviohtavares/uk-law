# The Pensions Act 2007 (Supplementary Provision) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2715

Social Security

The Pensions Act 2007 (Supplementary Provision) Order 2009

Made

7th October 2009

Laid before Parliament

12th October 2009

Coming into force

6th April 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(1)(a) of the Pensions Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2007 (Supplementary Provision) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 6th April 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999

In regulation 26 (decisions against which an appeal lies) of the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999[^f00002], at the end of paragraph (d) add—

> ; or
>
> - (e) under, or by virtue of regulations made under, section 23A (contributions credits for relevant parents and carers) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

[^f00003].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Angela Eagle

Minister of State

Department for Work and Pensions

7th October 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision supplementary to section 3 (contributions credits for relevant parents and carers) of the Pensions Act [2007 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/22), which inserts section 23A (contributions credits for relevant parents and carers) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) (“the 1992 Act”).

This Order amends the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/991)) to provide that an appeal lies to the First-tier Tribunal established under Chapter 2 (First-tier Tribunal and Upper Tribunal) of Part 1 (tribunals and inquiries) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act [2007 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15) against decisions of the Secretary of State under, or by virtue of regulations made under, section 23A of the 1992 Act.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
