# The British Nationality Act 1981 (Amendment of Schedule 6) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2744

British Nationality

The British Nationality Act 1981 (Amendment of Schedule 6) Order 2009

Made

15th October 2009

Laid before Parliament

22nd October 2009

Coming into force

14th November 2009

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of October 2009

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue of the powers conferred upon Her by section 50(13) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is ordered, as follows—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the British Nationality Act 1981 (Amendment of Schedule 6) Order 2009.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 14th November 2009.

## 2. Amendment of Schedule 6 to British Nationality Act 1981

Schedule 6 to the British Nationality Act 1981 is amended by substituting the words “St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha” for the words “St Helena and Dependencies”.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the list of British overseas territories in Schedule 6 to the British Nationality Act 1981 because the St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha Constitution Order 2009 changes the name of the territory of St Helena and Dependencies to St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
