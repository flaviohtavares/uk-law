# The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 3323 (C. 150)

Identity Cards

The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2009

Made

15th December 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 44(3) and (4) of the Identity Cards Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement

(1) Section 2(1) and (2), and section 5(1)(b), of the Identity Cards Act 2006 shall come into force on 4<sup>th</sup> January 2010 in relation to any person to whom paragraph (2) applies, except to the extent that those provisions are already in force in respect of such persons.

(2) This paragraph applies to a British citizen who –

- (a) holds, or has at any time on or after 1<sup>st</sup> January 2009 held, a United Kingdom passport (within the meaning of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00002]) which provides evidence of his British citizenship; and
- (b) either –
  - (i) the address of his place of residence is located in an area in England to which one of the postcodes listed in the Schedule corresponds; or
  - (ii) is employed and his place of work is permanently or temporarily at premises located in an area in England to which one of the postcodes listed in the Schedule corresponds.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 2(1) and (2), and section 5(1)(b), of the Identity Cards Act 2006 on 4<sup>th</sup> January 2010 in relation to any person to whom paragraph (2) of article 2 applies, except to the extent that those provisions are already in force in respect of such persons by virtue of commencement orders made before the date of this Order. These provisions relate to entries being made in the National Identity Register for persons who are entitled to be registered in that Register and who apply for an entry to be made, and how such an application may be made.

Article 2(2) applies to a British citizen who holds, or who has held at any time on or after 1<sup>st</sup> January 2009, a United Kingdom passport which shows he has such citizenship, and either the address of his place of residence is located in an area in England to which one of the postcodes listed in the Schedule corresponds, or is employed and his place of work is permanently or temporarily at premises located in an area in England to which one of the postcodes listed in the Schedule corresponds.
