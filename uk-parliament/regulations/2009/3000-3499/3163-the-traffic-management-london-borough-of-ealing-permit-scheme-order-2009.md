# The Traffic Management (London Borough of Ealing) Permit Scheme Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 3163

Highways, England

The Traffic Management (London Borough of Ealing) Permit Scheme Order 2009

Made

30th November 2009

Coming into force

11th January 2010

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Traffic Management (London Borough of Ealing) Permit Scheme Order 2009 and is to come into effect on 11th January 2010.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order in terms commonly known as the “London Permit Scheme” which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by the London Borough of Ealing and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and
- “specified streets” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

## 3. Commencement of Permit Scheme

The London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme comes into effect on 11th January 2010.

## 4. Application of Part 8 of The Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

C. D. Mole

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme.

The London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Ealing by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from London Borough of Ealing before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 11th January 2010. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme.

A copy of the London Borough of Ealing Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at the offices of the London Borough of Ealing, Perceval House, 14-16 Uxbridge Road, London W5 2HL and is also available to inspect on the London Councils’ website www.londoncouncils.gov.uk.
