[^key-2359721f07082c0cc56e71ee1c8a6c30]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1122/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1122), which establishes cross-compliance requirements under Council Regulation [(EC) No 73/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073). They set out the requirements for claimants of subsidy under the single payment scheme. The requirements are set out in Schedule 1, and Schedule 2 lists exceptions from those requirements.

They revoke and replace [S. I 2009/3264](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/3264), where the period for casting up hedgebanks was misstated.

A full impact assessment has been prepared and placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament. It is available on the Defra website at www.defra.gov.uk.
