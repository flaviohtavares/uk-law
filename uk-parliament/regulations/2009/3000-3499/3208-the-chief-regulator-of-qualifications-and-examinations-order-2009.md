# The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 3208

Education, England

Education, Northern Ireland

The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2009

Made

9th December 2009

Coming into force

1st April 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of December 2009

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 9 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2009 and comes into force on 1st April 2010.

**2.** Kathleen Tattersall is appointed to chair the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Kathleen Tattersall to chair the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation, which is established under section 127 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009. In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 9 to that Act, the person appointed to that position is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations.
