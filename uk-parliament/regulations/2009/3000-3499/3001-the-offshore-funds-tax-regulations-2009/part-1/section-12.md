# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. General interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**HMRC**” means Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- “**period of account**”, in relation to an offshore fund, means any period for which accounts of the offshore fund are drawn up;
- “**proposed prospectus**” includes—
  - (a) any document supplementing or amending the proposed prospectus, and
  - (b) any document fulfilling the same function as a proposed prospectus;
- “**prospectus**” includes—
  - (a) any document supplementing or amending the prospectus, and
  - (b) any document fulfilling the same function as a prospectus;
- “regulated market” means—[^key-6979eb5cd06e3d9b19387d181b1c4ec5]
  - (a) a UK regulated market within the meaning given by Article 2.1(13A) of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments,
  - (b) an EU regulated market within the meaning given by Article 2.1(13B) of that Regulation, and
  - (c) a Gibraltar regulated market within the meaning given by Article 26(11)(b)(i) of that Regulation;[^key-254c2813e1e22f45ef19bfb7af09e253]
- the “**relevant group of sections**” means sections 40A to 42A of FA 2008 [^c19951971];
- “tax year”—
  - (a) in relation to income tax, has the meaning given by section 4(2) of ITA 2007, and
  - (b) in relation to capital gains tax, has the meaning given by section 288(1ZA) of TCGA 1992 [^c19951981];
- “**tribunal**” means the First-tier Tribunal or, where determined by or under Tribunal Procedure Rules, the Upper Tribunal;
- “UCITS fund” means—[^key-c0516e154b35c7b8be88e8be23b686aa]
  - (a) a UCITS within the meaning given by section 236A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, or
  - (b) an undertaking established in Gibraltar which is a UCITS under the law of Gibraltar which implemented [Directive 2009/65/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0065) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on the coordination of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to undertakings for collective investment in transferable securities.
