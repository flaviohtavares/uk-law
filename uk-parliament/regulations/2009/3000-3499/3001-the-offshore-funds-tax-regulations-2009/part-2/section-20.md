## 20. Application to gains of non-resident settlements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an offshore income gain arises to a settlement in a tax year and the trustees of the settlement are not[^c-8c94b3613684be6f6afd7b9a67b2c3d3] resident in the United Kingdom in the tax year, the gain is not regarded as income for the purposes of Chapter 5 of Part 5 of ITTOIA 2005 (settlements: amounts treated as income of settlor).
