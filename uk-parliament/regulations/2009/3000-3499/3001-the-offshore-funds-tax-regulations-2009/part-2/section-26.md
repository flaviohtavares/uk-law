# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 26

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Trading stock etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to tax arises under regulation 17 if condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that the interest in the fund is held as trading stock.

(3) Condition B is that the disposal of the interest is taken into account in computing the profits of a trade.
