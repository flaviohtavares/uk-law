# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 27

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Long-term insurance funds of insurance companies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to tax arises under regulation 17 in respect of disposals of assets of an insurance company's long-term insurance fund.

(2) In paragraph (1) “**insurance company**” and “**long-term insurance fund**” have the same meaning as in section 431(2) of ICTA [^c19952121].

## Annotations

[^c19952121]: Marginal citation: In section 431(2), the definition of “insurance company” was substituted by [S.I. 2001/3629](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3629) and amended by [S.I. 2006/3270](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3270). As regards the expression “long-term insurance fund”, a definition of “long term business fund” was inserted by paragraph 1(2) of the [Finance Act 1990 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/29) and amended by Part 5(26) of Schedule 41 to the Finance Act 1996. The definition was re-labelled as a definition of “long-term insurance fund” and further amended by [S.I. 2001/3629](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3629).
