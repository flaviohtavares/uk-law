## 21. Application of transfer of assets abroad provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Chapter 2 of Part 13 of ITA 2007 (transfer of assets abroad) applies in relation to an offshore income gain arising to a person resident or domiciled outside the United Kingdom as if the offshore income gain were income becoming payable to the person.

(2) Income treated as arising under that Chapter by virtue of paragraph (1) is regarded as “**foreign**” for the purposes of section 726, 730 or 735 [^c19952071] of that Act.

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to an offshore income gain if (and to the extent that) it is treated, by virtue of regulation 24, as arising to a person resident [^key-c73fec476946227130a97781d41bc8e6]... in the United Kingdom.
