# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 126

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Amendment of ICTA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) ICTA is amended as follows.

(2) In section 396(2) [^c19952431] (Case VI losses) for “section 761(1)(b)(ii)” substitute “ regulation 18(4) of the Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009 (S.I. 2009/3001) ”.

(3) In section 505(3)(b)(iii) [^c19952441] (charitable companies) for “section 761(6) below” substitute “ regulation 31 of the Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009 (S.I. 2009/3001) ”.

(4) In section 587B(9) [^c19952451] (gifts of shares, securities and real property to charities etc.), in the definition of “offshore fund”, for “Chapter 5 of Part 17” substitute “ section 40A of the Finance Act 2008 ”.

(5) In section 834A [^c19952461] (miscellaneous charges relating to the former Case VI of Schedule D)—

- (a) in Part 1 of the Table omit the entry relating to section 761(1)(b)(ii), and
- (b) in Part 3 of the Table insert at the end—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Regulation 18(4) of the Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009 (S.I. 2009/3001) | Offshore income gains |

(6) In section 842(3A) [^c19952471] (meaning of investment trust) for “section 761(1)(a)” substitute “ regulation 18(1) of the Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009 (S.I. 2009/3001) ”.
