# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 62

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Duty to provide computation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter explains how reportable income is computed.

(2) A reporting fund must provide a computation of its reportable income for a period of account.
