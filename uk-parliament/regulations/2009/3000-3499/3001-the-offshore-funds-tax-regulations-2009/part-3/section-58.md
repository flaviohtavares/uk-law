# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 58

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. General duties of reporting funds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A reporting fund must—

- (a) prepare accounts in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 4 (except in the case of transparent reporting funds)[^c-1b198b8f04c9403640a6f3afd0c74e79];
- (b) provide a computation of its reportable income in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 5 or 6A, as the case may be[^c-588006b088cc0deab089e8ebf691e955];
- (c) provide reports to participants in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 7; and
- (d) provide information to HMRC in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 9.
