# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 115

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Appeal against exclusion from the reporting fund regime

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If HMRC give notice to a fund under regulation 114(3) (an “exclusion notice”), the fund may appeal.

(2) The notice of appeal must be given to HMRC within a period of 42 days beginning with the day on which the exclusion notice is given.

(3) On an appeal, the tribunal may uphold or quash the exclusion notice.
