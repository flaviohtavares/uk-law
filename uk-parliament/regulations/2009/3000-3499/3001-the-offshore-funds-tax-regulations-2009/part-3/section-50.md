# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 50

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Meaning of “reporting fund”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations a “**reporting fund**” means an offshore fund to which this Part applies for a period of account.
