# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 123

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Modified application of Chapter 10

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For regulations 108 to 115 substitute—

> **108A. Consequences of rise in net asset value of fund**
>
> (1) This regulation applies if—
>
> - (a) this Part applies to a constant NAV fund,
> - (b) the net asset value of the fund (expressed in the currency in which units are issued) has risen by more than an insignificant amount, and
> - (c) the fund has not notified HMRC that it has ceased to be a constant NAV fund.
>
> (2) But this regulation does not apply if the net asset value of a constant NAV fund (expressed in the currency in which units are issued) has fallen by more than an insignificant amount.
>
> (3) A participant who disposes of an interest in the fund and who makes a chargeable gain on the disposal is treated as making an offshore income gain.
