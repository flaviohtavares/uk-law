# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 80

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Treatment of investment transactions carried out by diversely owned funds[^c-457384c1eababd565af04e8bd3fd3bd8][^c-bb7f668087ea270faf88d0b474199c55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if a diversely owned fund carries out an investment transaction in an accounting period.

(2) The investment transaction is treated as a non-trading transaction.

(3)[^c-cc3d56ba20d583803910d8ab186d278a] For the purposes of these Regulations an “investment transaction” is an investment transaction specified by regulation 2 of the Investment Transactions (Tax) Regulations 2014.
