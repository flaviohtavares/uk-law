# The Offshore Funds (Tax) Regulations 2009, section 59

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Accounts to be prepared in accordance with acceptable accounting policy

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A reporting fund must prepare accounts—

- (a) in accordance with international accounting standards, or
- (b) in accordance with the generally accepted accounting practice specified in the application.
