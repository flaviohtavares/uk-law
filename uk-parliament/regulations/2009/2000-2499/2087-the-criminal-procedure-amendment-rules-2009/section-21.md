# The Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 21

> Text as at 2009-10-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**21.**[^c-15232c1c65bd13a81352df33735d0b57] After rule 59.5 (Application for discharge of a restraint order by the person who applied for the order), insert—

> **59.6. Application to punish for contempt of court**
>
> (1) This rule applies where a person is accused of disobeying a restraint order.
>
> (2) An applicant who wants the Crown Court to exercise its power to punish that person for contempt of court must comply with the rules in Part 62 (Contempt of court).
>
> *\[Note. The Crown Court has inherent power to punish for contempt of court a person who disobeys its order: see section 45 of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00008].\]*

.
