# The Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 22

> Text as at 2009-10-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**22.**[^key-46d34f5d53b925f663c54d5d56b9738f] In Part 61 (Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 150 150 rules applicable to restraint and receivership proceedings)—

- (a) in rule 61.19 (Order for costs)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1), omit “under rule 78.1”,
  - (ii) in paragraph (5), omit “under rule 78.1”;
- (b) at the end of the note after rule 61.19, insert “See section 52 of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00009].”;
- (c) in rules 61.20 and 61.21, for “taxing” wherever it occurs, substitute “assessing”;
- (d) in rule 61.20(1)(b), for “rule 78.3”, substitute “rule 76.11”; and
- (e) in rule 61.21(1)(b), for “rule 78.3”, substitute “rule 76.11”.
