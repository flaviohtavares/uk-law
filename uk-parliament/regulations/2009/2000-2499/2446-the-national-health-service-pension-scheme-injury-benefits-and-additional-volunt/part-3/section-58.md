# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2009, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Amendment of regulation 3.A.13

After paragraph (4) of regulation 3.A.13 (meaning of pensionable earnings in relation to other practitioners), insert—

> (5) The pensionable earnings of a type 2 practitioner or a locum practitioner include the amount of any pensionable earnings the practitioner is entitled to count under Chapter 3.K.

.
