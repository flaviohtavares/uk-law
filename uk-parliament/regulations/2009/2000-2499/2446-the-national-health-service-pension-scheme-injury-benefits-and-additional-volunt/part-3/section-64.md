# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2009, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Amendment of regulation 3.C.12

(1) Regulation 3.C.12 (effect of payment of additional contributions under this Chapter) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3), after—

- (a) “Chapter 3.D” insert “or Chapter 3.K”;
- (b) “3.D.5” insert “and 3.K.17”.

(3) After paragraph (10), insert—

> (11) In the case of a 2008 Section Optant, this regulation is subject to regulation 3.K.4.
