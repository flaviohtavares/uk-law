## 59. Amendment of regulation 3.B.1

(1) Regulation 3.B.1 (eligibility: general) shall be amended as follows.

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “and meets any one of the other Scheme conditions (see paragraph (5))”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (b), insert (as full out words) “and meets any one of the other Scheme conditions (see paragraph (5)).”.
