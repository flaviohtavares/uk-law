## 55. Amendment of regulation 3.A.5

In paragraph (1) of regulation 3.A.5 (meaning of “qualifying service”)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (c), omit “, and”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (e), insert—

  > ; and
  >
  > - (f) in the case of a 2008 Section Optant, any period of qualifying service the member is entitled to count under Chapter 3.K.
