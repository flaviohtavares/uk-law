# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2009, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Amendment of regulation 3.E.10

In regulation 3.E.10 (amount of children’s pension under regulation 3.E.8: deceased active members and deceased non-contributing members), at the end of paragraph (3) insert (as full out words) “In the case of a 2008 Section Optant, this is subject to regulation 3.K.23.”.
