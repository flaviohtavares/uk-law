# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2009, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Amendment of regulation 3.A.7

After paragraph (9) of regulation 3.A.7 (meaning of “pensionable earnings”), insert—

> (10) The pensionable earnings of a type 1 practitioner include the amount of any pensionable earnings the practitioner is entitled to count under Chapter 3.K.
