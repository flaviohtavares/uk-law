# The National Health Service Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions (Amendment) Regulations 2009, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Amendment of the National Health Service (Injury Benefits) Regulations 1995

(1) The National Health Service (Injury Benefits) Regulations 1995[^f00011] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “NHS employment” after “1995” insert “and in the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008”.
