# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2362

Road Traffic

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

Made

31st August 2009

Laid before Parliament

3rd September 2009

Coming into force

28th September 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 89(3)[^f00001] of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Transport has consulted with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of the 1988 Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 28th September 2009.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

In regulation 40B (content of the tests) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999[^f00003] in paragraph (1)(a) omit “consisting of 50 questions, the questions being in either multiple choice or multiple response form and”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Clark

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport

31st August 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999 in relation to the content of the driving theory test for cars, motorcycles and mopeds by removing the requirement for that test to consist of 50 questions in multiple choice or multiple response form.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
