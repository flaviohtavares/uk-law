# The Infrastructure Planning (Model Provisions) (England and Wales) Order 2009, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Model provisions

The provisions set out in the Schedules to this Order, of which those contained in Schedules 1 and 4 are of general application and those contained in Schedules 2 and 3 are of application to development relating to railways and harbours, respectively, are prescribed as model provisions for the purposes of section 38 of the Planning Act 2008.
