# The Northern Ireland Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2466 (C. 102)

Charities, Northern Ireland

Judicial Appointments And Removals, Northern Ireland

Justice, Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009

Made

8th September 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(7) of the Northern Ireland Act 2009 [^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009.

**2.** The following provisions of the Northern Ireland Act 2009 shall come into force on 26th September 2009—

- (a) section 2(3) (judicial appointments and removals) so far as it relates to the provisions specified in paragraph (b), and
- (b) in Schedule 4, paragraph 35(1) and (3) (transfer of appointment and related functions) in so far as they relate to the President or other member of the Charity Tribunal for Northern Ireland.

Shaun Woodward

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

8th September 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 26th September 2009 the provisions of the Northern Ireland Act 2009 listed in article 2.
