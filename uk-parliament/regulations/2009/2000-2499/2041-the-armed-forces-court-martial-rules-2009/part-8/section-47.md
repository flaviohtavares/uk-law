# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 47

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Preliminary proceedings in chambers without notice to defendant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On application by the Director, the judge advocate for any preliminary proceedings may direct that the proceedings are, or that any part of the proceedings is—

- (a) where there is one defendant, to be held in his absence and without notice to him;
- (b) where there are two or more defendants, to be held in the absence of both or all of them and without notice to any of them.
