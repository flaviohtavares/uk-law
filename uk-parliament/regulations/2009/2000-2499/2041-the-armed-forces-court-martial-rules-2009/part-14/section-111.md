# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 111

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Application and interpretation of Part 14

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies in relation to any sentencing proceedings.

(2) In this Part—

- “**the offender**” means any offender who falls to be sentenced in the proceedings; and
- “**the offence**” means any offence for which the offender falls to be so sentenced.
