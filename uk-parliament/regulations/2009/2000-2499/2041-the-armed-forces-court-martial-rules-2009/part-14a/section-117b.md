# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 117B

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117B. Review of sentence[^key-95d0d03183061a38114703e74c691439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, on a single occasion, the court passes two or more sentences on a person, an application for review of any of those sentences is to be treated as an application in respect of both or all of them.
