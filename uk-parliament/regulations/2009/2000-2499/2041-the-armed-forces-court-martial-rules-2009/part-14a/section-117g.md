# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 117G

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117G. Effect of review of sentence[^key-95d0d03183061a38114703e74c691439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unless the court otherwise directs, any sentence substituted by the court under sections 304D(6)(b) or 304E(4) begins to run from the time that the original sentence began to run.
