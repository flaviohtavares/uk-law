# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 117F

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117F. Pronouncement of sentence[^key-95d0d03183061a38114703e74c691439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to section 304F (sections 304C to 304E: statements in open court), rule 117 (pronouncement of sentence) and sections 252 (duty to give reasons and explain sentence) and 253(2) (duties in complying with section 252) apply in relation to the court’s decision as to whether or not to review the sentence as they apply to the passing of a sentence.
