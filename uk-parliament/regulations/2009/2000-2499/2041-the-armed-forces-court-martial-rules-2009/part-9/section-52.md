# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 52

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Severance of charges

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a defendant is charged with more than one offence in the same charge sheet, a judge advocate may direct, before the commencement of trial proceedings in relation to the charge sheet, that the charges be divided between two or more charge sheets.
