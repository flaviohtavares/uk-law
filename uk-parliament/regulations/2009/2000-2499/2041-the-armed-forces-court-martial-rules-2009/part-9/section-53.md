# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 53

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Severance of defendants

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where two or more defendants are charged in a single charge, a judge advocate may direct, before the commencement of trial proceedings in relation to the charge, that the charge be replaced with charges against the defendants separately and in separate charge sheets.
