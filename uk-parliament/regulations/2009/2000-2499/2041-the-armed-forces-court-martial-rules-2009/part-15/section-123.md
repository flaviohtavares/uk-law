# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 123

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Announcement of sentence as varied

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the court varies the sentence, sections 252 and 253(2) (duty to give reasons and explain sentence) and rule 117 (announcement of sentence) shall apply as they apply to the passing of a sentence.
