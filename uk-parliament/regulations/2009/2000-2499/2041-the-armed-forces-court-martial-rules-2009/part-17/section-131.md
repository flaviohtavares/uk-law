# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 131

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Offence committed during operational period of suspended sentence of imprisonment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where the court administration officer is notified of the facts mentioned in paragraph 19(1)(a) and (b) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code, as modified by paragraph 12[^key-56bc55e59e0720decec215174dd18729] of Schedule 7 to the 2006 Act.

(2) Where this rule applies, in this Part—

- “**the relevant facts**” means the facts referred to in paragraph (1) above[^key-9dcd137648df361547c0d141a3da910a];
- “**the original sentence**” means the suspended sentence order mentioned in paragraph 19(1)(a) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code, as modified by paragraph 12 of Schedule 7 to the 2006 Act, or[^key-2c1413f1c8b055f2a0c8b263d74ae042] the suspended sentence mentioned in paragraph 12(2A) of Schedule 12 to the 2003 Act, as substituted by paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 7 to the 2006 Act;
- “**the relevant period**” means the operational period of that sentence;
- “**the new offence**” means the offence mentioned paragraph 19(1)(a) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code, as modified by paragraph 12 of Schedule 7 to the 2006 Act[^key-6ffcee1e5900bca5548a0d73cb173be6];
- “**summons**” means a summons under paragraph 19(2)(a) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code, as so modified[^key-8bc3c06ecd071400da0a4d12b3b49590];
- “**warrant**” means a warrant under paragraph 19(2)(b) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code, as so modified[^key-8933be61d7f1f740a6c0972b42bfd08c];
- “**relevant order**” means an order under paragraph 13(1) of Schedule 16 to the Sentencing Code[^key-9dc94a1818781cdad613fbf1c9976311] in respect of the original sentence.
