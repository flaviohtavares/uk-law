# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 108

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Summing up

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c-0ada9562d2bf10f61a005a9b9dda40e7] The judge advocate must give the lay members directions about the relevant law at any time at which to do so will assist the lay members to evaluate the evidence.

(1) After any closing addresses, the judge advocate shall summarise for the lay members, to such extent as is necessary, the evidence relevant to the issues they must decide[^c-221f30b97214c07c88f9cdfd84b5c127].

(2) The judge advocate may direct the lay members that if they record a finding of guilty on one charge they need not record a finding on another charge.
