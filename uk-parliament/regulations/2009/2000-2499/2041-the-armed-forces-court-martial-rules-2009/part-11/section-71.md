# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 71

> Text as at 2025-02-20.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Order for inspection of banker's books

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The powers conferred by section 7 of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879 [^c19793481] (orders for the inspection of bankers' books) may be exercised by a judge advocate, in relation to any proceedings of the court, on the application of any party to the proceedings.
