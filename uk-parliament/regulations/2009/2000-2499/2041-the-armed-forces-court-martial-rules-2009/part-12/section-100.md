# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 100

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Warning to lay members

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where in proceedings with lay members evidence has been given in accordance with a special measures direction, the judge advocate must give the lay members such warning (if any) as he considers necessary to ensure that the fact that the direction was given in relation to the witness does not prejudice any defendant.
