# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 167

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Supreme Court of Northern Ireland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Until paragraph 5 of Schedule 11 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 [^c19793591] comes into force, the reference in rule 39(2)(c) to the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland is to be read as a reference to the Supreme Court of Northern Ireland.
