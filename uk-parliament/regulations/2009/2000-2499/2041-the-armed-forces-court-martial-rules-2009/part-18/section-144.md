# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 144

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Extension of unpaid work requirement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application under paragraph 21 of Schedule 10 to the Sentencing Code[^c-ce43eec87ed10f05c6c956aa5e5c0365] must be made in writing to the court administration officer, specifying—

- (a) the period for which the applicant wants the court to extend the period of twelve months specified in paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 9 to that Code[^c-ab3c2d3ea34c68704c12c4a5ed0f8293]; and
- (b) the grounds on which the application is made.

(2) The court administration officer shall forward the application to the Judge Advocate General.

(3) The Judge Advocate General may—

- (a) grant the application;
- (b) extend the period specified in paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 9 to the Sentencing Code[^c-2916b6e6b14099ec5a11ede10c4a0fe9] by a period shorter than that proposed in the application;
- (c) dismiss the application; or
- (d) direct a hearing of the application.

(4) If the Judge Advocate General directs a hearing of the application, the court administration officer shall notify the responsible officer and the offender of the time and place appointed for the hearing.
