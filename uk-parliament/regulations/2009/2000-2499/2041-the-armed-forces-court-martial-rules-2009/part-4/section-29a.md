# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 29A

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29A. Disapplication of rule 29[^key-b3b5a4cc1007d83f6a65549a907820bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rule 29 does not apply to—

- (a) sentencing proceedings where the sole defendant has entered a guilty plea before the commencement of trial proceedings;
- (b) sentencing proceedings where there are two or more co-defendants, and each co-defendant has entered a guilty plea before the commencement of trial proceedings; [^key-6cbd586440781055d5953d08cba5f3c3]...
- (c) variation proceedings where paragraph (a) or (b) applied to the sentencing proceedings in which the sentence which falls to be varied was imposed ; and[^key-2be949eb4659edba955c855bc6e94528]
- (d) review of sentence proceedings where paragraph (a) or (b) applied to the sentencing proceedings in which the sentence which falls to be reviewed was imposed.
