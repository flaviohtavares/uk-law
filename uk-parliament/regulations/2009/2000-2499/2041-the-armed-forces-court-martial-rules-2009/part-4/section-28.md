# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 28

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Powers that may be exercised by a judge advocate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any power of the court may be exercised by a judge advocate, except—

- (a) the power to try a defendant or appellant; and
- (b) the power—
  - (i) to sentence an offender,
  - (ii) to vary a sentence, [^key-edba7ccab2ad570c6c7f4367d4bcfac0]...
  - (iii) to make an activation order, or[^key-0a51b96552f469c91c0c21b5f4dbbfe2]
  - (iv) to review the sentence of an offender under section 304D or 304E,

  otherwise than in proceedings to which rule 27 applies.
