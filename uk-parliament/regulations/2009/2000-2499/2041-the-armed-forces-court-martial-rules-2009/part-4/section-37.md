# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 37

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Swearing of lay members

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At the commencement of any proceedings to which rule 35 applies, an oath shall be administered—

- (a) subject to that rule, to each of the lay members; and
- (b) to any person in attendance for instruction.
