# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 29

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Proceedings requiring six lay members[^key-2a65dd89c45eaabe072e7d03ca44c134]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For proceedings to which this rule applies, the number of lay members shall be six[^key-86650707196ea14e3580e7a29cda6398].

(2) This rule applies to trial proceedings if—

- (a) any defendant is charged with an offence listed in Schedule 2 to the 2006 Act;
- (b) any defendant is aged 18 or over at the commencement of the proceedings and is charged with an offence for which he could, if convicted, be sentenced to more than two[^key-ffcab41bcfc3af4ca19bdac76d3a7da6] years' imprisonment; or
- (c) any defendant is aged under 18 at the commencement of the proceedings and is charged with an offence for which he could, if convicted while under the age of 18, be sentenced to more than two[^key-f9766e7077ca79d84d929f94d7b7511a] years' detention under section 209.

(3) Subject to rule 29A, this rule applies to sentencing proceedings[^key-b77164d0649e23739707e7fd01e88346] if any offender falls to be sentenced for—

- (a) an offence listed in Schedule 2 to the 2006 Act; or
- (b) an offence for which he could be sentenced to more than two[^key-ff3ba55f6f5f2406dfce0c22d0311ca5] years' imprisonment or more than two years' detention under section 209.

(4) Subject to rule 29A, this rule applies to variation proceedings[^key-08e6341ff7b117d3a51cdf8a41eeab87] if it applied to the proceedings in which the sentence that falls to be varied was imposed.

(5)[^key-f41e4b87b14919c7262762ab7b6b1a0b] Subject to rule 29A, this rule applies to review of sentence proceedings if it applied to the proceedings in which the sentence that falls to be reviewed was imposed.
