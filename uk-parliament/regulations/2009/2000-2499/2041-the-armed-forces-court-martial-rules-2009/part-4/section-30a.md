# The Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009, section 30A

> Text as at 2025-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30A. Reduction in lay members[^c-e844739df70a7c712667624561851ce6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A judge advocate may direct that the Court Martial is to remain validly constituted despite the reduction of lay members from six to five, or where a direction has been made under section 155(2A), from four to three if—

- (a) the number of lay members reduces in the course of the proceedings after each of the lay members has been sworn; and
- (b) the judge advocate considers it in the interests of justice that the proceedings continue.
