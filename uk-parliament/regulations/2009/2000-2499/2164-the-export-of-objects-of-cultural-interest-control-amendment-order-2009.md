# The Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2164

Customs And Excise

The Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

5th August 2009

Laid before Parliament

6th August 2009

Coming into force

28th August 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) (Amendment) Order 2009 and comes into force on 28th August 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) Order 2003

For the definition of “the Regulation” in article 1(2) of the Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) Order 2003[^f00002]substitute ““**the Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 116/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0116) of 18 December 2008 on the export of cultural goods[^f00003].”.

Gerry Sutcliffe

Minister of State

Department for Culture Media and Sport

5th August 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the definition of “the Regulation” in article 1(2) of the Export of Objects of Cultural Interest (Control) Order 2003 ([S.I. 2003/2759](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2759)) by deleting the reference to [Council Regulation (EEC) No. 3911/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/3911) (“the 1992 Regulation”) and substituting a reference to Council Regulation [(EC) No 116/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0116) (“**the codified Regulation**”). The codified Regulation does not make any substantive change; it consolidates the provisions of the 1992 Regulation and the amendments made to the 1992 Regulation by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2469/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2469); Council Regulation [(EC) No 947/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0947) and Council Regulation [(EC) No 806/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0806).
