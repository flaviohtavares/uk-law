# The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No.2494 (L.27)

Land Charges, England

The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 2009

Made

4th September 2009

Laid before Parliament

16th September 2009

Coming into force

1st January 2010

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred by section 14 of the Local Land Charges Act 1975[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 2009 and shall come into force on 1st January 2010.

## 2. Amendment to the Local Land Charges Rules 1977

The Local Land Charges Rules 1977[^f00002] are amended in accordance with rule 3.

**3.** In Schedule 3 (fees), in item 5 (personal search fee in respect of one parcel of land), in the second column, for “11.00” substitute “22.00”.

Signed on the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

28th August 2009

We concur,

Dave Watts

Bob Blizzard

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th September 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Local Land Charges Rules 1977 ([SI 1977/985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/985)) by substituting in Schedule 3 (fees) an increased fee of £22.00 for a personal search in the whole or in part of the register in respect of one parcel of land. The increased fee becomes payable from 1st January 2010.
