# The Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2079

Competition

Consumer Protection

The Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

21st July 2009

Laid before Parliament

28th July 2009

Coming into force

1st October 2009

The Secretary of State is satisfied that the Scottish Association of Citizens Advice Bureaux represents the interests of consumers and satisfies the other criteria published by the Secretary of State pursuant to section 11(6) of the Enterprise Act 2002[^f00001]. In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 of the Enterprise Act 2002, the Secretary of State makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) (Amendment) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 1st October 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) Order 2004

For the list in the Schedule to the Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) Order 2004[^f00002] there is substituted—

> The Campaign for Real Ale Limited.
>
> The Consumer Council for Water.
>
> The Consumers’ Association.
>
> The General Consumer Council for Northern Ireland.
>
> The National Association of Citizens Advice Bureaux.
>
> The National Consumer Council.
>
> The Scottish Association of Citizens Advice Bureaux.

.

Kevin Brennan

Minister for Further Education, Skills, Apprenticeships and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

21st July 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State may designate any body that appears to him to represent the interests of consumers and satisfies the other criteria published by him as a designated consumer body for the purpose of submitting a “super-complaint” under section 11 of the Enterprise Act 2002.

This Order amends the Enterprise Act 2002 (Bodies Designated to make Super-complaints) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1517)) by adding the Scottish Association of Citizens Advice Bureaux to the list of designated consumer bodies.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
