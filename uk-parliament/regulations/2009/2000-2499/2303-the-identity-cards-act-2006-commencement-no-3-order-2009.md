# The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2303 C. 100

Identity Cards

The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

Made

25th August 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 44(3) of the Identity Cards Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Identity Cards Act 2006 shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> October 2009 –

- (a) Section 22 (Appointment of National Identity Scheme Commissioner);
- (b) Section 23 (Reports by Commissioner);
- (c) Section 24 (Jurisdiction of Intelligence Services Commissioner and Tribunal); and
- (d) Section 42 (General interpretation) so far as necessary for the interpretation of the provisions specified above.

Brett

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

25th August 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1<sup>st</sup> October 2009 the provisions of the Identity Cards Act 2006 listed in article 2.
