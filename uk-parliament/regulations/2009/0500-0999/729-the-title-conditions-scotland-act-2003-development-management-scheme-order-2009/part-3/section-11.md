# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Development Management Scheme) Order 2009, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Further provision as respects deeds of variation

(1) Where a deed of variation is granted no grantee is required.

(2) A deed of variation may be registered by an owner of a burdened unit or by a granter.
