# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Development Management Scheme) Order 2009, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Development Management Scheme) Order 2009 and comes into force on 1st June 2009.

(2) This Order extends only to Scotland.
