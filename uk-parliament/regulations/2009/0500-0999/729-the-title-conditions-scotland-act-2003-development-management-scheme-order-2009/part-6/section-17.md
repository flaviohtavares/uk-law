# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Development Management Scheme) Order 2009, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Continued application of the rules following disapplication of the Scheme

Rules 6 and 17 and any other rule of the Development Management Scheme in so far as applicable as respects the winding up of the association, shall continue to have effect notwithstanding the disapplication of the Scheme to the development.
