# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Rules made be cited as the Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009 and come into force on 6th April 2009.

**2.** In these Rules—

- “the principal Rules” mean the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00002] (any reference to a numbered Part or Rule is a reference to a Part or Rule so numbered in the principal Rules unless the context otherwise requires);
- “the Act” means the Insolvency Act 1986 (any reference to a numbered section being a reference to a section of that Act).
