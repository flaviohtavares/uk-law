# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Amendment to Rule 6.3

In paragraph (3) of Rule 6.3, for “the demand may be advertised in one or more newspapers” substitute “the creditor may advertise the demand in such manner as the creditor thinks fit”.
