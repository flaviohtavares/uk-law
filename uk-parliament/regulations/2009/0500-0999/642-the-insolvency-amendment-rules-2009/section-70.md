# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Amendment to Rule 10.4

In Rule 10.4(2), after “paragraph (1)” insert “in connection with insolvency proceedings other than proceedings relating to debt relief orders or applications for debt relief orders”.
