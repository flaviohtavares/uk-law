# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Amendment to Rule 6.172

In Rule 6.172—

- (a) for paragraph (4) substitute—

  > (4) Where the official receiver thinks fit, a notice of the order shall be gazetted not less than 14 days before the day fixed for the hearing.

  ; and
- (b) after paragraph (4) add—

  > (5) The official receiver may advertise the notice under paragraph (4) of this Rule in such other manner as the official receiver thinks fit.

  .
