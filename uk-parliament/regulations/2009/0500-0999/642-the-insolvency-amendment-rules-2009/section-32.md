# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 2009, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Insertion of new Part 5A - debt relief orders

In the Second Group of Parts (individual insolvency: bankruptcy), immediately after Part 5 (individual voluntary arrangements), insert as Part 5A the Part set out in Schedule 1 to these Rules.
