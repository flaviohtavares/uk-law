# The Family Procedure (Adoption) (Amendment) Rules 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 638 (L. 7)

Family Proceedings, England And Wales

The Family Procedure (Adoption) (Amendment) Rules 2009

Made

10th March 2009

Laid before Parliament

12th March 2009

Coming into force

6th April 2009

The Family Procedure Rule Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75(2) and (4) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001].

In accordance with section 79(1) of the Courts Act 2003 the Committee has consulted with persons it considered appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Family Procedure (Adoption) (Amendment) Rules 2009 and shall come into force on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2009.

## 2. Amendment to the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005

In rule 95(3) of the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005[^f00002] for “This rule” substitute “Paragraph (2) of this rule ”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005 by amending paragraph (3) of rule 95 (Power of the court to proceed in the absence of a party) to provide that it is only paragraph (2) of the rule which does not apply to magistrates’ courts.
