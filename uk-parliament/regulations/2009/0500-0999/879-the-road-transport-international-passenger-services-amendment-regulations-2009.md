# The Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 879

Public Passenger Transport

The Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

2nd April 2009

Laid before Parliament

8th April 2009

Coming into force

3rd May 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988 [^f00002]:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 3rd May 2009.

**2.** Regulation 14 of the Road Transport (International Passenger Services) Regulations 1984[^f00003] is amended as follows —

- (a) In paragraph (2A), for “£12” substitute “£13.”
- (b) In paragraph (3), for “£168” substitute “£176.”
- (c) In paragraph (3A), for “£171” substitute “£180.”
- (d) In paragraph (4), for “£36” substitute “£38.”

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

2nd April 2009

We consent to the making of these Regulations

Bob Blizzard

Steve McCabe

Two Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

30th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fees payable under the Road Transport (International Passenger Services) Regulations 1984.

The fee increases, with percentage increases, are shown in the table below. The fees were last increased on 1<sup>st</sup> August 2008.

|  | Old Fee | New Fee | % increase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Application for a certified copy of an authorisation. | £12 | £13 | 8.3% |
| Application for a special regular service authorisation or international passenger transport authorisation. | £168 | £176 | 4.8% |
| Application for a shuttle service authorisation or regular service authorisation. | £171 | £180 | 5.3% |
| Annual renewal of a regular or special regular service authorisation. | £36 | £38 | 5.6% |

A full Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from: VOSA Corporate Office, Berkeley House, Croydon Street, Bristol BS5 0DA. The Impact Assessment is also annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the Instrument on the OPSI website at www.opsi.gov.uk.
