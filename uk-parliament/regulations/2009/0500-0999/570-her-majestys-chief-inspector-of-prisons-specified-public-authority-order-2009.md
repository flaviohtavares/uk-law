# Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons (Specified Public Authority) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 570

Prisons, England And Wales

Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons (Specified Public Authority) Order 2009

Made

5th March 2009

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2009

Coming into force

1st April 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 4(j) of Schedule A1 to the Prison Act 1952[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons (Specified Public Authority) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 1st April 2009.

## 2. Specified public authority

The Care Quality Commission is a specified public authority for the purposes of paragraph 4(j) of Schedule A1 (further provision about Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons) to the Prison Act 1952.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Hanson

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

5th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the Care Quality Commission as a public authority for the purposes of paragraph 4(j) of Schedule A1 to the Prison Act 1952; the effect is that the Chief Inspector is required to co-operate with the Care Quality Commission where it is appropriate to do so for the efficient and effective discharge of the Chief Inspector’s functions.
