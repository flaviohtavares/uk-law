# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 635

Security Industry

The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

11th March 2009

Laid before Parliament

13th March 2009

Coming into force

6th April 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(8)(b) of the Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00001].

In accordance with section 24(4)[^f00002] of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted with the Scottish Ministers and the Security Industry Authority.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 (Amendment) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2009.

## 2. Amendment to the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006

(1) The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(2) Omit article 1(2).

Alan Campbell

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

11th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 so as to extend that Order to Northern Ireland.
