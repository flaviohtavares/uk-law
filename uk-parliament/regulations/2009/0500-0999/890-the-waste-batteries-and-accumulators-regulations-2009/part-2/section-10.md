# The Waste Batteries and Accumulators Regulations 2009, section 10

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Effect of membership of a battery compliance scheme

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A producer who is a member of a battery compliance scheme is exempt from complying with any obligation under regulation 7(2) or 18.
