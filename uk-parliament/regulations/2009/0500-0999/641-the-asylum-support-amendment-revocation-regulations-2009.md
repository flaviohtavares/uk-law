# The Asylum Support (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 641

Immigration

The Asylum Support (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2009

Made

11th March 2009

Laid before Parliament

13th March 2009

Coming into force

5th April 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 166(3) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(a) of Schedule 8 to, the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Asylum Support (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 5<sup>th</sup> April 2009.

## 2. Revocation

The Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are revoked.

Phil Woolas

Minister of State

Home Office

11th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 revokes the Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2009. This revocation takes effect from the 5th April 2009.
