# The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Application to Service Courts) (Evidence) (Revocation) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 994

Defence

The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Application to Service Courts) (Evidence) (Revocation) Order 2009

Made

14th April 2009

Laid before Parliament

20th April 2009

Coming into force

31st October 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 8 of Schedule 13 to the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Application to Service Courts) (Evidence) (Revocation) Order 2009 and comes into force on 31st October 2009.

## 2. Revocation

The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Application to Service Courts) (Evidence) Order 2006[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Kevan Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

14th April 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order revokes the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Application to Service Courts) (Evidence) Order 2006, which relates to the giving of evidence by witnesses in service courts by a live television link.

Provisions for the giving of evidence by live link are now contained within the relevant court rules made under the [Armed Forces Act 2006 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52).
