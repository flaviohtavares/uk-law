# The Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 647

Government Trading Funds

The Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

12th March 2009

Laid before Parliament

13th March 2009

Coming into force

6th April 2009

The Chancellor of the Exchequer, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and (7) and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 2009 and comes into force on 6th April 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991

In article 3 of the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991[^f00002], for “OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund” substitute “the Buying Solutions Trading Fund”.

## 3. Revocation of amending Order

The Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) (Change of Name) Order 2001[^f00003] is revoked.

Alistair Darling

Chancellor of the Exchequer

11th March 2009

We concur

Steve McCabe

Tony Cunningham

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

12th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/875](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/875)) which established the OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund (formerly known as the Buying Agency Trading Fund).

Article 2 changes the name of the OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund to the Buying Solutions Trading Fund.

Article 3 revokes the earlier amending Order that changed the original name of the Fund to the OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
