# The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Road Traffic

Made

2nd April 2009

Laid before Parliament

8th April 2009

Coming into force

3rd May 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002].

In relation to the exercise of his powers under the Finance Act 1973 he has the consent of the Treasury.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 3rd May 2009.

## 2. Increase in fees

In regulation 3 of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00003], for “£344” substitute “£361” and for “£141” substitute “£148”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986 by increasing the fees for the approval of fitters or workshops for the installation or repair of recording equipment (tachographs).

The fee for the issue of an approval is increased by 4.9% from £344 to £361 and for the renewal of an approval by 5.0% from £141 to £148.

An impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the VOSA Corporate Office, Berkeley House, Croydon Street, Bristol BS5 0DA (telephone: 0117 954 3200). A copy has been placed in the library of each House of Parliament. The impact assessment is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website www.opsi.gov.uk.
