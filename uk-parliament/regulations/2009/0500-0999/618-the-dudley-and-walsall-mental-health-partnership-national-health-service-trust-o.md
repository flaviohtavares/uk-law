# The Dudley and Walsall Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 618

National Health Service, England

The Dudley and Walsall Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2009

Made

9th March 2009

Coming into force

31st March 2009

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 5 to that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Dudley and Walsall Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 31st March 2009.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of the Dudley and Walsall Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust[^f00002] shall be nil.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Bradshaw

Minister of State,

Department of Health

9th March 2009

We consent

Bob Blizzard

Frank Roy

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

5th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, provided for in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of the Dudley and Walsall Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust, which was established under that Act on 1st October 2008.
