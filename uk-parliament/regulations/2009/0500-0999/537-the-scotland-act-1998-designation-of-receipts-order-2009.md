# The Scotland Act 1998 (Designation of Receipts) Order 2009

> Text as at 2018-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2009 No. 537

Constitutional Law

Devolution, Scotland

The Scotland Act 1998 (Designation of Receipts) Order 2009

Made

9th March 2009

Laid before the House of Commons

9th March 2009

Coming into force

31st March 2009

The Treasury, after consultation with the Scottish Ministers, make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 64(5) and 115 of, and Schedule 7 to, the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-2880c63d92e1c6f1dd0615956de33ed5]

This Order may be cited as the Scotland Act 1998 (Designation of Receipts) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 31st March 2009.

## 2. Designated receipts[^key-9f517f83389ce5f0bde807654ccf9f73]

(1) Receipts of any description specified in paragraph (2) below, so far as they are payable into the Scottish Consolidated Fund (or would be but for provision made by or under an Act of the Scottish Parliament), shall be designated receipts for the purposes of subsections (5) to (7) of section 64 of the Scotland Act 1998.

(2)[^key-c8fd5f6447d4664afb588eead259a15b] The specified descriptions of receipts are—

- (a) dividends on public dividend capital;
- (b) interest, other than interest on—
  - (i) any loan that was provided by Scottish Homes under section 2(2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988[^f00003]; and
  - (ii) any voted loans issued by the Scottish Government since 1st June 1999.

## 3. Revocation[^key-f6e9914021e586c5fe8cca9bdd12b2e4]

The Scotland Act 1998 (Designation of Receipts) Order 2004[^f00004] is revoked.

Frank Roy

Tony Cunningham

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the receipts of the Scottish Ministers and other office holders in the Scottish Administration that are to be designated receipts. In accordance with section 64(6) of the Scotland Act 1998, the Scottish Ministers must pay to the Secretary of State sums equal to the amount of the designated receipts.

This Order revokes the Scotland Act 1998 (Designation of Receipts) Order 2004.
