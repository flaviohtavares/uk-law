# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 882

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2009

Made

8th April 2009

Coming into force

9th April 2009

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 8th day of April 2009

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2009 and comes into force on 9th April 2009.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### Person appointed on 9th April 2009

Stephen John Shaw Briggs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the person named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
