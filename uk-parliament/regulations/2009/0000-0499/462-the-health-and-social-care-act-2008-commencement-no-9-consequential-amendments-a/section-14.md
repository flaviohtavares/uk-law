# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.9, Consequential Amendments and Transitory, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2009, section 14

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Transitory modification relating to section 156 of the Act[^c-a1f385a4136317782ff8fc7d15fb25b2]

*Extent: E+W*

Until the coming into force of section 1 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00011], section 156(2) of the Act has effect as if for the definition of “company” there were substituted the following definition―

> “company” means a company as defined by section 735 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00012];

.
