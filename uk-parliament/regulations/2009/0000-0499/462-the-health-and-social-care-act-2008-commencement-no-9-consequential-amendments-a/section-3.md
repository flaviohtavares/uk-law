# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.9, Consequential Amendments and Transitory, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2009, section 3

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Appointed day for provisions relating to public health protection[^c-9af32e45ccd684c266546fbc493af16c]

*Extent: E+W*

1st April 2009 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 129 of the Act insofar as it relates to England and inserts sections 45A, 45C, 45D, 45E, 45F, 45P, 45Q, 45R, 45S and 45T into the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00006].
