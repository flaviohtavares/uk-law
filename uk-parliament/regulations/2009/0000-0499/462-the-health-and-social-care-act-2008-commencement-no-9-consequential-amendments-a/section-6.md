# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No.9, Consequential Amendments and Transitory, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2009, section 6

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Exercise by the Care Quality Commission of powers under section 113A of the 2000 Act[^c-3885049172659d6292eb722fbbb7cae3]

*Extent: E+W*

For the period beginning on 27th March 2009 and ending on 31st March 2009, the Care Quality Commission may exercise any power under section 113A of the 2000 Act (fees payable under Part 2)[^f00007] that is conferred by that section on the CHAI or the CSCI.
