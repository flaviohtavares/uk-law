# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Notice requiring statement of affairs

(1) Apply rule 4.7 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) Insert new rule 4.7(5)–

> (5) The bank liquidator shall, at the same time as sending a notice under paragraph (3), send a copy of such notice, to the registrar of companies.
