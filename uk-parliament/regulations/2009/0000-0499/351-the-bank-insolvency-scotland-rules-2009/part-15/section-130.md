# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 130

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 130. Sederunt book

(1) Apply rule 7.33 of the 1986 Rules[^f00063].

(2) In rule 7.33 for the words “the Act or of the Rules”, substitute “the Act as applied by the 2009 Act and these Rules”.

## Annotations

[^f00063]: Footnote: Rule 7.33 was amended by paragraph 51 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921), and paragraph 10 of Schedule 2(1) to [S.I. 2003/2111](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2111).
