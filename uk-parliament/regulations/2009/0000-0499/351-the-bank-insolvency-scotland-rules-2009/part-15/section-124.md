# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Right to list of creditors and copy documents

(1) Apply rule 7.26 of the 1986 Rules[^f00060].

(2) Apply rule 7.26 of the 1986 Rules.

(3) For rule 7.26(1) substitute “Paragraph (2) applies to bank insolvency proceedings.”.

(4) Ignore rule 7.26(2A) where it first occurs referring to Member State Liquidator.

## Annotations

[^f00060]: Footnote: Rule 7.26 was amended by paragraph 49 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921), and regulation 28(2) of Part II of [S.I. 2003/2109](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2109).
