# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Giving of notices, etc

(1) Apply rule 7.21 of the 1986 Rules[^f00058].

(2) In rule 7.21(1) for the words “the Act or the Rules” substitute “the Act as applied by the 2009 Act or these Rules”.

## Annotations

[^f00058]: Footnote: Rule 7.21 was amended by paragraph 47 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921).
