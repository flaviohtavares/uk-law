# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Insolvency practitioner’s caution

(1) Apply rule 7.28 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) For rule 7.28(2) substitute “It is the duty of the liquidation committee in a bank insolvency to review from time to time the adequacy of the bank liquidator’s caution.”.
