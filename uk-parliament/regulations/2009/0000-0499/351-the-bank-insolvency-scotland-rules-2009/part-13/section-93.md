# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. First excepted case

(1) Apply rule 4.80 of the 1986 Rules[^f00041].

(2) In rule 4.80(1)(b)(ii) ignore the reference to Administrator.

## Annotations

[^f00041]: Footnote: Rule 4.80 was amended by rule 3(2) of [S.I. 2007/2537](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2537).
