# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Preliminary

(1) Apply rule 4.78 of the 1986 Rules[^f00040].

(2) In paragraph (c) of that rule, omit the words from “whether” to the end.

## Annotations

[^f00040]: Footnote: Rule 4.78 was amended by paragraph 33 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921).
