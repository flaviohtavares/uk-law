# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Application for leave under section 216(3) before passing of full payment resolution

Where an application for leave under section 216 of the 1986 Act is made before a full payment resolution has been passed, it may only be made with the consent of the bank liquidator.
