# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Overview

The purpose of these Rules is to provide a procedure for the appointment of a bank liquidator and the operation of bank insolvency under Part 2 of the 2009 Act in Scotland.
