# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Representation of corporations

(1) Apply rule 7.20 of the 1986 Rules[^f00055].

(2) In rule 7.20(1) the reference to section 375 of the Companies Act should be read as section 323 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00056].

(3) For rule 7.20(2), substitute “The copy resolution must be signed or subscribed by or on behalf of the bank in accordance with the provisions of the Requirements of Writing (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00057] or certified by the secretary or director of the bank to be a true copy.”.
