# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Information as to pending liquidations

(1) Apply rule 4.11 of the 1986 Rules[^f00016].

(2) In rule 4.11(1) after “the Accountant in Bankruptcy” insert “and the registrar of companies”.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: Rule 4.11 was amended by paragraph 13 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921), and paragraph 141(6) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820).
