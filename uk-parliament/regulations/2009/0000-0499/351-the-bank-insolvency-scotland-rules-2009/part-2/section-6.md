# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Application for bank insolvency order

An application for a bank insolvency order under section 95 of the 2009 Act shall be in accordance with Part 2 of these Rules and Part VII of Chapter 74 of the Rules of the Court of Session[^f00009].
