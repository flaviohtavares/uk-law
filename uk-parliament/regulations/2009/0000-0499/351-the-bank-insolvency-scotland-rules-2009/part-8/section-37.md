# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Procedure on bank liquidator’s removal

(1) Apply rule 4.24 of the 1986 Rules[^f00023].

(2) At the end of rule 4.24(1)(a) and (b) after “Accountant in Bankruptcy” where it appears insert “the registrar of companies and the Bank of England.”.

## Annotations

[^f00023]: Footnote: Rule 4.24 was amended by paragraph 19 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921), and paragraph 141(9) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820).
