# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Bank liquidator deceased

(1) Apply rule 4.36 of the 1986 Rules[^f00027].

(2) In rule 4.36(1) after “court” insert “the Bank of England and liquidation committee”.

## Annotations

[^f00027]: Footnote: Rule 4.36 was amended by paragraph 141(15) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820).
