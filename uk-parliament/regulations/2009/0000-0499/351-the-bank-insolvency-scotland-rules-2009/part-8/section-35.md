# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. General qualification on powers

In exercising any power conferred on the bank liquidator by these Rules before a full payment resolution has been passed, the bank liquidator shall exercise it consistently with Objective 1.
