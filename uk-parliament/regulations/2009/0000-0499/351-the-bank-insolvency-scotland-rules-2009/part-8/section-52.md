# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Loss of qualification as insolvency practitioner

(1) Apply rule 4.37 paragraphs (1) and (2)[^f00028] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In rule 4.37(2) after “Accountant in Bankruptcy” insert “and the Bank of England”.

## Annotations

[^f00028]: Footnote: Rule 4.37(2) was amended by paragraph 141(16) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820).
