# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Power of court to set aside certain transactions

(1) Apply rule 4.38 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In rule 4.38(2) of that Rule –

- (a) leave out “either”;
- (b) leave out “or” at the end of sub-paragraph (a); and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (b) insert–

  > , or (c) it is shown to the court’s satisfaction that the transaction was entered into by the bank liquidator for the purpose of achieving Objective 1.
