# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Formalities of establishment

(1) Apply rule 4.42 of the 1986 Rules[^f00030].

## Annotations

[^f00030]: Footnote: Rule 4.42 was amended by paragraph 24 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921), and paragraph 141(17) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820). Rule 4.42(3) refers to section 375 of the [Companies Act 1985 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6). That section was replaced by section 323 of the [Companies Act 2006 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46) with effect from 1st October 2007.
