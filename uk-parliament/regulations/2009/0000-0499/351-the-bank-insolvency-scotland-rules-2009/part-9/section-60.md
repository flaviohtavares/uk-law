# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Committee established by contributories

(1) Apply rule 4.43 of the 1986 Rules[^f00031].

(2) For rule 4.43(1) of that rule substitute–

> (1) This rule applies where the outcome of the creditors’ meeting summoned by the bank liquidator under rule 23 of the 2009 Rules is, by virtue of rule 24(3), that the liquidation committee ceases to exist at the end of the meeting

.

(3) In rule 4.43(2) omit “Section 138 or”.

(4) In rule 4.43(4) omit “at least 3 and not more than 5” and substitute “3 or 5”.

## Annotations

[^f00031]: Footnote: Rule 4.43 was amended by paragraph 25 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921).
