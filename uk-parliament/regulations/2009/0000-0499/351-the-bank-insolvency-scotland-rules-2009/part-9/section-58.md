# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Membership of committee

(1) Apply rule 4.41 of the 1986 Rules[^f00029].

(2) For rule 4.41(1) substitute–

> (1) Subject to rule 4.43 of the 1986 Rules as applied by rule 56 of the 2009 Rules, the liquidation committee shall consist of either 3 or 5 creditors of the bank, elected by the meeting of creditors held under rule 23 of those Rules.

.

(3) Ignore paragraphs (3) and (6) and in paragraph (5) ignore any reference to contributory members.

## Annotations

[^f00029]: Footnote: Rule 4.41 was amended by paragraph 23 of Schedule 1(I) to [S.I. 1987/1921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1921).
