# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Application of rules

The rules in this Part apply only in relation to the liquidation committee established under rule 23 after a full payment resolution has been passed.
