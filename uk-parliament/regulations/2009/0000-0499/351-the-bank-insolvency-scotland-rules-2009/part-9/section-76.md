# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Composition of committee when creditors paid in full

(1) Apply rule 4.59 of the 1986 Rules[^f00035].

(2) For rule 4.59(4) substitute “The members of the liquidation committee will cease to be members at the end of the final meeting held under rule 44 of the 2009 Rules.”

## Annotations

[^f00035]: Footnote: Rule 4.59 was amended by paragraph 141(18) of Schedule 2(II) to [S.I. 1999/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1820).
