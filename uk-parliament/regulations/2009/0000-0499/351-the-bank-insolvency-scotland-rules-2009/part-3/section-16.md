# The Bank Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2009, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Termination of appointment

(1) Apply rule 4.6(1)[^f00013] and (2)[^f00014] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In rule 4.6(1) ignore “Except in relation to winding up petitions under section 124A”.

(3) After rule 4.6(2) insert–

> (3) On the making of a bank insolvency order, the appointment of the provisional bank liquidator shall terminate.

.
