# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 261

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 261. Costs, expenses, etc.[^c-93cf43dc09f93d8de20b2bf923e4356c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of bank insolvency, except for any money paid by the FSCS to eligible depositors in pursuance of Objective 1[^c-27bbc9a9864253a498659f0eb393e9bb], and any expense incurred by the FSCS in this process, are to be regarded as expenses of the bank insolvency.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part[^c-7051de3861fe5f988676b5cc45899d74] shall be paid out of that prescribed part.
