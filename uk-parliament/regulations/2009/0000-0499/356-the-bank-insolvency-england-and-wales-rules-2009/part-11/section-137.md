# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 137

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 137. General qualification on powers[^key-4d568a30191e59698663558d6ce93d34]

*Extent: E+W*

In exercising any power conferred on the bank liquidator by this Part before a full payment resolution has been passed, the bank liquidator shall exercise it consistently with Objective 1.
