# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 112

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Power of court to set aside certain transactions[^c-c0f89510c88ac0dbfdc5dead59657ec5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.149 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) leave out “either”, and
- (b) leave out “or” at the end of sub–paragraph (a), and after sub–paragraph (b) insert—

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) it is shown to the court’s satisfaction that the transaction was entered into by the liquidator for the purpose of achieving Objective 1.

  .
