# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 86

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Appointment to be advertised and registered[^key-d2a24f6f4fc930a6d85b1a0f62c931cb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the bank liquidator is appointed by a meeting of the creditors or by the Bank of England under rule 106.

(2)[^key-480e17ab46f68517017f10a23275dd6a] The bank liquidator shall, after receiving the certificate of appointment, give notice of that appointment as soon as reasonably practicable. Such notice —

- (a) shall be gazetted; and
- (b) may be advertised in such other manner as the bank liquidator thinks fit.

(3) The expense of giving notice under this rule shall be borne in the first instance by the bank liquidator; but he is entitled to be reimbursed as an expense of the bank insolvency.

(4) The bank liquidator shall immediately notify his appointment to the registrar of companies.
