# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 100

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 100. Bank liquidator’s remuneration where he realises assets on behalf of chargeholder[^c-a30a8ba9a3c1a8ac238893a34604c66a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.127B[^f00041] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for the words from “liquidator” to “and” substitute “bank liquidator”.

(3) In paragraphs (2) and (3), after “Schedule 6” add “to the 1986 Rules as applied by the Schedule to the 2009 Rules[^c-c7366d773eb362be654afca8cfa6a70b]”.
