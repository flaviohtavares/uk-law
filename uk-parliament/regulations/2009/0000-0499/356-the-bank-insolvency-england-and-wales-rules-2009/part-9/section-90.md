# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 90

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 90. Meeting of creditors to remove bank liquidator[^key-37cf48be0493bc49a480a993623781cf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.113 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for “section 174(4)” substitute “section 109 of the Banking Act 2009”.

(3) In paragraph (2), for “official receiver” substitute “Bank of England and the appropriate regulator[^key-d3bc7214a78daf56d267a9a24d024431]”.

(4) For paragraph (4)[^key-e1d9cf1a01d53ca6ebfa84a905190803] substitute—

> (4) Where the meeting passes a resolution that—
>
> - (a) the bank liquidator be removed;
> - (b) a new bank liquidator be appointed, or
> - (c) the bank liquidator not to be given their release
>
>   the bank liquidator shall[^key-2f865486bd8771b245aa4069710b5a33] be given a copy of that resolution and if it has been resolved to remove the bank liquidator, the bank liquidator shall be given a certificate to that effect.

(5) For paragraph (5) substitute—

> (5) If the creditors have resolved to appoint a new bank liquidator, the certificate of that[^key-1aa2221ccab2e70e4b0598308a1e2d37] appointment shall also be sent to the registrar of companies within that time and rule 4.100 shall apply.
