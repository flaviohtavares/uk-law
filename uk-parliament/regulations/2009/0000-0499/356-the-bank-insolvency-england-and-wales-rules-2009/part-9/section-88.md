# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 88

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Action following acceptance of resignation[^key-6ffef0440c2ee236ea2f8f9b2b2adee5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where a meeting is summoned to notify the creditors of the bank liquidator’s resignation

(2) The meeting shall[^key-03471b6d4f9629e1fe7756a4ece20f60] resolve whether to give the bank liquidator their release.

(3)[^key-c0a615b6ca54452d6450c071e08ebe04] If the meeting resolves not to release the bank liquidator, the bank liquidator shall be given a copy of that resolution and rule 95 applies.

(4) After the meeting the bank liquidator shall file the notice of his resignation in court and shall[^key-dfb6c82257c8e5e241d0293f4fc277fc] send copies of the notice to the Bank of England, the appropriate regulator[^key-d3bc7214a78daf56d267a9a24d024431] and the registrar of companies.

(5) The bank liquidator’s resignation is effective as from the date on which the court receives the notice of that[^key-53bacea3702525835fdf6f567d8e38cc] resignation, and the court shall endorse that date on the notice.
