# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 42

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. General power to call meetings[^key-d842e77343efc577e8207047e1fb1a4d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.54 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) Where the bank liquidator has been directed to summon a meeting of creditors under section 195 of the Act (as applied by section 109 of the 2009 Act) for the purpose of removing the bank liquidator, the bank liquidator shall give notice of the meeting to the Bank of England and the appropriate regulator[^key-d3bc7214a78daf56d267a9a24d024431].
