# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 45

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Requisitioned meetings: reforming the liquidation committee[^c-b2ac4e640a75b185f60bc481f2155d5e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Rule 4.57 of the 1986 Rules also applies where—

- (a) the liquidation committee has ceased to exist at the end of the first meeting of creditors under rule 41 and no further steps have been taken to re-establish[^c-acc958c63ece8c3cab45782254ceca55] that committee; and
- (b) the bank liquidator has been requested, by no less than one-tenth in value of the bank’s creditors, to summon a meeting for the purpose of re-establishing the liquidation committee.

(2) Where a meeting is requisitioned to re-establish the liquidation committee, the time periods set out in rule 4.57 of the 1986 Rules may be expedited by the bank liquidator at the request[^c-fc0913de03b6f73cf5c32bdbdcaea1b7] of the bank’s creditors.

(3) The bank liquidator shall give notice of the meeting to the appropriate regulator[^c-d3bc7214a78daf56d267a9a24d024431] and Bank of England.

(4) Rule 41(1) and (2) shall apply at this meeting as if it were the first meeting of creditors.
