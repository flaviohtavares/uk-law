# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 28

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Verification and filing[^key-8616f09aadcd2d31ef09c3d76af1cc79]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.33 of the 1986 Rules. For “official receiver” substitute “bank liquidator”[^key-dac61eb13076eb662f2a2019cd15191f].

(2) For paragraph (6), substitute—

> (6) The bank liquidator shall file the statement of affairs in court and shall send a copy of it to the registrar of companies.

(3) Ignore paragraph (7).
