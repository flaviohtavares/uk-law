## 37. Statement of affairs dispensed with[^c-d3dd17b4913733bed42290250a79bc93]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 4.46 of the 1986 Rules. In paragraphs (2) and (3), for “official receiver” substitute “bank liquidator”.[^c-31bbfa4f78b9fe696accc8a3ab5371d0]
