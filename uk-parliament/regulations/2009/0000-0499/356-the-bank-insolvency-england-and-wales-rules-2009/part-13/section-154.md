# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 154

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 154. Primacy of Objective 1[^c-ad6ccf935ea7228c5f845284442bef4b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the bank liquidator considers that the carrying out of a duty imposed by a rule in this Part would prejudice the achievement of Objective 1, the bank liquidator shall postpone the carrying out of that duty until the bank liquidator[^c-a8922867e85c6fde2fe0a1b41f22953f] considers that the carrying out of the duty would no longer be likely to prejudice the achievement of that Objective.
