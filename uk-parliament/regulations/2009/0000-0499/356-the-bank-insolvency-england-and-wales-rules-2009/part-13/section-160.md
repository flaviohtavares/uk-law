# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 160

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 160. Costs not to fall on bank liquidator[^c-89c99ea51e0bd91f9a25e0ae597bdcd7]

*Extent: E+W*

The bank liquidator is not personally liable for any costs incurred by a person in respect of an application to set aside or vary the bank liquidator’s act or decision in settling the list of contributories, or varying or adding to the list, unless the court makes an order to that effect.
