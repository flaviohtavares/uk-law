> Text as at 2013-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 287. Notice to joint bank liquidators[^c-07375149f16dfadb8adcca26c8f9984e]

*Extent: E+W*

Where 2 or more persons are acting jointly as the bank liquidator, delivery of a document to 1 of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
