# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 115

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 115. Membership of committee[^key-00278094a06a2c67ea5b4c512de10efc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.152 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1)[^key-480722c4258f24d7afa1ac5df51b38bd] Subject to rule 41(1) of the 2009 Rules and rule 4.154 as applied by rule 117 of the 2009 Rules, the liquidation committee shall consist of either 3 or 5 creditors of the bank, elected by the meeting of creditors held under rule 41 of the 2009 Rules.

(3) Ignore paragraphs (2), (4) and (7)[^f00047] and ignore any reference to contributory members in paragraph (6).
