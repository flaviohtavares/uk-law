# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 117

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 117. Committee established by contributories[^c-f4314387d24fc45c1f70cb7e53651ea9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.154 of the 1986 Rules[^f00050].

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) This rule applies where the outcome of the creditors’ meeting summoned by the bank liquidator under rule 41 of the [^c-7ff03f3a23430113acc761e8511c2b13]... 2009 Rules is, (by virtue of rule 41 (2)), that the liquidation committee ceases to exist at the end of the meeting.

.

(3) In paragraph (2), for “that section” substitute “section 141 of the Act”.

(4) In paragraph (4) for “at least 3 and not more than 5”, substitute “3 or 5”.
