# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 187

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 187. Further exception[^key-1e5e8bf171fbb93ba91abf33a810687c]

*Extent: E+W*

The court’s leave under section 216(3) of the Act is not required in respect of anything done by a person in connection with the exercise of a stabilisation power under Part 1 of the 2009 Act.
