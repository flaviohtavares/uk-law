# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 182

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 182. Application for leave under s 216(3) before passing of full payment resolution[^key-6c83fefd5f27fe85d86d61ba3992f405]

*Extent: E+W*

Where an application for leave under section 216 of the 1986 Act is made before a full payment resolution has been passed, it may only be made with the consent of the bank liquidator.
