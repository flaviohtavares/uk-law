# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 180

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 180. Procedure following appeal under section 116 of the 2009 Act[^c-71aad87e86dff51dba69f89b9409e693][^c-4a76ab7b4a6f0387ef35ac7077bc7ce9]

*Extent: E+W*

Following an appeal under section 116 (2) of the 2009 Act (against a decision of the Secretary of State under that section) the court shall send 2 sealed copies of its order to the person in whose favour the appeal was determined, and that party shall send 1 of the copies to the registrar of companies.
