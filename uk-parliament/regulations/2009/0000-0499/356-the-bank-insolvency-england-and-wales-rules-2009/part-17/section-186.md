# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 186

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 186. Third excepted case[^c-5b964fdfcea89fd5d1b47a4570b7fd3b]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 4.230 of the 1986 Rules. In paragraph (a), for “liquidating company went into liquidation” substitute “bank went into bank insolvency”.
