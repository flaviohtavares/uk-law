# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 217

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 217. Application for costs[^c-03eb2720a56bbfd1dc4083e87430b8c2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 7.40 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1) for “insolvency” substitute “bank insolvency”.

(3) In paragraph (2), leave out the words from “, and, in winding up” to the end.

(4) In paragraph (3), leave out “and, where appropriate, the official receiver”.

(5)[^c-39ba3d056c6c01ea4ec3f41545e3c071] Ignore paragraph (3A).
