# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 227

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 227. Principal court rules and practice to apply[^key-84cde7ce7434a58eaffe13578222b72e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The CPR and the practice and procedure of the High Court (including any practice direction) apply to bank insolvency proceedings in the High Court, with any necessary modifications, except so far as inconsistent with these Rules.

(2) All bank insolvency proceedings shall be allocated to the multi–track for which CPR Part 29 makes provision [^key-78f88d88cf4b45ac8a96235ad44914b1]..., accordingly those provisions of the CPR which provide for allocation questionnaires and track allocation do not apply.
