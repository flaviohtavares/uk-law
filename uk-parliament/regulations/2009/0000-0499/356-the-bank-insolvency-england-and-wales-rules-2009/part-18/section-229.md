# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 229

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 229. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies[^c-c41ea59ddb91dcb4a9c32414313089d1]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in a bank insolvency, the court makes an order staying any action, execution or legal process against the property of the bank, service of the order may be effected by delivering a sealed copy to the address for service of the claimant or other person having carriage of the proceedings to be stayed[^c-bca574b5bb21884e2a7dae652477ee58].
