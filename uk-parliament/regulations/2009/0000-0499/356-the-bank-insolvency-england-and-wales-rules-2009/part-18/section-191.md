# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 191

> Text as at 2013-04-01.

## 191. Application under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A[^key-fad167dc64d4414a56e568aa0d7d706e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 7.3A[^f00067] of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), ignore “administrator or receiver.”

(3) Ignore paragraph (2)(a).
