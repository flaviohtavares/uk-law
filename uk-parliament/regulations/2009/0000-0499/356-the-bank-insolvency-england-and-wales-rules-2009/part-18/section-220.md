> Text as at 2013-04-01.

## 220. Introductory[^key-a93e998ec539149e7ff542b44182a518]

*Extent: E+W*

Apply rule 7.43 of the 1986 Rules[^f00073]. In paragraph (1), for (a), substitute “ by reason of being a protected person within the meaning of Part 21 of the CPR or”.
