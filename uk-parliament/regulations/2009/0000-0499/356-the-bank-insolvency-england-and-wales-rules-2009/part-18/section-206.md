# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 206

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 206. Title of proceedings[^key-0013a66e36bc5c1d3d751b1fce483c57]

*Extent: E+W*

Every proceeding under Part 2 of the 2009 Act shall, with any necessary additions, be titled “IN THE MATTER OF …………….. (naming the bank to which the proceedings relate) AND IN THE MATTER OF THE BANKING ACT 2009”.
