# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 188

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 188. Preliminary[^key-2a5f88151bcbcf4ef8bf82222bd80e46]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part applies to any application made to the court under Part 2 of[^key-827656092b714267bfbbf547bb6a99a4] the 2009 Act or these Rules except an application under section 95 of the 2009 Act for a bank insolvency order.
