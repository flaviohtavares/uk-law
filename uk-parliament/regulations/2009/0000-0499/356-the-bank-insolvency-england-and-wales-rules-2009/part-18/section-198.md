# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 198

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 198. Use of reports[^key-ec3c2572cd2507946726fee14a3e482b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Unless the application involves other parties, or the court orders otherwise, a report may be filed in court instead of a witness statement by—

- (a) the bank liquidator,
- (b) the provisional bank liquidator, or
- (c) the special manager.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of a witness statement, the report shall be treated for the purposes of rule 197, and any hearing before the court, as if it were a witness statement.
