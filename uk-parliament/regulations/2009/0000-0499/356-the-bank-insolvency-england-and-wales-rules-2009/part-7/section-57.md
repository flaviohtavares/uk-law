# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 57

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Meaning of “prove”[^c-8032123ddef20e65cb9af006c9ce7566]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.73 of the 1986 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (5), for “or a Government Department” substitute “, a Government Department or the FSCS”.

(3) Ignore paragraphs (2), and (8)[^f00021].
