# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 21

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Notice of appointment[^c-5c83f12ec560484f29e67771955d5a56]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-43c4919a1b55df1d7e9b8265ddb0d813] Where a provisional bank liquidator has been appointed, the court shall notify the applicant and the person appointed.

(2)[^c-1a65e1660472a99317ba18ed5693cb34] Unless the court otherwise directs, on receipt of the notification under paragraph (1) the provisional bank liquidator shall give notice of that appointment as soon as reasonably practicable. Such notice—

- (a) shall be gazetted; and
- (b) may be advertised in such other manner as the provisional bank liquidator thinks fit.
