# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 26

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Termination of appointment[^key-05932d0dcfdf30e4777e2a7cfd7f388a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 4.31 of the 1986 Rules[^f00013].

(2) At the end insert—

> (3) On the making of a bank insolvency order the appointment of the provisional bank liquidator shall terminate.

.
