# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 247

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 247. Preliminary[^key-ef2816e396da87a6807efd4ffc22ce96]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The rules in this Part relate to the declaration and payment of dividends in a bank insolvency.

(2) In this Part—

- “**creditors**” means those creditors of the bank of whom the bank liquidator is aware, or who are identified in the bank’s statement of affairs and
- “**the insolvent**” means the bank.
