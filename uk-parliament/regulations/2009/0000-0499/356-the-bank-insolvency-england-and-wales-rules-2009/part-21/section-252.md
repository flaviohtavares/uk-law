# The Bank Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2009, section 252

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 252. Notice of declaration[^c-6bd217be4af3f42723ed11b2a56c1288]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Apply rule 11.6 of the 1986 Rules[^f00083].

(2) Ignore paragraph (1)(b)[^f00084].

(3) In paragraph (2), after (b), add—

> - (ba) expenses incurred by the bank liquidator in the achievement of objective 1 under section 99 of the Banking Act 2009.
