# The Home Information Pack (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2008/3107](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3107) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2009 No. 34

Housing, England And Wales

The Home Information Pack (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

14th January 2009

Laid before Parliament

21st January 2009

Coming into force

6th April 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 163 and 250(2) of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

In accordance with section 250(3) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the Welsh Ministers[^f00002] in relation to residential properties in Wales.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Home Information Pack (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 6th April 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Home Information Pack (No.2) Regulations 2007

The Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 14

In regulation 14(1) (time at which pack documents are to be included)—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (e) omit “and”; and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (f) add—

  > - (g) paragraph (m) (property information questionnaire relating to a property that is not a new home); and
  > - (h) paragraph (n) (property information questionnaire for a property that is a new home).

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Margaret Beckett

Minister of State for Housing

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Home Information Pack (No. 2) Regulations 2007 ([SI 2007/1667](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1667)) (“the principal Regulations”). Under sections 155 to 159 of the Housing Act 2004, a home information pack must comply with the principal Regulations.

Regulation 3 amends regulation 14 of the principal Regulations by adding the property information questionnaire to the list of required documents that must be in the home information pack before or at the first point of marketing. The requirement to include a property information questionnaire in the home information pack was introduced in the [Home Information Pack (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2008 (S.I. No 3107)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3107) and comes into force on 6th April 2009.
