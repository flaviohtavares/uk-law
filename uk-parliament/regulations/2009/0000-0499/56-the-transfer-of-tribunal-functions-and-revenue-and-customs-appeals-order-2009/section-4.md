# The Transfer of Tribunal Functions and Revenue and Customs Appeals Order 2009, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Abolition of existing tribunals[^c-341f73ff6d66b2b5dd7e1966fa7e7763]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The existing tribunals (apart from the Commissioners for the general purposes of the income tax) are abolished.
