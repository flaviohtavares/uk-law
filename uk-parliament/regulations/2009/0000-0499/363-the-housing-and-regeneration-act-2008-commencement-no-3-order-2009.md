# The Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 363 (C. 18)

Housing, England

The Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

Made

16th February 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 325 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement of provisions relating to dissolution of the Housing Corporation

Section 64(1) and (2) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 shall come into force on 16th February 2009.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Iain Wright

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

16th February 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions in section 64 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008. The provisions commenced enable the Secretary of State to make provision for the dissolution of the Housing Corporation.
