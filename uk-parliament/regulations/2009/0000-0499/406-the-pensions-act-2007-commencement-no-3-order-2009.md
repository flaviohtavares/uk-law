# The Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No.3) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 406 (C. 27)

Pensions

The Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No.3) Order 2009

Made

26th February 2009

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2) of the Pensions Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No.3) Order 2009.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 14 of the Pensions Act 2007 (conversion of guaranteed minimum pensions) is—

- (a) for the purpose of conferring power to make regulations, 1st March 2009, and
- (b) for all other purposes, 6th April 2009.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

William D.McKenzie.

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

26th February 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 14 of the Pensions Act [2007 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/22) on 1st March 2009 for the purpose of making regulations and on 6th April 2009 for all other purposes. Section 14 introduces new provisions to allow Guaranteed Minimum Pensions to be converted into other pension benefits.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
