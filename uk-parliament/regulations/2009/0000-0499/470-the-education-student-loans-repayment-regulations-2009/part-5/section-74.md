# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 74

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Repayment by fixed instalments[^c-a0921e5aa0e2d349bf9da9a240e4f1d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-bf2b010202e24965ebc0baa0bd090171] A borrower must pay to the Authority a fixed instalment [^c-b0445eb8b653deb163ce168351cd76b9]...—

- (a) no later than the day specified in a notice served under regulation 73; and
- (b) no later than the same day of each subsequent month.

(2)[^c-7f9f9faded2480a4d8ae2aac6e3728a8] The amount of the fixed instalment is the product of—

- (a) the fixed instalment rate for the borrower’s loan, and
- (b) the country-specific multiplier for the borrower’s country of residence.

(3)[^c-9ffc973cf3c0cb11101b10c1809d8911] The fixed instalment rate for a plan 1, 2, 3 or 5 loan is calculated in accordance with regulation 74A.
