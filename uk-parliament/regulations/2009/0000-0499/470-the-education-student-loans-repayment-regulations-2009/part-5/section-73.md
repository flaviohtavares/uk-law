# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 73

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Notice of liability to make repayments[^key-e7598cd482585a77f446ba4d58bda17b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 75, where the Authority is satisfied that a borrower is resident outside the United Kingdom, it may serve a notice on the borrower requiring the borrower to make repayments on a plan 1, 2, 3 or 5 loan[^key-ecd43997685575e6b3857a95bc8f5e42] in accordance with regulation 74.

(2) In a notice served under paragraph (1), the Authority may require a borrower, if the borrower has failed—

- (a) to give the notice required by regulation 72(1); or
- (b) to provide any information required by the authority under regulation 72(2),

to repay immediately such part of the loan[^key-25f1f32edae7b8d7f322432c8b5d0e58] as will reduce the amount outstanding to the amount which the Authority considers would have been outstanding if the borrower had given the notice or provided the information required.
