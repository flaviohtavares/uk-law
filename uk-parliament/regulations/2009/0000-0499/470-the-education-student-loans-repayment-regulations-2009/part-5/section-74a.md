## 74A. How to calculate fixed instalment rate[^c-3d91c6ef81d9168757dd1fb5f3b5e768]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The fixed instalment rate for a plan 1, 2[^c-98f1423faa56cd711ede01737e0a64d5], 3 or 5 loan is the result of the formula in paragraph (2) or (2A) (as the case may be)[^c-ae46f2ab4776cb9234353a1dc79b6619]—

- (a) calculated on the 6th of April of each year, and
- (b) rounded down to the nearest £1 (if necessary).

(2) The formula applicable to a[^c-a25521eb3e53a8c45f9388415fba1272] plan 1, 2[^c-4acff680e964966a010601555b9ffec5] or 5 loan is—

(2A)[^c-fbaa55cde2022ac85fcae702ac40c54b] The formula applicable to a plan 3 loan is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470)

(3) In the formulae in paragraphs (2) and (2A)[^c-6fd05ff3751d20d77f50eae28564ac84]—

- “**x**” means the median salary for graduates (to the nearest £500) in the most-recent year published by the Department for Education;
- “**y**” means the repayment threshold for the loan.

  | --- |
  | On 6th April, £36,000 is the median salary and £27,295 is the repayment threshold.<br>- £36,000 multiplied by two is £72,000.<br>- £27,295 subtracted from £72,000 is £44,705.<br>- £44,705 multiplied by 0.09 is £4,023.45.<br>- £4,023.45 divided by 12 is £335.29.<br>The fixed instalment rate is £335 (rounded down to the nearest £1). |
