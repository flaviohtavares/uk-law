# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 78

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Foreclosure[^c-ea6a5513703226e449480e79483e228d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a borrower does not pay an instalment or other amount when it is due, the Authority may require the borrower to repay the loan in full immediately.
