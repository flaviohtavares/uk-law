# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 36

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Late payment penalties[^c-0f03a362e252f80107fbf3d85e8497e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-bbd3e4d1c1b36e1ad13802d69c751f98] Schedule 56 to the Finance Act 2009 (penalty for failure to make payments on time) applies to repayments which have become payable by a borrower under this Part as it applies to an amount of income tax which—

- (a)[^c-d6f6b0c8af898799d196103a53040c53] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) falls within—
  - (i) item 1, 12, 18 or 19 of the Table in paragraph 1 of that Schedule, or
  - (ii) insofar as the tax falls within item 1 of that Table, item 17, 23 or 24 of that Table.

(2)[^c-1e1cccc1524ba9ec8622057d05c6c7d2] Schedule 26 to the Finance Act 2021 (penalties for failure to pay tax) applies to repayments which have become payable by a borrower under this Part as it applies to an amount of income tax which falls within item 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 9 or 10 of the table headed “Income tax or capital gains tax” in paragraph 1 (1) of that Schedule.
