# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 22

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Information requests[^key-d86371c350d3ac5d6b3aa8832ad313b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a borrower-

- (a) changes home address; or
- (b) changes name,

the borrower must inform, and provide particulars of the change to, the Authority within six weeks of the relevant change.
