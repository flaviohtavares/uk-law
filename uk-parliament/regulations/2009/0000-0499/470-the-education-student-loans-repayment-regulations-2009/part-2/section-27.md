> Text as at 2026-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Foreclosure[^c-2ffb9d78ff5f389ff5e6e9db9b9bab93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a borrower fails to comply with an Information or Penalty Notice or both, the Authority may require the borrower to repay the student loan [^c-af6991fcac55eb267a5bc2323b71a018]... in full immediately.
