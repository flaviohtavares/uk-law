# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 67

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Cessation of employment[^key-8c45fec9070683fce7ff7029ff348ef3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an employer completes a Form P45, the employer must state in it that the employee is a borrower if, on the date of the Form P45—

- (a) the employer has received notice that the employee is a borrower as described in regulation 49(1); [^key-efc25d53d3ab8376efb07ccada43a0c2]...
- (b)[^key-3a122ef27cc0e64827aabafd0ff97afc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ba)[^key-c3fbb354f55b1d04217c0bf55b67d6f4] the employer has received information under regulation 43[^key-23c9bc185ef068f14c877765da83fc63] that the employee has any liability to repay any student loan [^key-98da3e5f06aadc7f7e19138bfd681f49]...; and
- (c) if paragraph (a) or (ba) apply,[^key-8ae0bffdf1ab5ae1c6c921718991ae49] the employer has not received a notice from HMRC under regulation 49(3) requiring the employer not to make deductions from the employee’s earnings on and after a date before the date on which the person ceased to be an employee.
