# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 69

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Collection and recovery of penalties[^c-d2d2e8be0bea94e1289af4f9f6ad8ac3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 69 of the 1970 Act (recovery of penalties etc) applies to penalties imposed under this Part as it applies to penalties imposed under that Act, but does not apply to penalties under regulation 59(8)(b)[^f00054].

[^f00054]: Footnote: Schedule 24 to the Finance Act [2007 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/11) makes similar provision to section 69 of the 1970 Act in relation to penalties incurred under regulation 58(8)(b).
