# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 3E

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3E. Meaning of “plan 5 loan”[^key-0dab0ab49ec96437facf6c12476fbd0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-d42fe7e75911b2b93b9c4fff19373971] A “plan 5 loan” is a loan which is—

- (a) a plan 5 LLE loan, or
- (b) a plan 5 non-LLE loan.

(1) A “plan 5 non-LLE loan[^key-06d383fb204e643915b6e8af51c9d98a]” is a loan—

- (a) taken out by a person in respect of a course the person begins on or after 1st August 2023, and
- (b) paid under any of the following—
  - (i) the 2011 Support Regulations;
  - (ii) the Further Education Loans Regulations 2012.

(2) Despite paragraph (1), a loan taken out by a person for a course mentioned in regulation 3B(2)(a)(ii) or (iii) is a plan 2 loan.

(3)[^key-dfc7720469517866bff363357973959d] A “plan 5 LLE loan” is a loan paid under the 2026 Support Regulations.
