# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 8

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Service by post or electronic communication[^key-77bd775f74281108940f30de84ed3003]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any notice or other document which is authorised or required to be given, served or issued under these Regulations may be sent by post or by an electronic format[^key-0d1c39929563326be50ed529ff7f08e4].

(2) In particular, any notice which the Authority may give to HMRC may be transmitted electronically, and for these purposes a notice is transmitted electronically where the content and form of a notice is sent and received by electronic media between computer systems operated by the Authority and HMRC respectively.
