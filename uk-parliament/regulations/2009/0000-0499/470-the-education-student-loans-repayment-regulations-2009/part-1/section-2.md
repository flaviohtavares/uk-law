# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 2

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Revocation of previous Regulations[^c-59006b5dc4fe34729769d73c5ee44819][^c-a2299fb7273109c169d631083fc03a40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-59006b5dc4fe34729769d73c5ee44819] The Instruments listed in Schedule 1[^c-4a7a6208f368f4372898e635bbaf6ffc] are revoked.

(2)[^c-a2299fb7273109c169d631083fc03a40] Regulations 4(4), 93 and 94 of the Education (Student Support) (No. 2) Regulations 2008[^f00004] and regulation 51 of the Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2008[^f00005] are revoked.
