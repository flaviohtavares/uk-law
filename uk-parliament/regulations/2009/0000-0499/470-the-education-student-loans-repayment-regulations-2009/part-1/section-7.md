> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Application to transferred loans in England and Wales[^key-d28160d2a92bfc9a62c5ec0677eb37cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where these Regulations apply to a transferred loan—

- “the Authority” means, for the purpose of those regulations listed in paragraph (2), the Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers acting on behalf of the loan purchaser (regardless of whether any person is also exercising functions on behalf of the Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers pursuant to section 23(4) of the 1998 Act); [^key-597eb09b0ac50d2a7a98783755264bd6]...
- [^key-45fd128d3fe83f3b6255e8e68b3a7b87]...
- [^key-614ffc62f6110cf3a4415779c70e9d18]...

(2) The regulations referred to in paragraph (1) are: regulations 15 , 16[^key-11c892a182191341192ffb164bc44f4a] to 20, 22 to 27, and 73 to 79.
