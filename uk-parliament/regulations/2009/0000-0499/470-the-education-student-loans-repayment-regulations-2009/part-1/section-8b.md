# The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009, section 8B

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8B. How to calculate repayment threshold variable[^c-8ff74ad59fe6b521d06a459a75973246]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The repayment threshold variable is, in relation to a repayment threshold year, the sum of—

- (a) one, and
- (b) the percentage increase, expressed as a decimal, in the retail price index between the two Marches immediately before the commencement of the previous repayment threshold year.

  | ***Example*** |
  | --- |
  | 2.4% is the percentage increase in the retail price index.<br>- 2.4% expressed as a decimal is 0.024.<br>- The sum of 1 and 0.024 is 1.024.<br>The repayment threshold variable is 1.024. |
