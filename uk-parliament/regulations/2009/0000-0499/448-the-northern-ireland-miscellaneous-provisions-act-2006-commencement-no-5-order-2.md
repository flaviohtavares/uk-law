# The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No.5) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 448 (C. 30)

Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No.5) Order 2009

Made

2nd March 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(3) of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

(1) The provisions of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 11 March 2009.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 16 (conditions for devolving policing and justice matters);
- (b) section 17 together with Schedule 2 (department with policing and justice functions);
- (c) section 18 (power of Assembly to call for witnesses and documents);
- (d) section 19 (provision for transfer of functions relating to extradition etc.);
- (e) section 20 (provision for entrenching enactments);
- (f) section 30(1) (minor and consequential amendments etc.) in so far as it relates to the provision referred to in sub-paragraph (g); and
- (g) Part 3 of Schedule 4 (devolution of policing and justice functions etc.).

Shaun Woodward

One of Her Majesty’s Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

2nd March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 11 March 2009 the provisions of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 set out in article 2.
