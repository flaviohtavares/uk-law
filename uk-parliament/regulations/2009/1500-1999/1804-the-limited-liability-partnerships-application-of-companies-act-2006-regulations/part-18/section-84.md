## 84. Transitional provisions: Northern Ireland LLPs

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 2 to these Regulations contains transitional provisions and savings in connection with—

- (a) the extension to Northern Ireland of the enactments in force in Great Britain relating to limited liability partnerships [^c19765411], and
- (b) the consequent repeal of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 [^c19765421].
