# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009, section 9A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1804

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9A. Names containing computer code[^c-a8bb470469e04f605b4d26677ba7a697]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 57A applies to LLPs, modified so it reads as follows—

> **57A. Names containing computer code**
>
> An LLP must not be registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 by a name that, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, consists of or includes computer code.
