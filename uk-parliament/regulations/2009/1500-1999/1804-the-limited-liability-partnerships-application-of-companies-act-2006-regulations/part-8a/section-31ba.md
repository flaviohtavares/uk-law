## 31BA. References to “confirmation” etc of information[^key-2eb2981c34ecbeb5063f790e2e8bd3e7][^key-cbd587a2ddd5805ac892a01382fd8b5c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 790CA applies to LLPs, modified so that references to a company are to be read as references to an LLP.
