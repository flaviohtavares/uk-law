## 75A. Communications in electronic form[^key-60c194211172e17c3769909ae0198d78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1141(1) (service addresses) of and paragraph 4A of Schedule 4 (documents and information sent or supplied to a company) to the Companies Act 2006 have effect as if any reference to “company” in that paragraph is a reference to “LLP”.
