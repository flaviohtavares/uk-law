# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1804
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Consents for certain prosecutions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1126 applies to LLPs, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **1126. Consents required for certain prosecutions**
>
> (1) This section applies to proceedings for an offence under section 448, 449, 450, 451 or 453A of the Companies Act 1985 or under section 1112[^c-2a0c894d3405ba7be11f57033ac1a8be] or 1112A[^c-b261e50a3d4670d5d11de9ef16616ffc] of this Act, as applied to LLPs.
>
> (2) No such proceedings are to be brought in England and Wales except by or with the consent of the Secretary of State or the Director of Public Prosecutions.
>
> (3) No such proceedings are to be brought in Northern Ireland except by or with the consent of the Secretary of State or the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.
