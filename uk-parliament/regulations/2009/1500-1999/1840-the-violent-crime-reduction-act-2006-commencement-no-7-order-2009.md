# The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1840 (C. 89)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2009

Made

8th July 2009

The Secretary of State, being the relevant national authority under section 66(3)(b) of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 66(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2009.

(2) In this Order, the “2006 Act” means the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the 2006 Act shall come into force on 31st August 2009—

- (a) section 1 (drinking banning orders),
- (b) section 2 (duration of drinking banning orders),
- (c) section 3 (orders on an application to magistrates’ court),
- (d) section 4 (orders in county court proceedings), and
- (e) section 5 (variation or discharge of orders under s. 3 or 4).

**3.** The following provisions of the 2006 Act shall come into force on 31st August 2009 in so far as they relate to a drinking banning order under section 3 or 4 of the 2006 Act—

- (a) section 9 (interim orders),
- (b) section 10 (appeals),
- (c) section 11 (breach of drinking banning orders),
- (d) section 12 (approved courses),
- (e) section 13 (certificates of completion of approved courses), and
- (f) section 14 (interpretation of Chapter 1).

Alan Campbell

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

8th July 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 31st August 2009 the provisions of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 listed in articles 2 and 3.
