# The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1604 (C. 83)

Supreme Court Of The United Kingdom

The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2009

Made

26th June 2009

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 148(1) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

In accordance with section 148(4) and (5) of that Act, the Lord Chancellor has approved written plans for the accommodation of the Supreme Court and is satisfied that the accommodation which will be provided is in accordance with those plans.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2009.

**2.** The following provisions of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00002] come into force on 1st October 2009—

- (a) sections 23 to 44;
- (b) sections 47 to 60;
- (c) sections 137 and 138;
- (d) Schedules 8, 9, 10, 11 and 16;
- (e) paragraphs 9 to 35 of Schedule 17 (and section 145 so far as relating to those provisions);
- (f) Parts 5 and 6 of Schedule 18 (and section 146 so far as relating to those provisions).

Jack Straw

Lord Chancellor

26th June 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Constitutional Reform Act [2005 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4) listed in Article 2. The effect is to bring into force the whole of Part 3 of the Act and the relevant Schedules, which make provision for the Supreme Court. The only exceptions are sections 45 and 46, which have already been commenced. Section 138, Schedule 16 and Part 6 of Schedule 18, which make provision for the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council, are also commenced.
