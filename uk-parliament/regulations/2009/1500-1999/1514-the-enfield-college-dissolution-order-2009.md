# The Enfield College (Dissolution) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1514

Education, England

The Enfield College (Dissolution) Order 2009

Made

17th June 2009

Laid before Parliament

24th June 2009

Coming into force

1st August 2009

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”).

This Order is made with the consent of the further education corporation known as The College of North East London[^f00002]. It is made for the purpose of giving effect to a proposal for the dissolution of the further education corporation known as Enfield College[^f00003] (“the corporation”) made by the Learning and Skills Council for England. The Secretary of State has consulted the corporation.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Enfield College (Dissolution) Order 2009 and comes into force on 1st August 2009.

**2.** On 1st August 2009 the corporation is to be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities are to be transferred to The College of North East London, being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

**3.** Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st August 2009 as if the reference in that section —

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;
- (b) to the operative date were to 1st August 2009;
- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and
- (d) to the corporation were to The College of North East London.

Kevin Brennan

Minister of State

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

17th June 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st August 2009 the further education corporation established to conduct Enfield College. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to The College of North East London and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no wider impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
