# The Non-Contentious Probate (Amendment) Rules 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1893 (L. 18)

Supreme Court Of England And Wales

The Non-Contentious Probate (Amendment) Rules 2009

Made

14th July 2009

Laid before Parliament

16th July 2009

Coming into force

1st September 2009

The President of the Family Division of the High Court (the judicial office holder nominated by the Lord Chief Justice), with the agreement of the Lord Chancellor, makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 127 of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00001] and in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Non-Contentious Probate (Amendment) Rules 2009 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2009.

## 2. Amendment to the Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987

The Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987[^f00003] are amended in accordance with rule 3.

**3.** In rule 32(1)(a) (grants on behalf of minors), for sub-paragraph (i) substitute—

> - (i) section 2(1), 2(1A), 2(2), 2(2A), 4 or 4ZA of the Children Act 1989[^f00004],

.

Sir Mark Potter

President

10th July 2009

I agree

Signed on the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Ministry of Justice

14th July 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Non-Contentious Probate Rules ([SI 1987/2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024)) by substituting in rule 32(1)(a) (grants on behalf of minors) a new sub-paragraph (i) to reflect relevant amendments made to the Children Act 1989 by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008. The Rules come into force on the same date as the 2008 Act provisions in question.
