# The Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Material Detriment Test) Appointed Day Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1565 (C.80)

Pensions

The Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Material Detriment Test) Appointed Day Order 2009

Made

24th June 2009

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 91(9) of the Pensions Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Material Detriment Test) Appointed Day Order 2009.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into effect of the Pensions Regulator Code of Practice No.12: Circumstances in relation to the material detriment test is 29th June 2009.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Angela Eagle

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

24th June 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 29th June 2009 as the day for the coming into effect of the Pensions Regulator Code of Practice No.12: Circumstances in relation to the material detriment test.

A full Impact Assessment has not been published for this Order as no impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
