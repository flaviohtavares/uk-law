# The Lyme Bay Designated Area (Fishing Restrictions) (Amendment) Order 2009

> Text as at 2009-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2008/1584](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1584) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs and the Secretary of State concerned with the sea fishing industry in Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(3) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Lyme Bay Designated Area (Fishing Restrictions) (Amendment) Order 2009; it comes into force on 31st July 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Lyme Bay Designated Area (Fishing Restrictions) Order 2008

(1) The Lyme Bay Designated Area (Fishing Restrictions) Order 2008[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2) of article 3 omit “or disembarkation”.

Huw Irranca-Davies

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Paul Goggins

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Lyme Bay Designated Area (Fishing Restrictions) Order 2008 to correct the wording of article 3(2). The amendment aligns the words of article 3(2) with those of section 8(2) of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order, as no impact on the private, public or voluntary sector is foreseen.
