# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 59. Legibility of displays and disclosures

Any display or disclosure of information required by this Part must be in characters that can be read with the naked eye.
