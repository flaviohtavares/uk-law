# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 62. Company's name to appear on communications

A company to which this Part applies must state the company's name on all—

- (a) its business letters, notices and other official publications;
- (b) its bills of exchange, promissory notes, endorsements and order forms;
- (c) cheques purporting to be signed by or on behalf of the company;
- (d) orders for money, goods or services purporting to be signed by or on behalf of the company;
- (e) its bills of parcels, invoices and other demands for payments, receipts, and letters of credit;
- (f) its applications for licences to carry on a trade or activity;
- (g) other forms of its business correspondence and documentation; and
- (h) its websites,

that are used in carrying on the activities of its business in the United Kingdom.
