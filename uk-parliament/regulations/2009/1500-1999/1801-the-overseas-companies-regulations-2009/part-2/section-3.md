# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 3. Application and interpretation of Part

(1) This Part applies to an overseas company that opens a UK establishment.

(2) In this Part—

- “**director**” includes shadow director; and
- “**secretary**” includes any person occupying the position of secretary by whatever name called.
