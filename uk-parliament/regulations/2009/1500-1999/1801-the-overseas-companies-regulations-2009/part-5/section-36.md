# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 36. Application of Chapter

This Chapter applies to an overseas company to which this Part applies that is not a company to which Chapter 2 of this Part applies.
