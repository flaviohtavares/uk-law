# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 30. Application of Part

This Part applies to every overseas company that has an establishment in the United Kingdom and is not—

- (a) a credit or financial institution (as to which, see Part 6), or
- (b) a company whose constitution does not limit the liability of its members.
