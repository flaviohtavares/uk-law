# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 34. Period allowed for filing copies of accounting documents[^c-651e60f2064d64655562a4c27daf98d6]

The period allowed for delivery, in relation to a copy of a document required to be delivered under regulation 32, is three months from the date on which the document is required to be disclosed in accordance with the company's parent law.
