# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 79. Revocations

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Oversea Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations 1992 [^c1976210],
- (b) the Part XXIII Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 [^c1976211].
