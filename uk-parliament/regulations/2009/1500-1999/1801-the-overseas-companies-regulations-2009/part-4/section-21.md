# The Overseas Companies Regulations 2009, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2009/1801

## 21. Protected information: restriction on ... disclosure by registrar[^c-73c499ddf042aad100950718b5ef876c]

(1) The registrar must omit protected information from the material on the register that is available for inspection where—

- (a) it is contained in a document delivered to the registrar in which such information is required to be stated, and
- (b) in the case of a document having more than one part, it is contained in a part of the document in which such information is required to be stated.

(2) The registrar is not obliged—

- (a) to check other documents or (as the case may be) other parts of the document to ensure the absence of protected information, or
- (b) to omit from the material that is available for public inspection anything registered before 1st October 2009.

(3) The registrar must not disclose[^c-bf1e6db4036eb610fef5641e3f0beb1d] protected information except—

- (a)[^c-848cf1b63b7e568712034fd4c6b3addd] as permitted by regulation 24 (permitted disclosure by registrar: disclosure to credit reference agency),
- (b) in accordance with regulation 26 (disclosure under court order) , or[^c-655a7abc959c79d5a67da92d883fdeb8]
- (c)[^c-f6bad1423c18e3fc5f945fefd869209a] in accordance with section 1110F (disclosure by the registrar) of the Companies Act 2006.
