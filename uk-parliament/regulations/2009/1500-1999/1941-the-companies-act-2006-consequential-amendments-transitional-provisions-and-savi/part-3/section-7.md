# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2009, section 7

> Text as at 2018-08-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Functions of registrar of companies for Northern Ireland: contracting out

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On the coming into force in relation to the registrar of companies for Northern Ireland of an order under Part 2 of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994 [^c19814111], as amended by this Order, the provisions of the Deregulation and Contracting Out (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 [^c19814121] relating to that registrar shall cease to have effect.

(2) Those provisions are—

- (a) in Article 2(2), the definition of “registrar of companies”;
- (b) Article 11; and
- (c) Part 1 of Schedule 2.
