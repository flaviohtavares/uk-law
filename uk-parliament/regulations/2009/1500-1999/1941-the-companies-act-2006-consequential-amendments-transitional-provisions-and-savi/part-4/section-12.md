# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2018-08-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Saving for earlier consequential amendments, transitional provisions and savings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 3 to this Order contains provisions preserving the effect of the provisions of [^key-9a5f28d259ce1117ec17c127d245cfaf]... the Companies Consolidation (Consequential Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 [^c19814221] relating to old public companies.

(2) The repeal of the other provisions of that [^key-e0873dc80c0b42ab3ed546eb89ce5ec0]... Order does not affect the operation of—

- (a) any provision amending an enactment that remains in force;
- (b) any transitional provision that remains capable of having effect in relation to the corresponding provision of the Companies Act 2006;
- (c) any saving that remains capable of having effect in relation to the repeal of an enactment by that [^key-e0873dc80c0b42ab3ed546eb89ce5ec0]... Order.
