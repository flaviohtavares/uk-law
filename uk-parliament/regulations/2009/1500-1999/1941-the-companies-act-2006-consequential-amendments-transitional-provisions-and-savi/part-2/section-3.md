# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2009, section 3

> Text as at 2018-08-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. References to companies registered or re-registered under earlier legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A reference in any enactment to—

- (a) a company formed and registered under the Companies Act 2006,
- (b) a company registered but not formed under that Act, or
- (c) a company re-registered under that Act,

includes a company treated as so formed and registered, registered or re-registered by virtue of section 1297(3) of that Act, including that provision as applied by paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 2 to the Companies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 8, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2008 [^c19814051].
