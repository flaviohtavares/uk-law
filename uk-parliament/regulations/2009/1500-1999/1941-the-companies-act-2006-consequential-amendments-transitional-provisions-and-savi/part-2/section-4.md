# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2009, section 4

> Text as at 2018-08-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. References to Northern Ireland directors disqualification orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A reference in any enactment to a disqualification order under the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 [^c19814061] includes a disqualification order made under Part 2 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 [^c19814071] that by virtue of section 29(3)(a) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^c19814081] has effect as if made under the 2002 Order.
