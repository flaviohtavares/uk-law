# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2009, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Application of contribution – current system students who are not 2008 or 2009 cohort students

In the case of a current system student who is not a 2008 or 2009 cohort student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 101.
