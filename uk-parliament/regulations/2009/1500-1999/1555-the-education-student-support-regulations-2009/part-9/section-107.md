# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2009, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. General

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) —

- (a) fee loans and fee contribution loans are payable in accordance with regulation 110;
- (b) college fee loans are payable in accordance with regulation 111;
- (c) loans for living costs and long courses loans are payable in accordance with regulation 112;

(2) Regulations 108 and 109 apply in respect of the payment of all loans.
