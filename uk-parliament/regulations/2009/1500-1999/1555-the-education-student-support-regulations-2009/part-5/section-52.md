# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2009, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Qualifying conditions for the grant for travel – overseas study

A grant is available to an eligible student in respect of the reasonable expenditure which the student is obliged to incur in each qualifying quarter within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending as part of the student’s course the overseas institution or the Institute.
