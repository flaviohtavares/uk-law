# The Limited Liability Partnerships (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2009/1804](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1804) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2009 No. 1833

Limited Liability Partnerships

The Limited Liability Partnerships (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

8th July 2009

Laid before Parliament

10th July 2009

Coming into force

1st October 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 15 and 17 of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Limited Liability Partnerships (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 1st October 2009.

## 2. Amendment

(1) Part 2 of Schedule 3 to the Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009 (consequential amendments of the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001)[^f00002] is amended as follows

(2) In paragraph 13(5) (amendment of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001[^f00003]: applied provisions of Companies Act 1985), in sub-paragraph (a), after “Part 14 of that Act (investigations etc)” insert “or Part 18 of that Act (floating charges in Scotland)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1804](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1804)). Those Regulations (together with the Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1911](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1911))) revoke most of the provisions in the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/1090](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1090)) which applied provisions of the Companies Act [1985 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6) (“the 1985 Act”) to limited liability partnerships (LLPs). However, certain provisions in Parts 14 (investigations) and 18 (Scottish floating charges) of the 1985 Act remain in force and should continue to be applied to LLPs. These Regulations effect the continued application of the provisions of Part 18 of the 1985 Act to LLPs.

The Impact Assessment prepared for the Limited Liability Partnerships (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2009 was based on those regulations as if these amending Regulations had effect. It is available from the Department for Business, Innovation and Skills, Corporate Law and Governance Directorate, 1 Victoria Street, London SW1H 0ET. It is also available electronically at http://www.bis.gov.uk/bbf/llp/page39897.html. Copies have also been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament. No separate Impact Assessment has therefore been prepared for these Regulations.
