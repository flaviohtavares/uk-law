# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 976 (C. 64)

RETAINED EU LAW REFORM

The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Regulations 2024

Made

17th September 2024

Coming into force

18th September 2024

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(3) and (4)(a) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (“**the Act**”)[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is a “**relevant national authority**” for the purposes of section 22(4) of the Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the day after these Regulations are made.

## 2. Revocation of Regulations

The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2024[^f00003] are revoked.

Justin Madders

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department for Business and Trade

17th September 2024

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2024 ([S.I. 2024/714](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/714)) (“**the Commencement Regulations**”). The Commencement Regulations were made to commence section 6 of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act [2023 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/28) (“**the Act**”) on 1st October 2024 and create appropriate saving provisions. The revocation of the Commencement Regulations by these Regulations means that section 6 of the Act will no longer come into force on 1st October 2024.
