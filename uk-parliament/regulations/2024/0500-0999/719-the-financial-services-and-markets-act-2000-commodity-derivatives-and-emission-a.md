# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) (Amendment) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 719

FINANCIAL SERVICES AND MARKETS

The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) (Amendment) Order 2024

Made

28th May 2024

Laid before Parliament

29th May 2024

Coming into force

31st December 2024

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(1) and (5) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) (Amendment) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 31st December 2024.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) Order 2023

In article 2 (amendment of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) Order 2023[^f00002], omit paragraphs (2) to (4).

Amanda Solloway

Amanda Milling

Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury

28th May 2024

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order omits amendments to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 ([S.I. 2001/544](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/544)), relating to the repeal of Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2017/592](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0592), in the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Commodity Derivatives and Emission Allowances) Order 2023 ([S.I. 2023/548](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/548)) (“**the Order**”). The Order is not yet in force.

A de minimis impact assessment of the effect of this instrument is available from HM Treasury, 1 Horse Guards Road, London, SW1A 2HQ and is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
