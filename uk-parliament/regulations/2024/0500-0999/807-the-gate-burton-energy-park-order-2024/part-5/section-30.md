# The Gate Burton Energy Park Order 2024, section 30

> Text as at 2024-11-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Apparatus and rights of statutory undertakers in temporarily closed streets[^key-78fd550fd0d5090ee22671a05ba9ee06]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a street is altered or diverted or its use is temporarily prohibited or restricted under article ‎8 (street works), article ‎9 (power to alter layout, etc., of streets), article ‎10 (construction and maintenance of altered streets) or article ‎11 (temporary closure of streets and public rights of way) any statutory undertaker whose apparatus is under, in, on, along or across the street has the same powers and rights in respect of that apparatus, subject to Schedule 14 (protective provisions), as if this Order had not been made.
