# The Gate Burton Energy Park Order 2024, section 39

> Text as at 2024-11-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. No double recovery[^c-356843cac194a5e6f3c542216f240bbb]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
