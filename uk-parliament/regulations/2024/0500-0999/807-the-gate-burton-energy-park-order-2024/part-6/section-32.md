# The Gate Burton Energy Park Order 2024, section 32

> Text as at 2024-11-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Benefit of the Order[^c-2585258d62ad3f0123b09850d477f894]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to article ‎33 (consent to transfer the benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.

(2) Sub-paragraph ‎(1) does not apply to Work No. 4C in respect of which the provisions of this Order are for the benefit of the undertaker and National Grid.
