# The Insolvency (England and Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 622

Insolvency

Individuals

The Insolvency (England and Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2024

Made

8th May 2024

Laid before Parliament

10th May 2024

Coming into force

28th June 2024

The Lord Chancellor has consulted the committee existing for the purposes of section 413 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00001].

The Lord Chancellor, with the concurrence of the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 412[^f00002] of that Act, makes the following Rules.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and transitional provision

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Insolvency (England and Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2024 and shall come into force on 28<sup>th</sup> June 2024.

(2) These Rules extend to England and Wales only.

(3) The amendments made by the Rules apply in relation to an application for a debt relief order made under section 251B(1)[^f00003] of the Insolvency Act 1986 on or after the date on which these Rules come into force.

## 2. Amendments to the Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016

(1) The Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In rule 9.9(1)(a)(ii), for “£2,000” substitute “£4,000”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016 ([S.I. 2016/1024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1024)). Rule 9.9 of those Rules lists property the official receiver must disregard for the purposes of determining the value of a person’s property in connection with a debt relief order. This includes a single domestic motor vehicle whose maximum potential realisable value is less than £2,000. These Rules change that amount to £4,000.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared and is available alongside these Rules at www.legislation.gov.uk.
