# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 790

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2024

Made

10th July 2024

Coming into force

11th July 2024

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of July 2024

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2024 and comes into force on 11th July 2024

## 2. Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — The persons appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 11th July 2024

Article 2

- Stephen John Bailey
- Mark Care
- Monique Natasha Lindsay
- Lisa Kelly Smith
- Claire Stylianides

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
