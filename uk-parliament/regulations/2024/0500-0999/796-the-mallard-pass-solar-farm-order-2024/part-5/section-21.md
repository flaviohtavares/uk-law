# The Mallard Pass Solar Farm Order 2024, section 21

> Text as at 2024-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-095e66a852c1eef8f7fd8baf359d363a]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00046] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (c) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated.
