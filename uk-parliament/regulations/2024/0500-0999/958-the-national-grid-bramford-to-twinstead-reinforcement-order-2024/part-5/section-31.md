# The National Grid (Bramford to Twinstead Reinforcement) Order 2024, section 31

> Text as at 2025-04-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-933bc9c21d7f97ce7ebbde26deb439bc]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00029] (minerals) are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
