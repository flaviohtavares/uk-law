# The National Grid (Bramford to Twinstead Reinforcement) Order 2024, section 53

> Text as at 2025-04-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. No double recovery[^key-ee172c6da79a3bd8b1770262018d05d1]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, contract or deed or rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
