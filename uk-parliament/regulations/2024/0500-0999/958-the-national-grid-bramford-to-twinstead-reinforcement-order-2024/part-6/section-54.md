## 54. Application, disapplication and modification of legislative provisions[^key-c1c3e31bb606ff24996cbee86fd75550]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 15 (public general legislation) to this Order, which makes provision applying, modifying and excluding statutory provisions which relate to matters for which provision may be made by this Order, has effect.
