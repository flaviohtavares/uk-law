# The National Grid (Bramford to Twinstead Reinforcement) Order 2024, SCHEDULE 15

> Text as at 2025-04-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 15 — PUBLIC GENERAL LEGISLATION

*Extent: E+W*

Article 54

### 1. Hedgerow Regulations 1997[^c-82432a2eec1d9a00b2bedf45ab54680f]

For the purposes of regulation 6(1) of the Hedgerow Regulations 1997[^f00055], the removal of any hedgerow to which those regulations apply is permitted if it is required for the purposes set out in article 47 (felling or lopping) of this Order.

### 2. Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976[^c-5a8dffb19f4e219e5073f2bee9e72b7d]

Section 42 of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976[^f00056] (certain future local Acts etc. to be subject to the planning enactments etc. except as otherwise provided) will not apply to the extent that it would make provisions of this Order authorising the authorised development subject to other provisions.

### 3. Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017[^c-564b2c5cf6ccf98de7517c96391a3301]

The provisions of the Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017[^c-7f2cff19752c0573884d6cd675c351ef][^f00057] in so far as they relate to temporary possession of land under articles 25 (temporary use of land by National Grid), 26 (temporary use of land by UKPN) and 27 (temporary use of land for maintaining the authorised development) of this Order will not apply.

### 4. Building Act 1984[^c-029890017a810738fd1e52bca8706ca0]

Nothing in Part 1 of the Building Act 1984[^f00058] with respect to building regulations, and nothing in any building regulations, will apply in relation to a building used, altered or demolished, or intended for use, alteration, or demolition, by the undertaker for the purposes of the authorised development before completion of construction.
