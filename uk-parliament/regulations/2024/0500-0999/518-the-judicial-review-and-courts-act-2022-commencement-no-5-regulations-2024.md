# The Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 518 (C. 33)

Registration Of Births, Deaths & Marriages, Etc.

The Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2024

Made

15th April 2024

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(4) of the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Provision coming into force

Section 44 of the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (provision of information to registrar when investigation discontinued) comes into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force section 44 of the Judicial Review and Courts Act [2022 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/35) (“**the Act**”). They are the fifth commencement regulations to be made under the Act.

Section 44 of the Act inserts paragraph (4) in section 23 of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953. New paragraph (4) enables the registrar to register a death on the basis of information furnished by the senior coroner, on request by the registrar, provided the senior coroner has discontinued their investigation and authorised the disposal of the body. This provision may be relied upon by the registrar where a qualified informant (ordinarily a family member) is unable or unwilling to provide such information, and the senior coroner has established the relevant information as part of their initial investigation.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
