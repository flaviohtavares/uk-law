# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Purpose of Part 5

Pursuant to paragraphs 7, 8 and 30 of Schedule 5 to the Act[^f00014], the initiation and conduct of bilateral safeguarding investigations are to be carried out in accordance with this Part.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: Paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 was amended by paragraph 9 of Schedule 19 to the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, and modified by paragraph 5 of Schedule 5A to the Act.
