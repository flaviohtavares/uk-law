# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Market share requirement

For the purpose of paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 to the Act, the market share requirement is met where the Secretary of State is satisfied that UK producers' “share” of the market is—

- (a) at least 1 per cent, or
- (b) such other higher share as the Secretary of State considers appropriate taking into account the goods and the particular market for those goods.
