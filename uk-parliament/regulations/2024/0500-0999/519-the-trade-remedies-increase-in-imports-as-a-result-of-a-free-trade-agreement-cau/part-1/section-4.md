# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application

In applying the provisions of these Regulations, the TRA and the Secretary of State must have regard to any relevant free trade agreement.
