# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Determination to revoke

(1) This regulation applies if the TRA determines the application to goods of a definitive bilateral safeguarding remedy should be revoked.

(2) The TRA must recommend to the Secretary of State that the application to goods of the definitive bilateral safeguarding remedy is revoked.
