# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Investigation in light of an international dispute decision

The Secretary of State may direct the TRA to investigate whether the application to goods of a definitive bilateral safeguarding remedy should be maintained, varied, or revoked in light of an international dispute decision.
