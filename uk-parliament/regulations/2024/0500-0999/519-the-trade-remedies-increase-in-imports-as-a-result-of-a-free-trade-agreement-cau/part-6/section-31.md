# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Purpose of Part 6

Reviews by the TRA of the continuing application, including variation, revocation, extension and replacement, of a definitive bilateral safeguarding remedy to goods pursuant to paragraph 21 of Schedule 5 to the Act[^f00016] are subject to the provisions of this Part.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: Paragraph 21 was amended by paragraph 12 of Schedule 19 to the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023.
