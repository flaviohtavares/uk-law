# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports as a Result of a Free Trade Agreement Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) Regulations 2024, section 53

## 53. Appeals to the Upper Tribunal

In regulation 17—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “determination” insert “or decision”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) after sub-paragraph (a), insert—

    > - (aa) Part 1A contains a list of decisions made under Schedule 5 as applied by Schedule 5A (bilateral safeguarding remedies);

    ;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (d), insert—

    > - (e) Part 5 contains a list of decisions made under the Bilateral Safeguards Regulations.

    .
