# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. New paragraph 9A

After paragraph 9 insert—

> **9A. Misuse of the cascade**
>
> A person must not promote or facilitate any purported use of the cascade which is not in accordance with this Schedule.
