# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Amendment to paragraph 1

In paragraph 1 (administration under the cascade)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (4)—
  - (i) for the words “Any pharmacologically active substances” substitute “All substances”;
  - (ii) at the end insert “or substances which do not fall within the scope of Regulation [(EC) No 470/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0470) of the European Parliament and of the Council”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (4) insert—

  > (5) Where a substance mentioned in sub-paragraph (4) is administered, the maximum residue limits established in accordance with Regulation [(EC) No 470/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0470) of the European Parliament and of the Council must be complied with.
