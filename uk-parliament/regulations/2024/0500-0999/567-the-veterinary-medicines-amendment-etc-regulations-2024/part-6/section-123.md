# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. New paragraph 6A

After paragraph 6 (administration by veterinary surgeons from other countries) insert—

> **6A. Administration of autogenous vaccines**
>
> (1) An autogenous vaccine may only be administered to animals in exceptional circumstances where no suitable immunological veterinary medicinal product has been authorised in relation to the target species and indication.
>
> (2) Where a vaccine is used in accordance with sub-paragraph (1) it must be administered in accordance with a prescription under the cascade.
