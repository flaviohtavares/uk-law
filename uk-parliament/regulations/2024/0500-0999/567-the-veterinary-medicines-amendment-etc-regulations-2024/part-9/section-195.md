# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 195

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 195. New paragraph 54A

After paragraph 54 (provision of advice) insert—

> **54A. Provision of scientific advice**
>
> The fee for an application for written advice from the Secretary of State in relation to scientific matters is £4,487.
