# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 178

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 178. New paragraphs 30A and 30B

After paragraph 30 insert—

> **30A. Autogenous vaccines**
>
> (1) The fee for the scientific assessment of an authorisation (or an application for authorisation) to manufacture an autogenous vaccine is £6,962.
>

> **30B. Assessment of a variation of an authorisation to manufacture an autogenous vaccine**
>
> The fee for the scientific assessment of an application for the variation of an authorisation to manufacture an autogenous vaccine is—
>
> - (a) £2,895 if the variation requires complex scientific or pharmaceutical assessment;
> - (b) £885 if the variation requires simple scientific or pharmaceutical assessment;
> - (c) £455 in relation to an administrative variation.
