# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 189

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 189. Amendment to paragraph 42

For paragraph 42 (inspection of wholesale dealer’s premises) substitute—

> **42. Inspection of a wholesale dealer’s sites**
>
> The fee for inspection of a wholesale dealer’s site is—
>
> - (a) £1,177; or
> - (b) £877 if—
>   - (i) the authorisation only relates to products classified as AVM-GSL or homeopathic remedies; or
>   - (ii) the authorisation only relates to products marketed under Schedule 6 (exemptions for small pet animals).
