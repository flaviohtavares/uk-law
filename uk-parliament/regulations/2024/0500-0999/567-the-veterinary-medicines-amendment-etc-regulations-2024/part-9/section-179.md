> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 179. Amendment to paragraph 31

For paragraph 31 (annual fees) substitute—

> **31. Annual fee (manufacturing authorisations)**
>
> An annual fee of £575 is payable in respect of each manufacturing authorisation held.
