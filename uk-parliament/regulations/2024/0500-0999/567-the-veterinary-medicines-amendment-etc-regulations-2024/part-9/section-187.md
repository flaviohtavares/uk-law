# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 187

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 187. Amendment to paragraph 40

For paragraph 40 (variation of wholesale dealer’s authorisation) substitute—

> **40. Variation of a wholesale dealer’s authorisation**
>
> The fee for an application to vary a wholesale dealer’s authorisation is—
>
> - (a) £265 if the variation requires scientific or pharmaceutical assessment;
> - (b) £105 for a change of ownership or other administrative variation.
