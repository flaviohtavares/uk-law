# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 165

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 165. New paragraph 7A

After paragraph 7 insert—

> **7A. Application for a marketing authorisation for specific applications**
>
> The fee for an application for a marketing authorisation which involves one or more of the following is £45,000—
>
> - (a) any biotechnical process involving recombinant DNA or the controlled expression of genes;
> - (b) a veterinary medicinal product containing a new active substance;
> - (c) a biopharmaceutical product.
