# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 191

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 191. Amendment to paragraph 44

In paragraph 44 (inspection fees for feedingstuffs in Great Britain)—

- (a) in the words before the table for “establishments” substitute “premises”;
- (b) for the table substitute—

  > Inspection Fees
  >
  > | Type of premises inspected | Fee payable (£) |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Manufacturer of a specified feed additive (SFA) | 1,610 |
  > | Manufacturer of an intermediate feedingstuff (including balancers) containing a medicinal premix or an SFA | 976 |
  > | Manufacturer of a feedingstuff for sale containing—<br>a medicinal premix and/or an SFA, and/or<br>an intermediate feedingstuff containing a medicinal premix or an SFA | 841 |
  > | Manufacturer of a feedingstuff for feeding to their own animals only, containing—<br>a medicinal premix and/or an SFA incorporated at a rate of at least 2kg/t, and/or<br>an intermediate feedingstuff containing a medicinal premix and/or an SFA incorporated at a rate of at least 2kg/t | 476 |
  > | Distributor or trader of Schedule 5 products<br>(A distributor of specified feed additives, or intermediate feedingstuffs containing specified feed additives or medicinal premixes; or feedingstuffs containing a medicinal premix) | 350 |

  .
