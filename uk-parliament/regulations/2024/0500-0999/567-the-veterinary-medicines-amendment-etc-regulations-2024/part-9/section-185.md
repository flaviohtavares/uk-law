# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 185

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 185. Amendment to paragraph 38

For paragraph 38 (animal blood bank or equine stem cell centre authorisations) substitute—

> **38. Animal blood bank or non-food animal stem cell centre authorisations**
>
> (1) The fee for the inspection of a blood bank is—
>
> - (a) £3,212 for a site in the United Kingdom; and
> - (b) £4,507 for a site outside the United Kingdom.
>
> (2) The fee for the inspection of a non-food animal stem cell centre is—
>
> - (a) £2,142 for a site in the United Kingdom; and
> - (b) £3,436 for a site outside the United Kingdom

.
