# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 186

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 186. Amendment to paragraph 39

For paragraph 39 (application for wholesale dealer’s authorisation) substitute—

> **39. Application for a wholesale dealer’s authorisation**
>
> (1) The fee for an application for a wholesale dealer’s authorisation is £344.
>
> (2) Fees relating to an application for a wholesale dealer’s authorisation are payable with the application.
