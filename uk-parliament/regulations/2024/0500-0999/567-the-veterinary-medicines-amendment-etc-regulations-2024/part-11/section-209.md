# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 209

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 209. [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1234/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1234)

[Commission Regulation (EC) No 1234/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1234) concerning the examination of variations to the terms of marketing authorisations for medicinal products for human use and veterinary medicinal products[^f00013] is revoked.

## Annotations
