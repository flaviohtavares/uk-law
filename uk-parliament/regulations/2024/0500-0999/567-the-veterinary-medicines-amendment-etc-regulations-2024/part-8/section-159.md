# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 159

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 159. Amendment to paragraph 9

In paragraph 9 (adverse reactions)—

- (a) for “manufacturer, importer or retailer” substitute “manufacturer or importer”;
- (b) in paragraph (a) for “adverse reactions (as defined in paragraph 57 of Schedule 1)” substitute “adverse events”;
- (c) in paragraph (b) for “adverse reaction”, in both places it occurs, substitute “adverse event”;
- (d) omit sub-paragraph (2);
- (e) in the heading for “Adverse reactions” substitute “Adverse events”.
