# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Amendment to paragraph 10

In paragraph 10 (refusal or revocation of appointment)—

- (a) for “or revoke” substitute “, revoke, suspend or vary”;
- (b) for the heading substitute—

  > Refusal, revocation, suspension or variation of appointment
