# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Amendment to regulation 25

In regulation 25 (importation of an unauthorised veterinary medicinal product) after paragraph (6) insert—

> (6A) A pharmacist may supply a product to which paragraph (6) applies to a veterinary surgeon for the purposes mentioned in that paragraph notwithstanding paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3.
