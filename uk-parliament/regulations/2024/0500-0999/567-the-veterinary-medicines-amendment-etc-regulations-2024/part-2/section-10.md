# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Amendment to regulation 15

In regulation 15(3) (exemptions)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “Part 3” substitute “Part 2”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “Part 4” substitute “Part 2”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c)—
  - (i) omit “equine” in both places it occurs;
  - (ii) for “horses” substitute “non-food producing animals”;
  - (iii) for “Part 5” substitute “Part 2”.
