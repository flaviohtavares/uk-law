# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendment to regulation 7

In regulation 7(2) (prohibition of supply past expiry date) after “veterinary medicinal product” insert “(including a veterinary medicinal product which has been incorporated into a medicated feedingstuff or intermediate feedingstuff)”.
