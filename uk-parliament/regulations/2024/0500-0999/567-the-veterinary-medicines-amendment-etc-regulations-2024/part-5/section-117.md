# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Amendment to paragraph 23

In paragraph 23(1) (use of sheep dip) for the words from the beginning to “holds either” substitute “No person may use sheep dip which contains a veterinary medicinal product unless they hold, or they are acting under the supervision and in the presence of a person who holds, either”.
