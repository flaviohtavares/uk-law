# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Amendment to paragraph 10

In paragraph 10(1) (supply by a pharmacist)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), after “NFA-VPS” insert “, or prescribed under the cascade,”;
- (b) in paragraph (c) for “approved” substitute “authorised”.
