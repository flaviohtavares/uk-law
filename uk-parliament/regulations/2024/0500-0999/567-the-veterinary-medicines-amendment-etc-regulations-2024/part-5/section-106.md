# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Amendment to paragraph 13

In paragraph 13(2)(a) (supply for use under the cascade)—

- (a) for “veterinary surgery” substitute “veterinary practice premises”;
- (b) for “approved” substitute “authorised”.
