# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Amendment to paragraph 7

In paragraph 7 (duties when a product is prescribed or supplied)—

- (a) the existing text is renumbered as sub-paragraph (1);
- (b) in that sub-paragraph after “who prescribes” insert “a veterinary medicinal product under the cascade or”;
- (c) after that sub-paragraph insert—

  > (2) A person who prescribes antimicrobials must ensure that the product is prescribed for the most limited period that is consistent with the risk to be addressed.
