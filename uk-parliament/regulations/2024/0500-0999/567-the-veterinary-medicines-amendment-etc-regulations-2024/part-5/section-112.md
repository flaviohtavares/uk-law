# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Amendment to paragraph 19

For paragraph 19 (authorisation) substitute—

> **19. Periodic inspections and suspension etc. for lack of use**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State must, from time to time, inspect the sites from which wholesale dealing of veterinary medicinal products takes place pursuant to a wholesale dealer’s authorisation basing the frequency of the inspection on the risks associated with each site’s history and the nature of the products handled at the site.
>
> (2) The Secretary of State may suspend, vary or revoke a wholesale dealer’s authorisation if, in respect of any one of the sites covered by that authorisation, the holder does not deal in veterinary medicinal products from that site for five years.
