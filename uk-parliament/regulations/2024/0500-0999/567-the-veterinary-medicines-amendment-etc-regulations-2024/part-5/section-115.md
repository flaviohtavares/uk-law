# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Amendment to paragraph 21

In paragraph 21 (duties on holder of wholesale dealer’s authorisation)—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) comply with good distribution practice;

  ;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (c) (and the “and” following it);
- (c) in sub-paragraph (d), at the end insert—

  > ; and
  >
  > - (e) notify the Secretary of State (and in relation to paragraph (ii), the holder of the relevant marketing authorisation) where it has reason to suspect—
  >   - (i) a threat to the continued supply of a veterinary medicinal product;
  >   - (ii) that it has been offered veterinary medicinal products which are counterfeit

  .
