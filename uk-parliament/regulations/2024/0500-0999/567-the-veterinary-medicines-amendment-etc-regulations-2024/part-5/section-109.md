# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Amendment to paragraph 16

For paragraph 16 (application) substitute—

> **16. Wholesale dealer’s authorisation**
>
> No person may carry out any wholesale dealing in veterinary medicinal products otherwise than in accordance with an authorisation granted under paragraph 18(2) (a “wholesale dealer’s authorisation”).
