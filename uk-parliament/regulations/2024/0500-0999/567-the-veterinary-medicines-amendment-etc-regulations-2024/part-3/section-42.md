# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Amendment to paragraph 18

For paragraph 18 (place of establishment of applicant) substitute—

> **18. Place of establishment of applicant**
>
> Only an applicant established in the United Kingdom or in a country which the Secretary of State considers to have demonstrated equivalent standards to those in the United Kingdom may be granted (or hold) a marketing authorisation or a veterinary homeopathic registration.
