# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Amendment to paragraph 32

For paragraph 32 (duration and validity of marketing authorisation) substitute—

> **32. Duration and validity of marketing authorisation**
>
> Subject to any power of revocation provided under these Regulations a marketing authorisation is valid indefinitely.
