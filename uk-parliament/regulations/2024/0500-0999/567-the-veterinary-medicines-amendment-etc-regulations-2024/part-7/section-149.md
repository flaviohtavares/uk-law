# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 149

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 149. New paragraph 26A

After paragraph 26 insert—

> **26A. Unused and expired medicated feedingstuffs**
>
> No person may feed medicated feedingstuffs which have passed their expiry date to an animal.
