# The Veterinary Medicines (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2024, section 129

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 129. Amendment to paragraph 3

In paragraph 3(3) (enforcement of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831)) for “a premixture” substitute “an intermediate feedingstuff”.
