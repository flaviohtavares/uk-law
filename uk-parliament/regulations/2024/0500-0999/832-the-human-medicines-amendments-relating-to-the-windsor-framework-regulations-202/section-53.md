# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 53

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Amendment to regulation 65C[^key-45421807db53f6484cd2e96676bf450f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 65C (variation of a UKMA(GB))[^f00064], in the heading, and in paragraphs (1) and (3), after “UKMA(GB)” insert “or a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
