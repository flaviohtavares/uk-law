# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 48

## 48. Amendment to regulation 60[^c-beccc24895d8936cf84824f51f8bfd6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 60(9) (conditions of UK marketing authorisation or parallel import licence: exceptional circumstances)[^f00059], for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
