# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 30

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Amendment to regulation 50F[^c-b1b855920f6124f319c13815a4cdc508]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50F(1) (other applications including paediatric indications)[^f00041]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), at the end insert “or a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
