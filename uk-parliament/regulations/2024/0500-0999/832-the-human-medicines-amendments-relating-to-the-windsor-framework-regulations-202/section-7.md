# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 7

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Amendment to regulation 8[^key-f99693aca881def520a31be600eb3b52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 8 (general interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) omit the definition of “advanced therapy medicinal product”;
  - (ii) in the definition of “Annex I to the 2001 Directive”[^f00007], after “UKMA(GB)” insert “or UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
  - (iii) omit the definition of “Commission Regulation 2016/161”[^f00008];
  - (iv) in the definition of “conditional marketing authorisation”[^f00009], for “UKMA (GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
  - (v) omit the definition of “healthcare institution”[^f00010];
  - (vi) in the definition of “marketing authorisation”, omit paragraph (b) and the “or” which precedes it;
  - (vii) in the definition of “name”, omit paragraph (b) (but not the “and” which follows it);
  - (viii) in the definition of “pharmacovigilance system”[^f00011], omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
  - (ix) in the definition of “pharmacovigilance system master file”[^f00012], omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
  - (x) in the definition of “post-authorisation safety study”, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
  - (xi) omit the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No 1394/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1394)”;
  - (xii) in the definition of “UK marketing authorisation”[^f00013], in paragraph (a), after “authorisation” insert “, within the definition of UKMA(UK)(Category 1) or UKMA(UK)(Category 2),”;
  - (xiii) after the definition of “UK marketing authorisation”, insert—

    > - “**UKMA(UK)(Category 1)**” means a marketing authorisation of the description in regulation 49(1ZB);
    > - “**UKMA(UK)(Category 2)**” means a marketing authorisation of the description in regulation 49(1ZC);

    ;
  - (xiv) in the definition of “under the unfettered access route”, in paragraph (a), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) in paragraph (9)[^f00014], for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
