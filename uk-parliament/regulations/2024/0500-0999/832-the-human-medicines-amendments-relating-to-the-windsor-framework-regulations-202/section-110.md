# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 110

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Amendment to regulation 257[^key-a2798c39d395a4bcb2711f71a4547a4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 257 (packaging requirements: general)[^f00118],—

- (a) in paragraph (6), omit “where the product is for sale or supply in Great Britain only”;
- (b) in paragraph (8), omit “for sale or supply in Great Britain only”;
- (c) after paragraph (8) insert—

  > (9) This regulation is subject to regulation 257AA.
