# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 76

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Amendment to regulation 181[^c-e1d239c2ee1cc19ae6cfc9974dc33f97]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 181(1) (delegation of obligations under this Part)[^f00086], after “Northern Ireland” insert “pursuant to a UKMA(NI) or a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
