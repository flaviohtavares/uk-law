# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 141

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Amendment to Schedule 17[^key-7851920f9331a4463f254d1671df9dc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 17 (exemption for sale, supply or administration by certain persons)[^f00148]—

- (a) in the table in Part 1, in column 1 in entry 10, omit “EU marketing authorisations”;
- (b) in the table in Part 4, in columns 1 and 2 in entry 9, omit “EU marketing authorisation”.
