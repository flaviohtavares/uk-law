# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 45

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Amendment to regulation 58A[^key-66cd469c11bc4af555dda8edaf7b8aac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 58A (paediatric rewards)[^f00056]—

- (a) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) at the end of sub-paragraph (a), for “; or” substitute a comma;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (b);
- (b) in paragraph (4)(b), for “regulation 51A(6), under regulation 51A(12)” substitute “regulation 51B(6), under regulation 51B(7)”;
- (c) in paragraph (8), for “regulation 51A(1) and (6)” substitute “regulation 51B(1) and (6)”.
