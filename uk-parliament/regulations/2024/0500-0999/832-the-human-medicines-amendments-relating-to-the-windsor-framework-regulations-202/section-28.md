# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 28

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Amendment to regulation 50A[^c-27f8906ecf32abeae2205b6d24140699]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50A (requirement for certain applications to include results of paediatric investigation plan)[^f00039]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), in sub-paragraph (a), omit “UKMA(GB) or”;
- (b) in paragraph (7), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (c) in paragraph (8), for “a UKMA(UK)” substitute “a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
