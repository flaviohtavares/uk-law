# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 134

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Amendment to Schedule 1[^key-8a415889591478201f9003020dcb7f7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 (further provisions for classification of medicinal products)[^f00141]—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (e)(i), omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (f)(i), omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
  - (iii) in sub-paragraph (g)(i), omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
- (b) in paragraph 4, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”.
