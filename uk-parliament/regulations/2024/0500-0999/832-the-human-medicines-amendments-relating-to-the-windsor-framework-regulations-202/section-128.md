# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 128

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Amendment to regulation 327[^c-ded2ad195dcccf120de9a38681b5dc23]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 327 (powers of inspection, sampling and seizure)[^f00135]—

- (a) omit paragraph (1)(c)(va);
- (b) omit paragraph (2)(h);
- (c) omit paragraph (4A);
- (d) in paragraph (5)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “, (g) or (h)” substitute “or (g)”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), omit “or (4A)”.
