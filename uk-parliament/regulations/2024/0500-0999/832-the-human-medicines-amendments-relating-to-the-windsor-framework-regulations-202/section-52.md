# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 52

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Amendment to regulation 64[^c-215c4376231d5ded910513b6b0964ce3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 64(4)(d) (duties of licensing authority in connection with determination)[^f00063]—

- (a) in paragraph (i), for “or UKMA(UK)” substitute “or UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) in paragraph (ii), after “UKMA(GB)” insert “or UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
