# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 119

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Amendment to regulation 269A[^key-64c839ae05bc38ef6e582f379ff6dcca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 269A (offences relating to packaging and package leaflets in Northern Ireland: other persons)[^f00126]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a), omit “, Article 9 of Commission Regulation 2016/161”.
