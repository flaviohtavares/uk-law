# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 139

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139. Amendment to Schedule 9A[^key-c73c0ad6aa4b3fbecbfd9d7819185849]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 9A (meaning of terms used in the orphan criteria and in regulation 58D)[^f00146], in each of the following provisions, for “Great Britain” substitute “the United Kingdom”—

- (a) the heading of paragraph (1);
- (b) paragraph 1(1), 1(2)(a) and (c);
- (c) paragraph 2(1), 2(2)(c), (d), (g) and (h);
- (d) paragraph 3(1) and 3(2)(a) in both places.
