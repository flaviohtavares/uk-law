# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 20

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Amendment to regulation 44[^key-eb229f1d35c02cf2e0286e3290bf85df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 44 (requirement for wholesale dealers to deal only with specified persons)[^f00027]—

- (a) in paragraph (6)—
  - (i) at the end of sub-paragraph (d), for “; and” substitute a full stop;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (e);
- (b) in paragraph (8), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
