# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 10

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Amendment to regulation 17[^key-d2d418fdf0f218bcdfb0e977cc1d3290]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 17 (manufacturing of medicinal products)[^f00017]—

- (a) in paragraph (4), omit sub-paragraph (b) and the “or” which precedes it;
- (b) in paragraph (7), omit sub-paragraph (b) and the “and” which precedes it;
- (c) after paragraph (9)(c), insert “and”;
- (d) omit paragraph (9)(e) and the “and” which precedes it.
