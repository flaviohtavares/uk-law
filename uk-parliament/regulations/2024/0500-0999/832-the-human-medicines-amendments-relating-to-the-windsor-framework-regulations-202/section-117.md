# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 117

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Amendment to regulation 260[^key-513b4e83e1c5703a45f2ec85d02c8f55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 260 (package leaflets)[^f00124]—

- (a) in paragraph (1A), omit “for sale or supply in Great Britain only”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), omit “for sale or supply in Great Britain only”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), omit “, EU marketing authorisation”.
