# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 33

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Amendment to regulation 50I[^c-6f225f6b948461b97f0e573dd860db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50I (applications relating to conditional marketing authorisations)[^f00044]—

- (a) in the heading, omit “for sale or supply in Great Britain only”;
- (b) in paragraph (1), for “a UKMA(GB)” substitute “a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
