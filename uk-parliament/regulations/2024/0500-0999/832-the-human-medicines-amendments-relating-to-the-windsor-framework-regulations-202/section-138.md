# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 138

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Amendment to Schedule 8C[^c-9f7c510206a1df8726ce7cf34fb0eaaa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 8C (material to accompany an application under the unfettered access route)[^f00145]—

- (a) in paragraph 1, omit “EU marketing authorisation or”;
- (b) in paragraph 2, omit “EU marketing authorisation or”;
- (c) in paragraph 3, omit “EU marketing authorisation or”.
