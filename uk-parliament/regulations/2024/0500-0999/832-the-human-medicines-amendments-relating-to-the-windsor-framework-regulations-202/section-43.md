# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 43

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Amendment to regulation 55[^key-d8a5ef0ac670aa5f96902a630760077a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 55 (applications relating to new combinations of active substances)[^f00054]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(b)—
  - (i) omit paragraph (ii) (but not the “or” which follows it);
  - (ii) in paragraph (iii), for sub-paragraph (bb) substitute—

    > - (bb) a UKMA(NI).

    ;
- (b) omit paragraph (3).
