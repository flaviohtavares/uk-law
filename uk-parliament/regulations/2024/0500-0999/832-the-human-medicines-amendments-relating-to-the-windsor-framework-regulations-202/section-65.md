# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 65

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Revocation of regulations 81 to 88[^c-a0fe97b97bcab506d0aef77bda0a14c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit regulations 81 to 88 (offences relating to EU marketing authorisations)[^f00076].

## Annotations

[^c-a0fe97b97bcab506d0aef77bda0a14c8]: Commencement: [Reg. 65](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/832/regulation/65) in force at 1.1.2025, see [reg. 1(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/832/regulation/1/1)

[^f00076]: Footnote: Regulations 81 to 88 were to have been revoked by [S.I. 2019/775](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/775) but amendments to that instrument by [S.I. 2020/1488](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1488) retained them, in a manner limited by regulation A81 which [S.I. 2020/1488](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1488) inserted.
