# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 108

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Amendment to regulation 251[^key-6d1f01a18313c385f71d1e7316f0d174]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 251(7) (compliance with standards specified in certain publications: definition of “relevant marketing authorisation”)[^f00116]—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (a);
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
