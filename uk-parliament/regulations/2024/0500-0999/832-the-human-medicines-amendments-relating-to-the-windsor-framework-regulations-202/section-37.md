# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 37

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Amendment to regulation 52[^c-e73cee0029557a49b9915cdf1ac30f99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 52(3) (application for UKMA(NI) relating to certain medicinal products that do not qualify as generic etc.)[^f00048], for “Paragraphs (2) to (4) of regulation 51 apply to the application as they apply” substitute “Paragraph (2) of regulation 51 applies to the application as it applies”.
