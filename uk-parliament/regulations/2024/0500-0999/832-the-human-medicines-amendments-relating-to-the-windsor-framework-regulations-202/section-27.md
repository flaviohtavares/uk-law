# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 27

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Amendment to regulation 50[^key-a6e8d5906bf354002ed69113b00aaba4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50 (accompanying material)[^f00038]—

- (a) in paragraph (4)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in paragraph (5A)—
  - (i) omit “in respect of Great Britain”;
  - (ii) for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (c) in paragraph (6), omit sub-paragraphs (aa), (ba) and (ca).
