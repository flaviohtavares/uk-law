# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 99

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Amendment to regulation 210A[^key-b2761a0abc52582e8c6154a9c4f446cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 210A (offences in relation to pharmacovigilance obligations under the Implementing Regulation and Schedule 12A)[^f00108]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (aa), after “UKMA(GB)” insert “, UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) in paragraph (2A), in the opening words, for “(1)(a)” substitute “(1)(aa)”.
