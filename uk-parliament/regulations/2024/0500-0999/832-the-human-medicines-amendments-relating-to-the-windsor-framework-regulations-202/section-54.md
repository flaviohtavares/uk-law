# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 54

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Amendment to regulation 66[^c-9a36ce80bdf1e105f1154fbf733e88b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 66(2) (application for renewal of authorisation)[^f00065]—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) a UK marketing authorisation, must, subject to sub-paragraph (b), be established in the UK or an EEA State; and

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)—
  - (i) for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) omit paragraph (ii);
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (c).
