## 78. Amendment to regulation 186[^key-65dbddad6423e811eddd577b44813129]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 186(1) (reporting obligations on the licensing authority)[^f00088]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (d), for paragraph (ii) substitute—

  > - (ii) a UKMA(UK)(Category 2),

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (e), for paragraph (ii) substitute—

  > - (ii) a UKMA(UK)(Category 2),

  .
