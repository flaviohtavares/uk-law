# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 31

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Amendment to regulation 50G[^key-558b721e468008050f75d15efffc81c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50G (applications relating to orphan medicinal products)[^f00042]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) at the end of sub-paragraph (a), for “, and” substitute a full stop;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (b);
- (b) in paragraph (2), in both places, for “Great Britain” substitute “the United Kingdom”.
