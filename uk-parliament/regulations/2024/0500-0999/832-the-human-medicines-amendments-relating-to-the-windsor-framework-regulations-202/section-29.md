# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 29

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Amendment to regulation 50E[^key-70c4ca5acaa64bca47fbef9bf875df28]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50E (application for paediatric use marketing authorisation)[^f00040]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), omit “UKMA(GB) or”;
- (b) in paragraph (5), for “a UKMA(UK)” substitute “a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
