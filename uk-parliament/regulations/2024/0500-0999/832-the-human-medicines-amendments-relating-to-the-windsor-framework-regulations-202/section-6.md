# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 6

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Amendment to regulation 5[^c-3a4de5c1572b7a17dfb7477668abb0dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 5 (classification of medicinal products)[^f00006]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) at the end of sub-paragraph (a), for “; or” substitute a full stop;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (b);
- (b) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) omit sub-paragraph (b);
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (d) omit “or (b)”;
- (c) in paragraph (5)—
  - (i) omit sub-paragraph (b);
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (d) omit “or (b)”.
