# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 92

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Amendment to regulation 201[^key-9ff6b77d72d937d4ea7ffa7d584b35e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 201(2) (submission and evaluation of final study reports for required studies)[^f00102]—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), after “a UKMA(GB)” insert “or UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)(b), for “in all other cases” substitute “where the study was conducted in the EU or an EEA State”.
