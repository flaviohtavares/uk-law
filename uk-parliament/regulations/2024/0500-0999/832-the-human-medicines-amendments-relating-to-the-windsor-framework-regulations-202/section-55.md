# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 55

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Amendment to regulation 67[^c-adee347792250acaf6add45e0300443f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 67 (failure to place on the market etc.)[^f00066], in paragraphs (1) and (2) omit “or, in the case of a UKMA(GB) granted after an application under the unfettered access route, in Great Britain”.
