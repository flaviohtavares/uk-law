# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 91

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Amendment to regulation 200[^key-8ec0853383ac7857b8548c96320b5afe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 200 (amendment to study protocols for required studies)[^f00101]—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), after “a UKMA(GB)” insert “or UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)(b), for “in all other cases” substitute “where the study is being conducted in the EU or an EEA State”.
