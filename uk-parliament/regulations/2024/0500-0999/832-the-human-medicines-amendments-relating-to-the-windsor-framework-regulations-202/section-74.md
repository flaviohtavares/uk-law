# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 74

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Amendment to regulation 177[^c-bb0840a309428d5440b2b4be197758b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 177 (application of Part and interpretation)[^f00084]—

- (a) in paragraph (1A), for “UKMA(GB) ora THR(GB)” substitute “UKMA(GB), UKMA(UK)(Category 1) or a THR(GB)”;
- (b) omit paragraph (4);
- (c) in paragraph (5), in the definition of “signal”, after “UKMA(GB)” insert “, UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
