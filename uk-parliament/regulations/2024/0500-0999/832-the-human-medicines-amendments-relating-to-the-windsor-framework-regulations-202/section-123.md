## 123. Amendment to regulation 281[^key-2219e157297c86f390fdaf5ecce8c853]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) insert “or” at the end of sub-paragraph (c);
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (e) and the “or” which precedes it.
