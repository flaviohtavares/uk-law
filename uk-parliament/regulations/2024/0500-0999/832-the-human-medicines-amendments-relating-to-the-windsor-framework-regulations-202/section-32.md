# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 32

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Amendment to regulation 50H[^c-1212a379d0a9da6b835ccb6bcfdf81be]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 50H (applications relating to advanced therapy medicinal products)[^f00043], in paragraphs (1) and (3), for “a UKMA(GB)”, substitute “a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
