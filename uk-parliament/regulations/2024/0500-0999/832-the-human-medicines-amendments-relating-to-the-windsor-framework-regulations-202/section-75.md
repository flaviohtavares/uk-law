# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 75

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Amendment to regulation 180[^key-94f33ab987b3415a1e7bcd27197bc462]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 180 (obligation on licensing authority to audit pharmacovigilance system)[^f00085]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “in Great Britain” substitute “pursuant to a UKMA(GB) or a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in paragraph (1A), after “Northern Ireland” insert “pursuant to a UKMA(NI) or a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
