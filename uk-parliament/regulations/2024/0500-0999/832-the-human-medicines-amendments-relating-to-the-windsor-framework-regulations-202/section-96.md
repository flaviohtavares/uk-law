# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 96

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Amendment to regulation 205A[^c-c5ffa9754b70dcedcbf90cbd1950ae57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 205A (further obligations in respect of pharmacovigilance activities)[^f00106]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “a UKMA(GB)” insert “, UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), omit “in respect of Great Britain”.
