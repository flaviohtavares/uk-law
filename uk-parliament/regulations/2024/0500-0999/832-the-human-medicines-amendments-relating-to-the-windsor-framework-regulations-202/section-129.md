# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 129

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Amendment to regulation 331[^c-7bc6652b661879f87cae99e788deaa0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 331(1) (findings and reports of inspections)[^f00136]—

- (a) in the opening words, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
