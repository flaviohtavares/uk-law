# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 18

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Amendment to regulation 43[^c-c6b68299596d7191ac20fcfb109f57d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 43 (obligations of licence holder)[^f00025], omit—

- (a) “, and EU marketing authorisation” in paragraph (5)(a)(ii);
- (b) “, EU marketing authorisation” in paragraph (7)(b)(ii)(bb);
- (c) paragraph (7)(c)(vii);
- (d) paragraph (10);
- (e) paragraph (11);
- (f) “or EU marketing authorisation holder” in paragraph (13).
