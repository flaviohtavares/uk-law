# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 60

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Amendment to regulation 76[^c-df39e31d4a92e717b665d27cbb63dd74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 76(2) (obligation in relation to product information)[^f00071]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), after “UKMA(GB)” insert “or a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
