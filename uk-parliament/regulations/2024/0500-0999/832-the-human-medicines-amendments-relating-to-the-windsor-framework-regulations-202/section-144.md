# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 144

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Amendment to Schedule 30[^c-a2466f4a2772c003f64ceb760a655116]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 30 (particulars for advertisements to persons qualified to prescribe or supply)[^f00151]—

- (a) in paragraph 1, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
- (b) in paragraph 2, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”;
- (c) in paragraph 6, omit “, EU marketing authorisation”.
