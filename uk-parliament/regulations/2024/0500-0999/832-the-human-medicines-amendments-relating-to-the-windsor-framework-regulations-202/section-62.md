# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 62

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amendment to regulation 79[^key-d7278adcd2f9d0eb6050c03ad22dceb6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 79(1) and (2) (failure to provide information on marketing authorisations to EMA)[^f00073] for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
