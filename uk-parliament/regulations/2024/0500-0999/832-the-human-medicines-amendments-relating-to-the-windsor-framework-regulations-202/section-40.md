# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 40

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Amendment to regulation 53[^c-f2d6a18daa8318c05a7757376ceea488]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 53(3) (application for UKMA(NI) relating to similar biological medicinal products)[^f00051], for “Paragraphs (2) to (4) of regulation 51 apply to the application as they apply” substitute “Paragraph (2) of regulation 51 applies to the application as it applies”.
