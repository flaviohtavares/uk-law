# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 57

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Amendment to regulation 71[^key-9ca42335d1cfd7f46e9ea2327df22db5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 71(1), for sub-paragraph (b) (withdrawal of medicinal product from the market)[^f00068] substitute—

> - (b) under regulation 69 the licensing authority suspends the use, sale, supply or offer for sale or supply within the United Kingdom of a product to which a UK marketing authorisation relates.

.
