# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 59

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Amendment to regulation 73[^c-4c66f40feed80b2df88b2006edb8cee9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 73(5C) (obligation to notify placing on the market etc.)[^f00070], for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”.
