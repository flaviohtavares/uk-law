# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 95

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Amendment to regulation 205[^key-62f76c0d99bfccb74d67a3ea4dac9b90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 205(2) (obligations on holders in relation to public announcements)[^f00105], in the opening words—

- (a) for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) after “UKMA(GB)” insert “, UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
