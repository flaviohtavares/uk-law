# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 86

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Amendment to regulation 195[^c-c66e703c440e85ee5e960a2640296113]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 195 (obligation on licensing authority to assess PSURs)[^f00096]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
- (b) in paragraph (1A), after “UKMA(GB)” insert “, UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”.
