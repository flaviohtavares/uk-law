# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 89

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Amendment to regulation 198[^key-1fbd40a73a084c1e9e2bf3291d4327fb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 198(2) (post-authorisation safety studies: general provisions)[^f00099]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “marketing authorisation” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2), UKMA(UK)(NI)”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “marketing authorisation” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1), a UKMA(GB)”.
