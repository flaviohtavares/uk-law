# The Human Medicines (Amendments relating to the Windsor Framework) Regulations 2024, section 25

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Amendment to regulation 48[^key-4eaccb576cc56aa416e94a69679296e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 48 (application of Part) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “EU reference medicinal product”[^f00032];
- (b) in the definition of “excluded reference product”[^f00033], in paragraph (c), for “53A(1)” substitute “53B(1)”;
- (c) in the definition of “generic medicinal product”[^f00034]—
  - (i) in paragraph (a), for “UKMA(UK)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 2)”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), in the opening words, for “UKMA(GB)” substitute “UKMA(UK)(Category 1)”;
- (d) in the definition of “reference medicinal product”[^f00035]—
  - (i) omit paragraph (b);
  - (ii) in paragraph (c)—
    - (aa) at the end of sub-paragraph (i), for “; or” substitute a comma;
    - (bb) omit sub-paragraph (ii).

(3) In paragraph (7)[^f00036], for “51A(1) and (6)” substitute “51B(1) and (6)”.

(4) Omit paragraphs (8) and (9).
