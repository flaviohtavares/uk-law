# The M3 Junction 9 Development Consent Order 2024, section 46

> Text as at 2024-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Certification of plans etc.[^c-54dd97bbe4ddf125af13081cbd3d60ca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit to the Secretary of State copies of each of the plans and documents set out in Schedule 11 (documents to be certified) for certification that they are true copies of the plans and documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
