# The Sheringham Shoal and Dudgeon Extensions Offshore Wind Farm Order 2024, section 44

> Text as at 2025-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44. Modification of DOW section 36 consent[^c-ebec6546c9e55f70970e030685983ad8]

*Extent: E+W*

Upon commencement by the undertaker of any Work Nos. 1B, 2B, 3B or 3C, 4B or 4C, 5B or 5C the provisions of the DOW section 36 consent shall be amended as follows—

- (a) In condition 3, for “560MW” substitute “402MW”; and
- (b) In Annex B, under the heading “Maximum Number of Turbines” for “77” substitute “67”.
