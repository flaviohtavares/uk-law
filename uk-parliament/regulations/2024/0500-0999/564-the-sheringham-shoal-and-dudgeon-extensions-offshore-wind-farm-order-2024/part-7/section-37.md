# The Sheringham Shoal and Dudgeon Extensions Offshore Wind Farm Order 2024, section 37

> Text as at 2025-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Certification of plans and documents, etc.[^key-d3153d16da12c7fcd64104a1333c5256]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after this Order is made, submit to the Secretary of State all of the documents listed in Schedule 18 (documents to be certified) for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
