# The Sheringham Shoal and Dudgeon Extensions Offshore Wind Farm Order 2024, section 4

> Text as at 2025-02-07.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Maintenance of the authorised project[^c-02549b181b33980010a0b2bae67bf025]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised project except to the extent that this Order or any agreement made under this Order provides otherwise.

(2) Paragraph ‎(1) does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any further licence under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act for licensable activities not covered by the deemed marine licences.
