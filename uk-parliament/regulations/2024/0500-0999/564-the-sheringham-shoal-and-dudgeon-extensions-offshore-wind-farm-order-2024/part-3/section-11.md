# The Sheringham Shoal and Dudgeon Extensions Offshore Wind Farm Order 2024, section 11

> Text as at 2025-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Temporary closure of public rights of way[^key-defb89bd61865aeb00f87ee39c69a45c]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker may in connection with the carrying out of the authorised project, temporarily close each of the public rights of way specified in column (2) of Schedule 4 (public rights of way to be temporarily closed) to the extent specified in column (3) of that Schedule, by reference to the letters shown on the public rights of way plan.
