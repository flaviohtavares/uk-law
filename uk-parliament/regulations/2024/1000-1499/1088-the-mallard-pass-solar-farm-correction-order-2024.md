# The Mallard Pass Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1088

Infrastructure Planning

The Mallard Pass Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2024

Made

31st October 2024

Coming into force

1st November 2024

The Mallard Pass Solar Farm Order 2024 (“**the Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed South Kesteven District Council and Rutland County Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Mallard Pass Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st November 2024.

## 2. Corrections

The Mallard Pass Solar Farm Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signatory by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero.

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

31st October 2024

Department of Energy Security and Net Zero

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors identified in the Mallard Pass Solar Farm Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/796](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/796)), which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.
