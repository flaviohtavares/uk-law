# The Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to the East Midlands Combined County Authority) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1145

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to the East Midlands Combined County Authority) Order 2024

Made

11th November 2024

Laid before the House of Commons

12th November 2024

Coming into force

3rd December 2024

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 33(3)(k) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to the East Midlands Combined County Authority) Order 2024 and comes into force on 3rd December 2024.

## 2. Body specified for the purposes of section 33 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994

The East Midlands Combined County Authority, established by regulation 3 of the East Midlands Combined County Authority Regulations 2024[^f00002], is specified for the purposes of section 33 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994.

Jeff Smith

Vicky Foxcroft

11th November 2024

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the East Midlands Combined County Authority for the purposes of section 33 of the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23). The effect of this Order is that the East Midlands Combined County Authority is able to claim refunds of value added tax charged on supplies to it, or on importations by it, where those supplies or importations are not for the purpose of any business carried on by it.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering this instrument will be published on the HMRC website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
