# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1281

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) Regulations 2024

Made

3rd December 2024

Laid before Parliament

4th December 2024

Coming into force

1st January 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 58(1)(a) and 59a(3)(a) of Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006 on shipments of waste[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the International Waste Shipments (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st January 2025.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Amendments to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013)

Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006 on shipments of waste is amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 6.

## 6. Amendments to Annex 5

(1) Part 1 of Annex 5 is amended as follows.

(2) In the text in section A1 under the heading “METAL AND METAL BEARING WASTES”, for entry A1180 substitute—

> A1181
>
> — Waste electrical and electronic equipment
>
> - containing or contaminated with cadmium, lead, mercury, organohalogen compounds or other Annex I constituents to an extent that the waste exhibits an Annex III characteristic, or
> - with a component containing or contaminated with Annex I constituents to an extent that the component exhibits an Annex III characteristic, including but not limited to any of the following components:
>   - glass from cathode-ray tubes included on list A
>   - a battery included on list A

> - Waste components of electrical and electronic equipment containing or contaminated with Annex I constituents to an extent that the waste components exhibit an Annex III characteristic, unless covered by another entry on list A.
> - Wastes arising from the processing of waste electrical and electronic equipment or waste components of electrical and electronic equipment, and containing or contaminated with Annex I constituents to an extent that the waste exhibits an Annex III characteristic (e.g. fractions arising from shredding or dismantling), unless covered by another entry on list A.
>
> (note the related entry Y49 in Annex 4 to this Regulation)

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement changes to the Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal (adopted on 22nd March 1989) (the “Basel Convention”)[^f00004].

In May 2022, the Conference of the Parties to the Basel Convention adopted decision BC-15/18 by which it amended Annexes II, VIII and IX to the Convention in relation to Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE)[^f00005].

Regulations 3 to 6 amend Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) as follows. Annex 1C of that regulation is amended by regulation 3 to include a reference to WEEE classified as Y49, which has been added to Annex II to the Basel Convention.

Annex 3 is amended by regulation 4 to remove defunct references to WEEE classified as B1110, which is no longer used in the Basel Convention, and GC010 and GC020, which are also no longer classifications accepted for exports from Great Britain.

Annexes 4 and 5, are amended by regulations 5 and 6 to remove references to A1180, B1110 and B4030, which are no longer used in the Basel Convention, and to remove references to codes GC010 and GC020. References are also added to new codes Y49 and A1181 which have been introduced in the Basel Convention.

Regulation 7 and the Schedule also amend the Annex to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) in order to remove defunct references to WEEE classified as B4030, GC010 and GC020.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no significant impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
