# The M3 Junction 9 Development Consent (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 2024/752](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/752) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2024 No. 1020

Infrastructure Planning

The M3 Junction 9 Development Consent (Correction) Order 2024

Made

9th October 2024

Coming into force

10th October 2024

The M3 Junction 9 Development Consent Order 2024[^f00001] (“**the 2024 Order**”) which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”) contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors and omissions in the 2024 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed the local planning authority for the area in which the land to which the 2024 Order relates is situated as the relevant planning authority, that this request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the M3 Junction 9 Development Consent (Correction) Order 2024 and comes into force on 10th October 2024.

## 2. Corrections

(1) The M3 Junction 9 Development Consent Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made, and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Natasha Kopala

Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

Department for Transport

9th October 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects correctable errors in the M3 Junction 9 Development Consent Order 2024 [S.I. 2024/752](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/752)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a written request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
