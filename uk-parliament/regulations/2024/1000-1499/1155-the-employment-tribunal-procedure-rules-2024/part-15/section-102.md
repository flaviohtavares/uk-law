# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 102

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 102. Levy appeals[^key-ff0c6c28fe982a4854810dd3200e1990]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of a levy appeal, references in these Rules to a claim or claimant must be read as references to a levy appeal or to an appellant in a levy appeal respectively.

(2) “**Levy appeal**” means an appeal against an assessment to a levy imposed under a levy order under section 11 of the Industrial Training Act 1982[^f00044].
