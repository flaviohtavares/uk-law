# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 94

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 94. Interim relief proceedings[^key-daf034ddeecbd03ac9c34adc4af55640]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When the Tribunal hears an application for interim relief (or for its variation or revocation) under section 161 or 165 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00035] or section 128 or 131 of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00036], rules 52 to 54 (preliminary hearings) apply to the hearing and the Tribunal must not hear oral evidence unless it directs otherwise.
