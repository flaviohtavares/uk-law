# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 99

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 99. Transfer of proceedings from a court[^key-c4e504256c54ca4b4fd30944405b28f6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where proceedings are referred to the Tribunal by a court, these Rules apply as if the proceedings had been presented by the claimant.
