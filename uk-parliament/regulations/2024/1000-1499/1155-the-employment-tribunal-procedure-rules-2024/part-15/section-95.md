# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 95

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 95. Proceedings involving the National Insurance Fund[^key-1498d19c6f36cb9099ed8426b526961d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State is entitled to appear and be heard at any hearing in relation to proceedings which may involve a payment out of the National Insurance Fund and must be treated as a party for the purposes of these Rules.
