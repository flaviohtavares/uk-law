# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 101

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 101. Information to the Commission for Equality and Human Rights[^key-c1b23ae2a38f703fbd718881bae7e398]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Tribunal must send to the Commission for Equality and Human Rights copies of all judgments and written reasons relating to complaints under sections 120, 127 or 146 of the Equality Act[^f00043].

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any proceedings where a Minister of the Crown has given a direction or the Tribunal has made an order under rule 93 (national security proceedings), and any of the Security Service, the Secret Intelligence Service or the Government Communications Headquarters is a party to the proceedings.
