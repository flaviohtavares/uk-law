# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 75

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 75. Procedure[^c-f36812c04a6b54bc6baca3c7a043dcda]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A party may apply for a costs order or a preparation time order at any stage up to 28 days after the date on which the judgment finally determining the proceedings in respect of that party was sent to the parties.

(2) The Tribunal must not make a costs order or a preparation time order against a party unless that party has had a reasonable opportunity to make representations (in writing or at a hearing, as the Tribunal may order).
