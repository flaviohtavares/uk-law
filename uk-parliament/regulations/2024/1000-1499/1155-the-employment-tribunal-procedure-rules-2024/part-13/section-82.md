# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 82

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 82. Ability to pay[^c-f036fd9c54eb7b902ca8b5d966648911]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In deciding whether to make a costs order, preparation time order, or wasted costs order, and if so the amount of any such order, the Tribunal may have regard to the paying party’s (or, where a wasted costs order is made, the representative’s) ability to pay.
