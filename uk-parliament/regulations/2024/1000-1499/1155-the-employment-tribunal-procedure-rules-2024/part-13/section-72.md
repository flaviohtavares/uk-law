# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 72

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 72. Definitions[^c-419c38a3bac06ceec7beb18fdefd5e47]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “**paying party**” means a party liable to pay costs;
- “**preparation time**” means time spent by the receiving party (including by any of the receiving party’s employees or advisers) in working on the case, except for time spent at any final hearing;
- “**receiving party**” means a party entitled to be paid costs.
