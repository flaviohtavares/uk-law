# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 42

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Written representations[^c-9fde3fe404a51279fdc0c1878125b78f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Tribunal may consider any written representations from a party, including a party who does not propose to attend the hearing, if they are sent to the Tribunal and the other parties, and must do so if they are sent to the Tribunal and the other parties not less than 7 days before the hearing.
