# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 45

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Timetabling[^key-4a7e1d82c766cef4abc66f690fff257a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Tribunal may impose limits on the time that a party may take in presenting evidence, questioning witnesses or making submissions, and may prevent the party from proceeding beyond any time so allotted.
