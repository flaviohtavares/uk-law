# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 48

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Conversion from preliminary hearing to final hearing and vice versa[^c-7b8dcf3807220f787816129f5d340988]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Tribunal conducting a preliminary hearing may order that it be treated as a final hearing, or vice versa, if the Tribunal is properly constituted for the purpose and if it is satisfied that no party is materially prejudiced by the change.
