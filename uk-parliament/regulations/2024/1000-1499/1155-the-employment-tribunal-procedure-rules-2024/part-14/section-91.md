# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 91

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 91. Digital case management[^c-fbf9f9024788812476d23f8b01b3d598]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Rules, where a party or the Tribunal is required to send any document or communication to the Tribunal or another party and does so through the Tribunal’s digital case management system and in accordance with any practice direction, there shall be deemed compliance.
