# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 31

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Applications for case management orders[^key-a4eac683a61ce1668777f7be9eee9c51]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A party may apply for a case management order at a hearing or by sending a written application to the Tribunal.

(2) Where a party makes a written application under paragraph (1) they must notify the other parties that any objections to the application should be sent to the Tribunal as soon as possible.

(3) The Tribunal may deal with an application made under paragraph (1) in writing or order that it be dealt with at a preliminary hearing or final hearing.
