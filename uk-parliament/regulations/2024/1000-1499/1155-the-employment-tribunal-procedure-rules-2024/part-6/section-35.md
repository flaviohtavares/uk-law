# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 35

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Addition, substitution and removal of parties[^key-8b7064322050817fd682b6ffb9ea1924]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Tribunal may on its own initiative, or on the application of a party or any other person wishing to become a party, add any person as a party, by way of substitution or otherwise, if it appears that there are issues between that person and any of the existing parties falling within the jurisdiction of the Tribunal which it is in the interests of justice to have determined in the proceedings.

(2) The Tribunal may on its own initiative, or on the application of a party, remove any party that appears to have been wrongly included.
