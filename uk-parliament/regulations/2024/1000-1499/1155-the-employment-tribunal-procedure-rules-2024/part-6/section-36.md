# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 36

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Other persons[^c-6faf66dde586273712871557dc74c669]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Tribunal may permit any person to participate in proceedings, on such terms as may be specified, in respect of any matter in which the Tribunal considers that that person has a legitimate interest.
