# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 33

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Disclosure of documents and information[^key-82bc682c1249d70154440f681e57e385]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Tribunal may order any person in Great Britain to disclose documents or information to a party (by providing copies or otherwise) or to allow a party to inspect such material as might be ordered by the county court or, in Scotland, by a sheriff.
