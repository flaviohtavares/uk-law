# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 25

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Notification of employer’s contract claim[^c-6e246c97234cb2454f5c50f3c7811d1d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When the Tribunal sends a copy of the response form to the other parties in accordance with rule 23 (notification of acceptance) it must notify the claimant that the response includes an employer’s contract claim and include information on how the claimant may submit a reply, the time limit for doing so, and what will happen if the Tribunal does not receive a reply in time.
