# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 8

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Presidential Guidance[^key-c77462f6c922dc0899f06f9e03d52cfe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The President may issue guidance for the area for which they are responsible, as to matters of practice and as to how the powers conferred by these Rules may be exercised.

(2) Any guidance issued under paragraph (1) must be published by the President in an appropriate manner to bring it to the attention of the persons to whom it is addressed.

(3) The Tribunal must have regard to any guidance published by the President for the area in which the Tribunal is constituted, but is not bound by it.
