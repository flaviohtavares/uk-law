# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 1

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and extent[^c-5f808f944debbfd409d3e64f111a1cff]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024 and come into force immediately after the Employment Tribunals (Procedure Rules) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2024[^f00008] come into force.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Rules (excluding Schedules 1 and 2) apply to all proceedings before the Tribunal.

(3) Schedules 1 and 2 apply to modify Parts 1 to 15 of these Rules in relation, respectively, to—

- (a) national security proceedings, and
- (b) equal value claims.

(4) These Rules extend to England and Wales and Scotland.
