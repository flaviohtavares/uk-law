# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 57

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Final hearing held in public[^key-b0afd2d121fa671b2848d982e75a9ee6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to rules 49 (privacy and restrictions on disclosure) and 93 (national security proceedings), any final hearing must be in public.
