# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 69

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Application for reconsideration[^key-76d9f9fbb68f8975a430afeb8e011ebb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Except where it is made in the course of a hearing, an application for reconsideration must be made in writing setting out why reconsideration is necessary and must be sent to the Tribunal no later than[^key-daafd4d2837442366befa8ec672cf7f2] 14 days of the later of—

- (a) the date on which the written [^key-8f516d83e8a5c0fb7c55333b8bbb244b]... judgment sought to be reconsidered was sent to the parties, [^key-6c7f81ed74d57f18a99aa9c605d9698f]...
- (b)[^key-d30b02bed0166463d477b77fd43c41bd] the date that any written summary reasons were sent, if these were sent separately from the judgment, or
- (c) the date that any written full reasons were sent, if these were sent separately from the judgment.
