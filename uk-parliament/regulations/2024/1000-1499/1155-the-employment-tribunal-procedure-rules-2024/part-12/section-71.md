# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 71

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Reconsideration by the Tribunal on its own initiative[^c-d379ea5734eb7a09fb8339b0fb82d4db]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Tribunal proposes to reconsider a judgment on its own initiative, it must inform the parties of the reasons why the decision is being reconsidered and the judgment must be reconsidered (as if an application had been made and not refused) in accordance with rule 70(3) to (5) (process for reconsideration).
