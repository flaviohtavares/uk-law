# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 61

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Absence of presiding member[^c-55876a1705af268bd63e2d8f20221b34]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If it is impossible or not practicable for the written record or written reasons to be approved by the presiding member as a result of death, incapacity or absence, it must be approved—

- (a) in the case of a Tribunal composed of more than one member, by the other member or members;
- (b) in the case of a Tribunal composed of a single member, by the relevant post-holder.

(2) In this rule, the relevant post-holder is—

- (a) in England and Wales, the President (England and Wales) or a Regional Employment Judge;
- (b) in Scotland, the President (Scotland) or the Vice President.
