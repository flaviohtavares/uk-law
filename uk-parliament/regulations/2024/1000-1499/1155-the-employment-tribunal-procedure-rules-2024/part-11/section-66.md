# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 66

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Copies of decisions for referring court[^c-386f700cd3b6c0f6beca59c3c1acb4a4][^c-093c3020168c18ed09354ec9d515527a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the proceedings were referred to the Tribunal by a court, the Tribunal must send a copy of any decision[^c-05287d79e1e9e2eb99e5f4d587d97e91] and any written reasons to that court.
