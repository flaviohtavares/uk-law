# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, section 63

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. When a judgment or order takes effect[^c-682cf6c503bd4f2bf724f71986a140e3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A judgment or order takes effect from the day when it is given or made, or on such later date as is specified by the Tribunal.
