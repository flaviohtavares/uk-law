# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1118

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2024

Made

6th November 2024

Coming into force

7th November 2024

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 6th day of November 2024

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2024 and comes into force on 7th November 2024.

## 2. Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — The persons appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 6th November 2024

Article 2

- Elizabeth Anne Dayton
- Stephanie Claire Nixon

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
