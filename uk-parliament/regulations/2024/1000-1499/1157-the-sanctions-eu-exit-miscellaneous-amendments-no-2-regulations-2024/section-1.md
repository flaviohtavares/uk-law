# The Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2024, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2024 and come into force in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) These Regulations come into force on the 21st day after the day on which they are laid before Parliament, except the following provisions come into force six months after the day on which these Regulations are laid—
