# The Export and Investment Guarantees (Limit on Exports and Insurance Commitments) (No. 3) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1299

Export And Investment Guarantees

The Export and Investment Guarantees (Limit on Exports and Insurance Commitments) (No. 3) Order 2024

Made

9th December 2024

Coming into force

30th December 2024

The Secretary of State makes this Order, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(4)(a) of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991[^f00001].

In accordance with section 15(3) of that Act a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the House of Commons.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Export and Investment Guarantees (Limit on Exports and Insurance Commitments) (No. 3) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on the 21st day after the day on which it is made.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Increase in aggregate exports and insurance commitment limit

The limit in section 6(1) of the Export and Investments Guarantees Act 1991 is increased to 82,700 million special drawing rights.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 6(1) of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 limits the aggregate amount of the Secretary of State’s commitments under arrangements made pursuant to that Act relating to exports and insurance to 67,700 million special drawing rights. Section 6(4)(a) of that Act provides that the Secretary of State may by order increase, or further increase, that limit by a sum not exceeding 5,000 million special drawing rights. Section 6(4A) of that Act provides that the power to make such an order cannot be exercised on more than three occasions. The power was exercised for the first time to make [S.I. 2024/1297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1297) which increases the limit to 72,700 million special drawing rights. The power was exercised for the second time to make [S.I. 2024/1298](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1298) which further increases the limit to 77,700 million special drawing rights. This Order is the third occasion on which the power has been exercised. This Order increases the limit from 77,700 million to 82,700 million special drawing rights to accommodate prospective future business.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
