# The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1031

Infrastructure Planning

The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline (Correction) Order 2024

Made

15th October 2024

Coming into force

16th October 2024

The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002] contains correctable errors as defined in paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] to correct errors in the HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Cheshire West and Chester Council and Flintshire County Council as the local planning authorities[^f00004] that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline (Correction) Order 2024.

**2.** This Order comes into force on 16th October 2024.

## 3. Correction of original order

(1) The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be submitted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

David Wagstaff

Deputy Director, Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

15th October 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/436](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/436)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
