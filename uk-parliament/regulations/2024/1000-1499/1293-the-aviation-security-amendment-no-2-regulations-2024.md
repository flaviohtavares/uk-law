# The Aviation Security (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1293

CIVIL AVIATION

The Aviation Security (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2024

Made

5th December 2024

Laid before Parliament

9th December 2024

Coming into force

31st January 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 4(3) and 15A(4)(b) of Regulation [(EC) No 300/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0300) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008 on rules in the field of civil aviation security[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Aviation Security (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 31st January 2025.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2015/1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/1998)

(1) The Annex to Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2015/1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/1998) of 5 November 2015 laying down detailed measures for the implementation of the basic standards on aviation security[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Chapters 1 (airport security) and 5 (hold baggage).

(3) In Chapter 9 (airport supplies) omit point 9.0.4.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Michael Kane

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

5th December 2024

Department for Transport

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to legislation in the field of aviation security.

Regulation 2 amends Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2015/1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/1998) of 5 November 2015 laying down detailed measures for the implementation of the basic standards on aviation security. In particular, the amendments remove current requirements for airport security and hold baggage, as well as one provision which cross-refers to airport security requirements.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum has been published alongside these Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
