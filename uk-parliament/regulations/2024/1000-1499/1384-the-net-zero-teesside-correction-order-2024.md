# The Net Zero Teesside (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1384

Infrastructure Planning

The Net Zero Teesside (Correction) Order 2024

Made

20th December 2024

Coming into force

21st December 2024

The Net Zero Teesside Order 2024[^f00001] (“**the 2024 Order**”) which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”) contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors and omissions in the 2024 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council and Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council as the relevant planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Net Zero Teesside (Correction) Order 2024 and comes into force on 21st December 2024.

## 2. Corrections

The 2024 Order is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

20th December 2024

(This note is not part of the Order)
