# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1342

WILDLIFE, ENGLAND

The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2024

Made

16th December 2024

Laid before Parliament

17th December 2024

Coming into force

31st January 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(5)(a) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has, in accordance with the requirements of section 26(4)(a) of that Act, given any local authority affected and any other person affected an opportunity to submit objections or representations with respect to the subject matter of this Order. The Secretary of State has also, in accordance with section 26(4)(b) of that Act, consulted with Natural England, being the advisory body he considered was best able to advise him as to whether this Order should be made.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2024 and comes into force on 31st January 2025.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No.2) Order 2021

(1) The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No. 2) Order 2021[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Article 4.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (England) (No.2) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/548](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/548)) (the “**2021 Order**”). The 2021 Order made an addition to Part 1 of Schedule 9 to the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (“**the Act**”), but this is subject to an expiry date.

Article 2 amends the 2021 Order to remove the expiry date. The effect is that the addition made by the 2021 Order will continue.
