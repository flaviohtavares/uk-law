## 8. Benefit of Order[^c-d173eb91e5ebcc1e5684cf0ca784a695]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to article 9 (transfer of benefit of Order, etc.) the provisions of this Order conferring powers on the undertaker have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.
