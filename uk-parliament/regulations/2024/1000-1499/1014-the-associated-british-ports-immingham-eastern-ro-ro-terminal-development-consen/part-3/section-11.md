# The Associated British Ports (Immingham Eastern Ro-Ro Terminal) Development Consent Order 2024, section 11

> Text as at 2025-02-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Time limit for exercise of powers of compulsory acquisition[^c-9a1aa70a11afbfa65c7251dc0652d523]

*Extent: E+W*

After the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the day on which this Order comes into force—

- (a) no notice to treat is to be served under Part 1 (compulsory purchase under Acquisition of Land Act of 1946) of the 1965 Act as modified by article 14 (modification of Part 1 of the 1965 Act); and
- (b) no declaration is to be executed under section 4 (execution of declaration) of the 1981 Act as applied by article 15 (application of the 1981 Act),

in relation to any part of the Order land.
