# The Sunnica Energy Farm (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 1154

Infrastructure Planning

The Sunnica Energy Farm (Correction) Order 2024

Made

12th November 2024

Coming into force

13th November 2024

The Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024[^f00001] (“**the Order**”), which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”), contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed East Cambridgeshire District Council and West Suffolk Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sunnica Energy Farm (Correction) Order 2024 and comes into force on 13th<sup></sup>November 2024.

## 2. Corrections

The Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to the Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

12th November 2024
