# The Network Rail (Leeds to Micklefield Enhancements) Order 2024, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Certification of plans etc.

Network Rail must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the deposited plans and the deposited sections to the Secretary of State for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the book of reference, the deposited plans and the deposited sections referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
