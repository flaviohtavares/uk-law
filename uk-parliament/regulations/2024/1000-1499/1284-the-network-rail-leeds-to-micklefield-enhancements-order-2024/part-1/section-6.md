# The Network Rail (Leeds to Micklefield Enhancements) Order 2024, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Application of local railway enactments

(1) The following provisions of the Leeds and Selby Railway Act 1830[^f00029] do not apply to any works executed under the powers conferred by this Order—

- (a) section 5 (width of rails of railway); and
- (b) section 10 (ascent of bridges for carrying public roads over railway).
