# The Network Rail (Leeds to Micklefield Enhancements) Order 2024, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Disapplication of legislative provisions

Sections 80(2)(b), 81 and 82 of the Building Act 1984[^f00028] (which concern a local authority’s power to serve notice about demolition) do not apply to the demolition of the whole or part of a building carried out in exercise of the powers under this Order and to which section 80 of the Building Act 1984 otherwise applies.
