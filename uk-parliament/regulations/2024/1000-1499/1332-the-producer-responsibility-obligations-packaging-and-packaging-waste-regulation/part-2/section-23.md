# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 23

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Large and small producers[^key-3d10a0f60d9f06c9f94ac37c38feb42f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- (a) a “**large producer**” means a producer who, in relation to a relevant year—
  - (i) satisfies the criteria in regulation 24(1), read together with paragraphs (3) to (7) of that regulation; or
  - (ii) is a large producer by virtue of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 9 (groups of companies);
- (b) a “**small producer**” means a producer who, in relation to a calendar year—
  - (i) satisfies the criteria in regulation 24(2), read together with paragraphs (3) to (7) of that regulation, but is not a large producer; or
  - (ii) is a small producer by virtue of paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 9.
