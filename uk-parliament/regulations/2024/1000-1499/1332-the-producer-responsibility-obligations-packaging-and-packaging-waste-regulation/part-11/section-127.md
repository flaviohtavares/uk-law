# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 127

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Policy statement[^key-79a23f74d01e825bbc1f8725e8773680][^key-79a23f74d01e825bbc1f8725e8773680]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate authorities must jointly publish a statement setting out—

- (a) the intended environmental effects of the extended producer responsibility for packaging policy;
- (b) how that policy is intended to achieve those effects.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**the extended producer responsibility for packaging policy**” has the meaning given in regulation 59(9).
