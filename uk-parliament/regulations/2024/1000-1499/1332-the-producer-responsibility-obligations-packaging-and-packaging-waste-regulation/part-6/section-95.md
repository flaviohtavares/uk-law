# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 95

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Applications for accreditation: exporters[^key-58d0aa513318674d07b835119d1bf246]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to an exporter wishing to be accredited under this Chapter to issue PERNs in a year or part of a year (“**E**”).

(2) E must make a separate application to the appropriate agency for accreditation for each category of packaging waste exported by E for which E wishes to issue PERNs.

(3) Each application must—

- (a) state—
  - (i) the category of packaging waste for which the application is being made;
  - (ii) the tonnage band of packaging waste in that category which E intends to export for recycling, for which E wishes to be accredited to issue PERNs;
  - (iii) if known at the time of making the application, the name and address of each overseas reprocessing site to which E intends to export that category of packaging waste;
  - (iv) all information specified on the application form; and
- (b) be accompanied by—
  - (i) E’s sampling and inspection plan; and
  - (ii) E’s business plan, which must include the information specified in regulation 96(4).

(4) E must provide the appropriate agency with any other information the appropriate agency reasonably requires in order to determine the application.
