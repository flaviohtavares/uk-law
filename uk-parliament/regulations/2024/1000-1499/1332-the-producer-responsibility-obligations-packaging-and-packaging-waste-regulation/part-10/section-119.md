# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 119

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Penalties for offences[^c-fc93900a4d07f220cf1218f0df909a78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An offence under any of regulations 115 to 118 is punishable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, by a fine; or
- (b) on summary conviction—
  - (i) in England and Wales, by a fine;
  - (ii) in Scotland or Northern Ireland, by a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum.
