# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Information required for producer registration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 29

**1.**[^key-3bb7660d68461aa0d0b1bf755dcf1fd6] The name, address and telephone number of—

- (a) the place where the producer is habitually resident, or
- (b) the registered office of the producer or, if the producer does not have a registered office, the head office or principal place of business of the producer.

**2.**[^key-8dfb775573d9abd139ffdb0ebc9cbbc6] If the producer is not habitually resident in the United Kingdom, or if the registered office, head office or principal place of business of the producer is not in the United Kingdom, the address of the branch or postal address of the producer in the United Kingdom.

**3.**[^key-30982979028791d9117361093595bbc0] The business name of the producer if different from that referred to in paragraph 1 above.

**4.**[^key-fc32a365026463f081788495254a9851] The name and contact details, including an email address, of an individual at the producer who is responsible for dealing with enquiries from the appropriate agency.

**5.**[^key-e87ee4e9fa3187dadea034a9dfc0b8bb] The address for service of notices on the producer if different from that referred to in paragraph 1 above.

**6.**[^key-4e2237e5327c5337cc2319e59023a4e8] Where the producer is a partnership, the names of all the partners.

**7.**[^key-174529e561cb48eac6bd2f5b9a390d71] Which of the functions described in regulations 16 to 22 the producer performs in relation to packaging.

**8.**[^key-59e0ccba47b32a56ef4b595b8060d232] If the producer performs more than one such function, which function constitutes the producer’s main activity as a producer.

**9.**[^key-465facf3656dda61360dcf084fd3b083] The relevant SIC code for the function the producer performs in relation to packaging or, where the producer performs more than one such function, for the function which constitutes the producer’s main activity as a producer.

**10.**[^key-d3c55f724a66818ba4e3454b178692a6] The producer’s turnover in the financial year referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (2)(a) of regulation 24, calculated in accordance with that regulation.

**11.**[^key-7ce8785a05f94ed3490120bdf80846df] Except where the application is made under regulation 28(2), the amount in tonnes of packaging which the producer has supplied, in the calendar year referred to in paragraphs (1)(b) and (2)(b) of regulation 24, calculated in accordance with that regulation.

**12.**[^key-58507d492100306dab8b67f7f07e2abf] Whether or not the producer is liable to pay disposal fees under regulation 60 for the assessment year starting on 1st April in the calendar year for which the producer is applying for registration.

**13.**[^key-9e1d12863f88a688ed7cc5c2b857748c] Where the producer is a brand owner—

- (a) details of all names, trade marks and other distinctive marks which appear on packaging for which the brand owner is responsible, and
- (b) whether the brand owner also produces packaging on which no name, trade mark or other distinctive mark appears.
