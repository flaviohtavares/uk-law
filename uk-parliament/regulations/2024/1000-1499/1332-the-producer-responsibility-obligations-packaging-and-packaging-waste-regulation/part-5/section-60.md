# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 60

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Liability of producers to pay annual disposal and administration fees[^c-790fa9358ee01cb6d179d31a1a528847]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-45193d8341c636ca858cadaa42fd732e] A person is a liable producer in relation to an assessment year if—

- (a) the person is a producer in all or part of that assessment year; and
- (b) in the calendar year ending on the 31st December preceding the start of that assessment year, the person—
  - (i) was a brand owner, a packer/filler, an importer or first UK owner, a distributor, an online marketplace operator or a service provider;
  - (ii) was a large producer; and
  - (iii) supplied household packaging.

(2) A person[^c-e83411ffc401462a42cc1789ba9cf381] must, for each assessment year in relation to which the person is a liable producer, pay to the scheme administrator in accordance with regulation 68—

- (a) a disposal fee; and
- (b) an administration fee.

(3) The scheme administrator must for each assessment year, as soon as reasonably practicable after the start of the assessment year—

- (a) calculate for each liable producer the amounts of—
  - (i) the disposal fee payable by that producer, in accordance with regulations 61 to 64 and 66; and
  - (ii) the administration fee payable by that producer, in accordance with regulations 65 and 66; and
- (b) serve a notice of liability on each liable producer in accordance with regulation 67.

(4) In relation to corporate bodies that are registered with the appropriate agency as a group under paragraph 4 of Schedule 9, this Chapter applies subject to the modifications in paragraph 6 of Schedule 9.
