# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 62

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Calculation of disposal fee: household packaging waste[^key-14b6001bc07e13841c555cde40fad85b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme administrator must, for each liable producer, calculate the fee for each packaging category of household packaging supplied by that producer in accordance with the following formula—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1332)

where—

- “D” is the sum of the chargeable disposal costs of all relevant authorities for the assessment year in relation to household packaging waste in that packaging category;
- “E” is the total weight in tonnes of household packaging in that packaging category which the scheme administrator calculates has been supplied by all liable producers in the calendar year ending before the start of the assessment year , after subtraction of the total weight in tonnes of waste in that packaging category which is off-set under paragraph (2) for all liable producers[^key-d3912108e86b3ce2b2ee664efa0ed426];
- “CW” is the weight in tonnes of household packaging in that packaging category reported by or in relation to the producer , or, where regulation 67A(4) applies, estimated by the scheme administrator,[^key-58bbcadf8ad5597c94afce025353b683] as having been supplied in the calendar year ending before the start of the assessment year, after subtraction of the weight in tonnes of waste in that packaging category which is off-set under paragraph (2).

(2) In calculating CW for a packaging category for the purposes of paragraph (1), the scheme administrator must, subject to paragraphs (2A) and (2B), off-set the weight in tonnes of relevant packaging waste and closed loop packaging waste in that packaging category reported by or in relation to the liable producer under paragraph 12(1) or (2) of Schedule 4[^key-d0f0eeb00234946d189fbcb6c0cfaeaa].

(2A)[^key-e6716632c0d744d825b9ea3bde83fec5] The scheme administrator must calculate CW without off-setting any closed loop packaging waste reported under paragraph 12(2) of Schedule 4, if the liable producer has not paid the additional registration charge under regulation 29(3)(c) for the calendar year in question.

(2B) If, for a packaging category, the weight referred to in paragraph (2) is greater than the weight of household packaging reported by or in relation to the producer as having been supplied in the calendar year ending before the start of the assessment year, CW is 0.

(3) In calculating E for the purposes of paragraph (1), the scheme administrator—

- (a) must take into account all household packaging reported by or in relation to all liable producers; and
- (b) may estimate the weight, if any, of household packaging which has been supplied by liable producers where it appears to the scheme administrator that reporting obligations have not been complied with, or have not been fully complied with, in relation to the household packaging they have supplied.

(4) In this regulation, “**relevant packaging waste**” has the meaning given in regulation 34(4).
