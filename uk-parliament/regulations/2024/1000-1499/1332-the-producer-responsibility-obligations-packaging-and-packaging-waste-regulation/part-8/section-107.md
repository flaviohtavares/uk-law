# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 107

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Status pending appeal[^c-ef93cc9c103ad83aaa3f64e5e3fb52e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an appeal is made to the appeal body under regulation 104 or 105, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the decision, civil sanction or notice which is the subject of the appeal has effect until the appeal is determined or withdrawn, unless the appeal body decides otherwise.

(2) Any—

- (a) civil sanction imposed under regulation 121, other than a compliance notice; or
- (b) enforcement cost recovery notice issued under regulation 123,

which is the subject of an appeal is suspended until the appeal is determined or withdrawn.

(3) In the case of an appeal under regulation 104(1)(b), the decision appealed against does not have effect until the appeal is dismissed or withdrawn; and if the appeal is dismissed or withdrawn the decision has effect from the day after the day on which the appeal is dismissed or withdrawn.
