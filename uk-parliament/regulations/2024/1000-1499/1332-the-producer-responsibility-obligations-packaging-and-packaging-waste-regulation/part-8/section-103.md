# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 103

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Interpretation[^key-c05b7906ab2a9f94ef08c94b3a7a841a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**appeal body**” means—

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the First-tier Tribunal;
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, the Planning Appeals Commission;
- (c) in relation to Scotland, for an appeal under—
  - (i) regulation 104, the Scottish Ministers;
  - (ii) regulation 105, the sheriff.
