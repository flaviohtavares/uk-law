# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 108

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Determination of appeals[^key-7e92cf7fb3f90d5211d18d5161068a8d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, following an appeal under regulation 104 or 105, the appeal body determines that a decision of the scheme administrator or the appropriate agency (“the decision maker”) is to be varied or remitted to the decision maker for reconsideration, the decision maker must—

- (a) comply with any directions given to it by the appeal body; and
- (b) take any steps necessary to give effect to the appeal body’s determination.
