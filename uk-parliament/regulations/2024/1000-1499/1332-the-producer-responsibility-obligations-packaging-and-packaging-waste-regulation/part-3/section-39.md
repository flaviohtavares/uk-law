# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 39

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Reporting obligations: general provisions[^c-7d569fd35ec2f7874269794496479270]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The obligations of producers under regulations 35 to 38 are subject to—

- (a) regulation 42(2)(a) (producers: effect of compliance scheme membership); and
- (b) paragraph 5 of Schedule 9 (corporate groups: effect of group registration).

(2) All reports submitted to the appropriate agency or to the Environment Agency under this Chapter must be—

- (a) as accurate as reasonably possible;
- (b) verified by the signature of an approved person of the producer; and
- (c) submitted in such form as that agency specifies.

(3) Where a producer becomes aware that any information contained in a report which the producer has submitted to the appropriate agency or the Environment Agency under this Chapter was wrong or inaccurate, the producer must—

- (a) submit an amended report to that agency containing the correct information; and
- (b) pay that agency the charge in paragraph 2(3) of Schedule 1 for resubmitting a report.
