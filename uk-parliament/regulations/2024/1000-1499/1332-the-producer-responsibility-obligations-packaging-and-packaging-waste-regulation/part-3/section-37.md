# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Obligation to report results of recyclability assessments[^c-ea9df4305a986beeb99ab400ce7f18af]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A producer that is a liable producer for the purposes of Part 5 must report to the appropriate agency every 6 months the results of its recyclability assessments of the household packaging supplied by the producer, in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3) and regulation 39.

(2) Reports under this regulation must be submitted to the appropriate agency—

- (a) on or before 1st October each year, for the period from 1st January to 30th June in that year; and
- (b) on or before 1st April each year, for the period from 1st July to 31st December in the previous year.

(3) The first reports under this regulation must be submitted to the appropriate agency on or before 1st October 2025, for the period 1st January to 30th June 2025.
