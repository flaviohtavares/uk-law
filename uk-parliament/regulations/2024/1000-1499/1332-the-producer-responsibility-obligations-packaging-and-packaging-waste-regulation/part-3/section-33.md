## 33. Information to be collected and reported by producers[^key-16f97c203e64677bafcf9483ce4bb972]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 specifies descriptions of information—

- (a) about which producers are required to collect data under regulation 34; and
- (b) which, subject to regulation 39(1), certain producers are required to report under regulations 35 to 38.
