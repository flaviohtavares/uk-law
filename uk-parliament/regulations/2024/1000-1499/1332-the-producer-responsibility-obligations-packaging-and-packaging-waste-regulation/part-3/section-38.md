# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations 2024, section 38

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Obligation to report data on plastic or paper bags supplied in England[^key-fcf00592af3e5e621f6881670c45ee9e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A large producer or small producer who—

- (a) is a seller; and
- (b) supplies plastic or paper bags in England,

must report to the Environment Agency every 12 months, in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3) and regulation 39, the information specified in paragraph 14 of Schedule 4.

(2) Reports under this regulation must be submitted to the Environment Agency on or before 1st April each year, for the previous calendar year.

(3) The first reports under this regulation must be submitted to the Environment Agency on or before 1st April 2026, for the period 1st January to 31st December 2025.
