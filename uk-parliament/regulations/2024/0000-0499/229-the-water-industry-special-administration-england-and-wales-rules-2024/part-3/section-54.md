# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Notice of intention to resign (rule 3.63)

Rule 3.63 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (2), for sub-paragraph (c) there were substituted—

  > - (c) the name of the person who made the application for a special administration order;

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (3)(b), from “where” to “order,” were omitted;
- (c) for paragraph (4) there were substituted—

  > (4) The notice must be delivered—
  >
  > - (a) to any continuing special administrator of the company; and
  > - (b) to the relevant authority and the Water Services Regulation Authority.

  .
