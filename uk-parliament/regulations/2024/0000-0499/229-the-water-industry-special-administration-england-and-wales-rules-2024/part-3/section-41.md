# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Moratorium (rule 3.27A)

Chapter 5 of Part 3 has effect as if, after rule 3.27, there were inserted—

> **3.27A. Moratorium**
>
> Where an application is made under paragraph 43 (moratorium on other legal process) of Schedule B1
>
> - (a) the applicant must, within two working days of making the application, serve notice of the application on—
>   - (i) the relevant authority, and
>   - (ii) the Water Services Regulation Authority; and
> - (b) the relevant authority and the Water Services Regulation Authority may make representations concerning the application to the court by—
>   - (i) filing written submissions to the court, and
>   - (ii) appearing at any hearing concerning the application.
