# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Moving from administration to creditors’ voluntary winding up (paragraph 83 of Schedule B1) (rule 3.60)

Rule 3.60 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(b), there were omitted “the appointment or” and “, as the case may be”;
- (b) for paragraph (6) there were substituted—

  > (6) For the purposes of paragraph 83(7)(a) of Schedule B1—
  >
  > - (a) a person is nominated by the creditors as liquidator by—
  >   - (i) their approval of the statement of the proposed liquidator in the special administrator’s proposals or revised proposals; or
  >   - (ii) their nomination of a different person, through a decision procedure under rules 3.35(1)(j)(ii)(cc) and 3.42A;
  > - (b) the creditors’ approval is deemed to have been given under sub-paragraph (a)(i) where no decision procedure is sought under rules 3.35(1)(j)(ii)(cc) and 3.42A.

  ;
- (c) after paragraph (7), there were inserted—

  > (8) The Water Services Regulation Authority must notify the relevant authority before consenting to the special administrator delivering to the registrar of companies a notice of moving from special administration to creditors’ voluntary liquidation.

  .
