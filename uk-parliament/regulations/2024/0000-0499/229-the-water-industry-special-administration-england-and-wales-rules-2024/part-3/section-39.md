# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Notice of administration order (rule 3.15)

Rule 3.15 has effect as if, after paragraph (2), there were inserted—

> (2A) The applicant must as soon as reasonably practicable deliver a copy of the order—
>
> - (i) where the relevant authority was the applicant, to the Water Services Regulation Authority; or
> - (ii) where the Water Services Regulation Authority was the applicant, to the relevant authority.
