# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Publication of administrator’s appointment (rule 3.27)

Rule 3.27 has effect as if, in paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (d), “and” were omitted;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (e), there were inserted—

  > - (f) any person who has appointed, or is or may be entitled to appoint, an administrative receiver in relation to the company;
  > - (g) the Environment Agency or Natural Resources Wales (as applicable);
  > - (h) the Consumer Council for Water;
  > - (i) the Chief Inspector of Drinking Water; and
  > - (j) any creditor who has served notice in accordance with section 26(1)(c) of the 1991 Act of the creditor’s intention to enforce the creditor’s security over property of the company.
