# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Appointment of replacement or additional administrator (rule 3.69)

Rule 3.69 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **3.69. Appointment of replacement or additional special administrator**
>
> Where a replacement or additional special administrator is appointed to act—
>
> - (a) rule 3.27 (publication of administrator’s appointment), paragraphs (1), 2(a) and (b), (3) and (4), apply;
> - (b) the replacement or additional special administrator must deliver notice of the appointment to the registrar of companies;
> - (c) all documents must clearly identify the appointment as an appointment of a replacement special administrator or an additional special administrator.
