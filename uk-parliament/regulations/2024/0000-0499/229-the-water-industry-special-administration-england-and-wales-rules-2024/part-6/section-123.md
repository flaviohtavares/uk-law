# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. Notice to company officers, bankrupts etc. in respect of meetings (rule 15.14)

Rule 15.14 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “In a decision procedure” to “by the court notice” there were substituted “Notice”;
- (b) paragraphs (2) and (3) were omitted.
