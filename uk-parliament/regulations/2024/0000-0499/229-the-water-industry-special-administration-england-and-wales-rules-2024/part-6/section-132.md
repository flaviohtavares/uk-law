# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Requisite majorities (rule 15.34)

Rule 15.34 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **15.34. Requisite majorities**
>
> (1) A decision is made by creditors when a majority (in value) of those voting have voted in favour of the proposed decision, except where this rule provides otherwise.
>
> (2) A decision is not made if those voting against it include more than half in value of the creditors to whom notice of the decision procedure was delivered who are not, to the best of the special administrator’s or chair’s belief, persons connected with the company.
