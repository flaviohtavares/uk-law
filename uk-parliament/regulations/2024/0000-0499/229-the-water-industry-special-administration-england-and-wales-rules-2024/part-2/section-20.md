# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Application of Chapter (rule 1.36)

Rule 1.36 has effect as if, in paragraph (1), for “under Parts 1 to 11 of the Act or the EU Regulation” there were substituted “within a special administration”.
