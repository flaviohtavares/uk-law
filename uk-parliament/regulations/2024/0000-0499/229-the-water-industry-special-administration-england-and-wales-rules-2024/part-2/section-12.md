# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Registrar of companies: covering notices (rule 1.20)

Rule 1.20 has effect as if—

- (a) paragraph (1)(b) were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (1A), sub-paragraphs (a), (c), (e), (f), (g), (l), (m), (n)(iii) and (iv) and (o) were omitted;
- (c) in paragraph (2), “or the directors (as the case may be)” were omitted.
