## 16. Standard contents of notices to be delivered to persons other than the registrar of companies (rule 1.28)

Rule 1.28 has effect as if, in paragraph (1), for “Part A1 to 11 of the Act or the EU Regulation” there were substituted “Parts 1 and 6 and Schedule B1”.
