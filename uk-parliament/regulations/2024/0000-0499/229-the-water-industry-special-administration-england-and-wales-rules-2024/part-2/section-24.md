# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Electronic delivery of documents (rule 1.45)

Rule 1.45 has effect as if, in paragraph (4), for “person who is the subject of the insolvency proceedings” there were substituted “company in special administration”.
