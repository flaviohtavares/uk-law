# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 108

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 108. Notice of declaration of a dividend (rule 14.35)

Rule 14.35 has effect as if, in paragraph (4), for “In an administration” to “or liquidator” there were substituted “Where the special administrator”.
