# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Administration and winding up: division of unsold assets (rule 14.13)

Rule 14.13 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), “in an administration or in a winding up of a company (other than a members’ voluntary winding up)” were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “the required permission” there were substituted “the agreement of the creditors”;
- (c) paragraph (3) were omitted.
