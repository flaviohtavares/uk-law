# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Exclusion of proof by the court (rule 14.11)

Rule 14.11 has effect as if, in paragraphs (1)(b) and 3(b), for “, a contributory or a bankrupt” there were substituted “or a contributory”.
