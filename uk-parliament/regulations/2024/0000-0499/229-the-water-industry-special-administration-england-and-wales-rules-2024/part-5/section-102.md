# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Interest (rule 14.23)

Rule 14.23 has effect as if, in paragraph (4)—

- (a) after “notice was delivered” there were inserted “before the relevant date”;
- (b) the words after “date of the payment” to the end were omitted.
