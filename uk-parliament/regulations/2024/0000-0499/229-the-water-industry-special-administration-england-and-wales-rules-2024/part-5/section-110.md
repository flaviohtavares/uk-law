## 110. Sole or final dividend (rule 14.38)

Rule 14.38 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in the words before sub-paragraph (a), “, in an administration or winding up,” were omitted;
  - (ii) sub-paragraphs (a), (b)(i) and (c) were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (2), “and (c)” were omitted.
