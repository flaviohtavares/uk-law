# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Proving a debt (rule 14.3)

Rule 14.3 has effect as if—

- (a) for paragraph (1) there were substituted—

  > (1) A creditor wishing to recover a debt must submit a proof to the special administrator unless this rule or an order of the court provides otherwise.

  ;
- (b) paragraph (2) were omitted.
