# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Office-holder not liable for costs under rule 14.8 (rule 14.9)

Rule 14.9 has effect as if—

- (a) paragraph (1) were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (2), “other than the official receiver” were omitted.
