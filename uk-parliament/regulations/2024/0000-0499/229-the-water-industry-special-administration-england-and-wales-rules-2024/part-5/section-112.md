# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Supplementary provisions as to dividends and distributions (rule 14.40)

Rule 14.40 has effect as if—

- (a) after paragraph (1)(a), “or” were inserted;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b), “in an administration, a creditors’ voluntary winding up or a winding up by the court” were omitted;
- (c) paragraph (1)(c), and the “or” preceding it, were omitted.
