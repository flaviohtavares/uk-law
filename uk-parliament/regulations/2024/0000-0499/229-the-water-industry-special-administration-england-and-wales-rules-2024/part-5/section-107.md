## 107. Further contents of notice to creditors owed small debts etc. (rule 14.31)

Rule 14.31 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), “or bankrupt” were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a), “or the bankrupt (as the case may be)” were omitted.
