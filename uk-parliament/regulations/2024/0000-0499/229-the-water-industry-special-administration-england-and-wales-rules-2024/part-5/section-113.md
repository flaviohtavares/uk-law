# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Administration and winding up: non-payment of dividend (rule 14.45)

Rule 14.45 has effect as if for paragraph (1) there were substituted—

> (1) No action lies against the special administrator for payment of a dividend.
