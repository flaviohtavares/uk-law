# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024 and come into force 21 days after the day on which they are laid.
