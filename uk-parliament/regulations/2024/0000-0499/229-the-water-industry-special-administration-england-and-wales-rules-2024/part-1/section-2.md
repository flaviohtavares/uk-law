# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Extent and application

(1) These Rules extend to England and Wales.

(2) These Rules apply to water industry companies in respect of which a court has made, or may make, a special administration order having effect at any time after these Rules come into force (including where such an order took effect before then).
