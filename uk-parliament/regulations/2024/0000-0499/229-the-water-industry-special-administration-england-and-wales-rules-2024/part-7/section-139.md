> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 139. Right of inspection and retention of proxies (rule 16.6)

Rule 16.6 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(b), “, or a meeting of contributories” were omitted;
- (b) after paragraph (3)(b), there were inserted “or”;
- (c) paragraph (3)(d), and the “or” preceding it, were omitted.
