# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 138

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 138. Use of proxies by the chair (rule 16.5)

Rule 16.5 has effect as if, in paragraph (3), “in an administration, creditors’ voluntary winding up, winding up by the court or a bankruptcy” were omitted.
