# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Notice of an order under section 176A(5) (rule 12.16)

Rule 12.16 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraphs (2), (3) and (5), “liquidator,” and “or receiver” were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (4)(b), “liquidator,” and “, or receiver” were omitted.
