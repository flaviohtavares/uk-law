# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Special administrator’s solicitor (rule 12.13A)

Chapter 3 of Part 12 has effect as if, after rule 12.13, there were inserted—

> **12.13A. Special administrator’s solicitor**
>
> If the attendance of the special administrator’s solicitor is required in special administration proceedings, the special administrator need not attend in person unless the court so directs.
