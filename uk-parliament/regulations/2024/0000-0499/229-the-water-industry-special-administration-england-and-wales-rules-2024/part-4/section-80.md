# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Costs and expenses of petitioners and other specified persons (rule 12.49)

Rule 12.49 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **12.49. Costs and expenses of petitioners and other specified persons**
>
> (1) Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court may be made to a relevant officer of the company to which the proceedings relate.
>
> (2) A person making any application to court within the special administration is not to be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the application, but a costs officer may allow that person’s expenses of travelling and subsistence.
