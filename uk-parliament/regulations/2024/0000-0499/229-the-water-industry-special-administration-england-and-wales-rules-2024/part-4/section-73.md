# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court (rule 12.31)

Rule 12.31 has effect as if, for that rule, there were substituted—

> **12.31. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court**
>
> Where a special administration is commenced in a court other than the High Court, that court may order the transfer of the proceedings to the High Court.
