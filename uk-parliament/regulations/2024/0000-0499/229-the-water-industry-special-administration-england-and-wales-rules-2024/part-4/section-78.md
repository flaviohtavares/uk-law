# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Awards of costs against an office-holder, the adjudicator or the official receiver (rule 12.47)

Rule 12.47 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **12.47. Awards of costs against a special administrator**
>
> Where a special administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, the special administrator is not to be personally liable for the costs unless the court otherwise directs.
