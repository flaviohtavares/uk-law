# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Preliminary (rule 12.6)

Rule 12.6 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **12.6. Preliminary (rule 12.6)**
>
> This Chapter applies to applications to the court except an application for a special administration order.
