# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Application of this sub-division and interpretation (rule 12.17)

Rule 12.17 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **12.17. Application of this sub-division and interpretation**
>
> (1) The rules in this sub-division apply to applications to the court for an order under section 236 (inquiry into company’s dealings).
>
> (2) In this sub-division—
>
> - “**applicable section**” means section 236; and
> - “**the insolvent**” means the company.
