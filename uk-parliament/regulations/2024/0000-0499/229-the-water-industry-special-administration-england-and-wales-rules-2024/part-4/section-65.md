# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Applications under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A (rule 12.14)

Rule 12.14 has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), “liquidator,” and “or receiver” were omitted;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for sub-paragraph (a) there were substituted—

  > that the company is in special administration.
