# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 145

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 145. Remuneration principles (rule 18.16)

Rule 18.16 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **18.16. Remuneration: principles**
>
> (1) A special administrator is entitled to receive remuneration for services as special administrator.
>
> (2) The basis of such remuneration is to be fixed by reference to the time properly spent by the special administrator and the special administrator’s staff in attending to matters arising in the special administration.
