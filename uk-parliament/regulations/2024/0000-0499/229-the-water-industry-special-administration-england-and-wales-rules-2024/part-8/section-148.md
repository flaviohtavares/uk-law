# The Water Industry (Special Administration) (England and Wales) Rules 2024, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Remuneration: new administrator, liquidator or trustee (rule 18.31)

Rule 18.31 has effect as if for that rule there were substituted—

> **18.31. Remuneration: new administrator, liquidator or trustee**
>
> (1) If a new special administrator is appointed in place of a special administrator, any court order in effect under rule 18.23 (remuneration: application to the court to fix the basis) immediately before the former special administrator ceased to hold office continues to apply in respect of the remuneration of the new special administrator until a further court order is made in accordance with those provisions.
>
> (2) Where a special administrator has ceased to act as such for any reason, the special administrator (or the special administrator’s estate, as the case may be) is to be entitled to any amounts outstanding on the basis of the services which that special administrator provided in accordance with Rule 18.23.
