# The A12 Chelmsford to A120 Widening Development Consent Order 2024, section 28

> Text as at 2025-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-a31d5ac6fb715155db06be9d9b67e4b9]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00066] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
