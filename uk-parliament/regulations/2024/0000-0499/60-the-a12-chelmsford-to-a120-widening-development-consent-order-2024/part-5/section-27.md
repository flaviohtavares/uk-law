# The A12 Chelmsford to A120 Widening Development Consent Order 2024, section 27

> Text as at 2025-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-892b6ba071b2b4711e0cf3785bb60c9e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or is incidental to it, or is required as replacement land.

(2) This article is subject to—

- (a) article 30(2) (compulsory acquisition of rights and imposition of restrictive covenants);
- (b) article 40(9) (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development); and
- (c) article 52 (crown rights).
