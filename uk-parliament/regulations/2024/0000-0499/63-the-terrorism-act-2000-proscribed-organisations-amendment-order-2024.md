# The Terrorism Act 2000 (Proscribed Organisations) (Amendment) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 63

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Terrorism Act 2000 (Proscribed Organisations) (Amendment) Order 2024

Made

18th January 2024

Coming into force

19th January 2024

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(3)(a) of the Terrorism Act 2000[^f00001].

The Secretary of State believes that the organisation to be added to Schedule 2 to that Act[^f00002] by article 2 of this Order is concerned in terrorism[^f00003].

In accordance with section 123(4)(a) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Terrorism Act 2000 (Proscribed Organisations) (Amendment) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

## 2. Proscribed organisation

In Schedule 2 to the Terrorism Act 2000 (proscribed organisations), after the entry for “Wagner Group”, insert—

> |  |
> | --- |
> | Hizb ut-Tahrir |

.

Tom Tugendhat

Minister of State

18th January 2024

Home Office

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Part 2 of the Terrorism Act [2000 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11) makes provision for proscribed organisations (including setting out offences in relation to such organisations in sections 11 to 13). An organisation is proscribed if it is listed in Schedule 2 to that Act or, in most cases, it operates under the same name as an organisation so listed (section 3(1)).

Article 2 of this Order adds Hizb ut-Tahrir to the list in Schedule 2 as a new entry.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
