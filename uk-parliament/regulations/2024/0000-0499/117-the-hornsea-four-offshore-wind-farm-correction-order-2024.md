# The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been published in substitution of [S.I. 2024/113](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/113) to correct the omission of that instrument not being printed.

2024 No. 117

Infrastructure Planning

The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2024

Made

30th January 2024

Coming into force

31st January 2024

The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023 under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed East Riding of Yorkshire Council as the relevant local planning authority[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on 31st January 2024.

## 2. Corrections

The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to the Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

30th January 2024
