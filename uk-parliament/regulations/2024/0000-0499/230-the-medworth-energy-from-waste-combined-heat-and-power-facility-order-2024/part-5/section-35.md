# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 35

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Apparatus and rights of statutory undertakers in altered or closed streets[^key-25e3c159bcd7b2c28cae8ba0009094e6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a street is altered or diverted or its use is temporarily prohibited or restricted under article 10 (street works), article 11 (power to alter layout, etc., of streets), article 12 (construction and maintenance of new or altered means of access) or article 13 (temporary prohibition or restriction of use of streets and public rights of way) any statutory undertaker whose apparatus is under, in, on, along or across the street has the same powers and rights in respect of that apparatus, subject to Schedule 11 (protective provisions), as if this Order had not been made.
