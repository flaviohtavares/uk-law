# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 7

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 7. Benefit of this Order[^c-161f4c5849f60cd1bdd197b93c4c6886]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), paragraph (3) and article 8 (consent to transfer benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to Work No. 6A and 6B in respect of which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of the undertaker and Anglian Water.

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to Work No. 9 in respect of which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of the undertaker and EPN.
