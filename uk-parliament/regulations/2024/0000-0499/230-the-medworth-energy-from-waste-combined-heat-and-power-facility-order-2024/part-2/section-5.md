# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 5

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Operation of the authorised development[^key-df0f5587b7890177f126b41142a88595]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to operate and use the authorised development for which development consent is granted by this Order..

(2) Other than as set out in this Order, this article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required to authorise the operation of an electricity generating station.
