# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 3

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent granted by the Order[^key-523573c1efe366d95afa17a0f404ef05]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the requirements, the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Each numbered work must be situated within the corresponding numbered area shown on the works plans.
