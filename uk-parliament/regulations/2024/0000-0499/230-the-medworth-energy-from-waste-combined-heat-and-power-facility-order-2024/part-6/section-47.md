# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 47

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Incorporation of the mineral code[^c-4a08c53228cbb3cbde972d3ad552e58f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00036] are incorporated in this order subject to the following modifications—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
