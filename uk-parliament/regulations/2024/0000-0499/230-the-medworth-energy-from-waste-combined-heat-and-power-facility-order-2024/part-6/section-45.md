# The Medworth Energy from Waste Combined Heat and Power Facility Order 2024, section 45

> Text as at 2024-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. No double recovery[^c-dd9b2cdbb31da08292605eada0b2714d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
