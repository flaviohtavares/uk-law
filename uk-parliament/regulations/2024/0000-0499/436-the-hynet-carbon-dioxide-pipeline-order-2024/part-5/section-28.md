# The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024, section 28

> Text as at 2024-10-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^key-3bf4bdaf6c824c39eb460b3be8c2e274]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00030] are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
