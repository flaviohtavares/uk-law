# The Securitisation Regulations 2024, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-11-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2024/102
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement[^key-233bad933114602dd750a506c8de34e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions come into force on the day after that on which these Regulations are made—

- (a) this Part,
- (b) Part 2 (designated activities),
- (c) regulation 8 (matters to which FCA and PRA must have regard when making rules relating to securitisation),
- (d) regulation 13 (designation of country or territory in relation to securitisations), for the purpose only of enabling the Treasury to make regulations, and
- (e) the remaining provisions, for the purposes only of enabling the FCA or the PRA—
  - (i) to make rules,
  - (ii) to give directions or guidance, or
  - (iii) to issue statements of policy.

(2) So far as not already in force by virtue of paragraph (1), these Regulations come into force on 1st November 2024[^key-e763d22dea48484e83c24c29f1bbcc73] (“the main commencement day”).
