## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendments to other legislation and consequential amendments

Regulation 51

### 1. Amendment to EMIR[^key-bd4e693a5ddcbf959c0aa4277f264992]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### 2. Amendment to the Capital Requirements Regulation[^key-a79881c55b34d38a3637e0785774a8cf]

For Article 242(10)(b) (meaning of “simple, transparent and standardised securitisation” or “STS securitisation”) of the Capital Requirements Regulation[^f00064] substitute—

> - (b) an overseas STS securitisation as defined in regulation 12(2) of the Securitisation Regulations 2024;

.

### 3. Amendment to the Solvency 2 Delegated Regulation[^key-83ae48e3fa924d35b74360b7929f1059]

For Article 1(18b)(b) (meaning of “**STS securitisation**”) of Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2015/35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/0035) of 10 October 2014 supplementing [Directive 2009/138/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0138) on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II)[^f00065] substitute—

> - (b) an overseas STS securitisation as defined in regulation 12(2) of the Securitisation Regulations 2024;

.

### 4. Amendment to the Money Market Funds Regulation[^key-c301d573adc2da11bc8bb01c791e0781]

For Article 11(1)(d) of [Regulation (EU) 2017/1131](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1131) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 on money market funds (eligible securitisations and ABCPs)[^f00066] substitute—

> - (d) an overseas STS securitisation as defined in regulation 12(2) of the Securitisation Regulations 2024.

.
