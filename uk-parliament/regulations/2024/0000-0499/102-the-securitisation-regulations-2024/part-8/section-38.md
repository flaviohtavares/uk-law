# The Securitisation Regulations 2024, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-11-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2024/102

## 38. Temporary prohibition relating to management functions: obligations on originator, sponsor or SSPE[^c-a0ab867cff6358b4c1489a6701694e75]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An originator, sponsor or SSPE must take reasonable care to ensure that no individual holds an office or position involving responsibility for taking decisions about the management of that entity in contravention of a temporary prohibition imposed under regulation 37(1) or (2).
