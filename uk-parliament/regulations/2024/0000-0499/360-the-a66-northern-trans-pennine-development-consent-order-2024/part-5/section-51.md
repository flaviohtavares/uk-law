# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024, section 51

> Text as at 2026-04-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Arbitration[^c-078628ff8fa806ed9bb8b41946a2c26f]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where otherwise expressly provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed in writing between the parties, any difference under any provision of this Order (other than a difference which falls to be determined by the tribunal) must be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
