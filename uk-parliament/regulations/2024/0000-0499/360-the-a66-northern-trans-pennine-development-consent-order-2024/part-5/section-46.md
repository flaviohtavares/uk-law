# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024, section 46

> Text as at 2026-04-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Operational land for the purposes of the 1990 Act[^key-d7dd179721c303ffd170bda738dd0d19]

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order for development on Order land is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act) of the 1990 Act.
