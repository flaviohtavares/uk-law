# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024, section 55

> Text as at 2026-04-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Time limit for when development must begin[^key-75591143597ab6899148f6d5c6b49e4c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The authorised development must not begin later than the expiration of 5 years beginning with the date on which this Order comes into force.

(2) Section 155 (when development begins) of the 2008 Act applies to this article.
