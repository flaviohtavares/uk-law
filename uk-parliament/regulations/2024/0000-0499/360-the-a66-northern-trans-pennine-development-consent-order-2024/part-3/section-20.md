# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024, section 20

> Text as at 2026-04-16.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the minerals code[^key-1921c854ee09a2f1e6dc97ddc126857a]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
