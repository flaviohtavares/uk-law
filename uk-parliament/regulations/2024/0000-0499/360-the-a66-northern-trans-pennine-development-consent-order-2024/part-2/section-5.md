# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024, section 5

> Text as at 2026-04-16.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Maintenance of the authorised development[^key-4e8dc6eec58b6a70f2903735295bb458]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
