# The Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, section 5

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. “Relevant securities”[^key-4051f5be2295b79e16e759c1a0f98270]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations “**relevant securities**” means—

- (a) transferable securities, other than excluded securities, and
- (b) investments that—
  - (i) are of a kind specified for the purposes of section 22 of FSMA 2000 (regulated activities) as a result of article 77 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001[^f00012] (instruments creating or acknowledging indebtedness), but
  - (ii) are not transferable securities or excluded securities.

(2) In this regulation “**excluded securities**” has the meaning given in regulation 6.
