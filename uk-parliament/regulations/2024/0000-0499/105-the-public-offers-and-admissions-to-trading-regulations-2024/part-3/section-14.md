# The Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, section 14

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. FCA rules relating to admissions to trading on regulated market[^key-fd07125fcd9fd98b94f008ff7934319e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may make designated activity rules relating to—

- (a) the carrying on of any of the activities specified in regulation 9—
  - (i) in connection with an admission, or proposed admission, of transferable securities to trading on a regulated market, or
  - (ii) in relation to transferable securities that have already been admitted, or are of a class that has already been admitted, to trading on a regulated market, or
- (b) the carrying on of any of the activities specified in regulation 10.

(2) Designated activity rules made by virtue of this regulation are referred to in these Regulations as “**regulated market admission rules**”.
