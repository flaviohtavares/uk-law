# The Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, section 19

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Matters to which FCA must have regard when making certain rules[^key-79b09926260a1dc1bea17ad2956b284d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The desirability of facilitating offers of transferable securities in the United Kingdom being made to a wide range of investors is a matter specified for the purposes of section 138EA of FSMA 2000[^f00029] in relation to the making of—

- (a) regulated market admission rules, and
- (b) rules made by virtue of regulation 15.
