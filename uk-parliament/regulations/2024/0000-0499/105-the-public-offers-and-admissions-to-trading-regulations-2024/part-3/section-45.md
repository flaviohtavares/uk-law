# The Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, section 45

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Reporting of infringements[^c-64bbc1d2922b7f999520334dce0f7b73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to an employer who—

- (a) provides regulated financial services,
- (b) carries on regulated activities in reliance on the exemption in section 327 of FSMA 2000 (exemption from the general prohibition), or
- (c) is a recognised investment exchange, a recognised clearing house, a recognised CSD or a third country central counterparty[^f00037].

(2) The employer must have in place appropriate internal procedures for their employees to report, through an independent channel, contraventions or potential contraventions of an applicable provision.

(3) In this regulation—

- “**an applicable provision**” has the same meaning as in regulation 44;
- “employer” and “employee” each has the meaning given in section 230 of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00038];
- “**regulated financial services**” has the meaning given in section 1H(2) of FSMA 2000[^f00039].
