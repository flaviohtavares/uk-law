# The Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, section 9

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Designated activities: public offers of relevant securities[^c-13c5de145c857b8902d569d7dcd20a85]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following activities are specified under section 71K of FSMA 2000[^f00026] as designated activities for the purposes of that Act—

- (a) offering relevant securities to the public in the United Kingdom;
- (b) communicating an advertisement relating to an offer of relevant securities to the public in the United Kingdom;
- (c) disclosing, otherwise than in an advertisement, information relating to such an offer.
