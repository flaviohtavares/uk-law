# The Net Zero Teesside Order 2024, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Deemed marine licence[^key-59f27cf9506d7951fc0f4c5168ac1f53]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 10 and 11 are deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensed activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 2, of each licence.
