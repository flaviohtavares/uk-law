# The Net Zero Teesside Order 2024, section 21A

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21A. Supplemental land[^c-66db9fedace8fd1435777c28cfcabb34]

*Extent: E+W+S*

— The undertaker may not compulsorily acquire any interest in or rights over or take temporary possession of any part of the supplemental land pursuant to articles 22, 25, 30, 31, 32 and 33.
