# The Net Zero Teesside Order 2024, section 6

> Text as at 2026-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Operation of authorised development[^key-2240758d62fe2736437d26ee462bf017]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is hereby authorised to use and to operate the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of the authorised development.
