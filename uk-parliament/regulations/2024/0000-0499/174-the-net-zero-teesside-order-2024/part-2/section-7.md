> Text as at 2026-07-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 7. Benefit of this Order[^c-da39174bc874a2a26d8ae336fdb0640c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 8 (consent to transfer benefit of this Order), the provisions of this Order have effect—

- (a) solely for the benefit of NZT Power in respect of Project A; and
- (b) solely for the benefit of NZNS Storage in respect of Project B.
