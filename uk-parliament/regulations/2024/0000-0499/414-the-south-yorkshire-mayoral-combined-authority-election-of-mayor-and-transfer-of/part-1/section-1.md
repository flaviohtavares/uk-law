# The South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (Election of Mayor and Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions) Order 2024, section 1

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-4ba5a6bffa00531119e98d27776f243d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Order may be cited as the South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (Election of Mayor and Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions) Order 2024.

(2) This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.
