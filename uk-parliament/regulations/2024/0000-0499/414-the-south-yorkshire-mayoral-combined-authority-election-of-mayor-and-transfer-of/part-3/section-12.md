# The South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (Election of Mayor and Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions) Order 2024, section 12

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Modification to the Local Government Act 1972[^key-e7d0ad659d7f03e111b71d66097c3533]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 86 of the Local Government Act 1972 (declaration by local authority of vacancy in office in certain cases)[^f00020] applies in relation to the Mayor with the following modification.

(2) After section 86(1)(c), insert—

> or
>
> - (d) ceases to be the Mayor by virtue of section 63 (vacancy where acting commissioner acts for 6 months) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011,

.
