# The South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (Election of Mayor and Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions) Order 2024, section 13

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Consequential amendments[^key-ad8947d11fb69e28a4bc634d1c972b3b]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections Order 2012[^f00021]—

- (a) in article 34 (prohibition of candidate election expenses not authorised by election agent), in table 1 in paragraph (4), omit the entry for “South Yorkshire”;
- (b) in article 35 (limitation of candidate election expenses), in table 2 in paragraph (2), omit the entry for “South Yorkshire”.
