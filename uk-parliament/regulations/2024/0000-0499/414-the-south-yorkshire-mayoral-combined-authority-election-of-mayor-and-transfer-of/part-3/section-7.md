# The South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (Election of Mayor and Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions) Order 2024, section 7

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Secondments[^c-d534f76ed3ad23aa3da9a5ddebd9f1d7]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a person who immediately before 7th May 2024 is seconded to the police and crime commissioner for South Yorkshire, the secondment is to have effect, after that time, as a secondment to the Combined Authority.
