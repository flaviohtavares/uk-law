# The Sea Fisheries (International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 1st April 2024.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
