# The Sea Fisheries (International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Amendment of Regulation 2015/98

(1) Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2015/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/0098) on the implementation of the Union’s international obligations, as referred to in Article 15(2) of [Regulation (EU) 1380/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1380) of the European Parliament and of the Council, under the International Convention for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas and the Convention on Future Multilateral Cooperation in the Northwest Atlantic Fisheries[^f00023] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 (subject matter and scope)—

- (a) omit “the International Convention for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas and”;
- (b) omit “in United Kingdom waters or”.

(3) Omit Chapter 2 (ICCAT Convention area).
