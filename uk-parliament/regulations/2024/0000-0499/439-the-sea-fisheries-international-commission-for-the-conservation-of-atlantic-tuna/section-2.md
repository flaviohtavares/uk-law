# The Sea Fisheries (International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1936/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1936)

(1) Council Regulation [(EC) No 1936/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1936) laying down control measures applicable to fishing for certain stocks of highly migratory fish[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 2 (zones) omit point (a).

(3) Omit Chapter 1 (control and inspection measures applicable in zone 1).

(4) Omit Annexes 1a and 2.
