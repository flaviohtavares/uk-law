# The Sea Fisheries (International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of the Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00046] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 15 (Council Regulation [(EC) No 520/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0520) laying down technical measures for the conservation of certain stocks of highly migratory species) omit paragraphs (3) to (10).
