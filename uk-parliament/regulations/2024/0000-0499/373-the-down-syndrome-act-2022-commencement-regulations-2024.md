# The Down Syndrome Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 373 (C. 20)

MENTAL HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Down Syndrome Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2024

Made

13th March 2024

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(2) of the Down Syndrome Act 2022[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Down Syndrome Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2024.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Down Syndrome Act 2022 come into force on 18th March 2024.

Maria Caulfield

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health and Social Care

13th March 2024

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force, on 18 March 2024, the Down Syndrome Act 2022. The Down Syndrome Act 2022 imposes a duty on the Secretary of State to give guidance to certain public authorities specified in the Act about how to meet the needs of persons with Down Syndrome when exercising certain specified functions.
