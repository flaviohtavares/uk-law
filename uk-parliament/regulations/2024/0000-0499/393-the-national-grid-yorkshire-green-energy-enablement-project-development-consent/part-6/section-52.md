# The National Grid (Yorkshire Green Energy Enablement Project) Development Consent Order 2024, section 52

> Text as at 2024-06-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Arbitration[^c-543bfeb83a60dd1c97a46a737fcf94f0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 50 (procedure regarding certain approvals, etc.) and except where otherwise expressly provided for in this Order or unless otherwise agreed between the parties, any difference under any provision of this Order must be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
