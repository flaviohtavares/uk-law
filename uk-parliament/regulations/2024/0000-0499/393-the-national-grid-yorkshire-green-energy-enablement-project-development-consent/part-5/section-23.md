# The National Grid (Yorkshire Green Energy Enablement Project) Development Consent Order 2024, section 23

> Text as at 2024-06-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-1548084e327be972e36b331624e4ec35]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00057] are incorporated into this Order subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
