# The Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1598

ARBITRATION

The Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 1988

Made

13th September 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9(2) of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 (Appointed Day No. 1) Order 1988.

**2.** The appointed day for sections 1 to 5 of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 is 1st October 1988.

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry.

13th September 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order provides that the appointed day for sections 1 to 5 of the Consumer Arbitration Agreements Act 1988 (which are concerned with England, Wales and Northern Ireland) is 1st October 1988.
