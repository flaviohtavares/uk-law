# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 34) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1901

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 34) Order 1988

Made

30th October 1988

Coming into force

1st December 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 34) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st December 1988.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

30th October 1988

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### PART I — DESIGNATED OWNERS

- Britoil p.l.c., whose registered office is at 301 St. Vincent Street, Glasgow G2 5DD.
- Conoco Developments Ltd., whose registered office is at Park House, 116 Park Street, London W1Y 4NN.
- Esso Exploration and Production UK Ltd., whose registered office is at Esso House, Victoria Street, London SW1E 5JW. Shell U.K. Ltd., whose registered office is at Shell-Mex House, Strand, London WC2 0DX.
