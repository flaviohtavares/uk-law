# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 4

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Persons of prescribed description[^key-058b8d3e81f95b70258f5d43b82fe43f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 20(11) of the Act (definition of family) as it applies to housing benefit is a person aged 16 or over but under 19 who is treated as a child for the purposes of section 2 of the Child Benefit Act 1975[^f00020] (meaning of child), and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “young person”.

(2) Paragaph (1) shall not apply to a person who is on income support.
