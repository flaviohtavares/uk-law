# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 72

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 72. Effect of revising a decision[^c-333130bdc71a8fb55aaa9fd4749892b7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a Review Board has decided that a determination or, as the case may be, a revised determination of an authority shall be revised, the authority shall alter its determination or, as the case may be, revised determination in accordance with that decision with effect from the date of determination or, as the case may be, revised determination.
