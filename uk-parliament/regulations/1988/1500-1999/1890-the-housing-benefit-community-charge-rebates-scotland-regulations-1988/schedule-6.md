# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — CONSTITUTION OF REVIEW BOARDS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 68(3)

**1.**[^c-85150fe4261449e6476ffad9edaa4560] A Review Board appointed by an authority listed in column (1) of the Table below shall consist of not less than three of the persons specified in relation to that authority in column (2) of that Table.

Table

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
<tr><th><em>Authority</em></th><th><em>Composition of Board</em></th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. A levying authority or district council.</td><td>1. Councillors of that authority or council.</td></tr>
<tr><td>2. A New Town Corporation.</td><td>2. Members of that development corporation established under the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3. The Scottish Special Housing Association.</td><td>3. Members of the Association.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^M_F_611a2307-2806-4937-bee0-f59507b19820][^f00100]

**2.**[^c-8001677d9fb5b9886b480f20bab82d40] The members of a Review Board shall appoint one of their number to be the Chairman.
