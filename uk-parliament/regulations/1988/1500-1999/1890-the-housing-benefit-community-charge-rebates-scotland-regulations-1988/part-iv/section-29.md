# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 29

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^key-6167758351992ef467c1510c23ba9902]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant’s family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
