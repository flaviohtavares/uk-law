# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 35

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Calculation of tariff income from capital[^key-e0150c00d289ff902e8af9f48c9d01d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the claimant’s capital calculated in accordance with this Part exceeds £3000 it shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly tariff income of £1 for each complete £250 in excess of £3000 but not exceeding £8000.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) where any part of the excess is not a complete £250 that part shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly tariff income of £1.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1), capital includes any income treated as capital under regulation 30 (income treated as capital)[^key-90322d8c93d2663aa2d508fcab812986].
