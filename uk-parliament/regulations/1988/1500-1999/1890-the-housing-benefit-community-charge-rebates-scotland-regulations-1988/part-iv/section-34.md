# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 34

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Capital jointly held[^key-45af0ae305ef088e154458276b60c14e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Except where a claimant possesses capital which is disregarded under regulation 33(4) (notional capital) where a claimant and one or more persons are beneficially entitled in possession to any capital asset they shall be treated as if each of them were entitled in possession to the whole beneficial interest therein in an equal share and the foregoing provisions of this Chapter shall apply for the purposes of calculating the amount of capital which the claimant is treated as possessing as if it were actual capital which the claimant does possess.[^key-67ba86728142e0322307386a916027d7]
