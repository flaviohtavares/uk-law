# The Housing Benefit (Community Charge Rebates) (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 51

> Text as at 1990-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Minimum housing benefit[^c-b617fc758e2a7966832943c224431a02]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Housing benefit shall not be payable in respect of any claim where the weekly amount awarded in consequence of that claim would be less than 50 pence.
