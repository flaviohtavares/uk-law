# The Tyne and Wear Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument corrects an error in [S.I. 1988/1590](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1590) and is to be issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Instrument.

1988 No. 1615

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Tyne and Wear Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made

21st September 1988

Laid before Parliament

21st September 1988

Coming into force

10th October 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 10th October 1988.

**2.** In article 11 of the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body (Winding Up) Order 1988[^f00002] there shall be inserted before the figures “1988” the words “31st October”.

Nicholas Ridley

Secretary of State for the Environment

21st September 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order inserts the date 31st October 1988 in article 11 (functions relating to accounts of the Residuary Body) of the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body (Winding Up) Order 1988 ([S.I. 1988/1590](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1590)), making good the omission of a date from that article.
