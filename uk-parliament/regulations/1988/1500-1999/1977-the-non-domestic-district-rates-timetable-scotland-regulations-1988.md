# The Non-Domestic District Rates (Timetable) (Scotland) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1977 (S.189)

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic District Rates (Timetable) (Scotland) Regulations 1988

Made

10th November 1988

Coming into force

2nd December 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 110A(3) and 235(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973( [^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic District Rates (Timetable) (Scotland) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1988.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the 1973 Act” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973.

## 3. Prescribed date for notifying estimate of non-domestic district rate

For the purposes of section 110A(3) of the 1973 Act (date before which a regional council is to notify a district council of their estimate of amount due to the district council for the non-domestic district rate for a financial year), the date prescribed in relation to the financial year 1989-90 and each subsequent financial year is 15th December in the financial year immediately preceding each of those years.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe, in relation to the financial year 1989-90 and each subsequent financial year, 15th December in the immediately preceding financial year as the date before which a regional council is required to notify a district council of its estimate of the amount which it is liable to pay to the district council in respect of the non-domestic district rate for the following financial year.
