# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Condition of grant

It shall be a condition of the payment of any grant under section 73(c) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00006] to the governing body of any central institution that the governing body of that institution shall comply with the requirements imposed on them by these Regulations.
