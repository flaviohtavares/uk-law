# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Variations and revocations of provisions of instruments

The provisions of the enactments, schemes, articles of association and other instruments specified in Column (1) of Schedule 2 to these Regulations (being, by virtue of regulation 21(2), the provisions of such instruments without any variation or revocation effected by the Regulations or Schemes revoked by regulation 21(1)) are hereby varied or revoked (or both varied and revoked), as the case may be, to the extent (being the extent to which the provisions of such instruments are in the opinion of the Secretary of State inconsistent with these Regulations) specified opposite such instruments in Column (2) of that Schedule.
