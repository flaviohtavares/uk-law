# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Allowances

The governing body of any central institution may pay to any of its members such financial loss allowances and travelling and subsistence allowances, subject to the same limits, as are payable to members of local authorities in Scotland under sections 45(4) and 46 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00005].
