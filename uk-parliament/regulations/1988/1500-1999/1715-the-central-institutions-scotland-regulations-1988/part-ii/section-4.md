# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. The composition of the governing bodies

The governing body of each central institution shall be composed of a core membership appointed in accordance with regulation 5 and a co-opted membership appointed in accordance with regulation 6.
