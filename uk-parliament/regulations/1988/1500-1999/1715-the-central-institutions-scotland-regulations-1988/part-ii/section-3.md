# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Recomposition of governing bodies

As from 1st January 1989, and subject to regulation 19, the composition and appointment of the membership of the governing bodies of the central institutions shall be in accordance with these Regulations.
