# The Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Central Institutions (Scotland) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force for the purposes of regulation 19(4) on 1st November 1988 and for all other purposes on 1st January 1989.
