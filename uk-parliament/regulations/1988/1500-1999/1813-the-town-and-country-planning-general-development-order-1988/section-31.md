# The Town and Country Planning General Development Order 1988, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Revocations and savings

(1) The statutory instruments specified in Schedule 7 hereto are hereby revoked.

(2) Any direction made by a local highway authority under article 12 of the Town and Country Planning General Development Order 1977[^f00044] shall cease to have effect except insofar as it relates to applications made before the date of coming into force of this order.
