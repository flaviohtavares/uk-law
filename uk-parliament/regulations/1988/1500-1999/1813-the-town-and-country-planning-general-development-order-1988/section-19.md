# The Town and Country Planning General Development Order 1988, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Consultation with county planning authority

Where a district planning authority are required by paragraph 19 of Schedule 16 to the Local Government Act 1972[^f00036] to consult the county planning authority before determining an application for planning permission, they shall not determine the application until the expiry of at least 14 days after the date of the notice given to the county planning authority in accordance with sub-paragraph (5)(a) of that paragraph.

## Annotations

[^f00036]: Footnote: [1972 c. 70](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70); a substituted paragraph 19 was inserted into Schedule 16 to the Local Government Act 1972 by section 86(2) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980.
