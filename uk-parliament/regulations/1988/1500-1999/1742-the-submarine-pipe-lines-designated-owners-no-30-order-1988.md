# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 30) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1742

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 30) Order 1988

Made

6th October 1988

Coming into force

31st October 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabing him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 30) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 31st October 1988.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of so much of each of the pipe-lines specified in Part II of the Schedule hereto as is a controlled pipe-line.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

6th October 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of that part of each of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule which lies in controlled waters, that is, the territorial sea and areas designated under the Continental Shelf Act [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29). The pipe-lines lie between the Hewett gas field off the Norfolk coast and Phillip’s terminal at Bacton in Norfolk.
