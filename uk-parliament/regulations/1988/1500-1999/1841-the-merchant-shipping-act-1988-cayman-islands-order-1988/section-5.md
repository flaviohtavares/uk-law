# The Merchant Shipping Act 1988 (Cayman Islands) Order 1988, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**5.** Subject to the modifications specified in Schedule 1 to this Order, Schedule 7 to the Merchant Shipping Act 1988 in so far as it relates to the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, the Merchant Shipping Act 1970, the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) Act 1971 and the Merchant Shipping Act 1974[^f00005] shall extend to the Cayman Islands as part of the law thereof.
