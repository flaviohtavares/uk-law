# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section D3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D3. Past period for which additional contributions have been paid

(1) A person—

- (a) who has paid additional contributions for a past period in accordance with Part II or Part IV of Schedule 4, or
- (b) who has completed payment of additional contributions in accordance with Part I or Part III of Schedule 4, or with Schedule 5,

is entitled to count the past period as reckonable service.

(2) A person who has begun to pay additional contributions in accordance with Part I or Part III of Schedule 4, or has continued to pay them in accordance with Schedule 5, but who has not completed payment is entitled to count as reckonable service a period calculated in accordance with Schedule 7.
