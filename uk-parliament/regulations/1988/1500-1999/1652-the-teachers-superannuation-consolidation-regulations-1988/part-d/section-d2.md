# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section D2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D2. Exclusion from reckonable service

The following do not count as reckonable service:

- (a) any contributions refund period, and
- (b) any period in respect of which a transfer value has been paid under regulation F1 or a cash equivalent has been paid,

and where a period related to war service counted by virtue of regulation 4(1)(d) or regulations 7 and 72(1)(a) of the 1976 Regulations, regulations D5 and D6 do not apply.
