# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section E16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E16. Short service annuity

(1) A person who—

- (a) has not been in pensionable employment at any time after 5th April 1980, and
- (b) is not qualified for retirement benefits, and
- (c) would but for regulation 46(2) of the 1976 Regulations (salary exceeding £5,000) have been entitled to be repaid the balance of his contributions,

is entitled to be paid, from his 60th birthday, an annuity for life.

(2) The rate of the annuity is the actuarial equivalent of the balance of his contributions calculated in accordance with regulation C11 as at his 60th birthday.
