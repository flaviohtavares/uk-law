# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section E28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E28. Commencement and duration of long-term family pensions

(1) A pension under regulation E25 payable to a surviving spouse or a nominated beneficiary (“an adult pension”) is to be paid—

- (a) from the day on which any short-term pension that became so payable under regulation E23 ceases to be payable, or
- (b) if no short term pension became payable, from the day after that of the death.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), an adult pension is payable for life.

(3) Unless the Secretary of State determines otherwise in the particular case, and subject always to regulation E1(3)(c) and (d) (guaranteed minimum pension for surviving spouse), an adult pension is not payable during or after any marriage or period of cohabitation outside marriage.

(4) A pension under regulation E25 payable to or for the benefit of a child or children (“a children’s pension”) is to be paid—

- (a) from the day on which any short-term pension that became so payable, or payable to a surviving spouse or a nominated beneficiary, under regulation E23 ceases to be payable, or
- (b) if no short-term pension became payable, from the day after that of the death.

(5) A children’s pension ceases to be payable to a person or for his benefit when he ceases to be a child.
