# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section E10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E10. National Insurance modification of pensions

Part I of Schedule 10 has effect for modifying the retirement pensions of persons who were in pensionable employment before 1st April 1980.
