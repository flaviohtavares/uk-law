# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section G1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G1. Teachers' superannuation account

Subject to the transitional provisions in Part I of Schedule 13, an account substantially in the form set out in Part II of Schedule 13 is to be prepared by the Secretary of State for every financial year.
