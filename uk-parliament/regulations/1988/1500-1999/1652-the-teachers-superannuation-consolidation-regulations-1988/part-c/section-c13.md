# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section C13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C13. Application for repayment

(1) An amount to which a person is entitled under regulation C10 or C12 is to be paid to him only if the Secretary of State receives a written application for payment and paragraph (2), if applicable, is complied with.

(2) If the Secretary of State notifies him in writing that he so requires, the applicant is to provide any relevant information specified by the Secretary of State that is in his possession or that he can reasonably be expected to obtain.
