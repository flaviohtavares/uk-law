# The Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988, section C2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C2. Ordinary contributions

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), a person, other than a services education officer, who is in pensionable employment is to pay contributions of 6 per cent of this contributable salary for the time being.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), a services education officer is to pay contributions of the aggregate of—

- (a) 6 per cent of his contributable salary for the time being, and
- (b) the employer’s contributions that would have been payable if regulation G5 had applied.

(3) No contributions are to be paid by anyone to whom regulation E30(2)(a) (restriction of reckonable service to 45 years) has become applicable.
