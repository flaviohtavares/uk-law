# The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment (No.2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1893 (S.181)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment (No.2) Order 1988

Made

31st October 1988

Laid before Parliament

9th November 1988

Coming into force

30th November 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895( [^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sheriff Court Fees Amendment (No.2) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 30th November 1988.

**2.** Article 2 of the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985( [^f00002]) shall be amended by adding- “” small claim “ has the meaning assigned to it by the Small Claims (Scotland) Order 1988.” .

**3.** Article 9 of the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985 shall be amended by adding at the end the following paragraph:—

> (2) Except in relation to the fees specified in item 27A in Table A Fees, the fees otherwise payable by a debtor or a creditor in terms of this Order shall not apply to any proceedings under the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987( [^f00003]).

.

**4.** In Part II of the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985 at item 16- (a) insert after the word “Cause” the words “(including small claim)” ;

- (b) in Column 1 (Matters) below “2. Other actions” in item 16 insert—

  > **3.** In relation to a small claim summons, citation of, or intimation to, any party by sheriff officer.

  ;
- (c) in Column 2 (Fee payable) insert “ £5.50” ;
- (d) in Column 3 (Fee formerly payable) insert “—” .

**5.** In Part II of the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985 below item 27- (a) in Column 1 (Matters) insert “27A Inspection of report of sale and the auditor of court’s report” ;

- (b) in Column 2 (Fee payable) insert “ £3.50” ; and
- (c) in Column 3 (Fee formerly payable) insert “—” .

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

26th October 1988 We concur,

David Lightbown

David Maclean

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

31st October 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order imposes two new fees relating to new legislation. Item 16.3 of the Table of Fees is for citations or intimations by sheriff officer in cases heard under the Small Claims procedure. Item 27A is for the inspection of reports of sales and auditor of court’s reports under the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987. Fees prescribed under item 27A are the only court fees payable in relation to proceedings under the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987.
