# The Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1670 (C. 59)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made

26th September 1988

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 4(2) of the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988.

**2.** The Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 shall come into force on 1st November 1988.

Home Office

Douglas Hurd

26th September 1988

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 into force on 1st November 1988.
