# The Education (Publication of Proposals for Reduction in Standard Number) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1515

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Publication of Proposals for Reduction in Standard Number) Regulations 1988

Made

24th August 1988

Laid before Parliament

8th September 1988

Coming into force

1st October 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 28(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001]the Secretary of State for Education and Science, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Publication of Proposals for Reduction in Standard Number) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st October 1988.

**2.** Any proposals which are required to be published by section 28(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988 in its application to secondary schools (proposals for reducing any standard number applying to such a school under section 27 of that Act) shall be published—

- (a) in at least one newspaper circulating in the area served by the school to which the proposals relate;
- (b) by being posted in at least one conspicuous place within that area; and
- (c) by being posted at or near any main entrance to the school concerned.

Kenneth Baker

Secretary of State for Education and Science

19th August 1988

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

24th August 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the manner in which proposals by a local education authority or the governing body of a county or voluntary secondary school for a reduction in any standard number applying to the school under section 27 of the Education Reform Act 1988 are to be published before application is made to the Secretary of State for an order bringing about such a reduction.
