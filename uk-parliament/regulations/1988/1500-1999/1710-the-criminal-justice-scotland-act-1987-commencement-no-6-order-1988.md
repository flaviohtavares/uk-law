# The Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1710 (C. 61) (S.162)

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

The Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1988

Made

30th September 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1988.

**2.** The Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987 so far as not already in force shall come into force on 12th October 1988.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

30th September 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th October 1988 the remaining provisions of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987.
