# The Fowey Harbour, Par and Charlestown (Application of Pilotage Act 1987) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1518

PILOTAGE

The Fowey Harbour, Par and Charlestown (Application of Pilotage Act 1987) Order 1988

Made

1st September 1988

Laid before Parliament

9th September 1988

Coming into force

1st October 1988

Whereas the Secretary of State considers that in the interests of efficiency and safety of navigation, the Fowey Harbour Commissioners, being the competent harbour authority for Fowey Harbour, should exercise pilotage functions both as respects the harbour and as respects Par and Charlestown; in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(3) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] he, now, therefore, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Fowey Harbour, Par and Charlestown (Application of Pilotage Act 1987) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st October 1988.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the Commissioners” means the Fowey Harbour Commissioners.

## 3. Application of Pilotage Act 1987

The Pilotage Act 1987 shall apply to Fowey Harbour Commissioners as if their harbour included the area specified at Article 4.

**4.** The area so specified shall comprise in addition to Fowey Harbour—

- (a) Such waters at Par as may be bounded by an imaginary straight line drawn from Fishing Point (latitude 50° 20'"23 N longitude 4° 43'"00 W) to a point on the East side of the Bay at latitude 50° 19'"50 N longitude 4° 40'"89 W.
- (b) Such waters at Charlestown as may be bounded by an imaginary straight line drawn due south (true) from Polmear Island in the West, a line drawn due South (true) from Landrion Point (latitude 50° 19'"87 N longitude 4° 44'"68 E) in the East and a line joining the ends of the two aforementioned lines at latitude 50° 19'"5 N.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

Michael Portillo

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

1st September 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the Pilotage Act 1987 shall apply to the Fowey Harbour Commissioners as if their harbour included waters at Par and Charlestown.

This has the effect that the Commissioners shall exercise the pilotage functions conferred on them by the Act as competent harbour authority in respect of this extended area.
