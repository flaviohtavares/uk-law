# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1502 (S.147)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1988

Made

25th August 1988

Laid before Parliament

6th September 1988

Coming into force

3rd October 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907 [^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926 [^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1988 and shall come into force on 3rd October 1988.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

In Parts I and II of the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1978 [^f00003] in the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the fees specified in column 2 substitute the fees specified in column 3 of the table, and the fees so substituted shall be payable in respect of work done on or after the date in which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.
