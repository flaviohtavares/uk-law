# The Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1818 (C. 66) (S.170)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

The Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988

Made

17th October 1988

The Lord Advocate, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 109(2) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988.

**2.** The provisions of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 so far as not already in force shall come into force on 30th November 1988.

Cameron of Lochbroom

Lord Advocate Crown Office, Edinburgh

17th October 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th November 1988 the provisions of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 not so far implemented.

Four sections of the Act are already in force. Section 109 came into force on 15th May 1987 which was the date of enactment. Sections 75, 76 and 97 were brought into force on 2nd November 1987 by the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 (Commencement No.1) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1838](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1838)).
