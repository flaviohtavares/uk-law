# The Social Security Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1857 (c. 69)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988

Made

26th October1988

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 18(3) of the Social Security Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Act 1988 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Social Security Act 1988 specified below is 10th April 1989—

- (a) section 2(1) and (2) (retirement allowance); and
- (b) Schedule 4, paragraph 11 to the extent that it is not already in force, and section 16(1) in so far as it relates to it.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Nicholas Scott

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

26th October 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force on 10th April 1989 of the following provisions of the Social Security Act 1988 relating to retirement allowance—

- section 2(1) and (2);
- Schedule 4, paragraph 11 in so far as it is not already in force.

(This note is not part of the Order)
