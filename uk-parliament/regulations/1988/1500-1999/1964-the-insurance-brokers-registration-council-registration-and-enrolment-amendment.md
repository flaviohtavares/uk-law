# The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1964

INSURANCE

The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1988

Made

9th November 1988

Laid before Parliament

21st November1988

Coming into force

1st January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 27(1) and 28(1) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st January 1989.

**2.** The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 1988 made on 25th October 1988 by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of their powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977 are hereby approved.

**3.** The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1985[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Francis Maude

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

9th November 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves Rules made by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of their powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977.

The Rules amend the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) Rules 1978, as amended by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 1979, 1982 and 1985, by altering the fees payable under the 1978 Rules as amended by the 1982 and 1985 (Amendment) Rules. The Order also revokes the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1985 which approved the 1985 (Amendment) Rules.
