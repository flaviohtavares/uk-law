# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1503 (S.148)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1988

Made

25th August 1988

Coming into force

3rd October 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926 [^f00001], section 16 of the Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1933 [^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1988 and shall come into force on 3rd October 1988.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

In the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1978 [^f00003], in the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the fees specified in column 2 substitute the fees specified in column 3 of the table, and the fees so substituted shall be payable in respect of work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.
