# The Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 106 (C.5)

INTERNATIONAL IMMUNITIES AND PRIVILEGES

The Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988

Made

26th January 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 9 of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988.

**2.** The provisions of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 which are not already in force shall come into force on 3rd February 1988.

Geoffrey Howe

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Foreign and Commonwealth Office

26th January 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd February 1988 those provisions of the Act which are not already in force.
