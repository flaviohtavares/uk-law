# The London-Carlisle-Glasgow-Inverness Trunk Road (Barton-in-the-Clay Diversion) (Revocation) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 273

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The London-Carlisle-Glasgow-Inverness Trunk Road (Barton-in-the-Clay Diversion) (Revocation) Order 1988

Made

12th February 1988

Coming into force

7th March 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The London-Carlisle-Glasgow-Inverness Trunk Road (Barton-in-the-Clay Diversion) Order, 1937[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 7th March 1988 and may be cited as the London-Carlisle-Glasgow-Inverness Trunk Road (Barton-in-the-Clay Diversion) (Revocation) Order 1988.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

G. D. Crane

Regional Director Eastern Region

Department of Transport

12th February 1988
