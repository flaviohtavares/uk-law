# The Unemployment Benefit (Disqualification Period) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 487

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Unemployment Benefit (Disqualification Period) Order 1988

Laid before Parliament in draft

Made

11th March 1988

Coming into force

11th April 1988

Whereas a draft of this Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with the provisions of section 167(1) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00001] and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 20(1A) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Unemployment Benefit (Disqualification Period) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 11th April 1988.

## 2. Alteration of period of disqualification

In section 20(1) of the Social Security Act 1975 (other disqualifications, etc)[^f00003] for the figure “13” there shall be substituted the figure “26”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Services.

Skelmersdale

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Security

11th March 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 20(1) of the Social Security Act 1975 to extend the maximum period of disqualification for receiving unemployment benefit from 13 weeks to 26 weeks in the circumstances specified in that section (voluntary unemployment etc).
