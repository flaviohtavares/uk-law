# The (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986 (Variation) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 64

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986 (Variation) Order 1988

Made

15th January 1988

Coming into force

15th February 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986[^f00003] is hereby varied so that in article 3, for the words “the date when the new trunk roads are open to traffic.” are substituted the words “1st April 1988”.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 15th February 1988 and may be cited as the (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986 (Variation) Order 1988.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

J. A. Owen

Regional Director Northern Region

Department of Transport

15th January 1988
