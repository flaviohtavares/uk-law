# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Introductory

Subject to the following provisions of this Part relating to judicial examination, the procedure to be followed in relation to examination of the accused under sections 20, 20A and 20B[^f00011] (judicial examination) on any charge shall be in accordance with existing law and practice.

## Annotations

[^f00011]: Footnote: Section 20 was amended by, and sections 20A and 20B inserted by, the 1980 Act, section 6.
