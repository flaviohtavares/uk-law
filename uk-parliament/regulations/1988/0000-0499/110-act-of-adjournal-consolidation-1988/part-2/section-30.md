# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Calling postponed diet

If in relation to any case a trial diet has been postponed by virtue of an order mentioned in rule 29, any requirement to call that diet at any sitting of the court shall have effect only in relation to a sitting on the date to which the diet has been postponed.
