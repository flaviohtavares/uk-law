# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Notice of use of autopsy and forensic science reports in evidence

Any notice given by an accused under section 26(6) of the 1980 Act shall be in writing and shall be given to the prosecutor.
