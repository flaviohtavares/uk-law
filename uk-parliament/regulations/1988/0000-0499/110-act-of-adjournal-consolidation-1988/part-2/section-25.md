# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Intimation of minute

(1) A formal execution of prior intimation of the minute to all other parties shall be lodged at the same time as the minute.

(2) If it is not so lodged, the Clerk of Justiciary or the sheriff clerk, as the case may be, shall refuse to accept the minute for lodging.
