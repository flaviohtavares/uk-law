# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Order fixing diet

The minute shall be placed before the court and the court (if section 77A(3) does not apply) shall make an order endorsed on the minute—

- (a) fixing a diet for a hearing of the application.
- (b) for intimation of the minute and of the diet to all parties.
