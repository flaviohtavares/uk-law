# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Application of other provisions

The following rules shall apply to an application under rule 57 as they apply to an application under rule 51:—

- rule 51(3) to (5);
- rule 52(1);
- rule 53;
- rule 54(3);
- rule 55;
- rule 56.
