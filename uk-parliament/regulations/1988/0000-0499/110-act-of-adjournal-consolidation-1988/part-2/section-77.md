# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Compensation orders: application

The provisions of rules 78 to 82 apply to compensation orders made by courts against convicted persons under Part IV of the 1980 Act.
