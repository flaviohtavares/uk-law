# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Warrants for citation

(1) The forms specified in column 3 of the Table set out below and set out under those numbers in Schedule 1 are the forms prescribed for the purposes of the sections specified in column 1 of the Table relating respectively to the matters summarised in column 2 of the Table, and shall have effect for those purposes.

(2) In section 69 (warrants for citation)—

- (a) for the words “conform to Schedule B to the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1887” and also for the words “conform to Schedule C of that Act” substitute the words “in such form as may be prescribed by Act of Adjournal, or as nearly as may be in such form”;
- (b) in the last paragraph, omit the words “The warrant and”, and for the words “B to E” substitute the words “D and E”.
