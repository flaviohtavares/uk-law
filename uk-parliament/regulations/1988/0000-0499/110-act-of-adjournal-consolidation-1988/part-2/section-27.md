# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Order for preliminary diet

(1) An order made under rule 26(2) shall not be invalid by reason only of having been made in the absence of the parties or of any person acting on their behalf.

(2) Any such order shall be—

- (a) endorsed on the minute,
- (b) authenticated by the judge subscribing his signature,
- (c) attached together with the minute to the record copy indictment.
