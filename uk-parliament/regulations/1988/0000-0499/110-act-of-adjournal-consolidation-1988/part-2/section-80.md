# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Variation

(1) The judge may, at any time before a compensation order is fully complied with, and after such further enquiries as the court may order, vary the terms of the order as he thinks fit.

(2) A variation order under paragraph (1) may be made in chambers and in the absence of the parties, or any of them.
