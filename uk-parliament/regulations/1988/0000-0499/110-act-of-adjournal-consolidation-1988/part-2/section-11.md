# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. List of jurors

The clerk of the court before which the trial is to take place, in preparing a list of jurors for the trial diet under section 96[^f00008], shall have regard in determining the number of jurors to be listed to the powers of postponing or adjourning any trial diet exercisable by the court under sections 76, 77, 77A[^f00009] and 102(3)[^f00010].

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: Section 96 was amended by the 1980 Act, Schedule 4, paragraph 13.

[^f00009]: Footnote: Sections 76, 77 and 77A were inserted by the 1980 Act, Schedule 4, paragraphs 5, 6 and 7.

[^f00010]: Footnote: Section 102 was substituted by the 1980 Act, section 16.
