# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Order for taking precognition

If, after the hearing, the sheriff is satisfied that it is reasonable to require such precognition on oath in the circumstances, he shall—

- (a) order the precognition to be taken;
- (b) fix a diet for it to be taken;
- (c) grant warrant to cite the person from whom it is to be taken.
