# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Act of Adjournal, unless the context otherwise requires—

- (a) “the 1975 Act” means the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975;
- (b) “the 1980 Act” means the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00004].

(2) Except where the context otherwise requires, expressions used in this Act of Adjournal and in the 1975 Act shall have the same meaning in this Act of Adjournal as in that Act and any reference to a section by number is a reference to the section of that number in the 1975 Act.

(3) A reference to a numbered form includes a form as nearly as may be in that form having regard to the particular circumstances.
