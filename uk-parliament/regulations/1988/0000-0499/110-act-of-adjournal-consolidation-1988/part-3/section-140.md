# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 140

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 140. Interpretation

In this rule and in rules 141 to 147—

- “the Act of 1937” means the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1937[^f00040];
- “the Act of 1968” means the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00041];
- “child” means a child within the meaning of Part III of the Act of 1968;
- “court” means the sheriff sitting as a court of summary jurisdiction.
