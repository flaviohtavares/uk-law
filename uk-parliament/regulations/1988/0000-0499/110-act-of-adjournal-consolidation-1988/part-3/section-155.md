# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 155

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 155. Discharge and variation

(1) A person, in respect of whom an order has been made under said section 27(2) or (5), may apply to the sheriff for discharge or variation of the order in question.

(2) The sheriff may, after hearing the parties, either grant or refuse to grant the discharge or variation sought.
