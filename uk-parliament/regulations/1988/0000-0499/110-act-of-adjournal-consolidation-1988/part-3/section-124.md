# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Forms for fines enquiry and related matters

(1) The forms set out in Forms 57 to 69 of Schedule 1 shall have effect in summary proceedings for the purpose of determining means in respect of fines and related matters.

(2) Those forms may be used for that purpose with such variations as circumstances may require.

(3) This rule does not affect the Second Schedule to the Summary Jurisdiction (Scotland) Act 1954[^f00028].
