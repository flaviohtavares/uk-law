# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 127. Forms for appeal

For the purposes of appeals in summary proceedings the forms specified by numbers in column 3 of the Table set out below and set out under those numbers in Schedule 1 are the forms prescribed under the 1975 Act for the purposes of the sections of that Act specified in column 1 of the Table relating respectively to the matters summarised in column 2 of the Table, and shall have effect for those purposes.
