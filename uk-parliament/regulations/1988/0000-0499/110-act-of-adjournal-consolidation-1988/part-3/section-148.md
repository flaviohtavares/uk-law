# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Interpretation

In this rule and rules 149 to 152—

- “the Act of 1965” means the Backing of Warrants (Republic of Ireland) Act 1965[^f00042];
- “judicial authority” means a court, judge or justice of a court, or peace commissioner;
- “the Republic” means the Republic of Ireland.
