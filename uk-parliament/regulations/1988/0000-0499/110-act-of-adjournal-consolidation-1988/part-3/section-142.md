# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 142

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 142. Commencement of proceedings

Proceedings against a child shall be commenced only on the instructions of the Lord Advocate by complaint at the instance of the procurator fiscal.
