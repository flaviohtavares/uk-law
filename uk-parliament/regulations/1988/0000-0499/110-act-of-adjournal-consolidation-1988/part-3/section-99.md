# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 99

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 99. Joint application

(1) Where the prosecutor and the accused propose to make a joint application orally to the court under section 314(4)[^f00024] for postponement of a diet that has been fixed, they may only do so at a diet which has been duly assigned and which has been regularly called.

(2) An application by an accused under section 314(6) shall be made in the form set out in Form 53 of Schedule 1.

## Annotations

[^f00024]: Footnote: Paragraphs (4) to (6) of section 314 were added by the 1980 Act, section 11(c).
