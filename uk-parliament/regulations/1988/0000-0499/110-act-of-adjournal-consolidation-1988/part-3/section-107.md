# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Application

(1) An application to the court by the prosecutor or the defence under section 32(1)(b) of the 1980 Act for the appointment of a commissioner to examine a witness to whom that section applies shall be made by way of petition in the form set out in Form 56 of Schedule 1.

(2) An application so made shall be lodged with the sheriff clerk.
