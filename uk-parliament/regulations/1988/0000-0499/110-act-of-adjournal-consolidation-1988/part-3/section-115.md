# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Proceedings on appeal, etc.

(1) If a question is raised in the High Court in any proceedings on appeal or on a petition for the exercise of the Court’s nobile officium the court shall proceed to make a reference.

(2) In this rule references to proceedings on appeal are references to proceedings on appeal under the 1975 Act and on appeal by bill of suspension, bill of advocation or otherwise.
