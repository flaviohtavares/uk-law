# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 137

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 137. List of appeals

(1) The Clerk of Justiciary shall, after consultation with the Lord Justice General or Lord Justice Clerk, issue a list of appeals with the respective dates of hearing on the Justiciary Roll.

(2) He shall give the respective Edinburgh solicitors representing appellants whose appeal is so listed at least 14 days notice of the date fixed for the hearing of the appeal.
