# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 131

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 131. Intimation to Crown of abandonment

The Clerk of Justiciary or the clerk of court, as the case may be, on the lodging with him of—

- (a) a minute abandoning an appeal by stated case under section 449;
- (b) a minute abandoning a note of appeal against sentence under section 453B(7),

shall notify immediately the Crown Agent or the prosecutor, as the case may be, of the lodging of the minute.
