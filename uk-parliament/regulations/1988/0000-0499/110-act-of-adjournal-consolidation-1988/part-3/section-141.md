# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 141

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 141. Application of normal procedure

The procedure in summary proceedings shall apply in relation to proceedings against a child as it applies to proceedings against an adult subject to the provisions of the Acts of 1937, 1968, 1975 and 1980 and this Part.
