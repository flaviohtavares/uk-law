# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Validity

The validity of any proceedings against an accused person shall not be affected by reason only of the failure of the procurator fiscal to comply in any respect with the provisions of rule 88.
