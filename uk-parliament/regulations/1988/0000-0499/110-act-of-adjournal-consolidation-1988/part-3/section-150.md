# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 150

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 150. Notice of consent to early removal

(1) A notice given under section 3(1)(a) of the Act of 1965 (consent to removal earlier than is otherwise permitted) shall be in the form set out in Form 83 of Schedule 1, and shall be signed in the presence of a sheriff, a justice of the peace or a sheriff clerk who shall also sign it.

(2) Any such notice given by a person who has been remanded in custody shall be delivered to the governor of the prison in which he is detained.

(3) If a person remanded on bail gives such notice, he shall deliver or send it to the clerk of the court which so remanded him.
