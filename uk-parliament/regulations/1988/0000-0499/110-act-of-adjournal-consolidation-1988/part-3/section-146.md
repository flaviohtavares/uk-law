# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 146

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 146. Remand

The court may from time to time and at any stage of a case remand a child for information to be obtained with respect to him.
