# Act of Adjournal (Consolidation) 1988, section 163

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 163. Legal aid in High Court of Justiciary

Where an application for legal aid is made to the High Court of Justiciary under section 23 of the Act of 1986, the court may—

- (a) determine the application itself; or
- (b) remit the application to the sheriff court for determination.
