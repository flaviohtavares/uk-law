# The Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 51 (C.1)

CHRONICALLY SICK AND DISABLED PERSONS

The Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988

Made

18th January 1988

The Secretary of State for Social Services in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(2) of the Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988.

(2) This Order shall not apply to Scotland.

(3) In this Order“the Act” means the Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986.

## 2. Day specified for the coming into force of certain provisions of the Act

The provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force on 1st February 1988.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Services.

Nicholas Scott

Minister of State,

Department of Health and Social Security

18th January 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st February 1988 sections 5 and 6 of the Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986. Section 5 imposes a duty on local education authorities to require appropriate officers of local social services authorities to give their opinion whether children who are receiving special education are disabled persons and imposes duties on local education authorities to notify local social services authorities when disabled persons receiving special education will leave full-time education and on local social services authorities to carry out assessments of their needs under certain welfare enactments; section 6 requires local education authorities to keep under review the dates when disabled children receiving special education are expected to leave full-time education at school or further education establishments to ensure that notices are given to local social services authorities and assessments made under section 5 of the Act.
