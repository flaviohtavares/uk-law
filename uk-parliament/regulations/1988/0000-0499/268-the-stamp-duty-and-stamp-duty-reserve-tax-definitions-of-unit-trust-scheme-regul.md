# The Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 268

TAXES

The Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988

Made

18th February 1988

Laid before the House of Commons

18th February 1988

Coming into force

11th March 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 57 of the Finance Act 1946[^f00001] and section 28 of the Finance (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946[^f00002], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 11th March 1988.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations unless the context otherwise requires—

- “limited partnership” means a limited partnership registered under the Limited Partnerships Act 1907[^f00003] and“general partner” and“limited partner” have the same meanings as in that Act;
- “limited partnership scheme” means a unit trust scheme of the description specified in regulation 4;
- “Part III” means Part III of the Finance (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946;
- “Part VII” means Part VII of the Finance Act 1946;
- “participant” in relation to a unit trust scheme, has the meaning given by section 75(2) of the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00004];
- “scheme property” means, in relation to a unit trust scheme, property of any description, including money, which is held on trust for the participants in the scheme;
- “unit trust scheme” means a scheme which, apart from these Regulations, is a unit trust scheme for the purposes of Part VII or Part III as the case may be.

## 3. Exception of certain unit trust schemes from Part VII and Part III

A unit trust scheme which is—

- (a) a limited partnership scheme, or
- (b) a profit sharing scheme which has been approved in accordance with Part I of Schedule 9 to the Finance Act 1978[^f00005],

shall be treated as not being a unit trust scheme for the purposes of Part VII or Part III as the case may be.

## 4. Description of a limited partnership scheme

A unit trust scheme is a limited partnership scheme when the scheme property is held on trust for the general partners and the limited partners in a limited partnership.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for certain unit trust schemes to be excepted from the definitions of unit trust scheme given by section 57(1) of the Finance Act 1946 (“section 57(1)”) and section 28(1) of the Finance (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946 (“section 28(1)”). As substituted by section 48 of the Finance Act 1987, those sections provide that“unit trust scheme” is to have the same meaning as in the Financial Services Act 1986. Two types of unit trust scheme are excepted from the definitions in section 57(1) and section 28(1) by these Regulations: limited partnership schemes and approved profit sharing schemes. In these cases the trust instrument relating to the scheme will not be liable to unit trust instrument duty and units under the scheme will not be treated as stock for transfer duty purposes or (by virtue of section 99(9) of the Finance Act [1986 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/41)) as chargeable securities for the purposes of stamp duty reserve tax.

Regulation 1 provides for the title and commencement.

Regulation 2 contains definitions.

Regulation 3 provides that limited partnership schemes and approved profit sharing schemes are to be excepted from the definitions of“unit trust scheme” in section 57(1) and section 28(1).

Regulation 4 describes a limited partnership scheme.
