# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Continuance) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 274

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Continuance) Order 1988

Made

18th February 1988

Coming into force

22nd March 1988

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 17(2)(a) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Continuance) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 22nd March 1988.

**2.** In this Order, “the temporary provisions of the Act of 1984” means sections 1 to 13, 14 (except in so far as it relates to orders under subsection (2)(a) or (b) of section 17) and 17(2)(c) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 and Schedules 1 to 3 thereto.

**3.** The temporary provisions of the Act of 1984 shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beginning with 22nd March 1988.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

18th February 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues in force the temporary provisions of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 for a period of 12 months from 22nd March 1988. These provisions are currently in force by virtue of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Continuance) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/273)).
