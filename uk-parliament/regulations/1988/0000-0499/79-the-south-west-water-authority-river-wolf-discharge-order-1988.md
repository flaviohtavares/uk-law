# The South West Water Authority (River Wolf Discharge) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 79

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The South West Water Authority (River Wolf Discharge) Order 1988

Made

19th January 1988

Coming into force

20th January 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment, on the application of the South West Water Authority, and in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 1 and 2(3)[^f00001] of the Water Resources Act 1971[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the South West Water Authority (River Wolf Discharge) Order 1988, and shall come into force on the 20th January 1988.

**2.** The South West Water Authority may for the purpose of augmenting the flow of the River Wolf discharge water from the Roadford Reservoir into the River Wolf at a discharge point at National Grid Reference SX 419899 at a rate not exceeding 1,000,000 cubic metres per day.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Dinah Nichols

An Under Secretary in the

Department of the Environment

19th January 1988
