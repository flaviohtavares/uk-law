# The Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Amendment Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 422 (S.44)

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, SCOTLAND

The Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made

5th March 1988

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1988

Coming into force

1st April 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 14A and 15 of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1967[^f00001]as read with section 45 of, and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 4 to, the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Regulations 1984[^f00003].

**2.** For the Table of Fees in Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**3.** The amendments to the principal Regulations made by regulation 2 of these Regulations shall apply only to fees in relation to work done on or after 1st April 1988.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

New St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th March 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Regulations 1984 so as to increase the fees allowable to counsel for legal aid in civil proceedings given under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1967. The increased fees will apply to fees for work done on or after 1st April 1988.

The Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1967 continues in effect, despite its general repeal by the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986, in respect of legal aid applications which were granted before commencement of the 1986 Act on 1st April 1987. (See paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 4 to the 1986 Act.) It is thus only in respect of work done following upon such applications that these Regulations increase the fees.

The overall increase is around 4½ per cent.
