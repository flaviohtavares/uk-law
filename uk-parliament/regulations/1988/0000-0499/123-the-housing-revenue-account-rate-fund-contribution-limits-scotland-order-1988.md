# The Housing Revenue Account Rate Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 123 (S.16)

HOUSING, SCOTLAND

The Housing Revenue Account Rate Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1988

Made

27th January 1988

Laid before Parliament

5th February 1988

Coming into force

26th February 1988

The secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 [^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account Rate Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 26th February 1988.

## 2. Rate fund contribution limit

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which each of the local authorities listed in Column 1 of the Schedule hereto may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 1988-89 shall not exceed the amount set out in relation to that authority in Column 2 thereof.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

New St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th January 1988
