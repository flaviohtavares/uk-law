# The Combined Probation Areas (Northumbria) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 258

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Northumbria) Order 1988

Made

17th February 1988

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001] and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Northumbria) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988, except that for the purpose of making any appointment under article 3(2) below to have effect from 1st April 1988 this Order shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Northumbria Probation Committee shall be amended by —

- (a) omitting the entry in column 1 in respect of the group of petty sessions areas comprising Berwick-Upon-Tweed, Coquetdale and Glendale Ward; and
- (b) inserting in alphabetical order —
  - (i) in column 1 “Berwick-Upon-Tweed” and corresponding to this in column 3 “1”, and
  - (ii) in column 1 “Coquetdale” and corresponding to this in column 3 “1”.

**3.** (1) The two justices appointed to be members of the Northumbria Probation Committee by the justices for the group of petty sessional divisions comprising Berwick-Upon-Tweed, Coquetdale and Glendale Ward before the two divisions of Berwick-Upon-Tweed and Glendale Ward were combined to form a new division known as the Berwick-Upon-Tweed division shall cease to hold office as members of that Committee but shall be eligible for re-appointment.

(2) The justices for each of the new division of Berwick-Upon-Tweed and the existing division of Coquetdale shall appoint one of their number to serve, with effect from 1st April 1988, as a member of the Northumbria Probation Committee; and the two members so appointed shall hold office until 30th November 1989 and shall then cease to hold office but shall be eligible for re-appointment.

Home Office

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

17th February 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the combination of the petty sessional divisions of Berwick-Upon-Tweed and Glendale Ward in the new petty sessional division of Berwick-Upon-Tweed, effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Northumberland) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1962](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1962)) which comes into force on 1st April 1988. The new division is no longer to be grouped with that of Coquetdale for the purposes of representation on the Northumbria Probation Committee.
