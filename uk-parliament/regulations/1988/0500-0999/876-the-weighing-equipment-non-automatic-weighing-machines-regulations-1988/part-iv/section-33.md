# The Weighing Equipment (Non-automatic Weighing Machines) Regulations 1988, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**33.** (1) Every non-automatic weighing machine, where the maximum load does not exceed 5 tonnes, shall be tested by the use of weights complying with the Weights and Measures (Local and Working Standard Weights and Testing Equipment) Regulations 1986[^f00008] as set out in the following Table:

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
<tr><th>Accuracy classification</th><th>Weights to be used</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Class I</td><td rowspan="2">} Local standard weights, or working standard weights which fall within the prescribed limits of error relating to the equivalent local standard weight.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Class II</td></tr>
<tr><td>Class III and having more than 5000 scale intervals</td><td>Local standard weights, working standard weights, or test weights which fall within half the prescribed limits of error for the test weight in question.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Class III and having no more than 5000 scale intervals</td><td rowspan="2">} Local standard weights, working standard weights or test weights.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Class IIII</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

(2) Where the maximum load of a machine exceeds 5 tonnes, the test loads shall consist of a minimum of 5 tonnes of weights complying with the Regulations referred to in paragraph (1) above and may consist of loose material for the remainder up to the maximum load.
