# The Weighing Equipment (Non-automatic Weighing Machines) Regulations 1988, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Weighing Equipment (Non-automatic Weighing Machines) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st November 1988.

(2) The Regulations set out in Schedule 5 to these Regulations are hereby revoked to the extent specified in column 2.
