# The Weighing Equipment (Non-automatic Weighing Machines) Regulations 1988, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Prescribed limits of error

The prescribed limits of error relating to non-automatic weighing machines shall be those set out in Schedule 4 to these Regulations.
