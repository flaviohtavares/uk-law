# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 741

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 2) Order 1988

Made

18th April 1988

Coming into force

23rd May 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as“the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 2) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 23rd May 1988.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, Texaco North Sea UK Company, whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is situated at 1 Knightsbridge Green, London SW1X 7QJ, is hereby designated the owner of each pipe-line described in the Schedule hereto.

Peter Morrison

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

18th April 1988
