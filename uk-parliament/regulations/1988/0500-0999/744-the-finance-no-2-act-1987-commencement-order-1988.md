# The Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 744 (C.23)

TAXES

The Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made

19th April 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 81(8) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1988.

**2.** Section 81 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 shall come into force on 29th April 1988.

Mark Lennox-Boyd

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

19th April 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 81 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 (“the section”) into force on 29th April 1988.

Subsections (1) and (2) of the section make substitutions in section 72(1) and (2) respectively of the Finance Act [1985 (c. 54)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/54) and subsection (3) thereof makes an insertion after section 72(2) of that Act. Subsection (4) of the section makes a substitution in section 137(4) of the Capital Gains Tax Act [1979 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/14), subsection (5) thereof makes a substitution for section 137(9) of that Act and subsections (6) and (7) thereof make substitutions in section 138(1) and (4) respectively of that Act.
