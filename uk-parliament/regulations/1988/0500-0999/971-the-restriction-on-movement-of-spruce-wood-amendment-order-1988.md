# The Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood (Amendment) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 971

PLANT HEALTH

The Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood (Amendment) Order 1988

Made

27th May 1988

Laid before Parliament

7th June 1988

Coming into force

28th June 1988

The Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 3(1), (2) and (4) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 28th June 1988.

## 2. Amendment of the Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood Order 1982

The Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood Order 1982[^f00002] shall be further amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto (areas in respect of which the movement of spruce wood is restricted) the following Schedule—

## 3. Revocation

The Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood (Amendment) Order 1986[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Forestry Commissioners is hereunto affixed on 27th May 1988.

P. J. Clarke

Secretary to the Forestry Commissioners

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Restriction on Movement of Spruce Wood Order 1982 by adding to the list of areas in respect of which the movement of spruce wood is restricted the remaining parts of the counties of Dyfed and Gwynedd not included in the previous Order and the county of West Glamorgan.
