# The Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1988 No. 643

FEES AND CHARGES

The Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988

Made

29th March 1988

Coming into force

1st April 1988

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by a resolution of the House of Commons in pursuance of section 102(5) of the Finance (No.2) Act 1987[^f00001];

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 102 of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-90755d583d78be816511db38fa0aad78]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Finance (No.2) Act 1987.

**2.**[^key-fd28da57a548f4ef75f8626b95f17997] In relation to each of the powers to fix a fee listed in column 1 of the Tables in Schedule 1 to this Order in respect of the subject matters set out in that column the functions specified for the purposes of section 102(3) of the Act shall, as respects each subject matter, be those functions listed against that power in column 2 of the Tables.

**3.**[^key-1409bc8b391a038ee23e99357d4ffa1a] In relation to each function listed in column 2 of the Tables in Schedule 1 to this Order as respects a subject matter set out in column 1 the matters specified for the purposes of section 102(4) of the Act shall be such of the matters set out in Schedule 2 to this Order as are listed in column 3 of the Tables against that subject matter.

Paul Channon

Secretary of State for Transport

## SCHEDULE 2

**1.**[^key-bb39895994f81f565a1333644b21c0dc] All costs incurred by the Secretary of State, [^key-6c7029d218d2633e3d35d6ce93fe3537]... licensing authorities, traffic commissioners and the Registrar of approved driving instructors which are directly attributable to the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**2.**[^key-b05d2ae58d3f2aadbca277953dfaa3f7] That proportion of the costs, not falling within paragraph 1 above, incurred by, or on behalf of, any of the persons specified in paragraph 1 above, in relation to staff, equipment, premises, facilities and matters connected, whether directly or indirectly, therewith, being the proportion which falls to be attributed to any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**3.**[^key-795bc8f088f921fe0525852806d089b1] A return on the resources employed in carrying out any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**4.**[^key-827649c3d9c41d5f112c4d9c8bdbd7f0] The allocation of a sum in respect of matters which would otherwise be covered by insurance, the allocation of a sum in respect of superannuation payments and provision for bad debts, in relation to any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**5.**[^key-9b1c6d4f59300e86e19b1986517d43ab] Amounts recovered in relation to any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above other than from such fees as are referred to in this Order.

**6.**[^key-21dbff8e334255d22502f0419163822d] The allocation, over a period of years, of an initial or exceptional cost in relation to any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**7.**[^key-9e14d49c3c7e298be7b2acd8b9519d76] In respect of any function of any of the persons specified in paragraph 1 above consisting of the payment or remittance of any sum or amount, both the sum or amount in question and the cost incurred in effecting the payment or remittance.

**8.**[^key-a523e0378fb6eb9d83a3ae0ce5e79103] The recovery of any past deficits incurred in relation to any of the functions specified in Schedule 1 above.

**9.**[^key-89cfb7fe50b49a1be9a149876b2aa92b] Value added tax.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies functions and matters which are to be taken into account in the determination of fees to be fixed by the Secretary of State for Transport in respect of certain activities concerning road vehicles, road transport, drivers, goods vehicle operators, public service vehicle operators and bus services.
