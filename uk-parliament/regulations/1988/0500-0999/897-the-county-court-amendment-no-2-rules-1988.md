# The County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 897 (L. 11)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1988

Made

12th May 1988

Coming into force

13th June 1988

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1988.

(2) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires, an Order referred to by number means the Order so numbered in the County Court Rules 1981[^f00001] and Appendix A, B or C means Appendix A, B or C to those Rules.

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the provisions relating to costs in the County Court Rules 1981 by increasing fixed costs by approximately 5.4% (rules 2 to 4 and 6 to 9) and by introducing a new item for which costs are fixed (rule 5).
