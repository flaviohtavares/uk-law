# The Finance Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 780 (C. 25)

TAXES

The Finance Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988

Made

26th April 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 49(2) of the Finance Act 1987[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1987 (Commencement No.2) Order 1988.

**2.** Section 49(1) of the Finance Act 1987 shall come into force on 29th April 1988.

Michael Neubert

Tony Durant

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th April 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 29th April 1988 the only provision of the Finance Act 1987 (section 49(1) ) which is not already in force or which has not been repealed.

Section 49(1) of the Finance Act 1987 provides that sections 77 to 79 of the Finance (1909—10) Act [1910 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1910/8) (which contain provisions relating to contract notes), so far as unrepealed, shall cease to have effect.
