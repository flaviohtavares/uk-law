# The Prison (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 537 (S.59)

PRISONS

The Prison (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988

Made

10th March 1988

Coming into force

14th March 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 35 as read with section 7 of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1952[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering that a draft of the following rules has been laid before Parliament, that the period of 40 days has expired and that neither House has resolved that the rules be not made, hereby making the following rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988 and shall come into force on 14th March 1988.

## 2. Amendment of the Prison (Scotland) Rules 1952

In the Schedule to the Prison (Scotland) Rules 1952[^f00002]after the entry relating to Edinburgh Prison, there shall be inserted the following entry:—

> | *Name of Prison* | *Names of Appointing Authorities* | *Numbers of members to be appointed* |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Glenochil | Central Regional Council | 5 |
> |  | Clackmannan District Council | 52 |
> |  | City of Dundee District Council | 1 |
> |  | Falkirk District Council | 1 |
> |  | Stirling District Council | 1 |
> |  | Fife Regional Council | 2 |
> |  | Dunfermline District Council | 51 |
> |  | Lothian Regional Council | 2 |
> |  | Edinburgh District Council | 1 |

> |  | Glasgow District Council | 1 |

These Rules amend the Prison (Scotland) Rules 1952 to provide for the constitution of a Visiting Committee for Glenochil Prison.
