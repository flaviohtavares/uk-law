# The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 798 (S.82)

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY, SCOTLAND

The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1988

Made

20th April 1988

Laid before Parliament

10th May 1988

Coming into force

1st June 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st June 1988.

**2.** For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the High Court of Justiciary Fees Order 1984 [^f00002] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1987 [^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th April 1988

We concur,

Mark Lennox-Boyd

Peter Lloyd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th April 1988

This Order increases the fees payable to the Principal Clerk of Session and Justiciary or any officer acting for him in relation to proceedings in the High Court of Justiciary, other than the fees payable for copying and certain of the search fees. The previous Order increasing fees is revoked.
