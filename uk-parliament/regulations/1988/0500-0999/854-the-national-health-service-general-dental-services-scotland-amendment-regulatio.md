# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 854 (S.86)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made

10th May 1988

Laid before Parliament

11th May 1988

Coming into force

1st June 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 25(1) and (2), and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st June 1988.

## 2. Amendments

In regulation 26(1) of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1974([^f00002]) (statement of dental remuneration)-(a) in column 1 (Determination) below the number “IV” there shall be inserted the number “IVA” ; and

- (b) in column 2 (Subject matter of determination), there shall be inserted, so as to relate to determination IVA, the words “Allowances to dentists with respect to their training of recently registered dentists;” .

Michael B Forsyth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th May 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1974 which make arrangements for the provision by dentists of general dental services under the National Health Service. These Regulations extend the scope of the Statement of Dental Remuneration, so as to make provision for allowances to dentists providing general dental services who train recently registered dentists.
