# The National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 535 (S.58)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made

16th March 1988

Laid before Parliament

21st March 1988

Coming into force

11th April 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 72 and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 11th April 1988.

**2.** In regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 1976([^f00002]) for the reference to

Michael B Forsyth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

New St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

16th March 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 1976 by substituting for the reference to the Welfare Food Order 1975 a reference to the Welfare Food Regulations 1988.
