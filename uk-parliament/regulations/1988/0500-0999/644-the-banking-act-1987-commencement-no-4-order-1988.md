# The Banking Act 1987 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 644 (C.20)

BANKS AND BANKING

The Banking Act 1987 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988

Made

29th March 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 110(2) of the Banking Act 1987[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Banking Act 1987 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1988.

**2.** All the provisions of the Banking Act 1987 which are not already in force shall come into force on 29th April 1988.

Peter Lloyd

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th March 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 29th April 1988 all the provisions of the Banking Act 1987 which are not already in force.
