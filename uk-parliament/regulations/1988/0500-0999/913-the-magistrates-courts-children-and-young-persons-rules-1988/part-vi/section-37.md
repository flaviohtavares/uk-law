# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Register of proceedings in juvenile court.

Such part of the register kept in pursuance of rules made under the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980[^f00021] as relates to proceedings in a juvenile court shall be kept in a separate book.
