# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Form of warrant where young person is committed to remand centre or prison.

Where a young person is committed to a remand centre under section 22(5) or section 23(2) of the Act of 1969 or is committed to a prison under the said section 23(2), the court shall include in the order of committal a certificate that the young person is of so unruly a character that he cannot safely be committed to the care of a local authority.
