# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Notice of order made under section 49 of the Child Care Act 1980.

Where an order is made under section 49(1) of the Act of 1980 in respect of an affiliation order, payments under which have been ordered to be made to a collecting officer, notice of the making of the order shall be given by the clerk of the court to the collecting officer, either personally or by written notice sent or delivered to his address by post or otherwise.
