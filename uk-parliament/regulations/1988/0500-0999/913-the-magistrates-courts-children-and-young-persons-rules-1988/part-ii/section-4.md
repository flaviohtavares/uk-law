# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application of Part II.

This Part shall apply in the case of a child or young person brought before a court charged with an offence except that only rules 5 and 8(2) shall apply where the court is inquiring into the offence as examining justices.
