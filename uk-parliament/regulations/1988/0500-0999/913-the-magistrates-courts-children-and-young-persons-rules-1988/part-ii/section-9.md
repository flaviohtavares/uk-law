# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Evidence for defence.

If it appears to the court that a*prima facie* case is made out, the child or young person shall, if he is not legally represented, be told that he may give evidence or address the court, and the evidence of any witnesses shall be heard.
