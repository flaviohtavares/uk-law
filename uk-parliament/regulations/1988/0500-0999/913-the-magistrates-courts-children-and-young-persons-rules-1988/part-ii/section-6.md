# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Duty of court to explain charge in simple language.

The court shall explain to the child or young person the substance of the charge in simple language suitable to his age and understanding.
