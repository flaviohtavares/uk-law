# The Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1988, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) Subject to rule 29 of these Rules, in these Rules the following expressions have the meanings hereby respectively assigned to them, that is to say:—

- “the Act of 1933” means the Children and Young Persons Act 1933[^f00002];
- “the Act of 1963” means the Children and Young Persons Act 1963[^f00003];
- “the Act of 1969” means the Children and Young Persons Act 1969[^f00004];
- “the Act of 1980” means the Child Care Act 1980[^f00005];
- “the Act of 1986” means the Children and Young Persons (Amendment) Act 1986[^f00006];
- “child” means a person under the age of fourteen;
- “court” means a juvenile court except that in Part VII it means a magistrates' court, whether a juvenile court or not;
- “register” means the separate register kept for the juvenile court pursuant to rule 37 of these Rules;
- “young person” means a person who has attained the age of fourteen and is under the age of seventeen.

(2) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires, any reference to a rule, Part or Schedule shall be construed as a reference to a rule contained in these Rules, a Part thereof or a Schedule thereto, and any reference in a rule to a paragraph shall be construed as a reference to a paragraph of that rule.
