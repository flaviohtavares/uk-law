# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 588

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made

23rd March 1988

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 23rd day of March 1988

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1988.

(2) The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1987[^f00002] is revoked.

**2.** The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00003] is amended by deleting Schedule 1 thereto[^f00004] and substituting therefor the following —

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It specifies that the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, The Hague, 25th October 1980 (Cm.33) has entered into force for the Northwest Territories of Canada.
