# The Opencast Coal (Rate of Interest on Compensation) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 890

COAL INDUSTRY

The Opencast Coal (Rate of Interest on Compensation) Order 1988

Made

16th May 1988

Laid before Parliament

17th May 1988

Coming into force

7th June 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 35(8) and 49(4) of the Opencast Coal Act 1958[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Opencast Coal (Rate of Interest on Compensation) Order 1988, and shall come into force on 7th June 1988.

**2.** The rate of interest for the purposes of section 35 of the Opencast Coal Act 1958 shall be 9¼ per cent. per annum.

**3.** The Opencast Coal (Rate of Interest on Compensation) Order 1987[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Michael Neubert

Tony Durant

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

16th May 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 35 of the Opencast Coal Act 1958 provides that interest shall be payable in addition to compensation in certain circumstances. This Order decreases the rate of interest from 10¼ per cent. to 9¼ per cent. per annum and revokes the Opencast Coal (Rate of Interest on Compensation) Order 1987.
