# The Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 715 (C.21)

OVERSEAS DEVELOPMENT AND CO-OPERATION

The Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made

5th April 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9(2) of the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988.

**2.** The Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 shall come into force on 12th April 1988.

Geoffrey Howe

Her Majesty’s Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs

5th April 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th April 1988 the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988.
