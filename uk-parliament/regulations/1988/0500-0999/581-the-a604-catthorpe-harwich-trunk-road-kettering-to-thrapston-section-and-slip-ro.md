# The A604 Catthorpe — Harwich Trunk Road (Kettering to Thrapston Section and Slip Roads) Supplementary Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 581

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A604 Catthorpe — Harwich Trunk Road (Kettering to Thrapston Section and Slip Roads) Supplementary Order 1988

Made

7th March 1988

Coming into force

22nd April 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** For the purpose of connecting the trunk road with another highway, so as to effect a junction at the place stated in the Schedule to this Order, the Secretary of State is authorised to construct along the routes described in that Schedule new highways. Those new highways shall become trunk roads as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre lines of the new highways are indicated by heavy black lines on the deposited plan.

**3.** In this Order

- (i) “the deposited plan” means the plan folio numbered HA10/2E/265 marked “The A604 Catthorpe — Harwich Trunk Road (Kettering to Thrapston Section and Slip Roads) Supplementary Order 1988”, containing one map, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (ii) “the trunk road” means the trunk road to be provided in pursuance of article 1(a) of the A604 Catthorpe — Harwich Trunk Road (Kettering to Thrapston Section and Slip Roads) Order 1987[^f00003]
- (iii) “the new highways” means the highways mentioned in article 1 of this Order, and “a new highway” means one of those highways.

**4.** This Order shall come into force on 22nd April 1988 and may be cited as the A604 Catthorpe — Harwich Trunk Road (Kettering to Thrapston Section and Slip Roads) Supplementary Order 1988.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

P. M. Hewitt

Regional Director East Midlands Region

Department of Transport

7th March 1988

## THE SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE NEW HIGHWAYS

Four routes to connect the eastbound and westbound carriageways of the trunk road with the Ringstead and Denford Bypass, as proposed to be constructed by the Northamptonshire County Council, in the parish of Thrapston, in the County of Northamptonshire, which are given the reference numbers i,ii,iii and iv respectively in the deposited plan.
