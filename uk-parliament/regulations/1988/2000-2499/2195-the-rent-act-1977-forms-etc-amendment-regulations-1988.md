# The Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2195

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

14th December 1988

Coming into force

15th January 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 81A and 84 of the Rent Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 15th January 1989.

**2.** In Schedule 1 to the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) Regulations 1980[^f00002] omit the second sentence from the first paragraph of form No. 15.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

14th December 1988

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

14th December 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make an amendment to form No. 15 (application for cancellation of a rent registered by a rent tribunal where the dwelling is not subject to a restricted contract) in the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) Regulations 1980, consequential on provisions in the Housing Act [1988 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50).
