# The Milk (Special Designation) Regulations 1988, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Revocation and savings

(1) Any licence to use a special designation granted under the Milk (Special Designation) Regulations 1986[^f00005] and in force immediately before the coming into force of these Regulations shall continue in force and have effect as if it had been granted under and subject to the provisions of these Regulations.

(2) The Milk (Special Designation) Regulations 1986 and the Milk (Special Designation) (Amendment) Regulations 1988[^f00006] are revoked.
