# The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2043

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

22nd November 1988

Laid before Parliament

29th November 1988

Coming into force

20th December 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 20th December 1988.

**2.** In regulation 3 of the Passenger and Good Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00002] for “£200” there shall be substituted “£225” and for “£80” there shall be substituted “£90”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Peter Bottomley

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Transport

16th November 1988

We consent to the making of the Regulation

David Maclean

David Lightbown

Two of the Lord Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

22nd November 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986 to increase the fees for the approval of fitters or workshops for the installation or repair of recording equipment (Tachographs).

The fee for the issue of an approval is increased from £200 to £225 and for the renewal of an approval from £80 to £90.
