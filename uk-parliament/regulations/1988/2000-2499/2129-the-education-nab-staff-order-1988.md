# The Education (NAB Staff) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2129

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (NAB Staff) Order 1988

Made

7th December 1988

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1988

Coming into force

31st December 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 136(3) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001]the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (NAB Staff) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 31st December 1988.

**2.** The persons employed by NAB listed in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated for transfer under section 136 of the Education Reform Act 1988 to the employment of the Polytechnics and Colleges Funding Council on 31st December 1988.
