# Act of Sederunt (Form of charge for payment) 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2059 (S.195)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Form of charge for payment) 1988

Made

24th November 1988

Coming into force

30th November 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001], section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Form of charge for payment) 1988 and shall come into force on 30th November 1988.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Form of charge for payment

The form of charge for payment of money to be used by a messenger-at-arms or a sheriff officer under section 90 of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00003] shall be in the form in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt or a form substantially to the same effect with such variation as circumstances may require.

Emslie

Lord President, IPD

Edinburgh

24th November 1988
