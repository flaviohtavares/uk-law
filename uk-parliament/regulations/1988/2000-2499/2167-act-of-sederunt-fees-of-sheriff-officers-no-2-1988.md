# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No.2) 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2167 (S.210)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No.2) 1988

Made

9th December 1988

Laid before Parliament

21st December 1988

Coming into force

11th January 1989

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No.2) 1988 and shall come into force on 11th January 1989.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.
