# The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Wages and Accounts) (Fishing Vessels) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2064

MERCHANT SHIPPING

MASTERS AND SEAMEN

The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Wages and Accounts) (Fishing Vessels) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

25th November 1988

Laid before Parliament

5th December 1988

Coming into force

26th December 1988

The Secretary of State, after consulting with the organisations referred to in section 99(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1970[^f00001], in exercise of powers conferred by section 9 of that Act and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Wages and Accounts) (Fishing Vessels) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 26th December 1988.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Wages and Accounts) (Fishing Vessels) Regulations 1972[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting, in regulation 8, in both places, “£200” for “£50”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Michael Portillo

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

25th November 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Merchant Shipping (Seamen’s Wages and Accounts) (Fishing Vessels) Regulations 1972 so as to increase from £50 to £200 the maximum contribution that an employer can recover from a fisherman who is in breach of his contractual obligations.
