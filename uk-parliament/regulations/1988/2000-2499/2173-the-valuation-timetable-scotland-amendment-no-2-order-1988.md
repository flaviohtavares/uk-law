# The Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2173 (S.211)

RATING AND VALUATION

The Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Order 1988

Made

8th December 1988

Coming into force

28th December 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 13(1) and 42(1) of the Valuation and Rating (Scotland) Act 1956 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 28th December 1988.

**2.** The entry in column 2 of the Schedule to the Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Order 1984 [^f00002]relating to the last date for the disposal by a valuation appeal committee of appeals and complaints mentioned in column 1 of that Schedule shall be amended by substituting for the words “31st December 1988” the words “30th June 1989”.

Ian Lang

Minister of State. Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

8th December 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the entry in the Schedule to the Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Order 1984 which contains the timetable for the disposal by valuation appeal committees of appeals and complaints against entries in a valuation roll. That Order was most recently amended by the Valuation Timetable (Scotland) Amendment Order 1988, which extended the period for disposal of such appeals and complaints from 31st March 1988 to 31st December 1988.

This Order extends the period for the disposal of such appeals and complaints to 30th June 1989.
