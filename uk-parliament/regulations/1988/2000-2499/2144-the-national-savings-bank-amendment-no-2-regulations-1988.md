# The National Savings Bank (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2144

SAVINGS BANKS

The National Savings Bank (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1988

Made

7th December 1988

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1988

Coming into force

1st January 1989

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 2(1) and 8(1) of the National Savings Bank Act 1971[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the National Savings Bank (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1988, and shall come into force on 1st January 1989.

**2.** The National Savings Bank Regulations 1972[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) by substituting for regulation 20 the following regulation–

  > **20.** An ordinary deposit of less than £5 may only be made at the principal office of the National Savings Bank unless the Director of Savings, in any case in which he thinks fit, accepts any such deposit made at any other savings bank office.

  ;
- (b) by substituting for regulation 28(5) the following regulation–;

  > (5) An investment deposit of less than £5 may only be made at the principal office of the National Savings Bank unless the Director of Savings, in any case in which he thinks fit, accepts any such deposit made at any other savings bank office.

  .

David Lightbown

David Maclean

Two of the Lord Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th December 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations, which amend the National Savings Bank Regulations 1972, raise the minimum ordinary deposit from £1 to £5. They also give to the Director of Savings a discretion to accept ordinary deposits or investment deposits of less than that amount which are made at any savings bank office where previously such deposits could only be made if presented directly to the principal office of the National Savings Bank.
