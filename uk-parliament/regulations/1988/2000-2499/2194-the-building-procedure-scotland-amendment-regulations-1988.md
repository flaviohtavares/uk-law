# The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2194 (S.216)

BUILDING AND BUILDINGS

The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made

10th December 1988

Laid before Parliament

29th December 1988

Coming into force

30th January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 20 and 29(1) of the Building (Scotland) Act 1959 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 30th January 1989.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 1981 [^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th December 1988
