# The Littlehampton (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2157

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Littlehampton (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988

Made

6th December 1988

Coming into force

7th December 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and on the application of the Littlehampton Harbour Board in accordance with section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Littlehampton (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988 and shall come into force on 7th December 1988.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order —

- “the Board” means the Littlehampton Harbour Board;
- “the harbour” means the harbour as defined in section 36 (Limits of harbour) of the Littlehampton Harbour and Arun Drainage Outfall Act 1927[^f00004];
- “the signed plan” means the plan signed in triplicate by an Under Secretary in the Department of Transport, two copies of which have been deposited at that department and one copy of which has been deposited at the principal office of the Board situated at 1 Pier Road, Littlehampton, West Sussex BN17 5LR.

## 3. Extension of limits of jurisdiction for purposes of pilotage

The limits within which the Board shall have jurisdiction for the purposes of pilotage under Part I of the Pilotage Act 1987 shall include (in addition to the harbour) the area coloured pink on the signed plan, being the waters of the sea for a distance of three nautical miles from low water mark of ordinary spring tides bounded on the west by an imaginary line joining Halnaker Mill and Middleton Church and on the east by an imaginary line joining Chanctonbury Ring and Goring Church.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of state

J.W.S. Dempter

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

6th December 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987 a direction given by a harbour authority for the purpose of making pilotage compulsory for ships navigating in a specified area shall not apply to any area which is outside the authority’s harbour unless the limits of jurisdiction of the authority for pilotage purposes have been extended to cover that area.

This Order accordingly extends the limits of jurisdiction of the Littlehampton Harbour Board for pilotage purposes under Part I of the Pilotage Act 1987 so as to include an area outside the harbour in respect of which the Board considers that pilotage should be compulsory.

The applicant for this Order is the Littlehampton Harbour Board.
