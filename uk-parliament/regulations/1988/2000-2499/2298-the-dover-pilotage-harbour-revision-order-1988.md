# The Dover (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 2298

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Dover (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988

Made

21st December 1988

Coming into force

22nd December 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and on the application of the Dover Harbour Board in accordance with section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Dover (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1988 and shall come into force on 22nd December 1988.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “The Board” means the Dover Harbour Board;
- “the harbour” means the harbour as defined in section 4 of the Dover Harbour Consolidation Act 1954[^f00004];
- “the signed plan” means the plan signed in triplicate by an Under Secretary in the Department of Transport, two copies of which have been deposited at the Department and one copy of which has been deposited at the offices of the Board situated at the Harbour House, Dover, Kent, CT17 9BU.

## 3. Extension of limits of jurisdiction for purposes of pilotage

The limits within which the Board shall have jurisdiction for the purposes of Pilotage under Part I of the Pilotage Act 1987 shall include (in addition to the harbour) the area coloured yellow on the signed plan, being so much of the sea as lies within one international nautical mile of the seaward limits of the harbour.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

J. W. S. Dempster

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

21st December 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987 a direction given by a harbour authority for the purpose of making pilotage compulsory for ships navigating in a specified area shall not apply to any area which is outside the authority’s harbour unless the limits of jurisdiction of the authority for pilotage purposes have been extended to cover that area.

This Order accordingly extends the limits of jurisdiction of the Dover Harbour Board for pilotage purposes under Part I of the Pilotage Act 1987 so as to include an area outside the Harbour in respect of which the Dover Harbour Board considers that pilotage should be compulsory.

The applicant for this Order is the Dover Harbour Board.
