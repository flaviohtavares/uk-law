# The Crown Court (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1322 (L.14)

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

The Crown Court (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1988

Made

26th July 1988

Laid before Parliament

1st August 1988

Coming into force

22nd August 1988

We, the Crown Court Rule Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred upon us by sections 84(1) and (2) and 86 of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00001], hereby make the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Crown Court (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1988 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1988.

**2.** The Crown Court Rules 1982[^f00002] shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in the Schedule to these Rules.

## SCHEDULE — AMENDMENTS TO THE CROWN COURT RULES 1982

Rule 2

**1.** In each of rules 7(2)(b), 7(7)(c) and 8(d) there shall be inserted, in each case after the word “section”, the words “67B[^f00003] or”.

**2.** In rule 11(2)(b) there shall be inserted after the word “section” in the second place where it occurs the words “67B or”.

**3.** In rule 12(3) there shall be inserted after the words “section 21” the words “or 67B”.

**4.** In Schedule 3, in the entries in Parts I and III relating to the Licensing Act 1964[^f00004] there shall be inserted in the third column (Section or Schedule) in each case after the figure “50” the figure “67B”.

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Crown Court Rules 1982 to take account of section 67B of the Licensing Act 1964 which confers a right of appeal to the Crown Court against a decision of licensing justices or a magistrates' court to grant a restriction order under section 67A of that Act (which restricts the afternoon permitted hours in licensed premises or a registered club) or as to the terms of such an order. (Section 67B has similar application in relation to licensed canteens by virtue of section 156A of the 1964 Act.)
