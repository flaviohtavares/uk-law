# The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1464 (S.142)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988

Made

15th August 1988

Coming into force

1st January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8(1) and 135 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 [^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st January 1989.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976.

**2.** The fee payable by any applicant to a licensing board in respect of any matter described in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order and arising under the provision of the Act specified in column 2 of that Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 3, instead of the amount specified opposite thereto in column 4 [^f00002].

**3.** The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1987 [^f00003] is revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th August 1988
