# Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1033 (S.98)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1988

Made

9th June 1988

Laid before Parliament

21st June 1988

Coming into force

18th July 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writer’s Fees) 1988 and shall come into force on 18th July 1988.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.
