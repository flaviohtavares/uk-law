# The Dundee College of Technology (Change of Name) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1448 (S.138)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Dundee College of Technology (Change of Name) Regulations 1988

Made

11th August 1988

Laid before Parliament

17th August 1988

Coming into force

8th September 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 77(1) and (4) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Dundee College of Technology (Change of Name) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 8th September 1988.

(2) In these Regulations “the Scheme” means the Dundee College of Technology Scheme 1975([^f00002]).

## 2. Change of name of college

Dundee College of Technology shall be re-named and known as “Dundee Institute of Technology” .

## 3. Amendment of the Scheme

(1) In paragraph 2 of the Scheme, for the definitions of “College” and “governing body” there shall be substituted the following definitions:—

> “College” means Dundee Institute of Technology;
>
> “governing body” means the Governors of Dundee Institute of Technology constituted under the provisions of paragraph 3;

.

(2) In paragraph 3 of the Scheme, for the words “” The Governors of Dundee College of Technology “” there shall be substituted the words “” The Governors of Dundee Institute of Technology “” .

(3) In paragraph 34(1) of the Scheme, for the words “Dundee College of Technology” there shall be substituted the words “Dundee Institute of Technology” .

## 4. Supplementary

Any reference to Dundee College of Technology, or to the Governors of Dundee College of Technology, in an agreement, deed, bond, instrument or other writing, operative or effective immediately before the coming into force of these Regulations, shall be read as a reference to Dundee Institute of Technology or to the Governors of Dundee Institute of Technology, respectively, and any like reference to the College or to the Governors shall be construed accordingly; and no rights, interests, obligations or liabilities of any person shall be affected by these Regulations.

Ian Lang

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

11th August 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations re-name Dundee College of Technology as “Dundee Institute of Technology” and make consequential amendments to the Dundee College of Technology Scheme 1975.
