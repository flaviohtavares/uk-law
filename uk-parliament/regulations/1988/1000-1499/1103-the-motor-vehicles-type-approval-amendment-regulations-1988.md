# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1103

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

24th June 1988

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1988

Coming into force

25th July 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 25th July 1988.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980[^f00003] are hereby amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendments relating to the issue of type approval certificates

In regulation 5 after paragraph (1) there is added —

> (1A) Where the Secretary of State is satisfied that a vehicle component conforms with the requirements referred to in paragraph (1) but it appears to him that it fulfills its function or offers a specific feature only in conjunction with another vehicle component of a particular class, he may approve the first mentioned vehicle component as a type subject to —
>
> - (a) restrictions relating to the use of components of that type, or
> - (b) conditions relating to the fitting of components of that type,
>
> and any such restrictions and conditions shall be shown on the type approval certificate.

.

**4.** In regulation 9(1) after sub-paragraph (b) there is added —

> and
>
> - (c) the delivery by the manufacturer with any component which —
>   - (i) is supplied by him other than as part of a complete vehicle, and
>   - (ii) is of a type to which the type approval certificate relates,
>
>   of a document giving details of any restrictions and conditions imposed under regulation 5(1A).

.

## 5. Amendments relating to form of information document

In the form set out in Part I of Schedule 1, in paragraph (a) of the notes at the end, there is inserted after the definition of “Category N3” —

“off-road vehicle: Off road vehicles are defined in paragraph (b) of the notes to Annex 1 of Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) as last amended by Council Directive [87/403/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0403).”.

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980.

Regulations 3 and 4 are made in consequence of Council Directive [87/358/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0358) (O.J. L192, 11.7.87, p.51) and regulation 5 is made in consequence of Council Directive [87/403/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0403) (O.J. L220, 8.8.87, p.44).

Regulation 3 enables a type approval certificate relating to a component to be issued subject to restrictions of use and to conditions relating to fitting.

Regulation 4 enables a type approval certificate relating to a vehicle component to be issued subject to conditions requiring the manufacturer to deliver with each component details of any restrictions or conditions imposed under the new power referred to above.

Regulation 5 amends the form of information document set out in Schedule 1 to the 1980 Regulations by adding to the notes a definition of “off-road vehicle”.

Regulation 6 amends Schedule 2 to the 1980 Regulations so that —

- (a) type approval requirements relating to exhaust emissions from vehicles with petrol engines are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) as amended by Directives [74/290/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0290), [77/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0102), [78/665/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0665), [83/351/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0351) and [88/76/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0076);
- (b) type approval requirements relating to braking devices are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320) as amended by Directives [74/132/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0132), [75/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0524), [79/489/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0489), [85/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0647) and [88/194/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0194);
- (c) type approval requirements relating to gaseous exhaust emissions from diesel vehicles are the requirements of Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077) in addition to the existing requirements of Council Directive [72/306/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0306); and
- (d) type approval requirements relating to engine power of motor vehicles are, as an alternative to existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [80/1269/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/1269) as amended by Commission Directive [88/195/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0195).

Copies of the EEC Directives referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
