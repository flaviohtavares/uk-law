# The Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1333 (C. 49)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988

Made

28th July 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 20(3) of the Licensing Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988.

**2.** The provisions of the Licensing Act 1988 specified in the Schedules to this Order shall come into force on the dates mentioned in the headings of those Schedules.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

28th July 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd August 1988 and 1st March 1989 respectively the provisions of the Licensing Act 1988 set out in Schedules 1 and 2 to the Order. All other provisions of the Act not set out in those Schedules will come into force on 1st August 1988 (S.I. [1988/1187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1187)).
