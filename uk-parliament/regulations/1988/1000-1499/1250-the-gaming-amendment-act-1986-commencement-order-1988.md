# The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1250 (C.46)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made

19th July 1988

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 3(3) of the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1988.

**2.** The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 shall come into force on 19th September 1988.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

19th July 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 into force on 19th September 1988.
