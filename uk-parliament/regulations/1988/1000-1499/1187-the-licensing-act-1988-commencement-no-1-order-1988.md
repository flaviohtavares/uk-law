# The Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1187 (C. 44)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1988

Made

11th July 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 20(3) of the Licensing Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1988.

**2.** The provisions of the Licensing Act 1988 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st August 1988.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

11th July 1988
