# The Savings Contracts (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1358

NATIONAL DEBT

The Savings Contracts (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

29th July 1988

Laid before Parliament

1st August 1988

Coming into force

22nd August 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 11 of the National Debt Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Savings Contracts (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1988.

## 2. Amendment of principal Regulations

The Savings Contracts Regulations 1969[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) regulation 20 is hereby revoked;
- (b) in regulation 26(2) there shall be inserted at the end the words “nor shall it prevent the authority from complying with a notice served on them under section 20(4)(b) of the Taxes Management Act 1970[^f00003] (persons who may be required to produce documents relating to liability of taxpayer arising from business)”.

Mark Lennox-Boyd

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th July 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Savings Contracts Regulations 1969.

First, they revoke regulation 20, which provided that certain warrants, receipts and powers of attorney should be exempt from stamp duty, as those documents are no longer liable to stamp duty. Secondly, they amend regulation 26, which imposes an obligation of secrecy, to make it subject to section 20(4)(b) of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (persons who may be required to produce documents relating to liability of taxpayer arising from business) which was extended to cover the Director of Savings by section 126 of the Finance Act 1988.
