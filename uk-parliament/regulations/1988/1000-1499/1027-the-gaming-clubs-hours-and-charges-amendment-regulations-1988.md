# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1027

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

13th June 1988

Laid before Parliament

20th June 1988

Coming into force

18th July 1988

In pursuance of sections 22(4) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain, I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 18th July 1988.

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Scotland.

**2.** Regulation 3 of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the word “noon”, wherever it occurs, the words “ten in the morning”.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

13th June 1988

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984 to extend the permitted hours for gaming on bingo club premises in England and Wales so that gaming may commence on weekdays and Saturdays at 10 a.m. instead of noon.
