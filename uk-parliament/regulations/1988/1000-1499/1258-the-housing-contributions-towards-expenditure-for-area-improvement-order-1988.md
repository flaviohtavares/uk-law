# The Housing (Contributions Towards Expenditure for Area Improvement) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1258

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing (Contributions Towards Expenditure for Area Improvement) Order 1988

Made

18th July 1988

Laid before the House of Commons

27th July 1988

Coming into force

1st September 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 245(4)(a) and 259(4)(a) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Contributions Towards Expenditure for Area Improvement) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st September 1988.

## 2. Substitution of amount

In sections 245(3) and 259(3) of the Housing Act 1985 (calculation of contributions towards expenditure of local housing authorities in respect of environmental works relating to housing action areas and of general improvement areas respectively) for the amount of £400 there shall be substituted the amount of £600.

Nicholas Ridley

Secretary of State for the Environment

14th July 1988

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

14th July 1988

We consent,

Mark Lennox-Boyd

Michael Neubert

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

18th July 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from £400 to £600 the amount for each dwelling which is to be taken into account in calculating the maximum expenditure of a local housing authority in respect of —

- (a) environmental works relating to housing action areas, or
- (b) general improvement areas,

towards which contributions may be paid by the Secretary of State.
