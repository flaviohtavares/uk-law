# The Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (Tyne and Wear) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1093

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (Tyne and Wear) Order 1988

Made

23rd June 1988

Laid before Parliament

1st July 1988

Coming into force

22nd July 1988

Whereas the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body, acting pursuant to section 67(2) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], has submitted proposals to the Secretary of State for the transfer of certain of its properties to local authorities, and he has decided to give effect to those proposals without modification;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (Tyne and Wear) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 22nd July 1988.

(2) In this Order “the Body” means the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body.

## 2. Specified Land

There shall vest in each local authority mentioned below the land of the Body which is specified in the corresponding Schedule—

| *Local Authority* | *Schedule* |
| --- | --- |
| Gateshead Metropolitan Borough Council | 1 |
| Newcastle City Council | 2 |
| North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council | 3 |
| South Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council | 4 |
| Sunderland Metropolitan Borough Council | 5 |

## 3. Bowes Railway

Any land forming the course or former course of the Bowes Railway, and any land associated with and adjoining such land, which is vested in the Body shall vest in the local authority in whose area the land is situated.

## 4. Rights and liabilities

All rights and liabilities acquired or incurred by the Body exclusively in connection with any land transferred by this Order shall vest in the local authority to which that land is transferred.

Nicholas Ridley

Secretary of State for the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers certain land owned by the Tyne and Wear Residuary Body to the district councils in whose area the land is situated. The transfers are in addition to those effected by the Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (Tyne and Wear) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1288](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1288)).

Article 2 transfers the land specified in Schedules 1 to 5.

Article 3 transfers land relating to the Bowes Railway.

Article 4 provides for the transfer of rights and liabilities ancillary to the land transferred.
