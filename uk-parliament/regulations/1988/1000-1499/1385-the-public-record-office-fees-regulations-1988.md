# The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1385

PUBLIC RECORDS

The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1988

Made

29th July 1988

Coming into force

1st August 1988

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(5) of the Public Records Act 1958[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st August 1988.

**2.** (1) Subject to the following provision, the fees to be charged for the authentication of copies of or extracts from records under the charge of the Keeper of Public Records and for other services afforded by officers of the Public Record Office shall be those set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(2) The Keeper of Public Records may remit a fee where the service performed by him is likely to include any research in consequence of a postal inquiry or has been or is likely to be exceptionally simple.

**3.** The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

27th July 1988

We concur,

David Lightbown

Tony Durant

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th July 1988
