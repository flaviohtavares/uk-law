# The Copyright (Singapore) (Amendment) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1297

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright (Singapore) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made

26th July 1988

Laid before Parliament

3rd August 1988

Coming into force

On a date to be notified in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of July 1988

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas Her Majesty is satisfied that, in respect of the matters provided for in this Order, provision will be made under the laws of Singapore whereby adequate protection will be given to owners of copyright under the Copyright Act 1956[^f00001]:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, and by virtue of the authority conferred on Her by sections 31, 32 and 47 of the said Act, is pleased to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Singapore) (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the date on which the laws of Singapore are amended to extend to Bermuda the protection given to owners of copyright under the Copyright Act 1956; this date shall be notified in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes.

**2.** The Copyright (Singapore) Order 1987[^f00002] shall extend to Bermuda, subject to the modification set out in article 5 of that Order.

**3.** Article 4 of that Order shall have effect in respect of this Order as it has effect in respect of that Order, references in that article to the commencement of that Order being taken to be references to the commencement of this Order.

G. I De Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends to Bermuda the Copyright (Singapore) Order 1987, which provides for the copyright protection in the United Kingdom and other dependent countries of the Commonwealth of works and other subject-matter originating in Singapore.
