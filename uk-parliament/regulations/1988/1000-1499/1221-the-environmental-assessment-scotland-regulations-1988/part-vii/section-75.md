# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Confidentiality

Any authority, body or person required to provide information under these Regulations shall not be required to provide information which that authority, body or person is entitled or bound to hold in confidence.
