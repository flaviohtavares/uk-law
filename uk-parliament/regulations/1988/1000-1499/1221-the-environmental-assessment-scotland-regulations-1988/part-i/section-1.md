# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Assessment (Scotland)Regulations 1988.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on the third day after the days on which they are made.

(3) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
