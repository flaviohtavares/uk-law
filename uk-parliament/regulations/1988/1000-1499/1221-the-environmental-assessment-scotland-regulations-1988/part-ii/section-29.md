# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Intimation of decision

Where the Secretary of State under section 32 grants planning permission for development by a planning authority or planning permission is deemed to have been granted under regulation 6(3) of the 1981 Regulations, the Secretary of State shall inform the authorities, bodies or persons consulted under regulation 27(3) of the decision.
