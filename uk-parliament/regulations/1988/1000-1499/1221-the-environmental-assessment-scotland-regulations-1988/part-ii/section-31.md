# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Application to the Court of Session

For the purposes of sections 231 and 233, the references in section 233(1)(b) and (2) to action on the part of the Secretary of State which is not within the powers of the Act shall be taken to extend to a grant of planning permission by the Secretary of State in breach of the prohibition imposed by regulation 6.
