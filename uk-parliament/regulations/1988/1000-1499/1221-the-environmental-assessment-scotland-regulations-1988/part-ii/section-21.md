# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Copy of environmental statement for the Secretary of State

Where an environmental statement is provided in relation to an application for planning permission which is directed to be referred to the Secretary of State for determination, or is to be the subject of an appeal to him, the applicant or appellant shall provide the Secretary of State with a copy of the environmental statement unless (in the case of a referred application) the planning authority have already forwarded one when referring the application.
