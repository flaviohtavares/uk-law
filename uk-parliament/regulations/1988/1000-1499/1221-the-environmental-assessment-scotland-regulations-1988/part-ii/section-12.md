# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Consideration of environmental effects

In respect of an application for planning permission, the person to whom it would fall to grant planning permission shall on receipt of the application or appeal consider whether it is one to which regulation 6 applies.
