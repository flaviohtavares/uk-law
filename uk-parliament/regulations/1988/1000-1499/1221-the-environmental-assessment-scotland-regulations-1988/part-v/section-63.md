# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Application

This Part of these Regulations applies to proposed drainage works which are likely to have a significant effect on the environment by virtue inter alia of their nature, size or location and for which authority under the Land Drainage (Scotland) Act 1958[^f00018] to execute these works is sought from the Secretary of State on or after 15th July 1988.
