# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Submission and publication of environmental statement

(1) When an owner of agricultural land applies under section 1 of the Act to the Secretary of State for an improvement order authorising drainage works likely to have a significant effect on the environment by virtue inter alia of their nature, size or location, he shall submit an environmental statement to the Secretary of State.

(2) Where an environmental statement is submitted in relation to an application for an improvement order, the Secretary of State shall notify and publicise the environmental statement in the same manner as a draft improvement order under paragraph 1 of the First Schedule to the Act for the purpose of obtaining comments.
