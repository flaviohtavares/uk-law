# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. New definitions in section 151(1)

The following definitions shall be inserted in section 151(1) at the appropriate places in alphabetical order:—

> - “appropriate environmental body” is to be construed in accordance with section 20A(6) of this Act;

;

> - “environmental assessment” means an assessment in accordance with Council Directive No [85/337/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0337):

; and

> - “environmental statement” has the meaning provided by section 20A(2) of this Act;

.
