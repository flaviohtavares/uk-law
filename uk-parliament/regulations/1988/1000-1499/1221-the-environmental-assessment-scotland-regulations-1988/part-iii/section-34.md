# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Interpretation

In this Part of these Regulations, unless the contrary intention appears—

- “annex 1 application” or “annex 2 application” means an application for consent or approval under—
  - (a) the Electricity (Supply) Acts 1882-1936[^f00012];
  - (b) the Schedule to the Electric Lighting (Clauses) Act 1899[^f00013]; or
  - (c) the Electricity (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00014],

  in respect of development of any description in paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 or in paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 respectively;
- “electricity application” means an annex 1 application or an annex 2 application.
