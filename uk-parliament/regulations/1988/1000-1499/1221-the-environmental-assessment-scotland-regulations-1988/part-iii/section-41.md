# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Copies of environmental statement for the public

Where an environmental statement is provided in relation to an electricity application, the applicant shall ensure that a reasonable number of copies of the statement are available at the address named in his public notice made under regulation 40 as the address at which copies may be obtained.
