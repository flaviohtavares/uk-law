# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Application

This Part of these Regulations applies in any case where an electricity application is received by the Secretary of State on or after 15th July 1988.
