# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Provision of relevant information

Any authority, body or person mentioned in Schedule 4 who have been informed under regulation 50 that a specific development application to which regulation 48 applies has been made without an environmental statement shall, if requested by the applicant, or may, without any such request, enter into consultation with him with a view to ascertaining whether they have any information in their possession which he or they consider relevant to the preparation of the environmental statement, and shall make any such information available to him.
