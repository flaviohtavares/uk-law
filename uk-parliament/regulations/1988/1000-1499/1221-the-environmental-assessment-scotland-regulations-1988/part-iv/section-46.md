# The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1988, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Application

This Part of these Regulations applies in any case where a specific development application is made to a development corporation on or after 15th July 1988, or where a development corporation first proposes on or after that date to carry out a specific development.
