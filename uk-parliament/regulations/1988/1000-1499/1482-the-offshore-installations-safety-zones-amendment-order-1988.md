# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1482

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made

12th August 1988

Coming into force

3rd September 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 22(1) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 3rd September 1988.

**2.** The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 48) Order 1987[^f00002] shall have effect with the deletion from the Schedule thereto of the entries set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Cecil Parkinson

Secretary of State for Energy

12th August 1988
