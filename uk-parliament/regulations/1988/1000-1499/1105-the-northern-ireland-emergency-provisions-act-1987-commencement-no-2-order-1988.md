# The Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1105 (C. 37)

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988

Made

24th June 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 26(1) of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988.

**2.** Section 12 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st July 1988.

Tom King

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, Northern Ireland Office

24th June 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st July 1988 section 12 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987, which substitutes new provisions for section 28 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act [1978 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/5) (providing for compensation for acts done under the 1978 Act interfering with private rights of property). The new provisions provide for a decision by the Secretary of State on an application for compensation and a right of appeal to the county court against his decision. Provision is also made for time limits for applications which may be extended by the Secretary of State or, in case of his refusal, by the county court.

As from the 1st July l988. the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1987 will have been brought fully into force.
