# The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1293

DEFENCE

The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1988

Made

26th July 1988

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of July 1988

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of the following Order in Council has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Armed Forces Act 1986[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** The Army Act 1955[^f00002], the Air Force Act 1955[^f00003] and the Naval Discipline Act 1957[^f00004] shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beyond 31st August 1988 that day being the day on which they would otherwise expire.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1988.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council
