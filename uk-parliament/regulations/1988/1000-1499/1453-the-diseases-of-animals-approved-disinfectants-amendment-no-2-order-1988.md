# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1453

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1988

Made

9th August 1988

Coming into force

18th August 1988

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1) (a), (b) and (c) and (2), 23(f) and (g) and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby Order as follows:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 18th August 1988.

## 2. Amendment

At the end of Schedule 1 to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be added the entries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 8th August 1988.
