# The Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1143 (S.116)

LAND REGISTRATION, SCOTLAND

The Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988

Made

30th June 1988

Laid before Parliament

15th July 1988

Coming into force

1st October 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(1) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Lord President of the Court of Session, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Land Registration (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1988 and shall come into force on 1st October 1988.

**2.** In place of Forms 1, 2, 3 and 6 of Schedule A to the Land Registration (Scotland) Rules 1980[^f00002], there shall be substituted the Forms similarly numbered in the Schedule to these Rules.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

30th June 1988
