# The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1198

EVIDENCE

The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made

12th July 1988

Laid before Parliament

19th July 1988

Coming into force

15th August 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 22 of the Family Law Reform Act 1969[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 15th August 1988.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971[^f00002]there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**3.** The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1987[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

12th July 1988
