# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1988 No. 1181

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1988

Made

6th July 1988

Coming into force

6th July 1988

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1988 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1989.

Tom King

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

6th July 1988

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1989 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1207](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1207))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
