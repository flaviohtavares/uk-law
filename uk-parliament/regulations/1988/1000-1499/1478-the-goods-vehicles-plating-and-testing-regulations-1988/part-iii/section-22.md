# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 22

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Goods vehicle test[^c-6507fc255cba0a63ac29fd63dbca72c4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After an examination for plating has been carried out on a vehicle, or in any case where a plating certificate is not required, in accordance with regulation 20(1A),[^c-031673bc816aa2724d92872e36b0b5bf] a goods vehicle examiner shall arrange for the vehicle to undergo a goods vehicle test.
