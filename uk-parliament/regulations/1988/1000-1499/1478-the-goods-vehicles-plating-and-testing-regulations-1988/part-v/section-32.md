# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 32

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Other amendments to the plating certificate[^key-bc2d2cf98a5a5bc282d9bcefd932aebd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where, otherwise than by reason of a notifiable alteration, any particular (with reference to a plated weight or any other matter) contained in a plating certificate for a vehicle becomes or may have become no longer applicable to that vehicle, an application on a form approved by the Secretary of State may be sent to him [^key-d32f5d38d84b092545814f473b2a9ce8]..., for the purpose of having the vehicle re-examined with a view to that particular being amended.
