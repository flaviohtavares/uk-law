# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 29

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Appeals[^key-8f4fd355b90f18f9956e8e8cf81eb914]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any person aggrieved by a determination made on a periodical test of a vehicle, or on a consequent re-test of a vehicle, by the person in charge of that test may appeal to [^key-2cfa7335a196cc9e51de6e926775d4d9]... the Secretary of State.

(2) Paragraphs (2) to (11) of regulation 25 apply in relation to any appeal under paragraph (1) as they apply in relation to an appeal under regulation 25 relating to a determination made on a goods vehicle test, but for the purposes of such application each reference in regulation 25 to a plating certificate shall be treated as being omitted.
