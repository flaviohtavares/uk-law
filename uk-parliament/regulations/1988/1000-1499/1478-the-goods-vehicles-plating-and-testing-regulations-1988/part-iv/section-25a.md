# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 25A

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25A. Interpretation of this Part[^c-e1beff4a09300cfaba561a07eefea46f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part, a reference to a periodical test includes a goods vehicle test carried out pursuant to regulation 22.
