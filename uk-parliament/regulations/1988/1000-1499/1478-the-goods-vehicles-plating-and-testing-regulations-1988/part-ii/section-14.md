## 14. Notice of place and time of first examinations or periodical tests[^key-1f8e0424a9c98acffcd3b9bb6e52e95b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) As soon as reasonably practicable after the date of the receipt of an application for a first examination or periodical test of a vehicle under regulation 12(1)[^key-015ab96376c1057f7c983f923085b860] the Secretary of State shall send to the applicant notice of the vehicle testing station at which the examination or test is to take place, and the date and time reserved by the Secretary of State for that examination or test[^key-0f58b5db82cfa869ca4039983e06dce1].
