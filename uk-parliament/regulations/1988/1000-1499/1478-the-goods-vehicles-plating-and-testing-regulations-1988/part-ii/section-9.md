# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 9

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Dates by which vehicles are to be submitted for first examinations[^c-fb98c3eb23e58ccc02448e93b4af8c92][^c-ddcb6a65a098040bc5c0f9d97095c507]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Every motor vehicle to which paragraph (3) or paragraph (3A)[^c-3fbea1dc1e22be3b689a0d73885beba6] applies shall be submitted for a goods vehicle test on or before the appropriate day.

(2) Every motor vehicle and trailer to which neither of paragraphs (3) and (3A) applies[^c-41ff27030a55e16c749834d9004e7a64] shall be submitted for both a plating examination and a goods vehicle test on or before the appropriate day.

(3) This paragraph applies to a motor vehicle or trailer[^c-7b9d7ca237c3b5c4dc1614dff967a35a] if the following requirements are met, namely—

- (a) a plating certificate is in force for the vehicle; and
- (b) that plating certificate is a certificate of conformity or a Minister’s approval certificate that is treated as a plating certificate by virtue of section 59(4) of the 1988 Act.

(3A)[^c-3cbaa0898e18df21ee7c8353dbcc8e9a] This paragraph applies to a motor vehicle or trailer for which a plating certificate is not required, in accordance with regulation 20(1A).

(4) Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall not prevent the Secretary of State authorising the submission of a vehicle for a first examination after the date by which the vehicle is required by those paragraphs to be submitted for first examination.
