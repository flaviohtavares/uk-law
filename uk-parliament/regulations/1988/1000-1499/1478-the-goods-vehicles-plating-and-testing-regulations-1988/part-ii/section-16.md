# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988, section 16

> Text as at 2026-06-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Fees for re-tests[^c-13eb163336ca1a5ba621d443765979cc][^M_F_531d163d-68c2-40c7-c68a-7980001f3558]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (4)[^c-a9ecefc0d363c062928816c02cb69114] [^c-c1a33f11954b21f6043fd408bcb70fc8]... and (6), where the applicant for a re-test requests that a re-test under regulation 15 be carried out within 14 days after the vehicle was submitted for its first examination or periodic test (as the case may be) the fee for the re-test shall be determined in accordance with the Table below by reference to the description of vehicle—[^c-68542517207ca9418f09ae563cd61c3f]

Table[^c-6d8033a4f70567ba5f1e43df5f7fa837]

| *(1)*<br>*Description of vehicle* | *(2)*<br>*Fee where re-test is carried out at a vehicle testing station provided by the Secretary of State under section 52(2)(a) of the 1988 Act[^c-04cacef778d951b4addd45d42b0355dd] (£)* | *(3)*<br>*Fee where the re-test is carried out at premises designated by the Secretary of State under section 52(2)(b) of the 1988 Act[^c-867dedf33784608a0e18096be3c43330] (£)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motor vehicle with 2 axles | 49 | 35 |
| Motor vehicle with 3 axles | 69 | 49 |
| Motor vehicle with 4 or more axles | 91 | 65 |
| Trailer with 1 axle | 25 | 18 |
| Trailer with 2 axles | 35 | 25 |
| Trailer with 3 or more axles | 46 | 33 |

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (5), in circumstances other than those described in paragraph (1), the fee for a re-test under regulation 15 shall be the fee determined in accordance with the Table in regulation 12 by reference to the description of vehicle, as if the re-test was a first examination or periodical test.[^c-4474812d86477eadb8b2789c38f7af79]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply to a further re-test in a case where—

- (a) a previous re-test has been carried out on the vehicle in circumstances other than those described in paragraph (6)[^c-ebf6789a470e43edb1bb7b4cb586b1a3], and
- (b) the applicant for the further re-test requests that the further re-test be carried out within 14 days after the vehicle was submitted for the previous re-test;

and, subject to paragraphs (4)[^c-1db598f05ae786ac2225684aed9c10ea] [^c-521c83f7797463426faa25958e7374ae]... and (6), in such a case the fee for the further re-test shall be determined in accordance with the Table in paragraph (1) by reference to the description of vehicle[^c-99cc7f788970586c03ffaea5d7577d8f].

(4)[^c-dac92c59fdc428774be1ffa48c77c0ab] Where[^c-e0698cb9892bdf98396c0e5a55785999] the time arranged for a re-test is, at the applicant’s request, out of hours, the fee payable under paragraph (1) or (3) in respect of the re-test shall be increased by £20.00[^c-5620f0dc6726020126379deb684e1320] in the case of a motor vehicle and £13.00 in the case of a trailer.

(4A)[^c-60390f7f13882a655032edced32dea6c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-9810710efdb1e9f9816217448e289578] Where[^c-9264c930e8d18a9f60eb026321c7b5c5] the time arranged for a re-test is, at the applicant’s request, out of hours, the fee payable under paragraph (2) in respect of the re-test shall be increased by £38.00[^c-3d01d8adeee549a5a52d54220b565476] in the case of a motor vehicle and £24.00 in the case of a trailer.

(5A)[^c-ff9927bc79a7b36b909cb2fb0a02a1b4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Notwithstanding[^c-6b632a9d8e6c77142a661ace1da48efd] paragraphs (1), (3) and (4)[^c-7caacf9381891202bf6de7d61e02e159], the fee for a re-test under regulation 15 shall be £13.00[^c-925bf5dd7439aafab4fd4093c8395c80] in the case of a motor vehicle and £7.00 in the case of a trailer if—

- (a) the vehicle is submitted for a re-test on the same day as the day on which the first examination or periodical test (as the case may be), was carried out, or on the first day[^c-a40537185c9f6202fd56574a12a4a2e4] thereafter on which the relevant vehicle testing station is open.
- (b) the fee for the first examination, periodical test or last preceding re-test, as the case may be, has been paid; and
- (c) the re-test is due only to one or more defects in the vehicle as a result of which any of the following prescribed construction and use requirements are not complied with, namely those contained in—
  - (i) the following items in paragraphs 1 and 2 in Part I of Schedule 3, namely items 4, 5, 7, [^c-46228d367ed66e6e2d8d812ea162794f]... 9, 11, 12, 14 (in so far as it relates to the emission of an oily substance) and 16;
  - (ii) items 17, 18 , 18A[^c-4eb80a4c6710f8439daf545e7cfa011a] and 19 in paragraph 2 in Part I of Schedule 3 [^c-38de5edfdadc769f7a2bac83994ced60]...;
  - (iii) paragraphs 3, 3A and 3B[^c-fbf657851dcb0629a3d3ded6ac9780c2] in Part I of Schedule 3;

  and the requirements of Part II of Schedule 3 so far as those requirements relate to the condition of the spare wheel carrier, fuel tanks and system, bumpers and the cab.

(7) The fees payable under this regulation shall be paid on submission of the vehicle for the re-test.

(8) In paragraph (6), “the relevant vehicle testing station”, in relation to a re-test, means the testing station at which the re-test is to be carried out pursuant to arrangements made under regulation 15.
