# The Harbours Act 1964 (Commencement) Order 1964

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1964 No. 1424 (C.18)

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Harbours Act 1964 (Commencement) Order 1964

Made

8th September 1964

The Minister of Transport in exercise of his powers under section 63(4) of the Harbours Act 1964 and of all other powers enabling him hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** Sections 26 to 40 and section 63(3) of the Harbours Act 1964 shall come into operation on the 1st October 1964.

**2.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbours Act 1964 (Commencement) Order 1964.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this Order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister of Transport the 8th September 1964.

Denis O'Neill

An Under Secretary of the Ministry of Transport

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation sections 26 to 40 of the Harbours Act 1964 which relate to harbour charges and to the conditions as to use of harbour services and facilities provided by certain harbour authorities and section 63(3) of the Act which repeals certain enactments.
