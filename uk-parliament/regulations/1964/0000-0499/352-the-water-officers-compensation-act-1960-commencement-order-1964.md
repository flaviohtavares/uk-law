# The Water Officers Compensation Act 1960 (Commencement) Order 1964

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1964 No. 352 (c. 5)

WATER SUPPLY, ENGLAND AND WALES WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Officers Compensation Act 1960 (Commencement) Order 1964

Made

6th March 1964

The Minister of Housing and Local Government and the Secretary of State acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 2(2) of the Water Officers Compensation Act 1960 hereby order as follows:—

**1.** The date appointed under section 2(2) of the Water Officers Compensation Act 1960 shall be 1st April 1964.

**2.** This order may be cited as the Water Officers Compensation Act 1960 (Commencement) Order 1964.

Given under the seal of the Secretary of State on 4th March 1964.

Michael Noble

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Given under the official seal of the Minister of Housing and Local Government on 6th March 1964.

Keith Joseph

Minister of Housing and Local Government
