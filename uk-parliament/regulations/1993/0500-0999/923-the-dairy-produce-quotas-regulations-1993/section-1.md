# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1993, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

**1.** Minor changes have been made to certain of the definitions in order to update them (regulation 2).
