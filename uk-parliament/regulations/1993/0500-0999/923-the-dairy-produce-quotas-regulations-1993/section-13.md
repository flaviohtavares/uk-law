# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1993, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. National reserve

(1) The national reserve shall comprise such wholesale and direct sales quota as is not for the time being allocated to any person, including any quota withdrawn pursuant to the provisions of regulation 32.

(2) The Minister may make allocations from the national reserve in accordance with the Community legislation and these Regulations.
