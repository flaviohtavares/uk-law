# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Return by employer where deductions working sheets not required

A return shall be made in respect of every employee to whom the employer paid emoluments at any time during the year at a rate exceeding the minimum rate, but in respect of whom the employer was not required under these Regulations to prepare a deductions working sheet.
