These Regulations consolidate the Regulations relating to income tax subject to Pay As You Earn.

The principal Regulations consolidated are the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1973 (S.I.[1973/334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1973/334)) (as amended); but these Regulations also consolidate the Income Tax (Councillors' Attendance Allowances) Regulations 1974 (S.I.[1974/340](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1974/340)), the Income Tax (Reserve and Auxiliary Forces) Regulations 1975 (S.I.[1975/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/91)) (as amended), the Income Tax (Repayment Supplement) Regulations 1975 (S.I.[1975/1283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/1283)) and the Income Tax (Holiday Pay) Regulations 1981 (S.I.[1981/1648](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/1648)).
