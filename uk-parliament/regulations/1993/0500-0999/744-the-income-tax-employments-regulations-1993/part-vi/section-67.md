# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Ministry records

(1) The Ministry shall record, on a deductions working sheet, the particulars specified in paragraph (2) regarding every payment of reserve pay made to a reservist.

(2) The particulars specified in this paragraph are—

- (a) the date of the payment,
- (b) the amount of the payment, and
- (c) the amount of tax, if any, deducted on making the payment.
