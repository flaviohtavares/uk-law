# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Fund records

(1) A fund shall record, on a deductions working sheet, the particulars specified in paragraph (2) regarding every payment of holiday pay made to a recipient.

(2) The particulars specified in this paragraph are—

- (a) the recipient’s name,
- (b) the tax year to which the deductions working sheet relates,
- (c) the date of the payment,
- (d) the amount of the payment, and
- (e) the amount of tax deducted on making the payment.
