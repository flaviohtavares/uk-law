# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Other emoluments of recipient

Nothing in this Chapter shall affect the application of these Regulations to any other emoluments of a recipient.
