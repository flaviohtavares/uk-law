# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Repayment to reservist during the year

(1) The Ministry shall not repay tax in respect of reserve pay to a reservist.

(2) On application being made to him by the reservist, the inspector may make such repayment to the reservist as may be appropriate at any time during the year having regard to the matters specified in paragraph (3).

(3) The matters specified in this paragraph are—

- (a) the reserve pay of the reservist for the period from the beginning of the year up to and including the date of that application,
- (b) the amount of tax deducted from that reserve pay as evidenced by certificates of pay and tax supplied under regulation 65, and
- (c) any reliefs from income tax to which the reservist is entitled, and his income for the year from all other sources and his liability to tax on that income, as estimated by the inspector.
