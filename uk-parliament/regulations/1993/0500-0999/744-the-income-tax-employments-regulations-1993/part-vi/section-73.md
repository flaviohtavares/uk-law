# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Deduction of tax

A fund, on making any payment of holiday pay to a recipient, shall deduct income tax at the basic rate in force at the time the payment is made.
