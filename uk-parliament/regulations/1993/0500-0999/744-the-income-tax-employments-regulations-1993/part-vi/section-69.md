# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Application of Parts V and VIII of these Regulations

(1) Part V of these Regulations shall apply with any necessary modifications to tax liable to be deducted under regulation 61.

(2) Part VIII of these Regulations shall apply with any necessary modifications to reserve pay.
