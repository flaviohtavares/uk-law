# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 6th April 1993 immediately after the Income Tax (Employments) (No. 24) Regulation 1993[^f00003], the Income Tax (Employments) (No. 25) Regulations 1993[^f00004] and the Income Tax (Employments) (No. 26) Regulations 1993[^f00005].
