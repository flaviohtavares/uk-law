# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Aggregation of emoluments in non-cumulative cases

Where under these Regulations tax is deductible otherwise than by reference to cumulative emoluments and cumulative tax, the amount of tax to be deducted in any week or income tax month shall be calculated by reference to the aggregate of the emoluments paid to the employee in that week or month.
