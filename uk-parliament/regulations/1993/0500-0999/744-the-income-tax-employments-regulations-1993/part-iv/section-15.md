# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. First payment in the year

Where the payment of emoluments is the first payment in the year—

- (a) the employer shall deduct the cumulative tax from the emoluments on making the payment in question, and paragraphs (2) to (4) of regulation 14 shall not apply, but
- (b) the deduction shall not exceed the overriding limit.
