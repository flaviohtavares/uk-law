# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Emoluments not paid weekly or monthly

Where emoluments are paid at regular intervals other than regular intervals of a week or a month—

- (a) the employer shall record the actual date of every such payment, and
- (b) any payment of such emoluments shall be deemed for the purposes of these Regulations to be made on the date on which it would have been made if a payment had been made in the last day of the preceding year.
