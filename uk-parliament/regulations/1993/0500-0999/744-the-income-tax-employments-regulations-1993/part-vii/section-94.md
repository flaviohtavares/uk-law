# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Interpretation of Chapter III

In this Chapter “the Department” means—

- (a) in Great Britain, the Department of Employment or, as the case may be, the Department of Social Security, or
- (b) in Northern Ireland, the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland

by whom a payment of benefit is made.
