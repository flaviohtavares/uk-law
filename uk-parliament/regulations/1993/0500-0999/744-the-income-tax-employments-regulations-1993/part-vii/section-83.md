# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. Interpretation of Chapter II

(1) In this Chapter “the Department” means—

- (a) in Great Britain, the Department of Employment whether or not a particular payment including taxable benefit was actually made by that Department, or
- (b) in Northern Ireland, the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland.

(2) Where a payment of taxable benefit is made by the Department of Social Security that Department shall make arrangements to enable the Department to comply with these Regulations.

(3) For the purposes of this Chapter any reference to the deduction or repayment of tax on making any payment shall be taken to refer to the duties imposed upon the Department by regulations 88 and 89.
