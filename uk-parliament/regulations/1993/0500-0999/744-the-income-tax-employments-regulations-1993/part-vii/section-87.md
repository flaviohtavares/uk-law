# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Determination and recording of amount of taxable benefit

On each occasion that a payment of benefit is made to a claimant to whom regulation 86 does not apply, the Department shall determine and record the taxable benefit included in the payment.
