# The Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Adjustments

If, after the payment of benefit by an employer or the Department, the Department recovers part or all of that payment from the employed claimant, the Department shall notify the inspector and the employed claimant of the adjustment to the figure of taxable benefit, showing the amounts appropriate to each year.
