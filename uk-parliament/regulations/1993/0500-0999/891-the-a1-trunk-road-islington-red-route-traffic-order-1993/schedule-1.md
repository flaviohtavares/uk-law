# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — THE TRUNK ROAD RED ROUTE

Articles 3 and 4

### PART 1 — lengths of road where waiting (if not prohibited by article 5) is restricted between 8 am–7 pm mondays to fridays inclusive

**1.** Archway Road.

**2.** Archway Roundabout.

**3.** City Road between its junction with Islington High Street, St John Street and Pentonville Road, and its junction with Wakley Street.

**4.** Goswell Road between its junction with City Road and its junction with Wakley Street.

**5.** Highbury Corner and roundabout.

**6.** Holloway Road.

**7.** Wakley Street.

### PART II — lengths of road where waiting (if not prohibited by article 5) is restricted between 8 am–7 pm mondays to saturdays inclusive

**8.** Islington High Street.

**9.** Upper Street.
