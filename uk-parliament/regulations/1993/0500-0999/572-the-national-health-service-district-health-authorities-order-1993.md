# The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 572

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1993

Made

9th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by sections 8(1)(b) and (1A)(c) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993, immediately after the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 2) Order 1992[^f00002] comes fully into force.

## 2. Abolition of Authorities

The District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby abolished.

## 3. Establishment and names of new Authorities

For each of the districts determined by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) Order 1993[^f00003] and specified in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Order there is established a District Health Authority which shall be known by the name specified in column (2) of that Schedule in relation to that district.

## 4. Amendment of the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990

In consequence of articles 2 and 3 of this Order, for Schedule 1 to the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990[^f00004] there shall be substituted the new Schedule 1 set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

9th March 1993

This Order abolishes on 1st April 1993 the District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 1 (Article 2), and establishes the new District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 2 in relation to the districts for which they are established (Article 3).

This Order also substitutes a new Schedule 1 in the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990 which lists all the District Health Authorities in England (Article 4 and Schedule 3).
