# The Nuclear Installations (Application of Security Provisions) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 687

ATOMIC ENERGY AND RADIOACTIVE SUBSTANCES

The Nuclear Installations (Application of Security Provisions) Order 1993

Made

12th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

12th March 1993

Coming into force

24th March 1993

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted a permit to Urenco (Capenhurst) Limited under section 2(1) of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00001] authorising the use of its site at Capenhurst in the County of Cheshire for the treatment of uranium such as to increase the proportion of the isotope 235 contained therein for purposes other than, or not limited to, research and development;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 2(1B) of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00002] and all other powers in that behalf enabling him, hereby orders as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Nuclear Installations (Application of Security Provisions) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 24th March 1993.

**2.** The provisions set out in Schedule 1 to the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00003] shall have effect in relation to Urenco (Capenhurst) Limited.

Tim Eggar

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

Dated 12th March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order applies to Urenco (Capenhurst) Limited the security provisions set out in Schedule 1 to the Nuclear Installations Act 1965, as amended.
