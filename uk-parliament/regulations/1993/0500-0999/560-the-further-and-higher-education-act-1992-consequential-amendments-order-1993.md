# The Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (Consequential Amendments) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 560

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (Consequential Amendments) Order 1993

Made

8th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 24(1) of the Sex Discrimination Act 1975[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (Consequential Amendments) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

## 2. Amendment of the Sex Discrimination (Designated Educational Establishments) Order 1975

The Sex Discrimination (Designated Educational Establishments) Order 1975[^f00002] is amended by the omission in the Schedule of Part I (polytechnics) and Section A of Part II (establishments in respect of which grants in aid of university education are payable out of money provided by Parliament).

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

8th March 1993

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

8th March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to the Sex Discrimination (Designated Educational Establishments) Order 1975 which are consequential upon the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
