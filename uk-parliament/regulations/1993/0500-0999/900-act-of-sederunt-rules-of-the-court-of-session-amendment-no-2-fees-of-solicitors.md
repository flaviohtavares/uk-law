# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Solicitors) 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 900 (S.125)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Solicitors) 1993

Made

25th March 1993

Coming into force

26th April 1993

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Solicitors) 1993 and shall come into force on 26th April 1993.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of the Rules of the Court of Session (Table of Fees of Solicitors in the Court of Session)

(1) Rule 347 of the Rules of the Court of Session[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

(2) In Chapters I and III of the Table of Fees set out in rule 347, for the fees and outlays specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt as applying to the paragraphs specified in column 1 of that Schedule, there shall be substituted the fees and outlays specified in column 3 of that Schedule; and the fees and outlays specified in column 3 shall apply to work done and outlays incurred, in respect of which these fees and outlays are chargeable, on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

## 3. Saving

The fees and outlays having effect before the substitution under paragraph 2(2) above, which are specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt, shall continue to apply to work done and outlays incurred before this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh,

25th March 1993
