# The Combined Probation Areas (Surrey) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No.750

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Surrey) Order 1993

Made

14th March 1993

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Surrey) Order 1993.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st April 1993 save that, for the purpose of making appointments to the probation committee for the Surrey Probation Area to take effect on that date, this Order shall come into force forthwith.

(3) For the purpose mentioned in paragraph (2) above, the Petty Sessional Divisions (Surrey) Order 1992[^f00002], which except for limited purposes comes into force on 1st April 1993, is to be treated as having come into force when this Order is made.

**2.** For that part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00003] which relates to the Surrey Probation Area there shall be substituted the following—

| *(1) Petty Sessions Area*[^f00004] | *(2) Probation Area* | *(3) Number of justices on probation committee* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North & East Surrey | Surrey | 2 |
| North West Surrey |  | 3 |
| South East Surrey |  | 3 |
| South West Surrey |  | 2 |

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

14th March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the reorganisation of petty sessions areas in Surrey effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Surrey) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/2368](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2368)), which comes into force on 1st April 1993.
