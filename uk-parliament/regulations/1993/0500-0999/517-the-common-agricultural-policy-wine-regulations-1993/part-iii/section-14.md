# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1993, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Specified regions

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, the specified regions in the United Kingdom for the purposes of Article 3 of Regulation 823/87 are–

- (a) Southern Counties, comprising the areas of the counties, subject to any alteration of boundaries under Part IV of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00018], described in Part I of Schedule 4, and
- (b) Northern Counties, comprising the areas of the counties, subject to any alteration of boundaries under Part IV of the Local Government Act 1972, described in Part II of Schedule 4.

(2) There shall be excluded from the areas described in Part I and Part II of Schedule 4 any land situated at an altitude of more than 220 metres above sea level.
