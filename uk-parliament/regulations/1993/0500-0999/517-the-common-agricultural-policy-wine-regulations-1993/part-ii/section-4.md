# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1993, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Definition of medium dry

For the purposes of Article 14(7)(b) of Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 3201/90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1990/3201) laying down detailed rules for the description and presentation of wines and grape musts[^f00013] wine may be labelled and described as “medium dry” if it has a residual sugar content not exceeding 18 grams per litre where the total acidity content of that wine expressed as grams of tartaric acid per litre is not more than 10 grams per litre below its residual sugar content.
