# The St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 695

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993

Made

11th March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to that latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trust

The St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust is hereby dissolved, and accordingly the St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

Virginia Bottomley

Secretary of State for Health

11th March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the St Thomas' Hospital National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1993.
