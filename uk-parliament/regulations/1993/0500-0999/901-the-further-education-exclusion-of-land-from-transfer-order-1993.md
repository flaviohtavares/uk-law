# The Further Education (Exclusion of Land from Transfer) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 901

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Further Education (Exclusion of Land from Transfer) Order 1993

Made

26th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

29th March 1993

Coming into force

31st March 1993

Whereas applications have been made to the Secretary of State under section 23(5) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] by local authorities for the several interests in land mentioned in this Order to be excluded from transfer under section 23(2) of that Act, no agreement having been reached in relation thereto under section 23(4) of that Act;

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 23(5) and (6) and 89(4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992, the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Further Education (Exclusion of Land from Transfer) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 31st March 1993.

**2.** (1) It is hereby directed that the interests in land described in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order held by the local authorities named in column (2) of that Schedule, together with any rights or liabilities of those authorities related to those interests, shall be excluded from transferring under section 23(2) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 to the further education corporations specified in column (3) of that Schedule.

(2) The notes to the Schedule to this Order shall have effect for the purpose of conferring rights and imposing liabilities, being rights and liabilities that could have been conferred or imposed by an agreement under section 23(4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
