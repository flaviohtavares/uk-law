# The United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Legal Assessors) (Amendment) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 892

NURSES, MIDWIVES AND HEALTH VISITORS

The United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Legal Assessors) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made

22nd March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Lord Chancellor and, in relation to proceedings in Scotland, the Lord Advocate, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 12(5) of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to, the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1979[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Legal Assessors) (Amendment) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

**2.** Article 1(2) of the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Legal Assessors) Order 1983[^f00002] shall be amended by adding the words “, except the Preliminary Proceedings Committee,” after the words “means any committee”.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 22nd March 1993

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord Advocate

Lord Advocate’s Chambers

22nd March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Legal Assessors) Order 1983 in consequence of the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors (Professional Conduct) Rules 1993 which provide for the creation of a Preliminary Proceedings Committee which may sit without a legal assessor being present.
