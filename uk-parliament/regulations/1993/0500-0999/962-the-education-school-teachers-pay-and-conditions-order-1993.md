# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 962

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1993

Made

30th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

1st April 1993

Coming into force

22nd April 1993

The review body appointed under section 1(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] having made a report following the reference to them of certain matters, the Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act and after consulting such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of voluntary and grant-maintained schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to him to be concerned, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 22nd April 1993.

## 2. Application

This Order applies to all school teachers (as defined in the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991) in England and Wales.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 1992 by uprating the amounts of scales and allowances and making consequential changes where necessary. There is a new provision for payment of a lump sum (article 3(c)(iv)). Redundant provisions relating to the calculation of pupil numbers have been removed (article 3(j) and (l)(iii). Part B of Appendix I to the Document has been amended to take account of special schools with delegated budgets (article 3(k) (l)(i) and (ii)).

The retrospective effect of these changes is authorised by section 2(4)(f) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991.
