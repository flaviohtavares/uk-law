# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 968

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

31st April 1993

Laid before Parliament

2nd April 1993

Coming into force

1st May 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sections 14(2) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain, I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st May 1993.

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Scotland.

**2.** Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984[^f00002], shall be amended by substituting for the figure “£6.20”, in both places where it occurs, the figure “£6.40”.

**3.** The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1992[^f00003], are hereby revoked.

Home Office

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

31st March 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming on bingo club premises in England and Wales from £6.20 to £6.40.
