# The Chester Port Health Authority (Revocation)Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 786

PORT HEALTH AUTHORITIES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Chester Port Health Authority (Revocation)Order 1993

Made

11th March 1993

Coming into force

31st March 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 2 and 4(2) of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having given notice required by section 4(2) of that Act of his proposal to make this Order and not having received any notice of objection thereto, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chester Port Health Authority (Revocation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 31st March 1993.

## 2. Revocation

The Orders specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.

## 3. Compensation Payment

There shall be paid as compensation for loss of office to the Clerk of Chester Port Health Authority the sum of #1,032.00 payable in the following proportions by the constituent authorities for the Port Health Authority:—

- Delyn Borough Council 10/20ths
- Alyn and Deeside District Council 4/20ths
- Rhuddlan Borough Council 1/20th
- Chester City Council 5/20ths

## SCHEDULE — REVOCATIONS

Article 2

Orders relating to the Chester Port Health Authority:—

- the Order of the Local Government Board dated 28th March 1888;
- the Order of the Local Government Board dated 4th January 1890;
- the Order of the Local Government Board dated 4th December 1897;
- the Order of the Local Government Board dated 7th July 1902;
- the Chester Port Sanitary Order dated 21st May 1936;
- The Chester Port Amendment Order dated 14th March 1938[^f00002].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

R. Jarman

An Under Secretary in the Welsh office

11th March 1993
