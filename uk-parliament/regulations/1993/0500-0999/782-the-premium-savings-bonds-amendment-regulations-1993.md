# The Premium Savings Bonds (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 782

NATIONAL DEBT

The Premium Savings Bonds (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

17th March 1993

Laid before Parliament

18th March 1993

Coming into force

13th April 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 11 of the National Debt Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Premium Savings Bonds (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 13th April 1993.

**2.** The Premium Savings Bonds Regulations 1972[^f00002] shall be amended in regulation 5(1) *(maximum holding of bonds)* by substituting for the figures “10,000” the figures “20,000”.

Tim Wood

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

17th March 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which amend the Premium Savings Bonds Regulations 1972, increase the maximum permitted holding of premium savings bonds from 10,000 to 20,000 bond units.
