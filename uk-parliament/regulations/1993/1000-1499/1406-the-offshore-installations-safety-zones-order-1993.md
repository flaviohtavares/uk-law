# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1406

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1993

Made

4th June 1993

Coming into force

5th July 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(1) and (2) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 5th July 1993.

**2.** A safety zone is hereby established around each of the installations specified in column 1 of the Schedule hereto (being installations stationed in waters to which sub-section (7) of section 21 of the Act applies) having a radius of five hundred metres from the point as respects that installation which has the co-ordinates of latitude and longitude according to European Datum (1950) specified in columns 2 and 3 of the Schedule.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Michael Forsyth

Minister of State,

Department of Employment

4th June 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes, under section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987, safety zones having a radius of 500 metres from a specified point, around the installations specified in the Schedule to this Order and stationed in waters to which section 21(7) of that Act applies (these include territorial waters and waters in areas designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29)).

Vessels (which for this purpose include hovercraft, submersible apparatus and installations in transit) are prohibited from entering or remaining in a safety zone except with the consent of the Secretary of State or in accordance with regulations made under section 23(1) of the Act (currently the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1331))).
