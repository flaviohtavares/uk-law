# The Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1418 (C.27)

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1993

Made

3rd June 1993

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6(2) of the Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1993.

**2.** Sections 1(2) and 4 of the Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993 shall come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

John Selwyn Gummer

Secretary of State for the Environment

3rd June 1993.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force sections 1(2) and 4 of the Non-Domestic Rating Act 1993 on the day after the day the Order is made.
