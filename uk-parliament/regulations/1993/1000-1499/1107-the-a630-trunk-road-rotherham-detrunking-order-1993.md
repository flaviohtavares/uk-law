# The A630 Trunk Road (Rotherham) (Detrunking) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1107

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A630 Trunk Road (Rotherham) (Detrunking) Order 1993

Made

15th April 1993

Coming into force

14th May 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A630 Trunk Road (Rotherham) (Detrunking) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 14th May 1993.

**2.** In this Order—

- (i) “the plan” means the plan numbered HA 10/YHNMD/11 marked “The A630 Trunk Road (Rotherham) (Detrunking) Order 1993”, signed by authority ofthe Secretary of State for Transport and deposited atthe Department of Transport, Romney House,43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (ii) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State;
- (iii) “the trunk road” means the A630 Trunk Road.

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which this order comes into force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J. P. Henry

Regional Director Yorkshire and Humberside Region

Department of Transport

15th April 1993

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is situated between the former Rotherham County Borough Boundary at Dalton (marked A on the plan) and the boundary of Doncaster Metropolitan Borough and Rotherham Metropolitan District at Hill Top Conisbrough (marked B on the plan) and is shown by broad black dashes on the plan.
