# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1357 (S.185)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1993

Made

25th May 1993

Coming into force

28th June 1993

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.3) (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1993 and shall come into force on 28th June 1993.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Table of Fees

In Chapter IV of the Table of Fees in rule 347 of the Rules of the Court of Session([^f00002]), in the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the figures set out in column 2 as applying to those paragraphs, substitute the figures set out in column 3 of that table; and the fees so substituted shall apply to work, in respect of which those fees are chargeable, done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force:–

## 3. Saving

The fees set out in column 2 of the table in paragraph 2 above shall continue to apply to work done before this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh,

25th May 1993

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends Chapter IV of the Table of Fees in rule 347 of the Rules of the Court of Session by increasing the fees payable to shorthand writers in the Court of Session by about 3.5 per cent.
