# The Lothian Region (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1191 (S.175)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Lothian Region (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1993

Made

27th April 1993

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1(2)

Whereas under section 17(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973([^f00001]) the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland on 27th November 1992 submitted to the Secretary of State a report on a review together with its proposals for a change of the electoral arrangements for the Lothian Region;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 233 of the said Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Lothian Region (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1993.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 5th May 1994 provided that for the purpose of all proceedings preliminary or relating to an election to be held on or after that day the Order shall come into force on 10th October 1993.

## 2. Interpretation

In the Schedule to this Order–

- (a) a reference to–
  - (i) an electoral division shall be construed as a reference to that electoral division as constituted in this Order;
  - (ii) a road, footpath, railway line, river, burn or canal shall, where the context so admits, be construed as the centre line of the feature concerned; and
  - (iii) the boundary or boundaries of a parish is to a line corresponding as nearly as may be to the line of the boundary or boundaries of the parish concerned as already published on Ordnance Survey Maps;
- (b) the expedient of defining a line beyond Mean Low Water Springs has been adopted to apportion islands to the appropriate electoral division; and
- (c) the following abbreviations are used:–

- “ED” for Electoral Division; and
- “grid reference” for National Grid Reference as published on Ordnance Survey Maps.

## 3. Change of electoral arrangements

The existing electoral divisions of the Lothian Region shall be abolished and the said Region shall be divided into forty-nine electoral divisions having the names, contents and boundaries set forth in the Schedule to this Order.

**4.** The boundaries of the electoral divisions constituted by this Order shall be defined and mered by Ordnance Survey.

## 5. Revocation

Without prejudice to its continuing in effect for the purposes of defining the electoral divisions prior to the coming into force of this Order, the Lothian Region (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1978([^f00002]) is hereby revoked.

Ian Lang

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

This Order gives effect to proposals made by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland, for the change of electoral arrangements for the Lothian Region (the Region) as described in the Schedule to the Lothian Region (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1978 (the 1978 Order).

The effect of the proposals is to divide the Region into 49 electoral divisions as described in the Schedule to the Order.

The 1978 Order is revoked by this Order.
