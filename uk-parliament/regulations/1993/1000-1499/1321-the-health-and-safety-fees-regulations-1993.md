# The Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1321

HEALTH AND SAFETY

The Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 1993

Made

19th May 1993

Laid before Parliament

27th May 1993

Coming into force

21st June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 43(2), (4) and (5) of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974[^f00001] (“the 1974 Act”) and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf and for the purpose of giving effect without modifications to proposals submitted to her by the Health and Safety Commission under section 11(2)(d) of the 1974 Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 21st June 1993.

**2.** The following table shall be substituted for the table set out in sub-paragraph (a) of the Schedule to the Offshore Installations (Life-saving Appliances) Regulations 1977[^f00002] and for the table set out in sub-paragraph (a) of the Schedule to the Offshore Installations (Fire-fighting Equipment) Regulations 1978[^f00003]:–

**TABLE**

| Time spent | Rate per hour or part thereof |
| --- | --- |
| On any day except a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday – |  |
| between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. | £42.00 |
| before 8 a.m. or after 6 p.m. | £63.00 |
| On a Saturday | £63.00 |
| On a Sunday or public holiday | £84.00 |

**3.** In each of regulations 5(2)(c) and 5(5)(b) of the Road Traffic (Training of Drivers of Vehicles Carrying Dangerous Goods) Regulations 1992[^f00004], there shall be substituted for the figure “2.50”, the figure “2.70”.

**4.** The Offshore Installations (Life-saving Appliances and Fire-fighting Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 1990[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

Patrick McLoughlin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

19th May 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations update fees payable under the Offshore Installations (Life-saving Appliances) Regulations 1977 ([S.I. 1977/486](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/486), amended by [S.I. 1984/419](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/419) and [S.I. 1990/707](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/707)), the Offshore Installations (Fire-fighting Equipment) Regulations 1978 ([S.I. 1978/611](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/611), amended by [S.I. 1984/419](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/419) and [S.I. 1990/707](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/707)) and the Road Traffic (Training of Drivers of Vehicles Carrying Dangerous Goods) Regulations 1992 ([S.I. 1992/744](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/744)).
