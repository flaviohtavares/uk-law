# The Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1347 (S.183)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1993

Made

21st May 1993

Coming into force

30th June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1993 and shall come into force on 30th June 1993.

**2.** The period during which the Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws 1982 are to remain in force([^f00002]) is hereby extended to 30th June 1994.

T.D. Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department 27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

21st May 1993
