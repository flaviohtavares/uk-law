# The Oldham National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1068

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Oldham National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

Made

14th April 1993

Laid before Parliament

16th April 1993

Coming into force

7th May 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by Oldham Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Oldham National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Oldham National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 7th May 1993.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means Oldham Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Oldham National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 15th December 1992, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 7th May 1993 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health

14th April 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Oldham National Health Service Trust on 7th May trustproperty formerly held by Oldham Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trustproperty to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, NationalHealth Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
