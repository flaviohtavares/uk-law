# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No.2) (Partial Revocation) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1151 (S.169)

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No.2) (Partial Revocation) Order 1993

Made

23rd April 1993

Laid before Parliament

27th April 1993

Coming into force

24th April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) and (3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No.2) (Partial Revocation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 24th April 1993.

## 2. Partial revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No.2) Order 1993([^f00002]) is hereby revoked except insofar as it applies in relation to shellfish and farmed salmon.

E Davison

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

23rd April 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibition) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No.2) Order 1993 insofar as it applies to fish other than shellfish and farmed salmon.

That Order contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food rendered unsuitable for that purpose by virtue of fish having been affected by oil and other chemical pollutants. The prohibitions in that Order will remain in place for shellfish and farmed salmon.
