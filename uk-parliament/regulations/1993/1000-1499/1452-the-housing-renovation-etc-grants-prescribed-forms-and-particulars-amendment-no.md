# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1993/552](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/552) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 1452

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1993

Made

7th June 1993

Laid before Parliament

15th June 1993

Coming into force

6th July 1993

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 102(2) and (4), 138(1)[^f00001] and 190(1) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 6th July 1993.

## 2. Amendments

The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00003]are amended as follows:

- (a) in Schedule 1 (amendments to Forms 1 and 2)—
  - (i) in paragraph 2, before “substitute” insert the words “in Form 1”;
  - (ii) after paragraph 2, insert the following—

    > **2A.** For question 1.16 in Form 2 substitute—
    >
    > > **1.16.** Will you or a member of your family carry out the works? **Note 8A**
    > >
    > > - Yes \[ \]
    > > - No \[ \]

    ; and
  - (iii) in paragraph 3, after the word “against” insert the words ““Attendance allowance”and against”;
- (b) In Schedule 2 (amendments to Form 3), in paragraph 3 after the word “against” insert the words ““Attendance allowance” and against”.

Michael Howard

Secretary of State for the Environment

28th May 1993

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

7th June 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct minor defects in Schedules 1 and 2 to the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/552](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/552)).
