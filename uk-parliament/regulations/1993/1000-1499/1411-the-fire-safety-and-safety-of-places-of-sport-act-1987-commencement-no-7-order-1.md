# The Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1411 (C.26)

FIRE PRECAUTIONS

The Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1993

Made

4th June 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 50(2) of the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1993.

**2.** The provisions of the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st August 1993.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

4th June 1993
