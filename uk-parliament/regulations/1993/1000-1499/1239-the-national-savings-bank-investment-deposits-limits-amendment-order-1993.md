# The National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) (Amendment) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1239

SAVINGS BANKS

The National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made

7th May 1993

Coming into force

10th May 1993

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament and a period of 40 days beginning with the day of laying has expired and neither House of Parliament has resolved that the Order be not made;

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 4 of the National Savings Bank Act 1971[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) (Amendment) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th May 1993.

**2.** The National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) Order 1977 [^f00002] shall be amended in article 3 by the substitution of the figure “£100,000” for the figure “£25,000”.

**3.** The National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) (Amendment) Order 1989[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Tim Wood

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th May 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which further amends the National Savings Bank (Investment Deposits) (Limits) (Order) 1977, increases the limit on the aggregate amount which can be accepted by the Director of Savings from any person by way of an investment deposit in the National Savings Bank from £25,000 to £100,000
