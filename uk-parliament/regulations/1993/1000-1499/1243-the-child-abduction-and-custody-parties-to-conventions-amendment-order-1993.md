# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1243

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made

12th May 1993

Coming into force

1st July 1993

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of May 1993

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 13 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st July 1993.

(2) The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992[^f00002] is revoked.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following—

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It adds Greece to the list of the Contracting States to the European Convention on Recognition and Enforcement of Decisions Concerning Custody of Children and on the Restoration of Custody of Children, signed at Luxembourg on 20th May 1980 (Cm. 191).
