# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1355 (S.184)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1993

Made

25th May 1993

Laid before Parliament

7th June 1993

Coming into force

28th June 1993

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1993 and shall come into force on 28th June 1993.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Table Of Fees

(1) The Table of Fees set out in Schedule 2 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Witnesses and Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1992([^f00002]), shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraphs.

(2) In paragraph 5 (*Carbon Copies*), for–

> - *Carbon Copies*
> - Carbon copies of notes of evidence

substitute–

> - *Copies*
> - Copies of notes of evidence by carbon or any other means

.

(3) In the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the figures set out in column 2 as applying to those paragraphs, substitute the figures set out in column 3 of that table; and the fees so substituted shall apply to work, for which those fees are chargeable, done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force:–

## 3. Saving

The fees set out in column 2 of the table in paragraph 2(3) above, shall continue to apply to work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Edinburgh,

25th May 1993

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Table of Fees for shorthand writers in civil proceedings in the sheriff court:–

- (a) to provide for a fee for copying notes of evidence by means other than carbon; and
- (b) to increase the fees payable by about 3.5 per cent
