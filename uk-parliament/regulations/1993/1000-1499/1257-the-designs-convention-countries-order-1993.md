# The Designs (Convention Countries) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1257

DESIGNS

The Designs (Convention Countries) Order 1993

Made

12th May 1993

Coming into force

14th June 1993

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of May 1993

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of powers conferred upon Her by section 13(1) of the Registered Designs Act 1949[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Designs (Convention Countries) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 14th June 1993.

**2.** Belarus, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Kazakhstan, the Slovak Republic, Slovenia, the Russian Federation and the Ukraine are convention countries for the purposes of all the provisions of the Registered Designs Act 1949.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Governments of Belarus, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Kazakhstan, the Slovak Republic, Slovenia, the Russian Federation and the Ukraine have given notice of their respective accessions as such to the International Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property (as revised at Stockholm in 1967) (Cmnd. 4431). This Order declares each of those countries to be a convention country for the purposes of the Registered Designs Act 1949.
