# The Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1992/3098](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3098) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 1223

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

5th May 1993

Laid before the House of Commons

10th May 1993

Coming into force

31st May 1993

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 23 of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 31st May 1993.

**2.** In paragraph (b) of regulation 4 of the Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) Regulations 1980[^f00002]–

- (a) after sub-paragraph (i) “and” shall be omitted;
- (b) for sub-paragraph (ii) there shall be substituted the following–

  > - (ii) services where the tax on the supply is payable solely by the person to whom they are supplied as provided for in section 7 of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00003]; and

  ; and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (ii) there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph–

  > - (iii) goods where the tax on the supply is payable solely by the person to whom they are supplied as provided for in section 32B of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00004].

  .

E. Woods

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House 22 Upper Ground London SE1 9PJ

5th May 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 4 of the Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) Regulations 1980 (“the 1980 Regulations”) as amended by the Value Added Tax (Repayment to Community Traders) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 (“the 1992 Regulations”). Regulation 4 of the 1992 Regulations did not achieve what was intended to be achieved, namely the implementation of the modification of Article 17.4 of the European Economic Community Sixth Directive (No. [77/388/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0388) OJ No. 445, 13.7.1977, p.1) which modification enables Community traders whose taxable supplies fall to be taxed in accordance with section 32B of the Value Added Tax Act 1983 to be entitled to repayment of tax under the 1980 Regulations.

These Regulations achieve what was intended to be achieved by inserting sub-paragraph (iii) into regulation 4(b) of the 1980 Regulations. These Regulations also update a statutory reference in sub-paragraph (ii) of that regulation.
