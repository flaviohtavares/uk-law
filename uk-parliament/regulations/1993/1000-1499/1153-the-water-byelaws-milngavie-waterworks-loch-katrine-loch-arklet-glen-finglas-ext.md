# The Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1153 (S.170)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1993

Made

23rd April 1993

Coming into force

25th May 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1993 and shall come into force on 25th May 1993.

**2.** The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws 1982 and the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force([^f00002]) is hereby extended to 25th May 1994.

T D Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department 27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

23rd April 1993
