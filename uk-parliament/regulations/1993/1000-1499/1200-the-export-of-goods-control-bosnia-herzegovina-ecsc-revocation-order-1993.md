# The Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) (Revocation) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1200

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) (Revocation) Order 1993

Made

29th April 1993

Coming into force

30th April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) (Revocation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 30th April 1993.

**2.** The Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) Order 1993[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

M. V. Coolican

An Assistant Secretary,

Department of Trade and Industry

29th April1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) Order 1993 which prohibited the export of goods covered by the ECSC Treaty to Bosnia-Herzegovina, except under the authority of a licence granted by the Secretary of State or which was in accordance with an export authorisation issued by the competent authority of a Member State in conformity with Decision [92/470/ECSC](https://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1992/470/oj), as amended.

That Decision has now been revoked and prohibition on the export of all goods to Bosnia-Herzegovina except under the authority of a licence granted by the Secretary of State is now contained in the Export of Goods (Control) (Croatian and Bosnian Territories) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/1189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1189)), in accordance with Resolution 820 (1993) of the Security Council of the United Nations adopted on 17th April 1993.
