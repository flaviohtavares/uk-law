# The Stirling Royal Infirmary National Health Service Trust (Appointment of Trustees) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1134 (S.162)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Stirling Royal Infirmary National Health Service Trust (Appointment of Trustees) Order 1993

Made

19th April 1993

Coming into force

30th April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12G(2) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Stirling Royal Infirmary National Health Service Trust (Appointment of Trustees) Order 1993, and shall come into force on 30th April 1993.

(2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires–

- “the Act” means the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978;
- “the Trust” means the Stirling Royal Infirmary National Health Service Trust.

## 2. Appointment of trustees

The chairman, non-executive directors and executive directors of the Trust are appointed trustees*ex officiis*for the purpose of holding any property which is to be held in trust on behalf of the Trust under section 12G of the Act.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th April 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the chairman, non-executive directors and executive directors and their successors in office of the Stirling Royal Infirmary National Health Service Trust as trustees for the purposes of holding any property which is to be held in trust on behalf of the Trust.
