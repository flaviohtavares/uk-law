# The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3120

COUNTY COURTS

The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1993

Made

9th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1993

Coming into force

4th January 1994

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment No.2) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 4th January 1994.

**2.** The holding of the East Grinstead, Hexham, Malton, Thorne and Whitby County Courts shall be discontinued and Schedule 3 to the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00002] shall accordingly be amended by deleting the entries relating to those courts[^f00003].

Mackay of Clashfern, C

Dated 9th December 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to close the East Grinstead, Hexham, Malton, Thorne and Whitby County Courts.
