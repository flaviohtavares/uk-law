# The Parliamentary Pensions (Consolidation and Amendment) Regulations 1993, section N6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## N6. Deemed contributions

For the purposes of regulations N3(4) and N4(3) any payments made by a person for the purchase of added years shall be treated as contributions.
