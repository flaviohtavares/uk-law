# The Parliamentary Pensions (Consolidation and Amendment) Regulations 1993, section P5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P5. Certification by Government Actuary

For the purposes of regulations P1, P2 and P3, any transfer value of the whole or part of a person’s accrued pension rights under these Regulations or under Part II of the 1965 Act shall be such sum as shall satisfy the requirements prescribed under Schedule 1A to the Pensions Act 1975 and shall be certified by, or calculated in accordance with tables prepared by, the Government Actuary.
