# The Parliamentary Pensions (Consolidation and Amendment) Regulations 1993, section H2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H2. Early retirement for office holders who have been Members

A person who is entitled to receive a pension under regulation H1 who is or has been a participating office holder shall (subject to Part G (Commutation)) be entitled also to receive a pension under regulation F3 calculated in accordance with regulation F4 and abated in accordance with Schedule 4 and payable from the same date as the pension payable under regulation H1.
