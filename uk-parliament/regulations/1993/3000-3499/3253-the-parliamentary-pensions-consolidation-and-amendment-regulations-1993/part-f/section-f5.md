# The Parliamentary Pensions (Consolidation and Amendment) Regulations 1993, section F5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F5. Permitted maximum pensions: pensioners who joined before 1st June 1989

- The annual amount of the pension payable under regulation F1 or regulation F3 to a person who became a participant before 1st June 1989 shall not exceed whichever is the less of—
  - (a) the amount equal to two-thirds of the multiple; or
  - (b) such maximum pension as is calculated in respect of that person in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2,

  except to the extent that it is calculated by reference to so much (if any) of that person’s actual period of reckonable service as a participant as fell after he attained the age of sixty-five years.
