# The Parliamentary Pensions (Consolidation and Amendment) Regulations 1993, SCHEDULE 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 14 — Refunds

Regulation R3

### 1. Contributions paid under section 5 of the 1965 Act

Any contributions paid under section 5 of the 1965 Act by a person who—

- (a) ceased to be a Member of the House of Commons before 1st January 1972 and has not been a Member of that House at any time on or after that date; and
- (b) did not complete ten years reckonable service within the meaning of Part II of that Act,

shall at his request (if they have not previously been refunded) be refunded to him by the Trustees, with interest from the dates on which the contributions were paid respectively.
