# The Education (School Curriculum and Assessment Authority) (Orders for Transfer of Property and Staff) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3105

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Curriculum and Assessment Authority) (Orders for Transfer of Property and Staff) Order 1993

Made

9th December 1993

The Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 247(5) and 248(8) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Curriculum and Assessment Authority) (Orders for Transfer of Property and Staff) Order 1993.

## 2. Substitution of shorter period for making orders transferring property and staff to School Curriculum and Assessment Authority

There is substituted for the period specified in sections 247(2) and 248(6) of the Education Act 1993 (“the Act”), being in both sections a period of six months beginning with the day on which section 244 of the Act came into force[^f00002], the period of two months and fifteen days beginning with the day on which section 244 of the Act came into force.

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

9th December 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes the period of two months and fifteen days beginning with the day on which section 244 of the Education Act 1993 came into force (1st October 1993) for the period of six months beginning with that day as the period during which orders may be made by the Secretary of State under sections 247(1) and 248(1)(b) of that Act for the transfer of property and staff respectively from the National Curriculum Council and the School Examinations and Assessment Council to the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority.
