# The Commercial Agents (Council Directive) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 1993/3053](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3053) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 3173

COMMERCIAL AGENTS

The Commercial Agents (Council Directive) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

16th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

17th December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to relations between commercial agents and their principals, in the exercise of the powers conferred by him by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Commercial Agents (Council Directive) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

**2.** The Commercial Agents (Council Directive) Regulations 1993[^f00003] are hereby amended in regulation 18 by adding after the opening word “The” the words “indemnity or”.

Strathclyde

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

16th December 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Commercial Agents (Council Directive) Regulations 1993 by adding some omitted words to regulation 18 (Grounds for excluding payment of indemnity or compensation under regulation 17).
