# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 6) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3264

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 6) Order 1993

Made

31st December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994 Y

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No.6) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

**2.** The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992[^f00003] shall be further amended as follows—

- (a) in Schedule 2 “the Czech Republic” and “the Slovak Republic” shall be deleted.

M.V. Coolican

An Assistant Secretary,

Department of Trade and Industry

31st December 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992 by deleting the Czech and Slovak Republics from the list of countries in Schedule 2.
