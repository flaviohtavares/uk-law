# The Public Services Contracts Regulations 1993, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Amendment of the Public Supply Contracts Regulations

The Public Supply Contracts Regulations 1991[^f00027] shall be amended—

- (a) in regulation 2(1) by omitting from the definition of “public supply contract” subparagraph (ii);
- (b) by deleting regulation 7(7).
