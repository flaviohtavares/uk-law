# The Public Services Contracts Regulations 1993, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Supplementary information

The contracting authority may require a services provider to provide information supplementing the information supplied in accordance with regulations 14, 15 and 15 or to clarify that information, provided that the information so required relates to the matters specified in regulations 14, 15 and 16.
