# The Public Services Contracts Regulations 1993, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Sub-contractors

A contracting authority may require a services provider to indicate in his tender what part of the public services contract he intends to sub-contract to another person.
