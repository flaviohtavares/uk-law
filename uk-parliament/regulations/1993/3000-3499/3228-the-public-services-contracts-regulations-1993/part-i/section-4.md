# The Public Services Contracts Regulations 1993, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Services providers

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations, a “services provider” means a person—

- (a) who sought, or who seeks, or who would have wished,
  - (i) to be the person to whom a public services contract is awarded, or
  - (ii) to participate in a design contest, and
- (b) who is a national of and established in a member State.

(2) When these Regulations apply a contracting authority shall not treat a person who is not a national of and established in a member State more favourably than one who is.
