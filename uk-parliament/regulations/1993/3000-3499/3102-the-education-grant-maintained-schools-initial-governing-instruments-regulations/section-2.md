# The Education (Grant-maintained Schools) (Initial Governing Instruments) Regulations 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Initial instrument of government

(1) For the purposes of section 56 of the 1993 Act, the initial instrument of government of a grant-maintained school shall be as set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

(2) In this regulation and in regulations 3 and 4 below, references to the 1993 Act are to the Education Act 1993.
