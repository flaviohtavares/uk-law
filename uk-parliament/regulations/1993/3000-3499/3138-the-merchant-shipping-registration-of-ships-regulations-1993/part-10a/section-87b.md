# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 87B

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87B. Suspension under a Certificate of Permission[^key-40aa6408905daf1d4c29c47a3896521c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The registration of a ship is suspended if the Registrar has issued a certificate of permission under regulation 87D and has not, after the issue of that certificate of permission, either restored the registration of the ship under regulation 87L or removed the entry relating to the ship from the Register under regulation 87M.
