# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 87G

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87G. Cancellation of Certificate of Registry[^key-40aa6408905daf1d4c29c47a3896521c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Upon issuing a certificate of permission, the Registrar must record in the Register the period of validity remaining in respect of the certificate of registry, and then cancel the certificate of registry.
