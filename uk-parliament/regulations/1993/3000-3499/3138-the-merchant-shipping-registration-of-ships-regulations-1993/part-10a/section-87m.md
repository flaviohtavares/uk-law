# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 87M

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87M. Removal of an Entry Relating to a Suspended Ship from the Register[^key-40aa6408905daf1d4c29c47a3896521c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Registrar may remove an entry from the Register where—

- (a) the Registrar has revoked a certificate of permission under regulation 87K(1)(a);
- (b) the Registrar has revoked a certificate of permission under regulation 87K(1)(b), (c) or (d); or
- (c) the Registrar has cancelled a carving and marking note under regulation 87L(3).

(2) Where the Registrar removes the entry relating to the ship from the Register, the Registrar must issue a closure transcript and notify any mortgagees of the removal of the entry from the Register.
