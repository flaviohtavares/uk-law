# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 87H

> Text as at 2026-04-17.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87H. Renewal of Certificate of Permission[^key-40aa6408905daf1d4c29c47a3896521c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application for renewal of a certificate of permission must be in a form approved by the Secretary of State, and must include or be accompanied by the information and documents specified in regulation 87C, and, subject to regulation 111, the current certificate of permission.

(2) The Registrar must renew a certificate of permission where the Registrar is satisfied that the conditions in sub-paragraphs (b) to (e) of regulation 87D(2) are satisfied.

(3) Where the Registrar renews a certificate of permission, the Registrar must issue a new certificate of permission, and cancel the previous certificate of permission.
