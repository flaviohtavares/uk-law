# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 87J

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87J. Cancellation[^c-40aa6408905daf1d4c29c47a3896521c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application to cancel a certificate of permission must—

- (a) be in a form approved by the Secretary of State,
- (b) include the written consent of all owners, and
- (c) subject to regulation 111, must be accompanied by the certificate of permission.

(2) Upon receipt of a valid application under paragraph (1), the Registrar must cancel the certificate of permission.
