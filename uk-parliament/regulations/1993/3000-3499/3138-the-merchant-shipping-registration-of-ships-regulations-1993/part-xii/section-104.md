# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 104

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Witnessing of documents[^key-eca6b77b6df83795be103b49aab2ffbb][^key-61cddf756d0cf0df646c3aeddbc7bc5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the signature on any document made under these Regulations is required to be witnessed any witness to the signature shall be a person of full age and shall not be the spouse of the signatory.
