# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 103

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Documents not in the English language to be accompanied by a translation[^key-e79d5edf160776e5eadcf48cb126b321][^key-1abb5b7dbfb23ca60bee19436636c1e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any document which is not in the English language and is produced in support of any application under these Regulations shall be accompanied by a notarised translation of the document in the English language.
