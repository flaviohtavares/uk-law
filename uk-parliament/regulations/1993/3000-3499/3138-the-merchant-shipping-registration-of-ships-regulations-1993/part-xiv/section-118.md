# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 118

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Issue of new certificates of registry[^key-67ada88fca849e4241e4a70d1f3fdee1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding regulations 51 (Change in registered particulars of ship) and 55 (Registration of changes of name or port of choice) any certificate of registry of a ship, other than a fishing vessel, which:—

- (a) is issued during the transitional period[^key-9d86eb4d69f8e35dc122526a3699a053], and
- (b) replaces an old certificate of registry

shall be valid for a period of five years from the date of issue.

(2) Any duplicate certificate issued to a ship including a small ship under regulation 108 (Duplicate certificate) during the transitional period[^key-6f1dc433a6dd735a7ea366bd968de40c] shall be a certificate issued in accordance with these Regulations and:—

- (a) for ships [^key-9e7c5fd81e07e116515a342ee1fbb7f0]..., shall be valid for a period of five years from the date of issue; and
- (b) for [^key-ee3bbba7d9fd2feb880cc9880ccd5cb8]... small ships shall expire on the date that the old certificate would have expired.

(3)

- (a) The Registrar may, subject to (b) below, during the transitional period[^key-2eab91f98e405a871fc87834b9d1cb33], at his discretion replace a ship’s old certificate with a new certificate of registry which shall be valid for a period of five years from the date of issue.
- (b) Any such new certificate issued in respect of a fishing vessel shall expire on the date that the old certificate would have expired.
