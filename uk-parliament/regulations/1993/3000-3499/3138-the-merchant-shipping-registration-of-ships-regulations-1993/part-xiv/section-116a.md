# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 116A

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116A. Continuation in force of old certificates[^key-925ce5a6e8720e55cc87b8370426007a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding regulation 116, an old certificate shall remain in force beyond the transitional period if–

- (a) an application to renew the certificate has been correctly made in accordance with regulation 42 above; and
- (b) the Registrar has not issued a new certificate by the date of expiry of the transitional period,

but any new certificate shall commence at that date.
