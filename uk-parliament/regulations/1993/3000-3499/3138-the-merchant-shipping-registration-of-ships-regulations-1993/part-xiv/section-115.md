# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 115

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Definitions for this Part[^c-771fec00135b53f50ba4940aa2a8cb2d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part,

- [^c-01f2868dcf93a5faab1a7d956b8a9d78]...
- ‘new certificate’ means a certificate of registry issued in accordance with these Regulations;
- ‘old certificate’ means any certificate of registry, other than a provisional certificate, issued under:—
  - (i) the Merchant Shipping Act 1894;
  - (ii) the Merchant Shipping Act 1983; or
  - (iii) the Merchant Shipping Act 1988.
- “transitional period” in relation to a ship means the period commencing on 21st March 1994 and ending on the date of expiry of the certificate which is specified in relation to such a ship in the table in regulation 116.[^c-3be44e6c8a023323266f9e974e96cb61]
