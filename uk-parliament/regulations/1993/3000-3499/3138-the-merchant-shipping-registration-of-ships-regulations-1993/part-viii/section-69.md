# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 69

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Provisional registration not to be renewed[^key-3706b11e8ca7a189a217a1eb2f8d7a02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a ship has been provisionally registered once, it shall not be provisionally registered again within one year of the date of the issue of the certificate of provisional registration, except with the consent of the Registrar.
