# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 65

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Application for provisional registration[^c-1df88bccf264eca0f613181dc8c305a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An application for provisional registration shall be in a form approved by the Secretary of State and shall be accompanied by the particulars required by regulation 22(1)[^c-70baaf36270735d92b738fa52412ef21] (Applications for registration) and regulation 24 (Applications by bodies corporate).
