# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 70

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Condition of provisional registration for fishing vessels[^key-7ec274e90450ab4e0227cdb1647b2e03]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be a condition of provisional registration for fishing vessels that the vessel shall not fish for profit while so registered; and if any provisionally registered vessel does fish for profit its provisional registration shall immediately thereon terminate and the owner shall as soon as practicable surrender the certificate of provisional registry to the Registrar.
