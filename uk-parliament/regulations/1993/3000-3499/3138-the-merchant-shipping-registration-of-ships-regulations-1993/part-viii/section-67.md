# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 67

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Period of provisional registration[^key-bea524c4e512486111bd3ac856c8b8b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Registrar, on being satisfied that the ship is eligible for registration, or the appropriate person on receipt of that notification (but not otherwise), may proceed to register the ship provisionally for a period of 3 months.
