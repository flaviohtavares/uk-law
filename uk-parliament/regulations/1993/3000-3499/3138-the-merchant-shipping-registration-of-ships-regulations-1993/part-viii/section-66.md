# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 66

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Scrutiny of particulars of eligibility[^key-0cbfe10cfa66a2905dbec1dc313f5efe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where application is made to the appropriate person he shall forward those particulars to the Registrar who shall, if he is satisfied that the ship is eligible for registration, notify the appropriate person accordingly.
