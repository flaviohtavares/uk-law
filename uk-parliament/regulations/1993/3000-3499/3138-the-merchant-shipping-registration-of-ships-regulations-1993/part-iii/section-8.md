# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 8

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. British connection and majority interest[^c-ba30ac4a6c6076733d773a6b8bf0d4dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the relevant provisions of the sanctions regulations,[^c-60a7d0d831cba4a703ff48400a57f85c] regulation 36(4) (Refusal of registration) and paragraphs (2), (3), (4) and (5)[^c-a99d3629c9237dd62c5d6e439f0d1fda] below a ship shall be entitled to be registered if a majority interest in the ship is owned by one or more persons qualified under regulation 7(1)[^c-8a099aa4f6185d40453fd02b53e1a9aa].

(2) Where a majority interest in a ship is owned by[^c-1a2c8c67de7aee5d5aa7770cdd3dc55c] a person or persons qualified by reason of regulation 7(1), other than sub-paragraphs (c), (d) or (g)[^c-d1f836e8c9e90b1ec7e2704601fae879], the ship shall, subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) below, be registered only if that person or (as the case may be) any of those persons is resident in the United Kingdom.

(3) Where the condition in paragraph (2) is not satisfied the ship shall be registered only if a representative person is appointed in relation to the ship under Part V.

(4) Where[^c-84e883e806140935d426093b18657acd] the majority interest is owned by persons one or more of whom are persons who are qualified by reason of:—

- (a) regulation 7(1)(c) or (d), the ship shall be registered only if:—
  - (i) that person, or (as the case may be) any of those persons, is resident in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) where that condition is not satisfied, the Secretary of State furnishes a declaration that he consents to the ship being registered, and, in addition, a representative person is appointed in relation to the ship;
- (b) regulation 7(1)(g), the ship shall be registered only if:—
  - (i) the body corporate has a place of business in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) where that condition is not satisfied, if a representative person is appointed in relation to the ship.

(5) Where[^c-c19c2850abdf750246c32b8b64605c44] the majority interest is owned by the following persons:—

- (a) one or more persons who are qualified by reason of regulation 7(1), other than sub-paragraphs (c), (d) or (g)[^c-101ac17e1c0214cedd76a97b96b7b48e], and
- (b) one or more persons qualified by reason of regulation 7(1)(c) or (d), or
- (c) one or more persons qualified by reason of regulation 7(1)(g),

the ship shall only be[^c-2984002139a56b5adb0ef6486df5cb4e] registered—

- (i) if any of those persons is resident in the United Kingdom, or
- (ii) (where that condition is not satisfied) if a representative person is appointed in relation to the ship.
