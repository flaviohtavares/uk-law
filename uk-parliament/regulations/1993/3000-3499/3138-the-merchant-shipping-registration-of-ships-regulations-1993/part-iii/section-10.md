# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 10

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Government ships[^key-5bc6c7ffa62e78108419302fe91a72ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part applies to a ship to which section 80 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1906[^f00008] applies (Government ships).
