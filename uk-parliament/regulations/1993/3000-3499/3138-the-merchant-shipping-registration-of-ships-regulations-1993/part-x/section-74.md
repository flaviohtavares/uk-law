# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 74

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Qualification and entitlement for registration of a fishing vessel as a bareboat charter ship[^key-61a231a9488a1d32779fe2941031f373]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The persons prescribed by regulation 12 who charter a fishing vessel on bareboat charter terms shall be eligible to register it on Part IV of the Register under section 7 of the Act.

(2) A fishing vessel shall not be registered on Part IV of the Register unless it is managed, and its operations controlled and directed, from within the United Kingdom.
