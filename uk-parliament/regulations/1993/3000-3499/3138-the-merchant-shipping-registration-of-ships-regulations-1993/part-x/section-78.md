# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 78

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Names[^key-cea3c40c2b077d8f6cc698eed269c129]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On making an application for registration of a bareboat charter ship the applicant shall propose a name which the ship is to be called while so registered.

(2) If the Registrar is satisfied that the name is in compliance with the provisions of Schedule 1 he shall approve the name.
