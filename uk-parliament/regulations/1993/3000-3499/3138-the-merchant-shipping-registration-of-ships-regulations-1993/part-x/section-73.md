# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 73

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Qualification and entitlement for registration of bareboat charter ships other than fishing vessels[^c-5ede66551fb2193dba2d277988c1c12a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The persons qualified to be the owners of British ships by virtue of regulation 7(1) who charter a ship (other than a fishing vessel) on bareboat charter terms shall be qualified to register a bareboat charter ship under section 7 of the Act.
