# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 76

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Dispensations for the bareboat charterers of fishing vessels[^key-17ec0ecfc919976b0d1333a77f8fcd10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The charterers of fishing vessels which are, or are to be, registered as bareboat charter ships may apply for dispensation from the eligibility requirements in accordance with regulation 15 (Dispensations).
