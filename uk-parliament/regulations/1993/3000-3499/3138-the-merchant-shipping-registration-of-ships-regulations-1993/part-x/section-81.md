# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 81

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Inspection of marks etc[^c-917f769a61ff3f089af5820e91ee6499]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 33 (Inspection of marks) and 35 (Cancellation of carving and marking note) shall apply to this Part as if any reference in them to the owner was a reference to the charterer[^c-fcfa45d36c9f2fd84f48a0bc7eebb01e].
