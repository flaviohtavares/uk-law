# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 79

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Allocation of identifying number; port of choice and port numbers[^c-7717d25fc66484c93e0d4c6d3698dde8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On making application for registration of a bareboat charter ship the applicant shall specify one of the ports listed in part 1 or 2 of Schedule 2, as is appropriate, which it is intended shall be the port of choice.

(2)[^c-df2b613fba2112350cf05cfeee9d7395] Where the application is made in respect of a fishing vessel and the Registrar is satisfied that the vessel is eligible to be registered as a bareboat charter ship, he shall:

- (a) allocate a port number, and
- (b) allocate an identifying number, whether or not the vessel already has a number allocated by its primary register.

(3) In the case of any other ship the Registrar may allocate an identifying number, whether or not the ship already has a number allocated by its primary register.
