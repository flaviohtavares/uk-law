# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 75

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Appointment of representative person[^key-263f45df82f70b038db181686ccadca0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the charterer is not resident in the United Kingdom he shall appoint a representative person and Part V shall apply as if the charterer were the owner.
