# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 85

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Application of other regulations to this Part[^c-179e5d3a2c937798034a5982c300f856]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 51 (Change in registered particulars of ship), 52 (Change of name), 53 (Transfer of port of choice) and 54 (Re-marking of ship) shall apply to this Part as if any reference in them to the owner was a reference to the charterer[^c-86a131bfd25e525e0bcd627781e9d00c].
