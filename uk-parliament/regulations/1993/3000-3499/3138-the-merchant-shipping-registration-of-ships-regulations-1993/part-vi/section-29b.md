# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 29B

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**29B.**[^key-b3790c1267e3cd64871ab5147804391b] Where the Registrar is not satisfied that the engine power details notified to him, or recorded, for any fishing vessel are correct, he may require the owner to have the engine power measured in accordance with Article 5 of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930) and to notify the details to him.
