# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 55

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Registration of changes of name or port of choice[^key-d06e829ac08428d88c151867d2cb4a9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On receipt of the marking note duly completed the Registrar shall re-register the ship with its new name and/or port of choice and shall cancel the existing certificate and shall issue to the owner a new certificate of registry expiring on the same date as the existing one.
