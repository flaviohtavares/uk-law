# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 40

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Documents to be retained by the Registrar[^key-94ce2a928bfb7eb1af77e55589c363bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)

- (a) On registering a ship the Registrar shall retain in his possession a copy of any builder’s certificate or bill of sale or other evidence of title produced on first registration, any certificate of measurement or survey, and all declarations of eligibility.
- (b) On a fishing vessel changing from simple registration to full registration the Registrar shall retain in his possession a copy of the evidence adduced for that change [^key-5370e7eb826f2fbadef6c67afad7a3fb]....
- (c)[^key-d8bc9b6ed840b70bbce6cc0cf1cf88c2] On registering a fishing vessel which has had its engine power permanently de-rated the Registrar shall retain in his possession the modification explanation which has been submitted to him in accordance with regulation 29A(2).

(2) All original documents[^key-46251dc0fab6d0e495fa48e32b500ba8] which have been produced to the Registrar to establish title shall be returned to the applicant once the ship has been registered. [^key-a88909d0ea8fa38b73d2c65e4755f209]...
