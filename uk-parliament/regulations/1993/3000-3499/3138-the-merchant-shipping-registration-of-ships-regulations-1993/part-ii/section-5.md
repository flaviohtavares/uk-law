# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 5

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Registration on only one Part of the Register[^c-6e5af3ee144a850e6ff2e0a33575f0a8][^c-a1368fb692a26ae8b407ea43534d8b4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No ship, including a small ship, may be registered on more than one part of the Register at any one time.
