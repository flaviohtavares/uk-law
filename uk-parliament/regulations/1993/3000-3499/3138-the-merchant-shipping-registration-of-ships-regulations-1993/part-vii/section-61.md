# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 61

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Transfer or transmission of registered mortgage[^key-9b350474f9b2a50fb5f362d7fbc6cf70][^key-afdaf91f3d035d3e9378585d7fed6918]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a transfer of a registered mortgage or evidence of a transmission is produced to the Registrar, he shall:—

- (a) enter the name of the transferee, or the name of the person to whom the mortgage has been transmitted, in the Register as mortgagee of the ship or share in question;
- (b) in respect of a transfer, where an original is provided,[^key-dd6b0a07a9a983e15080262114a669ec] endorse on the instrument of transfer the date and time the entry was made.
