# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 57

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Form of mortgage[^c-d345fa1a3ff5c770947abe14bf1e485d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) A mortgage produced for registration under Schedule 1 to the Act, and
- (b) a transfer of a registered mortgage, and
- (c) a discharge of a registered mortgage,

shall be in a form approved by the Secretary of State[^c-56ba7732f9414be83546795fe833ca2d], in each case with appropriate attestation.
