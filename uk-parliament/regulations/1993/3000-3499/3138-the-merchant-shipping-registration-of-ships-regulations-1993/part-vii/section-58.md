# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 58

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Registration of mortgage[^c-cd416460b0d71ba95044f381a144c8a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a mortgage executed in accordance with regulation 57 (Form of mortgage) is produced to the Registrar for registration, he shall:—

- (a) register the mortgage, and
- (b) where an original is provided,[^c-1f489b314ca4931ac7af633bb0f3da1c] endorse on it the date and time it was registered.
