# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Discharge of mortgages[^key-81601e20eb3ee53930e796beb8c20a2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a registered mortgage has been discharged, the Registrar shall, on production of the mortgage deed and with such evidence of the discharge as satisfies him that the mortgage has been discharged, record in the Register that the mortgage has been discharged.

(2) If for good reason the registered mortgage cannot be produced to the Registrar, he may, on being satisfied that the mortgage has been properly discharged, record in the Register that the mortgage has been discharged.
