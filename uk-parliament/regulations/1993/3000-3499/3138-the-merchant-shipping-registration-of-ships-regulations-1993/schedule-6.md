## SCHEDULE 6 — Approved Countries[^c-814bac8d26b9d0470efb8608b59df2a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 7 and 87D

The approved countries are—

- (a) Argentina
- (b) Aruba
- (c) Bahrain
- (d) Brazil
- (e) the Canary Islands
- (f) China
- (g) the Faroe Islands
- (h) Haiti
- (i) Israel
- (j) Japan
- (k) Liberia
- (l) Madeira
- (m) the Marshall Islands
- (n) Monaco
- (o) Panama
- (p) South Korea
- (q) Switzerland
- (r) Suriname
- (s) the United Arab Emirates, and
- (t) the United States of America.
