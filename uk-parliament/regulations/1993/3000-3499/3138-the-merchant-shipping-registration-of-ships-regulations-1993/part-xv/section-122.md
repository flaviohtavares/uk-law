# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 122

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**122.**[^key-82ba4ad76d42a670ba40d955d425734f] Any reference in any Act other than the Act or in any other instrument made under any such other Act to the port of registration of the ship or the port to which the ship belongs shall be construed as a reference to the port of choice required to be marked by regulations 31, 53, 72 or 79.
