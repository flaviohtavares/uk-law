# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 96

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Period of registration[^key-193110827958b6c633d23d8663d245a7][^key-8f9a8913dc556fe0f7774e0b1461708b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The registration of a ship under this Part shall, unless terminated under these Regulations, be valid for a period of 5 years beginning with the date of registration specified in the certificate of registry and shall expire at the end of that period unless it is renewed under regulation 97 (Renewal).
