# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 97

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Renewal[^key-da7b8b74493881b09919ae80979254a6][^key-57fcc8ac482b5316ebb3645bcaefaa9f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Application for renewal of registration may be made during the last 3 calendar months of the current registration period.

(2) Application for renewal shall be in writing and be accompanied by a declaration as required by regulation 92(e) (Applications).
