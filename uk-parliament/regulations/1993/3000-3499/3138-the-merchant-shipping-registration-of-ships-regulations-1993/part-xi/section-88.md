# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 88

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Qualification and entitlement to be registered as a small ship on Part III of the Register[^key-130e0ab2cc369f6038a0128babc28d71][^key-ad8562749a4e061214fd114d4da3f1c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

To be eligible to be registered on Part III of the Register a ship must be a small ship other than:—

- (a) a fishing vessel, or
- (b) a submersible vessel.
