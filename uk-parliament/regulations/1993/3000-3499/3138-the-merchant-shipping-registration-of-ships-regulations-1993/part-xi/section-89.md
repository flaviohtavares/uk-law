# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 89

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Persons qualified to be the owners of a small ship to be registered on Part III of the Register[^key-5391689db0acbb455329ccc928f19b8e][^key-ee7548ea841f1e9adf4e96d533c53d2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following persons are entitled to be the owners of a small ship to be registered on Part III of the Register:—

- (a)[^key-f4a25715ecaf1fae27e7e0035ddaeb05]
  - (i) British citizens; or
  - (ii) non-United Kingdom nationals exercising their right of freedom of movement of workers or right of establishment;
- (b) British Dependent Territories citizens;
- (c) British Overseas Citizens;
- (d) persons who under the British Nationality Act 1981 are British subjects;
- (e) persons who under the Hong Kong (British Nationality) Order 1986 are British Nationals (Overseas); [^key-3c999ffb1467f8997468429c57260392]...
- (f) Commonwealth citizens not falling within those paragraphs ; and[^key-d4b46e9496a59b3bb6927bb2eee94c96]
- (g) non-United Kingdom nationals who are settled in the United Kingdom
