# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 91

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Disapplication of Schedule I to the Act in respect of small ships[^c-c49cfc2c4f1d7c3d31fb92759091e15a][^c-cefb08d536b1b6b0ef9780259c123e0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule I to the Act relating to the private law provisions (Transfers by bill of sale and the registration of mortgages) shall not apply to small ships.
