# The Merchant Shipping (Registration of Ships) Regulations 1993, section 94

> Text as at 2026-04-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Certificate of registry[^key-ad63d3a5bdb7e10265ad77ea6bd76f2e][^key-e403a393407c3f579b462c0480c15f5d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On registration the Registrar shall issue a certificate which shall contain the details recorded in the Register in accordance with regulation 93 (Details to be registered and refusal) save for the address of any owner.
