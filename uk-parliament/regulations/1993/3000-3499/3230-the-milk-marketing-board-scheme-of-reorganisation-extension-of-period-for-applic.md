# The Milk Marketing Board Scheme of Reorganisation (Extension of Period for Application) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3230

AGRICULTURE

The Milk Marketing Board Scheme of Reorganisation (Extension of Period for Application) Order 1993

Made

22nd December 1993

Coming into force

31st December 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(7) of the Agriculture Act 1993[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Milk Marketing Board Scheme of Reorganisation (Extension of Period for Application) Order 1993, shall apply in England and Wales and shall come into force on 31st December 1993.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Agriculture Act 1993;
- “the Milk Marketing Board” means the board administering the Milk Marketing Scheme 1933[^f00002], being a scheme having effect under the Agricultural Marketing Act 1958[^f00003] for the marketing of milk.

## 3. Extension of period for making application under section 2 of the Act

The period for the making of an application under section 2 of the Act by the Milk Marketing Board is extended to 31st March 1994.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 21st December 1993.

LS

Gillian Shephard

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

22nd December 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Milk Marketing Board (“the Board”) administers the Milk Marketing Scheme 1933 (“the 1933 Scheme”) which applies to England and Wales. The Agriculture Act 1993 makes provision for the revocation of the 1933 Scheme and empowers the Board to apply jointly to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State for Wales for approval of a scheme for the reorganisation of the arrangementsrelating to the marketing of milk in England and Wales. This Order extends the last day on which an application may be made from 31st December 1993 to 31st March 1994.
