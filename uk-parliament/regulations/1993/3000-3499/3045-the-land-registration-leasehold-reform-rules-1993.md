# The Land Registration (Leasehold Reform) Rules 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3045

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration (Leasehold Reform) Rules 1993

Made

6th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

7th December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

The Lord Chancellor, with the advice and assistance of the Rule Committee appointed in pursuance of section 144 of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section and sections 34(10) and 57(11) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993[^f00002], hereby makes the following rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These rules may be cited as the Land Registration (Leasehold Reform) Rules 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

## 2. Statement to be inserted in a conveyance or transfer

The statement required by section 34(10) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 to be inserted into any conveyance executed for the purposes of Chapter I of Part I of that Act shall be in the following form:

> - This conveyance \[or transfer\] is executed for the purposes of Chapter I of Part I of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993.

## 3. Statement to be inserted in a lease

The statement required by section 57(11) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 to be inserted into any new lease granted under section 56 of that Act shall be in the following form:

> - This lease is granted under section 56 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 6th December 1993

(This note does not form part of the Rules)

These Rules have been made pursuant to sections 34(10) and 57(11) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 and prescribe the forms of the statements required by those sections.
