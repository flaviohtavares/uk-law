# The Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages and Divorces (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3153 (S.302)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages and Divorces (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1993

Made

14th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

16th December 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1994

The Registrar General, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 28A(4), 37(2) and (3), 38(2) and (3), 40(1), 43(8), 47, 54(1) and 56 of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the approval of the Secretary of State, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages and Divorces (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations–

- “the Act” means the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965;
- “general search” means a search, over any period of years for any number of entries, made by any person with the permission of the Registrar General or the district registrar;
- “particular search” means a search for a specified entry caused to be made by the Registrar General or made by a district registrar.

## 3. Fees payable to Registrar General

Subject to regulation 5, the fees payable under the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations for the services described in column 2 of that Schedule shall be the amounts specified in column 4 thereof (the amounts previously prescribed[^f00002] are specified in column 3 of that Schedule).

## 4. Fees payable to district registrars

Subject to regulation 5, the fees payable under the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to these Regulations for the services described in column 2 of that Schedule shall be the amounts specified in column 4 thereof (the amounts previously prescribed[^f00003] are specified in column 3 of that Schedule).

## 5. Remission of fees

The Registrar General may remit any fee or part of a fee where–

- (a) the payment of the fee is likely to cause hardship to the person by whom it is payable; or
- (b) the service in respect of which the fee is payable is performed for research purposes; or
- (c) an extract has been requested by the father or mother of a still–born child within one month of the date of registration of the still–birth, and the Registrar General consents in terms of section 37(3) of the Act to the extract being issued from the register of still–births.

## 6. Revocation

The Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages and Divorces (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe, with effect from 1st April 1994, the fees payable to the Registrar General and district registrars for services provided under the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965.

The fees for the services listed in Schedule 1 are increased except items 2(b), 9(b) and 10(b), which are decreased, and items 10(a), 12 and 13, which remain the same.

The fees for the services listed in Schedule 2 are increased except item 11 which remains the same.

Regulation 5(iii) gives the Registrar General power to remit the fee in circumstances where the extract is from the register of still-births and has been requested by the father or mother of the still-born child.

A new discount fee is introduced where payment is made in advance for the service listed in Schedule 1, item 11(a).

The Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages and Divorces (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1992 are revoked.
