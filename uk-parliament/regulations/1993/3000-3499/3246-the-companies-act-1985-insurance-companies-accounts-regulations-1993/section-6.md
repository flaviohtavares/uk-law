# The Companies Act 1985 (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations 1993, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Exempted companies

(1) A company to which paragraph (2) below applies may, with respect to any financial year, prepare such annual accounts as it would have been required to prepare had the modifications to the 1985 Act effected by these Regulations not been made.

(2) This paragraph applies to—

- (a) any company which is excluded from the scope of Council Directive [73/239/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0239)[^f00006] by Article 3 of that Directive, and
- (b) any company referred to in Article 2(2) or (3) or 3 of Council Directive [79/267/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0267)[^f00007].

(3) The modifications effected by regulations 2 to 5 above shall, where a company prepares accounts under paragraph (1) of this Regulation, be treated (as regards that company) as not having been made.

**6.** Regulation 7 sets out a transitional provision whereby a company may prepare accounts in accordance with the unamended Part VII, as that Part applies to insurance companies and groups, rather than under the amended provisions for a financial year commencing before 23rd December 1994.
