# The Companies Act 1985 (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations 1993, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Form and content of accounts

For Schedule 9A to the 1985 Act[^f00004] there shall be substituted Schedule 1 to these Regulations (form and content of accounts of insurance companies and groups).

**4.** Regulation 5 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Regulations make minor and consequential amendments to the 1985 Act and to the [Companies Act 1989 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 1990 (S. I. 1990/355)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/355).

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: Parts I and II of Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act were formed into a new Schedule numbered 9A by regulation 5(1) of the Bank Accounts Regulations.
