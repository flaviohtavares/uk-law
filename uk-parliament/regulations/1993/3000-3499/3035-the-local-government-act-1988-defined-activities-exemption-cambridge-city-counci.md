# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cambridge City Council) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3035

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cambridge City Council) Order 1993

Made

2nd December 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1993

Coming into force

31st December 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cambridge City Council) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 31st December 1993.

**2.** The management of sports and leisure facilities by the Cambridge City Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is undertaken at the Parkside Pool, Mill Road, Cambridge, before 1st January 1995.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Tony Baldry

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

2nd December 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the management of sports and leisure facilities by the Cambridge City Council so long as it is carried out at the Parkside Pool, Mill Road, Cambridge, before 1st January 1995.
