# The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3066

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

7th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1993

Coming into force

2nd January 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 2nd January 1994.

**2.** In regulation 3 of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00003] for “£254” there shall be substituted “£240” and for “£103”there shall be substituted “£97.30”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Key

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

2nd December 1993

We consent to the making of these Regulations

Tim Wood

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th December 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Approval of Fitters and Workshops) (Fees) Regulations 1986 to reduce the fees for the approval of fitters or workshops for the installation or repair of recording equipment (Tachographs).These fees were last increased in April 1992.

The fee for the issue of an approval is reduced from £254 to £240 and for the renewal of an approval from £103 to £97.30.
