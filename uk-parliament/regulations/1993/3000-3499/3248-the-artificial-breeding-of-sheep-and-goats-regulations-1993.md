# The Artificial Breeding of Sheep and Goats Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3248

AGRICULTURE LIVESTOCK INDUSTRIES

The Artificial Breeding of Sheep and Goats Regulations 1993

Made

24th December 1993

Laid before Parliament

30th December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in relation to England, and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 10(1) and (2) of the Animal Health and Welfare Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Artificial Breeding of Sheep and Goats Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

## 2. Approval of centres and teams

(1) The appropriate Minister, upon being satisfied that a semen collection centre complies with the provisions of Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065)[^f00002] laying down animal health requirements governing trade in and imports into the Community of animals, semen, ova and embryos not subject to animal health requirements laid down in specific Community rules referred to in Annex A(1) to Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) (in particular Chapters I, IIB and C and III of Annex D) (so far as it relates to ovine and caprine semen), shall, for the purposes of export of ovine and caprine semen to another member State, approve the semen collection centre.

(2) The appropriate Minister, upon being satisfied that a collection team is capable of complying with the provisions of Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065) in relation to the collection of ova and embryos of sheep and goats may approve that team for the purposes of these Regulations.

(3) Any approval granted under this Regulation shall be in writing and may be made subject to conditions and may be modified, suspended or revoked by notice in writing at any time.

## 3. Intra Community trade

(1) No person shall collect, process, store or transport for the purpose of export to another member State any ovine or caprine semen, ovum or embryo unless it meets the conditions laid down in Article 11 of Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065).

(2) No person shall use semen for the insemination of any sheep or goat for the purpose of production of ova or embryos for export to another member State unless—

- (a) the sheep or goat meets the conditions laid down in Chapter IV of Annex D to Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065), and
- (b) the semen meets the conditions laid down in Article 11(2) of that directive.

(3) No person shall collect any ovum or embryo from any sheep or goat for the purpose of export to another member State unless he is a member of a team approved under Regulation 2(2) above.

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

These Regulations implement Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065) as regards the production for intra Community trade of the semen, ova and embryos of sheep and goats and providing for the approval of semen collection centres and embryo collection teams.

Contravention of the Regulations is an offence under section 10(6) of the Animal Health and Welfare Act 1984.

A Compliance Cost Assessment has been prepared and deposited in the library of the House.
