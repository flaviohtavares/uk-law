# The Vocational Training (Tax Relief) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 3118

INCOME TAX

The Vocational Training (Tax Relief) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1993

Made

10th December 1993 Laid before the House of Commons10th December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 32(3) of the Finance Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Vocational Training (Tax Relief) (Amendment No.2) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

**2.** In the Vocational Training (Tax Relief) Regulations 1992[^f00002], in regulation 4(4), after sub-paragraph (c) there shall be inserted—

> - (ca) at the time the training payment was made, he had attained school-leaving age and, if under the age of nineteen, was not a person who was being provided with full-time education at a school,
> - (cb) he has undertaken, or is undertaking, the course neither wholly nor mainly for recreational purposes or as a leisure activity,

.

L.J.H. Beighton

C.W. Corlett

Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue

10th December 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

These Regulations, which come into force on 1st January 1994, amend the Vocational Training (Tax Relief) Regulations 1992 (S.I.[1992/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/746)) by adding two additional requirements to the certificate to be given by the individual making the training payment.
