# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 322 (S.30)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1993

Approved by the House of Commons

Made

2nd February 1993

Laid before the House of Commons

4th February 1993

Coming into force

18th February 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be appropriate in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of the said Schedule, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

## 2. Revenue support grants for 1990-91

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1990-91 are specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91 shall be the amount redetermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 3. Revenue support grants for 1991-92

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1991-92 are specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order in respect of the financial year 1991-92 shall be the amount redetermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 4. Revocations

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1991 (which determined the amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of the financial year 1991-92)[^f00002] and article 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1992 (which previously redetermined the amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of the financial year 1990-91)[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st February 1993

This Order redetermines for the third time the amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in Schedule 1 to the Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91, and redetermines for the first time the amount of such grant payable to each local authority specified in Schedule 2 in respect of the financial year 1991-92.

As a consequence, the Order revokes the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1991 (which determined the original amount of revenue support grant payable in respect of 1991-92) and certain provisions in the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1992 (which previously redetermined the amount of such grant payable in respect of 1990-91).
