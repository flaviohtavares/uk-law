# The Council Tax (Valuation of Dwellings) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 354 (S.38)

COUNCIL TAX, SCOTLAND

The Council Tax (Valuation of Dwellings) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993

Made

19th February 1993

Laid before Parliament

4th March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 86(2), 87(1) and (3), 113(1) and 116(1) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Council Tax (Valuation of Dwellings) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

(1) The Council Tax (Valuation of Dwellings) (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this regulation.

(2) In regulation 2(1), after “1992” there shall be inserted the words “and valuations carried out in connection with proposals for the alteration of a valuation list”.

(3) The words “or, in the case of a valuation carried out in connection with a proposal for the alteration of a valuation list, as at the date from which that alteration would have effect” shall be inserted after the word “made” in each of the following sub-paragraphs:—

- (a) sub-paragraph (c) of regulation 2(2);
- (b) sub-paragraph (a) of regulation 3(1);
- (c) sub-paragraph (b) of regulation 3(1); and
- (d) sub-paragraph (c) of regulation 3(1).

Allan Stewart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th February 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Council Tax (Valuation of Dwellings) (Scotland) Regulations 1992 (“the 1992 Regulations”) prescribed the assumptions and principles which were to be applied in valuing properties for the purposes of drawing up council tax valuation lists in Scotland. Such lists come into force on 1st April 1993. These Regulations amend the 1992 Regulations so as to make provision for the assumptions and principles to be applied where a property is valued after 1st April 1993 in connection with a proposal for the alteration of a valuation list.
