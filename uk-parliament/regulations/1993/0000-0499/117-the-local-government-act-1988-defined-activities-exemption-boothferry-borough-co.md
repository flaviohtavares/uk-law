# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Boothferry Borough Council) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 117

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Boothferry Borough Council) Order 1993

Made

25th January 1993

Laid before Parliament

29th January 1993

Coming into force

19th February 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Boothferry Borough Council) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 19th February 1993.

## 2. Exemption of maintenance of ground

Maintenance of ground by the Boothferry Borough Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it consists of grass-cutting work and is undertaken before 1st April 1994.

Department of the Environment

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

25th January 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the maintenance of ground by the Boothferry Borough Council so long as it consists of grass-cutting work and is carried out before 1st April 1994.
