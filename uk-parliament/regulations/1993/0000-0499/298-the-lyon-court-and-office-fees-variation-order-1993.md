# The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 298 (S.28)

ARMORIAL BEARINGS, ENSIGNS AND FLAGS

The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1993

Made

9th February 1993

Laid before Parliament

26th February 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of, as read with paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

## 2. Variation of fees

Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with article 3 of, and the Schedule to, this Order.

**3.** In relation to those matters in Schedule B which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, the fees payable shall be varied by substituting the fees specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order for the fees specified in column 3 of that Schedule (being the fees payable immediately prior to the coming into force of this Order).

**4.** The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

9th February 1993

This Order varies the fees for those matters listed in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867 which are specified in the Schedule to the Order. The fees were last varied in 1992. These fees are exclusive of stamp duty when stamp duty is exigible.
