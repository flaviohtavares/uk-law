# The St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management (Variation) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 198

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management (Variation) Order 1993

Made

5th February 1993

Laid before Parliament

12th February 1993

Coming into force

5th March 1993

The Secretary of State for Health in exercise of powers conferred by sub-paragraph (5) of paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, after consultation in accordance with that sub-paragraph with the Catholic Child Welfare Society, the voluntary organisation by which the St Vincent’s assisted community home is provided, and with the Humberside County Council, the local authority specified in the St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management Order 1983[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management (Variation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 5th March 1993.

## 2. Variation of Order

The St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management Order 1983 shall be varied as follows:—

- (a) in article 2(1) (interpretation) for the definition of “the home” there shall be substituted ““the home” means the assisted community home situated at 82 Marlborough Avenue, Kingston-upon-Hull and known as St Vincent’s Home”;
- (b) in article 3(1) (purpose and nature of home) for the number “18” there shall be substituted the number “6”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health,

Tim Yeo

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

5th February 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Instrument of Management for the assisted community home known as St Vincent’s Home. It redefines what constitutes the home excluding one of the sites at which the home was situated and consequentially reduces the number of children for which the home is suitable from 18 to 6.

Copies of the St Vincent’s Home Instrument of Management Order 1983 may be obtained free of charge from Branch CS2C, Department of Health, Wellington House, 133—155 Waterloo Road, SE1 8UG or St Vincent’s Home, 82 Marlborough Avenue, Kingston-upon-Hull, HU5 5JT.
