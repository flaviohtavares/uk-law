# The Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 121 (S.9)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) RevocationRegulations 1993

Made

22nd January 1993

Laid before Parliament

26th January 1993

Coming into force

16th February 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 16th February 1993.

## 2. Revocation

The Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) Regulations 1990[^f00002] and the Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1991[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

22nd January 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Testing in Primary Schools (Scotland) Regulations 1990 as amended. They thus end the requirement under those Regulations for education authorities to test at the Primary 4 and Primary 7 stages the level of educational attainment in language and mathematics of pupils attending primary schools under their management.
