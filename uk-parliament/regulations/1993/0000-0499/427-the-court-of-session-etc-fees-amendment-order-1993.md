# The Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 427 (S.47)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

The Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment Order 1993

Made

2nd March 1993

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1993

Coming into force

1st April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

**2.** For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Court of Session etc. Fees Order 1984([^f00002]) there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment Order 1992([^f00003]) and the Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment (No. 2) Order 1992([^f00004]) are hereby revoked.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th February 1993

We concur,

T.J.R. Wood

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

2nd March 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from 1st April 1993 most of the fees payable to the Principal Clerk of Session and Justiciary, or any officer acting for him, in relation to proceedings in the Court of Session and the specialised courts. It also introduces a new fee at items B13 and C12 payable by each party on a diet of proof or jury trial being fixed, and extends the scope of the existing fee at item C3A (petition to be admitted as a Solicitor) for additional applicants.

There are omitted from this Order fees payable in respect of matters relating to the Accountant in Bankruptcy. These will be dealt with separately.
