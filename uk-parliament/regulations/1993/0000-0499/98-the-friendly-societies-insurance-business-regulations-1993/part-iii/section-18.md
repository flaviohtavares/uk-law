# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) Regulations 1993, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Exclusions from regulations 13 and 17

Nothing in regulation 13 or 17 shall apply to insurance business carried on outside the United Kingdom.
