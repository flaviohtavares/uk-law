# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulationa 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Thsi Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S. I. 1992/3280](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3280) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 152

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulationa 1993

Made

29th January 1993

Laid before Parliament

1st February 1993

Coming into force

1st February 1993

The Secretary of State and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 122(4) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st Febraury 1993.

## 2. Keeping of the Register

In regulation 18 of the Genertically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 1992[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (2), for the words “paragraph (3)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraphs (30 and (3A)”;
- (b) after paragraph (3) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

  > (3A) The information prescribed in regulation 17(3)(h) shall be placed on the register within fourteen days of the receipt by the Secretary of State of the committee’s advice.

  .

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct a defect in regulation 18 of the [Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 1992 (S. I. 1992/3280)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3280). They provide that the advice of the committee appointed under section 124 of the Environmental Protection Act [1990 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43) as to applications for consents to release or market genetically modified organisms must be placed on the public register of information kept under section 122 of that Act within fourteen days of it being received by the Secretary of State.
