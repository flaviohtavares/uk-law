# The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 158

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1993

Made

31st January 1993

Laid before Parliament

1st February 1993

Coming into force

2nd February 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 96(10) of the Transport Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other enabling powers, after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 101(6) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 2nd February 1993.

**2.** The Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) Regulations 1993[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister for Public Transport,

Department of Transport

31st January 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Drivers' Hours (Passenger and Goods Vehicles) (Exemption) Regulations 1993.
