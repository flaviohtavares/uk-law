# The Superannuation (Children’s Pensions) (Earnings Limit) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 220

PENSIONS

The Superannuation (Children’s Pensions) (Earnings Limit) Order 1993

Made

8th February 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th February 1993

Coming into force

12th April 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(5) of the Judicial Pensions Act 1981([^f00001]) and now vested in them([^f00002]), and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf,hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Children’s Pensions) (Earnings Limit) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 12th April 1993.

## 2. Increase in children’s limit

The sum mentioned in section 21(1)(c)(ii) of the Judicial Pensions Act 1981 (which excludes from the definition of “period of childhood and full-time education” a person over 16 who is undergoing training if the emoluments payable by his employer are more than the said sum) is hereby increased from £1,614 to £1,672.

## 3. Revocation of previous order

The Superannuation (Children’s Pensions) (Earnings Limit) Order 1992([^f00003]) is hereby revoked.

Tim Wood

Gregory Knight

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

8th February 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

Subject to certain conditions section 21 of the Judicial Pensions Act 1981 permits the payment of a children’s pension under that Act to continue after the child reaches the age of 16 if the child is undergoing training for a trade, profession or vocation. One of the conditions is that the emoluments received during training, excluding any emoluments receivable or payable by way of return of any premium paid in respect of the training, do not exceed £1,614 a year. This Order revokes the Superannuation (Children’s Pensions) (Earnings Limit) Order 1992 and increases the limit on such emoluments from £1,614 to £1,672 a year.
