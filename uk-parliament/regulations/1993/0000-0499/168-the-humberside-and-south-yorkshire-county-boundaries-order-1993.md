# The Humberside and South Yorkshire (County Boundaries) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order which gives effect to proposals submitted to him under sections 47, 48(1) and 51(1) of that Act, more than six weeks before the making of this Order, by the Local Government Boundary Commission for England:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Humberside and South Yorkshire (County Boundaries) Order 1993.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st December 1993 for any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976[^f00002].

(3) For all other purposes, this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “electoral division” means an electoral division of the county of Humberside[^f00003];
- “Humberside” means the county of Humberside, and “Boothferry” means the district of Boothferry in that county;
- “the map” means one or more of the 4 maps prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Maps of the Humberside and South Yorkshire (County Boundaries) Order 1993”, duly numbered for reference, and deposited in accord ance with regulation 5 of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;
- “South Yorkshire” means the metropolitan county of South Yorkshire, and “Doncaster” means the metropolitan borough of Doncaster in that county; and
- “ward” means a ward of the metropolitan borough or district to which an area is transferred[^f00004], except in article 5, where it means a ward of Doncaster.

## 3. Transfers from Humberside (Boothferry) to South Yorkshire (Doncaster)

Each area which is marked as shown in column 1 of the following Table on the map indicated in column 2 shall be transferred from Humberside, from Boothferry, and from the parish named in column 3 to–

- (a) South Yorkshire;
- (b) Doncaster;
- (c) the parish and ward mentioned in column 4; and to
- (d) the Doncaster petty sessional division and the South Yorkshire East coroner’s district.

## 5. Consequential change to parish and district ward boundaries within Doncaster

In consequence of the boundary changes effected by articles 3 and 4, the following alteration shall be made to parish and metropolitan borough ward boundaries within Doncaster–

The part of the parish of Hatfield marked K on map 1 shall be transferred to the parish of Blaxton, and to the South East ward.

## 6. Town and country planning

(1) In this article, “provision” means a provision of a development plan (as defined in section 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00005]).

(2) Any provision applying to an area transferred by this Order shall continue to apply to that area, notwithstanding the transfer, until replaced by a provision which so applies, and which is expressed to supersede the preserved provision. by authority of the Secretary of State
