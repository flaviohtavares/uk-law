# The Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 154

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

Made

29th January 1993

Laid before Parliament

5th February 1993

Coming into force

26th February 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of establishments and facilities formerly managed by Chester Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 26th February 1993.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means Chester Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 29th October 1992, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 26th February 1993 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

29th January 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Mersey Regional Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust on 26th February 1993 trust property formerly held by Chester Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, 4N34A Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS1 7UH.
