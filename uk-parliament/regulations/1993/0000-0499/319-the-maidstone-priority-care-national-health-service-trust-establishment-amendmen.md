# The Maidstone Priority Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 319

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Maidstone Priority Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

Made

22nd February 1993

Coming into force

1st March 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Maidstone Priority Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st March 1993.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In article 4 of the Maidstone Priority Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00003] for the words “4 non-executive” there shall be substituted “5 non-executive” and for the words “4 executive” there shall be substituted “5 executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary under Secretary of State

Department of Health

22nd February 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust, established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of executive and non-executive directors from 4 to 5.
