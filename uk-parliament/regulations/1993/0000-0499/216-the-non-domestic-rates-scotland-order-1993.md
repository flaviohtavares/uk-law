# The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 216 (S.14)

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1993

Made

3rd February 1993

Laid before Parliament

15th February 1993

Coming into force

8th March 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7A(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 8th March 1993.

## 2. Non-domestic rates for 1993-94

For the purposes of section 7A of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes in respect of the financial year 1993-94 for each local authority set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order the non-domestic rate, expressed as an amount of pence per pound, set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.
