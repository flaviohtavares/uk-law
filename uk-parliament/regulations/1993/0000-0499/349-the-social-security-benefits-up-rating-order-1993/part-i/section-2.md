# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the 1992 Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00003];
- “the Administration Act” means the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00004];
- “the Pensions Act” means the Social Security Pensions Act 1975[^f00005];
- “the Family Credit Regulations” means the Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00006];
- “the Disability Working Allowance Regulations” means the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991[^f00007];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00008];
- “the Housing Benefit Regulations” means the Housing Benefit (General) Regula tions 1987[^f00009]; and
- “the Council Tax Benefit Regulations” means the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992[^f00010].
