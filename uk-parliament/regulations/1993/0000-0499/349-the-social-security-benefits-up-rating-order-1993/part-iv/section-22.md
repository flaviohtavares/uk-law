# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1993, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Revocations

The following provisions are hereby revoked—

- (a) the whole of the Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 1991[^f00029]; and
- (b) the whole of the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1991[^f00030], in so far as not previously revoked.
