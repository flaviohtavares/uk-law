# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1993, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Transitional Provisions

Notwithstanding the increases of the sums payable in respect of family credit and disability working allowance made by articles 14 and 15 of this Order, where a period of entitlement to family credit or disability working allowance is running at 13th April 1993, the rate at which it is payable shall be the weekly rate in force for the period prior to the 13th April 1993 until the end of the period of entitlement.
