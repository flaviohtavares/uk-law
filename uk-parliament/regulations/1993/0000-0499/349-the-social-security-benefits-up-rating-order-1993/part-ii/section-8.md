# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1993, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Earnings Limits

In section 80(4) of the 1992 Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) for the sums £115, £15 and £115 there shall be substituted the sums £120, £16 and £120 respectively.
