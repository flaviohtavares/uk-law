# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1993, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Increase in rate of graduated retirement benefit and increments thereof

(1) In the National Insurance Act 1965[^f00017] the sum of 7.09 pence referred to in section 36(1) (graduated retirement benefit) shall be increased by 3.6 per cent; and from and including 12th April 1993 the reference in that provision to that sum shall have effect as a reference to 7.35 pence.

(2) The sums which are the increases of graduated retirement benefit under Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations 1978[^f00018] (increases for deferred retirement) shall be increased by 3.6 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
