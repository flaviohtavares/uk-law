# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 120 (S.8)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1993

Made

22nd January 1993

Laid before Parliament

1st February 1993

Coming into force

22nd February 1993

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1993 and shall come into force on 22nd February 1993.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to the provisions of the following sub-paragraphs, for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990[^f00003], substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

(2) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 as substituted by sub-paragraph (1) above shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

(3) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 having effect before the substitution under sub-paragraph (1) above shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, IPD

Edinburgh,

22nd January 1993

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 (S.I. [1990/381](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/381)) by increasing the fees payable to Sheriff Officers by about 4%. The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1992 (S.I. [1992/82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/82)) as amended by the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (Amendment) 1992 (S.I. [1992/773](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/773)).
