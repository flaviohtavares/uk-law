# The Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 353

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

Made

23rd February 1993

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 1993

Coming into force

23rd March 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her bysection 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of the establishments and facilities formerly managed by Chester Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 23rd March 1993.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means Chester Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 12th November 1992, which has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of Trust Property

On 23rd March 1993 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

23rd February 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Chester and Halton Community National Health Service Trust on 23rd March 1993 trust property formerly held by Chester Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, 4N34A Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE.
