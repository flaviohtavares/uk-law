# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Inspection of applications

The applicant shall make a copy of the application available for inspection at a place within the locality of the land to which the application relates during the period or periods allowed for making representations pursuant to regulations 6(1)(a)(iii), 7(2) and 8(2).
