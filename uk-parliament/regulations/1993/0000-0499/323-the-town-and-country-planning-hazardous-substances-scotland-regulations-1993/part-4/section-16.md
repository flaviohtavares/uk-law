# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Conditions on deemed consent

The conditions set out in Schedule 3 are the prescribed conditions forthe purposes of section 38(9)(b) of the 1986 Act[^f00021].

## Annotations

[^f00021]: Footnote: Section 38(9)(b) was amended by the Environmental Protection Act 1990, Schedule 13, paragraph 12(3)(b).
