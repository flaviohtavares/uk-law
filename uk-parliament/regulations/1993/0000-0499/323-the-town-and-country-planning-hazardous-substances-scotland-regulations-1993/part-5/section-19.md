# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices

(1) Section 85 (other than subsections (2B),(3),(5A) and (5B)) shall apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 1 of Schedule 4.

(2) The provisions of section 85 which apply to such appeals, as modified in accordance with paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.
