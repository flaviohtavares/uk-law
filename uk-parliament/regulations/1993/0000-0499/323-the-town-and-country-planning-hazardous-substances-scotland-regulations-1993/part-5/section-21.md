# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Effect of hazardous substances contravention notices, etc

(1) Sections 86 (other than subsections (4) and (5))[^f00025], 88[^f00026], 89[^f00027] and 89A (other than subsection (2))[^f00028] shall have effect in relation to hazarous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 2 of Scheule 4.

(2) The provisions of sections 86, 88, 89 and 89A which apply to such notices, as modified in accordance with paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00025]: Footnote: Section 86 was substituted by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991, section 40.

[^f00026]: Footnote: Section 88(1) was amended by the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (“the 1973 Act”), section 172(2) and by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991 (“the 1991 Act”), Schedule 19, Part IV; subsection (1A) was inserted by the Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982 (“the 1982 Act”), Schedule 2, paragraph 23(a); subsection (2) was amended by the 1973 Act, section 172(2) and by the 1991 Act, Schedule 13, paragraph 23; subsections (3)-(5) were substituted by the 1982 Act, Schedule 2, paragraph 23(b); subsection (6) was inserted by the 1991 Act, section 39.

[^f00027]: Footnote: Section 89(1), (3) and (4) was amended by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991, Schedule 13, paragraph 24; section 89(4) was also amended by virtue of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act [1975 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/21) section 289G (as inserted by the Criminal Justice Act [1982 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/48) section 54).
