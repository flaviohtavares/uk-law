# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Enforcement register

(1) Section 87A[^f00029] (register of enforcement and stop notices) shall have effect in relation to hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 3 of Schedule 4.

(2) The provisions of section 87A, as modified in accordance with paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00029]: Footnote: Section 87A was inserted by the Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, sections 44 and 69(2) and amended by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991, Schedule 13, paragraph 22.
