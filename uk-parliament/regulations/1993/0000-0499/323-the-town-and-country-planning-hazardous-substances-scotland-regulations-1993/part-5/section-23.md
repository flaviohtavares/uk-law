# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Validity

Section 231[^f00030] (as read with section 233(3))[^f00031] shall apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 4 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00030]: Footnote: Section 231 was amended as follows: by the Town and Country Planning (Minerals) Act [1981 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/36), Schedule 2, paragraph 8; by the Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act [1982 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/43), sections 47(c) and 69(2), Schedule 2, paragraph 35(a) and (b) and Schedule 4, Part I; by the 1986 Act, Schedule 6, Part IV, paragraph 4, Schedule 7, Part II, paragraph 2, and Schedule 11, paragraph 50; and by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991, Schedule 13, paragraph 33 and Schedule 12, paragraph 22.

[^f00031]: Footnote: Section 233(3) was amended by the 1986 Act, Schedule 12, Part IV.
