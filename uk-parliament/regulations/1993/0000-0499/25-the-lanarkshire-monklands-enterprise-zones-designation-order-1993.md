# The Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zones Designation Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 25 (S.4)

ENTERPRISE ZONES

The Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zones Designation Order 1993

Made

11th January 1993

Laid before Parliament

11th January 1993

Coming into force

1st February 1993

Whereas by a resolution dated 24th September 1992 Monklands District Council adopted the Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zone Scheme;

And whereas advertisements in accordance with the requirements of paragraph 3(4)(c), (7) and (8) of Schedule 32 to the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001] were published in the Edinburgh Gazette and the Herald relating to the said Scheme;

And whereas six weeks have expired since the date of the first publication of the said advertisements in the Edinburgh Gazette on 30th October 1992 and no application in relation to the said Scheme has been made under paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 32 to the said Act to the Court of Session:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 179 of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 32 to, the said Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zones Designation Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st February 1993.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the deposited map” means the map marked “map referred to in the Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zones Designation Order 1993”, prepared in duplicate and signed by an Assistant Secretary in the Scottish Office Industry Department, of which one duplicate has been deposited and is available for inspection at the principal offices of Monklands District Council at Municipal Buildings, Coatbridge and of which the other duplicate has been deposited and is available for inspection at the offices of the Secretary of State at New St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh;
- “the Scheme” means the Lanarkshire (Monklands) Enterprise Zone Scheme 1992 adopted by Monklands District Council by resolution dated 24th September 1992.

## 3. Designation of areas as enterprise zones

The areas to which the Scheme relates, being the sites named as (1) Calder Street, Coatbridge, (2) Dunalastair and (3) Shawhead, Coatbridge, and which are shown on the deposited map and marked accordingly are hereby designated as enterprise zones.

## 4. Effective date of designation

The designation of the enterprise zones referred to in article 3 shall have effect on 1st February 1993.

## 5. Period of enterprise zones

The period for which the areas referred to in article 3 are to remain enterprise zones is a period of 10 years from the date when the designation takes effect.

## 6. Definition of boundaries of enterprise zones

The boundaries of the areas referred to in article 3 are defined by the outside edge of the black delineating line in respect of each area shown coloured pink on the deposited map.

## 7. Enterprise zone authority

Monklands District Council is hereby designated as the enterprise zone authority for the enterprise zones designated in article 3.
