# The M66 Motorway (Manchester Outer Ring Road, Denton to Middleton Section) A663 Broadway All-Purpose Connecting Road Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 363

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The M66 Motorway (Manchester Outer Ring Road, Denton to Middleton Section) A663 Broadway All-Purpose Connecting Road Order 1993

Made

23rd February 1993

Coming into force

5th March 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** The new highway which the Secretary of State proposed to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when the Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** In this Order:

(1) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/1CNW316 marked “The M66 Motorway (Manchester Outer Ring Road, Denton to Middleton Section) A663 All-Purpose Connecting Road Order 1993” signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY, and

(2) “the new trunk road” means the highway mentioned in article 1 above.

**4.** This Order shall come into force on 5th March 1993 and may be cited as the M66 Motorway (Manchester Outer Ring Road, Denton to Middleton Section) A663 Broadway All-Purpose Connecting Road Order 1993.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J. P. Henry

Grade 3 Under Secretary

Department of Transport

23rd February 1993

## SCHEDULE — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

### 1. All-purpose road to connect the motorway with A663 Broadway

A route to connect the slip road from the southbound carriageway of the M66 Motorway with the A663 Broadway (as proposed to be improved by the Secretary of State pursuant to the M66 Motorway (Manchester Outer Ring Road, Denton to Middleton Section) and Connecting Roads (Side Roads) Order No. 3 199), the road along this route being given the reference number 1 on the order plan in the deposited plan.
