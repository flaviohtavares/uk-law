# The Combined Probation Areas (Powys) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 52

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Powys) Order 1993

Made

10th January 1993

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Powys) Order 1993 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Powys Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for “Llandrindod Wells” in column 1 the words “Radnorshire and North Brecknock”.

**3.** The two members of the Powys Probation Committee appointed before 1st January 1993 by the justices for the petty sessions area of Llandrindod Wells shall from the date on which this Order comes into force be members of the Committee as if they had been appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of Radnorshire and North Brecknock and shall hold office until 31st March 1993.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

10th January 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the renaming of the petty sessions area of Llandrindod Wells to that of Radnorshire and North Brecknock effected by the Petty Sessional Division (Powys) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/2729](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2729)) which came into force on 1st January 1993.
