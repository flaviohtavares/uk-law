# The Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Bromley) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 50

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Bromley) Order 1993

Made

13th January 1993

Laid before Parliament

20th January 1993

Coming into force

10th February 1993

The Secretary of State, being satisfied that in all the circumstances it is not expedient to make adequate provision in the area of the London Borough of Bromley for the accommodation of gipsies residing in or resorting to that area, on the application of the council of the London Borough of Bromley, and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(1) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Bromley) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th February 1993.

**2.** The area of the London Borough of Bromley is designated as an area to which section 10 (prohibition of unauthorised camping in designated areas) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Department of the Environment

13th January 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the London Borough of Bromley as an area to which section 10 of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

It is an offence within a designated area for a gipsy to station a caravan for the purpose of residing for any period on any land within the boundaries of a highway, or on any other unoccupied land, or on any occupied land without the consent of the occupier.

Under section 11 of the Act (substituted by section 174 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980), unlawfully stationed caravans and their occupants may be removed by order of a magistrates' court.850
