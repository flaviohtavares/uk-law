# The Forth Ports Authority (Dissolution) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2916

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Forth Ports Authority (Dissolution) Order 1993

Made

29th November 1993

Whereas the Secretary of State for Transport is for the purposes of Part I of the Ports Act 1991[^f00001] the appropriate Minister in relation to the Forth Ports Authority;

And whereas the Secretary of State is satisfied as to the matters mentioned in paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of subsection (1) of section 7 of that Act:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, after consulting the Authority in pursuance of the said subsection (1), in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that subsection and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Forth Ports Authrity (Dissolution) Order 1993.

## 2. Dissolution of the Authority

The Forth Ports Authority shall be dissolved on 1st December 1993.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Caithness

Minister of State

Department of Transport

29th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the Forth Ports Authority on 1st December 1993. On that day any property, rights and liabilities to which the Authority are entitled or subject immediately before that day are by virtue of section 7 of the Ports Act 1991 transferred to and become property, rights and liabilities of the Authority’s successor company, Forth Ports PLC.
