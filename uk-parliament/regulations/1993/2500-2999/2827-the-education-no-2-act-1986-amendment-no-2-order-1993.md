# The Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2827

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1993

Made

17th November 1993

Laid before Parliament

19th November 1993

Coming into force

10th December 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the Education (No. 2) Act 1986[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th December 1993.

**2.** Paragraph 7 of Schedule 3 to the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 shall be amended by the substitution in sub–paragraph (c) of the word “and” for the word “or” after the words “to the committee”.

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

16th November 1993

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

17th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends paragraph 7 of Schedule 3 to the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 by substituting “and” for “or”. Schedule 3 is concerned with appeals against school exclusions on grounds of discipline and the effect of the amendment is to give governing bodies and local education authorities the right to make both written and oral representations to an appeal committee on an appeal by a pupil or parent.
