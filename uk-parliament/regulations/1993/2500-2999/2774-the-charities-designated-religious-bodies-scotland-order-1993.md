# The Charities (Designated Religious Bodies) (Scotland) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2774 (S.257)

CHARITIES

The Charities (Designated Religious Bodies) (Scotland) Order 1993

Made

5th November 1993

Laid before Parliament

16th November 1993

Coming into force

7th December 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(1) of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Charities (Designated Religious Bodies) (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 7th December 1993.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “the Act” means the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990; and
- “recognised body” has the meaning given by section 1 of the Act.

## 3. Designated Religious Bodies

The Secretary of State hereby designates for the purposes of section 3 of the Act the recognised bodies which are specified in the Schedule to this Order.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th November 1993

## SCHEDULE — Designated Religious Bodies

Article 3

- The Church of Scotland;
- The Free Church of Scotland;
- The Free Presbyterian Church of Scotland;
- The Roman Catholic Archdiocese of St Andrews and Edinburgh;
- The Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Glasgow;
- The Roman Catholic Diocese of Argyll and the Isles;
- The Roman Catholic Diocese of Dunkeld;
- The Roman Catholic Diocese of Galloway;
- The Roman Catholic Diocese of Paisley; and
- The Scottish Episcopal Church.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates for the purposes of section 3 of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 the recognised bodies (or Scottish charities) which appear to the Secretary of State to meet the criteria set out in that section (designated religious bodies).
