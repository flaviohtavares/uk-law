# The Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2857

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993

Made

22nd November 1993

Laid Before Parliament

30th November 1993

Coming into force

21st December 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that hehalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Wigan Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 21st December 1993.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Wigan Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 5th June 1993, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 21st December the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

22nd November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust on 21st December 1993 trust property formerly held by the Wigan Health Authority.A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
