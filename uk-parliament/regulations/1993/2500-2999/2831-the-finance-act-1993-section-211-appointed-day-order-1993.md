# The Finance Act 1993, section 211, (Appointed Day) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2831 (C.60)

NATIONAL DEBT

The Finance Act 1993, section 211, (Appointed Day) Order 1993

Made

18th November 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 211(9) of the Finance Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1993, section 211, (Appointed Day) Order 1993.

**2.** The day appointed for the coming into force of section 211 of the Finance Act 1993 is 9th December 1993.

Irvine Patnick

Andrew Mackay

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

18th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 9th December 1993 section 211 of the Finance Act 1993, which makes provision in relation to the acquisition and transfer by the National Debt Commissioners of securities issued under section 12 of the National Loans Act 1968 (power of Treasury to borrow).
