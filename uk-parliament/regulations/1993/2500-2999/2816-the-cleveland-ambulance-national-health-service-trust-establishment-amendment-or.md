# The Cleveland Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2816

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Cleveland Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

Made

16th November 1993

Coming into force

26th November 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 [^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Cleveland Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993 and shall come into force on 26th November 1993.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In article 4 of the Cleveland Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00003] for the words from “5 non-executive” to the end of the article there shall be substituted the words “, 4 non-executive directors and 4 executive directors”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

16th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Cleveland Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991 to alter the number of non-executive and executive directors to be appointed to the Trust from 5 to 4 in each case.
