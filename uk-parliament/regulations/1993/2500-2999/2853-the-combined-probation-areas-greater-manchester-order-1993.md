# The Combined Probation Areas (Greater Manchester) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2853

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Greater Manchester) Order 1993

Made

21st November 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Greater Manchester) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Greater Manchester Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by–

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Eccles and Salford;
- (b) inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “City of Salford”, and corresponding to this in column 3 “2”.

**3.** The four members of the Greater Manchester Probation Committee appointed before 1st January 1994 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Eccles and Salford shall decide by agreement, or in default of agreement by lot, which two of them shall continue in office as if appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of City of Salford[^f00004].

Home Office

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

21st November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Eccles and Salford into the new petty sessions area of City of Salford effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (City of Salford) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/2149](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2149)) which comes into force on 1st January 1994.
