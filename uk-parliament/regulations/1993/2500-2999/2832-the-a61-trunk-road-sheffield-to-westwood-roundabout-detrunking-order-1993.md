# The A61 Trunk Road (Sheffield to Westwood Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2832

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A61 Trunk Road (Sheffield to Westwood Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1993

Made

9th November 1993

Coming into force

10th December 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A61 Trunk Road (Sheffield to Westwood Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th December 1993.

**2.** In this Order—

- (i) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a princial road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State;
- (ii) “the trunk road” means the A61 Trunk Road.

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which this Order comes into force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J. P. Henry

Regional Director Yorkshire and Humberside Region,

Department of Transport

9th November 1993

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is situated between the former Sheffield County Borough boundary (marked A on the deposited plan) and the Westwood Roundabout (marked B on the deposited plan) a distance of approximately 6.59 kilometres and is shown by broad black dashes on the plan numbered HA10/YHNMD/10 marked “The A61 Trunk Road (Sheffield to Westwood Roundabout)(Detrunking) Order 1993”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.
