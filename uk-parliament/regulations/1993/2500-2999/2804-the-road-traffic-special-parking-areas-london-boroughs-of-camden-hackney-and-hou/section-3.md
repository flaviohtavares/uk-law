# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Camden, Hackney and Hounslow) Order 1993, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Designation of special parking areas

(1) The Secretary of State hereby designates each of the following areas as a special parking area:—

- (a) the London borough of Camden except the roads named or described in Part I of Schedule 1 and the areas described in Part II of that Schedule;
- (b) the London borough of Hackney except the roads named or described in Part I of Schedule 2 and the areas described in Part II of that Schedule;
- (c) the London borough of Hounslow except the roads named or described in Parts I and III of Schedule 3 and the areas described in Part II of that Schedule; and
- (d) that part of the London borough of Hounslow which consists of the roads named or described in Part III of Schedule 3.

(2) A reference in the following provisions of this Order to a special parking area is a reference to an area designated by this article.
