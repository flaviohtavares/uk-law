### PART II — AREAS IN THE LONDON BOROUGH OF CAMDEN EXCLUDED FROM THE SPECIAL PARKING AREA

The areas referred to in–

- (a) article 3(2) of the Barnet, Camden and Westminster (London Borough Boundaries) Order 1993[^f00003] (areas to be transferred from the London borough of Camden to the London borough of Barnet); and
- (b) articles 3(2) and 6 of the City and London Borough Boundaries Order 1993[^f00004] (areas to be transferred from the London borough of Camden to the City of London and to the London Borough of Islington).
