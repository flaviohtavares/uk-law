# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business No. 2) Regulations 1993, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Application: Part V

This Part of these Regulations applies with respect to the determination of the amount of liabilities of a friendly society for the purposes of–

- (a) section 48 of the 1992 Act;
- (b) any actuarial investigation to which section 46 or 47 of the 1992 Act applies; and
- (c) such other actuarial investigation as may be required by the Commission in the exercise of its powers under Part V of the 1992 Act.
