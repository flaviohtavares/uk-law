# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business No. 2) Regulations 1993, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Revocations

(1) The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) Regulations 1993[^f00020] are hereby revoked.

(2) The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987[^f00021] are hereby revoked.
