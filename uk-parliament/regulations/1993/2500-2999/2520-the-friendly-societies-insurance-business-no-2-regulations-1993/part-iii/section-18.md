# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business No. 2) Regulations 1993, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Exclusions from regulations 13 and 17

(1) Nothing in regulation 13 or 17 shall apply to long term or general business carried on outside the United Kingdom.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) above, the term “carried on” has the same meaning–

- (a) in relation to long term business, as it has for the purposes of Article 17(2) of the first life Directive; and
- (b) in relation to general business, as it has for the purposes of Article 15(2) of the first general insurance Directive.
