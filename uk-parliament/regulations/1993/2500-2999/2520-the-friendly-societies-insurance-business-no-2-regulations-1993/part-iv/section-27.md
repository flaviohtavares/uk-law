# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business No. 2) Regulations 1993, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Unit trusts

The value of any holding of units, or other beneficial interest, under an authorised unit trust scheme within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00019] shall be the price at which the managers under the unit trust scheme would purchase the holding of units or other beneficial interest if required to do so.
