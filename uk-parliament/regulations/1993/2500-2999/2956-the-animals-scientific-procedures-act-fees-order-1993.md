# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Fees) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2956

ANIMALS

The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Fees) Order 1993

Made

29th November 1993

Laid before Parliament

8th December 1993

Coming into force

1st January 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8 of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Fees) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st January 1994.

(2) This Order does not extend to Northern Ireland.

(3) In this Order “the Act” means the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986.

(4) The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Fees) Order 1992[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

**2.** The annual fee payable by the holder of a certificate issued under section 6 of the Act (scientific procedure establishments) in respect of any calendar year during the whole or part of which the certificate is in force shall consist of the fixed element specified in article 3 below and the additional element calculated in accordance with article 4 below.

**3.** The fixed element shall be £122 payable on each occasion that an annual fee falls due.

**4.** The additional element shall be £110 payable for each person who holds —

- (a) a personal licence which specifies the establishment to which the certificate relates as the place where the licence is primarily available; or
- (b) a licence which is treated as a personal licence under paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act and which names the establishment to which the certificate relates as the only or first-named place where the person is authorised to perform experiments,

at any time during the period to which the fee relates when the certificate is in force.

**5.** The annual fee payable by the holder of a certificate issued under section 7 of the Act (breeding and supplying establishments) in respect of any calendar year during the whole or part of which the certificate is in force shall be £553 unless the place specified in the certificate is also specified in a certificate issued under section 6 of the Act.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

29th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which does not extend to Northern Ireland, prescribes the fees payable by the holders of certificates under section 6 of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 (scientific procedure establishments) and under section 7 of the Act (breeding and supplying establishments).

For scientific procedure establishments the fee is made up of £122 plus £110 for each personal licensee whose place of primary availability is the establishment in question.This represents an increase of £2 in respect of establishments and of £2 in respect of personal licensees.For a breeding and supplying establishment which is not also a scientific procedure establishment the fee is increased by £8 to £553.

The Order also revokes the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Fees) Order 1992, which it replaces.
