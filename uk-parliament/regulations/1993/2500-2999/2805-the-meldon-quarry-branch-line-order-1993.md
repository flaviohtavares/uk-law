# The Meldon Quarry Branch Line Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2805

TRANSPORT

The Meldon Quarry Branch Line Order 1993

Made

11th November 1993

Laid before Parliament

19th November 1993

Coming into force

1st February 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4 of, and paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to, the Transport Act 1981[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Meldon Quarry Branch Line Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st February 1994.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order

- “the Acts” means the Okehampton Railway Act 1862[^f00002], the Okehampton Railway Act 1864[^f00003] and the Devon and Cornwall Railway Act 1868[^f00004];
- “the Company” means Euston Holdings Limited; and
- “the Designated Land” means the land in the County of Devon occupied by the line of railway (including Meldon Viaduct and the discontinued line of railway) from a point at Coleford Junction at 296 kilometres 31 metres from London Waterloo to a point 323 kilometres 580 metres from London Waterloo at the bridge to the south-west of Sourton Down known as bridge number 618.

## 3. Amendment to the Acts

In so far as the Designated Land is thereby affected, for the references in the Acts to the British Railways Board or their predecessors there shall be substituted references to the Company.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

11th November 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order has the effect of transferring from the British Railways Board to Euston Holdings Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Board, certain rights and liabilities conferred or imposed by the Okehampton Railway Acts of 1862 and 1864 and the Devon and Cornwall Railway Act 1868, so far as such rights and liabilities relate o the lands described in article 2 of the Order.
