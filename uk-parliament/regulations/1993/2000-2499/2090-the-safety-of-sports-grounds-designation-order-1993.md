# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2090

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1993

Made

18th August 1993

Laid before Parliament

24th August 1993

Coming into force

15th September 1993

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 15th September 1993.

**2.** The sports grounds at—

- (a) The Den, Bolina Road, London SE16,
- (b) Bescot Stadium, Bescot Crescent, Walsall, and
- (c) Adams Park, Hillbottom Road, Sands, High Wycombe

(being sports grounds each of which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators) are hereby designated as sports grounds requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

**3.** In the Schedule to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1979[^f00002], the entry in respect of Bristol Stadium, Eastville, Bristol shall be omitted.

**4.** In the Schedule to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Association Football Grounds) (Designation) Order 1985[^f00003], the entries in respect of—

- (a) The Den, Cold Blow Lane, London, and
- (b) Fellows Park, Walsall

shall be omitted.

Peter Brooke

Secretary of State for National Heritage

18th August 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order designates the sports grounds specified therein as sports grounds requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 as amended by Schedule 2 to the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987.

Articles 3 and 4 of this Order vary the orders referred to therein by omitting entries which are no longer sports grounds.
