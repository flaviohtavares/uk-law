# The Register of County Court Judgments (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2173 (L.21)

COUNTY COURTS

The Register of County Court Judgments (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1993

Made

17th August 1993

Laid before Parliament

15th September 1993

Coming into force

1st November 1993

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 73(2) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Register of County Court Judgments (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st November 1993.

(2) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, a regulation referred to by number means the regulation so numbered in the Register of County Court Judgments Regulations 1985[^f00002].

**2.** (1) Regulation 5 shall be amended by inserting, after paragraph (2A)[^f00003], the following new paragraph—

> (2B) Where in an action for the recovery of land an order is made for the payment of money (whether for costs, payments due under a mortgage, arrears of rent or otherwise) that order shall be exempt from registration until the judgment creditor takes steps to enforce it.

.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

17th August 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Register of County Court Judgment Regulations 1985 so as to exempt from registration until the judgment creditor takes steps to enforce payment any order for the payment of money which is made in proceedings to recover possession of land.
