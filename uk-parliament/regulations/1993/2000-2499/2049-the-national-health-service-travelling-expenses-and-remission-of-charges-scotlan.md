# The National Health Service (Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2049 (S.227)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service (Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1993

Made

13th August 1993

Laid before Parliament

24th August 1993

Coming into force

14th September 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 75A, 105 and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 14th September 1993.

(2) In these Regulations–

- “the principal Regulations” means the National Health Service (Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 1988([^f00002]).

## 2. Amendment of regulation 2 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 2(1) of the principal Regulations (Interpretation) the definition of “Secretary of State” shall be deleted.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th August 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make a further amendment of the National Health Service (Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 1988 in that the definition of “Secretary of State” as being the Secretary of State for Health is removed.
