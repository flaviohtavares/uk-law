# The Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Modifications to the Right to Buy

(1) Subject to regulation 3 below, Part V of the Housing Act 1985 (the right to buy) as it applies in the circumstances described in section 171A(1) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00002] (cases in which right to buy is preserved) has effect subject to the exceptions, adaptations and other modifications specified in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

(2) Subject to regulation 3, Part V of the Housing Act 1985 as it applies by virtue of paragraph (1) is set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: Section 171A was inserted by section 8 of the Housing and Planning Act 1986.
