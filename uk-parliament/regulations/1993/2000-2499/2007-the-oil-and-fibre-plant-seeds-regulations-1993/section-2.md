# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds Regulations 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocation of previous regulations

The Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds Regulations 1985[^f00003], the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations 1987[^f00004], the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations 1989[^f00005] the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations 1991[^f00006] and the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations 1992[^f00007] are hereby revoked.
