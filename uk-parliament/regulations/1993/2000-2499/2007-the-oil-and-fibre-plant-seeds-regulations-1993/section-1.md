# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds Regulations 1993, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds Regulations 1993, shall apply to Great Britain and shall come into force on 1st September 1993.
