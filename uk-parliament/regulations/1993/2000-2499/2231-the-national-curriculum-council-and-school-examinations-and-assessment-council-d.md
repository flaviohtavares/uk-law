# The National Curriculum Council and School Examinations and Assessment Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Curriculum Council and School Examinations and Assessment Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1993

Made

10th September 1993

Laid before Parliament

10th September 1993

Coming into force

1st October 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 248(1) and (5) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Curriculum Council and School Examinations and Assessment Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1993, and shall come into force on 1st October 1993.

## 2. Designation of staff to be transferred from existing bodies

(1) Any employee of the National Curriculum Council who is named in Part I of the Schedule to this Order is designated as respects the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority.

(2) Any employee of the School Examinations and Assessment Council who is named in Part II of the Schedule to this Order is designated as respects the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority.

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order concerns certain staff of the National Curriculum Council and the School Examinations and Assessment Council established under the Education Reform Act 1988. These bodies are to be dissolved under section 246 of the Education Act 1993 in consequence of the establishment (under section 244 of that Act) of the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority. Under section 248 of the Education Act 1993, the Secretary of State may designate (either individually or by class or description) employees of the existing bodies for automatic transfer to the new Authority. Where an individual is so designated there is no termination of, or change in the terms of, his contract of employment. This Order lists those staff so designated.
