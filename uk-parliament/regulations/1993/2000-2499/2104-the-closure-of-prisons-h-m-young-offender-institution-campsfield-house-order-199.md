# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Campsfield House) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 2104

PRISONS

The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Campsfield House) Order 1993

Made

23rd August 1993

Laid before Parliament

2nd September 1993

Coming into force

1st October 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Campsfield House) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st October 1993.

**2.** The young offender institution known as H.M. Young Offender Institution Campsfield House and situate in the county of Oxfordshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

23rd August 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Young Offender Institution Campsfield House on 1st October 1993.
