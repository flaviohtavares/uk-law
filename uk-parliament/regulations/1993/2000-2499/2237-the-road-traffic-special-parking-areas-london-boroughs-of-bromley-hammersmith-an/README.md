NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 76(1) and 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the whole of the London boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham, excluding the roads referred to in Schedules 2, 3 and 4 respectively, as special parking areas for the purposes of Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991. It also makes consequential modifications to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 and to Part II of the 1991 Act and amends the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (The London Borough of Wandsworth) Order 1993.
