# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (London Borough of Wandsworth) (Amendment) Order 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002];
- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991;
- “centre line”, in relation to a road, means the imaginary line along the centre of the carriageway;
- “carriageway” has the same meaning as in the Highways Act 1980[^f00003];
- a reference in a Schedule to a number followed by the letter “m” is a reference to that number of metres; and
- a reference to a Schedule followed by a number is a reference to the Schedule to this Order bearing that number.
