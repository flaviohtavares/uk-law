# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (London Borough of Wandsworth) (Amendment) Order 1993, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — ROADS IN THE LONDON BOROUGH OF HAMMERSMITH AND FULHAMEXCLUDED FROM THE SPECIAL PARKING AREA

Article 4(1)(b)

**1.** A4 between the borough boundary with the London borough of Hounslow at Chiswick and the borough boundary with the Royal borough of Kensington and Chelsea at Earls Court, including its slip roads to Hammersmith Bridge Road and Butterwick.

**2.** A40 between the borough boundary with the London borough of Ealing at its junction with Old Oak Common Lane and the borough boundary with the Royal borough of Kensington and Chelsea at the junction with the M41, including the slip roads to and from Wood Lane and the M41.

**3.** A40(M) between its junction with the A40 Westway and the borough boundary with the Royal borough of Kensington and Chelsea.

**4.** M41.

**5.** Bentworth Road between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40.

**6.** Black Lion Lane between its junction with the A4 and a point 19m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**7.** Bloemfontein Road between its junction with the A40 and a point 20m south of the southern kerbline of the A40.

**8.** Bridge View

- (a) (east side) between its junction with the A4 and a point 18m south of the southern kerbline of the A4;
- (b) (west side) between its junction with the A4 and a point 10m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**9.** Church Lane between its junction with the A4 and a point 8m north of the northern kerbline of the A4.

**10.** Eyot Gardens between its junction with the A4 and a point 10m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**11.** Gliddon Road

- (a) between its junction with the A4 and a point 42m north of the northern kerbline of the A4;
- (b) between its junction with the A4 and a point 50m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**12.** Heathstan Road

- (a) (west arm) between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40;
- (b) (east arm) between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40.

**13.** Maurice Street between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40.

**14.** North End Road

- (a) (north arm) between its junction with the A4 and an imaginary straight line drawn between the common boundary between 153 and 155 North End Road on the east side and the end wall of 62 North End Road on the west side;
- (b) (south arm) between its junction with the A4 and a point 80m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**15.** Oil Mill Lane between its junction with the A4 and a point 12m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**16.** Old Oak Common Lane between its junction with the A40 and a point opposite the northern flank wall of 86 Old Oak Common Lane.

**17.** Old Oak Road between its junction with the A40 and a point adjacent to the boundary of 189 and 201 Old Oak Road.

**18.** Primula Street

- (a) (west arm) between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40;
- (b) (east arm) between its junction with the A40 and a point 10m north of the northern kerbline of the A40.

**19.** Rivercourt Road

- (a) between its junction with the A4 and a point 15m north of the northern kerbline of the A4;
- (b) between its junction with the A4 and a point 10m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.

**20.** Shortlands between its junction with the A4 and a point 20m north of the northern kerbline of the A4.

**21.** Verbena Gardens.

**22.** Weltje Road

- (a) between its junction with the A4 and a point 8m north of the northern kerbline to the southern boundary wall of 35 Weltje Road;
- (b) between its junction with the A4 and a point 10m south of the southern kerbline of the A4.
