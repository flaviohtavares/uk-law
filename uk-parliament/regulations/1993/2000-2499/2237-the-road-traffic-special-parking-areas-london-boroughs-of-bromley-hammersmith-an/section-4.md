# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (London Borough of Wandsworth) (Amendment) Order 1993, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Designation of special parking areas

(1) The Secretary of State hereby designates each of the following areas as a special parking area:—

- (a) the London borough of Bromley except the roads named or described in Schedule 2;
- (b) the London borough of Hammersmith and Fulham except the roads named or described in Schedule 3; and
- (c) the London borough of Lewisham except the roads named or described in Schedule 4.

(2) A reference in the following provisions of this Order to a special parking area is a reference to an area designated by this article.
