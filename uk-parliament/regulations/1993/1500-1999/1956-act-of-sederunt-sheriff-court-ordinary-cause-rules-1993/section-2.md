# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause Rules) 1993, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Ordinary Cause Rules

For Schedule 1 to the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00010] there shall be substituted the Schedule in Schedule 1 to this Act of Sederunt.
