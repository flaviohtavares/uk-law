# The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1969

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made

2nd August 1993

Laid before Parliament

5th August 1993

Coming into force

1st September 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 218(1)(a), (2) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st September 1993.

## 2. Amendment

In paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 to the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1993[^f00002]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1), for the words “institution or university” there shall be substituted the words “school, city technology college, institution, or university”;
- (b) for sub-paragraph (1)(a), there shall be substituted the following—

  > - (i) is for the degree of Bachelor of Education, a certificate in Education, a post-graduate certificate in Education or a comparable qualification awarded by a university in England or Wales; or
  > - (ii) is a post-graduate course of teacher training at a school or city technology college

  .

Emily Blatch

Minister of State

Department for Education

2nd August 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1993 to cover courses at schools (including city technology colleges); sub-paragraph (1)(a) has been amended in consequence and a redundant reference to the Council for National Academic Awards omitted.
