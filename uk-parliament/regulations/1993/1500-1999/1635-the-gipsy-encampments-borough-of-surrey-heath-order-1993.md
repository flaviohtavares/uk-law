# The Gipsy Encampments (Borough of Surrey Heath) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No.1635

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Gipsy Encampments (Borough of Surrey Heath) Order 1993

Made

29th June 1993

Laid before Parliament

7th July 1993

Coming into force

28th July 1993

The Secretary of State, being satisfied that adequate provision is made in the area of the Borough of Surrey Heath for the accommodation of gipsies residing in or resorting to that area, on the joint application of the councils of the County of Surrey and the Borough of Surrey Heath, and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(2) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gipsy Encampments (Borough of Surrey Heath) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 28th July 1993.

**2.** The area of the Borough of Surrey Heath is designated as an area to which section 10(prohibition of unauthorised camping in designated areas) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

D. M. Curry

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

29th June 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the Borough of Surrey Heath as an area to which section 10 of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

It is an offence within a designated area for a gipsy to station a caravan for the purpose of residing for any period on any land within the boundaries of a highway, or on any other unoccupied land, or on any occupied land without the consent of the occupier.

Under section 11 of the Act (substituted by section 174 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980), unlawfully stationed caravans and their occupants may be removed by order of a magistrates' court.
