# The Assisted Areas (Amendment) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 1993/1877](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1877) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 1894

INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT

The Assisted Areas (Amendment) Order 1993

Made

27th July 1993

Laid before Parliament

27th July 1993

Coming into force

1st August 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 1(1), (3), (4) and (7) of the Industrial Development Act 1982[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Assisted Areas (Amendment) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st August 1993.

**2.** The Assisted Areas Order 1993[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting “5th April 1981” for “2nd April 1988” in article 2(2)[^f00002].

Tim Sainsbury

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

27th July 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Assisted Areas Order 1993 (“the 1993 Order”) by substituting 5th April 1981, the date of the 1981 Census, as the date on which a ward in a travel to work area in England and Wales referred to in the 1993 Order is taken to have existed for the purposes of the 1993 Order.
