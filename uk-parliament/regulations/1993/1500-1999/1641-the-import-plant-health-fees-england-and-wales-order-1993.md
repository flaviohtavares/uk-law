# The Import (Plant Health Fees) (England and Wales) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1641

PLANT HEALTH

The Import (Plant Health Fees) (England and Wales) Order 1993

Made

30th June 1993

Laid before Parliament

1st July 1993

Coming into force

19th July 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food in relation to England and the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1(2)(b), 2, 3(1) and 4(A) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Import (Plant Health Fees) (England and Wales) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 19th July 1993.

(2) In this Order “import licence” means a licence issued under article 30 of the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993[^f00003].

## 2. Revocations

The Import and Export (Plant Health Fees) (England and Wales) Order 1988[^f00004] and the Import and Export (Plant Health Fees) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Order 1991[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Import fees

There shall be paid to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food in respect of any service described in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order the fee prescribed in column 3 of the Schedule in relation to that service.
