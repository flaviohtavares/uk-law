# The Education (Access Funds) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1892 (S.220)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Education (Access Funds) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993

Made

23rd July 1993

Laid before Parliament

27th July 1993

Coming into force

1st September 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 73(a) and (c) and 74(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Access Funds) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st September 1993.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Education (Access Funds) (Scotland) Regulations 1990([^f00002]).

## 2. Amendments to the principal Regulations

Regulation 2 of the principal Regulations is amended–

- (a) by substituting for paragraph (b) the following paragraph:–

  > - (b) an institution within the higher education sector, within the meaning of section 56(2) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992([^f00003]), to the managers of the institution;

  ; and
- (b) by adding at the end of paragraph (e) the words “to the managers of the college.”.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

23rd July 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations widen the scope of the Education (Access Funds) (Scotland) Regulations 1990 to enable the Secretary of State to pay access funds to all Universities in Scotland and not just to the Universities designated under section 44 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992.
