# The Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1636

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1993

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

29th June 1993

Coming into force

30th June 1993

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 60 and 62(1)(a) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the council of the London Borough of Tower Hamlets in accordance with section 61(1) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which has been laid before Parliament and has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1993 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, “the map” means the map entitled “Map referred to in the Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1993”, of which prints, signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment, are deposited and available for inspection at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment and the offices of the council of the London Borough of Tower Hamlets.

## 3. Designation of housing action trust area

The area of land shown on the map bounded externally by a black line and edged internally with a stippled band is hereby designated as a housing action trust area.

## 4. Establishment of the Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust

For the area designated by article 3 above there is hereby established a housing action trust, named the Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

G. S. K. Young

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

29th June 1993

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates an area of land totalling approximately 9 hectares in the London Borough of Tower Hamlets as a housing action trust area, and establishes a housing action trust for that area to be called the Tower Hamlets Housing Action Trust. The location of the designated area is shown on the map forming part of this note.
