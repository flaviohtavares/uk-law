# The Civil Legal Aid (General) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1756

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Civil Legal Aid (General) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1993

Made

14th July 1993

Laid before Parliament

21st July 1993

Coming into force

1st September 1993

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Aid (General) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st September 1993.

**2.** In regulations 96(3)(b) and 97(4) of the Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989[^f00002] for the words “at the rate of 10 ½ per cent per annum” there shall be substituted, in respect of interest accruing on or after 1st September 1993, the words “at the rate of 8 per cent per annum”.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 14th July 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Legal Aid (General) Regulations 1989 by reducing the rate of interest chargeable where enforcement of the statutory charge is postponed from 10 ½ per cent to 8 per cent.
