# The Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 1511

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1993

Made

10th June 1993

Laid before Parliament

22nd June 1993

Coming into force

13th July 1993

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 66(2), 67(2), 67A(5), 73(3), 74(1) and 79(2) of the Rent Act 1977[^f00001] as extended by subsections (2) and (3) of section 2 of the Welsh Language Act 1967[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 13th July 1993.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) Regulations 1980[^f00003].

**2.** The forms set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby prescribed as versions in Welsh which may be used instead of the corresponding forms in the Schedule to the principal Regulations for the purposes set out in regulations 3 and 4 of the principal Regulations.

**3.** Forms substantially to the like effect to the forms set out in the Schedule to these Regulations may be used.

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

10th June 1993

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe Welsh versions of Forms no. 5, 6, 7, 9A, 10, 11, 12 and 13 of the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) Regulations 1980[^f00009] as amended by the Rent Act 1977 (Forms etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1984[^f00010], 1987[^f00011], 1988[^f00012], and 1993[^f00013] .

Forms 5, 6 and 7 are letters concerning the “Application for Registration of Fair Rent”.

Form 9A is a letter concerning the “Application for an Interim Registration of Fair Rent”.

Forms 10 and 11 are letters concerning the “Application for Cancellation of Registered Rent”.

Forms 12 and 13 are letters concerning the “Notification of Application for Registration of Fair Rent”.

The forms are a direct translation of the corresponding English forms with no amendment.
