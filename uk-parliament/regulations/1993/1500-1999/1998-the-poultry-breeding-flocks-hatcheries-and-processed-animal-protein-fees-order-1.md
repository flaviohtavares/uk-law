(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order replaces the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1992. It provides for fees to recover the full cost of the transactions specified in the Order in respect of breeding flocks and hatcheries and for the purpose of authorising laboratories under the Poultry Breeding Flocks and Hatcheries Order 1993 and the Processed Animal Protein Order 1989. The number of fees payable has been reduced.
