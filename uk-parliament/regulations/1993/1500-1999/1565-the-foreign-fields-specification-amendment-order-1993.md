# The Foreign Fields (Specification) (Amendment) Order 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [S.I. 1991/1982](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1982) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1993 No. 1565

TAXES

The Foreign Fields (Specification) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made

18th June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 9(5) and 12(2) of the Oil Taxation Act 1983[^f00001]and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Foreign Fields (Specification) (Amendment) Order 1993.

**2.** The Foreign Fields (Specification) Order 1991[^f00002](hereinafter referred to as “the 1991 Order”) shall have effect subject to the amendment set out in article 3 below.

**3.** The Schedule set out in this Order shall be substituted for the Schedule to the 1991 Order.

Tim Eggar

Minister for Energy,

Department of Trade and Industry

18th June 1993
