# The Driving Licences (Designation of Relevant External Law) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 116

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Driving Licences (Designation of Relevant External Law) Order 1994

Made

22nd January 1994

Coming into force

12th February 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 88(8)[^f00001] and 89(2)(b) and (c) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Driving Licences (Designation of Relevant External Law) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 12th February 1994.

## 2. Designation of relevant external law

The law for the time being in force in the Isle of Man which corresponds to Part III of the Road Traffic Act 1988 is hereby designated as one which makes satisfactory provision for—

- (a) tests of competence to drive all classes of goods vehicle and all classes of passenger-carrying vehicle; and
- (b) the granting of licences to drive all classes of goods vehicle and all classes of passenger-carrying vehicle.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Key

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

22nd January 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates Manx law as one which makes satisfactory provision for tests of competence to drive goods and passenger-carrying vehicles under section 89(2)(b) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 and for the granting of licences to drive such vehicles under section 89(2)(c) of that Act.
