# The Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 202 (C.6)

TRANSPORT

The Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1994

Made

1st February 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 154(2) of the Railways Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1994.

**2.** Section 117 of the Railways Act 1993 shall come into force on 2nd February 1994.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

1st February 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 2nd February 1994 section 117 of the Railways Act 1993, which makes provision with respect to the safety of railways and other guided transport systems.
