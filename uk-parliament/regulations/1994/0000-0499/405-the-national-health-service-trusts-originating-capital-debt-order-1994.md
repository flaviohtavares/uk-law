# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 405

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1994

Made

23rd February 1994

Coming into force

14th March 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 9(1) and (4) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1994 and shall come into force on the 14th March 1994.

(2) In this Order—“ the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. Originating capital debt of NHS trusts

(1) The originating capital debt of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified opposite it in column 2 of the Schedule.

(2) Each originating capital debt specified in column 2 of the Schedule shall be divided between an initial loan of an amount specified opposite it in column 3 of the Schedule and public dividend capital of an amount specified opposite it in column 4 of the Schedule.

Virginia Bottomley

Secretary of State for Health

21st February 1994

We consent,

Andrew Mackay

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

23rd February 1994

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital debt provided for in section 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 of NHS Trusts established under the Act with an operational date of 1 April 1993. It provides also for the splitting of the originating capital debts into loan and public dividend capital.
