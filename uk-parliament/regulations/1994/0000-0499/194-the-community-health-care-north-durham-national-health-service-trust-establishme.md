# The Community Health Care: North Durham National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 194

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Community Health Care: North Durham National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994

Made

31st January 1994

Coming into force

10th February 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Community Health Care: North Durham National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994, and shall come into force on 10th February 1994.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In sub–paragraph (a) of article 3(2) of the Community Health Care: North Durham National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003] (functions of the trust) for the words “Dryburn Hospital, Durham DH1 5TW” there shall be substituted the words “County Hospital, Durham DH1 4ST”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

31st January 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Community Health Care: North Durham National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 to alter the address at which hospital accommodation and services (which the NHS trust established by that Order is to own and manage) are to be provided.
