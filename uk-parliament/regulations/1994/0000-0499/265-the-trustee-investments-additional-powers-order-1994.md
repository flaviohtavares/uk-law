# The Trustee Investments (Additional Powers) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 265

TRUSTEES

The Trustee Investments (Additional Powers) Order 1994

Made

8th February 1994

Laid before Parliament

18th February 1994

Coming into force

11th March 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of February 1994

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 12 of the Trustee Investments Act 1961[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Trustee Investments (Additional Powers) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 11th March 1994.

## 2. Addition to Part I of the First Schedule to the Trustee Investments Act 1961

The powers of investment conferred by section 1 of the Trustee Investments Act 1961 shall be extended by adding National Savings Pensioners Guaranteed Income Bonds to paragraph 1 of Part I of the First Schedule thereto[^f00002].

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds National Savings Pensioners Guaranteed Income Bonds to the list of investments in Part I of the First Schedule to the Trustee Investments Act 1961. This Part of the Schedule sets out narrower-range investments that may be made by trustees without previously obtaining advice.
