# The Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1993/2935](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2935) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1994 No. 107 (S.3)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994

Made

18th January 1994

Coming into force

24th January 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 12A(1) of and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(a) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994 and shall come into force on 24th January 1994.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993([^f00002]).

## 2. Amendment of definition of “the trust”

In article 1 of the principal Order in the definition of “the trust” for the words “the Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust” there are substituted the words “the Greater Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust”.

## 3. Amendment of Article 2 of the principal Order

In article 2 of the principal Order for the words “the Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust” there are substituted the words “the Greater Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust”.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

18th January 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends articles 1 and 2 of the Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 to change the name of the Trust to “the Greater Glasgow Community and Mental Health Services National Health Service Trust”.
