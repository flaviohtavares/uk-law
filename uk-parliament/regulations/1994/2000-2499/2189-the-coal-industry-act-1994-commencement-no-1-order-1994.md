# The Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2189 (C.45)

COAL INDUSTRY

The Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

Made

21st August 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 68(4) and (5) of the Coal Industry Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Coal Industry Act 1994.

## 2. Coming into force of certain provisions of Act

The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in the second column thereof) shall come into force on 19th September 1994.

Tim Eggar

Minister for Industry and Energy,

Department of Trade and Industry

21st August 1994
