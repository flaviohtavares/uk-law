# The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2422

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 1994

Made

14th September 1994

Laid before Parliament

23rd September 1994

Coming into force

14th October 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2), (4) and (7) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 14th October 1994.

## 2. Revocation of 1993 Regulations

The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) Regulations 1983[^f00002]are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Curry

Minister of State

Department of the Environment

14th September 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) Regulations 1983, which required local authorities to publish the information specified in the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (England) Code 1983.
