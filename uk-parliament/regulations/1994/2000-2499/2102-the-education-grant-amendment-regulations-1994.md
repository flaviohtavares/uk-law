# The Education (Grant) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2102

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Grant) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

5th August 1994

Laid before Parliament

9th August 1994

Coming into force

1st September 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(1)(b) of the Education Act 1944 ft(a) and vested in the Secretary of State ft(b), the Secretary of State for Education, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st September 1994.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Grant) Regulations 1990

The Education (Grant) Regulations 1990ft(c) are amended by the insertion after regulation 16 of–

> **16A.** The Secretary of State may pay grants to the Engineering Council in respect of approved expenditure incurred or to be incurred by them in connection with the payment of bursaries for students attending courses leading to a degree in engineering.

.

Eric Forth

Minister of State

Department for Education

3rd August 1994

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

5th August 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Grant) Regulations 1990 to enable the Secretary of State to pay grants to the Engineering Council to enable them to pay bursaries to students attending any engineering degree course.
