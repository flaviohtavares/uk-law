# The Potato Marketing Scheme (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2404

AGRICULTURE

The Potato Marketing Scheme (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

7 September 1994

Coming into force

8 September 1994

Whereas the Potato Marketing Board duly submitted to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State concerned with agriculture in Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales (hereinafter called “the Ministers”) certain amendments of the Potato Marketing Scheme[^f00001], in accordance with sub-paragraphs (1) to (3) of paragraph 1 of the First Schedule to the Agricultural Marketing Act 1958[^f00002] (hereinafter called “the 1958 Act”);

And whereas the Ministers caused notice of those amendments to be published in accordance with section 2(1) of the 1958 Act as applied to amendments by paragraph 1(4) of the First Schedule thereto;

And whereas the Ministers proposed to modify those amendments;

And whereas the Ministers gave notice of their proposed modifications to the Potato Marketing Board;

And whereas the Potato Marketing Board, within four weeks after that notice had been so given, notified the Ministers that they assented to those modifications;

And whereas the Ministers are satisfied that those amendments as so modified will conduce to the more efficient operation of the Potato Marketing Scheme;

And whereas those amendments as so modified are set out in the Schedule hereto;

Now, therefore, the Ministers, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the 1958 Act, and now vested in them[^f00003], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Potato Marketing Scheme (Amendment) Order 1994.

**2.** The amendments of the Potato Marketing Scheme which are set out in the Schedule hereto are hereby approved and shall come into force on 8 September 1994.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on

William Waldegrave

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

This Order reduces the total size of the Potato Marketing Board from 23 members to 16 members. The number, and the boundaries of, the Board’s electoral districts are altered and the number of district members is reduced from 18 to 13. There will no longer be a special member for Scotland and a special member for England and Wales. These changes in the composition of the Board will take effect from such day in the month of September or October next following the coming into force of this Order as the Board shall fix (“the first retirement date”). There will be an election of all new district members before the first retirement date and thereafter an election of all district members in every fourth subsequent year. Other minor and consequential amendments to the Potato Marketing Scheme are made.
