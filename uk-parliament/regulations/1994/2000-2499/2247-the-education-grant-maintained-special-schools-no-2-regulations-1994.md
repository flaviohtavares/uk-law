# The Education (Grant-maintained Special Schools) (No. 2) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2247

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Grant-maintained Special Schools) (No. 2) Regulations 1994

Made

26th August 1994

Laid before Parliament

31st August 1994

Coming into force

21st September 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 14 of Schedule 11 to the Education Act 1993[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant-maintained Special Schools) (No. 2) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 21st September 1994.

## 2. Application of section 12 of the Education Act 1994

Section 12 of the Education Act 1994[^f00002] shall have effect in relation to the governing bodies of grant-maintained special schools as it has effect in relation to the governing bodies of grant-maintained schools.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

23rd August 1994

Rod Richards

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

26th August 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply section 12 of the Education Act 1994 (power of schools to provide courses of initial teacher training) to the governing bodies of grant-maintained special schools.
