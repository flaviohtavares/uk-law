# The Education (Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2017

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 1994

Made

28th July 1994

Laid before Parliament

1st August 1994

Coming into force

1st October 1994

Whereas it appears to the Secretary of State that the full-time equivalent enrolment number of the institution conducted by Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation[^f00001] for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number;

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988 [^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st October 1994.

**2.** On the date on which this Order comes into force Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation shall cease to be a further education corporation and shall become a higher education corporation.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

28th July 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the transfer of Norwich School of Art and Design Further Education Corporation to the higher education sector. On 1st October 1994 that corporation becomes a higher education corporation.
