# The Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2408 (C.48)

MEDICINES

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

Made

12th September 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994.

## 2. Appointed Day

3rd October 1994 is the day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 and 2 of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992.

Virginia Bottomley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, (Department of Health)

12th September 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd October 1994 sections 1 and 2 of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992.
