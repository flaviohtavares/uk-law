# The County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2170

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1994

Made

15th August 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

**1.** The County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994 is hereby confirmed without modifications.

**2.** The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plan are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY, and at the offices of the Northumberland County Council, County Hall, Morpeth, Northumberland NE61 2EF.

**3.** The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 of the Highways Act 1980.

**4.** This Instrument may be cited as The County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1994.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Pamela Denham

Regional Director Government Office for the North East

15th August 1994

## THE SCHEDULE — THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF NORTHUMBERLAND (DUPLICATE KITTY BREWSTER BRIDGE) SCHEME 1994

The County Council of Northumberland (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under Section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other enabling powers.

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the River Blyth the bridge specified in the Schedule to this scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to improve or construct between Bebside in the District of Blyth Valley and Bedlington Station in the District of Wansbeck in the said County.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as the County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994.

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME — Plans and Specifications of the Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge

The location and general design of the Bridge are shown on the plan numbered 1068/11/A189/24 marked “the County Council of Northumberland (Duplicate Kitty Brewster Bridge) Scheme 1994”, sealed with the Common Seal of the Council and deposited at County Hall, Morpeth, Northumberland NE61 2EF and at the offices of the Secretary of State for Transport.

### Points of Commencement and Termination

From a point on the south bank of the River Blyth, 660 metres north of the centre line of the A193. To a point on the north bank of the River Blyth, 120 metres south of the centre line of the B1331.

### Spans

Five spans each of 24.38 metres.

### Headway

Not less than 10.39 metres above the mean high water spring tide level (MHWS) of 2.69 metres AOD.

### Waterway

A minimum clear navigable waterway of 21.5 metres.

### Overall Dimensions

The bridge will be 121.9 metres in length and will carry a 7.3 meters wide carriageway, with a one metre hard strip on either side, a footway of 2.0 meters width on the west side and a 0.6 metre wide verge on the east side.
