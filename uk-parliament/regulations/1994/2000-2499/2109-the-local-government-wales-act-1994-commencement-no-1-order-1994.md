# The Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2109 (C.40)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

Made

6th August 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 66(3) of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994.

**2.** The date appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 is 15th August 1994–

- Section 41 (continuity of employment in certain cases of voluntary transfer);
- Section 52 (application of Part I of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00002] during transitional period):Section 65 (expenses).

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

6th August 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 15th August 1994 sections 41, 52 and 65 of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994.
