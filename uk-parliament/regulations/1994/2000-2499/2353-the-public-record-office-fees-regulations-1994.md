# The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2353

PUBLIC RECORDS

The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1994

Made

6th September 1994

Coming into force

1st October 1994

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(5) of the Public Records Act 1958[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st October 1994.

**2.** (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the fees to be charged for the authentication of copies of or extracts from records under the charge of the Keeper of Public Records and for other services afforded by officers of the Public Record Office shall be those set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(2) The Keeper of Public Records may remit a fee where the service performed by him is likely to include any research in consequence of a postal inquiry or has been or is likely to be exceptionally simple.

**3.** The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Mackay of Clashfern ,C.

Dated 19th August 1994

We concur,

Andrew Mitchell

Tim Wood

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 6th September 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1992 and prescribe a fresh range of fees to be charged for authentication of copies of records and for other services provided by the Public Record Office. New charges are introduced for photographs of records, a service withdrawn in 1991 but now re-introduced. These are listed in paragraph 11 of the Schedule.

The changes to existing fees are as follows:
