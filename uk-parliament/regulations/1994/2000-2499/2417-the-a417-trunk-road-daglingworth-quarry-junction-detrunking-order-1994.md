# The A417 Trunk Road (Daglingworth Quarry Junction) (Detrunking) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2417

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A417 Trunk Road (Daglingworth Quarry Junction) (Detrunking) Order 1994

Made

12th September 1994

Coming into force

29th September 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

**1.** This Order shall come into force on 29th September 1994 and may be cited as the A417 Trunk Road (Daglingworth Quarry Junction) (Detrunking) Order 1994.

**2.** The length of trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order and shown by broad striped hatching on the deposited plan given the reference letter \`A' shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a classified road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Gloucestershire that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

**3.** In this Order—

(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

(2)

- (i) “classified road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is not a principal road for the purposes of enactments or instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads but is a classified road for the purpose of every enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State and which does not specifically refer to their classification as principal roads;
- (ii) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/2/SWM106 marked “The A417 Trunk Road (Daglingworth Quarry Junction) (Detrunking) Order 1994” signed by the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (iii) “the new trunk road” has the same meaning as in the A417 Trunk Road (Daglingworth Quarry Junction) Order 1994;
- (iv) “the trunk road” means the A417 Trunk Road.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

J. D. Gill

Deputy Director South West Construction Programme Division Highways Agency

12th September 1994

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be trunk road is that length of the A417 Trunk Road in the District of Cotswold in the County of Gloucestershire as follows:—

That length of the trunk road shown on the deposited plan, given the reference letter \`A', about 125 metres in length starting at a point 15 metres southeast of its junction with Dowers Lane (3/ 189) southeastwards for a distance of 125 metres.
