# The Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2484 (S.116)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1994

Made

15th September 1994

Coming into force

1st October 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 105(6) of and paragraph 25(1) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service as a whole to make this Order[^f00002], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 25(3) of the said Schedule 7A[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st October 1994.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trust and consequent revocation

The Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust established by the Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00004] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Ian Lang

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th September 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the Royal Scottish National Hospital and Community National Health Service Trust on 1st October 1994.
