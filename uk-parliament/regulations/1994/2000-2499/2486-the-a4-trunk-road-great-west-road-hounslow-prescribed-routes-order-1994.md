# The A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2486

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1994

Made

19th September 1994

Coming into force

30th September 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 30th September 1994.

**2.** In this Order—

- “Great West Road” means that length of the A4 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hounslow known as Great West Road;
- “Syon Lane” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Hounslow;
- “eastern traffic island” means the island site in the westbound carriageway of Great West Road which extends eastwards from its junction with Syon Lane for a distance of 9.3 metres; and
- “western traffic island” means the island site in the eastbound carriageway of Great West Road which extends westwards from its junction with Syon Lane for a distance of 10.8 metres.

**3.** Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the eastern traffic island or the western traffic island shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Syon Lane.

**4.** Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the left of the eastern traffic island or the western traffic island shall cause that vehicle to proceed straight ahead or turn left.

**5.** Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

I. E. W. Brown

A Grade 6 Officer for Transport Highways Agency London Office

19th September 1994
