# The Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994, section 6

> Text as at 2024-01-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**6.** *Article 16 and Schedule 8* apply specified provisions of the Company Directors Disqualification Act [1986 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/46) where an insolvent partnership is wound up as an unregistered company under Part V of the Act.
