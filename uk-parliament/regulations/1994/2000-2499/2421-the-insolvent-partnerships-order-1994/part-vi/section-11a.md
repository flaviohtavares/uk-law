> Text as at 2024-01-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11A. Decision procedure in insolvency proceedings in relation to insolvent partnerships[^c-ca0130063383b0bea64af7760f383861]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 246ZE, 246ZF, 379ZA and 379ZB of the Act apply in insolvency proceedings in relation to insolvent partnerships with the modifications set out in Schedule 7A to this Order.
