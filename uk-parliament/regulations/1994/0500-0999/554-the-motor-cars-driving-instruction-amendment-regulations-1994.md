# The Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 554

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

4th March 1994

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1994

Coming into force

1st April 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 125(3), 132 and 141 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on the 1st April 1994.

**2.** (1) The Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) Regulations 1989[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4—

- (a) for paragraph (f) there shall be substituted—

  > - (f) the book “Your Driving Test” issued by the Department of Transport and published by HM Stationery Office;

  ; and
- (b) in paragraph (j) for ““Driving—The Department of Transport Manual”” there shall be substituted ““The Driving Manual””.

(3) In the Table in regulation 13, for the entry in column (3) in items 2 and 3 there shall be substituted in each case “£55”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Key

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

4th March 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) Regulations 1989 by increasing the fees payable for the driving ability and fitness test and for the instructional ability and fitness test from £54 (this was last revised on 3rd August 1992) to £55 and by updating the references to certain publications.
