# The Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust(Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 991

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE,ENGLAND AND WALES

The Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust(Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

Made

30th March 1994

Laid before Parliament

7th April 1994

Coming into force

28th April 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the East Berkshire Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having conslted Berkshire Health Authority[^f00003]and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 28th April 1994.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Berkshire Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust propertyprepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 8th November 1993, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 28th April 1994 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

30th March 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Heatherwood and Wexham Park Hospitals' National Health Service Trust on 28th April 1994 trust property formerly held by the Berkshire Health Authority. Acopy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
