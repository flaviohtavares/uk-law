# The National Health Service Act 1977 (Composition of Medical Practices Committee) Modification Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 545

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service Act 1977 (Composition of Medical Practices Committee) Modification Order 1994

Made

4th March 1994

Laid before Parliament

7th March 1994

Coming into force

1st April 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 7(1A) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Act 1977 (Composition of Medical Practices Committee) Modification Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Modification of section 7(1)(b) of the National Health Service Act 1977

In section 7(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977 (composition of Medical Practices Committee) paragraph (b) shall be modified[^f00002] by substituting “five of the” for “the chairman and five of the other”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Brian Mawhinney

Minister of State,

Department of Health

4th March 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further modifies section 7(1)(b) of the National Health Service Act 1977. Section 7(1) specifies the composition of the Medical Practices Committee. The Committee exercises functions under Part II of that Act in connection with the distribution of registered medical practitioners providing general medical services pursuant to that Part. This Order removes the requirement that the chairman of the Committee be a registered medical practitioner.
