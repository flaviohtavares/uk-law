# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations 1994, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Earnings of self-employed earners

“Earnings”, in the case of employment as a self-employed earner, means the gross income of the employment and

- (a) shall include any allowance paid under section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973[^f00039] or section 2 of the Enterprise and New Towns (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00040] to the relevant person for the purpose of assisting him in carrying on his business, unless at the date of the application the allowance has been terminated; but
- (b) shall not include any payment to which paragraph 24 of Schedule 3 refers (payments in respect of a person accommodated with the relevant person under arrangements made by a local authority or voluntary organisation).
