# The Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1993/1511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1511) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1994 No. 725

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

10th March 1994

Laid before Parliament

16th March 1994

Coming into force

6th April 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 66(2), 67(2), 67A(5), 73(3) and 74(1) of the Rent Act 1977[^f00001] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 6th April 1994.

## 2. Amendment

The Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc.) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1993[^f00003] are amended as follows:–

- (a) in regulation 2 after the words “the corresponding forms in” delete the words “the Schedule” and insert “Schedule 1”;
- (b) in the Schedule–
  - (i) in question 4 of “Ffurflen Rhif 5”, “Ffurflen Rhif 6”, “Ffurflen Rhif 7”, “Ffurflen Rhif 9A” and “Ffurflen Rhif 10”, add after the words “ar ba lawr” the words “neu loriau”;
  - (ii) in question 1 of “Ffurflen Rhif 10” for the words “yr ydych am gofrestru rhent ar ei gyfer” there are substituted the words “y mae'r rhent wedi ei gofrestru ar ei gyfer”;
  - (iii) at the end of paragraph 5 of “Ffurflen Rhif 13” add the word “teg” after the word “rhent”;
  - (iv) in question 15 of “Ffurflen Rhif 5” and in question 14 of “Ffurflen Rhif 7” add after the words “unrhyw waith” the words “neu waith”;
  - (v) in question 11 of “Ffurflen Rhif 10” for the word “Os” substitute “A” and add after the words “unrhyw waith” the words “neu waith”;
  - (vi) in question 11 of “Ffurflen Rhif 9A” after the word “teg” add the words “gan gynnwys y dreth gyngor”;
  - (vii) for the third question in question 11 of “Ffurflen Rhif 6”, there is substituted:–

    “A oedd unrhyw waith trwsio cychwynnol i'w wneud? Oedd/Nac oedd Ticiwch un blwch”;
  - (viii) for the fourth question in question 11 “Ffurflen Rhif 6”, for the word ““Do”” there is substituted the word ““Oedd”” and for the final question, for the word ““Naddo”” there are substituted the words ““Nacoedd””;
  - (ix) in the final question of question 11 of “Ffurflen Rhif 6”, for the word “wneud” there is substituted the word “orffen”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Sir Wyn Roberts

Minister of State Welsh Office

10th March 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1993[^f00004] (“the Welsh Regulations”) prescribed Welsh versions of forms number 5, 6, 7, 9A, 10, 11, 12 and 13 of the Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc) Regulations 1980[^f00005] as amended by the Rent Act 1977 (Forms, etc) (Amendment) Regulations 1984[^f00006], 1987[^f00007], 1988[^f00008] and 1993[^f00009]. These Regulations are made to correct defects in the Welsh Regulations.
