# The Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument supersedes [S.I.1993/3140](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3140) published on 23rd December 1993 and is being issued free of charge to all known receipients of that Statutory Instrument

1994 No. 745

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1994

Made

14th March 1994

Coming into force

for the purposes of article 3

24th March 1994

for all other purposes

1st April 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to that latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1994 and shall come into force—

- (a) for the purposes of article 3 of this Order, on 24th March 1994;
- (b) for all other purposes, on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust established by the Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

## 3. Revocation

The Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993[^f00006] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

14th March 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order supersedes the Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust Dissolution order 1993, which is defective and is revoked by this Order, and provides for the dissolution of the Mid Essex Community Health National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1994.
