# The Plant Breeders~ Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 675

PLANT BREEDERS' RIGHTS

The Plant Breeders\~ Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

9th March 1994

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1994

Coming into force

1st April 1994

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for North ern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section s 9(1) and 36 of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002], and the said Minister and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, as respects the Isle of Man, in exercise of the said powers as extended to the Isle of Man[^f00003], and in exercise of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with section 9(1) of that Act and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00004], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990

For the Schedule to the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990[^f00005] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Revocation

The Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00006] are revoked.

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990 by prescribing revised fees in respect of matters arising under the Plant Breeders' Rights Regulations 1978 (as amended) (regulation 2).

The matters in respect of which the revised fees are prescribed are listed in the Schedule to these Regulations, and the fees which were chargeable before these Regulations came into force are printed in italics beside the revised fees.

Application and renewal fees have been reduced by approximately 10 per cent. Test fees have been reduced by approximately 20 per cent. Fees payable in respect of other matters remain unchanged.
