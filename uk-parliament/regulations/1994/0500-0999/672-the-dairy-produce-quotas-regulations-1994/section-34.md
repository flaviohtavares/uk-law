# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1994, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Withdrawal of Special Quota

Where a producer has special quota registered in his name and it appears to the Minister that the producer has made a false or misleading statement in his application for special quota, has failed to comply with the requirements in relation to special quota, or has failed to comply with any of the undertakings referred to in regulation 8(5), the Minister may withdraw the whole or any part of the special quota.
