# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1994, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Determination of levy

For the purposes of Article 2(1) of the Council Regulation (which deals with the calculation of the levy), the contribution of producers who make wholesale deliveries towards the levy shall be established, in accordance with the provisions of that Article, at the level of the purchaser.

**4.** The functions of the Intervention Board are extended to include those previously exercised by the milk marketing boards in respect of the preparation and maintenance of direct sales and wholesale registers, and purchaser notices (regulation 25).
