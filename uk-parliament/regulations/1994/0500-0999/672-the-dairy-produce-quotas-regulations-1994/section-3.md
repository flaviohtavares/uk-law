# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Establishment of quota

Total direct sales quota and total wholesale quota for any person and purchaser quota for any purchaser in respect of any quota year shall be established in accordance with these Regulations and the Community legislation.

**3.** Provisions on the temporary transfer of quota are extended to any agreement between producers to make a temporary transfer of unused quota, as notified to the Intervention Board (regulation 15).
