# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1994, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Reallocation of quota and calculation of levy liability

Schedule 5 shall apply in respect of the reallocation of quota and the calculation of levy liability for the purposes of Article 2(1) of the Council Regulation (which deals with the calculation of the levy).
