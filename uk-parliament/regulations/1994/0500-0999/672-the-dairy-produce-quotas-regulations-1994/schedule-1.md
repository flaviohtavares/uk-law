## SCHEDULE 1 — “MEANING OF COMMUNITY LEGISLATION”

Regulation 2(1)

**1.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 857/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/0857), adopting general rules for the application of the levy referred to in Article 5c of Regulation [(EEC) No. 804/68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1968/0804) in the milk and milk products sector, OJ No. L90, 1.4.84, p.13.

**2.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 764/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/0764), amending Regulation [(EEC) No. 857/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/0857) adopting general rules for the application of the levy referred to in Article 5c of Regulation [(EEC) No. 804/68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1968/0804) in the milk and milk products sector, OJ No. L84, 29.3.89, p.2.

**7.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1560/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/1560) amending Regulation [(EEC) No. 3950/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/3950) establishing an additional levy in the milk and milk products sector, OJ No. L54, 25.6.93, p.30.
