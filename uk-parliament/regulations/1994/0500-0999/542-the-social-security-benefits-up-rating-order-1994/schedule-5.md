## SCHEDULE 5 — PART IV OF SCHEDULE 2 TO THE INCOME SUPPORT REGULATIONS AS AMENDED BY THIS ORDER

Article 16(5)

> **PART IV — WEEKLY AMOUNTS OF PREMIUMS SPECIFIED IN PART III**
>
> | Premium | Amount |
> | --- | --- |
> | **15.** (1) Lone parent premium. | (1) £5.10. |
> | (2) [^f00036] Pensioner premium for persons aged under 75—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9(a); | (2)<br>- (a) £18.25; |

> | (2A) Pensioner premium for persons aged 75 and over—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(a); | (2A)<br>- (a) £20.35; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(b). | - (b) £30.40. |
> | (3) Higher Pensioner Premium—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(1)(a) or (b); | (3)<br>- (a) £24.70; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(2)(a) or (b). | - (b) £35.30. |
> | (4) Disability Premium—<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(a); | (4)<br>- (a) £19.45; |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(b). | - (b) £27.80. |

> | - (ii) if no-one is in receipt of such an allowance. | - (ii) £68.60. |

> | (7) Carer Premium[^f00038]. | (7) £12.40 in respect of each person who satisfied the condition specified in paragraph 14ZA. |
