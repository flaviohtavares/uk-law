# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1994, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. The Relevant Sum for Income Support

In section 126(7) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (trade disputes: the relevant sum) the substituted sum is £24.50.
