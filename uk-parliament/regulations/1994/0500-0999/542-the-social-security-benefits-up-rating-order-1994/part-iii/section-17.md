# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1994, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Income Support Transitional Protection

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations 1987[^f00023] shall be increased by 3.5 per cent.of the amount apart from this Order.
