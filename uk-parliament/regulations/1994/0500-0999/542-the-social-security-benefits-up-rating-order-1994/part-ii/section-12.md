# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1994, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Increase in rates of Disability Living Allowance

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991[^f00019] (rate of benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1), for the sum of £44.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £45.70;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (1), for the sum of £30.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £30.55;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) of paragraph (1), for the sum of £11.95 there shall be substituted the sum of £12.15;
- (d) in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (2), for the sum of £31.40 there shall be substituted the sum of £31.95; and
- (e) in sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (2), for the sum of £11.95 there shall be substituted the sum of £12.15.
