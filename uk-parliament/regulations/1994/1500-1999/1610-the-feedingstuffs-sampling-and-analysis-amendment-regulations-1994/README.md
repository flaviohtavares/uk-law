(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Feedingstuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1982 (“the principal Regulations”). Regulation 3 amends Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations, by modifying a method relating to Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub>, replacing methods relating to Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub>, protein and fibre and by deleting a method relating to Copper—Diethyldithiocarbamate.

These Regulations implement the following Community legislation:

- (a) Commission Directive [92/89/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0089) (OJ No. L 344, 26.11.92, p. 35), amending Fourth Commission Directive [73/46/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0046) (OJ No. L 83, 30.3.73, p. 21) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs;
- (b) Commission Directive [92/95/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0095) (OJ No. L 327, 13.11.92, p. 54), amending Seventh Commission Directive [76/372/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0372) (OJ No. L 102, 15.4.76, p. 8) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs.
- (c) Commission Directive [93/28/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0028) (OJ No. L 179, 22.7.93, p. 8), amending Third Commission Directive [72/119/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0119) (OJ No. L 123, 29.5.72, p. 6) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs;
- (d) Commission Directive [94/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0014) (OJ No. L 94, 13.4.94, p. 30), amending Seventh Commission Directive [76/372/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0372) (OJ No L 102, 15.4.76, p. 8) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs.

A compliance cost assessment relating to these Regulations has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
