# The Feedingstuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1994, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) The Regulations may be cited as the Feedingstuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 11th July 1994.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Feedingstuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1982[^f00002].
