# The Feedingstuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1994, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

The principal Regulations are hereby further amended in accordance with regulation 3 below.

**3.** In Schedule 2 (**METHODS OF ANALYSIS**)—

- (a) in paragraph 3 (Methods of Analysis)—
  - (i) item 16 shall be deleted,
  - (ii) for item 26a there shall be substituted the following item:

    > **26a.** Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub> — one-dimensional thin layer chromatographic method

    , and
  - (iii) for item 26b there shall be substituted the following item:

    > **26b.** Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub> — high performance liquid chromatographic method

    ;
- (b) for the provisions relating to method 4: Protein, there shall be substituted the provisions set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
- (c) for the provisions relating to method 9: Fibre, there shall be substituted the provisions set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations;
- (d) the provisions contained in method 16: Copper — Diethyldithiocarbamate spectrophotometric method, shall be deleted;
- (e) in method 26a: Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub> — for certain straight feedingstuffs—
  - (i) for the heading and paragraph 1 there shall be substituted the following provisions:

    > **26a. AFLATOXIN B<sub>1</sub> — ONE-DIMENSIONAL THIN LAYER CHROMATOGRAPHIC METHOD**
    >
    > **1. Scope and field of application**
    >
    > This method is for the determination of aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub> in raw materials and straight feedingstuffs. This method is not applicable to raw materials and straight feedingstuffs containing citrus pulp. The lower limit of determination is 0.01 mg/kg (10 ppb). In the presence of interfering substances it is necessary to repeat the analysis using method 26b (high performance liquid chromatography).

    ;
  - (ii) in paragraph 5.0 (Defatting) for the word “before” there shall be substituted the word “after”;
- (f) for the provisions relating to method 26b: Aflatoxin B<sub>1</sub> — for all other feedingstuffs, there shall be substituted the provisions set out in Schedule 3 to these Regulations.
