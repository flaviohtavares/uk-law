# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) Order 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Designation of special parking area

(1) The Secretary of State hereby designates the City of Westminster as a special parking area except—

- (a) the roads named or described in Part I and Part II of the Schedule to this Order; and
- (b) St James’s Park, Green Park, Hyde Park, and those parts of Kensington Gardens and Regent’s Park which lie within the City of Westminster.

(2) A reference in the following provisions of this Order to the special parking area is a reference to the area designated by this article.
