# The Mental Health (Class of Nurse) (Scotland) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1675 (S.74)

MENTAL HEALTH

The Mental Health (Class of Nurse) (Scotland) Order 1994

Made

14th June 1994

Coming into force

5th July 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 25(2) and (6) of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Mental Health (Class of Nurse) (Scotland) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 5th July 1994.

## 2. Class of nurse

For the purposes of section 25 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984 a nurse of the prescribed class shall be a nurse registered in any of the following parts of the register prepared and maintained in accordance with section 10 of the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1979[^f00002]:—

- (a) Part 3 (first level nurses trained in the nursing of persons suffering from mental illness);
- (b) Part 5 (first level nurses trained in the nursing of persons suffering from mental handicap);
- (c) Part 13 (nurses qualified following a course of preparation in mental health nursing); and
- (d) Part 14 (nurses qualified following a course of preparation in mental handicap nursing).

## 3. Revocation

The Mental Health (Prescription of Class of Nurse) (Scotland) Order 1984[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

New St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

14th June 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and re-enacts with amendments the Mental Health (Prescription of Class of Nurse) (Scotland) Order 1984. It prescribes the class of nurse who is empowered under section 25 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984 to detain for up to 2 hours a person already being treated in hospital for mental disorder but not liable to be detained, by reference to a nurse registered in parts 3, 5, 13 or 14 of the register kept under the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1979. The previous order referred only to parts 3 and 5.
