# The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in History) (England) (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1816

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in History) (England) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

6th July 1994

Laid before Parliament

11th July 1994

Coming into force

1st August 1994

Having made such arrangements for consultation on his proposals pursuant to section 242(1) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001] as he considered appropriate, the Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2)(a) and (b) and (4) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in History) (England) (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st August 1994.

**2.** The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in History) (England) Order 1991[^f00003] is amended as follows:

- (a) in article 1(1) for “articles 2 to 5” there is substituted “articles 2 to 4”;
- (b) in article 1(3) for “, third and fourth” there is substituted “and third” and for “(a) to (d)” there is substituted “(a) to (c)”;
- (c) article 5 is omitted;
- (d) in article 6 after “history” there is inserted “in the first, second and third key stages”;
- (e) in article 7 for “each key stage” there is substituted “the first, second and third key stages” and paragraph (d) and the word “and” preceding it are omitted;
- (f) in article 8(1) “Subject to paragraph (2),” and paragraph (2) are omitted;
- (g) after article 8, there is inserted the following article—

  > **8A.** In the Document, the references to key stage 4 in the contents and title pages are omitted; in attainment targets 1, 2 and 3 the statements of attainment for levels 8-10 are omitted; and the programmes of study (Models 1 and 2) for the fourth key stage are omitted.

  ; and
- (h) in the Schedule “Fourth key stage” in column 1 and “Either Model 1 or Model 2 as set out in the Document” in column 2 are omitted.

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

6th July 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Education (National Curriculum) (Foundation Subjects at Key Stage 4) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/1814](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1814)) amends section 3 of the Education Reform Act 1988 so that history ceases to be a foundation subject of the National Curriculum for pupils in the fourth key stage. This Order amends the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in History) (England) Order 1991 and the Document given effect by that Order to reflect that change.
