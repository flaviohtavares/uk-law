# The Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1648 (C.29)

TRANSPORT

The Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1994

Made

22nd June 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 143(3) and 154(2) of the Railways Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Railways Act 1993 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Order1994.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

Subject to article 3 below, the following provisions of the Railways Act 1993 shall come into force on 15th July 1994:

- sections 139 and 140;
- section 152(3) for the purpose of bringing into force the provisions of Schedule 14 referred to in this article;
- in Schedule 14, the repeals relating to section 8 of the Railways Act 1974[^f00002]and section 36 of the Transport Act 1981[^f00003].

## 3. Transitional provisions

(1) The repeal of section 8 of the Railways Act 1974 shall not affect the operation of that section in relation to any application for grant made pursuant to that section before 15th July 1994 but in respect of which no decision has been made by the Secretary of State before that date.

(2) The repeal of section 36 of the Transport Act 1981 shall not affect the operation of that section in relation to any application for grant made pursuant to that section before 15th July 1994 but in respect of which no decision has been made by the Secretary of State before that date.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

22nd June 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 15th July 1994, sections 139 and 140 of the RailwaysAct 1993 which relate to the making of grants to assist the provision of facilities for freight haulage by railway and inland waterway, and related repeals in Schedule 14. Article 3 of the Order makes transitional provisions.
