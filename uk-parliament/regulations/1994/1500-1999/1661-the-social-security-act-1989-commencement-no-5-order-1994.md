# The Social Security Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1661 (C. 30)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1994

Made

22nd June 1994

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of powers conferred upon him by section 33(2) of the Social Security Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security Act 1989 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1994.

(2) In this Order references to “the Act” are references to the Social Security Act 1989.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) the provisions of the Act specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, for the purposes only of giving effect to the provisions of the Act specified in Part II of that Schedule;
- (b) the provisions of the Act specified in Part II of the Schedule to this Order; and
- (c) section 23 of the Act in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in the Schedule to this Order,

is 23rd June 1994.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

William Hague

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

22nd June 1994

### PART II — PROVISIONS OF THE ACT COMING INTO FORCE ON 23RD JUNE 1994

| *Provision* | *Subject matter* |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule 5, paragraph 5 (except sub-paragraph (2)(b) and (c)) | Unfair maternity provisions |
| Schedule 5, paragraph 6 (except sub-paragraph (3)(b) and (c)) | Unfair family leave provisions |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force of certain provisions of Schedule 5 to the Social Security Act 1989, and section 23 of the Act in so far as it relates to them, on 23rd June 1994. These are—

- Schedule 5, paragraph 5 (except 5(2)(b) and (c)) relating to unfair maternity provisions;
- Schedule 5, paragraph 6 (except 6(3)(b) and (c)) relating to unfair family leave provisions.

It also provides for the coming into force on that date of paragraphs 1, 2 (in part), 3 (in part), 7 (in part), 9 and 10 of Schedule 5 but for the purposes only of giving effect to paragraphs 5 and 6.

(This note is not part of the Order)
