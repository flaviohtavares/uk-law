# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1519

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994

Made

26th May 1994

Laid before Parliament

22nd July 1994

Coming into force

12th August 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting in the manner and in exercise of the powers specified in Appendix 1 to this Instrument, hereby—

- (a) after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001] and section 195(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00002], make the Regulations set out in Part I, and
- (b) give the Directions set out in Part II,

of this Instrument.
