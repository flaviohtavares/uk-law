# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Sutton) Order 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Designation of special parking area

(1) The Secretary of State hereby designates the London borough of Sutton except the roads named or described in the Schedule to this Order as a special parking area.

(2) A reference in the following provisions of this Order to the special parking area is a reference to the area designated by this article.
