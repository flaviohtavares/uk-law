## THE SCHEDULE — ROADS IN THE LONDON BOROUGH OF SUTTON EXCLUDED FROM THE SPECIAL PARKING AREA

Article 3(1)

**1.** A24 between the borough boundary with the borough of Epsom and Ewell and the borough boundary with the London borough of Merton.

**2.** A217 between the borough boundary with the borough of Reigate and Banstead and a point 10m north east of the common boundary between 329 and 331 Bishopsford Road.

**3.** A232 between the borough boundary with the borough of Epsom and Ewell and the borough boundary with the London borough of Croydon, including:

- (a) Chalk Pit Way;
- (b) Grove Road from a point 17m west of its south west junction with Sutton Park Road to its junction with Brighton Road (B2230) and High Street;
- (c) Sutton Court Road from its junction with Brighton Road (B2230) and High Street to its junction with Chalk Pit Way; and
- (d) Sutton Park Road.
