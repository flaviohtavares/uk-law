# The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1951 (C.36)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1994

Made

20th July 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 78(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 1993[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1994.

**2.** Section 72 of the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Backing of Warrants: Safeguards) shall come into force on 22nd August 1994.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

20th July 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd August 1994 section 72 of the Criminal Justice Act 1993. Section 72 amends the Backing of Warrants (Republic of Ireland) Act 1965 so as to allow provision to be made, by order, for a statutory speciality rule to be introduced into the backing of warrants arrangements with the Republic of Ireland. The Backing of Warrants (Republic of Ireland) (Rule of Speciality) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/1952](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1952)), which comes into force on 22nd August 1994, introduces such a rule into those arrangements.
