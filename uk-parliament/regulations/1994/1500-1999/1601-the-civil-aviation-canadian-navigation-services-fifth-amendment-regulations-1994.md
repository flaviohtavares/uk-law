# The Civil Aviation (Canadian Navigation Services) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1601

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Canadian Navigation Services) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

16th June 1994

Laid before Parliament

17th June 1994

Coming into force

1st July 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of his powers under section 73(1)(a), (4) and (6)(a) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Canadian Navigation Services) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

**2.** In regulation 4(1)(b) of the Civil Aviation (Canadian Navigation Services) Regulations 1986[^f00002] for the sum of “$83.65 Canadian” there shall be substituted the sum of “$92.02 Canadian”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mackay of Ardbrecknish

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

26th May 1994

We consent to the making of these Regulations.

Tim Wood

Tim Kirkhope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

16th June 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Canadian Navigation Services) Regulations 1986 as follows—

The charge for the use of air navigation services provided by or on behalf of the Government of Canada by the Edmonton Area Control Centre is increased from $83.65 Canadian to $92.02 Canadian.

Charges under the Regulations are payable to the Civil Aviation Authority which is required to remit them to the Government of Canada.
