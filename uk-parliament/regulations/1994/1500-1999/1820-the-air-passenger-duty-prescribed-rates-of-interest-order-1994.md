# The Air Passenger Duty (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1820

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Air Passenger Duty (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Order 1994

Made

8th July 1994

Laid before the House of Commons

11th July 1994

Coming into force

1st November 1994

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 11 of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Air Passenger Duty (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st November 1994.

**2.** For the purposes of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 1994 the prescribed rates shall be—

- (a) in the case of paragraph 7 (interest payable to the Commissioners of Customs and Excise), 5.5 per cent. per annum; and
- (b) in the case of paragraph 9 (interest payable by the Commissioners of Customs and Excise), 8 per cent. per annum.

Tim Wood

Timothy Kirkhope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

8th July 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st November 1994, prescribes the rate of interest for the purposes of paragraph 7 of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act [1994(c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/9) (interest on duty recovered or recoverable by assessment); and the rate of interest for the purposes of paragraph 9 of Schedule 6 to that Act (interest in certain cases of official error).
