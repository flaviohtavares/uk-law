# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) Regulations 1994, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. General business liabilities

The amount of the general business liabilities shall be determined in compliance with the rules applicable to such liabilities laid down in Part VI of Schedule 6 to the Friendly Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Regulations 1994[^f00019].
