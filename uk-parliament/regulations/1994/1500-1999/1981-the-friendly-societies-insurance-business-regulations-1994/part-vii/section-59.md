# The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) Regulations 1994, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Default in complying with regulations 55 to 58

(1) A society shall be guilty of an offence if it makes default in complying with any of regulations 55 to 58 above.

(2) A person shall be guilty of an offence if either—

- (a) he causes or permits to be included in a document deposited with the Commission under regulation 57(1) above a statement which he knows to be false in a material particular; or
- (b) he recklessly causes or permits to be so included a statement which is false in a material particular.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this regulation shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in Great Britain, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale; and
- (b) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding £2,000.
