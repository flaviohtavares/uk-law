# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1633

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1994

Made

22nd June 1994

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1994

Coming into force

31st August 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of June 1994

Present,

The Queens Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 1(1) and 1(4) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 31st August 1994.

**2.** Christopher Anthony Woodhead is hereby appointed Her Majestys Chief Inspector of Schools in England for a period of five years from 1st September 1994.

**3.** The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1992[^f00002]is hereby revoked.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Concil

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Christopher Anthony Woodhead to be Her Majestys Chief Inspector of Schools in England for a period of five years from 1st September 1994. He succeeds Professor Stewart Ross Sutherland who has resigned with effect from 31st August 1994 and who was appointed under the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/1295](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1295)).
