# The Finance Act 1994 (Appointed Day) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1773 (C.32)

INSURANCE PREMIUM TAX

The Finance Act 1994 (Appointed Day) Order 1994

Made

6th July 1994

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 61 and 74(1) of the Finance Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1994 (Appointed Day) Order 1994.

**2.** The day appointed as the day on which sections 59 and 60 of the Finance Act 1994 come into force is 1st October 1994.

Leonard Harris

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House,

22 Upper Ground,

LONDON,

SE1 9PJ

6th July 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1994 sections 59 (review of Commissioners' decisions) and 60 (appeals) of the Finance Act 1994.
