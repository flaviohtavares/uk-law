# The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 6) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1680

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 6) Order 1994

Made

24th June 1994

Laid before Parliament

24th June 1994

Coming into force

15th July 1994

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with sea fishing in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 30(2) and (3) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 6) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th July 1994.

(2) In this Order the principal Order means the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

The principal Order shall be amended in accordance with article 3 below.

**3.** In article 2 thereof (interpretation) there shall be substituted for the definition of the Council Regulation the following definition—

> - “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094) laying down certain technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources[^f00003], as amended by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4026/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4026)[^f00004], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2968/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/2968)[^f00005], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3953/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/3953)[^f00006], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1555/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/1555)[^f00007], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2024/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/2024)[^f00008], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3287/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/3287)[^f00009], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4193/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/4193)[^f00010], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2220/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/2220)[^f00011], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4056/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/4056)[^f00012], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3500/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3500)[^f00013], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 345/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/0345)[^f00014], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1465/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/1465)[^f00015], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2120/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2120)[^f00016], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3034/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/3034)[^f00017], and Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3676/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/3676)[^f00018];

.

## 4. Revocation

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 5) Order 1992[^f00019]is hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986, as amended (the principal Order), which makes provision for the enforcement of certain of the enforceable Community restrictions and obligations concerning technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources which are contained in Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094), as previously amended. The principal Order creates offences in respect of breaches of the provisions of that Council Regulation referred to in column 1 (and briefly described in column 2) of Schedule 1 to that Order and in article 3(2) of that Order. The principal Order also specifies penalties for such offences and confers powers of enforcement.

In consequence of that Council Regulation having been amended by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3676/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/3676), article 3 of this Order further updates the definition of the Council Regulation in article 2 of the principal Order (interpretation).

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 5) Order 1992 is superseded by this Order and is revoked (article 4).
