# The Reservoirs (Panels of Civil Engineers) (Application Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1533

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Reservoirs (Panels of Civil Engineers) (Application Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

8th June 1994

Coming into force

1st July 1994

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England, the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales and the Secretary of State for Scotland as respects Scotland, in exercise of powers conferred on them by sections 4(2) and 5 of the Reservoirs Act 1975[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Reservoirs (Panels of Civil Engineers) (Application Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1994, and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

## 2. Application fees

Regulation 2(2) of the Reservoirs (Panels of Civil Engineers) (Applications and Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£200” substitute “£230”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£160” substitute “£185”;

John Selwyn Gummer

Secretary of State for the Environment

17th May 1994

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

25th May 1994

Hector Monro

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Scottish Office

8th June 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Reservoirs (Panels of Civil Engineers) (Applications and Fees) Regulations 1992, which prescribe the manner in which a civil engineer may apply to be appointed or reappointed to an existing panel of civil engineers set up for the purposes of the Reservoirs Act 1975, and the fee to accompany such an application, by increasing the fee for a first appointment to any panel to £230, and the fee for reappointment to any panel to £185.
