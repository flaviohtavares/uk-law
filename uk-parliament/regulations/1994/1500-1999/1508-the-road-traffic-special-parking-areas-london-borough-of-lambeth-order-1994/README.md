# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Borough of Lambeth) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1508

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Borough of Lambeth) Order 1994

Made

6th June 1994

Laid before Parliament

13th June 1994

Coming into force

4th July 1994

Whereas the council of the London borough of Lambeth has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under section 76 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and the Secretary of State has consulted the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 76(2) of that Act:

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

6th June 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates areas in the London borough of Lambeth as special parking areas for the purposes of Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991 with effect from 4th July 1994. The Order also makes consequential modifications to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 and to Part II of the 1991 Act.
