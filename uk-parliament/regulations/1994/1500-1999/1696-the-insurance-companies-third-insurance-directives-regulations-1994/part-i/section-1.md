# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 1994-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1994/1696

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-4ff01ddb2497a81a40bdafd3d9242315]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

(2) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland.
