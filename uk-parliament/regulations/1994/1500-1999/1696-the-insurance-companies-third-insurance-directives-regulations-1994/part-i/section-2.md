# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 2

## 2. Interpretation[^c-4d9ecf2e077759835fa29f23190f1a10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “the 1982 Act” means the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00003];
- “the 1986 Act” means the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00004];
- “the commencement date” means 1st July 1994.

(2) In these Regulations expressions which are also used in the 1982 Act or the 1986 Act have the same meanings as in that Act.
