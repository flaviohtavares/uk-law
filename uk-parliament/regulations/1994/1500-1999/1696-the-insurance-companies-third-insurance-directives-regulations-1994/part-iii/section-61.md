# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Qualifications of designated agency

Paragraph 5 of Schedule 7 to the 1986 Act (qualifications of designated agency) shall have effect as if it included provision that, for the purpose of complying with the third long term insurance Directive, the agency must be able and willing to co-operate, by the sharing of information and otherwise, with supervisory authorities in other member States.
