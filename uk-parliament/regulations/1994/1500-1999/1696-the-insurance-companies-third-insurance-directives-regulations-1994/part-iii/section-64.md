# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Insurance contracts effected in contravention of 1982 Act

Section 132 of the 1986 Act (insurance contracts effected in contravention of section 2 of 1982 Act) shall have effect in relation to a contract of insurance which is entered into by a person—

- (a) in the course of carrying on insurance business in contravention of paragraph 1 or 4 of Schedule 2F to the 1982 Act or paragraph 1 of Schedule 2G to that Act; or
- (b) in the course of providing insurance in contravention of paragraph 8, 11, 18, 19 or 23 of the said Schedule 2F or paragraph 5, 9 or 11 of the said Schedule 2G,

as it has effect in relation to a contract of insurance which is entered into by a person in the course of carrying on insurance business in contravention of section 2 of the 1982 Act.
