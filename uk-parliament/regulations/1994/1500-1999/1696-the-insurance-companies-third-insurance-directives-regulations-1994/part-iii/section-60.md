# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Information and auditors

(1) Section 104 of the 1986 Act (power to call for information) shall have effect as if references to functions under that Act included references to functions under this Part of these Regulations.

(2) Section 107 of that Act[^f00048] (appointment of auditors) shall have effect as if the reference in subsection (1)(b) to a member of a recognised self-regulating organisation did not include a reference to an EC company.

## Annotations

[^f00048]: Footnote: Section 107 has been amended by the Companies Act [1989 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/40), section 206(1) and Schedule 23, Part I, para 10.
