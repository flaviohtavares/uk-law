# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 1994-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1994/1696

## 63. Application of 1986 Act to insurance companies[^c-70c3d0da1c0cc760ca8f4710a69c538e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 129 of the 1986 Act (application of investment business provisions to regulated insurance companies) shall have effect as if the reference to regulated insurance companies included a reference to EC companies.

(2) In paragraph 2 of Schedule 10 to that Act (regulated insurance companies) shall have effect as if—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (2), the reference to an insurance company to which Part II of the 1982 Act applies included a reference to an EC company; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (3A)[^f00051], the references to section 81B of that Act were references to paragraph 18 of Schedule 2F to that Act.
