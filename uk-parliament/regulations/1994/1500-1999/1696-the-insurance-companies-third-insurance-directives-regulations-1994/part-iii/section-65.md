# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Industrial assurance

Section 139(1) of the 1986 Act (industrial assurance) shall have effect as if the reference to carrying on insurance business in contravention of section 2 of the 1982 Act included a reference to carrying on insurance business in contravention of Part I of Schedule 2F to that Act.
