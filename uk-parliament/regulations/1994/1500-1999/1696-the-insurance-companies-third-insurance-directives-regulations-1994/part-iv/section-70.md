# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 1994-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1994/1696
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Transitional provisions: UK insurers[^c-af4ab5b6534afeb6cd02d9ce1e4839e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If—

- (a) an insurance company which immediately after the commencement date is a UK company, or
- (b) a member of Lloyd's,

was immediately before that date lawfully carrying on insurance business of a class or part of a class in a member State other than the United Kingdom, it shall be treated for the purposes of the 1982 Act as if the requirements of paragraph 1 of Schedule 2G to that Act had been complied with in relation to insurance business of that class or part of a class.

(2) If—

- (a) an insurance company which immediately after the commencement date is a UK company, or
- (b) a member of Lloyd's,

was immediately before that date lawfully providing insurance of a class or part of a class in a member State other than the United Kingdom, it shall be treated for the purposes of the 1982 Act as if the requirements of paragraph 5 of Schedule 2G to that Act had been complied with in relation to insurance of that class or part of a class.
