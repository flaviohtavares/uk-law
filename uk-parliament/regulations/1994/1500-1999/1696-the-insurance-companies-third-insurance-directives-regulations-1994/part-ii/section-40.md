# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies

(1) After section 72 of the 1982 Act insert—

> **72A. Information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies.**
>
> Schedule 2E to this Act (which makes provision with respect to information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies) shall have effect.

(2) After Schedule 2D to that Act insert Schedule 5 to these Regulations (information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies), as Schedule 2E.
