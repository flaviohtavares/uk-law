# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 26

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Restriction on disclosure of information[^c-330843517a14dcd6d5dee3e153c950f4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For section 47A of the 1982 Act[^f00013] substitute—

> **47A. Restriction on disclosure of information.**
>
> Schedule 2B to this Act (which, subject to certain exceptions, restricts the disclosure of information obtained under or by virtue of this Act) shall have effect.

(2) After Schedule 2A to that Act insert Schedule 2 to these Regulations (restriction on disclosure of information), as Schedule 2B.
