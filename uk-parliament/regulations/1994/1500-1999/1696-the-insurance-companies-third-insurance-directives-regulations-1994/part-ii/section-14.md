# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 1994-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1994/1696
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Margins of solvency[^c-754374256894852ebf7e21970c8b268d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In subsection (3) of section 32 of the 1982 Act[^f00008] (margins of solvency), for the words “Community margin of solvency” substitute the words “EEA margin of solvency”.

(2) In subsections (5) and (6) of that section—

- (a) for the words “Community margin of solvency” substitute the words “EEA margin of solvency”; and
- (b) for the words “in member States (taken together)” substitute the words “in EEA States (taken together)”.

(3) In subsections (1) and (3) of section 33 of that Act (failure to maintain minimum margin), for the words “Community margin of solvency” substitute the words “EEA margin of solvency”.
