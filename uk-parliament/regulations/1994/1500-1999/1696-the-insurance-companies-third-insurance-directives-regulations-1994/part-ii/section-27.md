# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Privilege from disclosure

In subsection (1) of section 47B of the 1982 Act[^f00014] (privilege from disclosure), for the words “section 44(2) to (4)” substitute the words “section 43A or 44(2) to (4)”.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: Section 47B was inserted by the Companies Consolidation (Consequential Provisions) Act [1985 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/9), section 25.
