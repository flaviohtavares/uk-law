## 27. Privilege from disclosure[^c-813a8c07c438b7a908a796fdcdb98bc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subsection (1) of section 47B of the 1982 Act[^f00014] (privilege from disclosure), for the words “section 44(2) to (4)” substitute the words “section 43A or 44(2) to (4)”.
