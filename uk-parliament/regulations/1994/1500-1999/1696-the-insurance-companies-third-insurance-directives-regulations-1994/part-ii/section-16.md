# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Form and situation of assets

In subsection (2) of section 35 of the 1982 Act[^f00009] (form and situation of assets), for the words “an insurance company whose head office is in a member State” substitute the words “an insurance company whose head office is in an EFTA State”.
