# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Change of manager etc. of company from outside United Kingdom

(1) In subsection (1) of section 63 of the 1982 Act[^f00020] (change of manager etc.of company from outside United Kingdom), for the words from “an insurance company” to “in relation to” substitute the words “a non-EC company whose head office is in an EFTA State or”.

(2) Section 63A of that Act[^f00021] (duty to notify change of control) shall cease to have effect.
