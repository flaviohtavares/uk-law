# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 1994-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1994/1696
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers[^c-ea5a895632ffd41457822f25aaba0914]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For sections 81B to 81J of the 1982 Act[^f00027] substitute—

> **81B. Recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers.**
>
> Schedule 2G to this Act (which makes provision for or in connection with the recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers) shall have effect.

(2) After Schedule 2F to that Act insert Schedule 7 to these Regulations (recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers), as Schedule 2G.
