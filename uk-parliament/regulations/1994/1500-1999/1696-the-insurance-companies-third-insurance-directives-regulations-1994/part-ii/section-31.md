# The Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Winding up on petition by Secretary of State

(1) In subsection (1) of section 54 of the 1982 Act (winding up on petition by Secretary of State), for paragraph (bb)[^f00017] substitute—

> - (bb) that the company is a UK company and has failed to satisfy an obligation to which it is subject by virtue of any provision of the law of another EEA State which—
>   - (i) gives effect to the general or long term insurance Directives; or
>   - (ii) is otherwise applicable to the insurance activities of the company in that State;

.

(2) In subsection (2) of that section, for paragraph (bb)[^f00018] substitute—

> - (bb) that the company is a UK company and has failed to satisfy an obligation to which it is subject by virtue of any provision of the law of another EEA State which—
>   - (i) gives effect to the general or long term insurance Directives; or
>   - (ii) is otherwise applicable to the insurance activities of the company in that State;

.
