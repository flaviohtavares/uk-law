# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Particulars of changes of director, controller or manager, etc.

The particulars prescribed for the purposes of sections 60(1), 61(1) and 62(1) of the Act[^f00014] shall be those set out in Schedule 6 to these Regulations.
