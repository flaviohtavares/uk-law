# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Default in complying with regulation 76(1)

A UK or non-EC company commits an offence if it makes default in complying with regulation 76(1) above and is liable, on summary conviction in England and Wales and Scotland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale and, on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding £2,000.
