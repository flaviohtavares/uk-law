# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Long term class V

For long term business of class V the required margin of solvency shall be equal to 1 per cent. of the assets of the relevant tontine.
