# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Making and amount of deposit

Every deposit made pursuant to section 9(1)(c) of the Act shall (subject to section 9(2)(b) of the Act) be made with the Accountant General and shall be maintained by the depositor at a level equal to at least the minimum.
