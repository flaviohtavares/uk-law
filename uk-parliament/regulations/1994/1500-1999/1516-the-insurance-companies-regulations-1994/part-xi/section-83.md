# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. Application of regulations 80 to 82 to Lloyd's

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) below, regulations 80 to 82 above apply in relation to Lloyd’s as they apply in relation to a UK company.

(2) The information required in the case of Lloyd’s to be included in—

- (a) the statements and underwriting account referred to in regulation 80(7) or the notification referred to in regulation 80(9); or
- (b) the statement referred to in regulation 81(4) or the notification referred to in regulation 81(6),

is that relating to the members of Lloyd’s taken together.

(3) Any such statements, underwriting account or notification shall be signed by the Chairman or a Deputy Chairman, for and on behalf of the Council of Lloyd's.
