# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Reversionary interests etc.

The value of any long term business asset of a company consisting of an interest in property which is a remainder, reversionary interest, right of fee subject to a liferent or other future interest, whether vested or contingent, shall be the amount which would reasonably be paid by way of consideration for an immediate transfer or assignment thereof.
