# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. General business liabilities

The amount of the general business liabilities shall be determined in compliance with the rules laid down in Section D of Schedule 9A to the Companies Act 1985[^f00037].
