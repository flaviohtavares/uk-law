# The Insurance Companies Regulations 1994, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Rates of mortality and disability

The amount of the liability in respect of any category of contract shall, where relevant, be determined on the basis of prudent rates of mortality and disability that have regard to the State of the commitment.
