# The British Nationality (South Africa) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1634

BRITISH NATIONALITY

The British Nationality (South Africa) Order 1994

Made

22nd June 1994

Laid before Parliament

4th July 1994

Coming into force

26th July 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of June 1994

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred upon Her by section 37(2) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the British Nationality (South Africa) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 26th July 1994.

**2.** Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act 1981 (countries whose citizens are Commonwealth citizens) shall be amended by inserting the words “South Africa” under the words “Solomon Islands”.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(this note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds South Africa to Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act 1981 with the result that citizens of South Africa shall have the status of Commonwealth citizens.
