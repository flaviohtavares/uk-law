# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Supervision of the prison and control of prisoners

(1) Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the Governor shall be responsible for–

- (a) the supervision of the whole prison; and
- (b) the control of prisoners confined therein.

(2) The Governor shall as far as practicable visit and inspect daily those parts of the prison where prisoners are employed or accommodated.
