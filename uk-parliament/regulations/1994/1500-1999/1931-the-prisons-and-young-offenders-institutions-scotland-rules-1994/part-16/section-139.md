# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 139

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 139. Annual report

(1) The visiting committee shall make an annual report for the period of 12 months ending on 31st March each year to the Secretary of State concerning the state of the prison and its administration and may include in it any advice and suggestions it considers appropriate.

(2) The said annual report shall be delivered as soon as possible after the last day of March in each year.
