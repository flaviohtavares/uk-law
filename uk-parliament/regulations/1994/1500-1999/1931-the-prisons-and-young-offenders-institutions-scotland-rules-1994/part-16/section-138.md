# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 138

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 138. Inspection of prison records

(1) The visiting committee or any member of the committee may inspect prison records other than–

- (a) personnel records;
- (b) prisoners' records; and
- (c) security manuals or other papers which have implications for security.

(2) The visiting committee shall record particulars of any inspection of prison records in its minute book.
