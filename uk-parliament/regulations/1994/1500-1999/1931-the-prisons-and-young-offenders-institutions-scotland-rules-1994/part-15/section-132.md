# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Code of Discipline

The Secretary of State may approve a Code of Discipline to have effect in relation to officers and employees, or such categories of officers and employees as it may specify, setting out the offences against discipline, the awards which may be made in respect of them and the procedure for dealing with charges and any appeals against findings of guilt or disciplinary awards.
