# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 128

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 128. Transactions with prisoners or in connection with the prison

(1) No officer or employee shall take part in any business or pecuniary transaction with, or on behalf of, a prisoner, except with the authority of the Secretary of State.

(2) Except with the authority of the Governor, no officer or employee shall–

- (a) bring in or take out, or attempt to bring in or take out, or knowingly allow to be brought in or taken out, to or for any prisoner; or
- (b) deposit in any place with intent that it shall come into the possession of any prisoner,

any article whatsoever.
