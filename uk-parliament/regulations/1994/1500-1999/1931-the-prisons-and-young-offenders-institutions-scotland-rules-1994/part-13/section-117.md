# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Pre-release preparation

The Governor shall at the appropriate time discuss with every prisoner his immediate needs on release and may also arrange for any other person to see the prisoner to discuss such needs.
