# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Circumstances where prisoners are disqualified from fi consideration for temporary release

A prisoner shall be disqualified from being considered for temporary release in terms of rules 120 to 123 if he is for the time being–

- (a) an appellant;
- (b) subject to proceedings under the Extradition Act 1989;
- (c) in the opinion of the medical officer, suffering from mental disorder; or
- (d) in the opinion of the medical officer, otherwise unfit.
