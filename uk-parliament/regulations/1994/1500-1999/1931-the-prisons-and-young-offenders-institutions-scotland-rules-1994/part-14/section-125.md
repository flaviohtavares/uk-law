# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Recall of prisoners granted temporary release

The Secretary of State may make an order recalling to prison any prisoner who has been granted temporary release, whether the conditions upon which he has been granted such release have been broken or not.
