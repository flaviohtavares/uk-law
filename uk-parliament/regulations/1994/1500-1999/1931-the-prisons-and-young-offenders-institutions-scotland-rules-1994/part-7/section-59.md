# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Visits by procurators fiscal

(1) A procurator fiscal or any person authorised by him may, for the purpose of discharging his public duties, visit and examine a prisoner at any reasonable time.

(2) A visit to a prisoner by a procurator fiscal or any person authorised by him may take place in such area of the prison and under such conditions as the Governor may specify except that such a visit shall take place–

- (a) within the sight of an officer; but
- (b) outwith the hearing of any officer.
