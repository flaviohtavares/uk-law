# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Application of Part 8

(1) Rules 67 to 70, 72(3) and 73(2) shall not apply to untried and civil prisoners.

(2) Rules 67 to 70, 72(3), 73(2) and 74 shall not apply to young prisoners.
