# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Earnings

Where—

- (a) a prisoner undertakes work in terms of rule 68 or 71; or
- (b) a prisoner (other than an untried or civil prisoner) takes part in an educational class arranged in terms of rule 72 or any counselling provided in terms of rule 73,

he shall be entitled to be paid earnings at such rates and in accordance with such conditions as may be specified in a direction by the Secretary of State.
