# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Work undertaken by untried and civil prisoners

(1) An untried prisoner or a civil prisoner shall not be required to work in prison but may undertake, if he so chooses and with the agreement of the Governor, such work or an educational class arranged in terms of rule 72.

(2) Where an untried prisoner or a civil prisoner undertakes work, he shall be entitled to be paid earnings in accordance with rule 74.

(3) Except with the authority of the Governor and with the consent of the prisoner, no untried prisoner or civil prisoner shall work in the service of another prisoner or of an officer.
