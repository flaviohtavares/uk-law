# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Requests and complaints to the visiting committee

Every prisoner intimating to an officer his desire to write a letter of request or complaint to the visiting committee shall be supplied with paper for the purpose, and the Governor shall ensure that every such letter is posted without delay.
