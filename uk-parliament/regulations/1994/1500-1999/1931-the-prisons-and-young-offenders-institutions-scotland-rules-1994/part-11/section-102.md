# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Requests to speak to an officer of the Secretary of State, a member of the visiting committee, a sheriff or a justice of the peace

(1) Where a prisoner makes a request to speak to–

- (a) an officer of the Secretary of State;
- (b) a member of the visiting committee, or
- (c) a sheriff or a justice of the peace visiting the prison in terms of section 15 of the Act,

the request shall be recorded by the officer to whom it is made and conveyed without delay to the Governor.

(2) The Governor shall inform any such person as mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) to (c) of paragraph (1) as soon as reasonably practicable of any request by a prisoner to speak to him.
