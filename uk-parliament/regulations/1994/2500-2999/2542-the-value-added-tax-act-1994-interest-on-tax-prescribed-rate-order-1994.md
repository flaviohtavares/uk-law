# The Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2542

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) Order 1994

Made

28th September 1994

Laid before the House of Commons

30th September 1994

Coming into force

6th October 1994

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 74(6) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 6th October1994.

**2.** For the purposes of subsections (1), (2) and (4) of section 74 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 the prescribed rate shall be 6.25 per cent.

Derek Conway

Tim Wood

Two of the Lords Commissioners

28th September 1994of Her Majesty’s Treasury

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of section 74 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (interest on VAT recovered or recoverable by assessment) from 5.50 per cent. to 6.25 per cent. with effect from 6th October 1994.
