# The National Lottery etc. Act 1993 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2659 (C. 58)

NATIONAL LOTTERY

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The National Lottery etc. Act 1993 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994

Made

10th October 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 65 of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994.

**2.** Part IV of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 shall come into force on 14th November 1994.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majestys Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

10th October 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 14th November 1994 Part IV of the National Lottery etc. Act 1994. The provisions of Part IV relate to pool betting and make amendments to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act [1963 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1963/2).

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order has been preceded by the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/2632](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2632)) and by the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/1055](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1055)). Those Orders brought certain provisions of that Act into force on four different dates.
