# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 12

## 12 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Notification to appropriate nature conservation body (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-05cfd7ad8ccb87a1dd8232ffd39445e3][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Secretary of State shall notify the appropriate nature conservation body as soon as may be *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] after—[^key-a48d7ae6403fb4cf1e1c589da5d21555] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a site becomes a European site; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] the specification of a European site is amended; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a site ceases to fall within any of the categories listed in regulation 11(2). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-b3e534231b1f9f142569352d1b8e01dc] Except where arrangements are made authorising the appropriate conservation body to establish and maintain the register, the notification under paragraph (1) of a site becoming a European site must be accompanied by a copy of the register entry. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-7369de6ab3139f5937dc3ff90afb31a1] Except where arrangements are made authorising the appropriate conservation body to establish and maintain the register, the notification under paragraph (1) of the amendment of the specification of a European site must be accompanied by a copy of the amended entry. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Each nature conservation body shall keep copies of the register entries relating to European sites in their area available for public inspection at all reasonable hours and free of charge. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
