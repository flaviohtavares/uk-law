# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 36

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Byelaws for protection of European marine site (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-276f98ead3e315f9e435b14505ec617b][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate nature conservation body may make byelaws for the protection of a European marine site under section 37 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (byelaws for protection of marine nature reserves). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2) The provisions of subsections (2) to (11) of that section apply in relation to byelaws made by virtue of this regulation with the substitution for the references to marine nature reserves of references to European marine sites. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3) Nothing in byelaws made by virtue of this regulation shall interfere with the exercise of any functions of a relevant authority, any functions conferred by or under an enactment (whenever passed) or any right of any person (whenever vested). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
