# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Chapter 3 of Part 2 of the 2004 Act (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2fa549cc64761b117328c54eb07b3e4a][^key-e8e70e094f2f6bb85c133fb6043cb6d2][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of Chapter 3 of Part 2 of the 2004 Act apply for the purposes of these Regulations as if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to a “management agreement” included a reference to a management agreement entered into under regulation 16 of these Regulations; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in section 29(2)(b) (proposals for land management orders) for “specified in an SSSI notification” there was substituted *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] “ by reason of which land is a European site ” *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in section 36(3)(a) (offences in relation to land management orders) the reference to “£40,000” was a reference to “level 5 on the standard scale”. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
