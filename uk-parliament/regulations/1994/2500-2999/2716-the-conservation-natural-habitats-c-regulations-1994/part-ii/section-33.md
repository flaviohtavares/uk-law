# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 33

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Marking of site and advice by nature conservation bodies (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ea18b6afcde7c092c4ee7311fad74b44][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The appropriate nature conservation body may *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] deposit or remove[^key-d07fdf2854e3cca5106ccfd9b7c69f10] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] markers indicating the existence and extent of a European marine site. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

This power is exercisable subject to the obtaining of any necessary *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] marine licence under Part 4 of the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010[^key-792ae894da37642a80566b199121d7d2] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] As soon as possible after a site becomes a European marine site, the appropriate nature conservation body shall advise other relevant authorities as to– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] the conservation objectives for that site, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any operations which may cause deterioration of natural habitats or the habitats of species, or disturbance of species, for which the site has been designated. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
