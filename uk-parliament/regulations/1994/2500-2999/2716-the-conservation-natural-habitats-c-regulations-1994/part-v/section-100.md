# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 100

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 100 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Attempts and possession of means of committing offence (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c5b2cbb70058f9989cc6b22dee132864][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] A person who attempts to commit an offence under Part III of these Regulations is guilty of an offence and punishable in like manner as for that offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] A person who, for the purposes of committing an offence under Part III of these Regulations, has in his possession anything capable of being used for committing the offence is guilty of an offence and punishable in like manner as for that offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] References below to an offence under Part III include an offence under this regulation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
