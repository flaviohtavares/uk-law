# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 115

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 115. Regulations[^c-cc32a5b873bd5e57926dad5605e38883]

*Extent: S*

(1) Regulations made under regulation 113(2) amending schedule 2, or under regulation 114 amending Annex IV to the Habitats Directive, are subject to the affirmative procedure (see section 29 of the Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010).

(2) Regulations made in any other case are subject to the negative procedure.
