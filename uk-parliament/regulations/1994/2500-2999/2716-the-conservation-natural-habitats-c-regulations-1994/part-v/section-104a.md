# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 104A

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 104A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Application of criminal offences to the Crown (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-90e109ed2085d2a9f7159ae0809ede45][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] No contravention by the Crown of any provision of these Regulations makes the Crown criminally liable; but the Court of Session may, on the application of any public body or office holder having responsibility for enforcing that provision, declare unlawful any act or omission of the Crown which constitutes such a contravention. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Despite paragraph (1), the provisions of these Regulations apply to persons in the public service of the Crown as they apply to other persons. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
