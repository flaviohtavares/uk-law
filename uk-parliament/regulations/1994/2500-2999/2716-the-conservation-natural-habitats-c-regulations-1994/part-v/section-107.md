# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 107

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 107 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Local inquiries (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e90cadb33eadbc671efe3f59e78fc759][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Secretary of State may cause a local inquiry to be held for the purposes of the exercise of any of his functions under these Regulations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The provisions of section 250(2) to (5) of the Local Government Act 1972 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00112] or section 210(4) to (8) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00113] (local inquiries: evidence and costs) apply in relation to an inquiry held under this regulation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
