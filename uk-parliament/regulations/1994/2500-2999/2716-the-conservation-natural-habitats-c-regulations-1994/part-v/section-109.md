# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 109

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 109 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Advisory role of the JNCC (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c82d5cccaeb32b91f97628501d0d9927][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Joint Nature Conservation Committee may provide advice or make representations to any competent authority in relation to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any question as to whether that authority is obliged to carry out an appropriate assessment in relation to a European offshore marine site under these Regulations; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any appropriate assessment on which that authority is obliged to consult the Committee under these Regulations; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any application made pursuant to regulation 62 of these Regulations and sent to the Committee by that authority pursuant to regulation 62(4); and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any decision of the Secretary of State in respect of which notice has been served on it by him under regulation 72(2)(b), 76(2)(b) or 80(2)(b) of these Regulations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Joint Nature Conservation Committee may undertake, commission or support (whether by financial means or otherwise) such research and scientific work as it considers is required for the purposes of providing advice or making representations under this regulation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In this regulation and regulation 110 of these Regulations, “research” includes inquiries and investigations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
