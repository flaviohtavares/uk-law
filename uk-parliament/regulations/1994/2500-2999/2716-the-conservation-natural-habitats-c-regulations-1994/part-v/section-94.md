## 94 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Procedure for making byelaws, penalties, &c. (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2c86fc0809868bf1b89f56f8d4bf8d11][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Sections 236 to 238 of the Local Government Act 1972 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00104] or sections 201 to 204 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00105] (procedure, &c. for byelaws; offences against byelaws; evidence of byelaws) apply to all byelaws made under section 20 of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00106] as it applies by virtue of regulation 28 as if the appropriate nature conservation body were a local authority within the meaning of that Act. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In relation to byelaws so made the confirming authority for the purposes of the said section 236 or section 201 shall be the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The appropriate nature conservation body shall have power to enforce byelaws made by them: *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

Provided that nothing in this paragraph shall be construed as authorising the institution of proceedings in Scotland for an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
