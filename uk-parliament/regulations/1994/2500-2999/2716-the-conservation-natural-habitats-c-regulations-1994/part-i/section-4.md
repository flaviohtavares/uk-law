# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 4

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Nature conservation bodies (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-707057b892d31708b2d4469fd11ff93d][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Subject to paragraph (2), in these Regulations “nature conservation body” means Natural England, the Countryside Council for Wales or Scottish Natural Heritage; and references to “the appropriate nature conservation body”, in relation to England, Wales or Scotland shall be construed accordingly. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In Parts IV and IVA of these Regulations, “nature conservation body” also includes the Joint Nature Conservation Committee, and references to “the appropriate nature conservation body” in relation to a European offshore marine site shall be construed as meaning the Joint Nature Conservation Committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
