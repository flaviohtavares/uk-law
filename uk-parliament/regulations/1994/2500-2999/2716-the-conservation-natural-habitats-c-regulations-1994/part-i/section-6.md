# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 6

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Competent authorities generally (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d8d31e9cacdb1763471393bed76ff780][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] For the purposes of these Regulations the expression “competent authority” includes any Minister, government department, public or statutory undertaker, public body of any description or person holding a public office. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

The expression also includes any person exercising any function of a competent authority in the United Kingdom. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In paragraph (1)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] “public body” includes any local authority, joint board or joint committee; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] “public office” means– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
  - (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an office under Her Majesty, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
  - (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an office created or continued in existence by a public general Act of Parliament, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
  - (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an office the remuneration in respect of which is paid out of money provided by Parliament *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] or money paid out of the Scottish Consolidated Fund[^c-fbb993313bf114969ab7b08f243c97c9] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In paragraph (2)(a)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

“local authority”– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in relation to England, means a county council, district council or London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London, the sub–treasurer of the Inner Temple, the under treasurer of the Middle Temple or a parish council, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in relation to Wales, means a county council, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] county borough, *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-82756005612e8e07ff0dd5d7d05250c4] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] council or community council, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-584d5661f8720f5bd1d07b026b42f2c3][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- “joint board” and “joint committee” in relation to England and Wales mean– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
  - (a) a joint or special planning board constituted for a National Park by order under paragraph 1 or 3 of Schedule 17 to the Local Government Act 1972 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00027], or a joint planning board within the meaning of section 2 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
  - (b) a joint committee appointed under section 102(1)(b) of the Local Government Act 1972, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

  and in relation to Scotland have the same meaning as in the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00028]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
