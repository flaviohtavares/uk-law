# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 5

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Relevant authorities in relation to marine areas and European marine sites (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_I_323dc2b2-d554-48ab-b12b-44ccfc7d3258][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of these Regulations the relevant authorities, in relation to a marine area or European marine site, are such of the following as have functions in relation to land or waters within or adjacent to that area or site– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a nature conservation body; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a county council, district council, London borough council or, in Scotland, a regional, islands or district council; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] the National Rivers Authority, a water undertaker or sewerage undertaker, or an internal drainage board; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a navigation authority within the meaning of the Water Resources Act 1991 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00024]; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a harbour authority within the meaning of the Harbours Act 1964 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00025]; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (f (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a lighthouse authority; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (g (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] the Scottish Environment Protection Agency *(Extent: S)*[^V_S_e3ae18c9-0d9d-d8dc-f6f7-cf443d9a90b3] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] or a district salmon fishery board; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (h (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a local fisheries committee constituted under the Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00026] or any authority exercising the powers of such a committee; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: S*

- (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^V_S_0b9047ca-6f5e-9060-4f5a-c84323e908f6][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a National Park authority established by order under section 6 of the National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
