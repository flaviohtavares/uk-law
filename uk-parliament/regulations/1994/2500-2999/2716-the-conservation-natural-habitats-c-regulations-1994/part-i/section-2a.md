# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 2A

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Interpretation: the Directives (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-85698affdea67d511ac1a9f0c9af2173][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Habitats Directive is to be construed for the purposes of these Regulations as if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to “the European territory of the Member States to which the Treaty applies” included a reference to the United Kingdom; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to “Member State” or “Member States” included a reference to the United Kingdom; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to “of Community interest” included, in relation to the United Kingdom, a reference to “of national interest”; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in Article 1(d) and (h), references to “the Community” were references to “the European Union or the United Kingdom”; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] in Article 1(l), the reference to “a site of Community importance designated by the Member States” included, in relation to the United Kingdom, a reference to a site of national importance designated under any of the retained transposing regulations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Wild Birds Directive is to be construed for the purposes of these Regulations as if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to “the European territory of the Member States to which the Treaty applies” included a reference to the United Kingdom; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any reference to “Member State” or “Member States” included a reference to the United Kingdom. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Any reference in these Regulations to “the requirements of the Directives” is to be construed as if the objectives of the Directives included the objectives referred to in regulation 9D(2). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Scottish Ministers may, after consultation with the appropriate nature conservation body and such other bodies or persons as they consider appropriate, issue guidance as to the interpretation of the requirements of the Directives. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Any guidance issued under paragraph (4) must be published by the Scottish Ministers in such manner as they consider appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
