## 3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Duties relating to compliance with the Directives (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-656f1ed9d03c92336bd175e9a04bd4b8][^key-f692c4605ae0844609ca565f5c0d1f5e][^key-16f082cd3e6a9b373f620ae21b631025][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The Scottish Ministers, the appropriate nature conservation body and, in relation to the Scottish marine area, a competent authority must exercise their functions which are relevant to nature conservation, including marine conservation, so as to secure compliance with the requirements of the Directives. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Without prejudice to the preceding provisions, a competent authority, in exercising their functions, must have regard to the requirements of the Directives so far as they may be affected by the exercise of those functions. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: S*

(3A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7a4964ac59a373db7a57d84f59cb1443][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] The duties in paragraphs (1) and (3) do not apply to functions under regulation 53ZA (compensatory measures for relevant offshore wind activity). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-1cc99481eed36ad74ab1ae08c96338d9][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] In complying with their duties under paragraphs (1) and (3), the Scottish Ministers, the appropriate nature conservation body and a competent authority must have regard to any guidance issued under regulation 2A(4). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
