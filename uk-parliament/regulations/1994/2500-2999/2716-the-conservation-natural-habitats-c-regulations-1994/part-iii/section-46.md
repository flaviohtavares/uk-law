## 46 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). False statements made for obtaining licence (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5e24c16d93524b0d8d66719bb7656c2d][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] A person commits an offence who, for the purposes of obtaining, whether for himself or another, the grant of a licence under regulation 44— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] makes a statement or representation, or furnishes a document or information, which he knows to be false in a material particular, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] recklessly makes a statement or representation, or furnishes a document or information, which is false in a material particular. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] A person guilty of an offence under this regulation is liable on summary conviction *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or *(Extent: S)*[^V_S_4746fc23-6a58-a7c6-3a01-aafa54eaa1dd] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] to a fine not exceeding level *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] 5 *(Extent: S)*[^V_S_23351e5d-7fa4-6920-7f01-1e2f6c4a4bd1] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] on the standard scale *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] , or to both *(Extent: S)*[^V_S_3f30bfa9-f8ff-ed95-08d5-291848367f7e] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
