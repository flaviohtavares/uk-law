# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 42

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). European protected species of plants (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-70ede7999e054695930b6d7e5674d70f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The species of plants listed in Annex *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] IV *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f](b) to the Habitats Directive whose natural range includes any area in Great Britain are listed in Schedule 4 to these Regulations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

References in these Regulations to a “European protected species” of plant are to any of those species. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
