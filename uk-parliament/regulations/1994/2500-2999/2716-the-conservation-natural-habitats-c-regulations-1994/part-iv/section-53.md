# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 53

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Compensatory measures (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ee9ff2ec3201ba6de0ec0062011279b9][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-770a26be7ecfa34c2232e4a1637915d7][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Where in accordance with regulation 49 (considerations of overriding public interest)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a plan or project is agreed to, notwithstanding a negative assessment of the implications for a European site *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] or European offshore marine site[^key-90fea229592af3af26976f5bf5ad8b74] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f], or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] a decision, or a consent, permission or other authorisation, is affirmed on review, notwithstanding such an assessment, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

the Secretary of State shall secure that any necessary compensatory measures are taken to ensure that the overall coherence of Natura 2000 is protected. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2)[^key-8055e82733940896e511828b4403d376] This regulation does not apply in respect of relevant offshore wind activity.
