## 59 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Planning permission: supplementary provisions as to compensation (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-df59996612562ff7442eb906adb31b59][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Where the Secretary of State determines not to confirm– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 97 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (revocation or modification of planning permission) which has taken effect under regulation 57(1), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 42 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 (revocation or modification of planning permission) which has taken effect under regulation 58(1), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

and claim for compensation under section 107 of the Act of 1990 or section 153 of the Act of 1972 shall be limited to any loss or damage directly attributable to the permission being suspended or temporarily modified for the duration of the period between the order so taking effect and the Secretary of State determining not to confirm the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Where the Secretary of State determines not to confirm– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 102 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (order requiring discontinuance of use, &c.) which has taken effect under regulation 57(3) above, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 49 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 (order requiring discontinuance of use, &c.) which has taken effect under regulation 58(3) above, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

any claim for compensation under section 115 of the Act of 1990 or section 159 of the Act of 1972 shall be limited to any loss or damage directly attributable to any right to continue a use of the land being, by virtue of the order, suspended or subject to conditions for the duration of the period between the order so taking effect and the Secretary of State determining not to confirm the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Where compensation is payable in respect of– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 97 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any order mentioned in section 115(1) of that Act (compensation in respect of orders under s.102, &c.), or to which that section applies by virtue of section 115(5), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

and the order has been made pursuant to regulation 50, the question as to the amount of the compensation shall be referred, by the authority liable to pay the compensation, to and be determined by the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Upper Tribunal[^key-5218af66c8bf4d5368b149e7b164ded2] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] unless and to the extent that in any particular case the Secretary of State has indicated in writing that such a reference and determination may be dispensed with. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Where compensation is payable in respect of– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] an order under section 42 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 (revocation or modification of planning permission), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] any order mentioned in section 153(1) of that Act (compensation in respect of orders under s.49), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

and the order has been made pursuant to regulation 50, the question as to the amount of the compensation shall be referred, by the authority liable to pay the compensation, to and be determined by the Lands Tribunal for Scotland unless and to the extent that in any particular case the Secretary of State has indicated in writing that such a reference and determination may be dispensed with. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
