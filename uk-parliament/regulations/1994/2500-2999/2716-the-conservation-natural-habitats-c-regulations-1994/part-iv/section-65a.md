# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 65A

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Simplified planning zones and European offshore marine sites (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0dcbebd011211c43d337365e98bb390b][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The adoption or approval of a simplified planning zone scheme on or after 21st August 2007 shall not have effect to grant planning permission for development which is likely to have a significant effect on a European offshore marine site (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
