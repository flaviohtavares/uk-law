## 60 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). General development orders (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-db7a1250ef47e2c174ed631ac7a32d63][^key-c255816fc5ba3adbc9e6a1e176028213][^key-3b7c68468b4c8d61e8ca7862d8554b5c][^key-abedfb1624563513e9d0d92c10ae5069][^key-8eee07c75b8f968d349d86ac8a8ef7c9][^key-ccae13a7f6c4eecf3b8bb4aa3e941ea1][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] It shall be a condition of any planning permission granted by a general development order, whether made before or after the commencement of these Regulations, that development which– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is likely to have a significant effect on a European site in Great Britian *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] or a European offshore marine site[^key-10c3bf283767d26a2d0719cc0a98fb86] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

shall not be begun until the developer has received written notification of the approval of the local planning authority under regulation 62. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] It shall be a condition of any planning permission granted by a general development order made before the commencement of these Regulations that development which– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is likely to have a significant effect on a European site in Great Britain (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

and which was begun but not completed before the commencement of these Regulations, shall not be continued until the developer has received written notification of the approval of the local planning authority under regulation 62. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] Nothing in this regulation shall affect anything done before the commencement of these Regulations. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
