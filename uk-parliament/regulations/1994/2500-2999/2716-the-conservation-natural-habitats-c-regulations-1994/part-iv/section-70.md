# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994, section 70

> Text as at 2026-10-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Cycle tracks and other ancillary works (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-967241704ba639a4e6a4c31c6d6c9098][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

As from the commencement of these Regulations, section 3(10) of the Cycle Tracks Act 1984 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f][^f00081] and section 152(4) of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 shall cease to have effect to deem planning permission to be granted for development which– *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is likely to have a significant effect on a European site in Great Britain (either alone or in combination with other plans or projects), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f] is not directly connected with or necessary to the management of the site, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]

whether or not the development authorised by the permission has been begun. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2dd38f22e3f0e7272da0337fa848725f]
