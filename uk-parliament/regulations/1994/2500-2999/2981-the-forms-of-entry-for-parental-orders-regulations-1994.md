# The Forms of Entry for Parental Orders Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2981

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC. ENGLAND AND WALES

The Forms of Entry for Parental Orders Regulations 1994

Made

23rd November 1994

Laid before Parliament

24th November 1994

Coming into force

15th December 1994

The Registrar General in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(5) of, and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the Adoption Act 1976[^f00001] as applied, with modifications, by regulation 2 of and paragraphs 6 and 8(a) of Schedule 1 to the Parental Orders (Human Fertilisation and Embryology) Regulations 1994[^f00002], with the approval of the Secretary of State in accordance with section 67(6) of that Act as applied with modifications by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to those Regulations, of powers conferred by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Forms of Entry for Parental Orders Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 15th December 1994.

## 2. Form of entry in the Parental Order Register

The entry to be made in the Parental Order Register pursuant to a direction made by—

- (a) a court sitting in England, shall be in the form set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
- (b) a court sitting in Wales, shall be in the form set out in Sched ule 2 to these Regulations.

P J Wormald

Registrar General

23rd November 1994

I approve,

Virginia Bottomley

Secretary of State for Health

23rd November 1994

## SCHEDULE 2

Regulation 2(b)

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2981/images/uksi_19942981_en_002)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the forms of the entries to be made in the Parental Order Register pursuant to directions made by a court granting a parental order under section 30 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act [1990 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/37). A parental order may be granted in respect of a child who is born as a result of a surrogacy arrangement and who is genetically related to at least one of the applicants for the order.

The [Parental Order (Human Fertilisation and Embryology) Regulations 1994 (S. I. 1994/2767)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2767) make provision, by paragraph 4(a) of Schedule 1 to those Regulations, for a certified copy of an entry in the Register to be received as evidence of the date and place of birth of the child in all respects as if it were a certified copy of an entry in the Registers of Births.
