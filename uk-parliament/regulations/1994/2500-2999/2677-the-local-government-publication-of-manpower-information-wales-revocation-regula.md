# The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2677

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1994

Made

17th October 1994

Laid before Parliament

18th October 1994

Coming into force

9th November 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2), (4) and (7) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with such associations of authorities to whom section 2 of that Act applies as appeared to him to be concerned and any such authority with whom consultation appeared to him to be desirable, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 9th November 1994.

## 2. Revocation of 1983 Regulations

The Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) Regulations 1983[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Gwilym Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

17th October 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) Regulations 1983, which required local authorities to publish the information specified in the Local Government (Publication of Manpower Information) (Wales) Code 1983.
