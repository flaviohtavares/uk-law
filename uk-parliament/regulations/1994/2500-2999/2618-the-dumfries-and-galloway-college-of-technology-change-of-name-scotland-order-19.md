# The Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2618 (S.129)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1994

Made

3rd October 1994

Laid before Parliament

17th October 1994

Coming into force

7th November 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(4) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having undertaken such consultation as appeared to him to be appropriate, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 7th November 1994.

## 2. Change of name of Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology

The name of Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology is changed to Dumfries and Galloway College, and a corresponding change is made to the name of the board of management of that college.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd October 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of Dumfries and Galloway College of Technology to Dumfries and Galloway College. That College is under the control of a board of management in terms of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 and the Order therefore also changes the name of the board of management to reflect the new name of the College.
