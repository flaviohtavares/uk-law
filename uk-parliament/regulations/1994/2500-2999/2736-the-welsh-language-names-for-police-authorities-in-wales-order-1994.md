# The Welsh Language (Names for Police Authorities in Wales) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2736

POLICE

The Welsh Language (Names for Police Authorities in Wales) Order 1994

Made

24th October 1994

Laid before Parliament

27th October 1994

Coming into force

21st November 1994

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 25 and 27(4)(b) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Welsh Language (Names for Police Authorities in Wales) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 21st November 1994.

**2.** There are hereby conferred on those police authorities established under section 3 of the Police Act 1964[^f00002] which are, by virtue of section 3 (2) of that Act, known by the names listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, the alternative names in Welsh specified opposite thereto in column 2 of that Schedule.

David Maclean

Minister of State

Home Office

24th October 1994
