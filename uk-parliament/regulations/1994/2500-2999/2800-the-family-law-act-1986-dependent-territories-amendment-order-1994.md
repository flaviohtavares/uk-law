# The Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2800

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

2nd November 1994

Laid before Parliament

14th November 1994

Coming into force

6th December 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of November 1994

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 43 of the Family Law Act 1986[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 6th December 1994.

**2.** Paragraph 19(1) of Schedule 2 to the Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) Order 1991[^f00002] shall be amended by the insertion of the words “(within the meaning of the Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) Order 1991)” after the words “or out of any specified dependent territory”.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Family Law Act 1986 (Dependent Territories) Order 1991 in order to provide a definition of the term “specified dependent territory” for the purposes of the Domestic Proceedings (Northern Ireland) Order 1980 (S.I. [1980/563](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1980/563)).
