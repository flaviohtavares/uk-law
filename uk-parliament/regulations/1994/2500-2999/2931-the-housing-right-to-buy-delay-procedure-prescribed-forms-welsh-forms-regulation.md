# The Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) (Welsh Forms) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2931

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) (Welsh Forms) Regulations 1994

Made

9th November 1994

Coming into force

12th December 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 176(1) and (5) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) (Welsh Forms) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 12th December 1994.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 1989[^f00003].

**2.** The forms set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby prescribed as versions in Welsh which may be used instead of the corresponding forms in the Schedule to the principal Regulations for the purposes set out in regulation 2 of the principal Regulations.

**3.** Forms substantially to the like effect to the forms set out in the Schedule to these Regulations may be used.

*Extent: Explanatory Note: English version*

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe Welsh versions of the forms of notice of the Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 1989 as amended by the Housing (Right to Buy Delay Procedure) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1993.

The forms for which Welsh versions are prescribed by these Regulations are:–

- a. The form for an initial notice of delay under section 153A(1) of the Housing Act 1985 (form RTB6).
- b. The form for a counter notice under section 153A(3)(b) of the Housing Act 1985 (form RTB7).
- c. The form for an operative notice of delay under section 153A(5) of the Housing Act 1985 (form RTB8).
