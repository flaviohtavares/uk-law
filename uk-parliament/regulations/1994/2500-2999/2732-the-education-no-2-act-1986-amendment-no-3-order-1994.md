# The Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1994/692](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/692) and is being issuedfree of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1994 No. 2732

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1994

Made

24th October 1994

Laid before Parliament

25th October 1994

Coming into force

16th November 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(5) of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 16th November 1994.

## 2. Amendment and Revocation

For section 30(2)(i) of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Governors' annual report to parents) there shall be substituted—

> - (i) to give such information about public examinations and other assessments of pupils' achievements, pupils' absences from the school, the continuing education of pupils leaving the school and the employment or training taken up by such pupils as is required to be published by virtue of section 8(5) of the 1980 Act;

.

**3.** Article 3 of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) Order 1994[^f00002] is revoked.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

17th October 1994

John Redwood

Secretary of State for Wales

24th October 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 30(2)(i) of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986. This section was previously amended by Article 3 of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Amendment) Order 1994 which is now revoked (article 3). Section 30(2)(i) is re-amended by virtue of this Order. The articles of government for schools maintained by local education authorities must now require the governors' annual report to include such information about pupils' absences as is required to be published by virtue of section 8(5) of the Education Act 1980 (article 2).
