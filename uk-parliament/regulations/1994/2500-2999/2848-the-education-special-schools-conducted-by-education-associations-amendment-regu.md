# The Education (Special Schools Conducted by Education Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1994/1084](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1084) and is being issued free of charge to all recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1994 No. 2848

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Special Schools Conducted by Education Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

3rd November 1994

Laid before Parliament

10th November 1994

Coming into force

1st December 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 228(3)(a) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Regulations—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Special Schools Conducted by Education Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st December 1994.

**2.** The Education (Special Schools Conducted by Education Associations) Regulations 1994[^f00002] are amended by the omission in the third column of Schedule 2 to those Regulations, opposite the entry in the first column of that Schedule relating to the Education Act 1993, of the words “For the reference in section 130(2) to section 68(7)(b) of the Act there is substituted a reference to paragraph 1(3)(b) of Schedule 12 to the Act;”.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

3rd November 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Special Schools Conducted by Education Associations) Regulations 1994 by omitting an unnecessary reference.
