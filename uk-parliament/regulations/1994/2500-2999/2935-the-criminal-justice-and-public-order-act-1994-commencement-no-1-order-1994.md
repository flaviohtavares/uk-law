# The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2935 (C.66)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

Made

19th November 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 172(2) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994 and extends to the United Kingdom and to the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man.

**2.** Section 159(1), (2) and (4) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Backing of Warrants: Republic of Ireland) shall come into force on 19th December 1994.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

19th November 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, the first Commencement Order to be made in respect of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, brings into force two amendments to the Backing of Warrants (Republic of Ireland) Act [1965 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1965/45) on 19th December 1994.
