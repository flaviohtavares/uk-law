# The Local Government (Magistrates' Courts etc.) (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2812

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government (Magistrates' Courts etc.) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

2nd November 1994

Laid before Parliament

3rd November 1994

Coming into force

28th November 1994

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 101 of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Magistrates' Courts etc.) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 28th November 1994.

**2.** Paragraph 5 of the Schedule to the Local Government (Magistrates' Courts etc.) Order 1985[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 2nd November 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes paragraph 5 of the Schedule to the Local Government (Magistrates' Courts etc.) Order 1985 which amended the Magistrates' Courts Committees (Constitution) Regulations 1973 (S.I. [1973/1522](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1973/1522)). The 1973 Regulations were revoked by the Magistrates' Courts Committees (Constitution) Regulations 1994 (S.I. [1994/2811](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2811)).
