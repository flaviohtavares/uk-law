# The Wrexham and East Denbighshire and Chester (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2915

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Wrexham and East Denbighshire and Chester (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1994

Made

25th October 1994

Whereas Wrexham and East Denbighshire Water Company and Chester Waterworks Company each hold an appointment under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001] as a water undertaker (“the Companies”):

And whereas each company has, pursuant to section 182(1)(a) of that Act, submitted to the Secretary of State for his approval a Code of Practice[^f00002] with respect to the exercise by the Companies of powers conferred by or by virtue of sections 159 and 161(2) of that Act (“the Codes of Practice”):

And whereas the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to approve the Codes of Practice for the purpose of promoting what appear to him to be desirable practices with regard to the exercise by the Companies of those powers:

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers under section 182(2) of the Water Industry Act 1991, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Wrexham and East Denbighshire and Chester (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1994.

## 2. Codes of Practice

The Codes of Practice prepared by the Companies and certified as such by the Secretary of State are hereby approved.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

G. C. G. Craig

An Under Secretary in the Welsh Office

25th October 1994
