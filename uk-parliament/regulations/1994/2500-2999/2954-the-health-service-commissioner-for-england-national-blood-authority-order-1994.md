# The Health Service Commissioner for England (National Blood Authority) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 2954

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Health Service Commissioner for England (National Blood Authority) Order 1994

Made

24th November 1994

Laid before Parliament

6th December 1994

Coming into force

1st January 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of November 1994

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 2(5) of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Health Service Commissioner for England (National Blood Authority) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

## 2. Designation as body subject to investigation

The National Blood Authority is hereby designated as a Special Health Authority to which section 2 of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993 (health service bodies subject to investigation by the Health Service Commissioner) applies.

R. P. Bulling

Deputy, Clerk of the Privy Council

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the National Blood Authority as a health service body subject to investigation by the Health Service Commissioner under section 2 of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993.
