# The Milk Marketing Board (Residuary Functions) Regulations 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Effect of provisions of the old marketing scheme as preserved, inserted or continuing in force

The provisions of the old marketing scheme which–

- (a) are preserved, whether or not subject to modifications,
- (b) are inserted by virtue of these Regulations, or
- (c) by virtue of section 13 of the Act continue in force subject to the provisions of Part VII of these Regulations,

shall in aggregate have effect as set out in Schedule 3.
