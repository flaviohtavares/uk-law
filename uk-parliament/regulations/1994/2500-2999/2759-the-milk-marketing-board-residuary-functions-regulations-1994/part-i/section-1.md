# The Milk Marketing Board (Residuary Functions) Regulations 1994, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Milk Marketing Board (Residuary Functions) Regulations 1994, shall apply in England and Wales and shall come into force on 1st November 1994.
