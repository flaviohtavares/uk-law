> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Contracts

(1) Paragraph 29 of the old marketing scheme is preserved and shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.

(2) Paragraph 30 of the old marketing scheme is preserved, subject to modifications, and shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.
