## 23. Assessment of sales

(1) Paragraph 71(2) of the old marketing scheme is preserved, subject to modifications, and shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.

(2) Paragraph 71(5) of the old marketing scheme is preserved, subject to modifications, and shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.
