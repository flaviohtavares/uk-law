# The Milk Marketing Board (Residuary Functions) Regulations 1994, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Prohibition on buying and selling milk etc

Nothing in these Regulations shall be construed as authorising the Board to carry out functions prohibited by paragraph 16 of Schedule 1 to the Act from inclusion within a qualifying scheme (as that expression is used in paragraph 1 of that Schedule).
