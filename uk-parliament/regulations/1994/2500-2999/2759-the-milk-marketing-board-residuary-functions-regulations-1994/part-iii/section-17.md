# The Milk Marketing Board (Residuary Functions) Regulations 1994, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Death etc of producers

(1) Paragraph 41(2) of the old marketing scheme is preserved, subject to modifications, and shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.

(2) After paragraph 41(2) of the old marketing scheme there shall be inserted new sub–paragraphs (3) and (4), which shall have effect as set out in Schedule 3.
