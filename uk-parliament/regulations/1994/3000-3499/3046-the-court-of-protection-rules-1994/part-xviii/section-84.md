# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Winding up fee

A winding up fee shall be payable on the death of a patient in cases where the Public Trustee had been appointed receiver.
