# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Mode of service

Except where these Rules otherwise provide, any document required by these Rules to be served on any person shall be served by delivering it to him personally or, if in any particular case or class of case the court so directs, by sending it to him by first class post at his last known address.
