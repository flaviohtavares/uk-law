# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Amendment of application

(1) The court or the Public Trustee may allow or direct an applicant, at any stage of the proceedings, to amend his application in such manner and on such terms as to costs or otherwise as may be just.

(2) The amendment may be effected by making in writing the necessary alterations to the application, but if the amendments are so numerous or of such a nature or length that written alterations would make it difficult or inconvenient to read, a fresh application amended as authorised or directed may be issued.
