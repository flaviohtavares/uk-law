# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Clerical mistakes and slips

The court or the Public Trustee as appropriate may at any time correct any clerical mistakes in an order or direction or any error arising in an order or direction from any accidental slip or omission.
