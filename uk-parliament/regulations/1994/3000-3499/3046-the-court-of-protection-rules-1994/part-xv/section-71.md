# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Inquiries as to prior dealing with the patient’s property

In any proceedings relating to a patient the court may make or cause to be made such inquiries as it thinks fit as to any dealing with the patient’s property before the commencement of the proceedings and as to the mental capacity of the patient at the time of such dealing.
