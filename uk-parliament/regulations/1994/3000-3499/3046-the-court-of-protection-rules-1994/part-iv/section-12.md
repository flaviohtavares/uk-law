# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Power to direct application by the Public Trustee or Official Solicitor

Where in the opinion of the court an application ought to be made for the appointment or discharge of a receiver or for the exercise of any other function with respect to the property and affairs of a patient, and there appears to be no other suitable person able and willing to make the application, or the court for any other reason thinks fit, the court may direct that the application be made by the Public Trustee or, if he consents, by the Official Solicitor.
