# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Privacy of applications

(1) Every application shall be heard in chambers unless, in the case of an application for hearing by the judge, the judge otherwise directs.

(2) The Public Trustee shall give such directions as he thinks fit concerning the privacy of applications made to him.
