# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Persons attending hearing

Subject to rule 16, the court may determine what persons are to be entitled to attend at any stage of the proceedings relating to a patient.
