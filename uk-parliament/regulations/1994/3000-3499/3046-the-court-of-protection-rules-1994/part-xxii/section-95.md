# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Revocation of previous Rules

The Court of Protection Rules 1984[^f00016] and the Court of Protection (Amendment) Rules 1992[^f00017] are hereby revoked.
