# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Sealing and filing of orders

Every order, certificate, direction or authority of the court or the Public Trustee which is drawn up shall, when entered, be sealed and filed.
