# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Supreme Court costs rules to apply

Subject to the provisions of these Rules, Order 62 of the Rules of the Supreme Court 1965[^f00015] shall apply, with such modifications as may be necessary, to costs incurred in relation to proceedings under these Rules as they apply to costs incurred in relation to proceedings in the Chancery Division and may be taxed where required accordingly.
