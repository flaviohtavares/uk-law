# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Applications under sections 36(9) and 54 of the Trustee Act 1925

The court may make any such order with respect to the costs of an application under section 36(9) or 54 of the Trustee Act 1925 as the High Court could make under section 60 of that Act in relation to any matter mentioned in that section.
