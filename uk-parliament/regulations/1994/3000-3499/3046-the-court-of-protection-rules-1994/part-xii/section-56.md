# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**56.** (1) Any person who is aggrieved by a decision of the court not made on a hearing, or a decision of the Public Trustee, may apply to the court within eight days of the date on which the decision was given to have the decision reviewed by the court.

(2) No review shall lie from any decision under rule 86 of these Rules.

(3) On the hearing of the application the court may either confirm or revoke the previous decision or make any other order or decision which it thinks fit.

(4) Any person aggrieved by any order or decision made on the hearing of the application for review may appeal to a nominated judge in accordance with rule 57.
