# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Filing of written evidence

(1) Before an affidavit, certificate or other written evidence is used in any proceedings under these Rules it shall be filed but the court may make an order on the basis of such evidence before it is filed if the person tendering it undertakes to file it before the order is drawn up.

(2) There shall be endorsed on every affidavit, certificate or other written evidence the name and address of the solicitor, if any, for the person on whose behalf it is filed.
