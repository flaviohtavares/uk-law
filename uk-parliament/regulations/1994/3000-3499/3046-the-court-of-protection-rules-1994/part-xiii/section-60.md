# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Lodgment of security

Any security given by lodgment of money or stock shall be dealt with in accordancewith the terms of the direction filed when the lodgment was made.
