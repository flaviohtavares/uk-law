# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Power to vary time

The court may extend or abridge the time limited by these Rules or any order or direction of the court for doing any act or taking any proceeding upon such terms as the court thinks fit and notwithstanding, in the case of an extension, that the time so limited has expired.
