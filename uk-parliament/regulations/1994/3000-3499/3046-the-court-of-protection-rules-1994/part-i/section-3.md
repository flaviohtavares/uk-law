# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Exercise of court’s functions

Where any function is (in whatever words) expressed by these Rules to be exercisable by the court then, subject to the provisions of the Act, that function may be exercised—

- (a) by a judge;
- (b) by the Master;
- (c) to the extent to which he is authorised to exercise it under section 94 of the Act, by any nominated officer.
