# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Accounting by other persons

Rules 63 to 67 shall also apply, to the extent directed by the court or the Public Trustee, to any person who is—

- (a) directed to deal with the patient’s property or affairs under rule 9;
- (b) directed or authorised to act under rule 44(1)(a); or
- (c) appointed an interim receiver under rule 44(1)(b),

as they apply to a receiver.
