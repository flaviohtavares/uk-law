# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Passing of accounts

(1) Every receiver shall, annually or at such other interval as the court or the Public Trustee shall specify, deliver his accounts to the Public Trustee within such time as the court or the Public Trustee shall direct.

(2) The receiver shall answer such requisitions on his accounts as the court or the Public Trustee shall raise and in such manner and in such time as the court or the Public Trustee shall direct.

(3) On the passing of any accounts the Public Trustee shall make all proper allowances out of the patient’s estate, including an allowance in respect of the reasonable and proper costs of the receiver of passing the accounts.

(4) The court or the Public Trustee may direct that a receiver need not account under this rule or may dispense with the passing of any accounts at any time at which they would otherwise require to be passed.
