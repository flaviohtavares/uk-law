# THE COURT OF PROTECTION RULES 1994, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Exercise of jurisdiction

Except where these Rules otherwise provide, any function may be exercised—

- (a) without fixing an appointment for a hearing;
- (b) by the court or the Public Trustee as appropriate of its or his own motion or at the instance or on the application of any person interested;
- (c) whether or not any proceedings have been commenced in the court with respect to the patient.
