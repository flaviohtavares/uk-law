# The Merchant Shipping (Liability of Shipowners and Others) (Rate of Interest) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3049

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Liability of Shipowners and Others) (Rate of Interest) Order 1994

Made

29th November 1994

Laid before Parliament

8th December 1994

Coming into force

1st January 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 8(1) of Part II of Schedule 4 to the Merchant Shipping Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Liability of Shipowners and Others) (Rate of Interest) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Liability of Shipowners and Others) (Rate of Interest) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

**3.** The rate of interest for the purposes of article 11(1) of the Convention on Limitation of Liability for Maritime Claims 1976[^f00003] shall be—

- (a) where the occurrence takes place before 1st January 1995 but the fund is constituted on or after that date—
  - (i) 12 per cent from the date of the occurrence until 31st December 1994,
  - (ii) 6.75 per cent on and after 1st January 1995;
- (b) where the occurrence takes place on or after 1st January 1995, 6.75 per cent.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Goschen

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

23rd November 1994

We concur,

Tim Wood

Derek Conway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th November 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the rate of interest to be included in the limitation fund constituted by a person seeking to limit his liability by virtue of the Convention on Limitation of Liability for Maritime Claims 1976 shall be 6.75 per cent in respect of any period after 31st December 1994 and revokes the former Order (under which the rate was 12 per cent.)
