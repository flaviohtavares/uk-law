# The Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3053 (S.159)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1994

Made

29th November 1994

Coming into force

10th December 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1994 and shall come into force on 10th December 1994.

**2.** The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch an Sgoltaire) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 11th December 1995.

T D Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, The Scottish Office

The Scottish Office Environment Department,

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

29th November 1994
