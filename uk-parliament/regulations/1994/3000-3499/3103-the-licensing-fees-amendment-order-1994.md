# The Licensing (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3103

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Licensing (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

5th December 1994

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1994

Coming into force

3rd January 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 29(1) and 198(2) of the Licensing Act 1964[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 3rd January 1995.

**2.** The Schedule to the Licensing (Fees) Order 1978[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting at the end the following entry:

> - Grant of Children’s Certificate — s. 168A[^f00003] 6.50

.

M.B. Forsyth

Minister of State

Home Office

5th December 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Licensing (Fees) Order 1978 by prescribing a fee of £6.50 chargeable by justices' clerks for the grant of a children’s certificate under section 168A of the Licensing Act 1964.
