# The Marriage Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3116 (c. 73)

MARRIAGE

The Marriage Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994

Made

7th December 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Marriage Act 1994[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Marriage Act 1994 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1994.

## 2. Appointed day

1st January 1995 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Marriage Act 1994—

- (a) section 2(1) (registration districts in which marriages may be solemnised), so far as it inserts subsection (2A) in section 35 of the Marriage Act 1949[^f00002];
- (b) section 2(2) (superintendent registrar not normally to issue licences for marriages in registered buildings outside his district); and
- (c) section 3 (short title and commencement).

Virginia Bottomley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

7th December 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1995 those provisions of the Marriage Act 1994 which amend the Marriage Act 1949 to permit marriage in a register office outside the couple’s district(s) of residence.
