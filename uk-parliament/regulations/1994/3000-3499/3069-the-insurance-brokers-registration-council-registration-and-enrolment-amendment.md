# The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3069

INSURANCE

The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1994

Made

1st December 1994

Laid before Parliament

6th December 1994

Coming into force

1st January 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 27(1) and 28(1) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

**2.** The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 1994 made on 29th November 1994 by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of their powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977 are hereby approved.

**3.** The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1991[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Jonathan Evans

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

1st December 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves rules made by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of its powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977.

The Rules amend the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) Rules 1978, as amended by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 1979, 1982, 1985, 1988 and 1991 by altering fees payable under the 1978 Rules as amended by the 1982, 1985, 1988 and 1991 (Amendment) Rules. The Order also revokes the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 1991 which approved the 1991 (Amendment) Rules.

Copies of the Rules may be obtained from the Insurance Brokers Registration Council, 15 St Helen’s Place, London EC3A 6DS.

A Compliance Cost Assessment has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament. Copies are also available from DTI, Room 817, 10-18 Victoria Street, London SW1H 0NN.
