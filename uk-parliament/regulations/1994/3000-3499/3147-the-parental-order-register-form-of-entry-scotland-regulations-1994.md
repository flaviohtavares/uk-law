# The Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3147 (S.171)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1994

Made

5th December 1994

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1994

Coming into force

1st January 1995

The Registrar General for Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the Adoption (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and with the approval of the Secretary of State, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Parental Order Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

## 2. Form of entry in the Parental Order Register

The entry to be made in the Parental Order Register pursuant to a direction contained in a parental order shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — PARENTAL ORDER REGISTER

Regulation 2

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3147/images/uksi_19943147_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify the form of entry to be made in the Parental Order Register which is to be maintained by the Registrar General for Scotland in consequence of the Parental Orders (Human Fertilisation and Embryology) (Scotland) Regulations 1994 (S.I. [1994/2804](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2804)). The entry will be made following a direction in a parental order.

The form of entry is similar to the form of entry specified for the Adopted Children Register by the Adopted Children Register (Form of Entry) (Scotland) Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/1261](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1261)).
