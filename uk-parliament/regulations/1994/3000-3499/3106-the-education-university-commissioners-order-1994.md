# The Education (University Commissioners) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3106

UNIVERSITY AND COLLEGES

The Education (University Commissioners) Order 1994

Made

6th December 1994

Laid before Parliament

7th December 1994

Coming into force

30th December 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 3(2)(b) of Schedule 11 to the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 30th December 1994.

## 2. Continuation of duties and powers

The University Commissioners' duties and powers shall continue for the period from 1st January 1995 until 1st April 1995.

Gillian Shephard

Secretary of State for Education

6th December 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order continues the University Commissioners' powers and duties until 1st April 1995. By virtue of paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 11 to the Education Reform Act 1988, their powers and duties would have ceased on 29th July 1991, but they were extended until 31st December 1992 by the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1991 ([S. I.1991/1427](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1427)), extended until 1st January 1994 by the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1992 ([S. I.1992/3064](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3064)), and further extended by the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1993 ([S. I.1993/3056](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3056)) until 1st January 1995.
