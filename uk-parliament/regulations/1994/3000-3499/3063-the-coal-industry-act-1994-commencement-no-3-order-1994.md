# The Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3063 (C.71)

COAL INDUSTRY

The Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994

Made

1st December 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 68(4) and (5) of the Coal Industry Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Coal Industry Act 1994.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 24th December 1994

Part III of Schedule 11 to the Act shall, so far as it relates to the repeals set out in the Schedule to this Order, come into force on 24th December 1994.

Charles Wardle

Parliamentary Under Secretary for Industry and Energy,

Department of Trade and Industry

1st December 1994
