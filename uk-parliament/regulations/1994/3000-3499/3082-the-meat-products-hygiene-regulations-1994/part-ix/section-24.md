# The Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendments

(1) After regulation 4A(b) of the Food Hygiene (Docks, Carriers, etc.) Regulations 1960[^f00024], there shall be inserted—

> - (c) the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994.

.

(2) At the end of sub-paragraph (b)(iv) of the definition of “food business” in regulation 2 of the Food Hygiene (Markets, Stalls and Delivery Vehicles) Regulations 1966[^f00025] there shall be added—

> or
>
> - (i) to which the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994 apply;

.

(3) At the end of regulation 3(2)(b) of the Food Hygiene (General) Regulations 1970[^f00026] there shall be added—

> - (iv) the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994;

.

(4) At the end of regulation 3(2)(e) of the Food Premises (Registration) Regulations 1991[^f00027] there shall be added “or an approval is required under regulation 4 or 5 of the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994;”.
