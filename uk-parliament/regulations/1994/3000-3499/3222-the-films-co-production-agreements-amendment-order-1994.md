# The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 3222

CINEMA AND FILMS

The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1994

Made

14th December 1994

Coming into force

15th December 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 1994

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th December 1994.

**2.** The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule the following—

R P Bulling

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985 (“the principal Order”) provides that films made in accordance with the agreements between the Government of the United Kingdom and the Governments of the countries listed in the Schedule, as substituted by [SI 1993/1805](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1805), are to be treated as British Films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985 (Certification for the purposes of section 68 of the Capital Allowances Act 1990 in case of British Films). The present Order extends the list of agreements scheduled to the principal Order to include an agreement with France of 8th November 1994 (Cm. 2697).
