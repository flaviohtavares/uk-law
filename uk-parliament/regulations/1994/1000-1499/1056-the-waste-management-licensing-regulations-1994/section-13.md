# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Health at work

No conditions shall be imposed in any waste management licence for the purpose only of securing the health of persons at work (within the meaning of Part I of the Health and Safety at Work etc.Act 1974[^f00032]).

## Annotations

[^f00032]: Footnote: [1974 c. 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/37); see section 52(1) for the meaning of “at work”.
