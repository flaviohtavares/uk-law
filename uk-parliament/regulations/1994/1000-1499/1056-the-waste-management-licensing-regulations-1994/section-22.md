# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Amendment of the Collection and Disposal of Waste Regulations 1988

(1) The Collection and Disposal of Waste Regulations 1988[^f00051] shall be amended as follows.

(2) At the beginning of regulation 3, there shall be inserted “Subject to regulations 4 and 7A,”.

(3) At the beginning of each of regulations 6 and 7, there shall be inserted “Subject to regulation 7A,”.

(4) After regulation 7, the following shall be inserted—

> **7A. Waste not to be treated as household, industrial or commercial waste**
>
> (1) For the purposes of all the provisions of Part I of the Act, waste which is not Directive waste shall not be treated as household waste, industrial waste or commercial waste.
>
> (2) In this regulation, “Directive waste” has the meaning given by regulation 1(3) of the Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994.
