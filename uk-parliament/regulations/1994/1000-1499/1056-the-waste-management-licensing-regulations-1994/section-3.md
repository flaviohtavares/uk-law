# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Relevant offences

An offence is relevant for the purposes of section 74(3)(a) of the 1990 Act if it is an offence under any of the following enactments—

- (a) section 22 of the Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897[^f00019];
- (b) section 95(1) of the Public Health Act 1936[^f00020];
- (c) section 3, 5(6), 16(4), 18(2), 31(1), 32(1), 34(5), 78, 92(6) or 93(3) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00021];
- (d) section 2 of the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act 1978[^f00022];
- (e) the Control of Pollution (Special Waste) Regulations 1980;
- (f) section 9(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00023];
- (g) the Transfrontier Shipment of Hazardous Waste Regulations 1988[^f00024];
- (h) the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution by Garbage) Regulations 1988[^f00025];
- (i) section 1, 5, 6(9) or 7(3) of the Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989;
- (j) section 107, 118(4) or 175(1) of the Water Act 1989[^f00026];
- (k) section 23(1), 33, 34(6), 44, 47(6), 57(5), 59(5), 63(2), 69(9), 70(4), 71(3) or 80(4) of the 1990 Act;
- (l) section 85, 202 or 206 of the Water Resources Act 1991;
- (m) section 33 of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00027].
