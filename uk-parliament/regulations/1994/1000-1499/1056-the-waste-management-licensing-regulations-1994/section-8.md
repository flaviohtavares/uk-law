# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Reports of hearings

The person hearing an appeal under section 43(2)(c) of the 1990 Act[^f00030] shall, unless he has been appointed to determine the appeal under section 43(2)(b) of that Act, make a written report to the Secretary of State which shall include his conclusions and recommendations or his reasons for not making any recommendations.

## Annotations

[^f00030]: Footnote: Section 43(2) is applied by section 66(6) to appeals under section 66(5).
