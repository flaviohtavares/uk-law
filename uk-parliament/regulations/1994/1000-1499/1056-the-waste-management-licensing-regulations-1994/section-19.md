# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Waste Framework Directive

Schedule 4 (which implements certain provisions of Council Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442) on waste[^f00044]) shall have effect.
