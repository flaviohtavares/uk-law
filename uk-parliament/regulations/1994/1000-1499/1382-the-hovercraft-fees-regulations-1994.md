# The Hovercraft (Fees) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1382

HOVERCRAFT

The Hovercraft (Fees) Regulations 1994

Made

19th May 1994

Coming into force

1st July 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by article 35 of the Hovercraft (General) Order 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** The Regulations may be cited as the Hovercraft (Fees) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

**2.** The Schedule to the Hovercraft (Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following table, by substituting, in the paragraph in the Schedule listed in column 1, for the amount in column 2 the amount in column 3.

19th May 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Hovercraft (Fees) Regulations 1992.

These Regulations prescribe an average increase of 8% in the fees to be paid to the Civil Aviation Authority for the issue of Type and Safety Certificates, and other matters under Part I (Certification and Maintenance) of the Hovercraft (General) Order 1972, and an increase of about 21.6% in the fees to be paid to the Department of Transport for the issue of operating permits and other matters under Part II (Duties of Operator and Captain) of the Hovercraft (General) Order 1992.
