# The County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1289 (L. 8)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1994

Made

11th May 1994

Coming into force

6th June 1994

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1994.

(2) In these Rules, a form referred to by number means the form so numbered in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001] and “the Schedule” means that Schedule.

**2.** There shall be inserted in the Schedule, after forms N.1(SPC) and N.11R, the forms contained in the schedule to these Rules.

The undersigned members of the Rule Committee, appointed by the Lord Chancellor under section 75 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002], having made these Rules, certify them and submit them to the Lord Chancellor.
