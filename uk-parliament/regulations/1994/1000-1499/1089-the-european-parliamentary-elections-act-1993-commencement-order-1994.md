# The European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1089 (C.17)

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994

Made

15th April 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 3(3) of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994.

**2.** Section 1 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993 shall come into force for all purposes on 1st May 1994.

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

15th April 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order brings section 1 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1993 into force for all purposes on 1st May 1994. The remainder of that Act came into force on Royal Assent.
