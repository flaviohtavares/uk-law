# The Education (Special Educational Needs) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1994/1047](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1047) and is being issued free of charge to all recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1994 No. 1251

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Special Educational Needs) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made

5th May 1994

Laid before Parliament

9th May 1994

Coming into force

1st September 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 168(2) of and paragraph 3 of Schedule 9 to the Education Act 1993[^f00001]the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Special Educational Needs) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st September 1994.

**2.** The Education (Special Educational Needs) Regulations 1994[^f00002]are amended—

- (a) in regulation 11(6)(d) by substituting “regulation 6(1)(c)” for “regulation 6(c)”, and
- (b) in paragraph 2 of Part I of the form set out in Part B of the Schedule by substituting “10” for “8”.

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education

5th May 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Special Educational Needs) Regulations 1994 by substituting two cross references to regulations for the existing cross references, which were defective.
