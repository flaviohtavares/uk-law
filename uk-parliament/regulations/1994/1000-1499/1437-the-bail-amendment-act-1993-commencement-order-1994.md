# The Bail (Amendment) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1437 (C.24)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Bail (Amendment) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994

Made

25th May 1994

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 2(2) of the Bail (Amendment) Act 1993[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Bail (Amendment) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1994.

**2.** 27th June 1994 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Bail (Amendment) Act 1993 apart from section 2[^f00002].

Home Office

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

25th May 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 27th June 1994 the operative provisions of the Bail (Amendment) Act 1993. On that date the whole of the Act will be in force.
