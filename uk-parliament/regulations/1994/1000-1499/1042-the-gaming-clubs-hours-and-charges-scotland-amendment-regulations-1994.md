# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1042 (S. 52)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1994

Made

7th April 1994

Laid before Parliament

8th April 1994

Coming into force

1st May 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 14(2) and (3) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain in accordance with section 51(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st May 1994.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

**2.** Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 1984([^f00002]) shall be amended as follows:–

- (a) for the sum “£6.40” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the sum “£6.60”; and
- (b) for the sum “£4.80” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the sum “£4.97”.

**3.** The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1993([^f00003]) are hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th April 1994

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming on bingo club premises in Scotland.

The maximum charge in respect of a charging period other than the shorter period permitted on a Sunday is increased from £6.40 to £6.60.

The maximum charge in respect of the shorter charging period permitted on a Sunday is increased from £4.80 to £4.97.
