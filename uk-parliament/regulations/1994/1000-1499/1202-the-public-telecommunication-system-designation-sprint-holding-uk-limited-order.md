# The Public Telecommunication System Designation(Sprint Holding (UK) Limited) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1202

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation(Sprint Holding (UK) Limited) Order 1994

Made

28th April 1994

Laid before Parliament

5th May 1994

Coming into force

3rd June 1994

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Sprint Holding (UK) Limited on 31st March 1994 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001](“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in the Licence (“the Applicable Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Sprint Holding (UK) Limited) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 3rd June 1994.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Patrick McLoughlin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Trade and Industry

28th April 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Sprint Holding (UK) Limited a licence on 31st March 1994 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in that licence in the United Kingdom. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 5th May 1994. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Sprint Holding (UK) Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 3rd June 1994.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications,50 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JJ.
