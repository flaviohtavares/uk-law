# The Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1994 No. 1313

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

Made

16th May 1994

Laid before Parliament

23rd May 1994

Coming into force

13th June 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Chorley and South Ribble Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the South Lancashire Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 13th June 1994.

(2) In this Order–

- “the Authority” means the South Lancashire Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Chorley and South Ribble Health Authority and the Trust on 31st January 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both that Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 13th June 1994 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

16th May 1994

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Chorley and South Ribble National Health Service Trust on 13th June 1994 trust property formerly held by the South Lancashire Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
