# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Greenwich) Order 1994, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Greenwich) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 4th July 1994.
