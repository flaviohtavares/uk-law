# The Milk and Dairies and Milk (Special Designation) (Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

31st May 1994

Laid before Parliament

3rd June 1994

Coming into force

24th June 1994

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Health and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in relation to England and Wales in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(4) and 45 of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations, after consultation in accordance with section 48 of the said Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests substantially affected by the Regulations:—

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Milk and Dairies and Milk (Special Designation) (Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 24th June 1994.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Milk and Dairies and Milk (Special Designation) (Charges) Regulations 1990

The Milk and Dairies and Milk (Special Designation) (Charges) Regulations 1990[^f00002] (“the Milk Charges Regulations”) shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Milk Charges Regulations the definition of “the Board” shall be deleted.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 3

Regulation 3 (liability to charges) of the Milk Charges Regulations shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for the words “paragraphs (2) and (3) hereof” there shall be substituted the words “paragraph (2) below”;
- (b) paragraph (2) shall be deleted;
- (c) for paragraph (3) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

  > (2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) above, no charge shall be due in respect of—
  >
  > - (a) any dairy farm visit for the purpose of ascertaining whether there is a particular threat to public health, or for the purpose of taking measures to reduce such a threat,
  > - (b) any sampling dairy farm visit in connection with an untreated milk producer’s licence granted in accordance with the Milk (Special Designation) Regulations 1989 solely for the sale of milk under the conditions described in paragraph A4(1)(c) of Schedule 2 to those Regulations (farmhouse catering), or
  > - (c) any sampling dairy farm visit in connection with a consent granted under regulation 2B of the Milk (Special Designation) Regulations 1989 to sell milk without the use of a special designation.

  .

## 5. Amendment of regulation 4

For regulation 4 (recovery of charges) there shall be substituted the following regulation:—

> **4.** The registered dairy farmer shall pay to the Minister on demand the charge payable under regulation 3.

.

## 6. Replacement of Schedule

For the Schedule to the Milk Charges Regulations there shall be substituted the new Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

These Regulations, which apply to England and Wales, further amend the Milk and Dairies and Milk (Special Designation) (Charges) Regulations 1990, as amended, (“the Milk Charges Regulations”).

The Milk Charges Regulations are amended so as to provide for payment to the Minister on demand of charges for dairy farm visits which are currently recoverable on her behalf by the Milk Marketing Board (regulations 3 and 5).

The Regulations also—

- (a) abolish the lower rate of charge specified by the Milk Charges Regulations and restrict the exemption from charging (other than in relation to public health) to sampling dairy farm visits (regulation 4);
- (b) increase the charge for a general dairy farm visit to £94 (regulation 6).

The charge for a sampling dairy farm visit remains unchanged at £63.
