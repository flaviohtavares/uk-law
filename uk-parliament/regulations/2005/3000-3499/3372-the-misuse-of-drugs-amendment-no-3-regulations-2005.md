# The Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3372

DANGEROUS DRUGS

The Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2005

Made

6th December 2005

Laid before Parliament

8th December 2005

Coming into force

1st January 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7, 10, 22 and 31 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001].

In accordance with section 31(3) of that Act he has consulted with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st January 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001

In Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001[^f00002] (which specifies some of the controlled drugs that are subject to the requirements of regulations 22, 23, 26 and 27 and for which provision is made by certain other regulations), in paragraph 1 after “4-Hydroxy-n-butyric acid” insert “Ketamine”[^f00003].

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

6th December 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations insert Ketamine into Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001 (“the 2001 Regulations”), which specifies some of the controlled drugs that are subject to the record keeping, information and destruction requirements set out in regulations 22, 23, 26 and 27 of the 2001 Regulations and for which provision is made by certain other regulations of the 2001 Regulations.
