# The Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations 2005, section 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Revocation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the 1992 Regulations are hereby revoked.

(2) Regulations 6, 9, 10, 15 and 17 of and Schedule 4 to the 1992 Regulations shall remain in force until 6 October 2007 insofar as they relate to combined operations.
