# The Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 6th April 2006.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**1.** These Regulations replace the Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations 1992 (S.I. 1992/2885) (“**the 1992 Regulations**”) to provide for the preparation of safety cases for offshore installations and for the notification of specified activities to the Health and Safety Executive (“**the Executive**”).
