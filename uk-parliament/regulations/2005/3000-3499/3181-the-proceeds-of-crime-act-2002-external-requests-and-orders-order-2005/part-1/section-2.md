# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 2

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-f9fd3d531de9dbd9ef63c4861888c82f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- “The 2014 Regulations” means the Criminal Justice and Data Protection (Protocol No. 36) Regulations 2014;[^c-9ffd9f712733ddaffdaa59aad4383a7f]
- “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002;
- [^c-066d72f5f986788beaf80103674be5c0]...
- “country” includes territory;
- “external order” has the meaning set out in section 447(2) of the Act;
- “external request” has the meaning set out in section 447(1) of the Act;
- “overseas confiscation order” has the same meaning as in the 2014 Regulations;
- “a relevant officer of Revenue and Customs” means such an officer exercising functions by virtue of section 6 of the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005[^f00002]**.**
