# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213Y

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213Y. Action on receipt of an external request[^c-9c617700803fb83acc47c3b52cf46ec6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may refer an external request to prohibit dealing with money held in an account maintained with a relevant financial institution[^c-c43281fcc68e028748b9f6ae71a58080] to—

- (a) the Chief Constable of a police force in England and Wales , the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland[^c-13c04bfc8eb811c2bd3a4f0a9859974b] or the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Scotland;
- (b) the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs;
- (c) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office;
- (d) the Director of the National Crime Agency.
