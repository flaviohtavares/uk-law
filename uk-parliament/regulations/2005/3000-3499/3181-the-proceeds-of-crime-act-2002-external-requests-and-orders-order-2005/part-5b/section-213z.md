# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213Z

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213Z. Action to give effect to an external order[^key-9c617700803fb83acc47c3b52cf46ec6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may forward an external order in relation to money held in an account maintained with a relevant financial institution[^key-2f05d2c070ebadd030d59fbe68911374] to—

- (a) the Chief Constable of a police force in England and Wales , the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland[^key-48bab420e96fa69f48d1ffc293f41fac] or the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Scotland;
- (b) the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs;
- (c) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office;
- (d) the Director of the National Crime Agency.
