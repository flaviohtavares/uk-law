# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213Z6

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213Z6. Restriction on proceedings and remedies[^key-9c617700803fb83acc47c3b52cf46ec6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a court in which proceedings are pending in respect of an account maintained with a relevant financial institution[^key-3dca0ce1fe697bf0e452b73e027d6674] is satisfied that an account freezing order has been applied for or made in respect of the account, it may either stay the proceedings or allow them to continue on any terms it thinks fit.

(2) Before exercising the power conferred by paragraph (1), the court must (as well as giving the parties to any of the proceedings concerned an opportunity to be heard) give an opportunity to any person who may be affected by its decision to be heard.

(3) In relation to Scotland, the reference in paragraph (1) to staying the proceedings is to be read as a reference to sisting the proceedings.
