# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213Z10

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213Z10. Application of money forfeited under account forfeiture order[^c-9c617700803fb83acc47c3b52cf46ec6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Money forfeited by an order under article 213Z7 (forfeiture order), and any interest accrued on it whilst in the account referred to in paragraph (9)(a)[^c-e5a21ed28d1b90ca6a7388e054e18407] of that article is to be applied in payment of the amount payable under the external order.

(2) But it is not to be paid in—

- (a) before the end of the period within which an appeal under article 213Z9 (appeal against decision under article 213Z7) may be made, or
- (b) if a person appeals under that article, before the appeal is determined or otherwise disposed of.
