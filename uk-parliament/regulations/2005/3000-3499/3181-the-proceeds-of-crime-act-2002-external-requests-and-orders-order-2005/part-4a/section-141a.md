# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141A

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141A. General[^key-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part has effect for the purpose of enabling an enforcement authority to obtain from the High Court a prohibition order in relation to relevant property in England and Wales or Northern Ireland which is the subject of an external request.

(2) The powers conferred by this Part are exercisable in relation to relevant property whether or not proceedings for criminal conduct have been brought in connection with the property in the country from which the external request was sent.
