# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141L

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141L. Registration[^c-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registration Acts—

- (a) apply in relation to prohibition orders as they apply in relation to orders which affect land and are made by the High Court for the purpose of enforcing judgments or recognisances,
- (b) apply in relation to applications for prohibition orders as they apply in relation to other pending land actions.

(2) The registration Acts are—

- (a) the Land Charges Act 1972[^f00010], and
- (b) the Land Registration Act 2002[^f00011].

(3) But no notice may be entered in the register of title under the Land Registration Act 2002 in respect of a prohibition order.
