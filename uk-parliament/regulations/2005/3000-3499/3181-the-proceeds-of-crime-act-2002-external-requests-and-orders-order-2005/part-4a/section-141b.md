# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141B

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141B. Action on receipt of external request[^c-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may refer to an enforcement authority an external request to prohibit dealing with relevant property in England and Wales or Northern Ireland.

(2) The enforcement authority may on receipt of the request act under this Part.
