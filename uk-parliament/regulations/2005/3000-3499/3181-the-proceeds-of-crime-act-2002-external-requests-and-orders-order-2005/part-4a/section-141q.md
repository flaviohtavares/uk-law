# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141Q

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141Q. Insolvency: interpretation[^key-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies for the purposes of article 141P.

(2)[^key-821c3bfe108f655cea068d3542607d71] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The 1986 Act means the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00015].

(3A)[^key-c96dbbf8586cf90e15315a9a2a248949] The 2016 Act means the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016.

(4) The 1989 Order means the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00016].

(5) The appropriate court means the court which, in relation to the resolution, arrangement, order or trust deed mentioned in article 141P(2), is the court for the purposes of the applicable enactment or, in relation to Northern Ireland, the High Court.

(6) In paragraph (5) above and (8) below “the applicable enactment” means whichever enactment mentioned in paragraphs (2) to (4) above is relevant to the resolution, arrangement, order or trust deed mentioned in article 141P(2).

(7) Acting as an insolvency practitioner has the same meaning as in article 4.

(8) Other expressions used in article 141P and in the applicable enactment have the same meaning as in that enactment.
