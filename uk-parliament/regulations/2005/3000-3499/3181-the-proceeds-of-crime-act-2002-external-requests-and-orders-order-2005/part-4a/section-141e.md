# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141E

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141E. Application, variation and setting aside of order[^c-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A prohibition order may—

- (a) be made only on an application by the enforcement authority,
- (b) be made by a without notice application to a High Court judge.

(2) An application to vary or set aside a prohibition order may be made to the High Court by—

- (a) the enforcement authority,
- (b) any person affected by the order.

(3) The High Court may at any time—

- (a) vary the prohibition order,
- (b) set aside the prohibition order.

(4) Before exercising power under this Part to vary or set aside a prohibition order, the High Court must (as well as giving the parties to the proceedings an opportunity to be heard) give such an opportunity to any person who may be affected by its decision.
