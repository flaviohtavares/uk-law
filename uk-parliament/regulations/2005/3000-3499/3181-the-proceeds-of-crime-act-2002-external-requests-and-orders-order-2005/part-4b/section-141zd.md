# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141ZD

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141ZD. Application, variation and recall of prohibition order[^key-60d328b1519329044b0e7f9eeb27db3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A prohibition order may—

- (a) be made only on an application by the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) be made to the court without notice.

(2) An application to vary or recall a prohibition order may be made to the court by—

- (a) the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) any person affected by the order.

(3) The court may at any time vary or recall the prohibition order.

(4) Before exercising a power under this Part to vary or recall a prohibition order, the court must (as well as giving the parties to the proceedings an opportunity to be heard) give such an opportunity to any person who may be affected by its decision.
