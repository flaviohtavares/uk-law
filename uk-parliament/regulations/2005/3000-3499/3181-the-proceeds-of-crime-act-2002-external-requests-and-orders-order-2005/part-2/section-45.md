# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 45

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Appeal to the Supreme Court about receivers[^key-2c4b8dae1342111d398c2e8b5e51c1c2][^key-f343501f0c145ef75478fd925877639b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court[^key-c83a439b45fae98e0ee69bdcc8565f0d] from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under article 44.

(2) An appeal under this article lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this article the Supreme Court[^key-c8c552a2e523d199584947ca33513c14] may—

- (a) confirm the decision of the Court Appeal, or
- (b) make such order as it believes is appropriate.
