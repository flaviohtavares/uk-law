## 15. Appointment of management receivers[^c-36bc3c701ebb828ee99f48d58b60b3b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies if—

- (a) the Crown Court makes a restraint order, and
- (b) the relevant Director applies to the court to proceed under paragraph (2) (whether as part of the application for the restraint order or at any time afterwards).

(2) The Crown Court may by order appoint a receiver in respect of any property which is specified in the restraint order.
