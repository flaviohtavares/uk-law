# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 54

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Meaning of “defendant”[^c-6271af316a6c55a75a524b9bc26fc7df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “defendant”—

- (a) in relation to a restraint order means—
  - (i) in a case in which the first condition in article 7 is satisfied, the alleged offender;
  - (ii) in a case in which the second condition in article 7 is satisfied, the person against whom proceedings for an offence have been started in a country outside the United Kingdom (whether or not he has been convicted);
- (b) in relation to an external order, the person convicted of criminal conduct;
- (c)[^c-9f656b7d42e043d4a8c26f7ed072a637] for the purpose of Chapter 1A, has the meaning given in article 17A(4).
