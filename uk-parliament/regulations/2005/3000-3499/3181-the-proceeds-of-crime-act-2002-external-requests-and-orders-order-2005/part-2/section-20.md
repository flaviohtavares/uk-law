# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 20

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Applications to give effect to external orders[^key-c399ec8e58df192a1fd8d2e1626948fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application may be made by the relevant Director to the Crown Court to give effect to an external order.

(2) No application to give effect to such an order under this Order[^key-d15a37df6cea71dea951167e3dee6406] may be made otherwise than under paragraph (1).

(3) An application under paragraph (1)—

- (a) shall include a request to appoint the relevant Director as the enforcement authority for the order;
- (b) may be made on an ex parte application to a judge in chambers.
