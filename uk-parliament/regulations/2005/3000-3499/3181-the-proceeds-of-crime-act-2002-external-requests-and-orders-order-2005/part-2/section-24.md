# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 24

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Appeal to Supreme Court about external orders[^key-4ff31a42d9e8941d588f2c07abf529fc][^key-becb690ec8b27f5d90ba4228e0819678]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under article 23.

(2) An appeal under this article lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this article the Supreme Court may—

- (a) confirm or set aside the decision of the Court of Appeal, or
- (b) direct the Crown Court to register the external order (or so much of it as relates to property other than property to which article 21(6) applies).
