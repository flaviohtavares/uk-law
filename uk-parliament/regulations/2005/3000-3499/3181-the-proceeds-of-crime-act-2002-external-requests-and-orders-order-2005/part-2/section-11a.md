# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 11A

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11A. Detention of property pending appeal[^c-125031a9e01cdc1840239d965eb5cf73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) a restraint order includes provision under article 8A authorising the detention of property; and
- (b) the restraint order is discharged under article 9(4)(a) or 10(3)(b).

(2) This article also applies where—

- (a) a restraint order includes provision under article 8A authorising the detention of property; and
- (b) the restraint order is varied under article 9(4)(b)[^c-481f098695c602942915dd14d07521b0] or 10(3)(b) so as to omit any such provision.

(3) The property may be detained until there is no further possibility of an appeal against—

- (a) the decision to discharge or vary the restraint order; or
- (b) any decision made on an appeal against that decision.
