# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 48

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Procedure on appeal to Supreme Court under Part 2[^key-682f14cba0d6c35c36c7fc0668c4647e][^key-b9e12326442cbfdd28af1fc09a7ae2f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 33(3) of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968 (limitation on appeal from criminal division of the Court of Appeal) does not prevent an appeal to the Supreme Court[^key-f36ecff95886661d700646a2d4f91ebc] under this Part.

(2) In relation to appeals to the Supreme Court[^key-f057f037833fabf4a69b5675ec0240b9] under this Part, the Secretary of State may make an order containing provision corresponding to any provision in the Criminal Appeal Act 1968, subject to any specified modifications.
