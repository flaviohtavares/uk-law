# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 55

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Other interpretation[^c-4dadbc93d799e68ea3f38c7bf911c332][^c-9aed252c60496d7a937eea16e4f4a1bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “appropriate officer” means—[^c-1067a3450d0adbdd406c0e6af8518917]
  - (a) an officer of Revenue and Customs;
  - (b) a constable; [^c-eeaa894f3d33faa6f51939f2eb3319e0]...
  - (ba) an SFO officer; or[^c-8839ec362d6d47b310a40f2d1c9eae6e]
  - (d) an an accredited financial investigator who is under the direction of a relevant Director as defined in section 352(5A) of the Act;[^c-8e01f9c1e092d6abf68cf663c7eee273]
- “relevant Director” has the meaning—
  - (a) in the context of an external request, set out in article 6(4);
  - (b) in the context of an external order, set out in article 18(4);
- “relevant property” means property which satisfies the test in section 447(7) of the Act;
- “relevant seizure power” means a power to seize property which is conferred by or by virtue of—
  - (a) article 17B (power to seize property);
  - (b) article 13 (search and seizure warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013;
  - (c) article 13 (search and seizure warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2014;
  - (d) Part 2 of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 as applied by section 16 of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003; or
  - (e) section 17 of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003;
- “SFO officer” means a member of staff of the Serious Fraud Office;[^c-cd74fac10bcba13b536dd7c06e5d92fc]
- “specified property” means property specified in an external order (other than an order that specifies a sum of money).
