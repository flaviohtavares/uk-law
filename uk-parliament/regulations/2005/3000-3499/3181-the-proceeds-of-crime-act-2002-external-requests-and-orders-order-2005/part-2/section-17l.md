# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 17L

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17L. Codes of Practice[^c-32be29f38ac892446022db40ea081599]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Code of Practice made under section 47S of the Act (codes of practice) applies to the powers conferred by this Chapter as it applies to the powers conferred by Part 2 of the Act in relation to search and seizure and detention of property.
