# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213W

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213W. Interpretation[^c-403d5302d229bcbcdcc03dacdb8f36e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “excepted joint owner” has the meaning given by article 181(4) (associated and joint property);
- “forfeiture order” means an order made under article 213L(5) for the forfeiture of listed assets;
- “interest” includes any right (including a right to possession of the property);
- “premises” has the same meaning as in the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984;
- “recoverable property” is to be read in accordance with articles 202 to 207 (recoverable property);
- “relevant officer” has the meaning given by article 213C(9) (searches);
- “share”, in relation to an excepted joint owner, has the meaning given by article 181(4);
- “value” means market value.
