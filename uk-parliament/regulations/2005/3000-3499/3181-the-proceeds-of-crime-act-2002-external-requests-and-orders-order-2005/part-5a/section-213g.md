# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 213G

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213G. Codes of Practice[^key-403d5302d229bcbcdcc03dacdb8f36e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Code of Practice made under section 303G of the Act (code of practice: Secretary of State), or a Code of Practice made under section 303H of the Act (code of practice: Scotland) applies to the powers conferred by article 213C (searches) as it applies to the powers conferred by section 303C of the Act (searches).
