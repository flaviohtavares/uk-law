# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 157

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Interim receiving orders: restrictions on dealing etc. with property[^key-ff7eee24c93953e346d2f8a992fa3aae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An interim receiving order must, subject to any exclusions made in accordance with this article, prohibit any person to whose property the order applies from dealing with the property.

(2) Exclusions may be made when the interim receiving order is made or on an application to vary the order.

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e] An exclusion may, in particular, make provision for the purpose of enabling any person— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e] to meet his reasonable living expenses, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e] to carry on any trade, business, profession or occupation, *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e] and may be made subject to conditions. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-852ac7d279447c6dc2e95ca7015e072e]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S)) An exclusion may, in particular, make provision for the purpose of enabling any person— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) to meet their reasonable living expenses; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) to meet their reasonable legal expenses in connection with the interim receiving order; or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) to carry on any trade, business, profession or occupation. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(3A (Extent: E+W+S)) An exclusion may be made subject to conditions. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(4) Where the court exercises the power to make an exclusion for the purpose of enabling a person to meet legal expenses that he has incurred, or may incur, in respect of proceedings under this Part, it must ensure that the exclusion—

- (a) is limited to reasonable legal expenses that the person has reasonably incurred or that he reasonably incurs,
- (b) specifies the total amount that may be released for legal expenses in pursuance of the exclusion, and
- (c) is made subject to the required conditions (see article 198) in addition to any conditions imposed under paragraph (3).

(5) The court, in deciding whether to make an exclusion for the purposes of enabling a person to meet legal expenses of his in respect of proceedings under this Part—

- (a) must have regard (in particular) to the desirability of the person being represented in any proceedings under this Part in which he is a participant, and
- (b) must, where the person is the respondent, disregard the possibility that legal representation of the person in any such proceedings might, were an exclusion not made, be funded by the Legal Services Commission or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9dbcba42986c05b55b7c322ffa0c4785] made available under arrangements made for the purposes of Part 1 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 or be funded by *(Extent: E+W)* the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission.

(6) If the excluded property is not specified in the order it must be described in the order in general terms.

(7) The power to make exclusions must, subject to paragraph (5), be exercised with a view to ensuring so far as practicable, that the satisfaction of any right of the enforcement authority to recover the property obtained through conduct which satisfies the test in article 202(2) is not unduly prejudiced.
