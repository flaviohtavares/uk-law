# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 179

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Recording of recovery order (Scotland)[^c-969e4c23bb4d44f361c53a454c4ba41e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The clerk of the court must immediately after the making of a recovery order which relates to heritable property situated in Scotland send a certified copy of it to the keeper of the register of inhibitions and adjudications for recording in that register.

(2) Recording under paragraph (1) is to have the effect as from the date of the recovery order, of an inhibition at the instance of the trustee for civil recovery against the person in whom the heritable property was vest prior to that date.
