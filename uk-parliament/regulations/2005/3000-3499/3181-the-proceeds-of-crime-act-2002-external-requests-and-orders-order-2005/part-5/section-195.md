# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 195

> Text as at 2025-04-26.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Payment of interim administrator or trustee (Scotland)[^c-b901a3c221d20e454e2749ce0345d58d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any fees or expenses incurred by an interim administrator, or a trustee for civil recovery appointed by the Court of Session, in the exercise of his functions are to be reimbursed by the Scottish Ministers as soon as is practicable after they have been incurred.

(2) The Scottish Ministers may apply a sum received by them under article 191(2) in making payment of such fees or expenses.

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply in relation to the fees of a trustee for civil recovery if the trustee is a member of their staff.
