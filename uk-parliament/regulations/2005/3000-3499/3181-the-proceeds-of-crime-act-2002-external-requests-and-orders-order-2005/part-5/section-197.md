# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 197

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 197. Scope of powers (Scotland)[^c-82b5c8f912edbe1b260c998ce93580aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Orders under this Chapter may be made by the Court of Session in respect of a person wherever domiciled, resident or present.

(2) But such an order in respect of a person’s moveable property may not be made by the Court of Session where—

- (a) the person is not domiciled, resident or present in Scotland, and
- (b) the property is not situated in Scotland,

unless the conduct which satisfies the test in article 202(2) took place in Scotland.
