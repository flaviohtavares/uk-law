# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 140

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Meaning of “defendant”[^c-95e0dfe6c586a27a6e92c13205e69f60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “defendant”—

- (a) in relation to a restraint order means—
  - (i) in a case in which the first condition in article 94 is satisfied, the alleged offender;
  - (ii) in a case in which the second condition in article 94 is satisfied, the person against whom proceedings for an offence have been started in a country outside the United Kingdom (whether or not he has been convicted);
- (b) in relation to an external order, the person convicted of criminal conduct;
- (c)[^c-bf9635a22c62c7096df329e0681f0c95] for the purpose of Chapter 1A, has the meaning given in article 103A(4).
