# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 103I

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103I. Initial detention of seized property[^key-91aacc5d822454192a27006b1b5713b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies if an appropriate officer seizes property under article 103B.

(2) The property may be detained initially for a period of 48 hours.

(3) The period of 48 hours is to be calculated in accordance with article 103G(7).
