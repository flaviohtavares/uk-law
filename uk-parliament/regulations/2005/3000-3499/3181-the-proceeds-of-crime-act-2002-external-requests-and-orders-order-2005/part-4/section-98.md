> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Appeal to Supreme Court about restraint orders[^key-64e0a425ff8b03fd3b93dbcfc537848c][^key-2578c325e40e2105d4f56d8796174c66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under article 97.

(2) An appeal under this article lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this article the Supreme Court may—

- (a) confirm the decision of the Court of Appeal, or
- (b) make such order as it believes is appropriate.
