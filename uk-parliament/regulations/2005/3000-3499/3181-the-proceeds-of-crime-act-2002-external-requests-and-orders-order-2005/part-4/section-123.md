# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 123

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Satisfaction of external order[^key-689f3ad66b3e627c30afe59bad0d8a45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A registered external order is satisfied when no amount is due under it.

(2) Where such an order authorises the recovery of property specified in it, no further amount is due under the order when all of the specified property has been sold.
