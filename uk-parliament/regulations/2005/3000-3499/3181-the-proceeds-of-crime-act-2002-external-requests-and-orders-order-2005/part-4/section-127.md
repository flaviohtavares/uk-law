# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 127

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Further applications by receivers[^c-062879af4196a7f51044adec60864b9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to a receiver appointed under article 101 or 113[^c-4ecd12cc43e808d36112539e4a79ff5e].

(2) The receiver may apply to—

- (a) the High Court if he is appointed under article 101;
- (b) the Crown Court if he is appointed under article 113 [^c-d3f321078f94de1135b7e001caf42a32]...,

for an order giving directions as to the exercise of his powers.

(3) The following persons may apply to the High Court if the receiver is appointed under article 101 or to the Crown Court if the receiver is appointed under article 113 [^c-4328c5187a1047787d3db16966f21ce4]...—

- (a) any person affected by action taken by the receiver;
- (b) any person who may be affected by action the receiver proposes to take.

(4) On an application under this article the court may make such order as it believes is appropriate.
