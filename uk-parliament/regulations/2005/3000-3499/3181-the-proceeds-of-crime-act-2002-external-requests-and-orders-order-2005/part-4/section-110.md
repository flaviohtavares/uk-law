# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 110

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Appeal to Supreme Court about external orders[^c-07bab623d8480ed5f13d832fb72797c2][^c-7f0c842d08763c1cf55cb54b129c5247]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under article 109.

(2) An appeal under this article lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this article the Supreme Court may—

- (a) confirm or set aside the decision of the Court of Appeal, or
- (b) direct the Crown Court to register the external order (or so much of it as relates to property other than that to which article 107(6) applies).
