# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 103L

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103L. Codes of Practice[^key-91aacc5d822454192a27006b1b5713b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Code of Practice made under section 195S of the Act (codes of practice: Secretary of State), or a Code of Practice made under section 195T of the Act (codes of practice: Department of Justice), applies to the powers conferred by this Chapter as it applies to the powers conferred by Part 4 of the Act in relation to search and seizure and detention of property.
