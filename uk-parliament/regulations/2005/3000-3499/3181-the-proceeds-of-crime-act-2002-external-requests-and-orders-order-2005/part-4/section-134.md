# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 134

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Procedure on appeal to Supreme Court under Part 4[^key-7221a7da5650674c27d968ca3203c7d1][^key-dd53cb971fb4f405c9e6e5ba8089af97]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to appeals to the Supreme Court[^key-78e980ea7f8283b4d27733f47bbb04c3] under this Part, the Secretary of State may make an order containing provision corresponding to any provision in the Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Act 1980, subject to any specified modifications.
