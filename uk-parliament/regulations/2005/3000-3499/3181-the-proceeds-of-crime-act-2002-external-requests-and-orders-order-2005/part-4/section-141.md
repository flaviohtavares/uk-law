# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 141

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Other interpretation[^key-b9f90b359e1b6c7285cf831f0fa5dcb6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “appropriate officer” means—[^key-a231cca53b694524b0d1e31f46ea7110]
  - (a) an officer of Revenue and Customs;
  - (b) a constable; or
  - (c) an accredited financial investigator who is under the direction of a relevant Director as defined in section 352(5A) of the Act;
- “relevant Director” has the meaning—
  - (a) in the context of an external request, set out in article 93(4);
  - (b) in the context of an external order, set out in article 104(4);
- “relevant property” means property which satisfies the test in section 447(7) of the Act;
- “relevant seizure power” means a power to seize property which is conferred by or by virtue of—
  - (a) article 103B (power to seize property);
  - (b) article 13 (search and seizure warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013;
  - (c) article 13 (search and seizure warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2014;
  - (d) Part 3 or 4 of the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (including as applied by order under Article 85(1) of that Order.
- “specified property” means property specified in an external order (other than an order that specifies a sum of money).
