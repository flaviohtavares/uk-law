# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 131

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Appeal to the Supreme Court about receivers[^key-dfbfbad5d2325e3f4f45253a5198c530][^key-c65a074c8d77a44ab2c7e47bf053e15d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court[^key-2c1782d9b75b5475350c2b43403f25f2] from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under article 130.

(2) An appeal under this article lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this article the Supreme Court[^key-c4acbf5314dd13e3fba4e73169498c35] may—

- (a) confirm the decision of the Court Appeal, or
- (b) make such order as it believes is appropriate.
