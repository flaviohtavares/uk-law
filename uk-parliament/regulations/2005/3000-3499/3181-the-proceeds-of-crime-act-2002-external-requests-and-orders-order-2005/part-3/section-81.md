# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 81

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Protection of persons affected[^c-1344f49d7ddfe6f735a407e6e1e2c5f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This paragraph applies where an administrator is appointed under article 63 or 73.

(2) The following persons may apply to the court—

- (a) any person affected by action taken by the administrator;
- (b) any person who may be affected by action the administrator proposes to take.

(3) On an application under this article the court may make such order as it thinks appropriate.
