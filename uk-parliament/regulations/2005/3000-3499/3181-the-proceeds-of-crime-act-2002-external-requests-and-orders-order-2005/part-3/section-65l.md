# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 65L

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65L. Guidance by Lord Advocate[^key-975775a42ef1622cf6b07daab5659aef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Lord Advocate may issue guidance in connection with—

- (a) the carrying out by appropriate officers of the functions conferred by articles 65B to 65G;
- (b) the carrying out by senior officers of their functions under article 65F; and
- (c) the detention of property under or by virtue of articles 58A, 60A, 65I and 65J.

(2) The Lord Advocate must publish any guidance issued under this article.
