# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 60

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Appeals[^key-94becf50b6ad982e081c7eac05166369]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If on an application for a restraint order the court decides not to make one, the Lord Advocate may reclaim against the decision.

(2) The Lord Advocate and any person affected by the order may reclaim against the decision of the court on an application under article 59(4).
