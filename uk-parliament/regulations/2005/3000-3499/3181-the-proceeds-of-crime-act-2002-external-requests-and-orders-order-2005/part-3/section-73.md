# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 73

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Appointment of enforcement administrators[^key-290607bb3603b833369bcb3e4ba7f1a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies if—

- (a) an external order is registered;
- (b) it is not satisfied; and
- (c) in the case of an external order for the recovery of a specified sum of money, any period specified by order under article 72 has expired.

(2) On the application of the Lord Advocate the court may appoint an administrator in respect of—

- (a) where the external order is for the recovery of a specified sum of money, realisable property;
- (b) where the external order is for the recovery of specified property, that property.
