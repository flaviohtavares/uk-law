# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, section 92

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Other interpretation[^c-d15c56104f68b2a5546b37e4e26328aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “appropriate officer” means—[^c-e14d88298bc2c7483dc46f94fa3cda83]
  - (a) an officer of Revenue and Customs; or
  - (b) a constable;
- “court” means the Court of Session;
- “relevant property” means property which satisfies the test set out in section 447(7) of the Act;
- “relevant seizure power” means a power to seize property which is conferred by or by virtue of—
  - (a) article 65B (power to seize property);
  - (b) article 47 (search warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013;
  - (c) article 13 (search warrants) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) (Scotland) Order 2015; or
  - (d) section 18 (warrants in Scotland) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003;
- “specified property” means that property specified in the external request (other than a request that specifies a sum of money).
