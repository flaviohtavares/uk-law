# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — POWERS OF TRUSTEE FOR CIVIL RECOVERY

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 178

### 1. Sale[^key-844fb7fb7d9dd7aba5d4553890001a74]

Power to sell the property or any part of it or interest in it.

### 2. Expenditure[^key-f6ecf89ea7461726d0956de470b3afe2]

Power to incur expenditure for the purpose of—

- (a) acquiring any part of the property, or any interest in it, which is not vested in him,
- (b) dscharging any liabilities, or extinguishing any rights, to which the property is subject.

### 3. Management[^key-a1e920dd6716024fed6e4cd895a4446d]

(1) Power to manage property.

(2) Managing property includes doing anything mentioned in paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 1.

### 4. Legal proceedings[^key-f40b1f1dbc417d6954184a447738437f]

Power to start, carry on or defend any legal proceedings in respect of the property.

### 5. Compromise[^key-98d4ec7c2f0f7b80866c117fbbbe59e8]

Power to make any compromise or other arrangement in connection with any claim relating to the property.

### 6. Supplementary[^key-55e33bcaea9fe4376c079812323d354a]

(1) For the purpose of, or in connection with, the exercise of any of his powers—

- (a) power by his official name to do any of the things mentioned in sub-paragraph (2),
- (b) power to do any other act which is necessary or expedient.

(2) Those things are—

- (a) holding property,
- (b) entering into contracts,
- (c) suing and being sued,
- (d) employing agents,
- (e) executing a power of attorney, deed or other instrument.
