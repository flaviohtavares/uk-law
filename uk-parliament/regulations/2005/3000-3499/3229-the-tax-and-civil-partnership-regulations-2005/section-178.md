# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 178

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178.[^c-ee1f8ba0be0897192b6e5a4ade30aab7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 203 (inheritance tax exemptions: amendments of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984), in subsection (5), in the words inserted into section 152 of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984, after “widower” insert “, surviving civil partner”.
