# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 179

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179.[^key-b2cc6410987f30dc57c39c3a64641207]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 15 (charge to income tax on benefits received by former owner of property) —

- (a) in paragraph 8(1)(b), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”, and
- (b) in paragraph 10(1)(b) and (c), (2)(a) and (b) and (3), after “spouse”, in each place, insert “or civil partner”.
