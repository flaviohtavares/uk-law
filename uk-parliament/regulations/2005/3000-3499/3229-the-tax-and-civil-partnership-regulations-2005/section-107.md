# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 107

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107.[^key-080e893fe85176b6d92ebada60e1ba9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 58 (husband and wife) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for the words from the beginning to “both” substitute —

> - If, in any year of assessment, —
>   - (a) an individual is living with his spouse or civil partner, and
>   - (b) one of them disposes of an asset to the other,
>
>   both

.

(3) In the heading, for “Husband and wife” substitute “Spouses and civil partners”.
