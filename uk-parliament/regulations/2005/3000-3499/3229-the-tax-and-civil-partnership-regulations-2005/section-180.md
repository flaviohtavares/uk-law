# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 180

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180.[^c-8889ecc9b7c93d3432ad6beff5ce1ec0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Part 2 of Schedule 28 (pension death benefit rules: defined benefits and money purchase arrangements), in paragraph 15 —

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1), after “married to” insert “, or a civil partner of,”,
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1A), after “married to” insert “, or a civil partner of,”, and
- (c) in the opening words of sub-paragraph (3), after “married to” insert “, or a civil partner of,”.
