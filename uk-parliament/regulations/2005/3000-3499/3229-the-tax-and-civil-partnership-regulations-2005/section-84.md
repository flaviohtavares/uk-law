# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 84

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84.[^key-e054f429adc42210034d0c94083215a8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 621 (other approved contracts) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(a), for “wife or husband” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.

(3) In subsection (3) —

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “wife or husband” substitute “spouse or civil partner”, and
- (b) in paragraph (c), for “wife or husband” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.

(4) In subsection (5), for “wives, husbands” substitute “spouses, civil partners”.
