# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 103

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103.[^key-90bee0ce9a1f38a09fd54772005e414a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*
