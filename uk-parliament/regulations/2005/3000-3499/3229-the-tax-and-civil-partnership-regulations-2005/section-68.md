# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 68

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68.[^c-8cb401f20e8fe10a76512c8a47bd2575]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 360 (loan to buy interest in close company), in subsection (3A)[^f00020], for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
