# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 151

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151.[^key-0ddc521ee1b8ab1689d78ebe919923f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 400 (interpretation), in subsection (1) —

- (a) after the definition of “ex-spouse” insert —

  > - “former civil partner” means a member of a civil partnership that has been dissolved or annulled and, in relation to any person, means the other member of a civil partnership with that person that has been dissolved or annulled;

  , and
- (b) in the definition of “relative” —
  - (i) in paragraph (a), for “wife or husband” substitute “spouse or civil partner”, and
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), after “widower” insert “or surviving civil partner”.
