# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 58

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58.[^c-7ee24adf43786e60ee08feda59186191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 266A[^f00014] (life assurance premiums paid by employer), in subsection (3)(b), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner” and after “widower” insert “or surviving civil partner”.
