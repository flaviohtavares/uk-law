# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 83

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83.[^c-3a29b5c42bce4a042bab5a993edd1fa5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 620 (retirement annuities: qualifying premiums) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2) —

- (a) in paragraph (c), after “widower”, in both places, insert “or surviving civil partner”, and
- (b) in paragraph (d), after “widower” insert “or surviving civil partner”.

(3) In subsection (4) —

- (a) in paragraph (a), after “widower” insert “or surviving civil partner”, and
- (b) in paragraph (d), after “(or re-marriage)” insert “or on the formation of a civil partnership (or another civil partnership)”.
