# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 136

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136.[^c-aa71ad78a6bee218509e50549b7654ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 29 (gains and losses of a company from intangible fixed assets), in paragraph 101(2), for “wife or husband”, in each place, substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
