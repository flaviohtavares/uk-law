# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 65

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65.[^c-e3185d45072cd389bbab9d3cc2b594c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 304 (husband and wife) —

- (a) in subsection (5), as that section applies to shares issued on or before 31st December 1993 (business expansion scheme), and
- (b) in subsection (1), as that section applies to shares issued after 31st December 1993 (enterprise investment scheme),

for “a married man to his wife or a married woman to her husband” substitute “an individual to his spouse or civil partner”, and

- (c) in the heading, for “Husband and wife” substitute “Spouses and civil partners”.
