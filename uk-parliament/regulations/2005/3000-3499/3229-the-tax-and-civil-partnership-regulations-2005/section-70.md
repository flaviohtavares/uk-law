# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 70

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70.[^c-c70d52221cc601d7d304de42ac2a9e59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 381 (further relief for individuals for losses in early years of trade), in subsection (5)(a), for “and living with another individual” substitute “, or a civil partner of, another individual with whom he is living together and”.
