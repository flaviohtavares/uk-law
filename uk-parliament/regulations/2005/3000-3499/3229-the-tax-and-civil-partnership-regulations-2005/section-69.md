# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 69

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69.[^key-b2599f15f5098d475878c665c6dfc927]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 360A[^f00021] (meaning of “material interest” in section 360), in subsection (10), for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
