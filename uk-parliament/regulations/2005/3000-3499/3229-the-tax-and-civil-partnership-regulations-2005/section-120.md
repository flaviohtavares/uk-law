# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 120

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120.[^c-3d8d15af2a67d5a8f94fe88619d328d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 253 (relief for loans to traders) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3)(c), after “spouses” insert “or civil partners”.

(3) In subsection (4)(d), after “spouses”, in both places, insert “or civil partners”.

(4) In subsection (14), after paragraph (a) insert —

> - (aa) “civil partners” means civil partners who are living together (construed in accordance with section 288(3)),

.
