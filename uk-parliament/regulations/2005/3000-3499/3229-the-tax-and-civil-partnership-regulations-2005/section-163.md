# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 163

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 163.[^key-4e9ee75081e7a67b7fded72281feea92]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 665 (income support: exempt unless payable to member of couple involved in trade dispute) —

- (a) in subsection (1)(a), omit “married or unmarried”, and
- (b) in subsection (2), for ““married couple” and “unmarried couple” have” substitute ““couple” has”.
