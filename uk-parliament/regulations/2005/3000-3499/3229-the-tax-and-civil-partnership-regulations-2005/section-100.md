# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 100

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100.[^key-0b3313def0c37a22509a66715f967d3a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 839 (connected persons), in subsections (2) and (4), for “wife or husband”, in each place, substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
