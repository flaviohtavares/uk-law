# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 197

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 197.[^key-90e27c5aa5bd57a9f5ee735c268a0ff5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 730 (foreign maintenance payments) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (4)(a), after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(3) In subsection (5), after “annulled” insert “, and “civil partnership” includes a civil partnership that has been dissolved or annulled”.
