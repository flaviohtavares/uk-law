# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 189

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189.[^c-b61dfca6c1657b2f01750992c4ab8f20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 626 (exception for outright gifts between spouses) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(b), after “other” insert “or one civil partner to the other”.

(3) In the heading, after “spouses” insert “or civil partners”.
