# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 88

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88.[^c-3e8378a5ff463d1ab2380345305ca442]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 634 (annuity to member), in subsection (5)(a), for “marriage of the annuitant” substitute “annuitant marrying or forming a civil partnership”.
