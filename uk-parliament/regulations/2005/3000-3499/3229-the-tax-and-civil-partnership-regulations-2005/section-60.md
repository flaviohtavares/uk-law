# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 60

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60.[^c-aa9b93c7b1312a7a9c1753a03f151b4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 278 (personal reliefs: non-residents), in subsection (2)(e), for “, or a widower whose late wife,” substitute “, a widower whose late wife or a surviving civil partner whose late civil partner,”.

(2) In consequence of paragraph (1), omit section 31(2) of the Finance Act 1988[^f00015].
