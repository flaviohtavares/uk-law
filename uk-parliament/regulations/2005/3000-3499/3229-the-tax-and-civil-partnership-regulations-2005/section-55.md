# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 55

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55.[^c-e8b15729ce9fa43861d627694271ecb7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 257C[^f00013] (indexation of amounts in sections 257 and 257A) —

- (a) in the opening words of subsection (1), for “and 257A” substitute “, 257A and 257AB”,
- (b) in subsection (1)(a), for “and 257A(5)” substitute “, 257A(5) and 257AB(4)”,
- (c) in subsection (3), for “and 257A” substitute “, 257A and 257AB”, and
- (d) in the heading, for “and 257A” substitute “, 257A and 257AB”.
