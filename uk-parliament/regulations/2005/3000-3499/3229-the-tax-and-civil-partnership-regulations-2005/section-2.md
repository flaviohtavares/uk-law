# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 2

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Finance Act 1984[^key-3af50c038fa65f4aa9fa7eeb89354299]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 14 to the Finance Act 1984[^f00002] (beneficiary’s liability for tax on gains of non-resident trustees), in paragraph 1(1), in the definition of “close relative”, after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
