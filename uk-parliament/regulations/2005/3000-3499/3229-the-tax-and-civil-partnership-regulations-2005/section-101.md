# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 101

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101.[^key-e8a7eabe60872c691fb1986515193110]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 14 (provisions ancillary to section 266) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(1) —

- (a) for “The references in section 266” substitute —

  > - In section 266 —
  >   - (a) references

  , and
- (b) at the end insert —

  > - (b) references to an individual’s civil partner shall include any person who was that individual’s civil partner at the time the insurance or contract was made.

  .

(3) In the heading before paragraph 1, for “*Husband and wife*” substitute “*Spouses and civil partners*”.
