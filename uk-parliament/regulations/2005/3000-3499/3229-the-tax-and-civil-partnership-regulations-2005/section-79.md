# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 79

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79.[^c-0012fcfee0bdc3f961d47b1624ec0ac7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 590 (conditions for approval of retirement benefit schemes) —

- (a) in subsections (2)(aa), (3)(ba) and (d), (3A), (4B), (4C) and (4F), after “ex-spouse”, in each place, insert “or former civil partner”,
- (b) in subsection (2)(aa)(ii), after “widower,” insert “surviving civil partner,”, and
- (c) in subsections (3)(b) and (ba), (3A), (4B), (4C) and (4F), after “widower”, in each place, insert “or surviving civil partner”.
