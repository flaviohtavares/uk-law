# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 125

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125.[^c-0185cdb634e582a02de5a999a0a45262]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 4A (disposal of interest in settled property: deemed disposal of underlying assets), in paragraph 7(5)(b) —

- (a) after “spouse”, in each place, insert “or civil partner”, and
- (b) after “married” insert “or to be civil partners of each other”.
