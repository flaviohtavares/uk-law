# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 157

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157.[^c-bffffca476c43a5ea794c5f0b212ae59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 588 (meaning of “employee” and “ex-spouse”) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), after the definition of “ex-spouse” insert —

> - “former civil partner” means a member of a civil partnership which has been dissolved or annulled and, in relation to any person, means the other member of a civil partnership with that person which has been dissolved or annulled;

.

(3) In the heading, after ““employee”” insert “, “former civil partner””.
