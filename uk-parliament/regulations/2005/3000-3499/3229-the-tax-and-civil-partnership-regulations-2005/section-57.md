# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 57

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57.[^c-ea3c02f9aee96b19fe36831f63de2992]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 266 (life assurance premiums) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)(b) and (c ), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.

(3) In subsection (9), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.

(4) In subsection (11)(a) —

- (a) after “spouse,” insert “civil partner,”, and
- (b) after “widower” insert “, surviving civil partner”.
