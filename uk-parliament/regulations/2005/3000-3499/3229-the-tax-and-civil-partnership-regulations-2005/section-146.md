# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 146

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146.[^key-3726f631a91c5005f0fa7e5dae266912]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 371 (travel costs and expenses where duties performed abroad: visiting spouse’s or child’s travel) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsections (1)(a)(i) and (5), after “spouse” insert “, civil partner”.

(3) In the heading, after “spouse's” insert “, civil partner's”.
