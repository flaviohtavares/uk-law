# The Tax and Civil Partnership Regulations 2005, section 104

> Text as at 2010-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Finance Act 1990[^key-bb447cc370fded9232ed268b051be975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 25 of the Finance Act 1990 [^f00027] (donations to charity by individuals), in subsection (9A)[^f00028], for “and 257A(5)” substitute “, 257A(5) and 257AB(4)”.
