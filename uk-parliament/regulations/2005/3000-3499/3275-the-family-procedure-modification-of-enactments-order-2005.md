# The Family Procedure (Modification of Enactments) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3275

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Family Procedure (Modification of Enactments) Order 2005

Made

22nd November 2005

Coming into force

30th December 2005

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 80 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Family Procedure (Modification of Enactments) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 30th December 2005.

**2.** In section 97(1)(a) and (2) of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (summons to witness)[^f00002] after “hearing of a complaint” insert “or of an application under the Adoption and Children Act [2002 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/38)”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Catherine M. Ashton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

22nd November 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 97(1)(a) of the Magistrates' Courts Act [1980 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/38) so as to enable a justice of the peace at the hearing of an application under the Adoption and Children Act [2002 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/38) to issue a witness summons. It also amends section 97(2) to prevent a warrant being issued instead of a summons in the hearing of an application under the 2002 Act.
