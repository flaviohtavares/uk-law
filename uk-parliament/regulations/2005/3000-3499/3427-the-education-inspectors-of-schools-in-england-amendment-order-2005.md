# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3427

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

14th December 2005

Coming into force

15th December 2005

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2005

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Education Act 2005 [^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (Amendment) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 15th December 2005**.**

**2.** In the Schedule to The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2005 [^f00002] the name “Richard Henry Harris” is omitted.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2005 by removing Richard Henry Harris as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
