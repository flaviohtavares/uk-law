# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 17

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Finance

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to[^c-2441a3271f46403b5e005dbc33895d0f] regulation 20C[^c-e15bef43a88d90339970613739562922], the agreement must contain a term which has the effect of requiring—

- (a) NHS England[^c-d1caf22cc98adae401c0bdbf330d1379] to make payments to the contractor under the agreement promptly and in accordance with both the terms of the agreement and any other conditions relating to the payment contained in directions given by the Secretary of State under section 28E(3A) of the Act [^c12419121] (personal medical or dental services: regulations);
- (b) the contractor to make payments promptly to NHS England and in accordance with both the terms of the agreement and any other conditions relating to the payment contained in directions given by the Secretary of State under section 17 (Secretary of State's directions: exercise of functions) or 28E(3A) of the Act.

(2) The obligation referred to in paragraph (1) is subject to any right the Relevant Body has to set off against an amount payable to the contractor an amount that—

- (a) is owed by the contractor to NHS England under the agreement;
- (b) has been paid to the contractor owing to an error or in circumstances when it was not due; or
- (c) NHS England may withhold from the contractor in accordance with the terms of the agreement or any other applicable provisions contained in directions given by the Secretary of State under section 28E(3A).

(3) Subject to[^c-05cf3a27c578699f23f89b3e225be2ed] regulation 20C[^c-6691b50fea9afe5c10dfb44764316f60], the agreement must contain a term to the effect that where, pursuant to directions under section 17 or 28E(3A) of the Act, NHS England is required to make a payment to a contractor under an agreement but subject to conditions, those conditions are to be a term of the agreement.
