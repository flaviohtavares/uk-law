# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 13A

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13A. Required number of urgent treatments[^key-cb12bab3c2afa65e9681e89b8fd203a9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an agreement with a relevant contractor includes the provision of mandatory services, the agreement must specify the required number of urgent treatments the contractor must complete in each financial year.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3), (4) and (6), the required number of urgent treatments is calculated as follows, and rounded up to the nearest integer—

[formula: UT equals RCV over £10,000 multiplied by 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3373)

where—

- “**RCV**” means the relevant contract value;
- “**UT**” means the required number of urgent treatments.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), NHS England may, at any time during the period beginning with 30th October and ending with 31st March, reduce the required number of urgent treatments for that financial year.

(4) Any reduction in the required number of urgent treatments in accordance with paragraph (3) must not exceed 15% of the number required to be specified under paragraph (1).

(5) Any reduction by NHS England in the required number of urgent treatments under paragraph (3) must be communicated to the relevant contractor in writing.

(6) If the RCV is adjusted at any time in the financial year, except as a result of an annual uprating to the contract value as determined by the Secretary of State,[^key-77b2a5a191bd6667f8cacbbd6061139c] the required number of treatments under paragraph (1) must be recalculated in accordance with paragraph (2) and adjusted accordingly.

(7) In this regulation—

- “**community dental services**” has the meaning given by regulation 5 of the National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012;
- “**pre-2006 child-only contractor**” means a contractor who—
  - (a) entered into the agreement before 1st April 2006, and
  - (b) only provides dental services to children under that agreement;
- “**relevant contractor**” means a contractor who provides primary dental services other than—
  - (a) a contractor who is required to provide 99 or fewer units of dental activity every financial year under the agreement;
  - (b) a contractor who only provides community dental services;
  - (c) a pre-2006 child-only contractor;
  - (d) a contractor who only provides either or both of the following—
    - (i) dental public health services;
    - (ii) dental services in a secure and detained estate[^key-76ecc76f444a272b21b1af76572fd9f2].
