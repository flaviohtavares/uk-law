# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 10

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. NHS contracts

*Extent: E+W*

If the contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the agreement must state that it is an NHS contract.
