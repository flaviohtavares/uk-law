# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Units of dental activity

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to[^c-8127cf063e6be4921f61c18b815986e9] regulation 20C[^c-80b6b981bdec993aff50746d23373bb8], where an agreement includes the provision of mandatory or advanced mandatory services, the agreement must specify the number of units of dental activity to be provided by the contractor—

- (a) where the agreement begins on 1st April, in each financial year or, by virtue of the duration of the agreement, part financial year; or
- (b) where the agreement begins on a date other than 1st April, in the remainder of the financial year in which the agreement begins, and in each financial year thereafter.

(2) Subject to regulation 20C, an agreement must contain terms which have the effect of those specified in Parts 1 and 3[^c-dfba5def3f0ffd68902eb3f80b2cc4c7] of Schedule 2 in relation to the calculation of the number of units of dental activity that the contractor has provided under the agreement.
