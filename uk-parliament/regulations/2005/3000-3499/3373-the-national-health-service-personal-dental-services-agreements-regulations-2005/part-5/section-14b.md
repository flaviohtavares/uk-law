# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 14B

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14B. Capitated patient figures for contractors who elect to enter into a prototype agreement[^key-e74ceaa9e7919408336fcf2aa8d4cc76]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a contractor has elected to enter into a prototype agreement, except where the contractor is a CDS prototype contractor,[^key-34cc0bfae7af228657f2c58b818cb954] the prototype agreement must specify the number of patients to whom the contractor is expected to have provided primary dental services (known as the capitated patient figure) by the end of each financial year.
