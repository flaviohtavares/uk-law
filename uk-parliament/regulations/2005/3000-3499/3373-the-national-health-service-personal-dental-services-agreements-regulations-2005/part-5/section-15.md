# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 15

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Under-provision of units of dental activity, units of orthodontic activity or required number of urgent treatments[^c-2961ab698c5092396a2c6c6e3497c784]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The agreement shall provide that NHS England[^c-d1caf22cc98adae401c0bdbf330d1379] shall not, pursuant to Part 9 of Schedule 3 (variation and termination of agreements), be entitled to take any action for breach of a term of the agreement giving effect to regulation 13 , 13A[^c-afa18c2770f340aa9303ae7994a93bc1] or 14 (including termination of the agreement) where paragraph (2) applies.

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), this paragraph applies where the contractor has failed to provide—

- (a) the number of units of dental activity [^c-c55b699af7253b67c34c984d47ac7dfb]...; [^c-52c9e552b7646c6c353b7dc5537dd854]...
- (b) the number of units of orthodontic activity,or[^c-dae7a5d7b0aa90d3740a028f42b48db6]
- (c)[^c-b93adc0183a40061ed043cfc6a4b396e] the required number of urgent treatments,

it is contracted to provide pursuant to a term of the agreement giving effect to regulation 13 , 13A[^c-1bc2b0de1e2e70c55b7af83c9cd66e53] or 14 where—

- (i) that failure amounts to 4 per cent or less of the total number of units of dental activity referred to in sub-paragraph (a)[^c-0eae45470c611afdf999f7ec5e40f026] , units of orthodontic activity or the required number of urgent treatments[^c-6d9e64cb522166172e36d54a9a8ea779] that ought to have been provided; and
- (ii) the contractor agrees to provide and does so provide the units or urgent treatments[^c-e09fa2d8d54329b3c74e2843bcb852c5] it has failed to provide within such period as NHS England specifies in writing, such period to consist of not less than 60 days.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall not prevent NHS England from taking action under Part 9 of Schedule 3 for breach of contract (including terminating the agreement) on other grounds.

(4) In the case of an agreement with a duration period of less than 12 months, the period of 60 days in paragraph (2)(ii) may not apply if NHS England considers it not appropriate or considers that another period of less than 60 days should apply.
