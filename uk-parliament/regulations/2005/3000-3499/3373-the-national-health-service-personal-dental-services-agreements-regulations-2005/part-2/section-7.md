## 7. Appeal

*Extent: E+W*

A person who has been served with a notice under regulation 6(1) may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-a0b5399fa97997697dc18c864e8de5d7] against the decision of NHS England[^key-d1caf22cc98adae401c0bdbf330d1379] that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 are not met [^key-ce81d0b9d7cfb5ee8fedc5bcb8df80e2]... .
