# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005, section 6

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Reasons

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where NHS England[^key-d1caf22cc98adae401c0bdbf330d1379] is of the view that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 for entering into an agreement are not met it shall notify in writing the person or persons intending to enter into the agreement of its view and its reasons for that view and of his, its, or their right of appeal under regulation 7.

(2)[^key-cea58eba6dab51ff2441097930590dae] NHS England shall also notify in writing of its view and its reasons for that view to—

- (a) a director, chief executive or secretary of a dental corporation;
- (b) a director, chief executive or secretary of a company limited by shares; or
- (c) the members of a limited liability partnership,

who is, or are, notified under paragraph (1) where its reasons for the decision relates to that person or persons.
