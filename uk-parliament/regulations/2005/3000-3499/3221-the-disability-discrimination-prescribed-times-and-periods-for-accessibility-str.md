# The Disability Discrimination (Prescribed Times and Periods for Accessibility Strategies and Plans for Schools) (England) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3221

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

DISABLED PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Disability Discrimination (Prescribed Times and Periods for Accessibility Strategies and Plans for Schools) (England) Regulations 2005

Made

18th November 2005

Laid before Parliament

29th November 2005

Coming into force

31st December 2005

In the exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 28D(1)(b), (2), (8)(b), and (9) and section 67(3)(a) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Disability Discrimination (Prescribed Times and Periods for Accessibility Strategies and Plans for Schools) (England) Regulations 2005 and, except for regulation 4, shall come into force on 31<sup>st</sup> December 2005.

(2) Regulation 4 shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2006.

(3) These Regulations apply only in relation to England.

(4) In these Regulations “the Act” means the Disability Discrimination Act.

## 2. Prescribed times and periods for further accessibility strategies

(1) For the purposes of sub-section (1)(b) of section 28D of the Act, the prescribed time shall be 1<sup>st</sup> March 2006 and subsequently at three yearly intervals.

(2) For the purposes of sub-section (2) of section 28 D of the Act, the prescribed period shall be a period of three years commencing on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2006 and subsequently commencing on 1<sup>st</sup> April at three yearly intervals.

## 3. Prescribed times and periods for further accessibility plans

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), for the purposes of subsection (8)(b) of section 28D of the Act, the prescribed time shall be 1<sup>st</sup> March 2006 and subsequently at three yearly intervals.

(2) Where a school is established after any such prescribed time—

- (a) the first prescribed time for that school shall be one month after the date on which the school is established; and
- (b) the prescribed time thereafter shall be the same as for any school which was open on 1<sup>st</sup> March 2006.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), for the purposes of sub-section (9) of section 28D of the Act, the prescribed period shall be a period of three years, commencing on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2006 and subsequently commencing on 1<sup>st</sup> April at three yearly intervals.

(4) Where a school is established after the commencement of any such prescribed period—

- (a) the first prescribed period for that school shall be the period commencing one month after the prescribed time referred to in paragraph (2) and ending at the same time as the prescribed period for any school which was open on 1<sup>st</sup> March 2006; and
- (b) the prescribed period thereafter shall be the same as for any school which was open on 1<sup>st</sup> March 2006.

## 4. Revocation

The Disability Discrimination (Prescribed Periods for Accessibility Strategies and Plans for Schools (England) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are revoked.

Andrew Adonis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education and Skills

18th November 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe—

- (a) the times by which local education authorities must prepare, in relation to schools for which they are the responsible body, further accessibility strategies to facilitate the participation of disabled pupils in education and associated services provided by schools *(regulation 2(1))*; and
- (b) the periods to which such accessibility strategies must relate *(regulation 2(2))*.

These Regulations also prescribe—

- (c) the times by which the responsible bodies for schools must prepare further accessibility plans to facilitate the participation of disabled pupils in education and associated services provided by schools*(regulation 3(1 and (2)))*; and
- (d) the periods to which such plans must relate *(regulation 3(3)and (4))*.
