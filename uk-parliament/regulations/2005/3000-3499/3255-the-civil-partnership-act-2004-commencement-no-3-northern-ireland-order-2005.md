# The Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3255 (C. 139)

CIVIL PARTNERSHIP, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 2005

Made

23rd November 2005

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 263 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001], and after having consulted the Secretary of State for Trade and Industry, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- (a) “the Act” means the Civil Partnership Act 2004; and
- (b) “the 1984 Order” means the Family Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1984[^f00002].

## 2. Commencement

(1) The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule shall come into force on 5th December 2005.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), Schedule 29 shall, in so far as it is not already in force, come into force on 5th December 2005.

(3) Paragraph 64(3) of Schedule 29 shall only be commenced in so far as it substitutes Parts 1 and 2 of the Table in article 18 of the 1984 Order.

(4) Paragraphs 64(6), 64(7) and 93 of Schedule 29 shall not be commenced by this Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 23rd November 2005.

Ethne E. Harkness

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th December 2005 the provisions of the Civil Partnership Act [2004 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33) (“the Act”) listed in the Schedule, with the following exceptions:

- Paragraph 3 of Schedule 12 to the Act (Prohibited degrees of relationship: Northern Ireland) is not commenced by this Order;
- Part 6 of Schedule 15 to the Act and paragraph 10(4)(b), (5)(b) and (9)(c) of Schedule 17 to the Act, which relate to pension protection fund compensation, are not commenced by this Order;
- Paragraph 19 of Schedule 22 to the Act (which specifies Article 2(4) of the Registered Homes (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/3204 (N.I. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1992/3204)) for the purposes of section 246 of the Act), is not to be commenced by this Order;
- Paragraph 64(3) of Schedule 29 to the Act, which amends Article 18 of the Family Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 (S.I. [1984/1984 (N.I. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1984/1984)) (marriages with prohibited degrees of relationship), is only partially commenced by this Order;
- Paragraph 64(6) and 64(7) of Schedule 29 to the Act, which amends Article 18 of the Family Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 (S.I. [1984/1984 (N.I. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1984/1984)) (marriages within prohibited degrees of relationship), is not commenced by this Order;
- Paragraph 93 of Schedule 29 to the Act (which amends section 97E(1)(b) of the Pensions Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act [1993 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49)), is not commenced by this Order.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument. A regulatory impact assessment identifying the impact on business and the exchequer of the provisions in the Act was published at the time the Act received Royal Assent. It is available from http://www.dti.gso.uk/access/ria/index.htm.

(This note is not part of the Order)
