# The Docklands Light Railway (Capacity Enhancement) Order 2005, section 25

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Application of Part I of the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965[^key-4b4ddeb0a139fe9c8a7c31fae692e48c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Part I of the 1965 Act, in so far as not modified by or inconsistent with the provisions of this Order, shall apply to the acquisition of land under this Order—

- (a) as it applies to a compulsory purchase to which the 1981 Act applies; and
- (b) as if this Order were a compulsory purchase order under that Act.

(2) Part I of the 1965 Act, as so applied, shall have effect as if section 4 (which provides a time limit for compulsory purchase of land) and paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 3 (which makes provision as to the giving of bonds) were omitted.
