# The Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships No.2) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument revokes and replaces article 4 of [S.I. 2005/3042](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3042) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2005 No. 3284

CIVIL PARTNERSHIP, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships No.2) Order 2005

Made

28th November 2005

Laid before Parliament

30th November 2005

Coming into force

5th December 2005

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 259 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001]:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships No.2) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 5<sup>th</sup> December 2005.

(2) In this Order “recognised overseas relationship” means an overseas relationship which is treated as a civil partnership pursuant to chapter 2 of Part 5 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004.

**2.** (1) Where—

- (a) a person is treated as a civil partner by virtue of having registered a recognised overseas relationship under the relevant law before 5<sup>th</sup> December 2005; and
- (b) he has, before 5<sup>th</sup> December 2005, made a will,

section 18B of the Wills Act 1837[^f00002] is not to apply to that will.

(2) Article 4 of the Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships) Order 2005[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Catherine. M. Ashton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

Dated 28th November

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces article 4 of the Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/3042](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3042)).

Article 2(1) saves a will made before 5<sup>th</sup> December 2005 by a person who has before that date formed an overseas relationship, from becoming invalid on recognition of that relationship as a civil partnership.
