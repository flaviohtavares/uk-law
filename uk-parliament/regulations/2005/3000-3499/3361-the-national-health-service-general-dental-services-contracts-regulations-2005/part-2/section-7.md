## 7. Appeal

*Extent: E+W*

A person who has been served with a notice under regulation 6(1) may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-dc2d6f623f99e77e3ae7cdb23705dddb] against the decision of NHS England[^key-b28b1bbfa1e4e2e4b3d10e1a627c8660] that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 are not met [^key-0de8731e79f38a939af93935a48146c8]....
