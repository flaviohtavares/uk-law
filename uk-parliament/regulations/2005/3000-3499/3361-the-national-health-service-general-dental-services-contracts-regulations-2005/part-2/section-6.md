# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts) Regulations 2005, section 6

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Reasons

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where NHS England[^key-b28b1bbfa1e4e2e4b3d10e1a627c8660] is of the view that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 for entering into a contract are not met it shall notify in writing the person or persons intending to enter into the contract of its view and its reasons for that view and of his, its, or their right of appeal under regulation 7.

(2)[^key-bf111eab9f9a3764f6958e3280e71253] NHS England shall notify in writing its view and its reasons for that view to—

- (a) a director, chief executive or secretary of a dental corporation; or
- (b) the members of a limited liability partnership;

who is, or are, notified under paragraph (1) where its reasons for the decision relates to that person or those persons.
