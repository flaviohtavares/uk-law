# The Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3445 (L. 31)

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

20th December 2005

Laid before Parliament

20th December 2005

Coming into force

10th January 2006

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 92 and 108(6) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001] and sections 414 and 415 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00002], makes the following Order.

The Lord Chancellor has obtained the consent of the Treasury in accordance with section 92(1) of the Courts Act 2003, and the sanction of the Treasury in accordance with sections 414(1) and 415(1) of the Insolvency Act 1986, to the making of this Order.

The Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice and the Deputy Head of Civil Justice in accordance with section 92(5) of the Courts Act 2003, and the Civil Justice Council in accordance with section 92(6) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Civil Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 10th January 2006.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2004[^f00003].

## 2. Amendments to the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2004

In article 4(2)(b)(i) of the Order, for the words from “married couple” to “includes the party” substitute “couple (as defined in section 3(5A) of the Tax Credits Act 2002[^f00004]) which includes the party”.

**3.** For Schedule 1 to the Order substitute the Schedule to this Order.
