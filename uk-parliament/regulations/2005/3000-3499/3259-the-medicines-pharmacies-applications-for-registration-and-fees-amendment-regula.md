# The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3259

MEDICINES

The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 2005

Made

24th November 2005

Laid before Parliament

1st December 2005

Coming into force

1st January 2006

The Secretary of State for Health and the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety, acting jointly, make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 75(1), 76(1), (2) and (6) and 129(5) of the Medicines Act 1968[^f00001] or, as the case may be, the powers conferred by those provisions and now vested in them[^f00002].

In accordance with section 129(6) of that Act, they have consulted such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be affected by the Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st January 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973

In regulation 3 of the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973[^f00003] (fees)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) (fee for registration of premises), for “£460” substitute “£474” and for “£90” (fee where premises are in Northern Ireland) substitute “£93”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) (retention fee), for “£137” substitute “£150” and for “£118” (fee where premises are in Northern Ireland) substitute “£130”;
- (c) in paragraph (3) (additional sum by way of penalty), for “£460” substitute “£474” and for “£262” (sum where premises are in Northern Ireland) substitute “£270”.

## 3. Revocation

The Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applicants for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 2004[^f00004] are revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Regulations 1973 (“the principal Regulations”) as follows—

- (a) regulation 2(a) increases the fees for registration of premises at which a retail pharmacy business is, or is to be, carried on from £460 to £474 (where the premises are in Great Britain) and from £90 to £93 (where the premises are in Northern Ireland);
- (b) regulation 2(b) increases subsequent annual fees (retention fees) from £137 to £150 (where the premises are in Great Britain) and from £118 to £130 (where the premises are in Northern Ireland);
- (c) regulation 2(c) increases the penalty for failure to pay retention fees (payable in circumstances specified in section 76(2) of the Medicines Act 1968) from £460 to £474 (where the premises are in Great Britain) and from £262 to £270 (where the premises are in Northern Ireland).

These Regulations also revoke the Medicines (Pharmacies) (Applications for Registration and Fees) Amendment Regulations 2004, which increased the fees in the principal Regulations, the effect of which is spent on the coming into force of these Regulations.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Department of Health, Pharmacy and Prescription Branch, Room 520, Eileen House, 80-90 Newington Causeway, London SE1 6EE. Copies have been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
