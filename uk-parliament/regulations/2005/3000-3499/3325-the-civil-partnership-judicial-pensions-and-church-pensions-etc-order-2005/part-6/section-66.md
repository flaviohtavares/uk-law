# The Civil Partnership (Judicial Pensions and Church Pensions, etc.) Order 2005, section 66

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**66.**[^key-1154e893ab5061ff44915315fcfe6255] In Schedule 1A (transfer of accrued benefits) (to be inserted by the amendments set out in Schedule 13 to the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00020]), in paragraph 6(7), for “his widow, or her widower” substitute “his widow, her widower or his or her surviving civil partner”.
