# The Civil Partnership (Judicial Pensions and Church Pensions, etc.) Order 2005, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**46.**[^c-6dc1f096fb01e2b6fad67a334e973760] In Schedule 3 (transfer of accrued benefits) (to be inserted by the amendments set out in paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 to the Judicial Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00015]), in paragraph 6(7), for “his widow, or her widower” substitute “his widow, her widower or his or her surviving civil partner”.
