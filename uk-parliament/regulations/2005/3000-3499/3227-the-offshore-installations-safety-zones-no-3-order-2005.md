# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 2005

> Text as at 2013-01-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2005 No. 3227

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 2005

Made

23rd November 2005

Coming into force

14th December 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 22(1) and (2) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001] (“the Act”), and for the purpose of giving effect to proposals submitted to him by the Health and Safety Commission under section 24(2A) of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-6facf4005baab597096c96e4196ddf77]

This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 14th December 2005.

## 2. Establishment of safety zones[^key-ffbf19430c1db930e359cb29307d7668]

A safety zone is established around each installation specified in column 1 of the Schedule (being an installation stationed in waters to which subsection (7) of section 21 of the Petroleum Act 1987 applies) having a radius of five hundred metres from the point as respects that installation which has the co-ordinates of latitude and longitude according to European Datum (1950) specified in columns 2 and 3 of the Schedule.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Philip A Hunt

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes, under section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987, safety zones having a radius of 500 metres from the point around each installation specified in the Schedule and stationed in waters to which section 21(7) of the Act applies (these include territorial waters and waters in areas designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Action [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29)) (article 2).

Vessels, which for this purpose include hovercraft, submersible apparatus and installations in transit, are prohibited from entering or remaining in a safety zone except with the consent of the Health and Safety Executive or in accordance with regulations made under section 23(1) of the Act (currently the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Regulations (S.I. [1987/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1331))).
