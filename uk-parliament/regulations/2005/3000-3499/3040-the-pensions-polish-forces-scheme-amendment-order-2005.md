# The Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3040

PENSIONS

The Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

26th October 2005

Coming into force

5th December 2005

The Secretary of State for Defence, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Polish Resettlement Act 1947[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and commencement

This Order, which may be cited as the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2005, amends the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme 1964[^f00003] (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) and shall come into force on 5th December 2005.

## 2. Amendment of article 11 of the Scheme

In article 11 (Awards not payable to or in respect of persons in Poland) after the word “widow,” insert “surviving civil partner,”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Defence

Don Touhig

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State Ministry of Defence

25th October 2005

We consent

Tom Watson

Gillian Merron

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th October 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme 1964 in consequence of the introduction of civil partnerships by the Civil Partnership Act 2004 ([2004 c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
