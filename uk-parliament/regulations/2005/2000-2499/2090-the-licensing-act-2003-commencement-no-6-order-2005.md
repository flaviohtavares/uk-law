# The Licensing Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2090 (C.91)

LICENCES AND LICENSING

The Licensing Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2005

Made

27th July 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 201(2) of the Licensing Act 2003 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 2003 (Commencement No.6) Order 2005.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Licensing Act 2003.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule to this Order, in so far as they are not already into force, shall come into force on 7th August 2005.

James Purnell

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

27th July 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the sixth Commencement Order made by the Secretary of State under the Licensing Act 2003 (“the Act”). This Order brings into force provisions in respect of the variation of premises licences (sections 34 to 40), the transfer of premises licences (sections 42 to 46, except section 43(4)), the variation of club premises certificates (sections 84 to 86) and related appeals (Schedule 5, paragraph 1 (b) and (c), 4, 5, 6, 9, 10(b) and 12). These provisions, so far as they are not already in force, come into force on 7th August 2005.
