# The National Health Service Appointments Commission (Establishment and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2028

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The National Health Service Appointments Commission (Establishment and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

13th July 2005

Laid before Parliament

27th July 2005

Coming into force

1st September 2005

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 11(1) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Appointments Commission (Establishment and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2005.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England.

(3) In this Order “the principal Order” means the National Health Service Appointments Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of article 3 of the principal Order

In article 3 of the principal Order (functions of the commission), after sub-paragraph (v), insert the following—

> - (vi) trustees for NHS foundation trusts all or most of whose hospitals, establishments and facilities are situated in England;

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Warner

Minister of State,

Department of Health

13th July 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Health Service Appointments Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001 to give the Commission the additional function of appointing trustees to NHS foundation trusts.
