# The Finance Act 2004, Section 18 (Appointed Day) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2356 (C.98)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Finance Act 2004, Section 18 (Appointed Day) Order 2005

Made

20th August 2005

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the power conferred by section 18(4) of the Finance Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2004, Section 18 (Appointed Day) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed Day

The 14th October 2005 is appointed as the day on which section 18 of the Finance Act 2004 is to have effect in relation to vehicle licences or trade licences issued on or after that day.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

S J Ladyman

Minister of State

Department for Transport

20th August 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that section 18 of the Finance Act [2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12) shall have effect in relation to the issue of vehicle licences or trade licences on or after 14th October 2005 (*article 2*).

Section 18 of the Finance Act 2004 amends the Vehicle and Excise Registration Act [1994 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/22) to provide that a prescribed fee is payable when a credit card payment is accepted in respect of the vehicle excise duty payable upon the issue of a licence.
