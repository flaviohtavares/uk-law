## 45. Saving for Trinity House and protection of navigation[^c-11ded6c669429bb947c27a67bfe274a5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Nothing in this Order shall prejudice or derogate from any of the powers, rights or privileges, or the jurisdiction or authority of Trinity House.

(2) Schedule 9 shall have effect.
