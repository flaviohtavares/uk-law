# The River Tyne (Tunnels) Order 2005, SCHEDULE 12

> Text as at 2023-02-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 12 — APPLICATION OF LOCAL LEGISLATION

*Extent: E+W*

Article 49

**1.**[^c-b1b4398613e11d5d0443383a0b1977cc] Section 53(4), (6), (7), (8), (9), (10), (12) and (13) of the Tyne Tunnel Act 1946[^f00050] shall not apply to works carried out under the powers conferred by this Order.

**2.**[^c-4bf31fd224a383680341db6b9a3a4fbe] Sections 42 to 48 of and Schedule 2 to the Tyne and Wear Act 1976[^f00051] shall not apply to works carried out under the powers conferred by this Order.

**3.**[^c-fe022e67d5e1d17f04c23722775763ec] Articles 5 to 7 of the Port of Tyne Harbour Revision Order 2001[^f00052] shall not apply in respect of activities carried out under the powers conferred by this Order.
