# The River Tyne (Tunnels) Order 2005

> Text as at 2023-02-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2005 No. 2222

TRANSPORT AND WORKS, ENGLAND

TRANSPORT, ENGLAND

The River Tyne (Tunnels) Order 2005

Made

5th August 2005

Coming into force

26th August 2005

Whereas an application has been made to the Secretary of State in accordance with the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2000[^f00001] made under sections 6, 6A, 7 and 10 of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00002] (“the Act”) and section 4 of the Tyne Tunnels Act 1998[^f00003] for an Order under sections 3 and 5 of the Act;

And whereas the Secretary of State caused an inquiry to be held for the purposes of the application pursuant to section 11 of the Act;

And whereas the Secretary of State, having considered the objections made and not withdrawn, and the report of the person who held the inquiry, has determined to make an Order giving effect to the proposals comprised in the application with modifications which in his opinion do not make any substantial change in the proposals;

And whereas the Secretary of State is of the opinion that the primary object of this Order cannot be achieved by means of an order under the Harbours Act 1964[^f00004];

And whereas the Secretary of State is satisfied that the provision of an alternative right of way for each of the streets mentioned in Part 2 of Schedule 3 to this Order is not required;

And whereas notice of the Secretary of State’s determination was published in *The London Gazette* on 22nd July 2005;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3 and 5 of, and paragraphs 1 to 5, 7 to 13 and 15 to 17 of Schedule 1 to, the Act, section 4 of the Tyne Tunnels Act 1998 and the Transport and Works (Description of Works Interfering with Navigation) Order 1992[^f00005], hereby makes the following Order:

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order authorises the Tyne and Wear Passenger Transport Authority to construct, operate and maintain a tunnel for vehicles beneath the River Tyne and, for that purpose, compulsorily or by agreement to acquire land and rights in land and to use land. It provides for the new tunnel and the existing vehicular and pedestrian and cycle tunnels under the River Tyne to be operated as a single undertaking and makes arrangements for the levying of tolls on those who use the new and existing tunnels in vehicles.

A copy of the works plans, land plans and book of reference mentioned in this Order and certified in accordance with article 51 of this Order may be inspected free of charge during working hours at the offices of Tyne and Wear Passenger Transport Authority at Civic Centre, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE99 2BN.
