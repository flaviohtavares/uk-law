# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 168

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 168. Right to copy documents[^key-ac43425ed960578025100725f42d52af]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the 1986 Act or the Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00025] and
- (b) otherwise, of the appropriate fee.
