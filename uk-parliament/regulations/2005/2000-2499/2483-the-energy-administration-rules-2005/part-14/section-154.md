# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 154

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 154. Costs, expenses, etc[^key-0a0a78bf139197f2a8f70d6f1eb8b5a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the energy administration proceedings are to be regarded as expenses of the energy administration.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part shall be paid out of the prescribed part.
