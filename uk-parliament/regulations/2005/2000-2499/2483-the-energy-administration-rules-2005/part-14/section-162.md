# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 162

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 162. Time-limits[^key-4c8703dcdbc4d47c94ff4dddb9ee8315]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of CPR[^f00024] Rule 2.8 (time) apply, as regards computation of time, to anything required or authorised to be done by the Rules.

(2) The provisions of CPR rule 3.1(2)(a) (the court’s general powers of management) apply so as to enable the court to extend or shorten the time for compliance with anything required or authorised to be done by the Rules.
