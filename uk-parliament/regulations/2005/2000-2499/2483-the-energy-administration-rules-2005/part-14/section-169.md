# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 169

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 169. Charge for copy documents[^c-0b9c8754db69154ac153861e22143ba1]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the energy administrator is requested by a creditor or member to supply copies of any documents he is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee in respect of the supply of the documents.
