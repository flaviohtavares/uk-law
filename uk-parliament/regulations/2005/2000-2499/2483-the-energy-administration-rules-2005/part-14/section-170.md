# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 170

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 170. Non-receipt of notice of meeting[^key-4c87410c1607eaf8b4f1d916ee3dfef2]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in accordance with the 1986 Act, Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act or the Rules a meeting of creditors or other persons is called or summoned by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly summoned and held, notwithstanding that not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
