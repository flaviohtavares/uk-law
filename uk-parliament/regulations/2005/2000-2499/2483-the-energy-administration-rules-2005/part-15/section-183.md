# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 183

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 183. “The appropriate fee”[^c-5c5145788356dfbbd05a9f22a96be25e]

*Extent: E+W*

“The appropriate fee” means 15 pence per A4 or A5 page and 30 pence per A3 page.
