# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 182

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 182. “Energy administration proceedings”[^c-2012704202a99dbb6129ffa597eb053f]

*Extent: E+W*

“Energy administration proceedings” means any proceedings under sections 154 to 171 of, and Schedules 20 and 21 to, the 2004 Act or the Rules.
