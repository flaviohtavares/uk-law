# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 180

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 180. Notice to joint energy administrators[^c-b8ec6af0e346a6ddeb25a04c5720d136]

*Extent: E+W*

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the energy administrator in energy administration proceedings, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
