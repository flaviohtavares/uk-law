# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 82

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Notice of intention to resign[^key-15dfead65d255de691bfa2e08e3b26bd]

*Extent: E+W*

The energy administrator shall in all cases give at least 7 days notice in Form EA23 of his intention to resign, or to apply for the court’s permission to do so, to the following persons—

- (a) the Secretary of State;
- (b) GEMA;
- (c) if there is a continuing energy administrator of the protected energy company, to him; and
- (d) if there is no such energy administrator, to the protected energy company and its creditors.
