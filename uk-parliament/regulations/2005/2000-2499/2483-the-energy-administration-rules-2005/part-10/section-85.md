# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 85

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Notice of vacation of office when energy administrator ceases to be qualified to act[^c-f101c70fcbed95963376e0bf27a7e829]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the energy administrator who has ceased to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the protected energy company gives notice in accordance with paragraph 89 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act, he shall also give notice to the registrar of companies in Form EA25.
