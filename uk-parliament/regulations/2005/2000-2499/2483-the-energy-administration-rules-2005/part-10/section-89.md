# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 89

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint energy administrator[^key-1eee751da4d6fdc2d69741f863777c90]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, after an initial appointment has been made, an additional person or persons are to be appointed as joint energy administrator the same Rules shall apply in respect of giving notice of and advertising the appointment as in the case of the initial appointment, subject to Rule 90.
