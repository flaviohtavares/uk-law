# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 80

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Provision of information to the Secretary of State[^key-cf15ed7726cf3fc140ea1a75dd1adf80]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the energy administration ends pursuant to paragraph 79, 83 or 84 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act, the energy administrator shall, within 5 business days from the date of the end of the energy administration, provide the Secretary of State with the following information—

- (a) a breakdown of the relevant debts (within the meaning of section 169(4) of the 2004 Act) of the protected energy company, which remain outstanding; and
- (b) details of any shortfall (within the meaning of section 169(3)(a) of the 2004 Act) in the property of the protected energy company available for meeting those relevant debts.
