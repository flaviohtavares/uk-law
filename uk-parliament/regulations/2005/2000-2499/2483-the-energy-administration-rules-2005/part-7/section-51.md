# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 51

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Secured creditors[^key-d93265da7e2420b44c1287247fa52812]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a secured creditor realises his security, he may prove for the balance of his debt, after deducting the amount realised.

(2) If a secured creditor voluntarily surrenders his security for the general benefit of creditors, he may prove for his whole debt, as if it were unsecured.
