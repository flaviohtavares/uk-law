# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 52

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Discounts[^key-317852b4d6abe402f80846891f937e31]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the protected energy company but for its energy administration except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
