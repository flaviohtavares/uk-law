# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 62

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Realisation of security by creditor[^key-67b3849ca9219435386b39aad9e30da7]

*Extent: E+W*

If a creditor who has valued his security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the energy administrator)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security; and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by him.
