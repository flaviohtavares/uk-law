# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 47

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Withdrawal or variation of proof[^key-eb403684273b4b5a2f1ee1e21ae1486f]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement between himself and the energy administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
