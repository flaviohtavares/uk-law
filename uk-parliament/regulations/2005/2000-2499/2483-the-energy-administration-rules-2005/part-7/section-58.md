# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 58

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Value of security[^key-9374b43be38fc095e8372574f458998a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A secured creditor may, with the agreement of the energy administrator or the leave of the court, at any time alter the value which he has, in his proof of debt, put upon his security.

(2) However, if a secured creditor has voted in respect of the unsecured balance of his debt he may re-value his security only with permission of the court.
