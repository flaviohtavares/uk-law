# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 119

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Award of costs against energy administrator[^key-b6c0312e46b93bfb2a0ead64f04b33c8]

*Extent: E+W*

Without prejudice to any provision of the 1986 Act, Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act or the Rules by virtue of which the energy administrator is not in any event to be liable for costs and expenses, where an energy administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, he shall not be personally liable for costs unless the court otherwise directs.
