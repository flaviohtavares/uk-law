# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 106

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 106. Enforcement of court orders[^c-8df44bc1e9903785ec09fc7c86f1ed84]

*Extent: E+W*

In any energy administration proceedings, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
