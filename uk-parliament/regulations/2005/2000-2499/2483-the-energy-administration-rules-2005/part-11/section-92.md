# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 92

> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Preliminary[^key-3b5b7fada601ec23157d01c52f089096]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in energy administration proceedings, except an application for an energy administration order.
