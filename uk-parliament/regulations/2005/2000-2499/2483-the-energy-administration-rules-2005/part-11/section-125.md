# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 125

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 125. Affidavit in support of application[^c-2546bbcbedc1f6bc9197c41639deda01]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under Rule 124(3) shall be supported by an affidavit of a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
