# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 112

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. File of court proceedings[^key-8cf8895d888ff9820ba02c8f0e5afe85]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In respect of all energy administration proceedings, the court shall open and maintain a file for each case; and (subject to directions of the registrar) all documents relating to such proceedings shall be placed on the relevant file.

(2) No energy administration proceedings shall be filed in the Central office of the High Court.
