> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 130. Right of audience[^c-a60429a360fcdcfb4be0a689bdc5ed7c]

*Extent: E+W*

Rights of audience in energy administration proceedings are the same as obtain in insolvency proceedings.
