# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 102

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 102. Use of reports[^c-2b418ba7faa811dd9638c9fbacaba3b7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A report may be filed in court instead of an affidavit, unless the application involves other parties or the court otherwise orders, by the energy administrator.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of an affidavit, the report shall be treated for the purpose of Rule 101(1) and any hearing before the court as if it were an affidavit.
