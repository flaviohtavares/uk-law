# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 97

> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 97. Notice of application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act[^key-4d735a21c25a5d859bf86a86b436345e]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act may be made without the application being served upon or notice being given to any other party.
