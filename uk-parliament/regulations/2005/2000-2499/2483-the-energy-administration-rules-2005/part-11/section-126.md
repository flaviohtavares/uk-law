> Text as at 2023-05-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Services of notices following appointment[^c-bdaa0c0550b488ce74545e1640897a4a]

*Extent: E+W*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 124 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
