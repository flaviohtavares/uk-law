# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 137

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 137. Payment into court[^c-2af4e60cf70b5043a346d9f7473b60eb]

*Extent: E+W*

The CPR relating to payment into and out of court of money lodged in court as security for costs apply to money lodged in court under the Rules.
