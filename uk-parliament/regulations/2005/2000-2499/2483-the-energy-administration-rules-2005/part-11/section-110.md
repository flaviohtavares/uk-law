# The Energy Administration Rules 2005, section 110

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. Court records[^c-952f26dc8e8417105a2dc6645e25d86a]

*Extent: E+W*

The court shall keep records of all energy administration proceedings, and shall cause to be entered in the records the taking of any step in the proceedings, and such decisions of the court in relation thereto, as the court thinks fit.
