# The Income Tax (Construction Industry Scheme) Regulations 2005, section 3

## 3. Multiple contractors[^c-45c62fc56b5c377895c47c5d5a0fdc1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A contractor may elect, for the purposes of these Regulations, to be treated as different contractors in relation to different groups of sub-contractors.

(2) Where the contractor makes an election these Regulations apply as if—

- (a) in respect of each group the contractor were a different contractor; [^c-1dcf686223fe34d24eaa53f8e52a7d68]...
- (b) each group constituted all the sub-contractors to whom the contractor makes payments under contracts relating to construction operations and;[^c-c22c42e01fbdfded67b2b16ef65eaa0c]
- (c) a sub-contractor who is allocated to more than one group has more than one contractor from whom it receives payments under contracts relating to construction operations.

(3) While an election is in force, a contractor must allocate any new sub-contractors to at least[^c-84a1f4ee7b1897dac5fa55c86e869126] one of the groups.

(4) An election must be made by notice to an officer of Revenue and Customs containing—

- (za)[^c-be6b7c9b05907b83e22d108746084d8c] the tax month from which the election is to have effect,
- (a) such information as may be necessary to identify the groups of sub-contractors, and
- (b) a certificate that the contractor makes no payments under contracts relating to construction operations other than to sub-contractors in the groups so identified.

(5) A contractor must [^c-8ea0064b1f2c16b30eb4e1d48c27d80b]... make an election before the beginning of the tax month immediately preceding the tax month from which the election[^c-43846259ee15e9669d7f73a578a50574] is to have effect.

(5A)[^c-177a95ac8e42b47ac92c4035177e0023] A late election will be treated as if it had been made in relation to the tax month immediately following the tax month from which the election was stated to have had effect.

(6)[^c-2171fa98c2158e2eedef98c05267aba2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-e590b649fe38ed75de77624885f175a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) An election continues in effect until revoked by notice given to an officer of Revenue and Customs.

(9) A notice of revocation must be given before the beginning of the tax month[^c-8144cad497dd5bb09868594c32735fe8] for which the election is to be revoked, but the revocation of an election does not prevent the making of a new election for that or a later tax month.

(10) An election which has not yet come into effect may be revoked at any time before the beginning of the tax month[^c-f6057361696ade8111ca3c7a7dc92f86] for which it is to have effect.
