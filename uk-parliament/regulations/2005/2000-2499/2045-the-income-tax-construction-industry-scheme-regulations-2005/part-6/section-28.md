# The Income Tax (Construction Industry Scheme) Regulations 2005, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2021-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/2045

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Minimum turnover[^c-71221679f6568f5d8bd84a6c88a01352]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The minimum turnover for the purposes of paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 11 to the Act is £30,000.

(2) The amount specified for the purposes of paragraph 7 of Schedule 11 to the Act is £100,000[^c-c7834fac80ea509983c38ab9f1468597].

(3) The amount specified for the purposes of paragraph 11 of Schedule 11 to the Act is £100,000[^c-56d0adfabfc255c229a4c16d87e26864].
