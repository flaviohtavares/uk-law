# The Income Tax (Construction Industry Scheme) Regulations 2005, section 24B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2021-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/2045
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24B. Delivery of notices, notifications, information and applications[^c-b4034d3f7223725c8f9250a91521f275]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Unless regulation 67D of the PAYE Regulations (exceptions to regulation 67B) applies to a claimant—

- (a) any notice, notification or information to be given by a claimant or by Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs under this Part, or
- (b) any application to be made by a claimant under this Part,

must be delivered using a method of electronic communications approved by the specific Directions made by the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs on 13th March 2012 under regulations 189 and 205(2) of the PAYE Regulations, and published on 16th March 2012[^f00025].

(2) Where regulation 67D of the PAYE Regulations applies to a claimant—

- (a) any notice, notification or information to be given by a claimant or by Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs under this Part, or
- (b) any application to be made by a claimant under this Part,

must be made in a document or format provided or approved by the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.
