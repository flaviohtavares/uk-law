## 17J. Appeals: further provision for cases which fall within regulation 17E(2)[^c-457911a30ab1c6563cf4bceb8c32386d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In a case which falls within regulation 17E(2), if the request mentioned in that provision is made before an appeal under regulation 17I(1), regulation 17I(3)(a)(i) applies as if the words “the day after the day on which the notice was given” were “the day after the day on which HMRC notifies C of its decision”.
