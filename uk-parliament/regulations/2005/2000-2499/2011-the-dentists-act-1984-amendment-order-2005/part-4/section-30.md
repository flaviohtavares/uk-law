# The Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 30

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Insertion of section 36J[^key-eca7590ba8d25690854b0bd6c4b46ace]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) After section 36I insert—

> **Registration appeals**
>
> **36J. Registration appeals**
>
> Schedule 4A to this Act (registration appeals: dental care professionals register) shall have effect.

.

(2) Schedule 3 to this Order, which inserts a new Schedule 4A into the Act, shall have effect.
