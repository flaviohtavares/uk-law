# The Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 41

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Insertion of section 49A[^c-3d074c02c4c25d346bc5b4bc8cdbf0a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) After section 49 insert—

> **49A. Advisers**
>
> Schedule 4C to this Act (advisers) shall have effect.

.

(2) Schedule 5 to this Order, which inserts a new Schedule 4C into the Act, shall have effect.
