# The Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Insertion of section 25A[^c-340588ebe287713eee45d26bf09d6622]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) After section 25 insert—

> **Registration appeals**
>
> **25A. Registration appeals**
>
> Schedule 2A to this Act (registration appeals: dentists register) shall have effect.

.

(2) Schedule 1 to this Order, which inserts a new Schedule 2A into the Act, shall have effect.
