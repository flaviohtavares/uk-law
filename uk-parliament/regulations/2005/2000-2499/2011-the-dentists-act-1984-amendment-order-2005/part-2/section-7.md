# The Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Amendment of section 18[^key-8d999895a4eeb3306af96637ae74eaed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 18 (procedure for registration)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “registration” insert “in the register”;
- (b) in subsection (2), after “applying to be registered” insert “in the register”; and
- (c) in subsection (3), after “duly register” insert “in the register”.
