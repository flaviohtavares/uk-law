# The Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Repeal of sections 20 and 21[^key-03e027e625a777193c418314779e3a47]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 20 (selection of proper abbreviations of overseas diplomas) and section 21 (notification of reasons for refusal of application for registration etc.) are hereby repealed.
