## 5. Joint complainants[^key-0d80a491fd50b13a59a0f0dfb589e192]

*Extent: E*

Where an allegation of misconduct[^key-a57a4569c9969718c70b7709dd9eb403] is signed by two or more persons jointly, they shall nominate one of them to be the correspondent for the purposes of these rules.
