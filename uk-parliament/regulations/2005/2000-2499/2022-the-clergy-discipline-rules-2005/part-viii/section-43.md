## 43. Admissions by the respondent[^c-65da3c7cfafd02c0fa0f656519831e25]

*Extent: E*

If, after referral of the allegation of misconduct[^c-2cc6498a585002c757acbaf558fdacb4] under rule 29*,* the respondent makes an admission before or at the hearing, the tribunal may make a finding of misconduct on the basis of that admission without considering any or any further evidence, and the tribunal may then proceed under section 19 of the Measure.
