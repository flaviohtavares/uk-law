## 39. The tribunal and the overriding objective[^key-6fd26b655f7cb4abe648f33093064f6b]

*Extent: E*

The tribunal shall in accordance with the overriding objective in rule 1—

- (a) conduct the hearing in the manner it considers most appropriate to the issues before it and to the just handling of the allegation of misconduct[^key-9cc6dff1e27c2a3fa9572e3ec36a80b4] generally,
- (b) set a suitable timetable for the hearing.
