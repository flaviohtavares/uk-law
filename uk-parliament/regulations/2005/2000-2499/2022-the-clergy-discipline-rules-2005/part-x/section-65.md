# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 65

## 65. Notification of cessation of suspension[^key-148b24af065aeb4c391cbc670e410101]

*Extent: E*

Where—

- (a) a suspension expires and no further notice of suspension is given by the bishop to the priest or deacon, or
- (b) the proceedings under the Measure [^key-422b6a58f84ea5e13e9575e13e5e90f7]... are concluded without the imposition of any penalty of prohibition or removal from office or revocation of licence, or[^key-d8405b59f1f302621c87468fc8096c5b]
- (c)[^key-064de37f70c1b41597f0d18644e84bdc] following conviction for a criminal offence the bishop does not impose a penalty under section 30(1) of the Measure,

the bishop shall notify in writing the priest or deacon concerned and the persons to whom[^key-b18afb1a41c63c9fb6e0c3eee3d12c31] notification of suspension was given[^key-039721e69edc0ca58a518cab95aafc0b] under rule 63 that the suspension has ended.
