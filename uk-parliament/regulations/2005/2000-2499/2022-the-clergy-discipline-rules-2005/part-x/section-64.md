# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (181).** Text as at 2024-03-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/2022
>
> Extent: England.

## 64. Revocation of suspension[^key-dd249243a20d9ccb7efcd3adbbcb2906]

*Extent: E*

(1) The bishop may at any time during any period of suspension under rule 60, rule 61, rule 61A[^key-3819402eba376063bcbd96af4f95f5aa] , rule 61B, rule 61C or rule 61D[^key-028a4bf3921fdba674ea29b81eea4731] revoke a notice of suspension, and on so doing the bishop shall serve notification[^key-1d16473a3dfddd64db0a29dd627b4f28] in writing on the priest or deacon concerned stating that the notice of suspension has been revoked and that the period of suspension has been terminated.

(2)[^key-bf8ea6c446887747c65d71bce41193e5] Written notification of the revocation of a suspension shall be given by the bishop to the persons to whom notification of suspension was sent or delivered under rule 63.
