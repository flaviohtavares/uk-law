## 90. Application by bishop or archbishop for review of penalty[^c-23e428b10fd78983892a063a79ff836a]

*Extent: E*

An application under rule 70 for a review of a penalty imposed under section 31(2) of the Measure shall be made by a bishop to the other archbishop, and by an archbishop to the President, and rules 70 to 73 shall be construed accordingly.
