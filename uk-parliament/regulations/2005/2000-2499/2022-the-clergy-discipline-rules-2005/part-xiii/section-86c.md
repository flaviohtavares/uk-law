## 86C. Notice of suspension of a bishop or archbishop under section 37(1)(e) of the Measure[^key-13f1f5633510b82ddb0ce68d57ab0971]

*Extent: E*

For the purposes of rule 61C a notice of suspension on a bishop or archbishop shall be—

- (a) in form 16b in the Schedule [^key-68ef69a67d3bb140ac889a1ba3974145]...,
- (b) signed by the archbishop of the province in which the bishop to be suspended holds office, or, in the case of an archbishop to be suspended, signed by the other archbishop, and
- (c) countersigned by the two most senior diocesan bishops in that province or the province of the other archbishop, as the case may be,

and rule 62(2) shall be construed accordingly.
