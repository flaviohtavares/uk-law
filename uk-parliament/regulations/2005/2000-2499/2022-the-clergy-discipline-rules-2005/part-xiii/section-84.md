## 84. Death or serious illness or incapacity of the complainant[^c-1529a5761141ae4c2e1821985f187491]

*Extent: E*

Rules 55 and 56 shall be read as if the words “the parochial church council” wherever they appear are replaced by “the bishop’s council” or “the archbishop’s council”, as the case may be.
