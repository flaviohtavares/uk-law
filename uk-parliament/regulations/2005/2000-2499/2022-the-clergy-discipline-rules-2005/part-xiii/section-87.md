## 87. Notification to others of suspension of bishop[^key-ea907136e9911d9532815f5894e0850d]

*Extent: E*

(1)[^key-3ae88d99e9bdc7e7d011bed2f8542fa1] For the purposes of rule 63(1) written notification[^key-858604b04e39c05e125f0bf506f57259] of suspension of a bishop shall be given by the archbishop[^key-186bfa2d6b622938dc8b5ccd0ea5caf0] to—

- (a) the other archbishop,
- (b) other bishops of the diocese of the suspended bishop, including assistant bishops,
- (c) the secretary of the bishop’s council of the diocese of the suspended bishop,
- (d) the diocesan registrar,
- (e) the provincial registrar

[^key-b83182a8c0295071e67bc49d2821b616]... .

(2)[^key-3f9ce3e987885fb3f2504310d9df8a74] For the purposes of rule 63(2) the archbishop may give written notification of suspension of a bishop to any of the following—

- (a) any relevant chief constable of police,
- (b) any relevant local authority designated officer,
- (c) the relevant diocesan safeguarding officer,
- (d) the national safeguarding adviser of the Archbishops’ Council,
- (e) any diocesan bishop of the diocese,
- (f) any other person whom the archbishop considers should be notified of the suspension.

(3) For the purposes of rule 63(3) the archbishop shall give written notification of suspension of a bishop to each person referred to in paragraph[^key-90b266f712d96ec42b936c45f6a4f59f] (1) and paragraph (2)(a) to (e) above.

(4) For the purposes of rule 63(4) the archbishop may give written notification of suspension of a bishop to any other person whom the archbishop considers should be notified of the suspension.

(5) Rules 64(2), 65 & 66(4) shall be construed in accordance with paragraphs[^key-4067f8b7e2d623ce55546e267528daf1] (1) to (4) above.
