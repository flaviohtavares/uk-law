# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 82

## 82. Institution of proceedings against bishops or archbishops[^c-5c7318a73f2dc1162ffec95613aacd5a]

*Extent: E*

(1) No disciplinary proceedings under section 10 of the Measure against a bishop or archbishop may be instituted except by way of a written allegation of misconduct[^c-04828d8bbc24a831dbf1644a16239ccd] made in form 1b in the Schedule [^c-566f879070bbbae6d430a48cacbb6fb3]....

(2) Where the complainant has been nominated by the bishop’s council or the archbishop’s council of the relevant diocese the allegation of misconduct in writing shall have attached to it a certified copy of the council’s resolution passed in accordance with section 10(1)(b)(i) or (c)(i) as the case may be.

(3) An allegation of misconduct[^c-7b0978e9e562974987d241c03133bfd7] shall be referred for preliminary scrutiny in accordance with rule 9 to the provincial registrar in the case of a bishop, and in the case of an archbishop to the provincial registrar of the other province.
