# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 68

## 68. Bishop proposes a penalty after sentence of imprisonment or matrimonial order , etc.[^key-498a859ce3f7414be4647f2cd9d54ee3][^key-47747fe358b549c9e57b8b734666b6dd]

*Extent: E*

Where the bishop proposes to impose a penalty of prohibition or removal from office, or both, upon a priest or deacon falling within section 30(1)(a), (b) or (c)[^key-03e3eca087f8029e515eaf753a0c1c1f] of the Measure, the bishop, after consultation with the President, shall—

- (a) inform the priest or deacon in writing of the details of the proposed penalty, [^key-8bb6c64f8c6f0a81ece591178758310a]...
- (aa)[^key-df841538f4cd936bc5257ee78c2db645] send the priest or deacon a copy of the bishop’s letter to the President and the President’s response, and
- (b) invite the priest or deacon to send to the bishop any representations in writing about the proposed penalty[^key-0d11fcd3ac126cfab43b85033151b229] within 28 days of the date of that invitation.
