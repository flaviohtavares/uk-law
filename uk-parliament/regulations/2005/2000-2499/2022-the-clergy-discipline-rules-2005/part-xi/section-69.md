# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 69

## 69. Bishop makes decision on imposition of penalty[^c-ebd69486d62dbff6e449512280b3c740]

*Extent: E*

After expiry of the 28 days specified in rule 68 the bishop shall decide whether or not to impose the proposed penalty under section 30 of the Measure and shall—

- (a) give the priest or deacon written notification of the decision, and
- (b) if the decision is to impose the penalty, inform the priest or deacon that an application may be made to the archbishop of the relevant province to review the decision, and that the penalty will not be imposed unless and until the time for making an application for a review has expired or the bishop’s decision has been upheld by the archbishop following a review.
