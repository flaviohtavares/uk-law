## 107. Citation and commencement[^key-6f6094b1051fc83a2b460cfe3dd9fde1]

*Extent: E*

(1) These rules shall be known as the Clergy Discipline Rules 2005.

(2) These rules shall come into force on the day appointed under section 48(2) of the Measure for the coming into operation of section 8 of the Measure.
