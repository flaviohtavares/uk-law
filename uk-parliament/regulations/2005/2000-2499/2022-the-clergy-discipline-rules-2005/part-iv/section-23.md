## 23. Consent to conditional deferment[^key-faa8ced6583330afdc8356fadf88471a]

*Extent: E*

Consent by the respondent, given under section 14(1) of the Measure, to a conditional deferment of the allegation of misconduct[^key-9eaa6c6591a98789c469dc210bd5bf65] shall—

- (a) be in writing and be given in form 6 in the Schedule [^key-838b53fa158e85e8b7974d88c5d371e7]..., and
- (b) be signed and dated by the respondent.
