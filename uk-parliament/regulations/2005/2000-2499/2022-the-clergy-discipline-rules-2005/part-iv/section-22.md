## 22. No further action: President’s decision[^key-deb0c4f8b481f99afa2e7acf2efc8833]

*Extent: E*

Within 28 days of receipt of the complainant’s referral, the President’s decision shall be given in writing with reasons and sent to the complainant, the respondent and the bishop, and if the President decides that the bishop’s determination was plainly wrong the President may —[^key-9abeac1b11f0bec2f0416c806d60ebc9]

- (a) direct the bishop to pursue such of the courses specified in section 12(1)(b) to (e) as the President considers appropriate, or
- (b) remit the allegation of misconduct[^key-10ed0ea4f3aa08eb82102fdf77e29a09] to the bishop, and direct the bishop to reconsider the determination that there is to be no further action.
