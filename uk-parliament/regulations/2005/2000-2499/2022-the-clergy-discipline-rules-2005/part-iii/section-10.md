## 10. Notifying the respondent about the allegation[^key-2e3f8678a9f954f8719097b55a4fb711][^key-e827c0eb02819f862dedcfb547bf9784]

*Extent: E*

(1) Subject to paragraph[^key-90b266f712d96ec42b936c45f6a4f59f] (2), rule 4(5) and rule 6(5),[^key-88036fde56d0037c689dcca0de858950] within 7 days of receiving the allegation of misconduct[^key-45cbdff9209ddb9548c67719af0bb77c] from the bishop, the registrar shall notify the respondent in writing—

- (a) that an allegation[^key-1d61dfaa93463326c9644b47d3b5df8c] has been made,
- (b) that the registrar’s function is limited to scrutinising the allegation in order to produce a written report for the bishop setting out the registrar’s views on—
  - (i) whether, at the date when proceedings were instituted, the complainant was entitled under section 10 of the Measure to make the allegation, and
  - (ii) whether there is sufficient substance in the allegation to justify proceeding with it,
- (c) that no formal response or detailed evidence is required from the respondent at this preliminary stage as the registrar’s function is limited to the matters in paragraph (b) above,
- (d) that the respondent will be requested by the bishop to respond to the allegation in detail with evidence in support if it proceeds beyond preliminary scrutiny,
- (e) of the date when the registrar expects to submit the written report to the bishop.

(2)[^key-82cdde75d4913d8bb3def21fa0cf3514] The registrar may in exceptional circumstances for no longer than is necessary delay notifying the respondent that an allegation of misconduct[^key-03fd3b55489259621da4365343a478c4] has been made.

(3)[^key-22c0ca8d2fa14f543772213c4736a93b] When notifying the respondent under paragraph (1), the registrar shall send the respondent a copy or, where rules 4(4), 6(4) or 8(1B) apply, an edited transcript of the allegation of misconduct[^key-2ad4e4d9900a3fd5ef0218476adec0ad] or the relevant written evidence in support, as the case may be.
