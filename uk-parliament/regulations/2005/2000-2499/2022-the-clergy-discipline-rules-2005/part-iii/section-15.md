# The Clergy Discipline Rules 2005, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (181).** Text as at 2024-03-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/2022
>
> Extent: England.

## 15. Dismissal of the allegation by the bishop under section 11(3) of the Measure[^c-4cc7c28b57fea1e1a542beacf3c29c3c][^c-565aa4cba08c38102512821cf2a755fc]

*Extent: E*

(1) If the bishop, after considering the registrar’s report, dismisses an allegation of misconduct[^c-e605c6646b5ecc343bf55897335f80bd] under section 11(3) of the Measure, the bishop shall within 28 days of receiving the registrar’s written report send to the complainant and the respondent written notice of dismissal together with reasons for the dismissal and a copy of the registrar’s report.

(2) The written notice shall explain the complainant’s right to request the President to review the dismissal.
