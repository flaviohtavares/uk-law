## 14. Multiple allegations and the written report[^c-75bd0f77852b77ef8eda884a6243a2b7][^c-15d350f8373c12845ee27a13b5f10fca]

*Extent: E*

(1) Where the complainant makes more than one allegation of misconduct[^c-080cef650c396dcf979ab2aceb8260ed] against the same respondent, the registrar may prepare one written report dealing with all the allegations[^c-a4e43a918165ea80e7b3108d12e025dd] referred to the registrar by the bishop.

(2) Where the complainant makes an allegation of misconduct[^c-5d777795603a54d870bf61144784917d] against two or more respondents, the registrar shall prepare separate written reports in respect of each respondent.

(3) Where two or more complainants make separate allegations of misconduct[^c-c574ba151438e3cbfb585a85f5cb5ca6] against the same respondent in respect of the same alleged misconduct, the registrar may prepare one written report dealing with all the allegations[^c-f96418cb16d6143934ddb6ce0591d25f].
