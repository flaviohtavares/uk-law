## 99. Removal of prohibition for life and deposition: bishops and archbishops[^c-d6533b435d3dbc0dfc21a6e7c67bd173]

*Extent: E*

For the purposes of rule 97 an application for a prohibition or deposition to be nullified under section 26(3) of the Measure shall be made—

- (a) by a bishop to the archbishop of the other province,
- (b) by an archbishop to the Dean of the Arches and Auditor,

and in the case of an application by an archbishop rule 97(4) shall be read as if reference to any consultation were omitted.
