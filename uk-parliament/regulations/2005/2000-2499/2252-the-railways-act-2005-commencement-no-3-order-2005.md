# The Railways Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2252 (C. 94)

TRANSPORT

RAILWAYS

The Railways Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made

11th August 2005

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60(2) of the Railways Act 2005[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Railways Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

## 2. Commencement

Section 5 of the Railways Act 2005 shall come into force on 21st August 2005.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Derek Twigg

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

11th August 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 5 of the Railways Act 2005 on 21st August 2005.

Section 5 enables the Scottish Ministers to prepare a strategy for carrying out their functions in relation to railways and railway services, and to revise that strategy from time to time.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Railways Act 2005 have been brought into force by commencement orders made before the date of this Order.
