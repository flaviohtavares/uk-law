# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1821 (C. 77)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made

5th July 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 60 and 61 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

## 2. Commencement

Section 27 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (powers of authorised officers executing warrants) shall come into force on 18th July 2005.

Scotland of Asthal, Q.C.

Minister of State

Home Office

5th July 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 set out in article 2 on 18th July 2005.

(This note is not part of the Order)
