# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Communications from customers and potential customers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication made by or on behalf of a person (“customer”) to one other person (“supplier”)—

- (a) in order to obtain information about a controlled investment or controlled claims management activity *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-72c4ab5f73abd236def95bc3758796d8] available from or a controlled service provided by the supplier; or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5f0d07f8d0105d24967737bca9f578]
- (b) in order that the customer can acquire a controlled investment from that supplier or be supplied with a controlled service by that supplier; or *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-8e0e87ac39157b3d933914818aab989c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

- (c)[^c-ac9e2211b2dc6f66fc4d7e55d9971d70] in order that the customer can be supplied with services in respect of a controlled claims management activity by that supplier.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a controlled service is a service the provision of which constitutes engaging in a controlled activity by the supplier.
