# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 17

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Generic promotions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication which—

- (a) does not identify (directly or indirectly) a person who provides the controlled investment to which the communication relates; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24767c735419085e5015b3cf3c0afe50]
- (b) does not identify (directly or indirectly) any person as a person who carries on a controlled activity in relation to that investment; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^key-f2b25139bf0229aae25f9316ac8c0ee4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

- (c)[^key-c2b0028d2af31804a87ba8b487f75b49] does not identify (directly or indirectly) any person as a person who carries on a controlled claims management activity.
