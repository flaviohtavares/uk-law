# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 73A

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 73A. Application of exemptions in this Part[^c-f11698a7cd0ad40f9f946fbec67d6282]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The exemptions in this Part apply to any communication which relates to a controlled claims management activity of a kind specified in paragraph 11A of Schedule 1.
