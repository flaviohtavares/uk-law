# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Takeovers of relevant unlisted companies: application forms

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication made in connection with a takeover offer for a relevant unlisted company which is a form of application for—

- (a) shares in or debentures of the unlisted company; or
- (b) investments falling within paragraphs 17 or 18 of Schedule 1 so far as relating to the shares in or debentures of the company which are the subject of the offer.
