# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 72F

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72F. Credit agreements offered to employees by employers[^key-a223ba3c9d15f861fc38bccb51c7da98]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication which is made to an employee by or on behalf of a person in relation to an exempt staff loan.

(2) In this article, “an exempt staff loan” means a credit agreement within the meaning of article 60B (regulated credit agreements) of the Regulated Activities Order which is—

- (a)[^key-0f0c815828826f081e0cab787ea39d8a] offered by a lender to a borrower as an incident of employment with the lender, or with an undertaking in the same group as the lender; and
- (b) an exempt agreement for the purposes of Chapter 14A (regulated credit agreements) of the Regulated Activities Order by virtue of a provision of article 60G (exempt agreements: exemptions relating to the total charge for credit) of that Order other than paragraph (2) of that article.
