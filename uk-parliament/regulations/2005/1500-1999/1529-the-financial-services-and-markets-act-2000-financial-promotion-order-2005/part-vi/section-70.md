# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 70

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Promotions included in listing particulars etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any non-real time communication which is included in—

- (a) listing particulars;
- (b) supplementary listing particulars;
- (c)[^c-29732ed770f200e89c6ba6595e6b24fe] a prospectus or supplementary prospectus published in accordance with regulated market admission rules;
- (d) any other document required or permitted to be published—
  - (i) by listing rules under Part 6 of the Act, or
  - (ii) by regulated market admission rules,

  except an advertisement as defined in regulation 3 of the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024;
- (e)[^c-ed91d272ac47946a21aae09aaf8abd37] a qualifying cryptoasset disclosure document or a supplementary disclosure document as defined by regulation 6 (“**qualifying cryptoasset disclosure document**” and “**supplementary disclosure document**”) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026.

(1A)[^c-f393a33108243735a4c7a0be2a4309e0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In this article “**listing particulars**”, and “[^c-a06e780bb2155f3ae7953d2d400b32ad]**listing rules**” have the meaning given by Part VI of the Act.
