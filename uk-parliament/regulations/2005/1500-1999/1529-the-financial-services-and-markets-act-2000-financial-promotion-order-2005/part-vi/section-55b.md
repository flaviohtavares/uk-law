# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 55B

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55B. Insolvency practitioners[^key-dae5ddc8246d10f1162384bc6726ebde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any non-real time communication or solicited real time communication by a person acting as an insolvency practitioner (within the meaning of the Regulated Activities Order) in the course of carrying on[^key-81ce09a7169d6087048ecabc1daa3a6b] an activity which would be a regulated activity but for article 72H of the Regulated Activities Order (insolvency practitioners).
