# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 46

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Qualifying credit to bodies corporate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication which relates to a controlled activity falling within paragraph 10, 10A, 10B, 10BA or 10BB[^key-238eb988c7d679b032e2b408e7ec7e58] of Schedule 1 (or within paragraph 11 so far as it relates to that activity) if the communication is—

- (a) made to or directed at bodies corporate only; or
- (b) accompanied by an indication that the qualifying credit to which it relates is only available to bodies corporate.
