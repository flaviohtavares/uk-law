# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 73ZA

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73ZA. Certain promotions of qualifying cryptoassets[^c-59dd77fe7a491ba98603802c2c891840]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280] The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication which relates only to one or more qualifying cryptoassets and which is communicated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]

- (a) by a registered person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
- (b) on behalf of a registered person provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
  - (i) the communication is a non-real time communication; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280] the registered person prepared the content of the communication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply where a registered person makes or directs the communication, or causes it to be made or directed, in breach of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]

- (a) a requirement imposed by the FCA under section 55L (imposition of requirements by FCA) of the Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
- (b) a direction given under section 137S (financial promotion rules: directions given by FCA) of the Act, as those provisions have been applied and modified by the Schedule to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) (Amendment) Order 2023. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]

(3) In this article, a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]**registered person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]**” means a person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]

- (a) a cryptoasset exchange provider or a custodian wallet provider as defined in regulation 14A (cryptoasset exchange providers and custodian wallet providers) of the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
- (b) included on the register maintained by the FCA pursuant to regulation 54(1A) (duty to maintain registers of certain relevant persons) of those Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
- (c) not an authorised person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d5c2933bb5f20e4c780b5779363f2280]
