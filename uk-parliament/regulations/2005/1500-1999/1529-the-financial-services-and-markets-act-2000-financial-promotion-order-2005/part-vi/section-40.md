# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 40

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Participants in certain recognised collective investment schemes[^c-6014f91be074148638512fd22557f93a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any non-real time communication or solicited real time communication which is made—

- (a) by a person who is the operator of a scheme recognised under section 271A or[^c-0482d0564918597e62ff59ebb4049058] section [^c-6bbce3e0fdf699369169a9bf5b462de5]...272 of the Act; and
- (b) to persons in the United Kingdom who are participants in any such recognised scheme operated by the person making the communication,

and which relates only to such recognised schemes as are operated by that person or to units in such schemes.
