# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005, section 35

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Registered societies[^key-a6c44a963bfe9bc0edbc927c885476e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The financial promotion restriction does not apply to any communication which—

- (a) is a non-real time communication or a solicited real time communication;
- (b) is communicated by a registered society[^key-146a76a1975c610501edf09ca435c5ed]; and
- (c) relates only to an investment falling within paragraph 15 or 15A[^key-a96a588330fc118cfeb85c8401ba08d0] of Schedule 1 issued, or to be issued, by the society in question.
