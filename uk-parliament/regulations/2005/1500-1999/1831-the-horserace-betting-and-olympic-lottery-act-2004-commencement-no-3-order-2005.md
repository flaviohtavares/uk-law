# The Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1831 (C.78)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made

6th July 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 40 of the Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 8th July 2005:

- sections 25, 26, 27, 29, 30, 31, 32;
- subsections (6) and (9)(c) of section 34; and
- Schedule 5.

James Purnell

Minister for Creative Industries and Tourism

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

6th July 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order brings into force on 8th July 2005 those provisions in Part 3 of the Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004 which are not already in force. In particular, commencement of section 29 allows for the Olympic Lottery Distributor to be set up.

(This note is not part of the Order)
