# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order 2005, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment to Schedule 2 to the Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994

For Schedule 2 to the 1994 Order (modified provisions of Part II of the Act) substitute the provisions set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.

**7.** Article 9 amends Schedule 4 to the 1994 Order to provide for the modified application of sections 283A and 313A of the 1986 Act which were inserted by the 2002 Act regarding treatment of the matrimonial home in bankruptcy.
