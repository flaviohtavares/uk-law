# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order 2005, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment to Article 8 of the Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994

In Article 8(5)(a) after “Part IV” insert “(other than section 176A)”.

**4.** Article 5 amends Article 10 of the 1994 Order to remove a reference to summary administration consequent upon the abolition of that procedure by the 2002 Act.
