# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order 2005, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Amendment to Schedule 10 to the Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994

Schedule 10 to the 1994 Order is amended as follows—

- (a) for “The Insolvency Regulations 1986” substitute “The Insolvency Regulations 1994”[^f00004];
- (b) omit “The Insolvency Fees Order 1986”;
- (c) for “The Companies (Disqualification Orders) Regulations 1986” substitute “The Companies (Disqualification Orders) Regulations 2001[^f00005];
- (d) for “The Insolvent Companies (Reports on Conduct of Directors) No. 2 Rules 1986” substitute “The Insolvent Companies (Reports on Conduct of Directors) Rules 1996[^f00006];
- (e) for “The Insolvency Practitioners Regulations 1990” substitute “The Insolvency Practitioners Regulations 2005[^f00007];
- (f) at the end insert—

  > - The Insolvency Practitioners and Insolvency Services Accounts (Fees) Order 2003[^f00008];
  > - The Insolvency Proceedings (Fees) Order 2004[^f00009]

  .
