# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order 2005, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Transitional provisions

The amendments to the 1994 Order set out in articles 3, 7 and 12 of this Order do not apply where a petition for an administration order has been presented in relation to an insolvent partnership before the commencement date.

**2.** Article 7 of this Order gives effect to Schedule 1 to this Order which substitutes Schedule 2 of the 1994 Order (modified provisions of Part II of the 1986 Act). The substituted Schedule 2 contains modifications to Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act.
