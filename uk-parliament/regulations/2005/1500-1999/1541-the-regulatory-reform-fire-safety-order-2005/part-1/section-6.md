# The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/1541
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Application to premises

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Order does not apply in relation to —

*Extent: W*

- (a) domestic premises, except to the extent mentioned in paragraph (1A) or[^c-1635cb5f3b3679096f071906203850f3] article 31(10);
- (b) an offshore installation within the meaning of regulation 3 of the Offshore Installation and Pipeline Works (Management and Administration) Regulations 1995 [^c12250121];
- (c) a ship, in respect of the normal ship-board activities of a ship's crew which are carried out solely by the crew under the direction of the master;
- (d) fields, woods or other land forming part of an agricultural or forestry undertaking but which is not inside a building and is situated away from the undertaking's main buildings;
- (e) an aircraft, locomotive or rolling stock, trailer or semi-trailer used as a means of transport or a vehicle for which a licence is in force under the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994 [^c12250131] or a vehicle exempted from duty under that Act;
- (f) a mine within the meaning of section 180 of the Mines and Quarries Act 1954 [^c12250141], other than any building on the surface at a mine;
- (g) a borehole site to which the Borehole Sites and Operations Regulations 1995 [^c12250151] apply.
- (1A (Extent: W))[^c-975c4491388b2483210a4508da5a2133] Where a building contains two or more sets of domestic premises, the things to which this order applies include— *(Extent: W)*
- (a (Extent: W)) the building's structure and external walls and any common parts; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) all doors between the domestic premises and common parts (so far as not falling within sub-paragraph (a)). *(Extent: W)*
- (1B (Extent: W)) The reference to external walls includes— *(Extent: W)*
- (a (Extent: W)) doors or windows in those walls, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) anything attached to the exterior of those walls (including balconies). *(Extent: W)*

(2) Subject to the preceding provisions *(Extent: W)*[^c-3f35a937a34b8bed8b3cbd7a2f329931] of this article, this Order applies in relation to any premises.
