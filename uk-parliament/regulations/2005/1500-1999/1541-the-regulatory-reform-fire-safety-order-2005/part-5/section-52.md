# The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, section 52

## 52. Subordinate provisions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^c12250421]For the purposes of section 4(3) of the Regulatory Reform Act 2001 (subordinate provisions) the following are designated as subordinate provisions—

- (a) article 1(3);
- (b) in article 2, the definition of “relevant local authority”;
- (c) article 9(6) and (7);
- (d) in article 10, the reference to “Part 3 of Schedule 1”;
- (e) article 11(2);
- (f) article 14(2);
- (g) article 16(1)(a) to (d);
- (h) article 16(4);
- (i) article 18(6) and (7);
- (j) article 25;
- (k)[^key-52c09cc3427be38fbf33522a48210604] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (l) article 49; and
- (m) Schedule 1.

(2) A subordinate provisions order [^c12250431] made in relation to article 1(3) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3) A subordinate provisions order made in relation to any of the provisions mentioned in article 52(1)(b) to (m) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.
