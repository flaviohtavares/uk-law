## 51. Application to visiting forces, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

This Order applies to a visiting force or an international headquarters or defence organisation designated for the purposes of the International Headquarters and Defence Organisations Act 1964 [^c12250411] only to the extent that it applies to the Crown.
