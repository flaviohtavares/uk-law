# The Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1871

EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS

The Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2005

Made

2nd July 2005

Laid before Parliament

11th July 2005

Coming into force

1st October 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 30(1), (2)(b) and (2A) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001] and after consultation with the Lord President of the Court of Session, hereby makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2005 and shall come into force on 1st October 2005.

(2) In these Rules, any reference to a rule or to the Schedule is a reference to a rule in, or to the Schedule to, the Employment Appeal Tribunal Rules 1993[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of Rules

In Rule 30A(2)(a)—

- (a) insert “in relation to particular proceedings before it” between the first “do” and “anything”; and
- (b) insert “in relation to particular Crown employment proceedings” between the second “do” and “by”.

**3.** In the Schedule—

- (a) for Form 1 substitute—

  [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1871/images/uksi_20051871_en_001)
- (b) in Form 3—
  - (i) insert “Appeal from decision of employment tribunal/certification officer” beneath “FORM 3”;
  - (ii) in paragraph 3, insert “judgment,” before “decision”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Rules come into force on 1st October 2005 and they amend the Employment Appeal Tribunal Rules 1993 ([SI 1993/2854](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2854)). These Rules make the following amendments:

Rule 2 implements section 36 of the Employment Relations Act [2004 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/24) (“the 2004 Act”), which, by amending section 10(6) of the Employment Tribunals Act [1996 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/17) (“the 1996 Act”), provides that certain powers available to employment tribunals under employment tribunal procedure regulations for use in national security proceedings can be used in particular proceedings, whether or not they are Crown employment proceedings, if the tribunal considers it expedient in the interests of national security. Section 30(2A) of the 1996 Act provides that appeal tribunal rules may make provision of a kind which may be made by employment tribunal procedure regulations under section 10(6) (and other sub-sections) of the Act.

Rule 3(1) replaces Form 1 with an amended version.

Rule 3(2) makes minor amendments to Form 3.

The full Regulatory Impact Assessment (“RIA”) for the 2004 Act indicated that any impact on business arising from section 36 (the national security amendment) would be likely to be negligible. In view of this, and because none of the other amendments made by these Rules is substantive, and will not therefore impact on business, charities, voluntary bodies or the public sector, a full RIA in respect of these Rules has not been prepared.
