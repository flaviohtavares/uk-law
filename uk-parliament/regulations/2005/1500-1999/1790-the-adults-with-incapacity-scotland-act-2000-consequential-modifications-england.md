# The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Consequential Modifications) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1790 (S.5)

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, SCOTLAND

SOCIAL SECURITY, ENGLAND AND WALES

SOCIAL SECURITY, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Consequential Modifications) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2005

Made

29th June 2005

Coming into force

30th June 2005

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 115 of, and paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 of Schedule 7 to, the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001];

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 104, 112(1) and 113 of that Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Consequential Modifications) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2005 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), this Order extends to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

(3) Articles 2 and 3 of this Order extend to England and Wales only[^f00002].

(4) Article 4 of this Order extends to Northern Ireland only[^f00003].

## 2. Child Support Act 1991

In section 50 of the Child Support Act 1991 (unauthorised disclosure of information)[^f00004], in subsection (8)(c), for sub-paragraphs (i) and (ii) substitute “a guardian or other person entitled to act on behalf of the person under the [Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2000/4)”.

## 3. Social Security Administration Act 1992

In section 123 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (unauthorised disclosure of information relating to particular persons)[^f00005], in subsection (10)(c), for sub-paragraphs (i) and (ii) substitute “a guardian or other person entitled to act on behalf of the person under the [Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2000/4)”.

## 4. Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992

In section 117 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (unauthorised disclosure of information relating to particular persons)[^f00006], in subsection (10)(c), for sub-paragraphs (i) and (ii) substitute “a guardian or other person entitled to act on behalf of the person under the [Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2000/4)”.

DAVID CAIRNS

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Scotland Office

Department for Constitutional Affairs

Dover House, London

29th June 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision where necessary in consequence of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (“the 2000 Act”). The 2000 Act amended the Child Support Act 1991 and the Social Security Administration Act 1992 including the definition under these Acts as regards Scotland of a mental health custodian.

Articles 2 and 3 make identical amendment to these Acts as regards England and Wales.

Article 4 makes the same amendment to an equivalent Act, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, as regards Northern Ireland.
