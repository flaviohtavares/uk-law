# The Land Registration (Proper Office) (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1765

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration (Proper Office) (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

30th June 2005

Laid before Parliament

7th July 2005

Coming into force

24th October 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 100(3) of the Land Registration Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Land Registration (Proper Office) (Amendment) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 24th October 2005.

## 2. Amendment of the Land Registration (Proper Office) Order 2003

(1) The Land Registration (Proper Office) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(2), after “Executives” in (c) of the definition of “conveyancer” there is inserted “, or (d) a duly certificated notary public”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Cathy Ashton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

30th June 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Land Registration (Proper Office) Order 2003 designates particular offices of the land registry as the proper office for the receipt of specified descriptions of application under the Land Registration Act 2002 ([2002 c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/9). The Order does not apply to, amongst other applications, an application to the registrar delivered in accordance with a written agreement as to delivery made between the registrar and the applicant’s conveyancer. This Order amends the definition of “conveyancer” in article 2(2) of the Land Registration (Proper Office) Order 2003 to include a certificated notary public.
