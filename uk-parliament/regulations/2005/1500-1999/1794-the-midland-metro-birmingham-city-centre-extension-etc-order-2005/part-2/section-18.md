# The Midland Metro (Birmingham City Centre Extension, etc.) Order 2005, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Agreements with street authorities

(1) A street authority and the Executive may enter into agreements with respect to—

- (a) the construction of any new street (including any structure carrying the street over or under any authorised tramway) under the powers conferred by this Order;
- (b) the maintenance of the structure of any bridge carrying a highway over any authorised tramway or carrying any authorised tramway over a highway;
- (c) any stopping up, alteration or diversion of a street under the powers conferred by this Order; or
- (d) the execution in the street of any of the works referred to in article 11.

(2) Such an agreement may, without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1)—

- (a) provide for the street authority to carry out any function under this Order which relates to the street in question; and
- (b) contain such terms as to payment and otherwise as the parties consider appropriate.
