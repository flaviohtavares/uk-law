# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Approved National Allocation Plan) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1387

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Approved National Allocation Plan) Regulations 2005

Made

24th May 2005

Laid before Parliament

24th May 2005

Coming into force

14th June 2005

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to greenhouse gas emission allowance trading, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by that subsection, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Approved National Allocation Plan) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 14th June 2005.

## 2. Specification of approved national allocation plan

For the purposes of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2005[^f00003], the document entitled “Approved National Allocation Plan 2005-2007” published by the Secretary of State on 24th May 2005 is specified as the approved national allocation plan for the three year period beginning on 1st January 2005.

Elliot Morley

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

24th May 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify the document which is the approved national allocation plan for the first three-year period (beginning on 1st January 2005) of the European Union’s Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme for the purposes of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2005 (S.I. [2005/925](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/925)). These Regulations extend to the United Kingdom.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared and placed in the library of each House of Parliament. Copies can be obtained from National Climate Change Policy Division, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Ashdown House, 123 Victoria Street, London SW1E 6DE.
