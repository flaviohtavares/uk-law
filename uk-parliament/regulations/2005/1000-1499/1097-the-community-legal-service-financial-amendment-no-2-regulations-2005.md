# The Community Legal Service (Financial) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1097

LEGAL SERVICES COMMISSION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Community Legal Service (Financial) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2005

Made

5th April 2005

Laid before Parliament

5th April 2005

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Community Legal Service (Financial) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** In regulation 3 of the Community Legal Service (Financial) Regulations 2000[^f00003]–

- (a) in paragraph (1), at the end insert—

  > ;
  >
  > - (i) Legal Help or Legal Representation for a person who is subject to a control order, consisting of advice in connection with that order, help with an application to the Secretary of State under section 7(1) of the Prevention of Terrorism Act 2005[^f00004], or representation in control order proceedings

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (2), after the definition of “Central Authority”, insert—

  > - “control order” and “control order proceedings” have the same meanings as in the Prevention of Terrorism Act 2005;

  .

Falconer of Thoroton, C.

5th April 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the Community Legal Service (Financial) Regulations 2000 to make legal help and representation under the Community Legal Service available to a person subject to a control order under the Prevention of Terrorism Act 2005 without reference to the person’s financial resources.
