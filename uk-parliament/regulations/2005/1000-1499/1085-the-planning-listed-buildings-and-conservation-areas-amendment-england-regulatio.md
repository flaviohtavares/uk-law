# The Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1085

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND

The Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005

Made

1st April 2005

Laid before Parliament

4th April 2005

Coming into force

1st May 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 2(3)[^f00001] and 93(1) and (7)[^f00002] of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990[^f00003], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st May 2005.

**2.** These Regulations apply in relation to England only.

## 3. Amendment of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Regulations 1990

For Schedule 4 to the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Regulations 1990[^f00004] substitute the following—

> **SCHEDULE 4**
>
> Regulation 14

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/1519](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1519), as amended).

Regulation 3 amends Schedule 4 to the 1990 Regulations to reflect the fact that the Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport is responsible for the compilation of lists of buildings of special architectural or historic interest under section 1 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990. It also amends the note to the prescribed form notifying the inclusion of a building in one of the lists and inserts a note to the prescribed form notifying the exclusion of a building.
