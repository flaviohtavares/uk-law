# The A428 Trunk Road (Cambourne to Hardwick Improvement) (Detrunking) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1152

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A428 Trunk Road (Cambourne to Hardwick Improvement) (Detrunking) Order 2005

Made

17th March 2005

Coming into force

11th April 2005

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A428 Trunk Road (Cambourne to Hardwick Improvement) (Detrunking) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 11th April 2005.

**2.** In this Order—

(1) all measurements of distance are approximate only, and are measured along the route of the relevant highway. Where junctions are referred to as points of reference in measurements, those measurements are made from the centre line of the junction if it is a \`T'–junction and from the centre of the roundabout island if it is a roundabout junction;

(2)

- (i) “the plan” means the plan contained in the folio numbered HA 10/MP/025 marked “The A428 Trunk Road (Cambourne to Hardwick Improvement) (Detrunking) Order 2005”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at Records Management Branch, Room F13, Ashdown House, Sedlescombe Road North, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA;
- (ii) “classified road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is not a principal road for the purposes of enactments or instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads but is a classified road for the purpose of every enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State and which does not specifically refer to their classification as principal roads;
- (iii) “the new trunk roads” means the new highways which the Secretary of State proposes to construct referred to in article 2 (iii) of the A428 Trunk Road (Cambourne to Hardwick Improvement and Slip Roads) Order 2005[^f00003]; and
- (iv) “the trunk road” means the A428 Trunk Road.

**3.** The length of trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a classified road from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the Cambridgeshire County Council that the new trunk roads are open for traffic.

**4.** The centre line of the trunk road is indicated by heavy black dashes on the plan.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Peter Wallwork

Divisional Director Highways Agency

17th March 2005

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The route of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is a 4.7 kilometre length from a point on the A428 Trunk Road 670 metres west of the road to Knapwell to a point on the A428 Trunk Road 710 metres east of Hardwick roundabout.
