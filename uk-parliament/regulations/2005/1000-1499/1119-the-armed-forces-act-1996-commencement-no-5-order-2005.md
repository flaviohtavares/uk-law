# The Armed Forces Act 1996 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1119 (C.48 )

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Act 1996 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2005

Made

6 April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 36(2) of the Armed Forces Act 1996[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1996 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2005.

**2.** Section 35 of and Schedule 7 to the Armed Forces Act 1996, so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 29 April 2005.

Ivor Caplin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Ministry of Defence

6 April 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force those repeals in Schedule 7 to the Armed Forces Act 1996 which are not already in force. The effect is to bring into force the repeal of section 46 of the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00002], section 69 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00003] and Article 52 of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00004].

(This note is not part of the Order)
