# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 4

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Fire and explosion measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-69370ec0ea5c88127f6e712cf7bca336][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any person who manufactures or stores explosives shall take appropriate measures — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) to prevent fire or explosion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) to limit the extent of fire or explosion including measures to prevent the spreading of fires and the communication of explosions from one location to another; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) to protect persons from the effects of fire or explosion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), the reference to the manufacture or storage of explosives shall be deemed to include a reference to any handling, on-site transport and testing of explosives which is associated with that manufacture or storage. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) In this regulation, “fire or explosion” means unplanned fire or explosion at the site of manufacture or storage. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
