# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 5

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Separation distances (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a96a02eb5f2b7c74a55f137bd0d963a8][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), every person who stores explosives at a site shall ensure that the relevant separation distance prescribed by Schedule 2 is maintained between a store and a building or other place not within that site to which that Schedule applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2)[^c-fd2c14445759443350c328bc4237e3aa] Paragraph (1) shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) desensitised explosives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) explosives which are stored under a licence granted by the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-3564bb7fc6770b3f5b7d7138ebfa15d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] in cases — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) where the assent of the local authority was required pursuant to regulation 13(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] where that assent was not required by virtue of regulation 13(4)(c), (e) or (f); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the storage of explosives where the total quantity of explosives stored at a site, excluding, in the case of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] sub-paragraphs (b) and (d)[^c-9d84f23625644a2cfde7d791b626051d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], any amount of small arms ammunition, does not exceed — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) 100 grams; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-eac5f08726acbb995c59fa35e57e4618][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a combined total of 5 kilograms of shooters’ powder and model rocket motors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) 30 kilograms of shooters' powder and 300 grams of percussion caps; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) 200 detonators and — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) 5 kilograms of water-based explosive and detonating cord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] 5 kilograms of water-based explosive or detonating cord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) in the case of explosives kept by a police force — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i)[^c-2d5609d4e879c270c3d306d67b540527] 4 kilograms of explosive kept for operational purposes, including ordnance disposal and the training of dogs for the detection of explosives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] 30 kilograms of explosives kept *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] solely[^c-f905d77aefc984d49417eeda51b85685] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] for the purposes of ordnance disposal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

and the explosives are stored in a safe and suitable place with all due precautions for public safety. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) Every person to whom the duty under paragraph (1) applies shall ensure that the separation distance referred to in paragraph (5) is maintained between a store and any building on the site in which the store is situated which is used either for accommodation or for work, but not including any building used for work which is normally unoccupied by any person and is not a store. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) The separation distance referred to in paragraph (4) is that which is equal to half the relevant separation distance determined in accordance with Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(6) Every person who, in a case to which paragraph (3) of regulation 13 applies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or a case to which that paragraph does not apply by virtue of regulation 13(4)(f)[^c-5877b8dc345cc0a4083911142d64f349] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], is granted a licence to manufacture or store explosives shall ensure that the local planning authority in whose area the manufacture or storage takes place is, within 28 days of the licence being — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) granted; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) varied in a way which affects the separation distances required to be maintained, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

given a plan of the site and its immediate surrounding area showing the separation distances required to be maintained pursuant to the licence or varied licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(7) “Local planning authority” in paragraph (6), in relation to an area — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) in England and Wales has the same meaning as it has in Part I of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00025] save that, where there is more than one local planning authority, it means the district planning authority for the district; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) in Scotland means the council for the local government area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(8)[^c-f2bbffc01bd9f826da532f94ab3c8b18] In this regulation, “model rocket motors” means explosive articles which — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) are allocated the U.N. no. 0186, 0272, 0349, 0351 or 0471; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) are intended to be used for the propulsion of model rockets or similar articles; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) in respect of each individual explosive article, contain no more than 1 kilogram of explosive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
