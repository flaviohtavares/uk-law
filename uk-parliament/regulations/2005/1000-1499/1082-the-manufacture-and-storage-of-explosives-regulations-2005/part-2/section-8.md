# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 8

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Unauthorised access (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0aab55b11aa15a5f7929444254865978][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No person shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) without the permission of the occupier, enter — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) any store in or at a site; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] any building used for the manufacture of explosives in or at a site, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] any site with clearly marked boundaries at which explosives are stored or manufactured, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  operating under a licence or registration; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) having so entered, refuse to leave that site when requested to do so by a constable or the occupier, his employee or agent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) Where following a request referred to in paragraph (1)(b) the person who has entered that place without permission refuses to leave that site, a constable or the occupier, his employee or agent may remove that person from the site using reasonable force, if necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) “Enter” for the purposes of this regulation, includes entering onto a roof of a store or a building in which explosives are manufactured. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
