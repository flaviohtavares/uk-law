# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 27

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Savings and transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3ab8ad61feee890bba8523128077ee64][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A licence, amending licence, continuing certificate or store licence granted or issued under, as the case may be, section 8, 12, 14 or 15 of the 1875 Act or a licence granted under article 3 of the Ammonium Nitrate Mixtures Exemption Order 1967 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00029] which was valid immediately before the relevant date shall be deemed to be a licence granted under regulation 13 and shall continue in force, notwithstanding the repeal by these Regulations of those provisions, on its existing terms and conditions, subject to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) any variation under regulation 16(1)(a) to (c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) any variation for the purpose of requiring the licensee to maintain a separation distance greater than a separation distance which is required before the variation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) its expiry on the date it was due to expire or its revocation under regulation 17, whichever is the sooner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) A registration effected under section 21 of the 1875 Act which was valid immediately before the relevant date shall be deemed to be a registration under regulation 11 and shall continue in force, notwithstanding the repeal by these Regulations of the said section 21, until the date it was due to expire or it is revoked, whichever is the sooner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Where the manufacture or storage of explosives at any place — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) was immediately before the relevant date exempt from— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the provisions of the 1875 Act by virtue of section 97 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00030]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the requirement for a licence in respect of such manufacture or storage under that Act by virtue of an exemption certificate granted under the Explosives Act 1875 (Exemptions) Regulations 1979 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00031]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) is not manufacture or storage which regulation 3(5) relates to, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

the person carrying on such manufacture and storage shall be deemed to hold a licence granted by the Executive under regulation 13 with an expiry date of 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) In a case to which paragraph (3) applies, regulation 13(3) shall not apply in relation to an application for a licence made to, and received by, the Executive before 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) In relation to the application of these Regulations to the manufacture of any ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate by virtue of regulation 2(2), where a person is manufacturing any ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate on the relevant date, regulations 9, 12, 13, and 15 to 23 shall not apply to that manufacture by that person until 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(6) The requirements of regulation 5 and Schedule 2 shall not apply until 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008 to a police force storing explosives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(7) The requirements of regulation 5 and Schedule 2 shall not apply until 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2010 to a person who stores explosives in respect of which storage there is a deemed registration in force on the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(8) The requirements of regulation 5 and Schedule 2 shall not apply until 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008 to a person who stores explosives in respect of which storage there is a deemed licence in force on the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(9) A person who — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) has a deemed licence in respect of the storage of explosives, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) has a deemed licence which has expired and been replaced by a licence granted under regulation 13 in respect of that storage, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

may apply at any time to the Executive, which shall be the licensing authority, for a licence in respect of that storage, to replace that existing one, which provides for different separation distances to apply in respect of that storage to any which would otherwise apply on and after 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008 under regulation 5 and Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(10) A licence applied for pursuant to paragraph (9) shall not be granted by the Executive unless the Executive is satisfied that it would not be reasonably practicable for the applicant to comply with the separation distances required by regulation 5 and Schedule 2 to which the application relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(11) Where, on the relevant date, a person holds more than one deemed licence, each relating to the storage of explosives at separate places within the same site and the aggregate total of explosives allowed to be stored at that site pursuant to those deemed licences exceeds 2 tonnes, that person shall apply before 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008 to the Executive, which shall be the licensing authority, for a licence to replace those deemed licences for the storage of any explosives that he wishes to store at that site; and, on the date that a licence is granted pursuant to such an application, any such deemed licence shall be treated as revoked under regulation 17 as from that last mentioned date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(12) Where, before the relevant date, a person would not have been required to apply for a licence under the 1875 Act because he was manufacturing or storing explosives in a part of premises already licensed under that Act to cover that manufacture or storage but, by virtue of regulation 9(1) or 10(1) he would need to hold a licence in respect of such manufacture or storage on and after that date — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) he shall be deemed to hold a licence granted under regulation 13 containing the same terms and conditions as the existing licence until 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2008 or the expiry of that existing licence, whichever is the sooner; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) he shall have until that earliest date to apply for a licence under these Regulations in respect of such manufacture or storage carried on by him thereafter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(13) Regulation 13(3) shall not apply in relation to an application for a licence which paragraph (9), (11) or (12) relates to, save where — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) if the licence applied for were to be granted, it would result in an increase in the quantity, or a change in the hazard type, of any explosive presently permitted at the site under a deemed licence; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the application is received by the Executive after 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> April 2006; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the application is for a licence to replace a deemed licence which was, before the relevant date, a store licence granted by a local authority under section 15 of the 1875 Act on or after 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]</sup> May 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(14) Where an application for a licence pursuant to section 6 of the 1875 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00032] has been made to, and received by, the Executive before the relevant date and the application has not been refused nor a licence granted by that date, the application shall be deemed to be an application for a licence under these Regulations and the provisions of these Regulations shall apply to the application, subject to the paragraph (15) in relation to the application of any requirements of regulations 13(3) and 14 to any such licence application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(15) In relation to the application of the requirements of regulations 13(3) and 14 to an application which paragraph (14) relates to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) a draft licence approved by the Executive pursuant to section 6 of the 1875 Act before the relevant date shall be deemed to be a draft licence for the purposes of regulation 14(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where notice under section 7 of the 1875 Act in respect of the application and of the time and place at which the local authority will be prepared to hear the applicant has been published before the relevant date — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the provisions of regulation 14(3) and (8) relating to notices shall not apply, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] if the hearing to which the notice relates would be held or continue to be held on or after the relevant date, it may continue to be so held and it shall be deemed to be a hearing for the purposes of regulation 14; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) where a notice under section 7 of the 1875 Act referred to in sub-paragraph (b) has not been published before the relevant date, regulation 14(3) shall have effect as if after “paragraph (2)” there were inserted “or within 28 days of the coming into force of these Regulations, whichever is the later,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) the assent to the application by the local authority pursuant to section 7 of the 1875 Act, or the assent by both local authorities to the application where the assent of both is required under that section, given before the relevant date shall be deemed to be assent for the purposes of regulations 13(3) and 14 and the Executive shall grant a licence under regulation 13 which accords with the draft licence approved by the Executive pursuant to section 6 of the 1875 Act, with the addition, if the assent was on conditions submitted to by the applicant, of the additional restrictions and precautions required by those conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(16) An application for an amending licence under section 12 of the 1875 Act which is made to and received by, but not decided by, the Executive before the relevant date, shall be deemed to be an application for a variation of a licence under these Regulations and the provisions of these Regulations shall apply to the application accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(17) Despite the repeal by these Regulations of section 40 of the 1875 Act, paragraph (9) of that section (as it had effect before the commencement of Schedule 4 to the Placing on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00033]) shall continue to apply to acetylene as it applied before the commencement of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) “deemed licence” means — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) any licence, amending licence, continuing certificate or store licence deemed by virtue of, as the case may be, paragraph (1) or (12) to be a licence granted under regulation 13; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a licence deemed to be held by a person pursuant to paragraph (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  and “deemed licensee” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) “deemed registration” means a registration deemed by virtue of paragraph (2) to be a registration under regulation 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) “licence under the 1875 Act” in paragraph (12) means any of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
