# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 26

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Power to grant exemptions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-78b2f9bde3e79487847c0b358b6e9bef][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Executive may, by a certificate in writing, exempt any person or class of persons or any explosive or class of explosives from any requirement or prohibition imposed by these Regulations, and any such exemption may be granted subject to such conditions and to a limit of time and may be revoked in writing at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) The Executive shall not grant any such exemption unless, having regard to the circumstances of the case, and in particular to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the conditions, if any, which it proposes to attach to the exemption; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) any other requirements imposed by or under any enactment which apply to the case, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

it is satisfied that the health and safety of persons who are likely to be affected by the exemption will not be prejudiced in consequence of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) The Secretary of State for Defence may, in the interests of national security, by a certificate in writing, exempt any of Her Majesty’s Forces, any visiting force, any headquarters or any civilian employee or class of civilian employees of the Ministry of Defence from all or any of the requirements or prohibitions imposed by these Regulations and any such exemption may be granted subject to conditions and to a limit of time and may be revoked by the said Secretary of State by a further certificate in writing at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
