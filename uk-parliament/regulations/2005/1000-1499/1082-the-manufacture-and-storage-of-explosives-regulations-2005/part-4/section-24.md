# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 24

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Prohibition concerning the manufacture, storage and importation of certain explosives (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-87c9f66616b87fd7a527586858ae57d7][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no pyrotechnic which consists of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) sulphur; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) phosphorus, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

mixed with chlorate of potassium or other chlorates or which contains any such mixture shall be manufactured, stored or imported. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) This regulation does not apply to any pyrotechnic named in a list, approved by the Executive, of pyrotechnics falling within the description referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) A contravention of paragraph (1) concerning importation shall be punishable only under the 1974 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00028].
