# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 25

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Prohibition concerning the acquisition and supply of fireworks (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ec6012f86fb3c68f300a7b38a380e85c][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No person shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) acquire more than 50 kilograms of fireworks unless he (“Person A”) or another person holds a valid licence or certificate of registration for the storage by Person A of those fireworks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) sell or otherwise transfer to any person (“Person B”) more than 50 kilograms of fireworks unless Person B shows a valid licence or certificate of registration for the storage by Person B of those fireworks, to the person selling or otherwise transferring the fireworks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) This regulation does not apply to a person who is transporting fireworks on behalf of another person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
