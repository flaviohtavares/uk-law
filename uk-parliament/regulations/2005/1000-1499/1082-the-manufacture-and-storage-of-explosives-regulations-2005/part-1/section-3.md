# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 3

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-01734a759d8a151fd42f5b8395468eea][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as provided for in paragraph (8), these Regulations shall apply — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) in Great Britain; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) outside Great Britain as sections 1 to 59 and 80 to 82 of the 1974 Act apply by virtue of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application outside Great Britain) Order 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00022].

(2) Regulations 4 to 25 shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) any activity to which Part IX of the Dangerous Substances in Harbour Areas Regulations 1987 (explosives) applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) any activity to which the Carriage of Dangerous Goods and Use of Transportable Pressure Equipment Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] 2007[^c-78849e7a2beff1e3a142af87369eb481] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] apply, apart from any activity which is to be treated as storage by virtue of regulation 2(6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the master or crew of a ship or to the employer of such persons in respect of the normal shipboard activities of a ship’s crew which are carried out solely by the crew under the direction of the master and in this sub-paragraph the reference to the normal shipboard activities of a ship’s crew shall include — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the construction, reconstruction or conversion of a ship outside, but not inside, Great Britain; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the repair of a ship save repair when carried out in dry dock; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) the transport of explosives by air; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e) an offshore installation within the meaning of regulation 3 of the Offshore Installations and Pipeline Works (Management and Administration) Regulations 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00023];
- (f)[^c-0d49d94ce0700401eac66e0dd75998a7] the storage of explosives below ground at a mine, where the explosives are for use at that mine for the getting of minerals or ensuring the safety of the mine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Regulations 8 and 10 shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) a constable in the execution of his duties; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) an inspector appointed under section 19 of the 1974 Act in the performance of his functions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) a customs officer in the performance of his functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) Regulation 10 shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the Executive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-304ba9d28903e14b1154ba2d16233309][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the ONR; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) a local authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the Commissioners of Customs and Excise; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) a police force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e) a person employed as mentioned in section 15 of the Police Act 1996 (civilian employees) or section 9 of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967 (employees other than constables) who, in either case, is duly authorised in writing by the chief officer of police to store explosives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) Regulations 5 and 9 to 21 shall not apply to the manufacture or storage of explosives, at any site under the control of the Secretary of State for Defence, or held for the purposes of a visiting force or headquarters, under a scheme approved by him which — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) provides for their safe manufacture and storage; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) prescribes — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) separation distances, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a combination of separation distances and other safety measures, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- which are designed to ensure a standard of safety which is equivalent to that ensured by the separation distances prescribed by regulation 5 and Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(6) Regulations 5 to 21 and 24 shall not apply to explosives — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) seized by a constable in the execution of his duties, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) received by a police force from a member of the public; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) which, for reasons of public safety or protection of property, are undergoing ordnance disposal by — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) persons under the direction of a member of Her Majesty’s Forces or civilian employees of the Ministry of Defence authorised in writing by the Secretary of State for Defence to carry out ordnance disposal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] persons under the direction of a constable or authorised in writing by the chief officer of police to carry out ordnance disposal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(7) In relation to the application of these Regulations to ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate by virtue of regulation 2(2), regulations 10 and 11 shall not apply to the storage of ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(8) To the extent that these Regulations concern the importation of pyrotechnics into the United Kingdom, they shall extend to Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00024].
