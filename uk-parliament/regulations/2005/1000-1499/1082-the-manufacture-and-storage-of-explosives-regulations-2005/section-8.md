# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 8

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**8.** Grounds for refusing or varying a licence or registration are set out in, respectively, regulations 15 and 16. The grounds for revoking a licence or registration are set out in regulation 17. A person may appeal against a refusal or revocation of registration (regulation 19). Licences and registration may be transferred (regulation 20).
