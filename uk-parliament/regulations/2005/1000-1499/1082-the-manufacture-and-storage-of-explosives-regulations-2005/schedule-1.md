# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — MEANING OF LICENSING AUTHORITY

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 2(1)

**1.**[^key-f794391f5e5f7a3320064bab032592fa][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] Subject to regulation 27(9) and (11) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] paragraphs 2 to 4[^key-6effe158889f10eadd1b4e19a8907fa1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , “licensing authority” means — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) in relation to an application for registration, or for a licence for the storage within one site of no more than 2000 kilograms of explosives to which paragraph (a)(i) or (b) of the definition of “explosive” in regulation 2(1) applies — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the chief officer of police for the area in which the storage is to take place where any of the explosives are of a type not listed in Schedule 1 to the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00035];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the local authority for the area in which the storage is to take place where all the explosives are of a type listed in Schedule 1 to the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the Executive where the explosives are to be stored *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] on the surface at a mine, whether in a building or not,[^key-f7a2fb998d8eff89bd070ee10a7d9fe1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or within a harbour; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where the application for a licence relates to the manufacture of any ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate, the Executive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) in relation to an application for a licence in any other case, the Executive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**2.**[^key-48658a445b4c283101ccfd3639e545c2] In any case falling within paragraph 1(a)(i) or (ii), the applicant may apply instead to the Executive for a licence, in which case the Executive shall be the licensing authority in place of the chief officer of police or local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**3.**[^key-6dc82ab31f4c54c1c4ab29fc54eee180] Where a person wishes to manufacture and store explosives at the same site *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] and requires a licence for that manufacture and for that storage[^key-1d5df4c1bae034c108e0bf0f9a92fb43] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], the Executive shall be the licensing authority in respect of any application for a licence relating to that site and the reference to “an application” in paragraph 1(c) includes any such application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**4.**[^key-409608d42301955cc96d3e58d9a94fdc] The ONR shall be the licensing authority in relation to any application for registration in relation to storage, or for a licence for the manufacture or storage of, explosives on an ONR regulated site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
