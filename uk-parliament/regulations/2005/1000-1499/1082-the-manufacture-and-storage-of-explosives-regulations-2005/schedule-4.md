# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — REGISTERS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 22

**1.**[^c-54d463c1d63b96255db858668e0de958][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] The licensing authority shall maintain a register (“the register”) containing the information listed in paragraph 2 which relates to licences and registrations granted or made by it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**2.**[^c-e154e84d993b598013bccf60775aa94b] The following information shall be included in the register — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the name of the licensee or, as the case may be, the person registered under regulation 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) his permanent address, unless his home address is his only permanent address; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the address of the site where the explosives are manufactured or stored (where that differs from any address included pursuant to sub-paragraph (b)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) the hazard type and maximum amount of explosive which may be stored at any one time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e) the nature of the business of the licensee or the person registered under regulation 11 and the intended use of the explosives; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (f) the kind of explosives manufactured or stored; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (g)[^c-4d1d850b9e759d7c8a658590e4aff777] where separation distances are required by regulation 5 or a condition of the licence to be maintained around the store or the building where explosives are manufactured, a plan in a suitable scale sufficient to show those separation distances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (h) a map in a suitable scale sufficient to show the location of any stores; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (i) the kind of store concerned, including the material out of which it is constructed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**3.**[^c-5570a9578b5100e30f7cb94e2579585a] Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] paragraphs 7 and 7A[^c-349f93485fb3fff4fb4309cf096953d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], where the licence or registration relates to explosives which do not require an explosives certificate under the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00037], the licensing authority shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) ensure that the information referred to in paragraph 2(a) to (d) in respect of that licence or registration is available for inspection at an office of the licensing authority, at all reasonable times and free of charge, by members of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) provide a copy of the entry in the register relating to the information referred to in paragraph 2(a) to (d) in respect of that licence or registration to a member of the public who requests a copy and pays a charge which shall not exceed the reasonable cost of providing the copy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**4.**[^c-bbb172d0a9a1cea0aca52a3b9269b85e] Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] paragraphs 7 and 7A[^c-d7af6b0291cb2ab803b6e52a067de68e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], where the licence or registration relates to explosives which require an explosives certificate under the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991, the licensing authority shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) ensure that the information referred to in paragraph 2(a) to (d) in respect of that licence or registration is available for inspection at an office of the licensing authority, at all reasonable times and free of charge, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^c-447d8ed65d9800191fb045d5d223fde7]... by a person who resides or, in the case of an undertaking, is situated within a public consultation zone concerned in relation to the licence or registration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) provide a copy of the entry in the register relating to the information referred to in sub-paragraph 2(a) to (d) in respect of that licence or registration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^c-447d8ed65d9800191fb045d5d223fde7]... to such a person as is referred to in sub-paragraph (a) who requests a copy and pays a charge which shall not exceed the reasonable cost of providing the copy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**5.**[^c-a9f31991ca63615932db83ce7216ac29] The register may be kept in any form, including electronically. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**6.**[^c-849a0ff32a9c9b97c710030c70037489] The licensee or the person registered under regulation 11 in relation to any site in which explosives are manufactured or stored, shall, where requested in writing to do so by the owner or, if not the same person, the occupier, of any premises falling within any separation distance applying in relation to that site, provide to the requesting person within twenty-eight days of the request a scale plan of the area of land falling within that separation distance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**7.**[^c-f2793cf6cb343ac5fe6e94d46a101536] The requirements of paragraphs 3 and 4 shall not apply where the information referred to in paragraph 2 is in respect of any site to which a licence or registration relates which is used only for the storage of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) less than 500 kilograms of hazard type 1 explosive or hazard type 2 explosive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) less than 2 tonnes of hazard type 3 explosive or hazard type 4 explosive; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) any explosives for a period of less than four weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**7A.**[^c-746247985a30cc40d2f069be52f42313] The requirements of paragraphs 3 and 4 shall not apply where the information referred to in paragraph 2 is in respect of a licensed site in relation to which regulation 13(3) did not apply to the licence application for that site by virtue of regulation 13(4)(f). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**8.**[^c-6ff6de28a89fa4d69024b3e292190c21] Where the licensing authority is a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-042419f558a0fafdd7b87bd15c16647f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , that licensing authority shall notify the Executive in writing, within twenty-eight days of receipt of a written request by the Executive to do so, as to such information as is contained in the register it maintains relating to the information referred to in paragraph 2 as the Executive may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**9.**[^c-f7f2d9da3ab5bde1d3fd48c1d33be5cc] Nothing in this Schedule shall prevent a licensing authority from disclosing any of the information included in the register it maintains to a local planning authority or a joint planning board for the purposes of the exercise of their respective functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

**10.**[^c-d36e4bbe447bf9f5e3bf5f0ff3122ac9] For the purposes of paragraph 9, “local planning authority” and “joint planning board” have the meanings they are given in, respectively, sections 1 and 2 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00038].
