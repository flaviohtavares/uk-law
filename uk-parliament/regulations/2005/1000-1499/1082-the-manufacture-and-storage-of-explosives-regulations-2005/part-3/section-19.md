# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 19

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Appeal against decisions concerning registrations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bacdf8088aa9fe198c25375175c720f1][^key-3f63a3bb6b390c3c2b788d369b113e98][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person may appeal to the Secretary of State against a decision of a licensing authority — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) refusing to register him, to renew his registration or to transfer to him a registration held by another; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) issuing him with a registration subject to a term which aggrieves him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) varying a registration without his agreement to the variation or refusing to vary a term of his registration; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) revoking his registration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

and the provisions of section 44(2) to (6) of the 1974 Act (appeals in connection with licensing provisions) shall apply in respect of any such appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
