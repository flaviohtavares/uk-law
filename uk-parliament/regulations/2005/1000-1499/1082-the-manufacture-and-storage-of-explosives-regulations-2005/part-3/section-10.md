# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 10

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Explosives not to be stored without a licence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4a95b3685fda36d8508328a101c1d3c8][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no person shall store explosives unless he holds a licence for their storage and complies with the conditions of that licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the storage of explosives by a person registered in accordance with regulation 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the storage of one or more of the following — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) no more than 10 kilograms of black powder; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] no more than 5 kilograms of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shooters' powder; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] any explosive or combination of explosives listed in Schedule 1 to the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a combination of shooters' powder and any one or more of the explosives listed in Schedule 1 to those Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] no more than 15 kilograms of percussion caps or small arms ammunition or a mixture of them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the storage of no more than 7 kilograms of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) hazard type 1 or 2 explosives, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a combination of hazard type 1 or 2 explosives with explosives of another hazard type, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  for no longer than 24 hours; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) the storage of hazard type 3 or 4 explosives for no longer than 24 hours; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e) the storage of no more than 100 kilograms of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) hazard type 3 explosives consisting of fireworks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shooters' powders; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a combination of shooters' powders and hazard type 3 and 4 explosives consisting of fireworks, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  provided that the explosives are stored for no longer than 3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] consecutive[^key-08eb921bfe72b8872023fe8b9b2b6b89] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] days in their place of intended use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (f) the storage of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) no more than 250 kilograms of hazard type 4 explosives provided that the explosives are stored for no more than 3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] consecutive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] days in their place of intended use; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] no more than 50 kilograms of hazard type 4 explosives consisting solely of fireworks provided that the fireworks are stored for no longer than 21 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] consecutive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] days and are not for sale or for use at work; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (g) the storage of desensitised explosives which have been allocated in accordance with the United Nations Recommendations the U.N. nos. 2059, 2555, 2556, 2557, 1336 or 1337; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (h)[^key-1e2aa77008b7647ded9ed0bb4ccab433] the storage of explosives by a company which is a wholly-owned subsidiary of another company at a site in relation to which that other company holds — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) a registration, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a licence to store explosives, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  and that storage by the wholly-owned subsidiary is in accordance with any term of that registration or condition of that licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2) — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) no more than one of the exceptions listed in subparagraphs (a) to (f) of paragraph (2) may be relied on in relation to explosives stored at the same site at the same time, irrespective of the person who is storing them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^key-b71d3dcb86442ee37d57d59e740625e2]...
- (b) the quantities referred to in that paragraph are the maximum quantities of the explosives *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^key-4f43954c37f8b9c437c6fafbc0d66359]... they respectively relate to which may be present at a site at any one time *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] and[^key-52bdbed2316ead6f96271821320ec65b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c)[^key-ce13a3e4ed4850f225fd51b1aabf3abe] in sub-paragraphs (e) and (f), Christmas Day, Good Friday and, to the extent they are not the same as those, any day which under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 is a bank holiday shall not be included for the purposes of determining the period of days referred to in those sub-paragraphs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
