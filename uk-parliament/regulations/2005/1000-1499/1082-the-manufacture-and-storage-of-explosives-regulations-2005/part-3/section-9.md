# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 9

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Explosives not to be manufactured without a licence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-382faca9bb6c090b175f937cb5cefdf6][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no person shall manufacture explosives unless he holds a licence for that manufacture and complies with the conditions of that licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the manufacture of explosives for the purpose of laboratory analysis, testing, demonstration or experimentation (but not for practical use or sale) where the total quantity of explosives being manufactured at any time does not exceed 100 grams, but nothing in this sub-paragraph shall be taken as authorising any acquisition or keeping of explosives for which an explosives certificate is required by virtue of regulation 7 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991[^c-c25fb66cfeb3d6701d83288a21a54415] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], without such a certificate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the making or unmaking of small arms ammunition, or the preparation of cartridges for use with firearms which are to be used at historical re-enactment events, where the total quantity of primer and propellant used at any one time does not exceed 2 kilograms and, for these purposes, the quantity of propellant used includes propellant removed from cartridges; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the preparation of shot firing charges in connection with their use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) the preparation, assembly, disassembly and fusing of firework displays at the place of intended use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e) the preparation, assembly and fusing of fireworks, in quantities of no more than 10 kilograms at a time, at a site in relation to which a person holds a licence or registration for the storage of explosives, for the purposes of a firework display to be put on by that person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (f) the preparation, assembly and fusing of explosives commissioned for use in theatrical, television or cinematic special effects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (g) the reprocessing of an explosive to form a pharmaceutical product which is not in itself an explosive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (h) the mixing for immediate use of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) ammonium nitrate with fuel oil; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate with another substance, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  at a mine or quarry, to produce an explosive which is not cap-sensitive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (i) the use of desensitised explosives in the manufacture of products which are not in themselves explosives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (j) the manufacture of explosives by a company which is a wholly-owned subsidiary of another company at a site in relation to which that other company holds a licence to manufacture explosives and that manufacture by the wholly-owned subsidiary is in accordance with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^c-f3a5175e3bc47d7b6a36d822c1efd520]... conditions of that licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) In this regulation — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) in paragraph (2)(c), “shot firing charges” means charges used in shot firing operations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) in paragraph (2)(h), “cap-sensitive” means an explosive which gives a positive result when tested in accordance with the Series 5(a) test of the Manual of Tests and Criteria, third edition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^f00026] supporting the United Nations Recommendations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
