# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 14

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Local authority assent (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c27f2f5d77cc7e072a2ccff4b5a72b14][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where this regulation applies by virtue of regulation 13(3), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-6d1e02c53e0a28509ca2697c02be84f2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shall, subject to regulations 15 and 18, issue the applicant with a draft licence containing the conditions, if any, which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] proposes to attach to the licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) The applicant shall as soon as reasonably practicable send a copy of the application and draft licence to the local authority in whose area the manufacture or storage is proposed to take place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Within 28 days of sending to the local authority the information specified in paragraph (2), the applicant shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) cause to be published in a newspaper circulating in the locality where the manufacture or storage of explosives is proposed to take place a notice which shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) give details of the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] invite representations on matters affecting the health and safety of persons other than the applicant’s employees to be made in writing to the local authority within 28 days of the date that the notice is first published; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] give an address within the area of the local authority at which a copy of the application and draft licence may be inspected and the address of the local authority to which any representations must be sent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) take other reasonable steps to give that information to every person who resides or carries on a business or other undertaking within the public consultation zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) The local authority shall send a copy of any representations referred to in paragraph (3)(a)(ii) to the applicant as soon as reasonably practicable after receiving them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) In considering whether to assent, the local authority shall have regard only to health and safety matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), the local authority shall, before deciding whether to assent to the application, hold a public hearing within 4 months of the date of its receipt of the copy of the application and draft licence referred to in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(7) If, after the period of 28 days referred to in paragraph (3)(a)(ii) has elapsed, the local authority has received no objection to the application, or has only received objections which in its opinion are frivolous or immaterial, it may assent to the application without holding a hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(8) Not less than 28 days before the hearing referred to in paragraph (6), the local authority shall publish notice of the date, time and place fixed for the hearing in a newspaper circulating in the locality and shall send a copy of the notice to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) any person who made representations referred to in paragraph (3)(a)(ii); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-c014ec1750f1446aa1165e3f185d7201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] ,

within 7 days from its publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(9) The local authority shall notify the applicant and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-5873d8391c8ef43808b24159fe2136c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] of its decision within 7 days of making it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(10) Where the local authority fails to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) send a copy of the notice referred to in paragraph (8) to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-6288635960d21048206474151353adb5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] within 3 months from the date that a copy of the application and draft licence was sent to it pursuant to paragraph (2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) notify the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-35df0ac2485f54461e370fa3531199d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] of its decision in accordance with paragraph (9), within 2 months from the date of publication of the notice referred to in paragraph (8), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^c-bbe6173a2f7936a0206ebe28fa20830c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] may make a written request to the local authority for it to state in writing whether it assents to the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(11) Where the local authority does not respond to the written request within 28 days from the date of the request, the local authority shall be deemed to have assented to the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(12) The applicant shall pay a fee to the local authority for the performance by that authority of their functions under this regulation, which fee shall not exceed the sum of the costs reasonably incurred by that authority in performing those functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(13) In this regulation, “applicant” means the applicant for a licence or variation of a licence and “application” means his application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(14)[^c-3b6073c723263bac205f0b1f1ad10c97] In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**appropriate authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where the application is made to the Executive, the Executive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where the application is made to the ONR, the ONR. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
