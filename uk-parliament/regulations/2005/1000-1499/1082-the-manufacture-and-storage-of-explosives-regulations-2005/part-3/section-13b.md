# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 13B

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13B. Licences for sites which become ONR regulated sites (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-769a6e5d7181f57d5d9125ec9ba71fbb][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where this regulation applies a licence granted by, or treated as granted by, a licensing authority (other than the ONR) is to be treated on and after the relevant date as a licence granted by the ONR. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the site in respect of which the licence was granted becomes an ONR regulated site; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the licence mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) remained in force immediately before the date on which the site became an ONR regulated site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**” means, in relation to a site, the date on which the site became an ONR regulated site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
