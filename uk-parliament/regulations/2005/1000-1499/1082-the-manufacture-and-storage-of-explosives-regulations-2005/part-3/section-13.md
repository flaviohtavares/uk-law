# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Grant of licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-15e8733c5ac135342dd88d6b03e0492a][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-a5db123657860a73e1d02f568c13bf2d] A licence, not being a renewal of a licence, may be granted — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where the licensing authority is a local authority, for such period not exceeding two years as the licensing authority determines; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where the licensing authority is a chief officer of police *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , the Executive or the ONR[^c-cce2f1c149ad78c75755b0ce78a4578b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766], for such period not exceeding five years as that licensing authority determines, save that, where the applicant for the licence has been granted an explosives certificate, the licence may be granted for any period not exceeding the due expiry date of that explosives certificate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) for any period or without a time limit in a case — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) to which paragraph (3) applies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] to which paragraph (3) does not apply by virtue of paragraph (4)(e) or (f); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] where the licence application relates only to the manufacture of ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the licensing authority shall grant a licence unless any of the grounds for refusing to do so referred to in regulation 15 apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), where the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-a1e3d0cf5e300270f27d7ca129cb1d9c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] is the licensing authority, the procedure set out in regulation 14 for obtaining the assent of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the local authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) each local authority where the proposed site which is the subject of the application for a licence is situated partly within the area of one local authority and partly within the area of another, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

shall apply and the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or, as the case may be, the ONR[^c-bd304815f788bd7bd7649f1b71ceb6ec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shall refuse to grant a licence unless the local authority, or each local authority, as the case may be, has so assented. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) Paragraph (3) shall not apply — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-caf713e15a42d8ddfe930a4e1e172cec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] is the licensing authority in a case where the application is for a licence to store at a mine or within a harbour explosives of no more than 2000 kilograms to which paragraph (a)(i) or (b) of the definition of “explosive” in regulation 2(1) applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) to an application for a licence relating to the manufacture of explosives by means of on-site mixing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) to an application for a licence relating to the manufacture of ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^c-e8092df77831bdaf57c8e9346a06a487]...
- (d) to an application for a licence relating to the manufacture or storage of explosives by a person who wishes to carry on such manufacture or storage within a part of a site where another person already holds a licence for the manufacture or storage of explosives; and either — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the application relates to manufacturing or storage activities which would be permitted at that part of the site under the existing licence; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] in the opinion of the Executive or a local authority whose assent would otherwise be required, no significant new health and safety issues are raised by the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (e)[^c-5d00e3b5788431fe47737b5708aa66e6] to an application for a licence which is to follow, without a gap in time, a previous licence granted to follow, without a gap in time, a deemed licence for the purposes of regulation 27(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (f) to an application for a licence relating to the manufacture of explosives by a police force maintained pursuant to section 2 of the Police Act 1996 for their operational purposes or the training of members of that police force in relation to those purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4A)[^c-49f0d51b84ea961a1c57f3115346d38c] In paragraph (4)(d)(ii) the reference to the Executive is to be read, in relation to an application relating to manufacture or storage of explosives on, or within any part of, a site which is an ONR regulated site, as a reference to the ONR. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) Every licence shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] include conditions which[^c-07a465b4edde62b3931e9d2659cb77f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] specify — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the site and, within it, the places where the explosives may be manufactured or stored; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the hazard type and maximum amount of explosive which may be manufactured, stored or otherwise present, as the case may be, at any one time at or in any place so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(6) In addition to the matters specified in paragraph (5), a licence which is granted by the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-5c9f9c91c2df6d5fdd4b3a5111bc769b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] in cases where the assent of the local authority was required pursuant to paragraph (3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or in cases where that assent was not required by virtue of paragraph (4)(c), (e) or (f)[^c-fddbcfd76d4b6a10a90a898b64e3321d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] —

- (a) shall be granted subject to such conditions as the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or, as the case may be, the ONR[^c-a19cb90044853ec46a771042130f8b6d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] considers appropriate which relate to separation distances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) may be granted subject to such conditions as the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or, as the case may be, the ONR[^c-0af8b30d17c5f9f7aca2efd7418c6de1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] considers appropriate which relate to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) the construction, siting or orientation of any building (including any protective works around the building) where the activity will be carried on; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] the activities which may be undertaken in specified buildings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

  and in this sub-paragraph — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- “activity” means the manufacture or storage of explosives and it includes any handling, on-site transport, testing and disposal of explosives and “activities” shall be construed accordingly; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- “construction” means the materials used in, and the design of, a building; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (a) may, where both the manufacture and storage of explosives at the same site was applied for, cover both that manufacture and storage for the purposes of, respectively, regulations 9 and 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(7) In addition to the matters specified in paragraphs (5) and (6), where a licensing authority grants a licence which relates to the storage of pyrotechnic articles at any site where those articles are to be offered for sale, the licensing authority may attach such conditions to the licence as it considers appropriate which relate to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the storage and display of those articles in areas where they can be purchased; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the prevention of risk of fire arising in respect of those articles; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the safe use of fire escapes in that area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(8) A licence granted pursuant to this regulation shall be in a form approved for the time being for the purposes of this regulation by the Executive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(9)[^c-7de2dfbae9d07bd970006d813afe1327] A renewal of a licence may be granted — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where the licensing authority is a local authority, for such period not exceeding two years as the licensing authority determines; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where the licensing authority is a chief officer of police *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , the Executive or the ONR[^c-14c00d18560343750e6cd5712b63421c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , for such period not exceeding five years as that licensing authority determines, save that, where the applicant for the licence has been granted an explosives certificate, the licence may be granted for any period not exceeding the due expiry date of that explosives certificate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) for any period or without a time limit in a case — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) where paragraph (3) applied to the application for the original licence; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] where paragraph (3) did not apply to the application for the original licence by virtue of paragraph (4)(e) or (f); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] where the application for the original licence related only to the manufacture of ammonium nitrate blasting intermediate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(10) In this regulation, “on-site mixing” means the mixing at any place of non-explosive substances or preparations to form an explosive for immediate use at that place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
