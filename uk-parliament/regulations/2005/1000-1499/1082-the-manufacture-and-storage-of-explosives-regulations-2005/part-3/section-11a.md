# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 11A

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11A. Sites which cease to be an ONR regulated site – effect on registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b7bde182ba3cd6c8867d523408090fc9][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where this regulation applies a registration granted by, or treated as granted by, the ONR is to be treated on and after the relevant date as a registration granted by the relevant licensing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the site in respect of which the registration was granted ceases to be an ONR regulated site; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the registration mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) remained in force immediately before the date on which the site ceased to be an ONR regulated site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) “**relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**” means, in relation to a site, the date on which the site ceased to be an ONR regulated site; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) “**relevant licensing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**” means the licensing authority for that site determined in accordance with Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
