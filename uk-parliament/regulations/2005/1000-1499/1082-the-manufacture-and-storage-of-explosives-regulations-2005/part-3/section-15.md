# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 15

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Refusals of licences, registration and draft licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-604d2b94b617d86d14f1204b9b478e53][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 18, the licensing authority shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) refuse an application for a licence or registration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) where regulation 14(1) applies, refuse to issue the draft licence referred to in regulation 14(1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

where paragraph (2) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) This paragraph applies when the licensing authority is of the opinion that — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the proposed site or, within it, any place where the manufacture or storage of explosives is proposed to take place is unsuitable for that manufacture or storage; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) the applicant is not a fit person — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) to store explosives, in the case of an application for registration or a licence to store explosives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] to manufacture explosives, in the case of an application for a licence to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) A refusal by the licensing authority, pursuant to paragraph (1), to issue the draft licence referred to in regulation 14(1) shall be treated for the purposes of these Regulations as a refusal of an application for a licence and the provisions of regulation 18 shall apply to a refusal to issue a draft licence as if the references in that regulation to “refuse an application for a licence” included refusing to issue a draft licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
