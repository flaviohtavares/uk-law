# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 18

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Further provisions concerning refusals, variations and revocations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0de24ff16c1bde9b3c10e6c923bf8dd1][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a licensing authority proposes to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a)[^key-1f49bf64525199dcc20ae92f61d7c9cb] refuse an application for — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) a licence or registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a renewal of a licence or registration; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a transfer of a licence or registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) vary a licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registration[^key-3a1a7a081f0430154201443a8dc68fcc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] without the agreement of the licensee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registered person[^key-9044d4a96b86111f2ffc63b837549452] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) revoke a licence or registration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

it shall, before taking any such action, notify the applicant, licensee or registered person, as the case may be, of its proposed course of action and afford him the opportunity of making representations to the licensing authority about it, within a period of 28 days from the date of the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) Representations made for the purpose of paragraph (1) may be made in writing, or both in writing and orally. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Where the licensing authority decides to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a)[^key-d614c5e1855a781db8f233c33a55cf8a] refuse an application for — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (i) a licence or registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a renewal of a licence or registration; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a transfer of a licence or registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) vary a licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registration[^key-8c9ed5dd66e96a450198fc1cf45ec52c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] without the agreement of the licensee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registered person[^key-38c23f0597d8aea033f7b709cd1faa77] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) revoke a licence or registration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

it shall provide in writing to the applicant, licensee or registered person, as the case may be, the reasons for its decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) Where the licensing authority varies a licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registration[^key-d2e473958667810e29809f44d02e9911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] without the agreement of the licensee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or registered person[^key-a37e4edb06e5cb14962888d977ade3c8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or revokes a licence or registration, that variation or revocation shall take effect from a date to be determined by the licensing authority which shall be a date after the 28 day period referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
