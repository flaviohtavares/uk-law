# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 23

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Defences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67dfd9ef6ca9b88864e7ceb0d8b6990a][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In proceedings against a person for a contravention of regulation 9(1) which involves using a building or part of a building licensed for the manufacture of explosives, for another manufacturing process not specified in the licence, it shall be a defence for that person to prove that — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) that use was temporary; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) that other process of manufacture involved explosive of the same, or a lower, hazard type than the explosives which the conditions of the licence permit in, as the case may be, that building or part of a building; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) the maximum quantity of explosives in that building or part of a building at any one time permitted under the conditions of the licence was not exceeded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (d) he informed the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] appropriate authority[^key-017ab897c696ef16fffc47892bc03a89] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] as soon as was reasonably practicable after the start of that use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(1A)[^key-f213a5b16d51298f38a2ae885a477c6d] In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**appropriate authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) in the case of a licence granted by the ONR, the ONR; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) otherwise, the Executive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) In proceedings against a person for a contravention of regulation 10(1), it shall be a defence for that person to prove that the storage of explosives without a licence or in breach of a condition of a licence was caused by an emergency being an emergency which that person took all reasonable precautions and exercised all due diligence to avoid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) In proceedings against a person for a contravention of regulation 10(1) where it is alleged against that person that the storage concerned was for a period longer than a period (“the permitted period”) referred to in regulation 10(2)(c), (d), (e) or (f)(i) or (ii), it shall be for that person to prove that the storage concerned was for no longer than the permitted period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
