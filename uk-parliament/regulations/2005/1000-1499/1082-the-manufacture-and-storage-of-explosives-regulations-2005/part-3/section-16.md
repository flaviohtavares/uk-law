# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 16

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Variation of licences (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fbac941eb4ed5164f41b7e063e70ba0a][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2014-10-01).**

(1) The licensing authority which grants a licence may vary it — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where there has been a change in circumstances such that the separation distances can no longer be maintained and a consequent reduction in the maximum amount of explosive that may be stored is required; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) (where the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-7e55e7941a585bd9f5ef64d8e249cd6b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] is the licensing authority in cases where the assent of the local authority was required pursuant to regulation 13(3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] , or in cases where that assent was not required by virtue of regulation 13(4)(e) or (f),[^c-15259098e277bf3ec1ab1c6694dea5c3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] before the grant of the licence) where there has been a material change in circumstances so that a variation is necessary to ensure safety; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) in relation to any of the matters it relates to, by agreement with the licensee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) A licence may be varied on the grounds referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) without the agreement of the licensee, subject to regulation 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2A)[^c-4f54e2552e9be796c66bc760d0de133e] The licensing authority which grants a registration may vary it — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) where there has been a change of circumstances such that the separation distances can no longer be maintained and a consequent reduction in the amount of explosives that may be stored is required so as to reduce that amount to a quantity specified by the licensing authority which is below the maximum amount referred to in regulation 11(1) for the kind or kinds of explosives concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) so as to change the period for which the registration is in force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) in relation to any of the matters it relates to, by agreement with the person registered. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2B) A registration may be varied on the grounds referred to in paragraph (2A)(a) and (b) without the agreement of the person who is registered, subject to regulation 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Where the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the ONR[^c-7b11a81e6b1ec7aeff3c71acf22863cb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] is the licensing authority in cases where the assent of the local authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) was required under regulation 13(3) before the grant of the licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) would have been so required but for the operation of regulation 27(4) or (13), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

the provisions of regulation 14 shall apply in respect of a proposed variation referred to in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(4) A proposed variation for the purposes of paragraph (3) is one which — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) relates to changes in the permitted quantities or types of explosive as a result of which the licensee could be required to maintain a separation distance greater than the separation distance required before the variation and, in the opinion of the Executive or the local authority concerned, significant new health and safety issues are raised by that proposed variation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) would increase the period of the licence by more than twelve months; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) would remove the period of the licence so that it would be unlimited as to time, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

and the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or (as the case may be) the ONR[^c-7b67531490fd19c526c9dd72e4987666] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shall refuse to grant a varied licence unless the local authority, or each local authority in the case referred to in regulation 13(3), has so assented. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(5) In this regulation any reference to varying a licence includes varying its conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
