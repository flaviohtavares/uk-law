# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 21

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Death, bankruptcy or incapacity (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7b65851ce68f38b95958b79243dc828d][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a licensee or registered person dies or becomes incapacitated, a person manufacturing or storing explosives in accordance with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] conditions[^c-2298dd177eadf9501fade1e2564744eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] of the first-named person’s licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or the terms of his registration[^c-d9a6e53ed1d24d573e56afeacbeb12de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] shall be treated as being licensed or registered in accordance with the first-named person’s licence or registration until either — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the expiration of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] a period of 60 days starting with the date of[^c-9230b7909c599502d8e644dc09d9fcdb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] such death or incapacity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] [^M_F_68a01085-42eb-41ca-fcf9-4e8503df43fc]...
- (b) the grant or refusal of a new licence or registration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766] or[^c-ad81ac1adcbe71dd320835abf28c4c62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c)[^c-729cc99d365a3b98e84192d15deeb9d0] the transfer of, or a refusal to transfer, a licence or registration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

whichever is the earlier. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) If a licensee or registered person becomes bankrupt or, in the case of a company, goes into liquidation or receivership or has a receiving order made against it, any receiver, trustee in bankruptcy or liquidator shall be treated as being the licensee or registered person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
