# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 20

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Transfer of licences and registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-eba3dd27f942b2dacf43fddca189b5b5][^key-908913bc8db5522bd107a478e6b8b472][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A licence or registration may be transferred in writing by the licensing authority which issued the licence or registration, to any other person who wishes to manufacture or store explosives in place of the licensee or the person who is registered and who applies to the licensing authority for the transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) A licensing authority shall grant an application for a transfer of a licence or registration unless it is of the opinion that the applicant is not a fit person — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) to store explosives, in the case of an application to transfer a registration or a licence to store explosives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) to manufacture explosives, in the case of an application to transfer a licence to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(3) Where a licensing authority is of an opinion referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) of paragraph (2), it shall, subject to regulation 18, refuse the application to transfer the licence or registration, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
