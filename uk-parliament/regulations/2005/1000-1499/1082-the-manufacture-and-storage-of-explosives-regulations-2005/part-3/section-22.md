# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 22

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Registers and retention of documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d1a99a2af5542b0753ed3a2a907ad7d8][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766][^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The licensing authority shall — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) maintain a register in accordance with Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) keep a copy of any licence granted or certificate of registration issued by it (together with a copy of the application for the licence or registration) for as long as the licence or registration remains valid; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) (except where the Executive is the licensing authority), send to the Executive on request a copy of any part of the register or other document specified in this paragraph within such time as the Executive may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation and Schedule 4, in the case to which regulation 3(5) applies disapplying regulations 5 and 9 to 21, any reference to — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]

- (a) the licensing authority or licensee shall be construed as a reference to the Secretary of State for Defence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (b) any licence granted shall be construed as a reference to the scheme referred to in regulation 3(5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
- (c) separation distances shall be construed as a reference to the separation distances prescribed in the scheme approved by the Secretary of State for Defence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c8f710f05d44de617588a65a6843d766]
