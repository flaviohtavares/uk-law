# The Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005, section 12

> Text as at 2014-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**12.** The Regulations also amend the Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/676](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/676)), so as to include fee provisions in those Regulations for the new licensing and registration provision made by these Regulations. The amendments also add fees in relation to the issue of explosives certificates under the Control of Explosives Regulations 1991 (regulation 28(1) and Schedule 5, paragraph 45).
