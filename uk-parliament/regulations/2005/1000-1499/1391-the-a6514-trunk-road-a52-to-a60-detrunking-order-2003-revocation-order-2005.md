# The A6514 Trunk Road (A52 to A60) (Detrunking) Order 2003 (Revocation) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1391

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A6514 Trunk Road (A52 to A60) (Detrunking) Order 2003 (Revocation) Order 2005

Made

12th May 2005

Coming into force

12th July 2005

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A6514 Trunk Road (A52 to A60) (Detrunking) Order 2003 (Revocation) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 12th July 2005.

**2.** The A6514 Trunk Road (A52 to A60) (Detrunking) Order 2003 is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Pearman

A Divisional Director Highways Agency

12th May 2005
