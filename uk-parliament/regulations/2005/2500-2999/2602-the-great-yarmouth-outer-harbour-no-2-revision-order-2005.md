# The Great Yarmouth Outer Harbour (No. 2) Revision Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2602

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Great Yarmouth Outer Harbour (No. 2) Revision Order 2005

Made

14th September 2005

Coming into force

6th October 2005

Whereas the Great Yarmouth Port Authority has applied for a Harbour Revision Order under section 14 of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001];

And whereas the Secretary of State is satisfied as mentioned in subsection 2(b) of the said section 14;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State (being the appropriate Minister under subsection (7) of the said section 14[^f00002]), in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and now vested in him[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Great Yarmouth Outer Harbour (No. 2) Revision Order 2005 and shall come into force on 6th October 2005.

(2) This Order shall be included amongst the Orders which may be cited together as the Great Yarmouth Port Authority Acts and Orders 1866 to 2005.

## 2. Repeal of enactment

Section 35 (Saving for revenues of Haven) of the Great Yarmouth Outer Harbour Act 1986[^f00004] is hereby repealed.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Phil Carey

Head of Ports Division

Department for Transport

14th September 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the repeal of section 35 of the Great Yarmouth Outer Harbour Act 1986. This section prohibits the use of ship passenger and goods dues from the existing harbour for the purposes of the construction and operation of the Outer Harbour.

The applicants for the Order are the Great Yarmouth Port Authority.
