# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Cross-examination on a witness statement

Where a witness is called to give evidence at final hearing, he may be cross-examined on his witness statement whether or not the statement or any part of it was referred to during the witness’s evidence in chief.
