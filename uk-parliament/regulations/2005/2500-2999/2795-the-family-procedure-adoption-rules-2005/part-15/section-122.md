# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Making an admission

(1) A party may admit the truth of the whole or any part of another party’s case by giving notice in writing.

(2) The court may allow a party to amend or withdraw an admission.
