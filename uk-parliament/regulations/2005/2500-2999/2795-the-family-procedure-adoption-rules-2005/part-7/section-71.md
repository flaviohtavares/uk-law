# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. The duties of the reporting officer

The reporting officer must witness the signature by a parent or guardian on the document in which consent is given to—

- (a) the placing of the child for adoption;
- (b) the making of an adoption order; or
- (c) the making of a section 84 order.
