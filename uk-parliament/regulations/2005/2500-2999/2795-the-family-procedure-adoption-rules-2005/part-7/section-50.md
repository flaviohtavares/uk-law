# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Requirement for litigation friend in proceedings

(1) Subject to rule 51, a non-subject child must have a litigation friend to conduct proceedings on his behalf.

(2) A patient must have a litigation friend to conduct proceedings on his behalf.
