# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Rules not to limit other powers of the court to order disclosure

(1) Rule 79 does not limit any other power which the court may have to order—

- (a) disclosure before proceedings have started; and
- (b) disclosure against a person who is not a party to proceedings.

(2) This rule does not apply to proceedings in a magistrates' court.
