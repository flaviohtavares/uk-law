# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Custody of documents

All documents relating to proceedings under the Act must, while they are in the custody of the court, be kept in a place of special security.
