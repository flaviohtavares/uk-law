# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 158

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 158. General requirement for expert evidence to be given in a written report

Expert evidence is to be given in a written report unless the court directs otherwise.
