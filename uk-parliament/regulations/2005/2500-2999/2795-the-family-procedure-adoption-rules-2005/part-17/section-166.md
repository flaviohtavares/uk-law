# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 166

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 166. Consequence of failure to disclose expert’s report

A party who fails to disclose an expert’s report may not use the report at the final hearing or call the expert to give evidence orally unless the court gives permission.
