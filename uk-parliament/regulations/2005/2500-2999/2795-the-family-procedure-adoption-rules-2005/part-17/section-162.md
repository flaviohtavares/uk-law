# The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005, section 162

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 162. Power of court to direct a party to provide information

(1) Where a party has access to information which is not reasonably available to the other party, the court may direct the party who has access to the information to prepare and file a document recording the information.

(2) A court officer will send a copy of that document to the other party.
