# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2005, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Publication of notices

The Secretary of State shall publicise improvement notices and seizure notices in such manner as she shall see fit.
