# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2005, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Products to which these Regulations do not apply

(1) These Regulations do not apply to a veterinary medicinal product based on radio-active isotopes.

(2) They do not apply to a veterinary medicinal product that is the subject of a licence granted under the Animals (Scientific Procedure) Act 1986[^f00006], except that, if the animals used under that licence are to be put into the human food chain, the veterinary medicinal product must be administered in accordance with an animal test certificate granted under regulation 8(3).
