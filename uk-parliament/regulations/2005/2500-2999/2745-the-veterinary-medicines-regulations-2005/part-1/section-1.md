# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2005, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2005 and come into force—

- (a) except for regulation 14 and Schedule 5, on 30th October 2005;
- (b) in the case of regulation 14 and Schedule 5, on 1st January 2006.
