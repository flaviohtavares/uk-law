# The Cambridgeshire Guided Busway Order 2005, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Temporary closure of part of Great Ouse

(1) In this article “the waterway” means so much of the river Great Ouse as lies within the limits of deviation.

(2) The undertaker, during and for the purposes of the construction of Work No. 1, may temporarily close the waterway and divert vessels from it.

(3) Any person who suffers loss by the suspension of any right under this article shall be entitled to be paid compensation by the undertaker, to be determined in the case of dispute by the tribunal.
