# The Cambridgeshire Guided Busway Order 2005, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Substitute road services

(1) The undertaker may provide or secure the provision by other persons of services for the carriage of passengers by road (“substitute services”) where the authorised guided busway has been temporarily interrupted, curtailed or discontinued.

(2) The route, frequency and stopping places of any substitute service need not correspond with the route of the interrupted, curtailed or discontinued service.

(3) Section 6 of the Transport Act 1985 shall not apply to any substitute services.
