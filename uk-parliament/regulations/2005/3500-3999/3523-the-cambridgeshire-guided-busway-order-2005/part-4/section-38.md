# The Cambridgeshire Guided Busway Order 2005, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. As to quality partnership schemes

Section 114(6)(b) of the Transport Act 2000 (quality partnership schemes not to include requirements as to frequency or timing of services) shall not apply to any quality partnership scheme made in relation to services operating on any authorised guided busway.
