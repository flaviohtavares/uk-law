# The Cambridgeshire Guided Busway Order 2005, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Registration of busway services

For the purposes of the application of Part I of the Transport Act 1985 but subject to article 37, the authorised guided busway shall be a road within the meaning of section 137(1) of that Act.
