# The Terrorism Act 2000 (Continuance of Part VII) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 350

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Terrorism Act 2000 (Continuance of Part VII) Order 2005

Made

15th February 2005

Coming into force

19th February 2005

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament under section 123(4)(f) of the Terrorism Act 2000[^f00001], and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 112(2)(a) and (b) of the Terrorism Act 2000, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Terrorism Act 2000 (Continuance of Part VII) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 19th February 2005.

**2.** (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the provisions of Part VII of the Terrorism Act 2000 which are in force on 18<sup>th</sup> February 2005[^f00002] shall continue in force for the period beginning 19<sup>th</sup> February 2005 and ending with 18<sup>th</sup> February 2006.

(2) The following provisions of the Terrorism Act 2000 shall cease to have effect:—

- (a) Section 67(3) and (4);
- (b) Section 70; and
- (c) Section 71.

Ian Pearson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Northern Ireland Office

15th February 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues in force for a further period of twelve months the provisions of Part VII of the Terrorism Act 2000 (which extend to Northern Ireland only) with the exception of the following provisions:

- (a) section 100, which has not been brought into force;
- (b) sections 67(3) and (4), 70 and 71, which by virtue of this Order shall cease to have effect on 19<sup>th</sup> February 2005;
- (c) section 76[^f00003];
- (d) section 97(1) and (2)[^f00004];
- (e) section 97(3)[^f00005];
- (f) paragraph 36 of Schedule 4[^f00006];
- (g) paragraph 19 to 21 of Schedule 5[^f00007].

The provisions continued in force by this Order will continue in force up to and including 18<sup>th</sup> February 2006.
