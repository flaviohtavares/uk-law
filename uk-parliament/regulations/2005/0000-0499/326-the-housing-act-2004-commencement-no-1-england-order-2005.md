# The Housing Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 326 (C.11)

HOUSING, ENGLAND

The Housing Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2005

Made

9th February 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 270(4) and (5) of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Housing Act 2004 (Commencement No.1) (England) Order 2005.

(2) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Housing Act 2004 shall come into force on 17th February 2005—

- (a) section 220, insofar as it is not already in force [^f00002]; and
- (b) sections 221 and 227.

Keith Hill

Minister of State Office of the Deputy Prime Minister

9th February 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the following provisions of the Housing Act 2004 into force in England on 17th February 2005—

- section 220 (which extends the powers of the Housing Corporation to give grants to persons other than registered social landlords);
- section 221 (which is related to section 220 and which extends the right to acquire in relation to a dwelling provided by means of a grant to a person other than a registered social landlord);
- section 227 (which removes the duty on local housing authorities to send annual reports to tenants).

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [2004 c. 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34). The powers conferred by section 270(4) and (5) are exercisable by the National Assembly for Wales, where a provision is to come into force in relation only to Wales.

[^f00002]: Footnote: Section 220 so far as it confers a power to make orders is already in force by virtue of section 270(2)(b) of the Housing Act 2004.
