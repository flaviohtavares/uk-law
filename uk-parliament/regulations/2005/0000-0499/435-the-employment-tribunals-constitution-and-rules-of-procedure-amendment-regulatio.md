# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 435

EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS

The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made

22nd February 2005

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 2005

Coming into force

5th April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 4(6) and (6A), 7(1), (3) and (5), 9(1), 10(7) and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001], and paragraph 36 of Schedule 8 to the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002], and paragraph 37 of Schedule 6 to the Scotland Act 1998[^f00003], and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00004], makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and they shall come into force on 5th April 2005.

## 2. Amendment

In Rules 1(3) and 4(2) of Schedule 1 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004[^f00005] for “6 April 2005” there shall be substituted “1st October 2005”.

Gerry Sutcliffe

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Postal services and Consumers,

Department of Trade and Industry

22nd February 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations come into force on 5 April 2005 and they amend the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004 (S.I. [2004/1861](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1861), amended by S.I. [2004/2351](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2351)) (“the main Regulations”). Their purpose is to change the date, from 6 April 2005 to 1 October 2005, on or after which all claims and responses to Employment Tribunals, other than in specified proceedings, must be presented on a form prescribed in accordance with Regulation 14 of the main Regulations.

No regulatory impact assessment has been undertaken because these Regulations will have no impact on the costs of business.
