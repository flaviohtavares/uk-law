# The Family Proceedings (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2004/3375 (L.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3375) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2005 No. 412 (L. 5)

FAMILY PROCEEDINGS, ENGLAND AND WALES

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Family Proceedings (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005

Made

27th February 2005

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2005

Coming into force

3rd March 2005

We, the authority having power under section 40(1) of the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984[^f00001] to make rules of court for the purposes of family proceedings in the High Court and county courts, in the exercise of the powers conferred by section 40, make the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Family Proceedings (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005 and shall come into force on 3rd March 2005.

**2.** For Forms C1, C2 and C7 in Appendix 1 to the Family Proceedings Rules 1991[^f00002] substitute the forms set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

Falconer of Thoroton, *C*

Elizabeth Butler-Sloss, *P*

Angela Finnerty
