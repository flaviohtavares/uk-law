# The Education (Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (Primary Schools) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 2

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (Primary Schools) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made

6th January 2005

Laid before Parliament

14th January 2005

Coming into force

28th February 2005

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 89B (1), (4), (5) and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (Primary Schools) ( England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 28th February 2005.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

The Education (Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (Primary Schools) (England) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are further amended in accordance with these Regulations.

**3.** In regulation 1 after paragraph (2 ) insert—

> (3) These Regulations do not apply to the Council of the Isles of Scilly

Stephen Twigg

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

6th January 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (Primary Schools) (England) Regulations 2002 (“the principal regulations”).

By virtue of regulation 3, which inserts paragraph (3) into Regulation 1 of the principal regulations, the Council of the Isles of Scilly is not under a duty to formulate a qualifying scheme which makes provision for co-ordinating the admission of pupils to primary schools in their area.
