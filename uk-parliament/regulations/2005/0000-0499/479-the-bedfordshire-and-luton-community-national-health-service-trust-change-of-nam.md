# The Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 479

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005

Made

3rd March 2005

Coming into force

13th March 2005

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (c) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005 and shall come into force on 13<sup>th</sup> March 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- (a) “the Establishment Order” means the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00003]; and
- (b) “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Establishment Order.

## 2. Change of name of trust and transitional provision

(1) The trust shall be called the Bedfordshire and Luton Mental Health and Social Care Partnership National Health Service Trust instead of the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust, and accordingly in article 1(2) of the Establishment Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Establishment Order, for the words “Bedfordshire and Luton Community” there shall be substituted the words “Bedfordshire and Luton Mental Health and Social Care Partnership”.

(2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before or on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## 3. Amendment of nature and functions of the trust

(1) Article 3(2) of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) shall be replaced by the following—

> (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

.

(2) The Schedule to the Establishment Order shall be deleted.

## 4. Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “5 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “6 non-executive directors”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

3rd March 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999, which established the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the Bedfordshire and Luton Community National Health Service Trust to the Bedfordshire and Luton Mental Health and Social Care Partnership National Health Service Trust, and makes consequential amendments to the Establishment Order. Article 3 of the Order removes the references to the establishments from which the trust carries out its functions. Article 4 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 5 to 6.
