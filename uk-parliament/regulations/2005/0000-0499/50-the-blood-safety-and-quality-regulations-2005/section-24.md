# The Blood Safety and Quality Regulations 2005, section 24

> Text as at 2025-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Transitional provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), these Regulations, other than regulations 13 and 16, shall not apply before 8th November 2005 in relation to—

- (a) any blood establishment licensed under section 8 of the Medicines Act 1968 [^c12124341];
- (b) any hospital blood bank.

(2) From the date these Regulations come into force, a blood establishment licensed under section 8 of the Medicines Act 1968 may apply for, and the Secretary of State may grant, an authorisation under regulation 4 to have effect as from 8th November 2005.
