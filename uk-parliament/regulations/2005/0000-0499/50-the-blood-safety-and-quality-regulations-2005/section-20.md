# The Blood Safety and Quality Regulations 2005, section 20

> Text as at 2025-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Defence of due diligence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In any proceedings for an offence under any of the preceding provisions of these Regulations, it shall be a defence for the person charged to prove that he took all reasonable precautions and exercised all due diligence to avoid commission of the offence.

(2) Where evidence is adduced which is sufficient to raise an issue with respect to that defence, the court or jury shall assume that the defence is satisfied unless the prosecution proves beyond all reasonable doubt that it is not.
