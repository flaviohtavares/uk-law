# The Blood Safety and Quality Regulations 2005, section 23E

> Text as at 2025-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23E. Scrutiny of regulations made by the Scottish Ministers[^c-26e157358a5548961646637a765aea29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as specified in paragraph (2), regulations made by the Scottish Ministers under regulation 23A are subject to the negative procedure (see section 28 of the Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (“**the 2010 Act**”) (instruments subject to the negative procedure)).

(2) Regulations made by the Scottish Ministers under regulation 23A(1)(h) are subject to the affirmative procedure (see section 29 of the 2010 Act (instruments subject to the affirmative procedure)).
