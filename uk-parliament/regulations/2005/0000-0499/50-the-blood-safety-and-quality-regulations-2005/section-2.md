# The Blood Safety and Quality Regulations 2005, section 2

> Text as at 2025-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Designation of the competent authority for Northern Ireland and scope of the Regulations[^c-455e769972721d56b6e1217c32a7ff50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-866f967f960f116f76a4be0adbc65bb6]  The Secretary of State is designated the competent authority in relation to Northern Ireland for the purposes of the Directive.

(2) Subject to the following paragraphs, the requirements of these Regulations apply to the collection and testing of blood and blood components, whatever their intended purpose, and to their processing, storage, and distribution when they are intended to be used for transfusion.

(3)[^c-b5b6bcd0cd900f3e6976cef05c4f0e1d] These Regulations apply without prejudice to—

- (a) the Medical Devices Regulations 2002,
- (b) [Regulation (EU) 2017/745](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0745) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2017 on medical devices, amending [Directive 2001/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0083), Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) and Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) and repealing Council Directives [90/385/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0385) and [93/42/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042), and
- (c) [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2017 on *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and repealing [Directive 98/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0079) and Commission [Decision 2010/227/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2010/0227).

(4) These Regulations do not apply to blood stem cells.
