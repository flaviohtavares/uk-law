# The Blood Safety and Quality Regulations 2005, section 1B

> Text as at 2025-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1B. References to the requirements set out in the Annex to Commission Directive [2005/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/62)[^c-2f98da3a04c4bd4e1b8efb3bc204bbdc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in regulations 7, 9 and 13, as they apply in relation to Great Britain, to the requirements set out in the Annex to Commission Directive [2005/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/62) are to be read as a reference to the requirements which that Annex would require to be imposed if that Annex formed part of domestic law.
