# The Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 30th December 2005.

(2) These Regulations apply to England only.
