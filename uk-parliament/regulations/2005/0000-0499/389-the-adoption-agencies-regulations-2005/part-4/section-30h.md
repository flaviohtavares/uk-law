# The Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005, section 30H

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30H. Prospective adopter matching plan[^key-a6790a297221401376e94bf718b934f2]

*Extent: E+W*

Except in a section 83 case, where an adoption agency has approved a prospective adopter as suitable to adopt a child in accordance with regulation 30B, the agency must prepare a written plan in consultation with the prospective adopter (“the prospective adopter matching plan”) which includes—

- (a) information about the duties of the adoption agency under Parts 5 and 6 of these Regulations;
- (b) information about the role of the prospective adopter in identifying a child for whom they would be an appropriate adopter;
- (c) information about the process for making a representation (including a complaint) under the 1989 Regulations; and
- (d) any other matters that the agency consider relevant.
