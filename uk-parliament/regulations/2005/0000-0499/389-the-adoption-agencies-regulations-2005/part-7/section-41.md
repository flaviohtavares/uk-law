# The Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005, section 41

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Confidentiality of case records

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to regulation 42, the contents of the child's case record and the prospective adopter's case record shall be treated by the adoption agency as confidential.
