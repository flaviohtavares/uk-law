# The Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005, section 44

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44. Application of regulations 40 to 42

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Part applies to the information which an adoption agency must keep in relation to an adopted person by virtue of regulations made under section 56 of the Act.
