# The Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 456

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2005

Made

2nd March 2005

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1), 96(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to proposed changes in the functions of the Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust[^f00002] and having consulted that Trust and the South of Tyne and Wearside Mental Health National Health Service Trust[^f00003] in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust;
- “the new Trust” means the South of Tyne and Wearside Mental Health National Health Service Trust; and
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 13<sup>th</sup> January 2005 and on behalf of the new Trust on 11<sup>th</sup> January 2005.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

(2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the trustees of the trust property or, as the case may be, the old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the trustees of the new Trust or, as the case may be, the new Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Melanie Johnson

Minister of State,

Department of Health

2nd March 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the South of Tyne and Wearside Mental Health National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the Sunderland Teaching Primary Care Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
