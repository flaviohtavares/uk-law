# The Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2004/3376 (L.26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3376) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2005 No. 413 (L.6)

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005

Made

27th February 2005

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2005

Coming into force

3rd March 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 144 of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980[^f00001], and after consultation with the rule committee appointed under that section, makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005 and shall come into force on 3rd March 2005.

**2.** For Forms C1, C2 and C7 in Schedule 1 to the Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) Rules 1991[^f00002] substitute the forms set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

Falconer of Thoroton, *C.*

Dated 27th February 2005
