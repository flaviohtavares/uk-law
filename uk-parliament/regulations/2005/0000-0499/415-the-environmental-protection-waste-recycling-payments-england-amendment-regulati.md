# The Environmental Protection (Waste Recycling Payments) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 415

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND

The Environmental Protection (Waste Recycling Payments) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made

1st March 2005

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 52(8) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Protection (Waste Recycling Payments) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

## 2. Amendment of regulations

For the Schedule to the Environmental Protection (Waste Recycling Payments) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00002] substitute–

> **SCHEDULE**
>
> Regulation 3(6)
>
> | Type of waste disposal authority | Net saving per tonne of waste |
> | --- | --- |
> | A London waste disposal authority for an area which includes an inner London borough.<br>The council of an inner London borough.<br>The Common Council of the City of London. | £64.22 |
> | A London waste disposal authority which comprises outer London boroughs.<br>The council of an outer London borough. | £57.20 |
> | The Greater Manchester Waste Disposal Authority.<br>The Merseyside Waste Disposal Authority.<br>The council of a metropolitan district. | £48.82 |
> | Any other waste disposal authority. | £40.41 where the authority incurs any transport costs in disposing of similar wastes, and £30.61 in other cases. |

.

Elliot Morley

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

1st March 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations substitute a new Schedule for that in the Environmental Protection (Waste Recycling Payments) (England) Regulations 2004, which make provision for determining the net savings of expenditure payable by waste disposal authorities to waste collection authorities and other persons who collect and retain waste for recycling (regulation 2).

The new Schedule provides figures for determining the net saving of expenditure where this cannot otherwise be determined because sufficient accurate information is not available or could only be obtained at a disproportionate cost. These figures are increased to take account of rises in the Retail Price Index (by 3.5%) and increases in landfill tax (to £18 a tonne).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of the public sector, business, charities or voluntary bodies.
