The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10800951] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10800961] in relation to recognition of higher-education diplomas, formal qualifications, or experience in the occupation, required for the pursuit of professions or occupations, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 9th February 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) In these Regulations the following words and phrases have the following meanings (and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- “**adaptation period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a period of practice of a regulated profession in the United Kingdom under the supervision of a qualified member of that profession, required by a designated authority pursuant to regulation 6(1)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**another relevant State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a relevant State other than the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**appeal body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means, in respect of any regulated profession, the body, court or person specified in relation to that profession in Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**aptitude test *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a test required by a designated authority pursuant to regulation 6(1)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**competent authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means, in relation to any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) document, certificate, diploma or qualification, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) period of professional experience, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

  referred to in these Regulations, the authority, body or person in a relevant State authorised under the laws, regulations or administrative provisions of that State, to issue, award or recognise such document, certificate, diploma or qualification, or to certify any such period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**corresponding profession *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a profession, the pursuit of which in another relevant State includes a substantial number of the professional activities comprised in the pursuit of the profession in the United Kingdom which is the subject of the migrant's application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**designated authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a body or authority designated in relation to a regulated profession by regulation 4 and any qualifying body referred to in regulations 11(2) and 12(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**diploma *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means any diploma, certificate or other evidence of formal qualifications awarded by a competent authority in a relevant State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) which shows that the holder: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (i) has successfully completed a post-secondary course of at least three years' duration (or of an equivalent duration part-time) at a university or an establishment of higher education or an establishment of equivalent level; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (ii) has successfully completed any additional professional training required; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (iii) has the qualifications required for the practice of a regulated profession in that State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

    provided that either: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

    - (A) the education and training attested were received mainly within the relevant States; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (B) the holder has had at least three years' professional experience certified by a competent authority in that State (being a State which recognised a diploma, certificate or other evidence of formal qualifications obtained in a non relevant State); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) which was awarded on the successful completion of education and training received within the relevant States, and which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (i) has been recognised by a competent authority in that State as equivalent in level to a diploma to which sub-paragraph (a) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (ii) confers the same rights in respect of the practice of a regulated profession in that State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**the Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [89/48/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/48)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10800971] on a general system for the recognition of higher-education diplomas awarded on completion of professional education and training of at least three years' duration as amended by Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [2001/19/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2001/19)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10800981];
- “**migrant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a national of a relevant State applying under these Regulations to a designated authority for authorisation to practise; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**native applicant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a national of a relevant State applying to a designated authority for authorisation to practise, the qualifications of whom were obtained wholly within the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**practise *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” in relation to any regulated profession, includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) the taking up or pursuit of the profession in a relevant State, whether in a self-employed capacity or as an employed person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) the right to use, in the course of such pursuit, a professional title or designatory letters, or the enjoyment of any special status corresponding to a diploma, granted by a designated authority for that profession; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**professional association *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means an association or organisation, recognised in special form by a relevant State, the purpose of which is, in particular, to promote and maintain a high standard in the professional field with which it is concerned and which, to that end— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) prescribes and enforces respect within it membership for, rules of professional conduct; and awards a diploma to its members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) confers on its members the right to use one or more professional titles or designatory letters or to benefit from a status corresponding to that diploma; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**professional experience *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means the lawful pursuit in a relevant State of a profession corresponding to the regulated profession which is the subject of the migrant's application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**regulated education and training *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means education or training which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) is directly geared to the practice of a defined profession in a relevant State and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) comprises a post-secondary course of at least three years' duration, or an equivalent duration part-time, at a university or higher education establishment or in another establishment of equivalent level, and any additional professional training, professional traineeship or professional practice required, the structure and level of which is determined by the laws, regulations or administrative provisions of that relevant State or monitored or approved by the competent authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**regulated profession *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) in the United Kingdom— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (i) a profession in respect of which a designated authority is specified in Schedule 1 and the profession of company auditor as defined in section 24(2) of the Companies Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10800991] and Article 27(2) of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] [^c10801001], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
    - (ii) at any time when a designated authority is not specified, if and in so far as any professional activity or range of activities which constitutes the profession is a regulated professional activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) in another relevant State if and in so far as any professional activity or range of such activities which constitutes the profession is regulated as a professional activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**regulated professional activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means that a professional activity is regulated in a relevant State if and in so far as— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (a) the practice, or any mode of practice, of that activity in that relevant State is subject, directly or indirectly, by virtue of laws, regulations or administrative provisions to the possession of a diploma; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (b) it is practiced under a professional title, or designatory letters the use of which is reserved to holders of a diploma governed by laws, regulations or administrative provisions; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (c) it relates to health and the remuneration or reimbursement received for the practice of the activity in the relevant state in question is, by virtue of national arrangements for the administration of social security, subject to the possession of a diploma; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (d) it is practiced by members of a professional association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- “**relevant State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means a member State, Iceland, Norway, Liechtenstein or Switzerland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) A reference in these Regulations to “the United Kingdom” shall, as the context requires, include a reference to any of the following: England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(3) Any reference in these Regulations to a numbered regulation or Schedule is a reference to the regulation or Schedule so numbered in these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 3. Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

These Regulations shall apply notwithstanding any enactment or rule of law, including any provision in any charter, bye-law or other rule (however described) governing any designated authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 4. Designated authorities (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) The designated authority for a regulated profession in the United Kingdom, other than the profession of company auditor as defined in section 24(2) of the Companies Act 1989 and Article 27(2) of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, is the body or authority specified in relation to that profession in Schedule 1, or at any time when a designated authority is not so specified is the competent authority for any regulated profession or regulated professional activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) Without prejudice to its other powers and functions, a designated authority shall have the function of considering applications and granting authorisations under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 5. Right to practise in the United Kingdom (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) Subject to regulation 6, a designated authority for a regulated profession may not, on grounds of inadequate qualifications, refuse to authorise a migrant to practise the profession on the same conditions as apply to someone who holds the diploma required of native applicants, if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (a) the migrant holds the diploma required in another relevant State for the practice of a corresponding profession regulated by that State, the diploma having been awarded in a relevant State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (b) the migrant produces evidence that he holds formal qualifications which satisfy the requirements of sub-paragraph (2) below and either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (i) the qualifications were awarded on completion of regulated education and training; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] the migrant has within the ten years immediately prior to his application pursued a corresponding profession for at least two years full-time (or equivalent duration part-time) in a relevant State in which the profession is not regulated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) Formal qualifications satisfy the requirements of this sub-paragraph if they are: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (a) formal qualifications awarded by a competent authority in a relevant State which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (i) show that the migrant has successfully completed a post-secondary course of at least three years' duration (or equivalent duration part-time) at a university or an establishment of higher education or an establishment of equivalent level in a relevant State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] show the migrant has successfully completed any additional professional training required; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] prepared the migrant for the corresponding profession; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

  or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (b) formal qualifications awarded by a competent authority in a relevant State on the successful completion of education and training within the relevant State, and recognised by a competent authority in that State as equivalent to the qualifications specified in sub-paragraph (2)(a) of this regulation; provided that notification of such recognition has been duly given in accordance with Article 3(b) of the Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 6. Professional experience, adaptation periods and aptitude tests (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) A designated authority may, before authorising the migrant to practise the regulated profession, require him to satisfy one or other of the following conditions (but not both)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (a) where the duration of the education and training received by the migrant, as appears from the matters established by him pursuant to regulation 5, is at least one year less than that required of native applicants, he may be required to provide evidence of a period of professional experience; and the provisions of Schedule 2 shall have effect with respect to the length of that period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (b) in the circumstances specified in paragraph (3) of this regulation (but subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the migrant may be required either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (i) to complete an adaptation period not exceeding three years with a successful assessment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] to pass an aptitude test. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) Where a requirement is imposed pursuant to sub-paragraph (1)(b) of this regulation, the choice between an adaptation period and an aptitude test shall be that of the migrant, except in the case of the regulated professions specified in Schedule 3 (where the provisions of that Schedule shall apply). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(3) If a designated authority intends to require the migrant to complete an adaptation period or take an aptitude test it must first examine whether the knowledge acquired by the migrant in the course of his professional experience is such that it fully or partly covers the substantial difference referred to in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(4) The circumstances referred to in sub-paragraph (1)(b) of this regulation are where the matters covered by the education and training received by the migrant, as established by him pursuant to regulation 5, differ substantially from those covered by the diploma required of native applicants. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 7. Requirements in relation to aptitude tests (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) An aptitude test shall be limited to the professional knowledge of the migrant and shall have the aim of assessing his ability to pursue the relevant regulated profession in the United Kingdom. The test shall take into account that he is a qualified professional in another relevant State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) The designated authority shall determine the matters to be covered by the aptitude test as follows: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (a) the designated authority shall establish which subjects covered by the diploma required of native applicants are not already covered by the migrant's diploma or other evidence of formal qualifications; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (b) the test shall cover subjects selected from those so established, the knowledge of which is essential for the pursuit of the regulated profession in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (c) the test may include knowledge of the relevant rules of professional conduct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(3) The professional status of a migrant preparing for the aptitude test shall be a matter for the designated authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 8. Requirements in relation to adaptation periods (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) The designated authority shall determine the detailed requirements of the adaptation period, having regard to the circumstances of each individual migrant and, in particular, to the fact that he is a qualified professional in another relevant State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) The migrant may be required to undergo further training during the adaptation period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(3) The migrant's performance during the adaptation period shall be assessed by the designated authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(4) The professional status of a migrant during the adaptation period shall be a matter for the designated authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

## 9. Evidence from competent authorities (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(1) A designated authority shall, in connection with any matter requiring to be established pursuant to regulation 5 or 6, accept as sufficient evidence thereof the certificates or other documents duly issued by a competent authority of the relevant State for the purpose of attesting such matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(2) A designated authority which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (a) requires of a migrant for authorisation to practice proof that he is of good character or repute, or that he has not been declared or adjudged bankrupt, or had a bankruptcy or similar order made against him or in relation to his estate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (b) suspends or prohibits practise in the event of serious professional misconduct or the commission of a criminal offence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

shall accept as sufficient evidence in relation to the foregoing: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

- (i) the documents showing the relevant conditions are met, duly issued by the competent authorities of the relevant State of origin of the migrant, the relevant State from which the migrant comes or the relevant State in which the migrant formerly qualified or practised; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902] where those authorities do not issue such documents, a declaration on oath or solemn declaration to the required effect, made by the migrant before a competent judicial or administrative authority or (where appropriate) a notary or duly qualified professional body of the relevant State; provided that in each case such declaration is authenticated by a certificate issued by the authority, notary or body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(3) A designated authority which requires of persons wishing to practise a certificate of physical or mental health, shall accept as sufficient evidence thereof the documents required for such a purpose in the relevant State of origin of the migrant, the relevant State from which the migrant comes or the relevant State in which the migrant formerly qualified or practised, or (where none are required in those States) a certificate issued by a competent authority there which corresponds to the certificate issued for that purpose in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

(4) A designated authority may require any document or certificate referred to in paragraph (2) or (3) of this regulation to be presented no more than three months after the date of its issue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

**1.** For the purposes of this Schedule, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**the shortfall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]**” means the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-abfbaef9e02c382ee48dbcbe345aa902]

These Regulations revoke the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 1991 and two amending Regulations and consolidate those provisions which are substantially re-enacted and incorporated. In addition, these Regulations have included a wider definition of “regulated profession” in the United Kingdom. Designated authorities are therefore authorities listed in Schedule 1, or when not so listed, the competent authority for any regulated profession or regulated professional activity, this could include new professions established after the Regulations are in force to ensure continued effective implementation of EU obligations. Schedules 1, 3 and 4 within the Regulations have also been updated.

These Regulations give effect in the United Kingdom to Council Directive [89/48/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/48) (OJ No. L19, 24.1.89, p.16) on a general system for the recognition of higher-education diplomas awarded on completion of professional education and training of at least three years' duration as amended by Article 2 of Directive 2001/19 EC (OJ No L 206, 31.07.2001, p 1).

[^c10801041][^c10801051]These Regulations also take note of the decisions of the EEA Joint Committee No 7/94 of 21 March 1994 and No 55/01 of 18 May 2001 amending Annex VII to the EEA Agreement as well as the EEA Enlargement Agreement dated 14 October 2003 and agreement relating to Switzerland dated 21 June 1999 in relation to the Directive. The Regulations therefore also extend to the States of the European Economic Area (EEA) and Switzerland.

The Regulations apply to all professions that are regulated by law or public authority or by membership of a chartered body. Schedule 1 includes professions which are regulated by law or public authority or by membership of a chartered body and for which the entry qualification is of degree level or equivalent. Regulation 4 designates a body or authority (a “designated authority”) in relation to regulated professions. The designated authorities have the function of processing applications from migrant applicants having qualifications obtained wholly or partly in another relevant State and wishing to practise the relevant profession in the United Kingdom.

The scheme of the Regulations (subject to certain conditions and exceptions) is to impose a duty upon the designated authorities not to refuse, on grounds of inadequate qualifications, an application from a migrant to practise the relevant profession in the United Kingdom if he holds the qualification required by a relevant State to practise the profession in that State (Regulation 5). The Regulations further provide for the designated authorities, in certain cases, to require a migrant applicant to complete an adaptation period or to pass an aptitude test (Regulations 6, 7 and 8) and make provision for a migrant applicant, who has been granted authorisation to practise, to have the right to use the professional title and designatory letters applicable to that profession in the United Kingdom (Regulation 10).

Special provision is made for the profession of company auditor (Regulations 11 and 12) having regard to the detailed legislation already governing that profession and an appeals procedure is provided (Regulation 13) for migrant applicants to challenge decisions of the designated authorities made under the Regulations.
