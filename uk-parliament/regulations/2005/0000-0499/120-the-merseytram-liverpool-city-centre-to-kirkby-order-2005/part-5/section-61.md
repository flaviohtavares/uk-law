# The Merseytram (Liverpool City Centre to Kirkby) Order 2005, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Notice of penalty fare provisions

(1) It shall be the duty of Merseytravel to secure that a warning notice meeting the requirements of paragraph (2) shall be posted—

- (a) at every tram system stop, in such a position as to be readily visible to prospective passengers; and
- (b) in every tramcar, in such a position as to be readily visible to passengers travelling on that tramcar.

(2) A warning notice posted pursuant to paragraph (1) shall (however expressed) indicate the circumstances (as provided in article 58) in which persons travelling on a tramcar may be liable to pay a penalty fare and state the amount of the penalty fare.
