# The Merseytram (Liverpool City Centre to Kirkby) Order 2005, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Access to works

Merseytravel may, for the purposes of the construction or operation of the scheduled works, form and lay out such means of access or improve such existing means of access, to any street in which the authorised tram system is or is to be laid or any street having a junction with such a street, as may be approved by the highway authority, but such approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.
