# The Merseytram (Liverpool City Centre to Kirkby) Order 2005, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Certification of plans, etc.

Merseytravel shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the works and land plans and the traffic regulation order plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference, the works and land plans and the traffic regulation order plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
