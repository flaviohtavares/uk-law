# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Two and three-wheel motor vehicles

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to two and three-wheel motor vehicles.

(2) In this regulation, in accordance with Directive [97/24/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024) on certain components and characteristics of two and three-wheel motor vehicles [^c10808431], “**two and three-wheel motor vehicles**” has the meaning given to two or three-wheel motor vehicles referred to in Article 1 of Directive [2002/24/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0024) [^c10808441] relating to the type approval of two and three-wheel vehicles.
