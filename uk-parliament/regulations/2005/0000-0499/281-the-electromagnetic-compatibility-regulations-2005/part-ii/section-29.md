## 29. Radio equipment and telecommunications terminal equipment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations do not apply to telecommunications terminal equipment and radio equipment as defined in regulation 2(1) of the Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000 [^c10808481].
