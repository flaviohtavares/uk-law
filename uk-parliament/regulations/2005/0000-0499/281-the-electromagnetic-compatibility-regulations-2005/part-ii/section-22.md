# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Medical devices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to medical devices.

(2) Insofar as the Wireless Telegraphy (Control of Interference from Electro Medical Apparatus) Regulations 1963 [^c10808351] impose electromagnetic compatibility requirements which must be complied with if a relevant medical device is to be supplied, used or taken into service, those Regulations shall cease to apply except where the manufacturer elects to comply with the Wireless Telegraphy (Control of Interference from Electro Medical Apparatus) Regulations 1963 if applicable.

(3) In this regulation “**medical device**” has the meaning given by Article 1.2(a) of Directive [93/42/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042) [^c10808361] concerning medical devices.
