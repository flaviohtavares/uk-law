# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Marine Equipment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to marine equipment.

(2) In this regulation, “**marine equipment**” has the meaning given to “equipment”, as defined in Article 2 (b) of Directive [96/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0098) [^c10808451] on marine equipment, for use on board as provided for in Article 3 of that Directive.
