# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Agricultural or forestry tractors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to agricultural or forestry tractors insofar as the electromagnetic disturbance generated by them is liable to cause radio interference.

(2) In this regulation, “**agricultural or forestry tractor**” has the meaning given to “vehicle” in Article 1 of Council Directive [75/322/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0322) on the suppression of radio interference produced by agricultural or forestry tractors (electromagnetic compatibility) [^c10808411] as amended by Commission Directive [2000/2/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0002) of 14 January 2000 [^c10808421].
