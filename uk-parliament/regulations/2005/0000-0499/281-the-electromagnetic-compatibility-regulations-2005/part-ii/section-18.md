# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Radio amateur apparatus

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to radio amateur apparatus which is not available commercially.

(2) In this regulation, “**available commercially**” means manufactured in the course of a business of manufacture of relevant apparatus.
