# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Apparatus supplied or taken into service before 28th October 1992

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations do not apply to an item of electrical apparatus supplied or taken into service in the Community before 28th October 1992.
