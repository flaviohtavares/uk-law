# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Vehicles, components and separate technical units

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to vehicles, components or separate technical units.

(2) In this regulation, in accordance with Council Directive [72/245/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245) relating to the radio interference (electromagnetic compatibility) of vehicles [^c10808371] as adapted to technical progress and amended by Commission Directive [95/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0054) [^c10808381], “**vehicle**”, “**component**” and “**separate technical unit**” have the meanings respectively given to them by Article 2 of Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) on the approximation of the laws of the member States relating to the type-approval of motor vehicles and their trailers [^c10808391], as amended by Council Directive [92/53/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0053) [^c10808401].
