# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Requirements for supply

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The requirements of this regulation are that—

- (a) the relevant apparatus conforms with the protection requirements;
- (b) the conformity assessment requirements have been complied with;
- (c) the CE marking has been properly affixed by the manufacturer or his authorised representative in relation to the relevant apparatus in accordance with regulation 35 below; and
- (d) the manufacturer or his authorised representative has properly issued an EC declaration of conformity in respect of the relevant apparatus in accordance with the requirements of regulation 36 below.
