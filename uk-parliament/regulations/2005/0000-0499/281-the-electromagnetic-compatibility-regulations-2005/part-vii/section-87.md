# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. False or misleading information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person who, in giving any information which he is required to give under regulation 84(c) or (d) above—

- (a) makes any statement which he knows is false or misleading in a material particular; or
- (b) recklessly makes any statement which is false or misleading in a material particular,

shall be guilty of an offence.
