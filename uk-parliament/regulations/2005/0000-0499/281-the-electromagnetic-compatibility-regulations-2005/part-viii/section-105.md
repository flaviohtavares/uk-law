## 105. Enterprise Act 2002 (Part 9 Restrictions on Disclosure of Information) (Specification) Order 2004

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the Enterprise Act 2002 (Part 9 Restrictions on Disclosure of Information) (Specification) Order 2004 [^c10808611] for the reference to the Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992 there shall be substituted the words “ The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005 ”.
