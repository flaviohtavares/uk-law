# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2005, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2005-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2005/281
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Savings for action taken under other enactments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations shall be construed as preventing the taking of any action in respect of any relevant apparatus under the provisions of any other enactment.
