## 104. The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 1(3) of the Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, the words “the Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, except for regulations 2(2) and (3), 5 and 31,” shall be deleted.
