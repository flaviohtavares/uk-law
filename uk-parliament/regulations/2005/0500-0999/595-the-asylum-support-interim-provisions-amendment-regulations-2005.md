# The Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 595

IMMIGRATION AND ASYLUM

The Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made

9th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2005

Coming into force

3rd April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 15 of Schedule 9 to the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 [^f00001] and having regard to the definition of “prescribed” in section 167(1) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 3rd April 2005.

## 2. Extension of the interim period in the Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) Regulations 1999

In regulation 2(5) of the Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) Regulations 1999 [^f00002] for the words “4th April 2005” there is substituted “3rd April 2006”.

Des Browne

Minister of State

Home Office

9th March 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) Regulations 1999.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 2(5) of the Asylum Support (Interim Provisions) Regulations 1999 by extending the “interim period” during which local authorities are required to provide support to asylum-seekers or their dependants who appear to be destitute or likely to become destitute. The day prescribed for the ending of the interim period is now 3rd April 2006.
