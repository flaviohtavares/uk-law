# The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 526

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005

Made

8th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2005

Coming into force

31st March 2005

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 45A and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 31st March 2005.

(2) Regulation 2 amends the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (England) Regulations 2004[^f00002] (“the Budget regulations”).

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2005.

## 2. Amendment to the Budget regulations

In respect of each of the local education authorities named in the first column in the table in the Schedule, the words set out in the second column against each authority’s name are added after the formula and before the word “where” in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2.

Stephen Twigg

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

8th March 2005
