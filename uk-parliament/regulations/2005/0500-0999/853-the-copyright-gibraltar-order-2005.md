# The Copyright (Gibraltar) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 853

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright (Gibraltar) Order 2005

Made

22nd March 2005

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of March 2005

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 157(2) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001] and all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Gibraltar) Order 2005 and shall come into force on such date as the Governor of Gibraltar may appoint by proclamation published in the Gazette of Gibraltar.

**2.** The provisions of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto shall extend to Gibraltar subject to the modifications specified in Parts II and III of that Schedule.

**3.** The Copyright (Gibraltar) Order 1960[^f00002] and the Copyright (Gibraltar) (Amendment) Order 1985[^f00003] are revoked.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

### PART I — PROVISIONS OF THE COPYRIGHT, DESIGNS AND PATENTS ACT 1988 EXTENDED TO GIBRALTAR

All the provisions of Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, except sections 31A–F, 44A, 98, 107A, 114B, 115, 128A, 128B, 135A–135H, 143, 144, 145–152, 157, 158, 161, 166A, 166B and 177, and all the provisions of Schedule 1 except paragraphs 13, 21, 34, 36, 37, 38 and 42.
