# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 520

FAMILY PROCEEDINGS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2005

Made

4th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 92(9) and (10) of, and Part I of Schedule 11 to, the Children Act 1989[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment)Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

(2) Any reference in this Order to an article or Schedule by number alone is a reference to the article or Schedule so numbered in the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002].

## 2. Amendments to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991

In article 1(2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “London commission area”; and
- (b) for the definition of “petty sessions area” substitute—

  > - “local justice area” has the same meaning as in the Courts Act 2003[^f00003]; and

  .

**3.** In article 9(1) and article 18(3) for “petty sessions area or London commission area” substitute “local justice area”.

**4.** In Schedule 1—

- (a) At the beginning of the Schedule insert—

  > **London Region**
  >
  > - Barnet County Court
  > - Bow County Court
  > - Brentford County Court
  > - Bromley County Court
  > - Croydon County Court
  > - Edmonton County Court
  > - Ilford County Court
  > - Kingston-upon-Thames County Court
  > - Romford County Court
  > - Wandsworth County Court
  > - Willesden County Court

  ;
- (b) for “Midland Circuit” substitute “Midlands Region”;
- (c) for “Northern Circuit” substitute “North West Region”;
- (d) for “North Eastern Circuit” substitute “North East Region”;
- (e) for “South Eastern Circuit” substitute “South East Region”;
- (f) in the entries after “South Eastern Circuit” omit the entries for—
  - (i) Barnet County Court;
  - (ii) Bow County Court;
  - (iii) Brentford County Court;
  - (iv) Bromley County Court;
  - (v) Croydon County Court;
  - (vi) Edmonton County Court;
  - (vii) Ilford County Court;
  - (viii) Kingston-upon-Thames County Court;
  - (ix) Romford County Court;
  - (x) Wandsworth County Court; and
  - (xi) Willesden County Court;
- (g) for “Wales and Chester Circuit” substitute “Wales and Cheshire Region”; and
- (h) for “Western Circuit” substitute “South West Region”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

It has been decided that Exeter County Court should become a Care Centre as defined in article 2 of the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 (“the 1991 Order”). A Care Centre is a county court designated for the purpose of hearing cases under Parts III, IV or V of the Children Act 1989. This Order effects this change and amends the 1991 Order to re-organise the transfer arrangements between magistrates' courts and Care Centres on the Western Circuit to reflect this change.

Amendments to the Order have also been made to replace references to “petty sessions areas” and “London commission area” with references to “local justice area” to reflect the changes to the organisation of the courts made by the Courts Act 2003 which are also being brought into force on 1st April 2005. References to circuits are changed to references to the new administrative regions which will be used after that date.

At the same time the opportunity has been taken to up-date the names of the petty sessions areas (local justice areas as they will become) in consequence of changes to their boundaries which have not previously been reflected in the 1991 Order.
