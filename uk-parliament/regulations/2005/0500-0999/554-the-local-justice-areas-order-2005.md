# The Local Justice Areas Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 554

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Justice Areas Order 2005

Made

8th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 8 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Justice Areas Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

## 2. Local Justice Areas

The local justice areas into which England and Wales is divided are specified in the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

8th March 2005

This Order specifies the local justice areas into which England and Wales is divided under section 8 of the Courts Act 2003. The Act requires the first Order under section 8 to specify as local justice areas the areas which were petty sessions areas before 1st April 2005.

Maps showing the local justice areas are available for inspection at any reasonable time at the Department for Constitutional Affairs, Selborne House, 54-60 Victoria Street, London SW1E 6QW.
