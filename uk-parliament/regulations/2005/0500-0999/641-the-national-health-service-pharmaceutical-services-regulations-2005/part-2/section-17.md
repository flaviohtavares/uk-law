# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2005, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Refusal: distance selling premises

A Primary Care Trust shall not grant an application to which regulation 13(1)(d) applies if the premises to which the application relates are on the same site or in the same building as the premises of a provider of primary medical services with a patient list.
