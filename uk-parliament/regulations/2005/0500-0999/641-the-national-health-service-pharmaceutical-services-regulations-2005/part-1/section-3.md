# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2005, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Terms of service

The arrangements for the provision of pharmaceutical services which it is the duty of a Primary Care Trust to make under sections 41 to 43 of the Act, and to administer under section 15(1) of the Act, shall incorporate—

- (a) in the case of arrangements with a pharmacist, the terms of service in Schedule 1;
- (b) in the case of arrangements with a doctor who provides pharmaceutical services, the terms of Service in Schedule 2; and
- (c) in the case of arrangements with a supplier of appliances, the terms of service in Schedule 3.
