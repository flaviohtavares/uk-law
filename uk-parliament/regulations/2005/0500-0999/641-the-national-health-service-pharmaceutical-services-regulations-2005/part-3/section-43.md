# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2005, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Appeals following a review

A person whose application has been granted subject to conditions under regulation 21(2) may appeal to the FHSAA against any decision of the Primary Care Trust under regulation 42—

- (a) to vary a condition;
- (b) to vary his terms of service; or
- (c) on any review of an earlier such decision of the Primary Care Trust,

and the appeal shall be by way of redetermination of the Primary Care Trust’s decision.
