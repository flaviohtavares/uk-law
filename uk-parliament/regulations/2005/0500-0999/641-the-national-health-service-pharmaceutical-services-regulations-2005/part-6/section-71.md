# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2005, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. List of chemists providing an ETP service

(1) A Primary Care Trust shall prepare, maintain and publish a list (to be called the ETP list) of all chemists in its area who participate in the ETP service.

(2) The list referred to in paragraph (1) shall include—

- (a) the name of the chemist; and
- (b) the address of the premises at which the ETP service is provided.
