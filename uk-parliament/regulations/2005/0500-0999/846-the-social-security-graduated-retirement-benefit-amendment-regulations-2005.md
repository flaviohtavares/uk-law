# The Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I.2005/454](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/454) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2005 No. 846

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made

19th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

24th March 2005

Coming into force

5th April 2005

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 62(1)(a) and (c) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 5th April 2005.

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) Regulations 2005

In regulation 1(2) of the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) Regulations 2005[^f00003] (effect), for “paragraphs 3(2) and (3) and 13(2) and (3)” there shall be substituted “paragraphs 4(2) and (3) and 14(2) and (3)”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Malcolm Wicks

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

19 March 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) Regulations 2005 ([S.I.2005/454](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/454)) to correct an erroneous cross-reference in those Regulations relating to when certain provisions come into effect.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been produced for this Instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
