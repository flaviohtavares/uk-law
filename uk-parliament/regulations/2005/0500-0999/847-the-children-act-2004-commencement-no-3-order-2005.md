# The Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 847 (C.35)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made

17th March 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 67(7)(j) of the Children Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

**2.** Section 62 of the Children Act 2004 shall come into force on the 12th April 2005.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Cathy Ashton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

17th March 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the third Commencement Order made under the Children Act 2004.

This Commencement Order brings section 62 of the Children Act [2004 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31) into force on 12th April 2005. Section 62 concerns publication of material relating to legal proceedings.

(This note is not part of the Order)
