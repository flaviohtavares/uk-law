# The Hazardous Waste (England and Wales)Regulations 2005, section 58

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Agency to retain registers etc

*Extent: E+W*

The Agency shall retain registers and any accompanying records sent to it pursuant to regulation 51(3) for a period of not less than three years commencing with the receipt thereof by the Agency.
