# The Hazardous Waste (England and Wales)Regulations 2005, section 15

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Radioactive waste

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-8a03c96fdb7e9cfa593936a2b8f500d7] This regulation applies to radioactive waste where—

- (a) a radioactive substances activity in relation to that radioactive waste does not require an environmental permit by virtue of a radioactive substances exemption[^key-00a91b9d11cba857915882e020bee1e5]; and
- (b) that radioactive waste has one or more hazardous properties arising other than from its radioactive nature.

(2) Notwithstanding regulation 2(1)(b)(ii), radioactive waste to which this regulation applies is treated as waste for the purposes of these Regulations, and accordingly it is treated as hazardous waste and these Regulations apply to that waste.
