# The Hazardous Waste (England and Wales)Regulations 2005, section 16

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Agricultural waste

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to agricultural waste before 15th May 2007[^key-057cfc9d71cf06c7adf5603ef8a32c34], but apply on and after that date to agricultural waste whenever it became waste.

(2) For the purpose of this regulation, “**agricultural waste**” means waste from premises used for agriculture within the meaning of the Agriculture Act 1947 [^c12130031].
