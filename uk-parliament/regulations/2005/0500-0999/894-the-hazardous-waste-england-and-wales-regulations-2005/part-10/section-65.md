# The Hazardous Waste (England and Wales)Regulations 2005, section 65

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Offences[^c-31254f2877ce8a92eeab0552755ddf9e]

*Extent: E+W*

It is an offence for a person to fail to comply with any requirement imposed on them by or under any of the following provisions of these Regulations—

- (a) Part 4;
- (b)[^c-03c726ee6087f8cecfb308c6289eec37] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) regulations 34 to 43[^c-ca10686358cb44418deb667211e34590];
- (d) regulation 46 and Schedule 7;
- (e) Part 7 (except regulation 52);
- (f) regulation 62.
