# The Pension Protection Fund (Review and Reconsideration of Reviewable Matters) Regulations 2005, section 28

> Text as at 2014-07-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Representation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An interested person may by sending written notice to the Board appoint a person to act as his representative for the purposes of these Regulations.

(2) Where an interested person—

- (a) dies;
- (b) is a minor; or
- (c) is otherwise incapable of acting for himself,

the Board may allow a person to represent him.

(3) For the purposes of paragraphs (1) and (2) an interested person includes a person who is an interested person for the purposes of regulation 7 or 19 of these Regulations.
