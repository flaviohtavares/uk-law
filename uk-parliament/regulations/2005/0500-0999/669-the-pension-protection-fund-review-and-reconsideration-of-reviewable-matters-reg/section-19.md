# The Pension Protection Fund (Review and Reconsideration of Reviewable Matters) Regulations 2005, section 19

> Text as at 2014-07-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Notice of decision to reconsider other than on an application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Board decides that the Reconsideration Committee should[^c22579061] reconsider a reviewable matter and give a reconsideration decision under regulation 18(1) it must notify the Reconsideration Committee[^c22579081].

(2)[^c22579101] Where the Reconsideration Committee decides to reconsider a reviewable matter otherwise than on an application it must notify the Board and any interested person.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2) an interested person means a person who, in the opinion of the Board, may be materially affected by the reconsideration decision or, as the case may be, a representative of that person appointed under regulation 28.
