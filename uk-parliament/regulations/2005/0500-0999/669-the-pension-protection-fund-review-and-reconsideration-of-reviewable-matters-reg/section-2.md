# The Pension Protection Fund (Review and Reconsideration of Reviewable Matters) Regulations 2005, section 2

> Text as at 2014-07-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Duty to give a review decision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Board must give a review decision on any reviewable matter on the written application of an interested person.
