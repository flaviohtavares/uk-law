# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.8 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2005

> Text as at 2020-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

2005 No. 950 (C.42)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.8 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2005

Made

24th March 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 330(4)(b), 336(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 [^c11912891] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1.

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.8 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- “**the 2003 Act**” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003;
- “**the Sentencing Act** ” means the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 [^c11912901].

## 2.

(1) Subject to [^key-5b9b2cfab1a0a35182a691c94dd44cfd]... Schedule 2, the provisions of the 2003 Act specified in Schedule 1 shall come into force on 4<sup>th</sup> April 2005.

(2)[^key-6cabf9cc3f7fcc3808a582302366b8ca] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 3.

(1) The provisions of the 2003 Act specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 18<sup>th</sup> April 2005.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 96 (application of Part 10 to Northern Ireland);
- (b) Part 2 of Schedule 5 (list of offences for Northern Ireland);
- (c) in Schedule 36 (further minor and consequential amendments), paragraphs 91, 92(6) and 94.

Articles 2 and 4

### 1. Interpretation

In this Schedule—

- “**the 1991 Act**” means the Criminal Justice Act 1991;
- “**the 1997 Act**” means the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997;

### Pre commencement offences

#### 2. Savings relating to Parts 2, 5 and 9 of the 2003 Act

The coming into force of the provisions referred to in paragraphs 2, 43(a) and 44(2) of Schedule 1 to this Order is of no effect in relation to alleged offences into which a criminal investigation within the meaning of section 1(4) of the Criminal Procedure and Investigation Act 1996 [^c11913151] has begun before 4<sup>th</sup> April 2005.

**3.** (1) In the case of any criminal proceedings falling under paragraph (2)—

- (a) the coming into force of sections 18, 57 to 61, 67 to 72 and 74 of the 2003 Act confers no additional prosecution right of appeal;
- (b) the coming into force of sections 309 and 310 of the 2003 Act confers no additional power to order a preparatory hearing on a judge of the Crown Court; and
- (c) the coming into force of section 311 of the 2003 Act does not alter the jurisdiction in which reporting restrictions may apply in those proceedings.

(2) The criminal proceedings to which this paragraph applies are those in which one of the following occurred before 4<sup>th</sup> April 2005—

- (a) the defendant was committed for trial;
- (b) the proceedings were transferred to the Crown Court under section 53 of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 [^c11913161] or section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1987 [^c11913171];
- (c) an order was made by a magistrates court that the accused be sent for trial for an indictable only offence under section 51 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 [^c11913181]; or
- (d) a bill of indictment was preferred by the direction or with the consent of a judge of the High Court.
