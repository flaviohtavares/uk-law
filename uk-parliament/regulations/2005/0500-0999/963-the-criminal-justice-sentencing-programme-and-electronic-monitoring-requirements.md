# The Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Programme and Electronic Monitoring Requirements) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 963

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Programme and Electronic Monitoring Requirements) Order 2005

Made

24th March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 202(3)(b), 215(3) and 330(3)(a) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Programme and Electronic Monitoring Requirements) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 2003 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003; and
- “police area” has the meaning given by section 1 of the Police Act 1996[^f00002].

**2.** The body designated for the purposes of section 202 of the 2003 Act (programme requirement) shall continue to be the Correctional Services Accreditation Panel[^f00003].

**3.** The description of person responsible for the monitoring under an electronic monitoring requirement for the purposes of section 215(3) of the 2003 Act (electronic monitoring requirement)—

- (a) in relation to such a condition imposed on an offender residing in a police area specified in Schedule 1 to this Order, is one employed by Premier Monitoring Services, PO Box 45, Norwich, NR3 1BF to monitor offenders; and
- (b) in relation to such a condition imposed on an offender residing in a police area specified in Schedule 2 to this Order, is one employed by Securicor Justice Services, Sutton Park, 15 Carshalton Road, Sutton, Surrey, SML 4LD to monitor offenders.

**4.** The Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Programme and Electronic Monitoring Requirements) Order 2004[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

Scotland of Asthal Q.C.

Minister of State

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order identifies the accreditation body and electronic monitoring provider for the purposes of the programme requirement and electronic monitoring requirement respectively. (Both requirements can be imposed as part of a “relevant order” as defined by section 196 of the Criminal Justice Act [2003 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44)). It continues the designation in relation to the accreditation body made under the Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Programme and Electronic Monitoring Requirements) Order 2004 (S.I. [2004/117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/117)) which is revoked, and reflects changes made to the police areas covered by the two electronic monitoring providers.
