# The Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Curfew Condition) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 986

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Curfew Condition) Order 2005

Made

24th March 2005

Coming into force

4th April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 253(5) and 330(3)(a) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Sentencing) (Curfew Condition) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 4<sup>th</sup> April 2005.

(2) In this Order “police area” has the meaning given by section 1 of the Police Act 1996[^f00002].

**2.** The description of person responsible for monitoring the whereabouts of an offender subject to a curfew condition for the purposes of section 253(5) (curfew condition to be included in licence under section 246) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00003]—

- (a) in relation to such a condition imposed on an offender residing in a police area specified in Schedule 1 to this Order, is one employed by Premier Monitoring Services, PO Box 45, Norwich, NR3 1BF to monitor offenders; and
- (b) in relation to such a condition imposed on an offender residing in a police area specified in Schedule 2 to this Order, is one employed by Securicor Justice Services, Sutton Park, 15 Carshalton Road, Sutton, Surry, SML 4LD to monitor offenders.

Scotland of Asthal Q.C.

Minister of State

This Order makes provision for the different persons responsible for the electronic monitoring of offenders subject to a curfew condition imposed under section 246 of the Criminal Justice Act [2003 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44) (power to release prisoners on licence before required to do so). The areas covered by the monitoring providers are set out in Schedules 1 and 2 to the Order.
