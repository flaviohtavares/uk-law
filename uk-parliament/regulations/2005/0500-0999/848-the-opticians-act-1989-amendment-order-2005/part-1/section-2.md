## 2. Amendment of the Opticians Act 1989[^key-28f92a403ba43fc613dc015793e5bef9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Opticians Act 1989[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

(2) Except where the context otherwise requires, references in this Order to Parts, sections and Schedules are references to Parts and sections of, and Schedules to, the Opticians Act 1989.
