# The Opticians Act 1989 (Amendment) Order 2005, section 24

> Text as at 2012-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Amendment of section 33[^c-24ab9041303a39f0449048b50804012f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For section 33(3) (default powers of Privy Council not applicable) substitute—

> (3) This section applies to all functions of the Council under this Act except—
>
> - (a) their functions under sections 1, 5D, 8, 8A, 9, 12, 13 and 32(1) above; and
> - (b) their powers to make rules under sections 23C, 23D and 23E above.
