# The Midland Metro (Wednesbury to Brierley Hill and Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2005, section 46

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Obstruction of construction of authorised works

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who, without reasonable excuse, obstructs another person from constructing the authorised tramway or any other authorised works under the powers conferred by this Order shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
