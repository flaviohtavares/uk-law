# The Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 566

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2005

Made

7th March 2005

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2005.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment to section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968

(1) This article applies to the provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 (which relate to fees charged in relation to those matters mentioned in column 2).

(2) Each of the provisions shall have effect as if for any reference in the provision to a column 3 sum there were substituted a reference to the sum specified in the associated entry in column 4.

(3) In paragraph (2), a “column 3 sum”, in relation to a provision, is a sum—

- (a) specified in column 3, and
- (b) which is referred to in that provision, but as that reference has effect by virtue of the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2003[^f00003].

(4) In this article a reference to a numbered column is to the column so numbered in the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Revocation

The Gaming (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2003 is hereby revoked.

Andrew McIntosh

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Culture Media and Sport
