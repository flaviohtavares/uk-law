# The Social Security (Contributions) (Re-rating and National Insurance Funds Payments) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 878

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Contributions) (Re-rating and National Insurance Funds Payments) Order 2005

Made

24th March 2005

Coming into force

6th April 2005

As a result of carrying out in the tax year 2004-05 a review of the general level of earnings in Great Britain in accordance with section 141(1) and (2) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Administration Act”), the Treasury have determined that an Order should be made under that section amending Part 1 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002] by altering the rates of Class 2 and Class 3 contributions, the amount of earnings below which an earner may be excepted from liability for Class 2 contributions and the lower and upper limits of profits or gains to be taken into account for Class 4 contributions.

This Order makes provision for Northern Ireland, which corresponds to that mentioned in relation to Great Britain in the preceding recital, in accordance with section 129 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00003] (“the Northern Ireland Administration Act”).

With a view to adjusting the level at which the National Insurance Fund stands for the time being, and having regard to estimated benefit expenditure for the financial year ending 31st March 2006 the Treasury think it expedient that an Order should be made under section 2(2) of the Social Security Act 1993 (“the 1993 Act”)[^f00004].

With a view to adjusting the level at which the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund stands for the time being, and having regard to estimated benefit expenditure for the financial year ending 31st March 2006, the Treasury think it expedient that an Order should be made under Article 4(3) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 (“the 1993 Order”)[^f00005].

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with the provisions of sections 141(3) and 190(1) of the Administration Act, section 166(10A) of the Northern Ireland Administration Act[^f00006], section 2(8) of the 1993 Act and Article 4(8) of the 1993 Order[^f00007] and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

Accordingly the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 141(4) and (5), 142(2) and (3), 143(1) and (3) and 144(2) of the Administration Act[^f00008], sections 129 and 165(11A) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00009], section 2(2) of the 1993 Act and Article 4(3) of the 1993 Order and now exercisable by them[^f00010], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Contributions) (Re-rating and National Insurance Funds Payments) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 6th April 2005.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992; and
- “the Northern Ireland Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00011].

## 2. Rate of and small earnings exception from Class 2 contributions

In both section 11 of the Act[^f00012] and section 11 of the Northern Ireland Act[^f00013] (Class 2 contributions)–

- (a) in subsection (1) (weekly rate) for “£2.05” substitute “£2.10”; and
- (b) in subsection (4) (small earnings exception) for “£4,215” substitute “£4,345”.

## 3. Amount of Class 3 contributions

In both section 13(1) of the Act [^f00014] and section 13(1) of the Northern Ireland Act [^f00015] (amount of Class 3 contributions) for “£7.15” substitute “£7.35”.

## 4. Lower and upper limits for Class 4 contributions

In each of sections 15(3), 18(1) and 18(1A) of the Act[^f00016] and sections 15(3), 18(1) and 18(1A) of the Northern Ireland Act[^f00017] (Class 4 contributions recoverable under the Income Tax Acts and under regulations)—

- (a) for “£4,745” (lower limit) in each place where it appears, substitute “£4,895”; and
- (b) for “£31,720” (upper limit) in each place where it appears, substitute “£32,760”.

## 5. Prescribed percentage of estimated benefit expenditure – Great Britain

Section 2(2) of the Social Security Act 1993 (payments into the National Insurance Fund out of money provided by Parliament) shall have effect with respect to the tax year 2005-06 and the prescribed percentage of estimated benefit expenditure for the financial year ending with 31st March in that year shall be 2 per cent.

## 6. Prescribed percentage of estimated benefit expenditure – Northern Ireland

Article 4(3) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 (payments into the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund out of appropriated money) shall have effect with respect to the tax year 2005-06 and the prescribed percentage of estimated benefit expenditure for the financial year ending with 31st March in that year shall be 2 per cent.

Joan Ryan

Nick Ainger

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

24th March 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the rates of Class 2 and Class 3 contributions specified in sections 11(1) and 13(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4): “the Act”) from £2.05 to £2.10 and from £7.15 to £7.35 respectively. It also increases the amount of earnings specified in section 11(4) of the Act, below which an earner may be excepted from liability for Class 2 contributions from £4,215 to £4,345 (Articles 2 and 3).

The Order also increases, from £4,745 to £4,895 and from £31,720 to £32,760 respectively, the lower and upper limits of profits or gains specified in those sections, between which Class 4 contributions are payable at the main Class 4 percentage rate (Article 4).

Articles 2 to 4 of the Order also make provision for Northern Ireland corresponding to that in the preceding two paragraphs (the section numbers of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7) are the same as those in the Act).

The Order provides for section 2(2) of the Social Security Act [1993 (c. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/3) to have effect for the tax year 2005-06. It also provides that the amount of any money that may be provided by Parliament to be paid into the National Insurance Fund in that year shall not exceed in aggregate 2 per cent. of the estimated benefit expenditure for the financial year ending 31st March 2006 (Article 5). The Order also makes corresponding provision for Northern Ireland (Article 4(3) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 ([S.I. 1993/592 (N.I.2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1993/592)) being the provision brought into effect (Article 6).

In accordance with sections 142(1) and 144(1) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5), a copy of the report by the Government Actuary (Cm..6457), giving his opinion on the likely effect on the National Insurance Fund of the making of the Order, insofar as it amends sections 11(1) and (4), 13(1), 15(3), 18(1) and 18(1A) of the Act, was laid before Parliament with the draft of this Order.

This Order does not impose any new costs on business.
