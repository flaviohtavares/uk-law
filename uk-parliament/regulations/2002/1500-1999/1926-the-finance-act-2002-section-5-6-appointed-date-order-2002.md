# The Finance Act 2002, section 5(6), (Appointed Date) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1926 (C. 61)

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Finance Act 2002, section 5(6), (Appointed Date) Order 2002

Made

25th July 2002

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 5(6)(a) of the Finance Act 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2002, section 5(6), (Appointed Date) Order 2002.

**2.** The date appointed under section 5(6)(a) of the Finance Act 2002 is 25th July 2002.

Ray McAfee

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House 22 Upper Ground London SE1 9PJ

25th July 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 25th July 2002 under section 5(6)(a) of the Finance Act 2002 (“the Finance Act”).

Section 5 of the Finance Act makes provision in respect of two new duties of excise to be charged on biodiesel and bioblend under the provisions of the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act [1979 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5). This Order determines the date from which the charges to duty under the new provisions apply.
