# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 1A. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Social Security Administration Act[^c-1d8f3cd01d8d4b2e95d0a6fa62847bb9]

Section 1(1A) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (requirement to state a national insurance number) shall not apply to a person who—

- (a) is a person in respect of whom a claim for state pension credit is made;
- (b) is subject to immigration control within the meaning of section 115(9)(a) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999;
- (c) does not satisfy the conditions of entitlement to state pension credit as specified in section 1(2); and
- (d) has not previously been allocated a national insurance number.
