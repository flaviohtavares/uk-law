# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 7. Savings Credit

(1) The percentage prescribed for the purposes of determining—

- (a) the maximum savings credit is 60 per cent.;
- (b) “amount A” in section 3(4) is 60 per cent.;
- (c) “amount B” in section 3(4) is 40 per cent.

(2) The amount prescribed for the savings credit threshold is £208.07[^c-1e9b943f9310a935c9f3fe44ff25ee32] for a claimant who has no partner and £329.75 for a claimant who has a partner.

(3) The maximum savings credit shall be taken to be Nil in the case of—

- (a) prisoners; and
- (b) members of religious orders who are fully maintained by their order.

(4)[^c-f9bf8523cb644c8730faba722850abbb] If a calculation made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b) or (c) results in a fraction of a penny, that fraction shall, if it would be to the claimant’s advantage, be treated as a penny; otherwise it shall be disregarded.
