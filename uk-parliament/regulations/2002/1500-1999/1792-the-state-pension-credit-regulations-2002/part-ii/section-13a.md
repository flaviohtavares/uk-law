## 13A. Part-weeks[^key-19f19e63f84b3d9addcf90c2a2041112]

(1) The guarantee credit shall be payable for a period of less than a week (“a part-week”) at the rate specified in paragraph (3) if—

- (a) the claimant was entitled to universal credit,[^key-78261384f63767736dbd5e3a06122633] income support, an income-related employment and support allowance[^key-8ad193eabaad6e1991d064516ae80782] or an income-based jobseeker’s allowance immediately before the first day on which the conditions for entitlement to the credit are satisfied; and
- (b) the claimant’s entitlement to the credit is likely to continue throughout the first full benefit week which follows the part-week.

(3) The amount of the guarantee credit payable in respect of the part-week shall be determined—

- (a) by dividing by 7 the weekly amount of the guarantee credit which[^key-545f7910dcf7a8517f73a98ed0f89881]... would be payable in respect of a full week; and then
- (b) multiplying the resulting figure by the number of days in the part-week,
