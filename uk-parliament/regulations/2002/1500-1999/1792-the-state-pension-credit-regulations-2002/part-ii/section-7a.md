# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 7A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 7A. Limitation of savings credit for certain mixed-age couples[^c-471de24b2a61f207af11ec98eb041760]

A person who is a member of a mixed-age couple, is not entitled to a savings credit unless one of the members of the couple—

- (a) has been awarded a savings credit with effect from a day before 6th April 2016 and was entitled to a savings credit immediately before 6th April 2016, and
- (b) remained entitled to a savings credit at all times since the beginning of 6th April 2016.
