# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 13B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 13B. Date on which benefits are treated as paid[^key-19f19e63f84b3d9addcf90c2a2041112]

(1) The following benefits shall be treated as paid on the day of the week in respect of which the benefit is payable—

- (a) severe disablement allowance;
- (b) short-term and long-term incapacity benefit;
- (c) maternity allowance;
- (d) contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance[^key-ce57db00b506d512cb140c647ddf86c6]....
- (e)[^key-83a8008ba9f89a7e441642b0b8e0d06c] contributory employment and support allowance.

(2)[^key-e6d3f9e5e1a907ea80e666d7f6a6716c] All benefits except those mentioned in paragraph (1) shall be treated as paid—

- (a) where the benefit is paid in advance, on the first day of the benefit week in which the benefit is payable;
- (b) where the benefit is paid in arrears, on the last day of the benefit week in which the benefit is payable.
