# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 12. End of assessed income period

An assessed income period shall end [^c-2f1e04b2e6bf1d8051d35548a8405d2a]...—

- (a) at such time as[^c-e151b4977af497f0d96cba86aa3d0fd1] the claimant no longer satisfies a condition of entitlement to state pension credit;
- (b) at such time as[^c-127ca5d2b95772593b95b9ab787e5606] payments of an element of the claimant’s retirement provision which is due to be paid to him stops temporarily or the amount paid is less than the amount due and in consequence his award of state pension credit is superseded under section 10 of the Social Security Act 1998[^c262948];
- (c) at such time as[^c-88a816181a1c8f134e5fd0258f633ea2] a claimant who has no partner is provided with accommodation in a care home or an independent hospital[^c-d3a7193214e6ee98dbd1f0240c56df8c] other than on a temporary basis;[^c-6532eb4f8c4231534270fea18b678a06]
- (d) if, apart from this sub-paragraph, it would have ended on a date falling within the period specified in column 1 of the table in Schedule IIIA, on the corresponding date shown against that period in column 2 of that table
