# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 19. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom[^c-5eff11132b2278bcba81a8a605d3ca02]

Capital which a claimant possesses in the United Kingdom shall be calculated at its current market or surrender value less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10 per cent; and
- (b) the amount of any encumbrance secured on it.
