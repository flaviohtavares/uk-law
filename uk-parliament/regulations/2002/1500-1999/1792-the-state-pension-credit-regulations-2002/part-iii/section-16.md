# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 16. Retirement pension income

There shall be added to the descriptions of income listed in section 16(1) (retirement pension income) the following paragraphs[^c-b3f8c9388651486171c9008423b37de5]—

> - (k) any sum payable by way of pension out of money provided under the Civil List Act 1837[^c262959], the Civil List Act 1937[^c262960], the Civil List Act 1952[^c262961], the Civil List Act 1972 [^c262962] or the Civil List Act 1975[^c-be4872cb9bafad4ace428472520f1cf0] [^c262963];[^c-89268e75019d439a638c908d40e16430]
> - (1) any payment, other than a payment ordered by a court or made in settlement of a claim, made by or on behalf of a former employer of a person on account of the early retirement of that person on grounds of ill-health or disability.
> - (m)[^c-fd92382ea2f40365634c86c9111960f1] any payment made at regular intervals under an equity release scheme.
> - (n)[^c-9996311047e872d62cdd8382be0e3cff] any payment made under the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005.
