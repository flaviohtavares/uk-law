# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 14. Calculation of income and capital

The income and capital of—

- (a) the claimant; and
- (b) any partner of the claimant,

shall be calculated in accordance with the rules set out in this Part; and any reference in this Part to the claimant shall apply equally to any partner of the claimant.
