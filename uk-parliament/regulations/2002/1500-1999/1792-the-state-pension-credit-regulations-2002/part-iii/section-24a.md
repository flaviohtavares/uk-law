# The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002, section 24A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/1792

## 24A. Rounding of fractions[^key-9f45981b7a52b4db7ca669bbbfe1a834]

Where any calculation under this Part results in a fraction of a penny that fraction shall, if it would be to the claimant’s advantage, be treated as a penny; otherwise it shall be disregarded.
