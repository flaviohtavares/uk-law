# The Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1837

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002

Made

16th July 2002

Laid before Parliament

17th July 2002

Coming into force

8th August 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 3(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 8th August 2002.

**2.** The amount of the penalty payable in respect of a penalty offence[^f00002] is—

- (a) in the case of an offence falling under Part I of the Schedule to this Order, £80; or
- (b) in the case of an offence falling under Part II of that Schedule, £40.

John Denham

Minister of State

Home Office

16th July 2002

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### PART I — OFFENCES ATTRACTING £80 PENALTY

| Offence creating provision | Description of offence |
| --- | --- |
| Section 31 of the Fire Services Act [1947 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1947/41) | Knowingly giving a false alarm to a fire brigade |
| Section 5(2) of the Criminal Law Act [1967 (c. 58)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/58) | Wasting police time or giving false report |
| Section 43(1)(b) of the Telecommunications Act [1984 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/12) | Using public telecommunications system for sending message known to be false in order to cause annoyance |
| Section 5 of the Public Order Act [1986 (c. 64)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64) | Behaviour likely to cause harassment, alarm or distress |

### PART II — OFFENCES ATTRACTING £40 PENALTY

| Offence creating provision | Description of offence |
| --- | --- |
| Section 12 of the Licensing Act [1872 (c. 94)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1872/94) | Being drunk in a highway, other public place or licensed premises |
| Section 80 of the Explosives Act [1875 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1875/17) | Throwing fireworks in a thoroughfare |
| Section 55 of the [British Transport Commission Act 1949 (c.xxix)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1949/29) | Trespassing on a railway |
| Section 56 of the [British Transport Commission Act 1949 (c.xxix)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1949/29) | Throwing stones etc. at trains or other things on railways |
| Section 169C(3) of the Licensing Act [1964 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/26) | Buying or attempting to buy alcohol for consumption in a bar in licensed premises by a person under 18 |
| Section 91 of the Criminal Justice Act [1967 (c. 80)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/80) | Disorderly behaviour while drunk in a public place |
| Section 12 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act [2001 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16) | Consumption of alcohol in designated public place |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the amount of penalty payable in respect of a penalty offence, in accordance with section 3 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act [2001 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16). “Penalty offence” is defined in section 1 of that Act.
