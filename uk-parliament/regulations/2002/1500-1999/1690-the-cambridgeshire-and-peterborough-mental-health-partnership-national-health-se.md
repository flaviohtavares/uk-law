# The Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Lifespan Health Care Cambridge National Health Service Trust and the North West Anglia Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Amendment Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2002/647](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/647) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2002 No. 1690

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Lifespan Health Care Cambridge National Health Service Trust and the North West Anglia Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Amendment Order 2002

Made

26th June 2002

Coming into force

4th July 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3, of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, [^f00003], and having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of and paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to that Act [^f00004], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Lifespan Health Care Cambridge National Health Service Trust and the North West Anglia Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Amendment Order 2002 and, shall come into force on 4th July 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Lifespan Health Care Cambridge National Health Service Trust and the North West Anglia Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00005].

## 2. Amendment of article 2 of the Order

In article 2 of the Order for “Cambridgeshire and Peterborough National Health Service Trust” there shall be substituted “Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hazel Blears

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health

26th June 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Lifespan Health Care Cambridge National Health Service Trust and the North West Anglia Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002 to correct the name of the National Health Service Trust established by that Order.
