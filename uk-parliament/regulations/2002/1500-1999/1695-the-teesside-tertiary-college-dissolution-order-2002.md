# The Teesside Tertiary College (Dissolution) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1695

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Teesside Tertiary College (Dissolution) Order 2002

Made

1st July 2002

Laid before Parliament

3rd July 2002

Coming into force

1st August 2002

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education and Skills has received a proposal from the Learning and Skills Council for England, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the dissolution under section 27 of the Act of the further education corporation known as Teesside Tertiary College[^f00002] (“the corporation”).

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 27 of the Act, the Secretary of State, after consulting the corporation and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Middlesbrough College[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Teesside Tertiary College (Dissolution) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st August 2002.

**2.** On 1st August 2002 the corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to Middlesbrough College being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

**3.** Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st August 2002 as if the references in that section—

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;
- (b) to the operative date were to 1st August 2002;
- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and
- (d) to the corporation were to Middlesbrough College.

Ivan Lewis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Skills

1st July 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st August 2002 the further education corporation established to conduct Teesside Tertiary College. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Middlesbrough College and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
