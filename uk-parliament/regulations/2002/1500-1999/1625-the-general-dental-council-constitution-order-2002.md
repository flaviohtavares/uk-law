# The General Dental Council (Constitution) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1625

DENTISTS

The General Dental Council (Constitution) Order 2002

Made

26th June 2002

Laid before Parliament

27th June 2002

Coming into force

18th July 2002

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 26th day of June 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 1(2A) and (2B) of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00001], and all other powers enabling her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the General Dental Council (Constitution) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 18th July 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Dentists Act 1984;
- “the new Council” means the Council as constituted under section 1(2A) of the Act;
- “the old Council” means the Council as constituted prior to the date on which the amendments made to the Act by Part II of the Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2001[^f00002] are wholly in force.

## 2. Constitution of the Council

The numbers of each of the three categories of member mentioned in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the Act shall be as follows—

- (a) 15 members who are registered dentists;
- (b) 4 members who are members of classes of dental auxiliaries established by regulations under the Act;
- (c) 10 lay members.

## 3. Appointment of lay members

Her Majesty may appoint as lay members persons who She, on the advice of Her Privy Council, considers to have such qualifications or experience as will be of value to the new Council in the performance of their duties.

## 4. Transitional provision

Until the date on which the amendments made to the Act by Part II of the Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2001 are wholly in force, any rules which are to be made pursuant to paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to the Act or, anything required to be done, for the purposes of the election to the new Council of the members mentioned in paragraph 1(1)(a) and (b) of that Schedule and of the President of the new Council may be made or, as the case may be, be done by the old Council and on or after that date shall have effect as if made or, as the case may be, done by the new Council.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order provides for the numbers of each of the three categories of members of the General Dental Council constituted under section 1(2A) of the Dentists Act 1984. Article 3 provides for the appointment of lay members of the Council and Article 4 makes transitional provision.
