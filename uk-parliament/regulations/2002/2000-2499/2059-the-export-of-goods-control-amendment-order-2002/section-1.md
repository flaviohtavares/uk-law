# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment) Order 2002, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 28th August 2002.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1994[^f00003].
