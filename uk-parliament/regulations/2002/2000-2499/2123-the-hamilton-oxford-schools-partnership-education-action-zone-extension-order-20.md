# The Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2123

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made

8th August 2002

Laid before Parliament

14th August 2002

Coming into force

6th September 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the power conferred upon on the Secretary of State by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order shall be cited as the Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 6th September 2002.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00001].

## 3. The extension of the Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone

The Hamilton Oxford Schools Partnership Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 6th September 2002.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

8th August 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)
