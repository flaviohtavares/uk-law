# The Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2294 (C. 74)

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

CRIMINAL LAW

The Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made

5th September 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 3(2) of the Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

**2.** Sections 1 and 2 of the Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002 shall come into force on 4th October 2002.

John Denham

Minister of State

Home Office

5th September 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 1 and 2 of the Mobile Telephones (Re-programming) Act 2002 into force on 4th October 2002.
