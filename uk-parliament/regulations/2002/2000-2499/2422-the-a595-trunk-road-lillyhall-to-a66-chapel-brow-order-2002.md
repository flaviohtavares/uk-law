# The A595 Trunk Road (Lillyhall to A66 Chapel Brow) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2422

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A595 Trunk Road (Lillyhall to A66 Chapel Brow) Order 2002

Made

19th September 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the A595 Trunk Road (Lillyhall to A66 Chapel Brow) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

(2) In this Order, all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway.

**2.** The length of highway described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road from the date when this Order comes into force.

**3.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan numbered HA 10/OD/341, marked “The A595 Trunk Road (Lillyhall to A66 Chapel Brow) Order 2002”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the DTLR Records Management Branch, Floor 13 (IMD) Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kevin Lasbury

A Divisional Director, Highways Agency

19th September 2002

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF HIGHWAY BECOMING A TRUNK ROAD

The length of highway becoming a trunk road is:

(1) The A595 from its junction with A596 at Lillyhall in the County of Cumbria to its junction with A66 at Chapel Brow, a distance of 5.7 kilometres.
