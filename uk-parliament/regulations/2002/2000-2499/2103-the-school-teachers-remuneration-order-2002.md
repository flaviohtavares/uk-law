# The School Teachers' Remuneration Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2103

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The School Teachers' Remuneration Order 2002

Made

8th August 2002

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2002

Coming into force

1st September 2002

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 5(3) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the School Teachers' Remuneration Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st September 2002.

**2.** In this Order—

- “authority” means a local education authority; and
- “Excellence Fellowship” means a project agreed by a school and a higher education institution to enable teachers to encourage pupils at the school to undertake higher education courses.

**3.** For the purposes of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991, a personal bonus payment made by a governing body of a school maintained by an authority to a school teacher who undertakes an Excellence Fellowship shall not be regarded as remuneration.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

8th August 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (the Act), the Secretary of State is authorised to make pay and conditions orders containing provisions about the statutory conditions of employment of school teachers, including their remuneration.

This Order provides that for the purposes of the Act personal bonus payments to school teachers in taking part in the Excellence Fellowships scheme with a higher education institution shall not be regarded as remuneration.
