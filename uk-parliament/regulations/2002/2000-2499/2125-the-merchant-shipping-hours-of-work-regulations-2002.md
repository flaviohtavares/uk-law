These Regulations implement—

the majority of the provisions of Council Directive [1999/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0063) concerning the Agreement on the organisation of working time of seafarers concluded by the European Community Shipowners’ Association and the Federation of Transport Workers’ Unions in the European Union (OJ L 167, 2.7.99, p.33) and

European Parliament and Council Directive [1999/95/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0095) concerning the enforcement of provisions in respect of seafarers’ hours of work on board ships calling at Community ports (OJ L 014, 20.1.2000, p.29).

The Regulations:

require employers to ensure seafarers have at least the specified minimum hours of rest (regulations 4 to 6 and 8),

require records to be kept of seafarers’ daily hours of rest,

prohibit the employment on a ship of a person under 16 years of age,

establish seafarers’ entitlement to annual leave, and

make provision about the enforcement of the Regulations.

The Regulations are made under the powers contained in the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 except in respect of amendments in Schedule 2, where the power is provided by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.
