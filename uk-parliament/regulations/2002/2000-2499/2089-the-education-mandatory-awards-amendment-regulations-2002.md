# The Education (Mandatory Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2089

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Mandatory Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made

6th August 2002

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2002

Coming into force

1st September 2002

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 4(2) of, and paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to, the Education Act 1962[^f00001], and by sections 3(1) and (3) of the Education Act 1973[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Mandatory Awards) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 1st September 2002.

**2.** The Education (Mandatory Awards) Regulations 2002[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with these Regulations.

**3.** In paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 for “subject to paragraph (2)” substitute “subject to paragraph (3)”.

**4.** (1) Schedule 5 (Sandwich Courses) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(1)(e) omit “a necessary”.

(3) For paragraph (1)(1)(g)(i) substitute—

> - (i) a Health Authority or a Strategic Health Authority established pursuant to section 8 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00004] or a Special Health Authority established pursuant to section 11 of that Act[^f00005];

.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

6th August 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Mandatory Awards) Regulations 2002.

The amendments to paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 and 1(1)(e) of Schedule 5 correct drafting errors.

The amendment to paragraph 1(1)(g) of Schedule 5 refers to the new Health Authorities and Strategic Health Authorities established under the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002.
