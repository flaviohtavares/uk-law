# The National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (Supplementary, Consequential etc. Provisions) Regulations 2002, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (43).** Text as at 2002-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/2469
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Change of name of Health Authorities[^key-66c3e72b08712ec70d79d0802f45421b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the name of each Health Authority listed in column 1 of Schedule 1 to the Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Order 2002[^f00002] for “Health Authority” in each place it occurs there is substituted “Strategic Health Authority”.

(2) The changes of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before or on or after the date on which these Regulations come into force) which refers to the Authorities by their previous names;

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the Authorities under their previous names shall be construed as if referring to them under their new names.
