# The National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (Supplementary, Consequential etc. Provisions) Regulations 2002, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (43).** Text as at 2002-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/2469
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Continuing validity of forms[^key-fc369f0c43fa28afd08270000792d78b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding the amendment made by Schedule 5 to the form of certificate set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976[^f00010], a form which complies with those regulations as in force on 30th September 2002 shall continue to be valid.
