# The National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (Supplementary, Consequential etc. Provisions) Regulations 2002, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (43).** Text as at 2002-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/2469
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Community Health Councils (Access to Information) Act 1988[^c-39b0be5b18e70ff416aea80dd3c50b7b]

*Extent: E+W*

Until section 22 of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 comes into force, the reference in section 1(6)(a) of the Community Health Councils (Access to Information) Act 1988[^f00009] to any Health Authority is to have effect as if it included a reference to any Strategic Health Authority.
