## 19. Repeals and revocations[^c-0b4104863bc6a9d623d1a8eeafd358e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments listed in Schedule 13 are repealed to the extent there specified.

(2) The National Health Service Trusts and Primary Care Trusts (Pharmaceutical Services Remuneration-Special Arrangement) Order 2000[^f00011] is revoked.
