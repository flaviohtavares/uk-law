# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2062

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made

5th August 2002

Laid before Parliament

6th August 2002

Coming into force

2nd September 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 48(1) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 2nd September 2002.

(2) These Regulations shall apply only in relation to England.

## 2. Amendment of the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002

(1) The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) The following shall be added to Schedule 4:—

> **28.** Expenditure incurred by a governing body in the exercise of the power conferred by section 27 of the Education Act 2002.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

5th August 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002, by adding a provision to Schedule 4 to enable local education authority schemes for financing schools to include provisions in relation to the expenditure by governing bodies on community facilities under section 27 of the Education Act 2002.
