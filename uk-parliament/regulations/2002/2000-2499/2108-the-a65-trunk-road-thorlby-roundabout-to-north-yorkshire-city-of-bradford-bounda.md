# The A65 Trunk Road (Thorlby Roundabout to North Yorkshire/City of Bradford Boundary) (Detrunking) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2108

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A65 Trunk Road (Thorlby Roundabout to North Yorkshire/City of Bradford Boundary) (Detrunking) Order 2002

Made

6th August 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** This Order may be cited as The A65 Trunk Road (Thorlby Roundabout to North Yorkshire/City of Bradford Boundary) (Detrunking) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

**2.** In this Order—

- (i) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;
- (ii) “the plan” means the plan numbered HA10/OD/351, marked “The A65 Trunk Road (Thorlby Roundabout to North Yorkshire/City of Bradford Boundary) (Detrunking) Order 2002.”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, and deposited at ODPM-DFT Records Management Branch, Ashdown House, 123 Victoria Street, London SW1 6DE;
- (iii) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State; and
- (iv) “the trunk road” means the Doncaster to Kendal Trunk (A65).

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which this Order comes into force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kevin Lasbury

A Divisional Director, Highways Agency

6th August 2002

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is shown by broad black dashes on the plan and is situated between Thorlby Roundabout (marked “A” on the plan) and the North Yorkshire/City of Bradford boundary (marked “B” on the plan) and measures approximately 9.4 km in length.
