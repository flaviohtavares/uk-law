# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2050 (C.66)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

POLICE

The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002

Made

2nd August 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002.

(2) In this Order “the 2001 Act” means the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001.

**2.** The following provisions of the 2001 Act shall come into force on 12th August 2002—

- (a) section 1 (offences leading to penalties on the spot), in so far as not already in force; and
- (b) section 2 and sections 4 to 11 (penalty notices and penalties, procedure and interpretation).

**3.** Section 127 (Pensions for ACPO staff) of the 2001 Act shall come into force on 1st September 2002.

Charles Falconer

Minister of State

Home Office

2nd August 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th August 2002 the remaining sections of Chapter 1 of Part 1 (on the spot penalties for disorderly behaviour) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001. It also brings into force section 127 (pensions for ACPO staff) of the Act on 1st September 2002.

(This note is not part of the Order)
