# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3145 (C.106)

PROCEEDS OF CRIME

The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2002

Made

17th December 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 458(1) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2002.

**2.** The following provisions of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 shall come into force on 30th December 2002—

- (a) section 456 (Amendments), for the purposes of the provisions of Schedule 11 to the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 commenced by this Order; and
- (b) paragraph 10(1), (3) and (4) of Schedule 11.

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

17th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 30th December 2002, paragraph 10(1), (3) and (4) of Schedule 11 to the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 which makes changes to the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 so that parties to cash forfeiture proceedings under Chapter 3 of Part 5 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 may receive legal aid.

(This note is not part of the Order)
