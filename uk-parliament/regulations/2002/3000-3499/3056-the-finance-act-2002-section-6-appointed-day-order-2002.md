# The Finance Act 2002, section 6, (Appointed Day) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3056 (C.103)

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Finance Act 2002, section 6, (Appointed Day) Order 2002

Made

11th December 2002

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 6(3) of the Finance Act 2002[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2002, section 6, (Appointed Day) Order 2002.

**2.** The day appointed as the day on which section 6(1) of the Finance Act 2002, so far as relating to paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to that Act, comes into force is 1st April 2003.

T D Byrne

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House 22,

Upper Ground LONDON SE1 9PJ

11th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 6(1) of the Finance Act [2002 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/23) into force on 1st April 2003, insofar as it is not already in force. Section 6 gives effect to Schedule 3 to that Act (which makes provision for regulating trade in certain heavy oil on which rebate of excise duty has been allowed). Schedule 3 provides for the insertion of new sections 23A, 23B and 24AA into the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act [1979 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5) and for other consequential amendments.
