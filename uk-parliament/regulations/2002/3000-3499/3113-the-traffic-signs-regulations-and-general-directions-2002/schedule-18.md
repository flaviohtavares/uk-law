## SCHEDULE 18 (affected by repeal, see note) — INTERPRETATION OF “UNLADEN VEHICLE” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

**1.**[^c-498757816abd9887a1682b12e080bfa1] In these Regulations “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**unladen vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means, subject to paragraph 3— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a motor vehicle not drawing a trailer or otherwise forming part of a combination of vehicles; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a combination of vehicles comprising one motor vehicle drawing one or more trailers, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

in relation to which the conditions specified in paragraph 2 below are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

**2.**[^c-96ebe00e9e05b5a85b9bb365a8f0dc82] The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) that the motor vehicle is a motor car, a heavy motor car, or a motor tractor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) that no goods or burden are being carried in the motor vehicle or combination of vehicles; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) that not more than 2 persons (excluding the driver) are being carried in the motor vehicle or combination of vehicles. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

**3.**[^c-2ba3e95b6ccd0e9d90986bf186908a9f] For the purposes of this Schedule, the following are to be deemed not to constitute goods or burden— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) in the case of a motor vehicle constructed or adapted for use and used for the conveyance of a machine or device which is built in as part of the vehicle, that machine or device and any articles used in connection with it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) water, fuel or accumulators used for the purpose of the supply of power for the propulsion of a vehicle or, as the case may be, of any vehicle by which a trailer is drawn, and loose tools and loose equipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
