# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 41

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Light signals for the control of tramcars (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b5a5542eb4b6160ae59e2074f3d8f86e][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Light signals for the control of tramcars shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 3013; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) display the aspects shown in diagrams 3013.1, 3013.2, 3013.3, 3013.4 and 3013.5 in the sequence prescribed by paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The sequence for the illumination of the light signals prescribed by paragraph (1) shall be as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the horizontal line shown in diagram 3013.1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the vertical line shown in diagram 3013.2 or either of the diagonal lines shown in diagram 3013.3 or 3013.4, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the central circle shown in diagram 3013.5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) When the light signals prescribed by paragraph (1) (“tram signals”) are affixed to the light signals mentioned in regulation 33 (“standard signals”) in accordance with any of the options shown in diagrams 3000.7, 3000.8, 3000.9 and 3000.10 their aspect may be such that they convey to the driver of a tramcar a different significance from that conveyed at the same time in accordance with regulation 36 to the drivers of other vehicular traffic by the aspect of the standard signals to which the tram signals are affixed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
