# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Warning lights at school crossing places (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b6dde0b885baf84725b21b675664b108][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A sign for conveying a warning to vehicular traffic that a school crossing place lies ahead and is being patrolled by a school crossing patrol or is otherwise in use by such children— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) shall be a light signal of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4004, each lamp of which when operated shall show an intermittent amber light at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute and in such a manner that one light is always shown when the other light is not shown; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) may be erected on or near part of the road in advance of a crossing place in relation to oncoming traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
