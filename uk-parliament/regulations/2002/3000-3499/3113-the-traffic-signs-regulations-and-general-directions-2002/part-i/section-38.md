# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 38

## 38. Significance of light signals prescribed by regulation 37(1) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-345ce1242ac6a73571c43f3144242c02][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The significance of the light signals prescribed by regulation 37(1) shall be as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Subject to paragraph (5), the signals shown in diagram 6032.1 shall convey the prohibition that vehicular traffic on the carriageway beside which the signals are mounted shall not proceed beyond the signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Subject to paragraph (5), when displayed over the carriageway of a road, the signals shown in diagram 6031.1 shall convey the prohibition that vehicular traffic proceeding in the traffic lane immediately below the signals shall not proceed beyond them in that lane. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), when displayed over an actively managed hard shoulder, the signals shown in diagram 6031.1 shall convey the prohibition that vehicular traffic shall not enter the hard shoulder beyond the signals for any purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) The prohibition mentioned in paragraph (2), (3) or (4), as the case may be, shall not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to a vehicle being used— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) for any of the purposes mentioned in regulation 36(1)(b); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] for traffic officer purposes, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

  when the observance of that prohibition would be likely to hinder the use of that vehicle for the purpose for which it is being used; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to a vehicle being used for special forces purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
