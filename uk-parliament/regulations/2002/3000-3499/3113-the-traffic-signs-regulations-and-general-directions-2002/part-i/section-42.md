# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 42

## 42. Significance of light signals prescribed by regulation 41 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-12d558041cf9ffe5a8a8d2e597640ff7][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The significance of the light signals prescribed by regulation 41 shall be as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the aspect shown in diagram 3013.1 shall convey the prohibition that a tramcar shall not proceed beyond the stop line; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the aspect shown in diagram 3013.2 shall indicate that a tramcar may proceed beyond the stop line and proceed straight ahead; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the aspect shown in diagram 3013.3 shall indicate that a tramcar may proceed beyond the stop line and proceed to the left; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the aspect shown in diagram 3013.4 shall indicate that a tramcar may proceed beyond the stop line and proceed to the right; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the aspect shown in diagram 3013.5 shall convey the prohibition that a tramcar shall not proceed beyond the stop line except that, as respects a tramcar which is so close to the stop line that it cannot safely be stopped without proceeding beyond the stop line, it shall convey the same indication as the aspect which was shown immediately before it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
