# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Refuge indicator lamps (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8c465f8cd9625373180478c8b7660f35][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A lamp in the form of an illuminated spherical globe for conveying the warning that drivers of vehicles are approaching a street refuge may be placed subject to the following conditions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the globe shall be white; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the globe shall have a diameter of not less than 275 nor more than 335 millimetres; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the height of the centre of the globe above the surface of the carriageway in the immediate vicinity shall be not less than 3800 millimetres nor more than 5000 millimetres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
