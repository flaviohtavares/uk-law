# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 37

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Light signals for the control of vehicular traffic on motorways and all-purpose dual carriageway roads (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e8cdc77846e6fb837908ecd739dd0a19][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (4), light signals for the control of vehicular traffic entering or proceeding along a motorway, shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 6031.1 or 6032.1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) operated in accordance with the requirements specified in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The requirements are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) each lamp shall show an intermittent red light at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute, and in such a manner that the lights of one vertical pair are always shown when the lights of the other vertical pair are not shown; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the red cross or the white symbol shown in diagram 6031.1 or 6032.1 shall be illuminated by a steady light when the red lights are flashing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Light signals for the control of vehicular traffic entering or proceeding along an all-purpose dual carriageway road may also be the size, colour and type prescribed by paragraph (1) and operated in accordance with the requirements specified in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Light signals for the control of vehicular traffic— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) entering a motorway by means of a slip road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) entering a motorway which is a roundabout may, instead of complying with paragraphs (1) and (2), be of the size, colour and type prescribed by regulation 33 or 34. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
