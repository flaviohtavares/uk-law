# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 45

## 45. Warning signal for motorways and all-purpose dual carriageway roads (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0a9ad7ede8b7964b1b96057256ac0bd0][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A traffic sign for conveying the warning specified in paragraph (2) to vehicular traffic on a motorway or an all-purpose dual carriageway road shall be a light signal of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 6023. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The warning conveyed by the light signal shall be that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) there is a hazard ahead on the motorway or all-purpose dual carriageway road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) drivers should drive at a speed which does not exceed 30 mph until they are certain that the hazard has been passed or removed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) When the light signal prescribed by this regulation is operated, each lamp shall show an intermittent amber light at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute and in such a manner that one light is always shown when the other light is not shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
