# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 24

## 24. Bus symbols (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f416f20363fa312d9d883272a8d9cbb4][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A bus symbol when incorporated into any sign refers to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) motor vehicles constructed or adapted to carry more than 8 passengers (exclusive of the driver); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) local buses not so constructed or adapted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) In the signs shown in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] diagrams 958, 958A, 959 and 959A[^key-565914cfd0f2684e280894eead1483a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and in the permitted variants of diagrams 953, 953.1 and 960, the word “local” on a bus symbol indicates that the road or the traffic lane on or near which the sign has been placed shall be used only by local buses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**bus symbol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a symbol depicting a bus in the form shown in diagram 952, 953 or 962.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
