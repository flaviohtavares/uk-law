# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 9

## 9. Temporary obstructions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-47d8a1a3521fd3c6d449543bf055aa8f][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these Regulations shall have effect so as to authorise any persons not otherwise authorised to do so to place on or near a road any object or device for warning traffic of a temporary obstruction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
