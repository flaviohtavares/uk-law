# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Cattle crossing signs and warning lights (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7cce6b4e683d4405a17746a99469550e][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A sign of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , and which has ES compliant signal heads,[^c-f45ef922317573e7d7e4479933d9c38e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] may be erected on or near a road in advance of a place in that road where cattle under the supervision of a herdsman on their way from one part of a farm to another cross the road (“a cattle crossing”) to convey to oncoming traffic the warning specified in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The warning conveyed by the sign shall be that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a cattle crossing lies ahead and may be in use; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) traffic should be prepared to stop. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) When the sign is operated, each lamp shall show an intermittent amber light at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute and in such a manner that one light is always shown when the other light is not shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
