# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 47A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47A. Light signals at portable signal-controlled pedestrian facilities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c6a0ca0490a983e12e66d0e2d2ad685e][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation “the portable crossing”, in relation to a sign, means the portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility in relation to which the sign is placed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A sign for conveying to pedestrian traffic the warning and information specified in paragraph (4) shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4002.1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) comply with the requirements of paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The requirements are that the sign is so designed and constructed that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the red figure in the sign (“the red signal”) can be internally illuminated by a steady light; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the green figure in the sign (“the green signal”) can be internally illuminated by a steady light; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) when one signal is illuminated the other is not; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the green signal is illuminated only when there is at the same time conveyed to vehicular traffic a prohibition against entering the portable crossing and the prohibition is indicated by the light signals prescribed by regulation 35; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the signal heads are ES compliant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) The red signal, whilst it is illuminated, shall indicate the period during which, in the interests of safety, pedestrians should not use the portable crossing and the green signal, whilst it is illuminated, shall indicate the period during which pedestrians may use the portable crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) A device for causing the green signal in the sign shown in diagram 4002.1 to become illuminated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) be of the size, colour and type of only the part of the sign shown in diagram 4003.1 which includes the push button and the legend above it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] whilst the red signal is illuminated, in some way indicate to pedestrians whether the push button device has been activated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) The sign shown in diagram 4003 shall, whilst the word “WAIT” is illuminated, convey the same indication as the red signal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) Any audible or tactile signal emitted by a device provided in conjunction with the green signal for the benefit of disabled persons shall convey to pedestrians the same indication as the green signal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
