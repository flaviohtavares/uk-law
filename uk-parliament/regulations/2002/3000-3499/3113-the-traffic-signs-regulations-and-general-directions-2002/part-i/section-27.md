# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Road marking shown in diagram 1001.3: zig-zag lines—no stopping (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-303234d2a2cb1ce7a753593211ef9be8][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation and regulation 28— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- “**controlled area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a length of carriageway— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (a) which is adjacent to a signal-controlled crossing facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility[^key-87a0e42eedd885ad5e27124db73f3db9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and has a zig-zag line marked along each of its edges (with or without zig-zag lines also marked down its centre); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (b) in or near which no other signs or markings have been placed except ones comprised in the combination of signs and markings indicating the presence of the facility or shown in diagram 610, 611, 612, 613, 616, 810, 1029 or 1062; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- “**local service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” does not include an excursion or tour as defined by section 137(1) of the Transport Act 1985; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- “**vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” does not include a pedal bicycle not having a sidecar attached to it, whether or not additional means of propulsion by mechanical power are attached to the bicycle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) and without prejudice to regulation 28, a zig-zag line shall convey the requirement that the driver of a vehicle shall not cause any part of it to stop in the controlled area in which it is marked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not prohibit the driver of a vehicle from stopping it in a controlled area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) if the driver has stopped it for the purpose of complying with an indication given by a light signal for the control of vehicular traffic or the direction of a constable in uniform *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , a traffic officer in uniform[^key-a6a7742ecc5611bccbaa6f7cc8e3539f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or a traffic warden; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) if the driver is prevented from proceeding by circumstances beyond his control or it is necessary for him to stop to avoid injury or damage to persons or property; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-c61e0a787a5cb49b816c010d779fd711]...
- (c) when the vehicle is being used for police, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] fire brigade *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d5c881dd8de960e43c4c83cc9e70df6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] Scottish Fire and Rescue Service[^key-0ff8649c233c6dfb5cd1b4a0da2d7e7e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or, in England and Wales, fire and rescue authority,[^key-43f41216230bb03f28384903bb16256c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-853fa781f1b48f38cc842978b9875bba]... special forces or traffic officer purposes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-6a893ff0d1594843769ad4c7a0685235]..;[^key-4dd7d1f5917c250b1ab20172653b4c79] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d01fc8241d940aba0e2803f182e08a9b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] when the vehicle is being used for ambulance purposes or for the purpose of providing a response to an emergency at the request of an NHS ambulance service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) as regards England and Wales, and so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which relate to reserved matters (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998), when the vehicle is being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or[^key-5974f57c3f5afc939e8d85ce4155fadd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which do not (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998) relate to reserved matters, when the vehicle is being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Paragraph (2) does not prohibit the driver of a vehicle from stopping it in a controlled area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) for so long as may be necessary to enable the vehicle to be used for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) any operation involving building, demolition or excavation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the removal of any obstruction to traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the maintenance, improvement or reconstruction of a road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the laying, erection, alteration, repair or cleaning in or near the controlled area of any sewer or of any main, pipe or apparatus for the supply of gas, water or electricity, or of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus[^key-d8be35712033def2182935ec1972d9fa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] kept installed for the purposes of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications code system *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or of any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] lawfully kept installed in any position; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in the provision of a local service, and the vehicle, having proceeded past the light signals to which the controlled area relates, is waiting in that area in order to take up or set down passengers; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) if he stops the vehicle for the purpose of making a left or right turn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
