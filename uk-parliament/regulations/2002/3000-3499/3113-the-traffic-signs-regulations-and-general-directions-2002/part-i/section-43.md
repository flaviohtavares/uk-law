# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 43

## 43. Meaning of stop line and references to light signals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0392af167b0fa9a6ad97ceec0d09ef0e][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**stop line *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” in relation to light signals for the control of vehicular traffic means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) in relation to any vehicle except a tramcar the road marking shown in diagram 1001 placed in conjunction with the light signals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in relation to a tramcar, the road marking shown in diagram 1001.1 placed in conjunction with those light signals, or when that marking has not been so placed, the marking shown in diagram 1001 so placed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Where the road marking shown in diagram 1001.2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or 1001.2A[^c-7586a512e5c6f4b0c7e28803098537c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] has been placed in conjunction with light signals, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**stop line *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” in relation to those light signals means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the first stop line, in the case of a vehicle (other than a pedal cycle proceeding in the cycle lane *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or through the cycle entry[^c-db83f9d3c06f720fac4e3d24988faf3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]) which has not proceeded beyond that line; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the second stop line, in the case of a vehicle which has proceeded beyond the first stop line or of a pedal cycle proceeding in the cycle lane *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or through the cycle entry[^c-c5f78ce833e1cccfb0b9eb3ea3a46cce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b].

(3) Where no stop line has been provided in conjunction with light signals or the stop line is not visible, references in relation to those signals to the “stop line” are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) in a case where the sign shown in diagram 7011, 7011.1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , 7011.2[^c-08367bd5b33a012ba9ff549b4550b9ee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or 7027 is placed in conjunction with the light signals, to be treated as references to that sign; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in any other case, to be treated as references to the post or other structure on which the primary signals are mounted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) A reference in this regulation or in regulations 33 to 42 to light signals, to the signals or to a signal of a particular colour is, where secondary signals as well as primary signals have been placed, a reference to the light signals displayed by both the primary and secondary signals or, as the case may be, by the primary signals operating without the secondary signals or by the secondary signals operating without the primary signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) “**primary signals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means light signals erected on or near the carriageway of a road and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) where a stop line is placed in conjunction with the signals, sited beyond that line and near one end or both ends of the line; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] where there is no stop line, sited at either edge or both edges of the carriageway or part of the carriageway which is in use by traffic approaching and controlled by the signals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) “**secondary signals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means light signals erected on or near the carriageway facing traffic approaching from the direction of the primary signals but sited beyond those signals as viewed from the direction of travel of such traffic; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) in paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) “**the first stop line *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the transverse white line or lines appearing below the pedal cycle symbol in the road markings shown in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] diagram 1001.2A or in[^c-bfdf795508a82ebbb7a6b6590930cf72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] either version of diagram 1001.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] “**the second stop line *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the transverse white line appearing above the pedal cycle symbol in the road markings shown *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] in diagram 1001.2A or in either version of diagram 1001.2[^c-310a0c337a4eaa75ee0429c51294c9ea] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]; [^c-118cd0ee13e7ba5fcd38ea32faeff6e5]...
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] “**the cycle lane *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the lane so marked in either version of that diagram *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and[^c-10089ac8768bda6123a74c5d1fdc1211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] “the cycle entry” means the diagonal broken line shown in diagram 1001.2A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
