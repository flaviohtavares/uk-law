# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Green arrow light signals for the control of vehicular traffic (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ea2ae3ebb7746c186fdedfe124e380db][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A lens or lenses of the size and colour shown in diagram 3001.2 or 3001.3 which, when illuminated, shows a green arrow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) may be substituted for the lens showing the green light in the light signals referred to in regulation 33(1) using any of the methods shown in diagram 3000.8 or 3000.10; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) may be affixed to the light signals referred to in regulation 33(1) or to those signals as altered in accordance with sub-paragraph (a) using any of the methods shown in diagrams 3000.7, 3000.8, 3000.9 and 3000.10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The direction of the arrow shown in indication B in diagram 3001.2 or 3001.3 may be varied so that the head of the arrow points to any position lying between indication A and indication C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The direction of the arrow shown in indication D in diagram 3001.2 or 3001.3 may be varied so that the head of the arrow points to any position lying between indication C and indication E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
