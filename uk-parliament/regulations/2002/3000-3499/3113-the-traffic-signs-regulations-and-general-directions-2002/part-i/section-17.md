# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 17. Permitted variants of upright signs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94ae0ff8f661ff91b60f0a25ff191d64][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to a sign shown in a diagram in a Schedule other than Schedule 6 and such a sign is in this regulation called an “upright sign”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The form of an upright sign shall, if the circumstances in which it is placed so require, or may, if appropriate in those circumstances, be varied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) in the manner (if any) allowed or required in item 4 of the untitled table below or beside the diagram; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in the manner allowed or required in column (3) of an item in Schedule 16, if the diagram is one whose number is given in column (2) of that item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) A symbol in the form of a prescribed sign to which direction 7 of the Traffic Signs General Directions 2002 applies shall not be incorporated in a sign in accordance with item 31 of Schedule 16, except in circumstances where it could be placed as a sign in accordance with that direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) A symbol incorporated as mentioned in paragraph (3) shall or may be varied in the same manner as the sign which the symbol represents or from which it is derived. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) In each of the signs shown in diagrams 780A, 780.1A and 780.2A the safe height shown on the sign shall be varied where necessary so that it is between 1 foot 3 inches and 2 feet (380 to 600 millimetres) less than the height of the lowest part of the overhead wire, of which the sign gives warning, over the highest part of the surface of the carriageway beneath that wire. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) Where a sign shown in a diagram in Schedule 7 indicates a road or a route, and that road or route is temporarily closed, there may be affixed to the sign or to that part of the sign where that road or route is indicated, in order to cancel temporarily the indication, a board coloured red and displaying in white lettering the words “Road temporarily closed” or “Route temporarily closed”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) In this paragraph and paragraphs (8) and (9)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) “**combination sign *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a sign shown in diagram 7201, 7210, 7211.1, 7212, 7213, 7214, 7215, 7216, 7217, 7218, 7220, 7221, 7230, 7231, 7232, 7233, 7234, 7235, 7236, 7237, 7238, 7239 or 7240; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) “**panel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a sign shown in diagram 7260, 7261, 7262, 7263, 7264, 7270, 7271, 7272, 7274 or 7275 when used as part of a combination sign and references to a panel whose number is shown in a table of combinations are to a sign shown in a diagram having a number so shown; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) “**permitted combination *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means one of the combinations specified in paragraph (9); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) “**the table *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” in relation to a combination sign means the table of combinations appearing below or beside the diagram in which that sign is shown; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) “**top panel” means a panel shown at the top of a combination sign and “bottom panel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a panel shown at the bottom of such a sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) If and only if the top and bottom panels of the sign as varied together constitute a permitted combination, a combination sign may be varied in the following ways— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) by substituting for the top panel or, where a top panel is not shown, by adding as a top panel, a panel whose number is shown in item (1) of the table; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) by substituting for the bottom panel or, where a bottom panel is not shown, by adding as a bottom panel, a panel whose number is shown in item (2) of the table; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) if the word “none” appears in item (1) of the table, by omitting the top panel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) if the word “none” appears in item (2) of the table, by omitting the bottom panel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(9) Each of the following is a permitted combination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a top panel whose number appears in item (1) of a column in the table and a bottom panel whose number appears in item (2) of the same column; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a top panel whose number appears in item (1) of a column in the table and, if the word “none” appears in item (2) of the same column, no bottom panel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) a bottom panel whose number appears in item (2) of a column in the table and, if the word “none” appears in item (1) of the same column, no top panel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) if the word “none” appears in both items of the same column of the table, no top panel and no bottom panel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(10)[^key-54d9d26048225c42f8d465d5bdc64e95] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(11) Where the form of an upright sign is varied in accordance with these Regulations, the information, warning, requirement, restriction, prohibition or speed limit conveyed to traffic by the sign is varied to accord with the form of the sign as varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
