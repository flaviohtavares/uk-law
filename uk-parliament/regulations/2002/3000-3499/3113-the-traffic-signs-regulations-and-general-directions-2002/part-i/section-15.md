# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 15

## 15. Sign shown in diagram 610 and its significance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0220c806427260c7d66a6d1723ba7138][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Except as provided in paragraphs (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , (2A)[^key-8b07b7b173e6e0c25206d6871dcb04dd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , (2B)[^key-84a3874f5a01de6fe7e93ae07b657d96] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , (2C)[^key-5d5606c681c1c5cb28f924dd28057ca2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and (3), the requirement conveyed by the sign shown in diagram 610 shall be that vehicular traffic passing the sign must keep to the left of the sign where the arrow is pointed downwards to the left, or to the right of the sign where the arrow is pointed downwards to the right. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) On an occasion where a vehicle is being used for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] fire brigade *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5220894f6cae3990ccb39e18a9861438] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] Scottish Fire and Rescue Service[^key-37cf9b3cd9bc54966f6b631ac1289bd7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or, in England, fire and rescue authority[^key-50ccaeb341a12685053e4c2d1fb22f44] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or, in England or Wales, fire and rescue authority[^key-9ede86aa9c0eb8bd71d1797d401be356] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b], [^key-f4187efd64aa8bb7c54b6ccdad50b1ca]... bomb or explosive disposal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-4e4500be90815adc8a336d4c0b663b7f]... or police purposes and the observance of the requirement specified in paragraph (1) would be likely to hinder the use of that vehicle for one of those purposes then, instead of that requirement, the requirement conveyed by the sign in question shall be that the vehicle shall not proceed beyond that sign in such a manner or at such a time as to be likely to endanger any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c77f0bad92294d8e9c1ec41b29b99d3][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] On an occasion where a vehicle is being used for ambulance or national blood service purposes or for the purpose of providing a response to an emergency at the request of an NHS ambulance service and the observance of the requirement specified in paragraph (1) would be likely to hinder the use of that vehicle for one of those purposes then, instead of that requirement, the requirement conveyed by the sign in question shall be that the vehicle shall not proceed beyond that sign in such a manner or at such a time as to be likely to endanger any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2A)[^key-8c823c73c231e7bf05ff371e686667e0] As regards England and Wales, and so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which relate to reserved matters (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998), on an occasion where a vehicle is being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes and the observance of the requirement specified in paragraph (1) would be likely to hinder the use of that vehicle for those purposes then, instead of that requirement, the requirement conveyed by the sign in question shall be that the vehicle shall not proceed beyond that sign in such a manner or at such a time as to be likely to endanger any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2B)[^key-1d9c25cd10ace3040da5eb827d29699c] So far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which do not (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998) relate to reserved matters, on an occasion where a vehicle is being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes and the observance of the requirement specified in paragraph (1) would be likely to hinder the use of that vehicle for those purposes then, instead of that requirement, the requirement conveyed by the sign in question shall be that the vehicle shall not proceed beyond that sign in such a manner or at such a time as to be likely to endanger any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2C)[^key-f12cfbb281675eedbd0cf4a33a56d7e3] On an occasion where a vehicle is being used for special forces purposes the requirement conveyed by the sign in question shall be that the vehicle shall not proceed beyond that sign in such a manner or at such a time as to be likely to endanger any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The requirement specified in paragraph (1) does not apply to a tramcar or trolley vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
