# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Light signals at signal-controlled pedestrian facilities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-650b597515156abb58f5d793483a9cef][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**the crossing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”, in relation to a sign, means the signal-controlled pedestrian facility in relation to which the sign is placed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A sign for conveying to pedestrian traffic the warning and information specified in paragraph (4) shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4002.1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) consist of either a single unit of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003.1 or of two units, one comprising the upper and the other the lower part of that unit placed close together, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

and shall comply with the requirements of paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2A)[^key-3d05c543423bd842f95c923215eaee47] Where a sign consisting of either a single unit of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003.1 or of two units, one comprising the upper and the other the lower part of that unit placed close together, is placed in relation to a crossing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) any additional device for causing the green signal in that sign to become illuminated shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) be of the size, colour and type of only the part of that sign which includes the push button and the legend above it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] comply with the requirements of paragraph (3)(e) so far as relevant to it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) any additional sign for conveying to pedestrian traffic the warning and information specified in paragraph (4) shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003.1A; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] comply with the requirements of paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The requirements are that the sign is so designed and constructed that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the red figure in the sign (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**the red signal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”) can be internally illuminated by a steady light; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the green figure in the sign (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**the green signal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”) can be internally illuminated by a steady light; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) when one signal is illuminated the other is not; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the green signal is illuminated only when there is at the same time conveyed to vehicular traffic a prohibition against entering the crossing and the prohibition is indicated by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) the light signals prescribed by regulation 33 (whether or not varied in accordance with regulation 34) or 41; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] those light signals and the sign shown in diagram 606, 612, 613 or 616; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) in the case of the sign shown in diagram 4003.1, a push button or other switching device is included which, whilst the red signal is illuminated, in some way indicates to pedestrians whether it has been activated; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) in the case of the sign shown in diagram 4002.1, the signal heads are ES compliant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) The red signal, whilst it is illuminated, shall indicate the period during which, in the interests of safety, pedestrians should not use the crossing and the green signal, whilst it is illuminated, shall indicate the period during which pedestrians may use the crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5)[^key-e58350012dd5088222a873ae19af442c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) A push button device giving instructions to pedestrians on how to cause the green signal in the sign shown in diagram 4002.1 to become illuminated and explaining the significance of the red and green signals shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) The sign shown in diagram 4003 shall, whilst the word “WAIT” is illuminated, convey the same indication as the red signal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) Any audible or tactile signal emitted by a device provided in conjunction with the green signal for the benefit of disabled persons shall convey to pedestrians the same indication as the green signal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
