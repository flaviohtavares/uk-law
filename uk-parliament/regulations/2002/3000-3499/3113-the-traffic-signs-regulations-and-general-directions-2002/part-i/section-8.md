# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Authorisations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-17ea646d0ef348f9ad60f5f46131eb40][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these Regulations shall be taken to limit the powers of the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers and the National Assembly for Wales under section 64(1) and (2) of the 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00039] to authorise the erection or retention of traffic signs of a character not prescribed by these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
