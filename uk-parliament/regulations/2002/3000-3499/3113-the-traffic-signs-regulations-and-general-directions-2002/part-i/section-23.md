## 23. Bus lanes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-55221a97febff0811f3a07c8ce456863][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the signs shown in the permitted variants of diagrams 877 and 878 in which the expression “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**bus lane” appears and in diagrams 962, 962.2, 963, 963.2, 964, 1048 and 1048.1, “bus lane *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” has the meaning given in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) “Bus lane” in the signs referred to in paragraph (1) means a traffic lane reserved for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) motor vehicles constructed or adapted to carry more than 8 passengers (exclusive of the driver); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) local buses not so constructed or adapted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^c-0321631bee0b23fdb86914e29b142a71]...
- (c) pedal cycles and taxis where indicated on the sign shown in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] diagram 958, 958A, 959 or 959A[^c-f310dc872d5060beda24cbbc67625eec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and pedal cycles where indicated on the sign shown in diagram 960, 962.2, 963.2 or 1048.1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and[^c-feae4b6834c403cc6f417c319031432a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) solo motor cycles where indicated on the sign shown in diagram 958A or 959A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
