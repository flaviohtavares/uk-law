## 22. Buses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-56525838037e33d363f7f73f6293ca22][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In Part I of Schedule 19 and in the signs shown in[^key-722013f78bb517c64cdbebdfcec0c6bf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]—

- (a) the permitted variants of diagrams 618.1, 618.2, 618.3, 618.3A, 620 and 820; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) diagrams 877, 953.3. 954, 954.2, 954.3, 954.5, 954.6, 954.7, 970, 973.2, 973.3, 974, 975, 1025.1, 1025.3 and 1025.4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the permitted variants of diagram 1028.3; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) diagrams 1048, 1048.1, 1048.2, 1048.3 and 1048.4, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

the expressions “bus” and “buses” have the meanings given in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) “Buses” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] in Part I of Schedule 19 and in the signs referred to in paragraph (1)[^key-77b0612ab4e5e6d8c142c48328822685] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) motor vehicles constructed or adapted to carry more than 8 passengers (exclusive of the driver); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) local buses not so constructed or adapted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**bus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
