# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

## 56. Cones, delineators and cylinders (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4fb567122518587ae27df6fed2ac79f2][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- “**cone *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the sign shown in diagram 7101.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- “**delineator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the sign shown in diagram 7102; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- “**cylinder *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means the sign shown in diagram 7103. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2)[^c-b58e15b6330bba1e9e63d5b1a3695298] A cone shall consist of a conically shaped device— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the base of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) is of any single colour; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] is a polygon, having not more than eight sides, which would be contained wholly within a circle with a diameter of three quarters of the height of the cone; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) which, subject to paragraph (15), conforms to British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009 or to a corresponding EEA Standard. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The part of a cone coloured white shall, subject to paragraph (5), be illuminated with white retroreflecting material and the part coloured red may be illuminated with red retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Information about the ownership of a cone may be moulded— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) into the base in characters not more than 80 mm high in the same colour as the base; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) into the conical body in characters not more than 40 mm high in the same colour as that body; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) as mentioned in both sub-paragraphs (a) and (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) Information about the manufacture of a cone, required to comply with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009[^c-59a77d4ab4f1b5e75aa9932f5a38a0b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or a corresponding EEA Standard and occupying an area not exceeding 30 square centimetres, may be indicated on the part coloured white in characters not exceeding 5 millimetres in height, leaving at least 90% of the remaining area of white colour illuminated with white retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) A rotating device may be mounted on top of a cone and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) that device shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) be red and not reflectorised; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] display one or more signs shown in either diagram 560 or diagram 561; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) those signs shall be coloured amber, intermittently whilst rotating and constantly whilst static. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) A delineator shall consist of a flat device— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) of which the side intended to be exposed to traffic is coloured as shown in diagram 7102 and the reverse side is either so coloured or coloured only grey or only red; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b)[^c-33462a509850dfb27ff1317fac96aa63] which conforms to British Standard BS 8442:2006 or to a corresponding EEA Standard; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) of which the base is of any single colour, except that a reflectorised white line 100 millimetres wide at an angle of not more than 60 degrees to the road surface may be marked on one side of the base at right angles to the face of the delineator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) The white part of a delineator shall, subject to paragraph (10), be illuminated with white retroreflecting material and the red part (except on the reverse side when that side is coloured only red) may be illuminated with red retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(9) Information about the ownership of a delineator may be moulded into the base in characters not more than 80 mm high and in the same colour as the base. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(10) Information about the manufacture of a delineator, required in order to comply with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009[^c-1df9094f4bbec53fdaf9ba6c79ef364e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or a corresponding EEA Standard and occupying an area not exceeding 30 square centimetres, may be indicated on the part coloured white in characters not exceeding 5 millimetres in height, leaving at least 90% of the remaining area of white colour illuminated with white retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(11) A cylinder shall consist of a cylindrically shaped device *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] which, subject to paragraph (15), conforms to British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009 or to a corresponding EEA Standard[^c-17cb7399b91c72281da3fb5f3c4b8ff9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b].

(12) The white part of a cylinder shall, subject to paragraph (14), be illuminated with white retroreflecting material and the part coloured red may be illuminated with red retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(13) Information about the ownership of a cylinder may be moulded into the lower of the two red parts in red characters not more than 40 mm high. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(14) Information about the manufacture of a cylinder, required in order to comply with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009[^c-8aec0792e95c5dd04f8e3832983e9087] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or a corresponding EEA Standard and occupying an area not exceeding 30 square centimetres, may be indicated on the part coloured white in characters not exceeding 5 millimetres in height, leaving at least 90% of the remaining area of white colour illuminated with white retroreflecting material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(15)[^c-543d8463d126151dbbb91ba49db4f14a] In relation to each aspect of performance specified in column (2) of an item in the Table, a cone or cylinder shall comply with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the requirement or test specification of British Standard BS EN 13422:2004+A1:2009 specified in column (3) of the item; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) an equivalent requirement or test specification specified in a corresponding EEA Standard. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
