# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 21

## 21. Illumination of signs shown in diagrams 560 and 561 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-08ecf935c81ea026b5b7031c12f9f93d][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The signs shown in diagrams 560 and 561 shall not be illuminated by the fitting of a means of internal or external lighting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A sign shown in a diagram whose number appears in column (2) of an item in the Table and having the dimension specified in column (3) of that item shall be illuminated by either of the methods prescribed by paragraph (3) which are shown in column (4) of the item, and by no other method. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The prescribed methods of illumination are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the use of 14 circular reflectors of the corner cube type, each reflector having a diameter of 22 millimetres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the use of retroreflecting material extending over the whole surface of the sign; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the use of a single circular reflector of the corner cube type extending over the whole surface of the sign; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the use of reflectors consisting of bi-convex lenses extending over the whole surface of the sign; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the use of a single rectangular reflector of the corner cube type extending over the whole surface of the sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
