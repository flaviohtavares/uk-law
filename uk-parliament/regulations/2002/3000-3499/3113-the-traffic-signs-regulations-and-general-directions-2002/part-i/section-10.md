# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 10

## 10. Application of section 36 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 to signs and disqualification for offences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-129a96a1a291fce6c5b1f957f6fac896][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 36 of the 1988 Act shall apply to each of the following signs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the signs shown in diagrams 601.1, 602, 606, 609, 610, 611.1, 615, 616, 626.2A, 629.2, 629.2A, 784.1, 953, 953.1, 7023, 7029 (except when varied to omit the legend “NO OVERTAKING”), 7031 and 7403; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the road marking shown in diagram 1001.3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the road marking shown in diagram 1003; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the road markings shown in diagrams 1013.1, 1013.3 and 1013.4 insofar as those markings convey the requirements specified in regulation 26; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the road markings shown in diagrams 1025.1, 1025.3 and 1025.4 insofar as those markings convey the prohibition specified by regulation 29(1) and Part I of Schedule 19; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) the road markings shown in diagrams 1042, 1042.1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 1043, 1044, 1044.1, 1044.2 and[^key-f989dc0db6d9785e930c9afd1443d984] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 1045;
- (g) the red light signal when displayed by the light signals prescribed by regulation 33 or by regulation 35; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (h) the light signals prescribed by regulation 33 as varied in accordance with regulation 34 when they are displaying one or more of the green arrow signals shown in diagrams 3001.2 or 3001.3 insofar as they convey any of the restrictions specified in regulation 36(1)(f) or (g); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (i) the light signal shown in diagram 3013.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (j) the intermittent red light signals when displayed by the sign shown in diagram 3014; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (k) the light signals prescribed by regulation 37 and shown in diagrams 6031.1 and 6032.1 when indicating one of the prohibitions prescribed by regulation 38. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The following signs are hereby specified for the purposes of column 5 of the entry in Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00040] relating to offences under section 36 of the 1988 Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the signs shown in diagrams 601.1, 616, 629.2, 629.2A and 784.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the road marking shown in diagram 1001.3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the road markings shown in diagram 1013.1, 1013.3 or 1013.4 insofar as those markings convey the requirements specified in regulation 26; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the red light signal when displayed by the light signals prescribed by regulation 33 or by regulation 35; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the light signals prescribed by regulation 33 as varied in accordance with regulation 34 when they are displaying one or more of the green arrow signals shown in diagrams 3001.2 or 3001.3 insofar as they convey any of the restrictions specified in regulation 36(1)(f) or (g); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) the intermittent red light signals when displayed by the sign shown in diagram 3014; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (g) the light signals prescribed by regulation 37 and shown in diagrams 6031.1 and 6032.1 when indicating one of the prohibitions prescribed by regulation 38. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
