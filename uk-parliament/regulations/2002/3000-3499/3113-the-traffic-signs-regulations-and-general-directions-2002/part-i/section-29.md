## 29. Road markings shown in diagrams 1025.1, 1025.3, 1025.4, 1043, 1044, 1044.1 and 1044.2: bus stop and bus stand clearways and box junctions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c6d6d9653092a5a2b4a86c89e2a6ea4c][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-7e8491aa52b8e6fb58a46801c80bca33][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The road markings shown in diagrams 1025.1, 1025.3 and 1025.4 shall convey the prohibition specified in Part I of Schedule 19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The road markings shown in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] diagrams 1043, 1044, 1044.1 and 1044.2[^c-b88c506d2efaf0c78ec00a2a6f422c3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] shall convey the prohibition specified in Part II of Schedule 19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
