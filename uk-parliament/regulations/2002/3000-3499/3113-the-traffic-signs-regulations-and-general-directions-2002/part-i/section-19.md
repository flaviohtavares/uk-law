# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 19

## 19. Illumination of signs—further provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3053ccdc9dad24120b76158f11557b76][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Nothing in this regulation shall apply to the signs shown in diagrams 560, 561, 776 and 781. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Subject to the provisions of regulation 18 and paragraph (1), any sign shown in a diagram in Schedules 1 to 5 and 7, Part II of Schedule 10 and Schedule 12— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) when placed in consequence of the execution of road works must, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in other situations may, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

be reflectorised in accordance with the following provisions of this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), where retroreflecting material is used on any part of a sign shown in a diagram, all other parts of that sign shall also be reflectorised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) No retroreflecting material shall be applied to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) any part of a sign coloured black; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) that part of the sign shown in diagram 7031 which is coloured fluorescent yellow, unless the retroreflecting material is applied to that part in horizontal strips with a gap between each strip, or unless the retroreflecting material is itself also fluorescent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) those parts of the signs shown in diagrams 2505, 2505.1, 2506, 2507, 2508, 2509.1, 2510, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2511.1, 2512.1[^c-8c6dde89db83ed9f2237d49edebb6917] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2513 which give information about the availability of parking places when that information is conveyed by means of a legend which is internally illuminated or formed of light-emitting characters, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

and in this paragraph the word “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”, in relation to a sign, means any part of that sign which is uniformly coloured and bounded by parts of a different colour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
