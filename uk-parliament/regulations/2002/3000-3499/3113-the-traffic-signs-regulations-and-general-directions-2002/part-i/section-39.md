# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 39

## 39. Light signals to control traffic at level crossings etc (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fab8ac5256ca514bd9304f1c76c892cf][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Light signals for the control of traffic at level crossings, swinging or lifting bridges, tunnels, airfields or in the vicinity of premises used regularly by fire, police or ambulance service vehicles shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 3014; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) be illuminated in the sequence prescribed by paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) have ES compliant signal heads. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The sequence for the illumination of the light signals prescribed by paragraph (1) shall be as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a single steady amber light, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) two intermittent red lights, each of which will be shown at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute, and in a such a manner that one light is always shown when the other light is not shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
