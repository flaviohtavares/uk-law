## 13. Proportions and form of letters, numerals, symbols and other characters (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-efab155ae4f2b6770e27f05740700112][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (5), (6) and (9) all letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in the signs or parts of the signs shown in the diagrams in Schedules 1 to 5 and 7, Part II of Schedule 10 and Schedule 12 which have a red, blue, brown, black or green background shall have the proportions and form shown in Part I of Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to the letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the bottom panel of diagram 674; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) diagrams 970, 971, 973.2, 973.3, 2401 and 2403.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the parts of diagrams 2505, 2505.1, 2506, 2507, 2508, 2509.1, 2510, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2511.1, 2512.1[^key-1d7686106eb70c6e07c5f7620d5bcff4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2513 which indicate the availability of parking places in accordance with regulation 19(4)(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) diagrams 2607, 2610, 2610.1 and 2610.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the top panels of diagrams *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2919.2[^key-88a7b0ac0af19a869f09df33e3726803] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2920.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) the petrol price display in diagram *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2919.2[^key-f16d05d9ea1ae65eee17b629014be045] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b];
- (g) the reference to the traffic authority in diagrams 7003.1, 7006, 7006.1 and 7007.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (h) the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (i) the words “National Trust for Scotland” used in conjunction with the symbol shown in diagram T303 in Part IV of Schedule 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Subject to paragraphs (4), (5), (6), (8) and (9) all letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in the signs or the parts of signs shown in the diagrams in Schedules 1 to 5, 7, 10 and 12 which have a white, yellow or orange background shall have the proportions and form shown in Part II of Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Paragraph (3) does not apply to the letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the bottom panel of diagram 674; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) diagrams 970, 971, 973.2, 973.3, 2401 and 2403.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) diagrams 2607, 2610, 2610.1 and 2610.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) the top panels of diagrams *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2919.2[^key-fc720407ba051d3aa5d0303c38d7f5e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2920.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the references to the traffic authority in diagrams 7003.1, 7006, 7006.1 and 7007.1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) Letters and numerals used for the purpose of indicating a route number on any sign shown in a diagram in Part X of Schedule 7 (other than those incorporated in diagrams 2913, 2913.2, 2913.3, 2913.4, 2914, 2915, 2927.1, 2929 and 2929.1) shall have the proportions and form shown in Part III of Schedule 13, except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) where a route number is indicated in brackets on a sign shown in diagram 2904, 2904.1, 2906, 2908, 2908.1, 2909, 2913.1 or 2914.1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) where a route number is shown on a green or white panel indicating a route that is not a motorway on a sign shown in diagram 2913.1 or 2914.1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

in either of which cases those letters and numerals shall have the proportions and form shown in either Part I or Part III of Schedule 13 as appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) Letters and numerals used for the purpose of indicating a route number on any sign shown in a diagram in Part III of Schedule 12 when used on a motorway shall have the proportions and form shown in Part IV of Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) Subject to and within the limits of any dimension specified as a maximum or minimum in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the bottom panel of diagram 674; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) diagrams 970, 971, 973.2, 973.3, 2401 and 2403.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the parts of diagrams 2505, 2505.1, 2506, 2507, 2508, 2509.1, 2510, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2511.1, 2512.1[^key-c90252d115e4ab7e2fab61c22c04678a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2513 which indicate the availability of spaces in accordance with regulation 19(4)(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) diagrams 2607, 2610, 2610.1 and 2610.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the top panels of diagrams *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2919.2[^key-f1df2364155069cdbb4f2b71ad980f54] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] and 2920.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) the petrol price display in diagram *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] 2919.2[^key-27210a4d6846a608fd3c528779e75c79] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b];
- (g) the references to the traffic authority in diagrams 7003.1, 7006, 7006.1 and 7007.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (h) the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (i) the words “National Trust for Scotland” used in conjunction with the symbol shown in diagram T303 in Part IV of Schedule 14, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

any letters or numerals or other characters incorporated in those diagrams may have proportions and form other than the proportions and form shown in Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) Letters and numerals used on a sign in diagram 2714 or 2715 shall have the proportions and form shown in Part I of Schedule 13 where those letters and numerals are white and the proportions and form shown in Part II of Schedule 13 where they are black. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(9) All letters, numerals, symbols and other characters incorporated in variable message signs shall have the general proportions and form shown in Part V of Schedule 13 where the construction or method of operation of the sign does not permit the use of letters, numerals and other characters of the proportions and form shown in Part I, II, III or IV of Schedule 13 or of symbols shown in diagrams in Schedules 1 to 5, 10 or 12. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(10) All letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in the road markings shown in the diagrams in Schedule 6 shall have the proportions and form shown in Part VI of Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(11) Symbols incorporated in signs for the purpose of indicating diversion routes to be followed in an emergency shall have the proportions and form shown in Part VII of Schedule 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(12) Symbols incorporated in signs for the purpose of indicating types of tourist destination shall have the proportions and form shown in Schedule 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
