# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Road markings shown in diagrams 1013.1, 1013.3 and 1013.4: double white lines (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ccd6abb08fdf17743a4fe8f6c6a019a0][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A road marking for conveying the requirements specified in paragraph (2) and the warning specified in paragraph (7) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 1013.1, 1013.3 or 1013.4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The requirements conveyed by a road marking mentioned in paragraph (1) shall be that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) subject to paragraphs (3) and (5), no vehicle shall stop on any length of road along which the marking has been placed at any point between the ends of the marking; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) subject to paragraph (6), every vehicle proceeding on any length of road along which the marking has been so placed that, as viewed in the direction of travel of the vehicle, a continuous line is on the left of a broken line or of another continuous line, shall be so driven as to keep the first-mentioned continuous line on the right hand or off side of the vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Nothing in paragraph (2)(a) shall apply so as to prevent a vehicle stopping on any length of road so long as may be necessary for any of the purposes specified in paragraph (4) if the vehicle cannot be used for such a purpose without stopping on the length of road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) The purposes are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to enable a person to board or alight from the vehicle, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to enable goods to be loaded on to or to be unloaded from the vehicle, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) to enable the vehicle to be used in connection with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) any operation involving building, demolition or excavation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the removal of any obstruction to traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the maintenance, improvement or reconstruction of the length of road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the laying, erection, alteration, repair or cleaning in or near the length of road of any sewer or of any main, pipe or apparatus for the supply of gas, water or electricity, or of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus[^key-7b0b649b435a35add756d6215a72b0dd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] kept installed for the purposes of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications code system *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or of any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] lawfully kept installed in any position. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) Nothing in paragraph (2)(a) shall apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) so as to prevent a vehicle stopping in a lay-by; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to a vehicle for the time being used for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] fire brigade *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-32bdd7b83aac84f8afa33804005f6e03] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] Scottish Fire and Rescue Service[^key-d78ba04d9b2651ffcb956c1a3cabd4b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or, in England and Wales, fire and rescue authority,[^key-763190658912779243749498b473ea5d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-7ddf98d496cd9f936b91486b9e34965e]... special forces, police or traffic officer purposes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b];
- (bza (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ba93ee7d0869323ba90abd8744a7b465][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] to a vehicle for the time being used for ambulance purposes or for the purpose of providing a response to an emergency at the request of an NHS ambulance service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (ba (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f1d41de20213bb3b87559324fd117c24][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] as regards England and Wales, and so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which relate to reserved matters (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998), to a vehicle for the time being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-16d02ab1056de00c379ec6617599063b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which do not (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998) relate to reserved matters, to a vehicle for the time being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) to a pedal bicycle not having a sidecar attached thereto, whether additional means of propulsion by mechanical power are attached to the bicycle or not; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) to a vehicle stopping in any case where the person in control of the vehicle is required by law to stop, or is obliged to do so in order to avoid an accident, or is prevented from proceeding by circumstances outside his control; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] traffic officer in uniform or[^key-1b9d25996443fa5ff9883c380cd14367] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] in accordance with the direction of a traffic warden; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) to a vehicle on a road with more than one traffic lane in each direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) Nothing in paragraph (2)(b) shall be taken to prohibit a vehicle from being driven across, or so as to straddle, the continuous line referred to in that paragraph, if it is safe to do so and if necessary to do so— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to enable the vehicle to enter, from the side of the road on which it is proceeding, land or premises adjacent to the length of road on which the line is placed, or another road joining that road; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in order to pass a stationary vehicle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) owing to circumstances outside the control of the driver; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) in order to avoid an accident; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) in order to pass a road maintenance vehicle which is in use, is moving at a speed not exceeding 10 mph, and is displaying to the rear the sign shown in diagram 610 or 7403; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) in order to pass a pedal cycle moving at a speed not exceeding 10 mph; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (g) in order to pass a horse that is being ridden or led at a speed not exceeding 10 mph; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (h) for the purposes of complying with any direction of a constable in uniform *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] , a traffic officer in uniform[^key-15472fde2714508977e34f879f922e77] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or a traffic warden. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) The warning conveyed by a road marking mentioned in paragraph (1) shall be that no vehicle while travelling next to a broken line placed on the left of a continuous line, as viewed in the direction of travel of the vehicle, should cross or straddle the first-mentioned line unless it is seen by the driver of the vehicle to be safe to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
