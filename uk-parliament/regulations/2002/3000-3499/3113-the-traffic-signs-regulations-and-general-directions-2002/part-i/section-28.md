# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 28

## 28. Road marking shown in diagram 1001.3: zig-zag lines—no overtaking (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-972449466d373341d5c51bc4b9298627][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Without prejudice to regulation 27, a zig-zag line shall convey the requirement that, whilst any motor vehicle (in this regulation called “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**the approaching vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”) or any part of it is within the limits of a controlled area and is proceeding towards the signal-controlled crossing facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility[^key-233f4f7cdfb60adfd1e080c67c46ea2b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] to which the controlled area relates, the driver of the vehicle shall not cause it or any part of it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to pass ahead of the foremost part of any other motor vehicle proceeding in the same direction; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to pass ahead of the foremost part of a vehicle which is stationary for the purpose of complying with the indication given by a traffic light signal for controlling vehicular traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) In paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the reference to a motor vehicle in sub-paragraph (a) is, in a case where more than one motor vehicle is proceeding in the same direction as the approaching vehicle in a controlled area, a reference to the motor vehicle nearest to the signal-controlled crossing facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility[^key-d0caa01496fcde268dde67fdeaf13195] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] to which the controlled area relates; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the reference to a stationary vehicle is, in a case where more than one vehicle is stationary in a controlled area for the purpose of complying with the indication given by a traffic light signal for controlling vehicular traffic, a reference to the stationary vehicle nearest the signal-controlled crossing facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] to which the controlled area relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
