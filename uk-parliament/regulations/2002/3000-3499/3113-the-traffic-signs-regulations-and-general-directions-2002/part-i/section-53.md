# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Temporary signs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bb4451704d7250855621c5ea9898a916][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**temporary sign *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a sign placed on or near a road for the purpose of conveying to traffic— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) information about convenient routes to be followed on the occasion of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) a sporting event; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] an exhibition; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] any other public gathering, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

  which is in each case likely to attract a large volume of traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) information about diversions or alternative traffic routes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) information about the availability of new routes or destinations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) information about changes in route numbers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) warnings about, or information on how to avoid, any temporary hazards caused by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) works being executed on or near a road; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] adverse weather conditions or other natural causes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the failure of street lighting or malfunction of or damage to any other apparatus, equipment or facility used in connection with the road or anything situated on or near or under it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] damage to the road itself; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^c-a589d1b956218417882761e203ac59da]...
- (f) requests by the police for information in connection with road traffic accidents *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or[^c-fe3619d42b6c2d39bf5cc3f17639edf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (g) warnings, or information, about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) a civil emergency; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the prospect of a civil emergency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A temporary sign— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) which conveys to traffic any information, warning, requirement, restriction or prohibition of a description which can be conveyed by a sign shown in a diagram in Schedules 1 to 12 (whether on its own or in conjunction or in combination with another such sign) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in that diagram; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) which does not fall within paragraph (a) (“a non-prescribed temporary sign”) shall be of such size, colour and type as is specified in paragraphs (3) to (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The shape of a non-prescribed temporary sign shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) rectangular but with the corners rounded; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) as in sub-paragraph (a) with a rounded point at one end. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) A non-prescribed temporary sign shall be of a size appropriate to the circumstances in which it is placed and may incorporate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) wording; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) numerals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) arrows or chevrons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) any appropriate symbol taken from any diagram in any Schedule; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the arms, badge or other device of a traffic authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] local policing body,[^c-a8606ca8f9b501ba1b1faf1f52d00d18] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] police authority or an organisation representative of road users. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) Every letter and numeral incorporated in a non-prescribed temporary sign other than any letter incorporated in the sign in accordance with paragraph (4)(e) shall be not less than 40 nor more than 350 millimetres in height, and every arrow so incorporated shall be not less than 250 nor more than 1000 millimetres in length. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) Every letter, numeral, arrow, chevron or symbol, other than a sign shown in a diagram in Schedules 1 to 5 when used as a symbol, incorporated in a non-prescribed temporary sign shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) black on a background of white or of yellow; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) white on a blue background; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) blue on a white background; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) if the sign conveys information or warnings of the kind mentioned in paragraph (1)(e), white on a red background, except where it is placed on a motorway when it shall be black on a yellow background; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) if the sign is a variable message sign, white, off-white or yellow on a black background or black on a yellow background, except when the sign is not in use when it shall display a plain black or grey face. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7)[^c-a3b16c86c1a88e422b9e5ae4e19eb069] In this regulation, “a civil emergency” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) an emergency within the meaning of section 1 of the Civil Contingencies Act 2004; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) terrorism within the meaning of section 1 of the Terrorism Act 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
