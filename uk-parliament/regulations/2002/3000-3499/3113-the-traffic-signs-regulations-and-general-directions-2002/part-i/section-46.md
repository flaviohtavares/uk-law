# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 46

## 46. Matrix signs for motorways and all-purpose dual carriageway roads (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-33078bfdb5926fb70ffec7708c365846][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**matrix sign *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a sign shown in a diagram in Part I of Schedule 11 for conveying to traffic on a motorway or an all-purpose dual carriageway road information or a warning, requirement, restriction, prohibition or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] advised maximum speed[^key-2516ded2a128630b0bed686b7d52885f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]—

- (a) relating to or arising out of temporary hazardous conditions on or near the motorway or dual carriageway road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) specified in the caption to a diagram contained in Part I of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A matrix sign shall be a light signal and shall be of the size, colour and type prescribed by this regulation and shown in a diagram in Part I of Schedule 11. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Where a matrix sign is placed beside the carriageway of a road the warning, requirement, restriction, prohibition or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] advised maximum speed[^key-a80c54898e57544f14d07018627f86fb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] conveyed by the sign shall apply to all vehicular traffic facing that sign and proceeding along the carriageway beside which the sign is placed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) For the purposes of this regulation a sign which is mounted on a post situated beside the carriageway but is projected over it or part of it shall be treated as a sign placed beside the carriageway of that road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) Where a matrix sign mounted on a gantry or other structure is so placed that a traffic lane of the carriageway *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or an actively managed hard shoulder[^key-3a0a3967de694fd828057c99c8beb04d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] passes directly beneath it, the warning, requirement, restriction, prohibition or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] advised maximum speed[^key-7da188ad44e6fdaef3b9c8a42ab69b28] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] conveyed by the sign shall apply only to vehicular traffic facing that sign and proceeding along the traffic lane *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or actively managed hard shoulder[^key-76d576140a77ddbb0d3ecf688db3604c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] passing directly beneath it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) A legend or symbol shown on a matrix sign shall be displayed by means of white or off-white light and except in the case of the signs shown in diagrams 6006.2, 6008.1, 6009.3 and 6012 shall be accompanied by the four lamps prescribed by paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) The four lamps mentioned in paragraph (6)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 6022 when placed beside the carriageway or in diagram 6021 when mounted on a gantry or other structure over the carriageway; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) when a matrix sign other than those shown in diagrams 6006.2, 6008.1, 6009.3 and 6012 is displayed, each lamp shall show an intermittent amber light at a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 90 flashes per minute and in such a manner that one horizontal pair of lights is always shown when the other horizontal pair of lights is not shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) The signs shown in diagrams 6006.2, 6008.1 and 6009.3 shall be accompanied by the four red lamps prescribed by regulation 37. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
