# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Light signals for the control of vehicular traffic—standard form (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-785bf524b037e7c26ef3db982426cd9b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulation 34, light signals for the control of vehicular traffic (other than tramcars) at junctions, at places where the headroom or the width of the road is permanently restricted, or at signal-controlled crossing facilities— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 3000, 3000.7, 3000.8, 3000.9 or 3000.10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) shall be illuminated in the sequence prescribed by paragraph (3); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) shall have ES compliant signal heads. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Light signals for the control of vehicular traffic consisting exclusively of pedal cycles— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) shall be of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 3000.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) shall be illuminated in the sequence prescribed by paragraph (3); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) shall have ES compliant signal heads. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) The sequence of illumination of the lights shown by the signals prescribed by paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) red, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) red and amber together, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) green, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) amber. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) Where the light signals are varied as prescribed by regulation 34, one or more green arrows shown in diagram 3001.2 or 3001.3 may be illuminated whilst any of the lights referred to in paragraph (3) are illuminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) For the purposes of these Regulations a signal head is “ES compliant” if, in relation to each aspect of its performance specified in column (2) of an item in the Table, it complies with the requirement or test specification of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] British Standard BS EN 12368:2006[^c-bde5cfe4860fe9c88065df31e84cb4bc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] specified in column (3) of the item or to an equivalent requirement or test specification specified in a corresponding EEA standard. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
