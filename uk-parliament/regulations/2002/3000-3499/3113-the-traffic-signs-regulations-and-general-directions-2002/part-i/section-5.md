# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 5

## 5. Interpretation of speed limit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-96eae6036fe3ea2b5dafebef44e75d44][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In these Regulations “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**speed limit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a maximum or minimum limit of speed on the driving of vehicles on a road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) imposed by an order under section 14 of the 1984 Act (temporary prohibition or restriction of traffic on roads); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) imposed by an order under section 16A of the 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00035] (special events); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) imposed by regulations under section 17 of the 1984 Act (traffic regulation on special roads); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) arising by virtue of the road being restricted for the purposes of section 81 of the 1984 Act (general speed limit for restricted roads); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) imposed by an order under section 84 of the 1984 Act (speed limits on roads other than restricted roads); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (f) imposed by an order under section 88 of the 1984 Act (temporary speed limits); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (g) imposed by or under a local Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**maximum speed limit” and “minimum speed limit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) In these Regulations “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**national speed limit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means any prohibition imposed on a road by the 70 miles per hour, 60 miles per hour and 50 miles per hour (Temporary Speed Limit) Order 1977 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00036] or by regulation 3 of the Motorways (Speed Limits) Regulations 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00037].
