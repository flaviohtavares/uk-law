# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Variable message signs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1f0c74fb99d246c251469034a25d615f][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In these Regulations a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**variable message sign *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**” means a device capable of displaying, at different times, two or more aspects falling within the following descriptions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a sign shown in a diagram in Schedule 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 11 or 12; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a legend of a type shown in Schedule 15 in accordance with the provisions of that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) a non-prescribed temporary sign as defined by regulation 53(2)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) a blank grey or a blank black face. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) A variable message sign shall be of a size appropriate to display the messages referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph (1), having regard to the normal speed of traffic on the road on or near which the sign is situated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2A)[^c-eaad0701847bb5dbd84d53c1b1e1cd49] When a variable message sign displays— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a sign shown in a diagram in Schedule 11 (except diagram 6031.1); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a legend of the type shown in Schedule 15, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

any symbol or legend may be displayed in white, off-white or yellow on a dark background. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) If the construction or method of operation of a variable message sign prevents a sign from being displayed in the colours shown for it in the appropriate diagram in Schedule 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 or 12, a black legend or symbol on a white or yellow background may be displayed as a white, off-white or yellow legend or symbol on a dark background, provided that any red triangle or red circle forming part of the sign is included. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(4) A legend or symbol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] displayed[^c-7e87b9a0d7ba111f0be6d4bee5322ef2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] as mentioned in paragraph (3) may be in the form of a white, off-white or yellow outline of the legend or symbol. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(5) The size of any symbol or legend, including any red triangle or circle, displayed on a variable message sign, which is displayed by means of light-emitting characters or symbols, may be varied so that the size of the symbol or legend appears to road users to be the same as it appears to them to be when it is not displayed in this manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6) When a variable message sign displays— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the sign shown in diagram 670; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) any sign shown in a diagram in Part I of Schedule 11 except diagrams 6006.2, 6008.1, 6009.3 and 6012; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) a legend of the type shown in Schedule 15, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

it may also display four lamps, which are of the size, colour and type specified in regulation 46(7) and have a rate of flashing of not less than 60 nor more than 150 flashes per minute, and the distance between the lamps shall accord with the overall size of the variable message sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(6A)[^c-5e1c40eaf2c434d4a6cd2636a4398706] Where a variable message sign displays the sign shown in diagram 670 by means of light emitting characters and symbols, the diameter of the sign may be varied to 1,300 millimetres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(7) Where a variable message sign displays by means of light-emitting characters or symbols— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) the sign shown in diagram 504.1, 505.1, 506.1, 507.1, 510, 512, 512.1, 512.2 or 513; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the sign shown in diagram 670 (when showing the numerals “20”, “30”, “40” or “50”) in such a way that the sign is only visible when associated equipment detects the presence of a vehicle exceeding the speed limit indicated by the sign, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

the variable message sign may display, beneath the sign displayed on it, the legend “SLOW DOWN” in characters having a height not less than one quarter of the height of the displayed sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(8) The display of a blank black or grey face on a variable message sign accompanied by four flashing lamps of the kind mentioned in paragraph (6) shall indicate to drivers that they should take special care. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(9) A variable message sign which displays alternately the signs shown in diagrams 7023 and 7024 shall be manually operated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(10)[^c-1f7e060275b601a144ac3824dff9f2c2] When a variable message sign displays any of the following messages— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) a sign referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a legend referred to in paragraph (1)(b); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) a sign referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) of paragraph (7) accompanied, in accordance with that paragraph, by the legend “SLOW DOWN”, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

the variable message sign shall display the whole of that message at the same time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
