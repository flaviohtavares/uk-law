# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 7

## 7. Interpretation of Schedules 1 to 12 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c4533d983299180428a4e47736fc1d66][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In any untitled table under or beside any diagram (in this paragraph referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**the diagram *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]**”) in Schedules 1 to 12— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) in item 1 any regulations which are specified are regulations in these Regulations in which a specific reference is made to the diagram, to a regulation or Schedule which refers to the diagram or to a symbol which appears in the diagram or a variant of the diagram; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in item 2 any directions which are specified are directions in the Traffic Signs General Directions 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00038] containing a reference to the particular diagram or to a regulation or Schedule which refers to the diagram; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) in item 3 any diagrams which are specified are diagrams in the Schedules to these Regulations which show signs which may or must be placed in conjunction or in combination with the sign shown in the diagram; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) in item 4 any item which is specified is an item in Schedule 16 which specifies permitted variants to the diagram; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) in item 5 any item which is specified is an item in Schedule 17 which specifies the illumination requirements for the sign shown in the diagram. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) The table entitled “Table of combinations” under or beside any diagram in Part III of Schedule 12 indicates the manner in which the sign shown in that diagram may be varied in accordance with paragraphs (7) to (9) of regulation 17. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(3) Dimensions indicated on any diagram shown in Schedules 1 to 12 are expressed in millimetres unless otherwise specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
