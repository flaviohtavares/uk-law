# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 35

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Portable light signals for the control of vehicular traffic (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-293b45641163a3b11f177cf51cd4353e][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Portable light signals for the control of vehicular traffic other than tramcars shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 3000.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) illuminated in the sequence prescribed by regulation 33(3); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) so constructed that, if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] British Standard BS EN 12368:2006[^key-cf8a4ba7fcc3b1a8c92d6e1a1dfd72cd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] applied to portable signals, they would be ES compliant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
