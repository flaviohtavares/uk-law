### 1. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b944c80d0ce3a021e9b71b62849b7ed1][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

In this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- “**approaching vehicular traffic *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” in relation to a crossing means vehicular traffic approaching the crossing along the carriageway across which the crossing is placed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “**crossing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means a signal-controlled crossing facility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “**far side signals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means light signals of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4002.1, 4003.2 and 4003.5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “**indicator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means a sign of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003, 4003.3 and 4003.6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “**near side signals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means a sign of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003.1, 4003.4 and 4003.7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “supplementary near side signals” means a sign of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003.1A, 4003.4A and 4003.7A;[^c-5201f291f04cf67d085413f4d7628db0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- [^c-d68aa6aee82006bef5a2d582cc08bb88]...
- “**system of staggered crossings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means two crossings provided on a road on which there is a central reservation and where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (a) there is one crossing on each side of the central reservation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (b) taken together the two crossings do not lie in a straight line; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- [^c-d68aa6aee82006bef5a2d582cc08bb88]...

references to the left or right hand side of a crossing are to the left or right hand side as viewed by approaching vehicular traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 2. Vehicular light signals on a two-way street (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-972191edf5ac21525eeaecc958484091][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

Subject to paragraph 4, on a two-way street, the number and disposition of light signals of the kind prescribed by regulation 33 which are placed at a crossing to control approaching vehicular traffic shall, in the case of a crossing of the type specified in column (2) of an item in the table, be as specified in column (3) of that item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 4. Additional vehicular light signals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6182b5678a2a208be990eb4146e6ad87][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

In addition to the signals required to be placed at a crossing by paragraph 2 or 3, the traffic authority may place such other primary or secondary signals as it thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 5. Far side light signals at crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d598b7d6928ccac9f7a2ca2b8514b2f4][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1) Except where near side signals have been placed at a crossing in accordance with paragraph 6, at least one set of far side signals and one indicator shall be placed at each end of a crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Each set of far side signals shall be so placed as to be clearly visible to any person on the opposite side of the crossing who wishes to use the crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Where a crossing has a refuge or central reservation within its limits, at least one indicator shall be placed on the refuge or central reservation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Each indicator shall be so placed that the push button on it may be reached by any person wishing to press it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) Where far side signals and indicators have been placed at a crossing in accordance with this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) there may be placed as many devices of the kind prescribed by regulation 49(8) as the traffic authority think fit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) no near side signals shall be placed at that crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 6. Near side signals at crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a97f9661a5bc1d6977cfec93f0fea27c][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1) Except where far side signals have been placed at a crossing in accordance with paragraph 5, at least one set of near side signals shall be placed at each end of a crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Where a crossing has a refuge or central reservation within its limits, at least one set of near side signals shall be placed on the refuge or central reservation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Each set of near side signals shall be so placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) as to be clearly visible to any person who wishes to use the crossing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) that the push button on it may be reached by any person wishing to press it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) In addition to the near side signals required by this paragraph to be placed at a crossing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) there may be placed as many devices of the kind prescribed by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] regulation 47(2A), 48(2A), 49(2A) or 49(8)[^c-f9f9bd5c00cd96e62b895ce18b57d414] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] as the traffic authority think fit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] [^c-344e2d3798ee002e49060a880f5c40b6]...
- (b) no far side signals or indicators shall be placed at that crossing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] ; and[^c-d7122f499d904a8bb68cbb637d2e3368] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) one set of supplementary near side signals may be placed above each set of near side signals at the crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
