# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**29.**[^c-f54bb8ecfa505d8eeaa995a1972ba1f3][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] The sign shown in diagram 574 may be placed only in or near an infected place or area for the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00057] and shall be removed as soon as it ceases to be in or near such a place or area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
