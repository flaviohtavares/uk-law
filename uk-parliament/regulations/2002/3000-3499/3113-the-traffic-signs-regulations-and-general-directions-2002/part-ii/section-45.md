# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Mounting of refuge indicator lamps (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-041163cdf59234b4345bf9632c07d593][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This paragraph applies where the lamp prescribed by regulation 57 is mounted on a post specially provided for the purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), if the post is placed at a zebra crossing and yellow globes are attached to the post, the part of the post between ground level and the point where the yellow globes are mounted or attached shall be coloured black and white in alternate horizontal bands and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the lowest band shall be coloured black and not less than 275 millimetres nor more than 1 metre deep; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) each other band shall be not less than 275 nor more than 335 millimetres deep, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

the remaining part of the post shall be coloured in accordance with paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), if the post is placed elsewhere or at a zebra crossing, but without yellow globes attached to it, the post shall be coloured grey or black but with two white bands (each band being not less than 275 nor more than 335 millimetres deep) so arranged that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) not less than 275 nor more than 335 millimetres extend between the nearest edges of the two bands; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the upper edge of the uppermost band is not less than 275 nor more than 335 millimetres below the lowest part of the lamp, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

and the white bands may be internally illuminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Where the post consists of aluminium, concrete or galvanised metal it may remain in its natural colour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) In this direction “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**yellow globes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means globes in relation to which Part I of Schedule 1 to the Zebra, Pelican and Puffin Pedestrian Crossings Regulations 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00062] is complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
