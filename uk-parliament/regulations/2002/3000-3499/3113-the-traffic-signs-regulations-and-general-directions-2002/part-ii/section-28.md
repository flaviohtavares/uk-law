# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**28.**[^key-4307f5d428c45bdab95bce5cb77eec04][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] The sign shown in diagram 551.1 may be placed on or near a road only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) at a site which is approved as a migratory toad crossing by or on behalf of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^key-cc7c73bfad75881c4354051fcdf7eab3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) during *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] January,[^key-d5a10f5a7eaa4257dfc14d8df3575fa3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] February, March, April and May in each year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
