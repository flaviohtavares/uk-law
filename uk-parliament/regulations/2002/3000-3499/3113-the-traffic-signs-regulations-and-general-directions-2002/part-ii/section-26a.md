# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 26A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**26A.**[^key-e639fb90369560dc154ac455860f6247][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The sign shown in diagram 637.2A may be placed only in a restricted parking zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 663.2 may be placed only at the boundary of a restricted parking zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The sign shown in diagram 663.3 or 664.1 may be placed only at the boundary of a permit parking area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) The sign shown in diagram 664 may be placed only at the boundary of a controlled parking zone or of a restricted parking zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
