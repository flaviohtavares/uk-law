# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 30

**30.**[^key-d5305d93fd571fdd51a7132149ef2a58][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] The sign shown in diagram 601.1 may be placed on or near a road at a junction with another road only if a sign has not been placed on the other road so as to control traffic passing through the junction on that other road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
