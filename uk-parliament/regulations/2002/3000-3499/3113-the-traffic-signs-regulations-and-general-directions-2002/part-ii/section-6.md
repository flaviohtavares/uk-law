## 6. Sign to be placed only at sites approved by the Secretary of State (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fc371cb33ba6307aeb4292cf2e1db066][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The sign shown in diagram 601.1 shall not be placed for the first time at any site except with the approval of the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
