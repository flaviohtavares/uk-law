# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**34.**[^c-95371af5327b0bd4af732ee955eff7a1][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The marking shown in diagram 1003 may be placed on or near a road only in the following cases— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) at a junction with another road on which no marking has been placed to control traffic passing through the junction on that other road; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) at a roundabout in conjunction with the sign shown in diagram 602 and the road marking shown in diagram 1023; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) at a level crossing in the circumstances described in regulation 25(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) in the circumstances described in regulation 25(4), when such a marking has not been placed to control vehicles travelling in the opposite direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The marking shown in diagram 1003 may only be placed on the carriageway of a road in circumstances such that regulation 25(6) (transverse lines placed in advance of a cycle track crossing a road) applies, if the length of the road which is crossed by a cycle track consists of a road hump extending across the full width of the carriageway and constructed pursuant to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) section 90A of the Highways Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00058] and in accordance with the Highways (Road Humps) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00059]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) section 36 of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00060] and in accordance with the Road Humps (Scotland) Regulations 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00061].
