## 58. Approval of types of stud by the Secretary of State (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d942fb32db8ce2bd45d4f396ec089d26][^key-a2b74f632aaa8cf76964e7aa3b58843c][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^key-15cacffc0e1c6c56186ec172561453b8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] may approve in writing a type of stud for the purposes of direction 57(1)(b), (2) or (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) To the extent that a type of stud incorporates reflectors or retroreflecting material, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] may approve it under paragraph (1) only for the purpose of testing it in order to determine whether it meets the requirements of direction 57(5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) When any stud has been placed in accordance with an approval under paragraph (1), the stud shall cease to be regarded as being of an approved type— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) in a case where approval has been granted for a specified period only, on whichever is the earlier of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) the date on which that period expires; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the date specified in a notice in writing given by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] to the supplier and to the traffic authority as the date after which the stud is no longer to be so regarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in a case where approval has been granted without any express time limit, on the date specified in a notice in writing given by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] to the supplier and to the traffic authority as the date after which the stud is no longer to be so regarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) All studs ceasing to be of an approved type shall be removed on or before the appropriate date in accordance with paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5)[^M_F_2da7fd6c-9ed4-4e3a-9e46-46ba3849bafc][^key-61dbc60a951fe712bd3eeb6eb013d4fa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Paragraph (5) shall cease to have effect on 31st January 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
