# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 38A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**38A.**[^c-a09a6e996d6738eb62d8ff44365663ae][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) A warning light which shows an intermittent light may not be placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) more than 50 metres from a street lamp lit by electricity; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) on a road subject to a speed limit of more than 40 mph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) In this direction, “warning light” has the same meaning as in regulation 55. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
