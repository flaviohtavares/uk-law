## 7. Signs to be placed only to indicate the effect of a statutory prohibition (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7e94a7f9d4a6aedaade03bfb7b9aff7b][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) The sign shown in diagram 626.2A may be placed only to indicate the effect of a statutory provision which restricts the use of a road carried by a bridge or other structure— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) in the case of vehicles required to be marked with their maximum gross weight, to any vehicle with a maximum gross weight not exceeding that indicated on the sign; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in the case of vehicles not required to be marked with their maximum gross weight but required to be marked with their unladen weight, to any vehicle with an unladen weight not exceeding the maximum gross weight indicated on the sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
