**24.**[^key-73f9e40a20d0a22e359a8a2c16782163][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) Except where it is placed to indicate the effect of a temporary statutory provision and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] subject to paragraphs (2) and (5)[^key-647c580a95c77912751ef08043a1286e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], a sign shown in a diagram specified in column (2) of an item in the Table shall not be placed except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) on or near the side of a road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in conjunction with, and on the same side of the road as, a road marking which is shown in a diagram and is in the form (if any) indicated in column (3) of that item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the signs shown in diagrams 637.3, 639 and 640 when placed to indicate restrictions applying to the same road as restrictions indicated by the sign shown in diagram 650.1, 974 or 975. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4) the sign shown in diagram 637.2 may be placed only on a road within a pedestrian zone where the sign shown in diagram 618.3 or 618.3A is placed at each entrance into the zone for vehicular traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) The sign shown in diagram 637.2 shall not be used on a road within the pedestrian zone to which the sign relates except in conjunction with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the road marking shown in diagram 1017 or 1018.1 unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) the road does not comprise a carriageway and footway which are separately defined; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the entry of vehicular traffic into the pedestrian zone is restricted at the same times as those at which the waiting of vehicles is prohibited as indicated on the upper panel of the sign in diagram 637.2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the prohibition on the waiting of vehicles applies uniformly throughout every road in the zone, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) where there is a prohibition on the loading and unloading of vehicles, the road marking shown in diagram 1019 or 1020.1 unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) the road does not comprise a carriageway and footway which are separately defined; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the entry of vehicular traffic into the pedestrian zone is restricted at the same times as those at which the loading and unloading of vehicles is prohibited as indicated on the lower panel of the sign in diagram 637.2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the prohibition on the loading and unloading of vehicles applies uniformly throughout every road in the zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5)[^key-180ead091b42fdd44ea44ad00cb082df] Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the signs shown in diagrams 660 and 660.3 when placed on a road within a permit parking area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
