(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a road marking placed on a road within a controlled parking zone, if signs shown in diagram 663 or 663.1 have been placed at the entrances for vehicular traffic into the zone, except where the road marking is placed to indicate restrictions different from the restrictions indicated on those signs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3)[^c-25169be1279e4c4b8c061fe08e41289e] Except for the road markings shown in diagrams 1017, 1018.1, 1019 and 1020.1, none of the road markings shown in the diagrams whose numbers appear in, and which are in the form (if any) specified in, the items in column (2) of the table may be placed in a permit parking area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
