# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**15.**[^c-44753880cbbaad5ee804fbbb4d36eee2][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The signs shown in diagrams 823, 824, 825, 868, 868.1, 872.1, 873, 874, 875, 876 and 2713 may be placed only on or near— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a motorway when the colour of the background of the sign is blue; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a primary route when the colour of the background of the sign is green; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) a non-primary route when the colour of the background of the sign is white. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 872.1 may only be placed on a dual carriageway road or a one-way road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The sign shown in diagram 877 may be placed only on or near— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a motorway exit slip road when the colour of the background of the sign is blue; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a primary route when the colour of the background of the sign is green; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) a non-primary route when the colour of the background of the sign is white. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
