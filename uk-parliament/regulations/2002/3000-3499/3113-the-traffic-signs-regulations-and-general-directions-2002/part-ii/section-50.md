# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 50

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Placing of signs and light signals shown in Schedule 11 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-68219e8b9c1e076902c57716b16585e2][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The light signals prescribed by regulation 37(1) and 45 and the signs and light signals shown in any diagram in Schedule 11 may be displayed only on or near— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a motorway; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) an all-purpose dual carriageway road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
