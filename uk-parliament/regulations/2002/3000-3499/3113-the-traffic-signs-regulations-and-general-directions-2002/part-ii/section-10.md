# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 10

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. The placing of certain signs to indicate the end of a restriction, requirement, prohibition or speed limit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4ced0b284e687fe9a18fd6264e3ee700][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) When the sign shown in diagram 618.4, 622.2, 645, 647, 664, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 664.1,[^key-0718ef573831d19930de57788078c692] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 666, 667.2, 668.2, 673, 675 or 964 is placed to indicate the point at which traffic on a road ceases to be subject to a restriction, requirement, prohibition or speed limit, the sign shall be placed on the road as near as practicable to that point. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), where a length of road ceases to be subject to a speed limit and becomes subject to a national speed limit, the sign shown in diagram 671 shall be placed at or as near as practicable to the point where the speed limit ends and the national speed limit begins *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] , except where the sign shown in diagram 675 with the permitted variation shown in item 4 of the table below that diagram is placed at that point.[^key-f023894a4125091b4b6d2c2e2a1ac0ba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Where a temporary restriction of speed has been imposed under section 14 of the 1984 Act along a length of road by reason of works which are being or are proposed to be executed on or near that road, there shall be placed at or as near as practicable to the point where the temporary restriction of speed ends— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the sign shown in diagram 7006 or in diagram 7001 (placed in combination with the plate shown in diagram 645); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in a case where, but for the temporary speed limit, a change in speed limit would at some point have occurred along the length of road, the sign shown in diagram 670 (varied as appropriate) or 671. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) When the sign shown in diagram 671, 673 or 675 is placed to indicate the point at which traffic on a road ceases to be subject to a speed limit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) where the road has only one carriageway, one such sign shall be placed on each side of the carriageway of the road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) where the road has more than one carriageway, one such sign shall be placed on each side of each carriageway on which the speed limit ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) Where a road (“the relevant road”) has a junction with the side of another road (“the other road”) and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a maximum speed limit is in force on the other road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a national speed limit is in force on the relevant road, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

then, for the purpose of indicating the national speed limit to traffic entering the relevant road from the other road, the sign shown in diagram 671 or 675 shall be placed on the relevant road in accordance with paragraph (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(6) The sign shall be placed not more than 20 metres from the junction with the other road on the left hand or near side of the relevant road as viewed in the direction of travel of a vehicle entering the relevant road from the other road or, if the relevant road is a dual carriageway road, on the left hand or near side of the carriageway by which a vehicle may pass into the relevant road from the other road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) Where the relevant road has a junction with the side of the other road and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a national speed limit is in force on the other road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a maximum speed limit is in force on the relevant road, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

then, for the purpose of indicating the national speed limit to traffic entering the other road from the relevant road, traffic signs shown in diagram 671 or 675 shall be placed in accordance with paragraph (8) on the relevant road not further than 20 metres from the junction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(8) If the relevant road has *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] only[^key-ddb6011959347f0fe9f687394bcc4291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] one carriageway, one such sign shall be placed on each side of that carriageway and, if the relevant road is a dual carriageway road, one such sign shall be placed on each side of the carriageway by which traffic may pass from the relevant road into the other road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
