# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Mounting of the signs shown in diagrams 560 and 561 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-92e97e3048ef582f0aa3ca158ad834ce][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The sign shown in diagram 560 or 561 shall be so placed that the top of the sign is not less than 550 nor more than 1000 millimetres above the surface of the adjacent carriageway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Where the sign shown in diagram 560 or 561 is mounted on a post specially provided for the purpose that part of the post which extends above ground level may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) of any single colour; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) coloured black and white in alternate horizontal bands, each band being not less than 225 nor more than 350 millimetres deep. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The signs shown in diagrams 560 and 561 shall be so erected as to display— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the colour red on the left hand edge of the carriageway as viewed by the drivers of approaching vehicles; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the colour white on the right hand edge of the carriageway when so viewed, unless the edge is the edge of the carriageway of a dual carriageway road or a one-way road when the colour amber shall be displayed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
