# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 13A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**13A.**[^c-4ec7b8bf7f4ab9e0edac97d852390a37][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The signs shown in diagrams 2703, 2704, 2705, 2706 and 2707 may not be placed on the main carriageways of a motorway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) In this direction, “main carriageways of a motorway” means all those parts of a motorway— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) onto which vehicular traffic may pass from a motorway entrance slip road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) from which vehicular traffic may pass onto a motorway exit slip road, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

includes the carriageways, the central reservation, the hard shoulders and the verges. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2), “carriageway” and “verge” each has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) in relation to a motorway in England or Wales, the same meaning as in the Motorways Traffic (England and Wales) Regulations 1982; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in relation to a motorway in Scotland, the same meaning as in the Motorways Traffic (Scotland) Regulations 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
