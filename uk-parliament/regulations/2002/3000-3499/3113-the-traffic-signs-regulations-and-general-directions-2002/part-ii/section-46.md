# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Mounting and backing of light signals, matrix signs and warning lights (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ac7d35927ca3d206a57d4fe422449260][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The light signals prescribed by regulation 33, 34, 35, 37, 39, 41, 44, 45, 47, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 47A,[^key-64709c72a11ef55b0159662f544f196b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 48, 49 or 52, the matrix signals prescribed by regulation 46 and the warning lights prescribed by regulations 50 and 51 may be placed on or near a road only if they are so placed that they face the stream of traffic to which they are intended to convey the warnings, information, requirements, restrictions or prohibitions prescribed by the Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) A container enclosing the lamps of a type of light signals, matrix sign or warning light mentioned in paragraph (1) shall be coloured black except that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) if a container encloses lamps of the light signals prescribed by regulation 37, 44 or 45, the matrix sign prescribed by regulation 46 or the warning lights prescribed by regulation 50 or 51, it may be coloured grey instead of black; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the back of a container enclosing the signals shown in diagrams 4003, 4003.1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 4003.1A,[^key-299a1d91d66fdfcc03f5523bd18bc25c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 4003.3, 4003.4, 4003.4A,[^key-76414648c6fef3c6557c62459a9e13bb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 4003.6 , 4003.7 or 4003.7A[^key-8e92fd3a7a4c2b492338aee1c2362148] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] may be coloured yellow; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) the container may have a white border not less than 45 nor more than 55 millimetres wide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Any of the kinds of light signals mentioned in paragraph (1) other than the signals prescribed by regulation 39(1) may be mounted on a backing board, which shall be coloured black and may have a white border not less than 45 nor more than 55 millimetres wide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) In the case of the light signals prescribed by regulation 39(1) the back of the backing board shown in diagram 3014 may be coloured black or grey. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) In the case of the matrix signs prescribed by regulation 46, an identification number may be shown on the front of the backing board below the signal or on the post on which the signal is mounted and, in the case of other light signals, an identification number and name, and a telephone number for use in an emergency, may be shown on the post on which the signals are mounted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(6) Subject to paragraphs (7), (8) and (9)[^key-d5346298bcf3d263ad42c325efe5320f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], where light signals prescribed by any of the regulations specified in paragraph (1) or shown in a diagram in Part I of Schedule 10 or in Schedule 11 are mounted on a post specially provided for the purpose, that part of the post which extends above ground level shall be either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) coloured grey, black, brown, dark green or dark blue; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in a non-reflective metallic finish. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) Paragraph (6) is subject to the following exceptions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the post may be marked with a yellow or white band not less than 140 nor more than 160 millimetres deep, the lower edge of the band being not less than 1500 nor more than 1700 millimetres above ground level; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) where the light signals prescribed by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] regulations 33(1) or (2), 41, 47, 48 or 49[^key-00fef85636c79ef66e76722509872b96] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] are installed temporarily, the containers in which the posts are placed may be coloured with alternate red and white horizontal stripes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(8) The portable light signals prescribed by regulation 35, instead of being mounted on a post coloured in accordance with paragraph (6), may be mounted on a post coloured yellow (but having no yellow or white band as specified in paragraph (7)) or on a tripod or other support coloured yellow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(9)[^key-31d3d136470b58163b6906292e71d126] The light signals prescribed by regulation 47A, instead of being mounted on a post coloured in accordance with paragraph (6), may be mounted on a post coloured yellow (but having no yellow or white band as specified in paragraph (7)) or on a tripod or other support coloured yellow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
