# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Mounting and backing of signs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cea06735db94f15b3ec4b183e9118be1][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (3A)[^c-a2e5e88d528dddea7501bd78fd81bf4b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], where a sign (other than a sign referred to in paragraph (4), (5) or in direction 43 or 44) in a diagram in Schedules 1 to 5 and 7, Part II of Schedule 10 or Schedule 12, or a sign of the type prescribed by regulation 53, is mounted on a post or other support specially provided for the purpose (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**the post *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**”), that part of the post which extends above ground level shall be of any single colour or in its natural colour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Where the post is not likely to be readily visible to pedestrians or cyclists, a yellow or white band not less than 140 nor more than 160 millimetres deep may be provided on the post, the lower edge of the band being not less than 1500 nor more than 1700 millimetres above ground level. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Where the support provided for a portable sign comprises several components, instead of complying with paragraphs (1) and (2), the components need not all be of the same colour provided that each is of a single colour or in its natural colour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3A)[^c-6e421ed621db4be7772abf853808aad5] The signs shown in diagrams 606, 610, 611, 616, 951, 955, 956 and 957 may be mounted on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a bollard which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) is illuminated by means of internal lighting; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] conforms to British Standard *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EN 12899-2:2007 or to a corresponding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] Standard; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a self-righting bollard which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) is illuminated by the use of retroreflecting material; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] conforms to British Standard *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 8442:2006 or to a corresponding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] Standard. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Where a beacon or lamp of a kind prescribed by regulation 54 or 55 is mounted on a structure specially provided for the purpose, the structure shall be coloured grey, red, white or yellow, or in alternate bands of red and white or of black and white. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) The post provided for the mounting of the sign shown in diagram 7031 shall be coloured in alternate black and yellow bands each 300 millimetres deep with a black band uppermost below the bottom of the sign face, and the yellow bands may be reflectorised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(6) The sign shown in diagram 7104 or 7105 may be mounted on an object of the size, colour and type of the sign shown in diagram 7101.1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) An identification code for maintenance purposes may be indicated, in characters not exceeding 25 millimetres in height, on the post on which a sign is mounted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
