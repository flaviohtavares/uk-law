# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Placing of road marking shown in diagram 1001, 1001.2 or 1001.2A in conjunction with light signals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-48734dc30c9e5819c0837b87a18f4c74][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The light signals prescribed by regulation 33(1) may be placed only in conjunction with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the road marking shown in diagram 1001; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) subject to direction 18(2), the road marking shown in diagram 1001.2 or 1001.2A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The light signals prescribed by regulation 33(2) or 39(1) may be placed only in conjunction with the road marking shown in diagram 1001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall not apply to light signals while works which necessitate the temporary removal of a road marking are being executed on a road near the light signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
