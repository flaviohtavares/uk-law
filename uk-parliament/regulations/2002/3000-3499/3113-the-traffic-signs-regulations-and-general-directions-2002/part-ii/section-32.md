# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**32.**[^c-2212f21c2e00aba4a946e76ba091c167][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) A sign shown in diagram 878, 879, 880 or 880.1 may be placed only in an area or along a route where enforcement cameras are from time to time in use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 880 may be placed only on or near a road on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) there is provided a system of carriageway lighting furnished by means of lamps placed not more than 183 metres apart in England and Wales or 185 metres apart in Scotland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) which is subject to a speed limit of 30 mph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The sign shown in diagram 880.1 may be placed only on or near a road on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) there is not a system of carriageway lighting furnished by means of lamps placed not more than 183 metres apart in England and Wales or 185 metres apart in Scotland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a national speed limit is in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) No more than two signs of the type shown in diagram 880 or 880.1 may be placed on any approach to an enforcement camera. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
