## 11. Repeater signs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-907fd3b5fca446123871eda90299d3a7][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to the signs shown in diagrams 614, 632, 636, 636.1, 636.2, 637.1, 637.2, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 637.2A,[^c-693292cb10ea97486a6c01622988649c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 637.3, 638, 638.1 when the arrow is omitted, 639 when the arrow is omitted, 639.1B, 640 when the arrow is omitted, 642, 646, 650.1, 650.2, 650.3, 660, 660.3, 660.4, 660.5, 660.6, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]660.7, 660.8, 660.9,[^c-a0460b7ab60d2f92f71cfa3473229b3a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 661A, 661.1, 661.2A,[^c-e16e4f86852440a62df3c1c49d7d993e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], 662, 670 (except when displayed on a variable message sign in the manner mentioned in regulation 58(7)(b)), 672, 956, 957, 959, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 959A,[^c-6a9d080c5589616d1bd9ff5afde7a64e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 959.1, 960, 960.1 , 960.2[^c-940a5d08f50f970f22eda7b74d39cf1f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] and 961. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2)[^c-445e34e2ddd6d16621c3053853e0e4eb] Subject to paragraphs (2A), (2B), (2C), (2D), (2E), (2F), (2G), (3) and (4), at least one of each of the signs to which this paragraph applies shall be placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) along a road which is subject to a restriction, requirement, prohibition or speed limit which can be indicated by the sign; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in the case of the signs shown in diagrams 614, 632, 642, 670 (except as mentioned in paragraph (1)), 672, 956, 957, 959, 959A, 959.1, 960, 960.1, 960.2 and 961, to face each stream of traffic to which the sign is intended to convey that restriction, requirement, prohibition or speed limit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2A)[^c-568a847759bedba487efdbfc43aed6c9] The sign shown in diagram 614 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) where the prohibition indicated by that sign applies only at a point on a road and not along a length of road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) along a length of road which is subject to the prohibition indicated by that sign if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) has only one carriageway; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] is shorter than 100 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2B) The sign shown in diagram 632 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) along a length of road which is subject to the prohibition indicated by that sign if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) has no junction with another road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) is shorter than 400 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2C) The sign shown in diagram 642 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) along a length of road which is subject to the prohibition indicated by that sign if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) is subject to a speed limit of 30 mph and is shorter than 800 metres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) is subject to a speed limit of 40 mph and is shorter than 1100 metres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) is subject to a speed limit of 50 mph and is shorter than 1350 metres; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) is subject to a speed limit of 60 mph or to a national speed limit and is shorter than 1600 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2D) The signs shown in diagrams 660 and 660.3 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) on a road within a permit parking area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2E) The sign shown in diagram 670 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) along a length of road which is subject to the speed limit indicated by that sign if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) is subject to a speed limit of 20 mph and is shorter than 200 metres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) is subject to a speed limit of 30 mph, is shorter than 200 metres and does not have a system of carriageway lighting furnished by lamps lit by electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) is subject to a speed limit of 40 mph and is shorter than 250 metres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) is subject to a speed limit of 50 mph and is shorter than 350 metres; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (e) is subject to a speed limit of 60 mph, is shorter than 400 metres and is a dual carriageway road or a motorway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2F) The sign shown in diagram 672 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) along a length of road which is subject to the speed limit indicated by that sign if that length of road is shorter than 250 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2G) The signs shown in diagrams 959 and 959A need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) along a length of road along which, or along part of which, there is a bus lane if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) has no junction with another road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) is shorter than 300 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3)[^c-aadc50f22dae76a7f730d71c4462a953] The sign shown in diagram 670— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) when varied to “20”, need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) on a road within an area into which each entrance for vehicular traffic has been indicated by the sign shown in diagram 674; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) when displayed on a variable message sign, need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (2) when the variable message sign is mounted on a gantry or other structure and is so placed that a traffic lane of the carriageway or an actively managed hard shoulder passes directly beneath it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) The sign shown in diagram 670 (except when displayed on a variable message sign in the manner mentioned in regulation 58(7)(b)) shall not be placed along— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a road on which there is provided a system of carriageway lighting furnished by lamps lit by electricity placed not more than 183 metres apart in England and Wales or not more than 185 metres apart in Scotland and which is subject to a speed limit of 30 mph; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a road[^c-d347dc1d013fb05a775f6a3302ff395a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] on which a national speed limit is in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5)[^c-2d657bbb5e91d5060bd651e8796b6522] Subject to paragraph (5A), at least one sign shown in diagram 671 shall be placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) along a length of road (other than a motorway) on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) there is a system of street or carriageway lighting furnished by lamps lit by electricity placed not more than 183 metres apart in England and Wales or not more than 185 metres apart in Scotland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] a national speed limit is in force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) to face each stream of traffic to which the sign is intended to convey that speed limit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5A)[^c-6a0487bf949a03bf18d389874cfddfb7] The sign shown in diagram 671 need not be placed in accordance with paragraph (5) along a length of road which is subject to the speed limit indicated by that sign if that length of road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) has only one carriageway and is shorter than 400 metres; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) is a dual carriageway road and is shorter than 450 metres. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5B) In this direction, “bus lane” has the meaning given in regulation 23. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
