## 18. Road markings to be placed only in conjunction with other road markings or specified signs (except road markings for prohibitions and restrictions on waiting etc) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-abdb64692518291365d64e2cfaac13c6][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) A road marking shown in diagram 1003 or 1023 when varied in size to conform with any of the smaller dimensions prescribed for it may be placed on a road only in conjunction with the marking shown in diagram 1057 and with one or more of the signs shown in diagrams 955, 956 and 957, or where a contra-flow cycle lane ends at a junction with another road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) The road marking shown in diagram 1064 may be placed on the carriageway of a road if and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
