**31.**[^key-cdf343529672259cdd2760319822ef0b][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] The sign shown in diagram 833, 834, 835 or 836 may be placed only to regulate the movement of vehicular traffic into and out of premises with more than one access to a road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
