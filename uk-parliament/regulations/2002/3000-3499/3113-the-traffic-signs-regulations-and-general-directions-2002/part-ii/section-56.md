# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Approval of types of sign and signals by the Secretary of State (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f9ac0c503fb3b09c580aa9fcda621b04][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The signs to which this direction applies may be placed on or near a road only if at the time that they are first placed they meet the requirements specified in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) This direction applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the signals prescribed by regulations 33, 34, 35, 37, 39, 41, 44, 45, 47, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 47A,[^key-83fa972458eb30404af6406bc715223b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 48, 49 and 52; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the matrix signs prescribed by regulation 46; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) the warning lights prescribed by regulations 50 and 51; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) the audible and tactile signals prescribed by regulations 47 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] , 47A[^key-6e2a6a9fff99f273de8d789abef13047] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] and 49; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (e) the signs shown in diagrams 618.3A, 776 and 2509.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (f) the signs shown in diagrams 2505, 2505.1, 2506, 2507, 2508, 2510, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 2511.1, 2512.1[^key-e2babab763dad6f0c56189e511fa34ca] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] and 2513, if varied to include a variable element; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (g) any light signals or signs shown in a diagram in Schedule 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (h) variable message signs and any other signs not continuously in use over a period of 24 hours and which are capable of being brought into and taken out of use by the operation of any electrical or other apparatus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The requirements referred to in paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) in all cases, that any equipment (including the content of all instructions stored in, or executable by it) used in connection with the signal or sign is of a type approved in writing by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^key-06ea6c6920a04d021666679943356178] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) in the case of the signals prescribed by regulation 39 or regulation 52, that any such equipment is of a type so approved as appropriate having regard to the number and disposition and the site of those signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Paragraph (3)(b) does not apply to signals displayed to indicate the effect of orders made, or having effect as if made, under section 1 of the Level Crossings Act 1983 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00063].

(5) If, after a signal or sign has been placed in accordance with an approval under paragraph (3), the signal, sign or any equipment used in connection with the signal or sign is altered, the signal or sign shall not be further used unless that alteration is approved in writing by or on behalf of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^key-656f117b0922c376564ae4e41e47fb27] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5].

(6) When any signal or sign has been placed in accordance with an approval under paragraph (3), the signal, the sign or any equipment used in connection with the signal or sign, including any alterations approved in accordance with paragraph (5), shall be regarded as continuing to be approved until notice is given in writing by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]—

- (a) to the traffic authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) to the supplier of the sign, signal or equipment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] where an alteration has been approved in accordance with paragraph (3), to the person who carried out the alteration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

of a date after which the signal, sign or equipment is no longer to be so regarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) Where notice is given under paragraph (6) that a signal, sign or any equipment used in connection with the signal or sign is no longer to be regarded as being approved that signal, sign or equipment shall be removed from the road on or before the date given in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
