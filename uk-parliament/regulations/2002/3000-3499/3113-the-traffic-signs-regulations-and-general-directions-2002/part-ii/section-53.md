# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 53

## 53. Placing of portable light signals prescribed by regulation 35 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5fa00b06f317cc61529eae3db4cdb04f][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A traffic authority may, pursuant to section 65(1) of the 1984 Act, cause or permit the placing of the portable light signals prescribed by regulation 35 if and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) permission is given in writing, or in a case of urgency, given orally and confirmed in writing as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) the written permission of the authority includes express approval to the placing of the signals at a particular site; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the authority is satisfied that the circumstances of the particular case fall within one of the sets of circumstances specified in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sets of circumstances referred to in paragraph (1)(b)(ii) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) where the signals are to be operated and maintained by, and are under the regular supervision of, a constable in uniform during the progress of a temporary scheme of traffic control; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) where the signals are placed on a length of road along which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) there is no junction carrying vehicular traffic to or from it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the width of the carriageway is temporarily restricted so as to carry only one line of traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) where the signals are placed at a level crossing when work in relation to that crossing is being carried out; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) where the signals are placed on a road which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) adjacent to the temporary site of road, building or engineering works; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] is used for the movement of vehicles, materials, plant or equipment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] into and out of[^key-6fcbe7e09945632ead54bd092bb8cc17] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the site of the works. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Nothing in this direction is to be taken to limit or affect the powers of a constable or the chief officer of police under section 67(1) of the 1984 Act (placing of temporary traffic signs on roads in extraordinary circumstances). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
