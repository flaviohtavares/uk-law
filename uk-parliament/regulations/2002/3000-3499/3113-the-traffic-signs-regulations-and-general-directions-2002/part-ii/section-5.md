# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Saving (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-04b3d1fadefb90e69f9bae4140914795][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any sign, which immediately before the coming into force of these Directions was— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) placed on or near any road in conformity with the Traffic Signs General Directions 1994; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) was by virtue of those Directions to be treated as placed in conformity with those Directions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

shall, so long as it would have continued to conform or be treated as conforming with those Directions if they had not been revoked, be treated as if placed in conformity with these Directions, notwithstanding any provision of these Directions to the contrary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
