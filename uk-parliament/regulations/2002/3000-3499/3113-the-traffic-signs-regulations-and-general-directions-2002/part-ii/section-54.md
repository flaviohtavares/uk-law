# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 54

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Placing of signals and other signs at crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-407000490e342eff9b5141d66de8dfa7][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 1 to these Directions[^key-48858ada7f5656d1f05c9e5f8631c6fa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] shall have effect with regard to the placing of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) vehicular light signals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) pedestrian light signals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) other signs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

at signal-controlled crossing facilities where all streams of vehicular traffic are stopped only for the purpose of enabling pedestrians, cyclists or equestrians to cross the carriageway.[^key-a237de8d264d27e14176451357c53079] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
