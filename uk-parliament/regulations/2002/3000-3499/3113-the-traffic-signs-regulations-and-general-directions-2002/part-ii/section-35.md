# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**35.**[^key-58bbda55a9dce8cd3b803920b72f69f4][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The road marking shown in diagram 1043 or 1044 shall not be placed at a gyratory system or roundabout. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The road marking shown in diagram 1044.1 may be placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) only on an area of carriageway at a gyratory system or roundabout; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) only if the entry of traffic into that area of carriageway is at all times controlled by traffic light signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The road marking shown in diagram 1044.2 may be placed only on an area of carriageway which is not greater than 4.5 metres wide at its narrowest point. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
