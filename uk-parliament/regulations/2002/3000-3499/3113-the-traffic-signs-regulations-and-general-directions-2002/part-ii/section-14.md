# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**14.**[^key-0e66fcf45bb1cfa78c7a6bc73a04c595][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The signs shown in diagrams 832.3, 1012.2, 1064, 2901, 2903, 2904, 2904.1, 2905, 2906, 2908, 2908.1, 2909, 2910, 2910.1, 2911, 2912, 2913, 2913.1, 2913.2, 2913.3, 2913.4, 2914, 2914.1, 2915, 2917, 2918, 2918.1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 2919.2[^key-259b8b7b2d40dc05a0d16c675be9d5ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], 2920.1, 2921, 2921.1, 2922, 2924, 2925, 2926, 2927, 2927.1, 2928, 2929, 2929.1, 2930, 2931, 2932, 2933, 2934, 7241 and 7286 may be placed only on or near a motorway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The signs shown in diagrams 2902 and 2902.1 may be placed only on a motorway or on another road at a junction with a motorway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
