# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**37.**[^key-54d925894e7aaca512ad0041560d6aa2][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The sign shown in diagram 790 or 7014— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) may be placed only in connection with works involving— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) an alteration in the layout of the carriageway of a road or level crossing; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the placing, removal or alteration of traffic signs on or near a road or at a level crossing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) may be retained not later than the end of the period of 3 months beginning with completion of those works. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1A)[^key-1933dc9b9775fc116ba0f10eddafa182] The sign shown in diagram 7014.1 may, in relation to a bridge under which a road passes, be placed only in connection with works involving a temporary or permanent reduction in the maximum height of vehicle able to pass, on the road, under the bridge (in this direction, “temporary reduction” and “permanent reduction” respectively). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1B) Where the sign shown in diagram 7014.1 is placed in connection with works involving a temporary reduction, it may be retained only until completion of those works. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1C) Where the sign shown in diagram 7014.1 is placed in connection with works involving a permanent reduction, it may be retained not later than the end of the period of 6 months beginning with completion of those works. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 7032— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) may be placed only to indicate the point at which a recently imposed speed limit of 30 mph begins on a road— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) on which there is a system of street or carriageway lighting furnished by lamps lit by electricity placed not more than 183 metres apart in England and Wales or not more than 185 metres apart in Scotland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] which had previously been subject to a higher speed limit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) shall be placed as near as practicable to that point; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) shall not be placed unless that point could not otherwise be indicated in accordance with these Directions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) may be placed only during the period of 6 months beginning with the day on which the 30 mph speed limit comes into force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (e) shall not be retained after the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
