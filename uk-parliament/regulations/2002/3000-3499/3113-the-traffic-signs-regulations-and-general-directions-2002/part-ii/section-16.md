# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Speed limits of 20 mph (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cb8bf71d2f135327161dc7d7d5f71882][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The sign shown in diagram 674 may only be placed on a road if no point on any road (not being a cul-de-sac less than 80 metres long), to which the speed limit indicated by the sign applies, is situated more than 50 metres from a traffic calming feature. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**traffic calming feature *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) a road hump constructed pursuant to section 90A of the Highways Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00048] (“the 1980 Act”) or section 36 of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00049] (“the 1984 Act”) and in accordance with the Highways (Road Humps) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00050] or the Road Humps (Scotland) Regulations 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00051];
- (b) traffic calming works constructed in accordance with section 90G of the 1980 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00052] or section 39A of the 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00053] and in accordance with the Highways (Traffic Calming) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00054] or the Roads (Traffic Calming) (Scotland) Regulations 1994 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00055];
- (c) a refuge for pedestrians which was constructed pursuant to section 68 of the 1980 Act or section 27(c) of the 1984 Act after 15th June 1999 and is so constructed as to encourage a reduction in the speed of traffic using the carriageway; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) a variation of the relative widths of the carriageway or of any footway pursuant to section 75 of the 1980 Act or section 1(1) or 2(1) of the 1984 Act which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) was carried out after 15th June 1999 for the purpose of encouraging a reduction in the speed of traffic using the carriageway; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] had the effect of reducing the width of the carriageway; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (e) a horizontal bend in the carriageway through which all vehicular traffic has to change direction by no less than 70 degrees within a distance of 32 metres as measured at the inner kerb radius. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1) the distance of 50 metres shall be measured along roads to which the speed limit indicated by the sign shown in diagram 674 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
