# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 49

## 49. Placing of road marking shown in diagram 1001.3 (zig-zag lines) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fcb6add0b135c5eeefddbcd5a4f1afa2][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this direction “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**relevant crossing facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” means a signal-controlled crossing facility— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) at which there is placed the signal shown in diagram *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 4002.1,[^c-7710c4e73e0358314e34140a6017c9f6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 4003.2, 4003.4 (whether or not placed with the signal shown in diagram 4003.4A)[^c-e3afb8d7b10f945d5c71f238c731a48e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], 4003.5 or 4003.7 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (whether or not placed with the signal shown in diagram 4003.7A)[^c-4c5bd393992e4f3096f98cd65ff04547] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) which is not situated at a junction at which the priority between motor vehicles is regulated by traffic light signals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (4) to (6), zig-zag lines shall be placed on the approaches to each side of a relevant crossing facility so as to form controlled areas as defined by regulation 27(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Zig-zag lines shall not be placed on a road except in accordance with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) paragraph (2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) regulations made, or having effect as if made, under section 25 of the 1984 Act (pedestrian crossings regulations). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) So long as the condition in paragraph (5) is complied with, paragraph (2) shall not apply where, in accordance with an authorisation given under section 64(1) or (2) of the 1984 Act, a relevant crossing facility has been provided on a road before 1st April 2003 but without the placing of zig-zag lines on the approaches as mentioned in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) The condition is that the signs and markings indicating the facility are maintained in accordance with the authorisation under which they were placed and any special directions under section 65 of the 1984 Act that were given in conjunction with the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(6) Paragraphs (4) and (5) shall cease to have effect on 1st January 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
