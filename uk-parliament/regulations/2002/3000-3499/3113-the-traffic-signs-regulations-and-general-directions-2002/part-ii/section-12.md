# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12. Signs to be placed only on specified types of road (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-95072c437da36132bb321762621bb54a][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The signs shown in diagrams 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2005.1, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010.1, 2010.2, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2021.1, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2030, 2030.1, 2032, 2033, 2034, 2035 and 2717 may be placed only on or near a primary route. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The signs shown in diagrams 2026, 2027, 2027.1, 2028 and 2029 may be placed only on a primary route, or on a non-primary route at a junction with a primary route. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
