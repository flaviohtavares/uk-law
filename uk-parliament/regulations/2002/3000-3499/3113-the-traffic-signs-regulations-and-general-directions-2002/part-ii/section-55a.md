# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 55A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55A. Signs used in conjunction with actively managed hard shoulders (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-547fcf2e463bd6205d46f5d9f89880c0][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The sign shown in diagram 5003 or 5003.1 may be displayed above an actively managed hard shoulder to convey the prohibition specified in regulation 44(4)(aa) only whilst variable message signs displaying the sign shown in diagram 670 or 671 are displayed above so much (if any) of the adjacent carriageway as is open to traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 6002 may be displayed over an actively managed hard shoulder only whilst variable message signs displaying the sign shown in diagram 670 are displayed above so much of the adjacent carriageway as is open to traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The sign shown in diagram 6031.1 may be displayed over an actively managed hard shoulder to convey the prohibition specified in regulation 38(4) only whilst variable message signs displaying the sign shown in diagram 670 are displayed above so much (if any) of the adjacent carriageway as is open to traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
