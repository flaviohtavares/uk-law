# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**42.**[^key-a72472c34826f926982ba00187e15de4][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) The back of any sign shown in a diagram in Schedules 1 to 5, 7, Part II of Schedule 10 or in Schedule 12, or prescribed by regulation 53, other than the sign shown in diagram 651, 970, 971, 972, 973.2, 973.3, 2610, 2610.1, 2610.2, 7101.1, 7102, 7103, 7104 or 7105 shall be coloured— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) black if the sign is mounted on the same post as that on which light signals prescribed by regulation 33(1) (or those signals as varied in accordance with regulation 34) or light signals prescribed by regulation 33(2) and regulation 35 are mounted; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) grey, black or in a non-reflective metallic finish in any other case, except that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) information about sites for placing and the ownership of the sign and an identification code for maintenance purposes may be indicated on the back of the sign in characters not exceeding 25 millimetres in height, where they are shown in a contrasting colour, or in characters not exceeding 50 millimetres in height, where they are embossed in the same colour; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] information about the manufacture of the sign required in order to comply with British Standard Specification *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS[^key-e48acc79e95be20d014c2fd459c95df9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EN 12899-1:2007 Incorporating corrigenda May 2008 and June 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] or a corresponding EEA Standard, occupying an area not exceeding 30 square centimetres, may be indicated on the back of the sign in characters not exceeding 5 millimetres in height. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall apply to the back of any backing board or other fitting provided for the assembly of such a sign as is referred to in paragraph (1) (including any container enclosing apparatus for the illumination of such a sign). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The back of the sign shown in diagram 7104 or 7105 shall be coloured grey, red, white, black or yellow, except that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) information about sites for placing and the ownership of the sign may be indicated on the back of the sign in characters not exceeding 50 millimetres in height, where they are shown in a contrasting colour, or in characters not exceeding 80 millimetres in height, where they are embossed in the same colour; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) information about the manufacture of the sign required in order to comply with British Standard Specification *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS[^key-576cd9093df66e31523e3ede41aec1a9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EN 12899-1:2007 Incorporating corrigenda May 2008 and June 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] or a corresponding EEA Standard, occupying an area not exceeding 30 square centimetres, may be indicated on the back of the sign in characters not exceeding 5 millimetres in height. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) The back of the sign shown in diagram 651, 970, 971, 972, 973.2, 973.3, 2610, 2610.1 or 2610.2 may be of any colour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) The front of any backing board for a sign mounted otherwise than as described in paragraph (1)(a) shall be coloured either grey or yellow *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] and, if coloured yellow, may be— .[^key-f67f1a87c499211a127181d5144b8a30] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) reflectorised; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) fluorescent; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) both reflectorised and fluorescent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(6)[^key-edc93be933590b49306a75c6ceeb8716] Except as provided in paragraph (7), a yellow backing board for a sign shall be rectangular in shape. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(7) A backing board for a combination of the sign shown in diagram 2402.1 with the sign shown in diagram 670 may be of any shape including rectangular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(8) The sign shown in diagram 2403.1 shall not be mounted on a backing board with any other sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(9) In this direction “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**backing board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]**” in relation to a sign includes any background (except a wall to which the sign is affixed) against which the sign is displayed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
