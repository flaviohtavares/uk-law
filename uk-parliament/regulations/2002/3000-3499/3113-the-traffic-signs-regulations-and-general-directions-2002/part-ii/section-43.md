# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 43. Mounting of the sign shown in diagram 781 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bffbf637504790685eeaeec3293ab551][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The sign shown in diagram 781 shall be mounted on two posts and so much of each post as extends above ground level shall be coloured black and white in alternate horizontal bands, each band being not less than 250 nor more than 335 millimetres deep. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
