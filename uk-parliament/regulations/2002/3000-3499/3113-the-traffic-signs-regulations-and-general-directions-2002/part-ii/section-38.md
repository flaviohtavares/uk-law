# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**38.**[^key-ec836d1e79b83505ab85e57297b8c067][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (1) No sign of the kind referred to in regulation 53 may be retained at any place for more than 6 months (or in the case of a sign of the kind referred to in regulation 53(1)(d) for more than 2 years) or such longer period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^key-cc9bd12a38619405c8103a97f36e8dee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] may approve after the placing of the sign there or in any event after the sign has ceased to be needed at that place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) The sign shown in diagram 2701 or 2701.1 may be retained on or near a road for not more than six months after completion of the housing development to which it refers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
