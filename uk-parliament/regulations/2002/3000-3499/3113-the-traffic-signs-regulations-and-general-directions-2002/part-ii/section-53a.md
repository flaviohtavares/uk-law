# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 53A

## 53A. Placing of portable signal-controlled pedestrian facilities (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-093c84aa5029705b4d932b28409cf2e6][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A traffic authority may, pursuant to section 65(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5], cause or permit the placing of a portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility if and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) permission is given in writing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the written permission of the authority includes express approval to the placing of the facility at a particular site. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Nothing in this direction is to be taken to limit or affect the powers of a constable or the chief officer of police under section 67(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] (placing of temporary traffic signs on roads in extraordinary circumstances). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
