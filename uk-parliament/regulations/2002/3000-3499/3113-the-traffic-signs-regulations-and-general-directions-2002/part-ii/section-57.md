# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

## 57. Studs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1801e5aadddbc70f62ece31e8816dd3a][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A stud incorporating reflectors or retroreflecting material, but no steady or intermittent light source, may be placed on a road if and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) it is a stud of a type which meets the requirements of paragraph (5); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) it is of a type approved by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority[^c-13cbe6e32896ecc50e751a8c585edc59] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] in accordance with direction 58. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) A stud incorporating both reflectors or retroreflecting material and a steady or intermittent light source, may be placed on a road if and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) so far as it incorporates reflectors or retroreflecting material it is of a type that meets the requirements of paragraph (5) and, so far as it incorporates a steady or intermittent light source, it is of a type approved by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] in accordance with direction 58; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) it is of a type approved by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] in accordance with direction 58. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) A stud incorporating a steady or intermittent light source and no reflectors or retroreflecting material may be placed on a road if and only if it is of a type approved by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] in accordance with direction 58. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) In paragraph (5) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] “the British Standards” means British Standards[^c-899a52728e007ba9a17310e7ccec6d67] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EN 1463-1:1998 Incorporating Amendment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] 1 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] BS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] EN 1463-2:2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] for retroreflecting road studs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
