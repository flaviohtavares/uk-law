# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 9

## 9. Beginning of a speed limit—further provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-77320d0ea90631fd4ba77359fd7277aa][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Direction 8(3) does not apply where a speed limit in force on the relevant road begins— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) at a point where the relevant road begins, being a point where it has no junction with another road; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) at a point where the relevant road has a junction with another road and the same speed limit is in force on both roads. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) This paragraph applies where the relevant road has a junction (“the junction”) with the side of another road (“the other road”) and a maximum speed limit is in force on the other road which is different from the speed limit in force on the relevant road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Where paragraph (2) applies, it is sufficient compliance with direction 8(2), for the purpose of indicating the beginning of the speed limit on the relevant road to traffic entering it from the other road, if the sign shown in diagram 670, 674 or 675 is placed not further than 20 metres from the junction, on the left hand or near side of the carriageway of the relevant road as viewed in the direction of travel of such traffic or, where the relevant road is a dual carriageway road, on the left hand or near side of the carriageway by which traffic may pass into the relevant road from the other road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Where paragraph (2) applies, for the purpose of indicating the speed limit in force on the other road to traffic entering that road from the relevant road, the sign shown in diagram 670, 674 or 675 shall (subject to paragraph (5)) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] be placed—[^c-930d8db697ba2afc38cf231da4cb3124] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) so as to be visible to such traffic; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) not further than 20 metres from the junction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) if the relevant road has only one carriageway, on each side of that carriageway; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] if the relevant road is a dual carriageway road, on each side of the carriageway by which traffic may pass from the relevant road into the other road. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(5) Paragraph (4) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the maximum speed limit in force on the other road is greater than that in force on the relevant road; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b)[^c-ece69508b3f3a0250eb2fef2cc0ea1bc] signs indicating the maximum speed limit have been placed on the other road in accordance with direction 11 not more than 100 metres from the junction and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) on each side of the junction; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] where traffic is permitted to enter the other road from the relevant road by proceeding onto the carriageway on only one side of the junction, on that side of the junction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
