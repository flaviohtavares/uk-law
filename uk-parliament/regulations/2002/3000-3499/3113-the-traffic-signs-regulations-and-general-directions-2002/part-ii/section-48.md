# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Placing of road marking shown in diagram 1014 in conjunction with the road marking shown in diagram 1013.1 or 1013.3 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b6033e90900e2ede221f22ead63e08a1][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) At least one road marking of the type shown in diagram 1014 shall be placed in conjunction with a road marking of the type shown in diagram 1013.1 or 1013.3 on the length of carriageway which extends backwards from the commencement of any continuous line marked on the carriageway as a part of the road marking shown in diagram 1013.1 or 1013.3, such commencement being viewed in the direction of travel of a vehicle driven so as to have and keep that continuous line on its right hand or off side in accordance with regulation 26(2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply where the commencement so viewed of a continuous line on the carriageway of a road falls immediately after— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the point where a central reservation ends; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) a roundabout from which traffic enters the carriageway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1), where a continuous line marked on the carriageway of a road as part of the road marking shown in diagram 1013.1 or 1013.3 is interrupted at a junction or by a refuge and recommences immediately after the interruption, the line shall be treated as if it continued through the interruption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) If more than one road marking of the type shown in diagram 1014 is placed on a length of carriageway, then the road markings shall be so spaced apart that one follows in line behind the other. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
