# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Placing of road markings and signs to indicate prohibitions and restrictions on waiting, loading and unloading and parking (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-30b7f32f2e79899acac5227b695640e3][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the road marking shown in diagram 1018.1 may be placed on a side of a road only for the purpose of indicating a statutory prohibition or restriction on the waiting of vehicles which applies on that side of the road at all times of day on every day of the year or on every day in a period of at least four consecutive months; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the road marking shown in diagram 1017 may be placed on a side of a road only for the purpose of indicating a statutory prohibition or restriction on the waiting of vehicles which is not a restriction of the kind mentioned in sub-paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) In this direction, references to a statutory prohibition or restriction on the waiting of a vehicle do not include references to any such prohibition or restriction imposed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) on waiting for the purpose of loading or unloading goods from a vehicle or picking up or setting down passengers from a vehicle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) and expressly to limit the duration of waiting of vehicles within a particular period; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) on the waiting of goods vehicles (as defined by section 192(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^f00056]) but of no other class of vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) The road marking shown in diagram 1017 or 1018.1 shall not be placed on a road on which is placed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the marking shown in diagram 1025.1, 1025.3, 1025.4 or 1028.2 (in conjunction with the sign shown in diagram 650.1); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) the marking shown in diagram 1027.1 in conjunction with the sign shown in diagram 642.2A, unless there are times at which the restrictions to be indicated by the marking shown in diagram 1017 or 1018.1 apply and those indicated by that sign do not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
