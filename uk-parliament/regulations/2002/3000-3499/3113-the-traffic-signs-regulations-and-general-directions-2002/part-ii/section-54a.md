# The Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002, section 54A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2016-05-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3113

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54A. Placing of signals and other signs at portable signal-controlled pedestrian facilities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-aedbbc175ce8e26acb22839e41534a19][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 2 to these Directions shall have effect with regard to the placing of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) vehicular light signals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) pedestrian light signals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) other signs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

at portable signal-controlled pedestrian facilities where all streams of vehicular traffic are stopped only for the purpose of enabling pedestrians to cross the carriageway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
