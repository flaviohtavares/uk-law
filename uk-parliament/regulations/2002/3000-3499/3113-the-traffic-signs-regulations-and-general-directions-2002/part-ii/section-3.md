## 3. Interpretation—general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-af059fb7538c53504e3ae45e7d9285b7][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-9d005aeb8bdc2505ae1a8758000816b8] In these Directions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) “one-way road” means a road on which the driving of vehicles otherwise than in one particular direction is prohibited; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) “permit parking area” means an area into which each entrance for vehicular traffic has been indicated by the sign shown in diagram 663.3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (c) “restricted parking zone” means an area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) into which each entrance for vehicular traffic has been indicated by the sign shown in diagram 663.2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] in which none of the road markings shown in diagrams 1017, 1018.1, 1019 and 1020.1 has been placed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (d) “two-way street” means a road which is not a one-way road; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (e) “the Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]” means the Traffic Signs Regulations 2002; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (f) the expressions listed in paragraph (2) have the same meaning as in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5].
