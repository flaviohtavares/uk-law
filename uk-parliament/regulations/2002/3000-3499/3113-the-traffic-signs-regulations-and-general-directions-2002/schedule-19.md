### PART I (affected by repeal, see note) — SIGNIFICANCE OF BUS STOP AND BUS STAND CLEARWAY MARKINGS (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 1. Interpretation of Part I of Schedule (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8348f7fd298fd9b84355f970fde16005][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

For the purposes of this Part of this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) “clearway” means an area of carriageway bounded by the continuous and broken straight yellow lines comprised in the road marking in diagram 1025.1, 1025.3 or 1025.4 and “bus stop clearway” means a clearway on which the words “BUS STOP” are marked; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a vehicle shall be taken to have stopped within a clearway if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) any point in the clearway is below the vehicle or its load (if any); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] the vehicle is stationary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 2. Prohibition conveyed by road markings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-34f72ccbc4fdad08d41077c4b4ad45c3][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

The road markings in diagrams 1025.1, 1025.3 and 1025.4 shall each convey the prohibition that, subject to the exceptions specified in paragraphs 3 and 4, no person driving a vehicle shall cause it to stop within the clearway— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) at any time, if the sign shown in diagram 974 or 975 placed in conjunction with the markings is varied so as to omit the reference to times of day; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) in any other case, during the period specified on that sign. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 3. Exceptions in favour of buses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a454a513b07ac96a9d65a3336f3c6466][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(1) Where the sign shown in diagram 974 or 975 is placed and is not varied to “except local buses”, nothing in paragraph 2 applies to the driver of a bus who causes the bus to stop within the clearway for so long as may be necessary— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to maintain a published timetable for the service (provided, in the case of a bus stop clearway, that the bus is not stopped within the clearway for a period exceeding two minutes); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to enable passengers to board or alight from the bus; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) to enable the crew of the bus to be changed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) Where the sign shown in diagram 974 or 975 is placed and is varied to “except local buses”, nothing in paragraph 2 applies to the driver of a bus being used in the provision of a local service who causes the bus to stop within the clearway for so long as may be necessary— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) to maintain a published timetable for the service (provided, in the case of a bus stop clearway, that the bus is not stopped within the clearway for a period exceeding two minutes); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) to enable passengers to board or alight from the bus; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) to enable the crew of the bus to be changed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 4. Other exceptions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3279cdaedfc6c34eb28b19767da88361][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(1) Nothing in paragraph 2 applies in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (aza (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b7e8a86f776b23f5c00ddcf318009f99][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] a vehicle being used for ambulance purposes or for the purpose of providing a response to an emergency at the request of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] NHS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] ambulance service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (ab (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5e4228d97389253eaeaf9cdac71cf1c6][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] so far as relating to the functions of the Serious Organised Crime Agency which are exercisable in or as regards Scotland and which do not (within the meaning of the Scotland Act 1998) relate to reserved matters, a vehicle being used for Serious Organised Crime Agency purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) anything done with the permission or at the direction of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) a constable in uniform; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] a traffic warden; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] [^key-29122e8cb05bb675a20f05ce534b4402]...
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] where the clearway is in a special parking area designated under Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991 or Schedule 3 to that Act, a parking attendant appointed under section 63A of the 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^f00041]; or[^key-03709c44cd0852c1b63883994f6b780f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] a traffic officer in uniform; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) a vehicle which is prevented from proceeding by circumstances beyond the driver’s control or which has to be stopped in order to avoid injury or damage to persons or property; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) a taxi which is stationary only for so long as may be reasonably necessary for a passenger to board or alight and to load or unload any luggage of the passenger; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) a marked vehicle which, whilst used by a universal service provider in the course of the provision of a universal postal service, is stationary only for so long as may be reasonably necessary for postal packets to be collected; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (g) a vehicle which is stationary in order that it may be used for one or more of the purposes specified in paragraph 5 and which cannot be used for such a purpose without stopping in the clearway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1)(e) the expressions “universal service provider”, “provision of a universal postal service” and “postal packet” shall bear the same meanings as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] Part 3 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^key-c5f0d515786c818bfa72e89104862d4c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] .

#### 5. Permitted purposes (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-707608374e383e5dd5068859b7031647][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

The purposes referred to in paragraph 4(g) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) any operation involving building, demolition or excavation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) the removal of any obstruction to traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (c) the maintenance, improvement or reconstruction of a road; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (d) constructing, improving, maintaining or cleaning any street furniture including bus stop infrastructure; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (e) the laying, erection, alteration, repair or cleaning of any sewer or of any main, pipe or apparatus for the supply of gas, water or electricity, or of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus[^key-bac4034281912184f82d4ee2142b0f40] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] kept installed for the purposes of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications code system *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] or of any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] electronic communications apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] lawfully kept installed in any position. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

### PART II (affected by repeal, see note) — SIGNIFICANCE OF BOX JUNCTION MARKINGS (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 6. Interpretation of Part II of Schedule (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-15171a56c8a22c71635fac5b7c67524f][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

In this Part of this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a)[^key-515efab1cafeb13c89b05410872a0c6e] “box junction” means the area of carriageway marked with yellow cross-hatching— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) at a junction between two or more roads; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] at a gyratory system or roundabout; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] where that area of carriageway is not greater than 4.5 metres wide at its narrowest point; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] on the length of road adjacent to the vehicular entrance to the premises of a fire, police or ambulance station, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) a reference (however expressed) to a vehicle which is stationary or stops within a box junction includes a vehicle which is stationary whilst part of it is within the box junction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

#### 7. Prohibition conveyed by markings in diagram 1043 or 1044 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-85bcb856345cfa5d42a87e6da4b568d2][^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(1) Subject to paragraph 9, the road markings shown in diagrams 1043, 1044 and 1044.1[^key-a75e72c678a7168aae5013c296df0130] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] shall each convey the prohibition that no person shall cause a vehicle to enter the box junction so that the vehicle has to stop within the box junction due to the presence of stationary vehicles. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

(2)[^key-76f322b463f98ffab9126a81a91bbe21] The prohibition in sub-paragraph (1) does not apply to any person where the box junction is at a junction between two or more roads (but not at a gyratory system or roundabout) and the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]

- (a) causes a vehicle to enter the box junction for the purpose of turning right; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
- (b) stops the vehicle within the box junction for so long as the vehicle is prevented from completing the right turn— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (i) by oncoming vehicles; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b] by vehicles which are stationary whilst waiting to complete a right turn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c04181b99fd16d713999f801bf7e1d8b]
