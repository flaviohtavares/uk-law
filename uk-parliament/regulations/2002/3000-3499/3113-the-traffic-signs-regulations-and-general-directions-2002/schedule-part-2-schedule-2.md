## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — PLACING OF SIGNALS AND OTHER SIGNS AT PORTABLE SIGNAL-CONTROLLED PEDESTRIAN FACILITIES (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a7d78c142bd76d1e00c2ef79f0af76af][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5][^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Direction 54A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 1. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

In this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- “approaching vehicular traffic” in relation to a portable crossing means vehicular traffic approaching the crossing along the carriageway across which the crossing is placed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “far side signals” means light signals of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4002.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “push button device” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (a) a sign of the size, colour and type shown in diagram 4003; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (b) a sign— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
    - (i) of the size, colour and type of only the part of the sign shown in diagram 4003.1 which includes the push button and the legend above it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
    - (ii) which, whilst the red signal is illuminated, in some way indicates to pedestrians whether the push button device has been activated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “system of staggered portable crossings” means two portable crossings provided on a road on which there is a central reservation and where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (a) there is one portable crossing on each side of the central reservation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (b) taken together the two portable crossings do not lie in a straight line; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- “portable crossing” means a portable signal-controlled pedestrian facility; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

references to the left or right hand side of a portable crossing are to the left or right hand side as viewed by approaching vehicular traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 2. Vehicular light signals on a two-way street (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

Subject to paragraph 4, on a two-way street, the number and disposition of light signals of the kind prescribed by regulation 35 which are placed at a portable crossing to control approaching vehicular traffic shall, in the case of a crossing of the type specified in column (2) of an item in the table, be as specified in column (3) of that item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

Subject to paragraph 4, on a one-way road, the number and disposition of light signals of the kind prescribed by regulation 35 to be placed at a portable crossing to control approaching vehicular traffic shall, in the case of a crossing of the type specified in column (2) of an item in the table, be as specified in column (3) of that item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 4. Additional vehicular light signals at portable crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

In addition to the signals required to be placed at a portable crossing by paragraph 2 or 3, the traffic authority may cause or permit to be placed such other primary or secondary signals as it thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 5. Far side light signals at portable crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(1) At least one set of far side signals and one push button device shall be placed at each end of a portable crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(2) Each set of far side signals shall be so placed as to be clearly visible to any person on the opposite side of the portable crossing who wishes to use the crossing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(3) Each push button device shall be so placed that the push button on it may be reached by any person wishing to press it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

(4) Where far side signals and push button devices have been placed at a portable crossing in accordance with this paragraph, there may be placed at the crossing as many devices of the kind mentioned in regulation 47A(7) as the traffic authority thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

### 6. Additional signs and road markings at portable crossings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

In addition to the signals required by this schedule to be placed at a portable crossing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]

- (a) the sign shown in diagram 7011.2— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) shall be placed to control approaching vehicular traffic; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] shall be so placed that it faces the stream of traffic to which it is intended to convey the meaning prescribed by regulation 43; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
- (b) any or all of the following may be placed at the crossing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (i) the road marking shown in diagram 1001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the road marking shown in diagram 1001.3 (but only when placed in conjunction with the road markings shown in diagrams 1001 and 1055.1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5] the road marking shown in diagram 1055.1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-719e96ccad5bb6f3737643df272d45a5]
