# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, shall apply in England and shall come into force on 31st January 2003.
