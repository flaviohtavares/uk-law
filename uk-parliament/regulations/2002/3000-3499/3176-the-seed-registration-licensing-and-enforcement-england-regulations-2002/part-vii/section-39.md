# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Form of records

Any record required to be kept under these Regulations may be in written or electronic form and shall be kept for at least three years.
