# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Fees

A licensed crop inspector may charge reasonable fees for carrying out his functions as a licensed crop inspector but shall not derive any private gain in connection with the carrying out of such functions.
