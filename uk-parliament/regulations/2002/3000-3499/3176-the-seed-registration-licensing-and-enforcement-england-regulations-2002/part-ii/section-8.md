# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Revocation of registrations

Subject to regulation 36, the Secretary of State may revoke a person’s registration relating to his entitlement to engage in a seed industry activity if—

- (a) she is satisfied that the person is not complying with any of the conditions to which his registration is subject;
- (b) she is satisfied that there is no longer available to the person such premises, machinery, equipment, facilities or personnel and services as are necessary to enable him to engage in the seed industry activity;
- (c) the person has been found guilty of committing any offence under section 16(7) of the Act; or
- (d) she is otherwise no longer satisfied that the person is a suitable person to engage in the seed industry activity.
