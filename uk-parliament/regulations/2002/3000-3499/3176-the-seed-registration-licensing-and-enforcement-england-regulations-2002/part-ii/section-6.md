# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Conditions to be complied with by registered persons

A registration granted under regulation 5 may be subject to any of the conditions specified in Part II of Schedule 1 and such other conditions as the Secretary of State may think necessary or desirable.
