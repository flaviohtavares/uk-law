# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Obligation to be registered

No person shall engage in a seed industry activity unless he is registered by the Secretary of State as a person who may engage in that activity.
