# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Suspension and revocation of licences

Subject to regulation 36, the Secretary of State may suspend or revoke a LSTS licence if it appears to her that—

- (a) there has been a breach of any conditions of the licence;
- (b) a person acting as a Analyst in Charge of a LSTS—
  - (i) is no longer qualified to act as such,
  - (ii) has failed to comply with the duties imposed on him by regulation 28, or
  - (iii) is otherwise unfit to perform his duties; or
- (c) a seed analyst carrying out statutory seed testing at the LSTS is no longer qualified to do so.
