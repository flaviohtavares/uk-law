# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Termination of licences

If a seed sampler requests the Secretary of State to terminate his licence the Secretary of State shall comply with the request.
