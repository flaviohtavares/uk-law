# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Fees

A licensed seed sampler may charge reasonable fees for carrying out his functions as a licensed seed sampler but shall not derive any private gain in connection with the carrying out of such functions.
