# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Applications for licences

(1) An individual may apply to the Secretary of State for a licence authorising him to take samples of seed of the species specified in Part I of Schedule 3 for the purposes of the Seed Marketing Regulations.

(2) An application made under paragraph (1) shall be made in such form and manner as the Secretary of State may require and shall be accompanied by such information as the Secretary of State may reasonably require.
