# The Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2002, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Variation of licences

Subject to regulation 36, the Secretary of State may vary a seed sampler’s licence as she thinks fit.
