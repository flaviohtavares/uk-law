# The Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Croydon Tramlink Class CR4000 Vehicles) Exemption (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3001

DISABLED PERSONS

TRANSPORT

The Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Croydon Tramlink Class CR4000 Vehicles) Exemption (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

4th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

5th December 2002

Coming into force

1st January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 47 of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted the Disabled Persons Transport Advisory Committee and such other persons as he considered appropriate in accordance with section 47(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Croydon Tramlink Class CR4000 Vehicles) Exemption (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st January 2003.

## 2. Amendment

In article 9 of the Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Croydon Tramlink Class CR4000 Vehicles) Exemption Order 2001[^f00002], for the words “31st December 2002” there shall be substituted “31st March 2005”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

4th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Croydon Tramlink Class CR4000 Vehicles) Exemption Order 2001 (S.I. [2001/3952](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3952)) by extending the time limit, from 31st December 2002 to 31st March 2005, on the exemption granted from the Rail Vehicle Accessibility Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/2456](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2456), amended by S.I. [2000/3215](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3215)) because the wheelchair space does not have a structure or fitting to prevent a wheelchair moving or tipping.
