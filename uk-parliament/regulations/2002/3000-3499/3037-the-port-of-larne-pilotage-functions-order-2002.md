# The Port of Larne (Pilotage Functions) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3037

PILOTAGE

The Port of Larne (Pilotage Functions) Order 2002

Made

9th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

10th December 2002

Coming into force

31st December 2002

Whereas Larne Harbour Limited is a harbour authority within the meaning of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] (“the 1987 Act”) for the port of Larne but is not a competent harbour authority within the meaning of the 1987 Act;

And whereas Larne Harbour Limited has applied to be treated as a competent harbour authority for the purposes of the 1987 Act;

And whereas the Secretary of State has informed the persons he considers may be affected by the Order, in accordance with section 1(7) of the 1987 Act;

And whereas no objection has been made to the making of this Order;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(4) of the 1987 Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the Port of Larne (Pilotage Functions) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 31st December 2002.

## 2. Application of the Pilotage Act 1987

Larne Harbour Limited shall be a competent harbour authority for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

9th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that Larne Harbour Limited is to be a competent harbour authority for the port of Larne for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

The applicant for the Order is Larne Harbour Limited which is a company registered in Northern Ireland having its registered office at 9 Olderfleet Road, Larne, Northern Ireland, BT40 1AS.
