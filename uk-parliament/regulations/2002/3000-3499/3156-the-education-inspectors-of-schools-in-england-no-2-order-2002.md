# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3156

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made

17th December 2002

Coming into force

18th December 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of December 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 18th December 2002.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 18th December 2002.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Paul Wagstaff
- Mary Hamby
- Sonja Oyen
- Andrew Bennett
- David Jones

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
