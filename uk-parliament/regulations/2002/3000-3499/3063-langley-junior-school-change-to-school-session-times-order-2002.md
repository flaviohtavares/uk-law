# Langley Junior School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3063

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

Langley Junior School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2002

Made

11th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

13th December 2002

Coming into force

6th January 2003

Whereas the governing body of Langley Junior School has, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001], consulted the local education authority which maintains the school, the parents of registered pupils and the staff working at the school:

Whereas it is the opinion of the Secretary of State, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2) of the Education Act 2002, that the implementation by the school of this project will contribute to the raising of educational standards achieved by children in England:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2 (1) of the Education Act 2002, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Langley Junior School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2002 and shall come, into force on 6th January 2003.

## 2. Exemption from School Sessions Regulations

The Changing of School Session Times Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body of Langley Junior School of Pendeen Crescent, Southway, Plymouth PL6 6QS.

## 3. Time limit

This Order shall have effect until 31st July 2003.

Charles Clarke

Secretary of StateDepartment for Education and Skills

11th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of Langley Junior School, Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31st July 2003.
