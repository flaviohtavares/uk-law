# The Asylum Support (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3110

IMMIGRATION

The Asylum Support (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2002

Made

16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

18th December 2002

Coming into force

8th January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 95(12) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 8 to, the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Asylum Support (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 8th January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 3 of the Asylum Support Regulations 2000

For regulation 3(4) of the Asylum Support Regulations 2000 (“the 2000 Regulations”)[^f00002], there is substituted—

> (4) The application may not be entertained by the Secretary of State—
>
> - (a) where it is made otherwise than in accordance with paragraph (3); or
> - (b) where the Secretary of State is not satisfied that the information provided is complete or accurate or that the applicant is co-operating with enquiries made under paragraph (5).

.

## 3. Amendment of the Schedule to the Asylum Support Regulations 2000

The Schedule to these Regulations, which provides for amendments to be made to the Application Form and Notes found in the Schedule to the 2000 Regulations, shall have effect.

Beverley Hughes

Minister of State

- (a) After “If you do tick “Yes”, please provide details in the box about your claim for asylum or appeal.”, insert—

  > This includes information on your route to the United Kingdom, method of entry to the United Kingdom and when and where you applied for asylum. If you did not apply for asylum at the port immediately on arrival, you will also need to provide an explanation.

**8.** In Note 13, for the words “or in the area where we may offer you accommodation”, substitute “, in the area where we may offer you accommodation or in the area where you may seek support when asylum support ends”.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Asylum Support Regulations 2000 (“the 2000 Regulations”) ([S.I. 2000/704](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/704)) by providing that the Secretary of State may not entertain an application for support where he is not satisfied that the information provided is complete or accurate or that the applicant is co-operating with his enquiries. The Regulations also amend the Application Form for support set out in the Schedule to the 2000 Regulations.
