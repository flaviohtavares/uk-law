# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) Order 2002, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2007-10-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/3135
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Main objective of the General Council

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1 (the General Medical Council) after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) The main objective of the General Council in exercising their functions is to protect, promote and maintain the health and safety of the public.
