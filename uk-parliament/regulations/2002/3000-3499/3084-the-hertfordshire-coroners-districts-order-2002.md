# The Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been reprinted in consequence of an error in the original print and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2002 No. 3084

CORONERS

The Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 2002

Made

16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

17th December 2002

Coming into force

1st January 2003

Whereas

(1) the Council of the County of Hertfordshire have, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the Coroners Act 1988[^f00001] and after due compliance with the provisions of the Coroners (Orders as to Districts) Rules 1927[^f00002], submitted to the Secretary of State a draft Order providing for the alteration of the existing division of that county into coroners' districts; and

(2) the Secretary of State has made such modifications of the draft Order as he thinks fit;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 2002.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 2003.

(3) This Order shall not have effect in relation to any inquest begun before the day on which it comes into force or to any post-mortem examination which, before that day, a coroner has directed or requested a medical practitioner to make.

**2.** The existing coroners' districts of Hitchin (as constituted by the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1974[^f00003]) and West Hertfordshire (as constituted by the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00004]) shall be amalgamated to form a single coroner’s district called the West and North Hertfordshire Coroner’s District.

**3.** Accordingly, the County of Hertfordshire shall be divided into two coroners' districts and each such coroner’s district shall consist of the area specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order opposite to the name of the coroner’s district in question as given in column 1 of that Schedule.

**4.** The Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1974 and the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts (Amendment) Order 1998 are hereby revoked.

Hilary Benn

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

16th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

The County of Hertfordshire was divided into five coroners' districts by the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1974. Two of these, Watford and St Albans were amalgamated to form the Watford/St Albans Coroner’s District by the Hertfordshire (Coroners' Districts) (Amendment) Order 1974. This district was then amalgamated with Hemel Hempstead, to form the West Hertfordshire Coroner’s District, by the Hertfordshire (Coroner’s Districts) (Amendment) Order 1998.

This Order replaces the two coroners' districts of Hitchin and West Hertfordshire by the district of West and North Hertfordshire (as set out in the Schedule to this Order). The Schedule also sets out the remaining district (Hertford Coroner’s District) of the two districts into which the County of Hertfordshire is now divided.
