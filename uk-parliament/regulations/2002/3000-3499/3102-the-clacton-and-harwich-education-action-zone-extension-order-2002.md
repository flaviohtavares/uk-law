# The Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3102

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made

16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

20th December 2002

Coming into force

10th January 2003

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating therein.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 10th January 2003.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order—

- “Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

## 3. The extension of the Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone

The Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 10th January 2003.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

16th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Clacton and Harwich Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
