# The Air Quality Limit Values (Amendment) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3117

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Air Quality Limit Values (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made

16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament

17th December 2002

Coming into force

10th January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by subsection (2) of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001], being a Minister designated[^f00002] for the purpose of that subsection in relation to the control of air pollution, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Quality Limit Values (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 10th January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Air Quality Limit Values Regulations 2001

(1) The Air Quality Limit Values Regulations 2001 are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (definitions)—

- (a) in the definition of “relevant pollutants”, for the words “particulate matter and lead” there are substituted the words “particulate matter, lead, benzene and carbon monoxide”; and
- (b) the website address appearing in the definition of “zone” is replaced by “http://www.defra.gov.uk/environment”.

(3) There is inserted a new regulation 2A—

> **2A. Designation of competent authority**
>
> The Secretary of State is designated as the competent authority for the purposes of article 3 (implementation and responsibilities) of Council Directive [96/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0062) on ambient air quality assessment and management[^f00003].

(4) In regulation 7(6) (reference methods for relevant pollutants)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (b) the word “and” is omitted; and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (c) there is added—

  > - (d) the sampling and analysis of benzene; and
  > - (e) the analysis of carbon monoxide

  .

(5) In regulation 7(9) (measurement of volume of relevant pollutants), for the words “nitrogen dioxide and oxides of nitrogen” there are substituted the words “nitrogen dioxide, oxides of nitrogen, benzene and carbon monoxide”.

(6) In regulation 11(1) (powers to give directions relating to air quality)—

- (a) after the word “management” the word “and” is replaced by a comma; and
- (b) after the words “lead in ambient air” there are added the words “and of European Parliament and Council Directive [2000/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0069) relating to the limit values for benzene and carbon monoxide in ambient air[^f00004]”.

(7) After regulation 13(3) (public information) there is added—

> (3A) Information on ambient concentrations of benzene, as an average value over the last 12 months, shall be updated—
>
> - (a) where practicable, on a monthly basis;
> - (b) in all other cases, as a minimum on a three-monthly basis.
>
> (3B) Information on ambient concentrations of carbon monoxide, as a maximum running average over eight hours, shall be updated—
>
> - (a) where practicable, on an hourly basis;
> - (b) in all other cases, as a minimum on a daily basis.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made to implement in England European Parliament and Council Directive [2000/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0069) relating to limit values for benzene and carbon monoxide in ambient air[^f00007]. They amend the Air Quality Limit Values Regulations 2001[^f00008] which in turn implement Council Directive [96/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0062) on ambient air quality assessment and management[^f00009] (the Air Framework Directive) and Council Directive [1999/30/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0030) relating to limit values for sulphur dioxide, nitrogen dioxide and oxides of nitrogen, particulate matter and lead in ambient air[^f00010].

The parts of the Air Quality Limit Values Regulations 2001 which these Regulations amend apply only in England.

Regulation 2 amends those Regulations by: adding a new regulation 2A designating the Secretary of State as competent authority for the purposes of the Air Framework Directive; adding benzene and carbon monoxide as “relevant pollutants”; setting limit values and margins of tolerance for those substances; determining criteria for location and numbers of sampling points; setting reference methods for the analysis and sampling of those substances; and making consequential amendments.

Regulation 2(10) amends Schedule 2 to those Regulations to give effect to Commission Decision [2001/744/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0744) amending Annex V to Council Directive [1999/30/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0030) relating to the limit values for sulphur dioxide, nitrogen dioxide and oxides of nitrogen, particulate matter and lead in ambient air[^f00011].
