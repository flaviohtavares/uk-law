# The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 256

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

12th February 2002

Laid before Parliament

22nd February 2002

Coming into force

18th March 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 96 of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 18th March 2002.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

**2.** There shall be inserted in Schedule 1 to the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1991[^f00002] (designation of countries and territories for the purposes of sections 96 and 97 of the Criminal Justice Act 1988), at the appropriate places in alphabetical order, the entries for those countries and territories specified in the Schedule to this Order.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

| Designated country or territory | Appropriate authority |
| --- | --- |
| The Bailiwick of Guernsey | Her Majesty’s Attorney General for the Bailiwick of Guernsey |
| The Bailiwick of Jersey | Her Majesty’s Attorney General for the Bailiwick of Jersey |
| Liechtenstein |  |
| Russia |  |
| Slovakia |  |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1991. It adds the countries and territories listed in the Schedule to this Order to the list of countries in Schedule 1 to that Order.
