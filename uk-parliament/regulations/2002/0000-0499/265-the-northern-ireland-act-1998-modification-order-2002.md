# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 265

NORTHERN IRELAND

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2002

Made

12th February 2002

Laid before Parliament

22nd February 2002

Coming into force

15th March 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

WHEREAS Her Majesty deems it expedient to modify the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00001] and to do so by Order in Council in pursuance of section 87(7) of that Act:

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 87(7) of the said Act and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased by and with the advice of Her Privy Council to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 15th March 2002.

## 2. Modification of the Northern Ireland Act 1998

At the end of subsection (6) of section 87 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00002] there shall be added the following paragraph—

> - (p) the Social Security Fraud Act 2001[^f00003] and the Social Security Fraud Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00004]

.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the Social Security Fraud Act [2001 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/11) and the Social Security Fraud Act (Northern Ireland) [2001 (c. 17 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2001/17)) to the legislation listed in section 87 of the Northern Ireland Act [1998 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/47). The effect of this amendment will be to render those Acts subject to the provisions in that section concerning consultation and co-ordination between the Secretary of State and the Northern Ireland Minister having responsibility for social security.

This Order does not impose any charge on business.
