# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocation of the 2000 Regulations and operation of the 2001 Regulations

(1) The 2000 Regulations and the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (No.2) Regulations 2000[^f00009] shall be revoked on 1st April 2002.

(2) A school’s budget share for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2001 may only be redetermined in the financial year beginning on 1st April 2002 in accordance with the provisions of the 2001 Regulations or the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001[^f00010] if such redetermination makes provision for—

- (a) pupils permanently excluded or admitted following a permanent exclusion; or
- (b) the correction of errors, whether in calculation or data, under any of the provisions of those Regulations.
