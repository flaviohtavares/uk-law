# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Pupil numbers: Dual registration

Where a pupil is, in accordance with regulations made under section 434 of the 1996 Act[^f00013], a registered pupil at more than one school then that pupil shall be treated as being a full-time pupil at each such school unless the authority expressly provide otherwise in their formula.

## Annotations

[^f00013]: Footnote: The relevant regulations, as at the date that these Regulations are made, are the Education (Pupil Registration) Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/2089](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2089)) which remain in force by virtue of paragraph 1 of Schedule 39 to the 1996 Act. Amending instruments are [S.I. 1997/2624](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2624) and [S.I. 2001/2802](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2802).
