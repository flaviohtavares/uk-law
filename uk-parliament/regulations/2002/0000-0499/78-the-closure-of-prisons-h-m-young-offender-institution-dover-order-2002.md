# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Dover) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 78

PRISONS

The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Dover) Order 2002

Made

17th January 2002

Laid before Parliament

18th January 2002

Coming into force

8th February 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Dover) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 8th February 2002.

**2.** The young offender institution known as H.M. Young Offender Institution Dover and situate in the county of Kent shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

Beverley Hughes

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

17th January 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Young Offender Institution Dover on 8th February 2002.
