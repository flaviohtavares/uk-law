# The Chester Guided Busway Order 2002, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Town and country planning

The busway system shall be regarded as a road transport undertaking for the purposes of section 262(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (meaning of “statutory undertakers”).
