# The Chester Guided Busway Order 2002, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Power to charge fares

The Council may demand, take and recover or waive such charges for carrying passengers or goods on the busway system, or for any other services or facilities provided in connection with the operation of that system, as it thinks fit.
