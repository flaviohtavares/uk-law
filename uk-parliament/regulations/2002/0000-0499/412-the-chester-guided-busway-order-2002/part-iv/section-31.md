# The Chester Guided Busway Order 2002, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Power to contract for police services

(1) Agreements may be made—

- (a) between the Council and the chief officer of police of any police force and the police authority; or
- (b) between the Council and the Strategic Rail Authority,

for making available to the Council for the purposes of the operation of its busway system the services of members of the police force or, as the case may be, members of the British Transport Police Force on such terms as to payment or otherwise, and subject to such conditions, as the parties to them think fit.

(2) In this article—

- (a) “chief officer of police”, “police authority” and “police force” have the same meaning as in the Police Act 1964[^f00017], and
- (b) “the British Transport Police Force” means the force organised under the British Transport Police Scheme 1963[^f00018].
