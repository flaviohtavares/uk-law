# The Chester Guided Busway Order 2002, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Withholding of consent

Where it is provided in this Order that any consent or approval is not to be unreasonably withheld, any dispute as to whether it is unreasonably withheld or as to any conditions subject to which it is given shall be determined by arbitration.
