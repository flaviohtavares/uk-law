# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 252

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2002

Made

12th February 2002

Laid

22nd February 2002

Coming into force

31st March 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty In Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 1(1) and 1(4) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 31st March 2002.

**2.** David Robert Bell is hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England for a period of 5 years from 1st May 2002.

**3.** The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2000[^f00002] and the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2001[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints David Robert Bell to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England for the period from 1st May 2002 to 30th April 2007.
