# The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) (Revocation) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 468

ANIMALS, ENGLAND

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) (Revocation) Regulations 2002

Made

4th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

4th March 2002

Coming into force

6th March 2002

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, being designated [^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) (Revocation) Regulations 2002 and shall extend to England.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 6th March 2002.

## 2. Revocation of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) Regulations 2002

The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) Regulations 2002[^f00003] are revoked.

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

4th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision in relation to England to give effect to article 3 of Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (OJ No. L50, 21.2.2002, p. 98) which repealed Commission Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740) as amended concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L277, 20.10.2001, p. 30). The Regulations revoke the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) Regulations 2002 (S.I. [2002/119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/119)) which implemented the last amendment to Commission Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
