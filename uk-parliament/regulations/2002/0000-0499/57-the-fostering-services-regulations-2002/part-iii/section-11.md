# The Fostering Services Regulations 2002, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Independent fostering agencies—duty to secure welfare

The registered person in respect of an independent fostering agency[^f00011] shall ensure that—

- (a) the welfare of children placed or to be placed with foster parents is safeguarded and promoted at all times; and
- (b) before making any decision affecting a child placed or to be placed with foster parents due consideration is given to—
  - (i) the child’s wishes and feelings in the light of his age and understanding; and
  - (ii) his religious persuasion, racial origin and cultural and linguistic background.

## Annotations

[^f00011]: Footnote: Similar duties already apply to a fostering agency within the meaning of section 4(4)(b) of the 2000 Act by virtue of section 61 of the Children Act 1989, and to a local authority by virtue of section 22 of the Children Act.
