# The Fostering Services Regulations 2002, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Revocation

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Foster Placement (Children) Regulations 1991[^f00022];
- (b) regulation 2 of the Children (Short-term Placements) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 1995[^f00023];
- (c) regulation 3 of the Children (Protection from Offenders) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 1997[^f00024];
- (d) regulation 2 of the Children (Protection from Offenders) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00025] so far as it amends the Foster Placement (Children) Regulations 1991; and
- (e) regulation 2 of the Foster Placement (Children) and Adoption Agencies Amendment (England) Regulations 2001[^f00026].
