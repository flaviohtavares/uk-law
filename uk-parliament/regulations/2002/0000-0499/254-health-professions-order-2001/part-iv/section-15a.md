# Health Professions Order 2001, section 15A

## 15A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Exercise of function of approving courses for approved mental health professionals (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7][^c-0ebb3333d43071847978e30e4bfb861e][^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7] The function under section 114ZA(1) of the Mental Health Act 1983 (approval of courses for approved mental health professionals in England) is exercisable by the Education and Training Committee. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7] In relation to AMHP courses run outside the United Kingdom, section 114ZA(1) of that Act applies only in relation to courses run by institutions which are approved in accordance with article 15B(3). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7] In this article and articles 15B to 18, “ *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]**AMHP course *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]**” means a course of the kind referred to in section 114ZA(1) of the Mental Health Act 1983. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8672ee5af0f51739d241916dd76058f7]
