## 15B (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Criteria for approving courses for approved mental health professionals (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867][^c-0ebb3333d43071847978e30e4bfb861e][^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] The Council must set the criteria to be applied in exercising the function under section 114ZA(1) of the Mental Health Act 1983. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] The Education and Training Committee must— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] ensure that universities and other bodies in the United Kingdom concerned with the provision of AMHP courses are notified of the criteria set under paragraph (1); and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] take appropriate steps to satisfy itself that the AMHP courses provided by such bodies meet those criteria. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] In performing the function under paragraph (2)(b), the Committee may, in particular, approve or arrange with others to approve institutions which the Committee considers to be properly organised and equipped for conducting AMHP courses. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] The Council must from time to time publish a statement of the criteria set under paragraph (1). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867] An AMHP course is to be treated for the purposes of articles 16 to 18 as a relevant course of education or training. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed0d7c4f47d6e8138000065e8bc2a867]
