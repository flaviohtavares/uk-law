# Health Professions Order 2001, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/254
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Supplementary and transitional provisions and extent[^key-cca056a6f74790b28438fc6fb43b1bbe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-4253ae3ae9b8d4d021a26cfb60a152a5][^key-741f303110fcbf7d897424f20d2b3beb] The transitional provisions contained in Schedule 2 to this Order shall apply.

(2)[^key-4253ae3ae9b8d4d021a26cfb60a152a5][^key-a3651e5a413c6f57927ee9f4c4665c37] The Privy Council may by order make such further transitional provisions as it considers appropriate.

(3)[^key-22da1ffcce994de0bede5984ac30011c][^key-8a115d5ffbad6bf79d9da03d78a9f8b0][^key-4eab8fcb7192fa172246357db06f0b70] The consequential amendments contained in Schedule 4 to this Order shall apply.

(4)[^key-cca056a6f74790b28438fc6fb43b1bbe] This Order extends to the whole of the United Kingdom.
