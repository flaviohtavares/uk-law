# Health Professions Order 2001, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/254
>

## 28. The Health Committee[^c-589cfde54718c98736f6b541f54cb8a1][^c-bb32de125d85378fad07a4c5f912f2b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Health Committee shall consider—

- (a) any allegation referred to it by the Council, Screeners, the Investigating Committee or the Conduct and Competence Committee; and
- (b) any application for restoration referred to it by the Registrar.
