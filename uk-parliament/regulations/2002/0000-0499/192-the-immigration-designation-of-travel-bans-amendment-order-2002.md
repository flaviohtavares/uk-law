# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 192

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

1st February 2002

Laid before Parliament

5th February 2002

Coming into force

6th February 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 6th February 2002.

**2.** At the beginning of Part 1 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] (Resolutions of the Security Council of the United Nations) there shall be inserted:

- “Resolution 1390 (2002) of 16th January 2002 (Afghanistan)”.

Jeff Rooker

Minister of State

Home Office

1st February 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (“the 2000 Order) by adding Resolution 1390 (2002) of 16th January 2002 to the list of resolutions adopted by the Security Council of the United Nations which are designated for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 (as inserted by section 8 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999). Any person named by or under a designated instrument, or of a description specified in it, will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).
