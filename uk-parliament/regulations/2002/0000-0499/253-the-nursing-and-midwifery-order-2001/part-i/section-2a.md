# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 2A

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2A. Application to nursing associates[^key-b1f5c9ba87e2d18aba32796cf8b94861]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Order that apply to nursing associates, and only to the extent that they apply to nursing associates, apply in respect of England only.
