# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 54

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Supplementary and transitional provisions and extent[^c-889fabfd14aa3d6e7c792747b8d80817][^c-21f13504c753e983a491bf86b213d6d2][^c-175560dfbe2003e25fd3d4d12a333c73][^c-f4af4b4ef620d8ca27052bf57fc14007][^c-501f272c400d9e6c555af0372c25e3aa][^c-65fa47edbda0346521a3f16cbcd32f4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions contained in Schedule 2 to this Order shall apply.

(2) The Privy Council may by order make such further transitional provisions as it considers appropriate.

(3) The consequential amendments contained in Schedule 5 to this Order shall apply.

(4) This Order extends to the whole of the United Kingdom.
