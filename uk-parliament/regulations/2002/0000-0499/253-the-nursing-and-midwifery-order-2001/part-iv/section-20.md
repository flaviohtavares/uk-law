# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 20

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Wales[^c-ca80099d927b89d388ee696afb6de781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The National Assembly for Wales may create or designate a body with which the Council may enter into any such arrangements as are referred to in article 15(6) of this Order in order to perform its function under article 15(5)(b) in respect of the standards established under article 15(1) or 19(4) or (6).
