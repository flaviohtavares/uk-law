# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 5

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Establishment and maintenance of register[^c-4c91179ea0e3f5ea638cf72cdf8d550d][^c-febe62511e63cc4f06f6d2278cab873b][^c-6e03e142e926a4072d8188241f7ff27c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In accordance with the provisions of this Order the Council shall establish and maintain a register of qualified nurses, midwives and nursing associates[^c-51d119d404c09c88fb08072e0d9cc863].

(2) The Council shall from time to time—

- (a) establish the standards of proficiency necessary to be admitted to the different parts of the register being the standards it considers necessary for safe and effective practice under that part of the register; and
- (b)[^c-4c91179ea0e3f5ea638cf72cdf8d550d] prescribe the requirements to be met as to the evidence of good health and good character in order to satisfy the Registrar that an applicant is capable of safe and effective practice as a nurse, midwife or nursing associate[^c-8d7a90951b114f8632339243968140a6].

(3) The Council shall—

- (a)[^c-febe62511e63cc4f06f6d2278cab873b] before prescribing the requirements mentioned in paragraph (2)(b), consult [^c-fef6a8a555cd34c7b8d8f1806a5f1c45]... the persons referred to in article 3(14); and
- (b) publish those requirements.

(4) The register shall show, in relation to each registrant, such address and other details as the Council may prescribe.
