# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 26B

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26B. Review of decisions by the Council[^c-b7c194f510110413dc17c01ac26e3f9b][^c-66502f12c73077ee8d20134de9c65df3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Council may review a decision—

- (a) that there is no case to answer made by the Investigating Committee under article 26(2)(d)(i);
- (b) that there is no case to answer made by the Registrar or any other officer of the Council pursuant to rules made under article 26A;
- (c) to agree undertakings with the person concerned made by the Investigating Committee under article 26(5A);
- (d) to agree undertakings with the person concerned made by the Registrar or any other officer of the Council pursuant to rules made under article 26A;
- (e) made pursuant to rules made under article 26(5B) that undertakings no longer apply and that the allegation must not be considered further.

(2) The Council may make rules in connection with carrying out reviews under paragraph (1).
