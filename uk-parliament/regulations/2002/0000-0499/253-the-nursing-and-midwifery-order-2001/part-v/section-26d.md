# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, section 26D

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26D. The Fitness to Practise Committee[^key-ca9e7dfc02ea8f8a3c8169f3f25a585c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Fitness to Practise Committee must consider—

- (a) an allegation referred to it by the Council [^key-5716d6ea2e314828b7cacbcd0df669f6]... or the Investigating Committee; and
- (b) an application for restoration referred to it by the Registrar.
