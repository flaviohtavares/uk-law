# The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 3) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2624

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 3) Order 2002

Made

17th October 2002

Laid before Parliament

24th October 2002

Coming into force

14th November 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 1(5) and (6) of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 3) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 14th November 2002.

## 2. Designation as a public body

Active Life Limited[^f00002], being a person appearing to the Secretary of State to be exercising a function of a public nature, shall be a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970, in its application to England[^f00003].

## 3. Restrictions on agreements—Active Life Limited

Agreements which may be entered into by Active Life Limited by virtue of this Order shall be subject to the following restrictions—

- (a) that any such agreements are for the supply of goods or materials, or the provision of administrative, professional or technical services to it by Canterbury City Council; and
- (b) that any such agreements are entered into during a period in respect of which it is in receipt of a grant from Canterbury City Council; and
- (c) that any such agreements are made in connection with the provision of recreational facilities in the area of Canterbury City Council.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister

17th October 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order designates Active Life Limited as a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 (“the 1970 Act”).

The effect of the designation is to allow Canterbury City Council to provide goods and services to Active Life Limited by way of an agreement for any purposes prescribed by section 1(1) of the 1970 Act.

Article 3 imposes restrictions on the agreements that can be made by Active Life Limited by virtue of this Order.
