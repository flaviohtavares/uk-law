# The Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2749 (C. 84)

COPYRIGHT

TRADE MARKS

CRIMINAL LAW

The Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made

5th November 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 7(2) of the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002[^f00001] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

**2.** The Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 shall come into force on 20th November 2002.

Melanie Johnson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Competition, Consumers and Markets,

Department of Trade and Industry

5th November 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 into force on 20th November 2002.
