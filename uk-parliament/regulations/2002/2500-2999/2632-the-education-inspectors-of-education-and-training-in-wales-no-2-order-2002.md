# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2632

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made

22nd October 2002

Coming into force

20th November 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of October 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 20th November 2002.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru with effect from 20th November 2002.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Susan Jean Willan
- Susan Lesley Roberts
- Elizabeth Jane Arthur

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or, in Welsh, Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.
