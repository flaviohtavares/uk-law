# The Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Modification) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2587

NORTHERN IRELAND

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

The Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Modification) Order 2002

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

14th October 2002

Laid before Parliament

15th October 2002

Coming into force

15th October 2002

Whereas the Secretary of State considers it expedient for this Order to be made without a draft having been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6 of the Northern Ireland Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Modification) Order 2002 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

**2.** At the end of paragraphs 8(3) and 9(4) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000, for “money provided by Parliament” there shall be substituted “the Consolidated Fund of Northern Ireland”.

John Reid

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

14th October 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order modifies the Northern Ireland Act 2000 in consequence of provision made under section 2 of that Act, suspending devolved government in Northern Ireland, on 14th October 2002.

The Order provides that during suspension expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State in exercising the functions of the Assembly Commission and in relation to members' salaries etc. is to be defrayed from the Consolidated Fund of Northern Ireland.
