# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002, section 91

## 91. Effect of demise of the Crown[^c-80f38e271b737d76d114f1ad9d4c5d3d]

*Extent: S*

A proclamation dissolving the Parliament as mentioned in section 2(5) of the 1998 Act shall not be affected by the demise of the Crown.
