# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2002-11-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/2779
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Consequential amendment[^c-c699181a4000be850054e1b8a66be838]

*Extent: S*

In the definition of “election petition” in rule 69.1 of the Rules of the Court of Session[^f00038] (interpretation of Chapter 69 - election petitions), for “article 85 of and Schedule 6 to the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 1999” substitute “article 81 of and Schedule 6 to the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002”.
