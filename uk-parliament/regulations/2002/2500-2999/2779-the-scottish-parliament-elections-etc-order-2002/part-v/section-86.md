# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002, section 86

## 86. Advertisements[^c-9af0a30f27aca4d5d30dfcce146143ed]

*Extent: S*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00036] shall have effect in relation to the display on any site in Scotland of an advertisement relating specifically to a Scottish parliamentary election as they have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a parliamentary election.
