# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2002, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocations

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, the following Orders are hereby revoked:–

- (a) the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 1999[^f00003];
- (b) the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) (Amendment) Order 2001[^f00004];
- (c) the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) (Amendment (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00005];
- (d) the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order[^f00006],

as from the coming into force of this Order.

(2) Notwithstanding the revocation of the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 1999, the modification of enactments contained in Schedule 8 to that Order shall continue to have effect.
