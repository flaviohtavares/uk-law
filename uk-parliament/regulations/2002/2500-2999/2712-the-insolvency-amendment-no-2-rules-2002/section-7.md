# The Insolvency (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 2002, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment to Rule 13.9

After paragraph (2) of Rule 13.9 there is inserted—

> (3) A reference to an “**authorised person**” is a reference to a person who is authorised pursuant to section 389A[^f00003] of the Act to act as nominee or supervisor of a voluntary arrangement proposed or approved under Part I or Part VIII of the Act.

.

## Annotations

[^f00003]: Footnote: Section 389A was inserted into the Insolvency Act [1986 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/45) by section 4(4) of the Insolvency Act [2000 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/39).
