# The Insolvency (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 2002, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In these Rules references to the “**principal Rules**” are to the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00002] and a Rule referred to by number alone means the Rule so numbered in the principal Rules.

(2) These Rules shall be construed as one with the principal Rules.
