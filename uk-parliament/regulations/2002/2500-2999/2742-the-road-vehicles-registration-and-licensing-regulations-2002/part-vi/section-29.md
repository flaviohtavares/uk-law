# The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 2002, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Application of Regulations to Crown vehicles

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as provided by this Part of these Regulations, nothing in these Regulations applies to a vehicle kept by the Crown.

(2) Nothing in these Regulations or this Part of these Regulations applies to a vehicle kept by the Crown which is used or appropriated for use for naval, military or air force purposes.
