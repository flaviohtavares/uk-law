# The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 2002, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Change of keeper: general provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations 21 to 25 have effect subject to the provisions of this regulation.

(2)[^key-03db24e14a0e421330a2a394287080c5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) So far as they provide for the issue of a new registration document, regulations 21 to 25 have effect subject to the provisions of regulation 15 and 15A[^key-4223144b5780008d835e7071701634ac].

(4) Paragraph (5) shall apply and regulations 21 to 25 shall not apply where—

- (a)[^key-c27ac4e853ce11d7023273611b0d3c51] a vehicle has sustained structural damage and the cost of repair, or the total cost of repair and associated ancillary costs, would exceed the value of the vehicle when repaired or it has been replaced with another vehicle under a policy of insurance; and
- (b) either the keeper of the vehicle does not have the benefit of a policy of insurance or cover note which covers the damage or the keeper is an insurer.

(5) On a change of keeper to which this paragraph applies the keeper shall forthwith surrender the registration document to the Secretary of State or, if an insurer, destroy it.
