# The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 2002, section 17

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Notification of destruction or permanent export of a vehicle[^c-542dd1659612927e27b9745c4ee3b39f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registered keeper of a vehicle must immediately notify the Secretary of State if that vehicle is sent permanently out of the UK.

(2) The Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit, accept a notification under paragraph (1) by electronic means.

(3) A notification under paragraph (1) may be made on the registered keeper’s behalf by a vehicle trader by, or to whom, the vehicle is sold or disposed of.
