# The Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) (Revocation) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2691

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) (Revocation) Order 2002

Approved by the House of Commons

Made

28th October 2002

Laid before the House of Commons

29th October 2002

Coming into force

1st December 2002

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 13 of the Customs and Excise Duties (General Reliefs) Act 1979[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) (Revocation) Order 2002 and comes into force on 1 December 2002.

**2.** The Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) Order 1992[^f00002] and the Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) (Amendment) Order 1999[^f00003] are revoked.

M J Eland

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House,

22 Upper Ground,

LONDON SE1 9PJ

28 October 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1 December 2002, revokes the Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/3155](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3155)) and the Excise Duties (Personal Reliefs) (Amendment) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/1617](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1617)).
