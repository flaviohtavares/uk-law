# The National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2621

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE ENGLAND

The National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2002

Made

17th October 2002

Laid before Parliament

18th October 2002

Coming into force

11th November 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 11(1), (2) and (4)(a), and, 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 11th November 2002.

(2) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Amendment of the National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1995

In article 4(b) of the National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1995[^f00002] (constitution of the Authority) in paragraph (b) for the word “three” substitute the word “four”.

David Lammy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

17th October 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Health Service Litigation Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1995 to increase the number of members of the Authority who are not officers of the Authority from three to four.
