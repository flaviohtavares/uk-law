# The District of Waveney (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2983

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The District of Waveney (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

2nd December 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Electoral Commission[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 17[^f00002] and 26[^f00003] of the Local Government Act 1992, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the District of Waveney (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment to the 2001 Order

(1) The District of Waveney (Electoral Changes) Order 2001[^f00004] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Article 6(2) the words “Wangford with Henham” shall be removed.

(3) In Article 6(3) the words “Wangford with Henham” shall be inserted before the words “and Wrentham”.

(4) In Article 6(4) the words “Wangford with Henham” shall be inserted before the words “and Wrentham”.

(5) In Article 6(7) the words “Wangford with Henham” shall be inserted before the words “and Wrentham”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the District of Waveney (Electoral Changes) Order 2001 by providing for the parish of Wangford with Henham to hold elections in 2003 rather than in 2002.

That Order was made by the Secretary of State. The functions of the Secretary of State under sections 13 to 15 and 17 of the Local Government Act 1992, so far as they relate to electoral changes, were transferred with modifications to the Electoral Commission by the Local Government Commission for England (Transfer of Functions) Order 2001 (S.I. [2001/3962](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3962)) with effect from 1st April 2002.
