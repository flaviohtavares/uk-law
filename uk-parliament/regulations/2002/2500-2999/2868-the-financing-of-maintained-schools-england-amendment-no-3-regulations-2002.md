# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2868

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 2002

Made

15th November 2002

Laid before Parliament

26th November 2002

Coming into force

17th December 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 47 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 17th December 2002.

(2) These Regulations shall apply only in relation to England.

## 2. Revocation of the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2002

The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Amendment of the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002

(1) The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(2) After the words “In this regulation” in regulation 17(4), there shall be inserted “and in regulation 17A”.

(3) The following shall be inserted after regulation 17:—

> **17A.** (1) A secondary school’s budget share for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2002 shall be redetermined by 31 March 2003 or, if earlier, within one month of the receipt by the local education authority of written notification from the LSC of a revised allocation (“the revised allocation”) in respect of the sum referred to in paragraph (1) of regulation 17 (“the original amount”).

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2002 and further amend the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2002. They provide for the redetermination of a school’s budget share in accordance with a specific formula in the event of the revision by the Learning and Skills Council of the amount allocated in respect of a school’s sixth form.
