# The Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2955 (C. 94)

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

The Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002

Made

29th November 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on her by section 7(2) of the Office of Communications Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002.

**2.** Subsections (7) and (8) of section 1 of the Office of Communications Act 2002 shall come into force forthwith.

Patricia Hewitt,

Secretary of State for Trade and Industry

29th November 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 1(7) and (8) of the Office of Communications Act 2002 into force upon signature of the Order.

Sections 4 to 7 came into force upon Royal Assent (19th March 2002).

Sections 1 (except for subsections (7) and (8)), 2 and 3 and the Schedule (except for paragraph 12) came into force on 1st July 2002.

Paragraph 12 of the Schedule is not yet in force.
