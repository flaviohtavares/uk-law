# The Heart of Slough Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2775

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Heart of Slough Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made

7th November 2002

Laid before Parliament

8th November 2002

Coming into force

1st December 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating therein.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Heart of Slough Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st December 2002.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “Heart of Slough Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Heart of Slough Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

## 3. The extension of the Heart of Slough Education Action Zone

The Heart of Slough Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st December 2002.

David Miliband

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

7th November 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)
