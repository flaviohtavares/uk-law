# The Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2846

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made

20th November 2002

Laid before Parliament

2nd December 2002

Coming into force

23rd December 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 20th day of November 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 39 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 [^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002, and shall come into force on 23rd December 2002.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

**2.** There shall be inserted in Schedule 1 to the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1996[^f00002] (designation of countries and territories for the purposes of sections 39 and 40 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1994), at the appropriate place in alphabetical order, the territory specified in the Schedule to this Order.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE 1

| Designated territory | Appropriate authority |
| --- | --- |
| The Bailiwick of Guernsey | Her Majesty’s Attorney General for the Bailiwick of Guernsey |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the territory listed in the Schedule to this Order to the list of designated countries and territories in Schedule 1 to the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1996.
