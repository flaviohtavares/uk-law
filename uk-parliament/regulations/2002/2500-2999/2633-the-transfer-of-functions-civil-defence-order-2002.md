# The Transfer of Functions (Civil Defence) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2633

MINISTERS OF THE CROWN

The Transfer of Functions (Civil Defence) Order 2002

Made

22nd October 2002

Laid before Parliament

4th November 2002

Coming into force

25th November 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of October 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1 of the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transfer of Functions (Civil Defence) Order 2002.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 25th November 2002.

## 2. Civil Defence Act 1948

A function of the Secretary of State which arises by virtue of the proviso to section 9(2) of the Civil Defence Act 1948[^f00002] (ministerial responsibility) shall be exercisable concurrently with the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster to exercise any function of the Secretary of State arising under the proviso to section 9(2) of the Civil Defence Act 1948. The effect of the Order is that the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster will be able, in the same way as a Secretary of State, to exercise any function under the Act which has not been specifically designated by Order in Council as the function of another Minister.
