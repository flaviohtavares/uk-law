# The Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises) Revocation Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No.721

DISABLED PERSONS

The Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises) Revocation Order 2002

Made

14th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

22nd March 2002

Coming into force

27th May 2002

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 52(11) of the Disability Discrimination Act[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises) Revocation Order 2002 and shall come into force on 27th May 2002.

## 2. Revocation

The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 Code of Practice on the Rights of Access to Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises (ISBN No. 0-11-271055-7) referred to in the Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises) Order 1999[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Maria Eagle

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

14th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes with effect from 27th May 2002 the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 Code of Practice on the Rights of Access to Goods, Facilities, Services and Premises, issued by the Secretary of State on 29th June 1999.
