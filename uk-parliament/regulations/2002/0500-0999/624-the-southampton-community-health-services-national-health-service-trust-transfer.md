# The Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 624

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002

Made

8th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and (4) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes or proposed changes in the administration of the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust and having consulted that Trust, the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust, the New Forest Primary Care Trust and the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust[^f00002];
- “the new Trusts” means the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003], the New Forest Primary Care Trust[^f00004] and the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust[^f00005];
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the four schedules of trust property more specifically described in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of article 2 of this Order.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the two schedules of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 28th September 2001 and 19th November 2001 and on behalf of the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 16th October 2001 and on 22nd November 2001 and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00006], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

(2) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the New Forest Primary Care Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 28th September 2001 and on behalf of the New Forest Primary Care Trust on 29th October 2001, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00007], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the New Forest Primary Care Trust.

(3) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 28th September 2001 and on behalf of the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust on 29th October 2001, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00008], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust.

**3.** (1) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust and any reference to the old Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust.

(2) In paragraph (1) of this article references to the relevant new Trust are to be read as references to the new Trust to which the property which is the subject of the trust instrument has been transferred under article 2 of this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

8th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Southampton University Hospitals National Health Service Trust, the New Forest Primary Care Trust and the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedules specifying the trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
