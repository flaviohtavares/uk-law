# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 637

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2002

Made

7th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 48 and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales[^f00002].

**2.** The provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to fees charged in relation to those matters mentioned in column 2 of that Schedule) shall have effect as if, for the references to the sums specified in those provisions (which, by virtue of the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 2002[^f00003] and the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2001[^f00004] had effect immediately before the coming into force of this Order as if, for those references, there were substituted references to the sums specified in column 3 of that Schedule), there were substituted references to the sums specified in column 4 of that Schedule.

**3.** In the Schedule to the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 2000, the entry relating to section 48(3)(b) of the Gaming Act 1968 is hereby revoked, and the Schedule to the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) (England and Wales) Order 2001 is hereby revoked.

Richard Caborn

Minister of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

7th March 2002
