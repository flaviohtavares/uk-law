# The Scottish Administration (Offices) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 801 (S. 3)

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, SCOTLAND

The Scottish Administration (Offices) Order 2002

Made

26th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

27th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th March 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of March 2002

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 126(8)(b) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Scottish Administration (Offices) Order 2002 and shall come into force on the appointed day.

(2) In this Order–

- “the appointed day” means the day on which section 7(1) of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00002] comes into force; and
- “ the Drinking Water Quality Regulator for Scotland” means the person appointed under that section.

## 2. Specification of office

The office of the Drinking Water Quality Regulator for Scotland is hereby specified for the purposes of section 126(8)(b) of the Scotland Act 1998.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the office of the Drinking Water Quality Regulator as an office in the Scottish Administration which is not a ministerial office for the purposes of the Scotland Act 1998.

This enables funds to be paid to the Regulator under section 65 of the Scotland Act 1998 and places the Regulator under specific accounting obligations under the Public Finance and Accountability (Scotland) Act 2000.
