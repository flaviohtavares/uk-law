# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 51. Products incorrectly marked with a UK marking[^M_E_e256cee2-e40b-4508-cdd7-50edbf23ba8e][^c-264a205b1f0507af4ce872b959433e3b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No person shall—

- (a) affix the UK marking[^c-f27ad25696d09ff09dac7348c40ab625] for a medical device to a product which is not a medical device; or
- (b) supply a product (if that supply is also a placing on the market, or if that supply is of a product which has been placed on the market) which has affixed to it the UK marking for a medical device if that product is not a medical device.

(2) No person shall provide information comprising a UK marking for a medical device on a product, the instructions for use for a product, or the sales packaging for a product if the product is not a medical device.

## 51. Products incorrectly marked with a CE marking[^M_E_6ef5d008-96a3-4e1c-9b2f-0454a43416b9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person shall—

- (a) affix the CE marking for a medical device to a product which is not a medical device; or
- (b) supply a product (if that supply is also a placing on the market, or if that supply is of a product which has been placed on the market) which has affixed to it the CE marking for a medical device if that product is not a medical device.

(2) No person shall provide information comprising a CE marking for a medical device on a product, the instructions for use for a product, or the sales packaging for a product if the product is not a medical device.
