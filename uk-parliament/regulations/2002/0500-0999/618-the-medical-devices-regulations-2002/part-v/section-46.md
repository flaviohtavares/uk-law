# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Choice of approved bodies and conformity assessment bodies[^M_E_404224d9-7eda-4bea-a10a-293d14a7a86e][^c-c306276ed4b60ccc04b40f669913e26d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a conformity assessment procedure involves the intervention of an approved body[^c-56bac5baf950c97b874b9d2afe605d94], including work which may be carried out by a third country conformity assessment body, the manufacturer of a device or the manufacturer’s UK responsible person[^c-30fdf6516b10a11a75db54e4d0b940d2] may apply to any approved body[^c-749d5555606c2d0290bd946d034ebe2f] or third country conformity assessment body to carry out tasks under that procedure which are within the framework of tasks which the body is designated to carry out.

## 46. Choice of notified bodies and conformity assessment bodies[^M_E_cb168b7b-4ad6-49c0-b3fc-6c9764f122a6]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a conformity assessment procedure involves the intervention of a notified body, including work which may be carried out by a third country conformity assessment body, the manufacturer of a device or his authorised representative may apply to any notified body or third country conformity assessment body to carry out tasks under that procedure which are within the framework of tasks which the body is designated to carry out.
