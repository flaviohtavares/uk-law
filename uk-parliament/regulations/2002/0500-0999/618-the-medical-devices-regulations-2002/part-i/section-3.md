# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 3. Scope of these Regulations[^M_E_42f89574-2304-40d1-ef2c-12f80e3ce13b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations shall not apply to—

- (a) medicinal products governed by the Human Medicines Regulations 2012[^c-486839a141fb8b8a9ae3a747c4e445c8] (including medicinal products derived from human blood or human plasma [^c-afad7da28d8925bcbdc590ffdd18efc6]...);
- (b) human blood, human blood products, plasma or blood cells of human origin;
- (c) devices that incorporate, at the time of placing on the market, human blood, blood products, plasma or blood cells of human origin, except for —[^c-8c2e7ad861e81ffd3a3116a532e6ebc9]
  - (i) stable derivatives devices,
  - (ii) active implantable medical devices and accessories to such devices, and
  - (iii) *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and accessories to such devices,;
- (d) transplants or tissues or cells of human origin or products incorporating or derived from tissues or cells of human origin , except for[^c-fcbc3e101f1f9b84c4f650112b67cce5] [^c-84e06f47d2c55300f790ab3748060cb6]..., *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and accessories to such devices save where medicinal products are incorporated as ancillary to the device[^c-9edd093cc6f2f9fc0c10b8cf1a419ee9];
- (e)[^c-ce1db6c7fa7fd713474a6a97a5d97eb0] transplants or tissues or cells of animal origin, unless—
  - (i) a device is manufactured utilising animal tissue which is rendered non-viable or non-viable products derived from animal tissue, or
  - (ii) a product is [^c-04f01c34aaec7fa7e71c485315f51768]... an *in vitro* diagnostic medical device, or an accessory to such a device;
- (f) cosmetic products governed by Regulation[^c-d16a53929a1f841b30839a0ee72bcc53] [(EC) 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1223) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30th November 2009 on cosmetic products; or
- (g)[^c-a1ed0c13a0daa9e32f020f308b4718a5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 3. Scope of these Regulations[^M_E_855db655-938f-4aa0-e7e5-19d9a40d3ae3]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations shall not apply to—

- (a) medicinal products governed by Directive 2001/83 (including medicinal products derived from human blood or human plasma governed by Title X of Directive 2001/83);
- (b) human blood, human blood products, plasma or blood cells of human origin;
- (c) devices that incorporate, at the time of placing on the market, human blood, blood products, plasma or blood cells of human origin, except for —[^V_NI_c80480e1-c507-2d7c-4406-024b32ebe0f4]
  - (i) stable derivatives devices,
  - (ii) active implantable medical devices and accessories to such devices, and
  - (iii) *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and accessories to such devices,;
- (d) transplants or tissues or cells of human origin or products incorporating or derived from tissues or cells of human origin , except for[^V_NI_95a5d6a1-529f-a1ee-8430-83dff7670ac9] [^V_NI_c2dc0a64-b433-1bc5-df97-122faeb067fc]..., *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and accessories to such devices save where medicinal products are incorporated as ancillary to the device[^V_NI_a7ed39e2-9fbe-9dc9-7e19-2e51de884b41];
- (e)[^V_NI_1a74a4b8-80d8-877a-859d-6401c7abdaaa] transplants or tissues or cells of animal origin, unless—
  - (i) a device is manufactured utilising animal tissue which is rendered non-viable or non-viable products derived from animal tissue, or
  - (ii) a product is [^V_NI_fc3f4d35-e139-6961-3795-f11010831f7b]... an *in vitro* diagnostic medical device, or an accessory to such a device;
- (f) cosmetic products governed by Council Directive [76/768/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1976/768) [^V_NI_af3d41cb-68d0-a622-fb97-9a2365e1414c], as amended [^V_NI_760918c3-c3c8-347d-d879-46c8f05f8d81]; or
- (g)[^V_NI_0f8fd2f2-b58b-f4ca-100b-3c2066c61285] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
