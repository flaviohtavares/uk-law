## 4I. Revocation of Commission Decision 2010/227[^c-71c76f52f3cab801a75b265a945fa2e0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Commission Decision 2010/227/EU of 19 April 2010 on the European Databank on Medical Devices (Eudamed) is revoked.
