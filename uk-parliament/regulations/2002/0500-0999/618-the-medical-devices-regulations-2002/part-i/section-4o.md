# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 4O

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4O. Revocation of Regulation (EU) 2017/745[^c-71c76f52f3cab801a75b265a945fa2e0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulation (EU) 2017/745 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2017 on medical devices amending Directive [2001/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2001/83), Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/178) and Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1223) and repealing Council Directives [90/385/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1990/385) and [93/42/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1993/42) (“**the Medical Devices Regulation**”) (insofar as it is retained EU law) is revoked.
