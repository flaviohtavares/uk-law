# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 2A

## 2A. Medical devices which are qualifying Northern Ireland goods[^c-41c3ff483063ca319edb58a580def5c9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (1A),[^c-8982d39f915224a2ef67960892225e20] notwithstanding the effect of regulations 19B, 19C, 30A, 44ZA and 44ZB and the expiry of the period during which those regulations apply by virtue of regulation 1ZA, any medical device—

- (a)[^c-a607472b0851c4afff1886a93d22476e] which meets the requirements of—
  - (i) these Regulations as they apply in Northern Ireland;
  - (ii) [Regulation (EU) 2017/745](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0745); or
  - (iii) [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746); and
- (b) which is a qualifying Northern Ireland good,

may be placed on the Great Britain market as if it meets the requirements of these Regulations as they apply in Great Britain.

(1A)[^c-6c9a14d506f35638fdb564ff62ce5dd6] Before 25 July 2024, paragraph (1) only applies to a coronavirus test device that meets the requirements of [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746) if the device also meets the requirements of—

- (a) regulation 34A (approval requirement for coronavirus test devices); or
- (b) the common specifications set out in Annex I and XIII to [Regulation (EU) 2022/1107](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2022/1107).

(2) In this regulation,

- (a)[^c-29e9271dd4e66d833fe0fd957038a3a2] “qualifying Northern Ireland good” has the meaning given in regulations made under section 8C(6) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018;
- (b)[^c-9844e00bb777904f9a597b414a497004] “Regulation (EU) 2017/745” means Regulation (EU) 2017/745 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2017 on medical devices, amending [Directive 2001/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0083), Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) and Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) and repealing Council Directives [90/385/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0385) and [93/42/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042).
