# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 6

## 6. Scope of Part II

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The requirements of this Part in respect of relevant devices apply in respect of medical devices (including stable derivatives devices), accessories to such devices, single-use combination products, and systems and procedure packs, other than—

- (a) active implantable medical devices and accessories to such devices; and[^c-39397e20a427b56ffb6ff9ea311ef489]
- (b) *in vitro* diagnostic medical devices and accessories to such devices; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be614a949e31d2cb6693ff8d8bca039a]
- (c) devices that come within the scope of Directive 93/42 and another Directive (“the other Directive”) issued by one or more of the institutions of the Community, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6ed9be893f425212aede04c88c95c8e]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6ed9be893f425212aede04c88c95c8e] the other Directive includes a provision allowing the manufacturer of the device to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, which set of arrangements applies to it, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6ed9be893f425212aede04c88c95c8e]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6ed9be893f425212aede04c88c95c8e] the manufacturer chooses to follow the set of arrangements in the other Directive. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6ed9be893f425212aede04c88c95c8e]
