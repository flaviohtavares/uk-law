(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] Paragraph (2) applies— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] in relation to relevant devices that are neither Class I devices nor custom-made devices, to— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] a manufacturer with a registered place of business in Northern Ireland who, under their own name, places on the market in Northern Ireland any general medical device of any class, other than a system or procedure pack which is not CE marked; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ac169835b9902bb0365198cba792911c][^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (iii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] a manufacturer’s authorised representative who has a registered place of business in Northern Ireland; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (iv (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] a manufacturer with a registered place of business in Great Britain whose authorised representative does not have a registered place of business in Northern Ireland; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] in relation to Class I devices *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] that are not custom-made devices[^c-14785b52ab6cf681e2f9d2178b50d1b9] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db], to— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] a manufacturer who places a device on the Northern Ireland market and has a registered place of business in Northern Ireland; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] an authorised representative with a registered place of business in Northern Ireland; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] to a person with a registered place of business in Northern Ireland who sterilises before use any devices designed by their manufacturer to be sterilised before use. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] For the purpose of enabling the Secretary of State to exercise the Secretary of State’s functions under these Regulations, any person to whom this paragraph applies must— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]

- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] supply the Secretary of State with a description of each category of device concerned; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60770717bcc4869859f859999a028f3e][^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
- (d (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] in the case of an authorised representative, supply the Secretary of State with— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] written evidence that they have been designated as an authorised representative; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] details of the person who has so designated them; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
  - (iii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] where the person placing the devices concerned on the market is neither the manufacturer nor the authorised representative, the name and address of the registered place of business of the person placing the devices concerned on the market; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
- (e (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] inform the Secretary of State of any changes to the information referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) to (d) as and when such changes arise. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db] The obligation in paragraph 2(2)(e) to inform the Secretary of State of any changes in relation to the information referred to in sub-paragraphs (2)(a) to (d) continues to apply following the passing of any of the dates specified in paragraph (4) that apply in respect of a particular case. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d73bfbee6a45e3391a0a52b3b77d31db]
