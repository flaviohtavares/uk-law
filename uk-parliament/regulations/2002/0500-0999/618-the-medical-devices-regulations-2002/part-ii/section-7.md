# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 7. Classification of general medical devices[^M_E_a73921dd-bbcb-4471-e132-1b7e54f08f26]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of this Part and Part VI, devices are classified as belonging to Class I, IIa, IIb or III in accordance with the classification criteria set out in Annex IX of Directive 93/42 , read with Directive 2003/12[^c-aa504f8f26add175b5320922e2dbb0b4] and Directive 2005/50[^c-a91dab04b9cf3925d48111fbf74f66ec].

(2) In the event of a dispute between a manufacturer and an approved body[^c-56de218bdf74892db91275be97e6fbb3] over the classification of a device, the matter shall be referred to the Secretary of State, who shall determine the classification of the device in accordance with the classification criteria set out in Annex IX of Directive 93/42 , read with Directive 2003/12 and Directive 2005/50.

## 7. Classification of general medical devices[^M_E_bcf320bf-d50a-4c65-c84f-f26488551ca1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part and Part VI, devices are classified as belonging to Class I, IIa, IIb or III in accordance with the classification criteria set out in Annex IX of Directive 93/42 , read with Directive 2003/12[^V_NI_1b9b854c-4104-0b4d-54a8-e79adb1373ce] and Directive 2005/50[^V_NI_cb10a0d3-7d50-f38f-bbee-2c15eddbe950].

(2) In the event of a dispute between a manufacturer and a notified body over the classification of a device, the matter shall be referred to the Secretary of State, who shall determine the classification of the device in accordance with the classification criteria set out in Annex IX of Directive 93/42 , read with Directive 2003/12 and Directive 2005/50.
