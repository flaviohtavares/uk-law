# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 8. Essential requirements for general medical devices[^M_E_c4f406a1-e574-4f8f-a74b-6a2a0194fe8b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulation 12, no person shall place on the market or put into service a relevant device unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex I which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^c-4ad737077025bc412ab3fa5f3ac400ed]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

(2) Subject to regulation 12, no person shall supply a relevant device—

- (a) if that supply is also a placing on the market or putting into service of that device; or
- (b) in circumstances where that device has been placed on the market or put into service,

unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex I which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^c-c75d039ec1be653656aab2a2d77f1287]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

(3)[^c-40edf50161a7376ec06f3d8fa4572b06] Where a hazard exists, devices which are also machinery shall also meet the essential health and safety requirements set out in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008[^c-5e1e6b036e5a577d96c1bea553e7058b] to the extent to which those essential health and safety requirements are more specific than the essential requirements to Directive 93/42.

## 8. Essential requirements for general medical devices[^M_E_a3891a9d-c3d4-4e17-fc5e-f198c2a2a71a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 12, no person shall place on the market or put into service a relevant device unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex I which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^V_NI_6d49408f-954a-f4e2-778d-2ea383613ac3]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

(2) Subject to regulation 12, no person shall supply a relevant device—

- (a) if that supply is also a placing on the market or putting into service of that device; or
- (b) in circumstances where that device has been placed on the market or put into service,

unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex I which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^V_NI_64748380-a08c-a136-95d5-854d49e7347c]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

(3)[^V_NI_89b9ca79-d322-0aa7-39d9-a1690a69a434] Where a hazard exists, devices which are also machinery shall also meet the essential health and safety requirements set out in Annex I to Directive 2006/42 to the extent to which those essential health and safety requirements are more specific than the essential requirements to Directive 93/42.
