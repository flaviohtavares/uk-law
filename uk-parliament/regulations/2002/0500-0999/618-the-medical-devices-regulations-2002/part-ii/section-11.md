# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. UK marking of general medical devices that come within the scope of this Part and other legislation[^M_E_d3ab7c91-3719-42ba-a367-0289f916e0b8][^c-eb341c9e082139499450473271403f76]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a relevant device (within the meaning of this Part) comes within the scope of this Part and other product safety or health and safety legislation (“the other legislation”) a person must not affix a UK marking to the device unless the relevant requirements of the other legislation are also satisfied.

## 11. CE marking of general medical devices that come within the scope of more than one Directive[^M_E_74d91690-b3bc-4e35-de23-bd09957d5bac]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a relevant device comes within the scope of Directive 93/42 and another Directive (“the other Directive”) issued by one or more of the institutions of the Community, no person shall affix a CE marking to the device unless the relevant requirements of the other Directive are also satisfied, except where—

- (a) the other Directive includes a provision allowing the manufacturer of the device to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, which set of arrangements applies to it;
- (b) the manufacturer chooses to follow the set of arrangements in Directive 93/42;
- (c) the marking of the device indicates that the device only satisfies the set of arrangements chosen by the manufacturer; and
- (d) the particulars of Directive 93/42, as published in the Official Journal of the European Union[^M_F_e34b8ac2-d592-4fc5-c531-fcfc66f54326], are given in the documents, notices or instructions accompanying the device.
