# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — MUTUAL RECOGNITION AGREEMENTS[^M_E_e6cf1992-d626-406a-feb7-66ed11fcbf9e]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 2(1)

**1.** The agreement on mutual recognition in relation to conformity assessment certificates and markings between the European Community and Australia, initialled on 19th July 1996 [^c17037151].

**2.** The agreement on mutual recognition in relation to conformity assessment between the European Union and New Zealand, initialled on 19th July 1996 [^c17037161].

**3.** The agreement on mutual recognition between the European Community and Canada, signed in London on 14th May 1998 [^c17037171].

**4.** The agreement on mutual recognition between the European Community and the United States of America, signed in London on 18th May 1998 [^c17037181].

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**5.**[^c-e42eac1e7683c39990636406f02fbaaf] The agreement between the European Community and the Swiss Confederation on mutual recognition in relation to conformity assessment signed in Luxembourg on 21st June 1999.
