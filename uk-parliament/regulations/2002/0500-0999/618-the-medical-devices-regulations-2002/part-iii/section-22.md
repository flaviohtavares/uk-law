# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Essential requirements for active implantable medical devices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 26, no person shall place on the market or put into service a relevant device unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex 1 which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^key-673fe7646fbea166c2d09146de1e88d6]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

(2) Subject to regulation 26, no person shall supply a relevant device—

- (a) if that supply is also a placing on the market or putting into service of that device; or
- (b) in circumstances where that device has also been placed on the market or put into service,

unless that device meets those essential requirements set out in Annex 1 which apply to it and the requirements set out in Regulation ([^key-b50f28dc11df686c12c09237a839d968]EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).
