# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Procedures for affixing a UK marking to active implantable medical devices[^M_E_ad2aed32-3512-4095-ffbe-9021efcfb4f1][^c-687a37fdf127a9bb1fc9bbc121ca97c4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A relevant device may bear a UK marking[^c-5345fc031814f545154db4db57a3cd0e] only if its manufacturer or their UK responsible person[^c-fad086d034d351aa1c8bbbc429083491]—

- (a) fulfils the applicable obligations imposed by—
  - (i) Annex 2, or
  - (ii) Annex 3, together with Annex 4 or 5;
- (b) declares, in accordance with a declaration of conformity procedure set out in those Annexes, that the device meets the provisions of this Part[^c-9958ffa55dc29dbf987886f8a8a59af4] that apply to it; [^c-874f4e033e68d04c6d72f2e580a67c38]...
- (c) ensures that the device meets the provisions of this Part which apply to it; and[^c-d7b6df8a2e2a2b81e7e3112405aa2289]
- (d)[^c-4cf570e94746881e14b6b809195c00a5] fulfils the obligations imposed by Regulation (EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).

## 27. Procedures for affixing a CE marking to active implantable medical devices[^M_E_9cdecb5d-5f74-4d9d-d118-f0a97416b753]

*Extent: N.I.*

A relevant device may bear a CE marking only if its manufacturer or his authorised representative—

- (a) fulfils the applicable obligations imposed by—
  - (i) Annex 2, or
  - (ii) Annex 3, together with Annex 4 or 5;
- (b) declares, in accordance with a declaration of conformity procedure set out in those Annexes, that the device meets the provisions of Directive 90/385 that apply to it; [^V_NI_e49ce772-7a19-4e3c-42e9-29c55d48df01]...
- (c) ensures that the device meets the provisions of Directive 90/385 which apply to it; and[^V_NI_e311f367-7a66-3a7f-4755-fe1ad57393a5]
- (d)[^V_NI_676fffe3-3784-265e-e438-b0351eee380a] fulfils the obligations imposed by Regulation (EU) No 722/2012 (if applicable).
