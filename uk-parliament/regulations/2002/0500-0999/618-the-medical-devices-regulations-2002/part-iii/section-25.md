# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 25. UK marking of active implantable medical devices that come within the scope of this Part and other legislation[^M_E_fca8c960-fac7-4c00-e0d6-9731b05b78de][^c-7df58b0f0c0b98b4069b9250669dcc56]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a relevant device (within the meaning of this Part) comes within the scope of this Part and other product safety or health and safety legislation (“the other legislation”) a person must not affix a UK marking to the device unless the relevant requirements of the other legislation are also satisfied.

## 25. CE marking of active implantable medical devices that come within the scope of more than one Directive[^M_E_a557d3a1-7f76-4d95-b586-2fb802190f68]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a relevant device comes within the scope of Directive 90/385 and another Directive (“the other Directive”) issued by one or more of the institutions of the Community, no person shall affix a CE marking to the device unless the relevant requirements of the other Directive are also satisfied, except where—

- (a) the other Directive includes a provision allowing the manufacturer of the device to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, which set of arrangements applies to it;
- (b) the manufacturer chooses to follow the set of arrangements in Directive 90/385;
- (c) the marking of the device indicates that the device only satisfies the set of arrangements chosen by the manufacturer; and
- (d) the particulars of Directive 90/385, as published in the Official Journal of the European Union[^M_F_ff6ca096-69b5-4354-dc19-9ff8f8f2b09a], are given in the documents, notices or instructions accompanying the device, and in a manner in which those particulars are accessible without it being necessary to destroy the packaging which keeps the device sterile.
