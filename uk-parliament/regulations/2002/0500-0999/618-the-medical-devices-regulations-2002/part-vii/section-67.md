# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Review[^M_E_ac2ded94-14bc-4cb5-8bb1-06cb14290174][^c-592cfa7d7b334f15639acb04c08079ad]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before the end of 31st December 2025[^c-3d9ea61f1c81694c4398f8e97eb97af1], the Secretary of State must—

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations;
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and
- (c) publish the report.

## 67. Review[^M_E_9b4b4e73-f1ec-4820-c91e-5d88440399e9][^V_NI_f8e9f85c-abaf-efb7-bea0-7854c5a92c63]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before the end of 31st December 2019, the Secretary of State must—

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations;
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and
- (c) publish the report.
