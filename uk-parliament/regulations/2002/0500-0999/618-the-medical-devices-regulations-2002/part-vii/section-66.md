# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions are hereby revoked—

- (a) the Active Implantable Medical Devices Regulations 1992 [^c17037071];
- (b) the Medical Devices Regulations 1994 [^c17037081];
- (c) the Active Implantable Medical Devices (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1995 [^c17037091];
- (d) the Medical Devices Fees Regulations 1995 [^c17037101];
- (e) the Medical Devices Fees (Amendment) Regulations 1997 [^c17037111];
- (f) the*In Vitro* Diagnostic Medical Devices Regulations 2000 [^c17037121]; and
- (g) regulations 6 and 13 of the Medicines (Codification Amendments Etc.) Regulations 2002 [^c17037131].
