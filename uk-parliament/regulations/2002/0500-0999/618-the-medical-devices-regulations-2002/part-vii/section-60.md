## 60. Status of UK responsible person[^M_E_d67ba951-5c29-4a03-fd87-468f1e38489c][^c-2168d907df39b552c43043a8b2db75cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-58ca07f9bac470b2c11acec1ea444272] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-58ca07f9bac470b2c11acec1ea444272] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-4ef50b32cffb363b6940bbbce2b9dd91] A UK responsible person—

- (a) may be proceeded against as a person placing the device on the market for the purposes of these regulations;
- (b) in relation to the supply of the device to a person within the United Kingdom after it has been placed on the market, may be proceeded against as a person supplying the device after it has been placed on the market.

(4) If a person claims or purports to act as a UK responsible person[^c-929698f3aeae7f7936531a2f53e84557], the Secretary of State may, for the purposes of enabling the Secretary of State to exercise his functions under these Regulations, require that person to furnish the Secretary of State with sufficient evidence that he is[^c-e7f021bc6a7343cdd9b06b6e2c42f781] a UK responsible person[^c-be64982d194c45db4b0ecec6c92b66aa].

## 60. Designation etc. of authorised representatives[^M_E_d7389e3a-9f43-4295-d369-1b2f46da51ac]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where these Regulations place any obligation[^c-40af47a53bd853b672cc41d927fb3190]... on a manufacturer of a device or his authorised representative, and the manufacturer does not have a registered place of business in a relevant state[^c-81f1ff89f479d4e7dd2d1ef2044a4d1b], no person shall—

- (a) place that device on the market; or
- (b) supply that device in circumstances where it has been placed on the market,

unless the manufacturer of the device has designated a single authorised representative[^V_NI_19230b38-a875-499d-6a33-ff504ebe1023] to perform that obligation, but once the manufacturer has designated a single authorised representative to perform that obligation, that obligation shall be performed by the authorised representative (although in all other cases it shall be performed by the manufacturer).

(2) If the manufacturer of a registrable device does not have a registered place of business in a relevant state[^c-5ca9148da023819641bf7bd5301b70c0], no person shall place that device on the market or supply that device in circumstances where it has been placed on the market unless its manufacturer has designated a single authorised representative as—

- (a) the person responsible for marketing the device in a relevant state[^M_F_509c63c8-4471-40be-bed8-6e6d79140d57]; and
- (b) the person responsible for registering in respect of that device with—
  - (i) the Secretary of State in accordance with regulation 19 or, as the case may be, 44, or
  - (ii) the competent authorities of another Member State or (where appropriate) a State which is a Party to an Association Agreement.

(3) Where a manufacturer of a registrable device, or of a relevant device that is not registrable, has designated a single authorised representative as the person responsible for marketing the device within a relevant state[^c-529dbd18a7dfcdaa7620d6db08687168], that authorised representative—

- (a) may be proceeded against as a person placing the device on the market for the purposes of these Regulations;
- (b) in relation to any supply of the device to a person within Northern Ireland[^c-3a7ab65ef13f3470284eff4da3fb7068] after it has been placed on the market, may be proceeded against as a person supplying the device after it has been placed on the market, unless that supply is due to an act of another person established in a relevant state[^c-c4d8aa1af195f0c248cc1566df0ccf38].

(4) If a person claims or purports to act as an authorised representative of a manufacturer of a device, the Secretary of State may, for the purposes of enabling the Secretary of State to exercise his functions under these Regulations, require that person to furnish the Secretary of State with sufficient evidence that he is the single authorised representative of the manufacturer[^V_NI_7c747a77-9dd6-e284-0801-79f1e67b26df].
