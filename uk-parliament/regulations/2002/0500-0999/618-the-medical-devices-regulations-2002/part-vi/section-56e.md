## 56E. Fees payable in connection with regulatory advice meetings[^key-080d37e1c2465918882f5ed0d0068e97]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Unless regulation 56(3B)(a) applies, or regulation 17A(2)(a) of the Medical Devices (Northern Ireland Protocol) Regulations 2021 applies, the fee payable by a person with whom the Secretary of State holds a meeting in order to provide regulatory advice relating to a medical device is £987 for each hour that meeting takes.

(2) Any fee payable under this regulation must be paid within 14 days following written notice from the Secretary of State requiring payment of that fee.
