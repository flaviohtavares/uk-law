## 44ZR. Requests for post-market surveillance documentation[^c-7aac0da14fb1953d3c468abc4b16422d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where any provision of this Part requires information or documentation to be provided to the Secretary of State within 3 working days of a request, the Secretary of State may extend the time within which the information or documentation is to be provided if the Secretary of State considers it reasonable to do so.
