# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. In vitro diagnostic medical devices not ready for use

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulation 39, no person shall—

- (a) put into service a relevant device;
- (b) supply a relevant device—
  - (i) if that supply is also a putting into service of that device, or
  - (ii) in circumstances where that device has been placed on the market or put into service,

  which is not ready for use.
