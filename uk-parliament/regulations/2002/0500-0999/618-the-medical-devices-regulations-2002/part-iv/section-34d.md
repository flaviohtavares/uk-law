# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 34D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34D. Exemption for coronavirus test devices in conformity with [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746) and [Regulation (EU) 2022/1107](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2022/1107)[^key-65b6270f02898f0d07f65f0a8f35dd10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 34A does not apply in Northern Ireland—

- (a) in relation to a coronavirus test device that is in conformity with [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746) and the common specifications set out in Annex I and XIII to [Regulation (EU) 2022/1107](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2022/1107);
- (b) after 24 July 2024, in relation to a coronavirus test device that is in conformity with [Regulation (EU) 2017/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0746).
