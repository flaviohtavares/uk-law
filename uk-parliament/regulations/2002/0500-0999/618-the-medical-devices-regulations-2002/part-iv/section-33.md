# The Medical Devices Regulations 2002, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2002/618
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Scope of Part IV

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The requirements of this Part in respect of relevant devices apply in respect of*in vitro* diagnostic medical devices (including coronavirus test devices)[^c-e43c7717ecd0e11f99c54cce30d3c35f] and accessories to such devices, except for—

- (a) products manufactured and used within the same health institution and either on the premises of their manufacture or on premises in the immediate vicinity without having been transferred to another legal entity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b3548e3028cdd347dad64df496f22ce7]
- (b) devices that come within the scope of Directive 98/79 and another Directive (“the other Directive”) issued by one or more of the institutions of the Community, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d3358e3b452052f437f57f21bbb5e22]
  - (i) the other Directive includes a provision allowing the manufacturer of the device to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, which set of arrangements applies to it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d3358e3b452052f437f57f21bbb5e22]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d3358e3b452052f437f57f21bbb5e22] the manufacturer chooses to follow the set of arrangements in the other Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d3358e3b452052f437f57f21bbb5e22]

(2) The requirements of this Part in respect of devices for performance evaluation do not apply in respect of—

- (a) products manufactured and used only within the same health institution and either on the premises of their manufacture or on premises in the immediate vicinity without having been transferred to another legal entity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4a00133919ae8ffaf2b6405267bef7ce]
- (b) devices that come within the scope of Directive 98/79 and another Directive (“the other Directive”) issued by one or more of the institutions of the Community, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7f286c12bdbe454ba81d5eebc28ec675]
  - (i) the other Directive includes a provision allowing the manufacturer of the device to choose, during a transitional period that has not ended, which set of arrangements applies to it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7f286c12bdbe454ba81d5eebc28ec675]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7f286c12bdbe454ba81d5eebc28ec675] the manufacturer chooses to follow the set of arrangements in the other Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7f286c12bdbe454ba81d5eebc28ec675]
