# The Finance Act 2001, section 16, (Appointed Day) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 809 (C.20)

AGGREGATES LEVY

The Finance Act 2001, section 16, (Appointed Day) Order 2002

Made

25th March 2002

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 16(6) of the Finance Act 2001[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2001, section 16, (Appointed Day) Order 2002.

**2.** The date appointed as the commencement date for the purposes of section 16 of the Finance Act 2001 is 1st April 2002.

Nick Ainger

Anne McGuire

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

25th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st April 2002 as the commencement date for the purpose of section 16 of the Finance Act [2001 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/9), which contains the charging provisions for aggregates levy.
