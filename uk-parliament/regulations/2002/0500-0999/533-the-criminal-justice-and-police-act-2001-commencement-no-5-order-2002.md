# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 533 (C.15)

POLICE

COMPANIES

The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002

Made

7th March 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002.

(2) In this Order, “the 2001 Act” means the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001.

## 2. Commencement on 1st April 2002

The following provisions of the 2001 Act shall come into force on 1st April 2002—

- (a) Part 4 (Police Training) in so far as not already in force;
- (b) section 137 (repeals) in so far as it relates to Schedule 7, Part 3;
- (c) Schedule 3 (the Central Police Training and Development Authority);
- (d) Schedule 4 (Consequential amendments relating to police training); and
- (e) Schedule 7, Part 3 (Police training).

## 3. Commencement on 2nd April 2002

Section 45 of the 2001 Act (addresses of directors and secretaries of companies) in so far as not already in force shall come into force on 2nd April 2002.

John Denham

Minister of State

Home Office

7th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2002 the provisions of Part 4 (police training) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001, and the associated schedules to the Act, which are not currently in force. It brings into being the Central Police Training and Development Authority which will provide police training and promote best practice in policing matters generally.

Article 3 brings into force on 2nd April section 45 of the Act, which makes provision for confidentiality orders in relation to the addresses of directors and secretaries of companies.

(This note is not part of the Order)
