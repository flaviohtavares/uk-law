# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2002, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Sums specified for child benefit

In regulation 2(1) of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations 1976[^f00031] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i) for “£15.50”, substitute “£15.75”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(ii), “£17.55” remains unchanged; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£10.35”, substitute “£10.55”.
