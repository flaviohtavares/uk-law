# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2002, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995[^f00033], in regulation 18(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£14.65”, substitute “£14.90”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£9.30”, substitute “£9.50”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£4.65”, substitute “£4.75”.
