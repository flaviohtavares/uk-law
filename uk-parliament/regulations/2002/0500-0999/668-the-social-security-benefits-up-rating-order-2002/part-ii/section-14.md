# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2002, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Increase in rates of age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations 1994[^f00032], in regulation 10(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£14.65”, substitute “£14.90”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£7.35”, substitute “£7.45”.
