# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2002, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. The Prescribed Sum for Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 172 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations[^f00053] (trade disputes: prescribed sum) for “£28.50”, substitute “£29.00”.
