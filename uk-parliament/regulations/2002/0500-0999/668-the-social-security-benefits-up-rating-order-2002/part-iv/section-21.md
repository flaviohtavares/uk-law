# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2002, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Increase in age-related amounts of contribution-based Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£31.95”, substitute “£32.50”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£42.00”, substitute “£42.70”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£53.05”, substitute “£53.95”.
