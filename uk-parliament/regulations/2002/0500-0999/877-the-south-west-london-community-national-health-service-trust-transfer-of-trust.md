# The South West London Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) No. 2 Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 877

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The South West London Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) No. 2 Order 2002

Made

27th March 2002

Laid before Parliament

28th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes or proposed changes in the administration of South West London Community National Health Service Trust and having consulted that Trust, the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust and the Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South West London Community National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) No. 2 Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the South West London Community National Health Service Trust[^f00002];
- “the new Trust” means the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust[^f00003] or the Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust[^f00004] as the context requires;
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the two schedules of trust property more specifically described in paragraphs (1) and (2) of article 2 of this Order.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 15th March 2002 and on behalf of the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust on 18th March 2002, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00005], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust.

(2) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 15th March 2002 and on behalf of the Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust on 18th March 2002, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00006], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust.

**3.** (1) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust and any reference to the old Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust.

(2) In paragraph (1) of this article references to the relevant new Trust are to be read as references to the new Trust to which the property which is the subject of the trust instrument has been transferred under article 2 of this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

27th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Nelson and West Merton Primary Care Trust and Teddington, Twickenham and Hamptons Primary Care Trust, trust property held by the South West London Community National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedules specifying the trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
