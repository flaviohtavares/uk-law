# The Osteopaths Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 500 (C.14)

OSTEOPATHS

The Osteopaths Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002

Made

26th February 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 42(2) and (4) of the Osteopaths Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Osteopaths Act 1993 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2002.

## 2. Appointed Day

3rd March 2002 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the whole of the Osteopaths Act 1993 in so far as it is not already in force.

Alan Milburn

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State,

Department of Health

26th February 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd March 2002 the remaining provisions of the Osteopaths Act 1993, which are section 1(4), so far as not already in force, and paragraphs 3, 9, 10, 12, 14(1), 14(3)(d), 14(4) and 14(5)(a) of Part 1 of the Schedule.

(This note is not part of the Order)
