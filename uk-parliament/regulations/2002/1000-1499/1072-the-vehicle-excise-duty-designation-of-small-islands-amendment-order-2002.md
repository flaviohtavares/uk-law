# The Vehicle Excise Duty (Designation of Small Islands) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1072

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Vehicle Excise Duty (Designation of Small Islands) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

14th April 2002

Laid before Parliament

17th April 2002

Coming into force

1st June 2002

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 18(4) of Schedule 1 to the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Vehicle Excise Duty (Designation of Small Islands) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st June 2002.

## 2. Designation of small islands

The Schedule to the Vehicle Excise Duty (Designation of Small Islands) Order 1995[^f00002] shall be amended by the insertion of—

- (a) “Mainland” after “Hoy” and “Shapinsay” after “Sanday” in the list headed “*Orkney Islands*”;
- (b) “Harris and Lewis” after “Eriskay” in the list headed “*Outer Hebrides*”; and
- (c) “Mainland” after “Foula” in the list headed “*Shetland Islands*”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Transport, Local Government and the Regions

14th April 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order amends the Vehicle Excise Duty (Designation of Small Islands) Order 1995 and designates Shapinsay, Orkney Mainland, Harris and Lewis and Shetland Mainland as small islands for the purposes of paragraph 18 of Schedule 1 to the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994, which defines the expression “island goods vehicle” for the purposes of Part VIII of that Schedule.
