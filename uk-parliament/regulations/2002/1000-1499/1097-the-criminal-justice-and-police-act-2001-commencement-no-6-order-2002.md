# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1097 (C. 27)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2002

Made

16th April 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2002.

**2.** Section 130 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 shall come into force on:

- (a) 22nd April 2002 for the purpose of remands and committals by a court in any of the police areas[^f00002] listed in the Schedule to this Order; and
- (b) 16th September 2002 for all other purposes.

Beverley Hughes

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

16th April 2002

## SCHEDULE — POLICE AREAS IN WHICH SECTION 130 OF THE CRIMINAL JUSTICE AND POLICE ACT 2001 SHALL COME INTO FORCE ON 22ND APRIL 2002

Article 2

- Avon and Somerset
- City of London
- Greater Manchester
- Lancashire
- Merseyside
- Metropolitan police district
- Nottinghamshire
- South Yorkshire
- Thames Valley
- West Midlands
- West Yorkshire

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 130 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (remands and committals to secure accommodation etc) on 22nd April 2002 for the eleven areas listed in the Schedule to this Order and on 16th September 2002 for all other areas.

(This note is not part of the Order)
