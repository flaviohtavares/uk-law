# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1336

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2002

Made

25th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

(3) This Order extends to England only.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified opposite it in column 2 of the Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton

Minister of State

Department of Health

22nd March 2002

We consent,

Nick Ainger

Anne McGuire

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

25th March 2002
