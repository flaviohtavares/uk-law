# The North Stoke Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1392

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The North Stoke Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2002

Made

12th May 2002

Coming into force

16th May 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North Stoke Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 16th May 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the North Stoke Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00003]; and
- “the trust” means the trust established by the Order.

## 2. Amendment of the Schedule to the Order

(1) The Schedule to the Order shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraph.

(2) For the entry for the City of Stoke on Trent, there shall be substituted the following entry:

> - Abbey Green; Bentilee and Townsend; Berryhill and Hanley East; Burslem North; Burslem South; Chell and Packmoor; East Valley; Norton and Bradeley; Northwood and Birches Head; Tunstall.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

12th May 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the establishment order of North Stoke Primary Care Trust, a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977 by making changes to the area in which the North Stoke Primary Care Trust is to undertake its functions.
