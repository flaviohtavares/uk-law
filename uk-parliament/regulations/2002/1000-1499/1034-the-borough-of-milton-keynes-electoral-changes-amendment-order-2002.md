# The Borough of Milton Keynes (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1034

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Borough of Milton Keynes (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

26th March 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

Whereas the Local Government Commission for England, acting pursuant to section 15(4) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001], has submitted to the Secretary of State a report dated August 2001 on its review of the borough[^f00002] of Milton Keynes together with its recommendations;

And whereas the Secretary of State has decided to give effect to those recommendations;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17[^f00003] and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Borough of Milton Keynes (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment to the 2001 Order

In article 1(4)(b) of the Borough of Milton Keynes (Electoral Changes) Order 2001[^f00004] for the words “16th May 2004” there shall be substituted “6th May 2004”.

Alan Whitehead

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

26th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Borough of Milton Keynes (Electoral Changes) Order 2001, in order to give full effect to recommendations by the Local Government Commission for England by correcting an error in relation to the date of commencement of articles 4 and 5 of the 2001 Order.
