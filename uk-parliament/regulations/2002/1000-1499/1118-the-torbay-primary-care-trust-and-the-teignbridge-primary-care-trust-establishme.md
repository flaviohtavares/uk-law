# The Torbay Primary Care Trust and the Teignbridge Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1118

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Torbay Primary Care Trust and the Teignbridge Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

Made

25th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in the exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1) and (2), and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Torbay Primary Care Trust and the Teignbridge Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the First Order” means the Torbay Primary Care Trust Establishment Order 2000[^f00003];
- “the Second Order” means the Teignbridge Primary Care Trust Establishment Order 2001[^f00004].

## 2. Amendment of the First Order and of the Second Order

Article 8 of the First Order and of the Second Order (exercise of functions) shall be deleted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hazel Blears

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

25th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Establishment Orders of the Torbay Primary Care Trust and the Teignbridge Primary Care Trust, Primary Care Trusts established by Orders made under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977. It removes a restriction on the exercise of functions by these Primary Care Trusts so that they can provide services directly to patients.
