# The South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1498

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

Made

24th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00004];
- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

## 2. Change of name of NHS trust and transitional provisions

(1) The trust shall be called the South Essex Partnership National Health Service Trust instead of the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust, and accordingly in article 1 of the Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Order, for “South Essex Mental Health and Community Care” there shall be substituted “South Essex Partnership”.

(2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

24th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000, which established the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust, and changes the name of that trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the South Essex Mental Health and Community Care National Health Service Trust to the South Essex Partnership National Health Service Trust, and makes consequential amendments to the Establishment Order and transitional provisions.
