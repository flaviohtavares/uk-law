# The Railways (Interoperability) (High-Speed) Regulations 2002, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Notification to the Commission of additional checks

(1) The supervisory authority shall notify the Commission in writing forthwith of any additional checks it requires pursuant to regulation 14(5).

(2) Where a notice under paragraph (1) is given by the supervisory authority, that notice shall specify—

- (a) the additional checks required; and
- (b) the reasons for requiring those additional checks.
