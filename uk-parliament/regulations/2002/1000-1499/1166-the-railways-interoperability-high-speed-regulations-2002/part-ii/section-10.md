# The Railways (Interoperability) (High-Speed) Regulations 2002, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. The Strategic Rail Authority: fees

The Strategic Rail Authority may charge such reasonable fee in connection with, or incidental to, carrying out its functions under regulation 5 as it may determine.
