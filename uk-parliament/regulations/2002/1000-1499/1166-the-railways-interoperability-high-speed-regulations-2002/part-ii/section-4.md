# The Railways (Interoperability) (High-Speed) Regulations 2002, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Notified bodies

For the purposes of these Regulations, a notified body is a body which has been—

- (a) appointed by the Authority as a notified body for the purpose of these Regulations and notified to the Commission and other Member States pursuant to regulation 5; or
- (b) appointed by a Member State other than the United Kingdom, and notified by the Member State concerned to the Commission and the other Member States pursuant to Article 20(1) of the high-speed Directive.
