# The Railways (Interoperability) (High-Speed) Regulations 2002, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application of the Regulations

These Regulations apply to subsystems and interoperability constituents other than any—

- (a) structural subsystem that is placed into service; or
- (b) interoperability constituent that is placed on the market,

within three months after the date of the coming into force of these Regulations.
