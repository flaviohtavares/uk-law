# The Docklands Light Railway (Silvertown and London City Airport Extension) Order 2002, section 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Certain lands not to be acquired compulsorily

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Order shall authorise DLRL to acquire compulsorily any of the lands mentioned in Schedule 8 to this Order, or any rights over those lands, but DLRL may acquire by agreement any part of those lands, or any rights over them, for the purpose of the authorised works.
