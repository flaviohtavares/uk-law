# The Docklands Light Railway (Silvertown and London City Airport Extension) Order 2002, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Stations

*Extent: E+W*

DLRL may construct the stations specified in Schedule 3 to this Order on the lands specified in that Schedule and maintain and operate the stations so constructed, and it may construct, maintain and operate all necessary works and conveniences connected with those stations.
