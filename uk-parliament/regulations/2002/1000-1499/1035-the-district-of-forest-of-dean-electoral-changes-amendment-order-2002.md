# The District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1035

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made

26th March 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

Whereas the Local Government Commission for England, acting pursuant to section 15(4) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001], has submitted to the Secretary of State a report dated June 2001 on its review of the district of Forest of Dean together with its recommendations;

And whereas the Secretary of State has decided to give effect to those recommendations;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17[^f00002] and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment to the 2001 Order

(1) The District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) Order 2001[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In article 1(3) for the words from “any reference to” to “Order 2001” there shall be substituted “any reference to “the map” is a reference to the map prepared by the Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions marked “Revised (2002) Map of the District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) Order 2001””.

Alan Whitehead

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

26th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new map for that originally prepared in relation to the District of Forest of Dean (Electoral Changes) Order 2001, in order to give full effect to recommendations by the Local Government Commission for England for electoral changes in the District of Forest of Dean.
