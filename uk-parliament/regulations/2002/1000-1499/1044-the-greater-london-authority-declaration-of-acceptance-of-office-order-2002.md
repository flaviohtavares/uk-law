# The Greater London Authority (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1044

LONDON GOVERNMENT

The Greater London Authority (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) Order 2002

Made

10th April 2002

Laid before Parliament

15th April 2002

Coming into force

6th May 2002

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 28 and 420(1) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001], sections 50(1) and 105(2), (3) and (4) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Greater London Authority (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 6th May 2002.

(2) This Order applies to the Greater London Authority only.

## 2. Form of declaration of acceptance of office

The declaration of acceptance of the office of—

- (a) Mayor;
- (b) Assembly member; and
- (c) acting Mayor by virtue of being Deputy mayor,

shall be in the form in the Schedule to this Order or a form to the like effect.

## 3. Revocation of the Greater London Authority (Elections and Acceptance of Office) Order 2000

The Greater London Authority (Elections and Acceptance of Office) Order 2000[^f00003] shall be revoked.

## 4. Amendment to the Local Authorities (Model Code of Conduct) (England) Order 2001

(1) In article 4(1) of the Local Authorities (Model Code of Conduct) (England) Order 2001[^f00004], sub-paragraph (g) shall be omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

Alan Whitehead

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

10th April 2002

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

DECLARATION OF ACCEPTANCE OF OFFICE

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1044/images/uksi_20021044_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the form of declaration of office for the Mayor of London, members of the London Assembly and any acting Mayor of London in accordance with section 28 of Greater London Authority Act 1999. The declaration must be made within two months of assumption of office.

Article 2 provides that the form (or a form to like effect) in the Schedule to the Order shall be used for such declarations.

Article 3 revokes a previous order made under section 28 of Greater London Authority Act 1999. Article 4 revokes article 4(1)(g) of the Local Authorities (Model Code of Conduct) (England) Order 2001, which disapplies any orders made under section 28 until 27th July 2002.

The Schedule to the Order prescribes the form of the declaration.

When the Greater London Authority adopts a code of conduct for the first time as required by section 51 of the Local Government Act 2000, or where a person becomes a member (as defined by that Act to include the Mayor of London and a London Assembly member) or co-opted member of the Authority at any time after such a code is adopted, he must in addition to the declaration prescribed in this Order (where he is required to make one), give a written undertaking pursuant to section 52 of the Local Government Act 2000 that he will observe the code of conduct.
