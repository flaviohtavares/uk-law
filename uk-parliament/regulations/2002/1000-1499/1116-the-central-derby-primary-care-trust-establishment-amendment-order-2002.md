# The Central Derby Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1116

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Central Derby Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

Made

21st March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) and (4) of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 5A to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Central Derby Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Central Derby Primary Care Trust Establishment Order 2000[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of Schedule to the Order

In the Schedule to the Order (Area of Trust) after “Sinfin” there shall be added “Blagreaves”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

21st March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Establishment Order of the Central Derby Primary Care Trust, a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977, by adding to the area of the trust the electoral ward of Blagreaves in the city of Derby.
