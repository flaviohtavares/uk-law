# The Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1359 (C. 37)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, WALES

The Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2002

Made

22nd May 2002

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 108(3) and (7) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2002.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Commencement of section 93

The following provisions—

- (a) subsection (2) of section 93 of the Local Government Act 2000, and
- (b) subsections (3) to (12) of that section insofar as they relate to local authorities in Wales,

shall come into force on 30th June 2002.

Paul Murphy

Secretary of State for Wales

22nd May 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force subsection (2) of section 93 of the Local Government Act 2000 which enables grants to be made to local authorities in Wales for welfare services, and subsections (3) to (12) of that section insofar as they apply to local authorities in Wales.

(This note is not part of the Order)
