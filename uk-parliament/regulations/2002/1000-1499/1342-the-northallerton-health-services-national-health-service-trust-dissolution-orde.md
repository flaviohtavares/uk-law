# The Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 1342

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002

Made

19th March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

The Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust is hereby dissolved and accordingly the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Jacqui Smith

Minister of State,

Department of Health

19th March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 2002:

- the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust.
