# The Marine, &c., Broadcasting (Offences) Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1967

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1967 No. 1149 (C.19)

TELEGRAPHS

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY

The Marine, &C., Broadcasting (Offences) Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1967

Made

28th July 1967

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 28th day of July 1967

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 11(2) of the Marine, &c., Broadcasting (Offences) Act 1967, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** The Marine, &c., Broadcasting (Offences) Act 1967 shall come into operation on the 15th August 1967.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Marine, &c., Broadcasting (Offences) Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1967.

W.G. Agnew
