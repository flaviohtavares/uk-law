# The Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1967

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1967 No. 819 (C. 13)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Carriage of Goods By Road Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1967

Made

24th May 1967

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of May 1967

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 14 of the Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965, and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** The Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965 shall come into operation on 5th June 1967.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1967.

W. G. Agnew
