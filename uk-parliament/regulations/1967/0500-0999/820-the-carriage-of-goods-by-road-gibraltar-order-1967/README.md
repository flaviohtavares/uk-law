EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under the Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965 the provisions of which give effect to the Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road signed at Geneva on 19th May 1956. The Order extends certain provisions of the Act to Gibraltar.
