# The Carriage of Goods by Road (Gibraltar) Order 1967, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application of sections of Act of 1965

The provisions of sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7(1), 13 and 14(2) of the Act of 1965 subject to the exceptions, adaptations and modifications as set out in the schedule to this Order are extended to Gibraltar.
