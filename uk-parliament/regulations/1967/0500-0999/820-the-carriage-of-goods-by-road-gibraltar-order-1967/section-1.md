# The Carriage of Goods by Road (Gibraltar) Order 1967, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Carriage of Goods by Road (Gibraltar) Order 1967, and shall come into operation on the 5th June 1967.
