# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.2) Order 1998 Partial Revocation (No.2) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.2) Order 1998 Partial Revocation (No.2) Order 1999

Made

30th March 1999

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

Laid before Parliament

6th April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 2) Order 1998 Partial Revocation (No. 2) Order 1999 and shall come into force at 18.00 hours on 30th March 1999.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 2) Order 1998[^f00002] insofar as it remains in force is revoked.

J R Wildgoose

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

30th April 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No.2) Order 1998 (“the principal Order”) insofar as it remains in force. The principal Order prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the area designated in the Schedule to that Order (article 4);
- (b) movement of such scallops out of that area (article 5); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food, supplying and other specified activities in relation to such scallops from the designated area within the United Kingdom (article 6).

The principal Order was partially revoked by the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 2) Order 1998 Partial Revocation Order 1999 which removed the prohibitions in respect of the area of sea designated in the Schedule to that Order.

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect of the remainder of the area of sea designated in the Schedule to the principal Order.
