# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions)(Standing Orders and Parliamentary Publications) Order 1999, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “the Act” means the Scotland Act 1998;
- “court” includes tribunal;
- “legal proceedings” means any legal proceedings (whether civil or criminal) in any court in the United Kingdom;
- “the Parliament” means the Scottish Parliament; and
- “statement” has the same meaning as in the Defamation Act 1996[^f00002].
