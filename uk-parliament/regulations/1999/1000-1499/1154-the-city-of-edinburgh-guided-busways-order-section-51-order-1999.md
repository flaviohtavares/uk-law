# The City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order (Section 51) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1154 (S. 95)

TRANSPORT

The City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order (Section 51) Order 1999

Made

11th April 1999

Coming into force

14th April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order maybe cited as the City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order (Section 5l) Order 1999.

## 2. Specified date

For the purposes of section 2(2) of the City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order, 14th April 1999 is hereby specified as the day on which section 51 (Disapplication) of that Order comes into force.

Calum MacDonald

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

11th April 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 14th April 1999 as the date of coming into force of section 51 of the City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order, which was confirmed by the [City of Edinburgh (Guided Busways) Order Confirmation Act 1998 (c.III)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1998/3).

Section 51 disapplies certain sections of the Transport Act [1985 (c. 67)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/67) dealing with competition in the provision of public passenger transport services and the procedure for entering into agreements as subsidies for such services.
