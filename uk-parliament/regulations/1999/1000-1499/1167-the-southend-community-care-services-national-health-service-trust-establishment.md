# The Southend Community Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1167

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Southend Community Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

14th April 1999

Coming into force

26th April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act [^f00003] and considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Southend Community Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 26th April 1999.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

For Article 3 of the Southend Community Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] there shall be substituted the following Article:–

> **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be:–
>
> - (a) to own and manage, pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act, Community House, Union Lane, Rochford, Essex SS4 1RB and associated premises, and there and from there to provide and manage community health services;
> - (b) to provide and manage, pursuant to section 5(1)9b) of the Act, premises in Southend, Rochford and Basildon and there to provide hospital accommodation and services, and there and from there to provide and manage community health services.

.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State of Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

14th April 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Southend Community Care Services National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)9b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage premises in Southend, Rochford and Basildon.
