# The Air Navigation (General) (Second Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1324

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (General) (Second Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

10th May 1999

Coming into force

14th May 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 5 to the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (General) (Second Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 14th May 1999.

**2.** The Air Navigation (General) Regulations 1993[^f00002] shall be further amended by inserting after regulation 18 the following:

> **18A. Airborne Collision Avoidance System**
>
> For the purposes of paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 5 to the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, the prescribed requirements for an airborne collision avoidance system shall be the requirements for ACAS II equipment set out in Volume 4 Chapter 4 of Annex 10 (first edition) to the Chicago Convention.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Glenda Jackson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

10th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Air Navigation (General) Regulations 1993 by prescribing requirements which must be met by an airborne collision avoidance system carried in accordance with the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995.

Annex 10 to the Chicago Convention can be purchased from Westward Digital Limited, 37 Windsor Street, Cheltenham, Gloucestershire GL52 2DG.
