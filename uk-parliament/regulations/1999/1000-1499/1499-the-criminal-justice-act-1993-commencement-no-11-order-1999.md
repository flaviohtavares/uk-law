# The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 11) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an omission in [S.I. 1999/1189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1189) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1999 No. 1499 (C. 42)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 11) Order 1999

Made

26th May 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 78(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 1993[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 11) Order 1999.

**2.** Section 5(3), (4) and (5) of the Criminal Justice Act 1993[^f00002] shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

26th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order rectifies an omission in the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1189)), by bringing into force the remaining provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1993 extending the jurisdiction of courts in England and Wales in relation to certain offences of dishonesty and blackmail on 1st June 1999. By virtue of section 78(5) of the Act, nothing in any of those provisions applies to any act, omission or other event occurring before that date.
