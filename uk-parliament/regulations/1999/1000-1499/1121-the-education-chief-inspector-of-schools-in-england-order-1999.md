# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1121

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1999

Made

13th April 1999

Laid before Parliament

23rd April 1999

Coming into force

31st August 1999

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 13th day of April 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 1(1) and 1(4) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 31st August 1999.

**2.** Christopher Anthony Woodhead is hereby re-appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England for a period of four years from 1st September 1999.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order re-appoints Christopher Anthony Woodhead to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England for a period of four years from 1st September 1999.
