# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Order 1999, section P5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P5. Certification by the Government Actuary

For the purposes of articles P1, P2 and P3, any transfer value of the whole or part of a person’s accrued pension rights under this Scheme shall be such sum as shall satisfy the requirements prescribed under section 95(2) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 and shall be certified by, or calculated in accordance with tables prepared by, the Government Actuary.
