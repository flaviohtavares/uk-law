# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Order 1999, section N3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## N3. Deduction of tax from refunds of contributions

On making any repayment of contributions (including interest on contributions) under article N1, the Parliamentary corporation shall be entitled to deduct from the repayment any tax to which it may become chargeable under section 598(2) of the Taxes Act 1988 (charge to tax: repayment of employee’s contributions).
