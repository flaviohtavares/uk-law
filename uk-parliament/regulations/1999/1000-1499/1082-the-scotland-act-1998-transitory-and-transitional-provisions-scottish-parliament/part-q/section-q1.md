# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Order 1999, section Q1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Q1. Purchase of added years by participating members

Schedule 5 shall have effect with respect to the purchase of added years by a participating member, and subject to the provisions of that Schedule, his aggregate period of reckonable service as a participating member shall be treated as increased by the period of added years so purchased.
