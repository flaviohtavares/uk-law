# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Order 1999, section T1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## T1. Non-assignability of benefits

Any benefit under this Scheme shall not be assignable or chargeable with debts or other liabilities.
