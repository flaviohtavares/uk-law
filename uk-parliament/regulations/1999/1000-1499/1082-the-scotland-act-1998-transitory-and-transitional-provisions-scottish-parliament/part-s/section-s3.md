# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Order 1999, section S3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## S3. Pensions met out of Scottish Consolidated Fund

Any pension payable under article S1 or S2 shall be charged on and paid out of the Scottish Consolidated Fund.
