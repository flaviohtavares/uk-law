# The Government Stock (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1410

NATIONAL DEBT

The Government Stock (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

20th May 1999

Coming into force

24th May 1999

Whereas a draft of these Regulations has been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 6(1) of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946[^f00001] (which is virtue of section 6(2) of that Act replaces section 47(3) of the Finance Act 1942[^f00002]) and a period of forty days beginning with the date of laying has expired and neither House has resolved that the Regulations be not made:

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 47(1) of the Finance Act 1942, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Government Stock (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on the Monday after the day on which they are made.

**2.** Regulation 23(1) of the Government Stock Regulations 1965[^f00003] shall be amended–

- (a) by omitting the definition of “the CGO”; and
- (b) in the definition of “CGO Service member”, by substituting for the words “the Bank of England” the words “CRESTCo Limited (which is now responsible for operating the CGO Service)”.

Bob Ainsworth

Jim Dowd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Government Stock Regulations 1965 so as to ensure that provisions relating to the CGO service continue to apply after responsibility for operating that Service is transferred from the Bank of England to CRESTCo Limited (which will occur on a contractual basis on the day on which these Regulations come into force).
