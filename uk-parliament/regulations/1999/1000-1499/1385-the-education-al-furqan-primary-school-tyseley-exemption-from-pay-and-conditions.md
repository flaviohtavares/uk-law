# The Education (Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley) (Exemption from Pay and Conditions) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1385

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley) (Exemption from Pay and Conditions) Order 1999

Made

9th May 1999

Coming into force

1st June 1999

Whereas an application has been made by notice dated 30th March 1999 to the Secretary of State under section 3(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (“the Act”)[^f00001] by the governing body of the grant-maintained school known as Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley, Birmingham for exemption from pay and conditions orders, specifying 1st June 1999 as the date from which they intend to make their own provision as to the statutory conditions of employment of the school teachers employed by them;

Now, therefore, in performance of the duty under section 3(4) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley) (Exemption from Pay and Conditions) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

**2.** With effect from 1st June 1999, subsections (6) and (7) of section 2 of the Act shall not apply to the statutory conditions of employment of the school teachers in Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley, Birmingham.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

9th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 3(4) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (“the Act”), comes into force on 1st June 1999. With effect from that date, section 2(6) and (7) of the Act, which provides that the statutory conditions of employment of school teachers (their remuneration and such of their other conditions of employment as relate to their professional duties and working time) are governed by pay and conditions orders made under section 2(1) of that Act, shall no longer apply to the statutory conditions of employment of school teachers in Al-Furqan Primary School, Tyseley, Birmingham.

With effect from 1st September 1998 a new section 3 of the Act was substituted by section 13 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. The School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/2115](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2115)) made transitional provision so as to allow the Secretary of State to continue to make orders under section 3 as in force immediately before the amendment in respect of applications received by him before 31st March 1999.
