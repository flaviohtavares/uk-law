# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Breckland) Designation (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1364

AGRICULTURE

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Breckland) Designation (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

13th May 1999

Laid before Parliament

20th May 1999

Coming into force

15th June 1999

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (“the Minister”) has by order designated an area in Breckland as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now therefore the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)(d)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Breckland) Designation (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 15th June 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Breckland) Designation Order 1993[^f00003].

**3.** In Schedule 2 to the principal Order (management activities), in paragraph 1 (column 2), for “140” there shall be substituted “125”.

## 4. Saving

Article 3 of this Order shall not have effect for the purposes of any annual payment to a farmer which relates to any period before 1st May 1999.

Elliot Morley

Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

12th May 1999

We consent,

Jane Kennedy

Jim Dowd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

13th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Breckland) Designation Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/455](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/455)) (“the principal Order”), which designated an area in Breckland as an environmentally sensitive area. The principal Order implemented Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2078/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2078) (OJ No. L215, 30.7.92, p. 85) on agricultural production methods compatible with the requirements of protection of the environment and the maintenance of the countryside, as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2772/95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1995/2772) (OJ No. L288, 1.12.95, p. 35), as in turn rectified by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1962/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1962) (OJ No. L259, 12.10.96, p. 7).

Subject to a saving provision, the Order amends the maximum rate of payment to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food in respect of the management of heathland.

No regulatory appraisal has been made in respect of this Order.
