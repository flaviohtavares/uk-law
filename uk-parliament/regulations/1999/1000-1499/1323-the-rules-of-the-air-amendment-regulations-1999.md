# The Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1323

CIVIL AVIATION

The Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

10th May 1999

Coming into force

14th May 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 74(1) of the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 14th May 1999.

**2.** The Schedule to the Rules of the Air Regulations 1996[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

(1) In rule 5(2)(c)(ii):

- (a) for “an exhibition of flying” there shall be substituted “a flying display”;
- (b) for “exhibition” there shall be substituted “display”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (aa) for “the exhibition of flying” there shall be substituted “the flying display” and for “the organiser of the exhibition of flying” there shall be substituted “the flying display director”; and
- (d) in sub-paragraph (bb) for “display authorisation” there shall be substituted “pilot display authorisation”.

(2) In rule 5(2)(c)(iii) for “an exhibition of flying” there shall be substituted “a flying display” and for “exhibition” there shall be substituted “display”.

(3) In rule 20(2) for “flight information unit” there shall be substituted “flight information service unit”.

(4) In rule 35, after “air traffic control unit” there shall be inserted “or an aerodrome flight information service unit”.

(5) In rule 36(2) after “air traffic control unit” there shall be inserted “or aerodrome flight information service unit”.

(6) In the Table at rule 39(1) in sub-paragraph (b) for “aerodrome flight information unit” in both column 1 and column 2 there shall be substituted “aerodrome flight information service unit”.

(7) In rule 39(2) for “aerodrome flight information unit” on both occasions on which it occurs there shall be substituted “aerodrome flight information service unit”.

(8) In rule 39(3) for “aerodrome flight information unit” there shall be substituted “aerodrome flight information service unit”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Glenda Jackson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

10th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Rules of the Air Regulations 1996.

(1) Following changes to article 61 of the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995, references in Rule 5(2) are now made to a flying display, a flying display director and a pilot display authorisation (regulation 2(1) and (2)).

(2) Permission required by an aircraft to taxi on the apron or the manoeuvring area of an aerodrome or by a vehicle or person to go or move on the manoeuvring area of an aerodrome may be obtained from the aerodrome flight information service unit (regulation 2(4) and (5)).
