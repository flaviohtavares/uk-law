# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Provision of Technical Service

The approval authority shall notify to the European Commission and the competent approval authorities of the other member States, the names and addresses of the bodies that will be the technical service body for the purposes of these Regulations.
