# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Transitional provisions in respect of placing on the market

Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 4, a manufacturer may place on the market, before the date specified in the third column of the table in this regulation, an engine within a category specified in the first column of the table with a production date prior to the date specified in the second column in respect of that category:

| Category | Producton date | Final date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Category A | 31 December 1998 | 31 December 2000 |
| Category B | 31 December 1998 | 31 December 2000 |
| Category C | 31 March 1999 | 31 March 2001 |
| Category D | 31 December 2000 | 31 December 2002 |
| Category E | 31 December 2001 | 31 December 2003 |
| Category F | 31 December 2002 | 31 December 2004 |
| Category G | 31 December 2003 | 31 December 2005. |
