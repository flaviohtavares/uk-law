# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**5.** Where changes to the type approval are required for example because the particulars of the engine have altered since the granting of type approval, regulation 10 requires manufacturers to apply to the approval authority for an amendment or extension to the original type approval on payment of a fee.
