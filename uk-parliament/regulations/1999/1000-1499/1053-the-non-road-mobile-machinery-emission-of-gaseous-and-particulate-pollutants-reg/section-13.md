# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**13.** Regulations 16 to 20 relate to the enforcement of the Regulations. The enforcement authorities will be the Secretary of State and the Weights and Measures Authorities and the Department of Economic Development in Northern Ireland. Regulation 17 provides for offences and regulation 18 for penalties. Regulation 19 includes provision for the defence of due diligence and regulation 20 provides for the liability of persons other than the principal offender.
