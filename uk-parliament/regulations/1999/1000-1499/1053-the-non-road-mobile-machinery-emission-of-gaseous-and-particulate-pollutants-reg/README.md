# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1053

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999

Made

28th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

31st March 1999

Coming into force

26th April 1999

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the reduction of emissions of pollutants from internal combustion engines, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:
