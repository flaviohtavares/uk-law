# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**11.** Certain categories of engines are exempt from the Regulations. These include engines produced for use by the armed services and engines which have been type approved by the approval authorities of other member States.
