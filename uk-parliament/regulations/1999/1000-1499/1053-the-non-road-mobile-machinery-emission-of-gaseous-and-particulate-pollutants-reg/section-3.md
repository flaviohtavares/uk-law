# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** Regulation 6(1) and (2) sets emmission standards for these engines for the first time and these standards come into force in two stages commencing with the coming into force of these Regulations. A type approval certificate cannot be issued to a manufacturer unless the engines are approved to these new standards.
