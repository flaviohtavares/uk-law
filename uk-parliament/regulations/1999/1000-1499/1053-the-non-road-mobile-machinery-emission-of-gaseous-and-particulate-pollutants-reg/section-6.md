# The Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants) Regulations 1999, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**6.** Transitional arrangements have been included in these Regulations to enable manufacturers to continue to sell stocks of engines which have been made prior to the relevant production date. Regulation 7 enables manufacturers to continue to place these engines on the market for a further period of two years up to the final date specified.
