# The Consular Relations (Merchant Shipping) (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) (Revocation) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1124

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Consular Relations (Merchant Shipping) (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) (Revocation) Order 1999

Made

13th April 1999

Laid before Parliament

14th April 1999

Coming into force

6th May 1999

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 13th day of April 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by sections 4, 5, 6 and 16(2) of the Consular Relations Act 1968[^f00001], or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Consular Relations (Merchant Shipping) (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) (Revocation) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 6th May 1999.

**2.** The Consular Relations (Merchant Shipping) (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) Order 1970[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Consular Relations (Merchant Shipping) (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) Order 1970, which gave effect to the relevant provisions of the Treaty on Merchant Navigation between the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics signed at London on 3rd April 1968 (Cmnd. 5008). The Order comes into force on 6th May 1999.
