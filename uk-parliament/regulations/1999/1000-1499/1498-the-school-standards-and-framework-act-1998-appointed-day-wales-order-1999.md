# The School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (Appointed Day) (Wales) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1498 (C. 46)

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (Appointed Day) (Wales) Order 1999

Made

26th May 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 138(7) and (8) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 32 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Wales hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (Appointed Day) (Wales) Order 1999.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the purposes of paragraph 4(3) and (6) of Schedule 32 to the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 in relation to the payment of grant under the Education (Schedule 32 to the School Standards and Framework Act 1998) (Wales) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall be 1st September 1999.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Peter Hain

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

26th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st September 1999 as the appointed day for certain provisions of Part I of Schedule 32 to the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. It applies only to Wales.
