# The Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1998/3082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/3082) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1999 No. 1392

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

18th May 1999

Coming into force

1st June 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In article 4 of the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004], for “6 executive” substitute “5 executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

18th May 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by decreasing the number of executive directors from 6 to 5. The amendment is required because the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 ([S.I. 1998/3082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/3082)), in error, increased from 5 to 6 the number of the Trust’s executive directors. Under the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/1975](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1975)), the maximum permitted number of executive directors of an NHS trust is 5.
