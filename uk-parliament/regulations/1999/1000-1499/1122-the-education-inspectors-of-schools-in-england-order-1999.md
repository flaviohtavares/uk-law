# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1122

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1999

Made

13th April 1999

Laid before Parliament

23rd April 1999

Coming into force

14th May 1999

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 13th day of April 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 14th May 1999.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 14th May 1999.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
