# The NCIS (Secretary of State’s Objectives) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 822

POLICE

The NCIS (Secretary of State’s Objectives) Order 1999

Made

11th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

22nd March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 26(1) of the Police Act 1997[^f00001], and after consultation with the organisations mentioned in section 26(2) of that Act, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the NCIS (Secretary of State’s Objectives) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

**2.** The NCIS (Secretary of State’s Objectives) Order 1998[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

**3.** The objectives for the National Criminal Intelligence Service (“NCIS”) are–

- (a) to provide high quality and relevant criminal intelligence leading to–
  - (i) the dismantling or disruption of criminal enterprises engaged in serious and organised crime, and
  - (ii) the arrest and prosecution of criminals, whose activities take place in or impact on the United Kingdom;
- (b) to provide high quality and relevant criminal intelligence and information to law enforcement agencies, having regard to the Command Paper entitled “Tackling Drugs to Build a Better Britain – the Government’s Ten-Year Strategy for Tackling Drugs Misuse”[^f00003] and in order to suppress the availability of controlled drugs in the United Kingdom.

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

11th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order sets out the Secretary of State’s objectives for the National Criminal Intelligence Service (“NCIS”). These objectives replace those set out in the NCIS (Secretary of State’s Objectives) Order 1998, which is revoked by this Order. Section 2(4)(a) of the Police Act 1997 requires the NCIS Service Authority to have regard to these objectives in the discharge of its functions. Section 4 of the 1997 Act requires the NCIS Service Authority to issue a service plan which must give particulars of these objectives.
