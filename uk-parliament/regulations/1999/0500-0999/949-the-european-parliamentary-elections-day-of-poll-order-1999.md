# The European Parliamentary Elections (Day of Poll) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 949

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The European Parliamentary Elections (Day of Poll) Order 1999

Made

23rd March 1999

Laid before Parliament

30th March 1999

Coming into force

1st May 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3D(1) of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Day of Poll) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st May 1999.

**2.** The poll at the next general election of MEPs shall be held on 10th June 1999.

George Howarth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

23rd March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 10th June as the day on which the poll at the next general election of MEPs will be held.
