# The Supreme Court Fees Order 1999, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Determination of costs

Old fees apply where documents filed pursuant to a court order, or application made for review of taxing officer’s decision, made before this Order came into force.
