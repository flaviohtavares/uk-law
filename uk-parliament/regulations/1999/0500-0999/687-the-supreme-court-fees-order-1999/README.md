(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Supreme Court Fees Order 1980 and replaces it by specifying new fees for proceedings in the Supreme Court following the introduction of the Civil Procedure Rules 1998 which come into force on the same date as this Order.

In particular, the fee structure specified in this Order specifies fees payable at the two new stages of allocation and listing introduced by the Civil Procedure Rules. Where there are procedures similar to previous procedures, the fees have in most cases been increased.
