# The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Appropriations) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 674 (S. 45)

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, SCOTLAND

The Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Appropriations) Order 1999

Made

10th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of March 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the House of Commons[^f00001];

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 112(1) and (2), 113, and 129(1) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Appropriations) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Appropriation of sums forming part of the Scottish Consolidated Fund

(1) The sums specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order (amounting in total to £14,038,349,000) are appropriated for the year ending on 31st March 2000 out of sums forming part of the Scottish Consolidated Fund for the several purposes respectively specified in the corresponding entry in column 1 of the Schedule.

(2) There are appropriated in aid of the sums appropriated by this article for the several purposes specified in column 1 of the Schedule the sums respectively specified in the corresponding entry in column 3.

A.K.Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provisions for transitory and transitional purposes in connection with the coming into force, on 1st April 1999, of section 64(1) of the Scotland Act 1998, which establishes the Scottish Consolidated Fund. The Order appropriates sums out of sums forming part of the Fund for the financial year ending on 31st March 2000 for the purpose of meeting expenditure in that year in connection with functions for which expenditure will, by virtue of the 1998 Act and provision made under it, be payable out of that Fund during that year. It also appropriates in aid certain sums for the year ending on 31st March 2000 in connection with those purposes.
