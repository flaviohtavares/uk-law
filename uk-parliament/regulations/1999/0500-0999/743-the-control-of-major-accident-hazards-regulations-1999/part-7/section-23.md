# The Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations 1999, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Amendments

(1) The Petroleum (Consolidation) Act 1928[^f00023] shall be amended by the insertion, after section 25, of the following section—

> **25A.** The provisions of this Act shall not apply in respect of—
>
> - (a) any establishment to which the Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations 1999 \[S.I. [1999/743](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/743)\] apply by virtue of regulation 3 of those Regulations; and
> - (b) any site in respect of which notification of an activity is required pursuant to regulation 3 of the Notification of Installations Handling Hazardous Substances Regulations 1982 \[S.I. [1982/1357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/1357)\]..

(2) The Petroleum-Spirit (Motor Vehicles etc) Regulations 1929[^f00024] shall be amended by the insertion, after regulation 15, of the following regulation—

> **15A. Disapplication**
>
> The provisions of these Regulations shall not apply in respect of—
>
> - (a) any establishment to which the Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations 1999 \[S.I. [1999/743](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/743)\] apply by virtue of regulation 3 of those Regulations; and
> - (b) any site in respect of which notification of an activity is required pursuant to regulation 3 of the Notification of Installations Handling Hazardous Substances Regulations 1982 \[S.I. [1982/1357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/1357)\]..

(3) The Petroleum-Spirit (Plastic Containers) Regulations 1982[^f00025] shall be amended by the insertion, after regulation 7, of the following regulation—

> **8. Disapplication**
>
> The provisions of these Regulations shall not apply in respect of—
>
> - (a) any establishment to which the Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations 1999 \[S.I. [1999/743](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/743)\] apply by virtue of regulation 3 of those Regulations; and
> - (b) any site in respect of which notification of an activity is required pursuant to regulation 3 of the Notification of Installations Handling Hazardous Substances Regulations 1982 \[S.I. [1982/1357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/1357)\]..
