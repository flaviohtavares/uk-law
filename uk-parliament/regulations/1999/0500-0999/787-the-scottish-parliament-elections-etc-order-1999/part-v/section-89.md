# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 1999, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Dissolution of Parliament: minimum period

The “minimum period” for the purpose of section 2(3) of the 1998 Act (ordinary general elections) is a period of 25 days, computed in accordance with rule 2 of the Scottish Parliamentary Election Rules.
