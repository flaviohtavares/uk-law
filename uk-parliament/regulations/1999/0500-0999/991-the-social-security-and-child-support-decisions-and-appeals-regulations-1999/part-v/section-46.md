## 46. Appeals which may be struck out[^c-f687b42196c4b9211348c22c5e3cb9fd][^c-0dd94d105f70701d36ac5c664a108173]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), an appeal may be struck out by the clerk to the appeal tribunal— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] where it is an out of jurisdiction appeal and the appellant has been notified by the Secretary of State that an appeal brought against such a decision may be struck out; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] for want of prosecution including an appeal not made within the time specified in these Regulations; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] [^c-fc60302d79ded3253889bfe04e52bc57] ... *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] [^c-78c1f2e0f127dfef1297e69196fd77a1] ... for failure of the appellant to comply with a direction given under these Regulations where the appellant has been notified that failure to comply with the direction could result in the appeal being struck out *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] ; or[^c-1013ce97d39eeb1eca3ac42ec1a06cd1] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (d (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-73de3ffb9e14dfa210492d34fdf03b66][^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] for failure of the appellant to notify the clerk to the appeal tribunal, in accordance with regulation 39, whether or not he wishes to have an oral hearing of his appeal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Where the clerk to the appeal tribunal determines to strike out the appeal, he shall notify the appellant that his appeal has been struck out and of the procedure for reinstatement of the appeal as specified in regulation 47. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] The clerk to the appeal tribunal may refer any matter for determination under this regulation to a legally qualified panel member for decision by the panel member rather than the clerk to the appeal tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
