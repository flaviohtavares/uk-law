## 56. Correction of accidental errors[^c-2db4c8fbb46443228bb68dc4f271c00e][^c-d83c2e754e8e7e09016baa41e1982560]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] The clerk to the appeal tribunal *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] or a legally qualified panel member[^M_F_5f74a3be-f442-4f26-ef53-f560fe4136fe] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] that member, may at any time correct accidental errors in *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the notice of any decision[^c-37037c37f3c666aeb8a014633ce4c2fc] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] of an appeal tribunal made under a relevant enactment, the Child Support Act or the Vaccine Damage Payments Act. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-67fd02ad2f2dd827539a483b92cb1565][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] A correction made to a decision notice shall be deemed to be part of the decision notice and written notice of the correction shall be given as soon as practicable to every party to the proceedings. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] In this regulation and regulation 57, “relevant enactment” has the same meaning as in section 28(3). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
