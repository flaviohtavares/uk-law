## 40. Withdrawal of appeal or referral[^c-d94e0ad709f935734618a9d5d23c3ab9][^c-3f301c9e2de5de568e991cf6decb1ab4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] An appeal may be withdrawn by the appellant or an authorised representative of the appellant and a referral may be withdrawn by the *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-efa6d40b2feb331db63f277b33d74192] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]the Board or an officer of the Board, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-efa6d40b2feb331db63f277b33d74192] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c], as the case may be, either— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] at an oral hearing; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] at any other time before the appeal or referral is determined, by giving notice in writing of withdrawal to the clerk to the appeal tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] If an appeal or a referral is withdrawn (as the case may be) in accordance with paragraph (1)(a), the clerk to the appeal tribunal shall send a notice in writing to any party to the proceedings who is not present when the appeal or referral is withdrawn, informing him that the appeal or referral (as the case may be) has been withdrawn. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] If an appeal or a referral is withdrawn (as the case may be) in accordance with paragraph (1)(b), the clerk to the appeal tribunal shall send a notice in writing to every party to the proceedings informing them that the appeal or referral (as the case may be) has been withdrawn. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
