## 57B. Interpretation of Chapter V[^c-799ab18136ef64ff7c9ee2d14e0db3fd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] In Chapter V, except in regulations 58 and 58A— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- “Commissioner” includes Child Support Commissioner; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- “decision” includes a determination on a referral. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] In Chapter V— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- “decision notice” has the meaning given in regulation 53(1) and (2). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
