## 47. Reinstatement of struck out appeals[^key-9e8042b47735074733ac9a8a9b61aedf][^key-8ed559ea7bd634798e4c3dbb8e6eac7b]

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4291555375429c0416d6e7dea41516a9][^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] The clerk to the appeal tribunal may reinstate an appeal which has been struck out in accordance with regulation *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] 46(1)(d)[^key-53506ed2cce9e3186c21c9f43e6facb0] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] where— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the appellant has made representations to him or, as the case may be, further representations in support of his appeal with reasons why he considers that his appeal should not have been struck out; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the representations are made in writing within one month of the order to strike out the appeal being issued; and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the clerk is satisfied in the light of those representations that there are reasonable grounds for reinstating the appeal *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

but if the clerk is not satisfied that there are reasonable grounds for reinstatement a legally qualified panel member shall consider whether the appeal should be reinstated in accordance with paragraph (2). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-564e486f7cbacb91b134d5fac1eb04bb][^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] A legally qualified panel member may reinstate an appeal which has been struck out in accordance with regulation 46 *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] [^key-4ba9456fe489e9d6564a8018a75c02b4] ... where— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the appellant has made representations, or as the case may be, further representations in support of his appeal with reasons why he considers that his appeal should not have been struck out, to the clerk to the appeal tribunal, in writing within one month of the order to strike out the appeal being issued, and the panel member is satisfied in the light of those representations that there are reasonable grounds for reinstating the appeal; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c22ddb50a92ec4ad485c2734da40ab95][^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the panel member is satisfied that the appeal is not an appeal which may be struck out under regulation 46; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (d (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] the panel member is satisfied that notwithstanding that the appeal is one which may be struck out under regulation 46, it is not in the interests of justice for the appeal to be struck out. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
