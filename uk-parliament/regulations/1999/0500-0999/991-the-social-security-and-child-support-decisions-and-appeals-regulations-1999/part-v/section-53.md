# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 53

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

## 53. Decisions of appeal tribunals[^key-53a0fa15620ddec676e7605b8f53df8c][^key-972332d788cb81a7d2112042de259d31]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Every decision of an appeal tribunal shall be recorded in summary by the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, by that member. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] The decision notice specified in paragraph (1) shall be in such written form as shall have been approved by the President and shall be signed by the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, by that member. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] As soon as may be practicable after an appeal or referral has been decided by an appeal tribunal, a copy of the decision notice *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] [^key-e42bc4c18e62298e70d378b0cf321474] ... shall be sent or given to every party to the proceedings who shall also be informed of— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] his right under paragraph (4); and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4319e7a6f4ef098d4aff820ff81a93c2][^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] except in the case of an appeal under the Vaccine Damage Payments Act, the conditions governing appeals to a Commissioner. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(4 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a66c9af757ead28e5c911687525efe1c][^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Subject to paragraph (4A),[^key-bb2cf2901552b9cfac2117672263b4b3] *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a party to the proceedings may apply in writing to the clerk to the appeal tribunal for a statement of the reasons for the tribunal’s decision within one month of the sending or giving of the decision notice to every party to the proceedings or within such longer period as may be allowed in accordance with regulation 54 and following that application the chairman, or in the case of a tribunal with only one member, that member shall record a statement of the reasons and a copy of that statement shall be given to every party to the proceedings as soon as may be practicable. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(4A (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2aea63a87b50912a3ec70af9c163afda][^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Where— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the decision notice is corrected in accordance with regulation 56; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] an application under regulation 57 for the decision to be set aside is refused for reasons other than a refusal to extend the time for making the application, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

the period specified in paragraph (4) shall run from the date on which notice of the correction or the refusal of the application for setting aside is sent to the applicant. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(5 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] If the decision is not unanimous, the decision notice specified in paragraph (1) shall record that one of the members dissented and the statement of reasons referred to in paragraph (4) shall include the reasons given by the dissenting member for dissenting. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
