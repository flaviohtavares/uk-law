# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 39

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39. Choice of hearing[^c-a1dfedac21f85a79ca85ebf893b0a203][^c-28afee0ac58868aae8cc7d529568f7a2][^c-104170b079093216a2d1a939c42ccdc0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Where an appeal or a referral is made to an appeal tribunal the appellant and any other party to the proceedings shall notify the clerk to the appeal tribunal, on a form approved by the Secretary of State, whether he wishes to have an oral hearing of the appeal or whether he is content for the appeal or referral to proceed without an oral hearing. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Except in the case of a referral, the form shall include a statement informing the appellant that, if he does not notify the clerk to the appeal tribunal as required by paragraph (1) within the period specified in paragraph (3), the appeal may be struck out in accordance with regulation 46(1). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Notification in accordance with paragraph (1)— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] if given by the appellant or a party to the proceedings other than the Secretary of State, must be sent or given to the clerk to the appeal tribunal within 14 days of the date on which the form is issued to him; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] if given by the Secretary of State, must be sent or given to the clerk— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] in the case of an appeal, within 14 days of the date on which the form is issued to the appellant; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] in the case of a referral, on the date of referral, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

  or within such longer period as the clerk may direct. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(4 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] Where an oral hearing is requested in accordance with paragraphs (1) and (3) the appeal tribunal shall hold an oral hearing unless the appeal is struck out under regulation 46(1). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]

(5 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c] The chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, that member, may of his own motion direct that an oral hearing of the appeal or referral be held if he is satisfied that such a hearing is necessary to enable the appeal tribunal to reach a decision. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e78fce3de58c3b46636fb49a93701a9c]
