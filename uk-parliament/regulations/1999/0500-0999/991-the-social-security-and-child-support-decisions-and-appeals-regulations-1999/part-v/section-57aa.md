> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57AA. Service of decision notice by electronic mail[^key-9768442576ea9684f924dcd16a18bf17]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of the time limits in regulations 53 to 57, a properly addressed copy of a decision notice sent by electronic mail is effective from the date it is sent. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
