> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Assignment of clerks to appeal tribunals: function of clerks

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State shall assign a clerk to service each appeal tribunal and the clerk so assigned shall be responsible for summoning members of the panel constituted under section 6 to serve on the tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcaaadc1e9fc7818d099c2aace584b3d]
