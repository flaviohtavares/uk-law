## 57. Setting aside decisions on certain grounds[^c-73b297458f8cc1db8f39d9e52af97c34][^c-be9f2cfedd633128af1ece02c42bdff2]

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] On an application made by a party to the proceedings, a decision of an appeal tribunal made under a relevant enactment, the Child Support Act or the Vaccine Damage Payments Act, may be set aside by a legally qualified panel member in a case where it appears just to set the decision aside on the ground that— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a document relating to the proceedings in which the decision was made was not sent to, or was not received at an appropriate time by, a party to the proceedings or the party’s representative or was not received at an appropriate time by the person who made the decision; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a party to the proceedings in which the decision was made or the party’s representative was not present at a hearing relating to the proceedings. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] In determining whether it is just to set aside a decision on the ground set out in paragraph (1)(b), the panel member shall determine whether the party making the application gave notice that he wished to have an oral hearing, and if that party did not give such notice the decision shall not be set aside unless *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] [^c-03a34d967cf99d9374a1bfb6dd64c18f] ... that member is satisfied that the interests of justice manifestly so require. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3a1137d1122b0dd17bb0eacf9ca7c552][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] An application under this regulation shall— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] be made within one month of the date on which— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a copy of the decision notice is sent or given to the parties to the proceedings in accordance with regulation 53(3); or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the statement of the reasons for the decision is given or sent in accordance with regulation 53(4), *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

  whichever is later; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] be in writing and signed by a party to the proceedings or, where the party has provided written authority to a representative to act on his behalf, that representative; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] contain particulars of the grounds on which it is made; and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (d (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] be sent to the clerk to the appeal tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(4 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Where an application to set aside a decision is entertained under paragraph (1), every party to the proceedings shall be sent a copy of the application and shall be afforded a reasonable opportunity of making representations on it before the application is determined. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(4A (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4bfd170a289f85fd6ab358ebc971d19][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Where a legally qualified panel member refuses to set aside a decision he may treat the application to set aside the decision as an application under regulation 53(4) for a statement of the reasons for the tribunal’s decision, subject to the time limits set out in regulation 53(4) and (4A). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(5 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Notice in writing of a determination on an application to set aside a decision shall be sent or given to every party to the proceedings as soon as may be practicable and the notice shall contain a statement giving the reasons for the determination. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(6 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (7 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (8 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] An application for an extension of time shall not be granted unless the panel member is satisfied that— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] if the application is granted there are reasonable prospects that the application to set aside will be successful; and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] it is in the interests of justice for the application for an extension of time to be granted. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(9 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] For the purposes of paragraph (8) it is not in the interests of justice to grant an application for an extension of time unless the panel member is satisfied that— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the special circumstances specified in paragraph (10) are relevant to that application; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] some other special circumstances exist which are wholly exceptional and relevant to that application, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

and as a result of those special circumstances, it was not practicable for the application to set aside to be made within the time limit specified in paragraph (3)(a). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(10 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] For the purposes of paragraph (9)(a) the special circumstances are that— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the applicant or a partner or dependant of the applicant has died or suffered serious illness; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the applicant is not resident in the United Kingdom; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] normal postal services were disrupted. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(11 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] In determining whether it is in the interests of justice to grant an application for an extension of time, the panel member shall have regard to the principle that the greater the amount of time that has elapsed between the expiry of the time within which the application to set aside is to be made and the making of the application for an extension of time, the more compelling should be the special circumstances on which the application for an extension is based. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
