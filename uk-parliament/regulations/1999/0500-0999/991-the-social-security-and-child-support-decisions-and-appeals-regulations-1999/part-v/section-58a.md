# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 58A

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58A. Appeal to the Upper Tribunal by a partner[^key-e3f9d87115407cfffc0af27a6ec8c563][^key-2580685357b2090b98d7de03f61c7de8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A partner within the meaning of section 2AA(7) of the Administration Act (full entitlement to certain benefits conditional on work-focused interview for partner) may appeal to the Upper Tribunal[^key-c533c4c98066eee96d3768d412020ca3] under section 14 from a decision of the First-tier Tribunal[^key-40a4f1fd4f7e02404e80093f607a0716] in respect of a decision specified in section 2B(2A) and (6) of the Administration Act .
