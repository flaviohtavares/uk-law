## 49. Procedure at oral hearings[^c-42b6ea79b28c55965045f65d458cb18e][^c-388be2cd09b702afd08f2ad47903e5f7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Subject to the following provisions of this Part, the procedure for an oral hearing shall be such as the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, such as that member, shall determine. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Except where paragraph (3) applies, not less than 14 days notice (beginning with the day on which the notice is given and ending on the day before the hearing of the appeal is to take place) of the time and place of any oral hearing of an appeal shall be given to every party to the proceedings, and if such notice has not been given to a person to whom it should have been given under the provisions of this paragraph the hearing may proceed only with the consent of that person. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(3 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Any party to the proceedings may waive his right to receive not less than 14 days notice of the time and place of any oral hearing by giving notice to the clerk to the appeal tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(4 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] If a party to the proceedings to whom notice has been given under paragraph (2) fails to appear at the hearing the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, that member, may, having regard to all the circumstances including any explanation offered for the absence, proceed with the hearing notwithstanding his absence, or give such directions with a view to the determination of the appeal as he may think proper. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(5 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] If a party to the proceedings has waived his right to be given notice under paragraph (2) the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, that member, may proceed with the hearing notwithstanding his absence. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(6 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5426850b4a493ad925825ac1bdbe6c98][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] An oral hearing shall be in public except where the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, that member, is satisfied that it is necessary to hold the hearing, or part of the hearing, in private— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] in the interests of national security, morals, public order or children; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] for the protection of the private or family life of one or more parties to the proceedings; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] in special circumstances, because publicity would prejudice the interests of justice. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(7 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f6ffc3b4d1886b0da601f47a75998d5d][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] At an oral hearing— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] any party to the proceedings shall be entitled to be present and be heard; and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the following persons may be present by means of a live television link— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a party to the proceedings or his representative or both; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] where an appeal tribunal consists of more than one member, a tribunal member other than the chairman, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

  provided that the person who constitutes or is the chairman of the tribunal gives permission *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] [^c-7a24c8112f4b61fb5b4f2ae734a101b7] ... . *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(8 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] A person who has the right to be heard at a hearing may be accompanied and may be represented by another person whether having professional qualifications or not and, for the purposes of the proceedings at the hearing, any such representative shall have all the rights and powers to which the person whom he represents is entitled. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(9 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] The following persons shall also be entitled to be present at an oral hearing (whether or not it is otherwise in private) but shall take no part in the proceedings— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the President; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] any person undergoing training as a chairman or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] [^c-0efd809a82db65cbd0642ac8e1da58b1] ... member of an appeal tribunal or as a clerk to an appeal tribunal; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] any person acting on behalf of the President in the training or supervision of panel members or in the monitoring of standards of decision-making by panel members; *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (d (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] with the leave of the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, with the leave of that member, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] [^c-c0da7eb6c6a5b60f608bee27bf4f9513] ... any other person; and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (e (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] a member of the Council on Tribunals or of the Scottish Committee of the Council on Tribunals. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(10 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71dd670e52a8d446d5400d390d834510][^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Nothing in paragraph (9) affects the rights of— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] any person mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of that paragraph where he is sitting as a member of a tribunal or acting as its clerk; or *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] the clerk to the tribunal, *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

and nothing in this regulation prevents the presence at an oral hearing of any witness or of any person whom the chairman, or in the case of an appeal tribunal which has only one member, that member, permits to be present in order to assist the appeal tribunal or the clerk. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

(11 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737] Any person entitled to be heard at an oral hearing may address the tribunal, may give evidence, may call witnesses and may put questions directly to any other person called as a witness. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]

may remain present at any such sitting. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b147cf522dc234ef43289fd6ebd4737]
