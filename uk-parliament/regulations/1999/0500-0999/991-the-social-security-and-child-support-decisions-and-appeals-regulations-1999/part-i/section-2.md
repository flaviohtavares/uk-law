# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 2

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Service of notices or documents

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where, by any provision of the Act , of the Child Support Act[^key-077a0950afeb0e13ab8006fd6cdce037] or of these Regulations—

- (a) any notice or other document is required to be given or sent [^key-f3f36131410948f4cc2e1a97cef7ba37]... to an officer authorised by the Secretary of State or to an officer of the Board[^key-e1b64c594cc742ed049bb3516a5d03fb], that notice or document shall be treated as having been so given or sent on the day that it is received [^key-efb2fdb321ea52bb8b9204b842ff5a79]... by an officer authorised by the Secretary of State or by an officer of the Board[^key-1530f147c55e0a063b63b0308596177f], as the case may be, and
- (b)[^key-0b6ebc01a897661dce27dd86f9e0189a] any notice (including notification of a decision of the Secretary of State) or other document is required to be given or sent to any person other than [^key-c51798735d7e8c15800eb0ed176be90e]... to an officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8e86800c04f8cfb9f085d8407e6b7d8] [^key-c51798735d7e8c15800eb0ed176be90e]... an officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8e86800c04f8cfb9f085d8407e6b7d8] authorised by the Secretary of State or an officer of the Board[^key-bbad7ebc4be47c9de6d5260425e28096], as the case may be, that notice or document shall, if sent by post to that person’s last known address, be treated as having been given or sent on the day that it was posted.
