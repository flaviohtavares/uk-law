# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 24

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Child support appeals involving issues that arise in other cases

*Extent: E+W+S*

The circumstances prescribed under section 28ZB(6)(c) of the Child Support Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] [^c1661862] , where an appeal is pending against a decision in the case described in section 28ZB(1)(b) even though an appeal against the decision has not been brought (or, as the case may be, an application for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] permission[^c-ba8381c2d82a1fab5438c32d42cd0c23] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] to appeal against the decision has not been made), is where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] Secretary of State[^c-c7c3e175f74eca5fe6b98a42ea617ec4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] —

- (a) certifies in writing that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] the Secretary of State[^c-d15d8bfdfde8a1a0031a302cd54948de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb] is considering appealing against that decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb]
- (b) considers that, if such an appeal were already determined, it would affect the determination of the appeal described in section 28ZB(1)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4574393df60b0aef3dad0e3f5b9a96eb]
