# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 23

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Child support decisions involving issues that arise on appeal in other cases

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] For the purposes of section 28ZA(2)(b) of the Child Support Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] [^c1661861] (prescribed cases and circumstances in which a decision may be made on a prescribed basis), a case which satisfies either of the conditions in paragraph (2) is a prescribed case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

(2) The conditions referred to in paragraph (1) are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

- (a) if a decision were not made on the basis prescribed in paragraph (3), the parent with care would become entitled to income support if a claim were made, or to an increased amount of that benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] non-resident parent[^M_F_ac8ec7bd-48aa-490a-c805-343a1988a426] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] is an employed earner or a self-employed earner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

(3) For the purposes of section 28ZA(2)(b) of the Child Support Act, the prescribed basis on which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] Secretary of State[^key-f61d0a901916513d9ee1aefc2b7a8767] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] may make the decision is as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

- (a) the appeal in relation to the different maintenance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] calculation[^M_F_151a4e1b-f6b9-4fab-d376-52bf1869e2a7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8], which is referred to in section 28ZA(1)(b) of that Act had already been determined; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
- (b) that appeal had been decided in a way that was the most unfavourable to the applicant for the decision mentioned in section 28ZA(1)(a) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

(4) The circumstances prescribed under section 28ZA(4)(c) of the Child Support Act (where an appeal is pending against a decision for the purposes of that section, even though an appeal against the decision has not been brought or, as the case may be, an application for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] permission[^key-149031eef7f5c4e5d8fe48822715efc1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] to appeal against the decision has not been made but the time for doing so has not expired), are that the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] Secretary of State[^key-0969582c9a5765b3856d1cbcd7634207] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]—

- (a) certifies in writing that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] is considering appealing against that decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
- (b) the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] considers that, if such an appeal were to be determined in a particular way— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
  - (i) there would be no liability for child support maintenance, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8] such liability would be less than would be the case were an appeal not made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]

- “non-resident parent[^M_F_c00e4d85-3f35-4600-a5d9-f7306da8066a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]” and “parent with care” have the same meaning as in section 54 of the Child Support Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
- “employed earner” and “self-employed earner” have the same meaning as in section 2(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d2d64c359d001b14b5d8e4a0e73d8cc8]
