# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 22

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Appeals involving issues that arise in other cases

*Extent: E+W+S*

The circumstance prescribed under section 26(6)(c), where an appeal is pending against a decision in the case described in section 26(1)(b) even though an appeal against the decision has not been brought (or, as the case may be, an application for permission[^key-7756ebc01095cd7950871ec4a5fa198c] to appeal against the decision has not been made) but the time for doing so has not yet expired, is where the Secretary of State or the Board[^key-5ec6139b549efef9182de82425d3c541]—

- (a) certifies in writing that he is , or certify in writing that they are,[^key-89c533f6ac80fed218324613d724c5b5] considering appealing against that decision; and
- (b) considers , or consider,[^key-6b229509e0aead18a8a5dddd1eb2c85c] that, if such an appeal were already determined, it would affect the determination of the appeal described in section 26(1)(a).
