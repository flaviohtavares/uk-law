## 3A. Revision of child support decisions[^c-6effdf97093fb149db1f70a3c5f04da0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Subject to paragraph (2), any decision as defined in paragraph (3) may be revised under section 16 of the Child Support Act by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-8bb9d0b1f4abacc9ecde7953f1382a8b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]—

- (a) if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] the Secretary of State[^c-8e9da4eee5f51cc9329db2f562e72f59] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] receives an application for the revision of a decision either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
  - (i) under section 16; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] by way of an application under section 28G, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

of the Child Support Act, within one month of the date of notification of the decision or within such longer time as may be allowed under regulation 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

- (b) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
  - (i) the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] notifies the person who applied for a decision to be revised within the period specified in sub-paragraph (a), that the application is unsuccessful because the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] is not in possession of all of the information or evidence needed to make a decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] that person reapplies for the decision to be revised within one month of the notification described in head (i) above, or such longer period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] is satisfied is reasonable in the circumstances of the case, and provides in that application sufficient information or evidence to enable a decision to be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (c) if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] is satisfied that the decision was erroneous due to a misrepresentation of, or failure to disclose, a material fact and that the decision was more advantageous to the person who misrepresented or failed to disclose that fact than it would have been but for that error; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_aa62d3e0-7c5c-41b7-d75e-25767154f387][^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] if an appeal is made under section 20 of the Child Support Act against a decision within the time prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] by Tribunal Procedure Rules[^c-70a334c33a47f965490733d5159a470d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] but the appeal has not been determined; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (d) if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] the Secretary of State[^c-1bd9aa327a42db71383aa2a7389030f0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] commences action leading to the revision of the decision within one month of the date of notification of the decision; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (e) if the decision arose from an official error *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]; or[^M_F_1a2b157d-2555-415a-e95b-8eddfd61eccf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (f) if the grounds for revision are that a person with respect to whom a maintenance calculation was made was not, at the time the calculation was made, a parent of a child to whom the calculation relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(2) Paragraph (1)(a) to (d) shall not apply in respect of a change of circumstances which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

- (a) occurred since the date on which the decision had effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (b) according to information or evidence which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-81087d5e89fab6591aa5efffd157826c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] has, is expected to occur. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(3)[^M_F_d6f671d7-91db-464b-95a9-9b2c783fa520] In paragraphs (1), (2) and (5A) and in regulation 4(3) “decision” means a decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-a4bd6dc73f99b4aa1926678afdd98f3c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] under section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] 11 or 12[^c-2484c09c155070fe5777e3553c2db3a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] of the Child Support Act, or a determination of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] the First-tier Tribunal[^c-2877c47a0e57cc95204c721e93cf2c37] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] on a referral under section 28D(1)(b) of that Act, or any supersession of a decision under section 17 of that Act, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(4) A decision made under section 12(2) of the Child Support Act may be revised at any time before it is replaced by a decision under section 11 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(5) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-61982bb8e826efb35197347a8ce736e9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] revises a decision made under section 12(1) of the Child Support Act in accordance with section 16(1B) of that Act, that decision may be revised under section 16 of that Act at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(5A)[^M_F_d862cd4c-a8dc-47dd-ad32-7008603a672f] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-adba6c98158b1db69625b06c26529fdf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] makes a decision (“decision A”) and there is an appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (b) there is a further decision in relation to the appellant (“decision B”) after the appeal but before the appeal results in a decision by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] the First-tier Tribunal[^c-36d33594583ded76eb40d03fe0d4017a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] (“decision C”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
- (c) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] would have made decision B differently if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] [^c-c2eaa0a8e0c3199eb590593f5fd050e0] ... aware of decision C at the time *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] of making *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] decision B, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

decision B may be revised at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(6)[^c-0fd7931e4d3b4d08b64b97db4c5ad545] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-0fd7931e4d3b4d08b64b97db4c5ad545] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-42ec5db81f68caadfcc7d1eed499d5c2] Subject to paragraph (9), section 16 of the Child Support Act shall apply in relation to any decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State[^c-3ec8b82e0832a39b05ea202915fff9e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] not to make a maintenance calculation, as it applies in relation to any decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] under sections 11, 12 or 17 of that Act, or the determination of an appeal tribunal on a referral under section 28D(1)(b) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]

(9) Paragraph (8) shall not apply to any decision not to make a maintenance calculation where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d] makes a decision under section 12 of the Child Support Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22d62ec8b37cd5ad98900a98f254c27d]
