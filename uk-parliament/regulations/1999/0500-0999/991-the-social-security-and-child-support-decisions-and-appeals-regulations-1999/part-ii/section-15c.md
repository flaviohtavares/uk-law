# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 15C

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15C. Notification of a decision made under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act[^key-f9bf926d8c5bba50ee40b207ac9b6343]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Subject to paragraphs (2) and (5) to (11), a notification of a decision made following the revision or supersession of a decision made under section 11, 12 or 17 of the Child Support Act, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act, shall set out, in relation to the decision in question— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

- (a) the effective date of the maintenance calculation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (b) where relevant, the non-resident parent’s net weekly income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (c) the number of qualifying children; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (d) the number of relevant other children; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (e) the weekly rate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (f) the amounts calculated in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act and, where there has been agreement to a variation or a variation has otherwise been taken into account, the Variations Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (g) where the weekly rate is adjusted by apportionment or shared care or both, the amount calculated in accordance with paragraph 6, 7 or 8, as the case may be, of Part I of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (h) where the amount of child support maintenance which the non-resident parent is liable to pay is decreased in accordance with regulation 9 of the Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases Regulations (care provided in part by local authority) or 11 (non-resident parent liable to pay maintenance under a maintenance order) of those Regulations, the adjustment calculated in accordance with that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(2) A notification of a revision or supersession of a maintenance calculation made under section 12(1) of the Child Support Act shall set out the effective date of the maintenance calculation, the default rate, the number of qualifying children on which the rate is based and whether any apportionment has been applied under regulation 7 of the Maintenance Calculation Procedure Regulations (default rate) and shall state the nature of the information required to enable a decision under section 11 of that Act to be made by way of section 16 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(3) Except where a person gives written permission to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State[^key-cfc543c15f3ae8165ec1d47fed675856] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] that the information in relation to him, mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), may be conveyed to other persons, any document given or sent under the provisions of paragraph (1) or (2) shall not contain— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

- (a) the address of any person other than the recipient of the document in question (other than the address of the office of the officer concerned who is exercising functions of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] under the Child Support Act) or any other information the use of which could reasonably be expected to lead to any such person being located; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (b) any other information the use of which could reasonably be expected to lead to any person, other than a qualifying child or a relevant person, being identified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(4) Where a decision as to the revision or supersession of a decision made under section 11, 12 or 17 of the Child Support Act, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act, is made under section 16 or 17 of that Act, a notification under paragraph (1) or (2) shall include information as to the provisions of sections 16, 17 and 20 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(5) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State[^key-1121b19acc6a4801812c3f8f3d45225a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] makes a decision that a maintenance calculation shall cease to have effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

- (a) the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall immediately notify the non-resident parent and person with care, so far as that is reasonably practicable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (b) where a decision has been superseded in a case where a child under section 7 of the Child Support Act ceases to be a child for the purposes of that Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall immediately notify the persons in sub-paragraph (a) and the other qualifying children within the meaning of section 7 of that Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (c) any notice under sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) shall specify the date with effect from which that decision took effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(6) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Commission[^key-d31eafcafac9f34b64f39e4a25c49ee7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5], under the provisions of section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act, has made a decision that an adjustment shall cease, or adjusted the amount payable under a maintenance calculation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall immediately notify the relevant persons, so far as that is reasonably practicable, that the adjustment has ceased or of the amount and period of the adjustment, and the amount payable during the period of the adjustment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(7) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] has made a decision under section 16 of the Child Support Act, revising a decision under section 41A or 47 of that Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall immediately notify the relevant persons so far as that is reasonably practicable, of the amount of child support maintenance payable, the amount of arrears, the amount of the penalty payment or fees to be paid, as the case may be, the method of payment and the day by which payment is to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(8) Where the non-resident parent appeals against a decision made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] under section 41A or 47 of the Child Support Act and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] makes a decision under section 16 of that Act, before the appeal is decided *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall notify the relevant persons, so far as that is reasonably practicable of either the new amount of the penalty payment or the fee to be paid or that the amount is no longer payable, the method of payment and the day by which payment is to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07f46927bd85ab1bde07a8f48fd52a51] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(9) Paragraphs (1) to (3) shall not apply where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State[^key-3f8f4a6e213d9f8f56f99fd960fb79de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] has decided not to supersede a decision under section 17 of the Child Support Act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall, so far as that is reasonably practicable, notify the relevant persons of that decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(10) A notification under paragraphs (6) to (9) shall include information as to the provisions of sections 16, 17 and 20 of the Child Support Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(11) Where paragraph (9) applies, and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State[^key-9c66b16f0f2a2350f439904bcd823c3a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] decides not to supersede under regulation 6B, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall notify the relevant person, in relation to the decision in question of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

- (a) the fact that regulation 6B applies to the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (b) the non-resident parent’s net income figure fixed for the purposes of the maintenance calculation in force in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (c) the non-resident parent’s net income figure provided by that parent to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] with the application for supersession under regulation 6A(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (d) the decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] not to supersede; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
- (e) the right to appeal against the decision under section 20 of the Child Support Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]

(12) Where an appeal lapses in accordance with section 16(6) or 28F(5) of the Child Support Act, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] Secretary of State[^key-163506b84e8fe3a000110894d4c44d6d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5] shall, so far as that is reasonably practicable, notify the relevant persons that the appeal has lapsed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53c288c276bdb8a155be0e6d32b189a5]
