# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 15B

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15B. Procedure in relation to an application made under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act in connection with a previously determined variation[^c-f9bf926d8c5bba50ee40b207ac9b6343]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Subject to paragraph (3), where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-d0d43b9261e4f20d2e79b4f683cfb30a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] has received an application under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act in connection with a previously determined variation which has effect on the maintenance calculation in force, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]—

- (a) shall give notice of the application to the relevant persons, other than the applicant, informing them of the grounds on which the application has been made and any relevant information or evidence the applicant has given, except information or evidence falling within paragraph (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (b) may invite representations, which need not be in writing but shall be in writing if in any case *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] so directs, from the relevant persons other than the applicant on any matter relating to that application, to be submitted to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] within 14 days of notification or such longer period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] is satisfied is reasonable in the circumstances of the case; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (c) shall set out the provisions of paragraphs (2)(b) and (c), (4) and (5) in relation to such representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(2) The information or evidence referred to in paragraphs (1)(a), (4)(a) and (7), is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

- (a) details of the nature of the long-term illness or disability of the relevant other child which forms the basis of a variation application on the ground in regulation 11 of the Variations Regulations (special expenses — illness or disability of relevant other child) where the applicant requests they should not be disclosed and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-d4b56213047d411e6cc952d7bec72d80] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] is satisfied that disclosure is not necessary in order to be able to determine the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (b) medical evidence or medical advice which has not been disclosed to the applicant or a relevant person and which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] considers would be harmful to the health of the applicant or that relevant person if disclosed to him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (c) the address of a relevant person or qualifying child, or any other information which could reasonably be expected to lead to that person or child being located, where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] considers that there would be a risk of harm or undue distress to that person or that child or any other children living with that person if the address or information were disclosed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-cfa8551c8e0f2acb461cd81a2fd1fa12] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] need not act in accordance with paragraph (1) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

- (a) satisfied on the information or evidence available that a variation of the maintenance calculation in force will not be agreed[^c-c514d31e7376896d31ab574279da81ad] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a], but if, on further consideration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State[^c-a9587d664f5373ae20f24f2fb18280f2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] is minded to do so *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] shall, before doing so, comply with the provisions of this regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (b) were the application to succeed, the decision as revised or superseded would be less advantageous to the applicant than the decision before it was so revised or superseded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(4) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-e5c88b8dc1e9f376d00cfab1e1bc00db] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] receives representations from the relevant persons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State[^c-9842bb0f5d575cfb4a0de2df3aa2a546] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]—

- (a) may, if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State[^c-72fad1aea084e5c67b4460f8bfa6b231] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] considers it reasonable to do so, send a copy of the representations concerned (excluding material falling within paragraph (2) above) to the applicant and invite any comments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] to be provided[^c-f2335920b801baf6ce7b11a65dab8ea0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] within 14 days or such longer period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] is satisfied is reasonable in the circumstances of the case; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (b) where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] acts under sub-paragraph (a), shall not proceed to make a decision in response to the application until *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State[^c-76738df99e3881d9505e6aeb146fa665] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] has received such comments or the period referred to in sub-paragraph (a) has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(5) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-5716a7b575dceeb058110be9c91d8c29] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] has not received representations from the relevant persons notified in accordance with paragraph (1) within the time limit specified in sub-paragraph (b) of that paragraph, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] may proceed to make a decision under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act in response to the application, in their absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(6) In considering an application for a revision or supersession the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] shall take into account any representations received at the date upon which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] makes a decision under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act, from the relevant persons including any representations received in connection with the application in accordance with paragraphs (1)(b), (4)(a) and (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(7) Where any information or evidence requested by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-504d118ee7ecc071e2bd1074f4d6cefe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] under regulation 15A is received after notification has been given under paragraph (1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] may, if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] considers it reasonable to do so and except where such information or evidence falls within paragraph (2), send a copy of such information or evidence to the relevant persons and may invite them to submit representations, which need not be in writing unless the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] so directs in any particular case, on that information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(8) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] Secretary of State[^c-87aec277cb0f10d3461778f117103892] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] is considering making a decision under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act in accordance with this regulation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a] shall apply the factors to be taken into account for the purposes of section 28F of the Child Support Act set out in regulation 21 of the Variations Regulations (factors to be taken into account and not to be taken into account) as factors to be taken into account and not to be taken into account when considering making a decision under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

(9) In this regulation “relevant person" means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]

- (a) a non-resident parent, or a person treated as a non-resident parent under regulation 8 of the Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases Regulations (persons treated as non-resident parents), whose liability to pay child support maintenance may be affected by any variation agreed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
- (b) a person with care, or a child to whom section 7 of the Child Support Act applies, where the amount of child support maintenance payable by virtue of a calculation relevant to that person with care or in respect of that child may be affected by any variation agreed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-beaa1178273b54f7a53d07769d75fe7a]
