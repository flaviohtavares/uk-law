## 7C. Procedure where the Secretary of State proposes to supersede a decision under section 17 of the Child Support Act on his own initiative[^c-7b0c0c4a65e3ed069450c81edbdd6b9c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] Secretary of State[^c-971b8c689f1646b6cd555cb98eaa8530] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] the Secretary of State’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] own initiative proposes to make a decision superseding a decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49] shall notify the relevant persons who could be materially affected by the decision of that intention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75ef2264be05ae4c8d69916391263d49]
