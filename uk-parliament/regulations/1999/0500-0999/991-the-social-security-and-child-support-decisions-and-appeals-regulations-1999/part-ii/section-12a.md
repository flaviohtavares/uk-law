# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 12A

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12A. Recrudescence of a prescribed disease[^key-f6da60fa71de2cd71532197dfa123c2d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to a decision made under sections 108 to 110 of the Contributions and Benefits Act where a disease is subsequently treated as a recrudescence under regulation 7 of the Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Prescribed Diseases) Regulations 1985.

(2) Where this regulation applies Chapter II of Part I of the Act shall apply as if section 8(2) did not apply.
