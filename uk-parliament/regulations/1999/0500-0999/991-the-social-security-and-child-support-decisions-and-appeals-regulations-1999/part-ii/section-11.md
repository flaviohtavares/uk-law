# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 11

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Secretary of State to determine certain matters

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where, in relation to a determination for any purpose to which Part XIIA of the Contributions and Benefits Act or Part 1 of the[^c-2cb10c76dd4528d0eb34e8ce70500641] Welfare Reform Act applies, an issue arises as to—

- (a) whether a person is, or is to be treated as, capable or incapable of work in respect of any period; or
- (aa)[^c-562cbbd06bd7e44d3493de788af9dbfd] whether a person is, or is to be treated as, having or not having limited capability for work; or
- (b) whether a person is terminally ill,

that issue shall be determined by the Secretary of State, notwithstanding that other matters fall to be determined by another authority.
