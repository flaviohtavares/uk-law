## 6A. Supersession of child support decisions[^key-aac7aab2be21f59de231e5230b8fe80a]

(1)[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2][^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Subject to paragraphs (7) and (8), the cases and circumstances in which a decision (“a superseding decision") may be made by the Secretary of State for the purposes of section 17 of the Child Support Act are set out in paragraphs (2) to (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(2) A decision may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State acting on his own initiative where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) there has been a relevant change of circumstances since the decision had effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) the decision was made in ignorance of, or was based upon a mistake as to, some material fact. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(3) Subject to regulation 6B, a decision may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) an application is made on the basis that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
  - (i) there has been a change of circumstances since the date from which the decision had effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2][^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] it is expected that a change of circumstances will occur; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) the Secretary of State is satisfied that the change of circumstances is or would be relevant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(4) A decision may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) an application is made on the basis that the decision was made in ignorance of, or was based upon a mistake as to, a fact; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) the Secretary of State is satisfied that the fact is or would be material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(4A)[^key-93cf1c843afd2bf909c5218524848fe1] A decision may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) where an application is made on the basis that; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) acting on his own initiative where, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

the decision to be superseded is a decision of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] an appeal tribunal, the First-tier Tribunal, the Upper Tribunal or of a Child Support Commissioner[^key-b4b01f8078ad6133201862d3611d1698] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] that was made in accordance with section 28ZB(4)(b) of the Child Support Act, in a case where section 28ZB(5) of that Act applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(5) A decision, other than a decision made on appeal, may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) acting on his own initiative, where he is satisfied that the decision was erroneous in point of law; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) where an application is made on the basis that the decision was erroneous in point of law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(6) A decision may be superseded by a decision made by the Secretary of State where he receives an application for the supersession of a decision by way of an application made under section 28G of the Child Support Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(7) The cases and circumstances in which a decision may be superseded shall not include any case or circumstance in which a decision may be revised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(8) Paragraphs (2) to (6) shall not apply in respect of a decision to refuse an application for a maintenance calculation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(9) For the purposes of section 17 of the Child Support Act, paragraphs (2) to (6) shall apply in relation to any decision of the Secretary of State that an adjustment shall cease or with respect to the adjustment of amounts payable under a maintenance calculation for the purpose of taking account of overpayments of child support maintenance and voluntary payments, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7b88a20bcbc704806dfd43d972a2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(1) This regulation and regulation 6B set out the circumstances in which a decision may be made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Secretary of State[^key-a4d824aef67b302a71099cab45fb6b3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] under section 17 of the Child Support Act (decisions superseding earlier decisions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(2) A decision may be superseded by a decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Secretary of State[^key-fc699d603435ad3b56bc4bbfd40993bb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef], on an application or acting under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] the Secretary of State’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] own initiative, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

- (a) there has been a relevant change of circumstances since the decision had effect or it is expected that a relevant change of circumstances will occur; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (b) the decision was made in ignorance of, or was based on a mistake as to, some material fact; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
- (c) the decision was wrong in law (unless it was a decision made on appeal). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(3) The circumstances in which a decision may be superseded include where the relevant change of circumstances causes the maintenance calculation to cease by virtue of paragraph 16 of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act or where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Secretary of State[^key-fc4e42aa43cfa7080262e5d4900b8dec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] no longer has jurisdiction by virtue of section 44 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(4) A decision may be superseded by a decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef] receives an application for a variation of the decision under section 28G of the Child Support Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(5) A decision may not be superseded in circumstances where it may be revised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]

(6) A decision to refuse an application for a maintenance calculation may not be superseded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7bc26cfc6820185d4e19f76b841d0ef]
