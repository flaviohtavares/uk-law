# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 6B

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6B. Circumstances in which a child support decision may not be superseded[^key-aac7aab2be21f59de231e5230b8fe80a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] Except as provided in paragraph (4), and subject to paragraph (3), a decision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] Secretary of State[^key-b38f08eb27ab32fd5fbb757902533789] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03], an appeal tribunal, the First-tier Tribunal, the Upper Tribunal or a Child Support Commissioner[^key-d9d40bebb761d56226ba39e6f88ebce3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03], on an application made under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] 6A(2)(a)[^key-adaecb5cca9c9ec9b880a5a2942ff18c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03], shall not be superseded where the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

- (a) the non-resident parent’s net income figure fixed for the purposes of the maintenance calculation in force in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
- (b) the non-resident parent’s net income figure which would be fixed in accordance with a superseding decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

is less than 5% of the figure in sub-paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

(2) In paragraph (1) “superseding decision" means a decision which would supersede the decision subject to the application made under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] 6A(2)(a) but for the application of this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

(3)[^M_F_432effd9-3cd9-4ae4-d001-4e74613e01e6] Where the application for a supersession is made on more than one ground, if those grounds which do not relate to the net income of the non-resident parent lead to a superseding decision this regulation shall not apply to the ground relating to the net income of that parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

(4) This regulation shall not apply to a decision under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] 6A(2)(a) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

- (a) the superseding decision is made in consequence of the determination of an application made under section 28G of the Child Support Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
- (b) the superseding decision affects a variation ground in a decision made under section 11 or 17 of the Child Support Act, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
- (c) the decision being superseded was made under section 12(2) of the Child Support Act, or was a decision under section 17 of that Act superseding an interim maintenance decision, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
- (d)[^key-649cc19af670287014457cee197ef64c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ; [^key-bf90466bd4cd23de091cfc9a314a7da6] ...
- (e) the superseding decision takes effect from the dates prescribed in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] paragraph 4 of Schedule 3D[^key-2db845443e3b359db262b971d4a49a05] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]; or[^key-7b37281e593b3764c2c6e56f7f3facba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
- (f) a decision is superseded and in relation to that superseding decision a maintenance calculation is made to which paragraph 15 of Schedule 1 to the Child Support Act applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]

(5)[^M_F_d75f521d-c1a9-436a-d51d-5a058c8c88db] Where an application has been made to which paragraph (1) applied (“application A”) and a further application (“application B”) is made for a supersession on a ground other than one relating to the net income of the non-resident parent, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] Secretary of State[^key-30fe944e8b86632e4bf5efdddce25874] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03] may make a superseding decision on the basis that application A was made at the same time as application B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-09ad8e4a0fa3270d0775ba86545f0e03]
