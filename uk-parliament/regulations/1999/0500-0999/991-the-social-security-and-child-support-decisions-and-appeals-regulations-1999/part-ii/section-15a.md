> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15A. Provision of information[^key-f9bf926d8c5bba50ee40b207ac9b6343]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] Secretary of State[^key-8da07ea85ac8c216f010b3b6b57954ec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] has received an application under section 16 or 17 of the Child Support Act in connection with a previously determined variation which has effect on the maintenance calculation in force, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] may request further information or evidence from the applicant to enable a decision on that application to be made and any such information or evidence shall be provided within one month of the date of notification of the request, or such longer period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] is satisfied is reasonable in the circumstances of the case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a]

(2) Where any information or evidence requested in accordance with paragraph (1) is not provided within the time limit specified in that paragraph, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] may, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a] is able to do so, proceed to make the decision in the absence of that information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b95a96d1b814f740ad85e424d7d9d06a]
