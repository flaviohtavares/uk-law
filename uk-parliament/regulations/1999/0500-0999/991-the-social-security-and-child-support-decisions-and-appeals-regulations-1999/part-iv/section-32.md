## 32. Late appeals[^key-3e25dd8640824ef5b653fc870b347780][^key-1f1943036a908e7b2846484f15f6b343][^key-cfc1394c4b7111654d3892ae39e003bf][^key-4596f34deffa9d2c15e0a8ccd220a846]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-495e2a6f23858f0409634774d10d161b][^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] Where a dispute arises as to whether an appeal was brought within the time specified under Tribunal Procedure Rules the dispute shall be referred to, and determined by, the First-tier Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

(2) The Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] [^key-4a7f58b56fc32daac0009ef133cdd7f1]... or the Board, as the case may be, may treat a late appeal as made in time in accordance with Tribunal Procedure Rules if the conditions in paragraphs (4) to (8) are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

(4)[^key-5338e9f22035f6749fdeb16cfc5124c9] An appeal may be treated as made in time if the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f][^key-67a23a05c18131a2d45ade4209cd268d]... or the Board, as the case may be, is satisfied that it is in the interests of justice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (4) it is not in the interests of justice to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] treat the appeal as made in time unless[^key-55ea9f0f0e9a3bedcb98ec5b7f5752ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f], the Secretary of State or the Board, as the case may be,[^key-9af7cfe314441028ab0d9bf56181d75f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

- (a) the special circumstances specified in paragraph (6) are relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] [^key-bf4110a704ae53152c83c8d34db9815e] ...; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]
- (b) some other special circumstances exist which are wholly exceptional and relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] [^key-bf4110a704ae53152c83c8d34db9815e] ...,

and as a result of those special circumstances, it was not practicable for the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] appeal to be made[^key-5c77d064a144f9bd977a0da9be0f22ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] within the time limit specified in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] Tribunal Procedure Rules[^key-e52a565e65d9b574d688c3442fadde68] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f].

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (5)(a), the special circumstances are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] appellant[^key-ac688bd64be02abbe18c3642c05c755e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] or a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] partner[^key-b2e70741a6706ed5df595b6472642e3f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] or dependant of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] appellant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] has died or suffered serious illness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] appellant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] is not resident in the United Kingdom; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]
- (c) normal postal services were disrupted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

(7) In determining whether it is in the interests of justice to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] treat the appeal as made in time[^key-3e7219e59945b5cb85ee44c38e7834ee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f], regard shall be had[^key-dcce6bb1260b9ca358e6aa1a79f7af5a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] to the principle that the greater the amount of time that has elapsed between the expiration of the time *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] limit under Tribunal Procedure Rules and the submission of the notice of appeal, the more compelling should be the special circumstances.[^key-a69105843892afc765209ace89b4d1d5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

(8) In determining whether it is in the interests of justice to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] treat the appeal as made in time[^key-e65e91160401e6eb1d0733387cffeabc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f], no account shall be taken of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]

- (a) that the applicant or any person acting for him was unaware of or misunderstood the law applicable to his case (including ignorance or misunderstanding of the time limits imposed by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] Tribunal Procedure Rules[^key-cc06a542093a38b31ab89f28d720ce48] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]
- (b) that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] the Upper Tribunal[^key-e2be05b77f672ff8bffa63946b4755b7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f] or a court has taken a different view of the law from that previously understood and applied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b1abb0ed3c9e67a85b65419f1e84b90f]
