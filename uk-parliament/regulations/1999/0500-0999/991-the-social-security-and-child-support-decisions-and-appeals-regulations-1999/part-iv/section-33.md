- (a) in the case of an appeal under the 1997 Act against a certificate of recoverable benefits *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] or, as the case maybe, recoverable lump sum payments[^key-c75fd15292ec77d287ab5132a009324d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7], the Compensation Recovery Unit of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Department for Work and Pensions[^key-9c7c4fae68c457e9ba2c2e341c6e1575] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] at *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Durham House, Washington, Tyne and Wear, NE38 7SF[^key-69b1b048b47c870600a7e32c99418506] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7];

- (ddd (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8cd2711a662b8c30f9f6744af2d161dd][^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] in a case where the decision appealed against was a decision arising from a claim to a designated office, an office of a designated authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]
- (e) in any other case, an office of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Department for Work and Pensions the address of which was indicated on the notification of the decision which is subject to appeal[^key-ab540fbfd709f792b46920e55b4d5db6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7].

(4) Where the Secretary of State is satisfied *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] or the Board are satisfied[^key-777754ec4714bcd1d78a5485888600a4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] that the form, although not completed in accordance with the instructions on it, includes sufficient information to enable the appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] [^key-dbbd19b69a1e6e1863f432cd5db26617] ... to proceed, he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]or they[^key-248d1a297698fcdc22c35eff99791025] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] may treat the form as satisfying the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Tribunal Procedure Rules[^key-a6c2f0be0489684bd1a8791e336e8cf4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] .

(5) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] a notice of appeal[^key-b86c246e8786d29b16cf60de7485b55a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] is made in writing otherwise than on the approved form (“the letter”), and the letter includes sufficient information to enable the appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] [^key-32c495014016e5c2da68f7d4d1eab22a] ... to proceed, the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] or the Board[^key-5cf3f0c77547941509fb0cbbb5b6e7de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] may treat the letter as satisfying the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Tribunal Procedure Rules[^key-97ea081801187b969de3991921c2603b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] .

(6) Where the letter does not include sufficient information to enable the appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] [^key-5ee34e6e65d39f43b15f51989865ec4d] ... to proceed, the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] or the Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] may request further information in writing (“further particulars”) from the person who wrote the letter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

(7)[^key-549d232ea3e88a8a6314f1a045a9e934] Where a person to whom a form is returned, or from whom further particulars are requested, duly completes and returns the form or sends the further particulars, if the form or particulars, as the case may be, are received by the Secretary of State or the Board within— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

- (a) 14 days of the date on which the form was returned to him by the Secretary of State or the Board, the time for making the appeal shall be extended by 14 days from the date on which the form was returned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]
- (b) 14 days of the date on which the Secretary of State’s or the Board’s request was made, the time for making the appeal shall be extended by 14 days from the date of the request; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]
- (c) such longer period as the Secretary of State or the Board may direct, the time for making the appeal shall be extended by a period equal to that longer period directed by the Secretary of State or the Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

(8) Where a person to whom a form is returned or from whom further particulars are requested does not complete and return the form or send further particulars within the period of time specified in paragraph (7)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] First-tier Tribunal[^key-6ca1b92c91478ab3c7ad313eac376ec6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] shall determine whether the form or the letter satisfies the requirement of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] Tribunal Procedure Rules.[^key-2088d1d4ac537dbeb303306c81e736f9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

(9) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

- (a) a form is duly completed and returned or further particulars are sent after the expiry of the period of time allowed in accordance with paragraph (7), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]
- (b) no decision has been made under paragraph (8) at the time the form or the further particulars are received by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] or the Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7],

that form or further particulars shall also be forwarded to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] First-tier Tribunal which[^key-59b150b7b3be5780ce02889420e89131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] shall take into account any further information or evidence set out in the form or further particulars. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]

(10)[^key-fd72f938d1ccf12ef9212d704d2aaa1d][^key-a26de01a2004152858e8c69713bfcf70] The Secretary of State or the Board may discontinue action on an appeal where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] notice of[^key-7f2e431b2bd9cf52f5c098f03d434c99] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] appeal has not been forwarded to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] First-tier Tribunal[^key-2909cce34bb8ff2a089c8fc4e239345f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7] and the appellant or an authorised representative of the appellant has given written notice that he does not wish the appeal to continue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-020ae43ff598c795a5b851e158d30dc7]
