# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 34

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Death of a party to an appeal[^key-c58913b2a77d573b03d4cf60c15f064a][^key-25cbd0ef712e4c1a7c6db4dc97788fbb][^key-e875a332b3f49622b66438fe03c4e39b][^key-0bd8ea2b3274424de2baa7e819025ba4][^key-5e881f59c4bdecd4b865ca1043ae2b11]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] In any proceedings, on the death of a party to those proceedings (other than the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] or the Board[^key-1df919fefaa075f93bf9bcbd6beb33b3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab]), the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] or the Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] may appoint such person as he thinks *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] or they think[^key-ce4a6922ebe0b91c5e534763514f5a71] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] fit to proceed with the appeal in the place of such deceased party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab]

(2) A grant of probate, confirmation or letters of administration to the estate of the deceased party, whenever taken out, shall have no effect on an appointment made under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab]

(3) Where a person appointed under paragraph (1) has, prior to the date of such appointment, taken any action in relation to the appeal on behalf of the deceased party, the effective date of appointment by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] or the Board[^key-691c6fb4a280fe6075f05ec12c374d93] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab] shall be the day immediately prior to the first day on which such action was taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5ab8e6854598d0b77f2748d048c1eab]
