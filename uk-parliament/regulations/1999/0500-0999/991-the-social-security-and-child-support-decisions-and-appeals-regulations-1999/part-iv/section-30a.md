## 30A. Appeals to the First-tier Tribunal in child support cases[^c-ffc1952bf911492098abe9518d75b967][^c-e5602d6255ccb9e0e7a9d25e73ab0333]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 20 of the Child Support Act shall apply to any decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3121b0d8c746925e11e9ba34ebe6864] of the Secretary of State that an adjustment shall cease or with respect to the adjustment of amounts payable under a maintenance calculation for the purpose of taking account of overpayments of child support maintenance and voluntary payments, or a decision under section 17 of that Act, whether as originally made or as revised under section 16 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-723645bb265a0e88710875b814bc780f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3121b0d8c746925e11e9ba34ebe6864] of the Commission with respect to the adjustment of amounts payable under a maintenance calculation for the purpose of taking account of overpayments of child support maintenance or voluntary payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-723645bb265a0e88710875b814bc780f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3121b0d8c746925e11e9ba34ebe6864]
