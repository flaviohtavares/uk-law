# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 29

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Further particulars required relating to certificate of recoverable benefits or, as the case may be, recoverable lump sum payments appeals ...[^key-968229d31dc3715928052db827edf838][^key-776f480d1a829a8905184547d37db64e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-ce42eb862fa5feb61605205ad492c6a0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-ce42eb862fa5feb61605205ad492c6a0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-669ab09afcb85ee3292fcb8f7018961d] Where it appears to the Secretary of State that a notice of appeal in respect of an appeal under the 1997 Act relating to a certificate of recoverable benefits or, as the case may be, recoverable lump sum payments does not contain the particulars required, the Secretary of State may direct the appellant to provide such particulars. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0]

(4) Where paragraph (3) applies, the time specified for making the appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] [^key-f0ef9d7eedf315e3f160c11d344a1657] ... may be extended by such period, not exceeding 14 days from the date of the Secretary of State’s direction under paragraph (3), as the Secretary of State may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0]

(5) Where further particulars *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] [^key-f5e491a0c90c49ab646ee402ed497db4] ... are required under paragraph (3) they shall be sent to or delivered to the Compensation Recovery Unit of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] Department for Work and Pensions[^key-aaf879be578c015cea7c2e1271fe8e6c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] at *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] Durham House, Washington, Tyne and Wear, NE38 7SF[^key-e28fe3110d18a66407d477de08cdbf3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0] within such period as the Secretary of State may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-55458f7c2c15ba1eb6b9aa10bb6d98f0]

(6)[^key-43cc73a878e089ef136e45b53376bef5] The Secretary of State may treat any—

- (a) purported appeal (where, as the result of regulation 9ZB(2) (consideration of review before appeal), there is no right of appeal);
- (b) appeal relating to the certificate of recoverable benefits; or
- (c) appeal relating to the certificate of recoverable lump sum payments,

as an application for review under section 10 of the 1997 Act.
