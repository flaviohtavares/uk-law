# The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, section 30

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Appeal against a decision which has been replaced or (affected by repeal, see note) revised[^key-cb9d9c6d30896293a2702cf1f1db9c5a][^key-6617120679c1552a83a2daf8eefd0988]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An appeal against a decision of the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] [^key-9d026ffe5637c9a118f0ee7c535e754b]... or the Board or an officer of the Board[^key-6fd9cab5e63a0016524e03a56c67603b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] shall not lapse where the decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] is treated as replaced by a decision under section 11 of the Child Support Act by section 28F(5) of that Act, or is revised under section 16 of that Act[^key-5e758c9a54d9118b24668b0dc2fda9e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] or section 9 before the appeal is determined and the decision as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] replaced or[^key-e46a48242e403202705e0f8582970e62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] revised is not more advantageous to the appellant than the decision before it was *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] replaced or[^key-d3279c07439015dce9369a7bbf0c3168] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597] revised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2662b81d023b7b280e36caaa68ee0597]

An appeal against a decision of the Secretary of State or the Board or an officer of the Board shall not lapse where—

- (a) the decision is revised under section 9 before the appeal is determined; and
- (b) the decision as revised is not more advantageous to the appellant than the decision before it was revised.

(2) Decisions which are more advantageous for the purposes of this regulation include decisions where—

- (a) any relevant benefit paid to the appellant is greater or is awarded for a longer period in consequence of the decision made under section 9;
- (b) it would have resulted in the amount of relevant benefit in payment being greater but for the operation of any provision of the Administration Act or the Contributions and Benefits Act restricting or suspending the payment of, or disqualifying a claimant from receiving, some or all of the benefit;
- (c) as a result of the decision, a denial or disqualification for the receiving of any relevant benefit, is lifted, wholly or in part;
- (d) it reverses a decision to pay benefit to a third party;
- (dd)[^key-1265ad25eac6ad0cc391b530f20c0403] it reverses a decision under section 29(2) that an accident is not an industrial accident;
- (e) in consequence of the revised decision, benefit paid is not recoverable under section 71, 71A or 74 of the Administration Act [^c1661865] or regulations made under any of those sections, or the amount so recoverable is reduced; or
- (f) a financial gain accrued or will accrue to the appellant in consequence of the decision.

(3) Where a decision as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] replaced under section 28F(5) of the Child Support Act or revised under section 16 of that Act[^key-f948d2f6bae324d5bdd8e2683c17f89b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] or under section 9 is not more advantageous to the appellant than the decision before it was *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] replaced or[^key-224bcdcfc26f6762a7036c4dc7321580] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] revised, the appeal shall be treated as though it had been brought against the decision as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] replaced or[^key-07b0c75eab587ddfbdd1549f92ec0efa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb] revised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-540b37de674891e5356bf8c81cb6c7eb]

Where a decision as revised under section 9 is not more advantageous to the appellant than the decision before it was revised, the appeal shall be treated as though it had been brought against the decision as revised.

(4) The appellant shall have a period of one month from the date of notification of the decision as replaced or[^key-044603bc5cfc020a1109559aa8c781fa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-81171d81ab6f04bf1c1aa189890ae0c8] revised to make further representations as to the appeal.

(5) After the expiration of the period specified in paragraph (4), or within that period if the appellant consents in writing, the appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-09e0d7027aaf69532855c485a9032179] shall proceed except where, in the light of the further representations from the appellant, the Secretary of State [^key-a35a2679b50f12b849055e14b14cc8a8]... or the Board or an officer of the Board[^key-b208bb01a2cc9936e970d4ab696f5f6e] further revises his, or revise their,[^key-bcc1639e643418511977a41cde5257f9] decision and that decision is more advantageous to the appellant than the decision before it was replaced or[^key-f0aef056b8b377692a583ed8028810bd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-81171d81ab6f04bf1c1aa189890ae0c8] revised.
