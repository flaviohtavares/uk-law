# The Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996 (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 525

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996 (Amendment) Order 1999

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

3rd March 1999

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 1999

Coming into force

4th March 1999

Whereas under section 30(3) of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996[^f00001] the Secretary of State may by order add to Schedule 2 to that Act any organisation that appears to her to be concerned in terrorism or in promoting or encouraging it:

And whereas it appears to me that by reason of urgency it is necessary to make this Order without a draft thereof having been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 30(3) of the said Act of 1996, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996 (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 4th March 1999.

**2.** The following organisations, that is to say—

> - The organisation using the name “The Orange Volunteers” and being the organisation in whose name a statement described as a press release was published on 14th October 1998

; and

> - The Red Hand Defenders

are hereby added to Schedule 2 to the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996[^f00002] (which specifies organisations which are proscribed organisations for the purposes of that Act).

Marjorie Mowlam

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Northern Ireland Office

3rd March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds two organisations to the list of proscribed organisations contained in Schedule 2 to the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996, with effect from 4th March 1999. The organisations are The Red Hand Defenders, and the organisation using the name “The Orange Volunteers” and being the organisation in whose name a statement described as a press release was published on 14th October 1998. A copy of such a statement has been placed in the Libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
