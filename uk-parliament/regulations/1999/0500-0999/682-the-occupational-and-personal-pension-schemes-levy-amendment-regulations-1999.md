# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Amendment Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 682

PENSIONS

The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Amendment Regulations 1999

Made

9th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 175(1) and (2), 181(1) and 182(2) and (3) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Amendment Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Regulations 1997

In Part I of the Schedule to the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Regulations 1997 (amount of general levy)[^f00002]–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£10.00” substitute “£11.00”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£1.10” substitute “£1.20”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c)(i) for “80p” substitute “85p”;
- (d) in sub-paragraph (c)(ii) for “£110” substitute “£120”;
- (e) in sub-paragraph (d)(i) for “65p” substitute “69p”;
- (f) in sub-paragraph (d)(ii) for “£800” substitute “£850”;
- (g) in sub-paragraph (e)(i) for “50p” substitute “53p”;
- (h) in sub-paragraph (e)(ii) for “£3,250” substitute “£3,450”;
- (i) in sub-paragraph (f)(i) for “35p” substitute “37p”;
- (j) in sub-paragraph (f)(ii) for “£5,000” substitute “£5,300”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Stephen C. Timms

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

9th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Levy) Regulations 1997 to specify revised rates in relation to the general levy payable in the case of certain occupational pension schemes.
