# The Southport and Formby, the Southport and Formby Community Health Services and the West Lancashire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 889

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Southport and Formby, the Southport and Formby Community Health Services and the West Lancashire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made

19th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Southport and Formby, the Southport and Formby Community Health Services and the West Lancashire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

(1) The Southport and Formby National Health Service Trust established by the Southport and Formby National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

(2) The Southport and Formby Community Health Services National Health Service Trust established by the Southport and Formby Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00006] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

(3) The West Lancashire National Health Service Trust established by the West Lancashire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00007] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

19th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 1999:–

- the Southport and Formby National Health Service Trust,
- the Southport and Formby Community Health Services National Health Service Trust, and
- the West Lancashire National Health Service Trust.

It should be read in conjunction with the North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00008], which establishes a new community NHS trust, and the Southport & Ormskirk Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00009], which establishes a new acute NHS trust.
