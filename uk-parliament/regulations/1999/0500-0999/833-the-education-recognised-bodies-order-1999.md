# The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 833

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1999

Made

24th March 1999

Coming into force

5th April 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 216(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 5th April 1999.

**2.** The bodies which are specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as bodies which appear to the Secretary of State to be recognised bodies.

**3.** The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1997[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Tessa Blackstone

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

24th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order lists all those bodies which appear to the Secretary of State to be recognised bodies within section 214(2)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988. These are universities, colleges or other bodies which are authorised by Royal Charter or by or under Act of Parliament to grant degrees and other bodies for the time being permitted by these bodies to act on their behalf in the granting of degrees. An award granted by such a body is not an award of a kind referred to in section 214(1), which makes it an offence to grant, offer to grant, or issue any invitation relating to certain unrecognised degrees and awards.

This Order updates and replaces the list of bodies contained in the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1997, which is revoked. It includes a number of institutions which have been authorised to award degrees under section 76 of the Further and Higher Education Act [1992 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/13), and section 48 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act [1992 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/37). It also includes a number of schools, colleges and institutes of London University which have been permitted by the University to award University of London degrees.
