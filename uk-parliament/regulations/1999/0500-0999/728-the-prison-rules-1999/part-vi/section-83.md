# The Prison Rules 1999, section 83

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Contracted out parts of prisons

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Secretary of State has entered into a contract for the running of part of a prison under section 84(1) of the Criminal Justice Act 1991, that part and the remaining part shall each be treated for the purposes of Parts II to IV and Part VI of these Rules as if they were separate prisons.
