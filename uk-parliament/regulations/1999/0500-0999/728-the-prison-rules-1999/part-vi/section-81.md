# The Prison Rules 1999, section 81

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81. Delegation by governor

*Extent: E+W*

The governor of a prison may, with the leave of the Secretary of State, delegate any of his powers and duties under these Rules to another officer of that prison.
