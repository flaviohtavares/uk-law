# The Prison Rules 1999, section 70A

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70A. List C Articles[^c-0427c3e742190fa3c0f9772a96ff9b45]

*Extent: E+W*

A List C article is any article or substance in the following list—

- (a) tobacco;
- (b) money;
- (c) clothing;
- (d) food;
- (e) drink;
- (f) letters;
- (g) paper;
- (h) books;
- (i) tools;
- (j) information technology equipment;
- (k)[^c-4addb34b78f48f9cb376cf1af658bbbb] electronic cigarettes;
- (l) matches;
- (m) lighters.
