# The Prison Rules 1999, section 64

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Search of officers

*Extent: E+W*

An officer shall submit himself to be searched in the prison if the governor so directs. Any such search shall be conducted in as seemly a manner as is consistent with discovering anything concealed.
