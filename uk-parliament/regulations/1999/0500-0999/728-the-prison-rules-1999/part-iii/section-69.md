# The Prison Rules 1999, section 69

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Emergencies

*Extent: E+W*

Where any constable or member of the armed forces of the Crown is employed by reason of any emergency to assist the governor of a prison by performing duties ordinarily performed by an officer of a prison, any reference in Part II of these Rules to such an officer (other than a governor) shall be construed as including a reference to a constable or a member of the armed forces of the Crown so employed.
