# The Prison Rules 1999, section 66

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Contact with former prisoners

*Extent: E+W*

No officer shall, without the knowledge of the governor, communicate with any person whom he knows to be a former prisoner or a relative or friend of a prisoner or former prisoner.
