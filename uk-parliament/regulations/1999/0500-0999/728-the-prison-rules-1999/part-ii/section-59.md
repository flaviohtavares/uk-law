# The Prison Rules 1999, section 59

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59. Prospective award of additional days

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where an offence against discipline is committed by a prisoner who is detained only on remand, additional days may be awarded by the adjudicator[^c-9aed95cf60f8b9ee13a203c723f30844] notwithstanding that the prisoner has not (or had not at the time of the offence) been sentenced.

(2) An award of additional days under paragraph (1) shall have effect only if the prisoner in question subsequently becomes a [^c-12c7cb6d4c669129dc7fe9d5521618e9]... fixed-term prisoner[^c-68a91ba1b63f4a9ee18613adfdf31558] whose sentence is reduced, under section 67 of the Criminal Justice Act 1967 [^c16513771] or[^c-7579f436a3bc1b35bdea27156d515628] section 240ZA[^c-49f3c5e2fd49d91acd8766c4b7849982] of the 2003 Act, by a period which includes the time when the offence against discipline was committed.
