# The Prison Rules 1999, section 54

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Rights of prisoners charged

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a prisoner is charged with an offence against discipline, he shall be informed of the charge as soon as possible and, in any case, before the time when it is inquired into by the governor or, as the case may be, the adjudicator[^key-bbe4c1e62219fcc40ce79cbe663897c5].

(2) At an inquiry into a charge against a prisoner he shall be given a full opportunity of hearing what is alleged against him and of presenting his own case.

(3)[^key-1399df18963d78c19b41d05b44232896] At an inquiry into a charge which has been referred to the adjudicator, the prisoner who has been charged shall be given the opportunity to be legally represented.

(4)[^key-cfbf8496fb610208f6bc9a37be8ecc45] The right to legal representation is rescinded if the charge is referred back to the governor by the adjudicator and inquired into by the governor.
