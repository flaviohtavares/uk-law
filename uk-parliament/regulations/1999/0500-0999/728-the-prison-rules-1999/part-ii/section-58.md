# The Prison Rules 1999, section 58

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Cellular confinement[^c-185704257a3ee22b155fef8d7d124e00]

*Extent: E+W*

Before deciding whether to impose a punishment of cellular confinement the governor, adjudicator or reviewer shall first enquire of a registered medical practitioner or registered nurse, working within the prison[^c-3b6f17654a026e8b3ba6b30b9d893170], as to whether there are any medical reasons why the punishment is unsuitable and shall take this advice into account when making his decision.
