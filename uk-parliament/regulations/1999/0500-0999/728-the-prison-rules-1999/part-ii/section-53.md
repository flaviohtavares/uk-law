# The Prison Rules 1999, section 53

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Disciplinary charges

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a prisoner is to be charged with an offence against discipline, the charge shall be laid as soon as possible and, save in exceptional circumstances, within 48 hours of the discovery of the offence.

(2) Every charge shall be inquired into by the governor or, as the case may be, the adjudicator[^c-5adfcadbf5efb20f68123c750d2852ce].

(3)[^c-224621ca9f7f88f15fa2c65a95145059] Every charge shall be first inquired into not later, save in exceptional circumstances or in accordance with rule 55A(5) or 53B(3)[^c-4346375ca731fb00fdac0b0b5e236afa], than:

- (a) where it is inquired into by the governor, the next day, not being a Sunday or public holiday, after it is laid;
- (b) where it is referred to the adjudicator under rule 53A(2) or 60(3)(b)[^c-240571cc7e6da5fb2e6c351cecc804c9], 28 days after it is so referred.

(4) A prisoner who is to be charged with an offence against discipline may be kept apart from other prisoners pending the governor’s first inquiry or determination under rule 53A[^c-efce5ec1b75fe4fede0fed443d4f22df].
