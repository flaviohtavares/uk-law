# The Prison Rules 1999, section 13

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Religious denomination

*Extent: E+W*

A prisoner shall be treated as being of the religious denomination stated in the record made in pursuance of section 10(5) of the Prison Act 1952 [^c16513691] but the governor may, in a proper case and after due enquiry, direct that record to be amended.
