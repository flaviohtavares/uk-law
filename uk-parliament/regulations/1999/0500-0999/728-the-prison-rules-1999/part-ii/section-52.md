# The Prison Rules 1999, section 52

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Defences to rule 51(9)

*Extent: E+W*

It shall be a defence for a prisoner charged with an offence under rule 51(9) to show that the controlled drug, pharmacy medicine, prescription only medicine, psychoactive substance or specified substance[^key-81e5c6b99e4b98e0de235ed705c3d90d]:

- (a) [^key-7129252a9e35dec098a6cd835a022430]... [^key-cfe2062132cf4ffa9633682fd459456f]... had been, prior to its administration, lawfully in his possession for his use or was administered to him in the course of a lawful supply of the drug , medicine or substance[^key-a9a05da5eb999b4c7c96688823074637] to him by another person;
- (b) [^key-a534f92833b8a6be9197e8e2eec739b5]... [^key-5461f4a91a0c0b14c3b23edf7ed27e7c]... was administered by or to him in circumstances in which he did not know and had no reason to suspect that such a drug , medicine or substance[^key-8f98b978c2679a5baddcc42534c2fd0c] was being administered; or
- (c) [^key-9d5f0d676d8ceae767b20f0ec826d4d0]... [^key-951655cdee8919bf7d484bff8498d99f]... was administered by or to him under duress or to him without his consent in circumstances where it was not reasonable for him to have resisted.
