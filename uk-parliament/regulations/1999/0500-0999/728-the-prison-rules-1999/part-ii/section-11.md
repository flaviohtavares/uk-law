# The Prison Rules 1999, section 11

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Requests and complaints

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-fc31aa60d868952f2d2ee4a5f5f57fc1] A prisoner may make a request or complaint to the governor or independent monitoring board relating to the prisoner’s imprisonment.

(2) [^key-228c686cacd24a235d98bb8adc7fdbaf]... The governor shall consider as soon as possible[^key-5a5647d0bf7e5ba274ce2cf1aa44e62c] any requests and complaints that are made to him under paragraph (1).

(3) A written request or complaint under paragraph (1) may be made in confidence.
