# The Prison Rules 1999, section 59A

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59A. Removal from a cell or living unit[^c-08bf14937c813dc6a4e1a911ba3d5451]

*Extent: E+W*

Following the imposition of a punishment of removal from his cell or living unit, a prisoner shall be accommodated in a separate part of the prison under such restrictions of earnings and activities as the Secretary of State may direct.
