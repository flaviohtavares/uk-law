# The Prison Rules 1999, section 61

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Remission and mitigation of punishments , variation of compensation requirements and quashing of findings of guilt[^c-54829c88205a974ff26bb5b1ec4906af]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except in the case of a finding of guilt made, or a punishment imposed, by an adjudicator under rule 55A(1)[^c-7c7cae31101abec67a53085268e79568] the Secretary of State may quash any finding of guilt and may remit any punishment or mitigate it either by reducing it or by substituting another award which is, in his opinion, less severe.

(1A)[^c-bec3751d97f2b1df3ec40fd27e638149] Where a compensation requirement has been imposed by a governor under rule 55AB(2), the Secretary of State may reduce the amount of the requirement.

(2)[^c-989252272f350685f2e7d241a9a44638] Subject to any directions given by the Secretary of State, the governor may, on the grounds of good behaviour, remit or mitigate any punishment already imposed by an adjudicator or governor[^c-50f199b85c89a37b8ba8204a3055c51f].
