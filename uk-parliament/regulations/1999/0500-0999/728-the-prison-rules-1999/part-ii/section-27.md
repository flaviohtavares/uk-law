# The Prison Rules 1999, section 27

> Text as at 2026-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Beds and bedding

*Extent: E+W*

Each prisoner shall be provided with a separate bed and with separate bedding adequate for warmth and health.
