# The Education (School Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 601

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

2nd March 1999

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 15(2), 17(3)(d) and 45(3) of, and paragraph 6(a)(ii) of Schedule 3 to, the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations, “the 1997 Regulations” means the Education (School Inspection) Regulations 1997[^f00002].

## 3. Notification of inspection

(1) In regulation 5(1)(c) of the 1997 Regulations there shall be substituted for the words “the funding authority” the words “a person appearing to them to be an appropriate officer of the local education authority”.

(2) For regulation 5(2)(a) of the 1997 Regulations there shall be substituted–

> - (a) a reference to “a school which has a delegated budget” is a reference to a school which has a delegated budget within the meaning of Part II of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00003]

.

## 4. Reports

In regulation 7(2) of the 1997 Regulations there shall be substituted for the words “five weeks” the words “six weeks”.

## 5. Reports and action plans

Regulation 13(4)(b) of the 1997 Regulations shall be omitted.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

2nd March 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (School Inspection) Regulations 1997 so as to refer to schools' delegated budgets within the meaning of Part II of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. References to the funding authority (the Funding Agency for Schools) are also removed. The Regulations also extend the period of time within which registered inspectors must complete reports of school inspections from 5 weeks to 6 weeks.
