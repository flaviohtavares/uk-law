# The Family Proceedings Fees Order 1999, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires–

- (a) a fee referred to by number means the fee so numbered in Schedule 1 to this Order;
- (b) a rule or form referred to by number alone means the rule or form so numbered in the Family Proceedings Rules 1991[^f00003]; and
- (c) expressions also used in the Family Proceedings Rules 1991 have the same meaning as in those Rules.
