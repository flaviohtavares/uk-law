# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 615 (S.39)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 1999

Made

2nd March 1999

Coming into force

1st May 1999

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988([^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2)(Fees of Shorthand Writers) 1999 and shall come into force on 1st May 1999.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Increase in fees

Chapter IV of the Table of Fees in rule 42.16(3) of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994 (fees of shorthand writers)([^f00002] shall be amended by substituting, in the provisions mentioned in column 1 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts respectively specified in column 2, the amounts so specified in column 3.

## 3. Saving

Paragraph 2 does not affect such fees as are chargeable for work done before 1st May 1999.

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh,

2nd March 1999.

This Act of Sederunt increases the fees payable to shorthand writers in the Court of Session by about 4.1%. The last increase was in the [Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.2) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 1998 (S.I. 1998 No. 993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/993)).
