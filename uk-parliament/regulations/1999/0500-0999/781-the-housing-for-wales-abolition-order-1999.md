# The Housing for Wales (Abolition) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 781

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing for Wales (Abolition) Order 1999

Made

9th March 1999

Coming into force

31st March 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 143(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00001] and being satisfied that the duties of Housing for Wales under section 142 of the said Act have been discharged, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Housing for Wales (Abolition) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 31st March 1999.

**2.** On the coming into force of this Order Housing for Wales shall cease to exist.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

9th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the abolition of Housing for Wales in accordance with section 143(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998.
