# The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1996 Experimental Variation Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 997

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1996 Experimental Variation Order 1999

Made

29th March 1999

Coming into force

9th April 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1996 Experimental Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 9th April 1999.

## 2. Variations

The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1996 and Amendment slip[^f00003] is varied by the following provision of this Order.

**3.** In Schedule 2 item 13 is deleted.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

29th March 1999
