# The Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 816

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

12th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

16th March 1999

Coming into force

12th April 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 13B(2), 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and transitional provisions

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 12th April 1999.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to applications for mediation made on or after 12th April 1999 and applications made before that date shall be treated as if these Regulations had not come into force.

## 2. Amendment to the Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) Regulations 1997

In regulation 3 of the Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) Regulations 1997[^f00002], for “£172” there shall be substituted “£178”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

G.W. Hoon

Minister of State,

Lord Chancellor’s Department

Dated 7th March 1999

We consent

Jane Kennedy

Jim Dowd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 12th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Aid (Mediation in Family Matters) Regulations 1997 so as to change the weekly disposable income limit for eligibility for mediation from £172 to £178.
