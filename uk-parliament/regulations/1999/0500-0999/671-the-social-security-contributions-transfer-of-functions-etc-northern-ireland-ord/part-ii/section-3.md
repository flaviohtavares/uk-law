# The Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1999/671
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Transfer to Board or Secretary of State of functions relating to contributions, etc.[^c-dbee8c447b9f005eb6512026ca89a24c][^c-85c60046e52523af5ba433ad92512c2f][^c-2062b7859c1910c70454875a8abd0562]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-dbee8c447b9f005eb6512026ca89a24c][^c-ae3f1f4e039960848d0ca10dc3fac078] Schedule 1 to this Order (which contains amendments transferring to the Board or the Secretary of State functions of the Department in relation to contributions and certain associated functions in relation to benefits and pensions, together with other amendments related to the transfer of those functions) shall have effect.

(2)[^c-363c02847f9600be9414f6bd6d5bedcf] The functions of the Department under the provisions of subordinate legislation specified in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby transferred to the Board.
