## 10. Appeals against decisions of Board[^key-a1f7159e6f9fcd74461330b06a6ad458][^key-2374d7e118a862ab86693ea8758236a3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e76c2eef00db828b319fdddadc803740] This Article applies to any decision of an officer of the Board under Article 7 of this Order or under regulations made by virtue of Article 9(1)(b) or (c) of this Order (whether as originally made or as varied under regulations made by virtue of Article 9(1)(a) of this Order).

(2)[^key-e76c2eef00db828b319fdddadc803740] In the case of a decision to which this Article applies—

- (a) if it relates to a person’s entitlement to statutory sick pay , statutory maternity pay,[^M_F_2c239958-2e4a-40ff-8167-d0d122deb31a] statutory paternity pay, statutory adoption pay[^key-62e2ebf4b2411a9b66a39d6742fe163f] , statutory shared parental pay or statutory parental bereavement pay[^key-5fa65cf796a4b44afaeb102ae9fcd5a0], the employee and employer concerned shall each have a right to appeal to the tribunal[^key-a1fece0fd31ff0caf5751e2124db175d], and
- (b) in any other case, the person in respect of whom the decision is made and such other person as may be prescribed shall have a right to appeal to the tribunal.

(3)[^key-e76c2eef00db828b319fdddadc803740] In paragraph (2)(b) of this Article “prescribed” means prescribed by the Board by regulations.

(4)[^key-1550c6f948f9e01bd4a843b9d645323d] This Article has effect subject to section 115C[^f00012] of the Administration Act (appeals in relation to personal liability notices).
