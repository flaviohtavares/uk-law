## 12. Regulations with respect to appeals[^c-63a654b6736d94aa5a3dc06e26908e89][^c-fed08fe0607a253379e56a7a7b200602][^c-5cd438f1079dbebd336bd2a017cf8fc3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Board may, by regulations made with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor, make provision with respect to appeals to the tribunal[^c-4a7f491fc9019128038919b20922bf1d] under this Part.

(2) Regulations under paragraph (1) of this Article may, in particular—

- (a) make provision with respect to any of the matters dealt with in the following provisions of the Taxes Management Act 1970—
  - (i)[^c-af8e1baacc4e1cfe03bd376b4f7feb96] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) sections 48 to 54 (appeals to the tribunal[^c-76f64f8b25a6b59b99112652281b395f]), and
  - (iii) section 56 (payment of tax where there is a further appeal)[^c-f61a0aa0ac2e4abf0dd7b58b7917fedf], or
- (b) provide for any of those provisions of that Act to apply, with such modifications as may be specified in the regulations, in relation to an appeal to the tribunal[^c-47cc16892ba819817486cb2bf92919a9] under this Part.
