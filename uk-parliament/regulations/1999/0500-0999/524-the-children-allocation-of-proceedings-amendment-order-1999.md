# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 524

FAMILY LAW

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

2nd March 1999

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Part 1 of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Amendments to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991

In Schedule 1 of the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002],

- (a) under the heading “**Northern Circuit**”, after “Manchester County Court”, there shall be inserted “Oldham County Court”;
- (b) under the heading “**Western Circuit**”, there shall be inserted “Barnstaple County Court”.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

2nd March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 so as to nominate Oldham County Court and Barnstaple County Court as Family Hearing Centres
