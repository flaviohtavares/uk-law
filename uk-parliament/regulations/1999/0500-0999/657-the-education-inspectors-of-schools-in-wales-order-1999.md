# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 657

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1999

Made

10th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

22nd March 1999

Coming into force

12th April 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of March 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 12th April 1999.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales with effect from 12th April 1999.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- James Bennett Esquire
- Mrs Una Mary Connolly
- Mark Dacey Esquire
- Edward William Graham Esquire
- Mrs Shan Lilian Mary Richards

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales.
