# The Housing Benefit (Permitted Totals) (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 642

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Housing Benefit (Permitted Totals) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

8th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 134(12) and 189(1) and (3) to (6) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with organisations appearing to him to be representative of the authorities concerned[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing Benefit (Permitted Totals) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Substitution of Schedule to the Housing Benefit (Permitted Totals) Order 1996

For the Schedule to the Housing Benefit (Permitted Totals) Order 1996[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Angela Eagle

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

8th March 1999
