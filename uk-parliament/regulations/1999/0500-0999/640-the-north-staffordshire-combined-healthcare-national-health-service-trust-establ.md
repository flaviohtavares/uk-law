# The North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1998 No. 2972](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2972) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

1999 No. 640

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

5th March 1999

Coming into force

17th March 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as The North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 17th March 1999.

(2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means The North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00005].

## 2. Amendment of Order

In article 3(2)(b) of the Establishment Order (functions of the trust), for the words “and at Hilton Road site”[^f00006] there are substituted the words “land at Hilton Road”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

5th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust.

It corrects an error introduced into the Establishment Order by The North Staffordshire Combined Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 by replacing the words “and at Hilton Road site” with the words “land at Hilton Road”.
