# The Trecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 634

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Trecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made

8th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Trecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Trecare National Health Service Trust established by The Cornwall and Isles of Scilly Mental Handicap National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00005] is dissolved, and accordingly that Order is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

8th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the Trecare National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1999.

This Order should be read in conjunction with The Cornwall Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999[^f00006].
