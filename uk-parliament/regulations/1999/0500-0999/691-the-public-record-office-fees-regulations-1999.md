# The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 691

PUBLIC RECORDS

The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1999

Made

8th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(5) of the Public Records Act 1958[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

**2.** (1) Subject to paragraph (3), the fees to be charged for the authentication of copies of or extracts from records under the charge of the Keeper of Public Records and for other services afforded by officers of the Public Record Office shall be those set out in column A of the Schedule in place of those set out in column B.

(2) In the Schedule, where a charge is made by reference to an hour, the full hourly charge shall be payable in respect of part of an hour.

(3) The Keeper of Public Records may remit a fee where the service performed or to be performed by him has been or is likely to be exceptionally simple.

**3.** The Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1998[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Irvine of Lairg,C.

Dated 5th March 1999

We concur,

Clive J.C. Betts

Bob Ainsworth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 8th March 1999
