# The Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 653 (S. 43)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made

4th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 105(6) of and paragraph 25(1) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service as a whole to make this Order[^f00002], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 25(3) of the said Schedule 7A[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trust and consequent revocation

The Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust established by the Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00004] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Sam Galbraith

Minister for Health, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the Scottish Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1999.
