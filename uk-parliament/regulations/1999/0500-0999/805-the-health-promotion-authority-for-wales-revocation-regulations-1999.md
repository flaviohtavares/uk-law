# The Health Promotion Authority for Wales (Revocation) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 805

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Health Promotion Authority for Wales (Revocation) Regulations 1999

Made

11th March 1999

Laid before Parliament

18th March 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(4) of and paragraphs 12 and 16 of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Promotion Authority for Wales (Revocation) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Revocation of Regulations

The Health Promotion Authority for Wales Regulations 1997[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

11th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Health Promotion Authority for Wales Regulations 1997 which provided for the membership and procedure of the Health Promotion Authority for Wales.
