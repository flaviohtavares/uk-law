# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 1.4

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.4. Application[^c-5ce18e79eba7c0ff608ba9c777e2b2e7]

*Extent: S*

Unless otherwise provided in this Act of Sederunt or in any other enactment, any application or appeal to the sheriff shall be by way of summary application and the provisions of Chapter 1A and[^c-877c82e842f9b503b8eb77c3d5d3e969] Chapter 2 of this Act of Sederunt shall apply accordingly.
