# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 2.23

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.23. Remuneration of assessors[^c-c8b5338baf5031f9094cffebbb1c3f8f]

*Extent: S*

Where an assessor is appointed by the sheriff to assist him in determining the summary application, the remuneration to be paid to such assessor shall be part of the expenses of the application.
