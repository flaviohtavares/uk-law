# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 2.8

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.8. Orders against which caveats may be lodged[^key-e00a3bb8ee012a44d4f9baf0cf2b1ccd]

*Extent: S*

A person may lodge a caveat against an interim order (other than an order under section 1 of the Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1972 (orders for inspection of documents and other property, etc.))[^f00015] sought before service of the initial writ.
