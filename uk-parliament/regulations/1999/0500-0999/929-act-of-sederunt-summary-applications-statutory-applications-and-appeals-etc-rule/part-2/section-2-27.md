# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 2.27

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.27. Form of warrant for execution[^key-407d7a3ad647ad630fede95555204eeb]

*Extent: S*

An extract of a decree on which execution may proceed shall include a warrant for execution in the following terms:–

> This extract is warrant for all lawful execution hereon.

.
