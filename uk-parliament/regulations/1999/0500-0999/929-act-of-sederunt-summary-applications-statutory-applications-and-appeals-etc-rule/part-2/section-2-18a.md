# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 2.18A

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.18A. Form of schedule of arrestment on the dependence[^c-859c4854c9312486968c47adda1b19ad]

*Extent: S*

(1) An arrestment on the dependence shall be served by serving the schedule of arrestment on the arrestee in Form 10A.

(2) A certificate of execution shall be lodged with the sheriff clerk in Form 10B.
