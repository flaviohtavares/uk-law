# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 2.28

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.28. Date of decree in extract[^c-c54d84b97f152934ebf0c19eb69ea35b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Sheriff Appeal Court[^c-b40380042cf8437b4fbcb89718af022e] has adhered to the decision of the sheriff following an appeal, the date to be inserted in the extract decree as the date of decree shall be the date of the decision of the Sheriff Appeal Court.

(2) Where a decree has more than one date it shall not be necessary to specify in an extract what was done on each date.
