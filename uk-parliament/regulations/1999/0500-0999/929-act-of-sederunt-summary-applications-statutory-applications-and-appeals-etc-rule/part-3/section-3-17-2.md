# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.17.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.17.2. Applications for extended detention of cash or seized property[^key-7c2a1a88c0e8fce5a4193f56fa2c37d0][^key-e6aae4e708745102e00db3aeb7474236]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application to the sheriff for an order under paragraph 3(2) (extended detention of seized cash) or paragraph 10D(1) (further detention of seized property)[^key-a84579ba72090b817cff27090af0743b] shall be made by summary application.

(2) An application for any further order for the detention of cash under paragraph 3(2) or seized property under paragraph 10D(1)[^key-08dba26efe9e938d660eb0e231dce9c2] shall be made by minute in the original process and shall be proceeded with in accordance with sub-paragraph (3) below.

(3) On the lodging of an application for any further order the sheriff shall–

- (a) fix a date for determination of the application; and
- (b) except where paragraph (4) below applies,[^key-37ffb663e1a89ba085721c0d27fbbd3a] order service of the application together with notice of such date for determination on any persons whom he considers may be affected.

(4)[^key-372fb79c70f78c371e9ef9270c1a452c] This paragraph applies where the sheriff determines under paragraph 3(3A) or 10D(4) that the application is to be made and heard without notice.
