# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.33.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.

## 3.33.2. Intimation to Commission[^M_F_5afff3ac-9e63-48b7-8f7d-a1ffd046039a][^key-6adfdc8da9b2fb678b73f2e77381cbcc]

*Extent: S*
