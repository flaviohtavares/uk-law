# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.46.3

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.46.3. Further applications for extended retention of travel documents[^c-88ed6d0b9b208b5def0a7cb3d66b4191][^c-cccd901cab88d8ae9ca54e9dc355a86e]

*Extent: S*

A further application under paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 1, by virtue of paragraph 12(1), is to be made by minute in the process relating to the extension of the 14-day period.
