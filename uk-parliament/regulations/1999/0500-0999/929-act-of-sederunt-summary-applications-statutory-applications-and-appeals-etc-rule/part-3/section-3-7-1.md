# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.7.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.7.1. Interpretation and application[^key-127c3283c0cdc259d33a064411e21483]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part, “the Act” means the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00036].

(2) This Part applies to appeals under section 39 of the Act[^f00037].
