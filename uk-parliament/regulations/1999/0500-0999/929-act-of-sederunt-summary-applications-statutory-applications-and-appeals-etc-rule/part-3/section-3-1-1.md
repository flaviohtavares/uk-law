# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.1.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.1.1. Interpretation and application[^c-a0017dee2e5ab7c07125d295cfd274ad]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part,

- (a)[^M_F_5ed88fdc-8c86-43a6-f44d-0927f00582d4] “the Act” means the Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1972[^f00022]; and[^M_F_1f0b4191-1425-40d8-ffc5-6aee9379b692]
- (b) “listed items” means a list of the documents and other property which the applicant in terms of rule 3.1.2 wishes to be made the subject of the order.

(2) This Part applies to applications under section 1(1) of the Act.
