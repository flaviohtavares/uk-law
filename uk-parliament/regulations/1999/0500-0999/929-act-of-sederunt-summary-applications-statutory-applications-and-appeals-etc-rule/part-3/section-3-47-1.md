# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.47.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.47.1. Interpretation[^key-d3599cb8149db72584c2124e037b9487]

*Extent: S*

In this part—

- “the 2007 Act” means the Serious Crime Act 2007;
- “person who is the subject of a serious crime prevention order” is to be construed in accordance with section 1(6) of the 2007 Act;
- “serious crime prevention order” has the meaning given by section 1(5) of the 2007 Act; and
- “subject” means the person who is the subject of a serious crime prevention order.
