## 3.32.1. Interpretation[^key-fa447fd09f0a7fc734f80396883d76d7]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the 1981 Act” means the Animal Health Act 1981; and
- “the 2006 Act” means the Animal Health and Welfare (Scotland) Act 2006.
