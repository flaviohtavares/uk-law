# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.17.8

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.17.8. Associated and joint property: transfer to Court of Session[^key-47cbc6dcab0b2106e33e6cd935b961c8][^key-7c2a1a88c0e8fce5a4193f56fa2c37d0]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the sheriff makes a transfer to the Court of Session under paragraph 10J (associated and joint property: default of agreement) of an application made under paragraph 10G(1)(b).

(2) No later than 4 days after the sheriff has pronounced an interlocutor transferring the application to the Court of Session under paragraph 10J, the sheriff clerk must—

- (a) send written notice of the transfer to the parties;
- (b) certify on the interlocutor sheet that sub-paragraph (a) has been complied with; and
- (c) transmit the process to the Deputy Principal Clerk of Session.

(3) Failure by a sheriff clerk to comply with paragraph (2)(a) or (b) above does not affect the validity of the transfer.
