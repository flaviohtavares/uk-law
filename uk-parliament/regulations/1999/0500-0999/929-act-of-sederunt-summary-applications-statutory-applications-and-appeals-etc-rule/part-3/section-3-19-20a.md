## 3.19.20A. Discharge or variation of detention order[^c-8b48dfef446cf63c7ba114afa9983f73]

*Extent: S*

An application to the sheriff under section 127N(2) (discharge, variation and lapse of detention order) shall be made by minute in the process of the application for an order extending the period for which property may be detained under section 127J.
