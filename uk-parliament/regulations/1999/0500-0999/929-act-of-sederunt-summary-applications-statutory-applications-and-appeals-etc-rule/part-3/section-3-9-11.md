# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.9.11

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.9.11. Procedure for fixing and finding caution[^c-f2e6e7fd6b19d7ae3c0b40789bf26e50]

*Extent: S*

Rules 9 to 12 of the Act of Sederunt (Judicial Factors Rules) 1992 (fixing and finding caution in judicial factories)[^f00047] shall, with the necessary modifications, apply to the fixing and finding of caution by an administrator under this Part as they apply to the fixing and finding of caution by a judicial factor.
