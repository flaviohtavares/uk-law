## 3.1.4. Modification of undertakings[^M_F_7a957a71-6fb4-48f6-ad08-0b773ca04371]

*Extent: S*

The sheriff may, on cause shown, modify, by addition, deletion or substitution, the undertaking mentioned in rule 3.1.3.
