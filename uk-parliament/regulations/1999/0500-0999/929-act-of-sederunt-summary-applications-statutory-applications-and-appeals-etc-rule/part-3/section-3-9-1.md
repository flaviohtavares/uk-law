# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.9.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.9.1. Interpretation and application[^c-39c688aed2b472663ceda4c52ea19a24]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part–

- “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00046]; and
- “**administrator**” means the person appointed under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 1 to the Act.

(2) This Part applies to proceedings under sections 28, 29, 30, 31 and 33 of, and paragraphs 1, 2, 4, 6 and 12 of Schedule 1 to, the Act.
