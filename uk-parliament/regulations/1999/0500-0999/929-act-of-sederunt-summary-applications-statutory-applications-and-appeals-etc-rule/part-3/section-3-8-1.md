# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.8.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.8.1. Interpretation and application[^key-5b4ade1d30c14961ed7a90bffbc25800]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part, “the Act” means the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00038].

(2) This Part applies to–

- (a) applications for admission submitted to a sheriff under section 21 of the Act[^f00039];
- (b) guardianship applications submitted to a sheriff under section 40 of the Act[^f00040]; and
- (c) community care applications submitted under section 35A of the Act[^f00041].
