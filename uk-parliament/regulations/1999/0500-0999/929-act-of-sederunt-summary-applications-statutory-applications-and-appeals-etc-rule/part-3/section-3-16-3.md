# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.16.3

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.16.3. Place, and privacy, of any hearing[^key-1007531e8f7df0351fd9832f72ba3788][^key-9d9cf3b73afc5cb443a087246935751a]

*Extent: S*

The sheriff may, where he considers it appropriate in all the circumstances, appoint that the hearing of an application or other proceedings shall take place–

- (a) in a hospital, or any other place than the court building;
- (b) in private.
