# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.48.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.48.1. Interpretation[^key-01b29d68321361bf5fb333fcecb790b9]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “the 2015 Act” means the Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Scotland) Act 2015;
- “trafficking and exploitation prevention order” means an order made under section 18 of the 2015 Act. and[^key-3083f761634112b07990b699d23e6efd]
- “trafficking and exploitation risk order” means an order made under section 26 of the 2015 Act.
