# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.21.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.21.1. Interpretation[^c-ee0f7080e7951d56d8ad4e3e3809c1ae]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the Act” means the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999; and
- “an appeal” means an appeal to the sheriff under section 35A(1) or section 40B(1) of the Act.
