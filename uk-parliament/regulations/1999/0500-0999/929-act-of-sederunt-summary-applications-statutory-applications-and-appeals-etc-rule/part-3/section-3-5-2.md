# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.5.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.5.2. Orders for delivery up, forfeiture, destruction or other disposal[^key-8fa3e25d96399c84272d3ded659b945c]

*Extent: S*

An application to the sheriff made under sections 99, 114, 195, 204, 230, 231 or 298 of the 1988 Act[^f00034], under sections 16 or 19 of the 1994 Act or under Regulation 3 or 5 of the 1995 Regulations, shall be made–

- (a) by motion or incidental application, as the case may be, where proceedings have been commenced; or
- (b) by summary application where no proceedings have been commenced.
