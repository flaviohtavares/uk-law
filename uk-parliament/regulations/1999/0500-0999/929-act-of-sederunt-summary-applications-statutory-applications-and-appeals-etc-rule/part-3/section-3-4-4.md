# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.4.4

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

**3.4.4.**[^key-860a02382f1f8e92ffdd49619cebf833] On an application being submitted under rule 3.4.3, the sheriff must—

- (a) fix a hearing;
- (b) appoint service and intimation of the initial writ and Form 11C.
