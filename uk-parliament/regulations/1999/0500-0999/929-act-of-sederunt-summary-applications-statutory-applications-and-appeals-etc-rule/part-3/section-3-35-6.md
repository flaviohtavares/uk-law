# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.35.6

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.35.6. Certificate of delivery of documents[^key-cf33d0e2a05e24828510d94ab6800104]

*Extent: S*

Where a person is in any circumstances required to comply with section 26(2), 27(1) or 27(2)[^key-f993ec582e6a8526a8a4466c42d2e302] of the Act he shall, after such compliance, lodge in process a certificate of delivery in Form 34.
