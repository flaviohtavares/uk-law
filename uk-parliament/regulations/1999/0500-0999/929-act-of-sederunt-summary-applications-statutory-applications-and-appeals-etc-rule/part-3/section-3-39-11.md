# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.39.11

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.39.11. Intimation of orders on the person to whom they apply[^key-57e17c302403de12e9badf5f1cb6d28b]

*Extent: S*

Where a sheriff, in the absence of the person to whom it applies, grants—

- (a) a quarantine order under section 40(1) of the Act;
- (b) a short term detention order under section 42(1) of the Act;
- (c) an exceptional detention order under section 45 of the Act,

and the order is intimated to the person to whom it applies, a copy of Form 52 shall be delivered to that person along with the order.
