# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.8.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.8.2. Appointment of hearing[^c-83d07d471ae3696e83f03ee154acdd5a]

*Extent: S*

(1) On an application being submitted, the sheriff shall appoint a hearing subject, in the case of an application for admission, to section 21(3A) of the Act[^f00042].

(2) The sheriff may, where he considers it appropriate in all the circumstances, appoint that the hearing of an application shall take place in a hospital or other place.
