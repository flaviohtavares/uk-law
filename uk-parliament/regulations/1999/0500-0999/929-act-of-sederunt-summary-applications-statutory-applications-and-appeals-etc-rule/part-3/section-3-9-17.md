# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.9.17

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.9.17. Appeals against determination of outlays and remuneration[^key-c593efaebd0fecfb3d2c4054de92d5f0]

*Extent: S*

An appeal to the sheriff under paragraph 6(2) of Schedule 1 to the Act (appeal against a determination by the Accountant of Court) shall be made by note in the process of the application in which the administrator was appointed.
