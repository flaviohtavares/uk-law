# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.44.3

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

**3.44.3.**[^key-c77e4e6e2324ee0d25f2d1f47f8110d6] When a full gender recognition certificate has been issued on an application under section 4E of the 2004 Act, an application for a corrected gender recognition certificate under section 6 of the 2004 Act (Errors in certificates) shall be made by minute in the process in the application pursuant to which the full gender recognition certificate was issued.
