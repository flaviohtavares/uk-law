# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.41.1A

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.41.1A. Application for an order[^c-c51f3846380a0bfba68cfa27421de6c8][^c-5e873d681686b174c9286dbf0f22f8ec]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party to the proceedings may apply to the court for an order under this Part to restrict the reporting of the proceedings.

(2) An application for an order under this Part must be made by motion and be accompanied by Form 58A.
