# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.12.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.12.1. Interpretation[^c-409118e6071723e679dd4efa892c8927]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, “**the Model Law**” means the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law Model Law on International Commercial Arbitration as set out in Schedule 7 to the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00052].
