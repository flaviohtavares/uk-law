# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.35.5

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.35.5. Notification to adult at risk etc.[^c-4c012f4c669c4a77fba73b7a0f8274b4][^c-cf33d0e2a05e24828510d94ab6800104]

*Extent: S*

Where section 26(1)(b) of the Act (notification to the adult at risk etc. on the variation or recall of a banning order or temporary banning order) applies, the person prescribed for the purposes of section 26(2) is the sheriff clerk.
