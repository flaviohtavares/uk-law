# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.5.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.5.1. Interpretation[^c-072742f5206fad57171ef5fe25728947]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the 1988 Act” means the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00031];
- “the 1994 Act” means the Trade Marks Act 1994[^f00032]; and
- “the 1995 Regulations” means the Olympics Association Right (Infringement Proceedings) Regulations 1995[^f00033].
