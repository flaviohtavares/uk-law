# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.19.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.19.1. Interpretation and application[^c-c6edaaaa829a82df73a312bf3064eb61][^c-11dda865ee3ea5bd922d881e17908272]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part–

- “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002;
- “the 2005 Order” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005;[^c-b8c5fed6d136f980ba83416e5da340e0]
- “external order” has the meaning set out in section 447(2) of the Act;
- references to an administrator are to an administrator appointed under section 125(1) or 128(3);
- a reference to a specified section is a reference to the section bearing that number in the Act; and any reference to a specified paragraph in a specified Schedule is a reference to the paragraph bearing that number in the Schedule of that number in the Act.

(2)[^c-cd7a8e8ad6c648b8b69720b2d08a5216] This Part applies to applications to the sheriff under Parts 3, [^c-d334cdbfbd902eda147a3ccb8d2b456f]... 5 and 8 of the Act; but it only applies to applications under Part 8 in relation to property that is the subject of a—

- (a) civil recovery investigation;
- (b) detained cash investigation;
- (c) detained property investigation;
- (d) frozen funds investigation;
- (e)[^c-7e1a5df66a778a7c091957820eca4ff9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-9d2747a5f0a39ecdf39e5c0684cde49e] This Part applies to applications to the sheriff under Parts 5A and 5B of the 2005 Order.
