## 3.35.1. Interpretation[^key-cf33d0e2a05e24828510d94ab6800104]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the Act” means the Adult Support and Protection (Scotland) Act 2007;
- “the adult at risk” has the same meaning as in section 3 of the Act.
