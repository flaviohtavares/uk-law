# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.51.1

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.51.1. Interpretation[^c-2dfd10a6725564d64ab53d0d2ba742df]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “the 2015 Act” means the Serious Crime Act 2015;
- “DDTRO Regulations” means the Drug Dealing Telecommunications Restriction Orders Regulations 2017; and

unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions used in this Part and in the 2015 Act or the DDTRO Regulations have the meaning given by the 2015 Act or the DDTRO Regulations, as the case may be.
