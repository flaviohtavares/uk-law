# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.41.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.41.1. Interpretation and application of this Part[^c-5e873d681686b174c9286dbf0f22f8ec]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Part applies to orders which restrict the reporting of proceedings.

(2) In this Part, “interested person” means a person—

- (a) who has asked to see any order made by the sheriff which restricts the reporting of proceedings, including an interim order; and
- (b) whose name is included on a list kept by the Lord President for the purposes of this Part.
