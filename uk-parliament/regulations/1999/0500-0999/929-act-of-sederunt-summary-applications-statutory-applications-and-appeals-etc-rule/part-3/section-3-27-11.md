# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.27.11

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.27.11. Applications by summary application[^key-c5f274493532a530c78070f0011164cb]

*Extent: S*

An application under section 35 (Reimbursement of expenditure), 63 (Appeal against graffiti removal notice) or 64 (Appeal against notice under section 61(4)) shall be by summary application.

**3.27.12.** An application under section 71 (Failure to comply with notice: order as to rental income), 74 (Failure to comply with notice: management control order) or 97 (Appeals against notice under section 94) shall be by summary application.
