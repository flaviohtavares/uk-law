# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.10.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.10.1. Interpretation and application[^c-46db480d3e9c5eeb0a44f43c22de8441]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part, “the Act” means the Rating (Disabled Persons) Act 1978[^f00048].

(2) This Part applies to appeals under section 6(5) or 6(5A) of the Act[^f00049].
