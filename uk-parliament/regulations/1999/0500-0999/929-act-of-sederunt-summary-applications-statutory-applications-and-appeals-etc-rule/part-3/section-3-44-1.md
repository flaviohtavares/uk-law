# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.44.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

**3.44.1.**[^c-c77e4e6e2324ee0d25f2d1f47f8110d6] In this Part,—

- “the 2004 Act” means the Gender Recognition Act 2004;
- “full gender recognition certificate” and “interim gender recognition certificate” have the meanings assigned by section 25 of the 2004 Act;
- “Gender Recognition Panels” is to be construed in accordance with Schedule 1 to the 2004 Act.
