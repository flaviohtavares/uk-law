# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.42.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.42.1. Interpretation[^key-4dea6755b010b07abef86cb798ced6e0]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “the 2000 Act” means the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000;

  and words and expressions used in this Part and in the 2000 Act shall have the same meaning given in the 2000 Act.
