# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.36.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.36.1. Interpretation[^c-6754ba6f6020358f1c36dfbf3c1ba0c1]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the Act” means the UK Borders Act 2007; and
- “an appeal” means an appeal to the sheriff under section 11(1) of the Act.
