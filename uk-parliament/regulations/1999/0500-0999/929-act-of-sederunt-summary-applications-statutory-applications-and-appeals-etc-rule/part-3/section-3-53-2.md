# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.53.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.53.2. Form of application[^c-53ac967347e3e86a47bec466f58b049e]

*Extent: S*

An application under—

- (a) section 34 of the 2019 Act must be made in Form 76;
- (b) section 42 of the 2019 Act must be made in Form 77;
- (c) section 61 of the 2019 Act must be made in Form 78.
