# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.32.3

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.32.3. Interim orders pending appeal[^c-fa447fd09f0a7fc734f80396883d76d7]

*Extent: S*

An application for an interim order under–

- (a) section 28H(2) of the 1981 Act; or
- (b) section 43(5) of the 2006 Act, or[^c-1d4c46233adae4f320247fc3efbe7023]
- (c) section 49(5) of the Animal Welfare Act 2006,

where a seizure order is suspended or inexecutable shall be made by motion.
