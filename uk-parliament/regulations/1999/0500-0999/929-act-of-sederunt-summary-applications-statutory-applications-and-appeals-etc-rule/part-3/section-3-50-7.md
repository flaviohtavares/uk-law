# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.50.7

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.50.7. Procedure where no notice of opposition is lodged[^key-33842adc0f1b35f06eee5399a11e4374][^key-602f27fb984c6fb935f1ec3ddf9e667c]

*Extent: S*

Where no notice of opposition has been lodged with the court within the period of notice—

- (a) the pursuer may return the application, together with a completed certificate of service, to the sheriff clerk requesting that the sheriff make an order granting the application in absence; and
- (b) the sheriff may, thereafter—
  - (i) determine the application without a hearing; and
  - (ii) grant decree in absence.
