# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.19.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.19.2. Applications for extended detention of cash or property[^key-11dda865ee3ea5bd922d881e17908272][^key-62f9bdf6854fb8caa617f7872412eacc]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application to the sheriff for an order under sections 295(2) and (7) (extended detention of seized cash) or 303L(1)(b) (further detention of seized property)[^key-8d9c66e44dd6a87ea31a1d71f313d918] shall be made by summary application.

(2) An application for any further order for the detention of cash under section 295(2) or seized property under section 303L(1)(b)[^key-43f6c70229ba90adc41581bb71e8c7bb] shall be made by minute in the process of the original application for extended detention of seized cash or seizure of property[^key-3b8762386fcfeb4c8c433b6832f161ea] and shall be proceeded with in accordance with sub-paragraph (3) below.

(3) On the lodging of an application for any further order the sheriff shall–

- (a) fix a date for determination of the application; and
- (b) order service of the application together with notice of such date for determination on any persons whom he considers may be affected.
