# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.4.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

**3.4.2.**[^c-860a02382f1f8e92ffdd49619cebf833] (1) This rule applies to an application or counter-application made by virtue of paragraph (3)(2)(b) of the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Rules) (Enforcement of Securities over Heritable Property) 2010.

(2) An interlocutor of the sheriff disposing of an application or counter-application is final and not subject to appeal except as to a question of title or as to any other remedy granted.
