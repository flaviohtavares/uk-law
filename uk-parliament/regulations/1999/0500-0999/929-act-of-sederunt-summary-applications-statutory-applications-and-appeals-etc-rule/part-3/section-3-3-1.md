# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.3.1

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.3.1. Interpretation and application[^c-96d9e7ebc129f81512bbc3d5361a71bd]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part–

- “the Act” means the Coal Mining Subsidence Act 1991[^f00027];
- “**agreement or consent**” means the agreement or consent referred to in section 41 of the Act[^f00028];
- “**person**” means a person referred to in section 41 of the Act;
- “**any person with responsibility for subsidence affecting any land**” has the meaning given in section 43 of the Coal Industry Act 1994[^f00029].

(2) This Part applies to proceedings under section 41 of the Act.
