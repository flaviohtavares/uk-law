# Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999, section 3.41.2

> Text as at 2025-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.41.2. Interim orders[^c-5e873d681686b174c9286dbf0f22f8ec][^c-d364ea5df794ae6e0c6055c8edf8e29f]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the sheriff is considering making an order, the sheriff must first[^c-3020cf6582b71767c775e439144a4c44] make an interim order.

(2) [^c-e510fdd58181085d1d027d60c08135c5]... The sheriff clerk shall immediately send a copy of the interim order to any interested person.

(3) The sheriff shall specify in the interim order why the sheriff is considering making an order.
