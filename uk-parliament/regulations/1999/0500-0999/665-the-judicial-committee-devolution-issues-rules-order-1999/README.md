(This Note is not part of the Order)

The rules set out in the Schedule to this Order regulate the procedure to be followed and the fees to be taken in the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council in respect of proceedings for the determination of devolution issues under—

- (a) the Scotland Act 1998, section 33 and Schedule 6;
- (b) the Government of Wales Act 1998 Schedule 8; and
- (c) the Northern Ireland Act 1998, section 11 and Schedule 10.
