# The Scottish Parliament (Regional Returning Officers) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1999/270](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/270) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12(6) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and revocation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Scottish Parliament (Regional Returning Officers) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 10th March 1999.

(2) In this Order–

- “local authority” means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00002]; and
- “local government area” means the area of a local authority.

(3) The Scottish Parliament (Regional Returning Officers) (Scotland) Order 1999[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## 2. Regional returning officers

For the purposes of the Scotland Act 1998, the person designated as the regional returning officer for a region specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order[^f00004] shall be the person who, under section 41 of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00005], is the returning officer at elections of councillors for the local authority for the local government area specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that Schedule.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 12(6) of the Scotland Act 1998. It designates persons to be the regional returning officers for each region of the Scottish Parliament. The regional returning officer for each region will perform functions, conferred by or under the 1998 Act, in relation to the election and return of regional members of the Scottish Parliament. The Order revokes a previous Order made under section 12(6) of the 1998 Act which owing to a clerical mistake omitted a designation for the region of the Lothians.
