# The Education (School Government) (Wales) Regulations 1999, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application

Regulations 6 to 13 apply to any maintained school conducted by an incorporated governing body constituted under an instrument of government (but not including a maintained school with a transitional governing body partly constituted under an instrument in accordance with regulatioin 18(2) of the First Transitional Regulations)[^f00012].

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: This Part should be read together with Schedules 9 and 12 to the 1998 Act. The constitution or reconstitution of the governing body under the first instrument of government made on transition for the new schools framework is dealt with in the First Transitional Regulations, or the Second Transitional Regulations.
