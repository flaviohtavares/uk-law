# The Aeroplane Noise (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Aeroplane Noise (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

Coming into force

16th August 1999

Whereas a draft of these Regulations has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to air transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Aeroplane Noise (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on the seventh day after the day on which they are made.

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the Aeroplane Noise Regulations 1999. They make provision to implement the Community obligations of the United Kingdom contained in Commission Directive [1999/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0028) of 21 April 1999[^f00004]. This Directive amends the Annex to Council Directive [92/14/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0014) of 2 March 1992 on the limitation of the operation of aeroplanes covered by Part II, Chapter 2, Volume 1 of Annex 16 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation, second edition (1988) as amended by Council Directive [98/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0020) of 30 March 1998[^f00005].

The Schedule to the Aeroplane Noise Regulations 1999 lists certain aeroplanes of developing countries which are excepted from the requirement to be certified to the standards specified in Chapter 3 of the 1988 edition of Volume 1 of Annex 16.

These Regulations remove aeroplanes from that Schedule which have been destroyed or removed from the register of the relevant developing nation.
