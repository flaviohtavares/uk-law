# The Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1999/1925](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1925) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

1999 No. 2402

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

1st September 1999

Laid before Parliament

2nd September 1999

Coming into force

23rd September 1999

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to rights and obligations relating to employers and employees on the transfer or merger of undertakings, businesses or parts of businesses[^f00002], in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that provision hereby makes the following Regulations—

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) (Amendment) Regulations 1999.

(2) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement

These Regulations shall come into force on 23rd September 1999 but shall not apply to transfers of undertakings completed before 1st November 1999.

## 3. Amendment of Regulations

In Regulation 10 (2A)(b)(i) of the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 1981[^f00003] for “the proposed dismissals” substitute “the transfer”.

Alan Johnson,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competitiveness,

Department of Trade and Industry

1st September 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations are made to correct a defect in regulation 8 of the Collective Redundancies and Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 which amended regulation 10(2A) of the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 1981.
