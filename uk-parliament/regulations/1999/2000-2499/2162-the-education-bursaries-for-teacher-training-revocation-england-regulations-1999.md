# The Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2162

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 1999

Made

27th July 1999

Laid before Parliament

30th July 1999

Coming into force

1st September 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 50 and 63(3) of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st September 1999.

**2.** The Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) Regulations 1994[^f00002] and the Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) (Amendment) Regulations 1995[^f00003] are hereby revoked in relation to England.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

27th July 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) Regulations 1994 (S.I. [1994/2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2016)) and the Education (Bursaries for Teacher Training) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/603](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/603)) in relation to England.
