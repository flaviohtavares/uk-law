# The Education (School Organisation Proposals) (England) Regulations 1999, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Revocation

The Education (Publication of Proposals for Reduction in Standard Numbers) Regulations 1991[^f00010] and the Education (Publication of School Proposals and Notices) Regulations 1993[^f00011] (insofar as they continue to have effect, despite the repeal of the provisions containing the powers under which they were made, by virtue of the re-enactment of those powers with modifications) are hereby revoked in relation to England.
