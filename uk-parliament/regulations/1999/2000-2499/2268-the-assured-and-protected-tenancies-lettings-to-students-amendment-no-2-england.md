# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) (England) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2268

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND

The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) (England) Regulations 1999

Made

5th August 1999

Laid before Parliament

11th August 1999

Coming into force

1st September 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) (England) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st September 1999.

**2.** Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended in relation to England by the insertion at the appropriate place of “Willowbrook Properties Ltd”.

Malcolm Wicks

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

5th August 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998 to specify Willowbrook Properties Ltd for the purpose of paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988. The effect of specification is that a tenancy granted by the body to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1998 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.
