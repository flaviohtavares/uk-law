# The Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2176

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

29th July 1999

Coming into force

9th August 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 9th August 1999.

(2) In this Order, “the Principal Order” means the Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00005].

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

(1) For article 3 of the Principal Order there shall be substituted the following article:–

> **3. Nature and functions of the Trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be–
>
> - (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act hospital accommodation and services provided at Hope Hospital, Stott Lane, Salford M6 8HD and associated hospitals (including the management of its teaching and research facilities); and
> - (b) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act a neurological rehabilitation unit in the area of Salford and Trafford Health Authority, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tessa Jowell

Minister of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

29th July 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage a neurological rehabilitation unit in the area of Salford and Trafford Health Authority.
