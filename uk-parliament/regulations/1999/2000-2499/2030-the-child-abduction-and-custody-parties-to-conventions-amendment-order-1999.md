# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2030

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

21st July 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 2 and 13 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1999.

**2.** The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00002] is revoked.

**3.** The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00003] is amended by deleting Schedules 1 and 2 and substituting therefore Schedules 1 and 2 which are set out in the Schedule to this Order.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It adds Belgium and, in respect of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, China, to the list of Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at The Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm. 33).
