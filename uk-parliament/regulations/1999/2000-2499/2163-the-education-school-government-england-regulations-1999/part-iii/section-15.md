# The Education (School Government) (England) Regulations 1999, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Qualifications and disqualifications

Subject to Part VIII (transitional provisions), Schedule 5 sets out the circumstances in which a person is qualified or disqualified for holding or continuing in office, or for being elected, appointed or nominated, as a governor of a school.
