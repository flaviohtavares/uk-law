# The Education (School Government) (England) Regulations 1999, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Interpretation

In this Part, except where the context otherwise requires, any reference to a school is a reference to a maintained school or a new school.
