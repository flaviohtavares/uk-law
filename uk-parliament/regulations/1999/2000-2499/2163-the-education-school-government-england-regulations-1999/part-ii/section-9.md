# The Education (School Government) (England) Regulations 1999, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Appointment of parent and partnership governors

(1) Schedule 2 sets out the circumstances in which parent governors required by the instrument of government shall be appointed by the governing body, and the requirements which must be complied with in relation to any such appointment[^f00010].

(2) Schedule 3 provides for the nomination and appointment of partnership governors required by the instrument of government.

## Annotations

[^f00010]: Footnote: Generally parent governors are elected. *See* paragraph 4 of Schedule 9 to the 1998 Act.
