# The Ministerial and other Salaries Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2037

PARLIAMENT

The Ministerial and other Salaries Order 1999

Made

21st July 1999

Coming into force

22nd July 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1B(1) and (3)(b) of the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) This Order may be cited as the Ministerial and other Salaries Order 1999.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) The changes in salary and maximum salary provided for in articles 2 and 3 of this Order shall apply in respect of any period of a year beginning on or after 1st April 1999.

## 2. Increase in certain Ministerial salaries

(1) In Part II of Schedule 1 to the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (“the 1975 Act”)–

- (a) in paragraph 1 for “(a) to (g)” there shall be substituted “(a) to (f)”, and
- (b) after paragraph 3 there shall be added–

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | **4.** Minister of State for so long as the holder is not a member of the Cabinet: | Maximum 64,426<br>Minimum 31,125 |

  .

(2) For the annual amount of salary specified in Part IV of Schedule 1 to the 1975 Act in relation to each of the offices specified below there shall be substituted the amount specified below in relation to that office–

- (a) Captain of the Honourable Corps of Gentlemen-at-Arms, £64,426,
- (b) Parliamentary Secretary other than Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasury, £55,631,
- (c) Captain of the Queen’s Bodyguard of the Yeoman of the Guard, £55,631 and
- (d) Lord in Waiting, £51,161.

## 3. Increase in salaries of Opposition Leader and Whip

For the annual amount of salary specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1975 Act in relation to each of the positions specified below there shall be substituted the amount specified below in relation to that position–

- (a) Leader of the Opposition in the House of Lords, £55,631, and
- (b) Chief Opposition Whip in the House of Lords, £51,161.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases salaries payable under the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 to certain Ministerial office holders and to certain Members of the Opposition. The amounts specified in this Order (other than the minimum salary for certain Ministers of State) are the maximum salaries payable. The actual salaries in payment may therefore be less.
