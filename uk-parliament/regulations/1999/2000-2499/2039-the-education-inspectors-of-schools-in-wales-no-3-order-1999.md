# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) (No. 3) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2039

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) (No. 3) Order 1999

Made

21st July 1999

Laid before Parliament

30th July 1999

Coming into force

1st September 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1999

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) (No. 3) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st September 1999.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales with effect from 1st September 1999.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Mrs Wendy Mary Young
- Mrs Pauline Claire Yardley
- Mrs Sylvia Eileen Lindoe

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales.
