# The Funding Agency for Schools Dissolution Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2767

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Funding Agency for Schools Dissolution Order 1999

Made

6th October 1999

Laid before Parliament

8th October 1999

Coming into force

1st November 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 132(1) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Funding Agency for Schools Dissolution Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st November 1999.

**2.** The Secretary of State hereby specifies 1st November 1999 as the date on which the Funding Agency for Schools shall be dissolved.

Jacqui Smith

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

6th October 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 1st November 1999 as the date on which the Funding Agency for Schools is to be dissolved under section 132 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998.
