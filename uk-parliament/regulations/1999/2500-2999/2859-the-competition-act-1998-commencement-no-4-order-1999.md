# The Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2859 (C.74)

COMPETITION

The Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1999

Made

19th October 1999

Coming into force

10th November 1999

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 76 of the Competition Act 1998[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1999.

## 2. Provisions of the Act commenced by this Order

10th November 1999 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Competition Act 1998:

- (a) in Schedule 12 (minor and consequential amendments) paragraphs 1(3)(a), (b) and (d);
- (b) in Schedule 13 (transitional provisions and savings) paragraphs 40 and 41; and
- (c) in Part I of Schedule 14 (repeals and revocations) the entries relating to sections 10(2) and 54(5) of, and paragraphs 3(1) and (2) of Schedule 8 to, the Fair Trading Act 1973[^f00002].

Stephen Byers

Secretary of State for Trade and Industry

19th October 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 10th November 1999 the repeal of sections 10(2) and 54(5) of the Fair Trading Act 1973 which provide that, on a monopoly reference to the Competition Commission, the Commission may not take any account of any provisions of an agreement in so far as they are provisions by virtue of which it is an agreement to which the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976 ([1976 c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/33) applies, and may not find that such provisions operate or may be expected to operate against the public interest. It brings into force the repeal of paragraphs 3(1) and (2) of Schedule 8 to the Fair Trading Act 1973. These subparagraphs preclude any order made by the Secretary of State which makes agreements, or their carrying out, unlawful from extending to agreements to which the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976 applies. Finally it brings into force paragraphs 40 and 41 of Schedule 13 which make transitional provisions in respect of the repeals commenced by this Order.
