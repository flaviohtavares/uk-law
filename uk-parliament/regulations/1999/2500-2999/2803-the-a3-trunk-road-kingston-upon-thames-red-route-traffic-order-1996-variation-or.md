# The A3 Trunk Road (Kingston Upon Thames) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2803

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A3 Trunk Road (Kingston Upon Thames) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1999

Made

8th October 1999

Coming into force

18th October 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A3 Trunk Road (Kingston Upon Thames) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 18th October 1999.

## 2. Variations

The A3 Trunk Road (Kingston Upon Thames) Red Route Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

**3.** In Schedule 1, insert the following item after item 3:—
