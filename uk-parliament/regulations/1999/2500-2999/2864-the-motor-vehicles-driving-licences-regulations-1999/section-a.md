# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section a

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**a)** No licence may be issued for sub-category B1 (invalid carriages) to a person who did not hold one on 12th November 1999—although existing licences remain valid—and driving tests for that sub-category are discontinued with effect from the same date*(regulation 5(3) and Schedules 2 and 5)*.
