# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 41

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Nature of extended driving tests[^key-9b7f5a4a1e09e0ef9ad4eff17789c3fc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person is disqualified by order of a court under section 36 of the Offenders Act until he passes an extended driving test, the test which he must pass is a test conducted in accordance with regulations 40 to 40C[^key-bf9c64392d54a50248eb20e13b785297] as modified by virtue of paragraph (2) of this regulation.

(2)[^key-90024b4eca5c74a0e8e53266aa536cdd] For the purposes of an extended driving test, the provisions of regulations 40 to 40C shall apply as if regulation 40(1) were omitted and as if for sub-paragraph (c) of regulation 40B(5) there were substituted—

> - (c) the time during which that person is required to drive on roads is not less than 60 minutes.

.
