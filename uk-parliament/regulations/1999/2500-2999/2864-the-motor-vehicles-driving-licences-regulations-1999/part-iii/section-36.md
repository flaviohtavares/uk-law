# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 36

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Cancellation of tests

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of paragraph (b) of section 91 of the Traffic Act (which section specifies the cases in which a fee paid on an application for an appointment for a test may be repaid) notice cancelling an appointment—

- (a) subject to paragraph (ac),[^key-1eebb92c0cfa79ade8603218b00014d8] for a manoeuvres test,[^key-ee123e016b3d3621f6f20a6dc6531f99] large vehicle off road manoeuvres test,[^key-ed98de78c81f11fa3a434edf3d091d74] practical test or unitary test to be conducted by a DVSA[^key-475711a5a171bd47ba5ddeb0a2f4e400] examiner must be given to the Secretary of State not less than three[^key-adfb3c24c917dd85c382ee0ae3aeb9ac] clear working days before the day for which the appointment is made;
- (ab)[^key-1411e93f6ea3b4a1fff16bfda92c7262] for a large vehicle off road manoeuvres test to be conducted by a large vehicle off road manoeuvres test examiner must be given to the large vehicle off road manoeuvres test provider not less than three clear working days before the day for which the appointment is made;
- (ac)[^key-a3c9b89fcd23de1b71301a2b4e4f32f5] for a practical test for a licence authorising the driving of a vehicle of a class included in category B to be conducted by a DVSA examiner must be given to the Secretary of State not less than ten clear working days before the day for which the appointment is made;
- (b) for a theory test to be conducted by an appointed person must be given not less than three clear working days before the day for which the appointment is made.
