# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 25

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Revocation of authority to conduct tests

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may revoke—

- (a) an appointment made under regulation 23(1)(a) or (2)(a) or under regulation 24(1)(a) , (1ZA)[^key-837654721217560bb832030e0652c681] or (2)(a), or
- (b) an approval given under regulation 23 (1)(de)(ii), (1)(df)(ii),[^key-72c13cb53dba1bb0f0314ca71e9918fd] (3) or (8) or under regulation 24 (1)(ce)(ii), (1)(cf)(ii)[^key-aada3213336c5ff878f347d3d9cc365a] , (1ZA)(a), (3)[^key-acf52b8d52478372cb65a6ac447e36dd] (3) or (5),

by notice in writing and the authority of the person whose appointment is revoked or whose approval is withdrawn to conduct theory tests or large vehicle off road manoeuvres tests[^key-fc86aae2fc9c638a04972f817f85e71e] or, as the case may be, to appoint other persons to conduct unitary tests, manoeuvres tests,[^key-19aa6ffeab2046c3350013c6f5c9a5b3] large vehicle off road manoeuvres tests,[^key-6efbfe1000ec279e418dbf29915b0ca6] practical tests or theory tests, shall cease upon the date specified in the notice.

(2) Where a person has his appointment revoked or if an approval given in respect of him under regulation 23(1)(de)(ii), (1)(df)(ii), (3), 24(1)(ce)(ii), (1)(cf)(ii)[^key-dc90ebf24d14c99007f83a7b857654a7] ,[^key-0dad988a6e2e892c174406e905a03e03] (1ZA)[^key-b475ba60f02d518d3d65cd3e2eb59a3b] or (3) is withdrawn, that person shall immediately return to the Secretary of State all forms of pass certificates supplied to him under regulations 47(8) and 48(3) which he still holds.
