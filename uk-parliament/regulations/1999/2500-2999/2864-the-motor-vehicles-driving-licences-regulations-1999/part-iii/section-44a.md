# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 44A

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44A. Entitlement upon passing a test other than an appropriate driving test: category AM[^c-e2b6325c0b58e1fa2a18e1503dbc14fe][^c-4b3ee851a9f620422d6587023361070d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies where a person has passed a test (or Northern Ireland test of competence corresponding to such a test) for a licence authorising the driving of vehicles included in category AM[^c-4cac91098fa3ef012ac5e7b284437aac].

(2) Where this regulation applies the Secretary of State shall grant to the person who passed the test—

- (a)[^c-540177a8e20e4ea6b559d456c0621900] in a case where the test was passed on a three-wheeled moped or a light quadricycle, a licence authorising the driving of all vehicles having three or four wheels included in category AM;
- (b) in any other case, a licence authorising the driving of all vehicles included in category AM[^c-28f164467321575f078f0c4ed4d73772].
