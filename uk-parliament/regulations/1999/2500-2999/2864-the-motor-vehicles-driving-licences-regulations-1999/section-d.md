# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section d

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**d)** The option for a theory test candidate to pay a lower test fee in return for a delay in notification of the result will not be available from 4th January 2000 but from that date all tests must be marked and the results provided on the day of the test. The current lower fee (£15.50) will from that date be payable in the case of every theory test (*regulations 26(1), 27(1), 28(1), 30(1) and 47(1) and (2)*).
