# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section e

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**e)** Persons holding a full licence authorising the driving of vehicles in sub-category B1 (invalid carriages) are exempted from the fee when taking a test for a licence authorising the driving of vehicles in category B (*regulation 35(5)*).
