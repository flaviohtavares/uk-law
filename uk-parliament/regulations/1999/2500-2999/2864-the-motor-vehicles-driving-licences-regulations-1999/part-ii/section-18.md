# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 18

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Conditions attached to provisional licences: holders of driving permits other than licences granted under Part III of the Traffic Act

*Extent: E+W+S*

A holder of a provisional licence authorising the driving of vehicles of any class who also holds a permit by virtue of which he is at any time—

- (a) treated, by virtue of regulation 80, as the holder, for the purposes of section 87 of the Traffic Act, of a licence authorising the driving of vehicles of that class, or
- (b) entitled, pursuant to article 2(1) of the Motor Vehicles (International Circulation) Order 1975 [^c16598651], to drive motor vehicles of that class,

need not comply with regulation 16 at that time.
