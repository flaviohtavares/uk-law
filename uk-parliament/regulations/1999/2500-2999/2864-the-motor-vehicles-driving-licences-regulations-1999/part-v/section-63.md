# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 63

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Cessation of conduct of training

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a certified instructor authorised by an approved training body under regulation 60(2) [^c-41bb973b53d8334b8508dc18854ad446]... ceases to conduct instruction on behalf of the body who authorised him, he shall as soon as is reasonably practicable return the certificate issued under regulation 60(4)(c) [^c-fe865bda71092f2f47358651555a9dee]... and all forms of certificates which were supplied to him under regulation 68(3) to the approved training body who must, on receiving the certificate issued under regulation 60(4)(c) [^c-fe865bda71092f2f47358651555a9dee]..., return it to the Secretary of State.

(2) Where an approved training body who is also entitled under regulation 60(6) [^c-111c3a8f02e3ce0ba0b31310180d74ca]... to conduct instruction as a certified instructor ceases to conduct such instruction, he shall immediately return the certificate issued under regulation 60(4)(c) [^c-21de9c7f7f71ecb8b22bd555ac36ef97]... and (unless the Secretary of State agrees otherwise) all forms of certificates which were supplied to him under regulation 68(3) to the Secretary of State.
