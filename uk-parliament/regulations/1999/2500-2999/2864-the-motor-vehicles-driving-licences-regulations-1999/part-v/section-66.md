# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Eligibility to undertake approved training course

*Extent: E+W+S*

No person shall be eligible to undertake an approved training course unless at the time he undertakes it he holds a provisional licence authorising him to drive a motor bicycle or moped of the class on which the course is to be undertaken or is entitled, by virtue of section 98 or 99A of the Traffic Act and regulation 19, to drive a motor bicycle of that class subject to the same conditions as the holder of a provisional licence.
