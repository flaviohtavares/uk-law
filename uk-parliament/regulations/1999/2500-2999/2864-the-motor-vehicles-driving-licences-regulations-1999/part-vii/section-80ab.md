# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 80AB

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80AB. Modification of regulation 80: Homes for Ukraine Sponsorship Scheme: third scenario[^key-b00143bb69a854e1f69a3f25cba1f110]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies in respect of a person who—

- (a) has or had Homes for Ukraine Sponsorship Scheme leave,
- (b) applied for that leave at or after 3:00 p.m. on 19th February 2024, [^key-0859eac2ae07ac321bc5864bc7d8196b]...
- (ba)[^key-13550464187564e677a634223a5e7e57] in respect of Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme leave—
  - (i) has not applied for that leave, or
  - (ii) has been granted that leave, but has not applied for an extension to that leave, and
- (c) is the holder of a relevant permit within the meaning given by regulation 80(6) which was issued under the law of Ukraine.

(2) Where this regulation applies, regulation 80(1) has effect as if the reference to the period of one year after a person becomes resident in Great Britain were to the period of three years beginning with the day after the day on which that person becomes resident in Great Britain.
