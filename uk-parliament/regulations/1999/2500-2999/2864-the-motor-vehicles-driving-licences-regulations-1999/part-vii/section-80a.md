# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 80A

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80A. Modification of regulation 80: Homes for Ukraine Sponsorship Scheme: first scenario[^c-d2146956c64b7e29cb2548b4485ead88][^c-7b3ac5239bd41cd8b5773640c43d9132]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies in respect of a person who—

- (a)[^c-51bcf93d3b8f0846c635d41a2b7a5313] has Homes For Ukraine Sponsorship Scheme leave
- (aza)[^c-ef6c3ba8e99dd5a91d38545ed895d405] applied for that leave before 3:00pm on 19th February 2024,
- (aa)[^c-1e5c3e4901510758a53c3ec3922b5fad] has not applied for Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme leave, and
- (b) is the holder of a relevant permit within the meaning given by regulation 80(6) which was issued under the law of Ukraine.

(2) Where this regulation applies, regulation 80(1) has effect as if the reference to the period of one year after a person becomes resident in Great Britain were to the period of three years beginning with the day after the day on which that person becomes resident in Great Britain[^c-c721d5b697328e4ece90c3ff16b70b77].
