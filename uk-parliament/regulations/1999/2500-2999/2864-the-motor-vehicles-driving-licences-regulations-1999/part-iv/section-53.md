# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section 53

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Part IV of the Traffic Act: dual purpose vehicles

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Except in the case of a vehicle mentioned in paragraph (2), Part IV of the Traffic Act and regulations 54 to 57 shall apply to dual purpose vehicles to the extent that they apply to passenger-carrying vehicles.

(2) Part IV of the Traffic Act and regulations 54 to 57 shall apply to any dual purpose vehicle which is—

- (a) driven by a member of the armed forces of the Crown, and
- (b) used to carry passengers for naval, military or air force purposes,

to the extent that they apply to large goods vehicles.
