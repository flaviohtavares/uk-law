# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1999, section b

> Text as at 2026-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**b)** Members of the armed forces are authorised to drive dual purpose vehicles according to the class of licence held and the maximum authorised mass of the vehicle (*regulation 8*).
