## SCHEDULE 15 — MODIFICATION OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (COMPUTATION OF EARNINGS) REGULATIONS 1996

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(15)

**1.**[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] Subject to the following provisions of this Schedule, as if for the words “the adjudicating authority" and “an adjudicating authority" in each place where they occur there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ the Secretary of State ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**2.** As if in regulation 2(1) (interpretation), the definition of “adjudicating authority" were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]

**3.** As if in regulation 4 (notional earnings)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]

- (a) in paragraph (1) for the words “of the determination of the claim or of any subsequent review the adjudicating authority shall treat the claimant" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ on which a decision falls to be made by the Secretary of State under Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998 or regulations made thereunder the claimant shall be treated ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]
- (b) in paragraph (2) for the words “the adjudicating authority shall treat the claimant" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ the claimant shall be treated ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**4.** As if in regulation 6(8) (calculation of earnings of employed earners) in paragraph (b)(ii)(bb) of the definition of “part-time employment", for the words “of review" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ on which a revision or supersession of a decision falls to be made ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**5.** As if in regulation 13(8) (calculation of net profit of self-employed earners), for the words “The adjudicating authority shall refuse to make a deduction" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ A decision shall not be made ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**6.** As if in regulation 14(2) (deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners) for the words “of the determination of the claim or of any subsequent review" in both places where they occur there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ on which a decision is made by the Secretary of State under Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998 or regulations made thereunder ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**7.** As if regulation 16 (transitional provision to suspend benefit and make interim payments) were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]
