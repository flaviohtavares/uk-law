# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — MODIFICATION OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (PAYMENTS ON ACCOUNT, OVERPAYMENTS AND RECOVERY) REGULATIONS 1988[^c-790a73c03ee53dda110d6699f53cbb54]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(4)

**1.**[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] As if in regulation 1(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] [^c1646242] (interpretation), for the definition of “adjudicating authority" there were substituted the following definition— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

> “adjudicating authority" means, as the case may require, the Secretary of State, an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter I of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998, the Chief or other Commissioner, or a tribunal consisting of any three or more Commissioners constituted in accordance with section 16(7) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

.

**2.** As if in regulation 2(1)(b) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] [^c1646243] (making of interim payments), for the words “a reference, review," there were substituted the word *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] “ an ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7].

**3.** As if in regulation 5(2) (offsetting prior payment against a subsequent award)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

- (a) for Case 1 there were substituted the following case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

  > **Case 1: Payment pursuant to a decision which is revised or superseded, or overturned on appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]**
  >
  > Where a person has been paid a sum by way of benefit pursuant to a decision which is subsequently revised under section 9 of the Social Security Act 1998, superseded by a decision under section 10 of that Act or overturned on appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

  ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]
- (b) in Case 2, the words “, on review or appeal," were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

**4.** As if in regulation 8(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] [^c1646244] (duplication and prescribed payments) for the words “on review" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] “ by way of revision or supersession ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7].

**5.** As if in the heading to Part VI the words “REVISION OF DETERMINATION AND" were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

**6.** As if in regulation 12 (circumstances in which determination need not be revised) for the words— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

- (a) “or revision of determination" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] “ , revision or supersession ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]
- (b) “for reviewing and revising the determination under which payment was made" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] “ for the decision pursuant to which the payment was made to be revised under section 9 of the Social Security Act 1998 or superseded under section 10 of that Act ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7].
