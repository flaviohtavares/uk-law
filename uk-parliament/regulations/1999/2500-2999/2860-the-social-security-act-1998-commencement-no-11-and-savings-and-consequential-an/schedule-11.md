# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 11

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 11 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (INCAPACITY FOR WORK) (GENERAL) REGULATIONS 1995

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(11)

**1.** In regulation 2(1) [^c1646263] (interpretation) the definition of “disability appeal tribunal" shall be omitted.

**2.** In regulation 17(1)(c) the words “a disability appeal tribunal or" shall be omitted, and after the words “the Disability Living Allowance Advisory Board" there shall be inserted the words “ or as a panel member with a disability qualification, as defined in regulation 1(3) of the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999, acting as a member of an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter I of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998 ”.

## Annotations

[^c1646263]: Marginal citation: There are amendments to regulation 2 which are not relevant to this Order.
