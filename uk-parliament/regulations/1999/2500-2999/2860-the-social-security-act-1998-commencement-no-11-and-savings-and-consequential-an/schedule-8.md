# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (ATTENDANCE ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS 1991

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(8)

**1.** In regulations 7A(2)(a) [^c1646255] in the substituted paragraph (1A)(b) and (c) and 8(4)(b) and (c) for the word “review" there shall be substituted the words “ revision under section 9 of the Social Security Act 1998 or supersession under section 10 of that Act ”.

**2.** Regulations 8C, 8D and 8E [^c1646256] (medical examination, withholding of benefit and payment of withheld benefit) are hereby revoked.
