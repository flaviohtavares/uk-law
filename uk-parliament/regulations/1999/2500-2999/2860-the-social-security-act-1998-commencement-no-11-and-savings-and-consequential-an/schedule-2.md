# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — MODIFICATION OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFIT (DEPENDENCY) REGULATIONS 1977

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(2)

As if in regulation 1(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7] [^c1646230] (interpretation), for the definition of “the determining authority" there were substituted the following definition— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

> “the determining authority" means, as the case may require, the Secretary of State, an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter I of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998, the Chief or any other Social Security Commissioner, or a tribunal consisting of three or more such Commissioners constituted in accordance with section 16(7) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7684b04987272fa634e45db240a0db7]

.
