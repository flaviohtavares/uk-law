# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 14

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 14 — MODIFICATION OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (BACK TO WORK BONUS) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 1996

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(14)

**1.**[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] As if in regulations 5(4)(c)(ii) and (6) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] [^c1646268], 8(5)(b) and 25(1) for the words “the adjudication officer" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ the Secretary of State ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**2.** As if in regulation 8(4)(c) and (d) for the word “review" there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549] “ revision or supersession ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549].

**3.** As if in regulation 9(2) the words “upon the adjudication officer" were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-255dd801c505df73562cd5ce516c5549]
