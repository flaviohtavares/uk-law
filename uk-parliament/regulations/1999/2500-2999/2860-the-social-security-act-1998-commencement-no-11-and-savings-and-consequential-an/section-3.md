# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 11, and Savings and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, section 3

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Consequential amendments and modifications

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (15)[^c-4b2585ce677dd974a1dbc246ad957827] the amendments and modifications made by the following paragraphs of this article shall take effect as from 18th October 1999.

(2) The Social Security Benefit (Dependency) Regulations 1977 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] [^c1646197] shall as they relate to relevant benefit be modified in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]

(3)[^c-0acda3162fb94252c76b02665febed09] The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] [^c1646198] shall as they relate to relevant benefit be modified in accordance with Schedule 3 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]

(4)[^c-0acda3162fb94252c76b02665febed09] The Social Security (Payments on Account, Overpayments and Recovery) Regulations 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] [^c1646199] shall as they relate to relevant benefit be modified in accordance with Schedule 4 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]

(5) The Community Charges (Deductions from Income Support) (Scotland) Regulations 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] [^c1646200] shall as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance be modified in accordance with Schedule 5 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]

(6) The Community Charges (Deduction from Income Support) ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] 2) Regulations 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06] [^c1646201] shall as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance be modified in accordance with Schedule 6 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06637ed9be08f92faad4e65581bf4d06]

(7) The Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991 [^c1646202] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 7 to this Order.

(8) The Social Security (Attendance Allowance) Regulations 1991 [^c1646203] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 8 to this Order.

(9) The Fines (Deductions from Income Support) Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58645f1b0b3dd7db1ed67e8b12a7d745] [^c1646204] shall as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance be modified in accordance with Schedule 9 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58645f1b0b3dd7db1ed67e8b12a7d745]

(10) The Council Tax (Deductions from Income Support) Regulations 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58645f1b0b3dd7db1ed67e8b12a7d745] [^c1646205] shall as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance be modified in accordance with Schedule 10 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58645f1b0b3dd7db1ed67e8b12a7d745]

(11) The Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Regulations 1995 [^c1646206] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 11 to this Order.

(12) The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 [^c1646207] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 12 to this Order.

(13) The Employment Protection (Recoupment of Jobseeker’s Allowance and Income Support) Regulations 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6] [^c1646208] shall as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance be modified in accordance with Schedule 13 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6]

(14) The Social Security (Back to Work Bonus) ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6]No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6] 2) Regulations 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6] [^c1646209] shall be modified in accordance with Schedule 14 to this Order in relation to any sum to which a person may be entitled under section 26 of the Jobseekers Act by virtue of his having been in receipt of a jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6]

(15) The Social Security Benefit (Computation of Earnings) Regulations 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6] [^c1646210] shall as they relate to relevant benefit be modified in accordance with Schedule 15 to this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-770aaaa6330d6585bc1f8d6a990508f6]

(16)[^c-9a7f308a8c7f7ce0f1b137c43a052a7c] The amendment in paragraph (17) shall take effect from 16th October 2006.

(17) In regulation 14(1) of the Social Security (Introduction of Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991 (reviews) after “appellate authorities)” insert “and sections 9 and 10 of the Social Security Act 1998 (revision and supersession of decisions)”.
