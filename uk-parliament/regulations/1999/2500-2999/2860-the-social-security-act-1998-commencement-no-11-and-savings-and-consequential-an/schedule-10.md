## SCHEDULE 10 — MODIFICATION OF THE COUNCIL TAX (DEDUCTIONS FROM INCOME SUPPORT) REGULATIONS 1993

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(10)

**1.**[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e] As if in regulation 1(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e] [^c1646260] (interpretation)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]

- (a) the definition of “adjudication officer" were omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]
- (b) for the definition of “Commissioner" there were substituted the following definition— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]

  > “Commissioner" means the Chief or any other Social Security Commissioner appointed in accordance with section 14(12) of, and Schedule 4 to, the Social Security Act 1998 and includes a Tribunal of Commissioners constituted in accordance with section 16(7) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]

  ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]
- (c) for the definition of “tribunal" there were substituted the following definition— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]

  > “tribunal" means an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter I of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf6f7ec956ba4fee4f471a5a7383e29e]

  .
