# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 3) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2745

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 3) Order 1999

Made

6th October 1999

Coming into force

18th October 1999

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 6 and 124(1)(d) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 3) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 18th October 1999.

## 2. Variations

The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

**3.** In Article 4—

- (a) for paragraph (4) substitute the following paragraph—

  > (4) In this article “restricted hours” means the period from 0700 hours to 1900 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive.

**4.** In Schedule 1 Part I for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

**5.** In Schedule 1 after item 7. delete—“PART II LENGTHS OF ROAD WHERE WAITING (IF NOT PROHIBITED BY ARTICLE 5) IS RESTRICTED BETWEEN 8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

**6.** In Schedule 2 Part I for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

**7.** In Schedule 2 Part II for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

**8.** In Schedule 2 Part III for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

**9.** In Schedule 2 Part IV for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

**10.** In Schedule 2 Part V for “8 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

**11.** In Schedule 2 Part VI for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

**12.** In Schedule 2 Part VII for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

**16.** In Schedule 3 Part IV for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

**17.** In Schedule 3 Part V for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

**18.** In Schedule 3 Part VII for “1 pm to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “1 pm to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”
