# The River Thames (Hungerford Footbridges) Order 1999, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Arbitration

Any difference under any provision of this Order (other than in relation to any matter otherwise provided for in Schedules 9, 10, 11 or a difference which falls to be determined by the tribunal) shall be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
