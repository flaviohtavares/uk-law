# The River Thames (Hungerford Footbridges) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 13 — APPLICATION OF LOCAL LEGISLATION

Article 31

**1.** Section 27(2) of the Thames Embankment (North and South) Act 1868[^f00029] (restrictions on building near Charing Cross Railway Bridge or Station) shall not apply to any works authorised by this Order.

**2.** The following provisions of the Port of London Act 1968[^f00030], namely:

- (a) section 70 (works not to be constructed, etc. without works licence); and
- (b) section 73(3) (licensing and dredging, etc.)

shall not apply to the authorised works.
