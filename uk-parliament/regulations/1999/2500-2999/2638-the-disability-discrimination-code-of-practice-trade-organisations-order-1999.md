# The Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Trade Organisations) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 2638

DISABLED PERSONS

The Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Trade Organisations) Order 1999

Made

17th September 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 54(6) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Code of Practice (Trade Organisations) Order 1999.

## 2. Code of Practice

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 Code of Practice on the Duties of Trade Organisations to their Disabled Members and Applicants (ISBN 0-11-271071-9), issued by the Secretary of State under section 53(1) and (2) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 on 16th July 1999, is 1st October 1999.

Margaret Hodge

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment and Equal Opportunities,

Department for Education and Employment

17th September 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st October 1999 as the date on which the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 Code of Practice on the Duties of Trade Organisations to their Disabled Members and Applicants, issued by the Secretary of State on 16th July 1999, shall come into force. The Code may be obtained from The Stationery Office, PO Box 276, London SW8 5DT, The Stationery Office book shops and The Stationery Office’s accredited agents.
