# The Protection from Eviction (Excluded Licences) (The Shaftesbury Society) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1758

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Protection from Eviction (Excluded Licences) (The Shaftesbury Society) Order 1999

Made

21st June 1999

Laid before Parliament

28th June 1999

Coming into force

19th July 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3A(8)(i) of the Protection from Eviction Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order–

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Eviction (Excluded Licences) (The Shaftesbury Society) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 19th July 1999.

## 2. Specified person

The Shaftesbury Society (registered charity number 221948) is hereby specified for the purposes of section 3A(8)(i) of the Protection from Eviction Act 1977 (licences which confer rights of occupation in hostels provided by certain persons).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

21st June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Protection from Eviction Act 1977 (“the 1977 Act”) prohibits eviction without a court order (section 3), makes special provision for agricultural employees (section 4) and imposes requirements as to notices to quit (section 5). Excluded tenancies and licences are not subject to the requirements of these sections.

Section 3A of the 1977 Act provides which tenancies and licences are excluded; subsection (8) of that section provides that a licence is excluded if it confers rights of occupation in a hostel provided by a body mentioned in that subsection or by a person (or member of a class of person) specified by order.

This Order specifies The Shaftesbury Society (registered charity number 221948) for the purposes of section 3A(8) of the 1977 Act.
