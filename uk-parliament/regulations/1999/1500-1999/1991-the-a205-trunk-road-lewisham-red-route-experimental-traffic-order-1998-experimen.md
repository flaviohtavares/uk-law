# The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1991

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation Order 1999

Made

13th July 1999

Coming into force

2nd August 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 2nd August 1999.

## 2. Variations

The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.
