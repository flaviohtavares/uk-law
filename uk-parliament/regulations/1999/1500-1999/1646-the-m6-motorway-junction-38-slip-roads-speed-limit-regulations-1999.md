# The M6 Motorway (Junction 38 Slip Roads) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1646

ROAD TRAFFIC

The M6 Motorway (Junction 38 Slip Roads) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1999

Made

8th June 1999

Laid before Parliament

18th June 1999

Coming into force

12th July 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the M6 Motorway (Junction 38 Slip Roads) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 12th July 1999.

## 2. Imposition of speed limit

(1) No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 50 miles per hour on any specified slip road.

(2) Each of the following slip roads at junction 38 of the M6 Motorway is a “specified slip road”–

- (a) the northbound exit slip road between its junction with the northbound carriageway of the M6 and its junction with the A685 roundabout;
- (b) the northbound entry slip road between its junction with the A685 roundabout and its junction with the northbound carriageway of the M6;
- (c) the southbound exit slip road between its junction with the southbound carriageway of the M6 and its junction with the northbound exit slip road.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Larry Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

8th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose a speed limit of 50 miles per hour on specified slip roads at junction 38 of the M6 Motorway.
