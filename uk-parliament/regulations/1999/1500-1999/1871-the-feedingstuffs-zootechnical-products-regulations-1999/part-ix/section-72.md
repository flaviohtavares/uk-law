# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Restrictions on toxicological tests on vertebrates

No person applying, or intending to apply, for the Community authorisation of a zootechnical additive shall begin toxicological tests on vertebrates unless, before beginning the tests, he has—

- (a) carried out a check of the type specified in the first paragraph of Article 9c.6 of Directive 70/524/EEC, as amended by Directive 96/51/EC, and
- (b) otherwise complied with the requirements of the first and second paragraphs of Article 9c.6 of Directive 70/524/EEC, as amended by Directive 96/51/EC.
