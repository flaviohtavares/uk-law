# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Use of Article 9c data

No person shall use scientific data and other information of the type specified in Article 9c of Directive 70/524/EEC, as amended by Directive 96/51/EC, unless the use of the data is in accordance with Article 9c of Directive 70/524/EEC as so amended.
