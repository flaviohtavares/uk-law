# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999

For regulation 3 of the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999[^f00027](manner of taking, preparing, marking, sealing and fastening of samples) there shall be substituted the following regulation:

> **3. Manner of taking, preparing, marking, sealing and fastening of samples**
>
> The manner in which samples of feeding stuffs are to be taken, prepared, marked, sealed and fastened shall be as prescribed in Schedule 1, and paragraph 10 of Part II of that Schedule shall have effect for the purposes of the certificate referred to in regulation 7.
