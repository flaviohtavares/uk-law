# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — EC REGULATIONS DELETING ADDITIVES FROM ANNEX B OF DIRECTIVE [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524)

Regulation 2(1)

**1.** Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2788/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/2788) (OJ No. L347, 23.12.98, p. 31) amending Council Directive 70/524/EEC concerning additives in feedingstuffs as regards the withdrawal of authorisation for certain growth promoters.

**2.** Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2821/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/2821) (OJ No. L351, 29.12.98, p. 4) amending, as regards withdrawal of the authorisation of certain antibiotics, Directive 70/524/EEC concerning additives in feedingstuffs.

**3.** Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 45/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/0045) (OJ No. L6, 12.1.1999, p. 3) amending Council Directive 70/524/EEC concerning additives in feedingstuffs as regards withdrawal of the authorisation of certain additives belonging to the group of coccidiostats and other medicinal substances.
