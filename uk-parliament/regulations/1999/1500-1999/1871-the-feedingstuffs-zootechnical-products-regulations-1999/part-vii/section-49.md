# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Manufacture of zootechnical premixtures

No person shall manufacture a zootechnical premixture with a view to putting it into circulation except on a UK approved or permitted Chapter I.2 establishment.
