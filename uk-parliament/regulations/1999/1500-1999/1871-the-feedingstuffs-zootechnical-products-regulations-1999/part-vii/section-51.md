# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Packaging of zootechnical premixtures

No person shall market a zootechnical premixture unless the premixture is packaged in accordance with the requirements of Article 10 of Directive 70/524/EEC.
