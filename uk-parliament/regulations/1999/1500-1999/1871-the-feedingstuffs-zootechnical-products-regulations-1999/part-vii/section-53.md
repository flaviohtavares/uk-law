# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Wrapping, packaging and storage of zootechnical premixtures by intermediaries

No intermediary shall wrap, package or store a zootechnical premixture unless he is a UK approved or permitted Chapter I.2 intermediary.
