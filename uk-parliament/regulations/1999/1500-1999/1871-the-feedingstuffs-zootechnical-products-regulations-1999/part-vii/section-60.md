# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Importation of zootechnical premixtures

No person shall import into the United Kingdom, from a third country, a zootechnical premixture manufactured in a third country, unless it was manufactured on a UK approved or permitted third country Chapter I.2 establishment, or an EC approved or permitted third country Chapter I.2 establishment.
