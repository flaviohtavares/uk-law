# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** These Regulations, which extend to the United Kingdom, implement—

- (a) insofar as they relate to zootechnical products, the following European Community Directives—
  - Council Directive 70/524/EEC concerning additives in feeding stuffs (OJ No. L270, 14.12.70, p. 1, OJ/SE Vol. 18, p. 4), including the amendments thereto in Council Directive 96/51/EC (OJ No. L235, 17.9.96, p. 39);
  - Third Commission Directive [72/199/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0199) (OJ No. L123, 29.5.72, p. 6) (OJ/SE 1966—72 p. 74) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - Fifth Commission Directive [74/203/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0203) (OJ No. L108, 22.4.74, p. 7) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - First Commission Directive [76/371/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0371) (OJ No. L102, 15.4.76, p. 1) establishing Community methods of sampling for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - Eighth Commission Directive [78/633/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0633) (OJ No. L206, 29.7.78, p. 43) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - Commission Directive [81/680/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0680) (OJ No. L246, 29.8.81, p. 32) amending Directives [71/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0250), [71/393/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0393), [72/199/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0199), [73/46/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0046), [74/203/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0203), [75/84/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0084), [76/372/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0372) and [78/633/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0633) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - Commission Directive [84/4/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0004) (OJ No. L15, 18.1.84, p. 28) amending Directives [71/393/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0393), [72/199/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0199) and [78/633/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0633) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feeding stuffs;
  - The requirements of Council Directive 95/53/EC fixing the principles governing the organisation of official inspections in the field of animal nutrition (OJ No. L265, 8.11.95, p. 17);
  - Council Directive 95/69/EC laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector and amending Directives [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524), [74/63/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0063), [79/373/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0373) and [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) (OJ No. L332, 30.12.95, p. 15);
  - Council Directive 96/51/EC amending Directive 70/524/EEC concerning additives in feedingstuffs (OJ No. L235, 17.9.96, p. 39);
  - Articles 6, 8 and 9 of Commission Directive 98/51/EC laying down certain measures for implementing Council Directive 95/69/EC laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector (OJ No. L208, 24.7.98, p. 43);
  - Commission Directive [98/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0054) (OJ No. L208, 24.7.98, p. 49) amending Directives [71/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0250), [72/199/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0199), [73/46/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0046) and repealing Directive [75/84/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0084);
  - Commission Directive [98/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0064) (OJ No. L257, 19.9.98, p. 14) establishing Community methods of analysis for the determination of amino acids, crude oils and fats, and olaquindox in feedingstuffs and amending Directive [71/393/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0393);
  - Council Directive [1999/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0020) (OJ No. L80, 25.3.1999, p. 20) amending Directives [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) concerning additives in feedingstuffs, [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) concerning certain products used in animal nutrition, [95/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0053) fixing the principles governing the organisation of official inspections in the field of animal nutrition and [95/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0069) laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector; and
  - Commission Directive [1999/27/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0027) (OJ No. L118, 6.5.99, p. 36) establishing Community methods of analysis for the determination of amprolium, diclazuril and carbadox in feedingstuffs and amending Directives [71/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0250), [73/46/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0046) and repealing Directive [74/203/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0203); and
- (b) in full, the following Community Directives—
  - Ninth Commission Directive [81/715/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0715) (OJ No. L257, 10.9.81, p. 38) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs;
  - Tenth Commission Directive [84/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0425) (OJ No. L238, 6.9.84, p. 34) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs;
  - Eleventh Commission Directive [93/70/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0070) (OJ No. L234, 17.9.93, p. 17) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs; and
  - Twelfth Commission Directive [93/117/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0117) (OJ No. L329, 30.12.93, p. 54) establishing Community methods of analysis for the official control of feedingstuffs.
