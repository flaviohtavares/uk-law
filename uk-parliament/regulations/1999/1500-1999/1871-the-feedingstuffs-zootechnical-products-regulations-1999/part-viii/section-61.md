# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Manufacture of zootechnical compound feedingstuffs

No person shall manufacture a zootechnical compound feedingstuff with a view to putting it into circulation except on a UK approved or permitted Chapter I.3(M) establishment.
