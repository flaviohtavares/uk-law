# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Labelling of zootechnical feedingstuffs

No person shall put into circulation a zootechnical feedingstuff unless the labelling of the feedingstuff complies with the provisions of paragraphs 1(a), 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8 of Article 16 of Directive 70/524/EEC as amended by Directive 96/51/EC or, with effect from 1st October 1999, complies with those provisions as amended as aforesaid, and as further amended by Council Directive [1999/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0020).
