# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Putting zootechnical compound feedingstuffs into circulation

No person shall put a zootechnical compound feedingstuff into circulation unless it has been manufactured on—

- (a) a UK or EC approved or permitted Chapter I.3(M) establishment, or
- (b) a UK or EC approved or permitted third country Chapter I.3(M) establishment.
