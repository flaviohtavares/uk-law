# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Importation of zootechnical feedingstuffs

(1) No person shall import from an EEA State a zootechnical feedingstuff for marketing in the United Kingdom unless the details given on the package or container of, or label attached to, the feedingstuff and covered by Article 18 of Directive 70/524/EEC comply with that Article.

(2) No person shall import into the United Kingdom, from a third country, a zootechnical compound feedingstuff manufactured in a third country, unless it was manufactured on a UK approved or permitted third country Chapter I.3(M) establishment, or an EC approved or permitted third country Chapter I.3(M) establishment.
