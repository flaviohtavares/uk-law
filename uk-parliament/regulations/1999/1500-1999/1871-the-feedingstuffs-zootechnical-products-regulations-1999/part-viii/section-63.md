# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Production of zootechnical compound feedingstuffs

No person shall produce a zootechnical compound feedingstuff for the exclusive requirements of his holding except on a UK approved or permitted Chapter I.3(P) establishment.
