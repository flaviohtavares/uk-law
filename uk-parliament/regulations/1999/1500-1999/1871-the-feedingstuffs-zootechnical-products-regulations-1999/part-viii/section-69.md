# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Export of zootechnical feedingstuffs to EEA States

No person shall export a zootechnical feedingstuff for marketing in an EEA State unless the details given on the package or container of, or label attached to, the feedingstuff and covered by Article 18 of Directive 70/524/EEC comply with that Article.
