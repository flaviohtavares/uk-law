# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Duties on persons producing zootechnical compound feedingstuffs

A person producing a zootechnical compound feedingstuff for the exclusive requirements of his holding on a UK approved Chapter I.3(P) establishment shall fulfil the essential conditions contained in Chapter I.3(b) of the Annex to Directive 95/69/EC with the exception of point 7.
