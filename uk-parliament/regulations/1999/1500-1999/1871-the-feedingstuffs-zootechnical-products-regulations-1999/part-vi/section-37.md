# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Wrapping, packaging and storage of zootechnical additives by intermediaries

No intermediary shall wrap, package or store a zootechnical additive unless he is a UK approved or permitted Chapter I.1 intermediary.
