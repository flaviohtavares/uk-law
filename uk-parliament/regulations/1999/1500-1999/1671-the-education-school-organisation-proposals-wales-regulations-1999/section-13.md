# The Education (School Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Revocation

The Education (Publication of Proposals for Reduction in Standard Numbers) Regulations 1991[^f00004] and the Education (Publication of School Proposals and Notices) Regulations 1993[^f00005] (insofar as they have not ceased to have effect by virtue of the repeal without re-enactment of the provisions containing the powers under which they were made) are hereby revoked in relation to Wales.
