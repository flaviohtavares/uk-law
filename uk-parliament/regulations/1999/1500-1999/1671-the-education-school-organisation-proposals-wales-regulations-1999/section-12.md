# The Education (School Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Amendment of transitional Regulations

Regulation 12 of the Education (Transition to New Framework) (School Organisation Proposals) Regulations 1999[^f00003] shall be amended in relation to Wales by the substitution for the words “section 42(1)(b)” in paragraph (b)(i) and (ii) (in both places where they occur) of the words “section 41(1)(b)”.
