# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1753 (C. 48)

NORTHERN IRELAND

HUMAN RIGHTS AND EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES

The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1999

Made

18th June 1999

In exercise of the power conferred on me by section 101(3) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1999.

**2.** Section 74 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 shall come into force on 21st June 1999 for the purpose of making an order under subsections (5) and (6) of that section.

Marjorie Mowlam

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

18th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 21st June 1999, the enabling powers in section 74 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998, under which the Secretary of State may make an order consequent on the transfer of functions to the Equality Commission.
