# The Combined Probation Areas (Lincolnshire) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1870

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Lincolnshire) Order 1999

Made

28th June 1999

Coming into force

1st July 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of the Probation Services Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Lincolnshire) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st July 1999.

**2.** That part of the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Lincolnshire area[^f00003] shall be amended by–

- (a) substituting “Skegness” for the reference to “Spilsby and Skegness” in column 1;
- (b) substituting “3” for “2” in the entry in respect of Lincoln District in column 3;
- (c) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Caistor and Market Rasen.

Gareth Williams

Minister of State

Home Office

28th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) to take account of the Lord Chancellor’s reorganisation of the petty sessional divisions in Lincolnshire as effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Lincolnshire) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/64)) and the Petty Sessional Divisions (Lincolnshire) (No. 2) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/65](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/65)). The petty sessional division of Skegness was formerly named “Spilsby and Skegness”. The petty sessional division of Lincoln District was formerly represented by 2 justices. The former petty sessional districts of Caistor and Market Rasen now form part of the expanded Lincoln District.
