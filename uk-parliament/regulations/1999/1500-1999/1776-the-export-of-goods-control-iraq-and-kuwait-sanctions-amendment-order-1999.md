# The Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1776

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

23rd June 1999

Coming into force

14th July 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 14th July 1999.

**2.** In article 3 of the Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) Order 1990[^f00003], the words “in writing” shall be inserted after the word “licence” where it first appears.

Kim Howells

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competition and Consumer Affairs,

Department of Trade and Industry

23rd June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) Order 1990 by requiring all licences granted by the Secretary of State to be in writing.
