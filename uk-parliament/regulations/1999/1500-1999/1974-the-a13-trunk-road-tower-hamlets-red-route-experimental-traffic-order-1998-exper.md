# The A13 Trunk Road (Tower Hamlets) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation (No. 2) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1974

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A13 Trunk Road (Tower Hamlets) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation (No. 2) Order 1999

Made

8th July 1999

Coming into force

23rd July 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A13 Trunk Road (Tower Hamlets) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation (No. 2) Order 1999, and shall come into force on 23rd July 1999.

## 2. Variations

The A13 Trunk Road (Tower Hamlets) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

**3.** In Schedule 3A item 4 is substituted as follows:–

> **4.** Commercial Road (A13), south side, between a point 1m west of a point opposite the common boundary of Nos 562 and 564 Commercial Road and a point 1.0m east of a point opposite the common boundary of Nos 568 and 570 Commercial Road.

**4.** In Schedule 4 items 8 and 19 are substituted as follows:–

> **8.** Commercial Road (A13), south side, between the eastern kerbline of Butcher Row and a point 1m west of a point opposite the common boundary of Nos 562 and 564 Commercial Road.

> **19.** East India Dock Road (A13), north side, between a point opposite the common boundary of Nos 219 and 221 East India Dock Road and a point 4m east of a point opposite the common boundary of Nos 251b and 253 East India Dock Road.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

8th July 1999
