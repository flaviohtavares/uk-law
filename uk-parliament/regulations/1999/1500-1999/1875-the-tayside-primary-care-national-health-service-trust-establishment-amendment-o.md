# The Tayside Primary Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1875 (S. 112)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Tayside Primary Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

29th June 1999

Coming into force

5th July 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12A(1) and (4) of and paragraph 1 and 3(1)(b) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Tayside Primary Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 5th July 1999.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Tayside Primary Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

For article 3 of the principal Order there is substituted the following:–

> **3.** (1) The trust is established for the purpose specified in Section 12A(1)(b) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions (which include functions which the Secretary of State considers appropriate in relation to the provision of services by the trust for one or more Health Boards) shall be to provide and manage hospital accommodation facilities at Ashludie Hospital, Monifieth, and at any establishment associated with that hospital, and to provide and manage health care facilities comprising a new acute psychiatric unit and associated facilities adjacent to Ninewells Hospital, Dundee and the support services related thereto, and also including the management of any teaching and research facilities associated with these establishments.

.

Sewel

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new article 3 in the Tayside Primary Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998 ([S.I. 1998/2710](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2710)).
