# The Leicestershire and Rutland Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1825

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Leicestershire and Rutland Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

25th June 1999

Coming into force

8th July 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Leicestershire and Rutland Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 8th July 1999.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In article 4 of the Leicestershire and Rutland Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00004], for “5 non-executive” substitute “6 non-executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tessa Jowell

Minister of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

25th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of the Leicestershire and Rutland Healthcare National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 6.
