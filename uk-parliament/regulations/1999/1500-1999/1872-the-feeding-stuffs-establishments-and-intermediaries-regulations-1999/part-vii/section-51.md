# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Further control of putting into circulation by intermediaries of additives to which regulation 46 applies

No EC or UK registered Article 8.1(A) intermediary shall put into circulation an additive of any kind to which regulation 46 applies, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in point 7 of Chapter II(c) of the Annex.
