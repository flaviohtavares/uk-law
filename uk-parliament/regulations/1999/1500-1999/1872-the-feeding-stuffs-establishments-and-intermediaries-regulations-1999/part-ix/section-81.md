# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Importation of Directive 82/471 products referred to in Article 2.2(a) of Directive 95/69

No person shall import into the United Kingdom from a third country, a Directive 82/471 product of any kind referred to in Chapter I.1(a) of the Annex, manufactured in a third country, unless it was manufactured on a UK approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment, or an EC approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment.
