# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Putting into circulation by intermediaries of Directive 82/471 products to which regulation 76 applies

No intermediary shall put into circulation a Directive 82/471 product of any kind to which regulation 76 applies, other than an EC or UK approved or permitted Article 3.1(P) intermediary.
