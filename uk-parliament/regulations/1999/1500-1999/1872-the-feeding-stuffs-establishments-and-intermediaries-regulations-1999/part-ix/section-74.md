# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Manufacture of Directive 82/471 products referred to in Article 2.2(a) of Directive 95/69

No person shall manufacture a Directive 82/471 product, of any kind referred to in Chapter I.1(a) of the Annex, with a view to putting it into circulation, except on a UK approved or permitted Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment.
