# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Putting into circulation certain Directive 82/471 products

No person shall put into circulation a Directive 82/471 product, of any kind referred to in Chapter I.1(a) of the Annex, except a product of any such kind manufactured on—

- (a) a UK approved or permitted Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment,
- (b) an EC approved or permitted Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment,
- (c) a UK approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment, or
- (d) an EC approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(a)(P) establishment.
