# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Statement to accompany sample

There shall be sent, with the part of the sample sent to the agricultural analyst pursuant to regulation 99, a statement signed by the authorised person that the sample was taken in the manner referred to in regulation 98(8).
