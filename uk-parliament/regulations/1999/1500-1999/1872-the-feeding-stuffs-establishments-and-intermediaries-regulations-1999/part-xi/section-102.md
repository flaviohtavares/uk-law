# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Analysis by the Agricultural analyst

The agricultural analyst shall analyse the part of the sample sent to him under regulation 99, and send a certificate of the analysis, completed in the form set out in Schedule 2, and in accordance with the notes set out in that Schedule, to the authorised person, who shall send a copy to—

- (a) the person who was subjected to the official check concerned, and
- (b) any person to whom he has sent a part of the sample pursuant to regulation 100.
