# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 97

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 97. Official checks and enforcement

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), it shall be the duty of the competent body to enforce these Regulations and carry out official checks for that purpose.

(2) Nothing in these Regulations shall be taken as authorising any competent body in Scotland to institute proceedings for an offence.
