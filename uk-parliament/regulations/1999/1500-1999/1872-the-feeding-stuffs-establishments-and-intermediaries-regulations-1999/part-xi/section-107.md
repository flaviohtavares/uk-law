# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Offences

It shall be an offence for a person—

- (a) without reasonable excuse, to contravene any of regulations 40 to 96;
- (b) in connection with these Regulations to make a statement which he knows to be false in a material particular, or recklessly to make a statement which is false in a material particular;
- (c) intentionally to obstruct an authorised person in the exercise of any power conferred by regulation 98, or
- (d) without reasonable excuse to fail to comply with any requirement made of him, pursuant to regulation 98, by an authorised person.
