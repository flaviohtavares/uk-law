# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** These Regulations, which extend to the United Kingdom, implement—

- (a) the requirements of Council Directive [95/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0053) fixing the principles governing the organisation of official inspections in the field of animal nutrition (OJ No. L265, 8.11.95, p. 17);
- (b) Council Directive [95/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0069) laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector and amending Directives [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524), [74/63/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0063), [79/373/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0373) and [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) (OJ No. L332, 30.12.95, p. 15);
- (c) Article 1:10 of Council Directive [96/51/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0051) amending Directive [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) concerning additives in feeding stuffs (OJ No. L235, 17.9.96, p. 39);
- (d) Articles 6, 8 and 9 of Commission Directive [98/51/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0051) laying down certain measures for implementing Council Directive [95/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0069) laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector (OJ No. L208, 24.7.98, p. 43); and Article 3 of Council Directive [1999/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0020) (OJ No. L80, 25.3.1999, p. 20) amending Directives [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) concerning additives in feedingstuffs, [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) concerning certain products used in animal nutrition, [95/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0053) fixing the principles governing the organisation of official inspections in the field of animal nutrition and [95/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0069) laying down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector;

in relation to feeding stuffs, and related products, which do not contain zootechnical additives (i.e. antibiotics, coccidiostats and other medical substances or growth promoters).
