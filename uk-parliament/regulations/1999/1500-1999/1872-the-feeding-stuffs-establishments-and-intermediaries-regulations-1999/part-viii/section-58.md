# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Further control of manufacture of premixtures to which regulation 57 applies

No person shall manufacture a premixture of the kind to which regulation 57 applies on a UK approved Article 2.2(b) establishment, with a view to putting it into circulation, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in Chapter I.2(b) of the Annex.
