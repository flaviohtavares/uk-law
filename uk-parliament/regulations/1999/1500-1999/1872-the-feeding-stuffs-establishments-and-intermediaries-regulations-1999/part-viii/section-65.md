# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Putting into circulation by intermediaries of premixtures to which regulation 61 applies

No intermediary shall put into circulation a premixture of the kind to which regulation 61 applies, other than an EC or UK approved or permitted Article 3.1(PA) intermediary.
