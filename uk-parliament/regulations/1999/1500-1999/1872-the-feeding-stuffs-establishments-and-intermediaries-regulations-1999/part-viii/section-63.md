# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Wrapping, packaging and storage by intermediaries of premixtures referred to in Article 8.1 of Directive 95/69

No intermediary shall wrap, package or store a premixture which contains additives of any kind (other than copper, selenium, vitamin A and vitamin D) referred to in Chapter II(a) of the Annex, but does not contain an additive of any kind referred to in Chapter I.2(a) of the Annex, other than a UK registered or permitted Article 8.1(PA) intermediary.
