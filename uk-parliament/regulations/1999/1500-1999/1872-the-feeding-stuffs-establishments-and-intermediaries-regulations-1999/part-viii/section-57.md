# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Manufacture of certain premixtures referred to in Article 2.2(b) of Directive 95/69

No person shall manufacture a premixture, which contains additives of any kind referred to in the fourth or fifth indents of Chapter I.2(a) of the Annex, but does not contain a zootechnical additive, with a view to putting it into circulation, except on a UK approved or permitted Article 2.2(b) establishment.
