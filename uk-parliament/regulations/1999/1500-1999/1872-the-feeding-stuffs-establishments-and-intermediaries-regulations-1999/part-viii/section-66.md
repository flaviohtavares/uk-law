# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Further control of putting into circulation by intermediaries of premixtures to which regulation 61 applies

No EC or UK approved Article 3.1(PA) intermediary shall put into circulation a premixture of the kind to which regulation 61 applies, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in point 7 of Chapter I.2(b) of the Annex.
