# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Manufacture of premixtures referred to in Article 7.2(b) of Directive 95/69

No person shall manufacture a premixture, which contains additives of any kind (other than copper, selenium, vitamin A and vitamin D) referred to in Chapter II(a) of the Annex, but does not contain an additive of any kind referred to in Chapter I.2(a) of the Annex, with a view to putting it into circulation, except on a UK registered or permitted Article 7.2(b) establishment.
