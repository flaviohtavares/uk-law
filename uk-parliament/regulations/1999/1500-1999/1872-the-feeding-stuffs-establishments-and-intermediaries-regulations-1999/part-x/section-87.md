# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Further control of manufacture of compound feeding stuffs to which regulation 86 applies

No person shall manufacture a compound feeding stuff of the kind to which regulation 86 applies on a UK registered Article 7.2(c)(PA) establishment, with a view to putting it into circulation, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in Chapter II(c) of the Annex.
