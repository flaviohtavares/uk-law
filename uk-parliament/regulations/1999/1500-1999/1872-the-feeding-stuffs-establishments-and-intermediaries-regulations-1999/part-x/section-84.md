# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Production of compound feeding stuffs referred to in Article 2.2(f) of Directive 95/69

No person shall produce, for the exclusive requirements of his holding, a compound feeding stuff of any kind the production of which is regulated by Article 2.2(f) of Directive 95/69, except on a UK approved or permitted Article 2.2(f) establishment.
