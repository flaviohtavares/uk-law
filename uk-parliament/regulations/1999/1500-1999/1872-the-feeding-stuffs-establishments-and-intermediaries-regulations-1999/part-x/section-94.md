# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Importation of compound feeding stuffs referred to in Article 2.2(d) of Directive 95/69

No person shall import into the United Kingdom from a third country, a compound feeding stuff of any kind the manufacture of which is regulated by Article 2.2(d) of Directive 95/69, manufactured in a third country, unless it was manufactured on a UK approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(d) establishment, or an EC approved or permitted third country Article 2.2(d) establishment.
