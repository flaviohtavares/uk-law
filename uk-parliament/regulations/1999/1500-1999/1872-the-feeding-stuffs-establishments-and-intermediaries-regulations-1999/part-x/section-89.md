# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Further control of production of compound feeding stuffs to which regulation 88 applies

No person shall produce, for the exclusive requirements of his holding, a compound feeding stuff of the kind to which regulation 88 applies on a UK registered Article 7.2(d)(PA) establishment, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in Chapter II(c) of the Annex.
