# The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Further control of production of compound feeding stuffs to which regulation 84 applies

No person shall produce, for the exclusive requirements of his holding, a compound feeding stuff of any kind to which regulation 84 applies on a UK approved Article 2.2(f) establishment, unless he fulfils the essential conditions contained or referred to in points 1 to 6.2 of Chapter I.4 of the Annex.
