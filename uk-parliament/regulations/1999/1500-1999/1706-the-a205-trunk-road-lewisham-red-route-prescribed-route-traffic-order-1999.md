# The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1706

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1999

Made

11th June 1999

Coming into force

30th June 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1999, and shall come into force on 30th June 1999.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- (a) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this Order so numbered;
- (b) cause includes permit;
- (c) “Stanstead Road (south arm)” means that length of the A205 Trunk Road between Catford Hill and Stanstead Road (north arm) in the London Borough of Lewisham known as Stanstead Road;
- (d) “Stanstead Road (north arm)” means that length of the A205 Trunk Road fronting St. Dunstan’s College in the London Borough of Lewisham known as Stanstead Road;
- (e) “Glenwood Road” means that road so named in the London Borough of Lewisham.

## 3. Prescribed Routes

Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause any vehicle to travel in Stanstead Road (south arm) from its junction with Glenwood Road to its junction with Stanstead Road (north arm) in any direction except north-westwards.

## 4. Exemptions

The controls specified in article 3 do not apply in respect of anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## 5. Revocation of existing Orders

The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

11th June 1999
