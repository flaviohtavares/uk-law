# The Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1547 (C. 45)

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1999

Made

5th June 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(3) of the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1999.

## 2. Commencement

The Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998, so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 2nd July 1999.

Ian McCartney

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

5th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 2nd July 1999 all the provisions of the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (“the Act”) which did not come into force on the passing of the Act. The provisions of the Act which came into force on its passing are sections 17 and 18 and, for certain purposes, sections 1 and 8.
