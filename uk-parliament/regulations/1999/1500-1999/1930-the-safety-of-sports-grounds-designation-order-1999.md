# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1930

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1999

Made

7th July 1999

Laid before Parliament

7th July 1999

Coming into force

31st July 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 31st July 1999.

## 2. Designation

The sports grounds specified in the Schedule to this Order (being sports grounds in England at which association football matches are played and which are occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, each of which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) are hereby designated as sports grounds requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Association Football Grounds) (Designation) Order 1985

In the Schedule to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Association Football Grounds) (Designation) Order 1985[^f00003], the entry in respect of Springfield Park, Wigan shall be omitted.

## 4. Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1986

In Schedule 1 to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1986[^f00004], the entry in respect of Poole Stadium, Wimborne Road, Poole shall be omitted.

Chris Smith

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

7th July 1999
