# The A35 Trunk Road (Chideock Morcombelake Bypass) Order 1996 (Revocation Order) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1725

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A35 Trunk Road (Chideock Morcombelake Bypass) Order 1996 (Revocation Order) 1999

Made

16th June 1999

Coming into force

30th June 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A35 Trunk Road (Chideock Morcombelake Bypass) Order 1996 (Revocation Order) 1999, and shall come into force on 30th June 1999.

**2.** The A35 Trunk Road (Chideock Morcombelake Bypass) Order 1996[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Derek Oddy

Divisional Director Highways Agency

16th June 1999
