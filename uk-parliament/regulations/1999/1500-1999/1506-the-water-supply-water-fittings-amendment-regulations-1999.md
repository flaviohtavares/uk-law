# The Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument is being made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1999/1148](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1999 No. 1506

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made

28th May 1999

Laid before Parliament

28th May 1999

Coming into force

1st July 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions and the Secretary of State for Wales acting jointly, in exercise of their powers under sections 74 and 213(2) of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st July 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999

(1) The Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 5(3)(d) (notification) for “paragraph (1)(d)(iii)–(v) or (1)(e)” substitute “paragraph 4(a), (c), (h) or 5 in the Table”.

(3) In regulation 6(2) (contractor’s certificate) for “paragraph 5” substitute “regulation 5(1)”.

(4) In regulation 10(1) (enforcement) for “Part I” substitute “Part II”.

(5) In Schedule 2 (requirements for water fittings)–

- (a) in paragraph 14(2) (prevention of cross connection to unwholesome water), at the end insert “unless a device for preventing backflow is installed in accordance with paragraph 15.”
- (b) in paragraph 17(1)(a) (hot water services), for the words from “a vent pipe” to “relief valve” substitute “a temperature control device and either a temperature relief valve or a combined temperature and pressure relief valve”.
