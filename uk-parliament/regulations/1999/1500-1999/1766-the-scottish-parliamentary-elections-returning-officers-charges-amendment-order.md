# The Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument had been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1999/1094](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1094) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

1999 No. 1766

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, SCOTLAND

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) Amendment Order 1999

Made

16th June 1999

Coming into force

18 June 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 29(3), (4) and (8A) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] as applied by article 18 of the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 1999[^f00002] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order with the consent of the Treasury:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 18 June 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) Order 1999

(1) The Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) Order 1999 is amended as follows.

(2) In the definition of “by-election” in article 1(2) for “8” there is substituted “9”.

(3) In article 3(1)(b) for “7” where it appears in the second place there is substituted “8”.

Calum MacDonald

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

10th June 1999

We consent to this Order

Jim Dowd

Bob Ainsworth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

16th June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Scottish Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers' Charges) Order 1999 as a consequence of an error in articles 1(2) and 3(1)(b) of that Order.
