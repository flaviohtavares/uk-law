# The Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 1769

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

21st June 1999

Coming into force

1st July 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st July 1999.

## 2. Amendment of NHS Trust Establishment Order

In paragraph (2) of article 3 of the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00004] (nature and functions of the trust), after “UB8 3NN” there shall be added the words “; and to own and manage hospital accommodation at Mount Vernon Hospital, Rickmansworth Road, Northwood, Middlesex HA6 2RN and there to manage local acute services”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

21st June 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990 (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust.

Article 3 of the Establishment Order is amended so as to require the Trust to carry out the additional functions of owning and managing hospital accommodation at Mount Vernon Hospital, Rickmansworth Road, Northwood, Middlesex HA6 2RN and of managing local acute services at that hospital.
