# The A40 Trunk Road (Ealing and Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3416

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A40 Trunk Road (Ealing and Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 1999

Made

20th December 1999

Coming into force

17th January 2000

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A40 Trunk Road (Ealing and Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 17th January 2000.

## 2. Variations

The A40 Trunk Road (Ealing and Hammersmith & Fulham ) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

**3.** In Schedule 2B item 3 is substituted as follows:

> **3.** A40 Western Avenue, the south-west side from a point 6 metres north-west of a point opposite the party wall of 159 and 161 Western Avenue to a point opposite the party wall of 149 and 151 Western Avenue.

**4.** In Schedule 2B item 11 is substituted as follows:

> **11.** A40 Westway, the northside, from a point 2 metres west of the western boundary of 11 Westway westward for a distance of 14 metres.

**5.** In Schedule 4 item 3 is substituted as follows:

> **3.** A40 Western Avenue, the south-west side, from a point opposite the party wall of 233 and 235 Western Avenue to a point 6 metres north-west of a point opposite the party wall of 159 and 161 Western Avenue.

**6.** In Schedule 4 item 9 is substituted as follows:

> **9.** A40 Westway, the north side, from its junction with Old Oak Common Lane to a point 2 metres east of the party wall of 5 and 7 Westway.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

20th December 1999
