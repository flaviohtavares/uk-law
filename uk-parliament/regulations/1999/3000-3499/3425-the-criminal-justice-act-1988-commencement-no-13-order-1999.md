# The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 13) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3425 (C. 93)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 13) Order 1999

Made

15th December 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 171(1) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (Commencement No. 13) Order 1999.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 shall come into force on 1st January 2000—

- (a) the entries relating to sections 39 and 40 of the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00002] in Schedule 16 (repeals); and
- (b) section 170(2), to the extent necessary to bring the entries mentioned in paragraph (a) above into force.

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

15th December 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the repeal of sections 39 and 40 of the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, which is effected by section 170(2) of, and Schedule 16 to, the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (“the 1988 Act”). The repealed provisions relate to criminal bankruptcy orders. The power to make such orders was abolished on 3rd April 1989 by section 101 of the 1988 Act.
