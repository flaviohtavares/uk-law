# The Northern Birmingham Community Health and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3466

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Northern Birmingham Community Health and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made

22nd December 1999

Coming into force

1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Northern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northern Birmingham Community Health and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

The Northern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust established by the Northern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust Establishment Order[^f00005] and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust established by the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust Establishment Order[^f00006] are hereby dissolved, and accordingly those Orders are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

22nd December 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2000 of the Northern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust and the Southern Birmingham Community Health National Health Service Trust.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the Birmingham Specialist Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00007].
