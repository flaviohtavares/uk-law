# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 12 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL FUND (APPLICATION FOR REVIEW) REGULATIONS 1988

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(8)

In regulation 2 [^c16476931] (manner of making application for review or further review and time limits), for the words–

- (a) “a social fund officer" in each place in which they occur there shall be substituted the words “ an appropriate officer ”; and
- (b) “the social fund officer" in each place in which they occur there shall be substituted the words “ the appropriate officer ”.
