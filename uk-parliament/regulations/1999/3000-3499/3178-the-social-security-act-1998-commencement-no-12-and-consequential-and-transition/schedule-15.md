# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 12 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 15

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 15 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY (BACK TO WORK BONUS) (No. 2) REGULATIONS 1996

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(15)

**1.** In regulations 5(4)(c)(ii) and (6) [^c16477201], 8(5)(b) and 25(1), for the words “adjudication officer" there shall be substituted the words “ Secretary of State ”.

**2.** In regulation 8(4)(c) and (d), for the word “review" there shall be substituted the words “ revision or supersession ”.

**3.** In regulation 9(2), the words “upon the adjudication officer" shall be omitted.
