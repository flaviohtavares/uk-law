# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 12 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2014-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL FUND MATERNITY AND FUNERAL EXPENSES (GENERAL) REGULATIONS 1987

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(4)

In regulation 4(4) [^c16476651] (provisions against double payment)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for the word “reviewed" there shall be substituted the word “ revised ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), the words “on that review" shall be omitted.
