# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 12 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 18

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 18 — AMENDMENT OF THE SOCIAL FUND WINTER FUEL PAYMENT REGULATIONS 1998

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(18)

**1.** In regulation 1(2) [^c16477211] (interpretation), the definition of “the Administration Act" shall be omitted.

**2.** For paragraph (2) of regulation 4 (official records) there shall be substituted the following paragraph–

> (2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply so as to exclude the revision of a decision under section 9 of the Social Security Act 1998 or the supersession of a decision under section 10 [^c16477221] of that Act or the consideration of fresh evidence in connection with the revision or supersession of a decision.

.
