# The Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 12 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999, section 3

> Text as at 2014-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The amendments and revocations effected by this Order–

- (a) shall take effect as from 29th November 1999; and
- (b) shall not have effect in relation to–
  - (i) working families’ tax credit;
  - (ii) disabled person’s tax credit;
  - (iii) decisions to which article 4(6) of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999 applies.

(2) The Social Security Benefit (Dependency) Regulations 1977 [^c16476161] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Order.

(3) The Social Security (General Benefit) Regulations 1982 [^c16476171] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to this Order.

(4) The Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 1987 [^c16476181] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 4 to this Order.

(5) The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987 [^c16476191] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 5 to this Order.

(6)[^key-f34b006aa67c74179bdb392832671214] The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987 [^c16476201] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 6 to this Order.

(7)[^key-74c94d924f063f39cd4b9823dec6d6ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) The Social Fund (Application for Review) Regulations 1988 [^c16476231] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 8 to this Order.

(9)[^key-6644e953c27a82ae6d4601044e832823] The Social Security (Payments on Account, Overpayments and Recovery) Regulations 1988 [^c16476241] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 9 to this Order.

(10) The Community Charges (Deductions from Income Support) (Scotland) Regulations 1989 [^c16476251] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 10 to this Order.

(11) The Community Charges (Deductions from Income Support) (No. 2) Regulations 1990 [^c16476261] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 11 to this Order.

(12) The Fines (Deductions from Income Support) Regulations 1992 [^c16476271] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 12 to this Order.

(13) The Council Tax (Deductions from Income Support) Regulations 1993 [^c16476281] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 13 to this Order.

(14) The Employment Protection (Recoupment of Jobseeker’s Allowance and Income Support) Regulations 1996 [^c16476291] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 14 to this Order.

(15) The Social Security (Back to Work Bonus) (No. 2) Regulations 1996 [^c16476301] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 15 to this Order.

(16) The Social Security Benefit (Computation of Earnings) Regulations 1996 [^c16476311] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 16 to this Order.

(17) The Social Security (Recovery of Benefits) Regulations 1997 [^c16476321] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 17 to this Order.

(18) The Social Fund Winter Fuel Payment Regulations 1998 [^c16476331] shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 18 to this Order.

(19) The Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 1999 shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 19 to this Order.

(20) The No. 8 Order, the No. 9 Order and the No. 11 Order shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 20 to this Order.
