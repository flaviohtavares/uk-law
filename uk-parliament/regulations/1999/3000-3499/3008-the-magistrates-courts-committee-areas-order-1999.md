# The Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3008

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999

Made

3rd November 1999

Coming into force

1st December 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27A(2) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001] and paragraph 29 of Schedule 14 to the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00002], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st December 1999.

## 2. Magistrates' courts committee areas

(1) Each of the magistrates' courts committees in England outside Greater London specified in column 1 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order comprises the local government areas set out opposite the name of that committee in column 2 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order.

(2) Each of the magistrates' courts committees in Wales specified in column 1 of Part II of the Schedule to this Order comprises the local government areas set out opposite the name of that committee in column 2 of Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Jane Kennedy

Parliamentary Secretary

Lord Chancellor’s Department

3rd November 1999

This is the first order under section 27A(2) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997, inserted by section 81 of the Access to Justice Act 1999. It specifies the magistrates' courts committee areas for England outside Greater London and Wales. Each of the areas was a magistrates' courts committee area immediately before section 81 came into force.
