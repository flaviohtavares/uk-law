# The Combined Probation Areas (Wiltshire) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No.3343

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Wiltshire) Order 1999

Made

9th December 1999

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of the Probation Services Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Wiltshire) Order 1999.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 2000 save that, for the purposes of making appointments to the probation committee for Wiltshire to take effect on that date, this Order shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** For that part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Wiltshire area[^f00003], there shall be substituted the following–

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) to take account of the combination of the petty sessional divisions of North Wiltshire and West Wiltshire to form the new petty sessional division of North West Wiltshire and the combination of the petty sessional divisions of Kennet and Salisbury to form the new petty sessional division of South East Wiltshire. The petty sessional division of Swindon is not affected by this Order. The changes to the petty sessional divisions in question are effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Wiltshire) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/1500](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1500)), as amended by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Wiltshire) (Amendment) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/1850](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1850)), which comes into force, subject to certain exceptions, on 1st January 2000.
