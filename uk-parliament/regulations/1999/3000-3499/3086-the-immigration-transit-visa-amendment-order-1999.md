# The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3086

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made

16th November 1999

Laid before Parliament

18th November 1999

Coming into force

19th November 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1A(1) and (2) of the Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 1987[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 19th November 1999.

**2.** For the Schedule to the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment No. 3) Order 1998[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

16th November 1999

## SCHEDULE — COUNTRIES OR TERRITORIES WHOSE NATIONALS OR CITIZENS NEED A VISA FORPASSING THROUGH THE UNITED KINGDOM

- Afghanistan
- Democratic Republic of the Congo
- Eritrea
- Ethiopia
- Federal Republic of Yugoslavia
- Ghana
- Iran
- Iraq
- Libya
- Nigeria
- People’s Republic of China
- Republic of Croatia
- Slovak Republic
- Somalia
- Sri Lanka
- Turkey
- Uganda

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993 (as amended by the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment) Order 1998, the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1998 and the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment No. 3) Order 1998) by adding the Republic of Croatia to the list of countries or territories whose nationals or citizens need a visa for passing through the United Kingdom. It also revokes spent provisions.
