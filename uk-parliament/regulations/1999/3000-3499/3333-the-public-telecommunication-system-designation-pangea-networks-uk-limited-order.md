# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Pangea Networks (UK) Limited) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3333

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Pangea Networks (UK) Limited) Order 1999

Made

11th December 1999

Laid before Parliament

15th December 1999

Coming into force

13th January 2000

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Pangea Networks (UK) Limited on 2nd November 1999 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in the Licence (“the Applicable Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Pangea Networks (UK) Limited) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 13th January 2000.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Patricia Hewitt,

Minister of State for Small Business and E-Commerce,

Department of Trade and Industry

11th December 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Pangea Networks (UK) Limited a licence on 2nd November 1999 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in the licence. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 15th December 1999. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Pangea Networks (UK) Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 13th January 2000.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JJ.
