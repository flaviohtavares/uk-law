# The Education (Grants for Education Support and Training) (England) Regulations 1998 (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 447

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Grants for Education Support and Training) (England) Regulations 1998 (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1999

Made

25th February 1999

Laid before Parliament

26th February 1999

Coming into force

19th March 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 484 and 569(4) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grants for Education Support and Training) (England) Regulations 1998 (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 19th March 1999.

**2.** The Education (Grants for Education Support and Training) (England) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 2(1) in subparagraph (a) of the definition of “prescribed expenditure” for “20–24” there shall be substituted “20–36”.

**4.** In the Schedule for paragraph 35 there shall be substituted the following paragraph–

> **35.** Support for additional literacy and numeracy classes for pupils in maintained schools who will be completing key stage 2 in the summer of 1999.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

25th February 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Education (Grants for Education Support and Training) (England) Regulations 1998, by substituting a new paragraph 35 in the Schedule. This relates to additional literacy and numeracy classes for pupils in the final year of key stage 2 and replaces a provision which limited such support to classes provided out of school hours.
