# The Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 79 (S.3)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1999

Made

13th January 1999

Laid before Parliament

19th January 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 23(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Order 1996

Article 2(1)(a) of the Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Order 1996[^f00002] is amended by substituting for the word “immediately” the words “commencing two years”.

Helen Liddell

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th January 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Grants for Pre-school Education (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Order 1996. Its effect is to extend the power of the Secretary of State to make grants under Part II of the Education (Scotland) Act 1996 to providers of education for children under school age so as to authorise grants to those providing education for children on or after the beginning (1st August) of the school year two years before they would first become eligible to attend a public primary school. In general terms, this extends the grant scheme to cover nursery education for three as well as four year olds.
