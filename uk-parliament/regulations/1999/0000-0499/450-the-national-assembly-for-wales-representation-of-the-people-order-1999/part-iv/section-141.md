# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 141

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 141. Prosecution of offences committed outside the United Kingdom

Proceedings in respect of an offence under this Order alleged to have been committed outside the United Kingdom by a Commonwealth citizen, a citizen of the Republic of Ireland or a relevant citizen of the Union may be taken, and the offence may for all incidental purposes be treated as having been committed, in any place in the United Kingdom.
