# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 99

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 99. Petition at issue

The Assembly election petition shall be at issue—

- (a) on the expiry of the time limited for objections, or
- (b) if an objection is made, on that objection being disallowed or removed, whichever happens last.
