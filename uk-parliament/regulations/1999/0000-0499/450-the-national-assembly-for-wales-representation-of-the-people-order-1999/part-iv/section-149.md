# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 149

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 149. Computation of time for purposes of Part IV

Article 91 applies in computing any period of time for the purposes of this Part as it applies for the purposes of Part III of this Order.
