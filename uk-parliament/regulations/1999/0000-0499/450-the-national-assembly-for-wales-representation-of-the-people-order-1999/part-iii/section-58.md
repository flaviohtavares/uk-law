# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Where no return and declarations are needed at Assembly election

Notwithstanding anything in articles 54 to 57, no return or declaration as to election expenses shall be required in the case of a person—

- (a) who is a candidate at an Assembly election, but is so only because he has been declared by others to be a candidate, and
- (b) who has not consented to the declaration or taken any part as a candidate at the election.
