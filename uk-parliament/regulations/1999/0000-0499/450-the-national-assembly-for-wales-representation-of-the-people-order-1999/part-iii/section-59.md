# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Penalty for failure as respects return of declarations

Subject to the provisions of article 61, if any candidate, election agent or registered nominating officer fails to comply with the requirements of articles 54 to 57 he shall be guilty of an illegal practice.
