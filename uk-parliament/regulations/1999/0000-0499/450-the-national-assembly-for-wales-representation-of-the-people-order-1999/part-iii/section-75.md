# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Provisions supplemental to articles 73 and 74

Nothing in articles 73 and 74 prevents a carriage being let, hired, employed or used by an elector or his proxy or several electors or their proxies at their joint cost, for the purpose of being conveyed to or from the poll.
