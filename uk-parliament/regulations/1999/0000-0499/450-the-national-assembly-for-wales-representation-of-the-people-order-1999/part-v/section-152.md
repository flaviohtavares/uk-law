# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, section 152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 152. Forms: absent voting

The registration officer shall supply free of charge as many copies of forms for use in connection with applications made under article 8, 9, 11, or 12 and Schedule 2 as appear to that officer reasonable in the circumstances to any person who satisfies that officer of his intention to use the forms in connection with an Assembly election or Assembly elections.
