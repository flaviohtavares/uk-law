# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — ELECTORS LISTS AND REGISTERS: MISCELLANEOUS AND RELATED PROVISION

Article 4(2)

ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

1

Free copies of electors lists.

2

Free copies of register etc.

3

Sales of lists and register.

4

Supply of data.

5

Supply of labels.

6

Alterations to published register.

7

Interpretation.

### 1. Free copies of electors lists

(1) The registration officer shall—

- (a) on request, and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly constituency in respect of which the request is made, after consultation with that officer,

supply free of charge four copies of so much of the electors lists as relate to that constituency, so long as those lists are kept published, to any person who satisfies the registration officer that he requires them for use in connection with his own or some other person’s prospective candidature at an election for that constituency.

(2) But not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied and the person shall not be supplied with List A (a copy of the register in force for that part) if he has already been supplied with copies of the register in force under paragraph 2(2).

(3) The registration officer shall—

- (a) on request, and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly electoral region in respect of which the request is made, after consultation with that officer,

supply free of charge four copies of so much of the electors lists as relate to that electoral region, so long as those lists are kept published, in accordance with sub-paragraphs (4) to (7).

(4) Subject to sub-paragraphs (5) to (7), the registration officer shall supply such copies to any person who satisfies the registration officer that he requires them for use in connection with his own or some other person’s prospective candidature—

- (a) as an individual candidate at an election for that Assembly electoral region, or
- (b) as a party list candidate at an election for that Assembly electoral region.

(5) With reference to the right conferred on a person by sub-paragraph (4)(a), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied.

(6) With reference to the right conferred on a person by sub-paragraph (4)(b), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied and, following such supply, the right may not be exercised again with respect to any other prospective candidate for that list.

(7) A person shall not be supplied with List A under sub-paragraph (4) if he has already been supplied with copies of the register in force under paragraph 2(3) and (4).

(8) Where it appears to the registration officer that, under regulation 37(3) of the 1986 Regulations, it is necessary to make a correction to the electors lists which involves the addition of a person’s name (or person’s names) to the register, he shall (in addition to doing what is required by that provision) send a copy of the list to any person who has received a copy of the electors lists under this paragraph before making the correction.

### 2. Free copies of register etc.

(1) The registration officer shall, on request, supply free of charge to—

- (a) the Assembly member for an Assembly constituency, or
- (b) an Assembly member for an Assembly electoral region,

one copy of so much of the register as relates to that constituency or, as the case may be, electoral region.

(2) The registration officer shall—

- (a) on request, and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly constituency in respect of which the request is made, after consultation with that officer,

supply free of charge—

- (i) four copies of so much of the register (which may be printed on one side only) as relates to the Assembly constituency to any person who satisfies the registration officer that he requires them for use in connection with his own or some other person’s prospective candidature at an election for that constituency (but not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied), and
- (ii) two copies of so much of the register as relates to the Assembly constituency to each candidate (or his election agent) at an election for that constituency.

(3) The registration officer shall—

- (a) on request, and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly electoral region in respect of which the request is made, after consultation with that officer,

supply free of charge copies of so much of the register as relates to that electoral region in accordance with sub-paragraphs (4) to (6).

(4) The registration officer shall supply—

- (a) four such copies (which may be printed on one side only) to any person who satisfies the registration officer that he requires them for use in connection which his own or some other person’s prospective candidature—
  - (i) as an individual candidate at an election for that Assembly electoral region, or
  - (ii) as a party list candidate at an election for that Assembly electoral region, and
- (b) two such copies to each—
  - (i) individual candidate (or his election agent), and
  - (ii) election agent for a group of party list candidates,

  at an election for that Assembly electoral region.

(5) With reference to the right conferred on a person by sub-paragraph (4)(a)(i), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied.

(6) With reference to the right conferred on a person by sub-paragraph (4)(a)(ii), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied and, following such supply, the right may not be exercised again with respect to any other prospective candidate for that list.

(7) The registration officer who is not the returning officer for an Assembly consituency or electoral region shall supply free of charge to that officer as many copies of so much of the register as that officer needs for the purposes of an election for that Assembly constituency or, as the case may be, electoral region.

### 3. Sale of lists and register

(1) The functions imposed on a registration officer by paragraph 1 are to be treated as part of the registration duties of a returning officer for the purposes of regulation 54(1) of the 1986 Regulations.

(2) Regulation 54(1) of the 1986 Regulations shall also have effect as if it requires, subject to the provisions of that regulation and sub-paragraph (1), the registration officer to supply to any person copies of any part or parts of the electors lists on payment, in the case of a person who—

- (a) has been supplied under paragraph 1 with copies of any part of the electors lists, or
- (b) is a constituency or a regional returning officer,

of a fee at the rate of 25p for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) names in such copy (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 54(1)(a) of the 1986 Regulations).

(3) Regulation 54(3) of the 1986 Regulations shall have effect as if—

- (a) “any election” included a reference to an Assembly election, and
- (b) “regulation 53 above” (where it first appears) included a reference to paragraph 2.

(4) Regulation 54(3) of the 1986 Regulations shall also have effect as if it requires, subject to the provisions of that regulation and sub-paragraph (3), the registration officer to supply to any person copies of any part or parts of the register on payment, in the case of a person who—

- (a) has been supplied under paragraph 2 with a copy of any part of the register, or
- (b) if paragraph (a) does not apply, is a constituency or a regional returning officer,

of a fee at the rate of 25p for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) names in such copy (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 54(3)(a) of the 1986 Regulations).

### 4. Supply of data

(1) This paragraph applies only to a registration officer who is a data controller.

(2) In this paragraph—

- “data to which this paragraph applies” means data consisting of the names of those persons included in the register, together with any data (other than data to which sub-paragraph (3) applies), consisting of information relating to them including—
  - (a) their electoral numbers,
  - (b) except where the address is excluded from the register under regulation 49(2) of the 1986 Regulations, their addresses, and
  - (c) any distinguishing letter, and
- “processed” is to be interpreted in accordance with the definition of “processing” in section 1(1) of the Data Protection Act 1998[^f00029].

(3) The registration officer shall not supply data under this paragraph which consists of information (including addresses) which is not required to be published in the register in accordance with those provisions of the 1983 Act or the 1986 Regulations which relate to the registration of electors (but this restriction shall not apply to the supply of data to a returning officer for an Assembly constituency or electoral region).

(4) Any obligation on the registration officer to supply data under this paragraph imposes only an obligation to supply such data recorded in the form in which it is processed.

(5) An application under this paragraph may request the registration officer to supply data to which this paragraph applies in respect of part only of the area for which the registration officer is authorised by this paragraph to supply such data; and, if the registration officer supplies or is required to supply data under this paragraph, he shall comply with such a request where it is practicable to do so.

(6) The registration officer shall, on payment of a fee determined in accordance with sub-paragraph (9), supply to a person, who is entitled to a free copy (or copies) of the whole or part of the register under paragraph 2(1) or (7), so much of the data to which this paragraph applies as relates to the part or parts of the register to which such a person is so entitled.

(7) The registration officer shall—

- (a) on payment of a fee determined in accordance with sub-paragraph (9), and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly constituency in respect of which the application is made, after consultation with that officer, supply to a person, who is entitled to free copies of the whole or part of the register under paragraph 2(2) or (3) and (4), so much of the data to which this paragraph applies as relates to the part or parts of the register to which such a person is so entitled.

(8) But—

- (a) where the entitlement arises by virtue of paragraph 2(2)(i) or (4)(a)(i), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied, or
- (b) where the entitlement arises by virtue of paragraph 2(4)(a)(ii), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature as a party list candidate shall be so supplied and, following such supply, the right may not be exercised again with respect to any other prospective candidate for that party list.

(9) The fee for a person supplied with data under sub-paragraph (6) or (7) shall be at the rate of £1.80 for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) names in the data supplied or £30, whichever is the greater (or such other rates as may be set out in regulation 55(8) of the 1986 Regulations.).

(10) The registration officer may, on payment of a fee at the rate of £18 for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) names in the data supplied (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 55(9) of the 1986 Regulations), supply data to which this paragraph applies to a person other than a person to whom sub-paragraphs (6) and (7) apply.

### 5. Supply of Labels

(1) This paragraph applies only to a registration officer who is a data controller.

(2) In this paragraph, “relevant information” means the name and address of any elector whose name and address appear on the register, and such other information as appears on the register as the registration officer thinks fit, but not information constituting data to which paragraph 4(3) applies.

(3) An application under this paragraph may request the registration officer to supply labels containing relevant information extracted from data to which paragraph 4 applies in respect of part only of the area for which the registration officer is authorised by this paragraph to supply such labels; and, if the registration officer supplies labels under this paragraph, he shall comply with such a request.

(4) The registration officer may, on payment of a fee at the rate of £12 for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) labels supplied (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 56(4) of the 1986 Regulations), supply to a person, who is entitled to a free copy (or copies) of the whole or part of the register under paragraph 2(1) or (7), labels containing relevant information extracted from so much of the data to which paragraph 4 applies as relates to the part of parts of the register to which such a person is so entitled.

(5) The registration officer may—

- (a) on payment of a fee at the rate of £12 for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) labels supplied (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 56(5)(a) of the 1986 Regulations), and
- (b) if he is not the returning officer for the Assembly constituency in respect of which the application is made, after consultation with that officer,

supply to a person, who is entitled to free copies of the whole or part of the register under paragraph 2(2) or (3) and (4), labels containing relevant information extracted from so much of the data to which paragraph 4 applies as relates to the part or parts of the register to which such a person is so entitled.

(6) But—

- (a) where the entitlement arises by virtue of paragraph 2(2)(i) or (4)(a)(i), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature shall be so supplied, or
- (b) where the entitlement arises by virtue of paragraph 2(4)(a)(ii), not more than one person in respect of the same prospective candidature as a party list candidate shall be so supplied and, following such supply, the right may not be exercised again with respect to any other prospective candidate for that party list.

(7) The registration officer may, on payment of a fee at the rate of £25 for each thousand (or remaining part of one thousand) labels supplied (or such other rate as may be set out in regulation 56(6) of the 1986 Regulations), supply labels containing relevant information extracted from data to which paragraph 4 applies to a person other than a person to whom sub-paragraphs (4) and (5) apply.

### 6. Alterations to published register

(1) Where a registration officer publishes a notice under regulation 58(4)(b) of the 1986 Regulations stating that a new name has been added to the list of claims for inclusion in the register, regulation 58(7) of those regulations shall have effect as if it additionally requires the registration officer, as soon as practicable after publication of that notice, to supply free of charge to each person to whom he has supplied a copy of so much of the register as relates to a particular Assembly constituency or electoral region under paragraph 2(2)(i) or (3) and (4)(a), a copy of so much of the list of claims kept under regulation 58(4) of those regulations as relates to—

- (a) claims to which that notice applies, and
- (b) that Assembly constituency or electoral region.

(2) Where a registration officer makes an alteration in a register pursuant to section 11(1) or (2) of the 1983 Act, regulation 62 of the 1986 Regulations shall have effect as if it requires the registration officer to supply free of charge a copy of the alteration to each person to whom he has supplied a copy of the part of the register under paragraph 2, or by virtue of paragraph 3(4), to which the alteration relates.

### 7. Interpretation

In this Schedule—

- “data” has the same meaning as in section 1(1) of the Data Protection Act 1998, and
- “data controller” is to be interpreted in accordance with section 1(1) and (4) of that Act.
