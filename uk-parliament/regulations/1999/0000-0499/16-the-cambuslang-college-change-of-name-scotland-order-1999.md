# The Cambuslang College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 16 (S. 1)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Cambuslang College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1999

Made

7th January 1999

Laid before Parliament

14th January 1999

Coming into force

1st March 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(4) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having undertaken such consultation as appeared to him to be appropriate, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Cambuslang College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st March 1999.

## 2. Change of name of Cambuslang College

The name of Cambuslang College is changed to South Lanarkshire College, and the name of the board of management of that college is changed to the Board of Management of South Lanarkshire College.

Helen Liddell

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House Edinburgh

7th January 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of Cambuslang College to South Lanarkshire College. That college is under the control of a board of management in terms of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 and the Order therefore also changes the name of the board of management to reflect the new name of the College.
