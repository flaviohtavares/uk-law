# The Lifecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 423

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Lifecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made

22nd February 1999

Coming into force

1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Lifecare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Lifecare National Health Service Trust established by The Lifecare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00005] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

22nd February 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the Lifecare National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1999.
