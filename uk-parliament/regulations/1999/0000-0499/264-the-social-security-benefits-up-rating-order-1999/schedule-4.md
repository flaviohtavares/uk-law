> **2A.** (1) [^f00060] The weekly amount for the purposes of regulations 17(1)(bb) and 18(1)(cc) (residential allowance) in respect of a person who satisfies the conditions specified in sub-paragraph (2) shall be—
>
> - (a) except in a case to which head (b) applies, £59.40; and
> - (b) where the home in which the person resides is situated within the area described in Schedule 3C (the Greater London area), £66.10.
