# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1999, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Sums specified for child benefit

In regulation 2(1) of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations 1976[^f00029] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i), for the sum of £11.45 there shall be substituted the sum of £14.40;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(ii), the sum specified is £17.10; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £9.30 there shall be substituted the sum of £9.60.
