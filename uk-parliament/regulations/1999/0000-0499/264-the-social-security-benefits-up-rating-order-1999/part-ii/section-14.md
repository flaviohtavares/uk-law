# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1999, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Increase in rates of age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations 1994[^f00030], in regulation 10(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for the sum of £13.60 there shall be substituted the sum of £14.05; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £6.80 there shall be substituted the sum of £7.05.
