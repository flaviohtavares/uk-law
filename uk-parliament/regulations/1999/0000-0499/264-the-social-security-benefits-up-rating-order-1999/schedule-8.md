## SCHEDULE 8 — OTHER APPLICABLE AMOUNTS SPECIFIED IN THE INCOME SUPPORT REGULATIONS

Article 18(9)

| Provision in Income Support Regulations | Specified Sum |
| --- | --- |
| Regulation 22A(1)[^f00081] | Applicable amount to be reduced by a sum equivalent to 20 per cent. of the specified amount. |
| Regulation 71(1)(a)(i)[^f00082] | 90 per cent. of the amount applicable or, as the case may be, of the reduced amount. |
| Regulation 71(1)(b)(i) | 90 per cent. of the amount of the allowance for personal expenses or, as the case may be, of the reduced amount. |
| Regulation 71(1)(c)(i) | 98 per cent. of the applicable amount for persons in residential accommodation. |
| Regulation 71(1)(d) | 90 per cent. of the applicable amount. |
| Schedule 3[^f00083], paragraph 5(3) | The relevant fraction of the applicable amount is the amount calculated by the formula<br>[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/264) |

| Schedule 3, paragraph 6(1)(c) | Nil. |
| Schedule 3, paragraph 7(8) | 100 per cent. of eligible interest. |
| Schedule 3, paragraph 8(1)(b) | Nil. |
| Schedule 3, paragraph 10(1) | The weekly amount of housing costs is the amount calculated by the formula<br>[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/264) |
| Schedule 3, paragraph 11(5) | £100,000. |
| Schedule 3, paragraph 11(7)(a) | The alternative appropriate amount shall be calculated using the formula<br>[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/264) |
