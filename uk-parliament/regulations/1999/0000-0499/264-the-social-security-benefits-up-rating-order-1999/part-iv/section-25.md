# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1999, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. The Prescribed Sum for Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 172 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations[^f00047] (trade disputes: prescribed sum) the substituted sum is £27.50.

## Annotations

[^f00047]: Footnote: *See* section 15(6) and (7) of the Jobseekers Act [1995 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/18).
