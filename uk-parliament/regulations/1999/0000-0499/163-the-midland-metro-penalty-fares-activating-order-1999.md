# The Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) (Activating) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 163

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) (Activating) Order 1999

Made

14th January 1999

Coming into force

18th January 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, at the request of the West Midlands Passenger Transport Executive, and in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2) of the Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) (Activating) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 18th January 1999.

## 2. Activation of penalty fare provisions

Sections 4 to 7 of the Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) Act 1991 shall have effect on and after 18th January 1999 with respect to travel on any Metro vehicle.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions.

Richard Bird

Director of Integrated and Local Transport,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

14th January 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect, as from 18th January 1999, to the penalty fare provisions in sections 4 to 7 of the Midland Metro (Penalty Fares) Act 1991 with respect to travel on any vehicle of the Midland Metro system.
