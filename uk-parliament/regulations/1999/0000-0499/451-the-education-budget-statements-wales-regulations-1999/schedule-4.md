# The Education (Budget Statements) (Wales) Regulations 1999, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4

Regulation 4(d)

The authority shall set out in respect of each school which they maintain those factors in Part 3 of the authority’s budget statement which apply in respect of that school together with such other information referred to in that Part as applies in relation to the application of those factors to that school. The name of the authority and the financial year to which the budget statement relates must appear at the top of the form.
