# The Education (Budget Statements) (Wales) Regulations 1999, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Commencement, citation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Budget Statements) (Wales) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 24th March 1999.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to local education authorities in Wales.
