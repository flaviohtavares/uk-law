# The Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 422

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

22nd February 1999

Coming into force

10th March 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 10th March 1999.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

For article 3 of the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] there shall be substituted the following:–

> **3. Nature and functions of the Trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be:–
>
> - (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare Trust Headquarters, Southwell Road, Mansfield, Nottinghamshire NG18 4HH and associated hospitals, establishments and facilities, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services, and there and from there to provide and manage community health services; and
> - (b) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act Newark Hospital, Bowbridge Road, Newark, Nottinghamshire NG24 4DE, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services, and there and from there to provide and manage community health services.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

22nd February 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purposes specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage Newark Hospital, Bowbridge Road, Newark, Nottinghamshire NG24 4DE.
