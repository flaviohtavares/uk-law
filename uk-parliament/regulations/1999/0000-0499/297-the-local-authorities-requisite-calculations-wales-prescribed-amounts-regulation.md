# The Local Authorities (Requisite Calculations) (Wales) (Prescribed Amounts) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 297

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

FINANCE

The Local Authorities (Requisite Calculations) (Wales) (Prescribed Amounts) Regulations 1999

Made

16th February 1999

Laid before Parliament

17th February 1999

Coming into Force

18th February 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 33(3C), 33(3D) and 113 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Requisite Calculations) (Wales) (Prescribed Amounts) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 18th February 1999.

(2) In these Regulations a reference to a numbered section is a reference to a section of the Local Government Finance Act 1992.

## 2. Prescribed proportion of council tax benefit

For each local authority in Wales for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1999 the prescribed proportion of council tax benefit for the purpose of section 33(3C)[^f00002] is the proportion specified for the authority in column 2 of the Table set out in the Schedule.

## 3. Prescribed guideline budget requirement

For each local authority in Wales for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1999 the prescribed guideline budget requirement for the purpose of section 33(3D)[^f00003] is the amount specified for the authority in column 3 of the Table set out in the Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones
