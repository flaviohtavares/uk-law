# The Financing of Maintained Schools Regulations 1999, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Discrimination by reference to category

A local education authority shall not use in their formula any factors or criteria which discriminate between schools by reference to their category under the 1998 Act or their category under the 1996 Act except where—

- (a) in relation to the period before 1st September 1999, differences in the functions of the governing bodies of schools in their categories under the 1996 Act; or
- (b) in relation to the period on and after 1st September 1999, differences in the functions of the governing bodies of schools in their categories under the 1998 Act,

justify such discrimination.
