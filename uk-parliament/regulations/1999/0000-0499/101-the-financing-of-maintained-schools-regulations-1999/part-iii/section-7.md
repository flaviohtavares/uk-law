# The Financing of Maintained Schools Regulations 1999, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Allocation of individual schools budget

A local education authority shall allocate in each financial year in accordance with this Part all of their individual schools budget for that financial year as the budget shares of schools which they maintain.
