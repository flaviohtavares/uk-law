# The Financing of Maintained Schools Regulations 1999, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Determination of budget shares

This Part applies for the purpose of determining the amount which a local education authority may determine as a maintained school’s budget share for a financial year.
