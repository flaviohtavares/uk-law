# The Financing of Maintained Schools Regulations 1999, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Timing of initial determination of budget shares

The amount of a school’s budget share for a financial year shall be initially determined by the local education authority before the beginning of that year.
