# The Financing of Maintained Schools Regulations 1999, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Additional factors or criteria

A local education authority may, in determining budget shares for schools which they maintain, take into account in their formula, as they consider appropriate, any or all of the factors or criteria set out in Schedule 3.
