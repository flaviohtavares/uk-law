# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 1

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W*

(a) These Rules may be cited as the Young Offender Institution Rules 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

- (b) The Rules set out in Schedule 1[^c-9b3fd1f894a9cfbdcbd43e11a73931c5] to this Order are hereby revoked.
