# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 75

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Control of persons and vehicles

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-0d607ad68e03acab4eeaaee2c558851c] Any person or vehicle entering or leaving a young offender institution may be stopped, examined and searched and in addition any such person may be photographed, fingerprinted or required to submit to other physical measurement.

(1A)[^key-0974a4c7bdc49dab9fe04c016a2f0c5c] Any such search of a person shall be carried out in as seemly a manner as is consistent with discovering anything concealed about the person or their belongings.

(2) The governor may direct the removal from a young offender institution of any person who does not leave on being required to do so.
