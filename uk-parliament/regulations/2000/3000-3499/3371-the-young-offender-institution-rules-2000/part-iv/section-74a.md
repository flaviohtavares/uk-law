# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 74A

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 74A. List C Articles[^c-fee4c7369ba353bfdda0b18bfd7d7fba]

*Extent: E+W*

A List C article is any article or substance in the following list—

- (a) tobacco;
- (b) money;
- (c) clothing;
- (d) food;
- (e) drink;
- (f) letters;
- (g) paper;
- (h) books;
- (i) tools;
- (j) information technology equipment.
- (k)[^c-356915c9499602928551f153a3b9ce91] electronic cigarettes;
- (l) matches;
- (m) lighters.
