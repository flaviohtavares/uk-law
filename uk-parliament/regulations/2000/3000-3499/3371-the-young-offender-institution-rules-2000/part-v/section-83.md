# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 83

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Members visiting young offender institutions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The members of the independent monitoring board[^c-2b9f28a113ccda7c3d263d9a4885ff8f] for a young offender institution shall visit the institution frequently, and the board shall arrange a rota for the purpose.

(2) A member of the board shall have access at any time to every part of the institution and to every inmate, and he may interview any inmate out of the sight and hearing of officers.

(3)[^c-d61c604e5e37bb9db1220fab989c31b9] A member of the board shall have access to the records of the young offender institution, except that members of the board shall not have access to any records held for the purposes of or relating to conduct authorised in accordance with Part 2 of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000.
