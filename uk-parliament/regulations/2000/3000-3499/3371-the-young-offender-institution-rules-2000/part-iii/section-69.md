# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 69

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Search of officers

*Extent: E+W*

An officer shall submit himself to be searched in a young offender institution if the governor so directs. Any such search shall be conducted in as seemly a manner as is consistent with discovering anything concealed.
