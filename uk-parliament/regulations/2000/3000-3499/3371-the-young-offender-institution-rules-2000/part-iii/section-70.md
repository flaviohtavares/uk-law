# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 70

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Transactions with inmates

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No officer shall take part in any business or pecuniary transaction with or on behalf of an inmate without the leave of the Secretary of State.

(2) No officer shall, without authority, bring in or take out, or attempt to bring in or take out, or knowingly allow to be brought in or taken out, to or for an inmate, or deposit in any place with intent that it shall come into the possession of an inmate, any article whatsoever.
