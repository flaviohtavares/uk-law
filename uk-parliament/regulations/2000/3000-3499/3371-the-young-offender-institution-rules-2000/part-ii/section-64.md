# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 64

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Remission and mitigation of punishments , variation of compensation requirements and quashing of findings of guilt[^key-00fa71ce36afb1c5dda2eb6d642e815f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except in the case of a finding of guilt made, or a punishment imposed, by an adjudicator under rule 60A(1)(b) or rule 65(1A)(b)[^key-248a05f534337a212f65ef34120bc74c] the Secretary of State may quash any findings of guilt and may remit a disciplinary punishment or mitigate it either by reducing it or by substituting a punishment which is, in his opinion, less severe.

(1A)[^key-190e078d6db55c3e10f37da9cd5f8e0f] Where a compensation requirement has been imposed by a governor under rule 60AB(2), the Secretary of State may reduce the amount of the requirement.

(2)[^key-7814dd160813d188f2b40e8596d5c27a] Subject to any directions given by the Secretary of State, the governor may, on the grounds of good behaviour, remit or mitigate any punishment already imposed by an adjudicator or governor[^key-7b097f1d0c7daed0f241fe8f6c7cb733].
