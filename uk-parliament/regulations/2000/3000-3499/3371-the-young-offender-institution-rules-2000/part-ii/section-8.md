# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 8

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Requests and complaints

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-980d34aa0797eb4c834563e890a79296] An inmate may make a request or complaint to the governor or independent monitoring board relating to the inmate’s detention.

(2) [^c-fec9a7949bb17f73804174b37313bf0a]... The governor shall consider as soon as possible any[^c-0a69161060e5bce87e5948f167c16863] requests and complaints that are made to him under paragraph (1).

(3) A written request or complaint under paragraph (1) may be made in confidence.
