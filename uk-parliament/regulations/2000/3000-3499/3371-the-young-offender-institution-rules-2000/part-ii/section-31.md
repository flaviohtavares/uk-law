# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 31

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Special duties of chaplains and appointed ministers

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The chaplain or a minister appointed to a young offender institution shall (or during a coronavirus period shall so far as reasonably practicable)[^c-90e2ddf779dbb13e78f0018c28698638]—

- (a) interview every inmate of his denomination individually as soon as he reasonably can after the inmate’s reception into that institution and shortly before his release; and
- (b) if no other arrangements are made, read the burial service at the funeral of any inmate of his denomination who dies in that institution.

(2) The chaplain shall (or during a coronavirus period shall so far as reasonably practicable),[^c-21006fb72972569a5659b1eb023a91d1] visit daily all inmates belonging to the Church of England who are sick, under restraint or confined to a room or cell; and a minister appointed to a young offender institution shall do the same, as far as he reasonably can, for inmates of his own denomination.

(3) If the inmate is willing, the chaplain shall (or during a coronavirus period shall so far as reasonably practicable)[^c-d59701c846359acb462df16437581969] visit any inmate not of the Church of England who is sick, under restraint or confined to a room or cell, and is not regularly visited by a minister of his own denomination.
