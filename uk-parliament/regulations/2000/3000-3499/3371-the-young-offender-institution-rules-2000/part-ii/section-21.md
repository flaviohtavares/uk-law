# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 21

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Alcohol and tobacco

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No inmate shall be allowed to have any intoxicating liquor [^c-a31eb3163fe47d4d7d9a2b9b69608c8e]....

(2) No inmate shall be allowed to smoke or to have any tobacco except in accordance with any directions of the Secretary of State.
