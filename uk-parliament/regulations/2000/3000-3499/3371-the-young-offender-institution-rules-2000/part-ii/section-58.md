# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 58

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Disciplinary charges

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an inmate is to be charged with an offence against discipline, the charge shall be laid as soon as possible and, save in exceptional circumstances, within 48 hours of the discovery of the offence.

(2) Every charge shall be inquired into by the governor or, as the case may be, the adjudicator[^c-1569f6f4c23f895a4bd253c0c6c669a1].

(3)[^c-c2db876ff69cf9665134e4e9c48a20bb] Every charge shall be first inquired into not later, save in exceptional circumstances or in accordance with rule 60A(5) or rule 65(4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0de4c9b1e765a6f0f5e78af344e6878e] , rule 65(4) or rule 58B(3), than:

- (a) where it is inquired into by the governor, the next day, not being a Sunday or public holiday, after it is laid;
- (b) where it is referred to the adjudicator under rule 58A(2) or 63(3)(b)[^c-2995a08da93a9d073559c0804085b373], 28 days after it is so referred.

(4) An inmate who is to be charged with an offence against discipline may be kept apart from other inmates pending the governor’s first inquiry or determination under rule 58A[^c-c32a207a79a6ada573b9c0147c526bc4].
