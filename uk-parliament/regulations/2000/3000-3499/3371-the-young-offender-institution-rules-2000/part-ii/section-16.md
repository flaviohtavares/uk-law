# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 16

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Visits from legal advisers[^key-6821d9a898382465677520c122e7b47b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where[^key-735fe8d3a76ee97e8441418bf09e7736] the legal adviser of an inmate in any legal proceedings, civil or criminal, to which the inmate is a party visits the inmate, the legal adviser[^M_F_e25fecc2-0cba-45c8-9d41-39eb5f86958c] shall be afforded reasonable facilities for interviewing him in connection with those proceedings, and may do so out of hearing of an officer.

(2) On such a visit,[^key-5d1d1ec8667da05a5ff771cfc2997718] an inmate’s legal adviser may, with the leave of the Secretary of State, interview the inmate in connection with any other legal business.
