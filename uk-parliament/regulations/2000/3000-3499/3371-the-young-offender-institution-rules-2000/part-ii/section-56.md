# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 56

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Defences to rule 55(10)

*Extent: E+W*

It shall be a defence for an inmate charged with an offence under rule 55(10) to show that the controlled drug, pharmacy medicine, prescription only medicine, psychoactive substance or specified substance[^key-a94ce6ee745db02a607a594971b56e9e]—

- (a) [^key-7f210350f336b629b9540ccb9cf0f397]... [^key-61377d8e65da8fc9ba24f64db8797fa1]... had been, prior to its administration, lawfully in his possession for his use or was administered to him in the course of a lawful supply of the drug , medicine or substance[^key-0993b165405b28eacc54bcbfd746b549] to him by another person;
- (b) [^key-13e8856a28df077c30813af06233a894]... [^key-63ebd74d0e25b7bd737348b20f0a9ca3]... was administered by or to him in circumstances in which he did not know and had no reason to suspect that such a drug , medicine or substance[^key-d209b26ad4428e636da585b45c6e5a17] was being administered; or
- (c) [^key-788a89dd6220a74ad4a18770a95929e1]... [^key-592dfb59f4586f51fe9956c6ac02c7f9]... was administered by or to him under duress or to him without his consent in circumstances where it was not reasonable for him to have resisted.
