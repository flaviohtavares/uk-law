# The Young Offender Institution Rules 2000, section 85

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Delegation by governor

*Extent: E+W*

The governor of a young offender institution may, with the leave of the Secretary of State, delegate any of his powers and duties under these Rules to another officer of that institution.
