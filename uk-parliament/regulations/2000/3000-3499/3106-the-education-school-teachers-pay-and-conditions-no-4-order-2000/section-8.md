# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions)(No. 4) Order 2000, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Professional Characteristics

Teachers should demonstrate that they are effective professionals who challenge and support all pupils to do their best through:

- (a) inspiring trust and confidence,
- (b) building team commitment,
- (c) engaging and motivating pupils,
- (d) analytical thinking,
- (e) postitive action to improve the quality of pupils' learning.”
