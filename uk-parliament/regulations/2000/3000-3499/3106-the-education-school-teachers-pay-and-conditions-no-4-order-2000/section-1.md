# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions)(No. 4) Order 2000, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 4) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 13th December 2000.

## 1. Knowledge and Understanding

Teachers should demonstrate that they have a thorough and up-to-date knowledge of the teaching of their subject and take account of wider curriculum developments which are relevant to their work.
