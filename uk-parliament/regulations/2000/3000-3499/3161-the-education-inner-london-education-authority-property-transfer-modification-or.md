# The Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Modification) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 3161

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Modification) Order 2000

Made

24th November 2000

Laid before Parliament

30th November 2000

Coming into force

1st January 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 187(5) and (6), 231(2) and (3)(d) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Modification) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st January 2001.

**2.** Conditions A and C specified in article 2(2) of the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) Order 1990[^f00002], subject to which the playing fields and sports centres listed in Schedule 1 to this Order were transferred[^f00003], shall cease to have effect.

**3.** The instruments listed in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be revoked to the extent shown in the third column of the Schedule.

Jacqui Smith

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

24th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order modifies the effect of the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) Order 1990 (“the 1990 Order”).

The transfer of certain land to the councils of inner London boroughs under that Order was made subject to conditions.

The Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Modification and Amendment) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/860](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/860)) provided that Conditions A, B and C specified in article 2(2) of the 1990 Order should cease to have effect save that Condition C and Condition A, in so far as it was required for the continued operation of Condition C, continued to apply to certain playing fields and sports centres.

Condition A (as amended) provided that, if the land ceased to be used wholly or mainly for the purposes of the council’s functions as a local education authority before 1st April 2010, it was to be disposed of and the proceeds divided between all inner London councils.

Condition C provided that, if the land ceased to be used wholly or mainly for the purposes of the council’s functions as local education authority or if the council wished to dispose of it, it had first to be offered for transfer to the other inner London councils. If no council wanted the land it was to be disposed of and the proceeds divided as if it were a disposal to which Condition A applied.

This Order further modifies the 1990 Order so that Conditions A and C now cease to apply in their entirety.

Various other Orders which have become spent are revoked.
