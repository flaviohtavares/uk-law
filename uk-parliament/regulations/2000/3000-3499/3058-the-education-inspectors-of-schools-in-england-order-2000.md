# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 3058

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2000

Made

15th November 2000

Laid before Parliament

27th November 2000

Coming into force

18th December 2000

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of November 2000

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 18th December 2000.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 18th December 2000.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
