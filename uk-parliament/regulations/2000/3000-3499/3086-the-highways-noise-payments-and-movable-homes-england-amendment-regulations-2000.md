# The Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2000/2887](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2887) and is being issued, free of charge, to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2000 No. 3086

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND

The Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

18th November 2000

Laid before Parliament

21st November 2000

Coming into force

23rd November 2000

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of his powers conferred on him by section 20A of the Land Compensation Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Highways Noise Payments and Moveable Homes (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 23rd November 2000.

(2) These Regulations extend to England only.

**2.** The Highways Noise Payment and Moveable Homes (England) Regulations 2000[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in regulation 3(1) (power to make noise payments) for the word “shall” there shall be substituted the word “may”;
- (b) in regulation 5(1) (limitations on noise payments) for the words “shall be £1,650” there shall be substituted the words “shall not exceed £1,650”.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Larry Whitty

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Environment, Transport and the Regions

18th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct defects in the Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/2887](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2887)) to make it clear, in accordance with the enabling powers, that noise payments under regulation 3(1) are discretionary and that the figure of £1,650 mentioned in regulation 5(1) is the maximum noise payment that may be made.
