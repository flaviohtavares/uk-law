# The Town and Country Planning (Costs of Inquiries etc.) (Standard Daily Amount) (England) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 3089

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND

TRIBUNALS AND INQUIRIES, ENGLAND

The Town and Country Planning (Costs of Inquiries etc.) (Standard Daily Amount) (England) Regulations 2000

Made

20th November 2000

Laid before Parliament

27th November 2000

Coming into force

18th December 2000

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 303A(5) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Costs of Inquiries etc.) (Standard Daily Amount) (England) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 18th December 2000.

(2) These Regulations extend to England only.

## 2. Application

(1) These Regulations apply in relation to any person, other than a person described in paragraph (2) below, appointed by the Secretary of State to hold, or as one of the persons who are to hold, a qualifying inquiry opening on or after the date on which these Regulations come into force.

(2) These Regulations do not apply in relation to any person who is appointed to conduct, or is appointed as one of the persons who are to conduct, an examination in public under section 35B(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (“the 1990 Act”)[^f00002] and whose remuneration and travelling or subsistence allowances (if any) in respect of that appointment are to be paid as mentioned in section 303A(7) of the 1990 Act.

## 3. Standard daily amount

The standard daily amount prescribed under section 303A(5) of the 1990 Act is £467.

Beverley Hughes

20th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to England only, apply where the Secretary of State is authorised to recover costs borne by him in connection with unitary development plan, local plan or simplified planning zone inquiries or other hearings or examinations in public in respect of structure plans (“qualifying inquiries”).

The Regulations specify a standard daily amount which may be charged for each day the person appointed to hold it is engaged in the conduct of the inquiry or other hearing or is otherwise engaged on work connected with it. The amount is £467 per day in relation to qualifying inquiries opening on or after 18th December 2000. This is an increase of approximately 8.6% of the previous standard daily amount of £430, prescribed in 1999 ([S.I. 1999/327](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/327)).

These Regulations do not apply in relation to a person who is appointed to conduct an examination in public, under section 35B(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, where that person’s remuneration and travelling or subsistence allowances (if any) are to be paid by the local planning authority. The Town and Country Planning (Costs of Inquiries etc.) (Examination in Public) (England) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/2311](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2311)) apply in such cases.
