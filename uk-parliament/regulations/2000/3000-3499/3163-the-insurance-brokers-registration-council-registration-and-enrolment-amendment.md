# The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 3163

INSURANCE

The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 2000

Made

29th November 2000

Laid before Parliament

30th November 2000

Coming into force

31st December 2000

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 27(1)[^f00001] and 28(1) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules Approval Order 2000, and shall come into force on 31st December 2000.

**2.** The Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 2000 made on 28th November 2000 by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of their powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977 are hereby approved.

Jim Dowd

Greg Pope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves Rules made by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council in exercise of its powers under section 8(2) of the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977.

The Rules amend the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) Rules 1978, as amended by the Insurance Brokers Registration Council (Registration and Enrolment) (Amendment) Rules 1979 and 1994, by providing that no fee shall be payable to the Insurance Brokers Registration Council, by a registered insurance broker or by a practising insurance broker, on 1st January 2001.

Copies of the Rules may be obtained from the Insurance Brokers Registration Council, 60 Lombard Street, London EC3V 9EA.
