# The South Peterborough Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2000/284](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/284) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2000 No. 876

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The South Peterborough Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

25th March 2000

Coming into force

4th April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in the exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(4) of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 5A to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South Peterborough Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 4th April 2000.

(2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the principal Order” means the South Peterborough Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the Schedule to the principal Order

In the Schedule to the principal Order—

- (a) after “Wittering” insert “, Peterborough South”; and
- (b) at the end add the following—

  > In the District of Fenland—
  >
  > - the electoral wards of Whittlesey Bassenhally, Whittlesey Central, Whittlesey East, Whittlesey Kingsmoor, Whittlesey South, Whittlesey West.
  >
  > In the District of Huntingdonshire—
  >
  > - the electoral wards of Elton, Yaxley, Farcet, Stilton.

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

25th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the South Peterborough Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000 (“the principal Order”) in order to add areas omitted in error from the principal Order.
