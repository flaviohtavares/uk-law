> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 (affected by repeal, see note) — (Annex VII of Directive (affected by repeal, see note) [99/5/EC (affected by repeal, see note)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0005))[^c-ff3638cbbdf1fc2ed13bde1cff2e35aa][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 2(3), 5(2)(d) and 10 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

### THE CE MARK (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**1.** The CE conformity marking must consist of the initials \`CE' taking the following form: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/730/images/uksi_20000730_en_001)

If the CE marking is reduced or enlarged, the proportions given in the above graduated drawing must be respected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**2.** The CE marking must have a height of at least 5 mm except where this is not possible on account of the nature of the apparatus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**3.** The CE marking must be affixed to the product or to its data plate. Additionally it must be affixed to the packaging, if any, and to the accompanying documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**4.** The CE marking must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**5.** The equipment class identifier must take a form to be decided by the Commission in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- Where appropriate it must include an element intended to provide information to the user that the apparatus makes use of radio frequency bands where their use is not harmonised throughout the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83].
- It must have the same height as the initials “CE”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
