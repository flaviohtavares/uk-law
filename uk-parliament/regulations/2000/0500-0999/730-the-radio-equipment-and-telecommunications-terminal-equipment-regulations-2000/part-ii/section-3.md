# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Scope and Exclusions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-49c4429abe7b6444d671e116b05245f2][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (4) and (5), these Regulations shall apply to all apparatus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) Where apparatus incorporates, as an integral part or as an accessory— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) a medical device; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) an active implantable medical device, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

the apparatus shall be governed by these Regulations without prejudice to[^c-3f5846dd6f4f0b07101d10f73a9dfd9b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] the application of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [93/42/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 14 June 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00022]; or Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [90/385/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0385)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 20 June 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00023]; or the Medical Devices Regulations 1994 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00024]; or the Active Implantable Medical Devices Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00025].

(3) Where apparatus constitutes a component or a separate technical unit of a vehicle, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] the apparatus shall be governed by these Regulations without prejudice to[^c-a15a3ae25dbc6abe08b819b7465446cd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] the application of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [72/245/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 20 June 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00026]; or Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [92/61/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0061)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 30 June 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00027]; or the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00028]; or the Motorcycle (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00029].

(4) These Regulations shall not apply to apparatus exclusively used for the purposes of public security, defence, State security (including the economic well-being of the State) or the activities of the State in the area of criminal law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(5) These Regulations shall not apply to equipment listed in Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
