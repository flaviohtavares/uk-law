# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Essential Requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d69cefb1f4cd1521745b60c0cdb5cf2d][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Apparatus when properly installed and maintained and used for its intended purpose shall satisfy the essential requirements set out in this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) The following essential requirements shall apply to all apparatus: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) the protection of the health and safety of the user and any other person, including the objectives with respect to safety requirements contained in Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [73/23/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0023)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00030] (but as if there were no voltage limit); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) the protection requirements with respect to electromagnetic compatibility contained in Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [89/336/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0336)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00031];

(3) In addition, radio equipment shall be so constructed that it effectively uses the spectrum allocated to terrestrial/space radio communication and orbital resources so as to avoid harmful interference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(4) When a measure has been adopted by the Commission pursuant to Articles 3.3, 6.2 and 15 of the Directive and published in the Official Journal of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] determining that apparatus shall be so constructed that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) it interworks via networks with other apparatus and that it can be connected to interfaces of the appropriate type throughout the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]; or that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) it does not harm the network or its functioning nor misuse network resources, thereby causing an unacceptable degradation of service; or that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) it incorporates safeguards to ensure that the personal data and privacy of the user and of the subscriber are protected; or that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (d) it supports certain features ensuring avoidance of fraud; or that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (e) it supports certain features ensuring access to emergency services; or that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (f) it supports certain features in order to facilitate its use by users with a disability, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

apparatus within the scope of that measure shall meet the requirements of that measure from the date specified in that measure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
