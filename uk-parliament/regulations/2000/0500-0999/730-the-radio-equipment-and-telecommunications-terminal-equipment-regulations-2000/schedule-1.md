# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — (Annex 1 of Directive (affected by repeal, see note) [99/5/EC (affected by repeal, see note)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0005))[^key-61fefacf28229aad5a9c335bc87f6e5c][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 2(3) and 3(5) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

### EQUIPMENT NOT COVERED BY THIS DIRECTIVE AS REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 1(4) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**1.** Radio equipment used by radio amateurs within Article 1, definition 53, of the International Telecommunications Union (ITU) radio regulations unless that equipment is available commercially. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

Kits of components to be assembled by radio amateurs and commercial equipment modified by and for the use of radio amateurs are not regarded as commercially available equipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**2.** Equipment falling within the scope of Council Directive 96/98EC of 20 December 1996 on marine equipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00037]

**3.** Cabling and wiring. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**4.** Receive only radio equipment intended to be used solely for the reception of sound and TV broadcasting services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

**5.** Products, appliances and components within the meaning of Article 2 of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [(EEC) No. 3922/91 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3922)[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 16 December 1991 on the harmonisation of technical requirements and administrative procedure in the field of civil aviation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00038]

**6.** Air-traffic management equipment and systems within the meaning of Article 1 of Council Directive 93/65EEC of 19 July 1993 on the definition and use of compatible technical specifications for the procurement of air-traffic management equipment and systems. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00039]
