# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4b64b3b57b935268cc6969efbdecc10f][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations (except in Schedule 9)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- “the Act” means the Telecommunications Act 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00017];
- “**active implantable medical device *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” shall have the meaning in Article 1 of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [90/385/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0385)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 20 June 1990 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to active implantable medical devices *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00018];
- “**apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means any equipment that is either radio equipment or telecommunications terminal equipment or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**the CE marking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” has the meaning given in regulation 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “the Commission” means the Commission of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83];
- “**a component or a separate technical unit of a vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” shall have the meaning in Article 2 of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [92/61/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0061)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00019];
- “the Directive” means Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [1999/5/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0005)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of the European Parliament and of the Council on radio equipment and telecommunications terminal equipment and the mutual recognition of their conformity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00020];
- “the Director” means the Director General of Telecommunications appointed under section 1 of the Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**enforcement authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” has the meaning given in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**equipment class *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means a class identifying particular types of apparatus which under the Directive are considered similar and those interfaces for which the apparatus is designed. Apparatus may belong to more than one equipment class; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**harmful interference *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means interference which endangers the functioning of a radio navigation service or of other safety services or which otherwise seriously degrades, obstructs or repeatedly interrupts a radio communications service operating in accordance with the applicable Community or national regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**harmonised standard *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means a technical specification adopted by a recognised standards body under a mandate from the Commission in conformity with the procedures laid down in Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [98/34/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00021] for the purpose of establishing a European requirement, compliance with which is not compulsory; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**interface *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means either or both of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (i) a network termination point which is a physical connection point at which a user is provided with access to public telecommunications network, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (ii) an air interface specifying the radio path between radio equipment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

  and their technical specifications; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**medical device *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” shall have the meaning in Article 1 of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [93/42/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of 14 June 1993 concerning medical devices *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00022];
- “OFCOM” means the Office of Communications established under the Office of Communications Act 2002.[^c-c535b0c17c95b42191c7b5e487e00062] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**public telecommunications networks *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means telecommunications networks used wholly or partly for the provision of publicly available telecommunications services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**radio equipment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means a product, or a relevant component thereof, capable of communication by means of the emission and/or reception of radio waves utilising the spectrum allocated to terrestrial/space radio communication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**radio waves *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means electromagnetic waves of frequencies from 9 kHz to 3,000 GHz, propagated in space without artificial guide; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**responsible person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means the manufacturer of apparatus or his authorised representative within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83], or any other person who places the apparatus on the market; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**technical construction file *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means a file describing the apparatus and providing information and explanations as to how the essential requirements (within the meaning of regulation 4) applicable to the apparatus have been met; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- “**telecommunications terminal equipment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]**” means a product enabling communication, or a relevant component thereof, which is intended to be connected directly or indirectly by any means whatsoever to interfaces of public telecommunications networks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

and any other expression used in these Regulations which is also used in the Directive has the same meaning in these Regulations as it has in the Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, a reference to a numbered regulation or Schedule is a reference to the regulation or Schedule so numbered in these Regulations and a reference— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (i) in a regulation to a paragraph is a reference to a paragraph in that regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] to an Annex is a reference to an Annex of the Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) For the purposes of these Regulations, Annexes I, II, III, IV, V, VI and VII are respectively set out in Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, and a reference to a paragraph in an Annex is a reference to a paragraph in that Annex as set out in the respective Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(4)[^c-d9b0f059d451023c25b4593bafd88d95] Except for the references to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] in the definition of “the Commission” and in relation to the Official Journal, a reference to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] includes a reference to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83], and a reference to a member State includes a reference to an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] State: for this purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]” means the European Economic Area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) an “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] State” means a State which is a contracting party to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] Agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] Agreement” means the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed at Oporto on 2nd May 1992 as adjusted by the Protocol signed at Brussels on 17th March 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83].
