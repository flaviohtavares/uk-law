# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, revocations and disapplications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-160dc9897c319c13d049b49f8686f6e0][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 8th April 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) The following Orders and Regulations are hereby revoked: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- Telecommunication Apparatus (Advertisements) Order 1982 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00003]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Advertisements) Order 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00004]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Advertisements) (Amendments) Order 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00005]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Marking and Labelling) Order 1982 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00006]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Marking and Labelling) Order 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00007]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Marking and Labelling) (Amendment) Order 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00008]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Bell Noise Labelling) Order 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00009]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Approval Fees) (British Approvals Board for Telecommunications) Order 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00010]
- Telecommunication Apparatus (Approval Fees) (British Approvals Board for Telecommunications) Order 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00011]
- Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00012]
- Telecommunications Terminal Equipment (Amendment and Extension) Regulations 1994 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00013]
- Telecommunications Terminal Equipment (Amendment) Regulations 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00014]

(3) Sections 22 and 84 of the Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [^c-db3fae92c6a9657aeb06f6a392c98ccc]... [^c-5ae5df3d31d870cd70164cbb92fa3242]... shall cease to apply to apparatus covered by these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
