# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 18

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Enforcement Notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8bac5cf877a9c10d1080e0ea9250856f][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (5),[^c-b634c781715a75e213ecce7521f4f5ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] Schedule 9 shall have effect for the purposes of providing for the enforcement of these Regulations and for matters incidental thereto. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) Except in the case of apparatus which, in the opinion of an enforcement authority, is liable to endanger the safety of persons and, where appropriate, of property, where an enforcement authority has reasonable grounds for suspecting that the CE marking has been affixed to apparatus in relation to which any provision of these Regulations has not been complied with, it may serve notice in writing on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) the manufacturer of the apparatus or his authorised representative established within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) in a case where neither the manufacturer of the apparatus nor his authorised representative established within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] has placed the apparatus on the market, the person who places it on the market in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

and subject to paragraph (3), no other action pursuant to Schedule 9 may be taken in respect of apparatus until such notice has been given and the person to whom it is given has failed to comply with its requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (2), for the purpose of ascertaining whether or not the CE marking has been correctly affixed, action may be taken pursuant to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] paragraph 8 of Schedule 9[^c-fe83a3bb4ed42faa8ea8254fc7def6cc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83].

(4) A notice which is given under paragraph (2) shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) state that the enforcement authority suspects that the CE marking has not been correctly affixed to the apparatus; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) specify the respect in which it is so suspected and give particulars thereof; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) require the person to whom the notice is given— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (i) to secure that any apparatus to which the notice relates conforms as regards the provisions concerning the correct affixation of the CE marking within such period as may be specified in the notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] to provide evidence within that period, to the satisfaction of the enforcement authority, that the CE marking has been correctly affixed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (d) warn that person that if the non-conformity continues after, or if satisfactory evidence has not been provided within, the period specified in the notice, further action may be taken under the Regulations in respect of that apparatus or apparatus of the same type placed on the market by that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(5)[^c-275431703b0e9fe318f9a6953aa02e1e] sections 94–97 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] and section 98(1)–(3)[^c-f2aeea8d381ea74ed1fca524060dffd2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] of the Communications Act 2003 shall apply for the purposes of the enforcement of regulations 7 and 13 above as if the requirements in those regulations were conditions set under section 45 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
