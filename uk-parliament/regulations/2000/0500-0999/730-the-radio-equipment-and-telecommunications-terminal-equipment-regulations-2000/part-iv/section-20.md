# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-60fb96eb8326d886779372d226cee94e][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 19(a) shall be liable on summary conviction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 19(b) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
