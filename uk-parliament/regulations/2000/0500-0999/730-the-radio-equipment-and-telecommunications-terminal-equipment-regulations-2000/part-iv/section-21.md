# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 21

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Defence of due diligence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b04d431353ca91280e2cace43d529e7b][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, in proceedings against any person for an offence under regulation 19 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] or paragraph 11(1) of Schedule 9[^c-54820a73ba9357988a49747c8ded0a81] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] above it shall be a defence for that person to show that he took all reasonable steps and exercised all due diligence to avoid committing the offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) Where in any proceedings against any person for such an offence the defence provided in paragraph (1) involves an allegation that the commission of the offence was due— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) to the act or default of another; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) to reliance on information given by another, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

that person shall not, without the leave of the court, be entitled to rely on the defence unless, not less than seven clear days before the hearing of the proceedings (or, in Scotland, the trial diet), he has served a notice under paragraph (3) on the person bringing the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) A notice under this paragraph shall give such information identifying or assisting in the identification of the person who committed the act or default or gave the information as is in the possession of the person serving the notice at the time he serves it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(4) It is hereby declared that a person shall not be entitled to rely on the defence provided in paragraph (1) by reason of his reliance on information supplied by another, unless he shows that it was reasonable in all the circumstances for him to have relied on the information, having regard in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) to the steps which he took, and those which might reasonably have been taken, for the purpose of verifying the information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) to whether he had any reason to disbelieve the information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
