# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 23

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Savings for action taken under other enactments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4516b66a4c39cddf0d6b9bc71c96b0c9][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations shall be construed as preventing the taking of any action in respect of any relevant apparatus under the provisions of any other enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
