# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Offences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-388377e2f6d439e13f0871ef3a14cca4][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7d48802fb54458e877390988246b44bc] Any person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) (subject to paragraph (2))[^key-2224886fd963ab03571e8ce9a6aeb881] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] contravenes or fails to comply with regulation 5(1) or 12; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) fails to supply or retain a copy of the appropriate documentation as required by regulation 9(3), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2)[^key-489f30b1dcdcb722ae0e3e5edac6e8e5] No offence shall be committed merely by reason of failure to comply with regulation 12 in respect of equipment which uses frequency bands the use of which by that equipment is consistent with the United Kingdom Plan for Frequency Authorisation published under section 153 of the Communications Act 2003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
