# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Notified bodies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5fb95bb352b13c9ec672cab07ae3baa6][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of these Regulations, a notified body is a body which has been— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) appointed as a notified body pursuant to regulation 15; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) appointed by a Member State other than the United Kingdom and notified to the Commission and the other Member States pursuant to Article 11 of the Directive; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c)[^c-05d3deba5bd390ce7ccb72c5a3c33bc1] recognised for the purpose of carrying out those functions by inclusion in a mutual recognition agreement relating to the Directive or a similar agreement (including a Protocol to the Europe Agreement, or other Agreement, on Conformity Assessment and Acceptance of Industrial Products) which has been concluded between the European Union and a State other than an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
