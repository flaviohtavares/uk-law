# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Transitional provisions in respect of placing on the market and putting into service (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9e435146a514dad5be2529270afe811e][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 5, apparatus may be placed on the market before 8 April 2001, and apparatus so placed on the market may be put into service, if (in either case) it is in accordance with the provisions of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [98/13/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0013)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00033], the Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^f00034], or section 22 or 84 of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
