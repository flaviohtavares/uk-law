# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Exceptions in respect of placing on the market and putting into service (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d746c437504692d23d373188e07787ff][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of regulation 5, apparatus shall not be regarded as being placed on the market where that apparatus— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) is intended to be exported to a country outside the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) is imported into the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] for re-export to a country outside the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) is transferred from the manufacturer in a third country to his authorised representative established within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] who is responsible on behalf of the manufacturer for ensuring compliance with the Directive; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (d) is transferred to a manufacturer for further processing (for example, to modify the product or to integrate it into another product, or to put his own name on the product); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

save that this paragraph shall not apply if the CE marking, or any inscription liable to be confused therewith, is affixed thereto. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2)[^c-5d8f459db71917a972bf4d71c1c64279] Subject to paragraph (3), no offence under the Act or the Wireless Telegraphy Acts 1949 to 1967, nor any offence of incitement to commit such an offence, shall be committed by reason only that apparatus which does not comply with these Regulations is displayed at a trade fair, exhibition or demonstration if a notice is displayed in relation to the apparatus to the effect *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) that it does not satisfy the provisions of these Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) that it may not be placed on the market or put into service until those provisions are satisfied by a responsible person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply in any case in which radio equipment is switched on and thereby causes harmful interference or endangers public health. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(4) The Secretary of State and the Director shall so exercise their respective functions under Part VI of the Act and the Wireless Telegraphy Acts 1949 to 1967 that the putting into service of radio equipment which complies with these Regulations is restricted only for reasons related to the effective and appropriate use of the radio spectrum, avoidance or harmful interference of public health. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
