# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 16

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3957a06e2b1c5dc2da36a665c2db90ad][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notified body appointed by the Secretary of State may charge such fees in connection with, or incidental to, the performance of its functions as it may determine: provided that such fees shall not exceed the sum of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) the costs incurred or to be incurred by the notified body in performing the relevant function; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) an amount on account of profit which is reasonable in the circumstances having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (i) the character and extent of the work done or to be done by the body on behalf of the applicant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] the commercial rate normally charged on account of profit for that work or similar work. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) A notified body may require the payment of fees or a reasonable estimate thereof in advance of carrying out the work requested by the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
