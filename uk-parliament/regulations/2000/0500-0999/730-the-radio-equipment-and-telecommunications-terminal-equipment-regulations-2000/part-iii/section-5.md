# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. General duty relating to the placing on the market and putting into service of apparatus (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-31a43390db6f5284ce4895bcb59e7658][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] paragraph (4) and[^key-a6616d82ccfe01bc6d12c9f74cd775f6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] regulations 6, 7 and 8, no person shall place on the market or put into service any apparatus unless the requirements of paragraph (2) and (in the case of radio equipment) the requirements of paragraph (3) have been complied with in relation to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) The requirements in respect of any apparatus are that: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) it satisfies the essential requirements set out in regulation 4: and without prejudice to other means of complying for the purpose of satisfying those requirements, where a harmonised standard covers one or more of the relevant essential requirements, any apparatus or part thereof constructed in accordance with that harmonised standard or part thereof shall be presumed to comply with that or, as the case may be, those essential requirements; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) the information has been provided in accordance with regulation 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) the appropriate conformity assessment procedures in respect of the apparatus have been carried out in accordance with regulation 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (d)[^key-0f8cd086e8cdb90998a98f654dd08ec9] the requirements of regulation 10 and Schedule 7 (CE and other marking) have been complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (e) a declaration of conformity has been drawn up in respect of it by the manufacturer of the apparatus or other responsible person in accordance with Schedule 2, 3, 4 or 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) In respect of radio equipment using frequency bands whose use is not harmonised throughout the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^key-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] it is also a requirement that notification of intention to place it on the market shall have been given in accordance with regulation 12. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(4)[^key-6c1484b0c02cabe91ce33e6e815a5c88] Paragraph (1) does not prohibit the putting into service of apparatus in relation to which the requirements of paragraphs (2)(b), (c), (d) and (e) have not been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
