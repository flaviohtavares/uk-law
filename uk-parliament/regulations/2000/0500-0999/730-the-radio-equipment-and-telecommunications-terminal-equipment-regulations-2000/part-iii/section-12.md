# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 12

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Notice to be given to the (affected by repeal, see note) OFCOM (affected by repeal, see note) before placing radio equipment on the market (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-59f9a8e6e527c59fe145372c7fe50248][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^M_F_2fd170f1-4a3b-4ea0-cd0e-570ba4a5a544][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The responsible person shall, not less than four weeks before the date it is intended to place on the market in the United Kingdom radio equipment using frequency bands whose use is not harmonised throughout the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83], give notice in writing to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] OFCOM[^c-f97a34b7250567fb47c8ae5d9235d8da] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] which contains— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) such information as is required by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] OFCOM[^c-697dd1b69208b8dc25986ac2d0da869f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] about the radio characteristics of the equipment, in particular its frequency bands, channel spacing, type of modulation and RF power; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) where appropriate the identification number of all the notified bodies used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) Notice given under paragraph (1) shall be effective in respect of all items of equipment, whether placed on the market at the same time or at different times, which are in all material respects identical to each other. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
