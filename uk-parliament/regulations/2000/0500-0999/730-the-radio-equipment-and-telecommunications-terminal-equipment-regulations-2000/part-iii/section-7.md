# The Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 2000, section 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. The right to connect (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-125ef70a63fb8e5149519e6d712d7b28][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83][^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Operators of public telecommunications networks *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) shall connect or permit the connection, at an interface, of any telecommunications terminal equipment which meets the requirements of regulation 4 or is equipment which was placed on the market before 8th April 2001 and complied with the provisions of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [98/13/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0013)[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83], the Telecommunication Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992 or section 22 or 84 of the Telecommunications Act 1984; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) shall not discontinue such connection lawfully made of any such equipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(2) No apparatus is required under sub-paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) or otherwise to be, or to be permitted to be, connected or kept connected if that apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) met the requirements of regulation 4 at the time when the connection was made but no longer does so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) was placed on the market before 8th April 2001 and complied with the provisions of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] [98/13/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0013)[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83], the Telecommunication Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992 or section 22 or 84 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 at the time when the connection was made but no longer does so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) causes serious damage to a network or harmful radio interference or harm to the network or its functioning; and the operator may refuse connection of that apparatus, disconnect that apparatus or withdraw that apparatus from service provided the operator at the earliest practical opportunity informs the Secretary of State and the Director of its action; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (d) is, in case of emergency, required to be disconnected to protect the network, provided that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (i) the user may be offered, without delay and without costs, an alternative solution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] the operator immediately informs the Secretary of State and the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

(3) In any case in which a public telecommunications operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

- (a) refuses connection of apparatus declared to be compliant with the provisions of the Directive; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (b) disconnects such apparatus; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
- (c) withdraws it from service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

the Secretary of State and the Director may, if they are of the opinion that the apparatus would not cause serious damage to a network or harmful interference or harm to the network or its functioning, require the operator to provide connection, reconnect the apparatus or restore it to service (as the case may be). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
