### CONFORMITY ASSESSMENT PROCEDURE REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 10(4) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

#### (Internal production control plus specific apparatus tests). (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

This Annex consists of Annex II, plus the following supplementary requirement: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

For each type of apparatus, all essential radio test suites must be carried out by the manufacturer or on his behalf. The identification of the test suites that are considered to be essential is the responsibility of a notified body chosen by the manufacturer except where the test suites are defined in the harmonised standards. The notified body must take due account of previous decisions made by notified bodies acting together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]

The manufacturer or his authorised representative established within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] European Union[^c-8478d96791f6a502fdbba1cd938a48e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83] or the person responsible for placing the apparatus on the market must declare that these tests have been carried out and that the apparatus complies with the essential requirements and must affix the notified body’s identification number during the manufacturing process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b96f530bbc126fb8d7dbaf4bec1cb83]
