# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 933

LIBRARIES

The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 2000

Made

30th March 2000

Laid before Parliament

3rd April 2000

Coming into force

1st July 2000

Whereas the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982[^f00001] (“the Scheme ”) was brought into force on 14th June 1982;

Whereas the Scheme has been varied[^f00002];

And whereas the Secretary of State, after consultation with the representatives of authors and library authorities and of others who appear likely to be affected, has further varied the Scheme;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 2000.

**2.** The variation in the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 which was made on 30th March 2000 by the Secretary of State and is set out in the Appendix to this Order shall come into force on 1st July 2000.

Chris Smith

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

30th March 2000

This Order brings into force a variation to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 30th March 2000.

The variation is set out in the Appendix. The effect of the variation is to include within the Scheme as eligible persons under Article 5 of the Scheme those authors who are resident in any country of the European Economic Area.
