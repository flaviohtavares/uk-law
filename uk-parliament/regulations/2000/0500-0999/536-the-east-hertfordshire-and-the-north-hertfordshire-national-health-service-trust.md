# The East Hertfordshire and the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 536

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The East Hertfordshire and the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made

1st March 2000

Coming into force

1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the East Hertfordshire and the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

The East Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust established by the East Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00005] and the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust established by the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00006] are hereby dissolved, and accordingly those Orders are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

1st March 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2000 of the East Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust and the North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the East and North Hertfordshire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00007].
