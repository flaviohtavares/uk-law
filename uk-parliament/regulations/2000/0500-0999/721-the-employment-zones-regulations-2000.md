# The Employment Zones Regulations 2000

The Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec][^c1619309] and section 19 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec][^c1619310], hereby makes the following Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec][^c1619311]:

## 1. Commencement and citation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

These regulations may be cited as the Employment Zones Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on the 3rd of April 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

In these regulations– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

- “the Act" means the Jobseekers Act 1995; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]
- “an action plan" means a plan setting out the action which a claimant will take with a view to securing employment or improving his prospects of securing employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]
- “an employment zone" means an area within Great Britain designated by these regulations for the purposes of section 60 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 and “an employment zone programme" means a programme established for such an area or areas designed to assist claimants for a jobseeker’s allowance to obtain sustainable employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]
- “an initial interview" means an interview with an employment officer following notice sent to the claimant to attend for the purpose of initiating participation in an employment zone programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 3. Areas designated as employment zones (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

The areas within Great Britain designated as employment zones are the 15 areas comprising the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec] districts, London boroughs, counties and county boroughs[^key-f87a28d159f48408b85dee44c80b0fc9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec] set out in the schedule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec] as the boundaries to those districts, London boroughs, counties and county boroughs stand on the 5th June 2000[^key-c912d473a60ea40b41df72293b939eb9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec].

## 4. Meaning of employment (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

For the purposes of section 60 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 “employment" means employment whether under a contract of service or a contract of apprenticeship, or under a contract for services, or otherwise than under a contract, and includes in particular self employment and the holding of an office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 5. Eligibility for participation in an employment zone programme (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

A claimant for a jobseeker’s allowance is eligible to participate in an employment zone programme if he is ordinarily resident within an employment zone on the day when notice to attend an initial interview is sent to him pursuant to regulation 23 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec] [^c1619312].

## 6. The first stage (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

A claimant begins to participate in the first stage of an employment zone programme on the day when he attends an initial interview and ceases to participate on such day as may be specified by an employment officer by notice to the claimant in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 7. Modifications to the provisions of the Act which apply to claimants during the first stage (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

In relation to a claimant participating in the first stage of an employment zone programme the requirements for receipt of a jobseeker’s allowance set out in subsection 1(2) of the Act are modified by the suspension of the requirement for the claimant to have entered into a jobseeker’s agreement which remains in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 8. The Second Stage (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

A Claimant begins to participate in the second stage of an employment zone programme on such date as may be specified by an employment officer by notice to the claimant in writing and ceases to participate on such date as may be specified by an employment officer by notice to the claimant in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

## 9. Modifications to the provisions of the Act which apply to claimants during the second stage (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

In relation to a claimant participating in the second stage of an employment zone programme the requirements for receipt of a jobseeker’s allowance set out in subsection 1(2) of the Act are modified by the suspension of the requirements for the claimant– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

- (a) to be available for employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]
- (b) to have entered into a jobseeker’s agreement which remains in force, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]
- (c) to be actively seeking employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4b86443d18d151f53af2a1df28bb83ec]

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations designate 15 areas in Great Britain to be employment zones within which employment zone programmes can be run. The purpose of such a programme is to assist participants living within a zone and claiming a jobseeker’s allowance to obtain sustainable employment. Employment is defined widely to include any paid work whether carried out for an employer or in the course of any trade, business, profession, partnership, office or vocation.

Regulation 3 designates the areas by reference to lists of wards and electoral divisions set out in the schedule.

Regulation 4 contains the definition of employment.

Regulation 5 provides that eligibility to participate depends upon living within a zone.

Regulation 6 defines a first stage of participation during which Regulation 7 relaxes a requirement of the Jobseekers Act 1995 so as to allow participants to continue to be eligible to receive a jobseeker’s allowance without maintaining a jobseeker’s agreement.

Regulation 8 defines a second stage of participation during which Regulation 9 relaxes additional requirements of the Jobseekers Act 1995 so as to allow participants to continue to be eligible to receive a jobseeker’s allowance without remaining available for employment and without actively seeking employment.

Regulation 10 amends regulation 75 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 so as to apply the sanction provisions set out in section 19 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 to defaults arising in connection with employment zone programmes.

These regulations do not impose any charge on business.
