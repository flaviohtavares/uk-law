# The Pensions Increase (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 671

PENSIONS

The Pensions Increase (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Regulations 2000

Made

8th March 2000

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2000

Coming into force

30th March 2000

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) and (4) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] hereby make the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme) Regulations 2000, and shall come into force on 30th March 2000, but shall take effect as specified in regulation 3.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations:

- “the 1971 Act” means the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971;
- “pension” means a pension within the meaning of the 1971 Act.

## 3. Pensions to which the 1971 Act will apply

With effect from 21st December 1999, the 1971 Act shall apply to any pensions payable under the Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme[^f00003] as if they were pensions specified in Part II of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act.

Greg Pope

Clive Betts

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

8th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to any pensions payable under the Scottish Parliamentary Pension Scheme. Under the power conferred by section 5(4) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 the Regulations take effect from 21st December 1999.
