# The Tax Credits Up-rating Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 931

SOCIAL SECURITY

TAXES

The Tax Credits Up-rating Order 2000

Made

30th March 2000

Coming into force

11th April 2000

Whereas, the Treasury having made a review under section 150(1) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001], a draft of the following Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with the provisions of sections 150(2) and 190(1)(a) of that Act and paragraphs 31 and 33 of Schedule 2 to the Tax Credits Act 1999[^f00002] and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 150[^f00003] and 189(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 and section 132 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00004] and now vested in them[^f00005], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Tax Credits Up-rating Order 2000 and shall come into force on 11th April 2000.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “the Disability Working Allowance Regulations” means the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991[^f00006] and the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00007];
- “the Family Credit Regulations” means the Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00008] and the Family Credit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00009].

## 3. Working Families Tax Credit

In the Family Credit Regulations–

- (a) in regulation 46(4)[^f00010] (determination of appropriate maximum working families' tax credit) the capital sum prescribed is £3,000;
- (b) in regulation 46(4), (5) and (6), the amount specified for the credit in respect of a child or young person is NIL;
- (c) in regulation 47(1)[^f00011] (applicable amount) for the sum of £90.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £91.45;
- (d) in Schedule 4[^f00012] (determination of maximum working families' tax credit), the sums prescribed shall be as set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.

## 4. Disabled person’s tax credit

In the Disability Working Allowance Regulations–

- (a) in regulation 51(4)[^f00013] (determination of appropriate maximum disabled person’s tax credit) the capital sum prescribed is £3,000;
- (b) in regulation 51(4), (5) and (6)[^f00014], the amount specified for the credit in respect of a child or young person is NIL;
- (c) in regulation 52(1)[^f00015] (applicable amount)–
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a) for the sum of £70.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £71.10;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b) for the sum of £90.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £91.45;
- (d) in Schedule 5[^f00016] (determination of the appropriate maximum disabled person’s tax credit) the sums prescribed shall be as set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.

## 5. Transitional provisions

Notwithstanding the increases of the sums payable in respect of working families' tax credit and disabled person’s tax credit made by Articles 3 and 4, where a period of entitlement to working families' tax credit or disabled person’s tax credit is running at 11th April 2000, the rate at which it is payable shall be the weekly rate in force for the period prior to 11th April 2000 until the end of the period of entitlement.

Jim Dowd

Bob Ainsworth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

30th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made as a consequence of a review under section 150 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 in relation to working families' tax credit and disabled person’s tax credit. Following the transfer under the Tax Credits Act [1999 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/10) of functions relating to both those tax credits from the Secretary of State and the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland to the Treasury, the opportunity is being taken to combine the relevant annual changes to those tax credits for England, Wales and Scotland and Northern Ireland in a single instrument.

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement, and Article 2 for interpretation.

Article 3 and Schedule 1 specify the applicable amount for working families' tax credit and the amount of credits for an adult, child or young person which determines a family’s maximum working families' tax credit.

Article 4 and Schedule 2 specify the applicable amount for disabled person’s tax credit and the amount of credit for an adult, child or young person which determines the appropriate maximum disabled person’s tax credit.

Article 5 contains transitional provisions.
