# The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 740

CINEMA AND FILMS

The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 2000

Made

15th March 2000

Coming into force

13th April 2000

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of March 2000

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by paragraph 4(7) of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 13th April 2000.

## 2. Amendment to the Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985

The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule the following–

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985 (“the principal Order ”) provides that films made in accordance with the agreements between the Government of the United Kingdom and the Governments of the countries listed in the Schedule, as substituted by S.I. [1994/3222](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3222), are to be treated as British Films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985. The present Order corrects the Command Paper reference in the list of agreements scheduled to the principal Order for an agreement with France of 8th November 1994 (Cm. 2992) and extends the list of agreements scheduled to the principal Order to include an agreement with Italy of 5th May 1998 (Cm. 4029).
