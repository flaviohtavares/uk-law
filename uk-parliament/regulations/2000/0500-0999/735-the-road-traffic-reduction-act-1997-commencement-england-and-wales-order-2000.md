# The Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 735 (c. 15)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2000

Made

9th March 2000

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(3) of the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted such associations of local authorities as appeared to him to be concerned, in accordance with section 4(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2000.

## 2. Commencement

The Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 shall come into force, as regards to England and Wales, on 10th March 2000.

Signed by Authority of the Secretary of State.

Keith Hill

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

9th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997, as regards to England and Wales, on 10th March 2000.
