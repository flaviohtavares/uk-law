# The Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Transactions) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 635

BANKS AND BANKING

The Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Transactions) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

7th March 2000

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2000

Coming into force

1st April 2000

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 4(4) of the Banking Act 1987[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Transactions) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Revocation

Regulation 7 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Transactions) Regulations 1997[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Greg Pope

Bob Ainsworth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke regulation 7 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Transactions) Regulations 1997. The effect is that the acceptance of deposits by certain retail and other co-operative societies is no longer exempt from the prohibition on the acceptance of deposits in section 3 of the Banking Act 1987 by virtue of the society’s participation in the Co-operative Deposit Protection Scheme.
