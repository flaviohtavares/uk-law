# The Petty Sessions Areas (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 675

JUSTICES OF THE PEACE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Petty Sessions Areas (Amendment) Order 2000

Made

9th March 2000

Coming into force

1st April 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4 of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Petty Sessions Areas (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Petty sessions areas

For the Schedule to the Petty Sessions Areas Order 1999[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Jane Kennedy

Parliamentary Secretary,

Lord Chancellor’s Department

Dated 9th March 2000
