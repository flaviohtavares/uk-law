# The Oxford Radcliffe Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 961

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Oxford Radcliffe Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

2nd April 2000

Coming into force

11th April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Oxford Radcliffe Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 11th April 2000.

(2) In this Order, “the Order” means the Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004].

## 2. Additional non-executive director

In article 4(1) of the Order (directors of the trust), for “5 non-executive ” there shall be substituted “6 non-executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

2nd April 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of the Oxford Radcliffe Hospitals National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 6.
