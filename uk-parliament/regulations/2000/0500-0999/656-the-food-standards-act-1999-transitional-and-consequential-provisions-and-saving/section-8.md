# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Regulations relating to food irradiation and novel foods: consequential provisions

(1) The Food (Control of Irradiation) Regulations 1990[^f00031] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part I of Schedule 6.

(2) The Novel Foods and Novel Food Ingredients Regulations 1997[^f00032] shall be modified, in relation to England, in accordance with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 6.

(3) The Novel Foods and Novel Food Ingredients (Fees) Regulations 1997[^f00033] shall be modifed, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part III of Schedule 6.
