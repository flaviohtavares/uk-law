# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Monitoring of enforcement action, etc.: consequential provisions

The definition of “relevant legislation” in section 15(1) of the 1999 Act shall have effect as if the references in sub-paragraph (a) to the provisions of regulations or orders made under the 1990 Act included references to the provisions (so far as they relate to England and Wales) of the Regulations made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00042] specified in Schedule 9 to these Regulations.
