# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Emergency Control Orders: consequential provisions

(1) The Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) Order 1997[^f00034] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part I of Schedule 7.

(2) The Food (Peanuts from Egypt) (Emergency Control) (England and Wales) Order 2000[^f00035] shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 7.

(3) The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (England and Wales) Order 2000[^f00036] shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of Part III of Schedule 7.
