# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Regulations relating to meat hygiene, etc.: consequential provisions

(1) The Meat Hygiene Appeals Tribunal (Procedure) Regulations 1992[^f00017] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part I of Schedule 4.

(2) The Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994[^f00018] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 4.

(3) The Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00019] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part III of Schedule 4.

(4) The Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00020] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part IV of Schedule 4.

(5) The Animal By-Products (Identification) Regulations 1995[^f00021] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part V of Schedule 4.

(6) The Wild Game Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00022] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part VI of Schedule 4.

(7) The Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations 1995[^f00023] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part VII of Schedule 4.

(8) The Fresh Meat (Beef Controls) (No. 2) Regulations 1996[^f00024] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part VIII of Schedule 4.

(9) The Beef Bones Regulations 1997[^f00025] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part IX of Schedule 4.

(10) The Specified Risk Material Regulations 1997[^f00026] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part X of Schedule 4.

(11) The Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) (Charges) Regulations 1998[^f00027] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part XI of Schedule 4.

(12) The Specified Risk Material (Inspection Charges) Regulations 1999[^f00028] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part XII of Schedule 4.
