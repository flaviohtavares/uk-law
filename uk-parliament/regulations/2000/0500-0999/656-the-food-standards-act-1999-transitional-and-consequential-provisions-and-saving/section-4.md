# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Regulations relating to feedingstuffs: consequential provisions

(1) The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995[^f00010] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part I of Schedule 2.

(2) The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999[^f00011] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 2.

(3) Save in so far as they relate to zootechnical products (as referred to in section 29(2)(d) of the 1999 Act), the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) Regulations 1999[^f00012] shall be modified, in relation to England and Wales, in accordance with the provisions of Part III of Schedule 2.

(4) The Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00013] shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of Part IV of Schedule 2.
