# The East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2741

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

4th October 2000

Coming into force

14th October 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 14th October 2000.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In sub-paragraph (b) of article 3(2) of the East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00004] (nature and functions of the trust), for the words “Burlington House, Lypiatt Road, Cheltenham GL50 2QN” substitute the words “Trust HQ, Number 1 College Lawn, Cheltenham GL53 7AG”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Minister of State,

Department of Health

4th October 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990 (“the Establishment Order”) which established the East Gloucestershire National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended by the substitution in article 3(2)(b) of a new address for the Trust’s headquarters premises.
