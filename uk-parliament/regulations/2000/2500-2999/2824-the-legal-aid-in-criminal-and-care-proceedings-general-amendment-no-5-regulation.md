# The Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2824

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2000

Made

16th October 2000

Laid before Parliament

17th October 2000

Coming into force

18th October 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 18th October 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) Regulations 1989

In regulation 48(13)(c) of the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) Regulations 1989[^f00002], before “a High Court judge” there shall be inserted “the registrar,”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Lock

Parliamentary Secretary

Lord Chancellor’s Department

Dated 16th October 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 48 of the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) Regulations 1989 to permit the Registrar of Criminal Appeals to make or amend a legal aid order so as to provide for the services of a Queen’s Counsel or of more than one advocate in the Court of Appeal.
