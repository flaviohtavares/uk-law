## SCHEDULE 2 — FUNCTIONS WHICH MAY BE (BUT NEED NOT BE) THE RESPONSIBILITY OF AN AUTHORITY'S EXECUTIVE

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 3(1)

**1.**[^c-d7d52de9dfb34340fe56bfd5479681cf] Any function under a local Act other than a function specified or referred to in regulation 2 or Schedule 1.

**2.**[^c-028074c6e3c1d438458afcc145c65f41] The determination of an appeal against any decision made by or on behalf of the authority.

**3.**[^c-2daed22d048468d72c0a5065ca057209] The appointment of review boards under regulations under subsection (4) of section 34 (determination of claims and reviews) of the Social Security Act 1998[^f00056].

**4.**[^c-f54a0c7caa727af627e6b42d29ca3693] The making of arrangements in relation to appeals against the exclusion of pupils from maintained schools.

**5.** The making of arrangements pursuant to sections 94(1), (1A) and (4) of the 1998 Act (admissions appeals).

**6.** The making of arrangements pursuant to section 95(2) of the 1998 Act (children to whom section 87 applies: appeals by governing bodies).

**7.**[^c-55c10bfba3ac4147b46cd7af627ccb81] The making of arrangements under section 20 (questions on police matters at council meetings) of the Police Act 1996[^f00057] for enabling questions to be put on the discharge of the functions of a police authority.

**8.**[^c-6c94082ab0806da2815ccbd400daee0d] The making of appointments under paragraphs 2 to 4 (appointment of members by relevant councils) of Schedule 2 (police authorities established under section 3) to the Police Act 1996.

**9.**[^c-c805464d55442906dd00c1664faed56b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**10.**[^c-73e6d7cab21119185c3f9b7b3f403fd1] Any function relating to contaminated land[^f00059].

**11.**[^c-e6609f540e195c3454b1609062c6c28e] The discharge of any function relating to the control of pollution or the management of air quality[^f00060].

**12.**[^c-e10e3939e68fd49dcc0c1185c27263e1] The service of an abatement notice in respect of a statutory nuisance[^f00061].

**13.**[^c-ebbb14e1374e660c5d98cba87a70fb82] The passing of a resolution that Schedule 2 to the Noise and Statutory Nuisance Act 1993 should apply in the authority’s area[^f00062].

**14.**[^c-16c2316b9f127d2c6f1ec2305f299de8] The inspection of the authority’s area to detect any statutory nuisance[^f00063].

**15.**[^c-84f035f6637bef1a22c872955458ccb3] The investigation of any complaint as to the existence of a statutory nuisance[^f00064].

**16.**[^c-9783e0edc83cc23919cef1ae7161601a] The obtaining of information under section 330 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00065] as to interests in land.

**17.**[^c-7552031f4645bde25ecca6279578becb] The obtaining of particulars of persons interested in land under section 16 of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976[^f00066].

**18.**[^c-756a4538258a8a40f362706ae9f51d74] The making of agreements for the execution of highways works[^f00067].

**19.**[^c-61ec524d43ec0801b9606fbed53624d8] The appointment of any individual—

- (a) to any office other than an office in which he is employed by the authority;
- (b) to any body other than—
  - (i) the authority;
  - (ii) a joint committee of two or more authorities; or
- (c) to any committee or sub-committee of such a body,

and the revocation of any such appointment.

**20.**[^c-9cf9fe7dd90d83ec9ebe811f3e48cfe3] The making of agreements with other local authorities for the placing of staff at the disposal of those other authorities.

**21.** Any function of a local authority in their capacity as a harbour authority (to the extent that the function does not fall within paragraph 1 of this Schedule.

**22.**[^c-995d702b24b777a4b3d8d1485e385302] Functions under sections 106, 110, 111 and 113 of the 2007 Act relating to local area agreements.
