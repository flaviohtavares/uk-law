# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2670

FAMILY LAW

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2000

Made

26th September 2000

Laid before Parliament

2nd October 2000

Coming into force

23rd October 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Part I of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 23rd October 2000.

**2.** In Schedule 1 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002], under the heading “South Eastern Circuit”, there shall be inserted “Barnet County Court”.

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 26th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 so as to include Barnet County Court in the list of Family Hearing Centres.
