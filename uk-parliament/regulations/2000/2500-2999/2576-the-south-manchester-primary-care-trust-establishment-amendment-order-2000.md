# The South Manchester Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2576

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The South Manchester Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

21st September 2000

Coming into force

1st October 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South Manchester Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st October 2000.

(2) In this Order—

- “the principal Order” means the South Manchester Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

(1) Article 8 of the principal Order shall be omitted.

(2) In the Schedule to the principal Order, after the word “Brooklands,” there shall be inserted the word “Burnage,”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

21st September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the South Manchester Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000 by removing the restriction in article 8 concerning the provision of services directly to patients and by adding another electoral ward to the area for which the Primary Care Trust is established.
