# The Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2549

TAXES

The Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

20th September 2000

Laid before the House of Commons

20th September 2000

Coming into force

11th October 2000

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 17 of Schedule 19 to the Finance Act 1999[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 11th October 2000.

**2.** (1) Regulation 3 of the Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph (b) insert

> or
>
> - (c) an employee share ownership plan approved under Schedule 8 to the Finance Act 2000[^f00003],

.

Greg Pope

Jim Dowd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definitions of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988 (S.I. [1988/268](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/268)) so as to provide that an employee share ownership plan approved under Schedule 8 to the Finance Act [2000 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/17) is not a unit trust scheme for the purposes of Part VII of the Finance Act [1946 (c. 64)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1946/64) or Part III of the Finance (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [1946 (c. 17 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1946/17)) (unit trust schemes).
