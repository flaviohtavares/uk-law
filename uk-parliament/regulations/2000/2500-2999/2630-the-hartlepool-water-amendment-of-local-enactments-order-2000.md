# The Hartlepool Water (Amendment of Local Enactments) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2630

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND

The Hartlepool Water (Amendment of Local Enactments) Order 2000

Made

20th September 2000

Coming into force

21st September 2000

Whereas provision is made in relation to Hartlepool Water plc (“Hartlepool”) in local statutory provisions;

And whereas with effect from 1st April 2000 the Director General of Water Services has made a variation under section 8 of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001] (“the Act”) replacing Hartlepool as a water undertaker;

And whereas by virtue of that variation Anglian Water Services Limited (“Anglian”) holds an appointment as the water undertaker for the whole area for which Hartlepool holds an appointment as a water undertaker;

And whereas with effect from 1st April 2000 Hartlepool has transferred properties, rights and liabilities to Anglian by a transfer scheme under Schedule 2 to the Act;

And whereas it appears to the Secretary of State appropriate for the purpose of the above-mentioned variation and transfer scheme that provision be made corresponding to provisions originally made by Schedule 26 to the Water Act 1989[^f00002] and that local statutory provisions relating to Hartlepool be amended;

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Hartlepool Water (Amendment of Local Enactments) Order 2000, and shall come into force on 21st September 2000.

(2) In this Order—

- “Anglian” means Anglian Water Services Limited; and
- “Hartlepool” means Hartlepool Water plc.

## 2. Amendment of references in local statutory provisions

(1) Any reference to, or to the undertaking of, Hartlepool in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by Hartlepool under section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to paragraph (2), as a reference to, or to the undertaking of, Anglian.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall only apply to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or to whom that provision or scheme has effect before 1st April 2000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

A. H. Davis

A Senior Civil Servant in the

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

20th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of Hartlepool Water Limited as water undertaker by Anglian Water Services Limited.
