The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated[^c16083421] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^c16083431] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 28th October 2000.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000

In Schedule 3 Part I, paragraph 1, to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 [^c16083441] after the words “as amended by Commission Decision [2000/542/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0542) (OJ No. L231, 13.9.00, p. 12)" there shall be added the following—

“ and Commission Decision [2000/651/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0651) (OJ No. L272, 25.10.00, p. 46) ”.

Hayman

Minister of State (Lords) Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

27th October 2000

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations amend Schedule 3 to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 (S.I. 2000/1673 as amended) so as to implement the requirements of Commission Decision [2000/651/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0651) amending for the second time Decision [2000/528/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0528) concerning certain protection measures relating to Classical Swine Fever in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L272, 25.10.00, p. 46). The main effect of the Decision is to remove restrictions on the export of pigs and porcine semen, ova and embryos from Essex.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been made in respect of these Regulations.
