# The Social Fund Cold Weather Payments (General) Amendment Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2690

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security Cold Weather Payments (General) Amendment Regulations 2000

Made

1st October 2000

Laid before Parliament

6th October 2000

Coming into force

1st November 2000

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 138(2) and (4) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Fund Cold Weather Payments (General) Amendment Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 1st November 2000.

## 2. Substitution of Schedule 1 to the Social Fund Cold Weather Payments (General) Regulations 1988

For Schedule 1 to the Social Fund Cold Weather Payments (General) Regulations 1988[^f00003] (identification of stations and postcode districts), there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Alistair Darling

Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

1st October 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Fund Cold Weather Payments (General) Regulations 1988 (S.I. [1988/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1724) as amended). They substitute the list of weather stations and applicable postcode districts in Schedule 1 to those Regulations with a new list which re-enacts the list in the former Schedule 1 with amendments to take account of the replacement of a weather station and changes to postcodes.

The Postcode Atlas of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the postcode maps may be obtained from their publishers. A person may establish the current postcode for their address by contacting the Royal Mail Postcode Enquiries office (tel. no. 0345 111222).

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
