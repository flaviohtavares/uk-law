# The Greater London Authority Elections (No. 2) (Amendment) Rules 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before Parliament

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 36(2), (2A), (2B), 81(7) and 82(2A) and (5A) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Greater London Authority Elections (No. 2) (Amendment) Rules 2000 and shall come into force on 4th May 2000.

## 2. Amendment of Rules

(1) The Greater London Authority Elections (No. 2) Rules 2000[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs of this rule.

(2) In Schedule 2 (the London Members Election Rules)—

- (a) in rule 41 (the local count), in paragraph (7), after the word “may” there shall be inserted the words “, in so far as he and the agents agree,”; and
- (b) after Part VII (list candidates and the filling of vacancies) there shall be added the following Part:

  > **PART VIII — Returns And Declarations As To Election Expenses**
  >
  > **59. Returns as to election expenses**
  >
  > The form of the return to be delivered by the election agent under subsection (1) of section 81[^f00003] (return as to election expenses) of the 1983 Act in the circumstances mentioned in subsection (6) of that section shall be that set out in Form 16 in the Forms Schedule.
  >
  > **60. Declarations as to election expenses**
  >
  > (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the form of the declaration required by subsection (1) (declaration by agent) of section 82[^f00004] (declarations as to election expenses) of the 1983 Act in the circumstances mentioned in subsection (2A)(a) of that section shall be that set out in Part I of Form 17 of the Forms Schedule.
  >
  > (2) In a case to which subsection (5A) of section 82 of the 1983 Act applies, the declaration referred to in paragraph (1) shall be modified as specified in Part II of Form 17.
  >
  > (3) Subject to paragraph (4), the form of the declaration required by subsection (2) (declaration by candidate) of section 82 (declarations as to election expenses) of the 1983 Act in the circumstances mentioned in subsection (2A)(b) of that section shall be that set out in Part I of the Form 18 of the Forms Schedule.
  >
  > (4) In a case to which subsection (5A) of section 82 of the 1983 Act applies, the declaration referred to in paragraph (3) shall be modified as specified in Part II of Form 18.

  .

(3) In Schedule 3 (the Mayoral Election Rules), in rule 41(8) (the local count) after the word “may” there shall be inserted the words “, in so far as he and the agents agree,”.

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Greater London Authority Elections (No. 2) Rules 2000 (“the principal Rules”).

They amend rule 41(8) of the London Members Election Rules (set out in Schedule 2 to the principal Rules) and 41(7) of the Mayoral Election Rules (in Schedule 3) so as to make these provisions correspond more closely to rule 40(B) of the Constituency Members Election Rules (in Schedule 1). These Rules also insert a new Part VIII into the London Members Election Rules. The new Part introduces Forms 16 to 18 into Schedule 5 to the principal Rules (the Forms Schedule).

Form 16 is the form to be used for the return as to election expenses which the election agent for the candidates included in a registered political party’s list (“list candidates”) is required to make under section 81(1) of the Representation of the People Act 1983 in connection with the election of the London members of the London Assembly.

The form set out in Part I of Form 17 is to be used by election agents for the declaration as to election expenses of list candidates at elections of the London members. Where a list candidate is the election agent for himself and the other list candidates, the declaration is modified as specified in Part II of Form 17.

The form set out in Part I of Form 18 is to be used by list candidates for the declaration as to election expenses at elections of the London members. Where a list candidate is the election agent for himself and the other list candidates, the declaration is modified as specified in Part II of Form 18.
