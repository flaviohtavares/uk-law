# The Employment Tribunals Act (Application of Conciliation Provisions) Order 2000 (Revocation) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1336

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Employment Tribunals Act (Application of Conciliation Provisions) Order 2000 (Revocation) Order 2000

Made

16th May 2000

Laid before Parliament

17th May 2000

Coming into force

19th May 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(8)(a) and (b) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Employment Tribunals Act (Application of Conciliation Provisions) Order 2000 (Revocation) Order 2000 and comes into force on 19th May 2000.

## 2. Revocation

The Employment Tribunals Act (Application of Conciliation Provisions) Order 2000[^f00002] is revoked.

Alan Johnson,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competitiveness,

Department of Trade and Industry

16th May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 19th May 2000, revokes the Employment Tribunals Act (Application of Conciliation Provisions) Order 2000. The reason is that the revoked Order contains a discrepancy between the title in the heading and the title, set out in Article 1, by which the Order may be cited.
