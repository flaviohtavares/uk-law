# The Railways (Alternative Closure Procedure) (Croydon Tramlink) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1144

LONDON GOVERNMENT

TRANSPORT, ENGLAND

The Railways (Alternative Closure Procedure) (Croydon Tramlink) Order 2000

Made

20th April 2000

Laid before Parliament

4th May 2000

Coming into force

3rd July 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 5A of Schedule 5 to the Railways Act 1993[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Railways (Alternative Closure Procedure) (Croydon Tramlink) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 3rd July 2000.

**2.** In this Order “Tramlink” has the same meaning as in section 2(1) of the Croydon Tramlink Act 1994[^f00002].

**3.** The Secretary of State hereby designates all services on Tramlink as services in relation to which paragraph 5A of Schedule 5 to the Railways Act 1993 is to apply.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Keith Hill

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

20th April 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates services on Croydon Tramlink as qualifying London services for the purposes of Schedule 5 to the Railways Act 1993, which contains provisions relating to the discontinuance of passenger services. Qualifying London services are services in respect of which the Mayor is to exercise certain powers instead of the Secretary of State.
