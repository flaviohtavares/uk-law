# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Revocation) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1314

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Revocation) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2000

Made

12th May 2000

Laid before Parliament

15th May 2000

Coming into force

17th May 2000

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 104, 112(1) and (5) and 115 of and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 7 to the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001] and considering it necessary to do so in consequence of the provisions of the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Revocation) (Scotland) Order 2000[^f00002] makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Revocation) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2000; it extends to England, Wales and Northern Ireland only and comes into force on 17th May 2000.

## 2. Revocation

Insofar as it extends to England, Wales and Northern Ireland, the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

12th May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

**1.** This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Oil and Chemical Pollution of Fish) (No. 2) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/143](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/143)) insofar as it extends to England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

**2.** That Order—

- (a) designated the area described in the Schedule thereto (a specified area of sea around the Shetland Islands) for the puposes of Part I of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 ([1985 c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/48) (article 3);
- (b) prohibited fishing for or taking fish within that area (article 4);
- (c) prohibited the movement of fish out of that area (article 5);
- (d) imposed restrictions throughout the United Kingdom in relation to the use of any fish taken from that area (article 6).

**3.** S.I. [1993/143](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/143) had been partially revoked by S.I. [1993/1151](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1151), S.I. [1993/3058](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3058), S.I. [1994/2555](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2555) and S.I. [1995/292](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/292) in relation to the United Kingdom as a whole and only remained in force in relation to restictions on fishing for mussels (*Mytilis edulis*) and Norway lobsters (*Nephrops norvegicus*). By means of Scottish S.I. [2000/132](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/132) the Scottish Ministers have revoked S.I. [1993/143](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/143) insofar as it extends to Scotland.

**4.** No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in respect of this Order.
