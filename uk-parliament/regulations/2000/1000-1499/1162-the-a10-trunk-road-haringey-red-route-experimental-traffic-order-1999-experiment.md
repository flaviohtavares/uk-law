# The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1162

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 2000

Made

25th April 2000

Coming into force

4th May 2000

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 9 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999 Experimental Variation Order 2000, and shall come into force on 4th May 2000.

## 2. Variations

The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1999[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.
