# The Education (School Performance Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1089

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Performance Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

17th April 2000

Laid before Parliament

18th April 2000

Coming into force

15th May 2000

In exercise of powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 537 and 569(4) and (5) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], and after consulting with those persons with whom consultation appeared to him to be desirable, the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Performance Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000, and shall come into force on 15th May 2000.

## 2. Amendment

For Regulation 9 of the Education (School Performance Information) (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002], there shall be substituted—

> **9. Provision of information about authorised and unauthorised absence to the Secretary of State**
>
> (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the governing body of every maintained school and the proprietor of every non-maintained special school or independent school shall, within two weeks of receiving a written request by the Secretary of State, provide him with the information for the reporting school year specified in Schedule 4.
>
> (2) This regulation does not apply in respect of a school providing education for boarding pupils only.

Jacqui Smith

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

17th April 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations replace regulation 9 of the Education (School Performance Information) (England) Regulations 1999 (“the principal Regulations”) which relate to the collection and publication of information about the performance of schools.

The substituted regulation 9 requires the governing body of every maintained school to provide the information referred to in Schedule 4 of the principal regulations directly to the Secretary of State within two weeks of receiving a written request from him. This replaces the duty under the old regulation 9 to provide that information to the local education authority, and the authority’s duty to pass this on to the Secretary of State.

The substituted regulation preserves the duty on the proprietor of a non-maintained special school or an independent school to provide the same information to the Secretary of State.
