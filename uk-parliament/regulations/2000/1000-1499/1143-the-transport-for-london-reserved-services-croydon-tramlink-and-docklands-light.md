# The Transport for London (Reserved Services) (Croydon Tramlink and Docklands Light Railway) Exception Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1143

LONDON GOVERNMENT

TRANSPORT, ENGLAND

The Transport for London (Reserved Services) (Croydon Tramlink and Docklands Light Railway) Exception Order 2000

Made

20th April 2000

Laid before Parliament

4th May 2000

Coming into force

3rd July 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 207(5) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transport for London (Reserved Services) (Croydon Tramlink and Docklands Light Railway) Exception Order 2000 and shall come into force on 3rd July 2000.

**2.** In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Greater London Authority Act 1999;
- “Tramlink” has the same meaning as in section 2(1) of the Croydon Tramlink Act 1994[^f00002]; and
- “the Docklands Light Railway” means the railways authorised by the London Docklands Railway Act 1984[^f00003], the London Docklands Railway Act 1985[^f00004], the London Docklands Railway (City Extension) Act 1986[^f00005], the London Regional Transport Act 1989[^f00006], the London Docklands Railway (Beckton) Act 1989[^f00007], the London Docklands Railway Act 1991[^f00008] and the London Docklands Railway (Lewisham) Act 1993[^f00009].

**3.** The Secretary of State hereby excepts from section 207(2) of the Act all agreements under which an outside contractor is to provide, or secure the provision of, any reserved service in respect of Tramlink or Docklands Light Railway.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Keith Hill

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

20th April 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order excepts all agreements in respect of Croydon Tramlink and Docklands Light Railway, under which station or train operating functions are provided by a person other than Transport for London or its subsidiaries, from the requirement that the Secretary of State’s consent be obtained before they are entered into or carried out.
