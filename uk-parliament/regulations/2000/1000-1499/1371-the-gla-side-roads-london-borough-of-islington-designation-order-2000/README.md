# The GLA Side Roads (London Borough of Islington) Designation Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1371

LONDON GOVERNMENT

ROAD TRAFFIC

The GLA Side Roads (London Borough of Islington) Designation Order 2000

Made

18th May 2000

Laid before Parliament

24th May 2000

Coming into force

19th June 2000

Whereas the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions considers it appropriate for certain roads in the London borough of Islington to be GLA side roads in the interests of the management of traffic and the control of the waiting and loading of vehicles on or in the immediate vicinity of GLA roads:

Now therefore the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 124A of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:—

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 272 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 inserts a new section 124A into the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 whereby the Secretary of State may designate roads or proposed roads which are to be GLA side roads, for which Transport for London will be the traffic authority. This Order designates the roads in the London Borough of Islington specified in the Schedule as GLA side roads with effect from 3rd July 2000.
