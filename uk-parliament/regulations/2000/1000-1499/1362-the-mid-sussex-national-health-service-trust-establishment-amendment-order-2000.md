# The Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1999/199](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/199) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2000 No. 1362

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

15th May 2000

Coming into force

26th May 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 26th May 2000.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

Article 3(2)(c) of the Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00004] is amended by substituting for the entry “Stead Resource Centre” to the end the words “Stead Resource Centre, 50 Victoria Road, Burgess Hill, West Sussex RH15 9LW.”

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

15th May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994 (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Mid-Sussex National Health Service Trust.

Article 3(2)(c) of the Establishment Order is amended to correct the details of the Stead Resource Centre site, described defectively in [S.I. 1999/199](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/199).
