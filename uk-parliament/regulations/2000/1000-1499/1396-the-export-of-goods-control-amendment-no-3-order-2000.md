# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1396

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2000

Made

22nd May 2000

Coming into force

24th May 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 24th May 2000.

**2.** In Schedule 3 to the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1994[^f00003], “Zimbabwe” shall be inserted after “Yugoslavia (Federal Republic of)”.

Stephen Byers

Secretary of State for Trade and Industry

22nd May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1994 by adding Zimbabwe to the list of countries to which arms and other military goods in transit through the United Kingdom may not be exported without a licence.
