**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the International Transport of Goods under Cover of TIR Carnets (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 28th June 2000.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the International Transport of Goods under Cover of TIR Carnets (Fees) Regulations 1988 as follows.

The fee for an inspection of a vehicle design-type in connection with the grant of TIR design-type approval is increased from £64.60 to £68 where the inspection is of a design-type which is a variation of a design-type for which a TIR design-type approval has been granted and from £396.20 to £420 in any other case.

The fee for the issue of a TIR vehicle approval certificate for a vehicle which is of a design-type for which TIR design-type approval has been granted is increased from £5.70 to £10. The fee for the issue of a duplicate of such certificate is increased from £5.70 to £10.

The fee for an inspection of a vehicle in connection with the issue of a TIR vehicle approval certificate is increased from £43.70 to £46 where the inspection is carried out following refusal of such a certificate for the vehicle and from £62.60 to £66 in any other case.
