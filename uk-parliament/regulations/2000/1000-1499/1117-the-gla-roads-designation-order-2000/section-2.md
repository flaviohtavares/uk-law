# The GLA Roads Designation Order 2000, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Designation of GLA roads

(1) The highways specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as GLA roads.

(2) In accordance with section 14A(2) of the Highways Act 1980 3rd July 2000 is hereby specified as the date on which those highways are to become GLA roads.
