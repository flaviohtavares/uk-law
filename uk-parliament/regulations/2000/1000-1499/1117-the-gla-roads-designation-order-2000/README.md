(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 260 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 inserts a new section 14A into the Highways Act 1980 whereby the Secretary of State may designate highways or proposed highways which are to be GLA roads, for which Transport for London will be the highway authority. This Order designates the highways specified in the Schedule as GLA roads with effect from 3rd July 2000.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [1980 c. 66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/66); section 14A was inserted by section 260 of the Greater London Authority Act [1999 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/29) (“the 1999 Act”). For the definition of GLA road *see* the amendments made by section 263 of the 1999 Act. Section 14A(3) provides that orders under section 14A may be made only before the beginning of the term of office of the first Mayor of London. In accordance with section 3(1) of the 1999 Act the first election will be held on 4th May. The first Mayor will thereafter take office in accordance with section 2(8) of the 1999 Act.
