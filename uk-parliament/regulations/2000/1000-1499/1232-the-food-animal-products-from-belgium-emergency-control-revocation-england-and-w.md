# The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) (England and Wales) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1232

FOOD, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) (England and Wales) Order 2000

Made

7th May 2000

Laid before Parliament

8th May 2000

Coming into force

12th May 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 6(4), 13(1) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

This Order may be cited as the Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) (England and Wales) Order 2000, shall come into force on 12th May 2000 and shall extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Revocation

The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (England and Wales) Order 2000[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

7th May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies to England and Wales, revokes the Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (England and Wales) Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/587](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/587)) (“the revoked Order”), and implements in England and Wales, in relation to food, Commission Decision [2000/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0301) repealing the protective measures with regard to contamination by dioxins of certain products of porcine and poultry origin intended for human or animal consumption (OJ No. L97, 19.4.2000, p. 16). (The protective measures concerned were contained in Commission Decision [1999/788/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0788) (OJ No. L310, 4.12.1999, p. 62) as amended by Commission Decision [2000/150/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0150) (OJ No. L50, 23.2.2000, p. 25) and were implemented in England and Wales, in relation to food, by the revoked Order).
