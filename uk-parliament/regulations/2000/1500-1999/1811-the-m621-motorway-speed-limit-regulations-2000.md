# The M621 Motorway (Speed Limit) Regulations 2000

ROAD TRAFFIC

The M621 Motorway (Speed Limit) Regulations 2000

Made

10th July 2000

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2000

Coming into force

11th August 2000

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17(2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement[^key-c1f71f6eaeea11c8dd636bff8928cfc4]

These Regulations may be cited as the M621 Motorway (Speed Limit) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 11th August 2000.

## 2. Imposition of Speed Limit[^key-1c462dcea4904daa1e0e1f2ecbb4461a]

(1) No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 50 miles per hour on the lengths of road specified in Schedule 1 to these Regulations;

(2) No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 40 miles per hour on the lengths of road specified in Schedule 2 to these Regulations;

(3) No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 30 miles per hour on the lengths of road specified in Schedule 3 to these Regulations.

## 3. Revocation and Amendment[^key-f4e38c51e71794d67f170686e0e3124b]

(1) The Motorways Traffic (Speed Limit) Regulations 1974[^f00002] shall be amended by the omission of paragraphs 16 to 26 in Schedule 2 to those Regulations;

(2) The Motorways Traffic (M621 Motorway) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1983[^f00003] and the Motorways Traffic (Leeds South East Urban Motorway) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1973[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose revised speed limits on the slip roads to (and specified lengths of) the M621 Motorway at Leeds, to take account of improvements and realignments. They replace speed limits previously imposed under Regulations which are revoked, in whole or in part. The principal provisions are as follows:—

- (a) regulation 2(1) and Schedule 1 of these Regulations impose a 50 miles per hour speed limit on specified lengths of the motorway and slip roads comprised in the M621;
- (b) regulation 2(2) and Schedule 2 of these Regulations impose a 40 miles per hour speed limit on specified slip roads comprised in the M621;
- (c) regulation 2(3) and Schedule 3 of these Regulations impose a 30 miles per hour speed limit on specified slip roads comprised in the M621;
- (d) regulation 3(1) of these Regulations revokes paragraphs 16 to 26 of Schedule 2 to the Motorways Traffic (Speed Limit) Regulations 1974 (which imposed a 50 miles per hour speed limit on specified lengths of motorway and slip roads comprised in the M621);
- (e) regulation 3(2) of these Regulations revokes the Motorways Traffic (M621 Motorways) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1983 and the Motorways Traffic (Leeds South East Urban Motorway) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1973 which imposed speed limits of 30, 40 and 50 miles per hour on specified lengths of the motorway and slip roads comprised in the M621.
