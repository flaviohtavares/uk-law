# The Education (Funding for Teacher Training) Designation (No. 2) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1750

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Funding for Teacher Training) Designation (No. 2) Order 2000

Made

5th July 2000

Laid before Parliament

6th July 2000

Coming into force

1st August 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 4(2) of the Education Act 1994[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Funding for Teacher Training) Designation (No. 2) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st August 2000.

**2.** The institutions and bodies referred to in the Schedule are hereby designated as institutions eligible for funding under Part I of the Education Act 1994.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

5th July 2000

As a result of this Order, the Teacher Training Agency may make grants, loans or other payments in respect of expenditure incurred or to be incurred by the designated institutions for the purposes of the provision of teacher training and the provision of facilities, and the carrying on of other activities, which the institutions consider it necessary or desirable to provide or carry on for the purpose of or in connection with the provision of teacher training.
