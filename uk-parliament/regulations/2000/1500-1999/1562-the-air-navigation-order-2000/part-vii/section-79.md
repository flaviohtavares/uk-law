# The Air Navigation Order 2000, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Power to inspect and copy documents and records

An authorised person shall have the power to inspect and copy any certificate, licence, log book, document or record which he has the power pursuant to this Order and any regulations made thereunder to require to be produced to him.
