# The Air Navigation Order 2000, section 134

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 134. Competent authority

- (a) The CAA shall be the authority responsible in the United Kingdom for carrying out the functions of the Authority under JAR-145.
- (b) The Secretary of State shall be the competent authority under article 15 of Council Directive 96/29/Euratom of 13th May 1996[^f00030] for the purposes of article 42 of the Directive.
