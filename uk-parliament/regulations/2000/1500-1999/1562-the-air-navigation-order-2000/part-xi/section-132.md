# The Air Navigation Order 2000, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Exceptions for certain classes of aircraft

The provisions of this Order other than articles 58, 64, 85(1), 86, 87, 118(1)(b), 129(1), (2) and (5) shall not apply to or in relation to:

- (a) any small balloon;
- (b) any kite weighing not more than 2 kg;
- (c) any small aircraft; or
- (d) any parachute, including a parascending parachute.
