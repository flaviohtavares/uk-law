# The Air Navigation Order 2000, section 133

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 133. Approval of persons to furnish reports

In relation to any of its functions pursuant to any of the provisions of this Order the CAA may, either absolutely or subject to such conditions as it thinks fit, approve a person as qualified to furnish reports to it and may accept such reports.
