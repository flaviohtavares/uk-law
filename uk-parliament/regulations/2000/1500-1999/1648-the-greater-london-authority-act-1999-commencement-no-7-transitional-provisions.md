# The Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 7, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1648 (c. 44)

LONDON GOVERNMENT

POLICE

The Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 7, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order 2000

Made

21st June 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 420 and 425 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 7, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order 2000.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Greater London Authority Act 1999; and “the Receiver” means the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of the Schedule shall come into force on 3rd July 2000, where applicable, to the extent specified or subject to the transitional provision made in the second column.

## 3. Amendment of earlier commencement order

(1) The Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4 and Adaptation) Order 2000[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In the second column of Part 3 of the Schedule, opposite the entry for Part IV Chapter XIV, before “in so far as not already in force”, there shall be inserted “with the exception of section 287(2)”.

Lord Steve Bassam

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

21st June 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force on 3rd July 2000 the provisions of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 which relate to the Metropolitan Police Authority and members of the metropolitan police force in so far as they are not already in force. It also brings into force various consequential amendments and repeals subject to the transitional provisions set out in the Schedule to the Order. Where amendments and repeals relate to the functions of the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District, the provisions are commenced only in relation to his functions for police purposes.

Article 3 amends the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4 and Adaptation) Order 2000 so that section 287(2) which amends the definition of “London authority” in Part II (traffic in London) of the Road Traffic Act 1991, does not come into force on 3rd July 2000 as provided in that Order.
