# The Local Government Act 1999 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1724 (c. 45)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Government Act 1999 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2000

Made

29th June 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(2)(a) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1999 (Commencement No. 3) (England) Order 2000.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 1(1)(f), (i), (j), 1(2)(d), 1(4)(c) and 2(4) of the Local Government Act 1999 shall come into force on 3rd July 2000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

29th June 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd July 2000, those provisions in Part I of the Local Government Act 1999 (Best Value) which are not already in force[^f00002][^f00003]. These are the provisions which make the Greater London Authority (GLA) and its functional bodies best value authorities for the purposes of that Part. The GLA’s functional bodies are the London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority, Transport for London, the London Development Agency and the Metropolitan Police Authority.
