# The Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1749

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made

4th July 2000

Coming into force

1st October 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st October 2000.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust established by the Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

4th July 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st October 2000 of the Herefordshire Community Health National Health Service Trust.
