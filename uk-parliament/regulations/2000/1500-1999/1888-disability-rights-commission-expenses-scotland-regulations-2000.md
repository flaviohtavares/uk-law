# Disability Rights Commission (Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1888 (S. 6)

DISABLED PERSONS, SCOTLAND

Disability Rights Commission (Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

Made

17th July 2000

Laid before Parliament

25th July 2000

Coming into force

22nd August 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 8(5) of the Disability Rights Commission Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Disability Rights Commission (Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 22nd August 2000.

(2) In these Regulations, “the 1999 Act” means the Disability Rights Commission Act 1999.

(3) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Taxation of Commission Expenses

(1) This regulation applies in cases where section 8 of the 1999 Act applies.

(2) The expenses incurred by the Commission within the meaning of section 8(2) of the 1999 Act shall be taxed or assessed by the auditor of the sheriff court in which the proceedings in relation to which the Commission provided assistance to an individual under section 7 of the 1999 Act were taken or would have been taken but for any compromise or settlement, as if they were outlays incurred by a solicitor on behalf of the individual.

Margaret Hodge

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment and Equal Opportunities

17th July 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations, which relate to Scotland only, provide for determination by the auditor of the sheriff court of the expenses incurred by the Disability Rights Commission in providing assistance under section 7 of the Disability Rights Commission Act 1999 in relation to proceedings which an individual brought or proposed to bring in the sheriff court.
