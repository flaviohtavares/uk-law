# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1640

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 2000

Made

18th June 2000

Coming into force

9th July 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(1) and (2) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 9th July 2000.

## 2. Establishment of safety zones

A safety zone is hereby established around each installation specified in column 1 of the Schedule hereto (being an installation stationed in waters to which subsection (7) of section 21 of the Act applies) having a radius of five hundred metres from the point as respects that installation which has the co-ordinates of latitude and longitude according to European Datum (1950) specified in columns 2 and 3 of the Schedule.

## 3. Removal of safety zone

In Schedule 1 to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1999[^f00002] the entry relating to Gryphon Field is deleted.

## 4. Amendment of the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones)(No.3) Order 1999

In the Schedule to the Offshore installations (Safety Zones)(No.3) Order 1999[^f00003]—

- (a) in the first entry relating to Balmoral Satellite Well, for the words “01°08'05"” in column (3) there shall be substituted the words “01°08'03"”;
- (b) in the second entry relating to Balmoral Satellite Well—
  - (i) for the words “58°13'76"” in column (2) there shall be substituted the words “58°13'46"”;
  - (ii) for the words “01°05'25"” in column (3) there shall be substituted the words “01°05'15"”.

(This note is not part of the order)

This order establishes, under section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987, safety zones having a radius of 500 metres from the specified point, around each installation specified in the Schedule to this order and stationed in waters to which section 21(7) of that Act applies (these include territorial waters and waters in areas designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29) *(article 2)*.

Vessels, which for this purpose include hovercraft, submersible apparatus and installations in transit, are prohibited from entering or remaining in a safety zone except with the consent of the Health and Safety Executive or in accordance with regulations made under section 23(1) of the Act (currently the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Regulations [1987/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1331)).

The order removes the safety zone in respect of Gryphon Field by deleting the entry relating to it in Schedule 1 to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order [1999/547](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/547) *(article 3)*, because the installation has been abandoned.

The order amends the Schedule to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 3) Order [1999/2206](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2206) to correct the co-ordinates for two installations, each designated as Balmoral Satellite Well *(article 4)*.
