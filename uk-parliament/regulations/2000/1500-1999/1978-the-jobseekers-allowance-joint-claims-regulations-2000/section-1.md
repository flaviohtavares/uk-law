# The Jobseeker’s Allowance (Joint Claims) Regulations 2000, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations shall be cited as the Jobseeker’s Allowance (Joint Claims) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 19th March 2001.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00005].
