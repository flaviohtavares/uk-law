# Electronic Communications Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1798 (C. 46)

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

Electronic Communications Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000

Made

6th July 2000

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 16(2) of the Electronic Communications Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Electronic Communications Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000.

## 2. Appointed Day

Sections 7, 11 and 12 of the Electronic Communications Act 2000 shall come into force on 25th July 2000.

Patricia Hewitt,

Minister of State for Small Business and E Commerce,

Department of Trade and Industry

6th July 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 7, 11 and 12 of the Electronic Communications Act 2000 on 25th July 2000. Part I of the Act has not yet been brought into force. All other provisions came into force at the time of Royal Assent.
