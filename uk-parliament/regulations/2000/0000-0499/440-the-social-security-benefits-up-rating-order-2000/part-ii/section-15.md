# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2000, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995[^f00034], in regulation 18(2)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for the sum of £14.05 there shall be substituted the sum of £14.20;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £8.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £9.00; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for the sum of £4.45 there shall be substituted the sum of £4.50.
