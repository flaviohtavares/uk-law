# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2000, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Increase in rates of certain workmen’s compensation and industrial diseases benefits in respect of employment before 5th July 1948

In Schedule 8 to the Contributions and Benefits Act the sum of £39.95 referred to in paragraph 2(6)(c) (maximum weekly rate of lesser incapacity allowance supplementing workmen’s compensation) and paragraph 6(2)(b) (industrial diseases benefit schemes: weekly rate of allowance payable where disablement is not total) shall be increased; and from and including 12th April 2000 for that sum in paragraph 2(6)(c), and from and including 13th April 2000 for that sum in paragraph 6(2)(b), there shall be substituted the sum of £40.40.
