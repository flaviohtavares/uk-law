# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2000, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Earnings Limits

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases)–

- (a) the sum of £145.00 in both places where it occurs remains unchanged; and
- (b) the sum of £19.00 remains unchanged.
