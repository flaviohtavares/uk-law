# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2000, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Increase in rates of Disability Living Allowance

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991[^f00031] (rate of benefit)–

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for the sum of £52.95 there shall be substituted the sum of £53.55;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b), for the sum of £35.40 there shall be substituted the sum of £35.80;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(c), for the sum of £14.05 there shall be substituted the sum of £14.20;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(a), for the sum of £37.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £37.40; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(b), for the sum of £14.05 there shall be substituted the sum of £14.20.
