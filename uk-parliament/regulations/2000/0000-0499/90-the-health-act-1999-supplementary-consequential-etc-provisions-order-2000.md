The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 63 of the Health Act 1999[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-ee90e06c8d8a2082abddfe110bbd595c]

This Order may be cited as the Health Act 1999 (Supplementary, Consequential etc. Provisions) Order 2000 and shall come into force for the purposes of article 3(2) on 1st April 2000 and for all other purposes on 8th February 2000.

## 2. Extent and interpretation[^c-6a71626aa2758ec485c79398b6fe4ad1]

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs, this Order extends only to England and Wales.

(2) Article 1, this Article, paragraphs 3, 5, 8 and 29 of Schedule 1[^c-39eae2bb9ac3b8031c62247b067a7f94] to this Order and article 3(1), in so far as it relates to those paragraphs, extend to the whole of the United Kingdom.

(3) Paragraphs 9, 27 and 30 of Schedule 1[^c-4339f3c3f3c3d126282bed1dd6a6bd79] to this Order and article 3(1), in so far as it relates to those paragraphs, extend to England and Wales and to Scotland, but not to Northern Ireland.

(4) Paragraphs 2 and 4 of Schedule 2 to this Order and article 3(2), in so far as it relates to those paragraphs, extend to England and Wales and to Scotland, but not to Northern Ireland.

#### The Ashworth, Broadmoor and Rampton Hospital Authorities (Functions and Membership) Regulations 1996

**8.**[^c-9f0e883318b24146b4c5f1250c11fc4e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to certain enactments, and related provision, consequential upon provisions of the Health Act 1999 (“the Act”) concerning Primary Care Trusts and high security psychiatric services.

Article 2 of the Order deals with extent and interpretation.

Articles 3(1) and 4 and Schedule 1 make amendments, and related provision, consequential upon the creation, under the 1999 Act, of Primary Care Trusts.

Article 3(2) and Schedule 2 make amendments to certain Acts and to the Order and Regulations relating to the Ashworth, Broadmoor and Rampton Hospital Authorities consequent upon the amendments contained in the 1999 Act relating to high security psychiatric services.
