# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Vine Telecom Networks Limited) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 163

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Vine Telecom Networks Limited) Order 2000

Made

25th January 2000

Laid before Parliament

31st January 2000

Coming into force

29th February 2000

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Vine Telecom Networks Limited on 22 November 1999 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in the Licence (“the Applicable Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Vine Telecom Networks Limited) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 29th February 2000.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Patricia Hewitt,

Minister of State for Small Business and E Commerce,

Department of Trade and Industry

25th January 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Vine Telecom Networks Limited a licence on 22 November 1999 under secton 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in the licence. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 31st January 2000. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Vine Telecom Networks Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 29th February 2000.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
