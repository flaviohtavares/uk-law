# The Summary Appeal Court (Navy) Rules 2000, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — PROPER ADDRESS

Rule 3(2)

**1.** Subject to the following provisions of this Schedule, a reference to a person’s proper address is to the address of the ship, naval establishment or unit to which he belongs.

**2.** Where a person is on attachment to a ship, naval establishment or unit, a reference to his proper address is to the address of the ship, naval establishment or unit to which he is attached.

**3.** In the case of an appellant who is not subject to the Act, a reference to his proper address is to his usual or last known place of residence.
