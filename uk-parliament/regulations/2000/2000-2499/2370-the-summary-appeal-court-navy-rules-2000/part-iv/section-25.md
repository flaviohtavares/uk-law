# The Summary Appeal Court (Navy) Rules 2000, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Spare members

The court administration officer shall specify 2 persons, who are qualified for membership of the court under section 52FH of the Act or rule 23, to be reserve members of the court for the purposes of hearing the appeal (referred to in these Rules as “spare members”).
