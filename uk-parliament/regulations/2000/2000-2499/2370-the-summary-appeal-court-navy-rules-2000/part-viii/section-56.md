# The Summary Appeal Court (Navy) Rules 2000, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Evidence in rebuttal

With the leave of the judge advocate, the respondent may call or recall a witness to give evidence on any matter raised by the appellant in relation to a charge which the respondent could not—

- (a) properly have dealt with before the appellant disclosed his case; or
- (b) reasonably have foreseen.
