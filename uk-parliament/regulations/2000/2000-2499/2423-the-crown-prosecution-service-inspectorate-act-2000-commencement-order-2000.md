# The Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2423 (C.68)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made

3rd September 2000

The Attorney General, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2) of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

**2.** Sections 1 and 2 of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 shall come into force on 1 October 2000.

Ross Cranston

Solicitor General

3rd September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1 October 2000 the provisions of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000.
