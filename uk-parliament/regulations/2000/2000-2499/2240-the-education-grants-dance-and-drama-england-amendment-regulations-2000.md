# The Education (Grants) (Dance and Drama) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2000/2144](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2144) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2000 No. 2240

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Grants) (Dance and Drama) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

16th August 2000

Laid before Parliament

18th August 2000

Coming into force

1st September 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 485 and 569(4) and (5) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grants) (Dance and Drama) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 1st September 2000.

**2.** (1) The Education (Grants) (Dance and Drama) (England) Regulations 2000[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In the Table in Schedule 2, in the entry for the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art for “£5913” in the fifth column there shall be substituted “£5973”.

Tessa Blackstone

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

16th August 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Grants) (Dance and Drama) (England) Regulations 2000 by making an alteration to one of the figures in the Table in Schedule 2.
