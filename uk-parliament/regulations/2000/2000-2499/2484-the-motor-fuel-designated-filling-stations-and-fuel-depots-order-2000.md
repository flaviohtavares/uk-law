# The Motor Fuel (Designated Filling Stations and Fuel Depots) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2484

CONTROL OF FUEL AND ELECTRICITY

The Motor Fuel (Designated Filling Stations and Fuel Depots) Order 2000

Made at

4.00 p.m. on 12th September 2000

Laid before Parliament

12th September 2000

Coming into force at

5.00 p.m. on 12th September 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1)(a) of the Energy Act 1976[^f00001], there being in force an Order in Council[^f00002] under section 3 of that Act, and it appearing to him that the making of the Order is desirable for the purpose of conserving energy, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Motor Fuel (Designated Filling Stations and Fuel Depots) Order 2000 and shall come into force at 5.00 p.m. on 12th September 2000.

**2.** For the purpose of the exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1 and 2 of the Energy Act 1976 to make orders and give directions respectively the Secretary of State hereby designates the filling stations and fuel depots mentioned in the list entitled “Department of Trade and Industry: List of Designated Filling Stations and Fuel Depots” published by him on 12th September 2000.

Stephen Byers,

Secretary of State for Trade and Industry

12th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates certain filling stations and fuel depots in order that the Secretary of State may make a further order or orders or give directions under the Energy Act 1976 by reference to such designated premises. The List setting out the filling stations and depots concerned may be viewed on the world-wide web at www.dti.gov.uk.
