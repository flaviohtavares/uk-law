# The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 7) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2077

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 7) Regulations 2000

Made

29th July 2000

Laid before Parliament

31st July 2000

Coming into force

1st September 2000

The Treasury, with the concurrence of the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 3(2) and (3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 7) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 1st September 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979

In paragraph (1) of regulations 19 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979[^f00002] (payments to be disregarded) after sub-paragraph (za)[^f00003] insert—

> - (zaa) any amount in respect of expenditure which by virtue of section 200E of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (exemption for education and training funded by employers)[^f00004] is not taken to be included in the emoluments of the employee from the office or employment for the purposes of Schedule E;

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979 (S.I. [1979/591](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/591)).

Regulation 2 inserts a new sub-paragraph (zaa) into regulation 19(1) of the 1979 Regulations (payments to be disregarded in the computation of earnings). The new sub-paragraph relates to payments made by an employer to an employee’s individual learning account (for which grants are payable under section 108 of the Learning and Skills Act [2000 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/21) or the equivalent Scottish provision. Such payments by employers are to be disregarded in computing the amount of the employee’s earnings for the purposes of earnings-related contributions. To qualify for disregard, the payment must be taken not to be included in computing the emoluments of the office or employment for income tax purposes by virtue of section 200E (exemptions for education and training funded by employers) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act [1988 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/1).
