# The Summary Appeal Court (Army) Rules 2000, PART IV

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## PART IV — FORM OF SOLEMN AFFIRMATION

**10.** The form of affirmation shall in each case be the same as the form of oath set out above except that for the words “I swear by Almighty God” or, as the case may be, “I promise before Almighty God” there shall be substituted the words “I solemnly, sincerely, and truly declare and affirm”.
