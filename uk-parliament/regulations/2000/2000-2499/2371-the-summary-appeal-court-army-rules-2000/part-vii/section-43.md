# The Summary Appeal Court (Army) Rules 2000, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Questions of practice and procedure not covered by the Act or the Rules

Any matter relating to the practice or procedure of the court raised at the hearing of an appeal shall, to the extent that it is not provided by the Act or these Rules, be determined by the judge advocate; and, in exercising his powers under this rule, the judge advocate shall adopt such course as appears to him will best serve the interests of justice.
