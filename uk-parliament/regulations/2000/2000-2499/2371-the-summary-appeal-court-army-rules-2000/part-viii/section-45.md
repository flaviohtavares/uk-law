# The Summary Appeal Court (Army) Rules 2000, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Power of respondent to give notice that he no longer contests an appeal against finding

The respondent may at any time during the hearing of an appeal against finding give notice that he no longer intends to contest the appeal; and, where he does so, the court shall quash the finding.
