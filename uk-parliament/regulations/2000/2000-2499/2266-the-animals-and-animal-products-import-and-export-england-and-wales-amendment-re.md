The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated[^c16070621] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^c16070631] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 26th August 2000.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations" means the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 [^c16070641].

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

In Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations—

- (a) after the entry relating to Bristol Port (Royal Portbury Dock) there shall be inserted the following entry—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Gatwick Airport | All animals other than ungulates (ii) |

  ; and
- (b) the entry relating to Tilbury Port shall be deleted.

25th August 2000

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 (S.I. 2000/1673) (the principal Regulations) so as to modify the list of border inspection posts set out in Schedule 2, both to correct an omission and to implement Commission Decision [2000/497/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0497) updating the list of border inspection posts approved for veterinary checks (OJ No. L200, 8.8.00, p. 41).

The Regulations also amend Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations so as to implement the requirements of Commission Decision [2000/528/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0528) concerning certain protective measures relating to Classical Swine Fever in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L214, 25.8.00, p. 42) and to insert a reference to Directive [2000/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0020) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Council Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432) on animal health problems affecting intra-Community trade in bovine animals and swine (OJ No. L163, 4.7.00, p. 35).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been made in respect of these Regulations.
