# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2481

AGRICULTURE

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000

Made

10th September 2000

Laid before Parliament

22nd September 2000

Coming into force

29th October 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, acting in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 68(1) and (1A), 69(1), (3), (6) and (7), 70(1), 74(1), 74A, 77(4), 78(6) and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] (as read with regulation 14 of the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000)[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation as required by section 84(1) of the said Act with the persons or organisations appearing to represent the interests concerned, and acting, as a Minister designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the said section 2(2)[^f00005] (in so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers in the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:
