# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Revocations

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995[^f00030], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1996[^f00031], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00032], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998[^f00033] and the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00034] are hereby revoked in relation to England.
