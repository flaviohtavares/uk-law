# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Further exemption from these Regulations

The provisions of regulation 13(2) (in so far as they regulate the putting into circulation of additives and material containing additives), and the provisions of regulation 13(7)(a), shall not apply in the circumstances specified in Article 9q5 of the Additives Directive.
