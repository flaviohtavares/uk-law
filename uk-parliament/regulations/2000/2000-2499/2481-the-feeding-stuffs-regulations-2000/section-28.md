# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Modification of the Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999

(1) The provisions of the Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999[^f00037] specified in paragraph (2) below shall have effect in England as if, after the words “the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999”, wherever they occur, there were added the words “, as amended by the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999 and as modified by the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000,”[^f00038].

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) above are regulations 98(8) and (9), 99 and 106(1).
