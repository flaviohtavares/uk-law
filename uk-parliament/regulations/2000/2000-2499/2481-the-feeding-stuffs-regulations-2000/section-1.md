# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000, shall come into force on 29th October 2000 and shall extend to England, except that regulations 11 and 12 shall extend to England, Wales and Northern Ireland and, subject to paragraph (2) below, other provisions of these Regulations shall so extend in so far as they relate to regulations 11 and 12.

(2) In so far as regulation 24 applies to Northern Ireland, the references therein to Part IV of the Act shall be construed as references to that Part as modified by section 86.

**1.** These Regulations, which apply to England (save for regulations 11 and 12 and related provisions which apply to England, Wales and Northern Ireland) and come into force on 29th October 2000, revoke (regulation 26) and re-enact, or re-enact with amendments, provisions previously contained in the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995 as amended, implement the European Community Directives and Decision specified below, and provide for the enforcement of the European Community Regulations listed below.
