# The Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2460

ANIMALS, ENGLAND

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) (Amendment) Order 2000

Made

12th September 2000

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1 and 8 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

This Order may be cited as the Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) (Amendment) Order 2000; it extends to England only, and comes into force at 5.45 p.m. on 12th September 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) Order 2000

In article 3(1) of the Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) Order 2000[^f00002] for the word “pigs” there shall be substituted the word “animals”.

Hayman

Minister of State Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

12th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Swine Fever (Movement Restriction Areas) Order 2000 so as to permit the Minister, or an officer of the local authority acting under the written direction of the Minister, to grant a licence for the movement of animals in a movement restriction area which would otherwise be prohibited.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been made in respect of this Order.
