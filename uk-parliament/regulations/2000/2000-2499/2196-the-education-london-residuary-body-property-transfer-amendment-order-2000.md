# The Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2196

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) (Amendment) Order 2000

Made

10th August 2000

Laid before Parliament

11th August 2000

Coming into force

1st September 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 187(5) and (6), 231(2) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st September 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

In Article 4(7) of the Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) Order 1992[^f00002] (which imposes a condition regarding the bringing into use of the former Wandsworth Boys' Secondary School, Sutherland Grove, SW18 as a site for a school) for “1st September 2000” there shall be substituted “1st September 2003”.

Jacqui Smith

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

10th August 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 4(7) of the Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) Order 1992 as amended by the Education (London Residuary Body) (Property Transfer) (Modification and Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/723](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/723)), which imposes a condition that part of the site of the former Wandsworth Boys' Secondary School, Sutherland Grove, SW18 should be brought into use by the Council of the London Borough of Wandsworth as a site for a school by 1st September 2000.

A new date of 1st September 2003 is substituted as the date by which the part of the site subject to the condition must be brought into use as the site for a new school or as the site for a school transferring to that site.
