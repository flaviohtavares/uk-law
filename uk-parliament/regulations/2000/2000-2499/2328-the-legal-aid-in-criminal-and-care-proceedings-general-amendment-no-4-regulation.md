# The Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2328

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations 2000

Made

28th August 2000

Laid before Parliament

31st August 2000

Coming into force

2nd October 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 21, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 2nd October 2000.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations a reference to any regulation or Schedule by number alone means the regulation or Schedule so numbered in the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) Regulations 1989[^f00002].

## 3. Transitional provisions

These Regulations shall apply to all applications for legal aid made on or after 2nd October 2000 and applications made before that date shall be treated as if these Regulations had not been made.

These Regulations make amendments to the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) Regulations 1989 consequential on the Legal Aid (Prescribed Panels) Amendment Regulations 2000 (S.I. [2000/1930](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1930)), so as to provide that a solicitor assigned under a legal aid order in the magistrates' court must be a member of the Crime Franchise Panel.

The Regulations also make various minor amendments to the forms in Schedule 2. These include, in the Statement of Means, changes consequential on the exclusion of the system of the assessment of applicants' financial resources in certain circumstances introduced by the Legal Aid Act 1988 (Modification) Regulations 2000 (S.I. [2000/2227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2227)) and the Legal Aid in Criminal and Care Proceedings (General) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2000 (S.I. [2000/2226](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2226)). These circumstances are:

- (i) where a person appears before a magistrates' court following charge at a police station, other than in relation to an offence triable only on indictment or any other offence where the court sends a person to the Crown Court for trial under section 51 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998; and
- (ii) where a person appears before a youth court.
