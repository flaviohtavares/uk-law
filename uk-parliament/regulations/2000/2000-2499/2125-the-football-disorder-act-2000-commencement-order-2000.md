# The Football (Disorder) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2125 (C. 57)

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Football (Disorder) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made

3rd August 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 5(1) of the Football (Disorder) Act 2000[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Football (Disorder) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

**2.** Section 1 of the Football (Disorder) Act 2000 shall come into force on 28th August 2000.

Steve Bassam

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

3rd August 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 1 of the Football (Disorder) Act 2000 into force on 28th August 2000. The remainder of the Act came into force on Royal Assent.
