# The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 2408

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made

7th September 2000

Laid before the House of Commons

8th September 2000

Coming into force

1st October 2000

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 12(4) and 14(1) of the Finance Act 1997[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and come into force on 1st October 2000.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “quarter” means the first three months of an accounting period.

## 3. Application and revocation

(1) These Regulations apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2000.

(2) The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00002] are revoked.

## 4. The amount of payments on account

For the purpose of calculating payments on account of gaming duty in the cases to which these Regulations apply substitute the following Table for the Table in regulation 5 of the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997[^f00003].

**TABLE**

| Part of gross gaming yield | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| The first £235,250 | 2½ per cent |
| The next £522,750 | 12½ per cent |
| The next £522,750 | 20 per cent |
| The next £915,000 | 30 per cent |
| The remainder | 40 per cent |

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2196](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2196)), regulation 5, the amount of payments on account. They substitute a new Table reflecting changes to gaming duty made by s16 of the Finance Act 2000, and will apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2000.

The duty rates and bands in the Table are those for the first three months of each six monthly accounting period, hence the bands represent half of the new bands of gross gaming yield shown in the Finance Act 2000. The interim quarterly payments under the 1997 Regulations are intended to be roughly half of the expected duty liability for the six monthly period.
