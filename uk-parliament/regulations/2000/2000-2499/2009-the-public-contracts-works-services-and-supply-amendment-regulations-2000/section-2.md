# The Public Contracts (Works, Services and Supply) (Amendment) Regulations 2000, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2000-08-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2000/2009
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- (a) the “Public Works Regulations" shall mean the Public Works Contracts Regulations 1991 [^c16053501],
- (b) the “Public Services Regulations" shall mean the Public Services Contracts Regulations 1993 [^c16053511], and
- (c) the “Public Supply Regulations" shall mean the Public Supply Contracts Regulations 1995 [^c16053521].
