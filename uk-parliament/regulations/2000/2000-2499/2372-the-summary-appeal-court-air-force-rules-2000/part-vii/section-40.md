# The Summary Appeal Court (Air Force) Rules 2000, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Interruption of hearing where a member of the court is unable to continue

Where, after the commencement of the hearing of an appeal, a member of the court dies or is for any other reason unable to continue hearing the appeal, the hearing shall be discontinued and the court shall be reconstituted for the purposes of beginning again the hearing of the appeal.
