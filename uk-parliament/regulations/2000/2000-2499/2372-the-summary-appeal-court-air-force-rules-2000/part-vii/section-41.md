# The Summary Appeal Court (Air Force) Rules 2000, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Refixing of postponed and interrupted hearings

Where the hearing of an appeal is postponed in accordance with rule 39(5) or (6) or discontinued in accordance with rule 40, rule 22 shall have effect with respect to the reconvened hearing.
