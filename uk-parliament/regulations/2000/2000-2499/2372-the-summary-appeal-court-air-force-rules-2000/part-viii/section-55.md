# The Summary Appeal Court (Air Force) Rules 2000, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Witnesses for the appellant

Except with the leave of the judge advocate, if the appellant elects to give evidence he shall be called before any other witnesses for the appellant.
