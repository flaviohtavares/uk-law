# The Summary Appeal Court (Air Force) Rules 2000, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Application of Part IX

This Part shall have effect in relation to the practice and procedure of the court—

- (a) with respect to the exercise of its powers under section 83ZG(2)(b) or (3) of the Act (powers of the court to vary punishment awarded); and
- (b) on an appeal against any punishment awarded.
