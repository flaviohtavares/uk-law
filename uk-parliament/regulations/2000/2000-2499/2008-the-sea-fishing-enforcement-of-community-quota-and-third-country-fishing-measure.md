# The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Quota and Third Country Fishing Measures) (Amendment) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2000/827](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/827) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with sea fishing in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 30(2) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Quota and Third Country Fishing Measures) (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st September 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

For Schedule 1 to the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Quota and Third Country Fishing Measures) Order 2000[^f00002], there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which is made under section 30(2) of the Fisheries Act 1981, amends the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Quotas and Third Country Fishing Measures) Order 2000 ([S.I. 2000/827](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/827)) (“the principal Order”). The principal Order extends to England and Wales and to Northern Ireland but does not extend to Scotland.

The effect of the present Order is to add to Schedule 1 to the principal Order a reference to Article 7 of Council Regulation 2742/1999 (O.J. No. L341, 31.12.99, p.1), which prohibits fishing by European Community vessels in certain Norwegian and Icelandic waters and to remedy a typographical error in paragraph 5 of that Schedule. For convenience, the whole of Schedule 1 is restated in the present Order.
