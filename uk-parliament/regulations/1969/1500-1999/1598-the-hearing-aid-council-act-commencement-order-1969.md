# The Hearing Aid Council Act (Commencement) Order 1969

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1969 No. 1598 (C.48)

HEARING AID COUNCIL

The Hearing Aid Council Act (Commencement) Order 1969

Made

13th November 1969

The Board of Trade, in pursuance of the power conferred upon them by section 15 of the Hearing Aid Council Act 1968, hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Hearing Aid Council Act (Commencement) Order 1969.

**2.** The Hearing Aid Council Act 1968 shall come into force on 29th December 1969.

Gwyneth Dunwoody

Parliamentary Secretary to the Board of Trade

13th November 1969
