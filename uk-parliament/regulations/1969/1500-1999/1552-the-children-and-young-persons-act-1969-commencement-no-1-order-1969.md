# The Children and Young Persons Act 1969 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1969

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1969 No. 1552 (C.43)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children and Young Persons Act 1969 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1969

Made

3rd November 1969

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 73(2) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1969, I hereby make the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young Persons Act 1969 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1969.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “**the Act**” means the Children and Young Persons Act 1969.

## 3. Day appointed for coming into force of certain provisions of the Act

The provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into operation on 16th November 1969.

James Callaghan

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

3rd November 1969
