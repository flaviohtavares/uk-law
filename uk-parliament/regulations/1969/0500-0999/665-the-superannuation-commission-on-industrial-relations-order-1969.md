# The Superannuation (Commission on Industrial Relations) Order 1969

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1969 No. 665

PENSIONS

The Superannuation (Commission On Industrial Relations) Order 1969

Made

6th May 1969

Laid before Parliament

13th May 1969

Coming into Operation

14th May 1969

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 98(5) and (9) of the Superannuation Act 1965 and article 2(1)(c) of the Minister for the Civil Service Order 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Commission on Industrial Relations) Order 1969, and shall come into operation on 14th May 1969.

**2.** The Interpretation Act 1889. shall apply for the interpretation of this Order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

**3.** (1) Employment by the Commission on Industrial Relations shall be added to the employments listed in Schedule 8 to the Superannuation Act 1965.

(2) This article shall have effect as from 1st March 1969.

Given under the official seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on 6th May 1969.

J. E. Herbecq

Authorised by the Minister for the Civil Service

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 98(4) of the Superannuation Act 1965 provides that service in employment of the kinds listed in Schedule 8 to that Act shall be treated as service in the civil service of the State for the purposes of pensions and other superannuation benefits. This Order adds employment by the Commission on Industrial Relations to the list in that Schedule. Under the powers of section 98(9) of the Act the Order has retrospective effect as from 1st March 1969.
