# The Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1969

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1969 No. 1096 (C.28)

POULTRY

HUMANE SLAUGHTER

The Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1969

Made

29th July 1969

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 9(3) of the Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby order as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1969.

**2.** The Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 shall come into force on 1st January 1970.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 28th July 1969.

Cledwyn Hughes

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Given under the Seal of the Secretary of State for Scotland on 29th July 1969.

William Ross

Secretary of State for Scotland
