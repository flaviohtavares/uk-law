# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 25

## 25. Misleading the FCA: criminal offence[^c-037bbf88b2e94af0d3a10f4e8b8695cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 398 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (misleading the FCA or PRA: residual cases)[^f00005] applies to a requirement to provide information in accordance with a direction under regulation 2(2)(b) or 22(2)(b) as it applies to a requirement falling within subsection (1A) of that section.
