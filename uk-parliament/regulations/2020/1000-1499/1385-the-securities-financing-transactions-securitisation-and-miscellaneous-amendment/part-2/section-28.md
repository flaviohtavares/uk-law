# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 28

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Enforcement[^c-a6e415e258e67914b30b8b840985bc1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Transparency of Securities Financing Transactions and of Reuse (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00006] apply to this Part, as though contraventions of the requirements of this Part were a contravention of a requirement of those Regulations—

- (a) Chapter 2 in Part 4;
- (b) regulation 36;
- (c) regulation 38; and
- (d) regulation 39.
