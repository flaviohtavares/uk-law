# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Banking Act 2009[^c-03e87c3f033bb057358387381316b61b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Banking Act 2009[^f00014] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 3 (interpretation: other expressions)[^f00015], in the definition of “the capital requirements regulation”, for “had effect on the day on which the Financial Services (Electronic Money, Payment Services and Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1212](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1212)) were made” substitute “forms part of retained EU law”.

(3) In section 4(4)(a) (special resolution objectives)[^f00016], for “, as that regulation had effect on the day on which the Bank Recovery and Resolution and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/1394](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1394)) were made” substitute “as it forms part of retained EU law”.

(4) In section 11A(8) (private sector purchaser: marketing)[^f00017], for “had effect on the day on which the Bank Recovery and Resolution and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/1394](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1394)) were made” substitute “forms part of retained EU law”.
