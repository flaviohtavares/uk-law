# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Insolvency Act 1986[^key-be1c342dba8e74ec19ba7529e21c0d21]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 15C(4) of Schedule 6 to the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00010], for “had effect on the day on which the Bank Recovery and Resolution and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 were made” substitute “forms part of retained EU law”.
