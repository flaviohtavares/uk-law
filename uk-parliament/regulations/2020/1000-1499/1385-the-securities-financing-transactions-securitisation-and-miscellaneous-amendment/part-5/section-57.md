# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-07fa1938022ac53affb6ca292ce8af2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00043] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 121(2) (Article 3 (interpretation)), omit the references to “aircraft operator”, “auction platform”, “emission allowance auctioning regulation”, “greenhouse gas emissions allowances”, “operator”, “reception”, “transmission” and “submission”.

(3) Omit—

- (a) regulation 126 (Article 24A (bidding in emissions auctions));
- (b) 127 (Article 24B (miscellaneous exclusions));
- (c) regulation 154 (Article 82A (greenhouse gas emissions allowances));
- (d) regulation 155(2) (Article 82B (emission allowances));
- (e) regulation 178(3)(a) (the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Appointed Representatives) Regulations 2001);
- (f) regulation 179(2) (the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Professions) (Non-exempt Activities) Order 2001).
