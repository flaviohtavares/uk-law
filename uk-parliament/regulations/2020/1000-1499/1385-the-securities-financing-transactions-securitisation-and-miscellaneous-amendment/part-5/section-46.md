# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. The Deposit Guarantee Scheme and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^key-e538267e19bfa36847d190a0e9238aab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 5(5) of the Deposit Guarantee Scheme and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^f00024], in new regulation 7A, for paragraph (7) substitute—

> (7) The first review carried out in accordance with paragraph (5) must be completed no later than five years after regulation 5 of the Deposit Guarantee Scheme and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 comes into force (in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020).
