## 42. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Benchmarks) Regulations 2018[^key-75323a15303c551838b29572a3eb1961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Benchmarks) Regulations 2018[^f00020], in the definition of “the EU Benchmarks Regulation 2016”, at the end insert “as it forms part of retained EU law”.
