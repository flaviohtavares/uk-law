# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001[^c-f682c1185d0196a3d009d4f655982beb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3(1) (interpretation) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001[^f00018] is amended as follows.

(2) In the definition of “EU Securitisation Regulation 2017”, at the end insert “as it forms part of retained EU law”.

(3) In the definition of “markets in financial instruments regulation”, at the end insert “as it forms part of retained EU law”.
