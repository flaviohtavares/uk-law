# The Securities Financing Transactions, Securitisation and Miscellaneous Amendments (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/1385
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Disclosure of Confidential Information) Regulations 2001[^c-8238be4fda40abc19745cfc04b1f516a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Disclosure of Confidential Information) Regulations 2001[^f00019], at the end of the list of definitions, for the final “.” substitute—

> - , and
>
> any reference to an EU regulation, or part of an EU regulation, is to be read as a reference to that EU regulation, or that part of an EU regulation, as amended from time to time.

.
