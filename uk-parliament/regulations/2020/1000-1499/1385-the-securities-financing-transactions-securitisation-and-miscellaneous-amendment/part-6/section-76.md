## 76. Regulation (EU) 2019/877[^c-48bff17143351e7c6a59741078de110b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) 2019/877 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2019 amending Regulation (EU) No 806/2014 as regards the loss-absorbing and recapitalisation capacity of credit institutions and investment firms is revoked.
