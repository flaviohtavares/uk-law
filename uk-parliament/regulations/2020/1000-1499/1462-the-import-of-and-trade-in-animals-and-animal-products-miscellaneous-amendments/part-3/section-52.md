# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 52

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Commission Decision [2008/636/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0636) establishing the list of third countries from which Member States authorise imports of ova and embryos of the porcine species[^key-b9ec6fe5cab1eceb1a43596b5283f8de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Decision [2008/636/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0636) establishing the list of third countries from which Member States authorise imports of ova and embryos of the porcine species, is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “ The appropriate authority ”;
- (b) for “Article 1 of Decision [2002/613/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0613)” substitute “ Commission Implementing Decision 2012/137/EU ”;
- (c) at the end insert—

  > In this Decision:
  >
  > - — “**appropriate authority**” means the Secretary of State (in relation to England), the Welsh Ministers (in relation to Wales) and the Scottish Ministers (in relation to Scotland); but the “appropriate authority” is the Secretary of State if consent is given by:
  >   - (a) in relation to Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
  >   - (b) in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers;
  > - — “**third country**” means any country or territory other than the British Islands.

  .

(3) Omit Article 4.
