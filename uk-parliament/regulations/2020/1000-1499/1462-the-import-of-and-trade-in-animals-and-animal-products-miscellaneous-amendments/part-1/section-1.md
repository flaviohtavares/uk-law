# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-d36bf5d764f2810054243c1e39864349]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) This regulation and the following provisions come into force on 31st December 2020—

- (a) Chapter 2 of Part 2;
- (b) regulation 73(1).

(3) All other provisions come into force on IP completion day.
