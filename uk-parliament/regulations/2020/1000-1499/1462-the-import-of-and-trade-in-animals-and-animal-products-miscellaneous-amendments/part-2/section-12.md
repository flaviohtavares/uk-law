# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 12

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12.[^c-3f8dceaf9ad484c80e02299ddf644966]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 17—

- (a) for “the United Kingdom or a member State” substitute “ Great Britain ”;
- (b) for “Secretary of State of its arrival” substitute “ appropriate authority of its arrival, and for this purpose “**appropriate authority**” means the Secretary of State (in relation to England), the Welsh Ministers (in relation to Wales) and the Scottish Ministers (in relation to Scotland) ”.
