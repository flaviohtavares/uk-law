# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Amendment of the Trade in Animals and Animal Products (Legislative Functions) and Veterinary Surgeons (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-0cd7509465bbc6e801c436f460cf31de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Trade in Animals and Animal Products (Legislative Functions) and Veterinary Surgeons (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24364511] are amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1, in regulation 2, for the definition of “third country” substitute—

> “**third country**” means any country or territory other than the British Islands.

.

(3) In Part 2, in Chapter 3—

- (a) in the heading, for “and fishery products” substitute “, fishery products, frogs' legs and snails”;
- (b) in regulation 5—
  - (i) in the heading, for “Decision [2006/766/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/766)” substitute “ Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/626 ”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (1)—
    - (aa) for “Decision [2006/766/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/766) establishing” substitute “ Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/626, which, in particular, sets out ”;
    - (bb) for “and fishery products” substitute “, fishery products, frogs' legs and snails”;
    - (cc) for “or Annex 2” substitute “ , 2 or 3 ”.

(4) In Part 3, in regulation 18(1), for “the whole of the United Kingdom” substitute “ Great Britain ”.

(5) Omit Part 4.

(6) In Part 5, omit regulation 20.
