# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 21

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21.[^c-91579c0623c0f60a77ee2f859f0afe41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For regulation 28 substitute—

> **28. Admission of products into warehouses in compliance with Customs procedures and storage procedures**
>
> No person may bring into Great Britain a consignment of products that does not comply with the import requirements of these Regulations and the Customs procedures and storage procedures on imports in the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 [^c24364451].
