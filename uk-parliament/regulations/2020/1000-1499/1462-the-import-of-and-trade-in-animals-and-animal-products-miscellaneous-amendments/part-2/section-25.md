# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 25

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25.[^c-cc7b454d78c36a7058c31d4c8441cce2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 35—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “the Secretary of State”—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, substitute “ the appropriate enforcement authority ”;
  - (ii) in the second place it occurs, substitute “ that enforcement authority ”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “the Secretary of State” substitute “ the appropriate enforcement authority ”;
  - (ii) in the second place it occurs, for “the Secretary of State” substitute “ that enforcement authority ”;
  - (iii) for “Article 1(2) of the EU Regulation” substitute “ Chapter 5 of Title II of the Official Controls Regulation ”;
- (c) in paragraphs (4) to (6), in each place, for “Secretary of State” substitute “ enforcement authority ”.
