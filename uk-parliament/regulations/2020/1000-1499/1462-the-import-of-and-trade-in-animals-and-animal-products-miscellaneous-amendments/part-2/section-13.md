# The Import of, and Trade in, Animals and Animal Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 13

> Text as at 2021-07-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13.[^c-960c73ff942353292d4038dda0040a7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 18—

- (a) except in the first place it occurs in paragraph (3), in each other place, including in the heading, for “the United Kingdom” substitute “ Great Britain ”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) for “animal consigned to a destination outside the European Union” substitute “ imported animal ”;
  - (ii) after “country” insert “ or territory ”;
- (c) in paragraph (3) for “Products that arrive at a border control post for an ultimate destination outside the United Kingdom, and” substitute “ Imported products ”;
- (d) in paragraph (4)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “European Union” substitute “ British Islands ”;
  - (ii) in the second place it occurs, for “the European Union” substitute “ any of the territories of the British Islands ”.
