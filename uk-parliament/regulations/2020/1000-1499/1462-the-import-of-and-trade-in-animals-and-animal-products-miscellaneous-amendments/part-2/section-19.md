## 19.[^key-95966de05ac325e9707251ca5884308a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 26—

- (a) in the heading, after “Exclusions” insert “ , derogations and modifications ”;
- (b) the existing text becomes paragraph (1);
- (c) after paragraph (1), insert—

  > (2) The provisions of this Part apply subject to the derogations and modifications set out in Schedule 5.
  >
  > (3) Paragraph (2) (and Schedule 5) have effect and remain in force from immediately after IP completion day until the end of the day on 31st July 2021.
