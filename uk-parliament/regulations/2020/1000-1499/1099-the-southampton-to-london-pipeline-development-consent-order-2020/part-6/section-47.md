# The Southampton to London Pipeline Development Consent Order 2020, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. No double recovery

Compensation must not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or deed or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
