# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets and Network Codes) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendments to Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1447

Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1447 of 26<sup> </sup>August 2016 establishing a network code on requirements for grid connection of high voltage direct current systems and direct current-connected power park modules is amended in accordance with Schedule 3.
