# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets and Network Codes) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets and Network Codes) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) Regulations 1, 2 and 3 come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made and all other regulations come into force on IP completion day.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.
