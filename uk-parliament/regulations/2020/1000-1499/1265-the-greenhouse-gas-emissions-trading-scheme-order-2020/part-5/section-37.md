# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Remittance of charges[^key-1bb1ddc1311bdee04dedca4589545b31][^key-63ad5c558875446736ece8261622b4a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The regulator must pay any charge received in accordance with a charging scheme under article 36 to the appropriate national authority (as defined in paragraph (7) of that article).

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a charge received by the Secretary of State.

(3) The registry administrator must pay any charge received in accordance with a charging scheme under article 36A to the UK ETS authority.
