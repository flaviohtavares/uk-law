# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 34N

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34N. Aviation allocation table for 2021-2025 allocation period[^key-9df07e4cff1aa5b85e9f5e409534e082]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-a888bd4c94506ef60c8d871b6cb74a4a]

(1) The UK ETS authority must compile an aviation allocation table for the 2021-2025 allocation period as soon as reasonably practicable after 31st March 2021.

(2) The aviation allocation table must contain an entry for each person with an aviation free allocation entitlement, as approved by the UK ETS authority under article 34M.

(3) The person's entry must set out—

- (a) the person's full name and Eurocontrol Central Route Charges Office identification number;
- (b) the person's aviation free allocation entitlement for each scheme year in the 2021-2025 allocation period.

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6a650f4576804903e11829152e723d37] The UK ETS authority must update the aviation allocation table to take account of any approval of the UK ETS authority under article 34Q (transfers of allocations) or article 34R (errors in aviation allocation table) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a650f4576804903e11829152e723d37] of this Order or under article 29 of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) Order 2021 (aviation: recalculation of aviation free allocation entitlement of certain applicants)[^key-0476121ed423d7c6f18807034f4e5f21] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a650f4576804903e11829152e723d37] , article 42 of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2024 or article 34M of this Order (as applied by article 43 of that Order)[^key-28135eac3ac9fff9e4858e90dc412091] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a650f4576804903e11829152e723d37]. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a650f4576804903e11829152e723d37]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S)) The UK ETS authority must update the aviation allocation table to take account of any approval of the UK ETS authority under— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) article 34M(7)— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S)) of the applicant’s aviation free allocation entitlement following an application made in reliance on article 34L(7); *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S)) of an increase in a person’s aviation free allocation following a recalculation by the regulator under article 34M(6A); or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) article 34Q (transfers of allocations) or article 34R (errors in aviation allocation table). *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(5) To avoid doubt, the UK ETS authority may update the aviation allocation table under paragraph (4) so as to increase or reduce the number of allowances to be allocated to a person for a scheme year after allowances have already been allocated to the person for the scheme year under article 34O. (See article 34T in relation to the return of allowances where the number of allowances to be allocated to a person for a scheme year is reduced after allowances for the scheme year have been allocated.)

(6) The UK ETS authority must notify the registry administrator of the aviation allocation table as soon as reasonably practicable after it is compiled and of an updated aviation allocation table as soon as reasonably practicable after it is updated.

(7) The UK ETS authority must publish the aviation allocation table as soon as reasonably practicable after it is compiled and must publish an updated aviation allocation table as soon as reasonably practicable after it is updated.

(8) Paragraph (7) is subject to article 75C (national security).
