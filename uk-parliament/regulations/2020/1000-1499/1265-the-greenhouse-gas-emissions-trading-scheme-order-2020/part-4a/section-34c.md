# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 34C

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34C. Allocation tables: updates[^key-9df07e4cff1aa5b85e9f5e409534e082]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The UK ETS authority must update an allocation table to take account of any approval of the UK ETS authority under—

- (a) Article 18a of the Free Allocation Regulation (new entrants);
- (b) Article 6a of the Activity Level Changes Regulation (activity level changes);
- (c) Article 24 of the Free Allocation Regulation (renunciation);
- (d) Article 25 of that Regulation (mergers and splits);
- (e)[^key-7465fc1b68643287ea9a82a0b869d10e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) article 34H of this Order (installations: errors in applications for free allocation, etc.).
- (g)[^key-9a8a796098b3fd8e9abec712d96b3169] paragraph 4 of Schedule 8A to this Order (former hospital or small emitters and ultra-small emitters).
- (h)[^key-4bbe1bd61fc05082d50db6c5ce3da022] Article 5c(7) of the Activity Level Changes Regulation (activity level changes: average activity level omitting 2020 data);
- (i) article 20(5) of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) Order 2023 (free allocation for 2024 and 2025 scheme years: lime and malt extract);

*Extent: E+W+S*

- (j)[^key-cb85cba8e12da427c69e1f411e8bf7dc] Article 2b(6) of the Free Allocation Regulation (electricity generators that state they will not produce electricity for sale for consumption outside the installation but subsequently do so);
- (k)[^key-dbb6d1b9c4f929cd991d067efedb7ce6] Part 2 of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2025;
- (l)[^key-84987490aebf1fa689f107707dd3802b] where Article 16(15) of the Free Allocation Regulation applies (including as applied by Article 16(17)), Article 16b of that Regulation (incumbent installations).

(2) To avoid doubt, the UK ETS authority may update an allocation table under paragraph (1) so as to increase or reduce the final annual number of allowances to be allocated in respect of an installation for a scheme year after allowances have already been allocated in respect of the installation for the scheme year under article 34E. (See article 34S in relation to the return of allowances where the number of allowances to be allocated in respect of an installation for a scheme year is reduced after allowances for the scheme year have been allocated, for example, because of a decrease in activity levels.)
