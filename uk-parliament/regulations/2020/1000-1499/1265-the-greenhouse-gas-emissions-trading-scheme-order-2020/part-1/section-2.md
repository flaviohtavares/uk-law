# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 2

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement[^key-7230a00e767ce7bff5290af3cf90339e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as provided by paragraph (2), this Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) Article 25, Schedule 5 and paragraph 4 of Schedule 8 come into force—

- (a) on the day after the day on which this Order is made; or
- (b) immediately after IP completion day,

whichever is later.
