# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 8

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Exempt non-commercial air transport operators[^c-dd6d5a72384b8bd1d2ce9469c794dd05]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A non-commercial air transport operator is not an aircraft operator for the purposes of this Order in relation to a scheme year, where in respect of that year it operates full-scope flights with total annual emissions of less than 1,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide.
