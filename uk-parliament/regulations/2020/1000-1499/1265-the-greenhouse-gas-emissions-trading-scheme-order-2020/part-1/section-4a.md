# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 4A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4A. Meaning of FA installation, etc.[^c-48a6487c0fdb527f5d89fa3384967058]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Order, an installation is an “FA installation” if the installation is—

- (a) an FA installation for the 2021-2025 allocation period; or
- (b)[^c-0bcfff154862cd398b45b3b3009c6f7b] an FA installation for the 2026 and 2027-2030 allocation periods.

(2) For the purposes of this Order, an installation is an FA installation for the 2021-2025 allocation period from—

- (a) the date of publication of the allocation table for the 2021-2025 allocation period (including an updated allocation table) that first includes an entry for the installation; or
- (b) if earlier, the date on which the regulator gives notice of the final annual amount of allowances to be allocated in respect of the installation for any scheme year in the 2021-2025 allocation period under—
  - (i) article 34H(7) (installations: errors in applications for free allocation, etc.);
  - (ia)[^c-b03b0543ca9c3bdcfc8a3cfd64a26bf2] paragraph 4(7) of Schedule 8A (former hospital or small emitters or ultra-small emitters);
  - (ii) Article 18a(9) of the Free Allocation Regulation (new entrants);
  - (iii) Article 25(9) of that Regulation (mergers and splits).

(3) An installation ceases to be an FA installation for the 2021-2025 allocation period at the earliest of—

- (a) the end of the 2025 scheme year;
- (b)[^c-42fe6af20617027fdb817914f50fca6d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the date on which, following the partial transfer under paragraph 9 of Schedule 6 of the greenhouse gas emissions permit of an installation that is an FA installation, the regulator gives notice to the transferring operator (within the meaning of that paragraph) under Article 25(9)(b) of the Free Allocation Regulation that the installation is not an FA installation for the 2021-2025 allocation period;
- (d) if the installation's permit is surrendered under paragraph 11(1) of Schedule 6 or revoked under paragraph 12(1) of that Schedule, the end of the scheme year in which the installation ceases operation;
- (e) if the installation's permit is surrendered under paragraph 11(2) of Schedule 6 or revoked under paragraph 12(3) of that Schedule, the end of the scheme year in which the surrender or revocation takes effect;
- (f) the date on which, following the inclusion of an entry for the installation in the allocation table for the 2021-2025 allocation period in error, the regulator gives notice to the operator under article 34H(7)(c) that the installation is not an FA installation for the 2021-2025 allocation period.

(4) For the purposes of this Order, an installation is an FA installation for the 2026 and 2027-2030 allocation periods[^c-c05fd0817419b30ed0d5f17844bf8442] from the earliest of the following[^c-0bd54d1f173cd7c0d5e9e316f4a6ec46]—

- (a) the date of publication of the allocation table for the 2026 allocation period or the 2027-2030[^c-71e663d67a8ced7285efd9265e69c25c] allocation period (including an updated allocation table) that first includes an entry for the installation; [^c-2d7be0b53e2dc8e51e59a0da23879ac9]...
- (aa)[^c-323a7443fdf98cba21d6de256208158a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c-21b7e6a369d75e30578f1e7e272e8540]... the date on which the regulator gives notice of the final annual amount of allowances to be allocated in respect of the installation for any scheme year in the 2026 allocation period or the 2027-2030[^c-ad8705499b6864aab2bea91a6aab242d] allocation period under—
  - (i) article 34H(7) (installations: errors in applications for free allocation, etc.);
  - (ia)[^c-a32f47f7e48492f7ca2841a9cc4f3fa6] paragraph 4(7) of Schedule 8A (former hospital or small emitters or ultra-small emitters);
  - (ii) Article 18a(9) of the Free Allocation Regulation (new entrants);
  - (iii) Article 25(9) of that Regulation (mergers and splits).

(5) An installation ceases to be an FA installation for the 2026 and 2027-2030 allocation periods[^c-0a0154daa24574f6d87c1051d8b6d522] at the earliest of—

- (a) the end of the 2030 scheme year;
- (b) if the operator of the installation gives a renunciation notice under Article 24 of the Free Allocation Regulation on or after 1st January 2027[^c-553db1f5e8e8a6324d087c16a392d67b] in respect of the installation as a whole, the end of the scheme year in which the renunciation notice is given;
- (c) the date on which, following the partial transfer under paragraph 9 of Schedule 6 of the greenhouse gas emissions permit of an installation that is a FA installation, the regulator gives notice to the transferring operator (within the meaning of that paragraph) under Article 25(9)(b) of the Free Allocation Regulation that the installation is not an FA installation for the 2026 and 2027-2030 allocation periods[^c-2fb42f8a9b831b11081744f816b8b7b1];
- (d) if the installation's permit is surrendered under paragraph 11(1) of Schedule 6 or revoked under paragraph 12(1) of that Schedule, the end of the scheme year in which the installation ceases operation;
- (e) if the installation's permit is surrendered under paragraph 11(2) of Schedule 6 or revoked under paragraph 12(3) of that Schedule, the end of the scheme year in which the surrender or revocation takes effect;
- (f) the date on which, following the inclusion of an entry for the installation in the allocation table for the 2026 allocation period or the 2027-2030 allocation period in error[^c-16996dc2391278ea07fac8d61183c688], the regulator gives notice to the operator under article 34H(7)(c) that the installation is not an FA installation for the 2026 and 2027-2030 allocation periods[^c-94dab3c9e87f93c2d0bebb154b2915d1];
- (g)[^c-f5c66fdf7175fd98d0d091f9a3fdae72] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h)[^c-bbaaca3ba9988ad0fdd157426d8e0808] if the allocation table for the 2026 allocation period includes an entry for the installation but the allocation table for the 2027-2030 allocation period does not, the end of the 2026 scheme year.
