# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 13A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13A. Meaning of regulator: maritime operators[^c-c8266d7c4c7ea98656bd891bdadd335b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies for the purposes of article 9.

(2) The regulator of a maritime operator is—

- (a) the Environment Agency, where the maritime operator—
  - (i) has its registered office or place of residence in England; or
  - (ii) does not have a registered office or a place of residence in the United Kingdom;
- (b) NRW, where the maritime operator has its registered office or place of residence in Wales;
- (c) SEPA, where the maritime operator has its registered office or place of residence in Scotland;
- (d) the chief inspector, where the maritime operator has its registered office or place of residence in Northern Ireland.
