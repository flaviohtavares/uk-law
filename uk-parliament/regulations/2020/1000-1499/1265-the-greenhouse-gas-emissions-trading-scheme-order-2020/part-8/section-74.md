## 74. Procedure for appeals[^key-1fd598f9b26ba80e2c482b864492c56f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 9 (which makes provision in relation to appeals to the Scottish Land Court) has effect.

(2) Schedule 10 (which makes provision in relation to appeals to the Planning Appeals Commission) has effect.
