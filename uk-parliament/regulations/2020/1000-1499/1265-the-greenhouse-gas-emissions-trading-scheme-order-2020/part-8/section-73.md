> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Determination of appeals[^key-a6dfeb9f4dd5c4c8a008dc07b196cd37]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining an appeal under article 70, the appeal body may—

- (a) affirm the decision;
- (b) quash the decision or vary any of its terms;
- (c) substitute a deemed refusal with a decision of the appeal body;
- (d) give directions as to the exercise of the regulator's or the registry administrator's[^key-46465a929e4083f7fba3b6c6e1bfbec6] functions under this Order.

(2) The appeal body may not make a determination that would result in a decision which could not otherwise have been made under this Order.
