# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 69

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Interpretation[^c-6632daf97eb38a15a37a3dbf5d845015]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**appeal body**” has the meaning given in article 71;
- “**decision**” includes a deemed refusal under this Order;
- “**notice**” includes—
  - (a) in the case of a notice determining an application for a permit or the transfer of a permit, the provisions of any permit attached to the notice; and
  - (b) in the case of a notice determining an application for an emissions monitoring plan, the conditions included in the plan issued by the notice.
