> Text as at 2026-07-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Legal professional privilege[^c-b4a903c4617169e76fde042991cc73e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part requires any person to produce a document which that person would be entitled to withhold the production of on grounds of legal professional privilege.
