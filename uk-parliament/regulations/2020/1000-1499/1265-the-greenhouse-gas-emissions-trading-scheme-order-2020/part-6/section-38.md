# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Authorised persons[^key-b5527c37dbb3e5d02ac160d53a6aed0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The regulator may authorise a person to exercise, on behalf of the regulator and in accordance with the terms of the authorisation, the regulator's powers set out in this Part.

(2) In this Part, “**authorised person**” means a person authorised under—

- (a) paragraph (1); or
- (b) section 108(1) of the Environment Act 1995 [^c24438161].
