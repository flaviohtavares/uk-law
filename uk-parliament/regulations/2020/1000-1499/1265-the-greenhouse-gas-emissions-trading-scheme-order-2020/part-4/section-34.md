## 34. Surrender of allowances by aircraft operators[^key-9de8740df58b7982086526618bbd7b1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who is an aircraft operator in relation to a scheme year must surrender, on or before 30th April in the following year, an amount of allowances equal to its aviation emissions in that scheme year (expressed in tonnes).
