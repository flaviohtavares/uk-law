# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 27

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Installations: requirement to surrender allowances[^c-362d16e3afcf6eded7ae91726c454808]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the operator of an installation holds a greenhouse gas emissions permit, the operator must surrender allowances in accordance with the surrender condition of the permit for each scheme year (or part of a scheme year) that the permit is in force.
