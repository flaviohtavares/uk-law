# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 75A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75A. National authority may require regulator, etc. to provide information[^c-a1c59afd26d8ef25fd484d72c3bd9d20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The UK ETS authority or the relevant national authority may, by notice to a regulator or the registry administrator, require the regulator or registry administrator to provide any information that the UK ETS authority or relevant national authority considers necessary or expedient for the exercise of the authority's functions.

(2) The regulator or the registry administrator must comply with a notice under paragraph (1) so far as reasonably practicable.
