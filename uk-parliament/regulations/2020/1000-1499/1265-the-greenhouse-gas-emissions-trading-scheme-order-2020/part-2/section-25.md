# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 25

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Verification of data and accreditation of verifiers[^key-0f0194ffce44b943820d623bb7c83d94][^key-4698ea96679ac53c67995cdfcc76e23d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e3fa9b2ce64f546ac694bfed85e24df8] The Verification Regulation 2018 has effect for the purpose of the UK ETS, subject to the modifications in Schedule 5 (see also paragraph 4 of Schedule 8 which makes further modifications in relation to ultra-small emitters).

(2)[^key-5b1e9303c670895e7e1a1f49a2c59763]  Paragraph (1) does not apply to the verification of maritime emissions.
