# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 24

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Monitoring and reporting of emissions[^c-6ee1e53c5c841f346c33b661e8c52e11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c97b8fe5a25c92a14f05c72457e1c830] The Monitoring and Reporting Regulation 2018[^c-dd17f35d74d4686ab496af2ccc983cfc] has effect for the purpose of the UK ETS, subject to the modifications in Schedule 4 and to Part 4 (see also paragraph 13 of Schedule 7 which makes further modifications in relation to hospitals and small emitters and paragraph 5 of Schedule 8 which makes further modifications in relation to ultra-small emitters).

(2)[^c-d2f2c4caa419a5965ea0f096d3e9afe9] Paragraph (1) does not apply to the monitoring and reporting of maritime emissions.
