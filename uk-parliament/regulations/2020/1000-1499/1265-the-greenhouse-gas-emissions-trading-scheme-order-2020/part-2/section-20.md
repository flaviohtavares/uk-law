# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 20

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Cap for scheme years[^key-4b5439d371e406e9e54b393df9af2df5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-b2c1ad15919062701ff739d6dbe75bc1] The number of allowances created in a scheme year may not exceed [^key-924c4342c1378750a7b6dcd7c7e370a6]...—

- (a) the base for the scheme year multiplied by—
  - (i) if the scheme year is in the 2021-2025 allocation period, the 2021-2025 hospital and small emitter reduction factor;
  - (ii) if the scheme year is in the 2026-2030 [^key-4f460a447e1365f9ee86f249f37fa631]... period, the 2026-2030 hospital and small emitter reduction factor; [^key-e8e261086613a665ef798e5dc447cb5f]...
- (b)[^key-950b1ee8e8695227bee9efa9f7132413] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-bcf61a41158db95cfd5e5fa9c1c38e25] But the restriction in paragraph (1) does not apply to the creation of allowances—

- (a) for the flexible reserve (see article 23A);
- (b) for the new entrants’ reserve (see article 34G);
- (c) for allocation under the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Auctioning Regulations 2021 or any other regulations made under section 96 of the Finance Act 2020.

(3) Nothing in this article overrides[^key-a50ee4334f73f16b49adff938ab2f72c] article 19.
