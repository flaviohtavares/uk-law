# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 16

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. UK Emissions Trading Scheme[^key-d1c29ff34ae0d11928fbd1b21de20c07]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order establishes a trading scheme, known as the “UK Emissions Trading Scheme” or “UK ETS”.

(2) The purpose of the UK ETS is to limit, or encourage the limitation of, the emission of greenhouse gases [^c24438131] in the trading period from the carrying out of—

- (a) regulated activities by operators of installations; [^key-73c51549dc9af153f2899ad2a76650ae]...
- (b) aviation activities by aircraft operators;
- (c)[^key-f940aad61e95877df111309cd48abf91] maritime activities by maritime operators.
