# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Allowances[^c-66e00370aacf04f302cccd3659ac8556]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The UK ETS authority may create allowances in the registry[^c-d8722c1cafd5e3f761e354e115af9d2b] for the purposes of the UK ETS.

(2) An allowance is an allowance to emit 1 tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent.

(3)[^c-41e973f21bbb827358ce4c84fde060ab] Allowances may be held only in accounts in the registry.
