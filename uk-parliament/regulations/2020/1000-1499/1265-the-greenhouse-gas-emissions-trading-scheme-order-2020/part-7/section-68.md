> Text as at 2026-07-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Inspection: refusal to allow access to premises[^c-b365d79fde930cab4239b4a7a185f768]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person in control of premises is liable to a civil penalty of £50,000 where the person does not allow the regulator or authorised person (within the meaning of Part 6) access to the premises contrary to article 39(3).
