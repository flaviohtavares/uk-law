# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 60A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60A. Installations: failure to submit information under article 27A[^c-5e2cebcdbdf3071afc0c6feb7f8a636f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an installation is liable to a civil penalty where the operator fails to comply (or to comply on time) with the requirements of article 27A.

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £5,000; and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £500 for each day that the operator fails to comply with the requirements of article 27A, beginning with the day on which the initial notice is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
