> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Aviation: failure to report aviation emissions[^c-4b0df51725db2ce1bfec211a34b2dc4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft operator is liable to a civil penalty where the aircraft operator fails to submit (or to submit on time) a verified report of aviation emissions to the regulator, contrary to article 33(1).

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000; and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £500 for each day that the report is not submitted, beginning with the day on which the initial notice is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
