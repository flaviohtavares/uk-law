# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 64A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64A. Failure to comply with notice to return allowances[^c-7ed2b06ad00b6f4423aa3385ccee501a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is liable to a civil penalty where the person fails to comply (or to comply on time) with the requirements of a notice (the “notice to return allowances”) given under article 34V.

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000; and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £1,000 for each day that the person fails to comply with the requirements of the notice to return allowances, beginning with the day on which the initial notice is given.
