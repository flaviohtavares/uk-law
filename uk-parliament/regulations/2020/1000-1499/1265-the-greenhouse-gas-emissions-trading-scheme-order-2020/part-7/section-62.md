# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 62

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Aviation: failure to comply with condition of emissions monitoring plan[^c-135c19ab792b05f087cbe04683cc71ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft operator is liable to a civil penalty where the aircraft operator fails to comply (or to comply on time) with a condition of an emissions monitoring plan, contrary to article 32(2).

(2) The civil penalty is—

- (a) £20,000; and
- (b) a daily penalty at a daily rate of £500 for each day that the person fails to comply with the condition, beginning with the day on which the initial notice is given, up to a maximum of £45,000.
