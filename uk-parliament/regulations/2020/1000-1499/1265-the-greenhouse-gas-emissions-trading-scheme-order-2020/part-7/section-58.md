# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020, section 58

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Installations: failure to apply to surrender permit[^c-66fbee36b577545867f5427aa93c7b94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an installation is liable to a civil penalty of £5,000 where the operator fails to apply (or to apply on time) to surrender a permit, contrary to paragraph 11(1) of Schedule 6.
