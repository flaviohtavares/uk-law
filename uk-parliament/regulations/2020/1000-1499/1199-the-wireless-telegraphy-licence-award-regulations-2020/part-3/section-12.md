# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 12

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Publication of names of qualified applicants[^key-52e70908e1d0f3004358c32d08bcccc4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM shall notify each applicant qualified to participate of—

- (a) the name of each other applicant who is so qualified; and
- (b) the names of their associates.

(2) OFCOM shall publish the names of all applicants who are so qualified on OFCOM's website.
