# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 99

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Determination of 3.6 GHz lot additional prices where there is a valid full adjacency agreement[^c-a688a6bb3f79c06760b81cb438f47772]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where OFCOM determined the assignment of 3.6 GHz lots under regulation 96, there is no additional price to be paid by each winning bidder for 3.6 GHz lots, in respect of 3.6 GHz lots.
