# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 103

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Determination of 700 MHz individual frequency lot additional prices[^key-f63c398d256ae2d32cc76cc48fa4ab34]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In respect of each winning 700 MHz individual frequency lot assignment stage bid, OFCOM shall determine an amount in whole pounds (“700 MHz individual frequency lot additional price”) which shall be payable by the relevant winning bidder in accordance with Schedule 5.
