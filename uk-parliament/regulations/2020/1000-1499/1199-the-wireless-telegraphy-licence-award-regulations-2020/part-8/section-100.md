# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 100

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Determination of 3.6 GHz lot additional prices where there is one or more valid partial adjacency agreements[^c-a6a3b7eeba26a5b8801f4bd3b0684fb6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where OFCOM determined the assignment of 3.6 GHz lots under regulation 97—

- (a) there is no additional price to be paid by each winning bidder for 3.6 GHz lots which is party to a valid partial adjacency agreement, in respect of 3.6 GHz lots; and
- (b) in respect of each winning 3.6 GHz assignment stage bid made by a bidder which is not party to a valid partial adjacency agreement, OFCOM shall determine an amount in whole pounds (the “3.6 GHz lot additional price”) which shall be payable by the relevant winning bidder in accordance with Schedule 4.
