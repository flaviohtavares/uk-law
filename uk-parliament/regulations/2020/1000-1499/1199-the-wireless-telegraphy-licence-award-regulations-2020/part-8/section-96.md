# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 96

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Determination of assignment of 3.6 GHz lots where there is a valid full adjacency agreement[^key-fa15f9179e0de09a736c09e12c9e2073]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If there is a valid full adjacency agreement, OFCOM shall determine the assignment of the particular numbered 3.6 GHz lots in accordance with the valid full adjacency agreement after the deadline for paying the required assignment stage deposit under regulation 81 (and after any negotiation period has ended) and notify those assignments to each winning bidder for 3.6 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 106.
