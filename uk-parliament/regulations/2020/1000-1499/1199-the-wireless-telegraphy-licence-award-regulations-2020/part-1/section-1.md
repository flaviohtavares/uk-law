# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, and extent[^c-7aa3e4a25a38723acc08c33136e46bed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020 and shall come into force on 18th November 2020.

(2) These Regulations shall not extend to the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man.
