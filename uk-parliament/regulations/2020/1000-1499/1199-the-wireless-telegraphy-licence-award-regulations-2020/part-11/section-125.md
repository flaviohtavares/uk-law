# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 125

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. General power of exclusion[^key-7dc3a5200c23ad49c9a459a6d6c900dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

OFCOM may, at any time, notify any applicant or bidder of its exclusion from the award process, if required to do so by any direction given by the Secretary of State to OFCOM under section 5 of the Communications Act 2003 [^c24321371] or if, after OFCOM's determination under regulation 10(1) (and notwithstanding that determination), in their opinion the applicant or bidder is not a fit and proper person to hold a licence.
