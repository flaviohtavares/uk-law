# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 22

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Principal stage bids[^key-e5a5fa30a6011060021632a00e767099]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each principal stage bid is a bid for the inclusion in a licence of one lot of a particular type at the round price for a lot of that type.
