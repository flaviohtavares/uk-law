# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 20

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Principal stage rounds[^c-bc039cd1f2ea9ca5559acd3abb1b59b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) During the principal stage, there shall be one or more principal stage rounds.

(2) A principal stage round shall begin and end at the times notified by OFCOM under regulation 45.

(3) The number of principal stage rounds shall be determined by OFCOM in accordance with regulation 23.
