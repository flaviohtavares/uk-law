# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 50

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Submission of principal stage form to OFCOM[^c-a5d0a882fa2998257722d20fd19d32e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A bidder must submit the completed principal stage form to OFCOM by using the electronic auction system, except as provided for in regulation 51.

(2) The completed principal stage form must be received by OFCOM by the time notified by OFCOM under regulation 45(a) as the end of the principal stage round.
