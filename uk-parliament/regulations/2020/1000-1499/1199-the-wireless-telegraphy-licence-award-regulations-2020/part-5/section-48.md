# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 48

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Notification of results of the principal stage to bidders[^key-26c0e1cfe96624e35e8da17e0315a3a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After the end of the final principal stage round, and after the determination of the winning principal stage bids and the determination of the base price for each winning principal stage bid, OFCOM shall notify each bidder of—

- (a) the end of the principal stage;
- (b) the names of the winning bidders, and in respect of each of them—
  - (i) the number of 3.6 GHz lots won, the 3.6 GHz base price;
  - (ii) the number of 700 MHz individual frequency lots won, the 700 MHz individual frequency lot base price; and
  - (iii) the number of 700 MHz paired frequency lots won, the 700 MHz paired frequency lot base price; and
- (c) the number of 3.6 GHz lots, the number of 700 MHz individual frequency lots and the number of 700 MHz paired frequency lots not won by any bidder.
