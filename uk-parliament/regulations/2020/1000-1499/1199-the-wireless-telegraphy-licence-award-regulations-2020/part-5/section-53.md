# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 53

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Notification that principal stage form is not valid[^c-b34ffcfac7aec4f5b2ac72ae3a1171ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a bidder submits a principal stage form that is not a valid principal stage form during a principal stage round, OFCOM shall, after the end of that principal stage round, notify the bidder of that fact and shall give the reasons why the principal stage form is not a valid principal stage form.
