# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 47

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Publication of information at the end of each day during the principal stage[^c-d2fac8f83119bd96068831ceabd6b4ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After the end of the latest principal stage round to take place on any given day, OFCOM shall publish on their website, in relation to that latest principal stage round—

- (a) the round price for—
  - (i) a 3.6 GHz lot;
  - (ii) a 700 MHz individual frequency lot; and
  - (iii) a 700 MHz paired frequency lot;
- (b) for the 3.6 GHz lot type, the amount in MHz which is the smallest strictly positive whole multiple of twenty which is greater than the excess demand;
- (c) for the 700 MHz individual frequency lot type, the amount in MHz which is the smallest strictly positive whole multiple of ten which is greater than the excess demand; and
- (d) for the 700 MHz paired frequency lot type, the amount in MHz which is the smallest strictly positive whole multiple of twenty which is greater than the excess demand.
