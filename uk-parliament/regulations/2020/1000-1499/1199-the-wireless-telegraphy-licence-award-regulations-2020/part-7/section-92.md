# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 92

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Only one valid adjacency agreement[^c-c7c1c2584c2e8e40eef43bc56830ebba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

— Each winning bidder for 3.6 GHz lots may only be party to one valid adjacency agreement.
