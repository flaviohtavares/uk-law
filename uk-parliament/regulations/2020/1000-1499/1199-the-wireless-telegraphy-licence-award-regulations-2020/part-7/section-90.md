# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 90

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Rejection of full adjacency agreement forms submitted to OFCOM at a time outside the first phase and second phase of the negotiation period[^key-9191e45765100f586194b2fc5d3220a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a full adjacency agreement form is submitted to OFCOM at a time other than during the permitted times described in paragraph (2) that form (and the agreement which the form purports to describe) shall be rejected by OFCOM.

(2) The permitted times are—

- (a) after the start time of the first phase of the negotiation period and before the first phase of the negotiation period has ended in accordance with regulation 95; and
- (b) after the start time and before the end time of the second phase of the negotiation period.
