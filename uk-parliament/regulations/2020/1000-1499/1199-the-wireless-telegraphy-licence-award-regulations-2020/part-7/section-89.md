# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 89

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Consequence of additional required bids[^key-092e6036f0d7c7686bcc083edb7bb17a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If there is a valid partial adjacency agreement, when OFCOM is determining the winning 3.6 GHz assignment stage bids under regulation 97—

- (a) each 3.6 GHz assignment stage bid already submitted by that bidder under regulation 78 or 79, if any, shall be deemed invalid; and
- (b) OFCOM must instead take into account the additional required bids as if they were the valid assignment stage bids made by the bidder concerned.
