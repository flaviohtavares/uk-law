# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 109

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Licences which may be granted under this award process[^key-a36007605b197b9e87659f99cac754e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licences which may be granted to a bidder under this award process are—

- (a) for a bidder which won 3.6 GHz lots, a 3.6 GHz licence;
- (b) for a bidder which won—
  - (i) 700 MHz individual frequency lots;
  - (ii) 700 MHz paired frequency lots; or
  - (iii) both 700 MHz individual frequency lots and 700 MHz paired frequency lots,

  a 700 MHz licence.
