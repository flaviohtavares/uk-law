# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 110

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 3.6 GHz licence[^c-bad050a71f91877f8489e20d91ad4cde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 3.6 GHz licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder's total 3.6 GHz base price; and
- (b) that bidder's 3.6 GHz lot additional price (if any).
