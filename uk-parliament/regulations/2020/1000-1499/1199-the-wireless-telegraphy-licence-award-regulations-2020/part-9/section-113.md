# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 113

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Particular frequencies to be included in 700 MHz lot licence granted to a winning bidder[^c-c97a9619c5320994cc9c5ce6f82e8ce9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a winning bidder won 700 MHz individual frequency lots or 700 MHz paired frequency lots, any 700 MHz licence granted to that bidder shall authorise—

- (a) where that bidder made a winning 700 MHz individual frequency lot assignment stage bid, the frequencies corresponding to the 700 MHz individual frequency lot assignment stage option for which the bidder made that winning bid; and
- (b) where that bidder made a winning 700 MHz paired frequency lot assignment stage bid, the frequencies corresponding to the 700 MHz paired frequency lot assignment stage option for which the bidder made that winning bid.
