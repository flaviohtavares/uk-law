# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 111

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 700 MHz licence[^key-dddc9737a1ceae8fc95cf7c709cad4da]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 700 MHz licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder's total 700 MHz individual frequency lot base price;
- (b) that bidder's total 700 MHz paired frequency lot base price;
- (c) that bidder's 700 MHz individual frequency lot additional price (if any); and
- (d) that bidder's 700 MHz paired frequency lot additional price (if any).
