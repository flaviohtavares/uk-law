# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 108

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Determination of licences, licence fees and frequencies associated with particular licences[^key-29ed5653ef27b1b1c83296e9109a6ab0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After notification of the results of the assignment stage under regulation 106 OFCOM shall determine—

- (a) the licences which may be granted; and
- (b) for each licence—
  - (i) the licence fee payable to OFCOM; and
  - (ii) the particular frequencies.
