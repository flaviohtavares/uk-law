# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 118

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Grant of licences[^c-1620c22f8ed43a59efe8bceca5a27997]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licences referred to in regulation 117(1) are—

- (a) in respect of a winning bidder of 3.6 GHz lots, a 3.6 GHz licence which authorises the use of the frequencies determined in accordance with regulation 112; and
- (b) in respect of a winning bidder of—
  - (i) 700 MHz individual frequency lots,
  - (ii) 700 MHz paired frequency lots; or
  - (iii) both 700 MHz individual frequency lots and 700 MHz paired frequency lots,

a 700 MHz licence which authorises the use of the frequencies determined in accordance with regulation 113.
