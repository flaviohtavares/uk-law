# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 60

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Assignment of 700 MHz paired frequency lots without bidding[^c-8d5eef4863e9d1f4baff44dc6dfa5011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there is only one winning bidder for all of the 700 MHz paired frequency lots, OFCOM shall assign all of the 700 MHz paired frequency lots to that winning bidder.
