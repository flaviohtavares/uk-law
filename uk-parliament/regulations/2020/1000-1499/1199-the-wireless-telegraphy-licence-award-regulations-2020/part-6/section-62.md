# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 62

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Assignment of numbered 700 MHz individual frequency lots by bidding[^key-006c1cebe250ebc2b015228643332a7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where regulation 59 applies, the particular numbered 700 MHz individual frequency lots that are to be assigned to the winning bidder or bidders for the 700 MHz individual frequency lots shall be determined by bidding in the assignment stage round in accordance with the procedure set out in regulations 71 to 73.
