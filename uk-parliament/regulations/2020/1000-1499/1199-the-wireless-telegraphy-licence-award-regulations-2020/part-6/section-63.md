# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 63

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Assignment of numbered 700 MHz paired frequency lots by bidding[^key-08fb0fd3a9a610046a48b54ec615d8ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where regulation 60 applies, the particular numbered 700 MHz paired frequency lots that are to be assigned to the winning bidder or bidders for the 700 MHz paired frequency lots shall be determined by bidding in the assignment stage round in accordance with the procedure set out in regulations 74 to 76.
