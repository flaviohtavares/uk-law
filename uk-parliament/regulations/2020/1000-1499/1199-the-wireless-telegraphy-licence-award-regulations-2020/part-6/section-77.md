# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 77

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Notice to bidders before the assignment stage round[^c-29c4f85703eb0223acf221d0f47a8daa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the start of the assignment stage round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder that may make an assignment stage bid in the assignment stage round of—

- (a) the 3.6 GHz assignment stage options, 700 MHz individual frequency lot assignment stage options and the 700 MHz paired frequency lot assignment stage options, that are available to that bidder in the assignment stage round; and
- (b) the date on which, and the times on that date within which, the assignment stage round will take place.
