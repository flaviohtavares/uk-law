# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 61

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Assignment of 3.6 GHz lots by bidding[^c-96c7971c68e511f21a6d259b51295b56]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where regulation 58 applies, the particular numbered 3.6 GHz lots that are to be assigned to the winning bidders for 3.6 GHz lots shall be determined by—

- (a) bidding in the assignment stage round in accordance with the procedure set out in regulations 68 to 70; and
- (b) the outcome of the negotiation period (if any).
