# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 66

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Valid assignment stage form[^c-60e69e4b3f352ee5e0af72a1104a2192]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An assignment stage form is a valid assignment stage form if—

- (a) the bidder has complied with regulations 70(1) and 70(2), regulations 73(1) and 73(2) or regulations 76(1) and 76(2) in respect of all assignment stage bids specified on the form; and
- (b) the bidder has submitted the form in accordance with regulation 78 or 79.
