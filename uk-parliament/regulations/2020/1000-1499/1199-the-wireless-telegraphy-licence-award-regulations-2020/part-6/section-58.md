# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 58

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Assignment of numbered 3.6 GHz lots without bidding[^c-af1cab4c9c9df87fb6fef4a517705f87]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there is only one winning bidder for all of the 3.6 GHz lots, OFCOM shall assign all of the 3.6 GHz lots to that winning bidder.
