# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 56

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. No assignment stage if there are no winning principal stage bidders[^key-bda10b44f42f68a52802af3c84a9088d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there are no winning bidders following completion of the principal stage there shall be no assignment stage and no licences shall be granted.
