# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 67

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Notification that an assignment stage form is not valid[^key-de50207d4d2dec8ba20a426cf645b1fb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a bidder submits an assignment stage form which is not a valid assignment stage form, OFCOM shall, after the end of the assignment stage round, notify the bidder of that fact and shall give the reasons why the assignment stage form is not a valid assignment stage form.
