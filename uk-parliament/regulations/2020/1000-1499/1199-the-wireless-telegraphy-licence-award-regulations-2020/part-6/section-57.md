# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 57

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Assignment stage process[^c-60853769977bd873ed5a0f04721ee4a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there is one winning bidder, or more than one winning bidder, following completion of the principal stage, there shall be one assignment stage round and OFCOM shall determine the assignment of lots in light of that round (and in light of the negotiation period, if any).
