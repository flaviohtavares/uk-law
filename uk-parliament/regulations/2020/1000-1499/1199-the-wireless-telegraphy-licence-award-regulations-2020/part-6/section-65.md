# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2020, section 65

> Text as at 2021-03-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Assignment stage form[^key-376d4cd0a48a42d49eeb835139f2294b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM shall provide each winning bidder with an assignment stage form.

(2) To bid in the assignment stage round, a bidder must specify on the assignment stage form—

- (a) the bidder's 3.6 GHz assignment stage bids, if any, in accordance with regulation 70;
- (b) the bidder's 700 MHz individual frequency lot assignment stage bids, if any, in accordance with regulation 73; and
- (c) the bidder's 700 MHz paired frequency lot assignment stage bids, if any, in accordance with regulation 76.
