# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-b379948ba1fe2cd540cf2d972e2c57a0]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020;
- (b) come into force on IP completion day; and
- (c) extend to Northern Ireland only.
