## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendments of subordinate legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 3

### PART 1 — Amendments coming into force on 21st December 2020

#### 1. Electronic Communications (Universal Service) Order 2003[^c-6fdafb3d974a7206231b9992613d727a]

In the Electronic Communications (Universal Service) Order 2003 [^c24363081], in the Schedule, omit paragraph 5(2).

#### 2. Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy Regulations 2011[^c-a0f8523d9643f3984c3ea63babd4626b]

In the Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy Regulations 2011 [^c24363091], omit regulation 5 [^c24363101].

#### 3. Broadcasting (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-33b22d21905a3744b99337612aac7303]

In the Broadcasting (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24363111], in Schedule 1, in paragraph 19, omit sub-paragraphs (2)(b) and (3).

#### 4. Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-825bce3785a8f7f7aa2e278828fb2453]

(1) The Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24363121] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1, omit the following—

- (a) paragraph 3(2) and (10);
- (b) paragraph 4(2);
- (c) paragraph 5;
- (d) paragraph 13(2);
- (e) paragraph 17(2);
- (f) paragraph 21(2);
- (g) paragraph 23(3);
- (h) paragraph 31(5);
- (i) paragraph 37;
- (j) in paragraph 40, the words “and (3)”;
- (k) paragraph 45(4).

(3) In Schedule 2, omit paragraph 1.

#### 5. Electronic Communications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-ea2283f2d4894d450a36ebd19f7dafa7]

In the Electronic Communications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24363131], omit regulation 2.
