# The Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment) (European Electronic Communications Code and EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Review of legacy services[^key-bd237d1ad72fc640496f2eb3ffdfbb66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

During the period beginning with 21st December 2020 and ending with IP completion day, section 72A [^c24362381] of the Communications Act 2003 [^c24362391] is to have effect as if after subsection (3) there were inserted—

> (3A) OFCOM may on their own initiative review and report to the Secretary of State on the extent to which any provision falling within section 65(2BC) made by the universal service order continues to be necessary in the circumstances.

[^c24362401].
