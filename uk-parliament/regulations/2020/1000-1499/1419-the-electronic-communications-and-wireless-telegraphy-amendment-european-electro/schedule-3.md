# The Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy (Amendment) (European Electronic Communications Code and EU Exit) Regulations 2020, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Amendments of retained direct EU legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 4

### 1. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2243[^c-1786ede8bc9b57f0a167f8235a933d8b]

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2243 of 17 December 2019 establishing a template for the contract summary to be used by providers of publicly available electronic communications services pursuant to Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council is amended as follows.

(2) Before Article 1 insert—

> **Article A1 — Interpretation**
>
> In this Regulation “**electronic communications service**” has the same meaning as in the Communications Act 2003.

.

(3) After Article 3, omit “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.”.

(4) In Part A of the Annex, under the heading “Contract summary”, omit “as required by EU law”.

(5) In Part B of the Annex—

- (a) in the text under the heading “Section “Services and equipment””—
  - (i) for “bundles pursuant to Article 107 of Directive (EU) 2018/1972,” substitute “ bundled contracts as defined by section 51(8) of the Communications Act 2003, ”;
  - (ii) for “bundles”, in the second and third place it occurs, substitute “ bundled contracts ”;
- (b) in the text under the heading “Section “Duration, renewal and termination””, for “by Union or national law” substitute “ by an enactment or at common law ”;
- (c) in the text under the heading “Section “Other relevant information””, for “by Union or national law” substitute “ by an enactment or at common law ”.
