# The National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1335

National Health Service, England

The National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made

at 2.00 p.m. on 23rd November 2020

Laid before Parliament

at 5.30 p.m. on 23rd November 2020

Coming into force

14th December 2020

The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 176(1) and (2)(c) and 272(7) and (8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 14th December 2020.

## 2. Amendment of the National Health Service (Dental Charges) Regulations 2005

(1) The National Health Service (Dental Charges) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4 (calculation of charges)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “£22.70” substitute “£23.80”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “£62.10” substitute “£65.20”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), for “£269.30” substitute “£282.80”;
- (d) in paragraph (4), for “£269.30” substitute “£282.80”; and
- (e) in paragraph (5), for “£22.70” substitute “£23.80”.

(3) In regulation 12A (charges in respect of primary dental services provided under a prototype agreement), in paragraph (2) for “£22.70” substitute “£23.80”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care.

Jo Churchill

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

At 2.00 p.m. on 23rd November 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Dental Charges) Regulations 2005 (“the 2005 Regulations”).

The 2005 Regulations include the charges that are levied for NHS dental and orthodontic treatment in England. Regulation 2 amends regulation 4 of the 2005 Regulations to increase the charges for Band 1 courses of treatment from £22.70 to £23.80, Band 2 courses of treatment from £62.10 to £65.20 and Band 3 courses of treatment from £269.30 to £282.80. Regulation 2 also amends regulation 12A of the 2005 Regulations to increase the charge for a Band 1A interim care course of treatment provided by contractors participating in the Prototype Agreement Scheme, from £22.70 to £23.80, in line with the Band 1 charge.

An assessment of the impact of this instrument has been made. A copy of this impact assessment is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside this instrument on the legislation.gov.uk website. Copies may also be obtained from the Department of Health and Social Care, 39 Victoria Street, London SW1H 0EU.
