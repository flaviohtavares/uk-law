# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 45C(1), (3)(c) and (4)(d), 45F(2) and 45P of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001].

These Regulations are made in response to the serious and imminent threat to public health which is posed by the incidence and spread of severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) in England.

The Secretary of State considers that the restrictions and requirements imposed by the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) Regulations 2020[^f00002] as amended by these Regulations are proportionate to what they seek to achieve, which is a public health response to that threat.

In accordance with section 45R of that Act, the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020.

(2) They come into force on 21st November 2020.

## 2. Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) Regulations 2020

In the Schedule to the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) Regulations 2020—

- (a) in paragraph 30—
  - (i) number the existing text as sub-paragraph (1);
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (1), as so numbered, insert—

    > (2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not prevent a farm from operating a business selling or supplying Christmas trees in accordance with paragraph 54.

    ;
- (b) after paragraph 53, insert—

  > **54.** Businesses selling or supplying natural Christmas trees.

  .

Bethell

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Care

At 1.35 p.m. on 20th November 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (England) (No. 4) Regulations 2020 to permit shops, stalls, Christmas tree farms and other businesses selling or supplying natural Christmas trees to open.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
