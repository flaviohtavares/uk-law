# The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network (Correction) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1053

Infrastructure Planning

The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network (Correction) Order 2020

Made

28th September 2020

Coming into force

29th September 2020

The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network Order 2020 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003], for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Shropshire County Council, as the relevant local planning authority that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network (Correction) Order 2020 and comes into force on 29th September 2020.

## 2. Corrections

The North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network Order 2020 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Gareth Leigh

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

28th September 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network Order 2020, a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
