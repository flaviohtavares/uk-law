# The Animals, Aquatic Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Propagating Material and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application[^c-4b2917f3bb79c9821877963704148c73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals, Aquatic Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Propagating Material and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) They come into force—

- (a) as regards this Part, Part 5 and regulations 39 and 40, immediately before IP completion day;
- (b) as regards regulation 41, on 4th May 2021;
- (c) otherwise on IP completion day.

(3) These Regulations extend as follows—

- (a) Regulations 2, 4 to 19 and the Schedule extend to England and Wales and Scotland;
- (b) Regulation 41 extends to England and Wales.

(4) Regulations 20, 22(2), 23, 25, 27, 28(2)(d), (6)(c) and (d), 30(6) and (7), 31, 33 and 34 apply in relation to England and Wales and Scotland only.
