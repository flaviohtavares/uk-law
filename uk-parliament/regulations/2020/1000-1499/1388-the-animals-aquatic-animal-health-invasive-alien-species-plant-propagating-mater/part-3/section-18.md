# The Animals, Aquatic Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Propagating Material and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/878[^c-d241689769ac3e8e6c2c4c426374566b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/878 adopting the list of Member States, or parts of the territory of Member States, that comply with the rules for categorisation laid down in Article 2(2) and (3) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/772 concerning the application of preventive health measures for the control of *Echinococcus multilocularis* infection in dogs is revoked.
