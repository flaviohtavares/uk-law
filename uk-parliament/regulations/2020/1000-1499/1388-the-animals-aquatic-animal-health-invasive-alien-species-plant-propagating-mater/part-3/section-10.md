# The Animals, Aquatic Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Propagating Material and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 10

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Commission [Decision 2010/221/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2010/0221)[^key-fe8dbcddaccff8c78ce62642fed6a657]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Commission [Decision 2010/221/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2010/0221) approving national measures for limiting the impact of certain diseases in aquaculture animals and wild aquatic animals in accordance with Article 43 of Council [Directive 2006/88/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0088) is revoked.
