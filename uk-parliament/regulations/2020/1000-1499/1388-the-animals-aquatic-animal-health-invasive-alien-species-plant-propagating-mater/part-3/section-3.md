> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Council [Decision 2005/834/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0834)[^c-b812b1ccecf904b40c4d2ace90863d18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Decision [2005/834/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0834) on the equivalence of checks on practices for the maintenance of varieties carried out in certain third countries is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit “third”;
- (b) for “by the Member States” substitute—

  > :
  >
  > - (a) in relation to [Directive 66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401), in the United Kingdom;
  > - (b) in relation to the other Directives, in Great Britain

  .

(3) Omit Articles 2, 3 and 6.
