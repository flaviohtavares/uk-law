# The Animals, Aquatic Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Propagating Material and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 41

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. The Invasive Alien Species (Enforcement and Permitting) Order 2019[^c-b3db6e0f274003f14255b2b7da4841a5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 2 to the Invasive Alien Species (Enforcement and Permitting) Order 2019[^f00083] is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1, in the table—

- (a) after the entry for “Duck, Ruddy” insert—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Flatworm, New Zealand | *Arthurdendyus triangulates* |
- (b) after the entry for “Goose, Egyptian” insert—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Pumpkinseed | *Lepomis gibbosus* |

(3) In Part 2, in the table, after the entry for “Rhubarb, Giant (otherwise known as Chilean Rhubarb)” insert—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Salvinia Moss | *Salvinia molesta* (otherwise known as *salvinia adnata)* |
