> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 22. The Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Breeders’ Rights and Seeds (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-360d0b511b277035cd7917453e71a6a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Breeders’ Rights and Seeds (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00055] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 5—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) omit sub-paragraph (b);
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (d)(i), for “the United Kingdom” in the substituted text, in both places where it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”;
  - (iii) omit sub-paragraph (g);
- (b) omit paragraphs (3) and (4).

(3) Omit regulation 9.
