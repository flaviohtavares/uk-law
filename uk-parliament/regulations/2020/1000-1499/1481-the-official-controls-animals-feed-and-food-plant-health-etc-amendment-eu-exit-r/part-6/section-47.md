# The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 47

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Transitional modification and derogation for the purpose of listing third countries.[^c-addb84e0799427bd558c73c2cecf8841]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of Article 125 to 126 of Regulation (EU) 2017/625, the appropriate authority is not required to request the information specified in Article 125 of that Regulation before including the third countries specified in paragraph 2 in the list of third countries referred to in Article 126(2) of that Regulation.

(2) The third countries specified in this paragraph are—

- (a) EEA member States;
- (b) the Faroe Islands;
- (c) Greenland;
- (d) Switzerland.
