# The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, SCHEDULE

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE — Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 45

**1.**[^c-caefbf09bd104d29a5efb9c04ce30649] Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/329 designating a European Union Reference Centre for Animal Welfare.

**2.**[^c-cf202ee2817ad09108b203c6dc040013] Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/631 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council by establishing European Union reference laboratories for pests of plants.

**3.**[^c-7bfdf59cd87724148dca6bfd225ea71c] Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1587 revoking the designation of the Istituto Superiore di Sanita, Rome, Italy as a European Reference Laboratory for the residues listed in Annex 1, Group B(3)(c) to Council Directive [96/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0023).

**4.**[^c-bf0936e96a3faec1f22c629c4509d506] Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/530 designating European Reference laboratories for pests of plants on insects and mites, nematodes, bacteria, fungi and oomycetes, viruses, viroids and phytoplasmas.

**5.**[^c-90455961b432cd02133246bb880941db] Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1685 of 4 October 2019 designating a European Union Reference Centre for Animal Welfare for poultry and other small farmed animals.
