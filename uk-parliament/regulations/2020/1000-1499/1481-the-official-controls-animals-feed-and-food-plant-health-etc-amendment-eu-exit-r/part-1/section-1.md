## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-97e805d6ea2e34865737f6592e81c800]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health etc.) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 and come into force on IP completion day , except Part 5 which comes into force immediately before IP completion day[^c-f0561793cddd6867e4130ca77dbb781a].
