> Text as at 2026-05-13.

## 57. Monetary penalties[^c-5cd311c6dfe2358746a3db1a95b20b12][^c-c75aa7e9812134b6fcc332711ffa9fce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 5 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c24334921].
