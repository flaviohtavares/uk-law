# The Yemen (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020, section 56

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-8d3929cec7f7b49ab71ca7fd6650fb1e][^c-02c862b46aa9c8c3da78e30b4343efb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c24334911] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 36 (finance: licensing offences).
