# The Yemen (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020, section 66

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Revocations[^key-dcc06ebd066a5abe877a6d8c5aff5937][^key-61c5b1ec7ec33cb165112fb56f316130]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 1352/2014 of 18 December 2014, concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Yemen [^c24335031] is revoked.

(2) The Yemen (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2014 [^c24335041] are revoked.

(3) The Export Control (Yemen Sanctions) Regulations 2015 [^c24335051] are revoked.

(4) The Yemen (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 [^c24335061] are revoked.
