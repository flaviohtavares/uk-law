## 67. Amendment of the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017[^c-1b8ed67b19379fd30d5a802738005bd2][^c-c9e73b2ac6dfcd817b43082343746942]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Schedule to the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017 [^c24335071], omit the following row from the table—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | United Nations Security Council Resolution 2140 (2014) | Council Regulation (EU) No. 1352/2014 of 18th December 2014 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Yemen. |
