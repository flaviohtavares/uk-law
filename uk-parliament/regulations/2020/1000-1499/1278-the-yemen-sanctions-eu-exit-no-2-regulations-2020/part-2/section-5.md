# The Yemen (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-8facfbab03638c273a117a05afd42454][^key-4d53057066c05beea2de99406f4adf37]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons [^c24334581] by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-b2b06b1fcd437cd978f072cc1be17b71] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulation 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.);
- (aa)[^key-adc5c8990944a013d42ceab877561a99] regulation 17A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 18 (immigration);
- (c) regulations 20 to 27 (arms embargo etc.).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
