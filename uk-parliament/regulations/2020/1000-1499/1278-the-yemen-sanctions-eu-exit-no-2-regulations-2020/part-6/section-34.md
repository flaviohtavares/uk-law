## 34. Trade licences[^key-60c9da7a611364079869cba23069f89e][^key-1542a6236ea8f7de5adb266bcc517a08]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
