# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020, section 2

> Text as at 2025-12-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Extension of the Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^key-81ac3592ae8d22e7416c88ea9af93afb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00004] as amended from time to time extend to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1 with the modifications specified in Schedule 2.
