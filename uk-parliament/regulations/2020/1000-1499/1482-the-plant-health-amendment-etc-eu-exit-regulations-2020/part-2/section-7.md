## 7. Part 2[^key-3a90191e880617a85d6713c16b7789b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Part 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 6(3)—

- (a) in the definition of “forestry professional operator”—
  - (i) in the words before paragraph (a), omit “EU”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), for “the Union territory”, in both places where it occurs, substitute “ Great Britain or a CD territory ”;
  - (iii) after paragraph (c), insert—

    > - (ca) the introduction of forestry material into Northern Ireland from England;

    ;
- (b) omit the definition of “official controls”.
