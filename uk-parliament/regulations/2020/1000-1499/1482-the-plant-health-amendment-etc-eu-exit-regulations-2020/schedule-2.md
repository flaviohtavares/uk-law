# The Plant Health (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Transitional provision: EU plants, plant products and other objects

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 52(2)(a)

**1.**[^key-60d589033360949253587c6f64d51d3e] Machinery and vehicles which have been operated for agricultural or forestry purposes.

**2.**[^key-05125522a2c6bd147a59c0da8a02a551] Plants for planting, other than seeds.

**3.**[^key-9880fb05119f27eaf394ab6017068342] Tubers of *Solanum tuberosum* L. intended for planting (seed potatoes).

**4.**[^key-ccc0207d9cdd63ccbd1ea9400079f033] Tubers of *Solanum tuberosum* L. (ware potatoes).

**5.**[^key-1b2791c3cf24bf3130f2f3a7ba2d36d6] Seeds of—

- (a) *Allium cepa* L.,
- (b) *Allium porrum* L.,
- (c) *Castanea* Mill.,
- (d) *Capsicum* spp. L.,
- (e) *Helianthus annuus* L.,
- (f) *Medicago sativa* L.,
- (g) *Phaseolus cocineus*.,
- (h) *Phaseolus vulgaris* L.,
- (i) *Solanum lycopersicum* L.,
- (j) *Solanum tuberosum* L. (True potato seed).

**6.**[^key-a745db5f906bf40d2b69530525f9977a] Vegetable seeds of *Pisum sativum* L. and *Vicia faba* L.

**7.**[^key-8ff2b7e310e982ffafa1d4f2c7cce8c2] Seeds of oil and fibre plants of—

- (a) *Brassica napus* L.,
- (b) *Brassica rapa* L.,
- (c) *Sinapis alba* L.,
- (d) *Glycine max* (L.) Merrill,
- (e) *Linum usitatissimum* L.

**8.**[^key-0ca4f5329f22049829151a31f7c929d3] Isolated bark of—

- (a) *Castanea* Mill.,
- (b) Conifers (Pinales),
- (c) *Juglans* L.,
- (d) *Pterocarya* Kunth*.*

**9.**[^key-20adabfed00ff47e1f8651c46a5db453] Wood of *Platanus* L., including wood which has not kept its natural round surface.

**10.**[^key-1413e46c1593565ea1605e06d813aaa1] Wood of Castanea Mill., excluding wood which is bark-free.

**11.**[^key-69c86bd3fa5c17019e065f7602d9a7d4] Wood of Conifers (Pinales), including wood which has not kept its natural round surface.

**12.**[^key-f5dd0357da0f75687bff932a90cdd6d8] Wood of *Prunus* L. including wood which has not kept its natural round surface, other than wood of *Prunus laurocerasus* L.

**13.**[^key-5b433e7a691c1a92de69e1146a5216cf] Wood, including wood which has not kept its natural round surface of—

- (a) *Acer* L.,
- (b) *Aesculus* L.,
- (c) *Alnus* L.,
- (d) *Betula* L.,
- (e) *Carpinus* L.,
- (f) *Cercidiphyllum* Siebold & Zucc.,
- (g) *Corylus* L.,
- (h) *Fagus* L.,
- (i) *Fraxinus* L.,
- (j) *Koelreuteria* Medikus.,
- (k) *Platanus* L.,
- (l) *Populus* L.,
- (m) *Salix* L.,
- (n) *Tilia* L.,
- (o) *Ulmus* L.

**14.**[^key-b63cf01c6aecf53323f3461890a5c17d] Wood of *Juglans* L. and *Pterocarya* Kunth.
