# The Plant Health (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 43

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Commission Decision [2002/757/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2002/757) on provisional emergency phytosanitary measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of Phytophthora ramorum Werres, De Cock & Man in ‘t Veld sp. nov[^c-03e95b7a29bd587421a6826fd58ce42d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Decision [2002/757/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2002/757) on provisional emergency phytosanitary measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Phytophthora ramorum* Werres, De Cock & Man in ‘t Veld sp. nov [^c24375171] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, omit paragraphs 2 to 4.

(3) Omit Articles 2 to 9.

(4) In Annex 1—

- (a) omit paragraphs 1 to 3;
- (b) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “the Community” substitute “ Great Britain ”;
  - (ii) for “Member States” substitute “the competent authority.

(5) Omit Annex 2.
