## 39. Annex 4[^c-c265924ee8178ea0f0253c797ab0b8f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Annex 4—

- (a) in the heading, for “**THE UNION TERRITORY**” substitute “ **GREAT BRITAIN** ”;
- (b) for “the Union territory”, in each place where it occurs, substitute “ Great Britain ”;
- (c) in point (2)(a), for “pursuant to Article 5(2)” substitute “ in Annex 2 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation ”.
