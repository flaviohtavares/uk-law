# The Plant Health (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 55

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Savings[^c-e63136a2b732d1076337df89538bf55e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The revocations of the instruments listed in Schedule 3 and the amendments to Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 have no effect on the validity of any notice issued or any licence, authorisation or other approval given under or for the purposes of those instruments or that Regulation before IP completion day.

(2) Any such notice, licence, authorisation or other approval remains in force and continues to have effect on or after IP completion day in so far as it applies to anything done, or omitted to be done, before or after IP completion day.

(3) Paragraph (1) is subject to regulation 53.
