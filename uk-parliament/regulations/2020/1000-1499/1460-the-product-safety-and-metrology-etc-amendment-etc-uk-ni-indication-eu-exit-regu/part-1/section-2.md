# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (UK(NI) Indication) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Extent[^c-be67b855018a5ff3599a4604cd4f7015]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part and Part 2 extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2) Part 3 extends to Northern Ireland only.

(3) Any amendment or revocation made by Parts 4, 5 and 6 has the same extent as the provision amended or revoked.

(4) Part 7 extends to England and Wales and Scotland only.
