# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (UK(NI) Indication) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — UK(NI) indication[^key-b9665c9a585ec4bcf4436ec42c401781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 3

**1.**[^key-0866d21b1b14fb5f25420828d8fb5f36][^key-b9665c9a585ec4bcf4436ec42c401781] The UK(NI) indication consists of the initials UKNI taking the following form—

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1460/images/uksi_20201460_en_sld_001)

**2.**[^key-9a34f4cfa1bac4dee22c6cbbbccc9383][^key-b9665c9a585ec4bcf4436ec42c401781] Where the UK(NI) indication is reduced or enlarged, the proportions given in the graduated drawing in paragraph 1 must be respected.

**3.**[^key-000127a7221ddbc6a16ebbdc4a49ceb8][^key-b9665c9a585ec4bcf4436ec42c401781] Where a relevant enactment does not require specific dimensions, the UK(NI) indication must be at least 5 millimetres high.

**4.**[^key-0f4bf19944f04ec2d77038e9d8496275][^key-b9665c9a585ec4bcf4436ec42c401781] In this Schedule “**relevant enactment**” means any enactment which provides for the UK(NI) indication to be affixed to or in relation to a product.
