# The Medical Devices (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Devices (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) This regulation and regulation 4 come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

(3) Regulations 2 and 3 come into force immediately before IP completion day.

(4) Regulation 2 applies in relation to Northern Ireland only.
