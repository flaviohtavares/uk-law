# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 78

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Amendment of the British Nationality Act 1981[^key-3693b117bcc3c96a52e9381a1e5149bd][^M_I_8f167071-7f14-4eb4-e073-87c191ff7e52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The British Nationality Act 1981 [^c24399381] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 50A (meaning of references to being in breach of immigration laws) [^c24399391]—

- (a) in subsection (4)—
  - (i) after paragraph (b) insert—

    > - (ba) is not an Irish citizen who does not require leave to enter or remain in the United Kingdom (see section 3ZA of the Immigration Act 1971);

    ;
  - (ii) omit paragraphs (d) and (e);
- (b) omit subsection (5).
