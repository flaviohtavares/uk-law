> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Amendment of the Social Security (Immigration and Asylum) Consequential Amendments Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^c-c17f24ccb87c36cf2f598f0d16649246][^M_I_73851456-19b8-4cc2-dba5-ce1fb4dab373]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Social Security (Immigration and Asylum) Consequential Amendments Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 [^c24399181] are amended as follows.

(2) In Part II of the Schedule (persons not excluded from certain benefits under section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999), omit paragraph 1.
