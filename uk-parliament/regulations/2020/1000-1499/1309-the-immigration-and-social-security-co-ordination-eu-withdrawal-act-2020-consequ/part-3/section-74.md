# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 74

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^c-7bf1881a6025b6f9192140260070bdb9][^M_I_b5cb67c2-fa6d-4245-e344-208d96da176f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^c24399341] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 70 (special cases: supplemental – persons from abroad)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) at the end of sub-paragraph (b)(ii) insert “ or ”;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraphs (c) to (e);
- (b) after paragraph (4)(zd) insert—

  > - (ze) a frontier worker within the meaning of regulation 3 of the Citizens' Rights (Frontier Workers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020;
  > - (zf) a family member of a person referred to in sub-paragraph (ze), who has been granted limited leave to enter, or remain in, the United Kingdom by virtue of Appendix EU to the immigration rules made under section 3(2) of the Immigration Act 1971;

  ;
- (c) after paragraph (6) insert—

  > (7) References in this regulation to the Immigration (European Economic Area) Regulations 2016 are to be read with Schedule 4 to the Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020(Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) Regulations 2020.

  .
