## 57. Amendment of the Social Security (Immigration and Asylum) Consequential Amendments Regulations 2000[^c-e876a21f326a6f4f02555e7de2f66848][^M_I_5c5d1db8-cba5-4f3a-9ee9-aa1672e83562]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Social Security (Immigration and Asylum) Consequential Amendments Regulations 2000 [^c24399171] are amended as follows.

(2) In Part II of the Schedule (persons not excluded from certain benefits under section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999), omit paragraph 1.
