# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 36

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Amendment of the Immigration (Procedure for Marriage) Regulations 2011[^c-f440d980b768928d22a0f6efbd50154b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Immigration (Procedure for Marriage) Regulations 2011 [^c24398941] are amended as follows.

(2) For regulation 2 (specified registration districts in England and Wales), substitute—

> **2. Specified registration districts in England and Wales**
>
> Every registration district in England and Wales is specified for the purposes of section 19(2)(a) of the 2004 Act.

.

(3) Omit the Schedule (specified registration districts in England and Wales).
