# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 17

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Amendment and saving of UK Borders Act 2007[^key-e92be2c96cb85a602d9d509a422a30ff][^M_I_2fdeba7f-5d6f-4cc7-a161-2557b0b00b67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The UK Borders Act 2007 [^c24398661] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In section 33 (exceptions to automatic deportation) [^c24398671], omit subsection (4).

(3) In section 56A (no rehabilitation for certain immigration or nationality purposes) [^c24398681], omit subsection (3).

(4) The amendment made by paragraph (3) does not apply in relation to a relevant immigration decision or a relevant nationality decision made before commencement day.

(5) In paragraph (4) “relevant immigration decision” and “relevant nationality decision” have the meaning given in section 56A of the UK Borders Act 2007 as it had effect immediately before commencement day.
