# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 9

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Amendment of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1988[^c-2fef995d9f659870fe4dab64e5cbfc20][^M_I_118c4142-3755-4692-e99c-3141dfe425cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1988 [^c24398401] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 7A (persons not eligible for housing assistance), for paragraph (4A)(b) substitute—

> - (b) is not a person who, immediately before IP completion day, was—
>   - (i) a national of an EEA State or Switzerland, and
>   - (ii) within a class specified in an order under section 119(1) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 [^c24398411] which had effect at that time.

.
