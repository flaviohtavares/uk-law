# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 13

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Amendment and saving of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^c-7cbff87607182c40fb5e466c5b561247][^M_I_6620eb48-df06-4641-e10c-089737dd60cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 [^c24398521] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In section 58 (voluntary departure from the United Kingdom)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(a), omit “or an EEA national”;
- (b) omit subsection (4).

(3) In section 126(2) (physical data: compulsory provision)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (c) insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (e) [^c24398531] (and the “or” before it).

(4) In Schedule 3 (withholding and withdrawal of support)—

- (a) in paragraph 3 (exceptions) [^c24398541], omit sub-paragraph (b) (and the “or” before it);
- (b) omit paragraph 5 (second class of ineligible person: citizen of EEA state) [^c24398551];
- (c) in paragraph 8 (travel assistance) omit “or 5”.

(5) The amendments made by paragraph (3) do not affect the operation of any regulations made under section 126(1) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 before commencement day in relation to any application for a document referred to in section 126(2)(e) that was made before commencement day.
