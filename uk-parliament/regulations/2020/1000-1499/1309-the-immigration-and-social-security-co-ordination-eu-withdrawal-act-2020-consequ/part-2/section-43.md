# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 43

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Amendment of the Proposed Marriages and Civil Partnerships (Conduct of Investigations, etc.) Regulations 2015[^c-262b3aae309e42f312c1f570c722c07d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Proposed Marriages and Civil Partnerships (Conduct of Investigations, etc.) Regulations 2015 [^c24399021] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 18 (prescribed information about the investigation)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(e) for “, indefinite leave or a European residence document” substitute “ or indefinite leave ”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(f) for “, indefinite leave or a European residence document” substitute “ or indefinite leave ”;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (2)(b)(ii) (and the “and” before it).
