# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Amendment of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^key-b220ca7322e967ef65340d676e868543][^M_I_7593a831-52a9-4dcf-c76b-44dea9d7f00a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 [^c24398691] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 130 (designation for purposes of special immigration status) [^c24398701], in subsection (5), omit paragraph (b) (and the “or” before it).

(3) In section 136 (end of designation) [^c24398711]—

- (a) in subsection (1), omit paragraph (b);
- (b) in subsection (3), omit “or (b)”.
