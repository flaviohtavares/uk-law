# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 26

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Amendment and saving of the Channel Tunnel (International Arrangements) Order 1993[^key-336f3bc75b5af0ec743901656a642bc5][^M_I_d510660d-2c05-48ae-c7f9-216024b2ab6c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Channel Tunnel (International Arrangements) Order 1993 [^c24398821] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 4 (enactments modified), omit paragraph 5 (the Immigration (European Economic Area) Regulations 2006).

(3) The amendment made by paragraph (2) does not apply to a person who has a right of admission to the United Kingdom under the Immigration (European Economic Area) Regulations 2016 as they continue to have effect by virtue of the Citizens' Rights (Application Deadline and Temporary Protection) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.
