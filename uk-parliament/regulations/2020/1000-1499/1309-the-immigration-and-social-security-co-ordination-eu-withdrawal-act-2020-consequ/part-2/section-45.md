# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 45

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Amendment of the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Order 2016[^key-b80706c9914b1ecbc3350b76341f4034][^M_I_0553c3ee-539d-40ae-fc76-6c5972dd1584]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Order 2016 [^c24399041] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2 (interpretation), omit the definition of “European residence document”.

(3) In article 6 (documents and administration), in Table 3, omit the entry at row 3.1.8 (European residence document).
