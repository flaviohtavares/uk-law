## 31. Amendment of the Civil Partnership (Registration Provisions) Regulations 2005[^key-8b3f93bf38fa0badf6e3f3f8eb776e45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Civil Partnership (Registration Provisions) Regulations 2005 [^c24398871] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 3 (evidence), in paragraph 2 (evidence of name, surname, date of birth and nationality), omit sub-paragraph (1)(b).
