# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 46

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Amendment of the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Regulations 2018[^c-ce8333a984b4b4fe33f84372b516d304][^M_I_90079170-8b42-4b91-dd0b-3ede75aa6d84]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Regulations 2018 [^c24399051] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 3 (documents and administration)—

- (a) in paragraph 1, omit the definition of “the 2016 Regulations”;
- (b) in Table 10 (fees for miscellaneous documents and services), omit rows 10.5 and 10.5.1;
- (c) in Table 12 (fees for biometric immigration documents and the process used to take a record of biometric information), omit row 12.2.3.
