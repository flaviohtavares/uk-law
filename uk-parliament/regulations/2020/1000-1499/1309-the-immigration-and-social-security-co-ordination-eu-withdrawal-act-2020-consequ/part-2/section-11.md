# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 11

> Text as at 2023-02-02.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Amendment of the Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act 1997[^key-1a06ce2eb04c123b687bc805c5907163][^M_I_8b160587-8d66-42a1-a3b5-231a87431010]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act 1997 [^c24398431] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 2C (jurisdiction: review of certain exclusion decisions)—

- (a) in subsections (1) and (2), for “non-EEA national or relevant person”, in each place it occurs, substitute “ person ”;
- (b) in subsection (5), omit the words from the beginning to the second “and”.
