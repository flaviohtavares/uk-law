# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 30

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Amendment of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Part V Exemption: Relevant Employers) Order 2003[^key-efce5dbf437d9b80e3ba0a4264a65f4e][^M_I_a180764b-c3f6-4acd-9e74-5ac2681155e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Part V Exemption: Relevant Employers) Order 2003 [^c24398861] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In article 2 (interpretation), omit the definitions of “EEA national” and “family member of an EEA national”.

(3) In article 3(1) (exemption of relevant employers), omit sub-paragraph (c) (and the “or” before it).
