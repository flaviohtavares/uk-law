# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 37

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Amendment of the Immigration (Procedure for Formation of Civil Partnerships) Regulations 2011[^c-ae4bcaa2f1d6e2f8566dde12b70b9286]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Immigration (Procedure for Formation of Civil Partnerships) Regulations 2011 [^c24398951] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (specified registration authorities in England and Wales)—

- (a) for paragraph (1), substitute—

  > (1) Every registration authority in England and Wales is specified for the purposes of paragraph 4(1)(a) of Schedule 23 to the 2004 Act.

  ; and
- (b) in paragraph (2), for the words after “she” to the end, substitute “ is authorised by that authority to attest notices of proposed civil partnership. ”.

(3) Omit the Schedule (specified registration authorities in England and Wales).
