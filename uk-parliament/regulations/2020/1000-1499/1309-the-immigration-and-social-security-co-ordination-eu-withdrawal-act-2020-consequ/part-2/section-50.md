# The Immigration and Social Security Co-ordination (EU Withdrawal) Act 2020 (Consequential, Saving, Transitional and Transitory Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 50

> Text as at 2023-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Amendment of the Citizens' Rights (Frontier Workers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^key-6474b1082c82dab60e583cd8cfe6ba2d][^M_I_ad0d9e63-b434-40a6-d885-0cc1b384ece9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Citizens' Rights (Frontier Workers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 [^c24399091] are amended as follows.

(2) After regulation 20 (misuse of frontier workers' rights), insert—

> **21A. Exemption for Irish citizens**
>
> Regulations 7 and 13 to 20 do not apply to a frontier worker who is an Irish citizen unless any of paragraphs (2) to (4) of section 3ZA of the 1971 Act [^c24399101] also apply to that Irish citizen.

.
