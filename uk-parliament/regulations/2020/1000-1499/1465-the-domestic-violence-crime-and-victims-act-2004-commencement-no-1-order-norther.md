# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1465 (C. 40)

Criminal Law, Northern Ireland

The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

7th December 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 60 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020.

(2) This Order extends to Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement

Section 9[^f00002] of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (domestic homicide reviews: establishment and conduct of reviews), to the extent not already in force, comes into force on 10th December 2020.

Victoria Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

7th December 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 9 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (c. 28), in so far as it extends to Northern Ireland. This Order brings section 9 into force in Northern Ireland on 10th December. Section 9 came into force in England and Wales on 13th April 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1008)).

This provision gives the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland the discretion to direct a person or body specified in that section to establish or participate in a domestic homicide review, held with a view to identifying the lessons to be learnt from the death. The person or body establishing or participating in the review has a duty to have regard to any guidance issued by the Department of Justice as to the establishment and conduct of such reviews.
