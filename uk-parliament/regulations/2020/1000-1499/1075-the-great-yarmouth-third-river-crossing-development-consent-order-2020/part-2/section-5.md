# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-30e97edda8a2e9dd7217aed927087c19]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
