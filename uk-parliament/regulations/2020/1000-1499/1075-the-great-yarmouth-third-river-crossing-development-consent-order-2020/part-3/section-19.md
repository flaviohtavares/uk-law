# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 19

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Disapplication of obligations where undertaker is the street authority[^key-5f7ebb34c2599ba7fbe267597c2351f7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any obligation imposed on the undertaker by this Order in relation to a street or part of a street to—

- (a) obtain the consent or agreement of the street authority; or
- (b) to carry out any works or activity to the satisfaction of the street authority,

does not apply where, in relation to the relevant street or part of a street, the undertaker is the street authority.
