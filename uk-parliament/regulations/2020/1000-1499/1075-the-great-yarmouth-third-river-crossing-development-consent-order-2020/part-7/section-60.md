# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 60

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60. Undertaker's highway, road traffic and planning functions[^key-b244d45a89d35e9d526f3669b7070e13]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Except as expressly provided, nothing in this Order is to prejudice the rights, powers and duties of the undertaker under the 1980 Act, the 1984 Act, the 1990 Act and the 1991 Act in relation to the highways constructed as part of the authorised development.
