# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 56

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Deemed marine licence[^c-20ea96fc893016c6e3e55b0037e7bb7f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker is deemed to have been granted a marine licence under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act to carry out the licensable marine activities specified in Part 1 of Schedule 13 (deemed marine licence), subject to the conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule (which are deemed to have been attached to the licence under Part 4 of the 2009 Act).
