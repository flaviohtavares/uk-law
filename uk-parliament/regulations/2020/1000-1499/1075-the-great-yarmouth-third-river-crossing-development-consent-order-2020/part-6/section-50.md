# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 50

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Protection against dredging[^c-1095e50cd7749c68669475a66b0be61e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No person is to dredge within the area shown hatched yellow on the limits of dredging plan.

(2) No person is to dredge within the area shown hatched blue on the limits of dredging plan without the prior consent of the undertaker, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.
