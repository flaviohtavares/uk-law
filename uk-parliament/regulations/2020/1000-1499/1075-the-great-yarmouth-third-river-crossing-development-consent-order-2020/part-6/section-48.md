# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 48

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. No apparatus in the new bridge or new bridge approaches without consent[^key-c74b96eb1c050eda882a96499632c885]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regardless of anything contained in any enactment, no person is to enter upon, break up or interfere with the new bridge or the new bridge approaches, or any part of them, for the purpose of placing or doing anything in or in relation to any sewer, drain, main, pipe, wire or other apparatus or executing any work except with the written consent of the undertaker and in accordance with such terms and conditions as the undertaker may determine, including as to payment, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld and any disputes as to the withholding of consent or over terms and conditions to be subject to the arbitration provisions in article 67 (arbitration).
