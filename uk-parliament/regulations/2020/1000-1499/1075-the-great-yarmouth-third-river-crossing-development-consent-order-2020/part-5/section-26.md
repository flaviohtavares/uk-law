# The Great Yarmouth Third River Crossing Development Consent Order 2020, section 26

> Text as at 2021-06-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-4859ced07ab0631ab9923c8e1ed7ee7f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24294441] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “ the undertaker ”.
