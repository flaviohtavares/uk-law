# The Maritime Enforcement Powers (Specification of the Royal Navy Police) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1554

Defence

The Maritime Enforcement Powers (Specification of the Royal Navy Police) Regulations 2020

Made

16th December 2020

Laid before Parliament

17th December 2020

Coming into force

7th January 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 84(3)(g) of the Policing and Crime Act 2017[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Maritime Enforcement Powers (Specification of the Royal Navy Police) Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 7th January 2021.

## 2. Specification of Royal Navy Police

A member of the Royal Navy Police is a person of a specified description for the purposes of section 84(3)(g) of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 (application of maritime enforcement powers: England and Wales).

Ben Wallace

Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

16th December 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide that members of the Royal Navy Police are persons of a specified description, and are therefore law enforcement officers, for the purposes of section 84(3)(g) of the Policing and Crime Act [2017 (c.3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/3). This enables Royal Navy Police to exercise the maritime enforcement powers given to law enforcement officers in Chapter 5 of Part 4 of that Act.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
