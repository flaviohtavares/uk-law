# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. [Commission Regulation (EC) No 936/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0936)

(1) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 936/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0936) applying the agreements between the European Union and third countries on the mutual recognition of certain spirit drinks[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1(2), for the words from “referred to”, in the second place they occur, to “110/2008”, substitute “contained in the Spirit Drinks Regulations 2008”.

(3) After Article 3 omit the words from “This Regulation” to “Member States”.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: EUR 2009/936. Prospective amendments were included in [S.I. 2019/759](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/759). They would have come into force on IP completion day. They are omitted by regulation 14 of this instrument and replaced with the amendments in regulation 3 of this instrument.
