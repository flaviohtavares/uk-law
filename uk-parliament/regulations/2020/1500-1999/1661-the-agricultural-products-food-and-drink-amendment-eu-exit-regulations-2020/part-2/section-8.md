## 8. Commission Implementing Regulation 668/2014

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation 668/2014 laying down the rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs[^f00009] is amended as follows.

(2) In Annex 11, in paragraph 2, after “– Class 2.20. feather” insert—

> ,
>
> – Class 2.20a. rush
