# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) The following provisions of these Regulations come into force immediately before IP completion day—

- (a) this Part;
- (b) Part 3.

(3) Otherwise, these Regulations come into force on IP completion day.
