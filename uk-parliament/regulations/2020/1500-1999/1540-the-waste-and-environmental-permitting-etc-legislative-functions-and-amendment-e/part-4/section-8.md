# The Waste and Environmental Permitting etc. (Legislative Functions and Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. The Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005

In the Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00010], omit regulations 6(b), 8(1)(b), 9(1)(b) and 72(3).
