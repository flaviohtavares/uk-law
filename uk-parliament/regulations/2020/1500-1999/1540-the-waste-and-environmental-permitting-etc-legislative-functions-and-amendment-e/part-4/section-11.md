## 11. The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019

(1) The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019[^f00015] are amended as follows.

(2) After regulation 1 insert—

> **1A. Extent**
>
> (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amendments made by Parts 2 and 3 have the same extent as the Regulations being amended.
>
