# The Waste and Environmental Permitting etc. (Legislative Functions and Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Waste and Environmental Permitting etc. (Legislative Functions and Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations come into force on IP completion day, except as provided in paragraphs (3) and (4).

(3) Regulations 10 to 12 and 14 come into force immediately before IP completion day.

(4) Regulations 13 and 15, and this regulation, come into force immediately before the coming into force of the Environment and Rural Affairs (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00002].

(5) Parts 1 and 2 extend to the United Kingdom.

(6) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by Part 3, 4 or 5 or the Schedule has the same extent as the provision being amended, repealed or revoked, subject to paragraph (7).

(7) The revocations in paragraphs 7, 9, 10 and 11 of the Schedule extend to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: [S.I. 2019/1078](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1078). Regulation 1 states that those Regulations come into force immediately before exit day. By virtue of Schedule 5, paragraph 1(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act [2020 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/1) they instead come into force immediately before IP completion day.
