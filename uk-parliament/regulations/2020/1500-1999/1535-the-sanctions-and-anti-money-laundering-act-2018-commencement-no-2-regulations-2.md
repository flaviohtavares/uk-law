# The Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1535 (C. 43)

Companies

Financial Services

Sanctions

The Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made

14th December 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 64(2) and (3) of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2020.

## 2. Commencement

Section 51 of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 comes into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

**3.** The following provisions of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 come into force on IP completion day[^f00002]—

- (a) section 49[^f00003];
- (b) section 59(1) to (3);
- (c) section 59(5), so far as it relates to the provisions coming into force in accordance with paragraphs (e) and (f) of this regulation;
- (d) Schedule 2;
- (e) paragraph 9 of Schedule 3, except the revocations in the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/99](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/99)) and the Payment Services Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/752](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/752));
- (f) paragraph 10 of Schedule 3.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force certain provisions of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act [2018 (c.13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13) (“the Act”). Section 49 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Act enable the Treasury or the Secretary of State to make regulations relating to the detection, investigation or prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing. Section 51 of the Act relates to publicly accessible registers of beneficial ownership of companies in British overseas territories. The remaining provisions of the Act brought into force by these Regulations all relate to the repeal of Part 1 of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act [2010 (c.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/38).

Certain provisions of the Act came into force on Royal Assent by virtue of section 64(1) of the Act and others were brought into force by the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2018.
