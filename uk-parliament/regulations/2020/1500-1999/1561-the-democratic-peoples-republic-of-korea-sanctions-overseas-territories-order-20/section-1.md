# The Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2025-12-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-d17b2f17ae4e6d40734e39e49415e509]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020 and comes into force immediately after both the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2020[^f00004] and the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 3) Regulations 2020[^f00005] have come into force in the United Kingdom.

(2) This Order extends to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1.
