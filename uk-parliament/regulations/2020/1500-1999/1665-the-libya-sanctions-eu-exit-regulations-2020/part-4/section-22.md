# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 22

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Immigration[^c-1a897a394f415a9758aa3f885f21630e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c24411741].
