# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 74

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Monetary penalties[^c-7dc21a4ed8b46faa5bc5b8901486bcc3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 5 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 (monetary penalties) [^c24412071].
