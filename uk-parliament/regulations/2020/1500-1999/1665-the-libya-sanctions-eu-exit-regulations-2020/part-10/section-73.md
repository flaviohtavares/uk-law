# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 73

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-a031c74b121e461bc5dfbe14fcf1fd44]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c24412061] applies to an offence under Part 3 (Finance), regulation 38 (financial transactions relating to Libyan oil) or regulation 52 (finance: licensing offences).
