# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 24

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Import of arms and related materiel and internal repression goods[^key-8f41d343d05d789787821b96436b52ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of the following goods which are consigned from Libya is prohibited—

- (a) arms and related materiel;
- (b) internal repression goods.

(2) The import of goods mentioned in paragraph (1) which originate in Libya, is prohibited.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 8 (Exceptions and licences).
