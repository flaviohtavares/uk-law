> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Export of restricted goods[^key-52febd95f91088b91bb2dc23e7e76cb1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Libya is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 8 (Exceptions and licences).
