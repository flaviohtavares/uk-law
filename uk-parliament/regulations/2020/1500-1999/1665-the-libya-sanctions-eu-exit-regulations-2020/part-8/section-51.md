# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 51

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Licences: general provisions[^c-99b738c515623fd0234a69fb75b1b9f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-ba6b317338d6082a65b7f15b7c5d0388], trade licences and port licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may—

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“P”),[^c-700d448778adf6550ef945cb26762501] issues[^c-51757388c37951744fb0718a27494478] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-e5f51b91ac92dc7e5437f466eab63de0] must give written notice to P[^c-1b31c4a5611dad9357565c2c4b662002] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
