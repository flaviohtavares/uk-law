# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Financial transactions in relation to Libyan oil aboard UN designated ships[^c-2f598486deb9752bb18e199a360a991e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not enter into any financial transaction relating to Libyan oil aboard a designated ship.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 8 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the ship was a designated ship.

(4) In this regulation—

“**designated ship**” means a ship for the time being designated by the Committee for the purpose of paragraph 10(d) of resolution 2146; a “financial transaction relating to Libyan oil”—

- (a) includes its purchase or sale, its use as credit and the taking out of transport insurance in respect of it;
- (b) does not include the payment or receipt of any fees pursuant to the entry into port of a ship carrying that oil.
