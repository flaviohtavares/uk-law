# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 85

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Revocations[^key-beab5162a714208f895c80ef49c48ad1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) 2016/44 of 18 January 2016 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Libya and repealing Regulation (EU) No 204/2011 is revoked.

(2) The Libya (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2016 [^c24412191] are revoked.

(3) The Libya (Financial Sanctions) Order 2011 [^c24412201] is revoked.

(4) The Export Control (Libya Sanctions) Order 2016 [^c24412211] is revoked.
