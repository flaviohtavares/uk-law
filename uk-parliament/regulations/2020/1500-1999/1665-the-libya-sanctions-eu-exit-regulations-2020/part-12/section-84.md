## 84. Amendment of the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017[^c-6809ebe31a917210197b0f470706c23c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Schedule to the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017 [^c24412181], omit the following row from the table—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | United Nations Security Council Resolution 1970 (2011) | Council Regulation (EU) 2016/44 of 18th January 2016 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Libya and repealing Regulation (EU) No 204/2011. |
