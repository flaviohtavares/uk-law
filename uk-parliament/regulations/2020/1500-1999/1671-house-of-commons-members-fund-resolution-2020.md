# House of Commons Members’ Fund Resolution 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 1671

Parliament

House of Commons Members’ Fund Resolution 2020

Resolution of the House of Commons, dated 7th September 2020, passed in pursuance of the House of Commons Members’ Fund Act 2016[^f00001].

*Resolved*, That the sum to be deducted from the salaries of Members of the House of Commons in pursuance of section 4(1) and (2) of the House of Commons Members’ Fund Act 2016 shall continue to be £2 per month.
