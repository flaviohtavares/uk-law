# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The Quality Schemes (Agricultural Products and Foodstuffs) Regulations 2018[^c-754077d6faf5f9add69fd960bbf6c261]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Quality Schemes (Agricultural Products and Foodstuffs) Regulations 2018 [^c24413851] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(2), for the definition of “Regulation 1151/2012” substitute—

> Regulation 1151/2012” means Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^c24413861];

.

(3) In regulation 3(2)(a) (the competent authority etc.) omit “38,”.

(4) Omit regulation 5 (delegation to control bodies).
