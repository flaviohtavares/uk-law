# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 32

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/198[^c-c3ec5ea0dcaf6a902e2540639de47398]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/198 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the establishment of the register of geographical indications protected in the sector of aromatised wine products and the listing of the existing geographical designations in that register [^c24414651] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit paragraph 1;
- (b) in paragraph 2, after “register” insert “established and maintained by the Secretary of State under Article 21 of Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 (‘the register’)”.

(3) In Article 2, in the words before point (a)—

- (a) at the beginning insert “ The Secretary of State must list ”;
- (b) omit “are listed”.

(4) After Article 3 omit the words from “This Regulation” to “States.”.
