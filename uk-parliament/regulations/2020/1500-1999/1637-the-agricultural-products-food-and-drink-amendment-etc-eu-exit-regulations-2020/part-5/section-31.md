## 31. Regulation (EU) 2019/1753 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^c-a47acbfdc20a074b17609c1c3a6b1213]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) 2019/1753 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the action of the Union following its accession to the Geneva Act of the Lisbon Agreement on Appellations of Origin and Geographical Indications [^c24414641] is revoked.
