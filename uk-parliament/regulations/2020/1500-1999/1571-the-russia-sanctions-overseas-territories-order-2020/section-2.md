## 2. Extension of the Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-d23b55335c5576a05eddc73001fb7e9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24388481] as amended from time to time extend to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1 with the modifications specified in Schedule 2.
