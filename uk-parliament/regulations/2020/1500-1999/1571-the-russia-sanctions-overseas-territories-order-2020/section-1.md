# The Russia (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2025-12-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-958dbbba6a8a445abdb584f37fa32fda]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Russia (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020 and comes into force immediately after both the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 [^c24388461] and the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 4) Regulations 2020 [^c24388471] have come into force in the United Kingdom.

(2) This Order extends to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1.
