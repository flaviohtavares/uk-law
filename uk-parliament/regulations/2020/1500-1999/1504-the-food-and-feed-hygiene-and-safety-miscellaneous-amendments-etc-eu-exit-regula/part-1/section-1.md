# The Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-6800196a30f65a0c5e04c13ef890e89b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) Part 2 and Part 4 come into force on IP completion day. Part 3 and Part 5 come into force immediately before IP completion day.
