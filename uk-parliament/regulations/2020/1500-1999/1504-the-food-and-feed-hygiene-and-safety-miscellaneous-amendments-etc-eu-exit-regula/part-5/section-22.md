# The Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Revocation of retained direct EU legislation[^c-e33ff860f001ceafdbf5f4069441f346]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EU) No. 415/2013 laying down additional responsibilities and tasks for the EU reference laboratories for rabies, bovine tuberculosis and bee health, amending Regulation [(EC) No. 737/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0737) and repealing Regulation (EU) No. 87/2011 is revoked.
