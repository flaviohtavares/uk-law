# The Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 21

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Revocation of secondary legislation[^key-c1171230ecc7ce974772a5ea2d7b63e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375561];
- (b) the Official Controls for Feed, Food and Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375571];
- (c) the Food and Feed (Chernobyl and Fukushima Restrictions) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375581];
- (d) the Regulated Products (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375591];
- (e) the Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375601];
- (f) the Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Amendment No.2) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375611].
