## 7. Amendment of the Sprouts and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-42b555eba8bb10791bbc6d564a3ab0f7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Sprouts and Seeds (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375411] are amended as follows.

(2) After regulation 3, insert regulation 3A—

> **3A.**
>
> In Article 2, for “Article 2 of Commission Regulation (EU) No. 211/2013”, substitute “Article 3 of Regulation (EU) No. 2017/625.

.

(3) For regulation 4 substitute—

> **4.**
>
> In Article 4, for “the Union” substitute “ Great Britain ”.

.

(4) Omit regulations 8 to 13.
