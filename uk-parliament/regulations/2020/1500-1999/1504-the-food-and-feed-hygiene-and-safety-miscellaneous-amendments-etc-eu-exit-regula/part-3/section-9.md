## 9. Amendment of the Specific Food Hygiene (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-9102dbba90b5bff69f219be78100d50f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Specific Food Hygiene (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c24375431] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, after the definition of “Regulation 636/2014” insert—

> “**Regulation 2074/2005**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2074) laying down implementing measures for certain products under Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council and for the organisation of official controls under Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council, derogating from Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854);
>
> “**Regulation 2015/1375**” means Commission Implementing Regulation laying down specific rules on official controls for Trichinella in meat;
>
> “**Regulation 2016/759**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/759 drawing up lists of third countries, parts of third countries and territories from which Member States are to authorise the introduction into the Union of certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption, laying down specific requirements, amending Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2074) and repealing Decision [2003/812/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2003/812);
>
> “**Regulation 2017/185**” means Regulation (EU) 2017/185 laying down transitional measures for the application of certain provisions of Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council.
