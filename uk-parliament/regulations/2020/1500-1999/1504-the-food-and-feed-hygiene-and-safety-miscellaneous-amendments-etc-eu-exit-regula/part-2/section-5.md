# The Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendment of the Food Irradiation (England) Regulations 2009[^key-d226d7d2e3941bd3f8360d564f9a8e72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Food Irradiation (England) Regulations 2009 [^c24375391] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3(1)—

- (a) in the definition of “import”, for “another Member State or from a country outside the European Union” substitute “ outside the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) in the definition “official reference number”, for “the reference number allocated by the member State in connection with its approval as an irradiation facility (being the number shown for it in the list in Schedule 3)” substitute “ the number shown for it in the list in Schedule 3 ”.

(3) In regulation 5(1)—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) it has been irradiated in one of the facilities listed in the Table in Schedule 3 or Schedule 4;

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), in the opening words, for “another” substitute “ a ”;
- (c) for the heading to Schedule 3 substitute— “ List of facilities in Member States and the United Kingdom ”.
