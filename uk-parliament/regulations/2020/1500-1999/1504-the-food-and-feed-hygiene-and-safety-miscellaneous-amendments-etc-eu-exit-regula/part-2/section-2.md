# The Food and Feed Hygiene and Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (England) Regulations 2007[^c-ebffb14a5e8c3eb7cd0c273c59fc3376]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (England) Regulations 2007 [^c24375361] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1), for the definition of “third country” substitute—

> “third country”, except in the expression “third country import”, means any country or territory other than the British Islands;

.

(3) For regulation 4 substitute—

> **4.**
>
> Any reference in these Regulations to a specified number of Euros (“EUR”) is to be read as that sum converted into pounds sterling (“GBP”) using the exchange rate of GBP1 = EUR1.1413.

.
