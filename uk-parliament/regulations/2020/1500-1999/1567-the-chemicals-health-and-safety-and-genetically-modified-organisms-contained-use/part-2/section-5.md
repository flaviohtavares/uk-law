# The Chemicals (Health and Safety) and Genetically Modified Organisms (Contained Use) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Amendment of regulation 4 of the 2019 Regulations

For regulation 4 of the 2019 Regulations (Transitional provision in relation to Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272)) substitute—

> **4. Revocation of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 492/2014**
>
> Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 492/2014 of 7 March 2014 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the rules for the renewal of authorisations of biocidal products subject to mutual recognition is revoked.
