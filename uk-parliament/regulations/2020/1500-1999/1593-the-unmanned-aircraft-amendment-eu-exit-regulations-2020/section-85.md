# The Unmanned Aircraft (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Amendment of the Air Navigation Order 2016

(1) The Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00007] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 94A (small unmanned aircraft: permission for certain flights), in paragraph (6) and in the table in paragraph (7), omit “EASA” in each place it occurs.

(3) In Article 94B (interpretation of expressions used in the definition of “flight restriction zone”), in paragraph (1)(a), omit “EASA”.
