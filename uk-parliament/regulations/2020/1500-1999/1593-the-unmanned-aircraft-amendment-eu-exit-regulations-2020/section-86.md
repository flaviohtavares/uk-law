# The Unmanned Aircraft (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Amendment of the Operation of Air Services (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

(1) The Operation of Air Services (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^f00008] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 10 (operating licences: transitional and savings arrangements), in paragraph (1), for “exit day”, in both places it occurs, substitute “IP completion day”.

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: [S.I. 2018/1392](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1392), to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations. The reference to exit day in regulation 1 (commencement) is to be read as a reference to IP completion day, by virtue of paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act [2020 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/1).
