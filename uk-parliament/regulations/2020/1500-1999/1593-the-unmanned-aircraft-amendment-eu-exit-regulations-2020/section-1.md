# The Unmanned Aircraft (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Unmanned Aircraft (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on IP completion day.

(3) This regulation, in so far as it relates to regulation 86, and regulation 86 come into force immediately before IP completion day.
