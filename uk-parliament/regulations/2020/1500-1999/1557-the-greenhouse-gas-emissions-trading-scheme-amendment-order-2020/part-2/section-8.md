# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) Order 2020, section 8

> Text as at 2022-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Article 9 amended (meaning of regulator)[^c-665f3c4b47170cf2ca6a055f8a12ede4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 9 is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph (2) insert—

> (2A) Articles 11 to 13 apply for the purpose of determining the regulator of a person other than an aircraft operator in relation to—
>
> - (a) monitoring and reporting of the person's aviation emissions;
> - (b) free allocation to the person under Chapter 2 of Part 4A (aviation free allocation);
> - (c) the opening, operation or closure of the person's aircraft operator holding account,
>
> as if references to “aircraft operator” were to the person.

.
