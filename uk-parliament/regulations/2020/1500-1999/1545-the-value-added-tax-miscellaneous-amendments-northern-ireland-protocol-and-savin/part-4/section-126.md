# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 126

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126.[^c-99038981c43b6ab212f9ac0db23d19df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the application of regulation 121D of the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995 to goods which are in the United Kingdom as at IP completion day, the terms “unpaid” and “paid” in paragraph (5) are to have the additional meanings given in regulation 125(2).
