# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 115

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115.[^c-0a68f9ddafa250e9f20f66d8c5d19faf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an acquisition in Northern Ireland of goods from a member State is treated as taking place on or after IP completion day in accordance with paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 9ZA to VATA 1994 and the time of acquisition is determined by reference to a first removal of the goods occurring before IP completion day, the fact that the first removal occurs at that time does not affect the charge to VAT that is imposed under paragraph 1 of that Schedule.
