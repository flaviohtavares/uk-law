# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 127

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127.[^key-59990d79924ed24c641776556e016406]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 121D of the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995 does not apply to goods supplied at any time to any person pursuant to regulation 131 of those Regulations [^c24395911].
