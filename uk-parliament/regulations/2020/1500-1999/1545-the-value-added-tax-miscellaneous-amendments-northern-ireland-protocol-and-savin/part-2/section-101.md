# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 101

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101.[^c-f1f1e9c29d7c5ed2bfc0d2667f4b5b78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 3(a) [^c24395681] (the application of paragraph 6(1) of Schedule 4 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994), after “within” insert “ Northern Ireland or ”.
