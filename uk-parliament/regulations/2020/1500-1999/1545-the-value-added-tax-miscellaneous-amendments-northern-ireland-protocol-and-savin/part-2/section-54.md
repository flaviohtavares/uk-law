# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 54

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Part 12 (valuation of acquisitions)[^c-3e6e88df0f38c3da0788d0949520bd4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 97(1) [^c24395231] (valuation of acquisitions)—

- (a) for “the United Kingdom from another member State” substitute “ Northern Ireland from a member State ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a), for “the United Kingdom” substitute “ Northern Ireland ”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b), for “Economic Community” substitute “ EU ”.
