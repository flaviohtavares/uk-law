# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 58

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58.[^key-dd756481b32a7000bdc68f7c5a6894f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 103B(3)(a) [^c24395281] (attribution of input tax incurred on services and related goods used to make financial supplies), omit “and any supply of the same description which is made in another member State”.
