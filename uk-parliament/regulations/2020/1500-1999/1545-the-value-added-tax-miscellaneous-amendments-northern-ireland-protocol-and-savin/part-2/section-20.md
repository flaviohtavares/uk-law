# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20.[^key-df8c059f726de2f13a78846670b5b568]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 2—

- (a) in the definition of “motor dealer”, after “supplies of,” insert “ or acquiring in Northern Ireland from a member State ”;
- (b) in the definition of “stock in trade”—
  - (i) after “article 7(2A) below” insert “ or paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 2 to this Order ”,
  - (ii) in paragraph (a), after “supplied to” insert “ or acquired in Northern Ireland from a member State ”, and
  - (iii) in paragraph (b)(ii), after “their supply” insert “ , acquisition in Northern Ireland from a member State ”.
