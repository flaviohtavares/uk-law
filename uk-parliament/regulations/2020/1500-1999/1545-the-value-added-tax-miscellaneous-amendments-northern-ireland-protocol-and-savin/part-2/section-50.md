# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 50

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50.[^c-84e1349ecbc1c9f119d48945fa481b84]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 40(1) [^c24395191] (VAT to be accounted for on returns and payment of VAT)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c), after “regime,” insert “ or Northern Ireland fiscal warehousing regime, ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), after “regime” in each place it occurs insert “ or Northern Ireland warehousing regime ”.
