# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 80

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Part 17 (means of transport)[^key-8d8193f8463c4c11e2ecc831a24b9c11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 146 [^c24395511] (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “competent authority”—
  - (i) for “in any member State” substitute “ in a relevant territory ”, and
  - (ii) for “in that member State” substitute “ in that territory ”;
- (b) after the definition of “first entry into service” insert—

  > “**new means of transport**” has the meaning given by paragraph 83 of Schedule 9ZA to the Act [^c24395521];

  ;
- (c) after the definition of “registration” insert—

  > “**relevant territory**” means a member State or Northern Ireland.

  .
