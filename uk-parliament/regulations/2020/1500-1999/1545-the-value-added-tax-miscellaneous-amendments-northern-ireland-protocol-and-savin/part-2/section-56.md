# The Value Added Tax (Miscellaneous Amendments, Northern Ireland Protocol and Savings and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 56

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Part 14 (input tax and partial exemption)[^key-b6ff3661f37cdfd21dce2281cbc63f25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 101 [^c24395251] (attribution of input tax to taxable supplies) after sub-paragraph (3)(f) insert—

> - (g) where a removal of goods is treated as a taxable supply by virtue of paragraph 31B(1) of Schedule 9ZB [^c24395261] to the Act, the value of that supply.

.
