# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 73

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**73.**[^c-5288616293441294faa4a95ef7603e2e] In regulation 82—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in subparagraph (a) for “the United Kingdom” substitute “Northern Ireland”;
  - (ii) in subparagraph (b) for “another” substitute “an EU”;
- (b) in paragraph (3) insert “EU” before both occurrences of “Member State”.
