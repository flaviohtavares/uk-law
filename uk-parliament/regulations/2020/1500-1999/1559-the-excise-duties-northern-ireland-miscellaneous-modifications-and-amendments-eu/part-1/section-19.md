# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 19

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**19.**[^key-6004e82aa114e35034b1244150172a20] In regulation 19—

- (za)[^key-11ebddf4d9e26af6f3237b1c73f9e42a] in paragraph (1) for “any of paragraphs (2) to (5)” substitute “paragraph (2)”;
- (a) in paragraph (2) after “tax warehouse” (in each place it occurs) insert “in Northern Ireland”;
- (b) omit paragraphs (3) and (4);
- (c)[^key-d4d13d0c5a5e770887bd196e221ee936] omit paragraph (5);
- (d) in paragraph (6)—
  - (i) in subparagraph (a) after “tax warehouse” insert “in Northern Ireland”;
  - (ii) omit subparagraphs (b) and (c)[^key-77b82989e9624dd57494622b7223a3c7];
  - (iii)[^key-a1a22c9fc22eab78d31375cdde62172c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) omit paragraph (7)(b) and (c)[^key-62c08c72ef7695d110796dcd07344901].
