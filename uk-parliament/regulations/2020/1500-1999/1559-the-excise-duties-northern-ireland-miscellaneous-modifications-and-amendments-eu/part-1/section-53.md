# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 53

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**53.**[^key-e86bf4e60d10abf946ffee82329800fa] After regulation 55 insert—

> **PART A8**
>
> **55A.** For the avoidance of doubt Parts 8 and 9 of the UK HMDP Regulations apply in Northern Ireland in the same manner as those Regulations apply in the rest of the United Kingdom.
