# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**38.**[^key-dcd090feb995dc275a3b15de216e647f] In regulation 42—

- (a) in paragraphs (1) and (3) insert “EU” before “computerised system”;
- (aa)[^key-c5e1ce932840c5519c6a662b55601ca5] in paragraph (2) for “the requirements of Article 4(1)” to the end substitute “the EU requirements”;
- (b) in paragraph (4)—
  - (i) in subparagraph (b) for “another” substitute “an EU”;
  - (ii) in subparagraph (c) after “the EU” insert “or Northern Ireland”;
  - (iia)[^key-82e5c2be0cdd68422735fa7c4761220c] omit the “or” after subparagraph (c);
  - (iii) in subparagraph (d) for “another” substitute “an EU”;
  - (iv)[^key-d7690232615e70cfc00fd7860b3d9d79] after subparagraph (d) insert—

    > or
    >
    > - (e) the customs office of exit, where provided for in Article 329(5) of Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2015/2447](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2447), which is at the same time the customs office of departure for the external transit procedure, where provided for in Article 189(4) of Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2015/2446](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2446).

    ;
- (c)[^key-3de802d544f594b717e53a6a71653675] in paragraph (5) for “the requirements of Article 5(1)” to the end substitute “the EU requirements”;
- (d) omit paragraph (6).
