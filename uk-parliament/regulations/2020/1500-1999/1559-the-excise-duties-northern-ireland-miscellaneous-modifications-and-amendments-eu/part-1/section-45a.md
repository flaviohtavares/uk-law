# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 45A

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**45A.**[^c-b30ccb99f67d2afa87e6e79669055fcb] After regulation 48 insert—

> **48A. Report of export from territory of the EU or Northern Ireland – supplementary provision**
>
> For the purposes of regulations 46, 47 and 48, a report of export constitutes proof that the movement of the excise goods referred to in the report has ended.
