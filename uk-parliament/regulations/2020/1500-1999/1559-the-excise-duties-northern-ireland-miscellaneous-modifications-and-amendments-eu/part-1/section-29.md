# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 29

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**29.**[^c-9fbca6c3df26c2635ca83c75b99dc4ae] In regulation 30—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) after “dispatch excise goods” insert “within Northern Ireland or to an EU Member State”;
  - (ii) after “free circulation” insert “in Northern Ireland”;
  - (iii) [^c-9f12c2cd40b1a07ea2cca1d3d449a89d]... for “Article 79 of [Council Regulation 2913/92 EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2913)” substitute “Article 201 of the Union Customs Code Regulation[^c-92eaeeb2dae1f37fdcd617ad3137e547]”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) for “UK” substitute “NI”.
