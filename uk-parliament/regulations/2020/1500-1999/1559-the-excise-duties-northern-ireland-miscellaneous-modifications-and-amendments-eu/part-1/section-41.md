# The Excise Duties (Northern Ireland Miscellaneous Modifications and Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 41

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**41.**[^key-2e93e68bd36e8d8b58db3d10d6ca5b07] In regulation 45—

- (a) omit paragraph (1);
- (b) in paragraph (2) for “another” substitute “an EU”;
- (c) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) in subparagraph (b) for “another” substitute “an EU”;
  - (ii) in subparagraph (c) insert “EU” before “computerised system”;
- (d)[^key-7423f8e9b0b2d0d4706b042db6c99a03] in paragraph (4) for “the requirements of Article 6(1)” to the end, substitute “the EU requirements”;
- (e) omit paragraph (5).
