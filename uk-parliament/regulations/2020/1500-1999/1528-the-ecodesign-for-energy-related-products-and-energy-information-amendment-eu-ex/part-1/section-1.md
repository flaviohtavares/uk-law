# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-a0d17f6833bc734bcde785bec525544f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

(2) This Part and Part 2 come into force immediately before IP completion day.

(3) Regulation 28 (revocations) comes into force on 1st March 2021.

(4) The remainder of these Regulations come into force on IP completion day.
