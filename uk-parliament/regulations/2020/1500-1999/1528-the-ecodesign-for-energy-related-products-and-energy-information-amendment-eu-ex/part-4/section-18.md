# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Amendment to Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2019[^c-69e8aa7d26be9046acea677fbe57854d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After Article 10 (entry into force and application) of Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2019 of 1 October 2019 laying down ecodesign requirements for refrigerating appliances pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 643/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0643), omit “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.”.
