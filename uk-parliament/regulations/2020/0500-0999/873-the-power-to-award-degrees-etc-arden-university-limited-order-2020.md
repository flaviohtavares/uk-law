# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Arden University Limited) Order 2020

> Text as at 2021-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2020 No. 873

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Arden University Limited) Order 2020

Made

17th August 2020

Coming into force

1st September 2020

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 [^c24277211], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-b277b54eb4011d0e6a859cb17ff64078]

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Arden University Limited) Order 2020 and shall come into force on 1st September 2020.

## 2. Competence to grant awards[^key-9b3792f182e1c16c1e0fd69c53c650fa][^key-e6bd2e790741e01247a200be8973e660]

Arden University Limited shall be competent to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(a) and defined under section 42(3) of the said Act.

## 3.[^key-36182fd792735391e50a87ba88d86032]

Arden University Limited may authorise other institutions to grant such awards as specified in paragraph 2 of this Order on its behalf.

Susan Lapworth

Director of Competition and Registration

For the Office for Students

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Order, made under section 42(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 authorises Arden University Limited with the power to award taught awards for a one- year fixed term expiring on 31st August 2021. This Order will come into force on 1st September 2020.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
