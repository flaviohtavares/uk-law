# The Somalia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 82

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Amendment of the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017[^c-423e85e055fa3cfdae660a76bad943c7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Schedule to the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017 [^c24171841], omit the following row from the table—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “United Nations Security Council Resolution 1844 (2008) | Council Regulation (EU) No. 356/2010 of 26th April 2010 imposing certain specific restrictive measures directed against certain natural or legal persons, entities or bodies, in view of the situation in Somalia.”. |
