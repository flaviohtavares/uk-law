# The Somalia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 81

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Revocations[^key-8f8dc616338b775b1024690fece95bda]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 356/2010 of 26 April 2010 imposing certain specific restrictive measures directed against certain natural or legal persons, entities or bodies, in view of the situation in Somalia [^c24171791] is revoked.

(2) Council Regulation (EU) No 147/2003 of 27 January 2003 concerning certain restrictive measures in respect of Somalia [^c24171801] is revoked.

(3) The Somalia (Asset-Freezing) Regulations 2010 [^c24171811] are revoked.

(4) The Somalia (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2002 [^c24171821] is revoked.

(5) The Export Control (Somalia) Order 2011 [^c24171831] is revoked.
