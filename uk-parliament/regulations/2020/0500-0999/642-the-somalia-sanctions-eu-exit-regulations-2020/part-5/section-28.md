## 28. Export of military goods[^key-54755caa48d59687ceae00bb60eacf7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of military goods to a designated person is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
