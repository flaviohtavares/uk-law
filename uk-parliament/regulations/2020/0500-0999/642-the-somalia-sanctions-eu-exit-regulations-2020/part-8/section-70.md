# The Somalia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 70

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-327815725bfbf729f2c0341756a9dcca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c24171681] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 50 (finance: licensing offences).
