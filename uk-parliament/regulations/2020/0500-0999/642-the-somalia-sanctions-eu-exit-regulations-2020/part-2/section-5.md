# The Somalia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-c87207ffb7940f06346556592c8abfc3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons [^c24171341] by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-f6bc2a5b72ce5615b80abe2e941dfd46] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 12 to 16 (asset freeze etc.);
- (aa)[^key-6252528e2d6808b2b499397fb16863fe] regulation 17A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 18 (immigration);
- (c) regulations 28 to 34 (targeted arms embargo) and regulation 35 (enabling or facilitating the conduct of armed hostilities: designated persons).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
