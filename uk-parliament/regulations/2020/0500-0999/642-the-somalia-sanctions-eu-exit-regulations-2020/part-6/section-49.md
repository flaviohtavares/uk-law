## 49. Licences: general provisions[^c-0cd244a9b13f581c5d951797903329f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-34b0f0f8aeebcc38184330a043c739ae] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-3179b335c9f16a9e84e0a23916203ad1]**P**”), issues[^c-50eec6b510c13c8274500975ec815c5d] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-e2ccc33b467c5d13c76ec5ee57b8376e] must give written notice to P[^c-b3d78985fa0551ce9034e6e950dac55a] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
