## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-8ff5afcaf77eac4dc449c10e0cd92c6d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Somalia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.
