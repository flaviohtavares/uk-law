# The Cleve Hill Solar Park Order 2020, section 29

> Text as at 2021-08-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Deemed marine licence under the 2009 Act[^c-ac08a3112369e84595047b04f8047dbf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The deemed marine licence set out in Schedule 8 (deemed marine licence under the 2009 Act), is deemed to be granted on the date this Order comes into force to the undertaker under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act for the licensed marine activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule.
