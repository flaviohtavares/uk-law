# The Cleve Hill Solar Park Order 2020, section 28

> Text as at 2021-08-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Operation of generating stations[^key-c61ef75fc7ced1c30a518e5b8fb9d755]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to use and operate the generating stations comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence under any other legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of an electricity generating station.
