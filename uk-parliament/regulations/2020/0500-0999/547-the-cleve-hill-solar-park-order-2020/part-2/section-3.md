# The Cleve Hill Solar Park Order 2020, section 3

> Text as at 2021-08-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by this Order[^c-a7058c7805ecd91a1218f815b9b4c507]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and the requirements the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Each numbered work must be situated within the corresponding numbered area shown on the works plan.
