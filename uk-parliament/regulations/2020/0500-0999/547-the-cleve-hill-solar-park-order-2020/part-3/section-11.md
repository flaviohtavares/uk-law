# The Cleve Hill Solar Park Order 2020, section 11

> Text as at 2021-08-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Temporary stopping up of public rights of way[^key-00db7ae74dc2ec331e9cf77f21c69ee9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The undertaker may, in connection with the carrying out of the authorised development, temporarily stop up each of the public rights of way specified in column (2) of Schedule 4 (public rights of way to be temporarily stopped up) to the extent specified in column (3), by reference to the letters shown on the rights of way plan.
