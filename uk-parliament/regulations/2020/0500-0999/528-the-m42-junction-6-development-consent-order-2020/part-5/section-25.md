# The M42 Junction 6 Development Consent Order 2020, section 25

> Text as at 2020-10-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-888d1f3630ac8c70ac93f6a72bf7774f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00049] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
