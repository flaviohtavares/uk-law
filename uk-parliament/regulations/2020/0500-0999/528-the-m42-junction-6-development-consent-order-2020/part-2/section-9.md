# The M42 Junction 6 Development Consent Order 2020, section 9

> Text as at 2020-10-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Application of the 1990 Act[^key-82ff7d2fb1becaa819fe776b92b50d9d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This article applies where the land is used for the temporary construction works.

(2) Where this article applies, section 57(2) of the 1990 Act (planning permission required for development) applies as if the development consent granted by this Order were planning permission granted for a limited period.
