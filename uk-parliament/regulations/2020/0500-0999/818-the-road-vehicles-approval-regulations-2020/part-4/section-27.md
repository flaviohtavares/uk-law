## 27. End-of-Series vehicles: limits (Great Britain)[^key-12ea0bc9282a2446d296eb83e2368039][^key-9a73f6e846167bbfa187bf254a98bbdc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of Part B of Annex V of the Type Approval Regulation, the maximum number of complete vehicles and completed vehicles [^c24305211] entered into service in the Great Britain[^key-1491fe75a7f732e7454080c239eb7ee9] is restricted in accordance with paragraph 2 of that Part.
