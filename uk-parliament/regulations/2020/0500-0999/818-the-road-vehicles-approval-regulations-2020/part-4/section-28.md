# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818

## 28. Components and separate technical units: derogations[^c-e61500758ab800939a19e49f1b307ed5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where these Regulations or any other enactment make provision to the contrary (if at all), the derogations permitted by paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 of Article 50 of the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation[^c-5a8f566cccc9e3a4db81f1a832058c71] apply.
