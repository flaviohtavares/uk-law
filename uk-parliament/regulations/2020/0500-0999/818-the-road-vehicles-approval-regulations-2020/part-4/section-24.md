# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 24

## 24. Records of large trailers[^key-a4ee40992625de912991c512c63d3fa4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For a period of not less than 10 years from the date of the consent, the approval authority must keep a record of—

- (a) every consent given under regulation 23(3), and
- (b) the pertinent information about the large trailer in respect of which that consent is given.
