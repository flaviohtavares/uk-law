# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, SCHEDULE 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 — Other legislation for which penalties are applied

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 29(1)

**1.**[^key-b2237ba1109a1916dcff5cf42392cb42] Directive [2005/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/64) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 October 2005 on the type-approval of motor vehicles with regard to their reusability, recyclability and recoverability [^c24306381].

**2.**[^key-b21cc8828c99363b93a0d1d6a81e5cb5] Directive [2006/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/40) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 May 2006 relating to emissions from air conditioning systems in motor vehicles [^c24306391].

**3.**[^key-1645885b4fb2e5753633a8b4d6a7cad3] Regulation [(EC) No 715/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2007 on type approval of motor vehicles with respect to emissions from light passenger and commercial vehicles (Euro 5 and Euro 6) and on access to vehicle repair and maintenance information [^c24306401].

**4.**[^key-2689a594893aa6f3e98499702b3efd14] Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/78) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 January 2009 on the type-approval of motor vehicles with regard to the protection of pedestrians and other vulnerable road users [^c24306411].

**5.**[^key-e5f9482fe6a672d80df30a02b58dc441] Regulation [(EC) No 79/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/79) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 January 2009 on type-approval of hydrogen-powered motor vehicles [^c24306421].

**6.**[^key-be1c00999b91a201e6d7fdaffb48ad2a] Regulation [(EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/595) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009 on type-approval of motor vehicles and engines with respect to emissions from heavy duty vehicles (Euro VI) and on access to vehicle repair and maintenance information [^c24306431].

**7.**[^key-4c756b4d809e304db8bcf9cc6955aa06] Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/661) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning type-approval requirements for the general safety of motor vehicles, their trailers and systems, components and separate technical units intended therefor [^c24306441].

**8.**[^key-1fd2215747e3ed78418c7a7cfe8835fb] Regulation (EU) No 540/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 on the sound level of motor vehicles and of replacement silencing systems [^c24306451].

**9.**[^key-d6137f910bfe4aedef90c45bf7e40c76] Regulation (EU) 2015/758 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2015 concerning type-approval requirements for the deployment of the eCall in-vehicle system based on the 112 service and amending [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046) but only so far as that Regulation has effect by virtue of the Northern Ireland Protocol.

**10.** Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019 on type-approval requirements for motor vehicles and their trailers, and systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles, as regards their general safety and the protection of vehicles occupants and vulnerable road users but only so far as that Regulation has effect by virtue of the Northern Ireland Protocol.
