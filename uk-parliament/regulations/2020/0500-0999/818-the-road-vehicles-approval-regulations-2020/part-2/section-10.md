# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Withdrawal and suspension of approvals: effect[^c-bf55bf17d37901134d5179506b6b8de6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the holder of an approval which has been withdrawn or suspended pursuant to the Type Approval Regulation , the EU Type Approval Regulation[^c-8b5dc3ae706189f7be91e62ca8ae4192] or these Regulations purports by virtue of that approval to—

- (a) issue a certificate of conformity with respect to a vehicle, or
- (b) affix a statutory plate or mark pursuant to Article 38 of the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation, as applicable[^c-9da73353d31b0a9b6e34c442f759ac51],

the certificate, plate or mark is invalid.

(2) The approval authority may, by notice given to the holder, exempt from paragraph (1) certificates of conformity or classes of certificates of conformity specified in the notice.

(3) The suspension of an approval does not affect the validity of any certificate of conformity issued under it before that suspension.
