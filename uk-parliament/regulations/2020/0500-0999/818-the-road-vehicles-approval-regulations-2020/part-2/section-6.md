# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818

## 6. Refusal of ... type-approval application[^key-20927043c17463c29d494509c3feb60f][^key-99dc60ad61458f95a3b8c882ad37ed1c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The approval authority must refuse an application for a GB type-approval or a UK (NI) type-approval[^key-0e416661f5893189019f26a2ee1f2bc4] if the requirements of—

- (a) Articles 26 and 29 to 31, or
- (b) Article 39,

of the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation, as applicable,[^key-dde94106880de64b2f192b14eef5440d] have not been complied with.

(2) The requirements of Article 30 of the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation, as applicable[^key-17c838126d1e40513907820999d471c8] are not complied with if the tests required by that Article demonstrate that there is non-compliance with the technical requirements mentioned in paragraph 1 of that Article.

(3) The requirements of Article 31 of the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation, as applicable[^key-53025d81ce82f7f240ae25cda53e3c6b] are not complied with if the approval authority is not satisfied that the applicant has made or will make adequate arrangements to ensure that—

- (a) production will conform to the approved type, or
- (b) the data in the certificates of conformity are correct.
