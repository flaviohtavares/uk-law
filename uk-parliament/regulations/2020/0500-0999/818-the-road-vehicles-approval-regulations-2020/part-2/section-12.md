## 12. Provision of testing stations[^c-1bd81507d52daa2ae995d0cd25e1a5ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The approval authority may provide and maintain stations where examinations of relevant products may be carried out for the purposes of these Regulations , the Type Approval Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation[^c-673a9687b56b921ea0c7b7dde3e4dd6d] and may provide and maintain apparatus for carrying out such examinations.
