# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Market surveillance authority[^c-183fb60ec2cf29d2f038872fbd232244]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State is the market surveillance authority for the purposes of—

- (a) these Regulations , the Type Approval Regulation and the EU Type Approval Regulation[^c-eff92c8eff11e3bdbfd03d14f0ac517e], [^c-93ec550a991088601f68baf17213656a]...
- (b) where applied by the Type Approval Regulation or otherwise applicable to relevant products, Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0765) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 July 2008 setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/0339) [^c24305031], and[^c-4bc327cb52a2571c5e8e6c2d87eadb09]
- (c) where applied by the EU Type Approval Regulation, or otherwise applicable to relevant products, Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 on market surveillance and compliance of products, as that Regulation has effect by virtue of the Northern Ireland Protocol.
