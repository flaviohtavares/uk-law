## 3. Appointment of approval authority[^key-75d023a17f60ab04573ef99231e794e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State is the approval authority for the purposes of these Regulations , the Type Approval Regulation and the EU Type Approval Regulation[^key-3ffcda5251a6db69026f43f52b273d81].
