# The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2020/818

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Consequential amendments, revocations and savings[^key-88a5f7efd3ff354f810d7b840472a8e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 6 (consequential amendments and revocations) has effect.

(2) Anything—

- (a) done under, or by virtue of, any provision in Regulations revoked by these Regulations, if it could have been done under or for the purpose of the corresponding provision of these Regulations or the Type Approval Regulation, is deemed to have been done under or by virtue of the corresponding provision of these Regulations or the Type Approval Regulation, and
- (b) begun under, or by virtue of, any provision in Regulations revoked by these Regulations, if it may be done under or for the purpose of the corresponding provision of these Regulations or the Type Approval Regulation, may be continued under these Regulations or the Type Approval Regulation as if begun under these Regulations or the Type Approval Regulation.
