## 29. Offences, enforcement and civil penalties[^key-299715ffa3b563b0512777cbccc3e25b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 (offences, penalties, enforcement and other matters) and Schedule 5 (other legislation for which penalties are applied) have effect.

(2) Except in paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 or in relation to the expression “Officer of Revenue and Customs”, a reference in Schedule 4 to an officer is a reference to any person authorised by the enforcement authority to assist the authority in enforcing these Regulations , the Type Approval Regulation and the EU Type Approval Regulation[^key-41ba16310192d764e2f7f7b4cc64f988].
