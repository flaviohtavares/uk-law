## 1A. Application[^c-035d187460fb638280ae3491cd0f3e59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of these Regulations do not apply in England and Wales or Scotland—

- (a) in regulation 16, paragraphs (4) and (5);
- (b) regulation 17;
- (c) in regulation 18, paragraphs (8)(b) and (9).
