# The Single Digital Gateway Regulation (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 793

Exiting The European Union

The Single Digital Gateway Regulation (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

Sift requirements satisfied

14th July 2020

Made

22nd July 2020

Laid before Parliament

23rd July 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001] makes the following Regulations.

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Single Digital Gateway Regulation (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 and come into force on IP completion day.

## 2. Revocation of provisions in [Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724)

In [Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 October 2018 establishing a single digital gateway to provide access to information, to procedures and to assistance and problem-solving services and amending [Regulation (EU) No 1024/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1024), omit—

- (a) Articles 1 to 5;
- (b) Articles 7 to 12;
- (c) Articles 14(9) and (11);
- (d) Articles 16 to 37;
- (e) Article 39.

## 3. Revocation of provision in [Regulation (EU) 1024/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1024)

In [Regulation (EU) No 1024/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1024) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on administrative cooperation through the Internal Market Information System and repealing Commission [Decision 2008/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0049) (‘the IMI Regulation’), omit paragraph 12 of the Annex.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under sections 8(2)(a), 8(2)(b) and 8(2)(d)) arising from the withdrawal of the UK from the European Union.

[Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 October 2018 establishing a single digital gateway to provide access to information, to procedures and to assistance and problem-solving services and amending [Regulation (EU) No 1024/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1024) (“[Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724)”) facilitates online access to information, administrative procedures and assistance services for citizens and businesses.

Regulation 2 revokes those articles of [Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724) which are in force and apply immediately before IP completion day and constitute retained EU law under section 3(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

Regulation 3 revokes a provision in [Regulation (EU) No 1024/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1024) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on administrative cooperation through the Internal Market Information System and repealing Commission [Decision 2008/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0049) (‘the IMI Regulation’) which refers to [Regulation (EU) 2018/1724](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1724).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
