# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 150

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 150. Preliminary

(1) The Rules in this Part apply to applications to the court, made by the SMCL administrator, for an order under section 236 of the 1986 Act (inquiry into company’s dealings).

(2) In this Part, the following definitions apply—

- (a) the person in respect of whom an order is applied for is “the respondent”; and
- (b) “section 236” means section 236 of the 1986 Act.
