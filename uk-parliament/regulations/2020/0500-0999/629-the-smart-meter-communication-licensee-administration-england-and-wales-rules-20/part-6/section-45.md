# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Expenses

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the SMCL administration are to be regarded as expenses of the SMCL administration.

(2) The expenses associated with the prescribed part must be paid out of the prescribed part.

(3) The cost of the security required by section 390(3) of the 1986 Act for the proper performance of the SMCL administrator’s functions is an expense of the SMCL administration.
