# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Pre-smart meter communication licensee administration costs

Where the SMCL administrator has made a statement of pre-smart meter communication licensee administration costs under Rule 21(2)(k), the SMCL administrator (where the costs consist of fees charged or expenses incurred by the SMCL administrator) or other insolvency practitioner (where the costs consist of fees charged or expenses incurred by that practitioner) must, before paying such costs, apply to the court for a determination of whether and to what extent the unpaid pre-smart meter communication licensee administration costs are approved for payment.
