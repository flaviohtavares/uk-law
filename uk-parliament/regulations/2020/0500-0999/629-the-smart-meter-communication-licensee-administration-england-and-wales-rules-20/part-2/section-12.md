# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Notice of SMCL administration order

(1) If the court makes an SMCL administration order, it must as soon as reasonably practicable deliver two sealed copies of the order to the person who made the application.

(2) The applicant must deliver a sealed copy of the order as soon as reasonably practicable to the person appointed as SMCL administrator.

(3) If the court makes an order under section 157(1)(d) of the 2004 Act or any other order under section 157(1)(f) of the 2004 Act, it must give directions as to the persons to whom, and how, notice of that order is to be delivered.
