# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 163

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 163. Electronic delivery of notices to enforcement officers

Where anything in the 1986 Act or these Rules provides for the delivery of a notice to an enforcement officer or enforcement agent, it may be delivered by electronic means to a person who has been authorised to receive such a notice on behalf of a specified enforcement officer or enforcement agent or on behalf of enforcement officers or enforcement agents generally.
