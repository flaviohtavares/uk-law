# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 158

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 158. Postal delivery of documents

A document is delivered if it is sent by post in accordance with the rules for postal service in CPR Part 6 and sending by such means has effect as specified in those rules.
