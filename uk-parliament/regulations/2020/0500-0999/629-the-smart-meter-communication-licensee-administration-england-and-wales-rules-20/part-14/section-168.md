# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 168

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 168. Requirement for writing and form of documents

(1) A notice or statement must be in writing unless the 1986 Act or these Rules provide otherwise.

(2) A document in electronic form must be capable of being—

- (a) read by the recipient in electronic form; and
- (b) reproduced by the recipient in hard copy form.
