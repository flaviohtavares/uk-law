# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 157

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 157. Personal delivery of documents

A document is delivered if it is personally delivered in accordance with the rules for personal service in CPR Part 6[^f00052].
