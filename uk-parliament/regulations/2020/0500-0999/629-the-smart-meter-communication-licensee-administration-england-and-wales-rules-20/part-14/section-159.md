# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 159

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 159. Delivery of documents to authorised recipients

Where under the 1986 Act or these Rules a document is to be delivered to a person, it may be delivered instead to any other person authorised in writing to accept delivery on behalf of the first-mentioned person.
