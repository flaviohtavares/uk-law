# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 191

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 191. Charges for copies of documents

Except where prohibited by these Rules, the SMCL administrator is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee for copies of documents requested by a creditor, member or contributory.
