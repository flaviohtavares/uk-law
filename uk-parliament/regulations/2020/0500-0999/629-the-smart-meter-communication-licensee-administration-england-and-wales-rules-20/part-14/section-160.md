# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Delivery of documents to joint SMCL administrators

Delivery of a document to one of joint SMCL administrators is to be treated as delivery to them all.
