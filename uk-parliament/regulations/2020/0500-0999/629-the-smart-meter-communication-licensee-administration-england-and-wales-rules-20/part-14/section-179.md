# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 179

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 179. Non-Gazette notices – other provisions

(1) Information which this Chapter requires to be specified in a notice must be included in an advertisement of that notice in a way that is clear and comprehensible.

(2) Information required under this Chapter to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
