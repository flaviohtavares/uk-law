# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 180

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 180. Application of this Chapter

Where under the 1986 Act or these Rules a document is to be delivered to the registrar of companies, in addition to any content specifically required by the 1986 Act or any other provision of these Rules, the document must contain the contents set out in this Chapter.
