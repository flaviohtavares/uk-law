# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 156

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 156. Application of Chapter

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), this Chapter applies where a document is required under the 1986 Act or these Rules to be delivered, filed, forwarded, furnished, given, sent or submitted by any person unless the 1986 Act, a Rule or an order of the court makes different provision including one requiring service of the document.

(2) This Chapter does not apply to the delivery of documents to the registrar of companies.
