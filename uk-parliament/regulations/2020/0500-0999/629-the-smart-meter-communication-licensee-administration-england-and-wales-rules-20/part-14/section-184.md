# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 184

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 184. Documents relating to court orders

A document relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
