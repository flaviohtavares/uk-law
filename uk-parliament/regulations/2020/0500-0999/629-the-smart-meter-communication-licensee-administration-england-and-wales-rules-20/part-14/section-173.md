# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 173

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 173. Service of orders staying proceedings

(1) This Rule applies where the court makes an order staying an action, execution or other legal process against the property of the SMCL.

(2) The applicant must serve the order.

(3) The order may be served within the jurisdiction by serving a sealed copy at the address for service of—

- (a) the claimant; or
- (b) another party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
