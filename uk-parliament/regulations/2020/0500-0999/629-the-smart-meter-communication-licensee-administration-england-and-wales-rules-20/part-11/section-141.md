# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 141

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 141. Court orders

Notwithstanding any requirement in these Rules as to the contents of a court order, the court may make such other order or in such form as the court thinks just.
