# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Application of Chapter and interpretation

(1) This Chapter applies to costs of and in connection with SMCL administration proceedings.

(2) In this Chapter, “costs” includes charges and expenses.

(3) CPR Parts 44 and 47[^f00044] (which relate to costs) apply to such costs.
