# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 105

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 105. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in SMCL administration proceedings, except an application for an SMCL administration order.
