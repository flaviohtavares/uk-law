# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 130

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 130. Witness statement in support of application

An application under Rule 129(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
