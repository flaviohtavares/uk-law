# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Enforcement of court orders

In SMCL administration proceedings, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
