# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. Costs paid otherwise than out of the assets of the SMCL

Where the amount of costs is decided by detailed assessment under an order of the court directing that the costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the assets of the SMCL, the costs officer must note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
