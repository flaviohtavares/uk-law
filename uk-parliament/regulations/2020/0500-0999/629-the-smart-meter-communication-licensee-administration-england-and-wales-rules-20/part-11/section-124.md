# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Award of costs against SMCL administrator

Without prejudice to any provision of the 1986 Act or these Rules by virtue of which the SMCL administrator is not in any event to be liable for costs and expenses, where an SMCL administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, the SMCL administrator is not to be personally liable for the costs unless the court otherwise directs.
