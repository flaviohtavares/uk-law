# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Use of reports

(1) A report may be filed in court by the SMCL administrator instead of a witness statement, unless the application involves other parties or the court otherwise directs.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of a witness statement, the report is to be treated for the purposes of Rule 112 and any hearing before the court as if it were a witness statement.
