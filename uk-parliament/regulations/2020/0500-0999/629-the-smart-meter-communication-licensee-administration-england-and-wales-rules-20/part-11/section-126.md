# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Costs and expenses of witnesses

(1) An officer of the SMCL to which the SMCL administration proceedings relate is not to receive an allowance as a witness in an examination or other proceedings before the court except as directed by the court.

(2) A person making any application in SMCL administration proceedings is not to receive an allowance as a witness for attending the hearing of the application, but the costs officer may allow that person’s expenses of travelling and subsistence.
