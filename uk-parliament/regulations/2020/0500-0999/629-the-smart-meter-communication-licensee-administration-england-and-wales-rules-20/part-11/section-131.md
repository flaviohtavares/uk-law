# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 131

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 131. Service of notices following appointment

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 129 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or sent to, the incapacitated person.
