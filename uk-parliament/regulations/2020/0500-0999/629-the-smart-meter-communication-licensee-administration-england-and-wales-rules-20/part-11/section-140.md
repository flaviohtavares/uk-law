# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 140

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 140. Further information and disclosure

(1) A party to SMCL administration proceedings may apply to the court for an order—

- (a) that in accordance with CPR Part 18[^f00050] (further information) another party—
  - (i) clarify a matter that is in dispute in the proceedings; or
  - (ii) give additional information in relation to such a matter; or
- (b) for disclosure from any person in accordance with CPR Part 31 (disclosure and inspection of documents)[^f00051], save where Rules 18 or 24 apply.

(2) An application under this Rule may be made without notice to any other party.
