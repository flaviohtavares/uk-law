# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 128

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 128. Introductory

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where it appears to the court in SMCL administration proceedings that a person affected by the proceedings is unable to manage and administer that person’s own property and affairs by reason of—

- (a) lacking capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^f00045];
- (b) suffering from a physical affliction; or
- (c) disability.

(2) Such a person is referred to in this Chapter as “the incapacitated person”.
