# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Application to replace

(1) Where an application is made to court under paragraph 91(1) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act to appoint a replacement SMCL administrator, the application must be accompanied by a statement in accordance with Rule 6 by the person proposed to be the replacement SMCL administrator.

(2) In addition to those persons referred to in section 156(2) of the 2004 Act and Rule 9(3), the applicant must deliver a copy of the application to the person who made the application for the SMCL administration order.

(3) Rule 171 applies to the service of an application under paragraph 91(1) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act as it applies to service of an application for an SMCL administration order.

(4) Rules 11 and 12 apply to an application under paragraph 91(1) of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act as they apply to an application for an SMCL administration order.
