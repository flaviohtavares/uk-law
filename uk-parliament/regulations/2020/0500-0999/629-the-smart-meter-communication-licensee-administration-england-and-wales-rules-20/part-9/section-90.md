# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Final progress reports

In this Part—

- “final progress report” means a progress report which includes a summary of—
  - (a) the SMCL administrator’s proposals;
  - (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals;
  - (c) the steps taken during the SMCL administration; and
  - (d) the outcome; and
- “progress report” means a report which complies with Rule 26.
