# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Notification by SMCL administrator of court order

(1) Where the court makes an order to end the SMCL administration, it must, where the applicant is not the SMCL administrator, deliver a copy of the order to the SMCL administrator.

(2) The SMCL administrator must as soon as reasonably practicable deliver a copy of the order and a copy of the final progress report to—

- (a) the registrar of companies;
- (b) the directors of the SMCL; and
- (c) the Secretary of State, GEMA and all those persons to whom notice of the SMCL administrator’s appointment was delivered.
