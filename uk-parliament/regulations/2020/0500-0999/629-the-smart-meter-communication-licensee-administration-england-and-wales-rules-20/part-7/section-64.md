# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Voluntary surrender of security

A creditor who voluntarily surrenders a security may prove for the whole of the creditor’s debt as if it were unsecured.
