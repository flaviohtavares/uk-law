# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Assignment of right to dividend

(1) If a person entitled to a dividend (“the entitled person”) delivers notice to the SMCL administrator that the entitled person wishes the dividend to be paid to another person, or that the entitled person has assigned the entitlement to another person, the SMCL administrator must pay the dividend to that other person accordingly.

(2) A notice delivered under this Rule must specify the name and address of the person to whom payment is to be made.
