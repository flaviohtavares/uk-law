# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend

(1) The SMCL administrator may admit or reject a proof for dividend (in whole or in part).

(2) If the SMCL administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the SMCL administrator must deliver to the creditor a statement of the SMCL administrator’s reasons for doing so, as soon as reasonably practicable.
