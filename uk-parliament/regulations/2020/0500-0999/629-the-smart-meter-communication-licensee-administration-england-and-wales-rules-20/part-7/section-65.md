# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Value of security

(1) A secured creditor may, with the agreement of the SMCL administrator or the permission of the court, at any time alter the value which the secured creditor’s proof puts upon the secured creditor’s security.

(2) However, if a secured creditor has voted in respect of the unsecured balance of the secured creditor’s debt the secured creditor may re-value the secured creditor’s security only with permission of the court.
