# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Discounts

There must in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the SMCL but for its SMCL administration except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
