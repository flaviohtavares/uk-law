# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Withdrawal or variation of proof

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement between the creditor and the SMCL administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
