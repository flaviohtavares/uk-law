# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Debt payable at future time

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the SMCL entered SMCL administration, subject to Rule 86 (adjustment of dividend where payment made before time).
