# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. SMCL administrator to allow inspection of proofs

The SMCL administrator must, so long as proofs delivered to the SMCL administrator are in the possession of the SMCL administrator, allow them to be inspected, at all reasonable times on any business day, by any of the following persons—

- (a) any creditor who has delivered a proof (unless the creditor’s proof has been wholly rejected for purposes of dividend or otherwise);
- (b) any contributory of the SMCL; and
- (c) any person acting on behalf of either of the above.
