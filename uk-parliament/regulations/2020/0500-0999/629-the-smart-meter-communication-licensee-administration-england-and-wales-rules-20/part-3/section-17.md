# The Smart Meter Communication Licensee Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2020, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Filing of statement of affairs etc.

(1) Subject to Rule 18, the SMCL administrator must as soon as reasonably practicable deliver to the registrar of companies a copy of the statement of affairs and any statement of concurrence.

(2) However, the SMCL administrator must not deliver to the registrar of companies with the statement of affairs any schedule required by Rule 15(5)(b).
