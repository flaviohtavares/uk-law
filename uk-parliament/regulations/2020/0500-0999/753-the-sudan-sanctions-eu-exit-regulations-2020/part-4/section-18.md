# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Immigration[^c-1c63f6b9537243a7c22c4aa860dc51b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c24239491].
