# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Export of military goods[^c-6691f6246127e3857fcbe883f1c21aa7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of military goods to, or for use in, Sudan is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
