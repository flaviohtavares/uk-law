# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 24

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Transfer of military technology[^key-7265db3901d5d2463bcdc777638f32e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not—

- (a) transfer military technology to a place in Sudan;
- (b) transfer military technology to a person connected with Sudan.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the transfer was to a place in Sudan;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Sudan.
