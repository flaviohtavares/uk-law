# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-9d8dbc15b9d5317c841b5e3b310d0776]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-6bf5ce19091c6e70def90e32a8b301dd] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.); [^key-aa84d8f34cd421f25a07f1626d20b1ff]...
- (aa)[^key-bf9bb6ab176cbedc9e668158de014ec7] regulation 17A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 18 (immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purpose of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
