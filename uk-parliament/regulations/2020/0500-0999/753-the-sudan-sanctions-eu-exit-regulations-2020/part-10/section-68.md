# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Revocations[^c-6bf511832cc48ce4baa7a69011910c72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 747/2014 of 10 July 2014, concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Sudan and repealing Regulations [(EC) No 131/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/131) and [(EC) No 1184/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1184) is revoked.

(2) The Sudan (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2014 [^c24239921] are revoked.

(3) The Export Control (Sudan, South Sudan and Central African Republic Sanctions) Regulations 2014 [^c24239931] are revoked.
