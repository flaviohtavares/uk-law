# The Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 36

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Licences: general provisions[^c-50ba3f4fc79794e2671f4180044c3d3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-e609312fe1ccc3ca44795528c6aeeeb1] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-f0583528a4382d3072390608a5f27f2e]**P**”), issues[^c-a3b248e33d3b521b7bfbf065fc3e0c47] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-82bfbc41c64011c670a1d09b3a2e8d9b] must give written notice to P[^c-5a8131032f2f75d4358202e0284e67d8] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
