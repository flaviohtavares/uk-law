# The Immingham Open Cycle Gas Turbine Order 2020, section 6

> Text as at 2025-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Benefit of this Order[^c-09535dc0fb6c85f4a6ef9b96d2463222]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to article 7 (consent to transfer benefit of this Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of VPI Immingham B Limited save for Work No. 5, for which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of VPI Immingham B Limited and National Grid.
