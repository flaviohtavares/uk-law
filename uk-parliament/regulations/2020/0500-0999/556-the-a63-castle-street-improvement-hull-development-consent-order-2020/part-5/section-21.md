## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-f16d43295420a0df910d0d787556e792]

*Extent: E+W*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24104221] (minerals) is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “ the undertaker ”.
