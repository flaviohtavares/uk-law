# The A63 (Castle Street Improvement, Hull) Development Consent Order 2020, section 20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-8b253d96fe5118d0c93aeb3c74c99e29]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or is incidental to it, or is required as replacement land.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 23 (compulsory acquisition of rights and restrictive covenants) and paragraph (9) of article 29 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
