# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 35

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Licences: general provisions[^key-54e2ad4c85415f543e4335716ba632a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^key-eb45ea622dc1d9dfe76b5c178b55cbe1] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-62b4af4133b06b1454dcf05be615b910]**P**”), issues[^key-00af1afa10ee0340646334562f4154f8] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-e2c1a87d1a7ca1fc3b6f2151665964c5] must give written notice to P[^key-6ed27bea2ae9446e1350811b7d87a789] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
