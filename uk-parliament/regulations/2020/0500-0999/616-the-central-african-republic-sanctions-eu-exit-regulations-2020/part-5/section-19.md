# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 19

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Definition of “military goods” and “military technology”[^key-5d7758b77aeea859513e4529dea46861]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**military goods**” means—
  - (a) any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 [^c24162911], other than any thing which is military technology, and
  - (b) any tangible storage medium on which military technology is recorded or from which it can be derived;
- “**military technology**” means any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 which is described as software or technology.
