# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Export of military goods[^key-c4270aa370d73a11dc3cfdcf2f912af6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of military goods to an armed group operating in the Central African Republic or an associated individual[^key-83e512d9b58e4c927012d3245a98ed48] is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
