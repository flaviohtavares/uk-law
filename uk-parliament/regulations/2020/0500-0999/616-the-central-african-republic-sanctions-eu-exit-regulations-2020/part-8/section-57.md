# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 57

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Monetary penalties[^key-72292178a41edde47a4dcd6c51ed0925]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions are to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c24163221]—

- (a) regulation 23(1)(a) (making military goods and military technology available);
- (b) regulation 24(1)(b) (transfer of military technology);
- (c) regulation 26(1) and (2) (financial services and funds relating to military goods and military technology);
- (d) regulation 27(1)(f)(i) and (g) (brokering services relating to financial services and funds relating to military goods and military technology);
- (e) regulation 28(1) (enabling or facilitating the conduct of armed hostilities).
