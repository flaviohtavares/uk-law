# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 56

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-b29a76e4c8e972240cdf0fbd20032066]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c24163211] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 36 (finance: licensing offences).
