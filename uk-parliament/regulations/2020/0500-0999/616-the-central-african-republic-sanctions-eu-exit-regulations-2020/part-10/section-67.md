# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Revocations[^c-8ace0f15ef9f07466990a9eaf9ef59f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 224/2014 of 10 March 2014 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in the Central African Republic is revoked.

(2) The Central African Republic (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2014 [^c24163321] are revoked.
