## 11. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^key-b3ac51a79f14fada267c19e6b3044e19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means—

- (a) a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.), or
- (b) a person who is a designated person for the purposes of those regulations by reason of regulation 10.
