# The Central African Republic (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-ab0449ae3167052d7c888120ff5a8bc8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-f5fb0f69f070e04f0b14b5de90ef464e] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.); and
- (aa)[^key-165db9b0d18f2daa56b1c536db75b0ee] regulation 17A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 18 (immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purpose of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
