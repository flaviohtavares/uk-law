# The West Midlands Rail Freight Interchange Order 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2025-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Operation and use of railways

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The undertaker may operate and use the railway comprised in the authorised development and any other elements of the authorised development as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of goods.
