# The West Midlands Rail Freight Interchange Order 2020, section 28

> Text as at 2025-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24099071] are incorporated in this Order subject to the following modifications—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “ the undertaker ”;
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “ authorised development ”; and
- (c) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to the plots shown coloured pink, hatched pink or coloured orange on the land plans.
