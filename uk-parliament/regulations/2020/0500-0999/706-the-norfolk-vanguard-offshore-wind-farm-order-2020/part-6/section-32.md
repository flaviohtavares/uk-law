# The Norfolk Vanguard Offshore Wind Farm Order 2020, section 32

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Deemed marine licences under the 2009 Act[^key-f736bed819200b08ecf5d0e795792e4b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 9, 10, 11 and 12 are deemed to have been granted to the undertaker under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act for the licensed marine activities set out in Part 3, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 4, of each licence.
