# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 264

Coroners, England And Wales

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2020

Made

9th March 2020

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2020

Coming into force

1st April 2020

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 176(3)(a) of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to, the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001], and after consulting in accordance with that paragraph, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2020 and comes into force on 1st April 2020.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “coroner area” means a coroner area specified in the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013[^f00002];
- “new coroner area” means the new coroner area resulting from the combining of the coroner areas as set out in Article 3.

## 3. New coroner area

The following coroner areas are combined to create the new coroner area of Hampshire, Portsmouth and Southampton:

- (a) Central Hampshire;
- (b) North East Hampshire;
- (c) Portsmouth and South East Hampshire; and
- (d) Southampton and New Forest.

Alex Chalk

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroners Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013 ([SI 2013/1625](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1625)), which came into force on 25<sup>th</sup> July 2013, constituted the former coroners’ districts under the Coroners Act [1988 (c 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/13) as corresponding coroner areas under the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25).

This Order combines four coroner areas into one new coroner area, called Hampshire, Portsmouth and Southampton.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
