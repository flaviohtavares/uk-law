# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 147

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2020

Made

12th February 2020

Coming into force

13th February 2020

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2020

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2020 and comes into force on 13th February 2020.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
