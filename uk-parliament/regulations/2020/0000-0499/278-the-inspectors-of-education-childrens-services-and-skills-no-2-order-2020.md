# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 278

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2020

Made

11th March 2020

Coming into force

12th March 2020

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of March 2020

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2020 and comes into force on 12th March 2020.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Person appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 12th March 2020

Article 2

Hannah Stoten

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the person named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
