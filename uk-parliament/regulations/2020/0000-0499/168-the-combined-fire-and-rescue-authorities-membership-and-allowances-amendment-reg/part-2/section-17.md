# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Variation of the Shropshire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Shropshire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00019] is varied in accordance with Schedule 15.

## Annotations

[^f00019]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Shropshire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2702](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2702)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
