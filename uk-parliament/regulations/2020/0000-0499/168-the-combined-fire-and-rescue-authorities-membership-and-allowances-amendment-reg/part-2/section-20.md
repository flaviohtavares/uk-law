# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 20

## 20. Variation of the Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Authority Combination Scheme

The Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Authority Combination Scheme[^f00022] is varied in accordance with Schedule 18.

## Annotations

[^f00022]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Authority (Combination Scheme) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/2790](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2790)).
