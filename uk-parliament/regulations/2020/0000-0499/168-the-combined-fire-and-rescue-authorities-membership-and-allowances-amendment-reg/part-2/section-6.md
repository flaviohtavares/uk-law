# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Variation of the Leicestershire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Leicestershire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00008] is varied in accordance with Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Leicestershire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2912](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2912)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
