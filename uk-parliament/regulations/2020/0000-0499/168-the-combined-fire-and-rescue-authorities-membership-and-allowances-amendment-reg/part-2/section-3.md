# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 3

## 3. Variation of the Avon Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Avon Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00006] is varied in accordance with Schedule 1.

## Annotations

[^f00006]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Avon Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/3127](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3127)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
