# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Variation of the Cheshire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Cheshire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00016] is varied in accordance with Schedule 12.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Cheshire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2697](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2697)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
