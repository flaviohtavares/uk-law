# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Variation of the Humberside Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Humberside Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00007] is varied in accordance with Schedule 3.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Humberside Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/3132](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3132)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
