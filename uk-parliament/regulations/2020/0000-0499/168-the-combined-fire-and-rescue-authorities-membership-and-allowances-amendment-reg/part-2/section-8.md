# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Variation of the Derbyshire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Derbyshire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00010] is varied in accordance with Schedule 6.

## Annotations

[^f00010]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Derbyshire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2919](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2919)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
