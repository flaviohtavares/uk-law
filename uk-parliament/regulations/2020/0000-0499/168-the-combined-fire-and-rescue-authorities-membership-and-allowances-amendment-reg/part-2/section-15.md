# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Variation of the Hereford and Worcester Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Hereford and Worcester Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00017] is varied in accordance with Schedule 13.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Hereford and Worcester Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2700](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2700)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
