# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Variation of the Lancashire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Lancashire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00020] is varied in accordance with Schedule 16.

## Annotations

[^f00020]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Lancashire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2760](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2760)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
