# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Variation of the Buckinghamshire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Buckinghamshire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00013] is varied in accordance with Schedule 9.

## Annotations

[^f00013]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Buckinghamshire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2924](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2924)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
