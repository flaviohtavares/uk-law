# The Combined Fire and Rescue Authorities (Membership and Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Variation of the Cambridgeshire Fire Services Combination Scheme

The Cambridgeshire Fire Services Combination Scheme[^f00015] is varied in accordance with Schedule 11.

## Annotations

[^f00015]: Footnote: The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to the Cambridgeshire Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2696](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2696)); to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.
