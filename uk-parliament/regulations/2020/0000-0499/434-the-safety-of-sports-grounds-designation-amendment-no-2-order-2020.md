# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 434

Sports Grounds And Sporting Events

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2020

Made

17th April 2020

Laid before Parliament

21st April 2020

Coming into force

13th May 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that the conditions in section 1(1) of that Act are satisfied[^f00002] and has, in accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, consulted with such persons and bodies of persons as appear requisite to the Secretary of State.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2020 and comes into force on 13th May 2020.

## 2. Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015

(1) The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 2 (sports grounds designated under article 2(2)), after the entry for Griffin Park, occupied by Brentford Football Club, insert—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Brentford Community Stadium, Lionel Road South, Brentford TW8 0RU | Brentford Football Club and London Irish Rugby Football Club |

.

Nigel Huddleston

Minister for Sport, Tourism and Heritage

Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport

17th April 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661)) so as to designate Brentford Community Stadium, Brentford, as a sports ground for which a safety certificate is required under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act [1975 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52).

Brentford Community Stadium is occupied by Brentford Football Club (a member of the Football League Limited) and London Irish Rugby Football Club, and is a sports ground in England and Wales at which association football matches are played and which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or significant, impact on private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [1975 c. 52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52); section 1(1) was amended by Schedule 2 to the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act [1987 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/27).

[^f00002]: Footnote: By section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, the Secretary of State may by order designate as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under that Act any sports ground which, in the Secretary of State’s opinion, has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators. The Safety of Sports Grounds (Accommodation of Spectators) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/499](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/499)) substituted the number 5,000 for the number of spectators specified in section 1(1) of that Act in respect of sports grounds in England and Wales at which association football matches are played and which are occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited.

[^f00003]: Footnote: [S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661), to which there are amendments not relevant to this Order.
