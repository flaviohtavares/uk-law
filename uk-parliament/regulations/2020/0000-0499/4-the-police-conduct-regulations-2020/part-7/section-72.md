## 72. Amendment of the Special Constables Regulations 1965[^c-6a44122474f3bafe470be0c886fae52e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Special Constables Regulations 1965 [^c23960891] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3(1) (retirement), omit “: Provided that a special constable may not, without the consent of the chief constable, give notice for the purposes of this Regulation, or retire in pursuance of a notice previously given, while suspended under these Regulations”.
