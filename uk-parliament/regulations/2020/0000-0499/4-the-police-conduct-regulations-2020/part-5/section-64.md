> Text as at 2026-07-13.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Record of accelerated misconduct hearing[^key-d75906055417ed09e3ef7fc8180123bf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A verbatim record of the proceedings at the accelerated misconduct hearing must be taken.

(2) The officer concerned must, on request, be supplied with a copy of the record of the proceedings at the accelerated misconduct hearing.
