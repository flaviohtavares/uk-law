## 60. Notice to complainant and interested persons of accelerated misconduct hearing[^c-3beab6e0404d193f76b3655f4cfadaf3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies in the case of an accelerated misconduct hearing arising from the investigation of a—

- (a) conduct matter under Schedule 3 to the 2002 Act (handling of complaints and conduct matters etc.), or
- (b) complaint to which paragraph 19A of that Schedule (special procedure where investigation relates to police officer or special constable) applied.

(2) The appropriate authority must notify the complainant and any interested person of the date, time and place of the accelerated misconduct hearing and of their right to make representations under regulation 53(3).
