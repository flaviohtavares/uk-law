> Text as at 2026-07-13.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Record of disciplinary proceedings[^c-6317bdea9c4fe77a1c69f69a689a10c4]

*Extent: E+W*

The appropriate authority must cause a record to be kept of disciplinary proceedings brought against every officer concerned, together with the finding and decision on disciplinary action and the decision in any appeal by the officer.
