# The Police (Conduct) Regulations 2020, section 22

> Text as at 2026-07-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. General[^c-f3b1e5ce1d7beebcffbaee2bfb07acbf]

*Extent: E+W*

Any period of time specified in this Part in relation to misconduct proceedings may be reduced by agreement between the appropriate authority or, as the case may be, the originating authority, the officer concerned, where the Director General is presenting the case, the Director General, and the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings.
