> Text as at 2026-07-13.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Failure to engage with the reflective practice review process[^c-bc11d3a87d7b623b076f40161547d617]

*Extent: E+W*

If the reviewer considers that the participating officer is failing to engage with the reflective practice review process, the reviewer may refer that failure for assessment by the appropriate authority under regulation 14.
