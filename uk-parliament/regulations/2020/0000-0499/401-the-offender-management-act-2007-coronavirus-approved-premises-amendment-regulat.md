# The Offender Management Act 2007 (Coronavirus) (Approved Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 401

Probation, England And Wales

The Offender Management Act 2007 (Coronavirus) (Approved Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made

5th April 2020

Laid before Parliament

at 11.00 a.m. on 6th April 2020

Coming into force

at 12.01 a.m. on 6th April 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 13(2) and 36(2)(a) of the Offender Management Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Offender Management Act 2007 (Coronavirus) (Approved Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and shall come into force at 12.01 a.m. on 6th April 2020.

## 2. Amendment to the Offender Management Act 2007 (Approved Premises) Regulations 2014

In the definition of “licence” in regulation 4 (interpretation) of the Offender Management Act 2007 (Approved Premises) Regulations 2014[^f00002], after “rule 9” insert “or rule 9A”.

Lucy Frazer

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

5th April 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make consequential amendment to regulation 4 of the Offender Management Act 2007 (Approved Premises) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/1198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1198)) (“the 2014 Regulations”), following the creation of a new coronavirus restricted temporary release provision by the Prison and Young Offender Institution (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Rules 2020 ([S.I. 2020/400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/400)).

The instrument amends the definition of “licence” in regulation 4 of the 2014 Regulations to refer to the new coronavirus restricted temporary release provision, enabling those released under that provision to be housed in approved premises.

A full impact assessment will be produced for this instrument and published on the legislation.gov.uk website.
