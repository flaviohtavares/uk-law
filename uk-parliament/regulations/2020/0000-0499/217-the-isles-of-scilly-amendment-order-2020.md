# The Isles of Scilly (Amendment) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 217

Local Government, England

The Isles of Scilly (Amendment) Order 2020

Made

4th March 2020

Coming into force

1st April 2020

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 265(2) and 266(2) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001].

The Council of the Isles of Scilly has, in accordance with section 265(2) of the Local Government Act 1972, applied to the Secretary of State to make an order under that section of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Isles of Scilly (Amendment) Order 2020.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st April 2020.

## 2. Amendment to the Isles of Scilly Order 1978

In the Isles of Scilly Order 1978[^f00002], in the Schedule, omit the entries relating to—

- (a) section 181(1) (local authority for the purposes of certain water enactments);
- (b) section 181(2) (local authority for the purposes of certain sewerage enactments).

Rebecca Pow

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

4th March 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Isles of Scilly Order 1978 ([S.I. 1978/1844](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/1844)) to get rid of modifications relating to section 181(1) and (2) of the Local Government Act [1972 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
