# The Stalking Protection Act 2019 (Commencement) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 26 (C. 3)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Stalking Protection Act 2019 (Commencement) Regulations 2020

Made

9th January 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(3) of the Stalking Protection Act 2019[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Stalking Protection Act 2019 (Commencement) Regulations 2020.

## 2. Commencement

The Stalking Protection Act 2019 comes into force on 20th January 2020 in so far as it is not already in force.

Victoria Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

9th January 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring the provisions of the Stalking Protection Act [2019 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2019/9) into force on 20th January 2020 in so far as they are not already in force. Section 15 of that Act came into force on Royal Assent.
