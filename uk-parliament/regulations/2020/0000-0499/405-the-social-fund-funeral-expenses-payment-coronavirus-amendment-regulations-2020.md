# The Social Fund Funeral Expenses Payment (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 405

Social Security

The Social Fund Funeral Expenses Payment (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made

6th April 2020

Laid before Parliament

7th April 2020

Coming into force

8th April 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 138(1)(a) and (4) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001].

In accordance with section 173(1)(a) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002], it appears to the Secretary of State that by reason of the urgency of this matter it is inexpedient to refer the proposals in respect of these Regulations to the Social Security Advisory Committee.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Fund Funeral Expenses Payment (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 8th April 2020.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales only.

## 2. Amendments to the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 2005

In the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 2005[^f00003], in regulation 9 (amount of funeral payment)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(g), for “£700” substitute “£1,000”; and
- (b) in paragraph (10)(b), for “£700” substitute “£1,000”.

## 3. Transitional provision

The amendments made by regulation 2 only apply to claims in respect of a death that occurs on or after 8th<sup> </sup>April 2020.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Stedman-Scott

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/3061](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3061)) to increase the amount paid for expenses additional to those listed in the Regulations from £700 to £1,000 for deaths which occur on or after 8th April 2020.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
