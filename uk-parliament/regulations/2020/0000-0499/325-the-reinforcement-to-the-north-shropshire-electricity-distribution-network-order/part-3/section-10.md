# The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network Order 2020, section 10

> Text as at 2020-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Street works[^c-b70799e11020eca7b7ed198caac58350]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may, for the purposes of the authorised development, enter on so much of any of the streets specified in Schedule 3 (streets subject to street works) as is within the Order limits and may carry out the street works specified in column (3) of that Schedule.

(2) The authority given by paragraph (1) is a statutory right for the purposes of section 48(3) (streets, street works and undertakers) and 51(1) of the 1991 Act (prohibition of unauthorised street works).

(3) The powers conferred in paragraphs (1) and (2) are without prejudice to the powers of the undertaker under the 1989 Act.

(4) The provisions of sections 54 to 106 of the 1991 Act apply to any street works carried out under paragraph (1).
