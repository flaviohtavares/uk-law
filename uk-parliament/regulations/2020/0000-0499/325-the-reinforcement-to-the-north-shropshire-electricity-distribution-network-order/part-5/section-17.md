# The Reinforcement to the North Shropshire Electricity Distribution Network Order 2020, section 17

> Text as at 2020-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Compulsory acquisition of rights: incorporation of the mineral code[^c-e4304c90be1a0bd8bd37665b0442ab05]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 [^c24030521] (minerals) are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for the “acquiring authority” there is substituted “ the undertaker ”.
