## 3. Development consent etc. granted by the Order[^c-045b5fd1894c635e5847cdb39955fa5b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development as set out in Schedule 1 (authorised development), to be carried out within the Order limits and subject to the provisions of this Order including the requirements set out in Schedule 2 (requirements).

(2) Subject to article 4 (limits of deviation), each numbered work must be situated in the area shown on the works plans.
