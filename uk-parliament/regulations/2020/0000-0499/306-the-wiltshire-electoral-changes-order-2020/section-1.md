# The Wiltshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Wiltshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2020.

(2) This article and article 2 come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

(3) Articles 3 and 4 come into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on 15th October 2020;
- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England[^f00003] in 2021.

## Annotations

[^f00003]: Footnote: The ordinary day of election of councillors in England is prescribed by section 37(1) of the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2), as amended by section 18(2) of the Representation of the People Act [1985 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/50) and section 6(16) of the Wales Act [2017 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/4).
