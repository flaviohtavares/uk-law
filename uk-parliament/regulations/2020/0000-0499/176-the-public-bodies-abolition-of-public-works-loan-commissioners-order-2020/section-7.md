# The Public Bodies (Abolition of Public Works Loan Commissioners) Order 2020, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Repeal of reference to the Commissioners in the Public Bodies Act 2011

In Schedule 1 to the Act, omit “Public Works Loan Commissioners”[^f00004].

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: Public Works Loan Commissioners were inserted into Schedule 1 of the Public Bodies Act 2011 by section 54 of the Infrastructure Act [2015 c.7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/7).
