# The Public Bodies (Abolition of Public Works Loan Commissioners) Order 2020, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “Commissioner” means a Public Works Loan Commissioner[^f00002];
- “the transfer date” means the day on which this Order (other than as provided in article 1(3)) comes into force.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: The Public Works Loan Commissioners were established by section 4 of the Public Works Loans Act 1875.
