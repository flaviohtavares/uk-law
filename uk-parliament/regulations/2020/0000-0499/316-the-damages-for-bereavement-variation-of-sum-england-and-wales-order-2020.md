# The Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 316

Damages, England And Wales

The Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2020

Made

16th March 2020

Laid before Parliament

19th March 2020

Coming into force

1st May 2020

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1A(5) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2020 and comes into force on 1st May 2020.

(2) This Order applies only to causes of action which accrue on or after 1st May 2020.

## 2. Variation of sum

In section 1A(3) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976, for “£12,980” substitute “£15,120”.

Alex Chalk

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

16th March 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies only to causes of action accruing on or after 1st May 2020, amends section 1A(3) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976 by increasing the sum which may be awarded as damages for bereavement in England and Wales from £12,980 to £15,120.
