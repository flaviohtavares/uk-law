# The Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 45 (C. 4)

Terms And Conditions Of Employment

The Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2020

Made

17th January 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act 2018[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2020.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 and the Schedule to the Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act 2018 is the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

Kelly Tolhurst

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

17th January 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force the substantive provisions of the Parental Bereavement (Leave and Pay) Act [2018 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/24), including Schedule 1 which introduces a new Chapter 4 (Parental Bereavement Leave) in Part 8 of the Employment Rights Act [1996 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18) and a new Part 12ZD (Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay) in the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4).
