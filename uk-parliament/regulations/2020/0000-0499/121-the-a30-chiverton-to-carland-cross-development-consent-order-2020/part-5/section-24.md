# The A30 Chiverton to Carland Cross Development Consent Order 2020, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00044] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
