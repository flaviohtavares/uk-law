# The Riverside Energy Park Order 2020, section 33

> Text as at 2023-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Statutory undertakers[^c-cbdf9c8620bbf8e2ce17d3dd1fcc8e84]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of article 24(2) (compulsory acquisition of rights) and Schedule 10 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) exercise the powers conferred by article 22 (compulsory acquisition of land) and article 25 (acquisition of subsoil only) in relation to so much of the Order land as belongs to statutory undertakers;
- (b) extinguish or suspend the rights of or restrictive covenants for the benefit of, and remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to, statutory undertakers on, under, over or within the Order land.
