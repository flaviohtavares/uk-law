# The Riverside Energy Park Order 2020, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2023-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 13 — MODIFICATIONS TO THE SECTION 36 CONSENT AND RRRF PLANNING PERMISSION

*Extent: E+W*

Article 6

### PART 1 — SECTION 36 CONSENT

**1.**[^c-10e1a84be79fbb60f5c04e14c38728f8] Delete the words “associated open storage areas for ash container storage,” in paragraph 2(f).

**2.**[^c-ae39bdc32178143568850fe3d9ac49c7] Delete “and” at the end of paragraph 3(1)(i).

**3.**[^c-c512b5c616dbc8101a1f5b527d8f04eb] For the words “25 September 2014.” at the end of paragraph 3(1)(ii) substitute “ 25 September 2014; and ”.

**4.**[^c-6124a900297ff63c809ed9d7e93f353f] After paragraph 3(1)(ii) insert new paragraph 3(1)(iii) as follows, “ (iii) the Riverside Energy Park Order 2020 ”.

### PART 2 — RRRF PLANNING PERMISSION

**5.**[^c-1e0502e8ab4bbbff8d82cc7649874cfc] After RRRF condition 1(iii), insert new condition 1(iv) as follows, “ (iv) the Riverside Energy Park Order 2020 ”.

**6.**[^c-b812c6c9c4f3afe5e8fdf9cfd1469a89] In RRRF condition 7, insert the words “ (except for the development authorised by the Riverside Energy Park Order 202 ”)” after “ and for no other purpose ”.

**7.**[^c-89dbda69d3323921d29bb204e3335a22] For RRRF condition 23, substitute new condition 23 as follows “ 23. Bottom ash shall only be stored in the bunkers to the development hereby approved ”.
