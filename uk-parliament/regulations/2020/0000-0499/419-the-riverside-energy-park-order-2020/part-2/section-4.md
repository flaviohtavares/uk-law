> Text as at 2023-02-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-b3b9b46a6c174da79cf71e37b6f8b662]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development except to the extent that this Order and the requirements or an agreement made under this Order provides otherwise.

(2) This article only authorises the carrying out of maintenance works within the Order limits.
