# The Riverside Energy Park Order 2020, section 7

> Text as at 2023-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Port of London Act 1968[^key-c1cfd3ea40d71c53cb7ddbd5af429de7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Nothing in this Order relieves the undertaker of any obligation to obtain any permit or licence under the Port of London Act 1968 [^c24052581] in respect of works or operations carried out within the Thames under the powers of this Order.

(2) In this article “**the Thames**” means that part of the river Thames within the Order limits and within the limits of the Port of London Authority, as described in Schedule 1 (description of port limits) to the Port of London Act 1968.
