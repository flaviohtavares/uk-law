# The A585 Windy Harbour to Skippool Highway Development Consent Order 2020, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Deemed Marine Licence

The undertaker is deemed to have been granted the licence under Part 4 of the 2009 Act contained in Schedule 8 to this Order, to carry out works and make the deposits described in that licence and subject to the licence conditions which are deemed to have been attached to the licence by the Secretary of State under Part 4 of the 2009 Act.
