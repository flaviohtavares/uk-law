# The A585 Windy Harbour to Skippool Highway Development Consent Order 2020, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00049]is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for the “acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
