# The Lake Lothing (Lowestoft) Third Crossing Order 2020, section 55

> Text as at 2020-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Deemed marine licence[^c-d90441617a59d8adb9a8496420bbbd0d]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker is granted a deemed marine licence under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act to carry out the activities specified in Part 1 of Schedule 12 (deemed marine licence), subject to the conditions set out in Parts 2 to 4 of that Schedule.
