## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-e96246a63cfd33e155c3acf40450aeda]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for the “acquiring authority” there is substituted “ the undertaker ”.
