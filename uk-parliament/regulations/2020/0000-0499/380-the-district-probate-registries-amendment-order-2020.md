# The District Probate Registries (Amendment) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 380 (L. 10)

Senior Courts Of England And Wales

The District Probate Registries (Amendment) Order 2020

Made

30th March 2020

Laid before Parliament

31st March 2020

Coming into force

22nd April 2020

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 104(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00001], having consulted the President of the Family Division as nominee of the Lord Chief Justice[^f00002], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the District Probate Registries (Amendment) Order 2020 and comes into force on 22nd April 2020.

## 2. Amendments to the District Probate Registries Order 1982

(1) The Schedule to the District Probate Registries Order 1982[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(1), omit “column 1 of”

(3) Omit article 3.

(4) In article 4(1), omit “or of a district probate sub-registry”.

(5) In column 1 of the Schedule—

- (a) omit the heading “Column 1”; and
- (b) omit “Birmingham”.

(6) Omit column 2 of the Schedule.

Chris Philp

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

30th March 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the District Probate Registries Order 1982 ([S.I.1982/379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/379)) (the “Order”) to revoke the Lord Chancellor’s direction that a probate registry be established at Birmingham and that sub-registries be established at the places listed in column 2 of the Schedule to the Order.
