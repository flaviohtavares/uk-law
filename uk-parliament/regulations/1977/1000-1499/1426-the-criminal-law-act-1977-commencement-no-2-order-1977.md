# The Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1977

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1977 No. 1426 (C.51)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1977

Made

18th August 1977

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65(7) of the Criminal Law Act 1977, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Law Act 1977 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1977.

**2.** The provisions of the Criminal Law Act 1977 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force on 8th September 1977, in addition to the provisions coming into force on that date by virtue of the Criminal Law Act (Commencement No. 1) Order 1977[^f00001]

Albert Booth

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

18th August 1977
