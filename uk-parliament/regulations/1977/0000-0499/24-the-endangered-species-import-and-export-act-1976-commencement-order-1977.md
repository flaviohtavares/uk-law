# The Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1977 No. 24 (C.1)

ANIMALS

The Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977

Made

10th January 1977

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 13(3) of the Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977.

**2.** The Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976 shall come into operation on 3rd February 1977.

Peter Shore

Secretary of State for the Environment

10th January 1977

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order fixes the 3rd February 1977 as the day on which the Endangered Species (Import and Export) Act 1976 will come into operation.
