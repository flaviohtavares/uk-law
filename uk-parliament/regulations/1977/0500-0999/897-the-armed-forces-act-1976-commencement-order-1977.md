# The Armed Forces Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1977 No. 897 (C.32)

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977

Made

24th May 1977

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 22(8) of the Armed Forces Act 1976 and all other powers enabling him, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** Except as otherwise provided by section 22(7) of the Armed Forces Act 1976, the provisions of the said Act shall come into force on 1st July 1977 and, accordingly, all the provisions thereof which have not previously come into force shall come into force on that date.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1977.

Fred Mulley

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

24th May 1977

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on the 1st July 1977 those provisions of the Armed Forces Act 1976 which are not already in operation.
