# The Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries (Aircraft Industry Vesting Date) Order 1977

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1977 No. 539

AIRCRAFT AND SHIPBUILDING INDUSTRIES

The Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries (Aircraft Industry Vesting Date) Order 1977

Made

22nd March 1977

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 56(1) of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Act 1977(hereinafter referred to as “**The Act**”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries (Aircraft Industry Vesting Date) Order 1977.

**2.** The aircraft industry vesting date for the purposes of section 19(1) of the Act shall be 29th April 1977.

Eric Varley

Secretary of State for Industry

22nd March 1977

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies the aircraft industry vesting date for the purpose of section 19(1) of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Act 1977.
