# The Pensions Increase (Specification of Pensions) Regulations 1977

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1977 No. 1652

PENSIONS

The Pensions Increase (Specification of Pensions) Regulations 1977

Made

11th October 1977

Laid before Parliament

21st October 1977

Coming into Operation

11th November 1977

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Specification of Pensions) Regulations 1977, and shall come into operation on 11th November 1977.

(2) In these Regulations “**pension**” means a pension within the meaning of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

(3) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

**2.** The Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 shall have effect in relation to a pension payable by virtue of the Regulations specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto as if it were a pension specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

**3.** The Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 shall have effect in relation to a pensions payable by virtue of the Regulations specified in Part II of the Schedule hereto as if it were a pension specified in Part II of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

Given under the official seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on 11th October 1977.

C. R. Morris

Minister of State

Civil Service Department

## SCHEDULE — SPECIFIED REGULATIONS

Regulations 2 and 3

### PART I

**1.** The Vehicle and Driving Licences (Compensation to Officers) Regulations 1977[^f00001] or under any regulation amending or replacing the same.

### PART II

**2.** The New Towns Transfer Schemes (Compensation) Regulations 1977[^f00002] or under any regulation amending or replacing the same.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to pensions paid under the regulations specified in the Schedule thereto.
