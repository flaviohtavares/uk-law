# The Norfolk Vanguard Offshore Wind Farm Order 2022, section 32

> Text as at 2025-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Deemed marine licences under the 2009 Act[^key-34baf4114fbcac30fdfa3b463c346fe3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 9, 10, 11 and 12 are deemed to have been granted to the undertaker under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act for the licensed marine activities set out in Part 3, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 4, of each of those Schedules.
