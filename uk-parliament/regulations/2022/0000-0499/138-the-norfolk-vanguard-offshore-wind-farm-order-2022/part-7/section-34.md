# The Norfolk Vanguard Offshore Wind Farm Order 2022, section 34

> Text as at 2025-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-6a86ee15cd1b1884ce89ae2598c965da]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order is treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land).
