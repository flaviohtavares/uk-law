# The Norfolk Vanguard Offshore Wind Farm Order 2022, section 40

> Text as at 2025-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Abatement of works abandoned or decayed[^c-e8bf2a631ec65061e80dc1f4fa56789a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where Work No. 1(a) to (e) or Work No. 2 or any part of those works is abandoned or allowed to fall into decay the Secretary of State may, following consultation with the undertaker, issue a written notice requiring the undertaker at its own expense to repair and restore or remove Work Nos. 1(a) to (e) or Work No. 2 or any relevant part of those works and, without prejudice to any notice served under section 105(2) of the 2004 Act[^f00043], to restore the site of the relevant part(s) of Work Nos. 1(a) to (e) or Work No. 2 to a safe and proper condition within an area and to such an extent as may be specified in the notice.
