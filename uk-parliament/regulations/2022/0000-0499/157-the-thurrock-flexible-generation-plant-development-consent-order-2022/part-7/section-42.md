# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 42

> Text as at 2024-09-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Arbitration[^key-1d8acece1c281b660b18dcf9c6f3fb82]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Except where otherwise expressly provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed in writing between the parties, any difference under any provision of this Order (other than a difference which falls to be determined by the tribunal) must be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.

(2) Any matter for which the consent or approval of the Secretary of State is required under any provision of this Order shall not be subject to arbitration.
