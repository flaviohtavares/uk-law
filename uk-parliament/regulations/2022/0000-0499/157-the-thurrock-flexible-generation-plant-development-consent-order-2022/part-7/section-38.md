# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 38

> Text as at 2024-09-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Certification of plans, etc.[^key-f310c219823330c5f317bd47ac728f12]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit to the Secretary of State copies of—

- (a) the land plans and special category land plans (consisting of a key plan and sheets 1 to 5 inclusive) as approved by the Secretary of State under requirement 30;
- (b) the works plans (document number A2.3);
- (c) the illustrative cross section plans (document number A2.8);
- (d) the illustrative landscape plan (document number A2.9);
- (e) the conceptual drainage strategy (document number A7.3);
- (f) the book of reference as approved by the Secretary of State under requirement 30;
- (g) the environmental statement (document number A6.0);
- (h) the design principles statement (document number A8.4);
- (i) the outline code of construction practice (document number A8.6);
- (j) the outline ecological management plan (document number A8.7);
- (k) the outline construction traffic management plan (document number A8.8);
- (l) the outline construction worker travel plan (document number A8.9);
- (m) the outline written scheme of archaeological investigation (document number A8.11);
- (n) the outline employment and skills strategy (document number A8.13); and
- (o) any other plans or documents referred to in this Order as requiring certification,

for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
