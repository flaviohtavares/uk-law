# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 30

> Text as at 2024-09-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Statutory undertakers[^c-a4a3847d2830b8f3f53d3d570546edbe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 22 and the provisions of Schedule 8 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans and described in the book of reference;
- (b) extinguish or suspend the rights of, and remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to, statutory undertakers over or within the Order limits; and
- (c) acquire compulsorily the new rights over land belonging to statutory undertaker shown on the land plans and described in the book of reference.
