# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 31

> Text as at 2024-09-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Apparatus and rights of statutory undertakers in stopped up streets[^c-d17637f11b2a2df6f6a4893c72ad2d2f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a street is temporarily altered or diverted or its use is temporarily stopped up under article 13 (temporary restriction of use of streets) any statutory undertaker whose apparatus is under, in, on, along or across the street is to have the same powers and right in respect of that apparatus, subject to Schedule 8 (protective provisions), as if this Order had not been made.
