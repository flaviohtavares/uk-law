# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 3

> Text as at 2024-09-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by the Order[^key-f8b55fee92c257ed7c28bffcd5475bc3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of this Order, including the requirements, the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development to be carried out within the Order limits.
