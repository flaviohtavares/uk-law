# The Thurrock Flexible Generation Plant Development Consent Order 2022, section 5

> Text as at 2024-09-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-696859c8ce9513e1150a243b4d59fb29]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.

(2) This article only authorises the carrying out of maintenance works within the Order limits.
