# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) Regulations 2022, section 8

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Fit and proper persons requirement[^key-71ea6a02c1edcbfe6325726d7c1c8031]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 1 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in assessing, for the purposes of section 11 of the Act (fit and proper persons requirement), whether a person is fit and proper to act in a capacity mentioned in section 11(2) of the Act.

(2) [^key-6be7953d522286c7fc330fb790f2436f]... Where a person in a capacity mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 11(2) is a body corporate, the Regulator must assess whether each individual performing the functions of that person in relation to the scheme, in the exercise of a management or executive role, is a fit and proper person to act in relation to the scheme in that capacity.
