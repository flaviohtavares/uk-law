# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) Regulations 2022, section 9

> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Scheme design requirement[^key-83e1c242d5bb8cb17967b928c1342029]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 1 of Schedule 2 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 12 of the Act (scheme design requirement), whether it is satisfied that the design of a collective money purchase scheme is sound.
