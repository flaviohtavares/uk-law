# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Amendment of regulation 15 (determinations in respect of transport managers)

(1) In regulation 15(3)(a), for “for any road transport undertaking” substitute “in relation to any operator’s licence”.

(2) Omit regulation 15(3)(b).

(3) After regulation 15(6) insert—

> (7) Regulation 9 applies for the purposes of this regulation as it applies for the purposes of regulations 5 to 8.
