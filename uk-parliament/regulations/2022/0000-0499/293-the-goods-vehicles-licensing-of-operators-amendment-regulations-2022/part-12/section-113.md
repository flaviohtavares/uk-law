# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Amendment of regulation 5 (good repute)

(1) In the heading to regulation 5, for “Good” substitute “Determination of good”.

(2) In regulation 5(1), after “good repute” insert “under section 12A(2)(b) of the 2010 Act or regulation 13A(1)(b)”.

(3) In regulation 5(2), after “good repute” insert “under section 12A(2)(b) of the 2010 Act”.
