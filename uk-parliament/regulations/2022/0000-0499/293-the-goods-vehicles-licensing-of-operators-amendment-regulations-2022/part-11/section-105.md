# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 105

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 105. Amendment of regulation 3 (small goods vehicle)

In regulation 3(2)(a), for “including 3.5 tonnes and complies with Regulation (EC) No.1071/2009; or” substitute—

> including—
>
> - (i) 2.5 tonnes; or
> - (ii) 3.5 tonnes if it is used only for national transport operations; or

.
