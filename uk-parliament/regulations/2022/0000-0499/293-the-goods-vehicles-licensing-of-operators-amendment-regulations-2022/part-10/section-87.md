# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Amendment of section 16 (variation of operators’ licences)

(1) In section 16(1)(g), before “that a new place” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”.

(2) In section 16(5)(c), after “specified in a” insert “heavy goods vehicle”.
