# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Amendment of section 4 (vehicles authorised to be used under operator’s licence)

(1) After section 4(3) insert—

> (3A) An operator’s licence shall not authorise the use of any light goods vehicle unless the vehicle is registered under the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act [1994 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/22).

.

(2) In section 4(4), in the words before paragraph (a)—

- (a) for “An operator’s” substitute “A heavy goods vehicle”; and
- (b) after “use of any” insert “heavy goods”.
