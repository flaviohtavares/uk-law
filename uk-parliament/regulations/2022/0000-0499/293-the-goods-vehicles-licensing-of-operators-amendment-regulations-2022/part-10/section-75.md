# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Amendment of section 2A (temporary exemptions for international operators)

(1) In section 2A(6)[^f00023], for “is not established in the United Kingdom” substitute “has an effective and stable establishment in a country or territory other than the United Kingdom that is comparable to an effective and stable establishment in Northern Ireland (as determined in such manner as may be set out in regulations made for the purposes of section 12A(2)(a))”.

(2) Omit section 2A(7).

## Annotations

[^f00023]: Footnote: Section 2A was inserted by section 12(4) of the Haulage Permits and Trailer Registration Act 2018.
