# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Amendment of section 20 (conditions of licences)

In section 20(1)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) before “for preventing” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,” and
  - (ii) after “preventing” insert “heavy goods”; and
- (b) in paragraph (c), before “for preventing” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”.
