# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Amendment of section 28 (power to remove operating centres on review)

(1) In section 28(1), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 28(2), after “parking of” insert “heavy goods”.

(3) In section 28(3), in the words after paragraph (b)—

- (a) after “centre for” insert “heavy goods”; and
- (b) for “operator’s” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.
