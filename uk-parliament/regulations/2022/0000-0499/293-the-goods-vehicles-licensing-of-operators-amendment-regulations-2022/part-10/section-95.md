# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Amendment of section 29 (power to attach conditions on review)

(1) In section 29(1), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 29(4)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), after “that any” insert “heavy goods”;
- (c) in paragraph (b), after “maximum number” insert “of heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (d) in paragraphs (c) and (d), after “provision” insert “relating to heavy goods vehicles”.

(3) In section 29(5), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
