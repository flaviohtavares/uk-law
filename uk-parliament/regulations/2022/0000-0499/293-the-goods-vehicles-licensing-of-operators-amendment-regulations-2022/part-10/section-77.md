# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Amendment of section 6 (operating centres to be specified in operators’ licences)

(1) In the heading to section 6, for “operators’” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 6(1)—

- (a) after “centre for” insert “heavy goods”; and
- (b) after “under a” insert “heavy goods vehicle”.

(3) In section 6(3)—

- (a) after “relation to any” insert “heavy goods”;
- (b) for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”; and
- (c) after “centre for” insert “heavy goods”.
