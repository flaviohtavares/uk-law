# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Amendment of section 27 (periods of review for operating centres)

(1) In section 27(1), for “on the licence-holder” substitute “on the holder of a heavy goods vehicle licence”.

(2) In section 27(2), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
