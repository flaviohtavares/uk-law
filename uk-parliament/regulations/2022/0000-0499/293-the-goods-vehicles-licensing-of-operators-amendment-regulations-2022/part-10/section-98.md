# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Amendment of section 49 (certificates of qualification)

(1) In section 49(1)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a)—
  - (i) for “a road transport” substitute “an”;
  - (ii) after “undertaking” insert “established”; and
  - (iii) after “member State” insert “that involves the use of goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in the words after paragraph (c), for “road transport undertakings” substitute “such an undertaking established”.

(2) In section 49(2), omit paragraph (b).

(3) Omit section 49(5).
