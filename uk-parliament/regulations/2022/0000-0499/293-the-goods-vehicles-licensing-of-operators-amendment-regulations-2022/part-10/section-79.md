# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Amendment of section 10 (publication in locality affected of notice of application for licence)

(1) In the heading to section 10, after “application for” insert “heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 10(1), after “the application” insert “for a heavy goods vehicle licence”.

(3) In section 10(2), after “an application” insert “for a heavy goods vehicle licence”.

(4) In section 10(3), in the words before paragraph (a), after “an application” insert “for a heavy goods vehicle licence”.

(5) In section 10(4), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
