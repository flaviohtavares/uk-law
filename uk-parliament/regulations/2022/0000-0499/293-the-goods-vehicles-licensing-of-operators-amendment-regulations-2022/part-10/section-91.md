# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Amendment of section 23 (revocation, suspension and curtailment of operators’ licences)

(1) In section 23(1)(a)—

- (a) before “that a place” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”; and
- (b) after “centre for” insert “heavy goods”.

(2) In section 23(9)(d), before “that any one” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”.
