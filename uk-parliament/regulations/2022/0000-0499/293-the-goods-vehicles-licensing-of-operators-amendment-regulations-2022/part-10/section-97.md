# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 97

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 97. Amendment of section 31 (determinations as to environmental matters)

(1) In section 31(2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), in both places it occurs, for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”; and
- (c) in paragraph (c), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 31(3)(a) and (5)(a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
