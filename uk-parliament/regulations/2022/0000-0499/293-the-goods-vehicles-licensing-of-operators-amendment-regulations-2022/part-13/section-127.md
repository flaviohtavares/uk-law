# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 127. Amendment of regulation 13 (conditions which may be attached to a licence)

In regulation 13—

- (a) in paragraphs (a) and (b), for “motor vehicles or trailers” substitute “heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (c) and (d), for “motor vehicle or trailer” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.
