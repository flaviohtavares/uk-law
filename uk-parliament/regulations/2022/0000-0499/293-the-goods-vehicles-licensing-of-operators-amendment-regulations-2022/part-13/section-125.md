# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Amendment of regulation 2 (interpretation)

In regulation 2(1)—

- (a) after the definition of “firm” insert—

  > - “**heavy goods vehicle**” has the meaning given in section 58(1);

  ; and
- (b) after the definition of “the Qualification of Operators Regulations” insert—

  > - “**transport manager**” has the meaning given in section 12A(5);

  .
