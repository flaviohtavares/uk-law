# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 128

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 128. Amendment of regulation 14 (considerations relevant to determinations)

(1) In the heading to regulation 14, after “determinations” insert “as to environmental matters”.

(2) In regulation 14(1)—

- (a) in paragraphs (d) and (e), for “motor vehicles or trailers” substitute “heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in paragraph (h), for “vehicular” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.
