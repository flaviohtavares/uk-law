# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Amendment of section 3A (temporary exemptions for international operators)

(1) In section 3A(6)[^f00011], for “is not established in the United Kingdom” substitute “has an effective and stable establishment in a country or territory other than the United Kingdom that is comparable to an effective and stable establishment in Great Britain (as determined in accordance with paragraph A1 of Schedule 3)”.

(2) Omit section 3A(7).

## Annotations

[^f00011]: Footnote: Section 3A was inserted by section 11(4) of the Haulage Permits and Trailer Registration Act [2018 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/19).
