# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Amendment of section 34 (determinations as to environmental matters)

(1) In section 34(2)(a), (b) in both places it occurs and (c), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 34(3)(a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(3) In section 34(5)(a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
