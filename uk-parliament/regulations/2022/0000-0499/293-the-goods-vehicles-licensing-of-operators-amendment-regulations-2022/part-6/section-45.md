# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Amendment of section 31 (power to remove operating centres on review)

(1) In section 31(1), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 31(2), after “parking of” insert “heavy goods”.

(3) In section 31(3), in the words after paragraph (b)—

- (a) after “centre for” insert “heavy goods”; and
- (b) for “operator’s” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.
