# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Amendment of section 12 (objections to and representations against, issue of operators’ licences)

(1) In section 12(1)(b), before “that” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”.

(2) In section 12(4), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
