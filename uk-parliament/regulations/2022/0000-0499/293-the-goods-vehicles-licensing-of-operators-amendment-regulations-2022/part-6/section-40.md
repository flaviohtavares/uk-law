# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Amendment of section 22 (conditions as to matters required to be notified to traffic commissioner)

(1) In the heading to section 22, after “Conditions” insert “attached to operators’ licences”.

(2) In section 22(2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “13A(2)” substitute “13A”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) after “manager of” insert “one or more of”; and
  - (ii) for “mentioned in section 13A(3)” substitute “set out in paragraph 14A(1) and (2), or (1) and (3), of Schedule 3”.
