# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Amendment of section 32 (power to attach conditions on review)

(1) In section 32(1), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 32(4)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), after “that any” insert “heavy goods”;
- (c) in paragraph (b), after “maximum number” insert “of heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (d) in paragraphs (c) and (d), after “that a provision” insert “relating to heavy goods vehicles”.

(3) In section 32(5), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
