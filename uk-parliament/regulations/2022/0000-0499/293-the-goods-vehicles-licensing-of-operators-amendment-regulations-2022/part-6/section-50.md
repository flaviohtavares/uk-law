# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Amendment of section 49 (certificates of qualification)

(1) In section 49(1)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a)—
  - (i) for “a road transport” substitute “an”;
  - (ii) after “undertaking” insert “established”; and
  - (iii) for “other than the United Kingdom” substitute “that involves the use of goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in the words after paragraph (c)—
  - (i) omit “other”; and
  - (ii) for “road transport undertakings” substitute “such an undertaking established”.

(2) In section 49(2), omit paragraph (b) together with the “and” before it.

(3) In section 49(5), omit the words after paragraph (b).
