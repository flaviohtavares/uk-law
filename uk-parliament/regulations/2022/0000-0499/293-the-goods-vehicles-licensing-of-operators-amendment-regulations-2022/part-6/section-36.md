## 36. Amendment of section 17 (variation of operators’ licences)

(1) In section 17(1)(g), before “that a new place” insert “in the case of a heavy goods vehicle licence,”.

(2) In section 17(4)(c), after “specified in a” insert “heavy goods vehicle”.
