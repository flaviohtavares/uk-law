# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Amendment of section 23 (conditions as to use of operating centres)

(1) In the heading to section 23, after “Conditions” insert “attached to heavy goods vehicle licences”.

(2) In section 23(1), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(3) In section 23(2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “motor vehicles or trailers” substitute “heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b), after “provided” insert “for heavy goods vehicles”.

(4) In section 23(3), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(5) In section 23(4)(a) and (b), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
