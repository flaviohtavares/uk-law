# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Amendment of section 33 (transfer of operating centres)

In section 33—

- (a) for “operators’” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”; and
- (b) for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
