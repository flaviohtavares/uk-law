# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Amendment of section 30 (periods of review for operating centres)

(1) In section 30(1), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.

(2) In section 30(2), in the words before paragraph (a), for “an operator’s” substitute “a heavy goods vehicle”.
