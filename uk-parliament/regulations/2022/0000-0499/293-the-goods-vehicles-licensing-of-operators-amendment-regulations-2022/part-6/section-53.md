# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Amendment of Schedule 1 (meaning of “small goods vehicle”)

(1) In Schedule 1, in paragraph 1A—

- (a) for “permissible laden mass” substitute “maximum laden weight”; and
- (b) for “3.5” substitute “2.5”.

(2) In Schedule 1, after paragraph 1A insert—

> **1B.** A goods vehicle falls within this paragraph if the vehicle, or a vehicle combination including the vehicle—
>
> - (a) has a maximum laden weight not exceeding 3.5 tonnes and
> - (b) is used only for national transport operations.
