# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Amendment of regulation 3 (fees)

In regulation 3, after paragraph (8) insert—

> (9) Any fee prescribed in these Regulations payable in relation to a licence is also payable in relation to a licence under which one or more motor vehicles specified in the licence or otherwise authorised to be used is a light goods vehicle.
