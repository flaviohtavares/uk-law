# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Amendment of Schedule 1 (notice of application for a licence or a variation of a licence)

(1) For the heading to Schedule 1 substitute “Notice of application for heavy goods vehicle licence or variation of heavy goods vehicle licence”.

(2) In Schedule 1, in paragraph 1(f) and (g), for “motor vehicles and trailers” substitute “heavy goods vehicles”.
