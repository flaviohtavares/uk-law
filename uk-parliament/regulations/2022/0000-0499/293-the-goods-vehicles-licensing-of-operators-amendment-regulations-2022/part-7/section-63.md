# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Amendment of regulation 30 (holding companies and subsidiaries)

After regulation 30(7) insert—

> (8) In this regulation, “**road transport undertaking**” means an undertaking that involves the use of a goods vehicle authorised to be used under an operator’s licence.
