# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Amendment of Schedule 3 (classes of vehicles for which a licence is not required)

In Schedule 3, in paragraph 31(b) of Part I, for “permissible laden mass” substitute “maximum laden weight”.
