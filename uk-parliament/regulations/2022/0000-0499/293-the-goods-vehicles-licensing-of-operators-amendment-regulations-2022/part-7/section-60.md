# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Amendment of regulation 15 (considerations relevant to determinations)

(1) In the heading to regulation 15, after “determinations” insert “as to environmental matters”.

(2) In regulation 15(1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraphs (d) and (e), for “motor vehicles or trailers” substitute “heavy goods vehicles”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (h), for “vehicular” substitute “heavy goods vehicle”.
