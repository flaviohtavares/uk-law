# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Commencement

(1) These Regulations, except the regulations referred to in paragraph (2), come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

(2) Regulations 13, 24, 53, 74, 104 and 105 come into force at 11.00 p.m. on 20th May 2022.
