# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Extent

(1) Parts 1 to 4 and regulations 17(b) and 19(2) extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2) Parts 5 to 9, except regulations 17(b) and 19(2), extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

(3) Parts 10 to 14 extend to Northern Ireland.
