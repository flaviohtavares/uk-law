# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of Article 11 (examination and registration of applications)

After Article 11(5) insert—

> **6.** The obligation imposed by paragraph 5 on such an undertaking constitutes a condition attached to a qualifying licence for the purposes of:
>
> - (a) if the undertaking is established in Great Britain, section 26(1)(b) of the 1995 Act; or
> - (b) if the undertaking is established in Northern Ireland, section 23(1)(b) of the 2010 Act.
