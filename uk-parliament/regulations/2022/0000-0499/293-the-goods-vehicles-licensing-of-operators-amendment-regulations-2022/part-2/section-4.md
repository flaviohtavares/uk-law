# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1071/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1071)

Regulation [(EC) No 1071/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1071) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 establishing common rules concerning the conditions to be complied with to pursue the occupation of road transport operator and repealing Council [Directive 96/26/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0026)[^f00004] is amended in accordance with regulations 5 to 11.
