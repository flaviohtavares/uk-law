# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendment of Article 5 (conditions relating to the requirement of establishment)

(1) The existing text of Article 5 is renumbered as paragraph 1.

(2) In Article 5(1), in the words before point (a), after “undertaking” insert “that engages in the occupation of road passenger transport operator”.

(3) After Article 5(1)(c) insert—

> **2.** In order to satisfy the requirement laid down in Article 3(1)(a), an undertaking that engages in the occupation of road haulage operator must satisfy the requirements set out in:
>
> - (a) if the undertaking is established in Great Britain, paragraph A1 of Schedule 3 to the 1995 Act; or
> - (b) if the undertaking is established in Northern Ireland, any regulations made for the purposes of section 12A(2)(a) of the 2010 Act.
