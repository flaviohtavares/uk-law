# The Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 12

## 12. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1072/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1072)

Regulation [(EC) No 1072/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1072) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 on common rules for access to the international road haulage market[^f00007] is amended in accordance with regulations 13 and 14.
