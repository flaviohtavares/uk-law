> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Highway alterations[^c-6b52c7abc2b7f7d602f32cd7db47b61e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may carry out highway alterations comprised within Work Nos. 35, 36 and 37 in the plots numbered 148 to 182 on the land plans.

(2) The highway alterations must be carried out in accordance with plans approved by the highway authority, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld.

(3) If the highway authority fails to notify the undertaker of its decision within 28 days of receiving plans for approval under paragraph (2), the highway authority is deemed to have given approval.
