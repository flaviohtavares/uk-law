# The East Anglia ONE North Offshore Wind Farm Order 2022, section 31

> Text as at 2022-12-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Deemed marine licences under the 2009 Act[^key-61e427387ee9ac9001583a6dec05e266]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 13 (deemed licence under the 2009 Act – generation assets) and 14 (deemed licence under the 2009 Act – offshore transmission assets) are deemed to have been granted to the undertaker under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act for the licensed marine activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 2, of each licence.
