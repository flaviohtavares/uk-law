## 21. Calculation of “retirement index adjustment”[^c-73f9b689f229e56e792b249ebb972f9f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8b5f10b9f69bd91bbb127ac145849273] The retirement index adjustment for an amount of accrued pension is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/319)

- where—
  - “**amount of accrued pension**” means an amount of any description of accrued pension;
  - “**retirement index percentage**” means the retirement index percentage calculated under paragraph (2).

(2) The retirement index percentage is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/319)

where—

- A means the in-service revaluation index[^c-3d96cc67ccfe3ed184ea878524d7a2d7] that applies in relation to this scheme for the leaving year;
- B is the number of complete months in the period between the beginning of the leaving year and the end of the relevant last day; and
- “complete month” includes an incomplete month that consists of at least 16 days.

(3)[^c-07fc1eca6b312e401be919d48b21a748] In paragraph (2), “**in-service revaluation index**”, in relation to a pension scheme, means the percentage increase or decrease by which the pensionable earnings of a person, or a proportion of those earnings accrued as a pension, are revalued whilst the person is in pensionable service in that pension scheme.
