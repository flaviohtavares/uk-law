# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 39

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Amount of full retirement pension[^key-3c348b54e2f7aa9a316a4f3cf6429764]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A full retirement account under regulation 38(4) must specify the amount of full retirement pension.

(2) The amount of full retirement pension is the sum of—

- (a) the amount of the accrued standard earned pension calculated under regulation 24 (“accrued amount”); [^key-0d69c1f09e9c06e36075b5f4270c77f1]...
- (b) the assumed age addition (if any) for the accrued amount ; and[^key-74a190978080895cafd858d096f954fa]
- (c) the retirement index adjustment for the accrued amount.

(3) The full retirement account must specify—

- (a) the early payment reduction (if any);
- (b) the commutation amount (if any); and
- (c) the total allocation amount (if any).
