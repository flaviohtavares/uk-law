# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 116

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Effect of transfers-out[^c-fa8c499b84a3f5f7ce2f684d7646c2aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
