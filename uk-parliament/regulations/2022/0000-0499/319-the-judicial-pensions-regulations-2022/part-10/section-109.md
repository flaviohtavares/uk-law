# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 109

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Application of Part[^c-34d827b84c0a1080e36df4aac3ba4fd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 1 (transfer values) of Part 4ZA of PSA 1993[^f00038] and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 2 (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds) of Part 4ZA of PSA 1993 and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
