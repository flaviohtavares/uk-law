# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 112

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Application for a statement of entitlement[^key-0477ec4820c9495b8e5884e1db387270]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to an active or deferred member of this scheme (P) who requires a transfer value payment to be made in respect of P’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme.

(2) Before requesting the transfer value payment, P must apply for a statement of entitlement by notice to the scheme manager.

(3) P may withdraw the application by notice to the scheme manager at any time before the statement is provided.

(4) P may make a second application by notice to the scheme manager in the period of 12 months beginning with the date of the first application[^f00041].

## Annotations

[^key-0477ec4820c9495b8e5884e1db387270]: Commencement: Reg. 112 in force at 1.4.2022, see [reg. 1(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/319/regulation/1/4)

[^f00041]: Footnote: Regulation 6(3) of [S.I. 1996/1847](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1847) provides that a member may not make a second application within this period unless the rules of the scheme provide otherwise.
