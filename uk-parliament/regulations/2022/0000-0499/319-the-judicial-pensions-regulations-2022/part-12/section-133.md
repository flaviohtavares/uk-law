# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 133

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Financial provision[^c-cd3d53e32e8ac62cf74708bd432a3320]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any pension or lump sum payable under this scheme to or in respect of a person who has held an office specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993[^f00048] is to be charged on, and paid out of, the Consolidated Fund.
