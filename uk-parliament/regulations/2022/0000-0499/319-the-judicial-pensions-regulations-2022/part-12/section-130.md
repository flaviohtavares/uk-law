# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 130

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^key-a02adeb6e54b828198a0a5a1ea194037]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must provide benefit information statements to each active member of this scheme in accordance with—

- (a) section 14 (information about benefits) of the Act; and
- (b) directions given under that section (“Treasury directions”).

(2) The statement must contain the following information about the member’s benefits as at the date of the statement—

- (a) the amount of accrued pension specified in the each of the active member’s accounts as at that date;
- (b) the opening balance for that year and the index adjustment for that opening balance;
- (c) the amount of pension for that year as at that date; and
- (d) any other information required by Treasury directions.
