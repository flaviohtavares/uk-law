# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 129

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Evidence of entitlement[^key-0d4e0661230bd3977095377f14ec1314]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager may require any person who is receiving a pension under this scheme to provide evidence to establish—

- (a) the person’s identity; and
- (b) the person’s continuing entitlement to payment of any amount.

(2) If the person does not provide the required evidence, the scheme manager may withhold the whole or any part of any benefits payable under this scheme in respect of the person.
