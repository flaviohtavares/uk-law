> Text as at 2025-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-2ca44d76bed7a88eb9732d1c9104fcf3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 (reduction of benefit) of WRPA 1999.
