# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 86

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Meaning of “eligible child’s pension”[^c-ae95c94e9d1ce430346c42180da3bcfc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, “eligible child’s pension” means any of the following pensions payable under this Part—

- (a) a child’s earned pension;
- (b) a child’s ill-health pension.
