# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 83

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Annual rate of surviving adult’s pensions payable on death of deferred member[^key-ac522f2c635198fcc7c4b3c1eb82f7c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies on the death of a deferred member of this scheme and applies in respect of each account held by that member.

(2) The annual rate of a dependant’s earned pension is an amount equal to 37.5% of the provisional amount of deferred pension specified in the deferred member’s account.
