# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 80

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Meaning of dependant’s ill-health pension[^key-068f21d818f4125237e1ae6096e46be3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A dependant’s ill-health pension is a pension payable on the death of a member of this scheme if the member was entitled to the immediate payment of an ill-health pension as at the date of the member’s death.
