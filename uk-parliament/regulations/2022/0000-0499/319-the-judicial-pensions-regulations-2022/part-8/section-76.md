# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 76

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Surviving adults[^key-afb1d2f3c3fbccde0ec1fa0037870d3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “surviving adult”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means the member’s surviving spouse, surviving civil partner or surviving cohabiting partner;
- “surviving civil partner”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was in a civil partnership with the member as at the date of the member’s death; and
- “surviving spouse”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was married to the member as at the date of the member’s death.
