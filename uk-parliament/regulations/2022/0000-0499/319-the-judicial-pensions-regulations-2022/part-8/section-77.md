# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 77

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Meaning of “surviving cohabiting partner”[^c-f7ee8791372a05bdf0d6018fcf306b86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person (P) is a surviving cohabiting partner of a deceased member of this scheme if P satisfies the scheme manager that immediately before the member’s death—

- (a) P and the member were cohabiting as partners in an exclusive, committed long-term relationship;
- (b) P and the member were not prevented from entering into a marriage or a civil partnership; and
- (c) either P was financially dependent on the member or P and the member were financially interdependent.
