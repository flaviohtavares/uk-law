# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 99

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Recovery of payments[^key-483e39a358b052ed2e8b5c08d60585ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager may recover a lump sum death benefit paid to any person if the person’s nomination is subsequently found to be invalid.
