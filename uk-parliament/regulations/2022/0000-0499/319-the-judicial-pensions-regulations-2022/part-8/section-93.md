# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 93

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Payment of lump sum death benefit[^key-8f3375fa1b9f19318561d4f715d07ccf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A lump sum death benefit is payable in respect of—

- (a) an active, deferred or pensioner member of this scheme who dies; or
- (b) a pension credit member of this scheme who dies before any benefits attributable (directly or indirectly) to a pension credit become payable.

(2) Payment of a lump sum death benefit must be made within the period of two years beginning with the earlier of—

- (a) the day on which the scheme manager first knew of the member’s death; and
- (b) the day on which the scheme manager could reasonably be expected to have known of the member’s death.

(3) The scheme manager may decide not to pay a lump sum death benefit if it is impracticable to pay it.
