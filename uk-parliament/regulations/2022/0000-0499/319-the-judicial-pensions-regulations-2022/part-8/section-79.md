# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 79

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Meaning of dependant’s earned pension[^c-e20942bb03cd7ad1a129aa00fdaf3eed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A dependant’s earned pension is a pension payable on the death of a member of this scheme if the member was a pensioner member or would have become entitled to a full retirement pension had the member not died.
