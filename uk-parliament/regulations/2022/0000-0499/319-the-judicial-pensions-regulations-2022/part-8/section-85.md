# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 85

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Reduction in pensions in cases of wide age disparity[^key-bd29fe61e52c0ea4c855d01f98accb9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the death of a member of this scheme, a surviving adult’s pension is payable to a person (“the surviving adult”) who is more than 12 years younger than the member.

(2) The annual rate of the surviving adult’s pension is reduced by the lower of—

- (a) 50% of the amount of the annual rate of the pension calculated under regulation 82, 83 or 84 as applicable; and
- (b) 2.5 x (N –12) % of the amount of the annual rate of the pension so calculated,

where N is the number of whole years by which the surviving adult is younger than the member.
