# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 67

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Adjustment of allocated benefit (members who have reached the age of 75)[^c-416144aa650b4ffe0384ffdbe674e59a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary of an allocation election may be adjusted in a manner determined by the scheme manager if the member who made the allocation election dies after reaching the age of 75.

(2) In this regulation, “allocated pension” has the meaning given in regulation 66(1)(b).
