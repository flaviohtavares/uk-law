# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 54

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Meaning of “permanent breakdown in health”[^c-c4a0698f6d04095172e485f4fb8e7edc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter, a member’s breakdown in health is “permanent” if it is likely to continue until the member reaches prospective normal pension age.
