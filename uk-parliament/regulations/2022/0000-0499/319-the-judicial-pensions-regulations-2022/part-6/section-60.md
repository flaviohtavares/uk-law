# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 60

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Option to commute part of pension[^c-4cd9a75d21af079e025f63eefe7aa8b9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member who becomes entitled to the immediate payment of a retirement pension under this scheme may opt under this regulation to exchange part of the pension for a lump sum.

(2) An option under this regulation may only be exercised before the first payment of the pension is made.

(3) Where a member exercises the option mentioned in paragraph (1), for every £1 by which the amount of the member’s annual rate of pension is reduced, the member must be paid a lump sum of £12.

(4) A member may not exchange pension for lump sum under this regulation to the extent that it would result in more than 35.7% of the member’s pre-commuted pension being exchanged for the lump sum.
