# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2022, section 55

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Meaning of “incapacity for employment”[^c-8defed8598643db5308ae80e20d6a15a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter, a member’s permanent breakdown in health involves “incapacity for employment” if, as a result of the breakdown, the member is incapable of discharging the duties of their qualifying judicial office.
