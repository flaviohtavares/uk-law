## 59. Exercising an option under this Chapter[^c-27f9941d089ab7858363dac750142a13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by notice (a “commutation notice”) to the scheme manager in a form required by the scheme manager.
