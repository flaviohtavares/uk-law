# The Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 283 (C. 11)

Family Law, England And Wales

The Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

Made

2nd March 2022

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the power conferred by section 8(8) of the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Commencement) Regulations 2022.

## 2. Commencement

The Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 comes into force on 6th April 2022 in so far as it is not already in force.

Tom Pursglove

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

2nd March 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force on the 6th April 2022 those provisions of the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act [2020 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/11) which are not already into force. Sections 6(2) to (7), 7 to 9 came into force on Royal Assent.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
