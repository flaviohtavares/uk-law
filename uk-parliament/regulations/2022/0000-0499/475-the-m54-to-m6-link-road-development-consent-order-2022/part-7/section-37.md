# The M54 to M6 Link Road Development Consent Order 2022, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Operational land for purposes of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act) of the 1990 Act.
