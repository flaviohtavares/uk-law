# The Bridgwater Tidal Barrier Order 2022, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Arbitration

Unless otherwise provided in this Order, any difference under any provision of this Order is to be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Law Society.
