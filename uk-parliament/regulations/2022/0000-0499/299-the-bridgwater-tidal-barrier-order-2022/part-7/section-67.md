# The Bridgwater Tidal Barrier Order 2022, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Certification of plans etc.

(1) The Agency must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the sections, the land plans, the public rights of way plans and the works plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are respectively true copies of the book of reference, sections and plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**the book of reference**” means the book of reference certified by the Secretary of State as the book of reference for the purposes of this Order.
