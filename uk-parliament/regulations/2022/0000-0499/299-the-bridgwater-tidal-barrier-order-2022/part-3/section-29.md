# The Bridgwater Tidal Barrier Order 2022, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Survey of tidal works

The Secretary of State may at any time order a survey and examination of a tidal work or of the site upon which it is proposed to construct the tidal work, and any expenditure reasonably incurred by the Secretary of State in relation to any such survey and examination is recoverable from the Agency.
