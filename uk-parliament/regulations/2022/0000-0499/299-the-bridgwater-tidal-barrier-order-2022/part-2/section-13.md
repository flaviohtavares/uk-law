# The Bridgwater Tidal Barrier Order 2022, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Access to works

(1) The Agency may, for the purposes of the authorised works form and lay out—

- (a) means of access, or alter or improve existing means of access, in the locations marked with ‘A’ on the Order plans, and
- (b) such other means of access or alter or improve existing means of access at such locations within the Order limits as the Agency reasonably requires for the purposes of the authorised works, as may be first approved by the highway authority, but such approval must not be unreasonably withheld.

(2) If a highway authority which receives an application for approval under paragraph (1)(b) fails to notify the Agency of its decision before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the date on which the application was made, that authority is deemed to have granted approval.
