# The Bridgwater Tidal Barrier Order 2022, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Power to execute street works

The Agency may, for the purposes of and to the extent necessary for the construction of the authorised works—

- (a) enter upon so much of any existing path or street as is within the Order limits and any street abutting the Order limits or which has a junction with such a street, and
- (b) break up or open the path or street, or any sewer, drain, or tunnel under it, or tunnel or bore under the path or street.
