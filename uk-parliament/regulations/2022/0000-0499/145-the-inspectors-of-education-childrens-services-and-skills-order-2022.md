# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 145

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2022

Made

16th February 2022

Coming into force

17th February 2022

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 16th day of February 2022

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2022 and comes into force on 17th February 2022.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
