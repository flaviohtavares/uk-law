# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 179

National Health Service, England

Social Care, England

Public Health, England

The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

Made

23rd February 2022

Coming into force

30th March 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 8, 20(1) to (5A), 35, 86(2) and (4), 87(1) and (2) and 161(3) and (4) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001].

In accordance with section 20(8) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

A draft of these Regulations was laid before Parliament in accordance with section 162(3) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 and was approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 30th March 2022.

## 2. Extent and application

(1) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

(2) These Regulations apply to England only.

## 3. Amendment of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014

In regulation 1(6) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014[^f00002] for “31st March 2022” substitute “31st March 2025”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Maria Caulfield

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Care

23rd February 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014 (“**the 2014 Regulations**”).

These Regulations amend the expiry date of the 2014 Regulations so that they cease to have effect after 31st March 2025.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
