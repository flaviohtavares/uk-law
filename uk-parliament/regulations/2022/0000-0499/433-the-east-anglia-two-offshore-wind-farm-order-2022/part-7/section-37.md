# The East Anglia TWO Offshore Wind Farm Order 2022, section 37

> Text as at 2022-12-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Arbitration[^c-7f59e6413bdc49d9b6966c8c4cf66554]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to article 40 (saving provision for Trinity House), any dispute or difference arising out of or in connection with any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for, must be referred to and settled in arbitration in accordance with the rules at Schedule 15 (arbitration rules) to this Order by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties, within 14 days of receipt of the notice of arbitration, or if the parties fail to agree within the time period stipulated, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.

(2) Any matter for which the consent or approval of the Secretary of State or the Marine Management Organisation is required under any provision of this Order shall not be subject to arbitration.
