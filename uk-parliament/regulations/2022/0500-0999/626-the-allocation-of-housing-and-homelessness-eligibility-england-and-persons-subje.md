# The Allocation of Housing and Homelessness (Eligibility) (England) and Persons Subject to Immigration Control (Housing Authority Accommodation and Homelessness) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 160ZA(2), 172(4), 185(2) and 215(2) of the Housing Act 1996[^f00001] and sections 118(1)(a), 119(1) and 166(3) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Allocation of Housing and Homelessness (Eligibility) (England) and Persons Subject to Immigration Control (Housing Authority Accommodation and Homelessness) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 10 June 2022.

(2) The amendment made by these Regulations has the same extent as the provision amended.

## 2. Amendment of the Allocation of Housing and Homelessness (Eligibility) (England) and Persons Subject to Immigration Control (Housing Authority Accommodation and Homelessness) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022

In regulation 1(1) of the Allocation of Housing and Homelessness (Eligibility) (England) and Persons Subject to Immigration Control (Housing Authority Accommodation and Homelessness) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022[^f00003] for “22nd” substitute “10”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Allocation of Housing and Homelessness (Eligibility) (England) and Person Subject to Immigration Control (Housing Authority Accommodation and Homelessness) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/601](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/601)). Their purpose is to correct the coming into force date of those Regulations, so that they come into force on 10 June 2022.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because the impact on business is not likely to be significant.
