# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (England) (No. 2) Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

30th June 2022

Laid before Parliament

6th July 2022

Coming into force

27th July 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that the conditions in section 1(1)[^f00002] of that Act are satisfied and, in accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, has consulted with such persons and bodies of persons as appear requisite to the Secretary of State.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (England) (No. 2) Order 2022 and comes into force on 27th July 2022.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act [1975 (c.52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52) (“**the Act**”), where the Secretary of State is of the opinion that a sports ground has accommodation for more than the specified number of spectators, the Secretary of State may by order designate that sports ground as one requiring a safety certificate under the Act.

This Order amends the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661)) (“**the 2015 Order**”) so as to designate certain sports grounds under articles 2(1) and 2(2) of the 2015 Order as sports grounds for which a safety certificate is required under the Act.

This Order newly designates Blundell Park, occupied by Grimsby Town Football Club, and VBS Community Stadium, occupied by Sutton United Football Club. This Order also replaces the existing designations in respect of Boundary Park, occupied by Oldham Athletic Association Football Club, and Edgeley Park, occupied by Stockport County Football Club. This Order also removes the existing designation in respect of Glanford Park, occupied by Scunthorpe United Football Club.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [1975 c. 52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52); section 1(1) was amended by Schedule 2 to the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act [1987 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/27).

[^f00002]: Footnote: By section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, the Secretary of State may by order designate a sports ground as requiring a safety certificate under that Act any sports ground which, in the Secretary of State’s opinion, has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators. The Safety of Sports Grounds (Accommodation of Spectators) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/499](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/499)) substituted the number 5,000 for the number of spectators specified in section 1(1) of that Act in respect of sports grounds in England and Wales at which association football matches are played and which are occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited.

[^f00003]: Footnote: [S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661), to which there are amendments not relevant to this Order. Note that [S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661) was amended by the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) Order 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1116](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1116)), which designated Meadow Lane Stadium, occupied by Notts County Football Club.
