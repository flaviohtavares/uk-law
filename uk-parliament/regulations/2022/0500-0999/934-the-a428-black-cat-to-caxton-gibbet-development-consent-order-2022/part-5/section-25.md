# The A428 Black Cat to Caxton Gibbet Development Consent Order 2022, section 25

> Text as at 2023-01-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-e1d9c439918b5f140c61993ce27b9960]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00064] are incorporated into this Order subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
