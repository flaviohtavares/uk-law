# The A428 Black Cat to Caxton Gibbet Development Consent Order 2022, section 24

> Text as at 2023-01-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-5861b18067ef71e8e239a9a5cd472280]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (3) of article 27[^key-f2587e8a84c3a7b68dd5a7086ecca795] (compulsory acquisition of rights and imposition of restrictive covenants) and article 53 (crown rights).
