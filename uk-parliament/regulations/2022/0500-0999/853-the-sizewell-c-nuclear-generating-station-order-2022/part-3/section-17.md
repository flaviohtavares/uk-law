# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Status of public rights of way created or improved

With effect from the date on which the highway authority has confirmed that the public rights of way specified in column (2) of Schedule 11 (Status of public rights of way created or improved) have been created or improved to the standard specified in a public rights of way implementation plan, the public rights of way in question will be deemed to have the status specified in column (3) of that Schedule.
