# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Use of beach landing facility and marine bulk import facility

The undertaker may only use the temporary marine bulk import facility (Work No. 1A(aa)) for the purposes of, or in connection with, the construction of the authorised development and may only use the permanent beach landing facility for the purposes of, or in connection with the construction, operation, maintenance and decommissioning of the authorised development.
