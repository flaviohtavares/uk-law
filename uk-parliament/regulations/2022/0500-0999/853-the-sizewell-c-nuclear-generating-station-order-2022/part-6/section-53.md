# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Application of Pilotage Act 1987

The undertaker is a competent harbour authority in respect of the harbour for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00037].
