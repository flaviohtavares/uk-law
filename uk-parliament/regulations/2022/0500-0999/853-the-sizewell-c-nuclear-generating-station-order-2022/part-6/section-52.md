# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Agreements entered into by the undertaker

Any agreement or undertaking entered into by the undertaker before the coming into force of this Order in connection with the proposed exercise of its function as harbour authority must be binding upon the harbour authority notwithstanding that it was entered into by the undertaker before it was established as a harbour authority by article 51 (harbour authority).
