# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Master’s responsibility in relation to directions

The giving of a general direction under article 65 (power to make general direction to vessels) or a special direction under article 67 (power to make special directions to vessels) does not diminish or in any other way affect the responsibility of the master of the vessel to which the direction is given in relation to that vessel, persons on board, its cargo or any other person or property.
