# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Harbour authority

(1) The undertaker is the harbour authority in respect of the harbour.

(2) Without prejudice to any provision of the 1847 Act as incorporated in this Order by article 49 (incorporation of the Harbours, Docks, and Piers Clauses Act 1847), the area within which the harbour authority may exercise its functions under this Order must be the harbour.
