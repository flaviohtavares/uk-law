# The Sizewell C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2022, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Application, exclusion and modification of legislative provisions

Schedule 25 (Miscellaneous controls) to this Order, which makes provision applying, modifying and excluding statutory provisions which relate to matters for which provision may be made by this Order, has effect.
