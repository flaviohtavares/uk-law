# The Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 931 (C. 70)

Telecommunications

The Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

Made

1st September 2022

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(2) of the Telecommunications Security Act 2021[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 (Commencement) Regulations 2022.

(2) In these Regulations “**the Act**” means the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1 October 2022

The following provisions of the Act, so far as not already in force by virtue of section 28(1) of the Act, come into force on 1 October 2022–

- (a) Sections 1 to 3 (so far as not already in force by virtue of section 28(1) of the Act);
- (b) Sections 4 to 13;
- (c) Section 24 (so far as not already in force by virtue of section 28(1) of the Act); and
- (d) Section 25(2).

Matt Warman

Minister of State

Department of Digital, Culture, Media and Sport

1st September 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force those provisions of the Telecommunications (Security) Act [2021 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/31) which are not already in force. Certain provisions were brought into force on Royal Assent by virtue of section 28(1) of the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021.
