# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Amendment of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014

(1) The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014[^f00109] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “health service body”—

- (a) after paragraph (b) omit “or”;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > , or
  >
  > - (d) NHS England;

  .
