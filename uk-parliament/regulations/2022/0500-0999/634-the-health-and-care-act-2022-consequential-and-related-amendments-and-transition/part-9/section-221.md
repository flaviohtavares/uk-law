# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 221

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 221. Revocations

The following are revoked—

- (a) bythe National Health Service (Clinical Commissioning Groups) Regulations 2012[^f00210];
- (b) bythe National Health Service (Clinical Commissioning Groups—Disapplication of Responsibility) Regulations 2013[^f00211];
- (c) the Delayed Discharges (Continuing Care) Directions 2013[^f00212], which came into force on 11th November 2013.
