# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 192

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 192. Amendment of the Eastern and Coastal Kent Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010

(1) The Eastern and Coastal Kent Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010[^f00177] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “community health services”—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”.
