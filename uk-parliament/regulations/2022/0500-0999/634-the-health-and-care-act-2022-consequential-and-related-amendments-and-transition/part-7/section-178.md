# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 178

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 178. Amendment of the Shrewsbury and Telford Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Princess Royal Hospital National Health Service Trust and the Royal Shrewsbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2003

(1) The Shrewsbury and Telford Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Princess Royal Hospital National Health Service Trust and the Royal Shrewsbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2003[^f00163] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “community health services”—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”.
