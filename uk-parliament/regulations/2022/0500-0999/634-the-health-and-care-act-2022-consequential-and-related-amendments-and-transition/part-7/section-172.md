# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 172

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 172. Amendment of the Bradford District Care Trust (Establishment) and the Bradford Community Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002

(1) The Bradford District Care Trust (Establishment) and the Bradford Community Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00157] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “community health services”—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”.
