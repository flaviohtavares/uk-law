# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 189

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 189. Amendment of the Cambridgeshire Community Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010

(1) The Cambridgeshire Community Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010[^f00174] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “community health services”—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “section 3(1)(d) or (e)” substitute “section 3(1)(g) or (i)”.
