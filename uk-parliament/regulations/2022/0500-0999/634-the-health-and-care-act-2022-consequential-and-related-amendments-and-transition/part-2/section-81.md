# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Amendment of the Children’s Homes (England) Regulations 2015

(1) The Children’s Homes (England) Regulations 2015[^f00072] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 40, in paragraph 1(e) for the words from “clinical” to “14D” substitute “integrated care board (established under Chapter A3 of Part 2”.
