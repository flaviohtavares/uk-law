# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Amendment of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 (Welsh language text)

(1) The Welsh language text of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 (Rheoliadau Cynllunio Gofal, Lleoli ac Adolygu Achosion (Cymru) 2015) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “darparwr gofal iechyd” for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig”.

(3) In regulation 14, in paragraph (2)(f) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol” substitute “bwrdd gofal integredig”.
