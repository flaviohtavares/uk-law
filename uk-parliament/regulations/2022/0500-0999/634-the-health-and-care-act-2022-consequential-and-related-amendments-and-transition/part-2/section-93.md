# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Amendment of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018[^f00081] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 44, in paragraph (4)(j) for “a clinical commissioning group established under section 11 of that Act” substitute “an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of that Act”.
