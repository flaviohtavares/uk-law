## 47. Amendment of the Community Health Councils (Constitution, Membership and Procedures) (Wales) Regulations 2010 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Community Health Councils (Constitution, Membership and Procedures) (Wales) Regulations 2010[^f00040] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 12, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (aa);
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) omit “, a clinical commissioning group”.
