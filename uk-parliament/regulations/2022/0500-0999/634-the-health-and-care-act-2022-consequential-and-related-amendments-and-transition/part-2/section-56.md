# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Amendment of the Community Right to Challenge (Expressions of Interest and Excluded Services) (England) Regulations 2012

(1) The Community Right to Challenge (Expressions of Interest and Excluded Services) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00047] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 2, in paragraph 1—

- (a) omit the definition of “clinical commissioning group”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**integrated care board**” means an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

  .
