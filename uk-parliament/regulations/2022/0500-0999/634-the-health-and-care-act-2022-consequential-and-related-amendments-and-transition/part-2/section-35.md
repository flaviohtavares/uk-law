# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Amendment of the Mental Health (Hospital, Guardianship, Community Treatment and Consent to Treatment) (Wales) Regulations 2008 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Mental Health (Hospital, Guardianship, Community Treatment and Consent to Treatment) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00032] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 23, in paragraph (6)(b)—

- (a) for “clinical commissioning group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) for “trust, board, group or authority” substitute “body”.

(3) In regulation 25, in paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “clinical commissioning group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) for “trust, board, group or authority” substitute “body”.

(4) In regulation 26, in paragraph (6)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b) for “clinical commissioning group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) in the words after sub-paragraph (b) for “trust, board, group or authority” substitute “body”.
