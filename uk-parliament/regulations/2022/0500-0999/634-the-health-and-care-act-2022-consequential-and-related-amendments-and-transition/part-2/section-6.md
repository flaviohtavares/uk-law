# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendment of the Residential Accommodation (Determination of District Health Authority) Regulations 1992

(1) The Residential Accommodation (Determination of District Health Authority) Regulations 1992[^f00007] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “clinical commissioning group’s” substitute “integrated care board’s”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “clinical commissioning group’s” substitute “integrated care board’s”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “clinical commissioning group’s” substitute “integrated care board’s”.
