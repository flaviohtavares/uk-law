# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2012

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00046] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4 for paragraph (1)(g) substitute—

> - (g) each integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

.
