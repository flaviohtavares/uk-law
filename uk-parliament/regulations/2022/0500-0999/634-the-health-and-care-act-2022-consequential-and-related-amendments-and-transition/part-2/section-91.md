## 91. Amendment of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017 (Welsh language text)

(1) The Welsh language text of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017 (Rheoliadau Addysg (Cymorth i Fyfyrwyr) (Cymru) 2017) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 23, in paragraph (7)(e)(v) for “Grŵp Comisiynu Clinigol a sefydlwyd o dan adran 1I o’r Ddeddf honno” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig a sefydlwyd o dan Bennod A3 o Ran 2 o’r Ddeddf honno”.
