# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendment of the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990

(1) The National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 11(1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (e) omit “a clinical commissioning group or”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (ea).
