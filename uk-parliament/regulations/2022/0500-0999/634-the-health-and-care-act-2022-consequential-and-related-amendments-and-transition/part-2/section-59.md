# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Amendment of the Local Authority (Public Health, Health and Wellbeing Boards and Health Scrutiny) Regulations 2013

(1) The Local Authority (Public Health, Health and Wellbeing Boards and Health Scrutiny) Regulations 2013[^f00052] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 23, in paragraph (14), in the definition of “the responsible commissioner” for “groups” substitute “integrated care boards”.

(3) In regulation 24, in paragraph (a) for “a group” substitute “an integrated care board”.
