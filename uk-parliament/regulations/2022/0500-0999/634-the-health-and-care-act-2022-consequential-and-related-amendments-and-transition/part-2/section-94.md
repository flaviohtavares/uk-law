## 94. Amendment of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018 (Welsh language text)

(1) The Welsh language text of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018 (Rheoliadau Addysg (Cymorth i Fyfyrwyr) (Cymru) 2018) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 44, in paragraph (4)(j) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol a sefydlwyd o dan adran 11 o’r Ddeddf honno” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig a sefydlwyd o dan Bennod A3 o Ran 2 o’r Ddeddf honno”.
