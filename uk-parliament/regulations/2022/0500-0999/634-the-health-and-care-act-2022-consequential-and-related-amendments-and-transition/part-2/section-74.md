# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Amendment of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Default Scheme) (Wales) Regulations 2013 (Welsh language text)

(1) The Welsh language text of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Default Scheme) (Wales) Regulations 2013 (Rheoliadau Cynlluniau Gostyngiadau’r Dreth Gyngor (Cynllun Diofyn) (Cymru) 2013) is amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule—

- (a) in paragraph 41 (earnings of self-employed earners: pensioners), in sub-paragraph (2)(d)(iv) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol a sefydlwyd o dan adran 14D” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig a sefydlwyd o dan Bennod A3 o Ran 2”;
- (b) in Schedule 3 (applicable amounts: persons who are not pensioners), in paragraph 10, in sub-paragraph (1)(a)(vi) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig”.
