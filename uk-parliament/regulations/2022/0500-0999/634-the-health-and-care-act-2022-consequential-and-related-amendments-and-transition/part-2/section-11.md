# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of the Employment Protection (Continuity of Employment of National Health Service Employees) (Modification) Order 1996

(1) The Employment Protection (Continuity of Employment of National Health Service Employees) (Modification) Order 1996[^f00012] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1, in paragraph (2)(b)—

- (a) after paragraph (xi) omit “and”;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > ; and
  >
  > - (xiii) an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

  .
