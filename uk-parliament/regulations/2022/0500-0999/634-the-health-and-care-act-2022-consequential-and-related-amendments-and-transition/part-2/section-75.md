# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Amendment of the Local Audit (Auditor Resignation and Removal) Regulations 2014

(1) The Local Audit (Auditor Resignation and Removal) Regulations 2014[^f00066] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (3)(b)—

- (a) in paragraph (i) for “group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) in paragraph (ii) for “group”, in both places it occurs, substitute “integrated care board”.
