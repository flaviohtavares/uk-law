# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Amendment of the National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015

(1) The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015[^f00077] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3—

- (a) omit the definition of “CCG”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**integrated care board**” means an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the Act;

  .

(3) Omit regulation 14.
