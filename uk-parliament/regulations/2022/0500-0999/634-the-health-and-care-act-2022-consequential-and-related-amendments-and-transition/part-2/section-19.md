# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Amendment of the Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004

(1) The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004[^f00020] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 2—

- (a) in paragraph 3 for sub-paragraph (5)(b)(iii)(cc) substitute—

  > - (cc) an integrated care board.

  .
- (b) in paragraph 6, in sub-paragraph (6)(a)(ii) for “the governing body of a clinical commissioning group” substitute “an integrated care board”.
