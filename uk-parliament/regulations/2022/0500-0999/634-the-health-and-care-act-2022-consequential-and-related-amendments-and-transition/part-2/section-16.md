# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Amendment of the Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001

(1) The Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001[^f00017] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “clinical commissioning group”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**integrated care board**” means an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

  .
