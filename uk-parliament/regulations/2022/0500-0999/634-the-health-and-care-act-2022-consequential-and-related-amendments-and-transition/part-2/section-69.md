# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Amendment of the National Health Service (Licensing and Pricing) Regulations 2013

(1) The National Health Service (Licensing and Pricing) Regulations 2013[^f00062] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in the words before sub-paragraph (a) for “the group” substitute “the integrated care board”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (a) for “group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) for “a group” substitute “an integrated care board”;
- (c) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) for “a group” substitute “an integrated care board”;
  - (ii) for “the group” substitute “the integrated care board”;
- (d) in paragraph (4), for the definition of “revenue resource use”, substitute—

  > - “**revenue resource use**” means the local revenue resource use of an integrated care board which is attributable to matters specified in a direction given to the integrated care board under section 223N(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006

  .
