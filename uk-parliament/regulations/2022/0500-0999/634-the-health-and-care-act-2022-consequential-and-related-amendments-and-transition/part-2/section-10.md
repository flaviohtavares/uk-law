# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996

(1) The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00011] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 7, in paragraph 28 for sub-paragraph (da) substitute—

> - (da) an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

.
