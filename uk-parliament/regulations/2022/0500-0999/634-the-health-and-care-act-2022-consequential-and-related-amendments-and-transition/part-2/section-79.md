# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Amendment of the Community Care (Provision of Residential Accommodation Outwith Scotland) (Scotland) Regulations 2015

(1) The Community Care (Provision of Residential Accommodation Outwith Scotland) (Scotland) Regulations 2015[^f00070] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “health authority”, in sub-paragraph (b) for “clinical commissioning group established in accordance with Chapter A2 of Part 2” substitute “integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2”.
