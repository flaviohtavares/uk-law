# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013

(1) The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013[^f00059] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 37 for paragraph (7)(b)(v) substitute—

> - (v) an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

.
