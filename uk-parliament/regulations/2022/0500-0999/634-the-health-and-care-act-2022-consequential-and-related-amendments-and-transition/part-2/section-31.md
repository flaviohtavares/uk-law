# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Amendment of the Placement of Children (Wales) Regulations 2007 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Placement of Children (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00029] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “health care provider” for “a clinical commissioning group” substitute “an integrated care board”.
