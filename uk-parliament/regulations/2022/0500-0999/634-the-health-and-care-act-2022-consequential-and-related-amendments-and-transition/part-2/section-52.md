# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 52

## 52. Amendment of the National Health Service (Concerns, Complaints and Redress Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2011 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the National Health Service (Concerns, Complaints and Redress Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2011[^f00044] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 34 for paragraph (1)(ca) substitute—

> - (ca) an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006;

.
