# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Amendment of the Care and Support (Provision of Health Services) (Wales) Regulations 2015 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Care and Support (Provision of Health Services) (Wales) Regulations 2015[^f00078] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(c) for “clinical commissioning group” substitute “integrated care board”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) for “clinical commissioning group”, in both places it occurs, substitute “integrated care board”.
