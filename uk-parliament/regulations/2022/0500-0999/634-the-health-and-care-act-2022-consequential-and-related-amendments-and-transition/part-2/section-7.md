# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment of the Redundancy Payments (National Health Service) (Modification) Order 1993

(1) The Redundancy Payments (National Health Service) (Modification) Order 1993[^f00008] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1 after paragraph 2B insert—

> **2BA.** an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2 of the National Health Service Act 2006.

.
