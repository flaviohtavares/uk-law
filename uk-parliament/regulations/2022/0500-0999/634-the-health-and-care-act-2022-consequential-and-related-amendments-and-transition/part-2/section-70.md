# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Amendment of the National Health Service (Licence Exemptions, etc) Regulations 2013

(1) The National Health Service (Licence Exemptions, etc) Regulations 2013[^f00063] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 9, in paragraph (2) for “or a clinical commissioning group” substitute “(whether or not on the request of an integrated care board)”.
