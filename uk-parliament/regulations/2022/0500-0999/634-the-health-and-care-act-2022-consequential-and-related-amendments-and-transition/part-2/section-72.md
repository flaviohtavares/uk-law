# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Amendment of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013 (Welsh language text)

(1) The Welsh language text of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013 (Rheoliadau Cynlluniau Gostyngiadau’r Dreth Gyngor a Gofynion Rhagnodedig (Cymru) 2013) is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in paragraph 15, in sub-paragraph (2)(d)(iv) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol a sefydlwyd o dan adran 14D” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig a sefydlwyd o dan Bennod A3 o Ran 2”;
- (b) in paragraph 20, in sub-paragraph (1)(a)(vi) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig”.

(3) In Schedule 7, in paragraph 10, in sub-paragraph (1)(a)(vi) for “grŵp comisiynu clinigol” substitute “fwrdd gofal integredig”.
