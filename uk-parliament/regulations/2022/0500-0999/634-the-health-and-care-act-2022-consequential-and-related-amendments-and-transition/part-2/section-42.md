# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Amendment of the Local Health Boards (Directed Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2009 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Local Health Boards (Directed Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00036] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (2)(a), in the words after paragraph (ii) for “a clinical commissioning group” substitute “an integrated care board”.
