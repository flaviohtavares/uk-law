# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Amendment of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013 (English language text)

(1) The English language text of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013[^f00064] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in paragraph 15, in sub-paragraph (2)(d)(iv) for “a clinical commissioning group established under section 14D” substitute “an integrated care board established under Chapter A3 of Part 2”;
- (b) in paragraph 20, in sub-paragraph (1)(a)(vi) for “a clinical commissioning group” substitute “an integrated care board”.

(3) In Schedule 7, in paragraph 10, in sub-paragraph (1)(a)(vi) for “a clinical commissioning group” substitute “an integrated care board”.
