# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Amendment of the Local Authorities (Public Health Functions and Entry to Premises by Local Healthwatch Representatives) Regulations 2013

(1) The Local Authorities (Public Health Functions and Entry to Premises by Local Healthwatch Representatives) Regulations 2013[^f00056] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 7, in paragraph (6)(d) for the words from “clinical” to “inequalities)” substitute “integrated care board under section 14Z35 (duty to reduce inequalities)”.
