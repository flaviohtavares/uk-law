# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Amendment of the National Health Service (Licence Exemptions, etc) Regulations 2013

(1) The National Health Service (Licence Exemptions, etc) Regulations 2013[^f00096] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1) omit “Monitor:”.

(3) In regulation 8—

- (a) in paragraph (3) for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph (4)(b) for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”.

(4) In regulation 9—

- (a) in paragraph (2) omit “Monitor, on the request of”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a) for the words from “the Board” to the end substitute—

    > —
    >
    > - (i) NHS England that it will impose a condition under section 97(1)(i), (j) or (k) of the 2012 Act in relation to the services benefiting from the exemption, or
    > - (ii) an integrated care board that it has requested NHS England to impose such a condition,

    ;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b) for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”;
- (c) in paragraph (4) for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”.
