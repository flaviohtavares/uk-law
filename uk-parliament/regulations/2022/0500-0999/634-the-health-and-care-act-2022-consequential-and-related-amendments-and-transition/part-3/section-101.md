# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Amendment of the National Health Service Litigation Authority Regulations 1995

(1) The National Health Service Litigation Authority Regulations 1995[^f00086], as they apply to England, are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 7, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (ea) insert “or”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (f) omit “or”;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (g).
