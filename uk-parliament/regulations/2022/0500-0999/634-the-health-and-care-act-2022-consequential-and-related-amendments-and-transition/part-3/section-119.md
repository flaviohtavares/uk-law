# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Amendment of the Care Quality Commission (Membership) Regulations 2015

(1) The Care Quality Commission (Membership) Regulations 2015[^f00104] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2), in the definition of “NHS Body” at the end insert—

> - (s) a clinical commissioning group which, immediately before 1st July 2022, was established in accordance with Chapter A2 of Part 2 of the NHS Act 2006;

.
