# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Amendment of the National Health Service (Approval of Licensing Criteria) Order 2013

(1) The National Health Service (Approval of Licensing Criteria) Order 2013[^f00097] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2 for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In the Schedule—

- (a) in the words below the heading “Licensing criteria” for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, in sub-paragraph (2)(b) for “Monitor”, in both places it occurs, substitute “NHS England”.
