# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 116

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 116. Amendment of the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order 2014

(1) The Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order 2014[^f00100] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule—

- (a) omit the entry relating to Monitor;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | NHS England | Matters relating to the provision of services pursuant to a contract, agreement or arrangement under Part 4 (which relates to primary medical services), Part 5 (which relates<br>to primary dental services), Part 6 (which relates to primary ophthalmic services) or Part 7 (which relates to pharmaceutical services and local pharmaceutical services) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00101].<br>Matters relating to NHS England’s regulatory functions as defined in section 13SB(2) of the National Health Service Act 2006.<br>Matters relating to NHS England’s functions of oversight and support of NHS Trusts in section 27A of the National Health Service Act 2006. |

  .
