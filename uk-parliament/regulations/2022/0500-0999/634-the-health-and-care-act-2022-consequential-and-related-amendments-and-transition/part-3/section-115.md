## 115. Amendment of the Competition Act 1998 (Concurrency) Regulations 2014

(1) The Competition Act 1998 (Concurrency) Regulations 2014[^f00099] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 omit paragraph (2).

(3) In regulation 5—

- (a) in paragraph (4)(b) omit “(subject to paragraph (5))”;

(4) In regulation 8 in paragraph (1)—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (a) omit “and”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (b).
