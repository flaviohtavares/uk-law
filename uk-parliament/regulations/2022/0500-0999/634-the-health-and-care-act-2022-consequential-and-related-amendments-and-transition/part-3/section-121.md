# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 121

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 121. Amendment of the Equality Act 2010 (Specific Duties and Public Authorities) Regulations 2017

(1) The Equality Act 2010 (Specific Duties and Public Authorities) Regulations 2017[^f00106] are amended as follows.

(2) Omit regulation 7.

(3) In Schedule 2, in the list of public authorities, under the heading “Health, social care and social security” omit “Monitor.”.
