# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 105

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 105. Amendment of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 9, Consequential Amendments and Transitory, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2009

(1) The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 9, Consequential Amendments and Transitory, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2009[^f00090] is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 3, in paragraph 8—

- (a) in the heading for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1)(b) for “Monitor” substitute “NHS England”.
