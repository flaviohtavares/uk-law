# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Amendment of the Legislative and Regulatory Reform (Regulatory Functions) Order 2007

(1) The Legislative and Regulatory Reform (Regulatory Functions) Order 2007[^f00089] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule, in Part 1, in the list—

- (a) omit the entry relating to Monitor;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > NHS England under or by virtue of Chapters 3 and 5 of Part 3 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012

  .
