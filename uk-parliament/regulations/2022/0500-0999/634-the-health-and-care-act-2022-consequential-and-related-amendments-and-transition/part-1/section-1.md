# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st July 2022.
