# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Consequential and Related Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2022, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Extent

(1) This Part extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2) Any amendment or revocation made by the provisions in Parts 2 to 9 has the same extent as the provision amended or revoked.
