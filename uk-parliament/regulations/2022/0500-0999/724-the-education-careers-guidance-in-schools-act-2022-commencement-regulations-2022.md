# The Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 724 (C. 44)

Education, England And Wales

The Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2022

Made

29th June 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2022.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 1 and 2 of the Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022 come into force on 1st September 2022.

Alex Burghart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

29th June 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force, on the 1st September 2022, the substantive provisions of the Education (Careers Guidance in Schools) Act 2022. A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
