# The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) (Amendment) Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 786

Education, England

The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) (Amendment) Order 2022

Made

12th July 2022

Laid before Parliament

14th July 2022

Coming into force -

1st September 2022

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 123(4)(a) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) (Amendment) Order 2022 and comes into force on 1st September 2022.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019

(1) The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(2) (incentive payments to school teachers)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b) omit “and”; and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (c) insert—

  > and
  >
  > - (d) the Levelling Up Premium,

  .

Barran

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

12th July 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1133)). It adds the Levelling Up Premium to the list of schemes payment under which is not to be treated as remuneration for the purposes of section 122(1) of the Education Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations. The instrument has minimal impact on the public sector.
