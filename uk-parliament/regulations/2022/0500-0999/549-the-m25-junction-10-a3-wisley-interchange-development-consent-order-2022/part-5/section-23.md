# The M25 Junction 10/A3 Wisley Interchange Development Consent Order 2022, section 23

> Text as at 2026-07-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-d0cd7c6cf0dc88592bcc7adaa4f503d8]

*Extent: E+W*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00066] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
