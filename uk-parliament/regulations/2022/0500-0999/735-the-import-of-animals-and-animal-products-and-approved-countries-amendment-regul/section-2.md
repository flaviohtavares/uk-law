# The Import of Animals and Animal Products and Approved Countries (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999)

(1) Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 5 (BSE classification)—

- (a) in paragraph 6, for “inclusion in the list of countries or regions approved” substitute “approval”;
- (b) in paragraph 7—
  - (i) omit “to change”;
  - (ii) omit “from a date specified in the decision”.
- (c) after paragraph 7 insert—

  > **7A.** A decision under paragraph 7 must include the date from which the BSE classification for the country or region is to apply.
  >
  > **7B.** A decision under paragraph 7 must be set out in a document published by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this Article.

  .

(3) In Annex 9 (importation)—

- (a) in Chapter B—
  - (i) in Section A, in point (a), for “Commission [Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
  - (ii) in Section B, in point (a), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
  - (iii) in Section C, in point (a), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
- (b) in Chapter C—
  - (i) in Section B, in points (a), (d), (e), (f), (g) and (h), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
  - (ii) in Section C, in paragraphs 1(a) and 5(a), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
  - (iii) in Section D, in paragraph 5(a), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”;
- (c) in Chapter D in Section B, in paragraphs 1(a)(ii) and (iii), 1(b), and 2(a) and (b), for “[Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)” substitute “this Regulation”.
