# The Import of Animals and Animal Products and Approved Countries (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of Commission [Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453)

(1) Commission [Decision 2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0453) establishing the BSE status of Member States or third countries or regions thereof according to their BSE risk[^f00012] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 (BSE status of countries or regions)—

- (a) omit paragraph 1;
- (b) in paragraph 2, for “However, the” substitute “The”.

(3) Omit the Annex (list of countries or regions).
