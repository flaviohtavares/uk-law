# The Import of Animals and Animal Products and Approved Countries (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Import of Animals and Animal Products and Approved Countries (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) An amendment made by these Regulations has the same extent as the provision being amended.
