# The A47 Blofield to North Burlingham Development Consent Order 2022, section 6

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-e4b4528b2e97ca23cabbbe04ac0522cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
