# The A47 Blofield to North Burlingham Development Consent Order 2022, section 25

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-4e877ec7d72a885f633ad75e379c8c9b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00058] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
