# The A47 Blofield to North Burlingham Development Consent Order 2022, section 3

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Disapplication of legislative provisions[^key-fb1b981b9c11a769296e9dbc1ec1a098]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of the Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017[^f00028] insofar as they relate to temporary possession of land under articles 34 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development) and 35 (temporary use of land for maintaining the authorised development) of this Order do not apply in relation to the construction of works carried out for the purpose of, or in connection with, the construction or maintenance of the authorised project.
