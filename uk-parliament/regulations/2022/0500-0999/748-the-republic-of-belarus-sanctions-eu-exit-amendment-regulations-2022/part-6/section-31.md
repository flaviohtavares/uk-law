## 31. Exception for acts done for purposes of national security or prevention of serious crime

In regulation 31(1) (exception for acts done for purposes of national security or prevention of serious crime), for “or 5A (Aircraft)”, substitute “, 5A (Aircraft) or 5B (Ships)”.
