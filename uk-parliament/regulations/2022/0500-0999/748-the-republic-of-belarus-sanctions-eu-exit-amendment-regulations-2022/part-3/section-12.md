# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Loans and credit arrangements

In regulation 15B (loans and credit arrangements), for paragraph (5) substitute—

> (5) In this regulation—
>
> - “**category A loan**” means a loan or credit which—
>   - (a) has a maturity exceeding 90 days,
>   - (b) is made or granted to a relevant person, and
>   - (c) is first made or granted—
>     - (aa) after the date on which the Amendment Regulations 2021 come into force, and
>     - (bb) before the Amendment Regulations 2022 come into force;
> - “**category B loan**” means a loan or credit which—
>   - (a) is made or granted to a relevant person,
>   - (b) is first made or granted after the Amendment Regulations 2022 come into force;
> - “**category C loan**” means a loan or credit which—
>   - (a) has a maturity exceeding 30 days,
>   - (b) is made or granted—
>     - (i) to a person, other than an individual, which is connected with Belarus and which is not—
>       - (aa) a person which on the date on which the Amendment Regulations 2022 come into force is domiciled in a country other than Belarus,
>       - (bb) a person which is majority owned by a person falling within paragraph (aa), or
>     - (ii) to a person, other than an individual, which is majority owned by a person within sub-paragraph (i),
>   - (c) is first made or granted after the Amendment Regulations 2022 come into force, and
>   - (d) is not a category B loan;
> - “**relevant loan**” means a category A loan, a category B loan or a category C loan.
