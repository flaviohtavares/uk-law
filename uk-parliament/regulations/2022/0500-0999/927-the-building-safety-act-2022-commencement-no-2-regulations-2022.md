# The Building Safety Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 927 (C. 69)

Building And Buildings, England

The Building Safety Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022

Made

31st August 2022

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 170(5) of the Building Safety Act 2022[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Building Safety Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st September 2022

Sections 126 to 129 of the Building Safety Act 2022 (building industry schemes and prohibitions on development and building control) come into force on 1st September 2022.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities

Eddie Hughes

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the second commencement regulations made under the Building Safety Act [2022 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/30) (“**the Act**”).

Regulation 2 brings into force sections 126 to 129 of the Act on 1st September 2022. The effect is to commence the powers relating to a scheme for developers and the prohibitions on developing land and building control which may be imposed on persons, including on persons who are eligible to be a member of a scheme established under section 126 but who are not members.

An impact assessment has been produced in relation to the Act and copies can be obtained at https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/58-02/0139/BuildingSafetyBillImpactAssessment.pdf or from the Department for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities at 2 Marsham Street, London, SW1P 4DF, United Kingdom.
