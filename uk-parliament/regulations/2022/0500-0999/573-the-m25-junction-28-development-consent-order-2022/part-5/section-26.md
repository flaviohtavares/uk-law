# The M25 Junction 28 Development Consent Order 2022, section 26

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-d5addfe6f557d01a6323417a6e89a01e]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00063] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this order”.
