# The Professional Qualifications Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 936 (C. 71)

Professional Qualifications

The Professional Qualifications Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2022

Made

2nd September 2022

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(4)(c) and (d) of the Professional Qualifications Act 2022[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Professional Qualifications Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2022.

## 2. Extent

These regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 3. Commencement

The following provisions of the Professional Qualifications Act 2022 come into force on 28th October 2022—

- (a) section 9 (duty of regulator to provide information to regulator in another part of the United Kingdom); and
- (b) section 10 (duty of regulator to provide information to overseas regulator), in so far as it is not already in force.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force sections 9 and 10 of the Professional Qualifications Act 2022 (“**the Act**”).

Section 9 places a duty on a regulator in one part of the United Kingdom (“the UK”) to provide information to a regulator in another part of the UK. This duty applies when individuals entitled to practise a regulated profession in one part of the UK are seeking to practise a corresponding regulated profession in another part of the UK.

Section 10 places a duty on a regulator in the UK to provide information to a regulator overseas. This duty applies when the individual is entitled to practise a regulated profession in the UK, or a part of the UK, and is seeking to practise a corresponding regulated profession overseas. The individual concerned must have given their permission for the information to be shared.

An impact assessment has not been published for these Regulations, as they have no impact on costs to businesses, the public or voluntary sectors independent of the provision these Regulations bring into force. A full impact assessment has been published in relation to the Act and copies can be obtained at https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/2865/publications or from the Department for Business, Energy & Industrial Strategy at 1 Victoria Street, London, SW1H 0ET, United Kingdom.
