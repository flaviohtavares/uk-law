# The Manston Airport Development Consent Order 2022, section 20

> Text as at 2025-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-a40862b7d37ddafb9ebc0b5950320eda]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00048] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
