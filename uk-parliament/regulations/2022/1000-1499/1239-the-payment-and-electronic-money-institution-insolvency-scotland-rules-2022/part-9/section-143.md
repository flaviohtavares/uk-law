> Extent: Scotland.

## 143. Final progress reports[^c-33719a8e192d34809a78a10c78d7654e]

*Extent: S*

The final progress report means a progress report which includes a summary of the following—

- (a) the administrator’s proposals (including whether the FCA has given a direction under regulation 38 and whether that direction has been withdrawn),
- (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals,
- (c) the steps taken during the special administration,
- (d) the outcome.
