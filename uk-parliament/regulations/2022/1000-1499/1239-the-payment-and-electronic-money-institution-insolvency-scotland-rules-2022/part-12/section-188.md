> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 188. Notifications of more than one nature[^c-e8c0a509505d333942e554a0e3b82f60]

*Extent: S*

A notification which includes two or more of the types of notification set out in rules 183 to 187 must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
