# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 184

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 184. Notification relating to the administrator[^key-d92f287d96f818109c7364141a7f1be2]

*Extent: S*

In addition to the information required by rule 183, a notification relating to the office of the administrator must also specify—

- (a) the name and business address of the administrator,
- (b) the date of the event notified,
- (c) where the notification relates to an appointment, the person, body or court making the appointment,
- (d) where the notification relates to the termination of an appointment, the reason for that termination (for example, resignation).
