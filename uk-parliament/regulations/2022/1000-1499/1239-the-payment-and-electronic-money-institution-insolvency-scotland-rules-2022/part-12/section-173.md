## 173. Electronic delivery of special administration documents to court[^key-f130f7444ab739780149b3659d8942ea]

*Extent: S*

(1) A document may not be delivered to the court by electronic means unless this is expressly permitted by Rules of Court.

(2) A document delivered by electronic means is to be treated as delivered to the court at the time it is recorded by the court as having been received or otherwise as the Rules of Court provide.
