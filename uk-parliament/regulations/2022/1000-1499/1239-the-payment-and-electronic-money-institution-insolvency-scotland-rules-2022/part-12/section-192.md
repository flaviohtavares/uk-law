> Text as at 2025-02-26.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 192. Charges for copy documents[^key-e70c0666520394f262546521ac9349a0]

*Extent: S*

Except where prohibited by these Rules, the administrator is entitled to require the payment of the standard fee for copies of documents requested by a creditor, customer, member, contributory or member of the creditors’ committee.
