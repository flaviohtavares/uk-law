# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 170

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 170. Electronic delivery by administrator[^c-aabcece2a166af86565d4d833e773840]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the administrator gives, sends or delivers a notice or other document to any person by electronic means, the notice or document must contain or be accompanied by a statement—

- (a) that the recipient may request a hard copy of the notice or document,
- (b) specifying a telephone number, email address and postal address which may be used to make that request.

(2) Where a hard copy of the notice or other document is requested, it must be sent free of charge within five business days of receipt of the request by the administrator.
