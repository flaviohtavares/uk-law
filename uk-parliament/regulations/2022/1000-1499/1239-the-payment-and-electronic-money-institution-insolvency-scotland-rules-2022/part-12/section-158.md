## 158. Costs, expenses etc[^key-0d4e5538dd9441b77c2ff880cac8f206]

*Extent: S*

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the special administration are, unless otherwise stated, to be regarded as expenses of the special administration.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part must be paid out of the prescribed part.
