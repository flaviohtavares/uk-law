> Text as at 2025-02-26.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 200. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court[^c-2a093395aa33a15b2744177a7ee5c578]

*Extent: S*

Where a special administration is commenced in a court other than the Court of Session, that court may order the transfer of the proceedings to the Court of Session.
