# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 165

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 165. Application[^key-d5f354a8a1049943857ce66abe4951d1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) , (2A)[^key-84c4c0f786569c897f112e7605b16acc] and (3), this Chapter applies where a notice or other document is required to be given, delivered or sent under the Regulations or these Rules.

(2) This Chapter does not apply to the submission of notices or other documents to the registrar of companies.

(2A)[^key-018e3e80b56d3af8c0f4f237de915b91] Rule 166A does not apply where a notice is required to be given or delivered under the Regulations or these Rules.

(3) Rules 169 to 172 do not apply to the filing of any notice or other document with the court.
