# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 189

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 189. Notifications made to other persons at the same time[^key-8d18bbfe207f324b1329899817f15c73]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where under the Regulations or these Rules a notification is to be sent to another person at the same time that it is to be sent to the registrar of companies, that requirement may be satisfied by sending to that other person a copy of the notification sent to the registrar of companies.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) where additional information is prescribed for the notification to the other person, or
- (b) where the notification to the registrar of companies is incomplete.
