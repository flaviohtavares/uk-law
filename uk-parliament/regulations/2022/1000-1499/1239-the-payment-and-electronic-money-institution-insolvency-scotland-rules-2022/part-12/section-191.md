> Extent: Scotland.

## 191. Right to copy documents[^key-0a7401a869ed7d116b173df3cddf860a]

*Extent: S*

Where the Regulations or these Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, that person has a right to be supplied on request with copies of those documents, on payment of the standard fee for copies.
