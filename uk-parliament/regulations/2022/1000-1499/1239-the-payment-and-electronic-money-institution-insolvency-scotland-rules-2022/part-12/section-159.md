> Extent: Scotland.

## 159. False representation of status for purpose of inspecting documents[^key-617e0755c60a2598747650b7e45f09d5]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is an offence for a person who does not have a right under these Rules to inspect a relevant document falsely to claim to be a creditor, customer, member of the institution or contributory of the institution with the intention of gaining sight of the relevant document.

(2) A relevant document is one which is on the court file or in the hands of the administrator or any other person and which a creditor, customer, member of the institution or contributory of the institution has a right to inspect under these Rules.
