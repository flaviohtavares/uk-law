> Extent: Scotland.

## 174. Notice etc to joint administrators[^c-fd2b1822a287cf07a9a8211d9380946a]

*Extent: S*

Where there are joint office-holders in a special administration, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
