> Extent: Scotland.

## 181. Non-Gazette notices — other provisions[^key-1a531d3f444782e868e1af00bd905322]

*Extent: S*

(1) The information required to be contained in a notice to which rule 180 applies must be included in the advertisement of that notice in a manner that is reasonably likely to ensure, in relation to the form of the advertising used, that a person reading, hearing or seeing the advertisement will be able to read, hear or see that information.

(2) Information required under rule 180 to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
