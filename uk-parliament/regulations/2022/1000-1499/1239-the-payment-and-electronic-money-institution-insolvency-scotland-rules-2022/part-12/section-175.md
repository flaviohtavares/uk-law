# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 175

> Extent: Scotland.

## 175. Delivery of statements of claim and documentary evidence of debt[^c-6b3fe5f22bfd6494d5069b9fd5eeb201]

*Extent: S*

(1) Once a statement of claim or documentary evidence of debt has been delivered to an administrator in accordance with these Rules it need not be delivered again.

(2) Where these Rules require such delivery by a certain time, that requirement is satisfied if that statement or evidence has already been delivered.
