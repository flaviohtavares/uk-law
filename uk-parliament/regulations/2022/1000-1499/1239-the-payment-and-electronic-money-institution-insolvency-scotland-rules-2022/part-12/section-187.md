> Extent: Scotland.

## 187. Notifications relating to other events[^c-b3d19129b9fa422206a34cc0e0a321f4]

*Extent: S*

In addition to the information required by rule 183, a notification relating to any other event (for example the coming into force of a moratorium) must specify the following—

- (a) the nature of the event including the regulation or rule under which it took place,
- (b) the date the event occurred.
