## 186. Notifications relating to court orders[^c-c1656068fbb17b7dd64e0bd0dc719f6e]

*Extent: S*

In addition to the information required by rule 183, a notification relating to a court order must also specify the following—

- (a) the nature of the court order,
- (b) the name of the court,
- (c) the date of the order.
