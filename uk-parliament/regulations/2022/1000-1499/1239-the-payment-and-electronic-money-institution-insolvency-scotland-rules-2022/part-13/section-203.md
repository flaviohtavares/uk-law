# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 203

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 203. Venue[^key-fca799ba3c8025199b67c50899d99588]

*Extent: S*

Reference to a “venue”—

- (a) in relation to any proceeding or attendance before the court is to the time, date and place or platform for the proceeding or attendance,
- (b) in relation to a meeting is to the time, date and place for the meeting, but

in the case of a meeting to be conducted and held in accordance with rule 78(2) or section 246A(3) of IA 1986, is to the time and date for the meeting and the arrangements the administrator proposes to enable persons to exercise their rights to speak and vote at the meeting.
