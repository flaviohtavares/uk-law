# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 206

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 206. Application of the IA 1986 and the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^c-16c044eda4cfa6124686ab4eeeaf222f][^c-4b48659d6962a4a2ef9163b65d8b3b62]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of these Rules, any reference in IA 1986 or the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^f00023] to “leave” of the court is to be construed as meaning “permission” of the court.
