## 46. Adjournment in absence of chair[^key-73be7872cc905b912139e95583d26baa]

*Extent: S*

(1) If within 30 minutes from the time fixed for commencement of a meeting there is no person present to act as chair, the meeting stands adjourned to the same time and place in the following week or, if that is not a business day, to the business day immediately following.

(2) If within 30 minutes from the time fixed for the commencement of the meeting those persons attending the meeting do not constitute a quorum, the chair may adjourn the meeting to such time and place as the chair may appoint.
