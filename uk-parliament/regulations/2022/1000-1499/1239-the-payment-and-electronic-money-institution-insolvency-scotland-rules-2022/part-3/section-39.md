> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Gazetting and advertisement of meetings[^c-9f987e1fcabeeb61971afa76c7ac811d]

*Extent: S*

(1) The administrator, in calling a meeting under these Rules, must have gazetted a notice which, in addition to the standard contents, must state—

- (a) that a meeting of—
  - (i) creditors,
  - (ii) customers,
  - (iii) creditors and customers,
  - (iv) members, or
  - (v) contributories

is to take place,

- (b) the venue fixed for the meeting,
- (c) the purpose of the meeting,
- (d) the time and date by which, and place at which, those attending who wish to vote must lodge proxies and (in the case of a meeting of creditors, customers or both) statements of claim.

(2) Notice under this rule must be gazetted before or as soon as is reasonably practicable after notice is given to those attending.

(3) Information to be gazetted under this rule may also be advertised in such other manner as the administrator thinks fit.
