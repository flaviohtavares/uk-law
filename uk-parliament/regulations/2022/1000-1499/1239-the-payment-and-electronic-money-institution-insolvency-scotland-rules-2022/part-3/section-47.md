## 47. Statements of claim and proxies in adjournment[^c-5742ea3c6df125598a1eb62205b0922b]

*Extent: S*

Where a meeting under these Rules is adjourned, statements of claim and proxies may be used if lodged before the resumption of the adjourned meeting.
