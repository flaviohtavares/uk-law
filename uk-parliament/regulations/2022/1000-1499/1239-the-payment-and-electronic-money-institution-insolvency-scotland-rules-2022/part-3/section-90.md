# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 90

> Extent: Scotland.

## 90. Retention of proxies[^c-c8b06a1f8bef8dfb4dca210d2d421a7a]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), proxies used for voting at any meeting must be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair must deliver the proxies, as soon as is reasonably practicable after the meeting, to the administrator (where the administrator is someone other than the chair).

(3) The administrator must retain all proxies in the sederunt book.
