## 40. Non-receipt of notice of meeting[^key-b2f97db0259099d20c3f5aa3865adfb1]

*Extent: S*

Where, in accordance with the Regulations or these Rules, a meeting is called by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly called and held, even if not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
