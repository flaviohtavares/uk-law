# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 92

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Proxy-holder with financial interest[^c-0a95c9ba2824fab7cd02be557c73abca]

*Extent: S*

(1) A proxy-holder must not vote in favour of any resolution which would—

- (a) directly or indirectly place the proxy-holder, or any associate of the proxy-holder, in a position to receive any remuneration, fees or expenses out of the insolvent estate or the asset pool, or
- (b) fix or change the amount of or the basis of any remuneration, fees or expenses receivable by the proxy-holder or any associate of the proxy-holder from the insolvent estate or the asset pool.

(2) However, a proxy-holder may vote for a resolution described in paragraph (1) if the proxy specifically directs the proxy-holder to vote in that way.

(3) Where the administrator is appointed as proxy-holder and that proxy is used under rule 89(4) by another person acting as chair, the administrator is deemed to be an associate of the person acting as chair.
