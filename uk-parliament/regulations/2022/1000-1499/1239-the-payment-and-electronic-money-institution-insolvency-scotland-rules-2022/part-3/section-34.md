> Extent: Scotland.

## 34. Venue[^key-2a734c445c6e4917b4942029b65b9193]

*Extent: S*

(1) In fixing the venue for a meeting, the caller must have regard to the convenience of those attending.

(2) Meetings must be called for commencement between 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day (subject to any direction by the court).

(3) In this rule, “**meeting**” includes an adjourned meeting.
