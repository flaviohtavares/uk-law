> Text as at 2025-02-26.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Requisite majorities at contributories’ meetings[^key-ed8abe37a24a5085fb89ba0f212dc641]

*Extent: S*

At a meeting of contributories, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the institution, subject to any provision of the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the institution is in liquidation.
