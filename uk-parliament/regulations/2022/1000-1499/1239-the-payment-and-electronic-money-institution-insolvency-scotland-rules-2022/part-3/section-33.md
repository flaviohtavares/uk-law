## 33. Meetings generally[^c-bd59e110af9f68b9aa8efaf1e8956102]

*Extent: S*

Except where different provision is made in the Regulations or these Rules, this Chapter applies to meetings called by the administrator—

- (a) under paragraph 51, 54(2) or 62,
- (b) following a request or a direction from the court under paragraph 56.
