> Extent: Scotland.

## 149. Applications under section 176A(5) of the IA 1986 to disapply section 176A(2) of the IA 1986[^c-25b8f505cf6acd9dfb5bfeb0b735e204]

*Extent: S*

An application under section 176A(5) of the IA 1986 must include averments—

- (a) that the application arises in respect of a special administration,
- (b) as to the financial position of the institution,
- (c) as to the basis of the applicant’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
