## 139. Notification and advertisement of appointment of replacement administrator[^key-8d728601ee947b65ef439cf3d7b0d7d8]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to rule 141, where a replacement administrator is appointed, the same provisions apply in respect of giving notice of, and advertising, the replacement appointment as in the case of the initial appointment.

(2) All notices must clearly identify that the appointment is of a replacement administrator.
