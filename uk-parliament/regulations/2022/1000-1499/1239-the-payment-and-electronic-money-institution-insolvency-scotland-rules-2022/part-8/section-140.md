## 140. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint administrator[^c-e30027cbf7d80b46797277f40bb3cb59]

*Extent: S*

Subject to rule 141, where a person is to be appointed in accordance with paragraph 100 to act as administrator, jointly or concurrently with the person or persons then acting, the same provisions apply, subject to this rule and to such other modification as may be necessary, in respect of the making of this appointment, in the case of the original appointment of an administrator.
