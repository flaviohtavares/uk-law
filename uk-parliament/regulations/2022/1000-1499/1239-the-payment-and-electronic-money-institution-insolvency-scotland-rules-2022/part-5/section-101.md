> Extent: Scotland.

## 101. Costs of making a claim[^key-7575f21b626b4d3c16060016a87ecada]

*Extent: S*

Unless the court orders otherwise, every claimant under rule 99 bears the cost of making a relevant funds claim, including costs incurred in providing documents or evidence or responding to requests for further information.
