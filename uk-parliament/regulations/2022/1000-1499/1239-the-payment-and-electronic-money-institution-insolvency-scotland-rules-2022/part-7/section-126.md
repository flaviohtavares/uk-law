# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 126

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 126. Administrator to allow inspection of statements of claim[^c-d7e0969171a8f3459d8ec199bda051a4]

*Extent: S*

The administrator must, so long as submitted claims are in the administrator’s hands, allow them to be inspected, at all reasonable times on any business day, by any of the following persons—

- (a) any creditor who has submitted a claim (unless that claim has been wholly rejected for purposes of dividend or otherwise),
- (b) any contributory of the institution,
- (c) any person acting on behalf of either of the above.
