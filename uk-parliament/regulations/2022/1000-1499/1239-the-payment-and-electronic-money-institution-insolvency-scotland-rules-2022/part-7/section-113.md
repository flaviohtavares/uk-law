# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 113

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 113. Application and Interpretation of Part[^key-3333e9a22fdf85e9a228ef1ae9402eb7]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Part applies where the administrator proposes to make a distribution to creditors or any class of creditors.

(2) Where the distribution is to a particular class of creditors, references in this Part to creditors shall be a reference to that class of creditors only.
