# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 2022, section 10

> Text as at 2025-02-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Further notification[^key-42f79e9ed4c64b1faf8bf993486496ab]

*Extent: S*

As soon as is reasonably practicable after lodging the application, the applicant must notify—

- (a) any messenger-at-arms or sheriff officer whom the applicant knows to be charged with executing diligence or other legal process against the institution or its property,
- (b) any person whom the applicant knows to have executed[^key-6fe56b43f68bef976cc74c811a362b32] diligence or other legal process against the institution or its property.
