# The Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 1274

Seeds, England

The Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2022

Made

5th December 2022

Laid before Parliament

6th December 2022

Coming into force

30th December 2022

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) and (1A) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

The Secretary of State has consulted with representatives of such interests as appear to the Secretary of State to be concerned with these Regulations in accordance with section 16(1) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 30th<sup></sup>December 2022.

## 2. Extent and application

(1) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

(2) These Regulations apply to England only.

## 3. Amendment of Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017)

(1) Council Decision 2003/17 on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 6, for “31 December 2022” substitute “31 December 2029”.

Benyon

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

5th December 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Article 6 of Council Decision 2003/17 of 16 December 2002 on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries to extend the expiry date of this Decision.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
