The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

These Regulations may be cited as the EU Agencies (Revocations) Regulations 2022 and come into force on the twenty first day after the day on which they are laid.

**2.** These Regulations extend and apply to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 3. Revocation of Regulation [(EC) No 1339/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1339)

Regulation [(EC) No 1339/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1339) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 establishing a European Training Foundation is revoked.

## 4. Revocation of Regulation (EU) No 2019/128

Regulation (EU) 2019/128 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing a European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) and repealing [Council Regulation (EEC) No 337/75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1975/0337) is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies, in particular under 8(2)(a), (b) and (d), arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union. These Regulations revoke EU legislative provisions that have continued to operate as part of domestic law since the end of the implementation period despite the fact that the United Kingdom has left the two EU agencies to which they relate.

Regulation [(EC) No 1339/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1339) is revoked since the United Kingdom no longer participates in the European Training Foundation. Regulation (EU) No 2019/128 is revoked since the United Kingdom no longer participates in the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training, known as “Cedefop”.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
