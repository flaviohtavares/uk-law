# The Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 1255 (C. 99)

Agriculture, England

The Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Regulations 2022

Made

29th November 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(2) of the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Regulations 2022.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and apply in relation to England only.

## 2. Commencement of the Agriculture Act 2020

The following provisions of the Agriculture Act 2020 come into force on 30th November 2022—

- (a) Part 1 of Schedule 7 (exceptional market conditions: England);
- (b) Part 3 of Schedule 7 (marketing standards and carcass classification: England), except paragraph 11 of Schedule 7 so far as it relates to Article 88(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products[^f00002]; and
- (c) section 52 (consequential amendments), so far as it relates to the provisions brought into force by paragraphs (a) and (b) of this regulation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the second commencement regulations under the Agriculture Act [2020 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/21) (“**the Act**”) which apply in relation to England only. They bring into force, on 30th November 2022, Part 1 and Part 3 of Schedule 7 to the Act (with the exception of paragraph 11 of Schedule 7 so far as it relates to Article 88(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products (EUR 2013/1308) (“**the CMO Regulation**”)), together with section 52 so far as it relates to those provisions.

Part 1 of Schedule 7 to the Act makes amendments to Articles 219, 220, 221 and 222 of the CMO Regulation, in order that they are disapplied in relation to agricultural producers in England. The amendments made by Part 1 of Schedule 7 to the Act are made in consequence of the provision made by sections 20 and 21 of the Act.

Part 3 of Schedule 7 to the Act makes amendments to the CMO Regulation in relation to marketing standards and carcass classification in consequence of the provision made by Part 5 of the Act.
