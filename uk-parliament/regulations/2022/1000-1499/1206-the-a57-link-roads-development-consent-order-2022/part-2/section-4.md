# The A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022, section 4

> Text as at 2024-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised development[^c-d0e7f18c2ef811efbda1e52d1e8e5a01]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
