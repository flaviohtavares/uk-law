# The A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022, section 39

> Text as at 2024-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-d1caa6041646f75ad98d865d80b0d850]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act) of the 1990 Act.
