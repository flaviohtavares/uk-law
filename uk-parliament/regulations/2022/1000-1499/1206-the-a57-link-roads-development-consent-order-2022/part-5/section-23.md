# The A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022, section 23

> Text as at 2024-02-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-3c881dfd49225ff3355c58c3643d56e2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00053] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
