# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, section 44

> Text as at 2023-04-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Arbitration[^key-a40b2ee40aeed0aff6bb2dd9e4befea2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any difference under any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for, is to be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State or a person appointed by the Secretary of State.

(2) Any matter for which the consent or approval of the Secretary of State or the MMO is required under the provisions of this Order shall not be subject to arbitration pursuant to this article 44 (arbitration).
