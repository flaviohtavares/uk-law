# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, section 36

> Text as at 2023-04-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Restoration works[^key-391f345d812711a4b40a8cf8b2848259]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If the authorised works have not been commenced within the period specified in Requirement 2 the undertaker will within 6 months from the expiry of such period—

- (a) submit to the relevant planning authority for its written approval a scheme for the removal of the haul road, including its road bridges and associated infrastructure and restoration of the land, including the incorporation of biodiversity enhancements and a timetable for implementation; and
- (b) submit to the relevant planning authority for its written approval a scheme for the restoration of the land described in the Pilfrey laydown plans to its former condition.

(2) Both schemes shall be implemented by the undertaker as approved and thereafter retained unless agreed otherwise with the relevant planning authority.
