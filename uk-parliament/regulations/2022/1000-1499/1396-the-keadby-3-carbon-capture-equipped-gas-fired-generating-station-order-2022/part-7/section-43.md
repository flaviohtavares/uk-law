# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, section 43

> Text as at 2023-04-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 43. Procedure in relation to certain approvals etc.[^c-47cb08a9281f2198f304b771eee68750]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where an application is made to or request is made of any authority or body named in any of the provisions of this Order for any consent, agreement or approval required or contemplated by any of the provisions of the Order, such consent, agreement or approval to be validly given, must be given in writing and must not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

(2) Schedule 9 (procedure for discharge of requirements) has effect in relation to all consents, agreements or approvals granted, refused or withheld in relation to any provision of this Order.
