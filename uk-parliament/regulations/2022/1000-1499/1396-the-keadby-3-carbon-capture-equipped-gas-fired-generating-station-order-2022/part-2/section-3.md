# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, section 3

> Text as at 2023-04-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by the Order[^c-e377e25fce0f44ca2287c58e4dcdf02c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the Requirements the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Each numbered work may be situated only within the corresponding numbered area shown on the works plans.
