# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, section 5

> Text as at 2023-04-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Operation of authorised development[^c-034ac9b3de620b8e2304bd7415f5ed84]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to use and operate the generating station comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any other requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required to authorise the operation of a generating station.
