# The A47/A11 Thickthorn Junction Development Consent Order 2022, section 6

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Maintenance of authorised development[^c-523afb12491019ea18c4b17ff6087680]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
