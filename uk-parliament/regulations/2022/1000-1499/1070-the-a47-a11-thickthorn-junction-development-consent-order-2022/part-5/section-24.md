# The A47/A11 Thickthorn Junction Development Consent Order 2022, section 24

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-eafd0ab337ec86467a1e73937898b371]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to paragraph (2) of article 27 (compulsory acquisition of rights and imposition of restrictive covenants) and paragraph (8) of article 34 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development) and article 52 (crown rights).
