# The A47/A11 Thickthorn Junction Development Consent Order 2022, section 25

> Text as at 2023-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-b0261f6e768d8ce966665d0edf37dd0b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals)[^f00057] to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
