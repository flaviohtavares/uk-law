# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Amendments to Annex II

(1) Annex II (requirements for the purpose of EU type-approval of vehicles, systems, components or separate technical units) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading to Annex II, and in the heading to Part I (regulatory acts for EU type-approval of vehicles produced in unlimited series) of Annex II, for “EU” substitute “GB”.

(3) In Part I of Annex II, in Appendix 1—

- (a) in the heading to Appendix 1—
  - (i) for “EU” substitute “GB”;
  - (ii) for “small” substitute “medium”;
- (b) in table 1—
  - (i) in item 20A, in the column headed “applicability and specific requirements”—
    - (aa) before “Daytime Running Lights (DRL)”, insert “The requirement for”;
    - (bb) for the words “shall be fitted” to the end, substitute “does not apply to types which had EU whole-vehicle type-approval prior to 1st February 2026, in accordance with this Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation.”;
  - (ii) in item 63, in the column headed “applicability and specific requirements”, for “EU” substitute “GB”;
- (c) in table 2—
  - (i) in footnote (<sup>1</sup>), for “EU” substitute “GB”;
  - (ii) in item 20A, in the column headed “applicability and specific requirements”—
    - (aa) before “Daytime Running Lights (DRL)”, insert “The requirement for”;
    - (bb) for the words “shall be fitted” to the end, substitute “does not apply to types which had EU whole-vehicle type-approval prior to 1st February 2026, in accordance with this Regulation or the EU Type Approval Regulation.”;
  - (iii) in item 63, in the column headed “applicability and specific requirements”, for “EU” substitute “GB”.

(4) Omit Appendix 2 to Part I.

(5) In Part II (list of UN regulations recognised as an alternative to the Directives or Regulations referred to in Part I) to Annex II—

- (a) in the opening paragraph—
  - (i) for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) omit “by virtue of [Decision 97/836/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0836), or subsequent Council decisions as referred to in Article 3(3) of that Decision,”;
  - (iii) for “an EU” substitute “a GB”;
- (b) in the second paragraph—
  - (i) for “an EU” substitute “a GB”;
  - (ii) omit “, subject to the Community Decision as referred to in Article 4(2) of [Decision 97/836/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0836)”.

(6) In Part III (list of regulatory acts setting out the requirements for the purpose of EU type-approval of special purpose vehicles) to Annex II—

- (a) in the heading, for “EU” substitute “GB”;
- (b) in the explanatory notes following the table in Appendix 6—
  - (i) in note X, for “Member States” substitute “The approval authority”;
  - (ii) in note Q, for “An EU” substitute “A GB”;
  - (iii) in note R, for the existing text, substitute “Space must be provided for a registration plate meeting the requirements of the Road Vehicles (Display of Registration Marks) Regulations 2001[^f00092]”;
  - (iv) in note T, for “The vehicle can be tested in accordance with Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157)” substitute “The vehicle may be subjected to the test procedures described in Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157), as it had effect immediately before IP completion day”;
  - (v) in notes V and V1, for “[Directive 97/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0068)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2016/1628[^f00093]”;
  - (vi) in note W1, in the second subparagraph, for “An EU” substitute “A GB”;
  - (vii) in note W6, for the third sentence of the second subparagraph, substitute “An extension to the UNECE type-approval (or GB type-approval, if applicable) is not required.”;
  - (viii) in note W8, in the second subparagraph, for “EU” substitute “GB”.
