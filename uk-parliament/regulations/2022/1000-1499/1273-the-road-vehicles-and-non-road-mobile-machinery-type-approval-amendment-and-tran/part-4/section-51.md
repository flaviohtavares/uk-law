# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0078): Annexes

(1) The Annexes to Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0078) are amended as follows.

(2) In the list of Annexes, for “EC” each time it occurs, substitute “GB”.

(3) In Annex I (technical provisions for the testing of vehicles and frontal protection systems), in paragraph 1.8, for the definition of “windscreen” substitute “‘windscreen’ means the frontal glazing of the vehicle situated between the A-pillars;”.

(4) In Annex II (model information documents to be supplied by the manufacturer)—

- (a) in the list of Parts after the heading to Annex II, for “EC” each time it occurs, substitute “GB”;
- (b) in Part 1, in the heading, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (c) in Parts 2 and 3, for “EC type-approval” each time it occurs, substitute “GB type-approval”.

(5) In the notes following Part 3 of Annex II, in note (c), for the words “Section A of Annex II” to the end, substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018 on the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles[^f00097].”.

(6) In Annex III (EC type-approval model certificates)—

- (a) for “EC type-approval” each time it occurs, including in the heading, in the Appendix and the Addenda to Parts 1, 2 and 3, substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in Parts 1, 2 and 3, in each case in the words before section 1, for “as implemented by …” substitute “as it has effect in domestic law, as last amended by S.I. …./….”;
- (c) in Parts 1 and 2, in note 4 to point 0.4, in each case for the words “Section A of Annex II” to the end, substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018 on the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles.”;
- (d) in Part 3—
  - (i) at the end of the heading, insert “(SEPARATE TECHNICAL UNIT)”;
  - (ii) in paragraph 0.7 of Section 1, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
  - (iii) in paragraph 1.5.1 of Section II, in note (c) after the table, for “\[implementing legislation\]” substitute “Regulation (EU) 631/2020[^f00098]”;
- (e) in the notes to the Addendum to Part 1, in note 3, for “Commission \[implementing legislation\]” substitute “Commission Regulation (EU) 631/2010”.

(7) In Annex IV (EC type-approval mark)—

- (a) in the heading, and each other time it occurs, including in the Appendix, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in point 1.1, for the words “‘e’” to the end of the point, substitute “‘g’ followed by the number 11.”;
- (c) in point 1.2, for “Annex VII to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex IV to Regulation (EU) 2020/683”;
- (d) in the Appendix—
  - (i) for the image, substitute—

    > [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1273/images/uksi_20221273_en_002)
  - (ii) in the note after the image, omit “in Germany (e1)”.
