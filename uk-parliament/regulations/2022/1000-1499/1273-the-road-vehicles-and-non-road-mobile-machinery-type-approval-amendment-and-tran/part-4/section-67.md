# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Commission Regulation (EU) No 65/2012

(1) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 65/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0065) is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 4 (EC type-approval)—

- (a) in the heading, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in the opening words of paragraph 2 and in the second sub-paragraph of paragraph 3, for “an EC type-approval” substitute “a GB type-approval”.

(3) Omit Article 5 (monitoring the effects of legislation).

(4) After Article 7 (entry into force), omit “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.”

(5) In Annex II—

- (a) in Part 1 (information document)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
  - (ii) in the fourth paragraph, for “Regulation [(EC) No 692/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0692):” substitute “Regulation (EU) No 2017/1151;”;
- (b) in Part 3 (EC type-approval certificate)—
  - (i) in the heading and each time it occurs, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
  - (ii) for “Regulation (EU) No 65/2012 as last amended by Regulation (EU) No …/…” substitute “Regulation (EU) No 65/2012, as it has effect in domestic law, and as last amended by S.I. …../……”.
