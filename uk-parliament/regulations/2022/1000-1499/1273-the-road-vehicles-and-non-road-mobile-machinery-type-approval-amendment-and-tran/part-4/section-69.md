# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Commission Regulation (EU) No 347/2012

(1) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 347/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0347) is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 (scope)—

- (a) in the opening words, for “Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (b) in points (4) and (5), for “Part A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Part A of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”.

(3) In Article 2 (definitions)—

- (a) in the first paragraph, for “[Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (b) in the second paragraph, in point (3), for “point 1 of Section C of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “point 2.1 of Part C of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”.

(4) In Article 3 (obligations of member States)—

- (a) in the heading, for “Member States” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) omit paragraphs 1 and 2;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “national authorities” substitute “the approval authority”;
  - (ii) for “EC” substitute “GB”;
  - (iii) omit “or national type approval”;
- (d) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “national authorities” substitute “the Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) for “Article 26 of [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 48 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (e) in paragraph 5—
  - (i) in the opening words, for “national authorities” substitute “the Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) in points (a) and (c)—
    - (aa) for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
    - (bb) omit “or national type approval”.

(5) In Article 4 (EC type-approval of a vehicle re AEBS)—

- (a) in the heading, and in paragraph 1, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (b) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “an EC” substitute “a GB”;
  - (ii) for “Annex VII to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex IV to Regulation (EU) 2020/683”;
  - (iii) for “An approval authority” substitute “The approval authority”.
- (c) in paragraph 4, for “an EC” substitute “a GB”.

(6) Omit Article 5 (amendment to Appendix 2 of Annex II).

(7) After Article 6 (entry into force), omit “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States”.

(8) In Annex I (standard information document and EC type-approval certificate re AEBS)—

- (a) in the heading, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (b) in Part 1 (information document)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
  - (ii) in point 8.1, for “point 1.6 of Annex I to the Council Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320)” substitute “point 2.6 of UNECE Regulation No 13[^f00117]”;
  - (iii) in point 8.2, for “point “1.2 of Annex I to Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320)” substitute “point 2.3 of UNECE Regulation No 13”;
  - (iv) in point 8.6, for “the Appendix to point 1.1.4.2 of the Appendix to Annex II to Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320) or to the Appendix to Annex XI thereto” substitute “Annex 10 to UNECE Regulation No 13 or to Annex 14 to that Regulation”;
- (c) in the explanatory notes following Part 1—
  - (i) in note (<sup>c</sup>), for “Part A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L 263, 9.10.2007, p.1)” substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (ii) in footnote (<sup>g5</sup>), for “point 2.4.1 of Annex I to [Directive 97/27/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0027) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L 233, 25.8.1997, p.1)” substitute “point 22 of Article 2 of Commission Regulation (EU) 1230/2012, taking into account Appendix 1 to Annex I thereto”;
  - (iii) in footnote (<sup>g7</sup>), for “point 2.4.2 of Annex I to [Directive 97/27/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0027)” substitute “point 23 of Article 2 of Commission Regulation 1230/2012, taking into account Appendix 1 to Annex I thereto”;
- (d) in Part 2 (model EC type-approval certificate)—
  - (i) in the certificate heading, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
  - (ii) in words preceding Section I—
    - (aa) for “EC type-approval” each time it occurs, substitute “GB type-approval”;
    - (bb) for “as amended and complying with approval level 1 / approval level 2” substitute “as it forms part of domestic law and as lasted amended by S.I. …../… with approval level 2”;
- (e) in the explanatory notes following Part 2, in note (<sup>3</sup>), for “Section A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (f) in the Addendum to Part 2, in the heading, for “EC” substitute “GB”.

(9) In Annex II (requirements and tests for the type-approval of vehicles re AEBS)—

- (a) in point 2.4.2.1, omit subparagraph (a);
- (b) in point 2.4.2.2, omit the words “for approval level 1” to “Appendix 1”;
- (c) in point 2.4.5, omit “For approval level 1: Column D of the table in Appendix 1”;
- (d) in point 2.5.1, omit “For approval level 1: Column H of the table in Appendix 1”;
- (e) in point 2.5.2.1, omit “For approval level 1: Column E of the table in Appendix 1”;
- (f) in point 2.5.2.2, omit “For approval level 1: Column F of the table in Appendix 1”;
- (g) omit Appendix 1 to Annex II;
- (h) in the footnotes to Appendix 2, omit footnote (<sup>5</sup>).
