# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/78

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/78 is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 2 (EC type-approval: 112-based eCall in-vehicle systems)—

- (a) in the heading, and each other time it occurs, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, for “Article 3(29) of [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 3(36) of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) in the first subparagraph—
    - (aa) for “grant an” substitute “grant a”;
    - (bb) for “issue an” substitute “issue a”;
    - (cc) for “Annex VII to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex IV to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/683”;
  - (ii) in the second subparagraph, for “A Member State” substitute “The approval authority”.

(3) In Article 3 (EC type-approval: 112-based eCall in-vehicle STUs etc)—

- (a) in the heading, and each other time it occurs, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, for “Article 3(29) of [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 3(36) of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “grant an” substitute “grant a”;
  - (ii) after “issue” insert “a”;
  - (iii) for “Annex VII to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex IV to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/683”.
  - (iv) for “A Member State” substitute “The approval authority”.

(4) In Article 4 (EC type-approval mark)—

- (a) in the heading, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (b) for “an EC” both times it occurs, substitute “a GB”.

(5) After Article 6 (entry into force), omit “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States”.

(6) In Annex I (Administrative documents for EC type-approval: installation of 112-based eCall in-vehicle systems)—

- (a) in the heading, and each time it occurs, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in the explanatory notes to Part 1—
  - (i) in note (2), for “Part A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)”, substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (ii) in note (4), for “Annex II of [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (c) in Part 2—
  - (i) after “2015/758” insert “as it has effect in domestic law”;
  - (ii) for “by Regulation …/…” substitute “by S.I. …./….”;
  - (iii) in the explanatory notes to Part 2, in note (3), for “Part A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)”, substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (iv) in the heading to the Addendum to Part 2, for “EC” substitute “GB”.
- (d) in Part 3—
  - (i) in point 2.1—
    - (aa) for “Directives [95/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0046) and [2002/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0058) of the European Parliament and of the Council”, substitute “Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Data Protection Act 2018[^f00135] and the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003[^f00136]”;
    - (bb) for “Article 7(d) of [Directive 95/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0046)” substitute “Article 6(1)(d) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679[^f00137]”;
  - (ii) in point 2.3.2—
    - (aa) for “respective public authorities of the country on which territory they are located” substitute “Secretary of State”;
    - (bb) omit “single European”;
  - (iii) in point 2.5.1—
    - (aa) for “[Directive 95/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0046)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Data Protection Act 2018”;
    - (bb) for “this Directive” substitute “that Regulation and that Act”;
  - (iv) in point 3.2, for “Directives [95/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0046) and [2002/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0058)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003”.

(7) In Annex II (administrative documents for EC type-approval: 112 based eCall in-vehicle STU etc)—

- (a) in the heading, and each other time it occurs, for “EC type-approval” substitute “GB type-approval”;
- (b) in Part 1, in point 0.7, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (c) in the explanatory notes to Part 1, in note (2), for “Section A of Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (d) in Part 2—
  - (i) after “2015/758” insert “as it has effect in domestic law”;
  - (ii) in Section 1, in point 0.7, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
- (e) in Part 3—
  - (i) in point 1, for “EC” substitute “GB”;
  - (ii) for point 1.1, substitute—

    > **1.1.** A rectangle surrounding the lower-case letter “g” followed by the number 11.
  - (iii) in point 3—
    - (aa) in figure 1, for the image, substitute—

      > [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1273/images/uksi_20221273_en_009)
    - (bb) in the explanatory note following “figure 1”, for “EC” substitute “GB”, omit “by Bulgaria”, and for “0046” substitute “00026”;
    - (cc) in figure 2, for the image, substitute—

      > [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1273/images/uksi_20221273_en_010)
    - (dd) in the explanatory note following figure 2, for “EC” substitute “GB”, and omit “by Germany”.
