# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Amendments to Annex I

(1) Annex I (general definitions, criteria for vehicle categorisation, types of vehicle and types of bodywork) is amended as follows.

(2) In the Introductory Part, in paragraph 2.4.1, for “Council Directive [80/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0181)” substitute “the Units of Measurement Regulations 1986[^f00090].

(3) In Part A (criteria for vehicle categorisation)—

- (a) in paragraph 1, in the first subparagraph, for the words from the beginning to “national individual vehicle approval” substitute “For the purposes of GB type-approval, including GB small series type-approval, and individual vehicle approval”;
- (b) in paragraph 6.2, omit “national”.

(4) In Part B (criteria for types of vehicle, variants and versions), in paragraph 6.3.1(b), for “points 3.2 and 3.3 of Annex 1 to [Directive 96/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0053)”, substitute “column 4 of table 6 in Schedule 3 to the Road Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations 1998[^f00091]”.
