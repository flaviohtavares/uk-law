# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Amendment to Chapter XIII

(1) In Article 59 (information intended for users), in paragraph 3, for the words from “the official language” to “into service” substitute “English”.

(2) In Article 60 (information intended for manufacturers), in paragraph 1, for “EU” substitute “GB”.
