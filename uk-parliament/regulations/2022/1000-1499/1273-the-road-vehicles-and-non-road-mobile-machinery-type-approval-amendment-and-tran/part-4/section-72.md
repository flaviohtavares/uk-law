## 72. Commission Regulation (EU) No 1230/2012: Annexes

(1) The Annexes to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1230/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1230) are amended as follows.

(2) In Annex I (technical requirements)—

- (a) in Part A (vehicles of category M<sub>1</sub> and N<sub>1</sub>)—
  - (i) in point 1.1.2(c), for “Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (ii) omit point 1.1.3;
  - (iii) in points 1.2 and 1.3, for “length, width and height”, each time these words occur, substitute “length and width”;
  - (iv) omit point 2.7.2.2;
  - (v) in point 3.1.1.1(c), for “Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (b) in Part B—
  - (i) in point 1.1.3, for “4,00 m” substitute “4.57 m”;
  - (ii) in point 2.1.3.1, for “The additional weight required for alternative fuel or zero-emission technology in accordance with points 2.3. and 2.4. of Annex I to [Directive 96/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0053) shall be defined” substitute “Any increase in the maximum authorised weight of a vehicle that is permitted for alternative fuel or zero-emission technology in accordance with regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations 1998[^f00121] must be determined”;
  - (iii) in point 2.1.3.2—
    - (aa) for “the following additional symbol” substitute “one or both of the following additional symbols”;

      ;
  - (iv) in point 7.4, for “Annex XVI to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex VIII to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (v) in point 8.3, for “Annex XVI to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex VIII to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (c) in Part C—
  - (i) in point 1.1.2(b), for “Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
  - (ii) omit point 1.1.3;
  - (iii) in points 1.2 and 1.3. for “length, width and height” each time those words occur, substitute “length and width”;
  - (iv) in point 1.4.2—
    - (aa) for “the following additional symbol” substitute “one or both of the following additional symbols”;

  - (v) in point 2.1.4.1, for “The additional weight required for alternative fuel or zero-emission technology in accordance with point 2.3. of Annex I to [Directive 96/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0053) shall be defined” substitute “Any increase in the maximum authorised weight of a vehicle that is permitted for alternative fuel or zero-emission technology in accordance with regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations 1998 must be determined”;
  - (vi) in point 2.1.4.2—
    - (aa) for “the following additional symbol” substitute “one or both of the following additional symbols”;

  - (ix) in point 7.3, for “Annex XVI to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex VIII to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
- (d) in Part D—
  - (i) in point 1.1.2, for “Annex II to [Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046)” substitute “Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858”;
