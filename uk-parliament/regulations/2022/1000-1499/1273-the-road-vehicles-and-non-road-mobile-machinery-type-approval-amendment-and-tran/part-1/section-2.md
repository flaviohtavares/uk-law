# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/79**” means Commission Delegated Regulation 2017/79 of 12 September 2016 establishing detailed technical requirements and test procedures for the EC type-approval of motor vehicles with respect to their 112-based eCall in-vehicles systems, of 112-based eCall in-vehicle separate technical units and components and supplementing and amending Regulation (EU) 2015/758[^f00003];
- “**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/78**” means Commission Implementing Regulation 2017/78 of 15 July 2016 establishing administrative provisions for the EC type-approval of motor vehicles with respect to their 112-based eCall in-vehicles systems and uniform conditions for the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2015/758[^f00004];
- “**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/683**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/683 of 15 April 2020 implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regards to the administrative requirements for the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles[^f00005];
- “[Commission Regulation (EC) No 631/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0631)” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No 631/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0631) of 22 July 2009 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0078)[^f00006];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 406/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/0406)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 406/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/0406) of 26 April 2010 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 79/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0079) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00007];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 672/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/0672)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 672/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/0672) of 27 July 2010 concerning type-approval requirements for windscreen defrosting and demisting systems of certain motor vehicles and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00008];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1003/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1003)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1003/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1003) of 8 November 2010 concerning type-approval requirements for the space for mounting and the fixing of rear registration plates on motor vehicles and their trailers and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00009];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1005/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1005)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1005/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1005) of 8 November 2010 concerning type-approval requirements for motor vehicle towing devices and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00010];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1008/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1008)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1008/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1008) of 9 November 2010 concerning type-approval requirements for windscreen wiper and washer systems of certain motor vehicles and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00011];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1009/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1009)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1009/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1009) of 9 November 2010 concerning type-approval requirements for wheel guards of certain motor vehicles and implementing Regulations [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00012];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 19/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0019)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 19/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0019) of 11 January 2011 concerning type-approval requirements for the manufacturer’s statutory plate and for the vehicle identification number of motor vehicles and their trailers and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00013];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 109/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0109)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 109/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0109) of 27 January 2011 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards type-approval requirements for certain categories of motor vehicles and their trailers as regards spray suppression systems[^f00014];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 458/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0458)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 458/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0458) of 12 May 2011 concerning type-approval requirements for motor vehicles and their trailers with regard to the installation of their tyres and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00015];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 582/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0582)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 582/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0582) of 25 May 2011 implementing and amending Regulation [(EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0595) of the European Parliament and of the Council with respect to emissions from heavy duty vehicles (Euro VI)[^f00016];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 65/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0065)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 65/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0065) of 24 January 2012 implementing Regulation [(EC) 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards gear shift indicators[^f00017];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 130/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0130)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 130/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0130) of 15 February 2012 concerning type-approval requirements for motor vehicles with regard to vehicle access and manoeuvrability and implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)[^f00018];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 347/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0347)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 347/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0347) of 16 April 2012 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council with respect to type-approval requirements for certain categories of motor vehicles with regard to advanced emergency braking systems[^f00019];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 351/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0351)” of 23 April 2012 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards type-approval requirements for the installation of lane departure warning systems in motor vehicles[^f00020];
- “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1230/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1230)” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1230/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1230) of 12 December 2012 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to type-approval requirements for masses and dimensions of motor vehicles and their trailers[^f00021];
- “**Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1151**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1151 of 1 June 2017 supplementing Regulation [(EC) No 715/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0715)[^f00022];
- “**Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400 of 12 December 2017 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0595)[^f00023];
- “**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1812**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1812 of 1 December 2020 laying down rules on the online data exchange and the notification of EU type-approvals under Regulation (EU) 2018/858[^f00024];
- “Regulation [(EC) No 715/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0715)” means Regulation [(EC) No 715/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2007 on type approval of motor vehicles with respect to emissions from light passenger and commercial vehicles[^f00025];
- “Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0078)” means Regulation [(EC) No 78/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0078) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 January 2009 on the type-approval of motor vehicles with regard to the protection of pedestrians and other vulnerable road users[^f00026];
- “Regulation [(EC) No 79/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0079)” means Regulation [(EC) No 79/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0079) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 January 2009 on type-approval of hydrogen-powered motor vehicles[^f00027];
- “Regulation [(EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0595)” means Regulation [(EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0595) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009 on type-approval of motor vehicles and engines with respect to emissions from heavy duty vehicles (Euro VI)[^f00028];
- “Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661)” means Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning type-approval requirements for the general safety of motor vehicles, their trailers and systems, components and separate technical units intended therefor[^f00029];
- “**Regulation (EU) No 167/2013**” means Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013 on the approval and market surveillance of agricultural and forestry vehicles[^f00030];
- “**Regulation (EU) No 168/2013**” means Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 January 2013 on the approval and market surveillance of two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles[^f00031];
- “**Regulation (EU) No 540/2014**” means Regulation (EU) No 540/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 on the sound level of motor vehicles and of replacement silencing systems[^f00032];
- “**Regulation (EU) 2015/758**” means Regulation (EU) 2015/758 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2015 concerning type-approval requirements for the deployment of the eCall in-vehicle system based on the 112 service[^f00033];
- “**Regulation (EU) 2018/858**” means Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018 on the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles[^f00034].
