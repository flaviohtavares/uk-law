# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement, and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type-Approval) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022.

(2) They come into force at 11 p.m. on 31st December 2022.

(3) These Regulations, except for regulations 4, 5, 62(6) and 72(2)(a)(ii) and (iii), (b)(i), (c)(ii) and (iii), and (d)(ii), (iii) and (iv), extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(4) Regulations 4, 5, 62(6) and 72(2)(a)(ii) and (iii), (b)(i), (c)(ii) and (iii), and (d)(ii), (iii) and (iv) extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.
