# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 87

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Amendment of regulation 69 (additional requirements: applications for an emergency proxy signature in respect of a particular petition: England and Wales and Scotland)[^c-08e878ff9f5a8690eedbb76b03d06c53]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 69 (additional requirements: applications for an emergency proxy signature in respect of a particular petition: England and Wales and Scotland), in the heading after “petition” insert “on grounds relating to occupation, service or employment”.
