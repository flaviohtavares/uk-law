# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 84

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Amendment of regulation 45 (orders for production of documents: England and Wales and Northern Ireland)[^key-6b96d3c954a3ca40307dd88aef82504b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 45 (orders for production of documents: England and Wales and Northern Ireland), after paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) In respect of a petition held in England or Wales, an order may be made by the bodies and in the circumstances described in paragraph (2)—
>
> - (a) for the opening of the sealed packet containing a completed signing sheet refusal list, or
> - (b) for the inspection or production of that list.

.
