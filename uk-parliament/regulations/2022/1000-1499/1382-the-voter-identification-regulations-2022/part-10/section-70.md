# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 70

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70. Amendment of regulation 3 (interpretation)[^c-fd6f2c35d053b5903dc55c35d68144cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 3 (interpretation), in paragraph (1)—

- (a) after the definition of “allotted petition signing place” insert—

  > - “**anonymous elector’s document**” has the meaning given in regulation 3(1) of the Voter Identification Regulations 2022;

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “special lists” insert—

  > - “**temporary electoral identity document**” has the meaning given in regulation 3(1) of the Voter Identification Regulations 2022;

  .
