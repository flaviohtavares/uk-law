# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 71

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Amendment of regulation 17 (appointment of petition assistants)[^c-e0129763c93a22156136c4a53baf3ff3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 17 (appointment of petition assistants), in paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b)—
  - (i) after “sheet under” insert “paragraph (3A) of regulation 25 (questions to be put to persons signing the petition), paragraph (1E) of regulation 27 (signing procedure in England and Wales and Scotland) or”;
  - (ii) for “that paragraph” substitute “those paragraphs”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

  > - (c) resolve doubts over identity as mentioned in paragraph (1F) of regulation 27 (including that paragraph as applied by regulation 29 or regulation 30).

  .
