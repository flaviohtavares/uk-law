# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 57

> Text as at 2024-11-27.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Amendment of article 13A (combination of polls: National Assembly for Wales)[^c-26bdfa05a946b4d88e0f58620cdd96a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 13A—

- (a) in the heading, for “National Assembly for Wales” substitute “Senedd Cymru”;
- (b) for “an Assembly” substitute “a Senedd”.
