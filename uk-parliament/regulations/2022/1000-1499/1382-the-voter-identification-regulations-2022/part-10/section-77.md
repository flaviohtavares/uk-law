# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 77

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Amendment of regulation 29 (signing sheets endorsed by petition clerk)[^key-29e09e12aaa174d11efc9b43949a888e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 29 (signing sheets endorsed by petition clerk), after paragraph (2) insert—

> (2A) Paragraphs (1A) to (1N) of regulation 27 (signing procedure: England and Wales and Scotland) apply in the case of a person who applies under paragraph (1) as they apply in the case of a person who applies under regulation 27(1), but as if—
>
> - (a) references to delivering a signing sheet to a person were references to endorsing a signing sheet, and
> - (b) in regulation 27(1G), the reference to paragraph (1) of regulation 27 were a reference to paragraph (1) of this regulation.

.
