## 81. Amendment of regulation 37 (procedure on close of petition)[^c-57a32f9149d5060d5edbdf7b80fbec35]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 37 (procedure on close of petition), in paragraph (1) after sub-paragraph (e) insert—

> - (ea) in the case of a petition held in England, Wales or Scotland, the signing sheet refusal list completed in accordance with regulation 30A;
