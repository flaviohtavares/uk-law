# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 92

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 92. Amendment of Schedule 2 (forms)[^c-485fe79bc943ffed7e7761e0ef28addc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In Schedule 2 (forms)—

- (a) in Part 1 (United Kingdom)—
  - (i) for Form D (official petition notice for an elector who may sign the petition in person), substitute the corresponding form in Schedule 12;
  - (ii) for Form F (official petition notice for a proxy who may sign the petition in person), substitute the corresponding form in Schedule 12;
  - (iii) after Form G (official petition notice for a proxy who may sign the petition by post), insert new Form G1 (signing sheet refusal list) as set out in Schedule 12;
  - (iv) for Form H (guidance for person signing the petition), substitute the corresponding form in Schedule 12;
  - (v) for Form J (proxy paper), substitute the corresponding form in Schedule 12;
- (b) omit Part 2 (Welsh forms).
