# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, section 33

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Application of rule 40B of the parliamentary election rules[^key-ebb5f82a9490d9975d419d185537aaba]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Rule 40B of Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act (collection and disclosure of information relating to applications made under rules 37 to 40)[^f00055] applies in relation to a parliamentary general election described in section 59A(2) of the 1983 Act[^f00056].
