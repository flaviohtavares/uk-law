# The Voter Identification Regulations 2022, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2024-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Identity document applications: photograph requirements

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 4(3)(a)

**1.**[^key-67663b8459a41631875d97c0573095cd] A photograph included in an identity document application (“the photograph”) must meet the following requirements.

**2.**[^key-547f1f870a343da182f8c5bb67531b07] The photograph must be a close-up photograph of the head and shoulders of the applicant.

**3.**[^key-89d222482105c042eee47107d8ed608d] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the photograph must show the full head of the applicant (without any other person visible or any covering, unless it is worn for religious beliefs or medical reasons)—

- (a) facing forward,
- (b) with nothing covering the face,
- (c) looking straight at the camera,
- (d) with a plain facial expression, and
- (e) with eyes open and clearly visible (e.g. without sunglasses and not obscured with hair etc.).

(2) The requirements in paragraph (1)(d) and (e) do not apply where the applicant is unable to provide a photograph which complies with either or both of those requirements (as the case may be) due to any disability.

**4.**[^key-0fbf892d994599ebcd6c25ab64a6ffc8] The photograph must be in colour and taken against a plain, light background.

**5.**[^key-54a21247dfdef964156047155625588d] The photograph must be—

- (a) in sharp focus and clear,
- (b) free from “redeye”, shadows which obscure the face, or reflection,
- (c) undamaged, and
- (d) a true likeness of the applicant, without amendment or manipulation.

**6.**[^key-5d2710a8e343796945cd1d216dc5f99b] For an application made other than via the digital service, the photograph must be—

- (a) at least 45 millimetres in height and at least 35 millimetres in width, and
- (b) no more than 297 millimetres in height and no larger than 210 millimetres in width.

**7.**[^key-7400a091ac6b0747bd214f181cfb43f7] For an application made via the digital service, the photograph must be—

- (a) at least 750 pixels in height and at least 600 pixels in width, and
- (b) contained in an electronic file which is no more than 20MB in size.
