# The Network Rail (Huddersfield to Westtown (Dewsbury) Improvements) Order 2022, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Disapplication of the 1887 Agreement

(1) The provisions of the 1887 Agreement will on the day on which this Order comes into force cease to have effect.

(2) In this article “**the 1887 Agreement**” means the agreement dated 14<sup>th</sup> November 1887 between (1) the London and North Western Railway Company (2) the Local Board of the District of Ravensthorpe in the County of York and (3) the Local Board of the District Thornhill in the County of York.
