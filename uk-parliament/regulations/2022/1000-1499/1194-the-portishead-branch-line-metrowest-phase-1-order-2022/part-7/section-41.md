# The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022, section 41

> Text as at 2025-10-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. No double recovery[^c-9eebf37e480e13bb97eddca994630b93]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more or different provisions of this Order.
