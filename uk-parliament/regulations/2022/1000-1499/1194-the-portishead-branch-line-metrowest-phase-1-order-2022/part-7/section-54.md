# The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022, section 54

> Text as at 2025-10-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Special category land[^key-9abf688d87ab106a69a8ae626d87d42d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On the exercise by the undertaker of the relevant Order power, the cycle track special category land is to vest in the undertaker and be discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which it was previously subject.

(2) In this article—

- “**the relevant Order power**” means the powers exercisable over the cycle track special category land by the undertaker under article 24 (compulsory acquisition of land);
- “**the cycle track special category land**” means the land numbered 01/213 and 01/223 in the book of reference and on the land plan and forming part of an open space which may be acquired compulsorily under this Order.
