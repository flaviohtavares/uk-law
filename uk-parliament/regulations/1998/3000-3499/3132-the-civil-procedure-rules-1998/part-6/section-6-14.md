## 6.14. Deemed service[^c-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

A claim form served within the United Kingdom[^c-60229278be4daf64c4cccf51eee8c4ee] in accordance with this Part is deemed to be served on the second business day after completion of the relevant step under rule 7.5(1).
