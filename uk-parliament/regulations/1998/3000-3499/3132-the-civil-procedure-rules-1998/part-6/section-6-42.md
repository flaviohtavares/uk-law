## 6.42. Service through foreign governments, judicial authorities and British Consular authorities[^key-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a party[^key-cd75dbb67216eeffa0b447507627aa0e] wishes to serve a claim form or any other document [^key-910a7796ffe28d7aa6ba0a10e7e84504]... in any country which is a party to a Civil Procedure Convention or Treaty[^key-654e45469acf8498ed9ba9f82e9a2cf2] providing for service in that country, it may be served—

- (a) through the authority designated under the Hague Convention or any other Civil Procedure Convention or Treaty[^key-7a93aeac20117b0f4772a3b652696981] (where relevant) in respect of that country; or
- (b) if the law of that country permits—
  - (i) through the judicial authorities of that country, or
  - (ii) through a British Consular authority in that country (subject to any provisions of the applicable convention about the nationality of persons who may be served by such a method).

(2) Where a party[^key-ac84a6976e40b4c58cb9c73dce8395f3] wishes to serve a claim form or any other document [^key-491a2a3648386ce54d396cfd5ca54b1b]... in any country with respect to which there is no Civil Procedure Convention or Treaty[^key-5953fbb6a5f1b187a83b21f4192df06e] providing for service in that country, the claim form or other document may be served, if the law of that country so permits—

- (a) through the government of that country, where that government is willing to serve it; or
- (b) through a British Consular authority in that country.

(3) Where a party[^key-d22cdd3d60242d5644032be2e16df8fb] wishes to serve the claim form or other document in—

- (a) any Commonwealth State which is not a party to the Hague Convention or is such a party but[^key-9b47e9549d2bbaad9a9ede651a6d23d7] HM Government has not declared acceptance of its accession to the Convention;
- (b) the Isle of Man or the Channel Islands; or
- (c) any British overseas territory,

the methods of service permitted by paragraphs (1)(b) and (2) are not available and the party[^key-eb201e385bae473523af133123216c54] or the party’s[^key-7f33ef799c82e3f4287f83b23b171a21] agent must effect service direct, unless Practice Direction 6B[^key-b433a488cda9c4bac682a233f4802d35] provides otherwise.

(A list of British overseas territories is reproduced in paragraph 5.2 of Practice Direction 6B[^key-3b21e1a3569c6377f22618f42d646707].)
