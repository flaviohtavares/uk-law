# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 6.6

## 6.6. Where to serve the claim form – general provisions[^key-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The claim form must be served within the jurisdiction except where rule 6.7(2)[^key-c741e6bb9ab5adb1a518732175a567d5] [^key-ef8221e171001ae5d5c4340ca06741df]... or 6.11 applies or as provided by Section IV of this Part.

(2) The claimant must include in the claim form an address at which the defendant may be served. That address must include a full postcode [^key-8e812193bf38fed4d20236969426a614]..., unless the court orders otherwise.

[^key-1aa07dc1b9a9ba6a6574c658b6645652]...

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply where an order made by the court under rule 6.15 (service by an alternative method or at an alternative place) specifies the place or method of service of the claim form.
