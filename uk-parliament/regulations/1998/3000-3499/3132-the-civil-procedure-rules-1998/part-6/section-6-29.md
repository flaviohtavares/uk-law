## 6.29. Certificate of service[^key-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a rule, practice direction or court order requires a certificate of service, the certificate must state the details required by the following table—

| Method of Service | Details to be certified |
| --- | --- |
| - 1. Personal service<br>- 2. First class post, document exchange or other service which provides for delivery on the next business day | Date and time of personal service.<br>Date of posting, or leaving with, delivering to or collection by the relevant service provider. |
| 3\. Delivery of document to or leaving it at a permitted place | Date and time of when the document was delivered to or left at the permitted place. |
| 4\. Fax | Date and time of completion of the transmission. |
| 5\. Other electronic method | Date and time of sending the e-mail or other electronic transmission. |
| 6\. Alternative method or place permitted by the court | As required by the court. |
