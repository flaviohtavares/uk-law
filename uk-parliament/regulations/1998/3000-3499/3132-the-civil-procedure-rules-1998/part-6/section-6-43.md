## 6.43. Procedure where service is to be through foreign governments, judicial authorities and British Consular authorities[^c-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where a party[^c-7f9fc5b759bd1c3983c1654564d32707] wishes to serve a claim form or any other document under rule 6.42(1) or 6.42(2).

(2) Where this rule applies, that party[^c-911d4f0ed3e3a42eb1e509f1de6d0f19] must file—

- (a) a request for service of the claim form or other document specifying one or more of the methods in rule 6.42(1) or 6.42(2);
- (b) a copy of the claim form or other document;
- (c) any other documents or copies of documents required by Practice Direction 6B[^c-4760e70d4cdfd88796e2373fe571a493]; and
- (d) any translation required under rule 6.45.

(3) Where a party[^c-20c80c7f8da1c666782c1726b3f45cf4] files the documents specified in paragraph (2), the court officer will—

- (a) seal (GL) the copy of the claim form or other document; and
- (b) forward the documents to the Senior Master.

(4) The Senior Master will send documents forwarded under this rule—

- (a) where the claim form or other document is being served through the authority designated under the Hague Convention or any other Civil Procedure Convention or Treaty[^c-0c6e41202742c454093e208c3962c36d], to that authority; or
- (b) in any other case, to the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office[^c-cf4a638a5f7986dbd4afba837a3a5dd0] with a request that it arranges for the claim form or other document to be served.

(5) An official certificate which—

- (a) states that the method requested under paragraph (2)(a) has been performed and the date of such performance;
- (b) states, where more than one method is requested under paragraph (2)(a), which method was used; and
- (c) is made by—
  - (i) a British Consular authority in the country where the method requested under paragraph (2)(a) was performed;
  - (ii) the government or judicial authorities in that country; or
  - (iii) the authority designated in respect of that country under the Hague Convention a Civil Procedure Convention or Treaty[^c-1cbc8075d102f323d0aae34a00ea6158],

is evidence of the facts stated in the certificate.

(6) A document purporting to be an official certificate under paragraph (5) is to be treated as such a certificate, unless it is proved not to be.
