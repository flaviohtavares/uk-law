## 6.46. Undertaking to be responsible for expenses[^key-fe589970343ba157e4f46d596d14d251]

*Extent: E+W*

Every request for service filed under rule 6.43 (service through foreign governments, judicial authorities etc.) or rule 6.44 (service of claim form or other document on a State) must contain an undertaking by the person making the request—

- (a) to be responsible for all expenses incurred by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office[^key-6a9550ae4e1297587d6b5766b1beba41] or foreign judicial authority; and
- (b) to pay those expenses to the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office[^key-30eadd0f37e5a8254deaf03397b6a50a] or foreign judicial authority on being informed of the amount.
