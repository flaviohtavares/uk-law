# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 72.1

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72.1. Scope of this Part and interpretation[^key-43d16a20b9266fc6aff0cdddcda9a193][^key-5b372c69a116c8b4f0123d1bfd64d5d4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part contains rules which provide for a judgment creditor to obtain an order for the payment to him of money which a third party who is within the jurisdiction owes to the judgment debtor.

(2) In this Part, “bank or building society” includes any person carrying on a business in the course of which he lawfully accepts deposits in the United Kingdom[^M_F_8dd8baa8-8d5e-4ef1-f9e8-84a1afe30ae1].
