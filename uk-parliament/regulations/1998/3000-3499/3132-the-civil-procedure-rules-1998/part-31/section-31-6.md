## 31.6. Standard disclosure—what documents are to be disclosed[^c-70514842ac1e8ff87d1187102bb8b1b4]

*Extent: E+W*

Standard disclosure requires a party to disclose only—

- (a) the documents on which they rely[^c-fcfcd1d4f7df4016166e7585013f84af]; and
- (b) the documents which—
  - (i) adversely affect their[^c-75d0386aa9fe79bfd750163765d24ba0] own case;
  - (ii) adversely affect another party’s case; or
  - (iii) support another party’s case; and
- (c) the documents which they are[^c-60f264bb18833344ceac7e7937571170] required to disclose by a relevant practice direction.
