## 31.15. Inspection and copying of documents[^c-994460f4c161d32244aec70627ad990a]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a party has a right to inspect a document—

- (a) that party must give the party who disclosed the document written notice of their[^c-5e22bf257cb162a072a34a9a437adac2] wish to inspect it;
- (b) the party who disclosed the document must permit inspection not more than 7 days after the date on which they[^c-cc44bb706a36babca9a998c43417fce6] received the notice; and
- (c) that party may request a copy of the document and, if they also undertake[^c-af85654dea85c43f365d762c46f60522] to pay reasonable copying costs, the party who disclosed the document must supply them[^c-82ad06e99e542c82ef608b88eec58e44] with a copy not more than 7 days after the date on which they received[^c-0f2fbf65505be4fdc6f211ce1140a6f7] the request.

(Rule 31.3 and 31.14 deal with the right of a party to inspect a document)
