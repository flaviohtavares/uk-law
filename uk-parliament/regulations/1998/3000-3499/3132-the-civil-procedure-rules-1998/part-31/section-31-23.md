## 31.23. False, disclosure statements[^c-e568370da5772507b8d721e866a1b3ad]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Proceedings for contempt of court may be brought against a person if they make, or cause[^c-1f6478df78a1dc0ae1edda63d0f139e6] to be made, a false disclosure statement, without an honest belief in its truth.
