## 31.8. Duty of disclosure limited to documents which are or have been in party’s control[^c-3f4a59c45b84033acaac40032a1920b5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party’s duty to disclose documents is limited to documents which are or have been in their[^c-8c6621aaa373fd781b7e105a5ba8ce22] control.

(2) For this purpose a party has or has had a document in their control if—

- (a) it is or was in their physical possession;
- (b) they have or have had[^c-8ca53ce84f956fdd331158c814f51929] a right to possession of it; or
- (c) they have or have had a right to inspect or take copies of it.
