## 31.20. Restriction on use of a privileged document inspection of which has been inadvertently allowed[^c-61060c2b5a79356db54de47ff4c5e281]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a party inadvertently allows a privileged<sup>(GL)</sup> document to be inspected, the party who has inspected the document may use it or its contents only with the permission of the court.
