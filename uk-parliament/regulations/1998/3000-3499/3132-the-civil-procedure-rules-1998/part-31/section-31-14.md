## 31.14. Documents referred to in statements of case etc.[^c-b29dcdd1c8877d0202773342cbf05014]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-705db4fad63a50c8bb29e7cfc46be8b5] A party may inspect a document mentioned in—

- (a) a statement of case;
- (b) a witness statement;
- (c) a witness summary; or[^c-ee2610d8dea68add769904a3ea492f97]
- (d) an affidavit<sup>(GL)</sup>.[^c-74d4236fc87d229793f48dbc3bc93292]
- (e)[^c-3a8a9dbc590ec39cc12353ad8e0ae0e8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(Rule 35.10(4) makes provision in relation to instructions referred to in an expert’s report)

(2)[^c-8cd0004a3a40c671389421ee76a33468] Subject to rule 35.10(4), a party may apply for an order for inspection of any document mentioned in an expert’s report which has not already been disclosed in the proceedings.
