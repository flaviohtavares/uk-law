# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 42.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

## 42.1. Solicitor acting for a party[^key-2fe6e088100b62e6674df8e6738d8ca4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-966737a3859dda7fbe4c404068127312] Where the address for service of a party is the business address of that party’s[^key-fd07cfcb7a6c0398c155741ceaf9b237] solicitor, the solicitor will be considered to be acting for that party until the provisions of this Part have been complied with.

(2)[^key-a86b429bb329bdd12a07ade6122b0273] For the purposes of this Part, “solicitor” has the meaning set out in rule 6.2(d).

(Part 6 contains provisions about the address for service)
