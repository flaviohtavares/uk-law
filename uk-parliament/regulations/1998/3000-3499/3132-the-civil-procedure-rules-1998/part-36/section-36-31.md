# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 36.31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36.31. Deduction of benefits[^M_F_cef712d6-cc8c-40af-fea8-7b01e51792d6][^c-e9908447b00f164a4af1faf75a60688f]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of rule 36.30[^M_F_a6f1e1b1-0343-43fa-aa80-7ce1a9ac2fc3](1)(a) the amount of the judgment is less than the Protocol offer where the judgment is less than that offer once deductible amounts identified in the judgment are deducted.

(“Deductible amount” is defined in rule 36.20[^M_F_6a1ac481-7333-4f70-d2a6-50df2ed9fbad](1)(d).)
