# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 36.28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36.28. General provisions[^M_F_a156877b-813d-48c9-c285-e2aa70472cfc][^c-e9908447b00f164a4af1faf75a60688f]

*Extent: E+W*

A Protocol offer—

- (a) is treated as exclusive of all interest; and
- (b) has the consequences set out in this Section only in relation to the fixed costs of the Stage 3 Procedure as provided for in rule 45.29[^c-b2ef4e1d7d12d57cc06936855969f947], and not in relation to the costs of any appeal from the final decision of those proceedings.
