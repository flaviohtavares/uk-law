# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 36.29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36.29. Restrictions on the disclosure of a Protocol offer[^M_F_ff7dc15b-13a4-4eb8-ee0d-59768a0f687c][^key-e9908447b00f164a4af1faf75a60688f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The amount of the Protocol offer must not be communicated to the court until the claim is determined.

(2) Any other offer to settle must not be communicated to the court at all.
