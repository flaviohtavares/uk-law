## 69.10. Application for discharge of receiver[^key-76e09c6173a9121b7a071a713ba7dc87]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-8989e96263942a4f1cf6862a918d944c] A receiver or any party may apply for the receiver to be discharged on completion of his duties.

(2)[^key-cf49621357b73cea82f59e0d46fe4de7] The application notice must be served on the persons who were required under rule 69.4 to be served with the order appointing the receiver.
