# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 69.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69.1. Scope of this Part[^c-76e09c6173a9121b7a071a713ba7dc87]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part contains provisions about the court’s power to appoint a receiver.

(2) In this Part “receiver” includes a manager.
