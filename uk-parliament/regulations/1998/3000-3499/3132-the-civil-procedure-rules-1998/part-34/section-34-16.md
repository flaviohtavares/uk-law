## 34.16. Scope and interpretation[^c-d1f937413395e3e7c414da0fc9956eb4][^c-7b31d96dde400f1126f0b3e55e1bc6a8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to an application for an order under the 1975 Act for evidence to be obtained[^c-b57a583e134e7cfd863dc2603a73dbd7]....

(2) In this Section—

- (a) “the 1975 Act” means the Evidence (Proceedings in Other Jurisdictions) Act 1975 ; and[^c-7ec3350e08fda18c6e2977a8abdc2612]
- (b) “the Hague Evidence Convention” means the Hague Convention of 18 March 1970 on the Taking of Evidence Abroad in Civil or Commercial Matters[^c-7e3a4e5c44b707a2d921b532d1a9c1eb]
