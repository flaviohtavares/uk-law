## 79.14B. Modification of Part 52 (appeals)[^key-95c5663132a3644de62b054f08b40aae][^key-a1accc8b3c27de745fb74740b9e24225]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 52 (appeals) applies to an appeal under section 26 of the 2010 Act subject to—

- (a) rule 79.2;
- (b) Section 4 of this Part; and
- (c) the modifications set out in paragraph (2).

(2) Rule 52.2 (parties to comply with Practice Directions 52A to 52E) apply[^key-04f15a746ab592dca773c2c012cf4006], but the parties are not required to comply with paragraphs 5.1 to 5.3 of Practice Direction 52A and paragraphs 6.3 to 6.6 of Practice Direction 52B[^key-cf58942066c2ccb9812b41971f9f996c].
