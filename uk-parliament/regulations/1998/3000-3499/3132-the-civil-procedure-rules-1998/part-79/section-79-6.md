## 79.6. Application to set aside[^key-a1accc8b3c27de745fb74740b9e24225]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application to set aside a financial restrictions decision or sanctions decision[^key-068ec5f6e0219fc545a4b15515513971] must be started by a claim form.

(2) The claim form must set out—

- (a) the details of the financial restrictions decision or sanctions decision[^key-63bd9ae76206d3e639d186b3b76c0663];
- (b) details of how the claimant is affected by the financial restrictions decision or sanctions decision[^key-b2a93949091a345e6fa44303adc68f27]; and
- (c) the grounds on which the claimant seeks to set aside the decision.

(3) The claimant must file with the claim form—

- (a) a copy of—
  - (i) the written notice of the relevant financial restrictions decision or sanctions decision[^key-4a28e32fe3020e850b4d574c0432f0b4] made by the appropriate Minister[^key-f1e5c1da234a538538645b107d931355]; or
  - (ii) where relevant, any direction, order or licence made under Schedule 7 to the 2008[^key-5341e618a44e5bbf324fa8b88bdaf31b] Act or any freezing order made under Part 2 of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001; and
- (b) any evidence, including witness statements, on which the claimant relies at that stage.
