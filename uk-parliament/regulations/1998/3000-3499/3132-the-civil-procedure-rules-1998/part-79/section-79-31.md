## 79.31. Applications for a notification order[^c-8871c0cf1a15d97b94a1fd02b5af16b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for a notification order under Schedule 4 to the 2008[^c-a41061d36fd5dfb255c4e5229c0505ad] Act must be made in accordance with Part 8.

(2) Where the defendant wishes to serve a notice under paragraph 2(4) of Schedule 4 to the 2008[^c-67bbcb56e57e4103ea20a647c0dbf5bd] Act, the defendant must file and serve the notice with an acknowledgment of service not more than 14 days after service of the claim form.
