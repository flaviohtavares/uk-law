# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 79.22

## 79.22. Modification of the general rules of evidence and disclosure[^c-a1accc8b3c27de745fb74740b9e24225]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 31 (disclosure and inspection of documents), Part 32 (evidence) and Part 33 (miscellaneous rules about evidence) do not apply to any proceedings to which Section 2 or 3 of[^c-e178b0d0d559499f54a026711d2c04f4] this Part applies.

(2) Subject to the other rules in Section 2, 3 and this Section of[^c-fcaebc54d024206877f6d2c18822160f] this Part and to any directions of the court, the evidence of a witness may be given either—

- (a) orally before the court; or
- (b) in a witness statement.

(3) The court may also receive evidence in documentary or any other form.

(4) A party is entitled to adduce evidence and to cross-examine witnesses during any part of a hearing from which a party and that party’s legal representative are not excluded.

(4A)[^c-333c63cf308e8ec8784c7774a3809663] A special advocate is entitled to adduce evidence and to cross-examine witnesses.

(5) The court may require a witness to give evidence on oath or by affirmation.
