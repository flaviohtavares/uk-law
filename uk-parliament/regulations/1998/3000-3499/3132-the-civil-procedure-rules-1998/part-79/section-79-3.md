# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 79.3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

## 79.3. Scope of this Section[^key-a1accc8b3c27de745fb74740b9e24225]

*Extent: E+W*

This Section applies to an application to set aside a financial restrictions decision under section 63(2) of the 2008 Act or section 27(2) of the 2010 Act[^key-e70dc3bb06c2068d4f2c1b4b742df4d9] or a sanctions decision under section 38(2) of the 2018 Act[^key-a0d3155142b4b705ddc54d74b029d484].
