## 79.18. Appointment of a special advocate[^c-a1accc8b3c27de745fb74740b9e24225]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the appropriate Minister[^c-c7e3e4a5c9a76505f73bd9bf68d284c8] must immediately give notice of the proceedings to the Attorney General—

- (a) upon being served with any claim form, application notice or appeal notice; or
- (b) where the appropriate Minister intends[^c-73110c17371cbc1d4126644af09a717f] to file an appeal notice,

in proceedings to which Section 2 or 3 of[^c-59ca09b9f8b8abc95c4e9f58f98d67c1] this Part applies.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies unless—

- (a) the appropriate Minister does[^c-3377a37874808d12ef3519c85a117262] not intend to—
  - (i) oppose the claim, application or appeal; or
  - (ii) apply for permission to withhold closed material from a party and that party’s legal representative; or
- (b) a special advocate has already been appointed to represent the interests of a party other than the appropriate Minister and that special advocate is not prevented from communicating with that party by virtue of rule 79.20.

(3) Where any proceedings to which Section 2 or 3 of[^c-0c364686f362a4757dc01e9fe56fe670] this Part applies are pending but no special advocate has been appointed, any party may request the Attorney General to appoint a special advocate.
