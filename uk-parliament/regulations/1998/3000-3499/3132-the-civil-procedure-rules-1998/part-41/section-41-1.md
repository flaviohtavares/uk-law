# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 41.1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41.1. Application and definitions[^c-6f6a4a72c3a8392aa0ac740f6bd83c0e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section of this Part[^c-b44feaf5341fe77f04436976cb38af7b] applies to proceedings to which SCA s.32A or CCA s.51 applies.

(2) In this Section[^c-7648ed98c853873133b131af8429c92f]—

- (a) “SCA s.32A” means section 32A of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00052];
- (b) “CCA s.51” means section 51 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00053]; and
- (c) “**award of provisional damages**” means an award of damages for personal injuries under which—
  - (i) damages are assessed on the assumption referred to in SCA s.32A or CCA s.51 that the injured person will not develop the disease or suffer the deterioration; and
  - (ii) the injured person is entitled to apply for further damages at a future date if he develops the disease or suffers the deterioration.
