# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 19.11

## 19.11. Postal Services Act 2000[^M_F_aa121592-79ef-45b8-c64d-c9fdf727399d][^c-f81645039664bf70184edcf2dc4f1093]

*Extent: E+W*

A copy of an application notice under section 92 of the Postal Services Act 2000 for permission to bring proceedings in the name of the sender or addressee of a postal packet or their personal representative must be served on the universal service provider and on the person in whose name the applicant seeks to bring the proceedings.
