> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19.3. Provisions applicable where two or more persons are jointly entitled to a remedy[^key-f81645039664bf70184edcf2dc4f1093]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) All persons[^key-be0d91d5efff79e0d4780fcfb8d4c2b6] jointly entitled to the remedy claimed by a claimant[^key-577005bc7ff17633ded7f62921dbcff0] must be parties unless the court orders otherwise.

(2) If any such[^key-5a1f06d64969c81b8bff28746b80c334] person does not agree to be a claimant, he must be made a defendant, unless the court orders otherwise.

(3) This rule does not apply in probate proceedings.
