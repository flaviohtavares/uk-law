# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 19.18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19.18. Derivative claims arising in the course of other proceedings[^M_F_c983a2b5-b9a5-49f1-fa64-feae4864e21e][^key-6c58b0869f0ebc19dbb7b6951a04bc4a][^key-f81645039664bf70184edcf2dc4f1093]

*Extent: E+W*

If a derivative claim (except such a claim in pursuance of an order under section 996[^key-5a10107fb3078afaa4a91472b8e43764] of the Companies Act 2006) arises in the course of other proceedings—

- (a) in the case of a derivative claim under Chapter 1 of Part 11 of that Act, rule 19.15 or 19.16[^M_F_50f43f63-b869-45c4-b56f-f4f44ae9d876] applies, as the case requires; and
- (b) in any other case, rule 19.17[^M_F_050f2aad-32e0-48d7-985c-480b51841cd1] applies.
