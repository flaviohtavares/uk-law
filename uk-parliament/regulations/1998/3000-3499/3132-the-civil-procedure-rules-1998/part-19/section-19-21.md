# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 19.21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19.21. Definition[^M_F_0697880d-690c-4469-ab8a-c57e13f58eb1][^c-6c58b0869f0ebc19dbb7b6951a04bc4a][^c-f81645039664bf70184edcf2dc4f1093]

*Extent: E+W*

A Group Litigation Order (“GLO”) means an order made under rule 19.22[^M_F_b4aac6c9-a56d-49f0-a8ba-31aadd312af1] to provide for the case management of claims which give rise to common or related issues of fact or law (the “GLO issues”).
