# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 19.22

## 19.22. Group Litigation Order[^M_F_88812afe-547a-4f72-8ef0-63bd300ace07][^c-6c58b0869f0ebc19dbb7b6951a04bc4a][^c-f81645039664bf70184edcf2dc4f1093]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may make a GLO where there are or are likely to be a number of claims giving rise to the GLO issues. The multiple parties may be claimants or defendants.[^M_F_e3b70960-56fc-4936-959d-786fe72b87e7]

(Practice Direction 19B[^c-e13b3eb3b27a8eccc8bf1f080209e04f] provides the procedure for applying for a GLO where the multiple parties are claimants[^M_F_ce115422-b351-4237-bbf2-176afe9cfb2c])

(2) A GLO must—

- (a) contain directions about the establishment of a register (the “group register”) on which the claims managed under the GLO will be entered;
- (b) specify the GLO issues which will identify the claims to be managed as a group under the GLO; [^M_F_e68fb557-ccfe-4cdb-fb07-be032bfdc4e2]...
- (c) specify the court (the “management court”) which will manage the claims on the group register ; and[^M_F_3194d6f3-41a0-41c8-b7db-50b964565e61]
- (d)[^M_F_f0a25eee-0938-4586-a070-b2b422b06cea] be made in the King’s Bench Division with the consent of the President of the King’s Bench Division; in the Business and Property Division[^c-e2a2ccbb968ef7068a0d54d3e294c3cb] with the consent of the President of the Business and Property Division[^c-e5fa8b261b1af0c050c262ed802ddaf7]; or in the County Court with the consent of the Head of Civil Justice. Such consent will be sought by the court to which the application for the GLO is made.

(3) A GLO may—

- (a) in relation to claims which raise one or more of the GLO issues—
  - (i) direct their transfer to the management court;
  - (ii) order their stay <sup>(gl)</sup> until further order; and
  - (iii) direct their entry on the group register;
- (b) direct that from a specified date claims which raise one or more of the GLO issues should be started in the management court and entered on the group register; and
- (c) give directions for publicising the GLO.
