# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 82.5

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82.5. Case management[^key-c2b447dcce072522f2e795ce120ccaaf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-74d04cb4c53a17b26e0b6494cdeabf62] Proceedings to which this Section applies are to be treated as allocated (or, as the case may be, re-allocated) to the multi-track.

(2)[^key-28c17d1d8e4fee7d5b4df1af37a1103f] Subject to paragraph (3), Section II of Part 3 does not apply to proceedings in which a declaration has been made under section 6(2) of the Act.

(3) When a declaration is made under section 6(2) of the Act—

- (a) a costs management order that has already been made shall not apply to any costs incurred after the declaration is made; and
- (b) the court may make any other order in relation to costs incurred before the declaration is made that it considers appropriate.
