## 82.27. Scope of this Section[^key-c2b447dcce072522f2e795ce120ccaaf]

*Extent: E+W*

This Section contains rules about—

- (a) revocation—
  - (i) of the court’s own motion; or
  - (ii) on application,

  of a declaration made under section 6 of the Act; and
- (b) the court’s formal review of such a declaration.
