# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 40.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40.1. Scope of this section[^key-2e9af39ee079be4bab75a356b4898c7f]

*Extent: E+W*

This Section sets out rules about judgments and orders which apply except where any other of these Rules or a practice direction[^key-8d7addf68f1f5705bff6daab5350a334] makes a different provision in relation to the judgment or order in question.
