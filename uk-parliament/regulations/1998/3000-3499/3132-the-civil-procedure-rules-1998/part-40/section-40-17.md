# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 40.17

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40.17. Power to order delivery up of possession etc.[^key-09b9b75304f39c9e89e5a0ac018b0c78]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the court has made an order under rule 40.16, it may order any party to deliver up to the purchaser or any other person—

- (a) possession of the land;
- (aa)[^key-7d0595899922ec4110be91da0939dba3] where the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 applies, possession of the dwelling;
- (b) receipt of rents or profits relating to it; or
- (c) both.
