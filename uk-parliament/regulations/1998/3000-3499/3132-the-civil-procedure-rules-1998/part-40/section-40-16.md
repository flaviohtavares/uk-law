## 40.16. Power to order sale etc.[^key-09b9b75304f39c9e89e5a0ac018b0c78]

*Extent: E+W*

In any proceedings relating to land, the court may order the land, or part of it, to be—

- (a) sold;
- (b) mortgaged;
- (c) exchanged; or
- (d) partitioned.
