## 2.6. Court documents to be sealed[^key-49eec2d9b823549dbde72c9a4b89dbe6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court must seal<sup>(GL)</sup> the following documents on issue—

- (a) the claim form; and
- (b) any other document which a rule or practice direction requires it to seal.

(2)[^key-375169ef39cdace3d6c8a59d669da685] The court may place the seal on the document by hand, by printing or electronically.

(3) A document appearing[^key-8ad0e0528faf819f008a653fd795b72e] to bear the court’s seal<sup>(GL)</sup> shall be admissible in evidence without further proof.
