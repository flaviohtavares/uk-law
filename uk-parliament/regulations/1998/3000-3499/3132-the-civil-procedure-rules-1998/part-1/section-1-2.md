## 1.2. Application by the court of the overriding objective[^key-bc505485cd21ce9e5be42ccd9e4734ac]

*Extent: E+W*

The court must seek to give effect to the overriding objective when it—

- (a) exercises any power given to it by the Rules; or
- (b) interprets any rule , subject to[^key-b312f9a68fb787871a7f92e757b6af32] rules 76.2, 79.2[^key-cf61a75eded902f41925cccdbd46565d] , 80.2[^key-bb6273602053a64331ed68f4b7a77368] , 82.2 and 88.2[^key-6f97b21c9b332d8563bd1b5ad883591f].
