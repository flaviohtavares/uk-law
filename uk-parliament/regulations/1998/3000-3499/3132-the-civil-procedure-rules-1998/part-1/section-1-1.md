## 1.1. The overriding objective[^key-d03bb77cdffc1cb19d1fe3324724fdf3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Rules are a [^key-aa90477e70f58f9ed6c6a8d802e70ce2]... procedural code with the overriding objective of enabling the court to deal with cases justly and at proportionate cost[^key-1850248d41a570988c5e317175bc4731].

(2) Dealing with a case justly and at proportionate cost[^key-0b18126aa0d38391b7c0059119429655] includes, so far as is practicable—

- (a) ensuring that the parties are on an equal footing and can participate fully in proceedings, and that parties and witnesses can give their best evidence[^key-a71944c1907a59381a37a12166a5af62];
- (b) saving expense;
- (c) dealing with the case in ways which are proportionate—
  - (i) to the amount of money involved;
  - (ii) to the importance of the case;
  - (iii) to the complexity of the issues; and
  - (iv) to the financial position of each party;
- (d) ensuring that it is dealt with expeditiously and fairly; [^key-0a0e41c7ac846a94d4e1498d26acde0d]...
- (e) allotting to it an appropriate share of the court’s resources, while taking into account the need to allot resources to other cases; [^key-2e303d02d133b50afb56dc0278de5797]...
- (f)[^key-18808ef7c62d0bf4888be23c7cde5ddf] promoting or using alternative dispute resolution<sup></sup>;
- (g)[^key-151588d7dddb76a1b6e5c09e0e3acc05][^key-2b370ccecb82cb5212525659b2fbb327] enforcing compliance with rules, practice directions and orders.
