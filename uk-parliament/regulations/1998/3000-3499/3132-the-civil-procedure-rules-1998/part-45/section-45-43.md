## 45.43. Scope[^c-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to any claim which would normally be or is allocated to the fast track.

(2) This Section does not apply where Section IV, Section VII or Section VIII of this Part applies.
