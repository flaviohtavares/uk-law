## 45.65. Court’s power to direct assessment in conjunction with fixed costs determination[^key-eb3add40cf6a85ce59c922d22c4a8569]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, in any claim to which this Section applies, a party seeks an assessment of costs under rule 45.9, rule 45.10 or rule 45.50(3)—

- (a) that party must notify the court; and
- (b) the court may give such directions as it thinks appropriate for those costs to be assessed in conjunction with the fixed costs determination.
