## 45.61. Disbursements – Section VIII[^c-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to rule 45.15A[^c-bb836c80920fdb97e6c5a40eef724f85], in a claim to which Section VIII applies, the court may allow a claim for a disbursement of a type mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b)[^c-bdab37667b76ecdd48b8581d3988bf83] —

- (a) the fees for the intended trial advocate for any step reasonably undertaken where both the use of that advocate and the cost are justified; and
- (b) any other disbursement which has been reasonably incurred.
