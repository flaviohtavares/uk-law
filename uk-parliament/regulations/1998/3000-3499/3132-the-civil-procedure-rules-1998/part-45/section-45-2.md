# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 45.2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45.2. VAT[^c-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

Where appropriate, VAT may be recovered in addition to the amount of fixed costs in rules 45.8 and 45.15A and[^c-429ab680443fef9e6d2e45d52b23251a] Section IV, Section VI, Section VII ,[^c-6d5481f17a6a309455d5eb4f8f825563] Section VIII or Section X[^c-93e263df6677bd0dcee2a37d8d5f317e] of this Part and any reference to fixed costs in those Sections is a reference to those costs net of any such VAT.
