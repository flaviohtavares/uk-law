## 45.49. Scope[^key-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section sets out the costs which are to be allowed in any claim which would normally be or is allocated to the intermediate track.

(2) This Section does not apply where Section IV, Section VI or Section VIII of this Part applies.
