# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 45.37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45.37. Where the claimant obtains judgment for an amount equal to or less than the defendant’s relevant Protocol offer[^key-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

Where rule 36.30[^key-a0a82f95bc7e96222ec5c4207ff5d53c](1)(a) applies, the court shall order the claimant to pay

- (a) where the claim is determined—
  - (i) on the papers, Stage 3 Type A fixed costs; or
  - (i) at a hearing, Stage 3 Type A and B fixed costs;
- (b) any Stage 3 disbursements allowed in accordance with Section IX of this Part.
