# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 45.57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45.57. Scope of this section[^c-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section sets out the disbursements which are to be allowed in any claim to which Section IV**,** Section VI, Section VII or Section VIII of this Part applies.

(2) Subject to rule 45.62, the court—

- (a) may allow a claim for a disbursement of a type mentioned in rules 45.58, 45.59, 45.60 and 45.61, as applicable, and where it does so, the amount to be allowed is [^c-09ade715aa9317c8069e96f12621f659]... that which is reasonable and proportionate; but
- (b) shall not allow a claim for any other type of disbursement.

(3) Where appropriate, VAT may be recovered in addition to the cost of any disbursement allowable under this Section.
