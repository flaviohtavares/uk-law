## 45.27. Scope and interpretation[^key-8ed78e3eff468fc080a342c5659897de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to claims that have been or should have been started under Part 8 in accordance with Practice Direction 49F (‘the Stage 3 Procedure’).

(2) A reference to Claim Notification Form or Court Proceedings Pack is a reference to the form used in the relevant Protocol.
