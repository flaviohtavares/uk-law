## 25.7. Evidence[^key-7acf1619a8fbf92164f493742b9dd8de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Applications must be supported by evidence in the following form unless the court, a rule, a practice direction or an enactment requires an affidavit or affirmation—

- (a) a witness statement;
- (b) a statement of case; or
- (c) the application.

(2) The evidence must set out all relevant facts.
