## 54.4. Permission required[^c-fcc22b3d606b9d4d539ffc7c2ca9b2d2]

*Extent: E+W*

The court’s permission to proceed is required in a claim for judicial review whether started under this Section[^c-718a1f67522894e0f88318a0bc4033c7] or transferred to the Administrative Court.
