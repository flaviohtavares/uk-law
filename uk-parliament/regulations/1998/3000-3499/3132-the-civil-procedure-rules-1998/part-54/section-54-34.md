## 54.34. Where claimant seeks to rely on additional grounds[^key-e179eeac3b27308049ac6a3b16f7f6b0][^key-fcc22b3d606b9d4d539ffc7c2ca9b2d2]

*Extent: E+W*

The court’s permission is required if the claimant seeks to rely on grounds other than those set out in the original claim.
