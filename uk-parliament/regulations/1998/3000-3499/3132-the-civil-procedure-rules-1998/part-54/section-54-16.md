> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54.16. Evidence[^c-fcc22b3d606b9d4d539ffc7c2ca9b2d2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Rule 8.6 (1)[^c-5f3387e7f436a743c96add5b0481ac72] does not apply.

(2) No written evidence may be relied on unless—

- (a) it has been served in accordance with any—
  - (i) rule under this Section[^c-0d68199f2a1e5c302c0f22bb985ac32a]; or
  - (ii) direction of the court; or
- (b) the court gives permission.
