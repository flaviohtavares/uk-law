## 54.22. Specialist list[^key-8f130802f5d5257bf42485e4979dec26][^key-fcc22b3d606b9d4d539ffc7c2ca9b2d2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Planning Court claims form a specialist list.

(2) A judge nominated by the President of the King’s[^key-d693b98802f5e19d6691be13f3eb84c4] Bench Division will be in charge of the Planning Court specialist list and will be known as the Planning Liaison Judge.

(3)[^key-d0977e2c9cef16d448d7f8a44b653a65] The President of the King’s[^key-b78bbc06551ac56c3ac027b034928d04] Bench Division will be responsible for the nomination of specialist planning judges to deal with Planning Court claims which are significant within the meaning of Practice Direction 54D[^key-d75b1a396bcf4354963ce58152048125], and of other judges to deal with other Planning Court claims.
