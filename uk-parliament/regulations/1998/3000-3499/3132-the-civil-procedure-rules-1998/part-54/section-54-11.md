## 54.11. Service of order giving or refusing permission[^c-fcc22b3d606b9d4d539ffc7c2ca9b2d2]

*Extent: E+W*

The court will serve—

- (a) the order giving or refusing permission; and
- (ai)[^c-e93299c5dad24aaa9a5379bf80da3165] any certificate (if not included in the order) that permission has been granted for reasons of exceptional public interest in accordance with section 31(3F) of the Senior Courts Act 1981; and
- (b) any directions,

on—

- (i) the claimant;
- (ii) the defendant; and
- (iii) any other person who filed an acknowledgment of service.
