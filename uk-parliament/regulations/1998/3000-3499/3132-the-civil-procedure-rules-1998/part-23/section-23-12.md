# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 23.12

## 23.12. Applications that are totally without merit[^c-b4d0a54e6dd62f2e92faf6ca13497d4c][^c-27e35432ee090c3e21d9f780675f6114]

*Extent: E+W*

If the court dismisses an application (including an application for permission to appeal or for permission to apply for judicial review)[^c-f1d9cb940b419f625f863b79b125281b] and it considers that the application is totally without merit—

- (a) the court’s order must record that fact; and
- (b) the court must at the same time consider whether it is appropriate to make a civil restraint order.
