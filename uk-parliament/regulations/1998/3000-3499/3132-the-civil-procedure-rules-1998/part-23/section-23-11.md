## 23.11. Power of the court to proceed in the absence of a party[^c-0555682f26d0dd329589da09c9e3036d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the applicant or any respondent fails to attend the hearing of an application, the court may proceed in their[^c-76eaa1a9a1666ec4dd1dff2ff8a97d2a] absence.

(2) Where—

- (a) the applicant or any respondent fails to attend the hearing of an application; and
- (b) the court makes an order at the hearing,

the court may, on application or of its own initiative, re-list the application.

(Part 40 deals with service of orders)
