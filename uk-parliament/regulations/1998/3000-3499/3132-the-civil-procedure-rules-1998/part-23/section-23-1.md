## 23.1. Definitions[^key-fec1f4620f554b5076d153a01b243258][^key-75ff8b1442168b14b676a10d11f827bc]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**application notice**” means a document in which the applicant states their[^key-cefba8479d33a88570bef5443de97092] intention to seek a court order; [^key-fcdf400f6b7aa22a62cd5c207ce139e0]...
- “[^key-8bc6eb4dfefc19c15fa2262cd15c8398]**hearing**” means the occasion on which any interim or final decision is or may be made by a judge, at which a person is, or has a right to be, heard in person, by telephone, by video or by any other means which permits simultaneous communication; and
- “**respondent**” means—
  - (a) the person against whom the order is sought; and
  - (b) such other person as the court may direct.
