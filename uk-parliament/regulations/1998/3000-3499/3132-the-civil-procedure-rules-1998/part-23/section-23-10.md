## 23.10. Application to set aside or vary order made without notice[^key-74dc0f7135f3d470544cecc05a5c5a79]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-de308e57432c4bc5775c614526cda241] A person who was not served with a copy of the application notice before an order was made under rule 23.9 may apply to have the order set aside <sup>(gl)</sup> or varied.

(2) An application under this rule must , unless the court directs otherwise,[^key-486e55a55c284433d6c9cbb71a5290a1] be made within 7 days after the date on which the order was served on the person making the application.
