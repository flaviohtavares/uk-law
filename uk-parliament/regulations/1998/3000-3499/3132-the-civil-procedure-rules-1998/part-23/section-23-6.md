## 23.6. What an application notice must include[^key-f7a8e9aacbce3dd5903ecac0a42be794]

*Extent: E+W*

An application notice must state—

- (a) what order the applicant is seeking; and
- (b) briefly, why the applicant is seeking the order.

(Part 22 requires an application notice to be verified by a statement of truth if the applicant wishes to rely on matters set out in the[^key-c10799c041e5424f7095e83401224d9b] application notice as evidence)
