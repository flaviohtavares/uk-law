## 23.5. Time when an application is made[^c-ae78f835a4da5081d4db756fdd8740fe]

*Extent: E+W*

Where an application must be made within a specified time, it is made in time[^c-d5ddf3bf35c6aed8b2a5eca1d2546b7d] if the application notice is received by the court within that time.
