## 44.5. Amount of costs where costs are payable under a contract[^c-b941119c8cdc9da1893b8454ae2b36e6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3)[^c-8c52cf23d18c079896da0f3ce33465c0], where the court assesses (whether by summary or detailed assessment) costs which are payable by the paying party to the receiving party under the terms of a contract, the costs payable under those terms are, unless the contract expressly provides otherwise, to be presumed to be costs which—

- (a) have been reasonably incurred; and
- (b) are reasonable in amount,

and the court will assess them accordingly.

(2) The presumptions in paragraph (1) are rebuttable. Practice Direction 44 – General rules about costs sets out circumstances where the court may order otherwise.

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply where the contract is between a solicitor and client.
