## 76.12. Modification of Part 52 (appeals)[^key-aff08cb562443cad7c83cb0da0e8e7a6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 52 (appeals) applies to an appeal under section 10 of the Act, subject to—

- (a) rule 76.2;
- (b) the rules in section 5 of this Part; and
- (c) the modifications set out in paragraphs (2) and (3) of this rule.

(2) The following rules do not apply to appeals under section 10 of the Act—

- (a) rules 52.3, 52.4 and 52.6[^key-c41ec817516e2f2a9408eff1b968f77f] (permission);
- (b) 52.12[^key-02e7b061c059755986e266c46069a8d1] (appellant’s notice);
- (c) 52.13[^key-c98fc27ce4d27da4a7f748f61e86cee2] (respondent’s notice); and
- (d) rule 52.21[^key-170f90e209db59204e94240ba1d4a489] (hearing of appeals).

(3) Rule 52.2 (all parties to comply with Practice Directions 52A to 52E) apply[^key-701598e13700afb9cc0cfa6d13ff8837], but the parties shall not be required to comply with paragraphs 5.1 to 5.3 of Practice Direction 52A and paragraphs 6.3 to 6.6 of Practice Direction 52B[^key-1f1425ec1f0ae317b1321c72edb66510].
