## 76.34. Supply of court documents[^key-aff08cb562443cad7c83cb0da0e8e7a6]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise directs, rule 5.4 (Register of Claims), rule 5.4B (Supply of documents from court records – a party) and rule 5.4C (Supply of documents from court records – a non-party) do[^key-db6d672bb0db662c25973ac6351b5027] not apply to any proceedings to which this Part applies.
