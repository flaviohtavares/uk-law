## 20.6. Defendant’s additional claim for contribution or indemnity from another party[^key-5901d3f8919b858d7dbecf241e0051d7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A defendant who has filed an acknowledgment of service or a defence may make an additional claim for contribution or indemnity against a person who is already a party to the proceedings by filing and serving on that party a notice containing a statement of the nature and grounds of the additional claim[^key-2023e31e8cfeb68662982ecd956a3c65].

(2) A defendant may file and serve a notice under this rule—

- (a) without the court’s permission, if the defendant[^key-337eab6e0ee1e4323a264a11c8aa4813] files and serves it—
  - (i) with the[^key-82d1f4a72b21d2d0f613e00b7b429867] defence; or
  - (ii) if the[^key-82a46f437b20e6b56d03780d9ca7564e] additional claim for contribution or indemnity is against a party added to the claim later, within 28 days after that party files their[^key-b6990efa106a68f1609234146dfda9a9] defence; or
- (b) at any other time with the court’s permission.
