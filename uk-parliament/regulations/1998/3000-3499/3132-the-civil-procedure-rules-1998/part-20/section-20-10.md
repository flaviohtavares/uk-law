## 20.10. Effect of service of an additional claim[^key-68fd183cd6510e06c6f27dec38035832][^key-5901d3f8919b858d7dbecf241e0051d7]

*Extent: E+W*

A person on whom an additional claim is served becomes a party to the proceedings if they are not a party already.
