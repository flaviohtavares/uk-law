## 87.8. Form and directions as to the return to the writ[^key-18df3bea28ba5fc12e0f6a1d3db0acd8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A writ of habeas corpus for release must be in Practice Form No. 89 [^key-435a5258db26035e1fab571b78686a90]....

(2) A court or judge issuing a writ of habeas corpus for release must give directions as to the court or judge before whom, and the date on which, the writ is returnable.
