## 30.5. Transfer between Divisions and to and from a specialist list[^c-4fb3f7c9c6f2cb4cb8a16f7b3a72465e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The High Court may order proceedings in any Division of the High Court to be transferred to another Division.

(2)[^c-a974d6c4ad4a0ae1ad67fef93d49cf92] A judge dealing with claims in a specialist list may order proceedings to be transferred to or from that list.

(3) An application for the transfer of proceedings to or from a specialist list must be made to a judge dealing with claims in that list.

(4)[^c-2f3a60c0fba8a4afdae055424b879f1c] An order for transfer of proceedings between lists of the Business and Property Division[^c-079aa5e79e89ec29284e0f87a307721e] may only be made with the consent of the President of the Business and Property Division or their nominee[^c-0dcf5e041ff97e2298a82b01d1146800].
