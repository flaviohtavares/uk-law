## 30.1. Scope of this Part[^key-49a6223a3875a2a03d25373eb84ac92b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-2200db8495baab63d9120d89b78c2c69] This Part deals with the transfer of proceedings within the County Court[^key-56e4f8b5431712b59aaaec5711b2a190], between the High Court and the County Court[^key-79480a7000b823d08715ca1dc59d2610] and within the High Court.

[^key-d5efabe00286a0fa86fb9fc2015ebad8]...

(2)[^key-034d872b5a3c15c1d82ab84d78639118] Practice Direction 30 makes[^key-a6d5efe70d6b80ad295b2c60876c3825] provision about the transfer of proceedings between the court and a tribunal.
