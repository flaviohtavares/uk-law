# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 30.6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30.6. Power to specify place where hearings are to be held[^c-eac7d3e0a1e17bef215ac34865cbefb0]

*Extent: E+W*

The court may specify the place (for instance, a particular County Court hearing centre[^c-d5bd8b31bb4be52a2a7132ae07cfe0e0]) where the trial or some other hearing in any proceedings is to be held and may do so without ordering the proceedings to be transferred.

(Practice Direction 54D[^c-7d81cc653a3f6e34e37f1330590955eb] [^c-30d3c3e672eef82f330a3fb5480a2b15]... contains provisions about where hearings may be held in proceedings in the Administrative Court.)
