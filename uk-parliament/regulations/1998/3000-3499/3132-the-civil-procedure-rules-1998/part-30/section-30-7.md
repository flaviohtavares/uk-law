## 30.7. Transfer of control of money in court[^c-e3848fcb1673293fdbfdbe8f4b19b75e]

*Extent: E+W*

The court may order that control of any money held by it under rule 21.11 (control of money recovered by or on behalf of a child or protected party[^c-98dfbee0501546d3e0e14f57108a0e81]) be transferred to another court or County Court hearing centre[^c-10f587926ab43e38d1ead247ecd804af] if that court or hearing centre[^c-ea4f5ca1b79691c63dd27bab665cdcd0] would be more convenient.
