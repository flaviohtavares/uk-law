## 73.6. Interim charging order – venues other than the Civil National Business Centre[^key-fdbca829a1eadd8b270e2bd2b8a32cf5][^key-2c19800aed515831ce1f85e551322f5f][^key-73b6dcaf4622e5e8452215a81b98afd4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where an application for a charging order is made other than to the Civil National Business Centre[^key-3193c2567cffb6f3b852094d3ec4b990].

(2) An application for a charging order will initially be dealt with by a judge without a hearing.

(3) The judge may make an interim charging order—

- (a) imposing a charge over the judgment debtor’s interest in the asset to which the application relates; and
- (b) fixing a hearing to consider whether to make a final charging order as provided by rule 73.10A(3)(a).
