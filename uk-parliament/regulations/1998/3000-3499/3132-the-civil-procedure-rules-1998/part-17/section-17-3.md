# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 17.3

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17.3. Amendments to statements of case with the permission of the court[^c-bc79c36493ca248337dd5b5a472ff85b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the court gives permission for a party to amend their[^c-d1a8b8efd8b566c54611e3d59d229d45] statement of case, it may give directions as to—

- (a) amendments to be made to any other statement of case; and
- (b) service of any amended statement of case.

(2) The power of the court to give permission under this rule is subject to—

- (a) rule 19.2[^c-78012e9ccfe59ae3a76b21a57fe49b3f] (change of parties—general);
- (b) rule 19.6[^c-c5b004671f40f3539c75cd0158ec3643] (special provisions about adding or substituting parties after the end of a relevant limitation period<sup>(GL)</sup>); and
- (c) rule 17.4 (amendments of statement of case after the end of a relevant limitation period).
