## 17.2. Power of court to disallow amendments made without permission[^key-5c172ec9b1ea29f46fa62719ab7c36d3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a party has amended their[^key-e4f4f896a4b31532893063d755ad536f] statement of case where permission of the court was not required, the court may disallow the amendment.

(2) A party may apply to the court for an order under paragraph (1) within 14 days of service of a copy of the amended statement of case on them[^key-0267522a8f5bb1733121836dfa43f187].
