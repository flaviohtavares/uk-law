## 17.1. Amendments to statements of case[^key-f0d04555484820f8c36fa2faad486248]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party may amend their[^key-1ee877785b59bd96d2e9f1ff27abed78] statement of case , including by removing, adding or substituting a party,[^key-4286fb10f93db755f7c25887fdb4905c] at any time before it has been served on any other party.

(2) If his statement of case has been served, a party may amend it only—

- (a) with the written consent of all the other parties; or
- (b) with the permission of the court.

(3)[^key-7b108b2a949bba68941bb6265fc4a84f] If a statement of case has been served, an application to amend it by removing, adding or substituting a party must be made in accordance with rule 19.4.

(4)[^key-072c71b3898f12c10b7ff30cb1284342] A party who files a notice under Part 38 discontinuing all or part of a claim may amend their statement of case without the court’s permission to give effect to the discontinuance.

[^key-dc7b503b7887f625a51362929fb2b1fa]...

- (Part 22 requires amendments to a statement of case to be verified by a statement of truth unless the court orders otherwise)
