## 8.7. Counterclaims and other additional claims[^c-6d6853d8bb6cdf8a9e343a380445beb5][^c-cd088416aef49b0417ab36e56c8ca039]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Part 8 procedure is used, Part 20 (counterclaims and other additional claims) applies except that a party may not make a counterclaim or other additional claim[^c-8ea98fcd9339858049b238d1bfd9a9ac] (as defined by rule 20.2) without the court’s permission.
