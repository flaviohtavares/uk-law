# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 71.2

## 71.2. Order to attend court[^c-bcb827a6197dfcb6b0b65015934c3ac5][^c-cef6f1b64c51d0284e98b17fab03c0ef][^c-a4707be3d0e0790d2a09f5edacbc35d0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A judgment creditor may apply for an order requiring—

- (a) a judgment debtor; or
- (b) if a judgment debtor is a company or other corporation, an officer of that body,

to attend court to provide information about—

- (i) the judgment debtor’s means; or
- (ii) any other matter about which information is needed to enforce a judgment or order.

(2) An application under paragraph (1)—

- (a) may be made without notice; and
- (b)[^c-90e3ffca00339832024d5f0bdacec9c9] must be issued in the court or County Court hearing centre[^c-d0f18d244e451d3a99b22c23d1105951] which made the judgment or order which it is sought to enforce, except that—
  - (i) if the proceedings have since been transferred to a different court or hearing centre[^c-fff3b458209f565f6076b110be60e392], it must be issued in that court; or
  - (ii) subject to subparagraph (b)(i), if it is to enforce a judgment made in the[^c-ebc8ba43a76875ab3e50ac442d5af5cd] Civil National Business Centre[^c-3614a18b5a5ca44c51657431374d303f], it must be issued in accordance with section 2 of Practice Direction 70.

(3) The application notice must—

- (a) be in the form; and
- (b) contain the information

required by Practice Direction 71[^c-d5a455e1b4cc23aa59bfe2931f88bc7d].

(4) An application under paragraph (1) may be dealt with by a court officer without a hearing.

(5) If the application notice complies with paragraph (3), an order to attend court will be issued in the terms of paragraph (6).

(6)[^c-55be3a043adfe331a5bf7c2e879f0924] A person served with an order issued under this rule must—

- (a) attend court at the time and place specified in the order;
- (b) when he does so, produce at court documents in his control which are described in the order; and
- (c) answer on oath such questions as the court may require.

(7)[^c-55be3a043adfe331a5bf7c2e879f0924] An order under this rule will contain a notice in the following terms , or in terms to substantially the same effect[^c-c07b6db8d104d992266c912772c6ff52]—

> - If you the within-named \[ \] do not comply with this order you may be held to be in contempt of court and[^c-e4d8c9179ce8a10d95528f3043d1cd69] punished by a fine, imprisonment, confiscation of assets or other punishment under the law[^c-e0f2ba25c3baab21908ac5ad93c84f2e].

.
