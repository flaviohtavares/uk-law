## 71.7. Adjournment of the hearing[^c-bcb827a6197dfcb6b0b65015934c3ac5][^c-cef6f1b64c51d0284e98b17fab03c0ef][^c-a4707be3d0e0790d2a09f5edacbc35d0]

*Extent: E+W*

If the hearing is adjourned, the court will give directions as to the manner in which notice of the new hearing is to be served on the person ordered to attend court[^c-45fb81bda5f28650850d2492141b8540].
