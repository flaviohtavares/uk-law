## 39.4. Timetable for trial[^c-bd46c086f1ac1bbab02dc1a87ecb4f4b]

*Extent: E+W*

When the court sets a timetable for a trial in accordance with rule 28.5[^c-c04d52c26134dec92ea102ab0569348b] (fixing or confirming the trial date and giving directions [^c-7b8846266dc1dbebbde7c2c27de8e620]...) or rule 29.8 (setting a trial timetable and [^c-196a108d6528f2249fee9816f710a259]...confirming the trial date or week—multi-track) it will do so in consultation with the parties.
