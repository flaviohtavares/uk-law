# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 56.4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56.4. Scope[^key-083d3acaff142ff50b3490b86b713816]

*Extent: E+W*

A practice direction may set out special provisions with regard to claims under the following enactments—

- (a) the Chancel Repairs Act 1932[^f00022];
- (b) the Leasehold Reform Act 1967[^f00023];
- (c) the Access to Neighbouring Land Act 1992; [^key-7d83b4032dd651b33150e3ce7f7067e3]...
- (d) the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 ; and[^key-003c3b4ff2082b35e809ba7ddb82eaaf]
- (e) the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002
