# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 52.19A

## 52.19A. Orders to limit the recoverable costs of an appeal – appeals in Aarhus Convention claims[^c-e9cde36d0bffe4ff1bf0718e165dd931][^c-80838474f749f7ae040b777947f28e75]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this rule, “Aarhus Convention claim” and “prohibitively expensive” have the same meanings as in Section IX of Part 46[^c-3b47f08136033dd77db2d63572dc968c], and “claimant” means a claimant to whom rules 46.26 to 46.28[^c-2b8f4ee4a590c0ba39beb5b2b2837ed4] apply.

(2) In an appeal against a decision made in an Aarhus Convention claim to which rules 46.26 to 46.28[^c-b090a36e2e64e49113c7c4c4981ac9b9] apply, the court must—

- (a) consider whether the costs of the proceedings will be prohibitively expensive for a party who was a claimant; and
- (b) if they will be, make an order limiting the recoverable costs to the extent necessary to prevent this.

(3) When the court considers the financial resources of a party for the purposes of this rule, it must have regard to any financial support which any person has provided or is likely to provide to that party.
