## 77.2. Application for a SCPO[^key-e455d640811289f5ed1b2bb5d1e01619]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 8 of the 2007 Act for a SCPO must be started in accordance with Part 8 as modified by Practice Direction 77[^key-ce253f2d10284da0bf315078869608bc].
