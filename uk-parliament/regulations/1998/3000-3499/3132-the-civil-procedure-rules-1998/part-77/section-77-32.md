## 77.32. Supply of court documents[^c-668ba5c4be1857e3ce59d0f7e37a77a2]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise directs, rule 5.4B (supply of documents from court records – a party) and rule 5.4C (supply of court documents – a non-party) do not apply to any proceedings to which this Sub-section applies.
