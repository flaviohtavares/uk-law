## 77.16. Scope[^c-668ba5c4be1857e3ce59d0f7e37a77a2]

*Extent: E+W*

This Section applies where the Secretary of State directs the Parole Board to refer a release decision to the High Court under section 32ZAA of the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997 (“**the 1997 Act**”) or section 256AZBA of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (“**the 2003 Act**”).
