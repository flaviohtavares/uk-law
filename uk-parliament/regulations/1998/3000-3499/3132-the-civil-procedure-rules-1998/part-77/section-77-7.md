# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 77.7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77.7. Time limit for making the application[^key-9c7e5e155253a36f72b3993104579391][^key-bd31cc0b459af4ccdbb801894ab7dd58][^key-e455d640811289f5ed1b2bb5d1e01619]

*Extent: E+W*

An application for an order quashing an acquittal under section 54(3) of the 1996 Act must be made no later than 28 days after the issue of the certificate to which section 54(2) of that Act refers.
