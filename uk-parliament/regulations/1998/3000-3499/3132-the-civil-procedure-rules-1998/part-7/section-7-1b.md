## 7.1B. Claims against Welsh public bodies to be forwarded for issue in Wales[^c-cfc05e5b5547e14add4c52a3089658f3]

*Extent: E+W*

If a court or centre in England receives a claim which should pursuant to rule 7.1A[^c-607fb3095fc44313d772d9d8276d5599] be issued in Wales a court officer shall forward it for issue in the Administrative Court Office in Wales or other appropriate court office in Wales.
