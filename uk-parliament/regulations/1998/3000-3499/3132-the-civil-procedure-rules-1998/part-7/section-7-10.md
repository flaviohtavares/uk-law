## 7.10. Production Centre for claims[^c-eca2fa921f9a5da4ff1bc86a849d4bcf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) There shall be a Production Centre for the issue of claim forms and other related matters.

(2) Practice Direction[^c-7a6c26329fbb87e0e7f3f16169a7e2ee] 7B[^c-11003c285d295bac666e2808ad13e69a] makes provision for—

- (a) which claimants may use the Production Centre;
- (b) the type of claims which the Production Centre may issue;
- (c) the functions which are to be discharged by the Production Centre;
- (d) the place where the Production Centre is to be located; and
- (e) other related matters.

(3) Practice Direction[^c-06ffc560822bbff35db0752ec8eb75f3] 7B[^c-d7a8a5dea3a1f4ecdbdebed84bae2021] may disapply or modify these Rules as appropriate in relation to claims issued by the Production Centre.
