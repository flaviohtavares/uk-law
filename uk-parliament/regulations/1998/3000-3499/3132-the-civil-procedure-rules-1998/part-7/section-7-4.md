## 7.4. Particulars of claim[^c-86a734bcd0eb09175aaab281079795cc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Particulars of claim must—

- (a) be contained in or served with the claim form; or
- (b) subject to paragraph (2) be served on the defendant by the claimant within 14 days after service of the claim form.

(2) Particulars of claim must be served on the defendant no later than the latest time for serving a claim form.

(Rule 7.5 sets out the latest time for serving a claim form)

(3)[^c-7b22528c1a72af7adc3a8d92101e2946] Where the claimant serves particulars of claim on the defendant, the claimant must, within 7 days of service on the defendant, file a copy of the particulars unless a copy has already been filed.

(4) The claimant need not file a copy of the particulars of claim under paragraph (3) if the claim is being dealt with at the Production Centre (under rule 7.10) or the Civil National Business Centre[^c-904df07246d1de60369854215a2be4c2] (under Practice Direction 7C) and is not transferred to another court.

(Part 16 sets out what the particulars of claim must include)

- (Part 22 requires particulars of claim to be verified by a statement of truth)
