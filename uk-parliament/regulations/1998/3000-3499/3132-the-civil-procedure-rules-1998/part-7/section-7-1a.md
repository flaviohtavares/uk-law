## 7.1A. Claims against Welsh public bodies to be issued and heard in Wales[^c-cfc05e5b5547e14add4c52a3089658f3]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless required otherwise by any enactment, rule or practice direction, any claim against Welsh public bodies which challenges the lawfulness of their decisions must be issued and heard in Wales.
