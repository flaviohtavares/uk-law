## 47.6. Commencement of detailed assessment proceedings[^c-b941119c8cdc9da1893b8454ae2b36e6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Detailed assessment proceedings are commenced by the receiving party serving on the paying party—

- (a) notice of commencement in the relevant practice form; [^c-3815a29aa65ec967c3af19180a48709f]...
- (b) a copy or copies of the bill of costs, as required by Practice Direction 47; and[^c-f3e2f5bc7d6fb6dd7535ab6d6d8030cc]
- (c) if required by Practice Direction 47,[^c-66c28c6b5d2c70a41e5551be562c70ed] a breakdown of the costs claimed for each phase of the proceedings

(Rule 47.7 sets out the period for commencing detailed assessment proceedings.)

(2) The receiving party must also serve a copy of the notice of commencement , the bill and,[^c-502def2c30d2bfd7da0590ed6367f035] if required by Practice Direction 47,[^c-b9ee3ce48a2c097cdcdf00f15a0b4e4c] the breakdown on any other relevant persons specified in Practice Direction 47.

(3) A person on whom a copy of the notice of commencement is served under paragraph (2) is a party to the detailed assessment proceedings (in addition to the paying party and the receiving party).

( Practice Direction 47 deals with—

- other documents which the party must file when requesting detailed assessment;
- the court’s powers where it considers that a hearing may be necessary;
- the form of a bill; and
- the length of notice which will be given if a hearing date is fixed.)

(Paragraphs 7B.2 to 7B.7 of the Practice Direction - Civil Recovery Proceedings contain provisions about detailed assessment of costs in relation to civil recovery orders.)[^c-7178d21f753db204456a2feca6ea2c23]
