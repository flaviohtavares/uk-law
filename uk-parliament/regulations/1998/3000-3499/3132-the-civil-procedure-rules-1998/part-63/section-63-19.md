## 63.19. Enterprise judges and District Judges[^key-d620dee29153284a228643301d80b023][^key-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097][^key-9fc63139e250b6eb42173fa2d9d87434][^M_F_f37e69f2-1b83-4aa2-b121-4a5ad7a3dd23]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), proceedings in the Intellectual Property Enterprise Court will be dealt with by an enterprise judge.[^key-a595226056480eb16bc8bf0d5c758a58]

(1A)[^key-5ca02552d7a05279937d5e84391e96a8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-46ff6ad15453d86702b3c8af165c93eb] Unless the court otherwise orders, the following matters will be dealt with by a District Judge[^key-69c7df68a08f5511cb1a0d57cd3cecd5]—

- (a) allocation of claims to the small claims track or multi-track in accordance with rule 63.27(3);
- (b) claims allocated to the small claims track; and
- (c) all proceedings for the enforcement of any financial element of an Intellectual Property Enterprise Court judgment.

(3) For the purposes of the Practice Direction 52A – Appeals: General Provisions, a decision of a District Judge shall be treated as a decision by a District Judge hearing a [^key-dc71258f0ed945488a6b4527828c2630]... claim in the County Court. An appeal from such a decision shall be heard by an enterprise judge.
