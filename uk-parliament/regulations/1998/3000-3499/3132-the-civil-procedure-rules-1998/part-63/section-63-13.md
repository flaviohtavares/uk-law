# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 63.13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

## 63.13. Allocation[^key-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097]

*Extent: E+W*

Claims relating to matters arising out of the 1994 Act and other intellectual property rights set out in Practice Direction 63[^key-30e9aa6bacde6ecd17c72d0390f7116a] must be started in—

- (a)[^key-91fcf0f5e9f0a1d372d14568da2da725] a General IP List or the Intellectual Property Enterprise Court of the Business and Property Division; or
- (b) the County Court in accordance with paragraph 4.1(b) of Practice Direction 57AA.
