# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 63.28

## 63.28. Extent to which rules in this Part apply to small claims[^key-e85e569b5851c998c715e689b3a6960d][^key-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) To the extent provided by this rule, this Part shall apply to a claim allocated to, or requested to be allocated to, the small claims track in the Intellectual Property Enterprise Court[^key-80e95b39de4aee1115b70a5734000af2].

(2) Rules 63.1, 63.13, 63.18, 63.20, 63.21, 63.22, 63.25, 63.26(1) and (2), and 63.27 shall apply to the claim.

(3) No other rules in this Part shall apply.
