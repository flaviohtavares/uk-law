## 63.26. Costs[^c-d620dee29153284a228643301d80b023][^c-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the court will reserve the costs of an application to the conclusion of the trial when they will be subject to summary assessment.

(2) Where a party has behaved unreasonably the court may[^c-7070745038ea86ab77bdb6552d6b3a92] make an order for costs at the conclusion of the hearing.

(3) Where the court makes a summary assessment of costs, it will do so in accordance with Section VII of Part 46[^c-11a8ba7770c3fec0082f6f6be0971c5c].
