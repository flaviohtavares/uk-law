## 63.6. Claim for infringement or challenge to validity of a patent or registered design[^c-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097]

*Extent: E+W*

A statement of case in a claim for infringement or a claim in which the validity of a patent or registered design is challenged must contain particulars as set out in Practice Direction 63[^c-ad957063c9da8fd6e609c35efe9e6186].
