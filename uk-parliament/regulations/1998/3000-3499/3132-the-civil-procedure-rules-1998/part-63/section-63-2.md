## 63.2. Scope of Section I and allocation[^c-e9b41ec15b82ceef8274db1f25b38097]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to—

- (a) any claim under—
  - (i) the 1977 Act;
  - (ii) the Registered Designs Act 1949;
  - (iii) the Defence Contracts Act 1958; and
- (b) any claim relating to—
  - (i)[^c-7afe5d831a82f430565d28fc471dfafa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) semiconductor topography rights; or
  - (iii) plant varieties.

(2) Claims to which this Section applies must be started in—

- (a) the Patents Court; or
- (b) the Intellectual Property Enterprise Court[^c-49713978eb8f521b41df75ad277a3253].
