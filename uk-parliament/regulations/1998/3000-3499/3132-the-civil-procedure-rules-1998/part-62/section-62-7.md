## 62.7. Case management[^c-61ad487b8f6703fe6a308989f3dd26b8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 26 and any other rule that requires a party to file a directions questionnaire[^c-aac5c72283e825577c1dbd4d96a9577e] does not apply.

(2) Arbitration claims are allocated to the multi-track.

(3) Part 29 does not apply.

(4) The automatic directions set out in Practice Direction 62[^c-2223f27b739abc754dfa6d67d3f3633e] apply unless the court orders otherwise.
