## 80.8. Modification of Part 52 (appeals)[^key-b0028ea3ea761f9d3071a1825db0843b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 52 (appeals) applies to an appeal under section 16 of the TPIM Act or section 52 of the National Security Act[^key-8d7a297a65f5ba34f2dbcadcd5b9888e], subject to—

- (a) rule 80.2;
- (b) the rules in section 4 of this Part; and
- (c) paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) The following rules do not apply to appeals under section 16 of the TPIM Act or section 52 of the National Security Act[^key-6671f24ecd1f57e4a7a4acfb4226e421]—

- (a) rules 52.3, 52.4 and 52.6[^key-e283c9463430f730b8c0b293e7d8e43c] (permission);
- (b) rule 52.12[^key-32941f2de953f10b449979fe6a3875dd] (appellant’s notice);
- (c) rule 52.13[^key-5261555ad594442e9f77c4100c549b79] (respondent’s notice); and
- (d) rule 52.21[^key-9008c8ad5d2d765959047413b6cae729] (hearing of appeals).

(3) Rule 52.2 (all parties to comply with Practice Directions 52A to 52E) apply[^key-ef7418c8d6d505f9cede86e35e5c815f], but the parties shall not be required to comply with paragraphs 5.1 to 5.3 of Practice Direction 52A and paragraphs 6.3 to 6.6 of Practice Direction 52B[^key-6bcdc63f9b20689210a588f8adb348c7].
