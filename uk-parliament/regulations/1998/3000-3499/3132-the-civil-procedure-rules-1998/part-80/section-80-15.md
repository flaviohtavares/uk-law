## 80.15. Applications for anonymity[^c-b0028ea3ea761f9d3071a1825db0843b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The TPIM or Part 2[^c-45e090d5e10a625e1bf17257fee299d0] subject or the Secretary of State may apply for an order requiring anonymity for the TPIM or Part 2 subject.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) may be made at any time, irrespective of whether any TPIM proceedings or relevant proceedings[^c-6cb5977a292b230658185410989e92df] have been commenced.

(3) An application may be made without notice to the other party.

(4) The reference in this rule to an order requiring anonymity for the TPIM or Part 2[^c-c0217be81db7c4c38def8e51eda43bab] subject is to be construed in accordance with paragraph 6(3) of Schedule 4 to the TPIM Act or of Schedule 10 to the National Security Act.[^c-3a451f8db0525864a2b3f4c7bd24b5e1]
