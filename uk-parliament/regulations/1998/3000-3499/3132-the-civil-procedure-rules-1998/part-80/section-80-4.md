## 80.4. Application for permission to impose measures[^key-b0028ea3ea761f9d3071a1825db0843b]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 6(1)(b) of the TPIM Act or section 42(1)(b) of the National Security Act[^key-5771e01c61edc9ad32725bbaef6ec05c] for permission to impose measures must be made by the Secretary of State filing with the court—

- (a) a statement of reasons to support the application;
- (b) any relevant material of which the Secretary of State is aware at that stage;
- (c) any written submissions; and
- (d) the proposed TPIM or Part 2[^key-db8bd61661668fd49f70ae1e860cc4d2] notice.
