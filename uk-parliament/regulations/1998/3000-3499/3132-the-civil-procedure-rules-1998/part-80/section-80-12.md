## 80.12. Modification of Part 52 (appeals)[^c-b0028ea3ea761f9d3071a1825db0843b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 52 (appeals) applies to an appeal to the Court of Appeal against an order of the High Court in TPIM proceedings or relevant proceedings[^c-cd0c4abe4c711f2c41a3a466f1bcb8ec], subject to—

- (a) rule 80.2;
- (b) the rules in section 4 of this Part; and
- (c) paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) The following rules do not apply to appeals to the Court of Appeal—

- (a) rule 52.12(1)[^c-8099d230b6b5a921e81358021e6100e9] (appellant’s notice); and
- (b) rule 52.13[^c-703c8256d6b9a04cb00b2eaec9546f91] (respondent’s notice), but

the provisions of rules 80.9 and 80.11 shall apply with appropriate modifications.

(3) Rule 52.2 (all parties to comply with Practice Directions 52A to 52E) apply[^c-8840b8861ca93898e42300537e34de88], but the parties shall not be required to comply with paragraphs 6.3 to 6.6 of Practice Direction 52B and paragraph 28 of Practice Direction 52C[^c-c6f4fdcecd6bf9554313ddf196121b89].
