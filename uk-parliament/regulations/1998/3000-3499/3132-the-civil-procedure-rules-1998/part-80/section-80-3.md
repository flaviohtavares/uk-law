# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 80.3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80.3. Scope of this section[^c-b0028ea3ea761f9d3071a1825db0843b]

*Extent: E+W*

This section contains rules about—

- (a) applications under section 6(1)(b) of the TPIM Act or section 42(1)(b) of the National Security Act[^c-d6b4e0b0062ebf200ef6cb1f8af44a5b] (application for permission to impose measures);
- (b) references under paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 2 to the TPIM Act or of Schedule 8 to the National Security Act[^c-3a37d4c44f44df3d7e9fdd5903f7787a] (reference of the imposition of measures imposed without permission); and
- (c) appeals to the High Court under section 16 of the TPIM[^c-4b599a8cacb612300ee2e37e22c219f6] Act (appeals relating to a TPIM notice) or section 52 of the National Security Act (appeals relating to a Part 2 notice)[^c-eea3d0a593afe9dff87e9c1ae0d11f05].
