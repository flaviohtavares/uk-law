## 32.17. Affidavit made outside the jurisdiction[^c-e8b8a68f0cc2059817a2e6d0629b88ee]

*Extent: E+W*

A person may make an affidavit<sup>(GL)</sup> outside the jurisdiction in accordance with—

- (a) this Part; or
- (b) the law of the place where he makes the affidavit<sup>(GL)</sup>.
