## 32.11. Cross-examination on a witness statement[^c-dde6308c1e05351bbaaf12827c6dee73]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a witness is called to give evidence at trial, he may be cross-examined on his witness statement whether or not the statement or any part of it was referred to during the witness’s evidence in chief<sup>(GL)</sup>.
