## 75.1. Scope and interpretation[^key-90c898404514b11719f9cb6cd287012d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Practice Direction 75[^key-cb8a7815194129749bcbb253bc456679]—

- (a) sets out the proceedings to which this Part applies; and
- (b) may apply this Part with modifications in relation to any particular category of those proceedings.

([^key-38e52ef3d55b26f9ab511ada7dc3bce5]Rule 21.1(1)(c)(i)[^key-ebf8673144ac5bba81349e7837293232] provides that Part 21 (children and protected parties) does not apply to proceedings under this Part where one of the parties is a child.)

(2) In this Part—

- (a) “the Centre” means the Traffic Enforcement Centre established under the direction of the Lord Chancellor;
- (a1)[^key-c685c3584cb2a7c3a7369301229eb132] “enforcement agent” has the meaning given in paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007;
- (a2) “local authority warrant of control” means a warrant of control issued by a local authority under article 5 of the 1993 Order;
- (b) “no relevant return to the warrant” means that—
  - (i) the enforcement agent[^key-7b9f3972acfc000ccd4456ec27796ecc] has been unable to take control of[^key-3c00e92d17ec40b075bcd261e4e5782e] goods because the[^key-8b66bb4d02c67cdd746f250464b5f889] enforcement agent has been denied access to premises occupied by the defendant or because the goods have been removed from those premises;
  - (ii) any goods seized under a local authority warrant of control[^key-9bb74eebcb48f7f1a401e8276a5c2fd7] are insufficient to satisfy the debt and the cost of enforcement[^key-5392783d6e6d8a9e52eee4e56da73721]; or
  - (iii) the goods are insufficient to cover the cost of their removal and sale ;[^key-ac5213808ba41f14f4d0de513adf6360]
- (ba)[^key-c10b86f948813b1c3d3b5ab492bda346] ‘notice of enforcement’ has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Taking Control of Goods Regulations 2013;
- (bb) ‘order for recovery' means an order drawn up by the authority under rule 75.3(4) on receipt from the court of the registered request under rule 75.3(3);
- (c) “the 1993 Order” means the Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts Order 1993;
- (d) “relevant period”, in relation to any particular case, means—
  - (i) the period allowed for serving a statutory declaration or witness statement[^key-1be8809210fca80dd27f5baea95ba18a] under any enactment which applies to that case; or
  - (ii) where an enactment permits the court to extend that period, the period as extended;
- (e) “specified debts” means the debts specified in article 2 of the 1993 Order or treated as so specified by any other enactment; and
- (f) “the authority”, “notice of the amount due”, “order” and “the respondent” have the meaning given by Practice Direction 75[^key-532cc79e8d0fcfaf32247f228dcf46cb].
