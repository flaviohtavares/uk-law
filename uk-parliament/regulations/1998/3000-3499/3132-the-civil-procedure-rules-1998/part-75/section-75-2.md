# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 75.2

## 75.2. The Centre[^c-90c898404514b11719f9cb6cd287012d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Proceedings to which this Part applies must be started in the Centre.

(2) For any purpose connected with the exercise of the Centre’s functions—

- (a)[^c-239e26acdfe3b95acfd895f3ced7e56e] the Centre is deemed to be an office of the County Court; and
- (b) any officer of the Centre, in exercising its functions, is deemed to act as an officer of the County Court[^c-2c1d526b57953e3450ef5eeac1932a2b].
