# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 75.5A

## 75.5A. Review of decision of court officer[^key-5b71481b89e3af67d021519344f9eca6][^key-90c898404514b11719f9cb6cd287012d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any party may request any decision of a court officer to be reviewed by a District Judge[^key-69c7df68a08f5511cb1a0d57cd3cecd5].

(2) Such a request must be made within 14 days of service of the decision.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), unless[^key-891ad21412b0c17442e14ba35a3e1956]—

- (a) the party requesting the review requests a[^key-91364dd2263652ab058c92293026199c] hearing; or
- (b) the court orders a[^key-d91360ddf57856cf9f4637b608208626] hearing,

a request for a review under paragraph (1) shall be determined[^key-ea1e6518620ae573288727c1ff2b44da] without a[^key-51105ee3c511d680cb6e9ed13946e86b] hearing.

(4)[^key-cfc4cb08b6f90b858328fb9e017dedf9] Where a court officer has made an order on an application for a longer period to file a statutory declaration or witness statement, a request for a review under paragraph (1) shall be determined without a hearing unless the court otherwise directs.
