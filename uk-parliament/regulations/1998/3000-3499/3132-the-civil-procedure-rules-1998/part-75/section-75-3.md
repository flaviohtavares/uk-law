## 75.3. Request for registration[^key-90c898404514b11719f9cb6cd287012d][^key-a1557d1df501762c38004032dfd2e990]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The authority must file a request in the appropriate form scheduling the amount claimed to be due.

(2) The authority must, in that request or in another manner approved by the court officer—

- (a) certify—
  - (i) that 14 days have elapsed since service of the notice of the amount due;
  - (ii) the date of such service;
  - (iii) the number of the notice of the amount due; and
  - (iv) that the amount due remains unpaid;
- (b) specify the grounds (whether by reference to the appropriate code or otherwise), as stated in the notice, on which the authority claims to be entitled to claim that amount; and
- (c) state—
  - (i) the name, title and address of the respondent;
  - (ii) the registration number of the vehicle concerned;
  - (iii) the authority’s address for service;
  - (iv) the court fee; and
  - (v) such other matters as required by Practice Direction 75[^key-1d19617971131333398c7d96c03bd44e].

(3) On receipt of a request that meets the requirements of paragraphs (1) and (2), the court officer will order that the amount due may be recovered as if it were payable under a County Court[^key-af56c1680298a75d0dd91d98b629a87c] order by registering[^key-26549c28c47e57051e8880b4ed0917ef] the request and returning it to the authority.

(4)[^key-175911d098ad98fef821edb6ccd92fc5] On receipt of a registered request the authority may draw up the order for recovery[^key-9b0527c238d67a4394fec74d587ef475] and must—

- (a) insert in the order for recovery the date by which the respondent must either—
  - (i) comply with the order for recovery; or
  - (ii) file a statutory declaration or witness statement; and
- (b) attach to the order for recovery a form of statutory declaration or witness statement for the respondent’s use.

(5)[^key-abc87956a3a933408b17eac730d50fe8] The authority must serve in accordance with Part 6 the order for recovery[^key-c8442d9c11cf1e6f8fb1f9b04700d5bd] (and the form of statutory declaration or witness statement) on the respondent within 15 days of the date on which the request is registered by the court.
