## 75.8. Revocation of order[^key-90c898404514b11719f9cb6cd287012d]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, in accordance with any enactment, an order for recovery[^key-c0609a2b6af7af41d9bab3199c13d56b] is deemed to have been revoked following the filing of a statutory declaration or a witness statement[^key-86da77f8886f638a49af04c6cf385818]—

- (a) the court will serve a copy of the statutory declaration or witness statement[^key-d1ec15d6cdcaafa01779ed9b103c0494] on the authority;
- (b) any warrant of control issued pursuant to[^key-8efaa5863eca55e38a4014f4ea181d0c] the order will cease to have effect; and
- (c) if appropriate, the authority must inform any enforcement agent[^key-b2425694f11fefea3864fbdfc0c77ba1] instructed to exercise the enforcement power[^key-bccfa6436e7050205d3cbfd6668a2045] of the withdrawal of the warrant as soon as possible.
