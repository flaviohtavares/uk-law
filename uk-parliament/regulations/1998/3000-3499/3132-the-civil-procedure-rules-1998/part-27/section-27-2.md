# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 27.2

## 27.2. Extent to which other Parts apply[^c-f68c9585ff5ba24f13b461990eaacca2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following Parts of these Rules do not apply to small claims—

- (a) Part 25 (interim remedies) except as it relates to interim injunctions<sup>(GL)</sup>;
- (b) Part 31 (disclosure and inspection);
- (c) Part 32 (evidence) except rule 32.1 (power of court to control evidence);
- (d) Part 33 (miscellaneous rules about evidence);
- (e) Part 35 (experts and assessors) except rules 35.1 (duty to restrict expert evidence), 35.3 (experts—overriding duty to the court) , 35.7 (court’s power to direct that evidence is to be given by single joint expert)[^c-157b607731762fcb182e68b7a60262c7] and 35.8 (instructions to a single joint expert);
- (f) Subject to paragraph (3),[^c-94521f4aa5fec79e397ead0953d42a29] Part 18 (further information);
- (g) Part 36 (offers to settle [^c-fc3121c70d70075a74d4a19a3c950e6e]...); and
- (h) Part 39 (hearings) except rule 39.2 (general rule—hearing to be in public) , rule 39.8 (communications with the court) and rule 39.9 (recording and transcription of proceedings)[^c-afbb772df1210e62d0929389d401fed4].

(2) The other Parts of these Rules apply to small claims except to the extent that a rule or a practice direction under this Part[^c-22bcf6f712364c5807bfc73bfc454477] limits such application.

(2A)[^c-777d7ae571e0d4dda739b73660847f3b] A rule or practice direction may, in relation to a specified type of small claim—

- (a) require or permit the use of the procedure set out in this Part; and
- (b) disapply or modify any of the rules set out in this Part.

(3)[^c-676c92ebc479a60ba8c9032fb51cdf64] The court of its own initiative may order a party to provide further information if it considers it appropriate to do so.
