## 27.14. Costs on the small claims track[^c-d6244b256148b900cb9fb697b7c1e5f0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to any case which has been allocated to the small claims track [^c-76c003374a59dc964942a290968ef01c]....

(Rule 46.13 makes[^c-377dae34ef6e27c7bf13b6b7ac835919] provision in relation to orders for costs made before a claim has been allocated to the small claims track)

(2)[^c-4484135a41b480d0b5f0a6a798f7b01d] The court may not order a party to pay a sum to another party in respect of that other party’s costs, fees and expenses, including those relating to an appeal, except—

- (a) the fixed costs attributable to issuing the claim, calculated in accordance with Table 2 in Practice Direction 45;[^c-6a8a0260dfd66a2dd360cf0bd51967b2] [^c-d2325ea15a678e1a226b4ba60ba0ec08]...
- (b) in proceedings which included a claim for an injunction or an order for specific performance a sum not exceeding the amount specified in Practice Direction 27A[^c-e5b28a66ab051f94d3c32890619b9fc4] for legal advice and assistance relating to that claim;
- (c) any court fees paid by that other party;
- (d) expenses which a party or witness has reasonably incurred in travelling to and from a hearing or in staying away from home for the purposes of attending a hearing;
- (e) a sum not exceeding the amount specified in Practice Direction 27A[^c-c5100fde5174a4a2cd031b69f657c8b7] for any loss of earnings or loss of leave by a party or witness due to attending a hearing or to staying away from home for the purposes of attending a hearing;
- (f) a sum not exceeding the amount specified in Practice Direction 27A[^c-0b1ae0192146d998977fdf61be408586] for an expert’s fees; [^c-f25e33e15974d1cb6d1e435553cede61]...
- (g) such further costs as the court may assess by the summary procedure and order to be paid by a party who has behaved unreasonably ;[^c-8dd26b96c1698897364d83994590158d] [^c-742884505a2d09e9af5d507465ee0cb9]...
- (h)[^c-01e792b64d67bea09028f69261bf8b8c] the applicable Stage 1 and, where relevant, the Stage 2 fixed costs in Table 10 and Table 11 in Practice Direction 45[^c-a021b197e0cea268efc0e35e4223227a] where—
  - (i) the claim was within the scope of the Pre-Action Protocol for Low Value Personal Injury Claims in Road Traffic Accidents (“the RTA Protocol”) or the Pre-action Protocol for Low Value Personal Injury (Employers’ Liability and Public Liability) Claims (‘the[^c-9ac7cc7bc353ad9fb17d97feb5be17f2] EL/PL Protocol’);
  - (ii) the claimant reasonably believed that the claim was valued at more than the small claims track limit in accordance with paragraph 4.1(4) of the relevant Protocol[^c-2871acf5ccd1cccc0a6db366fca2f9fe]; and
  - (iii) the defendant admitted liability under the process set out in the relevant Protocol[^c-18659e4a6e4821601cdb52cbe38ae1ac]; but
  - (iv) the defendant did not pay those Stage 1 and, where relevant, Stage 2 fixed costs ; and[^c-13a3940a849de96297c32ffc3965a8a7]
- (i)[^c-1541c323f6d3d9890d8533c501efe365] in an appeal, the cost of any approved transcript reasonably incurred.

(3)[^c-9a2cb1c4876ff29d415a37018a5dcab8][^c-7c18e66fba1f25c3b6866cd59252cd7c] A party’s rejection of an offer in settlement will not of itself constitute unreasonable behaviour under paragraph (2)(g)[^c-6c701e55ffba8b92dec4f5af4778526e] but the court may take it into consideration when it is applying the unreasonableness test.

[^c-2db166f41ed7fe200dacbd4749e58ba8]...
