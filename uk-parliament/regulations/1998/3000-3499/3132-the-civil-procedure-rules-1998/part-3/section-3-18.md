# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 3.18

## 3.18. Assessing costs on the standard basis where a costs management order has been made[^key-a0854a3753d8762e6dfee5b8f83fb2f2][^key-4079a04c63c1990c9203ca9a6e2ec5ec]

*Extent: E+W*

In any case where a costs management order has been made, when assessing costs on the standard basis, the court will—

- (a) have regard to the receiving party’s last approved or agreed budgeted costs[^key-9defbda38adde5628aca3ed30d75886d] for each phase of the proceedings; [^key-7f33c96ac5bb1fbe2903daa12a689b55]...
- (b) not depart from such approved or agreed budgeted costs[^key-a388af3f54ad3c332b85f5866bccfccc] unless satisfied that there is good reason to do so ; and[^key-c4ab1aa9237356081853dced1fa1213d]
- (c) take into account any comments made pursuant to rule 3.15(4) or 3.17(3)[^key-d0f1a11054c95df37a76228ca78aad43] and recorded on the face of the order

(Attention is drawn to rules 44.3(2)(a) and 44.3(5)[^key-29296162a06a2074abdc9be563a7f4f9], which concern proportionality of costs.)
