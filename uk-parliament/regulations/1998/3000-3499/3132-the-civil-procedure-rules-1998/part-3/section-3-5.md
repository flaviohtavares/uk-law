# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 3.5

## 3.5. Judgment without trial after striking out[^c-b8b9fce902b2674e1a732d0334e227b7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) the court makes an order which includes a term that the statement of case of a party shall be struck out if the party does not comply with the order; and
- (b) the party against whom the order was made does not comply with it.

(2) A party may obtain judgment with costs by filing a request for judgment if—

- (a) the order referred to in paragraph (1)(a) relates to the whole of a statement of case; and
- (b) where the party wishing to obtain judgment is the claimant, the claim is for—
  - (i) a specified amount of money;
  - (ii) an amount of money to be decided by the court;
  - (iii) delivery of goods where the claim form gives the defendant the alternative of paying their value; or
  - (iv) any combination of these remedies.

(3)[^c-3f42cf57fea86dce035bcfbf04ec4733] Where judgment is obtained under this rule in a case to which paragraph (2)(b)(iii) applies, it will be judgment requiring the defendant to deliver the goods, or (if the defendant[^c-9d2744cc27d429c7eb845d30d488284f] does not do so) pay the value of the goods as decided by the court (less any payments made).

(4)[^c-3bb838d2350d90cbd536916a238fd3f5] The request must state that the right to enter judgment has arisen because the court’s order has not been complied with.

(5)[^c-ae18c0be9e622fbb95adcc8917ec7afb] A party must make an application in accordance with Part 23 if they wish[^c-688ebea643151d25be963fdb5b2b4fe3] to obtain judgment under this rule in a case to which paragraph (2) does not apply.
