# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 3.5A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.5A. Judgment without trial after striking out a claim in the Civil National Business Centre[^c-599f07f8530e94ff57dba22b87757bc7][^c-6b9a80fc530653a3a1dc59ef882057a3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a claimant files a request for judgment in the Civil National Business Centre[^c-5c70ebc0d34e7fa15cd6440558e3902e] in accordance with rule 3.5, in a claim which includes an amount of money to be decided by the court, the claim will be sent to the preferred hearing centre.

(2) If a claim is sent to a preferred hearing centre pursuant to paragraph (1), any further correspondence should be sent to, and any further requests should be made at, the hearing centre to which the claim was sent.
