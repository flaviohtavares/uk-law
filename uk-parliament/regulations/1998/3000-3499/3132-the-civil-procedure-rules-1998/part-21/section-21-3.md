# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 21.3

## 21.3. Stage of proceedings at which a litigation friend becomes necessary[^key-45ca8667c1772b0ff63d17f60708884a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule does not apply where the court has made an order under rule 21.2(3).

(2) A person may not, without the the court’s permission[^key-2d335ba393fe05ba308c22be3997ba46] —

- (a) make an application against a child or protected party before proceedings have started; or
- (b) take any step in proceedings except—
  - (i) issuing and serving a claim form; or
  - (ii) applying for the appointment of a litigation friend under rule 21.6,

until the child or protected party has a litigation friend.

(3) If during proceedings a party lacks capacity to continue to conduct proceedings, no party may take any further step in the proceedings without the the court’s permission[^key-da90bc0cc3b25edd195e298d7e761338] until the protected party has a litigation friend.

(4) Any step taken before a child or protected party has a litigation friend has no effect unless the court orders otherwise.
