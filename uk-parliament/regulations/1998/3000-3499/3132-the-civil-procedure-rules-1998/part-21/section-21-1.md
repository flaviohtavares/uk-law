# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 21.1

## 21.1. Scope of this Part[^key-45ca8667c1772b0ff63d17f60708884a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part—

- (a) contains special provisions which apply in proceedings involving children and protected parties;
- (b) sets out how a person becomes a litigation friend; and
- (c)[^key-a8dcb4688c7f9d29e11c2b9226df351c] does not apply to—
  - (i) proceedings under Part 75;
  - (ii) enforcement of specified debts by taking control of goods; or
  - (iii) applications in relation to enforcement of specified debts by taking control of goods,

  where one of the parties to the proceedings is a child.

(2) In this Part—

- (a) ‘the 2005 Act’ means the Mental Capacity Act 2005;
- (b) ‘child’ means a person under 18;
- (c) ‘lacks capacity’ means lacks capacity within the meaning of the 2005 Act;
- (d) ‘protected party’ means a party, or an intended party, who lacks capacity to conduct the proceedings;
- (e) ‘protected beneficiary’ means a protected party who lacks capacity to manage and control any money recovered by them[^key-1877bcdbfd8c012bf4d693e8797e4017] or on their[^key-f295d77fe610ecc39489bc607f5cb014] behalf or for their benefit in the proceedings;[^key-46ac25f14e865982b88f63b24343e206]
- (f)[^key-15a589c7b892b0f283b87d5362180c2c] “specified debts” has the same meaning as in rule 75.1(2)(e); and
- (g) “taking control of goods” means using the procedure to take control of goods contained in Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

(Rules 6.13 and 6.25 contain[^key-31334cf73fa5f4e613ce2e392ee4069c] provisions about the service of documents on children and protected parties.)

(Rule 46.4[^key-a43c052b10c416d974f9e4d8be4c72b7] deals with costs where money is payable by or to a child or protected party.)
