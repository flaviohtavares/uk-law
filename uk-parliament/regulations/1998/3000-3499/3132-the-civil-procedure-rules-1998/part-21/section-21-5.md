## 21.5. How a person becomes a litigation friend without a court order[^c-45ca8667c1772b0ff63d17f60708884a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the court has not appointed a litigation friend, a person who wishes to act as a litigation friend must follow the procedure set out in this rule.

(2) A deputy appointed by the Court of Protection under the 2005 Act with power to conduct proceedings on the protected party’s behalf must file an official copy<sup>(GL) </sup>of the order of the Court of Protection which confers their[^c-3cb97cd2621cd3fe5fe95613fe48a13f] power to act either—

- (a) where the protected party is to be[^c-83bcf0528745f4d2030d4f53f3fb6122] a claimant, when[^c-95f061e49a803faa41b8d7eb3e4decb5] the claim is made; or
- (b) where the protected party is to be[^c-56579d94d7b2ae021a95b88c795a4294] a defendant, at the time when the deputy[^c-8d65e8daa606a91d2f25071348572f42] first takes a step in the proceedings on the defendant’s behalf[^c-16bae36443e4f28bbea9eac294aca00d].

(3) Any other person must file a certificate of suitability stating that they satisfy[^c-fdd5f70316e62c4f004b4c035733178b] the conditions specified in rule 21.4(3) either—

- (a) where the person is to act as a litigation friend for a claimant, at the time when the claim is made; or
- (b) where the person is to act as a litigation friend for a defendant, at the time when they first take[^c-881b95562bd235b18ef13145564f271d] a step in the proceedings on the defendant’s behalf[^c-966b34107dc85ddcf54903de8f7f00f9].

(4)[^c-6ca43a93bd7bb46bb92183974525e077] The certificate of suitability must be verified by a statement of truth and must state in accordance with the prescribed form that the person—

- (a) agrees to act;
- (b) knows or believes the person concerned is a child or lacks capacity to conduct the proceedings (stating in the latter case the grounds for that knowledge or belief); and
- (c) meets the requirements of rule 21.4(3).

(5)[^c-f97307e72dc79624c9623d3b4532d253] The litigation friend must—

- (a) serve the certificate of suitability on every person on whom, in accordance with rule 6.13[^c-a96b96e66399035981aaee43a79ad16e] (service on a parent, guardian etc.), the claim form should be served; and
- (b) file a certificate of service when filing[^c-6d23f52d0ac55fda7a4646959e070d83] the certificate of suitability.

(6)[^c-1ce30544425c7e5a4076fbb8a352fd94] Where the grounds for believing that a protected party lacks capacity to conduct the litigation are based on expert opinion, a copy of such opinion must be served, either with the certificate of suitability or separately.

( Rules 6.17 and 6.29 set[^c-9abe5ccac8398a745eab45e8a6f00c65] out the details to be contained in a certificate of service.)
