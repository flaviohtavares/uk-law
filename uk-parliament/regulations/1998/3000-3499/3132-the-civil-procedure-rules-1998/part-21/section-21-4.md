## 21.4. Who may be a litigation friend without a court order[^c-45ca8667c1772b0ff63d17f60708884a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule does not apply if the court has appointed a person to be a litigation friend.

(2) A deputy appointed by the Court of Protection under the 2005 Act with power to conduct proceedings on the protected party’s behalf is entitled to be the protected party’s litigation friend[^c-3c925949127adda65e874843048ad326] in any proceedings to which the deputy’s[^c-68af983d882c08202de9854150481995] power extends.

(3) If nobody has been appointed by the court or, in the case of a protected party, [^c-a84a97f22d580bd29857174d146b232d]... appointed as a deputy as set out in paragraph (2), a person may act as a litigation friend if they[^c-a4ba4e6d0a4ae71a19254e4379daf0c2] —

- (a) can fairly and competently conduct proceedings on behalf of the child or protected party;
- (b) have[^c-11c90dc6d6fc7711d274f21b9c37795f] no interest adverse to that of the child or protected party; and
- (c)[^c-f2a2f81fd90f6114a64a8e3f55b17a4c] where the child or protected party is a claimant, undertake to pay any costs that the claimant is ordered to pay, subject to any right to be repaid from the assets of the child or protected party.
