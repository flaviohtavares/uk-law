## 26.15. Assignment within the fast track[^key-62406252147bc7fc9cba2c32481e196d]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the claim is one for noise induced hearing loss (in respect of which Sections I and IV of Part 28 and Section VIII of Part 45 make provision), the complexity band to which a claim will normally be assigned in the fast track is set out in Table 1.

Table 1

| Complexity band 1 | Complexity band 2 | Complexity band 3 | Complexity band 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (a) road traffic accident related, non-personal injury claims; and<br>(b) defended debt claims | (a) road traffic accident related, personal injury claims which are or should have been started under the RTA Protocol; and<br>(b) personal injury claims to which the Pre-action Protocol for Resolution of Package Travel Claims apply | (a) road traffic accident related, personal injury claims to which the RTA Protocol does not apply;<br>(b) employer’s liability (accident) and public liability personal injury claims;<br>(c) possession claims;<br>(d) housing disrepair claims; and<br>(e) other claims for a sum of money, whether the sum is specified or unspecified, except claims that fall under complexity band 1(b)[^key-435fe7cda354cb21405e08086a2c0b6f] | (a) employer’s liability disease claims (other than a claim for noise induced hearing loss);<br>(b) complex possession and housing disrepair claims;<br>(c) property and building disputes;<br>(d) professional negligence claims; and<br>(e) any claim which would normally be allocated to the fast track, but is nonetheless complex |
