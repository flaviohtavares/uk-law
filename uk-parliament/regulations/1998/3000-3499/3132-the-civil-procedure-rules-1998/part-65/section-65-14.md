# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 65.14

## 65.14. Starting a demotion claim, prohibited conduct standard contract order claim or suspension claim[^c-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3][^c-2171fbddf0faf2131d46360a4e72d879][^c-7420093824c034d03dc79c36f75bef26]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-da3d4991b97851834e04c04c67f70999]

- (a) The claim may be made at any County Court hearing centre;
- (b) the claim will be issued by the hearing centre where the claim is made; and
- (c) if the claim is not made at the County Court hearing centre which serves the address where the property or dwelling[^c-de931d9a471c8cb60185889d9731d9a3] is situated, the claim, when it is issued, will be sent to that hearing centre.

(Practice Direction 65 makes further provision in respect of claims which are not made at the County Court hearing centre which serves the address where the property or dwelling[^c-478fd27457b906ae26e24ad1e997ae05] is situated.)

(2) The claim form and form of defence sent with it must be in the forms set out in Practice Direction 65[^c-7aab9f43e67f8e7b0605473b4e077ca7].

(Part 16 and Practice Direction 65[^c-a50bd570de885108f11bde0aec05fa62] provide details about the contents of the particulars of claim)
