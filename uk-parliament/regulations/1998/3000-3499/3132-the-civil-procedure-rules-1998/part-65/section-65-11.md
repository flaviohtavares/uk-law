## 65.11. Scope of this Section and interpretation[^c-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to—

- (a) claims by a landlord for an order under section 82A of the Housing Act 1985 or under section 6A of the Housing Act 1988 (“a demotion order”); [^c-9e4269e83a8c7e629a5317df74a6f7ae]...
- (aa)[^c-52ae802eff527df2956879b03fbd69c9] claims by a landlord for an order under section 121A of the Housing Act 1985 (“a suspension order”); [^c-e8580500c0eaea3279f71f77e6a5f43f]...
- (b) proceedings relating to a tenancy created by virtue of a demotion order ; and[^c-03ad205cc4ab95589373d22eb557f4ec]
- (c)[^c-63e14fc714fbbb3e261afc39749e85da] claims by a landlord for an order under section 116 of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016.

(2) In this Section—

- (za)[^c-1b7fc83ad375aab19c4abde66929e3dd] “**the 2016 Act**” means the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016;
- (a) “a demotion claim” means a claim made by a landlord for a demotion order; [^c-250591459b9852f69576300609466685]...
- (b) “a demoted tenancy” means a tenancy created by virtue of a demotion order ;
- (ba)[^c-e86e02571f220d6140a1e66bcb8a719d] “**a dwelling**” means a dwelling let under an occupation contract;
- (bb) “**a prohibited conduct standard contract**” has the meaning provided by section 116(6) of the 2016 Act;
- (bc) “a prohibited conduct standard contract order” is an order imposed under section 116 of the 2016 Act;
- (bd) “**a prohibited conduct standard contract order claim**” means a claim for a prohibited conduct standard contract order;
- (be) “**a Renting Homes possession claim**” means a claim for the recovery of possession of a dwelling under the 2016 Act;
- (c)[^c-688f5de8c320e7bc7c30d96d7d1c1ea4] “suspension claim” means a claim made by a landlord for a suspension order; and
- (d) “suspension period” means the period during which the suspension order suspends the right to buy in relation to the dwelling house.
