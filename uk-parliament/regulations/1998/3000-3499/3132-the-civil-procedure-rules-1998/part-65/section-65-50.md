## 65.50. Scope of this Section and interpretation[^c-0854de1ebdadcfcab58f9d07fc8995c9][^c-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to applications for a respect order under Part A1 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014.

(2) In this Section, “**the 2014 Act**” means the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014.
