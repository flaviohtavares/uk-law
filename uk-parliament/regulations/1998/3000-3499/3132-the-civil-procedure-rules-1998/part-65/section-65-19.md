## 65.19. Allocation[^key-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3]

*Extent: E+W*

When the court decides the track for the claim[^key-ea17f76d08d86ae8210901007c3a47d4], the matters to which it shall have regard include—

- (a) the matters set out in rule 26.13[^key-d81d773f1e1909164e02bcd609886c1e]; and
- (b) the nature and extent of the conduct alleged.
