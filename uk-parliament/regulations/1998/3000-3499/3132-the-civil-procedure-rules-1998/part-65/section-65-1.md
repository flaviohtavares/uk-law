# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 65.1

## 65.1. Scope of this Part[^key-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part contains rules—

- (a) in Section I, about injunctions under the Housing Act 1996;
- (b) in Section II, about applications by local authorities under section 91(3) of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 for a power of arrest to be attached to an injunction;
- (c)[^key-8dd1ae6aefd8a36280bd58df8934ea4f] in Section III, about claims for demotion orders under the Housing Acts 1985 and 1988, and for prohibited conduct standard contract orders under the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, and related proceedings;
- (d) in Section IV, about anti-social behaviour orders under the Crime and Disorder Act 1998;
- (e) in Section V, about claims under section 3 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 ;[^key-86fe040c7144f4f3828bfb42d6cd9013] [^key-f934d5136a34624cf06157d182cbd235]...
- (f)[^key-b14191a306411464e9aa8cda5eb49468] [^key-26ea25bf854163c8bbaf14a1445280a9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^key-b0274332aed9a11dcccc99aa13a51286] in Section VI, about applications for drinking banning orders and interim drinking banning orders under sections 4 and 9 of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006; [^key-0b80d1342af4b58ed0ed3ba52694d909]...
- (g) in Section VII, about parenting orders under sections 26A and 26B of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 ;[^key-a6c80717c9e87e270260d9681d159ca0] [^key-45eed14de8dc21b0edf1b02a4a9e8ba5]...
- (h) in Section VIII, about injunctions under the Policing and Crime Act 2009 ; and[^key-f36eee4365bd5c8842b2f6d2a31d6955]
- (i)[^key-244571b6e79285299ba32e7d321f2d17] in Section IX, about respect orders under Part A1 of the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014
