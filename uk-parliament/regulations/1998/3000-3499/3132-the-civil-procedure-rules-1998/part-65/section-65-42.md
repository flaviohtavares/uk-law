# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 65.42

## 65.42. Scope of this Section and interpretation[^key-c39a8680db089b17b6fa9bcc62f4e999][^key-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to applications for an injunction and other related proceedings under Part 4 of the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Injunctions: gang-related violence) and under Part 1 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Injunctions)[^key-593fe3ca1f19e35be0ab0f2f1fe76337].

(2)[^key-070e740d80be65b08b995caf008a1acd] In this Section—

- (a) “the 2009 Act” means the Policing and Crime Act 2009”; and
- (b) “the 2014 Act” means the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014.
