## 65.13. Other demotion claims, prohibited conduct standard contract order claims or suspension claims[^key-fe983083cb3d124dd305ab0b59611da3][^key-64c639554e295511454b1998189ee11b][^key-8796056e98fd533ccc41d1f5f30dd0e5]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a demotion claim or suspension claim (or both)[^key-a2c2979570e02515cbbe0e2d98349e9a] , or a prohibited conduct standard contract order claim[^key-5c908d9db4f4a93737911f2475c46067] is made other than in a possession claim or a Renting Homes possession claim[^key-6cae5530702b3016c6780996bc1f7ee4], rules 65.14 to 65.19 apply.
