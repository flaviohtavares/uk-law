## 58.16. Enforcement[^key-909faf73fb3a482fc508eef591c2ec12][^key-255c3f534ee6812a0c822c87113f2483]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise orders, any jurisdiction relating to the enforcement of a Commercial Court judgment or order for the payment of money may be exercised by a Master of the King’s Bench Division or a District Judge.
