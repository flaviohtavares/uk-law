## 58.4. Proceedings in the commercial list[^key-255c3f534ee6812a0c822c87113f2483]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A commercial claim may be started in the commercial list.

(2) Rule 30.5 applies[^key-4647d7876f3d210bde0fafa8bdf0ce6a] applies to claims in the commercial list, except that a Commercial Court judge may order a claim to be transferred to any other specialist list.

(Rule 30.5(3) provides that an application for the transfer of proceedings to or from a specialist list must be made to a judge dealing with claims in that list)
