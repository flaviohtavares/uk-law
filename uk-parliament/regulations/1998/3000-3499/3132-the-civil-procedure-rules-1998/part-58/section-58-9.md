# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 58.9

## 58.9. Admissions[^c-2bdfbcca0f1a11d8763078c92f4e7357][^c-255c3f534ee6812a0c822c87113f2483]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the claim is for a specified amount of money and the defendant admits part of the claim, the claimant may apply under Part 14 for judgment on the admission but rules 14.2 and 14.6(5) to (7) do not apply.
