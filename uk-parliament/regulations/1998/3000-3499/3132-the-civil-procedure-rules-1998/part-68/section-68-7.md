## 68.7. Steps after reference accepted[^c-d395b6a9399799065b99ecd5c10f97e4]

*Extent: E+W*

When the Court of Appeal accepts a reference made to it under section 6A or 6B of the Act, it must give directions for the management of the case and set a timetable for the steps to be taken between the giving of directions and the hearing of the reference. (Section 6B(5) of the Act requires a court to which a reference is made under that section to accept the reference.)
