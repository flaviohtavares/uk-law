## 61.14. Enforcement[^key-ad7d9e4617a68545a60173e6c9e7e5e2][^key-15c3c276e68ba8db6c1696646926b71b]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise orders, any jurisdiction relating to the enforcement of an Admiralty Court judgment or order for the payment of money may be exercised by a Master of the King’s Bench Division.
