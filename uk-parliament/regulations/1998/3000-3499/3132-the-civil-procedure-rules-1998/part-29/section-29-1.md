# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 29.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29.1. Scope of this Part[^key-c4bcbdcf68a1c62043e204cedc570fd3]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part contains general provisions about management of cases allocated to the multi-track and applies only to cases allocated to that track.

- (Part 27 sets out the procedure for claims allocated to the small claims track)
- (Part 28 sets out the procedure for claims allocated to the fast track or intermediate track[^key-190ca3de4d7ca1c126084617aa153c47])

(2)[^key-647bbc96ea8891cf75c0e96371fa213f] When drafting case management directions both the parties and the court should take as their starting point any relevant model directions and standard directions which can be found online at www.justice.gov.uk/courts/procedure-rules/civil and adapt them as appropriate to the circumstances of the particular case.
