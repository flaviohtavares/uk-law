## 57.A3. Business and Property Work in the County Court[^key-2fe2ed1e7e079e68aba90d0b46d78d14]

*Extent: E+W*

Proceedings which may be started in the Business and Property Division may instead be started in and heard by the County Court in accordance with paragraph 4.1 of Practice Direction 57AA.
