## 57.A1. Interpretation[^key-2fe2ed1e7e079e68aba90d0b46d78d14]

*Extent: E+W*

In these Rules—

- (a) “**Business and Property Division at the Rolls Building**” means the Business and Property Division of the High Court at the Royal Courts of Justice, Rolls Building, London, EC4A 1NL;
- (b) “**BPD DR**” means a district registry of the High Court specified in Practice Direction 57AA;
- (c) “**Business and Property Division**” means—
  - (i) the Business and Property Division at the Rolls Building; and
  - (ii) the BPD DRs.
