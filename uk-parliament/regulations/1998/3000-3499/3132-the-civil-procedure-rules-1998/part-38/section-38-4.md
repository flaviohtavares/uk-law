## 38.4. Right to apply to have notice of discontinuance set aside[^key-cb57ee86992a44fcd0be880bb279654a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the claimant discontinues under rule 38.2(1) the defendant may apply to have the notice of discontinuance set aside<sup>(GL)</sup>.

(2) The defendant may not make an application under this rule more than 28 days after the date when the notice of discontinuance was served on them[^key-dc24d9e877f8f38fe83cc5cadfc55063].
