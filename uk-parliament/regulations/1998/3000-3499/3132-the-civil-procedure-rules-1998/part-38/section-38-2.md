## 38.2. Right to discontinue claim[^key-8d12533425a2d6af668845e8481d7a45]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) At any time, a claimant[^key-52767b05e301f72397bafa647689001b] may discontinue all or part of a claim against one or more defendants[^key-93013d8c553e3167b5946f1c28b6e10e].

(2) However—

- (a) a claimant must obtain the permission of the court if they wish to[^key-0732623cbab0fd79ff5ab7f712e8af0a] discontinue all or part of a claim in relation to which—
  - (i) the court has granted an interim injunction<sup>(GL)</sup>; or
  - (ii) any party has given an undertaking to the court;
- (b) where the claimant has received an interim payment in relation to a claim (whether voluntarily or pursuant to an order under Part 25), he may discontinue that claim only if—
  - (i) the defendant who made the interim payment consents in writing; or
  - (ii) the court gives permission;
- (c) where there is more than one claimant, a claimant may not discontinue unless—
  - (i) every other claimant consents in writing; or
  - (ii) the court gives permission.
