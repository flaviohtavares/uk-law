## 38.8. Stay of remainder of partly discontinued proceedings where costs not paid[^key-f9defd3119db3954d759bab790f5b00f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) proceedings are partly discontinued;
- (b)[^key-cf95bbd7363f2038a60feee2d8397baf] a claimant is liable to—
  - (i) pay costs under rule 38.6; or
  - (ii) make a payment pursuant to an order under section 194(3) of the Legal Services Act 2007; and
- (c) the claimant fails to pay those costs or make the payment[^key-9206a19b8b516cc074fd59222c51b735] within 14[^key-b8c3848abc557b3c4bf14f8e96821468] days of—
  - (i) the date on which the parties agreed the sum payable by the claimant; or
  - (ii) the date on which the court ordered the costs to be paid or the payment to be made[^key-3b5bb486bd84903fbba19b7f63038476].

(2) Where this rule applies, the court may stay<sup>(GL)</sup> the remainder of the proceedings until the claimant pays the whole of the costs which the claimant[^key-70528593bc6f8ec5720c6cb5ee54bd05] is liable to pay under rule 38.6 or makes the payment pursuant to an order under section 194(3) of the Legal Services Act 2007[^key-4cfa1e5a7ff3033b4c076707ef5f3a60]

(Rules[^key-b99208c90e912d2fb5e120cbc11b28af] 44.9 and 46.7[^key-2e41a73b9772afc6ccb1cd6c2716aacd] contain provisions about applying for an order under section 194(3) of the Legal Services Act 2007.)
