# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 38.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38.1. Scope of this Part[^c-d3bf9950caa40dedf0e61b0e441b860c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The rules in this Part set out the procedure by which a claimant may discontinue all or part of a claim. A “[^c-fda8a2e0d80b429c529c12f31a85c1c6]**claim**” includes a cause of action or part of one.

(2) A claimant who—

- (a) claims more than one remedy; and
- (b) subsequently abandons their[^c-05cc0cfa23f75c5085cf5c8aaf64637e] claim to one or more of the remedies but continues with their claim for the other remedies,

is not treated as discontinuing all or part of a claim for the purposes of this Part.

(The procedure for amending a statement of case, set out in Part 17, applies where a claimant abandons a claim for a particular remedy but wishes to continue with his claim for other remedies)
