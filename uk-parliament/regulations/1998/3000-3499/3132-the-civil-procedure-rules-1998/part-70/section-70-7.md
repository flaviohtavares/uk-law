## 70.7. Debt respite scheme[^c-b17933a8b519b0b4ab05be26550cdeb6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A practice direction may make provision for procedure relating to any debt respite scheme established under section 7 of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018.

(2) The practice direction may modify or disapply any provision of these Rules as appropriate in relation to such a debt respite scheme.
