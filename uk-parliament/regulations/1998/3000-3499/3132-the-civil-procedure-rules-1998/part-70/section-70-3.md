## 70.3. Transfer of proceedings for enforcement[^c-68a21c6df481fa6f1208544882566509][^c-c1743615e67bb33d19bb3a71e755f3f8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-89e81370a482cc59f62245d76cec3fca] Subject to rule 83.17, a judgment creditor wishing to enforce a High Court judgment or order in the County Court must apply to the High Court for an order transferring the proceedings.

(2) A practice direction may make provisions about the transfer of proceedings for enforcement.

( Rule 83.19[^c-8fdf63d25a112486df729100afe2f54e] contains provisions about the transfer of County Court[^c-58ddedc447e45f27157c93586a6e9864] proceedings to the High Court for enforcement.)
