## 83.27. Saving for enforcement by contempt proceedings[^key-137c8d25ed29d99d3830c1599c1e91c8]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in rules 83.23 and 83.26 prejudices any right to bring contempt proceedings under Part 81.
