## 88.9. Fixing of directions hearing date[^key-270bf180eeba0836ea41457ffcd264be]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) When the court issues the claim form it must[^key-c662dbd960c5a561c3590a7f266946b6] fix a date for a directions hearing.

(2) Unless the court directs otherwise, the directions hearing will be not less than 14 days but not more than 28 days after the date of issue of the claim form.
