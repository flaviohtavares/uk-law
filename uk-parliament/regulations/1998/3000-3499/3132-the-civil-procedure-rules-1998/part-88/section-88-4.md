## 88.4. Application for permission to impose a TEO[^key-270bf180eeba0836ea41457ffcd264be]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 3(1)(b) of the Act for permission to impose a TEO must be made by the Secretary of State filing with the court—

- (a) a statement of reasons to support the application;
- (b) any relevant material of which the Secretary of State is aware at that stage; and
- (c) any written submissions.
