## 59.1. Scope of this Part and interpretation[^c-b86c9615e686de7195c572935f3010d3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies to claims in Circuit Commercial Courts[^c-5a79b7cd4bd5a21d470d77d134615b1c].

(2) A claim may only be started in a Circuit Commercial Court[^c-8d3dfc2566e0c7e6b5c1c248dd5f1f24] if it—

- (a) relates to a commercial or business matter in a broad sense; and
- (b)[^c-71bfd241596d94efffe8a7893aa7aaf3] is not required to proceed in another specialist list of the Business and Property Division[^c-839dba673fade2dae7d40b4e131b2e7e].

(3) In this Part and Practice Direction 59[^c-69ed1e65eab6544cbe2fee54f0b72bd2]—

- (a)[^c-466aab2ea164d4ceed504f26e9a6341f] “Circuit Commercial Court” means a specialist list established within the courts listed in Practice Direction 59[^c-1829915568a78afdedf8602760586ee7];
- (b) “Circuit Commercial claim[^c-6518154761a4d5784babccb5c8b1e020]” means a claim proceeding in a Circuit Commercial Court; and
- (c) “Circuit Commercial judge[^c-7729e8f12b5804ab48683ef20a6fbd32]” means a judge authorised to sit in a Circuit Commercial Court.
