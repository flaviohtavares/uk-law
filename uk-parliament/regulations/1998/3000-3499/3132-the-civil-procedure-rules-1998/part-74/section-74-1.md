## 74.1. Scope of this Part and interpretation[^c-3bdde8a4ae2efc3f1bdb3d288be72b56]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section I of this Part applies to the enforcement in England and Wales of judgments of foreign courts.

(2) Section II applies to the enforcement in foreign countries of judgments of the High Court and of the County Court[^c-79480a7000b823d08715ca1dc59d2610].

(3) Section III applies to the enforcement of United Kingdom judgments in other parts of the United Kingdom.

(4)[^c-ccd07a97fadd336e91d3926b1b27a021] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^c-d8d91d494d9abc192e6d6b81d4938ea8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4B)[^c-22d5e93dea6511516cbd1d9eb9fef406] Section VI applies to—

- (a)[^c-5dc27ea306c11977d8485b2ec52670d8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the enforcement in England and Wales of certified protection measures from Member States of the European Union other than [^c-e718a15175349a5f2cb07c38f6345383]... Denmark.

(5) In this Part—

- (a) “the 1920 Act” means the Administration of Justice Act 1920[^f00016];
- (b) “the 1933 Act” means the Foreign Judgments (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1933[^f00017];
- (c) “the 1982 Act” means the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982[^f00018];
- (d)[^c-27507d9e78c241fa26ae8f2a410ad9ac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-27507d9e78c241fa26ae8f2a410ad9ac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-27507d9e78c241fa26ae8f2a410ad9ac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c-8564c3b5e4ca102b22325b268f9e8e51] “the 2005 Hague Convention” means the Convention on Choice of Court Agreements concluded on 30th June 2005 at The Hague[^c-3c4fab44caa89450850763db05f8c75f];[^c-6858b9879bd6ce6bdb728dc876446015]
- (h)[^c-ac4ef30082272735f355f8e8eb92050b] “**the 2019 Hague Convention**” means the Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments in Civil or Commercial Matters concluded on 2nd July 2019 at The Hague.
