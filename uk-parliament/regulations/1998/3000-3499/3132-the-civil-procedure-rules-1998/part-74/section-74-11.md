## 74.11. Authentic instruments and court settlements[^key-a1759f20c8ab8e5700e795b7b2cd1099][^key-3bdde8a4ae2efc3f1bdb3d288be72b56]

*Extent: E+W*

The rules governing the registration of judgments under the 1982 Act [^key-76b3e9f297d80c5b8c172cf4f3fae440]... apply as appropriate and with any necessary modifications for the enforcement of court settlements which are subject to article 12 of the 2005 Hague Convention[^key-fabd6d645eb9dbb9bd6569b96cfdf0a1] or article 11 of the 2019 Hague Convention[^key-8e31b9af91381691e9839092fee4129e].
