## 74.12. Application for a certified copy of a judgment[^key-3bdde8a4ae2efc3f1bdb3d288be72b56]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section applies to applications—

- (a) to the High Court under section 10 of the 1920 Act;
- (b) to the High Court or to the County Court[^key-06ee65836197b66ca787a749c33c5656] under section 10 of the 1933 Act; or[^key-cef95b31b8d758370a5c614c50c52d74]
- (c) to the High Court or to the County Court under section 12 of the 1982 Act;[^key-a9bb1ca7e5b85d0055f848ccc04771dc]...
- (d)[^key-b4875471d54ed3e7b19303fbc38c5f08] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-8149a180d79a9edc43ce79d09343d383] A judgment creditor who wishes to enforce in a foreign country a judgment obtained in the High Court or in the County Court must apply for a certified copy of the judgment.[^key-33445887a077d31022adbc88eaaa76e6]

(3) The application may be made without notice.
