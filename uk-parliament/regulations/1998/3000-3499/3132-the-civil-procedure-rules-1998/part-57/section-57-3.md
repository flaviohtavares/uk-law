## 57.3. How to start a probate claim[^c-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841]

*Extent: E+W*

A probate claim must be commenced—

- (a) in the relevant office (and in the Business and Property Division, in a Property, Trusts and Probate List)[^c-1760b88f6e5a2abbb799e589c90cbbc9]; and
- (b) using the procedure in Part 7.
