# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 57.12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**57.12.**[^c-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841] (1) This Section contains rules about claims for the rectification of a will.

(Section 20 of the Administration of Justice Act 1982[^f00010] provides for rectification of a will. Additional provisions are contained in rule 55 of the Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987[^f00011].)

(2) Every personal representative of the estate shall be joined as a party.

(3) Practice Direction 57[^c-8e8a979d54792b828c231226395d52d7] makes provision for lodging the grant of probate or letters of administration with the will annexed in a claim under this Section.
