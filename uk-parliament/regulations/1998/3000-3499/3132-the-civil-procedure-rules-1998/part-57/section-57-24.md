## 57.24. Requirement to send copy of declaration to the Public Guardian[^key-70a4dea53035679aabc8a0a080f0475c][^key-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a declaration of presumed death made under the 2013 Act satisfies section 3(3)(a) or (b) of that Act, the court must send a copy of the declaration to the Public Guardian.
