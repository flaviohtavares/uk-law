## 57.32. Requirement to send copy of all orders made to the Public Guardian[^c-a1440193a250a97940c9c0260892fbbe][^c-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the court makes a guardianship order, a revocation/variation order, an order relating to the functions of a guardian, or an order granting or refusing permission to intervene, it shall send a copy of such order to the Public Guardian.
