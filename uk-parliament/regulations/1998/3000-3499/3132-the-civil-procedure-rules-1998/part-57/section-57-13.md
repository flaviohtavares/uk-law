# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 57.13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**57.13.**[^key-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841] (1) This Section contains rules about claims and applications for substitution or removal of a personal representative.

(2)[^key-a546e2a1d7a28e58ee0a9d4373c82963] Claims under this Section must be started in a Property, Trusts and Probate List of the Business and Property Division.

(Section 50 of the Administration of Justice Act 1985[^f00012] gives the High Court power to appoint a substitute for, or to remove, a personal representative.)

(3) Every personal representative of the estate shall be joined as a party.

(4) Practice Direction 57[^key-5bccaeeb4dda7aa0b7bccb132619ef66] makes provision for lodging the grant of probate or letters of administration in a claim under this Section.

(5) If substitution or removal of a personal representative is sought by application in existing proceedings, this rule shall apply with references to claims being read as if they referred to applications.
