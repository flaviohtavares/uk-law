# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 57.2

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57.2. General[^key-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Section contains rules about probate claims.

(2) Probate claims in the High Court are assigned to a Property, Trusts and Probate List of the Business and Property Division[^key-2cf5b450caa6e5016bb6f62bbe0d8f11].

(3)[^key-0b268bd4ae6dddc58cd8c36bd68685eb] Probate claims in the County Court must be started in accordance with paragraph 4.1(b) of Practice Direction 57AA.

(4) All probate claims are allocated to the multi-track.
