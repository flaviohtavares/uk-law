## 57.18. Proceedings to be in the High Court[^key-735f277f3c86208ee3df2d01b2c996f0][^key-cde46d73152a6807962bb3825076a841]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Proceedings under the 2013 Act must be issued in the High Court in either—

- (a) a Property, Trusts and Probate List of the Business and Property Division[^key-1a6dbbfec56e34160671147217fa3b6b]; or
- (b) the Family Division.

(2) The Civil Procedure Rules apply to proceedings under the 2013 Act which are brought in the Family Division, except that the provisions of the Family Procedure Rules 2010 relating to the drawing up and service of orders apply instead of the provisions in Part 40 and Practice Direction 40B.
