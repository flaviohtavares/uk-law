# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 55.23

## 55.23. Service[^c-7f5468e2198be4b63f1b69338a07f362][^c-6d372728ca614d30331c2a6aa037d325]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Within 24 hours of the issue of the application, the claimant must serve on the defendant—

- (a) the claim form;
- (b) the application notice together with the written evidence in support; and
- (c) a blank form for the defendant’s witness statement (as set out in Practice Direction 55A[^c-6ba10bc16c558f6f0195861db910a743]) which must be attached to the application notice.

(2) The claimant must serve the documents listed in paragraph (1) in accordance with rule 55.6(a).

(3) At or before the hearing the claimant must file a certificate of service in relation to the documents listed in paragraph (1) and rule 6.17(2)(a)[^c-2f0589ea2d53af0e0822f0440d25a5fd] does not apply.
