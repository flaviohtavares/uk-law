## 55.14. Defence[^key-6d372728ca614d30331c2a6aa037d325]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A defendant who wishes to—

- (a) oppose the claim; or
- (b) seek a postponement of possession in accordance with rule 55.18,
  - must file his defence within 14 days after service of the claim form.

(2) The defence should be in the form set out in Practice Direction 55A[^key-12ce2ea81e6731cfbf2a45cb519128bd].
