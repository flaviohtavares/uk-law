## 55.24. Defendant’s response[^c-7f5468e2198be4b63f1b69338a07f362][^c-6d372728ca614d30331c2a6aa037d325]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) At any time before the hearing the defendant may file a witness statement in response to the application.

(2) The witness statement should be in the form set out in Practice Direction 55A[^c-91255a4fbf4ae51617adf8fc8c53430b].
