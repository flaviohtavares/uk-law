## 55.4. Particulars of claim[^key-6d372728ca614d30331c2a6aa037d325]

*Extent: E+W*

The particulars of claim must be filed and served with the claim form.

(Part 16 and Practice Direction 55A[^key-4657e86877b5fe1a7a7c9ed5716b6572] provide details about the contents of the particulars of claim)
