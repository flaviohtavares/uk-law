# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 5.4B

## 5.4B. Supply of documents to a party from court records[^M_F_c4b32ed4-5017-4d33-8a0e-58b4869fb79f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party to proceedings may, unless the court orders otherwise, obtain from the records of the court a copy of any document listed in paragraph 4.2A of Practice Direction 5A[^c-e4c0a0e03d92dfb47401cf9f30202e9e].

(2) A party to proceedings may, if the court gives permission, obtain from the records of the court a copy of any other document filed by a party or communication between the court and a party or another person.
