## 5.5. Filing and sending documents[^key-7fd1bbd4c4d7a5a6bbd76e2391c1f94e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A practice direction may make provision for documents to be filed or sent to the court by—

- (a) the use of an electronic filing and case management system[^key-126c3eb89c0f8366f759e4a32d62df7a]; or
- (b) other electronic means.

(2) Any such practice direction may—

- (a) provide that only particular categories of documents may be filed or sent to the court by such means;
- (b) provide that particular provisions only apply in specific courts; [^key-da97869572fa1e3099b923e71cbebf90]...
- (c) specify the requirements that must be fulfilled for any document filed or sent to the court by such means ;[^key-dce4d1d0e559bc76c8cdaba1702732c0]
- (d)[^key-4b10f487f2efb9ced2b6065b8102c7bf] modify or disapply any provision of these rules in relation to the use of any court electronic filing and case management system.
