# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 63A.2

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63A.2. Specialist list[^c-d8c0f345ffc2013396e365a50f61eb56]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Financial List is a single specialist list. Claims in the Financial List must[^c-f5a0a205737623ac63cebf332339a07a] be commenced in the Business and Property Division at the Rolls Building[^c-5d5318c5bd6555e604af6e5610943dee].

(2) The President of the Business and Property Division[^c-909c6464aeb7cb8b08c9720ab0ec4ced] and the Judge in Charge of the Commercial Court have joint overall responsibility for all claims in the Financial List.
