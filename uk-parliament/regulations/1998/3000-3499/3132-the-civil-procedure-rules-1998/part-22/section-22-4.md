## 22.4. Power of the court to require a document to be verified[^key-c02a77b7802c512fec0aab090212372d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may order a person who has failed to verify a document in accordance with rule 22.1 to do so[^key-05b0980549ef97ff304f91d6950e3188].

(2) Any party may apply for an order under paragraph (1).

(Rule 32.14 states that verifying a statement of case containing a false statement without an honest belief in its truth may result in proceedings for contempt of court.)[^key-4b1c1289c9dece8dba3716022708992b]
