## 22.2. Failure to verify a statement of case[^c-317d8de9905732a10649fcaf93e080db]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a party fails to verify their[^c-985a5c2e7c89ef9e9167945a144c3edc] statement of case by a statement of truth—

- (a) the statement of case shall remain effective unless struck out; but
- (b) the party may not rely on the statement of case as evidence of any of the matters set out in it.

(2) The court may strike out<sup>(GL)</sup> a statement of case which is not verified by a statement of truth.

(3) Any party may apply for an order under paragraph (2).
