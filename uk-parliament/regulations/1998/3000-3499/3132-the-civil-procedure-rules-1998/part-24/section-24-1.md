# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 24.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

## 24.1. Scope of this Part[^c-f30ac654e508e5ceefe71bac04fd4b93]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part—

- (a) sets out a procedure by which the court may decide a claim or issue without a trial;
- (b) is subject to other Parts making special provision for particular types of case.
