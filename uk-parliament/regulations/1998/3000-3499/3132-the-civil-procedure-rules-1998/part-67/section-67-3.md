## 67.3. Proceedings under Part III of the Act[^key-0db9e51b0f85054bd91a5747c4c888a8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A claim for an order under Part III of the Act for the assessment of costs payable to a solicitor by his client—

- (a) which—
  - (i) relates to contentious business done in the County Court[^key-06ee65836197b66ca787a749c33c5656]; and
  - (ii) is within the financial limit of the County Court’s[^key-ddbb40e7e5907cadebba293156b569a1] jurisdiction specified in section 69(3) of the Act[^f00005],

  may be made in the County Court[^key-af7a86deee608bc3ebaefce7315c94e6];
- (b) in every other case, must be made in the High Court.

(Rule 30.2 makes provision for the County Court[^key-19e20284dd1aa25912c6b77373fd0c8e] to transfer the proceedings to another County Court[^key-58ddedc447e45f27157c93586a6e9864] for detailed assessment of costs)

(Provisions about the venue for detailed assessment proceedings are contained in rule 47.4 and[^key-ddc90a1b5a83d3ce14bb034a143e4abb] paragraphs 4.1 to 4.3 of Practice Direction 47[^key-60284c55488dc0135a32c5d3416e7c11] [^key-71b215b0be799f01e853448bd4b45789]...)

(2) A claim for an order under Part III of the Act must be made—

- (a) by Part 8 claim form; or
- (b) if the claim is made in existing proceedings, by application notice in accordance with Part 23.

(A model form of claim form is annexed to the Costs Practice Direction)

(3) A claim in the High Court under Part III of the Act may be determined by—

- (a) a High Court judge;
- (b) a Master, a costs judge or a District Judge[^key-69c7df68a08f5511cb1a0d57cd3cecd5] of the Principal Registry of the Family Division; or
- (c) a District Judge, if the costs are for—
  - (i) contentious business done in proceedings in the District Registry[^key-f87ecd200387c28eba400f906eaa6696] of which he is the District Judge;
  - (ii) contentious business done in proceedings in the County Court within the district of that District Registry[^key-ad3c34dafa74d6b0f8c5778072ce7d27]; or
  - (iii) non-contentious business.
