## 15.7. Making a counterclaim[^c-a0c026b05ceb6edf957d3e1ef6fc9787]

*Extent: E+W*

Part 20 applies to a defendant who wishes to make a counterclaim. Where a defendant serves a counterclaim the defence and counterclaim must, other than for good reason[^c-3e57cd388945b5fe0a83b9a0c949930c] form one document with the counterclaim following the defence.
