## 60.8. Enforcement[^key-6099d8e41530bf2987819c59dc736dcc][^key-8edad6e67510684c2fbf23d2bf12608e]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise orders, any jurisdiction relating to the enforcement of a judgment or order for the payment of money of the Technology and Construction Court of the High Court in London may be exercised by a Master of the King’s Bench Division.
