## 60.6. Case management[^c-8edad6e67510684c2fbf23d2bf12608e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) All TCC claims are treated as being allocated to the multi-track and Part 26 does not apply.

(2) Part 29 and Practice Direction 29[^c-8752117b5dcc76571ff007d7b9355c60] apply to the case management of TCC claims, except where they are varied by or inconsistent with Practice Direction 60[^c-f952161d0f410ffca07726621870cf2d].
