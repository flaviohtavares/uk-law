## 60.4. Issuing a TCC claim[^c-8edad6e67510684c2fbf23d2bf12608e]

*Extent: E+W*

A TCC claim must be issued in—

- (a) the High Court in London;
- (b) a district registry of the High Court; or
- (c) a County Court hearing centre[^c-2b2eeaefd6c7a879c0218d59e4d8907b] specified in Practice Direction 60[^c-919b648bf46ef71cfcd68747338bedb5].
