## 35.8. Instructions to a single joint expert[^c-4ef2bc1b76d8f11021fbe0e473d338dc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the court gives a direction under rule 35.7 for a single joint expert to be used, any relevant[^c-ed91e609349bff6aa07bca928c40f089] party may give instructions to the expert.

(2)[^c-5248af2c1675e4c4d36281e642bdf627] When a party gives instructions to the expert that party must, at the same time, send a copy to the other relevant parties.

(3) The court may give directions about—

- (a) the payment of the expert’s fees and expenses; and
- (b) any inspection, examination or experiments which the expert wishes to carry out.

(4) The court may, before an expert is instructed—

- (a) limit the amount that can be paid by way of fees and expenses to the expert; and
- (b) direct that some or all of the relevant[^c-240a3f7e1d97b699e276088551a3030a] parties pay that amount into court.

(5) Unless the court otherwise directs, the relevant[^c-2ec3dd51f194a8b7fc17bfcceb66c730] parties are jointly and severally liable<sup>(GL)</sup> for the payment of the expert’s fees and expenses.
