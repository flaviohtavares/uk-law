## 35.5. General requirement for expert evidence to be given in a written report[^key-5c7e5fea3ef9676c81f39fdf9b1e3862]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Expert evidence is to be given in a written report unless the court directs otherwise.

(2) If a claim is on the small claims track or the[^key-49edc8e8164291c9b767f1be68d6b7f0] fast track, the court will not direct an expert to attend a hearing unless it is necessary to do so in the interests of justice.
