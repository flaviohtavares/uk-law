# The Civil Procedure Rules 1998, section 18.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/3132

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18.1. Obtaining further information[^key-526223d6bcfaed23fc64fcca213cc305]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may at any time order a party to—

- (a) clarify any matter which is in dispute in the proceedings; or
- (b) give additional information in relation to any such matter,

whether or not the matter is contained or referred to in a statement of case.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to any rule of law to the contrary.

(3) Where the court makes an order under paragraph (1), the party against whom it is made must—

- (a) file their[^key-2faa6ed31e669061d50615abfb814639] response; and
- (b) serve it on the other parties,

within the time specified by the court.

(Part 22 requires a statement of case, including a[^key-cfae7d70cc1f711ba70ce62ba7fe152d] response to be verified by a statement of truth)

(Part 53 (defamation) restricts requirements for providing further information about sources of information in defamation claims)[^key-f5adbac925889bd9e647f9d77598956a]
