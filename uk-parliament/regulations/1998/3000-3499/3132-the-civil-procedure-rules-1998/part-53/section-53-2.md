## 53.2. Specialist list[^key-8e32cbaee4bfbf09ac4502b3ebbc34e0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Media and Communications List is a specialist list of the High Court.

(2) One of the Judges of the King’s[^key-3a36723cc472c65e0c7a1e6423f7d28d] Bench Division shall be nominated by the President of the[^key-8ddcff453a68a7ca17a95480d7c86bc1] King’s Bench Division to be the Judge in Charge of the Media and Communications List.

(3) A Media and Communications List Judge is a judge authorised by the President of the King’s[^key-db36b8813644bfbb0b6a5a6cab7b8020] Bench Division, in consultation with the President of the Business and Property Division[^key-276d6b0f9e18e979aee1932b53f7760d], to hear claims in the Media and Communications List.

(4) All proceedings in the Media and Communications List will be heard by a Media and Communications Judge, or by a Master of the King’s[^key-f8a39fb6b5e156155b39ba4d7f5894c5] Bench Division, except that—

- (a) another judge of the King’s[^key-03f9e5419d5655cf2fa26831f7da8387] Bench Division or Business and Property Division[^key-5fd101d4c427d8c9e32e9b8eb92245aa] may hear urgent applications if no Media and Communications Judge is available; and
- (b) unless the court otherwise orders, any application relating to enforcement of a Media and Communications List order or judgment for the payment of money will be dealt with by a Master of the King’s[^key-9977824398f51701f2b1d117d635d969] Bench Division or District Judge.
