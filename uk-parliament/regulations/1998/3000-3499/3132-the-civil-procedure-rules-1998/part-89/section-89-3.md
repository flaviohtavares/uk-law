## 89.3. Where to make applications[^c-e1ad94f86f9129c74f4791752491e406]

*Extent: E+W*

An application to the County Court for an attachment of earnings order must be made to the Civil National Business Centre[^c-8bb8db4ef82a67cd34319a37fcf7394d].
