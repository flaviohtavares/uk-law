# The Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) (Revocation) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3148

TELEGRAPHS

The Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) (Revocation) Order 1998

Made

16th December 1998

Coming into force

7th January 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of 16th December 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 6 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) (Revocation) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 7th January 1999.

**2.** The Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) Order 1954[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) Order 1954 (S.I. [1954/488](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1954/488)).
