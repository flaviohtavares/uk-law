# The Scottish Borders (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3103 (S. 186)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Scottish Borders (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998

Made

4th December 1998

Coming into force

1st January 1999

Whereas under section 17(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00001], as read with Schedule 5 to that Act[^f00002], the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland on 6th August 1998 submitted to the Secretary of State a report on a review together with its proposals for the future electoral arrangements for the area of Scottish Borders Council;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 233(3) of, and Schedule 5 to, the said Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Borders (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st January 1999.

## 2. Interpretation

In the Schedule to this Order–

- (a) a reference to–
  - (i) an electoral ward is a reference to that electoral ward as constituted in this Order; and
  - (ii) a road, footpath, railway line, river, burn or canal shall, where the context so admits, be construed as a reference to the centre line of the feature concerned; and
- (b) the following abbreviations are used:–

- “EW” for electoral ward;
- “NG Ref” for National Grid Reference as published on Ordnance Survey Maps.

## 3. Electoral arrangements

The area of Scottish Borders Council shall be divided into 34 wards, having the names, contents and boundaries set forth in the Schedule to this Order.

Henry B McLeish

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to proposals made by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland for the future electoral arrangements for the area of Scottish Borders Council. The proposals follow on the review of the whole of Scotland undertaken by the Commission in terms of Schedule 5 to the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973. The area of Scottish Borders Council is divided into 34 electoral wards as described in the Schedule to the Order. The Order makes minor modifications to the boundaries of Wards 22 and 23 as compared to the proposals submitted to the Secretary of State by the Commission. In terms of paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994, the wards apply with effect from the ordinary election of councillors due to take place in May 1999.
