# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) (Fees) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3087

COMPANIES

The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) (Fees) Regulations 1998

Approved by Parliament

Made

9th December 1998

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1998

Coming into force

1st March 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 708(1) and (2) of the Companies Act 1985[^f00001] and by regulation 4(4) of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to, the Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) (Fees) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st March 1999.

**2.** In these Regulations–

- “the principal Regulations” means the Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996;
- “set of microfiche copies” means a set of copies of the records relating to one investment company with variable capital kept by the registrar of companies in the form of microfiche copies; and
- “investment company with variable capital” means a body incorporated by virtue of regulation 3(1) of the principal Regulations.

**3.** The fees set out in the second column of the Schedule to these Regulations shall be the fees payable in respect of the matters set out in the first column of that Schedule.

Kim Howells

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competition and Consumer Affairs,

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be paid in respect of functions performed by the registrar of companies under the Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996. Paragraph 5 of Schedule 1, and regulation 4(4) of those Regulations apply the fee-prescribing powers of the Secretary of State under section 708 of the Companies Act 1985 to investment companies with variable capital.

The Regulations prescribe the following fees:

- (a) for registration of an investment company with variable capital, the fee is £20 (Fee No. 1);
- (b) for registration of a copy annual report of an investment company with variable capital, the fee is £15 (Fee No. 2);
- (c) for inspection of the microfiche copies of the records of an investment company with variable capital, the fee is £5 (Fee No. 3);
- (d) for a set of microfiche copies of the records of an investment company with variable capital, if delivered at an office of the registrar, the fee is £5; if delivered by post, the fee is £8 (Fee No. 4);
- (e) for paper copies of particulars of an investment company with variable capital, which are accessible through a computer terminal, the fee varies according to the method of delivery. At an office of the registrar, the fee is 10p per screen of information on a computer terminal; if delivered by post, the fee is £4 per copy of information available on a computer terminal (Fee No. 5);
- (f) for a certified copy of, or extract from, any record relating to an investment company with variable capital, the fee is £25 (Fee No. 6);
- (g) for registration of an altered instrument of incorporation in respect of a change of name of an investment company with variable capital, the fee is £10 (Fee No. 7);
- (h) for paper copies of records relating to an investment company with variable capital, delivered at an office of the registrar, the fee depends on whether a request for inspection or for a set of microfiche copies of records is made at the same time. If such a request is made, the fee is 10p per page. If not, the fee is £5 plus £1 per document supplied (Fee No. 8); and
- (i) for paper copies of records relating to an investment company with variable capital delivered by post, the fee is £9 for one document and £2.50 for each further document relating to the same investment company with variable capital, requested at the same time (Fee No. 9).

Regulation 1 provides for the new fees to come into force on 1st March 1999.
