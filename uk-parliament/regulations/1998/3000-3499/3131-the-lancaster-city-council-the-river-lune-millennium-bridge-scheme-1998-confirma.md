# The Lancaster City Council (The River Lune Millennium Bridge) Scheme 1998 Confirmation Instrument 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3131

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Lancaster City Council (The River Lune Millennium Bridge) Scheme 1998 Confirmation Instrument 1998

Made

10th December 1998

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** This Instrument may be cited as “the Lancaster City Council (The River Lune Millennium Bridge) Scheme 1998 Confirmation Instrument 1998” and shall come into force on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**2.** The Lancashire City Council (River Lune Millennium Bridge) Scheme 1998 (“the Scheme”) is hereby confirmed with modifications.

**3.** The Scheme as confirmed is set out in the Schedule hereto (including the plan) and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plan as modified are deposited at the offices of the Department of the Environment, Transport, and the Regions, Great Minster House, 76 Marsham Street, LONDON SW1P 4DR; at the offices of Lancaster City Council at the Town Hall, Morecambe; and at the Town Hall, Lancaster.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Dr D. R. Dobbie

Regional Director

Government Office for the North East

10th December 1998

## THE SCHEDULE — THE LANCASTER CITY COUNCIL (RIVER LUNE MILLENNIUM BRIDGE) SCHEME 1998

The Lancaster City Council (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) acting on behalf of the Lancashire County Council, the local highway authority, pursuant to an agreement made under Section 101(1)(b) of the Local Government Act 1972, makes this Scheme in exercise of powers contained in Section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf:

**1.** The Council is hereby authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the River Lune at Lancaster in the district of Lancashire, the bridge (in this Scheme called “the River Lune Millennium Bridge”) specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the new River Lune Millennium Park wherein a new cycle track will be constructed connecting the existing Morecambe/Lancaster cycle track to St. Georges Quay and the existing permissive cycleway on the former railway viaduct all in the said district of Lancaster.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as the Lancaster City Council (The River Lune Millennium Bridge) Scheme 1998.
