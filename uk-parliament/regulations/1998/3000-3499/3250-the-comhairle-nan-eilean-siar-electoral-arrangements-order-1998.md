# The Comhairle nan Eilean Siar (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3250 (S. 206)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Comhairle nan Eilean Siar (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998

Made

21st December 1998

Coming into force

1st January 1999

Whereas under section 17(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00001], as read with Schedule 5 to that Act[^f00002], the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland on 15th October 1998 submitted to the Secretary of State a report on a review together with its proposals for the future electoral arrangements for the area of Comhairle nan Eilean Siar;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 233(3) of, and Schedule 5 to, the said Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Comhairle nan Eilean Siar (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st January 1999.

(2) This Order shall apply only for the purposes of elections to be held after 30th April 1999.

## 2. Interpretation

In the Schedule to this Order–

- (a) a reference to–
  - (i) an electoral ward is a reference to that electoral ward as constituted in this Order; and
  - (ii) a road, footpath, railway line, river, burn or canal shall, where the context so admits, be construed as a reference to the centre line of the feature concerned; and
- (b) the following abbreviations are used:–

- “EW” for electoral ward;
- “NG Ref” for National Grid Reference as published on Ordnance Survey Maps.

## 3. Electoral arrangements

The area of Comhairle nan Eilean Siar shall be divided into 31 wards, having the names, contents and boundaries set forth in the Schedule to this Order.

## 4. Revocation

The Western Isles Islands Area (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Henry B McLeish

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

21st December 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to proposals made by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland for the future electoral arrangements for the area of Comhairle nan Eilean Siar (the Western Isles Council). The proposals follow on the review of the whole of Scotland undertaken by the Commission in terms of Schedule 5 to the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973. The area of Comhairle nan Eilean Siar is divided into 31 electoral wards as described in the Schedule to the Order. The Order has effect for the purposes of elections held after 30th April 1999 and will thus first apply as regards the ordinary election of councillors due to take place in May 1999.
