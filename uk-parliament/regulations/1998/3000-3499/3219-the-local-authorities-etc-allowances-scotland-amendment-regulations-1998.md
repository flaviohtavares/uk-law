# The Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 3219 (S.197)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998

Made

17th December 1998

Laid before Parliament

23rd December 1998

Coming into force

14th January 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 45(4), 47 and 235(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00001] and sections 18 and 190(1) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 14th January 1999.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

The Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 5 below.

**3.** In regulation 20–

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£23.58” there shall be substituted “£24.29”; and
- (b) in each of paragraphs (b) and (c), for “£47.16” there shall be substituted “£48.57”.

**4.** In regulation 21, for “£27.70” there shall be substituted “£28.53”.

**5.** In column (2) of Schedule 2–

- (a) for “6,344” there shall be substituted “6,534”;
- (b) for “5,815” there shall be substituted “5,989”; and
- (c) for “5,286” there shall be substituted “5,445”.

## 6. Revocation

The Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1997[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Henry McLeish

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th December 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Authorities Etc. (Allowances) (Scotland) Regulations 1995, which make provision as to the payment of allowances of various sorts to members of Scottish local authorities and joint boards. The amendments all concern increases in the maximum amounts payable by way of allowances.
