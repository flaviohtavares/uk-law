# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in S.I. [1998/1296](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1296) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1998 No. 2232

AGRICULTURE

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1998

Made

9th September 1998

Laid before Parliament

11th September 1998

Coming into force

16th October 1998

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (“the Minister”) has by order designated an area in West Penwith as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 16th October 1998.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation Order 1986[^f00003].

## 3. Amendment of the principal Order

The principal Order shall have effect as if, in article 7 (saving) of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00004], for the words “Articles 4 and 5”, there were substituted “Articles 4, 5 and 6”.

Elliot Morley

Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

9th September 1998

We consent,

Jim Dowd

David Jamieson

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th September 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation Order 1986 (“the principal Order”), which designated an area in West Penwith as an environmentally sensitive area, under the power conferred by section 18 of the Agriculture Act 1986 which now enables the implementation of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2078/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2078) (OJ No. L215, 30.7.92, p. 85) on agricultural production methods compatible with the requirements of protection of the environment and the maintenance of the countryside, as last amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2772/95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1995/2772) (OJ No. L288, 1.12.95, p. 35) as rectified by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1962/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1962) (OJ No. L259, 12.10.96, p. 7).

This Order corrects an error made by the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/1296](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1296)) in amending the principal Order.

No regulatory appraisal has been prepared in respect of this Order.
