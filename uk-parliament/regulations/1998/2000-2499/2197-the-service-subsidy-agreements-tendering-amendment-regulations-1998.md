# The Service Subsidy Agreements (Tendering) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2197

TRANSPORT

The Service Subsidy Agreements (Tendering) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

4th September 1998

Laid before Parliament

11th September 1998

Coming into force

5th October 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions (as respects England), the Secretary of State for Scotland (as respects Scotland) and the Secretary of State for Wales (as respects Wales), in exercise of the powers conferred by section 91(1) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:–

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Service Subsidy Agreements (Tendering) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 5th October 1998.

**2.** (1) The Service Subsidy Agreements (Tendering) Regulations 1985[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3(1)(b)–

- (a) for “10,000”, in both places where it occurs, there shall be substituted “£12,000”, and
- (b) in paragraph (iii) for “£50,000” there shall be substituted “£70,000”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Glenda Jackson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

26th August 1998

Sam Galbraith

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

1st September 1998

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

4th September 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Service Subsidy (Tendering) Regulations 1985 so as to increase the financial threshold by reference to which certain service subsidy agreements are excluded from the tendering requirements of section 89(1) of the Transport Act 1985.
