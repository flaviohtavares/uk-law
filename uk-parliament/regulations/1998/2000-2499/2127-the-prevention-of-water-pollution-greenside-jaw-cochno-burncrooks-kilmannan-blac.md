# The Prevention of Water Pollution (Greenside, Jaw, Cochno, Burncrooks, Kilmannan, Black Linn, Greenland 1, 2 & 3, Garshake, Fin and Loch Humphrey) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2127 (S. 108)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Prevention of Water Pollution (Greenside, Jaw, Cochno, Burncrooks, Kilmannan, Black Linn, Greenland 1, 2 & 3, Garshake, Fin and Loch Humphrey) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998

Made

31st August 1998

Coming into force

19th September 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Water Pollution (Greenside, Jaw, Cochno, Burncrooks, Kilmannan, Black Linn, Greenland 1, 2 & 3, Garshake, Fin and Loch Humphrey) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 19th September 1998.

## 2. Extension of period of byelaws

The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council Prevention of Water Pollution (Greenside, Jaw, Cochno, Burncrooks, Kilmannan, Black Linn, Greenland 1, 2 & 3, Garshake, Fin and Loch Humphrey) Byelaws 1988 remain in force is hereby extended to 19th September 2000.

Philip Wright

Head of European Environment and Engineering Unit, Scottish Office

Victoria Quay Edinburgh

31st August 1998
