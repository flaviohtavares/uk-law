# The Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2190

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made

1st September 1998

Laid before Parliament

9th September 1998

Coming into force

30th September 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 52(4) of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 30th September 1998.

(2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) Order 1997[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

In article 3 (application of Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 during transitional period) of the principal Order for “31st May 1999” substitute “31st May 2000”.

## 3. Revocation

The Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1997[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

1st September 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/528](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/528)), as amended by S.I. [1997/1702](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1702), (“the principal Order”) provides that section 52(3) of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (application of Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 during transitional period) has effect as if, in relation to the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (Wales) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/339](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/339) as amended), the date mentioned in the definition of “exempting order” in section 52(3) were 31st May 1999 instead of 1st April 1997.

Article 2 of the present Order amends the principal Order to substitute the date of 31st May 2000 for that of 31st May 1999. This is in consequence of the further exemption in Wales for Welsh authorities from the competitive tendering requirements under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition) made by the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/2188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2188)).

Article 3 makes a consequential revocation of the Local Government Act 1988 (Direct Service Organisations) (Accounts etc.) (Extension) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/1702](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1702)).
