# The Horse Passports (Amendment) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct a defect in [S.I. 1997/2789](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2789) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1998 No. 2367

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Horse Passports (Amendment) Order 1998

Made

22nd September 1998

Coming into force

19th October 1998

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Horse Passports (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 19th October 1998.

## 2. Amendment to the Horse Passports Order 1997

In article 9 of the Horse Passports Order 1997[^f00002] after “The keeper of a horse” there shall be inserted the words “born on or after 1st January 1998”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 9 of the Horse Passports Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2789](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2789)) (movement of a horse) so that it only applies in relation to horses born on or after 1st January 1998. This amendment implements article 2 of Commission Decision [93/623/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0623) establishing the identification document (passport) accompanying registered equidae (OJ No. L298, 3.12.93, p.45).
