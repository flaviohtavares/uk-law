# Welsh Development Agency (Membership) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2490

INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT

WALES

Welsh Development Agency (Membership) Order 1998

Made

7th October 1998

Laid before Parliament

9th October 1998

Coming into force

1st November 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(1) of the Welsh Development Agency Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Welsh Development Agency (Membership) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st November 1998.

**2.** In this Order, references to “the Agency” are to the Welsh Development Agency.

**3.** The maximum number of members of the Agency (other than the Chairman and Deputy Chairman) shall be 13.

Ron Davies

Secretary of State for Wales

7th October 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the maximum number of members of the Welsh Development Agency (other than the chairman and deputy chairman) to 13.
