# The East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2034

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made

10th August 1998

Coming into force

1st September 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

For Article 3 of the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following Article:—

> **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be—
>
> - (a) to own and manage under section 5(1)(a) of the Act hospital accommodation and services provided at East Glamorgan General Hospital, Church Village, Pontypridd CF38 1AB and associated hospitals;
> - (b) to manage under section 5(1)(a) of the Act community health services provided from the said East Glamorgan General Hospital and to own the associated premises;
> - (c) to provide and manage under section 5(1)(b) of the Act hospital accommodation and services at Royal Glamorgan Hospital, Ynysmaerdy, Llantrisant, CF7 8XR.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Peter Hain

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

10th August 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order which established the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust to alter the purpose for which the trust has been established and to amend its functions.

The Order confers on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 and, in pursuance of that purpose, extends the functions of the trust to include the function of providing and managing hospital accommodation and services at Royal Glamorgan Hospital.
