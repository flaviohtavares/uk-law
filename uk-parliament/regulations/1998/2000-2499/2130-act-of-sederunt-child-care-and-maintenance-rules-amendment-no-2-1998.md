# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) (Amendment No.2) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1998/1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1993) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1998 No. 2130 (S. 109)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) (Amendment No.2) 1998

Made

26th August 1998

Coming into force

1st September 1998

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971([^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having approved draft rules submitted to them by the Sheriff Court Rules Council in accordance with section 34 of the said Act of 1971, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) (Amendment No.2) 1998 and shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Child Care and Maintenance Rules 1997

(1) The Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraph.

(2) In rule 3.59 (appeals)—

- (a) for paragraph (1), there shall be substituted the following:—

  > (1) An application to the sheriff to state a case for the purposes of an appeal under section 51(11)(a) of the Act shall specify the point of law upon which the appeal is to proceed or the irregularity in the conduct of the case concerned, as the case may be.

  ; and
- (b) in paragraph (2) and (3), for the words “a note of appeal”, there shall be substituted the words “the application for a stated case”.

## 3. Revocation

The Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) (Amendment) 1998[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord President, IPD

Edinburgh

26th August 1998

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends rule 3.59 (appeals) of the Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997 by:

- (a) removing references to notes of appeal and substituting therefor references to applications for a stated case; and
- (b) removing the provision relating to the time period for making appeals under section 51(11) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 which was erroneously stated as 14 days (section 51(13) of the 1995 Act provides that such appeals must be made within 28 days beginning with the date of the decision appealed against).

This Act of Sederunt entirely replaces the Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) (Amendment) 1998, which contained a drafting error and is being revoked prior to coming into force.
