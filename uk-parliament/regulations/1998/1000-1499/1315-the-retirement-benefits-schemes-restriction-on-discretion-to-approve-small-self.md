# The Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Small Self-administered Schemes) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1315

INCOME TAX

The Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Small Self-administered Schemes) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998

Made

27th May 1998

Laid before the House of Commons

29th May 1998

Coming into force

19th June 1998

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 591(6) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Small Self-administered Schemes) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 19th June 1998.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Small Self-administered Schemes) Regulations 1991[^f00002] and “regulation” means a regulation of the principal Regulations.

## 3. Amendments to the principal Regulations

In regulation 4(1)(c)[^f00003] after the words “purchase those assets” in the definition of “B” there shall be inserted “which are outstanding at that time”.

**4.** In regulation 7(2)[^f00004] after the words “purchase those assets” in the definition of “D” there shall be inserted “which are outstanding at that time”.

**5.** In regulation 7(3) after the words “purchase those assets” in the definition of “F” there shall be inserted “which are outstanding at that time”.

**6.** In regulation 9[^f00005] after sub-paragraph (b)(ii) there shall be inserted—

> - (iia) by virtue of section 3, 4 or 29 of the Pensions Act 1995[^f00006] or Article 3, 4 or 29 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00007] (prohibition, suspension or disqualification),

.

S C T Matheson

C W Corlett

Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue

27th May 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Small Self-administered Schemes) Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/1614](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1614)) (“the principal Regulations”) which impose restrictions on the Board of Inland Revenue’s discretion to approve under section 591 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 retirement benefits schemes that are small self-administered schemes.

Regulation 1 provides for citation and commencement, and regulation 2 for interpretation.

Regulations 3 to 5 make amendments of a drafting nature to regulations 4(1)(c) and 7(2) and (3) of the principal Regulations (as amended by S.I. [1998/728](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/728)).

Regulation 6 amends regulation 9 of the principal Regulations (as amended by S.I. [1998/728](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/728)) so as to provide that a pensioneer trustee’s appointment may be terminated where he is the subject of a prohibition or suspension order, or is disqualified from being a trustee, under the Pensions Act 1995 or the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.
