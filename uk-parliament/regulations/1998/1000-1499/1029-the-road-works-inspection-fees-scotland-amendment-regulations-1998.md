# The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1029 (S.58)

ROADS AND BRIDGES, SCOTLAND

The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998

Made

7th April 1998

Laid before Parliament

9th April 1998

Coming into force

1st May 1998

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 134 and 163(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st May 1998.

**2.** In regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00002] for the figure of “£12.50” there shall be substituted “£14.50”.

Henry McLeish

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th April 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These amendment Regulations provide for an increase in fee to £14.50 (previously £12.50) payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by road works authorities under regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/1676](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1676)).
