# The Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1417

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE,ENGLAND AND WALES

The Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made

5th June 1998

Coming into force

19th June 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 19th June 1998.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In the Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00004]:—

- (a) for Article 3 there shall be substituted the following Article:—

  > **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
  >
  > The trust’s functions (which include functions which the Secretary of State considers appropriate in relation to the provision of services by the trust for one or more Health Authorities) shall be—
  >
  > - (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act the premises specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services;
  > - (b) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act the premises specified in Part II of the Schedule to this Order, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services;
  > - (c) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act the premises specified in Part III of the Schedule to this Order, and there to provide and manage community health services; and
  > - (d) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act the premises specified in Part IV of the Schedule to this Order, and there to provide and manage community health services.

  ; and
- (b) at the end there shall be added the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage the premises specified in Part II and Part IV of the Schedule to the amended Order.
