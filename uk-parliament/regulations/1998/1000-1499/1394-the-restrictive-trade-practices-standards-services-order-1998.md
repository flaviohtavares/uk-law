# The Restrictive Trade Practices (Standards) (Services) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1394

RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES

The Restrictive Trade Practices (Standards) (Services) Order 1998

Made

4th June 1998

Laid before Parliament

4th June 1998

Coming into force

1st July 1998

The Secretary of State for Trade and Industry, in exercise of the power conferred on her by section 18(5) of the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Restrictive Trade Practices (Standards) (Services) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1 July 1998.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 1976 Act” means the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976;
- “standards” means, in respect of making available, supplying or obtaining any designated services within the meaning of the 1976 Act, standards of performance in the provision of the services or standards of dimension, design, quality or performance in respect of goods used in providing them.

## 2. Approval of standards

The standards specified in the Schedule to this Order, being standards adopted by Electricity Association Services Limited on 22 May 1998, are hereby approved for the purposes of section 18(5) of the 1976 Act.

Nigel Griffiths,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competition and Consumer Affairs,

Department of Trade and Industry

This Order approves standards of performance in the provision of services and standards of dimension, design, quality or performance in respect of goods used in providing them which have been adopted by Electricity Association Services Limited. The effect of the Order is that, for the purpose of determining whether an agreement is one to which the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976 applies, no account is taken of any term in the agreement by which parties or any of them agree to comply with, or to apply, the standards so approved.

The standards may be inspected at the premises of Electricity Association Services Limited, 30 Millbank, London SW1P 4RD.
