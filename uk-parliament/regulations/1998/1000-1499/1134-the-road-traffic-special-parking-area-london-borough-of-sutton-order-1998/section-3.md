# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Sutton) Order 1998, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002];
- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991;
- “the special parking area” means the area designated as a special parking area by article 4 of this Order;
- a reference in Schedule 2 to this Order to a number followed by the letter “m” is a reference to that number of metres; and
- where a road referred to in Schedule 2 to this Order (“the main road”) is joined by another road (“the side road”), whether or not that other road is referred to in that Schedule, and the footway to the main road runs on either side of the mouth of the side road, the junction between the two roads shall be taken to be an imaginary line drawn across the mouth of the side road between the respective ends of the back line of the footway to the main road on each side.
