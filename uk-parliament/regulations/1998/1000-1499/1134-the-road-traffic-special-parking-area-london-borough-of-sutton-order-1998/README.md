1998 No. 1134

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Sutton) Order 1998

Made

27th April 1998

Laid before Parliament

8th May 1998

Coming into force

1st June 1998

Whereas the council of the London borough of Sutton has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under section 76 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and the Secretary of State has consulted the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 76(2) of that Act:

NOW, the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

27th April 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and consolidates the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Sutton) Order 1994 and the Orders amending it, by continuing the designation of the London borough of Sutton, excluding the roads listed in Schedule 2, as a special parking area. Schedule 2 has been revised.
