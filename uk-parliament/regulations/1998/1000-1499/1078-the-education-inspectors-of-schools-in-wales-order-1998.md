# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1078

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1998

Made

22nd April 1998

Laid before Parliament

5th May 1998

Coming into force

26th May 1998

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 22nd day of April 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th May 1998.

**2.** Keith Robert Booker, Esquire, is hereby appointed as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales with effect from 26th May 1998.

N H Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Keith Robert Booker as one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales.
