# The Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Pitcairn) (Amendment) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1067

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Pitcairn) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made

22nd April 1998

Coming into force

23rd April 1998

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 22nd day of April 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 315(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001] and all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Pitcairn) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 23rd April 1998.

**2.** In the sections 166(1) and 177(1) set out in the Schedule to the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Pitcairn) Order 1997[^f00002] the words “damage done to a ship” shall be replaced by the words “damage done by a ship”.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Pitcairn) Order 1997 by correcting a typographical error which appears in two sections of the Schedule to the Order.
