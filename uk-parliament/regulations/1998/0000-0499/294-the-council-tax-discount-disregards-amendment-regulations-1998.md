# The Council Tax (Discount Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 294

COUNCIL TAX, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Council Tax (Discount Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

11th February 1998

Laid before Parliament

19th February 1998

Coming into force

1st April 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 116(1) of and paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 to the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Council Tax (Discount Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

## 2. Care workers' weekly remuneration

In paragraph 1(c) of the Schedule to the Council Tax (Additional Provisions for Discount Disregards) Regulations 1992[^f00002] for “£30” there is substituted “£36”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

11th February 1998

Win Griffiths

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Welsh Office

11th February 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Council Tax (Additional Provisions for Discount Disregards) Regulations 1992 provide for the conditions to be fulfilled where a care worker is to be disregarded for the purposes of discount, which has the effect of reducing the amount of council tax payable in respect of a dwelling in which he is resident. One of those conditions is that he is paid no more than a specified weekly amount. Regulation 2 raises this amount from £30 to £36.
