# The National Heritage Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 292 (C.6)

NATIONAL HERITAGE

The National Heritage Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made

16th February 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 4(2) of the National Heritage Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Heritage Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998.

**2.** The National Heritage Act 1997 shall come into force on 4 March 1998.

Chris Smith

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

16th February 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 4 March 1998 as the day for the coming into force of the National Heritage Act 1997.
