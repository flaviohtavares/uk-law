# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 69

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Application and investment of SCAVCs[^key-ac92f55e8379e53e0505781a5d34c05b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where the arrangements established by an employing authority provide for any of the SCAVCs to be used to provide death benefits, the appropriate administering authority must make such arrangements for the provision of those benefits as are specified in regulation 62(1).

(2) The administering authority must invest any SCAVCs which are not to be used to provide death benefits with an approved AVC body.

(3) Regulations 62(2) to (5) and 63(2) to (6) apply as respects SCAVCs as they apply as respects AVCs.
