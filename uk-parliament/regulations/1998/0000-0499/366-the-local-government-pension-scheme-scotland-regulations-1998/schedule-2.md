# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, SCHEDULE 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — SCHEME EMPLOYERS

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 3(3)

**1.**[^key-17b7c5479ca9c6487e7d616e789bd7a3] A council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.

**2.**[^key-eeb1ab8cc7fd60a201ba6f8b228ec9ff] A joint board or joint committee appointed under any enactment, order or scheme, all the constituent authorities of which are local authorities as aforesaid.

**3.**[^key-67d2048a6367dc1c983da2506d0b438f] A body established under section 62(1) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (new water and sewerage authorities).

**4.**[^key-af8a916a93c80c084d50c13080401c6e] The Scottish Children’s Reporter established under section 128 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.

**5.**[^key-4256ff27287b0dd079a38a9d8ae83b3b] The Scottish Environment Protection Agency established under section 20 of the Environment Act 1995.

**6.**[^key-16538da52cac561f7260ffa7652ae6f4] A board of management of a college of further education established in terms of Part I of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992.

**7.**[^key-d32831829731dce46909a1d036bed42f] A board of management of a self-governing school constituted and incorporated under section 19(2) of the Self-governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989.

**8.**[^key-a73ec32d5a449f9a9b6d9c9b57664a05] The Strathclyde Passenger Transport Authority established under section 40(1) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.

**9.**[^key-ba218c17174212a4663254235214f324] The Strathclyde Passenger Transport Executive established under section 9(1) of the Transport Act 1968[^f00093].

**10.**[^key-c454dedab818a3acb272feadec43c0e0] An area tourist board established by virtue of an order made or, as the case may be, approved under section 172, 173 or 174 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.
