# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 128

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 128. Transport employees[^c-7a2b4247a4722de4b2c9ea07f8dedf72]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) For these Regulations every employee of a subsidiary of a passenger transport executive is deemed to be in employment with that executive.

(2) In this regulation “subsidiary” has the meaning given in section 137(1) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00053].
