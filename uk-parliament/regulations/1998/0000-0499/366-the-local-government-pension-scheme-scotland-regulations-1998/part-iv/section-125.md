# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 125

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 125. Community scheme transferees[^c-3a5667ddadab556412ef92e49614c311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Community scheme transferees and their surviving spouses, dependants and children are entitled to such rights under the Scheme as are specified in guidance issued by the Government Actuary.

(2) A Community Scheme transferee is a person who became employed by a Community institution after having been employed in local government employment.
