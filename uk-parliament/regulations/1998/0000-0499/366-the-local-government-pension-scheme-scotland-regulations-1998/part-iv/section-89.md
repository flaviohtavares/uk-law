# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 89

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 89. Pension increases and cash equivalents under the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^c-5c7598122689f525b11b9745f7d96674]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter III of Part IV of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00033] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) must be paid from the appropriate fund.
