# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 127

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 127. Liability for combined benefits[^c-05a4099fb7636a59ce6882bd29e4e013]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where–

- (a) a benefit under regulation 28 or 47 of these Regulations or regulation E6(1)(c), (2)(e) or (3)(b), E16 or E18 of the 1987 Regulations is payable out of a pension fund (“the new fund”); and
- (b) by means of that benefit being payable another benefit ceases to be payable out of another fund,

such payments must be made to the authority maintaining the new fund by the authority maintaining the other fund as are indicated in guidance issued by the Secretary of State for this regulation.

(2) Such payments must be credited to the new fund.
