# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. General eligibility for membership: employees of Scheme employers etc.[^key-09b18a3799c38a4bf25e00a8fd145c0e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A person may be an active member only if this regulation or regulation 4 or Chapter I of Part V enables him to be one and he is not prevented by regulation 5.

(2) A person may be a member if he is employed by a Scheme employer.

(3) A Scheme employer is a body which is listed in Schedule 2.
