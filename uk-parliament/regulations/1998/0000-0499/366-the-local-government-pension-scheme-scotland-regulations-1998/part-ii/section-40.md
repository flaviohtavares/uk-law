# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 40

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Surviving spouse’s long-term pension[^c-a83b85e57ec946194a78861f1f1b914f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If a member dies leaving a surviving spouse, the spouse is entitled to a spouse’s long-term pension.

(2) If the deceased was an active or pensioner member, the long-term pension is payable from the end of the period for which the short-term pension is payable.

(3) The long-term pension payable on a deferred member’s death is payable from the death.

(4) If the deceased was an active member with a total membership of at least two years, the long-term pension is equal to half the ill-health pension to which the deceased would have been entitled under regulation 26 if he had become entitled to a pension under that regulation on the date he died.

(5) If the deceased was an active member with a total membership of less than two years or a deferred member, the multiplier for the long-term pension is–

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/366)

(but see regulation 41).

(6) If the deceased was a pensioner member, the long-term pension is equal to half his retirement pension immediately before the death (but see regulation 41).

(7) If there is more than one surviving spouse, they become jointly entitled under paragraph (1).
