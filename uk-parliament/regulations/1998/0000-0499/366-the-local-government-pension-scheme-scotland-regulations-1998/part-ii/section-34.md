# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1998, section 34

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Requirements as to time of payment[^c-237502907ec75acb9583b79ec5d4cb95]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Retirement benefits under this Chapter may not be paid to a person before he has retired from the employment in which he was a member.

(2) They must begin to be paid not later than the member’s 75th birthday even if he has not retired (and see also regulation 35(3)).
