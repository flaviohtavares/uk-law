# The Sole, etc. (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 1997/3054](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3054) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1998 No. 207

SEA FISHERIES

The Sole, etc. (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1998

Made

4th February 1998

Laid before Parliament

4th February 1998

Coming into force

26th February 1998

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 5(1), 20(1) and 22(2)(a) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sole, etc. (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th February 1998.

## 2. Variation

(1) The Sole, etc. (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1997[^f00002] shall be varied in accordance with paragraph (2) of this article.

(2) In article 2 paragraph (2) sub-paragraph (b), for the words “beginning with the date of coming into force of this Order and ending immediately before 1st January 1998” there shall be substituted the words “beginning immediately before 28th February 1998 and ending immediately before 1st January 1999”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Sole, etc. (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1997 to prohibit, during the period beginning immediately before 28 February 1998 and ending immediately before 1st January 1999, fishing for sole, plaice and certain other species of fish in certain sea areas.
