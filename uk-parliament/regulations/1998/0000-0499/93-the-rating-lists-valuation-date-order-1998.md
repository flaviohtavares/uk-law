# The Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 93

RATING AND VALUATION

The Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1998

Made

20th January 1998

Laid before Parliament

28th January 1998

Coming into force

18th February 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 2(3)(b) of Schedule 6 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 18th February 1998.

## 2. Valuation date

1st April 1998 is specified under paragraph 2(3)(b) of Schedule 6 (valuation for non-domestic rating) to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 as the day by reference to which the rateable values of non-domestic hereditaments are to be determined for the purposes of the local and central non-domestic rating lists which are to be compiled for England and Wales on 1st April 2000.

## 3. Revocation

The Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1992[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

20th January 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

Non-domestic rating lists for the purposes of Part III of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 are, by virtue of sections 41(2) and 52(2) of the Act to be compiled on 1st April in every fifth year after 1990. Article 2 of this Order provides for 1st April 1998 to be the day by reference to which valuations of non-domestic hereditaments are to be determined for the purposes of the lists to be compiled for England and Wales on 1st April 2000.

Article 3 revokes the Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1992.
