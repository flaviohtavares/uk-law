# The Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Turret) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 157 (S.5)

WATER, SCOTLAND

The Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Turret) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998

Made

26th January 1998

Coming into force

4th February 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Turret) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 4th February 1998.

## 2. Extension of period of byelaws

The period during which the Central Scotland Water Development Board Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Turret) Byelaws 1988 remain in force is hereby extended to 4th February 2000.

Philip Wright

Head of European Environment and Engineering Unit, Scottish Office

The Scottish Office Agriculture,

Environment and Fisheries Department Victoria Quay Edinburgh
