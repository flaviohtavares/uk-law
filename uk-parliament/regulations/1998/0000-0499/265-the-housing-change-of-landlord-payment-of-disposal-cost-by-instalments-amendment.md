# The Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 265

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

7th February 1998

Laid before Parliament

13th February 1998

Coming into force

6th March 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 104(2A), 111(a), 112 and 114(1) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 6th March 1998.

**2.** In regulation 3(3) of the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) Regulations 1990[^f00002] (payment by instalments) for “7.78 per cent.” substitute “8.17 per cent.”.

**3.** The Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1997[^f00003] are revoked.

**4.** Regulations 2 and 3 do not apply in respect of any period before the date these Regulations come into force (notwithstanding that interest included in the amount of an instalment is paid on or after that date).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Win Griffiths

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

7th February 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/1019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1019)) by increasing, from 7.78 per cent. to 8.17 per cent., the rate of interest on outstanding disposal costs which are payable by instalments under Part IV of the Housing Act 1988 (change of landlord: secure tenants). The Regulations also revoke the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2001)) which prescribed the previous interest rate. Regulation 4 preserves the previous interest rate in respect of any period before these Regulations come into force, that is, before 6th March 1998.

Paragraph 1 of Schedule 18 to the Housing Act 1996 provides that Part IV of the Housing Act 1988 is repealed, and article 3 of the Housing Act 1996 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1996 (S.I. [1996/2402](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2402)) brought that paragraph into force on 1st October 1996.

Paragraph 12 of the Schedule (transitional provisions and savings) to that Order provides that Part IV of the 1988 Act and the provisions mentioned in Part IX of Schedule 19 to the said Act of 1996 remain in force in respect of applications made under section 96 of the Housing Act 1988 (application to exercise right) which have not been disposed of before 1st October 1996 and acquisitions made before that date.
