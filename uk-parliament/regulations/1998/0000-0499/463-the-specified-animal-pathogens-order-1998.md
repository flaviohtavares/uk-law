## 1 (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Title and commencement (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a7b454f378916a9bf562ecf0d06ab378][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

This Order may be cited as the Specified Animal Pathogens Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998. *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

## 2 (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Extension of the definition of “animal”, “poultry” and “disease” (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-38fe4ab0065244bcc03eea7ead6e9bc9][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

For the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981 in relation to this Order— *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

- (a (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04] the definition of “animal” is hereby extended so as to comprise any kind of mammal except man, and any kind of four-footed beast which is not a mammal; *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
- (b (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04] the definition of “poultry” is hereby extended so as to comprise any species of bird; *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
- (c (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04] the definition of “disease” is hereby extended so as to comprise any disease of animals and poultry which may be caused by one or more specified animal pathogens. *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

## 3 (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Interpretation (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aaeb56e33e368a4f07cb1cd556861774][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04][^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

(1 (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04] In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires— *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

- “animal” includes poultry; *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
- “carrier” means any living creature except man which may carry or transmit a specified animal pathogen or the tissue, cell culture, body fluid, excreta, carcase or part of a carcase of such creature by or by means of which a specified animal pathogen may be transmitted; *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
- “specified animal pathogen” means an animal pathogen listed in the Schedule to this Order, including— *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
  - (a) intact pathogens; *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
  - (b) pathogens which have been attenuated or genetically modified by any means, and *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]
  - (c) any nucleic acid derived from an animal pathogen listed in the Schedule which could produce that pathogen when introduced into a biological system in which the nucleic acid is capable of replicating. *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

(2 (Extent: W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04] A licence or notice issued under this Order shall be in writing, may be subject to conditions and may be modified, suspended or revoked by notice in writing served on the licensee or recipient of the original notice at any time. *(Extent: W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60d71b5a5eaf6075aaaacefc1b99fa04]

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and re-enacts with amendments the Specified Animal Pathogens Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/3250](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3250)).

It extends the definition of “specified animal pathogen” to include nucleic acid derived from a pathogen listed in the Schedule and which may be capable of producing that pathogen (article 3). It prohibits the possession of the pathogens listed in Part I of the Schedule and the introduction into any animal of any pathogen listed in the Schedule, except under the authority of a licence issued by the appropriate Minister.

If any person who does not hold such a licence possesses anything which he suspects contains a pathogen listed in Part I of the Schedule he is required to notify a veterinary inspector (article 4). There are exceptions for veterinary and human medicines (article 5). There are provisions relating to powers of inspectors and production of licences (articles 6 and 7). The Order is enforced by the local authority (article 8).

The Schedule is amended to include in the list of specified animal pathogens *Babesia bovis, Babesia bigemina, Bovine leukosis virus, Brucella abortus, Cowdria ruminatum, Equine morbillivirus, Japanese encephalitis virus, Mycoplasma capricolum* sub species capripneumoniae, all rabies viruses, *Theileria parva, Trypanosoma brucei, Trypanosoma congolense, Trypanosoma simiae, and Trypanosoma vivax*. Only pathogenic or uncharacterised strains of Newcastle diesase are included. The live virus causing viral haemorrhagic disease of rabbits is included only in Part II of the Schedule. Horse pox virus and *Trypanosoma theileri* have been removed from the Schedule.

A Regulatory Appraisal has been prepared and placed in the Library of each House of Parliament. Copies can be obtained from the Animal Health (Disease Control) Division of the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, Government Buildings, Hook Rise South, Tolworth, Surbiton, Surrey KT6 7NF.
