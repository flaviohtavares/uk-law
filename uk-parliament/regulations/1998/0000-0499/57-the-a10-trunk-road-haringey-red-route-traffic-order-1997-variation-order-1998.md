# The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 57

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998

Made

15th January 1998

Coming into force

30th January 1998

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998, and shall come into force on 30th January 1998.

## 2. Variations

The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied as provided by the provisions of this Order.

**3.** In Schedule 3A item 1 is substituted as follows:

> Great Cambridge Road (A10)—east side, between a point 3.0 metres north of the party wall of Nos. 40 and 42 Great Cambridge Road and a point opposite the party wall of Nos. 36 and 38 Great Cambridge Road.

**4.** In Schedule 3A item 2 is substituted as follows:

> Great Cambridge Road (A10)—west side, between a point opposite the party wall of Nos. 19 and 21 Great Cambridge Road and a point opposite the party wall of Nos. 23 and 25 Great Cambridge Road.

**5.** In Schedule 2B new items are added as follows:
