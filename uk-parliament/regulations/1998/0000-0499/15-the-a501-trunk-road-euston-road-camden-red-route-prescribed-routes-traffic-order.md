# The A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) Red Route (Prescribed Routes) Traffic Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 15

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) Red Route (Prescribed Routes) Traffic Order 1998

Made

7th January 1998

Coming into force

9th January 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) Red Route (Prescribed Routes) Traffic Order 1998, and shall come into force on 9th January 1998.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this Order so numbered;
- (b) causing includes permitting;
- (c) “Midland Road” and “Judd Street” means those roads so named in the London Borough of Camden;
- (d) “the eastern island” means the easternmost pedestrian refuge situated in the mouth of Midland Road at its junction with the A501 Euston Road;
- (e) “the western island” means the westernmost pedestrian refuge situated in the month of Midland Road at its junction with the A501 Euston Road;

## 3. Prescribed Routes

(1) Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause any vehicle on entering the A501 Euston Road from Midland Road, Camden, on the eastern side of the eastern island, to proceed in any direction other than south into Judd Street.

(2) Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause any vehicle on entering the A501 Euston Road from Midland Road, Camden, between the eastern island and the western island, to proceed in any direction other than west into the A501 Euston Road.

## 4. Exemptions

The controls specified in article 3(1) and (2) do not apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## 5. Revocation of existing Orders

The A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) Red Route (Prescribed Routes) Experimental Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

7th January 1998
