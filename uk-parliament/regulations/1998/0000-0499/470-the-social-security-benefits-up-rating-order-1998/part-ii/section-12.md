# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1998, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Increase in rates of Disability Living Allowance

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991[^f00030] (rate of benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for the sum of £49.50 there shall be substituted the sum of £51.30;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b), for the sum of £33.10 there shall be substituted the sum of £34.30;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(c), for the sum of £13.15 there shall be substituted the sum of £13.60;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(a), for the sum of £34.60 there shall be substituted the sum of £35.85; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(b), for the sum of £13.15 there shall be substituted the sum of £13.60.
