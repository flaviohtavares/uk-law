# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1998, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Applicable amounts for Housing Benefit

(1) As from a date determined in accordance with article 1 of this Order, the sums relevant to the calculation of an applicable amount as specified in the Housing Benefit Regulations shall be the sums set out in the following provisions of this article and Schedules 9 and 10 to this Order; and for this purpose references in this article to a numbered regulation or Schedule are, unless the context otherwise requires, references to the regulation of, or the Schedule to, the Housing Benefit Regulations bearing that number.

(2) In regulations 16(b), 17(c) and paragraph 14(a) of Part III of Schedule 2 (applicable amounts), the sum specified is in each case £3,000.

(3) In regulation 18(1)[^f00041] (patients)—

- (a) in sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (d)(ii), for the sum of £15.60 there shall be substituted the sum of £16.20 in each case;
- (b) in sub-paragraphs (c)(i) and (d)(i), for the sum of £12.50 there shall be substituted the sum of £12.95 in each case; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c)(ii), for the sum of £31.20 there shall be substituted the sum of £32.40.

(4) In regulation 63[^f00042] (non-dependant deductions)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for the sum of £39.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £45.00;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(b) for the sum of £13.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £16.00;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(c) for the sum of £17.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £22.00;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(d) for the sum of £33.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £36.00; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(e) for the sum of £36.00 there shall be substituted the sum of £41.00.

(5) In paragraph 1A of Part I of Schedule 1[^f00043] (ineligible service charges), for the sums of £17.55, £17.55, £8.85, £11.65, £11.65, £5.85 and £2.10 there shall be substituted the sums of £17.95, £17.95, £9.05, £11.95, £11.95, £6.00 and £2.15 respectively.

(6) Subject to paragraph (7) of this article, the sums specified in Part I of Schedule 2 (applicable amounts: personal allowances) shall be as set out in Schedule 9 to this Order.

(7) In paragraph 2(d) of Schedule 2 as it has effect by virtue of regulation 10(1) of the Income-related Benefits and Jobseeker’s Allowance (Personal Allowances for Children and Young Persons) (Amendment) Regulations 1996[^f00044] (transitional provisions) for the sum of £38.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £39.85.

(8) In paragraph 3 of Part II of Schedule 2[^f00045] (applicable amounts: family premium)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) the sum specified is £22.05; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for the sum of £10.80 there shall be substituted the sum of £11.05.

(9) The sums specified in Part IV of Schedule 2 (applicable amounts: premiums) shall be as set out in Schedule 10 to this Order.
