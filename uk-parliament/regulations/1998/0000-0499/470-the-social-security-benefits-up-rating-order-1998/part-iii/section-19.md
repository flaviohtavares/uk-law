# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1998, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Income Support Transitional Protection

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations 1987[^f00039] shall be increased by 2.4 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
