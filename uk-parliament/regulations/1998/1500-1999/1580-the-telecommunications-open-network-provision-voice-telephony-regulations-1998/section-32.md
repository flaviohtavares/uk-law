# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Discounts and other special tariff provisions—systemless service providers

(1) Where a systemless service provider is under a duty for its tariffs to follow the principles of cost orientation in accordance with regulation 28(1), it shall be under a duty to make discount schemes for its subscribers fully transparent and to publish and apply them in a non-discriminatory manner.

(2) The Director may require that the discount schemes referred to in paragraph (1) above be modified or withdrawn and systemless service providers shall be under a duty to comply with any requirement imposed by the Director.
