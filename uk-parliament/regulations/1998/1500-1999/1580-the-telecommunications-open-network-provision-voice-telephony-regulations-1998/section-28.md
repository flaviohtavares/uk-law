# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Tariff principles—systemless service providers

(1) Systemless service providers providing voice telephony services which are operators having significant market power shall be under a duty to comply with regulation 27(1)(a) to (d) above as if they were public operators.

(2) The Director may determine that paragraph (1) above shall not apply in a specific geographical area in respect of which he is satisfied that there is effective competition in the fixed publicly available telephone services market.
