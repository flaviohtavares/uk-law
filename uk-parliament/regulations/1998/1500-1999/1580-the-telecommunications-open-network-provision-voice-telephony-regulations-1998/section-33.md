# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Standards for ISDN network termination points

In exercising their functions under Part II and sections 47 to 49 of the Act, the Secretary of State and the Director shall ensure that where fixed publicly available telephone services are supplied to subscribers over the ISDN network at the S/T reference point[^f00017], the ISDN network termination points comply with the relevant physical interface specifications, in particular, those for the socket, referenced in the ONP List of Standards referred to in Article 5 of the ONP Framework Directive.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: The S/T reference point refers to the point with the S/T reference in ETSI standards, the reference numbers of which are published from time to time in the list of standards in the Official Journal of the European Communities.
