# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Enforcement of regulations 10, 14, 16, 18, 20, 21, 23, 25, 28, 30, 32 and 35

Without prejudice to any right which any person may have by virtue of any provision in these Regulations or otherwise to bring civil proceedings in respect of any contravention or apprehended contravention of any duty referred to in such provision, or to have any dispute concerning that duty resolved pursuant to regulation 37, compliance shall be enforceable by the Director by civil proceedings for an injunction or interdict or for any other appropriate relief.
