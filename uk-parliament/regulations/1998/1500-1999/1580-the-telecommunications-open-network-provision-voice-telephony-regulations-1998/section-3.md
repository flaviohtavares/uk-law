# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. EEA Agreement

Consequent upon the extension of the Voice Telephony Directive to the EEA by decision No 25/98 of the EEA Joint Committee which came into force on 1 April 1998, Regulations 1 to 3 and 9 of the 1997 Regulations and Voice Telephony Conditions VTD1, VTD8, VTD9 and VTD10 in Schedule 1 to those Regulations are hereby extended to have effect in respect of Contracting Parties to the EEA Agreement[^f00014] and in Regulation 9 and the Voice Telephony Conditions the words “member State” shall be interpreted as meaning a Contracting Party to that Agreement.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: The Contracting Parties to the EEA Agreement are, in addition, to the European Community and the individual EC Member States, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.
