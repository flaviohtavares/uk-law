# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Consultation

The Secretary of State and the Director shall take into account the views of the representatives of organisations running telecommunication systems, of users, consumers, manufacturers and service providers on issues related to the scope, affordability and quality of publicly available telephone services.
