# The Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Incidental and consequential amendments

(1) At the end of section 1(6) of the Act (payment out of money provided by Parliament) there shall be added the words “or in consequence of the provisions of the Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998.”

(2) In section 53 of the Act (power to require information etc), at the end of paragraph (b) of the definition of “relevant purpose” in subsection (6) there shall be added the words “or under the Telecommunications (Open Network Provision) (Voice Telephony) Regulations 1998.”
