# The Wireless Telegraphy (Jersey) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1512

TELEGRAPHS

The Wireless Telegraphy (Jersey) Order 1998

Made

24th June 1998

Coming into force

24th July 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of June 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by subsection (3) of section 20 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949[^f00001] as that subsection has effect by virtue of section 9(2) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998[^f00002], and section 9(3) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Jersey) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 24th July 1998.

**2.** Sections 1 to 8 and 10(1) of, and the Schedules to, the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998 shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey with the following adaptations and modifications—

- (a) any reference to the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949 shall be construed as a reference to that Act as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Wireless Telegraphy (Channel Islands) Order 1952[^f00003];
- (b) after section 3(5) insert—

  > (5A) The Secretary of State shall, after consultation with the Finance and Economics Committee of the States of Jersey, pay to the Treasurer of the States of Jersey to be credited to the annual income of the States such proportion of the sums received under subsection (5) as appears to the Secretary of State to be appropriate.

  ;
- (c) in section 6—
  - (i) in subsection (2)(a), for the words “London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes” substitute “Jersey Gazette”; and
  - (ii) at the end, add—

    > (5) Any statutory instrument containing regulations made under this Act shall not have effect in the Bailiwick of Jersey unless it is registered in the Royal Court of Jersey; and where any such statutory instrument is so registered, it shall have effect on the day following the day of such registration or on the day of coming into force specified in the instrument, whichever is the later.

    ;
- (d) in Schedule 1—
  - (i) omit paragraph 3; and
  - (ii) in paragraph 4, after “Broadcasting Act 1990” insert “as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Broadcasting Act 1990 (Jersey) Order 1991”[^f00004];
- (e) in Part I of Schedule 2—
  - (i) omit the references to section 3A of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949 and section 74 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00005];
  - (ii) after “section 3(2) to (5)” insert “as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Wireless Telegraphy (Channel Islands) Order 1969”[^f00006]; and
  - (iii) after “paragraph 2(2)” insert “as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Broadcasting Act 1990 (Jersey) Order 1991”; and
- (f) omit Part II of Schedule 2.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends to the Bailiwick of Jersey sections 1 to 8 and 10(1) of, and the Schedules to, the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998, with the adaptations and modifications specified in the Order.
