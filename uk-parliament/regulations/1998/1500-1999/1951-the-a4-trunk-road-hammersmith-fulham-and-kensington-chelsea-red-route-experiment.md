# The A4 Trunk Road (Hammersmith & Fulham and Kensington & Chelsea) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1951

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A4 Trunk Road (Hammersmith & Fulham and Kensington & Chelsea) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998

Made

6th August 1998

Coming into force

24th August 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as The A4 Trunk Road (Hammersmith & Fulham and Kensington & Chelsea) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998, and shall come into force on 24th August 1998.

**2.** The A4 Trunk Road (Hammersmith & Fulham and Kensington & Chelsea) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

**3.** In schedule 2B items 1 and 2 are deleted.

**4.** In schedule 2B the following items are added:

> |  |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 1\. | Talgarth Road, the south side area of verge 0.25 metres from and parallel to the southern kerb-line between a point 3 metres west of the eastern wall of 1 Talgarth Road and a point 1.5 metres east of the party wall of 11 and 13 Talgarth Road and having a width of 1.7 metres throughout. | At any time | None | None |
> | 2\. | Talgarth Road, the south side area of verge 0.25 metres from and parallel to the southern kerb-line between a point 13 metres west of the party wall of 11 and 13 Talgarth Road and a point 1 metre west of party wall of 63 and 65 Talgarth Road and having a width of 1.7 metres throughout. |  |  |  |
> | 3\. | Talgarth Road, the south side area of verge 0.25 metres from and parallel to the southern kerb-line between a point 0.5 metres west of the party wall of 71 and 73 Talgarth Road and a point opposite the party wall of 101 and 103 Talgarth Road and having a width of 1.7 metres throughout. |  |  |  |
> | 4\. | Talgarth Road, the south side area of verge 0.25 metres from and parallel to the southern kerb-line between a point 15 metres west of the party wall of 101 and 103 Talgarth Road and a point 2 metres west of the party wall of 57 to 64 and 65 to 72 Talgarth Mansions, Talgarth Road and having a width of 1.7 metres throughout. |  |  |  |

**5.** In schedule 3B the following items are added:

> | 5\. | Talgarth Road, the south side area of verge 0.25 metres from and parallel to the southern kerb-line between a point 1.5 metres east of party wall of 67 and 69 Talgarth Road and a point 0.5 metres west of the party wall of 71 and 73 Talgarth Road and having a width of 2.0 metres throughout. |  |
