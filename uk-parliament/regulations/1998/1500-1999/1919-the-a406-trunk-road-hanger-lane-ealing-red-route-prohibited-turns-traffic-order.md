# The A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Traffic Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1919

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Traffic Order 1998

Made

3rd August 1998

Coming into force

14th August 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001]) and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Traffic Order 1998 and shall come into force on 14th August 1998.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) causing includes permitting;
- (b) “local bus” has the same meaning as in regulation 4 of the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994[^f00003];
- (c) “A406 Hanger Lane” means the Trunk Road of that name in the London Borough of Ealing;
- (d) “A4020 Uxbridge Road” means the Local Road of that name in the London Borough of Ealing.

## 3. Prohibited Turns

Subject to the provisions of article 4 below no person shall cause a vehicle proceeding in the A406 Hanger Lane to turn into the unnamed service road located at the north-west corner of the Hanger Lane/Uxbridge Road junction and running between the A406 Hanger Lane and the A4020 Uxbridge Road.

## 4. Exemptions

Nothing in article 3 of this Order shall apply to—

- (a) a local bus;
- (b) a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes;
- (c) anything done with the permission of or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## 5. Revocation or variation of existing Orders

The A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Experimental Traffic Order 1997[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

3rd August 1998
