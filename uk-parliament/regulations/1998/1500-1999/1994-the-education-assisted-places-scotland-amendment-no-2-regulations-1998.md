# The Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1994 (S.103)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1998

Made

5th August 1998

Laid before Parliament

11th August 1998

Coming into force

1st September 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 75A(9) and (9A)(c) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after having consulted such bodies as appear to him appropriate and to be representative of participating schools in accordance with section 75A(11) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 1995

After regulation 23B of the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00002] there shall be inserted–

> **23C. Transfer of assisted place for individual pupil**
>
> (1) This regulation applies to a pupil who holds (or has, at any time since the beginning of the first term of the 1997-98 school year, held) an assisted place at a school which is, or is treated as, a participating school.
>
> (2) The Secretary of State may, in a case where he is satisfied that it is reasonable to do so in view of any particular circumstances relating to a pupil to whom this regulation applies, authorise another school which is, or is treated as, a participating school to provide for the pupil the assisted place which the school mentioned in paragraph (1) was authorised to provide.

.

Calum MacDonald

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th August 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 1995. Their effect is to enable the Secretary of State to allow an assisted pupil to transfer to another school within the Assisted Place Scheme and still retain his assisted place, notwithstanding the phasing out of the Scheme provided for by the Education (Schools) Act 1997. Transfer of the assisted place may be authorised only if the Secretary of State is satisfied that it is reasonable to do so in view of the particular circumstances relating to the pupil concerned.
