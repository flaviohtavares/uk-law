# The Building Act 1984 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1836 (C. 37)

BUILDING AND BUILDINGS

The Building Act 1984 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1998

Made

17th July 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 120(1) and 134(1)(d) of the Building Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Building Act 1984 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1998.

## 2. Commencement

Paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 to the Building Act 1984 shall come into force on 7th August 1998.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

17th July 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings Paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 to the Building Act 1984 into force on 7th August 1998. The paragraph enables building regulations to be made which authorise local authorities to fix and to recover charges for or in connection with the performance of their functions relating to building regulations.

(This note is not part of the Order)
