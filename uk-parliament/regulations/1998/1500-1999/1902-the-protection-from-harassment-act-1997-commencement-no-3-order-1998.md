# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 1902 (C.42)

TORTS

The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1998

Made

30th July 1998

Coming into force

1st September 1998

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1998.

**2.** Subsections (3) to (9) of section 3 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 30th July 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that subsections (3) to (9) of section 3 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Act were brought into force on 16th June 1997 by the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/1418](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1418)).

Sections 3(1) and (2) and 6 of the Act were brought into force on 16th June 1997 by the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/1498](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1498)).

(Sections 13 to 16 of the Act came into force at Royal Assent on 21st March 1997).
