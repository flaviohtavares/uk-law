# The Individual Savings Account Regulations 1998, section 5DG

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (42).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/1870
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5DG. Single regulation 5D subscription[^key-8ef43955e91d7850ecd84c6f42a9af50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Only one defaulted cash account subscription, defaulted investment subscription, defaulted Lifetime ISA subscription[^key-b90f4811c6e566f4b62b54f034f2c086] or subscription permitted by regulation 5DE may be made in respect of a defaulted cash account or a defaulted investment payment as the case may be even if the subscription made is an amount less than that which could have been made by way of such subscription in accordance with regulation 5D.
