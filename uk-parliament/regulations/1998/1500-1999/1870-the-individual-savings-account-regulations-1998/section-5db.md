## 5DB. Defaulted investment subscription[^c-8ef43955e91d7850ecd84c6f42a9af50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A subscription is a defaulted investment subscription if—

- (a) it is made to an account[^c-be65ceac967fb8fcc492053bf1541f96] held by the account investor who held the account in respect of which a defaulted investment payment has been made no more than 180 days before the subscription is made; and
- (b) it does not exceed the amount of the defaulted investment payment.
