## 18. Account manager—appeal against withdrawal of Board’s approval

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An account manager to whom notice of withdrawal of approval has been given under regulation 17 or 17A[^c-96b8d715d1b551ac4e90b2cfe94f8c2a] may appeal against the withdrawal by notice given to the Board within 30 days after the date of the notice of withdrawal.

(2)[^c-6e65d252512ada19bf814a90662bc431] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The like provisions as are contained in Part V of the Management Act[^c16576561] (appeals and other proceedings) shall apply to an appeal and the tribunal, on an appeal notified to it, shall[^c-5842817b0f31096c36c31ce25670c0f9] confirm the notice unless [^c-9f1707dc341643b7614dcebb125526f2]... satisfied that the notice ought to be quashed.
