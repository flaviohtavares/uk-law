## 10. Qualifying individuals who may invest under an account that is not a junior ISA account nor a Lifetime ISA[^key-9665a97cad0334340df640c29a5a1482][^key-bee6fc7e38125dea362b37ddd4f9e447]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-50eec3b680850cf79e48ac379000071b] This regulation specifies the description of individual (“qualifying individual”) who may invest under an account that is not a junior ISA account nor a Lifetime ISA[^key-5381b114a84c90a85b8d1af1464b5395].

(2) A qualifying individual to whom paragraph (1) refers is an individual—

- (a)[^key-81c7d06fc77bf5bba8549a4615227c6a] who is 18 years of age or over;[^key-0c1bf05b2c7bee533df340ff96c323ff]
- (b)[^key-db02ffbebd75de80b408aba6fb9059e7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-ce2cab206ee7c41bfd591e4e38a2c19a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (cza)[^key-6043340f7f394c43de4a73a034a2fe19] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ca) who has not exceeded the [^key-ff389de58ae84e6f5f4d5622bd01c665]... overall subscription limit in regulation 4ZA(1)[^key-0ee29ca54f6d884ee12172988376d754][^key-ff389de58ae84e6f5f4d5622bd01c665]... in that year;
- (d)
  - (i) who is resident [^key-1162996fa3196fcc75fed262ce4e3972]... in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii)[^key-fbbd1dc151c3db95102ca6c18d7c55b3] who, though not resident in the United Kingdom, has general earnings from overseas Crown employment subject to United Kingdom tax within the meaning given by section 28 of ITEPA 2003, or[^key-e1ec7d0bb7f649b84274258f45240130]
  - (iii) who, though not resident in the United Kingdom, is married to or in a civil partnership with[^key-3bedc15768fe7c6fd4d7d9fe9e7f846b] a person mentioned in paragraph (ii); or[^key-8b9a4b5d021031327bfcc6e485a5dd35]
  - (iv)[^key-3acd9868a8c47842bf5eb612232fdb8a] who, where the terms and conditions of an account allow for it, though not resident in the United Kingdom, makes a subscription to the account in accordance with regulation 5D.

(3)[^key-da9334de7e81ee848c13afa4f2e96056] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
