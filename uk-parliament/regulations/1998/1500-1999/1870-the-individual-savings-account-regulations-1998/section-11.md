# The Individual Savings Account Regulations 1998, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (35).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1998/1870
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Account investor ceasing to qualify

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding any other provision of these Regulations an account investor who, after subscribing or making a qualifying addition[^c-8ccf5b202ccb946bdbc18947f07126d5] to an account, at any time ceases to fulfil the conditions of regulation 10(2)(d) or 10A(2)(f), as the case may be,[^c-3d8d080dd6d59d011db945f780077741] may retain the benefits of the account (including the right to any relief or exemption due under the account) subsisting at that time but, so long as he fails to fulfil those conditions, shall not be entitled to subscribe further or make a qualifying addition[^c-09bd76281bf42608d93b9c3c515e8ce5] to such an account.
