# The Frenchay Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2993

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Frenchay Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made

2nd December 1998

Coming into force

14th December 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003] and considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Frenchay Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 14th December 1998.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In the Frenchay Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005]:–

- (a) for Article 3 there shall be substituted the following Article:–

  > **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
  >
  > (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
  >
  > (2) The trust’s functions shall be:–
  >
  > - (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act hospital accommodation and services provided at the premises specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order;
  > - (b) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act hospital accommodation and services at any or all of the premises specified in Part II of the Schedule to this Order and at any other premises which are for the time being associated with any of those premises;
  > - (c) to manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act community health services provided from the premises specified in Part III of the Schedule to this Order and to own the associated premises; and
  > - (d) to manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act community health services provided from the premises specified in Part IV of the Schedule to this Order and to provide the associated premises.

  ; and
- (b) at the end of that Order there shall be added the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Frenchay Healthcare National Health Service Trust to alter the purpose for which the trust has been established, and to amend its functions.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 and, in pursuance of that purpose extends the functions of the trust to include the function of providing and managing hospital accommodation and services, and of managing community health services, from certain premises, principally those as specified in the Schedule to be added to the Establishment Order (article 2(3)).
