The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Health and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in relation to England and Wales, and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(4), 16(1), 17(2), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) of that Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the Regulations, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 10th November 1998.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1995[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

The principal Regulations shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4 below.

**3.** In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “the Commission Regulation”, there shall be substituted for the words “and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2181/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/2181)[^f00003]” the words “, Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2181/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/2181), Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 623/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0623)[^f00004] and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1298/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/1298)[^f00005]”.

**4.** In Schedule 1 (Community provisions), in paragraph 2, there shall be substituted in column 3 (subject matter) for the existing entry the following entry–

> Restrictions on use of the designation “butter” for composite products

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Great Britain, amend the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1995, as amended. The Regulations make provision for the enforcement and execution of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 623/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0623) and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1298/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/1298), both amending Regulation [(EC) No. 577/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0577) laying down certain detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2991/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/2991) laying down standards for spreadable fats and of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1898/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/1898) on the protection of designations used in the marketing of milk and milk products.

Regulation 623/98 substitutes new rules restricting use of the designation “butter” for composite products containing butter. There is a transitional provision in Article 5 of Regulation 577/97.

Regulation 1298/98 postpones until 1st January 1999 the date from which the specified procedure for verifying the declared fat content of spreadable fats is to apply.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
