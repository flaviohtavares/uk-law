# The Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2975

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

2nd December 1998

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1998

Coming into force

1st January 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 73(1)(a) and (6)(a) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st January 1999.

**2.** The Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) Regulations 1998 (“the principal Regulations”) as follows:

The charges payable to the CAA for navigation services provided in connection with the use of the aerodromes specified in the Table in regulation 2(1) of the principal Regulations have been changed as follows:–

- (a) In respect of the three London Airports, the standard charge for the first 100 metric tonnes maximum total weight authorised of an aircraft, or part thereof, is reduced from £1.21 per metric tonne to £1.06. For each additional metric tonne, or part thereof, in excess of 100 metric tonnes, the standard charge is reduced from £0.50 per metric tonne to £0.44.
- (b) The standard charge at Edinburgh is reduced from £2.20 per metric tonne to £1.96. The standard charge at Glasgow is reduced from £2.16 per metric tonne to £1.84. The standard charge at Aberdeen (Dyce) is unchanged (regulation 2).
