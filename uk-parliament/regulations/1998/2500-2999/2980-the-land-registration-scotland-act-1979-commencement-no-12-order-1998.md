# The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.12) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2980 (C. 75) (S. 173)

LAND REGISTRATION, SCOTLAND

The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.12) Order 1998

Made

27th November 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 30(2) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No.12) Order 1998.

**2.** Sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 shall come into force on 1st October 1999 in the following areas, for the purpose of registration of writs:–

- (a) the County of Berwick;
- (b) the County of East Lothian;
- (c) the County of Roxburgh;
- (d) the County of Selkirk; and
- (e) the County of Peebles.

Henry B McLeish

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th November 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1999 in the areas of the Counties of Berwick, East Lothian, Roxburgh, Selkirk and Peebles sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979. Section 2(1) and (2) provides for the circumstances in which an interest in land shall be registrable and section 3(3) provides that certain persons are to obtain a real right only by registration.

(This note is not part of the Order)
