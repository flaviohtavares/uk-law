# The Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 2797

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

The Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made

17th November 1998

Laid before Parliament

27th November 1998

Coming into force

18th December 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of November 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph 19 of Schedule 1 to the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997[^f00001], including as it applies by section 121(12)(b) of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 18th December 1998.

**2.** (1) The Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1997[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2, for the words from “subject to” onwards substitute “subject to the amendment made to paragraph 6(3) by paragraph 135(2)(b) of Schedule 8 to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998, and to the modifications and supplementary provisions specified in the Schedule to this Order”.

(3) For the sidenote after paragraph 7 of the Schedule substitute “Supplementary provisions”.

**3.** Paragraph 9 of the Schedule to the Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1997 is hereby revoked.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Transfer of Prisoners (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/1775](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1775)) to provide for the extension to, and application in relation to, the Isle of Man of the amendment of paragraph 6(3) of Schedule 1 to the Crime (Sentences) Act [1997 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/43) (meaning of “recall”) made by paragraph 135(2)(b) of Schedule 8 to the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37), being an amendment consequential on other provisions of that Act of 1998. The Order also, in consequence, revokes paragraph 9 of the Schedule to that Order.
