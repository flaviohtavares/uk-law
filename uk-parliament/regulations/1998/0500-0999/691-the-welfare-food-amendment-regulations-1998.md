# The Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 691

FOOD

The Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

11th March 1998

Laid before Parliament

16th March 1998

Coming into force

6th April 1998

The Secretary of State for Health in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(3) and (4) of the Social Security Act 1988[^f00001] and section 175(2) to (5) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 6th April 1998.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 7 of the Welfare Food Regulations 1996

In regulation 7(1) of the Welfare Food Regulations 1996[^f00003] (purchase of dried milk at a reduced price) for “£3.75” there shall be substituted “£3.85”.

Frank Dobson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State,

Department of Health

11th March 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 7 of the Welfare Food Regulations 1996 to increase the price payable for dried milk by a person entitled to purchase it at a reduced price, from £3.75 to £3.85 for 900 grammes per week.
