# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (North American Gateway Limited) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 513

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (North American Gateway Limited) Order 1998

Made

3rd March 1998

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1998

Coming into force

7th April 1998

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to North American Gateway Limited on 30th December 1997 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in the Licence (“the Applicable Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (North American Gateway Limited) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 7th April 1998.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Barbara Roche,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Small Firms, Trade and Industry,

Department of Trade and Industry

3rd March 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to North American Gateway Limited on 30th December 1997 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in the licence. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 9th March 1998. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, North American Gateway Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when this Order comes into force on 7th April 1998.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
