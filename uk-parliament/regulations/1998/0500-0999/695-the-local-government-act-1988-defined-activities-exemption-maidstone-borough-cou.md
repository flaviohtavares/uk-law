# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Maidstone Borough Council) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 695

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Maidstone Borough Council) Order 1998

Made

9th March 1998

Laid before Parliament

20th March 1998

Coming into force

10th April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Maidstone Borough Council) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 10th April 1998.

## 2. Exemption of supervision of parking work

Supervision of parking[^f00002] by the council of the borough of Maidstone shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out in their area before 30th January 1999.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

9th March 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the supervision of parking by Maidstone Borough Council so long as it is carried out in their area before 30th January 1999.
