# The Building Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Regulations 1998, section 13

> Text as at 2022-06-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Revocation[^c-3a739cd4fdd3c1bd65ac7a234e706932]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Building Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Regulations 1992, the Building Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 1994[^f00008] and the Building Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 1995[^f00009] are hereby revoked.
