# The Combined Probation Areas (Leicestershire and Rutland) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 584

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Leicestershire and Rutland) Order 1998

Made

5th March 1998

Coming into force

1st April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of the Probation Service Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Leicestershire and Rutland ) Order 1998.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

**2.** For the reference to Leicestershire as a probation area in Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] there shall be substituted a reference to Leicestershire and Rutland.

Joyce Quin

Minister of State

Home Office

5th March 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) so as to change the name of the Leicestershire probation area to Leicestershire and Rutland.
