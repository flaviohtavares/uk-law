Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

## THE SCHEDULE — NORTHAMPTONSHIRE COUNTY COUNCIL (A45 NENE VALLEY WAY WIDENING) (BRIDGE OVER THE RIVER NENE) SCHEME 1997

The Northamptonshire County Council (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) makes this Scheme in exercise of their power under Section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf:—

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the River Nene the Bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct from a point 45 metres south-west of the Hardingstone Dyke in a generally north-easterly direction to a point approximately 92.9 metres north-east of the Hardingstone Dyke in the Borough of Northampton in the said County.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as the Northamptonshire County Council (A45 Nene Valley Way Widening) (Bridge over the River Nene) Scheme 1997.

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME — PLANS AND SPECIFICATIONS OF THE BRIDGE CROSSING THE RIVER NENE

### PLAN TITLE

NORTHAMPTONSHIRE COUNTY COUNCIL (A45 NENE VALLEY WAY WIDEN ING) (BRIDGE OVER THE RIVER NENE) SCHEME 1997

(This plan being bound up within the Order)

### LOCATION

South east of Northampton Town Centre

The bridge is to the north-west of and runs parallel to the existing bridge on the A45 between Brackmills Interchange and Barnes Meadow Interchange.

### OVERALL DIMENSIONS

The Bridge will be 15.4 metres wide overall and carry a three lane carriageway, two lanes each 3.5 metres wide and one lane 3.3 metres wide with a one metre wide footway and a one metre wide hardstrip on the north-west side and a two metre wide footway on the south-east side. The overall length will be 137.9 metres.

### POINTS OF COMMENCEMENT AND TERMINATION

The bridge commences on the south-west bank of the Navigable River Nene approximately 45 metres to the south-west of the Hardingstone Dyke.

The bridge terminates on the north-east bank of the Navigable River Nene approximately 92.9 metres to the north-east of the Hardingstone Dyke.

### TYPE OF CONSTRUCTION

Steel/concrete composite deck supported on reinforced concrete piers and abutments.

### MAIN NAVIGATION SPAN

The main Navigation Span is 34.85 metres.

### HEADWAY

Not less than 3.8 metres above normal water level and 3.8 metres above towpath level.

### WATERWAY

A minimum width of navigable channel of 32.0 metres at water level.
