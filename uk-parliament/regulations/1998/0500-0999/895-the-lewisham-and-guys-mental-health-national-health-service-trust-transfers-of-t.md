# The Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 895

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1998

Made

25th March 1998

Laid before Parliament

2nd April 1998

Coming into force

23rd April 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities resulting from the establishment of the Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the bodies specified in the Schedule to this Order and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 23rd April 1998.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;
- “the Trust” means the Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means, in the case of each body specified in the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by, agreed upon by and signed on behalf of that body and the Trust on 28th May 1997.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 23rd April 1998, in the case of each body specified in the Schedule, the trust property shall be transferred from that body to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health

25th March 1998

## SCHEDULE — BODIES FROM WHICH TRUST PROPERTY TO BE TRANSFERRED

Articles 1(2) and 2

- Greenwich Healthcare National Health Service Trust
- Guy’s and St Thomas' National Health Service Trust
- Lambeth, Southwark and Lewisham Health Authority

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers on 23rd April 1998 to the Lewisham and Guy’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust trust property held by the bodies specified in the Schedule to the Order. A copy of the schedules of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, Accounts and Trust Allocations Branch, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
