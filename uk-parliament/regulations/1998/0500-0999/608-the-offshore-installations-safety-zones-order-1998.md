# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 608

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1998

Made

5th March 1998

Coming into force

26th March 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(1) and (2) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th March 1998.

## 2. Establishment of safety zones

A safety zone is hereby established around each installation specified in column 1 of the Schedule hereto (being an installation stationed in waters to which subsection (7) of section 21 of the Act applies) having a radius of five hundred metres from the point as respects that installation which has the co-ordinates of latitude and longitude according to European Datum (1950) specified in columns 2 and 3 of the Schedule.

## 3. Removal of safety zones

In Schedule 1 to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1997[^f00002] the entries relating to Emerald Central Location, Emerald Field FSU Template, Emerald Well WSE, Emerald Wells PSF and PSD and Emerald Wells WSA and WSB are deleted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

Angela Eagle

(This note is not part of the order)

This order establishes, under section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987, safety zones having a radius of 500 metres from the specified point, around each installation specified in the Schedule to this order and stationed in waters to which section 21(7) of that Act applies (these include territorial waters and waters in areas designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29)) *(Article 2)*.

Vessels (which for this purpose include hovercraft, submersible apparatus and installations in transit) are prohibited from entering or remaining in a safety zone except with the consent of the Health and Safety Executive or in accordance with regulations made under section 23(1) of the Act (currently the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1331))).

The order removes the safety zones around Emerald Central Location, Emerald Field FSU Template, Emerald Well WSE, Emerald Wells PSF and PSD and Emerald Wells WSA and WSB by deleting the entries relating to them in Schedule 1 to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order [1997/735](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/735) *(Article 3)*.
