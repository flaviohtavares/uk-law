# The Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 810

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

18th March 1998

Coming into force

13th April 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 132(4) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 13th April 1998.

## 2. Amendments

The form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the provisions of the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Application

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to applications for a relocation grant made before the 13th April 1998.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Nick Raynsford

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

18th March 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The principal relocation grant Regulations, the Relocation Grants Regulations 1997, apply the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/2890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2890)) as they have effect from time to time, with the modifications prescribed. Amendments to the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 by the Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/880](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/880)) have, accordingly necessitated amendments to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997, and these Regulations make the necessary amendments, as well as making minor drafting amendments.
