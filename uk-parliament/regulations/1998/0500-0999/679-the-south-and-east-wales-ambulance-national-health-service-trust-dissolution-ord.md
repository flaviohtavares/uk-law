# The South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 679

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1998

Made

26th February 1998

Coming into force

1st April 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trust

The South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust established by the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order and the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 1996[^f00006] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Win Griffiths

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

26th February 1998

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 1998 of the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust established on 16th November 1992.
