# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Enforcement of Parts II and III

Except where otherwise specified, each food authority shall enforce and execute Parts II and III within their area.
