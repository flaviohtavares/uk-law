# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Requirement for the registration of auction or wholesale markets

No person shall operate an auction or wholesale market unless that market has been registered with the food authority in whose area it is situated.
