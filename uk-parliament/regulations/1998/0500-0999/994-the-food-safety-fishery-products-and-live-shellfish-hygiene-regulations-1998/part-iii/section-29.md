# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Notice of arrival obligation for masters of third country factory vessels

The master of a factory vessel of a third country shall, before landing any fishery products in Great Britain which are for placing on the market, give the food authority for the port at which he intends to land those fishery products notice of arrival of at least 24 hours.
