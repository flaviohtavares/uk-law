# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Prohibition on placing certain fishery products on the market

No person shall sell, supply in the course of a food business or place on the market for human consumption—

- (a) poisonous fish of any of the following families: Tetraodontidae, Molidae, Diodontidae, and Canthigasteridae; or
- (b) fishery products containing biotoxins such as ciguatera toxins or muscle-paralysing toxins.
