# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Fishery products to be placed on the market alive

A person with control over the survival conditions of a fishery product for human consumption which is to be placed on the market alive shall ensure that it is at all times kept under the most suitable survival conditions.
