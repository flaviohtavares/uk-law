# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Designation of areas as prohibited areas for live shellfish production

The Ministers may at any time designate any area as an area unsuitable, for health reasons, for the production or collection of—

- (a) live bivalve molluscs;
- (b) live echinoderms, tunicates and marine gastropods;
- (c) live shellfish,

and any such area shall be designated, as appropriate, as a “designated prohibited area for live bivalve mollusc production”, a “designated prohibited area for live echinoderm, tunicate and marine gastropod production” or a “designated prohibited area for live shellfish production”.
