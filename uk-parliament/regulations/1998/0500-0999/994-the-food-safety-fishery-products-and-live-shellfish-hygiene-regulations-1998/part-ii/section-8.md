# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Collecting live shellfish from prohibited areas

No person shall collect—

- (a) live bivalve molluscs from a designated prohibited area for live bivalve mollusc production;
- (b) live echinoderms, tunicates or marine gastropods from a designated prohibited area for live echinoderm, tunicate and marine gastropod production;
- (c) live shellfish from—
  - (i) a designated prohibited area for live shellfish production, or
  - (ii) a laying which is the subject of a temporary prohibition order.
