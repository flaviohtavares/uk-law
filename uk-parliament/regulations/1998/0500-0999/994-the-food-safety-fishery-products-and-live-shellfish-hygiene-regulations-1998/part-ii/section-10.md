# The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Requirement for the approval of dispatch or purification centres

No person shall operate a dispatch or purification centre unless it has been approved by the food authority for the area in which the centre is situated.
