# The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1990/2145](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2145) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1998 No. 748

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made

18th March 1998

Coming into force

18th May 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of March 1998

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 108(1) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 18th May 1998.

**2.** In paragraph 14 of Part II of Schedule 1 to the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) Order 1990[^f00002] (hereinafter referred to as “the 1990 Order”)—

- (a) sub-paragraph (c) shall be omitted;
- (b) at the end, there shall be added—

  > ; and
  >
  > - (h) after subsection (12), there shall be added—
  >
  >   > (13) In subsections (1), (3), (4) and (5) above, “prescribed” means prescribed by order of the Committee.

  .

**3.** In paragraph 28 of Part II of Schedule 1 to the 1990 Order, at the end, there shall be added—

> ; and
>
> - (c) after subsection (5), there shall be added—
>
>   > (6) the Subordinate Legislation (Jersey) Law, 1960 as amended shall apply to an order made by the Committee under this Act as if this Act were an enactment for the purposes of that Law.

[^f00003].

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Part II of Schedule 1 to the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) Order 1990 (which specifies modifications to provisions of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 in their extension to the Bailiwick of Jersey) so as to provide that orders made under that Act by the Harbours and Airport Committee of the States of Jersey shall be made in accordance with the Subordinate Legislation (Jersey) Law, 1960, as amended; and, further, that a notice published or given by the Committee under section 78 of that Act (regulation of noise and vibration from aircraft) shall be made in the manner prescribed by an order of the Committee.
