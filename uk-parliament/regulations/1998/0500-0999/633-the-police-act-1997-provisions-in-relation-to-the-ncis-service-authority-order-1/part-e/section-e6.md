# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section E6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E6. Application of articles E1 to E5 to committees

Articles E1 to E5 above shall apply as respects members of a committee of the Authority, as they apply in respect of members of the Authority, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) references to meetings of any such committee shall be substituted for references to meetings of the Authority; and
- (b) in the case of members of a committee of the Authority the right of persons who are members of the committee but not members of the Authority to inspect the book kept under article E3(2) above shall be limited to an inspection of the entries in the book relating to the members of the committee.
