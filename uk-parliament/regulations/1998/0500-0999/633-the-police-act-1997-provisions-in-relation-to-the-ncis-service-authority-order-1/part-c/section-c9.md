# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section C9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C9. Exempt information

The descriptions of information which are, for the purposes of this Part, exempt information are those for the time being specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to this Order, but subject to any qualifications contained in Part II of that Schedule; and Part III has effect for the interpretation of that Schedule.
