# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section K4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## K4. Insurance by the Authority against accidents to members

(1) The Authority may enter into a contract of insurance of Class 1 in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00031] against risks of any member of the Authority meeting with a personal accident, whether fatal or not, while engaged on the business of the Authority.

(2) Any sum received by the Authority under any such contract shall, after deduction of any expenses incurred in the recovery thereof, be paid by it to, or to the general representatives of, the member of the Authority in respect of an accident to whom that sum is received.

(3) The provisions of the Life Assurance Act 1774[^f00032] shall not apply to any such contract.

(4) In this article the expression “member of the Authority” includes a member of a committee of the Authority who is not a member of that Authority.
