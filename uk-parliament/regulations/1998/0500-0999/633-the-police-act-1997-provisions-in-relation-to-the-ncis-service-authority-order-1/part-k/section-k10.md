# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section K10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## K10. Public notices

Save as otherwise expressly provided, a public notice required to be given by the Authority shall be given in such manner as appears to the Authority to be desirable for giving publicity to the notice.
