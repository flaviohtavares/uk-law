# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section D4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D4. Disqualification for membership of committees

A person who is employed by the Authority shall be disqualified for being a member of a committee (including a sub-committee) of the Authority, whether the committee is appointed under this Order or under any other enactment.
