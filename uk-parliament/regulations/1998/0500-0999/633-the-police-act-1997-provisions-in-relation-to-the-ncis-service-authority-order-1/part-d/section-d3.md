# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section D3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D3. Expenses of joint committees

The expenses incurred by a joint committee of two or more local authorities whether established under this Order or any other enactment shall be defrayed by those authorities in such proportions as they may agree or in case of disagreement as may be determined by a single arbitrator agreed on by the appointing authorities or, in default of agreement, appointed by the Secretary of State.
