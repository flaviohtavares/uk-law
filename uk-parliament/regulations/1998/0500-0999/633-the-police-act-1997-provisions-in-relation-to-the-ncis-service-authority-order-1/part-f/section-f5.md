# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section F5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F5. Information about meetings

(1) Where it is proposed to hold a meeting under article F4 above the Authority’s proper officer shall as soon as is reasonably practicable notify its auditor of the date, time and place of the proposed meeting.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after a meeting is held under article F4 above the Authority’s proper officer shall notify its auditor of any decision made at the meeting.

(3) For the purposes of this article the Authority’s auditor is the person who for the time being has the duty to audit its accounts.
