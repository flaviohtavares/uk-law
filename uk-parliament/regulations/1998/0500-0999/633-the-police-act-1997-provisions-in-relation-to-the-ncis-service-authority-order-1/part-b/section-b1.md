# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section B1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B1. Meetings and proceedings of the Authority and its committees

The provisions of Schedule 1 to this Order shall have effect with respect to the meetings and proceedings of the Authority and its committees.
