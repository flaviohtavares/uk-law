# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section J5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J5. Disqualification and political restriction of certain officers and staff

The terms of appointment or conditions of employment of every person holding a politically restricted post under the Authority (including persons appointed to such posts before the coming into force of this article) shall be deemed to incorporate such requirements for restricting his political activities as are prescribed for the purposes of subsection (5) of section 1(5) of the 1989 Act by regulations made by the Secretary of State.
