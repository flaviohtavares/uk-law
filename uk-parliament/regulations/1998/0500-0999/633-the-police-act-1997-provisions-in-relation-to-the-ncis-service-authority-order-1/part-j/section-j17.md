# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section J17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J17. Members of the Authority not to be appointed as officers

A person shall, so long as he is, and for twelve months after he ceases to be, a member of the Authority, be disqualified for being appointed by the Authority to any paid office.
