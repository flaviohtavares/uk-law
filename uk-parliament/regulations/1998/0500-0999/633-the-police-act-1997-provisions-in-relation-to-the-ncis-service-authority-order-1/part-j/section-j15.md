# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section J15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J15. Security to be taken in relation to officers

(1) The Authority shall, in the case of an officer employed by it, whether under this Order or any other enactment, who by reason of his office or employment is likely to be entrusted with the custody or control of money, and may in the case of any other officer employed by it, take such security, for the faithful execution of his office and for his duly accounting for all money or property which may be entrusted to him, as the Authority considers sufficient.

(2) The Authority may, in the case of a person not employed by it but who is likely to be entrusted with the custody or control of money or property belonging to the Authority, take such security as it thinks sufficient for the person duly accounting for all such money or property.

(3) The Authority shall defray the cost of any security taken under this article, and every such security shall be produced to the auditor at the audit of the accounts of the Authority.
