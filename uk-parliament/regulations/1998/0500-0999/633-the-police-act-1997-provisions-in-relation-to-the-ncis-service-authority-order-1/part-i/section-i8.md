# The Police Act 1997 (Provisions in relation to the NCIS Service Authority) Order 1998, section I8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## I8. Secretary of State’s power to require information

The Secretary of State may direct the Authority to inform him whether it holds a freehold or leasehold interest in land which is specified, or is of a description specified, in the direction.
