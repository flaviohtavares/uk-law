# The Road Vehicles Registration Fee (Amendment) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 1998/572](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/572) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

1998 No. 995

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Vehicles Registration Fee (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made

7th April 1998

Laid before Parliament

8th April 1998

Coming into force

9th April 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 21(3) and 57 of the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00001] and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles Registration Fee (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 9th April 1998.

**2.** In regulation 2(1) of the Road Vehicles Registration Fee Regulations 1998[^f00003] for the words “section 22” there shall be substituted “section 21”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Helene Hayman

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

7th April 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Road Vehicles Registration Fee Regulations 1998 provide that (subject to exemptions) a vehicle must not be registered under Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994 unless a fee of £25 is paid. Those Regulations refer to section 22 of the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994 as the provision under which vehicles are registered instead of section 21. These Regulations correct the erroneous reference.
