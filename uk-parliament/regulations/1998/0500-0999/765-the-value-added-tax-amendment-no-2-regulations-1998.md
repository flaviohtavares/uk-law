# The Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 765

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998

Made

17th March 1998

Laid before the House of Commons

17th March 1998

Coming into force

18th March 1998

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 6(14) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 18th March 1998.

**2.** Paragraph (5) of regulation 90 of the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the words “article 20” the words “article 18”.

Martin Brown

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New Ring’s Beam House 22 Upper Ground London SE1 9PJ

17th March 1998

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend, with effect from 18th March 1998, the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/2518](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518)) (“the Regulations”).

The amendment is a consequence of the amendments made to the Value Added Tax (Place of Supply of Services) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/3121](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3121)) (“the Order”) made by the Value Added Tax (Place of Supply of Services) (Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/763](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/763)) (“the amendment Order”). Regulation 90(5) of the Regulations formerly referred to article 20 of the Order which, by virtue of the amendment Order, has been omitted from the Order. The relevant provisions of article 20 have been re-enacted by the new article 18 of the Order.
