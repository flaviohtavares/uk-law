# The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Isle of Man) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1162

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Isle of Man) Order 1984

Made

31st July 1984

Laid before Parliament

8th August 1984

Coming into Operation

31st August 1984

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 31st day of July 1984

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Isle of Man) Order 1984 and shall come into operation on 31st August 1984.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Registration) Act 1984 (an Act of Tynwald) is hereby confirmed.

N.E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Merchant Shipping (Registration) Act 1984 (an Act of Tynwald) repeals, in relation to the registration of ships in the Isle of Man, certain provision made by Part I of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 (an Act of Parliament) regarding such registration. This Order is made in pursuance of section 735(1) of that Act of Parliament and confirms the Act of Tynwald.
