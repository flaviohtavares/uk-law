# The Consumer Credit (Entry and Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1046

CONSUMER CREDIT

The Consumer Credit (Entry and Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1984

Made

20th July 1984

Laid before Parliament

23rd July 1984

Coming into Operation

13th August 1984

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 162(5) and 189(1) of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Consumer Credit (Entry and Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1984 and shall come into operation on 13th August 1984.

**2.** In Regulation 2 of the Consumer Credit (Entry and Inspection) Regulations 1977, for sub-paragraph (b) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph:—

> - (b) any consumer hire agreement under which the owner is a public telecommunications operator within the meaning of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] and any book or document which contains particulars relating to such an agreement;

.

Alexander Fletcher

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Trade and Industry

20th July 1984

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations replace Regulation 2(b) of the Consumer Credit (Entry and Inspection) Regulations 1977. It now applies to all public telecommunications operators.
