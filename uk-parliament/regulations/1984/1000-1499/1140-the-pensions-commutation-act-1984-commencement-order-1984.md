# The Pensions Commutation Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1140 (C.23)

PENSIONS

The Pensions Commutation Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1984

Made

23rd July 1984

Coming into Operation

20th August 1984

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 3(2) of the Pensions Commutation Act 1984 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby orders as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Pensions Commutation Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1984.

**2.** The Pensions Commutation Act 1984 shall come into force on 20th August 1984.

Michael Heseltine

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

23rd July 1984

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Pensions Commutation Act 1984 into force on 20th August 1984.
