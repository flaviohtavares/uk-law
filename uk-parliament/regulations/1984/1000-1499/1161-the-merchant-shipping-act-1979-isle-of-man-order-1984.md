# The Merchant Shipping Act 1979 (Isle of Man) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1161

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping Act 1979 (Isle of Man) Order 1984

Made

31st July 1984

Laid before Parliament

8th August 1984

Coming into Operation

31st August 1984

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 31st day of July 1984

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 47 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1979, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping Act 1979 (Isle of Man) Order 1984 and shall come into operation on 31st August 1984.

**2.** Section 31(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1979 shall extend to the Isle of Man as part of its law, with the modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

N.E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATIONS OF SECTION 31(1) OF THE MERCHANT SHIPPING ACT 1979 AS EXTENDED TO THE ISLE OF MAN

Article 2

**1.** Any reference to an enactment shall be construed, unless the contrary intention appears, as a reference to it as it has effect in the Isle of Man.

**2.** For “Secretary of State” (in both places) there shall be substituted “Isle of Man Harbour Board”.

**3.** For “made by statutory instrument” there shall be substituted “made with the approval of Tynwald”.

**4.** All the words from “and any statutory instrument” to the end shall be omitted.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends to the Isle of Man section 31(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1979. That provision amends section 85 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 (c. 60) (which relates to dues for space occupied by deck cargo) by substituting a new subsection (3).
