# The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1984.

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1443

LANDLORD AND TENANT

The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1984

Made

6th September 1984

Laid before Parliament

17th September 1984

Coming into Operation

9th October 1984

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 56(4) of the Housing Act 1980, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1984 and shall come into operation on 9th October 1984.

**2.** The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 3) Order 1982 is hereby amended by the omission from the list set out in the Schedule thereto of the following body:

> **9.** Seddon Properties Limited

.

**3.** The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 8) Order 1983 is hereby amended by the omission from the list set out in the Schedule thereto of the following bodies:

> **2.** Alfred Samuel Bolwell, Peter Alfred David Bolwell and Richard Leighton Hayward trading as Bolwell, Bolwell and Hayward.
>
> **3.** Alfred Samuel Bolwell, Peter Alfred David Bolwell, Donald Hayward and Richard Leighton Hayward trading as Bolwell and Hayward-County Hall

.

6th September 1984

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 56 of the Housing Act 1980 deals with assured tenancies. Such tenancies are subject to Part II of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 (c. 56), as modified by Schedule 5 to the 1980 Act, if they would otherwise have been protected tenancies or housing association tenancies under the Rent Act 1977 (c. 42). They can only be granted by bodies approved under section 56 of the 1980 Act in respect of dwelling-houses erected after the passing of that Act.

This order amends the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 3) Order 1982 and the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 8) Order 1983 by omitting three bodies from the lists of approved bodies contained in the Schedules to these orders.
