# The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Appointed Day) (No. 1) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 749

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Telecommunications Act 1984 (Appointed Day) (No. 1) Order 1984

Made

29th May 1984

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 110(5) of the Telecommunications Act 1984, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Telecommunications Act 1984 (Appointed Day) (No. 1) Order 1984.

**2.** Sections 1, 108 and 110 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Telecommunications Act 1984 shall come into force on 18th June 1984.

Kenneth Baker

Minister of State

Department of Trade and Industry

29th May 1984

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 18th June 1984—

(a) Section 1 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Telecommunications Act 1984 (which relate to the Director General of the Telecommunications);

(b) Section 108 of that Act (Extension to The Isle of Man and the Channel Islands); and

(c) Section 110 of that Act (Short title, commencement and extent).
