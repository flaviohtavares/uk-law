# The National Heritage Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 217 (C.4)

OPEN SPACES

The National Heritage Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1984

Made

29th February 1984

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 41(2) of the National Heritage Act 1983, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Heritage Act (Commencement No. 5) Order 1984.

**2.** Sections 24 to 28 of the National Heritage Act 1983 (relating to the Board of Trustees of the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew) shall come into force on 1st April 1984.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 29th February 1984.

Michael Jopling

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1984 the provisions of the National Heritage Act 1983 relating to the Board of Trustees of the Royal Botanic Gardens, so far as they are not already in force.
