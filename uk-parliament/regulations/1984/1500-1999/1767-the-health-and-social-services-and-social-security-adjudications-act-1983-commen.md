# The Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order 1984

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1984 No. 1767 (c.42)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS SOCIAL WELFARE SERVICES PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order 1984

Made

9th November 1984

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 32(2) of the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This order may be cited as the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order 1984.

(2) In this order the “**No. 5 Order**” means the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1984.

## 2. Amendment of the Schedule to the No. 5 Order

In the Schedule to the No. 5 Order—

- (a) the entry to the Local Government Act 1966 shall be deleted;
- (b) at the end of that Schedule there shall be inserted the following entry—“

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Section 30(3) | Saving. |

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Services.

John Patten

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health and Social Security

9th November 1984

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order corrects an error in the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1984 by deleting from the provisions to be brought into force by that order, the entry relating to the repeal of the Local Government Act 1966, which repeal was brought into force by the No. 1 order. The order also adds to the provisions of the No. 5 order to be brought into force on 1st January 1985, section 30(3) of the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 which provides for certain enactments amended by the Residential Homes Act 1980 (c. 7) to continue to have effect as amended notwithstanding the repeal of that Act.
