# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Haringey) (Amendment) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 3057

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Haringey) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made

19th December 1997

Laid before Parliament

9th January 1998

Coming into force

2nd February 1998

Whereas the council of the London borough of Haringey has applied to the Secretary of State for Transport for an order to be made under section 76 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and the Secretary of State has consulted the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 76(2) of that Act:

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Haringey) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 2nd February 1997.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Haringey) Order 1994

The Schedule to the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Haringey) Order 1994[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Glenda Jackson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

19th December 1997
