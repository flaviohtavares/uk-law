# The Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) (No. 3) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 3068 (c. 113)

ANTARCTICA

The Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) (No. 3) Order 1997

Made

22nd December 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 35 of the Antarctic Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) (No. 3) Order 1997.

## 2. Commencement of Sections 3 and 4 of the Antarctic Act 1994

Sections 3 and 4 of the Antarctic Act 1994 shall come into force on 14th January 1998.

Tony Lloyd

For the Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs

22nd December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 14th January 1998 sections 3 and 4 of the Antarctic Act 1994. Section 3 requires permits for Antarctic expeditions organised in or departing from the United Kingdom. Section 4 requires permits for manned British stations in Antarctica.

The Act has been progressively brought into force by commencement orders made in [1995 (S.I. No. 2748)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2748), [1996 (S.I. No. 2666)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2666) and [1997 (S.I. Nos. 1411](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1411) and [2298](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2298)). As from 14th January 1998 the whole Act will be in force.
