# The Channel 4 (Application of Excess Revenues) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 3019

BROADCASTING

The Channel 4 (Application of Excess Revenues) Order 1997

Made

17th December 1997

Coming into force

18th December 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(7) of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order a draft of which has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Channel 4 (Application of Excess Revenues) Order 1997 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

**2.** In section 27(1)(a) of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00002], for “50 per cent” substitute “331/3 per cent”.

Chris Smith

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

17th December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reduces from 50% to 331/3% the percentage of the excess revenue of the Channel Four Television Corporation which must be paid to the Independent Television Commission under section 27(1)(a) of the Broadcasting Act 1990.
