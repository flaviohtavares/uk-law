# The Land Registration Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 3036 (C.111)

LAND REGISTRATION

The Land Registration Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made

9th December 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1998

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Land Registration Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Land Registration Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

**2.** The following provisions of the Land Registration Act 1997—

- (a) section 1,
- (b) Part I of Schedule 1 and section 4(1) so far as relating thereto, and
- (c) Part I of Schedule 2 and section 4(2) so far as relating thereto,

shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

**3.** Part I of Schedule 1 to, and section 4(1) (so far as relating thereto) of, the Land Registration Act 1997 shall apply only in relation to dispositions made on or after 1st April 1998.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 9th December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Land Registration Act 1997 came into force on 27th April 1997, apart from the provisions affected by this Order. This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of the Act on 1st April 1998.
