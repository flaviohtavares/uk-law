# The Rent Assessment Committees (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 3007

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Rent Assessment Committees (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made

16th December 1997

Laid before Parliament

22nd December 1997

Coming into force

15th January 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 74 of the Rent Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rent Assessment Committees (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 15th January 1998.

## 2. Amendments

The Rent Assessment Committees (England and Wales) Regulations 1971[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for regulation 2A[^f00003] from the start to the end of paragraph (1):

> **2A. 1988 and 1989 Act references**
>
> (1) This regulation applies where a reference is made under:
>
> - paragraph 5 of Schedule 5 to the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954[^f00004] as applied by paragraph 19 of Schedule 10 to the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00005];
> - section 6, 13, 14A or 22 of the Housing Act 1988[^f00006]; or
> - paragraph 6(2) or 10(2) of Schedule 10 to the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

12th December 1997

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Win Griffiths

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Wales, Welsh Office

16th December 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Rent Assessment Committees (England and Wales) Regulations 1971 (which regulate the procedure to be followed by rent assessment committees). The amendment takes account of the functions of rent assessment committees under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, as applied by Schedule 10 to the Local Government and Housing Act 1989, in relation to long residential tenancies.
