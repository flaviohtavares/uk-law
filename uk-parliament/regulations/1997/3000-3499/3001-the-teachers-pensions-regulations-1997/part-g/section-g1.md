# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 1997, section G1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G1. Teachers' Pension Account

An account substantially in the form set out in Schedule 13 is to be prepared by the Secretary of State for every financial year starting with the financial year ending with 31st March 1998.
