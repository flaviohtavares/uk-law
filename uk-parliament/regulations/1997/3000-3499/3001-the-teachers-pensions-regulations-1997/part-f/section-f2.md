# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 1997, section F2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F2. Amounts of transfer values and additional transfer values

(1) Part I of Schedule 12 has effect, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), for determining the amounts of transfer values payable under regulation F1.

(2) Unless the receiving scheme is a club scheme, where—

- (a) the person in respect of whom a transfer value is payable had become entitled to count reckonable service under regulation F4(8) (acceptance of transfer value), and
- (b) the amount determined in accordance with Part I of Schedule 12, together with that of any cash equivalent paid on the transfer, is less than the appropriate amount,

a transfer value of the appropriate amount is to be paid.

(3) The appropriate amount is

where—

- A is the transfer value accepted under regulation F4,
- B is the total of the contributions paid by the person under Part C, except any that fell to be treated for the purposes of regulation G2 as employers' contributions, and
- C is any cash equivalent paid on the transfer.
