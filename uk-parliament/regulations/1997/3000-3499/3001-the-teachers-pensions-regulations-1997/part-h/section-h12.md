# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 1997, section H12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H12. Revocations, savings and transitional provisions

(1) Subject to the provisions of Schedule 15 the Regulations specified in Schedule 14 are revoked.

(2) Schedule 15 has effect for the purpose of making savings and transitional provisions consequential on the making of these Regulations.

(3) Nothing in Schedule 15 affects the general operation of sections 16 and 17 of the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00041].
