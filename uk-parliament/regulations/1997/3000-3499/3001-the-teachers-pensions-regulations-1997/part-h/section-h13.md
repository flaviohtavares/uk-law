# The Teachers' Pensions Regulations 1997, section H13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H13. Amendments to, and modifications of, the Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988

(1) From 1st September 1997 the Teachers' Superannuation (Consolidation) Regulations 1988[^f00042] shall have effect with the amendments specified in Part I of, and the modifications specified in Part II of, Schedule 16.

(2) The amendments specified in Part I of that Schedule shall not however apply in relation to any person who became entitled to payment of retirement benefits before 1st September 1997 (whether or not he subsequently became entitled to such benefits by virtue of regulation E15 of those Regulations on or after that date).
