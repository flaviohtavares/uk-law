# The Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Lomond) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2354 (S.156)

WATER, SCOTLAND

The Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Lomond) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1997

Made

26th September 1997

Coming into force

1st October 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Lomond) (Extension of Period of Byelaws) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

## 2. Extension of period of byelaws

The period during which the Central Scotland Water Development Board Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Lomond) Byelaws 1987 remain in force is hereby extended to 1st October 1999.

Phillip Wright

Head of European Environment and Engineering Unit, Scottish Office

The Scottish Office Agriculture,

Environment and Fisheries Department Victoria Quay Edinburgh
