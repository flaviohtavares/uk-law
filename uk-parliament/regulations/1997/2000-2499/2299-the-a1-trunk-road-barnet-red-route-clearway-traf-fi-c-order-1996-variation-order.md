# The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) traf|fi|c Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2299

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) traf|fi|c Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

Made

22nd September 1997

Coming into force

3rd October 1997

The Traf|fi|c Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traf|fi|c Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traf|fi|c Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traf|fi|c Order 1996 Variation Order 1997, and shall come into force on the 3rd October 1997.

## 2. Variations

The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traf|fi|c Order 1996[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.
