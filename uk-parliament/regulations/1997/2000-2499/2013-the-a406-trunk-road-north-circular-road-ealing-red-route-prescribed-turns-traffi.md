# The A406 Trunk Road (North Circular Road, Ealing) Red Route (Prescribed Turns) Traffic Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2013

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A406 Trunk Road (North Circular Road, Ealing) Red Route (Prescribed Turns) Traffic Order 1997

Made

18th August 1997

Coming into force

29th August 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as the A406 Trunk Road (North Circular Road, Ealing) Red Route (Prescribed Turns) Traffic Order 1997, and shall come into force on 29th August 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) causing includes permitting; and cause shall be construed accordingly;
- (b) a reference to an article or a schedule followed by a number is a reference to the article of, or the schedule to, this Order so numbered;
- (c) “Hanger Lane” means the A406 Trunk Road so named in the London Borough of Ealing;
- (d) “Chatsworth Road”, “Beaufort Road”, “Audley Road” and “Corringway” means the roads so named in the London Borough of Ealing.

## 3. Prescribed Turns

Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause a vehicle proceeding in:

- (a) Chatsworth Road (south arm);
- (b) the unnamed road lying between Chatsworth Road (south arm) and Beaufort Road and opposite Priory Cottages;
- (c) Beaufort Road;
- (d) Audley Road; or
- (e) Corringway—in the London Borough of Ealing

to turn in any direction other than left on reaching the junction with A406 Hanger Lane.

**4.** The provisions of article 3 shall not apply—

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden; or
- (b) to a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes.

## 5. Revocation or variation of existing Orders

The A406 Trunk Road (North Circular Road, Ealing) Red Route (Prescribed Turns No. 2) Experimental Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

TRO 320

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London
