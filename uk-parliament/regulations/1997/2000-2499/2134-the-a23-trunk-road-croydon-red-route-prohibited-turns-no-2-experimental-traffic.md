# The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) (No. 2) Experimental Traffic Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2134

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) (No. 2) Experimental Traffic Order 1997

Made

1st September 1997

Coming into force

14th September 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as the A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) (No. 2) Experimental Traffic Order 1997, and shall come into force on 14th September 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this order so numbered;
- (b) Causing includes permitting;
- (c) “Purley Way” means that length of the A23 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Croydon known as Purley Way;
- (d) “Miller Road” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Croydon;

## 3. Prohibition

Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause a vehicle to turn right on entering Purley Way from Miller Road.

## 4. Exemptions

The controls specified in article 3 shall not apply to—

- (a) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden; or
- (b) a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes.

## 5. Power to modify or suspend this Order

If it appears to the Traffic Director for London, or some person authorised by him, essential in the interests of the expeditious, convenient and safe movement of traffic, or of the provision of suitable and adequate parking facilities on-street, or for preserving or improving the amenities of the area through which any road affected by this order runs, and after consulting with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, he may modify or suspend any provision of this Order, save that no modification shall make an addition.

## 6. Revocation

The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Experimental Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**TRO 341**

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

1st September 1997
