# The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2095

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (No. 2) Order 1997

Made

27th August 1997

Laid before Parliament

9th September 1997

Coming into force

30th September 1997

The Secretary of State for the Environment and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly in relation to England and Wales in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (No. 2) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 30th September 1997.

## 2. Designation of bodies

Each of the following bodies, being a person appearing to the Secretary of State to be exercising functions of a public nature, shall be a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 in its application to England and Wales—

- Bedford Bereavement Care Limited
- Wansbeck Energy Company Limited.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Michael Meacher

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment,Transport and the Regions

20th August 1997

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Win Griffiths

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Welsh Office

27th August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order designates two limited companies under the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970.

The effect of the designation is to allow a local authority to provide goods and services to the designated bodies.
