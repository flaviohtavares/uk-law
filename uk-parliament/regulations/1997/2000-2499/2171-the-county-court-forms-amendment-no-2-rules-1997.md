# The County Court (Forms) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an error in [S.I. 1997/1838](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1838) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1997 No. 2171 (L.36)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Forms) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1997

Made

5th September 1997

Coming into force

1st October 1997

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1997.

**2.** In question 8 in Form N.11B in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001], for ‘which stated that the plaintiff intended to make an application for possession’, substitute ‘which stated that possession was required’.

The undersigned members of the Rule Committee, appointed by the Lord Chancellor under section 75 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002], having made these Rules, certify them and submit them to the Lord Chancellor.

Frank J. White

Helen Paling

Hugh Jones

R. Winstanley

Margaret Wilby

W. A. Vincent

Peter Birts

E. C. Gee

I allow these Rules, which shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 5th September 1997

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules make a correction in Form N.11B (form of reply to application for accelerated possession under section 21 of the Housing Act 1988). The form was introduced by [S.I. 1997/1838 (L.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1838).
