# The Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2056 (C. 82)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1997

Made

24th August 1997

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 25(1) and (4) of the Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1997.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 20(1) of the Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (return of social security post) in relation to the area falling within the London Borough of Richmond and the area falling within the London Borough of Hounslow is 25th August 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Patricia Hollis

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

24th August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force on 25th August 1997 of section 20(1) of the Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (which concerns the return of social security post) in relation to the London Boroughs of Hounslow and Richmond.
