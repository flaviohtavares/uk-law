# The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2080

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1997

Made

23rd August 1997

Laid before Parliament

5th September 1997

Coming into force

1st October 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 18(1) and 24(2) of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

(2) This Order does not extend to Northern Ireland.

**2.** In section 16(3) of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00002] (conditions on which the provision of amusements is permitted at certain commercial entertainments), paragraph (b) shall have effect as if, for the sum specified in that paragraph, there were substituted the sum of £50.

**3.** The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1995[^f00003] and the Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) (Scotland) Order 1995[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

George Howarth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

23rd August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order (which extends to England and Wales and Scotland) increases from £30 to £50 the maximum amount permitted to be taken by way of the sale of chances in any one determination of winners in amusements provided at certain fairs and other commercial entertainments under section 16 of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976.
