# The Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 (Detention by Customs Officers) (Specification of Titles) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2062

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 (Detention by Customs Officers) (Specification of Titles) Order 1997

Made

26th August 1997

Laid before Parliament

27th August 1997

Coming into force

1st October 1997

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 26(11) of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 (Detention by Customs Officers) (Specification of Titles) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

**2.** The following titles are specified for the purposes of the definition of “superior officer” in section 26(11) of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995—

- (a) Anti-Smuggling Manager;
- (b) Cargo Operational Manager;
- (c) Investigation Team Leader;
- (d) Passenger Services Division Operations Manager; and

any other title within job band 9.

Graham Allen

Jim Dowd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the job titles of those Customs Officers who are “superior officers” for the purposes of the authorisation of detention of persons in connection with certain drug smuggling offences under section 26 of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995.
