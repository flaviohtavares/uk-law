# The A4 Trunk Road (Talgarth Road, Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2504

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A4 Trunk Road (Talgarth Road, Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 1997

Made

17th October 1997

Coming into force

20th October 1997

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 14 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as The A4 Trunk Road (Talgarth Road, Hammersmith & Fulham) Red Route (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 1997 and shall come into force on the 20th October 1997.

**2.** In this Order “North End Road” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham

**3.** This Order is made because the Traffic Director for London is satisfied that traffic in the A4 Talgarth Road in the London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham at its junction with North End Road should be prohibited because works are being or are proposed to be executed on or near the junction of those roads.

**4.** During the period starting at 00.01 hours on the 20th October 1997 and ending at 23:59 on 21st January 1998 or when those works have been completed, whichever is the sooner, subject to the provisions of article 5 below, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding eastbound on the A4 Talgarth Road in the London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham to turn right into North End Road.

**5.** (1) The provisions of article 4 above shall apply only during such times and to such extent as may from time to time be indicated by traffic signs.

(2) The provisions specified in article 4 above shall not apply in respect of—

- (i) any vehicle being used for the purpose of those works, or for fire brigade, ambulance or police purpose, or
- (ii) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

TRO 359

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

17th October 1997
