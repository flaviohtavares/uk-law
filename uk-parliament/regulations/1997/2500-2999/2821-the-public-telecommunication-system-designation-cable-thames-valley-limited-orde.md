# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable Thames Valley Limited) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2821

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable Thames Valley Limited) Order 1997

Made

26th November 1997

Laid before Parliament

27th November 1997

Coming into force

26th December 1997

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Cable Thames Valley Limited on 28th July 1997 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in the Licence (“the Applicable Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in excercise of the powers conferred on her by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable Thames Valley Limited) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 26th December 1997.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Barbara Roche,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Small Firms, Trade and Industry,

Department of Trade and Industry

26th November 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Cable Thames Valley Limited a licence on 28 July 1997 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in that licence in London and the counties of Bedfordshire, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hertfordshire, Hampshire, Leicestershire, Northamptonshire, Oxfordshire, Staffordshire, Surrey, Warwickshire, West Midlands and Wiltshire. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 27th November 1997. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Cable Thames Valley Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 26th December 1997.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
