# The Combined Fire Authorities (Protection from Personal Liability) (England) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2819

FIRE SERVICES

The Combined Fire Authorities (Protection from Personal Liability) (England) Regulations 1997

Made

25th November 1997

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 1997

Coming into force

27th December 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 19 and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Combined Fire Authorities (Protection from Personal Liability) (England) Regulations 1997, and shall come into force on 27th December 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the 1947 Act” means the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00002], and “combination scheme” means a combination scheme under that Act.

## 3. Protection from personal liability

(1) Where, by virtue of section 18(3) of the Local Government Act 1992, an order under section 17 of that Act provides, or has provided—

- (a) that, subject to any combination scheme, a district is to become the area of a fire authority for the purposes of the 1947 Act; or
- (b) that, for the purposes of the making of any combination scheme, the area of a district is to be treated as an area which would become the area of a fire authority by virtue of the order;

paragraph (2) below shall apply in relation to a combined fire authority constituted by a combination scheme which includes such an area.

(2) A combined fire authority constituted by such a combination scheme shall, for the purposes of section 265 of the Public Health Act 1875[^f00003] and section 39 of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976[^f00004] (protection of members and officers etc. from personal liability), be treated as if it were a local authority.

George Howarth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

25th November 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for the protection from personal liability of members and officers of combined fire authorities created as a consequence of structural changes in local government areas effected by orders under section 17 of the Local Government Act 1992.
