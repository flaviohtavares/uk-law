# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2825 (S.178)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1997

Made

21st November 1997

Coming into force

1st January 1998

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001] section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1997 and shall come into force on 1st January 1998.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt shall be substituted for the Table in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994[^f00003] and shall apply to work carried out by a messenger-at-arms on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) above does not affect the fees payable as respects any work so carried out before the date mentioned in that sub-paragraph.

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

21st November 1997

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1994 (S.I. [1994/391](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/391)) by increasing the fees payable to Messengers-at-Arms by about 3.7%.

The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1996 (S.I. [1996/2855](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2855)).
