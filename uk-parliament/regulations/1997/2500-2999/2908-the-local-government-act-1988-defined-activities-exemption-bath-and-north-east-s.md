# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Bath and North East Somerset District Council) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2908

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Bath and North East Somerset District Council) Order 1997

Made

8th December 1997

Laid before Parliament

16th December 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Bath and North East Somerset District Council) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

## 2. Exemption of housing management

The housing management work[^f00002] of the council of the district of Bath and North East Somerset shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is undertaken during the period beginning with 1 April 1998 and ending on 31 August 1999.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State,

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

8th December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the housing management work of Bath and North East Somerset District Council so long as that work is undertaken during the period beginning with 1st April 1998 and ending on 31st August 1999.
