# The Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) (Winding Up) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2880 (S.184)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) (Winding Up) Order 1997

Made

2nd December 1997

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1997

Coming into force

30th September 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 15, 19 and 181(1) and (2)(a) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) (Winding Up) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 30th September 1998.

## 2. Winding up of Property Commission

The Local Government Property Commission (Scotland), established by the Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) Order 1995[^f00002], are hereby wound up and their assets transferred to the Secretary of State.

## 3. Revocations

The following Orders are hereby revoked:—

- (a) the Local Authorities (Property Transfer) (Scotland) Order 1995[^f00003];
- (b) the Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) Order 1995; and
- (c) the Local Authorities (Property Transfer) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1996[^f00004]

Malcolm Chisholm

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

2nd December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government Property Commission (Scotland) were established in October 1995 to deal with property transfer issues arising from the reorganisation of local government in Scotland which took effect on 1st April 1996. This Order winds up the Commission on 30th September 1998 and provides for transfer of their assets to the Secretary of State. The Order which established the Commission, and related Orders, are revoked.
