# The Combined Probation Areas (Bedfordshire) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2623

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Bedfordshire) Order 1997

Made

28th October 1997

Coming into force

30th November 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of and paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Probation Service Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by section 2 thereof, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Bedfordshire) Order 1997.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 30th November 1997 save that for the purpose of making appointments to the probation committee for the Bedfordshire Probation Area to take effect on that date, this Order shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** For that part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] that relates to the Bedfordshire Probation Area[^f00003] there should be substituted the following:

28th October 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)), so as to reduce the number of justices on the committee for the Bedfordshire Area from 14 to 8. The petty sessional divisions of Ampthill and Leighton Buzzard were formerly represented by 1 justice each. The petty sessional divisions of Biggleswade and Dunstable were represented by 2 justices and those of Bedford and Luton by 4 justices each.

The Order also provides (pursuant to paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the 1993 Act), for the co-option of a member from each of Bedfordshire County and Luton Borough Councils to the committee.
