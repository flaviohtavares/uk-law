# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2936

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1997

Made

10th December 1997

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1997

Coming into force

31st December 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 63 and 66 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 31st December 1997.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982

(1) The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982[^f00002] shall be further amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 13A(2) (obligatory certificates) for “1st January 1998” there shall be substituted “1st May 1998”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Helene Hayman

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

10th December 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982 by postponing from 1st January 1998 to 1st May 1998 the date specified in regulation 18A(2) of those Regulations (obligatory certificates).
