# The Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2846 (C. 108)

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1997

Made

1st December 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 150(3) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (Commencement No.3) Order 1997.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 shall come into force on 16th December 1997—

- sections 131 to 135 in so far as they are not already in force,
- sections 136 to 138, and
- sections 139 and 140 in so far as they are not already in force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

1st December 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 131 to 140 (relocation grants) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, other than the provisions conferring powers to make subordinate legislation and to give guidance which have already been commenced.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Act have been brought into force by a commencement order made before the date of this Order.
