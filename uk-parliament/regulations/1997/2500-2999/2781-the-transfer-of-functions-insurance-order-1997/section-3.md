# The Transfer of Functions (Insurance) Order 1997, section 3

> Text as at 2001-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Functions retained by the Secretary of State[^c-40ccfdc259bfa47a6cc382f0f346c711]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The functions of the Secretary of State under sections 47(4) and 65(2) of the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00015] (Secretary of State’s powers to prescribe fees for inspecting certain documents deposited with or served on the registrar of companies) are not transferred to the Treasury.
