# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Protective provisions

Schedule 2 to this Order shall have effect for protecting the continuing use of the harbour for various defence-related purposes.
