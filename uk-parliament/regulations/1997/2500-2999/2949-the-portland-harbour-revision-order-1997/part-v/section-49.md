# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Liability for safety of goods

The Company shall not be responsible for the safekeeping of goods deposited in any part of the harbour premises not specifically set apart by the Company for the purpose of warehousing.
