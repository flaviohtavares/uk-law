# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Development, etc., of land

The Company may form and promote, or join with any other person in forming and promoting, a company for using or developing for any purpose, or carrying on any trade or business on, any land within the harbour premises.
