# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Saving for Weymouth and Portland Borough Council

Nothing in this Order shall restrict or otherwise affect the discharge by the Council of any functions under any enactment relating to public health, environmental protection, coast protection, flood defence or land drainage.
