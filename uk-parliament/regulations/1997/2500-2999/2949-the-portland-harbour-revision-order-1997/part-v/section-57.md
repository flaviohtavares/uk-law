# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Revocation of Dockyard Port Orders

On the coming into force of this Order the following Orders in Council or provisions thereof shall be revoked—

- (a) the Dockyard Port of Portland Order 1963[^f00019];
- (b) the Dockyard Port of Portland (Amendment) Order 1965[^f00020];
- (c) the Dockyard Port of Portland (Amendment) Order 1967[^f00021];
- (d) article 6 of the Dockyard Ports (Amendment) Order 1970[^f00022];
- (e) the Dockyard Port of Portland (Amendment) Order 1973[^f00023].
