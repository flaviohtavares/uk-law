# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Recovery of charges

In addition to any other remedy given by this Order and by the Act of 1847 as incorporated with this Order, (and, in a case where the master of a vessel in respect of which a charge is payable to the Company refuses or neglects to pay the same or any part thereof, whether or not the Company’s collector has gone on board the vessel and demanded the charge pursuant to section 44 of that Act), the Company may recover any charges payable to them as a debt in any court of competent jurisdiction.
