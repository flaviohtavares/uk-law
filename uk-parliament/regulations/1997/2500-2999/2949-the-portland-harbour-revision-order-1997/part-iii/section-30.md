# The Portland Harbour Revision Order 1997, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Harbour master may prevent sailing of vessels

The harbour master may prevent the removal or sailing from the harbour of any vessel until evidence has been produced to him of the payment of any charges payable in respect of the vessel or of passengers thereon or of the goods imported or exported therein.
