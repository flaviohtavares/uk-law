# The Income Tax (Manufactured Overseas Dividends) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 2706

INCOME TAX

The Income Tax (Manufactured Overseas Dividends) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997

Made

17th November 1997

Laid before the House of Commons

18th November 1997

Coming into force

9th December 1997

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraphs 1(1) and 4(8) of Schedule 23A to the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Manufactured Overseas Dividends) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on December 1997.

**2.** In regulation 12 of the Income Tax (Manufactured Overseas Dividends) Regulations 1993[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a) the words “subject to paragraph (3)” shall be omitted;
- (b) paragraph (3) shall be omitted.

Bob Ainsworth

Jon Owen Jones

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

17th November 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Income Tax (Manufactured Overseas Dividends) Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2004)) by omitting paragraph (3) of regulation 12 of those Regulations. Regulation 12 relates to the tax treatment of manufactured overseas dividends paid to collecting agents that are representative of foreign dividends. Paragraph (3) of regulation 12 is rendered obsolete by the Income Tax (Paying and Collecting Agents) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2705](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2705)) which come into force at the same time as these Regulations.
