# The Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1997/1325](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1325) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1997 No. 1482

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1997

Made

9th June 1997

Coming into force

18th June 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 18th June 1997.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

For Article 4 of the Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004] there shall be substituted—

> **4. Directors of the trust**
>
> The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman—
>
> - (a) until 31st October 1997, 4 non-executive directors, and
> - (b) on and after 1st November 1997, 3 non-executive directors,
>
> and 3 executive directors.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health

9th June 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by temporarily increasing the number of non-executive directors from 3 to 4 until 31st October 1997.

The number of non-executive directors was reduced from 4 to 3 by the Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/1325](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1325)) with effect from 30th May 1997. As this Order is made to correct a defect in that Order, it is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Order.
