# The Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1359

LAND DRAINAGE

The Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1997

Made

28th May 1997

Coming into force

1st June 1997

Whereas the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food considers—

- (a) it is necessary or expedient to make an order under section 16(5) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001] in relation to the regional flood defence committee specified in Article 1 of this Order;
- (b) it is inappropriate that the council of any county or London borough in relation to which an entry “none” appears in column (2) of the Schedule to this Order should appoint a member of the committee; and
- (c) that one member of the committee should be appointed jointly by the councils for the counties of Hertfordshire and Milton Keynes.

Now, therefore, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him[^f00002] by section 16(5) and (8) of the Environment Act 1995 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st June 1997.

(2) In this Order “the committee” means the Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee, being the regional flood defence committee established by section 14 of the Environment Act 1995 for which the councils of the counties and London boroughs specified in the Schedule to this Order are the constituent councils.

## 2. Number of members of the committee to be appointed by each constituent council

(1) The number of members to be appointed to the committee by the council or councils of any county[^f00003] or London borough specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order shall be the number specified in the corresponding entry in column (2) of that Schedule.

(2) Any appointment made to the committee by the councils for the counties of Milton Keynes and Hertfordshire shall be made jointly.

## 3. Revocation

The Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1996[^f00004] is revoked.

Elliot Morley

Parliamentary Secretary Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

28th May 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the number of members of the regional flood defence committee known as the Anglian Regional Flood Defence Committee who are to be appointed by each constituent council or group of councils. The order takes account of changes in local government responsibilities which took effect on 1st April 1997. Regional flood defence committees of the Environment Agency were established by section 14 of the Environment Act 1995. The previous order is revoked.
