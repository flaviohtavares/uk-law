# The Transfer of Crofting Estates (Scotland) Act 1997 Commencement Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1430 (C. 56) (S. 110)

CROFTERS, COTTARS AND SMALL LANDHOLDERS

The Transfer of Crofting Estates (Scotland) Act 1997 Commencement Order 1997

Made

2nd June 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 8(2) of the Transfer of Crofting Estates (Scotland) Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** The Transfer of Crofting Estates (Scotland) Act 1997 shall come into force on 6th June 1997.

Sewel

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

2nd June 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Transfer of Crofting Estates (Scotland) Act 1997 into force on 6th June 1997.
