# The Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1082 (C. 39)

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997

Made

22nd March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(3) of the Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997.

**2.** The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in the second column of that Schedule) shall come into force on 23rd March 1997.

George Young

Secretary of State for Transport

22nd March 1997
