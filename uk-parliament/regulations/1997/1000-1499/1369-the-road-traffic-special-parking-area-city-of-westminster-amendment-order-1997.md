# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) (Amendment) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1369

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made

28th May 1997

Laid before Parliament

5th June 1997

Coming into force

1st July 1997

Whereas the council of the City of Westminster has applied to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under section 76 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and the Secretary of State has consulted the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 76(2) of that Act:

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st July 1997.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) Order 1994

(1) The Schedule to the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) Order 1994[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Part II of the Schedule (the Whitehall enforcement area excluded from the special parking area) the following item shall be substituted for item 8—

> **8.** Buckingham Gate (B323).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Glenda Jackson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

28th May 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (City of Westminster) Order 1994 so as to exclude the whole of Buckingham Gate SW1 from the special parking area designated by that Order.
