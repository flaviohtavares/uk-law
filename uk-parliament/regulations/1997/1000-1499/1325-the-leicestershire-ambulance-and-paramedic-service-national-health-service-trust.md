# The Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been amended by [S.I. 1997/1482](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1482) which is printed herein as pages 3 and 4 and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of [S.I. 1997/1325](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1325).

1997 No. 1325

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997

Made

20th May 1997

Coming into force

30th May 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997 and shall come into force on 30th May 1997.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In Article 4 of the Leicestershire Ambulance and Paramedic Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004] for “4 non-executive” there shall be substituted “3 non-executive” and for “4 executive” there shall be substituted “3 executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health

20th May 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by reducing the number of non-executive and executive directors from 4 to 3 in each case.
