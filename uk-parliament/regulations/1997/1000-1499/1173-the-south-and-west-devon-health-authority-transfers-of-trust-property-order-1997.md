# The South and West Devon Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1173

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The South and West Devon Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

Made

7th April 1997

Laid before Parliament

7th April 1997

Coming into force

29th April 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the South and West Devon Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the National Health Service trusts specifed in the Schedule to this Order, having consulted that Health Authority and those trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South and West Devon Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 29th April 1997.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the South and West Devon Health Authority;
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;
- “the trust property” means, in the case of each NHS trust specified in column (1) of the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared, agreed upon and signed on behalf of the Authority and that NHS trust on the date specified in column (2) of the Schedule in relation to that NHS trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 29th April 1997 the trust property which is trust property in relation to an NHS trust specified in the Schedule shall be transferred from the Authority to that NHS trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

7th April 1997
