# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Croydon) Order 1997, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Designation of special parking area

The Secretary of State hereby designates as a special parking area the London borough of Croydon except the roads named or described in the Schedule to this Order.
