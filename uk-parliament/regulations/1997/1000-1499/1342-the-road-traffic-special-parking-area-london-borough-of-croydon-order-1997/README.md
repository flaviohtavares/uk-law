ROAD TRAFFIC

Made

Laid before Parliament

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order consolidates the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (London Borough of Croydon) Order 1994 and the Order amending it, by continuing the designation of most of the London Borough of Croydon as a special parking area. The list in the Schedule of roads excluded from the designated area has been revised.
