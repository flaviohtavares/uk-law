# The Dangerous Dogs (Fees) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1152

DOGS

The Dangerous Dogs (Fees) Order 1997

Made

3rd April 1997

Laid before Parliament

7th April 1997

Coming into force

8th June 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 4(1)(c) of the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Dogs (Fees) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 8th June 1997.

**2.** The fee payable to the Agency under article 9 of the Dangerous Dogs Compensation and Exemption Schemes Order 1991[^f00002] shall be £20.00 plus value added tax.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

3rd April 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 amends the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991 to enable the owners of certain dangerous dogs which were not registered under the Scheme provided for in that Act to be registered if particular conditions are met. The 1991 Scheme specified a fee of £12.50 plus VAT for a certificate of exemption. This Order replaces that fee with one of £20 plus VAT for certificates issued pursuant to the 1997 Act.
