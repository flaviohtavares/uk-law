# The Wiltshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1308

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Wiltshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

Made

15th May 1997

Laid before Parliament

22nd May 1997

Coming into force

12th June 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the Wiltshire Health Authority, having consulted that Authority and the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to this Order in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Wiltshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 12th June 1997.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Wiltshire Health Authority;
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;
- “the trust property” means, in the case of each NHS trust specified in column (1) of the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared, agreed upon and signed on behalf of the Authority and that NHS trust on the date specified in column (2) of the Schedule in relation to that NHS trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 12th June 1997 the trust property which is trust property in relation to an NHS trust specified in the Schedule shall be transferred from the Authority to that NHS trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health
