# The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1151 (C.42)

DOGS

The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made

3rd April 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 6(3) of the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

**2.** The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 shall come into force on 8th June 1997.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

3rd April 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 into force on 8th June 1997.
