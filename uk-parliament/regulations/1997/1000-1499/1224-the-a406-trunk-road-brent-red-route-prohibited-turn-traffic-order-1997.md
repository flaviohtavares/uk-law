# The A406 Trunk Road (Brent) Red Route (Prohibited Turn) Traffic Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1224

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A406 Trunk Road (Brent) Red Route (Prohibited Turn) Traffic Order 1997

Made

18th April 1997

Coming into force

26th April 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as The A406 Trunk Road (Brent) Red Route (Prohibited Turn) Traffic Order 1997, and shall come into force on 26th April 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) cause includes permit;
- (b) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this Order so numbered.

## 3. Prohibited turn

Subject to the provisions of article 4 no person shall cause any vehicle proceeding north-east in the A406 North Circular Road to make a right turn into Brentfield Road.

**4.** Nothing in article 3 shall apply—

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden; or
- (b) to a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes.

## 5. Revocation of existing order

The A406 Trunk Road (Brent) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Experimental Traffic Order 1995[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

18th April 1997
