# The Welfare of Animals (Transport) Order 1997, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. IATA and CITES

(1) No person shall transport an animal by air except in compliance with the standards set by the International Air Transport Association[^f00007].

(2) No person shall transport an animal to which the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species[^f00008] refers except in compliance with the CITES guidelines for transport and preparation for shipment of live wild animals or in compliance with the standards set by the International Air Transport Association.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: Published by the International Air Transport Association, 2000 Peel Street, Montreal, Quebec, Canada H3A 2R4.

[^f00008]: Footnote: Copies of CITES can be obtained from the Department of the Environment, Tollgate House, Houlton Street, Bristol BS2 2DJ.
