# The Welfare of Animals (Transport) Order 1997, SCHEDULE 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 12 — REVOCATIONS AND DISAPPLICATION

Article 22

### PART I — DISAPPLICATION

The provisions of the Export of Animals (Protection) Order 1981[^f00011] shall not apply to the export of animals to another member State of the European Union.

### PART II — ORDERS REVOKED IN FULL

The following orders are hereby revoked in full:

- (a) The Transit of Animals (Amendment) Order, 1927 (S.R. & O. 1927/399)
