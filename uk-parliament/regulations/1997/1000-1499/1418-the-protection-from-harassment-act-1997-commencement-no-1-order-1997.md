# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1418 (C.52)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

DELICT

The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997

Made

4th June 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 15(1) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997.

**2.** Sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 16th June 1997.

Alun Michael

Minister of State

Home Office

4th June 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 16th June 1997 sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Sections 13 to 16 of that Act came into force on 21st March 1997.
