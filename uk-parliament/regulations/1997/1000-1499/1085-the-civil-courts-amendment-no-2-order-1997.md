# The Civil Courts (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1085

COUNTY COURTS

The Civil Courts (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1997

Made

24th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

25th March 1997

Coming into force

30th June 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 30th June 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, “the 1983 Order” means the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00002], and a Schedule referred to by number alone means the Schedule so numbered in that Order.

## 3. Amendments to the 1983 Order

In Schedule 1, “Andover” shall be deleted from its position in the second column opposite the entry for “Salisbury” in the first column.

**4.** In Schedule 3, the entry relating to “Andover” shall be deleted[^f00003].

Mackay of Clashfern *, C.*

Dated 24th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to close the County Court at Andover on 30th June 1997.
