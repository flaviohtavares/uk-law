# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1498 (C.58)

TORTS

The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997

Made

10th June 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997.

**2.** Sections 3(1) and (2) and 6 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 16th June 1997.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 10th June 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 16th June 1997 sections 3(1) and (2) and 6 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Sections 13 to 16 of the Act came into force at Royal Assent on 21st March 1997.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Act are to be brought into force on 16th June 1997 by the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997 made before the date of this Order.
