# The Kingston and District Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1002

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Kingston and District Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997

Made

19th March 1997

Coming into force

31st March 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Kingston and District Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In Article 3(2) of the Kingston and District Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “KT6 9QT” there shall be substituted “KT6 7QU”; and
- (b) for sub-paragraph (b) there shall be substituted the following new sub-paragraph:

  > - (b) to manage community health services provided from Tolworth Hospital, Red Lion Road, Tolworth, Surrey KT6 7QU and to own the associated premises.

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

19th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the functions of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 by altering the premises from which the trust is to provide community health services.
