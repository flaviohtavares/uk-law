# The Local Authorities (Members' Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 589

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Authorities (Members' Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made

4th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

7th March 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England and the Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 173, 175(1A), 177(1)(b), 178(1) and 270(1) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001], sections 18 and 190(1) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Members' Allowances) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997 (“the commencement day”).

(2) In these Regulations \`the principal regulations' means the Local Authorities (Members' Allowances) Regulations 1991[^f00003].

## 2. Increase of allowances

(1) The principal regulations are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 17(2) (allowance in respect of conferences and meetings) for “£26.96” there is substituted, in relation to attendance at a conference or meeting on or after the commencement day, “£27.70”.

(3) In regulation 18(1) (attendance allowance) for “£26.96” there is substituted, in respect of an approved duty performed on or after the commencement day, “£27.70”.

(4) In regulation 18(2) (financial loss allowance), in respect of an approved duty performed on or after the commencement day—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “£23.10” there is substituted “£23.96”, and
- (b) in paragraphs (b) and (c), for “£46.21” there is substituted “£47.92”.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Authorities (Members' Allowances) Regulations 1991.

Regulation 2 increases, by 2.75 per cent., the maxima which may be payable to parish and community councillors and to non-elected members of local authorities by way of allowances under section 173(1) (attendance allowance) and section 175 (allowances in respect of attendance at conferences and meetings) of the Local Government Act 1972, and also increases by 3.7 per cent. the maximum payable by way of allowances under section 173(4) (financial loss allowance) of that Act.
