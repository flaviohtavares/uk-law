# The City of Manchester (Egerton Street Service Bridge Scheme) 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 761

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The City of Manchester (Egerton Street Service Bridge Scheme) 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1997

Made

6th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him [^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** This Instrument may be cited as “The City of Manchester (Egerton Street Service Bridge Scheme) 1994 Confirmation Instrument 1997” and shall come into force on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**2.** The City of Manchester (Egerton Street Service Bridge Scheme) 1994 (“the Scheme”) is hereby confirmed with modifications.

**3.** The scheme as confirmed is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans as modified are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport and at the offices of the Council of the City of Manchester at the Town Hall, Albert Square, Manchester, M60 2LA.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Pamela Denham

Regional Director, Government Office for the North East

6th March 1996

## THE SCHEDULE — THE CITY OF MANCHESTER (EGERTON STREET SERVICE BRIDGE SCHEME) 1994

The Council of the City of Manchester (“the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under Sections 106(3) and 108(4) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf:—

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the Bridgewater Canal the bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to improve between Chester Road and Regent Road in the City of Manchester

**2.** The Council are also authorised in connection with the construction of the said bridge to divert part of the navigable watercourse specified in the said Schedule in the manner shown in that Schedule

**3.** This Scheme may be cited as “The City of Manchester (Egerton Street Service Bridge Scheme) 1994”

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME — SPECIFICATION

### POINTS OF COMMENCEMENT AND TERMINATION (See Plan ‘C’)

From a point north of the canal whose co-ordinates are 382817.93E, 397485.83N to a point south of the canal whose co-ordinates are 382824.62E, 397473.99N.

### CONSTRUCTION

Precast reinforced concrete beams with insitu reinforced concrete slab deck on reinforced concrete abutments faced with brickwork.

### SPAN

13.60 metres clear square span.

### WIDTH

4.50 metre service bay (5.50 metre overall width)

### HEADROOM

3.60 minimum headroom over the canal above the nominal water level of 25.260 metres A.O.D. and 2.10 metres minimum headroom over the canal towpath.

### LENGTH OF WATERCOURSE TO BE DIVERTED

The length of watercourse to be diverted is the Bridgewater Canal between a point on the north bank whose co-ordinates are 382818.38E, 397486.10N, to a point on the north bank whose co-ordinates are 382755.24E, 397472.93N. This length is highlighted and marked “Length of canal to be filled” on the deposited Plan ‘B’.

The existing south bank retaining wall will be retained. A new abutment will be constructed and act as a retaining wall to the canal on the north side. The existing width of the canal varies from 8 metres to 13 metres and will be realigned to 10 metres.
