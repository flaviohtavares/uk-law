# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1997

> Text as at 2026-07-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1997 No. 735

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1997

Made

10th March 1997

Coming into force

31st March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(1) and (2) of the Petroleum Act 1987[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement[^c-8c8492ae1eb4c043f66c86c0b0413ff4]

This order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

## 2. Establishment of safety zones[^c-44621187aaa181295cb83d23664f06f8]

A safety zone is hereby established around each installation specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 hereto (being an installation stationed in waters to which subsection (7) of section 21 of the Act applies) having a radius of five hundred metres from the point as respects that installation which has the co-ordinates of latitude and longitude according to European Datum (1950) specified in columns 2 and 3 of that Schedule.

## 3. Revocation of orders[^c-caa685410015fd5d18cd19ae73af7165]

The orders listed in Schedule 2 hereto are revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

10th March 1997

This order establishes, under section 22 of the Petroleum Act 1987, safety zones having a radius of 500 metres from the specified point, around each installation specified in Schedule 1 to this order and stationed in waters to which section 21(7) of that Act applies (these include territorial waters and waters in areas designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act [1964 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/29)).

Vessels (which for this purpose include hovercraft, submersible apparatus and installations in transit) are prohibited from entering or remaining in a safety zone except with the consent of the Health and Safety Executive or in accordance with regulations made under section 23(1) of the Act (currently the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1331))).

The order revokes the orders listed in Schedule 2 which had previously established safety zones around all the installations specified in Schedule 1, except the new ones now established around Curlew B-Wellhead, Curlew D-Wellhead and Gannet F-Manifold, and had established twenty-four other safety zones, no longer required, around other installations.
