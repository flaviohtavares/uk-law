# The Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Extension) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 662

PENSIONS

The Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Extension) Order 1997

Made

4th March 1997

Coming into force

27th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Social Security, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Polish Resettlement Act 1947[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and commencement

This Order, which may be cited as the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme (Extension) Order 1997, shall be read as one with the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme 1964[^f00003], (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) and shall come into force on 27th March 1997.

## 2. Extension of the Scheme

For the purposes of Article 14(1) of the Scheme (duration of the Scheme) the extended period of five years from and including 27th March 1997 is hereby specified, and accordingly payments under the Scheme shall be limited to such as fall due for payment before 27th March 2002.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Mackay of Ardbrecknish

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

4th March 1997

We consent,

Roger Knapman

Richard Ottaway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme 1964 makes provision for the payment of war pensions and allowances to certain members of the Polish Forces, their widows and dependants. This Order extends the life of the Scheme, which would otherwise expire on 26th March 1997, until 26th March 2002.

This Order does not impose any costs on business.
