# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1997, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Earnings Limits

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases)—

- (a) for the sum of £130 in paragraphs (a) and (b) there shall be substituted the sum of £135; and
- (b) the first sum specified in paragraph (b) is £17.
