# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1997, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

In the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995[^f00033], in regulation 18(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for the sum of £12.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £13.15;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for the sum of £8.10 there shall be substituted the sum of £8.30; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for the sum of £4.05 there shall be substituted the sum of £4.15.
