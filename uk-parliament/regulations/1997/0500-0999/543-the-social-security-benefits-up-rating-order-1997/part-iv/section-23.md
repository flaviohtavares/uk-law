# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1997, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Increase in age-related amount of contribution-based Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance) —

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for the sum of £28.85 there shall be substituted the sum of £29.60;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for the sum of £37.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £38.90; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for the sum of £47.90 there shall be substituted the sum of £49.15.
