# The Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 978

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made

18th March 1997

Coming into force

14th April 1997

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 2(2) and (4), 101[^f00001] and 146(1) to (2) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 14th April 1997.

## 2. Amendments

The form set out in the Schedule to the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) Regulations 1996[^f00003] is amended as mentioned in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Application

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to applications for a grant made before 14th April 1997.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the form, set out in the Schedule to the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) Regulations 1996, to be used by owner-occupiers and tenants when applying for housing renewal grants under Chapter I of Part I of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996. Most of the amendments are consequential to amendments being made by the Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/977](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/977)) to the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/2890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2890), amended by S.I. [1996/3119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3119)). There are also minor and drafting amendments.

Regulation 3 stops the Regulations applying to applications made before 14th April 1997.
