# The Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 755

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 1997

Made

13th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

14th March 1997

Coming into force

4th April 1997

Whereas the review body appointed under section 1(1) of the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] (“the Act”) have made a report following the reference to them of certain matters;

And whereas the Secretary of State has consulted, in accordance with section 2(1) and (2) of the Act, such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of voluntary, grant-maintained and grant-maintained special schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to her to be concerned;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 4th April 1997.

## 2. Application

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Act 1991) in England and Wales.

## 3. School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Document 1996[^f00002] shall be amended with effect from 1st April 1997 as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 4.1(a), for the words “A head teacher or deputy head teacher shall, until 30th November 1996,” there shall be substituted the words “During the period 1st April 1997 to 30th November 1997 a head teacher or deputy head teacher shall”;
- (b) in paragraph 4.1(b), for the words “From 1st December 1996” there shall be substituted the words “From 1st December 1997”;

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Document 1996 (“the Document”) by uprating the amounts of salary and certain allowances. The following are increased:

- (a) the head and deputy head teachers’ pay spine and ranges set out in paragraph 4 of the Document;
- (b) the classroom teachers’ pay spine set out in paragraph 9 of the Document;
- (c) the unqualified teachers’ pay scale set out in paragraph 15 of the Document;
- (d) the London Area allowances set out in paragraph 21.1 of the Document.

In each case there are two increases, the first for the period 1st April 1997 to 30th November 1997 and the second as from 1st December 1997.

The Document is published by The Stationery Office Limited and is obtainable from Government book shops and through booksellers.
