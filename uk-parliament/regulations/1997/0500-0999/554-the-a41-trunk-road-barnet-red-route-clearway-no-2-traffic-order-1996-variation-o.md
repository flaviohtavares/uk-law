# The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) (No. 2) Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 554

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) (No. 2) Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

Made

28th February 1997

Coming into force

8th March 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) (No. 2) Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1997, and shall come into force on 8th March 1997.

**2.** The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) (No. 2) Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is varied such that in item 8 of the Table in Schedule 2, for the words “477 Watford Way” substitute the words “481 Watford Way”.

TRO242

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

28th February 1997
