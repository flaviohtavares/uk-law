# The Combined Probation Areas (Cleveland—Teesside) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 725

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Cleveland—Teesside) Order 1997

Made

22nd February 1997

Coming into force

1st March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of the Probation Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Cleveland—Teesside) Order 1997.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st March 1997.

**2.** For the reference to Cleveland as a probation area in Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] there shall be substituted a reference to Teesside.

Blatch

Minister of State

Home Office

22nd February 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Area Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) so as to change the name of the probation area comprising Hartlepool, Langbaurgh and Teesside petty sessions areas (formerly known as Cleveland), to Teesside.
