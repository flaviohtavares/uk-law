# The Sheep Scab Order 1997

> Text as at 2019-03-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

1997 No. 968

ANIMALS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

ANIMAL HEALTH *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

The Sheep Scab Order 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

Made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

19th March 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

Coming into force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

1st July 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1), 8(1), 14(1), 15(4), 23, 25, 28 and 83(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d][^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 1. Title and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e8aebc5781e8a1b9b599228d86127d10][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

This Order may be cited as the Sheep Scab Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st July 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4a9d392d92621d747578da8cf1210685][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- “clearance notice” means a notice publicised in accordance with article 3 below; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- “common land” means, in England and Wales, any common or unenclosed land, and in Scotland, land grazed in common including common grazings within the meaning of the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d][^f00002].

(2) For the purposes of this Order, a sheep is visibly affected with sheep scab if it exhibits clinical signs of the disease. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(3) A notice served under this Order shall be in writing, may be made subject to conditions and may be amended, suspended or revoked in writing at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 3. Notice of clearance of sheep from common land (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e01bd7e91b1cb6c7db9fd26577de3132][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) If a local authority is satisfied that any common land or any part of common land has on it sheep which are visibly affected with sheep scab, it may publish a notice in accordance with this article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) The notice shall be in writing and shall be publicised in such way as the local authority thinks fit in order to draw it to the attention of persons affected by it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(3) The notice shall require all persons keeping sheep on the land specified in the notice to move all those sheep off that land before the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(4) If the local authority thinks it is necessary, the notice may provide that, at least 48 hours before any treatment for sheep scab is carried out in accordance with the following article, the owner of the sheep shall notify the local authority of the time and place that the treatment will be carried out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(5) Following the publication of a notice under this article, any person keeping sheep on land specified in the notice shall ensure that those sheep are removed from that land before the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 4. Movement of sheep after the date specified in the notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-473f918f4b23200418bb536f49da3c37][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) No person shall move sheep on to the land specified in a clearance notice for three complete days from the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) No person shall move sheep on to the land specified in a clearance notice for three months from the date of the notice (or such earlier date as the notice shall specify), or keep sheep on that land, unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) those sheep have been treated against sheep scab with a product for the treatment of sheep scab which may be placed on the market under the provisions of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d] Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013[^key-26e7bb04822a747f745debdc7a9ffd8e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d] and the local authority has, if it thinks that it is necessary to do so, checked that the treatment has been carried out; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) the person moving the sheep on to the land has given the following information to the local authority in writing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
  - (i) the fact that the sheep have been treated against sheep scab; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d] the number of sheep which have been treated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d] the date or dates of the treatment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d] the product used; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (c) the local authority has authorised in writing the movement of those sheep on to the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(3) A local authority shall not authorise the movement of sheep in accordance with this article unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) it has received the information required in the preceding paragraph; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) if the movement is within 16 days of the date specified in the notice (or, if the land was not cleared by the date specified in the notice, 16 days from the date on which the land was cleared), it is satisfied that the sheep have been treated in such a way that they will not be affected by sheep scab mites remaining on the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 5. Seizure of unauthorised sheep (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ce82053e7fca1b18cf66ae3581ea8f8c][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) A local authority may seize and detain any sheep which are found on land specified in a clearance notice while that notice is in force and which have not been authorised to go on to that land by the local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) If the owner of a sheep seized by a local authority under this article establishes his right to ownership of it within 7 days of its seizure and pays to the local authority the expenses incurred in seizing and detaining the sheep, he may take possession of the sheep. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(3) Unless the owner of a sheep has established his right to ownership and paid the expenses of the local authority under paragraph (2) above within 7 days of seizure, the local authority may either *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) treat and sell the sheep, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) cause the sheep to be slaughtered and sell the carcase *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

and deduct from the proceeds of sale expenses incurred and retain any surplus for payment to any person who can establish that the sheep belonged to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 6. Movement of sheep with sheep scab (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5c01b866d00a0a30f126a687f342a202][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) No person shall move any sheep visibly affected with sheep scab, or any sheep from a flock containing one or more sheep visibly affected with sheep scab, on to or off any premises except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) for treatment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) for immediate slaughter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (c) in accordance with a notice under article 3 above; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (d) under the authority of a licence in writing granted by an inspector. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) Any movement of sheep under this article shall be carried out in such a way that other sheep are not contaminated with sheep scab by coming into contact with them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 7. Treatment of sheep with sheep scab (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a7c82946dee7f63dd647e21f8c3f26fd][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) Any person who is the keeper of sheep visibly affected with sheep scab shall, as soon as is reasonably practicable, treat those sheep and all other sheep in the flock with a product authorised in accordance with article 4(2)(a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) An inspector may serve a notice under this article on any person in charge of sheep visibly affected with sheep scab, requiring the isolation of the animals (or, if they are not on a holding occupied by the keeper, removal to that holding and isolation on that holding) pending the results of testing for sheep scab; and, if sheep scab is confirmed, a further notice may be served requiring the keeper to treat them within a specified period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(3) The notice may include other sheep kept by the person on whom the notice has been served, if those sheep have been in contact with the affected sheep. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(4) A person on whom a notice to treat sheep scab is served under this article shall within the period specified in the notice, either *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) treat or cause the sheep to be treated in accordance with paragraph (1) above, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) ensure that the sheep are slaughtered. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(5) A person on whom a notice to treat sheep scab has been served under this article shall, within two weeks of the date on which he complied with the notice, send to the inspector who served the notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) in the case of sheep treated for sheep scab, a document signed by a veterinary surgeon stating the date on which the treatment took place and the treatment used; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) in the case of sheep which were slaughtered, evidence of such slaughter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 8. Powers of local authority where sheep with sheep scab are not treated (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b243d38440ca4e798896c683940d9bb5][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

Where a local authority is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

- (a) a notice has been served under article 7(2) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
- (b) that notice has not been complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

it may cause any sheep specified in the notice to be treated for sheep scab at the expense of the person on whom the notice was served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 9. Powers to take samples (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dba5bf3f0b532be1e3fb6d79dd3fd800][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(1) A veterinary inspector who enters any premises for the purpose of ascertaining whether sheep scab exists there may carry out such inquiries, examinations and tests and take such samples as are necessary to ascertain whether sheep scab exists or has, within 56 days, existed there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(2) The occupier of any land, building or other place or any person in his employment shall render such reasonable assistance to a veterinary inspector as the veterinary inspector may require for the purposes of facilitating the exercise of his powers under this article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 10. Marking sheep (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-584df9913c459182eb636e9656a28ed8][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

An inspector may, for the purposes of identification, mark any animal or carcase when exercising powers under this Order; and no person shall deface, obliterate, or remove such mark. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

## 11. Enforcement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c431d2ebcbb4beda3be43b7a8e5365d3][^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

This Order shall be executed and enforced by the local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Welsh Office *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

(This note is not part of the Order) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

This Order gives a local authority power to serve a notice requiring the temporary clearance from common land of sheep affected with sheep scab (articles 3 and 4). If sheep are not moved in accordance with the notice the local authority may seize them (article 5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

The Order prohibits the movement of sheep affected with sheep scab from any premises other than in accordance with this Order or in other specified circumstances (article 6). It requires a keeper of a sheep affected with sheep scab to treat the animal and all other sheep in the flock, and gives to local authorities powers to serve notices requiring treatment, and default powers if the notice is not complied with (articles 7 and 8). It enables a veterinary inspector to take samples relating to sheep scab (article 9) and enables an inspector to mark the sheep (article 10). It is enforced by the local authority (article 11). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

Breach of this Order is an offence under section 73 of the Animal Health Act 1981. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]

A Compliance Cost Assessment has been prepared and placed in the library of each House of Parliament. Copies can be obtained from the Animal Health (Disease Control) Division of the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, Government Buildings, Hook Rise South, Tolworth, Surbiton, Surrey KT6 7NF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39fdab338f44b245570136a968436b7d]
