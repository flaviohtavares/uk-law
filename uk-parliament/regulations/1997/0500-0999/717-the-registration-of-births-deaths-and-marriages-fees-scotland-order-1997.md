# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 717 (S.60)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997

Made

6th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(1) and (2) of, and paragraph (1)(b) of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, extent, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) This Order shall apply to Scotland only.

(3) In this Order a reference to a numbered column is to the column in the Schedule to this Order bearing that number.

## 2. Fees

Each of the enactments mentioned in column 1 is hereby amended by varying the sum specified by that enactment as the amount or maximum amount payable in respect of the matter described in column 2 from the sum shown in column 3 to the sum shown in column 4.

## 3. Revocation

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1993[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House Edinburgh

6th March 1997

[^f00003][^f00004]

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, amends, with effect from 1st April 1997, the sum specified as the amount or maximum amount payable under certain statutory provisions for a certificate of birth, death or marriage issued for the purposes of certain Acts. The sum specified as the amount or maximum amount payable is increased as at that date from £7.00 to £8.00. The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriage (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1993 is revoked.
