# The Justices' Clerks (Amendment) Rules 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 710 (L. 15)

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

PROCEDURE

The Justices' Clerks (Amendment) Rules 1997

Made

10th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 144 of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980[^f00001], after consultation with the Rule Committee appointed under that section, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the Justices' Clerks (Amendment) Rules 1997 and, subject to paragraph (2) below, shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) Rule 4 of these Rules shall have effect in the same way as section 65 of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996[^f00002] has effect.

**2.** The Justices' Clerks Rules 1970[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Rules.

**3.** In rule 4(1)—

- (a) sub-paragraphs (b) and (c), and
- (b) the words “, member or officer”, wherever they appear, shall be omitted.

**4.** Paragraph 4A(2) of the Schedule shall be omitted.

Mackay of Clashfern *,C.*

Dated 10th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Justices' Clerks Rules 1970 by revoking sub-paragraphs (b) and (c) of rule 4(1). These sub-paragraphs, now redundant, which extend the categories of persons who, subject to that rule, could do the things specified in the Schedule to the Rules, correspond to section 28(1A)(b) and (c) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1979 which were repealed by the Police and Magistrates' Courts Act [1994 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/29).

These Rules also revoke paragraph 4A(2) of the Schedule to the 1970 Rules which deals with the making of witness orders in accordance with section 1 of the Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act [1965 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1965/69). That section was repealed by the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act [1996 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/25).
