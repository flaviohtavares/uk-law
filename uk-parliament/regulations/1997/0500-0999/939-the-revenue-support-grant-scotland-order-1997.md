# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 939 (S.81)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1997

Made

12th February 1997

Laid before the House of Commons

13th February 1997

Coming into force

5th March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be appropriate in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of the said Schedule, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1997 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

## 2. Revenue support grants for 1991–92

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1991-92 are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the financial year 1991-92 shall be the amount redetermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 3. Revenue support grants for 1992–93

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1992-93 are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the financial year 1992-93 shall be the amount redetermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 3 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 4. Revocation

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1996 (which previously redetermined the amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of the financial years 1991-92 and 1992-93)[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

This Order redetermines for the fifth time the amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of each of the financial years 1991-92 and 1992-93.

As a consequence, the Order revokes the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1996 which previously redetermined the amount of such grant payable in respect of those financial years.

The local authorities referred to in the Order ceased to exist as at 1st April 1996 and arrangements for additional payments, and repayments, arising from this Order accordingly fall to be made with the new authorities which came into existence on that date.
