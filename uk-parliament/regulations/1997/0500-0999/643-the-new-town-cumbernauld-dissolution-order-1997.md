# The New Town (Cumbernauld) Dissolution Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 643 (S. 41)

NEW TOWNS

The New Town (Cumbernauld) Dissolution Order 1997

Made

4th March 1997

Coming into force

31st March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 36G(1) of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with Cumbernauld Development Corporation, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the New Town (Cumbernauld) Dissolution Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

(2) In this Order, “Cumbernauld Development Corporation” means the Cumbernauld Development Corporation established by the New Town (Cumbernauld) (Development Corporation) Order 1956[^f00002].

**2.** The Secretary of State hereby appoints 31st March 1997 as the date for the dissolution of Cumbernauld Development Corporation.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 31st March 1997 as the date for the dissolution of the Cumbernauld Development Corporation.
