# The Treasure Act 1996 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 760 (C.29)

TREASURE

The Treasure Act 1996 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997

Made

12th March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 15(2) of the Treasure Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Treasure Act 1996 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1997.

**2.** Section 11 of the Treasure Act 1996 shall come into force on 13th March 1997.

Lord Inglewood

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department for National Heritage

12th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 13th March 1997 as the date for the coming into force of section 11 of the Treasure Act 1996.
