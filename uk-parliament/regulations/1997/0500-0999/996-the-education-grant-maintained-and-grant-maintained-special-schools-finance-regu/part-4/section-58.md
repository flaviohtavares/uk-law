# The Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1997, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Capital grants

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the funding authority may pay capital grants in respect of expenditure of a capital nature of a class or description specified in Schedule 8 incurred or to be incurred by the governing body of a school in respect of which these Regulations apply.

(2) In the case of a special school which is established in a hospital, paragraph (1) shall apply only in respect of expenditure of a class or description specified in paragraph l(d) of Schedule 8.
