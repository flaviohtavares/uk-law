# The Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1997, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Redetermination to take into account local education authority capital expenditure from the revenue account

(1) The funding authority may redetermine the amount of maintenance grant payable in respect of the school for the year and revise their determination by adding to the amount so determined such sum as appears to them to be fair and reasonable having regard to—

- (a) the amount of expenditure of a capital nature incurred by the local education authority in respect of relevant schools;
- (b) the proportion of the total amount of expenditure of a capital nature incurred by the local education authority charged to a revenue account of the authority as defined in section 41(2) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00018];
- (c) the amount of capital grants paid to the governing body of the school by the funding authority in the financial year in question.

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- “expenditure of a capital nature” means expenditure treated by the local education authority as expenditure of a capital nature;
- “relevant schools” means all primary or all secondary schools or all special schools maintained or formerly maintained by the authority required to be covered by a statement for the year under section 122(2) of the 1996 Act, according as to whether the school is a primary school, a secondary school or a special school.
