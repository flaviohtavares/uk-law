# The Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) Regulations 1997, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Contingencies

The funding authority shall determine the amount which it appears to them, were the school maintained by the local education authority, could have been allocated for the purposes of the school in accordance with the authority’s scheme in respect of contingencies.
