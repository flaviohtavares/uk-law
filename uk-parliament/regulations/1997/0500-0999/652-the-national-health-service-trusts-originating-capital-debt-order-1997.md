# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 652

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1997

Made

6th March 1997

Coming into force

17th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 9(1) and (4) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 17th March 1997.

(2) In this Order “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. Originating capital debt of NHS trusts

(1) The originating capital debt of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified opposite it in column 2 of the Schedule.

(2) Each originating capital debt specified in column 2 of the Schedule shall be divided between an initial loan of an amount specified opposite it in column 3 of the Schedule and public dividend capital of an amount specified opposite it in column 4 of the Schedule.

Stephen Dorrell

Secretary of State for Health

We consent, 4th March 1997

Gyles Brandreth

Roger Knapman

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital debt provided for in section 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 of NHS trusts established under that Act with an operational date of 1st April 1996. It provides also for the splitting of the originating capital debts into initial loan and public dividend capital.
