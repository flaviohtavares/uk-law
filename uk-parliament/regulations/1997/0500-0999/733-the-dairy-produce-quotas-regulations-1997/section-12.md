# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. National reserve

(1) The national reserve shall comprise such wholesale and direct sales quota as is not for the time being allocated to any person, including any quota withdrawn under these Regulations.

(2) The Minister may make allocations from the national reserve in accordance with the Community legislation and these Regulations.
