# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Notification by the Intervention Board of apportionment of quota by arbitration

(1) Where the Intervention Board has reasonable grounds for believing—

- (a) that the areas used for milk production on a holding are not as specified in a notice or application submitted for the purposes of regulation 7 or 9(1) respectively; or
- (b) that the areas used for milk production on a holding were not as agreed between the parties at the time of apportionment in a case where no notice or application has yet been submitted for the purposes of the aforementioned regulations,

it may give notice of this fact to the person who submitted the form, or in a case where no such notice or application was made, to the transferee.

(2) In any case to which paragraph (1) applies the apportionment or prospective apportionment of that quota shall be made—

- (a) in England and Wales and Northern Ireland by arbitration in accordance with Schedules 2 and 4 respectively;
- (b) in Scotland in accordance with Schedule 3.
