# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — MEANING OF COMMUNITY LEGISLATION

Regulation 2(1)

**1.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 857/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/0857), adopting general rules for the application of the levy referred to in Article 5c of Regulation [(EEC) No. 804/68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1968/0804) in the milk and milk products sector, OJ No. L90, 1.4.84, p. 13.

**2.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 764/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/0764), amending Regulation [(EEC) No. 857/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/0857), OJ No. L84, 29.3.89, p. 2.

**3.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1639/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/1639), amending Regulation [(EEC) No. 857/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/0857), OJ No. L150, 15.6.91, p. 35.

**4.** Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 1756/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/1756), fixing the operative events for the agricultural conversion rate applicable to milk and milk products, OJ No. L161, 2.7.93, p. 48.

**5.** Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2187/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/2187), providing for an offer of compensation to certain producers of milk and milk products temporarily prevented from carrying on their trade, OJ No. L196, 5.8.93, p. 6.

**6.** Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2562/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/2562), laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation 2055/93, OJ No. L235, 18.9.93, p. 18.

**7.** Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2648/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/2648), laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2187/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/2187), OJ No. L243, 29.9.93, p. 1.
