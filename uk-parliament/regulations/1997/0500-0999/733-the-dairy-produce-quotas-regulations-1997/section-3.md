# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Establishment of quota

Total direct sales quota and total wholesale quota for any person and purchaser quota for any purchaser in respect of any quota year shall be established in accordance with these Regulations and the Community legislation.

**3.** A producer who is awarded a temporary reallocation of quota under regulation 14(2)(b)(i) or (2)(b)(ii) of these Regulations is to have his award calculated on the basis of a maximum of 16 litres per qualifying cow per qualifying day in any quota year (regulation 14(3)(a)).
