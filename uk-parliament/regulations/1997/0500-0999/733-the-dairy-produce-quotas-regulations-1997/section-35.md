# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Revocation

The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1994[^f00021], the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations 1994[^f00022], the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1994[^f00023], the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations 1995[^f00024], the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations 1996[^f00025] and the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations 1997[^f00026] shall be revoked.
