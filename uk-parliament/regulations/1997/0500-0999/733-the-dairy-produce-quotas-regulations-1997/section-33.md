# The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 1997, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Withdrawal of Special Quota

Where a producer has special quota registered in his name and it appears to the Minister that the producer has made a false or misleading statement in his application for special quota or has failed to comply with the requirements in relation to special quota, the Minister may withdraw the whole or any part of the special quota.
