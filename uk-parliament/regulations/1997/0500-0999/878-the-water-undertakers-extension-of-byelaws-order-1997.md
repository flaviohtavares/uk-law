# The Water Undertakers (Extension of Byelaws) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 878

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Water Undertakers (Extension of Byelaws) Order 1997

Made

6th March 1997

Coming into force

31st March 1997

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of his powers conferred by section 19(6) of the Water Act 1945[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Water Undertakers (Extension of Byelaws) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

## 2. Extension of water byelaws

The byelaws listed in column (2) of the Schedule and made by, or having effect as if made by, the water undertakers listed in column (1) of the Schedule under section 17 of the Water Act 1945 (prevention of waste of water etc.) shall remain in force until 30th September 1998.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

George Craig

An Under Secretary in the Welsh Office

6th March 1997
