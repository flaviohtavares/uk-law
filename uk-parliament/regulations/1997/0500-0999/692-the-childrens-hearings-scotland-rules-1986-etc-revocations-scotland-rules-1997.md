# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Rules 1986 etc. (Revocations) (Scotland) Rules 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 692 (S.52)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Rules 1986 etc. (Revocations) (Scotland) Rules 1997

Made

7th March 1997

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 36(8) and 45 of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00001] and section 42(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Rules–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Rules 1986 etc (Revocations) (Scotland) Rules 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Revocations

The following Rules shall be revoked on 1st April 1997–

- The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Rules 1986[^f00003];
- The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1996[^f00004];
- The Reporter’s Duties and Transmission of Information, etc. (Scotland) Rules 1971[^f00005].

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th March 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Rules revoke the rules listed in the Schedule.
