# The Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 582

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1997

Made

28th February 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 1, 3 and 6(2)(d) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed by section 5(2) of that Act[^f00002], and considering it appropriate to include, among the functions of the NHS trust established by this Order, certain functions in relation to the provision of services by the trust for Health Authorities or Special Health Authorities[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990;
- “operational date” has the meaning assigned to it in paragraph 3(1)(e) of Schedule 2 to the Act;
- “the trust” means the Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust established by article 2 of this Order.

## 2. Establishment of the trust

There is hereby established an NHS trust which shall be called the Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

## 3. Nature and functions of the trust

(1) The trust is established for the purpose specified in section 5(1)(a) of the Act.

(2) The trust’s functions shall be to own and manage hospital accommodation and services provided at Pontefract General Infirmary, Friarwood Lane, Pontefract, West Yorkshire WF8 1PL, at Pinderfields General Hospital, Aberford Road, Wakefield WF1 4EE and at other premises associated with either of those hospitals (including the management of teaching and research facilities).

## 4. Directors of the trust

The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 5 non-executive directors and 5 executive directors.

## 5. Operational date and accounting date of the trust

(1) The operational date of the trust shall be 1st April 1997.

(2) The accounting date of the trust shall be 31 March.

## 6. Restriction on disposal of assets

The sum specified for the purposes of paragraph 6(2)(d) of Schedule 2 to the Act (maximum value of freely disposable assets) in relation to the trust shall be £1,000,000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

28th February

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes the Pinderfields and Pontefract Hospitals National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust provided for in section 5 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990. It also provides for the functions of the trust (article 3). It specifies the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions) and the accounting date of the trust (article 5), and it specifies the value of assets in excess of which the Secretary of State is to consider the disposal of the assets (article 6).
