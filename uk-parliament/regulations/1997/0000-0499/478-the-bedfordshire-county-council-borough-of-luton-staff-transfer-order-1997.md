# The Bedfordshire County Council (Borough of Luton) (Staff Transfer) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 478

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Bedfordshire County Council (Borough of Luton) (Staff Transfer) Order 1997

Made

24th February 1997

Laid before Parliament

28th February 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 17(1) and 26(5)(b) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Bedfordshire County Council (Borough of Luton) (Staff Transfer) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) This Order makes provisions which are consequential or supplementary to the Bedfordshire (Borough of Luton) (Structural Change) Order 1995[^f00002].

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order

- “contract of employment” means a contract under which a person works for another person whether under a contract of service or apprenticeship or otherwise but does not include a contract for services;
- “designated list” means the list marked “The Bedfordshire County Council (Borough of Luton) (Staff Transfer) Order 1997 Designated List” which is signed by the Secretary of State for the Environment, one copy of which is deposited with the Luton Borough Council, one copy with the Bedfordshire County Council and one in the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment;
- “employee” means a person employed under a contract of employment including a person employed under a fixed term contract the expiry date for which is after 31st March 1997, but does not include a person whose employment is on a temporary basis and who on 31st March 1997 has had a continuous period of employment (when calculated in accordance with sections 210 to 219 of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00003]) of less than two years.

## 3. Transfer of staff

(1) This article shall apply to any employee (a “designated employee”)—

- (a) who immediately before 1st April 1997 (“the reorganisation date”) was employed by the Bedfordshire County Council;
- (b) whose employment would continue but for the transfer of functions to the Borough of Luton; and
- (c) whose name is mentioned, or who falls within a class or description of employees which is mentioned, in the designated list.

(2) The contract of employment of a designated employee shall not be terminated on the reorganisation date but shall have effect as if originally made between that employee and the Luton Borough Council.

(3) This article is without prejudice to any provision of the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 1981[^f00004].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Curry

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

24th February 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is supplemental to the Bedfordshire (Borough of Luton) (Structural Change) Order 1995 which alters the structure of local government in the county of Bedfordshire. This Order specifies the staff employed by the Bedfordshire County Council whose employment is to be transferred to the Luton Borough Council.
