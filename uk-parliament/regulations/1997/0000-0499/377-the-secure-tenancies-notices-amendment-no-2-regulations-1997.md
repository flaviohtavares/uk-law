# The Secure Tenancies (Notices) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This statutory instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1997/71](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/71) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that statutory instrument.

1997 No. 377

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Secure Tenancies (Notices) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997

Made

11th February 1997

Coming into force

12th February 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 83(2) and (7) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Secure Tenancies (Notices) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 12th February 1997.

## 2. Amendment

(1) The notice in Part I of the Schedule to the Secure Tenancies (Notices) Regulations 1987[^f00002] (notice of seeking possession), as substituted by regulation 2 of the Secure Tenancies (Notices) (Amendment) Regulations 1997[^f00003], is amended as follows.

(2) In the last sentence of the words substituted as mentioned in paragraph (1), for “the date inserted above” substitute “the date this Notice is given”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

James Clappison

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

11th February 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Secure Tenancies (Notices) Regulations 1987 to rectify an error in an amendment made to those Regulations by the Secure Tenancies (Notices) (Amendment) Regulations 1997. The amendment is to the form of notice of seeking possession to be given by a landlord to a secure tenant with a periodic tenancy.
