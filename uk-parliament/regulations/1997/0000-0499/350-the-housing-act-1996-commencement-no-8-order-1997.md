# The Housing Act 1996 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 350 (C. 16)

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing Act 1996 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1997

Made

13th February 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 232 (3) of the Housing Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing Act 1996 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1997.

## 2. Commencement

Part II of the Housing Act 1996, so far as not already brought into force, except section 73, shall come into force on 3rd March 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

James Clappison

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

13th February 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd March 1997 Part II of the Housing Act 1996 so far as it is not already in force, other than section 73. Part II deals with the control of houses in multiple occupation, and the repeal of Part XII of the Housing Act 1985 which concerns common lodging houses.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Housing Act 1996 have been brought into force or will be brought into force by commencement orders made before the date of this Order.
