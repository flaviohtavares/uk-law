# The North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 395

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

Made

19th February 1997

Laid before Parliament

26th February 1997

Coming into force

20th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the Winchester Health Authority and the Basingstoke and North Hampshire Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to this Order, having consulted the North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority and those trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 20th March 1997.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority;
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;
- “the trust property” means, in the case of each body specified in column (1) of the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared, agreed upon and signed on behalf of the Authority and that body on 16th January 1997.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 20th March 1997 the trust property which is trust property in relation to an NHS trust specified in the Schedule shall be transferred from the Authority to that NHS trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

19th February 1997

## SCHEDULE — BODIES TO RECEIVE TRUST PROPERTY

Articles 1(2) and 2

- Andover District Community National Health Service Trust
- Hampshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust
- North Hampshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust
- North Hampshire, Loddon Community National Health Service Trust
- Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers on 20th March 1997 to the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to the Order trust property held by the North and Mid Hampshire Health Authority (article 2).
