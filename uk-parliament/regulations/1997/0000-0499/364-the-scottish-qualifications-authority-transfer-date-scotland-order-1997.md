# The Scottish Qualifications Authority (Transfer Date) (Scotland) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 364 (S.29)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Scottish Qualifications Authority (Transfer Date) (Scotland) Order 1997

Made

10th February 1997

Laid before Parliament

19th February 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Qualifications Authority (Transfer Date) (Scotland) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Transfer date

The date prescribed for the purposes of section 17(1) of the Act as the transfer date shall be 1st April 1997.

Raymond S Robertson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th February 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the date (the transfer date) when the property, rights, liabilities and obligations of the Scottish Examination Board (SEB) and the Scottish Vocational Education Council (SCOTVEC) are transferred to the Scottish Qualifications Authority (SQA) under section 17 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1996. Under section 18 of the Act, staff are also transferred at that date. Under section 19, the transfer date is also the start of the “winding down period” during which SEB and SCOTVEC remain in existence but have only limited functions of facilitating transfer to SQA and exercise by SQA of its functions. SEB and SCOTVEC will be dissolved at a later date to be fixed by order of the Secretary of State.

By virtue of the Education (Scotland) Act 1996 (Commencement No 2) Order 1997[^f00002], 1st April 1997 is also the date when SQA takes up its substantive functions under the Act.
