# The Courts-Martial (Royal Air Force) Rules 1997, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Inquiry into finding

Where the interests of justice require it, the judge advocate may in open court question the court on any finding of fact reached during its deliberation on the finding on any charge.
