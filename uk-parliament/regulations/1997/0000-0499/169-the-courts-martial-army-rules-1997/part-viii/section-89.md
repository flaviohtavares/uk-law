# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 1997, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Circumstances not provided for

In any circumstance not provided for by the Act or these Rules such course shall be adopted as appears best calculated to do justice.
