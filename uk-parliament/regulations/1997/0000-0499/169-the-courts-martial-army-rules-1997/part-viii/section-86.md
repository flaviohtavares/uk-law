# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 1997, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879

(1) The power to make an order conferred by section 7 of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879[^f00008] may be exercised for the purposes of a court-martial—

- (a) during the investigation of any offence and before the accused is notified that he is to be tried by court-martial, by the commanding officer of the accused;
- (b) at preliminary proceedings and during the court-martial, by the judge advocate.

(2) The order shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2 to these Rules.
