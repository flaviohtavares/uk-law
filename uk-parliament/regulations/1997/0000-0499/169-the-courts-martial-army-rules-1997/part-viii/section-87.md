# The Courts-Martial (Army) Rules 1997, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Custody of the record

The period during which the record of the proceedings of a court-martial shall be kept in the custody of the Judge Advocate General shall be six years from the conclusion of the trial.
