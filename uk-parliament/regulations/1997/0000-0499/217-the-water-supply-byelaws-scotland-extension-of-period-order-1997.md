# The Water Supply Byelaws (Scotland) Extension of Period Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 217 (S.17)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Supply Byelaws (Scotland) Extension of Period Order 1997

Made

30th January 1997

Coming into force

15th February 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Water Supply Byelaws (Scotland) Extension of Period Order 1997 and shall come into force on 15th February 1997.

## 2. Extension of byelaws

The period during which the byelaws specified in the Schedule to this Order remain in force is hereby extended to 31st March 2002.

George Calder

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

The Scottish Office Agriculture,

Environment and Fisheries Department Victoria Quay Edinburgh

30th January 1997
