# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.51

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.51. Intimation of registration of, or refusal to register, a Convention Maintenance Decision[^c-23e3ad055c4271ab275c45748fc5093e]

*Extent: S*

(1) Intimation of the registration of a Convention Maintenance Decision in accordance with Article 23 of the Convention shall be given by the sheriff clerk—

- (a) to the payer, by sending an intimation in Form 73D; and
- (b) to the payee, by sending a notice in Form 73E.

(2) Notice of a refusal to register a Convention Maintenance Decision shall be given by the sheriff clerk to the payee, by sending a notice in Form 73F.
