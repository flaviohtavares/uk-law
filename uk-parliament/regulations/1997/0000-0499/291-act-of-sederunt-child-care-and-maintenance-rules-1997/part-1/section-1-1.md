# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 1.1

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.1. Citation and commencement[^key-198471e6972ddf68796f148003ff74ee]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.
