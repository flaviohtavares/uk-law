# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.21

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.21. Representation[^c-31ce43511492d95501fa41d9b5263a2b]

*Extent: S*

(1) In any proceedings any party may be represented by an advocate or a solicitor or, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), other representative authorised by the party.

(2) Such other representative must throughout the proceedings satisfy the sheriff that he is a suitable person to represent the party and that he is authorised to do so.

(3) Such other representative may in representing a party do all such things for the preparation and conduct of the proceedings as may be done by an individual on his own behalf.
