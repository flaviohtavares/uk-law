# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.58B

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.58B. Hearing on application[^c-184101d6d3653005695ebf52ff564005]

*Extent: S*

(1) After lodging the application in terms of rule 3.58A, the sheriff clerk shall assign a date for hearing the application and shall issue a warrant to cite in Form 64B, which shall require any party to lodge answers if so advised within such time as the sheriff shall appoint.

(2) Subject to the provisions of rule 3.3 (power to dispense with service on child), after the issue of the warrant to cite, the applicant shall forthwith give notice of the application by serving a copy and the warrant on the persons referred to in rule 3.58A.

(3) At the hearing the sheriff may determine the application or allow such further procedure as the sheriff thinks fit.

(4) The provisions of rule 3.51 shall apply to any order made under this Part.
