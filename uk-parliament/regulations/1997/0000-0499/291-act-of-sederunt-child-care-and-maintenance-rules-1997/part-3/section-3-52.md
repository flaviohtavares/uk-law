# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.52

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.52. Signature of warrants[^key-b9b53d0377d86fd07705cc9a171652db]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3) a warrant granted under the 2011 Act may be signed by the sheriff or the sheriff clerk.

(2) A warrant signed by the sheriff clerk shall be treated for all purposes as if it had been signed by the sheriff.

(3) A warrant to secure attendance must be signed by the sheriff.
