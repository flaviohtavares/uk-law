# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.39

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.39. Power to make child protection order in an application for an exclusion order[^key-265e4e5141aee5f1975d87136a881662]

*Extent: S*

Where the sheriff, in terms of section 76(8) of the 1995 Act, has decided to make a child protection order under Part 5 of the 2011 Act pursuant to an application, rules 3.31 to 3.33 shall apply.
