# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.7

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.7. Appointment of safeguarder[^c-c2681a0851b102ebd285011af45830cb]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a safeguarder has not been appointed for the child, the sheriff[^c-1e36ec23bb7bc6f19da5d16d44880aef]—

- (a) shall, as soon as reasonably practicable after the lodging of an application or the commencing of any proceedings, consider whether it is necessary to appoint a safeguarder in the application or proceedings; and
- (b) may at that stage, or at any later stage of the application or proceedings, appoint a safeguarder.

(2)[^c-eeafedacb788528b7459ff036cb61024] Where a sheriff appoints a safeguarder, the appointment and the reasons for it must be recorded in an interlocutor.
