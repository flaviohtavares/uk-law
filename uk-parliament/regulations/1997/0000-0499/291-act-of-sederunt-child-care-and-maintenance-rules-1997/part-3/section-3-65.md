# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.65

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.65. Interpretation[^key-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a]

*Extent: S*

In this Part–

- “the Act of 2004” means the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004;
- [^key-c4d243ff74c88a7fbad81c97535be89e]...
- “child witness notice” has the meaning given in section 12(2) of the Act of 2004;
- “review application” means an application for review of arrangements for vulnerable witnesses pursuant to section 13 of the Act of 2004.
- “vulnerable witness application” has the meaning given in section 12(6)(a) of the Act of 2004”.[^key-f8ac81d04c7f6d44a92aa9d7f51d0437]
