# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.5A

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.5A. Confidentiality[^c-2f23fbdfb8f66d80baade536f7c33a33]

*Extent: S*

(1) Unless the sheriff otherwise directs, all documents lodged in process are to be available only to the sheriff, the reporter, the safeguarder, the curator *ad litem* and the parties; and such documents must be treated as confidential by all persons involved in, or party to, the proceedings and by the sheriff clerk.

(2) The safeguarder and the curator *ad litem* must—

- (a) treat all information obtained in the exercise of their duties as confidential; and
- (b) not disclose any such information to any person unless disclosure of such information is necessary for the purpose of their duties.

(3) This rule is subject to rule 3.5.
