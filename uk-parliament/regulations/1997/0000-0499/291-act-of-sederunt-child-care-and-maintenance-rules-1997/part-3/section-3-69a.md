## 3.69A. Vulnerable Witness Application[^c-59b8827573107411d7b34587fa3788ea][^c-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a]

*Extent: S*

A vulnerable witness application made in accordance with section 12(6)(a) of the Act of 2004 shall be in Form 76A.
