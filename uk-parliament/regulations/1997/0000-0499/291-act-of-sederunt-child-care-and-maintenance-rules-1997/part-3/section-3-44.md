## 3.44. Interpretation[^key-1b56fe49c407bd752ef8d27fc1b1bc2a]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, “**application**” means an application under section 93(2)(a) or 94(2)(a) of the[^key-a0916c6c1198ec8f4c923919dc33deb6] 2011 Act.
