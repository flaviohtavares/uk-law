# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.2

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.2. Application of rules 3.3 to 3.5A[^c-6d30072a82b3b2e4194119033d893410]

*Extent: S*

(1) Rules 3.3 to 3.5 apply where a sheriff is coming to a decision about a matter relating to a child within the meaning of section 27 of the 2011 Act.

(2) Rule 3.5A applies in the circumstances referred to in paragraph (1) and in respect of applications under Part V of this Chapter.

(3)[^c-65109f2e3a5803b39654544096ffbf7b] Rules 3.3, 3.3A, 3.5 and 3.5A apply in respect of appeals to the sheriff under section 44A of the CPSA 1995.
