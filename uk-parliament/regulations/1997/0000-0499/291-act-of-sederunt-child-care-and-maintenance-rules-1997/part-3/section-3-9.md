# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.9

> Text as at 2021-09-30.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.9. Representation of safeguarder[^c-9bb49b3060fc98c7368421e33a5ebbb8][^c-dd6f3cc7717254fb1cc2dc29fac13771]

*Extent: S*

(1) A safeguarder may appear personally in the proceedings or instruct an advocate or solicitor to appear on his behalf.

(2) Where an advocate or a solicitor is appointed to act as a safeguarder, he shall not act also as advocate or solicitor for the child in the proceedings.
