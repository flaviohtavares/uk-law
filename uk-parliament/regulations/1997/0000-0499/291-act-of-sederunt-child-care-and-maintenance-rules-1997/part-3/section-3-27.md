# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.27

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.27. Orders[^c-d53cd0a06aa4fb5e91304f42ab517093]

*Extent: S*

(1) After hearing parties and allowing such further procedure as he thinks fit, the sheriff shall make an order granting or refusing the application.

(2) Where an order is made granting the application, that order shall be made in Form 46 and shall contain the information specified therein.

(3) Where the sheriff, in terms of section 36(3) of the[^c-8824f80384dc6a26a99504b01bf05dbb] 2011 Act, has decided to make a child protection order pursuant to an application, rules 3.31 to 3.33 shall apply.
