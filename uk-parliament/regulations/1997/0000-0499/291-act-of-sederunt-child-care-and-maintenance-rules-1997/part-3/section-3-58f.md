> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.58F. Adjournment or continuation of appeals[^key-2b6e59d348895334c299bd0669dfa968]

*Extent: S*

(1) The sheriff may adjourn or continue the hearing of the appeal on the motion of any party or on the sheriff’s own motion.

(2) Where the sheriff adjourns or continues a hearing, the sheriff may make any order that is necessary to secure the expeditious determination of the appeal.
