# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.71

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

**3.71.**[^c-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a] (1) Where a review application is in Form 77, the applicant shall intimate a copy of the review application to all other parties to the proceedings and to any safeguarder [^c-306359b59ffab1154a3b97e34e14de58]... and complete a certificate of intimation.

(2) A certificate of intimation referred to in this rule shall be in Form 78 and shall be lodged together with the review application.
