# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.60

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.60. Lodging reports and information in appeals[^key-c61ef21d63f500c10dad57dcf8ae0328][^key-3c515e00f317eff46900e69f11b6d486]

*Extent: S*

Where, in an appeal–

- (a) it appears to the sheriff that any report or information[^key-0611986e9b91590c4b28e38e5f8ddb4c] lodged under section 155(2) of the[^key-437913b0d6ea3044a64e3e1800f3c4a8] 2011 Act is relevant to any issue which is likely to arise in the stated case; and
- (b) the report or information has been returned to the Principal Reporter,

the sheriff may require the Principal Reporter to lodge the report or information with the sheriff clerk.
