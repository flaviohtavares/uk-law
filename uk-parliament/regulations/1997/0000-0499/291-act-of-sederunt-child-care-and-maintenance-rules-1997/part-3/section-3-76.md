# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.76

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.76. Custody of audio or audio-visual recordings and documents[^key-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a][^key-91cefc04d1ffd0132c230d9853b23f4a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The audio or audio-visual recording[^key-836dc794e2a45275e92ce8b125db5738] and documents referred to in rule 3.75 shall, subject to paragraph (2), be kept in the custody of the sheriff clerk.

(2) Where the audio or audio-visual recording[^key-253d85847b4086464d795f8006dce986] of the evidence of a witness is in the custody of the sheriff clerk under this rule and where intimation has been given to that effect under rule 3.75(2), the name and address of that witness and the record of his evidence shall be treated as being in the knowledge of the parties; and no party shall be required, notwithstanding any enactment to the contrary–

- (a) to include the name of that witness in any list of witnesses; or
- (b) to include the record of his evidence in any list of productions.
