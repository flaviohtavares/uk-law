# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.75

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.75. Lodging audio and audio-visual recordings and documents[^key-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a][^key-f6e1e5d9ea3d8b69172e667b753e1485]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where evidence is taken on commission pursuant to an order made under section 12(1) ,12(6)[^key-d4d80268c5b11cd2023df9d39fa19299] or 13(2) of the Act of 2004 the commissioner shall lodge any audio or audio-visual recording[^key-b0b7025903541f25b5b1b6fec0cb0f7f] of the commission and relevant documents with the sheriff clerk.

(2) On any audio or audio-visual recording[^key-45dea28108a42adef71aa427397b819b] and any documents being lodged the sheriff clerk shall–

- (a) note–
  - (i) the documents lodged;
  - (ii) by whom they were lodged; and
  - (iii) the date on which they were lodged, and
- (b) intimate what he has noted to all parties concerned.
