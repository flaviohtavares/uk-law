# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.74

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.74. Intimation of an order under section 12(1) or 13(2)[^c-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a]

*Extent: S*

An order under section 12(1) ,12(6)[^c-9754bceafca947c138f75403f6dd8b1b] or 13(2) of the Act of 2004 shall be intimated by the sheriff clerk–

- (a) on the day the order is made; and
- (b) in such manner as may be prescribed by the sheriff,

to all parties to the proceedings and such other persons as are named in the order where such parties or persons are not present at the time the order is made.
