# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.48

> Text as at 2021-09-30.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.48. Amendment of the statement of grounds[^key-af8f58cf7835bdc13120ede87f47a61d][^key-d464a70deeb9b6edac3bb62b07b252eb]

*Extent: S*

The sheriff may at any time, on the application of any party or of his own motion, allow amendment of any statement of grounds[^key-4031a8335a7d32fad6a4b37791c162e8].
