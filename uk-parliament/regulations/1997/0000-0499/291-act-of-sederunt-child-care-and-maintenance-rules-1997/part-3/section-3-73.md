# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.73

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.73. Determination of special measures[^key-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a]

*Extent: S*

When making an order under section 12(1) ,12(6)[^key-fb7f84e9361522932586d16c2bf05b30] or 13(2) of the Act of 2004 a sheriff may, in light thereof, make such further orders as he deems appropriate in all the circumstances.
