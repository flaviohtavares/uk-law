# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.12

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.12. Service and notice to persons named in application[^c-d6c99bb60d4945a3535da11d2db1b68d]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of rule 3.4 (service on child), after the issue of the first order or warrant to cite, as the case may be, the applicant shall forthwith give notice of the application by serving a copy of the application and the first order or warrant to cite together with a notice or citation, as the case may be, on the persons named in the application or, as the case may be, a person who should receive notice of the application (subject to paragraph (2)) in–

- (a) Form 34 in respect of an application for a child assessment order under Part III of this Chapter;
- (b) Form 35 in respect of an application to vary or terminate[^c-f696424e91ad3ccab36f21e37fcc84a5] a child protection order in terms of rule 3.33;
- (c) Form 36 in respect of an application for an exclusion order in terms of rules 3.34 to 3.39;
- (d) Form 37 in respect of an application to vary or recall an exclusion order in terms of rule 3.40;
- (e)[^c-5b34e41d330297c8e174f732136527e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-e9f3d175e36b947b24e0706590c9bcb0] subject to subparagraph (g), in Form 39 in respect of an application under section 93(2)(a) or 94(2)(a) of the 2011 Act made under Part VII of this Chapter; or
- (g) in Form 39A where a procedural hearing has been fixed in respect of an application under section 94(2)(a) of the 2011 Act made under Part VII of this Chapter.

(2) Notice of the application shall be given in the case of a safeguarder or curator[^c-11210080f65ede679d71711c2e5de679] *ad litem* by serving a copy of the application and the first order or warrant to cite together with notice in Form 40.
