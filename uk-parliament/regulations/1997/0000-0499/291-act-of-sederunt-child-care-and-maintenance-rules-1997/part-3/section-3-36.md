# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.36

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.36. Hearing following interim order[^key-6fe55550a3a14d7968972a527c5c85ed]

*Extent: S*

Where an interim order is granted under subsection (4) of section 76 of the[^key-1327a68dc672b0514228aae5bb1e1229] 1995 Act, the hearing under subsection (5) of that section shall take place not later than 3 working days after the granting of the interim order.
