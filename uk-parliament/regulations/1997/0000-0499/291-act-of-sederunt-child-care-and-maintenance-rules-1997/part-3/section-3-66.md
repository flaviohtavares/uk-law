# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.66

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.66. Extent of application of this Part[^key-81be76e554a5ea29d5dd95bcaaf77647][^key-f3a34936d71d830ba99d7ba2732e222a]

*Extent: S*

This Part of Chapter 3 shall apply to proceedings where—

- (a) an application is made to the sheriff under section 93(2)(a), 94(2)(a) or 110 of the 2011 Act;
- (b) an appeal is made to the sheriff under Part 15 of the 2011 Act; or
- (c) an appeal is made under section 44A of the CPSA 1995.
