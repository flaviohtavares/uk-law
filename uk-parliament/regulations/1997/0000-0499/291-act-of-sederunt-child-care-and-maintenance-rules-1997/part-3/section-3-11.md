# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.11

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.11. Assigning of diet for hearing[^key-b37a2e9caf417b0aeb72942835ab6864]

*Extent: S*

Except where otherwise provided in these Rules, after the lodging of any application the sheriff clerk shall forthwith assign a diet for the hearing of the application and shall issue a first order or a warrant to cite in Form 32 , Form 32A[^key-397171e71a440af986e523b9a1ee215d] or Form 33, as the case may be.
