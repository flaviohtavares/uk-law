# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 3.6

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.6. Application[^key-7c835001d17517dd4f742501901fec8b]

*Extent: S*

Rules 3.7 to 3.9[^key-01a64c2e5a04c59842e266a9b9155f6a] apply, as regards a safeguarder, to all applications and proceedings to which this Chapter applies except for an application under section 37 of the[^key-1f4f004aede770b1012d3b8cc0e1409f] 2011 Act for a child protection order.
