# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 2.48

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.48. Orders for evidence[^key-db309ec78891d8cad21964f55de5220e]

*Extent: S*

(1) The sheriff may, before determining the cause, order—

- (a) production of further documents (including affidavits); or
- (b) parole evidence.

(2) A party may apply by motion for the evidence of a person to be received in evidence by affidavit; and the sheriff may make such order as the sheriff thinks fit.
