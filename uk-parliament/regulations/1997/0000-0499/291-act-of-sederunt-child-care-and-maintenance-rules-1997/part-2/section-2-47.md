# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 2.47

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.47. Confidentiality[^key-db309ec78891d8cad21964f55de5220e]

*Extent: S*

(1) Unless the sheriff otherwise directs, all documents lodged in process (including the reports by the curator *ad litem* and reporting officer) are to be available only to the sheriff, the curator *ad litem*, the reporting officer and the parties; and such documents must be treated as confidential by all persons involved in, or party to, the proceedings and by the sheriff clerk.

(2) The reporting officer and the curator *ad litem—*

- (a) must treat all information obtained in the exercise of their duties as confidential; and
- (b) must not disclose any such information to any person unless disclosure of such information is necessary for the purpose of their duties.

(3) This rule is subject to rule 2.53.
