# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 2.60

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.60. Appeals[^c-0f790a70f40abeebedc9d40cd4a40895][^c-db309ec78891d8cad21964f55de5220e]

*Extent: S*

An appeal to the Sheriff Appeal Court against an order of the sheriff under this Part is to be made in accordance with Chapter 6 of the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015.
