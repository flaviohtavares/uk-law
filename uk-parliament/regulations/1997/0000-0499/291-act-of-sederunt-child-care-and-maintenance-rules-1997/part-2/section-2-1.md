# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 2.1

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.1. Interpretation[^c-acd51e378002c017fb71775ee0c4ccbc]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter, unless the context otherwise requires–

- “the Act” means the Adoption (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00011];
- “the 1995 Act” means the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00012];
- [^c-ca75fa75c1855f6137c36b39e217a662]...
- [^c-ca75fa75c1855f6137c36b39e217a662]...
- “Her Majesty’s Forces” means the regular forces as defined in section 374 of the Armed Forces Act 2006; and[^c-6c6555b22f18c3157540fdbc5aa4653b]
- “Registrar General” means the Registrar General of Births, Deaths and Marriages for Scotland.
