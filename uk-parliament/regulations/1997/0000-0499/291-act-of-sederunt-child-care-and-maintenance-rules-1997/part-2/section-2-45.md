# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 2.45

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.45. Interpretation[^key-db309ec78891d8cad21964f55de5220e]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “2007 Act” means the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007;
- “2008 Act” means the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008;
- “parental order” means an order under section 54 or 54A(5)[^key-1647a0c902d5f5244e9f6527dcbd999b] of the 2008 Act; and
- “the Regulations” means the Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Parental Orders) Regulations 2010.
