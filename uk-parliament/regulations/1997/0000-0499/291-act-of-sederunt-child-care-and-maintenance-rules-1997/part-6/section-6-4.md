# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 6.4

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.4. Notice to Secretary of State under section 108(5) of the Act[^c-b0ff299ab66beecc07c05248ee630ef0]

*Extent: S*

The notice required to be given to the Secretary of State by the sheriff clerk under section 108(5) of the Act (notice of application to vary etc. a maintenance order), as read with regulation 3 of the Income Support (Liable Relatives) Regulations 1990[^f00053]), shall–

- (a) be in writing;
- (b) specify any date assigned for the hearing of the application;
- (c) be accompanied by a copy of the application; and
- (d) be sent by recorded delivery post.
