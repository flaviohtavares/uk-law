# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.8

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.8. Transmission[^c-470f8c218fa2a5fb1d7338d405a6d676]

*Extent: S*

On the grant of an application under rule 5.7, the sheriff clerk shall send to the clerk of the magistrates' court–

- (a) the affidavit of the applicant;
- (b) the certified copy of the order; and
- (c) a letter requesting registration of the order.
