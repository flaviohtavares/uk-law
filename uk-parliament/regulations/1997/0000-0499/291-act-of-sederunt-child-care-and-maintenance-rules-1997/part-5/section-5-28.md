# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.28

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.28. Cancellation of registration[^c-1e15edeed395be42471d48d569f3d65c]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application under section 24(1) of the 1950 Act for the cancellation of the registration of a maintenance order made by a court in England or Northern Ireland and registered in a sheriff court shall be made by lodging with the sheriff clerk–

- (a) an application for that purpose which shall state the date of the registration of the order; and
- (b) a copy of the order the registration of which is sought to be cancelled.

(2) Where under section 24(1) or (2) of the 1950 Act the sheriff clerk cancels the registration of a maintenance order, he shall–

- (a) notify the clerk of the court by which the order was made; and
- (b) notify the person liable to make payments under the order.
