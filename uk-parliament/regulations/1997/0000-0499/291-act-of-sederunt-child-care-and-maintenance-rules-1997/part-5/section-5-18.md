# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.18

> Text as at 2021-09-30.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.18. Provisional order made with a view to transmission to a reciprocating country[^c-c9d23e073582c4ec9f0f3b1579ededcb]

*Extent: S*

A certificate signed by the sheriff clerk in terms of section 3(5)(c) of the 1972 Act (certificate of grounds), as read with section 4(6) thereof, shall also be signed by the sheriff.
