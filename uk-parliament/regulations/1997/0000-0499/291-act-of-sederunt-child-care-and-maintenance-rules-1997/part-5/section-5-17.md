# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.17

> Text as at 2021-09-30.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.17. Service on defender in Hague Convention Country[^key-2d04fdbaff020d44e73e9fd65936e4ee]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of section 4(4) of the 1972 Act as applied in respect of a defender in a Hague Convention Country[^f00044], service on such a defender shall be effected in accordance with the Ordinary Cause Rules.
