# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.42

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.42. Appeal by applicant[^c-d599af637975df2fc7ede02bad5a2588]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the application for enforcement of a maintenance order is refused, the applicant may appeal by way of summary application to the sheriff within one month from the date of service of the notice under rule 5.40.

(2) The determination of the sheriff of such a summary application shall be subject to a final appeal on a point of law to the Inner House of the Court of Session in accordance with the Ordinary Cause Rules.
