# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.37

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.37. Application to set aside registration of, or to set aside decision not to register, an order made in a Hague Convention Country[^key-7eccbdffd1a85bc64b7cb7854c9af544]

*Extent: S*

Application to the court under section 6(9) or (12) of the 1972 Act as applied to an order made in a Hague Convention Country[^f00050] shall be made by summary application setting out the grounds of the application.

## Annotations

[^key-7eccbdffd1a85bc64b7cb7854c9af544]: Commencement: Rule 5.37 in force at 1.4.1997, see [rule 1.1(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/291/rule/1.1/1)

[^f00050]: Footnote: Sections 6(9) and 6(12) provide a time limit of one month from service of notice of registration or decision not to register.
