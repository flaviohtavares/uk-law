# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.43

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.43. Enforcement of registered order[^c-cd12e9d4533a8ecf1e9f1361a0fedaec]

*Extent: S*

The applicant may obtain an extract of a registered order and proceed to arrest in execution, to intimate the order (for the purposes of section 54(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00051]), to inhibit and to charge and attach[^c-609997dac131fde783b792d173fb1112] thereon, but may not proceed to an action of furthcoming in respect of an arrestment, serve a current maintenance arrestment schedule, make application for a conjoined arrestment order, proceed to adjudication in respect of inhibition or auction in respect of an attachment[^c-67b7d1bc3b0621c5b6f929f246cbbfc3] until the time for appeal against the determination of the sheriff under rules 5.41 or 5.42 has elapsed and any appeal has been disposed of.
