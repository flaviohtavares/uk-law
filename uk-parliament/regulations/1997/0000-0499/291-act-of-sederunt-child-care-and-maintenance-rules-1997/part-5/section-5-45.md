> Text as at 2021-09-30.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.45. Enforcement of a Maintenance Decision made by a court in a Maintenance Regulation State other than Denmark[^c-3318a4f770642e0ec94a74ba2d7015a0]

*Extent: S*

The “enforcing court” under paragraph 4(2) of Schedule 1 to the 2011 Regulations, means the sheriff court having jurisdiction in the matter in accordance with Schedule 8 to the 1982 Act.
