# Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997, section 5.14

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.14. Application for transmission of order for enforcement in a reciprocating country[^c-01b16cf1fb0de2e3d000a3f967c4219f]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for the transmission of a maintenance order for enforcement in a reciprocating country shall be made by letter addressed to the sheriff clerk.

(2) There shall be lodged with any such application–

- (a) a certified copy of the relevant order;
- (b) a statement signed by the applicant or his solicitor of any arrears outstanding in respect of the order;
- (c) a statement signed by the applicant or his solicitor giving such information as to the whereabouts of the payer as he possesses;
- (d) a statement signed by the applicant or his solicitor giving such information as the applicant possesses for facilitating the identification of the payer;
- (e) where available, a photograph of the payer.
