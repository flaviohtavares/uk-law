# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Expenditure not required to be charged to a revenue account[^key-8deb63ec1f314f258feddee21bf7c3b4]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Expenditure to which regulation 10 applies shall be expenditure falling within section 42(2).

(2) The following expenditure shall be expenditure falling within section 42(2), namely expenditure incurred by a local authority which—

- (a) is met by the application under regulation 155, 156 or 157 of an amount for the time being set aside by the authority (whether voluntarily or pursuant to a requirement under Part IV of the Act) as provision to meet credit liabilities;
- (b) consists of the transfer of such an amount to any of the bodies specified in regulation 158; or
- (c) consists of the application of such an amount to meet any liability of the authority in respect of any levy payable under section 136 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993[^f00006] (levy on disposals of dwelling-houses).
