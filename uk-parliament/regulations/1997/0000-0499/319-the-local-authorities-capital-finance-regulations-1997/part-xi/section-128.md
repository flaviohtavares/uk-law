# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 128

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 128. Amounts set aside—advances to housing associations[^key-b6d642306e986bd7284627319478acbe]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In determining their adjusted credit ceiling at any time, a local authority shall disregard any reduction in their credit ceiling which results from the setting aside under section 59(1) of the reserved part of a sum received which is a capital receipt by virtue of section 58(1) (c) or (d), and is paid in respect of an advance made by the authority on or before 30th September 1989—

- (a) to a housing association (within the meaning given to that expression in section 1 of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00108]); and
- (b) for the purposes of expenditure in respect of which the association in question received a grant under section 50 of the Housing Act 1988[^f00109], section 41(1) of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00110] or section 29(1) of the Housing Act 1974[^f00111].
