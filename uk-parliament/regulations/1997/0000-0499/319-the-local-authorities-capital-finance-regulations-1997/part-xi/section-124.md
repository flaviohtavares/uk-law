# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 124

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Adjusted credit ceiling[^key-90c4eb2262e046c8ff122d25cfa7b105]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

At any time on or after 1st April 1997, a local authority’s adjusted credit ceiling shall be their credit ceiling at that time, as determined under Part III of Schedule 3 to the Act, subject to the modifications prescribed for the purposes of Part IV of that Schedule in the following provisions of this Part.
