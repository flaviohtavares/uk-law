# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 125

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 125. Basis of determination[^key-9fbd6d32d0ca573983b864d083db30db]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The starting point for determining a local authority’s adjusted credit ceiling on 1st April 1997 shall be the amount which was the authority’s adjusted credit ceiling on 31st March 1997.
