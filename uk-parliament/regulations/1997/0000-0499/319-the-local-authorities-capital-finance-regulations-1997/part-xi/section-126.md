# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 126

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Amounts set aside—transferred property and deemed borrowing[^c-84ed043bb6f7c0ac6a8455296d8349e3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In determining their adjusted credit ceiling at any time, a local authority shall disregard any reduction in their credit ceiling which results from the setting aside under section 59(1) of the reserved part of a capital receipt where the sum received is a capital receipt by virtue of regulation 56, 57 or 58, and the reserved part is 100 per cent. by virtue of regulation 68.
