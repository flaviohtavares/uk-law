# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 116

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 116. Credit ceiling on or after 1st April 1997[^c-e2fe5c48adbc487f2b62e7ecb03743a2]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

At any time on or after 1st April 1997, a local authority’s credit ceiling shall be determined in accordance with paragraphs 11 to 14 of Schedule 3 to the Act, subject to the modifications prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 10 of that Schedule in the following provisions of this Part.
