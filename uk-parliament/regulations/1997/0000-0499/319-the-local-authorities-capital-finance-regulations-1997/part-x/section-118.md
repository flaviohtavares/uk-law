# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 118

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Credit ceiling increased by use of amount set aside to pay levy on disposals of housing[^c-0edce8cc5a3292d1bfadbfc224faeaee]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a local authority apply an amount set aside to meet any liability in respect of any levy payable under section 136 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (levy on disposals of dwelling-houses)[^f00099], the authority’s credit ceiling shall, at the same time, be increased by an amount equal to the amount so applied.
