# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 115

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 115. Interpretation[^key-a3bfff6aa51ccd46aea2dee5f67e9abc]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part, “amount set aside” means any amount for the time being set aside by a local authority (whether voluntarily or pursuant to a requirement under Part IV of the Act) as provision to meet credit liabilities.
