# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 28

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Leases under certain private finance transactions[^c-6778040654a401a7f510cddd2ed78be9]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The initial cost of a lease of land shall be nil where the authority become the lessees under the provisions of a private finance transaction, and the initial cost of that transaction falls to be determined under regulation 40.
