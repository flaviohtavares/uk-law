# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 51

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Cost of arrangements at any time after variation[^key-4a9d12ab36df7a433ff9ede1203843ee]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a local authority agree to a variation of a credit arrangement which is excluded from section 49(2), the cost of the arrangement at any time after the variation shall be the amount which would, in accordance with regulation 47, be the adjusted cost of the arrangement if the variation was made at the time in question, on the basis of an estimate made at that time and disregarding any consideration given by the authority in respect of the arrangement before that time.
