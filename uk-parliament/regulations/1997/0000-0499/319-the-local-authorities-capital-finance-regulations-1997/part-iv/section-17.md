# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Private finance transactions[^c-6571d72eebab373a079ad55c63acf5fd]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A private finance transaction which, apart from this regulation, would not be a credit arrangement is prescribed for the purposes of section 48(1)(c).
