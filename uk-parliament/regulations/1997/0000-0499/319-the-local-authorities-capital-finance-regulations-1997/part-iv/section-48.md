# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Leases having an adjusted cost of nil[^c-d5ed882621c64bf79562c22cb89239b5]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where—

- (a) a credit arrangement which is a lease of land (“the arrangement”) is varied by the grant to the authority of a new lease of the same land for a term which extends beyond the expiry date of the arrangement, and
- (b) if the new lease were granted upon the expiry of the arrangement, the initial cost of the new lease would fall to be determined under regulation 29, 30 or 32,

the adjusted cost of the arrangement shall be nil.
