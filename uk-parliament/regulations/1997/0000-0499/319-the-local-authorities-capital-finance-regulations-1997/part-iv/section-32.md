# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 32

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Consecutive leases of land for total term not exceeding ten years[^c-dbf772eee258b24950d771fb782b3821]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The initial cost of a lease (“the new lease”) of land shall be nil where—

- (a) the authority had an earlier leasehold interest (“the preceding interest”) in the land, and become the lessees upon the expiry of the preceding interest;
- (b) the preceding interest was a lease of which the initial cost was nil by virtue of regulation 29 or regulation 7(3) of the 1990 Regulations[^f00031];
- (c) the new lease is for a term which, added to the term of the preceding interest, produces a combined term of not more than ten years; and
- (d) but for the preceding interest, the initial cost of the new lease would be nil by virtue of regulation 31.
