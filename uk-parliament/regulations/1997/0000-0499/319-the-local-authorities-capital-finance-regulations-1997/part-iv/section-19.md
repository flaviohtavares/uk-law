# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 19

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Leases under which the consideration given mostly in first year[^c-63ce1cef0a22384f1bfffcfbc47aae01]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A lease is excluded from section 48 (credit arrangements) if, on the date on which the authority become the lessees, the authority estimate that the value of the consideration which will have been given by them in respect of the lease at the end of a period of one year comencing on that date, is not less than 90 per cent. of the capital cost of the lease.
