# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 23

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Investments made for the purposes of a superannuation fund[^key-16c8c608861b42a9808443da8e4883f2]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A lease or a contract is excluded from section 48 if the authority become lessees under the lease or enter into the contract as an investment for the purposes of a superannuation fund which the authority are required to keep by virtue of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00021].
