# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 27

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Cost of lease at any time[^key-b2e8d8e08b878d931bae10c929093332]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Subject to regulation 46, where, by virtue of this Part, the initial cost of a lease is nil, the cost of that lease at any time shall also be nil.
