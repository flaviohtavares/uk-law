# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Variation of arrangements excluded from section 49(2)[^c-eaa34ad854351b4621f102672be5402e]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a credit arrangement which is excluded from section 49(2) is varied as mentioned in section 51(1) (variation of credit arrangements), the adjusted cost and the cost of the arrangement at any time after the variation shall be determined in accordance with regulations 47 to 51.
