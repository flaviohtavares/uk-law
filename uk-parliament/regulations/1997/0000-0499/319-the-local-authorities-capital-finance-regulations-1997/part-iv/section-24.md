# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 24

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Other leases excluded from section 48[^c-6ad0ec7c3e857a79c39e2b5be643ad5c]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A lease of any property is excluded from section 48 if it is—

- (a) assigned to the authority by a new town corporation; or
- (b) granted to the authority in accordance with regulations made under section 100 of the Housing Act 1988 (tenants continuing as tenants of public sector landlord)[^f00022].

(2) A lease of any property is excluded from section 48 if a local authority become the lessees by virtue of—

- (a) the Local Government Changes for England (Property Transfer and Transitional Payments) Regulations 1995[^f00023];
- (b) article 9 of the Police and Magistrates' Courts Act 1994 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1994[^f00024];
- (c) a combination scheme under the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00025];
- (d) the Local Government Reorganisation (Wales) (Property etc.) Order 1996[^f00026]; or
- (e) article 15 of the National Park Authorities (England) Order 1996[^f00027], or an agreement under section 76 of the Environment Act 1995 (agreements as to incidental matters)[^f00028] or an award under subsection (3) of that section.
