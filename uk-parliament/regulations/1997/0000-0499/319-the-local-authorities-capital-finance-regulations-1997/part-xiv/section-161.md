# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 161

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 161. Approved investments[^key-d6f13431fba80a4e1b31d48ed4a15ef1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 1(2) of the Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Approved Investments) Regulations 1990[^f00134] shall be amended, in the definition of “relevant lender”, by the substitution of “paragraph 28, 29 or 30” for “paragraph 28 or 29”.
