# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 162

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 162. Revocations[^key-a03c073e8d3afbb8e86abd9cd1f87450][^key-388526e1bfc29961aab88027fbf0b175]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Subject to the savings mentioned in regulation 163—

- (a) regulation 15 of the Local Authorities (Capital Finance and Approved Investments) (Amendment) Regulations 1996[^f00135] is revoked; and
- (b) the subordinate legislation specified in Schedule 3 to these Regulations shall be revoked on 1st April 1997 to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
