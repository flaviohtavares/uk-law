# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 105

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 105. Interpretation[^c-a80300a6427d332d7fb83c659a1f9de1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part—

- “the authority”, in relation to any non-monetary consideration, means the local authority by whom the consideration is received;
- “non-monetary consideration” means any consideration such as is mentioned in section 61(1) (capital receipts not wholly in money paid to the authority); and
- “notional capital receipt”, in relation to any non-monetary consideration, means the amount of the notional capital receipt determined in accordance with section 61(2).
