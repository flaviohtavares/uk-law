# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 91

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 91. Disposal of shops and offices etc.[^key-5054f39141a809158671b7bcad3777af]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of regulation 86, a disposal of an interest in land shall be a relevant disposal if the disposal meets the condition specified in regulation 84, and—

- (a) there are shops or offices situated on the land; and
- (b) immediately before the disposal, each shop and office is in use by a person other than the authority, or is available for use by any person other than the authority.
