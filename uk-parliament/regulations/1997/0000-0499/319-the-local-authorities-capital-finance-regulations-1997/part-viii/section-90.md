# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 90

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 90. Disposal of leisure facilities[^key-0d6dd7558769d4a4288ca9e4bb8d2260]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of regulation 86, a disposal of an interest in land shall be a relevant disposal if the disposal meets the condition specified in regulation 84, and the land is used for the purposes of a leisure centre or other indoor recreation, or a swimming pool, or a park, playing field (other than a school playing field) or other outdoor recreation.
