# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 79

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 79. Meaning of authority[^c-238c519e3620ae729bd3b1daf5ef0c34]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part, “authority”, in relation to a capital receipt or a disposal of an asset, means the local authority by whom the receipt is received or the disposal is made.
