# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 87

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 87. Disposal of unoccupied dwellings[^key-2bd5614375a24b42e1da31be8c3d63e8]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this regulation—

- (a) “dwelling” has the extended meaning which it has in regulation 22(1); and
- (b) “relevant consent” means a consent to a disposal of land given by the Secretary of State to local authorities generally under section 32 or 43 of the Housing Act 1985[^f00080].

(2) For the purposes of regulation 86, a disposal of an interest in land shall be a relevant disposal if the disposal meets the condition specified in regulation 84, and—

- (a) there is situated on the land a dwelling which is unoccupied at the time of the disposal, and has normally been let, or available for letting, for the purposes of Part II of the Housing Act 1985 (provision of housing accommodation);
- (b) the disposal is not made under Part V of the Housing Act 1985 (the right to buy), or, with a relevant consent, to a person who, when he acquires that interest, occupies, or intends to occupy, the dwelling as his only or principal home; and
- (c) the disposal is not a qualifying disposal for the purposes of section 135 or 136 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Regeneration Act 1993[^f00081].
