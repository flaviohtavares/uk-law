# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 85

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Meaning of regeneration project[^c-c8ad22cbaae9c2d1b2854af3fb39b491]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of this Part, “regeneration project” means any project for the carrying out of works or activities on any land where—

- (a) the land, or a building on the land, is vacant or unused or under-used or ineffectively used or contaminated or derelict; and
- (b) the works or activities are carried out in order to secure that the land or the building will be brought into effective use.
