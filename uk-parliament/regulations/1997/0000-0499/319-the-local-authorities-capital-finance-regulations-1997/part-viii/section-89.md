# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 89

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Disposal of docks and harbours etc.[^c-3285653ba98914537146f4024e182366]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of regulation 86, a disposal of an interest in land shall be a relevant disposal if the disposal meets the condition specified in regulation 84, and the land is used for the purposes of a dock, quay, harbour, wharf, basin or pier, or for the purposes of an office or warehouse in connection with the operation of a port, marina or inland waterway.
