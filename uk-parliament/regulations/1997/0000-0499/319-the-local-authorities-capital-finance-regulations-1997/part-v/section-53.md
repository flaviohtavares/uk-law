# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 53

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Specified capital grants—all local authorities[^c-87b02e37f7861a5b873cc7e24ab146fc]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Contributions paid to a local authority under section 569(1) of the Housing Act 1985 (contributions towards expense of assisting owners of defective housing)[^f00045] are specified for the purposes of section 57, except to the extent that they are paid in annual sums payable for a period of ten years or more.
