# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Expenditure under private finance transactions[^c-16182909649269cf9441a136d898f6d2]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this regulation, “private finance transaction” has the same meaning as in Part IV of these Regulations.

(2) Expenditure incurred by a local authority under a private finance transaction, in so far as it is not expenditure for capital purposes by virtue of section 40(2), shall be expenditure for capital purposes.
