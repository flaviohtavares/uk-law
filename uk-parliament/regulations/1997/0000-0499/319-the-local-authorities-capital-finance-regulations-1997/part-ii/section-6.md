# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Housing disposal costs[^key-5d5cbbba5f752d2d9f4905f122bb7fcf]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where an amount in respect of interest is included in any instalment of a disposal cost payable by a local authority in accordance with regulation 3 of the Housing (Change of Landlord) (Payment of Disposal Cost by Instalments) Regulations 1990[^f00003], the expenditure incurred by the authority on paying that interest shall be expenditure for capital purposes.
