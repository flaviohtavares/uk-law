# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Issue of loan instruments[^key-67ff4f85ea8b6ccc06c838a0743c7cd9]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Expenditure of a local authority which is incidental to the issue of a loan instrument shall be expenditure for capital purposes unless all of the payments or repayments required to be made by the authority in respect of the loan instrument are required to be made not later than one year after the loan instrument is issued.
