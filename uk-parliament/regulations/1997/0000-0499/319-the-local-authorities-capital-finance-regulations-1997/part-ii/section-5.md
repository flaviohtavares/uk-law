# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Repayment of grants by local authority[^key-742bededb1377efa95b98a628953dcb7]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a local authority are required to repay or make a payment in respect of any grant or other financial assistance made or given to them by a Minister of the Crown or Community institution towards expenditure for capital purposes, the expenditure incurred by the authority on making that repayment or payment shall be expenditure for capital purposes.
