# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 133

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Percentage of the relevant amount[^c-9970cfdecbb9461280e14d9cc6796e64]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If the authority are not required to keep a Housing Revenue Account for the current year, they shall determine the amount equal to four per cent. of their relevant amount.

(2) In this Part, except for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1997, the relevant amount of any such authority is an amount determined in accordance with provisions of this Part referring to compenents of the authority’s adjusted credit ceiling.
