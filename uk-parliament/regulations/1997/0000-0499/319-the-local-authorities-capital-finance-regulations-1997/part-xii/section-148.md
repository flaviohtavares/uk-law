# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 148

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 148. Determining the non-housing reduction[^c-8514aa37cb3ef4eefb070a78f09b1af9]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In order to determine their non-housing reduction for the last year, the authority shall determine—

- (a) the total amount (“amount A”) by which their adjusted credit ceiling was reduced during the last year under paragraphs 12(1) and 14(1) of Schedule 3 to the Act;
- (b) the total amount (“amount B”) by which their adjusted credit ceiling was reduced during that year by the setting aside of any amounts determined under regulations 134 and 136; and
- (c) the amount (“amount C”) by which amount A exceeds amount B.

(2) For the purposes of regulation 147, the authority’s non-housing reduction in the last year is the amount, if any, by which amount C exceeds the housing reduction determined in accordance with regulation 144.
