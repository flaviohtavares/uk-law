# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 151

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 151. Relevant amount for financial year beginning 1st April 1997[^c-f0e7130849dd399f3291cf6c027e3cc1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The authority’s relevant amount for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1997 shall be the amount determined to be the relevant amount for that year under paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 to the 1990 Regulations.
