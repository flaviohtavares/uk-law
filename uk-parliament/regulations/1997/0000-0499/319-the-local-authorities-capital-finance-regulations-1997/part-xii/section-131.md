# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 131

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 131. Minimum revenue provision for financial years beginning after 31st March 1997[^c-a6562012472369c47b7a97b2b3cdb8d7]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Subject to regulation 138, for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1997 and every subsequent financial year of a local authority, the amount in respect of principal for the purposes of paragraph 15(1) of Schedule 3 to the Act shall be the aggregate of all amounts which the authority is required to determine under regulations 132 to 137.
