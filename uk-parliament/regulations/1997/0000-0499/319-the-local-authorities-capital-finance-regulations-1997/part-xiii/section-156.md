# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 156

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 156. Use of amounts set aside—expenditure for capital purposes[^c-bdba295aa21b760525da83f376b8b51c]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this regulation, “excluded expenditure” means expenditure for capital purposes on the making of a grant, an advance or other financial assistance which—

- (a) is to a housing association (within the meaning given to that expression in section 1 of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00118]); and
- (b) is towards expenditure for which the housing association receive or have received a grant under section 18 of the Housing Act 1996[^f00119], section 50 of the Housing Act 1988[^f00120], section 41(1) of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^f00121] or section 29(1) of the Housing Act 1974[^f00122].

(2) An amount set aside, other than an amount set aside in respect of a capital receipt to which regulation 71 applies, may be applied by a relevant authority under section 64(2)(a) to meet any expenditure for capital purposes other than excluded expenditure.
