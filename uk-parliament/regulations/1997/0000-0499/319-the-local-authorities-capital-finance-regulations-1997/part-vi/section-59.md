# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 59

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59. Payment made to redeem landlord’s share[^key-c4ec1afcc8fb7f31f6a57db82c334908]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A sum received by a local authority in respect of an interim or final payment made in accordance with Schedule 6A to the Housing Act 1985 (redemption of landlord’s share)[^f00051], if it is not a capital receipt by virtue of section 58(1)(a) (capital receipts), shall be a capital receipt.
