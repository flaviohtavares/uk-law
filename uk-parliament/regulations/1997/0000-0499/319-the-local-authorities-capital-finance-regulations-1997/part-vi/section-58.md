# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 58

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Repayment of money deemed to have been borrowed[^key-ef8070afe6ccaa405d7f3f17e7037d54]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A sum received by a local authority in respect of a repayment of principal in relation to money which, by virtue of an order made under section 66(1) or 67(3) of the Local Government Act 1985 (discharge of residuary functions)[^f00050], is deemed to have been borrowed from them by another local authority shall be a capital receipt.
