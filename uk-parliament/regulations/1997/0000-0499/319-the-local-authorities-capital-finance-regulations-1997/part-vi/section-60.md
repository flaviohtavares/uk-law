# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 60

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Disposal of premises transferred to grant-maintained school[^c-30f700dde530527dd3f49a2ed6c42c80]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this regulation, “grant-maintained school” has the same meaning as in section 183 of the Education Act 1996[^f00052].

(2) Where the governing body incorporated for the purpose of conducting a grant-maintained school are required by the Secretary of State under section 298(2)(b) of the Education Act 1996 to pay a sum to a local authority, the sum received by the authority shall be a capital receipt.
