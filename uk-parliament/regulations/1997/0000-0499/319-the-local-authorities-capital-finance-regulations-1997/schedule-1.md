# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, SCHEDULE 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1 — USE OF LAND FOR A SPECIFIED PURPOSE

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulations 41, 42 and 99

In relation to a local authority’s use of land, a purpose is specified in a category of this Schedule if it falls within any of the following categories, or if a dwelling is situated on the land and the purpose is use as a dwelling by a person employed for a purpose falling within any of the following categories except category 10—

**1.**[^c-08646900d29d69a4b3c90aa66d9c9c87] the fire service or police service, the magistrates' courts services, the probation service, or the functions conferred on the authority under section 2 of the Civil Defence Act 1948[^f00136]

**2.**[^c-1b2bfcd1ce29ca38b09a6f51b09626b0] a hostel for the homeless, or the social services functions of a local authority within the meaning given to that expression in the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970[^f00137];

**3.**[^c-a883f85901dbdb195eb254ad562cff8a] a school, or an institution providing further education, or training, or education for adults;

**4.**[^c-7ffaebb0c2d6c59a2d63fdc167a68c37] an archive, a library, a museum or an art gallery;

**5.**[^c-beb4da870684f9c631e4242e74b8f787] a community hall, a conference centre, a theatre, a concert hall, a leisure centre or other indoor recreation, a swimming pool, or a pier;

**6.**[^c-283585a1774e704138588c58505e7f00] a bus station, a vehicle depot, a garage, or a public park for cars, caravans, lorries or other vehicles;

**7.**[^c-cd9d2f79a37aaed2d820c525bdddffe8] an allotment, a smallholding, a cemetery, a playing field (other than a school playing field), a golf course, or a park or other public open space;

**8.**[^c-b281f713c1127e9507c73dcb87ae8c14] an industrial unit, a dog compound, a market, a public convenience, or the disposal of waste, or (except so far as falling within any other category) a workshop, a kitchen, a warehouse, or the storage of goods or equipment;

**9.**[^c-d0fb43c7d192603ef71dcb660192932f] a town hall, or local authority offices (other than offices which fall within any other category); or

**10.**[^c-87752639179ffabc8ef8f9adb5f18c40] a house or other dwelling provided by a local authority for a person in their employment who is entitled to occupy it as as a term of his employment (other than a dwelling falling within any of the preceding categories).
