# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 73

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Crematoria[^key-a27e46170d568cf4f9047c3a56f8f47f]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The reserved part shall be 10 per cent. in the case of a capital receipt, other than a nil per cent. receipt, which is received during the period beginning on 1st April 1997 and ending on 30th June 1997 in respect of a disposal of a freehold or leasehold interest in land comprising a crematorium within the meaning given to that expression in section 2 of the Cremation Act 1902[^f00071].
