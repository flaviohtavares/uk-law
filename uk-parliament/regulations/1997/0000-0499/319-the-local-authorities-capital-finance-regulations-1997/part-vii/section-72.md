# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 72

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Hostels and lodging houses[^c-412bb3b18d4feea237c2c0da828e4abd]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In the case of a capital receipt which is received in respect of the disposal of an interest in a hostel (within the meaning given to that expression in section 622 of the Housing Act 1985) or a lodging-house (within the meaning given to that expression in section 56 of that Act), the reserved part shall be 75 per cent. if—

- (a) the hostel or lodging-house is held for the purposes of Part II of that Act (provision of housing accommodation);
- (b) the receipt is not a nil per cent. receipt; and
- (c) where the authority is a local authority in Wales, the reserved part of the receipt does not fall to be determined in accordance with regulation 78.
