# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 76

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Disposal of land held for housing purposes[^key-759e4014e8aa3e9029c5ea7ecf97f48a]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The reserved part shall be nil per cent. in the case of a capital receipt which is received in respect of a disposal of an interest in land where—

- (a) the authority is a local authority in Wales;
- (b) the land is held for the purposes of Part II of the Housing Act 1985 (provision of housing accommodation); and
- (c) if there is a building situated on the land, the building does not, in whole or in part, comprise a dwelling (within the extended meaning given to that expression in regulation 22(1)).
