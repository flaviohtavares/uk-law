# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) Regulations 1997, section 69

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Payments for redemption of landlord’s share[^key-ed3acc801b7ce5442e101aecba62b0e3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The reserved part shall be 75 per cent. in the case of a sum which is a capital receipt by virtue of regulation 59.
