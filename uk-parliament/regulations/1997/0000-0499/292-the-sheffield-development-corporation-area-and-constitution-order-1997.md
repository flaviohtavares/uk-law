# The Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 292

URBAN DEVELOPMENT

The Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1997

Approved by Parliament

Made

6th January 1997

Laid before Parliament

14th January 1997

Coming into force

1st April 1997

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 134 and 135 of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 26 to, the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Urban development area

(1) Articles 2 and 3 of the Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

(2) So much of the Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988 as has not previously been revoked shall be revoked with effect from 1st July 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

David Curry

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

6th January 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Sheffield Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988 which designated the Sheffield urban development area and established the Sheffield Development Corporation. Articles 2 and 3 of that Order are revoked from 1st April 1997 and the remainder from 1st July 1997.
