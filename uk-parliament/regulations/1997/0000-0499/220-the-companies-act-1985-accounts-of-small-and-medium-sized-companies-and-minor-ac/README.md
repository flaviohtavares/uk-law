(This note does not form part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend provisions in Part VII of the Companies Act [1985 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6) (“the 1985 Act”) concerning the accounts of small and medium-sized companies (as defined in section 247 of the 1985 Act). They also make certain minor and consequential amendments to other accounting provisions of Part VII.
