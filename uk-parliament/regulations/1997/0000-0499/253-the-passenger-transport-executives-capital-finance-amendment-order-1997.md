# The Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 253

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made

6th February 1997

Laid before Parliament

13th February 1997

Coming into force

6th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(5), (6) and (7) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 6th March 1997.

**2.** The Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) Order 1990[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) In paragraph (1) of article 2 after “(a)” there shall be inserted “(subject to paragraph (2A) below)”;
- (b) After paragraph (2) of article 2 there shall be inserted the following—

  > (2A) Any sum which is received by the South Yorkshire Passenger Transport Executive in respect of the disposal of South Yorkshire Light Rail Limited or South Yorkshire Supertram Limited to which paragraph (1)(a) above applies shall be treated for the purposes of paragraph (1) above as reduced by so much of the sum as is applied by the South Yorkshire Passenger Transport Executive in defraying that Executive’s costs of and incidental to the disposal of either or both of those Companies

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Watts

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

6th February 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) Order 1990 to provide for the deduction from the proceeds arising from the disposal of South Yorkshire Light Rail Limited (formerly named South Yorkshire Supertram Limited) and South Yorkshire Supertram Limited (formerly named South Yorkshire Supertram (No. 2) Limited) of the expenses incurred by the South Yorkshire Passenger Transport Executive in connection with the disposal, before treatment of the balance of such proceeds as if received by the South Yorkshire Passenger Transport Authority under the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.
