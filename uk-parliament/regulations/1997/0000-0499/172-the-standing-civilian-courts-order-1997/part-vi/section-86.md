# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Modification of the 1955 Act for appeals

The provisions of the 1955 Act concerning courts-martial which are specified in column 1 below shall apply to appeals before courts-martial from Standing Civilian Courts subject to the modifications and exceptions specified in column 2 below—

<thead>
<tr><th><em>Column 1</em></th><th><em>Column 2</em></th></tr>
<tr><th>Provision applied</th><th>Modifications and exceptions</th></tr>

<tr><td><em>(finding and sentence)</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>section 97</td><td>after subsection (3) there shall be inserted the following subsection—<br>(4) Any finding or sentence of the court-martial shall replace the finding or sentence of the Standing Civilian Court and shall be announced as such.</td></tr>
<tr><td><em>(power of reviewing authority to authorise retrial)</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>section 113A</td><td>shall not apply</td></tr>
<tr><td><em>(limitation of time for trial of offences)</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>section 132</td><td>shall not apply</td></tr>
<tr><td><em>(persons not to be tried for offences already disposed of)</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>section 134</td><td>shall not operate so as to prevent any appeal being determined</td></tr>
