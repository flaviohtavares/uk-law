# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Notice of appeal

A notice of appeal addressed to the higher authority in accordance with paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to the 1976 Act shall be lodged with the higher authority in the form set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.
