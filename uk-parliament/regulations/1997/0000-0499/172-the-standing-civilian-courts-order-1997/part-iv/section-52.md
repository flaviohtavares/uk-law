# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. View by the court

If at any time during a trial before the court deliberates on its finding it appears to the court that it should, in the interests of justice, view any place or thing, it may adjourn for that purpose.
