# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Witnesses for the defence

Except with the leave of the court, if the accused elects to give evidence he shall be called before any other witness for the defence.
