# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Adjournment of case where court ceases to be properly constituted

Where the court ceases to be properly constituted, the proceedings shall be adjourned and there shall be sent to the court administration officer the charge sheet and the note of proceedings.
