# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Record of finding

(1) Where the court has reached a finding on a charge, it shall be recorded in writing, dated and signed by the magistrate.

(2) Each finding shall be announced in open court.
