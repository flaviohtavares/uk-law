# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Circumstances not provided for

In any circumstance not provided for by the 1955 Act, the 1976 Act or this Order, such course shall be adopted as appears best calculated to do justice.
