# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Application of certain enactments

The provisions of the enactments which are specified in column 1 of Schedule 4 to this Order shall apply to proceedings before the Standing Civilian Court as if the references to courts-martial, howsoever expressed, were references to the Standing Civilian Court and subject to the further modifications specified in column 2 of that Schedule.
