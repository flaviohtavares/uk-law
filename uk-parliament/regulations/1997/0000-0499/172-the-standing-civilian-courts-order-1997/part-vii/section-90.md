# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Loss of the proceedings

If the whole or any part of an original document referred to in article 89 above is lost, a copy of the document certified by the magistrate to be correct or a reconstituted document may be accepted and used in lieu of the original.
