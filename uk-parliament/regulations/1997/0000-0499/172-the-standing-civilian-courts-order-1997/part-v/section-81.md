# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Announcement of sentence and conclusion of trial

(1) The sentence and the reasons for it shall be announced in open court, and the date on which it is announced shall be recorded.

(2) The magistrate shall announce when the trial is concluded.
