# The Standing Civilian Courts Order 1997, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Unfitness to stand trial and insanity

(1) Where the court is required to adjourn the hearing and refer the case to the prosecuting authority by virtue of paragraph 6 of Schedule 3 to the 1976 Act (unfitness to stand trial and insanity), it shall give the prosecuting authority, where reasonably possible—

- (a) details of the accused’s condition;
- (b) the charge-sheet and the note of proceedings.
