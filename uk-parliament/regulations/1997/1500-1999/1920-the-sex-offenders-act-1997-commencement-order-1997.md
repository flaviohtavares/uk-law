# The Sex Offenders Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1920 (C.78)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Sex Offenders Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made

2nd August 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 10(2) of the Sex Offenders Act 1997[^f00001] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sex Offenders Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

**2.** The Sex Offenders Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1997.

Home Office

Alun Michael

Minister of State

2nd August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Sex Offenders Act 1997 on 1st September 1997.
