# The Birmingham Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1791

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Birmingham Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997

Made

24th July 1997

Laid before Parliament

1st August 1997

Coming into force

22nd August 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former South Birmingham Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to this Order, having consulted Birmingham Health Authority and the bodies specified in column (1) of that Schedule in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Birmingham Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1997.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;
- “the trust property” means, in the case of each body specified in column (1) of the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared, agreed upon and signed on behalf of that body and the Health Authority specified in relation to that body in column (2) of the Schedule on the date specified in relation to that body in column (3) of the Schedule.

## 2. Transfers of trust property

On 22nd August 1997 the trust property which is trust property in relation to a body specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be transferred from the Birmingham Health Authority to that body.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn

Minister of State,

Department of Health

24th July 1997
