# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1997, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Transitional and transitory provisions and savings

(1) Schedule 4 shall have effect for the purpose of making transitional and transitory provision and savings.

(2) Nothing in that Schedule affects the general operation of sections 16 and 17 of the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00016] (as applied by section 23 of that Act).
