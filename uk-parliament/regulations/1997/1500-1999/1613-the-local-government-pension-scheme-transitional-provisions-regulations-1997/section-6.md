# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1997, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Entitlement of active members to count old membership

Any person who continues as an active member of the Scheme on the commencement date by virtue of regulation 3 may count for regulation 9(1)(d) of the 1997 regulations the same length of membership as the total period he was entitled to count under the old provisions immediately before that date (but see regulations 8 to 13 and Schedule 2).
