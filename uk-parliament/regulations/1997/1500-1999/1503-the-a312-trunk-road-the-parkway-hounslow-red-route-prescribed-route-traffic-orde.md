# The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1503

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1997

Made

12th June 1997

Coming into force

24th June 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 1997, and shall come into force on 24th June 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- (a) cause includes permit;
- (b) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this Order so numbered;
- (c) “the trunk road” means the length of the A312 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hounslow, known as The Parkway;
- (d) “the traffic island” means the triangular island site situated adjacent to the junction of the southbound carriageway of the trunk road and the north-eastern arm of Cranford High Street.

## 3. Prescribed Routes and Prohibitive Turns

Subject to the provisions of article 4—

- (a) any person causing a vehicle to proceed on the north side of the traffic island shall not cause that vehicle to proceed in any direction other than eastwards towards the north-eastern arm of Cranford High Street and on reaching the junction with the north-eastern arm of Cranford High Street shall not cause that vehicle to continue in any direction other than left; and
- (b) any person causing a vehicle to proceed southward in the trunk road on the south-west side of the traffic island, shall not cause that vehicle to continue in any direction other than ahead.

## 4. Exemptions

Nothing in article 3 shall apply—

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable or a traffic warden in uniform.
- (b) to a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes.

## 5. Revocation

The Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (Prescribed Route) Order 1975[^f00003] is hereby revoked.
