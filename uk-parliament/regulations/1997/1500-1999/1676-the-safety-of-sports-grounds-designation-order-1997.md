# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1676

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1997

Made

9th July 1997

Laid before Parliament

10th July 1997

Coming into force

31st July 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st July 1997.

**2.** The sports grounds specified in the Schedule to this Order (being sports grounds in England at which association football matches are played and which are occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, each of which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) are hereby designated as sports grounds requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00002].

**3.** In the Schedule to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1978[^f00003], the entry in respect of Burnden Park, Bolton shall be omitted.

Chris Smith

Secretary of State for National Heritage

9th July 1997

Article 3 of the Order varies the Order referred to therein by omitting an entry which is no longer a sports ground.
