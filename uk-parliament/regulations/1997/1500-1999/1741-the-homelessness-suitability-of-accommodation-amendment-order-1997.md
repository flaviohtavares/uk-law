> **3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Circumstances in which accommodation is not to be regarded as suitable (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3cdee3ea196463d4a0f54ecefeb69b30][^c-3cdee3ea196463d4a0f54ecefeb69b30]**

> For the purposes of section 197(1) of the Housing Act 1996 (duty where other suitable accommodation available), accommodation shall not be regarded as suitable unless the local housing authority are satisfied that it will be available for occupation by the applicant for at least two years beginning with the date on which he secures it. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3cdee3ea196463d4a0f54ecefeb69b30]

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that, for the purposes of section 197(1) of the Housing Act 1996 (duty where other suitable accommodation available), accommodation shall not be regarded as suitable unless the local housing authority are satisfied that it will be available for occupation by the applicant for at least two years beginning with the date on which he secures it.
