# The Nottinghamshire (Police Area and Authority) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1850

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

POLICE

The Nottinghamshire (Police Area and Authority) Order 1997

Made

28th July 1997

Laid before Parliament

5th August 1997

Coming into force

Articles 1 and 2

1st October 1997

Article 3

1st April 1998

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17 and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Nottinghamshire (Police Area and Authority) Order 1997.

(2) In this Order “1996 Act” means the Police Act 1996[^f00002].

(3) This Order, except article 3, shall come into force on 1st October 1997 and article 3 shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

**2.** (1) The functions to which this article applies are—

- (a) all functions of the Nottinghamshire police authority on or after 1st April 1998; and
- (b) before that date (but on or after 1st October 1997), the functions of that police authority in respect of the financial year beginning on 1st April 1998 under—
  - (i) Chapter IV of Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (precepts)[^f00003], and
  - (ii) sections 7 (local policing objectives) and 8 (local policing plans) of the 1996 Act.

(2) For the purposes of the functions to which this article applies—

- (a) paragraph 26(1) of Schedule 2 to the 1996 Act (meaning of “relevant council”) shall have effect from 1st October 1997 as if the City of Nottingham had ceased to form part of the County of Nottinghamshire on that date; and
- (b) accordingly, the appointments which are required to be made to the Nottinghamshire police authority by paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule (appointments of members of police authority by joint committee appointed by relevant councils) shall be made, as soon as practicable after that date, by a joint committee appointed by Nottinghamshire County Council and the City of Nottingham Council (as relevant councils) in accordance with that provision.

(3) For the purposes of the functions referred to in paragraph (1)(b) above—

- (a) section 19(2)(b) of the 1996 Act (approval of decisions about precepts) shall have effect as though the reference to the members of the police authority appointed under paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to that Act was a reference to the members so appointed by virtue of paragraph (2) above; and
- (b) the members of the Nottinghamshire police authority appointed under paragraph 2 of that Schedule otherwise than under paragraph (2) above shall not participate in the discharge of those functions.

(4) In the exercise of its powers, the joint committee referred to in paragraph (2)(b) above—

- (a) shall, for the purposes of paragraph 4(1)(b) of Schedule 2 to the 1996 Act, ignore any member of Nottinghamshire County Council elected for an electoral division in the City of Nottingham, and
- (b) shall not appoint any such member to the Nottinghamshire police authority under paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule.

(5) A member of the Nottinghamshire police authority who was appointed under paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to the 1996 Act otherwise than by virtue of paragraph (2) above and who is not subsequently so appointed shall cease to hold office as a member of that Authority on 1st April 1998.

(6) In determining the period of a term of years for the purpose of paragraph 16 of Schedule 2 to the 1996 Act (term of appointment of members of a police authority), any period as a member of the Nottinghamshire police authority prior to 1st April 1998 under an appointment made by virtue of paragraph (2) above shall be disregarded except where the person in question had been a member of that authority by virtue of an appointment under paragraph 2 of that Schedule prior to his appointment by virtue of paragraph (2) above.

**3.** Schedule 1 to the 1996 Act (police areas) shall be amended by inserting after the word “Nottinghamshire” in the right-hand column opposite the name of the Nottinghamshire police area the words “and the non-metropolitan district of Nottingham”.

Alun Michael

Minister of State

Home Office

28th July 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision consequential on the structural change effected by the Nottinghamshire (City of Nottingham) (Structural Change) Order 1996 (S.I. [1996/1877](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1877)). That Order removed the City of Nottingham from the County of Nottinghamshire.

Article 2 of this Order makes transitional provision relating to the proceedings of the Nottinghamshire police authority. Article 3 amends the description of the Nottinghamshire police area in Schedule 1 to the Police Act 1996 to take account of the structural change.
