# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1997, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Rights to payment out of fund authority’s pension fund

(1) The amount of any transfer payment due in respect of a member under Chapter IV of Part IV of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 is payable by the fund authority from their pension fund.

(2) Where such a transfer payment is to be or has been paid from a fund, no other payment or transfer of assets may be made from the fund as respects the accrued rights covered by the transfer payment.

(3) Paragraph (2) overrides anything to the contrary in the former regulations, any local Act scheme, the 1974 regulations, the 1986 regulations, the 1995 regulations or any other provision of these Regulations or the Transitional Regulations.
