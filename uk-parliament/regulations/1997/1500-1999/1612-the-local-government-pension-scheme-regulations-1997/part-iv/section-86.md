# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1997, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Cost of calculations for transfer of AVCs or SCAVCs into the Scheme where no transfer is requested

Where—

- (a) at a member's request an administering authority give him information concerning the amount payable if he elects under regulation 66(2) (including that regulation as applied by regulation 72(1)) for the accumulated value of his invested additional contributions to be used to acquire transfer credits in the Scheme, but
- (b) he does not make such an election before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the date they give him the information,

they may deduct the cost of calculating the transfer value from the accumulated value of the additional contributions mentioned in regulation 64(2).
