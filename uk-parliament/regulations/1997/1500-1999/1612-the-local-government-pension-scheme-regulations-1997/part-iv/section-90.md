# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1997, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Pension increases and cash equivalents under the Pension Schemes Act 1993

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter III of Part IV of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00027] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) must be paid from the appropriate fund.
