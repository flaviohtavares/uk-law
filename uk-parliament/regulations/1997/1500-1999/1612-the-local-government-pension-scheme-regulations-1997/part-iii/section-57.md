# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 1997, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Effect of increases under this Chapter for older members

Regulation 54 applies as respects additional periods counted under the previous provisions of this Chapter as it applies as respects additional periods counted under Chapter II.
