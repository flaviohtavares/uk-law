# The Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1725 (C. 73)

POLICE

The Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made

18th July 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 2(2) of the Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

**2.** Section 1 of the Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st August 1997.

Alun Michael

Minister of State

Home Office

18th July 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st August 1997 section 1 of the Confiscation of Alcohol (Young Persons) Act 1997. Section 2 came into force on Royal Assent (21st March 1997).
