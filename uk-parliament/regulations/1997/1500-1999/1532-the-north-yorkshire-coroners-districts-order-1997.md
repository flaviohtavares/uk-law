# The North Yorkshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1532

CORONERS

The North Yorkshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1997

Made

16th June 1997

Laid before Parliament

25th June 1997

Coming into force

1st August 1997

Whereas the council of the county of North Yorkshire have, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the Coroners Act 1988[^f00001] and after due compliance with the provisions of the Coroners (Orders as to Districts) Rules 1927[^f00002], submitted to me a draft Order providing for the alteration of the existing division of the said county into coroners' districts:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by that section 4(2), I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the North Yorkshire (Coroners' Districts) Order 1997.

## 2. Commencement and application

(1) This Order shall come into force on 1st August 1997.

(2) This Order shall not have effect in relation to any inquest before the day on which it comes into force or to any post-mortem examination which, before that day, a coroner has directed or requested a medical practitioner to make.

## 3. Alteration of coroners' districts

(1) The existing Claro coroner’s district, North Eastern coroner’s district, Scarborough and Ryedale coroner’s district and Western coroner’s district of the county of North Yorkshire shall be abolished.

(2) From the area comprising the coroners' districts mentioned in paragraph (1) above there shall be formed the two coroners' districts named in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order which shall consist of the areas specified in column 2 of the Schedule.

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

16th June 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

The county of North Yorkshire is divided into the Claro, North Eastern, Scarborough and Ryedale, and Western coroners' districts.

This Order abolishes these four districts and divides North Yorkshire into two new coroner’s districts, Eastern (comprising the districts of Hambledon, Ryedale and Scarborough) and Western (comprising the districts of Craven, Harrogate, Richmondshire and Selby).
