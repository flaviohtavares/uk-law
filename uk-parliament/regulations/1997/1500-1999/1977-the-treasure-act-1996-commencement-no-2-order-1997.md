# The Treasure Act 1996(Commencement No. 2) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1977 (C. 81)

TREASURE

The Treasure Act 1996(Commencement No. 2) Order 1997

Made

7th August 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 15(2) of the Treasure Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Treasure Act 1996 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1997.

**2.** The provisions of the Treasure Act 1996 not already in force shall come into force on 24th September 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for National Heritage

Mark Fisher

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

7th August 1997

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 24th September 1997, with the exception of one provision, the Treasure Act 1996. The provision in section 11 of the Act was bought into force on 13th March 1997 (S.I. [1997/760 (C. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/760)).
