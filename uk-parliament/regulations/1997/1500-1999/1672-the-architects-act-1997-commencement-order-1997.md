# The Architects Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1672 (C.69)

ARCHITECTS

The Architects Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made

9th July 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(2) of the Architects Act 1997[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Architects Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

## 2. Commencement

The Architects Act 1997, except for section 28 (short title, commencement and extent)[^f00002], shall come into force on 21st July 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

9th July 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings the Architects Act 1997 into force with the exception of section 28 which came into force at Royal Assent.
