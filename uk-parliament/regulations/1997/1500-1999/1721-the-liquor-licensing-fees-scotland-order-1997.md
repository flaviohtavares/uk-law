# The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1721 (S. 130)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997

Made

10th July 1997

Coming into force

1st August 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8(1) and 135 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st August 1997.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 and “the 1990 Act” means the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00002].

## 2. Fees payable

The fee payable by any applicant to a licensing board–

- (a) in respect of any matter described in column 1 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order and arising under the provisions of the Act specified in column 2 of that Part shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 3, instead of the amount, where relevant, specified opposite thereto in column 4[^f00003]; and
- (b) in respect of the matter described in column 1 of Part II of the Schedule to this Order and arising under the provision of the 1990 Act specified in column 2 of that Part shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 3, instead of the amount specified opposite thereto in column 4[^f00004].

## 3. Revocations

The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1993[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

This Order determines the fees payable from 1st August 1997 by applicants to a licensing board under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 and the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 in respect of the matters listed in the Schedule to the Order. The fees are increased by approximately 8.3 per cent.
