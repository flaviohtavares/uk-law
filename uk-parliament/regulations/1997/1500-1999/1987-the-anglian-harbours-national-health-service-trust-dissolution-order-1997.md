# The Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1987

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE,ENGLAND AND WALES

The Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1997

Made

12th August 1997

Coming into force

1st October 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust established by the Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Frank Dobson

Secretary of State for Health

12th August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st October 1997 of the Anglian Harbours National Health Service Trust established on 21st December 1990.
