# The Accounts Commission for Scotland (Financial Year) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1979 (S. 141)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Accounts Commission for Scotland (Financial Year) Order 1997

Made

4th August 1997

Coming into force

1st September 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(3) of the Audit (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Accounts Commission for Scotland (Financial Year) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st September 1997.

## 2. Financial year of Commission

Subsections (1) and (2) of section 4 of the Audit (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1996 shall first have effect in relation to the period from 1st April 1997 to 31st October 1997.

Malcolm Chisholm

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th August 1997

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 4 of the Audit (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1996 makes provision for changing the financial year of the Accounts Commission for Scotland so that it will in future end on 31st October, rather than on 31st March as at present. This Order makes provision for the transition to the new system, so that the period from 1st April to 31st October 1997 will constitute a financial year of the Commission.
