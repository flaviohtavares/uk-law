# The Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 1771

MARINE POLLUTION

The Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made

22nd July 1997

Coming into force

22nd August 1997

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of July 1997

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 26(1) and (2) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1997.

**2.** In article 3 of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (Jersey) Order 1987[^f00002] (“the Order”) for the words “Schedules 2 to 4” there shall be substituted the words “Schedules 2 and 3”.

**3.** Section 14 of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00003] (“the 1985 Act”), shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey with the substitution for paragraph 10 of the Schedule to the Order of—

> **10.** In section 14—
>
> - (a) in subsection (1), for the words “each licensing authority, as respects licences for which it is the licensing authority, to maintain, in accordance with regulations,” there shall be substituted the words “the Committee to maintain”;
> - (b) in paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (1), for the words “that authority” there shall be substituted the word “it”;
> - (c) in subsection (5), for the words “each licensing authority” there shall be substituted the words “the Committee”, and for the words “the authority” there shall be substituted the word “it”;
> - (d) in subsection (7), for the words “in regulations” there shall be substituted the words “by Order made by the Committee”;
> - (e) for subsection (8), there shall be substituted—
>
>   > (8) The Subordinate Legislation (Jersey) Law, 1960 as amended by the Subordinate Legislation (Amendment) (Jersey) Law, 1976, shall apply to Orders made under this section.
>
>   .

**4.** Paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to the 1985 Act[^f00004] shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey with the substitution for sub-paragraph (b)(ii) of paragraph 17 of the Schedule to the Order of—

> - (ii) in sub-paragraph (3), for the words from “under” to “an officer” there shall be substituted the words “under Part II of this Act an officer”;.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that section 14 (duty to keep public register of information) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (“the 1985 Act”), as substituted by section 147 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (“the 1990 Act”), and paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 (powers in relation to vessels, aircraft etc.) to the 1985 Act, as amended by section 146(1) and (8) of the 1990 Act, shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey with the amendments to the Schedule to the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (Jersey) Order 1987 provided for (in particular, these amendments have the effect that sub-paragraphs (3) to (5) of paragraph 3 which, by virtue of that Schedule, do not currently extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey, shall now so extend).

It further provides that Schedule 4 to the 1985 Act (which has effect by virtue of section 14 of the 1985 Act as that section was enacted and which, consequent on the above mentioned extension to the Bailiwick of Jersey of the new section 14, will be spent) shall no longer extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey.
