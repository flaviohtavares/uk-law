# The Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1971

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1971 No. 698 (C.16)

NATIONAL ASSISTANCE SERVICES

The Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1971

Made

28th April 1971

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of his powers under section 1(3) of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby orders as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1971.

**2.** The appointed day for the coming into force in England and Wales of the provisions of section 1 of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 shall be 1st October 1971.

Keith Joseph

Secretary of State for Social Services

28th April 1971

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st October 1971 as the appointed day for the coming into force in England and Wales of section 1 of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970.
