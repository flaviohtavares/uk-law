# The Sea Fishing (Manx Boats) Order 1971

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1971 No. 1748

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fishing (Manx Boats) Order 1971

Made

27th October 1971

Coming into Operation

15th November 1971

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of October 1971

Present.

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 21(1)(b) of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968 is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (Manx Boats) Order 1971 and shall come into operation on 15th November 1971.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this Order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

**2.** The provisions of sections 5 to 14 of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968 shall apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in the Isle of Man as they apply in relation to British fishing boats registered in the United Kingdom.

W. G. Agnew

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies the provisions of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968, which regulates sea fishing operations, and certain ancillary provisions, to British fishing boats registered in the Isle of Man as they apply in relation to such boats registered in the United Kingdom.
