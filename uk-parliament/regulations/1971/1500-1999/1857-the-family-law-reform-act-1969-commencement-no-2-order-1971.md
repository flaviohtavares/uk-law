# The Family Law Reform Act 1969 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1971

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1971 No. 1857 (C.50)

FAMILY LAW

The Family Law Reform Act 1969 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1971

Made

12th November 1971

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 28(3) of the Family Law Reform Act 1969, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Family Law Reform Act 1969 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1971.

**2.** Part III of the Family Law Reform Act 1969 shall come into force on 1st March 1972.

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C

Dated 12th November 1971

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings Part III of the Family Law Reform Act 1969, which provides for the use of blood tests in determining paternity, into force on 1st March 1972.
