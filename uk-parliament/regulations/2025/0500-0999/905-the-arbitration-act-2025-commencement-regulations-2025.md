# The Arbitration Act 2025 (Commencement) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 905 (C. 41)

ARBITRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

ARBITRATION, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Arbitration Act 2025 (Commencement) Regulations 2025

Made

16th July 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 17(2) of the Arbitration Act 2025[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Arbitration Act 2025 (Commencement) Regulations 2025.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st August 2025

The Arbitration Act 2025 comes into force on 1st August 2025 to the extent that it is not already in force[^f00002].

Sarah Sackman

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

16th July 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force all those provisions of the Arbitration Act [2025 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/4) that did not come into force on Royal Assent.
