# The New Heavy-Duty Vehicles (Carbon Dioxide Emission Performance Standards) (Vocational Vehicles) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 2(3) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/1242) of 20 June 2019 setting CO<sub>2</sub> emission performance standards for new heavy-duty vehicles[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the New Heavy-Duty Vehicles (Carbon Dioxide Emission Performance Standards) (Vocational Vehicles) Regulations 2025 and come into force on 29th July 2025.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**Commission [Regulation (EU) 2017/2400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2400)**” means [Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/2400) of 12 December 2017 implementing [Regulation (EC) No 595/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/595) as regards the determination of the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions and fuel consumption of heavy-duty vehicles and amending [Directive 2007/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2007/46) and [Commission Regulation (EU) No 582/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/582)[^f00002];
- “**heavy-duty vehicle**” means a vehicle to which any of points (a) to (d) of Article 2(1) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/1242) apply;
- “**manufacturer**” has the meaning given in Article 3(10) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/1242)[^f00003];
- “**misreported vehicle**” means a heavy-duty vehicle that—
  - (a) was certified as a vocational vehicle based on the data reported by the manufacturer in accordance with Article 5 of [Regulation (EU) 2018/956](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/956)[^f00004]; and
  - (b) was registered as a non-vocational vehicle based on the data reported to the Secretary of State in accordance with Article 4 of [Regulation (EU) 2018/956](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/956);
- “**non-vocational vehicle**” means a heavy-duty vehicle other than a vocational vehicle;
- “**[Regulation (EU) 2018/956](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/0956)**” means [Regulation (EU) 2018/956 of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/956) on the monitoring and reporting of CO<sub>2</sub> emissions from and fuel consumption of new heavy-duty vehicles[^f00005];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 2019/1242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1242)**” means [Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/1242) setting CO<sub>2</sub> emission performance standards for new heavy-duty vehicles and amending [Regulations (EC) No 595/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/595) and [(EU) 2018/956](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/956) of the European Parliament and of the Council and [Council Directive 96/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/53);
- “**vocational vehicle**” has the meaning given in Article 3(16) of [Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/2400).

## 3. Identification of misreported vehicles

(1) Where a misreported vehicle is identified, the Secretary of State must notify the manufacturer of the vehicle.

(2) Within one month of receiving a notification under paragraph (1), a manufacturer may provide to the Secretary of State information to show that the vehicle was correctly certified as a vocational vehicle.

(3) Where, following receipt of any information under paragraph (2), the Secretary of State considers that a misreported vehicle was incorrectly certified as a vocational vehicle, the Secretary of State must correct the manufacturer’s average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions[^f00006] for the reporting period of the relevant year[^f00007] to take that vehicle into account.

## 4. Procedure for correcting average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions

(1) The Secretary of State may correct a manufacturer’s average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions using the correct CO<sub>2</sub> values of a misreported vehicle.

(2) Where the correct CO<sub>2</sub> values of a misreported vehicle are not already available, the Secretary of State may request that a manufacturer determine the correct CO<sub>2</sub> values according to the technical characteristics of that vehicle.

(3) Where a manufacturer determines the correct CO<sub>2</sub> values of a misreported vehicle following a request under paragraph (2), the manufacturer must use the same version of the simulation tool as used for the original determination of the CO<sub>2</sub> values of that vehicle, unless the Secretary of State permits otherwise.

(4) Where a manufacturer fails to determine the correct CO<sub>2</sub> values of a misreported vehicle within two months of receiving a request under paragraph (2), the Secretary of State may correct the manufacturer’s average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions using the formula set out in the Schedule.

(5) In this regulation—

- “**correct CO<sub>2</sub> values**” means the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions and fuel consumption of a misreported vehicle as a non-vocational vehicle, determined using the simulation tool in accordance with [Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/2400);
- “**simulation tool**” means an electronic tool used to determine CO<sub>2</sub> emissions and fuel consumption of vehicles as provided for in Article 5 of [Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2400](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/2400).

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations introduce a procedure to identify heavy-duty vehicles that have been misreported as vocational vehicles and to correct such misreporting for the purposes of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1242).

[Regulation (EU) 2019/1242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1242) sets CO<sub>2</sub> emission performance standards for new heavy-duty vehicles, exempting vocational vehicles from the calculation of average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions which inform the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions reduction target set for each manufacturer.

These Regulations enable the Secretary of State to ensure that vehicles which were incorrectly certified as vocational vehicles are not exempt from manufacturers’ average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions calculations.

These Regulations enable the Secretary of State to engage with manufacturers to obtain the correct information regarding such vehicles, and to amend the calculation of the average specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions of such manufacturers accordingly.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

These Regulations, along with an Explanatory Memorandum, are available on the UK legislation website at http://www.legislation.gov.uk.
