# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 655

CORONERS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2025

Made

5th June 2025

Laid before Parliament

10th June 2025

Coming into force

1st July 2025

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001], and after consulting in accordance with that paragraph, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Alteration of Coroner Areas) Order 2025 and comes into force on 1st July 2025.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “**coroner area**” means a coroner area specified in the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013[^f00002];
- “**new coroner area**” means the new coroner area resulting from the combining of the coroner areas set out in article 3.

## 3. New coroner area

The following coroner areas are combined to create the new coroner area of Kent and Medway—

- (a) Central and South East Kent,
- (b) Mid Kent and Medway,
- (c) North East Kent, and
- (d) North West Kent.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1625](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1625)), which came into force on 25th July 2013, established that the former “coroners’ districts” - as constituted under the Coroners Act [1988 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/13) - were to be specified as corresponding coroner areas under the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25).

This Order creates a new coroner area of Kent and Medway by combining the four coroner areas of Central and South East Kent, Mid Kent and Medway, North East Kent and North West Kent.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
