> Text as at 2025-09-20.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Apparatus and rights of statutory undertakers in stopped up and closed streets[^c-7b078c0973961ab37a4d72e6930543ce]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a street is altered or diverted or its use is temporarily or permanently prohibited or restricted under article 8 (street works), article 9 (power to alter layout, etc., of streets), article 10 (construction and maintenance of altered streets) or article 11 (temporary closure of streets and public rights of way) any statutory undertaker whose apparatus is under, in, on, along or across the street has the same powers and rights in respect of that apparatus, subject to Schedule 14 (protective provisions), as if this Order had not been made.
