> Text as at 2025-09-20.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by this Order[^key-588e7570d41b8dc16912bd410052811b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and the requirements in Schedule 2 (requirements), the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development in Schedule 1 (authorised development) to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Each numbered work must be situated within the corresponding numbered area shown on the works plans.
