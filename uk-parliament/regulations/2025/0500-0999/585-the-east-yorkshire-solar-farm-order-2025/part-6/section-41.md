# The East Yorkshire Solar Farm Order 2025, section 41

> Text as at 2025-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. No double recovery[^c-3e6320b83807c4470fce968dbb3399ea]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
