# The East Yorkshire Solar Farm Order 2025, section 37

> Text as at 2025-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Operational land for the purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-9256ebec51f2e7c989c1bb8dda11e7cd]

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land) of the 1990 Act.
