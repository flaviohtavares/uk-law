# The East Yorkshire Solar Farm Order 2025, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2025-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — LEGISLATION TO BE DISAPPLIED

*Extent: E+W*

Article 6

**1.**[^key-786a53e15cac95c85446606610e1fc72] The following provisions do not apply in so far as they relate to the construction of any numbered work or the carrying out of any operation required for the purpose of, or in connection with, the construction, operation, maintenance or decommissioning of the authorised development—

- (a) East Riding Drainage Act 1798[^f00049];
- (b) Seaton Ross Inclosure Act 1811[^f00050];
- (c) Keyingham (and others) Drainage \[Yorkshire\] Act 1845[^f00051];
- (d) York and North Midland Railway (East Riding Branches) \[No.1\] Act 1846[^f00052];
- (e) Wapentake of Ouse and Derwent Drainage Act 1854[^f00053];
- (f) Ouse (Lower) Improvement Act 1884[^f00054];
- (g) Aire and Calder Navigation Act 1914[^f00055];
- (h) Aire and Calder Navigation Act 1924[^f00056];
- (i) Boothferry Bridge Act 1925[^f00057];
- (j) Yorkshire Water Authority (Alteration of Boundaries of the Lower Ouse Internal Drainage District) Order 1989[^f00058]; and
- (k) The Selby Area Internal Drainage District Order 2017[^f00059].
