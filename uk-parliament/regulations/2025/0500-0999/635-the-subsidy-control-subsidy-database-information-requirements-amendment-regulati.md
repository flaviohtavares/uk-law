# The Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 635

SUBSIDY CONTROL

The Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Made

27th May 2025

Laid before Parliament

2nd June 2025

Coming into force

18th June 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(1), (2), and (3), and 87(2) of the Subsidy Control Act 2022[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 and come into force on the 22nd day after the date on which these Regulations are made.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 4 of the Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) Regulations 2022

In regulation 4 of the Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) Regulations 2022[^f00002]—

- (a) In the heading, after “subsidy schemes”, insert “and subsidies given under subsidy schemes”;
- (b) After paragraph (3), insert—

  > (4) Paragraph (5) of this regulation applies in the case of an entry in the subsidy database made under section 33(1)(a) of the Act, in respect of a subsidy given under a subsidy scheme.
  >
  > (5) The entry need not include the information required under regulation 3(p), (q), (r), (s), (t), (u), (v), (w) and (x).

  .

Justin Madders

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Business and Trade

27th May 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Subsidy Control (Subsidy Database Information Requirements) Regulations 2022, specifically regulation 4, so as to remove the requirement for a public authority’s entry in the subsidy database in respect of a subsidy given under a subsidy scheme to include the information required by regulation 3(p), (q), (r), (s), (t), (u), (v), (w) and (x). This information continues to be required for a public authority’s entry in the subsidy database in respect of a subsidy given other than under a subsidy scheme.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen, and it reduces the current impact of the subsidy control regime on the public sector. An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside these Regulations.
