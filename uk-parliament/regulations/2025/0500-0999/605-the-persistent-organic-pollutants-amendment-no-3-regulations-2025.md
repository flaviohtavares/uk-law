# The Persistent Organic Pollutants (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 605

Environmental Protection

The Persistent Organic Pollutants (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2025

Made

at 11.37 a.m. on 20th May 2025

Laid before Parliament

at 4.30 p.m. on 20th May 2025

Coming into force

21st May 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 15(1) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast) (“**the POPs Regulation**”)[^f00001].

The Scottish Ministers and the Welsh Ministers have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations, in accordance with Article 2A of the POPs Regulation.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Persistent Organic Pollutants (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Amendments to [Regulation (EU) 2019/1021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1021)

(1) The table in Part A of Annex 1 (Substances listed in the Convention and in the Protocol as well as substances listed only in the Convention) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit the entries for—

- (a) Dechlorane Plus[^f00002];
- (b) UV-328[^f00003].

Emma Hardy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

At 11.37 a.m. on 20th May 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 15(1) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast) (EUR 2019/1021, “the POPs Regulation”).

Regulation 2 amends provisions in Annex 1 to the POPs Regulation so as to remove the entries concerning Dechlorane Plus and UV-328.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector is available from  www.legislation.gov.uk, and from the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Seacole Building, 2 Marsham Street, London, SW1P 4DF.
