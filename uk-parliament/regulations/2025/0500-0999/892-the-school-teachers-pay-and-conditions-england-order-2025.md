# The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2025

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2025/892
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2025 No. 892

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2025

Made

16th July 2025

Laid before Parliament

18th July 2025

Coming into force

1st September 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1), 123 and 124 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“**the Act**”).

In accordance with section 125(1)(a) of the Act, the Secretary of State has referred certain matters to the School Teachers’ Review Body (“the STRB”) under section 120(1) of the Act.

After following the procedure set out in section 121 of the Act, the STRB has reported to the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State in accordance with section 120(3) of the Act.

In accordance with section 120(4) of the Act, the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State have arranged for the report of the STRB to be published.

In accordance with section 125(1)(b) of the Act, the Secretary of State has considered the report of the STRB.

In accordance with section 126 of the Act, the Secretary of State has consulted such of the persons and bodies referred to in that section as appeared to the Secretary of State to be appropriate.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application[^key-5c08bd1f4a3a2cf2aa981f62fd9b0488]

(1) This Order may be cited as the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2025 and comes into force on the 1st September 2025.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales and applies to school teachers[^f00002] in England.

## 2. The Document[^key-8463287bf1d070006bca3eea5ed8a3e2]

(1) The provisions set out in Section 2 of the Document have effect on and after 1st September 2025 for the purposes of determining—

- (a) the remuneration of school teachers; and
- (b) other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties or working time.

(2) In paragraph (1) “**the Document**” means the document entitled “School teachers’ pay and conditions document 2025 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions” dated 16 July 2025 and published on the gov.uk website[^f00003].

## 3. Revocation of the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2024[^key-2ae9e403745a28ae6a660ba310b2eee4]

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2024[^f00004] is revoked.

Catherine McKinnell

Minister of State (Minister for School Standards)

Department for Education

16th July 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the determination of the remuneration of school teachers (within the meaning of section 122 of the Education Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32)) in England and other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties and working time.

The Order makes this provision by reference to Section 2 of a document entitled “School teachers’ pay and conditions document 2025 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions” dated 16 July 2025. It can be accessed on the Government website, at www.gov.uk. Copies are available to purchase from TSO Customer Services, PO Box 29, Norwich, NR3 1GN, telephone 0333 202 5070 or for inspection at reasonable times at the offices of the Department for Education, Sanctuary Buildings, Great Smith Street, London, SW1P 3BT.

This Order revokes the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/1037](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1037)).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order as no impact on businesses or civil society organisations is foreseen. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
