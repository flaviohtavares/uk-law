# The Oaklands Farm Solar Park Order 2025, section 4

> Text as at 2025-10-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Power to maintain the authorised development[^key-e49aa87c96ef549c39beb08e9c8719ca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.

(2) This article only authorises the carrying out of maintenance works within the Order limits.

(3) This article does not authorise the carrying out of any works which are likely to give rise to any materially new or materially different environmental effects compared to those identified in the environmental statement.
