> Text as at 2025-10-09.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-22f1fc6b56f7e007b6001b1532353b47]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of this Part, the undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development or to facilitate, or is incidental, to it.
