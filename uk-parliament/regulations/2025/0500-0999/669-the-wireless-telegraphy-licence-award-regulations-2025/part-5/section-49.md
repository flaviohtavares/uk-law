# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Determination of excess demand by OFCOM

(1) After the completion of the selection process, OFCOM shall determine excess demand for each lot type.

(2) In respect of any particular lot type, the excess demand at the end of the round shall be the same number as that lot type’s current excess demand.
