# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Posted demand number in the case of sets of bids to maintain demand

If, for a particular lot type, a bidder specified a target number of lots which was the same as the notified posted demand number for that bidder for that lot type (following the most recent round) the target number of lots shall be that bidder’s posted demand number.
