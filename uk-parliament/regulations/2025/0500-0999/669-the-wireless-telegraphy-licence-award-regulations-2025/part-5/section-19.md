# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Notification of information to bidders before the first principal stage round

Before the first principal stage round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder of—

- (a) the eligibility limit which applies to that bidder for that round; and
- (b) the date on which, and the time period on that date in which bidding in the first principal stage round will take place.
