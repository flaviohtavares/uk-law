# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. The completion of the selection process

OFCOM shall continue considering sets of bids in turn until—

- (a) OFCOM has considered each set of bids that was submitted in the round at least once; and
- (b) the last set of bids (if any) on the list (or any list which has been remade in accordance with regulation 37) has been considered and not removed or accorded reduced status.
