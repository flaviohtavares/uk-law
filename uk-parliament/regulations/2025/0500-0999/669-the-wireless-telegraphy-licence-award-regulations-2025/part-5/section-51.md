# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Eligibility points associated with lots

The number of eligibility points associated with lots are—

- (a) 2 eligibility points for each 26 GHz lower lot;
- (b) 2 eligibility points for each 26 GHz upper lot; and
- (c) 1 eligibility point for each 40 GHz lot.
