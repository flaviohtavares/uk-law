# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Determination of the winning principal stage bids

When OFCOM have determined that there shall not be any principal stage rounds, each bidder shall be recorded as having won—

- (a) the number of lots corresponding to that bidder’s notified posted demand number for 26 GHz lower lots following the final principal stage round;
- (b) the number of lots corresponding to that bidder’s notified posted demand number for 26 GHz upper lots following the final principal stage round; and
- (c) the number of lots corresponding to that bidder’s notified posted demand number for 40 GHz lots following the final principal stage round.
