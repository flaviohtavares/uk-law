# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Lot prices for the first principal stage round

In the first principal stage round—

- (a) the price for each 26 GHz lower lot shall be two million pounds;
- (b) the price for each 26 GHz upper lot shall be two million pounds; and
- (c) the price for each 40 GHz lot shall be one million pounds.
