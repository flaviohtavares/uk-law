# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Determination by OFCOM of excess demand after the end of the first round

(1) After the end of the first round, OFCOM shall determine the excess demand for each lot type.

(2) Excess demand is equal to—

- (a) the sum of posted demand numbers for that lot type across all bidders; less
- (b) the number of lots of that type which are available in the award process.
