# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Principal stage bids

A principal stage bid is a bid for the inclusion in a licence of a specified number of lots (of a particular type) and a sum (which must be a whole number of thousands of pounds) which a bidder is willing to pay for a licence which includes those lots.
