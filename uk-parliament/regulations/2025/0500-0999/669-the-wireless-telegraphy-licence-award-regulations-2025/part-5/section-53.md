# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Eligibility limit in the second and subsequent rounds

In the second and subsequent principal stage rounds, a bidder’s eligibility limit shall be equal to the number of eligibility points used by the bidder in the most recent round.
