# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Determination by OFCOM of posted price after the end of the first round

The posted price for each lot type after the end of the first round shall be the same as the price for each lot type in regulation 20.
