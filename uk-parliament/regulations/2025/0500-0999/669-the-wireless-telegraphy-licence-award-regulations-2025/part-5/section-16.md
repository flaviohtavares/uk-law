# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Principal stage rounds

(1) During the principal stage, there shall be one or more principal stage rounds.

(2) A principal stage round shall begin and end at the times notified by OFCOM under regulations 19 and 25.

(3) The number of principal stage rounds shall be determined by OFCOM in accordance with regulation 58.
