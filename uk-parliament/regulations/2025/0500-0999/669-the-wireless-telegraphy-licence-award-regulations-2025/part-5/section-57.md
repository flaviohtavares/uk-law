# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Publication of information at the end of each day during the principal stage

After the end of the latest principal stage round to take place on any given day, OFCOM shall publish on their website, in relation to that latest principal stage round—

- (a) the posted price for each lot type; and
- (b) the excess demand for each lot type.
