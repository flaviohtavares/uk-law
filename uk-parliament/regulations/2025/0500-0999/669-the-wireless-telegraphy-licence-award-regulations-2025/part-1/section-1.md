# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025 and shall come into force on 30th June 2025.

(2) These Regulations shall not extend to the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man.
