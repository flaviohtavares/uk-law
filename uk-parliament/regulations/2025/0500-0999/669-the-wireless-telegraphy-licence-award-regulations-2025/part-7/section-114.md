# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 114. Notification of the total auction sum payable by winning bidders to OFCOM

Following the determination of the total auction sum payable by each winning bidder to OFCOM, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of the total auction sum payable by that winning bidder to OFCOM.
