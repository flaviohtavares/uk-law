# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Notification to bidders following the grant of licences

Following the grant of licences and the refund (if any) of deposits, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of the names of all of the winning bidders to whom licences were granted, and, in relation to each of them—

- (a) the frequencies in respect of which the licences were granted; and
- (b) the licence fees paid, indicating the total base prices and the additional prices.
