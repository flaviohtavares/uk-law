# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 105

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 105. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 40 GHz licence

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 40 GHz licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder’s total 40 GHz base price; and
- (b) that bidder’s 40 GHz lot additional price (if any).
