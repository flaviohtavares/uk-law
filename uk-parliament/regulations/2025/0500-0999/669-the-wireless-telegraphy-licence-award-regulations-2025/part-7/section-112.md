# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Particular frequencies to be included in a 26 GHz lot second period licence granted to a winning bidder

(1) Where a bidder was assigned 26 GHz lots—

- (a) without bidding under step three (in accordance with regulation 85) the 26 GHz lot second period licence granted to that bidder shall authorise the use of the frequencies assigned; and
- (b) under step four (in accordance with regulation 89) the 26 GHz lot second period licence granted to that bidder shall authorise the use of the frequencies assigned.
