# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Particular frequencies to be included in a 40 GHz licence granted to a winning bidder

Where a bidder was assigned 40 GHz lots under step one (in accordance with regulation 73) the 40 GHz licence granted to that bidder shall authorise the use of the frequencies assigned.
