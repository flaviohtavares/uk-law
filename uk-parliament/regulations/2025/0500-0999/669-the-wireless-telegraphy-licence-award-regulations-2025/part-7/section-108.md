# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 108

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 108. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 26 GHz lot second period licence

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 26 GHz lot second period licence shall be an amount equal to that bidder’s 26 GHz lot second period additional price (if any).
