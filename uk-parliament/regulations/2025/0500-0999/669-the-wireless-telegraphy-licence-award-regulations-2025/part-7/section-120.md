# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Completion of the award process

OFCOM shall complete the award process by publishing on OFCOM’s website—

- (a) the names of the winning bidders to whom licences were granted, and, in relation to each of them—
  - (i) the frequencies in respect of which the licences were granted; and
  - (ii) the licence fees paid;
- (b) the names of any winning bidders who, following the passing of the deadline under regulation 115, had an amount on deposit which is less than the total auction sum payable by them to OFCOM, and, in relation to each of them—
  - (i) the frequencies in respect of which, if they held on deposit an amount equal to the total auction sum, licences would have been granted; and
  - (ii) the total auction sum payable to OFCOM;
- (c) details of all valid principal stage bids;
- (d) details of all valid assignment stage bids for 40 GHz lots, 26 GHz lower lots, 26 GHz upper lots and 26 GHz second period lots; and
- (e) details of any lot assignments made without bidding.
