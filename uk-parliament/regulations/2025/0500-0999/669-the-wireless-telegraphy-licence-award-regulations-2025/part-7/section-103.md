# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Determination of licences, licence fees and frequencies associated with particular licences

After notification of the results of the assignment stage under regulation 101 OFCOM shall determine—

- (a) the licences which may be granted; and
- (b) for each licence—
  - (i) the licence fee payable to OFCOM; and
  - (ii) the particular frequencies.
