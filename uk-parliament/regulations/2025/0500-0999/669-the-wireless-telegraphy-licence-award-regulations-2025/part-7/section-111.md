# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Particular frequencies to be included in a 26 GHz upper lot first period licence granted to a winning bidder

Where a bidder was assigned 26 GHz upper lots under step two (in accordance with regulation 83) the 26 GHz upper lot first period licence granted to that bidder shall authorise the use of the frequencies assigned.
