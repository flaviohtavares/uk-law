# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Particular frequencies to be included in a 26 GHz lower lot first period licence granted to a winning bidder

Where a bidder was assigned 26 GHz lower lots under step one (in accordance with regulation 78) the 26 GHz lower lot first period licence granted to that bidder shall authorise the use of the frequencies assigned.
