# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 26 GHz upper lot first period licence

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 26 GHz upper lot first period licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder’s total 26 GHz upper base price; and
- (b) that bidder’s 26 GHz upper lot first period additional price (if any).
