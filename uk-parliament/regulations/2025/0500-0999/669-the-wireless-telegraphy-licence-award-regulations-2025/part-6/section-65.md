# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. No assignment stage if there are no winning principal stage bidders

Where there are no winning bidders following completion of the principal stage, there shall be no assignment stage and no licences shall be granted.
