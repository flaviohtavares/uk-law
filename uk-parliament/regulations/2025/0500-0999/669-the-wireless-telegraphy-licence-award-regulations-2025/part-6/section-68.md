# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Bidders

(1) Only 26 GHz lower lot winners and 40 GHz lot winners may bid in the first assignment stage round.

(2) Only 26 GHz upper lot winners may bid in the second assignment stage round.

(3) Only 26 GHz double lot winners may bid in the third assignment stage round.
