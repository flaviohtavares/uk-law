# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Determination of 40 GHz lot additional prices

In respect of each winning 40 GHz assignment stage bid, OFCOM shall determine an amount in whole pounds (“40 GHz lot additional price”) which shall be payable by the relevant winning bidder in accordance with Schedule 4.
