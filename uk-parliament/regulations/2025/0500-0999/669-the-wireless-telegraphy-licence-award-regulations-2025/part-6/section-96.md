# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Alternative method for submission of assignment stage form

(1) Where OFCOM are satisfied that a bidder is unable to submit an assignment stage form by using the auction system because of technical failure (or an event or circumstance with similar effect on the bidder’s ability to use the auction system), OFCOM shall notify the bidder of an alternative method of submitting the assignment stage form and any requirements relating to the authentication of communications made by means of the alternative method.

(2) The bidder must submit the assignment stage form by means of the alternative method and must comply with any requirements which have been notified to it under paragraph (1) relating to the authentication of communications.
