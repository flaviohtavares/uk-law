# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Valid assignment stage form

An assignment stage form is a valid assignment stage form if—

- (a) the bidder has complied with regulations 72(1) and 72(2), regulations 77(1) and 77(2), regulations 82(1) and 82(2) or regulations 88(1) and 88(2) as the case may be in respect of the assignment stage bids specified on the form; and
- (b) the bidder has submitted the form in accordance with regulation 93 or 96.
