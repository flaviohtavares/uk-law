# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Assignment stage

Where there is one winning bidder, or more than one winning bidder, following completion of the principal stage, OFCOM shall determine the assignment of particular numbered lots following the assignment stage process.
