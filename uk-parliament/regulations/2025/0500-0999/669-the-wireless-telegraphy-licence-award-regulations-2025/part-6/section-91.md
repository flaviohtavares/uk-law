# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Notification to bidders before each assignment stage round

Before the start of each assignment stage round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder eligible to make an assignment stage bid in that assignment stage round of—

- (a) the assignment stage options that are available to that bidder in that assignment stage round; and
- (b) the date on which, and the times on that date within which, the assignment stage round will begin and end.
