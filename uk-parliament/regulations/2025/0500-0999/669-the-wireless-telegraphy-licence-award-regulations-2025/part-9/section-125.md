# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2025, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. General power of exclusion

OFCOM may, at any time, notify any applicant or bidder of its exclusion from the award process, if required to do so by any direction given by the Secretary of State to OFCOM under section 5 of the Communications Act 2003[^f00005] or if, after OFCOM’s determination under regulation 8(1) (and notwithstanding that determination), in their opinion the applicant or bidder is not a fit and proper person to hold a licence.
