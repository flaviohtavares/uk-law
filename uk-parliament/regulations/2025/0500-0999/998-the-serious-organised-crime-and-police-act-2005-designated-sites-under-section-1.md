# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

4th September 2025

Laid before Parliament

8th September 2025

Coming into force

29th September 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 128(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 29th September 2025.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007

(1) The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(1)(g), for “(16)” substitute “(17)”.

(3) After article 2(16) insert—

> (17) So much of the site including Forest Lodge, Windsor Great Park, Windsor, Berkshire, SL4 2BU as is within the dotted black line in bold that is marked on the map in Schedule 16 (the Forest Lodge site).

.

(4) After Schedule 15 insert—

> **Schedule 16 — The Forest Lodge site**
>
> article 2(17)

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007 (“**the 2007 Order**”). The 2007 Order designates certain sites under section 128 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, which makes it an offence for a person to enter those sites as a trespasser.

Article 2 of this Order designates a new site, Forest Lodge, by amending the 2007 Order.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
