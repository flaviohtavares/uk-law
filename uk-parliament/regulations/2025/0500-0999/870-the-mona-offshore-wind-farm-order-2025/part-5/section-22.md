> Text as at 2025-10-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^c-d0925f147106195b1892e29191e506e9]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00035] are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”.
