# The M5 Junction 10 Development Consent Order 2025, section 22

> Text as at 2025-11-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-6552eddaf5bb2b05691b28d9e3e6dbc0]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00053] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
