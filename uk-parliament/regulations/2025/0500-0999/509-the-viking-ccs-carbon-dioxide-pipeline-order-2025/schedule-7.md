culverts, including by way of damming and overpumping within that part of the land over which the new rights are acquired;<br>- (h) alter, re-lay, maintain, adjust or remove existing pipes, cables or conduits or service media and associated apparatus (including apparatus of statutory undertakers) within that part of the land over which the new rights are acquired;<br>- (i) install, construct, electricity, communications, carbon dioxide, gas, oil, water supply, sewerage and drainage (including apparatus of statutory undertakers) within that part of the land over which the new rights are acquired;<br>- (j) erect, maintain and remove temporary fencing for site safety and to create, retain and remove temporary secure works compounds within that part of the land over which the new rights the prior erection of any temporary stock proof fencing as is reasonably required and the replacement, replanting or re-instatement of fences, hedges or other barriers following the exercise of the new rights);<br>- (l) install, maintain, use, inspect, modify, improve, adjust, repair, replace, extend, test, cleanse and remove temporary and permanent drainage;<br>- (m) carry out environmental surveys, mitigation works, maintenance and enhancement works within that part of the land over which the new rights are acquired;<br>- (n) enter for the purposes of intrusive surveys including for to be unreasonably withheld or delayed if the proposed surfacing would not cause damage to relevant part of the authorised development nor make it materially more difficult or expensive to maintain the authorised development);<br>- (u) prevent anything to be done by way of mole draining or excavation of any kind in the Land nor any activities which would alter, increase or decrease ground cover purposes of arable farming);<br>- (v) prevent the planting or growing within the Land of any trees, shrubs or underwood without the consent in writing of the undertaker (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed provided that the proposed trees, shrubs or underwood would not cause damage to the relevant part of the authorised development nor make it materially more difficult or expensive to access the relevant part of the authorised development);<br>- (w) prevent, without the written consent of the undertaker, the carrying out operations or actions (including but not limited to blasting and piling) which may obstruct, interrupt, or interfere with the exercise of the rights or damage the authorised development; and<br>- (x) prevent any activity which would in the reasonable opinion of the undertaker result in the disturbance of the drainage works, ecological mitigation areas or areas of habitat creation including any ploughing or grazing without the prior written consent of the undertaker |
