# The Tuberculosis in Animals (England) (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 748

Animals, England

The Tuberculosis in Animals (England) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

24th June 2025

Coming into force

15th July 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 8, 15(4), and 32(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Tuberculosis in Animals (England) (Amendment) Order 2025 and comes into force on 15th July 2025.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation (England) Order 2017

(1) The Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation (England) Order 2017[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit article 3 (power to slaughter animals affected with tuberculosis).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Tuberculosis in Animals (England) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/1001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1001)) (“**the Principal Order**”) to—

- (a) amend the Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1254](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1254)) to remove the application of the power in section 32 of the Animal Health Act 1981 (for the Secretary of State to slaughter any animal affected, or suspected of being affected by tuberculosis) and reapply the power in the Principal Order (*article 3(3)*);
- (b) remove a reference to direct payment entitlements payable under the former European Union Common Agricultural Policy (*article 3(4)*);
- (c) widen the scope of the power of the Secretary of State to publish information provided it is for the purpose of helping other persons protect against the spread of tuberculosis (*article 3(5)*);
- (d) add certain parishes to Schedule 3 (*article 3(6)*).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact, or no significant impact, on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
