# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 22.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2025/909
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22.1. When this Part applies[^key-8ac868d113c89792f586999251a5d089]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part applies in a magistrates’ court and in the Crown Court where—

- (a) section 41 of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999[^f00489] prohibits the introduction of evidence or cross-examination about any sexual behaviour of the complainant of a sexual offence; and
- (b) despite that prohibition, a defendant wants to introduce such evidence or to cross-examine a witness about such behaviour.

*\[Note. Section 41 of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 prohibits evidence or cross-examination about the sexual behaviour of a complainant of a sexual offence, subject to exceptions.*

*See also*—

- (a) *section 42 of the 1999 Act*[^f00490]*, which among other things defines ‘sexual behaviour’ and ‘sexual offence’;*
- (b) *section 34, which prohibits cross-examination by a defendant in person of the complainant of a sexual offence (Part 23 contains relevant rules).\]*
