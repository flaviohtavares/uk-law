## 18.16. Duty of court officer to notify the Director of Public Prosecutions[^c-788a6283838b2292dbe28d1c2a92cbd4]

*Extent: E+W*

The court officer must notify the Director of Public Prosecutions of an application, unless the prosecutor is, or acts on behalf of, a public authority.
