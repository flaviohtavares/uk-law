## 18.6. Court’s power to vary requirements under this Part[^key-df8872f60aecc267e2ae1f9f2130fe58]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may—

- (a) shorten or extend (even after it has expired) a time limit under this Part; and
- (b) allow an application or representations to be made in a different form to one issued under the Practice Direction, or to be made orally.

(2) A person who wants an extension of time must—

- (a) apply when serving the application or representations for which it is needed; and
- (b) explain the delay.
