## 35.4. Duty of justices’ legal adviser[^c-4b99b9bb4d597d3d4b6180308b93908e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies—

- (a) only in a magistrates’ court; and
- (b) unless the court—
  - (i) includes a District Judge (Magistrates’ Courts), and
  - (ii) otherwise directs.

(2) A justices’ legal adviser must assist the court by—

- (a) preparing and amending the draft case; and
- (b) completing the case stated.

*\[Note. For the functions of a justices’ legal adviser, see sections 28 and 29 of the Courts Act 2003*[^f00905]*. See also rule 2.12 (Duties of justices’ legal adviser).\]*
