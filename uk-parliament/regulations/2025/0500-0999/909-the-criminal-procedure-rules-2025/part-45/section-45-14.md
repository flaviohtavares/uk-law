## 45.14. Application for an extension of time[^key-dae81bb659143c6ba79839dd66a08bfa]

*Extent: E+W*

A party who wants an extension of time under rule 45.11, 45.12 or 45.13 must—

- (a) apply in writing;
- (b) explain the delay; and
- (c) attach the application, representations or appeal for which the extension of time is needed.
