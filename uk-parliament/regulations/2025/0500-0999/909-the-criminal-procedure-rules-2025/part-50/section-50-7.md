## 50.7. Discharge where warrant withdrawn[^c-4b7b2db0ccaba0d1d4bff3593d40ba13]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the authority that certified the warrant gives the court officer notice that the warrant has been withdrawn—

- (a) after the start of the hearing under rule 50.5; and
- (b) before the court orders the defendant’s extradition or discharge.

(2) The court must exercise its power to discharge the defendant.

*\[Note. See section 41 of the Extradition Act 2003.\]*
