## 3.18. Use of Welsh language at magistrates’ court trial[^key-312c4223e0140c9492a157aa85b3f7e0]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a magistrates’ court trial takes place in Wales—

- (a) any party or witness may use the Welsh language; and
- (b) if practicable, at least one member of the court must be Welsh-speaking.

*\[Note. See section 3 of the Courts Act 2003*[^f00122]*and section 22 of the Welsh Language Act 1993*[^f00123]*.\]*
