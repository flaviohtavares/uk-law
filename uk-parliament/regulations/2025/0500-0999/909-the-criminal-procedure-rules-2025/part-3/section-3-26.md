## 3.26. Commencement of preparatory hearing[^key-0915e3ca3465aa1a15460149f583fed8]

*Extent: E+W*

At the beginning of a preparatory hearing, the court must—

- (a) announce that it is such a hearing; and
- (b) take the defendant’s plea under rule 3.32 (Arraigning the defendant on the indictment), unless already done.

*\[Note. See section 8 of the Criminal Justice Act 1987*[^f00133] *and section 30 of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996*[^f00134]*.\]*
