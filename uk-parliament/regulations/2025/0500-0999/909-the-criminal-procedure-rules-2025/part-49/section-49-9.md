## 49.9. Overseas record[^c-d35e68ff250885c52f9347f8ede4beca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The overseas records of a magistrates’ court shall be part of the register (within the meaning of section 150(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980[^f01325]).

(2) The overseas records of any court shall not be open to inspection by any person except—

- (a) as authorised by the Secretary of State; or
- (b) with the leave of the court.

*\[Note. As to the making of court records, see rule 5.4.\]*
