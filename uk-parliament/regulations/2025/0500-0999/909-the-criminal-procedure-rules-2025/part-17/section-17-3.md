# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 17.3

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17.3. Application for summons, warrant or order: general rules[^key-413cf90a0d358f85cd026d134bbeaa5c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party who wants the court to issue a witness summons, warrant or order must apply as soon as practicable after becoming aware of the grounds for doing so.

(2) A party applying for a witness summons or warrant must—

- (a) identify the proposed witness;
- (b) explain—
  - (i) what evidence the proposed witness can give or produce,
  - (ii) why it is likely to be material evidence, and
  - (iii) why it would be in the interests of justice to issue a summons or warrant, as appropriate; and
- (c) in an application by the prosecutor for a summons or warrant to be issued under paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998—
  - (i) specify the date by which the prosecutor must serve the prosecution evidence in the Crown Court, and
  - (ii) if applicable, describe such steps as have been taken to apply for an extension or further extension of that period.

(3) A party applying for an order to be allowed to inspect and copy an entry in bank records must—

- (a) identify the entry;
- (b) explain the purpose for which the entry is required; and
- (c) propose—
  - (i) the terms of the order, and
  - (ii) the period within which the order should take effect, if 3 days from the date of service of the order would not be appropriate.

(4) The application may be made orally unless—

- (a) paragraph (3) of this rule applies (bank records);
- (b) rule 17.5 applies (Application for summons to produce a document, etc.: special rules); or
- (c) the court otherwise directs.

(5) The applicant must—

- (a) serve any order made on the witness to whom, or the bank to which, it is directed; and
- (b) notify the witness of the right to take legal advice.

*\[Note. The court may issue a warrant for a witness’ arrest if that witness fails to obey a witness summons: see section 97(3) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 3 to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 and section 4 of the Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act 1965. Before a magistrates’ court may issue a warrant under section 97(3) of the 1980 Act, the witness must first be paid or offered a reasonable amount for costs and expenses.\]*
