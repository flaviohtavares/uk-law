## 34.10. Court’s power to vary requirements under this Part[^key-28576aed8d148482fb4091c31ac86a84]

*Extent: E+W*

The Crown Court may—

- (a) shorten or extend (even after it has expired) a time limit under this Part;
- (b) allow an appellant to vary an appeal notice that that appellant has served;
- (c) direct that an appeal notice be served on any person; and
- (d) allow an appeal notice or a notice of abandonment to be in a different form to one issued under the Practice Direction, or to be presented orally.
