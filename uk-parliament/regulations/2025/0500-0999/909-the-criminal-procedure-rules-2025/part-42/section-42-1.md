## 42.1. Extension of time[^c-e4bf78c16ca804c8c0a546f82abd82e8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application to extend the time limit for giving notice of application for permission to appeal under Part 2 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00988] must—

- (a) be included in the notice of appeal; and
- (b) state the grounds for the application.

(2) The parties may not agree to extend any date or time limit set by this Part or by the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) Order 2003[^f00989].
