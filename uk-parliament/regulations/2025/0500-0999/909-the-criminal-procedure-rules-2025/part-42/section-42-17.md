## 42.17. Amendment and abandonment of appeal[^key-25ca21901ddf2dfd0d2b36005608dffe]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appellant may amend a notice of appeal served under rule 42.15 or abandon an appeal under section 13B, 43 or 65 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002—

- (a) without the permission of the court at any time before the Court of Appeal has begun hearing the appeal; and
- (b) with the permission of the court after the Court of Appeal has begun hearing the appeal,

by serving notice in writing on the Registrar.

(2) Where the appellant serves a notice under paragraph (1), the appellant must send a copy of it to each respondent.
