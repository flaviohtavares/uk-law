# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 31.12

## 31.12. Court’s power to vary requirements under this Part[^c-93fde8aa92cd92c55c615de5da518a03]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless other legislation otherwise provides, the court may—

- (a) shorten a time limit or extend it (even after it has expired);
- (b) allow a notice or application to be given in a different form, or presented orally; and
- (c) dispense with a requirement for service (even after service was required).
