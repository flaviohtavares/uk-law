## 14.3. Duty of justices’ legal adviser[^c-b6e00618bd8f1ed8f3d2fc1fc6204d0a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies—

- (a) only in a magistrates’ court; and
- (b) unless the court—
  - (i) includes a District Judge (Magistrates’ Courts), and
  - (ii) otherwise directs.

(2) A justices’ legal adviser must ask such questions as may be needed to obtain information sufficient to allow the court to make such decisions as are required.

*\[Note. For the functions of a justices’ legal adviser, see sections 28 and 29 of the Courts Act 2003*[^f00349]*. See also rule 2.12 (Duties of justices’ legal adviser).\]*
