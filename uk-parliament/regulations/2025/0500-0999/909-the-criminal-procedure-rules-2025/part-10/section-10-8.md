## 10.8. Draft indictment served by the prosecutor at the direction of the Court of Appeal[^key-cb529d8ae1b19b47aa7db279093878dc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the Court of Appeal orders a retrial.

(2) The prosecutor must serve a draft indictment on the Crown Court officer not more than 20 business days[^key-75533cf02986e5a4122c063a573d56c8] after that order.
