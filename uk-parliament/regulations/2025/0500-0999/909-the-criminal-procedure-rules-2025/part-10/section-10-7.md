## 10.7. Draft indictment served by the prosecutor on re-instituting proceedings[^key-65a28aad4eddef584bf646f30af3c948]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the prosecutor wants to re-institute proceedings in the Crown Court under section 22B of the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985.

(2) The prosecutor must serve a draft indictment on the Crown Court officer not more than 3 months after the proceedings were stayed under section 22(4) of that Act[^f00308].
