## 47.1. When this Part applies[^key-ba81d110cda307da914da653ea6c5063]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part applies to the exercise of the powers listed in each of rules 47.4, 47.24, 47.35, 47.42, 47.46, 47.51, 47.54, 47.59 and 47.63.
