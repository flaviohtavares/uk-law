## 13.7. Warrant issued when the court office is closed[^c-49160d61c58633bef3755619707b93ea]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the court issues a warrant when the court office is closed.

(2) The applicant for the warrant must, not more than 72 hours later, serve on the court officer—

- (a) a copy of the warrant; and
- (b) any written material that was submitted to the court.
