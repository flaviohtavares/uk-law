## 13.6. Warrants that cease to have effect on payment[^c-82f438ababed1ca05f8ae1517c08749f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to a warrant issued by a magistrates’ court under any of the following provisions of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980—

- (a) section 76[^f00335] (enforcement of sums adjudged to be paid);
- (b) section 83[^f00336] (process for securing attendance of offender);
- (c) section 86[^f00337] (power of magistrates’ court to fix day for appearance of offender at means inquiry, etc.); or
- (d) section 136[^f00338] (committal to custody overnight at police station for non-payment of sum adjudged by conviction).

(2) The warrant no longer has effect if—

- (a) the sum in respect of which the warrant was issued is paid to the person executing it;
- (b) that sum is offered to, but refused by, that person; or
- (c) that person is shown a receipt for that sum given by—
  - (i) the court officer, or
  - (ii) the authority to which that sum is due.

*\[Note. See sections 79*[^f00339] *and 125(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980.\]*
