# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 13.1

## 13.1. When this Part applies[^key-4f1a6a490072a90913d475a33236aa73]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies where the court can issue a warrant for arrest, detention or imprisonment.

(2) In this Part, ‘defendant’ means anyone against whom such a warrant is issued.
