## 16.3. Reference to exhibit[^key-a3f0199b766a5f8000cd29c3cf68281b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the statement refers to a document or object as an exhibit, it must identify that document or object clearly.

*\[Note. See section 9(7) of the Criminal Justice Act 1967*[^f00420]*.\]*
