## 48.17. Court’s power to vary requirements[^key-89e96312cfc86de5bd4b8f0792504952]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may shorten or extend (even after it has expired) a time limit under rule 48.11, 48.14 or 48.15.

(2) A person who wants an extension of time must—

- (a) apply when serving the statement, notice or application for which it is needed; and
- (b) explain the delay.
