## 48.13. Content of notice of other hearsay[^c-24556687d7e129491d3a508d7a5e2fcb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to a notice of hearsay, other than a written witness statement, served under rule 48.11.

(2) Such a notice must—

- (a) set out the evidence, or attach the document that contains it; and
- (b) identify the person who made the statement that is hearsay.
