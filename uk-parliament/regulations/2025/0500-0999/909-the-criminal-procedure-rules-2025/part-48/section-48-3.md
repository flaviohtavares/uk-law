## 48.3. Notice of suspension of imprisonment by Court of Appeal or Crown Court[^c-d1966c283eb99fbf6974ab7e53e74a71]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) the Court of Appeal or the Crown Court suspends an order of imprisonment for contempt of court; and
- (b) the respondent is absent when the court does so.

(2) The respondent must be served with notice of the terms of the court’s order—

- (a) by any applicant under rule 48.9; or
- (b) by the court officer, in any other case.

*\[Note. By reason of sections 15 and 45 of the Senior Courts Act 1981*[^f01272], *the Court of Appeal and the Crown Court each has an inherent power to suspend imprisonment for contempt of court, on conditions, or for a period, or both.\]*
