# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 48.16

## 48.16. Magistrates’ courts’ powers to adjourn, etc.[^key-2687f399c2ccc4d90f56085af34a6546]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where a magistrates’ court deals with unauthorised disclosure of prosecution material under sections 17 and 18 of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996[^f01313].

(2) The sections of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 listed in paragraph (3) apply as if in those sections—

- (a) ‘complaint’ and ‘summons’ each referred to an application or written statement under rule 48.9;
- (b) ‘complainant’ meant an applicant; and
- (c) ‘defendant’ meant the respondent.

(3) Those sections are—

- (a) section 51[^f01314] (issue of summons on complaint);
- (b) section 54[^f01315] (adjournment);
- (c) section 55[^f01316] (non-appearance of defendant);
- (d) section 97(1)[^f01317] (summons to witness);
- (e) section 121(1)[^f01318] (constitution and place of sitting of court); and
- (f) section 123[^f01319] (defect in process).

(4) Section 127 of the 1980 Act[^f01320] (limitation of time) does not apply.

*\[Note. Under section 19(3) of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996*[^f01321]*, Criminal Procedure Rules may contain provisions equivalent to those contained in Schedule 3 to the Contempt of Court Act 1981*[^f01322] *(which allows magistrates’ courts in cases of contempt of court to use certain powers such courts possess in other cases).\]*
