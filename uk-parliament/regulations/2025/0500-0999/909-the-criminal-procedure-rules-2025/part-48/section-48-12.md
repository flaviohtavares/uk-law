## 48.12. Content of written witness statement[^key-6de49490dcc32a5516dfeb710a0d0d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to a written witness statement served under rule 48.11.

(2) Such a written witness statement must contain a declaration by the person making it that it is true to the best of that person’s knowledge and belief.

*\[Note. By reason of sections 15 and 45 of the Senior Courts Act 1981*[^f01307]*, the Court of Appeal and the Crown Court each has an inherent power to imprison (for a maximum of 2 years), or fine (to an unlimited amount), or both, for contempt of court a person who, in a written witness statement to which this rule applies, makes, or causes to be made, a false statement without an honest belief in its truth. See also section 14 of the Contempt of Court Act 1981*[^f01308]*.\]*
