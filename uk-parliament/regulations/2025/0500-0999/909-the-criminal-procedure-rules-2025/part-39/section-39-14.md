## 39.14. Renewal or setting aside of order for retrial[^key-109c20282f88680870d1d57453f205fc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) a prosecutor wants a defendant to be arraigned more than 2 months after the court ordered a retrial under section 7 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968[^f00977]; or
- (b) a defendant wants such an order set aside after 2 months have passed since it was made.

(2) That party must apply in writing, with reasons, and serve the application on—

- (a) the Registrar; and
- (b) the other party.

*\[Note. Section 8(1) and (1A) of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968*[^f00978] *set out the criteria for making an order on an application to which this rule applies.\]*
