## 39.5. Reference by Criminal Cases Review Commission[^key-85e5cc9d8ff2a374b6f1601cf0a1c996]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Registrar must serve on the appellant a reference by the Criminal Cases Review Commission.

(2) The court must treat that reference as the appeal notice if the appellant does not serve such a notice under rule 39.2.
