## 33.37. Hearsay evidence[^key-7faca05103d0e85b1896fa23aaf96bb9]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 2(1) of the Civil Evidence Act 1995[^f00837] (duty to give notice of intention to rely on hearsay evidence) does not apply to evidence in restraint proceedings and receivership proceedings.
