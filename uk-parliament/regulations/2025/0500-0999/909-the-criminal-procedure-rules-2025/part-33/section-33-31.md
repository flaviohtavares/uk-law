## 33.31. Joining of applications[^key-ded4509b0caf4ef0a6da00e49de07926]

*Extent: E+W*

An application for the appointment of a management receiver or enforcement receiver under rule 33.50 may be joined with—

- (a) an application for a restraint order under rule 33.45; and
- (b) an application for the conferral of powers on the receiver under rule 33.51.
