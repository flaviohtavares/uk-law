# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 33.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2025/909
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33.1. Interpretation[^c-4070671aa0f951ae2da8e97112da21f8]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part words and expressions used have the same meaning as in Part 2 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and:

- ‘document’ means anything in which information of any description is recorded;
- ‘hearsay evidence’ means evidence consisting of hearsay within the meaning of section 1(2) of the Civil Evidence Act 1995[^f00785];
- ‘restraint proceedings’ means proceedings under sections 42 and 58(2) and (3) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00786];
- ‘receivership proceedings’ means proceedings under sections 48, 49, 50, 51, 54(4), 59(2) and (3), 62 and 63 of the 2002 Act[^f00787];
- ‘witness statement’ means a written statement signed by a person which contains the evidence, and only that evidence, which that person would be allowed to give orally.
