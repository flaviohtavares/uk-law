# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 15.9

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15.9. Court’s power to vary requirements under this Part[^c-71ba32c4d156e9692adcd501e17c5c2c]

*Extent: E+W*

The court may—

- (a) shorten or extend (even after it has expired) a time limit set by this Part;
- (b) extend (before it expires) a time limit to which rule 15.4 (Defence disclosure) refers, under regulation 3 of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 (Defence Disclosure Time Limits) Regulations 2011[^f00418];
- (c) allow a defence statement, or a defence witness notice, to be in a different written form to one issued under the Practice Direction, as long as it contains what the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 requires;
- (d) allow an application under this Part to be in a different form to one issued under the Practice Direction, or to be presented orally; and
- (e) specify the period within which—
  - (i) any application under this Part must be made, or
  - (ii) any material must be disclosed, on an application to which rule 15.5 applies (Defendant’s application for prosecution disclosure).
