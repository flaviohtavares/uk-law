## 37.6. Powers of Court of Appeal judge[^c-aabf707582cd40b205f433952c037a7e]

*Extent: E+W*

A judge of the Court of Appeal may give permission to appeal as well as exercising the powers given by other legislation (including these Rules).

*\[Note. See section 31 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968*[^f00926] *and section 49 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003*[^f00927]*.\]*
