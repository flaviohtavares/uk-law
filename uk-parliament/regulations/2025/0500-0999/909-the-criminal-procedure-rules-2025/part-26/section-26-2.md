## 26.2. Excusal from jury service by court[^key-f314c40db64e5cd96cb1c0db96bca9a3]

*Extent: E+W*

At any time before a juror completes the oath or affirmation, the court may exercise its power to excuse him or her from jury service for lack of capacity to act effectively as a juror because of an insufficient understanding of English—

- (a) on the court’s own initiative, or where the court officer refers the juror to the court; and
- (b) after enquiry of the juror.

*\[Note. See section 10 of the Juries Act 1974*[^f00625]*.\]*
