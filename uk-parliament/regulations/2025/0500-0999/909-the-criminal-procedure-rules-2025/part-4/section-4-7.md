## 4.7. Documents that must be served by specified methods[^c-ba09bf97789468ac438471303acc6880]

*Extent: E+W*

An application or written statement, and notice, under rule 48.9 alleging contempt of court may be served—

- (a) on an individual, only under rule 4.3(1)(a) (by handing it to him or her); and
- (b) on a corporation, only under rule 4.3(1)(b) (by handing it to a person holding a senior position in that corporation).
