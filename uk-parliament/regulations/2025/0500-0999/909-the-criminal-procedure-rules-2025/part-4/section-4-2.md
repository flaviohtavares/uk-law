## 4.2. Methods of service[^c-2ef3404c01d1eac158542630ffc17695]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A document may be served by any of the methods described in rules 4.3 to 4.6 (subject to rules 4.7 and 4.10), or in rule 4.8.

(2) Where a document may be served by electronic means under rule 4.6, the general rule is that the person serving it must use that method.
