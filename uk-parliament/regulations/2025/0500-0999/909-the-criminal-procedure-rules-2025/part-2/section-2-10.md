## 2.10. Court’s power to extend time under rule 2.6 or rule 2.7[^key-72b804ea0ffbf32f864a15fb1b9ac1fc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may extend (even after it has expired) a time limit under rule 2.6 (Exercise of functions of the High Court) or rule 2.7 (Exercise of functions of the Crown Court).

(2) A party who wants an extension of time must—

- (a) apply when serving the application for which it is needed; and
- (b) explain the delay.
