## 2.3. References to legislation, including these Rules[^key-64c6f5e4078a3c214bd53566ac4e74bc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Rules, where a rule refers to an Act of Parliament or to subordinate legislation by title and year, subsequent references to that Act or to that legislation in the rule are shortened: so, for example, after a reference to the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996[^f00050] that Act is called ‘the 1996 Act’; and after a reference to the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 (Defence Disclosure Time Limits) Regulations 2011[^f00051] those Regulations are called ‘the 2011 Regulations’.

(2) In the courts in which these Rules apply―

- (a) unless the context makes it clear that something different is meant, a reference to the Criminal Procedure Rules, without reference to a year, is a reference to the Criminal Procedure Rules in force at the date on which the event concerned occurs or occurred;
- (b) a reference to the Criminal Procedure Rules may be abbreviated to ‘CrimPR’; and
- (c) a reference to a Part or rule in the Criminal Procedure Rules may be abbreviated to, for example, ‘CrimPR Part 3’ or ‘CrimPR 3.5’.
