# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 21.6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21.6. Court’s power to vary requirements under this Part[^c-daf11afa6079fadfb47941e007394709]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may—

- (a) shorten or extend (even after it has expired) a time limit under this Part;
- (b) allow an application or notice to be in a different form to one issued under the Practice Direction, or to be made or given orally; and
- (c) dispense with a requirement for notice to introduce evidence of a defendant’s bad character.

(2) A party who wants an extension of time must—

- (a) apply when serving the application or notice for which it is needed; and
- (b) explain the delay.
