## 24.6. Evidence by admission[^c-b69ecb85c6386d47463d17b15e4fe7df]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) a party introduces in evidence a fact admitted by another party; or
- (b) parties jointly admit a fact.

(2) Unless the court otherwise directs, a written record must be made of the admission.

*\[Note. See section 10 of the Criminal Justice Act 1967*[^f00536]*. The admissibility of evidence that a party introduces is governed by rules of evidence.\]*
