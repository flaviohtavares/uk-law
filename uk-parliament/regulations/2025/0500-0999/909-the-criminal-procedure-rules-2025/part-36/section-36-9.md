## 36.9. Duty of person transcribing proceedings in the Crown Court[^key-b311acac257923bc9aec773a7a901a4b]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who transcribes a recording of proceedings in the Crown Court under arrangements made by the Crown Court officer must provide the Registrar with any transcript for which the Registrar asks, within such period as the Registrar may require.

*\[Note. See also section 32 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968*[^f00920] *and rule 5.5 (Recording and transcription of proceedings in the Crown Court).\]*
