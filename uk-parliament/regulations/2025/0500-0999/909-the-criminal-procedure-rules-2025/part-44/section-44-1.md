# The Criminal Procedure Rules 2025, section 44.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2025/909
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44.1. When this Part applies[^key-a93e0e67c9bfdf7045a83368db849ea3]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part applies in a magistrates’ court where—

- (a) under section 14 or section 16E of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980[^f01031], the defendant makes a statutory declaration of not having found out about the case until after the trial began;
- (b) under section 16M of the 1980 Act[^f01032] the court can set aside a conviction; or
- (c) under section 142 of the 1980 Act[^f01033], the court can—
  - (i) set aside a conviction, or
  - (ii) vary or rescind a costs order, or an order to which Part 31 applies (Behaviour orders).
