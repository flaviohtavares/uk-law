# The West Burton Solar Project (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 647

Infrastructure Planning

The West Burton Solar Project (Correction) Order 2025

Made

3rd June 2025

Coming into force

4th June 2025

The West Burton Solar Project Order 2025 (“**the Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Lincolnshire County Council, Nottinghamshire County Council, West Lindsey District Council and Bassetlaw District Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the West Burton Solar Project (Correction) Order 2025 and comes into force on 4th June 2025.

(2) In this Order, “**the 2025 Order**” means the West Burton Solar Project Order 2025[^f00005].

## 2. Corrections

(1) The 2025 Order is corrected in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) The Table in Schedule 1 (corrections) to this Order sets out the corrections, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

(3) For the table in Part 3 of Schedule 6 to the 2025 Order (temporary prohibition or restriction of the use of streets) substitute the table set out in Schedule 2 (table to be substituted for the table in Part 3 of Schedule 6 to the 2025 Order) to this Order.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery & Innovation

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

3rd June 2025
