# The Stonestreet Green Solar Order 2025, section 24

> Text as at 2026-02-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Time limit for exercise of authority to acquire land compulsorily[^key-f43faa10ad84111186d7786b6aa5f6a4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After the end of the period of five years beginning on the day on which this Order is made—

- (a) no notice to treat may be served under Part 1 (Compulsory Purchase under Acquisition of Land Act of 1946) of the 1965 Act; and
- (b) no declaration may be executed under section 4 (execution of declaration)[^f00041] of the 1981 Act as applied by article 27 (application of the 1981 Act),

in relation to any part of the Order land.
