# The Stonestreet Green Solar Order 2025, section 22

> Text as at 2026-02-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-ccb1f0819bfdcf99665e4bab22f40a22]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (Minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00040] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
