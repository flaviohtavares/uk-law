# The Oaklands Farm Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1070

INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING

The Oaklands Farm Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025

Made

8th October 2025

Coming into force

9th October 2025

The Oaklands Farm Solar Park Order 2025 (“**the 2025 Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”) contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period[^f00003], the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors in the 2025 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed each relevant local planning authority[^f00005] for the area in which the land to which the Order relates is situated that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Oaklands Farm Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025 and comes into force on 9th October 2025.

## 2. Corrections

The Oaklands Farm Solar Park Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Innovation

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero 

8th October 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Oaklands Farm Solar Park Order 2025 ([S.I. 2025/739](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/739)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
