# The A46 Newark Bypass Development Consent Order 2025, section 27

> Text as at 2026-01-12.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-a93706d83a60d16502400a1122873bfd]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00069] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
