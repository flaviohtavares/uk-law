# The A46 Newark Bypass Development Consent Order 2025, section 56

> Text as at 2026-01-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Arbitration[^key-60d6dbac8028e47f7eadb77bc73cd227]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except where otherwise expressly provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed between the parties, any difference under any provision of this Order (other than a difference which falls to be determined by the tribunal) must be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties, or failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.

(2) This article does not apply to any matter for which the consent or approval of the Secretary of State is required under any provision of this Order.
