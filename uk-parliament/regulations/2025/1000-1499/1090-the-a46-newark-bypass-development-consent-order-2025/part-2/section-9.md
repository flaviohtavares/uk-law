# The A46 Newark Bypass Development Consent Order 2025, section 9

> Text as at 2026-01-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Existing powers and duties of the undertaker[^c-815e863bdec0402f321d8495a5045660]

*Extent: E+W*

Except as expressly provided, nothing in this Order is to prejudice the operation of, and the powers and duties of the undertaker under, the 1980 Act, the 1991 Act and the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015[^f00037].
