# The Gatwick Airport (Northern Runway Project) Development Consent Order 2025, section 48

> Text as at 2026-01-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. No double recovery[^key-dea6cf909c74ef79b2dbe0118d9029f5]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation shall not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or deed or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
