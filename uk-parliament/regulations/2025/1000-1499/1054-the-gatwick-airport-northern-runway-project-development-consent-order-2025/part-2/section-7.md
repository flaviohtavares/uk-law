# The Gatwick Airport (Northern Runway Project) Development Consent Order 2025, section 7

> Text as at 2026-01-21.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Benefit of Order[^c-ff7c2dc10df8c29cc8392e68b739fa5c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to article 8 (consent to transfer benefit of Order), the provisions of this Order conferring powers on the undertaker have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to the works for which the consent is granted by this Order for the express benefit of owners and occupiers of land, statutory undertakers and other persons affected by the authorised development.
