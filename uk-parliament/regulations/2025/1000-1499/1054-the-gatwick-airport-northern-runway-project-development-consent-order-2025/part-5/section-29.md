## 29. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-77a622b6ead39f922c4eef172cbdaca9][^key-a863cc7db43fe81bb4f5685b4baaa40b]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00072] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
