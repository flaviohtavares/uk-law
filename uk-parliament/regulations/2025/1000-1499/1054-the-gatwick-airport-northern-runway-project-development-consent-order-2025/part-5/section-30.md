# The Gatwick Airport (Northern Runway Project) Development Consent Order 2025, section 30

> Text as at 2026-01-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Time limit for exercise of authority to acquire land compulsorily[^key-b726a03d8a49dc27d169c4936ab66d3a]

*Extent: E+W*

After the end of the period of seven years beginning on the start date—

- (a) no notice to treat is to be served under Part 1 (compulsory purchase under Acquisition of Land Act of 1946) of the 1965 Act; and
- (b) no declaration is to be executed under section 4 (execution of declaration) of the 1981 Act as applied by article 33 (application of the 1981 Act),

in relation to any part of the Order land.
