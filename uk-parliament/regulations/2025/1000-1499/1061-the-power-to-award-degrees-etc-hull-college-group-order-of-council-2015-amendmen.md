# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1061

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

1st October 2025

Coming into force

1st December 2025

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 45(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Order.

The Office for Students has requested advice from the relevant body regarding the quality of, and standards applied to, higher education provided by the provider, and has had regard to that advice in accordance with sections 46(1)(c) and (7) of that Act.

The Office for Students has complied with the procedure on giving notifications in accordance with section 48 of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 (Amendment) Order 2025 and comes into force on 1st December 2025.

## 2. Variation of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015

(1) The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) For article 2 substitute—

> **2.** Hull College Group shall be competent to grant awards of the kind mentioned in paragraph 2A of section 76 of the said Act for a fixed term beginning on 1st January 2016 and expiring at the end of the day on 30th March 2029.

.

Jean Arnold

Deputy Director of Quality

Office for Students

1st October 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015, as amended by the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 (Amendment) Order 2022 ([S.I. 2022/1205](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1205)), so that it remains valid until the end of the day on 30th March 2029. A copy of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Hull College Group) Order of Council 2015 can be requested from the Privy Council Office via the email address enquiries@pco.gov.uk.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
