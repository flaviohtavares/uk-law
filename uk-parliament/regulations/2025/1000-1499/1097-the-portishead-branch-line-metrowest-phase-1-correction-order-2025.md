# The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1097

Infrastructure Planning

The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) (Correction) Order 2025

Made

16th October 2025

Coming into force

17th October 2025

The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022 (“**the 2022 Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contained correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors and omissions in the 2022 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, The Secretary of State has informed the relevant local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the 2022 Order relates is situated, that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) (Correction) Order 2025 and comes into force on 17th October 2025.

## 2. Corrections to the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022

The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule of this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Marco Picardi

Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

Department for Transport

16th October 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
