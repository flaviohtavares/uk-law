# The Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Market Surveillance

(1) The Secretary of State may carry out market surveillance of equipment listed in Merchant Shipping Notice MSN 1874.

(2) In this regulation “**market surveillance**” means the measures set out in paragraph 6 of Merchant Shipping Notice MSN 1874.
