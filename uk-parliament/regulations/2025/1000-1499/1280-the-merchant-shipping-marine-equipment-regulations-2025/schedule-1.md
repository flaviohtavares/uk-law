### Part 2 — Revocations

#### 27. Revocation of regulations

The following instructions are revoked—

- (a) the Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2016[^f00043];
- (b) the Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00044]; and
- (c) the Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) (Amendment) (UK and US Mutual Recognition Agreement) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00045].
