# The Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Authorised Representatives

(1) Where a manufacturer is not located in the United Kingdom that manufacturer may, by a written mandate, appoint an authorised representative located in the UK.

(2) Where a manufacturer appoints an authorised representative, that representative must be authorised to carry out the manufacturer's obligations under regulation 18(1)(a) and 20(2).
