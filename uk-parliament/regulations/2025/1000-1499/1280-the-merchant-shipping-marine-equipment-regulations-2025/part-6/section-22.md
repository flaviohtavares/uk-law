# The Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Sample checks

Where equipment approved by an approved body under regulation 10 is made available on the market, but not yet placed on board a United Kingdom ship, the Secretary of State may carry out sample checks of that equipment to ensure it complies with the applicable international standards.
