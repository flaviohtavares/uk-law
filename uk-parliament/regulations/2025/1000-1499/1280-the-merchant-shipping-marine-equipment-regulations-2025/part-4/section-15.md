# The Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Application for type approval

(1) For equipment listed in Annex 2 of Merchant Shipping Notice MSN 1874, the manufacturer must apply to a nominated body for type approval in accordance with the procedures set out in paragraph 10 of Merchant Shipping Notice MSN 1874.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) must be—

- (a) in writing; and
- (b) accompanied by the documentation required by paragraph 10 of the Merchant Shipping Notice MSN 1874.
