# The Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Marine Equipment) Regulations 2025 and come into force 21 days after the day on which they are made.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
