# The M60/M62/M66 Simister Island Interchange Development Consent Order 2025, section 45

> Text as at 2025-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Disapplication and modification of legislative provisions[^c-d9d51899e6b245348dd8c3fe34d81a8b]

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of the Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017[^f00080], insofar as they relate to temporary possession of land under articles 30 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development) and 31 (temporary use of land for maintaining the authorised development) of this Order, do not apply in relation to the construction of any work or the carrying out of any operation required for the purpose of, or in connection with, the construction of the authorised development and, within the maintenance period defined in article 31(13), any maintenance of any part of the authorised development.
