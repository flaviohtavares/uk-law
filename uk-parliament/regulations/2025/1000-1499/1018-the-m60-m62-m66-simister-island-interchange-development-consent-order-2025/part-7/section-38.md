# The M60/M62/M66 Simister Island Interchange Development Consent Order 2025, section 38

> Text as at 2025-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^c-b48f04c683e91e87cf6a365a444e8841]

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as a specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act) of the 1990 Act.
