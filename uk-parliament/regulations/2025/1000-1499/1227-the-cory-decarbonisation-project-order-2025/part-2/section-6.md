# The Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025, section 6

> Text as at 2026-06-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Operation of the authorised development[^key-d8e1ca7e351af45d4da9a9fdf1db920a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to operate the authorised development.

(2) Other than as set out in this Order, this article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required to authorise the operation of the authorised development.
