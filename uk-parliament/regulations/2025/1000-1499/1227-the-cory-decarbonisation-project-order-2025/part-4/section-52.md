# The Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025, section 52

> Text as at 2026-06-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. No double recovery[^c-8be5b2acae3745cfe055f0b227e0257b]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
