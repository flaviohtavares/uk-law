# The Five Estuaries Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025, section 24

> Text as at 2026-10-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^key-23760cf9635eface3219066a36a44ee6]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00044] are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”; and
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”.
