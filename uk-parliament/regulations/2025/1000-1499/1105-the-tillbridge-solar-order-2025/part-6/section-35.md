# The Tillbridge Solar Order 2025, section 35

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Benefit of the Order[^c-9d2e3ab2573b0b0a712b710314a0fe1e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) and article 36 (consent to transfer the benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to Work No. 5 in respect of which the provisions of this Order are for the benefit of the undertaker and National Grid.
