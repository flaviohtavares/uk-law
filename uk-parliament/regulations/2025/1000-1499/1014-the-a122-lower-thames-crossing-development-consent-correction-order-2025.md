# The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1014

Infrastructure Planning

The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2025

Made

11th September 2025

Coming into force

12th September 2025

The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025[^f00001] (“**the 2025 Order**”) which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”), contained correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received written requests from the applicant[^f00003] and Kent Downs National Landscape Team for the correction of errors or omissions in the 2025 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State informed the relevant local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the 2025 Order relates is situated, that the requests had been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2025 and comes into force on 12th September 2025.

## 2. Corrections to the A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025 (“the 2025 Order”)

The 2025 Order is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Samantha Collins-Hill

A Senior Civil Servant in the

Department for Transport

11th September 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors and omissions in the A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025 ([S.I. 2025/462](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/462)), a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following two requests under paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
