# The Sanctions (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Overseas Territories) Order 2025, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendments to Overseas Territories Orders

(1) Schedule 2 to the Venezuela (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020[^f00002] (modifications to be made in the extension of the Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00003] to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1) is amended in accordance with Schedule 2.

(2) Schedule 2 to the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020[^f00004] (modifications to be made in the extension of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00005] to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1) is amended in accordance with Schedule 3.

(3) Schedule 2 to the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020[^f00006] (modifications to be made in the extension of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00007] to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1) is amended in accordance with Schedule 4.

(5) Schedule 2 to the Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (Overseas Territories) Order 2020[^f00010] (modifications to be made in the extension of the Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00011] to each British overseas territory listed in Schedule 1) is amended in accordance with Schedule 6.
