## 66. Interpretation of the Portsmouth (Camber Dock) Harbour Revision Order 1995

(1) The Portsmouth (Camber Dock) Harbour Revision Order 1995[^f00034] is to be construed as follows.

(2) References to “the Camber Dock undertaking” are to be construed as references to “**the Camber Undertaking**” as defined in article 2(1) of this Order.
