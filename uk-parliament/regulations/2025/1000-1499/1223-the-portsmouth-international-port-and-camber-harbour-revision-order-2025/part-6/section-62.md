# The Portsmouth International Port and Camber Harbour Revision Order 2025, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Amendment of the Portsmouth Camber Dock and Flathouse Wharf Order 1911

(1) The Portsmouth Camber Dock and Flathouse Wharf Order 1911[^f00031] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1(3) in the definition of “The Corporation” for “the Mayor Alderman and Burgesses of the borough of Portsmouth acting by the council of that borough” substitute “Portsmouth City Council”.

(3) Omit article 1(4).
