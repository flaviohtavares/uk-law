# The Portsmouth International Port and Camber Harbour Revision Order 2025, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Revocation / Repeal

The enactments mentioned in the first and second columns of Schedule 2 (revocation / repeal) are revoked or repealed (as appropriate) to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
