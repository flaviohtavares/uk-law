# The Portsmouth International Port and Camber Harbour Revision Order 2025, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — ACTS AND ORDERS

Article 2

### PART 1 – — THE CAMBER UNDERTAKING

**1.** Portsmouth Camber Dock and Flathouse Wharf Order 1911 (articles 1 and 3 only).

**2.** Portsmouth Corporation Act 1959 (insofar as it relates to the Camber undertaking).

**3.** The Portsmouth (Camber Dock) Harbour Revision Order 1995 (articles 1, 2 and 4 to 7 only).

**4.** Portsmouth (Millennium Waterbus Landing Stages) Harbour Revision Order 2000 (insofar as it relates to the Camber undertaking).

**5.** Portsmouth (Tall Ships Berths) Harbour Revision Order 2000.

### PART 2 – — THE PORTSMOUTH INTERNATIONAL PORT UNDERTAKING

**1.** Portsmouth Camber Dock and Flathouse Wharf Order 1911 (articles 1 and 3 only).

**2.** Portsmouth Corporation Act 1959 (insofar as it relates to the Portsmouth International Port undertaking).

**3.** The Portsmouth Mile End Quay Revision Order 1981 (articles 1(1), 2, 5, 6, 8 and 9 only).

**4.** The Portsmouth Flathouse Quay Harbour Revision Order 1990 (articles 1, 2 and 4 to 7 only).

**5.** The Portsmouth (Continental Ferry Port Berth 2 Extension) Harbour Revision Order 2011 (articles 1(1), 2 and 4 to 7 only).
