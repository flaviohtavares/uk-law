# The Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1341 (C. 74)

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2025

Made

15th December 2025

Coming into force

1st April 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 269(4) of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2025.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st April 2026

Section 246 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (recording and reporting the use of force in schools: England) comes into force on 1st April 2026.

Olivia Bailey

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department for Education

15th December 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 246 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 into force on 1st April 2026.

Section 246 inserts a new section 93A into the Education and Inspections Act 2006. The inserted section 93A requires the governing body of each school in England to ensure that their school has a procedure for recording significant incidents of the use of force on pupils by members of staff, and for reporting those incidents to the parents of pupils involved (unless informing a parent would put the pupil at risk).
