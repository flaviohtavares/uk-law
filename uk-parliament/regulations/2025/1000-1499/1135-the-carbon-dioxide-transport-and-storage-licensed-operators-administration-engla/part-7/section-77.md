# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Notice of no dividend, or no further dividend

(1) If the T&S administrator delivers notice to creditors that the T&S administrator is unable to declare any dividend or any further dividend (as the case may be), the notice must contain a statement to the effect either—

- (a) that no funds have been realised, or
- (b) that the funds realised have already been distributed or used or allocated for paying the expenses of T&S administration.

(2) The information required by paragraph (2) may be included in a progress report (see rule 26).
