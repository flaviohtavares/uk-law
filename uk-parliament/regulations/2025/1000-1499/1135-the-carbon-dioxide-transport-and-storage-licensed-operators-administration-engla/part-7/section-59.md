# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Debt payable at future time

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the T&S company entered T&S administration, subject to rule 82 (adjustment of dividend where payment made before time).
