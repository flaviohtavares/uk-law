# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Voluntary surrender of security

A creditor who voluntarily surrenders a security may prove for the whole of the creditor’s debt as if it were unsecured.
