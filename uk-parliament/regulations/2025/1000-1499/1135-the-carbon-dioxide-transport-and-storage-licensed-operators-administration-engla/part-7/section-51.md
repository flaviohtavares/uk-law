# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Withdrawal or variation of proof

(1) A creditor may withdraw a proof at any time by delivering a written notice to the T&S administrator.

(2) The amount claimed by a creditor’s proof may be varied at any time by agreement between the creditor and the T&S administrator.
