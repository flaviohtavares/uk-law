## 82. Adjustment where dividend paid before time

(1) Where a creditor has proved for a debt of which payment is not due at the date of the declaration of a dividend, the creditor is entitled to the dividend equally with other creditors, but subject as follows.

- (1) “*x*” is the value of the admitted proof, and
- (2) “*n*” is the period beginning with the date on which the T&S company entered T&S administration and ending with the date on which the payment of the creditor’s debt would otherwise be due expressed in years (part of a year being expressed as a decimal fraction of a year).
