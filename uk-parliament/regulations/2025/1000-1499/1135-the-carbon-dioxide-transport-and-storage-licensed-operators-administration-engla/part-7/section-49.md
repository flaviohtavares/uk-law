# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend

(1) The T&S administrator may admit or reject a proof for dividend, in whole or in part.

(2) If the T&S administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the T&S administrator must deliver to the creditor a statement of the T&S administrator’s reasons for doing so, as soon as reasonably practicable.
