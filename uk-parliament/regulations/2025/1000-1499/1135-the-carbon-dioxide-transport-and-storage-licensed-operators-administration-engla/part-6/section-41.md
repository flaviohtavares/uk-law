# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Expenses

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the T&S administration are to be regarded as expenses of the T&S administration.

(2) The expenses associated with the prescribed part must be paid out of the prescribed part.

(3) The cost of the security required by section 390(3) of the 1986 Act for the proper performance of the T&S administrator’s functions is an expense of the T&S administration.
