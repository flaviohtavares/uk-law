# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Notice to officers charged with distress or other legal process

The applicant must as soon as reasonably practicable after filing the application deliver a notice of it being made to—

- (a) any enforcement agent, enforcement officer or other officer who to the applicant’s knowledge is charged with distress or other legal process against the company or its property;
- (b) any person who to the applicant’s knowledge has distrained against the company or its property.
