# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Statement of affairs: filing, etc.

(1) The T&S administrator must as soon as reasonably practicable deliver to the registrar of companies a copy of the statement of affairs and any statement of concurrence (subject to rule 18).

(2) But the T&S administrator must not deliver to the registrar of companies the schedule to a statement of affairs required by rule 15(5)(b).
