# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Award of costs against T&S administrator

Without prejudice to any provision of the 1986 Act or these Rules by virtue of which the T&S administrator is not in any event to be liable for costs and expenses, where a T&S administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, the T&S administrator is not to be personally liable for the costs unless the court otherwise directs.
