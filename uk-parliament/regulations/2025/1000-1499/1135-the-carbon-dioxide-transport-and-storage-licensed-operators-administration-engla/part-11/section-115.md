# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Office copies of documents

(1) The court must provide an office copy of a document from the court file to a person who has under these Rules the right to inspect the court file where that person has requested such a copy and paid the fee under rule 114(9).

(2) A person’s right under this rule may be exercised on that person’s behalf by someone authorised to do so by that person.

(3) An office copy provided by the court under this rule must be in such form as the relevant Judge thinks appropriate, and must bear the court’s seal.

(4) In this rule, “**relevant Judge**” means the High Court Judge, Insolvency and Companies Court Judge or District Judge of the High Court before whom the proceedings are brought.
