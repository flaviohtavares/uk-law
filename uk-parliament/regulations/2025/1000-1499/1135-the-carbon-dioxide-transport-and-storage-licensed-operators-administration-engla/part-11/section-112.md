# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Orders enforcing compliance

(1) The court may, on an application by the T&S administrator, make such orders as it thinks necessary for the enforcement of obligations falling on any person in accordance with—

- (a) paragraph 47 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act (duty to submit statement of affairs), or
- (b) section 235 of the 1986 Act (duty to co-operate with T&S administrator).

(2) An order of the court under this rule may provide that all costs of and incidental to the application for it are to be borne by the person against whom the order is made.
