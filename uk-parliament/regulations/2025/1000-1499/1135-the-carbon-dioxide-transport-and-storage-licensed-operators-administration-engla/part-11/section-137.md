# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 137

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 137. Court orders

The court may make such other order or in such form as the court thinks just, despite any requirement in these Rules as to the contents of a court order.
