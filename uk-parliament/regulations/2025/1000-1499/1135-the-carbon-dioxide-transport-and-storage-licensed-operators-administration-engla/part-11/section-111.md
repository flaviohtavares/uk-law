# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Enforcement of court orders

In T&S administration proceedings, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
