# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 164

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 164. Omission of unobtainable information

Information required under this Chapter to be included in a notice to be gazetted may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
