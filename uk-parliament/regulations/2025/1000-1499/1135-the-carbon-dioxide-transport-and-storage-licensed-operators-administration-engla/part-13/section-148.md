# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Delivery of documents to joint T&S administrators

Delivery of a document to one of joint T&S administrators is to be treated as delivery to them all.
