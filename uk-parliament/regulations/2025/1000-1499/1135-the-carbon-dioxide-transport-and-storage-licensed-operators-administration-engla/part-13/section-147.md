# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 147. Delivery of documents to authorised recipients

Where under the 1986 Act or these Rules a document is to be delivered to a person, it may be delivered instead to any other person authorised in writing to accept delivery on behalf of the first-mentioned person.
