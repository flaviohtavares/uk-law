# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 179

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 179. Charges for copies of documents

Except where prohibited by these Rules, the T&S administrator is entitled to require the payment of the standard fee for copies of documents requested by a creditor, member or contributory.
