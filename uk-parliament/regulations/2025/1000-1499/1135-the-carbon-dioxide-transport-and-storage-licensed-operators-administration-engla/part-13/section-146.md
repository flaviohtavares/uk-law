# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 146

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 146. Delivery of documents by post (or document exchange)

A document is delivered if it is sent by post (or document exchange) in accordance with the rules for such service in CPR Part 6 and sending by such means has effect as specified in those rules.
