# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 159

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 159. Service of T&S administration application

(1) An application to the court for a T&S administration order must be served by delivering the documents as follows—

- (a) on the T&S company at its registered office;
- (b) on any other person at that person’s proper address.

(2) A person’s proper address is any which that person has previously notified as the address for service, but if the person has not notified such an address then the documents may be served at that person’s usual or last known address.
