# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Service on joint T&S administrators

Service of a document on one of joint T&S administrators is to be treated as service on all of them.
