# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 145

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 145. Personal delivery of documents

A document is delivered if it is personally delivered in accordance with the rules for personal service in CPR Part 6[^f00054].
