# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 178

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 178. Right to copies of documents

Where the 1986 Act or these Rules give a person the right to inspect documents, that person has a right to be supplied on request with copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court file, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003;
- (b) in any other case, of the standard fee.
