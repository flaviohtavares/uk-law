# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 169

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 169. Information to be contained in all documents delivered to the registrar

A document to be delivered to the registrar of companies must—

- (a) specify—
  - (i) the registered name of the T&S company;
  - (ii) its registered number;
  - (iii) the nature of the document;
  - (iv) the provision of the 1986 Act or the rule under which the document is delivered;
  - (v) the date of the document;
  - (vi) the name and postal address of the person delivering the document;
  - (vii) the capacity in which that person is acting in respect of the T&S company;
- (b) be authenticated by the person delivering the document.
