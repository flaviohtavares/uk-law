# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Creditors’ decisions

The T&S administrator may, and in the circumstances set out in rule 29 must, seek a decision from the T&S company’s creditors under paragraph 62 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act.
