# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Proxies and representatives

Part 16 of the Insolvency Rules applies in respect of the meeting of members as it applies to administration, with the following modifications—

- (a) for “administration”, in each place, substitute “T&S administration”;
- (b) for “company”, in each place, substitute “T&S company”;
- (c) in rule 16.6 the reference to rule 1.58 is a reference to rule 177 of these Rules.
