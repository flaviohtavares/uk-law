# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Application

This Chapter applies where the T&S administrator seeks a decision from creditors under paragraph 62 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act[^f00032].

## Annotations

[^f00032]: Footnote: Paragraph 62 was amended by the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, Schedule 9, paragraph 10.
