# The Carbon Dioxide Transport and Storage (Licensed Operators) Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2025, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Interpretation: final progress reports, etc.

In this Part—

- “**final progress report**” means a progress report which includes a summary of—
  - (a) the T&S administrator’s proposals,
  - (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals,
  - (c) the steps taken during the T&S administration, and
  - (d) the outcome;
- “**progress report**” means a report which complies with rule 26.
