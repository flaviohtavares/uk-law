# The Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1022

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

11th September 2025

Coming into force

13th October 2025

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and (8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

In accordance with section 25(3) of that Act, the Secretary of State has completed the consultation prescribed by regulations[^f00002] made under that section.

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 13th October 2025.

(3) In this Order “Establishment Order” means the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006[^f00003].

(4) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of interpretation provision

In article 1(2) of the Establishment Order (citation, commencement and interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definitions of “the Act”, “community health services” and “operational date”; and
- (b) in the definition of “the trust” for “Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust” substitute “Kent and Medway Mental Health National Health Service Trust”.

## 3. Change of name and savings

(1) In article 2 of the Establishment Order (establishment and name of the trust), for “Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust” substitute “Kent and Medway Mental Health National Health Service Trust”.

(2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) does not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) invalidate any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust, and all instruments and other documents which refer to that name, must be construed as referring to the Kent and Medway Mental Health National Health Service Trust.

## 4. Change to nature and functions of the trust

For article 3 of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust), and the heading to that article, substitute—

> **3. Functions of the trust**
>
> The trust’s functions are to provide goods and services for the purposes of the health service.

.

## 5. Removal of specification of “operational date”

For article 5 of the Establishment Order (operational date and accounting date of the trust), and the heading to that article, substitute—

> **5. Accounting date**
>
> The accounting date of the trust is 31st March.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Karin Smyth

Minister of State

Department of Health and Social Care

11th September 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order amends the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006 (“**the Establishment Order**”), which established the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (“**the trust**”).

Article 2 amends article 1 of the Establishment Order to omit definitions which are no longer needed and to update the definition of the trust in consequence of the amendments made by this Order.

Article 3 changes the name of the trust to “the Kent and Medway Mental Health National Health Service Trust” and provides savings in respect of the change of name.

Article 4 sets out the functions of the trust as being to provide goods and services for the purposes of the health service.

Article 5 removes the specification of the trust’s “operational date” as it is no longer required because the trust is already operational.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
