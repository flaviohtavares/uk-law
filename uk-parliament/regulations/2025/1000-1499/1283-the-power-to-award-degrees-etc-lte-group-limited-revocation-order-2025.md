# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (LTE Group Limited) (Revocation) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1283

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (LTE Group Limited) (Revocation) Order 2025

Made

8th December 2025

Coming into force

29th December 2025

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (LTE Group Limited) (Revocation) Order 2025 and comes into force on 29th December 2025.

## 2. Revocation

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (LTE Group Limited) Order 2025[^f00002] is revoked.

Jean Arnold

Deputy Director of Quality

Office for Students

8th December 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Power to Award Degrees etc. (LTE Group Limited) Order 2025 ([S.I. 2025/826](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/826)) which contained a drafting error naming the incorrect entity. A new Order naming the correct entity has been made ([S.I. 2025/1039](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1039)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
