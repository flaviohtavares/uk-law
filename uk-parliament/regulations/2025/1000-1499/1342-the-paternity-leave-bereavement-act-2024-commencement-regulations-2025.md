# The Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024 (Commencement) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1342 (C. 75)

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024 (Commencement) Regulations 2025

Made

15th December 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024 (Commencement) Regulations 2025.

## 2. Commencement of Act

The Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024 comes into force on 29th December 2025.

Kate Dearden

Minister for Employment Rights and Consumer Protection

Department for Business and Trade

15th December 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations commence the whole of the Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act [2024 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/17). The Paternity Leave (Bereavement) Act 2024 amends the Employment Rights Act [1996 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18) to make provision about paternity leave in cases where a mother, or a person with whom a child is placed or expected to be placed for adoption, dies.
