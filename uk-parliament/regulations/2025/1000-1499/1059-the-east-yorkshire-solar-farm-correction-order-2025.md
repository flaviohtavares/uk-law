# The East Yorkshire Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1059

Infrastructure Planning

The East Yorkshire Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2025

Made

19th September 2025

Coming into force

20th September 2025

The East Yorkshire Solar Farm Order 2025 (“**the Order**”)[^f00001], which was granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed East Riding of Yorkshire Council and North Yorkshire Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited at the East Yorkshire Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 20th September 2025.

## 2. Corrections

The East Yorkshire Solar Farm Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to the Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

(This note is not part of the Order)
