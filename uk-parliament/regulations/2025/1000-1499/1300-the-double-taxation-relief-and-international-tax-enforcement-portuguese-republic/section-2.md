# The Double Taxation Relief and International Tax Enforcement (Portuguese Republic) Order 2025, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Double taxation and international tax enforcement arrangements to have effect

It is declared that—

- (a) the arrangements specified in the Convention and Protocol set out in the Schedule to this Order have been made with the Government of the Portuguese Republic,
- (b) those arrangements have been made with a view to affording relief from double taxation in relation to income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, and taxes of a similar character imposed by the laws of the Portuguese Republic, and relate to international tax enforcement, and
- (c) it is expedient that those arrangements should have effect.
