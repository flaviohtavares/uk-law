# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Amendment of Schedule 2: scheme design requirement

In Schedule 2 (scheme design requirement)—

- (a) in paragraph 1 omit sub-paragraph (b);
- (b) in paragraph 9 omit sub-paragraph (a);
- (c) in paragraph 14, for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) when providing the viability certificate, the scheme actuary—
  >   - (i) has had regard to the matters specified in regulation 11(2), as applicable; and
  >   - (ii) is satisfied that the scheme has rules that meet the requirements of section 18 of the Act (calculation of benefits) and the requirements of regulation 17.
