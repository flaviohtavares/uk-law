# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 66

## 66. Amendment of regulation 10: viability report

In regulation 10(4) (viability report), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) the conclusions reached by the scheme actuary on—
>   - (i) the matters contained in regulation 11(2);
>   - (ii) whether the rules of the scheme meet the requirements of section 18 of the Act (calculation of benefits) and the requirements of regulation 17; and
