# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Amendment of Schedule 1: fit and proper persons requirement

(1) Schedule 1 (fit and proper persons requirement) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, in sub-paragraph (3) omit the definition of “the Accountant in Bankruptcy”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(v)—
  - (i) for “(including” substitute “or”;
  - (ii) at the end omit the closing bracket;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in paragraph (ii) omit “to the Accountant in Bankruptcy for sequestration”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (v)—
    - (aa) for “within the meaning given by”, in both places it occurs, substitute “made under”—
    - (bb) for “(including” substitute “or”;
    - (cc) at the end omit the closing bracket;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c)(v)—
  - (i) for “(including” substitute “or”;
  - (ii) at the end omit the closing bracket.
