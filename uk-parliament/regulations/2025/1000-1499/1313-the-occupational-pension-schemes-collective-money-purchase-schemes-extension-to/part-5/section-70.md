# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Amendment of regulation 19: actuarial valuations

In regulation 19(4) (actuarial valuations)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c) for “pensioner members and survivors” substitute “pension credit members and pensioner members, and of survivors”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d) after “deferred members,” insert “, pension credit members”.
