# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Application

After regulation 1 (citation, extent and commencement) insert—

> **1A. Application**
>
> (1) Parts 2 to 6 and Part 1 insofar as it relates to those Parts apply in relation to single or connected employer schemes[^f00051].
>
> (2) Part 7 and Part 1 insofar as it relates to Part 7 apply in relation to all collective money purchase schemes.

.

## Annotations

[^f00051]: Footnote: “Single or connected employer scheme” is defined for the purposes of Part 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 2021 in section 1(3) of that Act (inserted by regulation 3(1) of this instrument).
