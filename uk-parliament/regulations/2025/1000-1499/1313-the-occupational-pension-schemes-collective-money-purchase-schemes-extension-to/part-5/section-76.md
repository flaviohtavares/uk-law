# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Amendment of Schedule 5: systems and processes requirement

In Schedule 5 (systems and processes requirement)—

- (a) in paragraph 2(h), for “, the annual allowance and the lifetime allowance” substitute “and the annual allowance”;
- (b) in paragraph 12(a), for “policy” substitute “strategy”.
