# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Viability report: unconnected multiple employer schemes

In section 13 (viability report) after subsection (7) insert—

> (7A) Before providing a viability report to the Pensions Regulator in accordance with subsection (6)(a) the trustees of an unconnected multiple employer scheme must obtain the approval of the report by the scheme proprietor (see section 14B).
>
> (7B) The trustees of an unconnected multiple employer scheme must provide the scheme proprietor with any revised viability report—
>
> - (a) within seven days of the viability report being revised, and
> - (b) at any other time, on request from the scheme proprietor.
