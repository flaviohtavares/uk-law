# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Amendment of the Pensions Act 1995

In section 67A of the Pensions Act 1995 (the subsisting rights provisions: interpretation)[^f00061], in subsection (9)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (xv)[^f00062], after “sections” insert “31,”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (xv) insert—

    > - (xvi) regulations 40 and 56 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025 ([S.I. 2025/1313](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1313));

    ;
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (xii)[^f00063], after “18(7)(b),” insert “31(4A),”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (xii) insert—

    > - (xiii) regulation 40(1)(b) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025.

    .

## Annotations

[^f00061]: Footnote: Section 67A, together with other sections, was substituted for section 67 by section 262 of the Pensions Act 2004.

[^f00062]: Footnote: Sub-paragraph (xv) was inserted by section 24(1) and (8)(a) of the Pension Schemes Act 2021.

[^f00063]: Footnote: Sub-paragraph (xii) was inserted by section 24(1) and (8)(b) of the Pension Schemes Act 2021.
