# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Financial sustainability requirement

(1) Part 2 of Schedule 3 sets out matters that the Pensions Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 14 of the Act (financial sustainability requirement)[^f00031], whether it is satisfied that an unconnected multiple employer scheme is financially sustainable.

(2) Part 3 of Schedule 3 sets out the requirements to be met by an unconnected multiple employer scheme in relation to its financing for the purposes of section 14 of the Act.

## Annotations

[^f00031]: Footnote: Section 14 is amended by regulation 9 of this instrument.
