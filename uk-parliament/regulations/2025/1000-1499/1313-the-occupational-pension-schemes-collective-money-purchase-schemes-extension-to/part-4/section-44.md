# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2025, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Powers of the Pensions Regulator

A direction issued by the Pensions Regulator under section 23(2)(a) of the Act (powers of the Pensions Regulator) in relation to an unconnected multiple employer scheme must set out the matters that the Regulator has considered in determining to issue the direction.
