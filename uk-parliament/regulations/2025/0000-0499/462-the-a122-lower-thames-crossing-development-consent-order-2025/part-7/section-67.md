# The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025, section 67

> Text as at 2025-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. No double recovery[^key-b516f36f74f44f48cf0392d5d51860db]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation will not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more articles of this Order.
