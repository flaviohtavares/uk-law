# The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025, section 60

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Deemed marine licence[^c-34dec30346115e76ae6989c6e2a3342a]

*Extent: E+W*

The marine licence set out in Schedule 15 (deemed marine licence) is deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act for the licensed activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 2, of that licence.
