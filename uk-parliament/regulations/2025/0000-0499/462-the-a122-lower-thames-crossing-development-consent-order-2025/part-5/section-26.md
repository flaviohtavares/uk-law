# The A122 (Lower Thames Crossing) Development Consent Order 2025, section 26

> Text as at 2025-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-b8815a490470a1a4f7472b8753605d9e]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals)[^f00081] to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for the “acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this order”.
