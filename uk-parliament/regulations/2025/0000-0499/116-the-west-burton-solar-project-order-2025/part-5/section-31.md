# The West Burton Solar Project Order 2025, section 31

> Text as at 2025-06-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Statutory undertakers[^c-701125529b15f85e9fc61fc97961dada]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 15 (protective provisions) the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily, or acquire new rights or impose restrictive covenants over, the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plan within the Order land; and
- (b) extinguish the rights of, remove , relocate the rights of[^c-25732d3ef2a748fd4325f342c7ae00bd] or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers over or within the Order land.
