# The West Burton Solar Project Order 2025, section 21

> Text as at 2025-06-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-bb1d401157e706dbbeef3422601e87bc]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (c) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated.
