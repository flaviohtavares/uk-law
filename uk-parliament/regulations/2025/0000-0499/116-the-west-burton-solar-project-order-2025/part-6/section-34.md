# The West Burton Solar Project Order 2025, section 34

> Text as at 2025-06-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Benefit of the Order[^key-74c89f7e5d53bd79e142de193ffee277]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph ‎(2) and article ‎35 (consent to transfer the benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.

(2) Paragraph ‎(1) does not apply to Work No. 4 in respect of which the provisions of this Order are for the benefit of the undertaker and National Grid.
