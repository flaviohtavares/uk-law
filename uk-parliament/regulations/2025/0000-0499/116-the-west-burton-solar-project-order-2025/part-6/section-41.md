# The West Burton Solar Project Order 2025, section 41

> Text as at 2025-06-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. No double recovery[^c-29a206cfc678647e9903a979dce12a09]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
