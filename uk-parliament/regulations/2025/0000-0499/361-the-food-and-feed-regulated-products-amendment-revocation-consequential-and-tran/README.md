# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 361

Agriculture

Food

Retained Eu Law Reform

The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025

Made

17th March 2025

Coming into force

1st April 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(1), (3), (4)(a) and (b) and 20(1) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (“**the 2023 Act**”)[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is a relevant national authority for the purposes of section 14(1) and (3) of the 2023 Act[^f00002].

In accordance with paragraph 5(1) and (5) of Schedule 5 to the 2023 Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend, revoke and replace various provisions in secondary assimilated law within the meaning of section 12(2) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act [2023 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/28).

Part 2 contains amendments to Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) on additives for use in animal nutrition (EUR 2003/1831). It also contains consequential amendments to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 378/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0378) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) as regards the duties and tasks of the Community Reference Laboratory concerning applications for authorisations of feed additives (EUR 2005/378) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No 429/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0429) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) as regards the preparation and the presentation of applications and the assessment and the authorisation of feed additives (EUR 2008/429).

Part 3 contains amendments to the following assimilated direct legislation relating to food additives, food enzymes and food flavourings:

- (a) Regulation [(EC) No 1331/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1331) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common authorisation procedure for food additives, food enzymes and food flavourings (EUR 2008/1331);
- (b) Regulation [(EC) No 1332/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1332) of the European Parliament and of the Council on food enzymes, etc., (EUR 2008/1332);
- (c) Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333) of the European Parliament and of the Council on food additives (EUR 2008/1333);
- (d) Regulation [(EC) No 1334/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1334) of the European Parliament and of the Council on flavourings and certain food ingredients with flavouring properties for use in and on foods, etc., (EUR 2008/1334);
- (e) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 234/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0234) implementing Regulation [(EC) No 1331/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1331), etc., (EUR 2011/234).

Part 4 contains amendments to the following assimilated direct legislation relating to food contact materials:

- (a) Regulation [(EC) No 1935/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1935) of the European Parliament and of the Council on materials and articles intended to come into contact with food, etc., (EUR 2004/1935);
- (b) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 450/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0450) on active and intelligent materials and articles intended to come into contact with food (EUR 2009/450); and
- (c) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 10/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0010) on plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with food (EUR 2011/10).

Part 5 contains amendments to Regulation [(EC) No 1829/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1829) on genetically modified food and feed (EUR 2003/1829). It also contains consequential amendments to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 619/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0619) laying down the methods of sampling and analysis for the official control of feed as regards presence of genetically modified material, etc., (EUR 2011/619) and Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 503/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0503) on applications for authorisation of genetically modified food and feed, etc., (EUR 2013/503).

Part 6 contains amendments to [Regulation (EU) 2015/2283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2283) on novel foods, etc., (EUR 2015/2283). It also contains consequential amendments to Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/2468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2468) laying down administrative and scientific requirements concerning traditional foods from third countries in accordance with [Regulation (EU) 2015/2283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2283) (EUR 2017/2468) and Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/2469](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2469) laying down administrative and scientific requirements for applications referred to in Article 10 of [Regulation (EU) 2015/2283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2283) (EUR 2017/2469).

Part 7 contains amendments to Regulation [(EC) No 2065/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2065) of the European Parliament and of the Council on smoke flavourings used or intended for use in or on foods (EUR 2003/2065).

Part 8 sets out revocations, consequential amendments, savings and transitional provision, and gives effect to the Schedules.

Schedules 1 to 3, respectively, set out revocations of Scotland, Wales and England statutory instruments, with certain savings in respect of transitional provisions.

Schedules 4 to 8 contain revocations of assimilated direct legislation.

Schedules 9 to 12, respectively, set out consequential amendments to assimilated direct legislation, and to Scotland, Wales and England statutory instruments.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
