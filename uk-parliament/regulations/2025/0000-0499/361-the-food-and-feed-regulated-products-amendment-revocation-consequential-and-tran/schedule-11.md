# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, SCHEDULE 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 11 — Consequential amendments to Wales statutory instruments

Regulation 22

### 1. Amendment of the Specified Sugar Products (Wales) Regulations 2003

(1) The Specified Sugar Products (Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00670] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1, in the notes following the table—

- (a) in the Welsh language text, for note 7 substitute—

  > **7.** Caiff cynhyrchion siwgr penodedig gynnwys—
  >
  > - (a) toddydd echdynnu a ganiateir, fel y’i diffinnir yn rheoliad 11 o Reoliadau Ychwanegion, Cyflasynnau, Ensymau a Thoddyddion Echdynnu Bwyd (Cymru) 2013;
  > - (b) unrhyw ychwanegyn bwyd a awdurdodir o dan Reoliad [(EC) Rhif 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333) Senedd Ewrop a’r Cyngor ar ychwanegion bwyd.

  .
- (b) in the English language text, for note 7 substitute—

  > **7.** Specified sugar products may contain—
  >
  > - (a) a permitted extraction solvent, as defined in regulation 11 of the Food Additives, Flavourings, Enzymes and Extraction Solvents (Wales) Regulations 2013;
  > - (b) any food additive authorised under Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333) of the European Parliament and of the Council on food additives.

  .
