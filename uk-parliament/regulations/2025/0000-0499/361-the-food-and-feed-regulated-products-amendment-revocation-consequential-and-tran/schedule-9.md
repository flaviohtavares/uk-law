# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, SCHEDULE 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 9 — Consequential amendments to assimilated direct legislation

Regulation 22

### 1. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, etc.

(1) Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, etc.[^f00659], is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1(5)(e)(ii), omit “or Commission implementing [Regulation (EU) No 872/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0872)” to the end.

### 2. Amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1907) (REACH), etc.

(1) Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1907) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, etc.[^f00660], is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 2(5)(b)(ii) and (6)(d)(ii), in each place, omit “or Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 872/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0872)” to the end.

### 3. Amendment of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 101/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0101) concerning the use of lactic acid to reduce microbiological surface contamination on bovine carcases

(1) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 101/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0101) concerning the use of lactic acid to reduce microbiological surface contamination on bovine carcases[^f00661] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Annex, in Part 1, in paragraph 1, for “Regulation (EU) No 231/2012” substitute “accordance with authorisations for food additives under Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333)”.

### 4. Amendment of Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2019/934](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0934) supplementing [Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1308) as regards wine-growing areas where the alcoholic strength may be increased, etc.

(1) Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2019/934](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0934) supplementing [Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1308) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards wine-growing areas where the alcoholic strength may be increased, authorised oenological practices and restrictions applicable to the production and conservation of grapevine products, the minimum percentage of alcohol for by-products and their disposal, and publication of OIV files[^f00662] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 9(1), for “by [Commission Regulation (EU) No 231/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0231)” substitute “in specifications for food additives authorised under Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333)”.

(3) In Annex I—

- (a) in Part A, in Table 2 (authorised oenological compounds as referred to in Article 3(1)), in rows 4.1 and 4.1a (microcrystalline cellulose), in column 7 (conditions and limits of use), for “laid down in the Annex to [Regulation (EU) No 231/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0231)” substitute “for food additives authorised under Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333)”;
- (b) in Appendix 1 (tartaric acid (L(+)-) and derived products), in paragraph 2, for “Regulation (EU) No 231/2012” substitute “accordance with authorisations for food additives under Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333)”.
