# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2025.

(3) In these Regulations—

- (a) “Regulation [(EC) No 1829/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1829)” means Regulation [(EC) No 1829/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1829) of the European Parliament and of the Council on genetically modified food and feed[^f00004];
- (b) “Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831)” means Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition[^f00005];
- (c) “Regulation [(EC) No 2065/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2065)” means Regulation [(EC) No 2065/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2065) of the European Parliament and of the Council on smoke flavourings used or intended for use in or on foods[^f00006].
