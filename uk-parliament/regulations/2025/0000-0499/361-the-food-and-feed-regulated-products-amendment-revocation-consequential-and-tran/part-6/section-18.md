# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Amendment of Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/2468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2468)

(1) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/2468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2468) laying down administrative and scientific requirements concerning traditional foods from third countries in accordance with [Regulation (EU) 2015/2283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/2283), etc.[^f00028], is amended as follows.

(2) In Annexes I and II, in the template cover letter in each Annex, for “in order to update the list on” substitute “for a determination of authorisation status in relation to”.
