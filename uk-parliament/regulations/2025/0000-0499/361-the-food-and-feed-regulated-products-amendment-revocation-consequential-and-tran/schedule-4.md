# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Revocations: miscellaneous assimilated direct legislation

Regulation 21(2)

### 1. Revocation of assimilated direct legislation: miscellaneous instruments

[Commission Regulation (EU) No 231/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0231) laying down specifications for food additives listed in Annexes II and III to Regulation [(EC) No 1333/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1333) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

**2.** Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 872/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0872) adopting the list of flavouring substances provided for by Regulation [(EC) No 2232/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2232) of the European Parliament and of the Council, introducing it in Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No 1334/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1334), etc.

**3.** [Commission Regulation (EU) No 873/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0873) on transitional measures concerning the Union list of flavourings and source materials set out in Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No 1334/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1334) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00059].

**4.** Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 1321/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1321) establishing the Union list of authorised smoke flavouring primary products for use as such in or on foods and/or for the production of derived smoke flavourings[^f00060].
