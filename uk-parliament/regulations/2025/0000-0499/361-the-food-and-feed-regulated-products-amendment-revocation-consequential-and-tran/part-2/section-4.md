# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment of [Commission Regulation (EC) No 378/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0378) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) as regards the duties and tasks of the Community Reference Laboratory concerning applications for authorisations of feed additives

(1) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 378/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0378) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the duties and tasks of the Community Reference Laboratory concerning applications for authorisations of feed additives[^f00007] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 3(3), in the first subparagraph—

- (a) for “valid for the entire period of” substitute “for”;
- (b) for “those expired” substitute “any that expire”.

(3) In Article 5(4), omit point (c).

(4) In Annex IV, omit paragraph 5.
