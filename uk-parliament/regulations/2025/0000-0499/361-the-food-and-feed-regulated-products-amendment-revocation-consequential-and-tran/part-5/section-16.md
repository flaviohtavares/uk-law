# The Food and Feed (Regulated Products) (Amendment, Revocation, Consequential and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2025, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Amendment of Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 503/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0503) on applications for authorisation of genetically modified food and feed, etc.

(1) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 503/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0503) on applications for authorisation of genetically modified food and feed in accordance with Regulation [(EC) No 1829/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1829) of the European Parliament and of the Council, etc.[^f00026], is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, in the words before point (a), for “5, 11, 17 and 23” substitute “5 and 17”.

(3) In Article 8—

- (a) in the heading, for “5(3), 11(2), 17(3) and 23(2)” substitute “5(3) and 17(3)”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(4) In Annex III, in section 4 (certified reference material), in the second paragraph, for “throughout the period of validity of the authorisation” substitute “with respect to the authorisation, and whilst the authorisation continues to have effect”.
