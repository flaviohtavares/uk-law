## 15. Amendment of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 619/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0619) laying down the methods of sampling and analysis for the official control of feed as regards presence of genetically modified material, etc.

(1) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 619/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0619) laying down the methods of sampling and analysis for the official control of feed as regards presence of genetically modified material for which an authorisation procedure is pending or the authorisation of which has expired[^f00025] is amended as follows.
