# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 128

PENSIONS

The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

5th February 2025

Laid before Parliament

12th February 2025

Coming into force in accordance with article 2

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 5th day of February 2025

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty considers it expedient to amend the [Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606)[^f00001].

ACCORDINGLY, His Majesty, in pursuance of sections 12(1) and 24(3) of the [Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5)[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling Him to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2025.

## 2. Commencement

(1) This Order, subject to paragraph (2), comes into force on 21st March 2025.

(2) Articles 6 and 7 come into force on 7th April 2025.

## 3. Extent

This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 4. Amendment of the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006

The [Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606) (“**the principal Order**”) is amended as follows.

## 5. Amendment to article 20 (mobility supplement)

In article 20(1) (mobility supplement) of the principal Order—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c)(iii)—
  - (i) after “disability living allowance” insert “at the higher rate”;
  - (ii) delete “having been deemed, by virtue of section 74(1) of the relevant Act, to be suffering from such disablement as is mentioned in subsection (1)(a) of section 73 of the relevant Act and to satisfy the requirements of subsection (9) of that section”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (c)(v) insert—

  > - (vi) has been in receipt of the mobility component of Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance at the higher rate given in accordance with regulations made under section 31 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018; or[^f00003]

  .

## 6. Amendment to Schedule 1 (disablement due to service in the armed forces)

In Schedule 1 to the principal Order—

- (a) for the table in Part 2 substitute the table in Schedule 1 to this Order;
- (b) for tables 1 and 2 in Part 3 substitute tables 1 and 2 in Schedule 2 to this Order; and
- (c) for the table in Part 4 substitute the table in Schedule 3 to this Order.

## 7. Amendment to Schedule 2 (death due to service in the armed forces)

In Schedule 2 to the principal Order—

- (a) for tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 in Part 2 substitute tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 in Schedule 4 to this Order; and
- (b) for the table in Part 3 substitute the table in Schedule 5 to this Order.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 (“**the principal Order**”) ([S.I. 2006/606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606)), which makes provision for pensions and other awards in respect of disablement or death due to service before 6th April 2005 in the naval, military and air forces. The principal Order contains the scheme which provides the pensions and other awards.

Article 5 amends article 20 of the principal Order to include Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance (provided in regulations made under section 31 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act [2018 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2018/9)) in the list of benefits which confer entitlement to mobility supplement. It also removes a requirement that an individual must have been deemed to satisfy certain conditions in order to receive a mobility supplement under article 20.

The Tables in Schedules 1 to 5 to this Order replace tables in Schedules 1 and 2 to the principal Order, in order to adjust the rates prescribed for retired pay, pensions, gratuities and allowances in respect of disablement or death due to service in the armed forces.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
