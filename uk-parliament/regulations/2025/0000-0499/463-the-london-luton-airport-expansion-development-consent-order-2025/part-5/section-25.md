> Text as at 2026-03-21.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-66711e7356c86170500046c0406490ab]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00068] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
