# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 336

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2025

Made

12th March 2025

Coming into force

13th March 2025

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of March 2025

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2025 and comes into force on 13th<sup></sup>March 2025.

## 2. Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
