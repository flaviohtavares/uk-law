# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2025, section 3

> Text as at 2025-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Amendment of the Schools Forums (England) Regulations 2012[^c-c22d103b6b98035ad6ceaeaf149ff698]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 8 of the Schools Forums (England) Regulations 2012[^f00049], in each place where the words “the School and Early Years Finance and Childcare (Provision of Information About Young Children) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2024” occur, substitute “the School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2025”.
