# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 13 — Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate legislation

#### 1. Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Assessment of Environmental Effects) Regulations 1999

(1) The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Assessment of Environmental Effects) Regulations 1999[^f00243] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 3(1), in paragraph (b) of the definition of “appropriate particulars”;
- (b) regulation 6(3);
- (c) paragraph 2(c)(vi) of Schedule 1;
- (d) paragraph 10 of Schedule 2.

#### 2. Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 1999

(1) The Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 1999[^f00244] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 2(1), in paragraph (b) of the definition of “appropriate particulars”;
- (b) regulation 6(2)(a);
- (c) paragraph 10 of Schedule 1;
- (d) paragraph 2(c)(vi) of Schedule 2.

#### 3. Nuclear Reactors (Environmental Impact Assessment for Decommissioning) Regulations 1999

(1) The Nuclear Reactors (Environmental Impact Assessment for Decommissioning) Regulations 1999[^f00245] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 2(1), in the definition of “EU environmental assessment”;
- (b) paragraphs 5 and 8 of Schedule 1;
- (c) paragraph 2(c)(vi) of Schedule 2.

#### 4. Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2000

(1) The Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2000[^f00246] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 2, in paragraph (b) of the definition of “appropriate particulars”;
- (b) regulation 4(2);
- (c) paragraph 10 of Schedule 1;
- (d) paragraph 2(c)(vi) of Schedule 2.

#### 5. Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010

In regulation 3(3) of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010[^f00247] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 6. Offshore Combustion Installations (Pollution Prevention and Control) Regulations 2013

(1) The Offshore Combustion Installations (Pollution Prevention and Control) Regulations 2013[^f00248] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 7(1)(b);
- (b) regulation 12(3).

#### 7. Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014

In regulation 65(2)(b)(v) of the Electricity Capacity Regulations 2014[^f00249] for “a retained EU” substitute “an assimilated”.

#### 8. Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017

(1) The Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00250] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 12(3)(a);
- (b) regulation 15(1)(b);
- (c) regulation 42(4A)(d)(iii);
- (d) paragraph 2(c)(vii) of Schedule 3;
- (e) paragraph 8(2) of Schedule 4.

#### 9. Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00251] are amended as follows.

(2) In the text of the modifications made by the following provisions for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”—

- (a) regulation 37(4);
- (b) regulation 62(5);
- (c) paragraphs 6(c)(i), 7(b), (c) and (e)(i) and 8(e) of Schedule 4.

#### 10. Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020

(1) The Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020[^f00252] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 6(3)(c);
- (b) paragraph 3(b) of Schedule 4;
- (c) paragraph 2(c)(vi) of Schedule 5;
- (d) paragraph 5(d) of Schedule 6.

#### 11. Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

(1) The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^f00253] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”—

- (a) regulation 29(1);
- (b) regulation 30(1);
- (c) regulation 31(1);
- (d) regulation 32(1).

### Part 2 — Assimilated direct legislation

#### 12. Regulation [(EC) No 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0715)

(1) Regulation [(EC) No 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the natural gas transmission networks and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 1775/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1775)[^f00254] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) Article 6(1)(c);
- (b) Article 6(8), in each place it occurs.

(3) In Article 6(8)(b) for “retained direct minor EU” substitute “assimilated direct minor”.

(4) In Article 31 for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

#### 13. [Regulation (EU) No 1227/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/1227)

(1) [Regulation (EU) No 1227/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/1227) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2011 on wholesale energy market integrity and transparency[^f00255] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) Article 8(3);
- (b) Article 8(4)(e);
- (c) Article 11;
- (d) Article 17(3).

(3) In Article 8(7)(b) for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

#### 14. [Regulation (EU) 2017/1938](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1938)

(1) [Regulation (EU) 2017/1938](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1938) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2017 concerning measures to safeguard the security of gas supply and repealing [Regulation (EU) No 994/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/0994)[^f00256] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) Article 7(4)(e);
- (b) Article 15(3).

#### 15. [Regulation (EU) 2019/943](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0943)

(1) [Regulation (EU) 2019/943](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0943) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 on the internal market for electricity[^f00257] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) Article 3(j) (as it applies in England and Wales, and Scotland);
- (b) Article 18(8A)(a)(iii) and (b)(iii).

(3) In Article 70 (as it applies in England and Wales, and Scotland) for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.
