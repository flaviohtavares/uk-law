# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 1 — Scotland Act 1998 Related Instruments

Regulation 2

### 1. Scotland Act 1998 (Functions Exercisable in or as Regards Scotland) Order 1999

The Scotland Act 1998 (Functions Exercisable in or as Regards Scotland) Order 1999[^f00003] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 5 for “retained EU”, in both places it occurs, substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in article 7 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

### 2. Scotland Act 1998 (Modification of Functions) Order 1999

The Scotland Act 1998 (Modification of Functions) Order 1999[^f00004] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 5 for “retained EU”, in both places it occurs, substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in article 6 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
