# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 12 — Health and Safety Executive

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Chemicals (Health and Safety) and Genetically Modified Organisms (Contained Use) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

In paragraph 6 of Schedule 4 to the Chemicals (Health and Safety) and Genetically Modified Organisms (Contained Use) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00239] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

### Part 2 — Assimilated Direct Legislation

#### 2. Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272)

In Article 32(6) of Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, amending and repealing Directives [67/548/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1967/0548) and [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045), and amending Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1907)[^f00240] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 3. [Regulation (EU) No 649/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0649)

[Regulation (EU) No 649/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0649) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 concerning the export and import of hazardous chemicals[^f00241] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 3(4), (7), (8), (10)(a) and (11)(a) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 8(4) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in Article 14(11) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (d) in Article 17(1) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (e) in Article 23(1) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 4. Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No 1062/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/1062)

Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No 1062/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/1062) of 4 August 2014 on the work programme for the systematic examination of all existing active substances contained in biocidal products referred to in [Regulation (EU) No 528/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0528) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00242] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 22A(2) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 22B(2) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
