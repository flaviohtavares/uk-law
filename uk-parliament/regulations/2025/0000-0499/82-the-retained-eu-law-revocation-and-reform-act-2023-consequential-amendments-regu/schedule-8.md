# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 8 — Department for Transport

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Civil Aviation Authority Regulations 1991

The Civil Aviation Authority Regulations 1991[^f00050] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 6(1) and (8)(a)[^f00051] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in regulation 25(1)(c)[^f00052] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 2. Road Vehicles (Display of Registration Marks) Regulations 2001

The Road Vehicles (Display of Registration Marks) Regulations 2001[^f00053] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 5(3)[^f00054] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in regulation 9(3)[^f00055] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 3. Railways and Other Guided Transport Systems (Safety) Regulations 2006

The Railways and Other Guided Transport Systems (Safety) Regulations 2006[^f00056] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 2(1)—
  - (i) for “retained” substitute “assimilated” in the following definitions[^f00057]—
    - (aa) “2019 EU ECM Regulation”;
    - (bb) “cross-border UK-issued ECM certificate”;
  - (ii) omit the definition of “retained 2019 EU ECM Regulation”;
  - (iii) at the appropriate place insert—

    > - “**assimilated 2019 EU ECM Regulation**” means Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2019/779](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0779) laying down detailed provisions on a system of certification of entities in charge of maintenance of vehicles pursuant to Directive (EU) 2016/798 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing [Commission Regulation (EU) No 445/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0445), as it has effect in the UK after IP completion day;

    ;
- (b) in regulation 18A(1A)(b)[^f00058] for “retained” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in regulation 27—
  - (i) in paragraph (1)(de)[^f00059] for “retained” substitute “assimilated”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (1A)(c)[^f00060] for “retained”, in both places it occurs, substitute “assimilated”.

#### 4. Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006

The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006[^f00061] are amended as follows—

- (a) in rule 4(5)(b)[^f00062] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in rule 7(5) and (11)(b)[^f00063] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in rule 11(2)(c)[^f00064] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (d) in paragraph 8 of Schedule 1[^f00065] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (e) in paragraph 7(b) of Schedule 7[^f00066] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 5. Department for Transport (Fees) Order 2009

The Department for Transport (Fees) Order 2009[^f00067] is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph 8[^f00068] of Schedule 1 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in paragraph 45[^f00069] of Schedule 1 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 6. Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

In paragraph 6 of Schedule 3 to the Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00070] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

### Part 2 — Assimilated Direct Legislation

#### 7. Regulation [(EC) No 1072/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1072)

Regulation [(EC) No 1072/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1072) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 on common rules for access to the international road haulage market[^f00071] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 2(7) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 9(1) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in Article 13(1) for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.
