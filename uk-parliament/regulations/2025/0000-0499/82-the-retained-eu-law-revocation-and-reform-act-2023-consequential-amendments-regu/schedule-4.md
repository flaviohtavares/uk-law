# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 4 — Ministry of Justice

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Law Applicable to Non-Contractual Obligations (England and Wales and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008

In regulation 6 of the Law Applicable to Non-Contractual Obligations (England and Wales and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008[^f00010] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

#### 2. Law Applicable to Contractual Obligations (England and Wales and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2009

In regulation 5(1) of the Law Applicable to Contractual Obligations (England and Wales and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2009[^f00011] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

### Part 2 — Assimilated Direct Legislation

#### 3. Regulation [(EC) No 864/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0864)

Regulation [(EC) No 864/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0864) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 July 2007 on the law applicable to non-contractual obligations (Rome II)[^f00012] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 14(3) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 27 for “retained EU”, in both places it occurs including the heading, substitute “assimilated”.

#### 4. Regulation [(EC) No 593/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0593)

Regulation [(EC) No 593/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0593) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 2008 on the law applicable to contractual obligations (Rome I)[^f00013] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 3(4) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 23, for “retained EU”, in both places it occurs including the heading, substitute “assimilated”.
