# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 5 — Department for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations 2004

The Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations 2004[^f00014] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 12(4) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in paragraph 1(e) of Schedule 1 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 2. Construction Products Regulations 2013

In regulation 22(3)(b)(iii) of the Construction Products Regulations 2013[^f00015] for “a retained EU” substitute “an assimilated”.

#### 3. Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015

The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015[^f00016]are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 6(1)(a)(ii) and (iia) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in regulation 10(3)(a)(ii) and (iia) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in regulation 25(6), in the definition of “relevant plan or programme” for “retained EU”, in both places it occurs, substitute “assimilated”;
- (d) in regulation 26(2)(a)(ii) and (iia) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 4. Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017

The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017[^f00017] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 2(1)[^f00018] in the definition “EU environmental assessment”, for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in paragraph 2(1)(c)(vi) of Schedule 3[^f00019] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in paragraph 8 of Schedule 4[^f00020] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 5. Infrastructure Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017

The Infrastructure Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017[^f00021] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 3(1)[^f00022] in the definition of “EU environmental assessment”, for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in paragraph 2(1)(c)(vi) of Schedule 3[^f00023] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (c) in paragraph 8 of Schedule 4[^f00024] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

### Part 2 — Assimilated Direct Legislation

#### 6. [Regulation (EU) No 305/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0305)

In Article 2(9A) of [Regulation (EU) No 305/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0305) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2011 laying down harmonised conditions for the marketing of construction products and repealing Council Directive [89/106/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0106)[^f00025] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
