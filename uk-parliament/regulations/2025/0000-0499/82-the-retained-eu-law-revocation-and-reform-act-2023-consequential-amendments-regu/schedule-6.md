# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 6 — Department for Business and Trade

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Measuring Container Bottles (EEC Requirements) Regulations 1977

In regulation 2(1A) of the Measuring Container Bottles (EEC Requirements) Regulations 1977[^f00026] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 2. General Product Safety Regulations 2005

In regulation 2 of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005[^f00027] in the definition of “relevant enactment” for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 3. Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008

In regulation 24 of the Limited Liability Partnerships (Accounts and Audit) (Application of Companies Act 2006) Regulations 2008[^f00028] in the text of the modification of section 461(4)(h) of the Companies Act 2006[^f00029] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 4. Export Control Order 2008

(1) The Export Control Order 2008[^f00030] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) article 2(1)—
  - (i) in the definition of “the dual-use Regulation”, in paragraph (a);
  - (ii) in the definition of “general export authorisation”, in paragraph (a) (in each place it occurs).
- (b) article 8(2)(a) and (b);
- (c) article 26(4)(a);
- (d) article 28(3)(a);
- (e) article 31(1) and (2)(b);
- (f) article 32(2);
- (g) article 35(1), (2)(a) and (b), (4), (6) and (7) and in the heading;
- (h) article 40(1)(b)(i) and (2);
- (i) article 43(1)(b).

(3) In article 2(1), omit the definition of “the retained dual-use Regulation”.

(4) In article 2(1), before the definition of “category A goods” insert—

> “**the assimilated dual-use Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast)[^f00031];

#### 5. Aerosol Dispensers Regulations 2009

In regulation 2(1A) of the Aerosol Dispensers Regulations 2009[^f00032] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 6. Provision of Services Regulations 2009

(1) The Provision of Services Regulations 2009[^f00033] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 5(1)(b) and (c);
- (b) regulation 17(4);
- (c) regulation 22(2)(d)(i) and (ii);
- (d) regulation 31(3)(a).

(3) In the following provisions for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”—

- (a) regulation 6(2)(a) and (b);
- (b) regulation 6(4)(a) and (b);
- (c) regulation 14(3)(a) and (b).

#### 7. Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011

(1) The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011[^f00034] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 3B(1) and (3);
- (b) Part 6 of Schedule 2.

#### 8. Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015

In regulation 2(1A) of the Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015[^f00035] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 9. Lifts Regulations 2016

In regulation 2(1A) of the Lifts Regulations 2016[^f00036] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 10. Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016

In regulation 2(1A) and (1C) of the Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations 2016[^f00037] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 11. Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016

In regulation 2(1A) of the Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2016[^f00038] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 12. Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016

In regulation 2(1A) of the Measuring Instruments Regulations 2016[^f00039] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 13. Trade etc. in Dual-Use Items and Firearms etc. (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Trade etc. in Dual-Use Items and Firearms etc. (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00040] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 5(1) to (7);
- (b) in regulation 5(8), in each place it occurs, in the definitions of “corresponding retained export authorisation”, “retained authorisation for brokering services”, “retained general export authorisation”, “retained global export authorisation”, “retained individual export authorisation” and “retained national general export authorisation”;
- (c) regulation 6(1) to (2);
- (d) in regulation 6(3), in the definition of “retained firearms export authorisation”.

#### 14. Protecting against the Effects of the Extraterritorial Application of Third Country Legislation (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

(1) The Protecting against the Effects of the Extraterritorial Application of Third Country Legislation (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^f00041] are amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) regulation 5 (in each place it occurs) and in the heading;
- (b) regulation 6;
- (c) regulation 7, in each of the definitions.

### Part 2 — Assimilated direct legislation

#### 15. EEC Council Regulation No 1

In Article 1 of EEC Council Regulation No 1 determining the languages to be used by the European Economic Community[^f00042] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 16. Regulation [(EC) No 2157/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2157)

In Article AA1(2)(d) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2157/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2157) of 8 October 2001 on the Statute for a European company (SE)[^f00043] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 17. Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0765)

In Article 2(21) of Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0765) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 July 2008 setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/0339)[^f00044] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

#### 18. Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428)

(1) Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast)[^f00045] is amended as follows.

(2) In the following provisions for “retained” substitute “assimilated”—

- (a) Article 2(9);
- (b) Article 9(1) (in each place it occurs);
- (c) Annexes 2(a) to (f), in the heading.

#### 19. [Regulation (EU) 2019/1150](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1150)

In Article 1(5) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1150](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1150) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 on promoting fairness and transparency for business users of online intermediation services[^f00046] for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
