# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 27th February 2025.

(3) A provision of these Regulations that amends or revokes an enactment has the same extent as the enactment amended or revoked unless otherwise specified.
