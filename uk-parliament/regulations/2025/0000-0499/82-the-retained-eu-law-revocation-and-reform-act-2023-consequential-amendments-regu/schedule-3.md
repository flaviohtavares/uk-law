# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 3 — Department of Health and Social Care

Regulation 2

### Part 1 — Subordinate Legislation

#### 1. Food Supplements (England) Regulations 2003

In regulation 5(2)(a) of the Food Supplements (England) Regulations 2003[^f00006] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

#### 2. Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (England) Regulations 2009

In Schedule 1 to the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (England) Regulations 2009[^f00007], in the entries in the subject matter column that relate to Article 4(2) and Article 4(3), for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.

### Part 2 — Assimilated Direct Legislation

#### 3. Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924)

In Article 19(2) of Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006 on nutrition and health claims made on foods[^f00008] for “retained direct EU” substitute “assimilated direct”.

### 4. [Regulation (EU) No 609/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0609)

Regulation EU No 609/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on food intended for infants and young children, food for special medical purposes, and total diet replacement for weight control and repealing Council Directive [92/52/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0052), Commission Directives [96/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0008), [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021), [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125) and [2006/141/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0141), [Directive 2009/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0039) of the European Parliament and of the Council and Commission Regulations [(EC) No 41/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0041) and [(EC) No 953/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0953)[^f00009] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Article 6(1) and (2) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”;
- (b) in Article 15(4) and (5) for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
