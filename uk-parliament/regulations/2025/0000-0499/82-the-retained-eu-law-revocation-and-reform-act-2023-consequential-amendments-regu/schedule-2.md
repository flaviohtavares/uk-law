# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2025, Schedule 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## Schedule 2 — Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office

Regulation 2

### 1. European Institutions and Consular Protection (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

The European Institutions and Consular Protection (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^f00005] are amended as follows—

- (a) revoke regulation 3;
- (b) in the heading and in the text of regulation 10 for “retained EU” substitute “assimilated”.
