# The Heckington Fen Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 482

Infrastructure Planning

The Heckington Fen Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025

Made

11th April 2025

Coming into force

14th April 2025

The Heckington Fen Solar Park Order 2025 (“**the 2025 Order**”)[^f00001] which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002] contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors and omissions in the 2025 Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Lincolnshire County Council, North Kesteven District Council, and Boston Borough Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that this request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Heckington Fen Solar Park (Correction) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 14th April 2025.

## 2. Corrections

The Heckington Fen Solar Park Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made;
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery & Innovation

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

11th April 2025
