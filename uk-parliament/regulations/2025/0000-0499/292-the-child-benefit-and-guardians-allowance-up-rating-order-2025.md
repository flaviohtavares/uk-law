# The Child Benefit and Guardian's Allowance Up-rating Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 292

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Child Benefit and Guardian's Allowance Up-rating Order 2025

Made

5th March 2025

Coming into force

7th April 2025

Following a review of the sums specified in section 150(1)(a)(i) and (1)(i) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001] in the tax year 2024-25, the Treasury have determined that the general level of prices in Great Britain is greater at the end of the period under review than it was at the beginning. This Order makes provision under section 150 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 and makes corresponding provision for Northern Ireland in accordance with section 132(1) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002].

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with sections 150(2) and 190(1) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00003], and section 166(10A) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00004], and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 150(2), (5), (9) and (10)(a)(i) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00005], and section 132(1) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 and now exercisable by them[^f00006].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Child Benefit and Guardian's Allowance Up-rating Order 2025 and comes into force on 7th April 2025.

## 2. Amendment of the Child Benefit (Rates) Regulations 2006

Regulation 2(1) (rate of child benefit) of the Child Benefit (Rates) Regulations 2006[^f00007] is amended as follows—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) (enhanced rate) for “£25.60” substitute “£26.05”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) (other cases) for “£16.95” substitute “£17.25”.

## 3. Amendment of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992

In paragraph 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (weekly rate of guardian’s allowance)[^f00008] for “£21.75” substitute “£22.10”.

## 4. Amendment of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992

In paragraph 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (weekly rate of guardian’s allowance)[^f00009] for “£21.75” substitute “£22.10”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Benefit (Rates) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/965](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/965)); the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4); and the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7).

Article 2 increases the weekly rate of child benefit from £25.60 to £26.05 (enhanced rate) and from £16.95 to £17.25 (other cases).

Articles 3 and 4 increase the weekly rate of guardian’s allowance from £21.75 to £22.10.

The increases in the rates of child benefit and guardian’s allowance take effect from the week beginning with the first Monday in the tax year 2025-26.

In accordance with section 150(8) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5), a copy of the report by the Government Actuary giving their opinion on the likely effect on the National Insurance Fund of the making of this Order (in relation to the increases made to guardian’s allowance) was laid before Parliament with the draft of this Order.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary, or public sectors is foreseen.
