# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Determination of appeals

(1) Where, following an appeal under this Part, the appeal body determines that a decision of a decision maker is to be varied or remitted to the decision maker, the decision maker must—

- (a) comply with any directions given to it by the appeal body, and
- (b) take any steps necessary to give effect to the appeal body’s determination.

(2) In this regulation “**decision maker**” means—

- (a) the Secretary of State, or
- (b) the enforcement authority.
