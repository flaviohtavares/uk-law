# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Publication of scheme logo and scheme packaging logo

The deposit management organisation must publish—

- (a) the scheme logo, and
- (b) the scheme packaging logo (if any),

in such manner as the deposit management organisation considers appropriate to bring them to the attention of scheme suppliers and consumers.
