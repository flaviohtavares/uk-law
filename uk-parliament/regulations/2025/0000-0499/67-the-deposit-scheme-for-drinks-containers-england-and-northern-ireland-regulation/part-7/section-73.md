# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Co-operation

(1) The deposit management organisation must enter into and maintain arrangements with each national enforcement authority for securing co-operation and the exchange of information for the carrying out of any of the national enforcement authority’s functions other than any relevant enforcement function.

(2) The deposit management organisation and the national enforcement authority must—

- (a) review the arrangements from time to time, and
- (b) revise them when they consider it appropriate to do so.

(3) In these Regulations “**relevant enforcement function**” means a function of a national enforcement authority under Part 9 in respect of any act or omission by the deposit management organisation.
