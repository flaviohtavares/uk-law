# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Amounts to be paid to the national enforcement authorities

The deposit management organisation must make any payment required to be made to a national enforcement authority under this Part by such means or in such manner and at such intervals as are agreed with the national enforcement authority.
