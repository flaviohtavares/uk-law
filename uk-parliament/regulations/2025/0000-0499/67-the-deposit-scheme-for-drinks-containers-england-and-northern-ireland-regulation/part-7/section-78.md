# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Status of certain decisions pending outcome of internal review

(1) This regulation applies where an affected person applies for an internal review (“review”) of a relevant decision.

(2) The effect of the relevant decision is suspended during the period—

- (a) beginning with the day on which the application for the review is made, and
- (b) ending with the end of the day on which the person is notified of the deposit management organisation’s decision on that application in accordance with regulation 76(5).

(3) In this regulation “affected person” and “relevant decision” have the meanings given in regulation 76(2).
