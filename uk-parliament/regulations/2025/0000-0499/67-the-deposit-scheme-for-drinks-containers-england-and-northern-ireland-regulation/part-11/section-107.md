# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Appeals

Where an appeal body under regulation 100 determines that a decision of a national enforcement authority is to be varied or remitted to that authority, the national enforcement authority must comply with any directions given to it by the appeal body, and take any steps necessary to give effect to the appeal body’s determination.
