# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Annual reports from deposit management organisations

The national enforcement authorities—

- (a) must consider the annual reports submitted by a deposit management organisation under regulation 53;
- (b) may request such additional reports from the deposit management organisation as the national enforcement authorities consider necessary.
