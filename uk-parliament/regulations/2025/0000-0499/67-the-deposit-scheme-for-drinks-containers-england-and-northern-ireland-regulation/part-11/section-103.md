# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Co-operation with deposit management organisations

Each national enforcement authority must, under regulation 73, enter into arrangements with deposit management organisations for securing co-operation and the exchange of information for the carrying out of any of their functions other than any relevant enforcement functions.
