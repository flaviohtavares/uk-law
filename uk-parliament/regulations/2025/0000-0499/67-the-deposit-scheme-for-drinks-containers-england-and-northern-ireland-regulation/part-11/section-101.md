# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Operational plans

Each national enforcement authority must consult on, and where satisfied may approve, any revision of a deposit management organisation’s operational plan in accordance with regulation 52.
