# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2025, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Penalties and costs received under this Part

(1) The Environment Agency must pay any penalty and costs it receives under this Part into the Consolidated Fund.

(2) DAERA must pay any penalty it receives under this Part into the Consolidated Fund of Northern Ireland.

(3) A local weights and measures authority may retain any penalty and any costs that it receives under this Part.
