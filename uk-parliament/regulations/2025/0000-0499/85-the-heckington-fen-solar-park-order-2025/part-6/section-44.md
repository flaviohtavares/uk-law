# The Heckington Fen Solar Park Order 2025, section 44

> Text as at 2025-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. NGET extension works[^c-d8c1e73e204b050f7d89899b20b50964]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If NGET elects to undertake any elements of the works described in Works No. 6B or 6C pursuant to planning permission granted under Part 3 of the 1990 Act or Article 3 of the 2015 Order then the requirements contained in Schedule 2 will not have effect in so far as they relate to those works and NGET will serve written notice of the same on the relevant planning authority.

(2) As from the date on which Works No. 6A, 6B, or 6C is commenced any conditions of the NGET 2005 Permission that relate to the land at plot 99G and 99H cease to have effect to the extent they are inconsistent with the authorised development or with anything done or approved under the requirements in Schedule 2 (requirements).
