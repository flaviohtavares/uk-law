# The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 436

DIPLOMATIC SERVICE

The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

2nd April 2025

Coming into force

9th April 2025

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of April 2025

Present,

The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Him by section 1(1), (4A) and (4B) of the [Consular Fees Act 1980](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/23)[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 2025 and comes into force on 9th April 2025.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of the [Consular Fees Order 2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/798)

(1) The table in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the [Consular Fees Order 2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/798)[^f00002] (Table of general consular fees) is amended as follows.

(2) In row 19, column 3, for “100.00” substitute “125.00”.

(3) In row 20, column 3, for “75.00” substitute “125.00”.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order in Council)

This Order amends the Table of General Consular Fees in the Consular Fees Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/798](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/798) as amended).

Paragraphs (2) and (3) of Article 2 of this Order amend the fees relating to Emergency Travel Documents from £100 to £125 and relating to Emergency Passports from £75 to £125.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this Order as no, or no significant, impact is foreseen on the private, voluntary or public sectors in the United Kingdom.
