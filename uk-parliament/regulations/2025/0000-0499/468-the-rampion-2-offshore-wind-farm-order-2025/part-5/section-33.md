# The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025, section 33

> Text as at 2026-04-24.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-6832d01975ec73905fb5a80bb4d45280]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”; and
- (c) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
