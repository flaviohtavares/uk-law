# The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025, section 52

> Text as at 2026-04-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. No double recovery[^key-3e3704afe7b77064e1798966ef85312c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation will not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
