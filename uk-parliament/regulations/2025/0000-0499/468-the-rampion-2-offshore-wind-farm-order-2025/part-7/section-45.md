## 45. Procedure in relation to certain approvals etc.[^key-abac83cad7108cc259e4b75cfe0f8b96]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 14 (procedure for discharge of certain approvals) has effect in relation to all agreements or approvals granted, refused or withheld in relation to the requirements in Part 3 of Schedule 1 (requirements).
