# The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025, section 44

> Text as at 2026-04-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Abatement of works abandoned or decayed[^key-6b0402bf81d2ab862312a08e3341b878]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where Work Nos 1 to 6 or all of them or any part of them, is abandoned or allowed to fall into decay the Secretary of State may, following consultation with the undertaker, by notice in writing require the undertaker at its own expense either to repair, make safe and restore one or any of those Works, or any relevant part of them, or to remove them and, without prejudice to any notice served under section 105(2) (Decommissioning programmes) of the 2004 Act[^f00046] restore the site to a safe and proper condition, to such an extent and within such limits as may be specified in the notice.
