# The Associated British Ports (Immingham Green Energy Terminal) Order 2025, section 42

> Text as at 2025-07-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Limits of dock master’s jurisdiction[^key-0535f6675fad95e8e2fc162e9f8e9912]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of paragraph (2) of this article, the limits within which the powers of the dock master under any enactment may be exercised include the area of jurisdiction.

(2) The powers conferred by this article are, so far as applicable to vessels, limited to vessels going to, moored at or departing from any part of the area of jurisdiction and must not be exercised so as to affect vessels navigating or at anchor in the channels of the River Humber unless such vessels obstruct access to the area of jurisdiction.
