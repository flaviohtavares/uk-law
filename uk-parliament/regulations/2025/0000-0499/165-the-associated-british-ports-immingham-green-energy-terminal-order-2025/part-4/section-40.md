# The Associated British Ports (Immingham Green Energy Terminal) Order 2025, section 40

> Text as at 2025-07-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Authorisation of operation and use[^c-78b4e87840bccff848024694a0b81cd2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the requirements set out in Schedule 2 (requirements), the undertaker and any persons authorised by the undertaker may operate and use the authorised project for which development consent is granted by this Order.
