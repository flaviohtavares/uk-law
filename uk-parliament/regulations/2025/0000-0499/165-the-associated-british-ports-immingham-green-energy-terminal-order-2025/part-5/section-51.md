# The Associated British Ports (Immingham Green Energy Terminal) Order 2025, section 51

> Text as at 2025-07-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Permanent light on tidal works[^key-45003e1ab3a971fef377885acd4f127e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After a completion of a tidal work, the undertaker must at the outer extremity of the tidal work exhibit every night from sunset to sunrise such lights, and take such steps for the prevention of danger to navigation as Trinity House and the statutory harbour authority or Statutory Conservancy and Navigation Authority (as relevant) may from time to time direct.
