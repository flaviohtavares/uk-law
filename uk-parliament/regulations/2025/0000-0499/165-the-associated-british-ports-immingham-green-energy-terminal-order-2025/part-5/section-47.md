# The Associated British Ports (Immingham Green Energy Terminal) Order 2025, section 47

> Text as at 2025-07-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Deemed marine licence[^c-e662c87a63942d691ed897a4920473ba]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker is granted a deemed marine licence under Part 4 (marine licensing) of the 2009 Act to carry out the activities specified in Part 1 of Schedule 3 (deemed marine licence), subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule.
