# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Amendment of regulation 46

After regulation 46(2) (labelling of investigational medicinal products)[^f00033] insert—

> (3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a POC investigational medicinal product that is to be administered in its entirety immediately after manufacture.
