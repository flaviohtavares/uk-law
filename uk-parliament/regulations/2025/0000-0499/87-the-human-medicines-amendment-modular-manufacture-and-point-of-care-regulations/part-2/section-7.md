# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment of regulation 20

In regulation 20(2) (mixing of medicines)[^f00006], for “the combining of two or more medicinal products together” substitute “the combining together of two or more medicinal products, none of which are MM medicinal products nor POC medicinal products”.
