# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Amendment of Schedule 8

(1) Schedule 8 (material to accompany an application for a UK marketing authorisation)[^f00026] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 9A, at the beginning insert “Except in the case of an active substance that contains human cells or tissues, or that is part of a medicinal product which is (or is in a form which means a dose or part of it is) to be administered immediately after manufacture,”.

(3) For paragraph 16 substitute the following—

> **16.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), a mock-up, in accordance with Part 13 (packaging and leaflets) of—
>
> - (a) the outer packaging of the medicinal product;
> - (b) the immediate packaging of the medicinal product; and
> - (c) the package leaflet for the medicinal product.
>
> (2) Sub-paragraph (1)(a) and (b) does not apply to an application for a marketing authorisation for a POC medicinal product which is (or is in a form which means a dose or part of it is) to be administered immediately after manufacture.

.
