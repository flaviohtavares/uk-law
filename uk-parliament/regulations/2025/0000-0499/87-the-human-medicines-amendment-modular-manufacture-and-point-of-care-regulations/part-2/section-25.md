# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Amendment of regulation 175

(1) Regulation 175 (offences relating to exceptions)[^f00017] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), replace “or 170(1) (records in connection with special medicinal products etc)” with “, 170(1) (records in connection with special medicinal products etc), 170A(1) (MM medicinal products: pharmacovigilance requirements), or 170B(1) (POC medicinal products: pharmacovigilance requirements)”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), replace “or 170(2)” with “, 170(2), 170A(3) or 170B(3)”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), after “170(3)” insert “, 170A(4) or 170B(4)”.
