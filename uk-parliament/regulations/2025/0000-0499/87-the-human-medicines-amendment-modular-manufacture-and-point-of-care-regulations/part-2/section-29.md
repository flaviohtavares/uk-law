# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Amendment of regulation 257

After regulation 257(9) (packaging requirements: general)[^f00020] insert—

> (10) Nothing in this regulation applies to a POC medicinal product that is the subject of a UKMA(UK)(Category 1).

.
