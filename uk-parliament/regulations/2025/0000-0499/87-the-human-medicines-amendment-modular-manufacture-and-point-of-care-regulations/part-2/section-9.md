# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of regulation 26

In regulation 26(2) (general power to suspend, revoke or vary licences)[^f00007]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), omit “or” in the second place where it occurs; and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (c) insert—

  > - (d) in the case of a manufacturer’s licence (MM), limited to modular units specified in the MM master file associated with the licence; or
  > - (e) in the case of a manufacturer’s licence (POC), limited to POC sites specified in the POC master file associated with the licence.

  .
