# The Human Medicines (Amendment) (Modular Manufacture and Point of Care) Regulations 2025, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Amendment of regulation 202A

After regulation 202A(2)(c) (licensing authority power in relation to medicinal products subject to additional monitoring)[^f00019] insert—

> - (d) any MM medicinal product;
> - (e) any POC medicinal product.

.
