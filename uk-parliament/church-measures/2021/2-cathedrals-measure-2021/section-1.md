# Cathedrals Measure 2021, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 1. Duty to have due regard to cathedral's ecclesiastical purpose

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

A person on whom a function is conferred by or under this Measure must, in exercising that function, have due regard to—

- (a) the fact that the cathedral is the seat of the bishop and a centre of worship and mission, and
- (b) the importance of each cathedral's role in providing a focus for the life and work of the Church of England in the diocese.
