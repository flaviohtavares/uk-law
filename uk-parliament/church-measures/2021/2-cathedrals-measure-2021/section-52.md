# Cathedrals Measure 2021, section 52

## 52. Application and extent

*Extent: E*

(1) This Measure applies to cathedrals in England only but does not apply to the cathedral church of Christ in Oxford, except in so far as Schedule 4 amends a provision which applies to that cathedral church.

(2) An amendment or repeal made by this Measure has the same extent as the provision being amended or repealed.
