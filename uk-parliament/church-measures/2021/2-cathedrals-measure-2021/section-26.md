# Cathedrals Measure 2021, section 26

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 26. Allocation of housing

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

The Chapter of a cathedral may allocate a house vested in the Chapter for the use of a person holding office in connection with the cathedral as a residence from which to perform the duties of the office.
