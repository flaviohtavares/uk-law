# Cathedrals Measure 2021, section 23

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 23. Questions as to whether property permanently endowed

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If a question arises as to whether property held by or on behalf of the Chapter of a cathedral is or is not permanent endowment, it is for the Church Commissioners, on the application of the Chapter of the cathedral, to decide the matter.

(2) A decision made by the Church Commissioners under this section is final.

(3) A decision made by the Church Commissioners under this section must be set out in an instrument made under their seal.
