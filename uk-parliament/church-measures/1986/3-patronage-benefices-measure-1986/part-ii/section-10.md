# Patronage (Benefices) Measure 1986, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-01-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/1986/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Disqualification for presentation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the registered patron of a benefice or the representative of that patron, is a clerk in Holy Orders or is the spouse or civil partner[^c-69c6acc1d5dec7ca3f483ed1e2a52d13] of such a clerk, that clerk shall be disqualified for presentation to that benefice.
