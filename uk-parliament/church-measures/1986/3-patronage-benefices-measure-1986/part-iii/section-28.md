## 28. Presentation by Diocesan Board of Patronage in case of void benefice.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a benefice becomes void under section 4 of the [^c922680]Simony Act 1588 (simoniacal presentation etc. to a benefice declared void and the presentation to be made by the Crown for that turn) the presentation to that benefice upon that vacancy shall be made by the Diocesan Board of Patronage.
