# Patronage (Benefices) Measure 1986, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-01-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/1986/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Removal of certain disabilities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following enactments (which impose disabilities on patrons practising the Roman Catholic religion etc.) that is to say—

- (a) section 13 of the Presentation of Benefices Act[^m00001]1605;
- (b) section 2 of the Presentation of Benefices Act[^m00002]1688; and
- (c) section 1 of the Presentation of Benefices Act[^m00003]1713,

shall cease to have effect.

(2) Section 15 of the [^c922682]Roman Catholic Relief Act 1829 (Roman Catholic member of lay body corporate not to vote in election, presentation or appointment of persons to ecclesiastical benefice, etc., in the gift, patronage or disposal of that body) shall cease to have effect in so far as it relates to benefices.

(3) Section 17 of the Roman Catholic Relief Act 1829 (right of presentation to benefice to devolve upon the Archbishop of Canterbury for the time being where right belongs to office which is held by person professing the Roman Catholic religion) and, in section 4 of the [^c922683]Jews Relief Act 1858, the words from the beginning to “being; and” (similar provision relating to right of presentation belonging to office held by person professing the Jewish religion) shall cease to have effect.
