## 3. Functions under the Prayer Book Measures and the Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure.[^c13201521][^c13201511]

*Extent: E*

(1)—(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13201531]

(4) The powers exercisable by the said Convocations with the concurrence of the House of Laity under the Prayer Book (Versions of the [^c13201541]Bible) Measure 1965 shall be exercisable by the General Synod.

(5) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13201531]

(6) The powers exercisable by the said Convocations or the Houses thereof under the [^c13201561]Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1963 shall continue to be exercisable by them for their respective provinces.

(7) Section 1(2) of this Measure shall apply to the functions exercisable by the General Synod under this section, and accordingly the exercise of those functions shall be subject, in particular, to Article 7 of the Constitution of the General Synod.
