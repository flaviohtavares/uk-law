# Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-05-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/2007/1

> Extent: England.

## 1. General duty[^key-0ecc6519230ceffa8b45b95bc4ed784c]

*Extent: E*

It shall be the duty of any person or body carrying out functions under this Measure [^key-958c4098794f7b461cb9b79e9b50c2fb]... to have due regard to the furtherance of the mission of the Church of England.
