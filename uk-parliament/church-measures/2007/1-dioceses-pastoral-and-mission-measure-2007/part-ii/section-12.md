## 12. Duty of the bishop to keep episcopal ministry under review[^key-2dd63056893fe96070bcb3f7c793ad2c]

*Extent: E*

Each bishop of a diocese shall keep under review the provision of episcopal ministry and oversight in his diocese and shall, in carrying out that duty, consult widely such persons and bodies as he thinks fit.
