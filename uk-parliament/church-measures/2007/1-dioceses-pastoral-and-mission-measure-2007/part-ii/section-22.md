## 22. Interpretation of Part II[^c-17d06b6e0a30a9e15d33000f73028cb1]

*Extent: E*

In this Part of this Measure—

- “**the Commission**” means the Dioceses Commission established under section 2 above; and
- “**reorganisation scheme**” means a scheme made under section 7 above.
