# Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-05-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/2007/1

> Extent: England.

## 15. Special provision with respect to rights of collation[^c-7de9270b52cbb17b421cbeacb37b99d4][^c-6e1c7310b1c301087d57967c721f9bd5]

*Extent: E*

Where by virtue of any provision of an instrument made under section 13 or 14 above or a reorganisation scheme the right to collate to any benefice upon a vacancy is exercisable by a suffragan bishop of the diocese to which the instrument or scheme relates, that provision shall be of no effect during a vacancy in the see of the bishop of that diocese.
