## 70. Nominated persons to have exclusive right to promote complaint.

*Extent: E*

A person nominated under Part [^c-f56f130a05cda29c3fee5998c9ace170]... VI of this Measure to promote a complaint against an accused person shall have the right to do so to the exclusion of all others.
