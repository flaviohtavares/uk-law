## 80. Place where courts, &c. are to sit.[^key-3758a607a961b618c49e2863e7874544]

*Extent: E*

Proceedings in a court or commission exercising jurisdiction under this Measure or of a committee or inquiry under this Measure[^key-e8fccc2cb1dbc948ccc1999eb3f1be2b] [^key-6f023d26a8a15c8a8e627f81450d4664]... may be held in any place convenient to the court, commission, committee or person holding the inquiry, due regard being paid to the convenience of parties and witnesses.
