# Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1963, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2024-05-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/1963/1
>

## 75. Provisions as to lapse on avoidance of preferment.[^c-2f57aadd8b184a8b5a6ef17678d84caa]

*Extent: E*

Where by virtue of anything in or done under this Measure any preferment is vacant the time for lapse shall run from the date on which the prescribed notice of the vacancy is given.
