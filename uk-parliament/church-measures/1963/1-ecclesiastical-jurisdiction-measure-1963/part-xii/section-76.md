# Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1963, section 76

## 76. Rights of patronage during suspension or inhibition.[^c-bdf23089429d9881eb4ffcae00f9e34d]

*Extent: E*

(1) In any case in which by virtue of a censure pronounced against him a person is suspended or inhibited under this Measure for a specified time[^c-8c81987b85108f39478c15b524745f0d] from discharging all or any of the duties attaching to his preferment, any right of patronage vested in him by virtue of his preferment shall, during the period of suspension or inhibition, and subject to the provisions of the following subsection, vest in the person entitled to appoint to such preferment and so that in the case of a diocesan bishop, any such right of patronage shall vest in the archbishop of the relevant province, and in the case of an archbishop, shall vest in the archbishop of the other province.

(2) In any case in which an incumbent is himself the patron of his benefice, the right of patronage of such benefice shall, so long as the period of suspension or inhibition remains in force, vest in the archbishop of the relevant province.
