## 67. Rules for determining seniority of diocesan bishops.

*Extent: E*

For the purposes of this Measure the seniority of diocesan bishops (other than archbishops) [^c-13f01b66329225114eb9a8fffca346d6]... shall be determined by reference to the length of time that each of them has held office as diocesan in either province without interruption from any cause.
