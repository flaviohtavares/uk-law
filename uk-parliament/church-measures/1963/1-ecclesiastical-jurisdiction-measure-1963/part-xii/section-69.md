## 69. Criminal proceedings in ecclesiastical courts to be taken only in accordance with this Measure.

*Extent: E*

No proceedings by way of a criminal suit, other than those authorised by Part VI[^key-5e5b069aee9b74b1ad97e0a5385e58d8] of this Measure, shall be instituted against a person [^key-87e5ee8c5f857cd3171aae946af786bd]... in the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved, and no proceedings so authorised shall be instituted except in accordance with that Part[^key-0b08a5dbbce6bd7738169ed7f5d71593] of this Measure:
