## 3. Judges of the Arches and Chancery Courts.

*Extent: E*

(1) The judges of the Arches Court of Canterbury and the Chancery Court of York respectively for the purposes of proceedings on an appeal under section 20 of the Clergy Discipline Measure 2003[^key-62e4e53f71fcbfdd61f7b0f68febddff] shall be as set out in subsection (2) of this section[^c1021247], but proceedings which, by virtue of the following provisions of this Measure, are cognisable by either of those Courts shall be heard and disposed of by such of the judges thereof as may be determined in accordance with those provisions.

(2) Of the judges of each of the said Courts—

- (a)[^key-df274897193dc56c7b06bb87306827f7] one shall be the Dean of the Arches and Auditor;
- (b) four shall be appointed in accordance with section 20(2) and (3) of the Clergy Discipline Measure 2003.
- (d)[^key-8e1edc5afa6d49c04a71c4d19335581f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-67088813c3e6d6b9ad8a936974700ce3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-04cd98340ec928a264c6bc48fe65b7cd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-67088813c3e6d6b9ad8a936974700ce3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-67088813c3e6d6b9ad8a936974700ce3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) A person appointed under paragraph[^c1021259] (b)[^key-f7a9493868d01cd7ae226c53c6ce2a61] of subsection (2) of this section to hold the office of judge of either of the said Courts shall, before he enters on the execution of his office,—

- (a) take [^c1021256]... the oaths set out in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to this Measure[^key-8880e4323f87ed072f312ef64bda03c9] either before the archbishop of the relevant province and in the presence of the registrar of that province or in open court in the presence of that registrar; . . . [^c1021257]
- (b) . . . [^c1021258]

(8) A provincial registrar shall record the taking [^c1021257]... [^c1021264]..., of an oath [^c1021257]... in his presence in pursuance of subsection (7)[^key-5e73394d09e1da0f3195217b5dc8b64b].
