## 47. Proceedings in Arches and Chancery Courts.

*Extent: E*

(1)[^c1021305] Proceedings in the Arches Court of Canterbury or the Chancery Court of York under this Measure shall be heard and disposed of[^c-c917d26cc383b48157188ed809581a40] by the Dean of the Arches and Auditor and two diocesan chancellors designated by him for the purposes of the case.

(1A)[^c-0032ed861a5c5795743316386933bea2] The Dean of the Arches and Auditor sitting alone in either of the said Courts has jurisdiction to make interlocutory orders[^c-08ae7625134161282240e62f2039a76c]... .

(2) Proceedings under this Measure[^c-cfb4c6e316f26ac094e428a0ccf365bd] in the said Court shall be instituted and conducted in such manner as may be prescribed.
