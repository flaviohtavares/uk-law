## 36. Supplementary

*Extent: E*

(1)[^key-5acc39b999677166993c411167624dd6] This Part does not restrict or limit the powers of dealing under Part 1.

(4)[^key-5acc39b999677166993c411167624dd6] This Part does not enable a DBF to deal with an endowment belonging to a charity within the meaning of section 10 of the Charities Act 2011 except with the consent of the Charity Commission.
