# Church Property Measure 2018, section 40

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England.

## 40. Church Commissioners: information and advice[^key-0cd2ffcf2bd0b28342862933a6c75152]

*Extent: E*

(1) The Church Commissioners may seek information from a DBF on a matter concerning its functions under this Measure; and the DBF must provide the information sought.

(2) The Commissioners may give advice to a DBF on a matter concerning its functions under this Measure; and the DBF must have regard to the advice.
