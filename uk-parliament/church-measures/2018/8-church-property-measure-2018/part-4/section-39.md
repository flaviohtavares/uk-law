# Church Property Measure 2018, section 39

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England.

## 39. Memorandum and articles of association[^key-ce31231a373ce70d218a9d09b6954f88]

*Extent: E*

(1) The Memorandum and Articles of Association of a DBF are to be regarded as including the furtherance of the work of the Church of England by the DBF's exercise of functions under this Measure and whatever ancillary powers are necessary for that purpose.

(2) In the case of a DBF which is not a registered company, the reference to the Memorandum and Articles of Association is to be read as a reference to its constitution.
