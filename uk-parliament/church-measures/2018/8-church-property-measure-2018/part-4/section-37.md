# Church Property Measure 2018, section 37

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England.

## 37. Sequestrations[^c-960004976c68c79291d295e64cf1c240]

*Extent: E*

(1) The sequestrators of a vacant benefice must, at the close of the sequestration, pay to the DBF the balance in their hands, as certified by the bishop or a person authorised by him or her; and the DBF must allocate the amount received to the income account of the diocesan stipends fund.

(2) This section does not apply where a period of suspension of presentation follows, whether immediately or after an interval, a period during which the benefice has been vacant.
