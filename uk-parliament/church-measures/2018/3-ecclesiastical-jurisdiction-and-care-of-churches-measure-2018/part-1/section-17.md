## 17. Judges: appointment[^c-e372a65b969b6c514cc95413590879eb]

*Extent: E*

(1) The Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved is constituted of five judges, appointed by Her Majesty.

(2) Two of the five judges must be persons each of whom—

- (a) holds or has held high judicial office, and
- (b) has made a declaration that he or she is a communicant.

(3) Three of the five judges must be persons each of whom is or has been a diocesan bishop.
