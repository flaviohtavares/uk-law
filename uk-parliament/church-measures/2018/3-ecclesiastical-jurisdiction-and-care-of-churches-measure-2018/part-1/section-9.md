## 9. The Arches and Chancery Courts: continuation[^key-05bf110f03cb157ac4a5d9bd83782021]

*Extent: E*

(1) For each province there is to continue to be a court of the archbishop.

(2) The court for the province of Canterbury is to continue to be known as the Arches Court of Canterbury.

(3) The court for the province of York is to continue to be known as the Chancery Court of York.
