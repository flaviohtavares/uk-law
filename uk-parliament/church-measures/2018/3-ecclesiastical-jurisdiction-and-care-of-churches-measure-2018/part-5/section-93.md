## 93. Section 92: application to Crown land[^c-1b6b9b2f0e6ec5ffc6f25b17f40ef65e]

*Extent: E*

(1) Section 92 applies to Crown land and to buildings situated on Crown land as it applies to other land and buildings.

(2) But a condition or requirement within section 92(3)(b) may not be imposed by an order under section 92 in relation to Crown land or a building situated on Crown land without the consent of the appropriate authority.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), land which is used for the purposes of the Church of England and which will become Crown land on ceasing to be so used or on the exercise of a right of re-entry is to be treated as Crown land.

(4) “Crown land” and “appropriate authority” each have the same meaning as in Part 13 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (see section 293 of that Act).

(5) Any question as to which authority is the appropriate authority in relation to any land or building is to be determined by the Treasury.
