## 57. Curtilages of churches[^key-8ee3189505650290420436dfbe5608fb]

*Extent: E*

For the avoidance of doubt, it is hereby declared that, where unconsecrated land forms or is part of the curtilage of a church within the jurisdiction of the consistory court, that court has the same jurisdiction over that land as over the church.
