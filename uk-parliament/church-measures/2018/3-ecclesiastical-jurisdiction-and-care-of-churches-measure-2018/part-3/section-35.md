# Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction and Care of Churches Measure 2018, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2025-12-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/2018/3
>
> Extent: England.

## 35. Duty to have regard to church's purpose and environmental protection[^c-e03edf1dcee16a3836873eb10fe961c5][^c-7016c9170f53967faa5ee3258ebce366]

*Extent: E*

A person carrying out functions of care and conservation under this Measure, or under any other enactment or any rule of law relating to churches, must have due regard to —[^c-ab20df66dc7a2c6944c1fdea6734b81b]

- (a) the role of a church as a local centre of worship and mission , and[^c-679b347d6ddfb5c68e7e71bf60265cf1]
- (b) the importance of environmental protection.
