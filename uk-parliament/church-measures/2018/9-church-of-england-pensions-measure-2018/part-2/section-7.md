# Church of England Pensions Measure 2018, section 7

> Text as at 2024-01-25.
>
> Extent: England.

## 7. The scheme[^key-01b7fad7f0aaedc7e1c46aa9a6cfd964]

*Extent: E*

The funded scheme applies only to pensionable service performed by a clerk, deaconess , licensed lay worker or lay residentiary canon[^key-d1932a38314a7c9606a4e62bb4615d56] on or after 1 January 1998.
