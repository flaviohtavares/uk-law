## 2. The Church of England Pensions Board[^c-ef8129ede62937a45f302ec062ccc889]

*Extent: E*

(1) There is to continue to be a body corporate known as the Church of England Pensions Board (“**the Board**”).

(2) Schedule 1 (which makes further provision about the Board) has effect.
