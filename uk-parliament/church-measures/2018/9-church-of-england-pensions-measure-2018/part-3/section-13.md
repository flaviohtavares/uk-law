> Text as at 2024-01-25.

> Extent: England.

## 13. The scheme[^c-b3657e20753df8e44bd96fbe869c7bca]

*Extent: E*

(1) The past service scheme applies only to pensionable service performed by a clerk, deaconess or licensed lay worker before 1 January 1998.

(2) The past service scheme consists of the provisions set out in this Part.
