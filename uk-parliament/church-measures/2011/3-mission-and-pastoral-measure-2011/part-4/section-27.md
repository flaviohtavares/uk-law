## 27. Transmission of copies of scheme[^c-b429874265a50db9f3705a5a907f15d8]

*Extent: E*

(1) The Commissioners shall send a copy of every scheme made by them under this Part to the interested parties and to the Church Buildings Council.

(2) The Commissioners shall also send a copy of every such scheme to the registrar of the diocese concerned, who shall file it in the diocesan registry.
