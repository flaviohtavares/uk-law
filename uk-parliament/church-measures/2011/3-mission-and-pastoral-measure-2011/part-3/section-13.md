## 13. Transmission of copies of scheme or order[^c-8ed86edab4e419e598930f415644492d]

*Extent: E*

(1) The mission and pastoral committee shall send a copy of every scheme made by the Commissioners under this Part and of every order made by the bishop under section 11(7) to the interested parties.

(2) The mission and pastoral committee shall also send a copy of every such scheme or order to the registrar of the diocese concerned, who shall file it in the diocesan registry.
