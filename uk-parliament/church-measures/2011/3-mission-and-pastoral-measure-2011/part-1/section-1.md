# Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-01-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/2011/3
>
> Extent: England.

## 1. General duty[^key-ffa3c35aa6671097ee2bf6079f73fb8a]

*Extent: E*

It shall be the duty of any person or body carrying out functions under this Measure to have due regard to the furtherance of the mission of the Church of England.
