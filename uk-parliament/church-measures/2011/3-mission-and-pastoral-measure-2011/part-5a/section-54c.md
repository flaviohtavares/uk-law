## 54C. Amendment and revocation[^c-19834b94f23b1ff0eb3f543b583f4cf9]

*Extent: E*

(1) A bishop's pastoral order may be amended or revoked by a subsequent bishop's pastoral order.

(2) An amending order may provide for any matters for which provision could have been made by the order to be amended.

(3) A bishop's pastoral order, or a specified provision of it, may be amended or revoked under this section before it comes into operation.

(4) Where a bishop's pastoral order has made provision for the holding in plurality of two or more benefices and the provision has been terminated, the bishop may, by instrument, make such consequential amendments to the order as the bishop thinks fit.

(5) The bishop must send a copy of an instrument under subsection (4) to the Commissioners.
