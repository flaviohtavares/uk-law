# Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-01-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukcm/2011/3

> Extent: England.

## 107. Saving for planning legislation[^c-5bdf81bb6cab4994fc13b333109d4b39]

*Extent: E*

Notwithstanding that the development of any land is or may be authorised or regulated by or under this Measure, the provisions of the enactments relating to town and country planning, and any restrictions or powers thereby imposed or conferred in relation to land, shall apply and may be exercised in relation to any such development.
