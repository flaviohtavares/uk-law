# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, section 59

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 59. Panel of approved lawyers[^c-2cb9503abe1fa992fd7312b975f906b3]

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

The power to make rules under [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1994/3/section/4) of the [Church of England (Legal Aid) Measure 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1994/3/contents) includes power to make rules—

- (a) requiring the compilation and maintenance of a list of solicitors’ firms and barristers approved in accordance with those rules to act in connection with a complaint;
- (b) providing that an application for legal aid in connection with a complaint may, except in specified circumstances, be granted only if the solicitors’ firm or barrister chosen by the applicant is included on that list.
