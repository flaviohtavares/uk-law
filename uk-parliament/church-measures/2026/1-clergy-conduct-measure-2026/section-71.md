# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 71. Repeals[^c-06de30e1f1535dac681396f6c1104cc6]

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The CDM is repealed.

(2) In consequence of that, the following are repealed—

- (a) in the [Constitutional Reform Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4/contents), in [Schedule 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4/schedule/17), [paragraph 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4/schedule/17/paragraph/35) and the preceding cross-heading;
- (b) in the [Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2007/1/contents), [section 63](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2007/1/section/63)[(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2007/1/section/63/subsection/7);
- (c) in the [Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2013/2/contents), [sections 1 to 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2013/2/section/1) and [9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2013/2/section/9)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2013/2/section/9/subsection/1) and the Schedule;
- (d) in the [Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/contents), [sections 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/section/1)[(1) to (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/section/1) and [7 to 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/section/7);
- (e) in the [Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction and Care of Churches Measure 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/3/contents), in [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/3/schedule/3), [paragraphs 11 to 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/3/schedule/3/paragraph/11) and the preceding cross-heading;
- (f) in the [Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/4/contents), in [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/4/schedule), [paragraph 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/4/schedule/paragraph/7) and the preceding cross-heading;
- (g) in the [Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2019/1/contents), in [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2019/1/schedule/2), [paragraph 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2019/1/schedule/2/paragraph/28) and the preceding cross-heading;
- (h) in the [Cathedrals Measure 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/2/contents), in [Schedule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/2/schedule/4), [paragraph 26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/2/schedule/4/paragraph/26) and the preceding cross-heading and Part 2;
- (i) in the [Safeguarding (Code of Practice) Measure 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/3/contents), [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/3/section/2)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2021/3/section/2/subsection/4);
- (j) in the [Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/contents), [sections 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/9)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/9/subsection/3) and [11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/3), [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/4) and [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/5)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/5) and [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/5) and [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2024/1/section/11/subsection/6).

(3) The repeal in subsection (1) does not apply to the following provisions of the CDM—

- (a) in section 43(1) (interpretation), the definition of “the 1963 Measure”,
- (b) section 44 and Schedule 1 (amendments of EJM), and
- (c) section 48 (short title etc.).
