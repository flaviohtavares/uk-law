# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, section 4

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: England.

## 4. President and Deputy President of Tribunals: continuation[^key-b927aefbd572a06c02f402c9a695357b]

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The office of President of Tribunals and the office of Deputy President of Tribunals each continue to be established.

(2) The appointment to each office is made by the Appointments Committee on the recommendation of the Dean of the Arches and Auditor.

(3) A person may be appointed to either office only if that person—

- (a) holds or has held high judicial office, or
- (b) holds or has held the office of circuit judge or has the qualifications required for holding that office.
