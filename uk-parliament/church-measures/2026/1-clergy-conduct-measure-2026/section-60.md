# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, section 60

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 60. Place where court or tribunal to sit[^c-3b827c7dea25d85c02454bbab67c92d4]

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

Proceedings in a court or tribunal exercising jurisdiction under this Measure may be held in a place convenient to the court or tribunal, with due regard being had to the convenience of parties and witnesses.
