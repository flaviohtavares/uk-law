# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, Schedule 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## Schedule 2 — Transitional and saving provisions

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 72

### 1. Proceedings under the CDM[^key-c6d7789c0a0ab33ae59b5547e7944ca7]

Proceedings under the CDM which, immediately before the commencement of the repeal of the CDM under section 71 of this Measure, have yet to be completed are to continue as if the CDM had not been repealed.

### 2. Penalties etc. under the CDM[^key-c9c3c01e6f5a5b7451b7d58fac40e9db]

(1) A penalty imposed under the CDM, or a censure, deposition, declaration of deprivation and disqualification or notice of inhibition imposed under the EJM, and having effect immediately before the commencement of the repeal of the CDM under section 71 of this Measure is to be treated as if it were a penalty imposed under this Measure.

(2) Where the period of a conditional deferment or conditional discharge under section 14 or 25 of the CDM has, immediately before the commencement of the repeal of the CDM under section 71 of this Measure, yet to expire, the period is to continue despite the repeal.

(3) Misconduct which was committed before the period of a conditional deferment would have expired under the CDM, but for the repeal, may be dealt with together with the conduct to which the conditional deferment relates.

(4) Misconduct which was committed before the period of a conditional discharge would have expired under the CDM, but for the repeal, may be dealt with together with the misconduct to which the conditional discharge relates as if a finding of that misconduct had just been made under this Measure.

### 3. Provincial panels[^key-ceb96bbfff3b0e7e677746b2139d8a3f]

(1) Each person included on a provincial panel under section 21 of the CDM immediately before the commencement of section 10 of this Measure continues to be included on the panel after that commencement; and the composition of the panel is to be treated as complying with that section.

(2) In the case of a person included on a provincial panel under sub-paragraph (1) above, the six-year period under section 21 of the CDM that was running immediately before the commencement of section 11 of this Measure—

- (a) continues after that commencement until the date when it would have expired under section 21 of the CDM if that section were still in force, and
- (b) is to be treated as a period of six years for the purposes of section 11 of this Measure.

(3) If a casual vacancy occurs among those included on a provincial panel under sub-paragraph (1), the nomination of a person to fill the vacancy must—

- (a) be made by the bishop or archbishop who made the nomination under section 21 of the CDM in respect of which the vacancy has occurred, and
- (b) be made subject to the same conditions as that nomination was.

(4) A person nominated to fill a casual vacancy is included on the provincial panel concerned until the expiry of the six-year period that is continuing under sub-paragraph (2); and on the expiry of that period—

- (a) section 11(2) of this Measure applies where the nomination under section 21 of the CDM (or the first nomination under that section for the category concerned) was made under subsection (2)(a) or (b) or (3) of that section;
- (b) section 11(3) of this Measure applies where the nomination under section 21 of the CDM (or the first nomination under that section for the category concerned) was made under subsection (2)(c) of that section.

### 4. Safeguarding guidance[^key-742f6a2cd247feacb51135bb82f537d0]

In the case of guidance issued by the House of Bishops that is in operation by virtue of [article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/118/article/3) of the [Safeguarding (Code of Practice) Measure 2021 (Commencement and Transitional Provision) Order 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/118/contents), section 3(1)(b) of this Measure has effect as if it also referred to a failure to have due regard to that guidance.

### 5. General[^key-b23d0508e8d2b0699e26aaaf2b5a9029]

The provisions of this Schedule do not affect the application of [section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30/section/16) of the [Interpretation Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30/contents) (which provides for general savings on a repeal).
