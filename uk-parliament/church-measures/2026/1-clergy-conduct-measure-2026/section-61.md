# Clergy Conduct Measure 2026, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England.

## 61. Evidence[^key-5393b841e8a1d8123a119a2d269652f2]

*Extent: E*

**Not yet in force.**

A court or tribunal exercising jurisdiction under this Measure has the same powers as the High Court in relation to—

- (a) the attendance and examination of witnesses, and
- (b) the production and inspection of documents.
