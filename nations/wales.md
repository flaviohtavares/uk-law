# Wales

The Senedd (Welsh Parliament, formerly the National Assembly for Wales) makes laws on devolved matters. Laws of the UK Parliament also apply in Wales where they extend to it, often as "England and Wales"; each file says where it extends.

Repealed laws are not listed here; they are still in their folders. Laws marked *changes pending* have changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since.
