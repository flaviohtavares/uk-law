# Northern Ireland

The Northern Ireland Assembly makes laws on devolved matters. When the Assembly was suspended, laws were made by Order in Council at Westminster. Laws of the UK Parliament also apply in Northern Ireland where they extend to it; each file says where it extends.

Repealed laws are not listed here; they are still in their folders. Laws marked *changes pending* have changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since.
