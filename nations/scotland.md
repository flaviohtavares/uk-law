# Scotland

The Scottish Parliament makes laws on devolved matters such as health, education, justice and housing. Laws of the UK Parliament also apply in Scotland where they extend to it; each file says where it extends.

Repealed laws are not listed here; they are still in their folders. Laws marked *changes pending* have changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since.
