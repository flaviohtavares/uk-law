# England

England has no parliament of its own. Laws for England are made by the UK Parliament at Westminster, often together with Wales ("England and Wales"). Each file in [`uk-parliament/`](../uk-parliament/) says where it extends. Below are the Acts of the Parliament of England, before the union with Scotland in 1707, that are still in force.

Repealed laws are not listed here; they are still in their folders. Laws marked *changes pending* have changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since.
