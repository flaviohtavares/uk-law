# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2023-11-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2015/9
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Single jurisdiction: abolition of county court divisions and petty sessions districts[^c-8b63d789357e7b9c3dc47a0c85adf6b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Northern Ireland is no longer to be divided into county court divisions and petty sessions districts.

(2) The jurisdiction and powers of a county court or a magistrates' court are exercisable throughout the whole of Northern Ireland.
