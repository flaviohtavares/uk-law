## 6. Consequential amendments

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-d18114ce200d7a313649b15b6807b540] Schedule 1 (which contains amendments consequential on the preceding provisions of this Part) has effect.

(2)[^c-edcf2766f1deeb16a3d067e6f78cdab3] The Department may by order make such supplementary, incidental or consequential provision as it considers appropriate in consequence of, or for giving full effect to, this Part.

(3)[^c-6b84396fcc77486defea1cc5999193e5] An order under subsection (2) may amend, repeal, revoke or otherwise modify any statutory provision.
