## 59. Provisions that violent offences prevention orders may contain[^key-572006a46d55e6f3e2acd634e972c9e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A violent offences prevention order may contain provisions prohibiting D from doing anything described in the order or requiring D to do anything described in the order (or both).

(2) The only prohibitions or requirements that may be included in the order are those necessary for the purpose of protecting the public from the risk of serious violent harm caused by D.
