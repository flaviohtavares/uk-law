# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 67

## 67. Notification requirements: periodic notification

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] An offender subject to notification requirements must, within the applicable period after each notification date, notify to the police the information mentioned in section 65(2), unless the offender has already given a notification under section 66(1) within that period.

(2)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] A “**notification date**” means, in relation to the offender, the date of any notification given by the offender under section 65(1) or 66(1) or subsection (1).

(3)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] Where the applicable period would (apart from this subsection) end while subsection (4) applies, that period is to be treated as continuing until the end of the period of 3 days beginning with the date on which subsection (4) first ceases to apply.

(4)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] This subsection applies if the offender is—

- (a) remanded in or committed to custody by an order of a court,
- (b) serving a custodial sentence,
- (c) detained in a hospital, or
- (d) outside the United Kingdom.

(5) In this section “**the applicable period**” means—

- (a)[^key-e9694c50ad54f63e7c183a6c9ac97a1b] in any case where subsection (6) applies, such period as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Department, and
- (b)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] in any other case, the period of one year.

(6)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] This subsection applies if the last home address notified by the offender under section 65(1) or 66(1) or subsection (1) was the address or location of such a place as is mentioned in section 65(4)(b).

(7)[^key-8a7a24a8939b09b5d7fccf0135dab8fb] Nothing in this section applies to an offender who is subject to an interim violent offences prevention order.
