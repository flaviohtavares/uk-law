# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2023-11-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2015/9
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 66. Notification requirements: changes

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] An offender subject to notification requirements must, within the period of 3 days beginning with the date on which any notifiable event occurs, notify to the police—

- (a) the required new information, and
- (b) the information mentioned in section 65(2).

(2) A “**notifiable event**” means—

- (a)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the use by the offender of a name which has not been notified to the police under section 65 or this section;
- (b)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] any change of the offender's home address;
- (c)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the expiry of any qualifying period during which the offender has resided or stayed at any premises in the United Kingdom the address of which has not been notified to the police under section 65 or this section;
- (d)[^key-68a01931601baf63d3e64080092e184b] any prescribed change of circumstances; or
- (e)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the release of the offender from custody pursuant to an order of a court or from a custodial sentence or detention in a hospital.

(3) The “required new information” is—

- (a)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the name referred to in subsection (2)(a),
- (b)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the new home address (see subsection (2)(b)),
- (c)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the address of the premises referred to in subsection (2)(c),
- (d)[^key-b25a4ce3e7ac261f9ec07c41c372183a] the prescribed details, or
- (e)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] the fact that the offender has been released as mentioned in subsection (2)(e),

as the case may be.

(4)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] A notification under subsection (1) may be given before the notifiable event occurs, but in that case the offender must also specify the date when the event is expected to occur.

(5)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] If a notification is given in accordance with subsection (4) and the event to which it relates occurs more than 2 days before the date specified, the notification does not affect the duty imposed by subsection (1).

(6)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] If a notification is given in accordance with subsection (4) and the event to which it relates has not occurred by the end of the period of 3 days beginning with the date specified—

- (a) the notification does not affect the duty imposed by subsection (1), and
- (b) the offender must, within the period of 6 days beginning with the date specified, notify to the police the fact that the event did not occur within the period of 3 days beginning with the date specified.

(7)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] Section 65(3) applies to the determination of—

- (a) any period of 3 days for the purposes of subsection (1), or
- (b) any period of 6 days for the purposes of subsection (6),

as it applies to the determination of the period of 3 days mentioned in section 65(1).

(8)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] In this section—

- (a) “**prescribed change of circumstances**” means any change—
  - (i) occurring in relation to any matter in respect of which information is required to be notified by virtue of section 65(2)(h), and
  - (ii) of a description prescribed by regulations made by the Department;
- (b) “**the prescribed details**”, in relation to a prescribed change of circumstances, means such details of the change as may be so prescribed.

(9)[^key-c182b4cff17b2249ed16ec4593dd5c24] In this section “**qualifying period**” means—

- (a) a period of 7 days, or
- (b) two or more periods, in any period of 12 months, which taken together amount to 7 days.
