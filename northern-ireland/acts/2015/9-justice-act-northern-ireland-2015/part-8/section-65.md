# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2023-11-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2015/9
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 65. Notification requirements: initial notification

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] An offender subject to notification requirements must notify the required information to the police within the period of 3 days beginning with the date on which the violent offences prevention order or the interim violent offences prevention order comes into force in relation to the offender (“**the relevant date**”).

(2) The “required information” is the following information about the offender—

- (a)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] date of birth;
- (b)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] national insurance number;
- (c)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] name on the relevant date or, if the offender used two or more names on that date, each of those names;
- (d)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] home address on the relevant date;
- (e)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] name on the date on which the notification is given or, if the offender used two or more names on that date, each of those names;
- (f)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] home address on the date on which the notification is given;
- (g)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] the address of any other premises in the United Kingdom at which on that date the offender regularly resides or stays;
- (h)[^key-2c990bff686995d12bf82acd31c55bf5] any information prescribed by regulations made by the Department.

(3)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] When determining the period of 3 days mentioned in subsection (1), there is to be disregarded any time when the offender is—

- (a) remanded in or committed to custody by an order of a court;
- (b) serving a custodial sentence;
- (c) detained in a hospital; or
- (d) outside the United Kingdom.

(4)[^key-0722504bf22b45d8e1511afa60c6cc7e] In this Part “**home address**” means in relation to the offender—

- (a) the address of the offender's sole or main residence in the United Kingdom, or
- (b) if the offender has no such residence, the address or location of a place in the United Kingdom where the offender can regularly be found or, if there is more than one such place, such one of them as the offender selects.
