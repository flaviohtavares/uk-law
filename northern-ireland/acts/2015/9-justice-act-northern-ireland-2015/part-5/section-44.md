# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2023-11-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2015/9
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Electronic transmission of applications[^key-9401e818dc100db9ac65b0933e12027e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 113A of the Police Act 1997 (criminal record certificates) after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) But an application for a criminal record certificate need not be countersigned by a registered person if—
>
> - (a) the application is transmitted to the Department electronically by a registered person who satisfies conditions determined by the Department, and
> - (b) it is transmitted in accordance with requirements determined by the Department.

.

(2) In section 113B of that Act (enhanced criminal record certificates) after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) But an application for an enhanced criminal record certificate need not be countersigned by a registered person if—
>
> - (a) the application is transmitted to the Department electronically by a registered person who satisfies conditions determined by the Department, and
> - (b) it is transmitted in accordance with requirements determined by the Department.

.
