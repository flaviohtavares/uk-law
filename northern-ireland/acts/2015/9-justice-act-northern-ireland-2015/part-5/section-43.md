# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 43

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Applications for enhanced criminal record certificates

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 113B of the Police Act 1997 (enhanced criminal record certificates) in subsection (2) for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) be accompanied by—
>   - (i) a statement by the registered person that the certificate is required for the purposes of an exempted question asked for a prescribed purpose; or
>   - (ii) a statement by the applicant that the certificate is required for a prescribed purpose.
