# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 120

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 120. Change to the Institution of Civil Engineers

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

If the Institution of Civil Engineers ceases to exist, the Department may byorder amend references in this Act to the Institution and to its President.
