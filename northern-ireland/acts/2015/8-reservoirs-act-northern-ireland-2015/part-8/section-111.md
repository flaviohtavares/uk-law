# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 111

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 111. Fees in relation to appeals[^c-b39c7359d58d2263d5e1f343cd800813]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister may byregulations specify the fees to be paid to the Water Appeals Commission in connection with appeals referred to in section 109(2).
