# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 114

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 114. Grants

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Department may by regulations make provision as to the payment of grants to reservoir managers of controlled reservoirs for the purpose of enabling or assisting the managers to comply with their obligations arising by virtue of this Act.

(2) Regulations made under subsection (1) must require such grants to be subject to such terms and conditions as the Department may determine (including conditions as to repayment in the event of contravention of the other terms or conditions on which the grant is made).
