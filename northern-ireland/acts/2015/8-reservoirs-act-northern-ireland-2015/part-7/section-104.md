# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 104

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 104. Removal of panel members

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Department may remove a civil engineer from a panel where it is satisfied that the engineer is not suitable to continue to be a member of it.

(2) The Department must serve on a civil engineer removed from a panel under subsection (1) notice of the engineer's removal from the panel.

(3) Notice under subsection (2) must specify the grounds on which the engineer has been removed from the panel.
