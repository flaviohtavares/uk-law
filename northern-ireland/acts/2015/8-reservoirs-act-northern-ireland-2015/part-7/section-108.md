# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 108

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108. Reimbursement of costs incurred by Institution of Civil Engineers

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Department may reimburse the Institution of Civil Engineers theamount of any costs reasonably incurred by the Institution by virtue of—

- (a) arrangements entered into (whether directly or indirectly) between the Department and the Institution for the purposes of section 102(1)(b),
- (b) section 107.
