# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 42

> Text as at 2021-06-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Meaning of “relevant works” for purposes of Act

*Extent: N.I.*

Any reference in this Act to “**relevant works**” is a reference to any workscarried out for the purpose of the construction or alteration of a controlled reservoir.
