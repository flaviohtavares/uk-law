# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 50

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Preliminary and final certificates: compliance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The reservoir manager of a controlled reservoir must (subject to section62) ensure that the requirements of any preliminary certificate or final certificate for the time being applicable to the reservoir are complied with.
