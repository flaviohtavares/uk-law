# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 28

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Visual inspection directed under section 26(4)(a): further provision

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The reservoir manager must comply with any direction under section 26(4)(a).

(2) The reservoir manager must—

- (a) maintain a written record of each visual inspection carried out in pursuance of a direction under section 26(4)(a),
- (b) make the record available on request to the supervising engineer,
- (c) give notice to the supervising engineer and the Department of anything identified during any such visual inspection which might affect the safety of the reservoir.

(3) Notice under subsection (2)(c) must be given as soon as is reasonably practicable after the visual inspection during which the thing was identified.
