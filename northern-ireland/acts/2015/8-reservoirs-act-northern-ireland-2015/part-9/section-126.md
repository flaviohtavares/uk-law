# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 126

> Text as at 2021-06-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 126. Supplementary, incidental, consequential etc. provision

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may by order make—

- (a) such supplementary, incidental or consequential provision,
- (b) such transitional, transitory or saving provision,

as it considers appropriate for the purposes of, or in consequence of, or for giving full effect to, this Act or any provision made by or under it.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may amend, repeal, revoke or otherwise modify any statutory provision (including this Act).

(3) In subsection (2), “**modify**” includes the making of additions, omissions and exceptions.
