# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 131

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 131. Duty to report on operation of this Act

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Department must, not later than three years after this Act receives Royal Assent, publish a report on the operation of this Act.

(2) The Department must lay the report before the Assembly.
