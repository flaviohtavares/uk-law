# Reservoirs Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 129

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The statutory provisions set out in Schedule 3 have effect subject to the minor and consequential amendments specified in the Schedule.

(2) The provisions set out in the first column of Schedule 4 are repealed to the extent specified in the second column of that Schedule.
