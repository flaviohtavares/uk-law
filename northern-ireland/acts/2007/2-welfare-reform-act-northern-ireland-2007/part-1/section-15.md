## 15. Directions about work-related activity

*Extent: N.I.*

[(1)[^c22082961][^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e] In prescribed circumstances, the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e] [^key-5ec3f0f41dadc933ec661333d55216c5]... may by direction given to a person subject to a requirement imposed under section 13(1) provide that the activity specified in the direction is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]

- (a) to be the only activity which, in the person's case, is to be regarded as being work-related activity; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]
- (b) to be regarded, in the person's case, as not being work-related activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]

(1A) But a direction under subsection (1) may not specify medical or surgical treatment as the only activity which, in any person's case, is to be regarded as being work-related activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]

(2) A direction under subsection (1) given to any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]

- (a) must be reasonable, having regard to the person's circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]
- (b) must be given to the person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e] in such manner as the Department[^key-383e13e1e9574d38b681542a362ce404] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e] [^key-5ec3f0f41dadc933ec661333d55216c5]... thinks fit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e] ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]
- (c) may be varied or revoked by a subsequent direction under subsection (1).\] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]

(3) Where a direction under subsection (1) varies or revokes a previous direction, it may provide for the variation or revocation to have effect from a time before the giving of the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_128918d6-1c88-45d7-9a7c-2b52ebac6c8e]
