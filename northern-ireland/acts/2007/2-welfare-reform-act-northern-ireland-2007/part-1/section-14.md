# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Action plans in connection with work-focused interviews[^c21203361]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd] The Department[^c-13be95c1975f84b4df835867666450f6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd] shall in prescribed circumstances provide a person subject to a requirement imposed under section 12(1) with a document prepared for such purposes as may be prescribed (in this section referred to as an action plan). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]

(2) Regulations may make provision about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]

- (a) the form of action plans; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (b) the content of action plans; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (c) the review and updating of action plans. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]

(3) Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision for action plans which are provided to a person who is subject under section 13 to a requirement to undertake work-related activity to contain particulars of activity which, if undertaken, would enable the requirement to be met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]

(4) Regulations may make provision for reconsideration of an action plan at the request of the person to whom the plan is provided and may, in particular, make provision about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]

- (a) the circumstances in which reconsideration may be requested; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (b) the period within which any reconsideration must take place; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (c) the matters to which regard must be had when deciding on reconsideration whether the plan should be changed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (d) notification of the decision on reconsideration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
- (e) the giving of directions for the purpose of giving effect to the decision on reconsideration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6e8ebef7-701b-41e3-f076-c78f26cd99cd]
