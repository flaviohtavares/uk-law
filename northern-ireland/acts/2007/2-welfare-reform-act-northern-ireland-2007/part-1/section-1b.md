# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 1B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1B. Further entitlement after time-limiting[^key-adf299a82df42e31031a2726f3048a76]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person's entitlement to an employment and support allowance[^key-a0e23a255e503003d6a6eb6d62c1251e] has ceased as a result of section 1A(1) or (4) but—

- (a) the person has not at any subsequent time ceased to have (or to be treated as having) limited capability for work,
- (b) the person satisfies the basic conditions, and
- (c) the person has (or is treated as having) limited capability for work-related activity,

the claimant is entitled to an employment and support allowance by virtue of this section.

(2) An employment and support allowance entitlement which is based on this section is to be regarded as a contributory allowance for the purposes of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8fe5cf7797f4975e828d860b41fd43ee]
