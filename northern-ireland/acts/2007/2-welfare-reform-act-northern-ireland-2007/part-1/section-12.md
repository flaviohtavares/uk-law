# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Work-focused interviews[^c21203321]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c21203331][^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30] Regulations may make provision for or in connection with imposing on a person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

- (a) entitled to an employment and support allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (b) not a member of the support group *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30] or a lone parent of a child under the age of one[^c22081131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30],

a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews as a condition of continuing to be entitled to the full amount payable to him in respect of the allowance apart from the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(2) Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

- (a) prescribing circumstances in which such a person is subject to a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (b) for notifying such a person of any such requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (c) prescribing the work-focused interviews in which a person who is subject to such a requirement is required to take part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (d) for determining, in relation to work-focused interviews under the regulations, when and how the interview is to be conducted and, if it is to be conducted face to face, where it is to take place; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (e) for notifying persons who are required under the regulations to take part in a work-focused interview of what is determined in respect of the matters mentioned in paragraph (d); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (f) prescribing circumstances in which a person who is a party to a work-focused interview under the regulations is to be regarded as having, or not having, taken part in it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (g) for securing that the appropriate consequence follows if a person who is required under the regulations to take part in a work-focused interview— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
  - (i) fails to take part in the interview, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30] does not, within a prescribed period, show that he had good cause for that failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (h) prescribing matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person had good cause for any failure to comply with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (i) prescribing circumstances in which a person is, or is not, to be regarded as having good cause for any such failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2)(g), the appropriate consequence of a failure falling within that provision is that the amount payable to the person in question in respect of an employment and support allowance is reduced in accordance with regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(4) Regulations under subsection (3) may, in particular, make provision for determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

- (a) the amount by which an allowance is to be reduced, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (b) when the reduction is to start, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (c) how long it is to continue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

and may include provision prescribing circumstances in which the amount of the reduction is to be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(5) Regulations under this section shall include provision for a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews to cease to have effect if the person subject to the requirement becomes a member of the support group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(6) Regulations under this section may include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

- (a) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe a requirement to take part in a work-focused interview that would otherwise apply to a person by virtue of such regulations is not to apply, or is to be treated as not having applied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (b) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe such a requirement is not to apply until a prescribed time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
- (c) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe matters mentioned in subsection (2)(d) may be redetermined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]

(7) In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]**work-focused interview *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]**” means an interview by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30] the Department[^c-f1895469e5dd8757df0af23e01e18d02] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30] conducted for such purposes connected with getting the person interviewed into work, or keeping him in work, as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5a5e33b9-22f7-4e18-c829-b85ed032ee30]
