# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Financial provisions relating to Part 1

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) There shall be paid out of the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund any sums payable by way of employment and support allowance[^key-a54e0b308f0fce2400c8c49e26ddb01f].

(2) The Department shall pay into the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund sums estimated by it to be equivalent in amount to sums recovered by it in connection with payments of employment and support[^key-6dacea16cc30e5dabfd012a53eba4df8] allowance.

(3) The Department shall pay into the Consolidated Fund sums estimated by it to be equivalent in amount to sums recovered by it in connection with payments made by way of income-related allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44a58a23595e421214c201fc56696ceb]
