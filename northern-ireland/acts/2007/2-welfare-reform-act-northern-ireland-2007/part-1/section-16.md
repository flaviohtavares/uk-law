# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2
>

## 16. Contracting out[^c21203371]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64] The following functions of the Department may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as it may authorise for the purpose, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (za (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7cf36ac701dd310ea9406c601a8edcb7][^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64] any function under section 1C in relation to a claimant commitment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (a) conducting interviews under section 12; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) providing documents under section 14; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) giving, varying or revoking directions under section 15. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(2) Regulations may provide for any of the following functions of the Department to be exercisable by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as it may authorise for the purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) any function under regulations under any of sections 11 to 15, except the making of a decision to which subsection (3) applies (an “excluded decision”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) the function under Article 10(1) of the 1998 Order (revision of decisions), so far as relating to decisions, except excluded decisions, that relate to any matter arising under such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) the function under Article 11(1) of that Order (superseding of decisions), so far as relating to decisions, except excluded decisions, of the Department that relate to any matter arising under such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (d) any function under Chapter 2 of Part 2 of that Order (social security decisions), except Article 25(2) and (3) (decisions involving issues that arise on appeal in other cases), which relates to the exercise of any of the functions falling within paragraphs (a) to (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(3) This subsection applies to the following decisions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) a decision about whether a person has failed to comply with a requirement imposed by regulations under section 11, 12 or 13; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) a decision about whether a person had good cause for failure to comply with such a requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) a decision about reduction of an employment and support allowance in consequence of failure to comply with such a requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(4) Regulations under subsection (2) may provide that a function to which that subsection applies may be exercised— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as the regulations may provide, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) either generally or in such cases or areas as the regulations may provide, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as the regulations may provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(5) An authorisation given by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), may authorise the exercise of the function concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as may be specified in the authorisation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) either generally or in such cases or areas as may be so specified, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(6) In the case of an authorisation given by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), subsection (5) is subject to the provisions of the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(7) An authorisation given by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) may specify its duration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) may be revoked at any time by the Department, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (c) shall not prevent the Department or any other person from exercising the function to which the authorisation relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(8) Where a person is authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), anything done or omitted to be done by or in relation to him (or an employee of his) in, or in connection with, the exercise or purported exercise of the function shall be treated for all purposes as done or omitted to be done by or in relation to the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(9) Subsection (8) shall not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) for the purposes of so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Department as relates to the exercise of the function, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) for the purposes of any criminal proceedings brought in respect of anything done or omitted to be done by the authorised person (or an employee of his). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(10) Any decision which a person authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), makes in exercise of the function shall have effect as a decision of the Department under Article 9 of the 1998 Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(11) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) a person is authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) the authorisation is revoked at a time when a relevant contract is subsisting, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

the authorised person shall be entitled to treat the relevant contract as repudiated by the Department (and not as frustrated by reason of the revocation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(12) In subsection (11), the reference to a relevant contract is to so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Department as relates to the exercise of the function. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

(13) In this section, references to functions of the Department under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

- (a) a statutory provision contained in, or in regulations under, this Part; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
- (b) a statutory provision contained in Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the 1998 Order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]

include a reference to any function which the Department has by virtue of the application in relation to that provision of Article 9(1)(c) of that Order (decisions under certain statutory provisions to be made by the Department). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d04536bd-0d9b-4010-b4aa-8bab14706c64]
