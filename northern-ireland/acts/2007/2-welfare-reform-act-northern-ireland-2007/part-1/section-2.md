# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 2

## 2. Amount of contributory (affected by repeal, see note) allowance[^c21201101][^M_F_1bd3362d-8451-4856-9366-93a31511e9df]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amount payable by way of an employment and support allowance[^c-bdea44f9a220750a8040c7ffd3c7fe24] in respect of a claimant shall be calculated by—

- (a) taking such amount as may be prescribed,
- (b) if in his case the conditions of entitlement to the support component [^c-c2d21b2b63b954afa0acf0d1c491438c]... are satisfied, adding the amount of that component, and
- (c)[^c21201111] making
  - (i)[^c-d02f497f11beb65de15893892fa3ffcb] deductions in respect of earnings calculated in the prescribed manner (which may include multiplying some or all earnings by a prescribed percentage), and
  - (ii) prescribed deductions in respect of any payments to which section 3 applies.

(2) The conditions of entitlement to the support component are—

- (a)[^c21201121][^c-e997a737b25cc5a91ec85e34766633dc] that the assessment phase has ended,
- (b) that the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, and
- (c) that such other conditions as may be prescribed are satisfied.

(3)[^c-aad826d044f4321626f383d0ecdf5901] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Regulations may—

- (a) prescribe circumstances in which paragraph (a) of subsection (2) [^c-71143f12087fe44ed456eafdd8f3ddce]... is not to apply;
- (b) prescribe circumstances in which entitlement under subsection (2) [^c-71143f12087fe44ed456eafdd8f3ddce]... is to be backdated;
- (c) make provision about the amount of the component under subsection (2) [^c-71143f12087fe44ed456eafdd8f3ddce]....

(5) For the purposes of this Part, a person has limited capability for work-related activity if—

- (a) his capability for work-related activity is limited by his physical or mental condition, and
- (b) the limitation is such that it is not reasonable to require him to undertake such activity.

(6)[^c-1a3c50911d6af97e07cd9aba0a885963] In subsection (1)(c)(i) the reference to earnings is to be construed in accordance with sections 3, 4 and 112 of the Contributions and Benefits Act.
