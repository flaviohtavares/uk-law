# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 1

## 1. Employment and support allowance[^c21201091]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An allowance, to be known as an employment and support allowance, shall be payable in accordance with the provisions of this Part.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Part, a claimant is entitled to an employment and support allowance if he satisfies the basic conditions and either *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24c9395c3be364c027c97523169d4313]—

- (a) the first and the second conditions set out in Part 1 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-324c695ad43a1ce4a10d9954071b5a69] Schedule 1 (conditions relating to national insurance) or the third condition set out in that Part of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-324c695ad43a1ce4a10d9954071b5a69] that Schedule (condition relating to youth), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (b) the conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule (conditions relating to financial position). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

(3) The basic conditions are that the claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

- (a) has limited capability for work, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a5b48434b669ff67a01547a6e8fd3df0][^c-b35f365749b7c762d22e8878e6a66566][^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] has accepted a claimant commitment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (b) is at least 16 years old, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (c) has not reached pensionable age, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (d) is in Northern Ireland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] and[^c-da9c29cea8feb4fae8118f4f7f9188be] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (e) is not entitled to income support, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (f) is not entitled to a jobseeker's allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] (and is not a member of a couple who are entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f390b54a62af0a32ebdf6da0032d4cb3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408].

(3A)[^c-606863a32ad64a405e510dd08119ec37] After the coming into operation of this subsection no claim may be made for an employment and support allowance by virtue of the third condition set out in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] Part 1 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fa1580e120522f784825ec177d4ca76e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] Schedule 1 (youth). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

(4) For the purposes of this Part, a person has limited capability for work if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

- (a) his capability for work is limited by his physical or mental condition, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- (b) the limitation is such that it is not reasonable to require him to work. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

(5) An employment and support allowance is payable in respect of a week. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

(6) In subsection (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

- “[^c-8c3352a647423b7a2ff93e5caed990e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]**joint-claim jobseeker's allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8c3352a647423b7a2ff93e5caed990e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]**” means a jobseeker's allowance entitlement to which arises by virtue of Article 3(2B) of the Jobseekers Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8c3352a647423b7a2ff93e5caed990e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
- “**pensionable age *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]**” has the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]NI *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] 22).

(6A)[^c-d37aba05e69915a0c2fefe592ae97e0a] In subsection (3)(f), in relation to a contributory allowance, the reference to a couple entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance does not include a couple so entitled by virtue of regulations under paragraph 8A of Schedule 1 to the Jobseekers Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

(7) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]

- “**contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on subsection (2)(a) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] (and see section 1B(2))[^c-b8b75b206886207aaac5376a889d2145] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408] ;
- “**income-related allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on subsection (2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bde3379635b354e3dd723df4fe204442] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af5e59d1135f883bb5aeba76add1f408]
