## 23. Recovery of sums in respect of maintenance[^c21205141]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417] Regulations may make provision for the court to have power to make a recovery order against any person where an award of income-related allowance has been made to that person's spouse or civil partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]

(2) The reference in subsection (1) to a recovery order is to an order requiring the person against whom it is made to make payments to the Department or to such other person or persons as the court may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) may include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]

- (a) provision as to the matters to which the court is, or is not, to have regard in determining any application under the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]
- (b) provision as to the enforcement of orders under the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]
- (c) provision for the transfer by the Department of the right to receive payments under, and to exercise rights in relation to, orders under the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]

(4) In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]**the court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]**” means a court of summary jurisdiction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1e088cd345b23f2cae22a950c78f417]
