# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2007/2
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Relationship with statutory payments[^c21204971]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person is not entitled to an employment and support allowance in respect of a day if, for the purposes of statutory sick pay, that day—

- (a) is a day of incapacity for work in relation to a contract of service, and
- (b) falls within a period of entitlement (whether or not it is a qualifying day).

(2)[^c21205091] Except as regulations may provide, a woman who is entitled to statutory maternity pay is not entitled to an employment and support allowance[^c-6080fff1475aaadd5f57437798a36b6a] in respect of a day that falls within the maternity pay period.

(3) Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a woman's statutory maternity pay for a period shall be deducted from an employment and support allowance[^c-47251a93fdec1bfd9227797b83c5f6eb] in respect of the same period,
- (b) a woman shall only be entitled to an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a woman shall be entitled to an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(4)[^c21205101] Except as regulations may provide, a person who is entitled to statutory adoption pay is not entitled to an employment and support allowance[^c-39ae2c9a39b77d538c303bb76a14ed91] in respect of a day that falls within the adoption pay period.

(5) Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a person's statutory adoption pay for a period shall be deducted from an employment and support allowance[^c-301a0400caadd2eaae4c781990010bb2] in respect of the same period,
- (b) a person shall only be entitled to an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a person shall be entitled to an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(6)[^c21205111] Except as regulations may provide, a person who is entitled to statutory shared parental pay[^c-68dbe1d1f1fdc0d4490965afa01f5cb8] is not entitled to an employment and support allowance[^c-d568bbf17a9b50fdfc5ebb87c0c7c2a0] in respect of a day that falls within a period in respect of which statutory shared parental pay is payable[^c-658346545fe37c8a108cbb0e49d14c4d].

(7) Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a person's statutory shared parental pay for a period[^c-5d06f1a4b153b46cc012434d15d14612] shall be deducted from an employment and support allowance[^c-1cd6ed6cb4c66302211ebaa1bb0396e0] in respect of the same period,
- (b) a person shall only be entitled to an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a person shall be entitled to an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(8) In this section—

- [^c-2b37ef035c6d2b4abd4ae7dff440880e]...
- “**adoption pay period**” has the meaning given in section 167ZN(2) of that Act;
- “**maternity pay period**” has the meaning given in section 161(1) of that Act.
