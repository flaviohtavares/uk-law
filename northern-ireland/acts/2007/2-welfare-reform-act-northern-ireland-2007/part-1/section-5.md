# Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 5

## 5. Advance award of income-related allowance[^c21203261]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] This section applies to claims for an employment and support allowance by a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) would be entitled to an income-related allowance, but for the fact that he does not satisfy the condition in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) would satisfy that condition if he were entitled to the component mentioned in section 4(4) or (5), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (c) is not entitled to a contributory allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(2) In relation to claims to which this section applies, section 5(1) of the Administration Act (regulations about claims for benefit) shall have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) in paragraph (d) (power to permit an award on a claim for benefit for a future period to be made subject to the condition that the claimant satisfies the requirements for entitlement when the benefit becomes payable under the award), there were inserted at the end *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] “ and to such other conditions as may be prescribed ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) in paragraph (e) (power to provide for such an award to be revised or superseded under the 1998 Order if any of those requirements are found not to have been satisfied), for “any of those requirements” there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] “ any of the conditions to which the award is made subject ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1].

(3) Regulations may, in relation to claims to which this section applies, make provision enabling an award to be made on terms such that the time at which benefit becomes payable under the award is later than the start of the period for which the award is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
