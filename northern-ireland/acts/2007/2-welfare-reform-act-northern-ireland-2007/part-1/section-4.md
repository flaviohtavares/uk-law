## 4. Amount of income-related allowance[^c21203211]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] In the case of an income-related allowance, the amount payable in respect of a claimant shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) if he has no income, the applicable amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) if he has an income, the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the applicable amount for the purposes of subsection (1) shall be calculated by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) taking such amount, or the aggregate of such amounts, as may be prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) if in the claimant's case the conditions of entitlement to the support component *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] [^c-4668950e33b890bee4a6a1db55e1661d]... are satisfied, adding the amount of that component. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(3) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed cases, the applicable amount for the purposes of subsection (1) shall be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(4) The conditions of entitlement to the support component are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a)[^c21203221][^c21203231] that the assessment phase has ended, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) that the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (c) that such other conditions as may be prescribed are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(5)[^c-cbf6fea2007f9e12539254e097269b06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Regulations may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) prescribe circumstances in which paragraph (a) of subsection (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] [^c-d634fb404ff9efd3fbab6e528d90dbae]... is not to apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) prescribe circumstances in which entitlement under subsection (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] [^c-d634fb404ff9efd3fbab6e528d90dbae]... is to be backdated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (c) make provision about the amount of the component under subsection (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] [^c-d634fb404ff9efd3fbab6e528d90dbae]....
