## 6. Amount payable where claimant entitled to both forms of allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1] This section applies where a claimant is entitled to both a contributory allowance and an income-related allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(2) If the claimant has no income, the amount payable by way of an employment and support allowance shall be the greater of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) his personal rate, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) the applicable amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(3) If the claimant has an income, the amount payable by way of an employment and support allowance shall be the greater of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) his personal rate, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(4) Where the amount payable to the claimant by way of an employment and support allowance does not exceed his personal rate, the allowance shall be treated as attributable to the claimant's entitlement to a contributory allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(5) Where the amount payable to the claimant by way of an employment and support allowance exceeds his personal rate, the allowance shall be taken to consist of two elements, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- (a) an amount equal to his personal rate, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- (b) an amount equal to the excess. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(6) The element mentioned in subsection (5)(a) shall be treated as attributable to the claimant's entitlement to a contributory allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(7) The element mentioned in subsection (5)(b) shall be treated as attributable to the claimant's entitlement to an income-related allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]

- “**applicable amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]**” means the amount which, in the claimant's case, is the applicable amount for the purposes of section 4(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
- “**personal rate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]**” means the amount calculated in accordance with section 2(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8e5b37e1e730b58d7f9328009948abb1]
