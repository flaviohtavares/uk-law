## SCHEDULE 2 — EMPLOYMENT AND SUPPORT ALLOWANCE: SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 22.

### Limited capability for work

**1.**[^c21205741] Regulations may make provision—

- (a) for a person to be treated in prescribed circumstances as having, or as not having, limited capability for work;
- (b) for the question of whether a person has limited capability for work to be determined notwithstanding that he is for the time being treated by virtue of regulations under sub-paragraph (a) as having limited capability for work;
- (c) for the question of whether a person has limited capability for work to be determined afresh in prescribed circumstances.

### Waiting days

**2.**[^c21205751][^c-dd3d3e78b4861b155206b90c14934ca7][^c-eb38ad80e7829add72501feafa83f95c][^c-742c5bc2244a2b5a475b43dcc9b70f8e] Except in prescribed circumstances, a person is not entitled to an employment and support allowance in respect of a prescribed number of days at the beginning of a period of limited capability for work.

### Periods of less than a week

**3.**[^c21205761] Regulations may make provision in relation to—

- (a) entitlement to an employment and support allowance, or
- (b) the amount payable by way of such an allowance,

in respect of any period of less than a week.

### Linking periods

**4.**[^c21205771] (1) Regulations may provide for circumstances in which a period of limited capability for work which is separated from another period of limited capability for work by not more than a prescribed length of time is to be treated for the purposes of this Part as a continuation of the earlier period.

(2) Regulations may provide, in relation to periods which are linked by virtue of regulations under sub-paragraph (1), that a condition which was satisfied in relation to the earlier period is to be treated for the purposes of this Part as satisfied in relation to the later period.

### Exemption[^c-5348711267749f7cf65e3078d4051739]

**4A.** Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which a person may be entitled to employment and support allowance without having accepted a claimant commitment.

### Presence in Northern Ireland

**5.**[^c21205781] Regulations may make provision for the purposes of this Part as to the circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being, or not being, in Northern Ireland.

### Contributory allowance: (affected by repeal, see note) entitlement in case of absence from Northern Ireland[^M_F_933d8cfc-5da9-4a51-99d2-0c241357d99d]

**6.**[^c21205791] Regulations may provide that in prescribed circumstances a claimant who is not in Northern Ireland may nevertheless be entitled to an employment and support allowance[^c-e5f05b6d9e027b0d137e1cbdd25ff2bd].

### Contributory allowance: (affected by repeal, see note) modification in relation to employment on ships etc.[^M_F_75994e42-5664-4f08-91c2-2d073945ebe8]

**7.**[^c21205801] (1) Regulations may modify any provision of this Part, so far as relating to a contributory allowance, in its application to any person who is, has been, or is to be—

- (a) employed on board any ship, vessel, hovercraft or aircraft, or
- (b) outside Northern Ireland at any prescribed time or in any prescribed circumstances.

(2) Regulations under this paragraph may, in particular, provide—

- (a) for any provision of this Part to apply even though it would not otherwise apply;
- (b) for any such provision not to apply even though it would otherwise apply;
- (c) for the taking of evidence, in a country or territory outside Northern Ireland, by a consular official or other prescribed person;
- (d) for enabling the whole, or any part, of an employment and support allowance[^c-611aa1d985ec778e890814eeae4b6cf3] to be paid to such of the claimant's dependants as may be prescribed.

### Income-related allowance: entitlement in case of absence from Northern Ireland

**8.**[^c21205811] (1)[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48] Regulations may provide that in prescribed circumstances a claimant who is entitled to an income-related allowance immediately before ceasing to be in Northern Ireland continues to be entitled to such an allowance after ceasing to be in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48]

(2) Regulations may modify any provision of this Part, so far as relating to an income-related allowance, in its application to a person who is entitled to such an allowance by virtue of regulations under sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48]

(3) Regulations under sub-paragraph (2) may, in particular, provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48]

- (a) for any provision of this Part to apply even though it would not otherwise apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48]
- (b) for any such provision not to apply even though it would otherwise apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12ee1a44a6b259f7e290a3ce060e6f48]

### Limited capability for work-related activity

**9.**[^c21205821] Regulations may make provision—

- (a) for a person to be treated in prescribed circumstances as having, or as not having, limited capability for work-related activity;
- (b) for the question of whether a person has limited capability for work-related activity to be determined notwithstanding that he is for the time being treated by virtue of regulations under sub-paragraph (a) as having limited capability for work-related activity;
- (c) for the question of whether a person has limited capability for work-related activity to be determined afresh in prescribed circumstances.

### Effect of work

**10.**[^c21207191] Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which a person is to be treated as not entitled to an employment and support allowance because of his doing work.

### 10ZA.[^c-7f84ecb3cc81aefebbb4b6455da7c9cf]

Regulations may for any purpose of this Part provide for—

- (a) circumstances in which a person is to be treated as having or not having a good reason for an act or omission;
- (b) matters which are or are not to be taken into account in determining whether a person has a good reason for an act or omission.

### Good reason for failure to comply with certain regulations[^c22088081][^c-1685c8a28b28ecdf90b7c0acff753c1b]

**10A.** (1) This paragraph applies to any regulations made under paragraph 10ZA[^c-900728dcbdc2060b2546d155f19c5839] that prescribe matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person has a good reason[^c-f4290054e30fd9e3219bd677d24f0e9f] for any failure to comply with the regulations.

(2) The provision made by the regulations prescribing those matters must include provision relating to—

- (a) the person's physical or mental health or condition;
- (b) the availability of childcare.

### Responsibility for children[^c-d2da29a2db789115bce769f96d2e76e1]

**10B.** Regulations may for any purpose of this Part specify circumstances in which a person is or is not responsible for a child.

### Treatment of allowance as “benefit”

**11.** Regulations may provide for—

- (a) an employment and support allowance,
- (b) a contributory allowance, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3270b7e108d10aa669d40ff2c0cc64a2]
- (c) an income-related allowance, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3270b7e108d10aa669d40ff2c0cc64a2]

to be treated, for prescribed purposes of the Contributions and Benefits Act, as a benefit, or a benefit of a prescribed description.

### Attribution of reductions in cases where allowance taken to consist of two elements

**12.**[^c21208561] Where an employment and support allowance is taken by virtue of section 6(5) to consist of two elements, any reduction in the amount payable in respect of the allowance which falls to be made by virtue of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]

- (a) section 11, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]
- (b) section 12, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]
- (c) section 13, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]
- (d) section 2AA of the Administration Act (full entitlement to certain benefits conditional on work-focused interview for partner), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]

shall be treated as reducing such of those elements by such amount as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f2af363bcdd5cae39d5c469116bb8596]

### Treatment of information supplied as information relating to social security

**13.** Information supplied in pursuance of regulations under any of sections 8 to 11K[^M_F_1330a314-bfb3-4d47-a024-fded16e5101d] shall be taken for all purposes to be information relating to social security.

### Advance claims

**14.**[^c21208591] This Part shall have effect with prescribed modifications in relation to cases where a claim to an employment and support allowance is by virtue of regulations under section 5(1)(c) of the Administration Act (advance claims) made, or treated as if made, for a period wholly or partly after the date on which it is made.
