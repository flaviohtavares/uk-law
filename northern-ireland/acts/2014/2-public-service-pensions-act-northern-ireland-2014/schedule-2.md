# Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Responsible authorities

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 2(1).

### Civil servants

**1.**[^c-46d1641f68ede14bc21aa7264f10070d] Scheme regulations for civil servants may be made by the Department of Finance and Personnel.

### Holders of judicial office

**2.**[^c-b61cfdf4091bdc6879f4f4b2c2c5eac4] Scheme regulations for holders of judicial office may be made by the Department of Justice.

### Local government workers

**3.**[^c-319e0544be74febcaba6b7f98c5e5326][^c-0b0c41817d577726a41d321a384f4348] Scheme regulations for local government workers may be made by the Department of the Environment.

### Teachers

**4.**[^c-219ab7b5084f09c912ba17918d3324ed] Scheme regulations for teachers may be made by the Department of Education.

### Health service workers

**5.**[^c-8471e3823a921b21001a197431be5daf] Scheme regulations for health service workers may be made by the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety.

### Fire and rescue workers

**6.**[^c-9249fc7e4588c7d69fb71775e40ecd1f] Scheme regulations for fire and rescue workers may be made by the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety.

### Members of the police service

**7.**[^c-b2c631160a0ae02c703bb626e9fa983e] Scheme regulations for members of the police service may be made by the Department of Justice.
