# Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Existing injury and compensation schemes

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 19.

### Civil servants

**1.**[^key-aa2b3fd91b074b3695230619065bbf66] A scheme under Article 3 of the Superannuation Order.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

### Local government workers

**2.**[^key-72948647b6518c3a6a9d73c72df9a691] Regulations under Article 9 of the Superannuation Order.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Teachers

**3.**[^key-8152fd7f0b3cbc9b84d7307d9f241389] Regulations under Article 11 of the Superannuation Order.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Health service workers

**4.**[^key-731ab84e20fa31055e33d873deedaab4] Regulations under Article 12 of the Superannuation Order.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Fire and rescue workers

**5.**[^key-d7d1b2b07514ce17a4ba74421f55f408] Article 10 of the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

### Members of the police service

**6.**[^key-a54639848bc0e24e131364b761d86230] A scheme under section 25(2) or 26(2) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Compensation schemes for loss of office, etc.

**7.**[^key-0fa57ec662b477302831d59879f421cb] Regulations under Article 19 of the Superannuation Order.
