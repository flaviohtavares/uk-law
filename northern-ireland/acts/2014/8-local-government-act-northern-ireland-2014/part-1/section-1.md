# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Names of councils[^c-ac1a5fc30b1013b1a52bfeb145ca08d5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The name of a council is the name of the district with the addition of the words “District Council”.

(2) Regulations may provide for the name of a council to be other than that provided in accordance with subsection (1).

(3) Subsection (1) is subject to the provisions of this Act and Part 4 of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972.
