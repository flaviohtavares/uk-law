# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 37

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Standing orders[^c-953c744ac6df603efc819a4a887f0f0d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A council must make standing orders for the regulation of the proceedings and business of the council.

(2) A council may vary or revoke standing orders.

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) are subject to section 38 and the other provisions of this Act.

(4) This section applies to a joint committee as it applies to a council.
