# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 54

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Guidance[^key-71143e41ecac737c70961a98d2cebe70]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Commissioner may—

- (a) issue guidance on matters relating to the conduct of councillors; and
- (b) arrange for the guidance to be made public.
