# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 93

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Improvement information and planning[^c-90f4b9c1f1baa5095d8e11036ec0a475]

*Extent: N.I.*

In respect of each financial year, the local government auditor must carry out an audit for the purpose of determining—

- (a) whether a council has during that year discharged its duties under section 92; and
- (b) the extent to which the council has during that year acted in accordance with any guidance issued by the Department about the council's duties under that section.
