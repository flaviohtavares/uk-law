# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 119

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 119. Validity of acts done by unqualified person[^c-b22f66e892b6addcfcb5fd019e4ba020]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The validity of anything done by the holder of an office under this Act is not affected by that person not being qualified or being disqualified to be elected or to be a councillor.

(2) In subsection (1) “**office**” includes a position of responsibility.
