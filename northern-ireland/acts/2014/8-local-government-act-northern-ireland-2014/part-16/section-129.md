# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 129

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), this Act comes into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.

(2) The following provisions come into operation on the day after the day on which this Act receives Royal Assent—

- (a) this section;
- (b) section 130.
