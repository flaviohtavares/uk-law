# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 113

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 113. Commencement of the Local Government (Boundaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012[^key-475a75540542112bc1d032cb1f875b1d]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 1 of the Local Government (Boundaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 (SR 2012 No 421) (citation and commencement), in paragraph (4) (commencement for the purposes of making and levying rates), for the words from the beginning to “come into effect on” there shall be substituted the words “ This Order does not apply for the purposes of making and levying of rates, as provided in Part II of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977, in relation to a year beginning before ”.
