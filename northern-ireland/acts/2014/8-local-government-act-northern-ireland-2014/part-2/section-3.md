# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 3

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Disqualifications for being councillors[^key-dfe5dcecdcf88303eff3891f7aecefc9]

*Extent: N.I.*

A member of—

- (a) the Assembly;
- (b) the House of Commons;
- (c) the House of Lords;
- (d) the legislature of any other country; or
- (e) the European Parliament,

is disqualified for being a councillor.
