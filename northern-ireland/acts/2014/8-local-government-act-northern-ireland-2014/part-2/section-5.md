# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 5

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Disqualification of holders of prescribed offices and employments

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e1bd6eb198ea89d1db50cebb48952d33] In section 4(1)(a) of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 (disqualifications), for the words from “place of profit” to “council” there shall be substituted the words “ employment, appointments to which are made by a council if it is a prescribed office or employment ”.

(2)[^key-affb60061df6b4a4294f00fd85c947eb] In section 4 of that Act, after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) The Department must by 30th September 2014 make regulations under subsection (1)(a).

.
