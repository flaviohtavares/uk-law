# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 6

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Positions of responsibility[^M_C_ecb6805e-397c-4a7a-cd76-e7005d314351][^c-f918f7ad4e3330aa1d7c679aad603648]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In relation to a council the following are positions of responsibility—

- (a) chair of the council;
- (b) deputy chair of the council;
- (c) chair of any committee of the council;
- (d) deputy chair of any committee of the council;
- (e) member of a cabinet-style executive of the council;
- (f)[^c-681f19621ef4290251a302dc53f70a7d] external representative of the council.

(2) The clerk of a council must maintain a list of the council's positions of responsibility.

(3) Schedule 1 (positions of responsibility) has effect.

(4)[^c-436359eaecd1f47d2d316f534e42920c] In this Act “**external representative**” means a person nominated by the council to serve as a member of any public body.
