# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 10

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Exercise of functions not prevented by arrangements under this Part[^key-376c75a51ddb42cc894ed2df528977d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any arrangements made by a council or committee under this Part for the discharge of any functions by a committee, sub-committee, officer or council do not prevent the council or committee by which the arrangements are made from exercising those functions.
