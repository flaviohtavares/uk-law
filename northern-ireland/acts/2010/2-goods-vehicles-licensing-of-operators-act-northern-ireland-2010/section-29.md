# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 29

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Power to attach conditions on review[^key-1b9254714fd2362539feb4a316234f8f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If, having served notice under section 27 in respect of a place specified in a heavy goods vehicle[^key-d2d2c4b79520995ca5553d613ae669f0] licence, the Department does not give a direction in respect of the place under section 28, it may direct—

- (a) that conditions (or additional conditions) such as are mentioned in section 20(1)(a), (b) or (c) be attached to the licence;
- (b) that any conditions already attached to the licence under section 20(1)(a), (b) or (c) be varied.

(2) Any conditions attached to the licence under subsection (1)(a) shall relate or, in the case of conditions such as are mentioned in section 20(1)(b), shall only require the Department to be informed of events that relate—

- (a) only to the place referred to in subsection (1), or
- (b) only to that place and any other places in respect of which the Department has power to attach conditions under that subsection.

(3) Any variation under subsection (1)(b) shall be such as imposes new or further restrictions or requirements—

- (a) only in relation to the place referred to in subsection (1), or
- (b) only in relation to that place and any other places in respect of which the Department has power to attach conditions under that subsection.

(4) Where the Department gives a direction in respect of a heavy goods vehicle[^key-5f6e520ad1ecc0c1d3317a986f13f783] licence under section 28 or subsection (1)(a), it may also vary the licence by directing—

- (a) that any heavy goods[^key-2ab89e4a9bd375f4b89490bfae4819d8] vehicle cease to be specified in the licence;
- (b) that any maximum number of heavy goods vehicles[^key-d14ca646af01a31a52d6e567cd4da60a] specified in the licence under section 5 be reduced;
- (c) that a provision relating to heavy goods vehicles[^key-534e7ebb7b49d62f994ce64a94a90f0e] such as is mentioned in section 4(2) be included in the licence;
- (d) that a provision relating to heavy goods vehicles[^key-666e864a8681369d26c05283790b894d] such as is mentioned in section 5(1)(b) or (2)(b) be included in the licence.

(5) In this Act any reference, in relation to a heavy goods vehicle[^key-46fd4278626615f8aa6ccd5511e09d96] licence, to a condition attached to the licence under section 20(1)(a), (b) or (c) includes reference to any condition such as is mentioned in section 20(1)(a), (b) or (as the case may be) (c) attached to the licence under subsection (1)(a).
