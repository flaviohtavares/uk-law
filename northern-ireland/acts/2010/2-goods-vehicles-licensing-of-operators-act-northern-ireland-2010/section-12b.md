# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 12B

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12B. Requirements for restricted licences[^key-cec09dc6376f7e14b758a5ef0630eb0f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The requirement of this section is that the applicant is not unfit to hold an operator’s licence by reason of—

- (a)[^key-7457981dda81a660dc3a211e5ce7c098] any matter of which particulars are required to be given under section 7; or
- (b)[^key-7457981dda81a660dc3a211e5ce7c098] any event required to be notified in accordance with section 8(1).
