# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — AMENDMENTS

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 59

### The Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 (c. 37)

**1.**[^c-ef8ba159f36f9546bc7cb428b74cf7de] In section 31(1) for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”.

**2.**[^c-1b24741fec7b2ecafcc93f61fb6c0845] In section 34(b) for “mark or permit” substitute “ or control document ”.

**3.**[^c-f892a105d8ddec04efb7f3ddb0302ac8] In section 37(2) for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”.

**4.**[^c-00d224e2e7fc78605f69a42148d3d6fb] In section 43(1) for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”.

**5.**[^c-72eed2de5c2863fdbd1bbea3761637a9] In section 45(g) for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”.

**6.**[^c-fae5716cd63de0b9ff10f8264dde0fc8] In section 45(i) for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”

**7.**[^c-6a3dd9e5c1aef35bcb5a8f7a447123b7] In section 46 for “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”.

**8.**[^c-0394bca45390cc9143182d7bd02251e1] In section 46A(1) for “7, 10, 15, 15A, 28A and 29” substitute “ 7 and 10 ”.

### The International Road Haulage Permits Act 1975 (c. 46)

**9.**[^c-d314aaac5ff60d4466b348cfb7ff081c] In section 1(8) for “Part III of the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967” substitute “ the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 ”.

### The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (NI 1)

**10.**[^c-67fb5690383363b4b26fc81eb9f6caa1] In Article 87(1) after “the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) Act 1967” insert “ or section 57 of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, ”.

**11.**[^c-8b0c7955d5de7e7caa5c1f7c65a8c06c] In Schedule 1 at the end insert—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Sections 38, 39 and 40 of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010. | To enter and inspect goods vehicles, to inspect and copy documents required to be carried on such vehicles and to seize certain articles. |

.

### The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (NI 18)

**12.**[^c-0efaee24c8b9ff8945a514846f00b4f8] In Article 74(1) for “Parts II to IV” substitute “ Parts 2 and 4 ”.

**13.**[^c-fa37854e7012adffbf3c290c7862d857] In Article 111(2)—

- (a) for the words “Parts II and III” substitute “ Part 2 ”;
- (b) at the end add “ or the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 ”.
