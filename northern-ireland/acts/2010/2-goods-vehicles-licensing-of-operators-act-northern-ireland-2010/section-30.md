# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 30

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Transfer of operating centres[^c-f3c2f315c61e86b562002e8730456b83][^c-067b4bb7bb993b0d4e62d6b9296b7967]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 1 (which makes provision in relation to certain applications for, or for the variation of, heavy goods vehicle[^c-99d697a51620e5ea1e5e1385153e284c] licences where the proposed operating centres of the applicant are already specified in a heavy goods vehicle[^c-ebcecb9c5bb7f087a41098280d093586] licence) shall have effect.
