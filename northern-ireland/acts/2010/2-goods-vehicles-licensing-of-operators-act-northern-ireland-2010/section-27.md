# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 27

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Periods of review for operating centres[^key-5e52fe8f61da5d9ca28cf69891d02f54][^key-15d323c1d46446707a3c3592a11909d1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Within such time after any period of review as may be prescribed, the Department may serve a notice on the holder of a heavy goods vehicle licence[^key-7b09f31ea020c235a1beedd590a8320e] stating that the Department is considering whether to exercise any of its powers under sections 28 and 29 in relation to a place specified in the licence as an operating centre of the licence-holder.

(2) The periods of review in relation to a heavy goods vehicle[^key-3a852b3ae76eb83a831b9f54077c2503] licence are—

- (a) the period of 5 years beginning with the date specified in the licence as the date on which it came into force; and
- (b) each consecutive period of 5 years.

(3) Regulations may amend subsection (2) by substituting a higher or lower number for the number of years for the time being specified in paragraphs (a) and (b).
