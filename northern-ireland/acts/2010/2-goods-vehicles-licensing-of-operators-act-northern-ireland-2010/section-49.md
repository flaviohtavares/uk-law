# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 49

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Certificates of qualification[^c-c79a2d521bd29ef8fb830986c096c95e][^c-b7eff9bc88a88edfb906a8e8b1139ef0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) On an application made to the Department by a person wishing to engage in an[^c-655ec86480b277b9b3a33784fbdb3d56] undertaking established[^c-9902c92e58576015a954a54644308b04] in a member State that involves the use of goods vehicles[^c-584e170df0ff96d5272db856f967ccfe] [^c-d413094c8cfdf3336b905c1db9f39759]..., the Department shall issue to the applicant a certificate (a “certificate of qualification”) as to such matters relating to the applicant's—

- (a) repute,
- (b) professional competence, or
- (c) financial standing (where relevant),

as the Department is satisfied it may properly certify and as appear to the Department to be of assistance to the applicant in satisfying any requirements imposed by the law of the [^c-e0291504823d8996907c22642fb7dccc]... member State as regards the repute, professional competence and financial standing of persons engaged in such an undertaking established[^c-d540c0d41c25407b4226d82e24f1bf91] in that member State.

(2) A certificate of qualification shall—

- (a) be in such form as the Department may prescribe;
- (b)[^c-51fad47fb5e20595102285157b76856d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) No certificate of qualification shall be issued before a prescribed fee has been paid to the Department.

(4) The applicant shall give to the Department such information as it may reasonably require for the discharge of its duties in relation to the application.
