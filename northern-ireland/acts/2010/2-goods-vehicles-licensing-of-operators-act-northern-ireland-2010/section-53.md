# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 53

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 53. Application of Act to partnerships[^key-177fffffbfe4ba00afd047a8e922eae0][^key-adf5579d6cf0bb057915706c4864aff7]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations may provide for this Act to apply in relation to partnerships with such modifications as may be prescribed.
