## 17. Power of managers to modify by resolution[^key-ed8821c0483db197c050f44481dd01f3]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 32 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (power of trustees to modify by resolution) in subsection (1) and in the heading after “trustees” insert “ or managers ”.
