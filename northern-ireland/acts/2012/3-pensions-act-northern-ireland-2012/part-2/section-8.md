# Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2012/3
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Timing of automatic re-enrolment[^c21291281][^c-bdf680b45054c73e243413fad2deadf5]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 6 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (timing of automatic re-enrolment) in subsection (1)(b) for “three years” substitute “ 2 years and 9 months ”.
