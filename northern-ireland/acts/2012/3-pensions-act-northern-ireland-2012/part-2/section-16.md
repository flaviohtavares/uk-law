## 16. Arrangements where transitional conditions cease to be satisfied[^c21291401][^c-0818a3789f20e0d2a9cd664957db5ae9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Section 30 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (transitional period for defined benefits and hybrid schemes) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (5) after “money purchase scheme” insert “ or personal pension scheme ”.

(3) In subsection (6)(b) omit “(in accordance with section 20(1))”.
