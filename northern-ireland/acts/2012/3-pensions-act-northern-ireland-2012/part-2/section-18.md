## 18. No indemnification for civil penalties[^c-fc10d371863b813a0a2ef44e906defa8]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 233 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (no indemnification for fines or civil penalties) in paragraph (1)(b)—

- (a) for “or section” substitute “ , section ”;
- (b) after “the Pension Schemes Act” insert “ or section 40 or 41 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 ”.
