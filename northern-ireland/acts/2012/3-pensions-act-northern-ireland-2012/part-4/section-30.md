# Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2012/3
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Power to make further provision

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department for Social Development may by regulations amend for any purpose—[^c-c7f16dd0724e9003dbd93d428cd44371]

- (a) the definition of “money purchase benefit” in the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 or the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, or[^c-e7363f617e062311ce100c42cd17aa0b]
- (b) section 53 of the Pension Schemes Act 2021 (collective money purchase benefits: meaning of “qualifying benefits”).

(2) Regulations under this section may in particular amend the provisions inserted by section 27 or by Schedule 4 to the Pension Schemes Act 2021[^c-01f9963efd1efce457564311d926782a].

(3) Regulations under this section may include transitional, consequential or supplementary provision.
