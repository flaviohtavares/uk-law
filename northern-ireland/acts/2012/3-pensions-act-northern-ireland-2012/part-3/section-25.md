# Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 25

## 25. Technical amendment to Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes) in paragraph 59(4) for paragraphs (b) and (c) substitute—

> - (b) in the definition of “the percentage for contributing earners”—
>   - (i) omit the words “(a) in relation to a salary related contracted-out scheme,”;
>   - (ii) omit paragraph (b) and the preceding “and”;
> - (c) in the definition of “the percentage for non-contributing earners”—
>   - (i) omit the words “(a) in relation to a salary related contracted-out scheme,”;
>   - (ii) omit paragraph (b) and the preceding “and”.
