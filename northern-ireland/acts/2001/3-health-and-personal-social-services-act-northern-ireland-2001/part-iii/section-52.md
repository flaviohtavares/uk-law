## 52. Liability of officers of Health and Social Services Councils

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 97(1) of the principal Order (protection for officers of health and social care[^c21433161] bodies acting in execution of duty) after sub-paragraph (d) there shall be inserted—

> or
>
> - (e) a Health and Social Services Council,

.
