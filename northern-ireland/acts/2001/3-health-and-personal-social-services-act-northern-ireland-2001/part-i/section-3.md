# Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 3

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. The register

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Council shall maintain a register of—

- (a) social workers and[^key-ae67dd31bb443e3392678f19f31b5745] ; [^c20592861]. . .
- (b) social care workers of any other description specified by order made by the Department.; [^key-ab154147e6897b545bd7a1ca4c5a80ed]
- (c)[^key-ab154147e6897b545bd7a1ca4c5a80ed] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c20593191] There shall be a separate part of the register for—

- (a) social workers and[^key-126c3255775ec394b755c80ff9617d97] ;
- (b) each description of social care workers specified pursuant to subsection (1)(b); [^key-35c9fe45bf240b0471cfbaf2cc34650b]
- (c)[^key-35c9fe45bf240b0471cfbaf2cc34650b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A) For the purposes of this Part—

- (a) the part mentioned in subsection (2)(a) is the “principal part” of the register;
- (b) a part mentioned in subsection (2)(b) is an “added part” of the register;[^key-383719334118abe388424bae5fc1c2dc]
- (c)[^key-383719334118abe388424bae5fc1c2dc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Department may by order provide for a specified part of the register to be closed, as from a date specified by the order, so that on or after that date no further persons can become registered in that part.

(4) The Department shall consult the Council before making, varying or revoking any order under this section.

(5) The register may be kept by means of a computer.
