# Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 4

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Applications for registration

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application for registration in the principal part[^c20593341]**,** or an added part, of the register [^c-16ad0c0552840f8db63ea43d4ba1afca]... shall be made to the Council in accordance with rules made by it.

(2) An application under subsection (1) shall specify each part of the register in which registration is sought and such other matters as may be required by the rules.
