# Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 61

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Short title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.

(2) Parts I to III, sections 56 and 60 and the Schedules to this Act shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint[^c17100351]; but section 39 shall not be brought into operation by such an order before 1 April 2002.
