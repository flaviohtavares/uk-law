# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 141

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 141. Notification of fostering: public awareness[^key-aa04abaf770959df824088b0a7183ab5]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

After Article 112 of the Children Order insert—

> **112A. Notification of fostering: public awareness**
>
> Every authority must promote public awareness within its area of requirements as to notification for which provision is made under Article 112

.
