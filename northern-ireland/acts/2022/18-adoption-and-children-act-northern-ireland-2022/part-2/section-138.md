# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 138

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 138. Interests of children in proceedings[^key-972ceb1917f8bc7f266955a19494b9d6]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 60 of the Children Order (specified proceedings), in paragraph (6), after sub-paragraph (h) insert—

> - (ha) on an application for the making of a special guardianship order with respect to a child who is the subject of a care order;

.
