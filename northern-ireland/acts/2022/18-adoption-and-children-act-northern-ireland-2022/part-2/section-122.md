# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 122

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 122. Duty of authorities to promote etc. achievement, learning and development, and to prevent disruption to education or training[^c-3b7ab7b1dcc2eefea9514910f5aba82d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In Article 26 of the Children Order (general duty of authority), after paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) The duty of an authority under paragraph (1)(a) to safeguard and promote the welfare of a child looked after by the authority includes in particular a duty to promote, facilitate and support the child’s —
>
> - (a) learning and development; and
> - (b) achievement in relation to education or training.

.

(2) In Article 27 of the Children Order (accommodation and maintenance for children), in paragraph (8)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (a), omit the word “and”;
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (b) add

  > ; and
  >
  > - (c) the child’s education or training will not be disrupted.

  .
