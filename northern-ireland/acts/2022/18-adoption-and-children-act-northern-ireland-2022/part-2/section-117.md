# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 117

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 117. Article 8 orders: authority foster parents[^c-f23a2b8fde682def46b08295fe6604d7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 9 of the Children Order (restrictions on making Article 8 orders)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(c), for “three years” substitute “one year”; and
- (b) omit paragraph (4).
