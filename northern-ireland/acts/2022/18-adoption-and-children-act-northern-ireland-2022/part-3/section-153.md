# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 153

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 153. Research and investigations[^c-2f7cd60c5c79d23dc97315b83d2a1556]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Department may conduct or promote or assist (by grants or otherwise) any person in conducting research or investigations into any matter connected with the functions under Part 1 of an adoption authority or the Department.
