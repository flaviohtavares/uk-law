## 160. Commencement[^c-ca0ebacacf89035d5629994cea1c1961]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except as provided by subsection (2), the provisions of this Act come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.

(2) This section and sections 155, 157, 159 and 161 come into operation on the day after the day on which this Act receives Royal Assent.
