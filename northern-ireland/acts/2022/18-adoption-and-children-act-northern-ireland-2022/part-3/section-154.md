# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 154

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 154. Amendments, transitional and transitory provisions, savings and repeals[^key-53b3b5debff529096b00089112f03901]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The statutory provisions set out in Schedule 3 have effect subject to the amendments there specified.

(2) Schedule 4 (transitional and transitory provisions and savings) has effect.

(3) The statutory provisions set out in Schedule 5 are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the second column of that Schedule.
