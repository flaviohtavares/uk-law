# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 64

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64. Sections 55 to 64: interpretation[^c-dae4e3e56698d1d682b717ee153bcd6f]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In this group of sections—

- “**appropriate adoption agency**”, in relation to an adopted person or to information relating to that person’s adoption, means—
  - (a) if the person was placed for adoption by an adoption agency, that agency or (if different) the agency which keeps the information in relation to that person’s adoption;
  - (b) in any other case, the adoption authority to which notice of intention to adopt was given;
- “**prescribed**” means, except in section 59(4), prescribed by regulations;
- “**regulations**” means regulations under section 9.
