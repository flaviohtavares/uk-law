# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 111

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 111. Use of adoption records from Great Britain, etc.[^key-2aa02272d2a74c31e8a7ba0067cd7c6f]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any document which is receivable as evidence of any matter—

- (a) in Scotland under section 56(1) or (2) of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007;
- (b) in England and Wales under section 77(4) and (5) of the Adoption and Children Act 2002; or
- (c) in the Isle of Man or any of the Channel Islands under a statutory provision corresponding to section 76(3) of this Act,

is also receivable as evidence of that matter in Northern Ireland.
