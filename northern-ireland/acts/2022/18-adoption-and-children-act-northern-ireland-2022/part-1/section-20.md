# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 20

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Varying placement orders[^key-3c13df01040bcfad8454d87a906c0ef9]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The court may vary a placement order so as to substitute another adoption authority for the adoption authority authorised by the order to place the child for adoption.

(2) The variation may only be made on the joint application of both adoption authorities.
