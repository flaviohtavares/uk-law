# Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022, section 37

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Other non-agency cases[^key-6c5ed6c9f80eb646a220738ccaacc191]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In any case where sections 34 to 36 do not apply but—

- (a) the people concerned have given notice of intention to adopt; or
- (b) the people concerned have applied for leave under section 39(6) and the application has not been disposed of,

the following persons may remove the child.

(2) They are—

- (a) a person who has the court’s leave;
- (b) an adoption authority or other person in the exercise of a power conferred by any statutory provision, other than Article 22(2) of the Children Order.
