## 57. Dangerous offenders: serious and specified offences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The paragraph set out in subsection (2) is inserted—

- (a) in Schedule 1 to the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (NI 1) (serious offences) after paragraph 15; and
- (b) in Schedule 2 to that Order (specified offences) after paragraph 15.

(2) The paragraph is—

> **15A. The Criminal Jurisdiction Act 1975 (c. 59)**
>
> An offence under section 2 (hi-jacking of vehicles or ships).
