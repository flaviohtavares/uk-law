# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 51

## 51. Increase in maximum term of imprisonment for common assault or battery

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Offences against the Person Act 1861 (c. 100) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 42 (common assault or battery) for “three months” substitute “ 6 months ”.

(3) Section 43 (aggravated assault) is repealed.
