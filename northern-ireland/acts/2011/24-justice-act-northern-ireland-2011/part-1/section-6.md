# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2011/24
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Amount of the offender levy

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The offender levy for the purposes of section 1 is—

- (a)[^c-58ddee8266153ea9de4f73a3cb799258] £50, where the sentence imposed on the offender is or includes—
  - (i) a determinate sentence of imprisonment or detention for more than 2 years (not being a suspended sentence); or
  - (ii) an indeterminate sentence of imprisonment or detention;
- (b)[^c-58ddee8266153ea9de4f73a3cb799258] £25, where that sentence—
  - (i) is or includes a determinate sentence of imprisonment or detention for 2 years or less (not being a suspended sentence); and
  - (ii) does not include a sentence falling within paragraph (a);
- (c) £20, where that sentence—
  - (i) is or includes a community order or a suspended sentence of imprisonment or detention; and
  - (ii) does not include a sentence falling within paragraph (a) or (b);
- (d)[^c-58ddee8266153ea9de4f73a3cb799258] £15, where that sentence—
  - (i) is or includes a fine; and
  - (ii)[^M_C_324dd34e-5f02-4b58-f3db-0f0dfc35bff8] does not include a sentence falling within paragraph (a), (b) or (c).

(2)[^c-2fd47c6e23281b9fb25cf15396530cb5][^c-93ad0ffb6c45f695f8ec1e90843b0e8a] The offender levy for the purposes of section 5 is £5.

(3)[^c-58ddee8266153ea9de4f73a3cb799258] The Department may by order amend subsections (1) and (2).

(4)[^c-58ddee8266153ea9de4f73a3cb799258] No order shall be made under subsection (3) unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.
