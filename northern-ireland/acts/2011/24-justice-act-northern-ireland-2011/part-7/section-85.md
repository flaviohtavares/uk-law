# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 85

> This provision is not yet in force.
>

## 85. Litigation funding agreements

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Article 41 of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (NI 10) (prohibition on [^key-70aebdb29a59954cd62164dd07f959b5]... funding services under litigation funding agreements) is repealed.
