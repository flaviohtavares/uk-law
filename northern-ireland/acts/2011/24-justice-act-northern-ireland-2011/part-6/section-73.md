# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 73

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Variation of conditions

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A Public Prosecutor may, with the consent of the offender, vary the conditions attached to a conditional caution by—

- (a) modifying or omitting any of the conditions;
- (b) adding a condition.
