## 64. Guidance[^key-5aef93c92e2a7e93dd380f2b7e481c75]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may issue guidance—

- (a) about the exercise of the discretion given to police officers by this Chapter;
- (b) about the issuing of penalty notices;
- (c) with a view to encouraging good practice in connection with the operation of provisions of this Chapter.
