# Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2011/24
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 38. Going onto the playing area

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person at any time during the period of a regulated match to go onto the playing area, or any area adjacent to the playing area to which spectators are not generally admitted, without lawful authority or lawful excuse (which shall be for that person to prove).

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
