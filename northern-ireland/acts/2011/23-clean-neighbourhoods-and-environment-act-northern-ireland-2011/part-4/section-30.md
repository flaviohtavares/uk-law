# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 30

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Guidance[^c-0008aef5bc69e86e772c45e25b5cf27a][^c-a7e8a6474151b14f38573b9a09a6c1b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may issue guidance to district councils about—

- (a) the exercise of the discretion to give notices under section 26(1); and
- (b) the giving of such notices.

(2) A district council shall have regard to any guidance issued under this section.
