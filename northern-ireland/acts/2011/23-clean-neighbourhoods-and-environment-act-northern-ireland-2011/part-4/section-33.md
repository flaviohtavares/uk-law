# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 33

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Guidance[^key-7aff3897adb67a3cf7fe0b079084fbce][^key-7ea5cdc83cd63b9b958526a3a70dfdcf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department shall issue guidance to district councils for the purposes of sections 31 and 32.

(2) A district council shall have regard to any guidance issued under this section.
