# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 60

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Dealing with noise at night[^c-35fed6b61d828465d9385fce95156cd0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Noise Act 1996 (c. 37) is amended as follows.

(2) For section 1 (adoption of provisions by district councils or by order of the Department) substitute—

> **1. Application of sections 2 to 9**
>
> Sections 2 to 9 apply to the district of every district council.

.

(3) For section 2(1) (district council under duty to investigate complaint of noise from dwelling at night) substitute—

> (1) A district council may, if it receives a complaint of the kind mentioned in subsection (2), arrange for an officer of the council to take reasonable steps to investigate the complaint.

.

(4) In section 2(7) (power of district council to act in relation to dwelling within district of another council), omit the words from “and accordingly” to the end.
