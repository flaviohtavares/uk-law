> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Street litter: supplementary provisions[^key-6f734b40787a2dc138fcbe0e2b2f95d6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Article 14 of the Litter (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 (street litter: supplementary provisions) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4)(b) after the words “so specified” insert “ (including the standards to which any such thing must be done) ”.

(3) For paragraphs (8) and (9) substitute—

> (8) A person commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, he fails to comply with a requirement imposed on him by a notice.
>
> (9) A person guilty of an offence under paragraph (8) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.
