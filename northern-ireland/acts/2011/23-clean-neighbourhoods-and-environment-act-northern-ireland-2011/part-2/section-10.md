# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 10

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Guidance[^c-a6a5d3679ade7d7c5fe9ece290a31b45]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Pollution Control and Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (NI 19), after Article 31 (disposal of removed vehicles) insert—

> **31A. Guidance**
>
> A district council in exercising any function conferred under Article 30 or 31 shall have regard to any guidance given for the purpose by the Department.
