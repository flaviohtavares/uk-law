# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 158

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 158. Issue of listed buildings enforcement notices by Department[^key-1055b94ad3d793f84e3349964f0d5620][^key-6ee5e502f05d9e73d89fe394fd3c2926][^key-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^key-683a681a9c52eddb7655b7a93fa51e31][^key-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c][^key-2e26e3a6d902a5d5b472823514015567][^key-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^key-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the Department to be expedient that a listed building enforcement notice should be issued in respect of any land, it may issue such a notice.

(2) Before the Department issues such a notice under subsection (1), it must consult the appropriate council.

(3) A listed building enforcement notice issued by the Department shall have the same effect as a notice issued by a council.

(4) Sections 139(2) to (4), 142, 146 and 147 shall, with any necessary modifications apply to a listed building enforcement notice issued by the Department as they apply to an enforcement notice issued by the Department.
