# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 174

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 174. Further provisions as to appeals under section 173[^c-d80d472962ce845d781a9916461b8bab][^c-d58a4cc3af3a4d50cc873229fd90e0e8][^c-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^c-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c][^c-c34b6bb36609284bf87fdfbe5f4caa88][^c-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^c-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before determining an appeal to it under section 173(1), the planning appeals commission must, if either the appellant or the council so wish, afford to each of them an opportunity of appearing before, and being heard by, the planning appeals commission.

(2) Where the planning appeals commission grants a certificate under section 169 or 170 on such an appeal, it shall give notice to the council of that fact.

(3) The decision of the planning appeals commission on such an appeal shall be final.
