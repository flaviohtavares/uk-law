# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 151

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 151. Service of stop notices by Department[^key-f8f9a8d2f05d64941131eb06bc5da63d][^key-cf16a9ab8432f292170d4fbca0c5ae7c][^key-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^key-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c][^key-323eda43319f52f0646660f5acc40a68][^key-3a9e71a09b04f54cb4e6ad3c729ef376][^key-2e510eafa4593ec05008815beedc306c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may serve a stop notice.

(2) The Department must not serve a stop notice without consulting the appropriate council.

(3) A notice served by the Department under subsection (1) shall have the same effect as if it had been served by a council.

(4) The provisions of section 150 shall apply, with any necessary modifications, to the service of a stop notice by the Department as they apply to the service of a stop notice by a council.
