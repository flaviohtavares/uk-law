# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 228

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 228. Review of Act[^c-0bb765fc90b39d7759e9c5b692d048ca][^c-00ddc72d92903a5c402ea63e5c9d1e55][^c-086c01b45aafecb1b63673a7c231c35e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must—

- (a) not later than 3 years after the commencement of Part 3 of this Act, and
- (b) at least once in every period of 5 years thereafter,

review and publish a report on the implementation of this Act.

(2) Regulations under this section shall set out the terms of the review.
