> Text as at 2023-12-26.

## 246. Directions[^key-f477b4f094af7007b9d53b12557717c4][^key-47a52e6d3998c18ccc0f1190379bf026]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any directions which may be given, or must be given, under this Act may be withdrawn or may be varied or revoked by a subsequent direction.
