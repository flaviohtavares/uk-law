# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 244

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 244. Time limit for certain summary offences under this Act[^c-ea43b5bf90682649c7e1289818c97ac6][^c-af4bbbd596af84637917399f292cd655]

*Extent: N.I.*

Notwithstanding anything in Article 19(1)(a) of the Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (NI 26), a magistrates' court shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine a complaint charging the commission of an offence under section 152 if the complaint is made within 3 years from the time when the offence was committed or ceased to continue.
