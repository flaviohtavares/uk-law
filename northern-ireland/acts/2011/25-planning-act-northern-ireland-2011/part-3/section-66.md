# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 66

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 66. Power of Department to serve completion notices[^key-90ba87b5fa9d482d4ca42077ce5edc0c][^key-3dec58e4cc400d83c6d0b8d5dca8ef20][^key-2f5f0ed73ae267031cc18d32f95f44b5][^key-2f5f0ed73ae267031cc18d32f95f44b5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the Department to be expedient that a completion notice should be served in respect of any land, the Department may itself serve such a notice.

(2) A completion notice served by the Department shall have the same effect as if it had been served by the appropriate council.

(3) The Department shall not serve such a notice without consulting the appropriate council.
