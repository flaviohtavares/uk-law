# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 217

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 217. Applications for planning permission, etc. by Crown[^key-addd3beec2d5bb8a5bfc91656e2d3780][^key-6336ed4d6b33cb6293728f7ff459de7b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies to an application made by or on behalf of the Crown for—

- (a) planning permission, listed building consent, hazardous substances consent or conservation area consent; or
- (b) a determination under section 102 or a certificate under section 170.

(2) The Department may by regulations modify or exclude any statutory provision relating to the making and determination of such applications.
