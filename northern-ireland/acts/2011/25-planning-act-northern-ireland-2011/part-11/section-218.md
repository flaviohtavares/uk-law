# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 218

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 218. Service of notices on the Crown[^key-95faf051c79c59389f1d1608a065f744][^key-6a45fe9d71052d91309aec5ba126de1b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any notice or other document required under this Act to be served on the Crown shall be served on the appropriate authority.

(2) Section 24 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33) does not apply for the purposes of the service of such a notice or document.
