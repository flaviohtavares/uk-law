# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 190

> Text as at 2023-12-26.

## 190. Interpretation of Part 6[^c-2d5fd0b8ddd51af98ee7c2933032ad96][^c-45e1c0a25a5b7ecb5de5612589293956]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part, “**compensatable estate**” has the same meaning as in the Act of 1965.
