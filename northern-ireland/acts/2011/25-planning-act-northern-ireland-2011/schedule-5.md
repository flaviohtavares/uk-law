## SCHEDULE 5 — THE HISTORIC BUILDINGS COUNCIL[^key-9bb89b4c149d7db8a7e97a7cf46ae88d]

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 198(3).

**1.**[^key-c98e0a0f1ddb5d23116dd1d5a097f63a][^key-7c842c05ddef8511874f101404dbd5d1] The Historic Buildings Council (in this Schedule referred to as “**the Council**”) shall consist of a Chair appointed by the Minister for Communities[^key-5ac8c4adab1fb2c80032047ba51845ea] and such number of other members so appointed as the Minister for Communities may determine.

**2.**[^key-9e52e0afa40763d80cb5845b42f62c1b][^key-7650eb087d5c7a28f6a1c91d22acb940] A member of the Council shall hold office for a maximum period of 3 years but shall be eligible for re-appointment.

**3.**[^key-90f5ca2224633a416a3486d114bb63d9][^key-4877d81598a512a2ad2e78c8f1c006b1] The Department may pay to the Chair and members of the Council allowances for travelling and other out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the business of the Council at such rates as the Department, with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel, may determine.

**4.**[^key-b36aeb63c92312650ec8c50abcc026d4][^key-ab0ef0fbd1353602f43c484a65dae495] (1) The Council shall, subject to sub-paragraph (4), appoint such committees as the Department may determine.

(2) A committee appointed under this paragraph may include persons who are not members of the Council.

(3) Every member of a committee appointed under this paragraph, who, at the time of his or her appointment, was a member of the Council shall, if he or she ceases to be a member of the Council, also cease to be a member of the committee.

(4) The Department may, by regulations or direction, make provision with respect to the appointment, constitution or functions of committees appointed under this paragraph.

**5.**[^key-7a04409150a2f7f00c05be1e23b4b967][^key-9cec42b634bae625a1f4e13ba91ecb03] The Council may regulate its own quorum and procedure and that of its committees which shall be subject to the approval of the Department.

**6.**[^key-2416a16cf86db57bc646c24358b5d5fd][^key-f9805b32221917e459ff5b4d3f702f11] The Council must, at such times and in respect of such periods as the Department may direct, prepare and submit to the Department a report on its activities and the Department shall lay a copy of every such report before the Assembly.

**7.**[^key-22d06cecd9027bb49a10625d65639091][^key-4320cbb793fba17b2a4eb46fde20562a] The secretary to the Council shall be such person as the Department may appoint.
