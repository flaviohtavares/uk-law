# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 224

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 224. Grants for research and bursaries[^c-3fe71356738a8b67231805a4a5d4d00c][^c-5b03ffd4c00f46ed779b5a957038b8fb][^c-6f6709a9242098434f85b6e813630a26]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, make grants for assisting establishments engaged in promoting or assisting research relating to, and education with respect to, the planning and design of the physical or built environment.

(2) For the purpose of enabling persons to undertake a course in town planning, the Department may, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, make provision for the payment by the Department of sums by way of bursaries in respect of those persons.
