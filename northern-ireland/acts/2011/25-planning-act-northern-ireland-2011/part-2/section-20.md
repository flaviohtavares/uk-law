# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 20

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Guidance[^key-b49cfc1c304047be45628bed94f60390][^key-305792263c182f75fd89e5babb3cc5b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the exercise of any function conferred under or by virtue of this Part, the council must have regard to any guidance issued by—

- (a) the Department;
- (b) the Department for Regional Development;
- (c) the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister.
