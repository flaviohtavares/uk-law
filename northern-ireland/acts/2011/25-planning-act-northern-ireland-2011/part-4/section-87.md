# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 87

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 87. Notification of applications for listed building consent to certain persons[^key-bb299c0adde183ab2eac632a4f35a0a7][^key-6265b73e0f815002408a368d1bdba855][^key-5785d75fab88b46bca7625e648919cb3][^key-9204b165643c0ae733b1d157a4357309][^key-d2d1706f57de4b4bcea88fb86e971052][^key-97e39ce414f03726ecb058ab715dd8c1][^key-9d2cc3eab200e050cabb1fd0938728eb][^key-bf998a0b63e9e76b820c488607e869e7][^key-5785d75fab88b46bca7625e648919cb3][^key-97e39ce414f03726ecb058ab715dd8c1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Section 42 shall, with appropriate modifications, apply to applications for listed building consent in relation to any building as it applies to applications for planning permission in relation to any land.

(2) In the application of section 42(7) by virtue of subsection (1) for the words “specified in a development order” and “form so specified” there shall be substituted the words “ prescribed ” and “ prescribed form ”.

(3) References in the following provisions of this Part to section 42 are to that section as it applies by virtue of this section.
