# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 106

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 106. Grants in relation to conservation areas[^c-e47411eba2dd5a6d1958fd5b4f5cd16c][^c-f021f79ceda2747e56b03fe8b23677b4][^c-5785d75fab88b46bca7625e648919cb3][^c-97e39ce414f03726ecb058ab715dd8c1][^c-323eda43319f52f0646660f5acc40a68][^c-5785d75fab88b46bca7625e648919cb3][^c-97e39ce414f03726ecb058ab715dd8c1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may, in relation to any conservation area, make grants or loans for the purpose of defraying in whole or in part any expenditure incurred or to be incurred in or in connection with, or with a view to the promotion of, the preservation or enhancement of the character or appearance of the area or any part thereof.

(2) A grant or loan under this section may be made subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit.

(3) Any loan under this section must be made on such terms as to repayment, payment of interest and otherwise as the Department may with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel determine; and all sums received by the Department by way of interest on, or repayment of, such a loan shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.
