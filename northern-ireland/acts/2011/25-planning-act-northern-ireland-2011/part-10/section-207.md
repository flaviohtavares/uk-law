# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 207

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 207. Assessment of council's performance[^c-9fb8ed8cd2b57401b5bdceb5bd7af44a][^c-e5e4267810beb8eedd4b3e2b3493b00a]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may conduct, or appoint a person (referred to in sections 209 and 210 as the “appointed person”) to conduct on its behalf, an assessment of a council's performance—

- (a) of functions generally under this Act, or
- (b) of particular functions under this Act,

(not being an assessment which may be conducted under section 208).
