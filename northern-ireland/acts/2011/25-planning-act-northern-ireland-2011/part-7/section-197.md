# Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 197

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 197. Special provision as to compensation under this Part[^c-b4f0853031d5222ffad6d9440a6593cf][^c-f08c2cefd697922551ac1772d012367e][^c-9a5a21b6c5c0f1985d025a3cc575a083][^c-9a5a21b6c5c0f1985d025a3cc575a083]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where by virtue of section 26 of the Act of 1965 compensation is payable in respect of expenditure incurred in carrying out any work on land, then, if a purchase notice is served in respect of an estate in that land, any compensation payable in respect of the acquisition of that estate in pursuance of the purchase notice shall be reduced by an amount equal to the value of the works in respect of which compensation is payable under that section.
