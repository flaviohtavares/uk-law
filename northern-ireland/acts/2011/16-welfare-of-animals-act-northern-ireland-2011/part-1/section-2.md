# Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Protected animals

*Extent: N.I.*

An animal is a “**protected animal**” for the purposes of this Act if—

- (a) it is of a kind which is commonly domesticated in Northern Ireland,
- (b) it is under the control of man whether on a permanent or temporary basis, or
- (c) it is not living in a wild state.
