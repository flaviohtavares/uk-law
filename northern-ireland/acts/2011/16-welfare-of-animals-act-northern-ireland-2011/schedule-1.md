# Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — DOGS SPECIFIED FOR THE PURPOSES OF SECTION 6(7)

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 6(7).

**1.**[^key-7fdacadbd721103a6639356c80f87718] Spaniels of any breed or combination of breeds.

**2.**[^key-5d3631de3bf564c0c615d09218945882] Terriers of any breed or combination of breeds.

**3.**[^key-af3b76df21d0d3374be2e0f4a0b0dc59] Any breed commonly used for hunting, or any combination of such breeds.

**4.**[^key-b7ae792a428284cdb0c1ce1e706f2339] Any breed commonly used for pointing, or any combination of such breeds.

**5.**[^key-4dd856a34e318f531d599866b4ae5f7f] Any breed commonly used for retrieving, or any combination of such breeds.
