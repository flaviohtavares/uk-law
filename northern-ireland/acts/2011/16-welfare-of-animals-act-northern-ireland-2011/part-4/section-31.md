# Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 31

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Penalties

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under any of sections 6(10) and (12), [^c-6694f311aa8276cf5190156852020de5]..., 9, 10(6), 12(6), 14, 15, 17(12), 19(7), [^c-6694f311aa8276cf5190156852020de5]..., [^c-6694f311aa8276cf5190156852020de5]... and 50(3) or paragraph 17 of Schedule 3 or any regulations made under section 11, 12(7) or 13, where breach of those regulations is an offence, shall be liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both.

(1A)[^c-0d7a176821f12cccb3aaa79b57643bf7] A person guilty of an offence under section 4 or 8(1) or (2) shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months, or to a fine not exceeding £20,000, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years, or to a fine, or both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under any of sections [^c-446e96c6b8a8e12efc8eb80205d6173a]..., 5, 6(1), (2), (11) and (15), 7 , 8(3), 33(9) and 40(7)[^c-36545dee3c6ac4fb8a70baf99741f596] shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years[^c-15858bcd959d4c406b581ad4ecb627b4] , or to a fine, or both.
