# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 88

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 88. Extension of administrator’s term of office[^c-c17eaffb2375d0dd5376f5d0c443e3f0]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In paragraph 77(2)(b) of Schedule B1 (administrator’s term of office may be extended for up to 6 months by consent), for “6 months” substitute “1 year”.
