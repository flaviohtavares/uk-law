# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 31

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Directors’ statement of affairs to creditors[^key-e7b06e35e13b8268afaad68352464b33]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 85 (directors to lay statement of affairs before creditors) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) The directors of the company must, before the end of the period of 7 days beginning with the day after the day on which the company passes a resolution for voluntary winding up—
>
> - (a) make out a statement in the prescribed form as to the affairs of the company, and
> - (b) send the statement to the company’s creditors.

.

(3) For paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) If the directors without reasonable excuse contravene paragraph (1) or (2), they are guilty of an offence.

.
