# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 69

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 69. Additional effect on undischarged bankrupt[^key-632d8e82afb8ccde7326d519e186d7ac]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 235 (additional effect on undischarged bankrupt) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(a), for “the creditors’ meeting summoned under Article 231 approves” substitute “pursuant to Article 231 the debtor’s creditors decide to approve”.

(3) In paragraph (3)(a), for “the decision of the creditors’ meeting” substitute “the creditors’ decision”.
