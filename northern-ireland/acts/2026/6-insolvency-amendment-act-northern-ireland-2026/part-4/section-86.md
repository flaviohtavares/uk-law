# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 86

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 86. Offence of making false statements[^c-db3057db3418de98af02414c6e7588db]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 327(3)(c) (offence of making false statements)—

- (a) for “at any meeting of his creditors” substitute “in connection with any creditors’ decision procedure or deemed consent procedure”;
- (b) for “at such a meeting” substitute “in connection with such a procedure”.
