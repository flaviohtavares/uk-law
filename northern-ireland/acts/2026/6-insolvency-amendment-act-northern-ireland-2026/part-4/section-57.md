# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 57

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Protection of supplies of goods and services[^c-6313c90fce4fb02b07ed7ae2a887e6b1]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 197B(8) (protection of supplies of goods and services), in sub-paragraph (e)—

- (a) for the words from “when the liquidator has” to “the company’s creditors, or” substitute

  > —
  >
  > - (i) when the liquidator complies with Article 80(2), 92(2) or 124(3) (duties relating to final account), or

  ;
- (b) the words from “when the appointment” to “Companies Act 2006” become paragraph (ii) of that sub-paragraph.
