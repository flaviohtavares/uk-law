# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 75

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 75. Default in connection with voluntary arrangement[^key-8743830282e51bcd6f929a4a450bc185]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 250(1)(b)(ii) (default in connection with voluntary arrangement), for “at or in connection with a meeting summoned” substitute “in connection with a creditors’ decision procedure instigated”.
