# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 74

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Definition of “bankrupt’s estate”[^c-33f07a201c1d9eba9b32749316e0461e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 11(4)(a) (definition of “bankrupt’s estate”), for “a meeting summoned by the trustee of that estate under Article 304 has been held” substitute “the trustee of that estate has vacated office under Article 271(7)”.
