## 56. Misconduct in the course of winding up[^c-1ed270f11902e489bb30ae305109f768]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 172(2) (misconduct in course of winding up), for “at any meeting” substitute “in connection with any qualifying decision procedure or deemed consent procedure”.
