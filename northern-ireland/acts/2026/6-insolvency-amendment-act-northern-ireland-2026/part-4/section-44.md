# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 44

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Delegation of High Court’s powers to liquidator[^c-1925920eef26bc5975c1d1bd26b7c42e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 137(1) (delegation of High Court’s powers to liquidator), for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) the seeking of decisions on any matter from creditors and contributories,

.
