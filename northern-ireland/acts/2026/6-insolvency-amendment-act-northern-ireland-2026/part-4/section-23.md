## 23. Report by administrative receiver[^key-0b6585b5e27b56656b06a761588516b8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 58 (report by administrative receiver) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), after “such creditors” insert “, other than opted-out creditors,”.

(3) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “company” insert “, other than opted-out creditors”;
- (b) omit the words after sub-paragraph (b).

(4) Omit paragraph (3).
