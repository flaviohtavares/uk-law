# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 37

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Powers of directors where no liquidator appointed or nominated by company[^c-1e09efb526eec1f9a05edfd74bd186e9]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 99(2) (restriction on exercise of powers where no liquidator appointed or nominated by company)—

- (a) omit “Article 84 (creditors’ meeting) and”;
- (b) after “Article 85 (statement of affairs)” insert “and Article 86(1B) (nomination of liquidator by creditors)”.
