## 24. Committee of creditors[^key-ad0a451fd686d67e79a5269343106f3d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 59(1) (committee of creditors), for the words from the beginning to “fit” substitute “Where an administrative receiver has sent or published a report as mentioned in Article 58(2) the company’s unsecured creditors may, in accordance with the rules”.
