## 71. Prosecution of delinquent debtors[^key-f370afae15e9980d1aa3380af182e755]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 236B(1) (prosecution of delinquent debtors), for “creditors’ meeting summoned under” substitute “decision of the debtor’s creditors pursuant to”.
