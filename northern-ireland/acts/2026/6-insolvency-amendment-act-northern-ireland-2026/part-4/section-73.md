## 73. Implementation and supervision of approved voluntary arrangement[^c-6918e50bc00a9d1bd178637442cf29a2]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 237(1) (implementation and supervision of approved voluntary arrangement), for “creditors’ meeting summoned under” substitute “decision of the debtor’s creditors pursuant to”.
