# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Nominee’s report on company’s proposal[^key-17cf4ab51cfe32b8712e1018395967a2]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 15(2) (nominee’s report on company’s proposal), for sub-paragraphs (aa) and (b) substitute—

> - (b) whether, in his opinion, the proposal should be considered by a meeting of the company and by the company’s creditors, and
> - (c) if in his opinion it should, the date on which, and time and place at which, he proposes a meeting of the company should be held.

.
