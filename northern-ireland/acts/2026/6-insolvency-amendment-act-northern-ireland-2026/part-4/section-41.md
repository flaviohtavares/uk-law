## 41. Appointment of liquidator by High Court following administration or voluntary arrangement[^c-f8f0f919de9fb338bd97572e3ea37147]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 119(3) (appointment of liquidator by High Court following administration or voluntary arrangement), for the words from “he” to the end substitute “Article 116(5)(a) and (b) do not apply.”.
