## 35. Progress report to company and creditors[^key-5ecb5f31c4b79caf9f01c8dbd0e001a1]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 91 as substituted by section 3(2) of the Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (progress report to company and creditors), in paragraph (1)(b)(i), after “creditors” insert “, other than opted-out creditors,”.
