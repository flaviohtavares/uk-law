# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Regulated companies: modifications to Part 1A[^key-efa3fbe75296a7d6980ffabcf13f2dc7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 13H (regulated companies: modifications to Part 1A), in paragraphs (4) and (6), for the words from “any meeting” to “held” substitute “any qualifying decision procedure by which a decision of the company’s creditors is sought”.
