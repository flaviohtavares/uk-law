# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 52

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52. Meetings to ascertain wishes of creditors or contributories[^c-3e351a25f44373e1bba6459f021d7bb3]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 164 (meetings to ascertain wishes of creditors or contributories) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(b), for the words from “meetings” to the end substitute “qualifying decision procedures to be instigated or the deemed consent procedure to be used in accordance with any directions given by the Court, and appoint a person to report the result to the Court.”.

(3) In the heading, for “Meetings” substitute “High Court’s powers”.
