# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 87

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 87. Provision that may be included in individual insolvency rules[^c-a933229ae610300811d9a0bcc2a22c0f]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Schedule 6 (provision that may be included in individual insolvency rules) is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 10 insert—

> **10A.** Provision about how a bankrupt’s creditors may appoint a person as trustee.

.

(3) In paragraph 11 (creditors’ committee)—

- (a) after “as to the” insert “establishment,”;
- (b) for “established under” substitute “provided for by”.
