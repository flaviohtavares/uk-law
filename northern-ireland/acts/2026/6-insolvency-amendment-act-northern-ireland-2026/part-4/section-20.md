## 20. Supervisor of voluntary arrangement[^key-9270858fade870b1cc227f6283c5a20a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 20(2)(a) (supervisor of the voluntary arrangement), for “given at one or both of the meetings summoned under” substitute “of the voluntary arrangement by the company or its creditors (or both) pursuant to”.
