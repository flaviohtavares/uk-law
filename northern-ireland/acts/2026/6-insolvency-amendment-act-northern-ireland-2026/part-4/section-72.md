# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 72

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Arrangements coming to an end prematurely[^c-53ba5c6dbbc4d17c1db08b4f185d66c9]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Article 236C (arrangements coming to an end prematurely), for “creditors’ meeting summoned under” substitute “decision of the debtor’s creditors pursuant to”.
