# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 102

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 102. Miscellaneous provision about insolvency law[^key-89dd95f6bf551b722ae5e2c28e453d7d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 3 to this Act—

- (a) makes provision about the administration and winding up of companies and bankruptcy, and
- (b) contains other minor amendments relating to insolvency.
