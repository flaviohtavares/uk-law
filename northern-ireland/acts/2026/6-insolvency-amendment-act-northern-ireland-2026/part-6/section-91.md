# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 91

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Creditors not required to prove small debts: individual insolvency[^key-2bc209d12c93a821421dab023d6908f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Schedule 6 (provision that may be included in individual insolvency rules), after paragraph 16 insert—

> **16A.** Provision for a creditor who has not proved a small debt to be treated as having done so for purposes relating to the distribution of a bankrupt’s estate (and for provisions of, or contained in legislation made under, this Order to apply accordingly).
