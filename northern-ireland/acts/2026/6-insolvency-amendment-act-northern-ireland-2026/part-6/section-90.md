# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 90

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Creditors not required to prove small debts: company insolvency[^key-e95da77f0b1d6859a1dc7db9922eeb91]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Schedule 5 (provision that may be included in company insolvency rules), after paragraph 13 insert—

> **13A.** Provision for a creditor who has not proved a small debt to be treated as having done so for purposes relating to the distribution of a company’s property (and for provisions of, or contained in legislation made under, this Order to apply accordingly).
