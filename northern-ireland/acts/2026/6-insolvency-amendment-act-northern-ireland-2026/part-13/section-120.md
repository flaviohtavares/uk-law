# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 120

> Text as at 2026-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 120. Commencement[^key-3d6614eec1cbb21be8d0f3fb17989318][^key-11cd7e511f0a2609841b33729ef9d510]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Part 9 and this Part come into operation on the day after the day on which this Act receives Royal Assent.

(2) The other provisions of this Act come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may contain such transitional or saving provisions as the Department considers appropriate.
