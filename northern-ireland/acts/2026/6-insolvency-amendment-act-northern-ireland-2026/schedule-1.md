# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, Schedule 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 1 — Amendments to Schedule B1 to the 1989 Order

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 21

**1.**[^c-2876fcba52dc4e3f2158f6a464067443] Schedule B1 (administration) is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-d100487f97ba9f3104def81e93b19a0b] In paragraph 1(1), omit the definitions of “correspondence” and “creditors’ meeting”.

**3.**[^c-58f3e1975cf0b1a7cbb50496b6cf7303] In paragraph 50(4)(b), after “company” insert “, other than an opted-out creditor,”.

**4.**[^c-3de6fbfa4c3605ca48a37738c3e01727] Omit paragraph 51 and the heading before it.

**5.**[^c-9c518d8f083d9a813fd815ba5dbf811e] For the heading before paragraph 52 substitute “*Consideration of administrator’s proposals by creditors*”.

**6.**[^c-1dafc2e301b3b8a05a4ea21c345eb4b8] In paragraph 52, for sub-paragraphs (1) to (3) substitute—

> (1) The administrator must seek a decision from the company’s creditors as to whether they approve the proposals set out in the statement made under paragraph 50(1).
>
> (2) The initial decision date for that decision must be within the period of 10 weeks beginning with the day on which the company enters administration.
>
> (3) The “initial decision date” for that decision—
>
> - (a) if the decision is initially sought using the deemed consent procedure, is the date on which a decision will be made if the creditors by that procedure approve the proposals, and
> - (b) if the decision is initially sought using a qualifying decision procedure, is the date on or before which a decision will be made if it is made by that qualifying decision procedure (assuming that date does not change after the procedure is instigated).

.

**7.**[^c-1c7a15f691d9c990b227df59153778fa] In paragraph 53(2), for the words from “summon” to “requested” substitute “seek a decision from the company’s creditors as to whether they approve the proposals set out in the statement made under paragraph 50(1) if requested to do so”.

**8.**[^c-a476d743882aa43c2925075ed1dfb232] For paragraph 53(3) substitute—

> (3) Where a decision is sought by virtue of sub-paragraph (2) the initial decision date (as defined in paragraph 52(3)) must be within the prescribed period.

.

**9.**[^c-fc72c9a37b697b245be0c4d854afd463] For the heading before paragraph 54 substitute “*Creditors’ decision*”.

**10.**[^c-c3a317130fa468348cde7131dda6eddd] In paragraph 54, for sub-paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) The company’s creditors may approve the administrator’s proposals—
>
> - (a) without modification, or
> - (b) with modification to which the administrator consents.

.

**11.**[^c-82ddf9adb80825c7c5ca29273c85478d] In paragraph 54(2)—

- (a) for “After the conclusion of an initial creditors’ meeting the” substitute “The”;
- (b) after “taken” insert “by the company’s creditors”.

**12.**[^c-2e996e851056f2314b1a91f255c55d08] In paragraph 55(1)(a), for “at an initial creditors’ meeting” substitute “by the company’s creditors”.

**13.**[^c-e8f7f0ad46abc8800853f1e78c486ee8] Omit paragraph 55(2)(a).

**14.**[^c-4725fca67387f63bcb183216fb6ccecf] In paragraph 55(2)(b)—

- (a) omit “with the notice of the meeting sent”;
- (b) after “creditor” insert “who is not an opted-out creditor”.

**15.**[^c-d140fcb2aa2fc2baa93255b69b60d58a] For paragraph 55(2)(d) substitute—

> - (d) seek a decision from the company’s creditors as to whether they approve the proposed revision.

.

**16.**[^c-0e8af2b08e60782368206fc4c09fd0ba] For paragraph 55(5) substitute—

> (5) The company’s creditors may approve the proposed revision—
>
> - (a) without modification, or
> - (b) with modification to which the administrator consents.

.

**17.**[^c-13c5834afb309298d88b9d848bff43be] In paragraph 55(6)—

- (a) for “After the conclusion of a creditors’ meeting the” substitute “The”;
- (b) after “taken” insert “by the company’s creditors”.

**18.**[^c-b74bd7422bf4a5f8ddcb34cda31a6f0a] For paragraph 56(1) substitute—

> (1) This paragraph applies where an administrator—
>
> - (a) reports to the High Court under paragraph 54 that a company’s creditors have failed to approve the administrator’s proposals, or
> - (b) reports to the Court under paragraph 55 that a company’s creditors have failed to approve a revision of the administrator’s proposals.

.

**19.**[^c-264898bfe0dd275380f4845ffad8e320] In the heading before paragraph 57, for “*meetings*” substitute “*decisions*”.

**20.**[^c-186de40dede840ec3235c44e4a6c9774] In paragraph 57(1), for “summon a creditors’ meeting”—

- (a) in the first place that it occurs, substitute “seek a decision from the company’s creditors on a matter”;
- (b) in the second place that it occurs, substitute “do so”.

**21.**[^c-a4355204e3caf75ed530a30f89caef2c] In paragraph 57(2), for “summon a creditors’ meeting” substitute “seek a decision from the company’s creditors on a matter”.

**22.**[^c-73564bb1422d6dc80a916462627aaeb9] In paragraph 58(1), for “A creditors’ meeting may” substitute “The company’s creditors may, in accordance with the rules,”.

**23.**[^c-b488d65ab64f17d5cef296c5c6348968] Omit paragraph 59 and the heading before it.

**24.**[^c-a866dd7e89e735a3a2cfb4b2998ee3bb] In paragraph 63, for the words from “may” to the end substitute

> may—
>
> - (a) call a meeting of members of the company;
> - (b) seek a decision on any matter from the company’s creditors.

.

**25.**[^c-d4f466eff73459b6970009be3542cafd] For paragraph 75(4)(c) substitute—

> - (c) require a decision of the company’s creditors to be sought on a matter;

.

**26.**[^c-afd9a5d211166a7609609b201f0174d1] For paragraph 79(1)(b) substitute—

> - (b) if the company has unsecured debts, the unsecured creditors of the company.

.

**27.**[^c-6421cd8f5b4b2307c376035d7e437e72] For paragraph 79(2)(b)(ii) substitute—

> - (ii) the preferential creditors of the company.

.

**28.**[^c-404e19827d54e8abe2e3aa6de9fe0b9e] After paragraph 79(2) insert—

> (2A) Whether the company’s unsecured creditors or preferential creditors consent is to be determined by the administrator seeking a decision from those creditors as to whether they consent.

.

**29.**[^c-f9e18ed3cda4ada3900cfaa357cdb675] Omit paragraph 79(3).

**30.**[^c-a91fdd4beb8096fe5862f0ba3125157b] In paragraph 80(2)(c), for “a creditors’ meeting requires him to” substitute “the company’s creditors decide that he must”.

**31.**[^c-7fa8ef9af77cbccf7562ecbce98e744d] In paragraph 81(4), after “company” insert “, other than an opted-out creditor,”.

**32.**[^c-73740c6bd2fc24baa735c2eac482f99a] In paragraph 84(4)(b), after “creditor” insert “, other than an opted-out creditor,”.

**33.**[^c-6a78c0c42d4ae27d486dd67682bd44b0] In paragraph 84(7)(d), omit “84,”.

**34.**[^c-2a3599c023b61c59b11e9bfd7675fe7d] In paragraph 85(5)(b), after “creditor” insert “, other than an opted-out creditor,”.

**35.**[^c-ad2bbb52b8c0a3bd65c0cf0aa3388215] In the heading before paragraph 98, for “*meeting*” substitute “*decision*”.

**36.**[^c-b8410ae6fb79b0e7578e59880011e1d8] For paragraph 98(2) and (3) substitute—

> (2) The administrator may be replaced by a decision of the creditors made by a qualifying decision procedure.
>
> (3) The decision has effect only if, before the decision is made, the new administrator has consented to act in writing.

.

**37.**[^c-6b9eebc90a5052d1323970e06d5dad40] In paragraph 109(1), omit “, 51(1)(b)”.

**38.**[^c-aabb620d0a6e47d2e8cf4257e684b0e7] For paragraph 109(2)(b) substitute—

> - (b) if the company has unsecured debts, the unsecured creditors of the company.

.

**39.**[^c-0acfdadad300209ab91cf27bc4cae5af] For paragraph 109(3)(b)(ii) substitute—

> - (ii) the preferential creditors of the company.

.

**40.**[^c-cecd1806d205af61cadb260f1fcbf003] After paragraph 109(3) insert—

> (3A) Whether the company’s unsecured creditors or preferential creditors consent is to be determined by the administrator seeking a decision from those creditors as to whether they consent.

.

**41.**[^c-09195dc442bc8d0d41a36f81c450dc19] Omit paragraph 109(4).
