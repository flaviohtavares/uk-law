# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 112

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 112. Creditors’ winding-up: concurrent petition against member or members[^c-70bcbc1047ac933e3ee2749904b4c9c8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 8 of the 1995 Order is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), omit from “, of a liquidator” to “Article 38 of the EC Regulation)”.

(3) In paragraph (5)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “8” substitute “8A”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), for “XIV” substitute “15”.

(4) In paragraph (7)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “2,” insert “2B,”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a), for “and 11” substitute “, 11 and 11A”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for “XIV” substitute “15”.
