# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 113

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 113. Members’ winding-up: concurrent petitions against all members[^c-815a4fd984b64996d20f5b03eb62ceda]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Article 10 of the 1995 Order is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(a), for “it is” substitute “they are”.

(3) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “8” substitute “8A”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), for “XIV” substitute “15”.

(4) In paragraph (5)—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) Articles 2, 2B, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11 and 11A in Part I,

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c), for “XIV” substitute “15”.
