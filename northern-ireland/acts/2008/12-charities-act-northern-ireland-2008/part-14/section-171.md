> Text as at 2022-03-30.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 171. Supply by Commission of copies of documents open to public inspection

*Extent: N.I.*

The Commission shall, at the request of any person, provide[^key-ca32b5331434801aaf63b9601ef4e243] that person with copies of, or extracts from, any document in the Commission's possession which is for the time being open to inspection under this Act.
