# Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 185

> Text as at 2022-03-30.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 185. Commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Act, except section 180, this section and section 186, comes into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.

(2) Without prejudice to section 17(5) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33) (statutory powers and duties generally), an order under subsection (1) may provide that provisions are to come into operation on different days in relation to charities of different descriptions.
