# Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 62

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Interpretation of Part 7

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this Part—

- “**land**” means land in Northern Ireland;
- “**mortgage**” includes a charge; and
- “**rentcharge**” means a periodical payment charged on land.

(2) Nothing in section 57 or 58 applies to any disposition of land by way of mortgage or other security.
