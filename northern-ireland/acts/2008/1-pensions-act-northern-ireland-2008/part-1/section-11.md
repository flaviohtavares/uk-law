## 11. Increase in pensionable age for men and women

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Schedule 3 amends Article 123 of, and Part 1 of Schedule 2 to, the Pensions Order for the purpose of increasing the pensionable age for men and women[^key-4121f94e2547f3b80d9bdcd96800b4a2]....

(2) Part 8 of Schedule 1 contains consequential amendments.

(3) The amendments made by that Part of that Schedule have effect as from 6th December 2018[^key-4dc3f49d2310cbccc22aa845998e4dae].
