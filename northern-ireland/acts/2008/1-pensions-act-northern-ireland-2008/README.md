Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008

2008 CHAPTER 1

[^c21200091]An Act to make provision about pensions and other benefits payable to persons in connection with bereavement or by reference to pensionable age; to make provision about the functions of the Personal Accounts Delivery Authority; and for connected purposes.

\[11th February 2008\]
