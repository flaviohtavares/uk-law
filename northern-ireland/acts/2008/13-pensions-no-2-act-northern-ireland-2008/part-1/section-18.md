## 18. Occupational pension schemes[^c19557851][^key-2c7ac44c277ca0c176f61477f46f0689]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, each of these is an occupational pension scheme—

- (a) an occupational pension scheme within the meaning of section 1(1) of the Pension Schemes Act that has its main administration in the United Kingdom;
- (b)[^key-45905e2653d6c74f5214e5cba215c910] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a pension scheme that is prescribed or is of a prescribed description and that has its main administration elsewhere than in the United Kingdom[^key-8bb5e67595c681569e991aefea31e687].
