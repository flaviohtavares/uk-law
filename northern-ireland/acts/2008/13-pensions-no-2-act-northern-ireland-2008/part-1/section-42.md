> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2008/13
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Penalty notices: recovery[^key-ca9807ef4007f2bf22a04001ee34f76b][^key-2fd37e2ceaa73fc638995ba555cdbbc8][^key-b000eabf6f17336c4b56b37edf07715d][^key-d4b9705a2486ea3794e67d94455a9696][^key-d4488105d3e04631927a54b435ae1363]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any penalty payable under section 40 or section 41 is recoverable by the Regulator.

(2) The Regulator must pay into the Consolidated Fund any penalty recovered under this section.
