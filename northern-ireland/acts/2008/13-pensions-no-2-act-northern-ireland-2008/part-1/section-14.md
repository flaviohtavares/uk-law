## 14. Review of earnings trigger and qualifying earnings band[^c19557711][^c-1fb92e8c9bd429735310adbbf1f56399][^c-22a5e84b2e2272299da60f0745f9b0b6]

*Extent: N.I.*

Whenever the Secretary of State makes an order under section 14 of the Pensions Act 2008 (c. 30) (review of earnings trigger and[^c-bf10fff66f8cfabfb435406e13452bcf] qualifying earnings band), the Department may make a corresponding order for Northern Ireland.
