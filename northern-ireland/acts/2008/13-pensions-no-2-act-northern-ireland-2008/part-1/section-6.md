## 6. Timing of automatic re-enrolment[^c19555501][^c-56ec85bc159df36755ae718a66272a18][^c-295e4dd8d1f46032118beafb1e305989]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations under section 5(8) must either—

- (a) secure that for any jobholder there is no automatic re-enrolment date less than three years after the jobholder's automatic enrolment date, and that there is not more than one automatic re-enrolment date in any period of three years, or
- (b) secure that for any employer there is not more than one automatic re-enrolment date in any period of 2 years and 9 months[^c-6df99e782baccc4076f1ff9b1a46c0a5] .

(2) Subsection (1) does not restrict the provision that regulations may make about the timing of a jobholder's automatic re-enrolment date (“**the relevant date**”) in the following cases.

(3)[^c-e89e45c3bcafbb89fe066b3160dd5257] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The first case[^c-8d865480444c355dbcb1d79ad1a46044] is where—

- (a) [^c-af8040228068ca59dca5a5fc495c8127]... the jobholder ceases to be an active member of a qualifying scheme [^c-e11cd4c9876da3d47d0e3f1ee23eadb7]...,
- (b) that event is not the effect of any action or omission by the jobholder [^c-f2361d6b9f82ab474c1732db29b39fc4]..., and
- (c) the relevant date is the jobholder's first automatic re-enrolment date after that event[^c-545ac0841a507d1ebd535c9a603fea64] .

(5) The second case[^c-99dd2e97f39780580aff96bbed82ea52] is where—

- (a) there is a period beginning at any time after the jobholder's automatic enrolment date during which the requirements of section 1(1)(a) or (c) are not met (so that the person is not a jobholder for that period), and
- (b) the relevant date is the jobholder's first automatic re-enrolment date after that period.
