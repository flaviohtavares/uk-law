# Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2016-02-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2008/10
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Extinction of liability in respect of interest and fees

*Extent: N.I.*

Any outstanding liability in respect of the following is extinguished—

- (a) interest under the Child Support (Arrears, Interest and Adjustment of Maintenance Assessments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992 (SR 1992/342);
- (b) fees under the Child Support Fees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 (SR 1993/73)
