# Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2016-02-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2008/10
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Offence of failing to notify change of address[^c21205121]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 16A of the Child Support Order (information – offences), after paragraph (3) insert—

> (3A) In the case of regulations under Article 16 which require a person liable to make payments of child support maintenance to notify a change of address, a person who fails to comply with the requirement is guilty of an offence.

.

(2) In that Article, in paragraph (4), after “paragraph (3)” insert “ or (3A) ”
