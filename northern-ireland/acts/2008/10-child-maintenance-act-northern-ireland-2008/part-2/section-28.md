# Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 28

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Additional special case[^key-a1fc520a8d1f6aea93ece6aa5d9d79e9]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 39(2) of the Child Support Order (examples of cases in relation to which the power under paragraph (1) to prescribe circumstances in which a case is to be treated as a special case for the purposes of the Order may be exercised), at the end add—

> - (g) the same persons are the parents of 2 or more children and each parent is—
>   - (i) a non-resident parent in relation to one or more of the children, and
>   - (ii) a person with care in relation to one or more of the children.

.
