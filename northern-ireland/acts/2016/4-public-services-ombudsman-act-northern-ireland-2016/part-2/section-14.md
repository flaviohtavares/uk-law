# Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/4
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Matters which may be investigated: general[^c22561821]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ombudsman may investigate alleged maladministration through action taken in the exercise of administrative functions by a listed authority.

(2) This section does not apply to—

- (a)[^c-f274154f5ddede0e07378497f52cde28] health and social care bodies,
- (b) general health care providers,
- (c) independent providers,
- (d) the universities referred to in section 18.
