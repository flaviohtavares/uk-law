## 29. Procedure for own initiative investigations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before commencing an investigation under section 8, the Ombudsman must—

- (a) prepare an investigation proposal, and
- (b) submit that proposal to any listed authority which it is proposed be investigated.

(2) The investigation proposal must set out—

- (a) the reasons for the proposed investigation, and
- (b) how the criteria referred to in section 9 have been met.
