## 27. Meaning of exhausting the complaints handling procedure

*Extent: N.I.*

Acomplaints handling procedure is exhausted when—

- (a) a final decision is made on the complaint,
- (b) it is determined not to deal with the complaint, or
- (c) it is determined not to deal any further with the complaint.
