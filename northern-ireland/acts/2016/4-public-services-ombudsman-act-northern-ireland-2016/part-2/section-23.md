# Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/4

## 23. Decisions taken without maladministration

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Act authorises the Ombudsman to question the merits of a decision taken without maladministration by a listed authority in the exercise of a discretion.

(2) But this is subject to—

- (a) section 15(2)(b),
- (b) section 16(2)(b), and
- (c) section 17(2)(b).
