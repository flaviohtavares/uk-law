# Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 45

## 45. Publication of reports on own initiative investigations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ombudsman must publish a report of an investigation conducted under section 8.

(2) The Ombudsman may, for a reasonable fee, supply a copy of the report, or any part of it, to any person who requests it.
