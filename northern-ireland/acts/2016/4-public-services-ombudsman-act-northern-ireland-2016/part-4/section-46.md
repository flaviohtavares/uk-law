# Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/4
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Reports to the Assembly[^c22561841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c22801181][^c22801221] The Ombudsman must lay before the Assembly annually a general report on the exercise of the Ombudsman's functions.

(2) If, after conducting an investigation (other than one under section 8) it appears to the Ombudsman that—

- (a) an injustice has been sustained by a person aggrieved, and
- (b) the injustice has not been, or will not be, remedied or adequately remedied,

the Ombudsman may lay before the Assembly a special report on the investigation.

(3) Where the Ombudsman has conducted an investigation under section 8, the Ombudsman must lay the report on that investigation before the Assembly.

(4)[^c22801191][^c22801231] The Ombudsman may lay before the Assembly such other reports on the exercise of the Ombudsman's functions as the Ombudsman thinks fit.
