# Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 47

## 47. Reports and privileged information

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies where the Ombudsman has obtained documents or evidence under section 31(1) by virtue of section 32(2).

(2) A report made by the Ombudsman under sections 43 to 46—

- (a) may disclose that documents or evidence of this nature were obtained, but
- (b) must not disclose the content of those documents or that evidence.
