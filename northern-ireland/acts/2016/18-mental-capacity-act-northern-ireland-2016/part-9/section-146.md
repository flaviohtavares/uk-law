# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 146

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 146. Maximum period of detention under Part 9

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A person removed from a public place under section 139 may not be detained under any provision of this Part after the end of the period of 24 hours beginning with the time of that removal.

(2) The Department of Justice may by regulations amend subsection (1) so as to alter the period mentioned there.
