## 274. Renaming of Mental Health Review Tribunal[^key-ac5ca7544622330927280937409802fd]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Mental Health Review Tribunal for Northern Ireland constituted under Article 70 of the Mental Health Order is renamed the Review Tribunal.
