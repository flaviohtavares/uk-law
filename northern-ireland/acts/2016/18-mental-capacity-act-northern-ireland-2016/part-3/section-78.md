## 78. Notice declining to be a person's nominated person[^key-625f5e3950d96f6687c46987db764851]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person may at any time decline to be the default nominated person for another person (“P”) by giving notice in writing to that effect to P.

(2) A person who has given notice under subsection (1) may at any time withdraw the notice by giving a further notice in writing to P.
