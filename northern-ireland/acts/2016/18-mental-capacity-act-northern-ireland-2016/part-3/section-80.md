# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2022-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/18
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Application to Tribunal for appointment of nominated person

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] A qualifying person may apply to the Tribunal for an order under section 81 in relation to a person who is 16 or over (“P”) if the qualifying person reasonably believes that—

- (a) P lacks capacity to make decisions about who should be his or her nominated person; and
- (b) one of the conditions mentioned in subsection (2) is met.

(2)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] Those conditions are—

- (a) the person who is P's nominated person is not suitable to be so;
- (b) there is no-one who is P's nominated person;
- (c) it is not practicable to establish whether P has a nominated person;
- (d) someone is P's nominated person, but it is not practicable to establish who that is.

(3)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] The factors that may be taken into account in determining whether a person is not suitable to be P's nominated person include whether the person has behaved, is behaving or proposes to behave in a way that is not in P's best interests.

(4)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] An application may be made on the ground mentioned in subsection (2)(c) or (d) only if the applicant has taken reasonable steps to establish—

- (a) whether P has a nominated person; or (as the case may be)
- (b) who P's nominated person is.

(5) In this section “**a qualifying person**” means any of the following—

- (a)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] an appropriate healthcare professional;
- (b)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] if P is an in-patient in a hospital or care home, the managing authority of the hospital or care home;
- (c)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] if P is living in a place of a prescribed description, a prescribed person;
- (d) an attorney under a lasting power of attorney, or an enduring power of attorney, granted by P;
- (e) a deputy appointed for P by the court;
- (f)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] any relative of P;
- (g)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] any person interested in P's welfare.

(6)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] In this section “**appropriate healthcare professional**” means a person of a prescribed description.

(7)[^c-543da1222e96d73353ee70b7fd99a87a] Where the applicant is a person within subsection (5)(a) or (b), the applicant must send a copy of the application to RQIA as soon as practicable.
