# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 291

## 291. Provision of facilities by HSC trusts and the Department

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-9acb754aa34b9b5ad08b48154727057d] An HSC trust must provide to a relevant authority such facilities as are necessary to enable the relevant authority to perform its functions under this Act.

(2) In subsection (1) “**relevant authority**” means—

- (a)[^c-9acb754aa34b9b5ad08b48154727057d] the High Court;
- (b) the Public Guardian; or
- (c)[^c-9acb754aa34b9b5ad08b48154727057d] the Tribunal.

(3)[^c-9acb754aa34b9b5ad08b48154727057d] The Department must provide, to the Tribunal, such facilities as are necessary to enable the Tribunal to perform its functions under this Act.
