## 300. Medical practitioners who may make certain medical reports

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this section a “**medical report**” means—

- (a)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] a report under section 39;
- (b) a report under section 183;
- (c)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] a medical report under paragraph 7 of Schedule 1;
- (d)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] a medical report under paragraph 4 of Schedule 2;
- (e)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] a medical report under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3;
- (f) a medical report under paragraph 5 of Schedule 6.

(2)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] Regulations may make provision prescribing the descriptions of medical practitioners who may make medical reports.

(3)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] The regulations may in particular do any of the following—

- (a) prescribe conditions that must be met by a medical practitioner making a medical report;
- (b) provide that, except in any prescribed circumstances, the medical practitioner who makes a medical report—
  - (i) must be of a prescribed description; or
  - (ii) must not be of a prescribed description.

(4)[^c-d13619d82866b1ca124adafc8d71df27] Where any provision of this Act confers power to prescribe conditions that must be met by a medical practitioner making a report or certifying any matter, a condition that may be prescribed is that the practitioner is a person approved by RQIA for prescribed purposes.
