# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 289

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2022-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/18
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 289. Effect of code

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-381d5a0c9baaade4cdccf55e2b64a56b] A person acting in any of the ways mentioned in subsection (2) in relation to a person who is 16 or over and lacks capacity must have regard to any relevant code of practice.

(2) The ways of acting are—

- (a)[^c-381d5a0c9baaade4cdccf55e2b64a56b] in a professional capacity;
- (b)[^c-381d5a0c9baaade4cdccf55e2b64a56b] for remuneration;
- (c) as an independent mental capacity advocate;
- (d) as an attorney under a lasting power of attorney or an enduring power of attorney;
- (e) as a deputy appointed by the court;
- (f) as a person carrying out research in reliance on any provision made by or under this Act (see Part 8).

(3)[^c-381d5a0c9baaade4cdccf55e2b64a56b] If it appears to a court or tribunal conducting any criminal or civil proceedings that—

- (a) a provision of a code of practice, or
- (b) a failure to comply with a code of practice,

is relevant to a question arising in the proceedings, the provision or failure must be taken into account in deciding the question.

(4)[^c-381d5a0c9baaade4cdccf55e2b64a56b] In this section “**code of practice**” means a code of practice under section 288.
