## 301. Documents appearing to be duly made[^c-b6b26825dffa2f59320ca9527adf4ee4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations may make provision enabling documents of a prescribed description which appear to be duly made in pursuance of a prescribed provision of this Act to be acted on without further proof of prescribed matters.
