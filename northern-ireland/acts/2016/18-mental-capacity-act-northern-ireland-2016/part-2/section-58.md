## 58. Ways in which information must be provided[^c-9cee7114a6342dce0d02d8d9ab0eefca]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision about the way in which relevant information must be given to prescribed persons.

(2) In this section “**relevant information**” means information which is—

- (a) required to be given by any provision of this Part or of regulations made under this Part; and
- (b) specified by the regulations under this section.

(3) Regulations under this section may in particular require information to be given orally as well as in writing.
