# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2022-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/18
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Duty of HSC trust to notify the Attorney General

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a)[^c-3bceef07f5b7cfcaf320c04988f31ab0] the period of an authorisation under Schedule 1 has been extended (under section 38 or Schedule 3) for a period of one year;
- (b)[^c-5e6fdb59cfa5bd4b7853d16f1b406252] the authorisation authorises a measure within section 41(2)(b) or (d) (deprivation of liberty or community residence requirement); and
- (c)[^c-3bceef07f5b7cfcaf320c04988f31ab0] at the relevant time, it appears to the relevant trust that the person to whom the authorisation relates lacks (or probably lacks) capacity in relation to whether an application under section 45 (applications to Tribunal) should be made.

(2)[^c-3bceef07f5b7cfcaf320c04988f31ab0] The relevant trust must as soon as practicable give the Attorney General—

- (a) notice of the matters mentioned in subsection (1)(a) to (c); and
- (b) any prescribed information.

(3)[^c-3bceef07f5b7cfcaf320c04988f31ab0] In this section—

- “**the relevant time**” means the time 6 months after the beginning of the one year period mentioned in subsection (1)(a);
- “**the relevant trust**” has the same meaning as in section 48.
