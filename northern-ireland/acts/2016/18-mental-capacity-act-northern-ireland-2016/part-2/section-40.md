## 40. Extension of period where responsible person not of the requisite opinion[^c-2533fbe4fcb42e629e07429a8b158b3a]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule3 makes provision for cases where it is proposed to make an extension under section 37 or 38 but the responsible person is not of the opinion that the criteria for continuation are met.
