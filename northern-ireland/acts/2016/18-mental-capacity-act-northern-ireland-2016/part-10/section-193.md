# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 193

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 193. Reports by responsible medical practitioner

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) While a public protection order with restrictions is in force in respect of a person, the responsible medical practitioner must at such intervals (not exceeding one year) as the Department of Justice may direct examine and report to the Department of Justice on that person.

(2) A report under this section must contain any prescribed information.
