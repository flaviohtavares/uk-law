# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 215

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 215. Detention in hospital on removal under section 214

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where a person is admitted to a hospital under a direction given under section 214 (transfer of civil prisoners and immigration detainees), the person continues to be liable to be detained in hospital under that direction until that direction ceases to have effect under section 216 or 240.

(2) In sections 201 to 203 (which relate to detention under hospital directions) any reference to a hospital direction includes a reference to a direction under section 214.
