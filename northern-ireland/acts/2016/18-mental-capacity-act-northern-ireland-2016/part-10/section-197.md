# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 197

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 197. Detention under a hospital direction

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This section applies where a person is admitted to a hospital under a hospital direction.

(2) The person continues to be liable to be detained in hospital under the hospital direction until the direction ceases to have effect under section 198 or 240.
