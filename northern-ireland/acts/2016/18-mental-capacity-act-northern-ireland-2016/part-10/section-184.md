# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 184

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 184. Extension of period where responsible person not of the requisite opinion

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule6 provides a procedure for cases where it is proposed to make an extension under section 181 or 182 but the responsible social worker is not of the opinion that the criteria for continuation are met.
