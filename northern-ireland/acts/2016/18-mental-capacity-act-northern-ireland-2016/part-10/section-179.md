# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 179

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 179. Detention under a public protection order without restrictions

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a public protection order without restrictions is made in respect of a person under section 167; and
- (b) the person is admitted in pursuance of the order to the establishment specified in the order.

(2) The person may be detained in that establishment for a period not exceeding 6 months beginning with the date of the order.

(3) Subsection (2) is subject to (in particular)—

- (a) section 180 (discharge by responsible medical officer);
- (b) sections 181 to 186 (extension of period for which person liable to be detained);
- (c) section 231 (powers of Tribunal).
