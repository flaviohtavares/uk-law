# Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 272

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2022-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2016/18
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 272. Obstruction

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-10ea3556397c132ddf05cd2e7655b35e] It is an offence for a person—

- (a) to refuse to allow the visiting or examination of any person by a person who is authorised by virtue of a relevant provision to carry out the visit or examination;
- (b) to refuse to produce any record the production of which is required by virtue of a provision mentioned in subsection (4)(a) or (b); or
- (c) otherwise to obstruct a person who is—
  - (i) carrying out a visit or examination by virtue of a relevant provision; or
  - (ii) exercising any other function by virtue of a provision mentioned in subsection (4)(a) or (b).

(2)[^key-10ea3556397c132ddf05cd2e7655b35e] Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), it is an offence for a person to insist on being present when requested to withdraw by a person authorised by virtue of a relevant provision to visit or examine a person.

(3)[^key-10ea3556397c132ddf05cd2e7655b35e] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale or both.

(4) In this section “**relevant provision**” means—

- (a) section 18(2), 92, 121, 127 or 131;
- (b)[^key-38fbb82c02e2719186bc95b8c76125b2] section 46, 47, 48, 227, 228 or 229 (read with section 275); or
- (c)[^key-38fbb82c02e2719186bc95b8c76125b2] section 39 or 126 or any provision of Schedule 1, 2 or 3.
