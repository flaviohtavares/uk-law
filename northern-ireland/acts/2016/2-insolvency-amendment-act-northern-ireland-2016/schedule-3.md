## SCHEDULE 3 — MINOR AND CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 27(2).

### The Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 (NI 12)

**1.**[^c-fc091bb06358e8d0e95c1eb823fe99bb] In Article 13(1)(k), for the words from “has entered” to the end substitute “ a composition or scheme proposed by the solicitor has been approved under Chapter 2 of Part 8 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989. ”.

**2.**[^c-23813c72d18248eb66be8ac31c443347] In Article 14A(2)(b), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) a composition or scheme proposed by the solicitor has been approved under Chapter 2 of Part 8 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989; or

.

**3.**[^c-3b31ffa675c2ddaf5b3a90b116800005] In Article 41(3)(a), for the words from “or enters” to “dies” substitute “ , or a composition or scheme proposed by the solicitor has been approved under Chapter 2 of Part 8 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 or the solicitor dies ”.

### The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (NI 19)

**4.**[^c-f9ea4a153917a73b1c4160a9b45def70] In Article 14(2), omit “or authorised to act as nominee,”.

**5.**[^c-dab81be1c4f98ea98a8a93851301d38c] In Article 15(4), omit “, or authorised to act as nominee,”.

**6.**[^c-996da1b5c5ada468f6c2c74e7575b5b0] In Article 17(2), omit “or authorised to act as nominee,”.

**7.**[^c-27aff054da7c958c091fc0d99b4374df] In Article 20(5), omit “or authorised to act as supervisor,”.

**8.**[^c-ea3d8be4fb70a9a300de38013e9a332e] In Article 103(1)(a), for “a demand” substitute “ a written demand ”.

**9.**[^c-cd058cf482bd00eaa6d6e78de9f8febc] In Article 185, after paragraph (2) insert—

> (2A) For all purposes of winding up, the principal place of business in Northern Ireland of the unregistered company is deemed to be the registered office of the company.

.

**10.**[^c-3109beff8b0f47fea6f49e5c510c71d8] In Article 186(1)(a) after “written demand” insert “ (known as “**the statutory demand**”) ”.

**11.**[^c-33890a906b33c4aa3f4752fe087f1c19] In Article 242(1)(a) for “a demand” substitute “ a written demand ”.

**12.**[^c-c3df20c7be720de7757ed5b3cc9f1e15] In Article 242(2)(a) for “a demand” substitute “ a written demand ”.

**13.**[^c-7b5d7a042518ce0817ac09a2777b8262] In Article 343(1), after “the interim receiver” insert “ or ”.

**14.**[^c-d1ef6843d962c42584d0065763f15b8b] Omit Article 348(1A).

**15.**[^c-a0af8a991f116d2430d59f693528270a] Omit Article 348A.

**Not yet in force.**

**16.** Omit Article 361A(2).

**17.**[^c-b3775545f17d66b5bb5b0df55c29994a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**18.**[^c-3c47fbf1b9851d0e62254d04219d5a9b] In Schedule B1, in paragraph 1A, for “outside Northern Ireland” substitute “ outside the United Kingdom ”.

**19.**[^c-642583efc53861dbe6ec525f9c8ccbac] In Schedule 1, in paragraph 3, at the beginning insert “ Without prejudice to Article 28 or 30 of the Property (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, ”.
