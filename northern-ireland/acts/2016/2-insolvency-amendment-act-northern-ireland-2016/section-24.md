## 24. Company arrangement or administration provision to apply to a credit union[^c-df6bb692442226399495e7ad38a33a93]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 10(2) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (societies to whom a company arrangement or administration provision may apply) at the end add “ or the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985. ”.
