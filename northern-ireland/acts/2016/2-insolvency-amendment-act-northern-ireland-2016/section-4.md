# Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Requirements in relation to meetings under Articles 81 and 84 of the Insolvency Order

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Articles 81(2)(b)(i) and 84(1)(b)(i) of the Insolvency Order (notice of meeting of creditors), the words “by post” are repealed.
