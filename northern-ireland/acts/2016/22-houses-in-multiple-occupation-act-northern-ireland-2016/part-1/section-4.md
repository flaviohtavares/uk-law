# Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 4

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Persons who are members of the same household[^c-db38c0fc126a89693f3061ad4b456962]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Act persons are members of the same household only if—

- (a) they are members of the same family (as defined in section 88),
- (b) they are to be treated as being members of the same household by virtue of subsections (2) and (3), or
- (c) their circumstances are of a description set out in regulations made by the Department (as to which, see subsections (4) and (5)).

(2) Subsection (3) applies if—

- (a) a person (A) is engaged to provide domestic help or personal care to another person (B), and
- (b) the terms of that engagement require A to occupy the same living accommodation as B.

(3) A is to be treated as being a member of the same household as—

- (a) B, and
- (b) any member of B's family who also occupies that living accommodation.

(4) Regulations under subsection (1)(c) may, in particular, provide that a group of persons are to be regarded as being members of the same household only where each member of the group has a prescribed relationship, or at least one of a number of prescribed relationships, to any one or more of the others.

(5) In subsection (4) “**prescribed relationship**” means any relationship of a description set out in the regulations.
