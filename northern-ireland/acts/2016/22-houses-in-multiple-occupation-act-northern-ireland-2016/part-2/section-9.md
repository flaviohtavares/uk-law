## 9. Breach of planning control[^key-b2e3b3607a2ece0d02fe77df35bb81b0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 8(2)(a) (refusal of licence for breach of planning control), “**breach of planning control**” has the meaning given by section 131 of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

(2) For provisions under which a decision to refuse a licence under section 8(2)(a) is treated differently from a refusal on other grounds, see—

- (a) paragraphs 5 to 7 of Schedule 2 (procedure on refusal of application);
- (b) section 25 (restriction on making applications after refusal);
- (c) section 67(1)(d) (appeals).
