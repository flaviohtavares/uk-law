## 27. Surrender of HMO licence[^c-330297941884f3f38709bfda834d7c0a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The holder of an HMO licence may surrender the licence by giving notice to the council to that effect.

(2) Notice under subsection (1) is to be in such form as the council may specify by general notice.
