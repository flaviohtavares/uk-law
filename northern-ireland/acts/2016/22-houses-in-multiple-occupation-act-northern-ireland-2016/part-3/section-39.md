## 39. Revocations and disqualifications: appeals[^key-9941527f9b76b747364138b50f722b9f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person may appeal against a revocation order or disqualification order in the same manner as the convicted person may appeal against sentence.

(2) The court which made a revocation order or disqualification order may suspend its effect pending such an appeal.
