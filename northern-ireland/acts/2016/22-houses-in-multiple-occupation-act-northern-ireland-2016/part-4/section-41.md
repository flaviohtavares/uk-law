# Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 41

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Definition of overcrowding[^c-7581da25339f518179cc8dcdbe263977]

*Extent: N.I.*

An HMO is overcrowded for the purposes of this Chapter when the number of persons who sleep in it is such as to contravene—

- (a) the room standard (set out in section 42), or
- (b) the space standard (set out in section 43).
