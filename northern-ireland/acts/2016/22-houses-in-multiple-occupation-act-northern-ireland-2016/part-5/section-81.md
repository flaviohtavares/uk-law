# Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 81

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Application by person required to take action where consent withheld[^key-60df4bdfed5a1a350ec80d58ee1d71f7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a person is required, by a notice served under this Act, to take any action in relation to any premises,
- (b) the consent of another person is required for the action to be taken or to enter any premises in order for it to be taken,
- (c) that person has, in response to a written request for consent, unreasonably refused to give it or has failed to respond to the request.

(2) A court of summary jurisdiction may, on the application of the person required to take the action, make an order—

- (a) declaring that the consent has been unreasonably withheld, and
- (b) granting the necessary consent.
