# Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 90

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Sections 84 to 86, this section and section 91 come into operation on the day on which this Act receives Royal Assent.

(2) The other provisions of this Act come into operation on such day as the Department may by order appoint.
