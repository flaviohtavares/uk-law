# Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 70

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Powers to require information and documents: introductory[^key-122f29cfd8908a92d5507fa382b88b44]

*Extent: N.I.*

The powers conferred by sections71, 72 and 73 are exercisable by a council for the purposes of—

- (a) enabling or assisting the council to exercise any function conferred on it by this Act in relation to any premises;
- (b) investigating whether any offence has been committed under this Act in relation to any premises.
