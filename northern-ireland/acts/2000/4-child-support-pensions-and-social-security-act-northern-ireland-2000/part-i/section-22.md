# Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2017-07-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nia/2000/4

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Abolition of the child maintenance bonus[^c21164061]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 4 of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (NI 13) (the child maintenance bonus) shall cease to have effect.
