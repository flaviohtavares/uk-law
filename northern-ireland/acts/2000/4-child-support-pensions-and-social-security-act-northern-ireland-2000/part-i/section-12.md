# Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 12

## 12. Information required by the Department[^c21163781]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 16(1) of the Child Support Order (information required by the Department), after such an application, there shall be inserted “ (or application treated as made), or needed for the making of any decision or in connection with the imposition of any condition or requirement under this Order,”.
