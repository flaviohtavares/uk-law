## 43. Information to be given to the Authority

*Extent: N.I.*

*Subs. (1)(2) rep. by 2005 NI 1*

(3) In paragraph (2) of Article 115 of that Order (powers to provide for Articles 22 to 26 not to apply in the case of certain schemes), for Articles 22 to 26 there shall be substituted “some or all of the provisions of Articles 22 to 26C”.

*Subs. (4) rep. by 2005 NI 1*

(5) In section 173(b) of the Pension Schemes Act (managers of schemes), at the end there shall be added “or Articles 22 to 26C of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995”.
