Harbours (Grants and Loans Limit) Act (Northern Ireland) 2021

2021 CHAPTER 1

An Act to amend the Harbours Act (Northern Ireland) 1970 to increase the statutory limit on certain grants and loans for harbour works etc.

\[1st March 2021\]

BE IT ENACTED by being passed by the Northern Ireland Assembly and assented to by Her Majesty as follows:

## 1. Increase of limit on grants and loans for harbour works, etc.

(1) In section 4 of the Harbours Act (Northern Ireland) 1970 (limit on aggregate of loans and grants under sections 2 and 3 of that Act) for “£35 million” substitute “ £90 million ”.

(2) Article 4 of the Financial Provisions (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (increase of limit on grants and loans for harbour works) is repealed.

## 2. Commencement

This Act comes into operation on the day after this Act receives Royal Assent.

## 3. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Harbours (Grants and Loans Limit) Act (Northern Ireland) 2021.
