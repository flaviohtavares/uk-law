## SCHEDULE 1 — Minor and consequential amendments

Section 45

### Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996

**1.**[^key-3ae8d734db2985fd6ee47998cc1d55f9] The Licensing Order is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-5a4fb41d739727a9045d132e437bc82e] (1)[^key-15f07b30e53e6dd91db83fb29d24701d] Article 2 (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a)[^key-50ca28b1db8ea10afbe595f353e2f3b3] omit the definition of “children’s certificate”,
- (b)[^key-50ca28b1db8ea10afbe595f353e2f3b3][^key-e66ec0224c5b3632405d67f78dad7bb0] in the definition of “licensed premises”, in the full-out words at the end, after “Articles” insert “52C, 52D, 52E,”,
- (c)[^key-cd08d1e43fefb3c5d8c130b65467ff87] at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “major event order” means an order under Article 48A;

  ,
- (d)[^key-15f07b30e53e6dd91db83fb29d24701d] in the definition of “permitted hours”, after “44,” insert “44A”, and
- (e)[^key-cd08d1e43fefb3c5d8c130b65467ff87] in that definition, for “and 47” substitute “, 47 and 48A”.

(3)[^key-cd08d1e43fefb3c5d8c130b65467ff87] In paragraph (4), for “(l)” substitute “(m)”.

(4)[^key-15f07b30e53e6dd91db83fb29d24701d] After paragraph (10) insert—

> (11) In this Order (other than Part 4A and Article 77A(3)) any reference to a district commander for a police district includes a reference to any other member of the Police Service of Northern Ireland nominated by the district commander.

.

**3.**[^key-3c3bdba9f8848da58e85eae7dbddbc2e][^key-cfd33621959beaf63773d0d4115cdc40] In Article 5 (premises for which a licence may be granted), in paragraph (3)—

- (a) for “Article 51(1)(b)” substitute “Articles 48B(2) to (6), 51(1)(b) and 52C(1)”,
- (b) for “(l)” substitute “(m)”, and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b), at the beginning insert “except in the case of premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1)(m),”.

**4.**[^key-07b9d39e9045d9be50234ab74e368921][^key-52913fafd56f6fd4d78dc16cc010bdbb] In Article 15(2)(e) (renewal of licence), for “(l)” substitute “(m)”.

**5.**[^key-2d265c5a72c82c964b803042b2bb4c7a][^key-3c3cc5e57925bb2f777555ba67222255] In Article 17(3) (renewal of licence: certain provisions not to apply), after “44” insert “, 44A”.

**6.**[^key-5966e42b855b4b9d04f4765f3afe250a][^key-8d0abcefeec48eb4e988ed492abcf6b0] In Article 22(6)(c)(ii) (transfer of licence), for “(l)” substitute “(m)”.

**7.**[^key-bb40f1305749eea250a4656176aea651] (1)[^key-bb40f1305749eea250a4656176aea651] Article 34 (register of licences) is amended as follows.

- (a)[^key-95848ac51c041059981face4255b4427] in sub-paragraph (f), after “44”, insert “, 44A”, and
- (b)[^key-75eed1e56ba7d93d4fea0f3db4d255b8] omit sub-paragraph (g).

**8.**[^key-af2dfb92b2bd2a0eb4e938c398e4ff28][^key-cfdff21d5faf75a142b947b9c0a2ae0e] In Article 58(1)(b) (prohibition on young persons in premises which sell intoxicating liquor for consumption off the premises), at the end insert “in which intoxicating liquor is made available for purchase”.

**9.**[^key-f1fc5859c4b5b2bb215cf2f3b8033ead][^key-a1846da535f877e3d28b0a3d504680be] In Article 69F (consideration of closure order by court of summary jurisdiction), in paragraph (2)—

- (b) in sub-paragraph (c)(ii), after “Article 44(2)” insert “or Article 44A(3) or (4) (as the case may be)”.

**10.**[^key-c43c5a77fe9617d6a48e48bada8ea6ff][^key-407ec5eb0a3f4ce58bffcf5cdf73dd84] In Article 76D (young persons prohibited from certain pavement café areas), omit paragraphs (3) and (4).

**11.**[^key-7fc16639be79677fcac2e974948f0b59] In Article 77A (conditions and licences for indoor arenas and outdoor stadia), in paragraph (3), after “the indoor arena” insert “or outdoor stadium”.

**12.**[^key-8c319aac4b6bca8b148c8242c2f97568] In Schedule 1 (application for certain orders at same time as application for licence), in paragraph 7—

### Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996

**14.**[^key-0eae26e13af262ab6192ff5a3fe044f5] The Registration of Clubs Order is amended as follows.

**15.**[^key-4393d779ca51d22cf6d2f48d171eb9b8] (1)[^key-5e59801de9d991baa28eba51fa2b433b] Article 2 (interpretation) is amended as follows.

> (5) In this Order (other than Part 4A) any reference to a district commander for a police district includes a reference to any other member of the Police Service of Northern Ireland nominated by the district commander.

.

**16.**[^key-8058bbb38a7581a7815722a3bb0a920e] In Article 16(5) (alteration of register of clubs), for “Department” substitute “Department of Justice”.

**18.**[^key-ce050025f9e898ba9c7078b48708641d][^key-d0bb54bd0616498c5086ad2662d58db8] In Article 52(1) (modifications of Schedules), for “, 4 and 5” substitute “and 4”.
