# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 8

> Text as at 2023-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Licensed race tracks: Sunday sales[^c-dcaa813ea3b3f439cb62bd73c22ee49f][^c-6b0774c2b997cd15a4695aed57dd71b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 42 of the Licensing Order (permitted hours), in paragraph (3), after “a theatre” insert “or (subject to paragraph (4)) premises on a licensed track”.

(2) After that paragraph insert—

> (4) The permitted hours for premises on a licensed track do not include any time on Christmas Day.
>
> (5) In this Article, “licensed track” has the meaning given in the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.
