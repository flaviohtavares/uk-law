# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 40

> Text as at 2023-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Restrictions relating to advertisements[^c-abc3c8b7752acf4f84894607d6b70312][^c-18308ba5902a093e6ae393dcce655c33]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 38 of the Registration of Clubs Order (restrictions on advertisements), in paragraph (1), for the words from “no person shall” to “the premises of a registered club” substitute “any advertisement drawing attention to any function to be held in the premises of a registered club must include a clear statement to the effect that the function may be attended only by members of the club and guests of members of the club;”.

(2) For paragraph (2) of that Article substitute—

> (2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an advertisement in so far as it relates to a function the whole proceeds of which are, after deduction of the expenses of the function, to be devoted to charitable or benevolent purposes.

.
