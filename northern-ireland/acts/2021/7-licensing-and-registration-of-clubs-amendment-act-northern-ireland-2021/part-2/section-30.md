# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 30

> Text as at 2023-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Removal of additional restrictions at Easter[^key-f60c914efc171db284f3a1674dd2085b][^key-c8588a24b99f52f894d76f108fa08c94]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 24 of the Registration of Clubs Order (general permitted hours), in paragraph (1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “Good Friday or”, and
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (b) and the following “and”.

(2) In paragraph (2) of that Article—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i), omit “Good Friday or”,
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (a)(ii), and
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (b) and the following “and”.

(3) In Article 25 of the Registration of Clubs Order (consumption of liquor after permitted hours), omit sub-paragraph (b) and the preceding “or”.

(4) In Article 26 of the Registration of Clubs Order (authorisations for special occasions), in paragraph (3), omit “, Easter Day or Good Friday”.
