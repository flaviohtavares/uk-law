# Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 3A

## 3A. Provision of health care outside Northern Ireland[^key-d8abe2e070061e9543b8a7c1ceea6ba3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may provide or secure the provision of health care mentioned in Article 5(1), 6(1), 7(1), 8(1) or 10(1) of the Order of 1972 (hospital etc. accommodation and health care services, including diagnostic services, after-care and ancillary services) outside Northern Ireland.
