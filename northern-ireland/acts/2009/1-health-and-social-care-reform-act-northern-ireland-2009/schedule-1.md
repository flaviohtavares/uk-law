### Sub-committees

**8.**[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158] (1) The Regional Board or a committee may appoint a sub-committee to consider and report to the Regional Board or, as the case may be, the committee on any matter within the competence of the Regional Board or the committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

(2) References in this Schedule to a sub-committee are to a sub-committee appointed under this paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

(3) A sub-committee may include persons who are not members of the Regional Board or the committee which appoints the sub-committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

### Proceedings

**9.** Without prejudice to section 19(1)(a)(v) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33), the Regional Board shall make standing orders regulating the procedure of the Board, its committees and sub-committees, including provision regulating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

- (a) the convening of meetings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
- (b) the fixing of the quorum; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
- (c) the conduct of business at meetings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

### Validity of proceedings

**10.** The proceedings of the Regional Board or of any committee or sub-committee are not invalidated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

- (a) by any vacancy in the membership of the Regional Board or the committee or sub-committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
- (b) by any defect in the appointment of any of its members; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
- (c) by any failure to comply with paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

### Disclosure of pecuniary, etc., interests and related provisions

**11.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), sections 28 to 33 and 146 of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 (c. 9) and section 148 of that Act so far as it applies for the interpretation of those sections, shall apply to the Regional Board, a committee or sub-committee and to a member of the Regional Board, a committee or sub-committee as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

- (a) in those sections— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158] any reference to a councillor were a reference to a member of the Regional Board, a committee or sub-committee, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158] any reference to the clerk of the council were a reference to the chief officer of the Regional Board, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]

- (b) in section 28(4) of that Act the words “or 46” were omitted and for the words from “by any local elector” onwards there were substituted the words “by any person.”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-95f0952c51c313adfe73cc2b0d456158]
