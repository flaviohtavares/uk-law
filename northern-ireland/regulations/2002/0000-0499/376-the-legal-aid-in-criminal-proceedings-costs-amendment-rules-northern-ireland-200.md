# The Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 376

LEGAL AID, ADVICE AND ASSISTANCE

The Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

3rd December 2002

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation

30th December 2002

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 36(3) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice and the Attorney General and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Rules:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2002, and shall come into operation on 30th December 2002.

## 2. Alteration of date

In rules 6(3) and 9(5)(a) of the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] for the date “31st December 2002” there shall be substituted the date “31st December 2003”.

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 3rd December 2002

We approve

Phillip Woolas

Jim Fitzpatrick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 5th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Rules.)

These Rules amend the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992 so as to alter the date after which certain work may be remunerated at discretionary instead of prescribed rates from 31st December 2002 to 31st December 2003.
