# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 146 (C. 14)

POLICE

The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002

Made

28th March 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002.

**2.** The provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 15th April 2002.

John Reid

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

28th March 2002
