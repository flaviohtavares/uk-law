# Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 54

LANDLORD AND TENANT

Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

15th February 2002

Coming into operation

4th March 2002

The Department for Social Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 33(2) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00002], and now exerciseable by it[^f00003], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, having complied with the requirements of Article 33(1) of that Order and determined that the rents hereinafter mentioned should be increased, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002, and shall come into operation on 4th March 2002.

## 2. Increase of registered rents

The rents registered under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 for dwelling-houses which are let under regulated tenancies shall be increased by 3 percent.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 15th February 2002.

D. M. Crothers

A Senior Officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

The Order increases the rents registered with the Department for Social Development under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, for dwelling houses, which are let under regulated tenancies, by 3 percent from 4th March 2002. Under Article 22(4) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 a notice of increase of rent which gives effect to an increase made by virtue of this Order shall not take effect earlier than 4 weeks after the commencement of this Order, i.e., not earlier than 1st April 2002.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks' notice of the increase in accordance with Article 22(3)(*b*) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978.
