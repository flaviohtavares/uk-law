# Game Preservation (Amendment) (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 130 (C. 10)

GAME

Game Preservation (Amendment) (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

1st April 2002

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 4(2) of the Game Preservation (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Game Preservation (Amendment) (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 1 and 2 of the Game Preservation (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 is 1st April 2002.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 28th March 2002.

Dermot Nesbitt

Minister for the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings sections 1 and 2 of the Game Preservation (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 (“the Act”) into operation on 1st April 2002.
