# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 62

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

22nd February 2002

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation

8th April 2002

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 7(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 8th April 2002.

## 2. Revocation

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Amendment to Schedule 2

For the table set out in Schedule 2 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following table—

> | Disposable Income Range | Maximum Contribution |
> | --- | --- |
> | Exceeding £80 but not exceeding £87 a week | £7 |
> | Exceeding £87 but not exceeding £94 a week | £14 |
> | Exceeding £94 but not exceeding £101 a week | £21 |
> | Exceeding £101 but not exceeding £108 a week | £28 |
> | Exceeding £108 but not exceeding £115 a week | £35 |
> | Exceeding £115 but not exceeding £122 a week | £42 |
> | Exceeding £122 but not exceeding £129 a week | £49 |
> | Exceeding £129 but not exceeding £136 a week | £56 |
> | Exceeding £136 but not exceeding £143 a week | £63 |
> | Exceeding £143 but not exceeding £150 a week | £70 |
> | Exceeding £150 but not exceeding £157 a week | £77 |
> | Exceeding £157 but not exceeding £164 a week | £84 |
> | Exceeding £164 but not exceeding £171 a week | £91 |
> | Exceeding £171 but not exceeding £178 a week | £98 |
> | Exceeding £178 but not exceeding £185 a week | £105 |
> | Exceeding £185 but not exceeding £189 a week | £107 |

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 22nd February, 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 so as to substitute a new scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance under Article 7(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
