# Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 148 (C. 15)

CRIMINAL INJURIES

Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2002

Made

31st March 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2002.

**2.** Articles 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of the Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 shall come into operation on 9th April 2002

Desmond Browne

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

31st March 2002

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order brings into operation the provisions of the Criminal Injuries Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 which are necessary for the Secretary of State to make the Scheme for the payment of criminal injuries compensation under the Order.
