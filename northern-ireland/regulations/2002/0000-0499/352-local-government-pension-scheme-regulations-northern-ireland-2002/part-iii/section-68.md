# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 68

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 68. Elections as to use of accumulated value of AVCs[^key-9c541409e22626f2c7a91479512efe9d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (8), this regulation applies where a person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) leaves his employment with the employer who was his employing authority when he made an election under regulation 62(1) or (11) without entitlement to the immediate payment of retirement benefits; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) stops being an active member without leaving that employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) leaves his employment with the employer who was his employing authority when he made an election under regulation 62(1) or (11) with entitlement to the immediate payment of retirement benefits – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) under regulation 26 (normal retirement) or 28 (redundancy etc); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] by virtue of an election under regulation 33 (early payment); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) becomes entitled to an ill-health pension under regulation 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Subject to paragraph (7), a person must elect to have the accumulated value used – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) where paragraph (1)(a) applies to him, in one or more of the permissible ways; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) where paragraph (1)(b) applies to him, in the way mentioned in paragraph (3)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The permissible ways are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to subscribe to an occupational pension scheme (other than the Scheme); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to subscribe to a personal pension scheme (including an additional voluntary contributions scheme, other than an FSAVC scheme); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) to subscribe to a self-employed pension arrangement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) to purchase an appropriate policy from one or more AVC insurers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where paragraph (1)(c) or (d) applies to a person, he may elect for the accumulated value to be used to provide additional pension for him under the Scheme, or partly to provide such pension for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where a member makes an election under paragraph (4), he becomes entitled to such additional pension as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6)[^key-5359b9c545c6944de00e6f1d70a33c80] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Where a person who has stopped being employed by an employing authority or being a member receives – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^key-acd12760c2e73c4d0c6bd4c651a5dad8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a repayment of contributions under regulation 88; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a payment under regulation 89(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he must immediately be paid the accumulated value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8)[^key-4ab4f3ab14f8a1cdffb8565008c25b99] A person who made an election under regulation C24(1) of the 2000 Regulations prior to the commencement date shall continue to have the rights to make elections as to the use of the accumulated value as under the provisions of those Regulations as in operation prior to that date and accordingly, so far as is necessary to give effect to those rights and to make provision for any matters incidental to them, those provisions shall be treated as if they had continued in effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(9) In this regulation, “the accumulated value” means the accumulated value of the additional contributions invested under regulation 66(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
