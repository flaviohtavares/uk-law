# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 62

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Elections to pay AVCs[^key-af86d5d32818b0a3b74705a246163443]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] An active member may elect to pay contributions under this regulation (“AVCs”) in addition to any other contributions he may pay under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The election must specify the percentage of his remuneration he wishes to pay or the amount he wishes to pay on his usual pay days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2A)[^key-062188cbc0c90dc5888352d7e75a7061] An election may be made in respect of each employment in respect of which he is a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2B) The maximum a person may specify under paragraph (2) in respect of each employment for which he makes such an election is 50 per cent of the remuneration of that employment or an amount equal to 50 per cent of the remuneration of that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The election[^key-64bfb25daf3cd0a9aa47b95bf05d37b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] must also specify whether he wishes any of his AVCs to be used to provide benefits payable on his death (“death benefits”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) If he does, he must specify the proportion to be so used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The Committee may require the amount of the AVCs to be at least the specified minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) The Committee may not do so after AVCs are first paid under the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The specified minimum is the amount specified in regulation 2(8) of the Pension Schemes (Voluntary Contributions Requirements and Voluntary and Compulsory Membership) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00024].

(8) A member may elect to vary the amount of his AVCs or the proportion of them to be used to provide death benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) A member may elect to stop paying AVCs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) An election under this regulation must be made by notice in writing to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) An active member may elect to transfer into his additional voluntary contributions scheme constituted under this Chapter the accumulated value of any other additional voluntary contributions scheme to which he has subscribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
