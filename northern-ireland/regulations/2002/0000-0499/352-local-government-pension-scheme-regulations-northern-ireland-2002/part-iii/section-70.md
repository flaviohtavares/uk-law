# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 70

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Applications to pay SCAVCs[^c-aba36dc3f83b1f28f6c74fe5f0be94dd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If an active member whose employing authority has established arrangements for SCAVCs under regulation 69 wishes to pay SCAVCs he must apply to it in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The employing authority must notify the member in writing before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with its receipt of his application whether it has accepted or rejected it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A notification of acceptance must specify the percentage of the member’s pay which the employing authority will pay in contributions under the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) It must also specify whether any and, if so, what proportion of the contributions is to be used to provide death benefits and the nature of any such benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) A member may elect to stop paying SCAVCs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) The election must be made by notice in writing to his employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
