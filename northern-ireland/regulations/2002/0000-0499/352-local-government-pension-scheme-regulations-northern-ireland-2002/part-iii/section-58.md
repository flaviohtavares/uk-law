# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 58

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 58. Part-time employees[^c-e3d1f6d4d89851ce914addcc08baaeff]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a person in a part-time employment elects under regulation 57, the periods mentioned in that regulation must be reduced in the proportion his contractual hours bear to the number of contractual hours of a single comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But the amounts of his additional contributions must be calculated as a percentage of his actual pay (subject to paragraphs (7) and (8) of regulation 57). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Where any person has made such an election, if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his employment ceases to be part-time and becomes whole-time employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) his employment ceases to be whole-time and becomes part-time employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) his contractual hours in the part-time employment alter, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

his additional contributions continue to be payable at the same percentage of his pay, but the additional period counted by reason of contributions paid after the change must be calculated as if the change had occurred immediately before the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Paragraphs (1) to (3) do not apply to old elections. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) But the member may elect for paragraph (2) to apply to an old election and, if he does so, the additional period counted by reason of contributions paid after that election must be calculated as if paragraphs (1) to (3) had always applied as respects his old election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) A member’s election under paragraph (5) must be made by giving notice in writing to the Committee not later than one year before his normal retirement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) Old elections are elections made before 1st August 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
