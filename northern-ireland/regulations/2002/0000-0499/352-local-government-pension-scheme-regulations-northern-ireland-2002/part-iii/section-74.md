# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 74

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Termination[^c-e30b6be2bad7d353fb9cb9c144c3f428]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a member who is paying SCAVCs – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) leaves his employment and does not enter new employment in which he is an active member; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) stops being an active member without leaving that employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

regulation 68 applies as respects the elections he must or may make for the use of the accumulated value of the invested additional contributions specified in regulation 66(2) (as it applies by virtue of regulation 72(3)) as it would apply to a person in his circumstances as respects the accumulated value mentioned in regulation 68. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where neither paragraph (1)(a) nor (b) applies and an employing authority or a member stops paying SCAVCs (otherwise than by reason of the member having left his employment and entered new employment in which he is a member), the employing authority must give notice to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
