# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 63

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 63. Payment of AVCs[^c-ae7f7c0564a365376af19b05cb102c08]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] AVCs must normally be payable by an active member on his usual pay day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] and must be paid from his pay[^c-bcb4b271b99af63968a8fef7e9c320c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(2) No contributions may be paid to cover any period during which the person contributing is not an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A person may not pay AVCs after he leaves his employment with the employer who was his employing authority when he elected under regulation 62(1) to pay them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But he may do so if his last usual pay day with that employer falls after that time (or if he makes a fresh election in relation to another employment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
