# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 54

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Power of employing authority to increase total membership ...[^key-205a28926fb4c460dad36fd575ffb91f][^key-6ce0efbafbcc28ec3269f063c074885c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] An employing authority may resolve to increase the total membership of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] an active member[^key-1a6f309af6e1fae7e1bad7f8d572e8ad] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(2)[^key-fc550780ee7ba8d54841bdbb2f39bade] A member’s total additional membership (including additional membership in respect of different employments) must not exceed – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) 10 years[^key-eb66b7e63eb4ecd79c394ece87434237] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]; or[^key-0264898f5298f2b96ce4e6b6bcb0e6c3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b)[^key-09f39c7542437763cfe303e200b11522] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the period by which the member’s total membership falls short of the total membership the member will have if he continues as an active member until he is 65, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

whichever is the shortest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3)[^key-7bb858183adf9b3d22b0a1a3949f6267] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-7bb858183adf9b3d22b0a1a3949f6267] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-7bb858183adf9b3d22b0a1a3949f6267] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) The relevant additional period may only be counted as a period of membership if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the Committee and the employing authority agree before the expiry of the relevant period that the employing authority will pay increased contributions under regulation 79 to meet the cost of the increase in membership; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the employing authority makes the payment required by regulation 80(1) by reason of the resolution within that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The relevant period is the period of one month beginning – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) with the date on which the resolution was passed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b)[^key-e30162353968a35aa87706e0bf0de20e] such longer period as the employing authority and the Committee agree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) If neither paragraph (6)(a) nor (6)(b) applies, the resolution shall cease to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) If a person has been credited with a period of service under regulation 7, or has been paid compensation under regulation 31 of the Local Government (Discretionary Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00022] or regulation 7 of the Local Government (Early Termination of Employment) (Discretionary Compensation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^key-5ff4ea114ef7c388e19102ea55fe5a78] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] in respect of a cessation of employment, no resolution may be passed under this regulation by reason of that cessation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) If a person becomes entitled on leaving an employment to an ill-health pension under regulation 29 calculated by reference to an enhanced membership period, no resolution may be passed under this regulation by reason of his leaving that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11)[^key-3cd2e785bb21604426de4341cbf286cc] An additional period arising from a resolution under this regulation after 31 March 2008 shall be treated as a period of membership after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
