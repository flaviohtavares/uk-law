# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 64

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64. Functions of employing authorities[^c-916fdaa144a37d7fc88adcc3a65b2a4e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] An employing authority must send any notice of election to pay or to cease paying AVCs to the Committee as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The employing authority must make the arrangements necessary so as to enable a member to begin paying AVCs before the expiry of the period of two months (or such longer period as the Committee may allow) beginning with the date he elects to pay them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The employing authority must make the arrangements necessary to enable a member who has elected to vary his AVCs or to stop paying them to do so before the expiry of the period of two months (or such longer period as the Committee may allow) beginning with the date he so elects. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
