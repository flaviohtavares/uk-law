# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 73

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Changes of employment in which membership is continued[^key-27eefba2f465dffb59edf737a253587b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member who is paying SCAVCs leaves his employment and enters a new employment in which he is also a member, he may elect to have the accumulated value of the invested additional contributions specified in regulation 66(2) (as it applies by virtue of regulation 72(3)) used – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to make a contribution to the arrangements the new employing authority has made under this Chapter for AVCs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) his new employing authority has established arrangements under this Chapter for the payment of SCAVCs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he has made an application to contribute under those arrangements which has been accepted, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  to make a contribution to the new employer’s SCAVCs arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Such an election must be made by notice in writing to the member’s new employing authority and may only be made if the member enters the new employment before the expiry of the period of one month and one day beginning with the date he left the former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The new employing authority must send a copy of any election under this regulation to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where an election is made under paragraph (1)(a), it must specify – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) whether the member wishes the election to be treated as an election under regulation 62(1) in respect of the member’s new employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if he does, the matters which require to be specified in such an election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where the election is made under paragraph (1)(a), the Committee must apply and invest the sum received as mentioned in regulation 66, together with any additional contributions falling to be so invested under that regulation by virtue of contributions made in respect of the new employment by virtue of any election which is treated as made under paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
