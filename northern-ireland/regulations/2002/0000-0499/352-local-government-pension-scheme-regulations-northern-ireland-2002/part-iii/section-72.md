# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 72

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Application and investment of SCAVCs[^c-e835a87e19468e0b7c2f7a07c5497305]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where the arrangements established by an employing authority provide for any of the SCAVCs to be used to provide death benefits, the Committee must make such arrangements for the provision of those benefits as are specified in regulation 65(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The Committee must invest any SCAVCs which are not to be used to provide death benefits with an approved AVC body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Regulations 65(2) to (4) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] 66(2) to (4)[^c-d7be219749b878ff720edc850690c6b6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] apply as respects SCAVCs as they apply as respects AVCs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
