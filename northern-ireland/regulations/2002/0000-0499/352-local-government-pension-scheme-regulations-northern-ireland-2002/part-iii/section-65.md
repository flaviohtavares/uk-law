# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 65

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 65. Death benefits[^c-10f8117d1870f0bafeecdf6bdc977d95]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member elects for any of his AVCs to be used to provide death benefits, the Committee must make arrangements for those benefits to be provided under a pension policy with an AVC insurer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The policy must provide for the Committee to pay the AVC insurer the same amounts as the AVCs to be so used within one month after the member’s usual pay day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The policy must reflect the restrictions on AVCs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , the provisions which apply under these Regulations and must meet the conditions imposed under section 167 of the Finance Act 2004[^c-4333169344c33a790c4b63cef20041f9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(4) In entering into the pension policy the Committee must give effect to the member’s wishes about the benefits it provides, so far as is practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
