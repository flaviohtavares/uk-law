# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 57

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Payments to increase total membership[^c-8bea57758c48678840623dc04c6a3fb7][^c-1409e60b5d5d4691e715e9a8a5032de7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] An active member may elect to make additional contributions to the Scheme to increase his total membership by an additional period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2)[^c-7006d8fec34fe4f22742b295de8f623a] That period must not exceed 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]<sup>243</sup>/<sub>365</sub> years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(3) The election must be made by giving notice in writing to the Committee earlier than the member’s 64th birthday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(4) If – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) the Committee passes a resolution requiring a member to satisfy it that he is in good health by producing to it a report at his own expense provided by an independent registered medical practitioner approved by the Committee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) it is not so satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

the election is void. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(5) The amounts of the additional contributions must be such percentage of the member’s pay for the time being as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(6) A member’s pay for the time being is the pay received by him for the interval at the end of which the additional contribution falls to be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(7) Where a member is away from work (otherwise than because of illness or injury) with reduced or no pay, for paragraph (6) he is treated as having received the pay he would have received if he had not been away (unless his contract of employment has ceased). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(8) For paragraph (6) any reduction in pay by reason of the actual or assumed enjoyment of any statutory entitlement during any period in which the member is away from work (other than a period of maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] , paternity or adoption[^c-8ba5271a74d716365644f742b07252b1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] absence) shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(9)[^c-c876e7bd8895e9d178fed9f2fe21e183] If a member continues paying additional contributions until his last birthday before his normal retirement date (or if his normal retirement date is his birthday, that date), the whole of the additional period may be counted as part of his total membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(10) Otherwise, the part of that period which may be so counted must be calculated as specified in regulation 84 (discontinuance of additional contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(11) The additional contributions are payable from the member’s next birthday after his election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
