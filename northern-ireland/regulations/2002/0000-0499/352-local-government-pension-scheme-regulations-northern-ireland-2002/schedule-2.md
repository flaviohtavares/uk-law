# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — MATTERS TO BE INCLUDED IN AN ADMISSION AGREEMENT IN CERTAIN CASES[^key-5407fcede7b05543a194e6b8d699e92b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 4A(8)

**1.** A requirement for the transferee admission body to pay to the Committee all contributions and payments due under the Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**2.** If required by regulation 4A(6), a reference to the indemnity or bond in accordance with regulation 4A(7) and a warranty from the transferee admission body that such an indemnity or bond is in place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**3.** A provision requiring the transferee admission body to adopt the practices and procedures relating to the operation of the Scheme set out in the Regulations and in any employer’s guide published by the Committee and provided to the transferee admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**4.**[^key-59c30e128455976a5eac0391dc976adf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**5.** A representation and warranty from the transferee admission body to the Committee that all the transferee admission body’s employees or class of employees who are specified as members are employed in connection with the provision of the service or assets being carried out by the transferee admission body as a result of those matters referred to in 4A(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**6.** An undertaking from the transferee admission body that it will promptly notify the Committee in writing of any material change in the terms and conditions of employment which affect entitlement to benefits under the Scheme for its employees who are members and of any terminations of employment by virtue of redundancy or in the interests of efficiency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**7.** A requirement that the transferee admission body notifies the Committee of each occasion on which it exercises a discretion under the Regulations and the manner in which it exercises that discretion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**8.** A requirement that the transferee admission body notifies the Committee of any matter which may affect, or is likely to affect, its participation in the Scheme and that it gives immediate notice of any actual or proposed change in its status which may give rise to a termination, including take-over, reconstruction or amalgamation, liquidation or receivership and a change in the nature of its business or constitution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**9.** A minimum period of three months' notice to terminate the admission agreement but automatic termination, as required by regulation 4B(2), in the event that the transferee admission body ceases to be such. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**10.** A right for the Committee to terminate the agreement in the event of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the insolvency, winding up or liquidation of the transferee admission body, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a breach by the transferee admission body of any of its obligations under the admission agreement (but where the breach is capable of remedy only where it has not been remedied within a reasonable time), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the withdrawal of approval by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue to the participation of the transferee admission body as a Scheme employer, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a failure by the transferee admission body to pay any sums due to the fund within a reasonable period after receipt of a notice from the Committee requiring it to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**11.** A requirement that the admission agreement in its final form shall be available for public inspection at the appropriate offices of the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

**12.** In relation to a transferee admission body under regulation 4A(2)(a) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a reference to the date of the contract, other arrangement or direction by which the body met the requirements of that regulation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a provision whereby the Scheme employer may set off against any payments due to the transferee admission body an amount equal to any overdue employer and employee contributions and other payments (and interest payable under the Regulations) due from the transferee admission body as an employing authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a provision requiring the Scheme employer to keep under assessment the level of risk arising as a result of the matters set out in regulation 4A(6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a provision that where a representation or notification must be given to the Committee under paragraphs 5, 6, 7 and 8 of this Schedule, that representation or notification is also given to the Scheme employer, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) a requirement that the admission agreement in its final form shall be available for public inspection at the appropriate offices of the Scheme employer, if different from the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
