## 2. Interpretation[^key-1e45d7d8d75840bdf6661f13b5e91a9e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Schedule 1 contains definitions of expressions used in these Regulations which apply for their interpretation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
