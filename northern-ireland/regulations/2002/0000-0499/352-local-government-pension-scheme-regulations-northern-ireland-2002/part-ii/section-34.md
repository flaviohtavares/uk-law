# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 34

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Re-employed and rejoining deferred members[^c-c0733216462363bf7e8c1de481cf8977]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a deferred member becomes an active member again before becoming entitled to the immediate payment of retirement benefits in respect of his former membership, he may elect to have his former membership aggregated with his membership on or after the date he becomes an active member again. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(1A)[^c-f35ca4da5e224afa3b7dea21060178e8] A member cannot elect to aggregate any period of former membership which he could have elected to aggregate with another period of former membership but did not before the expiry of the period in paragraph (7A). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2)[^c-9b1ed20f19e94b6d7c16a09f4035ad16] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where a member elects under paragraph (1) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) he ceases to be entitled to rights under the Scheme in respect of his former membership (except in so far as he is entitled by virtue of having become an active member again to rights in respect of the aggregated total membership); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he ceases to count as a deferred member for these Regulations as respects his former membership (unless he becomes a deferred member again after ceasing to be an active member). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where an election under paragraph (1) is made by a member who has ceased to be an active member more than once, the election may be made as respects his total membership at each of the times he so ceased or only as respects such of those periods of membership as are specified in the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where a member who may elect under paragraph (1) does not do so or does not elect as respects all periods of his membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in applying regulations 19(2) and (3), 30(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] and 31[^c-ed780bc19f4909f132ceeea987df58c9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] as respects any later membership, his total membership excludes unaggregated periods; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b)[^c-a108bbd3e3a3cdadf05d0c086344ede6] for the purpose only of calculating – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) whether the member has the necessary total membership referred to in regulation 18(1)(a) to be entitled to benefits under this Chapter and regulation 30(1) to be entitled to calculate his ill-health pension and grant by reference to the enhanced membership period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] total membership in regulation 54 (power of employing authority to increase total membership), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  the unaggregated periods of membership shall be taken into account, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) as respects each unaggregated period of his former membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) paragraph (3) does not apply, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he shall continue to be treated as a deferred member or, as the case may be, as a pensioner member (and not as an active member), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he shall be entitled to the same rights as if he were not also an active member (but subject to regulation 37 and, in the case of a pensioner member, to regulation 31). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) For this regulation a period of membership is an unaggregated period if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) no previous election has been made under this regulation for its aggregation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a period as respects which the member was entitled to elect under regulation D12(1)(c) of the 2000 Regulations (elections to remain entitled to preserved benefits) or any previous corresponding provision of the Scheme, such an election was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) An election under paragraph (1) must be made by notice in writing to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7A)[^c-ee101ee574b4b0c6764550add3676b5f] The notice under paragraph (7) must be given before the expiry of the period of 12 months beginning with the date that he became an active member again (or such longer period as the Committee may allow). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) References in this regulation to former membership include all membership which the member was entitled to count as total membership immediately before he ceased his former active membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) Where a person ceases to be an active member in one employment and immediately becomes an active member in another employment, for paragraph (1) of this regulation he shall be treated as if he were a deferred member as respects the first employment, despite never having ceased to be an active member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10)[^c-5e592d521182faa27fa031d5a407d5d6] In the case of a member who first becomes a member on or after 2nd April 2001 any period of membership in the employment of a community admission body within – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) regulation 4(2)(a)(ii), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) regulation 4(2)(b) where, at the date the admission agreement is made, the contributions paid to the body by any one or more Scheme employers equal in total 50% or less of the total amount it receives from all sources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

shall not be aggregated with any other periods of membership for the purpose of calculating his retirement grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
