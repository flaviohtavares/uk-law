## 39. Guaranteed minimum pensions etc.[^c-c112878fde8d57c15e3866239d4a7cd9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a member’s local government employment is contracted-out employment and he has a guaranteed minimum in relation to service before 6th April 1997, from the date he attains state pensionable age he is entitled to a pension at a weekly rate equal to not less than that guaranteed minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But if the member attains state pensionable age while in local government employment, he is not so entitled until he leaves that employment, unless paragraph (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) If the member – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) continues in the same employment for a further period of five years after attaining state pensionable age; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) does not then leave it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he is entitled from the end of that period to so much of his retirement pension as equals that guaranteed minimum (unless he consents to a postponement of the entitlement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) For paragraph (1), a person has a guaranteed minimum if he has such a minimum under section 10 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 in relation to benefits under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) A person’s retirement pension is not to be reduced under regulation 33(4) to less than the aggregate of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any minimum rate of equivalent pension benefits applicable under the Insurance Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) one eightieth of his final pay (expressed as an annual rate), multiplied by the length in years of the whole period of his membership in contracted-out employment during the period beginning with the relevant date and ending with 30th April 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) In paragraph (5) “the relevant date” means – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a man, 17th May 1990; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a woman, 6th April 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) regulation 51 (commutation); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
