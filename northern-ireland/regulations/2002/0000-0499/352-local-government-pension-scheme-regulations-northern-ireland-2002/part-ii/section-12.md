## 12. Meaning of “pay”[^key-163cd2eaf5c363af696755f31860963c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] An employee’s pay is the total of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) all the salary, wages, fees and other payments paid to him for his own use in respect of his employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any other payment or benefit specified in his contract of employment as being a pensionable emolument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But an employee’s pay does not include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) payments for non-contractual overtime; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any travelling, subsistence or other allowance paid in respect of expenses incurred in relation to the employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any payment in consideration of loss of holidays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) any payment in lieu of notice to terminate his contract of employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) any payment as an inducement not to terminate his employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (f) any amount treated as the money value to the employee of the provision of a motor vehicle or any amount paid in lieu of such provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) For regulation 11, the pay of a part-time employee for any period is the pay he would have received if during that period he had worked the contractual hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But paragraph (3) does not apply to periods during which the employee was away from work by reason of illness or injury with reduced or no pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
