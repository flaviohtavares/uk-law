# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 22

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Final pay[^c-d5a4680a1877abf63ab5b1d1f978d757]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A member’s final pay for an employment is his pay for as much of the final pay period as he is entitled to count as active membership in local government employment (but see paragraphs (3) to (11), regulations 23 and 24(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-370a08af9cf28c8ce1eada304062a7f0]...).

(2) A member’s final pay period is the year ending with the day on which he stops being an active member (but see paragraph (10) and regulations 23 and 24). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3)[^c-43fd7baf31bbdc20e2ea360f13b8be67] For part-time employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a qualifying member subject to regulation 19(10) to (13); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a newly qualifying member subject to regulation 19(10) to (14); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) in the case of the Agreement No. 2 qualifying member subject to regulation 19(10) to 19(13) and 19(15); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

the final pay is the pay which would have been paid for a single comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But in calculating death grant or the rate of surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , civil partner's[^c-1f1d745fd70d467fbdb9f7683b9fbac2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or children’s short-term pension payable on the death of an active member, actual pay in part-time employment is to be used, or, in calculating death grant, three eightieths of final pay multiplied by total membership if greater. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Any reduction or suspension of a member’s pay during the final pay period because of his absence from work owing to illness or injury must be disregarded for this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) If a member’s final pay period includes reserve forces service leave, his final pay is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in a case where he has paid contributions by virtue of regulation 16(5), the amount it would have been if his reserve forces pay were pay received in his former local government employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) otherwise, the amount it would have been if he had continued to be employed in his former employment during the period of that leave. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) For the purposes of this Chapter, a member’s pay for any period of maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption[^c-83496100904da5eb1ab681be355f6fd8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] absence during the final pay period in respect of which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he[^c-52a40548ffc84a542d83dd06bfbcd9e4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] pays or is treated as paying contributions is the pay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] would have received had *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] not been absent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) If a member is absent from work for any other reason during his final pay period, he is only to be treated for this Chapter as having received the pay he would otherwise have received if he has made the appropriate contributions under Chapter III for the period he is absent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9)[^c-eb5a76c4b20dc5a9518be0c87e539592] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(10) If a member is only entitled to count part of the year specified in paragraph (2) as a period of active membership in relation to the employment which he ceases to hold, his final pay is his pay during that part multiplied by 365 and divided by the number of days in that part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) Final pay does not include any pension in payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
