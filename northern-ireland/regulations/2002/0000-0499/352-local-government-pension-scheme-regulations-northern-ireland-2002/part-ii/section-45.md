# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 45

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s guaranteed minimum pension[^c-081fa4d1237bee72cb331bd89e86457f][^c-db6aba20e75c8105547e0410acc096d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If the guaranteed minimum pension rule applies, the pension to which a person is entitled under regulation 42 or 43 must be not less than the surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s guaranteed minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The guaranteed minimum pension rule applies if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the employment in which the deceased was a member was contracted-out employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he had a guaranteed minimum in relation to benefits under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Paragraph (1) overrides any contrary provision in these Regulations except– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) regulation 51 (commutation of small pensions); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) regulation 114 (forfeiture); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) regulation 115 (interim payments directions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
