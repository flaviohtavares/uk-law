# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 7

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Leaving the Scheme[^c-d045a5c7688468acb9a8f8468474846a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A person stops being a member if he ceases to be eligible for membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A person may leave the Scheme if he wishes but must notify his employer accordingly in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A member who gives such a notification stops being a member from the date the notification specifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But, if a date earlier than the notification or no date is specified, he stops being a member at the end of the payment period during which the notification is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where notification is given by a person before he has been a member for three months, he must be treated as not having been a member in that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
