# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Length of period of membership: calculation of benefit[^c-d301d3a3e335195825d4e73a2af8edf7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] In calculating the amount of any benefit, fractions of years of membership count. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The numerator of such fractions is the number of complete days of membership and 365 is the denominator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Membership in part-time service is counted as the appropriate fraction of the duration of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4)[^c-96ea4647f482454c6a6c7b5ef9b277c8] The numerator of that fraction is the number of contractual hours during the part-time service and, subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] paragraphs (4A), (4B) and (4C)[^c-d55e485cbfb2183714b6ea75f093dc8c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], its denominator is the number of contractual hours of that employment if it were a whole-time basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4A) In the case of a qualifying member, the denominator is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) for the calculation of any benefits for any period between 1 January 1995 and 30th November 2007, 32.5 hours; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) for the calculation of any benefits for any period thereafter, the number of contractual hours of that employment if it were on a whole-time basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4B) In the case of a newly qualifying member paragraph (4A) applies as if for “30th November 2007” there was substituted “6th February 2009”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4C)[^c-cc6560faeedfea9be568927fa7c9d272] In the case of the Agreement No. 2 qualifying member, the denominator is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) for the calculation of any benefits for the period between 1st January 1995 and 27th March 2015, 32.5 hours; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) for the calculation of any benefits for the period thereafter, the number of contractual hours of that employment if it were on a whole-time basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Paragraph (3) does not apply in determining a member’s total membership for regulation 30(1) (entitlement to ill-health enhancement) (and see regulation 30(6) to (10) as to the determination of the enhanced membership period in such cases). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6)[^c-3a183cffa59a4a997cd41cafd25bc1e0] In this regulation and regulation 19— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- “the Agreement” means the Collective Agreement for the Joint Negotiating Council of the Education and Library Boards dated 30th November 2007; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “the Agreement No. 2” means the Collective Agreement for the Joint Negotiating Council of the Education and Library Boards reached on 27th March 2015;[^c-8a20d95c99bd6755adfb73f25db5884d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “the Agreement No. 2 qualifying member” means a member who was party to the Agreement No. 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “newly qualifying member” means a member who is employed as a classroom assistant by a voluntary grammar school or a grant maintained integrated school within the meaning of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “qualifying member” means a member who was party to the agreement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
