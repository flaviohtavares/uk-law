# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 16

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Obligatory contributions during absences[^key-ebade0ad997a66386318281e99984ee7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) is away from his employment with permission (otherwise than because of illness or injury) for a continuous period of less than 31 days or away on jury service for any period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) is receiving reduced pay or no pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he must make contributions at the standard contribution rate on the pay he would have received during that period but for his absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member goes on maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption[^key-05d2d3e6d5752af3b7803b7b0e9d21ad] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] leave, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he[^key-11fdc5df86acf1c9c85f67f0c188c1a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] must make contributions at the standard contribution rate, as respects any part of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] his *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] period of maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] absence for which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] is a member and entitled to receive pay (including any statutory maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] pay payable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to him[^key-20e9f467c5064fb8168a80f39adbdd78] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00012], on that pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) That pay includes any such statutory pay but not any amount by which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] his[^key-016d0d652c8eee083b93eab7460621e0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] actual pay is reduced on account of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] his *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] possible entitlement to such statutory pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) goes on ordinary maternity leave *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , ordinary adoption leave or paternity leave[^key-c9ea7f1106bf05ccc0e0d896fa6f6d83] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) is not entitled to receive pay (including statutory maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] pay payable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to him[^key-e6010d63b65dee7ebc78597a9a5949cf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (Northern Ireland) 1992) for all or any part of that period of leave, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

for these Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he[^key-332762ff531bb443f7b725b5e4c6723a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] shall be treated as if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] had paid contributions under paragraph (2) for the unpaid period of that ordinary maternity leave *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , ordinary adoption leave or paternity leave *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] and on the pay that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] would have received during that period but for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] his *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member goes on reserve forces service leave, he must pay contributions under regulation 11 and any payments under Chapter III of Part III which he was paying immediately before his relevant reserve forces service began, if (and only if) during that service his reserve forces pay equals or exceeds the pay he would have received if he had continued to be employed in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Those contributions continue to be payable to the fund at the same rates on that pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) If he is not obliged to pay contributions under paragraph (5), for these Regulations he is treated as if he had paid them and also any payments under Chapter III of Part III which he would have been liable to pay if he had continued to be employed in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member goes on reserve forces service leave – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) he may continue to pay any contributions under Chapter IV of Part III (AVCs and SCAVCs) which he was paying immediately before his leave began; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he may opt to discontinue payment under that Chapter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) unless he has opted, the Committee must continue throughout the period of his relevant reserve forces service leave to pay any such contributions which were to be used to provide benefits for him on his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member goes on reserve forces service leave – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his relevant reserve forces service counts as a period of membership in his former employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if during that service, he dies, attains his normal retirement age or becomes incapable for health reasons of working efficiently in local government employment, he shall be treated as if he were in that employment at that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
