# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 28

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Redundancy etc.[^key-26d30e44ea68a037e97f45a0ffa2a778]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a member who is aged 50 or more retires from a local government employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) his employing authority certifies the reason for his retirement was his redundancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he is entitled to a pension and retirement grant which are payable immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) In paragraph (1) “redundancy” includes retirement in the interests of efficiency, or because the member held a joint appointment which has been ended because the other holder has left it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
