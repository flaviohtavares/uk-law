# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 48

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 48. Children’s long-term pensions[^key-bd92a27e4e587b37d30276fbaa8b102e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member dies leaving one or more eligible children, they are entitled to a children’s long-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If the deceased was an active member or pensioner member, it is payable from the end of the period for which the short-term spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^key-0263f133744c90293a756860ab30eda2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] and short-term children’s pensions are payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The pension payable on the death of a deferred member is payable from the day after his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) An eligible child ceases to be entitled to a long-term pension when he ceases to be a child within regulation 46(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If the deceased was an active member, the pension is the appropriate fraction of the pension to which he would have been entitled if on the date of death he had become entitled under regulation 29(1) (ill-health). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) If the deceased was a deferred member, the pension is the appropriate fraction of the amount of the pension to which he would have been entitled if on the date of death he had become entitled under regulation 26 (normal retirement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) If the deceased was a pensioner member, the pension is the appropriate fraction of his retirement pension disregarding – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any increase made under regulation 57(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b)[^key-10985ef1121e8180a487fbd2ef401460] any reduction made under regulation 19(3A) or regulation 33, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any loss of pension as a result of an election made under regulation 31. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) But in the case of a member who was a pension debit member, the pension is calculated by reference to the pension to which the deceased would have been entitled had his shareable rights not been subject to a pension debit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) If, apart from this paragraph, the calculation of the long-term pension would be based on a member’s retirement pension calculated on the basis of membership of less than – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) 10 years; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the period he would have been entitled to count if his active membership had continued until his 65th birthday, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(whichever is the shorter), then that period should be used instead in calculating it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) The appropriate fractions are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) where there is one eligible child – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) if a surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^key-aaeb6e3bb54733a43c98a25c65e4ca63] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] long-term pension is payable, one quarter, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] otherwise one-third; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) where there is more than one eligible child – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) if a surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^key-a2474ab9dd0f0e3808759abed1137ab4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] long-term pension is payable, one half, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] otherwise two-thirds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) If a child in full-time training for a trade, profession or vocation is receiving pay at an annual rate exceeding the training rate – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the pension is reduced by the excess; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if the pension would be greater without the child, he need not be counted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(12) In paragraph (11) “the training rate” means the current annual rate of an official pension which began to be paid on 1st April 1994 at an annual rate of £1,450. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
