# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 20

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Pension debit member[^key-008124fe8382610a9931e19fac4d5a5b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The benefits payable to a pension debit member shall be reduced to take into account the debit to which the shareable rights of the pension debit member are subject under a pension sharing order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The amount of the reduction shall be calculated in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
