# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 42

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s short term pension[^key-e3ee9c14ecc3f87490ba993e4450bdb1][^key-840fad944c7447e3a74fdfe2aa82692a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If an active or pensioner member dies leaving a surviving spouse or civil partner, the spouse or civil partner is entitled to a short-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) It is payable for three months after the member’s death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) But if there are eligible children in the spouse’s or civil partner’s care, it is payable for a further three months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where the deceased was an active member, the annual rate of short-term pension is equal to the deceased’s final pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where the deceased was a pensioner member, the annual rate of short-term pension is equal to his retirement pension immediately before the death (but see regulation 44). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) In this regulation “pensioner member” includes a person whose retirement pension has been commuted under regulation 52 (exceptional ill-health). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) If there is more than one surviving spouse, they become jointly entitled under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
