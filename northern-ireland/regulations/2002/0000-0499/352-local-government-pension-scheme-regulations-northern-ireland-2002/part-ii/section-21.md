# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 21

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Replacement of rights[^c-a9195874b862df1c826eb52f15c4514b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Subject to paragraph (2), a pension debit member who is an active member may not replace any rights debited as a consequence of a pension sharing order with any rights which the member would not have been able to acquire (in addition to the debited rights) had the pension sharing order not been made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00013].

(2) The provisions of paragraph (1) shall not apply in circumstances which the Commissioners of Inland Revenue may stipulate in relation to “moderate earners” in any taxation exception or concession made by them from time to time under paragraph 18(10) and (11) of Schedule 10 to the Finance Act 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00014], or otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) For the purpose of this regulation, “moderate earners” has the meaning in paragraphs (4) to (6) of regulation 5 of the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Sharing of Pensions on Divorce) Regulations 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00015].
