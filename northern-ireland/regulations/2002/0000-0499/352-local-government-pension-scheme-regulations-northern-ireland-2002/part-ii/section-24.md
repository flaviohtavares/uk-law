# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 24

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Permanent reductions in pay: certificates of protection of pension benefits[^key-dd7b92d3d3908c5da6ea8ad31b7fa6b1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a certificate has been issued as respects a member’s pay under paragraph (3) or (5) and the date of reduction or, as the case may be, restriction specified in the certificate is not more than 10 years before the date on which he ceases to be an active member, he may elect that his final pay period should be – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a year ending with a day – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) falling within the period of five years ending with the last day he was an active member, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of which that last day is the anniversary; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any three consecutive years – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) falling within the period of 13 years ending with the last day he was an active member, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] ending with a day of which that last day is the anniversary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where a member elects for the period specified in paragraph (1)(b), his final pay is the annual average of his pay during that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) If, otherwise than by virtue of a member’s own circumstances – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his rate of pay is reduced; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the rate at which it may be increased is restricted in such a way that it is likely that the rate of his retirement pension will be adversely affected, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he is entitled to be issued with a certificate to this effect by his employing authority (but see paragraph (5)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A member is not entitled to be issued with a certificate under this regulation if the reduction in his rate of pay – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) is temporary; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) consists in the termination of, or a reduction in, a temporary increase in the rate of pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The employing authority may issue a certificate without an application from the member, but need not issue a certificate if he does not apply for one within 12 months after the date of reduction or restriction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) A certificate issued under this regulation must specify the date of the reduction or restriction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The employing authority must send a copy of the certificate to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) The employing authority must keep a record of the certificate including such information as would be necessary for applying paragraph (1) for the period of 10 years beginning with the date of reduction or restriction specified in it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) An election under this regulation by a member must be made by notice in writing given to the Committee before the expiry of the period of one month, or such longer period as the Committee may allow, beginning with the date on which he is notified of his entitlement to a benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) Where a member has died without having made an election under this regulation, the Committee may make an election on his behalf (whether or not the period within which he could have elected has expired). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
