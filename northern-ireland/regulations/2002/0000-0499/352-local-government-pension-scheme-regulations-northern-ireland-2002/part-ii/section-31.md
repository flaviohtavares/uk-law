# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 31

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Re-employed pensioners[^key-8057aade6e66c310bf7ea8f6bae86602]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a pensioner member becomes an active member again, his former membership shall be taken into account only for the purpose of calculating whether he has the necessary total membership referred to in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) regulation 18(1)(a) to be entitled to benefits under this Chapter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) regulation 30(1) to be entitled to calculate his ill-health pension and grant by reference to the enhanced membership period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where a member to whom benefits are paid in accordance with regulation 38(1A) continues to accrue membership after the date on which payment of such benefits takes effect (“the payment date”), his membership prior to the payment date shall only be taken into account after the payment date for the purpose of making the calculations referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
