# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 11

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Members' contributions[^c-ebe7dd207b554ac8062cdc9da948ea36]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Each member shall make contributions to the Scheme at the standard contribution rate on his pay in each employment in which he is an active member *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-7ff039f5dad15c7d62ae69903b68fa2d]....

(2) The standard contribution rate for a member is six per cent., unless he is a member with lower rate rights in accordance with regulation 13(2) or (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The standard contribution rate for a member with lower rate rights is five per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A person who is an active member in more than one employment must make contributions for each of those employments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The Committee may decide the intervals at which the contributions are made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) For this regulation any reduction in pay by reason of the actual or assumed enjoyment by the member of any statutory entitlement during any period away from work shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) Regulations 16 and 17[^c-92cf7a6b026e8fda8e3aceed50d819d0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] affect this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
