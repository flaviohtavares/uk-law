# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 10A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.

## 10A. High earners[^c-06420d35b80a79796e0ba640bf927cde]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] This regulation applies to a member who was subject to regulation 15(1) before 6th April 2006 and whose pay (calculated in accordance with regulation 12) in the year ending 5th April 2006 exceeds £105,600. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) In such a case, his period of membership for calculating his benefits payable is obtained by aggregating his period of membership after that date with the period obtained by applying the formula set out in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) That formula is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- M is his period of membership after 5th April 1989 and before 6th April 2006 (excluding any membership credited by virtue of a transfer under regulation 124 (inward transfer of pension rights)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- P is his pay as mentioned in paragraph (1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- T is the aggregate of his period of membership before 6th April 1989 and any membership credited by virtue of a transfer under regulation 124. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
