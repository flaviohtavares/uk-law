# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 47

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. Children’s short-term pensions[^c-0522005724695378825d7c871816a587]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If an active member or pensioner member dies leaving one or more eligible children, they are entitled to a children’s short-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If a short-term pension is payable to a surviving spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner[^c-5b06ecf8689806a4eaa9a3338962b147] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] under regulation 42, the children’s short-term pension is payable only for three months after the death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Otherwise, it is payable for six months after the death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where the deceased was an active member, the annual rate of children’s short-term pension is equal to the deceased’s final pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where the deceased was a pensioner member, the annual rate of children’s short-term pension is equal to his retirement pension immediately before the death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) But in the case of a pensioner member who was a pension debit member, the pension is calculated by reference to the retirement pension to which the deceased would have been entitled had his shareable rights not been subject to a pension debit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) If a short-term pension is payable to a surviving spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner[^c-1debca7f06ac7e913487cc70dd98c540] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] under regulation 42, the children’s short-term pension is reduced by that pension, if one or more of the children are in the care of the surviving spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(8) Where, if a children’s long-term pension and a surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^c-c5d6f868632c0cabab35826351772ebc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] long-term pension were payable instead of short-term pensions, that would result in a greater aggregate pension, long-term pensions shall be payable instead of short-term pensions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) Where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a children’s short-term pension is payable for one or more children who are not in the care of a person to whom a short-term pension is payable (or would be payable apart from paragraph (8)); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a children’s long-term pension would be greater, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

the long-term pension shall be payable instead of the short-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) In this regulation “pensioner member” includes a person whose retirement pension has been commuted under regulation 52 (exceptional ill-health). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
