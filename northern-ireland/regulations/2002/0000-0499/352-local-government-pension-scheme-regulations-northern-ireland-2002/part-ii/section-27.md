# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 27

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Retirement after the normal retirement date[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^c-73d6c309d3aae01df686e7dcd73d2a2b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member who remains in service after his 65th birthday is entitled to the immediate payment of a pension and retirement grant when he retires from service or, if earlier, on the day before his 75th birthday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A member is not entitled to count any period of service as a period of membership on or after the day before his 75th birthday and is not an active member after that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
