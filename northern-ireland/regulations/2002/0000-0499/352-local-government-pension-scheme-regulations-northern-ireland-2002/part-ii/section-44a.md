# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 44A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44A. Calculation of pension for surviving civil partner[^key-12f5f0dce2e621284c3bd7dc4dee58cc]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purpose of calculating the pension to which a surviving civil partner is entitled under regulation 42 or 43 (surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s short-term or long-term pension) account shall only be taken of the deceased member’s membership after 5th April 1988. This shall include relevant additional membership (as defined in regulation 44(4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
