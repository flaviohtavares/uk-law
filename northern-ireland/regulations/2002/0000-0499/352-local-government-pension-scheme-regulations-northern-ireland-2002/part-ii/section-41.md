## 41. Death grants[^c-8de7b8aa26dc5d9a94cc5022d789abc6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] If a member dies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] before his 75th birthday[^c-1441c18411c9ee5d23c29231cde1b505] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b], the Committee at its absolute discretion may make payments to or for the benefit of the member’s nominee or personal representatives or any person appearing to the Committee to have been his relative or dependant at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2) The aggregate amount paid under paragraph (1) must not exceed the member’s death grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(3) The multiplier for an active member’s death grant is 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(4) The multiplier for a deferred member’s death grant is the same as for his retirement grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(5) The multiplier for a pensioner member’s death grant is 5, but the amount so calculated is reduced by the amounts of any retirement pension paid to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(6) The multiplier for the death grant of a member who remains in service after his 65th birthday as referred to in regulation 27 is whichever of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) 2, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- gives the greater amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(8) For these Regulations, any payments made under paragraph (1) must be treated as payments made by way of death grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
