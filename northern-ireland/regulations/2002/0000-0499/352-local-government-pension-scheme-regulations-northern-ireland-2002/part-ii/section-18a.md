# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 18A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18A. Limit on total amount of benefits[^c-69d96512e699f333ec9d4815c7bfd2b1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A member and any dependant of his shall not be entitled, under any provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] except as provided in regulation 52(1) (commutation: exceptional ill-health)[^c-657911ec0fb5454e95f6b7341d45106d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], to receive benefits the capital value of which exceeds his lifetime allowance, increased where applicable by his primary protection or enhanced protection, except in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) In this regulation, “lifetime allowance”, “primary protection” and “enhanced protection” are to be construed in accordance with section 218 of, and Schedule 36 to, the Finance Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Any calculation of the capital value of a member’s benefits is to be carried out in accordance guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
