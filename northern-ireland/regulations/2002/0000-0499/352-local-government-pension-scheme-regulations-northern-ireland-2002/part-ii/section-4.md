# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Employees of non-Scheme employers: community admission bodies[^c-e584f8ba8f06b4308b300f7372c5c37d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Subject to the requirements of this regulation and regulation 4B, the Committee may make an admission agreement with any community admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The following are community admission bodies – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a body which provides a public service in the United Kingdom otherwise than for the purposes of gain and which either – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) has sufficient links with a Scheme employer for the body and the Scheme employer to be regarded as having a community of interest, whether because the operations of the body are dependent on the operations of the Scheme employer or otherwise, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] is approved by the Department for the purpose of admission to the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a body to the funds of which any Scheme employer contributes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a body representative – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) of local authorities, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of local authorities and officers of local authorities, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of officers of local authorities which is formed for the purpose of consultation as to the common interests of local authorities, and the discussion of matters relating to local government, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of Scheme employers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a statutory undertaker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) a non-statutory undertaker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (f) the managers of a voluntary school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or grant maintained integrated school[^c-a68af584d5e0c68609da5e6786d9a8a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (g) the governing body of an institution of further education within the meaning of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Approval under paragraph (2)(a)(ii) may be subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit and it may withdraw approval at any time if such conditions are not met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where, at the date the admission agreement is made with a body referred to in paragraph (2)(b), the contributions paid to the body by any one or more Scheme employers equal in total 50% or less of the total amount it receives from all sources, it must be a term of the admission agreement that the Scheme employer who provides funding (and, if more than one, all of them) guarantees the liability of the body to pay all amounts due from it under the Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) In this regulation – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) “a non-statutory undertaker” means a body who, though not authorised by any statutory provision to do so, is primarily engaged in carrying on – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock, harbour or pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) “a statutory undertaker” means a body authorised by any statutory provision to carry on – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock, harbour, or pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
