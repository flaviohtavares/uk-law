# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 49

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Discretions as to payment of children’s pensions[^key-2386b05ae70d080ec6db286669c87972]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a children’s pension is payable for more than one eligible child, the Committee may apportion it amongst the children as it thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The Committee may pay the whole or part of a children’s pension to a person other than an eligible child, to be applied for the benefit of one or more eligible children as it directs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
