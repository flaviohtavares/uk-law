# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 8

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Periods of membership: “total membership”[^key-b9625b423f08c9a1e26cbe7a461b696f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The following periods count as periods of membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any period for which a member has paid (or is treated as having paid) contributions under regulation 11, 16 or 17 or 88(5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any period during which a member is away from work because of illness or injury; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any period which a member is entitled to count as membership under – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) Chapter II or III of Part III, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] regulation 125(1) (periods credited on payment of transfer values); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) in the case of a member who belonged to the Scheme before the commencement date, any period he is entitled to count under the Transitional Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) For most purposes a member’s “total membership” is the total of the periods he is entitled to count under paragraph (1), disregarding any period which would otherwise count twice (but see *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] regulations 9, 10, 10A, 31 and 34 and the Transitional Regulations[^key-df6a30bcaabb505232b023ed8fd7d9ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]).

(3) A person may not count any period of membership if his contributions for that period have been returned to him and, if all his contributions to the fund are returned to him, he may not count any period of membership credited to him on the receipt of a transfer value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A person may not count any period of membership if his rights in respect of it have been transferred to a non-local government scheme, a personal pension scheme, a self-employed pension arrangement, a retirement annuity contract or an appropriate policy by payment of a transfer value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Where a person pays contributions under regulation 17(3) (trade disputes) for any period, that period counts as a period within paragraph (1)(a) even if his contract of employment did not subsist throughout that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Periods of membership before and after any unpaid period of maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or adoption[^key-44951c5415e27a30118fbdc78aeb7537] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] absence or period of parental leave in respect of which the member does not pay any contributions shall be treated as continuous. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
