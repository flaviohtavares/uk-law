# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 26

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Normal retirement[^c-58f703380e326a51f0317d42aeba2832]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member who has attained his normal retirement age retires from a local government employment, he is entitled to a pension and retirement grant which are payable immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The normal retirement date of a member is his 65th birthday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3)[^c-491a4f60a053affb9cf323bf029e4ead] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A member’s normal retirement age is his age on his normal retirement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
