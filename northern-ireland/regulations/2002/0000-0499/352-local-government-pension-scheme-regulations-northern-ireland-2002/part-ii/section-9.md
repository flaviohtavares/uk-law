# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 9

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Excluded membership[^key-ae9488746c681545129a6ef5e9595e5b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] Superannuable membership does not count towards the membership period required before a person is entitled to any benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2) Superannuable membership is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) any period of added years; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) any additional period of membership which counts as such by virtue of Chapter II of Part III. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(3) The Table in Schedule 3 sets out other sorts of membership which do not count for some purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(4) The first column of that Table describes the membership and the second column specifies the purposes for which such membership does not count. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(5) That Table has effect subject to the notes at the end. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
