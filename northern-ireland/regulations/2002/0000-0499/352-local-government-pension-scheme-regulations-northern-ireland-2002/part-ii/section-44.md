# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 44

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Reduction of some surviving spouses' pensions[^key-40a1994cc98eb4a8edd6f76da4533c64]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] Where a male pensioner member or deferred member marries and dies, the pension to which his widow is entitled under regulation 42 or 43 is calculated as if his retirement pension were only so much of his actual pension as is attributable to the period of his membership in contracted-out employment after 5th April 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2) Except in the case of a short term pension payable to the widower of an active member, the pension to which a widower is entitled under regulation 42 or 43 is calculated as if the member’s retirement pension were only so much of her actual pension as is attributable to her membership after 5th April 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(3) But relevant additional membership also counts as membership after 5th April 1988, where the widower was married to the member at some time while she was in local government employment after 31st March 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(4) Relevant additional membership is membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) forming the enhanced element of a member’s total period of membership by virtue of a retirement to which regulation 30 applied after 5th April 1988; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) added by virtue of a resolution under Chapter II of Part III or regulation 130 or under the Transitional Regulations after that date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (c) counted by reason of a payment made under Chapter III of Part III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] [^key-924aba51e98d37b5ac3ee438e412e492]...;
- (d) treated as membership after 5th April 1988 by virtue of the 2000 Regulations; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (e) attributable to a transfer value accepted by the Scheme after 5th April 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
