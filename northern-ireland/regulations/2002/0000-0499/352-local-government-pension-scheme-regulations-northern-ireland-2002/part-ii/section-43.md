# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 43

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s long-term pension[^c-0b315c3389221d2263b0bccff9b9b124][^c-397e6c16abb02ecddd98b3fa8e62706e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member dies leaving a surviving spouse or civil partner, the spouse or civil partner is entitled to a spouse’s or civil partner’s long-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If the deceased was an active or pensioner member, the long-term pension is payable from the end of the period for which the short-term pension is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The long-term pension payable on a deferred member’s death is payable from the death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) If the deceased was an active member with a total membership of at least *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] three months[^c-c16a7d25203be649ca26ed526ce13622] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], the long-term pension is equal to half the ill-health pension to which the deceased would have been entitled under regulation 29 if he had become entitled to a pension under that regulation on the date he died. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If the deceased was an active member with a total membership of less than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] three months *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or a deferred member, the multiplier for the long-term pension is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(but see regulation 44). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) But where the deceased was a deferred member and a pension debit member, the long-term pension is equal to half the pension to which the deceased would have been entitled under regulation 26 if he had become entitled to a pension under that regulation on the date that he died (but see regulation 44). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) If the deceased was a pensioner member, the long-term pension is equal to half his retirement pension immediately before the date of death *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or half his pension before any commutation under regulation 19(3A), whichever is greater[^c-d424ee040247acc64203a18736551a95] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] (but see regulation 44). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) For the purposes of this regulation – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any increase in the pensioner member’s retirement pension made under regulation 57(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any reduction in that pension under regulation 33 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-6f14d9a0a0640ffcfa9c43a656398ba8]...; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any loss of that pension as a result of an election made under regulation 31, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) If there is more than one surviving spouse, they become jointly entitled under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
