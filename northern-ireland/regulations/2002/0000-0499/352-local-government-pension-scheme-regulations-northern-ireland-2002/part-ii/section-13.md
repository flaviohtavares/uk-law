# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 13

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Members with lower rate rights[^c-a11a629b0c8efc8b89d6f50f61c7d73c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A member has lower rate rights for regulation 11 if he falls within paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A member falls within this paragraph if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) immediately before the commencement date he was entitled to contribute to the Scheme at the rate of five per cent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) since that date either – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) he has remained in continuous employment in the same capacity and a member, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he falls within paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A member falls within this paragraph if he is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a continuously-employed manual worker who immediately before the commencement date was in a local government employment in which he was entitled to contribute to the Scheme at the rate of five per cent. by reason of being a manual worker; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a statutory transferee who was so entitled immediately before he left the Scheme on statutory transfer and has applied to his employing authority to continue to be so entitled before the expiry of the period of 30 days beginning with the date he rejoins the Scheme or such longer period as that authority may allow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A continuously-employed manual worker is a person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) who on or after the commencement date has been transferred from the employment he held immediately before that date to another local government employment which is described by his employer as a manual employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) who on or after that date has been transferred more than once and all of whose employments since that date have been local government employments which are so described. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) A statutory transferee is a person who – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) left the Scheme by reason of his employment being transferred by virtue of an enactment to a person who was not a Scheme employer; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) has rejoined the Scheme on a further transfer by virtue of an enactment to a person who is a Scheme employer and for whom he is employed in the same capacity as that in which he was employed immediately before he left the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) For this regulation whether a person’s employment is continuous must be determined in accordance with Chapter III of Part I of the Employments Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00011] but any period not exceeding one month and one day when he is not employed may be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
