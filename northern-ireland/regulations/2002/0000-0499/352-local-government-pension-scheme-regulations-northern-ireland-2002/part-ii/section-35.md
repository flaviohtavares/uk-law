# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 35

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Concurrent employments[^c-61cc5fdbe9196576ab90f0aa8536c9dd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) ceases to be an active member in one employment (“the first employment”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) continues as an active member in another employment he held concurrently with the first employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he may elect to have his former membership in respect of the first employment aggregated with his membership in that other employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If he so elects, the provisions of regulation 34 shall apply as if references to – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his former membership or former active membership were references to his membership from his first employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the new employment were references to his concurrent employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the employment in which he becomes an active member again were references to that concurrent employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) In the case of a person to whom this regulation applies, the period of membership which will be aggregated with his membership from the concurrent employment will be equal to his membership from his first employment, as reduced under regulation 10(4) if the first employment was part-time, multiplied by the fraction – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- the rate of pay in each case is the annual rate of pay on the last day of the first employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
