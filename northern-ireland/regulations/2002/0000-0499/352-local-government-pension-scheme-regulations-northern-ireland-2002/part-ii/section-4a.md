# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 4A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4A. Employees of non-Scheme employers: transferee admission bodies[^key-e584f8ba8f06b4308b300f7372c5c37d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Subject to the requirements of this regulation and regulation 4B, the Committee may make an admission agreement with any transferee admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A transferee admission body is a body other than a community admission body, that is providing, or will provide – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a service or assets in connection with the exercise of a function of a Scheme employer as a result of the transfer of the service or assets by means of a contract or other arrangement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a public service and which is approved by the Department for the purposes of admission to the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Only those employees of the transferee admission body who are employed in connection with the provision of the service or assets referred to in paragraph (2) are eligible to be members of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) In the case of an admission agreement with a transferee admission body under paragraph (2)(a) the Scheme employer, if it is not also the Committee, must be a party to the admission agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Approval under paragraph (2)(b) may be subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit and it may withdraw approval at any time if such conditions are not met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) An admission agreement with a transferee admission body shall require that: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a body under – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) paragraph (2)(a), the Scheme employer; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] paragraph (2)(b), the transferee admission body, to the satisfaction of the Committee, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  shall carry out an assessment, taking account of actuarial advice, of the level of risk arising on premature termination of the provision of the service or assets by reason of the insolvency, winding up or liquidation of the transferee admission body; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) that where the level of risk identified by the assessment is such as to require it, the transferee admission body shall enter into an indemnity or bond to meet the level of risk identified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The indemnity or bond must be with – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a person who has permission under Part IV of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to accept deposits or to effect and carry out contracts of general insurance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) an EEA firm of the kind mentioned in paragraph 5(b) and (d) of Schedule 3 to that Act, which has permission under paragraph 15 of that Schedule (as a result of qualifying for authorisation under paragraph 12 of that Schedule) to accept deposits or to effect and carry out contracts of general insurance; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a person who does not require permission under that Act to accept deposits, by way of business, in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) An admission agreement with a transferee admission body shall make provision for the relevant matters set out in Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) Where a transferee admission body undertakes to meet the relevant requirements of this regulation, and – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a body under paragraph (2)(a), the Scheme employer undertakes to meet the relevant requirements of this regulation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a body under paragraph (2)(b), the Department approves the body for admission to the Scheme and the conditions, if any, to which the approval is subject, have been met; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

the Committee must admit to the Scheme the eligible employees of the transferee admission body specified by the body and where it does so, the terms on which it does so are the admission agreement for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
