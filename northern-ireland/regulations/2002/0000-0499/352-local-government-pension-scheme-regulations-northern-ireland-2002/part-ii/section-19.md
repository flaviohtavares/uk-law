# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 19

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Calculations[^key-41ebd5809e618c67bc80e8a4236f7d57]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Subject to paragraphs (10) to (13)[^key-bee7d1b3c040d70b73b9d67a3fcf6de8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the amount of any benefit payable as a result of a person’s membership is generally calculated by multiplying his final pay by the appropriate multiplier. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Unless another multiplier is indicated, the appropriate multiplier for a pension is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Unless another multiplier is indicated, the appropriate multiplier for a retirement grant is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3A)[^key-bea0e300852f8aa9261dd4e2e64dd811] A member in respect of whom a benefit crystallisation event occurs after 5th April 2006 may elect in writing to the Committee before any benefits become payable to increase the retirement grant payable under paragraph (3) by commuting his pension, or a part thereof, at a rate of £12 for every £1 of annual pension entitlement surrendered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] but the entitlement to the benefit shall not arise until the date on which the Committee receives the information it requires to calculate the increase[^key-a849045e1343ece53435d42c77af3a68] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(3B) But the total amount of the member’s retirement grant, including any sum received— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in accordance with an election under paragraph (3A); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) as benefits provided in the form of a lump sum in accordance with regulation 66 (retirement benefits); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) as additional lump sum benefits in accordance with regulation 68 (elections as to use of accumulated AVCs) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

shall not exceed 25% of the capital value of his accrued rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3C) For the purposes of this regulation, a member’s accrued rights include rights accrued in respect of any payments made by him or on his behalf in accordance with Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] IV *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of Part III (Additional Voluntary Contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3D) Any calculation of the capital value of a person’s accrued rights shall be carried out in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3E) In paragraph (3A), “member” includes a deferred member to whom regulation 4 (deferred members and pensioners: general) of the Local Government Pension Scheme (Amendment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] 2 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3F) A member may make an election under paragraph (3A) in respect of his pension, or any part thereof, payable by virtue of an election under regulation 68 (elections as to use of accumulated value of AVCs) notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in the former provisions of regulation 68 treated as continuing to have effect under paragraph (8) of that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) benefits payable to a pension debit member are reduced as provided in regulation 20; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) benefits payable on or after the death of a pensioner member are calculated by multiplying the amount of his former retirement pension by the multiplier specified for the benefit in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4A)[^key-ccdf5162f200a57191e283fdbdd7986c] Where no benefit crystallisation event has occurred before the day after a member’s 65th birthday in respect of any benefits payable under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], those benefits shall be increased at such a rate as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Unless otherwise indicated, references to the amounts of pensions are to their annual rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) The amount of a deceased person’s former retirement pension is the amount of the pension he would have received immediately before his death, but for – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^key-8ce95f8e6a7da8a7e42e0dc020145349] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) regulation 33 (elections for early payment); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c)[^key-0696a5efe12d3585626198dff795df35] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) regulation 52 (commutation in cases of exceptional ill-health); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e)[^key-0c6892d49d75b06387b88ebf3ca1c29e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^key-0c6892d49d75b06387b88ebf3ca1c29e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) regulation 113 (application of abatement policy in individual cases). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) Periods are measured in years and fractions of a year (calculated as specified in regulation 10). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) A pensioner member includes a person who would have been entitled to a pension but for regulation 113 (application of abatement policy in individual cases). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) But paragraph (8) does not stop a person to whom it applies from also being an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10)[^key-9f12804696eeff52a9ae98c378a18391] In the case of a qualifying member, the amount of benefit payable in respect of any period between 1st January 1995 and 30th November 2007 is calculated by multiplying his interim pay by the appropriate multiplier. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) The appropriate multiplier is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] where “x” represents the qualifying member’s membership between 1st January 1995 and 30th November 2007 calculated in accordance with regulation 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(12) In the case of a qualifying member, the amount of benefit payable for any period between 1st January 1995 and 30th November 2007 is calculated in accordance with paragraph (1) and (2) but as if for the words “the member’s total membership” in paragraph (2) there were substituted “the member’s membership from 30th November 2007”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(13) In this regulation interim pay means the final pay that would have been for a single comparable employment with a weekly contract of 32.5 hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(14) In the case of a newly qualifying member, paragraphs (10) to (13) apply as if for each reference to “30th November 2007” there was substituted “6th February 2009”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(15)[^key-2b9aa020e449065caa3da5b021ce6b19] In the case of the Agreement No. 2 qualifying member, paragraphs (10) to (13) apply as if for each reference to “30th November 2007” there was substituted “27th March 2015” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
