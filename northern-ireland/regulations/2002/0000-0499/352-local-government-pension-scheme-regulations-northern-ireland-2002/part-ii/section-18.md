# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 18

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. General qualification for benefits[^key-18e9d21a61d462cccbfb1b6e9bbc7cb3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Membership of the Scheme only entitles the member to benefits under this Chapter if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his total membership is at least *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] three months[^key-9409b2331c08d1e5cbf3e8b5f1c449ea] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc];
- (b) a transfer value is credited to him; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) neither paragraph (a) nor (b) applies and he – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) has attained state pensionable age; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] will attain that age before the following 6th April. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But paragraph (1) does not apply – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to a benefit under regulation 26 (normal retirement) in a case where the member’s normal retirement date is his 65th birthday *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-f75b8ab07f40eb2d17e775cfc45335e8]...; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to benefits in respect of a member under regulation 41 (death grants), regulation 42 (surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^key-ca007867e002849832dd4235885232a1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] short-term pension), regulation 47 (children’s short-term pensions) or regulation 48 (children’s long-term pensions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
