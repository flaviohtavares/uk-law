- (b) the pay he would have received but for any trade dispute absence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
