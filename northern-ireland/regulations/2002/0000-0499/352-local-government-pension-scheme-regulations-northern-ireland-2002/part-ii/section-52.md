# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 52

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52. Commutation: exceptional ill-health[^key-daf05d6fc7470692c0b042ceef0e7fd3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If, when a retirement pension first becomes payable to a member, the Committee is satisfied that his life expectancy is less than one year, it may pay him a lump sum equal to five times the amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of his annual rate of retirement pension, notwithstanding that such lump sum may exceed his lifetime allowance[^key-6914c68ab832402c5ed3ffeb16a3767c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(1A)[^key-fc86682103227feef0bb5d887f89374a] The Committee cannot be satisfied as mentioned in paragraph (1) unless they have first obtained a certificate from a fully registered person within the meaning of the Medical Act 1983 to the effect that the member’s life expectancy is less than one year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Such a payment discharges the Committee’s liability for that pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-e54096b4008d7c8936af9770450fef6a]... and for any lump sum death grant calculated by reference to that pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-e54096b4008d7c8936af9770450fef6a]... under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The Committee must deduct from any such payment any tax for which it may become liable under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Part 9 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003[^key-b28e112a0ee0d417a9e7eb44d8235c23] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].
