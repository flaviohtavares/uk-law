## 18B. Deduction of tax[^c-69d96512e699f333ec9d4815c7bfd2b1]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Committee is responsible for deducting from any payments of benefits under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] any tax to which they may become chargeable under the Finance Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
