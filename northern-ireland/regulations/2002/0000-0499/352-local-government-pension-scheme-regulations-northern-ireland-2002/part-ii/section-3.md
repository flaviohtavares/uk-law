# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. General eligibility for membership: employees of Scheme employers etc.[^key-67bf3da886850ec089c463f4757fcb99]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A person may only be an active member if this regulation, or regulation 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , regulation 4A[^key-448780ace7dacedb92de6136ac0a8e2d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] enables him to be one and he is not prevented by regulation 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A person may be a member if he is employed by a Scheme employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A Scheme employer is a local authority as defined in Article 2 of the Order of 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00003].
