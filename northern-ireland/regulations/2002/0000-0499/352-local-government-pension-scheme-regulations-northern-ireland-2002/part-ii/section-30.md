# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 30

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Amounts of ill-health pension and grant[^key-5be09da2b161bdd5b9651a7ce1e5c345]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where the member’s total membership is at least 5 years, the multiplier for an ill-health pension or grant is by reference to the member’s enhanced membership period instead of his total membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A member’s enhanced membership period is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) if his total membership is less than 10 years, twice his total membership; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if his total membership is at least 10 years, but not more than 13 122/365 years, 20 years; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) otherwise, his total membership plus 6 243/365 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) But the enhanced membership period must not exceed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-b5314a840ade7f62e0a9e9147b8d0ef7]... the total membership the member would have had if he had continued as an active member until he was 65, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^M_F_65eaffc5-89ff-47e9-ad16-343fc908a4bb]....

(4)[^key-e0327cf6a7c55a79cb05435ee7f89451] Subject to paragraph (5), where a member becomes entitled to a further ill-health pension and grant (because of having been a member in two employments) the multiplier for the further ill-health pension and grant will be the total membership without enhancement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Paragraph (4) does not apply to a member who becomes entitled to a further ill-health pension and grant as a result of being in concurrent employments which cease simultaneously. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Where membership includes membership in part-time employment, the enhanced membership period allowed under paragraph (2) must be calculated by first working out what it would be if the employment were all whole-time, and then reducing the resulting period by multiplying it by the appropriate fraction (but see paragraphs (7) and (10)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The member’s enhanced membership period must not be reduced below that which is calculated by reference to his membership in whole-time employment, disregarding his membership in part-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) The appropriate fraction is the fraction of which the numerator is the member’s total membership and the denominator is the period which would be his total membership if his employment had all been whole-time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) In the case of a member in part-time employment with non-cyclical fluctuating contractual hours, that fraction must be determined by making separate calculations for each period over which averaging of the member’s hours occurs in calculating his contractual hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) If the member’s total membership includes a period of at least 13 122/365 years in whole-time employment, paragraph (6) does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
