# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Joining the Scheme[^c-af56d56a4536b3f0c02ebbe7d0db0ae7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A person who wishes to become an active member must apply in writing to his employer or future employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) An eligible person who applies before he begins his employment becomes a member when his employment begins unless he applies to join later. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) An eligible employee who applies becomes a member on the first day of the first payment period following the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A payment period is a period of service to which the employee’s wages or salary payment relate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) An employee is deemed to have applied to become a member, unless he notified his employer in writing that he did not wish to do so – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) before his employment began; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if he only became eligible to be a member later, before the date he became eligible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) But paragraph (5) does not apply – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to casual employees; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to former members who opted to leave the Scheme by a notification under regulation 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) An application for membership may be withdrawn before membership begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) A former active member may reapply for such membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) But a person who has given more than one notification under regulation 7 may only reapply again if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his employer or future employer consents; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he is beginning a new employment with a new employing authority and he reapplies before he has been employed for three months; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) he is a person about whom information may be given under Article 164(1) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, as it has effect in the case of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
