# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 40

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Revaluation of guaranteed minimum[^key-ed1443832f3e8803c402b9b3822ca2f7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where the guaranteed minimum of a person who has ceased to be an active member is appropriately secured, his earnings factors for the purposes of section 10(2) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 must be determined – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) by reference to the last order under Article 23 of the Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00017] or section 130 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00018] (revaluation orders) to come into force before the end of the tax year in which he ceased to be an active member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) without reference to the last such order to come into force before the end of the final relevant year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) For such a person the weekly equivalent mentioned in section 10(2) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 is to be increased – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) by at least the prescribed percentage for each relevant year after the end of the tax year in which he ceased to be an active member, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in accordance with such additional requirements as may be prescribed for the purposes of section 12(3) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) In this regulation – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- “appropriately secured” has the meaning given in section 15(3) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “final relevant year” has the meaning given in section 12(5) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “relevant year” has the meaning given in section 10(8) of that Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- “prescribed percentage” has the meaning given in regulation 62 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00019].
