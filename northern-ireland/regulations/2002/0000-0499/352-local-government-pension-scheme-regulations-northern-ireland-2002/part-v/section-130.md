# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 130

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 130. Conversion of periods credited under Discretionary Payments Regulations etc. into membership[^key-6ed9133b2d12649b0e2b31dba80a402a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where, apart from paragraph (9) of regulation 54, an employing authority could pass a resolution under that regulation to increase a person’s total membership, it may resolve that his total membership be increased by the whole or part of the period credited to him as mentioned in that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any person has been credited by an employer (“the transferor employer”) under any scheme, the terms of which correspond to Part III of the Local Government (Discretionary Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00044] or Part IV of the Local Government (Early Termination of Employment) (Discretionary Compensation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^key-f454c4d1f77bc4fa465f9b9cea18e2eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] with a period of service in respect of a former employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the transferor employer’s liability in respect of payments falling due to that person as respects that credited period has been transferred to an employing authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) apart from paragraph (9) of regulation 54, if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) the person’s employment with the transferor employer had been employment with the employing authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] any actions taken by the transferor employer under the scheme mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) had been taken by that authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  that authority could pass a resolution under regulation 54 to increase his total membership, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

it may resolve that his total membership be increased by the whole or part of the period credited to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The additional period must not exceed the period which would be the maximum additional period under regulation 54 if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a resolution under that regulation could be passed in relation to him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the period of 10 years were substituted for the period of 6 243/365 years in paragraph (2)(d) of that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The additional period may only be counted as a period of membership if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the employing authority and the Committee agree that the employing authority will pay increased contributions under regulation 79 to meet the cost of the increase in membership; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the employing authority makes the payment which is required by regulation 80(2) within the period specified in that regulation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

but it may be so counted as from the date from which the resolution has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If the employing authority fails to pay those increased contributions or make that payment, the resolution shall cease to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Where the resolution has effect, the period credited as mentioned in regulation 54(9) is reduced or extinguished accordingly (but without prejudice to any payments which have fallen due or have been made before that date). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) No resolution may be passed under this regulation, the result of which would be to place any individual in a worse position than he would otherwise be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) If the person was credited as mentioned in regulation 54(9) on or after the commencement date, this regulation only applies if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the employing authority is an authority which has reasonable grounds to believe that it will cease to exist as a result of a provision made by or under an enactment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the employing authority has ceased to exist as a result of any such provision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

and where sub-paragraph (b) applies “employing authority” includes the body to which the former employing authority’s functions as respects the former employee in question have been transferred in connection with its cessation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
