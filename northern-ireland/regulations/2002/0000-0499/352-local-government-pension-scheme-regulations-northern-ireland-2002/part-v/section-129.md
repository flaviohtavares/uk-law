# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 129

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Separate employments etc.[^key-2a5e5434c6c6e08e2e4b97f9ae727a06]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a person holds separate employments under one Scheme employer, these Regulations apply as if each of them were with a different employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, a clerk of a district council who performs functions under Article 9(2) (conduct of elections) of the Electoral Law (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00043] shall, in relation to those functions, be treated as if he were employed by that district council and any fees paid to him in connection with those functions shall be treated as remuneration paid to him by that district council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
