# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 143

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 143. Commutation: serious ill-health[^key-137312d57813bb9aa047742675423f57]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] In circumstances where a pension credit member is suffering from serious ill-health at any time prior to the date when he first becomes entitled to receive a pension under this Part, the whole of that pension may be commuted for a lump sum and the total of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) that lump sum; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the lump sum grant (if applicable) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(“the commutation payment”) paid to the pension credit member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The lump sum referred to in paragraph (1)(a) shall be equal to five times the annual rate of the pension to which the pension credit member would have been entitled if on the date of commutation he had reached the normal benefit age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) If applicable, the lump sum grant shall be equal to three times that annual rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) In this regulation, “serious ill-health” means ill-health which is such as to give rise to a life expectancy of less than one year from the date on which commutation of the pension is to take effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Before making any decision as to whether a pension credit member may be entitled under paragraph (1), the Committee must obtain from an independent registered medical practitioner which it has appointed a certificate as to whether in his opinion the pension credit member is suffering from serious ill-health. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Payment of the commutation payment discharges the Committee’s liability to the pension credit member in respect of his pension credit benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The Committee must deduct from the commutation payment any tax to which it may become chargeable under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Part 9 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003[^key-2098383c3859877af5169999d88a2a72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].
