# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 139

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 139. Calculation[^c-b572c8b4a175e80bd9197002539d0378]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The annual rate of the pension at normal benefit age shall be the pension calculated as referred to in regulation 133(4), increased in accordance with the Pensions (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00047] and, if applicable, the Pensions Increase (Northern Ireland) Order 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] from the day on which the pension sharing order takes effect[^c-3388efdcd5bff2cac4d15853f5d52e1c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(2) The lump sum grant shall be equal to three times the annual rate of the pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
