# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 137

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 137. Valuation date[^key-48077144762f44fdfcb142feaec61640]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) calculating the cash equivalent referred to in Article 27(2) of the 1999 Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) regulation 133(3) and (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-3ab6f4ea5f998ec69fff3c4ffe7769cf]...,

the valuation date shall be the first day of the implementation period as defined in Article 31(1) of that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
