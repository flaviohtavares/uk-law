# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 136

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 136. Safeguarded rights[^key-d7c8290740b26b50fdbd62cd67fac4ea]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A pension credit member’s safeguarded rights for the purposes of the Scheme and of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 and regulations made under that Act are such of his rights falling within section 64A(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00046] of that Act as represent the safeguarded percentage of the rights acquired by him in the Scheme by virtue of the pension credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The “safeguarded percentage” is the percentage of the shareable rights by reference to which the amount of the pension credit is determined which are contracted-out rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) “Contracted-out rights” has the meaning given in section 64A(5) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
