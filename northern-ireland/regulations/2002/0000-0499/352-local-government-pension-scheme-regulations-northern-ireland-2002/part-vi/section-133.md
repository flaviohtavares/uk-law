# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 133

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 133. Discharge of liability for pension credit rights[^c-e22626ab22c57ebdb468235e47e9f638]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee may discharge its liability in respect of a pension credit in accordance with either sub-paragraph (2) or sub-paragraph (3) of paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the 1999 Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where the Committee discharges its liability by conferring pension credit rights on the person entitled to the pension credit, those rights shall be to – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a pension and a lump sum grant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a death grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A pension credit member is not entitled to a lump sum grant if the transferor has been paid a retirement grant before the valuation date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The pension at the valuation date shall be calculated – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) by reference to the value of the pension credit member’s pension credit rights calculated in accordance with regulation 10 of the Pension Sharing (Implementation and Discharge of Liability) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00045]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
