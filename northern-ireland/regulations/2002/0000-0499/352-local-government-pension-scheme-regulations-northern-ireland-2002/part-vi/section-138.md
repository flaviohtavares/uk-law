# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 138

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 138. Application of the Regulations to pension credit members[^c-a04a0510905ed55562c4a2c6a7d703ae]

*Extent: N.I.*

Part I (preliminary provisions), regulations 95 (interest on late payment of certain benefits), 96 (payments due in respect of deceased persons) and 97(non-assignability) and Chapter IV (determinations, information and records) of Part IV (Administration) of these Regulations apply to a pension credit member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
