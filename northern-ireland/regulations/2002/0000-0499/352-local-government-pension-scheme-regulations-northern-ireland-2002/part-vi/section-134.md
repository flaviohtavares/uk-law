# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 134

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. Aggregation[^c-b800868461be44be540d4f4189abffc9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Pension credit rights or pension credit benefits may not be aggregated with any other rights or benefits under the Scheme (including those attributable to a different pension credit). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where a pension credit member is also an active member, he may not count any period which may count for any purpose as a period of membership in connection with his pension credit benefits towards the membership period required before he is entitled to any benefit which relates to his active membership, or in the calculation of that benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
