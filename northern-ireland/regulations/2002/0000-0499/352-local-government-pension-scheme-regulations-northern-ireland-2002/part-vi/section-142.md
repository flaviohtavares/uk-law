# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 142

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 142. Commutation: small pensions[^key-abee64a047b90aff1602fba5b2631d8e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If the capital value of the pension to which a pension credit member is entitled would be a trivial commutation lump sum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] within the meaning of section 166 of[^key-aef5f4a75eeaf49e8d7b492d1fc24228] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Finance Act 2004, the Committee may pay to him a lump sum representing such capital value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If the pension credit member is entitled to more than one pension under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] a lump sum is only payable if the capital value of the aggregate amount payable to that member would be a trivial commutation lump sum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-97c63dad18a21ab308f3c7cea889d956]....

(3) The capital value of the pension must be calculated in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The payment of a trivial commutation lump sum in respect of a pension due to the pension credit member under this regulation discharges the Committee from its liability for the pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The Committee must deduct from any payment under this regulation any tax for which it may become liable under Part 9 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
