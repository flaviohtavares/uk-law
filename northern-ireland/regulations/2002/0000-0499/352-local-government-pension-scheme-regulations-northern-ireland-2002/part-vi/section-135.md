# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 135

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 135. Death of person entitled to a pension credit before discharge[^c-9b90ee2852bd383e4b4f4dc95ff1f2e2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where a person entitled to a pension credit dies before liability in respect of his pension credit has been discharged in accordance with regulation 133(1), such liability shall be discharged by the Committee by the payment of a lump sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The lump sum shall be equal to three times the annual rate of the pension that would have been paid to him if on the date of his death he had become entitled to a pension as a pension credit member, calculated in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The Committee shall pay the lump sum to the deceased’s personal representatives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
