# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 145

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 145. Transfers in[^c-441029021af5f32451c91f66eb1468df]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A pension credit member is not entitled to request the Committee to accept a transfer value for relevant pension rights and the Committee must not accept a transfer value of such rights where they have accrued to a pension credit member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) “Relevant pension rights” has the meaning given in regulation 124(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
