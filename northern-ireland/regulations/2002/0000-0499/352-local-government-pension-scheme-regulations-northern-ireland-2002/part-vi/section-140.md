# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 140

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 140. Payment of benefits[^key-90853efa2fa326c405d0c7a8a8b6e965]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A pension credit member who attains normal benefit age is entitled to the immediate payment of a pension and, if applicable, a lump sum grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(1A)[^key-72325e6a8bd5de25dae6806d903c338e] A pension credit member may apply in writing to the Committee for the early payment of benefits from age 55 up to normal benefit age and such benefits must be reduced in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The pension and the lump sum grant are payable from the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The pension is payable for life. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
