> Text as at 2019-11-18.

## 144. Transfers out[^c-7034dad2b31ea16ac260b28a4f94e857]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of Chapter II of Part IVA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00049] of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (requirements relating to pension credit benefits), the managers of the Scheme in relation to a pension credit member is the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
