## 97A. Treatment of other benefits[^c-d46dd6a34633ebffce6b2485582a218f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any payment of employment-related benefits made to a member by an employing authority in accordance with regulations made under Article 19 of the Order of 1972 shall be treated as if it had been made under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].
