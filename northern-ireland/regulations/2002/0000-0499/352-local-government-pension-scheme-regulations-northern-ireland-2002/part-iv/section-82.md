# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 82

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 82. Interest[^key-dad9325d6816400e20dedd2d29700c14]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee may require an employing authority from which payment of any amount due under regulation 79, 80 or 81 is overdue by more than ten days to pay interest on that amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Interest under paragraph (1) or under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] 88(3)[^key-31907ab80f7848d68c3c144e8c3dcb70] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], 90(5) or 95, must be calculated at one per cent. above base rate on a day to day basis from the due date to the date of payment and compounded with three-monthly rests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
