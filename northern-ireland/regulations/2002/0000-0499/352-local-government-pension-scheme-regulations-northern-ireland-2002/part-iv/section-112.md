# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 112

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 112. Statements of policy concerning abatement of retirement pensions in new employment[^c-c15cc66dea3f35e50be844a0ea830f32]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must formulate and keep under review its policy concerning abatement (that is, the extent, if any, to which the amount of retirement pension payable to a member from the fund maintained by it under the Scheme should be reduced (or whether it should be extinguished) where the member has entered a new employment with a Scheme employer). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Before formulating that policy the Committee must consult with employing authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Before the expiry of the period of four months beginning with the commencement date, the Committee shall publish a statement as to the policy which is being applied by it where a member who is so entitled enters such a new employment on or after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Where, as a result of reviewing its policy concerning abatement, the Committee determines to amend it, it must publish a statement of the amended policy before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date it determines to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) In formulating its policy concerning abatement, the Committee must have regard to – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the level of potential financial gain at which it wishes abatement to apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the administrative costs which are likely to be incurred as a result of abatement in the different circumstances in which it may occur; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the extent to which a policy not to apply abatement could lead to a serious loss of confidence in the public service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) In paragraph (5)(a) the reference to financial gain is a reference to the financial gain which it appears to the Committee may be obtained by a member as a result of his entitlement both to a pension and to pay under the new employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
