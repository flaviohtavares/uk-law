# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 105

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105. Notice of decisions by the Committee under regulation 104[^c-4f8b40f3fc18d01dea5b547cf9cfef62]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must issue its decision on the matters raised by an application under regulation 104 to the parties to the disagreement by notice in writing before the expiry of the period of two months beginning with the date the application was received (but see paragraph (2)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If no such notice is issued before the expiry of that period, an interim reply must be sent immediately to those parties, setting out the reasons for the delay and an expected date for issuing the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) must include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a statement of the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in a case where there has been a decision made under regulation 103, an explanation as to whether and, if so, to what extent that decision is confirmed or replaced; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a reference to any legislation or provisions of the Scheme relied upon; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) in a case where the disagreement relates to the exercise of a discretion, a reference to the provisions of the Scheme conferring the discretion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) a statement that OPAS (the Pensions Advisory Service) is available to assist members and beneficiaries of the Scheme in connection with any difficulties with the Scheme which remain unresolved and of the address at which OPAS (the Pensions Advisory Service) may be contacted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (f) a statement that the Pensions Ombudsman may investigate and determine any complaint or dispute of fact or law in relation to the Scheme made or referred in accordance with the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 and of the address at which he may be contacted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
