# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 108A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108A. Annual benefit statements[^key-c95aaba44c1fdce150c544431e253f25]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee shall issue an annual benefit statement to each of its active, deferred and pension credit members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The first such statement\[s\] must be issued on or before 1st April 2006 and subsequent statements must be issued on or before each 1st April thereafter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) An annual benefit statement shall contain an illustration of the amount of benefit entitlement, in respect of the rights that may arise under the Scheme, which – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) has been accrued by the member at the relevant date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of an active member, is capable of being accrued by the member if he remains in the Scheme until his normal retirement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The illustration shall be calculated – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of active members, on the member’s pensionable pay (or, in the case of part-time employees, the whole-time equivalent) for the twelve-month period ending with the relevant date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of deferred members, on the member’s final pay; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) in the case of pension credit members, in accordance with regulation 139, as if the “normal benefit age” is the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The relevant date – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) is 31st March prior to the date that the statement is issued, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) such later date as the Committee may choose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
