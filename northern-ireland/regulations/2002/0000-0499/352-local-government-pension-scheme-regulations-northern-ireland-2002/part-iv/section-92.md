# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 92

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Pension increases under the Pensions (Increase) Acts[^c-1bf9066bf35e487e8399cddc92e3ad40]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a pension to which the Pensions (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00029] applies is payable out of the fund, any increase under that Act or the Pensions (Increase) (Northern Ireland) Order 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00030] , must be paid from the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
