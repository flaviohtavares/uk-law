# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 117

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 117. Protection of GMP rights[^key-42ac3d2eeb89f447a1f11f25895464b4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The power – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to give directions under regulation 114(2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to recover or retain amounts under regulation 116(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

may not be exercised so as to deprive a person of his guaranteed minimum pension or any widow’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , widower’s or surviving civil partner's[^key-52c0633ac65c4b87c17428cc29dc2c3c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] guaranteed minimum pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But such a power may be so exercised if the person left his employment – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) because of the offence of treason; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) because of one or more offences under the Official Secrets Acts 1911 to 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00036] for which the former member has been sentenced on the same occasion – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) to a term of imprisonment of at least 10 years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to two or more consecutive terms amounting in the aggregate to at least 10 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
