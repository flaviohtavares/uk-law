# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 100

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 100. Notification of decisions[^key-9a3408b2a4f9508375d7517cf632e7f3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A body which has decided any question under regulation 99 shall, as soon as is reasonably practicable after doing so, send a written notification of its decision to every person whose rights or liabilities are affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The notification shall include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a decision that the person is not entitled to a benefit, the grounds for the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a decision as to the amount of a benefit, a statement showing how the amount is calculated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) in the case of a notification under sub-paragraph (a) or (b) a conspicuous statement directing the person’s attention – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) to the address from which he may obtain further information about the decision, including details of any calculation of service or benefits, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to his right under regulation 102 to make an application to an appointed person, the address at which he may be contacted and his job title, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to his right under regulation 104 to apply to the Committee for a reconsideration of the matter within the time limit referred to in that regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) in the case of a decision by an employing authority made under regulation 99, a conspicuous statement directing the person’s attention – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) to the address from which he may obtain further information about the decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to his right of appeal to the county court under regulation 107. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
