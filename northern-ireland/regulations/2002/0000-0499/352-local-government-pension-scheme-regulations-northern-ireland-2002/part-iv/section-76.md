# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 76

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 76. Accounts and audit[^key-0c012750fcac4fc385a5ba0e64c94416]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] The Committee shall keep accounts of all financial transactions of the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2) The Secretary of the Committee shall prepare the financial statements for the financial year ended *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] 31st March 2006[^key-471c022914044cd3978a6ab3e335967c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b] and subsequent financial years in accordance with paragraph (3) and shall forward three copies of the financial statements duly signed and dated by him to the Department not later than 30th June after the expiration of the financial year to which the financial statements relate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(2A)[^key-0bb7b3ee9c3089c257dd17122c0b5195] The input period for the purposes of section 238 of the Finance Act 2004 is the year ending 31st March 2007 and each year ending 31st March thereafter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(3) The financial statements shall comprise – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) a Foreword; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) a Statement of the Committee’s Responsibilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (c) an Accounting Officer’s Statement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (d) a Fund Account; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (e) a Net Assets Statement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

shall be prepared in accordance with guidance for the time being issued by the Department of Finance and Personnel.[^key-673e54e8d6d792fe99c7634101504614] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(4) The financial statements shall give a true and fair view of the Fund Account for the financial year, and the Net Assets Statement as at the end of the financial year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(5) The financial statements kept by the Committee shall be audited annually by a local government auditor who shall report on the financial statements audited and shall send his report, together with two audited copies of the financial statements duly signed and dated by him, to the Department within 14 days after completion of the audit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(6) The Department on receipt of the local government auditor’s report and the audited copies of the financial statements shall send a copy of such report and financial statements to the Secretary of the Committee who shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) lay such copy of the report and financial statements before the next meeting of the Committee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) forward a copy of such report and financial statements to each employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(7) The local government auditor may require – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

- (a) the production before him of all documents and financial records of the Committee which he thinks necessary for the purpose of the audit ; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (b) any person holding or accountable for any such documents or financial records to appear before him at the audit or any adjournment thereof ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
- (c) any such person to make and sign a declaration as to the correctness of the documents or financial records. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]

(8) The Committee shall annually at such time as the Department may direct make to it a report of its proceedings during the preceding year, and the Department shall lay a copy of such report before the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dcdb734706a4552e1fb8bed10b3bfd0b]
