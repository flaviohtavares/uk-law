# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 124

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 124. Inward transfers of pension rights[^key-a3e32d008dbd2a788c96b1429031272b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a person who becomes an active member has relevant pension rights, he may request the Committee to accept a transfer value for some or all those rights from the relevant transferor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Relevant pension rights are accrued rights under – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) an occupational pension scheme (other than the Scheme); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a personal pension scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a retirement annuity contract approved by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue under section 620 or 621 of the Taxes Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a self-employed pension arrangement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

but do not include rights to benefits under a scheme, contract or arrangement which are attributable (directly or indirectly) to a pension credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Accrued rights include rights to preserved benefits and rights appropriately secured under section 19 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The relevant transferor is the trustees or managers of the scheme, contract or arrangement under which the transferring person’s relevant pension rights arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) But the relevant transferor for the rights specified in paragraph (3) is the trustees or managers of the scheme, contract or arrangement, or the insurance company, to which a payment in respect of his accrued rights has been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) A request from a transferring person under paragraph (1) must be made by notice in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) That notice must be given before the expiry of the period of 12 months beginning with the date he became an active member (or such longer period as the Committee may allow). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) Where a request under paragraph (1) is duly made the Committee may accept the transfer value and credit it to the pension fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
