# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 99

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. Decisions as to status of employees and decisions as to benefits[^key-563d7fa2c0b2464a830acf15a19e7769]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The employing authority shall decide in relation to each of its employees who is a member – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) whether he is an officer or a manual worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) whether he is whole-time, a variable-time or a part-time employee, or whether his employment is of a casual nature; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) which of his emoluments are remuneration on which contributions are payable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) if he is a part-time employee the proportion which his contractual minimum hours of employment in each week bear to those of a comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) In relation to any employment in which a person is a member, the Committee is to decide – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) what previous service or employment (if any) he is entitled to count as a period of membership; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) whether any, and if so what, periods of service as a part-time employee are included in such a period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) what proportion of whole-time service his service during any such period represents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) whether a payment in lieu of contributions has been made or equivalent pension benefits have been assured under Part III of the Insurance Act in respect of any period of non-participating employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) what rate of contribution the employee is liable to pay to the fund; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (f) whether he is entitled to count as a period of membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) any, and if so how many, added years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] any, and if so what, additional *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] period.[^key-e7432bcf324af09502a67e090e158595] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (g)[^key-78f7fc5a99176703bba5eca8afe7d2cb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The questions specified in paragraph (1) shall be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the person becomes a member; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any change occurs in the number of the member’s regular or usual hours of employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any other material change occurs in or in relation to the employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The questions specified in paragraph (2) shall be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the person becomes a member of the Scheme in respect of the relevant employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Any question as to whether a person is entitled to a benefit under these Regulations is to be decided by the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Where a person is or may become entitled to a benefit payable out of the fund, the Committee shall decide the amount of the benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The decision under paragraph (5) shall be made as soon as is reasonably practicable after the cessation of the employment or, as the case may be, the death of the employee and that under paragraph (6) as soon as is reasonably practicable after the occurrence of the event by virtue of which the entitlement arises or may arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) In this regulation “benefit” includes a return of contributions and a benefit specified in regulation F6(11) or (14) of the 1992 Regulations and Part N of the 2000 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
