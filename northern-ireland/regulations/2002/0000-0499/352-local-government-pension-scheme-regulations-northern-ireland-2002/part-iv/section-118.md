# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 118

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 118. Transfer of sums from the fund to compensate for former member’s misconduct[^key-68f395489831e9b605032c4a04cb077c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] This regulation applies where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a person has left an employment in which he was a member because of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) an offence of a fraudulent character, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] grave misconduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  in either case in connection with that employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) his former employing authority in that employment has suffered direct financial loss by reason of the offence or misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) either – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) the former employee became entitled to benefits under Part II or the 2000 Regulations and a direction has been given under regulation 114(2), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he did not become so entitled and on leaving the employment became entitled to a return of contributions under regulation 88 (whether or not he has waived his right). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) If the former employing authority is the Committee, it may retain an appropriate amount in the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Otherwise, the Committee must pay the former employing authority an appropriate amount out of the fund, if requested to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But if a contributions equivalent premium or a payment in lieu of contributions is due or has been made in respect of the former employee, the Committee may reduce an amount retained under paragraph (2) or a payment under paragraph (3) by the amount of any contributions equivalent premium and by half the amount of any payment in lieu of contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) An appropriate amount is an amount not exceeding – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the amount of the direct financial loss; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the amount of any contributions which could have been returned to the former employee, or paid to his spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , civil partner[^key-5fa096785c80533d83696c4e4d366bb4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or a dependant, under regulation 89(2) or regulation C21(4) of the 2000 Regulations, less the amount of any which have been so returned or paid, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

whichever is the less. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) If after making a payment under paragraph (3) the Committee is required to make any transfer payment under Chapter IV of Part IV of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00037] or under regulation 122 for a former employee, the former employing authority must repay it, if requested to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
