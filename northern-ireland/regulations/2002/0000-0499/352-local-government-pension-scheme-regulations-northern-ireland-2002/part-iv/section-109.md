# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 109

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 109. Information to be supplied by employees[^c-d4826f421b3ea5d9bb4c50500ee478a5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the date a person becomes a member, the employing authority must ask him in writing for the documents specified in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Those documents are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a statement in writing listing all the person’s previous periods of employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) copies of all notifications previously given to him under these Regulations or the former regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The employing authority must also ask for those documents before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the occurrence of any change as respects his employment which is material for the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A request under paragraph (1) or (3) must include a conspicuous statement that it is important that the member gives full and accurate information, especially for ascertaining his rights under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The employing authority need not request any documents if satisfied that it or the Committee (if different) already has all material information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
