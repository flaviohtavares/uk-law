# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 103

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 103. Notice of decisions by the appointed person under regulation 102[^c-36ee157260eb64a14b50a3455b847457]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A decision on the matters raised by an application under regulation 102 must be issued by the appropriate appointed person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to the applicant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to the Committee, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

by notice in writing before the expiry of the period of two months beginning with the date the application was received. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But, if no such notice is issued before the expiry of that period, an interim reply must immediately be sent to the persons mentioned in paragraph (1) setting out the reasons for the delay and an expected date for issuing the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) must include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a statement of the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) reference to any legislation or provisions of the Scheme relied upon; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) in a case where the disagreement relates to the exercise of a discretion, a reference to the provisions of the Scheme conferring the discretion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a reference to the rights of the applicant to refer the disagreement for reconsideration by the Committee under regulation 104, specifying the time within which he may do so; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) a statement that OPAS (the Pensions Advisory Service) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00035] is available to assist members and beneficiaries of the Scheme in connection with any difficulty with the Scheme which remains unresolved and the address at which OPAS may be contacted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
