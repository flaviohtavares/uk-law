## 88. Rights to return of contributions[^key-f0b8c7e9b424c8446cdd45d7d0a29789]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If a member with less than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] three months'[^key-20f0b5ca4201936d01c9a256b2ac76c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] membership – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) ceases to be employed by a Scheme employer or to be an active member without becoming entitled to a retirement pension; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) ceases to be an active member by reason of a notification under regulation 7(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

he is entitled to be repaid his contributions from the fund *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-11a649f1dbc418107752c435512f7f11]....

(2)[^key-609cbc700e104ac2dd724fbc1cfd3c4f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If repayment of the contributions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^key-2126b14281ab0f77ab03c0534388c67d]... has not been made before the expiry of the period of one year beginning with the date when active membership ceases, the person is entitled to interest on the repayment which should have been made, calculated as provided in regulation 82(2), the due date being the date when active membership ceased. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3A)[^key-c3c6c64992cdfeb7a1897e7a8d9477b8] If a member dies before repayment of contributions have been made, these shall be treated as a lump sum death benefit for the purposes of section 168 of the Finance Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A person who is entitled to a repayment of contributions under paragraph (1) may waive his entitlement for any period and, if he becomes an active member again before the expiry of that period, he shall cease to be so entitled (but without prejudice to any entitlement arising later under that paragraph in respect of those contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) A person who continues as an active member in another employment he held concurrently with the employment in which he has ceased to be an active member may elect for an amount equal to the repayment to be treated as contributions to the Scheme as respects his membership in that concurrent employment, entitling him to a period of membership equal to the period of membership in the employment which has ceased, as reduced under regulation 10 (4) if the employment which has ceased was part-time, multiplied by the fraction – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/352) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- the rate of pay in each case is the annual rate of pay on the last day of employment in the employment which has ceased. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) A person who elects under paragraph (5) ceases to be entitled to that repayment (but without prejudice to any entitlement arising later in respect of the concurrent employment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The Committee must deduct from any repayment under this regulation any tax to which it may become chargeable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] as a short service refund lump sum charge under section 205 of the Finance Act 2004[^key-57b24815664f0871dba983fedfce3a5d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].

(8) The contributions which must be repaid under paragraph (1) are any contributions or payments paid by the member to any pension fund under Part II or Chapter III of Part III or by way of additional contributory payments or added period payments, or paid under any of the relevant old provisions (unless already returned and not repaid), which are attributable to a period of membership which might have counted under these Regulations in relation to the employment in which he has ceased to be a member, but not to any earlier period of membership in respect of which a benefit or transfer value has been paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) The relevant old provisions are regulations C3, C5, C6, C7, C13 and C14 of the 2000 Regulations and regulations C1, C2, C3, C6, C7, and C9 of the 1992 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) Added period payments are payments made for the purposes of the former regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) “Additional contributory payment” has the meaning given in Schedule A1 to the 2000 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
