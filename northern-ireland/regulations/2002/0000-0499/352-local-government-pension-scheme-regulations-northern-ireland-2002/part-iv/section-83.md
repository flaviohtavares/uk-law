# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 83

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Administration and management[^key-2aa06ba07f4a04dc2ee221b6ce7f53cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] For the purposes of these Regulations the Committee shall be constituted in accordance with Part I of Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The Committee shall have the powers specified in Part II of Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The expenses and allowances payable by the Committee shall be in accordance with Part III of Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
