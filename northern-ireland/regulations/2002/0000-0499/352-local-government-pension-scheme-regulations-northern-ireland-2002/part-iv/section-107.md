## 107. Referral of decisions under regulation 99(1) to the county court[^c-393886aa831de64cd9c93313a3fb2722]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an employing authority has either decided or failed to decide such question as is mentioned in regulation 99(1), that question shall be determined by the county court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-2cb9025bb35880caf2b5696ceff44f8c]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc].
