# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 85

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 85. Separate treatment of AVCs and SCAVCs from other contributions[^key-cd6cf27e2f90d16d75d66ee88e599843]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Regulations 88 and 89 (return of contributions) do not apply to AVCs or SCAVCs payable under (or interest on late payments which relate to AVCs or SCAVCs under) Chapter IV of Part III or under any pension policy or agreement made for the payment of AVCs before the commencement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The regulations mentioned in paragraph (3) do not apply in relation to benefits under such a policy or agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Those regulations are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) regulation 98 (first instance decisions); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) regulation 114 (forfeiture); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) regulation 115 (interim payments directions); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) regulation 116 (recovery and retention in cases of misconduct). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
