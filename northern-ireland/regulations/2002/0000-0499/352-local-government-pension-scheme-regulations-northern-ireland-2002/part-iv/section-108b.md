# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 108B

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108B. Communication policy statements[^c-002d3ec0343649b4761b09f356cf3cd5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must prepare, maintain and publish a written statement setting out its policy concerning communications with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) representatives of members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) prospective members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) employing authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) In particular, the statement must set out its policy on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the provision of information and publicity about *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to members, representatives of members and employing authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the format, frequency and method of distributing such information or publicity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the promotion of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] to prospective members and their employing authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The first such statement must be published before 1st April 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The statement must be revised and published by the Committee following such material change in their policy on any of the matters referred to in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
