# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 89

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Exclusion of rights to return of contributions[^key-0a47e17feed6034ce72e8ccce60b30a6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A person is not entitled to a repayment under regulation 88(1) if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) he becomes a member again within one month and one day (otherwise than in employment he held concurrently with the employment in which he was previously a member); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he left his employment because of – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) an offence of a fraudulent character, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] grave misconduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  in either case in connection with that employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) regulation 120(2) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But where paragraph (1)(b)(ii) applies, the employing authority may direct the payment out of the fund to him or, where paragraph (1)(b)(i) applies, to him or to his spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , civil partner[^key-3e5d768f57d26a2e717d55dc25c5f507] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or any dependant of his, of a sum equal to all or part of his contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A person is not entitled to a repayment under regulation 88(1) if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^key-e3164162c6dd936de45e6da10c0c5472] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a transfer value has been credited to the fund for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) A person who is entitled to a repayment under regulation 88(1)(a) ceases to be entitled to it if he returns to local government employment before receiving it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
