# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 75A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 75A. Governance policy statement[^c-4189a7df5c00ae6510266d8895eace29]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must, after consultation with such persons as it considers appropriate, prepare, maintain and publish a written statement setting out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) whether the Committee delegates its function or part of its function in relation to maintaining a pension fund to a sub-committee or an officer of the Committee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) if it delegates that function or part of that function to a sub-committee or an officer of the Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) the frequency of any sub-committee meetings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] the terms of reference, structure and operational procedures of the delegation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] whether the sub-committee includes representatives of employing authorities or members, and if so, whether those representatives have voting rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The first such statement must be published before 1st April 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The statement must be revised and published by the Committee following a material change in their policy on any of the matters referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
