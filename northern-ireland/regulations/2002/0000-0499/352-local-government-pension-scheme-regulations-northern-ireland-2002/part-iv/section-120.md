# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 120

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 120. Rights to payment out of the fund[^key-091d97f8aa4f2d734077b15886afa9f5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The amount of any transfer payment due in respect of a member under Chapter IV of Part IV of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 is payable by the Committee from the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where such a transfer payment is to be or has been paid from the fund, no other payment or transfer of assets may be made from the fund as respects the accrued rights covered by the transfer payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Paragraph (2) overrides anything to the contrary in the former regulations, any other provision of these Regulations or the Transitional Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
