# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 98

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 98. First instance decisions[^c-40b08c528a32b4f5c55f21eae6cc78d0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Any question concerning the rights or liabilities under these regulations of any person other than a Scheme employer shall be decided in the first instance by the relevant body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Where the body by which any such question falls to be decided is ascertainable by reference to this regulation or regulation 99, for the purposes of this Chapter that body shall be the relevant body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Where the Committee is considering whether a person who has ceased to hold a local government employment is entitled to a benefit under regulation 29 or 33(6), it shall refer for decision to an independent registered medical practitioner appointed by the Committee who is qualified in occupational health medicine, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-dd427e67a3938391fcf3b421ac14e74c]... as to whether in his opinion the member is permanently incapable of discharging efficiently the duties of the relevant local government employment because of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body.[^c-61d277e6af719d0a95d0060dd515a2b7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The decision of the independent registered medical practitioner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-147547d91b01aadb14d18cc91a79309e]... under paragraph (3) shall be expressed in the form of a certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The independent registered medical practitioner must be in a position to certify, and must include in his certification a statement that – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) he has not previously advised, or given an opinion on, or otherwise been involved in the particular case for which the certificate has been requested; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) he is not acting, and has not at any time acted, as the representative of the member, the Scheme employer or any other party in relation to the same case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) In paragraph (3) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^c-175e5b28e44e1469ce567694c5d22cde] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-58c8f4618c06f13cf0b60278bdeb6cf7] “qualified in occupational health medicine” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) holding a diploma in occupational medicine (D Occ Med) or an equivalent qualification issued by a competent authority in an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] State; and for the purposes of this definition, “competent authority” has the meaning given by the General and Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualification) Order 2003; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] being an Associate, a Member or a Fellow of the Faculty of Occupational Medicine or an equivalent institution of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
