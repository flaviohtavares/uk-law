# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 80

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Employer’s further payments[^c-725f3543a0dc13742a6d8575cf4a369a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where an employing authority passes a resolution under regulation 54 it must pay the appropriate sum to the fund before the expiry of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph (7) of that regulation) unless before the end of that period it has agreed as mentioned in paragraph (6)(a) of that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2)[^c-6e512df94f339bd3a8bead735ddcce19] Where an employing authority passes a resolution under regulation 130 in a case where paragraph (4)(a) of that regulation does not apply, it must pay the appropriate sum to the fund before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date on which the resolution is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The appropriate sum for a member is such sum as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Any extra charge on the fund resulting from – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a resolution under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] [^c-d788a2555895713fad7b048f44e27cbd]... [^c-d788a2555895713fad7b048f44e27cbd]... 54[^c-18fa5a8e7cc60764290852540d7c610c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a member becoming entitled to an ill-health pension calculated under regulation 30 by reference to an enhanced membership period; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a member becoming entitled to a pension calculated under regulation 28 (redundancy, etc) or regulation 33 (other early leavers, etc) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or regulation 38(1A) (requirements as to time of payment)[^c-91984d7dcd290483ba4b2c7841004e24] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc],

must be repaid to the fund by the employing authority concerned *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] (but, in the case of a resolution under regulation 54, only so far as not paid under paragraph (1)).[^c-d7e9c1a1471d16e22c5e948f5261cf86] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Any additional payments that are due under paragraph (4) shall be made, if the Committee agrees by – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a single payment of an amount determined by the Committee on the advice of an actuary appointed by it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) instalments, each of an amount determined by the Committee on the advice of an actuary appointed by it, covering a period not exceeding 5 years, or such longer period as the Committee may allow, the first and subsequent instalments becoming payable as agreed between the Committee and the employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
