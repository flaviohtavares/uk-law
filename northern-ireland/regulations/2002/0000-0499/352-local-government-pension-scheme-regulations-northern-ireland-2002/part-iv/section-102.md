# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 102

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 102. Right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision[^c-997fc94a34c450c83f52640c280e87c8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where there is a disagreement about a matter in relation to the Scheme between a member or an alternative applicant and the Committee, the member or, as the case may be, the alternative applicant may – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) apply directly to the appropriate appointed person to decide the disagreement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) apply to the Committee for it to refer the disagreement to an appointed person for decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) These persons are alternative applicants – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a widow, widower or surviving *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] civil partner or[^c-7e7c766e5c3868fc2f5f90694c907059] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] dependant of a deceased member or any other person to whom benefits in respect of him may be paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a prospective member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a pension credit member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (d) a person entitled to a pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (e) a person who ceased to be a member or to fall within sub-paragraph (a), (b), (c) or (d) during the period of six months ending with the date of the application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (f) in the case of a disagreement relating to the question as to whether a person claiming to be a member or to fall within sub-paragraph (a), (b), (c), (d) or (e) does so, the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The application for a decision must set out particulars of the disagreement, including a statement as to its nature with sufficient details to show why the applicant is aggrieved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) An application by – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a member or prospective member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a person who ceased to be such a person during the period of six months ending with the date of the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a person claiming to be a person within paragraph (a) or (b), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

must set out his full name, address, date of birth, his national insurance number (if any) and the name of his employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) An application by – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a person entitled to a pension credit or a pension credit member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a person who ceased to be such a person or member during the period of six months ending with the date of the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a person claiming to be a person or member within sub-paragraph (a) or (b), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

must set out his full name, address and date of birth. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) An application by any other person must set out – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his full name, address and date of birth; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) his relationship to the member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the member’s full name, address, date of birth and national insurance number and the name of his employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The application must be signed by or on behalf of the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) The application must be accompanied by a copy of any written notification issued under regulation 100. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) The application must be made before the end of the period of six months beginning with the relevant date or such further period as the appropriate appointed person considers reasonable . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) Where the disagreement relates to a decision under regulation 99, the relevant date is the date notification of it is given under regulation 100. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) Otherwise, the relevant date is the date of the act or omission which is the cause of the disagreement or, if there is more than one, the last of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
