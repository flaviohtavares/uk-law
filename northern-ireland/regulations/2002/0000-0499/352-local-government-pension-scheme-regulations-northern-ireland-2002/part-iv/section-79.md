# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 79

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Contributing body’s contributions[^c-d122f8813bf0f4fec6405698bd60eb93][^c-6e6ca9050b00dbccc502ae4348466b43]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] A contributing body[^c-1ecbe3966f87f0858a18f1096a2a084b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] must contribute to the fund in each year covered by a rates and adjustments certificate under regulation 77 or 78 the amount appropriate for that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] contributing body[^c-f9922e3fd4e398b13fa96fe0983c726f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] as calculated in accordance with the certificate and paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) During each of those years *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] a contributing body[^c-5cc197357a049739f4962f8f3f24b787] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] must make payments to the fund on account of the amount required for the whole year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Those payments on account must – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^c-24de12f1077f46690b1ead00888f9480] where the contributing body employs active members of the Scheme be paid at the end of the intervals determined under regulation 81 (payments by employing authorities to the Committee) and where the contributing body does not employ active members of the Scheme, be paid at intervals of not greater than 12 months as the Committee may determine; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) equal the appropriate proportion of the whole amount due under paragraph (1) for the year in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4)[^c-5892e6f5675c651fd3994c80bea507cd] A contributing body’s contribution for any year is the sum of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the common percentage of the pay on which contributions have during that year been paid to the fund or admission agreement fund under Part 2 by employees who are active members other than contributions under regulation 17(3) (which may be a nil amount); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any individual adjustment specified for that contributing body for that year or as the case may be, guarantor in the rates and adjustments certificate (which may be positive or negative and which may be expressed as an adjustment to the common percentage rate or a separate absolute amount). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The common percentage is the common rate of employer’s contribution specified in that certificate, expressed as a percentage. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Where an employee – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) is treated, under regulation 16(4), as if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he[^c-ff3689a5d7128cd5ae3d5a750c1c56ce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] had paid contributions; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) has paid contributions during a period of maternity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , paternity or adoption[^c-df192081e339d3e32f5e650a8d29fee1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] absence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

the pay on which the common percentage is calculated is the pay the employee would have received if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] he *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] has not been absent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
