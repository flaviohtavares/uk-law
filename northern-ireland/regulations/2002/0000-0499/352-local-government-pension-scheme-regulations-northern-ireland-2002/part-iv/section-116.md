# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 116

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 116. Recovery or retention where former member has misconduct obligation[^c-e0495662e6c74bc95964fe6ee808b78c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] This regulation applies where a person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) has left an employment, in which he was or had at some time been a member, in consequence of a criminal, negligent or fraudulent act or omission on his part in connection with that employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) has incurred some monetary obligation, arising out of that act or omission, to the body which was his employing authority in that employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) is entitled to benefits under Part II. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The former employing authority may recover or retain out of the fund – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the amount of the monetary obligation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the value at the time of the recovery or retention of all rights in respect of the former employee under the Scheme with respect to his previous membership (as determined by an actuary), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

whichever is less. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The rights specified in paragraph (2)(b) do not include rights enjoyed by virtue of the receipt of a transfer value or credited by virtue of regulation 68(4) (including that regulation as it applies by virtue of regulation 74). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The former employing authority must give the former employee– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) not less than three months' notice of the amount to be recovered or retained under paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a certificate showing the amount recovered or retained, how it is calculated, and the effect on his benefits or prospective benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If there is any dispute over the amount of the monetary obligation specified in paragraph (1)(b), the former employing authority may not recover or retain any amount under paragraph (2) until the obligation is enforceable under an order of a competent court or the award of an arbitrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
