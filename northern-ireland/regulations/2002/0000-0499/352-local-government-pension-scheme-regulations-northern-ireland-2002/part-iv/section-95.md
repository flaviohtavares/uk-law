# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 95

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Interest on late payment of certain benefits[^c-0491a6261c7c11975280992ff4cfa08b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Where all or part of a pension or lump sum payment due under these Regulations or the 2000 Regulations is not paid within the relevant period after the due date, the Committee must pay interest on the unpaid amount to the person to whom it is payable calculated from the due date as provided in regulation 82(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The relevant period – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in the case of a pension is one year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) in the case of a payment made under regulation 41(1) or 142, is the period ending one month after the date on which the Committee receives notification of the member’s death; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) otherwise is one month. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) In the case of a pension the due date is the date on which it becomes payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) In the case of a retirement grant, the due date is the date on which it is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) In the case of a death grant, the due date is the date on which the member dies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) In the case of an ill health grant, the due date is the day after the member ceased to hold his employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) In the case of a payment of a lump sum under regulation 51, 52, 142 or 143 the due date is the day after the member would otherwise become entitled to payment of a pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) In this regulation, references to “member” include a surviving spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , civil partner[^c-31e0cd7bf7ab33967f30bc80c1b2b835] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], eligible child or children or a pension credit member and the reference in paragraph (4) to the “retirement grant” includes the lump sum grant referred to in regulation 133(2)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
