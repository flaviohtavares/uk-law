# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 128

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 128. Community scheme transferees[^key-328edd0c388f6cfa77696884e50dde59]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Community scheme transferees and their surviving spouses *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] , civil partner[^key-13dcb50756a96b29e68fbe991383c93d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc], dependants and children are entitled to such rights under the Scheme as are specified in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) A Community scheme transferee is a person who became employed by a Community institution after having been employed in local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
