# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 96

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. Payments due in respect of deceased persons[^c-8e31a8cce12482764b0d8e05f016c304]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If when a person dies the total amount due to his personal representatives under the Scheme (including anything due to him at his death) (“the amount due”) does not exceed the small payments amount, the Committee may pay the whole or part of the amount due from the fund – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to his personal representatives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to the person, or to or among any one or more of any persons, appearing to the Committee to be beneficially entitled to the estate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

without the production of probate or letters of administration of his estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The small payments amount is the amount specified in any order for the time being in force under section 6 of the Administration of Estates (Small Payments) (Northern Ireland) Act 1967 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00032] and applying in relation to his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Such a payment discharges the Committee from accounting for the amount paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
