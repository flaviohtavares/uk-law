# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 81

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Payments by employing authorities to the Committee[^c-8fd9ad0341d29ee27c84f3c4712f1b21]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Every employing authority must pay to the Committee, on or before such dates falling at intervals of not more than 12 months as the Committee may determine (but in the case of the amounts mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) not later than the time required under Article 49(8) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00025]) –

- (a) all amounts from time to time deducted from the pay of its employees under these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any amount received by it under regulation 17, (by deduction or otherwise) during the interval; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) any extra charge payable under regulation 80 of which it has been notified by the Committee during the interval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Every payment under paragraph (1)(a) shall be accompanied by a statement showing – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the name and pay of each of the employing authority’s employees who is an active member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) which employees are paying voluntary contributions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the amounts which represent deductions from the pay of each of the employees and the periods covered by the deductions, distinguishing amounts representing deductions for voluntary contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The Committee may direct the information mentioned in paragraph (2) to be given to it instead in such form and at such intervals (not exceeding 12 months) as it specifies in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Paragraphs (1) and (2) do not apply to the employing authority which is the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Voluntary contributions are contributions other than those under Part II. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
