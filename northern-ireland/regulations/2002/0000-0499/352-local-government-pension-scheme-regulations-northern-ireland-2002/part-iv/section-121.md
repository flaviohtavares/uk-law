# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 121

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 121. Contracting-out requirements affecting transfers out[^c-cf9c084f21fc34d6c87881986d78c4e0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] There must be deducted from the transfer payment to be made in respect of any person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the amount of any contributions equivalent premium payable pursuant to section 51 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) an amount sufficient to meet the liability in respect of his contracted-out rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But the amount mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) may not be deducted where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the transfer payment is made to an occupational pension scheme which is contracted-out or an appropriate personal pension scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) that scheme’s trustees or managers undertake to accept liability for his contracted-out rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Where the amount mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) is deducted, if the Committee thinks fit, that amount may be used in preserving the liability mentioned in paragraph (2)(b) in the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) Otherwise, it must be used in paying the premium. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) Contracted-out rights, in relation to a member, are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) his and his surviving spouse’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] or civil partner's[^c-0b4ce77f8ffcf5724c2c4158297c21a4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] rights to guaranteed minimum pensions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) his section 5(2B) rights (as defined in regulation 1(2)) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00040].
