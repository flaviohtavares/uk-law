# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 77

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Actuarial valuations and certificates[^c-80465ad316119009e73eca0149af1d4c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must obtain – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) an actuarial valuation of the assets and liabilities of the fund as at 31st March 2004 and in every third year afterwards; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a report by an actuary; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) a rates and adjustments certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Each of those documents must be obtained before the first anniversary of the date (“the valuation date”) as at which the valuation is made or such later date as the Department may agree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) A rates and adjustments certificate is a certificate specifying – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^c-34662e7ad40111e122cf02ec31734678] the common rate of employer’s contribution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any individual adjustment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) the total contribution rate payable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

for each year of the period of three years beginning with 1st April in the year following that in which the valuation date falls. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The common rate of employer’s contribution is the amount which in the actuary’s opinion should be paid to the fund by all bodies whose employees contribute to it so as to secure its solvency, expressed as a percentage of the pay of their employees who are active members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The actuary must have regard – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to the existing and prospective liabilities of the fund arising from circumstances common to all those bodies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to the desirability of maintaining as nearly constant a rate as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5A)[^c-fd3d16b1b635cf0d5588098a711a36db] The actuary must have regard to the Committee’s funding strategy statement published under regulation 76A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6)[^c-b12d10c2897f3e4d7faae6e4414f09d7] An individual adjustment is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) any percentage or amount by which, in the actuary’s opinion contributions at the common rate should in the case of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) a particular contributing body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] a particular guarantor; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] both employing authority and inheriting body if agreed under regulation 78A (apportionment of liabilities), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  be increased or reduced by reason of any circumstances peculiar to that contributing body, guarantor or both of them; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) any other amount (whether or not expressed as a percentage of the pay of their employees who are active members) which, in the actuary’s opinion, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (i) a contributing body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] guarantor; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] both employing authority and inheriting body if agreed under regulation 78A, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

  should pay, by reason of any circumstances peculiar to that contributing body, guarantor or employing authority and inheriting body under regulation 78A or by reason of any liabilities in the Scheme for which responsibility rests with the contributing body, guarantor or both of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) A rates and adjustments certificate must contain a statement as to the assumptions on which the certificate is given as respects – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a)[^c-f717b4e7cc80d298663f72d9ed882ef9] the number of members who will become entitled to payment of pensions under the provisions of the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the amount of liabilities arising in respect of such members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) such other information that the actuary considers relevant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

during the period covered by the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(8) A report under paragraph (1)(b) must contain a statement as to the demographic assumptions used in making the valuation, showing how they relate to the events which have actually occurred in relation to members of the Scheme since the last valuation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(9) The Committee must provide the actuary preparing a valuation or a rates and adjustment certificate with the consolidated final accounts of the fund and such other information as he requests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(10) The Committee must send copies of any valuation, report or certificate under this regulation or revision under regulation 78 – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) to the Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) to each body with employees who contribute to the fund in question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (c) to any other body which is or may become liable to make payments to that fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(11) The Committee must also send to the Department – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a copy of the consolidated final accounts with which the actuary was provided under paragraph (9); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a summary of the assets of the fund at the valuation date (unless such a summary is contained in the report). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
