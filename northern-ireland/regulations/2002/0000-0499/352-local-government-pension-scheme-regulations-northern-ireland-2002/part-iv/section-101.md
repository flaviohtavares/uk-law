# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 101

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 101. Appointment of persons to resolve disputes[^c-0c2ff2bf9f75c2c4a15ea1b87267cb7b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The Committee must appoint a panel of persons it considers to be suitably qualified for the purpose of resolving disagreements in respect of which an application is made under regulation 102. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) For this Chapter the persons appointed under paragraph (1) are “appointed persons”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) An application under regulation 102 may be decided by one or more appointed persons (and references to “the appropriate appointed person”, in relation to any application, are to the appointed person or persons to whom the application in question is referred). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) An application must not be referred to a person who has previously been involved in the subject matter of the disagreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) An appointed person shall hold and vacate office under the terms of his appointment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) But he may resign by notice in writing to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) The Committee shall determine – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the procedure to be followed by the persons appointed by it when exercising their functions as appointed persons; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the manner in which those functions are to be exercised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
