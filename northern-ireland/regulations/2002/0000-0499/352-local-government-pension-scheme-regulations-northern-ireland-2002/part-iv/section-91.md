# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 91

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Pension increases and cash equivalents under the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^c-02feeea75d9fdef23a00db7e2027e836]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter III of Part IV of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00028] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) must be paid from the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
