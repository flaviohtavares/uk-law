# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 97

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 97. Non-assignability[^c-bea4d4fe349afb409192ea2c79df3ba9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] Every benefit to which a person is entitled under the Scheme is payable to or in trust for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) No such benefit is assignable or chargeable with his or any other person’s debts or other liabilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) On the bankruptcy of a person entitled to a benefit under the Scheme no part of the benefit passes to any trustee or other person acting on behalf of the creditors, except in accordance with an income payments order under Article 283 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00033].
