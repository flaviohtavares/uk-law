# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 90

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Deduction and recovery of member’s contributions[^c-fc434793d7d9a4e71179ecc97641299c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] An employing authority may deduct from a person’s pay any contributions payable by him under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) Sums payable under regulation 16(5) or (8)(c) (reserve forces) may be deducted by the member’s former employer from any payment made to him under Part V of the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Protection of Civil Interests) (Northern Ireland) Order 1953 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc][^f00027], to the extent that they are payable in respect of the same period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) The Committee may recover any such sum remaining due and not deducted under paragraph (1) or (2) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) as a simple contract debt in any court of competent jurisdiction; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) by deducting it from any payment by way of benefits to or in respect of the person in question under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) But the sums mentioned in paragraph (2) are only recoverable under paragraph (3) if unpaid for 12 months after the person ceases to perform relevant reserve forces service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) If – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) an employing authority deducts any amount in error from a person’s pay or any other sum due to him in respect of contributions, other than contributions due to be repaid to him by virtue of his having left without any rights under the Scheme, (which are dealt with under regulation 88); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the amount has not been repaid to him before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date of deduction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

the appropriate body must pay him interest on that amount calculated as provided in regulation 82(2), the due date being the date of deduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) Where the employee’s contributions have been paid into the fund, the repayment and interest shall be made out of the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) “The appropriate body” for the purpose of paragraph (5) is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) the Committee where the employee’s contributions have been paid into the fund; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) the person’s employing authority where the employee’s contributions have not yet been paid into the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
