# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 94

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 94. Commencement of pensions[^c-8656bcb687a221f180438c06c9030c6c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The first period for which any retirement pension which is payable immediately on a member leaving any employment is payable begins with the day after the date with which his employment ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The first period for which any retirement pension under regulation 33 is payable begins – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) in a case where he elects *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] under paragraph 1 or paragraph 6(a).[^c-5a2705257d98a2e7fc75eeb8e3fb1549] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] of that regulation, with the day on which he elects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b)[^c-e805fa74997beda5390866897426a256] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-ab0690e7545e4428daf711621c117418] otherwise, unless he elects to defer payment, with his normal retirement date (but any such deferral shall not extend beyond the day before his 75th birthday). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) Any short-term pension payable on the death of a member is payable in respect of a period beginning with the day after the date on which he dies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The first period for which any long-term pension is payable on the death of a member in a case where no short-term pension is payable begins with the day after the date on which he dies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
