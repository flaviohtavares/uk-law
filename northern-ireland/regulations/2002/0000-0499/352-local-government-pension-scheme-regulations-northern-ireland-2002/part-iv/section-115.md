# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 115

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 115. Interim payments directions[^key-68a5f2abb472113401f9da55e3a26a88]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] If – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

- (a) a person leaves an employment in which he was a member, because of an offence in connection with that employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
- (b) a forfeiture certificate has been issued under regulation 114(1) in respect of that offence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

his former employing authority may give an interim payments direction to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) But his former employing authority may not give such a direction if it has given any direction under regulation 114(2) (“a forfeiture direction”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) An interim payments direction is a direction to make interim payments to any person who appears to the former employing authority to be a person who would be entitled to receive payment of a benefit under the Scheme if no forfeiture direction were given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The person to whom payments must be made and the amounts must be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) The amounts must not exceed the amounts which the person specified would be entitled to be paid if no forfeiture direction were given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(6) An interim payments direction is not a decision under regulation 98 as to any person’s entitlement to a benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(7) Payments in accordance with an interim payments direction shall be deemed to be payments in respect of a benefit to which the recipient was entitled (regardless of any contrary forfeiture direction or decision under regulation 98). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
