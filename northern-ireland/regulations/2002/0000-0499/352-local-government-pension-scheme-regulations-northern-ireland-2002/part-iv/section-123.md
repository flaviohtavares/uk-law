# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002, section 123

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 123. Calculation of amount of transfer payment under regulation 122[^c-11d477b37554191ef062cb35c46b0db0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc] The amount of the transfer payment to be paid under regulation 122 is the amount determined by an actuary appointed by the Committee to be equal to the value at the date the members join the new scheme of the actual and potential liabilities payable from the fund which have then accrued in respect of the members and the persons who are or may become entitled to benefits under the Scheme through them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(2) The actuary may make such adjustments as he thinks fit in calculating that amount and, in particular, as respects the period from that date to the date of actual payment of the transfer value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(3) He must specify in his valuation the actuarial assumptions he has used in making it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(4) The employing authority shall bear the costs of determining the appropriate part of the fund and apportioning the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]

(5) But if there is more than one employing authority involved, each shall bear such part of the costs as the actuary determines to be appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e43ee9c1f4dc9422929532242b711adc]
