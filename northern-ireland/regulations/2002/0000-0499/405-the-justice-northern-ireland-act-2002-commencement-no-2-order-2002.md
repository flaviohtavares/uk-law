# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 405 (C. 33)

JUSTICE

The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002

Made

10th December 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 87 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002.

**2.** Section 66 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Display of Royal Arms at courts) shall come into operation on 6 January 2003.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

10th December 2002 9

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order, which is the second to be made under the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, brings into operation section 66 of the Act on 6th January 2003.
