# Electricity (Applications for Consent) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 364

ELECTRICITY

Electricity (Applications for Consent) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

28th November 2002

Coming into operation

1st January 2003

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 39(8) of and paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 8 to the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations–

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Electricity (Applications for Consent) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Electricity (Applications for Consent) (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

(1) The Electricity (Applications for Consent) (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In Regulation 2(1) (fee for application for consent required for construction etc of generating stations) for “£100” substitute “£350”.

(3) In Regulation 2(2) (fee for application for consent required for overhead lines) for “£20” substitute “£60”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 28th November 2002.

James McKeown

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revise upwards the fees which are payable on applications to the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment for its consent under Article 39 of the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 to the construction, extension or operation of a generating station, or under Article 40 of that Order to the installation or keeping installed of an overhead line.
