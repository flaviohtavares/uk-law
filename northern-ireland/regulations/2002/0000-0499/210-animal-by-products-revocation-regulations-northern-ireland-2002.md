# Animal By-Products (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 210

ANIMALS

Animal By-Products (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

29th May 2002

Coming into operation

8th July 2002

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], being a Department designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Communities, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal By-Products (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 8th July 2002.

## 2. Revocations

The Animal By-Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00004] and the Animal By-Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 29th May 2002.

R. S. Johnston

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke the Animal By-Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 and the Animal By-Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998. Those Regulations are re-enacted with amendments in the Animal By-Products Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 209](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/209)).
