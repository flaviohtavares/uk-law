# The Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 45 (C. 3)

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

13th February 2002

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 16(2) of the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2002.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Act” means the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.

## 2. Appointed day

The 22nd February 2002 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 13th February 2002.

P. Simpson

Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety
