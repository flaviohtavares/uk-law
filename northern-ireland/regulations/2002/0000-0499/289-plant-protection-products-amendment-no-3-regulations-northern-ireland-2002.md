# Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 289

PESTICIDES

Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

17th September 2002

Coming into operation

22nd October 2002

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 22nd October 2002.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

(1) Regulation 2(1) of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00004] shall be amended by substituting for the definition of “the Directive” the following definition –

> - “the Directive” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market[^f00005], as amended by –

> - (s) Commission Directive [2002/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0018)[^f00024];

.

## 3. Transitional arrangements for provisional approvals

The Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 shall be modified so that where there is an approval granted under regulation 7(1) (provisional approvals), or extended pursuant to regulation 7(5) –

- (a) the inclusion of the active substances acibenzolar-s-methyl and pymetrozine in Annex I to the Directive does not revoke any such approval in respect of any plant protection product containing either active substance, and that approval shall continue in effect until an approval is granted under regulation 5(1) (standard approvals), or 31st March 2003, whichever is the earlier;
- (b) the inclusion of the active substance flupyrsulfuron-methyl in Annex I to the Directive does not revoke any such approval in respect of any plant protection product containing that active substance, and that approval shall continue in effect until an approval is granted under regulation 5(1) (standard approvals), or 30th November 2002, whichever is the earlier; and
- (c) if the plant protection product contains any of the active substances mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b), together with another active substance not yet placed in Annex I to the Directive, such approval shall continue in effect unless revoked by the Department under regulation 13(5) (applications, reviews, revocations and modifications).

## 4. Revocation

The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00025] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 371](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/371)), as amended by [S.R. 1997 No. 471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/471), [S.R. 1997 No. 507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/507) and [S.R. 2002 No. 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/125) (“the principal Regulations”) which implement in Northern Ireland Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) (O.J. No. L230, 19.8.91, p. 1 to be read with the Corrigenda published in O.J. No. L170, 25.6.92, p. 40) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market (“the Directive”), as amended.

Regulation 2 amends the definition of the Directive in the principal Regulations so as to implement Commission Directives [2001/99/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0099) (O.J. No. L304, 21.11.2001, p. 14) (which adds the active substances glyphosate and thifensulfuron-methyl to Annex I of the Directive on the 1st July 2002), [2001/103/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0103) (O.J. No. L313, 30.11.2001, p. 37) (which adds 2,4-dichlorophenoxy acetic acid to the Annex on 1st October 2002) and [2002/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0018) (O.J. No. L55, 26.2.02, p. 29) (which adds, isoproturon to the Annex on 1st January 2003).

Transitional arrangements are included in regulation 3 to implement relevant saving provisions included in implementing Directives ([2001/87/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0087) for acibenzolar-s-methyl or pymetrozine and [2001/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0049) for flupyrsulfuron-methyl) for the purpose of disapplying regulation 13(5)(a)(i) of the principal Regulations so that provisional approvals are not revoked following the inclusion of these active substances in Annex I to the Directive.

The Regulations revoke the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/125)).
