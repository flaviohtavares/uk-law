# The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 273

PLANT HEALTH

The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

30th August 2002

Coming into operation

23rd September 2002

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00002], 3(1)[^f00003], 3A[^f00004], 3B[^f00005] and 4(1)[^f00006] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00007] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 23rd September 2002.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00008] shall be amended in accordance with Articles 3 to 9.

**3.** In Article 3(1) (Interpretation) for the definition of “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” there shall be substituted –

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 (“the principal Order”) so as to implement certain elements of Commission Directives [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) (O.J. No. L77, 20.3.02, p. 26) and [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (O.J. No. L77, 20.3.02, p. 23) which are not implemented in other legislation. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) amends the protected zones established under Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (O.J. No. L169, 10.7.00, p .1) (“the principal Plant Health Directive”) and scheduled in the Annex to Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) (O.J. No. L127, 9.5.01, p. 38). Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) provides for consequential amendments to certain Annexes of the principal Plant Health Directive.

The amendments in this Order implementing Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) are at Articles 3 to 8 inclusive. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) is implemented by Article 9 which amends Schedule 8 to the principal Order. The principal changes to the protected zones are removal of Great Britain from the protected zone in respect of Beet necrotic yellow vein virus (known as rhizomania), and re-definition of the Italian and Austrian protected zones in respect of the bacterium Erwinia amylovora (Burr.) Winsl. et al (known as fireblight). An amendment is made to the description of Citrus tristeza virus in Schedule 8 by Article 9.

An additional amendment is made to the definition of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) in Article 3(1) of the principal Order so as to incorporate amendments made to it by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (Article 3).
