# The Health and Personal Social Services (2001 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 180 (C. 17)

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Health and Personal Social Services (2001 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made

10th May 2002

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] in exercise of powers conferred on it by section 61(2) of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services (2001 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Act” means the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.

## 2. Appointed day

10th June 2002 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of section 48 of the Act.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 10th May 2002.

A. Hamilton

Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 10th June 2002 section 48 (evasion of charges, etc) of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.
