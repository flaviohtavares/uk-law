# The Specified Animal Pathogens (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 24

Animals

Animal Health

The Specified Animal Pathogens (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

29th January 2010

Coming into operation

22nd February 2010

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 2(3), 5(1), 19, 30(1) and 60(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Specified Animal Pathogens (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 22nd February 2010.

## 2. Amendment

The Specified Animal Pathogens Order (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00003] is amended as set out in this Order.

**3.** In Part 1 of Schedule 1 (Specified animal pathogens) after 35 (Peste des petits ruminants virus) insert——

> **35A.** Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome virus genotype 2

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 29th January 2010.

Colette McMaster

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Specified Animal Pathogens Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No. 336](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/336)) to add a further pathogen (Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome virus genotype 2) to Part 1 of Schedule 1.

This Order has been notified in draft to the European Commission in accordance with Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034), as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048).
