# The Foyle Area (Licensing of Oyster Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 310

Fisheries

The Foyle Area (Licensing of Oyster Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made

9th September 2010

Coming into operation

13th September 2010

The Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission, with the approval of the North/South Ministerial Council, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) and (2) of the Foyle Fisheries Act 1952[^f00001] and section 13(1) and (2) of the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952 [^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Foyle Area (Licensing of Oyster Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and shall come into operation on 13th September 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Foyle Area (Licensing of Oyster Fishing) Regulations 2008

For the Schedule to the Foyle Area (Licensing of Oyster Fishing) Regulations 2008 [^f00004] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule.

Given under the Seal of the Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission on 9th September 2010.
