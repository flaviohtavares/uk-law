# The Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 16

Magistrates’ Courts

The Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

26th January 2010

Coming into operation

1st March 2010

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] after consultation with the Lord Chancellor and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on the 1st March 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2004

In the Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00002], in the Schedule, in Form 1, in the Note, omit “issue a warrant for your arrest or”.

George Conner

John Rea

Dated 26th January 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Anti-social Behaviour Orders) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2004 to remove the reference in the note to Form 1, to the power of the court to issue a warrant of arrest for non-appearance by a defendant.
