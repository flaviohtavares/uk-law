# The Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 416 (C. 24)

Family Law

Child Support

The Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

13th December 2010

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 41(1) of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

(2) In this Order, unless the context requires otherwise, any reference to a numbered section or Schedule is a reference to a section of, or Schedule to, the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Appointed day

20th December 2010 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of section 16 (orders preventing avoidance).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 13th December 2010

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation a provision of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

Article 2 brings into operation section 16 (orders preventing avoidance) on 20th December 2010.
