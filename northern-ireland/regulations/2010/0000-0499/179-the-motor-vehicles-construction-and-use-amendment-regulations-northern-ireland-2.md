# The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 179

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

7th May 2010

Coming into operation

7th June 2010

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 55(1) and (2) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 7<sup>th</sup> June 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

(1) The table in regulation 7(1) of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] (width) is amended as follows.

(2) For the words in column 2 of item 4 substitute “A trailer drawn by a motor vehicle other than a motor cycle.”.

(3) Omit items 5 to 7.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 7<sup>th</sup> May 2010.

Deirdre Kenny

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 7 of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”) specifies the maximum permitted widths of road vehicles. It is an offence to tow a trailer which exceeds the relevant width. A maximum width of 2.3 metres is (with certain exceptions) specified in the case of a trailer drawn by a motor vehicle having a maximum gross weight of 3500 kg or less.

These Regulations amend the 1999 Regulations so that a maximum width of 2.55 metres applies to all types of trailer except for a trailer drawn by a motor cycle. 2.55 metres is the width referred to in section 7.3.2.1 of Annex I of Directive [97/27/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0027) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22<sup>nd</sup> July 1997 relating to the masses and dimensions of certain categories of motor vehicles and their trailers (O.J. No. L 233, 25.8.1997, p.1).

The Department of the Environment has produced an Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment, showing the effect this instrument will have on the costs of business and public sectors. These documents are available from the Road Safety Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB or may be accessed via the Office of Public Sector Information website at www.opsi.gov.uk.
