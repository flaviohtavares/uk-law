# The Home Repair Assistance Grant (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 422

Housing

The Home Repair Assistance Grant (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

9th December 2010

Coming into operation

7th February 2011

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 109 and 148(1) of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Home Repair Assistance Grant (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 7 February 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Home Repair Assistance Grant Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

(1) In Regulation 1 (2) of the Home Repair Assistance Grant Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] after the definition of “housing benefit” add—

- “(income related) employment and support allowance” means (income related) employment and support allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007;” and

(2) After the definition of “income-based jobseeker’s allowance” omit the definition of “pension credit”.

**3.** In Regulation 2 of the Home Repair Assistance Grant Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 after “is in receipt of” add—

- “(income related) employment and support allowance,”.

**4.** In Paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the Home Repair Assistance Grant Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 after “is in receipt of” add—

- “(income related) employment and support allowance,”.

Sealed with the official seal of the Department for Social Development on 9 December 2010

Heather Cousins

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Home Repair Assistance Grant Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 which deal with entitlement to home repair assistance under Chapter IV of Part III of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, the manner of making an application for such assistance, the content of, and the procedure for dealing with, such applications.

Regulation 2 adds a definition of (income related) employment and support allowance and removes an unnecessary definition on pension credit.

Regulation 3 adds (income related) employment and support allowance to the list of benefits that the Executive must be satisfied the applicant or his partner is in receipt of before entertaining an application for assistance.

Regulation 4 adds (income related) employment and support allowance to the list of benefits which if the applicant or his partner is in receipt of must be included in the application.
