# The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation Scheme, Compensation for Premature Retirement and Additional Voluntary Contributions), and Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Amendment of regulation 31

After paragraph (6) of regulation 31 (Contributions by employing authorities: general), add—

> (7) If a non-GP provider is—
>
> - (a) an employing authority which is a GMS practice or an APMS contractor; or
> - (b) a shareholder in such an employing authority,
>
> that non-GP provider must pay contributions, under this regulation to the Host Board.
>
> - This is subject to paragraph (8).
>
> (8) If a non-GP provider is a shareholder or partner in one or more employing authority referred to in paragraph (7), each employing authority must pay contributions under this regulation on any pensionable earnings it pays to the non-GP provider or, as the case may be, on the non-GP provider’s share of the partnership profits, to the Host Board.
