# The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation Scheme, Compensation for Premature Retirement and Additional Voluntary Contributions), and Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Amendment of regulation 70

(1) Regulation 70 (Amount of pensions under regulation 67: pensioner members) is amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) For paragraph (3), substitute—

> (3) For the purposes of paragraph (1) any reduction in the rate of the member’s pension under—
>
> - (a) Chapter 8 of this Part;
> - (b) regulation 58; or
> - (c) regulation 136L,
>
> is ignored.
>
> (3A) For the purpose of paragraph (2)—
>
> - (a) any reduction in the rate of the member’s pension under—
>   - (i) regulation 48,
>   - (ii) regulation 58, or
>   - (iii) regulation 136L; and
> - (b) any increase in the rate of the member’s pension under—
>   - (i) regulation 47, or
>   - (ii) regulation 136U,
>
> is ignored.

.

(3) Omit paragraph (8).
