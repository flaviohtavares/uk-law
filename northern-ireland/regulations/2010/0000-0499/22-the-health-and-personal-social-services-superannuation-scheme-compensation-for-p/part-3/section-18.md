# The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation Scheme, Compensation for Premature Retirement and Additional Voluntary Contributions), and Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Amendment of regulation 10

In paragraph (1) of regulation 10 (Meaning of “qualifying service”)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (d), omit “and”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (f), insert—

  > - ; and
  >   - (a) in the case of a 2008 Section Optant, any period of qualifying service the member is entitled to count under Chapter 10 of this Part.
