## 42. Amendment of regulation 76

In regulation 76 (Amount of children’s pension under regulation 74: deceased active members and deceased non-contributing members), at the end of paragraph (4) insert (as full out words) “In the case of a 2008 Section Optant, this is subject to regulation 136X.”.
