# The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation Scheme, Compensation for Premature Retirement and Additional Voluntary Contributions), and Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation Scheme, Compensation for Premature Retirement and Additional Voluntary Contributions), and Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010.

(2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 26th February 2010 and, save as provided in paragraphs (3) and (4), shall have effect from 1st October 2009.

(3) The following regulations shall have effect from 1st April 2008—

- (a) regulation 7;
- (b) regulation 8;
- (c) regulation 19(4) insofar as it relates to new sub-paragraph (11)(a) of regulation 15 of the Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002];
- (d) regulation 62;
- (e) regulation 87(2) insofar as it relates to new sub-paragraph (2)(b) of regulation 212 of the Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008;
- (f) regulation 107.

(4) The following regulations shall have effect from 1st April 2009—

- (a) regulation 3;
- (b) regulation 4(a);
- (c) regulation 14;
- (d) regulation 16(b) and (d);
- (e) regulation 33(2);
- (f) regulation 56(3) and (5)(b);
- (g) regulation 74(2).
