The Transfer Values (Disapplication) Regulations (Northern *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] Ireland) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] 2010

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 89(1B)(aa), 97F(6A), 177(2) and (3) and 178(1) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00001] and now vested in it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-96a7a198fb28b40fd218afdf5679629e][^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Transfer Values (Disapplication) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 5th July 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(2) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

- “incapacity” means physical or mental impairment which renders a member of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] incapable of carrying on any occupation, where that member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
  - (a) provides evidence to the scheme administrator from a registered medical practitioner that that member is (and will continue to be) suffering from that physical or mental impairment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
  - (b) certifies that that member has in fact ceased to carry on any occupation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
- “normal minimum pension age” has the meaning given in section 279(1) of the Finance Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00003];
- “the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]” means the pension scheme established by the Secretary of State under section 67(1) of the Pensions Act 2008 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00004];
- “scheme administrator” has the same meaning as in section 270 of the Finance Act 2004; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
- “the transfer value provisions” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA (transfers and contribution refunds)[^key-991d80658f1e162d6ca7186596d749d6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] and Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] of Part IVA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00005] (requirements relating to pension credit benefit); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
- “the Trustee” means the trustee corporation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00006] established under section 75 of the Pensions Act 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00007] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(4) In these Regulations any reference to a numbered section or Part is a reference to the section or Part of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 bearing that number. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

## 2. Disapplication of the transfer value provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f7a9b5d081a2b2ca5556c2d387e127fa][^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the transfer value provisions do not apply in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd].

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply (and so the transfer value provisions apply in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]) when a member of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] reaches normal minimum pension age and notifies the Trustee of that member’s intention to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

- (a) exercise a right to the entirety of a cash equivalent acquired under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] section 90(2)[^key-334492c42ddc44b0bcf55b7b91be7f08] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] in one of the ways listed in section 91(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00009], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
- (b) require the Trustee to use an amount equal to the entirety of the cash equivalent of that member’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] pension credit rights[^key-99915aa78a4122f83451803cb29d1bf8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] pursuant to section 97F (power to give transfer notice) for one or more of the authorised purposes listed in section 97F(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd][^f00010].

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply (and so the transfer value provisions apply in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]) when the Trustee is satisfied that a member is suffering from incapacity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

These Regulations disapply Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] IV *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] of Part IV, and Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] of Part IVA, of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (“the transfer value provisions”) which entitle members of occupational or personal pension schemes to acquire a right to a cash equivalent of any benefits which have accrued to or in respect of them. Those Chapters allow transfers out from pension funds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

Regulation 2(1) disapplies the transfer value provisions in respect of the pension scheme established by the Secretary of State under section 67(1) of the Pensions Act 2008 (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]”).

Regulation 2(2) provides that the transfer value provisions apply in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] when a member reaches normal minimum pension age and notifies the trustee corporation of their intention to take a right to a cash equivalent in certain ways. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]

Regulation 2(3) provides that the transfer value provisions also apply in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] the Scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd] when the trustee corporation is satisfied that a member is suffering from incapacity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c41ab5ecc0b1e5328fa50a9a8a7a5edd]
