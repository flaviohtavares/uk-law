# The Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> Text as at 2016-02-14.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force.**

2010 No. 355

Agriculture

[^key-8d221f6bc13262f3255cec0c52bc0133]The Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

*26*th October 2010

Coming into operation

23rd November 2010

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 66(1), 68(1), 74A(1), (2) and (4) and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970, as read with regulation 14 of the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000.

In so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers in the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above, the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes these Regulations in exercise of its powers as a department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, measures in the veterinary and phytosanitary fields for the protection of public health and measures relating to feed produced for or fed to food-producing animals, as read with paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to that Act.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety that it is expedient for references to the Annexes to the EU instruments mentioned in regulation 2(3) to be construed as references to those Annexes as they may be amended from time to time.

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety or, in the case of provisions relating to feed for non food-producing animals, of section 84(1) of the Agriculture Act 1970.

## PART 1 — Introductory and general

### 1. Citation and commencement[^key-5316c061d172843d606086d0925ea10d]

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and come into operation on 23rd November 2010.

### 2. Interpretation[^key-24885654904a12cb2ecdbe937e987e9b]

(1) In these Regulations —

- “the Act” means the Agriculture Act 1970;
- “Directive 82/475” means Commission Directive [82/475/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0475) laying down the categories of feed materials which may be used for the purposes of labelling compound feedingstuffs for pet animals[^f00009];
- “Directive 2002/32” means Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council on undesirable substances in animal feed[^f00010];
- “Regulation 1831/2003” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition[^f00011];
- “Directive 2008/38” means Commission Directive [2008/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0038) establishing a list of intended uses of animal feedingstuffs for particular nutritional purposes[^f00012];
- “Regulation 767/2009” means Regulation [(EC) No. 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the placing on the market and use of feed, amending European Parliament and Council Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) and repealing Council Directive [79/373/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0373), Commission Directive [80/511/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0511), Council Directives [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471), [83/228/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0228), [93/74/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0074), [93/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0113) and [96/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0025) and Commission Decision [2004/217/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0217)[^f00013];
- [^key-f1f6f77d4c9080aa467725d3a9e9b371]...

(2) Any expression used in these Regulations and in Regulation 1831/2003 or Regulation 767/2009 has the meaning in these Regulations that it bears in the Regulation concerned.

(3)[^key-8bf11bac5090571e60e1489aba23ee33] Any reference to an Annex to Directive 82/475, Directive 2002/32, Directive 2008/38 or Regulation 767/2009 is a reference to that Annex as it may be amended from time to time.

(4) These Regulations do not apply to any feed additive in category (d) or (e) of Article 6(1) of Regulation 1831/2003, with the exception of those in the functional groups listed in paragraph 4(a), (b) and (c) of Annex 1 to that Regulation[^f00015].

(5) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00016] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

  > - “Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831)” means Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition;”

  ; and
- (b) for subsection (2) substitute the following —

  > (2) For the purposes of this Part of this Act material shall be treated as sold for use as a fertiliser or feeding stuff whether it is sold to be so used by itself or as an ingredient in something which is to be so used.

  .

(3) In section 68 (duty of seller to give statutory statement) —

- (a) after subsection (5) insert the following subsection —

  > (5A) Nothing in subsections (1) to (5) applies to anyone to whom the requirements of Article 16 of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) (labelling and packaging of feed additives and premixtures) or of Chapter 4 of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) (labelling, presentation and packaging) apply.

  ;
- (b) after subsection (6) add the following subsection —

  > (7) Failure to comply with the labelling requirements of Article 16 of Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) or of Chapter 4 of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) shall not invalidate a contract of sale, but such labelling shall, regardless of any contract or notice to the contrary, have effect as a warranty by the person who gives it that the particulars contained in it are correct.

  .

(4) In section 69 (marking of material prepared for sale), in subsection (1) omit the words “or feeding stuff”.

(5) In section 70 (use of names or expressions with prescribed meanings) —

- (a) subsection (1)(a) in so far as it applies in relation to feeding stuffs shall apply as if the expression “under Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767)” were substituted for the expression “by regulations made for the purposes of this section”; and
- (b) after subsection (5) add the following subsection —

  > (6) Nothing in subsections (2) to (4) of this section shall apply to anyone to whom the labelling requirements of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) apply.

  .

(6) In section 71 (particulars to be given of certain attributes if claimed to be present) —

- (a) subsection (4) in so far as it applies in relation to feeding stuffs shall apply as if —
  - (i) the expression “the requirements of Article 13 of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767)” were substituted for the expression “subsection (1) of this section”; and
  - (ii) “that Article” were substituted for “that subsection”;
- (b) after subsection (5) add the following subsection —

  > (6) Nothing in subsections (1) to (3) of this section shall apply to anyone to whom Article 13 (claims) of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) applies.

  .

(7) Sections 73 and 73A shall cease to have effect.

(8) In section 74 (limits of variation) after subsection (2) add the following subsection —

> (3) Nothing in subsection (2) shall apply to anyone to whom Article 11.5 and Annex IV (permitted tolerances) of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) applies.

.

(9) In section 74A, subsection (3) shall not apply to regulations concerning feed.
