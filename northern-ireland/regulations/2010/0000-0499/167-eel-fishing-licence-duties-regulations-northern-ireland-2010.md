# Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 167

Fisheries

Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

27th April 2010

Coming into operation

1st June 2010

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure [^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 15(1) and 19(1) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^f00002] and now vested in it [^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2010.

## 2. Licence duties

The licence duties payable to the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure in respect of licences for the use of fishing engines for the taking of eels specified in column 1 of the Schedule are the amounts specified opposite in column 2 of the Schedule.

## 3. Revocation

The Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^f00004] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 27th April 2010.

M. Cory

A senior officer of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure

Regulation 2

| Fishing engine at the existing eel weir at Upper Row, Portna | £1770.00 |

| Long line having not more than 1,200 hooks | £93.00 |
| Long line having more than 1,200 hooks | £129.00 |
| Draft net | £129.00 |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the amount of licence duties payable to the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure in respect of licences which may be issued by the Department for the use of fishing engines for the taking of eels.

The Eel Fishing (Licences Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 are revoked.
