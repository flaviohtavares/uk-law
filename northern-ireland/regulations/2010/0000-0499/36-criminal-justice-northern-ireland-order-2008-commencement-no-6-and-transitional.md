# Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement No.6 and Transitional Provision) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 36 (C. 1)

Criminal Law

Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement No.6 and Transitional Provision) Order 2010

Made

13th February 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1 (4), and 100 (4) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 [^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Order 2010.

(2) In this Order “the 2008 Order” means the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement

Article 92 of the 2008 Order shall come into operation on 26th February 2010.

## 3. Transitional Provision

Article 92 of the 2008 Order applies in relation to warrants referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph (2) of that Article issued on or after 26th February 2010.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

13th February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the provisions of Article 92 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 on 26th February 2010. Article 92 makes provision regarding the proving of the execution of arrest warrants.
