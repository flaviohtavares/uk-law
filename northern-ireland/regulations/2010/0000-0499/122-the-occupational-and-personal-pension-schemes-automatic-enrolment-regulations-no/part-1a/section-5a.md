# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 5A

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5A. Exemption of European employers[^c-484ea22351226ca704d54a89a14d862b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Sections 2(1), 3(2), 5(2), 7(3), 9(2) and 54 (employer’s obligations regarding membership of a qualifying scheme) do not apply in relation to a person’s employment of an individual in relation to whom the person is a European employer.
