# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 12

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Automatic re-enrolment dates[^c-02052a600f481104447ae040daad5632]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4)[^c-382af6e7aaef64bc13dcabe78d2433e3], the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5—

- (a) is the date chosen at the discretion of the employer, within a period beginning 3 months before, and ending at the end of the period of 3 months beginning with,[^c-db4ca071654aecdb8d4046fc8c90f91a] the third anniversary of the staging date, and
- (b) thereafter, is the date chosen at the discretion of the employer, within a period beginning 3 months before, and ending at the end of the period of 3 months beginning with, the third anniversary of the date chosen for the previous automatic re-enrolment date.

(2)[^c-4dc27faa58ebd3d07c5aa2f40f3438bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In a case under section 6(4), the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5 is the day after the day on which the jobholder ceases to be an active member of the scheme.[^c-6aea6ba28418f4f6173882e7ff321795]

(4) In a case under section 6(5), the automatic re-enrolment date for the purposes of section 5 is the first day on which all the requirements of section 1(1) (jobholders) are met (so that the person is a jobholder from that date).
