## 19. Opting out[^key-56e45b6c689ebc23e037652ac6349521]

*Extent: N.I.*

The arrangements in regulations 9 and 10 are the arrangements for the purposes of section 8 (jobholder’s right to opt out) in relation to a jobholder who has given an employer an opt in notice, but with the modification that in regulation 9(1) for “section 3(2)” substitute “section 7(3)”.
