## 2. Enrolment information[^c-c85fa5ed5d5e5bae77f7e675e559e756]

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations “**enrolment information**” means the information described in paragraphs 1 to 15 and 24[^c-0dd8fa899e646432080635f4dc59e467] of Schedule 2.
