# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 47A

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47A. Meaning of “provider”[^c-40f9160d1b0c178951adb4a3fee0e6e0]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of “provider” in section 78 (interpretation of Part), a provider is a person whose normal business includes the provision of personal pensions.
