# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 44

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Description of an occupational pension scheme with its main administration outside the United Kingdom[^key-3b274cf5bcf72220a00165bb2f20f54a][^M_F_18ca24d9-527d-4163-8229-85e806c26052]

*Extent: N.I.*

A pension scheme that has its main administration outside the United Kingdom[^key-4db3bfe46d751dd699757248b2d2e92e] is an occupational pension scheme for the purposes of section 18(c) (occupational pension schemes) if it is an occupational pension scheme within the meaning of section 1(1) of the 1993 Act[^f00015].
