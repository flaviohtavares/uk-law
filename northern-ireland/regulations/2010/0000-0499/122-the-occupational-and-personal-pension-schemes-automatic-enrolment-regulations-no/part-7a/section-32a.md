# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 32A

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32A. Effect of a certificate under section 28[^c-dffb89b4fcb87c9c0f583cb7b572d605]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 32H, a scheme to which section 28 (certification that quality requirement or alternative requirement is satisfied) applies is to be taken to satisfy the relevant quality requirement in relation to each of an employer's relevant jobholders if the certificate in question is given in accordance with regulations 32B to 32D in relation to the employer and those jobholders.
