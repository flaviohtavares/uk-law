# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, section 37

> Text as at 2024-01-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Test scheme: requirements to revalue accrued benefits and increase pensions in payment[^key-10b6d84c1480b787cca30577036ce348]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies for the purposes of section 23(1)(c)[^key-30fdcd736cbd62f9e5ec7065a9bc60d7] (test scheme).

(2) The following must be satisfied in relation to a test scheme—

- (a) the requirements of section 80[^key-2a1e68803287ba35baf6f03eb1aa7224] of the 1993 Act (basis of revaluation), and
- (b) the requirements of Article 51 of the 1995 Order[^f00013] (annual increase in rate of pension) in relation to a scheme which provides for a member to be entitled to a pension commencing at the appropriate age and continuing for life[^key-7a4bc5f7100c599ce2e1fe0fadd05752].

(3)[^key-4f73ed4e7786ab8d99e71ae2e5f8faf5] For the purposes of paragraph (2)(a)—

- (a) a test scheme which falls within section 23(2)(a) and a test scheme which falls within regulation 39A(2) must satisfy the requirements of section 80 of the 1993 Act by reference to the final salary method, and
- (b) a test scheme to which regulation 39A(3) applies must satisfy the requirements of section 80 of the 1993 Act by reference to the average salary method or the final salary method.
