# The Biosecurity Guidance (Specification of Brucellosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 176

Animals

The Biosecurity Guidance (Specification of Brucellosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

6th May 2010

Coming into operation

1st June 2010

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Biosecurity Guidance (Specification of Brucellosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2010.

## 2. Biosecurity guidance

Brucellosis is hereby specified for the purposes of Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 6<sup>th</sup> May 2010.

Colette McMaster

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies brucellosis for the purposes of Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, enabling the Department to introduce statutory biosecurity guidance for that disease.
