# The Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 309 (C. 18)

Forestry

The Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

8th September 2010

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(1) of the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010.

## 2. Appointed Day

13th September 2010 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 8th September 2010.

David Small

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
