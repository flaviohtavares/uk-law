# Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 285

Landlord And Tenant

Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

24th August 2010

Coming into operation

1st October *2010*

The Department for Social Development, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 55(5) and (6) of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

The Department has accepted the recommendation of the Rent Officer made under Article 55(4) of that Order that the rents referred to in Article 55(1) of that Order should be increased.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1 October 2010.

## 2. Increase of registered rents

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) the rents registered under Part IV of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 during the period beginning on 2nd April 2007 and ending on 4th April 2010 shall be increased by 1.9 percent.

(2) The provision at paragraph (1) does not apply to a rent entered on the Register of Rents unless a certificate of fitness has been issued except where such a certificate is not required.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 24th August 2010.

Heather Cousins

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order increases the rents registered with the rent officer for Northern Ireland under Part IV of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 during the period beginning on 2nd April 2007 and ending on 4th April 2010 by 1.9 percent.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks’ notice of the increase in accordance with Article 49 of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.
