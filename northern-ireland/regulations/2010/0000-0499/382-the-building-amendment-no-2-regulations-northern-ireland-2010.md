# The Building (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3, 5(1), (2) and (3) of, and paragraphs 4, 6, 9, 13, 17, 17C, 17D and 22 of Schedule 1 to the Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5][^f00001] and now vested in it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5][^f00002], and after consultation with the Building Regulations Advisory Committee and such other bodies as appear to be representative of the interests concerned *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5][^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

These regulations may be cited as the Building (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, and shall come into operation on 31st December 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

In these regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5][^f00004].

## 3. Transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

(1) The principal Regulations shall continue to apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

- (a) plans deposited or a building notice given in accordance with the principal Regulations before the commencement of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
- (b) building work carried out in accordance with such plans or such building notice with or without any departure or deviation from those plans or that building notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
- (c) building work completed before the commencement of these Regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

as if the amendments effected by these Regulations had not been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

(2) In paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

- (a) “building notice” means a notice given under regulation A10 (Giving of notices and deposit of plans) of the principal Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
- (b) “building work” has the same meaning as in the principal Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

## 4. Amendment of Part A (Interpretation and general) of the principal Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

(1) After regulation A10 (Giving of notices and deposit of plans) there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

> **A10A. Deposit of plans for the erection of two or more buildings that are or contain dwellings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]**
>
> (1) This regulation shall apply only to plans deposited with a district council for the erection of buildings that are or contain dwellings, where work has not commenced on each building within three years of the date on which the plans were deposited. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
>
> (2) For the purposes of this regulation, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
>
> - (a) plans have been deposited in accordance with regulation A10; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
> - (b) those plans are for the erection of two or more buildings that are or contain dwellings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
>
> then if declaring the plans to be of no effect under Article 19 of the principal Order, a district council shall consider each building on the deposited plans as if a separate deposit of plans had been made for each building. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]
>
> (3) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

These Regulations amend the Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 (the principal Regulations). They do not apply to building work which has been completed, or for which plans or building notices have been deposited with a district council before the coming into operation of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

These Regulations amend Part A of, and Schedules 2 and 5 to, the principal Regulations as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

A new regulation A10A introduces provisions relating to applications for two or more buildings that are or contain dwellings. Under this regulation, where work has not been commenced on any individual building on such an application, a district council may declare the plans for those particular buildings to be of no effect under the provisions of Article 19 of the Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (as amended). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

Regulations A11(5)(e) and (f) have been amended to clarify that a Target carbon dioxide Emissions Rate, a Building carbon dioxide Emissions Rate or Dwelling carbon dioxide Emissions Rate shall be submitted where these have been calculated to demonstrate compliance when a building undergoes a material change of use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

Regulation A13(2)(b)(ii) has been amended to reflect changes to other fire safety legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

Schedule 2 has been amended to reflect consequential changes brought about by amendments to regulations A11 and A13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

Schedule 5 now takes account of the application of Amendments Booklet AMD5 to Technical Booklets E, F1, F2 and L. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared. Copies may be downloaded from *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5] www.buildingregulationsni.gov.uk *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5], or alternatively, hard copies may be requested from Building Standards Branch, Department of Finance and Personnel, Level 5, Causeway Exchange, 1-7 Bedford Street, Belfast, BT2 7EG. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5]

These Regulations were notified in draft to the European Commission in accordance with Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5] [98/34/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5], as amended by Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5] [98/48/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048)[^key-9024ffd0e7ef920268d070db288cabf5].
