# The Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 328 (C. 20)

Fire And Rescue Services

The Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

20th September 2010

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

In so far as not already in operation, the day appointed for the coming into operation of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 is 15th November 2010.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 20 September 2010.

Dr Bernie Stuart

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 15th November 2010 the provisions of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 which are not already in operation.
