# The Penalty Charges (Exemption from Criminal Proceedings) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 183 

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Penalty Charges (Exemption from Criminal Proceedings) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

17th May 2010

Coming into operation

1st July 2010

The Department for Regional Development in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 4(7) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001] makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Penalty Charges (Exemption from Criminal Proceedings) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st July 2010.

## 2. Amendment

In regulation 2(1) of the Penalty Charges (Exemption from Criminal Proceedings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] for sub paragraph (e) substitute—

> - (e) an offence under Article 27A(2) of the 1997 Order[^f00003] (contravention of a taxi regulation order)

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 17th May 2010

(L.S.)

D J Millar

A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations further amend the Penalty Charges (Exemption from Criminal Proceedings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 by substituting a new offence provision relating to taxi stands. The new provision is an offence under Article 27A(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
