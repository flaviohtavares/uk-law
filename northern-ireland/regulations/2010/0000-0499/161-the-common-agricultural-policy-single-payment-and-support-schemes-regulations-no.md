Made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community and in relation to the promotion of rural development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), hereby makes the following Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 1. Title and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]     15 May 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

(2) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

- “Commission Regulation 1120/2009” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1120/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1120)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00004] laying down detailed rules for the implementation of the single payment scheme provided for in Title *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No. 73/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] establishing common rules for direct support schemes for farmers under the common agricultural policy and establishing certain support schemes for farmers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]
- “Commission Regulation 1122/2009” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1122/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1122)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00005] laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation 73/2009 as regards cross-compliance, modulation and the integrated administration and control system, under the direct support schemes for farmers provided for in the Council Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]
- “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No. 73/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00006] establishing common rules for direct support schemes for farmers under the common agricultural policy and establishing certain support schemes for farmers, amending Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No 1290/2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1290)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd], [(EC) No 247/2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0247)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd], [(EC) No 378/2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0378)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] and repealing Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No 1782/2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd];
- “farmer” has the meaning given by Article 2(a) of the Council Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]
- “holding” has the meaning given by Article 2(b) of the Council Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]
- “competent authority” has the meaning given to it in regulation 3 of The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Integrated Administration and Control System) Regulations 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00007]

(3) Other expressions used in these Regulations that are also used in the Council Regulation, Commission Regulation 1120/2009 or Commission Regulation 1122/2009 have the meaning they bear in those instruments.; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 3. Regional application (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Northern Ireland shall be a region for the purposes of Article 46 of the Council Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 4. Minimum size of holding to establish entitlements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

For the purposes of Article 28(1) of the Council Regulation, the minimum size of a holding for which the establishment of payment entitlements may be requested shall be 0.3 hectares. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 5. Minimum claim (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

The minimum claim size pursuant to Article 28(1) of the Council Regulation is €100. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 6. Date on which eligible land must be at the farmer’s disposal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

In relation to any year in which the farmer makes a declaration in respect of parcels pursuant to Article 35(1) of the Council Regulation, the date on which those parcels must be at the farmer’s disposal is 15 May. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 7. Transfers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-54021dfdef3039dac21b71a83433c88a][^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

For the purposes of Article 12(3) of Commission Regulation 1120/2009 , a transferor of a payment entitlement must notify the transfer to the competent authority that allocated that entitlement by 2 May 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 8. Additional Modulation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

The rates in the third column of the Schedule are the voluntary modulation referred to in Article 1 of Council Regulation ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]) No 378/2007 (laying down rules for voluntary modulation of direct payments provided for in Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No 1782/2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00008]) (the rates in the second column are the compulsory rates of modulation provided for in Article 7 of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No 73/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] and are provided for the sake of completeness). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

## 9. Revocations and Savings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

(1) The following Regulations are revoked— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

- (a) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00009];
- (b) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00010]
- (c) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00011]
- (d) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Set-aside) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00012]
- (e) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Set-aside) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd][^f00013]

(2) The Regulations referred to in paragraph (1) continue to apply in relation to a single application made before the coming into force of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

(This is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

These Regulations make provision in Northern Ireland for the administration of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] [(EC) No. 73/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] (O.J. No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd] L 30, 31.01.2009, p 16), and other associated Community instruments in relation to direct support schemes under the Common Agricultural Policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 3 defines Northern Ireland as a region for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 4 prescribes the minimum size of a holding in respect of which the establishment of entitlements may be requested. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 5 sets the minimum claim size. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 6 fixes the date on which parcels used to claim under the Single Payment Scheme must be at the disposal of farmers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 7 provides that the transfer of payment entitlements must be communicated by the transferor to the relevant competent authority within the time limits specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 8 specifies the additional modulation deductions which will be made from direct payments payable to farmers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]

Regulation 9 revokes the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2005 and its amendments. It also revokes the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment Scheme (Set-aside) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2005 and its amendments. Those Regulations will continue to apply for certain purposes to single payment applications made before the coming into force of this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9777ccbada99fab7879db99148c2b4fd]
