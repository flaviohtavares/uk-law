# The Personal Accounts Delivery Authority Winding Up (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 212

Pensions

The Personal Accounts Delivery Authority Winding Up (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

11th June 2010

Coming into operation

5th July 2010

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 65(6) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has made an Order[^f00002] containing provision under section 23(7) of the Pensions Act 2007[^f00003].

Accordingly the Department for Social Development makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Personal Accounts Delivery Authority Winding Up (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 5th July 2010.

## 2. Repeals

The following provisions are repealed—

- (a) section 17 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00004] (management of the Authority);
- (b) sections 65(1) to (5) and 66 to 68 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (functions of the Authority).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 11th June 2010

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which corresponds to provision in an Order ([S.I. 2010/911](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/911)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under section 23(7) of the Pensions Act 2007, repeals section 17 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 and sections 65(1) to (5) and 66 to 68 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 which relate to the management and functions of the Personal Accounts Delivery Authority.
