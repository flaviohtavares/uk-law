These Regulations make the following principal changes. These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Cross Compliance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889][S.R. 2005 No. 6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/6)[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]) (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] principal Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]”). The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] principal Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] make provision in Northern Ireland for the administration of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] [(EC) No. 73/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] (OJ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] L 30, 31.1.2009, p.16) (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]”), [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1120/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1120)[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] (OJ. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] L 316, 2.10.2009 p.1) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1122/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1122)[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] (O.J. No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] L316, 2.10.2009, p.65) in relation to Cross Compliance under the system of direct support schemes (including the Single Payment Schemes) under the Common Agricultural Policy, which came into force on 1 January 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]

The Regulations establish the requirements associated with Good Agricultural and Environmental Condition ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]GAEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]) requirement 7, Irrigation Authorisations. These are set out in regulation 2 paragraph (6)(d) of the Regulations. In addition regulation 2(6)(a) makes minor amendments to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] GAEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] 4 Undergrazing, regulation 2(6)(b) amends *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] GAEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] 5 Field Boundaries and regulation 2(6)(c) amends *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] GAEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889] 6 Protection of Semi Natural Habitats and Historic Monuments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]

The Regulations also delete the reference to Commission Regulation 796/2004 which has been replaced by Commission Regulation 1122/2009 and inserts a definition for Commission Regulation 1120/2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-29993b727b3d88a3b07c891323f0a889]
