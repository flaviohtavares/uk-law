The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][^f00001], being the Department concerned *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][^f00003] (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] 1972 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]”) and Articles 17(1), (2), (3) and (5) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][^f00004], 40(2) and (4) and 55(2) of, and paragraphs 1(1), (4) and (5), 3(1), 12(1), 14(1) and 15 of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][^f00005] (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] 1978 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]”).

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-335647e75de78f58bc8a2ef4893dd963][^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Biocidal Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and come into operation on 19th May 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**1.** These Regulations amend the Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][S.R. 2001 No. 422 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2001/422)[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]) (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] 2001 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]”) to make further provision as regards Northern Ireland for the implementation of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] [98/8/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0008)[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] of the European Parliament and Council (O.J. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] L123, 24.4.98, p.1.) concerning the placing of biocidal products on the market. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**2.** These Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

- (a) implement Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] [2009/107/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0107)[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009 amending Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] [98/8/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0008)[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] concerning the placing of biocidal products on the market as regards the extension of certain time periods (“the Amending Directive”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]
- (b) update references and make minor corrections in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] 2001 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]
- (c) take account of developments that require further amendment to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] 2001 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6].

**3.** The main changes made by these Regulations are set out in the following paragraphs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**4.** Regulation 2(2) amends the definitions of “new active substance” and “placing on the market”. It also inserts a definition of “the fifth review regulation” and removes the definition of “the second review regulation”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**5.** Regulation 2(3) makes a minor amendment and corrects a cross reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**6.** Regulation 2(4) amends time periods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**7.** Regulation 2(5) extends the data protection periods for active substances from 14th May 2010 to 14th May 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**8.** Regulation 2(6) extends the data protection periods for biocidal products from 14th May 2010 to 14 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]</sup> May 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**9.** Regulation 2(7) adds the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 and the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 to Schedule 2 and removes the Medical Devices Regulations 1994 and the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**10.** Regulation 2(8) clarifies that a biocidal product can only remain on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6] market under its existing national authorisation if all the active substances within it are existing active substances and changes specific time periods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]

**11.** In Great Britain the corresponding Regulations are the Biocidal Products (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6][S.I. 2010/745](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/745)).

**12.** A transposition note in relation to the implementation of the Amending Directive has been prepared. A copy may be obtained from the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland, 83 Ladas Drive, Belfast, BT6 9FR. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc565bc25612de265abfe6f52063bfd6]
