# The Water and Sewerage Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 216 (C. 12)

Water And Sewerage

The Water and Sewerage Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

16th June 2010

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Water and Sewerage Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 2006 Order” means the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2006 Order specified in the Schedule is 16<sup>th</sup> June 2010.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 16<sup>th</sup> June 2010.

John Mills

A senior officer of the

Department for Regional Development
