The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 29A of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-34f9f0bd20d36109a1e06cd288320938][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

These Regulations may be cited as the Rates (Deferment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1b1b989531004b7eb43a4cb609f2c75a][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(1) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]” means the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 2003 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]” means the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^f00002];
- “agreement” means an agreement under regulation 3(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “agreement holder”, in relation to an agreement, means the person with whom the Department has entered into the agreement or, in the case of the death of an agreement holder, that person’s personal representatives; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “authorised person” means a person authorised by the Department in writing for the purposes of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “eligible person”, in relation to a qualifying hereditament, has the meaning assigned by regulation 3(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “hospital” has the same meaning as in Article 2 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 2003 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e];
- “market value”, in relation to a hereditament, means the amount which that hereditament might reasonably expect to realise if it were sold with vacant possession on the open market by a willing seller free from any mortgage or charge; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “nursing home” has the same meaning as in Article 11 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 2003 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e];
- “qualifying hereditament” means, subject to paragraph (3), a hereditament which is a dwelling-house or, though not a dwelling-house, is used partly for the purposes of a private dwelling; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- “residential care home” has the same meaning as in Article 10 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 2003 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e].

(2) For the purposes of Article 29A of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] and these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) “occupier” has the meaning it has for the purposes of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e];
- (b) “owner” means any person for the time being receiving or entitled to receive, on his own account, the rack rent of the hereditament in connection with which the word is used or who, if the hereditament were let at a rack rent, would so receive or be entitled to receive that rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (c) a person is of pensionable age if he has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit as determined under section 1(2)(b) and (6) of the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^f00003].

(3) For the purposes of these Regulations a hereditament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) which is not in use; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) which the Department considers will, when next in use, be used wholly or partly for the purposes of a private dwelling, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

shall be deemed to be a qualifying hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(4) In determining whether a person occupies a qualifying hereditament as his only or principal residence for the purposes of these Regulations, regard shall be had to any residence occupied by that person whether or not the residence is in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## 3. Deferment agreement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-06b430e89a4ba3ca0aa66dd5a93b4816][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(1) Subject to the conditions set out in Schedule 1 being satisfied, the Department may enter into an agreement with an eligible person for the payment of rates in respect of the capital value of a qualifying hereditament to be deferred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) A person is an eligible person in relation to a qualifying hereditament if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) he is both the occupier and owner of that hereditament; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) a person to whom paragraph (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(3) This paragraph applies to a person if the person is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) of pensionable age on 1st April in the year in which the agreement is entered into; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) the partner of a person who is eligible to make an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the same hereditament by virtue of sub-paragraph (a); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (c) the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the same hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(4) In deciding whether to enter into an agreement with an eligible person, the Department shall consider whether the value of that person’s beneficial interest in the qualifying hereditament is, at any time, likely to be less than 30 per cent. of the market value of that hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(5) Paragraph (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) is without prejudice to the power of the Department to consider other matters before deciding whether to enter into an agreement with an eligible person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) shall not apply in the case of an eligible person who is the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the same hereditament, but such a person will not be entitled to defer the payment of rates for a particular year if condition 4(a) or (b) of Schedule 2 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(6) Where an agreement (“the second agreement”) is made with an eligible person who is the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the same hereditament (“the first agreement”), the second agreement may provide for the transfer of the amount outstanding under the first agreement to the surviving partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(7) An agreement shall apply to the payment of the rates for the whole of the commencement year and where an amount in respect of such rates has already been paid by the agreement holder, the Department shall repay that amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(8) An agreement shall be in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## 4. Conditions of an agreement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5b72b4774655dea0d7d8df5fff451663][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(1) An agreement shall contain the conditions set out in Schedule 2 and may contain such other conditions as the Department thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) In Schedule 2 “hereditament” means the hereditament which is the subject of the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## 5. Amount outstanding under an agreement to be a statutory charge (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8ba101c97ee82217cebcafc87e40752e][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(1) The amount outstanding under an agreement shall be a charge on and payable out of the estate of the agreement holder in the land constituting the hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) The charge created by paragraph (1) shall be enforceable in all respects as if it were a valid mortgage by deed created in favour of the Department by the person on whose estate the charge has been created (with, where necessary, any authorisation or consent required by law) and the Department may exercise the powers conferred by sections 19, 21 and 22 of the Conveyancing Act 1881 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^f00004] on mortgagees by deed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(3) In this regulation “hereditament” means the hereditament which is the subject of the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Conditions to be satisfied before the Department can enter an agreement with an eligible person (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

Regulation 3(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**1.**[^key-194971361433d50e87384d4aefb27d03] The capital value of the qualifying hereditament shall not be less than £50,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**2.**[^key-3ce6fd09133fa377d52309af300f60e6] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the qualifying hereditament must be the only or principal residence of an eligible person, who is of pensionable age on 1st April in the year in which the agreement is entered into. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) The condition as to pensionable age in sub-paragraph (1) shall not apply in the case of an eligible person who is the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the qualifying hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**3.**[^key-1511be7ebd72b0dbd6babf5989ceed9d] (1) An eligible person has made an application to the Department and has furnished the Department with such information as the Department may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) An eligible person shall have informed the Department of any change of circumstances affecting the application made or the information furnished under sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**4.**[^key-e246f5c498d1c060aaeb3d281162e06b] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), there shall be no rates due and payable by an eligible person for a year earlier than that in which the agreement is entered into. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) shall not apply in the case of rates due and payable in respect of the qualifying hereditament by an eligible person who is the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of that hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**5.**[^key-1461583c810b56cca19b566072716611] Where an eligible person owns an estate in any of the land constituting the qualifying hereditament as a joint tenant or as a tenant in common, the other joint tenants or tenants in common must be eligible persons and enter into the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**6.**[^key-a70324dd6a48ae0d773868384c6fea0a] (1) The value of the beneficial interest of an eligible person in the qualifying hereditament, as ascertained by the Department, shall not be less than 40 per cent. of an authorised person’s estimate of the market value of the qualifying hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) shall not apply in a case of an eligible person who is the surviving partner of a person who had made an agreement under these Regulations in respect of the qualifying hereditament, but such a person will not be entitled to defer the payment of rates for a particular year if condition 4(a) or (b) of Schedule 2 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**7.**[^key-5b126830e38cb335b0f466180f438478] Any person, other than an eligible person, with an estate in any of the land constituting the qualifying hereditament shall have consented in writing to the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**8.**[^key-e8e48277798d19aeb211b894fd79bb61] Any person who is in, or may go into, occupation of the qualifying hereditament (other than an eligible person) and who has attained the age of 17 years shall have— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) consented in writing to the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) agreed in writing to postpone any rights or interest which he has, or may acquire in the future, in the qualifying hereditament to the rights and interest of the Department in that hereditament, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

and an eligible person shall have warranted that all such persons have done so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**9.**[^key-5c1beb0cfad279b63845843e77bf9637] The eligible person shall have acknowledged in writing that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) he is aware that, on termination of the agreement, the qualifying hereditament may have to be sold in order to pay the amount outstanding under the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) he has been advised by the Department to seek independent legal and financial advice before entering into the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Conditions of an agreement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

Regulations 3(5) and 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**1.**[^key-aac0464a5d090ec474fdfa31e66628a2] The agreement holder shall keep the hereditament insured for the full cost of rebuilding or reinstatement and shall furnish to the Department, on demand, evidence of such insurance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**2.**[^key-f7202c202502bafbbf7fe0919e0daa78] The agreement holder shall keep the hereditament in good and substantial repair and condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**3.**[^key-d7b0cba8042a49df69c7bd77a6eee80e] (1) The agreement holder shall only be entitled to defer payment of the total amount of rates chargeable in respect of the hereditament for any year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in respect of the year in which the agreement terminates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**4.**[^key-70565e8cffb42e920051d5a2cb3802ff] The agreement holder shall not be entitled to defer the payment of rates for a particular year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) the value of his beneficial interest in the hereditament, as ascertained by the Department, would, after the deferment, be less than 30 per cent. of an authorised person’s estimate of the market value of the hereditament; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) the value, as ascertained by the Department, of his beneficial interest in the hereditament disregarding the amount outstanding under the agreement would, on 1st April of that year, be less than 40 per cent. of an authorised person’s estimate of the market value of the hereditament on that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**5.**[^key-ccae621ced542e9ca7e4fa2cea2f387d] (1) Compound interest shall accrue on a day to day basis on the amount outstanding under the agreement and the annual rate of interest used to calculate that accrual shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) if there is only one official dealing rate in force on a particular day, 1 per cent. below that rate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) if there is more than one official dealing rate in force on a particular day, 1 per cent. below the rate in force at the end of that day, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

but in no case shall the annual rate of interest used to calculate the accrual be less than 1 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) In this condition “official dealing rate” means the rate announced from time to time by the Monetary Policy Committee of the Bank of England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e][^f00005] and for the time being in force as the official dealing rate, being the rate at which the Bank of England is willing to enter into transactions for providing short term liquidity in the money markets. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**6.**[^key-6ab53c8cb78a34afbf329338718967c6] (1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the agreement holder shall pay the Department the costs incurred, or to be incurred, by the Department in arranging for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) the registration of the charge created by regulation 5 of the Rates (Deferment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
  - (i) the Land Registry or the Registry of Deeds; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e] the Statutory Charges Register; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) the modification, vacation or cancellation of a registration mentioned in sub-paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(2) Where an agreement holder has paid a cost mentioned in paragraph (1), the Department may, if the agreement holder so requests, pay him an amount equal to that cost whereupon that amount shall be added, on the date that the Department pays it, to the amount outstanding under the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(3) A cost mentioned in paragraph (1) may be added to the amount outstanding under the agreement if the cost is not paid to the Department within 28 days of the Department having made a demand in writing for payment of that cost by the agreement holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

**7.**[^key-bdf7df97f5d3c6d32c9f0cf7ddbf4f54] The Department may, if the agreement holder so requests, pay him an amount equal to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) any legal costs which he has paid in connection with the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) any costs which he has paid in connection with obtaining information for the purposes of the agreement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

These Regulations provide that, subject to certain preconditions being satisfied, the Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”) may enter into an agreement with an eligible person for the deferment of the payment of domestic rates. An eligible person is a person who is both the occupier and owner of a hereditament in respect of which domestic rates are payable and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) who, on 1st April in the rating year in which the agreement is entered into, has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) is the partner of a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or the surviving partner of a person who had made an earlier agreement with the Department under these Regulations in respect of the same hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

Among the conditions to be satisfied before an agreement can be entered into are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) a condition that the capital value shown in the capital value list for the hereditament should not be less than £50,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) a condition that, subject to exceptions, the hereditament should be the only or principal residence of an eligible person, who has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit on 1st April in the rating year in which the agreement is entered into; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (c) a condition that the value of the beneficial interest of eligible persons in the hereditament, as ascertained by the Department, should not be less than 40 per cent. of the market value of the hereditament – this condition does not apply in the case of a surviving partner of a person who had made an earlier agreement in respect of the same hereditament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

The Regulations also provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]

- (a) that an agreement shall contain the conditions set out in Schedule 2 to the Regulations and may contain such other conditions as the Department thinks fit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (b) that the amount outstanding under an agreement shall be a charge on the hereditament; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
- (c) for the modification of various provisions of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f06ec5bf4db41d305764b38ea570629e]
