# The Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 214

Agriculture

Food

The Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made

14th June 2010

Coming into operation

16th June 2010

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002].

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety has been designated for the purposes of that section in relation to measures relating to food (including drink) including the primary production of food and measures relating to feed produced for or fed to food-producing animals[^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and come into operation on 16th June 2010.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Revocation

The Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005] are revoked.

Sealed with the official seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 14th June 2010.

Sean Donaghy

A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No. 99](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/99)).

Those Regulations implemented Commission Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism “LL RICE 601” in rice products (OJ No. L244, 7.9.2006, p.27) as last amended by Commission Decision [2008/162/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0162) amending Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism “LL RICE 601” in rice products (OJ No. L52, 27.2.2008, p.25). Commission Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601) has been repealed by Commission Decision 2010/315/EU repealing Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism ‘LL RICE 601’ in rice products, and providing for random testing for the absence of that organism in rice products (OJ No. L141, 9.6.2010, p.10).
