# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendment to Rule 0.6

In Rule 0.6 after paragraph (2), there shall be inserted the following paragraph –

> (3) A reference to an “authorised person” is a reference to a person who is authorised pursuant to Article 348A[^f00004] of the Order to act as nominee or supervisor of a voluntary arrangement proposed or approved under Part II or Part VIII of the Order.

.

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: Article 348A was inserted by Article 6(3) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 ([S.I. 2002/3152 (N.I. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2002/3152))
