# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendments to Part 6 of the principal Rules

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), Part 6 of the principal Rules shall have effect subject to the amendments set out in Part 4 of Schedule 1 to these Rules.

(2) The amendments to Part 6 of the principal Rules set out in Part 4 of Schedule 1 to these Rules shall not apply in relation to a bankruptcy where the petition was presented prior to the commencement date and, in such a case, Part 6 of the principal Rules as it stood before the commencement date shall continue to apply.
