## SCHEDULE 12 — JOBSEEKER'S ALLOWANCE: APPLICABLE AMOUNTS OF JOINT-CLAIM COUPLES IN SPECIAL CASES

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 22(9)

### PROVISIONS IN SCHEDULE 4A TO THE JOBSEEKER'S ALLOWANCE REGULATIONS AS AMENDED BY THIS ORDER[^f00092]

| Column (1) | Column (2) |
| --- | --- |
| Patients |  |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.** Subject to paragraphs 9 and 11, a joint-claim couple where one member– | *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.** |
| - (a) has been a patient for more than 6 weeks; | - (a) The applicable amount under regulation 86A reduced by £15·50; |

| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.** —<br>(1) A joint-claim couple where one member is temporarily separated from the other member where one of them is living in the home while the other is in accommodation provided by a Health and Social Services Board or an HSS trust of a kind specified in the definition of residential accommodation in regulation 85. | *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.** —<br>(1) The aggregate of the amount applicable for the claimant who remains in the home calculated as if he were a single claimant under regulation 83 or 85 and in respect of the other joint-claimant £77·45 of which £17·50 is for personal expenses. |

| (3) A joint-claim couple where both members or all the members of a polygamous marriage of which a member of the joint-claim couple is a member, are in accommodation referred to in sub-paragraph (1). | (3) For each member of that couple or marriage £77·45 of which £17·50 is for personal expenses plus, if appropriate, the amount applicable under regulation 84A(d) and 84B(e). |
| Members of joint-claim couples in residential accommodation |  |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.** —<br>(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), a joint-claim couple where one member is in or only temporarily absent from residential accommodation. | *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.** —<br>(1) £77·45 of which £17·50 is for personal expenses. |
| (2) A joint-claim couple where either member is a member of a polygamous marriage and one or more members of that marriage are in or only temporarily absent from residential accommodation. | (2) The amount specified in sub-paragraph (1) of this column multiplied by the number of members of the polygamous marriage in or only temporarily absent from that accommodation. |
