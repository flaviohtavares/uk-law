# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 18

## 18. Income support transitional protection

*Extent: N.I.*

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00037] shall be increased by 1·3 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
