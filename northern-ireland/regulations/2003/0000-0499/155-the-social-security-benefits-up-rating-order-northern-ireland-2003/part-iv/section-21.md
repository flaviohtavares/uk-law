## 21. Age-related amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£32·50” there shall be substituted “£32·90”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£42·70” there shall be substituted “£43·25”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£53·95” there shall be substituted “£54·65”.
