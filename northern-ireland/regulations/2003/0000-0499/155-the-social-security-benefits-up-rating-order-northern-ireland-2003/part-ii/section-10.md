## 10. Statutory maternity pay

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00025] (prescribed rate of statutory maternity pay) the sum of £100·00 remains the same.
