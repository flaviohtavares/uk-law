# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 9(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00031] (increase in rate of incapacity benefit where beneficiary is under prescribed age on the qualifying date)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£14·90” there shall be substituted “£15·15”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£7·45” there shall be substituted “£7·60”.
