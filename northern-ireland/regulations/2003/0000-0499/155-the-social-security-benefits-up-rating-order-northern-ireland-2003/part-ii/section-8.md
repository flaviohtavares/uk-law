# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 8

## 8. Earnings limits

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases)–

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “£155” there shall be substituted “£160”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “£155” there shall be substituted “£160” and the sum of £20 remains the same.
