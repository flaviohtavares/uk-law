# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 14

## 14. Child benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00030] (weekly rates of child benefit)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i) for “£15·75” there shall be substituted “£16·05”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(ii) the sum of £17·55 remains the same; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£10·55” there shall be substituted “£10·75”.
