## SCHEDULE 3 — REFERENCE METHODS OF MEASUREMENT[^c-07724dc70fca2de1c460cc40c8c42007]

Regulation 4(3)

### Chemical Fertilizer[^c-07724dc70fca2de1c460cc40c8c42007]

Nitrogen compounds shall be measured using the method described in Commission Directive [77/535/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0535) of 22nd June 1977 on the approximation of the laws of the member states relating to methods of sampling and analysis for fertilizers, as amended by Directive [89/519/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0519).

### Freshwaters, coastal waters and marine waters[^c-07724dc70fca2de1c460cc40c8c42007]

Nitrate concentration shall be measured in accordance with Article 4a(3) of Council Decision [77/795/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1977/0795) of 12th December 1977 establishing a common procedure for the exchange of information on the quality of surface fresh water in the Community, as amended by Decision [86/574/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1986/0574).
