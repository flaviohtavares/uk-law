# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 268

GOVERNMENT RESOURCES AND ACCOUNTS

The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003

Made

13th May 2003

Coming into operation

6th June 2003

Whereas the bodies listed in the Schedule to this Order appear to the Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”) –

- (a) to exercise functions of a public nature; or
- (b) to be entirely or substantially funded from public money.

And whereas the Department intends the accounts under section 14 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (“the Act”) for the financial year ending with 31st March 2003 to relate in part to those bodies.

Now, therefore, the Department in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 15(1) of the Act and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 and shall come into operation on 6th June 2003.

## 2. Designation

For the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, the Department of Finance and Personnel hereby designates the **bodies** listed in the Schedule in respect of the financial year ending with 31st March 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 13th May 2003.

Derek Lynn

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order designates **bodies**, for the financial year ending with 31st March 2003, for the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001. The Department of Finance and Personnel (DFP) intend to pass the information to HM Treasury for use in the preparation of Whole of Government Accounts (WGA) at United Kingdom level for that year. The designation enables DFP to require those **bodies** to provide financial information in relation to that year.

WGA are financial statements prepared on the basis of Generally Accepted Accounting Practice which will cover the whole of the public sector as far as practically possible.
