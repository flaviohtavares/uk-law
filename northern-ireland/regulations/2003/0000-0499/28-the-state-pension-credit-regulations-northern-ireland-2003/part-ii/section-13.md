## 13. Small amounts of state pension credit[^c-01b2938d4433395f862c324b57854e9b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the amount of state pension credit payable is less than 10p per week, the credit shall not be payable unless the claimant is in receipt of another benefit payable with the credit.
