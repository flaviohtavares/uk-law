# The State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 12

## 12. End of assessed income period[^c-5084667cad900b8428cc15822bcd0860]

*Extent: N.I.*

An assessed income period shall end [^c-2a5daf7e9f2767ad588b74e05472de08]... –

- (a) at such time as[^c-98b3934fe0ed02a6c0b27101915e020d] the claimant no longer satisfies a condition of entitlement to state pension credit;
- (b) at such time as[^c-257166c43b9e0901cb08b6931f77b9ce] payments of an element of the claimant’s retirement provision which is due to be paid to him stops temporarily or the amount paid is less than the amount due and in consequence his award of state pension credit is superseded under Article 11 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00028];
- (c) at such time as[^c-06304576ceffbcf84c803aec3b42fe32] a claimant who has no partner is provided with accommodation in a residential care home , nursing home or an independent hospital[^c-7115b8b829d7e898c1f61bf661113c77] other than on a temporary basis;[^c-66e7558146706c52ce7d32a33d348df9]
- (d)[^c-5dacb8950de93925972cca3c9910268e] if, apart from this sub-paragraph, it would have ended on a date falling within the period specified in column 1 of the table in Schedule 3A, on the corresponding date shown against that period in column 2 of that table.
