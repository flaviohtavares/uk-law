## 7. Savings Credit[^c-7922bad69edaaba3d3fbfa0002a24962]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The percentage prescribed for the purposes of determining –

- (a)[^c-8d39b3e610f49ae68d674d30f11019c4][^c-5331e07ed50ca8dfeb4e168c998aacf9][^c-da22e33860a61aee7f3b95e803fc1dcc][^c-f928af8d5968d7818f6d1acd8f5255fa][^c-e6637d12816dbc264009e0c98e447be5][^c-1eefdf6826d92ea37df5aad8f283ff8d][^c-2bb7f15ed049d95284b28ed62b886c41][^c-18d4eaf20482f186a3405d48c47685ae][^c-d9c99647928291e5f9fb1b6e9c68eed2][^c-a02db17ba821e2a1ec4473af8233d3b9][^c-4feec775c1a653a1d04b547545e771c2][^c-8ba66054ba95bf15dd7b2f21f4e9308d] the maximum savings credit is 60 per cent.[^c-11046c1942bb2e092c5b9b087e80c956];
- (b)[^c-8d39b3e610f49ae68d674d30f11019c4][^c-5331e07ed50ca8dfeb4e168c998aacf9][^c-da22e33860a61aee7f3b95e803fc1dcc][^c-f928af8d5968d7818f6d1acd8f5255fa][^c-e6637d12816dbc264009e0c98e447be5][^c-1eefdf6826d92ea37df5aad8f283ff8d][^c-2bb7f15ed049d95284b28ed62b886c41][^c-18d4eaf20482f186a3405d48c47685ae][^c-d9c99647928291e5f9fb1b6e9c68eed2][^c-a02db17ba821e2a1ec4473af8233d3b9][^c-4feec775c1a653a1d04b547545e771c2][^c-8ba66054ba95bf15dd7b2f21f4e9308d] “amount A” in section 3(4) of the Act is 60 per cent.;
- (c)[^c-8d39b3e610f49ae68d674d30f11019c4][^c-5331e07ed50ca8dfeb4e168c998aacf9][^c-da22e33860a61aee7f3b95e803fc1dcc][^c-f928af8d5968d7818f6d1acd8f5255fa][^c-e6637d12816dbc264009e0c98e447be5][^c-1eefdf6826d92ea37df5aad8f283ff8d][^c-2bb7f15ed049d95284b28ed62b886c41][^c-18d4eaf20482f186a3405d48c47685ae][^c-d9c99647928291e5f9fb1b6e9c68eed2][^c-a02db17ba821e2a1ec4473af8233d3b9][^c-4feec775c1a653a1d04b547545e771c2][^c-8ba66054ba95bf15dd7b2f21f4e9308d] “amount B” in section 3(4) of the Act is 40 per cent..

(2)[^c-07ff642217e5ce4b87b4a789f928f47d] The amount prescribed for the savings credit threshold is £126∙50[^c-7b4c2817a5e48c7de6bbe2765e272386] for a claimant who has no partner and £201∙80 for a claimant who has a partner.

(3)[^c-2ad7db09221d0eed973e34dc2b8427bf][^c-c405334fbd41320060fd42506cf28e0b][^c-8e7334138e428510cc48e2209c693890][^c-dae8881f5e2901fed55974aafb65f591][^c-6693fee1026dbed32c927a04acbf79bd][^c-ad8f8f379ace5079a9e39bdb932ad359][^c-07e46d40fca099d03af2e3c950841a16][^c-8b514c8414fd7d0fe37968666b8eef53][^c-1a1ddca6f81bf6b28d01f886c5d20dc7][^c-023006cfe04b3327ca11d5a367d757ef][^c-514870308937c2e5d568da56d6da8bd2][^c-2eaf3f719ef0da69ffbb9079b377684d][^c-e1ff62be6715de940339a88f1668e123] The maximum savings credit shall be taken to be nil in the case of –

- (a) prisoners, and
- (b) members of religious orders who are fully maintained by their order.

(4) If a calculation made for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b) or (c) results in a fraction of a penny, that fraction shall, if it would be to the claimant’s advantage, be treated as a penny, otherwise it shall be disregarded.
