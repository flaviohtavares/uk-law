## 8. Special groups[^key-9a51b53af9d9c05d2c18478be9a04bd9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 3 (special groups) shall have effect in the case of members of polygamous marriages and persons serving a sentence of imprisonment detained in hospital[^key-ef4332f1f62189ff8806b6e3db828c3e] .
