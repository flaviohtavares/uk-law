# The Environment (Designation of Relevant Directives) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 209

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Environment (Designation of Relevant Directives) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

2nd April 2003

Coming into operation

1st May 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by paragraph 20(2)(d) of Schedule 1 to the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environment (Designation of Relevant Directives) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2003.

**2.** The following Directives are designated as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(d) of Schedule 1 to the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 –

- (a) Council Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations[^f00002];
- (b) Council Directive [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0053) on end-of-life vehicles[^f00003];
- (c) Council Directive [2000/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0076) on the incineration of waste[^f00004]; and
- (d) Council Directive [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080) on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from large combustion plants[^f00005].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 2nd April 2003.

Judena Goldring

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order designates the Directives listed below as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(d) of Schedule 1 to the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002:

- (a) Council Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations;
- (b) Council Directive [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0053) on end-of-life vehicles;
- (c) Council Directive [2000/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0076) on the incineration of waste; and
- (d) Council Directive [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080) on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from large combustion plants.
