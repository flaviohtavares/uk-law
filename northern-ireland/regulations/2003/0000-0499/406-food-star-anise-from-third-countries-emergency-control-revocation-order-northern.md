# Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 406

FOOD

Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

15th September 2003

Coming into operation

16th September 2003

Whereas it appears to the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] that the import of certain Star Anise consigned from countries which are not Member States of the European Community no longer involves imminent risk of injury to health;

Now therefore that Department, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 12(1) and 47(2) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf and having regard, in accordance with Article 47(3A) of the said Order, to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 16th September 2003.

## 2. Revocation

The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 15th September 2003.

Julie Thompson

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/82)), which prohibited the import of Star Anise and Japanese Star Anise unless certain conditions were complied with. The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 implemented Commission Decision [2002/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0075) laying down special conditions on the import from third countries of Star Anise (O.J. No. L33, 2.2.2002, p. 31). That Decision was repealed by Commission Decision [2003/602/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0602) (O.J. No. L204, 13.8.2003, p. 60).
