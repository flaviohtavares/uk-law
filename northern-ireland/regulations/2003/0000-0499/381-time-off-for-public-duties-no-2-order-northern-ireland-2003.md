# Time Off for Public Duties (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule has been made to correct a drafting error in [S.R. 2003 No. 365](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/365)

2003 No. 381

EMPLOYMENT

Time Off for Public Duties (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

14th August 2003

Coming into operation

24th August 2003

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 78(8)(a) of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order –

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Time Off for Public Duties (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 24th August 2003.

## 2. Revocation

The Time Off for Public Duties Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

## 3. Time off for public duties

In Article 78(2) of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 –

- (a) after sub-paragraph (d) “or” shall be omitted; and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (e) the following shall be inserted –

  > or
  >
  > - (f) a district policing partnership,

  .

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 14th August 2003.

R. B. Gamble

A senior officer of the

Department for Employment and Learning

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes and replaces the Time Off for Public Duties Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 365](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/365)) to correct drafting errors in it.

Article 78 of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (“the 1996 Order”) requires an employer to allow time off during working hours, subject to the provisions of that Article, to an employee who is a member of a body specified in it. Article 3 of this Order modifies Article 78 of the 1996 Order by adding district policing partnerships, established on the coming into force of certain provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, to the list of bodies whose members are covered by that Article.
