# The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 443 (C. 34)

PLANNING

The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

9th October 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

(2) In this Order –

- “the 1991 Order” means the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002];
- “the 2003 Order” means the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the 2003 Order: –

- (a) Articles 3, 4, 6 to 17, 26, 35;
- (b) Article 37 as it applies Schedule 1 (Minor and Consequential Amendments); and
- (c) Article 37 as it applies Schedule 2 (Repeals) and gives effect to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order,

is 12th November 2003.

## 3. Savings

Nothing in this Order shall affect the operation of Article 41 of the 1991 Order in relation to applications made under that Article prior to 12th November 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 9th October 2003.

I. Maye

A Senior Officer of the

Department of the Environment
