# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicle (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

(1) The Motor Vehicle (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) For the table in regulation 26 (vehicles suitable for test) there shall be substituted the table set out in Schedule 1.

(3) For Schedule 7 there shall be substituted the provisions set out in Schedule 2.
