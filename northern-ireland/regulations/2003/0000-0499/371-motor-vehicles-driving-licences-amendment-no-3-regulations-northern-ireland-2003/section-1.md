# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation –

- (a) for the purposes of regulation 2(3) on 1st September 2003;
- (b) for all other purposes, on 30th September 2003.
