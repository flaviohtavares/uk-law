# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 66 (C. 5)

POLICE

The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

Made

8th February 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003.

**2.** Schedule 3 to the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (district policing partnerships) shall come into force on 11th February 2003.

**3.** Part 5 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (economy, efficiency and effectiveness) shall come into force on 28th February 2003.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

8th February 2003

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order brings into force those provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 which are not already in force. It brings into force, on 11th February 2003, Schedule 3 to the Act (district policing partnerships) and, on 28th February 20003, Part 5 of the Act.
