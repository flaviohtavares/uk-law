# Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 89

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

19th February 2003

To be laid before Parliament

24th February 2003

Coming into operation

7th April 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(2), 7(3), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations: –

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 7th April 2003.

(2) In these Regulations, “the Order” means the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

## 2. Revocation

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Persons eligible for advice and assistance

For the weekly sum of “£189” specified in Article 3(1) of the Order[^f00004] there shall be substituted the weekly sum of “£192”.

## 4. Contributions from persons receiving advice and assistance

For the weekly sum of “£80” specified in Article 7(2) of the Order[^f00005] there shall be substituted the weekly sum of “£81”.

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 19th February 2003.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 so as to:

- (a) increase the upper income limit to make legal advice and assistance available to those with disposable income of not more than £192 a week (instead of £189) (Regulation 3);
- (b) increase the lower income limit below which legal advice and assistance is available without payment of a contribution to £81 a week (instead of £80) (Regulation 4).

No changes are made to the capital limits.
