# Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 49 (C. 3)

ENVIRONMENT

Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

3rd February 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3)(a) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Part IV (together with Schedule 4) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 is 3rd February 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3rd February 2003.

J. Goldring

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings the provisions of Part IV (together with Schedule 4) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 into operation on 3rd February 2003.
