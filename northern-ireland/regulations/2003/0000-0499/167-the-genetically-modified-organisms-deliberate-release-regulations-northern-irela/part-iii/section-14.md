# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Requirement for consent to market[^c-cf051e6ad00d7a6b46e890c00b5000d1]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The cases and circumstances prescribed for the purposes of Article 8(1)(a) of the Order in relation to marketing genetically modified organisms are all cases and circumstances in relation to the marketing of genetically modified organisms.
