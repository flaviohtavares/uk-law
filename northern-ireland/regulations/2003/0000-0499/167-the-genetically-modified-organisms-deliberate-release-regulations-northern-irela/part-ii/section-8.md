# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Requirement for consent to release[^key-d662f32df057253e171a78e8a3b55288]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The cases and circumstances prescribed for the purposes of Article 8(1)(a) of the Order in relation to the release of any genetically modified organisms are all cases and circumstances in which genetically modified organisms are intended to be released.
