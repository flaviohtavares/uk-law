# The Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 (Commencement) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 140 (C. 9)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 (Commencement) Order 2003

Made

8th February 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Article 1(2) of the Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order: –

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 (Commencement) Order 2003.

**2.** The Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 shall come into operation on 24th February 2003.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretary’s of State

Northern Ireland Office

8th February 2003

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order commences in its entirety the provisions of the Financial Investigations (Northern Ireland) Order 2001.
