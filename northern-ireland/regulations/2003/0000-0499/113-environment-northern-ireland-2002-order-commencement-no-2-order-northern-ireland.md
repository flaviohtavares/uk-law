# Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 113 (C. 8)

ENVIRONMENT

Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

28th February 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Part III of Schedule 5 (together with so much of Article 53(1) as relates thereto) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 is 28th February 2003.

**3.** The day appointed for the coming into operation of Part II of Schedule 6 (together with so much of Article 53(2) as relates thereto) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 is 28th February 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 28th February 2003.

J. Goldring

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings Part III of Schedule 5 (together with so much of Article 53(1) as relates thereto) and Part II of Schedule 6 (together with so much of Article 53(2) as relates thereto) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 into operation on 28th February 2003.

Part IV and Schedule 4 to the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 were brought into operation on 3rd February 2003 by the Environment (Northern Ireland) (2002 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 49 (C. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/49)).
