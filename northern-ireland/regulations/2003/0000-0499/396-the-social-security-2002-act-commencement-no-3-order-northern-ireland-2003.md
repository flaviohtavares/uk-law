# The Social Security (2002 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 396 (C. 29)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (2002 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

9th September 2003

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 9(1) of the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (2002 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002, in so far as not already in operation, is 10th September 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 9th September 2003.

John O'Neill

Senior Officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of provisions of the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002, in so far as not already in operation, on 10th September 2003.
