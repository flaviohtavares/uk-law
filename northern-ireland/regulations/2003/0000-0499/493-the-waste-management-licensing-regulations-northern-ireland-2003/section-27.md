# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 27

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Revocations[^c-6615adf9297e8fec57ef0497af5b8f41]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Waste Collection and Disposal Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00062] in so far as not already revoked are hereby revoked.

(2) The Waste Collection and Disposal (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00063] are hereby revoked.
