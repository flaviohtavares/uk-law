# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 11E

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11E. Conditions of waste management licences: Materials facilities[^c-3b5ff4ec2de82251c8adf7444bbf1959]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall ensure that any waste management licence granted or varied on or after 1st April 2025 authorising the treatment of waste material at a materials facility contains a condition requiring the holder of the licence to comply with the Materials Facilities Code.
