## 11B. Conditions of waste management licences: waste batteries and accumulators[^key-1eed2b74cf0551a873bff0b438b208f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall ensure that any waste management licence granted or varied on or after 20th May 2009 authorising an establishment or undertaking to carry out treatment of waste batteries or accumulators contains such conditions as it considers necessary [^key-ca7dc496598afe2308db476ae251c0a3]....
