# The Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 16A

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16A. Exclusion from waste management licensing: waste batteries and accumulators[^key-2a2c9a767b5563888daae89935330c6b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 4(1)(a), (b) and (c) of the 1997 Order shall not apply in relation to the storage of waste portable batteries or accumulators at a collection point where “ collection point” means a place where end-users are able to deposit waste portable batteries or accumulators at the premises of a distributor fulfilling its duty to take back such waste portable batteries or accumulators [^key-39d9581902e3ad1f701dad8eed6ea604]....
