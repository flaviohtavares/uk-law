(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations, further amend the Cereal Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 as amended (“the cereal seeds Regulations”) and the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 as amended (“the fodder plant seeds Regulations”).

The Regulations exempt certain types of seed marketed in bulk from the labelling and packaging requirements in “the cereal seeds Regulations” and “the fodder plant seeds Regulations” (regulations 2(2) to (4) and 3(2) to (4)). These exemptions are derogations respectively from Council Directive [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0402) (O.J. No. L125, 11.7. 66, p. 2309) and Council Directive [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401) (O.J. No. L125, 11.7.66, p.2298/66) as last amended by Council Directive [2001/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0064) (O.J. No. L234, 1.9.01, p. 60). The derogations are permitted by Council Directive [2001/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0064).

The Regulations specify the conditions for the exemption, in implementation of Council Directive [2001/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0064) and Commission Decision [94/650/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0650) (O.J. No. L252, 28.9.1994, p. 15) as relevantly amended by Commission Decision [1998/174/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0174) (O.J. No. L63, 4.3.98, p. 31) and by Commission Decision [2000/441/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0441) (O.J. No. L176, 15.7.00, p. 50) (regulations 2(2), (5) and (6) and 3(2), (5) and (6)).
