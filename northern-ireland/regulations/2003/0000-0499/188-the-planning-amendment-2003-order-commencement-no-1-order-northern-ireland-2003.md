# The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 188 (C. 13)

PLANNING

The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made

20th March 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 5, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 32, 33, 34 and 36 of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 shall be 22nd April 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 20th March 2003.

J. McConnell

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 22nd April 2003, Articles 5, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 32, 33, 34 and 36 of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, which relate to enforcement, control over development and certain particular matters and miscellaneous provisions.
