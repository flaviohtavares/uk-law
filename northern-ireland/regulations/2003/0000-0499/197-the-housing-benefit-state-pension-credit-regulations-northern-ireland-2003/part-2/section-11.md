# The Housing Benefit (State Pension Credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 11

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Amendment of regulation 63[^c-33a616f5b5a7de23aec0d5ef3355cd68]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 63 (non-dependant deductions) –

- (a) in paragraph (1), for sub-paragraph (b)[^f00077], there shall be substituted –

  > - (b) in respect of a non-dependant who is engaged in remunerative work and in receipt of state pension credit –
  >   - (i) in calculating the amount of a rent rebate or allowance, £7·40 per week,
  >   - (ii) in calculating the amount of a rate rebate, £2·30 per week;
  > - (c) in respect of a non-dependant aged 18 or over to whom neither sub-paragraph (a) nor sub-paragraph (b) applies –
  >   - (i) in calculating the amount of a rent rebate or allowance, £7·40 per week,
  >   - (ii) in calculating the amount of a rate rebate, £2·30 per week;

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (7) after sub-paragraph (d)[^f00078], there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph –

  > - (dd) he is a full-time student and the claimant or his partner has attained the age of 65, or

  .
