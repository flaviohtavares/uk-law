# The Housing Benefit (State Pension Credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Regulation 5[^c-6b6a948d39a8848697a72a013bb20d35]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 5 (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as occupying a dwelling as his home) in paragraph (6)(c)(ii)[^f00012], for “or the claimant’s applicable amount includes a premium under paragraph 9, 9A, 10, 11, 13 or 14 of Schedule 2”, there shall be substituted “or the claimant is a person who has attained or whose partner has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit”.
