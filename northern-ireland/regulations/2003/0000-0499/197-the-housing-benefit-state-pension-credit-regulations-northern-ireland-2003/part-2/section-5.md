# The Housing Benefit (State Pension Credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Persons from abroad[^key-1ab4b977c282ba1f250daa8df4a5422b]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 7A(5)[^f00013] (persons from abroad) for sub-paragraph (d) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph –

> - (d) is on a state pension credit;

.
