# The Housing Benefit (State Pension Credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 18

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Minor amendments[^c-b3c7b93ec6d456f0cdcae9f2026e9ba8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In the Housing Benefit Regulations listed in paragraph (2), after “income support”, there shall be inserted “, state pension credit”.

(2) Those regulations are –

- (a) regulation 4(5)[^f00092] (remunerative work);
- (b) regulation 93(1)(a)[^f00093] (circumstances in which payment is to be made to a landlord or the Department of Finance and Personnel).
