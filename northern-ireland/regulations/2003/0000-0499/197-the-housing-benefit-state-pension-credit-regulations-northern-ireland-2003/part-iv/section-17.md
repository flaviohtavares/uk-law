# The Housing Benefit (State Pension Credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Amendments relating to recovery of overpaid housing benefit[^key-86a03db0539403f8f11f3fa1be00c427]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 105(1)[^f00091] of the Housing Benefit Regulations (recovery of overpayment from prescribed benefits) at the end there shall be added the following sub-paragraph –

> - (f) state pension credit.

.
