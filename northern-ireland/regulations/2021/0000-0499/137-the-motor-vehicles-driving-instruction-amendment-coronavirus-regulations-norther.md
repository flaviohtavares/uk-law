# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 137

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

26th May 2021

Coming into operation

31st May 2021

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 62(1) and 76 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 31<sup>st</sup> May 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) Regulations

In the Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00004], in regulation 7 (general provisions), after paragraph (4) insert—

> (4A) Notwithstanding paragraph (4)(c), a person may make an application to take the instructional ability and fitness test for the relevant vehicle category within three years after passing the theory test and having passed the driving ability and fitness test or the riding ability and fitness test, as the case may be, where the two year period would otherwise have expired or would otherwise expire between 31<sup>st</sup> May 2020 and 31<sup>st</sup> August 2021.

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Instruction) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 (“the 2010 Regulations”) in order to extend, in certain circumstances, the length of time that a person has to apply for the instructional ability and fitness test after passing the driving instructor theory test given the suspension of practical driver testing due to the COVID-19 (coronavirus) public health emergency.

Regulation 7 of the 2010 Regulations provides that a person taking the examination to be an approved driving or motorcycle instructor must apply to take the instructional ability and fitness test within two years after passing the theory test and having passed the driving ability and fitness test or the riding ability and fitness test, as the case may be. These regulations amend regulation 7 in order to allow a person to apply for the instructional ability and fitness test within three years after passing the theory test and having passed the driving ability and fitness test or the riding ability and fitness test, as the case may be, where the two year period would have expired or would expire between 31<sup>st</sup> May 2020 and 31<sup>st</sup> August 2021.
