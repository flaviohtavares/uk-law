# The Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 198

Health And Personal Social Services

The Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

1st July 2021

Laid before the Assembly on

1st July 2021

Coming into operation

23rd July 2021

The Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 23(1), (2)(a), (b), (f), and (h), (4), (7)(c) and 48(2) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] makes the following Regulations.

In accordance with Article 23(8) of that Order it has consulted with such persons as it considers appropriate.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 23rd July 2021.

## 2. Revocation

The Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 [^f00002] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 1st July 2021

Eilis McDaniel

A senior officer of the Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations revoke the Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.
