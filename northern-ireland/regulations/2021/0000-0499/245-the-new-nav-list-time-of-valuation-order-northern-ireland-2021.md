# The New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 245

Rates

The New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

7th September 2021

Coming into operation

1st October 2021

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 39A(1) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002]

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2021.

## 2. Specification of time

1st October 2021 is the time by reference to which a net annual value to be ascribed to any hereditament in a new NAV list coming into force on 1st April 2023 is to be ascertained.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 7th September 2021.

Sharon Magee

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 1st October 2021 as the time by reference to which net annual values are to be ascertained for the purposes of the new NAV list coming into force on 1st April 2023.
