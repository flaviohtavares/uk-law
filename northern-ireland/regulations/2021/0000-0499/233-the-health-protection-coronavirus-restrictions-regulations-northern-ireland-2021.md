# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 14) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Regulations made by the Department of Health and laid before the Assembly under section 25Q (Emergency procedure) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, for approval of the Assembly before the expiration of 28 days beginning with the day on which they are made.

2021 No. 233

Public Health

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 14) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

at 3.45 p.m. on 13th August 2021

Laid before the Assembly

at 5.00 p.m. on 13th August 2021

Coming into operation

16th August 2021

The Department of Health[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25C(1), (3)(c), (4)(d) and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

These Regulations are made in response to the serious and imminent threat to public health which is posed by the incidence and spread of severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) in Northern Ireland.

The Department of Health considers that the restrictions and requirements imposed by these Regulations are proportionate to what they seek to achieve, which is a public health response to that threat.

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 14) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021.

(2) These Regulations come into operation on 16th August 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00003] are amended in accordance with these Regulations.

**3.** In regulation 1(2), omit the definitions of “bubble” and of “educational activity”.

**4.** In the heading to regulation 4 (Restrictions on theatres, concert halls and other indoor venues), after “Restrictions on theatres, concert halls” insert “, conference and exhibition venues”.

**5.** (1) In regulation 4(1)(b) omit “and”;

(2) in regulation 4(1)(c) for the full stop at the end substitute “; and”; and

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 by—

- (a) permitting conferences and exhibitions with certain restrictions;
- (b) removing the limit on persons who may be seated at a table to consume food and drink;
- (c) removing the restriction on outdoor house parties, outdoor raves or outdoor gatherings at a private dwelling; other laws may still place restrictions on such events;
- (d) removing references to bubbles; and
- (e) making some technical changes.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
