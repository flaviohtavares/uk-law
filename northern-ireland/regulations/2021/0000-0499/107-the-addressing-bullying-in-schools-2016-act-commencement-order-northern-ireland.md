# The Addressing Bullying in Schools (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 107 (C. 1)

Education

The Addressing Bullying in Schools (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

21st April 2021

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5(3) of the Addressing Bullying in Schools Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Addressing Bullying in Schools (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

## 2. Appointed Day

The provisions of the Addressing Bullying in School Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2021.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 21<sup>st</sup> April 2021

Richard Irwin

A senior officer of the

Department of Education

21st April 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the sections of the Addressing Bullying in Schools Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.
