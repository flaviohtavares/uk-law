# The Female Genital Mutilation Protection Order (Relevant Third Party) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 297

Family Law

The Female Genital Mutilation Protection Order (Relevant Third Party) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

5th November 2021

Coming into operation

1st December 2021

The Department of Finance[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A of, and paragraph 19(7) of Schedule 2 to the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003[^f00002], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Female Genital Mutilation Protection Order (Relevant Third Party) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021.

(2) This Order comes into operation on 1st December 2021.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “**HSC trust**” means a Health and Social Care trust established under Article 10 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00003].

## 3. Specification of HSC trust as a relevant third party

A HSC trust is specified as a relevant third party for the purposes of paragraph 19(2)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 5th November 2021

Barry Armstrong

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies a Health and Social Care trust as a relevant third party under paragraph 19(2)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Female Genital Mutilation Act [2003 (c.31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/31).

A relevant third party can apply to the court for a Female Genital Mutilation protection order without the leave of the court.
