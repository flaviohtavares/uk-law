# The Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 48

Pensions

The Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

4th March 2021

Coming into operation

6th April 2021

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and now vested in the Department for Communities[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has made an Order[^f00003] under section 109(4) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004].

Accordingly the Department for Communities makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2021.

(2) In this Order “relevant period” has the same meaning as in section 109(3A)[^f00005] of the Pension Schemes Act 1993.

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Increase of guaranteed minimum pensions

For the purposes of section 105(1) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, the percentage by which there is to be an increase of the rate of that part of guaranteed minimum pensions which is attributable to earnings factors for the tax years in the relevant period is 0∙5 per cent.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 4th March 2021

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which corresponds to an Order ([S.I. 2021/163](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/163)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under section 109(4) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993, specifies 0∙5 per cent. as the percentage by which that part of guaranteed minimum pensions attributable to earnings factors for the tax years 1988-1989 to 1996-1997, and payable by contracted-out, defined benefit occupational pension schemes, is to be increased.
