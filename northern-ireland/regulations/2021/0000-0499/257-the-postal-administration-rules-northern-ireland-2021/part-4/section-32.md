# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Minutes

(1) The chair of the meeting shall cause minutes of its proceedings to be kept.

(2) The minutes shall be authenticated by the chair, and be retained by the chair as part of the records of the postal administration.

(3) The minutes shall include—

- (a) a list of the names of creditors who attended (personally or by proxy) and their claims; and
- (b) a record of every resolution passed.
