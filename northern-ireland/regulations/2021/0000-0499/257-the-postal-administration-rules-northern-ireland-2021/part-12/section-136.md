# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 136

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 136. Retention of proxies

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) of this Rule, proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chairman shall deliver the proxies, forthwith after the meeting, to the postal administrator (where that is someone other than the chair).
