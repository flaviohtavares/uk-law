# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Application to replace

(1) Where an application is made to court under paragraph 92(1) to appoint a replacement postal administrator, the application shall be accompanied by a written statement made in Form PA2 by the person proposed to be the replacement postal administrator.

(2) A copy of the application shall be served, in addition to those persons listed in section 70(2) of the 2011 Act and Rule 8(3), on the person who made the application for the postal administration order.

(3) Rule 10 shall apply to the service of an application under paragraph 92(1) as it applies to service in accordance with Rule 8.

(4) Rules 11, 12, and 13(1) and (2) apply to an application under paragraph 92(1).
