# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Notice of vacation of office when postal administrator ceases to be qualified to act

Where the postal administrator who has ceased to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company gives notice in accordance with paragraph 90, the postal administrator shall also give notice to the registrar.
