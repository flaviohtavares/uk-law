# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint postal administrator

Where, after an initial appointment has been made, an additional person or persons are to be appointed as joint postal administrator the same Rules shall apply in respect of giving notice of and advertising the appointment as in the case of the initial appointment, subject to Rule 93.
