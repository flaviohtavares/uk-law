# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Extent

The Rules apply in relation to a company which is a universal service provider which the High Court in Northern Ireland has jurisdiction to wind up.
