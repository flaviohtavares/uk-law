# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Notice etc to joint postal administrators

Where there are joint postal administrators, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
