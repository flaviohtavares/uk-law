# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 186

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 186. Charges for copy documents

Except where prohibited by the Rules, a postal administrator is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee for the supply of documents requested by a creditor or member.
