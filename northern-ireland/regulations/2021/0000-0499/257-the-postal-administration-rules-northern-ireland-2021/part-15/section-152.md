# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 152. Personal delivery of documents

Personal delivery of a notice or other document is permissible in any case.
