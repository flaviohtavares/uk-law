# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 167

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 167. Indication to excluded person

(1) A person who claims to be an excluded person may request an indication of what occurred during the period of that person’s claimed exclusion (an “indication”).

(2) A request under paragraph (1) of this Rule shall be made forthwith and, in any event, no later than 4.00 pm on the business day following the day on which the exclusion is claimed to have occurred.

(3) A request under paragraph (1) of this Rule shall be made to—

- (a) the chair, where it is made during the course of the business of the meeting; or
- (b) the postal administrator where it is made after the conclusion of the business of the meeting.

(4) Where satisfied that the person making the request is an excluded person, the person to whom the request is made under paragraph (3) of this Rule shall give the indication forthwith and, in any event, no later than 4.00 pm on the business day following the day on which the request was made under paragraph (1) of this Rule.
