# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 181

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 181. Notifications relating to other events

In addition to the information required by Rule 176, a notification relating to any other event (for example the coming in to force of a moratorium) shall specify—

- (a) the nature of the event including the Article of the 1989 Order or Rule under which it took place; and
- (b) the date the event occurred.
