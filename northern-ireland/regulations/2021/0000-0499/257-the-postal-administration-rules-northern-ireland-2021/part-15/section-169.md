# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 169

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 169. Forms for use in postal administration proceedings

(1) The forms contained in Schedule 1 to the Rules shall be used in postal administration proceedings as provided for in specific Rules.

(2) The forms shall be used with such variations, if any, as the circumstances may require.

(3) The Secretary of State or a postal administrator may incorporate a barcode or other reference or recognition mark into any form in Schedule 1 to the Rules a copy of which is received by any of them or is sent to any person by any of them.
