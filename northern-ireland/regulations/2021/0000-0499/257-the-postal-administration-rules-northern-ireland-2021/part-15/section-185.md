# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 185

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 185. Right to copy documents

Where the 1989 Order or the Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 116 of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00019], and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
