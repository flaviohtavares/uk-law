# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 174

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 174. Non-Gazette notices—other provisions

(1) The information required to be contained in a notice to which this Chapter applies shall be included in the advertisement of that notice in a manner that is reasonably likely to ensure, in relation to the form of the advertising used, that a person reading, hearing or seeing the advertisement, will be able to read, hear or see that information.

(2) Information required under this Chapter to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
