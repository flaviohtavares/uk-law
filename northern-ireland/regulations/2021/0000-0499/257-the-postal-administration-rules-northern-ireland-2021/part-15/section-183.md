# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 183

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 183. Notifications made to other persons at the same time

(1) Where under the 1989 Order or the Rules a notice or other document is to be sent to another person at the same time that it is to be sent to the registrar, that requirement may be satisfied by sending to that other person a copy of the notification sent to the registrar.

(2) Paragraph (1) of this Rule does not apply—

- (a) where a form is prescribed for the notification to the other person; or
- (b) where the notification to the registrar is incomplete.
