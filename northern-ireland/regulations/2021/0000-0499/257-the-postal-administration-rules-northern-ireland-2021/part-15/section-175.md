# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 175

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 175. Application of this Chapter

This Chapter applies where under the 1989 Order or the Rules information is to be sent or delivered to the registrar.
