# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 188

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 188. Time

The provisions of Order 3 of the Rules of Court of Judicature except rules 3 and 6 apply, as regards computation, extension and abridgement of time, to anything required or authorised to be done by the Rules.
