# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 189

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 189. Postal administrator’s security

(1) Wherever under the Rules any person has to appoint a person to the office of postal administrator that person shall, before making the appointment, be satisfied that the person appointed or to be appointed has security for the proper performance of the office of postal administrator.

(2) In any postal administration proceedings the cost of the postal administrator’s security shall be defrayed as an expense of the postal administration.
