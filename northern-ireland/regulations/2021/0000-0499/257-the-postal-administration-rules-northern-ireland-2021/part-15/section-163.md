# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 163

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 163. Application of Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature to service of court documents outside the jurisdiction

Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies to the service of court documents outside the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.
