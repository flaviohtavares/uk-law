# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 156

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 156. Application

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (3) of this Rule, this Chapter applies where a notice or other document is required to be given, delivered or sent under the 1989 Order or the Rules.

(2) This Chapter does not apply to the submission of documents to the registrar.

(3) Rule 160 does not apply to the filing of any notice or other document with the court.
