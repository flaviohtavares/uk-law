# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 155

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 155. Notice etc to solicitors

Where under the 1989 Order or the Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given, delivered or sent to a person, it may be given, delivered or sent instead to a solicitor authorised to accept delivery on that person’s behalf.
