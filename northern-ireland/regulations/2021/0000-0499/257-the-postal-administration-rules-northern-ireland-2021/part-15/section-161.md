# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 161

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 161. Application of Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature to service of court documents within the jurisdiction

(1) Except where different provision is made in the Rules, Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies in relation to the service of court documents within the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.

(2) In Order 65 rule 7, the expression “other originating process” does not include any application in insolvency proceedings.
