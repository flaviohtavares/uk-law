# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 197

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 197. “Venue”

References to the “venue” for any proceeding or attendance before the court, or for a meeting, are to the time, date and place for the proceeding, attendance or meeting.
