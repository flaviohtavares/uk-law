# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 199

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 199. Application

The Rules apply to postal administration proceedings commenced on or after the date on which the Rules come into operation. Nothing in the Insolvency Rules shall apply to such proceedings commenced on or after that date.
