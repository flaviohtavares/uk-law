# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 194

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 194. “The court”; “the Master”

(1) Anything to be done under or by virtue of the 1989 Order or the Rules by, to or before the court may be done by, to or before a judge of the High Court or the Master.

(2) The Judge or Master may authorise any act of a formal or administrative character which is not by statute the Judge or Master’s responsibility to be carried out by any officer of the court acting on the Master’s behalf, in accordance with directions given by the Chancery Judge.

(3) “the Master” means the Master (Bankruptcy).
