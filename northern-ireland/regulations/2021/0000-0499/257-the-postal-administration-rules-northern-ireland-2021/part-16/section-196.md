# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 196

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 196. “Petitioner”

In winding-up, references to “the petitioner” include any person who has been substituted as such.
