# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 198

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 198. Expressions used generally

(1) “Business day” has the same meaning as in Article 5 of the Order.

(2) “File with the court” means deliver to the court for filing.

(3) “The Gazette” means the Belfast Gazette.

(4) “gazetted” means advertised once in the Gazette.

(5) “Prescribed part” has the same meaning as it does in Article 150A(2)(a) of the 1989 Order.

(6) “Standard contents” means—

- (a) in relation to a notice to be gazetted, the contents specified in Rule 170; and
- (b) in relation to a notice to be advertised in any other way, the contents specified in Rule 173.
