# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 147. Costs, expenses, etc

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the postal administration are to be regarded as expenses of the postal administration.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part shall be paid out of the prescribed part.
