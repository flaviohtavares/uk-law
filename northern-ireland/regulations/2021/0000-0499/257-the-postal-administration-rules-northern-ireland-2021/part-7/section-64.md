# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Realisation of security by creditor

(1) If a creditor who has valued their security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the postal administrator)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security; and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by them.
