# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Payments of a periodical nature

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date when the company entered postal administration.

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
