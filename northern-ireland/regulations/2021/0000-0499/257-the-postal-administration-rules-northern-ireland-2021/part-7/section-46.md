# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. New postal administrator appointed

(1) If a new postal administrator is appointed in place of another, the former postal administrator shall forthwith transmit to the new postal administrator all proofs which the former postal administrator has received, together with an itemised list of them.

(2) The new postal administrator shall authenticate the list by way of receipt for the proofs, and return it to the new postal administrator’s predecessor.

(3) From then on, all proofs of debt shall be sent to and retained by the new postal administrator.
