# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 145

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 145. Costs of proceedings under Article 200

(1) Where the court has ordered an examination of any person under Article 200, and it appears to it that the examination was made necessary because information had been unjustifiably refused by the respondent, it may order that the costs of the examination be paid by the respondent.

(2) Where the court makes an order against a person under section 201(1) or (2) of the 1989 Order (court’s enforcement powers under Article 200) the costs of the application for the Order may be ordered by the court to be paid by the respondent.

(3) Subject to paragraphs (1) and (2) of this Rule, the postal administrator’s costs shall, unless the court otherwise orders, be paid out of the assets of the company.

(4) A person summoned to attend for examination under this Chapter shall be tendered a reasonable sum in respect of travelling expenses incurred in connection with their attendance. Other costs falling on them are at the court’s discretion.
