# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Proof of service

(1) Service of the application shall be verified by an affidavit of service.

(2) The affidavit of service shall be sufficient to identify the application served and shall specify—

- (a) the name and registered number of the company;
- (b) the address of the registered office of the company;
- (c) the name of the applicant;
- (d) that the application was made to the High Court and the court reference number;
- (e) the date of the application;
- (f) whether the copy served was a sealed copy;
- (g) the date on which service was effected;
- (h) the manner in which service was effected.

(3) The affidavit of service, with a sealed copy of the application exhibited to it, shall be filed with the court forthwith after service, and in any event not less than 1 business day before the hearing of the application.
