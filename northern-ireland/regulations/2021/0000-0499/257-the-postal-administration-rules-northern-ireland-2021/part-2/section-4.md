# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Affidavit in support of administration application

Where it is proposed to apply to the court for a postal administration order to be made in relation to a company, the administration application shall be in Form PA1 and an affidavit complying with Rule 6 shall be prepared and sworn with a view to its being filed with the court in support of the application.
