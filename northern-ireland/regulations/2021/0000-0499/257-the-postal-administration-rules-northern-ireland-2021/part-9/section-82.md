# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Moving from postal administration to dissolution

(1) Where, for the purposes of paragraph 85(1), the postal administrator sends a notice of moving from postal administration to dissolution to the registrar, the postal administrator shall attach to that notice a final progress report.

(2) Forthwith a copy of the notice and the attached document shall be sent to all other persons who received notice of the postal administrator’s appointment.

(3) Where a court makes an order under paragraph 85(7) it shall, where the applicant is not the postal administrator, give a copy of the order to the postal administrator.

(4) OFCOM shall notify the Secretary of State before directing the postal administrator to deliver a notice of moving from postal administration to dissolution to the registrar.
