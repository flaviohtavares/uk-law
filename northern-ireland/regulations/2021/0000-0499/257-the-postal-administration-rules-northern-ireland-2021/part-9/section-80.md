# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Notification by postal administrator of court order

(1) Where the court makes an order to end the postal administration, the postal administrator shall send to the registrar a copy of the court order and a copy of the postal administrator’s final progress report.

(2) Forthwith, the postal administrator shall send a copy of the order and the final progress report to all other persons who received notice of the postal administrator’s appointment.
