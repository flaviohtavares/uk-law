# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Costs and expenses of witnesses

(1) Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court shall be made to an officer of the company to which the proceedings relate.

(2) A person making any application in postal administration proceedings shall not be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the application, but the Taxing Master may allow their expenses of travelling and subsistence.
