# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 108

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 108. Enforcement of court orders

(1) In any postal administration proceedings under the Rules, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
