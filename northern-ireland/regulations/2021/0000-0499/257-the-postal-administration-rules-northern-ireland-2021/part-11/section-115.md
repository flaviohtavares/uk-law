# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Costs paid otherwise than out of the assets of the company

Where a bill of costs is taxed under an order of the court directing that those costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the assets of the company, the Taxing Master shall note on the certificate of taxation, by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
