# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Introductory

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where in postal administration proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who is incapable of managing and administering their property and affairs either—

- (a) by reason of mental disorder within the meaning of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “the incapacitated person”.
