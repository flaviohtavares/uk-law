# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Affidavits—general

(1) Subject to Rule 104, where evidence is required by the 1989 Order or the Rules as to any matter, such evidence may be provided in the form of an affidavit unless—

- (a) in any specific case a Rule or the 1989 Order makes different provision; or
- (b) the court otherwise directs.

(2) The court may, on the application of any party to the matter in question order the attendance for cross-examination of the person making the affidavit.

(3) Where, after such an order has been made, the person in question does not attend, that person’s affidavit shall not be used in evidence without the permission of the court.
