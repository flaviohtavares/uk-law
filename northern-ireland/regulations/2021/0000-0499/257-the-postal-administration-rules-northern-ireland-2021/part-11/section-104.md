# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Use of reports

(1) A report may be filed in court by the postal administrator instead of an affidavit, unless the application involves other parties or the court otherwise orders.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of an affidavit, the report shall be treated for the purposes of Rule 103 and any hearing before the court as if it were an affidavit.

(3) Where the affidavit is made by the postal administrator, the affidavit shall state the address at which the postal administrator works.
