# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 99

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 99. Notice of application under Article 150A(5) of the 1989 Order

An application under Article 150A(5) of the 1989 Order may be made without the application being served upon or notice being given to any other party.
