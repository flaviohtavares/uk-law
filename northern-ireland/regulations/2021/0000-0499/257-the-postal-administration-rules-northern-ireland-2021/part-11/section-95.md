# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in postal administration proceedings under the Rules, except an application for a postal administration order.
