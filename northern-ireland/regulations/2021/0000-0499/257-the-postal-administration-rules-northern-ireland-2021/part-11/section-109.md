# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Orders enforcing compliance with the Rules

(1) The court may, on application by the postal administrator, make such orders as it thinks necessary for the enforcement of obligations falling on any person in accordance with—

- (a) paragraph 48 (duty to submit statement of affairs in postal administration), or
- (b) Article 199 of the 1989 Order[^f00015] (duty of various persons to co-operate with postal administrator).

(2) An order of the court under this Rule may provide that all costs of and incidental to the application for it shall be borne by the person against whom the order is made.

## Annotations

[^f00015]: Footnote: Article 199(4)(a) was substituted by paragraph 35 of Schedule 2 to, the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1455 (N.I. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/1455)).
