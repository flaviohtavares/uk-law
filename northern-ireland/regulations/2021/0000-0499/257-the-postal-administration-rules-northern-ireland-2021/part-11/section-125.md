# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Procedure on appeal

(1) An appeal under the Order or the Rules, against a decision at first instance may only be brought with either the permission of the court which made the decision or the permission of the court which has jurisdiction to hear the appeal.

(2) An appellant shall file an appellant’s notice within 21 days after the date of the decision of the court that the appellant wishes to appeal.

(3) The procedure set out in Order 58 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies to any appeal to which this Chapter applies.
