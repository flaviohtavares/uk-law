# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Nomination and appointment of shorthand writers

(1) In the High Court the judge or Master may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.

(2) The court may, at any time in the course of the postal administration proceedings, appoint a shorthand writer to take down the evidence of a person examined under Article 200 of the 1989 Order.
