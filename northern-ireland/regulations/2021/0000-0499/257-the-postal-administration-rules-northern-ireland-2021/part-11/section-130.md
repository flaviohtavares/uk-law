# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 130

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 130. Payment into court

Order 22 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature (miscellaneous provisions about payment into court) applies to money lodged in court under the Rules.
