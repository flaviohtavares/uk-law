# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 131

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 131. Discovery

(1) Any party to postal administration proceedings may apply to the court for an order—

- (a) that any other party
  - (i) clarify any matter which is in dispute in the proceedings, or
  - (ii) give additional information in relation to any such matter, or
- (b) to obtain discovery from any other party, except so far as is otherwise provided by the Rules.

(2) An application under this Rule may be made without notice to any other party..
