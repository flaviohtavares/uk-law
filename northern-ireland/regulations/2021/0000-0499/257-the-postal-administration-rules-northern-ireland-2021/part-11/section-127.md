# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 127. Right of audience

Rights of audience in postal administration proceedings are the same as in insolvency proceedings.
