# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Principal court rules and practice to apply

Except so far as is inconsistent with the Rules, the Rules of the Court of Judicature and the practice of the High Court apply to postal administration proceedings with any necessary modifications.
