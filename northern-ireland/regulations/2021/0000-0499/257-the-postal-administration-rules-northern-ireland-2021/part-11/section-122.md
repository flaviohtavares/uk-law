# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Affidavit in support of application

An application under Rule 121(3) shall, subject to paragraph (2), be supported by an affidavit made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
