# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Appeals and reviews of postal administration orders

(1) The High Court may review, rescind or vary any order made by it in the exercise of its jurisdiction as regards postal administration proceedings.

(2) Appeals from decisions made in the exercise of that jurisdiction lie as follows—

- (a) to a single judge of the High Court from an order or decision of the Master, without prejudice to the power of the Master to review an order made under Article 371,
- (b) to the Court of Appeal from a decision of a single judge of the High Court.

(3) Order 58, rule 1(2) to (4) of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies to such an appeal, with the substitution in paragraph (3) of the words “28 days” for the words “5 days” and the words “7 days” for the words “2 clear days”.
