# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 129

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 129. Service of orders staying proceedings

Where in postal administration proceedings the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of the company, service of the order may be effected by sending a sealed copy of the order to whatever is the address for service of the plaintiff or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
