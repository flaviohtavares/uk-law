# The Postal Administration Rules (Northern Ireland) 2021, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Hearings without notice

Where the relevant provisions of the 1989 Order or the Rules do not require service of the application on, or notice of it to be given to, any person—

- (a) the court may hear the application forthwith without fixing a date as required by Rule 98(2); or
- (b) it may fix a date for the application to be heard in which case Rule 98 will apply to the extent that it is relevant;

but nothing in those provisions is to be taken as prohibiting the applicant from giving such notice if the applicant wishes to do so.
