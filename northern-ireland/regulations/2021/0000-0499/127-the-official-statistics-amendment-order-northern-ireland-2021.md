# The Official Statistics (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 127

Official Statistics

The Official Statistics (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Affirmed by resolution of the Assembly on 14th June 2021

Made

*13th May 2021*

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6 (1)(b)(iv) and (2) of the Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007 [^f00002], makes the following Order.

In accordance with section 6 (3) of that Act, the Department of Finance has consulted the Statistics Board [^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Official Statistics (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 1st July 2021.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^f00004] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Amendments to the Official Statistics Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

The Official Statistics Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 [^f00005] is amended in accordance with Article 4.

**4.** In the Schedule to the Official Statistics Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, where alphabetically appropriate, insert the persons listed in the Schedule to this Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 13th May 2021

(L.S.)

Tracy Power

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance

13th May 2021

## SCHEDULE — PERSONS SPECIFIED AS PRODUCERS OF OFFICIAL STATISTICS

Articles 3 and 4

Education Authority

Invest Northern Ireland

Labour Relations Agency

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends The Official Statistics Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 (″the Order″) by the addition to the Schedule of the persons specified in the Schedule to this Order.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this statutory rule as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
