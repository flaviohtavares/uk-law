# The Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 72

Building And Buildings

The Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

23rd March 2021

Coming into operation

1st April 2021

The Department of Finance[^f00001] being an appropriate authority for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 4 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00002] and in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 4 of the said Act, hereby makes the following regulations.

In accordance with paragraph 12(3) of Schedule 7 to that Act a draft of these Regulations was laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

The Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00003] are amended in accordance with the following regulation.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 25A

In regulation 25A(Fees for entering data onto the register)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£1.86” substitute “£1.64”; and
- (b) in paragraph (a), for “£9.84” substitute “£1.89”

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 23rd March 2021

Desmond C McDonnell

A senior officer of the

Department

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the Energy Performance of Buildings (Certificates and Inspections) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 (“**the principal Regulations**”)[^f00004]. The principal Regulations implement in part, [Directive 2010/31/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0031) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 May 2010 on the energy performance of buildings (recast)[^f00005] (“**the Directive**”). That Directive was amended by Directive (EU) 2018/844 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018[^f00006]. The principal regulations were made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00007]. That Act was repealed by section 1 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00008] but the principal regulations continue to have effect as retained EU law by virtue of section 2(2) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

Regulation 3 amends the fees for entering data onto the register for energy performance certificates, display energy certificates and air conditioning inspection reports kept under regulation 25 (Registration of certificates etc.) of the principal regulations.
