# The Edible Crabs (Undersized) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 335

Sea Fisheries

The Edible Crabs (Undersized) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

8th December 2021

Coming into operation-

8th January 2022

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 127(1) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Edible Crabs (Undersized) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and comes into operation on 8th January 2022.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] applies to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Edible Crabs (Undersized) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

(1) The Edible Crabs (Undersized) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00004] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Article 3 for “140” substitute “150”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 8th December 2021.

Owen Lyttle

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the minimum landing size of edible (brown) crab to 150 millimetres carapace width.
