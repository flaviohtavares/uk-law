# The Mental Health (1986 Order) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 8

Mental Health

The Mental Health (1986 Order) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

15th January 2021

Coming into operation

18th January 2021

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 64(2) of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the Mental Health (1986 Order) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on the 18th January 2021.

## 2. Amendment of Article 64(1)(b) of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986

In Article 64(1)(b)[^f00003], for “three months” substitute “six months”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 15th January 2021

(L.S.)

Mark Lee

A senior officer of the Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the length of the period which must elapse for a second opinion to be required for the continued administration of medicine to detained patients from three months to six months.
