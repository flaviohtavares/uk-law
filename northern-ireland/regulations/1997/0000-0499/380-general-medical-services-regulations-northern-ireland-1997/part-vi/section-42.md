# General Medical Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 42

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Appointment of medical adviser[^key-5d3e3d7f95503dd179ac38b17c40db30]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A Board shall appoint a doctor to assist it in the exercise of its functions pursuant to paragraph 55 of the terms of service for doctors (inquiries about prescriptions and referrals).
