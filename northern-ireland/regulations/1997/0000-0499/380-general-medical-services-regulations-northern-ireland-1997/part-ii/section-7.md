# General Medical Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. The Medical Committee[^c-cfa25f40d66e25bfb709163a5d587f7d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Medical Committee shall be constituted by the Agency in accordance with Schedule 14 for the purpose of carrying out duties in connection with the filling of medical practice vacancies and such other duties as the Agency thinks fit.
