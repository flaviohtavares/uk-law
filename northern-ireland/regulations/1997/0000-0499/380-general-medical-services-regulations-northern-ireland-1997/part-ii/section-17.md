# General Medical Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Amendment of local directory[^key-d56b5aae168afad7e8b4b45c72b4db56]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A doctor shall, unless it is impracticable for him to do so, notify the Board within 28 days of any occurrence requiring a change in the information recorded about him in the local directory.

(2) The Board shall, in the event of a notification pursuant to paragraph (1), make any necessary amendment to the local directory.
