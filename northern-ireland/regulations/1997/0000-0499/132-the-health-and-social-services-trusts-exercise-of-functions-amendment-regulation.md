# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 132

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

6th March 1997

Coming into operation

1st April 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the power conferred on it by Article 3(2) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Amendment of the Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994

The Schedule to the Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- in the second column (Relevant functions of Health and Social Services Board), opposite the reference to the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00003], after “15,” there shall be inserted “15A,”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

P. A. Conliffe

Assistant Secretary

6th March 1997.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations, which amend the Health and Social Services Trusts (Exercise of Functions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, extend the list of Relevant Functions of a Health and Social Services Board to include functions under Article 15A of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 (as inserted by the Personal Social Services (Direct Payments) (Northern Ireland) Order 1996).
