# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 43

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Relationship with amounts to be disregarded under Schedule 3[^c-6f655fe846b567ce7ff5b62d5dde28a8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

No part of a student’s grant income shall be disregarded under paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 (sums to be disregarded in the determination of income other than earnings).
