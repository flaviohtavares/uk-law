# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Persons from abroad[^key-fee04c9636c710892a48bb439e7cc421]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

No grant shall be payable under Part III of the Order if the applicant, or any of the applicants, is a person from abroad within the meaning of regulation 7A of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00022] (persons from abroad).
