# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. The applicable amount[^key-194abb76a49e3072dacf35eb9138b265]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The applicable amount in respect of any one application shall be the aggregate of—

- (a) the total of the weekly applicable amounts of all those persons who are relevant persons in the case of that application, and
- (b) £40.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), the weekly applicable amount—

- (a) as regards a relevant person who is in receipt of income support or an income-based jobseeker’s allowance, is £1;
- (b) as regards any other relevant person, is the amount determined in his case in accordance with regulation 13.
