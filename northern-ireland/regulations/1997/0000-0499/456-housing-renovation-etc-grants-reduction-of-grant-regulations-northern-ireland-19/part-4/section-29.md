# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 29

## 29. Annuity treated as income[^c-b534cbfa7ac510557e2ec01c9d1f980a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any payment received under an annuity shall be treated as income.
