# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 33

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^c-28a3ee2d0bae75fa9af7a77f3de06474]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the relevant person’s family shall not be treated as capital of the relevant person.
