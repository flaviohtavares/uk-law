# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Income and capital of relevant person[^c-1a57be44f0e6a11ddb668a0de961597b]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a relevant person is a member of a family, the income and capital of any member of that family shall, except where otherwise provided, be treated as the income and capital of that person.
