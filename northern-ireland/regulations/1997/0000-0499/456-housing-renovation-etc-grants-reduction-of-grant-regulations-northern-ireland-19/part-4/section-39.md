# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Determination of tariff income from capital[^key-fbc3696746613d9c9e449a0ef19b27af]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where the relevant person’s capital determined in accordance with this Part exceeds £5,000 it shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly tariff income of £1 for each complete £250 in excess of £5,000.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) where any part of the excess is not a complete £250 that part shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly tariff income of £1.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1), capital includes any income treated as capital under regulation 33 (income treated as capital).
