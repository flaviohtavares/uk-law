# Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 32

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Determination of capital[^c-57b50f1dee7c677ebb7f4ca949dd2b60]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The capital of a relevant person to be taken into account shall, subject to paragraph (2), be the whole of his capital determined in accordance with this Part and any income treated as capital under regulation 34 (income treated as capital).

(2) There shall be disregarded from the determination of a relevant person’s capital under paragraph (1), any capital, where applicable, specified in Schedule 4.
