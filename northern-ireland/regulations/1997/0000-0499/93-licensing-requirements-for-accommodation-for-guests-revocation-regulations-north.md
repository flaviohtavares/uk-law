# Licensing (Requirements for Accommodation for Guests) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 93

LICENSING

Licensing (Requirements for Accommodation for Guests) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

21st February 1997

Coming into operation

25th March 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 46(3) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, with the concurrence of the Department of Economic Development, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Revocation

These Regulations revoke the Licensing (Requirements for Accommodation for Guests) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00002].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Jeremy Harbison

Under Secretary

21st February 1997.

The Department of Economic Development hereby concurs with the foregoing Regulations

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on

Malcolm J. Briant

Assistant Secretary

24th February 1997.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These regulations revoke the Licensing (Requirements for Accommodation for Guests) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988.
