# Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Revocation) Regulations 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 325

FISHERIES

Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Revocation) Regulations 1997

Made

24th June 1997

Coming into operation

1st July 1997

The Foyle Fisheries Commission in exercise of its powers under Section 13 of the Foyle Fisheries Act 1952[^f00001] and section 13 of the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952[^f00002] with the approval of the Minister of the Marine in the Republic of Ireland[^f00003] and the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland[^f00004] hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Revocation) Regulations 1997 and shall come into operation on 1st July 1997.

## 2. Revocation

The Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) Regulations 1977[^f00005] and the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00006] are hereby revoked.

Given under the Common Seal of the Foyle Fisheries Commission on

N. McCutcheon

Member of the Commission appointed by the Minister for the Marine

24th June 1997.

The Minister for the Marine hereby approves the foregoing Regulations

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Minister for the Marine on

D. Doyle

A person authorised by the Minister for the Marine to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister

J. S. Allister

Member of the Commission appointed by the

Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland

26th June 1997

The Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland hereby approves the foregoing Regulations

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland

P. T. Toal

Assistant Secretary

Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland

on 30th June 1997

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) Regulations 1977 and the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 which controlled the netting of fish and other forms of angling in the waters of the Foyle area.
