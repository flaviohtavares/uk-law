1997 No. 78

REGISTRATION OF CLUBS

Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

21st February 1997

Coming into operation

25th March 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 6(1) of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-8f36250a8d44c04ddf6647e2974b2838]

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 25th March 1997.

## 2. Form of Certificate of Registration[^c-9e20121dd5f9e7aacc2fd419d6808d6f]

A certificate of registration shall be in the form set out in the Schedule and shall in addition to the matters specified in Article 6(1)(*a*) to (*d*) of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 also specify the matters prescribed in Parts IV to VI of that Schedule.

## 3. Revocation[^c-cce7481f11ac570a9b09b0202fe63083]

The Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Jeremy Harbison

Under Secretary

21st February 1997.

## SCHEDULE[^c-e45172536f648f39607bac000ed7974c]

Regulation 2

**REGISTRATION OF CLUBS (NORTHERN IRELAND) ORDER 1996**

**CERTIFICATE OF REGISTRATION**

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/78/images/nisr_19970078_en_001) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/78/images/nisr_19970078_en_002) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/78/images/nisr_19970078_en_003) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/78/images/nisr_19970078_en_004)

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These regulations—

- (a) set out the form of a certificate of registration to be issued and renewed by the courts in respect of clubs registered under the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 for the supply and keeping for supply of intoxicating liquor on club premises; and
- (b) prescribe certain matters which that certificate must specify.

The regulations revoke the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988.
