# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> Text as at 2021-07-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1997 No. 206

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

7th April 1997

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation

28th May 1997

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 50(4)(*a*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-d7f4450369af6e59e4bbcf4737f1c594]

This Order may be cited as the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 28th May 1997.

## 2. Increase of speed limit[^c-205f85e9ca09ef65808ea73d46b217a6]

The rate of speed fixed by Article 50(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 shall be increased from 30 miles per hour to 40 miles per hour in relation to the restricted roads specified in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

7th April 1997.

## SCHEDULE — Restricted roads on which the speed limit is increased from 30 mph to 40 mph

Article 2

**1.**[^c-0b3b5e90e554ae81bb87eab8171514f6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**2.**[^c-d54bccc64edcf23b48a66201aca12db3] Toome Road, Route B18, Ballymena, from a point approximately 130 metres south-west of its junction with Camphill Park, to its junction with Old Tullygarley Road.

**3.**[^c-7c713bebe00a496d526940b0c3de7240] Tullygarley Road, Route C47, Ballymena, from its junction with Old Tullygarley Road, to its junction with Toome Road, Route B18.

**4.**[^c-ffe6a0e9bcbe4b6d94a83fa2201f7586] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**5.**[^c-b395572d19418307111fea44be65418c] Larne Road, Route A2, Whitehead, from a point approximately 100 metres north-west of its junction with Slaughterford Road, to a point approximately 870 metres south-east of that junction.

**6.**[^c-5a8aa217304d637f4a6ec6af2499ec2d] Rawbrae Road, Unclassified No. 14, Whitehead.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
