# Parking Places on Roads (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 1997-01-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1997/9

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

1997 No. 9

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Parking Places on Roads (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

14th January 1997

Coming into operation

3rd March 1997

WHEREAS it appears to the Department of the Environment to be necessary to provide suitable parking places for vehicles on the parts of the roads specified in Schedule 1;

NOW THEREFORE the Department, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 104(1)(*c*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-133bc521bdbeb21bfb42c0bcba43c64c]

This Order may be cited as the Parking Places on Roads (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 3rd March 1997.

## 2. Amendment[^c-2f5fcd5b6e19011803812548ba12fe26]

The Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended by—

- (a) the addition to Schedule 1 to that Order of the items in Schedule 1;
- (b) the deletion from Schedule 1 to that Order of the items in Schedule 2.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

14th January 1997.
