# Food Safety (Enforcement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 492

FOOD

Food Safety (Enforcement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

13th November 1997

Coming into operation

8th December 1997

The Department of Agriculture and the Department of Health and Social Services, acting jointly as the Department concerned, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 26(1A) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of every other power enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Safety (Enforcement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 8th December 1997.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order —

“dairy farm”—

- (a) means any farm on which milk is produced, but
- (b) does not include any part of any such premises on which milk is manufactured into other products;

- “licensed premises” means premises licensed under the Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00002], the Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003] or the Wild Game Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004];
- “liquid milk plant”—
  - (a) includes any premises—
    - (i) in which milk is received, stored, treated, processed and packaged; or
    - (ii) from which milk is supplied or distributed for sale; or
    - (iii) in which milk is kept or used for purposes of sale; or
    - (iv) in which vessels and equipment used for the sale of milk are kept, but
  - (b) does not include—
    - (i) any part of any such premises on which milk is manufactured into other products, or
    - (ii) premises from which milk is sold by retail;
- “meat plant” means—
  - (a) licensed premises;
  - (b) premises approved under the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00005] or the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00006]; or
  - (c) premises registered under the Food Premises (Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00007] and in which minced meat or meat preparations are produced;
- “meat preparations” has the meaning assigned to it by regulation 2 of the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997;
- “meat products” has the meaning assigned to it by regulation 2(1) of the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997;
- “milk” includes cream and skimmed, semi-skimmed or separated milk;
- “minced meat” has the meaning assigned to it by regulation 2 of the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997;
- “other products of animal origin” has the meaning assigned to it by regulation 2(1) of the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

## 3. Enforcement of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991

(1) For the purposes of Article 26(1A) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 and subject to paragraph (2) the whole of that Order is specified in the case of—

- (a) dairy farms;
- (b) liquid milk plants;
- (c) the importation of milk;
- (d) the transport of milk;
- (e) meat plants other than—
  - (i) meat plants which are not, and do not share a common curtilage with, licensed premises and which are used exclusively for the production or storage of one or more of the following—
    - (aa) meat products;
    - (bb) minced meat which is not intended for sending out of the United Kingdom;
    - (cc) meat preparations;
    - (dd) other products of animal origin;
  - (ii) meat plants or parts of meat plants which are, or share a common curtilage with, licensed premises and which are used exclusively for the production or storage of one or more of the following—
    - (aa) meat products;
    - (bb) other products of animal origin.

(2) Paragraph (1)(*e*) shall apply only in relation to the Department of Agriculture’s functions under the Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995, the Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, the Wild Game Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 to be enforced and executed by the Department of Agriculture in the case of certain premises and activities.
