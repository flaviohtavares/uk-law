# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 1997-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1997/113
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Order—

- “the Administration Act” means the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00006];
- “the Pension Schemes Act” means the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00007];
- “the Computation of Earnings Regulations” means the Social Security Benefit (Computation of Earnings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00008];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00009];
- “the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations” means the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00010];
- “the Personal Allowances for Children and Young Persons Regulations” means the Income-Related Benefits and Jobseeker’s Allowance (Personal Allowances for Children and Young Persons) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00011].

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00012] shall apply to this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Assembly.
