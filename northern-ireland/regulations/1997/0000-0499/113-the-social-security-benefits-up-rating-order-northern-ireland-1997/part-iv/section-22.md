# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 22

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Age-related amount of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (*a*) for “£28·85” there shall be substituted “£29·60”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (*b*) for “£37·90” there shall be substituted “£38·90”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (*c*) for “£47·90” there shall be substituted “£49·15”.
