# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 9(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00030] (increase in rate of incapacity benefit where beneficiary is under prescribed age on the qualifying date)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (*a*) for “£12·90” there shall be substituted “£13·15”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (*b*) for “£6·45” there shall be substituted “£6·60”.
