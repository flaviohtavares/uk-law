# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Child benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 2(1) of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00029] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (*a*)(i) for “£10·80” there shall be substituted “£11·05”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (*a*)(ii) the sum specified is £17·10; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (*b*) for “£8·80” there shall be substituted “£9·00”.
