# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Workmen’s compensation

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In paragraph 2(6)(*c*) of Schedule 8 to the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00022] (maximum weekly rate of lesser incapacity allowance supplementing workmen’s compensation) for “£36·60” there shall be substituted “£37·35”.

## Annotations

[^f00022]: Footnote: Paragraph 2(6)(*c*) was amended by Article 7 of [S.R. 1996 No. 73](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/73)
