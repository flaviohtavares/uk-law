# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Statutory maternity pay

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00025] (lower rate of statutory maternity pay) for “£54·55” there shall be substituted “£55·70”.
