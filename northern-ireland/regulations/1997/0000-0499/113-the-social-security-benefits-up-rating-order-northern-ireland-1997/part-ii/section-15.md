# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 18(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00031] (rate of long-term incapacity benefit in transitional cases)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (*a*) for “£12·90” there shall be substituted “£13·15”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (*b*) for “£8·10” there shall be substituted “£8·30”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (*c*) for “£4·05” there shall be substituted “£4·15”.
