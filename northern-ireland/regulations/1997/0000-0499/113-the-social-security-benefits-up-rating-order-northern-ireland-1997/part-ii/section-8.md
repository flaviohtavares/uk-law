## 8. Earnings limits

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00023] (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) the sum specified in paragraph (*a*) is £135·00 and the sums specified in paragraph (*b*) are £17·00 and £135·00 respectively.
