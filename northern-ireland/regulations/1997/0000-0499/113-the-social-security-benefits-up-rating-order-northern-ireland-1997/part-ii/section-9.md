# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Statutory sick pay

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 153(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00024] (rate of payment) for “£54·55” there shall be substituted “£55·70”.
