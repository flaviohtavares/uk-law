# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Disability living allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00028] (rate of benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(*a*) for “£48·50” there shall be substituted “£49·50”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(*b*) for “£32·40” there shall be substituted “£33·10”;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(*c*) for “£12·90” there shall be substituted “£13·15”;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(*a*) for “£33·90” there shall be substituted “£34·60”; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(*b*) for “£12·90” there shall be substituted “£13·15”.
