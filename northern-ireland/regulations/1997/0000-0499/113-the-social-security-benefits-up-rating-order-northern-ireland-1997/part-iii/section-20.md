# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 20

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. The relevant sum for income support

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 125(7) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00037] (trade disputes) for “£26·00” there shall be substituted “£26·50”.
