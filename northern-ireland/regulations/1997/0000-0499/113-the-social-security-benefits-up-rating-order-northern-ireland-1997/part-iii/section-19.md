# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 19

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Income support transitional protection

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00036] shall be increased by 2·6 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
