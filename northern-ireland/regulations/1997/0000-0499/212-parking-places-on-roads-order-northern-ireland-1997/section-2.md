# Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Authorisation of parking places

The use as parking places of the parts of the roads specified in Schedule 1 is hereby authorised.
