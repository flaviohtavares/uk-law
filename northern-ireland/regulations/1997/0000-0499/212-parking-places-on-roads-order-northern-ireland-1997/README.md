# Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 212

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

10th April 1997

Coming into operation

22nd May 1997

WHEREAS it appears to the Department of the Environment to be necessary to provide suitable parking places for vehicles on the parts of the roads specified in Schedule 1;

NOW THEREFORE the Department, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 104(1)(*c*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

10th April 1997.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order consolidates (with minor amendments) the Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 as amended.

The amendments are to the descriptions of the parking places in item Nos. 1, 8, 20, 36, 37, 52, 59, 60, 92, 138, 182, 183, 184, 185, 188, 190, 203, 226, 228, 230, 243, 246, 248, 260, 261, 279, 281, 283, 284, 285, 289, 310, 360, 365, 369, 379, 413, 428, 434, 438, 439, 440, 465, 471 and 475 of Schedule 1.

The Orders set out in Schedule 2 are revoked as a consequence of this Order.
