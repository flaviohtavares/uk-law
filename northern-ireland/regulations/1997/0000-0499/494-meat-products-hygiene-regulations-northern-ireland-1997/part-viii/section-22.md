## 22. Information to be provided by enforcement authorities[^c-3f872560c3530da0ac11f07e9d50f1cd]

*Extent: N.I.*

Every district council shall provide the Department with such information relating to the exercise of its powers or the execution of its duties under these Regulations as the Department may from time to time require.
