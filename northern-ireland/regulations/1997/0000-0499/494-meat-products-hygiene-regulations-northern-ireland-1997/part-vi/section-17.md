## 17. Transportation condition for other products of animal origin[^key-965ba45a19422cce28f9660c907e531a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person shall not sell for human consumption other products of animal origin unless they are accompanied during transportation by a commercial document which specifies the premises of origin and the name and address of the consignee.

(2) Any person who receives a consignment of other products of animal origin together with the commercial document required by paragraph (1) shall keep that document for at least one year after receipt, and make it available on request for inspection by an authorised officer of an enforcement authority.
