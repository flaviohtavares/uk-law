## 15. Conditions for premises manufacturing other products of animal origin[^c-6663f37ae8fccbd626c6b1b2ac13ac68]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not manufacture other products of animal origin unless the premises where the manufacture takes place meets the requirements of—

- (a) Schedule 1;
- (b) Part I of Schedule 5;
- (c) in the case of rendered animal fats, greaves and by-products, Part II of that Schedule; and
- (d) in the case of stomachs, bladders and intestines, Part III of that Schedule.
