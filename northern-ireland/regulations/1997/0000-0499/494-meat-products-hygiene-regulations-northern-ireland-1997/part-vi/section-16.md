# Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 16

## 16. Sale of other products of animal origin — meat not to have been declared unfit for human consumption[^key-65aa0ba2ebed22a3e8b408917aab9159]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not sell for human consumption other products of animal origin manufactured in Northern Ireland unless they have been derived from meat which has not been declared unfit for human consumption.
