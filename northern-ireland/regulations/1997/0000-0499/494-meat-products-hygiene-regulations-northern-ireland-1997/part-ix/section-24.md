# Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (53).** Text as at 1997-12-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1997/494
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Amendments[^c-043af8e1148702d955d0e1a52810cb00]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) After regulation 3(2)(*i*) of the Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 and of the Food Safety (Temperature Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00027], there shall be inserted in each case—

> - (j) the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

.

(2) At the end of regulation 3(2)(*d*) of the Food Premises (Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00028] there shall be added—

> or in respect of which an approval is required under the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997;

.
