# Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 9

## 9. Wrapping etc.[^key-c2cbe2996e4ff0ce1519d573cd6aea16]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not—

- (a) sell for human consumption from approved meat products premises any meat product manufactured in Northern Ireland which is intended for consignment to a relevant EEA State; or
- (b) sell for human consumption any meat product handled or stored in Northern Ireland after 8th December 1998,

unless it has been wrapped, packaged or labelled at manufacturing premises in accordance with the provisions of Part V of Schedule 2 or at an approved rewrapping centre.
