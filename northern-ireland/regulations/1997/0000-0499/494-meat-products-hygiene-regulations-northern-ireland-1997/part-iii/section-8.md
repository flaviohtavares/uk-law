# Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 8

## 8. General conditions[^c-071bf1ff9f6ccef215052698ef0eb563]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not sell for human consumption from meat products premises approved under regulation 4 any meat product manufactured in Northern Ireland unless—

- (a) it has been handled and stored in accordance with the appropriate requirements of Schedule 1, 2 and 3;
- (b) it has been prepared from raw materials which complied with the requirements of Part III of Schedule 2; and
- (c) it has been checked in accordance with regulation 13.
