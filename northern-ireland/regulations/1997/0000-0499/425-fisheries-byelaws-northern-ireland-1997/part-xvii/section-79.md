# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 79

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**79.**[^c-12b2857749a73a430797f5316b1fd86e] A person shall not have any net for the taking of salmon, trout or any coarse fish on board any boat in any waters during the annual close season or weekly close time for salmon, trout or that coarse fish.
