# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 84

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**84.**[^c-b7b177ea4ae29f471d4ab44f2d361cab] A person shall not have in his possession any fish referred to in Byelaw 82 which has been taken by a mode other than angling in any fisheries to which section 25 of the Act applies
