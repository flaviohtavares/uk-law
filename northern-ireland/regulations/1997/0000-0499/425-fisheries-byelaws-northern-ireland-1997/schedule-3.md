## SCHEDULE 3 — List of rivers on which the use of Ground Bait or Maggots is Prohibited whilst Angling

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Byelaw 69

**1.**[^c-e2a187a1bc2cb31f70398fa1f91f3b5d] Agivey.

**2.**[^c-f1cc1cd18b762a05aa082e1703899898] Ballinamallard upstream from Ballycassidy Bridge on the B.3 Road.

**3.**[^c-144dee22501a4132723bb961886af0f7] Ballinderry (County Tyrone and County Londonderry) upstream from the footbridge over the River in the townland of Lower Mullan.

**4.**[^c-4c046db1b6d3c84eb16f6907dfded78e] Ballymoney.

**6.**[^c-5c56840e7ee432fc9676c940be8c6ee1] Callan.

**7.**[^c-c447f80087236a9361ac5bae3e8f21a0] Clady (County Londonderry).

**8.**[^c-db4cc609d24a303669ad152d8a5dc3a9] Colebrooke upstream from Ballindarragh Bridge on the B.514 Road.

**9.**[^c-97330dd2fedf285d86664fc20fe74981] Comber.

**10.**[^c-f477b818feb5c6a88a16991b31daa75d] Crumlin.

**11.**[^c-a5e9bb4fd283ea50faf47a569a80e65d] Garvary.

**12.**[^c-6a0ed2940cc233d3301d98fb2740236b] Glenavy.

**13.**[^c-73c9bea625273784e28c0e4627e164be] Kesh upstream from Kesh Bridge on the A.35 Road.

**14.**[^c-b546017e3f4b844fe5880b4cb0e6a3df] Maine.

**15.**[^c-ff419d1ff176d6832c38a35afcdf2d2a] Moyola.

**16.**[^c-4328dd8a2bbdced8df1ce98aa77f4571] Quoile upstream from the disused Railway Bridge near Inch Abbey.

**17.**[^c-40fbac626f9679e2ae40129f1c37dbb1] Ravarnette.

**18.**[^c-5854e449c609a5896bc4637e672c2c1f] Six Mile Water upstream from the Antrim Forum in Antrim Town on the A.26 Road.

**19.**[^c-3f5e6550a4c7e5385d7edc025f4ed647] Torrent upstream from the B.28 road bridge.

**20.**[^c-59b7c026300320363d1db1724bd470df] Upper Bann upstream from the B.2 road crossing.
