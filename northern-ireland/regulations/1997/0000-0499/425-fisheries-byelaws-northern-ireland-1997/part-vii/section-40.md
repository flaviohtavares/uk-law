# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 40

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**40.**[^c-d07102769d64835661f8239156b6ee27] A person shall not have in his possession or control on the waters of Lough Neagh or on or near the banks of the waters of Lough Neagh, a single wall set net or trammel net, for the taking of salmon or freshwater fish, with a mesh size of less than 27 millimetres from knot to knot or 108 millimetres to be measured all round each mesh.
