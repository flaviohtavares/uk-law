# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. General rules on fishing licences[^c-267670a7622ad3d9a177461594d2ac96]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

On application made in such form as it may require and accompanied by the appropriate amount of duty the Board shall, subject to Byelaw 24, issue to the person making application or renew a fishing licence in respect of any fishing engine subject to such conditions as may be set out in the licence.

**18.**[^c-3f2a937a4bdbee5b7b2cbf7b88e566ec] All fishing licences issued in pursuance to these Byelaws shall be valid only during the period stated thereon from the date and time of issue.

**19.**[^c-69bd65bd022de2f693eec9ab195484dc] Fishing licences issued under these Byelaws in respect of any fishing engine shall not authorise the use of that fishing engine for the taking or killing of fish other than those specified on the licence.

**20.**[^c-f9503cbb6d4598bb06b8de6751410e72] All fishing licences issued under these Byelaws (other than coarse fishing rod licences, game fishing rod licences and drift net licences) shall be valid only when used by the licence-holder or his agent previously authorised in writing by him.

**21.**[^c-540e32195457bd2f4bd48ed6e38d79cf] A fishing licence for angling shall be in the form and contain the conditions set out in Schedule 5.

**22.**[^c-c900c8853807332a4dd8243485fd5ca4] A commercial fishing licence shall be in the form and contain the conditions set out in Schedule 6.

**23.**[^c-35b403de2b2568734a1a12959c96b5b7] A licence mentioned in Byelaw 21 or 22 may be issued by a distributor approved by the Board in writing in that behalf.

**24.**[^c-e3ea62bcbcd732805ac594d68dc4ea29] Where a person has been convicted of any offence under the Act the Board may (subject to the provisions of these Byelaws) revoke any fishing licence held by such person or (as the case may be) refuse to issue such person a fishing licence or renew any fishing licence held by such person.
