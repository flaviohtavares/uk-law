# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 29

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**29.**[^key-57ca1bc4510625f150c09d6f112e2219] Where on an appeal under this Part, the Department reverses the Board’s decision, the Board shall forthwith issue to the appellant or renew the fishing licence for which the application was made by him, or (as the case may be) restore the fishing licence which was revoked.
