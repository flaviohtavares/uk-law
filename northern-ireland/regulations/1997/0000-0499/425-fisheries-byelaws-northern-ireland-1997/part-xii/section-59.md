# Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 59

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**59.**[^key-d12c55d6fc8dcec7b503e98ce17f1225] A person shall not use for the taking of salmon in the sea any drift net containing more than 45 full meshes measured vertically between the top rope and the bottom rope of the net.
