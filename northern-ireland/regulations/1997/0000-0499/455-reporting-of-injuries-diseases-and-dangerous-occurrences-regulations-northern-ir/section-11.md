# Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 11

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Defence in proceedings for an offence contravening these Regulations[^key-17f1db18aac4aaac1534bee04764bd8c]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

It shall be a defence in proceedings against any person for an offence under these Regulations for that person to prove that he was not aware of the event requiring him to notify or send a report to the enforcing authority and that he had taken all reasonable steps to have all such events brought to his notice.
