# Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-b43ed20a5858f7796252005abb6265b7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and, subject to paragraph (2), shall come into operation on 1st April 1998.

(2) Regulations 14 and 15(3) and Schedule 9 shall come into operation on 1st December 1997.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**1.** These Regulations—

- (a) revoke and re-enact with modifications, in a consolidated form, the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1986 ([S.R. 1986 No. 247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1986/247)) and certain provisions of the Offshore Installations (Inspectors and Casualties) Regulations 1973 ([S.I. 1973/1842](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1973/1842)) and the Submarine Pipe-lines (Inspectors etc.) Regulations 1977 ([S.I. 1977/835](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/835)) (see paragraph 2), and
- (b) repeal a provision of the Quarries (Northern Ireland) Order 1983 ([S.I. 1983/150 (N.I. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1983/150)) and make a consequential amendment to the Quarries (Returns and Records) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1963 ([S.R. & O. (N.I.) 1963 No. 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisro/1963/41)) (see paragraph 3).
