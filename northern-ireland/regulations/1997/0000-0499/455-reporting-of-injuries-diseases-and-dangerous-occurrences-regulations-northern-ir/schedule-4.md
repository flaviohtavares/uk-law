# Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, SCHEDULE 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Records

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 7

### Part I — Particulars to be kept in records of any event which is reportable under Regulation 3

**1.**[^key-2456c57881f3d025849555610ea39433] Date and time of the accident or dangerous occurrence.

**2.**[^key-383c627056201593644ca295af56bed9] In the event of an accident suffered by a person at work, the following particulars of that person—

- (a) full name;
- (b) occupation;
- (c) nature of injury.

**3.**[^key-66c3bdb70e2d6edad3b6284670ad68f8] In the event of an accident suffered by a person not at work, the following particulars of that person (unless they are not known and it is not reasonably practicable to ascertain them)—

- (a) full name;
- (b) status (for example “passenger”, “customer”, “visitor”, “bystander” or “trespasser”);
- (c) nature of injury.

**4.**[^key-5a18bb96ca6a75105fb325d3965a9737] Place where the accident or dangerous occurrence happened.

**5.**[^key-1f87b120c704ec76b507d6d99776721d] A brief description of the circumstances in which the accident or dangerous occurrence happened.

**6.**[^key-6355222587406eec54f2d50d47d43f38] The date on which the event was first reported to the enforcing authority.

**7.**[^key-404b3e9e30f1079d049c021e33ce0651] The method by which the event was reported.

### Part II — Particulars to be kept in records of instances of any of the diseases specified in Schedule 3 and Reportable under Regulation 5

**1.**[^key-a65579faae8e30b4583461528c567fa8] Date of diagnosis of the disease.

**2.**[^key-3123128c4ccee750c35517b85ab955b0] Name of the person affected.

**3.**[^key-89754e6aed0ad441c5bad452a0c46a03] Occupation of the person affected.

**4.**[^key-2f05f2799714e8b6ccc1c5ce2f959629] Name or nature of the disease.

**5.**[^key-4c217b7d6b79155f88c79611d5a857e4] The date on which the disease was first reported to the enforcing authority.

**6.**[^key-c4b661e91734b20e263d02e3b0e330c5] The method by which the disease was reported.
