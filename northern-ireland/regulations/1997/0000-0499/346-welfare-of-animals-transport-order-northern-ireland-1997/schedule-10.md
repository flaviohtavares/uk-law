# Welfare of Animals (Transport) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, SCHEDULE 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 10 — Information to be Provided on the Route Plan

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Article 13(3)

### Section 1 — Information to be completed before the journey

**1.**[^c-d0ab1a383eb86f0209834dc28676dc83] Name, full address, business name, telephone number and fax number of the transporter.

**2.**[^c-7f4d4dd48ef9af6781e8edd346cde16a] Number and species of animals to be transported.

**3.**[^c-f26df05105e51495158bf5915446ddb8] Health certificate number(s).

**4.**[^c-e96a1a064a0542195897026442a4b00b] Number of the transporter’s specific licence (if any).

**5.**[^c-c4ab9bf6cf937b7eb98b1db5802d2746] Registration number of the vehicles to be used, and the trailers if different.

**6.**[^c-3864e3cbfd033b6ef699a64f384bfa8d] Name of the person(s) in charge of the transport during the journey.

**7.**[^c-c06dd8d09c96a85fa5ef11a3a0b80e9d] Place where the animals are to be first loaded and full address.

**8.**[^c-58b617bdda3bfc6d379bb7ca25038771] Planned date and time of departure.

**9.**[^c-e356401edf77b9d241b56e05c73f2320] Full itemised itinerary of the journey.

**10.**[^c-04c7fde5da24cf03352a71daa15b5091] Full address of the final destination.

**11.**[^c-665ac1bb6d93d8f87ab598fdd9cc7bca] Estimated date and time of arrival at the final destination (local time).

### Section 2 — Information to be completed during the journey

**12.**[^c-d60216208c73825dbe24b21f6857e081] Actual date and time of loading the first animal.

**13.**[^c-25b02ac0cff3a2fb210681947894068c] Actual time of departure from the place of loading.

**14.**[^c-315edab72414fc89df48ea5bf095efa6] Full itemised itinerary of the journey.

**15.**[^c-9237fa8986accb9985a8e80f7a22f893] Actual date and time of arrival at the final destination (local time).
