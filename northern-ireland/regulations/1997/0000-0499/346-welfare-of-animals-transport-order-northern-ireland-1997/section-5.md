# Welfare of Animals (Transport) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Space allowance[^c-1312725d7dc2655eacc4be3714833c3b]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Without prejudice to the generality of Article 4(1), a person shall not transport any animal on a journey of over 50 kilometres in a way which is likely to cause injury or unnecessary suffering because of the amount of space available to each animal.

(2) In deciding whether or not the space available is adequate, a transporter shall—

- (a) have regard to the limits set out in Chapter VI of Council Directive [91/628/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0628) (on the protection of animals during transport and amending Directives [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) and [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496)[^f00006]); and
- (b) consider the animals' weight, size, physical condition and temperament, the means of transport, the weather and road conditions and the likely journey time.
