# Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**6.** The Regulations also provide—

- (a) for the payment of fees for approvals and surveillance inspections in respect of examination, testing and certification of tanks calculated in accordance with regulation 11 and Schedule 8; and
- (b) for the payment of a fee of—
  - (i) £200 (“application fee”) to a petroleum licensing authority on each application under the Petroleum (Consolidation) Act (Northern Ireland), 1929 for a Part III Licence; or
  - (ii) £30 (“transfer fee”) to such an authority on each transfer of such a licence.

  (*regulation 20 and Schedule 12*)

  - These application and transfer fees replace but do not increase the same fees fixed by [S.R. 1992 No. 260](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1992/260) which are revoked by the Regulations.
