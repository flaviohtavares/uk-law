# Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**8.** In Great Britain the corresponding Regulations are the Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2095](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2095)). The Department of Transport has prepared a compliance cost assessment in relation to those Regulations and other Regulations. A copy of that assessment together with the Northern Ireland supplement prepared by the Department is held at Health and Safety Division, 83 Ladas Drive, Belfast BT6 9FJ from where a copy may be obtained on request.
