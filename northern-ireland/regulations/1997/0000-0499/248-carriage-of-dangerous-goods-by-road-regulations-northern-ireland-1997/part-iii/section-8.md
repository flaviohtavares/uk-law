# Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Carriage in bulk[^c-5e332a6f31cb0f7392bdd5b73391461c]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An operator of a container or vehicle shall not cause or permit to be carried therein any dangerous goods in bulk unless the letter Y appears in column 8 of the Approved Carriage List in relation to those goods.

(2) Without prejudice to paragraph (1), an operator of a vehicle shall not cause or permit to be carried therein dangerous goods in bulk unless any requirements specified in Schedule 5 relating to those goods are complied with.

(3) Without prejudice to paragraph (1), an operator of a container shall not cause or permit to be carried therein dangerous goods in bulk unless any requirements specified in Schedule 6 relating to those goods are complied with.
