# Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Method of dispatch and restrictions on forwarding[^key-9c958bd44423228cb894968ab3d9c391]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The operator of any container, tank or vehicle which is being used for the carriage of certain dangerous goods shall ensure that any requirements specified in Schedule 4 relating to those goods are complied with.
