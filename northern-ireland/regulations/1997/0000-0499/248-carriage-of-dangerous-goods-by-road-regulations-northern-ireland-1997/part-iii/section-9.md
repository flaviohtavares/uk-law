# Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Carriage in tanks[^key-0d3615c56f3ce4412d7be3a56b362ace]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An operator of a tank shall not cause or permit to be carried therein any dangerous goods unless the letter Y appears in column 7 of the Approved Carriage List in relation to those goods.

(2) An operator of a tank shall not cause or permit to be carried therein any dangerous goods if the pressure of that tank exceeds the maximum working pressure stated in the most recent certificate issued in respect of that tank in accordance with regulation 11.

(3) The operator of any vehicle which is being used for the carriage of dangerous goods in a tank shall ensure that specialist advice in English concerning the goods can be obtained by telephone at any time during carriage.
