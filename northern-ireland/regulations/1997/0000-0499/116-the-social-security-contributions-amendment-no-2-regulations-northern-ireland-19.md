# The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 116

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

4th March 1997

Coming into operation

6th April 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 5 and 171(1) to (3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 6th April 1997.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 7 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations

In regulation 7 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00002] (lower and upper earnings limits for Class 1 contributions)—

- (a) for “6th April 1996” there shall be substituted “6th April 1997”;
- (b) for “£61” and “£455” there shall be substituted respectively “£62” and “£465”.

## 3. Revocation

The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland on

John O'Neill

Assistant Secretary

4th March 1997.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979 (“the principal Regulations”).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 7 of the principal Regulations by increasing the weekly lower and upper earnings limits for Class 1 contributions for the tax year beginning on 6th April 1997. The lower earnings limit is increased from £61 to £62 and the upper earnings limit from £455 to £465.

Regulation 3 contains a revocation.

These Regulations are made under section 5 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
