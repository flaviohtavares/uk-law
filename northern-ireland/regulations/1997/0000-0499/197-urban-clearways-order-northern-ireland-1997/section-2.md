# Urban Clearways Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Control of Waiting[^c-63f709f43c1deb44868ee0f88e1c0a8a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Save as provided in Article 3, no person shall cause or permit a vehicle to wait wholly or partly in a specified road during the hours and on the days specified in columns 2 and 3 of Schedule 1 respectively.
