# The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 177

FAMILY PROCEEDINGS

SUPREME COURT

COUNTY COURT

The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

19th March 1997

Coming into operation

1st April 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Fees to be taken in family proceedings

For the Schedule to the Family Proceedings Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] there shall be substituted the new Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 19th March 1997

We concur,

Richard Ottaway

Roger Knapman

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 19th March 1997
