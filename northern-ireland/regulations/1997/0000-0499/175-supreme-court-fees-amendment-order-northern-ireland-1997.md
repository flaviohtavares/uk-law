# Supreme Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 175

SUPREME COURT

Supreme Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

19th March 1997

Coming into operation

1st April 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1997.

## 2. Fees to be taken in proceedings in the Supreme Court

For Sections 1, 2 and 4 of the Schedule to the Supreme Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] there shall be substituted the new Sections 1, 2 and 4 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 19th March 1997

We concur,

Richard Ottaway

Roger Knapman

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 19th March 1997
