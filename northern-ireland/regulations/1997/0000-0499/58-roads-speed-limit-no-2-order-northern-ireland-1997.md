# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 58

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

11th February 1997

Coming into operation

18th March 1997

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 50(4)(*c*) and 211A of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 18th March 1997.

## 2. Speed restrictions on road

The Department hereby directs that Park Way, Route A42, Ballymena, from its junction with Thomas Street Roundabout, Route A42, to its junction with Chapel Corner Roundabout, Route A42, shall be a restricted road for the purposes of Article 50 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

11th February 1997.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order makes Park Way, Route A42, Ballymena, from its junction with Thomas Street Roundabout, Route A42, to its junction with Chapel Corner Roundabout, Route A42, a restricted road thereby imposing a 30 miles per hour speed limit on that length of road.
