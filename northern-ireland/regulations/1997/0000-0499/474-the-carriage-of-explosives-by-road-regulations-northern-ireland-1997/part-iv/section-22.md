## 22. Duty to ensure safe and secure carriage[^c-0df466c4970a4f8a90935a53bfdbd650]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The operator of a vehicle, which is being used for the carriage of explosives, and any person engaged in the carriage or having custody or control of the explosives during the carriage, shall take such steps as it is reasonable for them to take to prevent—

- (a) accidents and minimise the harmful effects of any accident which may occur; and
- (b) unauthorised access to or removal of all or part of the load.
