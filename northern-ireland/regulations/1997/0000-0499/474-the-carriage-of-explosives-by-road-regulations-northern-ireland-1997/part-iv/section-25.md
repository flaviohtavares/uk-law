## 25. Precautions against fire or explosion[^key-72c26d15590c84e2ef1e99e50bf9b594]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person shall not cause or permit anything to be done which is liable to create a significant risk or significantly increase any existing risk of a fire or an explosion when explosives are being carried in any vehicle or container.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1)—

- (a) the operator of a vehicle is being used for the carriage of explosives shall comply with the requirements specified in Schedule 8 except paragraph 7;
- (b) the driver of such a vehicle shall comply with paragraphs 3 and 7 of Schedule 8;
- (c) any attendant of such a vehicle shall comply with paragraph 3 of Schedule 8.

(3) Paragraphs 1(*b*), 2 and 3 of Schedule 8 shall not apply where the only explosives being carried are in Division 1.4 and Compatibility Group S.
