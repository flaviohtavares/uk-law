# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 21

## 21. Route of carriage and parking[^c-7df068289eb4d27b643aa2dac84ebd97]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The operator and driver of a vehicle used for the carriage of more than 5 tonnes of explosives in Division 1.1 shall ensure that the route followed is the route or one of the routes agreed with the Chief Constable.

(2) Where any vehicle, which is being used for the carriage of explosives, is part of a convoy of such vehicles, the driver of the vehicle, where practicable, shall ensure that there is a distance of more than 50 metres between that vehicle and any other vehicle in the convoy.

(3) When a driver parks a vehicle which is being used for the carriage of explosives, he shall apply the parking brake.

(4) Other than when the only explosives being carried are those specified in Parts I and II of Schedule 1, where any vehicle, which is being used for the carriage of explosives, has stopped for the purpose of loading or unloading the explosives in a public place, the driver of the vehicle shall ensure, where practicable, that there is a distance of not less than 50 metres between the vehicle and any other stationary vehicle, whether or not that vehicle is being used for the carriage of explosives.
