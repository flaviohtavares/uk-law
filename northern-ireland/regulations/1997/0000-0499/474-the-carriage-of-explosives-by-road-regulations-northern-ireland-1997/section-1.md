# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2000-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1997/474
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.** These Regulations impose requirements and prohibitions with regard to the carriage of explosives by road. They implement Council Directive [94/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0055) (O.J. No. L319, 12.12.94, p. 7) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by road, in so far as the Directive concerns explosives. This Directive seeks to apply the European Agreement Concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road signed at Geneva on 30th September 1957, as amended (“ADR”) (1995 edition: ISBN 92 1 139043 5).
