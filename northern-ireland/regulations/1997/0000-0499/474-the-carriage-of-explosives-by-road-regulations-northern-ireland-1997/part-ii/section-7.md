## 7. Prohibition of the carriage of certain explosives[^key-18a6e3c396931f001a1dbc302daeba0b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person shall not carry explosives in Compatibility Group K in a vehicle.

(2) A person shall not carry unclassified explosives in a vehicle except—

- (a) solely in connection with an application for their classification; and
- (b) in accordance with such conditions as are approved in writing for the time being by the Secretary of State or, in the case of military explosives, by the Secretary of State for Defence, for the purposes of this regulation.

(3) A person shall not carry explosives—

- (a) in Compatibility Group L; or
- (b) in Divisions 1.1, 1.2 or 1.5 in large containers,

in a vehicle other than in a load which is under sole use.
