## 12. Types of vehicle[^c-e1702451ac5716788e91c3c0d2e9dab6]

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations any reference to—

- (a) a type of vehicle is a reference to a vehicle of type I, II or III;
- (b) a vehicle of type I is a reference to a vehicle other than one of type II or type III;
- (c) a vehicle of type II or type III is a reference to a vehicle which satisfies the requirements for a type II or type III vehicle respectively in the Approved Explosives Vehicle Requirements.
