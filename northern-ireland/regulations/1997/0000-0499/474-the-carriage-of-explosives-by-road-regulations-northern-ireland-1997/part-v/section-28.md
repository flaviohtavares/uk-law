## 28. Minimum age limits for persons engaged in the carriage of explosives[^c-e5d5e954e39921532ca00a03de935bcc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) a person under the age of 18 years shall not—

- (a) use any vehicle for the carriage of explosives;
- (b) be employed as the driver or attendant of such a vehicle;
- (c) be made responsible for the security of explosives;
- (d) be allowed to go on or in such a vehicle except in the presence and under the supervision of a competent person who is at least 18 years of age.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the carriage of any explosives specified in—

- (a) Part I of Schedule 1; or
- (b) Part II of Schedule 1, if the total quantity of such explosives does not exceed 50 kilograms.
