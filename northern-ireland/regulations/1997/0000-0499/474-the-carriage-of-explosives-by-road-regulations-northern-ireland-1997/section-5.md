## 5. Part IV: Safety and security during carriage

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 19 and Schedule 7 make provision for the loading, stowage, unloading and cleaning of vehicles and containers. Regulation 20 requires the operator in certain circumstances to ensure that an attendant accompanies the driver, and when the vehicle is parked and the driver is not present, a person competent to ensure the security of the explosives is in attendance. Regulations 21 and 22 impose duties with regard to the route of carriage, parking, the prevention of accidents and unauthorised access to the explosives.

**6.** Regulation 23 prohibits smoking or the production of an open flame on or near the vehicle during loading and unloading. Regulation 24 requires the operator to ensure that the vehicle is equipped to take the measures set out in the emergency information in the Transport Documentation. Regulation 25 prohibits activity which is liable to create or increase the risk of fire and imposes the duties set out in Schedule 8. Regulation 26 makes provision for the notification of emergencies and for safety and security precautions.
