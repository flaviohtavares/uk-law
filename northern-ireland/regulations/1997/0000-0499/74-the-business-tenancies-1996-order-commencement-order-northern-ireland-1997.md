# The Business Tenancies (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1997 No. 74 (C. 4)

BUSINESS TENANCIES

The Business Tenancies (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made

20th February 1997

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Business Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001], paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Business Tenancies (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.

## 2. Appointed day

The Business Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 shall come into operation on 1st April 1997.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

James McKeown

Assistant Secretary

20th February 1997.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st April 1997 the Business Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.
