# The Tobacco Retailer (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2026/156

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 156

Public Health

The Tobacco Retailer (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

9th September 2026

Coming into operation

29th October 2026

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(2), 8(5), 12(7) and 24(3) of the Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement[^key-51dc66e5ed21ebbd43f5dcabfd5cb8b7]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as The Tobacco Retailer (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

(2) Except as provided in paragraph (3), these Regulations shall come into operation on 29<sup>th</sup> October 2026.

(3) Regulation 3(2)(b) comes into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> January 2027.

## 2. Amendment of the Tobacco Retailer (Registration and Display of Notices) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^key-d6a379ba299828d588e4ff544d67734f]

(1) The Tobacco Retailer (Registration and Display of Notices) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in section 3 (Premises details), after “Please specify \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_” insert—
- (b) in section 4 (Declaration)—
  - (i) for “tobacco” substitute “tobacco products, herbal smoking products, vaping products, nicotine products or cigarette papers”;
  - (ii) in the return postal address, for “Tobacco Register NI” substitute “The Northern Ireland Tobacco, Vape and Nicotine Retail Register”;
  - (iii) in the email address, for “tobaccoregister” substitute “nitvnretailregister”.
- (c) in section 5 (Additional premises sheet), after “Please specify \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_” insert—

(3) In Schedule 2—

- (a) in the heading of the notice, omit “Tobacco”;
- (b) in paragraph 2 of the notice, for “Tobacco” substitute “tobacco products, herbal smoking products, vaping products, nicotine products”.

## 3. Amendment of the Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^key-4f4ab52b8babbc7a348cee7b42f7a73a][^key-05d19edf85e7991f28b964d3af7a7d87]

(1)[^key-4f4ab52b8babbc7a348cee7b42f7a73a] The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule (fixed penalty notice)—

- (a)[^key-4f4ab52b8babbc7a348cee7b42f7a73a] in Parts 1 and 2, omit “/the Health (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016”;
- (b) in Parts 1 and 2, omit “/Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991”.
- (c)[^key-4f4ab52b8babbc7a348cee7b42f7a73a] in Part 3, in paragraph 5, after “cheque,”, insert “online or electronic payment,”.

## 4. Revocation[^key-3d9fdb118dc2f51138907fb551839acc]

The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00005] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 9<sup>th</sup> September 2026

Siobhan Broderick

A senior officer of the Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Tobacco Retailer (Registration and Display of Notices) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 so that they apply to tobacco products, herbal smoking products, vaping products, nicotine products and cigarette papers. They also amend the return postal address and email address for the Tobacco Register NI to reflect the updated contact details.

These Regulations also amend The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 to amend the form of a fixed penalty notice and which may be issued in respect of offences as listed under section 12(1) of the Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) 2014.

Regulation 4 revokes The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (SR ) as the amendments contained in those Regulations are now superseded.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
