# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 116

Nature Conservation

The Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

17th June 2026

Coming into operation

8th July 2026

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(1), (2) and (7) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 makes the following Regulations (“**the 2023 Act**”)[^f00001].

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs is a relevant national authority for the purposes of section 14(1) and (2) of the 2023 Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-4acde5c0af60ba7560391c269968fdc0]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and come into operation on 8th July 2026.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendments to the Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^c-69536d3379a5592faffbb05d53318c3d]

(1) The Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 6, after paragraph (9) add—

> - (10) Where a site which is designated under paragraph (1) is a European Site, the Department may not designate that site without the agreement of the Secretary of State.

.

(3) In regulation 8A(6), for subparagraph (b), substitute—

> (b) where a site which is classified under paragraph (1) is a European marine site, the Department may not classify that site without the consent of the Secretary of State.

.

(4) Omit regulation 8B(5).

(5) Omit Regulation 9A(5).

(6) In regulation 28, in each place where it occurs (including in the heading), for “Secretary of State” substitute “Department”.

(7) In regulation 29—

- (a) in paragraph (1), omit “, the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development”;
- (b) omit paragraph (4).

(8) In regulation30(1) and (3), for “The Secretary of State, or persons authorised on his behalf,” substitute “The Department or persons authorised on its behalf”.

(9) In regulation 30(2)—

- (a) in each place where it occurs, for “Secretary of State” substitute “Department”.
- (b) omit subparagraph (d).

(10) Omit regulation 36A(4).

(11) In regulation 66(1) omit “or, as the case may be, the Secretary of State”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This Note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 380](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/380)).

These Regulations make amendments to the role of the Secretary of State in designating or classifying European Marine sites, aligning with the procedure in the Marine Act (Northern Ireland) 2013 regarding the designation of Marine Conservation Zones.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
