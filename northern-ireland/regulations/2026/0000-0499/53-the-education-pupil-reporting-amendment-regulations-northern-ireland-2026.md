# The Education (Pupil Reporting) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-03-30.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 53

Education

The Education (Pupil Reporting) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

26th February 2026

Coming into operation

30th March 2026

The Department of Education[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 24(1) to (3) and (5) and 43(5) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00002], and after consulting as required by Article 24(4) of that Order, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-820eb790591155d9d192bc6d52fd41a6]

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Pupil Reporting) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 30th March 2026.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-354c92f219747d0c263667ce227399c0]

In these Regulations—

- “**the 2009 Regulations**” means the Education (Pupil Reporting) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00003].

## 3. Amendment of the 2009 Regulations[^c-7159d2a7a5b06fde68aed1620557d0e4]

(1) The 2009 Regulations are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) Regulation 7 and Schedule 2 are revoked.

(3) In regulation 9(1) for “regulations 5, 6 and 7” substitute “regulations 5 and 6”.

(4) In regulation 10(1) for “regulations 5(1), 7 and 9” substitute “regulations 5(1) and 9”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 26th February 2026

(L.S.)

Cynthia Curry

A senior officer of the Department of Education

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Pupil Reporting) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 by omitting regulation 7 and Schedule 2 which provided for the reporting of specified end of key stage 1-3 information. They also make a consequential amendment to regulations 9 and 10 to remove a reference to regulation 7.
