EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981 ([S.R. 1981 No. 225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1981/225)) to—

- make amendments consequential upon the increase in the general financial jurisdiction of the County Court to £60,000 and the increase in the general financial jurisdiction of the district judge to £20,000.
- amend Order 55, rule 11(1)(ba) to specify the lung diseases in respect of which enhanced costs may be awarded where a judge or district judge is satisfied that the issues are of particular complexity.
- extend the scale costs provisions in Appendix 2 to reflect the increase in the general financial jurisdiction of the County Court to £60,000 and the increase in the financial limit for defamation proceedings to £10,000. The Rules insert additional scale cost bands for ordinary civil bills, undefended actions, remitted actions and defamation proceedings.
- consolidate the scale costs for plaintiffs and defendants in defamation proceedings.
