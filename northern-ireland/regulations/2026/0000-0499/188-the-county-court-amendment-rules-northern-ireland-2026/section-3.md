# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendments to the principal Rules

The principal Rules are amended as follows—

(1) In Order 5, rule 2(6) for “£30,000” substitute “£60,000”.

(2) In Order 6A, rule 5(1) for “£10,000” substitute “£20,000”.

(3) For Order 55, rule 11(1)(ba) substitute –

> - (ba) claimed:
> - (i) pleural plaques;
> - (ii) pleural thickening with functional impairment;
> - (iii) lung cancer;
> - (iv) asthma;
> - (v) chronic bronchitis;
> - (vi) chronic obstructive airways disease; or
> - (vii) mild respiratory conditions, including mild bronchitis;
>
> or

.

(4) In Order 55, rule 19(1)(a) for “£10,000” substitute “£20,000”.

(5) For Appendix 2 substitute the new Appendix 2 set out in the Schedule to these Rules.
