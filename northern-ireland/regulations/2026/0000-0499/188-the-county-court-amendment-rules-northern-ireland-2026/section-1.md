# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-4ad5d733ef647f79d83c33eaa75f6220]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 30th November 2026.

**2.**[^c-0ce9175d09118f8729be9cb98a32b5b1] In these Rules—

- (a) “**the principal Rules**” means the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002]; and
- (b) a reference to an Order, Rule, paragraph, or Appendix is a reference to that Order, Rule, paragraph, or Appendix so numbered in the principal Rules.
