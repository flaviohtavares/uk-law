# The Farming with Nature Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-06-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

2026 No. 105

Agriculture

The Farming with Nature Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

4th June 2026

Coming into operation

25th June 2026

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 48 of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 6 to the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001] and Article 28(10)(2)(d) of [Regulation (EU) No. 1305/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1305) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-759a52039ca0d402f6db1bb30ff91ae3]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Farming with Nature Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 25 June 2026.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## PART 2 — Amendments to the Farming with Nature Scheme

### 2. Amendments to the Farming with Nature Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025[^key-9eaea34b4c7c0b0a8aec0d26098d20c1]

(1) The Farming with Nature Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1)–

- (a) for the definition of “permanent grassland area” substitute–

  > - “**permanent grassland sensitive area**” means as required by Article 32A of [Regulation (EU) No. 1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy[^f00005] areas including peat and wetlands covered by either or both the Wild Birds Directive[^f00006] and the Habitats Directive[^f00007] which are environmentally sensitive and which need protection to meet the requirements of these directives;

  .
- (b) at the appropriate place insert–

  > - “**machinery**” includes–
  >   - (i) digging machines;
  >   - (ii) mobile cranes;
  >   - (iii) generators or compressors;
  >   - (iv) construction equipment, including wheel loaders, bulldozers, crawler tractors, crawler loaders, truck-type loaders, off highway vehicles and hydraulic excavators;
  >   - (v) agricultural equipment;
  >   - (vi) self-propelled agricultural vehicles including tractors and quad vehicles; and
  >   - (vii) fork-lift trucks

  .

## PART 3 — Amendments to assimilated law

### 3. Amendment of the Horizontal Regulation[^key-9b4a68ece88432c09c5d1beeeac65b96]

(1) [Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1306) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy (the Horizontal Regulation)[^f00008] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 96–

- (a) in paragraph 3, for “Schedule 1, Part 1” substitute “this Title”;
- (b) in paragraph 4, for “Schedule 1, Part 1” substitute “this Title”.

(3) In Article 99–

- (a) in the second sub-paragraph of paragraph 1, for from “paragraphs 2” to the end of the sub-paragraph substitute “paragraph 2”;
- (b) for paragraph 2 substitute–

  > **2.** The minimum penalty to be applied in respect of any non-compliance determined shall be the sending of a warning letter by or on behalf of the competent authority and an obligation to complete mandatory training.
  >
  > The maximum penalty to be applied shall not exceed 100% of all payments made or due to be made in the scheme year in which that non-compliance was determined and exclusion from payment from all schemes for the two scheme years immediately following.
  >
  > The highest penalty in respect of each standard shall be applied as a reduction in accordance with paragraph 1.
  >
  > Completion of mandatory training will be required for any first occurrence of a breach of any underpinning requirement.
