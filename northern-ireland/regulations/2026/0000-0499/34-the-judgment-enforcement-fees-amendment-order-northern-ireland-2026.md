# The Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 34

Judgments (enforcement)

The Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

3rd March 2026

Coming into operation

1st April 2026

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], makes the following Order with the concurrence of the Department of Finance[^f00003].

The Department has, in accordance with section 116(1) of that Act, consulted with the Lady Chief Justice.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-e01b6c0e4ee2102362f1d67bccde772f]

This Order may be cited as the Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^c-dea887be699cfd70d2e89fd4e4409ae9]

The Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] is amended as follows.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/101)) to increase the fees payable in respect of the enforcement of judgments over a three-year period (5% increase effective from 1st April 2026, 2% increase effective from 1st April 2027, with a further 2% increase effective from 1st April 2028). It also amends the 1996 Order to increase the minimum fee and update the fee bands in respect of the enforcement of money judgements (Fee 2) and admitted or corresponding debts (Fees 14 and 16).

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment have been produced and are available from Northern Ireland Courts and Tribunals Service, Laganside House, 23-27 Oxford Street, Belfast BT1 3LA or online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
