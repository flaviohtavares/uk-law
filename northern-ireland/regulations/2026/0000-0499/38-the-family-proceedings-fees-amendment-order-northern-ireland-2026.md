# The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 38

Family Proceedings

Court Of Judicature

County Court

The Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

3rd March 2026

Coming into operation

1st April 2026

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], makes the following Order with the concurrence of the Department of Finance[^f00003].

The Department has, in accordance with section 116(1) of that Act, consulted with the Lady Chief Justice.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-c7b6b1a3d8a00746a29759f2f4f15d78]

This Order may be cited as the Family Proceedings Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Family Proceedings Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^c-804cd3ff03c42e732c36bd28f476271b]

The Family Proceedings Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] is amended as follows.

**3.**[^c-5f1bb197fda7a90af6157674e8237b39][^c-804cd3ff03c42e732c36bd28f476271b] For the Schedule substitute the new Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Family Proceedings Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 495](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/495)) to increase the majority of fees payable in respect of specified family proceedings in the High Court and County Court over a three year period (5% increase effective from 1st April 2026, 2% increase effective from 1st April 2027, with a further 2% increase effective from 1st April 2028). It also amends the 1996 Order to include a fee for an ex-parte application to a judge for an injunction (Fee 7(c)).

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment have been produced and are available from the Northern Ireland Courts and Tribunals Service, Laganside House, 23-27 Oxford Street, Belfast BT1 3LA or online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
