The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(3) and 9(1) and (2) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^c-705224e9c94f115f875b77887cf9bbaf]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 31st October 2026.

(2) Regulation 2(2) applies only in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after 31st October 2026 and who, immediately before their death, had diffuse mesothelioma.

## 2. Amendment of the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations[^c-5dc00d0b9d21dd525d97cc944e2c8825]

(1) The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) After regulation 5 (amount of lump sum payment) insert—

> **5A. Payment to personal representatives**
>
> (1) Where—
>
> - (a) a claim is made, and
> - (b) the person making the claim dies before payment is made, payment must be made to the person’s personal representatives, subject to the condition in paragraph (2) having been met.
>
> (2) The condition is that any document that is by law sufficient evidence of the grant of—
>
> - (a) probate of the will of the person,
> - (b) letters of administration of the estate of the person, or
> - (c) confirmation as executor of the person,
>
> has been provided to the Department.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 to:

- make it clear to whom payment can be made in the event the person making the claim dies before it is paid; and
- bring the dependant rates of payment in line with the rates for persons with diffuse mesothelioma.

By virtue of regulation 1(2), the increased rates of lump sum payments for a dependant apply only in relation to a case where the sufferer dies on or after 31st October 2026.
