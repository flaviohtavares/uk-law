# The Waste (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-01-19.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 8

Environmental Protection

The Waste (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

19th January 2026

Coming into operation

19th January 2026

The Department for Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] being an appropriate authority for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 4 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00002], in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 4 to that Act and Article 39(1), (2) and (3) of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00003] and now vested in it[^f00004], makes the following regulations.

In accordance with paragraph 12(3) of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 a draft of these Regulations was laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

## 1. Citation commencement and interpretation[^key-244205a8013117ceba764d88e7f8c509]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Waste (Fees and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 19th January 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Controlled Waste (Registration of Carriers and Seizure of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^key-7c0601250a7fde135e8b8c92f6a72f10]

(1) The Controlled Waste (Registration of Carriers and Seizure of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 4 (applications for registration)—

- (a) In paragraph (8)(a) for “£180” substitute “£191”;
- (b) In paragraph (8)(b) for “£90” substitute “£96”;
- (c) In paragraph (8)(c) for £46” substitute “£49”.

## 3. Amendment of the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^key-0ab34d57471e57974f8fa50c0be436f6]

(1) The Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 4 (registration of brokers and dealers of controlled waste)—

- (a) In paragraph 3(10)(a) for “£180” substitute “£191”;
- (b) In paragraph 3(10)(b) for “£90” substitute “£96”;
- (c) In paragraph 3(10)(c) for £46” substitute “£49”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs publishes a charging scheme to ensure that as far as practical, fees and charges payable for waste management licenses under the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (the 1997 Order) are sufficient to recover costs for the Department in exercising its functions under the 1997 Order. However, charges for processing applications for registration as carriers, brokers or dealers in controlled waste are required to be set out in legislation. The purpose of these Regulations is to increase those charges to ensure the Department operates in accordance with the principles of Managing Public Money Northern Ireland (MPMNI) by seeking full cost recovery. Details of the increased fees will also be included in the charging scheme which sets out the fees and charges payable in respect of waste management licenses.

These Regulations amend the regulation 4 of the Controlled Waste (Registration of Carriers and Seizure of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999. Regulation 4 sets out the process concerning applications for registration as a carrier, and for combined registration as both a carrier and a broker, of controlled waste, including the associated fees.

Also amended is paragraph 3(10) of Schedule 4 to the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, which sets out fees in relation to the registration of brokers and dealers in controlled waste.
