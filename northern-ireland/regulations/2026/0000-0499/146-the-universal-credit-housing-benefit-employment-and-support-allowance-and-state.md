2026 No. 146

Social Security

The Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Employment and Support Allowance and State Pension Credit (Carer’s Allowance Reassessment Capital Disregard) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

9th July 2026

Coming into operation

16th July 2026

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 132(3) and (4) and 171(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003], sections 15(3) and (6) and 19(1) to (3) of the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00004], sections 17(1) and (3) and 25(2) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00005] and Article 48 of, and paragraph 4(1) and (3) of Schedule 1 to, the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00006].

Regulations 3 and 4 are made with the consent of the Department of Finance[^f00007].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-f26237fdf85e30f24476160c53842fa1]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Employment and Support Allowance and State Pension Credit (Carer’s Allowance Reassessment Capital Disregard) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and come into operation on 16th July 2026.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00008] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the State Pension Credit Regulations[^key-8c5a7761bcf54b24e280ba5154082692]

After paragraph 20AA of Schedule 5 to the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00009] (income from capital), insert—

> **20AB.** A payment to the person made by the Department as a result of a recalculation of carer’s allowance, carried out in consequence of the report of the Independent Review of Carer’s Allowance Overpayments dated 21st July 2025[^f00010].

.

## 3. Amendment of the Housing Benefit Regulations[^key-8d7f1c9a5e0435558e8a8d659482f32a]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend various social security Regulations to make provision for the treatment, within the relevant benefits, of lump-sum payments of carer’s allowance made by the Department as a result of the reassessment exercise conducted in response to the Independent Review of Carer’s Allowance Overpayments, published on 21st July 2025.

Regulations 2 to 6 amend the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and the Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 respectively. Each amendment inserts a provision to ensure that lump-sum payments made as part of the carer’s allowance reassessment exercise are disregarded as capital when determining entitlement to the relevant benefit.

These Regulations make provision for Northern Ireland only corresponding to provision made by regulations of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain. Accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), they are not subject to the requirement in section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
