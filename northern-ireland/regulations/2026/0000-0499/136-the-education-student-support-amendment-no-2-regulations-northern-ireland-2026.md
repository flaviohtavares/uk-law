# The Education (Student Support) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 136

Education

The Education (Student Support) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

2nd July 2026

Coming into operation on

24th July 2026

The Department for the Economy[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002] and Articles 4(8) and 14(4) of the Higher Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00003] makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^c-4d8fc4aed1f6560dd286d926d43b649d]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Support) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and come into operation on 24th July 2026.

(2) Regulation 9 (current system students with full entitlement) applies only in relation to the provision of support, and to fees and awards applicable, in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1st August 2026, whether or not anything done under these Regulations is done before, on or after that date.

(3) In paragraph (2) an “academic year” is the period of twelve months beginning with 1st January, 1st April, 1st July or 1st September of the calendar year in which the academic year of the course in question begins, according to whether the academic year of the course in question begins—

- (a) on or after 1st January and before 1st April;
- (b) on or after 1st April and before 1st July;
- (c) on or after 1st July and before 1st August; or
- (d) on or after 1st August and on or before 31st December.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-d1d0e3a8a28e7606a3ecc0445d76d001]

In these Regulations —

- (a) “**the 2007 Regulations**” means The Student Fees (Qualifying Courses and Persons) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004]**;**
- (b) “**the 2009 Regulations**” means the Education (Student Support) (No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00005].

## 3. Amendments to the 2007 Regulations[^c-f4e73b36432702b8bfbe4fe6aefb3830]

Regulations 4 and 5 amend the 2007 Regulations.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 5 to allow overstayers satisfying the immigration rules to retain eligible student status[^c-1aa39e631b4e12ce7ba3466c8ea7db70]

In regulation 5(1D)(b) (prescribed description of a qualifying person), substitute—

> - (b) either—
>   - (i) further leave to remain has been granted, or paragraph SUI 13.1 of the immigration rules applies in respect of an application for such leave, or
>   - (ii) the person whose leave to remain has expired has become a British or Irish citizen,

.

## 5. Amendment of the Schedule (qualifying persons) to update references to the Ukraine Schemes[^c-489a5980c7fe72d33086657ac3f869e8]

In paragraph 1(1) of the Schedule (qualifying persons)—

- (a) in the definition of “person granted leave under the Ukraine Extension Scheme”, in paragraph (a), after “immigration rules”, insert “, as it had effect on the day such leave was granted”;
- (b) in the definition of “person granted leave under the Ukraine Family Scheme”, in paragraph (a)(i), after “immigration rules”, insert “, as it had effect on the day such leave was granted”;
- (c) in the definition of “person granted leave under the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme”, in paragraph (a), for “the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme” substitute “paragraph UKR 36.1 of Appendix Ukraine Scheme of the immigration rules”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend legislation which makes provision about, or in connection with, student finance. Those Regulations being amended are the Education (Student Support) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 “the 2009 Regulations” and The Student Fees (Qualifying Courses and Persons) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 “the 2007 Regulations”.

These Regulations come into force on 24th July 2026, unless otherwise specified.

Regulation 4 amends the 2007 Regulations allow overstayers satisfying paragraph SUI 13.1 of the immigration rules to retain eligible student status.

Regulation 5 amends the 2007 Regulations to update references to the Ukraine Schemes following amendments made to the immigration rules.

Regulation 7 amends the 2009 Regulations to update references to the Ukraine Schemes following amendments made to the immigration rules.

Regulations 8 and 10 to 13 amend the 2009 Regulations to allow overstayers satisfying paragraph SUI 13.1 of the immigration rules to retain eligible student status.

Regulation 9 amends the 2009 Regulations to update payment rate calculations for student support for an academic year which begins on or after 1st August 2026.
