# The Social Security (Income and Capital Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-10-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 181

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Income and Capital Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

16th October 2026

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1)(d), 129A(2), 131(1), 132(3) and (4), 132A(3) and 171(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003], sections 15(3) and (6)(b) and 19(1) to (3) of the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00004], sections 4(2), 17(1) and (3) and 25(2) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00005] and Article 48(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 4(1) and (3)(a) of Schedule 1 to, the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00006].

Regulations 3 and 4 are made with the consent of the Department of Finance[^f00007].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-b5c5eb17276f0828af388525d4758872]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Income and Capital Disregards) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into operation on 31st October 2026 or, if later, the day on which section 12 of the Abuse Redress Measure 2025[^f00008] comes into force.

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00009] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the State Pension Credit Regulations[^key-26ca15e9ef9005d992d4fae6f2337fc2]

(1) The State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00010] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In regulation 1(2) (citation, commencement and interpretation)—

- (a) after the definition of “child abuse payment”[^f00011] insert—

  > - “**Church of England Redress payment**” means a payment by the scheme administered by the redress body under the Abuse Redress Measure 2025;

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “qualifying person”[^f00012] after “a Mother and Baby Scheme payment” insert “, a Church of England Redress payment”.

(3) In paragraph 14(8) (persons residing with the claimant) of Schedule 2 (housing costs) after head (bea)[^f00013] insert—

> - (beb) any Church of England Redress payment;

.

(4) In paragraph 15 of Schedule 5 (income from capital)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1A)[^f00014] after “Mother and Baby Scheme payment” insert “, Church of England Redress payment”;
- (b) in each of sub-paragraphs (2) to (5) and (6)[^f00015] after “a Mother and Baby Scheme payment” insert “, a Church of England Redress payment”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/28)), the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 405](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/405)), the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 406](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/406)), the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No. 280](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/280)), and the Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 216](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/216)).

Regulation 1 provides for citation, commencement and interpretation.

Regulations 2 to 6 amend provisions regarding which payments are to be disregarded as income and capital for the purposes of calculating entitlement to benefits in order to create new disregards. The disregards cover payments made by the scheme administered by the Church of England under the Abuse Redress Measure 2025 ([2025 No. 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2025/4)).

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland only provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
