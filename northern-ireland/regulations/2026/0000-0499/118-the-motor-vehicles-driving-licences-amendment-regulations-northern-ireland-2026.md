# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-08-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 118

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

18th June 2026

Coming into operation

1st August 2026

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 9(2) and (4)(b), and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-a92975c7062219122d8351003ea5800d]

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 1st August 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations[^c-fa13ed99c2af2466d998c6df7920899c]

In regulation 51 of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] (disabilities prescribed in respect of Group 2 licences)—

- (a) in paragraph (6B)(a), for “blood glucose monitoring” substitute “appropriate monitoring to assess glucose levels and any risk of hypoglycaemia”; and
- (b) in paragraph (6C)(b), for the words from “a device” to “blood” substitute “an appropriate device that measures and records”

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 18th June 2026

Chris Hughes

A senior officer of the

Department for Infrastructure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 to allow holders of Group 2 licences (bus and lorry drivers) who have diabetes to use any appropriate glucose monitoring system to monitor their glucose levels instead of blood glucose testing.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Driving, Vehicle and Road Safety Policy Division, Department for Infrastructure, James House, 2-4 Cromac Avenue, Belfast BT7 2JA or online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
