# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-11-09.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

2026 No. 186

Social Security

The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

*09th November 2026*

Coming into operation

30th November 2026

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Sections 1(1) and 165(1), (4) and (6) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

The powers are exercisable by the Department for Communities by virtue of Article 2 of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Cessation of Transitory Provision) Order 2020[^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-298e0b5a46eb1d4d4f9aa29ef25b6235]

These Regulations may be cited as the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and come into operation on 30 November 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland)[^c-d076b0eba840eb721f3ced0844aa2e57]

After regulation 31A(2) (reclaims of universal credit after nil award due to earnings) of The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00005] insert—

> (3) Where, in the month that would otherwise be the fifth month for the purposes of paragraph (2), the claimant (or either of joint claimants) is a participant in a funded employment scheme, having been placed on such a scheme by the Department, the maximum of 5 months referred to in that paragraph is to be extended up to and including the first month in which the claimant is not (or in the case of joint claimants neither of them is) a participant in the scheme.
>
> (4) In this regulation “**funded employment scheme**” means a scheme under which the wages of the claimant are funded, in whole or in part, pursuant to arrangements by the Department under section 1(1) of the Employment and Training Act (Northern Ireland) 1950[^f00006].

Explanatory Note

(This is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 220](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/220)) so as to make provision for reclaims of universal credit for claimants taking part in a funded employment scheme.

Regulation 31A of the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 allows a person to be treated as claiming universal credit for up to 5 months after the assessment period in which their earnings cause them to lose entitlement. Regulation 2 of these Regulations extends that period while the claimant, or either of joint claimants, is participating in a funded employment scheme. This will allow them to be automatically re-awarded universal credit if the earnings from the scheme have ceased.

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
