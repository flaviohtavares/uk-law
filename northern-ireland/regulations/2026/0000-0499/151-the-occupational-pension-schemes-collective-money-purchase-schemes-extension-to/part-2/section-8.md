# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 8

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Viability report: unconnected multiple employer schemes[^c-64e160390e049f35e783e533e2063cb9]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 64 (viability report) after subsection (7) insert—

> (7A) Before providing a viability report to the Pensions Regulator in accordance with subsection (6)(a) the trustees of an unconnected multiple employer scheme must obtain the approval of the report by the scheme proprietor (see section 65B).
>
> (7B) The trustees of an unconnected multiple employer scheme must provide the scheme proprietor with any revised viability report—
>
> - (a) within seven days of the viability report being revised, and
> - (b) at any other time, on request from the scheme proprietor.

.
