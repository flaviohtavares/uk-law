# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 14

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Notification of significant events: unconnected multiple employer schemes[^c-e1c25553dfa3bbaf1486a08be2b96fb9]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 79(2) (duty to notify the Pensions Regulator of significant events) after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) where the scheme is an unconnected multiple employer scheme, the scheme proprietor;
> - (bb) where the scheme is an unconnected multiple employer scheme, a person who promotes or markets the scheme;
> - (bc) where the scheme is an unconnected multiple employer scheme, a chief or other senior financial officer of the scheme;
> - (bd) where the scheme is an unconnected multiple employer scheme, a chief or other senior investment officer of the scheme;

.
