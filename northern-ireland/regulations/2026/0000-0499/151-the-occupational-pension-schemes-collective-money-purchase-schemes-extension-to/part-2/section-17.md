# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 17

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. References to relevant former employers[^c-2737649f1eac0747cbdc5d31763651e5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 82(5) (triggering events), in the definition of “relevant former employer”, for “collective money purchase scheme” substitute “single or connected employer scheme”.

(2) In section 87(1)(c) (continuity option 1: discharge of liabilities and winding up)—

- (a) for “each employer and relevant former employer, and each beneficiary,” substitute “the following”;
- (b) for “the Department.” substitute—

  > the Department—
  >
  > - (i) each employer,
  > - (ii) each beneficiary, and
  > - (iii) where the scheme is a single or connected employer scheme, each relevant former employer.

  .

(3) In section 92(2) (trustees’ duties once implementation strategy approved) for “to employers or relevant former employers” substitute “to employers or, where the scheme is a single or connected employer scheme, relevant former employers”.

(4) In section 95(5)(b)(i) (pause orders) after “on behalf of employers or” insert “, where the scheme is a single or connected employer scheme,”.

(5) In Schedule 5 (pause orders), in paragraph 4(3)(c), at the beginning insert “where the scheme is a single or connected employer scheme,”.
