# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 35

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Financial sustainability requirement[^key-1b2faa0c4cc8a0cc45ffa737fde88c85]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Part 2 of Schedule 3 sets out matters that the Pensions Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 65 of the Act[^f00038] (financial sustainability requirement) whether it is satisfied that an unconnected multiple employer scheme is financially sustainable.

(2) Part 3 of Schedule 3 sets out the requirements to be met by an unconnected multiple employer scheme in relation to its financing for the purposes of section 65 of the Act.
