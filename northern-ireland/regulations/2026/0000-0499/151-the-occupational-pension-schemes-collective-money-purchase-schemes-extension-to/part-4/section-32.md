# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 32

> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Scheme design requirement[^key-42ea047d573fc5e850ea346fa07b171f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Part 1 of Schedule 2 sets out the matters that the Pensions Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 63 of the Act (scheme design requirement), whether it is satisfied that the design of an unconnected multiple employer scheme is sound.
