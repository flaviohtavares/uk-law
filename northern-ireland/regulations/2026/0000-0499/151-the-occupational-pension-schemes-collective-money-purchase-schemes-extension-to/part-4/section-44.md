# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 44

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Powers of the Pensions Regulator[^c-5eb0cd27273baba230d8d01a8bec94fb]

*Extent: N.I.*

A direction issued by the Pensions Regulator under section 74(2)(a) of the Act (powers of the Pensions Regulator) in relation to an unconnected multiple employer scheme must set out the matters that the Regulator has considered in determining to issue the direction.
