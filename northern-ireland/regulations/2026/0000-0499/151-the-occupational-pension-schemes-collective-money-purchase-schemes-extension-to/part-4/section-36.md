# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 36

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Communication requirement[^key-7449f937ba64aa87acdcf575ec0c5faa]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 4 sets out the matters that the Pensions Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 66 of the Act (communication requirement), whether it is satisfied that an unconnected multiple employer scheme has adequate systems and processes for communicating with members and others.
