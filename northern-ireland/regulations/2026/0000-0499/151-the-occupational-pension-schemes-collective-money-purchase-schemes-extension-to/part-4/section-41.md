# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 41

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Advice of scheme actuary[^key-529867c401a1625e2b6cfb2a2c28c52f]

*Extent: N.I.*

When advising the trustees of an unconnected multiple employer scheme in accordance with section 70(1) of the Act (advice of scheme actuary), the scheme actuary must have regard to any guidance which is relevant to determining the matters mentioned in section 69(1) and (2) of the Act (calculation of benefits) published, and from time to time revised, by—

- (a) the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries;
- (b) the Pensions Regulator;
- (c) the Financial Reporting Council Limited[^f00040].
