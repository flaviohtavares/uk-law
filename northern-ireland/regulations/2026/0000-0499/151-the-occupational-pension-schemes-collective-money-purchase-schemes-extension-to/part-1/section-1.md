# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-b8571bff05bdbb5afb8a879c7a927b95]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 31st July 2026.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.
