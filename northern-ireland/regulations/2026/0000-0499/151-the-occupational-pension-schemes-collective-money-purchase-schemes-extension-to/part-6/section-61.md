# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 61

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^key-9ba6ca7986f0477b58a65b6625a5a09c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00075] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In Article 2 (general interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (4)(a)—
  - (i) in head (xi)[^f00076], after “sections” insert “82,”;
  - (ii) after head (xii) insert—

    > - (xiii) regulations 40 and 56 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026;

    ;
- (b) in paragraph (4)(b)—
  - (i) in head (viii)[^f00077], after “69(7)(b),” insert “82(4A),”;
  - (ii) after head (ix) insert—

    > - (x) regulation 40(1)(b) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

    .

(3) In Article 88(2)(pb)[^f00078] (the Regulator’s procedure in relation to its regulatory functions), after “section 80(1)” insert “, (1A)”.
