# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 60

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Amendment of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^key-84b3c861064c1b22a0e7d78df36dea5b]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 67A of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00072] (the subsisting rights provisions: interpretation), in paragraph (9)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in head (xi)[^f00073], after “sections” insert “82,”;
  - (ii) after head (xii) insert—

    > - (xiii) regulations 40 and 56 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026;

    ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in head (viii)[^f00074], after “69(7)(b),” insert “82(4A),”;
  - (ii) after head (ix) insert—

    > - (x) regulation 40(1)(b) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

    .
