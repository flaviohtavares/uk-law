# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026, section 59

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Amendment of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^key-3eea2c212d9bc5233e76e1e3b1df867e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00065] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In section 96B[^f00066] (meaning of “scheme rules”: occupational pension schemes), in subsection (2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (xiv)[^f00067], after “sections” insert “82,”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (xiv) insert—

    > - (xv) regulations 40 and 56 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026;

    ;
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (x)[^f00068] after “69(7)(b)” insert “82(4A),”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (x) insert—

    > - (xi) regulation 40(1)(b) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

    .

(3) In section 97AI (right to cash transfer sum and contribution refund: further provisions)[^f00069], in subsection (7)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (xiii)[^f00070], after “sections” insert “82,”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (xiii) insert—

    > - (xiv) regulations 40 and 56 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026;

    ;
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (x)[^f00071], after “69(7)(b),” insert “82(4A),”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (x) insert—

    > - (xi) regulation 40(1)(b) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) (Extension to Unconnected Multiple Employer Schemes and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

    .
