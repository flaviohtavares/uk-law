# The Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-06-08.

Rates

Made

15th May 2026

Coming into operation

8th June 2026

The Department of Finance makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 37 (4), of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-3eb91383e60f272f21caa6d5526a0c66]

These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 8th June 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^key-694fdd826f881a1fea6dd41a3e19dfdc]

For Part 2 of the Schedule to the Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Part 2 set out in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 15th May 2026.

Andrew McAvoy

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, (“**the 2010 Regulations**”), provide that certain hereditaments of any company listed in the Schedule shall, for the purposes of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977, be treated as a single hereditament occupied by that company.

These Regulations substitute the list in Part 2 of the Schedule to the 2010 Regulations to take account of a change of company name within the market in Northern Ireland.
