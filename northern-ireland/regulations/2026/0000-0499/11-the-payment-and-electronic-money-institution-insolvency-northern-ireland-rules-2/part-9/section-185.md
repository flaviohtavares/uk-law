## 185. Moving from special administration to dissolution[^c-6f65622565923b304a497d84561e583f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where, for the purposes of paragraph 85(1), the administrator sends a notice of moving from special administration to dissolution to the registrar of companies, the administrator must attach to that notice a copy of the final progress report.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable, a copy of the notice and the attached document must be sent to all other persons who received notice of the administrator’s appointment.

(3) Where a court makes an order under paragraph 85(7) it must, where the applicant is not the administrator, give a copy of the order to the administrator.
