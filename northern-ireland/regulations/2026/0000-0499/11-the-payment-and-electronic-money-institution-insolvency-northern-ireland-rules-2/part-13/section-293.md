# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 293

> Text as at 2026-03-02.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 293. Application of the I (N.I.) O 1989 and the Company Directors Disqualification (N.I.) Order 2002[^key-7860a02f3263c51ff724e57b235fba3f]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of these Rules, any reference in I (N.I.) O 1989 or the Company Directors Disqualification (N.I.) Order 2002[^f00026] to “leave” of the court is to be construed as meaning “permission” of the court.
