# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 187

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 187. Shorthand writers — nomination, appointment, remuneration and costs[^key-c02d4b57e738af7c54a1ee7ab02e28d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the High Court, the judge or Master may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.

(2) The court may at any time in the course of the special administration appoint a shorthand writer to take down evidence of a person examined under Article 200 of the I (N.I.) O 1989[^f00017].

(3) The remuneration of a shorthand writer appointed under this rule must be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made, or out of the insolvent estate, or otherwise as the court may direct.

(4) Any question arising as to the rates of remuneration payable under this rule must be determined by the court.
