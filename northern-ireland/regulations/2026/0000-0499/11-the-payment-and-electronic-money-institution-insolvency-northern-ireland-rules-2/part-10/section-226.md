# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 226

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 226. Award of costs against the administrator[^key-55e63cdd34400f940c279284b93246b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to any provision of the Regulations or these Rules by virtue of which the administrator is not in any event to be liable for costs and expenses, where the administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, the administrator is not to be personally liable for the costs unless the court otherwise directs.
