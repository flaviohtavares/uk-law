## 190. Payments into court[^c-c918fa61a4635b9ab3fd0ae30ba97a39]

*Extent: N.I.*

Order 22 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature (miscellaneous provisions about payments into court) applies to money lodged in court under these Rules.
