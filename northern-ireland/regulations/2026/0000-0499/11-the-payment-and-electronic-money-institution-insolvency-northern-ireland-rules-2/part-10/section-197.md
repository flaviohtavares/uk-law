## 197. Service of court documents outside jurisdiction[^c-deb3cf9fd0a134417c378f844397157d]

*Extent: N.I.*

Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies to the service of court documents outside the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.
