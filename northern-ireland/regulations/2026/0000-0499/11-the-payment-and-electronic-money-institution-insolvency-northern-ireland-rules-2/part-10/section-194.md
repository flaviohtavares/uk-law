# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 194

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 194. Evidence provided by the administrator[^key-0590aa4c18010fe157dba2cd5de22b68]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where in the special administration an affidavit is made by the administrator, the affidavit must state—

- (a) the capacity in which that person makes the statement, and
- (b) the person’s business address.

(2) The administrator may file a report with the court instead of an affidavit unless the application involves other parties or the court otherwise orders.

(3) In any case where a report is filed instead of an affidavit, the report must be treated, for the purpose of rule 193 of these Rules and any hearing before the court, as if it were an affidavit.
