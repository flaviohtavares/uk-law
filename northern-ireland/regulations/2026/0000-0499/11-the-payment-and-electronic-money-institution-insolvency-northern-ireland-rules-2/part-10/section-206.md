## 206. Adjournment of the hearing of an application[^key-23ec7f7201306ca1741ece99c8d929ab]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The court may adjourn the hearing of an application on such terms as it thinks just.

(2) The court may give directions as to the manner in which any evidence is to be provided at a resumed hearing and in particular—

- (a) the taking of evidence wholly or partly by affidavit or orally,
- (b) the cross-examination of the maker of an affidavit, or
- (c) any report to be made by the administrator.
