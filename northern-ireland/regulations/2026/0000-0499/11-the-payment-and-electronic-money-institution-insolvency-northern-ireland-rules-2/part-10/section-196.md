> Text as at 2026-03-02.

## 196. Service of court documents within the jurisdiction[^c-be950788c57d5fae4301be6cd4f509f9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Except where different provision is made in the Regulations or these Rules, Order 65 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature applies in relation to the service of court documents with such modifications as the court may direct.
