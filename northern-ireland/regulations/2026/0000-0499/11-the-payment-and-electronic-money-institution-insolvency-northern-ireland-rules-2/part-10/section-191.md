# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 191

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 191. Further information and discovery[^key-f043fe18308e39d5acfdf2902d29b48b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any party to the special administration may apply to the court for an order—

- (a) that any other party—
  - (i) clarify any matter that is in dispute in the proceedings, or
  - (ii) give additional information in relation to any such matter, or
- (b) to obtain discovery from any other party except so far as is otherwise provided by these Rules.

(2) An application under this rule may be made without notice being served on any other party.
