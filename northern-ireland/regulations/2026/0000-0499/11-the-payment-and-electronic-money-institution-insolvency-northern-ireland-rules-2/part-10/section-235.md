## 235. Appeals and reviews of court orders[^c-f67a833b971ad3fb07ad723eb33b53f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The court may review, rescind or vary any order made by it in the exercise of its jurisdiction under the Regulations or these Rules.

(2) Appeals in special administration proceedings are as follows—

- (a) to a single judge of the High Court from an order or decision of the Master, without prejudice to the power of the Master to review an order made under Article 371 of the I (N.I.) O 1989,
- (b) to the Court of Appeal from a decision of a single judge of the High Court.
