## 220. Service of notices following appointment[^key-a22b4edc8cf8b66507e98204e692cc2f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under rule 218 of these Rules, has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
