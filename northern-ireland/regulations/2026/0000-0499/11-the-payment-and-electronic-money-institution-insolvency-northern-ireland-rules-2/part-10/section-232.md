## 232. Warrants (general provisions)[^key-f21414e7bf000cf708dcd4ef98c2fd88]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A warrant issued by the court under any provision of the Regulations must be addressed to any constable.

(2) In this Chapter, references to property include books, papers and records.
