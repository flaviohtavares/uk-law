# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 192

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 192. Affidavits — general[^key-97e77784d045409d61c8072eac550b3f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where evidence is required by the Regulations or these Rules as to any matter, such evidence may be provided in the form of an affidavit unless—

- (a) in any specific case a rule (including rule 194 of these Rules) or the Regulations makes different provision, or
- (b) the court otherwise directs.

(2) The court may, on the application of any party to the matter in question order the attendance for cross-examination of the person making the affidavit.

(3) Where, after such an order has been made, the person in question does not attend, that person’s affidavit must not be used in evidence without the leave of the court.
