> Text as at 2026-03-02.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 186. Principal court rules and practice to apply[^key-eeba03aa2e52d2c8a8e1c41e2479e840]

*Extent: N.I.*

Except so far as it is inconsistent with these Rules, the Rules of the Court of Judicature and the practice of the High Court apply to special administration with any necessary modifications.
