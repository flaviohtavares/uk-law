# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 219

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 219. Affidavit in support of application[^c-fea84a8be4a052bb719a8006eb765aba]

*Extent: N.I.*

An application under rule 218(3) of these Rules must be supported by an affidavit made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
