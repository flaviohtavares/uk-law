# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 208

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 208. Applications under Article 150A(5) to disapply Article 150A[^key-f7600907d24c2570bc6a8a477f384398]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application under Article 150A(5) must be accompanied by an affidavit by the administrator.

(2) The affidavit must state—

- (a) that the institution is in special administration,
- (b) a summary of the financial position of the institution, and
- (c) the information substantiating the administrator’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
