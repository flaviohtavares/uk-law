## 237. Appeal against decision of the Secretary of State[^c-0a6ffb8e6bd6aaba303ca3a471a40a11]

*Extent: N.I.*

An appeal under the Regulations against a decision of the Secretary of State must be brought within twenty-eight days of the notification of the decision.
