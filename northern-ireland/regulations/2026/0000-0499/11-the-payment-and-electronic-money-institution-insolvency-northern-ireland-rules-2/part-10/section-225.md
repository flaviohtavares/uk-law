# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 225

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 225. Costs paid otherwise than out of the institution’s estate[^key-6d65f6248118abac81f1fb3852412627]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a bill of costs is taxed under an order of the court directing that those costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the institution’s estate or out of the asset pool, the Taxing Master must note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
