> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 211. Application of Article 200 of the I (N.I.) O 1989[^c-6648814985dcd4dc219143167ac33795]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies to applications to the court for an order under Article 200 of the I (N.I.) O 1989.

(2) In this Chapter, “**the respondent**” means the person in respect of whom an order is applied for.
