> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 193. Filing and service of affidavits[^key-5f953d2ba2bec3ee9b5d66beaad504ce]

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless the provision of the Regulations or Rules under which the application is made provides otherwise, or the court otherwise allows—

- (a) if the applicant intends to rely at the first hearing on evidence in an affidavit, the applicant must file that affidavit with the court and serve a copy of it on the respondent not less than fourteen days before the date fixed for the hearing, and
- (b) where the respondent to an application intends to oppose it and rely for that purpose on evidence contained in an affidavit, the respondent must file the affidavit with the court and serve a copy on the applicant not less than five business days before the date fixed for the hearing.
