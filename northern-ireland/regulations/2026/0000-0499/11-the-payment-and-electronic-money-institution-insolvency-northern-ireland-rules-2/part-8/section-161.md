# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 161

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 161. Application for the exercise of court’s powers under Article 155[^c-6f84a7eac1b3519dd51cca43572516c4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies with respect to an application by any person under Article 155 of the I (N.I.) O 1989 for an order of the court to vest or deliver disclaimed property.

(2) The application must be made within three months of the applicant becoming aware of the disclaimer, or of the applicant receiving a copy of the administrator’s notice of disclaimer sent under rule 155 of these Rules, whichever is the earlier.

(3) The applicant must with the application file an affidavit—

- (a) stating whether the application is made under—
  - (i) paragraph (a) of Article 155(1), or
  - (ii) paragraph (b) of Article 155(1),
- (b) specifying the date on which the applicant received a copy of the administrator’s notice of disclaimer, or otherwise became aware of the disclaimer, and
- (c) specifying the grounds of the application and the order which the applicant desires the court to make under Article 155.

(4) The court must fix a venue for the hearing of the application, and the applicant must, not later than five business days before the date fixed, give to the administrator notice of the venue, accompanied by copies of the application and the affidavit required by paragraph (3) of this rule.

(5) On the hearing of the application, the court may give directions as to other persons (if any) who should be sent or given notice of the application and the grounds on which it is made.

(6) Sealed copies of any order made on the application must be sent by the court to the applicant and the administrator and any persons given notice of the application under paragraph (5) of this rule.

(7) In a case where the property disclaimed is of a leasehold nature, and Article 153 of the I (N.I.) O 1989 applies to suspend the effect of the disclaimer, there must be included in the court’s order a direction giving effect to the disclaimer.

(8) Paragraph (7) of this rule does not apply if, at the time when the order is issued, other applications under Article 155 are pending in respect of the same property.
