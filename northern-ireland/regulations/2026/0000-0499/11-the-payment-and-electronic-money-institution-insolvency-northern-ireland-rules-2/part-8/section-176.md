# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 176

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 176. Application to replace[^c-38721a6d1bac559e5a81f828cd0d0ee6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an application is made to court under paragraph 92(1), to appoint a replacement administrator, the application must be accompanied by a written statement by the person proposed to be the replacement administrator.

(2) The written statement must be in accordance with rule 6 of these Rules.

(3) A copy of the application must be served on—

- (a) the person who made the application for a special administration order,
- (b) the institution (if neither the institution nor its directors are the applicant),
- (c) on the person nominated for appointment as administrator, and
- (d) the FCA (if not the applicant).

(4) Rule 9 of these Rules, shall apply to the service of an application under paragraph 92(1) as it applies to service of the application for a special administration order.

(5) Rules 10 and 12 of these Rules apply to an application under this rule, and rule 15(1) and (2) of these Rules shall apply to the notice of appointment under paragraph 92(1) as it applies to notice of a special administration order.
