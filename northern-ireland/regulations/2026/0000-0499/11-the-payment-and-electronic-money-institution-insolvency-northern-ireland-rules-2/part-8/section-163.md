# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 163

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 163. Recourse to meeting of creditors and customers[^key-558d9b1a350cd72a3483c40d33e6f43e]

*Extent: N.I.*

If the basis of the administrator’s remuneration for either case in rule 162(1) of these Rules has been fixed by the creditors’ committee and the administrator considers, in either or in both cases, the rate or amount to be insufficient, or the basis to be inappropriate, the administrator may request that the rate or amount be increased or the basis changed by resolution of the creditors and the customers.
