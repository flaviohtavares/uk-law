# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 158

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 158. Application by interested party[^c-911f2f2fb9d1702a9b14a50c3322aea4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The following applies where, in the case of any property, application is made to the administrator by an interested party under Article 152(4) of the I (N.I.) O 1989.

(2) The application must be delivered to the administrator—

- (a) personally,
- (b) by electronic means in accordance with Part 12 of these Rules, or
- (c) by any other means of delivery which enables proof of receipt of the application by the administrator to be provided, if requested.
