## 178. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint administrator[^c-abac36416ed3d6a57125ce538806b7d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to rule 179 of these Rules, where, after an initial appointment has been made, an additional person or persons are to be appointed as joint administrator, the same Rules apply in respect of giving notice of and advertising the appointment as applied in relation to the initial appointment.
