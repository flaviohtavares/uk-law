# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 156

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 156. Additional notices[^c-16e2ce95ea6264f1da6c0e881e82026a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The administrator disclaiming property may at any time send or give copies of the notice of the disclaimer to any persons who in the administrator’s opinion ought, in the public interest or otherwise, to be informed of the disclaimer.

(2) Paragraph (1) of this rule is without prejudice to the administrator’s obligations under Articles 152, 153 and 154 of the I (N.I.) O 1989[^f00016] and rules 154 and 155 of these Rules.
