# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 160

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 160. Disclaimer presumed valid and effective[^c-3a7b493e1e37032af21a414e56e1bf4d]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any disclaimer of property by the administrator is presumed valid and effective, unless it is proved that the administrator has been in breach of their duty with respect to the giving of notice of disclaimer, or otherwise, under Articles 152 to 154 of the I (N.I.) O 1989 or under this Chapter of these Rules.
