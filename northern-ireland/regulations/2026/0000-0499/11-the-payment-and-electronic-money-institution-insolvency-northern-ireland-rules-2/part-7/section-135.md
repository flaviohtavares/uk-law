> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 135. Debt payable at a future time[^key-4d83a24edf522de7d54d82ab8b47abb6]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to rule 152 of these Rules, a creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the institution entered special administration.
