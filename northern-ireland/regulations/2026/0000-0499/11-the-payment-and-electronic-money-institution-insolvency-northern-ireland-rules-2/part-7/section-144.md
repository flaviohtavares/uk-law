# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 144

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 144. Declaration of a dividend[^c-a4c36339cd4e39578e4b6ffef48c498a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where rule 143(2) of these Rules does not apply and subject to paragraph (2) of this rule, within the two month period referred to in rule 141(5)(a) of these Rules, the administrator must proceed to declare the dividend to one or more classes of creditor who have been given notice under that rule.

(2) Except with the permission of the court, the administrator must not declare a dividend so long as there is pending any application to the court to reverse or vary the administrator’s decision on a proof, or to exclude a proof, or to reduce the amount claimed.

(3) If the court gives permission under paragraph (2) of this rule, the administrator must make such provision in respect of the proof in question as the court directs.
