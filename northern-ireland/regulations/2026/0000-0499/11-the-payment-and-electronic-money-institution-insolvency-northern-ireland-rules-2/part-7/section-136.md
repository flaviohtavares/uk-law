# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 136

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 136. Value of security[^key-28105dca83fff33877b63fe299bccd1f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) of this rule, a secured creditor may, with the agreement of the administrator or the permission of the court, at any time alter the value which that creditor has, in their proof of debt, put upon their security.

(2) A secured creditor may re-value their security only with the permission of the court if that secured creditor—

- (a) being the applicant for a special administration order, has in the application put a value on their security, or
- (b) has voted in respect of the unsecured balance of their debt.
