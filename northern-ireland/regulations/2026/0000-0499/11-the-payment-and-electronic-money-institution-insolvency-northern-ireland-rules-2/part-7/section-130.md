# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 130

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 130. Discounts[^c-1a3e2aa7708b4ffbaabbf97b91678bb4]

*Extent: N.I.*

All trade and other discounts, except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement, which would have been available to the institution but for it going into special administration, must in every case be deducted from the claim.
