> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 123. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend[^key-1b81618c2f70f80600dbfade53182c03]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The administrator may admit or reject a proof in whole or in part.

(2) If the administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the administrator must prepare a written statement of reasons for doing so, and send it as soon as is reasonably practicable to the creditor.
