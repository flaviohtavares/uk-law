# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 128

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 128. Negotiable instruments[^c-57aca07ef23b02f43693d2c7026ad16c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless the administrator allows, a proof in respect of money owed on a bill of exchange, promissory note, cheque or other negotiable instrument or security cannot be admitted unless there is produced the instrument or security itself or a copy of it, certified by the creditor or the creditor’s authorised representative to be a true copy.
