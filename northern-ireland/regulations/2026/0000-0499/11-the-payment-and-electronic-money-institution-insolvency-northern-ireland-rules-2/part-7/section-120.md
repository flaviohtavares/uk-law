# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 120

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 120. Costs of proving[^key-b79f590cb7486e87fd8ed4a2cbaab948]

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless the court otherwise orders—

- (a) every creditor bears the cost of proving their own debt, including costs incurred in providing documents or evidence under rule 119 of these Rules, except where the administrator has proved a debt in relation to a customer’s shortfall claim under rule 119(6) of these Rules, and
- (b) costs incurred by the administrator in estimating the quantum of a debt under rule 127 of these Rules, are payable out of the institution’s assets as an expense of the special administration.
