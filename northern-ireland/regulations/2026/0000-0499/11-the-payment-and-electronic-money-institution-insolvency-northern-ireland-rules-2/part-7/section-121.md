# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 121

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 121. Administrator to allow inspection of proofs[^c-1c03d197535e02b5fefa701b04c6e9a7]

*Extent: N.I.*

The administrator must, so long as proofs lodged are in the administrator’s hands, allow them to be inspected, at all reasonable times on any business day, by—

- (a) any creditor who has submitted a proof of debt (unless that proof has been wholly rejected for purposes of dividend or otherwise),
- (b) any contributory of the company, or
- (c) any person acting on behalf of either of the above.
