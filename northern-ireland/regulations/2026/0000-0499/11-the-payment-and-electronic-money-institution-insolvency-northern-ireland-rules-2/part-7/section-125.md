> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 125. Withdrawal or variation of proof[^key-b8de1611b0eeaa0f5725e948b4ee9beb]

*Extent: N.I.*

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement with the administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
