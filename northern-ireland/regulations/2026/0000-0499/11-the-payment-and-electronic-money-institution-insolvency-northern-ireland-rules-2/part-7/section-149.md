# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 149

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 149. Secured creditors[^key-e00190487ae097faee782a2fd9559bb8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where a creditor re-values their security at a time when a dividend has been declared.

(2) If the revaluation results in a reduction of the creditor’s unsecured claim ranking for dividend, the creditor must, as soon as is reasonably practicable, repay to the administrator, for the credit of the special administration, any amount received by the creditor as dividend in excess of that to which that creditor would be entitled having regard to the revaluation of the security.

(3) If the revaluation results in an increase of the creditor’s unsecured claim, the creditor is entitled to receive from the administrator, out of any money for the time being available for the payment of a further dividend and before any such further dividend is paid, any dividend or dividends which the creditor has failed to receive, having regard to the revaluation of the security.

(4) However, the creditor is not entitled to disturb any dividend declared (whether or not distributed) before the date of the revaluation.
