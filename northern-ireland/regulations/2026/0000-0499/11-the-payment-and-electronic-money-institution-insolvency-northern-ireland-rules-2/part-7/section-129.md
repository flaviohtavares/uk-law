# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 129

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Secured creditors[^c-b76bac36e48356159a961d41e6ad3f73]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a secured creditor realises their security, the creditor may prove for the balance of their debt, after deducting the amount realised.

(2) If a secured creditor voluntarily surrenders their security for the general benefit of creditors, they may prove for their whole debt, as if it were unsecured.
