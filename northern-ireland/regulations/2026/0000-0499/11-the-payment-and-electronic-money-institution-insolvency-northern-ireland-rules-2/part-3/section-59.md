# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 59

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Requisite majorities at contributories’ meetings[^c-3b150920704030e07c1d473087626bd7]

*Extent: N.I.*

At a meeting of contributories, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the institution, subject to any provision of the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the institution is in liquidation.
