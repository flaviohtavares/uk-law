## 48. Claims, proofs and proxies in adjournment[^c-a8a7be973639196ca74ed05195b36664]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a meeting under these Rules is adjourned, claims, proofs and proxies may be used if lodged at any time up to 12.00 hours on the business day immediately before the adjourned meeting.
