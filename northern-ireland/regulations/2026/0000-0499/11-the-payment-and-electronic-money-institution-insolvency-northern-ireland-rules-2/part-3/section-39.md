## 39. Content of notice for meetings[^key-9b801bfa2e10aafc6baab02c3d5ceee3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Notice of a meeting of the creditors, the customers or a meeting of creditors and customers, must contain the following information—

- (a) a statement that the proceedings are being held in the court and the court reference number;
- (b) the full name, registered address, registered number and any other trading names of the institution;
- (c) the full name and business address of the administrator;
- (d) details of the venue of the meeting;
- (e) whether the meeting is—
  - (i) an initial creditors’ and customers’ meeting under paragraph 52,
  - (ii) to consider revisions to the administrator’s proposals under paragraph 55(2),
  - (iii) a further creditors’, or creditors and customers’, or customers’ meeting under paragraph 57, or
  - (iv) a meeting under paragraph 63,

unless the court orders that it be given by advertisement only in accordance with rule 38 of these Rules.

(2) Where the court orders an extension to the period set out in paragraph 52(2)(b), the administrator must notify each person who was sent notice in accordance with paragraph 50(4).
