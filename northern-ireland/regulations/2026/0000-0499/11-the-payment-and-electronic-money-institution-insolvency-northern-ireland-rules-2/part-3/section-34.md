> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Meetings generally[^c-de59bcee2b618eceadaf486f1ae43e1c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Except where different provision is made in the Regulations or these Rules, this Chapter applies to meetings summoned by the administrator under—

- (a) paragraph 52,
- (b) paragraph 55(2),
- (c) paragraph 63,

or following a request or a direction from the court under paragraph 57.
