> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 85. Formal defects[^key-84f1843b8415139f7f6bad2c0bcf3f61]

*Extent: N.I.*

The acts of the creditors’ committee established for a special administration are valid despite any defect in the appointment, election or qualifications of any member of the creditors’ committee or any creditors’ committee-member’s representative or in the formalities of its establishment.
