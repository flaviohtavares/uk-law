> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 107. Withdrawal or variation of relevant funds claim[^c-01f9546e96a322c85b7a77ae09fff8bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

A relevant funds claim may at any time, with the agreement of the administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
