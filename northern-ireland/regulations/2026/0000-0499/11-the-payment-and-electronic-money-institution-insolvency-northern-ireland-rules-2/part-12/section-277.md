> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 277. Notifications of more than one nature[^key-ff0619fea4cf9de95f8ad7c123b14233]

*Extent: N.I.*

A notification which includes two or more of the types of notification set out in rules 272 to 276 of these Rules, must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
