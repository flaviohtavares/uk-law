## 285. Proceedings commenced in the wrong division of the High Court[^c-3f8aea327f43f16dbf4651c6702be75b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where proceedings relating to a special administration are commenced in one division of the High Court, the Judge may, of his own motion order the transfer of the proceedings to the Chancery Division of the High Court.
