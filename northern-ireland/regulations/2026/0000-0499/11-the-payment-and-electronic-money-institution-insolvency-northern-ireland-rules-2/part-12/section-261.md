> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 261. Execution overtaken by judgment debtor’s insolvency[^key-9da1f8ae6918460ff1ff1dfdb38d2b90]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where execution has been taken out against property of a judgment debtor, and notice is given to the Enforcement of Judgments Office that the judgment debtor has entered special administration.

(2) Subject to rule 262 of these Rules, the notice must be delivered to the office of the Enforcement of Judgments Office—

- (a) by hand, or
- (b) by any other means of delivery which enables proof of receipt of the document at the relevant address.
