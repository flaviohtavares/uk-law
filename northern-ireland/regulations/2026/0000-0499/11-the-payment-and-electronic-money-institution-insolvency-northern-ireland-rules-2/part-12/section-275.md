## 275. Notifications relating to court orders[^key-ada6e23eb4de4c732cd2c7592a77de9b]

*Extent: N.I.*

In addition to the information required by rule 272 of these Rules, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order, and
- (b) the date of the order.
