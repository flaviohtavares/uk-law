# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 266

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 266. Omission of unobtainable information[^c-c078c10f878728ec4ceda4dc20a68bcb]

*Extent: N.I.*

Information required under rule 264 or 265 of these Rules to be included in a notice to be gazetted may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
