## 271. Application of Chapter 4[^c-0ff454586a5dc62d0c8c20d6413d4efc]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies where a return, notice, document or other information is to be sent or delivered to the registrar of companies under the Regulations or these Rules. For the purposes of this Chapter, “**notification**” means any return, notice, document or other information which is to be sent or delivered to the registrar of companies,
