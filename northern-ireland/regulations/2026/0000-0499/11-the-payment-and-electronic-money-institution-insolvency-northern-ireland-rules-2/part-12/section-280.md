> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 280. Right to copy documents[^key-33723e98456ae6b52dad0427078b31ec]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Regulations or these Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of obtaining copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 116 of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00025], and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
