# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 259

## 259. Electronic delivery of special administration documents to court[^key-d1e6ae9fbdf1aa2094f0f54524dbef10]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A notice or other document may not be delivered to a court by electronic means unless expressly permitted by these Rules.

(2) A document delivered to the court by electronic means is to be treated as delivered to the court at the time it is recorded by the court as having been received or otherwise as these Rules provide.
