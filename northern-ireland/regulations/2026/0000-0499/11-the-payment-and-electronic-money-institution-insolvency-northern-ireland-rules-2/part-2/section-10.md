> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Proof of service[^key-c43abc2c1506c4356bf0487da2e7e85f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Service of the application must be verified by an affidavit specifying the date and time on which, and the manner in which, service was effected.

(2) The affidavit, with a sealed copy of the application exhibited to it, must be filed with the court—

- (a) as soon as is reasonably practicable, and
- (b) in any event, before the hearing of the application.
