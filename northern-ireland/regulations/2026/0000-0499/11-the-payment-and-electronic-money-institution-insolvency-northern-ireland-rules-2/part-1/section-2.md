# The Payment and Electronic Money Institution Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Rules 2026, section 2

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Commencement[^key-5994c13e49c3a5827e3c6480c6cc0190]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Rules come into operation on 2nd March 2026, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) of this rule.

(2) Rules 62, 63, 64 and 65 come into operation on the coming into operation of Article 208ZA of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.

(3) Rule 258 comes into operation on the coming into operation of Article 208ZB of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.
