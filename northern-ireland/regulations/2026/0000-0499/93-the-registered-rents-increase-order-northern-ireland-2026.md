# The Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

> Text as at 2026-07-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2026 No. 93

Landlord And Tenant

The Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

20th May 2026

Coming into operation

6th July 2026

The Department for Communities makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 55(5) and (6) of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

The Department has accepted the recommendation of the rent officer made under Article 55(4) of that Order that the rents referred to in Article 55(1) of that Order should be increased.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-ee1a578ef295c73f946a12aed889f8db]

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 6th July 2026.

## 2. Increase of registered rents[^key-0c03195b9d23c34084819804fdd2255d]

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) the rents registered under Part IV of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 during the period beginning on 2nd April 2007 and ending on 5th July 2026 shall be increased by 4.8 percent.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a rent entered on the register of rents unless a certificate of fitness has been issued except where such a certificate is not required.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 20th May 2026.

David Polley

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the rents registered with the rent officer for Northern Ireland under Part IV of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 during the period beginning on 2nd April 2007 and ending on 5th July 2026 by 4.8 percent.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks’ notice of the increase in accordance with Article 49 of the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.
