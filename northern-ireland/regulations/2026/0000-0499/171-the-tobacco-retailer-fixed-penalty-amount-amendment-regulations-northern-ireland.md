Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

5th October 2026

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(8) and 24(3) of the Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00002],

In accordance with section 24(2) of that Act, a draft of these Regulations has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the Assembly.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-86abc3c6f95e6fca2726a1ea3a14533e]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026.

(2) Except as provided in paragraph (3) and (4), these regulations come into operation on 29<sup>th</sup> October 2026.

(3) Regulation 4 comes into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> January 2027.

(4) Regulation 5 comes into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> March 2027.

## 2. Amendment of the Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^key-fbb3cca4a050d2bf848f9bab1c8c64f6]

The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 5.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 (“**the Regulations**”).

Regulation 3 and 4 amend the 2016 Regulations to refer to “herbal smoking products” in addition to “tobacco products” as well as prescribing the amount of fixed penalties for certain new tobacco product and herbal smoking product related offences under the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1978.

Regulation 5 amends the provisions in the 2016 Regulations relating to the registration of tobacco retailers, so they also apply to retailers of vaping and nicotine products by referring to a “registrable” business and prescribes the amount of fixed penalties for the related offences under the Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) 2014.

The amendments made by regulation 3 come into operation on 29<sup>th</sup> October 2026 and substitute the Schedule in the 2016 Regulations. Amendments to that substituted schedule are made by regulation 4 and 5 and are effective from 1<sup>st</sup> January 2027 and 1<sup>st</sup> March 2027 respectively.

Regulation 6 revokes The Tobacco Retailer (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (SR ).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
