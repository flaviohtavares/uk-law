## 20. Enforcement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Food Standards Agency enforces in slaughterhouses and cutting plants—

- (a) Schedule 7; and
- (b) paragraphs 1, 3 and 4 of Schedule 8.

(2) Otherwise these Regulations are enforced by the Department.
