# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 17

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Notices restricting movement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a notice is served restricting movement of any animal or product, inspectors may subsequently permit that movement under the authority of a licence.

(2) A person moving anything under the authority of a licence must carry the licence with them during the movement and produce it on demand to an inspector or an officer of the Police Service of Northern Ireland, and failure to do so is an offence.
