# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 4

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Exception for research

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The provisions of Schedules 2 to 8 do not apply in relation to an animal, a carcase or a sample kept for the purposes of research in premises approved for that purpose under this regulation by the Department.

(2) If a bovine, ovine or caprine animal, kept in approved research premises under this regulation or its progeny dies or is killed, the occupier must dispose of it as a Category 1 animal by-product in accordance with Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1069) and failure to do so is an offence.
