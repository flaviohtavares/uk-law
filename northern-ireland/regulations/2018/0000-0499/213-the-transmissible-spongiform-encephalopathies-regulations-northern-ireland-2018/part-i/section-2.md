# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 2

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “**bovine animal**” includes bison and buffalo (including water buffalo);
- “**BSE**” means bovine spongiform encephalopathy;
- “**cattle passport**” has the same meaning as in the Cattle Passport Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 [^c23700351];
- “**Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/411)**” means Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/411) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/999) and repealing Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2005/598) [^c23700361];
- “cutting plant” (except in Schedule 7, paragraph 9(3)(b)(iii)) has the meaning given to it in paragraph 1(17) of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853), and is an establishment which is—
  - (a) approved or conditionally approved as such by the Food Standards Agency [^c23700371] under Article 148(3) of Regulation (EU) 2017/625[^c-2e1a0eee36d1ee4a60def99a819f5ef1] ; or
  - (b) operating as such under Article 4(5) of Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853), pending such approval;
- “**the Department**” means the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs;
- “**EU TSE Regulation**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies [^c23700391], as read with—
  - (a) Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/411);
  - (b) Commission Decision [2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/453) establishing the BSE status of Member States or third countries or regions thereof according to their BSE risk [^c23700401]; and
  - (c) Commission Decision [2009/719/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2009/719) authorising certain Member States to revise their annual BSE monitoring programmes [^c23700411];
- “**inspector**” means an inspector appointed under regulation 13, and includes a veterinary inspector;
- “**keeper**” in relation to an animal means any natural or legal person responsible for that animal whether on a permanent or temporary basis including during transportation or at a market;
- “**market value**” means—
  - (a) in the case of a bovine animal, the price which might reasonably have been obtained for the animal at the time of valuation from a purchaser in the open market if the animal was not required to be killed under Schedule 3; and
  - (b) in the case of an ovine or caprine animal, the price which might reasonably have been obtained for the animal at the time of valuation from a purchaser in the open market if the animal was not from a flock or herd affected by a TSE;
- “official document” must be construed in accordance with regulation 7(1);
- “**offal**” has the meaning given to it in point 1(11) of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853);
- “**premises**” includes—
  - (a) domestic premises if they are being used for any purpose in connection with the EU TSE Regulation or these Regulations;
  - (b) land and outbuildings;
  - (c) a slaughterhouse;
  - (d) a place that is, for the purposes of point 4(1)(a) of Annex V, another place of slaughter; and
  - (e) any vehicle, container or structure (moveable or otherwise);
- “**Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853)**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin [^c23700421], as read with—
  - (a) Directive [2004/41/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/41) of the European Parliament and of the Council repealing certain directives concerning food hygiene and health conditions for the production and placing on the market of certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption and amending Council Directives [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/662) and [92/118/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1992/118) and Council Decision [95/408/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/1995/408) [^c23700431];
  - (b) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1688/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1688) implementing Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards special guarantees concerning salmonella for consignments to Finland and Sweden of certain meat and eggs [^c23700441]; and
  - (c) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2074) laying down implementation measures for certain products under Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council and for the organisation of official controls under Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/882) of the European Parliament and of the Council derogating from Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) [^c23700451];
- “Regulation (EU) 2017/625” means Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products;[^c-c13a2f08f14e763468dbd65d58e8ef5d]
- “**Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1069)**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1069) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules as regards animal by-products and derived products not intended for human consumption as implemented by Commission Regulation EU No. 142/2011 [^c23700471];
- “**restriction**” includes any prohibition under these Regulations;
- “**slaughterhouse**” has the meaning given to it in point 1(16) of Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853), and is an establishment which is approved or conditionally approved as such by the Food Standards Agency under Article 148(3) of Regulation (EU) 2017/625[^c-e7cf9179f2f7944e625a138a69029e7e] ;
- “**specified risk material**” has the meaning given to it in Article 3(1)(g);
- “**third country**” means any country which is not a member State of the EU;
- “**TSE**” means transmissible spongiform encephalopathy; and
- “**veterinary inspector**” means a person appointed by the Department as a veterinary inspector.

(2) Expressions that are not defined in these Regulations and occur in the EU TSE Regulation have the same meaning in these Regulations as they have for the purposes of the EU TSE Regulation, and references in these Regulations to Articles or Annexes are to Articles or Annexes of the EU TSE Regulation.

(3) References in these Regulations to the following EU instruments—

- (a) The EU TSE Regulation;
- (b) Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853);
- (c)[^c-f9a473e2999124403f7f5ed6e34b6a02] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/411);
- (e) Commission Decision [2007/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2007/453);
- (f) Commission Decision [2009/719/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2009/719);
- (g) Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1069); and
- (h) Commission Regulation (EU) No. 142/2011 [^c23700481];

are references to those EU instruments as amended from time to time.

(4) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^c23700491] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.
