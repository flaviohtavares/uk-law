# The Sea Fishing (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 185

Sea Fisheries

The Sea Fishing (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made

25th October 2018

Coming into operation-

1st December 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] is designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to the common agriculture policy and the environment of the European Union.

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes the following Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 127(1) and (3A) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00004], section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and section 30(2) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00005].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 1 December 2018.

## 2. Amendment to secondary legislation

Schedule 1 shall have effect.

## 3. Revocations

The Orders in Schedule 2 are revoked insofar as they relate to Northern Ireland.

**4.** The Regulations in Schedule 3 are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 25 October 2018.

Dr John Speers

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

SCHEDULES

## SCHEDULE 1

Article 2

### 1. Crabs and Lobsters (Minimum Size) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

In Article 2(1) (Interpretation) of The Crabs and Lobsters (Minimum Size) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00006] after the definition of “the Act” insert—

> - “British fishing boat” means a fishing boat which is either registered in the United Kingdom under Part II of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00007] or is owned by persons qualified to own British ships for the purposes of that part of the Act;
> - “foreign fishing boat” means a fishing boat which is not a British fishing boat; and

.

### 2. The Registration of Fish Buyers and Sellers and Designation of Fish Auction Sites Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

In regulations 14(3) of The Registration of Fish Buyers and Sellers and Designation of Fish Auction Sites Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00008] for “justice of the peace” and “justice” substitute “lay magistrate”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes minor amendments to a number of statutory rules and revokes a number of other statutory instruments,

Article 2 and Schedule 1 provide for a number of minor amendments which correct a drafting omission and some definitions.

Article 3 and Schedule 2 revoke 13 statutory instruments insofar as they extend to Northern Ireland.

Article 4 and Schedule 3 revoke 3 statutory rules in their entirety.
