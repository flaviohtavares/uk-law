# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — AMENDMENT OF WATER PROVISIONS

Regulation 3

### 1. Amendment of the Control of Asbestos in Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

In the Control of Asbestos in Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00091], in regulation 2(1) (Interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” substitute—

> - “the Directive” means Council [Directive 87/217/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0217) on the prevention and reduction of environmental pollution by asbestos as last amended by Decision (EU) 2018/853 as regards procedural rules in the field of environmental reporting[^f00092].

.

### 2. Amendment of the Urban Waste Water Treatment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

The Urban Waste Water Treatment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00093] are amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” substitute—

> - “the Directive” means Council [Directive 91/271/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0271) concerning urban waste water treatment as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)[^f00094];

.

**4.** In Schedule 1(CRITERIA FOR IDENTIFICATION OF SENSITIVE AREAS), Part 1, sub-paragraph (b), for the words from “the concentration” to “Member States” substitute “50mg/l of nitrates in 95% of the samples”.

### 5. Amendment of the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

The Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00095] are amended as follows.

**6.** In regulation 2(2) (Interpretation), for the definition of “Bathing Water Directive” substitute—

> - “Bathing Water Directive” means [Directive 2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0007) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the management of bathing water quality as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)[^f00096];

.

**7.** In Schedule 4, paragraph 2, for “the Directive” substitute “the Bathing Water Directive”.

### 8. Amendment of the Groundwater Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

The Groundwater Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00097] are amended as follows.

**9.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation), for the definition of “the Water Framework Directive” substitute—

> - “the Water Framework Directive” means [Directive 2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy as last amended by Commission [Directive 2014/101/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0101)[^f00098].

.

**10.** In regulation 13 (Review of existing disposal licences and existing resolutions of a District Council), for “Council [Directive 80/68/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0068)” substitute “the Directive”.

### 11. Amendment of the Water Supply (Domestic Distribution Systems) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

In the Water Supply (Domestic Distribution Systems) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00099], in regulation 1(2)(b) (Citation, commencement and interpretation), for the definition of “the Drinking Water Directive” substitute—

> - “the Drinking Water Directive” means Council [Directive 98/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0083) on the quality of water intended for human consumption as last amended by Commission Directive (EU) 2015/1787[^f00100];

.

### 12. Amendment of the Chemical Analysis of Water Status (Technical Specifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

In the Chemical Analysis of Water Status (Technical Specifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00101], in regulation 2(1) (Interpretation), for the definition of “the Water Framework Directive” substitute—

> - “the Water Framework Directive” means Directive 2000/60 EC of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy as last amended by Commission [Directive 2014/101/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0101)[^f00102].

.

### 13. Amendment of the Water Framework Directive (Classification, Priority Substances and Shellfish Waters) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

In the Water Framework Directive (Classification, Priority Substances and Shellfish Waters) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 [^f00103], in regulation 15(2)(b) (Watch List), for “2008 Directive” substitute “the Directive”.

### 14. Amendment of the Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

In the Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00104], for Schedule 3 (EU instruments for the protection of water) substitute—

> **SCHEDULE 3 — EU INSTRUMENTS FOR THE PROTECTION OF WATER**
>
> Regulation 20(2)(a)
>
> **1.** Council [Directive 91/271/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0271) concerning urban waste-water treatment as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)[^f00105].
>
> **2.** Council [Directive 91/676/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0676) concerning the protection of waters against pollution caused by nitrates from agricultural sources as last amended by Regulation [(EC) No. 1137/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1137)[^f00106].
>
> **3.** Council [Directive 92/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0043) on the conservation of natural habitats of wild fauna and flora as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)[^f00107].
>
> **4.** Council [Directive 98/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0083) on the quality of water intended for human consumption as last amended by Council Directive 2015/1787[^f00108].
>
> **5.** [Directive 2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0007) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the management of bathing water quality as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)[^f00109].
>
> **6.** [Directive 2006/118/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0118) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of groundwater against pollution and deterioration as last amended by Commission [Directive 2014/80/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0080)[^f00110].
>
> **7.** [Directive 2008/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0105) of the European Parliament and of the Council on environmental quality standards in the field of water policy as last amended by [Directive 2013/39/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0039)[^f00111].
>
> **8.** [Directive 2009/147/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0147) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of wild birds as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)[^f00112].
>
> **9.** [Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0075) of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control)[^f00113].
>
> **10.** [Directive 2011/92/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0092) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the assessment of the effects of certain public and private projects on the environment as last amended by the [Directive 2014/52/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0052)[^f00114].
>
> **11.** [Directive 2012/18/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2012/0018) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the control of major-accident hazards involving dangerous substances[^f00115].

.

### 15. Amendment of the Private Water Supplies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

In the Private Water Supplies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00116] in Schedule 4 (Sampling and Analysis), Part 3, paragraph 7 and Table D, footnote 1 for “laid down in Annex III, Table A of Directive 96/29/Euratom” substitute “referred to as “standard values and relationships” in Article 13, and recommended for the estimation of doses from internal exposure in the definition of “standard values and relationships” in Article 4(96), of the Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom laying down basic safety standards for protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation[^f00117]”.

### 16. Amendment of the Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

The Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00118] are amended as follows.

**17.** In Schedule 5 (Monitoring for Individual Radionuclides)—

- (a) In paragraph 2, for “laid down in Annex III, Table A of Directive 96/29/Euratom” substitute “referred to as “standard values and relationships” in Article 13, and recommended for the estimation of doses from internal exposure in the definition of “standard values and relationships” in Article 4(96), of the Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom laying down basic safety standards for the protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation [^f00119]”;
- (b) In footnote 1, to Table 1, for “laid down in Annex III of Directive 96/29/Euratom” substitute “referred to as “standard values and relationships” in Article 13, and recommended for the estimation of doses from internal exposure in the definition of “standard values and relationships” in Article 4(96), of the Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom laying down basic safety standards for the protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation”.
