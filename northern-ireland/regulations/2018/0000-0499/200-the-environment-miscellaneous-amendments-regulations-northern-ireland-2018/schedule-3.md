# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — AMENDMENT OF AIR AND ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY PROVISIONS

Regulation 4

### 1. Amendment to the Sulphur Content of Liquid Fuels Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

The Sulphur Content of Liquid Fuels Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00120] are amended as follows.

**2.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation) for the definition “Council [Directive 1999/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0032)[^f00121]” substitute—

> - “Directive (EU) 2016/802” means Directive (EU) 2016/802 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 May 2016 relating to a reduction in the sulphur content of certain liquid fuels[^f00122];

.

**3.** For regulation 2(3) (Interpretation), substitute “Expressions used in these Regulations that also appear in Directive (EU) 2016/802 have the same meaning as they do in that Directive.”.

**4.** In regulation 4(2) (Maximum sulphur content of heavy fuel oil), in paragraphs (a)(i) and (ii) and (b), for “[Directive 2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080)” substitute “[Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0075)[^f00123]”.

### 5. Amendment of the Air Quality Standards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

The Air Quality Standards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00124] are amended as follows.

**6.** In regulation 19(4) (Duty in relation to target values), for “[Directive 2008/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0001) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning integrated pollution, prevention and control”, substitute “[Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0075) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 on industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control)”.

**7.** In regulation 25(5) (Air quality plans) after “atmospheric pollutants” insert “as last amended by Directive (EU) 2016/2284[^f00125]” and after “noise” insert “as last amended by Commission Directive (EU) 2015/996[^f00126]”.

### 8. Amendment of the Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

The Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00127] are amended as follows.

**9.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation: general)—

- (a) in the definition of “poultry”, for “Council [Directive 90/539/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0539)” to the end, substitute “Council [Directive 2009/158/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0158) of 30 November 2009 on animal health conditions governing intra-Community trade in, and imports from third countries of, poultry and hatching eggs as last amended by Commission Implementing [Decision 2011/879/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2011/0879)[^f00128];”;
- (b) in the definition of “recovery” for “the Directive”, substitute “[Directive 2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)[^f00129]”.

**10.** In regulation 12(7) (Conditions of permits: specific conditions for all installations and mobile plant) after “2003/87/EC” insert “as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/410 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00130]”.

**11.** In regulation 26(6) (Duty of the enforcing authority to undertake inspections and to ensure compliance with conditions) after “25 November 2009” insert “as last amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1505[^f00131]”.

**12.** In Schedule 1, Part 1 (Activities, Installations and Mobile Plants)—

- (a) in Section 1.2, Interpretation of Part C, paragraph 2, after “stations” insert “as last amended by Council Regulation (EC) No.1137/2008[^f00132]”;
- (b) in Section 5.1, Part C, in paragraph (vii) in the definition of “excluded plant” after “consumption” insert “as last amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1385/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1385)[^f00133]”;
- (c) in Section 5.4, Part A, in paragraph (a) after “treatment” insert “as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)[^f00134]”;
- (d) in Section 5.4, Part A, in paragraph (b) after “91/271/EEC”, insert “as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)”;
- (e) in Section 6.10, Part A, in paragraph (a) after “dioxide” insert “as last amended by [Directive 2011/92/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0092)[^f00135]”;
- (f) in Section 6.11, Part A, in paragraph (a) after “[Directive 91/271/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0271)” insert “as last amended by [Directive 2013/64/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0064)”;
- (g) in Section 7, Interpretation of Part C, in paragraph 2, in the definition of “other coating activities” for “Directive” to the end, substitute “[Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046) as last amended by [Commission Regulation 2017/2400/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2400)[^f00136]”;
- (h) in Section 7, Interpretation Part C, in paragraph 2, in the definition of “specified substances or mixtures” after “Regulation No. 1272/2008”, in both places it occurs, insert “as last amended by Commission Regulation 2017/776/EU[^f00137]”; and,
- (i) in Section 7, Interpretation of Part C, in paragraph 2 in the definitions “vehicle coating” and “vehicle refinishing”, for “[Directive 70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)” in all places it occurs, substitute “[Directive 2007/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0046) as last amended by [Commission Regulation 2017/2400/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/2400)”.

**13.** In Schedule 4 (Grant of Permits)—

- (a) in Part 1, paragraph 4(2)(b), after “mixtures” the second time it occurs, insert “as last amended by Commission Regulation 2017/776/EU”; and,
- (b) in Part 2, paragraph 16, after “environment” insert “as last amended by [Directive 2014/52/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0052)[^f00138]”.

**14.** In Schedule 13A (Energy Efficiency Directive), paragraph 12(2), after “[2009/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0031)” insert “as last amended by [Directive 2011/92/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0092)[^f00139]”.

### 15. Amendment of the Emissions Performance Standard Monitoring and Enforcement Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

In the Emissions Performance Standard Monitoring and Enforcement Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00140] in regulation 2(2) (Interpretation) for the definition of “the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation” substitute—

> - “the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No. 601/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0601) on the monitoring and reporting of greenhouse gas emissions pursuant to [Directive 2003/87/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0087) of the European Parliament and of the Council as last amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 743/2014[^f00141];

.
