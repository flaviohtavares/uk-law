# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** (1) Schedule 3 of these Regulations amends the Air and Environmental Quality Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations “the Air and Environmental Quality Provisions” means—

- (a) the Sulphur Content of Liquid Fuels Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00028];
- (b) the Air Quality Standards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00029];
- (c) the Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00030]; and,
- (d) the Emissions Performance Standard Monitoring and Enforcement Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00031].
