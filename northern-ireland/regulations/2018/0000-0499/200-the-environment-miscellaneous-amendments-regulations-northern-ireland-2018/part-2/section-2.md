# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**2.** (1) Schedule 1 of these Regulations amends the Waste Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations “the Waste Provisions” means—

- (a) the Controlled Waste (Registration of Carriers and Seizure of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland)1999[^f00008];
- (b) the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00009];
- (c) the Landfill Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00010];
- (d) the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004[^f00011];
- (e) the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00012];
- (f) the Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00013];
- (g) the Environmental Liability (Prevention and Remediation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00014];
- (h) the Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00015];
- (i) the Single Use Carrier Bags Charge Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00016]; and,
- (j) the Controlled Waste and Duty of Care Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00017].
