# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, SCHEDULE 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 5 — Amendment of the Conservation and Environmental Impact Assessment Provisions

Regulation 6

### 1. The Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

The Conservation (Natural Habitats, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00155] are amended as follows.

(1) In regulation 2(2A)(a), for heads (i) and (ii) substitute—

> - (i) “the Habitats Directive” means Council [Directive 92/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0043)[^f00156] on the conservation of natural habitats and of wild fauna and flora, as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)[^f00157];
> - (ii) “the Wild Birds Directive” means [Directive 2009/147/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0147)[^f00158]of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of wild birds, as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017); and,

.

### 2. The Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

The Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00159] are amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation), in the definition of “the Habitats Directive”, for “[97/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0062)” substitute “2013/17/EU[^f00160]”;

**4.** In regulation 5(2) (Environmental assessment for plans and programmes: first formal preparatory act on or after 21 July 2004), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) sets the framework for future development consent of projects listed in Annex I or II to [Directive 2011/92/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0092) on the assessment of the effects of certain public and private projects on the environment as last amended by [Directive 2014/52/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0052) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 [^f00161]

;

**5.** In Schedule 2 (information on environmental reports), in paragraph 4, for “ Council [Directive 79/409/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0409) on the conservation of wild birds” substitute “Council [Directive 2009/147/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0147) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of wild birds as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)”.

### 6. The Environmental Impact Assessment (Forestry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

The Environmental Assessment (Forestry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00162] are amended as follows.

**7.** In Schedule 1 (Information for inclusion in Environmental Statements), in paragraph 9 after “nuclear installations” insert “as last amended by Council Directive 2014/87/Euratom [^f00163]”;

**8.** In Schedule 3 (Projects having significant effects on the environment: selection criteria), in paragraph 2(c)(v) at the end add “as each last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)[^f00164]”;

**9.** In Schedule 4 (Environmental factors), in paragraph (b) at the end add “as last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)”.

### 10. The Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

The Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00165] are amended as follows.

**11.** In Schedule 2 paragraph 2(c)(v) for “[Directive 92/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0043) and Directive [2009/147/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0147)” substitute “the Habitats Directive or the Birds Directive”;

**12.** In Schedule 3 (Information for inclusion in the environmental statements) in Part 2, paragraph 8 after “Council Directive 2009/71/Euratom” insert “(as amended by Council Directive 2014/87/Euratom[^f00166])”.

### 13. The Water Resources (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

The Water Resources (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00167] are amended as follows.

**14.** In regulation 4(b) at the end add “as each last amended by Council [Directive 2013/17/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0017)[^f00168]”.

**15.** In Schedule 3 (Information about Proposed Project), in paragraph 8 after “Council Directive 2009/71/Euratom” insert “(as amended by Council Directive 2014/87/Euratom)[^f00169]”.
