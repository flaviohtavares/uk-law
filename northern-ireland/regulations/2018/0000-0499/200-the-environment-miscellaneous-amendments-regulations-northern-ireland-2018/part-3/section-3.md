# The Environment (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** (1) Schedule 2 of these Regulations amends the Water Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations “the Water Provisions” means—

- (a) the Control of Asbestos in Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00018];
- (b) the Urban Waste Water Treatment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00019];
- (c) the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00020];
- (d) the Groundwater Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00021];
- (e) the Water Supply (Domestic Distribution Systems) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00022];
- (f) the Chemical Analysis of Water Status (Technical Specifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00023];
- (g) the Water Framework Directive (Classification, Priority Substances and Shellfish Waters) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00024];
- (h) the Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00025];
- (i) the Private Water Supplies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00026]; and,
- (j) the Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00027].
