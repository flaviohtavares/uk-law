# The Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 193

Harbours, Docks, Etc.

The Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made

20th November 2018

Coming into operation

14th January 2019

The Department for Infrastructure is a Northern Ireland department designated [^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the environment.

The Department for Infrastructure makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and come into operation on 14thJanuary 2019.

## 2. Revocations

The following Regulations are revoked:–

- (a) the Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003]; and
- (b) the Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 20th November 2018.

(L.S.)

Tom Reid

A senior officer of the Department for Infrastructure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke the Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and the Harbour Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.
