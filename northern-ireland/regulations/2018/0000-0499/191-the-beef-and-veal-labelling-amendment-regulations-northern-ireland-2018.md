# The Beef and Veal Labelling (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 191

Agriculture

The Beef and Veal Labelling (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made

14th November 2018

Coming into operation

17th December 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001]—

- (a) being a Department designated[^f00002], for the purposes of making Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to food (including drink) including the primary production of food; and
- (b) having carried out the consultation required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004],

makes these Regulations in accordance with section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Beef and Veal Labelling (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 17th December 2018.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Beef and Veal Labelling Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

(1) The Beef and Veal Labelling Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00006] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3—

- (a) after “Title II of Regulation 1760/2000”, insert “as last amended by Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00007]”;
- (b) after “Commission Regulation 566/2008”, insert “as last amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 565/2013[^f00008]”.

(3) In regulation 5(1), omit sub-paragraphs (a) (vii) and (viii).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 14th November 2018.

(L.S.)

Colette McMaster

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Beef and Veal Labelling Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 to update legislative references and to reflect provisions in Regulation (EU) No 652/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Regulation [(EC) No 1760/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1760) as regards electronic identification of bovine animals and labelling.

Regulation 2(2) (a) and (b) updates references to EU legislation by amending regulation 3 (The Competent Authority).

Regulation 2(3) removes the requirement for businesses to seek prior approval from the Department before labelling beef and veal with non-compulsory information by amending regulation 5 (Offences).
