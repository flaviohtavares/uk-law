# The Quality of Bathing Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 104

Environmental Protection

The Quality of Bathing Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made

1st May 2018

Coming into operation

31st May 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs being a department designated [^f00001] in relation to the environment for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Quality of Bathing Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 31<sup>st</sup> May 2018.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Amendments to the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

For Schedule 1 to the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00004] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2018.

(L.S.)

John Speers

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs
