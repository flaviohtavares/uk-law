# The Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 90

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made

13th April 2018

Coming into operation on

20th May 2018

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 20 and 56(1) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 20<sup>th</sup> May 2018.

## 2. Amendment of Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland)

(1) The Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) For regulation 9(1) (further application for a taxi licence) substitute—

> **9. Further application for a taxi licence within 21 days of refusal**
>
> (1) Where an applicant is notified in accordance with regulation 7(4), the applicant may make a further application for a taxi licence within 21 days from and including the date of notification.
>
> (1A) Where a further application is made in accordance with paragraph (1) the taxi must be presented for a further inspection no later than 60 days from and including the date of notification on a date and at a time appointed by the Department.

.

(3) In the table in Schedule 2 (items included in inspection)—

- (a) after item 4(5) “rear fog lamps” insert—

  > |  |
  > | --- |
  > | (5A) Front fog lights |
  > | (a) position |
  > | (b) condition and operation |
  > | (c) colour and visual efficiency |

  ; and
- (b) after item 4(11) “end outline marker lamps” insert—

  > |  |
  > | --- |
  > | (12) Reversing lamps |
  > | (13) Daytime running lamps |

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 (“the 2015 Regulations”) to give effect to the requirements of [Directive 2014/45/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0045) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3<sup>rd</sup> April 2014 on the periodic roadworthiness tests for motor vehicles and their trailers.

These Regulations amend regulation 9 (further application for a taxi licence) of the 2015 Regulations to prescribe a limit on the time allowed for retesting following an initial test failure.

They also amend Schedule 2 (item included in inspection) of the 2015 Regulations to include front fog lamps, reversing lamps and daytime running lamps as items to be inspected under regulation 4 (inspection of taxi) of the 2015 Regulations.

An Explanatory Memorandum and Transposition Note have been produced and are available from the Safe and Sustainable Travel Division, Department for Infrastructure, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online alongside these Regulations at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
