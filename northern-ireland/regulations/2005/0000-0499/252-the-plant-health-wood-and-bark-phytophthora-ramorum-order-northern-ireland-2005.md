## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 30th May 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) In this Order – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- “inspector” means any person authorised to be an inspector for the purposes of the principal Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- “land” means brought into Northern Ireland by any means whatsoever; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- “origin” means, in respect of susceptible material, the place where the material is grown or produced; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- “*Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*” refers to the pest *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* Werres, De Cock & Man in t Veld sp. nov; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- “the principal Order” means the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^f00008];
- “susceptible bark” means isolated bark of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Acer macrophyllum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* Pursh., *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Aesculus californica *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* Nutt., *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Lithocarpus densiflorus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* (H & A) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Quercus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* L;
- “susceptible material” means susceptible bark and susceptible wood; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- “susceptible wood” means wood described in the first column of the Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(2) In this Order other expressions have the same meaning as in the principal Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 3. Prohibition against the introduction and spread of (affected by repeal, see note) Phytophthora ramorum (affected by repeal, see note) into and within Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

Subject to Article 7, no person shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- (a) introduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* into Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (b) spread *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* within Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) Subject *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^c-16156aca7ad693951f23cf1e7f38a6c5]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] to Article 7, no person shall import into Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^f00009] susceptible wood originating in the United States of America (“USA”) unless it is accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate issued in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 5. Phytosanitary certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) The provisions of Articles 12(1) to (5), (6) and (7) and 13 of the principal Order shall apply to any phytosanitary certificate required under this Order in respect of any susceptible material as if that material were the wood, isolated bark or other object referred to in Article 12 of the principal Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(2) Where a consignment of susceptible material for which a phytosanitary certificate is required and has been issued under Article 4 has been consigned to, stored, repacked or split up in a third country other than that in which the certificate was issued, the original phytosanitary certificate or a certified copy of it shall accompany the material together with a reforwarding phytosanitary certificate issued by the official plant health service of that third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 6. Phytosanitary certificates issued outside Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

Any phytosanitary certificate issued for the purposes of this Order by or with the authority of an official plant health service of a third country shall be deemed to have been issued in accordance with the relevant requirements of the Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 7. Licences for scientific or research purposes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the provisions of Article 26A of the principal Order shall apply in respect of the importation, movement and keeping of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*, or any susceptible material on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* is present, which would otherwise be prohibited under this Order as if it or the material were a pest the importation, movement, or keeping of which, but for a licence granted under the principal Order, would be prohibited. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) shall affect the application of Article 26A(2)(d) of the principal Order in respect of a licence granted by virtue of this article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 8. Actions which may be required by an inspector (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) If an inspector has reasonable grounds for suspecting that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* is present or likely to be present on any premises, he may, for the purposes of enforcing Article 3(b), by notice in writing served on the occupier or person in charge of those premises or of any susceptible material present on those premises – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- (a) require any of the susceptible material to be treated, destroyed or otherwise disposed of in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (b) prohibit the removal of such susceptible material from premises specified in the notice or impose such other prohibitions as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the spread of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*;
- (c) require the removal of such susceptible material to premises specified in the notice in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be so specified; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (d) require the taking of such other steps, specified in the notice, in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice, as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the spread of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*.

(2) If an inspector has reasonable grounds for believing that it is necessary for the purpose of preventing the spread of or ensuring the eradication of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* from any premises, he may by notice in writing served on the occupier or other person in charge of any other premises impose such prohibitions and require the taking of such reasonable steps, specified in the notice, as appear to him to be necessary for that purpose, such steps to be taken in such manner and in such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(3) For the purposes of carrying out an examination of susceptible material upon its entry to Northern Ireland an inspector may, by notice in writing served on the consignee of any susceptible material which has been or is to be imported into Northern Ireland, specify premises and require the removal of that material to those premises in such manner and within such period as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

## 9. Actions which may be taken by an inspector (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions in Article 8, if an inspector has reasonable grounds for suspecting that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* is present or likely to be present on any premises he may, after giving the occupier or other person in charge of the premises reasonable notice of his intention and upon production if so required of his authority, enter such premises and either on those premises or elsewhere take steps – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- (a) to destroy or treat in some other way any susceptible material found on those premises; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (b) to destroy and to prevent the spread of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* found on those premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(2) An inspector may, on production if so required of his authority, at all reasonable times for the purpose of ascertaining whether *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* exists on any premises, or for any other purpose of this Order, including checking compliance with it, enter any premises to – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- (a) examine, photograph or mark any part of the premises or any susceptible material or other object on the premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (b) take samples of any susceptible material or other object and anything which has been or may have been in contact with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]*; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (c) require production of any documents or records (in whatever form they may be held) relating to the production of or trade in any susceptible material and may examine and copy such documents or records. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(3) For the purposes of the examinations referred to in paragraph (2), an inspector may open any container, bundle or other package, authorise any person to open on his behalf any container, bundle or other package or require the owner or any person in charge of any container, bundle or other package to open it in such manner as the inspector may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(4) An inspector may, so far as it is necessary for the purposes of the examinations referred to in paragraph (2), prohibit entirely or to such extent as he may indicate the movement of any susceptible material, container, bundle, other package or object by means of which in his opinion *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3] *Phytophthora ramorum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]* may spread. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(5) For the purposes of the examinations referred to in paragraph (2), an inspector may require the occupier or other person in charge of the premises in which the examination is taking place to provide adequate lighting and, where appropriate, suitable areas for inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(6) Where any such document or record as is mentioned in paragraph (2)(c) is kept by means of a computer, an inspector may – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

- (a) require access to, and inspect and check the operation of, any computer and any associated apparatus or material which is or has been in use in connection with the documentation or records; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]
- (b) require any person having charge of, or otherwise concerned with the operation of, the computer, apparatus or material, to afford him such assistance as he may reasonably require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

(7) An inspector on entering any premises under paragraphs (1) or (2) may take with him such other persons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3][^c-5f3861877dd63993732f72e510daf0d2]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3], and such equipment and vehicles as are necessary for the purpose of facilitating the exercise of his powers under those paragraphs, and such other persons whether or not accompanied by the inspector, upon production if so required of their authority given in that behalf by an inspector, may remain on the land and from time to time re-enter with such equipment and vehicles as aforesaid, and carry out such work for the purposes aforesaid and in such manner as the inspector may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-851ecb88b07e66165d6b14a2475238f3]

This Order implements in Northern Ireland –

- (a) Commission Decision [2002/757/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0757) of 19th September 2002 (O.J. No. L252, 20.9.2002, p. 37) as amended by Commission Decision [2004/426/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0426) of 29th April 2004 (O.J. No. L189, 27.5.2004, p. 1) on provisional emergency phytosanitary measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Phytophthora ramorum* insofar as they relate to wood and isolated bark but not in relation to plants and trees which are the subject of a separate Order; and
- (b) Commission Decision [2004/278/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0278) (O.J. No. L87, 25.3.2004, p. 31) on the Community position on the amendment of the Appendices to Annex 4 to the Agreement between the European Community and the Swiss Confederation on trade and agricultural products insofar as it relates to wood of *Quercus* L.

The Order prohibits the introduction into and spread within Northern Ireland of the pest *Phytophthora ramorum* (Article 3).

The Order places certain controls on the importation of susceptible wood from the USA, requiring such wood to be accompanied by phytosanitary certificates which may be issued only after specific checks have been carried out during production and before consignment; or alternatively, where the wood originates in a part of the USA recognised by plant health authorities as free from Phytophthora ramorum, confirming that fact (Article 4(1) and the Schedule). Imports of such wood must be notified to an inspector at least three days before landing (Article 4(2)). These requirements do not apply in the case of wood of *Quercus* L. imported from Switzerland (Article 4(3)). The importation of susceptible bark originating in the USA is prohibited (Article 4(4)).

Provisions similar to those in the principal Order apply to the issue of phytosanitary certificates in Northern Ireland (Article 5) and to the effect of phytosanitary certificates issued outside Northern Ireland (Article 6).

There is provision for licenses to be granted under the principal Order for the importation, movement and keeping of *Phytophthora ramorum* for research purposes (Article 7).

Provisions similar to those in the principal Order apply to actions which may be required or which may be taken by inspectors (Articles 8 to 10).

Offences similar to those created in the principal Order are created in respect of the introduction, spread and movement of *Phytophthora ramorum*, compliance with notices served under this Order and licences granted under the principal Order as applied for the purposes of this Order and the obstruction of inspectors exercising their powers under this Order (Article 11).

The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (*Phytophthora ramorum*) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003/175](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/175)) is revoked (Article 12).

No regulatory impact assessment has been undertaken in respect of the measures in this Order.
