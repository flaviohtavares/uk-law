# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

INDUSTRIAL TRIBUNALS (LEVY APPEALS) RULES OF PROCEDURE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*For use only in proceedings on levy appeals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]*

Regulation 12(3)(b) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 1. Application of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3be152594a022a44776763a1ceb46423][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Subject to rules 8 and 9, Schedule 1 shall apply to levy appeals. The rules in this Schedule modify the rules in Schedule 1 in relation to levy appeals. If there is conflict between the rules contained in this Schedule and those in Schedule 1, the rules in this Schedule shall prevail. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 2. Notice of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-393fa5f84a1d5ea10f1f263b9370a431][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

A person wishing to appeal an assessment to a levy (the appellant) shall do so by sending to the Board two copies of a notice of appeal which must be substantially in accordance with Form 1 in the Annex to this Schedule, and they must include *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-90c1df0eb97d2234a3d132c4da4cc70f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds of their appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 3. Action on receipt of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-238f3bad9ccd3e399d0d48f8fc40ff61][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Subject to rules 4 and 5, the Board shall, within 21 days of receiving the notice of appeal send the following documents to the Office of the Tribunals – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) one copy of the notice of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) a copy of the assessment notice and of any notice by the Board allowing further time for appealing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) a notice giving the Board’s address for service under these Rules where that address is different from the address specified in the assessment notice as the address for service of a notice of appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) any representations in writing relating to the appeal that the Board wishes to submit to the tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Failure to comply with any provision of this rule or rule 4 shall not make the appeal invalid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 4. Requests for further information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-84c4f6afb08d48dff160b13015d477e3][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Subject to rule 5, this rule applies when, on receiving the notice of appeal, the Board considers that it requires further information on the appellant’s grounds for the appeal and of any facts relevant to those grounds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The Board shall send the appellant a notice specifying the further information required by the Board within 21 days of receiving the notice of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The appellant shall send the Board two copies of the further information within 21 days of receiving the notice requesting the information, or within such further period as the Board may allow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), within 21 days of receiving the further information the Board shall send the following documents to the Office of the Tribunals – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the documents listed in rule 3(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) a copy of the notice requesting further information; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) any further information which has been provided to the Board; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) any representations in writing regarding such information which the Board wishes to submit to the tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) If further information is not received by the Board within the time limit, the documents listed in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph (4) shall be sent by the Board to the Office of the Tribunals – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) within 50 days of the receipt of the notice of appeal by the Board; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) if the Board has allowed a further period of time for delivery of further particulars under paragraph (3), within 7 days of the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 5. Withdrawal of appeal or assessment (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24e2cc4ad0ce7f26aa37224c5107cd44][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) The appellant may withdraw the notice of appeal by notice given to the Board at any time and in that event no further action shall be taken in relation to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) When an assessment is withdrawn by the Board, it shall notify the Office of the Tribunals and no further action shall be taken in relation to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 6. Entry of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6eed35a537ddee1c6464a73275406244][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The Secretary shall as soon as reasonably practicable after receiving from the Board the relevant documents in accordance with rule 3(1), 4(4) or 4(5) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) enter the following details of the appeal in the Register, namely – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) the case number; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the date the Secretary received the relevant documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the name of the appellant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the fact that the appeal is an appeal by a person assessed to levy imposed under a levy order made under Article 23(2) of the Industrial Training Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) give notice to the appellant and to the Board of the case number of the appeal (which must from then on be referred to in all correspondence relating to the appeal) and of the address to which notices and other communications to the Office of the Tribunals shall be sent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) give notice to the appellant of the Board’s address for service; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) send to the appellant a copy of any representations in writing that the Board has submitted to the tribunal under rule 3 or 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 7. Order for further information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4dc8a7da6bb828e92ada4cca01cd231f][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) In any case in which the appellant has not sent to the Board further information which has been requested by the Board in accordance with rule 4, a chairman or tribunal may, on the application of the Board, by notice order the appellant to supply such further information as may be specified in the notice, and the appellant shall send two copies of such information to the Office of the Tribunals within such time as the chairman or tribunal may order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after receiving the further information from the appellant, the Secretary shall send a copy of the information to the Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) An order made under paragraph (1) shall be treated as an order made under rule 10 of Schedule 1 for the purposes of rule 13 of Schedule 1 (compliance with orders and practice directions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 8. Provisions of Schedule 1 which do not apply to levy appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ae1f84d3cfbd001aed6d328449a0ebe7][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The following rules of Schedule 1 shall not apply in relation to levy appeals: rules 1 to 9, 16(1)(c), 18(2)(c) and (e), 20 to 25, 33, 34(1)(a), 34(2), 34(4), 38(4), 39, 42(4), 43, 47, 49 to 53, 55, and 60(4)(a), (8) and (9). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 9. Modification of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4c4a08b374ef33e8091c5fa1fcb77fa6][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Schedule 1 shall be further modified in relation to levy appeals as follows – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) all references in Schedule 1 to a claim or claimant shall be read as references to a levy appeal or to an appellant in a levy appeal respectively and as the context may require; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) in rule 60 (notices, etc.) after paragraph 4(f) insert: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

  > - (g) in the case of a notice of an appeal brought under the Industrial Training Order, the Board’s address for service specified in the assessment notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  > - (h) in the case of any other document directed to the Board, the Board’s address for service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

  .
