# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 2

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — INDUSTRIAL TRIBUNALS (NATIONAL SECURITY) RULES OF PROCEDURE (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 12(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 1. Application of Schedule 2 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f233e78537a4b74398369bc817bb86cc][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) The rules in this Schedule only apply to national security proceedings or proceedings where a power under rule 54(3) of Schedule 1 has been exercised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The rules in this Schedule modify the rules in Schedule 1 in relation to such proceedings. If there is conflict between the rules contained in this Schedule and those in any other Schedule, the rules in this Schedule shall prevail. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) Any reference in this Schedule to rule 54 is a reference to rule 54 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 2. Notification of national security proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d11a013ecf5ee2537c0fbf8846fec939][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

When proceedings before an industrial tribunal become national security proceedings the Secretary shall inform the parties of that fact in writing as soon as practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 3. Responding to a claim (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dfafc73f919be01424c4f6438bbcbb65][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) If before the expiry of the period for entering the response – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) a direction of the Secretary of State under rule 54(1)(b) (exclusion of claimant) applicable to this stage of the proceedings is given; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the Secretary of State has informed the Secretary in accordance with rule 54(3) that he wishes to address the tribunal or chairman with a view to the tribunal or chairman making an order under rule 54(2) applicable to this stage of the proceedings to exclude the claimant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

rule 4(4)(d) (grounds for the response) of Schedule 1 shall not apply and paragraphs (2) and (3) shall apply instead. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) In a case falling within paragraph (1)(b), if the tribunal or chairman decides not to make an order under rule 54(2), the respondent shall within 28 days of the decision present to the Office of the Tribunals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-bc63116c56e38812c3105a994ceea078] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the written grounds on which he resists the claim. On receiving *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the written grounds the Secretary shall send a copy of them to all other parties and they shall be treated as part of the response. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) In a case falling within paragraph (1)(b) where the tribunal or chairman makes the order, or in a case falling within paragraph (1)(a), the respondent shall within 44 days of the direction or order being made, present to the Office of the Tribunals (and, where applicable, to the special advocate) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the written grounds on which he resists the claim and they shall be treated as part of the response. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) The time limits in paragraphs (2) and (3) may be extended if it is just and equitable to do so and if an application is presented to the Office of the Tribunals before the expiry of the relevant time limit. The application must explain why the respondent cannot comply with the time limit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 4. Serving of documents by the Secretary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0a7850a13a98a4ab6ab634e0bdd4300d][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) The Secretary shall not send a copy of the response or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of the[^c-cbfd16aeee36f93d69222c3acb70fb06] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] grounds for the response to any person excluded from all or part of the proceedings by virtue of a direction or order given or made under rule 54. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Where the Secretary of State has informed the Secretary in accordance with rule 54(3) that he wishes to address the tribunal or chairman with a view to an order being made under rule 54(2)(a) to exclude the claimant’s representative from all or part of the proceedings, the Secretary shall not at any time before the tribunal or chairman has considered the Secretary of State’s representations, send a copy of the response or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-ae408468fdcd58c594915706201f5b53] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the response to any person who may be excluded from all or part of the proceedings by such an order if it were made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 5. Default judgement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-759dfabed4fac72cfdb142c3fb4ba232][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Rule 8(1) (default judgements) of Schedule 1 shall apply in relation to the time limit for presenting a response, but it shall not apply in relation to the time limits in paragraphs (2) and (3) of rule 3 in this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 6. Witness orders and discovery of documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c1c527d7b95a0277692fa4df324aaf18][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the Secretary of State has issued a direction or the tribunal or a chairman has made an order under rule 54 to exclude a claimant or his representative from all or part of the proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) a chairman or the tribunal is considering whether to make, or has made, an order described in rule 10(2)(c) or (d) of Schedule 1 (requiring a person to attend and give evidence or to produce documents) or under rule 7 of Schedule 4 or rule 6 of Schedule 5, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

the Secretary of State (whether or not he is a party to the proceedings) may make an application to the tribunal or chairman objecting to the imposition of a requirement described in rule 10(2)(c) or (d) of Schedule 1 or under Schedule 4 or 5. If such an order has been made the Secretary of State may make an application to vary or set aside the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The tribunal or chairman shall hear and determine the Secretary of State’s application in private and the Secretary of State shall be entitled to address the tribunal or chairman. The application shall be made by notice to the Secretary and the Secretary shall give notice of the application to all parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 7. Case management discussions and pre-hearing reviews (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ca59f2c15c27611d8c6069085e800077][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Rule 14(4) (hearings – general) of Schedule 1 shall be modified in accordance with paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) In proceedings in which a special advocate has been appointed in respect of the claimant, at case management discussions and pre-hearing reviews the claimant shall not have the right to advance oral argument, but oral argument may be advanced on the claimant’s behalf by the special advocate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 8. Special advocate (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-88cd1e0c54d3693b47ca494bbbe3a313][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) In any proceedings in which there is an excluded person the tribunal or chairman shall inform the Attorney General for Northern Ireland of the proceedings before it or him with a view to the Attorney General for Northern Ireland appointing, if he thinks it fit to do so, a special advocate to represent the interests of the claimant in respect of those parts of the proceedings from which – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) any representative of the claimant is excluded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) both the claimant and his representative are excluded; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the claimant is excluded, where he does not have a representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) A special advocate shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) have a general qualification within the meaning of section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) be an advocate or solicitor admitted in Scotland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) be a member of the Bar of Northern Ireland or a solicitor of the Supreme Court of Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) Where the excluded person is the claimant, he shall be permitted to make a statement to the tribunal or chairman before the commencement of the proceedings, or the part of the proceedings, from which he is excluded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) Except in accordance with paragraphs (5) to (7), the special advocate may not communicate directly or indirectly with any person (including an excluded person) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) (except in the case of the tribunal, chairman and the respondent) on any matter contained in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-f090adff67fac067b70518f85de4d4ce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the response referred to in rule 3(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) (except in the case of a person who was present) on any matter discussed or referred to during any part of the proceedings in which the tribunal or chairman sat in private in accordance with a direction or an order given or made under rule 54. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) The special advocate may apply for orders from the tribunal or chairman authorising him to seek instructions from, or otherwise to communicate with, an excluded person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) on any matter contained in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-6efe50fd25f768f88720fdf597ca3952] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the response referred to in rule 3(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) on any matter discussed or referred to during any part of the proceedings in which the tribunal or chairman sat in private in accordance with a direction or an order given or made under rule 54. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) An application under paragraph (5) shall be made in writing to the Office of the Tribunals and shall include the title of the proceedings and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] details of[^c-b35edd2a84880220a0dd5f34ecd6e107] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(7) The Secretary shall notify the Secretary of State of an application under paragraph (5) and the Secretary of State shall be entitled to address the tribunal or chairman on the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(8) In these Rules and those in Schedule 1, in any case in which a special advocate has been appointed to represent the interests of the claimant in accordance with paragraph (1), any reference to a party shall (save in those references specified in paragraph (9)) include the special advocate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(9) The following references to “party” or “parties” shall not include the special advocate – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) regulation 5(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) in Schedule 1, rules 2(2)(b), 9, 10(2)(r), 10(3), all references in rule 11(4) save that in rule 11(4)(c), 11(5), 18(7), 20, 22, 23, 27(3), 27(5), 29(3), 30(6)(f), 33(1), 34(2), all references in rule 38 save that in rule 38(10), 40, 41, all references in rule 42 save that in rule 42(8), 44 to 48, 51, 54(1), the first reference in rule 54(3), the first reference in rule 56(2), 60(3), (4)(a) and (b), (8); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) in Schedule 5, rules 4(b), 5(5) and 9; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) in Schedule 6, rule 3(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 9. Hearings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-adadab726ebdc15e9dbdbbce97933e28][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Any hearing of or in connection with a claim shall, subject to any direction of the Secretary of State or order of a tribunal or chairman under rule 54 that all or part of the proceedings are to take place in private and subject to rule 16 of Schedule 1, take place in public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Subject to any direction of the Secretary of State or order of a tribunal or chairman under rule 54, a party shall be entitled to give evidence, to call witnesses, to cross-examine any witnesses called by the other party and to address the tribunal at a hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 10. Reasons in national security proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e625bbaddf21eb6ba1f2fbd6423cde1b][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) This rule applies to written reasons given under rule 30 of Schedule 1 for a decision or order made by the tribunal or chairman in national security proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Before the Secretary sends a copy of the written reasons (“the full written reasons”) to any party, or enters them in the Register under rule 32 of Schedule 1, he shall send a copy of the full written reasons to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) If the Secretary of State considers it expedient in the interests of national security and he has given a direction or the tribunal or a chairman has made an order under rule 54 in those proceedings, the Secretary of State may – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) direct the tribunal or chairman that the full written reasons shall not be disclosed to persons specified in the direction, and to prepare a further document (“the edited reasons”) setting out the reasons for the decision or order, but with the omission of such of the information as is specified in the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) direct the tribunal or chairman that the full written reasons shall not be disclosed to persons specified in the direction, but that no further document setting out the tribunal or chairman’s reasons should be prepared. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) Where the Secretary of State has directed the tribunal or chairman in accordance with paragraph (3)(a), the edited reasons shall be signed by the chairman and initialled in each place where an omission has been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) Where a direction has been made under paragraph (3)(a), the Secretary shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) send a copy of the edited reasons referred to in paragraph (3)(a) to any person specified in the direction and to the persons listed in paragraph (7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) enter the edited reasons in the Register, but omit from the Register the full written reasons; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) send a copy of the full written reasons to the persons listed in paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) Where a direction has been made under paragraph (3)(b), the Secretary shall send a copy of the full written reasons to the persons listed in paragraph (7), but he shall not enter the full written reasons in the Register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(7) The persons to whom full written reasons should be sent in accordance with paragraph (5) or (6) are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the respondent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the claimant or the claimant’s representative if they were not specified in the direction made under paragraph (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) if applicable, the special advocate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) where the proceedings were referred to the tribunal by a court, to that court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) where there are proceedings before a superior court relating to the decision in question, to that court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 11. Correction of written reasons (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9774fd00c60364e1bc5d3aa65a454086][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Where written reasons (whether “full” or “edited”) have been omitted from the Register in accordance with rule 10 and they are corrected by certificate under rule 37 of Schedule 1, the Secretary shall send a copy of the corrected reasons to the same persons who had been sent the reasons in accordance with rule 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 12. Review of decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b6afcb5abd8a202ec15ce1a9caef95fa][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

In rule 34(3) of Schedule 1 (review of other decisions), the reference in sub-paragraph (c) to decisions being made in the absence of a party does not include reference to decisions being made in the absence of a party where this is done in accordance with a direction given or an order made under rule 54. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
