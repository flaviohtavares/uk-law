# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f17aa41a5e945ac61e816e50afec7e1b][^key-997488e5b0caac346e4f109d221c8079][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

INDUSTRIAL TRIBUNALS (HEALTH AND SAFETY – APPEALS AGAINST IMPROVEMENT AND PROHIBITION NOTICES) RULES OF PROCEDURE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*For use only in proceedings on an appeal against an improvement or prohibition notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]*

Regulation 12(3)(c) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 1. Application of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2b112ebbb40d13fc8ed550e2fb65c2fa][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Subject to rules 10 and 11, Schedule 1 shall apply to appeals against an improvement notice or a prohibition notice. The rules in this Schedule modify the rules in Schedule 1 in relation to such appeals. If there is conflict between the rules contained in this Schedule and those in Schedule 1, the rules in this Schedule shall prevail. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 2. Notice of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9951a80342e53a361eed918f45d28c89][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

A person wishing to appeal an improvement notice or a prohibition notice (the appellant) shall do so by sending to the Office of the Tribunals a notice of appeal which must include the following – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the name and address of the appellant and, if different, an address to which he requires notices and documents relating to the appeal to be sent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the date of the improvement notice or prohibition notice appealed against and the address of the premises or the place concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the name and address of the respondent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) details of the requirements or directions which are being appealed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) details of[^key-db95e97ec888890d2659284a7b5b6298] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 3. Time limit for bringing appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-78d3531e53f3dfe79f0d5298f10bb8ab][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the notice of appeal must be sent to the Office of the Tribunals within 21 days from the date of the service on the appellant of the notice appealed against. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) A tribunal may extend the time mentioned above where it is satisfied, on an application made in writing to the Secretary either before or after the expiration of that time, that it is or was not reasonably practicable for an appeal to be brought within that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 4. Action on receipt of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-925a01f00c318c5f3db6e0de6df56056][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

On receiving the notice of appeal the Secretary shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) enter the following details of the appeal in the Register, namely – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) the case number; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the date the Secretary received the notice of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the name of the appellant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the name of the respondent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (v) the fact that the appeal is an appeal against an improvement or prohibition notice, as the case may be, under Article 26 of the Health and Safety Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00068],
- (b) send a copy of the notice of appeal to the respondent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) inform the parties in writing of the case number of the appeal (which must from then on be referred to in all correspondence relating to the appeal) and of the address to which notices and other communications to the Office of the Tribunals shall be sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 5. Application for a direction suspending the operation of a prohibition notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f8b0c5d37539184e13e2431362c5cbe0][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) When an appeal is brought against a prohibition notice, an application may be made by the appellant under Article 26(2)(b) of the Health and Safety Order for a direction suspending the operation of the prohibition notice until the appeal is determined or withdrawn. The application must be presented to the Office of the Tribunals in writing and shall include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the case number of the appeal, or if there is no case number sufficient details to identify the appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) details of[^key-7c7c347fd3fc0012cd86ad3f49f3e4d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds on which the application is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Upon receiving the application, the Secretary shall, as soon as is practicable after it has been received – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) enter the fact of the application against the entry in the Register relating to the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) send a copy of the application to the respondent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) inform the respondent that he has the opportunity to submit representations in writing if he so wishes, but within a specified period of not less than 7 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The chairman shall consider the application and any representations submitted by the respondent, and may – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) order that the operation of the prohibition notice be suspended until the appeal is determined or withdrawn; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) dismiss the appellant’s application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) order that the application be determined at a hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) The chairman must give reasons for any decision made under paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) A decision made under paragraph (3) shall be treated as a decision which may be reviewed upon the application of a party under rule 34 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 6. General power to manage proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-633b470c7bc10ae5fc4ca69c53905554][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) The chairman may at any time on the application of a party, make an order in relation to any matter which appears to him to be appropriate. Such orders may be those listed in rule 10(2) of Schedule 1 (subject to rule 10 below) or such other orders as he thinks fit. Subject to the case management rules in Schedule 1, orders may be issued as a result of a chairman considering the papers before him in the absence of the parties, or at a hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) If the parties agree in writing upon the terms of any order to be made by the tribunal or chairman, the chairman may, if he thinks fit, order accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 7. Appointment of an assessor (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5906f8c7e1e238be303da09f4bb39f5e][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The President or the Vice-President may, if he thinks fit, appoint in accordance with Article 26(3) of the Health and Safety Order a person having special knowledge or experience in relation to the subject matter of the appeal to sit with the tribunal or chairman as an assessor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 8. Right to withdraw proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2a94b45c0647f61fa5ede0bda5cbfd55][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) An appellant may withdraw all or part of the appeal at any time. This may be done either orally at a hearing or in writing in accordance with paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) To withdraw an appeal or part of one in writing the appellant must inform the Office of the Tribunals in writing of the appeal or the parts of it which are to be withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The Secretary shall inform all other parties of the withdrawal. Withdrawal takes effect on the date on which the Office of the Tribunals (in the case of written notifications) or the tribunal or chairman receives notice of it and where the whole appeal is withdrawn proceedings are brought to an end against the respondent on that date and the tribunal or chairman shall dismiss the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] Where part of the appeal is withdrawn, the relevant part of the proceedings so withdrawn is brought to an end against the respondent on that date and the tribunal or chairman shall dismiss that part of the proceedings.[^key-8b8995ee5d2e2f833e4650a814c9d3fc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 9. Costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b148366d04ad17f19bea9d1a784b1eea][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) A tribunal or chairman may make an order (“a costs order”) that a party (“the paying party”) make a payment in respect of the costs incurred by another party (“the receiving party”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) “costs” shall mean fees, charges or disbursements incurred by or on behalf of a party in relation to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The amount of a costs order against the paying party can be determined in the following ways – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the tribunal may specify the sum which the paying party must pay to the receiving party, provided that sum does not exceed £10,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the parties may agree on a sum to be paid by the paying party to the receiving party and if they do so the costs order shall be for the sum so agreed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the tribunal may order the paying party to pay the receiving party the whole or a specified part of the costs of the receiving party with the amount to be paid being determined by way of detailed assessment in a county court in accordance with such of the scales prescribed by county court rules for proceedings in the county court as shall be directed by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) The tribunal or chairman may have regard to the paying party’s ability to pay when considering whether it or he shall make a costs order or how much that order should be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) For the avoidance of doubt, the amount of a costs order made under either paragraph (3)(b) or (c) may exceed £10,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 10. Provisions of Schedule 1 which do not apply to appeals against improvement notices or prohibition notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d6321487c4567e56c6fa58a59636b649][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The following rules in Schedule 1 shall not apply in relation to appeals against improvement and prohibition notices: rules 1 to 9, 10(1), 10(2)(g), (i), (k), (l) and (r), 12, 13, 16(1)(c), 18(2)(c) and (e), 18(8), 20 to 25, 29(3), 33, 34(1)(a), 34(2), 38 to 47, 49 to 53, 55, and 60(4)(a), (8) and (9). All references in Schedule 1 to the rules listed in this rule shall have no effect in relation to an appeal against an improvement notice or a prohibition notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 11. Modification of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b0bc46ec97342d77366a66eb862a046b][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Schedule 1 shall be further modified so that all references in Schedule 1 to a claim shall be read as references to a notice of appeal or to an appeal against an improvement notice or a prohibition notice, as the context may require, and all references to the claimant shall be read as references to the appellant in such an appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
