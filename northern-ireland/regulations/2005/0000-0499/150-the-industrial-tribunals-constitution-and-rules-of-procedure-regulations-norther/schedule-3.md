## SCHEDULE 3 (affected by repeal, see note) — INDUSTRIAL TRIBUNALS (EQUAL VALUE) RULES OF PROCEDURE (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 1. General (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fd966d79e62831a570c2050ad915d12b][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The rules in this Schedule shall only apply in proceedings involving an equal value claim and they modify and supplement the rules in Schedule 1. If there is conflict between Schedule 1 and this Schedule, the provisions of this Schedule shall prevail. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 2. General power to manage proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a9ab13e0720f96a520acee59a13617da][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) In addition to the power to make orders described in rule 10 of Schedule 1, the tribunal or chairman shall have power (subject to rules 3(3) and 6(4)) to make the following orders – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the standard orders set out in rules 4 or 7, with such addition to, omission or variation of those orders (including specifically variations as to the periods within which actions are to be taken by the parties) as the chairman or tribunal considers is appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) that no new facts shall be admitted in evidence by the tribunal unless they have been disclosed to all other parties in writing before a date specified by the tribunal (unless it was not reasonably practicable for a party to have done so); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) that the parties may be required to send copies of documents or provide information to the other parties and to the independent expert; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) that the respondent is required to grant the independent expert access to his premises during a period specified by the tribunal or chairman in order for the independent expert to conduct interviews with persons identified as relevant by the independent expert; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) when more than one expert is to give evidence in the proceedings, that those experts present to the tribunal a joint statement of matters which are agreed between them and those matters on which they disagree; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (f) where proceedings have been joined, that lead claimants be identified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Any reference in Schedule 1 or 2 to an order made under rule 10 of Schedule 1 shall include reference to an order made in accordance with this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 3. Conduct of stage 1 equal value hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f7c4ec09d5aa17a73d4d4c5f3e718292][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) When in an equal value claim there is a dispute as to whether any work is of equal value as mentioned in section 1(2)(c) of the Equal Pay Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00065], the tribunal shall conduct a “stage 1 equal value hearing” in accordance with both this rule and the rules applicable to pre-hearing reviews in Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Notwithstanding rule 18(1) and (3) of Schedule 1, a stage 1 equal value hearing shall be conducted by a tribunal composed in accordance with Article 6(1) of the Industrial Tribunals Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) At the stage 1 equal value hearing the tribunal shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) where section 2A(2A) of the Equal Pay Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00066] applies, strike out the claim (or the relevant part of it) if, in accordance with section 2A(2A) of that Act, the tribunal must determine that the work of the claimant and the comparator are not of equal value; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) decide, in accordance with section 2A(1) of the Equal Pay Act, either that – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) the tribunal shall determine the question; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] it shall require a member of the panel of independent experts to prepare a report with respect to the question, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) subject to rule 4 and with regard to the indicative timetable, make the standard orders for the stage 1 equal value hearing as set out in rule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) if the tribunal has decided to require an independent expert to prepare a report on the question, require the parties to copy to the independent expert all information which they are required by an order to disclose or agree between each other; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) if the tribunal has decided to require an independent expert to prepare a report on the question, fix a date for the stage 2 equal value hearing, having regard to the indicative timetable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (f) if the tribunal has not decided to require an independent expert to prepare a report on the question, fix a date for the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1, having regard to the indicative timetable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (g) consider whether any further orders are appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) Before a claim or part of one is struck out under paragraph (3)(a), the Secretary shall send notice to the claimant giving him the opportunity to make representations to the tribunal as to whether the evaluation contained in the study in question falls within paragraph (a) or (b) of section 2A(2A) of the Equal Pay Act. The Secretary shall not be required to send a notice under this paragraph if the claimant has been given an opportunity to make such representations orally to the tribunal as to why such a decision should not be issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) The tribunal may, on the application of a party, hear evidence upon and permit the parties to address it upon the issue contained in section 1(3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00067] of the Equal Pay Act (defence of a genuine material factor) before determining whether to require an independent expert to prepare a report under paragraph (3)(b)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) When the Secretary gives notice to the parties of the stage 1 equal value hearing under rule 14(4) of Schedule 1, he shall also give the parties notice of the matters which the tribunal shall and may consider at that hearing which are described in paragraphs (3) and (5) and he shall give the parties notice of the standard orders in rule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(7) The tribunal’s power to strike out the claim or part of it under paragraph (3)(a) is in addition to powers to strike out a claim under rule 18(7) of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 4. Standard orders for stage 1 equal value hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2ffe268718f17d14d86d08b92a54774][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) At a stage 1 equal value hearing a tribunal shall, unless it considers it inappropriate to do so and subject to paragraph (2), order that – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) before the end of the period of 14 days after the date of the stage 1 equal value hearing the claimant shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) disclose in writing to the respondent the name of any comparator, or, if the claimant is not able to name the comparator he shall instead disclose such information as enables the comparator to be identified by the respondent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] identify to the respondent in writing the period in relation to which he considers that the claimant’s work and that of the comparator are to be compared; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) before the end of the period of 28 days after the date of the stage 1 equal value hearing – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) where the claimant has not disclosed the name of the comparator to the respondent under sub-paragraph (a), if the respondent has been provided with sufficient detail to be able to identify the comparator, he shall disclose in writing the name of the comparator to the claimant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the parties shall provide each other with written job descriptions for the claimant and any comparator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the parties shall identify to each other in writing the facts which they consider to be relevant to the question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the respondent is required to grant access to the claimant and his representative (if any) to his premises during a period specified by the tribunal or chairman in order for him or them to interview any comparator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) the parties shall before the end of the period of 56 days after the date of the stage 1 equal value hearing present to the tribunal a joint agreed statement in writing of the following matters – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) job descriptions for the claimant and any comparator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] facts which both parties consider are relevant to the question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] facts on which the parties disagree (as to the fact or as to the relevance to the question) and a summary of their reasons for disagreeing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) the parties shall, at least 56 days prior to the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1, disclose to each other, to any independent or other expert and to the tribunal written statements of any facts on which they intend to rely in evidence at that hearing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (f) the parties shall, at least 28 days prior to the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1, present to the tribunal a statement of facts and issues on which the parties are in agreement, a statement of facts and issues on which the parties disagree and a summary of their reasons for disagreeing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Any of the standard orders for the stage 1 equal value hearing may be added to, varied or omitted as the tribunal considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 5. Involvement of independent expert in fact finding (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-41b77250a3ffd40e75917816be3504b9][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) This rule applies only to proceedings in relation to which the tribunal has decided to require an independent expert to prepare a report on the question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) In proceedings to which this rule applies a tribunal or chairman may if it or he considers it appropriate at any stage of the proceedings order an independent expert to assist the tribunal in establishing the facts on which the independent expert may rely in preparing his report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) Examples of the circumstances in which the tribunal or chairman may make an order described in paragraph (2) may include – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) a party not being legally represented; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the parties are unable to reach agreement as required by an order of the tribunal or chairman; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the tribunal or chairman considers that insufficient information may have been disclosed by a party and this may impair the ability of the independent expert to prepare a report on the question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) the tribunal or chairman considers that the involvement of the independent expert may promote fuller compliance with orders made by the tribunal or a chairman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) A party to proceedings to which this rule applies may make an application under rule 11 of Schedule 1 for an order under paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 6. Conduct of stage 2 equal value hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5ed26dbf8be204a9cc5924b9fc71d7e6][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) This rule applies only to proceedings in relation to which the tribunal has decided to require an independent expert to prepare a report on the question. In such proceedings the tribunal shall conduct a “stage 2 equal value hearing” in accordance with both this rule and the rules applicable to pre-hearing reviews in Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Notwithstanding rule 18(1) and (3) of Schedule 1, a stage 2 equal value hearing shall be conducted by a tribunal composed in accordance with Article 6(1) of the Industrial Tribunals Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) At the stage 2 equal value hearing the tribunal shall make a determination of facts on which the parties cannot agree which relate to the question and shall require the independent expert to prepare his report on the basis of facts which have (at any stage of the proceedings) either been agreed between the parties or determined by the tribunal (referred to as “the facts relating to the question”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) At the stage 2 equal value hearing the tribunal shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) subject to rule 7 and having regard to the indicative timetable, make the standard orders for the stage 2 equal value hearing as set out in rule 7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) make any orders which it considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) fix a date for the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1, having regard to the indicative timetable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), the facts relating to the question shall, in relation to the question, be the only facts on which the tribunal shall rely at the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) At any stage of the proceedings the independent expert may make an application to the tribunal for some or all of the facts relating to the question to be amended, supplemented or omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(7) When the Secretary gives notice to the parties and to the independent expert of the stage 2 equal value hearing under rule 14(4) of Schedule 1, he shall also give the parties notice of the standard orders in rule 7 and draw the attention of the parties to paragraphs (4) and (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 7. Standard orders for stage 2 equal value hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e8437a45f12a640166eb545cad071ebe][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 8. The hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-102a1d3f8f6cdc29cd08ded9603cdf5d][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) In proceedings in relation to which an independent expert has prepared a report, unless the tribunal determines that the report is not based on the facts relating to the question, the report of the independent expert shall be admitted in evidence in those proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) If the tribunal does not admit the report of an independent expert in accordance with paragraph (1), it may determine the question itself or require another independent expert to prepare a report on the question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The tribunal may refuse to admit evidence of facts or hear argument as to issues which have not been disclosed to the other party as required by the rules in this Schedule and in Schedule 1 or any order made under them, unless it was not reasonably practicable for the party to have so complied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 9. Duties and powers of the independent expert (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8026011161b9bf8b64cb31b196489a24][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) When a tribunal requires an independent expert to prepare a report with respect to the question or an order is made under rule 5(2), the Secretary shall inform that independent expert of the duties and powers he has under this rule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) When an independent expert has been required to prepare a report in proceedings the Secretary shall give the independent expert notice of all hearings, orders or decisions in those proceedings as if the independent expert were a party to those proceedings and when these Rules require a party to provide information to another party, such information shall also be provided to the independent expert. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 10. Use of expert evidence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c5ff6fc0429d879db17d7b7eac80a5b3][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Expert evidence shall be restricted to that which, in the opinion of the tribunal, is reasonably required to resolve the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) An expert shall have a duty to assist the tribunal on matters within his expertise. This duty overrides any obligation to the person from whom he has received instructions or by whom he is paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) No party may call an expert or put in evidence an expert’s report without the permission of the tribunal. No expert report shall be put in evidence unless it has been disclosed to all other parties and any independent expert at least 28 days prior to the hearing under rule 26 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) In proceedings in which an independent expert has been required to prepare a report on the question, the tribunal shall not admit evidence of another expert on the question unless such evidence is based on the facts relating to the question. Unless the tribunal considers it inappropriate to do so, any such expert report shall be disclosed to all parties and to the tribunal on the same date on which the independent expert is required to send his report to the parties and to the tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) If an expert (other than an independent expert) does not comply with these Rules or an order made by the tribunal or a chairman, the tribunal may order that the evidence of that expert shall not be admitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) Where two or more parties wish to submit expert evidence on a particular issue, the tribunal may order that the evidence on that issue is to be given by one joint expert only. When such an order has been made, if the parties wishing to instruct the joint expert cannot agree who should be the expert, the tribunal may select the expert. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 11. Written questions to experts (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3586ee71bdfb5d3823dae0d336d5778d][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) Where any expert (including an independent expert) has prepared a report, a party or any other expert (including an independent expert) involved in the proceedings may put written questions about the report to the expert who has prepared the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Unless the tribunal or chairman agrees otherwise, written questions under paragraph (1) – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) Where a party has put a written question in accordance with this rule to an expert instructed by another party and the expert does not answer that question, or does not do so within 28 days, the tribunal may order that the party instructing the expert may not rely on the evidence of that expert. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 12. Procedural matters (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-471e0887b4983ac500a4763d999d10d4][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) For the avoidance of doubt, any requirement in this Schedule to hold a stage 1 or a stage 2 equal value hearing does not preclude holding more than one of each of those types of hearing or other hearings from being held in accordance with Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) Any power conferred on a chairman in Schedule 1 may (subject to the provisions of this Schedule) be carried out by a tribunal or a chairman in relation to proceedings to which this Schedule applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 13. National security proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-be7c7c21d8fbc4f71690409a89f1d436][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(1) In equal value cases which are also national security proceedings, if a tribunal has required an independent expert to prepare a report on the question, the independent expert shall send a copy of the report to the tribunal and shall not send it to the parties. In such proceedings if written questions have been put to the independent expert under rule 11, the independent expert shall send any answers to those questions to the tribunal and not to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Before the Secretary sends to the parties a copy of a report or answers which have been sent to him by the independent expert under paragraph (1), he shall follow the procedure set out in rule 10 of Schedule 2 as if that rule referred to the independent expert’s report or answers (as the case may be) instead of written reasons, except that the independent expert’s report or answers shall not be entered on the Register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) If the Secretary of State does not give a direction under rule 10(3) of Schedule 2 within the period of 28 days from the date on which the Secretary of State was sent the report or answers to written questions the Secretary shall send a copy of the independent expert’s report or answers to written questions (as the case may be) to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
