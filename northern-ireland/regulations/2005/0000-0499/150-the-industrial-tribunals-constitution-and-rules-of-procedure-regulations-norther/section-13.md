# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Register (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-44bd2fa638446ebc532762a188a5bc2b][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Secretary shall maintain a Register which shall be open to inspection by any person without charge at all reasonable hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The Register shall contain – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) details of all claims in accordance with rule 2(2)(g) of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) details of appeals in accordance with rule 6(a) of Schedule 4, rule 4(a) of Schedule 5 and rule 3(a) of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the fact of applications in accordance with rule 5 of Schedule 5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) a copy of all decisions and any written reasons issued by any tribunal or chairman which are required to be entered in the Register in accordance with the rules in Schedules 1 to 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The Register, or any part of it, may be kept by means of a computer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
