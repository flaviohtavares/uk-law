# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

INDUSTRIAL TRIBUNALS (NON-DISCRIMINATION NOTICES APPEALS) RULES OF PROCEDURE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*For use only in proceedings on an appeal against a non-discrimination notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]*

Regulation 12(3)(d) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 1. Application of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-23a4b4dda0a1c065cc14be2ea0edc4c0][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Subject to rules 4 and 5, Schedule 1 shall apply to appeals against a non-discrimination notice. The rules in this Schedule modify the rules in Schedule 1 in relation to such appeals. If there is conflict between the rules contained in this Schedule and those in Schedule 1, the rules in this Schedule shall prevail. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 2. Notice of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9fe6bb36a6671035615a67e6c16f7535][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

A person wishing to appeal a non-discrimination notice (the appellant) shall do so by sending to the Office of the Tribunals a notice of appeal which must be in writing and must include the following – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the name and address of the appellant and, if different, an address to which he requires notices and documents relating to the appeal to be sent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) the date of the non-discrimination notice appealed against; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) the name and address of the respondent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) details of the requirements which are being appealed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (e) details of[^c-7fab9a3945093be1374dcd15ef3f4220] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the grounds for the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 3. Action on receipt of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-52f03f688fa0a0a66afaa6a507cec62b][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

On receiving the notice of appeal the Secretary shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) enter the following details in the Register, namely – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) the case number; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the date the Secretary received the notice of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the name of the appellant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] the name of the respondent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (v) the fact that the appeal is an appeal against a non-discrimination notice under Article 68(1)(a) of the Sex Discrimination Order, Article 56(1)(a) of the Race Relations Order or paragraph 10(1) and (2)(a) of Schedule 1 to the Disability Order, as the case may be, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) send a copy of the notice of appeal to the respondent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) inform the parties in writing of the case number of the appeal (which must from then on be referred to in all correspondence relating to the appeal) and of the address to which notices and other communications to the Office of the Tribunals shall be sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 4. Provisions of Schedule 1 which do not apply to appeals against non-discrimination notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b721cda93aee41d337059b634f35112f][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

The following rules in Schedule 1 shall not apply in relation to appeals against a non-discrimination notice: rules 1 to 9, 16(1)(c), 18(2)(c) and (e), 20 to 24, 33, 34(1)(a), 34(2), 34(4), 38(4), 39, 42(4), 43, 47, 49 to 53, 55, and 60(4)(a), (8) and (9). All references in Schedule 1 to the rules listed in this rule shall have no effect in relation to an appeal against a non-discrimination notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

### 5. Modification of Schedule 1 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a85f15bfc234cef466e068afef575032][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Schedule 1 shall be further modified so that all references in Schedule 1 to a claim shall be read as references to a notice of appeal or to an appeal against a non-discrimination notice, as the context may require, and all references to the claimant shall be read as references to the appellant in such an appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
