# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Proof of decisions of tribunals (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-11c227c83f510ee8ba38cbe5f00dd885][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

The production in any proceedings in any court of a document purporting to be certified by the Secretary to be a true copy of an entry of a decision in the Register shall, unless the contrary is proved, be sufficient evidence of the document and of the facts stated therein. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
