# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Panels of members of tribunals – national security proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9faf69b19c943e39a5bb9c5dfda89190][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

In relation to national security proceedings, the President shall – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) select a panel of persons from the panel of chairmen to act as chairmen in such cases; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) select – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) a panel of persons from the panel referred to in regulation 4(1)(b) as persons suitable to act as members in such cases; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] a panel of persons from the panel referred to in regulation 4(1)(c) as persons suitable to act as members in such cases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
