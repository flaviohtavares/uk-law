# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Composition of tribunals – national security proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b7f233bf656fed7ac0d1218e3b918c56][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

In relation to national security proceedings – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the President or the Vice-President shall select a chairman, who shall be the President, the Vice-President or a member of the panel selected in accordance with regulation 6(a), and the President or the Vice-President may select himself; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) in any such proceedings which are to be determined by a tribunal comprising a chairman and two other members, the President or the Vice-President shall select one of those other members from the panel selected in accordance with regulation 6(b)(i) and the other from the panel selected in accordance with regulation 6(b)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
