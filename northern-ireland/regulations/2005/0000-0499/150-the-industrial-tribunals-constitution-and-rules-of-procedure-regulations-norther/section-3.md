# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Overriding objective (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-97a5a274c9fcb277f8eb239d431eeb94][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The overriding objective of these Regulations and the rules in Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 is to enable tribunals and chairmen to deal with cases justly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) Dealing with a case justly includes, so far as practicable – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) ensuring that the parties are on an equal footing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) dealing with the case in ways which are proportionate to the complexity or importance of the issues; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) ensuring that it is dealt with expeditiously and fairly; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (d) saving expense. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) A tribunal or chairman shall seek to give effect to the overriding objective when it or he – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) exercises any power given to it or him by these Regulations or the rules in Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) interprets these Regulations or any rule in Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) The parties shall assist the tribunal or the chairman to further the overriding objective. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
