# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-01565628742fb6d4f80b862740678a0e][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and the Rules of Procedure contained in Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 to these Regulations may be referred to, respectively as – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) the Industrial Tribunals Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005[^c-25ac6fddf61e0c52a3dbf67ec318b87b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] ;
- (b) the Industrial Tribunals (National Security) Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005;
- (c) the Industrial Tribunals (Equal Value) Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005;
- (d) the Industrial Tribunals (Levy Appeals) Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005;
- (e) the Industrial Tribunals (Health and Safety – Appeals against Improvement and Prohibition Notices) Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (f) the Industrial Tribunals (Non-Discrimination Notices Appeals) Rules of Procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] 2005.

(2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 3rd April 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) Subject to the savings in regulation 15, the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00016] and the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00017] are revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
