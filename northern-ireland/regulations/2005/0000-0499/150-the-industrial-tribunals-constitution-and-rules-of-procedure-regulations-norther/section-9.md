# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Practice directions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-685a39a9fac18d5e7b30ac7c593dca72][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The President may make practice directions about the procedure of industrial tribunals, including practice directions about the exercise by tribunals or chairmen of powers under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The power of the President to make practice directions under paragraph (1) includes power – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) to vary or revoke practice directions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) to make different provision for different cases, including different provision for specific types of proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The President shall publish a practice direction made under paragraph (1), and any revocation or variation of it, in such manner as he considers appropriate for bringing it to the attention of the persons to whom it is addressed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
