# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Power to prescribe (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b97728f618fc461af7063af83023a7ca][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may prescribe – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) one or more versions of a form, one of which shall be used by all claimants for the purpose of instituting proceedings in an industrial tribunal (“claim form”) except any claim or proceedings listed in paragraph (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) one or more versions of a form, one of which shall be used by all respondents to a claim for the purpose of responding to a claim before an industrial tribunal (“response form”) except respondents to a claim or proceedings listed in paragraph (3); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) that the provision of certain information and answering of certain questions in a claim form or in a response form is mandatory in all proceedings save those listed in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) The Department shall publish the forms and matters prescribed pursuant to paragraph (1) in such manner as it considers appropriate in order to bring them to the attention of potential claimants, respondents and their advisers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) The proceedings referred to in paragraph (1) are – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) those referred to an industrial tribunal by a court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) proceedings to which any of Schedules 4 to 6 apply or; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) proceedings brought under any of the following statutory provisions – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) sections 19, 20 or 22 of the National Minimum Wage Act 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^f00037];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] Article 43 of the Employment Rights Order where the proceedings are brought by the employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
