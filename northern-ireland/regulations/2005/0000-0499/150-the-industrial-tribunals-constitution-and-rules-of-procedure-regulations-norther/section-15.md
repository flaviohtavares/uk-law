# The Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 15

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4f0c667e413f946b6279e1630e972c68][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338][^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations and Schedules 1 to 6 shall apply in relation to all proceedings where those proceedings were commenced on or after 3rd April 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(2) These Regulations and Schedules 1 and 2 (with the exception of rules 1 to 3 and 38 to 48 of Schedule 1) shall apply to proceedings – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) which were commenced prior to 3rd April 2005; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) to which Schedule 1 to the old Regulations applied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

provided that a copy of the originating application was not sent to the respondent prior to 3rd April 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(3) In relation to the proceedings described in paragraph (2), the following provisions of Schedule 1 to the old Regulations shall continue to apply – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) rule 1 (originating application); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) rule 2 (action upon receipt of originating application) with the exception of paragraphs (2), (4) and (5) of that rule; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) rule 14 (costs). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(4) In relation to proceedings described in paragraph (2) but where a copy of the originating application was sent to the respondent prior to 3rd April 2005, Schedules 1 and 2 to these Regulations shall apply with the exception of rules 1 to 9, 21 to 24, 33 and 38 to 48 of Schedule 1 and rules 2 to 5 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(5) In relation to proceedings described in paragraph (4), the following provisions of the old Regulations shall continue to apply – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) in Schedule 1 – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (i) rule 1 (originating application); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] rule 2 (action upon receipt of originating application) with the exception of paragraphs (2), (4) and (5) of that rule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] rule 3 (appearance by respondent); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338] rule 8 (national security); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
  - (v) rule 14 (costs); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) rule 1 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(6) In relation to proceedings – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) which were commenced prior to 3rd April 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) to which Schedule 3 to the old Regulations applied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) in which the tribunal has not, prior to 3rd April 2005, required a member of the panel of independent experts to prepare a report under section 2A(1)(b) of the Equal Pay Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

these Regulations and rules 1 to 12 of Schedule 3, with the exception of rule 3(3)(a), shall apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(7) In relation to proceedings – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

- (a) which were commenced prior to 3rd April 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (b) to which Schedule 3 to the old Regulations applied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
- (c) in which the tribunal has, prior to 3rd April 2005, required a member of the panel of independent experts to prepare a report under section 2A(1)(b) of the Equal Pay Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

Schedule 3 to the old Regulations shall continue to apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(8) In relation to proceedings described in paragraph (7), the following rules of Schedule 3 shall also apply and shall take precedence over any conflicting provision in Schedule 3 to the old Regulations, namely rules 2, 10(2), 10(4), 11, 12(1) and 12(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(9) Rule 13 of Schedule 3 shall apply to all proceedings to which, in accordance with this regulation, rule 10 of Schedule 2 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]

(10) In relation to proceedings commenced prior to 3rd April 2005 and to which Schedule 4, 5 or 6 to the old Regulations applied, the provisions of those schedules shall continue to apply to such proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4878625fac4cccf854b59a648a78d338]
