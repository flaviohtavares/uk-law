# The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 337 (C. 26 )

DISABLED PERSONS

EDUCATION

The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

14th July 2005

The Department for Employment and Learning, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1 (3) of the Special Educational Needs and Disability Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 [^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

(2) In this Order, “the 2005 Order” means the Special Educational Needs and Disability (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed Day

1st September 2005 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2005 Order listed in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 14th July 2005.

Bernie O'Hare

A senior officer of the

Department for Employment and Learning
