# The Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 409

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

The Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

31st August 2005

Coming into operation

24th November 2005

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 63(6) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 24th November 2005.

## 2. Amendment

Paragraph (2)(h) of regulation 6 of the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] (exemptions) shall be omitted.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 31st August 2005.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 by omitting paragraph (2)(h) of regulation 6. This removes hearses from the use of vehicles to which Article 63(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (obligatory test certificates) does not apply.
