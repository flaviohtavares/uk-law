# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 109 (C. 9)

JUSTICE

The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2005

Made

9th March 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 87 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2005.

**2.** The provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

9th March 2005
