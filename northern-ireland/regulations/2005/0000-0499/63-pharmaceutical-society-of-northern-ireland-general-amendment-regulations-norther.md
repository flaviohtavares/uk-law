# Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 63

PHARMACY

Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

3rd March 2005

Coming into operation

1st June 2005

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 5 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00002] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2005.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal regulations” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the principal regulations

In regulation 2 of the principal regulations (registration fees) for “£150” there shall be substituted “£217” and for “£100” there shall be substituted “£145”.

**3.** In regulation 3 of the principal regulations (restoration fees) for “£57” there shall be substituted “£87”.

**4.** In regulation 4 of the principal regulations (retention fees) –

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£170” there shall be substituted “£245”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£78” there shall be substituted “£113”;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£62” there shall be substituted “£90”;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(c) for “£28” there shall be substituted “£41”.

Sealed with the Common Seal of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland on 3rd March 2005 in the presence of

K. McClelland

President

T. Hannawin

Acting Chief Executive, Secretary & Registrar

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety hereby approves the foregoing Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 3rd March 2005.

D. C. Gowdy

Permanent Secretary,

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 ([S.R. 1994 No. 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/202)) by increasing fees payable in respect of –

- (a) registration as a pharmaceutical chemist and as a student (regulation 2);
- (b) restoration of a person’s name to the register (regulation 3);
- (c) retention fees payable in respect of members of the Society (regulation 4);
