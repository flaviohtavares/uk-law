# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3, Appointed Day, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

> Text as at 2024-07-05.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2005 No. 192 (C. 14)

PENSIONS

The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3, Appointed Day, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

5th April 2005

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 1(2) and (6), 86(7) and 287(3) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation[^key-d2c4a89f8688c9c8ccf0fb891c000e7d]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3, Appointed Day, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

(2) In this Order –

- “the Order” means the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005;
- “the Winding Up Regulations” means the Occupational Pension Schemes (Winding Up) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002].

## 2. Appointed days[^key-cd12578e8edbc625bb5640281b628c62]

(1)[^key-4bb0c1c6668b66bb95b580e2bb4374ae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subject to paragraph (3) the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions specified in the Schedule is 6th April 2005.

(3) Notwithstanding the repeals in Article 74 of the 1995 Order (discharge of liabilities by insurance, etc.) mentioned in the Schedule, that Article shall continue to have effect as it applied immediately before 6th April 2005 to any scheme which –

- (a) was regarded at that time by virtue of regulation 2 of the Winding Up Regulations as having begun to be wound up for the purposes of those Regulations, or
- (b) in accordance with Article 121(4) to (8)[^f00003] of the 1995 Order began to wind up before that date.

(4) In paragraph (3) the reference to a scheme includes a reference to any section of a scheme to which Article 74 of the 1995 Order applies as if it were a scheme by virtue of regulation 12[^f00004] of the Winding Up Regulations.

## 3. Amendment of the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order[^key-5d479dee58578ca85257f75bb0a2ff4c]

In Schedule 3 to the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00005] (provisions referred to in Article 5 which are to continue to have effect as if they were in operation immediately before their amendment or repeal by the Order) after the entry in the table relating to Article 101 –

- (a) in the first column there shall be inserted “Article 121(1)”, and
- (b) in the second column there shall be inserted “Interpretation of Part II”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 5th April 2005.

John O'Neill

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order makes further provision for the coming into operation of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (“the Order”).

Article 1 provides for citation and interpretation.

Article 2 appoints the day, for the purposes of Article 86(7) of the Order, for the coming into effect of the Pensions Regulator Code of Practice No. 1: Reporting breaches of the law as 6th April 2005. It also appoints the day for the coming into operation of certain other miscellaneous provisions set out in the Schedule as 6th April 2005. Article 2 also makes a transitional saving in respect of the repeals in Article 74 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 made by Schedule 11 to the Order.

Article 3 amends the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 (“the No. 2 Order”). For the purposes of Article 5 of the No. 2 Order, Article 3 adds Article 121(1) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 to the list of provisions specified in the table in Schedule 3 to the No. 2 Order which shall continue to have effect for particular purposes as they were in operation immediately before their amendment or repeal by the Order.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
