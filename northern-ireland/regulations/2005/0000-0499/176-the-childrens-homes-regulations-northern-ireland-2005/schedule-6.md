# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — MATTERS TO BE MONITORED BY THE REGISTERED PERSON

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 33(1)

**1.**[^c-472ca106e2e7fbbf8cf72745f13a4855] In respect of each child accommodated in the children’s home, compliance with the placing authority’s plan for the care of the child (where applicable) and the placement plan.

**2.**[^c-850ff5b7a79b6a2bc78a8a3361eb7671] The deposit and issue of money and other valuables handed in for safekeeping.

**3.**[^c-7784155828535a1352bd32a20504bf06] Daily menus.

**4.**[^c-548d25024b5f03e721dc9d2b9988d00e] All accidents and injuries sustained in the home or by children accommodated there.

**5.**[^c-d1f7c8681b120a81c83ffb9bdcc51496] Any illnesses of children accommodated in the home.

**6.**[^c-eaf948d3e4373aa6fda979e959a6d918] Complaints in relation to children accommodated in the home and their outcomes.

**7.**[^c-dd1361a64d7d896b8e50c92e74a52cb4] Any allegations or suspicions of abuse in respect of children accommodated in the home and the outcome of any investigation.

**8.**[^c-1e0ee6211fbf6e563f6fdfff6e59c094] Staff recruitment records and conduct of required checks for new workers in the home.

**9.**[^c-8fed69573c4de8736b38c9574c231922] Visitors to the home and to children in the home.

**10.**[^c-3cc9110b04fd5c37358aac6b88648ffc] Notifications of the events listed in Schedule 5.

**11.**[^c-ddc38d3701bf1affc78a54bbcb11bcc0] Any unauthorised absence from the home of a child accommodated there.

**12.**[^c-2279c2b303ce101a5d46db762a5e3b12] The use of measures of control, restraint and discipline in respect of children accommodated in the home.

**13.**[^c-94d1979cc478627977970faa532d963f] Risk assessments for health and safety purposes and subsequent action taken.

**14.**[^c-7be7ecd13b358980b8118b3a8197c8aa] Medicines, medical treatment and first aid administered to any child accommodated in the home.

**15.**[^c-a6382d9a314caa1f4852cc2e0adb2ad0] Duty rosters of persons working at the home, and the rosters actually worked.

**16.**[^c-c6343774d77b8f3485ca9deb5f084419] The home’s daily log of events.

**17.**[^c-cb5ed6fc355e2a00ef45762df055893b] Fire drills and tests of alarms and of fire equipment.

**18.**[^c-97122004b00be641bf89fb4fb76671dd] Records of appraisals of employees and the training and development plan.

**19.**[^c-ef9c200be010a7df05f4fad5278a9472] Minutes of staff meetings.

**20.**[^c-7e59e6531aa52bdf766c26ae384bb1e9] Records of educational progress and attainment of children accommodated in the children’s home.
