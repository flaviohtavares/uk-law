# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — MATTERS TO BE INCLUDED IN THE STATEMENT OF PURPOSE

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 4(1)

**1.**[^key-58ecd3d7082d1a4519a036ea73fe246a] A statement of the overall aims of the children’s home, and the objectives to be attained with regard to children accommodated in the home.

**2.**[^key-4caca35d0d5165284ac6af6a52ff9e01] A statement of the facilities and services to be provided for the children accommodated in the children’s home.

**3.**[^key-5993f82edf003509c61586c8789efbc1] The name and address of the registered provider, and of the registered manager if applicable.

**4.**[^key-1eb6fb3b7cbbe9ad1c9ef5c5c86cc058] The relevant qualifications and experience of the registered provider and, if applicable, the registered manager.

**5.**[^key-9ac22c4641c7d9132c5c3afdc52b126f] The number, relevant qualifications and experience of persons working at the children’s home, and if the workers are all of one sex, a description of the means whereby the home will promote appropriate role models of both sexes.

**6.**[^key-4914d65dfa20bd6b3bc65cf970775c66] The arrangements for the supervision, training and development of employees.

**7.**[^key-15f754e2eeda4c5c14d0fa457aceb312] The organisational structure of the children’s home.

**8.**[^key-44f3e507c69a0fd6b7de9fa6955bc53a] The following particulars –

- (a) the age-range, sex and numbers of children for whom it is intended that accommodation should be provided;
- (b) whether it is intended to accommodate children who are disabled, have special needs or any other special characteristics; and
- (c) the range of needs (other than those mentioned in sub-paragraph (b)) that the home is intended to meet.

**9.**[^key-05f6ae854b3391decdf2494dbf50aa85] Any criteria used for admission to the home, including the home’s policy and procedures for emergency admissions, if the home provides for emergency admissions.

**10.**[^key-94d9398410caf404cbb0c1b1bb6236f7] If the children’s home provides or is intended to provide accommodation for more than eight children, a description of the positive outcomes intended for children in a home of such a size, and of the home’s strategy for counteracting any adverse effects arising from its size, on the children accommodated there.

**11.**[^key-c7f9d6952f2166ac2a93034a0498dcbf] A description of the children’s home’s underlying ethos and philosophy, and where this is based on any theoretical or therapeutic model, a description of that model.

**12.**[^key-358da887816b42fa074990c1e26da030] The arrangements made to protect and promote the health of the children accommodated at the home.

**13.**[^key-e24b6e91d3d21d0526c03318930f3ce7] The arrangements for the promotion of the education of the children accommodated there, including the facilities for private study.

**14.**[^key-dcd296155d7c90264cd092c5465d4f9a] The arrangements to promote children’s participation in recreational, sporting and cultural activities.

**15.**[^key-7a37dfa4e29aeaaf7779bbec7af06540] The arrangements made for consultation with the children accommodated about their future care and the operation of the children’s home.

**16.**[^key-201466ce2b0e79e983810768a0688244] The arrangements made for the control, restraint and discipline of children.

**17.**[^key-b382d9281dd6d0eabf5585ea440b81a9] The arrangements made for child protection and to counter bullying.

**18.**[^key-a7bdfb0811671c6f8c14d2a6d9ebdf1a] The procedure for dealing with any unauthorised absence of a child from the children’s home.

**19.**[^key-96155ea7daf3050a0b961105521005e1] A description of any electronic or mechanical means of surveillance of children which may be used in the children’s home.

**20.**[^key-79987f5839eea868520fe4b406df1cea] The fire precautions and associated emergency procedures in the children’s home.

**21.**[^key-6a1fb29a028c0490d18afc6dbbceae3d] The arrangements for the children’s religious observance.

**22.**[^key-443bc66857d5d526efa98003dbfad74f] The arrangements for contact between a child and his parents, relatives and friends.

**23.**[^key-a0c2dc0701892a374d135aa52408d75b] The arrangements for dealing with complaints.

**24.**[^key-54550603cbae33e400e1e044884547ad] The arrangements for contributing to the review of placement plans under Article 45 of the Children Order.

**25.**[^key-255dfbc6f0536f3d843bdf0144d23f0e] The type of accommodation, including the sleeping accommodation, provided, and, where applicable, how children are to be grouped, and in what circumstances they are to share bedrooms.

**26.**[^key-20921a2dd392077fe47f39c18f93a541] Details of any specific therapeutic techniques used in the home, and arrangements for their supervision.

**27.**[^key-8d9525b75a7a4c2772d889d8adc527a7] A description of the children’s home’s policy in relation to anti-discriminatory practice as respects children and children’s rights.
