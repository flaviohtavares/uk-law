# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 28

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Other records[^key-21e0ac8b700da593cc162ac05273d7e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The registered person shall maintain in the children’s home the records specified in Schedule 4 and ensure that they are kept up to date, are accurate and necessary.

(2) The records referred to in paragraph (1) shall be retained for at least fifteen years from the date of the last entry, except for records of menus, which need be kept only for one year.
