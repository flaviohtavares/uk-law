# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Food provided for children[^c-b8d7557f61fa19a7d4778aa32d85379f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The registered person shall ensure that children accommodated in a children’s home are provided with –

- (a) food which –
  - (i) is served in adequate quantities and at appropriate intervals;
  - (ii) is properly prepared, wholesome and nutritious;
  - (iii) is suitable for their needs and takes account of their preferences; and
  - (iv) is varied; and
- (b) access to fresh drinking water at all times.

(2) The registered person shall ensure that any special dietary need of a child accommodated in the home, which is due to his health, religious persuasion, racial origin or cultural background, is met.
