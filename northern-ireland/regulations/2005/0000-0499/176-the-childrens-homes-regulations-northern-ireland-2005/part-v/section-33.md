# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 33

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Review of quality of care[^c-22c30ad0e7198c80d582f935c26df928]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The registered person shall establish and maintain a system for –

- (a) monitoring the matters set out in Schedule 6 annually; and
- (b) improving the quality of care provided in the children’s home.

(2) The registered person shall supply to the RQIA[^c-4ca5e5950155e725f54604a422e453f9] and the placing authority a report in respect of any review conducted by him for the purposes of paragraph (1), and make a copy of the report available on request to [^c-ae118ab5bf1edc4ff3cb70d1b092824e]... the Commissioner.

(3) The system referred to in paragraph (1) shall provide for consultation with children accommodated in the home, their parents and placing authorities.
