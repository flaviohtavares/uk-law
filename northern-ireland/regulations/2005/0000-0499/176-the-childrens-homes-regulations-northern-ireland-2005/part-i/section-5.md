# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, section 5

> Text as at 2020-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Review of the statement of purpose and children’s guide[^c-cc6b47e10b358add78d550a59c61adcf]

*Extent: N.I.*

The registered person shall –

- (a) keep under review and, where appropriate, revise the statement of purpose and the children’s guide;
- (b) notify the RQIA[^c-4ca5e5950155e725f54604a422e453f9] of any such proposed revision within 28 days; and
- (c) if the children’s guide is revised, supply a copy to each child accommodated in the home.
