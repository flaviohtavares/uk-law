> Text as at 2020-05-07.

## 42. Revocations[^c-52e406ceb1b5ae70b814b4219734550d]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following Regulations are revoked –

- (a) the Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00019]; and
- (b) regulation 11 of the Health Services (Pilot Schemes: Miscellaneous Provisions and Consequential Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00020].
