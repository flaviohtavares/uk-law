# The Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 242 (C. 16)

CRIMINAL EVIDENCE

The Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2005

Made

9th April 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2005.

**2.** The provisions of Part II and III of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 18th April 2005.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

9th April 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Commencement Order brings into operation Parts II and III of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 on 18th April 2005.
