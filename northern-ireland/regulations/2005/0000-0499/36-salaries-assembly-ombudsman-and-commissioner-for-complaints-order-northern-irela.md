# Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 36

SALARIES

Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

14th February 2005

Coming into operation

21st March 2005

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5(1) and (2) of the Ombudsman (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001] and Article 4(1) and (2) of the Commissioner for Complaints (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 21st March 2005.

## 2. Revocation

The Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

## 3. Increase in Salaries

The annual salaries payable to the Assembly Ombudsman and the Commissioner for Complaints shall be increased from £106,196 to £109,892 from 1st April 2004.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 14th February 2005.

Dorothy Angus

A senior officer of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for an increase in the annual salaries payable to the Assembly Ombudsman for Northern Ireland and the Northern Ireland Commissioner for Complaints.

This provision is made with retrospective effect from 1st April 2004 under Article 5(2) of the Ombudsman (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 and Article 4(2) of the Commissioner for Complaints (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

Article 2 revokes the Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.
