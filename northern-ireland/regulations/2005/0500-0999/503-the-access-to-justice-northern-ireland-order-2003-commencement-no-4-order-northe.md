# The Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 503 (C. 35)

ACCESS TO JUSTICE

The Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

8th November 2005

Coming into operation

9th November 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 1(2) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 9th November 2005.

**2.** Article 46(2) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 shall come into operation.

## 3. Revocation

The Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 1) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] shall be revoked.

Falconer of Thoroton, *C.*

Dated 8th November 2005

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 9th November 2005 Article 46(2) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003.
