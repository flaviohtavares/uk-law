# The Brucellosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 585

ANIMALS

The Brucellosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made

22nd December 2005

Coming into operation

12th January 2006

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development [^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 5(1), 10(6), 18(7), 19, 44, 46(7A) and 60(1) of, and paragraph 5(1) of Part I and paragraph 5(1) of Part II of Schedule 2 to, the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Brucellosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 12th January 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Brucellosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

In paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to the Brucellosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00003] for the word “Ballyclare” there shall be substituted the word “Ballymena”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 22nd December 2005.

Liam McKibben

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Brucellosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

Under paragraph 2 of Schedule 1, certain markets are required to submit returns to the Department. The list of markets is amended by the removal of Ballyclare which has now closed and the inclusion of Ballymena.
