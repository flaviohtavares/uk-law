# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Surveys

A Board or the Agency may conduct or commission surveys or other research relating to the provision of general dental services.
