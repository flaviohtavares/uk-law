# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Delegation of duties to Dental Officer

The Committee may delegate to a Dental Officer such of its duties with respect to the approval of estimates for dental treatment and appliances under these regulations as it thinks fit.
