# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Constitution of the Dental Committee

The provisions set out in Schedule 8 shall apply in relation to the Committee which shall be constituted by the Agency for the purpose of carrying out the duties assigned to it by these regulations.
