# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Information about associateship agreements

(1) Where an associateship agreement is made, written notice of the parties to that agreement and of the date on which the agreement was made shall be given to the Board within 7 days of the date on which the agreement was made.

(2) Written notice of any change in the parties to, or of the termination of, any associateship agreement shall be given to the Board within 7 days of the date of such change or termination.

(3) Any notice required by this regulation shall be given by the person or, if more than one, one of the persons, liable by virtue of the associateship agreement to provide the use of premises and facilities.

(4) A Board shall supply to the Agency the information it receives under paragraphs (1) and (2) within 7 days of the date of its receipt of the information.
