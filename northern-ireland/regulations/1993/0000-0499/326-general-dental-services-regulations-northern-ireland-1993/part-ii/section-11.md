# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Information about assistants

A Board shall send to the Agency any information it receives under paragraph 35(5) of Schedule 2 (employment of assistants) within 7 days of the date of its receipt of the information.
