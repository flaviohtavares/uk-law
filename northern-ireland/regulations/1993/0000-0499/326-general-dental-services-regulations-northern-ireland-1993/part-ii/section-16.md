# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Board patient information leaflets

A Board shall compile and make available to any person who may reasonably require one, a document about the provision of general dental services by salaried dentists at any health premises in its area (in this regulation called a “**Board patient information leaflet**”) which shall include the information specified in Schedule 7.
