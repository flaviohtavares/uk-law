# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Arrangements for emergency cover

It shall be the responsibility of a Board to make any arrangements that may be necessary to enable a salaried dentist whose name is included in the dental list to comply with his obligation under paragraph 6 of Schedule 2.
