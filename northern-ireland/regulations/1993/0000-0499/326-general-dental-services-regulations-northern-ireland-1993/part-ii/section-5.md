# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Application for inclusion in the dental list

(1) A dentist who wishes to have his name included in the dental list shall make an application to the Board in writing which—

- (a) shall include—
  - (i) in the case of a salaried dentist or a dentist undertaking to provide only emergency dental services, the information specified in paragraphs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 11 of Part I of Schedule 3 and the undertaking specified in that Part of that Schedule, and
  - (ii) in any other case, all the information and the undertaking specified in Part I of Schedule 3; and
- (b) may include the information specified in Part II of that Schedule.

(2) The Board shall determine an application made by a dentist under paragraph (1) within 14 days of the date of its receipt of the application or, where the Board considers the proposed practice premises should be inspected, within 14 days of the date of that inspection.
