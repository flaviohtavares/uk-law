# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Restrictions on the right to be included in the dental list

(1) A dentist shall not be entitled to have his name included in the dental list if he has previously withdrawn his name from a list in circumstances where—

- (a) the Department has certified for the purposes of regulation 7(7) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1984[^f00012]; or
- (b) the Secretary of State has certified for the purposes of regulation 8(7) of the National Health Service (Superannuation) Regulations 1980[^f00013]; or
- (c) the Secretary of State for Scotland has certified for the purposes of regulation 10(4) of the National Health Service (Superannuation) (Scotland) Regulations 1980[^f00014];

that, in the interests of the efficiency of general dental services, he should cease to provide such services.

(2) Where the Board is not satisfied (following an inspection) that the proposed practice premises meet the requirements of paragraph 33 of Schedule 2 it may refuse to include a dentist's name in the dental list.
