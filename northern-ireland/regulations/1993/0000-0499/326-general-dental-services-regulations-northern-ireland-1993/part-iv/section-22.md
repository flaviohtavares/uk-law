# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Drugs

The fees payable by the Agency to a dentist in respect of listed drugs supplied by him under paragraph 22(1) of Schedule 2 for use before a supply can otherwise be obtained under paragraph 23 of that Schedule shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Tariff.
