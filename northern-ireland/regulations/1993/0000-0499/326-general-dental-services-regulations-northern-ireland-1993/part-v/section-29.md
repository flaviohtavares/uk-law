# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Overpayments

Where the Agency considers that a payment has been made to a person owing to an error, or in circumstances where it was not due, it shall, except to the extent that the Department on the Agency's application directs otherwise, draw the overpayment to the attention of that person and—

- (a) where he admits the overpayment; or
- (b) where he does not admit the overpayment but the matter having been referred under regulation 4(2) of the Services Committee Regulations for investigation, the Agency, or the Department on appeal, decides that there has been an overpayment,

the overpayment shall be recoverable either by deduction from that person's remuneration or payments or in some other manner.
