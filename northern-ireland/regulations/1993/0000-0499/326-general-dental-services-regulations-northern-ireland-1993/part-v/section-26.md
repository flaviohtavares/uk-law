# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Application for payments

An application for payment shall be made to the Board and shall—

- (a) be made in writing;
- (b) be made by the claimant or, where he is incapable of applying, on his behalf; and
- (c) contain or be supported by such information as the Board may reasonably require for the purpose of establishing the claimant's entitlement to payments.
