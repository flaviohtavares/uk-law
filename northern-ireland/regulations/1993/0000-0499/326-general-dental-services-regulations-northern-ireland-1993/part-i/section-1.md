# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 and shall come into operation on 1st September 1993.
