# General Dental Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Service of documents

Any notice or other document which a Board or the Agency is required or authorised by these regulations (including the terms of service) to give or send to a dentist may be given or sent by delivering it to the dentist or by sending it by post to the dentist at his usual or last-known address.
