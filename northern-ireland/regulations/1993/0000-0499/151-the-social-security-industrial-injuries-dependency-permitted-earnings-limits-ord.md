# The Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1993 No. 151

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

Made

29th March 1993

Coming into operation

12th April 1993

The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 and shall come into operation on 12th April 1993.

## 2. Increase of earnings limits in respect of dependent children

In paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992—

- (a) in head (a) for “£115” there shall be substituted “£120”; and
- (b) in head (b) for “£15” and “£115” there shall be substituted “£16” and “£120” respectively.

## 3. Revocation

The Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland on

A. Devlin

Assistant Secretary

29th March 1993.

(this note is not part of the Order.)

Where a disablement pension with unemployability supplement is increased in respect of a child and the beneficiary is one of two persons who are spouses residing together or an unmarried couple, paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 provides that an increase shall not be payable in respect of the first child if the other person’s earnings are £115 a week or more and in respect of a further child for each complete £15 where the earnings exceed £115. This order substitutes the amount of £120 for the amount of £115 and £16 for the amount of £15.

Article 3 contains a revocation consequential upon the coming into operation of this order.
