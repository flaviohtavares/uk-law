# Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Revocations

Subject to regulation 23, the following Regulations are hereby revoked—

- (a) the Classification, Packaging and Labelling of Dangerous Substances Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1985[^f00050];
- (b) the Classification, Packaging and Labelling of Dangerous Substances (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00051];
- (c) the Classification, Packaging and Labelling of Dangerous Substances (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00052];
- (d) the Classification, Packaging and Labelling of Dangerous Substances (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00053];
- (e) the Classification, Packaging and Labelling of Dangerous Substances (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00054].
