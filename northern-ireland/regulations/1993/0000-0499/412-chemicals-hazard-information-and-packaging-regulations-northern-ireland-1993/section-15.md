# Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Labelling deemed to satisfy the requirements of certain enactments

Where a substance or preparation dangerous for supply or carriage is required to be labelled in accordance with these Regulations and is so labelled, that labelling shall be deemed to satisfy the requirements of—

- (a) section 5 of the Petroleum (Consolidation) Act (Northern Ireland), 1929[^f00030] including that section as applied to any dangerous substance by an Order in Council made under section 19 of that Act; and
- (b) regulations 6 and 7 of the Highly Flammable Liquids and Liquefied Petroleum Gases Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1975[^f00031].
