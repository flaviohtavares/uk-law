# Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Amendments to the Road Traffic Regulations

(1) The Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Packages etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00047] shall be amended in accordance with Part I of Schedule 11.

(2) The Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00048] shall be amended in accordance with Part II of Schedule 11.

(3) The Road Traffic (Training of Drivers of Vehicles Carrying Dangerous Goods) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00049] shall be amended in accordance with Part III of Schedule 11.
