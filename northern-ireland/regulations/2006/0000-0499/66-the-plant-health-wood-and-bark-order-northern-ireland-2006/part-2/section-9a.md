# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 9A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9A. Prohibitions applying to notifiable relevant material on entry: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-468a02b12da27c171d066cf1e58c9350][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies to notifiable relevant material, other than notified EU material, which is brought into a point of entry that is located in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) No person may move any notifiable relevant material or cause any notifiable relevant material to be moved from its point of entry unless the material is being moved to a designated area of plant health control or an approved place of inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) No person may remove or cause any notifiable relevant material to be removed from its point of entry, or where the material is moved to a designated area of plant health control or an approved place of inspection in Northern Ireland, the designated area of plant health control or approved place of inspection, unless an inspector has discharged the material under Article 11 or the removal of the material is permitted under Part 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) Any notifiable relevant material which is being held at a point of entry or a designated area of plant health control under paragraph (3) must be stored by the importer under the supervision and in accordance with the instructions of an inspector. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) The importer is liable for the costs of storing the notifiable relevant material pending its release. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
