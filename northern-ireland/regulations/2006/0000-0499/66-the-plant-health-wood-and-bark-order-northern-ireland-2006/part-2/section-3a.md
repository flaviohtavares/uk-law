# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 3A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3A. Application of Part 2: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fb2bc4cfba5f6df7ae1c7a522dd08fd6][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part applies to plant pests and relevant material which are brought into Northern Ireland from a third country, whether directly or via another UK territory. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
