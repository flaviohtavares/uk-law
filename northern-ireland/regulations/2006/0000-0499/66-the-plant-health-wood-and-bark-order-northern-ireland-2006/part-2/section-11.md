# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 11

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Plant health discharge (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-40299bfdb42215e38339e29c773aa134][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(A1)[^key-fe7d281757cea73e5b6be501f12ce2c8] Paragraph B1 applies to any notifiable relevant material, other than notified EU material, which is brought into a point of entry that is located in Northern Ireland and is not in the course of its consignment to an approved place of inspection in another UK territory. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(B1) An inspector may discharge notifiable relevant material from its point of entry, designated area of plant health control or approved place of inspection in Northern Ireland if the inspector is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the material meets the prescribed requirements; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the relevant material corresponds with the description given to it in the phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export which accompanied the material on entry; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) the relevant material is accompanied by the correct phytosanitary certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(1)[^key-45478ceef4b02487213f260ec1c65c4e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-45478ceef4b02487213f260ec1c65c4e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purpose of satisfying himself as to any of the matters *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in paragraph (B1)(a) (in its application to Northern Ireland)[^key-688a91aff1d0cfdf0091341a1420c1f5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in paragraph (2)(a) to (e), an inspector may carry out an examination of a consignment or lot of relevant material and its packaging, including any wood packaging material and, where necessary, the vehicle transporting that consignment or lot— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) in its entirety; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) on the basis of one or more representative samples from the consignment or from each lot forming part of the consignment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4)[^key-485b8fd62321e4aad134962d68e8f2d6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^key-b3bd65e834f5d3c44a51798fda2894d7] An inspector may, for the purpose of being satisfied as to matters in paragraph (B1)(b), carry out an examination of a consignment of relevant material to determine whether it corresponds to its description in the documents that accompany it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5)[^key-5616b8303419a531ec2665bf003883a1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-5616b8303419a531ec2665bf003883a1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Where the official body of point of entry of any relevant material that has been consigned to Northern Ireland via another part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] European Union[^key-675d0209b5b686fed5f772cbd2976e75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] by way of transit has agreed with the Department, as the official body of destination, that the Department shall be responsible for some or all of the matters referred to in paragraph (2)(a) to (f), the matters as to which an inspector shall satisfy himself shall be limited accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(8) Where an inspector is satisfied as to the matters referred to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in paragraph (B1)(c) or[^key-24a3041d664b19a7b24231436b4d1ad7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in paragraph (2)(g) he shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) stamp the phytosanitary certificate, phytosanitary certificate for re-export or industry certificate with the official stamp of the Department and the date the certificate was delivered in accordance with Article *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] 8(A1) or[^key-65c1973a03e7b305f1075d48aa43b9a7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] 8(1); [^key-a687b93f7f09bac06827f19d2ea059ca]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- [^key-a687b93f7f09bac06827f19d2ea059ca] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) An inspector may, for the purpose of performing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] an examination under paragraph (3)[^key-df0c7bff4baba09a378ed2fd43096d64] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] , require the occupier or other person in charge of the premises in which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] examination[^key-ea1f57da0da2197d65b05d8a13802009] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] is to take place to provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) where appropriate, suitable areas of inspection; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) adequate lighting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
