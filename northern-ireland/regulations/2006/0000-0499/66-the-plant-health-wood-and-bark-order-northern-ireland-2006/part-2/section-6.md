# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Requirements for certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0714a3c14309bd0d2c523437b6041f26][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(A1)[^c-3742ebf786380b5bd20e79d33b796aff] Subject to Article 7 and to paragraph (6), no person may bring any notifiable relevant material into a point of entry that is located in Northern Ireland unless the material is accompanied by one of the following certificates which certifies that the material meets the prescribed requirements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) a phytosanitary certificate issued in the country in which that material originates or in the country from which it was consigned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) where paragraph (2) applies, by a phytosanitary certificate for re-export. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(1)[^c-df8cc5549beff90da59248464418fca4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where relevant material consigned to Northern Ireland via any third country by way of transit has been split up, combined with other consignments or repackaged, that material shall be accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate for re-export issued in the country of transit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3)[^c-6f43d7990125ab148eac656e70019ddf] Where relevant material consigned to Northern Ireland via any third country by way of transit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) has or may have been exposed to infection or contamination by any pest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) no longer remains the same material specified in the phytosanitary certificate which accompanies it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) has been processed so as to change its nature, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

the phytosanitary certificate required to accompany it shall be issued in the country of transit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4)[^c-634be675d7fa61763aa621b9cd8fa6c0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Where a phytosanitary certificate is required by paragraph (2) to be accompanied by a phytosanitary certificate for re-export, it may consist of a copy of the certificate certified as a true copy of the original by an authorised officer but in all other cases shall be the original certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(6) Provided the material is transported in such a way as to prevent the accidental escape of pests and does not undergo any change in its customs status, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (A1) and paragraph (1) do[^c-1dacf3a2c5677e78616de67e31e6fcf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] does not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) relevant material landed in Northern Ireland which, under appropriate customs procedures, is in the course of its consignment between two third countries; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) relevant material *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] brought into[^c-e1d13405a6775db4057b10728723fb8c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Northern Ireland which has been consigned to Northern Ireland from another part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] United Kingdom[^c-1686fb0cc5f835681fdad8c3ce0b1e89] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] via a third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(7)[^c-8f1f48ab39e4981bda14fd5383b61d91] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
