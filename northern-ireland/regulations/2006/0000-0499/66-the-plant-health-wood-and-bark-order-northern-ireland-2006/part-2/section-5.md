# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 5

## 5. Advance notification of (affected by repeal, see note) arrival (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-85ba6cec9497047b1064e03840c98231][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-c85b2d69d0b6a0d4794f0a9a198f860d][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(A1)[^key-fe08ffb43eedddb0856f2acfe347eef8] No person may bring any notifiable relevant material into a point of entry that is located in Northern Ireland, unless notice is given in accordance with this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(1)[^key-aa102a2f1fd1fca292aabc12214a334d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-aa102a2f1fd1fca292aabc12214a334d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A notice under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (A1)[^key-bf375f488ffb9149424ca5c663da7f72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) be in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 12; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) subject to paragraph (5), be given in time to arrive at the office of the Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) in the case of any relevant material brought by air; at least four hours, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in any other case; at least three working days, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

  before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] its arrival[^key-bdb169fbb6b2a4d0a3ff5d1c4523390e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] .

(4) The address to which a notice shall be given under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (A1)[^key-1d5e23ee876bf3acfef71ea06e489ba0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall be such address as the Department shall specify from time to time, which may include an address for electronic communications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) Where a person who is required by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (A1)[^key-7d0dac814650beeb1d5d0cf597af2f4e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] to give notice of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] arrival[^key-74ab8a364be50b7e1ab1a393694c1f54] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] of any relevant material can reasonably show that he was unable to comply with the periods specified in paragraph (3)(b)(i) or (ii) because he was unaware that the material had been consigned, he shall give notice as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
