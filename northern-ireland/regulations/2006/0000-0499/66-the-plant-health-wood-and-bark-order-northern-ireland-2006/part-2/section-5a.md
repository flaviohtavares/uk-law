# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 5A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5A. EU transit material: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0e9f07de895bf1c49a97b73ae45e0d57][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person may bring any EU transit material into Northern Ireland unless that material is destined for a single approved place of inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Article 7(A1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
