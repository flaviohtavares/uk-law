# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 14

## 14. General provisions relating to certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0f147d4b54824d46ebc80244e921959e][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export shall be completed by an authorised officer in accordance with the relevant requirements of this Article and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) until 31st December 2009, shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) where it is issued by a contracting party to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] IPPC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf], be in the form set out in Part A or B, respectively, of either Schedule 10 or Schedule 11; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in any other case, be in the form set out in Part A or B, respectively, of Schedule 10; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) on and after 1st January 2010, shall be in the form set out in Part A or B, respectively, of Schedule 11. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2)[^key-4754bc7f256bea24a426a2077a3252f1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) be issued by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] [^key-74b1c8de447420c0902959b5238c4a6e]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or the national plant protection organisation of the country of export or re-export in accordance with the provisions of Article V(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] IPPC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) be issued in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] English[^key-fe77fe75cef9a5f2d1193c626a3bf29c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ;
- (c)[^key-9b2eb7644af13644dc21dd3fd4ee9c92] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) be addressed to the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] he Plant Protection Organisation of the United Kingdom[^key-f7e7e1d5524be6e2c7a55d84c526917e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) be completed in typescript or block capitals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4)[^key-58bbc24d53c8d047971c3c20a1ec9145] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^key-ed0509d41f73b6accc7db337bcd7abb9] Where in relation to any relevant material of a description specified in column 2 of Part A, C or D of the list of regulated material, more than one set of entry requirements is specified in the corresponding entry in column 3 of Part A, C or D of that list, the phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export issued in respect of any relevant material of that description must specify under the heading “Additional declaration” which particular requirement has been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) A phytosanitary certificate or a phytosanitary certificate for re-export shall be based on an inspection carried out not more than 14 days before the date of dispatch of the relevant material to which the certificate relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(6) A phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export shall be completed not more than 14 days before the date of the dispatch of the consignment of relevant material which it is to accompany. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
