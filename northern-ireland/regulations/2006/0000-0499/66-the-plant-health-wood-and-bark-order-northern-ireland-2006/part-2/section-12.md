# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 12

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Request to an officer of Revenue and Customs for material to be detained (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b742b04c611876013e2716cc07f0ea99][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where he has reasonable grounds for suspecting that there is a risk of spread of any pest from any relevant material, an inspector may request an officer for Revenue and Customs to exercise the power in Article 13(1) for the purpose of enabling the inspector to enforce any provision of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) A request under this Article— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) may identify the relevant material in any way; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) shall be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) in writing; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] orally and confirmed in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) Where an inspector issues a notice or takes any other action under this Order in respect of relevant material detained by an officer for Revenue and Customs under Article 13(1) he shall advise that officer in writing of that notice or action. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
