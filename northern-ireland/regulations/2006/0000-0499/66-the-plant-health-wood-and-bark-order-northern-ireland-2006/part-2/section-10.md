# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Exceptions from prohibition on removal of relevant material from area of plant health control (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a16cd28340745c731d02a5b9982200d9][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

The prohibition imposed by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 9A(3)[^c-c06f7859d4c3c2022403ae2ff955c744] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] on the removal of relevant material from an area of plant health control unless it has been discharged by an inspector shall not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any relevant material which is in the course of its consignment between two third countries under appropriate customs procedures and without any change in its customs status and which is transported in such a way as to prevent the accidental escape of pests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any wood excepted by Article 7 from the requirements in Article 6; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) any relevant material which is consigned to Northern Ireland from another part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] United Kingdom[^c-35990fc023bd3aa1162df6e9ad167ae3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] via a third country without any change in its customs status and which is transported in such a way as to prevent the accidental escape of pests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
