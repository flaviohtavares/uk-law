# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Exceptions from certain prohibitions and requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c1b17f19baf3de7fe1407e396a8ac12a][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(A1)[^c-b37b919ee58e999a78f39562d6198603] The provisions referred to in paragraph (A2) do not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any wood described in paragraph (2) originating in any third country, other than the European Union or Switzerland, which is brought into Northern Ireland in the baggage of a passenger or other traveller coming from any such third country and meets the conditions in paragraph (A3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any small quantity of relevant material originating in the European Union or Switzerland which is brought into Northern Ireland in the baggage of a passenger or other traveller coming from the European Union or Switzerland and meets the conditions in paragraph (A3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(A2) the provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) Article 4(A1)(e) and (h); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) Article 5(A1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) Article 5A(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) Article 6(A1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) Article 9A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (f) Article 11A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(A3) The conditions are that the relevant material— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) does not show any signs of the presence of a pest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) is not intended for use in the course of a trade or business; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) is intended for household use; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) in the case of any wood originating in a third country, other than the European Union or Switzerland, has been consigned from the Euro-Mediterranean area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(1)[^c-1a2a2512c410a85c02cfc89df4213033] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The wood referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (A1)[^c-b5781033d798c50fdcc5226fc1e1a1a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] is that without bark which does not exceed 5 pieces and 1 metre each in length. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
