# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 11A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11A. Requirements applicable to notified EU material: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-393adfad94c82ff2d72b3c13f0f50798][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies to notified EU material which is brought into a point of entry that is located in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) An inspector must carry out an examination of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export accompanying a consignment of notified EU material to confirm that the consignment is accompanied by the correct phytosanitary certificate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the trade documents that accompany the consignment to confirm that those documents correspond to the description of the relevant material in the phytosanitary certificate or phytosanitary certificate for re-export. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
