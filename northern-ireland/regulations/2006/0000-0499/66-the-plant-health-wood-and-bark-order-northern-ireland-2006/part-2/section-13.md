# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Power of an officer for Revenue and Customs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e02d58608f0b5b993acb06fac7a40b62][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An officer for Revenue and Customs may, where requested by an inspector in accordance with Article 12(1), detain for not more than two working days any relevant material or any container, package or cargo of any kind which has been or may have been in contact with that material and which is referred to in that request insofar as the material, container, package or cargo is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] subject to the control of an officer of Revenue and Customs within the meaning of Schedule 1 to the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018[^key-6166ae775c83de8cad6381753e165c4d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] .

(2) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs may direct that any relevant material detained under paragraph (1) shall be dealt with during the period of its detention in such manner as they may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) The importer of any relevant material detained under paragraph (1) shall be responsible for the costs of storage which arise during the period of its detention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
