# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 36

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Licences for trial or scientific purposes and for work on varietal selections (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-2168c2a412c3ef3d77dad1739902487d][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-4732c7f655074deee1576e60d0129800]

*Extent: N.I.*

(A1)[^c-53a72c120d3020b14b9deaefc1fd2cd6] The Department must by licence authorise the importation, movement or keeping of any pest or relevant material for any activity for trial or scientific purposes or for work on varietal selections in Northern Ireland, where the importation, movement or keeping of the pest or relevant material for any such purpose would otherwise be prohibited by this Order, if the Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) have received an application for a licence containing the information set out in Part A of Schedule 13A; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) are satisfied that the general conditions set out in Part B of Schedule 13A are met in relation to the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(B1) A licence granted under paragraph (A1) must be in writing and include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the conditions specified in Part C of Schedule 13A which are relevant to any pest or relevant material that is the subject of the activities to which the licence relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any other conditions as the Department may determine in relation to licence quarantine measures that are appropriate in respect of those activities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(1)[^c-8d82b18ad6dc1cc26dea186480cd6ee7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-8d82b18ad6dc1cc26dea186480cd6ee7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where it is established to their satisfaction that the licensee has not fulfilled any condition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] paragraph (B1)(b)[^c-f07623f239409b0bf30d8f04f09e29d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] imposed on a licence, the Department shall revoke the licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) At the conclusion of any activities to which a licence granted under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] (A1)[^c-7d44fc171dc321f5c5824606258daa2a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] relates the licensee shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) subject to paragraph (5), destroy or sterilise any pest or relevant material that was the subject of the activities and any other relevant material which has come into contact with or which may have been contaminated by any such pest or relevant material; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) sterilise, or clean in such other manner as may be specified by the Department, the premises and facilities at which the activities were undertaken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) The Department may authorise the licensee to refrain from destroying any relevant material under paragraph 4(a) if they are satisfied that it has been subjected to appropriate quarantine measures and that it has been found by testing in such manner as may be specified by the Department to be free from the pests listed in this Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or, as regards Northern Ireland, any regulated pest[^c-4f331885745edc5f538e6f8a624ff3bd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] and from other pests considered by them to pose a risk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(6)[^c-21d28155be64507f1169626064e109c7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In this Article— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) “appropriate quarantine measures” means such quarantine measures as may be specified by the Department; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c441b7b17196cbd00b3f72fddb776470][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] “licence quarantine measures”, as regards Northern Ireland, means the measures specified in Part D of Schedule 13A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b)[^c-f9227fbfea1a58c216d23d135bf8cc1f][^c-344cc1bc82f8ccaf93f995983dbe0532] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
