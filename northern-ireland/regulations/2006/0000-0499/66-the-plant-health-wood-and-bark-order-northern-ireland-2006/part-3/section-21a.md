# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 21A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21A. Validity of UK plant passports: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-62ac7c73e52e8bd26cebb23fed3d71b1][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies to relevant material of a description specified in the list of pest free area controlled material which relates to a pest free area and which is moved through a pest free area to a destination outside the relevant UK pest free area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) The requirements in Article 19A(2) do not apply if the relevant material— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) originates outside the relevant UK pest free area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) is accompanied during its transit through the pest free area by a document of a type normally used for trade purposes which certifies that the material originates outside the relevant UK pest free area and is in transit to a final destination outside the relevant UK pest free area and the conditions in paragraph (3) are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the packaging in which the relevant material is transported and any vehicle which is used to transport the material is free from soil and plant debris and any relevant pest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the material was sealed immediately after packaging or, where appropriate, after loading, and remains sealed during its journey through the relevant UK pest free area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) the nature or construction of the packaging in which the material is transported and any vehicle which is used to transport the material are sufficient to ensure that there is no risk of any relevant pest which may be present in or on the relevant material escaping. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) In this Article— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) “relevant UK pest free area”, in relation to any relevant material of a description specified in the list of pest free area controlled material, means the pest free area which is, or is part of, the UK pest free area that has been designated in respect of that material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) “relevant plant pest”, in relation to a UK pest free area, means the plant pest in respect of which the UK pest free area has been designated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
