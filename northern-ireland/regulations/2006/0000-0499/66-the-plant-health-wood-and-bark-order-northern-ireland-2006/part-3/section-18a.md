# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 18A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18A. Prevention of the spread of pests: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-003cfbe160a7871628c502354e1fbf13][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person may knowingly keep, store, sell, plant or move or knowingly cause or permit to be kept, stored, sold, planted, moved— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any pest of a description specified in Part A, B or D of the list of prohibited plant pests; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any relevant material of a description specified in column 2 of Part A, B or D of the list of prohibited infested material which is carrying or infected with a plant pest of a description specified in the corresponding entry in column 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) any pest which, although not specified in Part A, B or D of the list of prohibited plant pests, or in column 3 of Part A, B or D of the list of prohibited infested material, is not normally present in Northern Ireland and which is likely to be injurious to plants in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) any relevant material of a description specified in column 2 of Part E of the list of regulated material which originates in the United Kingdom or a CD territory unless the requirements specified in the corresponding entries in respect of that description of relevant material in column 3 are complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) any relevant material originating in a third country which is brought into Northern Ireland in contravention of Article 4(A1)(d) or (e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) No person may knowingly keep, store, plant, sell or move within a pest free area or knowingly cause or permit to be kept, stored, planted, sold or moved within such an area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any pest of a description specified in column 2 of Part C of the list of prohibited plant pests which relates to a pest free area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) in the case of any pest free area specified in column 4 of Part C of the list of prohibited infested material, any relevant material of a description specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that Part which is carrying or infested with a pest of a description specified in the corresponding entry in column 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) any relevant material originating in a third country which is brought into a pest free area in contravention of Article 4(A1)(g); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) in the case of any pest free area specified in column 4 of Part C of the list of regulated material, any relevant material of a description specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that Part which originates in the United Kingdom or a CD teritory, unless the requirements specified in the corresponding entries in respect of that relevant material in column 3 are complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) The prohibitions in paragraphs (1) and (2) do not apply to any pest or relevant material which is required to be kept, stored or moved in compliance with a requirement imposed by an inspector under Part 6 or 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) In this Article, “move” means “move or otherwise dispose of” and “moved” is to be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
