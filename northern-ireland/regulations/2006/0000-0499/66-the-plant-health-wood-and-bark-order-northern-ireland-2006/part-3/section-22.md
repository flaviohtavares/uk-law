# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. General provisions relating to (affected by repeal, see note) UK plant passports (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d99d921198607cfdf2399e45e78e3546][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-e29f61374e041160785423a87a242592][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any alteration or erasure in a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^c-bf64c6330518636a23b554245fd918be] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall automatically invalidate that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] unless the alteration or erasure is certified by the authorised officer or the forestry trader authorised under Article 26 to issue the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] placing his hand written initials next to the alteration or erasure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) A UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] relating to any relevant material shall be treated as accompanying that relevant material only if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) affixed to the relevant material or to the packaging of that material by an authorised officer, the forestry trader authorised to issue it or an inspector; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) carried in the vehicle transporting that material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) A UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf], insofar as it comprises an official label, shall be affixed in such a way that it cannot be re-used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) A person may only issue a replacement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]—

- (a) to replace a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] issued in respect of a consignment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) that has been divided up; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] that has been combined, or part of which has been combined with another consignment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] whose plant health status has changed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) if he is satisfied that the relevant material to which the replacement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] will relate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) can be identified; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] is free from any risk of infestation by a pest specified in either Schedule 1 or 2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] to the Plant Health Regulations[^c-2549121fc38b153594f12917f532811e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] .
