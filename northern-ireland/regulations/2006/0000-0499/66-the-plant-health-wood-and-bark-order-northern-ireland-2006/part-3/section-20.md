# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Exceptions from certain prohibitions and requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-15a0e0fd651480986da7a0b8837aca88][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] [^key-3d02dda077761b772d725a162f746261]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]requirements in Article 19(1), (2), (5) and (6) for certain relevant material to be accompanied by a plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or, as regards Northern Ireland, Article 19A(1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)[^key-fca2c4fded3ac2aa278179b2982663ce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall not apply to small quantities of any relevant material, other than wood and bark of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] *Fraxinus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]* L., not showing any signs of the presence of any pest, which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) is not intended for use in the course of a trade or business; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) is intended for household use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
