# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 41

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8829bd84c84b96368b01456fea426a6c][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under this Order shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
