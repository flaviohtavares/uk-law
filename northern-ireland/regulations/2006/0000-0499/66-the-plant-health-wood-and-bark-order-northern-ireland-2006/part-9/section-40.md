# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 40

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Offences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-71320d67e89b3945a3a91201faf3e567][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person shall be guilty of an offence if without reasonable excuse, proof of which shall lie with him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) subject to paragraph (2), he contravenes or fails to comply with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i)[^key-2d968de84ed6d9bef19b1339f2d88b85] Article 5(A1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ia (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 5A(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 8(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 9A(2) or (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 15A(2), (3) or (4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (v) Article 18A(1) or (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 19A(1), (2) or (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 22(3) or (4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (viii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 24 (3), (4) or (7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ix (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 25(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (x) Article 33; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (xi (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 36(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (xii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 37(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (xiii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Article 38(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) he contravenes or fails to comply with a provision or condition of a notice served, or licence granted, under this Order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f504ad2b61eb70b8666881df477f1c41][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] contravenes or fails to comply with any prohibition or restriction in a notice issued under Article 28A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) he intentionally obstructs an inspector or any person authorised by an inspector in exercise of his powers given by or under this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Paragraph 1(a) shall not apply where an article of any description is landed in Northern Ireland in contravention of a prohibition in this Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^f00034] other than the prohibition in Article 5(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) A person shall be guilty of an offence if, for the purpose of procuring the issue of a plant passport or a replacement plant passport, a phytosanitary certificate, a phytosanitary certificate for re-export or a licence under this Order, he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) knowingly or recklessly makes a statement which is false in a material particular, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) intentionally fails to disclose any material information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) A person shall be guilty of an offence if he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) dishonestly issues a plant passport; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) dishonestly alters a plant passport, or re-uses a plant passport. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) Where an offence under this Order is committed by a Scottish partnership and is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or attributable to any neglect on the part of, a partner, he, as well as the partnership, shall be guilty of the offence and be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(6) Where the commission by any person of an offence under this Order is due to the act or default of some other person, that other person may be charged with and convicted of the offence by virtue of this paragraph whether or not proceedings for the offence are taken against the first-mentioned person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
