# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 25

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Conditions for maintaining registration as a forestry trader (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e5a5430cc958a4dda2c768717a054061][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A registered forestry trader shall in relation to the activities and premises to which his registration relates comply with the following conditions: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) he shall keep an accurate plan of the premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) he shall keep a record of relevant material purchased by him or brought onto the premises for storage or production on those premises, and of relevant material under production on or dispatched from those premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) he shall keep all documents, created or received by him, relating to the records kept under subparagraph (b), for at least one year from the date he created or received them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) he shall designate an individual (whether himself or another) who is technically experienced in relation to the activities carried out on the premises and related plant health matters affecting the premises who shall be available to liaise with the Department in relation to matters arising under this Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) he shall examine his premises and relevant material at such times and in a manner specified in guidelines issued from time to time by the Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (f) he shall make a declaration at such time and in such form as the Department may from time to time require that he is able and willing to comply with the conditions specified in sub-paragraphs (a) to (e); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (g) he shall comply with any other conditions which may be specified by the Department which they consider necessary to enable them to assess the presence of or spread of any pest on the premises by reason of the condition of those premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Where the Department are satisfied that a registered forestry trader has failed to comply with any of the conditions specified in paragraph (1) they may suspend his registration until they are satisfied that he is able and willing to comply with those conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
