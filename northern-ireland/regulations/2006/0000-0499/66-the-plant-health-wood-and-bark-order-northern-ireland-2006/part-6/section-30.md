# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 30

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Actions which may be taken by an inspector (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d1dd614685b0468e2a6be06e091e24b4][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to Article 29, if an inspector has reasonable grounds for suspecting that there is present or likely to be present on any premises any pest referred to in paragraph (2) or any relevant material referred to in paragraph (3), he may, after giving the occupier or other person in charge of the premises reasonable notice of his intention and upon production if so required of his authority, enter such premises and either on those premises or elsewhere take steps— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) to destroy any pest referred to in paragraph (2) and to prevent the spread of any such pest; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) to destroy or treat any relevant material referred to in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) The tree pests referred to in paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) a pest of a description specified in Schedule 1 or column 3 of Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or, as regards Northern Ireland, a regulated pest[^c-5b20b8d63e20c136095d0fd0d4c689be] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any pest not normally present in Northern Ireland and in respect of which there is, in the opinion of the inspector, an imminent danger of its spreading or being spread in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) The relevant material referred to in paragraph (1) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any relevant material which is carrying or is infected with, or which may be carrying or infected with, a pest referred to in paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any relevant material not carrying or infected with a pest referred to in paragraph (2) but in respect of which there is, in the opinion of the inspector, an imminent danger of such a pest spreading or being spread. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) An inspector on entering any premises under paragraph (1) may take with him such persons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-2ffaefae49fb980fe3f98c98cf2fb5dd]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf], and such equipment and vehicles as he considers necessary for the purposes of facilitating the exercise of his powers under that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) Any person whom an inspector takes with him on to premises in accordance with paragraph (4) may, whether or not accompanied by an inspector, upon production if so required of his authority given in that behalf by the Department, remain on the premises and from time to time re-enter the premises with any equipment or vehicles that person considers necessary, and carry out such work in such manner as the inspector may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
