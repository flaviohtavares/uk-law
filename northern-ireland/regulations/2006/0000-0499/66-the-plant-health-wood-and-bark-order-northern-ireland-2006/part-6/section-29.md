# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Actions which may be required by an inspector (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6fa5604926da3d1cb8231b795a7e703c][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If an inspector has reasonable grounds for suspecting that any pest or relevant material is likely to be, or has been *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] brought into a point of entry located[^c-0f0b83d47ad3be0ef5d0285373deea70] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] in Northern Ireland in contravention of this Order he may serve a notice in writing in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) An inspector may serve a notice under paragraph (1) on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) a forestry trader or other person who is in possession of or in any way entitled to the custody or control of the pest or relevant material which has been *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] brought into the point of entry[^c-64fc8eef67ae95616a50e59100fc12ed] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any person in charge of the premises from which any pest or relevant material is likely to be or has been *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] brought into the point of entry *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf].

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) prohibit the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] bringing in[^c-9a7a9b5815e2f6e7abd66099d2a5b049] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] of any pest or relevant material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) specify the manner in which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] any pest or relevant material is to be brought in[^c-38a588daa7615c25147a4f375e39e6da] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] and the precautions which are to be taken during and subsequent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] to its entry[^c-395d8df02eb2332f67fda4b3e0af2a70] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ;
- (c) require any pest or relevant material to be treated, re-exported, destroyed or otherwise disposed of in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) prohibit the removal of any pest or relevant material from premises specified in the notice for such period as may be so specified and, where appropriate, impose such other prohibitions as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the introduction or spread of any pest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) require the removal of any pest or relevant material from premises specified in the notice in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (f) require the taking of such other steps, specified in the notice, as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the introduction or spread of any pest in such a manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) If an inspector has reasonable grounds for suspecting that there is present or likely to be present on any premises anything referred to in paragraph (5) or any relevant material referred to in paragraph (6), he may by notice in writing served on the occupier or other person in charge of the premises or such pest or relevant material— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) require any pest or relevant material to be treated, destroyed or otherwise disposed of in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) prohibit the removal of any pest or relevant material from premises specified in the notice for such period as may be so specified and, where appropriate, impose such other prohibitions as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the spread of any pest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) require the removal of any pest or relevant material to premises specified in the notice in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) require the taking of such other steps, specified in the notice, as appear to the inspector to be necessary to prevent the spread of any pest in such manner and within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) The pests referred to in paragraph (4) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) a pest of a description specified in Schedule 1 or in column 3 of Schedule 2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or, as regards Northern Ireland, a regulated plant pest[^c-c79e1bc6c1ebf615a2e6774389c787d3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ;
- (b) any pest which is not normally present in Northern Ireland and in respect of which there is, in the opinion of the inspector, an imminent danger of its spreading or being spread in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] [^c-0cb9cc6667d620702fd318c7bc7cdf19]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- [^c-0cb9cc6667d620702fd318c7bc7cdf19] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) The relevant material referred to in paragraph (4) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) any relevant material which is carrying or is infected with, or which may be carrying or infected with, a pest referred to in paragraph (5); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) any relevant material the landing of which in Northern Ireland is prohibited under Article 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-96c84ae6b1a37e67bf97cd088d0fa48c]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or the movement of which in Northern Ireland is prohibited under Article 18 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or, as regards Northern Ireland, Article 18A[^c-1396fa6a011cd1ab9fd7c651166dd1b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] .

(6A)[^c-a595a2cb7e25964393a887bbc6c74d15] Subject to this Article, in the case of relevant material imported from the EU which did not require a plant passport immediately before exit day, such material will not be subject to checks under Articles 11A and 28 of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(7) If an inspector has reasonable grounds for believing that it is necessary for the purpose of preventing the spread of or ensuring the eradication of any pest from the premises mentioned in paragraph (4), he may by notice in writing served on the occupier or other person in charge of any other premises impose such prohibitions and require the taking of such reasonable steps, specified in the notice, as appear to him to be necessary for that purpose, such steps to be taken in such manner and in such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
