# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 31

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Miscellaneous provisions as to notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-295d3dae41601f2a197cf798665d58ef][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A notice served under paragraph (1) or (2) of Article 29 may specify one or more requirements or alternative requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Any treatment, re-export, destruction or disposal required by a notice served under Article 29 shall be carried out or arranged to be carried out by the person on whom the notice is served to the satisfaction of an inspector from or at a place designated by an inspector and, except with the written authority of an inspector, no pest or relevant material to which the notice relates shall be moved otherwise than directly from or to such a place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) An inspector may amend or withdraw a notice served by an inspector under this Order by a further notice served on the person on whom the original notice was served or on the person who is the occupier or in charge of the premises in respect of which the further notice is intended to be served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) A notice under paragraph (3) may be subject to such conditions, if any, as the inspector considers expedient to impose for the purpose of preventing the introduction or spread of any pest or re-infection or re-infestation by the pest to which the original notice relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(5) Any notice served under this Part may define by reference to a map or plant or otherwise the extent of the premises referred to in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(6) Where a notice is served under paragraph (2) or (4) of Article 29 (“an Article 29 notice”), an inspector may, either in that notice or in a separate notice served on the owner or on such other person as appears to him to be in charge of the premises to which the Article 29 notice relates, require the person on whom the notice is served to inform— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the Department of any change in the occupation of the premises to which the Article 29 notice relates together with the date of such change and the name of the new occupier; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the new occupier of the premises of the contents of the Article 29 notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
