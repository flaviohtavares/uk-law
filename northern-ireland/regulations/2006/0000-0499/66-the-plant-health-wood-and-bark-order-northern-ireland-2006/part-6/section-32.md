# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 32

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Service of notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-95706b3d8bc37ad2bc4812154ed02f05][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (4), a notice under this Order may be served on any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) by delivering it to him personally; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) by leaving it for him at his last known place of abode or business; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) by sending it through the post addressed to him at his last known place of abode or business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Where a notice under this Order must be served on the occupier or other person in charge of premises, and the last known place of abode or business of that person cannot be ascertained after reasonable inquiry, the notice shall be taken to be served seven days after it has been addressed to “the occupier” and affixed conspicuously to an object on the premises to which the notice relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), a notice served under this Order may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) in the case of a body corporate (other than a limited liability partnership), be served on the secretary or clerk of that body at the address of the registered or principal office of that body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) in the case of a partnership including a Scottish partnership (other than a limited liability partnership), be served on a partner or person having the control or management of the partnership business at the address of the principal office of the partnership; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) in the case of a limited liability partnership, be served on a member of the partnership at the address of the registered or principal office of that partnership, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

and for the purposes of this paragraph the principal office of a company registered outside the United Kingdom or of a partnership carrying on business outside the United Kingdom shall be its principal office within the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) In the case of a registered forestry trader a notice under this Order shall be served on the trader either by delivering it to him personally, or by leaving it for him, or sending it through the post addressed to him, at the address of his premises listed in the register or, if more than one such address is registered, any address specified by the trader as his principal address. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
