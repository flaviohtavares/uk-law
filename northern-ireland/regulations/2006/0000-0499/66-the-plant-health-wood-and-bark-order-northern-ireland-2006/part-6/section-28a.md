# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 28A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28A. Emergency measures: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-267c6cdbcf2c5d1eebed1ff950595018][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a regulated plant pest is found to be present in Northern Ireland the Department may by notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) demarcate an area in relation to that infestation for the purpose of eradicating or containing that plant pest; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) specify the prohibitions and restrictions which are to apply in the demarcated area for that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) A notice under paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) must be in writing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) must describe the extent of the demarcated area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) must specify the date on which any such prohibitions or restrictions are to commence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) must be published in a manner appropriate to bring it to the attention of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) must be amended or revoked, in whole or in part, by further notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
