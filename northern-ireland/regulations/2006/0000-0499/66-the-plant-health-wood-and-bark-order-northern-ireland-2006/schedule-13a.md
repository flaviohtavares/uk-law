# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, SCHEDULE 13A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 13A (affected by repeal, see note) — Licences for trial or scientific purposes or for work on varietal selections (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-87a8d9498014ceaae5cecf445459307d][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 36 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**1.** In this Schedule, “specified activity” means any activity for trial or scientific purposes or for work on varietal selections. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

### PART A (affected by repeal, see note) — Information to be included in an application for a scientific licence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**2.** The name and address of the person responsible for the specified activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**3.** The following details in relation to the relevant material and plant pests to be used in the specified activity— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) their scientific name or names; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the type of relevant material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) the quantity of relevant material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (d) the place of origin of the relevant material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) the place at which the relevant material is to be first stored or planted after its official release (where relevant); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (f) the proposed method of destruction or treatment of the relevant material on completion of the specified activity (where relevant); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (g) in the case of any relevant material or plant pests which is to be imported from a third country, their proposed point of entry into the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**4.** Appropriate documentary evidence for the material to be imported from a third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**5.** The duration, nature and objectives of the specified activity, including a resume and a specification of the work to be conducted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**6.** The address and description of the specific site or sites at which the specified activity is to be carried out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

### PART B (affected by repeal, see note) — General conditions to be met on application for a scientific licence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**7.** The nature and objectives of the specified activity comply with the concept of trial or scientific purposes or for work on varietal selections. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**8.** The premises and the facilities at the site or sites at which the specified activity is to be carried out meet any conditions relating to their quarantine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**9.** The personnel carrying out the specified activity have appropriate scientific and technical qualifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

### PART C (affected by repeal, see note) — Licence conditions relating to any plant pest or relevant material to be used in a specified activity (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**10.** For the purposes of Article 36(B1)(a), the conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) in the case of any relevant material, the material is accompanied on its entry into the United Kingdom by a letter of authority which has been issued by the relevant national plant protection organisation on the basis of appropriate documentary evidence as regards the place of origin of the material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) in the case of any relevant material of a description specified in Schedule 5 to the Plant Health Regulations, the material is, wherever possible, accompanied on its entry into the United Kingdom, by a phytosanitary certificate issued in the country of origin which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) confirms that the material is free from any regulated plant pest, other than any plant pest whose importation is authorised by the licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] includes the statement under the heading ‘Additional declaration’, ‘This material is imported under Article 36 of the Plant Health(Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006’; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] includes the name of any authorised plant pest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**11.** The relevant material must be held under quarantine containment conditions and on arrival is directly and immediately moved to the site specified in the licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

### PART D (affected by repeal, see note) — Licence quarantine measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**12.** The licence quarantine measures are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) in the case of the premises, facilities and working procedure which relate to the specified activity: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) their physical isolation from all other material which may provide a pathway for a regulated pest, including control of vegetation in surrounding areas, where appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the designation of a contact person responsible for the specified activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the implementation of restrictions on access to the premises and facilities and to the surrounding area, as appropriate, to named personnel only; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the appropriate identification of the premises and facilities being used indicating the type of activities and the personnel responsible; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (v) the maintenance of a register of the activities performed and a manual of operating procedures, including procedures in the event of escape of plant pests from containment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the maintenance of appropriate security and alarm systems; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the implementation of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] appropriate control measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the premises of plant pests; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] controlled procedures for sampling and for transfer between premises and facilities, of the material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] controls for the disposal of waste, soil and water as appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (dd (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] appropriate hygiene and disinfection procedures and facilities for personnel, structures and equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (ee (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] appropriate measures and facilities for disposal of experimental material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
    - (ff (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] appropriate indexing (including testing) facilities and procedures; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) other appropriate quarantine measures according to the specific biology and epidemiology of the type of material involved and the activities approved, including: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) the maintenance of facilities with separate chamber ‘double door’ access to personnel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the maintenance of facilities under negative air pressure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the use of escape-proof containers with appropriate mesh size and other barriers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the maintenance of the material in isolation from other plant pests and material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (v) the maintenance of any material for breeding in breeding cages with manipulation devices; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the prohibition on interbreeding of the plant pests with indigenous strains or species; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (vii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] the implementation of controls on the continuous culture of plant pests; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
