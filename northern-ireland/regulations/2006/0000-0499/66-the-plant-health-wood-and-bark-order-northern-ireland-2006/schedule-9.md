# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, SCHEDULE 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 9 (affected by repeal, see note) — Requirements for (affected by repeal, see note) UK plant passports (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^key-66cc7ada172c4ac546821b635d3cf1b4][^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 2(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**1.**[^key-2c8b965bc588af594669735620fee378] A UK plant passport[^key-df5179cab402a05bbb383ef06d1dacbb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] may only be issued in respect of relevant material that has been the subject of a satisfactory inspection at its place of production. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**2.**[^key-a0096f17bd57539cb6cf1e6f8cbdeb4b] A UK plant passports[^key-9595fd8e54785a61a812bb8b7eff0f2f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall comprise either an official label or an official label together with a document of a kind normally used for trade purposes, containing the information specified in paragraph 5 or (as the case may be) 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**3.**[^key-955c0b41248e0d30aaffa90038e4fdd1] The references in paragraph 2 above to an official label are references to a label which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) has not been previously used and is made of a material suitable for its purpose, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) in the case of an adhesive label, is in a form approved for use as an official label by the Department, for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passports[^key-0c7137bca80af4cdb36ea62199e1ed29] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] issued in Northern Ireland, or by the authority with responsibility or such authorisation for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passports *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] issued in other member States. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**4.**[^key-2dbc132c08a806c31e488ee9335c5bba] (1) The information contained in a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^key-e5d622dde89f0e2bf592c0bf2b36ee0f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a)[^key-5907111cf514f944df1c4b6318786f2b] English *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) printed, except where it would not reasonably be practicable to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) Where the information is printed, it shall be printed in block capitals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) Where the information is not printed, it shall be given in typescript or written in block capitals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**5.**[^key-582696db8066648e71dfb391fdac6a49] Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^key-8ecbba026e17982f7f56b45cac602639] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] comprises only an official label, it shall contain the information specified in paragraph 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**6.**[^key-5f9f402da3448ac1fbe1b4c16988d166] Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^key-be72760429fe48c67124d9103c012f3a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] comprises an official label and an additional document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the official label shall contain at least the information specified in sub-paragraphs (a) to (e) of paragraph 7, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) the additional document shall contain all the information specified in paragraph 7, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

**7.**[^key-8a5a9295498451b6012c62043b6e5e81] The information referred to in paragraph 5 and 6 above shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) the title ‘UK plant passport[^key-e9b5a5d199f1ff3b76db906dac3f6d0c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ”;
- (b)[^key-95e72c9267d16608c7d43774fc6d55af] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the name or code of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] appropriate UK plant health authority[^key-7b2d9b20539e98c730458d54f50bab9a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ;
- (d) the registration number of the producer, importer or other person to whom the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^key-8a849c7faaadb3a741ceb14121446165] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] was issued; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (e) the week number of the date when the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] is attached to material, or a serial or batch number identifying that material; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (f) the botanical name in Latin of the material to which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (g) the quantity of the material to which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] relates (volume or weight of wood or isolated bark, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]);
- (h) where the relevant material fulfils the requirements for a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK pest free area[^key-7eb3588b20f0d54ebbc8ac70d727180c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] , the marking “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] PFA[^key-4adf2a281c9e8c61e728f5ce1a75a787] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ” and the code for the protected zone; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (i) in the case of a replacement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport[^key-49e7d19905968a272be944b69cfba669] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] , the marking “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]RP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]” and where appropriate the registration number of the producer or importer who was authorised to issue the original *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] or to whom the original *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK plant passport *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] was issued; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (j) in the case of material originating in a third country, the name of the country of origin of the material or (if appropriate) the country from which the material was consigned to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] United Kingdom or a CD territory[^key-c51992c7129164016a5f4defca830e74] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] .
