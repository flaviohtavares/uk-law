# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 39

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Information to be given (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ce3beac2e438438230d5aa8de6b31d98][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An inspector or any other officer of the Department may by notice in writing require any person referred to in paragraph (2) to give the inspector or officer within such reasonable time as may be specified in that notice any information referred to in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(2) A person to which paragraph (1) refers is any person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) is the owner or occupier or other person in charge of premises in respect of which a notice has been served under this Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) has or has had or is reasonably suspected by an inspector or other officer of the Department to have or have had in his possession or under his charge— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (i) any pest which is of a description specified in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] Schedule 1 or 2 to the Plant Health Regulations;[^c-7e1a20484c63dac408a058e0260d552b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] ;
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf][^c-6efe0c058d681dcef6cb244af41bf4f8] any pest other than a regulated pest which, although not specified in Schedule 1 or 2 to the Plant Health Regulations, is not normally present in Northern Ireland and is likely to be injurious to trees in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] any relevant material carrying or infected with a pest mentioned in sub-paragraph (i) or (ii); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] any relevant material which an inspector or any other officer of the Department knows to have been landed or suspects has been landed in, or exported from, Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) as auctioneer, salesman or otherwise, has sold, offered for sale or otherwise disposed of any of the things mentioned in sub-paragraph (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(3) The information referred to in paragraph (1) is any information that a person referred to in paragraph (2) may possess— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

- (a) as to products stored at any time on the premises referred to in paragraph (2)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (b) as to any pest or relevant material referred to in paragraph (2)(b); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
- (c) as to the persons who have had or are likely to have had any pest or relevant material referred to in paragraph (2)(b) in their possession or under their charge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]

(4) A person who is required to give an inspector or other officer any information under paragraph (1) shall produce for examination by that inspector or other officer any licences, official statements, certificates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] UK[^c-1b365d916864fab455cdba1270600d02] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf] plant passports, records, invoices or other documents relating to any pest or relevant material to which that information relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4e0a7ee3a5028ca90807cb4b6293cf]
