# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 95 (C. 7)

PENSIONS

The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

8th March 2006

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in—

- (a) Part 1 of the Schedule—
  - (i) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 9th March 2006, and
  - (ii) for all other purposes, is 6th April 2006;
- (b) Part 2 of the Schedule is 1st April 2006, and
- (c) Part 3 of the Schedule is 6th April 2006.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 8th March 2006.

Philip Angus

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

This Order provides for the coming into operation of provisions of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 as follows—

- 9th March 2006 for provisions relating to the obligations of trustees of occupational pension schemes for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, and for all other purposes, on 6th April 2006;
- 1st April 2006 for provisions relating to applications for reconsideration and amounts to be raised by the pension protection levies, and
- 6th April 2006 for provisions relating to the Pensions Regulator, the Board of the Pension Protection Fund, other miscellaneous provisions, consequential amendments and repeals.
