# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 9

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Time limits (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bd417178b31aa3131ef1215a9d662be9][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The general rule is that the application must reach the Department within a period of nine months beginning with the first day of the academic year in respect of which it is submitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The general rule does not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) one of the events listed in regulation 14 occurs after the first day of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support, in which case the application must reach the Department within a period of nine months beginning with the day on which the relevant event occurred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the applicant is making a separate application for a fee loan, a fee contribution loan, a loan for living costs or a college fee loan or is applying for an additional amount of fee loan under regulation 20(4) or (10), an additional amount of fee contribution loan under regulation 31(5), an additional amount of loan for living costs under regulation 69(3) or an additional amount of college fee loan under paragraph 14(2) of Schedule 5, in which case the application must reach the Department not later than one month before the end of the academic year in respect of which he is applying for support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the applicant is applying to borrow an additional amount of fee contribution loan under regulation 31(3) or an additional amount of loan for living costs under regulation 69(1), in which case the application must reach the Department not later than one month before the end of the academic year to which the application relates or within a period of one month beginning with the day on which the applicant receives notice of the increased maximum amount, whichever is the later; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-1b9d62992b8747473ca78ff1f06cea91][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the applicant is a person mentioned in paragraph 12 of Part 2 of Schedule 1, in which case the application must reach the Department by 11 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]<sup>th *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]</sup> April 2008 or within a period of nine months beginning with the first day of the academic year in respect of which it is submitted, whichever is the later, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) the applicant is applying for the disabled students' allowance, in which case the application must reach the Department as soon as is reasonably practicable; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) the Department considers that having regard to the circumstances of the particular case the time limit should be relaxed, in which case the application must reach the Department not later than such date as it specifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
