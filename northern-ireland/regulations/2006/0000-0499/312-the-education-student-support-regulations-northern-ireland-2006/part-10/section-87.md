# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 87

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 87. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a81564920f1af2630e0ba0f0da731c77][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible part-time student retains his status as an eligible part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course until the status terminates in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The period for which an eligible part-time student retains his status is the “period of eligibility”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the academic year in which the eligible part-time student completes the designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible part-time student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) withdraws from his designated part-time course in circumstances where the Department has not transferred or converted or will not transfer or convert his status under regulation 96 or 97; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from his designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The Department may terminate the period of eligibility where the eligible part-time student has shown himself by his conduct to be unfitted to receive support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) If the Department is satisfied that an eligible part-time student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under this Part or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Department may take such of the following actions as it considers appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for any particular support or particular amount of support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulation 100. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) Where the period of eligibility terminates before the end of the academic year in which the eligible part-time student completes the designated part-time course, the Department may, at any time, renew the period of eligibility for such period as it determines. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
