# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 96

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. Transfer of status (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c8fcc85e963ce3ceb88ac733e20d09ff][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an eligible part-time student transfers to another part-time course, the Department must transfer the student’s status as an eligible part-time student to that course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) it receives a request from the eligible part-time student to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) it is satisfied that one or more of the grounds for transfer in paragraph (2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the period of eligibility has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The grounds for transfer are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the eligible part-time student starts to undertake another designated part-time course at the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the eligible part-time student starts to undertake a designated part-time course at another institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) after commencing a designated part-time course for a first degree (other than an honours degree) the eligible part-time student is, before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated part-time course for an honours degree in the same subject or subjects at the institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), an eligible part-time student who transfers under paragraph (1) shall receive in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers the remainder of the support for which the Department has determined he qualifies in respect of the academic year of the course from which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The Department may re-assess the amount of support payable after the transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An eligible student who transfers under paragraph (1) after the Department has determined his support in connection with the academic year of the course from which he is transferring but before he completes that year may not apply for another grant under regulation 88(1)(b) or regulation 92 in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) Where a student transfers under paragraph (1), the maximum amount of assistance under regulation 88(1)(a) in respect of the academic years to and from which he transfers is the amount of assistance with fees available in connection with the course which has the highest intensity of study as defined in regulation 89. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
