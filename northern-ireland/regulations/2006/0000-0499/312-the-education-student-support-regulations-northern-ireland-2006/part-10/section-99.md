# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 99

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. Payment of grants for fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4e0742710c5ffb069a001fb59dfd4b6][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the Department must pay the grant in respect of fees for which the student qualifies to the appropriate academic authority after a valid request for payment has been received. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The Department may make payments under paragraph (1) at such times and in such instalments as it sees fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department may make provisional payments under paragraph (1) in such cases as it deems appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
