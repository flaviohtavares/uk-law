# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 92

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Disabled part-time students' allowances (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dad8838bb55fda97359ec8944e101824][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this Part for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Department is satisfied he is obliged to incur by reason of a disability to which he is subject in respect of his undertaking a designated part-time course (the “disabled part-time students' allowance”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for the disabled part-time students' allowance if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which he falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for the disabled part-time students' allowance unless the Department considers that he is undertaking the designated course in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Subject to the following paragraphs, the amount of disabled part-time students' allowance under this regulation is the amount that the Department considers appropriate in accordance with the student’s circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The amount of the disabled part-time students' allowance must not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £9,105 in respect of an academic year for expenditure on a non-medical personal helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,795 in respect of all the academic years during the period of eligibility for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as a part of his course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purpose of attending the British Institute in Paris; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £1,200 in respect of an academic year for any other expenditure including expenditure incurred for the purposes referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) which exceeds the specified maxima. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
