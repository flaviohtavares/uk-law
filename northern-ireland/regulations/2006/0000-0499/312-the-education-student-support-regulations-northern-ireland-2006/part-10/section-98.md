# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 98

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 98. Payment of grants for books, travel and other expenditure and disabled part-time students' allowances (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bae56030e14f5e6782be84b5000a9752][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Payments of the grant for books, travel and other expenditure and the disabled part-time students' allowance may be made in such manner as the Department considers appropriate and it may make it a condition of entitlement to payment that the eligible part-time student must provide it with particulars of a bank or building society account in the United Kingdom into which payments may be made by electronic transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Where the Department cannot make a final assessment on the basis of the information provided by the student, it may make a provisional assessment and payment of the grant for books, travel and other expenditure and the disabled part-time students' allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department may pay the grant for books, travel and other expenditure and the disabled part-time students' allowance in instalments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), the Department may pay the grant for books, travel and other expenditure and the disabled part-time students' allowance at such times as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The Department must not pay the first instalment or, where it has been determined not to pay support in instalments, make any payment of the grant for books, travel and other expenditure or the disabled part-time students' allowance before it has received a declaration under regulation 93 unless an exception applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) An exception applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a disabled part-time students' allowance is payable in which case that particular grant may be paid before the Department has received a declaration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the Department has determined that owing to exceptional circumstances it would be appropriate to make a payment without receiving a declaration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
