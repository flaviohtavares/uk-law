# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 93

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Applications for support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-85d494544afd149e4c070e2d7a31c1fe][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (the “applicant”) must apply for support in connection with each academic year of a designated part-time course by completing and submitting to the Department an application in such form as the Department may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The application must be accompanied by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a declaration completed by the academic authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) such additional documentation as the Department may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department may take such steps and make such inquiries as it considers necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible part-time student, whether he qualifies for support and the amount of support payable, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The Department must notify the applicant of whether he qualifies for support and, if he does qualify, the amount of support payable in respect of the academic year, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The general rule is that the application must reach the Department within a period of six months beginning with the first day of the academic year of the course in respect of which it is submitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) The general rule does not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) one of the events listed in paragraph (13) of regulation 85 occurs after the first day of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support, in which case the application must reach the Department within a period of six months beginning with the day on which the relevant event occurred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fe5cc1301014a2724dc0815e5d7c60eb][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the applicant is a person mentioned in paragraph 12 of Part 2 of Schedule 1, in which case the application must reach the Department by 11th January 2008 or within a period of six months beginning with the first day of the academic year in respect of which it is submitted, whichever is the later; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the applicant is applying for the disabled part-time students' allowance, in which case the application must reach the Department as soon as is reasonably practicable; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the Department considers that having regard to the circumstances of the particular case the time limit should be relaxed, in which case the application must reach the Department not later than such date as it specifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
