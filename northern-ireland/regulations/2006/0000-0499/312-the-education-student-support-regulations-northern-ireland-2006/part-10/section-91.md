# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 91

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Assistance with fees in respect of attendance on a course in England, Wales or Scotland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-08ee1663a8d7cc0bf0d7a224e266d872][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may pay support to assist with fees to an eligible part-time student in connection with his attendance on a designated part-time course in England, Wales, or Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The assistance paid under paragraph (1) must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the maximum amount of assistance that would have been payable to the eligible part-time student under regulation 88(1)(a) had he been undertaking the part-time course in Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the maximum amount of support to assist with fees that in the opinion of the Department would have been payable to him according to whether he attends the designated part-time course in England, Wales or Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) pursuant to regulations made by the Secretary of State for Education and Skills under section 22 of the 1998 Act had he been ordinarily resident in England and undertaking the part-time course in England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] pursuant to regulations made by the National Assembly for Wales under section 22 of the 1998 Act had he been ordinarily resident in Wales and undertaking the part-time course in Wales; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] from funds of the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00066] had he been ordinarily resident in Scotland and undertaking the part-time course in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
