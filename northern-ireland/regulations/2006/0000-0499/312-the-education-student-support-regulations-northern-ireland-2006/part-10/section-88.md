# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 88

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 88. Assistance for part-time courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-07f726baef9b90cd613cfd58573bfab7][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this regulation, the assistance available is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a grant in respect of fees not exceeding the lesser of the following amounts— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the basic grant, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the “actual fees”, being the amount of fees charged to the student in respect of an academic year of the designated part-time course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a grant not exceeding £250 for books, travel and other expenditure in connection with the designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for assistance under paragraph (1)(b) if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which he falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An eligible part-time student qualifies for assistance— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) under paragraph (1)(a) if the Department considers that he is undertaking the designated part-time course in Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) under paragraph (1)(b) if the Department considers that he is undertaking the designated part-time course in a part of the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under this regulation if he has undertaken one or more part-time courses for eight academic years in aggregate and he has received in respect of each of those academic years a loan or a grant of the kind described in paragraph (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The loans and grants are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a part-time course by the Department pursuant to regulations made under Article 3 of the Order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a part-time course pursuant to regulations made under section 22 of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a loan in respect of an academic year of a part-time course made pursuant to regulations made under sections 73(f), 73B and 74(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00063].

(6) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under this regulation if he holds a first degree from an educational institution in the United Kingdom or a first degree or comparable qualification from an educational institution outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) For the purposes of paragraph (6), a degree is not to be treated as a first degree where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) it is a degree (other than an honours degree) that has been awarded to an eligible part-time student who has completed the required modules, examinations or other forms of assessment for his first degree course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the eligible part-time student is registered to continue the course at the same educational institution after the award of his degree so as to obtain an honours degree on completion of the required modules, examinations or other form of assessment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
