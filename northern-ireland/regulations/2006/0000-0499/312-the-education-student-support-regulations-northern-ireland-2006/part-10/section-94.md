# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 94

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 94. Declarations provided by academic authorities (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2437d6539902bc7d6a05da93d6ac79bb][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the appropriate academic authority must, on the request of the applicant, complete a declaration in such form as may be required by the Department to accompany the application for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An academic authority is not required to complete a declaration if it is unable to give the confirmation required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) In this Part, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**declaration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the applicant is applying for support in connection with the designated part-time course for the first time, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] confirms that the applicant has undertaken at least two weeks of the designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in any other case, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] confirms that the applicant has enrolled to undertake the academic year of the designated part-time course in respect of which he is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**course information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the amount of fees being charged in respect of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the intensity of study; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) certification by the academic authority that it considers the course to be a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
