# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 4

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5a0749585cc73db0f0dca367f9e7c76a][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for support in connection with a designated course subject to and in accordance with these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), a person is an eligible student in connection with a designated course if the Department has determined in connection with that course that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A person is not an eligible student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) an old award has been made to him in respect of his attendance on the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) he is eligible for a loan in relation to an academic year of the course under the Education (Student Loans) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 or the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) there has been made to him or paid to him in connection with the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) subject to sub-paragraph (g), a bursary or award of similar description under Article 44 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00032], (other than a bursary paid in respect of attendance on a degree in social work course at an educational institution in Northern Ireland, which the student commenced to attend on or after 1st September 2004), or section 63 of the Health Services and Public Health Act 1968 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00033] the amount of which is not calculated by reference to his income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00034];
- (d) he is in breach of any obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) he has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with him when he was under the age of 18; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) he has, in the opinion of the Department, shown himself by his conduct to be unfitted to receive support; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (g) he is, on or after 1st September 2005, commencing a course for a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland as a Regional Social Work Degree Trainee under arrangements made by the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety under Article 44 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(d) and (3)(e), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**loan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (3)(e) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) before 25th September 1991; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when he had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) An eligible student in respect of whom the first academic year of the specified designated course begins on or after 1st September 2000 may not, at any one time, qualify for support for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) more than one designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a designated course and a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a designated course and a designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) Despite paragraphs (2) and (3), a person is an eligible student for the purposes of these Regulations if he satisfies the conditions in paragraph (8), (9) or (10). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the person qualified as an eligible student in connection with an earlier academic year of the current course pursuant to regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the person’s status as an eligible student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the current course is an end-on course (other than one of the kind referred to in paragraph (c) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2) that the person begins on or after 1st September 2006; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the person qualified as an eligible student in connection with the course in relation to which the current course is an end-on course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the period of eligibility in respect of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b) only ceased on the grounds that the student had completed the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department has previously determined that the person is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) an eligible part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an eligible student in connection with a designated course other than the current course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the person’s status as an eligible part-time student or as an eligible student in connection with the course referred to in sub-paragraph (a) has been converted or transferred from that course to the current course as a result of one or more conversions or transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the person’s status as an eligible student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
