# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 5

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Designated courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f3b8271d7ce9085783ee6dc67d07c947][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a course is a designated course for the purposes of Article 3(1) of the Order and regulation 4 if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) mentioned in Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) one of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) a full-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a sandwich course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a part-time course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) of at least one academic year’s duration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) wholly provided by a publicly-funded educational institution or institutions in the United Kingdom or by a relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland which (in either case) are maintained or assisted by recurrent grants out of public funds or provided by such institution or institutions in conjunction with an institution or institutions outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A course that is taken as part of an employment-based teacher training scheme is not a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a course is provided by an institution if it provides the teaching and supervision which comprise the course, whether or not the institution has entered into an agreement with the student to provide the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a university and any constituent college or institution in the nature of a college of a university is to be regarded as publicly funded if either the university or the constituent college or institution is publicly funded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) an institution is not to be regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00035].

(4) A course to which this paragraph applies is considered to be a single course for a first degree or for an equivalent qualification even though— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the course may lead to another degree or qualification being conferred before the degree or equivalent qualification; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) part of the course may be optional. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) Paragraph (4) applies to a course the standard of which is not higher than a first degree which leads to a qualification as a medical doctor, dentist, veterinary doctor, architect, landscape architect, landscape designer, landscape manager, town planner or town and country planner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) For the purposes of Article 3 of the Order and regulation 4(1) the Department may designate courses of higher education which are not designated under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
