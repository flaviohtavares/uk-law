# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 6

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b87ce399cd841628422be5ff520b5dec][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student retains his status as an eligible student in connection with a designated course until the status terminates in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The period for which an eligible student retains the status is the “period of eligibility”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the academic year in which the student completes the designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) withdraws from his designated course in circumstances where the Department has not transferred or converted or will not transfer or convert his status as an eligible student under regulation 7 or regulation 97; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from his designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The Department may terminate the period of eligibility where the eligible student has shown himself by his conduct to be unfitted to receive support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) If the Department is satisfied that an eligible student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under these Regulations or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Department may take such of the following actions as it considers appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for any particular support or particular amount of support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulation 84. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) Where the period of eligibility terminates before the end of the academic year in which the student completes the designated course, the Department may, at any time, renew the period of eligibility for such period as it determines. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
