# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 7

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Transfer of status (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8da5671e125bf5c019dc624e8cf80a8b][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an eligible student transfers to another course, the Department must transfer the student’s status as an eligible student to that course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) it receives a request from the eligible student to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) it is satisfied that one or more of the grounds for transfer in paragraph (2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the period of eligibility has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2)[^c-f6f38dc51bed983f73b54dc327964e68] The grounds for transfer are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) on the recommendation of the academic authority the eligible student starts to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) attend another designated course at the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] undertake another compressed degree course in the United Kingdom at the institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] undertake a compressed degree course in the United Kingdom at the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the eligible student starts to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) attend a designated course at another institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] undertake a compressed degree course in the United Kingdom at another institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) after starting a course for the Certificate in Education, the eligible student is, on or before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated course for the degree of Bachelor of Education either at the same institution or at another institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) after starting a course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education, the eligible student is, on or before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated course for the honours degree of Bachelor of Education either at the same institution or another institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) after starting a course for a first degree (other than an honours degree) the eligible student is, before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated course for an honours degree in the same subject or subjects at the institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), an eligible student who transfers under paragraph (1) shall receive in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers the remainder of the support assessed by the Department in respect of the academic year of the course from which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The Department may re-assess the amount of support payable after the transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An eligible student who transfers under paragraph (1) after the Department has assessed his support in connection with the academic year of the course from which he is transferring but before he completes that year may not, in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers, apply for another grant or loan of a kind that he has already applied for under these Regulations in connection with the academic year of the course from which he is transferring unless otherwise provided. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
