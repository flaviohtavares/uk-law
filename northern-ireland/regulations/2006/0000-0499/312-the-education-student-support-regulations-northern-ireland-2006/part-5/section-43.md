# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 43

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Childcare grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-20265d27b42737d8d029e3b5cb2f720e][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student qualifies, in connection with his attendance on a designated course, for a grant in respect of childcare costs for each dependent child in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), an eligible student qualifies for a childcare grant in respect of an academic year where childcare is provided by an approved or registered childcare provider if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the child is under the age of 15 immediately before the beginning of the academic year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the child has special educational needs within the meaning of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00045] and is under the age of 17 immediately before the beginning of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this regulation if he or his partner has elected to receive the childcare element of the working tax credit under Part I of the Tax Credits Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00046].

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), the basic amount of childcare grant for each week is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for one dependent child, 85 per cent. of the costs of the childcare, subject to a maximum amount of £148.75 per week; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) for two or more dependent children, 85 per cent. of the costs of the childcare, subject to a maximum amount of £255 per week *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

except that the student does not qualify for any such grant in respect of each week falling within the period between the end of the course and the end of the academic year in which the course ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) For the purposes of calculating the basic amount of childcare grant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a week runs from Monday to Sunday; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where a week in respect of which childcare costs are incurred falls partly within and partly outside the academic year in respect of which childcare grant is payable under this regulation, the maximum weekly amount of grant is calculated by multiplying the relevant maximum weekly amount in paragraph (4) by the number of days of that week falling within the academic year and dividing the product by seven. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- “**approved childcare provider *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means a childcare provider approved in accordance with the Tax Credit (New Category of Child Care Provider) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00047]or the Tax Credits (Approval of Child Care Providers) Scheme 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00048]or the Tax Credits (Approval of Home Child Care Providers) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00049]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- “**registered childcare provider *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means a person who acts as a child minder or provides day care and is registered within the meaning of Articles 118, 119 and 120 of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00050] (registration of child minders and persons providing day care for young children). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
