# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 41

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. General (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-59bf68b2569b737798941608ff7820a1][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The grant for dependants consists of the following elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) adult dependants' grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) childcare grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) parents' learning allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element and the amounts payable are set out in regulations 42 to 45. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
