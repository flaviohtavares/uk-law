# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. Qualifying conditions for the special support grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-51d9d073e9f104f66fd601bc5e433d04][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A new system student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a special support grant in connection with his attendance on a designated course to defray the costs of books, equipment, travel or childcare incurred for the purpose of attending that course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A new system student qualifies for a special support grant if he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) falls within a prescribed category of person for the purposes of section 124(1)(e) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00055]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) is treated as being liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling prescribed by regulations made under section 130(2) of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00056].

(3) If a new system student does not qualify for a fee loan in respect of an academic year of the designated course, he cannot qualify for a special support grant for that year unless the reason that he does not qualify for a fee loan is because— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the year is an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
