# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 50

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Amount of the maintenance grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a3c37fb47342865dbbe066b1596d283f][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The maximum amount of maintenance grant available in respect of an academic year is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) in the case of a type 1 teacher training student, £1,600; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in the case of a type 2 teacher training student, £3,200; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) in the case of a new system student other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student, £3,200. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A type 1 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the household income is £17,500 or less, he receives £1,600; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £17,500 but does not exceed £26,500, he receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

  where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

  - *M* is £1,600 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.50 by which the household income exceeds £17,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £26,500 or he opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, he receives £600. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A type 2 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the household income is £17,500 or less, he receives £3,200; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £17,500 but does not exceed £26,500, he receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *M* − *A* where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *M* is £3,200 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.50 by which the household income exceeds £17,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £26,500 or he opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, he receives £1,200. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A new system student other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the household income is £17,500 or less, he receives £3,200; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £17,500 but does not exceed £26,500, he receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *M* − *A*where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *M* is £3,200 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.50 by which the household income exceeds £17,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £26,500 but does not exceed £37,425, he receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *RM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* − *A*where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *RM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* is £1,200 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £9.50 by which the household income exceeds £26,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) where the household income exceeds £37,425, no maintenance grant is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
