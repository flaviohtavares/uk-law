# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 49

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Qualifying conditions for the maintenance grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-46888b5e46b33691ea70566f6dd527fd][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A new system student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a maintenance grant in connection with his attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A new system student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if he qualifies for a special support grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) If a new system student does not qualify for a fee loan in respect of an academic year of the designated course, he cannot qualify for a maintenance grant for that year unless the reason that he does not qualify for a fee loan is because— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the year is an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
