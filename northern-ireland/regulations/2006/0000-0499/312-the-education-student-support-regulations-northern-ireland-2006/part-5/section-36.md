# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 36

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Old system students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-693995b81c1866e82d01628a073bd748][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following grants are available to an old system student in connection with a designated course if he meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) disabled students' allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) grant for students who have left care; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) grant for dependants; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) grant for travel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) higher education bursaries. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
