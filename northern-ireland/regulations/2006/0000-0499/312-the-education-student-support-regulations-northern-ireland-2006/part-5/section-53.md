## 53. Qualifying conditions for and amounts of the higher education bursary (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-517ec439cd8a1c04c032f1aadbeba651][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-e9e0bb0df2f91754b434adfb1bdcb54c] Subject to paragraph (2), an old system student who qualifies for a grant for fees under regulation 21 and who is under the age of 60 on the relevant date qualifies for a higher education bursary in accordance with his attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) If an old system student does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of the designated course, he cannot qualify for a higher education bursary for that year unless the reason that he does not qualify for a grant for fees is because— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the year is an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The amount of bursary payable in respect of any academic year shall be determined by reference to the household income ascertained under paragraph 3 of Schedule 4 in accordance with the following table— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
