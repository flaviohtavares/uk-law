# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 46

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Interpretation of Chapter 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5ceca7c7020c5cad1279eb0193b6d2d1][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In regulations 41 to 45— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) “**adult dependant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means, in relation to an eligible student, an adult person dependent on the student other than his child, his partner (including a spouse or civil partner from whom the Department considers the student is separated) or his former partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) “**child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” in relation to an eligible student includes any child of his partner who is dependent on him and any child for whom he has parental responsibility who is dependent on him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) “**dependant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means, in relation to an eligible student, his partner, his dependent child or an adult dependant, who in each case is not an eligible student and does not hold a statutory award; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) “**dependent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means wholly or mainly financially dependent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) “**lone parent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means an eligible student who does not have a partner and who has a dependent child or dependent children; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) “**net income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” has the meaning given in paragraph (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (g) subject to sub-paragraphs (h), (i) and (j), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the spouse of an eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the civil partner of an eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a person ordinarily living with an eligible student as if he were his spouse where an eligible student falls within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 4 and began the specified designated course on or after 1st September 2000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a person ordinarily living with an eligible student as if he were his civil partner where an eligible student falls within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 4 and began the specified designated course on or after 1st September 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (h) unless otherwise indicated, a person who would otherwise be a partner under sub-paragraph (g) is not to be treated as a partner if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) in the opinion of the Department, that person and the eligible student are separated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the person is ordinarily living outside the United Kingdom and is not maintained by the eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (i) for the purposes of the definition of “adult dependant”, a person is to be treated as a partner if he would be a partner under sub-paragraph (g) but for the fact that the eligible student with whom he is ordinarily living does not fall within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (j) for the purposes of the definitions of “child” and “lone parent”, a person is to be treated as a partner if he would be a partner under sub-paragraph (g) but for the date on which the eligible student began the specified designated course or the fact that the eligible student with whom he is ordinarily living does not fall within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), a dependant’s net income is his income from all sources for the academic year in question reduced by the amount of income tax and social security contributions payable in respect of it but disregarding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) any pension, allowance or other benefit paid by reason of a disability or incapacity to which the dependant is subject; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) child benefit payable under Part IX of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00051];
- (c) any financial support payable to the dependant by an adoption agency in accordance with regulations made under Article 59A of the Adoption (Northern Ireland) Order 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00052];
- (d) any guardian’s allowance to which the dependant is entitled under section 77 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) in the case of a dependant with whom a child in the care of a Health and Social Services Board or a Health and Social Services Trust is boarded out, any payment made to him in pursuance of Article 27(2)(a) of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00053];
- (f) any payments made to the dependant under Article 15 of and Schedule 1 to the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 in respect of a person who is not the dependant’s child or any assistance given by a Health and Social Services Board or Health and Social Services Trust pursuant to Articles 34A, 34C, 35A and 35B of that Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00054]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (g) any child tax credit to which the dependant is entitled under Part I of the Tax Credits Act 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where an eligible student or his partner makes any recurrent payments which were previously made by the student in pursuance of an obligation incurred before the first academic year of the student’s course, the partner’s net income is the net income calculated in accordance with paragraph (2) reduced by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) an amount equal to the payments in question for the academic year, if in the opinion of the Department the obligation had been reasonably incurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) such lesser amount, if any, as the Department considers appropriate if, in its opinion, a lesser obligation could reasonably have been incurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (2), where the dependant is a dependent child and payments are made to the eligible student towards the child’s maintenance, those payments are to be treated as the child’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
