# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 48

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 48. Amount of the grant for travel (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d21da76598d93e08dc941789f264dce0][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amount of grant payable in respect of an academic year is equal to the reasonable expenditure that the Department determines the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purposes set out in regulation 47(1) or (2) less £285. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter any reference to expenditure incurred for the purpose of attending an institution or period of study— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) includes expenditure both before and after so attending; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) does not include any expenditure in respect of which a grant is payable under Chapter 3 of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where an eligible student attends for a period of at least eight weeks as part of his course an overseas institution or the British Institute in Paris and he reasonably incurs any expenditure in insuring against liability for the cost of medical treatment provided outside the United Kingdom for any illness or bodily injury contracted or suffered during that period he qualifies for additional grant under this regulation equal to the amount so incurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A deduction may be made from a grant under this Chapter in accordance with Part 8. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
