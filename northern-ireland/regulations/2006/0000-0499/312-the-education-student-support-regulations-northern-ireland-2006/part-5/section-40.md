# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 40

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Grants for students who have left care (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9da2c4688db9557ed69ad8b560dd7fe4][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student shall qualify for a grant under this regulation in connection with his attendance on a designated course if the conditions in paragraph (2) are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The conditions referred to in paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the eligible student is under the age of 21 on the first day of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the eligible student has left care, that is to say last ceased to fall within paragraph 2(f) of Schedule 4 before 1st September 2005; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) in the opinion of the Department, the eligible student is subject to greater financial hardship by virtue of falling within paragraph 2(f) of Schedule 4 than he would otherwise have been. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), the amount of grant shall be such amount as the Department considers appropriate in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The maximum amount of grant is £100 for each week or part of a week in an academic year which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) falls within the longest vacation taken; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) during no part of which week the student attends his course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
