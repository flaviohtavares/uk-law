# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 42

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Adult dependants' grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bc9964a6090a5d51cae41cf1b7baad42][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for an adult dependants' grant in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The adult dependants' grant is available in respect of a dependant of an eligible student who is either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the eligible student’s partner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) an adult dependant whose net income does not exceed £3,530. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The amount of adult dependants' grant payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulation 45, the basic amount being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £2,455; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the person in respect of whom the eligible student is applying for adult dependants' grant is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, such amount not exceeding £2,455 as the Department considers reasonable in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
