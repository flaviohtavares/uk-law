# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 37

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. General qualifying conditions for grants for living and other costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1b1c9bbec575372e7fc0629ccec381a0][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for a grant under this Part provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) he is not excluded from qualification by any of the following paragraphs; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) he satisfies the qualifying conditions for the particular grant for which he is applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An eligible student does not qualify for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a bursary or grant under this Part if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which he falls is paragraph 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a bursary under regulation 53 in respect of any academic year of a part-time course for the initial training of teachers referred to in paragraph 4 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of any academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) during which he is eligible to receive any bursary or award of similar description under Article 44 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, or section 63 of the Health Services and Public Health Act 1968 the amount of which is calculated by reference to his income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) during which he is eligible to receive a Scottish healthcare allowance the amount of which is calculated by reference to his income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) of a course for the initial training of teachers during which the periods of full-time attendance, including attendance for the purpose of teaching practice, are in aggregate less than 6 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Paragraph (3)(c) does not apply for the purposes of the disabled students' allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs under this Part in respect of any academic year of a sandwich course where the periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks unless the periods of work experience constitute unpaid service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (5), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**unpaid service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) unpaid service in a hospital or in a public health service laboratory or with a primary care trust in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) unpaid service with a local authority in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland acting in the exercise of its functions relating to the care of children and young persons, health or welfare or with a voluntary organisation providing facilities or carrying out activities of a like nature in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) unpaid service in the prison or probation and aftercare service in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) unpaid research in an institution in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland or, in the case of a student attending an overseas institution as part of his course, in an overseas institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) unpaid service with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) a Health and Social Services Board established under Article 16 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a Health and Social Services Trust established under Article 10 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00040];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a Health Authority or a Strategic Health Authority established pursuant to section 8 of the National Health Service Act 1977 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00041], a Special Health Authority established pursuant to section 11 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00042] or a Local Health Board established pursuant to section 16BA of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00043];or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a Health Board or a Special Health Board constituted under section 2 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00044].

(7) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (8) occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a particular grant in accordance with this Part in respect of all or part of that academic year but he does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student, his spouse, his civil partner or his parent is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the European Community where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and Islands throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) the student acquires the right of permanent residence as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b85c08077bea97cc3d766d8a0e1d522f][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9)[^c-1d937ef86714e06160b5486988878194] A student to whom this paragraph applies is treated as if he were in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for the following grants— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) grant for the students who have left care; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) grants for dependants; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) maintenance grant or special support grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) higher education bursary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) Paragraph (9) applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a compressed degree student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a disabled student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland but is not in attendance because he is unable to attend for a reason which relates to his disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
