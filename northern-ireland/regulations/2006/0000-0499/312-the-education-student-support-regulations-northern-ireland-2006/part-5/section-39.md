# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 39

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Amount of the disabled students' allowance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-27d9b33f053b004aff992480de75b32d][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs, the amount of the disabled students' allowance is the amount that the Department considers appropriate in accordance with the student’s circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Except where paragraph (4) applies, the amount of the disabled students' allowance must not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £12,135 in respect of an academic year for expenditure on a non-medical personal helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,795 in respect of all the academic years during the period of eligibility for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as a part of his course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purpose of attending the British Institute in Paris; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £1,605 in respect of an academic year for any other expenditure including expenditure incurred for the purposes referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) which exceeds the specified maxima. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where the eligible student has received payments to assist with expenditure on major items of specialist equipment in connection with the course by virtue of holding a transitional award, the maximum amount of grant under paragraph (2)(b) is reduced by the amount of those payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The maximum amount under paragraph (2)(a) and (d) is £9,105 and £1,200, respectively where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) an eligible student undertakes a course for the initial training of teachers; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in any academic year of that course, the periods of full-time study and full-time teaching practice are in aggregate less than 6 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
