# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 101

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 101. Eligible postgraduate students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3fc4e3fdda865f31d1f122cf2a5e3632][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible postgraduate student qualifies, subject to and in accordance with this Part, for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Department is satisfied he is obliged to incur by reason of a disability to which he is subject in respect of his undertaking a designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), a person is an eligible postgraduate student in connection with a designated postgraduate course if he satisfies the conditions in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department has determined in connection with the designated postgraduate course that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the Department is satisfied that, by reason of a disability to which the person is subject, he will be obliged to incur additional expenditure in respect of his undertaking the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A person is not an eligible postgraduate student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) there has been made to him or paid to him in connection with the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) a bursary or award of similar description under Article 44 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 or section 63 of the Health Services and Public Health Act 1968; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00067];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made by a Research Council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made by his institution which includes any payment for the purpose of meeting additional expenditure incurred by the student by reason of his disability; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (v) any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made under section 14 (4) (a) of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00068] which includes payment for meeting additional expenditure incurred by the student by reason of his disability; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) he is in breach of an obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) he has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with him when he was under the age of 18; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) he has, in the opinion of the Department, shown himself by his conduct to be unfitted to receive support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (4)(b) and (c), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**loan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (4)(c) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) before 25th September 1991; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when he had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) An eligible postgraduate student does not qualify for a grant under this Part if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which he falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) An eligible postgraduate student does not qualify for a grant under this Part unless the Department considers that he is undertaking his course in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) Despite paragraphs (3)(a) and (4), a person is an eligible postgraduate student for the purposes of this Part if he satisfies the conditions in paragraph (3)(b) and in paragraph (10) or (11). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the person qualified as an eligible postgraduate student in connection with an earlier academic year of the current designated postgraduate course pursuant to regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the person’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(11) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department has previously determined that the person is an eligible postgraduate student in connection with a designated postgraduate course other than the current designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student’s status as an eligible postgraduate student in connection with the course referred to in sub-paragraph (a) has been transferred from that course to the current designated postgraduate course as a result of one or more transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the person’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(12) An eligible postgraduate student may not, at any one time, qualify for support for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) more than one designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a designated postgraduate course and a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a designated postgraduate course and a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(13) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (14) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a grant of the kind available under this Part is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(14) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student, his spouse, his civil partner or his parent is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the European Community where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and Islands throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) the student acquires the right of permanent residence as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-92d64366619fa0dc47c8dc4d2a7052bf][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
