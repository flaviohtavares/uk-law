# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 103

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 103. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d132c2d83b61c56430d9985091259604][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible postgraduate student retains his status as an eligible postgraduate student in connection with a designated postgraduate course until the status is terminated in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The period for which an eligible postgraduate student retains the status is the “period of eligibility”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the period ordinarily required for the completion of the designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible postgraduate student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) withdraws from his designated postgraduate course in circumstances where the Department has not transferred or will not transfer his status as an eligible postgraduate student to another course under regulation 104; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from his designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The Department may terminate the period of eligibility where the eligible postgraduate student has shown himself by his conduct to be unfitted to receive support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) If the Department is satisfied that an eligible postgraduate student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under this Part or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Department may take such of the following actions as it considers appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for a grant or any particular amount of grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulation 109. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) Where the period of eligibility terminates on or before the expiry of the period ordinarily required for the completion of the designated postgraduate course, the Department may, at any time, renew the period of eligibility for such period as it determines. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
