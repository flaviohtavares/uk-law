# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 109

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 109. Overpayments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-705a8a2728bc2ddb3e64c4471935aab2][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible postgraduate student must, if so required by the Department, repay any amount paid to him under this Part which for whatever reason exceeds the amount of grant to which he is entitled under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The Department must recover an overpayment of grant under this Part unless it considers it is not appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The methods of recovery are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) subtracting the overpayment from any kind of grant payable to the student from time to time pursuant to regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) taking such other action for the recovery of an overpayment as is available to the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A payment of grant under this Part made before the relevant date is an overpayment if the student withdraws from the course before the relevant date unless the Department decides otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) In this regulation, the “relevant date” is the date on which the first term of the academic year in question actually begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In the circumstances in paragraphs (7) and (8), there is an overpayment of grant under this Part unless the Department decides otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department applies all or part of the grant under this Part to the purchase of specialist equipment on behalf of the eligible postgraduate student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course after the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the equipment has not been delivered to the student before he withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the eligible postgraduate student withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course after the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a payment of grant under this Part in respect of specialist equipment is made to the student after he withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) Where there is an overpayment of the grant under this Part, the Department may accept the return of specialist equipment purchased with the grant by way of recovery of all or part of the overpayment if it considers it is appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
