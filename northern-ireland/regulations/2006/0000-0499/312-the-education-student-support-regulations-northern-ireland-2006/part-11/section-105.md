# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 105

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105. Applications for support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-74ed30ed52071fb187bb982a75da0465][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (the “applicant”) must apply for a grant under this Part in connection with each academic year of a designated postgraduate course by completing and submitting to the Department an application in such form and accompanied by such documentation as the Department may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The application must reach the Department as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department may take such steps and make such inquiries as it considers necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible postgraduate student, whether he qualifies for a grant and the amount of grant payable, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The Department must notify the applicant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) whether he qualifies for a grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) if he does qualify, the amount payable in respect of the academic year, if any; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) how that amount is allocated between the types of eligible expenditure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
