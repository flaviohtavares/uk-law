# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 104

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 104. Transfer of status (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6e8b9a8f956adfea1302a6a6d505a4ff][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an eligible postgraduate student transfers to another postgraduate course, the Department must transfer the student’s status as an eligible postgraduate student to that course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) it receives a request from the eligible postgraduate student to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) it is satisfied that one or more of the grounds for transfer in paragraph (2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the period of eligibility has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The grounds for transfer are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) on the recommendation of the academic authority the eligible postgraduate student starts to undertake another designated postgraduate course at the institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the eligible postgraduate student starts to undertake a designated postgraduate course at another institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), an eligible postgraduate student who transfers under paragraph (1) shall receive in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers the remainder of the support for which the Department has determined he qualifies in respect of the academic year of the course from which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The Department may re-assess the amount of support payable after the transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An eligible student who transfers under paragraph (1) after the Department has determined his support in connection with the academic year of the course from which he is transferring but before he completes that year may not apply for another grant under this Part in connection with the academic year of the course to which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
