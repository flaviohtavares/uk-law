# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 80

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Payment of fee loans and fee contribution loans (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a265b1414bb7b67efa66c5d8afe03103][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must pay the fee loan or fee contribution loan for which an eligible student qualifies to an institution to which the student is liable to make payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The Department may pay the fee loan or fee contribution loan in instalments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department must not pay the fee loan or fee contribution loan before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) it has received a valid request for payment from the academic authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a period of three months beginning with the first day of the academic year has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Where assessment of an old system student’s contribution or other matters have delayed the final calculation of the amount of fee contribution loan for which the student qualifies, the Department may make a provisional assessment and payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) No payment of fee loan or fee contribution loan can be made in respect of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) before the expiry of a period of three months beginning with the first day of the academic year the eligible student ceases to attend *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] or, in the case of a student treated as in attendance under regulation 15, undertake[^c-97cd05b89b1199ad50833e5a4dbff7e9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the academic authority has determined or agreed that the student will not commence attending *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] in the UK or the Republic of Ireland, or, as the case may be, undertaking in the United Kingdom[^c-6d81be098ba98bffda4df797f9bf1758] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] again during the academic year in respect of which the fees are payable or at all. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
