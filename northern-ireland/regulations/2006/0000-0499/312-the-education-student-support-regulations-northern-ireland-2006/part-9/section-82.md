# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 82

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 82. Payment of bursaries and grants for living and other costs and loans for living costs – timing of payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7f61153c454d2bbea330bb2b7176e760][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may pay support under Part 5 or Part 6 in instalments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the Department may pay support under Part 5 or Part 6 at such times as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where an institution is required to send an attendance confirmation to the Department, the Department must not pay the first instalment or, where it has been determined not to pay support under Part 5 or Part 6 by instalments, make any payment of support under Part 5 or Part 6 to the eligible student before it has received that confirmation unless an exception applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) An exception applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a disabled students' allowance is payable in which case that particular grant may be paid before the Department has received an attendance confirmation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the Department has determined that owing to exceptional circumstances it would be appropriate to make a payment without receiving an attendance confirmation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An institution is required to send an attendance confirmation to the Department in respect of an academic year of the current course where the year begins on or after 1st September 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**attendance confirmation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) confirmation from the institution that the student has enrolled for the academic year where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the student is applying for support in connection with a designated course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student has a disability; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student is undertaking the course but not attending (regardless of whether the reason for not attending relates to his disability); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) confirmation from the institution that the student has presented himself at the institution and begun to attend the course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the student is applying for support in connection with a designated course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student has not had his status as an eligible student transferred to the course from another designated course at the same institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] sub-paragraph (a)(iii) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) confirmation from the institution that the student has enrolled for the academic year where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the student is applying for support in connection with a designated course other than for the first time; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student is applying for support in connection with a designated course for the first time after having his status as an eligible student transferred to that course from another course at the same institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
