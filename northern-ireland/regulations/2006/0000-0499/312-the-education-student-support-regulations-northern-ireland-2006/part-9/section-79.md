# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 79

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Payment of grants for fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5e755ef279f7d57032babc5f36e32132][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must not pay the grant for fees for which a student qualifies until it has received a valid request for payment from the academic authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Payment must be made to the academic authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) not before the expiry of a period of three months beginning with the first day of the academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) not later than 10 weeks after the expiry of the period in sub-paragraph (a), or promptly after a valid request for payment has been received, if that is later. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where assessment of the student’s contribution or other matters have delayed the final calculation of the amount of grant for fees for which the student qualifies, the Department may make a provisional assessment and payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) No payment of the grant for fees can be made in respect of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) before the expiry of a period of three months beginning with the first day of the academic year the eligible student ceases to attend *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] or, in the case of a student treated as in attendance under regulation 15, undertake[^c-aa6d17165e2df2d91fccec0546a6b9e4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the academic authority has determined or agreed that the student will not commence attending *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland, or, as the case may be, undertaking in the United Kingdom[^c-1d11c078fb3d4045a7b9c5efd8927474] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] again during the academic year in respect of which the fees are payable or at all. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
