# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 84

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 84. Overpayments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c5dd9d93331fe94852e8ec74a2188d18][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any overpayment of fee support is recoverable by the Department from the academic authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An eligible student must, if so required by the Department, repay any amount paid to him under Part 5 or 6 which for whatever reason exceeds the amount of support to which he is entitled under Part 5 or 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The Department must recover an overpayment of any grant for living and other costs unless it considers it is not appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The methods of recovery are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) subtracting the overpayment from any kind of grant payable to the student from time to time pursuant to regulations made by the Department under Article 3 of the Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) taking such other action for the recovery of an overpayment as is available to the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) A payment of any bursary or grant for living and other costs made before the relevant date is an overpayment if the student withdraws from the course before the relevant date unless the Department decides otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In this regulation, the “relevant date” is the date on which the first term of the academic year in question actually begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) In the circumstances in paragraph (8) or (9), there is an overpayment of the disabled students' allowance unless the Department decides otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department applies all or part of the disabled students' allowance to the purchase of specialist equipment on behalf of the eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course after the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the equipment has not been delivered to the student before he withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the eligible student withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course after the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a payment of the disabled students' allowance in respect of specialist equipment is made to the student after he withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) Where there is an overpayment of the disabled students' allowance, the Department may accept the return of specialist equipment purchased with the grant by way of recovery of all or part of the overpayment if it considers it is appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(11) Any overpayment of a loan for living costs in respect of any academic year may be recovered if in the opinion of the Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the overpayment is a result of a failure of the student to provide promptly information which might affect whether he qualifies for a loan for living costs or the amount of loan for living costs for which he qualifies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) any information which the student has provided is inaccurate in a material particular; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the student has failed to provide information which the Department considers to be material in the context of the recovery of the loan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(12) Where an overpayment of a loan for living costs is recoverable under paragraph (11), it may be recovered in whichever one or more of the following ways the Department considers appropriate in all the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) by subtracting the overpayment from the amount of any loan for living costs payable to the student from time to time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) by taking such other action for the recovery of an overpayment as is available to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(13) Where there has been an overpayment of a loan for living costs which is not recoverable under paragraph (11), the Department may subtract the overpayment from the amount of any loan for living costs payable to the student from time to time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
