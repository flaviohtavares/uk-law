## 60. Old and new system students with reduced entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7547412ead16a78a0411a48c22b824d9][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 37(3)(a) or (b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,620; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] for a student in category B, £3,030; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] for a student in category C, D or E, £2,160. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 37(3)(c) or (5)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,620; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (c) where a new system student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *X* − *Y*where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - *X* is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
    - (i) for a student in category A, £2,560; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
    - (ii) for a student in category B, £4,630; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
    - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £3,940; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
    - (iv) for a student in category E, £3,305; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (d) the specified amount is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) £600 where the student is a type 1 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £600; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] £1,200 where the student is a type 2 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £1,200; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] nil where the student is not a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) Where an old system student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide information needed to calculate the household income— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1,960; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] for a student in category B, £4,715; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] for a student in category E, £2,950. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
