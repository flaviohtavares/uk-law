# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 71

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71. Interpretation of Part 6 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fbad94e346776dea7e43899a8851ffba][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a “new system student with full entitlement” is a new system student other than a student with reduced entitlement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) an “old system student with full entitlement” is an old system student other than a student with reduced entitlement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]**” means the first day of the first academic year of the specified designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) a “student with reduced entitlement” is an eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in respect of the academic year because of regulation 37(3)(a) or (b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in respect of the academic year because of regulation 37(3)(c) or (5); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] opts when applying for a loan for living costs not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) where the duration of a graduate-entry or postgraduate-level course for the initial training of teachers is only one academic year, that year is not to be treated as the final year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
