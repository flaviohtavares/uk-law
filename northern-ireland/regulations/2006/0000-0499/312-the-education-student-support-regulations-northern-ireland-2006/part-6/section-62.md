# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 62

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Quarters in respect of which the loan for living costs is payable (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0f517a40cdbb72170e82dfeec7aaf211][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 65, the loan for living costs is payable in respect of three quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2)[^key-1dcfd21ed24506bff700a9ecb0b5122e] The loan for living costs is not payable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) in the case of a compressed degree student, in respect of the quarter nominated by the Secretary of State for Education and Skills; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in any other case, in respect of the quarter in which, in the opinion of the Department, the longest of any vacation occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
