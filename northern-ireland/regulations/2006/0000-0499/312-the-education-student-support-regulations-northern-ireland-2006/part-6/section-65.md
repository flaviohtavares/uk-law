# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 65

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 65. Students becoming eligible in the course of an academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8eebf601f2776a99106f2c1e648946ea][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (2) occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a loan for living costs in respect of such quarters in respect of which a loan for living costs is payable as begin after the relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student, his spouse, his civil partner or his parent is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a state accedes to the European Community where the student is a national of that state or a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of a national of that state; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) the student becomes a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of an EC national; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e83d5629d1ae6d6c1cb75648cb11a82][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) the student acquires the right of permanent residence (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An eligible student to whom paragraph (1) applies does not qualify for a loan for living costs in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The maximum amount of loan for living costs payable is the aggregate of the maximum amount of loan for living costs for each quarter in respect of which the student qualifies for the loan for living costs under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The maximum amount of loan for living costs for each quarter is one third of the maximum amount of loan for living costs which would apply for the academic year if the student fell into the category which applies to the relevant quarter for the duration of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
