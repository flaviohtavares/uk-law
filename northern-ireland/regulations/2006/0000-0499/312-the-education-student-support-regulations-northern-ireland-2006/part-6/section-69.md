# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 69

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 69. Applying for an additional amount of loan for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b4386d2d909d96243f2585c9df18e4f9][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount of loan for living costs where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department determines that the maximum amount of loan for living costs in relation to an academic year should be increased (including an increase from nil) as a result of a reassessment of the student’s contribution or otherwise; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the Department considers that the increase in the maximum amount does not result from the eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) failing to provide information promptly which might affect his ability to qualify for a loan for living costs or the amount of loan for living costs for which he qualifies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] providing information that is inaccurate in any material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The additional amount under paragraph (1) is an amount which when added to the amount already applied for does not exceed the increased maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where an eligible student has applied for a loan for living costs of less than the maximum amount to which he is entitled in relation to the academic year, he may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed the relevant maximum applicable in his case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
