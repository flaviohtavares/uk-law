# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 58

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 58. Type 1 and type 2 teacher training students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-be76a564f24066929f1cc9d9e0374e92][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a new system student with full entitlement who is a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year (other than the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course) is — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,415; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,170; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) for a student in category C or D, £5,255; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) for a student in category E, £4,405. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,085; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) for a student in category B, £5,620; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) for a student in category C or D, £4,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) for a student in category E, £4,080. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
