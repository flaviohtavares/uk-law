# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 63

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 63. Students falling into more than one category (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-33bed8ee066cc846e5427e8728972e69][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an eligible student falls into more than one of the categories set out in regulation 70 in the course of the academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the maximum amount of loan for living costs for the academic year is the aggregate of the maximum amount of loan for living costs for each quarter in respect of which the loan for living costs is payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the maximum amount of loan for living costs for each such quarter is one third of the maximum amount of loan for living costs which would apply for the academic year if the student fell into the category which applies to the relevant quarter for the duration of the academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) subject to paragraph (2), the category which applies to a quarter is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) the category into which the student falls for the longer or longest period in that quarter; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] if the student falls into more than one category for an equal period in that quarter, the category with the higher or highest rate of loan for living costs for the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Category C cannot be the category applicable to a quarter unless the student is attending an overseas institution for at least half of the period covered by that quarter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
