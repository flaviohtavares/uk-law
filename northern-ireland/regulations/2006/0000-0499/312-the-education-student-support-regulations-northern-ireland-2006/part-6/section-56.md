# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 56

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. General (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70003794db65c9aca45aa2d2368b944f][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The maximum amount of a loan for living costs in respect of an academic year is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the eligible student is a new system student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 57 or 58; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the eligible student is an old system student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 59; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) where the eligible student is a student with reduced entitlement, in accordance with regulation 60. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
