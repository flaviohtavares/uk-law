# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 59

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Old system students with full entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-386a1ac092a6de6d166a24ba8d7af73d][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year (other than the final year of a course that is not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course[^c-d1fde0b9adff580eef6be22631c7592e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] ) is equal to X – Y where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

X is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,415; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) for a student in category B, £6,170; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) for a student in category C or D, £5,255; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) for a student in category E, £4,405; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

Y is the reduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]is equal to X – Y where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

X is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for a student in category A, £3,085; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) for a student in category B, £5,620; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) for a student in category C or D, £4,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) for a student in category E, £4,080; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

Y is the reduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) In this regulation, “the reduction” is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) for students eligible for the maximum bursary of £2,000, £1,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in all other cases the amount of bursary for which the student is eligible, subject to paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Where the maximum amount of loans set out in paragraphs (1) and (2) are reduced under paragraph (3), such reductions shall not have effect so as to reduce the amount of loan below the amount applicable to the student under regulation 60(1)(e) or (2)(e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
