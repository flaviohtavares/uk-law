# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 70

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Categories of student (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d7347c939c0d3f24101e4b338c526477][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a student is in category A if he resides at his parents' home while attending the designated course or if he is a member of a religious order who resides in a house of that order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a student is in category B if he is not in category A and he attends one or more of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) a course at the University of London; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a course at an institution which requires attendance for at least half the time in aggregate of any quarter of the course in the academic year at a site wholly or partly within the area comprising the City of London and the former Metropolitan Police District; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] a sandwich course at an institution which requires the eligible student to undertake work experience or a combination of work experience and study provided that he undertakes such work experience or combination of work experience and study for at least half the time in aggregate of any quarter of the course in the academic year at a site or sites wholly or partly within the area comprising the City of London and the former Metropolitan Police District; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a student is in category C if he is not in category A and he attends an overseas institution as part of his course for at least eight consecutive weeks in the academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) a student is in category D if he is not in category A and he attends the British Institute in Paris; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) a student is in category E if he is not in categories A to D. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
