# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 57

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. New system students with full entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e98380c39fe01bd8211f774ed8f68b8b][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a new system student with full entitlement (other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year (other than the final year of a course that is not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course[^c-be0899e8ea6ebe2c4cc7ee0f6dc4bb0e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] ) is equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *X* − *Y*where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *X* is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,415; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £6,170; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £5,255; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iv) for a student in category E, £4,405; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] is equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *X* − *Y*where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *X* is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,085; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £5,620; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £4,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iv) for a student in category E, £4,080; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant not exceeding £1,200, the amount of maintenance grant payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,200, but less than £3,200, £1,200 with the addition of the amount calculated at paragraph (5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) where the student qualifies under regulation 50(2)(b) for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,200 but not exceeding £1,600, £1,200 with the addition of the amount calculated at paragraph (6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant of £3,200, £1,700; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) where no maintenance grant is payable, nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (4)(b) the maintenance grant amount is the amount by which the grant calculated at sub-paragraph (b) exceeds the amount calculated at sub-paragraph (a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where A is the household income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (4)(c) the maintenance grant amount is the amount by which the grant calculated at sub-paragraph (b) exceeds the amount calculated at sub-paragraph (a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where A is the household income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
