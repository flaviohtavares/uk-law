# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. End-on courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fb710444211b14d2e7449bffacbbd512][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An old system student who is on an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (a) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2 that he began before 1st September 2006 qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of that course in accordance with regulation 21. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An old system student who is on an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (c) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2 qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of that course in accordance with regulation 21. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Paragraphs (4) to (10) apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) an old system student in respect of an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (a) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2 that he begins on or after 1st September 2006; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) an old system student in respect of an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (b) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) An old system student to whom this regulation applies does not qualify for fee support in respect of a course to which this paragraph applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) he has an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland for which he received financial support under previous regulations and the exemption in regulation 34(1) or (2) does not apply; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4A)[^c-adbc9b42902a142fffdfd53b2529a951] Notwithstanding paragraph (4) (a) an old system student qualifies for fee support in respect of a course leading to a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) An old system student to whom this paragraph applies does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a course to which this paragraph applies that is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a course to which this paragraph applies, the Department must determine the “standard entitlement”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) Subject to paragraph 7A,[^c-52d764bf4cdbbb9d1b58b1172c84bde6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the standard entitlement is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *D* is the greater of 3 and the number of academic years that make up the ordinary duration of the course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *X* is 1 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was less than three years and 2 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was three years *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *PrC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* is the number of academic years that the student spent on the preliminary course excluding any years of repeat study for compelling personal reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7A)[^c-00e3fbe91a8896c89d7ddddd293c48dc] The standard entitlement of an old system student to whom paragraph (3) (a) applies whose course is a course leading to a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a course to which this paragraph applies, the Department must allocate a grant for fees from the standard entitlement first to the final standard academic year of the course and then to each preceding standard academic year in turn until the standard entitlement is exhausted or a grant for fees has been allocated to each standard academic year of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) An old system student to whom this paragraph applies qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of a standard academic year of a course to which this paragraph applies if the Department allocates a grant for fees to that year when assessing the application for support for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) The amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a course to which this regulation applies is determined in accordance with regulation 27, 28 or 29 and may be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
