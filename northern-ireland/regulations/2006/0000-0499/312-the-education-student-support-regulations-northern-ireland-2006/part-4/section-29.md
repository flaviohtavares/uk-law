# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 29

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Amount of the grant for fees for a course at a private institution (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8180eb78f71d5d65093c9bf5877c19c8][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2)[^c-9b370075510147e4689cefd0b393fbd7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] , the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a private institution including courses or academic years for the degrees of Bachelor of Divinity, Bachelor of Theology, Bachelor of Arts (Joint Honours) or the Diploma in Theology of the Queen’s University of Belfast, undertaken at the Union Theological College, the Edgehill College, the Irish Baptist College, Belfast or the Belfast Bible College is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,125; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) In the case of a designated course at the University of Buckingham, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £2,840. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
