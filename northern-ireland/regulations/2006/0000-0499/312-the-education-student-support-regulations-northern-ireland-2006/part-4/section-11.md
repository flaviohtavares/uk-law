# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 11

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. New system students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-df5b9456707b7a154d281c5051fa32dd][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A new system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 2 of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) To receive a fee loan, a new system student must enter into a contract with the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
