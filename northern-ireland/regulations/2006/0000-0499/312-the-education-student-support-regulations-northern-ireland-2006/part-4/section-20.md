# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 20

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Amount of the fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f825523b655f1ffa1efb197799665ba3][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Unless one of the cases set out in paragraph (3) applies, the amount of a fee loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £3,000; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) In the cases set out in paragraph (3), the amount of a fee loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The cases are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the final academic year of a designated course where that academic year is normally required to be completed after less than 15 weeks' attendance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) in respect of a sandwich course, an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) in respect of a course for the initial training of teachers, an academic year during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) in respect of a course provided in conjunction with an overseas institution, an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) during which any periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) an academic year of a course provided by the British Institute in Paris where the course began before 1st September 2001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) If a student’s status as an eligible student is transferred from one designated course to another under these Regulations and the circumstances in paragraph (5) apply, the student may apply to the Department to borrow an additional amount by way of a fee loan in respect of the academic year of the course to which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the fees payable in respect of the academic year of the course to which the new system student transfers exceed the fees payable in respect of the academic year of the course from which the student is transferring; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the academic year of the course to which the new system student transfers does not begin on a later date than the academic year of the course from which he is transferring. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) If a student’s status as an eligible student is transferred from one designated course to another under these Regulations and the circumstances in paragraph (7) apply, the student may apply to the Department for another fee loan in respect of the academic year of the course to which he transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) The circumstances are that the academic year of the course to which the new system student transfers begins on a later date than the academic year of the course from which he is transferring. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) Where the circumstances in paragraph (5) apply, the maximum additional amount that the new system student may borrow in respect of the academic year to which he transfers provided that he qualifies for a fee loan in respect of that year is determined by deducting the amount of any fee loan he has taken out under these Regulations in respect of the academic year from which he is transferring from the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £3,000 or, where one of the cases set out in paragraph (3) applies, £1,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of the academic year to which he is transferring. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) Where the circumstances in paragraph (7) apply, the maximum amount of fee loan that a new system student may borrow in respect of the academic year to which he transfers provided that he qualifies for a fee loan in respect of that year is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £3,000 or, where one of the cases set out in paragraph (3) applies, £1,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) Where a new system student has applied for a fee loan of less than the maximum amount available in relation to an academic year, he may apply to borrow an additional amount which when added to the amount already applied for does not exceed the relevant maximum applicable in his case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
