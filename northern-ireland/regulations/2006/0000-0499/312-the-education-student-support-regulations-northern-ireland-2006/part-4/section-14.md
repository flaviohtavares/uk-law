# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 14

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Events (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-14c119d2b962ffbdfcb7b37343bd2bac][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student, his spouse, his civil partner or his parent is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a state accedes to the European Community where the student is a national of that state or a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of a national of that state; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) the student becomes a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of an EC national; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) the student acquires the right of permanent residence (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (ea (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-82b83cb4895b0975c09d1e86a00a9edf][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
