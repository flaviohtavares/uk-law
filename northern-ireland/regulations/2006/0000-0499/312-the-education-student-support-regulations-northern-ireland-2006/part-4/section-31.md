# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 31

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Amount of the fee contribution loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-525be9388d7c09fa4438ab389455f178][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an old system student applies for a grant for fees and a fee contribution loan, the amount of the fee contribution loan in respect of an academic year of the designated course is the amount for which the student applies not exceeding the difference between the basic amount of the grant determined under regulation 27 or 28 and the amount of the grant that is payable after the application of the contribution in accordance with regulation 76. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Where the only fee support for which an old system student applies is a fee contribution loan, the maximum amount for which the student may apply in respect of an academic year is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,200 or, if any of the cases set out in regulation 20(3) apply, £600; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) An old system student may apply to borrow an additional amount of fee contribution loan where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the Department determines that the maximum amount of fee contribution loan should be increased (including an increase from nil) as a result of a reassessment of the student’s contribution or otherwise; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the Department considers that the increase in the maximum amount does not result from the old system student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) failing to provide information promptly which might affect his ability to qualify for a grant for fees or fee contribution loan or the amount of grant for fees or fee contribution loan for which he qualifies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] providing information which is inaccurate in any material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The additional amount under paragraph (3) is an amount which when added to the amount already applied for does not exceed the increased maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) Where an old system student has applied for a fee contribution loan of less than the maximum amount to which he is entitled, he may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed the relevant maximum applicable in his case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
