# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 27

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Amount of the grant for fees for a course at a publicly-funded institution in the United Kingdom or relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1292bb00c4fd5a3b4b7714590642a537][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Unless one of the cases set out in regulation 20(3) applies, the basic amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a publicly-funded institution is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,200; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) In the cases set out in regulation 20(3), the basic amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £600; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where a contribution exceeding nil is calculated under Schedule 4, a deduction will be made from the basic amount of the grant for fees determined under paragraph (1) or (2) in accordance with regulation 76. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4)[^key-f51a9ca7cc6dd8f3421acea2e7f4c267] Paragraphs (1) to (3) do not apply to designated courses at Heythrop College or at Guildhall School of Music and Drama. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) In the case of a designated course at Heythrop College, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £2,145. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5A)[^key-4aed308514493367d5ed686a9187f7ac] In the case of a designated course at Guildhall School of Music and Drama, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £4,355. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) In the case of a course in respect of an academic year at a relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland, an amount specified by the Department in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
