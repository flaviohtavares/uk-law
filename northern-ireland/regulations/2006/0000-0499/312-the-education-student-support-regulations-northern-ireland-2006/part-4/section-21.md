# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 21

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Continuing students and students commencing courses in the Republic of Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-69036aaf574646f3fff98fbc6b845e7d][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where an old system student (a “continuing student”) began a designated course before 1st September 2006 and is continuing on that course after 31st August 2006, or began the current course on or after 1st September 2006 at a relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A student to whom this regulation applies does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of any academic year of the course that begins on or after 1st September 2006 where in the course of assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of the designated course that began before 1st September 2006 the Department determined in accordance with regulations made by it under Article 3 of the Order that the student did not qualify for fee support in respect of the designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A student to whom this regulation applies does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of a designated course if the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A student to whom this regulation applies does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course that is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of the designated course that begins after 31st August 2006, the Department must determine the “standard entitlement”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) The standard entitlement is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *SAY *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* is the number of standard academic years of the designated course that begin after 31st August 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *X* is the number of academic years of the designated course that begin after 31st August 2006 in respect of which the Department determined in accordance with regulations made by it under Article 3 of the Order that the student did not qualify for a grant for fees in the course of assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of the designated course that began before 1st September 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of the designated course that begins after 31st August 2006, the Department must allocate a grant for fees from the standard entitlement first to the final standard academic year of the course and then to each preceding standard academic year in turn until the standard entitlement is exhausted or a grant for fees has been allocated to each standard academic year of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) A student to whom this regulation applies qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of a standard academic year of the designated course if the Department allocates a grant for fees to that year when assessing the application for support for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) The amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is determined in accordance with regulation 27, 28 or 29 and may be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
