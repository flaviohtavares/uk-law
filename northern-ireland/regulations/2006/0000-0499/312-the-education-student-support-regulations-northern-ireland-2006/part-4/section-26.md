# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 26

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Availability of the grant for fees to old system students for years of repeat study (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f8bcefe2b17f85cc3fe72860ac07e254][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In addition to the standard entitlement, if the Department determines that the student is repeating an academic year of the designated course because of compelling personal reasons, an old system student qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of the year of repeat study provided that the academic year that the student is repeating was a qualifying year of study and the year of repeat study is not a bursary year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An old system student qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course that is a year of repeat study which the student is taking other than for compelling personal reasons if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the academic year which he is repeating was a qualifying year of study; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the academic year of repeat study is not a bursary year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) when the academic year of repeat study is added to the number of any other academic years of repeat study that the student has already taken on the current course other than for compelling personal reasons, it does not exceed the number of additional years of support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) In this regulation, the “number of additional years of support” is the number of years which make up the standard entitlement less the number of standard academic years (plus one where the student qualifies for a grant for fees under regulation 25(8)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
