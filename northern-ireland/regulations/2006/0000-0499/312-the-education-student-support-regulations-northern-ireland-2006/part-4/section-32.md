# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 32

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Previous course (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-33832b3e47118f37f3e71e48c68c4e9c][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the exceptions in paragraphs (3), (4) and (5), a “previous course” is any full-time higher education course or any part-time course for the initial training of teachers which the student began to attend *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] or, in the case of a compressed degree course, undertake in the United Kingdom[^key-2bde0a1355da91c07cb5e3d40458f871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] before the current course and which meets any of the conditions in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the course was provided by an institution in the United Kingdom which was publicly funded for some or all of the academic years during which the student took the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the course was provided by an institution in the Republic of Ireland and the student was funded under previous regulations for some or all of the academic years during which he took the course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) any scholarship, exhibition, bursary, grant, allowance or award of any description which was paid in respect of the student’s attendance on the course to defray fees was from public funds or funds attributable to public funds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A course which would otherwise be a previous course will not be treated as such if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the current course is a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the duration of the current course does not exceed two years (the duration of a part-time course being expressed as its full-time equivalent); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the student is not a qualified teacher. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) A course for the Certificate in Education which would otherwise be a previous course will not be treated as such if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the current course is a course for the degree of Bachelor of Education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student transferred to the current course from the course for the Certificate in Education before the completion of that course or began the current course on completion of the course for the Certificate in Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) A course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education will not be treated as a previous course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the current course is a course for the honours degree of Bachelor of Education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student transferred to the current course from the course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education before the completion of that course or began the current course on completion of the course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) Subject to paragraphs (7), (8) and (9), for the purpose of determining *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* in the formulae in regulations 18 and 25— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) each academic year that the student completed on a previous course is counted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) an academic year of a previous course that the student began or ceased to attend part of the way through the year is counted as one academic year on a previous course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) For the purpose of determining *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* in the formulae in regulations 18 and 25, an academic year of a previous course is not to be counted as a year spent on a previous course if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]*—*

- (a) the student did not qualify for fee support for that year other than because the academic year was a bursary year or an Erasmus year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the student qualified for fee support for some but not all of the academic years of that previous course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) For the purpose of determining *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* in the formulae in regulations 18 and 25, an academic year of a previous course is not to be counted as a year spent on a previous course if it was a year of repeat study that the student was taking for compelling personal reasons or a year in relation to which the student qualified for fee support because he had failed to complete a previous course for compelling personal reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) For the purpose of determining *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] *PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* in the formulae in regulations 18 and 25, where a student transfers from an academic year of one designated course to an academic year of another designated course before the Department considers that he has completed the year from which he is transferring, the time spent by the student during the academic year in which the transfer takes place on the course from which he is transferring is not counted as a year spent on a previous course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) A student who undertook a previous course but was not in attendance because he was unable to attend for a reason which related to his disability is only treated as if he were in attendance on the previous course in respect of periods of study beginning on or after 1st September 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
