# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 28

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Amount of the grant for fees for a course that is provided at a private institution on behalf of a publicly-funded institution (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-38ab3d0a339c5eb2a0cc3562916b460d][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The basic amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year at a private institution is the lesser of £1,200 and the fees payable by the student in connection with that year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the designated course began on or after 1st September 2001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is provided on behalf of a publicly-funded institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) none of the circumstances in regulation 20(3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) The amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year at a private institution is the lesser of £600 and the fees payable by the student in connection with that year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the designated course began on or after 1st September 2001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is provided on behalf of a publicly-funded institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) one or more of the circumstances in regulation 20(3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Where a contribution exceeding nil is calculated under Schedule 4, a deduction will be made from the basic amount of the grant for fees determined under paragraph (1) or (2) in accordance with regulation 76. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
