# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 13

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Students becoming eligible in the course of an academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-58ae22218a6a6bdcb9f4a1242587c838][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where one of the events listed in regulation 14 occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a student may qualify for fee support in accordance with this Part in respect of that academic year provided that the relevant event occurred within the first three months of the academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) fee support is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
