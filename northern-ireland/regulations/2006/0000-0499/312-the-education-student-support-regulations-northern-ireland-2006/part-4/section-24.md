# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 24

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Gap year students who have not studied on a previous course (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7a6b53334421a01538a83dd2171218fd][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to an old system student who is a gap year student who has not studied on a previous course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) A gap year student does not qualify for fee support in respect of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) he has an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland for which he received financial support under previous regulations and the exemption in regulation 34(1) or (2) does not apply; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2A)[^c-d1f18ca896ad3db30674741d4c791ff9] Notwithstanding paragraph (2) (a) an old system student qualifies for fee support in respect of a course leading to a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A gap year student does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course that is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a designated course, the Department must determine the “standard entitlement”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The standard entitlement is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *OD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* is the number of academic years that make up the ordinary duration of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a designated course, the Department must allocate a grant for fees from the standard entitlement first to the final standard academic year of the course and then to each preceding standard academic year in turn until the standard entitlement is exhausted or a grant for fees has been allocated to each standard academic year of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) A gap year student qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of a standard academic year of the designated course if the Department allocates a grant for fees to that year when assessing the application for support for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) The amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is determined in accordance with regulation 27 or 28 and may be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
