# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 34

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Miscellaneous (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-367fc03b221383e80f067753ca452ff4][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student is not prevented from qualifying for fee support under this Part by virtue of having an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland for which he received financial support under previous regulations if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the current course is a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the duration of the current course does not exceed two years (the duration of a part-time course being expressed as its full-time equivalent); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) the student is not a qualified teacher. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Where the current course is considered to be a single course because of regulation 5(5) and (6) and it leads to an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom or relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland being conferred on the eligible student before the final degree or equivalent qualification, the eligible student is not prevented from qualifying for fee support under this Part in respect of any part of the single course by virtue of having that honours degree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) For the purposes of calculating the amount of fee support, an institution that provides courses designated by regulation 4 of the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00038] is not to be regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00039].

(4) Where an institution allows an eligible student to study the content of one standard academic year of the designated course over two or more academic years, for the purpose of determining whether the student qualifies for fee support for those years, the last of such years of study is to be treated as a standard academic year and the preceding years of that kind are to be treated as years of repeat study other than for compelling personal reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
