# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 19

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Standard entitlement of new system students on end-on courses and certain degree courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-09b64542e21323fbf95cade778b08dfb][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a new system student who is on an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a new system student who has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) completed a full-time course mentioned in paragraph 2 or 3 of Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] is on a full-time first degree course (other than a first degree course for the initial training of teachers) that he did not begin immediately after the course referred to in paragraph (i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] has not taken a full-time first degree course after the course referred to in paragraph (i) and before the current course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) a new system student who has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) completed a full-time foundation degree course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] is on a full-time honours degree course that he did not begin immediately after the course referred to in paragraph (i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] has not taken a full-time first degree course after the course referred to in paragraph (i) and before the current course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Regulations 17 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] , 17A[^c-5419b7e7fa6eb2dbe182286bec0419db] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] and 18 do not apply to students to whom this regulation applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) The standard entitlement of a student to whom this regulation applies is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *D* is the greater of 3 and the number of academic years that make up the ordinary duration of the current course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *X* is 1 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was less than three years and 2 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was three years *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *PrC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]* is the number of academic years that the student spent on the preliminary course excluding any years of repeat study for compelling personal reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
