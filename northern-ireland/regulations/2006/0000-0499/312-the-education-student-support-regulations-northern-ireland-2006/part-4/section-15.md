# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 15

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Students treated as in attendance on a course (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e0d7407f23110efd71c18c49aa679149][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as if he were in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for fee support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) This regulation applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) a compressed degree student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a disabled student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland but is not in attendance because he is unable to attend for a reason which relates to his disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
