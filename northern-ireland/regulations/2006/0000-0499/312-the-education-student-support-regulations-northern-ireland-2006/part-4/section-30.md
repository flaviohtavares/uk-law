# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 30

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Availability of fee contribution loans to old system students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-910ec1735ea6ff98684c753a6965ace7][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

An old system student, other than a student undertaking a course at a relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] or whose course is a course leading to a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland[^c-93e79f88e9f100db4f04ba551d41ceaf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] qualifies for a fee contribution loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) he qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of that year or would have qualified if he had applied for the grant (even if the amount is or would have been nil); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is provided by or on behalf of an institution that was publicly funded as at 1st August 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
