# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 12

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Old system students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6c82b0184e7b9214d65d0a672c70acc7][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An old system student qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 3 of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) An old system student qualifies for a fee contribution loan in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 4 of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) To receive a fee contribution loan, an old system student must enter into a contract with the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
