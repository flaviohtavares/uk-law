# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 16

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Availability of fee loans to new system students - general (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bfc3c066ce5898d492fb6e558c014756][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A new system student does not qualify for fee support in respect of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) he has an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland for which he received financial support under previous regulations and the exemption in regulation 34(1) or (2) does not apply; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) (a) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-93249d7e70149581e012c20b2434a557]..., a new system student qualifies for fee support in respect of a course leading to a degree in social work at an educational institution in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) A new system student does not qualify for a fee loan in respect of:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) an academic year of a designated course that is a bursary year or an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) a year for which he is receipt of funding from the Department to participate in a management development programme known as the Business Education Initiative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a designated course, the Department must determine the “standard entitlement”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) The standard entitlement is calculated in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] regulation 17, 17A, 18 or 19[^c-2d9b03263315e616247027c424f66179] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] .

(6) When assessing an application for support in respect of an academic year of a designated course, the Department must allocate a fee loan from the standard entitlement first to the final standard academic year of the course and then to each preceding standard academic year in turn until the standard entitlement is exhausted or a fee loan has been allocated to each standard academic year of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) A new system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of a standard academic year of the designated course if the Department allocates a fee loan to that year when assessing the application for support for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) In addition to the standard entitlement, a new system student who falls within regulation 18 qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the first academic year that he takes of the designated course that is not a bursary year or an Erasmus year if he failed to complete the most recent previous course because of compelling personal reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) Where a new system student qualifies for a fee loan under paragraph (8), the Department must not allocate a fee loan under paragraph (6) to the first academic year that the student takes of the designated course that is not a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) In addition to the standard entitlement, if the Department determines that the student is repeating an academic year of the designated course because of compelling personal reasons, a new system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the year of repeat study provided that the academic year that the student is repeating was a qualifying year of study and the year of repeat study is not a bursary year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(11) A new system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course that is a year of repeat study which the student is taking other than for compelling personal reasons if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) the academic year which he is repeating was a qualifying year of study; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) the academic year of repeat study is not a bursary year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) when the academic year of repeat study is added to the number of any other academic years of repeat study that the student has already taken on the current course other than for compelling personal reasons, it does not exceed the number of additional years of support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(12) In this regulation, the “number of additional years of support” is the number of years which make up the standard entitlement less the number of standard academic years (plus one where the student qualifies for a fee loan under paragraph (8)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(13) The amount of the fee loan in respect of an academic year is determined in accordance with regulation 20 and may be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
