## 78. Application of contribution to loan for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-506dd3346fb628f3a7ebb2159dd236dc][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (5), the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 77(d) for a new system student is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £2,560, in the case of a student in category A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,630, in the case of a student in category B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) £3,940, in the case of a student in category C or D; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £3,305, in the case of a student in category E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) Subject to paragraph (5), where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course[^key-d8dd0f7046a10dfc6f25cd483ae7c4de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] , the “minimum level for the academic year” is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £2,315, in the case of a student in category A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,215, in the case of a student in category B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) £3,430, in the case of a student in category C or D; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £3,060, in the case of a student in category E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Subject to paragraphs (4) and (5), the “minimum level for the academic year” for an old system student is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,960, in the case of a student in category A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,715, in the case of a student in category B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) £3,800, in the case of a student in category C; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £3,800, in the case of a student in category D; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) £2,950, in the case of a student in category E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] an intensive course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12], the “minimum level for the academic year” for an old system student is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) £1,630 in the case of a student in category A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) £4,165, in the case of a student in category B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) £3,115, in the case of a student in category C; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) £3,115, in the case of a student in category D; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) £2,625, in the case of a student in category E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(5) Where different categories apply for different quarters of the academic year, the minimum levels in paragraphs (1) and (2) are the aggregate of the amounts determined under paragraph (4) for each of the quarters in respect of which a loan is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(6) The amount determined for each quarter is one third of the amount in paragraph (1) or (2) which corresponds to the rate applicable for the quarter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(7) The rate applicable for a quarter is determined in accordance with regulation 63. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(8) Paragraph (9) applies to type 1 and type 2 teacher training students who qualify for a maintenance grant and whose contribution exceeds nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(9) The loan for living costs payable in respect of an academic year to a student to whom this regulation applies is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/312) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- *A* is the amount of loan for living costs left after applying the contribution in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- *B* is the amount of maintenance grant payable to the student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(10) Categories A to E have the meaning given in regulation 70. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
