# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 77

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Order of application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-22010ab22e23f060d0ef6aa35d1e87d8][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

The contribution or the remainder of the contribution, as the case may be, is to be applied as follows until it is extinguished— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) first, to reduce the amount of adult dependants' grant calculated in accordance with regulation 45; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) second, to reduce the childcare grant calculated in accordance with regulation 45; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (c) third, to reduce the parents' learning allowance calculated in accordance with regulation 45 (except the first £50 of the allowance); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (d) fourth, to reduce the loan for living costs for which the eligible student (other than a student with reduced entitlement) qualifies under Part 6 to no less than the minimum level for the academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (e) fifth, to reduce the grant for travel calculated in accordance with regulation 48. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
