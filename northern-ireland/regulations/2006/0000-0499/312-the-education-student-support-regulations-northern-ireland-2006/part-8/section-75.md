## 75. Application of contribution – new system students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5d32d8a803cca0004da8636c24ae07c2][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the case of a new system student, the Department must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 77 until it is extinguished. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
