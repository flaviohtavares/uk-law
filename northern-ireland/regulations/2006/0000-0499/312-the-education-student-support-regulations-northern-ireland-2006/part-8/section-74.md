# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 74

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Calculation of contribution (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3980b5f0a9ee808a0ab8a9f73bf09377][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible student’s contribution in respect of an academic year is the amount, if any, calculated under Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) For the purposes of the exercise of the Department’s functions under the Order and regulations made under it it may require an eligible student to provide from time to time such information as it considers necessary as to the income of any person whose means are relevant to the assessment of the student’s contribution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
