# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 73

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Insolvency (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a914f58112181bea62aafa8b73054286][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Northern Ireland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) there shall not be treated as part of a bankrupt’s estate or claimed for his estate under Article 280 or 283 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00059] any sum payable to an eligible student by way of a loan and which he receives or is entitled to receive after the commencement of the bankruptcy, whether his entitlement arises before or after the commencement of his bankruptcy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) there shall not be included in a person’s bankruptcy debts any debt or liability to which he is or may become subject in respect of any sum payable to an eligible student by way of loan and which he receives or is entitled to receive— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) in the case of a bankruptcy commencing before 15th January 2005, after the commencement of the bankruptcy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] in the case of a bankruptcy commencing on or after 15th January 2005, before or after the commencement of the bankruptcy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) In England and Wales— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

- (a) there shall not be treated as part of a bankrupt’s estate or claimed for his estate under section 307 or 310 of the Insolvency Act 1986 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00060] any sum payable to an eligible student by way of a loan and which he receives or is entitled to receive after the commencement of the bankruptcy, whether his entitlement arises before or after the commencement of his bankruptcy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
- (b) there shall not be included in a person’s bankruptcy debts any debt or liability to which he is or may become subject in respect of any sum payable to an eligible student by way of a loan and which he receives or is entitled to receive— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (i) in the case of a bankruptcy commencing before 1st September 2004, after the commencement of the bankruptcy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12] in the case of a bankruptcy commencing on or after 1st September 2004, before or after the commencement of the bankruptcy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
