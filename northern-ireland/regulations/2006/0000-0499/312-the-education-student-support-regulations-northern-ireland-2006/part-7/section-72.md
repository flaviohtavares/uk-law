# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 72

> Text as at 2007-07-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Interest (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ebf8f2ab3aa14fc73710dde9f7633271][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), loans bear interest at the rate which will result in an annual percentage rate of charge determined in accordance with the Consumer Credit (Total Charge for Credit) Regulations 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00057] equal to the percentage increase between the retail prices all items index published by the Office for National Statistics for March 2005 and that index so published for March 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(2) If the rate referred to in paragraph (1) exceeds the rate for the time being specified for the purposes of any exemption conferred by virtue of section 16(5)(b) of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12][^f00058] loans bear interest at the rate so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(3) Interest is calculated on the principal outstanding daily and is added to the principal monthly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]

(4) The index of prices to which the Department is required by Article 3(8) of the Order to have regard in prescribing the rate of interest which loans bear is the retail prices all items index mentioned in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-38e66e2f9723c92ebe23389f944f7e12]
