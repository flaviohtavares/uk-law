## 2. Meaning of certain expressions and references - general provisions[^c-9dcc7e6ca6a8e06149b70037b5dd9933]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-b0c94c5500291a8bab75c8f8780dfaa4] In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

- (a) the expressions contained in the glossary set out in Schedule 1 shall be construed as provided in that Schedule;
- (b) any reference to a police officer, however expressed, includes a reference to a person who has been a police officer;
- (ba)[^c-207fd204d073d8496783cfbb60d8ff34] any reference to a police officer being a member of a pension scheme, however expressed, includes a reference to a police officer entitled to be a member of that scheme who has opted out of making contributions, or who was entitled to be a member but has died;
- (c) any reference to an award, however expressed, is a reference to an award under these Regulations.
