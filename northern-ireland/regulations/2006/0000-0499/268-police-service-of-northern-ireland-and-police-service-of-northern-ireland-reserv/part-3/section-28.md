# POLICE SERVICE OF NORTHERN IRELAND AND POLICE SERVICE OF NORTHERN IRELAND RESERVE (INJURY BENEFIT) REGULATIONS 2006, section 28

> Text as at 2024-07-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Increase of awards by reference to the Pensions (Increase) Acts[^c-848d2723fffada8bf4dcd533c37179ce]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where it is provided that, for the purpose of calculating an award by way of periodical payments or a gratuity (“the relevant award”), an amount shall be increased in accordance with this regulation, it shall be increased by the amount, if any, by which a corresponding pension, within the meaning of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00012], of the amount first mentioned would from time to time be increased under the Pensions (Increase) Acts if—

- (a) it were payable to the person entitled to the relevant award;
- (b) it were one of the pensions specified in paragraph 43 of Part II of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971;
- (c) it were not a pension to which section 1(2)(a) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1974[^f00013] applies, and
- (d) it began, within the meaning of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971, and became payable when the relevant award so began and became payable.

(2) Where the relevant award is a child’s special allowance, the Pensions (Increase) Acts as applied by paragraph (1) shall have effect as if section 3 were omitted from the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 and, accordingly, the amount first mentioned in paragraph (1) shall be increased so long as the special allowance is payable.
