# POLICE SERVICE OF NORTHERN IRELAND AND POLICE SERVICE OF NORTHERN IRELAND RESERVE (INJURY BENEFIT) REGULATIONS 2006, section 37

## 37. Withdrawal of pension during service as a police officer[^key-e1efdbe582f5ba01ab0b54e6b825b00b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3)[^key-163d3319efbf51d53292ca4d1b24f725], in paying an injury pension under regulation 10 (police officer’s injury award) or under regulation 19 (adult dependent relative’s special pension) the Board may, in its discretion, withdraw the whole or any part of the pension for any period during which the pensioner is serving as a police officer; and, where it has done so, it shall be discharged from all actual or contingent liability in respect of the pension or the part thereof withdrawn for the period in question.

(2) This regulation does not apply to a pension under regulation B9(7)(b) of the 1988 Regulations (allocation).
