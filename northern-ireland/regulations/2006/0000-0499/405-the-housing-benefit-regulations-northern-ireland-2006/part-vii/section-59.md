## 59. Relationship with amounts to be disregarded under Schedule 6[^key-45c3142c9e5da761da7e1e2141c0b616]

*Extent: N.I.*

No part of a student’s covenant income or grant income shall be disregarded under paragraph 14 of Schedule 6.
