# The Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 17

## 17. Persons of prescribed description[^c-732f3797c2eef1ae1a077163cfbf025a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 133(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of family[^f00062]) is a person who falls within the definition of qualifying young person in section 138 of the Act[^f00063] (child and qualifying young person), and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “**young person**”.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a person who is—

- (a) on income support , an income-based jobseeker’s allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^c-46febdf3e84e3c3a546e7f26c9757a3c]; [^c-10efd4d72e9a9312d0719282f946c4a1]...
- (b) a person to whom section 6 of the Children (Leaving Care) Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00064] (exclusion from benefits) applies or[^c-44035b25a7107066b3b6a100950813bc]
- (c)[^c-f684ff08da2f387720840f42ab6cc6c7] entitled to an award of universal credit.

(3) A person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 133(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of family) includes a child or young person in respect of whom section 141A of that Act[^f00065] applies for the purposes of entitlement to child benefit but only for the period prescribed under section 141A(1) of that Act.
