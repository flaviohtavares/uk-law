## 13B. Eligible rent and maximum rent[^c-5f56bebf5956e73edb22b70e1374f729]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a maximum rent has been, or is to be, determined in accordance with regulation 14.

(2) Where this regulation applies, except where paragraph (3) applies, the amount of a person’s eligible rent shall be the maximum rent, subject to paragraphs (3), (4) and (7) of regulation 13A.

(3) In a case where the maximum rent is derived from a single room rent decided under paragraph 5 of Schedule 2 the eligible rent shall be the maximum rent subject to paragraphs (3) and (7) of regulation 13A.
