## 73A. Circumstances in which a benefit cap will apply[^c-03e6a9fe44a05c3fa8bd78f00b59e7f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless regulation 73E or 73F applies, a benefit cap applies where the relevant authority makes a determination that during the reference period the total amount of welfare benefits to which—

- (a) where the claimant is a member of a couple, each member of the couple is or jointly are entitled, or
- (b) in any other case, the claimant is entitled,

exceeds the relevant amount.
