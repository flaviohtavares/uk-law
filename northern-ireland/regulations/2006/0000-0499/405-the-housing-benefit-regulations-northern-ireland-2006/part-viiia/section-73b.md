# The Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 73B

## 73B. Determinations[^key-e369a44735d7fcfb1e037c4c2fba7a56][^key-03e6a9fe44a05c3fa8bd78f00b59e7f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A relevant authority may make a determination as to whether the benefit cap applies or whether to change the amount of any reduction made in accordance with regulation 73D (reduction of housing benefit) but a relevant authority is not required to do so unless paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies where a relevant authority receives notification from the Department that—

- (a) the benefit cap may apply;
- (b) there has been a change in the amount of a welfare benefit to which the claimant is entitled; or
- (c) there has been a change to the relevant amount which may affect the amount of housing benefit to which a claimant is entitled.
