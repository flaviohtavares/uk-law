# The Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 73CA

## 73CA. Determination of the relevant amount[^c-83a3b318aa6dd227f2ce40194616fd35][^c-03e6a9fe44a05c3fa8bd78f00b59e7f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The relevant amount is determined by dividing the applicable annual limit by 52 and rounding that amount by disregarding any amount of less than half a penny and treating any amount of half a penny or more as a whole penny.

(2) The applicable annual limit is—

- (a) £14,753[^c-62fc88c7a2dc7872993c78a651448942] in the case of single claimants;
- (b) £22,020[^c-61879396e3f2a0f0de18b9bf67f7473a] in the case of all other claimants.
