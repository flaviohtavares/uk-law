# The Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 4

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Cases in which section 1(1A) of the Administration Act is disapplied[^c-91bc5acef399c522e110e252f22a1490][^c-a511e480e0d24fd18229cc4246b95444]

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act[^f00051] (requirement to state national insurance number) shall not apply—

- (a) to a claim for housing benefit where the person making the claim, or in respect of whom the claim is made, is liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling which is a hostel; [^c-8b2d07c1adc5675fa90697dc50f10875]...
- (b) to any child or young person in respect of whom housing benefit is claimed ; or[^c-fc578e165b6e7d1cc9f8b1d38540cb92]
- (c)[^c-6b486e3c50b43b50f66f49ab16166551] to a person who—
  - (i) is a person in respect of whom a claim for housing benefit is made;
  - (ii) is subject to immigration control within the meaning of section 115(9)(a) of the Immigration and Asylum Act;
  - (iii) is a person from abroad for the purposes of these Regulations as defined in regulation 10(2), and
  - (iv) has not previously been allocated a national insurance number.
