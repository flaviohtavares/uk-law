## 70D. Calculation of an extended payment where the benefit cap applies[^key-1f93832afea841a84cbbdc88c9dd1ba9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a claimant’s housing benefit in the benefit week with respect to which the extended payment is to be calculated is reduced in accordance with regulation 73D, the extended payment must be calculated using the amount of housing benefit before any such reduction is made.
