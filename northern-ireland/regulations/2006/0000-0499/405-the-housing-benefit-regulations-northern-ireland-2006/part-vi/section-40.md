## 40. Capital limit[^c-ea06687d72639ae47481c92253c846d1]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of section 130(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £16,000.
