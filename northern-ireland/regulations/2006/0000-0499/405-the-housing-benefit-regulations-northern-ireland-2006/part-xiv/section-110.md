## 110. Criminal offence[^c-e838bd615934bfaab86d32a0c959131c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any supplier who fails to supply relevant information to the requirer as, when and how required under this Section shall be guilty of an offence under section 107 of the Administration Act.
