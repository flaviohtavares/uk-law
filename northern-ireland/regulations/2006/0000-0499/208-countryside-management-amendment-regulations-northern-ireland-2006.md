# Countryside Management (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 208

AGRICULTURE

Countryside Management (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

9th May 2006

Coming into operation

3rd July 2006

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 3 of the Agriculture (Conservation Grants) (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and being a Department designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Countryside Management (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 3rd July 2006.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the principal Regulations” means the Countryside Management Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00005].

## 2. Amendment of the Schedules to the principal Regulations

(1) In Schedule 4 to the principal Regulations (Management Activities (Optional Habitats)), after paragraph 7,—

- (a) in column 1 there is inserted—

  > **8.** In relation to indigenous rare breeds – breeding of Irish Moiled Cattle
- (b) in column 2 there is inserted—

  > - £125 per female animal or bull kept for a full 12 months.

(2) In Schedule 6 to the principal Regulations (Interpretation of Schedules), after the definition of “heritage feature”, there is inserted—

> - “indigenous rare breeds” means domestic livestock breeds indigenous to Northern Ireland;

## 3. Transitional provision

Regulation 2 shall not apply in a case where payment has been approved by the Department for the purposes of a grant under regulation 3 of the principal Regulations before the coming into operation of these Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 9th May 2006.

David Small

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby approves the foregoing Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 9th May 2006.

Jack Layberry

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Countryside Management Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 (“the principal Regulations”) by providing for payment of a grant in respect of breeding indigenous rare breeds, specifically the breeding of Irish Moiled Cattle (regulation 2).

A transitional provision has been included for applications approved by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development before the coming into operation of these Regulations (regulation 3).
