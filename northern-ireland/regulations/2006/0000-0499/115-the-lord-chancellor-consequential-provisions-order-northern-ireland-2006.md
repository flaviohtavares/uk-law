# The Lord Chancellor (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 115

LORD CHANCELLOR

LORD CHIEF JUSTICE

TRANSFER OF FUNCTIONS

The Lord Chancellor (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

13th March 2006

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation

3rd April 2006

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 143 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Lord Chancellor (Consequential Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 3rd April 2006.

## 2. Consequential provision

The Schedule has effect with respect to the making of provision consequential to that under the Constitutional Reform Act 2005.

Falconer of Thoroton, *C.*

Dated 13th March 2006

## SCHEDULE — CONSEQUENTIAL PROVISION

Article 2

### 1. County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981

(1) Order 54 rule 2 of the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2) for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.

### 2. Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992

The Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] is amended as follows.

**3.** (1) Rule 9 is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In paragraph 3(a)(i) for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.

**4.** (1) Part II of Schedule 1 is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In paragraph 1(2)(a) for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.

### 5. Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

(1) Article 1 of the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] is amended as follows.

(2) In the definition of “petty sessions districts” in paragraph (2), after “Lord Chancellor” insert “after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order introduces the Schedule which makes amendments to certain functions of the Lord Chancellor which are contained in secondary legislation. These amendments are consequential to amendments that are effected by Schedule 5 to the Constitutional Reform Act [2005 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4) which transfers certain of the Lord Chancellor’s functions to the Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland, and requires certain functions to be exercised by the Lord Chancellor only after consulting, or obtaining the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland.
