# The Employment Equality (Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Assisting persons to obtain employment etc.[^key-85569edb3ba6674c802098b212b3d66e][^key-00a9b27dc5d6953534775f1fd6957a92]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It is unlawful for the Department for Communities or the Department for the Economy[^key-3249e57c4adb4502a6e4e9645155643d] to discriminate against any person by subjecting him to a detriment, or to subject a person to harassment, in the provision of facilities or services under section 1 of the Employment and Training (Northern Ireland) Act 1950[^f00020] (general functions of the Department for Communities or the Department for the Economy[^key-4c43dd817a56bea304f420cd134385d9] as to employment and training for employment).

(2) This regulation does not apply in a case where—

- (a) regulation 21 (the provision of vocational training) applies, or would apply but for the operation of any other provision of these Regulations, or
- (b) the Department for Communities or the Department for the Economy[^key-685bc0b3b4d4da8a45929074d5687ed0] is acting as an employment agency within the meaning of regulation 22 (employment agencies, careers guidance etc.).
