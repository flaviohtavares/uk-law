# The Gangmasters (Appeals) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 235

EMPLOYMENT

The Gangmasters (Appeals) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

25th May 2006

Coming into operation

19th June 2006

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 10 and 25(2) of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Gangmasters (Appeals) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 19th June 2006.

**2.** (1) The Gangmasters (Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by paragraph 2.

(2) In regulation 3(1) for the words “Employment Tribunal Chairmen appointed under the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004” there shall be substituted “chairmen appointed under the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004]”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 25th May 2006.

Liam McKibben

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This amendment corrects the reference to Tribunal Chairmen in regulation 3(1).
