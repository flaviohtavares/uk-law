# The Immobilisation and Release of Vehicles (Charge) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 339

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

The Immobilisation and Release of Vehicles (Charge) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

16th August 2006

Coming into operation -

30th October 2006

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 20(2)(c) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Immobilisation and Release of Vehicles (Charge) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 30th October 2006.

## 2. Prescribed charge

The prescribed charge in respect of the immobilisation and release of a vehicle for the purposes of Article 20(2)(c) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 shall be £40.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 16th August 2006.

R. Sherman

A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the charge in respect of the immobilisation and release of vehicles in contravention of traffic offences subject to civil enforcement by the Department for Regional Development for the purposes of Article 20(2)(c) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005. The charge is set at £40.
