# The Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of [SR 2006 No 417](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/417).

2006 No. 497

dangerous wild animals

The Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

1st December 2006

Coming into operation

28th December 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 3 (2) of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 28<sup>th</sup> December 2006.

## 2. Fee payable in connection with an application for a licence

An application made to the Department for a licence shall be accompanied by a fee of £80.

## 3. Revocation

The Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 1<sup>st</sup> December 2006.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that an application to the Department of the Environment under the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall be accompanied by a fee of £80.
