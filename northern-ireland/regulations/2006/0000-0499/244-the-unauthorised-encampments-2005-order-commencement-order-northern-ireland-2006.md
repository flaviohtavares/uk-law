# The Unauthorised Encampments (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 244 (C. 14)

UNAUTHORISED ENCAMPMENTS

The Unauthorised Encampments (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

5th June 2006

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 1(3) of the Unauthorised Encampments (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Unauthorised Encampments (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 9 of the Unauthorised Encampments (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 is 19th July 2006.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 5th June 2006.

Linda MacHugh

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 19th July 2006 for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 9 of the Unauthorised Encampments (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.
