# The Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 108

PENSIONS

The Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

10th March 2006

Coming into operation

6th April 2006

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and now vested in the Department for Social Development[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has made an Order[^f00003] under section 109(4) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004].

Accordingly the Department for Social Development makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Guaranteed Minimum Pensions Increase Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2006.

(2) In this Order “relevant period” has the same meaning as in section 109(3A)[^f00005] of the Pension Schemes Act 1993.

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Increase of guaranteed minimum pensions

For the purposes of section 105(1) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, the percentage by which there is to be an increase in the rate of that part of any guaranteed minimum pension which is attributable to earnings factors for the tax years in the relevant period shall be 2.7 per cent.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 10th March 2006.

John O'Neill

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which corresponds to an Order (S.I. [2006/673](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/673)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under section 109(4) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993, specifies 2.7 per cent. as the percentage by which that part of any guaranteed minimum pension attributable to earnings factors for the tax years 1988-1989 to 1996-1997, and payable by contracted-out, defined benefit occupational pension schemes, is to be increased.
