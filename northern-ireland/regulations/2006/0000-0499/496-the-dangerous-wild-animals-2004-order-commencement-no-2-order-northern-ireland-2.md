# The Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of [SR 2006 No 416 (C.23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/416).

2006 No. 496 (C. 31)

dangerous wild animals

The Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

1st December 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 1(3) of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 11 of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 is 28th December 2006.

## 3. Revocation

The Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 1<sup>st </sup>December 2006.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 28<sup>th</sup> December 2006 as the day for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 11 of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004.
