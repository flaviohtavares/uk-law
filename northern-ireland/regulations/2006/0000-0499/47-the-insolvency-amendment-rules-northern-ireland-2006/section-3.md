# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendments to Rule 0.2

(1) In Rule 0.2 (interpretation) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “appropriate fee” after the words in brackets there shall be inserted “or Rule 6.190B(6) or Rule 6.190C(6) (payor under income payments agreement entitled to clerical etc costs)”.

(2) In the definition of “file in court” after the words “file in court” there shall be inserted “and file with the court”.

(3) After the definition of “prescribed order of priority” there shall be inserted the following definition—

> - “prescribed part” has the same meaning as it does in Article 150A(2)(a)

.
