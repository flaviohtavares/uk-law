# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2006/406
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Cases in which section 1(1A) of the Administration Act is disapplied[^c-a707fd85db7f8912613f656edbb42f80][^c-de310a985569828ae67dbb4f13c0364f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act[^f00050] (requirement to state national insurance number) shall not apply—

- (a) to a claim for housing benefit where the person making the claim, or in respect of whom the claim is made, is liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling which is a hostel; [^c-a0d25d0ae99cdae8df2bd96184e61605]...
- (b) to any child or young person in respect of whom housing benefit is claimed ; or[^c-1a8955c2ab5a59e3576a52da4beabe9f]
- (c)[^c-107aeb98f5b6e49afb9fe39626ec3bc6] to a person who—
  - (i) is a person in respect of whom a claim for housing benefit is made;
  - (ii) is subject to immigration control within the meaning of section 115(9)(a) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999;
  - (iii) is a person from abroad for the purposes of these Regulations as defined in regulation 10(2), and
  - (iv) has not previously been allocated a national insurance number.
