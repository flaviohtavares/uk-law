## 13B. Eligible rent and maximum rent[^c-d43277c64475d53c42499e02ecb28fd8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a maximum rent has been, or is to be, determined in accordance with regulation 14.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the amount of a person’s eligible rent shall be the maximum rent, subject to paragraphs (3), (4) and (7) of regulation 13A.
