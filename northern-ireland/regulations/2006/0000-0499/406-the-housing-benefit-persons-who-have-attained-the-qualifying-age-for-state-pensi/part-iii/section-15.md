# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2006/406
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Decisions[^c-2256b4ee08f636a9ab9b3829c95b0b3a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), in respect of a claim for rent allowance where a maximum rent ([^c-83b5b8e0a8dcb81df9dced844c1c9ae6]LHA) does not fall to be determined under regulation 14C the Executive shall—

- (a) make the decision in accordance with Part I of Schedule 2; and
- (b) comply with Part II of Schedule 2 when making the decision.

(2) The Executive, on the first working day of each month, shall—

- (a) make a decision in accordance with Part III of Schedule 2; and
- (b) comply with paragraph 8(2) of Schedule 2 when making the decision.

(3) No decision shall be made—

- (a) under paragraph 3 or 4[^c-2b03ea0afa1d20700f159742d7bc43f3] of Schedule 2 if the tenancy is of residential accommodation, within the meaning of regulation 9(4), or in a hostel; or[^c-f6c25d48ff59042c02e0c2770edfd88e]
- (b) for a tenancy under Schedule 3; [^c-b5a7f4cb49e4ab00e8de95d988d723ae]...
- (c)[^c-74313289baa1768163ea454087dbaea8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) This regulation shall apply as specified in Part V of Schedule 2 in relation to—

- (a) mooring charges payable for a houseboat;
- (b) payments in respect of the site on which a caravan or a mobile home stands; or
- (c) payments under a rental purchase agreement.
