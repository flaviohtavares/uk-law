# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 40

## 40. Income paid to third parties[^c-05d885ebeca73eb62750348466e20b28]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any payment of income, other than a payment specified in paragraphs (2) or (3)[^c-72921e433baf7033463188e2f7da60a3] , to a third party in respect of the claimant shall be treated as possessed by the claimant.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in respect of a payment of income made under an occupational pension scheme, in respect of a pension or other periodical payment made under a personal pension scheme or a payment made by the Board of the Pension Protection Fund where—

- (a) a bankruptcy order has been made in respect of the person in respect of whom the payment has been made or, in Scotland, the estate of that person is subject to sequestration or a judicial factor has been appointed on that person’s estate under section 41 of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00122];
- (b) the payment is made to the trustee in bankruptcy or any other person acting on behalf of the creditors; and
- (c) the person referred to in sub-paragraph (a) and his partner does not possess, or is not treated as possessing, any other income apart from that payment.

(3)[^c-3699cdee9fe857a3333209785d9be278] Paragraph (1) shall not apply in respect of any amount of income other than earnings, or earnings derived from employment as an employed earner, arising out of the claimant participating as a service user[^c-7ef8d7cca4493138624628feb3fc3486] .
