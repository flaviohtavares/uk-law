# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2006/406
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 76. Offsetting[^c-6fe70292468543d2f3db002c64413a7b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person has been paid a sum of housing benefit under a decision which is subsequently revised or superseded or further revised or superseded[^c-5cda4cf7042eeeb95940a5a9843ed1ef] , any sum paid in respect of a period covered by a subsequent decision shall be offset against arrears of entitlement under the subsequent decision except to the extent that the sum exceeds the arrears and shall be treated as properly paid on account of them.

(2) Where an amount has been deducted under regulation 82(1) an equivalent sum shall be offset against any arrears of entitlement under the subsequent decision except to the extent that the sum exceeds the arrears and shall be treated as properly paid on account of them.

(3) No amount may be offset under paragraph (1) which has been determined to be an overpayment within the meaning of regulation 77.
