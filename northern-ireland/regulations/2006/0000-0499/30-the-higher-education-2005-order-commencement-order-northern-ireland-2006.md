# The Higher Education (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 30 (C. 3)

EDUCATION

The Higher Education (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

6th February 2006

The Department for Employment and Learning, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Higher Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001] makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Higher Education (2005 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

(2) In this Order “the 2005 Order” means the Higher Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the 2005 Order is 13th February 2006:

- Article 4 to the extent that it is not already in operation;
- Article 5;
- Articles 8 and 9 to the extent that they are not already in operation;
- Article 12;
- Article 15 (together with the Schedule).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 6th February 2006

David McAuley

A senior officer of the

Department for Employment and Learning

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 13th February 2006 for the coming into operation of the remaining provisions of the Higher Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

Article 2 commences provisions concerning the imposition by the Department for Employment and Learning of conditions to control fees at higher education institutions.
