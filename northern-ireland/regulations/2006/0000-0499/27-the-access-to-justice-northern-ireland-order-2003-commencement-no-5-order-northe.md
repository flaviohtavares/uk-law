# The Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 27 (C. 2)

ACCESS TO JUSTICE

The Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

31st January 2006

Coming into operation

20th March 2006

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 1(2) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 20th March 2006.

**2.** Paragraph 15 of Schedule 4 to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 shall come into operation.

**3.** In Schedule 5 to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, in the entry in respect of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, the repeal of Article 23 shall come into operation.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Bridget Prentice

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

Dated 31st January 2006

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order, which is the fifth to be made under the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, brings into operation on 20th March 2006 further provisions in that Order.

Paragraph 15 of Schedule 4 to the 2003 Order amends the Freedom of Information Act 2000 to list the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission as a public authority for the purposes of that Act.

This Commencement Order also repeals the provision in the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 relating to the appointment and function of the Lord Chancellor’s Legal Aid Advisory Committee.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
