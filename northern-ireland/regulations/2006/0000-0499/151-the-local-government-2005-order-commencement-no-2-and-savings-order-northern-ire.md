# The Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Savings) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 151 (C. 10)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT

The Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Savings) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

22nd March 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) and (3) of the Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Savings) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

(2) In this Order “the 2005 Order” means the Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2005 Order specified in the Schedule shall be 1st April 2006.

## 3. Savings

Articles 3(1), 6 and 9 to 24 shall not have effect in relation to the audit of accounts of local government bodies for financial years beginning before the 1st April 2006.

**4.** The amendments made by Article 28 of, and the repeals in Schedule 1 to, the 2005 Order shall not have effect in relation to the accounts of local government bodies for financial years beginning before the 1st April 2006.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 22nd March 2006.

John Ritchie

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment
