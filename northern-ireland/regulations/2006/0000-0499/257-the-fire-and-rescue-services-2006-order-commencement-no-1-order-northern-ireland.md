# The Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 257 (C. 15)

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES

The Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

9th June 2006

Coming into operation

1st July 2006

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1 (3) of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 [^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

## 2. Commencement

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 is 1st July 2006—

- (a) Articles 3 to 24;
- (b) Articles 53 to 63;
- (c) Schedules 1 and 2;
- (d) Schedule 3, paragraphs 1 to 18 and 20 to 23; and
- (e) Schedule 4 in so far as it relates to—
  - (i) The Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975[^f00002];
  - (ii) Articles 3 to 20 and 49A to 52 of and Schedules 1, 2, 3 and 5 to the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984[^f00003];
  - (iii) The Commissioner for Complaints (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00004];
  - (iv) Articles 3, 5 and 6 of the Fire Services (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00005];
  - (v) The Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00006]; and
  - (vi) The Audit and Accountability (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00007].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 9th June 2006.

Paul Simpson

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 1st July 2006, Articles 3 to 24 and 53 to 63 of, Schedules 1 and 2 to, and parts of Schedules 3 and 4 of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.
