# The Planning Reform (2006 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 381 (C. 22 )

planning

The Planning Reform (2006 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

18th September 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Planning Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Planning Reform (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into operation of Article 5 of the Planning Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 is 17th October 2006.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18 September 2006.

Marianne Fleming

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 17th October 2006*,* Article 5 (independent examination of development plans) of the Planning Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.

NOTE AS TO EARLIER COMMENCEMENT ORDERS

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Planning Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 have been brought into operation by a commencement order made before the date of this Order
