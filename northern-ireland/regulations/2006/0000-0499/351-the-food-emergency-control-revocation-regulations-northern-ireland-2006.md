# The Food (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 351

food

The Food (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

30th August 2006

Coming into operation

1st October 2006

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002].

The Department has been designated[^f00003] for the purposes of that section in relation to measures relating to food (including drink) including the primary production of food.

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the following Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2006.

## 2. Revocations

The statutory rules specified in column 1 of the Schedule are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 30th August 2006.

Don Hill

A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for the revocation of specified statutory rules which implemented Commission Decisions imposing conditions on the import of specific products that could be contaminated with excessive levels of aflatoxins.
