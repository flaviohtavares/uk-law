# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 7

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Disinfection[^key-c2def1072d31160106cb61d12d04fd2d]

*Extent: N.I.*

Disinfection under this Order shall be carried out with a disinfectant which is—

- (a) approved for use for the purpose of these regulations, the foot and Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) and by the Diseases of Animals (Approval of Disinfectants) Order (Northern Ireland) 1972 [^f00013]; and
- (b) used in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions or recommendations (if any) and in particular, if use is recommended before any date, used before that date.
