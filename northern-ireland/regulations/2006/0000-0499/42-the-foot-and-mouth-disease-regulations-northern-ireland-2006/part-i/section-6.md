# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 6

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Dissemination of information concerning prohibitions and requirements[^key-e44926f9f98e7e243c51c3ba2f8bf401]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department shall take such steps as it considers fit to ensure that licences, declarations and notices are brought to the attention of those who may be affected by them as soon as is reasonably practicable.

(2) It shall also ensure that—

- (a) the extent of any zone declared under these Regulations;
- (b) the nature of the prohibitions and requirements applicable within the zone;
- (c) the date of its declaration; and
- (d) the date that declaration ceases to have effect in respect of the zone or any part of it,

are publicised.
