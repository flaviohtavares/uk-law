# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 61

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment to the Diseases of Animals (Approval of Disinfectants) Order (Northern Ireland) 1972[^key-0d623b5555c0270845a6e155eb403f01]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 3 of the Diseases of Animals (Approval of Disinfectants) Order (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00021], for the definition of “special disease order” there shall be substituted,

> - “special disease order” means any order made or having effect under the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 or any Regulations made or having effect under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 which deals with one or more particular special diseases;

.
