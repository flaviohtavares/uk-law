> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Enforcement, offences and proceedings[^key-1a46363f4f8cd9d1f193abd852e98f5f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following provisions of the Order shall apply as if these Regulations were an order made under the Order—

- (a) Article 18 (6) (general provisions relative to slaughter and compensation) and the Diseases of Animals (Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00020];
- (b) Article 42 (functions of constables);
- (c) Article 52 (offences);
- (d) Article 53 (defences and evidence);
- (e) Article 55 (extension of time for bringing summary proceedings);
- (f) Article 56 (venue);
- (g) Article 57 (proceedings under Customs Acts for unlawful landing or shipping).
