# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 41

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Duty of the Department to erect signs[^key-b1d5c073f402ded18db0f2f724b85fbc]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall ensure that the boundaries of the following zones are indicated by signs erected in a conspicuous position on all roads entering the zones on which it considers susceptible animals are likely to be moved as soon as is reasonably practicable after declaration of the zones—

- (a) every temporary control zone;
- (b) every supplementary movement zone;
- (c) every protection zone;
- (d) every surveillance zone;
- (e) every restricted zone;
- (f) every wild animal infected zone.
