# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — MEASURES APPLICABLE IN RESPECT OF A WILD ANIMAL INFECTED ZONE

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations 38(1) and 54

### 10. Notification of a dead wild susceptible animal in a wild animal infected zone[^c-e9e599798c375829eb66f159dfd36ede]

Any person who kills a wild susceptible animal or finds such an animal dead in a wild animal infected zone shall notify a Divisional Veterinary Officer of that fact.

### 11. Record keeping in a wild animal infected zone[^c-bd5b716d482bc0d312c9bad248144b72]

The occupier of any holding in a wild animal infected zone where a susceptible animal is kept shall create a record of the number of each species of susceptible animals kept on the premises and shall maintain that record by updating it every time that number changes.

### 12. Isolation of a susceptible animal in a wild animal infected zone[^c-5917fa58cbb8c4b3850cd67797af00f0]

The occupier of any premises in a wild animal infected zone where a susceptible animal is kept shall ensure that—

- (a) a susceptible animal is isolated as far as is reasonably practicable from contact with wild animals; and
- (b) fodder, used litter and any other material which may come into contact with a susceptible animal is isolated as far as is reasonably practicable from contact with wild animals.

### 13. Movement of a susceptible animal in a wild animal infected zone[^c-dd203bfcf93ec44bf5533c3573f6f7ff]

A person shall not move a susceptible animal from or to premises in a wild animal infected zone except under the authority of a licence granted by the Department.

### 14. Cleansing and disinfection in a wild animal infected zone[^c-54fc33e90c7a86a8497eba2469fe9866]

(1) The occupier of any premises in a wild animal infected zone shall ensure that means of disinfection are provided and used at all entrances to buildings where a susceptible animal is kept in accordance with the directions of an inspector.

(2) Any person coming into contact with a wild susceptible animal shall cleanse and disinfect himself and launder his clothing after such contact in accordance with the directions of the Department.

### 15. Carcases and things which may be contaminated in a wild animal infected zone[^c-4a9849b2dc877c8a331a7b6307b411a2]

A person shall not bring the following on to a holding in a wild animal infected zone where a susceptible animal is kept—

- (a) any carcase of a wild animal;
- (b) any material, equipment or other thing which may be contaminated.

### 16. Movement of semen, ova and embryos out of a wild animal infected zone for trade[^c-3ed9f461afae3057e0b1d9bd3ad182a8]

A person shall not move any semen, embryo or ovum of a susceptible animal out of a wild animal infected zone for the purpose of trade with a[^c-5c772cc980b7b4bc7682b456e7369ba0] member State, Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein.
