# The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 330

agriculture

The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

4th August 2006

Coming into operation

28th August 2006

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002], after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to it to be concerned, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 28th August 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

(1) The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In regulation 1(2) the definition of “official document” shall be omitted.

(3) In regulation 3(a)(i), for the word “indicted” there shall be substituted “indicated”.

(4) In regulation 3(b), for the word “who” there shall be substituted “whom” and the words “or official documents” shall be omitted.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Department on 4th August 2006.

R Jordan

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations remove the definition of “official document” in the Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/187)) and correct a spelling and a grammatical error.
