# The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 408

road traffic and vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

*12th October* 2006

Coming into operation

*24th November* 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 79A(2) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel as regards regulation 2:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 24th November 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

(1) In regulation 4 (fee for a licence) for paragraph (1) there shall be substituted—

> (1) The fee for a first licence shall be £110.00 and for renewal of a licence shall be £75.00.

.

(2) In regulation 5(4) (grant of new licences to replace lost, stolen, defaced or destroyed licences) for “£9” there shall be substituted “£19”.

## 3. Revocation

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 as follows—

(1) The single fee of £58.50 for a licence is replaced by a two-tier system of fees. The cost of a first licence is £110.00 and the cost of a renewal licence is £75.00.

(2) The fee for a new licence to replace a lost, stolen, defaced or destroyed licence is increased from £9 to £19.

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 are revoked.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and may be obtained from the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Northern Ireland website at www.dvli.gov.uk.
