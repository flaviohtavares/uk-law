# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Amendment to Article 10 of the Insolvent Partnerships Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

For Article 10(6) of the 1995 Order substitute—

> (6) Certain of the provisions referred to in paragraphs (2) and (4) are modified in their application in relation to the corporate or individual members of insolvent partnerships in such manner that, after modification, they are as set out in Part II of Schedule 4.

.

**5.** Article 8 amends Schedule 3 to the 1995 Order consequent upon amendments made in the 2005 Order.
