# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment to Schedule 2 to the Insolvent Partnerships Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

For Schedule 2 to the 1995 Order (modified provisions of the Order applying for purposes of Article 6) substitute the provisions set out in Schedule 1.

**7.** Articles 10 and 11 amend the application of Article 185 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 to ensure that the requirement to set aside a “prescribed part” (Article 150A of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989) does not apply to the winding up of an insolvent partnership.
