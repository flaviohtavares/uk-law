# The Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Insolvent Partnerships (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 8th January 2007 (“the commencement date”).

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, and
- “the 1995 Order” means the Insolvent Partnerships Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00004]

**1.** Article 4 substitutes Article 6 of the 1995 Order to take account of the amendment to Part III of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 ([S.I. 1989/2405 (N.I. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1989/2405)) (“the 1989 Order”) by Article 3 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1455 (N.I. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/1455)) (“the 2005 Order”). Article 3 of the 2005 Order substituted Part III of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 ([S.I. 1989/2405 (N.I. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1989/2405)). The substituted Part III consists only of Article 21 which gives effect to Schedule B1 to the Order, which was set out in Schedule 1 to the 2005 Order. Schedule B1 makes provision for companies to go into administration by court order on an administration application or upon the appointment of an administrator by the company, its directors or the holder of a qualifying floating charge.
