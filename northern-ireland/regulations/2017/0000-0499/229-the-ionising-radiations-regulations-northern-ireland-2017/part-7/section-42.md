# The Ionising Radiations Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 42

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Modifications and revocation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Schedule 9, which contains modifications to primary and secondary legislation, has effect.

(2) The Ionising Radiations Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 [^c22879051] are revoked.
