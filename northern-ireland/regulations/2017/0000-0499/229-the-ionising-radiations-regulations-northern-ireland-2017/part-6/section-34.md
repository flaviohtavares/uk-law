# The Ionising Radiations Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 34

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Misuse of or interference with sources of ionising radiation

*Extent: N.I.*

No person may intentionally or recklessly misuse or without reasonable excuse interfere with any radioactive substance or any electrical equipment in respect of which these Regulations apply.
