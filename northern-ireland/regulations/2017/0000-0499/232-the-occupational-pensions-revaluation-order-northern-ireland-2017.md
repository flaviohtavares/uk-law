# The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 232

Pensions

The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made

6th December 2017

Coming into operation

1st January 2018

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and now vested in the Department for Communities[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has made an Order[^f00003] under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004].

Accordingly the Department for Communities makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2018.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. The higher and lower revaluation percentages for each revaluation period

For the purposes of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (methods of revaluing accrued pension benefits), for each revaluation period specified in column 1 of the Schedule—

- (a) the higher revaluation percentage is the percentage specified in column 2, and
- (b) the lower revaluation percentage, where applicable, is the percentage specified in column 3,

in relation to that period.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 6th December 2017

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which corresponds to an Order ([S.I. 2017/1104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1104)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993, specifies appropriate revaluation percentages. The percentages specified are relevant to the revaluation of benefits under occupational pension schemes, as required by section 80 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. The lower revaluation percentage introduced by the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 13 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13)) does not apply to pensionable service before 6th April 2009.
