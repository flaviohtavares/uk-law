# The Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 217 (C. 14)

Credit Unions

Co-operative Societies

Community Benefit Societies

The Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made

18th October 2017

The Department for the Economy[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) of the Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2016 is 6th April 2018—

- (a) section 4 (Attachment of shares);
- (b) section 8 (Registration of societies as co-operative or community benefit societies etc.);
- (c) section 16(1)(b) (Minor and consequential amendments);
- (d) Part 2 of Schedule 1 (Amendments consequential on section 8).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for the Economy on 18th October 2017.

Colin Jack

A senior officer of the

Department for the Economy

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 6th April 2018 sections 4, 8 and 16(1)(b), and Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Credit Unions and Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.

Section 9 of the Act came into operation at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day of Royal Assent.

The other provisions of the Act came into operation on the day after Royal Assent.
