# The Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 155

Seeds

The Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made

28th July 2017

Coming into operation

1st August 2017

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and 2(2) of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and come into operation on 1st August 2017.

## 2. Amendment of the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

The Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

## 3. Regulation 11 (labelling of packages and containers of seed potatoes)

In regulation 11(3)(a), for “paragraph 6 of Schedule 2” substitute “paragraph 5 of Schedule 2”.

## 4. Schedule 2 (official labels and official documents)

In Part 1 of Schedule 2 (official labels), in paragraph 1(3)(b), after the entry “Certification authority and member State or their initials”, insert “Officially assigned serial number;”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 28th July 2017.

Malcolm Beatty

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 190](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/190)) (“the principal Regulations”).

Regulation 4 implements Article 5 of Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/317 (OJ L 60, 5.3.16, p 72), which amends Section (A) of Annex III to [Directive 2002/56/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0056), to add an “Officially assigned serial number” to the label particulars required for the official label on a package or container of basic seed potatoes under Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations.

Regulation 3 makes a minor amendment to regulation 11(3)(a) of the principal Regulations to correct an erroneous reference.
