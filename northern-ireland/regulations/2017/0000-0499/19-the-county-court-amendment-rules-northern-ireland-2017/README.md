EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981 ([S.R. 1981 No. 225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1981/225)) to—

- provide for enforcement of interrogatories served without order;
- make provision for lodgements to be made after service of medical evidence other than the plaintiff’s;
- amend Orders 19, 24, 33 and 55 and insert a new Appendix 2 to increase costs over a two year period (1.5% in 2017 with a further 1.5% increase to take effect in 2018).
