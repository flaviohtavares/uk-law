# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 13th February 2017.

**2.** In these Rules—

- (a) “the principal Rules” means the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002]; and
- (b) a reference to an Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form is a reference to that Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form so numbered in the principal Rules.
