# The Jobseeker’s Allowance (Hardship) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 185

Social Security

The Jobseeker’s Allowance (Hardship) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made

11th September 2017

Laid before Parliament

13th September 2017

Coming into operation

25th October 2017

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 2(2), 21C(1), (2)(a) and (e) and 36(2) of, and paragraph 10(1) and (2) of Schedule 1 to, the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

Those powers are exercisable by the Secretary of State by virtue of Article 4(1)(b) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Jobseeker’s Allowance (Hardship) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and come into operation on 25th October 2017.

## 2. Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

(1) The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In regulation 140(1) (meaning of “person in hardship”)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (g)[^f00004] after “physical” insert “or mental”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (j)(ii) for “hardship.” substitute “hardship; or”;
- (c) at the end of sub-paragraph (k)[^f00005] insert “; or”;
- (d) after sub-paragraph (k) add—

  > - (l) is a person who is homeless within the meaning of Article 3 of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1988[^f00006]

  .

(3) In regulation 141(2)[^f00007] (circumstances in which an income-based jobseeker’s allowance is payable to a person in hardship) for “Article 3(2)(d)(ii)” substitute “Article 3(2A)(b)”.

(4) In regulation 146A(1)[^f00008] (meaning of “couple in hardship”)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (d) after “physical” insert “or mental”;
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (g) for “, or” substitute “; or”;
- (c) at the end of sub-paragraph (h) insert “; or”;
- (d) after sub-paragraph (h) add—

  > - (i) either member of the couple is a person who is homeless within the meaning of Article 3 of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1988.

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Damian Hinds

Minister of State

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations amends the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/198)).

The amendments in paragraph (2)(a) and (d) provide that a person with a long-term mental impairment, or who is homeless within the meaning of legislation applying in Northern Ireland, is a person in hardship for the purposes of these Regulations. This means that the person may receive immediate hardship payments of jobseeker’s allowance in cases where that allowance would otherwise not be paid or the amount paid is being reduced.

The amendments in paragraph (4)(a) and (d) make identical provision in relation to a couple claiming jobseeker’s allowance where either member of the couple has a mental impairment or is homeless.

Paragraphs (2)(c) and (4)(c) make consequential amendments and paragraphs (2)(b), (3) and (4)(b) make previously missed consequential amendments.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
