# The Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 34 (C. 3)

Roads

The Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made

25th January 2017

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(2) of the Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 specified in the Schedule is 4th September 2017.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 25th January 2017

(L.S.)

Tom Reid

A senior officer of the Department for Infrastructure

## SCHEDULE — Provisions coming into operation on 4th September 2017

Article 2

| Provision of the Act | Subject Matter |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6 | Prohibition or restriction of use of public roads in connection with special events. |
| Schedule 1 | Schedule to be inserted as Schedule 3A to the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997. |
| Schedule 2, paragraphs 3 to 5 and 6(1) and (2) and section 8(1) so far as relating to those paragraphs | Minor and consequential amendments. |
| Schedule 3, the entries relating to the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 and the Street Works (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (and section 8(2) so far as relating to those entries) | Repeals. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the last remaining provisions of the Roads (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010.
