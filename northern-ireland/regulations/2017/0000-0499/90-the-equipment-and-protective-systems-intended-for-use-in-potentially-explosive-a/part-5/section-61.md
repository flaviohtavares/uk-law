# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 61

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Offences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for any person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of regulations 5 to 15, 16(2), 18 to 25, 26(2), 27 to 32, 33(2), 35, 36 or 41A[^key-48dd0365448f3ffff756bc985176b6d0].

(2) It is an offence for any person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of a withdrawal or recall notice served on that person by the Executive under these Regulations.
