# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 60

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Restrictive measures

*Extent: N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, the Executive shall comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS (as amended from time to time) in relation to any measure to—

- (a) prohibit or restrict a product being made available on the market;
- (b) withdraw a product; or
- (c) recall a product.
