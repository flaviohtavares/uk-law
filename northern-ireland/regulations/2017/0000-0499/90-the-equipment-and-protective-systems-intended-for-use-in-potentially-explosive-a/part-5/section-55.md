# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 55

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Evaluation of a product presenting a risk

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Executive has sufficient reason to believe that a product presents a risk, the Executive shall carry out an evaluation in relation to the product covering the relevant requirements of Part 2.
