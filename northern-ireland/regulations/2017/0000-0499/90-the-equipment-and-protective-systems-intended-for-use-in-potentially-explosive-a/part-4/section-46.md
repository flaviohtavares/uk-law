# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 46

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Monitoring

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-0c375f661ea5295be16e7b6ce65d826b] shall monitor each notified body with a view to verifying that the notified body—

- (a) continues to meet the notified body requirements;
- (b) meets any conditions set in accordance with regulation 44(6)(b); and
- (c) carries out its functions in accordance with these Regulations.

(2) The Secretary of State shall inform the European Commission of Northern Ireland's procedures for the monitoring of notified bodies and any changes to those procedures.
