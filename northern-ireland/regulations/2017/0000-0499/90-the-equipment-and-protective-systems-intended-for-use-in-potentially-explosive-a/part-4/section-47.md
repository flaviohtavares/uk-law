# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 47

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. United Kingdom Accreditation Service

*Extent: N.I.*

The Secretary of State[^key-4c2a9085faff20ade24fcd7ce0e72171] may authorise the United Kingdom Accreditation Service [^c22851971] to carry out the following activities on behalf of the Secretary of State —

- (a) assessing whether a conformity assessment body meets the notified body requirements; and
- (b) monitoring notified bodies in accordance with regulation 46 (Monitoring).
