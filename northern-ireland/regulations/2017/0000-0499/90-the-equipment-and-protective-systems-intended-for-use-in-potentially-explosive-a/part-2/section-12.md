# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 12

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Labelling and packaging of products, other than components

*Extent: N.I.*

Save for where a product is a component, before placing a product on the market a manufacturer shall ensure that it—

- (a) bears the specific marking of explosion protection as referred to at paragraph 5(1)(f) of Schedule 1; and
- (b) where applicable, bears the other markings and information referred to at paragraph 5 of Schedule 1.
