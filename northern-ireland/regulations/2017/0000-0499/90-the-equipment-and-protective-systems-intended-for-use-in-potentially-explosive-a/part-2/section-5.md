# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 5

> Text as at 2023-08-10.

## 5. Design and manufacture in accordance with essential health and safety requirements

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing a product on the market or using a product for their own purposes, a manufacturer shall ensure that it has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the essential health and safety requirements.
