# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 28

> Text as at 2023-08-10.

## 28. Duty to act with due care

*Extent: N.I.*

When making a product available on the market, a distributor shall act with due care to ensure that it is in conformity with Part 2.
