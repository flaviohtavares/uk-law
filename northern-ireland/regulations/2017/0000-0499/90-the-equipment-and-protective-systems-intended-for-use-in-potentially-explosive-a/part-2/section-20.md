# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 20

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Prohibition on placing on the market products considered not to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an importer considers, or has reason to believe, that a product is not in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements, the importer shall not place the product on the market.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the importer shall inform the manufacturer and the market surveillance authority of that risk.
