# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 18

> Text as at 2023-08-10.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Prohibition on placing on the market products which are not in conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

An importer shall not place a product on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements.
