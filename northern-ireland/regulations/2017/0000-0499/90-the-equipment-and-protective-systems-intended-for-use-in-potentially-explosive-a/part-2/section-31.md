# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 31

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Prohibition on making available on the market where product not considered to be in conformity with safety objectives

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a distributor considers, or has reason to believe, that a product is not in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements, the distributor shall not make the product available on the market.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the distributor shall inform the following persons of the risk—

- (a) the manufacturer or the importer; and
- (b) the market surveillance authority.
