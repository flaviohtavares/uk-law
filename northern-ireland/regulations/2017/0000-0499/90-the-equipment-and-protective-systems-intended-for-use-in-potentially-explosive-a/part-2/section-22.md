# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 22

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Instructions and safety information

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing a product on the market, an importer shall ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by end-users in the relevant state[^key-bcfa669ee4e79d216a505aa5d57d78c7] in which the product is to be made available.

(2) Where the relevant state[^key-092fe56768fb7d4a5f6a9d543e1ebc8a] referred to in paragraph (1) is Northern Ireland[^key-3aafd941767e061494b7a3f31733e39e], the language referred to in that paragraph shall be English.
