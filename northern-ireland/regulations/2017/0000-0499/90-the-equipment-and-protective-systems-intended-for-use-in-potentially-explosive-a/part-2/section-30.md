# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 30

> Text as at 2023-08-10.

## 30. Storage and transport

*Extent: N.I.*

Each distributor shall ensure that, whilst a product is under that distributor's responsibility, its storage or transport conditions do not jeopardise its conformity with the essential health and safety requirements.
