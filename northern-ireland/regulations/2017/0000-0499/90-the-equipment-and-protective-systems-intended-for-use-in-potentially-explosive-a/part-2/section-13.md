# The Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 13

> Text as at 2023-08-10.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Information identifying manufacturer

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before placing a product on the market, a manufacturer shall indicate on the product—

- (a) the name, registered trade name or registered trade mark of the manufacturer; and
- (b) a postal address at which the manufacturer can be contacted.

(2) Where it is not possible to indicate the information specified in paragraph (1) on the product, the manufacturer shall indicate that information—

- (a) on the product packaging; or
- (b) in a document accompanying the product.

(3) The information specified in paragraph (1) shall be in a language which can be easily understood by end-users and the competent national authority in the relevant state[^key-4a4dcb44cf84616db5cf3fb0e82ec6df] in which it is to be made available to such end-users.
