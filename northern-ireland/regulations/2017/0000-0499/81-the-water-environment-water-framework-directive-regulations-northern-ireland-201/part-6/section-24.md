> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Application of this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part applies in relation to—

- (a) the most recent version of each river basin management plan prepared and updated in accordance with the 2003 Regulations; and
- (b) any subsequent version of such a plan updated in accordance with regulation 26.
