# The Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017, section 1

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations–

- (a) may be cited as the Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 22nd May 2017; and
- (b) shall apply only in relation to river basin districts identified in accordance with regulation 4(1).
