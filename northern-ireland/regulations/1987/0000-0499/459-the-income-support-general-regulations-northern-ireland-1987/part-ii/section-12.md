# The Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Relevant education

For the purposes of these regulations a child or young person is to be treated as receiving relevant education if, and only if—

- (a) he is receiving full-time education not being advanced education for the purposes of Article 4 of the Child Benefit (Northern Ireland) Order 1975[^f00025] (meaning of child); or
- (b) although he is not receiving such full-time education he is treated as a child for the purposes of that Article,

except that, in a case to which paragraph (b) applies, he shall not be treated as receiving relevant education beyond the terminal date in his case.
