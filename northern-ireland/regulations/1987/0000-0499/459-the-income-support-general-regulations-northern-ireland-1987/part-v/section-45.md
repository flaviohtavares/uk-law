# The Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Capital limit

For the purposes of Article 23(6) of the Order as it applies to income support (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £6,000.
