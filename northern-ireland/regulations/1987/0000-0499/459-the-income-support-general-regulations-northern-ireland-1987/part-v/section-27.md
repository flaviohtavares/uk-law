# The Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Disregard of fractions

Where any income or capital calculated in accordance with this Part includes a fraction of a penny that fraction shall be disregarded.
