## 51. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part of these Rules:—

- “**the 1996 Order**” means the Proceeds of Crime (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00104]; an Article referred to by number is a reference to the Article so numbered in the 1996 Order; and expressions which are defined in the 1996 Order have the same meaning as in the 1996 Order.
