# The Crown Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979, section 3

## 3. Taxation of costs

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where \[[^f00041]...\] the Court has made an order for the costs of any proceedings to be paid by a party and the Court has not fixed a sum, the amount of the costs to be paid shall be ascertained as soon as possible by the Taxing Master.

(2) On taxation there shall be allowed such sum as is reasonably sufficient to compensate the party for the expenses properly incurred by him.
