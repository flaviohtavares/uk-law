## 30. Saving for section 33(3) of the Children and Young Persons Act (Northern Ireland) 1968

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Rules shall affect the provisions of subsection (3) of section 3 of the Children and Young Persons Act (Northern Ireland) 1968[^f00044].
