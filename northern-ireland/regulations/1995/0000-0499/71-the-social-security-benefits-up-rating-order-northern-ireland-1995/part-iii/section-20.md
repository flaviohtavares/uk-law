# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. The relevant sum for income support

In section 125(7) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (trade disputes) for “£24.50” there shall be substituted “£25.00”.
