# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00008];
- “the Pension Schemes Act” means the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00009];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00010].

(2) The Interpretation Act 1978[^f00011] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the United Kingdom Parliament.
