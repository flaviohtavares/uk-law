# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Increase in rates of age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

In regulation 9(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00027] (increase in rate of incapacity benefit where beneficiary is under prescribed age on the qualifying date)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£12.15” there shall be substituted “£12.40”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£6.10” there shall be substituted “£6.20”.
