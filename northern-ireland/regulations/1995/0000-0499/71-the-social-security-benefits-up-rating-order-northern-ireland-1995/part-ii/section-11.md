# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Increase in rate of graduated retirement benefit and increments thereof

(1) The sum of 7.48 pence referred to in section 35(1) of the National Insurance Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00023] (graduated retirement benefit) shall be increased by 2.2 per cent. and accordingly the reference in that provision to that sum shall have effect as a reference to 7.64 pence.

(2) The sums which are the increases of graduated retirement benefit under Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1978[^f00024] (increases for deferred entitlement to a Category A or Category B retirement pension) shall be increased by 2.2 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
