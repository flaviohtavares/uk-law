# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Statutory sick pay

In section 153(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00021] (rate of payment) the sum specified is £52.50.

## Annotations

[^f00021]: Footnote: Section 153(1) is amended from 6th April 1995 by Article 10(1) of the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (Northern Ireland) Order 1994
