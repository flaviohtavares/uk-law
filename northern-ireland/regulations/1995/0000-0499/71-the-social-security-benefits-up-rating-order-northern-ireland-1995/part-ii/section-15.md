# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity, benefit cases

In regulation 18(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00028] (rate of long-term incapacity benefit in transitional cases)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£12.15” there shall be substituted “£12.40”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£7.60” there shall be substituted “£7.80”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£3.80” there shall be substituted “£3.90”.
