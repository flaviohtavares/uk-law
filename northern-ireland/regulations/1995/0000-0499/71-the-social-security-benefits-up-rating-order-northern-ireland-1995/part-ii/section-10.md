# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Statutory maternity pay

In regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00022] (lower rate of statutory maternity pay) the sum specified is £52.50.
