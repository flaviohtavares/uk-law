# The Child Support (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 428 (C. 8)

FAMILY LAW

CHILD SUPPORT

The Child Support (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

Made

16th November 1995

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Caution

This Order may be cited as the Child Support (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995.

## 2. Day appointed for the coming into operation of certain provisions of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1995

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 specified in the Schedule to this Order is 16th November 1995.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

W. G. Purdy

Assistant Secretary

16th November 1995.
