# The Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1995 No. 73

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

Made

7th March 1995

Coming into operation

10th April 1995

The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraph 4(5) of schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Industrial Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995 and shall come into operation on 10th April 1995.

## 2. Increase of earnings limits in respect of dependent children

In paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002]—

- (a) in head (a) for “£120” there shall be substituted “£125”; and
- (b) in head (b) for “£120” thee shall be substituted “£125”.

## 3. Revocation

Article 2(a) of the Social Security (Industrual Injuries) (Dependency) (Permitted Earnings Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland on

W. G. Purdy

Assistant Secretary

7th March 1995.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Where a disablement pension with unemployability supplement is increased in respect of a child and the beneficiary is one of two persons who are spouses residing together or an unmarried couple, paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 provides that an increase shall not be payable in respect of the first child if the other person’s earnings are £120 a week or more and in respect of a further child for each complete £16 where the earnings exceed £120. This Order substitutes the amount of £125 for the amount of £120. The amount of £16 is unchanged.

Article 3 contains a revocation consequential upon the coming into operation of this Order.
