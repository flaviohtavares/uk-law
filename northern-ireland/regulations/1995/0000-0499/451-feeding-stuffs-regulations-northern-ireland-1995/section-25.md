# Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Revocations

The following Regulations are hereby revoked—

- (a) the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00019];
- (b) the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00020];
- (c) the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00021]; and
- (d) the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00022].
