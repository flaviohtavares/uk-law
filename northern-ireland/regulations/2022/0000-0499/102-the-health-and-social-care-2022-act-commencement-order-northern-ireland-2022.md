# The Health and Social Care (2022 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 102 (C. 11)

Health And Social Care Act

The Health and Social Care (2022 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

11th March 2022

The Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the Health and Social Care Act (Northern Ireland) 2022[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This order may be cited as the Health and Social Care (2022 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022.

## 2. Appointed day

1st April 2022 is the appointed day for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Health and Social Care Act (Northern Ireland) 2022-

- (a) section 1 (Dissolution of the Regional Health and Social Care Board);
- (b) section 2 and Schedule 1 (Transfer of the Regional Board’s functions);
- (c) section 3 and Schedule 2 (Continuation of Local Commissioning Groups);
- (d) section 6(1)(b) and Part 2 of Schedule 4 (Transitional provision).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 11th March 2022.

Martina Moore

A senior officer of the

Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order is made by the Department of Health under section 8(1) of the Health and Social Care Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 (“**the Act**”).

It brings into operation the following provisions on 1st April 2022:

- section 1 brings about the dissolution of the Regional Health and Social Care Board.
- section 2 and Schedule 1 contains amendments providing for the transfer of the Regional Board’s functions and amendments consequential on the transfer of those functions;
- section 3 allows for the continuation of Local Commissioning Groups and Schedule 2 contains provision for their operation and dissolution.
- section 6(1)(b) and Part 2 of Schedule 4 sets out the detail of transitional provisions connected with the coming into operation of the Act.
