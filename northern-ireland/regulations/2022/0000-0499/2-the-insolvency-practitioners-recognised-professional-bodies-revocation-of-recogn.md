# The Insolvency Practitioners (Recognised Professional Bodies) (Revocation of Recognition) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 2

Insolvency

Insolvency Practitioners

The Insolvency Practitioners (Recognised Professional Bodies) (Revocation of Recognition) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

23rd December 2021

Coming into operation

5th January 2022

The Department for the Economy[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 350N(1) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00002] makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement and effect

(1) This Order may be cited as the Insolvency Practitioners (Recognised Professional Bodies) (Revocation of Recognition) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022 and comes into operation on 5th January 2022.

(2) This Order has effect from 5th January 2022.

## 2. Revocation

The Insolvency Practitioners (Recognised Professional Bodies) (Revocation of Recognition) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00003] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for the Economy on 23rd December 2021

David Malcolm

A senior officer of the Department for the Economy

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Insolvency Practitioners (Recognised Professional Bodies) (Revocation of Recognition) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021.
