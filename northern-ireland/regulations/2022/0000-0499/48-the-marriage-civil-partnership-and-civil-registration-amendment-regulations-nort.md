# The Marriage, Civil Partnership and Civil Registration (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 48

Civil Registration

The Marriage, Civil Partnership and Civil Registration (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

17th February 2022

Coming into operation

11th March 2022

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 10(1), 10(4), 10(4A), 11(1), 21(1), 21(5), 39, 40 and 40A of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 [^f00002], Articles 3(3), 7(1), 35(3) and 36 of the Marriage (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 [^f00003], and sections 139(2), 143, 155(3), 156(1) and 159 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 [^f00004], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Marriage, Civil Partnership and Civil Registration (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 11<sup>th</sup> March 2022.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Amendments to Marriage Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

(1) The Marriage Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00006] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Regulation 6(1) after “Schedule 2” insert “, 2A or 2B”.

(3) In Regulation 10(b) after “Schedule 8” insert “, 8A or 8B”.

(4) In Regulation 35 –

- (a) in paragraph (a) after “Schedule 13” insert “, 13A or 13B”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b) after “Schedule 14” insert “, 14A or 14B”.

(5) In Regulation 36(1) after “Schedule 15” insert “, 15A or 15B”.

(6) Part 1 of the Schedule (which contains further amendments) shall have effect.

## 4. Amendments to Civil Partnership Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

(1) The Civil Partnership Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 [^f00007] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Regulation 6 after “Schedule 2” insert “, 2A or 2B”.

(3) In Regulation 32 –

- (a) in paragraph (a) after “Schedule 8” insert “, 8A or 8B”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b) after “Schedule 9” insert “, 9A or 9B”.

(4) In Regulation 33(1) after “Schedule 10” insert “, 10A or 10B”.

(5) Part 2 of the Schedule (which contains further amendments) shall have effect.

## 5. Amendments to Civil Registration Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

(1) The Civil Registration Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 [^f00008] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Regulation 11(1) after “Form 1” insert “, 1A or 1B”.

(3) In Regulation 12(4) after “Form 1,” insert “1A or 1B,”.

(4) In Regulation 16 –

- (a) in paragraph (1) after “Form 2” insert “, 2A or 2B”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2) after “Form 2” insert “, 2A or 2B” and after “Form 1, insert “1A or 1B”.

(5) In Regulation 20 after “Form 2,” insert “2A or 2B”.

(6) In Regulation 24(1) after “Form 3” insert “, 3A or 3B”.

(7) In Regulation 25(2) after “Form 3,” insert “3A or 3B”.

(8) In Regulation 31 after “Form 1” insert “, 1A or 1B”.

(9) In Regulation 35(2) after “Form 19” insert “, 19A or 19B”.

(10) In Regulation 37(2) after “Form 20” insert “, 20A or 20B”.

(11) Part 3 of the Schedule (which contains further amendments) shall have effect.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Marriage Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, the Civil Partnership Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and the Civil Registration Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 to enable certified copies of life events to be produced in English, Irish or Bi-lingual English/Irish.

The Regulations prescribe alternative forms for the registration of births, still-births and deaths and alternative forms for the production of certified copies.

The Regulations also prescribe updated marriage and civil partnership notice forms and provide alternative forms for marriage and civil partnership schedules and alternative forms for the production of certified copies of marriage or civil partnership registration.
