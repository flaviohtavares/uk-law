# The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 129

Police

The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2022

Made

11th March 2022

Coming into operation

18th April 2022

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 26(1) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], makes the following Regulations. Those powers are now vested in the Department[^f00002].

In accordance with sections 25(8) and 26(6) of that Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland before making these Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2022 and shall come into operation on 18th April 2022.

## 2. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday” for 2022 only, for the words “and last Monday in May” substitute “, 2<sup>nd</sup> and 3<sup>rd</sup> June”.

## 3. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 1 in the definition of “public holiday”, for 2022 only, for the words “and last Monday in May” substitute “, 2<sup>nd</sup> and 3<sup>rd</sup> June”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 11th March 2022

Naomi Long

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 and the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 to allow, for 2022 only, the public holiday on the last Monday in May to be replaced by public holidays on 2<sup>nd</sup> and 3<sup>rd</sup> June to mark Her Majesty the Queen’s Platinum Jubilee.
