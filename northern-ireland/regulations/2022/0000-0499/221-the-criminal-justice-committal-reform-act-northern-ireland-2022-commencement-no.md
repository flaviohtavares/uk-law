# The Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

> Text as at 2022-09-14.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2022 No. 221 (C. 23)

Committal Reform

The Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

14th September 2022

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation[^key-d2c2e2f8324db09960e5d6eb20ddef25]

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022.

## 2. Appointed Day[^key-8600e4bab46e6db559b0ff6a166fa722]

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 is 17th October 2022–

- (a) section 1 (Abolition of preliminary investigations);
- (b) section 2 (Abolition of mixed committals: evidence on oath not to be given at preliminary inquiry);
- (c) section 3 (Consequential amendments and repeals);
- (d) the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 14th September 2022

Naomi Long

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation sections 1 to 3 of, and the Schedule to, the Criminal Justice (Committal Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) [2022 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2022/4).
