# The Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

> Text as at 2023-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2022 No. 303

Sea Fisheries

The Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

30th December 2022

Coming into operation

1st January 2023

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 19(1) and 124(1), (2) and (2A) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-77606487746c2730072eba775a75967e]

These Regulations may be cited as the Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022[^key-9674d8564789f3f0460ffbf6480f0e1c]

(1) The Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3(1)(g) delete “restricted area”.

(3) In regulation 4(3) after “10” insert “metres”.

(4) In Schedule 5 (Red Bay Special Area of Conservation)—

- (a) In the longitude coordinate at Point A for “5° 36.670’W” substitute “006° 01.492’W”.
- (b) In the longitude coordinate at Point B for “5° 34.734’W” substitute “006° 00.516’W”.
- (c) In the latitude coordinate at Point C for “55° 08.450’N” substitute “55° 05.450’N”.
- (d) In the longitude coordinate at Point C for “5° 34.733’W” substitute “006° 00.516’W”.
- (e) In the longitude coordinate at Point D for “5° 35.203’W” substitute “006° 02.816’W”.

(5) In Schedule 6 (Skerries and Causeway Special Area of Conservation)—

- (a) In the latitude coordinate at Point C for “55° 11.540’N” substitute “55° 13.540’N”.
- (b) In the longitude coordinate at Point E for “006° 36.920’W” substitute “006° 26.920’W”.

(6) In Schedule 8 for “Area 2” where it first appears substitute “Area 1”.

(7) In Schedule 9 in the Schedule head for “Strangford Lough” substitute “Waterfoot”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 30th December 2022.

Owen Lyttle

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Marine Protected Areas (Prohibited Methods of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 (the Principal Regulations) to provide for minor corrections.

These corrections include the correction of the name of Strangford Lough Marine Conservation Zone in regulation 3(1)(g), the insertion of the word metres at regulation 4(3), the substitution of a number of correct latitude and longitude coordinates in Schedule 5 (Red Bay Special Area of Conservation) and Schedule 6 (Skerries and Causeway Special Area of Conservation) respectively, and the amendment of the heading at Schedule 9 so that it refers to the Waterfoot Marine Conservation Zone.
