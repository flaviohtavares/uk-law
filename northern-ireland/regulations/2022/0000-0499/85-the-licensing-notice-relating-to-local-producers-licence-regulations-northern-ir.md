# The Licensing (Notice Relating to Local Producer’s Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 85

Licensing

The Licensing (Notice Relating to Local Producer’s Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

7th March 2022

Coming into operation

7th April 2022

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 52B(6), 52C(6) and 52D(10) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing (Notice Relating to Local Producer’s Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 7th April 2022.

## 2. Form and dimensions of Notice

A notice under Article 52B(6) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 must –

- (a) contain the information specified in Schedule 1; and
- (b) be contained in a document the dimensions of which are no less than 297 mm in length and 210 mm in width.

**3.** A notice under Article 52C(6) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 must –

- (a) contain the information specified in Schedule 2; and
- (b) be contained in a document the dimensions of which are no less than 297 mm in length and 210 mm in width.

**4.** A notice under Article 52D(10) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 must –

- (a) contain the information specified in Schedule 3; and
- (b) be contained in a document the dimensions of which are no less than 297 mm in length and 210 mm in width.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 7th March 2022.

(L.S.)

Anthony Carleton

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the notice relating to the sale and consumption of intoxicating liquor that producers of intoxicating liquor must display at their own premises and when selling from other licensed and unlicensed premises, and the information the notices must contain.
