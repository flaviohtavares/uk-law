# The Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 45

Education

The Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

10th February 2022

Coming into operation

10th March 2022

The Department of Education[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 7(2) and 43(5) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022 and comes into operation on 10th March 2022.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

(1) The Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Part 5 of Schedule 2, in the Table headed “LEARNING FOR LIFE AND WORK: Personal Development”, in the Column entitled “Personal Health” after “Pupils should have opportunities to:” insert “Receive training in (i) cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR); and (ii) automatic external defibrillator (AED) awareness.”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 10th February 2022

(L.S.)

Suzanne Kingon

A senior officer of the Department of Education

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order amends the Education (Curriculum Minimum Content) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 to add cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) training and automatic external defibrillator (AED) awareness to the list of topics that must be taught as part of Learning for Life and Work: Personal Development at Key Stage 3 in post-primary schools.
