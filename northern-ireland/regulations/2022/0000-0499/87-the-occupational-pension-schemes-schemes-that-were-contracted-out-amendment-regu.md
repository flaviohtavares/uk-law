# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 87

Pensions

The Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made

9th March 2022

Coming into operation

6th April 2022

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(3) and 177(2) and (3) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2022.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) Regulations

In regulation 15(2) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00004] (fixed rate revaluation of guaranteed minimum pensions for early leavers)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b) for “2017.” substitute “2017 but before 6th April 2022;”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (b) add—

  > - (c) 3.25 per cent. compound, where the period of pensionable service under the scheme terminates on or after 6th April 2022.

  .

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 9th March 2022

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 to provide for a new fixed rate of revaluation of guaranteed minimum pension for early leavers. The new fixed rate of 3.25 per cent. applies to members of pension schemes that were contracted-out who leave pensionable service on or after 6th April 2022.
