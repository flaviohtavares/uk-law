# Prohibition of the Sale of Rod Caught Salmon Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 119

Fisheries

Prohibition of the Sale of Rod Caught Salmon Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

29th April 2013

Coming into operation

1st June 2013

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure [^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(1)(jj) [^f00002] of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^f00003] and now vested in it [^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Prohibition of the Sale of Rod Caught Salmon Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2013.

## 2. Prohibition of sale

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person shall neither sell nor offer nor expose for sale salmon caught by rod and line in any fishery to which section 25 of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 applies.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in relation to salmon so caught before the coming into operation of these Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 29<sup>th</sup> April 2013.

(L.S.)

M. Cory

A senior officer of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prohibit the sale or offer or exposure for sale of salmon caught by rod and line in certain inland fisheries.

This prohibition does not apply in relation to salmon so caught before the Regulations come into operation.
