# The Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 218

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

3rd September 2013

Coming into operation

30th September 2013

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 65(1) and 67(1)(m) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 30<sup>th</sup> September 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

In Schedule 2 to the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] (classes of vehicles to which these regulations do not apply) after item 40 add—

> **41.** Goods vehicles not exceeding 3,500 kilograms having a date of manufacture before 1<sup>st</sup> January 1960.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3<sup>rd</sup> September 2013

Iain Greenway

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 of the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 to allow goods vehicles not exceeding 3,500 kilograms manufactured before 1<sup>st</sup> January 1960 to be used on the roads without a vehicle test certificate. Article 4(2) of Directive [2009/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0040) (OJ L141, 6.6.2009, p.12) allows Member States to exempt such vehicles from roadworthiness testing.

A copy of Directive [2009/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0040) may be downloaded without charge from EUR-Lex, the European Law website (http://eur-lex.europa.eu/en/index.htm).
