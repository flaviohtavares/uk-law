# The Policing and Community Safety Partnerships (Designated Organisations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 38

Justice

Police

The Policing and Community Safety Partnerships (Designated Organisations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

22 February 2013

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1(2)

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by paragraph 7(3) of Schedules 1 and 2 to the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

In accordance with paragraph 7(4)(a) of Schedules 1 and 2 to that Act, the Department of Justice has consulted with each Policing and Community Safety Partnership (PCSP) and with each District Policing and Community Safety Partnership (DPCSP).

In accordance with paragraph 7(4)(b) of Schedules 1 and 2 to that Act, a draft of this Order was laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Policing and Community Safety Partnerships (Designated Organisations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Representatives of designated organisations

The Department designates the following organisations for the purposes of paragraph 7 of Schedules 1 and 2 to the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011:

- (a) the Police Service of Northern Ireland;
- (b) the Northern Ireland Housing Executive;
- (c) the Probation Board for Northern Ireland;
- (d) the Youth Justice Agency of Northern Ireland;
- (e) the Northern Ireland Fire and Rescue Service;
- (f) the Education and Library Board whose operational area includes the council district for which the relevant PCSP was established or the police district for which the relevant DPCSP was established[^f00002]; and
- (g) the Health and Social Care Trust whose operational area includes the council district for which the relevant PCSP was established or the police district for which the relevant DPCSP was established[^f00003].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 22 February 2013

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates organisations for the purposes of paragraph 7 of Schedules 1 and 2 to the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011. Those organisations must nominate a person to attend a meeting of a Policing and Community Safety Partnership or a District Policing and Community Safety Partnership, as the case may be, or a committee appointed under paragraph 14 of Schedule 1 or 2.

[^f00003]: Footnote: Health and Social Care Trusts were established under [S.R. 2006 No. 292](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/292), [S.R. 2006 No. 293](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/293), [S.R. 2006 No. 294](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/294), [S.R. 2006 No. 295](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/295) and [S.R. 2006 No. 296](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/296), as amended by [S.R. 2007 No. 269](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/269) and [S.R. 2008 No. 426](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/426).
