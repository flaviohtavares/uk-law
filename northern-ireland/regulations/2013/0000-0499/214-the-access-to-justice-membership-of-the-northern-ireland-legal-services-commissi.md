# The Access to Justice (Membership of the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 214

Access To Justice

The Access to Justice (Membership of the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

8th August 2013

Coming into operation

1st September 2013

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4(1) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Access to Justice (Membership of the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2013.

## 2. Amendments to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003

In the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (membership of the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission), in Article 4(1)(b)—

- (a) for “six” substitute “one”;
- (b) for “ten” substitute “three”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 8th August 2013

(L.S.)

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Article 4(1)(b) of the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, which prescribes the minimum and maximum number of members (other than the chair) of the Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission.

This Order—

- reduces the minimum number of members, other than the chair, from six to one; and
- reduces the maximum number of members, other than the chair, from ten to three.
