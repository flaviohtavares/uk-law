# The County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 18

County Courts

The County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

31st January 2013

Coming into operation

25th February 2013

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 22(1) of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 25th February 2013.

## 2. Increase of financial limits of civil jurisdiction of county courts

The County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980 shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) In Article 10(1) (general civil jurisdiction) for “£15,000” substitute “£30,000”; and
- (b) In Article 30(1) (general civil jurisdiction exercisable by District Judges) for “£5,000” substitute “£10,000”.

## 3. Revocations and saving

The entry relating to Article 10(1) of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980 in the Schedule to the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] is revoked.

**4.** Article 3(a) of the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) Order 2001[^f00003] is revoked.

**5.** The amendments made by Articles 2, 3 and 4 of this Order shall not apply to any proceedings commenced before 25th February 2013.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 31st January 2013

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the general civil jurisdiction of county courts in Northern Ireland from £15,000 to £30,000 and the jurisdiction of district judges in the county courts from £5,000 to £10,000.
