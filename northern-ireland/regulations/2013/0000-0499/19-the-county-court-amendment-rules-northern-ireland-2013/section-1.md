# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2013, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 25th February 2013.

**2.** In these Rules, a reference to an Order, rule, Appendix or Form is a reference to that Order, rule, Appendix or Form so numbered in the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002].
