# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2013, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Savings

The amendments made by rules 5, 7, and 19 shall not affect any proceedings which are pending immediately before these Rules come into operation and the Rules in operation before that date shall continue to apply to those proceedings.
