# The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 83 (C. 5)

Pensions

The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

26th March 2013

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(3) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

(2) In this Order any reference to a numbered section or Schedule is to the section of, or Schedule to, the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012 bearing that number.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) section 22 (Pension Protection Fund) in so far as it is not already in operation, and
- (b) in Schedule 4 (Pension Protection Fund)—
  - (i) paragraph 1 in so far as it is not already in operation;
  - (ii) paragraphs 21 to 28 (postponement of compensation), and
  - (iii) paragraphs 29 to 36 (pension compensation sharing: postponement of compensation),

is 27th March 2013.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 26th March 2013

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 22 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012, in so far as they are not already in operation, on 27th March 2013. Those provisions relate to the postponement of compensation from the Pension Protection Fund.
