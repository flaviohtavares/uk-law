# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013, section 14A

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14A. Conditions of permits: Materials facilities[^key-5a260d6ab8d60a86d9977be4a011e716]

*Extent: N.I.*

The enforcing authority shall ensure that a permit granted or varied on or after 1st April 2025 authorising the operation of a materials facility contains a condition requiring the holder of the permit to comply with the Materials Facilities Code.
