# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013, section 1

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-2ba8715f5a7320c1e99fbfc95f1c8641]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall, subject to Schedules 3 and 17, come into operation on 20th June 2013.
