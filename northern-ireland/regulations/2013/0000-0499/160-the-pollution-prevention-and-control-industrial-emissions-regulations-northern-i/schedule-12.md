# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013, SCHEDULE 12

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 12 — PETROL VAPOUR RECOVERY

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 7

### PART 1 — PVR I

#### 1. Application[^c-22ebaeaa8e63b7c5ebfa4093508be30d]

This Part applies in relation to every Part C activity falling within paragraphs (b) of Part B and paragraphs (a) and (b) of Part C of section 1.2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1.

#### 2. Interpretation[^c-a95d34bc0de456f1fae20e2ef2d3ea0f]

In this Part, the “PVR I” means European Parliament and Council Directive [94/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0063)[^f00057] of 20 December 1994 on the control of VOC emissions resulting from the storage of petrol and its distribution from terminals to service stations.

#### 3. Exercise of enforcing authority functions[^c-0b451dea19b872fda03277d38b139e1b]

(1) The enforcing authorities must exercise their relevant functions under regulation 7 so as to ensure compliance with the following provisions of the PVR I—

- (a) the first paragraph of Article 3(1), (storage installations at terminals);
- (b) the first and last paragraphs of Article 4(1), and 4(3), (loading and unloading of mobile containers at terminals); and
- (c) the first paragraph of Article 6(1) (loading into storage installations at service stations).

(2) When interpreting the PVR I for the purposes of this paragraph ignore points 2.3, 3.2 and 3.5 of Annex IV to the PVR I.

### PART 2 — PVR II

#### 1. Application[^c-825d5f0884322c6c7c160c4c7bb73cd2]

This Part applies in relation to every Part C activity falling within paragraphs (c) to (e) of Part C of section 1.2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1.

#### 2. Interpretation[^c-00060456934a2a3a25bc372ff0f61998][^c-dde6e7a10fb594741dc94e48a643701e]

(1) In this Part, “PVR II” means [Directive 2009/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0126) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 on Stage II petrol vapour recovery during refuelling of motor vehicles at service stations as last amended by Commission [Directive 2014/99/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0099).

(2) When interpreting the PVR II for the purposes of this paragraph—

- (a) in Articles 3, 4 and 5, ignore the words “Member States shall ensure that” where they occur; and
- (b) in Article 4, ignore the words “with effect from the date on which Stage II petrol vapour recovery systems become mandatory pursuant to Article 3”.

#### 3. Exercise of district council functions[^c-3feb37a83df2e171f9d95efc577f45d9]

The district councils must exercise their functions under regulation 7 so as to ensure compliance with the following provisions of the PVR II—

- (a) Article 3 (service stations);
- (b) Article 4 (minimum level of petrol vapour recovery); and
- (c) Article 5 (periodic checks and consumer information).
