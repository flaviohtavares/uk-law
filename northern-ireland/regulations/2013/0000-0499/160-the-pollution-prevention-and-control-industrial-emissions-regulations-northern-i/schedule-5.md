# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 — POLLUTANTS

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 12(2)

Indicative list of the main polluting substances to be taken into account if they are relevant for fixing emission limit values:

### 1. AIR[^c-2a3de003b0e2100b4bee6d2321dd510b]

Sulphur dioxide and other sulphur compounds.

**2.**[^c-b4e0ebeaebed1e8af275495e7ee3cdc1] Oxides of nitrogen and other nitrogen compounds.

**3.**[^c-2f94adf884e090f92d2b107090cde50b] Carbon monoxide.

**4.**[^c-76a71483fdd43a62c7edf0e090da2786] Volatile organic compounds.

**5.**[^c-325241cf58fb5897b5eddc2cc26bf2b4] Metals and their compounds.

**6.**[^c-31eae503656dc58a4151627c69816ee3] Dust including fine particulate matter.

**7.**[^c-0bbc65c0add1f580a7849eedc695250a] Asbestos (suspended particulates, fibres).

**8.**[^c-d0910ee67a07a41bd2ededfb962b68dd] Chlorine and its compounds.

**9.**[^c-52a17e1ac55b4198d1a6352386b46e83] Fluorine and its compounds.

**10.**[^c-3500f9d759cc7035e720d00d073d8814] Arsenic and its compounds.

**11.**[^c-8a875cf3daf7e08c71e70d7efafe0b60] Cyanides.

**12.**[^c-cd14404b14e5710e0bb512d885866f76] Substances and mixtures which have been proved to possess carcinogenic or mutagenic properties or properties which may affect reproduction via the air.

**13.**[^c-fa88d9b53afb884c386e1bea759c21c8] Polychlorinated dibenzodioxins and polychlorinated dibenzofurans.

### 1. WATER[^c-ad9fb857f4ec1aece526d5aacc2af824]

Organohalogen compounds and substances which may form such compounds in the aquatic environment.

**2.**[^c-f4d0c05641bfbe88c915add197f88681] Organophosphorus compounds.

**3.**[^c-93036b5311404daf39e518ad730e02c5] Organotin compounds.

**4.**[^c-4ff7dd4dc320de1923146f795a43a512] Substances and mixtures which have been proved to possess carcinogenic or mutagenic properties or properties which may affect reproduction in or via the aquatic environment.

**5.**[^c-f1005edf634cefa96f29cc96034001e1] Persistent hydrocarbons and persistent and bioaccumulable organic toxic substances.

**6.**[^c-431c2f50ee673ab7cd47dbe3bdd1257d] Cyanides.

**7.**[^c-fe5c95edfaab6ee488efc716aeec8e66] Metals and their compounds.

**8.**[^c-8475e68c8b6523e0eec3459e2910f461] Arsenic and its compounds.

**9.**[^c-5e9fee5b211a0aa054e1fc16b18dde0b] Biocides and plant protection products.

**10.**[^c-8e360a7dc167843d2dfd53cb8daf85a9] Materials in suspension.

**11.**[^c-21c128d4697f47063c00a95791cf15bd] Substances which contribute to eutrophication (in particular, nitrates and phosphates).

**12.**[^c-956093d8855b5ef486af4f242c8726e2] Substances which have an unfavourable influence on the oxygen balance (and can be measured using parameters such as BOD, COD, etc.).

**13.**[^c-a9a1a3aea13056a501b18c45410fabca] Substances listed in Annex X to Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060)[^f00052].
