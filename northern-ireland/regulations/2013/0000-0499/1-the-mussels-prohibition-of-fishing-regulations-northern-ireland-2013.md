# The Mussels (Prohibition of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

> Text as at 2018-12-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2013 No. 1

Sea Fisheries

The Mussels (Prohibition of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

3rd January 2013

Coming into operation

8th February 2013

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development [^c21522861] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 19(1) and 124(1) and (2) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^c21522871].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Mussels (Prohibition of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and come into operation on 8<sup>th</sup> February 2013.

## 2. Application

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), these Regulations apply in the Northern Ireland zone.

(2) These Regulations do not apply to fishing vessels within the meaning of Article 4(1)(4) of Regulation ([^c-0e87bbf5168a23fb0f6454bf3328086c]EU) No 1380/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the Common Fisheries Policy, as last amended by Regulation (EU) 2017/2092.

## 3. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966;
- “**mussels**” means shell-fish of the species *Mytilus Edulis*;
- “**mussel seed licence**” means a licence granted by the Department under section 4 of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 [^c21522891] for the collection of mussel seed.

## 4. Prohibition of fishing

Subject to regulation 6, fishing for mussels other than for direct human consumption is prohibited.

## 5. Prohibition of retaining on board

Subject to regulation 6, retaining mussels, other than mussels taken for direct human consumption, on board any sea-fishing boat is prohibited.

## 6. Exemptions

Regulation 4 and 5 do not apply in relation to any person operating under the authority and in accordance with the terms and conditions of—

- (a) a fish culture licence granted under section 11 of the Act;
- (b) a permit granted under section 14 of the Act; or
- (c) a mussel seed licence.

## 7. Revocation

The Mussels (Prohibition of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 [^c21522901] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 3<sup>rd</sup> January 2013.

John Speers

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations, which are made in reliance on Article 10 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2371/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/2371) on the conservation and sustainable exploitation of fisheries resources under the Common Fisheries Policy, revoke and re-enact with amendments the Mussels (Prohibition of Fishing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999. The Regulation prohibit, subject to certain exemptions, fishing for or retaining on board mussels, otherwise than for direct human consumption, in the Northern Ireland zone.
