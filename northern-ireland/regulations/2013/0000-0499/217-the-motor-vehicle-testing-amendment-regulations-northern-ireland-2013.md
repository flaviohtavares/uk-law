# The Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 217

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

3rd September 2013

Coming into operation

30th September 2013

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 63(5) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 30<sup>th</sup> September 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

In regulation 6(1) of the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] (exemptions) at the end of sub-paragraph (xxi) omit the full stop and add—

> ; and
>
> - (xxii) a vehicle having a date of manufacture before 1<sup>st</sup> January 1960.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3<sup>rd</sup> September 2013

Iain Greenway

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 6 of the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 to allow vehicles manufactured before 1<sup>st</sup> January 1960 to be used on the roads without a MoT test certificate. Article 4(2) of Directive [2009/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0040) (OJ L141, 6.6.2009, p.12) allows Member States to exempt such vehicles from roadworthiness testing.

A copy of Directive [2009/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0040) may be downloaded without charge from EUR-Lex, the European Law website (http://eur-lex.europa.eu/en/index.htm).

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment have been produced and are available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online athttp://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
