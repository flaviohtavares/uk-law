# The Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 138 (C. 9)

Forestry

The Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

15th May 2013

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(1) of the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010.

## 2. Appointed Day

17th June 2013 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 15th May 2013.

Malcolm Beatty

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
