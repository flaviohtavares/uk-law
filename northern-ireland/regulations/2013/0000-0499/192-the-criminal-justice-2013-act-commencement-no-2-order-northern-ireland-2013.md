# The Criminal Justice (2013 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No.192 (C. 12)

Justice

The Criminal Justice (2013 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

11th July 2013

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(3) of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00001] and, in accordance with section 13(5) of that Act with the approval of the First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly, and following a request from the Chief Constable, and after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (2013 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

(2) In this Order, “the 2013 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2013.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day for the coming into operation of section 13(1) of the 2013 Act is 13 July 2013.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 11 July 2013

David Ford

Minister of Justice

Approved by the First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly on 11 July 2013

Peter Robinson

First Minister

Martin McGuinness

deputy First Minister

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 13(1) of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) [2013 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2013/7) (“the 2013 Act”).

Section 13(1) of the 2013 Act disapplies section 7 of the Sunday Observance Act (Ireland) 1695 to allow for a magistrates’ court, in the exercise of its criminal jurisdiction, to be held on a Sunday.

In accordance with section 13(4) of the 2013 Act, this Order will cease to be in operation one month from the day appointed in Article 2 of this Order for the coming into operation of section 13(1) of the 2013 Act.
