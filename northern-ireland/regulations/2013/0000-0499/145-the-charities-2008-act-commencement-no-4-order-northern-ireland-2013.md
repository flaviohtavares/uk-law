# The Charities (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 145 (C. 10)

Charities

The Charities (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

29th May 2013

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 185(1) of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Charities (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 specified in the Schedule is 24th June 2013.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 29th May 2013

(L.S.)

Michael Daly

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation provisions of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 relating to the establishment and maintenance of a register of charities.

The Order also commences provisions relating to charity names, application of property cy-près, charities governed by Royal charter, relief of trustees from liability, alteration of objects and certain other consents required from the Commission by incorporated charities, requirement to disclose charitable status, powers of unincorporated charities, false statements concerning institutions which are not registered charities, charity mergers, designated religious charities, supply of documents open to public inspection and a restriction on the institution of proceedings for certain offences.
