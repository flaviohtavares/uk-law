# The Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 34 (C. 3)

Environmental Protection

The Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

15th February 2013

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(1) of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into operation of—

- (a) section 1 (fixed penalty notices for offences under Article 4); and
- (b) section 2 (detention of seized property),

of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 12<sup>th</sup> March 2013.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15<sup>th</sup> February 2013.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation sections 1 and 2 of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002] on 12<sup>th</sup> March 2013.

NOTE AS TO EARLIER COMMENCEMENT ORDERS

(This note is not part of the Order)
