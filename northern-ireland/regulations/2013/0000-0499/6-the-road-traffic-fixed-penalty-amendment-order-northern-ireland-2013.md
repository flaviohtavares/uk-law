# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 6

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made

15th January 2013

Coming into operation

18th February 2013

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 59(1) of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001].

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 18<sup>th</sup> February 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order

Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) omit item 2; and
- (b) after item 9 insert—

  > |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- |
  > | 9A | A fixed penalty offence under section 1(6) of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00003] | £200 |

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15<sup>th</sup> January 2013

Iain Greenway

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 as a consequence of the implementation of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (“the Goods Vehicles Act”) by omitting item 2 and re-introducing the fixed penalty offence as item 9A.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
