# The Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 148

HARBOURS, DOCKS ETC.,

The Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

7th April 1998

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation

19th May 1998

The Department of the Environment and the Department of Agriculture in exercise of the powers conferred on them by Article 19 of the Ports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994[^f00001] and of every other power enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 19th May 1998.

## 2. Amendment of the Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

(1) The Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Article 36(5) (disposal statements), for the words “paragraph 4(1)” there shall be substituted “paragraph 5(1)”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Ritchie

Assistant Secretary

6th April 1998.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

G. Lavery

Assistant Secretary

7th April 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Ports (Levy on Disposals of Land, etc.) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 by correcting a minor drafting error in Article 36(5) relating to disposal statements.
