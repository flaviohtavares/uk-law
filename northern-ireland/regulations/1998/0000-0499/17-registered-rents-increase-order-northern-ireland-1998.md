# Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 17

LANDLORD AND TENANT

Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

27th January 1998

Coming into operation

9th March 1998

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 33(2) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, having complied with the requirements of Article 33(1) of that Order and determined that the rents hereinafter mentioned should be increased, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 9th March 1998.

## 2. Increase of registered rents

The rents registered under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 for dwelling-houses which are let under regulated tenancies shall be increased by 4.8 per cent.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. McCormick

Assistant Secretary

27th January 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order increases the rents registered with the Department of the Environment under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, for dwelling houses which are let under regulated tenancies, by 4.8 per cent from 9th March 1998. Under Article 22(4) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 a notice of increase of rent which gives effect to an increase made by virtue of this Order shall not take effect earlier than 4 weeks after the commencement of this Order, i.e., not earlier than 6th April 1998.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks' notice of the increase in accordance with Article 22(3)(*b*) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978.
