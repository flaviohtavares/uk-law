# The North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 123

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

25th March 1998

Coming into operation

1st April 1998

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraphs 23(1) of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health and personal social services to make this order, and after completion of the consultation prescribed by paragraph 23(3) of Schedule 3 to the said Order[^f00002], hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This order may be cited as the North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1998.

## 2. Dissolution of HSS trust

The North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust is hereby dissolved.

## 3. Revocation

The North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

John McGrath

Assistant Secretary

25th March 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust on 1st April 1998.

It revokes the North Down and Ards Community Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993, as amended, from that date.
