# The Road Traffic Regulation (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 296 (C. 12)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic Regulation (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

19th August 1998

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Regulation (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in the Schedule shall be 2nd November 1998.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

19th August 1998.
