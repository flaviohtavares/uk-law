# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section E2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E2. Nature of retirement benefits[^c-3a877de2891126196046076c025888f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Retirement benefits consist of—

- (a) a retirement pension; and
- (b) a retirement lump sum,

calculated in accordance with this Part.
