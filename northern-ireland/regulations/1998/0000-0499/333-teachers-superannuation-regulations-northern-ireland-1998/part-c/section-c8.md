# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section C8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C8. Contributions for family benefits[^c-aac002106d593a1d83fb54c985b73129]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Parts I and III of Schedule 6 have effect for enabling men and unmarried women to pay additional contributions (“family benefit contributions”) in order to secure, or to increase, family benefits in respect of reckonable service before 1st April 1972.

(2) Parts II and III of Schedule 6 have effect for enabling married women to pay family benefit contributions in respect of reckonable service before 6th April 1988.
