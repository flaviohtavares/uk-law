# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section C2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C2. Ordinary contributions[^key-0f29dadb686b30898a3562ef89861803]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) a person who is in pensionable employment shall pay contributions of 6 per cent of his contributable salary for the time being.

(2) Contributions shall not be paid by any person whose reckonable service exceeds 45 years.
