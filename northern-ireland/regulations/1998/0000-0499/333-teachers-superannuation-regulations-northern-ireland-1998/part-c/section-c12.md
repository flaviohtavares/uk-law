# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section C12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C12. Application for repayment[^c-fa41d8cd4e82c379a0c4bf9dcfe1e34f]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A person shall not be entitled to a payment under regulation C9 or C11 unless he—

- (a) applies in writing to the Department for payment; and
- (b) provides such relevant information as the Department may reasonably require.
