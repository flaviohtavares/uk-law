# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section D2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D2. Exclusion from reckonable service[^key-6c825292d733a3c229e18694826f8200]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The following shall not count as reckonable service—

- (a) any contributions refund period; and
- (b) any period in respect of which a transfer value has been paid under regulation F1 or a cash equivalent has been paid.
