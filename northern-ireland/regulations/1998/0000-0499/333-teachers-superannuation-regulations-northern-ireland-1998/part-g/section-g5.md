# Teachers' Superannuation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section G5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G5. Employers' contributions[^key-6c6827c389b7e6c45e14b980fb259cf0]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), the employer of a person in pensionable employment shall, during every relevant period, pay contributions of the required percentage of his contributable salary for the time being.

(2) The required percentage is

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1998/333)

where—

- A is the percentage specified for the relevant period under regulation G4(3); and
- B is any percentage specified for the relevant period under regulation G4(7) and (8).

(3) No contributions shall be paid in respect of anyone to whom regulation E32(2)(*a*) (restriction of reckonable service to 45 years) has become applicable.

(4) For the purposes of this regulation “relevant period” shall be construed in accordance with regulation G4(3).
