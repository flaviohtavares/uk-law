# Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Amendment[^key-1aa30b6c5096d0d3bfcf96417d3e3404]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Food Safety (Sampling and Qualifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00015], in Schedule 1 (provisions to which those regulations do not apply), for the title and reference of the 1993 Regulations there shall be substituted the title and reference of these Regulations.
