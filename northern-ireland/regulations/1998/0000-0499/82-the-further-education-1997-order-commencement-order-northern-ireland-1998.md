# The Further Education (1997 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 82 (C. 4)

EDUCATION

The Further Education (1997 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

6th March 1998

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Further Education (1997 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998.

## 2. Appointed day

The provisions of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (except those already in operation) shall come into operation on 1st April 1998.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

R. S. Davison

Assistant Secretary

6th March 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st April 1998 all the provisions of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 except Articles 1(3), 2, 10 and 24 and Schedule 2 which came into operation on the expiration of 2 months from 22nd July 1997, the day on which the Order was made.
