# Museums and Galleries (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 65 (C. 3)

EDUCATION

Museums and Galleries (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

3rd March 1998

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Museums and Galleries (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001] and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Museums and Galleries (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Museums and Galleries (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 shall be 1st April 1998.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

R. Jordan

Assistant Secretary

3rd March 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings the Museums and Galleries (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 into operation on the 1st April 1998.
