# The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 16

PLANT HEALTH

The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

26th January 1998

Coming into operation

2nd March 1998

The Department of Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00001], and 3(1)[^f00002], 3A[^f00003], 3B(1)[^f00004] and 4(1)[^f00005] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00006] and of all powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 2nd March 1998.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

(1) The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00007] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In Article 3(1) (Interpretation) for the term “Directive [95/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0044)” there shall be substituted the following—

> - “Directive [95/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0044)” means Commission Directive [95/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0044)[^f00008] establishing the conditions under which certain harmful organisms, plants, plant products and other objects listed in Annexes I to V to Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) may be introduced into or moved within the Community or certain protected zones thereof, for trial or scientific purposes and for work on varietal selections, as amended by Commission Directive [97/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0046)[^f00009];

.

(3) In Schedule 14 there shall be added at the end—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Council Directive [97/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0003) | O.J. No. L.27, 30.1.97, p. 30. |
> | Commission Directive [97/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0046) | O.J. No. L.204, 31.7.97, p. 43. |

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

R. S. Johnston

Assistant Secretary

26th January 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order which comes into operation on 2nd March 1998, further amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 (“the 1993 Order”). This includes Commission Directive [95/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0044), which lays down rules for the import of material, for trial or scientific purposes and for work on varietal selections, which would otherwise be banned. This Directive has now been amended by Commission Directive [97/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0046) to incorporate provisions on potato breeding material which were previously included in a separate, temporary, Commission Decision. The effect of the change is to convert the temporary arrangements into a permanent part of the regime. (Article 2(2)).

The Order also updates Schedule 14 to the 1993 Order (Instruments amending and supplementing Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)) (Article 2(3)).
