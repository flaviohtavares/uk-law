# The Social Security (Minimum Contributions to Appropriate Personal Pension Schemes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 136

PENSIONS

The Social Security (Minimum Contributions to Appropriate Personal Pension Schemes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

2nd April 1998

Coming into operation

6th April 1999

Whereas the Secretary of State for Social Security has made an Order[^f00001] under section 45A of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00002]:

Now, therefore, the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 41A of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Minimum Contributions to Appropriate Personal Pension Schemes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 6th April 1999.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Assembly.

## 2. Appropriate age-related percentages

For the purposes of section 41 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00005] (amount of minimum contributions) the appropriate age-related percentages in respect of earners in the tax years 1999-2000 to 2001-2002 are those specified in the table in the Schedule by reference to the earners' ages on the last day of the preceding tax year.

## 3. Amendment of previous Order

In the Social Security (Minimum Contributions to Appropriate Personal Pension Schemes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00006]—

- (a) in Article 2, for “to 2001-02” there shall be substituted “and 1998-99”, and
- (b) in the Schedule, in the table, the columns relating to the tax years 1999-2000, 2000-2001 and 2001-2002 shall be omitted.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland on

John O'Neill

Assistant Secretary

2nd April 1998.

This Order, which corresponds to an Order (S.I. [1998/944](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/944)) made by the Secretary of State for Social Security under section 45A of the Pension Schemes Act 1993, specifies with effect from 6th April 1999 the appropriate age-related percentages of earnings payable as minimum contributions in respect of members of appropriate personal pension schemes. It also amends the Social Security (Minimum Contributions to Appropriate Personal Pension Schemes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 to restrict its application to the tax years 1997-98 and 1998-99.
