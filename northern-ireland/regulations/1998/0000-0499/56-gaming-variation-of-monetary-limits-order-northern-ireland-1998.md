# Gaming (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> Text as at 2025-01-29.

*Extent: N.I.*

1998 No. 56

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

Gaming (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

26th February 1998

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation

9th April 1998

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 77(4), 106(3) and 108(16) of the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-beb25f5d754dfb587dad52ed0217053c]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 9th April 1998.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.

## 5. Revocations[^c-f4c47696b564ae085e5bea844f34ebb0]

The following are hereby revoked—

- (a) Article 4 of the Gaming and Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00006];
- (b) Articles 2 and 3(*a*) of the Gaming (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00007].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Jeremy Harbison

Under Secretary

26th February 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order—

- (a) increases the maximum money prize which can be distributed or offered in gaming for prizes on bingo club premises from £0·30 to £5;
- (b) increases, in any one determination of winners in gaming for prizes on bingo club premises, the maximum permitted aggregate amount taken by way of the sale of chances from £30 to £50 and the maximum permitted aggregate amount or value of prizes from £30 to £50;
- (c) increases the maximum charge for playing a game once by means of a gaming machine in a registered club and in certain other premises from £0·25 to £0·30; and
- (d) increases the maximum entitlement to money prizes in gaming by means of a gaming machine in those other premises from £4 to £5.
