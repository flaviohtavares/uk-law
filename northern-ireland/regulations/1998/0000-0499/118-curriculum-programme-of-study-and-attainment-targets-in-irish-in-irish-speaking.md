# Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish in Irish Speaking Schools at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1998 No. 118

EDUCATION

Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish in Irish Speaking Schools at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made

19th March 1998

Coming into operation in accordance with Articles 2 and 3

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 7(1)(*a*) and (5) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish in Irish Speaking Schools at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of this Order shall come into operation in relation to Irish speaking schools—

- (a) on 1st August 1998 in respect of pupils in the third key stage and in the first year of the fourth key stage; and
- (b) on 1st August 1999 in respect of pupils in the second year of the fourth key stage.

**3.** The provisions of this Order shall come into operation in relation to schools other than Irish speaking schools—

- (a) on 1st August 1998 in respect of pupils in the first year of the third key stage;
- (b) on 1st August 1999 in respect of pupils in the second year of the third key stage and the first year of the fourth key stage; and
- (c) on 1st August 2000 in respect of pupils in the third year of the third key stage and the second year of the fourth key stage.

## 4. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Document” means the document published by The Stationery Office entitled “Key Stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish (in Irish Speaking Schools)”[^f00002]; and
- references to “level descriptions” are references to the levels set out in the Document in relation to each attainment target at the third key stage.

## 5. Specification of the programme of study and attainment targets

It is hereby directed that the provisions relating to the programme of study and attainment targets set out in the Document shall have effect, in relation to pupils who have followed the programme of study and attainment targets for Irish in Irish Speaking Schools specified for use at the first and second key stages under the Curriculum (Programmes of Study and Attainment Targets at Key Stages 1 and 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003], as provided in Articles 6 and 7 for the purpose of specifying in relation to Irish (in Irish speaking schools only)—

- (a) a programme of study; and
- (b) attainment targets.

**6.** The programme of study for the third and fourth key stages and attainment targets for the third key stage described in the Document (to which the level descriptions referred to in Article 8 relate) are specified in relation to those key stages as set out in the Document.

**7.** The attainment targets described in the Document are specified in relation to the third key stage.

**8.** The level descriptions related to the attainment targets described in the Document are specified in relation to the third key stage at levels 1 to 8.

**9.** The contents which are printed in italics in the Document or which are not printed on a tinted background serve to illustrate the programme of study and attainment targets described and do not form part of the provisions which have effect by virtue of this Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

C. Jendoubi (Mrs.)

Assistant Secretary

19th March 1998.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order, made under Article 7(1) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (“the 1989 Order”), specifies a programme of study for key stages 3 and 4 and attainment targets for key stage 3 in relation to Irish in Irish Speaking Schools.

Article 7(5) of the 1989 Order allows for an Order under Article 7(1), instead of containing the provisions to be made, to refer to provisions in a Document published by The Stationery Office and to direct that these provisions shall have effect according to the Order.

This Order accordingly refers to “Key Stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish (in Irish Speaking Schools)” and provides for the programme of study and attainment targets set out in it to have effect for all pupils at the third and fourth key stages who have followed the programme of study and attainment targets for Irish in Irish Speaking Schools as specified under the Curriculum (Programmes of Study and Attainment Targets at Key Stages 1 and 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996. The Order applies to all such pupils, whether or not they attend an Irish speaking post-primary school.

It further provides that any contents printed in italics in the Document, or which are not printed on a tinted background, are for illustrative purposes only and do not form part of the provisions which have effect by virtue of this Order.

This Order comes into operation on a phased basis from 1st August 1998.

Copies of the Document entitled “Key Stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in Irish (in Irish Speaking Schools)” may be obtained from The Stationery Office Bookshop, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast BT1 4GD.
