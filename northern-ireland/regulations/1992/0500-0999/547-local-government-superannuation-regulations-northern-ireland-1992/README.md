These regulations consolidate (with amendments) the Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 (“the 1981 regulations”) as amended.The Statutory Rules specified in Schedule 21 are revoked and re-enacted in a new presentation.This new presentation brings together regulations with the same subject matter in one Part.Each Part of these regulations is identified by a capital letter and the regulations within each Part are identified by the appropriate letter and by numbers which run from “1” within each Part.

Considerable drafting changes in the layout and terminology of the 1981 regulations have been made but in general the existing provisions have been reproduced.

The following new provisions are introduced—
