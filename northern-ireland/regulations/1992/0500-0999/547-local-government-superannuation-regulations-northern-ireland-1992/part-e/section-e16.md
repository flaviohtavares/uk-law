# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section E16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E16. Adjustment of superannuation rights on death of certain re-employed pensioners

(1) This paragraph applies where—

- (a) a person was entitled to a retirement pension other than one which was reduced under regulation E3(11); and
- (b) after becoming so entitled he entered further local government employment; and
- (c) he dies in the further employment; and
- (d) if he had then ceased to be employed otherwise than by reason of his death he would have been entitled to give notice under regulation E14 (combined benefits).

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies—

- (a) any benefits payable in respect of the person (except any widow’s short-term pension or children’s short-term pension) are to be calculated; and
- (b) any surrender of part of a retirement pension has effect,

as if immediately before his death he had become entitled to benefits under regulations E14 or E15, whichever is the more favourable to the person entitled to receive the benefits payable.
