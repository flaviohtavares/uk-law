# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**10.** Schedule 9 — this Schedule applies for the calculation of additional periods of reckonable service under regulation E3(9).

Article 14 of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 confers express power to make regulations retrospective in effect.These regulations are to a certain extent retrospective but, where rights in relation to ex-employees could be adversely affected, provision is made to opt out (regulation S2).
