# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section N6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## N6. Decisions by the Committee as to amount of benefits

(1) Where a person is entitled to a benefit which is or may become payable out of the fund, the Committee are to decide the amount of the benefit.

(2) The amount of a benefit is to be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the person becomes entitled to it.

(3) For the purposes of this regulation “benefit” includes a return of contributions and a benefit specified in regulation F6(11) or (14).
