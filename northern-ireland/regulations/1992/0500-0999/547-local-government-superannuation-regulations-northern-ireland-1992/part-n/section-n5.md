# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section N5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## N5. Decisions by the Committee as to entitlement to benefits

(1) Any question whether a person is entitled to a benefit under these regulations is to be decided by the Committee.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), “benefit” includes a return of contributions but does not include a benefit specified in Part L or regulation F6(11) or (14).

(3) Any such question as is mentioned in paragraph (1) is to be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the cessation of the employment or, as the case may be, the death of the employee.
