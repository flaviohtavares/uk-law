# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**6.** In Part P (Administration and management)—

- (a) P8 — this safeguards the rights of an employee where payments were being made under regulation 43 of the 1981 regulations;
- (b) P9 — under this regulation a scheduled body shall repay to the fund any extra charges resulting from an increase of reckonable service;
- (c) P10 — contains details of payments and statements to be made to the Committee by employing authorities.
