# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, SCHEDULE 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 14 — Public Service Schemes

Regulations J3(3), F3(10), F6(2), (4), (5) and (6)

### PART I

**1.** Regulations from time to time in force under the Police Pensions Act 1948[^f00089] or the Police Pensions Act 1976[^f00090].

**2.** Orders and regulations from time to time in force under the Constabulary Acts (Northern Ireland) 1922 to 1949[^f00091] or section 25 of the Police Act (Northern Ireland) 1970[^f00092].

**3.** The Firemen’s Pension Scheme brought into operation under section 26 of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00093], as in force from time to time.

**4.** Orders from time to time in force under section 10 of the Fire Services (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1950[^f00094] (“the Act of 1950”) or section 17 of the Fire Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1969[^f00095] (“the Act of 1969”) and schemes from time to time in force under section 13 of the Act of 1950 or section 26 of the Act of 1969.

### PART II

**5.** The Teachers' (Superannuation) Act 1925[^f00096] and rules from time to time in force under that Act, a Teachers' Superannuation Scheme within the meaning of section 145(47) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1962[^f00097], and regulations from time to time in force under section 102 of that Act or under the Teachers' Superannuation Act 1965[^f00098], the Teachers' Superannuation Act 1967[^f00099], the Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00100] or section 9 of the Act of 1972.

**6.** A 1923 Act scheme within the meaning of the Teachers' (Superannuation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1950[^f00101] and regulations from time to time in force under the Teachers' Superannuation Acts (Northern Ireland) 1950 to 1967[^f00102] or Article 11 of the Order.

### PART III

**7.** Provisions of or under the Local Government Superannuation Act 1937[^f00103] the Act of 1953, section 7 of the Act of 1972, or a local Act scheme, as in force from time to time, being provisions relating to the payment of pensions, allowances or gratuities by local authorities or other bodies in England.

**8.** Provisions of or under the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Act 1937[^f00104], the Act of 1953, section 7 of the Act of 1972, or a local Act scheme, as in force from time to time, being provisions relating to the payment of pensions, allowances or gratuities by local authorities or other bodies in Scotland.

**9.** Regulations from time to time in force under section 67 of the National Health Service Act 1946[^f00105], section 66 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1947[^f00106] or section 10 of the Act of 1972.

**10.** Regulations from time to time in force under section 61 of the Health Services (Northern Ireland) Act 1948[^f00107] or Article 12 of the Order.
