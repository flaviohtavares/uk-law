# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section S3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## S3. Revocations

(1)

- (a) Part II of the Local Government (Superannuation and Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00044] is hereby revoked; and
- (b) Regulation 10 of the Local Government (Superannuation)(Reserve Forces) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00045] is hereby revoked.

(2) The regulations specified in Schedule 21 are hereby revoked.
