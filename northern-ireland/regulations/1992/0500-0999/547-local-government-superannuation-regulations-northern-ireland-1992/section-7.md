# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**7.** In Part R (Miscellaneous powers and duties of authorities)—

- (a) R1 — information to be supplied by certain employees when requested by the Committee;
- (b) R2 — scheduled bodies are to keep a record of all decisions made under regulations N2 to N4;
- (c) R3 — information to be sent by employing authorities to the Committee and vice versa is set out in this regulation.
