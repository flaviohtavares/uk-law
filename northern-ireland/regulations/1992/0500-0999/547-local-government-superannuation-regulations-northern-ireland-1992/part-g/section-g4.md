# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section G4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G4. Application to certain female nurses, physiotherapists, midwives and health visitors

In their application to a pensionable employee to whom regulation 50 of the 1981 regulations applied, these regulations shall have effect subject to the modifications set out in Part II of Schedule 15.
