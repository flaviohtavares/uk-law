# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section R2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## R2. Records to be kept by authorities

(1) A scheduled body are to keep, in such form as they think fit, a record of—

- (a) the name of; and
- (b) all their decisions under regulation N2 in relation to,

each of their pensionable employees.

(2) The Committee are to keep, in such form as they think fit, a record of—

- (a) the name of; and
- (b) all their decisions under regulation N3 or N4 in relation to,

every pensionable employee.
