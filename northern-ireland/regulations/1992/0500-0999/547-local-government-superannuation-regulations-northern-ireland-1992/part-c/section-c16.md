# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section C16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C16. Effect of return to local government on right to a return of contributions

A person’s right to a payment under regulation C15 is extinguished if—

- (a) he returns to local government employment after leaving previous local government employment with a right to such a payment;
- (b) on the date on which he returns to local government employment he has not received the payment; and
- (c) he has not given, within 3 months after that date, or such longer period as the Committee may allow, written notice to the Committee that he wishes to receive an immediate payment.
