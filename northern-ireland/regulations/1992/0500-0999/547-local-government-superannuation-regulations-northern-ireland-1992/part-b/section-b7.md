# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section B7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B7. Treatment of certain separate employments

Where a person holds two or more separate employments under one scheduled body, these regulations apply in relation to each of those employments as if the other or others were held by him under another scheduled body.
