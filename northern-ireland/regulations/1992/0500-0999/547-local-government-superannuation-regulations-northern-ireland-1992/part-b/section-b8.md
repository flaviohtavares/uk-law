# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section B8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B8. Deemed employments

In these regulations, references to employees of a scheduled body shall be construed as including references to persons who are deemed for the purposes of these regulations to be in the employment of a scheduled body and other provisions relating to employment by or under a scheduled body shall be construed accordingly.
