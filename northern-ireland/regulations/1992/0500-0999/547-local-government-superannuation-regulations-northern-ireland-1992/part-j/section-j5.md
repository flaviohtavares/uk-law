# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section J5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J5. Termination of right to payment out of the fund

Notwithstanding any provision of these regulations or of the former regulations (except regulations J3 and J6), where a transfer value is to be or has been paid under regulation J2 no other payment or transfer of assets shall, subject to regulations J3 and J6, be made out of the fund on account of the service to which the transfer value relates.
