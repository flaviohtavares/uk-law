# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section P9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P9. Employer’s further payments

Any extra charge on the fund resulting from a resolution under regulation D7 (increase of reckonable service) shall be repaid to the fund by the scheduled body concerned.
