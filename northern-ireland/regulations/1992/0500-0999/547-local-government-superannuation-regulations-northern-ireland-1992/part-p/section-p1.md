# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section P1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P1. Constitution and powers of the Committee

(1) For the purposes of these regulations the Committee shall be constituted in accordance with Part I of Schedule 20.

(2) The Committee shall have the powers specified in Part II of Schedule 20.

(3) The expenses and allowances payable by the Committee shall be in accordance with Part III of Schedule 20.
