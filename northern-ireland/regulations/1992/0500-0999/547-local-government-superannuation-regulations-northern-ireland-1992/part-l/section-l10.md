# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section L10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L10. Finance

(1) Benefits under this Part are not to be met out of the fund.

(2) Where—

- (a) an allowance under regulation 21 of the 1981 regulations is payable in addition to any benefit under this Part; and
- (b) the relevant body are the employing authority for the purposes of regulation 21(5) of the 1981 regulations (repayment to the fund),

the allowance shall be treated for the purposes of paragraph (1) as a benefit under this Part.
