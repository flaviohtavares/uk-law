# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section L11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L11. Notice of decisions

The relevant body shall give notice in writing of any decision relating to any benefit under this Part to the person affected and shall inform him about his right of appeal under regulation N8.
