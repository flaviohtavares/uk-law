# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section D2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D2. Qualifying service

Subject to regulation D3, a pensionable employee’s qualifying service is—

- (a) any period which he is entitled to reckon as qualifying service by virtue of regulations D10, D11, D12 or J9(1)(b); and
- (b) any period which was reckonable at its full length by virtue of regulation 40(1)(a) of the 1981 regulations.
