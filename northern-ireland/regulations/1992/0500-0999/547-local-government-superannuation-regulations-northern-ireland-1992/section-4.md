# Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** In Part J (Interchange, etc.)—

- (a) J6 — this regulation safeguards the rights of a person who transfers to a European Community Scheme;
- (b) J13 — applies to a person who transfers to an approved non-local government scheme;
- (c) J14 — this is related to J13 and covers the rights of a person who transfers to an approved non-local government scheme.
