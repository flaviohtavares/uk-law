# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 270

AGRICULTURE

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

Made

11th June 1992

Coming into operation

27th July 1992

The Department of Agriculture, being a Department designated[^f00001], for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Economic Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section and sections 66(1), 68(1), (1A) and 3, 69(1), (3), (6) and (7), 70(1), 73(3), 74(1), 74A[^f00003], 84 and 86 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00004] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with such persons or organisations as appear to it to represent the interests concerned, hereby make the following Regulations:—
