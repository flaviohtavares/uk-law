# The Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Revocation

Subject to regulation 1(2) the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00013], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00014] and the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00015] are hereby revoked.
