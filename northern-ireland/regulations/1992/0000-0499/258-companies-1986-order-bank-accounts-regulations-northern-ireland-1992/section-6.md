# Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Other amendments of the 1986 Order

Part VIII of the 1986 Order shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2.

**7.** The 1986 Order shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 3 which are consequential on the amendments made by the foregoing Regulations.

**6.** Regulation 9 sets out a transitional provision whereby a company may prepare accounts in accordance with the unamended Part VIII, as that Part applies to banking companies and groups, rather than under the amended provisions for a financial year commencing prior to 23rd December 1992.
