# Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Insertion of new Schedule into the 1986 Order

(1) Parts I and II of Schedule 9 to the 1986 Order[^f00012] shall form a new Schedule to that Order numbered “Schedule 9A” and entitled “Special Provisions For Insurance Companies And Groups”, which shall be inserted after Schedule 9.

(2) Part III of Schedule 9 to the 1986 Order is repealed.

(3) The following is substituted for the heading of Schedule 9 to the 1986 Order: “Special Provisions For Banking Companies and Groups”.

(4) Parts I to III of Schedule I (Form and content of accounts of banking companies and groups) are inserted into the 1986 Order as Parts I to III of Schedule 9 to that Order.

**5.** Regulations 6 to 8 make further and consequential amendments.
