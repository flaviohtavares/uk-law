# Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992 and shall come into operation on 3rd August 1992.

**1.** These Regulations implement Council Directive [86/635/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1986/0635) (O.J. No. L372, 31.12.1986, pages 1-17) on the Annual Accounts and Consolidated Accounts of Banks and Other Financial Institutions, in so far as that Directive is applicable to bodies corporate to which Part VIII of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (“the 1986 Order”) applies.
