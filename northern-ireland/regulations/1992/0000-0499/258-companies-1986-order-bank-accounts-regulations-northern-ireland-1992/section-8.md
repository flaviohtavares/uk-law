# Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendment to the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981

In Article 141 of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00013], in the definition of “insurance company”, the words “Schedule 9A” shall be substituted for the words “Schedule 9”.
