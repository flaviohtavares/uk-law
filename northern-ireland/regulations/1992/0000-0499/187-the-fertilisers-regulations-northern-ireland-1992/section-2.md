# The Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Control of materials designated as EEC fertilisers

(1) A person shall not sell or have in possession with a view to sale, for use as a fertiliser, any material designated as an EEC fertiliser, or in respect of which any indication is given directly or indirectly that it is an EEC fertiliser, unless that material:

- (a) is specified in Groups 1(a), 2(a) or 3(a) of Section A, or in Groups 1 to 4 of Section B, or in Groups 1(a) or 2 of Section C, or in Section D, or Section E, of the table in Schedule 1; and
- (b) conforms with the requirements laid down for such materials in these Regulations as respects content and marking.

(2) A person shall not sell or have in possession with a view to sale, for use as a fertiliser, any material designated as an EEC fertiliser containing any pesticide or herbicide or any organic nutrient of animal or vegetable origin, which has been added in the course of manufacture or preparation for sale.

(3) A person shall not make available to any other person for the final use by that other person as a fertiliser any ammonium nitrate, as defined in column 3 of Section A of the table in Schedule 1, which is designated as an EEC fertiliser and contains more than 28% by weight of nitrogen, unless the material is in a container which complies with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 2.

(4) A person shall not sell or have in possession with a view to sale, for use as a fertiliser, any material specified in Section D of the table in Schedule 1 designated as an EEC fertiliser unless that product is packaged.

**2.** The Directives implemented are—

- Council Directive [76/116/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0116) (OJ No. L24, 30.1.76, p. 21) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to fertilisers;
- Council Directive [80/876/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0876) (OJ No. L250, 23.9.80, p. 7) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to straight ammonium nitrate fertilisers of high nitrogen content;
- Council Directive [88/183/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0183) (OJ No. L83, 29.3.88, p. 33) amending Directive [76/116/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0116) in respect of fluid fertilisers;
- Council Directive [89/284/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0284) (OJ No. L111, 22.4.89, p. 34) supplementing and amending Directive [76/116/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0116) in respect of the calcium, magnesium, sodium and sulphur content of fertilisers;
- Council Directive [89/530/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0530) (OJ No. L281, 30.9.89, p. 116) supplementing and amending Directive [76/116/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0116) in respect of the trace elements boron, cobalt, copper, iron, manganese, molybdenum and zinc contained in fertilisers.
