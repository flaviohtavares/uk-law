# The Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Limits of variation

For the purposes of section 74, the limits of variation in relation to any mis-statement as to the nature, substance or quality of any material specified in column 2 of the table in Schedule 1 shall, subject to the provisions of that Schedule, be the corresponding limits in relation to that material set out in column 5 and, as the case may be, in column 6 of that table.

**6.** The Regulations come into operation on 18th May 1992, but subject to regulation 1(2) in the case of material, not designated as an EEC fertiliser, sold or offered for sale loose or in large containers before 1st June 1992 or in small containers before 1st February 1993.
