# The Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 187

AGRICULTURE

The Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

Made

8th April 1992

Coming into operation

18th May 1992

The Department of Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 66(1)[^f00001], 68(1), (2) and (3), 69(1), (3), (6) and (7), 70(1), 74(1), 74A(1), (2) and (4), 84 and 86(1), (2), (3) and (9) of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with such persons or organisations as appear to it to represent the interests concerned, being a Department designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] in relation to the regulation and control of classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances and preparations, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—
