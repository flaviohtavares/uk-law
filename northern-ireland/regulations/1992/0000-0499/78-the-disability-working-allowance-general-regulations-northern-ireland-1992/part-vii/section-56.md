# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Overlapping awards of disability working allowance

An award of disability working allowance which is made on review or appeal shall be treated as a relevant change of circumstances affecting any other award of disability working allowance which it overlaps.
