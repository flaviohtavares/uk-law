# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Sums to be disregarded in the calculation of earnings

Regulations 22(2) and 25(2)

**1.** Any earnings derived from employment which are payable in a country outside the United Kingdom where there is a prohibition against the transfer to the United Kingdom of those earnings.

**2.** Any earnings of a child or young person.

**3.** Where a payment of earnings is made in a currency other than sterling, any banking charge or commission payable in converting that payment to sterling.
