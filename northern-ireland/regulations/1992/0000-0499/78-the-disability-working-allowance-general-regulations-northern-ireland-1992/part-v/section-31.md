# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Capital limit

For the purposes of Article 23(6) of the Order as it applies to disability working allowance, the prescribed amount is £16,000.
