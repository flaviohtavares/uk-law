# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Disregard of capital of child or young person

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant’s family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
