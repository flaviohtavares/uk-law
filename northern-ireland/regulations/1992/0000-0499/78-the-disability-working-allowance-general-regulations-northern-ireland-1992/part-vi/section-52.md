# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Applicable amount

(1) The applicable amount for the purposes of Article 21(6A)(c) of the Order[^f00056] shall, in the case of a claimant who is—

- (a) single, be £39.95 per week; and
- (b) a member of a married or unmarried couple, or a lone parent, be £66.60 per week.

(2) For the purposes of Article 21(6D) of the Order[^f00056] the prescribed date is the date on which the period under Article 21(6F) of the Order begins.

## Annotations

[^f00056]: Footnote: Paragraphs (6A), (6D) and (6F) were inserted by Article 8(4) of the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/1712 (N.I. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1991/1712))
