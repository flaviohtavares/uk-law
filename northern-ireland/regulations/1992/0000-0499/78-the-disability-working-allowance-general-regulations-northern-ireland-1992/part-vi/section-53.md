# The Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Entitlement to disability working allowance where income exceeds the applicable amount

The prescribed percentage for the purpose of Article 22(3B) of the Order[^f00057] (amount, etc.) shall be 70 per cent.

## Annotations

[^f00057]: Footnote: Paragraph (3B) was inserted by Article 8(8) of the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance (Northern Ireland) Order 1991
