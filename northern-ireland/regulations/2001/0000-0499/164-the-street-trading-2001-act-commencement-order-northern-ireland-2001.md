# The Street Trading (2001 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 164 (C. 11)

STREET TRADING

The Street Trading (2001 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

11th April 2001

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 29(1) of the Street Trading Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Street Trading (2001 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Street Trading Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 is 1st October 2001.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 11th April 2001.

J. V. Allister

Senior Officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Street Trading Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 as 1st October 2001.
