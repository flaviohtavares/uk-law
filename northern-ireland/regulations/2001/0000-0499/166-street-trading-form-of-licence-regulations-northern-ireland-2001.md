# Street Trading (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 166

STREET TRADING

Street Trading (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

11th April 2001

Coming into operation

1st October 2001

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 6(2) of the Street Trading Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Street Trading (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2001.

## 2. Form of licence for street trading

A street trading licence shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.

## 3. Revocation

The Street Trading Regulations 1929[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 11th April 2001.

J. V. Allister

Senior Officer of the

Department for Social Development
