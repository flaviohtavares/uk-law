# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 132 (C. 6)

POLICE

The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

Made

21st March 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001.

**2.** The provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 30th March 2001.

John Reid

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

21st March 2001
