# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, SCHEDULE 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Information to be contained in a dossier submitted in support of an application for the registration of a biocidal product

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulations 10(4) and 12(5) and (7)

**1.**[^c-c6028f79208032faa3a161f3a69f994f] The name and address of the applicant.

**2.**[^c-a9fcbbe0ba5633d9803f1daf9b09554d] The name and address of the manufacturer of the biocidal product.

**3.**[^c-9d9b1916388efe524be981f3a9d4273e] The name and address of the manufacturer of the active substance in the biocidal product, and the location of manufacture.

**4.**[^c-882d70636d678815b5685e1ffc30e252] The trade name of the biocidal product.

**5.**[^c-c7fbbabf9e79bc12db46c2a1fdd42825] The name of each substance in the biocidal product, including the name of its active substance, and the amount of each substance, as a percentage of the whole.

**6.**[^c-d849dd52131235d2872335d9441e9eec] The physical and chemical properties of the biocidal product relating to use, storage and transport.

**7.**[^c-e4fe2d9f72899f6304a42ae1ae1054f6] The product-type and field of use of the biocidal product.

**8.**[^c-0f35b39d726e3fb7ee82b96d6c550651] The intended category of users.

**9.**[^c-dbaf0964fe997d1d70b942ea72b6ca5c] The intended method of use.

**10.**[^c-699a73372fb73c858bce87811cd01f49] Efficacy data.

**11.**[^c-3a2659060187d2864157a33078f98b5d] Analytical methods.

**12.**[^c-61e1415774c4dd423a8c6308701abbf3] The classification, packaging and labelling of the biocidal product, including a draft label.

**13.**[^c-b30a5513a4f946d6275172c04f9effc8] Where the biocidal product is a substance or preparation dangerous for supply within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of the 1995 Regulations [^f00044], a safety data sheet for that biocidal product prepared in accordance with regulation 6 of those Regulations.
