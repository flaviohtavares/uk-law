## 37. Tests[^c-70e6222609853b1843a61881324a07c5]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Every test carried out in support of an application under regulations 9 to 15 and 17 shall be conducted in accordance with such guidance as may be issued by the Executive.
