# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 41

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Application within territorial waters[^key-9b82496e58183d836ab5b15fdc7b7ff7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Within territorial waters these Regulations shall apply only to and in relation to the premises and activities mentioned in paragraphs 2 to 6 of Schedule 13.
