# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 11

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**11.** The Executive may prohibit or restrict the sale or use of a biocidal product if it considers that the biocidal product constitutes an unacceptable risk to human or animal health or to the environment (*regulation 22*).
