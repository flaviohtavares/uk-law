# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, SCHEDULE 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Non-confidential information

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 26(6)

**1.**[^key-6654c497029cf77401a4631534a3aad1] The name and address of the applicant for the authorisation or registration of the biocidal product.

**2.**[^key-dfc81932cf265a3e15842fbd9329d758] The name of the biocidal product.

**3.**[^key-bee95fb221957ab85e936a5ebbb29871] The name and address of the manufacturer of the biocidal product.

**4.**[^key-af166d09fe2a9dcb98a119333ae5d6b4] The name and address of the manufacturer of the active substance in the biocidal product.

**5.**[^key-924214f24f64e3dcc81c7f3bbd396691] The name and content of the active substance in the biocidal product.

**6.**[^key-73f1dfd2589e8574c721ee41bf1f2f78] The name of any other substance in the biocidal product which—

- (a) is listed in Part I of the approved supply list; or
- (b) is classified as being in one or more of the categories of danger specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to the 1995 Regulations,

except a substance of which no account would be taken in the classification of that biocidal product by virtue of paragraph 18(1) of Part I of Schedule 3 to those Regulations.

**7.**[^key-236a1ca6055eb30168b44ad861b9c05d] Physical and chemical data concerning the biocidal product and the active substance contained in that biocidal product.

**8.**[^key-dff854bb037ce44155b61d28f0ec49eb] Any ways of rendering harmless the biocidal product and the active substance contained in that biocidal product.

**9.**[^key-a69ebfc32a9883881fe8296bba760005] A summary of the results of the tests, referred to in the dossiers submitted in support of an application under these Regulations, to establish—

- (a) the efficacy;
- (b) the effects on humans, animals and the environment; and
- (c) where applicable, any ability to promote resistance,

of the biocidal product and the active substance contained in that biocidal product.

**10.**[^key-5ee2c63e1c1f07460b4775abdb2d0dba] Recommended methods and precautions to reduce dangers from handling, storage, transport, use, fire or other hazards.

**11.**[^key-b915d45b988518d40e3ac12f754a0cbd] Safety data sheets.

**12.**[^key-c86c325fd263c5b65e74e6e4c65a8439] Methods of analysis necessary to enable the Executive to make the determination referred to in paragraph 3 of Schedule 3.

**13.**[^key-2ac808c2a3e339441704cb76a5aea353] Methods of disposal of the biocidal product and its packaging.

**14.**[^key-dd41eb74c065c992f71ce38cd32531e8] Procedures to be followed and measures to be taken in the case of spillage or leakage of the biocidal product and the active substance contained in that biocidal product.

**15.**[^key-24c19a070d10d9918e49999f90822cf2] First aid and medical advice to be given in the case of injury to persons.
