# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, SCHEDULE 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — Information to be notified to the Poison Information Service

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 29(2) and (7)

**1.**[^c-c6e0a62d5f2a1e8fbc7877aa67dfff4b] The name of the biocidal product.

**2.**[^c-317496c8845564bcbaa445a81f3964d2] If the biocidal product is authorised or registered under these Regulations—

- (a) the use for which it is so authorised or registered; and
- (b) the name, address and telephone number and any e-mail address and any fax number of the person to whom the authorisation or registration was granted.

**3.**[^c-370adaf8099bd0d9232a1923c7b2309d] The date on which the biocidal product was first placed on the market in Northern Ireland.

**4.**[^c-e30792e851213c98590d1624e81628d4] The name, address and telephone number and any e-mail address and any fax number of—

- (a) the manufacturer of the biocidal product;
- (b) any importer of the biocidal product; and
- (c) the individual to be contacted in an emergency in the event of an individual being affected by the biocidal product.

**5.**[^c-caeafc252526527d30ea4cbade79003d] A description of the packaging of the biocidal product, including its size and type.

**6.**[^c-8aea8e26169bd23035cd3c35279f1050] The pH, physical state and colour of the biocidal product.

**7.**[^c-ee043c56e746ef9c1eaa25ba320b1eea] The identity of the ingredients of the biocidal product, and their concentration in metric units.

**8.**[^c-799fe55d239878e1bdf94a8b1389bddf] The effects on human health of contact with the biocidal product.

**9.**[^c-da8ded0cf86706b70a99af0535403fdc] Particulars of the likely direct or indirect adverse side effects of the biocidal product and any directions for first aid.

**10.**[^c-09917e79a5eaee735c56d3565abc3822] Any other information relating to the health and safety of humans which is given on the label of the biocidal product.
