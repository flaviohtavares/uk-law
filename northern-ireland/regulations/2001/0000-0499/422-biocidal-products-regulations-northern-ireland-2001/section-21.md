# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 21

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**21.** In Great Britain the corresponding Regulations are The Biocidal Products Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/880](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/880)) and the Notification of New Substances (Amendment) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/1055](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1055)). The Great Britain Health and Safety Executive has prepared a regulatory impact assessment in respect of those Regulations and a copy of that assessment, together with a Northern Ireland supplement prepared by the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland, is held at the offices of that Executive at 83 Ladas Drive, Belfast BT6 9FR from where copies may be obtained.
