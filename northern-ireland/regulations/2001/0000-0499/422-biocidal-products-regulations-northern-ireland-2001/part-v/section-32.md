# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 32

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Samples, models and drafts[^key-490ebbd2b2074cc3f7e6168bd223e84a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

When required to do so by the Executive, a person who has submitted an application under regulations 9 to 15 or 17 or a person who places, or has placed, a biocidal product on the market shall provide it with—

- (a) a sample or model of the packaging of, or a sample or draft of the labelling of, the biocidal product in question;
- (b) a sample or draft of any accompanying leaflet integral to the packaging of the biocidal product in question.
