# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**13.** Provision is made for co-operation between applicants for, and the holders of, authorisations or registrations regarding information relating to biocidal products. Provision is also made for the exchange of information between the Executive and the European Commission and the competent authorities in other member States (*regulations 25 and 28*).
