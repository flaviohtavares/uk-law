# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**10.** Provision is made requiring a person to whom an authorisation or a registration has been granted to notify the Executive of information of which he is aware relating to the biocidal product in question (*regulation 21*).
