# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, SCHEDULE 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Information relating to Biocidal products to be given to the Commission and to the competent authorities

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 28(1)

**1.**[^c-64e1abe2bdc2600fd511dbcbf2b01a31] The name of the applicant for, or the person to whom, the authorisation or registration was granted.

**2.**[^c-29f340fa1b7ae514d1643a00b19dfa09] The trade name of the biocidal product.

**3.**[^c-04986c8841f5fdbd4be6776668f4ec21] The name and amount of each active substance which the biocidal product contains.

**4.**[^c-3257c05306d40b62d08a7c4724ba5eec] The name and amount of each substance which the biocidal product contains which is a substance dangerous for supply within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of the 1995 Regulations and its classification.

**5.**[^c-a46f92afe664296b40482f19f067195f] The product-type for the biocidal product and the use for which it is authorised or registered, as the case may be.

**6.**[^c-5c74109852ac75e2dc3119ea65765de8] The type of formulation of the biocidal product, namely whether it is in the form of a powder, granules, a solid, a liquid concentrate or some other form.

**7.**[^c-cece6e1f59627967d2b6d68687f84b0c] Any proposed limits on residues which have been determined by the Executive in accordance with paragraph 3(b) of Schedule 3.

**8.**[^c-2eff3059d89abea32a98bdf6d8ecba64] Any conditions subject to which the authorisation or registration was granted.

**9.**[^c-00ec7a753f07234f3b88132a3b258dbb] The reasons for the modification or cancellation of an authorisation or registration.

**10.**[^c-84a9fa3a30d131950f5ca3af1f396595] Whether the biocidal product is a low-risk biocidal product or within a frame-formulation.
