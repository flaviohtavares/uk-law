# Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**14.** The person responsible for first placing a biocidal product on the market is responsible for providing information to the Poisons Information Service. The information may only be disclosed for the purposes of medical treatment of a person affected by the biocidal product (*regulation 29*).
