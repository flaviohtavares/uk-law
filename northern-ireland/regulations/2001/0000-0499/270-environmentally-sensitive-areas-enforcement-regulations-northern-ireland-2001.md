## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

- “agri-environment scheme” means an arrangement which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

## 3. Department’s powers of recovery (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11][^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

(2) Subject to the provisions of Article 30 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] Commission Regulation 1750/1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] (force majeure) and Article 48 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] Commission Regulation 1750/1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] (which provides for recovery of wrongful payments with interest, a penalty system to be imposed in the event of breaches of the obligations entered into and exclusion for false declarations), and without prejudice to any rights *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] may have under an agreement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] may, where a farmer breaches any of the provisions of the agreement he has entered or any other requirement that applies to the making of any payments— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

(3) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] takes any step specified in paragraph (1) or (2) it may also terminate the agreement by notice in writing to the farmer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

(5) Any question or dispute between *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed56ce44a2c5c052618a657a2eb368d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed56ce44a2c5c052618a657a2eb368d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] and a farmer in relation to a matter arising under the agreement shall be referred to and determined by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or in default of such agreement to be appointed by the Chairman for the time being of the Northern Ireland Branch of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors and in accordance with the provisions of the Arbitration Act 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed56ce44a2c5c052618a657a2eb368d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11][^f00011] or any statutory modification or re-enactment thereof for the time being in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed56ce44a2c5c052618a657a2eb368d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

## 4. Recovery of interest (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

(1) Where a payment is made under an agreement by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] and, by virtue of Article 14 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] Commission Regulation 3887/92 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] (as applied by Article 48(1) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] Commission Regulation 1750/1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]), a reimbursement of all or part of the payment with interest is required, the rate of interest shall be one percentage point above LIBOR on a day to day basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

(3) In any proceedings relating to this regulation, a certificate of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] stating the LIBOR applicable during a period specified in the certificate shall be conclusive evidence of the rate applicable in the specified period if the certificate also states that the Bank of England notified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11] of that rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7108b1134bb8ce08dba2af755c947e11]

These Regulations supplement the Environmentally Sensitive Areas Designation Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 ([S.R. 2001 No. 269](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2001/269)) (“the 2001 Order”), which gives effect in part to the agri-environment element of the programming document based on the rural development plan for Northern Ireland submitted by the United Kingdom to the European Commission pursuant to Article 41 of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1257/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1257) (O.J. No. L160, 26.06.1999, p. 80) and which was approved by Commission Decision C (2000) 3638 of 4th December 2000.

The Regulations provide for the withholding and recovery of payments payable under an agreement made after the coming into operation of these Regulations under Article 3(2) of the Agriculture (Environmental Areas) (Northern Ireland) Order 1987, (S.I. [1987/458 (N.I. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1987/458)), as respects agricultural land in any of the areas designated by the 2001 Order. They also provide for the recovery of interest and the imposition of penalties (regulations 3 to 5);

These Regulations revoke, with a saving, the Environmentally Sensitive Areas Designation Orders (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, ([S.R. 1996 No. 606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/606)) (regulation 6).

The rural development plan for Northern Ireland, together with a copy of Commission Decision C (2000) 3638 approving it, is available for inspection at the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, Dundonald House, Upper Newtownards Road, Belfast, BT4 3SB.
