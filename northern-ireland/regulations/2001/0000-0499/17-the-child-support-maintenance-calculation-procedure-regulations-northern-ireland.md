### 3. Applications under Article 7 of (affected by repeal, see note) the Order (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d47402610954ef389f4afa9d019c6540][^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089][^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(1) A person who applies for a maintenance calculation under Article 7 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089] the Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089][^f00007] need not normally do so in writing, but if the Department directs that the application be made in writing, the application shall be made either by completing and returning, in accordance with the Department’s instructions, a form provided for that purpose, or in such other written form as the Department may accept as sufficient in the circumstances of any particular case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(2) An application for a maintenance calculation is effective if it complies with paragraph (1) and, subject to paragraph (4), is made on the date it is received. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(3) Where an application for a maintenance calculation is not effective the Department may request the person making the application to provide such additional information or evidence as the Department may specify and, where the application was made on a form, the Department may request that the information or evidence be provided on a fresh form. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(4) Where the additional information or evidence requested is received by the Department within 14 days of the date of its request, or at a later date in circumstances where the Department is satisfied that the delay was unavoidable, it shall treat the application as made on the date on which the earlier or earliest application would have been treated as made had it been effective. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(5) Where the Department receives the additional information or evidence requested by it more than 14 days from the date of the request and in circumstances where it is not satisfied that the delay was unavoidable, the Department shall treat the application as made on the date of receipt of the information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), a person who has made an effective application may amend or withdraw the application at any time before a maintenance calculation is made and such amendment or withdrawal need not be in writing unless, in any particular case, the Department requires it to be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(7) No amendment made under paragraph (6) shall relate to any change of circumstances arising after the effective date of a maintenance calculation resulting from an effective application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

### 4. Multiple applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a4661b87e82907ea05609395b7ab15d4][^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(1) The provisions of Schedule 2 shall apply in cases where there is more than one application for a maintenance calculation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(2) The provisions of paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 relating to the treatment of two or more applications as a single application shall apply where no request is received for the Department to cease acting in relation to all but one of the applications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]

(3) Where, under the provisions of paragraph 1, 2 or 3 of Schedule 2, two or more applications are to be treated as a single application, that application shall be treated as an application for a maintenance calculation to be made with respect to all of the qualifying children mentioned in the applications, and the effective date of that maintenance calculation shall be determined by reference to the earlier or earliest application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-338598fad93f802451484f21cd154089]
