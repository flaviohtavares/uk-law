# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 113

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

14th March 2001

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation

9th April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 7(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 9th April 2001.

## 2. Revocation

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Amendment to Schedule 2

For the table set out in Schedule 2 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following table—

> | ***Disposable Income Range*** | ***Maximum Contribution*** |
> | --- | --- |
> | Exceeding £79 but not exceeding £86 | £7 |
> | Exceeding £86 but not exceeding £93 | £14 |
> | Exceeding £93 but not exceeding £100 | £21 |
> | Exceeding £100 but not exceeding £107 | £28 |
> | Exceeding £107 but not exceeding £114 | £35 |
> | Exceeding £114 but not exceeding £121 | £42 |
> | Exceeding £121 but not exceeding £128 | £49 |
> | Exceeding £128 but not exceeding £135 | £56 |
> | Exceeding £135 but not exceeding £142 | £63 |
> | Exceeding £142 but not exceeding £149 | £70 |
> | Exceeding £149 but not exceeding £156 | £77 |
> | Exceeding £156 but not exceeding £163 | £84 |
> | Exceeding £163 but not exceeding £170 | £91 |
> | Exceeding £170 but not exceeding £177 | £98 |
> | Exceeding £177 but not exceeding £186 | £107 |

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 14th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 so as to substitute a new scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance under Article 7(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
