# Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions No. 2) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 399

PUBLIC HEALTH

Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions No. 2) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

2nd November 2001

Coming into operation

2nd November 2001

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 1(1) and (2) and 24(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, being of the opinion that there no longer exist or may exist circumstances which are likely to create a hazard to human health through human consumption of food and that in consequence food which is derived or may be in the future derived from bivalve molluscs in the area described in the Order revoked by this Order is not, or may not be, or may not become, unsuitable for human consumption, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions No. 2) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 2nd November 2001

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 2nd November 2001

W. B. Smith

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 which contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of scallops or food which is derived from scallops originating in waters in ICES (International Council for the Exploration of the Sea) Area VIIa which are within British fishery limits and which are adjacent to Northern Ireland.
