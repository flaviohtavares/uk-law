# Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 24

## 24. Revocations[^key-73d5a58949aafdef5f5e56bac121038d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00028], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00029], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00030], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00031] and the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00032] are hereby revoked.
