# Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, section 25

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Modification of the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^c-c7acb82dc3c3590d18024032198029f6]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00033]—

- (a) paragraph 5(1) of Part II of Schedule 1 shall have effect as if the words “section 76(1)(*b*) of the Act” read “section 76(7) of the Act, as that section is modified by regulation 10 of the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) Regulations 1999”[^f00034],
- (b) the references in paragraph 3(*e*)(ii) of Part I of Schedule 2, and paragraph 11(*a*) of Part II of Schedule 3, to “the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995”, shall have effect as if they read “the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001”, and
- (c) the second entry for Starch (polarimetric method), in Annex I to Part II to Schedule 2, shall have effect as if, for the provisions relating to that entry in columns 2 and 3, there were substituted the following provisions:
