2001 No. 47

AGRICULTURE

Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

7th February 2001

Coming into operation

2nd April 2001

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety acting in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 68(1), (1A) and (3), 69(1), (3), (6) and (7), 70(1), 73(3), 74(1), 74A, 77(4), 78(6), 84 and 86 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] (as read with regulation 14 of the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000)[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with such persons or organisations as appear to it to represent the interests concerned, and being a Department designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community (in so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers in the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:
