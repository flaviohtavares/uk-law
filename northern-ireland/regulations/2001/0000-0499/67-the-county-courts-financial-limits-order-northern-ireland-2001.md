# The County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

> Text as at 2013-02-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2001 No. 67

COUNTY COURTS

The County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

17th February 2001

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 22(1) of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-2b3f2fea09202cda063cd9399d71364c]

This Order may be cited as the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation—

- (a) for the purpose of Article 3(*a*), on 5th September 2001; and
- (b) for the purpose of Article 3(*b*), on 19th March 2001.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-3e2f0cb84c05a302d80a06f6775fb3d4]

In this Order, “the principal Order” means the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980.

## 3. Increase of financial limits of civil jurisdiction of county courts[^key-99225c86c921811ac871b15c4717cdb5]

Article 30 of the principal Order, shall be amended as follows—

- (a)[^key-503727dfc52aede182af755a0a327987] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-99225c86c921811ac871b15c4717cdb5] in paragraph (3) for the figure of “£1,000” there shall be substituted the figure of “£2,000”.

## 4. Revocation[^key-50abd64d474f091ffc2e15016f83461b]

The entry relating to Article 30(1) of the principal Order in the Schedule to the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] and the entry relating to Article 30(3) of the principal Order in the Schedule to the County Courts (Financial Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 17th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order increases the jurisdiction of district judges in the county courts in Northern Ireland—

- (a) from £3,000 to £5,000 in respect of defended actions; and
- (b) from £1,000 to £2,000 in respect of arbitrations (small claims).
