# The Street Works (Maintenance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 413

ROADS

STREET WORKS

The Street Works (Maintenance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

26th November 2001

Coming into operation

1st March 2002

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 41(3) and (4) of the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] and now exercisable by it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf hereby makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Street Works (Maintenance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 1st March 2002.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the Order” means the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995; and
- “relevant apparatus” means manholes or other surface boxes and covers at or about the level of the street surface and manhole chambers, tunnels and other structures affecting the integrity of the structure of the street.

**2.** If an undertaker has failed to afford the street authority the facilities required by Article 41(1) of the Order in relation to relevant apparatus the street authority may, where it has reasonable cause to believe, by reason of subsidence or disturbance of the road surface, that the undertaker’s apparatus has not been maintained as required by that sub-paragraph, execute such works as are needed to enable it to inspect the apparatus.

**3.** The street authority may execute any emergency works needed in consequence of the failure of an undertaker to secure that its relevant apparatus is maintained to the satisfaction of the street authority as required by Article 41(1) of the Order where it has made such an inspection in accordance with regulation 2 and has found that such apparatus has not been so maintained.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 26th November 2001.

J. Carlisle

A senior officer of the

Department for Regional Development

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations define the circumstances in which a street authority, in cases where the undertaker has failed to afford to the authority facilities for inspecting its relevant apparatus (as defined in regulation 1) in a street, may carry out works to enable it to inspect the apparatus where that authority has reasonable cause to believe that the apparatus has not been maintained as required by Article 41(1) and (2) of the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995. Where the inspection shows that the apparatus has not been so maintained the street authority may carry out emergency works needed in consequence of the undertaker’s breach of statutory duty.
