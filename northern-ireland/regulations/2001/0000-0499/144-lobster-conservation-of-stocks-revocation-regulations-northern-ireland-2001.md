# Lobster (Conservation of Stocks) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 144

FISHERIES

Lobster (Conservation of Stocks) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made

2nd April 2001

Coming into operation

7th May 2001

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 124(1) and (2)[^f00002] of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Lobster (Conservation of Stocks) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 7th May 2001.

## 2. Revocation

The Lobster (Conservation of Stocks) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 2nd April 2001.

E. Cummins (Mrs)

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke the Lobster (Conservation of Stocks) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 which provided that all berried or sexually mature female lobsters marked with a V notch or mutilated to hide or obliterate a V notch are returned to the sea.
