# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 288

Public Health

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

27th November 2020

Coming into operation

28th November 2020

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 28th November 2020.

## 2. Revocations

The following regulations are revoked—

- (a) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003];
- (b) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00004].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 27th November 2020

(L.S.)

Lourda Geoghegan

A senior officer of the Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Travel from Denmark) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
