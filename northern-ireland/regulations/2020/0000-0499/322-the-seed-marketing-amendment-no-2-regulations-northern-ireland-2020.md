# The Seed Marketing (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 322

Agriculture

Plant Health

Seeds

The Seed Marketing (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

*10*th December 2020

Coming into operation

27th December 2020

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Marketing (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 27th December 2020.

## 2. Amendment of the Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

In regulation 1 of the Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003], for “come into operation” until the end, substitute “come into operation on 28th December 2020.”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 10th December 2020.

Dave Foster

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 1 of the Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland 2020 ([SR 2020 No. 301](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2020/301), to correct the coming into operation date
