# The Sea Fishing (Licences and Notices) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 21

Sea Fisheries

The Sea Fishing (Licences and Notices) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

18th February 2020

Coming into operation-

17th March 2020

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(B) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Licences and Notices) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 17th March 2020.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Revocation

The Sea Fishing (Licences and Notices) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 18th February 2020.

Claire Vincent

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Sea Fishing (Licences and Notices) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019.
