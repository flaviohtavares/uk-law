# The Northern Ireland Screen Commission (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Order laid before the Assembly under Article 30(1)(b) of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982 and subject to affirmative resolution procedure of the Assembly

2020 No. 117

Industrial Development

The Northern Ireland Screen Commission (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

*23rd June 2020*

Coming into operation in accordance with article 1

The Department for the Economy[^f00001], with the consent of the Department of Finance[^f00002], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 30(1)(b) of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Screen Commission (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on the day after the day on which it is affirmed by resolution of the Assembly.

## 2. Specification of the Northern Ireland Screen Commission

The Northern Ireland Screen Commission[^f00004] is specified as a body to which the Department for the Economy may make grants under Article 30(1)(b) of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982.
