# The Education (Student Support) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 145

Education

The Education (Student Support) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

17th July 2020

Coming into operation

*7*th August 2020

The Department for the Economy [^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002] makes the following Regulations.

## PART 1 — GENERAL

### 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 7th August 2020.

(2) The following regulations apply in relation to the provision of support to a student in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1st August 2020, whether or not anything done under these regulations is done before, on, or after that date.

## PART 2 — AMENDMENT OF THE EDUCATION (STUDENT SUPPORT) (No.2) REGULATIONS (NORTHERN IRELAND) 2009

### 2. Amendment of the Education (Student Support) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

The Education (Student Support) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

**3.** In regulation 25(14)[^f00004] (amount of the fee loan)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b)(i) for ‘£4,275’ substitute ‘£4,395’.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for the Economy on 17th July 2020.

Ms Heather Cousins

A senior officer of the Department for the Economy

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amends the Education (Student Support) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009/373 (“the Student Support Regulations”).

Regulation 3 corrects an omission of a figure from the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and increases this figure in line with inflation.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
