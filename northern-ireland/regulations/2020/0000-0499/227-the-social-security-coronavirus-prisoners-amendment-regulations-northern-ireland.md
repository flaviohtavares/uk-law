# The Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 227

Social Security

The Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

*22nd* October 2020

Coming into operation

12th November 2020

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1)(a) and (d), 123, 129, 131(1), 133(2)(h) and (l) and 171(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002], Articles 6(5) and (12), 8(4), 9(4) and 36(2) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 to, the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00003], and now vested in it[^f00004], sections 2(3), (4), (6) and (9), 3(8), 17(2)(a) and 19(1) to (3) of the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00005],sections 4(2) and (3), 24(1) and 25(2) of, and paragraph 6(8) of Schedule 1 to, the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00006]and Articles 7(2), 11(1), 13(1) and 48(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 1 to, the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00007].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 12th November 2020.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00008] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) Regulations

In regulation 6(2) of the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00009] for “8 months” substitute “14 months”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 22nd October 2020

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department of Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 ([S.R. 2020 No. 63](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2020/63)) in response to the continuing outbreak of coronavirus in Northern Ireland.

Those Regulations made provision to enable individuals on temporary release from prison due to the outbreak of coronavirus in Northern Ireland to access means tested benefits during the period of that release.

Following a review by the Department of the operation of the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Prisoners) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, regulation 2 amends the provision by which those Regulations expire after 8 months so that they now expire 14 months after they came into operation.

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland only provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
