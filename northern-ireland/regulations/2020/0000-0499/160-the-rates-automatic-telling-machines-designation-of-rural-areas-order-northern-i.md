# The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 160

Rates

The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

29th July 2020

Coming into operation

24th August 2020

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 42(1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 24th August 2020.

## 2. Designation of rural areas

Wards as set out in the Schedule are designated as rural areas for the purpose of Article 42 (1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.

## 3. Revocation

The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00003] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 29th July 2020

(L.S.)

Alan Brontë

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order sets out those local government wards which are designated as rural areas for the purposes of Article 42(1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 which exempts from rates any automatic telling machine which is situated in an area so designated.
