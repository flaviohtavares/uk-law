## 47. Defence: lawful disclosure[^key-07f32f1a3edb4e77b363122b1183f4fd]

*Extent: N.I.*

It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under regulation 46 to prove that the person reasonably believed that—

- (a) the disclosure was lawful; or
- (b) the information had previously been made available lawfully.
