## 42. Defence: reasonable excuse[^c-be9cca69315ca3347c12bfc8b07c13ed]

*Extent: N.I.*

It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under regulation 40 or 41 to show that the person had a reasonable excuse for contravening or failing to comply with the prohibition or requirement in question.
