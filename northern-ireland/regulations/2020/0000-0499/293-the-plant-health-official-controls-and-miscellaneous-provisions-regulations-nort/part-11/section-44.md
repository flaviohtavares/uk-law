# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, section 44

## 44. Improper use of plant passports, Northern Ireland plant health labels or certificates[^c-300140df1e410ac032e8b42e7d90ae1b][^c-5b0e7135d471293302d3c83b5b6c6f15]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) dishonestly issues a plant passport, Northern Ireland plant health label[^c-9f0b469a82e6350466f7e97faebcbe72] or a certificate,
- (b) dishonestly alters a plant passport, Northern Ireland plant health label[^c-aa22c72092c5d82e86784cae161ffe1d] or a certificate,
- (c) dishonestly re-uses a plant passport, Northern Ireland plant health label[^c-be972663d13c0422bee2d0e0b024d325] or a certificate.

(2) In paragraph (1), “certificate” means a phytosanitary certificate for export, a phytosanitary certificate for re-export or a pre-export certificate.

(3)[^c-cc4972e3ca1b4d505f7b14a8e76f6839] In paragraph (1) “**Northern Ireland plant health label**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023.
