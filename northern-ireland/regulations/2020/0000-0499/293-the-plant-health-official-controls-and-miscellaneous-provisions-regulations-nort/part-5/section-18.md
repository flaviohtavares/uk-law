## 18. Temporary national measures relating to plant health[^c-88febe07cc0698bd8f034070a67b3123]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 1 contains additional temporary measures to prevent the entry of certain controlled plant pests into Northern Ireland, or their establishment in, or spread within, Northern Ireland.
