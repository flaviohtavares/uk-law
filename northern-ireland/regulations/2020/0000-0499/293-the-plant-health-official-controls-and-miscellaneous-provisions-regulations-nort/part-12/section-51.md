## 51. Amendment of the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^c-9e97a86efb67409a57ff97daad55e862]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00037] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(2), after the definition of “[Directive 2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072)”, insert—

> - “the EU Plant Health Regulation” means Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council on protective measures against pests of plants amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives [69/464/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1969/0464), [74/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0647), [93/85/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0085), [98/85/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0085), [98/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0057), [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029), [2006/91/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0091) and [2007/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0033);[^f00038]
