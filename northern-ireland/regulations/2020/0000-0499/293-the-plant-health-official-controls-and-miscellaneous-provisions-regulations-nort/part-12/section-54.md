## 54. Amendment of the Marketing of Fruit Plant and Propagating Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^key-fced9f2a410777219b8488d27fa84dd2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Marketing of Fruit Plant and Propagating Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017[^f00041] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1), omit the definition of “the Plant Health Official Controls Regulations”.

(3) In regulation 3(1), after the definition of “the Directive” insert—

> - “the EU Plant Health Regulation” means Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council on protective measures against pests of plants amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014, and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives [69/464/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1969/0464), [74/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0647), [93/85/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0085), [98/85/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0085), [98/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0057), [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029), [2006/91/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0091) and [2007/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0033);

.

(4) In regulation 11(7), for the words from “under regulation 4” to “Controls Regulations” substitute “for the purposes of the EU Plant Health Regulation”.

(5) In regulation 15(1)(g), for sub-paragraph (iii) substitute—

> - a plant pest of a description specified in Annex 2 or 3 to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 establishing uniform conditions for the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as regards protective measures against pests of plants;

.
