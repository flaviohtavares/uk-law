## 34. Recovery of expenses[^key-3043981909230f629a7af4d9dafa3f2e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any expenses incurred by the Department in carrying out official control measures under Article 66, 67, 69 or 138 of the Official Controls Regulation shall be charged to the operator responsible for the consignment and such expenses must be paid on written demand.

(2) Any unpaid sum under these Regulations may be recovered as a civil debt.
