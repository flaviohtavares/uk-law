## 32. Removal of ISPM 15 mark from wood packaging material[^c-1cac62e86da727eb063e17dd9415d32f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a plant health inspector knows, or has reasonable grounds for suspecting, that the ISPM 15 mark has been incorrectly applied to wood packaging material, the inspector may remove the mark or, by notice in writing, require another person to remove it.
