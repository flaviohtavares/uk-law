# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, section 35

> Text as at 2026-10-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Disclosure of information held by the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^c-7766094afbfd4c746df4a4a4a142e7f5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs may disclose any information in their possession to the Department for the purposes of enabling or assisting the Department to carry out any function conferred on it under or by virtue of the EU Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation or these Regulations.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects any other power or requirement of the Commissioners to disclose information.
