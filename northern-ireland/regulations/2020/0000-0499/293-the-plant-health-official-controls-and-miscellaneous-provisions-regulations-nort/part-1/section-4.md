# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, section 4

## 4. Measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) of the EU Plant Health Regulation[^c-a9468dc66a34f60bd2892395ffb2403e]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any reference in the EU Plant Health Regulation to a pest that is subject to the measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) includes the following plant pests—

- (a) *Epitrix cumeris* (Harris), *Epitrix* papa. (Orlova-Bienkowskaja), *Epitrix* subcrinita (Lec.) or *Epitrix tuberis* (Gentner);
- (b) *Pseudomonas syringae* pv*. actinidiae* Takikawa, Serizawa, Ichikawa, Tsuyuma & Goto*;*
- (c) Rose rosette virus;
- (d) Tomato brown rugose fruit virus.
