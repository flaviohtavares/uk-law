## 3. Interpretation: EU Decisions[^c-20623f3e8a1851c0b728fa2ffa4469b9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations—

- [^c-be7d675a83f69c260efddfe5b29ae046]...
- [^c-9da4109955fe5f574ae936e67af759ac]...
- [^c-aded197fe3a63d1d0ef107a5a283cfff]...
- “Commission Implementing [Decision 2011/787/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2011/0787)” means Commission Implementing [Decision 2011/787/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2011/0787) authorising member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of *Ralstonia solanacearum* (Smith) Yabuuchi et al. as regards Egypt[^f00013];
- [^c-f5eec6a87aaf85fa4950f7877f0951b9]...
- “Commission Implementing [Decision 2012/270/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2012/0270)” means Commission Implementing [Decision 2012/270/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2012/0270) as regards emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Epitrix cucumeris* (Harris), *Epitrix papa* sp. n., *Epitrix subcrinita* (Lec.) and *Epitrix tuberis* (Gentner)[^f00015];
- “Commission Implementing [Decision 2012/535/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2012/0535)” means Commission Implementing [Decision 2012/535/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2012/0535) on emergency measures to prevent the spread within the Union of *Bursaphelenchus xylophilus* (Steiner et Buhrer) Nickle et al. (the pine wood nematode)[^f00016];
- [^c-afc7c92bf07beaf285c8bf4b81b441fb]...
- [^c-03c998f3b141befb5e537550feddc0ff]...
- “Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/893” means Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/893 as regards measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Anoplophora glabripennis* (Motschulksy)[^f00019];
- [^c-c0e860cee1ed2a5a73b629eb5beecf4e]...
- [^c-1a92aaf74ed9657e0d982fac55e7f57b]...
- “Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1503” means Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1503 establishing measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Aromia bungii* (Faldermann)[^f00022];
- [^c-9b7409c7431778e35482a7a88bd1227c]...
- “Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/2032” means Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/2032 establishing measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Fusarium circinatum* Nirenberg & O’Donnell (formerly *Gibberella circinata*) and repealing [Decision 2007/433/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0433)[^f00024];
- [^c-23aeab14269a9be482fcfe16ab3d12c5]...
- [^c-f5e686b26c18066e673bf3d7f71aef7d]...

(2) In these Regulations, references to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation and to the European Union instruments referred to in paragraph (1) are to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.
