# The Statutory Sick Pay (General) (Coronavirus Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 167

Social Security

Terms And Conditions Of Employment

The Statutory Sick Pay (General) (Coronavirus Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

4th August 2020

Coming into operation

5th August 2020

The Department for Communities[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 147(4) and (4A), and 171(1), (3), (4) and (5A) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002]), and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Statutory Sick Pay (General) (Coronavirus Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 5th August 2020.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Statutory Sick Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland)

(1) Schedule 1 to the Statutory Sick Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1982[^f00005] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (5).

(2) In paragraph 1, for “5B” substitute “5D”.

(3) In paragraphs 2 and 4, for “7” substitute “10”.

(4) In paragraph 5B, for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) is staying at home until the date specified in a relevant notification.

.

(5) After paragraph 5B insert—

> **5C.** The person (P), or someone the person lives with or is in an extended household or a linked household with (S), has tested positive for coronavirus and P or S is staying at home —
>
> - (a) for 10 days beginning with the day (“day 1”) P or S first had the symptoms of coronavirus or, if earlier, the day on which P or S were notified that they had tested positive for coronavirus; or
> - (b) if P or S still has symptoms of coronavirus at the end of the period specified in sub-paragraph (a), until P or S no longer has those symptoms.
>
> **5D.** Where P or S is staying at home pursuant to paragraph 5C—
>
> - (a) paragraphs 2, 4 and 5B no longer apply to P;
> - (b) paragraph 2 to 5B no longer apply.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 4th August 2020

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Communities

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations amends the Statutory Sick Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1982 ([S.R. 1982 No. 263](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1982/263)) to ensure that a person is entitled to statutory sick pay for the full period for which they are required to self-isolate and for the period a person is required to stay at home after testing positive for coronavirus.

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland only provision corresponding to provisions in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.

No regulatory impact assessment has been produced for these Regulations in view of the urgency required to provide additional financial support to those unable to work as a result of coronavirus.
