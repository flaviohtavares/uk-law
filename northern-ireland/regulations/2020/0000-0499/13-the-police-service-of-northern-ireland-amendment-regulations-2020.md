# The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 13

Police

The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made

10th January 2020

Coming into operation

1st April 2020

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 26(1) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], makes the following Regulations. Those powers are now vested in the Department[^f00002].

In accordance with section**s** 25(8) and 26(6) of that Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland before making these Regulations**.**

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2020.

## 2. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2020 only, for the words “first Monday in May” substitute “Friday 8<sup>th</sup> May”.

## 3. Amendment of Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 1 in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2020 only, for the words “first Monday in May” substitute “Friday 8<sup>th</sup> May”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 10th January 2020

Peter May

A senior officer of the Department of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 and Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 to allow for the public holiday on the first Monday in May to be replaced by Friday 8<sup>th</sup> May 2020 to mark the 75<sup>th</sup> anniversary of VE Day.
