## 8. Specialist panels: specialist knowledge[^c-0b9211869cb9f1bf7feee7aa695e9e34]

*Extent: N.I.*

The President or the Vice-President may establish specialist panels consisting of persons from the panel of employment judges, the employee panel and the employer panel for use in proceedings where, in the opinion of the President or the Vice-President, specialist knowledge would be beneficial.
