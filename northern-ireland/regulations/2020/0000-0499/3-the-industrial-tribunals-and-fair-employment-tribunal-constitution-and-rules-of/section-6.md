# The Industrial Tribunals and Fair Employment Tribunal (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, section 6

> Text as at 2024-12-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Composition of tribunals[^key-be81d4f064a13da33b5837eb2506df9f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Tribunals shall consist of three panels of members, namely—

- (a) the panel of employment judges;
- (b) the employee panel; and
- (c) the employer panel.
