# The Industrial Tribunals and Fair Employment Tribunal (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020, section 4

> Text as at 2024-12-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Industrial Tribunals[^key-de284750d6a0e248bd5c2defd7ff0fcb]

*Extent: N.I.*

There shall continue to be tribunals known as industrial tribunals, established in accordance with Article 3 of the Industrial Tribunals Order, for the purposes of exercising the jurisdiction conferred on them by or by virtue of that Order or any other statutory provision.
