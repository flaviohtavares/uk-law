# The Provision of Health Services to Persons Not Ordinarily Resident (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 19

Health And Personal Social Services

The Provision of Health Services to Persons Not Ordinarily Resident (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

17th February 2020

Coming into operation

9th March 2020

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 42, 106(b) and 107(6) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00002], in conjunction with the Department of Finance[^f00003], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Provision of Health Services to Persons Not Ordinarily Resident (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 9th March 2020.

## 2. Revocation

The Provision of Health Services to Persons Not Ordinarily Resident (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 17th February 2020

(L.S.)

Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Provision of Health Services to Persons Not Ordinarily Resident (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019.
