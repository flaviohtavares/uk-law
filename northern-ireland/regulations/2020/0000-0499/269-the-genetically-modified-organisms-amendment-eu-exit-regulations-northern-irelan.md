# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment)(EU Exit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 269

Exiting The European Union

Environmental Protection

The Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment)(EU Exit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made

19th November 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Regulation 1(2)

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] make these Regulations in exercise of powers conferred by section 11 of, and Part 1C of Schedule 2, to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00002]

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment)(EU Exit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

(2) These regulations come into operation the day immediately before IP completion day, except regulation 2 which comes into force on IP completion day.

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendment of the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

(1) The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 23(1)(b) omit “the other”.

(3) In regulation 28(f) omit “and the competent authorities of the member states”.

(4) In regulation 32(3) omit “the other”

(5) In regulation 34(7) substitute the word “another” with “a”.

## 3. Amendment of the Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00005] are amended as follows.

(2) Omit regulation 2.

(3) In regulation 3—

- (i) in paragraph (2) omit sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (d),
- (ii) omit paragraph (3),
- (iii) in paragraph (5) omit sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (d), (e) and (f),
- (iv) omit paragraphs (6) and (9) to (15),
- (v) in paragraph (16) omit sub-paragraphs (c) and (d); and
- (vi) omit paragraph (17).

(4) Omit regulation 4.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 19th November 2020.

Dave Foster

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 11 of, and Part 1C of schedule 2, to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018. They deal with matters arising out of, or related to the Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland in the EU withdrawal agreement
