## 21. Effective date of a revision

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, on a revision under Article 10 of the 1998 Order, the Department decides that the date from which the decision under Article 9 or 11 of that Order (“**the original decision**”) took effect was wrong, the revision takes effect from the date from which the original decision would have taken effect had the error not been made.
