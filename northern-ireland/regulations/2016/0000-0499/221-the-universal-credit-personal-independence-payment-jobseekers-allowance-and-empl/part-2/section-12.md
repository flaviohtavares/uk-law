# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 12

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Award of another benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

Where—

- (a) the Department makes a decision to award a benefit to a claimant (“**the original award**”); and
- (b) an award of another relevant benefit or[^key-fbc9caf8d57ef0f6f1d91be71868c11e] Scottish disability or carer benefit[^key-c8f30f70fda7d152fd66d59e96bb4cdd] or of an increase in the rate of another relevant benefit or Scottish disability or carer benefit is made to the claimant or, in the case of universal credit, to a member of the claimant's family, for a period which includes the date on which the original award took effect,

the Department may revise the original award.
