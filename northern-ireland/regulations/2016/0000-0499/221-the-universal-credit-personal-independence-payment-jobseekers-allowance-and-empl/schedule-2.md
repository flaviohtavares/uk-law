## SCHEDULE 2 — Decision against which an appeal lies

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 49(1)

**1.** A decision as to whether a person is entitled to a benefit for which no claim is required by virtue of regulation 6, or 8(6) and (7) of the Claims and Payments Regulations.
