## 53. Appeals involving issues that arise in other cases

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of Article 26(6)(c) of the 1998 Order (appeals involving issues that arise on appeal in other cases) the prescribed circumstances are that the Department—

- (a) certifies in writing that it is considering appealing against that decision; and
- (b) considers that, if such an appeal were already decided, it would affect the determination of the appeal referred to in Article 26(1)(a) of the 1998 Order.
