# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 32

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Decisions which may not be superseded

*Extent: N.I.*

A decision which may be revised under Article 10 of the 1998 Order may not be superseded under Chapter 1 unless—

- (a) circumstances arise in which the Department may revise the decision under Part 2; and
- (b) further circumstances arise in relation to that decision which—
  - (i) are not set out in that Part, but
  - (ii) are set out in Chapter 1 or are circumstances where a superseding decision may be made in accordance with regulation 33(3).
