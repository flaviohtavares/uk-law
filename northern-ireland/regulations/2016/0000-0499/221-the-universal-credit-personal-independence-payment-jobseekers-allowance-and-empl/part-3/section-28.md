# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Loss of benefit cases

*Extent: N.I.*

A decision that a benefit is payable to a claimant may be superseded where that benefit ceases to be payable or falls to be reduced by virtue of section 5B, 6 or 8 of the Fraud Act (the loss of benefit provisions) [^c22846881].
