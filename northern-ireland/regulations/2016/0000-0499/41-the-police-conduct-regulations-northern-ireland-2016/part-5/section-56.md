# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Record of special case hearing

(1) A verbatim record of the proceedings at the special case hearing shall be taken.

(2) Where the member concerned has exercised his right of appeal in accordance with regulation 55(3) he shall, on request, be supplied with a copy of the record of the proceedings at the special case hearing.

(3) Where the member concerned is a senior officer, a verbatim record of the special case hearing shall be taken and the transcript of the record shall be made and sent to the Board.
