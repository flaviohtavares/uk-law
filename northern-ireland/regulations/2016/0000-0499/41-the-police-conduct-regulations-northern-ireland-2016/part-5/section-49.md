# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Documents to be supplied

(1) Prior to the hearing the appropriate authority shall supply the person or persons conducting the special case hearing with a copy of—

- (a) the notice given to the member concerned under regulation 44(1);
- (b) the other documents given to the member concerned under regulation 44(1);
- (c) the documents provided by the member concerned under—
  - (i) regulation 46; and
  - (ii) where paragraph (2) applies, regulation 24(2) and (3);
- (d) where the member concerned does not accept that his conduct amounts to gross misconduct, any other documents that, in the opinion of the appropriate authority, should be considered at the hearing.

(2) This paragraph applies where the case was certified as a special case following a determination made under regulation 42(3).

(3) Prior to the hearing the member concerned shall be supplied with a list of the documents supplied under paragraph (1) and a copy of any of such document of which he has not already been supplied with a copy.
