# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Persons conducting special case hearing: senior officers

(1) This regulation applies where the member concerned is a senior officer.

(2) The special case hearing shall be conducted by a panel of three persons appointed by the appropriate authority, comprising—

- (a) a barrister or solicitor of not less than seven years’ standing, who shall be the chair and who has been nominated by the Lord Chief Justice for the purpose of this regulation;
- (b) the chair of the Board or another member of the Board nominated by him; and
- (c) HMCIC or an inspector of constabulary nominated by him.
