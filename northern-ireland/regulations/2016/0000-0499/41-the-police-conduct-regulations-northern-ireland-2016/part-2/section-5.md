# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Application

(1) These Regulations apply where an allegation comes to the attention of an appropriate authority which indicates that the conduct of a member may amount to misconduct or gross misconduct.

(2) Where an appropriate authority is considering more than one allegation in relation to the same member concerned, the allegations may be taken together and treated as a single allegation for the purposes of any provision of these Regulations which requires a person to make an assessment, finding or decision in connection with conduct which is the subject matter of an allegation.
