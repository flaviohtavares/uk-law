# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Revocation and transitional provisions

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Conduct) Regulations 2000[^f00002], regulation 28 of the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2000[^f00003] (and the Schedule of modifications to the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Conduct) Regulations 2000 introduced by that regulation) and the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Conduct) (Senior Officer) Regulations 2000[^f00004] are revoked.

(2) Where a complaint or allegation in respect of conduct by a member came to the attention of the appropriate authority before 1<sup>st</sup> June 2016, nothing in these Regulations shall apply and the Regulations referred to in paragraph (1) shall continue to have effect.
