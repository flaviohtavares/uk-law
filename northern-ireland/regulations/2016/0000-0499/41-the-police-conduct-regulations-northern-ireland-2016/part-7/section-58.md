# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Amendment to the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2000

In regulation 19(7) of the Royal Ulster Constabulary (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2000[^f00013] for “Royal Ulster Constabulary (Conduct) Regulations 2000” substitute “Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016”.
