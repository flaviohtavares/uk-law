# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Application of this Part

Save for regulations 14, 17 and 18 this Part shall not apply where section 56(1) or (1A) or section 54(3)(b) of the 1998 Act applies.
