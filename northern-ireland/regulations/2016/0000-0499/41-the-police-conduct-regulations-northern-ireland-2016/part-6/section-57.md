# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Record of disciplinary proceedings

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Chief Constable shall cause a record to be kept of disciplinary proceedings and special case proceedings brought against every member concerned, together with the finding and decision on disciplinary action and the decision in any appeal by the member concerned.

(2) Where the member concerned is the Chief Constable, the Board shall cause such a record to be kept.
