# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Procedure and finding of the appeal

(1) Subject to the provisions of this regulation, the person determining the appeal shall determine the procedure at the appeal meeting.

(2) The person determining the appeal may—

- (a) confirm or reverse the decision appealed against;
- (b) deal with the member concerned in any manner in which the person conducting the misconduct meeting could have dealt with him under regulation 36.

(3) Before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the determination of the appeal, the member concerned shall be given written notice of that determination with a summary of the reasons.

(4) The decision of the person determining the appeal shall take effect by way of substitution for the decision of the person conducting the misconduct meeting and as from the date of the written notice of the outcome of that meeting.
