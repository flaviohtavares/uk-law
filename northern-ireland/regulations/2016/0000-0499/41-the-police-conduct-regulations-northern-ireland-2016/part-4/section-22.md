# The Police (Conduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Withdrawal of case

(1) Subject to section 59(6)(b) of the 1998 Act, at any time before the beginning of the misconduct proceedings, the appropriate authority may direct that the case be withdrawn.

(2) Where a direction is given under paragraph (1)—

- (a) the appropriate authority may—
  - (i) take no further action against the member concerned;
  - (ii) take management action against the member concerned; or
  - (iii) refer the matter to be dealt with under the Performance Regulations; and
- (b) the appropriate authority shall as soon as practicable give the member concerned—
  - (i) written notice of the direction, indicating whether any action will be taken under sub-paragraph (a); and
  - (ii) where the investigation has been completed, on request and subject to the harm test, a copy of the investigator’s report or such parts of that report as relate to the member concerned.
