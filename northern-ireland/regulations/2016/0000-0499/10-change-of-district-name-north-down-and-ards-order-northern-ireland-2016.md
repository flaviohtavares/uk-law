# Change of District Name (North Down and Ards) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 10

Local Government

Change of District Name (North Down and Ards) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

18th January 2016

Coming into operation

24th February 2016

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(1) of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

In accordance with that section 51(1), the Department of the Environment has received an application from North Down and Ards Borough Council to change the name of the district of the council from North Down and Ards to Ards and North Down.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Change of District Name (North Down and Ards) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 24th February 2016.

## 2. Change of district name

The name of the district of North Down and Ards shall be changed to Ards and North Down.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18th January 2016.

(L.S.)

Nichola Creagh

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of the local government district of North Down and Ards identified in Article 2(1) of, and Schedule 1 to, the Local Government (Boundaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 to Ards and North Down.
