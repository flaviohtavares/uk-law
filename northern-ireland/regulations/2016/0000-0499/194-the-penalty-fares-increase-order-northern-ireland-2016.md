# The Penalty Fares (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 194

Roads And Railway Transport

The Penalty Fares (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

24th March 2016

Coming into operation

13th June 2016

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 4(2) of Schedule 1B to the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Penalty Fares (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and comes into operation on 13th June 2016.

## 2. Increase in amount of penalty fare

In paragraph 4(1)(a)[^f00004] of Schedule 1B to the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 for “£20” substitute “£50”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 24th March 2016.

Tom Reid

A senior officer of the

Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Schedule 1B to the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 (as inserted by [SI 1990/994 (N.I. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1990/994)) makes provision for penalty fares to be paid by a person who travels on a bus or a train service without paying the proper fare.

Paragraph 4(1) of that Schedule provides that the amount of a penalty fare shall be the full single fare for the journey plus £20.

This Order increases that amount by an additional £30 to £50.
