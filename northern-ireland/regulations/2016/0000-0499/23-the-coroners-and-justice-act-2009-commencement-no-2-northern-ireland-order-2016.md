# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No.2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 23 (C. 1)

Inquests (northern Ireland)

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No.2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2016

Made

25th January 2016

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 182(5) and (6) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No.2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

29th February 2016 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 49(2) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 25th January 2016

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 49(2) of the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25) which introduces Schedule 11 of the Act. Schedule 11 substitutes new sections 17A to 17C for section 17 of the Coroners Act (Northern Ireland) [1959 (c.15) (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1959/15). Sections 17A to 17C make provision concerning witnesses, evidence and related offences at inquests in Northern Ireland.
