# The Food Information (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 249

Food

The Food Information (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

27th June 2016

Coming into operation

20th July 2016

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 15(1), 16(2) and 47(2) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003].

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appears to the Department of Health that it is expedient for references to provisions of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011[^f00004] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the provision of food information to consumers, amending Regulations [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924) and [(EC) No 1925/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1925) of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Commission [Directive 87/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0250), Council [Directive 90/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0496), Commission [Directive 1999/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0010), [Directive 2000/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0013) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Commission Directives [2002/67/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0067), and [2008/5/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0005) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No 608/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0608) or, to provisions of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 828/2014[^f00005] on the requirements for the provision of information to consumers on the absence or reduced presence of gluten in food, specified in Regulation 2(2)(b), to be construed as references to those provisions as amended from time to time.

Insofar as these Regulations are made in exercise of powers under the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991, the Department of Health has had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency in accordance with Article 47(3A) of that Order.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178)[^f00006] of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety, there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Information (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation on 20<sup>th</sup> July 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision to enforce the requirements of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 828/2014 on the requirements for the provision of information to consumers on the absence or reduced presence of gluten in food. The Regulations are made having regard in particular to Article 36(3)(d) of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25th October 2011 on the provision of food information to consumers (“FIC”) which were implemented by the Food Information Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 (“the 2014 Regulations).

Regulation 2 makes amendments to the 2014 Regulations to ensure that appropriate references to FIC and FIC provisions incorporate references to Regulation 828/2014.

Regulation 3 revokes the Foodstuffs Suitable for People Intolerant to Gluten Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016.
