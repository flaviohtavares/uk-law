# The Environmental Better Regulation (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 212 (C. 16)

Environmental Protection

The Environmental Better Regulation (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

15th April 2016

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(2) of the Environmental Better Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Environmental Better Regulation (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 7 to 14 of the Environmental Better Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 is 1<sup>st</sup> May 2016.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15<sup>th</sup> April 2016

Dave Foster

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation sections 7 to 14 of the Environmental Better Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.
