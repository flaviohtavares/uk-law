# The Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (Consequential Amendments) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 108

Insolvency

Disqualification Provisions – For Debt Relief Orders And Debt Relief Restrictions Orders

The Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (Consequential Amendments) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

2nd March 2016

Coming into operation

7th March 2016

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (Consequential Amendments) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and comes into operation on 7<sup>th</sup> March 2016.

## 2. Consequential amendments to or in connection with disqualification provisions

The Schedule, (which amends the effect of disqualification provisions) shall have effect.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 2 March 2016

Wendy Johnston

A senior officer of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order makes consequential amendments to or in connection with disqualification provisions to take account of debt relief order and debt relief restrictions orders.

The amendments made by this order are to primary and subordinate legislation which already provides for disqualification of persons from in the event of insolvency, so as to extend these disqualifications to persons subject to debt relief orders (under Part 7A of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989) and to debt relief restrictions orders (under Schedule 2ZA to the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on the costs on business.
