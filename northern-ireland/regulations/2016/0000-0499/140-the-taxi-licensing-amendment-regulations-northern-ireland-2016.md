# The Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 140

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

10th March 2016

Coming into operation

31st May 2016

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 20 and 56(1) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 31<sup>st</sup> May 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the Taxi Licensing Regulations

(1) The Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In regulation 4(1) (inspection of taxi) for “should” substitute “shall”.

(3) In regulation 22(1) (luggage) for “should” substitute “shall”.

(4) In regulation 25(2)(g) (roof sign) omit “yellow”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 10<sup>th</sup> March 2016

Elizabeth Loughran

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make minor amendments to the Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015.

They substitute the word “should” with “shall” in regulations 4(1) and 22(1) and omit the word “yellow” in regulation 25(2)(g).

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
