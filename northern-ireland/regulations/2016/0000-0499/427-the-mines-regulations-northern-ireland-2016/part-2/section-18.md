## 18. Power of safety representatives to report imminent risk[^key-8230a2b8a7ada86c08b0ba40a03c8922]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where two or more safety representatives (within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of the Safety Representatives and Safety Committees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00016]) consider that there is an imminent risk of serious personal injury arising from an activity carried out below ground at a mine, they—

- (a) may make a report in writing to the mine operator; and
- (b) may provide a copy of that report to the Executive.
