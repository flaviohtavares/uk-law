> Text as at 2022-03-21.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Record of persons below ground[^key-33ea7d166a74e7d9cbe985d4cd0531d3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The mine operator shall ensure that a record is kept of every person below ground at the mine.
