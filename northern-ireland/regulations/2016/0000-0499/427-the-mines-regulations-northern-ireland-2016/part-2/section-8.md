> Text as at 2022-03-21.

## 8. Co-operation[^key-55e928031f44e314542d10bfe6c35f03]

*Extent: N.I.*

Every employer of persons at work at a mine and every person at work at a mine shall co-operate with the mine operator to the extent necessary to enable the mine operator to comply with the relevant statutory provisions.
