# The Mines Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 17

> Text as at 2022-03-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Danger areas[^key-3b39450d41e465fc86bb8fc86b3e715d]

*Extent: N.I.*

The mine operator shall ensure that—

- (a) danger areas are clearly identified;
- (b) there is a suitable barrier or enclosure to prevent, so far as is reasonably practicable, inadvertent entry by any unauthorised person to a danger area; and
- (c) where any person is authorised to enter the danger area, appropriate measures are taken to protect that person’s health and safety.
