# The Mines Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 60

> Text as at 2022-03-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. General duty to ensure safety of tips[^c-96ed3d09cab0e33ff88c7d2d4bb7f346]

*Extent: N.I.*

The mine operator shall ensure that tips are designed, constructed, operated and maintained so as to ensure that—

- (a) instability; or
- (b) movement,

which is likely to give rise to a risk to the health and safety of any person is avoided.
