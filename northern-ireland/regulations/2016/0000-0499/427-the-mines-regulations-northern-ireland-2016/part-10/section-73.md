> Text as at 2022-03-21.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Repeals and revocations[^key-9fc51725cc334b807e2a4e5536d39b06]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The provisions of the 1969 Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 3 are repealed to the extent set out in the corresponding entry in column 2.

(2) The instruments specified in Part 1 of Schedule 4 are revoked in full.

(3) The instruments specified in column 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 4 are revoked to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in column 2.
