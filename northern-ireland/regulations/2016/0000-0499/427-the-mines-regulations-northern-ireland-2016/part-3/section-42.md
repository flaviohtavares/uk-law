## 42. Transport rules[^key-bf0e598b5ce13e7d6eb7039b7fd6820a]

*Extent: N.I.*

Rules required by regulation 12 shall include rules for the purpose of ensuring the safe operation of vehicles, conveyors and traffic routes.
