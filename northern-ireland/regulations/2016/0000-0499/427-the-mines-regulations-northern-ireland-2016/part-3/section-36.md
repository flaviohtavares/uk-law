> Text as at 2022-03-21.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Shaft construction and modification[^key-a603317c560a4e698b9f6a9d5dd507c2]

*Extent: N.I.*

The mine operator shall ensure that, so far as is reasonably practicable, the construction and modification of every shaft is planned, designed and carried out so as to be safe and without the risk of injury to any person.
