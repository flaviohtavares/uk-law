## 26. Risks of explosion in coal mines[^c-afcebcbf34e02ed4c7022c6b5e9cbfd3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The mine operator shall ensure that (where necessary) there are in place—

- (a) adequate arrangements to ensure that there is sufficient inert content in dust in roadways in the mine to ensure that risks of explosion are controlled; and
- (b) suitable and effective barriers against the spread of explosion.
