# The Mines Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2022-03-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Signalling and communication[^key-a285da379cd709b7e52adbf847133a78]

*Extent: N.I.*

In any shaft or outlet in which there is winding apparatus or other equipment, the mine operator shall ensure that there is provided suitable and safe means to communicate and signal, where such means of communication and signalling is necessary to enable the winding apparatus or equipment to be used safely.
