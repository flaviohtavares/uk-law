# The Areas of Natural Constraint Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> Text as at 2017-03-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2016 No.15

Agriculture

The Areas of Natural Constraint Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 (revoked with savings)

Made

Coming into operation

1st March 2016

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development is a department designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20] [^c22819851] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20] [^c22819861] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union and in relation to matters relating to the promotion of rural development. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Areas of Natural Constraint Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st March 2016. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 2. Interpretation

## 3. Payment of ANC allowance

The Department may pay an ANC allowance to any claimant who is eligible under regulation 4 in respect of land that meets the conditions of regulation 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 4. Claimant eligibility for ANC allowance

(1) A claimant shall be eligible for ANC allowance if, and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

- (a) the claimant is an active farmer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (b) the claim is made on the single application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (c) subject to paragraph (2) the notional livestock density relating to the forage area on the claimant's holding does not fall below 0.2 on any one of a number of dates determined by the Department during the minimum grazing period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (d) the area of land complying with regulation 5 is at least three hectares. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(2) The Department may determine that a notional livestock density of less than 0.2 is sufficient to meet the eligibility requirement for the purposes of paragraph (1)(c) if it is reasonably satisfied that it is appropriate to do so in all the circumstances of the case, having regard, in particular, to the sensitivity of the land in question to a stocking density of more than 0.2 and any obligation of the claimant under or in pursuance of any statutory provision in relation to the maintenance of a stocking density of less than 0.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(3) For the purposes of calculating notional livestock density in any case the number of heifers to be taken into account shall not exceed a number equal to two thirds of the number of suckler cows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(4) A claimant shall provide such information as the Department may reasonably require to enable it to make a determination under paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(5) Article 13 of the Horizontal Delegated Regulation (late submission) shall apply to a claim made in a single application after 15th May 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 5. Land eligibility for ANC allowance

Land shall be eligible for payment if, and only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

- (a) it complies with the definition of qualifying forage area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (b) it lies within the severely disadvantaged area; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (c) it has been entered in the single application at column H under either of the land use codes in column 1 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 6. Rates of payment

Subject to regulation 4 and Articles 91, 92 and 93 of the Horizontal Regulation, payment of an ANC allowance for an area of land that complies with regulation 5 and which falls within the qualifying forage area specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 shall be paid at the rate specified opposite thereto in column 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 7. Exclusion of forage area

(1) For the purpose of determining the qualifying forage area, a claimant's forage area shall be reduced by 1 hectare for every 1.71 of the average number of eligible dairy stocking units for which he is the keeper over a number of dates determined by the Department during the minimum grazing period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies, the reduction in the forage area shall first be applied to the forage area which is not severely disadvantaged land, followed by severely disadvantaged land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 8. Transfer of holdings

For the purposes of Article 8(3)(a) of the Horizontal Implementing Regulation, the period within which the transferee must inform the Department of the transfer and request payment under regulation 3 is 90 days beginning with the first day after the date of the transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 9. Breaches of commitment

Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

- (a) any information furnished to the Department by a claimant or beneficiary is false or misleading; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (b) a claimant or beneficiary is in breach of any requirement to which he is subject under these Regulations or the Rural Development Regulation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (c) a claimant or beneficiary is not an active farmer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

the Department may exercise any of the powers specified in regulation 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 10. Department's powers or recovery

The powers conferred by regulation 9 are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

- (a) to withhold the whole or part of any payment payable to the claimant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]
- (b) to recover on demand the whole or part of any such payment already paid to the beneficiary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

## 11. Recovery of interest

(1) Where the Department exercises the power conferred by regulation 10(b), it may also recover, on demand, interest on the sum to be recovered in respect of each day of the period referred to in Article 7(2) of the Horizontal Implementing Regulation, and the rate of interest applicable on any day is one percentage point above the Bank of England base rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

(2) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]**Bank of England base rate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

- (a) except where sub-paragraph (b) applies, the rate announced from time to time by the Monetary Policy Committee of the Bank of England as the official dealing rate, being the rate at which the Bank is willing to enter into transactions for providing short-term liquidity in the money markets; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e53c4d3eac448a9511167a5f17ff1b20]

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations provide a domestic legal framework for the implementation of Article 31 of Regulation (EU) No. 1305/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on support for rural development by the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and specifically the payment of an ANC allowance. These Regulations also provide for the implementation of Measure 13 of the Northern Ireland Rural Development Programme 2014-2020 approved on 25 August 2015 by Commission Decision CCI: 2014UK06RDRP002.

The Regulations define the conditions of eligibility for an ANC allowance (regulations 3 to 5) and the rate at which it is to be paid (regulation 6 and Schedule 2).

Regulation 7 provides for the exclusion of forage area in respect of claimants who had dairy cows on their holding in the minimum grazing period (1 April to 31 October 2015).

Regulation 8 sets the maximum time (90 days) which beneficiaries have to notify the Department of a transfer of a holding.

Regulations 9 and 10 grant the Department power to withhold or recover payments and take certain other action in the event of a breach under these Regulations and in certain other circumstances.

Regulation 11 sets out how interest to be charged on amount owed to the Department will be calculated.

Regulation 12 allows for payments owed to the Department by beneficiaries to be treated by the Department as debt.

Regulation 13 provides for debts owed to the Department to be deducted from future payments to that beneficiary.

Regulation 14 provides the powers of entry to land and premises for the purpose of enforcing these regulations to persons authorised by the Department.

Regulation 15 sets out the powers of inspection of such authorised persons.

Regulations 16 and 17 make it an offence to obstruct, fail to provide information without reasonable cause or furnish false or misleading information to a person acting to implement these Regulations and establishes the penalties which may be imposed.
