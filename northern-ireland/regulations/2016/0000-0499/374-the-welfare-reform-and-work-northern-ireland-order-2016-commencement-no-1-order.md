# The Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 374 (C. 28)

Social Security

The Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016

Made

13th October 2016

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 2(1) of the Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016[^f00001].

Those powers are exercisable by the Secretary of State by virtue of Article 4(1)(a) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00002].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 7th November 2016

Article 5 (benefit cap) of the Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016 comes into force for remaining purposes on 7th November 2016.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Freud

Minister of State

Department for Work and Pensions

13th October 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force Article 5 (benefit cap) of the Welfare Reform and Work (Northern Ireland) Order 2016 for remaining purposes on 7th November 2016.
