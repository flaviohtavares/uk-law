# The Departments (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 89 (C. 5)

Departments Of Northern Ireland

The Departments (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

1st March 2016

The First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Departments Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00001], make the following Order.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Departments (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Departments Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 1 and 2 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the Act is 8<sup>th</sup> May 2016.

Dated this 1st day of March 2016.

Arlene Foster

First Minister

Martin McGuinness

deputy First Minister

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Departments Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 —

sections 1 and 2 and the Schedules on 8<sup>th</sup> May 2016.
