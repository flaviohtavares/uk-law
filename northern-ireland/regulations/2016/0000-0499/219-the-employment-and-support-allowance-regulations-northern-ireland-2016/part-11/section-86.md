# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 86

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 86. Linking period[^c-d375348cfc57b6c74a4fea4f5d33a918]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any period of limited capability for work which is separated from another such period by not more than 12 weeks is to be treated as a continuation of the earlier period.
