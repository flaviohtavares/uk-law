# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 42

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Application of regulations where there is dual entitlement[^key-44f6f78ebfa40d25bd650aaf5aa5013d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a person is entitled to universal credit and an employment and support allowance.

(2) The work-related requirements under sections 11B to 11I of the Act[^f00053] and regulations 46 to 49 of these Regulations do not apply to such a person.

(3) Reductions relating to the award of an employment and support allowance under section 11J of the Act[^f00054] (sanctions) and regulations 50 to 60 of these Regulations do not apply to such a person.
