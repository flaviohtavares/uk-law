# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 45

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Claimant commitment – exceptions[^c-aafaa796d50702fc546d921d5e14752e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-63c6048f26336a74020495fb624b7b5b] A claimant may be entitled to an employment and support allowance without having accepted a claimant commitment if the Department considers that—

- (a) the claimant cannot accept a claimant commitment because they lack capacity to do so; or
- (b) there are exceptional circumstances in which it would be unreasonable to expect the person to accept a claimant commitment.

(2)[^c-016667bf780170d850c244e9cf420588] A claimant may be entitled to an employment and support allowance without having accepted a claimant commitment if the claimant is terminally ill.
