# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 44

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Claimant commitment – date and method of acceptance[^c-6df8e545c88c83186b6032e9da6a2a34]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 1(3)(aa) of the Act[^f00055], a claimant who has accepted a claimant commitment within such period after making a claim for an employment and support allowance as the Department specifies is to be treated as having accepted that claimant commitment on the first day of the period in respect of which the claim is made.

(2) In a case where an award of an employment and support allowance may be made without a claim, a claimant who accepts a claimant commitment within such period as the Department specifies is to be treated as having accepted a claimant commitment on the day that would be the first day of the first benefit week in relation to the award.

(3) The Department may extend the period within which a claimant is required to accept a claimant commitment or an updated claimant commitment where the claimant requests an extension and the Department considers that the request is reasonable.

(4) A claimant must accept a claimant commitment by one of the following methods, as specified by the Department—

- (a) electronically;
- (b) by telephone; or
- (c) in writing.
