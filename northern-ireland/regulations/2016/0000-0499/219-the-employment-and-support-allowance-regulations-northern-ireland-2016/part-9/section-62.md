> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Prescribed amounts[^c-ea6f90fa757c377a1bcc9a69a824de48][^c-b4ea6b8d715bf05e8ff95f0628cf3d9f][^c-9b80468fdb57f46030b356d122c1add3][^c-0a559b3947375faa025bc4a7051c5f25][^c-0728e4a3e2b9beefe838b1f8bcd97923][^c-8ee658f38cadffbdecf02b2f02ad59d5][^c-6408190c732bdc6471a3d566e3a31864][^c-bb060e4c955f86c1762589086d8592ac][^c-858eb54575e4030eb114d0bef3595fef][^c-46010749ef56d048b92b30df1e05d973][^c-6b0886fd8f7b6cbadec36b38f96c5618][^c-f5abddc8c8261ebbfd19e68126125735][^c-669b637714e59838bf36717e3bfbea16][^c-3b3d3543a46a058493af2122c469edde]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 63 (special cases) the amount prescribed for the purposes of the calculation of the amount of a claimant’s employment and support allowance under section 2(1)(a) of the Act is—

- (a) where the claimant satisfies the conditions set out in section 2(2) or (3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4aa92a01f66b7f8a7aac8248c0e43c7f] of the Act or the claimant is a member of the work-related activity group and satisfies the conditions set out in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act[^c-a78eac5d522633ede583f17e70f4780d], £95.55[^c-29b9c53bda9e2ec13ab3125c38b6f563]; or
- (b) where the claimant does not satisfy the conditions set out in section 2(2) or (3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4aa92a01f66b7f8a7aac8248c0e43c7f] of the Act or the claimant is a member of the work-related activity group and satisfies the conditions set out in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act—
  - (i) where the claimant is aged not less than 25, £95.55[^c-49a264beebe6e98ac27df5f117b3b16d], or
  - (ii) where the claimant is aged less than 25, £75.65[^c-e557a702e8980ccfef82c15a16627c1f].

(2) Subject to regulation 63, the amount of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650]

- (a) the work-related activity component is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650] £37.95[^c-ef1ab33e1a24d0bacea278eb6e00e50e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650]
- (b) the support component is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650] £50.35[^c-37fde18fd7ae11df6cc80ff9f058ffd5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-afc339ab052a57c1d73768fee79e2650]

Subject to regulation 63 the amount of the support component is £50.35[^c-3a381bf06b1f8d48e80614f557f26fa7].
