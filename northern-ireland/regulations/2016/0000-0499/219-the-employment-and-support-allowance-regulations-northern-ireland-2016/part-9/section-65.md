> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 65. Financial Assistance Scheme[^key-fe7d3e1f89a5d7a7c85ca0f832b1c16c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of sections 2(1)(c) and 3(3) of the Act “pension payment” is to include a Financial Assistance Scheme payment.

(2) In this regulation “Financial Assistance Scheme payment” means a payment made under the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005[^f00067].
