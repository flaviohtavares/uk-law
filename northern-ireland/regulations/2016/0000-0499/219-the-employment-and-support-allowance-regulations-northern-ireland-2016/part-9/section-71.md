# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 71

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71. Date from which a change in the rate of the payment takes effect[^c-449f1bbceb7ec9eddb33747bc538f7ac]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a payment or councillor’s allowance is already being made to a claimant and the rate of that payment or allowance changes, the deduction at the new rate must take effect, calculated where appropriate in accordance with regulation 79(1) or (5), from the first day of the benefit week in which the new rate of the payment or councillor’s allowance is paid.
