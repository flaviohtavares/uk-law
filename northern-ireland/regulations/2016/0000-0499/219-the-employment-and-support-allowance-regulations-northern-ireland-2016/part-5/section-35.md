# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 35

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Claimant may be called for a medical examination to determine whether the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity[^key-fefed4bc425aa91030fb0d26b6636d40]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where it falls to be determined whether a claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, that claimant may be called by or on behalf of a health care professional approved by the Department to attend for a medical examination in person, by telephone or by video[^key-ab6f8e44e73ad7110619e15ff7152574].

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), where a claimant fails without good cause to attend for or to submit to an examination mentioned in paragraph (1), the claimant is to be treated as not having limited capability for work-related activity.

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply unless—

- (a) written notice of the date, time and place for the examination was sent to the claimant at least 7 days in advance; or
- (b) the claimant agreed to accept a shorter period of notice whether given in writing or otherwise.
