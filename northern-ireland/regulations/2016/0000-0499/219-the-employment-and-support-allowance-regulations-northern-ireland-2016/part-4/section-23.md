# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 23

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Claimant to be treated as having limited capability for work throughout a day[^key-afe388f9f02c59d0c189b39a1079843c]

*Extent: N.I.*

A claimant who, at the commencement of any day has, or after that develops, limited capability for work as determined in accordance with the limited capability for work assessment is to be treated as having limited capability for work throughout that day.
