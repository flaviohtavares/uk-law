# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Additional circumstances where claimants are to be treated as having limited capability for work[^key-d3129538121eb2467cef696e0a79e5e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph 4(1)(d)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Act, a claimant is to be treated as having limited capability for work on any day in respect of which that claimant is entitled to statutory sick pay.
