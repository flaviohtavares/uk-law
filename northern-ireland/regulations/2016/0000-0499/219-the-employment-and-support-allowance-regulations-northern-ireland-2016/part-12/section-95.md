# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 95

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Treating a claimant as not having limited capability for work[^c-de8dd4dfa7b44395a9abcbaa270932fb]

*Extent: N.I.*

The claimant is to be treated as not having limited capability for work if the claimant is disqualified for receiving an employment and support allowance during a period of imprisonment or detention in legal custody if that disqualification is for more than 6 weeks.
