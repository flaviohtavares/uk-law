# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-30.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Time within which a claim to a personal independence payment is to be made

*Extent: N.I.*

A claim for personal independence payment must be made on the first day of the period in respect of which the claim is made.
