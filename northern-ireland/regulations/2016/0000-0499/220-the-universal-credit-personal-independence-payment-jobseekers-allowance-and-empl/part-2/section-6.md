# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 6

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Claims not required for entitlement to employment and support allowance in certain cases

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It is not to be a condition of entitlement to an employment and support allowance that a claim be made for it where—

- (a) the claimant has made and is pursuing an appeal against a relevant decision of the Department; and
- (b) the appeal relates to a decision to terminate or not to award an employment and support allowance for which a claim was made.

(2) In this regulation—

- “**appellate authority**” means an appeal tribunal, a Commissioner, the Court of Appeal or the Supreme Court;
- “**relevant decision**” means—
  - (a) a decision that embodies the first determination by the Department that the claimant does not have limited capability for work; or
  - (b) a decision that embodies the first determination by the Department that the claimant does not have limited capability for work since a previous determination by the Department or appellate authority that the claimant does have limited capability for work.
