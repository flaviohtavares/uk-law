# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 5

> Text as at 2026-04-30.

## 5. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Administration Act

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act [^c24174501] (entitlement to benefit dependent on claim) is not to apply to a child or a qualifying young person in respect of whom universal credit is claimed.

## Annotations

[^c24174501]: Marginal citation: Subsection (1A) was inserted by Article 18 of the Social Security Administration (Fraud) (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
