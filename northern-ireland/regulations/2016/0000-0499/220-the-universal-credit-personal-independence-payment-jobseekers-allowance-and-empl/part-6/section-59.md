# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 59

## 59. Restriction on duration of arrangements by the Department (Motability)

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department must terminate an arrangement under regulation 57(2) where the Department is satisfied that—

- (a) the vehicle to which the arrangement relates has been returned to the owner; and
- (b) the expenses of the owner arising out of the hire or hire-purchase agreement have been recovered following the return of the vehicle.
