# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 58

> Text as at 2026-04-30.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 58. Power of the Department to terminate an arrangement (Motability)

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may terminate an arrangement under regulation 57(2) on such date as the Department decides—

- (a) if requested to do so by the owner of the vehicle to which the arrangement relates; or
- (b) if it appears to the Department that the arrangement is causing undue hardship to the claimant and that it should be terminated earlier than provided for by regulation 57(3) or (4).
