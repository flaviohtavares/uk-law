# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 48

> Text as at 2026-04-30.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 48. Fractional amounts of benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the amount of any benefit payable would, but for this regulation, include a fraction of a penny, that fraction is to be disregarded if it is less than half a penny and is otherwise to be treated as a penny.
