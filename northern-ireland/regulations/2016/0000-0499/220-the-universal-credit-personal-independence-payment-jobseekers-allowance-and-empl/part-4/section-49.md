# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 49

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Payments to persons under age 18

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a benefit is paid to a person under the age of 18, a direct credit transfer under regulation 41 into any such person's account, or the receipt by the person of a payment made by some other means, is sufficient discharge for the Department.
