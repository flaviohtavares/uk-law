# The Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 132

Licensing Of Pavement Cafés

The Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

2nd March 2016

Coming into operation

1st October 2016

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) of the Licensing of Pavement Cafés Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Form of Licence) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2016.

## 2. Form of licence for Pavement Cafés

A pavement café licence shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 2nd March 2016

(L.S.)

Chris Hughes

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## SCHEDULE

Regulation 2

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations prescribe the form of a pavement café licence which is issued by a district council and authorises the licence holder to place on a public area (identified by the licence) temporary furniture for use for the consumption of food or drink supplied, in the course of a business carried on by the licence holder, in or from premises specified in the licence.
