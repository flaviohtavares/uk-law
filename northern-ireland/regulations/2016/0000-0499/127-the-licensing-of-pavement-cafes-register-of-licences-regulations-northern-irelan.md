# The Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 127

Licensing Of Pavement Cafés

The Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

2nd March 2016

Coming into operation

1st October 2016

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20 of the Licensing of Pavement Cafés Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> October 2016.

## 2. Matters to be notified

A district council must notify the person responsible for keeping the licensing register of matters as set out in Schedule 1.

## 3. Documents to be sent

A district council must send the person responsible for keeping the licensing register copies of documents as set out in Schedule 2.

## 4. Matters to be recorded

The person responsible for keeping the licensing register must record particulars of matters relating to a relevant pavement café licence as set out in Schedule 1. These particulars of matters must be recorded in column 6A of the Schedule to The Licensing (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003].

## 5. Documents to be kept

The person responsible for keeping the licensing register must keep all documents as set out in Schedule 2.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 2nd March 2016

(L.S.)

Chris Hughes

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## SCHEDULE 1

Regulation 2

- 1) The grant of a relevant pavement café licence
- 2) The renewal of a relevant pavement café licence
- 3) The revocation of a relevant pavement café licence
- 4) The suspension of a relevant pavement café licence
- 5) An alcohol condition attached to a relevant pavement café licence
- 6) The surrender of a relevant pavement café licence

## SCHEDULE 2

Regulation 3

- 1) A copy of a relevant pavement café licence, including the plan showing the location and dimensions of the public area to which it relates
- 2) A copy of any amended plan relating to a subsisting relevant pavement café licence
- 3) A copy of a subsisting relevant pavement café licence which has had an alcohol condition inserted or removed
- 4) A copy of a relevant pavement café licence renewal
- 5) A copy of a relevant pavement café licence revocation notice
- 6) A copy of a relevant pavement café licence suspension notice
- 7) A copy of a relevant pavement café licence surrender notice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations set out the matters a district council must notify, and the documents it must send, to the person responsible for maintaining the Register of Licences under Article 34 of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (licences permitting premises to sell intoxicating liquor by retail to the public) in relation to a relevant pavement café licence. It also sets out the matters the person responsible for maintaining the Register of Licences must record in the register and the documents that must be kept.
