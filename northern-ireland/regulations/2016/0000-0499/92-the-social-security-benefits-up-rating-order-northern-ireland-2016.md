# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 92

Social Security

The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Approved by resolution of the Assembly on 20th June 2016

Made

2nd March 2016

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 132A and 165(1), (4) and (5) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and now vested in the Department for Social Development[^f00002].

The Secretary of State has made an Order[^f00003] under sections 150A and 189(1), (4) and (5) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00004].

Accordingly, the Department for Social Development makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation for the purposes of—

- (a) this Article, Articles 2 and 3(c) and the Schedule so far as it relates to Part V of Schedule 4 to the Contributions and Benefits Act, on 6th April 2016;
- (b) Articles 3(a) and (b), 4 and 5, and the remaining parts of the Schedule, on 11th April 2016; and
- (c) Article 6, in so far as it relates to a particular beneficiary, on the first day of the first benefit week to commence for that beneficiary on or after 11th April 2016, and for the purpose of this sub-paragraph “benefit week” has the same meaning as in the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00005].

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00006].

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00007] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

(3) For the purposes of this Order and notwithstanding section 39(2) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954, where a period of time is expressed to begin on, or to be reckoned from, a particular day, that day shall be included in the period.

## 3. Amounts of certain benefits under the Contributions and Benefits Act

The following sums shall be increased to the amounts set out in the Schedule—

- (a) the sum specified in Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Contributions and Benefits Act for Category B retirement pension where section 48A(4) or 48AA(4)[^f00008] applies;
- (b) the sum specified in Part III of that Schedule for Category D retirement pension; and
- (c) the sum specified in Part V of that Schedule for widow’s pension (apart from the amount of the initial rate) and widower’s pension.

## 4. Increase in amount of basic pension in a Category A retirement pension under the Contributions and Benefits Act

In section 44(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00009] (basic pension in a Category A retirement pension) for “£115∙95” substitute “£119∙30”.

## 5. Increase in amount of Category C retirement pension under the Social Security (Widow’s Benefit and Retirement Pensions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979

In regulation 11(3) of the Social Security (Widow’s Benefit and Retirement Pensions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00010] (Category C retirement pension for widows of men over pensionable age on 5th July 1948) for “£69∙50” substitute “£71∙50”.

## 6. State Pension Credit

In regulation 6 of the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00011] (amount of the guarantee credit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£230∙85” substitute “£237∙55”; and
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£151∙20” substitute “£155∙60”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 2nd March 2016

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corresponds to an Order ([S.I. 2016/230](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/230)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under section 150A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 and includes details of the sums mentioned in that section.

Article 3 and the Schedule increase certain benefits specified in Parts I, III and V of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992. These are the amounts of a category B and D retirement pension and widow’s pension (apart from the amount of the initial rate) and widower’s pension in industrial death benefit.

Article 4 increases the amounts of the basic pension in a Category A retirement pension.

Article 5 increases the amount of a Category C retirement pension.

Article 6 increases the amount of the standard minimum guarantee in state pension credit.
