# The State Pension (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 88

Social Security

The State Pension (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Approved by resolution of the Assembly on 28th June 2016

Made

1st March 2016

Coming into operation

6th April 2016

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(1) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the State Pension (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2016, immediately after the coming into operation of the State Pension Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the State Pension Regulations

After Part 1 of the State Pension Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 (general) insert—

> **PART 1A — Full rate of State Pension**
>
> **1A. Full rate of state pension**
>
> The full rate of the state pension for the purposes of section 3(1) of the Act (full rate of state pension) is £155.65 per week.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 1st March 2016

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations set the full rate of the new state pension by amending the State Pension Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No. 315](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/315)). The Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) [2015 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/5) creates the new state pension for people reaching pensionable age on or after 6th April 2016 (see Part 1 of that Act). It replaces the existing state pension for those people. The full rate is set at £155.65 per week.

Section 3(1) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2015, the enabling power under which these Regulations are made, was brought into operation on 25th February 2016 for the purposes of authorising the making of Regulations by the Pensions (2015 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 68 (C. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/68)).
