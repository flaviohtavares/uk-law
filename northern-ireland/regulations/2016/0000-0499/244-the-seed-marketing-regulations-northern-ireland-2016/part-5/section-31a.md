# The Seed Marketing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 31A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31A. Marketing seed under a specific derogation[^key-e616b4f2652f8f1ea6f836559992464f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In order to remove any temporary difficulties in the general supply of seed, the Department may permit, temporarily the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements of minimum germination specified in Schedule 2 for the relevant species of seed.

## 31A. Marketing seed under a specific derogation[^key-e9fa7cee77328676ff3965f7cdaffba2]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department acts as the Member State for the purposes of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 217/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/217) laying down rules for the application of Council Directives [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/401), [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/402), [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/54), [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/55) and [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/57) as regards the authorisation of Member States to permit temporarily the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements in respect of the minimum germination
