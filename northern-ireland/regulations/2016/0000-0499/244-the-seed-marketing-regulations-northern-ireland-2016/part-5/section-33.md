> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Licensing in another part of the United Kingdom

*Extent: N.I.*

A crop inspector, seed sampler or seed testing station licensed to act as such in another part of the United Kingdom may act as such under these Regulations.
