## 14. Re-grading of seed at the time of marketing

*Extent: N.I.*

For the avoidance of doubt, if a crop or seed has been certified as belonging to one category, but complies with the minimum requirements of another category for that seed, it may be re-graded as any category for which it satisfies the requirements.
