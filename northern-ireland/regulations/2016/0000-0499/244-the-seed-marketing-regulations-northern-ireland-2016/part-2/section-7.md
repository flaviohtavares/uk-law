# The Seed Marketing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Maintainers for pre-basic and basic seed

*Extent: N.I.*

Pre-basic seed and basic seed may only be produced with the written authorisation of the person listed as the maintainer of that seed in the NI Variety List[^key-685e9ce9bd0a685e2629cc6c7f1a1358] or an equivalent list in a country referred to in the Annex to Council Decision[^key-95a33ea9efd3b6a21765c542130d40a7] [2005/834/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2005/834) on the equivalence of checks on practices for the maintenance of varieties carried out in certain third countries.
