# The Seed Marketing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Meaning of “marketing”

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations “**marketing**” means the sale, holding with a view to sale, offer for sale or any disposal, supply or transfer aimed in each case at commercial exploitation of seed to third parties, whether or not for consideration and “**market**” and “**marketing**” shall be construed accordingly.

(2) But marketing does not include trade that is not aimed at commercial exploitation, such as—

- (a) the supply of seed to official testing and inspection bodies; or
- (b) the supply of seed to a person who provides processing or packaging services but who does not acquire title to the seed.
