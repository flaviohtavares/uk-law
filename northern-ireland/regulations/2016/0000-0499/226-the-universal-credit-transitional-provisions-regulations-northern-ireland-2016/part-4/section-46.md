# The Universal Credit (Transitional Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 46

> Text as at 2026-10-30.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Extension of the deadline day[^c-e97a2f3efa41f940f3ec36113800032e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may determine that the deadline day should be changed to a later day either—

- (a) on the Department’s own initiative; or
- (b) if a notified person requests such a change before the deadline day,

where there is a good reason to do so.

(2) The Department must inform the other notified person or persons of the new deadline day.
