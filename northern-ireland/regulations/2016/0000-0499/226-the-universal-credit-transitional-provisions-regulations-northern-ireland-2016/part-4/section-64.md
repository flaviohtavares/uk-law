## 64. Claimants previously entitled to a severe disability premium[^key-e97a2f3efa41f940f3ec36113800032e]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedules 2 and 3 contain[^key-3309590544c36cbc53688ec5737c5341] provision in respect of certain claimants who have been entitled to a benefit which included a severe disability premium.
