## 59. Qualifying claim – Department may set later commencement day[^c-e97a2f3efa41f940f3ec36113800032e]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Department decides a qualifying claim, and it is not a case where the award is to commence before the date of claim by virtue of regulation 47(3) [^c-b07b3f38c2ed62f1a1bc4e41da47705c]... (claim made by the final deadline) or regulation 25 of the Claims and Payments Regulations (time within which a claim for universal credit is to be made) as modified by regulation 14 of these Regulations, the Department may determine a day on which the award of universal credit is to commence that is after, but no more than one month after, the date of claim.
