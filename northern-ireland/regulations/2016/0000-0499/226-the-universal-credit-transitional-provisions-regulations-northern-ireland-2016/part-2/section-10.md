# The Universal Credit (Transitional Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 10

## 10. Modification of tax credits legislation: overpayments and penalties[^key-7a97c6c28739cf583ca5eef9c29030da]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) a claim for universal credit is made, or is treated as having been made; and[^key-b0dd3c02d9ce3157ae081bfc13ba53c4]
- (b) the claimant is, or was at any time during the tax year in which the claim is made or treated as made, entitled to a tax credit; [^key-c1ab975f5805e358a164966406bfd213]...
- (c)[^key-c1ab975f5805e358a164966406bfd213] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where this regulation applies the Tax Credits Act 2002 applies in relation to the claimant with the following modifications.

(3) In section 28 —

- (a) In subsection (1)—
  - (i) after “tax year” in both places where it occurs, insert “or part tax year”,
  - (ii)[^key-4bc5b6567fed4dc7d20e377fb060ef12] in paragraph (b), for the words from “as if it were” to the end substitute “as an overpayment of universal credit”;
- (b)[^key-6e4924e2b45f602ac4179380d3eb3d06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) omit subsection (5);
- (d) in subsection (6) omit “(apart from subsection(5))”.

(4)[^key-de19a3afe183116586b4de26b8c05a6b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In section 48 after the definition of “overpayment” insert—

> - “**part tax year**” means a period of less than a year beginning with 6th April and ending with the date on which the award of tax credit terminated,

.

(6) In paragraph 6(1)(a) and (c) and (2)(a) of Schedule 2 after “for the tax year” insert “or part tax year”.
