## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-5ee09f4a1a42d0c454a2e59006649175]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Universal Credit (Transitional Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation immediately after the coming into operation of the Universal Credit Regulations.
