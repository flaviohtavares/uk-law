# The Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Transfer of certain functions of Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

The functions which immediately before the transfer date are exercisable by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development under or for the purposes of the statutory provisions set out in Schedule 2 are transferred as from that date to the Department for Infrastructure.
