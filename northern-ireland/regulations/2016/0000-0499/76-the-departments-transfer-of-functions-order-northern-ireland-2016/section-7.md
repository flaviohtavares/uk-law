# The Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Transfer of certain functions of Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment to Department for Communities

The functions which immediately before the transfer date are exercisable by the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment under or for the purposes of section 4 of the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00006] are transferred as from that date to the Department for Communities.
