# The Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Order laid before the Assembly under Article 8(4) of the Departments (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, and subject to affirmative resolution procedure of the Assembly

2016 No. 76

Northern Ireland Departments

The Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

25th February 2016

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 8 of the Departments (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 [^f00001], make the following Order.
