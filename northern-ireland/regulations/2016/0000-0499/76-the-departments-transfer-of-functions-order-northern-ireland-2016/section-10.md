# The Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Transfer of assets and liabilities

All assets and liabilities enjoyed or incurred by the transferor immediately before the transfer date in connection with any functions transferred by this Order shall on that date be transferred to, and by virtue of this Order vest in, the transferee.
