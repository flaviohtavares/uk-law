# The Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 1

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation on the day on which Article 6 of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (universal credit) comes into force for all purposes in respect of the whole or any part of Northern Ireland.
