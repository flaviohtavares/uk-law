## 78. Definition of Couple

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part “**couple**” means—

- (a) joint claimants, or
- (b) a single claimant who is a member of a couple within the meaning of Article 45 of the Order and the other member of that couple,

and references to a couple include each member of that couple individually.
