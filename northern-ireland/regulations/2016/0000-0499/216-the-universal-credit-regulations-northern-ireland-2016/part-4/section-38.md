## 38. Amounts of elements[^c-28629befd7fd6a9ce8089444b2a43544][^c-b89fc6f74f9e9b8329c64d71dfc5ed4f][^c-cbcef267d12877d5d7caf256eab54371][^c-aa1f16b257c658928147f2592e922ac2][^c-a285c068a781ce71185d83d190370f78]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amounts of the standard allowance, the child element, the LCWRA element[^c-b9aa45c1c7fc5cc4b2814ff8b0d664a0] and the carer element (which are all fixed amounts) and the maximum amounts of the childcare costs element are given in the following table.

(2) The amount of the housing costs element is dealt with in regulation 27**.**

(3) In the case of an award where the claimant is a member of a couple, but claims as a single person, the amounts are those shown in the table for a single claimant.
