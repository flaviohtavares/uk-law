## 28. Award to include LCWRA element[^c-1fa68ec8dda675eeef4fce79ed980b7a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-ecedd3fc80ea3df7443259d45f507cb1] An award of universal credit is to include an amount in respect of the fact that a claimant has limited capability for work and work-related activity (“the LCWRA element”).

(2) The amount of that element is[^c-e1a18658607520fcca57d44f91e823cc] given in the table in regulation 38 by reference to whether the claimant is—[^c-088b6f8c8df4076c31ad652459065818]

- (a) a pre-2026 claimant,
- (b) a severe conditions criteria claimant,
- (c) terminally ill, or
- (d) any other claimant.

(3)[^c-b07c09b1c4b75f3771baaa8094ab9718][^c-14a176a6a2ed53e080a461d0b557fcee][^c-ae2dbc62be9295b3e5c344d70d5d39d5][^c-5217a9918d5cd224a2639b6debaec6f9][^c-24d92e5be3757496daa9e63f32779cf6][^c-dd0b09dddf71b0d62fa9b59e54ca3aa9][^c-dce50cdf58c54888ac1f12a5e4606d22][^c-f82c54fa16a9d66c73492e945e9555dd][^c-d33daa6dfe5111b008a245c96337fda1] Whether a claimant has limited capability for [^c-fa3e912636f58db62937d0927aaf8298]... work and work-related activity or is a severe conditions criteria claimant[^c-fc0287c7cbecf0966d75a2d581db91db] is determined in accordance with Part 5.

(4)[^c-771fd59e132d78e4659b1273901c6816] In the case of joint claimants, where each of them has limited capability for work and work-related activity,

- (a)[^c-5d012bf30366ef2bb319b9095a4633f6] the award is only to include one LCWRA element , and[^c-08b945704814900feb5a4a17c910959c]
- (b) where the LCWRA element for each of them is a different amount, the LCWRA element to be included is the higher amount.
