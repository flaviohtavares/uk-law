# The Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 32

## 32. Award to include childcare costs element

*Extent: N.I.*

An award of universal credit is to include an amount in respect of childcare costs (“the childcare costs element”) in respect of an assessment period in which the claimant meets both—

- (a) the work condition (see regulation 33), and
- (b) the childcare costs condition (see regulation 34).
