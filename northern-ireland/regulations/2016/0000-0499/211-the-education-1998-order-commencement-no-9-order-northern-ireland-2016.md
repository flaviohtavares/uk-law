# The Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No.9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 211 (C. 15)

Education

The Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No.9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

14th April 2016

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No.9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

## 2. Appointed Day

Article 69 of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 shall come into operation on 14<sup>th</sup> April 2016.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 14<sup>th</sup> April 2016

Trevor Connolly

A senior officer of the

Department of Education

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation Article 69 of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, which provides for payment by the Department of capital and special purposes grants to grant-maintained integrated schools.
