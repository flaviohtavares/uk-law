# The Planning (Local Development Plan) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 27

Planning

The Planning (Local Development Plan) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

*29th January 2016*

Coming into operation

*29th February 2016*

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 247(6) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Local Development Plan) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 29th February 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the Planning (Local Development Plan) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

In paragraph 1 of the Schedule to the Planning (Local Development Plan) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002]—

- (a) for “the Planning (Northern Ireland) 1991 Order” substitute “the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00003] or the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991”; and
- (b) for “that Order” substitute “the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th January 2016

Angus Kerr

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the transitional provisions in the Planning (Local Development Plan) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 to include development plans prepared and adopted by the Department under the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 within the definition of a departmental development plan under the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.
