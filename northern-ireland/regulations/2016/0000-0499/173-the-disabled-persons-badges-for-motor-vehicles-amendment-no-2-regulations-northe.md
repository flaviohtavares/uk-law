# The Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 173

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

16th March 2016

Coming into operation-

31st March 2016

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 31st March 2016.

## 2. Amendment

The Disabled Persons (Badge for Motor Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00004] is amended by the substitution of the words “1st April 2017” for the words “1st April 2016” where they appear in Regulation 3 and in the Schedule to those Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 16th March 2016

(L.S.)

G F McKenna

A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 by changing the date when a new form of badge comes into effect from 1st April 2016 to 1st April 2017.
