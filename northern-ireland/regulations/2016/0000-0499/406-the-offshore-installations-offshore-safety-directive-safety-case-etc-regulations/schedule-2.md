# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — PARTICULARS TO BE ADDRESSED IN A CORPORATE MAJOR ACCIDENT PREVENTION POLICY

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 7(3)

**1.**[^key-883380d69f30642db754caba4f545f2c] The responsibility of officers of the duty holder for ensuring, on a continuous basis, that the corporate major accident prevention policy is suitable, implemented, and operating as intended.

**2.**[^key-71d53a0c91f6f18e0a2e1a670f6c2a29] Formal command and control systems that include officers and senior management of the duty holder.

**3.**[^key-2eda5ca749f4e154ac893ac942f3ec88] Measures for building and maintaining a strong safety culture with a high likelihood of continuous safe operation.

**4.**[^key-33e40b6e8920d9f70c88bba8b7c69e61] The approach to competency at all levels of the duty holder’s organisation.

**5.**[^key-46f7003476a6ebcfc1eabbe705aa5a5f] Measures for rewarding and recognising desired behaviours.

**6.**[^key-d806d1836ff89a2014ca8fc3336275f5] The evaluation of the duty holder’s capabilities and goals.

**7.**[^key-f340e1ffe713d0ccb42441d2c0c15d4d] The extent and intensity of process auditing.

**8.**[^key-f60c49380549b78de836e2433da0f84a] Measures for maintenance of safety and environmental protection standards as an organisational core value.

**9.**[^key-f0c867ea289a2d2f72925133360ca2c1] The extent to which the particulars in paragraphs 1 to 8 are applied in the duty holder’s offshore oil and gas operations conducted outside the European Union.
