# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2016 No. 406

Health And Safety

Environmental Protection

The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

22nd November 2016

Coming into operation

19th December 2016

The Department for the Economy[^f00001], being the Department concerned[^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 (“the 1972 Act”)[^f00003], Articles 17(1) to (6)[^f00004] and 55(2) of, and paragraphs 1(1) and (2), 7, 8, 13, 14 and 15 of Schedule 3 to the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00005] (“the 1978 Order”) and Article 3(2) of the Offshore, and Pipelines, Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00006].

The Department was designated[^f00007] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the 1972 Act in relation to measures relating to employers’ obligations in respect of the health and safety of workers[^f00008] and the environment[^f00009].

The Regulations give effect without modifications to proposals submitted to it by the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland under Article 13(1A)[^f00010] of the 1978 Order after the Executive had carried out consultations in accordance with Article 46(3)[^f00011].
