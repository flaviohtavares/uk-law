# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 — MATTERS TO BE PROVIDED FOR IN A VERIFICATION SCHEME AND A WELL EXAMINATION SCHEME

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations 10(1) and 12(1)

### PART 1 — MATTERS TO BE PROVIDED FOR IN A VERIFICATION SCHEME

**1.**[^key-364516c115a9e649acd8d5abb0c965dd] The principles to be applied by the duty holder—

- (a) in selecting a verifier to perform functions under the scheme; and
- (b) in keeping the scheme under review.

**2.**[^key-71799f1bcb8a9a2eec7d0f7c8314d644] The arrangements for the communication to the verifier of information necessary for the proper implementation, or revision, of the scheme.

**3.**[^key-ca5e9c30bb962669f7fca1b041ddf923] The nature and frequency of examination and testing.

**4.**[^key-0de6c51481ffe5ab0e24806e1b53c562] The arrangements for the making and preservation of records showing—

- (a) the examination and testing carried out;
- (b) the findings of the examination and testing;
- (c) any remedial action recommended; and
- (d) the remedial action performed.

**5.**[^key-b018f786266f977bf69e0cc1c4e4213a] The arrangements for communicating the matters specified in paragraph 4 to an appropriate level in the management system of the duty holder for the installation.

**6.**[^key-b03c3fc0a1f81c97d48efab4f9698a0b] The arrangements for review and revision of the scheme.

### PART 2 — MATTERS TO BE PROVIDED FOR IN A WELL EXAMINATION SCHEME

**7.**[^key-9ccf68107f96d7fea5b8a9e4c427bf76] The principles to be applied by the well operator—

- (a) in selecting a well examiner to perform functions under the scheme; and
- (b) in keeping the scheme under review.

**8.**[^key-da8b7ec6e141df0da03a49d1b4219d21] The arrangements for the communication to the well examiner of information necessary for the proper implementation of the scheme.

**9.**[^key-d2895049a5318ad0465561d711b304cd] The nature and frequency of examination.

**10.**[^key-fe7b97dacbba9939ba321c763f8eadee] The arrangements for the making and preservation of records showing—

- (a) the examination carried out;
- (b) the findings of the examination;
- (c) any remedial action recommended; and
- (d) the remedial action performed.

**11.**[^key-e7c270d33c5c94dab7f49407505fa9ac] The arrangements for communicating the matters specified in paragraph 4 to an appropriate level in the management system of the well operator.

**12.**[^key-02c78b170534e44a9293ed6b09de1bcc] The arrangements for review of the scheme.
