# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 35

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Exemptions[^c-7eb0314457073beffbd5b4fb56c4b3a5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), the competent authority may, by a certificate in writing, exempt any person, installation or well, or class of persons, installations or wells, from any requirement or prohibition imposed by these Regulations.

(2) Any such exemption may be granted subject to conditions and with or without limit of time and may be revoked by a certificate in writing at any time.

(3) The competent authority shall not grant any such exemption unless, having regard to the circumstances of the case, and in particular to—

- (a) the conditions, if any, which it proposes to attach to the exemption; and
- (b) any other requirements imposed by or under any enactments which apply to the case,

it is satisfied that the health and safety of persons who are likely to be affected by the exemption will not be prejudiced in consequence of it, and that the exemption will be compatible with Article 3(2) of [Directive 92/91/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0091) and with [Directive 2013/30/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0030).
