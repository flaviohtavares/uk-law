# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 33

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Notification of major accident etc.[^c-ff8c7f55acfa90f50353dca630b377ea]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The operator, well operator or, if appropriate, the owner shall notify the competent authority without delay of—

- (a) a major accident; or
- (b) a situation where there is an immediate risk of a major accident.

(2) The notification shall describe the circumstances, including, where possible, the origin, the potential impacts on the environment and the potential major consequences.
