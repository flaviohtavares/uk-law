# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — PARTICULARS TO BE ADDRESSED IN A SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 8(4)

**1.**[^key-17ee46793941863f346765cea15daae0] Organisational structure and personnel roles and responsibilities.

**2.**[^key-741de2b162e87bd08f2240fd86a47721] Identification and evaluation of major hazards as well as their likelihood and potential consequences.

**3.**[^key-d16e3f2b0001aaf989fc982c0da464e3] Integration of environmental impact into major accident risk assessments in the safety case.

**4.**[^key-7263ffe8f3ee11507426455087169fd7] Controls of the major hazards during normal operations.

**5.**[^key-248e5802bbf8f016ff50dc04348063e9] Emergency planning and response.

**6.**[^key-3e6ae4d922543097c3e79f4374ad6f9b] Limitation of damage to the environment.

**7.**[^key-a3b3a55d4052905da9f57f52dd656069] Management of change.

**8.**[^key-f5e180ad59558393cda3310206200675] Monitoring of performance.

**9.**[^key-535e92292d265a541afe91b3f90a2c13] Audit and review arrangements.

**10.**[^key-6070c87ea5f03934ad3ebe55c55492bd] The measures in place for participating in tripartite consultations and how actions resulting from those consultations are put into effect.
