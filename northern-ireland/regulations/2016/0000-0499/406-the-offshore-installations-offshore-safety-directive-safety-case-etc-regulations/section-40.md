# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 40

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Penalties[^c-b5c3dabf5c9091295fb1c4cb490667f3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The maximum penalty for an offence consisting of a contravention of a requirement or prohibition imposed by or under these Regulations is—

- (a) on summary conviction, imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both; and
- (b) on conviction on indictment, imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or a fine or both.
