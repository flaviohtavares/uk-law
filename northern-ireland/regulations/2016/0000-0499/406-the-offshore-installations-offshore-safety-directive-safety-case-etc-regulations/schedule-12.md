# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, SCHEDULE 12

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 12 — MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED IN PREPARING AND REVISING STANDARDS AND GUIDANCE ON BEST PRACTICE IN RELATION TO THE CONTROL OF MAJOR HAZARDS

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 32(4)

**1.**[^key-32f12d2a54cd969434af80302b4177b9] Effective risk management.

**2.**[^key-c6f4117448953a8bf253d4e793d046db] Management and supervision of major hazard operations.

**3.**[^key-adf55a5e0b4f29642ff6ca95f7731d16] Competency of key post holders.

**4.**[^key-92026ecb093aaa4a0945dcb2d96db92c] Reliable decision making.

**5.**[^key-186ebfc29c50849e7ab65d0e9c9652d4] Effectively integrating safety and environmental management systems between operators and owners and other entities involved in oil and gas operations.

**6.**[^key-f5e235401072c17736356c1631255bd6] Key performance indicators.

**7.**[^key-c3e38db851ebc2acc65569d7db60ff44] Improving well integrity, well control equipment and barriers and monitoring their effectiveness.

**8.**[^key-49007b590504e6f2de6cd974ce27ad05] Improving primary containment.

**9.**[^key-eb98d278c33acbeff2d980b36a0d4d9c] Improving secondary containment that restricts escalation of an incipient major accident, including well blow-outs.

**10.**[^key-33e40b42ee51867ad30cf66907a76c1f] Reliability assessment for safety and environmental-critical systems.
