# The Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 14

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Persons not eligible for discretionary support unless arising as a consequence of a disaster

*Extent: N.I.*

Discretionary support may not be awarded except when arising as a consequence of a disaster to—

- (a) a person who is (or treated as) in full time third level education;
- (b) a person subject to a trade dispute; or
- (c) a person under a sanction as applied by the Department,

where that person would otherwise be ineligible for discretionary support.
