# The Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 13

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Persons excluded from discretionary support

*Extent: N.I.*

Discretionary support may not be awarded in respect of a person who is—

- (a) a prisoner or a person who is lawfully detained or is on temporary release under rule 27 of the Prisons and Young Offenders Centres Rules (Northern Ireland) 1995 [^c22847681];
- (b) a resident in a residential care home, a nursing home or a hospital in-patient, unless it is expected that the person will be discharged within the following 2 weeks;
- (c) a member of a religious order who is fully maintained by that order;
- (d) listed in regulation 14 (a) to (c) unless regulation 14 applies.
