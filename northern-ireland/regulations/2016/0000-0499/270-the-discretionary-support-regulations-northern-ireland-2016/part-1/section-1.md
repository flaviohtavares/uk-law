> Text as at 2025-03-20.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on the commencement of Article 76 of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015.
