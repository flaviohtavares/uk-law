> Text as at 2025-03-20.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Evidence and information in connection with a claim

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall require the claimant to supply such further information or evidence in connection with the claim, or any questions arising out of it, as it considers appropriate.
