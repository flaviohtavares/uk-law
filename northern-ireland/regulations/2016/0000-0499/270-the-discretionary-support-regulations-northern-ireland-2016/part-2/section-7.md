# The Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 7

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Person unable to act

*Extent: N.I.*

If a claim for discretionary support is made by or on behalf of a person (“P”), who is unable for the time being to act, the claim shall be made by—

- (a) a person who has been appointed to act on P's behalf by the Department under regulation 33(1) of the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987 [^c22847641]; or
- (b) a person who has been appointed, with a general power or a power to claim or receive benefit, by P under the Powers of Attorney Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 [^c22847651], the Enduring Powers of Attorney (Northern Ireland) Order 1987 [^c22847661] or otherwise.
