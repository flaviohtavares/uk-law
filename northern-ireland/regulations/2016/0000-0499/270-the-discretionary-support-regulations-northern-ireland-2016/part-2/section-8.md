# The Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 8

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Decision to award a loan or a grant

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a claimant applies for a discretionary support award under regulation 4 a discretionary support officer may determine the claim as a claim for a loan or a grant.

(2) In making a decision in relation to a discretionary support award a discretionary support officer shall take into account any guidance issued by the Department.
