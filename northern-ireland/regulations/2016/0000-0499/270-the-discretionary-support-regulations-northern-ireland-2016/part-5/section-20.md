# The Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 20

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Capital jointly held

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the claimant and one or more persons are beneficially entitled in possession to any capital asset they shall be treated as if each of them were entitled in possession to the whole beneficial interest therein in an equal share.
