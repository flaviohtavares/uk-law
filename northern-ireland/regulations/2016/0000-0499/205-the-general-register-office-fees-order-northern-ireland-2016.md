# The General Register Office (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

> Text as at 2021-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2016 No. 205

Registration Of Vital Events

The General Register Office (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made

22nd March 2016

Coming into operation

4th April 2016

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 47(1) and (2) of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 [^c22823481], Articles 3(3)(b), 19(1)(a), 35(3), 36(1)(b) and (3) and 37 of the Marriage (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 [^c22823491], and Section 157(1) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 [^c22823501].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This order may be cited as the General Register Office (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 4th April 2016.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^c22823511] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Fees payable

The fee payable under a statutory provision specified in column 1 of the Schedule in respect of the matter specified in relation to it in column 2 shall be the fee specified in relation to it in column 3.

## 3. Revocation

The General Register Office (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014(**e**) is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 22nd March 2016.

T Power

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Order provides for the various fees payable under the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 relating to searches in registers, viewing records, and the provision of certificates. It also provides for fees in relation to change of name services, the provision of commemorative certificates, the individual notification of registration events and entries in the Record of Northern Ireland Connection. It also provides for the various fees payable under the Marriage (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 and the Civil Partnership Act 2004 relating to giving notice for intended marriage or civil partnership, the solemnisation of marriage and registration of civil partnership, searching and viewing marriage and civil partnership records and providing marriage and civil partnership certificates.

This Order revokes and replaces the General Register Office (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014
