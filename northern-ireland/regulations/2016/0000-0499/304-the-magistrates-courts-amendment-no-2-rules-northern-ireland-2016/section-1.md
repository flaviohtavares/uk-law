# The Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on the same day as section 1 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002].

**2.** In these Rules—

- (a) “the principal Rules” means the Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984[^f00003]; and
- (b) a reference to a rule, paragraph, Schedule or Form is a reference to that rule, paragraph, Schedule or Form so numbered in the principal Rules.
