# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**2.** [^c23912031][^c23912041][^c23912051][^c23912061]The Directive repeals and replaces Council Directive [93/15/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0015) on the harmonisation of the provisions relating to the placing on the market and supervision of explosives for civil uses  (the repealed Directive) as amended by Regulation [(EC) No 1882/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1882) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29th September 2003 , Regulation [(EC) No 219/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0219) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11th March 2009 and Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25th October 2012 .
