# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 21

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Duty to act with due care

*Extent: N.I.*

When making a civil explosive available on the market, a distributor must act with due care to ensure that it is in conformity with this Part.
