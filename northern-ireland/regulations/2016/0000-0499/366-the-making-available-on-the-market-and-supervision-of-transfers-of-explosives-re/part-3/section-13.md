# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Prohibition on placing on the market civil explosives which are not in conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

An importer must not place a civil explosive on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential safety requirements.
