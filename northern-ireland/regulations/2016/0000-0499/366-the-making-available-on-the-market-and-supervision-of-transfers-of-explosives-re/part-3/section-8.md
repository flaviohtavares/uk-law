# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a civil explosive for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the civil explosive is placed on the market.
