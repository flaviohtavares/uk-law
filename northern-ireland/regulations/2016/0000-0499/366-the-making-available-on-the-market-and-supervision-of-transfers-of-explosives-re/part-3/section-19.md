# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Duty to take action in respect of civil explosives placed on the market which are considered not to be in conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer or importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that a civil explosive which they have placed on the market is not in conformity with this Part must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the civil explosive into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the civil explosive; or
- (c) recall the civil explosive.

(2) Where the civil explosive presents a risk, the manufacturer or importer must immediately inform the competent national authorities of any EEA State in which the manufacturer or importer made the civil explosive available on the market, of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the civil explosive is considered not to be in conformity with this Part; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
