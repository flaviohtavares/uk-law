# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 26. Storage and transport

*Extent: N.I.*

Each importer and distributor must ensure that, while a civil explosive is under their responsibility, its storage or transport conditions do not jeopardise its conformity with the essential safety requirements.
