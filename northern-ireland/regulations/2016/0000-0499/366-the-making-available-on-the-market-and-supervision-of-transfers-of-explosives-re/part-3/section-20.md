# The Making Available on the Market and Supervision of Transfers of Explosives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Provision of information and cooperation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer or importer must, further to a reasoned request from a competent national authority, provide the authority with the information and documentation necessary to demonstrate that the civil explosive is in conformity with this Part—

- (a) in paper or electronic form; and
- (b) in a language which can be easily understood by the authority.

(2) A manufacturer or importer must, at the request of a competent national authority, cooperate with the authority on any action taken to—

- (a) evaluate a civil explosive in accordance with paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 (Evaluation of civil explosives presenting a risk);
- (b) eliminate the risks posed by a civil explosive which the manufacturer or importer has placed on the market.
