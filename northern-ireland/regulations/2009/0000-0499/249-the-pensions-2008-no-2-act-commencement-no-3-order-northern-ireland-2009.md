# The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 249 (C. 16)

Pensions

The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

25th June 2009

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(1) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the [Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/249).

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The following provisions of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 shall come into operation on 29th June 2009—

- (a) section 103 (amendments of provisions of the 2005 Order relating to contribution notices or financial support directions) in so far as it is not already in operation, and
- (b) in Schedule 8 (contribution notices and financial support directions under 2005 Order)—
  - (i) paragraph 4, and
  - (ii) paragraphs 1, 2, 8 and 15 in so far as they are not already in operation.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 25th June 2009

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the following provisions of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, relating to contribution notices and financial support directions, on 29th June 2009—

- section 103 in so far as it is not already in operation;
- paragraph 4 of Schedule 8, and
- paragraphs 1, 2, 8 and 15 of that Schedule in so far as they are not already in operation.
