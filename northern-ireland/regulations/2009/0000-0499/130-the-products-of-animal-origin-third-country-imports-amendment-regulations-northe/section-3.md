# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendment of the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

(1) The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1), in the definition of “product” in sub-paragraph (b)(iv), for the word “quality” substitute the word “quantity”.

(3) In paragraph (1)(c) of regulation 5 (enforcement authorities, exchange of information and powers to give directions), for the word “area” substitute the word “district”.

(4) In paragraph (1)(b) of regulation 7 (exercise of enforcement powers), for “to regulation 60” substitute “regulation 61”.

(5) In paragraph (a)(i) of regulation 64 (contraventions), for “8(2)” substitute “8(3)”.

(6) For Schedule 1 substitute the Schedule to these Regulations.
