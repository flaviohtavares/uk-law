# Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 375 (C. 27)

Rates

Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

20th November 2009

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009.

(2) In this Order “the 2009 Act” means the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) 23rd November 2009 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2009 Act listed in Schedule 1.

(2) 14th December 2009 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2009 Act listed in Schedule 2.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 20th November 2009

(L.S.)

Brian McClure

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel
