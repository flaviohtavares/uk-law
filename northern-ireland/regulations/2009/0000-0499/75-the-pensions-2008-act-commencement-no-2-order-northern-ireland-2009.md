# The Pensions (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 75 (C. 4)

Pensions

The Pensions (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

2nd March 2009

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(1) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 12 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (conversion of guaranteed minimum pensions)—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 3rd March 2009, and
- (b) for all other purposes, is 6th April 2009.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 2nd March 2009

(L.S.)

John O’Neill

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of section 12 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 on 3rd March 2009 for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations and 6th April 2009 for all other purposes.

Section 12 introduces new provisions into the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act [1993 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49) to allow guaranteed minimum pensions to be converted into other pension benefits.
