# The Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 16

> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**16.** In Great Britain the corresponding Regulations are the Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/716](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/716)). The Great Britain Health and Safety Executive (GB HSE) has prepared a regulatory impact assessment in respect of those Regulations and a copy of that assessment, together with a Northern Ireland supplement prepared by the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland, is held at the offices of that Executive at 83 Ladas Drive, Belfast BT6 9FR from where copies may be obtained. A copy of the transposition note in relation to the implementation of the Directives set out in paragraphs 2 and 3 is also available at that office.
