# The Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 2

> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**2.** These Regulations, as respects Northern Ireland—

- (a) provide for the enforcement of Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures (O.J. No. L353, 31.12.08, p.1) (“the CLP Regulation”), in addition to the enforcement of these Regulations;
- (b) implement parts of Directive [2006/121/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0121) (O.J. No. L396, 30.12.06, p.850) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 on the approximation of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances in order to adapt it to Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1907) (O.J. No. L142, 31.5.08, p.1) concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) and establishing a European Chemicals Agency;
- (c) implement Council Directive [1992/32/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0032) (O.J. No. L154, 5.6.92, p.1) amending for the 7th time Council Directive [67/548/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1967/0548) (O.J. No. L196, 16.8.67, p.1), in so far as its provisions relate to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances (“the substances Directive”); and
- (d) implement Council Directive [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) (O.J. No. L200, 30.7.99, p.1) on the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous preparations (“the preparations Directive”).
