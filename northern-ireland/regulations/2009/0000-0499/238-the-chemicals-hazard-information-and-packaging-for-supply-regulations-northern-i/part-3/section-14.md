# The Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 14

> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Enforcement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] To the extent that they would not otherwise do so, Articles— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

- (a) 18 to 30 (approval of codes of practice; enforcement; indemnification of inspectors; power to obtain information and restrictions on disclosure of information); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
- (b) 31 to 39 (provisions as to offences); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
- (c) 43(2) (civil liability), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] 1978 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] shall apply to these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] as if these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] were health and safety Regulations for the purposes of that Order, except that those Articles shall not apply to duties placed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] on the competent authority or the Member State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

(1A)[^key-d25d61eae22d28ec5b2fdab70a9003e5] The maximum penalty for an offence under this regulation is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

- (a) on summary conviction, imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or a fine or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

(2) Any function of the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland under any other provision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] 1978 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] under or in respect of health and safety Regulations (including their enforcement) shall be exercisable as if these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] were health and safety Regulations for the purposes of that Order to the extent that they would not otherwise do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

(3) Notwithstanding regulation 4 of the Health and Safety (Enforcing Authority) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071][^f00048] and subject to paragraphs (4) and (5), the enforcing authority for these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071].

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), where a substance or preparation is supplied, or a substance, mixture or article falling within the meaning of and the provisions of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] is placed on the market within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] in or from premises which are registered under section 75 of the Medicines Act 1968 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071][^f00049], the enforcing authority shall be the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

(5) The enforcing authority for these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] shall be the district council for the area in which are situated the premises in or from which such substance or preparation is supplied or such a substance, mixture or article is placed on the market— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]

- (a) where a substance or preparation is supplied or a substance, mixture or article falling within the meaning of and the provisions of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] is placed on the market within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] other than in the circumstances referred to in paragraph (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
  - (i) in or from any shop, mobile vehicle, market stall or other retail outlet, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] otherwise to members of the public, including by way of free sample, prize or mail order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
- (b) for regulation 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
- (c) for Articles 35(2) and 48 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071].

(6) In every case where, by virtue of this regulation and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071], these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] are enforced by the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety or by a district council, they shall be enforced as if they were safety regulations made under section 11 of the Consumer Protection Act 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071][^f00050] and the provisions of section 12 of that Act shall apply to these Regulations and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] CLP Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071] as if they were safety regulations for the purposes of that Act and as if the maximum period of imprisonment on summary conviction specified in subsection (5) thereof were 3 months instead of 6 months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aaac38b2e4d293e45f9caeda1c869071]
