# The Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 3

> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**3.** These Regulations also implement the Directives referred to below which adapt to technical progress and modify the substances Directive and the preparations Directive. These Directives are—

- (a) Commission Directive [91/410/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0410) (O.J. No. L228, 17.8.91, p.67) 14th adaptation to technical progress of the substances Directive;
- (b) Commission Directive [93/21/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0021) (O.J. No. L110, 4.5.93, p.20) 18th adaptation to technical progress of the substances Directive;
- (c) Commission Directive [2000/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0032) (O.J. No. L136, 8.6.2000, p.1) 26th adaptation to technical progress of the substances Directive;
- (d) Commission Directive [2001/59/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0059) (O.J. No. L225, 21.8.2001, p.1) 28th adaptation to technical progress of the substances Directive;
- (e) Commission Directive [2004/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0073) (O.J. No. L152, 30.4.04, p.1) 29th adaptation to technical progress of the substances Directive;
- (f) Commission Directive [2001/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0060) (O.J. No. L226, 22.8.2001, p.5) 1st adaptation to technical progress of the preparations Directive; and
- (g) Commission Directive [2006/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0008) (O.J. No. L19, 24.1.2006 p.12) amending, for the purposes of their adaptation to technical progress, Annexes II, III and V to the preparations Directive.
