# The Chemicals (Hazard Information and Packaging for Supply) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 17

> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**17.** A person who contravenes the Regulations is guilty of an offence under Article 31 of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 and is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or a fine not exceeding £20,000, or both; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or a fine, or both.
