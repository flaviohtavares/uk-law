# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 144

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 144. Applications for support[^key-c0ccd310042de998725704249929eafd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (the “applicant”) must apply for a grant under this Part in connection with each academic year of a designated postgraduate course by completing and submitting to the Department an application in such form and accompanied by such documentation as the Department may require.

(2) The application must reach the Department as soon as is reasonably practicable.

(3) The Department may take such steps and make such inquiries as the Department considers necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible postgraduate student, whether the applicant qualifies for a grant and the amount of grant payable, if any.

(4)[^key-d183ebf037afe66fb52f27969717a25d] The Department must notify the applicant of whether the applicant qualifies for support and, if the applicant does qualify, the amount of support payable in respect of the academic year, if any.
