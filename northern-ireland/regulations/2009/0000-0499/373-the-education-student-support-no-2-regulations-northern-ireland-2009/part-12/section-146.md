# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 146

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 146. Amount of grant[^c-e7bf425594606587a0b073780e5250da]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the grant under this Part is such amount as the Department considers appropriate to assist with one or more types of eligible expenditure.

(2)[^c-ee5fb2b57b88192db4dbdee42818c6f2] The amount of grant under this regulation in respect of an academic year—

- (a) for expenditure other than travel expenditure, must not exceed £27,500[^c-8b035a67124e74326844ae60ea564194];
- (b) for travel expenditure, must not exceed the additional expenditure incurred.

(3) For the purposes of this Part, the “types of eligible expenditure” are—

- (a) expenditure on a non-medical helper;
- (b) expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; and
- (c)[^c-cf992ebf888e4590b007441b889cae14] travel expenditure.

(4)[^c-a1043dccda5b568c246ad06f523987db] Subject to paragraph (5), the grant under this Part is payable in respect of the four quarters of the academic year[^c-447bf36829cebff5185fa7585faee185]....

(5) Where one of the events listed in regulation 140[^c-0c3edd23e51565498535dd44cda3f7f8]... occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a grant under this Part i in respect of—[^c-0181c05c734b64f145e7951b6e0c5a57]

- (a) the quarter in which the relevant event occurs, and
- (b) if relevant, such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs.
