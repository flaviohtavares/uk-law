# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Requirement to enter into a contract for a loan[^c-b60ba2ed23a163396542865ef4d70f00]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) To receive a loan a student must enter into a contract with the Department.

(2) Where the Department requires a contract to be signed by a student, an electronic signature in such form as the Department may specify satisfies the requirement.
