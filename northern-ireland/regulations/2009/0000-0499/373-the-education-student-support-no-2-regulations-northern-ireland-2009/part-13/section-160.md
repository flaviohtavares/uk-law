# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 160

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 160. Requirement to enter into a contract for a Master’s, etc. fee loan[^key-b18dabf49e521af0022cc55757f7307f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) To receive a Master’s, etc. fee loan a student must enter into a contact with the Department.

(2) Where the Department requires a contract to be signed by a student, an electronic signature in such form as the Department may specify satisfies such a requirement.
