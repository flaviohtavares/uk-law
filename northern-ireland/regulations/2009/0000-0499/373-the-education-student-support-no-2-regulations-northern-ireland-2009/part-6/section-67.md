# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 67

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Type 1 and type 2 teacher training students[^c-6b175eb673dc0d546ed8716dc2e738be]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a current system student with full entitlement who is a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil.

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is —

- (a) for a student in category A, £6,471[^c-64be6e3cb1434924bcc2e3be04af42a8];
- (b) for a student in category B, £11,699[^c-3fd2217e1ceb2681c98fe7c4633f1849];
- (c) for a student in category C, £9,956[^c-bc76fafd0b488876d443ebac5d16761c];
- (d) for a student in category D, £8,352[^c-be72662b71ef22496cec4cb4caee295f].

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £5,850[^c-184aad604165aba752e85eec9b4a22cd];
- (b) for a student in category B, £10,646[^c-26bf25410e6d4d156a8bbec6f57b1a18];
- (c) for a student in category C, £8,654[^c-60af30c19253df0fb6ba0e7e4645cdd4];
- (d) for a student in category D, £7,731[^c-eaa3d173b38bed71f4654328463613c9].
