# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 66. Current system students with full entitlement[^c-dcabf73e0d4b3dfe01414a60b9264cd7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a current system student with full entitlement (other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student where his contribution exceeds nil).

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—

- *X* is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £6,471[^c-9138ba2390f56862ea326ad261a98a0e];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £11,699[^c-6002a62ef21f040879e5d894b77c34e9];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £9,956[^c-48bc4531054ab2fc073734778f6081d1];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £8,352[^c-9fcdc390dac1df5013bd1137c4fc67ed];
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount.

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—

- *X* is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £5,850[^c-4134c41fda09d22a56950bc7f902299f];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £10,646[^c-9c597bb9e1d012f0e3a6076366b4f3d9];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £8,654[^c-fa12ae98755cf9472620ea9b7248be05];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £7,731[^c-81f2bc3c3bfcbe399ab5d3bc90e9314a];
- *Y* is the maintenance grant amount.

(4)[^c-6993a8c8ee513a2d39f088a54b9cbde7] “the maintenance grant amount” is, unless otherwise stated—

- (i) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant not exceeding £1,354, the amount of maintenance grant payable;
- (ii) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(2) for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,354 but less than £1,785, £1,700 less £1 in every complete £9.60 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (iii) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(2) for an amount of maintenance grant of £1,785, £1,700;
- (iv) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(3) for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,354 but less than £3,569, £2,685 less £1 in every complete £7.38 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (v) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(4) for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,354 but less than £1,785, £1,575 less £1 in every complete £10.94 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (vi) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(4) for an amount of maintenance grant of £1,785, £1,575;
- (vii) where the student qualifies under regulation 58(5) for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,354 but less than £3,569, £1,901 less £1 in every complete £17.95 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (viii) where the student qualifies under Chapter 6 of Part 5 for an amount of maintenance grant of £3,569, £1,901; and
- (ix) where no maintenance grant is payable, nil.
