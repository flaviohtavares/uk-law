# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 69

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 69. Students with reduced entitlement[^key-d14d18e7852e2544a707b15b3720dff3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year of a course other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 39(3)(a)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,072[^key-23a5f04861463ad0bfbe4ed495719615];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £5,747[^key-21f70c065f374f326fd2177a4770bdb7];
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £4,090[^key-53f583ee20e4041a7ad15a5fa53e8722].
- (b) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 39(3)(b) or (5)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £3,072[^key-7ed208e46f82619431b9b3c1bf6a1d57]
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £5,747[^key-fb8968b6df73a13a680d13e467335ab9];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £4,892[^key-d5191416b3387d8cebf5d30b38c1275d];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £4,090[^key-01332e885da7cb90d7ea8957aecdbd6a].
- (c) where a current system student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—
  - *X* is—
    - (i) for a student in category A, £4,853[^key-987c2834d68cbe8bf00a6069f6d447d4];
    - (ii) for a student in category B, £8,774[^key-ebcbcff5c3843c74f2f9c773fbdcbcdf];
    - (iii) for a student in category C, £7,468[^key-3cbfb443564c874a2a1c05ec67bab53d];
    - (iv) for a student in category D, £6,264[^key-c16395244bc68a047cf5b60a2b1c0906];
  - *Y* is the amount specified in paragraph (3).
- (d)[^key-2bc72a5bf8279b85f428ec1c0b867143] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 39(3)(a)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £2,330[^key-0a6153da1e39666158f182371bea2fb4];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £4,400[^key-9f98624e541054256e72b7a1370f7941];
  - (iii) for a student in category C or D, £3,192[^key-ca2a4c5d4ecaf72ec0ea20360d2dc746];
- (b) where the student does not qualify for a grant for living and other costs in relation to the academic year because of regulation 39(3)(b) or (5)—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £2,330[^key-9a9e63335229be1676b5d304e4eaf0b8];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £4,400[^key-1567d680b7c8e495ab1c8850dce34e2d];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £3,572[^key-ec4cc40f0a089e370a597d9ed951e4b0];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £3,192[^key-d645fe025f2fc6845f48dcf299e419f8];
- (c) where a current system student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—
  - *X* is—
    - (i) for a student in category A, £4,388[^key-3741305622bd90da5a6d0b453e7a13e5];
    - (ii) for a student in category B, £7,985[^key-ae0a1151052f9d847eac688ce0d660ef];
    - (iii) for a student in category C, £6,491[^key-f3614b69f1c000d2e062ab987375bf2c];
    - (iv) for a student in category D, £5,798[^key-15c48da1ea9f9aa7b81adcb633358c84];
  - *Y* is the amount specified in paragraph (3).
- (d)[^key-581f9f31b6d663b648588c464cf0e652] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The specified amount is—

- (a) £678[^key-45022434e2730df8e33ac50fee4bf7ee] where the student is a type 1 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £678;
- (b) £1,354[^key-38ecbc98891c85a5a522314b225a5a37] where the student is a type 2 teacher training student who opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income when applying for a maintenance grant and who qualifies for a maintenance grant of £1,354;
- (c) nil where the student is not a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student.
