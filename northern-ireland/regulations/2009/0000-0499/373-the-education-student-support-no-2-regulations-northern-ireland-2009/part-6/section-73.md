# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 73

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Students residing with parents[^key-3f65eee41ab4a2d160de29de07f0b3e8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an eligible student (“X”) resides at X’s parents’ home and the Department is satisfied that in all the circumstances X’s parents by reason of age, incapacity or otherwise cannot reasonably be expected to support X and that it would be appropriate for the amount of loan for living costs or long courses loan payable to a student in a category other than category A to apply in X’s case, X must be treated as if X were not residing at X’s parents’ home for the purposes of this Part.
