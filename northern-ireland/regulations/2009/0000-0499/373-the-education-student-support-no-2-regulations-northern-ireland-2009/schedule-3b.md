## SCHEDULE 3B — DESIGNATED MASTER’S, ETC. COURSES[^c-3a5767039cbe22997f9622adab579d3a]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 152

**1.** A Master’s degree.

**2.** A Postgraduate Diploma.

**3.** A Postgraduate Certificate.

**4.** A course recognised by the Department for the purposes of converting a Postgraduate Certificate into a Postgraduate Diploma or a Master’s degree.

**5.** A course recognised by the Department for the purposes of converting a Postgraduate Diploma into a Master’s degree.
