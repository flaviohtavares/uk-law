## 52. Amount of the grant for travel – courses in medicine and dentistry[^c-cdf9700e486ab3f12c95d2017a584086]

*Extent: N.I.*

The amount of grant payable under regulation 51 in respect of an academic year is equal to the reasonable expenditure that the Department determines the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purposes set out in that regulation less £309.
