# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 60

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Amount of the special support grant[^key-0323d54f74fdb5a3418fc4b78b015f17]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The maximum amount of special support grant available in respect of an academic year is—

- (a) in the case of a type 1 or type 3 teacher training student, £1,785[^key-7e65a981bb6d100e6d2dd07659f6d5ef];
- (b) in the case of a type 2 teacher training student, £3,569[^key-30c469982d93e133719d0914f915579d];
- (c) in the case of a current system student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student, £3,569[^key-1b1e54322a579f9dc2d81324e9373253].

(2) A type 1 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £1,785[^key-688e14fd192ef6b74710c8a3b7bbe02a];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *M* is £1,785[^key-d04b41e451ce6723a2968c8c3bb2d7b5]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £8.86 by which the household income exceeds £19,203; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £678[^key-9346e94dbdf0dca13d7a8906271bed76].

(3) A type 2 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £3,569[^key-13c0b2a5229c37022d7a739151fbe2e9];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *M* is £3,569[^key-f1a6614c761f362899d0fa696d94f58d]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.43 by which the household income exceeds £19,203; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £1,354[^key-b039874e54cae337fd9d3a9e01c631ac].

(4) A type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £1,785[^key-95de67c47b82fe661fb850ea37d1d459];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £41,065, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *M* is £1,785[^key-6b16d26e0185a1d0b3619ef72bf4f76c]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £8.86 by which the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019
  - *B* is £1 for every complete £18.50 by which the household income exceeds £29,019 but does not exceed £41,065; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £41,065, no special support grant is payable.

(5) A current system student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £3,569[^key-c08c361e4d78718e7efd4651595629d0];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *M* is £3,569[^key-a1d60999f9471fca8caa0123d69dfba9]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.43 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 but does not exceed £41,065, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *RM* is £1,354[^key-fd408eb79ccde9244c095dbd07725caf]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £9.25 by which the household income exceeds £29,019; and
- (d) where the household income exceeds £41,065, no special support grant is payable.
