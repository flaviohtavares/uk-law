# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 40

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Students who are treated as in attendance[^key-d260e6a3b09bc4660f6cd9dbad3ad27d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as being in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for the following grants—

- (a) grant for dependants;
- (b) disabled students’ allowance;
- (c) maintenance grant or special support grant .[^key-4d86462cef8e1bdf6d9a2a98ba4982be]
- (d)[^key-b2584ec1673273bbc1b2f0b2d5527383] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a compressed degree student;
- (aa)[^key-6d95be52777d67fdb6747f625a4d977a] a student on a period of study or period of overseas work placement in an Erasmus year; or
- (b) a disabled student who—
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and
  - (ii) is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom or at a relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland but is not in attendance because the student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the student’s disability.
