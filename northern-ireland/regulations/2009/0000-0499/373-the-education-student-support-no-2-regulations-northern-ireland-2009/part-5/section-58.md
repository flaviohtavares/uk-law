# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 58

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 58. Amount of the maintenance grant[^key-f2b8265df7b4a7b50ba7ef0d6a557d63]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The maximum amount of maintenance grant available in respect of an academic year is—

- (a) in the case of a type 1 or type 3 teacher training student, £1,785[^key-897164422fed04f78991c749d264dc5b];
- (b) in the case of a type 2 teacher training student, £3,569[^key-8edbd9cae7cc77077a68a30160f01bcd]; and
- (c) in the case of a current system student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student, £3,569[^key-eaf9094ca5247a1c1b2fae1ba923ee09].

(2) A type 1 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £1,785[^key-4d430561d56cc1eadf240827e6e34ee4];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019 the student receives an amount equal to[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)

  where

  - *M* is £1,785[^key-9f5cee6acf1f7079528b88fc83438b58]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £8.86 by which the household income exceeds £19,203; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £678[^key-cc3bf7778fa8bd9c99f4f23d81296b0f].

(3) A type 2 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year —

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £3,569[^key-84038c3bb649d40b1db4e16c1fc51792];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373) where—
  - *M* is £3,569[^key-43268570d0ddde419339995b0f82f33e]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.43 by which the household income exceeds £19,203; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the student receives £1,354[^key-fd022e371ed2f6ed99822e74288ba5ae].

(4) A type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £1,785[^key-06fe8eda0581853280897c6c1b83b8ce];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £41,065, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—
  - *M* is £1,785[^key-7c8d0b4ddff0f810c7699c83252f7d83]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £8.86 by which the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019
  - *B* is £1 for every complete £18.50 by which the household income exceeds £29,019 but does not exceed £41,065; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £41,065, no maintenance grant is payable.

(5) A current system student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £19,203 or less, the student receives £3,569[^key-38e78adb905f95ab7c4dbd38bd9c2300];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £19,203 but does not exceed £29,019, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—
  - *M* is £3,569[^key-6d55f78e6d570ad556065b7a3e5d6fe5]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £4.43 by which the household income exceeds £19,203;
- (c) where the household income exceeds £29,019 but does not exceed £41,065, the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/373)where—
  - *RM* is £1,354[^key-699fc267f5f828bae6f270113ecbf852]
  - *A* is £1 for every complete £9.25 by which the household income exceeds £29,019;
- (d) where the household income exceeds £41,065, no maintenance grant is payable.
