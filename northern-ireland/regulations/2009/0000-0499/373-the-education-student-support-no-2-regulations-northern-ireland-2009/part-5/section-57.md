# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 57

## 57. Qualifying conditions for the maintenance grant[^c-6ae7d8d9e3e6f1f6b0364db607e0a0d7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A current system student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a maintenance grant in connection with the student’s attendance on a designated course.

(2) A current system student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if the student qualifies for a special support grant.

(3) If a current system student does not qualify for a fee loan or relevant institution charge loan[^c-8fce01dd252eb9df56c71f7334c7dd35] in respect of an academic year of the designated course, the student cannot qualify for a maintenance grant for that year unless the reason that the student does not qualify for a fee loan or relevant institution charge loan is that—

- (a) the year is an Erasmus year in accordance with regulation 20(3)[^c-67fa87fb0b27aa9de9cd56f53126644e]; or
- (b) the designated course is an old flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers.

(4)[^c-57d9177eb8dbad0c0f1695186a3c4040] Subject to paragraph (5), a maintenance grant is payable to a current system student[^c-7619387ce6e410bb386fa75823fab490] in respect of the four quarters of the academic year.

(5)[^c-e4ed814fdc46a02be389df101bbd0149] Where a protected category event which results in a student becoming an eligible student occurs—

- (a) in the course of the first academic year of a course; and
- (b) on or before the course start date,

the student may qualify for a maintenance grant in respect of the four quarters of that academic year.

(5A) Where—

- (a) a course designation event which results in student’s course becoming a designated course occurs in the course of an academic year; or
- (b) an in-year qualifying event (other than a settled status event) which results in a student becoming an eligible student occurs in the course of an academic year,

the student may qualify for a maintenance grant in respect of such quarters of the academic year as begin after the event in question occurs.

(7)[^c-117d2a5015ea3c3a7ab15db0b5e53375] A current system student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if the current course is a graduate entry course[^c-9fb7cb1d27de7dca6a17e2be2adfa941].
