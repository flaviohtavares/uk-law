## 4. Revocation, savings and transitional provisions[^c-5ce49b9347c59c0adca0702386b1bd5c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the 2009 Regulations are revoked on 1st September 2010.

(2) Paragraph (3) of regulation 4 of the 2009 Regulations continues to apply.

(3) The 2009 Regulations continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1st September 2009 but before 1st September 2010.
