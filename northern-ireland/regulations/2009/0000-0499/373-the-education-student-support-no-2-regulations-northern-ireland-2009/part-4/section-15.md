# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 15

> Text as at 2026-07-24.

## 15. Current system students[^c-7d457928aba6bf45bdeeb26bee5d1748]

*Extent: N.I.*

A current system student (“A”) qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the fees or relevant institution charge loan in respect of student contribution charge[^c-02bd97b37fb82785da845e696d858660] payable by A in connection with A’s attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 3 of this Part.
