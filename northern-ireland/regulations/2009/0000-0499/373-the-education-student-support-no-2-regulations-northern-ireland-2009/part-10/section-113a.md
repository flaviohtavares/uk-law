# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 113A

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 113A. Requirement to enter into a contract for a distance learning fee loan[^key-14187a78575bb445ee8725a769116353]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) To receive a distance learning fee loan, a student must enter into a contract with the Department.

(2) Where the Department requires a contract to be signed by a student, an electronic signature in such form as the Department may specify satisfies such a requirement.
