# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 131A

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 131A. Requirement to enter into a contract for a part-time fee loan[^c-e37cc6ad845450257d84ce8d6e318ea7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) To receive a part-time fee loan, a student must enter into a contract with the Department.

(2) Where the Department requires a contract to be signed by a student, an electronic signature in such form as the Department may specify satisfies such a requirement.
