# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 130

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 130. Disabled part-time students’ allowance[^c-fa957b79b95858a01b648eed6c86827d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this Part for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Department is satisfied the student is obliged to incur by reason of a disability to which the student is subject in respect of undertaking a designated part-time course.

(1A)[^c-ee3e13e6f081deabf83b4d05c838c603] The expenditure for which the grant may be paid includes, in particular—

- (a) expenditure on a non-medical personal helper;
- (b) expenditure on major items of specialist equipment;
- (c) travel expenditure.

(2) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for the grant under this regulation if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 2 into which the student falls is paragraph 2A, 9, 9A, 9C or 9D,[^c-165239583a61a6b593c1336d73e157af] .

(3) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for the grant under this regulation unless the Department considers that the student is undertaking the designated part-time course in the United Kingdom.

(3A)[^c-d423605e7520e2b07aecf61e93da2027] Paragraph (3) does not apply to a person who is treated as being ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom by virtue of paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 2 on the basis of temporary employment falling within paragraph 1(5)(a) of that Schedule.

(3B)[^c-ad26805def86394fb876be74cf3aa38d][^c-6f0641945a8ac7a5e13a5cfc54012ea2] An eligible part-time student does not qualify for a grant under this regulation unless it is ordinarily possible to complete the designated part-time course in no more than four times the period ordinarily required to complete the full-time equivalent.

(4) Subject to the following paragraphs, the amount of grant under this regulation is the amount that the Department considers appropriate in accordance with the student’s circumstances.

(5)[^c-67a2bc15b4ed188cc38ec3996a729bc5] The amount of grant under this regulation in respect of an academic year—

- (a) for expenditure other than travel expenditure, must not exceed £27,500[^c-11985bc0ae115b62731d1ec3542aef0b];
- (b) for travel expenditure, must not exceed the additional expenditure incurred.

(6)[^c-96768ffd91a48eed26e92d9102edbf52] Subject to paragraph (7), the grant under this regulation is payable in respect of the four quarters of the academic year[^c-bb05f554ff8dc8bc1fcc4162f93b0dc2]....

(7) Where one of the events listed in regulation[^c-1cb14d6b9f3e495f06165d4afc92649c] 123(4)[^c-c35c4194fc3227f0a74434dc0f0cd000][^c-2301ec31131b2dd807b93f494e300e97] occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for the grant under this regulation in respect of—[^c-c766d4d989987ecbe8b5391f7caf797f]

- (a) the quarter in which the relevant event occurs, and
- (b) if relevant, such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs.

(8)[^c-eff43d022d9f0c8f7b48b22b91f09ebd] For the purposes of paragraph (3A) “full-time equivalent” and “period ordinarily required to complete the full time equivalent” is to be interpreted in accordance with regulation 124(6).
