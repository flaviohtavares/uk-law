>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. General[^key-0e23a7b72a9b5c2a93c434638a935213]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) —

- (a)[^key-4931d6950108228aff38be057ad6540e] fee loans and relevant institution charge loans are payable in accordance with regulation 99;
- (ab)[^key-adbf412ea0a692ac87bc667e549269ae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-1343989931acabe4bf49a3a07bfc3ddd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) loans for living costs and long courses loans are payable in accordance with regulation 101;

(2) Regulations 97 and 98 apply in respect of the payment of all loans.
