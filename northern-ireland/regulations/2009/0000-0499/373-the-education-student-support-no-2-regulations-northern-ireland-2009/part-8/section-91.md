# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 91

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Minimum level of loan for living costs[^key-4b53e4dde134566e11aa467dc3614a0d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (5), for a current system student, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 90(d) is—

- (a) £4,853[^key-c97f2be7d54f8e2ca892179a3c4035a0], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £8,774[^key-c4005b1b0c0a013d7c547b81a5abd8f6], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £7,468[^key-64d29d82ca9b3b6a95e94af09c4bf07a], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £6,264[^key-ede66b74899a261fcf167fdb37a5b3bf], in the case of a student in category D.

(2) Subject to paragraph (5), for a current system student where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 90(d) is—

- (a) £4,388[^key-51ac173e1ce4c92e92e9632eb12e686c], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £7,985[^key-fb27f005178a7c296e56c4528006c925], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £6,491[^key-bd3d275135a92d19bddf640e36152528], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £5,798[^key-50b3d27901ebdeb958e114f01e71aa4c], in the case of a student in category D.

(3)[^key-8e1c9ff082a5109d2c7b09b52e6b2490] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-f12bf28466a4474cce16c50e2836443a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Where different categories apply for different quarters of the academic year, the minimum levels in paragraphs (1) or (2)[^key-3e5ca7241229c4980892fe36e6a11489] are the aggregate of the amounts determined under paragraph (6) for each of the quarters in respect of which a loan is payable.

(6) The amount determined for each quarter is one third of the amount in paragraph (1) or (2)[^key-92a5d167891a2eafba18b2d2ee10a0a7] which corresponds to the rate applicable for the quarter.

(7) The rate applicable for a quarter is determined in accordance with regulation 72.

(8) Categories A to D have the meaning given in regulation 78.
