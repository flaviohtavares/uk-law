# The Education (Student Support) ( No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, SCHEDULE 3A

> Text as at 2026-07-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3A — DESIGNATED POSTGRADUATE COURSES (FOR THE PURPOSES OF DISABLED POSTGRADUATE STUDENTS’ ALLOWANCE PAYABLE UNDER PART 12)[^key-52f72160479bf16b91c2f3c3308f073d]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 141(1)(b)

**1.** A Postgraduate Certificate

**2.** A Postgraduate Diploma

**3.** A Master’s degree

**4.** A Doctoral degree

**5.** A course recognised by the Department for the purposes of converting a Postgraduate Certificate into either a Postgraduate Diploma, a Master’s degree or a Doctoral degree.

**6.** A course recognised by the Department for the purposes of converting a Postgraduate Diploma into a Master’s degree or a Doctoral degree.

**7.** A course recognised by the Department for the purposes of converting a Master’s degree into a Doctoral degree.
