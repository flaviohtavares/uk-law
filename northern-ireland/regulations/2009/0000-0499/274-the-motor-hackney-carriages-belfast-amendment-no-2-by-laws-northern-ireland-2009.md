# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 274

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

23rd July 2009

Coming into operation

17th August 2009

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 17<sup>th</sup> August 2009.

## 2. By-Laws amended

In the Schedule to the By-Laws made in respect of Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951[^f00002], after the entry for Brunswick Street insert—

> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | CHICHESTER STREET | North Side: from a point 38 metres west of the west kerbline of Victoria Street for a distance of 53 metres in a westerly direction. Carriages shall stand in a single line by the kerbline and parallel therewith facing east. | 10 | At any time |

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 23<sup>rd</sup> July 2009.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

These By-Laws further amend the Schedule to the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951 by making provision for a new taxi stand on the north side of Chichester Street.
