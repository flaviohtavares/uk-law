# Superannuation (Health and Social Care Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 150

PENSIONS

Superannuation (Health and Social Care Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

3rd April 2009

Coming into operation

24th April 2009

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Health and Social Care Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 24th April 2009.

## 2. Additions to list of employments

There shall be added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st April 2009, employment in—

- (a) the Regional Health and Social Care Board[^f00003];
- (b) the Regional Agency for Public Health and Social Well-being[^f00004]; and
- (c) the Regional Business Services Organisation[^f00005].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 3rd April 2009.

Grace Nesbitt

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds employment in the Regional Health and Social Care Board, the Regional Agency for Public Health and Well-being and the Regional Business Services Organisation to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes. It has retrospective effect by virtue of Article 3(7) of that Order.
