# The Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children))(Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 311

Magistrates’ Courts

The Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children))(Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

3rd September 2009

Coming into operation

30th September 2009

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chancellor and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 30th September 2009.

## 2. Amendment of the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999

After Rule 18 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] insert—

> 18A. A summons to enforce the attendance of a parent or guardian in accordance with Article 15 of the Order may be served in the same manner as a summons to be served under Rule 12A of the Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984[^f00003].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999 \[[S.R.1999 No. 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/7)\] to provide that service of a summons to enforce the attendance of a parent or guardian under Article 15 of the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 may be effected by post.
