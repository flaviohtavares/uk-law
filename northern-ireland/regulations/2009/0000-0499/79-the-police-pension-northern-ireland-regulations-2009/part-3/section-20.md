# THE POLICE PENSION (NORTHERN IRELAND) REGULATIONS 2009, section 20

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Pensionable pay[^c-8999df6e70f0f31bcc281e7390d25388]

*Extent: N.I.*

The pensionable pay of a police officer at any time means his pay at the rate to which he is or was then entitled, account being taken of any retrospective increase in that rate; except that during a period of part-time service it means so much of his pay at that rate as is attributable to his determined hours specified under his part-time appointment, and during any period in which a member of the police service is a specified[^c-62b4ad0ed57f4f0aedd9b960665c6772] NCA officer [^c-5a0befe211f3d306082650355357fca3]..., it means the pay at the rate to which he is or was then entitled under this contract of employment, account being taken of any retrospective increase in that rate.
