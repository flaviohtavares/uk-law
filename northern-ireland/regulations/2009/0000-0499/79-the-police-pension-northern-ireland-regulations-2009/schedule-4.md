## SCHEDULE 4 — PROGRESSIVE MEDICAL CONDITIONS[^c-1bb05a051897bfb8db9f6a3b3bed56a9]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 48(14)

[^c-1bb05a051897bfb8db9f6a3b3bed56a9]

- The progressive medical conditions mentioned in regulation 48(14) are:
- AIDS;
- Alzheimer’s disease;
- Cancer;
- Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Huntington’s chorea;
- Motor neurone disease;
- Multiple sclerosis;
- Nieman Pick disease;
- Non-variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Parkinson’s disease; and
- Variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease.
