## 62. Eligibility for awards - general[^c-273088e01ab119ce6700cc773233ae0c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the following provisions of this Part, the question whether a person is entitled to any, and if so what, awards under these Regulations shall be determined in the first instance by the Board.
