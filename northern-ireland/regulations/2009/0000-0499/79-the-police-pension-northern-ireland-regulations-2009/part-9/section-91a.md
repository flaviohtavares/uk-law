# THE POLICE PENSION (NORTHERN IRELAND) REGULATIONS 2009, section 91A

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91A. Members of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve.[^c-f512ab2bc6700a64979d1f52678d4bde]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations shall apply to members of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve subject to the modifications in Schedule 4A.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, in these Regulations a reference to—

- “police officer” shall be read as a reference to a part-time reserve officer,”,
- “police service” shall be read as a reference to the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve.
