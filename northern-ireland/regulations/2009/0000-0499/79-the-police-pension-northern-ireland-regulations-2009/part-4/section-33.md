## 33. Pension debit members - personal awards[^c-e6d55fcbe03363f0dba9b9265bd6baf5]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a pension debit member is entitled to an award under regulation 24, 26, 29 or 31—

- (a) the award shall be calculated by reference to the member’s rights under these Regulations as reduced by virtue of article 28 of the 1999 Order and in accordance with such tables and other guidance as are provided for the purpose by the Scheme actuary, and
- (b) regulations 34 and 35 have effect accordingly.
