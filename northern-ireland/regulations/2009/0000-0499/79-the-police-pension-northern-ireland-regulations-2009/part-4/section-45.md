## 45. Pension debit members – awards on death[^c-c33299cd362686cef72ef279a9ec4443]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a pension debit member dies any award in respect of him under regulation 41, 42 or 43 is calculated by reference to the member’s rights under these Regulations as reduced by virtue of article 28 of the 1999 Order and in accordance with such tables and other guidance as are provided for the purpose by the Scheme actuary.
