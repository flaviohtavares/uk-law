# THE POLICE PENSION (NORTHERN IRELAND) REGULATIONS 2009, section 72

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Interpretation - transfers[^c-53c1e07ee7e11afc1e77c1500f9ee80d]

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- (a) a reference to a police officer transferring to a police force in Great Britain shall be construed as a reference to his leaving the police service and joining a police force in Great Britain as a regular police officer with—
  - (i) in the case of the Chief Constable, Deputy Chief Constable or an Assistant Chief Constable, the consent of the Board and the approval of the Secretary of State; and
  - (ii) in any other case, the consent of the Chief Constable and after having given a month’s notice in writing to the Board, or such shorter period of notice as may have been accepted by the Board.
- (aa)[^c-12ce1974a4029087783e28e84a323f2c] a reference to a police officer transferring to a police force in Great Britain shall, in the case of a specified[^c-74f37c34d367ea7a15a83d5101a57bd3] NCA officer, be construed as his leaving NCA[^c-1bde3b429384155103405ebea09f7b16] for the purpose of becoming a regular policeman in Great Britain and becoming such a regular policeman, where he leaves NCA—
  - (i) after giving notice of such period as required by his contract of employment of his intention to do so to NCA[^c-bf2779f9c05954add283dda7233e8705], or
  - (ii) after giving such shorter period of notice as may be agreed between the specified[^c-20c18639392d47ba3182482e57375db8] NCA officer and NCA[^c-5e22a416c041ab887f7fb93a81b015de].
- (ab)[^c-b5386be6991dcf397247b5d18f818f9a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a reference to a regular police officer having transferred from a police force in Great Britain to the police service shall be construed as a reference to his leaving a police force in Great Britain and joining the police service as a police trainee or police officer, subject to—
  - (i) in the case of a Chief Officer of police, Deputy Chief Constable, or an Assistant Chief Constable, obtaining the consent of the police authority concerned; and
  - (ii) in any other case, obtaining the consent of the Chief Officer of police acting with the consent of the police authority concerned.
- (c)[^c-10cd90b4b86009c52a6057ae0d152968] A reference to a regular police officer having transferred from a police force in Great Britain shall, in the case of a specified[^c-47d1fd0a981ff3b6a7b5d11d01ed0043] NCA officer, be construed as his leaving NCA[^c-4c70922e82bdf4f8bc6f428da3829a73] for the purpose of becoming a police officer and his becoming a police officer, where he leaves NCA—
  - (i) after giving notice of such period as required by his contract of employment of his intention to do so to NCA[^c-31328d7437dabd030e8c8f3ec71b2dc4], or
  - (ii) after giving such shorter period of notice as may be agreed between the specified[^c-678b236a723375b8b11585f9b1e27236] NCA officer and NCA[^c-1996c52adc71ec0a11794f5268b8e5aa].
- (d)[^c-63abf08fb75c839e35ccf5cbcf3d3227] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
