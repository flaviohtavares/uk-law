> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Board responsibility for payment of awards[^c-0cf91dcebeaae1ee9cd9e301834ef098]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of his having served as a police officer shall be payable by the Board.

(2) An award which is payable to or in respect of a pension credit member under Part 6, and any sum paid in commutation of such an award, is payable by the Board.
