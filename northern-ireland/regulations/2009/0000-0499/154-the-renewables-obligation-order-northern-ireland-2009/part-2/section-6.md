# The Renewables Obligation Order (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 6

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Part of calculation A referable to Great Britain[^c-9096f64ab0317f7478e769eb0fb2d78f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The part of calculation A referable to Great Britain is the estimate of megawatt hours of electricity likely to be supplied to customers in Great Britain during a particular obligation period, as estimated by the Secretary of State under Article 6(1) of the Renewables Obligation Order 2009[^f00019] (Part of calculation A referable to Great Britain), multiplied by the figure which corresponds to that particular period in the second column of Schedule 1.
