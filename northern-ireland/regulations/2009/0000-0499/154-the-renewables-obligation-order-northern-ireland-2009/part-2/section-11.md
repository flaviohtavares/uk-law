> Text as at 2026-03-24.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Determining the number of renewables obligation certificates to be produced in an obligation period[^c-ab6a4ce0c4b85936e5434c94c762cc15]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Following the determination of calculations A and B[^c-accb25dace8366b91bc57f185adf49c1] for an obligation period, the Department is to determine the total number of renewables obligation certificates required to be produced by designated suppliers (“the total obligation”) for that period in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (5).

(2) Where calculation A is equal to or[^M_F_3fd1e66d-d67a-4b87-be4d-919ae8b7b76a] greater than calculation B for an obligation period, the total obligation for that period is calculation A.

(3) [^M_F_7779e8f0-0ee3-4d3d-934e-a4d1a4da8090]... Where calculation B is greater than calculation A for an obligation period, the total obligation for that period is calculation B.
