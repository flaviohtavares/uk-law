# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 288

Sports Grounds

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

29th July 2009

Coming into operation

31st December 2009

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 4 (1) and 27(1) and (2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 [^f00001], makes the following Order after consultation with persons or bodies as appeared to it appropriate:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 31st December 2009.

## 2. Designation of sports grounds

The sports grounds specified in the Schedule (being sports grounds which in the opinion of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure have accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) are hereby designated as sports grounds requiring a safety certificate.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 29th July 2009

M Cory

A senior officer of the

Department
