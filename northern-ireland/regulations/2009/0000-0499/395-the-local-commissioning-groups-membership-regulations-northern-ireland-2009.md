## 2. Membership of Local Commissioning Groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(1) The prescribed number of persons appointed to each Local Commissioning Group shall be 17. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(2) The persons appointed to each Local Commissioning Group shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

- (a) four general medical practitioners; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (b) four members of district councils; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (c) two representatives from voluntary organisations with an interest in health and social care; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (d) two social workers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (e) one dental practitioner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (f) one pharmacist; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (g) one nurse; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (h) one person who is registered as a public health medicine specialist on the Specialist Register maintained by the General Medical Council under article 13 of the General Specialist Medical Practice (Education, Training and Qualifications) Order 2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63][^f00012]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]
- (i) a chiropodist/podiatrist, dietician, occupational therapist, orthoptist, physiotherapist, radiographer or speech and language therapist registered under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63] Health Professions Order 2001[^key-db45b807193dcdcc11424850e96d48ba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63] .

(3) The persons appointed under paragraph (2)(a) and (e) must practise within the area of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(4) The persons appointed under paragraph (2)(b) must be members of district councils within the area of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(5) The persons appointed under paragraph (2)(c) must be from voluntary organisations with an interest in health and social care within the area of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(6) The person appointed under paragraph (2)(d), (g), (h) and (i) must be employees of the Regional Board or Regional Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(7) The person appointed under paragraph (2)(f) must provide pharmaceutical services within the area of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

## 3. Term of office (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(1) The term of office of members of a Local Commissioning Group shall be 4 years or such other period as may be determined by the Regional Board at the time the appointments are made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(2) Subject to regulation 4, a member of a Local Commissioning Group specified in regulation 2(2)(a), (b), (c), (e) or (f) may, on the termination of the term of office, be eligible for re-appointment for such further period, not exceeding 4 years as the Regional Board may in any particular case determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(3) The Regional Board may instead of re-appointing a member of a Local Commissioning Group specified in regulation 2(2)(a), (b), (c), (e) or (f) on the expiration of his term of office, extend his appointment for such further period as may appear reasonable in the particular circumstances; but the period by which an appointment is extended must not exceed 4 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(4) A general medical practitioner or a dental practitioner who ceases to practise within the area of the Local Commissioning Group shall cease to be a member of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(5) A district councillor who ceases to be a member of a district council within the area of the Local Commissioning Group shall cease to be a member of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(6) A person who ceases to be a representative from a voluntary organisation with an interest in health and social care within the area of the Local Commissioning Group shall cease to be a member of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(7) A pharmacist who ceases to provide pharmaceutical services within the area of the Local Commissioning Group shall cease to be a member of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(8) A person appointed under paragraph (2)(d), (g), (h) or (i) who is suspended from employment with the Regional Board or Regional Agency shall be suspended from membership of the Local Commissioning Group while suspended from that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(9) A person appointed under paragraph (2)(d), (g), (h) or (i) who ceases to be an employee of the Regional Board or Regional Agency shall cease to be a member of the Local Commissioning Group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

(10) Where for any reason, the place of a member becomes vacant before the expiration of the member’s term of office whether by death, resignation or otherwise, the term of office of any person appointed in that person’s place shall be for the remainder of the term of office of the former member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4af13f8223293b6a9e282e9f1a2c5f63]

These regulations make provision concerning the membership of the Local Commissioning Groups established by the Regional Health and Social Care Board under the provisions of the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009. They prescribe that the Regional Board shall appoint 17 persons to each Local Commissioning Group (regulation 2(1)), specify the composition of members (regulation 2(2)) and prescribe that the general medical practitioner and general dental practitioner must practice within the area of the Local Commissioning Group, the district councillors must be members of councils within the area of the Local Commissioning Group, the voluntary representative members must be from voluntary organisations with an interest in health and social care within the area of the Local Commissioning Group, the pharmacist member must provide pharmaceutical services within the area of the Local Commissioning Group and that the nurse, social worker, public health medicine specialist and a chiropodist/podiatrist, dietician, occupational therapist, orthoptist, physiotherapist, radiographer or speech and language therapist registered under the Health Professions Order 2001 must be employees of the Regional Board or Regional Agency.
