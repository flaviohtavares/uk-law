# The Presumption of Death (2009 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 356 (C. 26)

Presumption Of Death

The Presumption of Death (2009 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

*3rd* November 2009

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(2) of the Presumption of Death Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Presumption of Death (2009 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009.

## 2. Appointed day

In so far as not already in operation, the Presumption of Death Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 shall come into operation on 9<sup>th</sup> November 2009.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 3rd November 2009

O G Paulin

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 9<sup>th</sup> November 2009 the provisions of the Presumption of Death Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 not already in operation.

The following provisions of the Act came into operation one month after the day (2 July 2009) on which the Act received Royal Assent: sections 8, 9(1), 11(5), 13, 16, 17, 18, 20 and 21.
