# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 99

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. Bank liquidator’s entitlement to remuneration where it is not fixed under rule 98[^c-426a11148484d5f5b2bd5351f446e547]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.134A of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for the words from “liquidator” to “his” substitute “bank liquidator’s”.

(3) In paragraph (2), after “Schedule 4” add “to the 1991 Rules as applied by the Schedule to the Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009”.
