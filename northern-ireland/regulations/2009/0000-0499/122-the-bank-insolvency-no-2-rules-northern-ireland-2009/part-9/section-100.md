# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 100

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 100. Bank liquidator’s remuneration where he realises assets on behalf of chargeholder[^c-72755527d2df7a21bcf723734170c2d7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.134B of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for the words from “liquidator” to “and” substitute “bank liquidator”.

(3) In paragraphs (2), (3) and (3), after “Schedule 4” add “to the 1991 Rules as applied by the Schedule to the Bank Insolvency (No.2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009”.
