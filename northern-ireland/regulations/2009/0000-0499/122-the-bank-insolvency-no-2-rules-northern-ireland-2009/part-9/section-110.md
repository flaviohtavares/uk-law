## 110. Notice to Bank of England of intention to vacate office[^c-d17ea64ac1aaa9da944ffe2abfe98c7c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where the bank liquidator was appointed by a meeting of creditors.

(2) Apply rule 4.144 of the 1991 Rules.

(3) In paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) for “official receiver” substitute “Bank of England”.

(4) In Paragraph (1) for “receive his resignation” substitute “be notified of his resignation”.
