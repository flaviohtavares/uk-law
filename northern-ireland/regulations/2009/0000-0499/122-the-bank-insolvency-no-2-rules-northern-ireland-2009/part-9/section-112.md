>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 112. Power of court to set aside certain transactions[^c-2372acd26126bd66025750cedd7e57fd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.157 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) delete “either”;
- (b) delete “or” at the end of sub-paragraph (a), and after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) it is shown to the court’s satisfaction that the transaction was entered into by the liquidator for the purpose of achieving Objective 1.

  .
