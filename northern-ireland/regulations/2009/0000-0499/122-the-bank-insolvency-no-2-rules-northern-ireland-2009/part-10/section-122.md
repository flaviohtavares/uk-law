## 122. Committee-members’ representatives[^key-5e51858e0c3b6dcb58799cc24ed63de8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.167 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2), the reference to Article 383 of the Companies Order, shall, after that Article is repealed, be to section 323 of the 2006 Act.[^f00018].
