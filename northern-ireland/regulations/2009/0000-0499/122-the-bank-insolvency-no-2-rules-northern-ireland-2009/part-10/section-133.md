## 133. Composition of committee when creditors paid in full[^key-bfa3616b53847103028df4e8c06b5598]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.179 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For paragraph (4) substitute—

> (4) The members of the liquidation committee will cease to be members at the end of the final meeting held under rule 96.
