## 26. Termination of appointment[^key-54ec2c1cf8b0a2fb255fc45d98bf4b9a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.034 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) After paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) On the making of a bank insolvency order the appointment of the provisional bank liquidator shall terminate.

.
