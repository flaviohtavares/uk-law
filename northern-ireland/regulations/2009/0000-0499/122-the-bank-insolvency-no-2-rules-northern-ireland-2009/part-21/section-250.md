# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 250

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 250. Notice of declaration[^key-817b9b94c0e29a16a1f06557e8ebc029]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 11.06 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) Delete paragraph (1)(b).

(3) In paragraph (2), after (b) add—

> - (ba) expenses incurred by the bank liquidator in the achievement of objective 1 under section 99 of the Banking Act 2009;

.
