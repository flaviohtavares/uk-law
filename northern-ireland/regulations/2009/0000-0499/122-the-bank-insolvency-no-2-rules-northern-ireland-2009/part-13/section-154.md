# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 154

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 154. Primacy of Objective 1[^c-def2e73c15bb434fba0a9b9e7018bfbc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the bank liquidator considers that the carrying out of a duty imposed by a rule in this Part would prejudice the achievement of Objective 1, the bank liquidator shall postpone the carrying out of that duty until he considers that the carrying out of the duty would no longer be likely to prejudice the achievement of that Objective.
