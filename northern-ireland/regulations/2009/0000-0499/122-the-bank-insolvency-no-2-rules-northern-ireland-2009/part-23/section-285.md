## 285. Notice to joint bank liquidators[^c-4a520ebfa49f83410eb9db759da0a84a]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the bank liquidator, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
