## 287. “Responsible insolvency practitioner”, etc[^key-59e350cf8b438fd18f350b8d7f11e908]

*Extent: N.I.*

In relation to a bank insolvency, “the responsible insolvency practitioner” means the person acting in the bank insolvency as bank liquidator or provisional bank liquidator.
