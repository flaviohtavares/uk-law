## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation[^key-1a11626f8607f4e50088d48dfec3f403]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009 and come into operation on 1st April 2009.

(2) The Bank Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00004] are revoked.
