## 4. Overview[^c-aadef7757fbf953e2b6203f1f10705e0]

*Extent: N.I.*

The purpose of these Rules is to provide a procedure for the appointment of a bank liquidator and the operation of bank insolvency under Part 2 of the 2009 Act.
