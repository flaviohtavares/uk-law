# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 280

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 280. Notice of order under Article 150A(5)[^key-93db9b78fe69d015d90ee7e4688b3960]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 12.23 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraphs (2), (3) and (5) for “the liquidator, administrator or receiver, as the case may be”, substitute “the bank liquidator”.

(3) In paragraph (6), for “The liquidator, administrator or receiver” substitute “The bank liquidator”.
