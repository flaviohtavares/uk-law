## 185. Second excepted case[^c-9572a041c2e186f39c329ab8552f0689]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.239 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (b), the reference to Article 180(3) of the Companies Order should, after that Article is repealed, be to section 451 of the 2006 Act.[^f00021].
