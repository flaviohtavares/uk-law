# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 182

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 182. Application for leave under Article 180(3) before passing of full payment resolution[^key-84e7ef8dc5deeb4b4727f11f13e3df91]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an application for leave under Article 180(3) is made before a full payment resolution has been passed, it may only be made with the consent of the bank liquidator.
