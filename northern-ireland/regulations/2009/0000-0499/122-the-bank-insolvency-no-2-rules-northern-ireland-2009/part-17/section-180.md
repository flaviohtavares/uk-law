## 180. Procedure following appeal under section 116 of the 2009 Act[^key-68541bbc274d80ac84adf33319066b99]

*Extent: N.I.*

Following an appeal under section 116(2) of the 2009 Act (against a decision of the Department under the applicable section) the court shall send two sealed copies of its order to the person in whose favour the appeal was determined and that party shall send one of the copies to the registrar of companies.
