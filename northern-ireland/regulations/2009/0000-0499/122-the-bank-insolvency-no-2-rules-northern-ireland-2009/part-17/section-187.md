## 187. Further exception[^c-bc79b55b98baea466dcdd2d0eabe6356]

*Extent: N.I.*

The court’s leave under Article 180(3) of the Companies Order is not required in respect of anything done by a person in connection with the exercise of a stabilisation power under Part 1 of the 2009 Act.
