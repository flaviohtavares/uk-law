# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 28

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Verification and filing[^key-705f6bb3b127a543276118a6d082a97a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 4.036 of the 1991 Rules. For “the official receiver” substitute “the bank liquidator”.

(2) For paragraph (6), substitute—

> (6) The bank liquidator shall file the statement of affairs in court and shall send a copy of it to the registrar of companies.

.

(3) Delete paragraph (7).
