## 228. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies[^c-7911764f5dfbed4ffd32e01ce37c67cd]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where in a bank insolvency, the court makes an order staying any action, execution or legal process against the property of the bank, service of the order may be effected by delivering a sealed copy by personal service to the applicant for the bank insolvency order.
