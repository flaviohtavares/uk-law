# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 193

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 193. Notice of application under Article 150A(5)[^key-07eb64b71370a8555a1ce1b4089a0397]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 7.08A[^f00023] of the 1991 Rules.

(2) Delete from “save that notice” to “State liquidator”.
