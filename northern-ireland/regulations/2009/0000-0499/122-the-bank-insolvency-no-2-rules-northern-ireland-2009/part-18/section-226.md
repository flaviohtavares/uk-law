# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 226

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 226. Principal court rules and practice to apply[^c-13405d9670b063448a8bad256294dcef]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Rules of the Supreme Court (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00024] and the practice and procedure of the High Court (including any practice direction) apply to bank insolvency proceedings in the High Court, with any necessary modifications, except so far as is inconsistent with these Rules.
