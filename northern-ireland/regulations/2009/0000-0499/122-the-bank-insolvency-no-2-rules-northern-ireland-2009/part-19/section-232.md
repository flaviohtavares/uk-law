# The Bank Insolvency (No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 232

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 232. Definition of “proxy”[^key-42b368e05223ae028f74792dec603a4b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Apply rule 8.1 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2) delete “, company”.

(3) In paragraph (4), for “chairman of the meeting” to “official receiver” substitute: “chair of the meeting or the bank liquidator”.
