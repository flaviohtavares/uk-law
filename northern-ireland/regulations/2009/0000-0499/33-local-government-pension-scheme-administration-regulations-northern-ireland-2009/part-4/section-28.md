# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 28

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Admission agreement funds (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7c1af675b2cee58b38e58f062e6a3427][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the Committee has made an admission agreement, it may establish a further pension fund (an “admission agreement fund”) in addition to the fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Immediately the Committee establishes an admission agreement fund, it must give the Department written notice that it has done so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The notice must specify the admission bodies whose employees are eligible for benefits from the admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Where an admission agreement fund is established— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the liabilities of the fund as respects membership in employment with those specified bodies become liabilities of the admission agreement fund; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) assets of such value as an actuary appointed by the Committee determines to be appropriate must be transferred from the fund to the admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) When valuations under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 31 (actuarial valuations and certificates) of both the fund and the admission agreement fund are first obtained after the admission agreement fund is established, the Committee must obtain a transfer statement from the actuary appointed by the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The transfer statement must specify whether, in the opinion of the actuary, there is a need for further assets to be transferred from the fund to the admission agreement fund and, if so, their value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Where the transfer statement specifies that assets of a specified value need to be transferred, the Committee must arrange for assets of that value to be transferred as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
