# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 27

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Governance policy statement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3cf210cb0b671b074faddbbf94f277d2][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee must, after consultation with such persons as it considers appropriate, prepare, maintain and publish a written statement setting out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) whether the Committee delegates its function or part of its function in relation to maintaining a pension fund to a sub-committee or an officer of the Committee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) if it delegates that function or part of that function to a sub-committee or an officer of the Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the frequency of any sub-committee meetings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the terms of reference, structure and operational procedures of the delegation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] whether the sub-committee includes representatives of employing authorities or members, and if so, whether those representatives have voting rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The first such statement must be published before 1st April 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The statement must be revised and published by the Committee following a material change in its policy on any of the matters referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
