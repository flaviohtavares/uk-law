# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 25

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Administration and management (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b0bf3e00207b59883cbc076c3202b79c][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations and the Benefits Regulations the Committee shall be constituted in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 3 (constitution of the Committee). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee shall have the powers specified in Part 2 of Schedule 3 (powers of the Committee). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The expenses and allowances payable by the Committee shall be in accordance with Part 3 of Schedule 3 (expenses and allowances payable by the Committee). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
