# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 35

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Employer’s payment following resolution to increase membership or award additional pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f57241875ded8399d3bb2805170c74cf][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) This regulation applies where an employing authority makes a resolution under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) regulation 12 of the Benefits Regulations (which confers power to increase the membership of an member by an additional period); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation 13 of those Regulations (which confers power to award additional pension). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Unless paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4) applies, the employing authority must pay the appropriate sum for the person to whom the resolution relates to the fund or admission agreement fund before the expiry of the relevant period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The appropriate sum for a person is such sum as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) This paragraph applies where the Committee and the employing authority agree before the expiry of the relevant period that the employing authority will pay increased contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 34 (contributing body’s contributions)[^key-0807d0d0c9e54581b58d3d32e2c453f3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or an amount to meet the cost of the increase in membership or the additional pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Any extra charge on the fund or admission agreement fund resulting from the resolution must be repaid to the fund by the employing authority concerned but only so far as not paid under paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4).

(6) In the case of a resolution under regulation 12 of the Benefits Regulations, the additional period in question may only be counted as a period of membership if one of the conditions in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (8) is met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) In the case of a resolution under regulation 13 of those Regulations, a person is only entitled to the additional pension awarded if one of the conditions in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (8) is met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) The conditions are that either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the employing authority makes the payment required by paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) within the relevant period; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) The relevant period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the period of one month beginning with the date of the resolution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) such longer period as the employing authority and the Committee agree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(10) If neither of the conditions in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (8) is met, the resolution ceases to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
