# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 30

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Funding strategy statement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e79e625f84f784e8e6007a27317050ca][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee shall, after consultation with such persons as it considers appropriate, prepare, maintain and publish a written statement setting out its funding strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) In preparing and maintaining the statement Committee shall have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the guidance set out in the document published in March 2004 by Chartered Institute of Public Finance and Accountancy (“CIPFA”) and called “CIPFA Pensions Panel guidance on Preparing and Maintaining a Funding Strategy Statement (Guidance note issue No. 6)”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the statement of investment principles published by the Committee under regulation 10 (statement of investment principles) of the Local Government Pension Scheme (Management and Investment of Funds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00021].

(3) The first such statement shall be published before 1st April 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The statement shall be revised and published by the Committee following, and in accordance with, any— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) material change in its policy on the matters set out in the statement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) material change to the statement of investment principles under regulation 10(4) of the Local Government Pension Scheme (Management and Investment of Funds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
