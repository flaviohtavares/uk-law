# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 32

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Supply of copies of valuations, certificates, etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b3133d51ce99dc7b74645276660d896][^key-c79cee2bcbb412658e102557977f6c01][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) The Committee must send copies of any valuation, report, certificate or revised certificate obtained under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) regulation 31 (actuarial valuations and certificates) to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] each contributing body with employees who contribute to the fund in question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] any other contributing body, guarantor or inheriting body which is or may become liable to make payments to that fund; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation 33 (special circumstances where revised actuarial valuations and certificates may be obtained) to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the Department; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the contributing body, guarantor or inheriting body for which the Committee has commissioned the valuation, report, certificate or revised certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee must also send to the Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a copy of the consolidated revenue account with which the actuary was provided under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 31(9); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a summary of the assets of the fund at the valuation date (unless such summary is contained in the report under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 31(1)(b)).
