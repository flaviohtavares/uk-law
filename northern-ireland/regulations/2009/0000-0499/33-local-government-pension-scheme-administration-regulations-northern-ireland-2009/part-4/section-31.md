# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 31

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Actuarial valuations and certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8b65b704e9d8c84e3e1c75ee59ed3185][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee must obtain— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) an actuarial valuation of the assets and liabilities of the fund (including any admission agreement funds) as at 31st March 2010 and in every third year afterwards; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a report by an actuary in respect of the valuation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a rates and adjustments certificate prepared by an actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Each of those documents must be obtained before the first anniversary of the date (“the valuation date”) as at which the valuation is made or such later date as the Department may agree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A report under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(b) must contain a statement of the demographic assumptions used in making the valuation and the statement must show how the assumptions relate to the events which have actually occurred in relation to members of the Scheme since the last valuation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A rates and adjustments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] certificate[^c-a18b34d5c59ff17a14a73645b3398f8f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] is a certificate specifying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the common rate of employer’s contribution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any individual adjustments; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the total contribution rate payable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

for each year of the period of 3 years beginning with 1st April in the year following that in which the valuation date falls. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5)[^c-326bda391fed1e35789bf6ba14502eb4] The common rate of employer’s contribution is the amount, if any, which in the actuary’s opinion, should be paid to the fund by all contributing bodies whose employees contribute to it and any other contributing body liable to contribute to the fund under regulation 33(7) (special circumstances where revised actuarial valuations and certificates must be obtained), so as to secure the fund’s solvency, expressed as a percentage of the pay of their employees who are active members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The actuary must have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the existing and prospective liabilities of the fund arising from circumstances common to all those bodies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the desirability of maintaining as nearly constant a common rate of employer’s contribution as possible; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the current version of the Committee’s funding strategy statement mentioned in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 30 (funding strategy statement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7)[^c-d309f4e3cf615a6d0134f820ba957d96] An individual adjustment is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) any percentage or amount by which, in the actuary’s opinion contributions at the common rate of employer’s contribution should in the case of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) a particular contributing body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] a particular guarantor; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] both employing authority and inheriting body if agreed under regulation 33A (apportionment), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

be increased or reduced by reason of any circumstances peculiar to that contributing body, guarantor or both of them; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (b) any other amount (whether or not expressed as a percentage of the pay of their employees who are active members) which, in the actuary’s opinion, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) a contributing body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] guarantor; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] both employing authority and inheriting body if agreed under regulation 33A, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

should pay, by reason of any circumstances peculiar to that contributing body, guarantor, or employing authority and inheriting body or by reason of any liabilities in the Scheme for which responsibility rests with the contributing body, guarantor, or employing authority and inheriting body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) A rates and adjustments certificate must contain a statement of the assumptions on which the certificate is given as respects— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a)[^c-d81e920ac23eec5331f8fc090564af53] the number of members who will become entitled to payment of pensions under the provisions of the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the amount of liabilities arising in respect of such members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) such other information that the actuary considers relevant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

during the period covered by the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) The Committee must provide the actuary preparing a valuation or a rates and adjustments certificate with the consolidated revenue account of the fund and such other information as he requests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
