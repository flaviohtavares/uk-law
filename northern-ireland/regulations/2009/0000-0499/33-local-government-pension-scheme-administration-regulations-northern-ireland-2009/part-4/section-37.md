# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 37

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Payments by employing authorities to the Committee (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dc56e6ba15cab1392c3c006c5b8c90bb][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every employing authority must pay to the Committee on or before such dates falling at intervals of not more than 12 months as the Committee may determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) all amounts from time to time deducted from the pay of its employees under these Regulations or the Benefits Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any amount it has received by deduction or otherwise under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 14(5) (contributions during child-related leave), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 16 (contributions during trade dispute absence), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 17 (contributions during absences with permission) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21 (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions) during the interval; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) any extra charge payable under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 35 (employer’s payment following resolution to increase membership or award additional pension) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 36 (employer’s further payments) of which it has been notified by the Committee during the interval; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) a contribution towards the cost of the administration of the fund, which shall include any amount specified in a notice given in accordance with regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 38 (additional costs arising from employing authority’s level of performance). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) an employing authority must pay the amounts mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(a), not later than the time required under Article 49(8) of the Pensions Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00022]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(d) does not apply where the cost of the administration of the fund is paid out of the fund under regulation 5(4) (management of the fund) of the Local Government Pension Scheme (Management and Investment of Funds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00023].

(3) Every payment under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(a) must be accompanied by a statement showing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the name, pay and band (as set out in column 1 of the table in regulation 3(2) of the Benefits Regulations) of each employing authority’s employees who is an active member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) which employees are paying contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19(1) (payment of additional regular contributions) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21(1) (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the amounts which represent deductions in each of the pay bands from the pay of each of the employees and the periods covered by the deductions, distinguishing amounts representing deductions for voluntary contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The Committee may direct that the information mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) shall be given to it instead in such form and at such intervals (not exceeding 12 months) as it specifies in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If the annual amount payable under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(d) cannot be settled by agreement, it must be determined by the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) do not apply to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
