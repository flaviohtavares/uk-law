# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 40

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Deduction and recovery of a member’s contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cd0e3366c2e1250008659df447d9e702][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An employing authority may deduct from a person’s pay any contributions payable by him under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Sums payable under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 15(2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 15(5)(b) (contributions during reserve forces service leave) may be deducted by the member’s former employer from any payment made to him under Part 5 of the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Protection Civil of Interests) (Northern Ireland) Order 1953 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00024], to the extent that they are payable in respect of the same period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The Committee may recover any contributions or sum remaining due and not deducted under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)—

- (a) as a simple contract debt in an court of competent jurisdiction; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) by deducting it from any payment by way of benefits to or in respect of the person in question under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) But the sums mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) are only recoverable under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) if unpaid for 12 months after the person ceases to perform relevant reserve forces service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) an employing authority deducts any amount in error from a person’s pay or any other sum due to him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the amount has not been repaid to him before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date of deduction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the appropriate body must pay him interest on that amount; and the due date for the calculation of interest under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 39(4) (interest) is the date of deduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Where the employee’s contributions have been paid into the fund or an admission agreement fund, the repayment and interest must be made out of that fund or the admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) The “appropriate body” for the purpose of paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the Committee where the employee’s contributions have been paid into the fund or an admission agreement fund; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the person’s employing authority where the employee’s contributions have not yet been paid into the fund or an admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
