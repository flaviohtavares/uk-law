# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 42

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Exclusion of rights to return of contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a7c88ff0e9520261c5ab23820592510f][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person is not entitled to a repayment under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41 (rights to return of contributions) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) he becomes a member again within one month and one day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b)[^c-ca0f9c63a7ba0822b6ba9e8ee4f221b3] he left his employment because of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) an offence of a fraudulent character; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] grave misconduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

  in connection with his employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) regulation 75(2) (rights to payment out of the fund) applies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) he continues in a concurrent employment in which he is an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But where paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(b) applies the employing authority may direct the payment out of the fund or admission agreement fund to him or, in a case of an offence of a fraudulent character, to him or to his spouse, civil partner, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-4253d38980424d2f02917907f4d101c1]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting partner or any dependant of his, of a sum equal to all or part of his contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A person is not entitled to a repayment under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41(1), if a transfer value has been credited to the fund or admission agreement fund for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A person ceases to be entitled to such a repayment under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41(1) if he returns to local government employment before receiving it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
