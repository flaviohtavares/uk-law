# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 38

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 38. Additional costs arising from employing authority’s level of performance (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-13bc4fadf5208bba24390362bce8884f][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where, in the opinion of the Committee, it has incurred additional costs which should be recovered from an employing authority because of that employing authority’s level of performance in carrying out its functions under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee may give written notice to the employing authority stating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the Committee’s reasons for forming the opinion mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1);
- (b) the Committee’s opinion that the employing authority’s contribution under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 37(1)(d) should include an amount specified in the notice in respect of the additional costs attributable to that employing authority’s level of performance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the basis on which the specified amount is calculated; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) where the Committee has prepared a pension administration strategy under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 61 (pension administration strategy), the provisions of the strategy which are relevant to the decision to give the notice and to the matters in sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a), (b) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (c).
