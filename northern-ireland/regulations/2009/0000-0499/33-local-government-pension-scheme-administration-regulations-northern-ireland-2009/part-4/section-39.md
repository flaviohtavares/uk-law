# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 39

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Interest (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-acc5f9ba1a446075f2c8a8735bf0749e][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee may require an employing authority from which payment of any amount due under regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 34 to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 37 (employers’ contributions or payments) is overdue to pay interest on that amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The date on which any amount due under regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 34 to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 36 is overdue is the date 10 days from the date specified by the Committee for payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The date on which any amount due under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 37 (payments by employing authorities to the Committee) (other than an extra charge payable under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 34 or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 35 and referred to in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 37(1)(c)) is overdue is the day after the date when that payment is due. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Interest due under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) or payable to a person under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 40(5) (deduction and recovery of a member’s contributions), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41(2) (rights to return of contributions) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 46 (interest on late payment of certain benefits) must be calculated at 1% above base rate on a day to day basis from the due date to the date of payment and compounded with 3-monthly rests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
