# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 67

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Application of abatement policy in individual cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c01dd940ba4c7bb947762a4ba1042b21][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a member who is entitled to the payment of a retirement pension proposes to enter a new employment with an employing authority, he must inform the employer about that entitlement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If such a member enters such a new employment he must immediately notify the Committee in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) must have regard to regulation 12 (application of abatement policy in individual cases) of the Transitional Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) must apply the policy published by it under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 66 (statements of policy concerning abatement of retirement pensions in new employment) to the member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) it may reduce the annual rate of that pension or, as the case may be, may cease to pay it, during the period while he holds the new employment, in accordance with that policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Any retirement pension paid following a request under regulation 18(1) (flexible retirement) of the Benefits Regulations is not subject to abatement under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 66 in respect of any subsequent employment with the employing authority who is his employing authority at the date of his request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
