# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 72

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72. Transfer of sums from the fund to compensate for former member’s misconduct (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a5832bc96fc75e8267f61091c124fcd7][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a person has left an employment in which he was member because of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) an offence involving fraud; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] grave misconduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

in either case in connection with that employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (b) his former employing authority in that employment has suffered direct financial loss by reason of the offence or misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the former employee became entitled to benefits under the Benefits Regulations and these Regulations or the 2002 Regulations and a forfeiture direction has been given; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] he did not become so entitled and on leaving the employment became entitled to a return of contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41 (rights to return to contributions) (whether or not he has waived his right). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If the former employing authority is the Committee, it may retain an appropriate amount in the fund or admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Otherwise, the Committee must pay the former employing authority an appropriate amount out of the fund or admission agreement fund, if requested to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) But if a contributions equivalent premium or a payment in lieu of contributions is due or has been made in respect of the former employee, the Committee may reduce an amount retained under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) or a payment under paragraph (3) by the amount of the contributions equivalent premium and by half the amount of any payment in lieu of contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) An appropriate amount is an amount not exceeding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the amount of the direct financial loss; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the amount of any contributions which could have been returned to the former employee, or paid to his spouse, civil partner, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-e5b9411c374f1b8bb4a8baaa615f13f6]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting partner or a dependant, under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 42(2) (exclusion of rights to return of contributions) or regulation 89(2) (exclusion of rights to return of contributions) of the 2002 Regulations, less the amount of any contributions which have been so returned or paid, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

whichever is the less. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) If after making a payment under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) the Committee is required to make any transfer payment under Chapter 4 or Chapter 5 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00037] or under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 77 (bulk transfers (transfers of undertakings), etc.) for a former employee, the former employing authority must repay it, if requested to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
