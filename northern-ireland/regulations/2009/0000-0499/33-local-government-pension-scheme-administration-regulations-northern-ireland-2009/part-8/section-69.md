# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 69

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 69. Interim payments directions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2f967ff794276a0c59e3f696f5bdae5e][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a person leaves an employment in which he was a member, because of an offence in connection with that employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a forfeiture certificate has been issued under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 68(1) (forfeiture of pension rights after conviction of employment-related offences) in respect of that offence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

his former employing authority may give an interim payments direction to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But his former employing authority may not give such a direction if it has given any direction under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 68(2) (“a forfeiture direction”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) An interim payments direction is a direction to make interim payments to any person who appears to the former employing authority to be a person who would be entitled to receive payment of a benefit under the Scheme if no forfeiture direction were given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The person to whom payments must be made and the amounts must be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The amounts must not exceed the amounts which the person specified would be entitled to be paid if no forfeiture direction were given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) An interim payments direction is not a decision under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 50 (first instance decisions – general) as to any person’s entitlement to a benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Payments in accordance with an interim payments direction shall be deemed to be payments in respect of a benefit to which the recipient was entitled (regardless of any contrary forfeiture direction or decision under regulation 50). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
