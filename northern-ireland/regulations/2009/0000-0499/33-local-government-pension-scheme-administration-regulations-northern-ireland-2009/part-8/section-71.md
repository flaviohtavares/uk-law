# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 71

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71. Protection of guaranteed minimum pension rights (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c3a15848cead63c9840501bb845d60b3][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) to give directions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 68(2) (forfeiture of pension rights after conviction of employment-related offences); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) to recover or retain amounts under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 70(2) (recovery or retention where former member has misconduct obligation), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

may not be exercised so as to deprive a person of his guaranteed minimum pension or any widow’s, widower’s or surviving civil partner’s guaranteed minimum pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But such power may be so exercised if the person left his employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) because of the offence of treason; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) because of one or more offences under the Official Secrets Acts 1911 to 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00036] for which the former member has been sentenced on the same occasion to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) a term of imprisonment of at least 10 years; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] two or more consecutive terms amounting in the aggregate to at least 10 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
