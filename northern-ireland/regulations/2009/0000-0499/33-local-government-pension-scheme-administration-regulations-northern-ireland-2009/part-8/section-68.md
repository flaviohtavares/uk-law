# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 68

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 68. Forfeiture of pension rights after conviction of employment-related offences (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1a421f2435ada3aa881ec139a4478f3f][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a member is convicted of a relevant offence, his former employing authority may apply to a Minister of the Crown who may issue a forfeiture certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where a forfeiture certificate is issued the member’s former employing authority may direct that any of the rights in respect of him under the Benefits Regulations, these Regulations or the former Regulations as respects his previous membership are forfeited. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A relevant offence is an offence, committed in connection with an employment in which the person convicted is a member, and because of which he has left that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A forfeiture certificate is a certificate that the offence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) was gravely injurious to the State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) is liable to lead to serious loss of confidence in the public service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If the former employing authority incurred loss as a direct consequence of the relevant offence, it may only give a direction under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) if it is unable to recover its loss under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 70 (recovery or retention where former member has misconduct obligation) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 72 (transfer of sums from the fund to compensate for a former member’s misconduct) or otherwise, except after an unreasonable time or at disproportionate cost. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) A direction under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) may only be given if an application to a Minister of the Crown for a forfeiture certificate has been made by the former employing authority before the expiry of the period of 3 months beginning with the date of the conviction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Where a former employing authority applies for a forfeiture certificate, it must at the same time send the convicted person and the Committee a copy of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
