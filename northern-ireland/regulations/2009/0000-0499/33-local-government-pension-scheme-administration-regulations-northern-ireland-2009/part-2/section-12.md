# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Re-employed and rejoining deferred members[^key-5e355bc679c90009fc8460376036dd58]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-9f7f179c54cfa0b4de5ec86e901d1c17][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] Where a deferred member becomes an active member in an employment (“the new employment”) before becoming entitled to the immediate payment of retirement benefits, he may, by giving notice in accordance with paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4), choose to have his membership in any former employment aggregated with his membership in the new employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where he has ceased to be an active member more than once, he may choose to aggregate under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)—

- (a) his membership at each of the times he so ceased; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) only such periods of membership as are specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) But a member cannot choose to aggregate any period of former membership which he could have chosen to aggregate with another period of former membership but did not before the expiry of the period mentioned in paragraph (4)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Notice for the purposes of paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) must be given in writing to the Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) while the deferred member is an active member in the new employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) before the expiry of the period of 12 months beginning with the date that he became an active member again (or such longer period as the Committee may allow). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) References in this regulation to former membership include all membership which the member was entitled to count as membership immediately before he ceased his former active membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Where a person ceases to be an active member in one employment and immediately becomes an active member in another employment, he shall be treated for the purposes of this regulation as if he were a deferred member as respects the first employment, despite never having ceased to be an active member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) But paragraph (6) does not apply to a member who is the subject of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a transfer to which the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00010] (“the TUPE Regulations”) apply; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a transfer that is treated as if it were a relevant transfer within the meaning of regulations 2(1) and 3 of the TUPE Regulations, notwithstanding regulation 3(5) of those Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
