# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Employees of community admission bodies[^c-b039c63bb469ff0824380aee3d8279a2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] Subject to the requirements of this regulation and regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 6 (admission agreements – further provisions), the Committee may make an admission agreement with any community admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The following are community admission bodies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a body which provides a public service in the United Kingdom otherwise than for the purposes of gain and which either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) has sufficient links with an employing authority for the body and the employing authority to be regarded as having a community of interest whether because the operations of the body are dependent on the operations of the employing authority or otherwise, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] is approved by the Department for the purpose of admission to the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a body to the funds of which any employing authority contributes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a body representative of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) local authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] local authorities and officers of local authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] officers of local authorities where it is formed for the purpose of consultation on the common interests of local authorities and the discussion of matters relating to local government; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] employing authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) a statutory undertaker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) a non-statutory undertaker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (f) the managers of a voluntary school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or grant maintained integrated school[^c-93cac540e7676bd218dce7fa6c2ef988] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00003]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (g) the governing body of an institution of further education within the meaning of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00004].

(3) Approval under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(a)(ii) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] may be subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit and it may withdraw approval at any time if such conditions are not met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Where, at the date that an admission agreement is made with a body mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(b), the funding contributions paid to the body by one or more employing authorities equal in total 50% or less of the total amount it receives from all sources, it must be a term of the admission agreement that the employing authority which provides funding (or, if more than one, all of them) guarantees the liability of the body to pay all amounts due from it under these Regulations or the Benefits Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) “a non-statutory undertaker” means a body who, though not authorised by any statutory provision to do so, is primarily engaged in carrying on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock harbour or pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) “a statutory undertaker” means a body authorised by any statutory provision to carry on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock harbour or pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
