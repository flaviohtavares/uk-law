# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 13

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Concurrent employments[^c-b200acb322c03ac6121d78d329a59f64][^c-fe3830f182cef4e91f10d453ee0039b0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] Where a person ceases to be an active member in one employment (“the first employment”)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) in respect of which he has at least three months’ total membership; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in respect of which he has an entitlement to benefits under regulation 5 (benefits) of the Benefits Regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

continues as an active member in another employment which was held concurrently with the first employment, the person may elect to have the former membership in respect of the first employment aggregated with membership in that other employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If he so elects, the provisions of regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 12 (re-employed and rejoining deferred members) shall apply as if references to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) his former membership or former active membership were references to his membership from his first employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the new employment were references to his concurrent employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the employment in which he becomes an active member again were references to that concurrent employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) In the case of a person to whom this regulation applies, the period of membership which will be aggregated with his membership from the concurrent employment will be equal to his membership from his first employment, as reduced under regulation 7(3) and (4) (calculation of lengths of periods of membership) of the Benefits Regulations if the first employment was part-time, multiplied by the fraction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

[formula: Whole-time rate of pay in first employment](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/33) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

where the rate of pensionable pay in each case is the annual rate of pensionable pay on the last day of the first employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
