# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Ending of membership[^c-4899811c645c2a4e3b2b30d506d424a0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] A person stops being a member in an employment if he stops being eligible for membership in that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A person who wishes to leave the Scheme must notify his employer in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A person with more than one employment may leave the Scheme if he wishes in respect of one, some or all of the employments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A member who gives a notice under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) stops being a member in the specified employment from the date the notification specifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) But, if a date earlier than the date specified in the notice or no date is specified, he stops being a member at the end of the payment period during which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Where notice is given by a person before he has been a member for 3 months, he must be treated as not having been a member in that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) A person who is a member and an employee of a transferee admission body shall be treated for the purpose of these Regulations and the Benefits Regulations as leaving a local government employment when he ceases to be employed in connection with the provision of the service or assets under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 5(2) (employees of transferee admission bodies) by virtue of which he became eligible to join the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
