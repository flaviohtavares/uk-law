# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Employees of transferee admission bodies[^key-9315af3f1a47881f31a9f8880a0e5431]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] Subject to the requirements of this regulation and regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 6 (admission agreements – further provisions), the Committee may make an admission agreement with any transferee admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A transferee admission body is a body, other than a community admission body, that is providing or will provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a service or assets in connection with the exercise of a function of an employing authority as a result of the transfer of the service or assets by means of a contract or other arrangement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a public service and which is approved by the Department for the purposes of admission to the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) In the case of an admission agreement with a transferee admission body under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(a) the employing authority, if it is not also the Committee, must be a party to the admission agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3A)[^key-e3331d816903ef2378cc145c69dfc382] Where a (2)(a) transferee admission body is exercising the functions of an employing authority in connection with more than one contract or other arrangement under paragraph (2)(a), the Committee and the admission agreement body shall enter into a separate admission agreement in respect of each contract or arrangement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Approval under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(b) may be subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit and it may withdraw approval at any time if such conditions are not met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5)[^key-d006c3f56807fb202c25b62bc0c9741b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-d006c3f56807fb202c25b62bc0c9741b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-d006c3f56807fb202c25b62bc0c9741b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) This paragraph applies where a transferee admission body undertakes to meet *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the relevant requirements of this regulation and regulation 6, and[^key-47753cd967b018f1abc909ebad6b9eb4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]—

- (a) in the case of a body under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(a), the employing authority undertakes to meet the requirements of this regulation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in the case of a body under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)(b), the Department approves the body for admission to the Scheme and the conditions, if any, to which the approval is subject, have been met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) Where paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (8) applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the Committee must admit to the Scheme the eligible employees of the transferee admission body specified by the body; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) where it does so, the terms on which it does so are the admission agreement for the purposes of these Regulations and the Benefits Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(10) Only those employees of the transferee admission body who are employed in connection with the provision of a service or assets mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) are eligible to be designated, under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] regulation 6(2)[^key-bbad9a1af5606800052d4077f6e07bbc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc], members of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
