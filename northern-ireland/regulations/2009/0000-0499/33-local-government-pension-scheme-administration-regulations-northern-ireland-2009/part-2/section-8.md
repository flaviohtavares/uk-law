# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 8

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Further restrictions on eligibility[^key-1bb9bf21a28dd8da28abae97e6ee02b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] If a person’s employment entitles him to belong to another public service pension scheme, or would so entitle him were it not for his age, that employment does not entitle him to be a member, unless that other scheme was made under Article 9 of the Order of 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) “Public service pension scheme” has the meaning given by section 150 (meaning of “pension scheme”) of the Finance Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00007].

(3) An employee of an admission body may not be a member if he is a member of another occupational pension scheme in relation to the employment in respect of which he would otherwise be eligible to be a member of the Scheme under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] regulation 6(2)[^key-c62c02ffbdb75bbdd9e3047e647a7ab6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (admission agreements – further provisions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A person can not become a member after the day before his 75th birthday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Retained or volunteer membership with a fire and rescue authority (within the meaning given to that term by Article 3 of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00008]) on terms under which the retained or voluntary member is or may be required to engage in fire-fighting does not entitle the retained or voluntary member to be a member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Any person who as a member of staff of the University of Ulster is eligible to participate in the Universities’ Superannuation Scheme is not entitled to be a member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
