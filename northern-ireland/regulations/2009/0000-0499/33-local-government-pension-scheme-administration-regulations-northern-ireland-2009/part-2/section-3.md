# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. General eligibility for membership[^c-6c6e42924fc5b881b8e74d1f83997911]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] A person may only be an active member of the Scheme if he is employed by an employing authority under a contract of employment of a duration of 3 months or more, except where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (i) he has applied for membership under regulation 9 (joining the scheme); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] section 3 (automatic enrolment) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 applies to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Subject to paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) this regulation, or any of regulations 4 (employees of community admission bodies) to 6 (admission agreements – further provisions) enables him to be one; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation 2(2) (active members) of the Benefits Regulations applies to him, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

and he is not prevented by regulation 8 (further restrictions on eligibility). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Subject to paragraph (1), a person may be an active member if he is employed by an employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
