# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 76

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 76. Contracting-out requirements affecting transfers out (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b75fee48d56dbb3db0e858bd8e4a57b5][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) There must be a deduction from the transfer payment to be made in respect of any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the amount of any contributions equivalent premium payable pursuant to section 51 of the 1993 Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) an amount sufficient to meet the liability in respect of his contracted-out rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But the amount mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) may not be deducted where the transfer payment is made to a registered pension scheme which is contracted out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Where the amount mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(a) is deducted, the Committee must use that amount to pay the premium. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Where the amount mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(b) is deducted, the Committee may use the amount in preserving the liability mentioned in that paragraph in the fund or admission agreement fund, unless the member wishes a transfer payment in respect of it to be paid to the trustees or managers of a non-contracted-out registered pension scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Contracted-out rights, in relation to a member, are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) his and his surviving spouse’s, civil partner’s or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting[^c-b5a487dd0c70da1428eccb0861c82759] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] partner’s rights to guaranteed minimum pensions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) his section 5(2B) rights (as defined in regulation 1(2) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00044]).
