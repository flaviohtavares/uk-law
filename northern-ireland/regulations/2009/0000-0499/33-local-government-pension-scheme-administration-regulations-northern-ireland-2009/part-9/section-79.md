# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 79

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Inward transfers of pension rights (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e5d09931d976ff960645b927913c91e3][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) If a person who becomes an active member has relevant pension rights, he may request the Committee to accept a transfer value for some or all those rights from the relevant transferor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2)[^key-a31f1f39ed943276a3e0fba268cb1798] Relevant rights are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) accrued rights under a registered scheme other than rights to benefits under the scheme which are attributable (directly or indirectly) to a pension credit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) accrued rights under a European pensions institution as defined in Article 269(8) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Accrued rights under a registered scheme include rights to preserved benefits and rights appropriately secured under section 19 of the 1993 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The relevant transferor is the trustees or managers of the scheme under which the transferring person’s relevant pension rights arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) But the relevant transferor for the rights specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) is the trustees or managers of the scheme, or the insurance company, to which a payment in respect of his accrued rights has been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) A request from a transferring person under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) must be made by notice in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) That notice must be given before the expiry of the period of 12 months beginning with the date he became an active member (or such longer period as the Committee may allow). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) Where a request under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) is duly made the Committee may accept the transfer value and credit it to the fund or admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
