# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 81

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Community scheme transferees (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dee5827f428d799741ee4da1e9e7a079][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The persons mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) are entitled to such rights under the Scheme as are specified in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Those persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a person who became employed by a Community institution after having been employed in local government employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a surviving spouse, civil partner, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^key-4fe5efec8f2cf6a05a146bece77cc64c]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting partner, dependant or an eligible child of such a person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) “Community institution” means a body treated as one of the Communities’ institutions for the purposes of the Communities’ scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) “the Communities’ scheme” means the pension scheme provided for officials and other servants of the Communities in accordance with regulations adopted by the Council of the European Communities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
