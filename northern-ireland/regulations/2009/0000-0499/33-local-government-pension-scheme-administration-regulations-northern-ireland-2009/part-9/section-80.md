# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 80

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Right to count credited period (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dda45fd6c6d8abc266394e556c97dbdf][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the transfer value has been accepted under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 79 (inward transfers of pension rights), the member may count the credited period as a period of membership for these Regulations and the Benefits Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00045].

(2) If the transfer value— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) is paid by the trustees or managers of a club scheme and the member has made the request under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 79 before the expiry of 12 months beginning with the date he became an active member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) represents all the rights relating to the member in that scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) has been calculated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) in a case where Chapter 4 or 5 applies, in accordance with that Chapter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] otherwise, in a manner consistent with that prescribed under the relevant Chapter, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the credited period is the period which, if used to calculate a transfer value to be paid by the Scheme, would produce an amount equal to the transfer value received. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) If paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) does not apply, the credited period must be calculated in a manner consistent with Chapter 4 or 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) In calculating the credited period under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) due allowance must be given for the expected increase in the member’s pensionable pay between the date he became a member (or, if more than 12 months later, the date on which the transfer value is received) and his normal retirement age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The Committee must give the member a written notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) stating the period of membership he may count under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) containing a conspicuous statement giving the address from which further information may be obtained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
