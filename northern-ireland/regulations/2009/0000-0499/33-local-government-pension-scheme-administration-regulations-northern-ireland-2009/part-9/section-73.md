# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 73

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Interpretation of Part (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c3051c0aeab3e3ea5c3f84060fb401b3][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- “Chapters 4 and 5” means Chapters 4 and 5 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act (transfer values and early leavers) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- “club scheme” means a registered pension scheme which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (a) (except where it is established and maintained in the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man) is a registered scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (b) provides benefits calculated by reference to final pay; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (c) is open to new participants, or is a closed scheme the trustees or managers of which also provide an open scheme which is a club scheme for new employees of the same employer and of the same grade or level of post as the participants in the closed scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (d) complies with reciprocal arrangements for the payment and receipt of transfer values with the schemes made under Article 9 of the Order of 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00038].
