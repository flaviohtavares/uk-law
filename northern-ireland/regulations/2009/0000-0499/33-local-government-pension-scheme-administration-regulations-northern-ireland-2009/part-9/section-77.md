# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 77

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Bulk transfers (transfer of undertakings), etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-db61b3af00c957734808c0b8b14e3926][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) two or more members’ active membership ends on their joining a registered non-local government scheme (“the new scheme”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) it is agreed by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the Committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the members employing authorities (if different); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the trustees or managers of the new scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

that a payment should be made under this regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (c) the members— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) agree in writing that that payment should be made instead of any payment which they otherwise might require to be made under Chapter 4 or 5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] waive any rights they might have under those Chapters by virtue of the cessation of their active membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee must not give its agreement under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(b) unless it is satisfied that the rights that each of the members will acquire under the new scheme are at least equivalent to those which he would have obtained if the transfer value had been paid to the same scheme under Chapter 4 or 5, as it applies by virtue of regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 74 (application of Chapter 4, etc.) (assuming in any case where the member would not be entitled to such a payment that he was). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The Committee must provide each member with sufficient information in writing to check that the requirement of paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) is satisfied before he agrees as mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1)(c).

(4) The Committee must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) set aside (whether in cash or in assets or both) such part of the fund or admission agreement fund (“the transfer payment”) as an actuary appointed by it and an actuary appointed by the trustees or managers of the new scheme for the purpose may agree as appropriate for the acquisition of such rights in that scheme as they may so agree; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) pay or transfer it to the trustees or managers of the new scheme for the benefit of the relevant members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The Committee must certify to the new scheme’s trustees or managers the amount included in the transfer payment which represents each member’s contributions and interest on them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Where a transfer payment is to be or has been made under this regulation, no other payment or transfer of assets shall be made from the fund or admission agreement fund by reason of membership covered by the transfer payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (6) overrides anything to the contrary in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the former Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the Local Government Pension Scheme (Amendment No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) these Regulations, or the Benefits Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
