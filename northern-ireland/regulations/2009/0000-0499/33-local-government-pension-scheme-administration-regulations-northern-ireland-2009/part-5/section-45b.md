# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 45B

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45B. Survivor benefits: guaranteed minimum pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f08e875eb7e5a5feb28363c6903b23c8][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the guaranteed minimum pension rule applies, the pension to which a person is entitled under regulations 24 (survivor benefits: active members), 33 (survivor benefits: deferred members) and 36 (survivor benefits: pensioner members) of the Benefits Regulations must be no less than the surviving spouse’s, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-c43482efba4a5e3870b445332eb0ecc3]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting partner’s or civil partner’s guaranteed minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The guaranteed minimum pension rule applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the employment in which the deceased was a member was contracted-out employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) he had a guaranteed minimum in relation to benefits under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Paragraph (1) overrides any contrary provision in the Benefits Regulations or these Regulations except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) regulation 39 (commutation of small pensions) of the Benefits Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation 68 (forfeiture of pension rights after conviction of employment related offences) of these Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) regulation 69 (interim payments directions) of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
