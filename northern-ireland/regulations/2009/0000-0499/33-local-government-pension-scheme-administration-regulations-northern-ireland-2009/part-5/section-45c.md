# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 45C

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45C. Joint liability in respect of annual allowance charge (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bfc09f52e71a60da9fbf5487f5dd1169][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a member gives the Committee notice of joint and several liability under section 237B (liability of scheme administrator) of the Finance Act 2004 in respect of the member’s annual allowance charge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where the joint liability amount specified in the notice is met by the fund, the Committee shall reduce the value of the member’s rights accrued under the Scheme and the former Regulations (if applicable), in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) “annual allowance charge” has the meaning given to that expression by section 227 of the Finance Act 2004; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) “the joint liability amount” has the meaning given to that expression by section 237B(3) of the Finance Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
