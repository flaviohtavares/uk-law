# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 47

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. Payments due in respect of deceased persons (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d65276c4145a65e93ea297e41175befa][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) applies if, when a person dies, the total amount due to his personal representatives under the Scheme (including anything due to him at his death) (“the amount due”) does not exceed the amount specified in any order for the time being in force under section 6 of the Administration of Estates (Small Payments) (Northern Ireland) Act 1967 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00028] and applying in relation to his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee may pay the whole or part of the amount due from the fund or admission agreement fund to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) his personal representatives; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any person or persons appearing to the Committee to be beneficially entitled to the estate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

without the production of probate or letters administration of his estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Such a payment discharges the Committee from accounting for the amount paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
