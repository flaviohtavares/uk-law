# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 43

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Pension increases under the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cb419ec64c4d3df9b67cf093aaa94684][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00025] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) must be paid from the fund or an admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
