# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 45A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45A. Guaranteed minimum pensions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f08e875eb7e5a5feb28363c6903b23c8][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a member’s local government employment is contracted-out employment and he has a guaranteed minimum, the member is entitled from the date he attains pension age to payment of a pension at a weekly rate equal to not less than that guaranteed minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But if the member attains pensionable age while in local government employment, the member is not so entitled until he leaves that employment unless paragraph (3) or (4) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) If the member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) continues in local government employment for a further period of 5 years after attaining pensionable age; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) does not then leave that employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the member is entitled from the end of the period mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) to payment of so much of his retirement pension as equals that guaranteed minimum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) If the member attains pensionable age while in local government employment but subsequently changes employment to employment which is not local government employment the member is entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If the member changes employment to employment which is not local government employment and the member attains pensionable age while in that employment, the member is entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Subject to regulation 17 (retirement after normal retirement age) of the Benefits Regulations, where paragraph (3), (4) or (5) applies the member may consent to a postponement of entitlement for any period during which the member remains in employment beyond pensionable age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) For the purposes of this regulation, a person has a guaranteed minimum if they have such a minimum under section 10 (earner’s guaranteed minimum) of the 1993 Act in relation to benefits under these Regulations or the Benefits Regulations and references to entitlement are to entitlement to payment of a pension in accordance with paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) “contract-out employment” shall be construed in accordance with section 4 of the 1993 Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) “pensionable age” shall be construed in accordance with section 176 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
