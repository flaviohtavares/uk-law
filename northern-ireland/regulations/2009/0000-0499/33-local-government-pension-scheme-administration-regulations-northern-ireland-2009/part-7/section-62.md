# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 62

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Exercise of discretionary functions: policy statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f467b9be5b93d9feb8593e81d1f59b50][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Each employing authority must prepare a written statement of its policy in relation to the exercise of its functions under regulations 12 (power of employing authority to increase total membership of members), 13 (power of employing authority to award additional pension), 18 (flexible retirement) and 30 (choice of early payment of pension) of the Benefits Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Before the expiry of the period of 4 months beginning with the commencement date each employing authority shall send a copy of its statement to the Committee and must publish its statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) An employing authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) keep its statement under review; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) make such revisions as are appropriate following a change in its policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Where, as a result of a review under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3), an employing authority determines to amend its policy, it must send a copy of the statement of the amended policy to the Committee before the expiry of a period of one month beginning with the date any such revisions are made and must publish the revised statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) In preparing or reviewing and making revisions to its policy statement an employing authority must have regard to the extent to which the exercise of any of the functions mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) in accordance with its policy could lead to a serious loss of confidence in the public service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
