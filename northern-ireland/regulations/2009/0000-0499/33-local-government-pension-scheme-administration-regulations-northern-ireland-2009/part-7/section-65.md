# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 65

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 65. Information to be supplied by employees (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d0dc05b51270c774746aefc5f1613f4f][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before the expiry of the period of 3 months beginning with the date a person becomes a member, the employing authority must ask him in writing for the documents specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2).

(2) Those documents are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a statement in writing listing all the person’s previous periods of employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) copies of all notifications previously given to him under these Regulations or the former Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The employing authority must also ask for those documents before the expiry of the period of 3 months beginning with the occurrence of any change as respects his employment which is material for the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A request under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) must include a conspicuous statement that it is important that the member gives full and accurate information, especially for ascertaining his rights under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The employing authority need not request any documents if satisfied that it or the Committee (if different) already has all material information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
