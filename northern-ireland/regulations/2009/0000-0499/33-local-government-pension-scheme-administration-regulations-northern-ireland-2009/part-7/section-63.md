# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 63

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 63. Communication policy statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d935d6e889c49ed660248dbf259bdf50][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee must prepare, maintain and publish a written statement setting out its policy concerning communications with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) representatives of members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) prospective members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) employing authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) In particular, the statement must set out its policy on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the provision of information and publicity about the Scheme to members, representatives of members and employing authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the format, frequency and method of distributing such information or publicity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the promotion of the Scheme to prospective members and their employing authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The statement published under regulation 108B of the 2002 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00035] must be revised and published by the Committee following such material change in its policy on any of the matters referred to in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
