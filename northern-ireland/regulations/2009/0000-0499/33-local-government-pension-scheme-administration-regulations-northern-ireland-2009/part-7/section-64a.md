# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 64A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64A. Pension savings statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a372aaa979236543d54483f912ddcdd1][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee must issue a pension savings statement to a member who has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) been an active member for all or part of the pension input period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) exceeded the annual allowance in respect of the pension input period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

by 6th October following the end of each tax year in compliance with regulation 14A of The Registered Pension Schemes (Provision of Information) Regulations 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where paragraph (1) does not apply, a member may request in writing, a pension savings statement from the Committee and the Committee must provide it by the later of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) 3 months from the date of receiving the request; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) 6th October following the end of the relevant tax year, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

and in compliance with regulation 14B of The Registered Pension Schemes (Provision of Information) Regulations 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) “annual allowance” has the meaning given to that expression by section 228 of the Finance Act 2004; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) “pension input period” has the meaning given to that expression by section 238 of the Finance Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) “pension savings statement” has the meaning given to the expression by regulation 14A of The Registered Pension Schemes (Provision of Information) Regulations 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
