# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 64

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64. Annual benefit statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-10acee229cf7c6b36f2bc23aa0b5c98d][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee shall issue an annual benefit statement to each of its active, deferred and pension credit members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The first such statement must be issued on or before 1 April 2011 and subsequent statements must issue on or before 1st April in each year after that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) An annual benefit statement shall contain an illustration of the amount of benefit entitlement in respect of rights that may arise under the Scheme, which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) has been accrued by the member at the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in the case of an active member (excluding those who are members by virtue of being variable-time employees), is capable of being accrued by him if he remains in the Scheme until his normal retirement age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The illustration shall be calculated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) in the case of active members, on the member’s pensionable pay (or, in the case of part-time employees, the whole-time equivalent) for the 12 month period ending with the relevant date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in the case of deferred members, on the member’s final pay; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) in the case of pension credit members, in accordance with regulation 90 (calculation), but with the substitution in paragraph (1) of that regulation of “the relevant date” for “normal benefit age”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The relevant date is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) 31st March prior to the date that the statement is issued; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) such later date as the Committee may choose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
