# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 60

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Exchange of information by authorities (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dddc7c18ee5a7ce053c6fd343ce316eb][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An employing authority which is not the Committee must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) inform the Committee of all decisions made by the employing authority under Part 6 or this Part concerning members; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) give the Committee such other information as it requires for discharging its functions under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the Committee makes any decision under Part 6 or this Part about a person for whom it is not the employing authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) information about the decision is required by his employing authority for discharging that employing authority’s Scheme functions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the Committee must give the employing authority that information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
