# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 61

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Pension administration strategy (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-64fff3f627a28fc4ff8070d47f6e252c][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee may prepare a written statement of its policies in relation to such of the matters mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) as it considers appropriate (“its pension administration strategy”) and, where it does so, paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (7) apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The matters are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) procedures for liaison and communication with employing authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the establishment of levels of performance which the Committee and employing authorities are expected to achieve in carrying out their Scheme functions by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the setting of performance targets; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the making of agreements about levels of performance and associated matters; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] such other means as the Committee considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) procedures which aim to secure that the Committee and employing authorities comply with statutory requirements in respect of Scheme functions and with any agreement about levels of performance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) procedures for improving communication between the Committee and employing authorities in respect of information relating to Scheme functions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) the circumstances in which the Committee may consider giving written notice to such an employing authority under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 38(2) (additional costs arising from employing authority’s level of performance) on account of that authority’s unsatisfactory performance in carrying out its Scheme functions when measured against levels of performance established under sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (b);
- (f) the publication by the Committee of annual reports dealing with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the extent to which the Committee and employing authorities have achieved the levels of performance established under sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (b); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] such other matters arising from the pension administration strategy as it considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (g) such other matters as appear to the Committee, after consulting the employing authorities and such other persons as it considers appropriate, to be suitable for inclusion in that strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The Committee must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) keep its pension administration strategy under review; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) make such revisions as are appropriate following a material change in its policies in relation to any matters contained in the strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) In preparing or reviewing and making revisions to its pension administration strategy the Committee must consult employing authorities and such other persons as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The Committee must publish— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) its pension administration strategy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) where revisions are made to it, the strategy as revised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) When the Committee publishes its pension administration strategy, or that strategy as revised, it must send a copy of it to each employing authority and to the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) The Committee and employing authorities must have regard to the current version of any pension administration strategy when carrying out their Scheme functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) In this regulation references to the functions of the Committee include, where applicable, its functions as an employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
