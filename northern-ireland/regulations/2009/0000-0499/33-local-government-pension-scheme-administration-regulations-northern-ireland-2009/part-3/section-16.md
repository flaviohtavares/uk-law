# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 16

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Contributions during trade dispute absence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6e7359346e973f869099764e98fcde3a][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) is away from work without permission for a period of one or more days during and because of a trade dispute (“a trade dispute absence”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) was a member immediately before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) that period; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] where two or more periods of absence have occurred because of one dispute, the first such period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

he may make a contribution for the relevant contribution period at the rate of 16% on his lost pensionable pay for that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A person’s lost pensionable pay is the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) his actual pensionable pay (if any); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the pensionable pay he would have received but for any trade dispute absence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

and, in determining that difference, any guarantee payments under Part 3 of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 must be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A period is a person’s relevant contribution period if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) it is co-extensive with one of the intervals at which he is required under regulation 3 (contributions payable by active members) of the Benefits Regulations to make contributions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) it includes all or part of his trade dispute absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The termination of a person’s contract of employment because of a trade dispute does not prevent this regulation applying to him if he again becomes an employee of the same employing authority and a member not later than the day after the dispute ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) A member to whom paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) may continue to pay contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21 (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions) which he was paying immediately before the trade dispute absence began; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) must continue to make any payments he was making under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 (payment of additional regular contributions) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or 20A (payment of additional contributions: survivor benefits (ASBCs))[^key-79d3d0291d1bf8541517a0057a0170ec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] of these Regulations or regulation 57 (payments to increase total membership) of the 2002 Regulations on the pensionable pay he would have received during the period but for his absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) In this regulation, “trade dispute” has the meaning given in Article 127 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00017].
