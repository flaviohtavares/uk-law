# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 23

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Separate treatment of AVCs and SCAVCs from other contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5de7442c10d4aa3eec589a5d95d3ac41][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 41 (rights to return of contributions) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 42 (exclusion of rights to return of contributions) do not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) AVCs or SCAVCs payable under these Regulations or any other agreement made for the payment of AVCs before the commencement date; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) interest on late payments which relate to AVCs or SCAVCs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The regulations mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (3) do not apply in relation to benefits under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) such a policy as mentioned in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 22(3)(b); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any agreement made for the payment of AVCs or SCAVCs before the commencement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Those regulations are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 50 (first instance decisions – general); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 68 (forfeiture of pension rights after conviction of employment-related offences); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 69 (interim payments directions); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 70 (recovery or retention where former member has misconduct obligation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
