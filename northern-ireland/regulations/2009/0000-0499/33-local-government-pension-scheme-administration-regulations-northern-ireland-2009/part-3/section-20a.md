# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 20A

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20A. Payment of additional contributions: survivor benefits (ASBCs) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1874f520b96d8d893982a70b106568d5][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member who wishes to pay additional contributions under regulation 14A (election to pay additional contributions: survivor benefits) of the Benefits Regulations must make a request to do so, in writing, to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A copy of the request must be sent to the member’s employing authority (if different) and must state the length of period (“the ASB payment period”) over which the member wishes to pay additional survivor benefits contributions (“ASBCs”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Before agreeing to the request the Committee may pass a resolution requiring the member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) to satisfy it that the member is in reasonable health; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) to produce to it a report, by an independent registered medical practitioner approved by the Committee, of the results of a medical examination (obtained at the member’s own expense), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

and if the Committee is not satisfied, it may refuse the member’s request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The ASB payment period must end before the member’s normal retirement age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The member may only pay ASBCs if the Committee notifies the member in writing that it agrees to the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The Government Actuary shall from time to time determine the amount of ASBCs required for any given amount of increased pension and may determine different amounts of ASBCs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) persons of different ages; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] men or women; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) by reference to the length of different payment periods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Where the Committee agrees to the member’s request— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) it must notify the member and the member’s employing authority (if different) of the amount of ASBCs payable in accordance with the Government Actuary’s determination, expressed as— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) an amount in pounds sterling; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] a percentage of pensionable pay; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the member must pay those ASBCs from the next payment period (as defined in regulation 9(6)) following the date of the Committee’s notification under paragraph (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) The Government Actuary may from time to time redetermine any amount determined under paragraph (6) and if he does so, the member must pay the redetermined ASBCs from 1st April next following the redetermination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) If the member pays (or is treated under regulation 20B as having paid) ASBCs for the whole of the ASB payment period, the member must be credited with the additional survivor benefits of the amount that those ASBCs purchase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
