# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 19

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Payment of additional regular contributions (ARCs) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-28967cd8e6ff94eabee392a8b1344fea][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member who chooses to pay additional contributions under regulation 14 (election to pay additional regular contributions (ARCs)) of the Benefits Regulations must make his request in writing to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The member’s request must be copied to the member’s employing authority and must state the length of the period (“the ARC payment period”) over which he wishes to pay the additional regular contributions (“ARCs”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the Committee passes a resolution requiring a member to satisfy it that he is in reasonable health by producing to it a report by a registered medical practitioner, approved by the Committee, of the results of a medical examination undertaken at the member’s own expense; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) it is not so satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

it may refuse his request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The length of the ARC payment period must be such that it ends before the member’s normal retirement age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The member may only pay ARCs if the Committee notifies him in writing that it agrees to the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The Government Actuary shall from time to time determine the amount of ARCs required for any given amount of increased pension and may determine different amounts of ARCs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) persons of different ages; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] men or women; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) depending on the length of different ARC payment periods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Where the Committee agrees to the member’s request— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) it must notify him and his employing authority of the amount of ARCs payable by him in accordance with the Government Actuary’s determination, expressed as an amount in pounds sterling; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) the member must pay those ARCs from the next payment period (as defined in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 9(6) (joining the Scheme)) following the date of the Committee’s notification under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5).

(8) The Government Actuary may at any time redetermine any amount determined under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (6) and, if he does so, the member must, from 1st April following the redetermination, pay ARCs in accordance with the redetermination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) If the member pays (or is treated under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 20 (discontinuance of ARCs) as having paid) ARCs for the whole of the ARC payment period, he must be credited with the additional annual pension of the amount that those ARCs purchase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
